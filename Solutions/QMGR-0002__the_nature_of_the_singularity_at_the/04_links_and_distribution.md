## Wider literature that bears on the question

Four families of answer appear in the world sources, and they disagree with one another at the world level. That disagreement is recorded rather than smoothed over.

1. **Past incomplete.** The BGV theorem says inflationary models need physics beyond inflation at the past boundary. The singularity theorems say the classical description is past incomplete under plausible energy conditions. Neither says the world began; both say the current frame stops.
2. **No boundary.** The Hartle-Hawking no-boundary proposal, and Lehners' review of it, replace the boundary with a geometry finite in extent but without a boundary. This is a shape that matches "no bottom" at the structural level.
3. **Bounce.** Loop quantum cosmology, as reported by the SEP entry, replaces the singularity with a minimum finite size and treats the apparent singularity as an artifact of GR extrapolation.
4. **Past eternal.** Aguirre and Gratton construct a model with no initial singularity and no beginning of time, with a boundary condition on an infinite null surface.

The framework does not adjudicate among these. It says what shape any answer must have, refuses a first stratum, and keeps the physical boundary question as a figure `gap`. A world conflict among the four families sends the derivation back to the domain census, never to a flipped verdict (see `09_watson_answer.md` WORLD section).
