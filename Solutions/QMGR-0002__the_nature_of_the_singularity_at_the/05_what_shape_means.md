## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `SPACE(TIME)` with a first moment asserted is a **name** of one outcome.
- "A boundary of composition with no primitive first term, and a channel limit rather than a ground beginning" is a **shape**.

The framework returns the second. It cannot return the first as a result, because the first is a name that would have to be settled by an instrument.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect description is "a theory's quantities diverge and it stops returning readings at a past boundary". That is checkable by anyone. The cause name ("time began", "there was a bounce", "there was a prior universe") is not what the shape delivers.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical rule says the framework stops where instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one early-universe account as *the* answer to the boundary question turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a "first moment" named from the singularity is a forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green but "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogue is exact. A theory reads a divergence at its past boundary. Several sources can produce that reading: a true beginning, a bounce, a no-boundary cap, a past-eternal phase, or simply the extrapolation limit of the theory. The reading does not determine the source. Naming "a first moment" from the divergence is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid asking about time's beginning? ANSWERED

**No.** The canonical phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Time is already one of the six terms; its definition as "the ordered succession of differences" is held, not missing. Asking whether the succession has a first element is asking about the *arrangement* of held terms, which is exactly what the framework is built to constrain. What the rule does forbid is naming a new first primitive or a specific cosmological source from the divergence reading.

So this question is answerable at the level of shape, and it is not answerable at the level of a source name. Both statements are now recorded together.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 4

I initially wrote that the framework's ban on naming applies to any answer to this question. That overstates it. The ban applies to a missing primitive or to a source read off a projection; it does not apply to characterising the arrangement of primitives already held. What remains wrong about naming "time began" from the singularity is the metamerism point in section 4, not a general ban on naming. Both readings are kept visible so neither is quietly lost. ## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that time began is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against a ground.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds the caveat that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
