## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a name does not. The physical boundary question is a `gap` for the figures and stays open to instruments. The structural verdict is that a first moment cannot be a licensed term, because time is the ordered succession of differences and the composition has no bottom and no top; what remains is a boundary of composition with no primitive first element and a channel limit rather than a ground beginning. The bundle must be split. The four world accounts are candidates over the same six atoms, and a source-discriminating instrument is required before any of them can be named. This is a `general` and `specified` result, not a `grounded` one. The strongest outreach point is the honest gap, not a claim about what happened at t=0.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name where a shape is owed, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and the shape claim.
- **undoable:** yes. The rejected readings (a first moment as a conclusion, the strained Fig 6 application, a source named from the divergence) are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes a first element from no first element would settle Domain 3; an observation that discriminates among the four accounts would license a name; a reading from beyond the GR boundary that itself has a first moment would fire the pre-registered refuter; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or an admitted unrefusable first stratum, would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
