## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles two conditions: a divergence in the classical description of the early universe, and the topological claim that time has a first moment. The first has a real anchor (CMB, light elements, geodesic incompleteness) and no figure line governs it, so it is a `gap` for the framework. The second is structural. The framework defines time as the ordered succession of differences and has no bottom and no top, so a first moment taken as a first stratum of the composition cannot be licensed. The four early-universe accounts are candidates over the same six atoms, arranged differently; only a source-discriminating instrument can name which is right.
