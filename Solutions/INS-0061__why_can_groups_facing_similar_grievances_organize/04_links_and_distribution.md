## What the wider literature bears on the question

Read as secondary corroboration only. Figures decide; a conflict returns to the census and never flips the verdict.

1. **Grievance alone is not sufficient.** Political opportunity theory states it plainly: without opportunity, grievances plus resources are not enough. Resource mobilization makes the same move from an economics frame. Olson adds the free-rider condition. These are three statements from *one broad evidence pool* (rational-choice and mobilization collective-action research), so they count as one witness, not three. They corroborate F1 and F2 after the grievance is re-mapped as an operand.
2. **The outcome is discontinuous in individual inputs.** Schelling's *"there is no simple correspondence of individual incentive to collective results"* and the threshold literature corroborate the isomer claim (F3): identical inputs can yield different aggregate states. Different field (mathematics and mathematical sociology), so an independent witness for the mechanism shape.
3. **Coordination needs multiple reinforcers and a rule, not a single message.** Centola and Macy's complex contagion and the collective-animal-behavior quorum literature corroborate F4 and F7. Two low-overlap fields (network science and animal behaviour), independent witnesses.
4. **Self-organization is possible under bounded conditions.** Ostrom's design principles, especially the right to organize and congruence with local conditions, corroborate the habitat condition in F1 and F6. But Olson predicts large diffuse groups fail while Ostrom shows small bounded communities succeed. That is a genuine world-source conflict. Returned to the census as a scale and membrane question (Fig 4 :19-20): same loop, different actors, currency and anchor. Verdict unchanged.
5. **Tarrow's own correction matters.** He now calls "political opportunity structure" misleading because opportunities must be perceived and are situational, not structural. That corroborates the channel finding (F5) and the valence finding (Fig 11 :49-51): the habitat is enumerated, not composed, and an actor's valence is relative to the residual.
