# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output below must survive a refuter's check before any theory is shown. The two-stage offer collapses on contact otherwise.

---

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the shape is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a group's capacity to organize is a property of the group and its habitat, never of the group alone. So the fact that two groups share a grievance tells you almost nothing about why one organizes and the other fragments. Under the framework's composition rule, the same atoms under a different bond are a different substance, the way isomers are. The difference is carried by the arrangement, not by the ingredient list. Two conditions have to hold and they form a union, not one lever: contact with a ground that actually settles whether the harm stopped, and renewal of the group's repertoire through contact with a differing closure. Fragmentation follows when either fails, or when the groups were pooled into one "similar grievance" bucket and the live per-group signal disappeared into the average.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that political opportunity, resources, leader quality, or grievance severity is *the* answer. Those are names from the collective-action literature mapped onto the vocabulary. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. Until provenance is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. two groups with the same grievance atoms and the same measured habitat arrangement differ in outcome, and the difference is fully accounted for by something inside the group while contact is held fixed;
> 2. a group renews its repertoire purely from inside itself, with a ground-verified new tactic and no contact with a differing closure;
> 3. someone aggregates across groups and still predicts the per-group outcome, with the aggregation shown to preserve the live signal.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The verbatim figure text, with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this folder package
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the last link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]
