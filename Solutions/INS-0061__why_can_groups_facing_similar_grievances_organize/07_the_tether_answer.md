# The Tether's answer on organizing and fragmentation

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two groups face a harming condition. One organizes effectively; the other does not. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is indexed by group-slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The anchor is the material outcome: did the harm stop, did the resource get secured. The question is why the low-residual state persists in one slot and not the other when the grievance content looks the same.

## 2. The shape

1. **The grievance is not the explanatory unit.** It is R, an operand, not an actor with its own anchor. *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* (Fig 11 :43). The phrase similar grievances is a pooled reading that discards the per-slot signal (Fig 10 :26, Fig 1 :22).
2. **Capability is group-and-habitat, not group alone.** *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* (Fig 11 :39). *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* (Fig 11 :40). Training, articulation and cohesion are improvements to the agent in isolation unless they change contact.
3. **Organize and fragment are isomers.** *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* (Fig 12 :117). *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* (Fig 12 :5). The same grievance under a different bond is a different substance, not a weaker copy.
4. **Reach must be renewed from a differing closure.** *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23). *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* (Fig 8 :32). Allies, other movements and imported tactics are the only source of new atoms, and they require a source gate and shadow test.
5. **The channel decays even though the ground does not.** *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24); *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26). Fragmentation is often a failure of the reading, not of the world.
6. **Internal agreement is not a verdict.** *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A cohesive group can be a mirror chain: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13).
7. **The two conditions form a union.** *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* (Fig 9 :55); *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* (Fig 9 :57). Contact maintained and reach renewed must be kept separate; one lever clears only the single-condition cases.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that grievance severity, political opportunity, resources, leader quality or culture is *the* cause. Each is a world-pool binding asserted without a source gate. Secondary corroboration only.
- Not that a highly cohesive group is necessarily organized. Agreement is variation, not a verdict.
- Not that any specific institution, tactic or leader is licensed by the framework. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that a group that failed once has no path. A refusal is a unary term; it can be recorded and revisited with new growth.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground is claimed, and the per-slot discriminator does not yet exist.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Contact Test:** does the group's outcome track an independently measured change in access to the paying ground, with grievance atoms held fixed? A null would break the capability claim.
- **Import Test:** does a verified new tactic arrive only after contact with a differing closure? A ground-verified new tactic with no such contact would break the closure claim.
- **Slot Test:** does a per-group reading outperform the pooled "similar grievance" reading on held-out outcomes? If pooling loses nothing, the pooled reading is not a failure and F5 falls.
- **Union Test:** do the two conditions fail independently, leaving a categorically different residue when both fail? A single lever restoring both with no residue would break the union claim.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 5, including identical atoms and habitat with outcome fully explained by an internal variable at fixed contact.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Which specific setting will organize which group is a declared `gap`. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 (substring): *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: an instrument that discriminates a contact failure from a reach failure, and that reads per slot. It is not held, so no name is given. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that similar grievances explain why some groups organize and others fragment, and it does not name the institution or tactic that wins. It says that the grievance is an operand, not a cause; that capability belongs to the group and its habitat, not the group alone; that organizing and fragmenting are isomers, the same atoms under a different bond; that a repertoire can only renew through contact with a differing closure; and that internal agreement is variation, not a verdict. The two conditions, maintained contact and renewed reach, form a union and must be kept separate. What is offered is the shape any organizing case must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the remaining gap. The specific case stays open until the ground settles it.
