# WATSON answer, INS-0061

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Why can groups facing similar grievances organize collectively in some settings but remain fragmented in others?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small in some group-slots and persist or grow in others when the grievance content appears the same? R is indexed per group, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Anchor: the material outcome, whether the harm stopped or the resource was secured, named as the kind of thing that settles a question in Fig 2 :29.

The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): the conditions that must hold for a slot's R to fall and stay low, plus an instrument that would discriminate a contact failure from a reach failure.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among group library Γ, action a and outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. It carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones (Fig 12 :30, Operators table :48). Symmetry: "similar" is an identity or threshold claim over atoms; "enough participation" is a threshold over quantities. Scale: the slot is a group, but "collective" exists only at the population scale; the membrane between individual and group action is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** organizing is a bond-and-contact phenomenon, not a grievance-severity phenomenon. Fragmentation follows when contact with a paying ground fails, when reach cannot renew through a differing closure, when internal agreement is mistaken for a verdict, or when a pooled reading hides the live per-slot signal. The two conditions, maintained contact and renewed reach, form a union and are kept separate per domain, never averaged. Which specific institution or tactic organizes a given group is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. The collective-action evidence pool corroborates the operands after re-mapping; the Olson/Ostrom conflict was returned to the census as a scale question and did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism shape, not the institutional fact.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted effort. A group failing reach would be sold better internal communication; a group failing contact would be told to import more tactics. A single lever would be applied to a union that needs two, and an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The residual, the refuters and the census are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 5, in particular (1) identical atoms and habitat with outcome fully explained by an internal variable at fixed contact, (2) a ground-verified new tactic with no contact with a differing closure, (3) a pooled reading that preserves the per-group signal on held-out outcomes, (4) a single lever restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue, or (5) bond structure shown irrelevant at fixed atoms and habitat. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing per-group persistence fully predicted by averaged grievance severity would also reverse it.
