## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 (file line 981, figure line 134, substring): *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *organizing is a bond-and-contact phenomenon; the grievance atoms are not the explanatory unit; contact and reach form a union and must be kept separate.*
- A name: *political opportunity structure is why groups organize.* That is a world-pool binding, not a figure result.

The distinction decides the whole answer. The question as asked ("why do similar grievances produce different outcomes?") is a request for a name. The figures return a shape that dissolves the premise.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "grievance", "organize", "fragment"; find the nearer terms "bond", "habitat", "closure", "channel", "union".
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So describe the effect: R stays low per group when contact and reach hold. Do not name the cause.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :55 in essay form, and it is the reason "similar grievances" is unpacked rather than accepted.
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: the anchor is *"your own question"*. Same as Fig 2 :29: the ground is what settles, not what any frame thinks.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, no name is licensed. "Political opportunity", "resources", "selective incentives", "leader quality", "culture" are all bindings asserted without a source gate in the derivation. They are admitted as secondary corroboration in `09_watson_answer.md`, never as the verdict.

The *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives an unusual direct corroboration of the independence rule from provenance: the author says of Ostrom's work and his own multi-agent work, *"The paper I derived and hers were not a carbon copy. They rhymed... And because we came from different fields, I was able to explain things she left vague, and vice versa."* Different fields, different evidence pools, same shape. That is Fig 2 :31 (*"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence"*) stated as a first-person method. It is still framework-internal, so it is corroboration of the method, not an outside witness for this question.

## 4. Why the pooled reading is the specific error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: the label "similar grievances" is a projection that does not discriminate among sources. Two groups can read as "same grievance" while differing in bond, habitat contact, closure and channel state. Naming a source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument that discriminates a contact failure from a reach failure, not a better story.

## 5. Q: Does the framework license naming the mechanism?

**No.** The ground settles a term, or a provenanced import with a shadow test licenses a name. Neither exists here. The specific institution, leader or tactic that would organize a given group is a declared `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9 and `09_watson_answer.md`. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134, substring: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The stop is recorded, not hidden.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I initially treated the phrase *"a shape, not a name"* as forbidding any mention of contact, bond or closure. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Bond, habitat, closure and channel are all figure terms already in Γ; naming them is a derivation, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is naming *which* external institution occupies the slot as though the framework had derived it. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Mode of this correction: `general`, checked against the README and Fig 13 :115.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground (an outcome measurement, a comparison across settings) settle it. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
