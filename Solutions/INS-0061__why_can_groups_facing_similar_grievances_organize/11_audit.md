- **Arity:** a relation among group library Γ, action a, and outcome o′, indexed by slot and ordered by time. At least three operands plus an ordering. A two-place reading (grievance, outcome) loses the action and the prediction, so it is rejected. The comparison at the outcome is a threshold (Operators table :48).
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The ground is an anchor and does not update on the group's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Same grievance" is an identity claim over atoms; "enough" is a comparison. Neither is mere conjunction.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *groups organize when they keep contact with a paying ground and keep importing new repertoire; otherwise they fragment.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific group situation. Result: *build alliances and keep listening to members.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a group failing reach merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the per-slot indexing. "Groups" is already a pooled reading; the abstraction licenses the very aggregation the solution refuses (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. It loses the isomer claim. "Otherwise they fragment" sounds like a weaker degree of the same thing, not a different substance under a different bond (Fig 12 :117).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Build alliances" has no place for the possibility that an ally for one residual becomes an opponent for another (Fig 11 :49-51).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a contact failure from a reach failure, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version (Fig 4 :25). The paragraph in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
