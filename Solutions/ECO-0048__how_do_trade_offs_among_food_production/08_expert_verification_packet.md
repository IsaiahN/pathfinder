# Expert verification packet: the Tether on landscape trade-offs

**What this is:** a claim in a form a landscape ecologist, ecosystem-service modeller or hydrologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on per-landscape and per-scale checks, `gap` on a single aggregate value. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken by this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Figure quotations are verbatim substrings of `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Food production, carbon storage, water quality and biodiversity are four tracked slots, each in its own unit; a residual is indexed by slot and never aggregated. A landscape "trade-off" is therefore a signed relation between two slots under one action, at one place, at one scale, with the sign relative to the slot being tracked. On a single parcel the bond is subtraction, not conjunction: land in crop at a moment is not land in perennial grass at that moment. Couplings cascade beyond first contact, and the sign can change across scale, so the field-to-watershed round trip must be measured rather than extrapolated. Two landscapes with the same composition but different configuration are isomers under a fixed atom set and need not deliver the same bundle. The four services form a union of overlapping conditions, not one lever. The framework does not name a best aggregate landscape, because no non-updating instrument settles a common currency; that slot is reported as `gap`. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the checks that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal and mathematical; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** no aggregation over the four slots. A composite ecosystem-service score discards the signal that says where to look, and a Pareto treatment returns a frontier, not a rank. The problem is a union of overlapping conditions, so one lever clears single-condition regions and leaves intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **World corroboration (low overlap):** Wikipedia, Pareto efficiency, read 2026-11-23: "various solutions can be incomparable as there is no total order relation" and "Pareto dominance is a partial order, which leads to solutions (and solution sets) being incomparable". Predicts a non-dominated set rather than a scalar winner.
- **Refuter:** a single non-updating instrument that preserves every decision-relevant slot signal and is settled by the ground; or a demonstrated total order over the four services that does not depend on the analyst's weights. Until one is shown, an index is a budget filter, not a witness.

### C2 — ecology-facing; checkable by a modeller against measured service relations

- **Statement:** the sign of a coupling is relative to the tracked slot, not a property of a land use; a parcel is exclusive; effects cascade beyond first contact. Fertilizer is a benefactor to food and a malfactor to water quality; forest is the reverse for food and a benefactor to carbon and biodiversity. There is no standing list of good land uses.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades."
- **World corroboration:** Qiu et al. 2018, Environmental Research Letters 13 054020, open access, read 2026-11-23: relationships "even reversed signs over time"; "at a given time, a pixel of land can be devoted to either crop or perennial grass, but not both"; "persistent tradeoffs between crop production and water quality"; "tight coupling of nitrate leaching vs. phosphorus yield". Cross-check against the optimal-foraging currency point (Wikipedia, Optimal foraging theory, read 2026-11-23): "the currency could be defined as net energy gain per digestive turnover time instead of net energy gain per unit time" (choose the currency and you choose the verdict). These two sources share no evidence pool with each other.
- **Refuter:** a land use or action that is a benefactor to all four slots across all measured places, scales and seasons; or a landscape in which every measured coupling is first order with the third-order links absent; or a service pair whose measured sign never changes under any driver.

### C3 — cross-scale and configuration; checkable against a controlled landscape comparison

- **Statement:** the field-to-watershed transform is lossy and can flip the sign, so the round-trip gap must be measured. Over a fixed composition, configuration is the bond: land sharing and land sparing have the same atoms and different service bundles. One lever at one scale need not remove the trade-off at another.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not."
- **World corroboration:** Qiu et al. 2018: "local tradeoffs between food production and water quality do not necessarily scale up, so reducing local tradeoffs may be insufficient to mitigate such tradeoffs at the watershed scale" and relations "differed substantially across spatial scales". Wikipedia, Landscape ecology, read 2026-11-23: "Structure is determined by the composition, the configuration, and the proportion of different patches across the landscape". Wikipedia, Conservation agriculture, read 2026-11-23, on land sharing: "allows for the conservation of biodiversity while also allowing for production of agricultural products" with "much lower yields, but also an increase in biodiversity given time". The last two partly share an ecology pool and count as one witness.
- **Refuter:** a scaling rule that preserves both sign and magnitude of every pairwise relation across drivers; or a controlled comparison with composition and total area held fixed in which configuration changes no service and no coupling. If sparing introduces a land-use class that sharing lacks, the atom sets differ and the isomer claim narrows to composition plus import.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a single best aggregate landscape, a universal trade-off curve, or a total ecosystem-service score.
- Not that a monetary index or a stakeholder weighting settles the comparison. Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- Not that any land use is good or bad in itself, or that there is a standing list of beneficial practices.
- Not that a local sign transfers to the watershed, and not that configuration wins independently of composition without a controlled test.
- Not that the framework names which land use to adopt. It returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. All findings are `general` structural or `specified` pending measurement; the valuation slot is an explicit `gap`.
- Not corroborated by agreement. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

## 5. What would settle it

1. A non-updating, source-discriminating instrument per service, so that different bundles cannot produce the same score. This is the spectrometer analogue from `05_what_shape_means.md` section 4.
2. A measured field-to-watershed round trip with `R_T` reported, not an extrapolation. Fig 4 :30 governs.
3. A controlled sharing/sparing comparison with composition and total area fixed, reading all four slots. This is the isomer test of Fig 12 :117.
4. A cascade enumeration that finds, or rules out, third-order links before modelling, per Fig 11 :52.
5. A common-currency instrument that does not move when the analyst's weights move. Until it exists, the valuation gap is reported, not filled. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22, Fig 9 :54, Fig 9 :60 and Fig 12 :125/:130. Do the four slots survive any score you actually use? (5 minutes.)
2. Read Fig 11 :49-51 and Fig 11 :52. Take one action you know well and name its benefactor and malfactor slots, plus one third-order effect. Does a standing good-act use list survive? (5 minutes.)
3. Read Fig 4 :30 and Fig 12 :117. Recall one local trade-off that did not scale up, or one fixed-composition pair whose arrangement changed a service. (5 minutes.)
4. Write down the single observation that would refute C1, C2 or C3, and send that rather than an endorsement. A failure is more useful than agreement, and agreement from someone who has already read the framework is not a witness. (5 minutes.)
