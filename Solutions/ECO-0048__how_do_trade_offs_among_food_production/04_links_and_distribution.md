## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **Qiu, Carpenter, Booth, Motew, Zipper, Kucharik, Loheide and Turner 2018** is the closest existing work to this question. Its own typology (biophysical connections, dominant drivers, combined effects, artificial scale effects) is a world-frame corroboration of the figure claim that the problem is a union of overlapping conditions. Its scale result is the world's clearest statement that a local trade-off need not scale up. Treat it as one study, not independent witnesses: all eight indicators come from one model (Agro-IBIS) and four scenarios.
2. **Bennett, Peterson and Gordon 2009, Ecology Letters**, is the standard reference for the trade-off/synergy distinction. Only bibliographic metadata were retrieved; no quote is claimed.
3. **Land sharing versus land sparing** (Green et al. 2005; Fischer et al. 2014, as described on the Conservation agriculture page) is the closest world analogue of the figure's isomer claim: the same land and the same total area, arranged differently, give different bundles.
4. **Pareto efficiency and multi-objective optimization** supply the mathematical frame for "no total order over four objectives". This is the lowest-overlap witness in the set.
5. **Optimal foraging theory** supplies the animal-behaviour frame for "the choice of currency decides what counts as best". Also low overlap.
6. The eight framework essays are carried into `05_what_shape_means.md` from the completed exemplar's canonical mapping and marked as carried, not re-opened this session. Maximal overlap with the figures, so no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full source before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the four-slot output and its refuter.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" (the line continues onto the next SVG line). Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," so report the missing valuation anchor openly instead of filling it with an index.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-slot output and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the local figure text and the Qiu et al. link, with the note that the figures, not my summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, the formula as a method to reuse with their own ground and their own refuter.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the per-slot sign claims and the five refuters without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, they read the source. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the frame.

**Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.

The chain works only if stage 1 survives. The stage-1 output in this folder is a `general`/`specified` shape with a declared `gap`, not a result. Say that in the first sentence.
