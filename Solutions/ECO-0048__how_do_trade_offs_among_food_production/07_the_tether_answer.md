## 1. The residual it answers

Four services are four tracked slots: food production, carbon storage, water quality, biodiversity. For slot *s*, parcel, season and scale, an action predicts a level and the landscape returns another, giving `R_s = |Γ(b,a)_s - o'_s|`. The ordinary word "trade-off" hides this indexing. The question asks what governs the sign and magnitude of the coupling among slots, and why it changes from field to watershed and from year to year. It does not ask for one number summing the four services; that is a decision made below by the figures, not by preference.

R by arity, symmetry, scale: four slots indexed by parcel and time (multi-slot); an ordered, signed comparison under one action, not a symmetric join; measured at field, subwatershed and watershed scale over years to decades, with a lossy transform between scales that may flip the sign. The problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.").

2. **A trade-off is a valence relation, not a property of a land use.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." 3. **A parcel is a subtraction, not a conjunction.** On one parcel, crop and perennial grass are exclusive: Qiu et al. write that "at a given time, a pixel of land can be devoted to either crop or perennial grass, but not both". Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." You can add a use; you cannot add land. **Status: triangulated** across Fig 11, Qiu et al. and the land-use source (Wikipedia, Land use, read 2026-11-23: land is "a finite resource" with competing demands).

4. **Coupling cascades beyond first contact.** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades." The figure's example is DDT reaching eagles three steps out. Here nutrient application cascades to nitrate and phosphorus loss, to surface-water quality, to hypoxia and biodiversity. Qiu et al. report "persistent tradeoffs between crop production and water quality" and a "tight coupling of nitrate leaching vs. phosphorus yield". **Status: single-source on the figure rule**, corroborated by Qiu et al.; the particular chain is `specified` to each landscape.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single best aggregate landscape, a total ecosystem-service score, or a universal trade-off curve. Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- Not that any land use is good or bad in itself. Valence is relative to the tracked slot (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that a local sign survives at the watershed. The round trip has to be measured (Fig 4 :30).
- Not that configuration matters independently of composition without a controlled test; the isomer claim is conditional on fixed atoms.
- Not that six monetary valuation methods supply an anchor, and not a ranking of stakeholders by agreement. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by measurement, and this derivation cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 4. Falsifiers written before the conclusion

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is withdrawn if any of the following holds:

1. A single non-updating scalar preserves every decision-relevant slot signal and is settled by the ground (breaks 1 and 8).
2. A land use or action is a benefactor to all four services across all measured places, scales and seasons (breaks 2).
3. With composition held fixed, configuration changes no service and no pairwise coupling (breaks 6).
4. A scaling rule preserves both sign and magnitude of every pairwise relation from field to watershed across drivers (breaks 5).
5. All measured couplings in a landscape are first order, with no third-order cascade (breaks 4).
6. The four services are derived from the other members of the six-term substrate so that two collapse into one state (breaks the re-census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 2). Fig 13 :35 "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity."

Until one of these is run, no part of this answer may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The ecosystem-service analogue of the spectrometer point in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 4 is a per-slot instrument that does not move when the analyst's weights move, plus three specific checks:

- **Cross-scale round trip:** carry each measured pairwise sign from field up to watershed and back down, and measure `R_T` rather than assuming it is zero. Fig 4 :30 governs.
- **Isomer test:** hold composition and total area fixed, vary configuration, and read all four slots. This is the controlled version of Fig 12 :117.
- **Cascade test:** enumerate third-order links before modelling, then check whether the residual that mattered was three steps out, as in Fig 11 :52.
- **Valuation:** produce a non-updating common-currency instrument. Until then the gap stands, per Fig 13 :134.
