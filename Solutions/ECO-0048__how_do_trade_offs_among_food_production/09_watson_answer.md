## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the figures license about the question from `Questions/ecology.txt` line 59: how trade-offs among food production, carbon storage, water quality and biodiversity emerge across landscapes. Option A: answer with one aggregate "total ecosystem service" value and a best landscape. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Four service slots indexed by parcel, season and scale: `R_s = |Γ(b,a)_s - o'_s|`. Arity: multi-slot, with at least six pairwise compounds plus higher-order intersections. Symmetry: a coupling is an ordered, signed comparison under one action, not a symmetric join; on one parcel the bond is subtraction. Scale: field, subwatershed and watershed over years to decades, with a lossy transform. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The problem is a union, not one hard question.
- **D1 biophysical production.** Actors: crops, perennial grass, forest, wetland, soil, nitrogen, phosphorus, water, pollinators, species. Currency: biophysical flows, each in its own unit (yield, Mg C per ha, nutrient load, richness). Anchor: flux towers, gauges, soil and species surveys. Nameable per-slot anchor.
- **D2 land allocation and management.** Actors: farmers, planners, policies, parcels. Currency: finite exclusive land plus actions (tillage, nutrient application, set-aside). Anchor: the D1 outcomes, which do not update on the manager's belief. Nameable; union of conditions.
- **D3 spatial configuration.** Actors: patches, corridors, matrix, edges, ecotones. Currency: arrangement, composition, configuration, proportion. Anchor: mapped pattern plus measured flows. Nameable; isomer claim conditional on fixed atoms.
- **D4 valuation and aggregation.** Actors: analysts, models, economists, stakeholders. Currency: money or composite index scores. Anchor: none named that does not update on the analyst's own weights. **No nameable anchor, `gap`.**
- **D5 science and observation.** Actors: modelling teams, monitoring programs. Currency: evidence. Anchor: long-term measurements, but independence is low where teams share one model. Nameable; independence owed.
- **Partition or union.** D1, D2 and D3 are overlapping conditions on the same landscape, not a partition. D4 is a separate frame attempting to collapse four incommensurable slots into one scalar, and it is refused. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Enumerate the habitat, then measure each slot, then detect pairwise signs, then run the cross-scale round trip, then import a differing frame where the closure cannot hold, and only then attempt valuation. Valuation never precedes measurement because D4 has no anchor.
- **Strain test applied before concluding.** An early mapping of the four services onto the six substrate terms strained: two services would have to be the same state, against Fig 13 :35 "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity." Re-census puts the services in the enumerated habitat and the library, not in the six. A second live strain is the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim (D3).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the aggregate winner does not. Trade-offs emerge as valence-relative, signed couplings among four per-slot residuals on a finite exclusive habitat, propagated by cascades, reconfigured by spatial arrangement, changing across scale and forming a union of overlapping conditions. The valuation slot is a `gap` until a non-updating instrument settles a common currency. The isomer claim survives only over a fixed atom set. Verdicts are per domain, never averaged; this is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `general` structural core and an explicit `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices directly. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four-slot shape and its refuters can be superseded without withdrawing any figure line; adding a non-updating valuation instrument closes only the D4 gap, and a controlled configuration test narrows only D3. A sent claim can be corrected by a later packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot source-discriminating instrument that preserves every decision-relevant signal would break the no-aggregate claim; a land use beneficial to all four slots at all places, scales and seasons would break the valence claim; a scaling rule preserving sign and magnitude would break the round-trip claim; a fixed-composition comparison with no configuration effect would break the isomer claim; all-first-order couplings would break the cascade claim. Agreement from readers who already hold the framework is not such an instrument. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
