## Restatement as an effect

A landscape is a finite set of parcels. Each parcel can carry one of several uses: crop, pasture, forest, wetland, settlement. A management action on a parcel changes several quantities at once: yield, carbon stored or emitted, nutrient loss to water, and habitat for species. The question asks for the mechanism by which a change in one of these quantities comes to be coupled, positively or negatively, with changes in the others, and why that coupling looks different when you move from a field to a watershed.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term: a rule that, for a given residual service-slot and a given place and scale, names the conditions under which another slot rises or falls, and states what would reverse the sign. The sought-for shape is written down and made refutable before the run (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan and no request for trust or agreement.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless, and a reader who has absorbed the theory before checking the output has joined the frame being checked.
