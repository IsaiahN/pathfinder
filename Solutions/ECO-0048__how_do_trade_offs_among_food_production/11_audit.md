- **Partition or union.** D1 to D3 overlap on the same landscape. D4 is a separate frame trying to collapse incommensurable slots into one scalar. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat, index each slot, measure with its own instrument, detect signs, run the cross-scale round trip, import a differing frame where closure fails, refuse before mint, and only then attempt valuation.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (slot indexing), Fig 11 :49 (valence), Fig 4 :30 (round trip), Fig 12 :117 (isomer) and Fig 13 :134 (gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: four slots, sign relative to the slot, subtraction on a parcel, cascades, cross-scale gap, configuration as bond, union of conditions, no value anchor.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management recommendation for a watershed, for example set aside a fixed share of land and report one monetary ecosystem-service total.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which a single monetary total restores to one scalar, exactly the loss Fig 1 :22 names;
- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim, which a set-aside that introduces a new land-use class breaks, turning isomerism into composition plus import;
- the **declared valuation gap**, which an index quietly fills by authoring weights and then treating them as measured, against Fig 10 :47.

One thing survives cleanly: a coupling is a sign between two named slots at a named place and scale, and the cross-scale gap must be measured. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a reported gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
