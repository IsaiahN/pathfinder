## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form a trade-off answer must have before you can name which land use wins: four slots kept separate, sign stated relative to the tracked slot, parcel exclusivity, a measured cross-scale gap, configuration as bond, and an open valuation slot. It constrains; it does not fill. "There is a trade-off between food and carbon" is a shape claim. "Set aside 30 percent of the landscape" is a name, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" (the sentence continues on the next SVG line).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it".

For this question the vocabulary closest to the residual is the measured service indicators, not the six abstract substrate terms. That is why F9 enumerates the habitat before applying any composition rule.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as the answer to a service slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. This is the canonical reason F7 refuses the aggregate index until a non-updating anchor exists.

## 4. The metamerism point still stands

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogue is a measured composite "ecosystem service score". Several different bundles of yield, carbon, water and biodiversity can produce the same score, so the score cannot tell you which bundle you have, and therefore cannot tell you which action to take. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, per service, not a better aggregate.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own reading of the shape-not-a-name rule

I initially overstated the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in the library. Land-use types, nutrient loads, water quality indicators and species richness are not among the six substrate terms and are not missing primitives either: they are the enumerated habitat. Naming them is a binding from the world frame, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not the invention of a seventh substrate member.

What remains forbidden is claiming that such a binding moves the closure wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom") without a source gate, and treating a chosen valuation weight as a measured anchor (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"). The correction is kept visible so neither the original error nor its narrowing is silently lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known ecologists agreeing with the four-slot shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to check the shape against measurements.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What shape means here, in one sentence

The shape is a per-slot, valence-relative, cross-scale-checked, configuration-sensitive constraint on how four incommensurable services can move together, with the valuation slot left open until a non-updating instrument settles it.
