# The Tether's answer on CPT violations and a unified theory

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the name of a unified theory, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A frame predicts that the combined C, P, T transform leaves observables invariant. Per comparison slot (kaon mass and lifetime, antihydrogen spectrum, neutrino oscillations, matter-antimatter asymmetry), the predicted difference is zero and the measured difference is a bound. R = |Gamma(b,a) - o′| is indexed per slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The question asks what a positive violation would be and what it would license about a unified theory. The four domains form a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54, :57): a theorem premise (D1), a measurement ground (D2), a candidate framework space (D3) and the symmetry structure (D4) overlap, and one lever leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape

1. **A CPT violation is a refusal, not an addition.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. The regularity "CPT invariance holds" is held and generating predictions; a genuine positive signal would show it false. What is owed is a negation of its standing as a composition base, not a new particle or field to explain the signal (F1). `specified`.
2. **The refusal is unary and its price is open.** Fig 5 :56: *"Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain."* Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Do not price a refusal with the bond formula. `general`.
3. **Symmetry decides whether the mirror readings are one term or two.** Fig 12 :92: *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."* Under the symmetry, matter and antimatter are spellings of one term. Under a broken symmetry they become distinct terms, isomeric in Fig 12 :117's sense: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"*. `general` structurally, `specified` for the physics reading.
4. **The record does not shrink; the reach does.** Fig 6 :42-43: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* The theorem stays in the record with its provenance. What falls is the family of theories that depended on composing over exact CPT invariance. `general`.
5. **A surviving theory must come from a differing closure, with a gate.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Any candidate (string theory, loop quantum gravity, noncommutative geometry) is an import and needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-14). None is returned as a name. `specified`.
6. **No final theory.** A unified theory in the sense of a last room is forbidden: Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. "A unified theory" is the sought-for shape held as a preference (Fig 5 :62), so it was bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 before being reasoned about. `gap` for the name, `general` for the refusal of the final room.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that CPT has been violated. Every current result is an upper bound (Greenberg 2002; ALPHA 2025; CPLEAR 2000; Kostelecky and Russell data tables).
- Not that Greenberg's theorem (CPT violation implies Lorentz violation) is figure-derived. It is a real world result and secondary corroboration; figures are primary and decide.
- Not that Lorentz invariance is exact or that locality holds at all scales. Those are premises under test, not conclusions here.
- Not that the framework returns the name of a unified theory, or that any candidate (string theory, the SME, loop quantum gravity) is the answer.
- Not that a matter-antimatter spectral difference is automatically CPT violation. It may be a Lorentz effect in a CPT-symmetric theory if the frames differ (Baker et al. 2025).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,phi)` for a unified theory, and no positive signal exists.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Same-frame comparison.** A hydrogen and antihydrogen 1S-2S comparison performed in the same reference frame, with sidereal and annual variations controlled, would settle whether a difference is a CPT signal or a Lorentz artifact.
- **Independent slot replication.** A positive signal in the kaon system and a different instrument (antihydrogen or neutrinos), with genuinely low overlap (Fig 2 :31-32), would count as two frames ranging against a ground rather than one pool talking.
- **Theorem-premise discriminator.** An instrument that distinguishes a failure of locality from a failure of Lorentz invariance would name which held term the refusal points at. Greenberg (2002) says at least one must fail; the figures say which one is owed a refusal, and that is an instrument question.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a positive signal with all premises intact and no refusal owed; a figure line returning a final theory; a binary-bond violation; a demonstration that the four programmes are one evidence pool.
- **Threshold test:** does a CPT-odd SME coefficient move from an upper bound to a measured nonzero value in a same-frame, independently replicated reading? Until it does, the verdict at D2 is `unreached`, and that is a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instruments already return readings (bounds, sidereal-variation limits, mass and frequency comparisons), so the instrument exists and must be sharpened rather than replaced (Fig 6 :32-33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading"*). What is missing is a positive same-frame signal and a discriminator among sources. Indiscernibility is not identity: a null result and an exact symmetry look the same from inside the frame, and the record of the route is what keeps that stop checkable. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest.

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say CPT is violated and does not produce a unified theory. It says that a CPT violation, if one were observed, would not be a new field to add to physics: it would be a refusal of a held invariance, owed a negation, which changes which theories are reachable while leaving the record of the theorem intact. Symmetry decides whether matter and antimatter are two spellings of one term or two distinct, isomeric terms, and breaking CPT would change the bond, not the atoms. Any theory that could accommodate such a violation would have to come from a differing closure, with a provenance gate and a shadow test; it could not be a final theory, because there is no last room. What the framework returns is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the same-frame instrument that would close the question. The ground has not yet paid for any of it.
