## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **A violation is not a small correction.** Greenberg (2002) proves that CPT violation in an interacting theory entails Lorentz violation, and that theories with different particle and antiparticle masses must be nonlocal. This is the world-side counterpart of the figure finding that a violation refuses a held premise rather than adding a term (F1 and F2). It is secondary corroboration, not the authority.
2. **All current readings are bounds.** The ALPHA bound on a CPT-odd SME coupling is (0.4 +/- 1.2) x 10^-9 GeV^-1; CPLEAR reaches a few 10^-4 in the kaon delta_CPT parameter; the SME data tables compile maximal attained sensitivities across matter, photon, neutrino and gravity sectors. Fig 8 :39 applies: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
3. **Independence is a property of instruments, not of agreement.** Antihydrogen spectroscopy, kaon interferometry, Penning traps and neutrino oscillations are genuinely different instruments. Their agreement is worth something under Fig 2 :31, but only if the comparison is in the same frame. The ALPHA paper explicitly warns that a hydrogen-antihydrogen difference across frames may be a Lorentz effect in a CPT-symmetric theory, not a CPT violation.
4. **Mirror pairs as isomers, at low overlap.** The chemistry literature on parity violation in chiral molecules gives the same structural shape as Fig 12 :117: same atoms, mirror arrangement, tiny energy difference, and no claim that the effect has been seen in the relevant reaction. This is the one genuinely low-overlap witness for the symmetry-to-distinct-term reading.
5. **A framework cannot validate itself.** Both framework essays say so in different words. This is why the first contact carries the stage-1 claim and its refuter, not the architecture.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer the links after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if a CPT violation were a refusal before giving them the source.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people who already like the idea produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Record the stage-1 verdict before stage 2 is shown. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
