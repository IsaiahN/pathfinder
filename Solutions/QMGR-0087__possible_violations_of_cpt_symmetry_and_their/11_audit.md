- **Symmetry:** the question is about the symmetry of an arrangement, not the name of a group. Fig 12 :92 (*"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*) governs. ## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's four-domain shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a broken CPT invariance is a refusal of a held premise, and a theory that survives it must come from outside the current closure.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific working situation. Result: *add a CPT-odd operator to the Lagrangian and fit its coefficient.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57 (*"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Coming back down, it tells a researcher to add an operator even where the real failure is a cross-frame reading or a missing same-frame comparison (F6).
2. It loses the unary/binary distinction. "Refusal" becomes "an added term", which is exactly the mistake recorded as a conflict with the SME representation in `09` WORLD. The operator price is open (Operators :77-78) and the abstraction cannot carry that.
3. It loses the per-slot indexing. The abstraction says "CPT invariance broke" as if it were one global event; coming back down it licenses averaging the kaon, antihydrogen and neutrino bounds into a single verdict, against Fig 1 :19.
4. It loses the provenance gate. "Outside the current closure" does not say which frame, with what source gate, or which gap the import explains (Fig 8 :12-14).
5. It loses the honest stop. The abstraction is silent on whether a positive signal exists; coming back down it invites a theory of a violation that has never been observed (Fig 8 :39).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
