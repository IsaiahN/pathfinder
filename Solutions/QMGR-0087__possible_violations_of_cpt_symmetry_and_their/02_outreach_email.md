# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive the reader's check. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the CPT claim is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a CPT violation, if one were ever observed, would not be a new particle or field to add to the Standard Model. In the framework's own vocabulary it is the fifth kind of residual: a case where a regularity we hold turns out to be false, so what is owed is a refusal of that regularity, not an explanation of the signal. The figures put it plainly: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. And the refusal is a negation, which is not one of the bonds that join things: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*
>
> The second part of the output is about the phrase "unified theory" in the question. The framework cannot return a name for it. A theory of everything would be a final room, and there is no last room. What it can return is a shape: any theory that survives a genuine CPT violation would have to come from a frame whose closure differs from local Lorentz-invariant quantum field theory, with a record of where the new assumption came from and a check that it fixes the gap it was brought in for.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that CPT has been violated. Every current test, from neutral kaons to antihydrogen spectroscopy, is a bound, and a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence. I am also not claiming that Greenberg's theorem (CPT violation implies Lorentz violation) is derived from these figures; it is a real physics result and it corroborates the shape, but it is not the authority here. I am not claiming the measurement is a verdict on the framework: the ground settles the measurement, frames do not negotiate with it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a positive CPT signal that leaves every held premise intact and is fully accommodated by adding a new composable term, with nothing refused;
> 2. a figure line that returns the name of a final theory, or permits a last room;
> 3. a CPT violation shown to be a binary relation between two operands rather than a unary refusal;
> 4. the antihydrogen, kaon, neutrino and cosmological asymmetry programmes shown to be one evidence pool rather than independent instruments.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text and formula (SVG and text): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Worked architecture and the six-term derivation: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - The two public papers that bear directly on this question (Greenberg 2002 on CPT implying Lorentz violation; ALPHA 2025 on antihydrogen 1S-2S): links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## What is deliberately absent from the email

- No request for belief, endorsement, or a show of hands.
- No personal emails of named individuals, no receptiveness ranking, no bulk send.
- No claim that this is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
- No claim that the framework is the only true account of CPT or of unification.
- No request to accept the framework before judging the output. The framework is offered only at stage 2, after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
