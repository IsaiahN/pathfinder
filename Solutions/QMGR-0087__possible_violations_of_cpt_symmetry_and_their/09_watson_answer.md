## QUESTION

Possible violations of CPT symmetry and their implications for a unified theory.

Restated as an effect: for each comparison slot s (kaon mass and lifetime, antihydrogen 1S-2S frequency, neutrino oscillation parameters, matter-antimatter asymmetry), the frame predicts a zero matter-antimatter difference and the ground returns o′. R = |Gamma(b,a) - o′| is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The anchor is measurement and experiment (Fig 2 :29). What is unbound is the phrase "a unified theory", the sought-for shape that the loop cannot see until it is made an ordinary term (Fig 5 :62).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict, kept per domain and never averaged: a CPT violation, if real, is a refusal of a held regularity and owes a unary negation, not an added atom; it changes the reach while the record keeps the theorem; a broken symmetry turns the matter and antimatter spellings into distinct isomeric terms; any theory accommodating it must come from a differing closure with a source gate and shadow test; and no final theory is returned. Mode `specified` on domains D1 to D4, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` for the name of a unified theory. The world evidence corroborates the mechanism shape at low overlap (chemistry) and the experimental discipline at high overlap (particle pool), and the one conflict (SME addition versus refusal) was returned to census and re-mapped as an instrument. All current results are bounds; the positive signal is `unreached`, which is a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134).

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected theoretical work. A community that treats a CPT violation as a new field to add will spend its budget minting atoms inside a closure that the violation itself refutes, and will average per-slot bounds into a false "CPT is exact" verdict. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :43). The shape, its six falsifiers and the provenance of every quote are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 4, in particular (1) a positive signal with all premises intact and nothing refused, (2) a figure line returning a final theory, (3) a violation shown to be a binary bond, (4) mirror readings still one term after the symmetry breaks, (5) all CPT programmes shown to be one evidence pool, or (6) a pure re-arrangement of the current closure accommodating a genuine violation with no import. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool returning a measured, same-frame, nonzero CPT coefficient would also move D2 from `unreached` to `grounded` and would fire F1.
