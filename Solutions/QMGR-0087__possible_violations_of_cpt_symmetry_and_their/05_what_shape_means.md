## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the carried *Library of Babel* mapping: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Not a shape:** "the unified theory is string theory" or "the unified theory is the SME". Those are names from the world frame.
- **A shape:** a theory that can accommodate a genuine CPT violation must come from a closure that differs from local Lorentz-invariant QFT, must carry provenance for the imported assumption, must explain the gap it was brought in for, and cannot be a final room.
- **Also a shape:** a CPT violation is a refusal of a held regularity, not an added atom.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A positive, replicated, same-frame CPT signal would settle the *existence* of a violation at that slot. It would not settle the name of a unified theory.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. String theory, loop quantum gravity and noncommutative field theory are candidates from the world frame. Each must be recorded as an import, with the gap it explains, before any verdict weight is given.

The carried *They're Scanning Books* mapping: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess. Turning a guess into *the* answer to a physics question would make the choice invisible.

## 4. The source error and this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here the analogous lesson is sharp and documented: the ALPHA paper states that a hydrogen-antihydrogen difference taken in different frames *"would not necessarily be a signal of CPT violation but could instead be due to a Lorentz violation in a CPT-symmetric theory"* (Baker et al. 2025). A single reading does not determine its source. What is owed is a same-frame instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A unified theory claiming to validate itself is the named collapse. Fig 8 :34: *"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency. Tarski: truth for a language is not definable"*.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier overstatement

An earlier reading of "shape, not a name" was applied as a blanket ban on naming anything. That is too broad. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive: *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*. CPT and Lorentz invariance are already held terms in local QFT; a specific violating operator (an SME coefficient) is a binding from the physics frame onto a term the theory can already express, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (i) inventing a term the framework cannot compose, (ii) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :25 - *"An import adds no atom to the world."*), and (iii) presenting a name as a returned result. Both the original reading and this correction are kept visible; neither is silently erased.
