**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structure, `gap` on the name of a unified theory, not `grounded`.** No positive CPT signal is claimed. ## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A CPT violation, if one were observed, is not a new particle or field to add to a theory. In the framework's notation it is the fifth kind of residual: a case where a regularity we hold is false, so what is owed is a refusal (a unary negation), not an explanation. Symmetry decides whether the matter and antimatter readings are two spellings of one term or two distinct isomeric terms; breaking CPT changes the bond, not the atoms. The record keeps the theorem; the reach loses the theories that composed over exact CPT invariance. Any theory that could accommodate the violation must come from a closure differing from local Lorentz-invariant quantum field theory, with provenance and a shadow test, and cannot be a final theory. What is returned is that shape, not a name. All current tests are bounds.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5, 6 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** a genuine CPT violation is a REFUTED residual and owes a refusal, not an added atom. The record is monotone; the reach is not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :53 - *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 5 :56 - *"Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain."* Fig 6 :42 - *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* Operators :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*
- **Refuter:** a positive CPT signal that leaves all premises (locality, Lorentz invariance, Hermitian Hamiltonian) intact and is fully accommodated by adding a composable term to the existing Lagrangian, with nothing refused. Also: a demonstration that the reach is monotone under refutation. If either holds, C1 fails.

### C2 - symmetry structure; checkable by reading Figure 12, then by comparing with the chemistry analogue

- **Statement:** while CPT is a symmetry of the arrangement, matter and antimatter readings are spellings of one term. If the symmetry breaks, the relation is no longer symmetric and the readings become distinct, isomeric terms: same atoms, different bond, different substance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :92 - *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."* Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :23 - *"this bond is symmetric, so the four are one term and nothing consults the bond to find that out"*.
- **What to check:** is the mirror-pair reading faithful? In the world, hydrogen and antihydrogen share a bound-state structure with charge-conjugate constituents, and the ALPHA experiment constrains their 1S-2S difference. Chemistry supplies a genuinely low-overlap structural analogue: enantiomers are isomers with a predicted parity-violating energy difference (Demming 2019), unobserved at the required magnitude in the Soai reaction.
- **Refuter:** a measured matter-antimatter difference that disappears under a same-frame coordinate change (it was a frame effect, not a bond change), or a case where a broken discrete symmetry still leaves the two readings one term under every figure composition test.

### C3 - physics-facing, at the ground; checkable by an experimentalist or phenomenologist

- **Statement:** all current CPT results are bounds, and a hydrogen-antihydrogen difference taken in different frames is not a clean CPT signal. Independence is a property of instruments, not of agreement among papers, and a surviving theory needs a differing closure with a provenance gate.
- **What to check:** the ALPHA 2025 bound on a CPT-odd SME coupling, (0.4 +/- 1.2) x 10^-9 GeV^-1; the CPLEAR bound on Re(delta_CPT) at a few 10^-4; the SME data tables across matter, photon, neutrino and gravity sectors. The ALPHA paper states that *"CPT violation could still arise in ways that are not captured by the SME"* and warns that a cross-frame difference could be a Lorentz violation in a CPT-symmetric theory. Greenberg (2002) proves that CPT violation in an interacting theory entails Lorentz violation; this corroborates the structural claim but is not the authority.
- **Figure lines governing the check:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a positive, independently replicated, same-frame CPT signal; or a demonstration that the antihydrogen, kaon, neutrino and cosmological programmes are one evidence pool; or a theory that accommodates a genuine violation by pure re-arrangement of the current closure with no new atom.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that CPT has been violated. No positive signal exists in the sources read.
- Not that Greenberg's theorem is derived from the figures. It is a world result and corroboration only; figures are primary.
- Not that Lorentz invariance or locality is exact. They are premises under test.
- Not that the SME is the final framework. The ALPHA paper itself says violation could arise outside it.
- Not that the framework returns the name of a unified theory, and not that string theory, loop quantum gravity or any other candidate is the answer.
- Not that a cross-frame matter-antimatter difference is automatically a CPT violation.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the shape claim, and the entire packet is a simulation checked for internal consistency.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A same-frame hydrogen and antihydrogen 1S-2S comparison with sidereal and annual variations controlled.
2. A positive signal in the kaon slot replicated by a genuinely different instrument (antihydrogen or neutrinos), with low overlap (Fig 2 :31).
3. An instrument that discriminates a failure of locality from a failure of Lorentz invariance, naming which premise the refusal points at.
4. A figure-grounded derivation that returns the name of a unified theory (would refute the `gap`), or a positive CPT signal with all premises intact and no refusal owed (would refute C1).
