## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form of a design that could settle the source question, stated before any culture or item is named. It constrains: per slot, low overlap, non-updating anchor, familiarity manipulation, open attribution. It does not fill: it does not name Group A or Item 7 as the source.

- Shape: *a source-discriminating design over item-and-person slots.*
- Name: *"the gap is due to test familiarity in culture X."* The framework does not return that.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The effect description here is: holding the local task fixed, changing exposure changes the score by a measurable amount. Anyone can run that check.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*; the paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question that means: once you can state the exact manipulation that would settle the source, you no longer need the framework to tell you the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical rule: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here that would be a psychometric frame (DIF, invariance) or an experimental frame (familiarity manipulation) brought in with its provenance recorded, not a new construct invented inside the framework.

The poetic line from the carried mapping still applies: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six"* (*They're Scanning Books*, carried via the exemplar, not re-opened). Declaring "reasoning ability" as the measured construct makes a choice visible; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why "reading the source off the score" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The cross-cultural test score is the green reading. "Reasoning ability" and "test familiarity" are candidate sources. Naming one from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (DIF, invariance, a familiarity manipulation, a locally anchored task), not a better definition of "reasoning."

Fig 11 :70 closes the loop: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A standardised test transplanted into a new habitat proves that the items can be answered; it does not prove capability in that habitat.

## 5. Q answered: what licenses a particular source attribution in this question?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a design shape. A particular attribution is licensed only by a source-discriminating instrument that pays against a local non-updating ground, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the attribution is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. `D4` is therefore a `gap` (section 6 of `01`), of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind: instruments already exist that return something failing to resolve, so no new atom is owed.

Does the record commit the forbidden error? The naive framing does. It names "reasoning ability" as the source of a score gap, which is a source named from a projection. The correct form keeps the attribution open and binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term with a refuter (Fig 5 :65-66).

## 6. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I first wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any construct in this domain. It does not. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. "Reasoning ability" and "test familiarity" are bindings onto vocabulary the research frame already holds; naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong is naming one as *the* source of a reading without a discriminator, and calling a standardised test an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*; a familiarity manipulation adds no atom). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of researchers selected because they are likely to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration.
