## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a cross-cultural logical-reasoning score gap can be attributed to reasoning ability without confusing test familiarity with ability. Option A: read the gap as ability. Option B: require a source-discriminating design before any attribution. What each leads to: A produces a culture-level verdict whose source may be the instrument talking, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a design shape, six pre-registered refuters, and an open attribution until a local ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** One score gap with two candidate sources: ability and familiarity. Arity: at least two overlapping conditions, so a union compound, not a partition (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*). Symmetry: the reading is a symmetric function of the sources, so it cannot choose an arrow between them; ⋛ compares quantities (Operators table :48-49), it does not attribute causes. Scale: first order at the item-and-person slot, second order at the group comparison, third order at the study design that selects the instrument.
- **Domain 1, measurement and instrument.** Actors: items, scoring key, format, language, administrator, the two groups. Currency: observed response versus prediction, per slot. Anchor: reasoning performance in the local habitat, which does not update on the key. Union: the two sources overlap in every item.
- **Domain 2, the two conditions.** Actors: ability-bearing and familiarity-bearing processes. Currency: score mass per slot. Anchor: the ground for each condition separately; no single anchor settles the compound. Union, not partition.
- **Domain 3, verification and community.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, replicators. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. This is a separate seat even when the same people fill it.
- **Domain 4, the specific attribution.** Actors: a named culture, item or fraction. Currency: a source label. Anchor: none available in this run, because no discriminator was operated. `gap`, unreachable at this budget.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the measurement unit before the conditions (Fig 1 :19); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second"*); refuse the naive attribution before minting anything (Formula step 2); run SEARCHED before INWARD and OUTWARD (Formula step 7); leave D4 open until a discriminator fires.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 governs what may serve as an anchor; Fig 11 governs where capability lives. Different objects, so no strain after re-census (see `01` section 7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A cross-cultural reasoning score gap cannot be attributed to ability rather than familiarity from the reading alone. What is identifiable is a **shape**: index the residual per item and person, treat ability and familiarity as a union with an overlapping intersection, treat the test as a substituted habitat with two silent failures, give familiarity residual-relative valence, keep the local anchor non-updating, and buy independence with a second low-overlap frame. Run SEARCHED first; the discriminators already exist. The specific attribution is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not a silence and not underivable in principle. This is `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`, and a candidate until an instrument pays.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim states six refuters before the conclusion and keeps verdicts per slot and per condition, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a group-level verdict instead of a checkable design; Fig 2 :42 prices that as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The naive attribution is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. No construct has to be withdrawn, and no new atom has to be imported.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot DIF showing item-specific group differences at matched local difficulty, a familiarity manipulation that moves the score, a second low-overlap instrument reproducing the per-slot pattern, or a local anchor that stays fixed when the key moves would all close D4. Conversely, a lossless group mean, a habitat-invariant gap, an empty-intersection partition, a fixed-sign familiarity effect, or no gain from a disjoint second frame would fire the pre-registered falsifiers and break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
