## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the framework before the recipient has described the residual sends the interior, not the check. Lead with the source-indistinguishability claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 2 :35** — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask whether the recipient agrees; ask what they would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if familiarity were the sole source before showing the essay.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* — a send selected for people likely to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 4 :25** — *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the design method, not a write-up of one successful study.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the claim and the six refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method with its own mode and refuter rule.

## Two-stage offer (authorised form)

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, pre-registered refuter. Their verdict is recorded before any framework material is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the three source links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only:** the formula as a reusable method; each user names a ground, writes a refuter before the run, and states a mode.

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 does not work unless the claim is stated in the form that can actually fail. As drafted, it is: one reading, two sources, per-slot residual, low-overlap frames, local anchor, six named refuters.
