## Wider literature worth reading (not cited as read here)

These are the standard names surfaced by the sources actually opened. They are listed so a checker can find the field, not quoted here, because this agent did not open them.

- Thomas Kuhn, *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* (1962), especially the chapters on incommensurability and on the social organization of a scientific community.
- David Bloor, Barry Barnes and the Edinburgh "Strong Programme"; Harry Collins and the Bath School's empirical programme of relativism.

- Sheila Jasanoff, *Designs on Nature*; Thomas Gieryn, *Cultural Boundaries of Science* (1999), both named in the ASA section's award record.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a history of who won before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the framework.
- **Seat Map :163** — *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) offer the loop as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
