## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `AUTHORITY(???)GROUND` is a shape: authority must be a maintained relation to something that does not update.
- "The Royal Society was authoritative because of X" is a name, a date, and a historical verdict. The figures do not return those.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* For this question the nearest available vocabulary is anchor, frame, closure, ground, convention, room, habitat. That is why the census in `01` is stated in those terms before any historian is read.
- *Library of Babel*, on recursion: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The domain census narrowed from one question to four domains with different anchors.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was described as "a frame scoring itself with what it produces," not as "peer review is broken."
- *Library of Babel*, on the tether: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether."* The anchor here is the non-updating ground, not agreement among historians.
- *Library of Babel*, on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly why D1 to D4 were not averaged.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the ground would be archival evidence plus a non-updating test of what actually settled a claim.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Sociology of scientific authority and STS are such frames. Their agreement with the figure shape is one witness, not corroboration, because they were selected after the residual was described (Fig 9 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."*).

## 4. Why a "winning institution" is the specific forbidden error

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap gives the mechanism: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

A historian who names the victor by counting how many other historians agree has built a mirror chain (Fig 7 :12-13 *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*). The agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other.

The same essay gives the source-discriminator shape: *"I used my ignorance as a shield against misinformation. I used analogy as a bridge across domains that i did not have vocabulary or jargon to understand."* The practical form here: describe the residual first, then look for a frame whose closure predicts its shape. Do not pick a prestigious institution and map the figures onto it.

## 5. Correction to my own §1 and §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any institution or any historical actor. That overstates the rule.

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- Naming an actor that is already in the frame's library is a **binding**, not the invention of a missing primitive. For this question, "the Royal Society," "a journal," "a regulator," and "peer review" are all bindings onto held atoms of the institutional habitat (Fig 11 :45-47).
- What remains forbidden is claiming the framework *derives* which actor occupies a slot, or treating the binding as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- So naming a venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is permitted. Naming a historical victor as if the figures returned it is not.

The correction is visible rather than silently substituted: both the stricter reading and the narrowed reading are recorded here.

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to this question

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A competition in which already-authoritative institutions win because they are already authoritative is the named collapse. The legitimate ask of a checker is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let a ground (archives, a reproducible measurement, a counter that does not update on the historian's opinion) settle it. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

## 7. My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** readings of the historical ground and cannot settle the question they report on. Seat Map :281: *"An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*
