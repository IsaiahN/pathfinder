## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likely agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that voids their agreement.
3. Asking for trust before a check spends independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
