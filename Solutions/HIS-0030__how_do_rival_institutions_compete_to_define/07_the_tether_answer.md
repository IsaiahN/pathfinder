# The Tether's answer: how rival institutions compete to define authoritative knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

Institutional frames each claim the standing to say what counts as knowledge. The residual is the gap between what each predicts its authority rests on (its own criteria, credentials, and citation share) and what a non-updating ground actually pays. It must be indexed per institutional slot, not averaged into "the authority of science" (Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**). It is a **union compound** across four domains with different anchors, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*).

## 2. The shape, per domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

### D1, the intra-institutional frame
Authority is not a stock a frame possesses. It is maintained contact with a ground that does not move when the frame is pushed. Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** An institution that certifies itself with its own credibility has installed a frame as its anchor. Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** Mode: `general` shape, triangulated across Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 10, Symbols table.

### D2, the inter-institutional contest
Rivalry has a surplus only where the two closures differ at the evidence level. Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** But Fig 8 :50 warns that two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, *"which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Convergence and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the provenance of the closure separates them (Fig 8 :51-52). The competition is not negotiation: Fig 2 :35 **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Mode: `specified`, triangulated.

### D3, the authority convention
A convention is legitimate while it only coordinates meaning across a seam. The moment it decides an outcome (who is published, hired, funded), it is a verdict and must be answerable to the ground. Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** A ground with a reputation to protect is not a ground: Fig 10 :93 **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."** Mode: `specified`, triangulated with the Seat Map.

### D4, historical selection
A chain of like-minded institutions is one long room, motion without progress. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** The next room must differ from the last and match the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36). Progress per handoff needs both bracket fidelity and ground pressure; zero either and the chain runs in place. Mode: `specified`, triangulated.

**Across all four:** capability lives in the institution-and-habitat, never the institution alone. Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Prestige and citation counts are budget filters, never verdicts (Fig 9 :60).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking of institutions, a winning academy, a date, or a credit share. That is a `gap` (F10 in `01`); the figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not "sociology explains it all." The conflict is constitutively about a ground (Fig 2 :36), and a negotiation cannot settle it. World sources are secondary corroboration only.
- Not "peer review is corrupt." A convention that only coordinates meaning is the product, not a corruption of it (Fig 2 :41). The verdict applies when the convention decides an outcome.
- Not "all rivalry is epistemic." Some rivalry is over resources and standing. Those are habitats and conditions around the one residual, not the residual itself (Fig 11 :46).
- Not a derivation from one institution to another, and no new atom is minted here (Fig 6 :21).
- Not grounded. No archival reading or non-updating instrument was consulted by this agent.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A historian with archives and a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The four refuters from `01` §4, restated so they can fire:

1. **Anchor:** an institution whose authority survives a non-updating change in the ground, channel open and credibility held fixed.
2. **Convention:** an authority convention that changes no outcome and cannot be checked against any ground.
3. **Room:** a run of near-identical rooms with nonzero ground pressure that still progresses.
4. **Habitat:** an institution whose authority rises while contacts, instruments, and habitat are held fixed.

Plus the two structural falsifiers from `06`: two institutions sharing all atoms and all bonds yet behaving differently; or two institutions with low overlap whose union yields no surplus and whose agreement still tracks the same ground (that would split convergence from rivalry and force a re-census).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question asks for a mechanism, and the mechanism is returned. It also invites a victor, and no figure line can name one: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134, local extract). ## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning institution, and it does not reduce the rivalry to sociology. It says that authority is not a stock but maintained contact with a ground that does not change when the institution is pushed; that rival institutions have a real surplus to fight over only where their closures differ at the evidence level, because convergence and adopted import look identical in the contents and only provenance separates them; that a convention such as peer review stops being a convention the moment it decides who is published or funded, and must then answer to a ground; and that a chain of like-minded institutions is one long room, motion without progress, unless the next room differs and matches the residual the last one surfaced. Prestige and citation counts are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where the figures cannot name a victor.
