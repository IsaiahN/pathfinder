## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's table, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what an institutional frame predicts its authority rests on and what a non-updating ground pays. Arity: N greater than or equal to 2 frames plus one contested object, so a plural contest rather than a dyad. Symmetry: the claims share a surface form but authority is a threshold relation, not a symmetric join. Scale: three nested ranges, intra-frame, inter-frame, and diachronic, with recursion because a settled institution becomes an atom at the next level.
- **Domain 1, the intra-institutional frame.** Actors: members, editors, reviewers, credentialing bodies. Currency: credibility, predicates minted. Anchor: a ground only if it does not update on the institution's verdict. Question type: partition plus anchor test.
- **Domain 2, the inter-institutional contest.** Actors: rival closures. Currency: authority claims as conventions. Anchor: a common external ground only if the question is constitutively about it. Question type: union compound with an unassignable shared fraction.
- **Domain 3, the authority convention.** Actors: the seat that authors conventions across a seam. Currency: legitimacy and provenance. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking arrangement, not loop.
- **Domain 4, historical selection.** Actors: successive rooms and the habitat around them. Currency: survival and arrival. Anchor: the non-decaying ground; the channel decays. Question type: selection between rooms.

Order by dependency: establish anchor and reachability; acquire terms for what a rival claim affords; state what counts as progress; choose the comparison; carry what settled to the next room. The maintainer seat sits outside this chain.

**My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading.** Where the primary derived from Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 8 and Fig 10, I derived from Fig 3 (dependency order) and Fig 7 (selection between rooms) as the entry points. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes inside the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

I checked one thing the primary did not emphasize: Domain 3 is not a peer of the other three. It is the position from which the check is made, not an object inside the contest. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: authority is contact with a ground; rivalry needs differing closures; conventions that decide outcomes are verdicts; a chain of agreeing rooms is one long room.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a historian would test: this academy, this journal, this credential, in this sequence of years.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **provenance of the closure** (Fig 8 :51-52), because two archives can yield the same claim text by convergence or by adoption, and the shorthand does not say which;
- the **S2 phase boundary** between a meaning-coordinating convention and an outcome-deciding one (Fig 2 :41 versus Fig 10 :47), because a real credentialing rule can be in both phases at once;
- the **operand type of comparison** at the historical scale (Operators table :48-51), because institutions are not obviously quantities;
- the **victor gap** itself, because the shorthand says "shape, not a name" while the historical question asks for a name.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an institution that scores itself with its own credibility has not located its ground. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no archival ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formulas and essays are the same thing in fewer words" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
