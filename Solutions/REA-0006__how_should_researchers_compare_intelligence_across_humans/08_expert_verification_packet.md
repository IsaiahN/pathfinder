# Expert verification packet: the Tether on cross-kind intelligence comparison

**What this is:** a claim in a form a comparative cognition researcher, psychometrician or AI evaluator can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal currency. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement of any subject was taken.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Asking how intelligent humans, animals and machines are is the wrong object of comparison. The habitat figure replaces it with the contact question: what makes a system capable, in which habitat, against which ground. Capability is a property of agent and habitat together, never of the agent alone, and a test harness is a substituted habitat, so a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability. What follows is a `specified` protocol: describe the residual per capacity in effect terms; enumerate each subject's habitat; install an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes on it and say why it is legitimate for that domain; use two or more instruments drawing on disjoint evidence; compare the invariant loop and the arrangement over a shared atom set; keep verdicts per slot and never average them. One universal currency or scalar rank is a declared `gap`, not a filler. Three failures were written down before this run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The decisive replacement, checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** the substrate question dissolves into the contact question; capability belongs to agent-and-habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :35: "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it." Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** an instrument that reads a habitat-independent intelligence off an agent, with a ground that pays for the reading; or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground. The figure names the second itself at Fig 11 :71-72.
- **Status:** `general` structural replacement plus `specified` falsifiable capability claim. Triangulated across Fig 11 and two low-overlap world fields (comparative cognition and machine evaluation).

### C2: The measurement separator, checkable by any comparative methodologist

- **Statement:** a shared test is a shared meaning, efficient for a convention and disqualified as a verdict; a score cannot say where to look until the channel and the anchor are checked.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check against your own field:** does a score difference persist after sensory access, motor access, motivation and attention are reproduced? Schubiger, Fichtel and Burkart 2020 (Frontiers in Psychology) report that non-cognitive factors act mainly via attention and that "applying a physically identical task is not sufficient to warrant fair species comparisons". Colombo and Scarf 2020 report that pigeon and monkey transfer become indistinguishable once contextual variables are set.
- **Refuter:** two instruments that demonstrably draw on disjoint evidence, each ranged separately against a non-updating ground, returning the same per-slot verdict; or a score difference that persists after all listed conditions are reproduced and predicts a held-out ground reading.
- **Status:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 plus Fig 2, low overlap between the figure routes; world corroboration from two independent fields).

### C3: The arrangement claim and its type strain, checkable from Figures 4, 12 and 13

- **Statement:** over a fixed atom set, the three kinds stand as isomers if their full atom sets match and arrangements differ; otherwise as members of a functional group. A universal currency is not derivable and is reported as a gap. Two currencies, description cost and action cost, are held apart and do not sum.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **Refuter:** either kind requires an atom the others do not, which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim; a currency is shown composing across kinds without import; or a seventh primitive is derived from the six (Fig 13 :41: "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it.").
- **Status:** `specified` with a stated falsifier for the isomer claim; `general` for the kernel invariance; `gap` for the currency; one live type strain, the single efficiency scalar of Chollet 2019 versus Fig 12 :125-130, named and not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal rank, one number, or a common currency for humans, animals and machines.
- Not that intelligence is meaningless. The word is refused as an intrinsic property, not as a topic (Fig 11 :36-38).
- Not that a shared benchmark is morally unfair. It is a convention, efficient for a shared meaning and disqualified as a verdict (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that human judgment is useless. It is the right anchor when the question is constitutively about people and only an instrument for code (Fig 2 :37-38).
- Not that Chollet's efficiency measure is wrong, and not that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess (05 §3).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not another battery. In order of directness:

1. A measure that survives all three refuters in §2, with an anchor that does not move when any designer pushes on it, and held-out ground readings at each slot.
2. A source-discriminating instrument that separates bought priors, task format, motivation, habitat and contact from the score. Fig 11 :41 uses the lift analogue: the substrate question dissolves, the contact question does not.
3. A seventh primitive or a cross-kind currency, derived rather than declared. That would break Fig 13 :41 and :115.
