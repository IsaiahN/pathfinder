# The Tether's answer on comparing intelligence across humans, animals and machines

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators and the protocol, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal currency or scalar rank. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 1. The residual it answers

Researchers want a comparison that says where to look and would be wrong if the world were different. What they have is a score that mixes contact, habitat, priors, motivation and the test's own measuring quantity. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The residual is per capacity and per subject, and a scalar rank throws it away.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

### Domain 1, the measurement frame: `specified`, triangulated

A shared test is a shared meaning. It is efficient as a convention and disqualified as a verdict. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not".

So a score difference does not tell us where to look until the channel is checked. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :26: "saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 10 :28: "you have lost touch". The broken link is usually the objective link, for which there is no instrument (Fig 3 :27). World corroboration is low-overlap: Chollet 2019 on bought priors, Schubiger et al. 2020 on attention and non-cognitive confounds, Colombo and Scarf 2020 on contextual variables.

### Domain 2, the capability and contact frame: `specified`, triangulated

The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not. Fig 11 :34: "What is intelligence?" Fig 11 :35: "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it." Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

A harness is a substituted habitat: Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." The habitat is read off the world, not composed (Fig 11 :43-48), and an actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." So no standing battery ranks the three kinds across tasks.

### Domain 3, the cross-scale comparison frame: `general` structural, `specified` where a falsifier exists

The kernel is invariant; currency is a per-domain parameter. Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Over a fixed atom set, different arrangements are isomers: Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." If only the skeleton is shared, they are a functional group (Fig 12 :123). This is a `specified` claim with the falsifier in `06` Q2.

The universal currency is not returned. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". This is a `gap`, not silence and not a proof that no comparison is possible.

### The protocol that follows

1. Describe the residual in effect terms, per slot, before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
2. Enumerate the habitat for each subject (Fig 11 :43).
3. Install a ground that does not move when a frame pushes on it, and name why it is legitimate for that domain (Fig 2 :29, :36).
4. Use two or more instruments that draw on disjoint evidence, each ranged separately against the anchor (Fig 2 :31-32; Fig 8 :45).
5. Build the instrument from a worse instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32).
6. Compare kernel shape and arrangement, keep verdicts per slot, record the round trip gap and the refuters (Fig 1 :19; Fig 12 :117; Fig 4 :30).

Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict: Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal rank, a common currency, or one number for all three kinds. That is a declared `gap`.
- Not that intelligence is meaningless. Fig 11 :36 says the word does no work where nothing maintains anything; where a seat exists, the practice is enumeration, ranking by cascade, introduction, instruments and ground reading (Fig 11 :37). The word is refused as an intrinsic property, not as a topic.
- Not that a shared benchmark is unfair in a moral sense. It is a shared meaning, efficient for a convention and disqualified as a verdict (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that human judgment is useless. It is the right anchor when the question is constitutively about people and only an instrument for code (Fig 2 :37-38).
- Not that Chollet's efficiency measure is wrong. It is a useful world-side instrument inside its own frame, and its reduction to one accounting is a named strain against Fig 12 :125-130, not a verdict against it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of any subject was taken.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

The protocol is refuted by any of the following, written before the run:

1. One measure that ranks all three kinds on one scale, stays per-slot rather than averaged, has an anchor that does not move when a test designer pushes on it, and predicts a held-out ground reading at each slot.
2. A ground reading in which cross-kind differences persist after habitat, priors, sensory and motor access, motivation and attention are reproduced, with the difference attributable to the agent alone. Fig 11 :71-72 says an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim.
3. An instrument that reads intelligence off an agent independently of habitat and ground, and the ground pays for the reading.
4. A seventh primitive derived from the six, or a currency composing across kinds without import (Fig 13 :41).

What is owed is a discriminator, not another test battery. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The comparison stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. The six-term set and the kernel are visible guesses; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. *They're Scanning Books* (carried in `05`): "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank humans, animals and machines, and it does not claim a common currency. It says the question asks the wrong object: capability is a property of agent and habitat together, never of the agent alone, and a test harness is a substituted habitat whose solve proves wiring rather than capability. What it offers is a protocol: describe the failure per capacity in effect terms, enumerate each subject's habitat, install a ground that does not move when a frame pushes on it, use independent instruments ranged separately against that ground, and compare the invariant loop and the arrangement rather than a score. Over a fixed atom set the three kinds may be isomers, with a stated falsifier; the universal rank stays a declared gap. What would turn the shape into a name is a ground reading, never agreement or prestige.
