**Question:** How should researchers compare intelligence across humans, animals, and machines with very different abilities?

**Status:** done

## What this solution says in one line

The question as posed asks for a scalar over agents; Fig 11 replaces the substrate question with the contact question and makes capability a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone. So researchers should compare per-slot capability against a non-updating ground in a reproduced habitat, using low-overlap instruments and a named kernel, and should refuse the universal ranking as a shape-not-a-name gap rather than invent a common currency.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people, species or systems by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are figure-derived: Fig 2 :35 ("Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."); Fig 2 :40 (agreement from a shared evidence pool is worthless as a verdict); Fig 10 :42 ("A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."). The public contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional, opt-in, submission or society venues only.
