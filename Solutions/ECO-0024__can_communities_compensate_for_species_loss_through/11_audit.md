## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the residual line alone and the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

When two slots share a compensator, the case is a **union compound**, because a lever that clears the region where only one condition holds leaves the intersection standing. Symmetry: broken. The relation between an actor and a function is not preserved under swapping the function, so valence is relative to the slot. Scale: first order at the actor and the habitat, second and third order through the cascade. Fig 1 gives the per-slot rule and Fig 11 gives the cascade.

Anchor: the figures for legality, the ground for settlement. Question type: partition for cross-kind composition, with a union overlap when two slots compete for one actor.

- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: experiments, datasets, instruments, independent frames. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: a pre-declared per-function measurement. Question type: triangulation. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence across roles is zero.

Order by dependency: perceive the failed slot; hold a term for the function and the group that could perform it; state what counts as restored; enumerate the habitat outward; settle and carry. The middle link is the vocabulary link, and a link with no instrument is not a link that works.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot R), Fig 6 :21 (no added atom), Fig 11 :40 and :49-52 (contact, valence, cascade), Fig 12 :117 and :123 (isomer, functional group), Fig 13 :115 (closed kinds, open arrangements).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: compensation is bounded rearrangement inside a functional group; it requires contact change; it is relative to the function; it settles by measurement; the aggregate is not evidence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field claim such as "this pollinator guild compensated for the loss of species X, because total fruit set recovered".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot identity** of the function. The shorthand carries "the function that failed"; the field claim often substitutes a community total. That is the exact F1 error.
- the **contact record**. The shorthand requires a change in contact with the failed function; a field claim from abundance data alone cannot show it.
- the **union check**. The shorthand says valence is relative to the slot; a field claim that credits one compensator without measuring its effect on other functions has dropped the multi-slot constraint.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure that persists inside a functional group can take over a function, and an actor absent from the held set cannot be used as a compensator. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no field data exist in this folder; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
