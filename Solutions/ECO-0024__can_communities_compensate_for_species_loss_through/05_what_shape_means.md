## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 records that the system can characterise what is missing in effect terms and that *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, while it cannot name the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (directly fetched): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

Applied here, a **shape** is the narrowed form of a compensation verdict that can be stated before one can name which species did the work or whether any real community did it. The shape for ECO-0024 is:

> A compensation verdict is a per-function verdict. It holds only if a change in the remaining species closes the specific failed function, the change alters contact with that function, and a measurement of that function agrees. It is reachable by rearranging held species within a functional group and is not reachable when a new actor or condition would be required. Which species is the compensator is not part of the shape.

The named claim *"species X compensated for the loss of species Y"* is a name, not a shape. So is *"the community is resilient."* The shape constrains both and fills neither.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (directly fetched): *"use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the closest held vocabulary is the ecological vocabulary of functional groups, redundancy, response diversity, and trophic cascades. Those terms are used to narrow the residual, not to import a verdict.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual is described as *a function no longer performed at its prior rate*, not as *a loss of redundancy* or *a decline in insurance value*, because those are cause-shaped words borrowed from particular frames.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (via local exemplar mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the outreach email asks what a failure would look like before offering theory.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping (directly fetched): *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For ECO-0024 we are not done. The slot measurement has not been taken. The honest stop is therefore a recorded stop, not a claimed arrival.

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A per-function measurement taken before the comparison can license a name in a particular community.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning a functional group into *the* answer to a compensation question would make that choice invisible again.

## 4. Why naming the compensator from a single reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (directly fetched) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here, a stable community total is a reading. From that reading one cannot name the source, meaning the species or interaction that restored the function. Several arrangements of the remaining species can give the same total. Naming a compensator from the total is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: a measurement of the specific function that can distinguish which actor performed it.

This is also why F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is decisive for the question's form. The figures do not say that compensation never happens. They say the function slot, not the aggregate, is the object that can be settled.

## 6. The question this essay answers for ECO-0024

**What does "shape" mean here?** It means a per-slot constraint with four parts: bounded rearrangement rather than import; contact change rather than abundance change alone; valence relative to the failed function rather than a fixed species value; and measurement rather than agreement. It does not mean a list of compensating species, a general yes, or a reserve of redundancy that guarantees compensation.

**Where does the shape stop?** At the limit of the available instruments. No function-specific measurement was taken in this folder, so the empirical yes or no is unreachable here and is owed to the ground. As the figures README puts it, *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A community that looks compensated at the level of a total is not thereby shown to have compensated at the level of the function that failed.
