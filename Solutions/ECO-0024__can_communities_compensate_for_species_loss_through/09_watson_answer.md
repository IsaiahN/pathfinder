## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about compensation after species loss. A species disappears and a function it performed is under-supplied. Option A: communities compensate in general through changes in abundance or behavior of the remaining species, with functional redundancy as the guarantee. Option B: compensation is a conditional shape, reachable by bounded rearrangement inside a functional group when the change alters contact with the failed function, bounded by the atoms held, and settled only by a measurement of that function. What each leads to: A licenses a general conservation conclusion from an aggregate and spends the independence of the people asked to endorse it; B sends a per-slot claim with stated refuters and an open verdict until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Under-supply of a function after a species loss. Arity: multiple function slots, so a union compound when slots share a compensator, not one hard question. Symmetry: non-symmetric, because an actor that helps one slot can harm another, so valence is relative to the slot. Scale: first-order at the actor and habitat, second- and third-order through the cascade. Fig 1 says R is per slot, and Fig 9 says a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the community as composition.** Actors: remaining species as atoms, interactions as bonds, functional groups as substructures. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ), with a function as a molecule rather than an ingredient list. Anchor: the figures for legality of composition, the ground for settlement. Question type: partition for cross-kind composition, with a union overlap when two failed functions compete for one actor.
- **Domain 2, habitat and contact.** Actors: remaining species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (predation, pollination, competition, dependency). Currency: contact with the residual. Anchor: the measured function, which does not update. Question type: enumeration, not composition; the habitat is read off the world by contact cascades.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: field experiments, long-term datasets, instruments, independent frames. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: a pre-declared measurement of the specific function. Question type: triangulation, with shared datasets counted as one witness.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the failed slot; have a term for the function and the group that could perform it; state what counts as restored; enumerate the habitat outward by contact; settle and carry. Effort above a broken middle link cannot rescue it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not return a yes or a no. They return the shape a checkable verdict must have. Compensation is a bounded rearrangement: a qualifier swap inside a functional group or a bond change making an isomer of the community. It holds only for the function that failed, only when the abundance or behavior change alters contact with that function, only with valence relative to that function, and only if a measurement of that function agrees. It cannot create a function that needs an actor or condition absent before the loss; that is import. A stable aggregate is not evidence. Managed cases are permanent work in a habitat the manager did not author. The empirical verdict is unreachable from these figures and is owed to a pre-declared measurement. This result is `specified` on structure, `general` on shape, and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** a conservation decision could be based on an aggregate that looks stable while a function stays unperformed, or a compensator could be credited for one function while degrading another. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Here the cost is a real management decision, so independent checking is worth buying. The cost is bounded because the packet states its refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim and its refuters can be superseded without withdrawing any figure line. A slot measurement can overturn a verdict for one community without affecting the structure. No primitive is committed. A sent packet can be corrected.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-declared measurement showing the failed function restored with contact changed and no relocation or import would confirm the claim for that case; a slot residual that persists while the aggregate is flat, a change requiring a new actor, a change with no contact effect and no function response, or a third-order failure recreating the original residual would refute it. A figure re-census that permits a new function from rearrangement alone would also reverse the structure. Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
