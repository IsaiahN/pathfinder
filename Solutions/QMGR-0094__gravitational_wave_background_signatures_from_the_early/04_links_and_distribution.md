## 3. Wider literature that bears on the shape claim

1. **Source discrimination is the live problem, not detection.** NANOGrav reports the background and immediately declines the source attribution; Wikipedia states the sources *"cannot be identified without further observations and analyses."* This is the world-facing counterpart of Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Treat the four-PTA result as a detector anchor, not as a naming event.
2. **The search is defined by signal and noise models.** Romano and Cornish make explicit that the likelihood, the signal model, the noise model and the prior define what is being searched for. That is the reason to bind the sought shape as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :62-66) and to state the modeling choices that move the Bayes factors.
3. **The band structure is a union.** Ground detectors see roughly 10 Hz to 1000 Hz, PTAs see nanohertz, LISA sees millihertz. A primordial background could in principle span them, but an upper limit in one band is not a global absence statement (Fig 8 :39). This is why per-band verdicts are kept separate.
4. **Anisotropy and individual sources are the slow discriminator.** SciAm reports that hotspots may be artifacts of sparse pulsar coverage, and that if no anisotropy appears, stranger sources gain plausibility. That is Fig 6 :33-35 in practice: a better instrument grows out of a reading that already fails to resolve.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The physics sources all draw on the same GWB literature pool, so their agreement is one witness on the physics side, not several; the detection-methods review is the lowest-overlap of them because it is about inference machinery rather than source claims. The essays share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures and carry no independent witness weight.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Send the shape claim and its refuter first.
- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the residual, not with the theory that produced it.
- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a recording of one derivation is the wrong object; send the generator, which is the refuter and the instrument condition.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if a primordial signature were present before giving them any model list.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One to one contact through opt-in institutional venues; no list ranked by likely agreement. Seat Map :163 calls high correlation disqualifying for a verdict.
- **Prestige rule:** *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it"* (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap). The ask is for a check, not an endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim with its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and what observation would name the source; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, offer the figure extract, the verification packet and the methods review. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
