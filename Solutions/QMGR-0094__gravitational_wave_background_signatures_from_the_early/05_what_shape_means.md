## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

*The Library of Babel* says: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* It also describes the bootstrapping route: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the contact condition: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

For QMGR-0094, the shape is the narrowed form of a signature you can state before you can name its source. Concretely:

- **Bound shape:** a deviation of the GWB's spectral or statistical form, indexed by frequency bin or sky pixel, that survives an independent instrument and that a source-discriminating observation would settle.
- **Not a shape:** "the background is primordial"; "the spectral index is 2/3"; "the source is a phase transition." Each of those is a name or a number where the framework returns a constraint.
- **Why the slots are in the shape:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A broadband average removes the very structure that would make the shape checkable.

The searcher's vocabulary comes from proximity. The terms already held are stochastic background, Hellings and Downs correlation, spectral index, anisotropy, non-Gaussianity and polarization. Recursing on those narrows the space toward a source-discriminating observable rather than toward a source name.

## 3. How a shape is obtained, for this question

- Describe the effect, not the cause. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is a correlated strain field with a frequency-dependent form; the cause, inflation or binaries, is the thing not yet read.
- Cast it in effect terms so it travels. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* A claim that a specific source is present only makes sense inside one analysis pipeline cannot be checked by someone using another.
- Stop when the returned set is descriptive enough (Library of Babel, section 2). For this question that point is reached when the discriminating observable is named as a kind of measurement, without claiming which source it will find.
- Mind the paradox of arrival. Library of Babel: by the time the exact search terms are known, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The shape is not a consolation prize; it is the part that survives until the instrument pays.

## 4. What licenses a name, and why the source is a gap

Only two things license a name in this framework: the ground settles a term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). For QMGR-0094:

1. **The ground has settled a background, not a source.** NANOGrav's correlation is a ground reading in D1. Its own abstract refuses the attribution: *"more exotic cosmological and astrophysical sources cannot be excluded."*
2. **A source model is an import from the physics frame.** Naming a phase transition or cosmic strings as the source would bind an external prediction onto the shape. Fig 8 :50-52 requires the record of where it came from; absent that, convergent derivation and adopted import look identical.
3. **An instrument that reads a spectrum cannot name its source.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the detector case: a color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources produce the same reading, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer resolved the color case; here the analogue is a source-discriminating observation. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Therefore the source is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle. This is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 5. Shape, isomer and functional group

The figures permit a sharper statement than "we do not know the source." Over a shared atom set, different sources are Fig 12 :117 isomers: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Wikipedia says different sources produce *"a background with different statistical properties"*; Romano and Cornish require searches to allow anisotropy, non-Gaussianity and non-standard polarization. Those are arrangements, not new atoms.

As the sources are currently written, with different atoms and assumptions, they are a functional group. Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* A stochastic background in a detector band fixes the kind; the spectral slope, bump, anisotropy, non-Gaussianity or polarization fixes the member. The shape claim is about the qualifier. The full derivation and its falsifier are in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A detection announced by a large collaboration earns a budget to be checked, not a verdict by reputation. A campaign that gathers names to agree with a primordial reading is the named collapse. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

**A description of effects travels better than a theory.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why: text carries context and some subtext and almost never the pretext, so what can be checked by anyone is a plain description of what one would expect to see. That is exactly the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the shape and the refuter, record the verdict, then offer the theory.

## 8. Correction retained from the first pass

An earlier reading applied *"shape, not a name"* to every named entity in the solution. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Here the source of the GWB is genuinely not held in the detector frame, so the limit applies to it. The same phrase must not be used to forbid naming an already-held atom or a public measured quantity such as a Hellings and Downs correlation; those are bindings or readings, with provenance, not invented primitives. Both the limit and its scope are kept visible here rather than quietly narrowed.
