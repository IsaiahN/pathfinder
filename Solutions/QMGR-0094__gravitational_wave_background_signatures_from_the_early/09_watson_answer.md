## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Something is registering in the detector channel whose source the current frame cannot resolve. Arity: several conditions, so a union compound of astrophysical and cosmological sources (Fig 9 :54-58). Symmetry: the background is statistically isotropic to first approximation, which is why a Hellings and Downs correlation certifies gravitational origin; the discriminating information is asymmetric across frequency and sky (spectral slope, bump, anisotropy, non-Gaussianity, polarization). Scale: nanohertz PTA, millihertz LISA, audio-band ground interferometers. The source is the part that cannot yet be read (Symbols table :38-40).
- **Domain 1, detector and ground.** Actors: PTA consortia, LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA, LISA. Currency: strain sensitivity, observation span, correlation signal to noise. Anchor: the measured timing residuals and cross-correlation, which do not update on what a frame thinks. Question type: which band is being read, a partition boundary between instruments.
- **Domain 2, source models.** Actors: SMBH binaries, inflation, phase transitions, cosmic strings, reheating. Currency: predicted spectral and statistical form. Anchor: none of its own; settles only through Domain 1. Question type: union of overlapping conditions; the source name is an import that needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).
- **Domain 3, inference and model selection.** Actors: Bayesian and frequentist searches, MDL model comparison. Currency: evidence, description length, leftover residual. Anchor: Domain 1 data through a lossy transform. Question type: a bargain (Fig 5 :42-44), not an anchor.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, collaborations, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero (Fig 2 :40).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception first (a correlation registers), vocabulary second (terms for spectral and statistical form), objective third (a discriminating deviation, not another detection), planning fourth (which band and instrument), learn and carry fifth (preserve per-instrument readings when pooling). Refusal precedes mint (formula step 2).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's spectrum primitive is not the measured Ω_gw(f); the measured spectrum is a composition over held atoms. Fig 13's gradient is not the strain h by name resemblance. Both strains are recorded in `01` and `06`, not smoothed over. S3 (independence versus pooling) and S4 (isomer versus functional group) remain live and narrow.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the attribution does not. A signature of the early universe must be a per-frequency or per-sky-slot deviation of the background's form, read over a union of overlapping source conditions, with candidate sources standing as isomers over a shared atom set and as a functional group as currently written. The figures return the shape of the discriminator and refuse the source name. The observed nanohertz background is a ground reading in the detector domain; any primordial attribution is `specified`, ungrounded and `candidate` until a source-discriminating observation pays. The source name and the identity of the discriminating instrument are declared `gap`s, reported as unreached rather than as silence. Two live strains are kept visible: independence versus pooling, and isomer versus functional group.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a source name instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and a claim about the early universe is expensive if wrong. The cost is avoidable by leading with C1 to C3.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective reading, that the background is already an early-universe signature, is removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. Pooling can be undone at the reporting level by preserving per-instrument readings.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An anisotropy map that resolves individual sources or shows hotspots track sparse pulsar coverage; a multi-band spectral shape that selects one source class; non-Gaussianity or polarization statistics that discriminate candidates; or a multi-messenger counterpart with a source gate and a shadow test. Any of these could name the source and close the gap. A correlation that moves with the pipeline, or a proof that two candidates are indistinguishable at all resolutions, would break C1 or C2. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
