## 1. The question restated in effect terms

The ordinary phrasing asks what signatures of the early universe appear in the gravitational-wave background (GWB). Stated as an effect and a choice:

- **Observed effect:** a stochastic, correlated background of gravitational waves is measured across detector bands (nanohertz pulsar-timing arrays, audio-band ground interferometers, future millihertz space interferometers). The 2023 pulsar timing array results report a common-spectrum process with the Hellings and Downs angular correlation, a reading that fails to resolve which source produced it.
- **Decision:** (A) treat the measured background, or a fitted spectral index, as already identified with an early-universe process; or (B) derive what any early-universe signature must look like before it is named, bind that shape as an ordinary term, and state the instrument that would name the source.
- **What each leads to:** A delivers a `general`-mode verdict in the clothing of a measurement, with a name where the framework returns a shape, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it. B delivers a checkable shape, per-band verdicts written before the run, an instrument owed, and an honest `unreachable` where no current reading discriminates sources.
- **What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, per the formula's "Direction"):** a signature is *a per-frequency-slot deviation of the background's spectral or statistical form that survives independent instruments and that a source-discriminating observation would settle*. This term is bound here so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Fig 5 :62-66: a frame searching for a shape holds it as a preference, unbound, predicting nothing).

## 4. What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a figure line returns the source name, a spectral index, an amplitude, or an instrument design. Those are `gap`s and are recorded as such (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).

Agreement is not a witness here. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The four pulsar timing consortia are independent instruments only to the degree that they do not draw on one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31-32); pooling every data set into one pipeline improves sensitivity and does not multiply witnesses.
