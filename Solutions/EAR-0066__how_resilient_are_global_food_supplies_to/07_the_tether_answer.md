# The Tether's answer on simultaneous harvest failures

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the numerical resilience of global supply. Not `grounded`. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.**

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how resilient global food supplies are to simultaneous harvest failures in major producing regions. Restated as an effect: for each crop x region x population slot, does food stay physically available and economically obtainable above a declared need threshold when several breadbaskets fail in one season, and does the gap close afterwards? Symbols table :38-40: *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :55-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Production, trade, policy and access conditions overlap in the high-risk slots. They have different anchors and their verdicts are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Index the gap per slot, never as a global average.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** World: Kuhla et al. 2026 find regional consumption risk from negligible to over 100 Mcal per capita per year, and Zurek et al. 2022 ask *"Resilience of what? Resilience to what? Resilience from whose perspective? Resilience for how long?"* The four questions are the operational form of the slot.

2. **Simultaneous failures share a cause; they are not N independent trials.** Fig 2 :6-7 names **"collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)"**. Fig 2 :18-21: **"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"**. Climate modes organise which regions get drought and heat (Anderson et al. 2019: *"strong El Niño or La Niña events in effect organize which regions experience drought and extreme temperatures"*). The effective-sample-size correction in Fig 2 :44 is the arithmetic warning. Independent-looking breadbaskets can be one evidence pool under several country names.

3. **Optimisation inside the present closure cannot supply the missing buffer.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** The efficiency, specialisation and just-in-time program improved the description and consumed redundancy (Zhang et al. 2025). More of the same search visits more of the same space.

4. **Only an import from a closure that differs at the evidence level moves the wall.** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 6 :14 calls import **"the only operator that moves the wall"**. Concretely: a different climate exposure, a different supplier base, a different input source, a reserve with an independent trigger, a source-discriminating instrument. Each carries provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52). Diversification that shares one climate driver is two names for one frame and adds no surplus (Fig 8 :14-15).

5. **Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged into a resilience score.** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** A stock-to-use ratio, diversification index or price variance allocates attention; the witness is the measured outcome at D4. Béné et al. 2023 keep resilience capacity distinct from realised resilience and report that rigorous measurement methods are still missing.

6. **Cascades and feedback sign decide amplification.** Fig 11 :52-54: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** Enumerate outward to fertilizer, energy, feed, transport and trade rules. Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Export bans are a positive feedback; a well-triggered reserve release is a negative feedback whose closure is empirical. Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."** A reserve is a catalyst only if replenished. Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** Self-sufficiency protects one slot and harms another (Kuhla et al. 2026).

7. **The channel decays; the ground does not.** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. Fig 10 :25: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**. Keep a per-slot instrument reachable. Chen et al. 2024 show that univariate heat trends and the full multivariate model read opposite trends; the channel, not the ground, was wrong.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a resilience percentage, ranking, or threshold value. No figure line returns one (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134-135).
- Not "diversification solves it." Only imports that differ at the evidence level count, and they are priced (Fig 6 :24).
- Not that all breadbasket failures are coupled. The claim is that the coupling must be measured before independence is assumed; Gaupp et al. 2020 say the correlation structure can mitigate or aggravate risk.
- Not that ENSO explains every failure. Anderson et al. 2019 find other modes matter in at least one region and that offsetting anomalies reduce the global share for wheat and soybean.
- Not that more stock is always better. Poorly designed stockholding can distort markets or crowd out private storage (Kuhla et al. 2026).
- Not a derivation of climate or crop physics from the figures. Climate sources are provenanced bindings, not figure outputs.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken by this author; the whole folder is a simulated derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Coupling discriminator:** a joint monitoring measure that estimates cross-breadbasket shock correlation under strong modes, with published uncertainty, so that independence is measured rather than assumed (Anderson 2019, Gaupp 2020, Chen 2024).
- **Per-slot threshold instrument:** availability and access measured at the population slot against a declared need threshold, with the perspective and horizon stated in advance (Zurek 2022, Béné 2023). This is the link that currently has no settled instrument (Fig 3 :27).
- **Import test:** for each proposed diversification or reserve, show where the atom came from (source gate) and which previously unexplained gap it explains (shadow test). Reject it if it shares the driver it is meant to hedge.
- **Feedback test:** measure the sign of the ban/reserve loops in a real crisis, not in a model that deliberately excludes policy responses (Kuhla et al. 2026 excluded them and call their estimates conservative).
- Standing refuters from `01`: global scalar that predicts every slot; near-zero cross-region correlation; within-closure efficiency gain that survives a ground test; decay-free monitoring instrument; fixed-valence policy; ground-settled resilience measure replicated by two non-overlapping frames.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 13 :134-135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The atom may already exist in food-security science; Béné et al. 2023 say the measurement methods are missing, not impossible. The stop is recorded with its lineage: what was borrowed (climate-mode evidence, agent-based propagation results, resilience measurement frameworks), what was derived (the slot, union, closure and cascade shape), and what would reverse it. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give global food supply a resilience score. It says that the thing which fails is a per-crop, per-region, per-population slot, and that simultaneous breadbasket failures are usually one cause wearing several country names rather than several independent accidents. It says that the efficiency program of recent decades cannot add the missing buffer, because more search inside the same set of actors, routes and rules does not create a new option; only a genuine difference in climate exposure, suppliers, inputs, reserve triggers or instruments does, and that difference has to be provenanced and shown to explain a real gap. It keeps verdicts for production, trade, policy and nutrition separate, treats ratios and indices as ways of allocating effort rather than as witnesses, and enumerates the shock outward to fertilizer, energy, feed and trade rules. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the source-discriminating instrument that would close the question. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
