# Expert verification packet - the Tether on simultaneous harvest failures

**What this is:** a claim in a form a food-security researcher, climate-impact modeller or trade economist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the mechanism, `gap` on the numerical resilience. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Global food supply resilience" is the wrong object. The object is a per-crop, per-region, per-population slot: does availability and access stay above a declared need threshold through a season in which several breadbaskets fail, and does the gap close afterwards? Simultaneous failures are a union of overlapping conditions with a common cause, not independent trials; more optimisation inside the current set of actors, routes and rules cannot add the missing buffer; only an import from a closure that differs at the evidence level, with provenance and a shadow test, widens the reach. Verdicts for production, trade and price, policy, and nutrition stay separate and are never averaged. The magnitudes are a declared gap, closed only by a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framing and independence; checkable by a food-security or climate-impact researcher

- **Statement:** the residual must be indexed per slot, and simultaneous breadbasket failures must be tested for a shared driver before independence is assumed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22 - **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 2 :6-7 - **"collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)"**. Fig 2 :31 - **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** Fig 9 :55-57 - **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
- **What to check:** do the shocks in the main breadbaskets share modes (ENSO, IOD, NAO) strongly enough that a geographic diversification claim is overstated? Are the high-risk slots those where import dependency, small supplier base and policy response coincide?
- **Refuter:** near-zero cross-breadbasket failure correlation under strong modes plus a validated global scalar that predicts every slot's threshold outcome. If shown, F1 and F2 fall.

### C2 - remedy and reach; checkable by a trade economist or supply-chain researcher

- **Statement:** efficiency and specialisation inside the current closure do not add the buffer; only an import from a closure differing at the evidence level does, and it needs a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :32 - **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 6 :21 - **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :23 - **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 6 :24 - **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."**
- **What to check:** do the proposed buffers (extra suppliers, reserves, alternative routes) fail on the same driver they are meant to hedge? Kuhla et al. 2026 note that new trade links may shift rather than eliminate dependencies and that badly designed stockholding can crowd out private storage. Are those failures measured?
- **Refuter:** an efficiency change made entirely inside the existing atom set that raises measured independence and buffer in all four domains and survives a ground test. If shown, the reach claim fails.

### C3 - measurement and cascade; checkable by a resilience-measurement researcher

- **Statement:** indices are budget filters, not witnesses; the outcome anchor is measured per population slot; the habitat must be enumerated outward and feedback sign measured.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 - **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 1 :27 - **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** Fig 10 :24 - **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. Fig 11 :52-54 - **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** Fig 12 :121 - **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- **What to check:** is there a working D4 instrument with a published error rate that is actually used to choose D1 to D3 levers? Béné et al. 2023 say rigorous measurement methods are still missing. Are input shocks (fertilizer, energy, feed) and policy loops (export bans) in the habitat, or scoped out?
- **Refuter:** a decay-free per-slot monitoring instrument with held-out failure rates plus a ground-settled resilience measure replicated by two non-overlapping frames. If shown, C3 and the gap close.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a resilience number, country ranking or universal prescription.
- Not that all failures are coupled; the coupling must be measured.
- Not that ENSO explains every event; other modes matter by region (Anderson et al. 2019).
- Not that more stock is always better, or that self-sufficiency always helps; valence is relative to the slot (Fig 11 :51, Kuhla et al. 2026).
- Not a derivation of climate or crop physics from the figures; those are provenanced bindings.
- Not grounded. No measurement was made by this author.
