## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between food physically available and economically obtainable, and food needed to avoid a declared harm, when several breadbaskets fail in one season. Arity: at least four overlapping conditions. Symmetry: failures are not independent symmetric trials; they share drivers, so the relevant operator is a threshold comparison (`⋛`), not a conjunction or a disjunction of independent events. Scale: sub-annual shock, one to three year trade and storage carry-over, multi-decadal trend in failure probability. Symbols table :38-40: the residual *"is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*
- **Domain 1, production / biophysical.** Actors: crops, soil, water, pollinators, pests, climate modes. Currency: yield anomaly, biomass. Anchor: measured yield and harvest statistics. Union member, not a partition cell.
- **Domain 2, trade, price and stock.** Actors: exporters, importers, traders, stockholders. Currency: price, trade flow, stock-to-use. Anchor: realised transactions and physical stocks, never sentiment. Union member; its anchor is the one most at risk of updating.
- **Domain 3, policy / governance.** Actors: states, reserve agencies, aid donors. Currency: rules, bans, reserves, releases. Anchor: enacted events and outcomes. Union member; the feedback-sign domain.
- **Domain 4, nutrition / food security outcome.** Actors: households, net food buyers, smallholders. Currency: calories, access, affordability. Anchor: measured food security outcomes per population slot. The witness domain; the one whose instrument is missing.
- **No fifth domain admitted.** Candidates without a non-updating anchor were rejected under Fig 2 :29-30.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54 and :55-57. One lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Measure the production shock, translate through trade and stock into price and availability, add the policy response, read the household outcome, then enumerate the habitat outward to inputs and rules. The chain breaks at the outcome instrument.

Strain test applied: the Fig 13 six-term physical substrate is not forced onto food-system terms; the domain-agnostic laws govern with actors, currency and anchor renamed at this scale (Fig 4 :19-20). Strains S1 to S4 are recorded, not hidden, in `01` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed asks for the wrong object. The figures return a per-slot union shape with four separate domain verdicts, not a resilience coefficient. Production shocks must be measured per crop and region; the failing regions must be tested for shared drivers before independence is assumed; the existing efficiency closure cannot add the missing buffer; only provenanced imports that differ at the evidence level widen the reach; ratios and indices allocate budget but do not witness the outcome; the habitat extends outward to fertilizer, energy, feed and trade rules; feedback sign decides whether a ban or a reserve amplifies or damps; and the channel to the outcome anchor must be kept reachable because it decays while the ground does not. The numerical magnitude is a `gap`, reported as unreachable at this budget rather than as silence and rather than filled with a model number. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `gap` on the magnitude, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the refuters before the conclusion and keeps the four domains separate, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a resilience score or a standing prescription instead of a separator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be revised per domain without withdrawing the slot structure or the union reading. A proposed import can be rejected and replaced when its provenance or shadow test fails. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No atom has to be deleted; a refusal adds a term and the reach falls safely (Fig 6 :42-43).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A joint monitoring instrument showing near-zero cross-breadbasket correlation under strong modes breaks F2; a global scalar that predicts every slot breaks F1 and F5; a within-closure efficiency gain that survives a ground test breaks F3; a decay-free per-slot outcome instrument with published error rates breaks F6 and closes the gap; a source-discriminating resilience measure replicated by two non-overlapping frames closes F8. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
