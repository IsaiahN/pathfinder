## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134-135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can settle it. It constrains; it does not fill. Concretely:

- shape: "per crop-region-population slot, a production shock becomes a food-security failure only when it crosses a threshold and is carried by a coupling that shares its driver with other regions."
- not a shape: "global food supply resilience is 7.4 out of 10", or "diversify exports", or "country X is the most resilient."

The shape survives the absence of a number. The number does not survive the absence of a shape, because without the slot and coupling structure there is nothing to measure.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**. So the residual is described as a gap between availability and need, not as "low resilience."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the outreach email asks what a recipient would observe if the common-cause claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stopping point is the separator plus the instrument owed, not a rounded number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, neither of which is the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, the things still needing a name are: which coupling dominates in a given slot, which reserve trigger counts as independent, and which instrument can discriminate a coupled failure from a set of independent ones. Naming ENSO as the common driver is a provenanced binding from climate science, not a term generated by the figures. It is recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

**Correction to my own §1 phrasing, kept visible.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. It does not forbid naming an atom already held, or a binding imported with provenance. So saying "availability crosses a need threshold" or "ENSO couples the regions" is permitted as a binding; claiming the figures derived those names is not. This matches the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` §5b and `06` Q1.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or an undiscovered scheme can all produce the reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a simultaneous price spike and a consumption shortfall can each be produced by several sources, a production failure, an export ban, a fuel or fertilizer shock, a finance-driven demand surge, or a combination. Reading the source off the spike is the metamerism error. Chen et al. 2024 make the same point empirically: a univariate heat trend reads "rising risk" while the full multivariate model reads falling realised shocks. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a composite index.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A resilience score computed by the same actors it grades is frame-internal by Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "resilient" mean, canonically, for this question?** Not a stock, not a coefficient, and not a standing list of good policies (Fig 11 :51). It means: for each declared slot, measured availability and access stay above a declared threshold through the shock and its carry-over, the threshold being stated with the perspective and time horizon that chose it. The four framing questions from Zurek et al. 2022, opened this session, are the operational form: resilience of what, to what, from whose perspective, for how long.

The shape therefore has four parts:

1. **Slots:** crop x region x population x time (Fig 1 :19).
2. **Couplings:** shared drivers and overlapping conditions make this a union with a common cause (Fig 2 :6-7, Fig 9 :54-57).
3. **Reach:** only imports from a closure differing at the evidence level add options (Fig 6 :21, :23).
4. **Anchor:** the outcome measured at the household or population slot, not the price index or the model score (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24).

Anything beyond those four parts is a name that needs a ground or a provenanced import. The universal resilience number is a `gap` in this solution (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134-135), reported as unreachable at this budget, not as silence and not as an invitation to invent a coefficient.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated."* Also: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering well-known names to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. Every substantive file in this folder carries that label.

**Practical contact rule.** Find people whose public work touches the residual, not people likely to agree with the shape. Send the residual and its refuter; record the verdict before showing the framework; never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :39-40). The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional and opt-in for exactly this reason.
