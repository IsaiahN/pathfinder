## 1. Independent domain census, reconstructed from the residual line alone

I rebuilt the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a projected saving and a registered outcome after a change in who controls a firm. Arity: at least four, because cost, price, innovation and input terms do not share an anchor. Symmetry: the two conjuncts are not a symmetric pair; one is a claim about a cost function against a counterfactual, the other about the strategic dependence of remaining firms and the terms offered to trading partners. No Fig 12 bond identifies the relation, so the either/or is not a comparison. Scale: firm, market, welfare convention.
- **Domain 1, the firm as composition.** Actors: two firms, then one. Currency: cost, output, investment. Anchor: a counterfactual not dependent on the merging parties' projections. Weak until a retrospective measures it.
- **Domain 2, the market as measurement device.** Actors: remaining firms, consumers, workers, suppliers. Currency: price, wage, quantity, variety. Anchor: competition, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Union with D1 and D4 because the same event enters all three.
- **Domain 3, the evaluation frame.** Actors: agencies, courts, economists. Currency: evidence, burden of proof. Anchor: a declared welfare convention. Independent loop that consumes D1 and D2.
- **Domain 4, the question-posing frame.** Actors: the asker of "usually". Currency: frequency. Anchor: none without a defined population and per-slot results.

Order by dependency: settle D2's anchor before admitting D1's claim; unbundle D4 first (Fig 9 :54); refuse the scalar before minting a direction (formula step 2, Fig 5 :53).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

- the **slot identity**, which collapses into a single "competition versus efficiency" opposition in the concrete form;

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the demand that a claim be ranged against an anchor that does not move when the claimant pushes on it. That is the residue worth sending.
