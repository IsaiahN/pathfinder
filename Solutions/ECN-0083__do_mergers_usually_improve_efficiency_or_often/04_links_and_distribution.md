## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Williamson tradeoff and the efficiency defence.** Williamson 1968, the DOJ historical account, and Bet and Blair 2019 (Review of Industrial Organization) on why antitrust authorities have not generally adopted an explicit social welfare standard. The disagreement is about a declared convention, not about a figure fact.
- **Retrospective evidence.** FTC Bureau of Economics program, Ashenfelter, Hosken and Weinberg 2010, 2013, 2015, Hosken, Olson and Smith on grocery retailing, and Bhattacharya, Illanes and coauthors 2025. Read together they support the per-slot and heterogeneity reading, not a single "usually".
- **Input-side power.** Azar, Marinescu and Steinbaum 2022, Thompson 2024, NBER Digest 2018, and the Annual Reviews 2024 survey by Azar and Marinescu. One broad labour evidence pool, cited once.
- **Selection and measurement.** The FTC retrospective program notes enforcement-margin selection; the Goodhart's law page gives the cross-field pattern of a measure ceasing to be good once it becomes a target.
- **Framework essays.** Eight essays by Isaiah N map "shape", ground, provenance, prestige and the simulated status of this kind of derivation. Two were fetched or re-read this session (How Intelligence Actually Works directly, the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap recorded in the exemplar and declared carried); the remainder are carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping and marked as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the four slots and the refuter. Do not open with the framework.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** An outside frame that has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking"** Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before providing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the same material was shown to everyone.
- **Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Send the generator, the derivation and the refuter, not a finished conclusion.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Do not rank recipients by likely agreement. A receptiveness estimate is a contact cost at most, never an evidence column.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and four slots; (2) ask what would count as a failure; (3) record the answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.
