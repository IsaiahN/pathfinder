## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133 "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0083, a shape is therefore not "mergers are good" or "mergers are bad". It is the form that any admissible answer must have: per-slot, anchor-invariant, with a stated failure condition. The concrete shape derived in `01` is:

> four slot-indexed before-and-after comparisons, each ranged against an anchor the merged firm cannot move, each with its own refuter, with no scalar aggregate licence.

That is a constraint on answers. It does not fill the slots.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened directly this session): the replicator "provides the shape of something that you described", and the recursion "will have carved away at so many aspects, that it will fall out of perceptible range and be indistinguishable, in your frame, from the answer." The same essay adds the limit: "recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

Applied here: describe the effect first. "A composition that changes who can set price" is an effect description. "A conglomerate merger" is a vocabulary choice borrowed from the field under study. The census in `01` section 1 deliberately uses effect terms before naming any study or agency.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is licensed here

Only two things license a name: the ground settles a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame with a different closure (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question the concrete slots (cost, price, wages, innovation) and the instruments (retrospectives, HHI thresholds, concentration measures) are imported terms with provenance. Naming them is allowed, provided the provenance is recorded. What is not allowed is treating an imported classificatory term, such as "horizontal merger" or "highly concentrated", as if the framework had generated it. Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world."

## 4. Why the merging parties' projection is the specific forbidden move

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism: metamerism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The merger analogue: profit rose. Did it rise because cost fell, or because the firm gained the power to move price? The projection does not determine the source. A source named from the projection alone is the metamerism error. Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The DOJ verifiability requirement in `01` F2 is the field's own version of the same demand.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A projected synergy validated by the projecting firm is exactly this failure.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade all concrete claims about mergers. It does not. The phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Here no primitive is missing. Cost, output, price, wages, innovation, concentration and welfare standards are all terms already held in the economics frame and imported with provenance. What the figures forbid is (a) inventing a seventh primitive, (b) claiming an import moves the wall when it adds no atom, and (c) naming the source of a lossy reading without a discriminator.

So the correct reading for ECN-0083 is: the figures constrain the *form* of the answer, and concrete per-slot measurements are allowed once a ground settles them. The correction is kept visible so the earlier overstatement is not quietly lost. Triangulated (figures README + Fig 13 :133 + Fig 6 :25).

## 6. Ground versus agreement

*Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* (carried) and *How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened directly): the ground "must be immovable by persuasion and not bi-directional like the frames are." A room of agreeing economists produces an outcome everyone can live with, "having come from diverse frames with different vantage points of the problem. It does not mean that the output was objective truth."

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not." A literature of retrospective studies that share industries, datasets and the enforcement-margin selection rule is one pool, not several witnesses. That is why `01` F9 refuses to count their agreement as corroboration of the shape claim.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Output is "simulacra".

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and every other substantive file in this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of any claim about mergers. They cannot settle anything they report on. The empirical sign of "usually" remains a `gap` owed to a retrospective with a valid control and an anchor outside the merging parties' influence.

## 8. Outline of an answer that satisfies the shape

A shape-compliant answer would read: "For the class of mergers defined by [population, market, period], on slot S1 the cost counterfactual measured by [instrument] moved by [amount] against anchor [A1]; on S2 price and variety moved by [amount] against anchor [A2]; on S3 entry and innovation moved by [amount] against anchor [A3]; on S4 wages and supplier terms moved by [amount] against anchor [A4]. Here is what would have refuted each reading. The welfare convention used is [declared], and the stake of the person applying it is [declared]." Every bracket is owed to a ground reading. No figure supplies it. Reporting the brackets as empty is the honest stop, not a failure to try.
