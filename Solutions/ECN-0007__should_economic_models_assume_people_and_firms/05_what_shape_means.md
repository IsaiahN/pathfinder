## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a
shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135:
*"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies
your question"*. Also: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So for ECN-0007 a **shape** is the set of constraints any admissible expectation assumption must satisfy
before we can name one. It is not a verdict between two camps. The shape here is:

- the belief term b must be distinguishable from the observation o′ and from the model's own prediction;
- the residual must be readable per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the assumption must be violable by a ground reading, so a declared meaning does not count as a settled
  one (Fig 10 :18, :49);
- if the assumption is imported from psychology or institutional analysis, its provenance and its
  shadow test must be recorded (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

A named answer would be "agents form rational expectations" or "agents use prospect theory". A shape is
the test both must pass.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, note recurring new
  terms that overlap with it, and recurse. The anchor is the question itself: *"the finish line will also
  be the starting place"*.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So do not
  begin with the words "rational" or "behavioural"; begin with the effect, a per-slot residual that does
  not shrink.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in
  effect terms."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar record): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a
  plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

Applied here: describe the forecast failure first, then ask which frame predicts its shape. Picking
Muth or Kahneman first and mapping the residual onto them afterwards is the apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 warns
against: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` (local) step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by
   what pays."* For expectations, that means an instrument measuring beliefs independently of the model,
   ranged against out-of-sample outcomes per slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate
   and a shadow test. Bounded rationality, prospect-theory weights, noise-trader risk and institutional
   constraints are atoms already held in other disciplines. Importing them is legitimate, but the record
   must say where they came from and which gap they explain.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (exemplar record): *"The real import is not a new term in the
existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them
neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A model that assumes rational expectations has made a choice
visible; it has not discovered a neutral starting point.

## 4. Why the metamerism point still bites

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior,
then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on
the source"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source.

For ECN-0007, an observed price path is the reading. Many belief-generating mechanisms can produce the
same price path. Naming "rational expectations" as the source of the reading, with no instrument that
discriminates among sources, is the metamerism error. The essay states the remedy: *"The answer is
unreached until a better instrument exists."* This is why F1 and F11 in `01` demand an independent belief
instrument rather than a better argument.

## 5. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund
an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can
settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous names on one side of the expectations debate is the named
collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape claim a budget to be tried and lets a
ground settle it.

**This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real
architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated
derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the expectations
question.

## 5b. Correction to my own §1 to §3

I initially wrote the shape as if it forbade naming any expectation rule, on the model of the exemplar's
first reading of "shape, not a name". That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing**
primitive, one not held in Γ. Rational expectations, bounded rationality and prospect theory are all
already-held atoms in the economics and psychology libraries. Selecting one is a **binding from a world
frame** onto an atom, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong is treating the binding as
if the figures derived it, or as if declaring it settled it (Fig 10 :49). Both the original claim and this
narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README plus Fig 13 :135 plus Fig 10 :49).

## 6. One-paragraph canonical reading

For this question, "shape" means the constraints that any expectation assumption must satisfy before it
can carry verdict weight: a belief term distinct from the observation and from the model's own
prediction, a residual read per slot, a claim that some ground reading could violate, and a provenance
record if the atom was imported from psychology or institutional analysis. The figures do not name the
winning rule; they return a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Naming rational expectations or
prospect theory is a binding from a world frame onto an atom already held, admissible only with a source
gate and a shadow test. The result stops at the limit of the available belief-measuring instruments and
records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
