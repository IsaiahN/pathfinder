**Source:** economics questions.txt line 11. **Section:** 1. Macroeconomic fluctuations and recessions.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive structural claims, `general` on the rules that decide a claim's standing. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement is taken in this package.

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending.

`FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` (verbatim figure text, cited as `Fig N :line`),

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with either modelling camp.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :39-40). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
