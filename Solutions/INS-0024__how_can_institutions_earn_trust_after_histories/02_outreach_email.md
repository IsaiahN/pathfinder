# Outreach draft: validate the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 is still standing and the institution-specific residual has been named per affected group. If the claim cannot survive that check, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about institutional trust repair before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific, checkable claim about how institutions rebuild trust after discrimination, violence, or corruption. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it or to trust the institution. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified` on the shape, `general` on the anchor rule; not `grounded`):** "earned trust" is a relation to a ground the institution cannot move. The legitimate anchor is the observed experience and judgment of the people who were harmed, measured separately for each affected group. It is not an institution-wide trust score, which can be near zero as an average while one group's residual is live. Three things follow: the channel to that judgment has to be reachable and correctable (voice, disaggregated reporting, a way to be found wrong); the verifier must be one the institution cannot reconstruct and whose own reputation does not depend on the finding; and the repair must change contact with affected people, not only the institution's internal conduct. The history of harm cannot be subtracted, only added to. Rebranding substitutes a different habitat and inherits new actors.
>
> **What this does not say:** it does not name a single mechanism as the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Truth commissions, reparations, vetting, apologies and procedural-justice voice are candidates with provenance; which one closes a given case is for the ground to settle. It also does not say a sincere apology is worthless. It says an apology is a molecule, not a word: acknowledgment of wrongdoing, admission of responsibility, and action to compensate, and the arrangement between them carries the meaning.
>
> **What would refute the claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a replicated case where an institution-issued reassurance, with no independent verification and no change in contact, durably lowers the residual for each harmed group across two low-overlap frames;
> 2. a case where aggregate trust rises while the harmed group's own outcome measure does not trend down, and the aggregate cannot be refused;
> 3. a case where a verifier the institution could fully reconstruct, with a reputation to protect, agrees at maximum independence with a genuinely independent verifier;
> 4. a single repair mechanism that clears both the reachable-anchor condition and the renewed-reach condition and leaves no categorically different residue.
>
> I wrote these down before writing to you. If any holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Canonical figures text with line numbers (local extract in this solution's framework): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Full architecture (Medium): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an institutional case you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Claim and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Asking for trust before a verdict converts a possible outside frame into a participant. |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287 "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Per-group measurement, not a single score | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." |
| Independent verifier, not a friendly reviewer | Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." |
| No named mechanism as the answer | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns a shape, not a name. |
| No request for prestige or endorsement | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask users to write their own refuter and mode | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." |

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Record the recipient's verdict before any theory is shown. Ask what they would count as a failure.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links with the note that the figures are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors (Fig 10 :42, Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode. Each stage change is a generator crossing, not a recording pushed at a list (Fig 4 :22-25).

No personal emails are used. Contact goes only through the public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
