## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Psychology of trust (one witness).** The asymmetry of trust ("very hard to regain") and distress-maintaining attributions explain why a history persists in the prior b and why a single reform act does not close a per-group residual. Corroborates F1 and F2, not the verdict itself.
2. **Procedural justice and legitimacy (one witness, distinct field).** Voice and correctable procedure make a process seem fair even when the voice is non-instrumental; rational-legal legitimacy rests on procedure, not proclamation. Corroborates the "reachable and correctable channel" part of the shape (F4).
3. **Transitional justice and truth commissions (one witness pool).** The field itself is defined by rebuilding social trust through a union of mechanisms. The South African "where's the justice?" critique corroborates F5: truth-telling whose consequences the examined institution controls is a convention, not a verdict.
4. **Restorative justice (one witness, but note the internal conflict).** The stakeholder definition corroborates the habitat claim (Fig 11 :39). The finding that programmes become offender-focused conflicts with a naive "bring everyone together" reading. Per the WATSON rule, the conflict was returned to the census: it strengthens the requirement for an independent verification seat (F5), it does not flip the verdict.
5. **Complex systems / philosophy of science (low-overlap witness).** Marks et al. show that syntax transfers across domains while semantics must be rebuilt. Corroborates Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." It is the one peer-reviewed source with no overlap with the framework author.

**Overlap warning.** Sources 1 to 4 are Wikipedia pages. They draw on distinct named literatures (Tyler, Braithwaite, Hayner, Weigand), but they share a platform and a citation style, so they are treated as one broad evidence pool with declared field separation, not as four independent witnesses. The three framework essays share the maximum overlap with the figures (same author) and carry no independent corroboration weight. The figures decide; the world sources only corroborate the shape.

## Distribution: offer, not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-group residual, the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the literature.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The harm here is discrimination, violence or corruption, so spend real effort on a verifier outside the institution's closure.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-group residual and the shape claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record their verdict; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a method, with their own refuter written first.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: the recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-written refuter, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question."

**Hard precondition:** all stages work only if the stage-1 shape survives the pre-registered refuter. As written it does, provided the institution-specific residual is named per affected group before sending. Do not send a generic "institutions should be trusted" claim in its place.
