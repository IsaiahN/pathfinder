# The Tether's answer: How can institutions earn trust after histories of discrimination, violence, or corruption?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the anchor and independence laws. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No institution was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution's model of its own trustworthiness (Γ) and what affected people actually observe (o′) diverge, and the divergence is indexed per affected group: `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`. The history of harm weights the prior b heavily, so the residual persists. A population-average trust score can be near zero while one group's slot is live; that is a legal state and not an anomaly (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). "Earn trust" is therefore a union compound (C1: a reachable and correctable anchor; C2: renewed reach through contact with a differing closure), not one hard question (Fig 9 :55-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is the harmed people's judgment, read per group.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question is constitutively about the people harmed, so their observed experience settles it. The institution's own reputation metric moves when pushed, so it is an instrument, not an anchor (Fig 2 :30, :38).

2. **Agreement cannot manufacture trust.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Internal review, stakeholder affirmations selected for receptiveness, and PR all live inside the institution's closure.

3. **Repair changes contact, not just the institution.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." An ethics code that never reaches a harmed group improves the agent, not the contact.

4. **Keep the channel reachable; the ground does not decay, the channel does.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Voice, correctability, and reporting disaggregated by affected group are instruments for keeping the reading live. Procedural justice research independently finds that giving people a voice, even a non-instrumental one, is enough for a process to be seen as fair.

5. **Buy independence in proportion to the harm.** Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." Because the cost here is discrimination, violence or corruption, the verifier must be outside the institution's closure and must not have a stake in a favourable finding. Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on": past good standing is itself a disqualifying stake, not evidence.

6. **The history cannot be subtracted; rebranding substitutes habitat.** Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Accountability, restitution and voice can be introduced. The historical fact of harm remains in contact, and a spin-off or new name inherits actors that were not chosen.

7. **Repair is an ordered molecule and a union of mechanisms, not one lever.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information." An apology is acknowledgment → admission → compensation, not a word (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`). Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Truth commissions, reparations, vetting and procedural voice are candidate mechanisms with provenance, not a single prescription.

8. **The framework does not name the mechanism that will work.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The specific policy that closes a given case is a declared `gap` to be settled by a ground reading, not hidden as an answer.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution is trustworthy or untrustworthy. No institution was measured.
- Not that a sincere apology is worthless. It is a molecule whose arrangement carries the meaning, and its compensating action is part of the term.
- Not that truth commissions, reparations or restorative conferences are *the* answer. They are imports with source gates and shadow tests.
- Not that the harmed group's judgment is infallible. It is the legitimate anchor because the question is constitutively about people, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure (Fig 2 :37-38). How to keep that judgment unpressured is an open instrument question.
- Not that agreement from experts settles anything. Prestige funds a trial; it cannot settle one.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was paid for by a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." A trust reading can come from genuine repair, a silenced group, a pooled metric or an exclusionary survey. What would close the gap:

- **Per-group outcome measures** with voice and correctability, so a satisfied average cannot hide a live slot.
- **An independent verifier test:** can the institution reconstruct the finding before it is returned? If yes, the verifier is not independent (Fig 10 :42).
- **A stake test:** does the verifier's own reputation depend on the result? If yes, it is disqualified (Fig 10 :93).
- **A contact test:** did any measurement of contact with affected people change, or only internal conduct (Fig 11 :40)?
- **A lever test:** after a single mechanism, is the residue categorically different (Fig 9 :57)?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §3: reassurance alone lowering every group's residual; aggregate rising while the harmed slot is flat; a reconstructible verifier matching an independent one; one lever clearing both conditions; a source-discriminating instrument showing one source only.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached in shape, unreached in name.** The figures settle the form any answer must take: a non-movable anchor, per-slot reading, reachable channel, independent verifier, changed contact, union of mechanisms. They do not settle which policy works for a given institution. That is underivable from the figures and must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test and debited against independence. The six-term substrate and the bond set are "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring them makes a choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing sources, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that an institution can talk its way back into trust. It says that "earned trust" is a relation to a ground the institution cannot move, namely the observed experience and judgment of the people it harmed, read separately for each group so an average cannot hide a live wound. Agreement inside the institution is not evidence, because a mirror cannot correct you and agreeing frames that share an evidence pool are one witness wearing many names. What changes the residual is change in contact: voice, correctability, disaggregated reporting, a verifier the institution could not have authored and whose reputation does not depend on the finding, and mechanisms introduced as a union rather than a single lever. The history cannot be subtracted, and rebranding only swaps one habitat for another. The framework stops there. It returns the shape any repair must have and a refuter written in advance; it does not name the policy, because that is settled by the ground and not by a diagram.
