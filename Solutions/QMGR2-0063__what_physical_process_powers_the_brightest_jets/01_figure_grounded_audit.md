## 0. The claim under audit

> The question asks for a physical process. The ordinary-language answer offered by the literature is a named mechanism: the Blandford-Znajek process, in which magnetic field lines threading a spinning black hole extract rotational energy and carry it away as a Poynting-flux dominated outflow. Rival bindings name the Blandford-Payne disc wind, the Penrose process, or a combination.

The audit does not decide which name is right. It decides what the figures license us to say before any name is imported, and what would have to be true for a name to earn verdict weight.

The sought-for shape was written down in the README before the run: `POWER(???)JET`, a spendable gradient on a finite energy store producing a directed flow plus collimation. That bound term is refusable by the same test as everything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction").

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** At least two conditions, not one. (a) A reservoir must be spent (where the energy comes from: black-hole spin, accretion disc, or both). (b) Directedness and collimation must be supplied (why a narrow bipolar outflow rather than isotropic radiation). A third condition, (c) conversion of the outflow into the observed brightness (the emission mechanism), is in contact with the residual but is not the same object. Fig 9 :4-5: **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."** The world literature supplies the matching observation: the astrophysical-jet overview says the causes "are not yet fully understood" and that the frequency of jetted sources "suggests combinations of different mechanisms". So this is a **union compound**, not a single hard question.
- **Symmetry.** The residual is not spherically symmetric. The jet is bipolar and aligned with the rotation axis. The energy store is described by the substrate as "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (Fig 13 :14); the observed flow has direction. The process must therefore supply directedness. Fig 13 :46-47: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."** The spendable difference is the candidate directedness carrier.
- **Scale.** The reservoir is global (the spin state of a black hole, or the whole inner disc), the extraction is local (horizon, ergosphere, magnetosphere, a few gravitational radii), and the observed flow extends from parsecs to megaparsecs. The question crosses the membrane (Fig 4 :19-20). A mechanism specified at one scale is not automatically a mechanism at another.

**R stated in effect terms:** what spends a finite, directionless energy store and supplies the directedness that produces the narrow, bright, bipolar outflow we see, and which of the candidate reservoirs does the spending in a given jet.

Fig 9 :57: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** A single lever (for example, "name Blandford-Znajek") clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. ## 3. Findings

### F1 The shape is fixed by the substrate: energy plus a spendable gradient compose a flow (decisive for the shape)

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (energy). Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** (gradient). Fig 13 :46-47: **"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."** Fig 13 :75-76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**

Parse: the observed jet is a flow. A flow is not an atom; it is a composition of a state (energy) with a structure that has directedness (gradient). The "brightest" qualifier is the magnitude of that flow, and finiteness of the store is what makes "powers" a meaningful question at all.

Triangulation: the Symbols table gives the same two constraints in different words. Symbols table :91: "Conserved, finite, and directionless on its own." Symbols table :97-98: "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness." Two figure documents, different phrasing, low overlap, same shape. Mark **triangulated** on the figure side.

**Refuter:** show a jet that carries a directed flow with no spendable difference, for example a purely thermal, isotropic expansion that nevertheless produces a collimated relativistic outflow without a gradient; or derive the gradient from energy alone under the six-term set (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility). Either fires and the shape claim is withdrawn.

### F2 The question is a union, and the world disagreement is the signature, not a defect

Fig 9 :4-5 (quoted in §1.1) gives the rule. The world sources supply the case. McKinney 2005 (arXiv:astro-ph/0506367, opened): "A rotating black hole probably powers ultrarelativistic jets in gamma-ray bursts, relativistic jets from some active galactic nuclei, and jets from some black hole x-ray binaries" and "Of all jet mechanisms, we suggest that only the BZ mechanism is able to produce an ultrarelativistic jet." Livio, Ogilvie and Pringle 1999 (arXiv:astro-ph/9809093, opened): "Thus the spin (or not) of the hole is probably irrelevant to the expected electromagnetic power output from the system." Each rule keeps working on a subset: McKinney on ultrarelativistic, Poynting-dominated jets; Livio on disc-fed systems where the field threading the hole is not stronger than the field threading the inner disc. Neither has shrunk the other away after decades of work. By Fig 9 :4-5 that is not one unresolved question but several well-formed ones.

The astrophysical-jet overview adds a third condition: "Their causes are not yet fully understood, but they are believed to arise from dynamic interactions within accretion disks", and "some jets are possibly powered by spinning black holes. However, the frequency of high-energy astrophysical sources with jets suggests combinations of different mechanisms". Verdict: the literal question "what physical process" is a union, so there is no single name to return. This is a figure-side verdict from Fig 9, corroborated (not decided) by the world sources.

**Refuter:** a single mechanism that demonstrably accounts for the power and collimation of every observed jet class, with one anchor and no residual left over, would dissolve the union into a partition. Until then verdicts stay per jet class.

### F3 The named mechanism is a `gap`, not a figure result

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

No figure line selects spin over disc, or magnetic extraction over the Penrose process. Re-census was run twice (§8). After a genuine census the name is still not governed. Therefore `gap`, of the **shape-not-a-name** kind, not of the unreachable-in-principle kind: world sources already hold candidate names, and a source-discriminating instrument is owed. The honest report is that the figures return `POWER(GRADIENT)JET` and stop.

This is the central refusal of the solution. It is not a silence and not a failure to search. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

**Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that derives a specific reservoir (spin or disc) from the six-term set without importing the astrophysics frame. If such a line exists, the `gap` is closed figure-side. None was found after two censuses.

### F4 "Powers" is a production relation, and the operands are not at the same level

Operators table :36-39 defines production: "A produces B; B did not exist before", with the test "is there a B before A fires? No." Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** The question asks what produces the jet; it does not ask which ingredients the jet contains. A name that lists ingredients (spin, field, disc, plasma) without the bond is the flat-formula collapse named in Fig 12.

The energy store is directionless and the flow is directed, so the bond carries the directedness. Calling the mechanism "spin energy" alone records the operand, not the production.

**Refuter:** show that the observed jet is fully specified by its energy budget alone, with no bond information beyond the store, so that production collapses to conjunction. Not observed.

### F5 Per-slot indexing: the brightest jets are not one slot

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**

A radio-loud quasar, a blazar, a gamma-ray burst and a microquasar are different slots. Spin is a benefactor for one residual and possibly irrelevant for another (Livio et al.). A single averaged "process powers jets" answer throws away the only signal that says where to look. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and per jet class, never averaged.

**Refuter:** a measurement in which two different jet classes return the same reservoir signature with the same collimation signature would collapse the slots into one, and the per-slot verdict would be withdrawn.

### F6 The ground is a source-discriminating instrument, not agreement

Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 1 :23: **"The ground enters as the only metric."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

The observable here is a bright, narrow, relativistic outflow. That reading does not by itself identify its source. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism in the framework's own essays: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source", and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The essay's resolution is instrumental: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Transferred here: brightness and collimation are projections of several possible sources (spin extraction, disc extraction, or both). A spectrometer analogue that discriminates them is owed.

Crucially, the instruments already exist and are already returning something that fails to resolve. Fig 6 :34-35: **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."** VLBI, the Event Horizon Telescope, X-ray timing and GRMHD simulations are all returning readings; the dispute between Livio et al. and McKinney is precisely a failure to resolve source from reading. This is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** The collimation link has instruments; the source-discriminating link does not yet have one that pays.

**Refuter:** name a working instrument that returns the reservoir signature directly (spun-down black hole correlated with jet power at fixed accretion rate, or an equivalent discriminator), and the gap becomes reachable at this budget. Until it is run, no verdict is `grounded`.

### F7 The name is a world import with a source gate, and carries zero figure verdict weight

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."** Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."**

"Blandford-Znajek" is an import from the astrophysics frame into the framework vocabulary. It has a genuine source gate in the world literature (Blandford and Znajek 1977, MNRAS 179, 433, doi:10.1093/mnras/179.3.433) and a shadow test (it explains the observed power of radio-loud jets). But those gates are world-side. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The name is a filter for where to look, not a witness for the verdict. The `09_watson_answer.md` world section records the same name as secondary corroboration only.

An earlier draft of this audit wrote "the answer is Blandford-Znajek" as if the figure census returned it. That was retracted; see the log in §10.

**Refuter:** show a figure-side derivation of the name with provenance and a shadow test run inside closure(Gamma). The world citation alone is insufficient.

### F8 A hint of second order: the bipolar jet composes over a settled flow

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**. Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

The energy-to-flow molecule is settled in the sense that outflows are observed across many systems. It can therefore serve as an atom for the next composition: a bipolar, collimated structure with a network of field lines relating the two sides. That is a second-order composition, not a new primitive. Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

This is a structural hint, not a derivation of collimation. The actual collimation physics is world-side and unresolved. Refuter: show a settled flow that cannot compose into a bipolar jet without a new primitive, or that the bipolar structure is a first-order molecule after all. Mark `specified`, unmeasured.

### F9 Isomers over a shared atom set

Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Written over the full shared atom set, spin-extraction and disc-extraction are isomers: the same six terms arranged with the gradient bonded to a different state (time/spin versus space/disc). As the literature usually writes them they share only the outflow and the magnetic network, which makes the weaker **functional group** claim. Full derivation and falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Refuter:** if either mechanism requires an atom the other does not, the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group claim stands. This is stated before the derivation in `06`.

## 7. Strain test and re-census

**S1. Apparent conflict: Fig 13 :76 says a gradient over space and a gradient over time are different terms, while the BZ literature treats a single magnetic field as both extractor and collimator.**

Re-census: Fig 13 governs which *state* a gradient is bonded to. The spin-down gradient is over time (energy changes through the spin state); the magnetic field geometry is a gradient over space. They are different terms, exactly as the figure says. The literature's single field is a world-side binding of two framework terms, not one term doing two jobs. Not strained after re-census. Kept visible because the first pass tried to force them together.

**S2. Apparent conflict: Fig 13 :14 says energy is directionless alone, yet the observed jet is intensely directed.**

Re-census: the figure says "alone". Energy plus a gradient composes a flow (:75-76). The direction is carried by the bond, not by the store. Not strained. The first pass had treated "directionless" as a claim about jets, which was a category error.

**S3. Apparent conflict: Fig 11 :51 says no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual, yet the question asks for a standing process.**

Re-census: the process is a composition shape, not a list of actors. Spin and disc are habitat actors whose valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49). The question's standing-process preference was the term to refuse, not the figure. Not strained after refusing the preference.

**S4. Live strain: the emission chain (D4) has no anchor in this folder.**

This is not a figure conflict; it is an unclosed census line. It is recorded as an excluded domain, not smoothed into the verdict. A synchrotron or inverse-Compton specialist would have to supply the anchor before "brightest" is fully accounted for. `gap`, unreached.

**S5. Live type question: "brightest" as a threshold.**

Fig 12 comparison is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Operators table :49). "Brightest" is a comparison over jet luminosity, and the threshold is set by instrument selection and flux limit, not by the substrate. No figure line supplies the cut. Recorded as a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

After re-census: no figure contradiction; one excluded domain (S4) and one threshold type question (S5) recorded openly.

## 10. Conclusion

The shape survives and the name does not. A jet is a flow; a flow needs a finite energy store and a spendable, directed difference. The question as posed bundles the reservoir condition with the collimation condition and the emission chain, so no single answer is licensed. The figures return `POWER(GRADIENT)JET` and stop. The name is an import from the astrophysics frame, recorded with provenance and given zero verdict weight. What would close the gap is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument or another agreeing paper. Mode `specified` on the shape, `general` on the split, `gap` on the name, not `grounded`.
