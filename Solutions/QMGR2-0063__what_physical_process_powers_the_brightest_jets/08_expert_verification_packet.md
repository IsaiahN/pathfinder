# Expert verification packet: the Tether on what powers the brightest jets

**What this is:** a claim in a form a relativistic-jets researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-slot verdicts, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the named mechanism. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the source-discriminating level is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and the whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A relativistic jet is a flow, and under the six-term substrate a flow is a finite energy store composed with a spendable, directed difference (a gradient). Energy is directionless alone; the directedness is carried by the bond, which is what the word "powers" denotes. The question bundles at least two conditions, which reservoir is spent and what supplies collimation, so no single named mechanism is licensed by the figures. Spin extraction and disc extraction are isomers over the full shared atom set and members of one functional group as usually written. Which reservoir wins in a given jet is a `gap` owed to a source-discriminating observation, not to a famous name. What is offered is the shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument condition.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 13 and the Symbols table

- **Statement:** a directed outflow requires a spendable gradient bonded to a finite energy store. The process is that spending, not the bare store.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75-76, *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 13 :14, *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (energy). Fig 13 :22, *"a difference that can be spent"* (gradient). Symbols table :91, "Conserved, finite, and directionless on its own." Symbols table :97-98, "A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a directed relativistic outflow with no spendable difference; or derive the gradient from energy alone under the six-term set. Either fires and the shape claim is withdrawn.

### C2 World-facing, checkable by a jet researcher against the two candidate bindings

- **Statement:** the question is a union, not one mechanism. Spin extraction (Blandford-Znajek) and disc extraction (the Livio et al. position, and Blandford-Payne) are two members of one functional group over the atom set, and strict isomers over the full shared set.
- **What to check:** do the two characterisations split the observed jet classes as claimed? McKinney 2005 (arXiv:astro-ph/0506367, opened 2026-11-23) says "Of all jet mechanisms, we suggest that only the BZ mechanism is able to produce an ultrarelativistic jet." Livio, Ogilvie and Pringle 1999 (arXiv:astro-ph/9809093, opened) say "Thus the spin (or not) of the hole is probably irrelevant to the expected electromagnetic power output from the system." The astrophysical-jet overview says "some jets are possibly powered by spinning black holes" while the causes "are not yet fully understood" and "suggests combinations of different mechanisms". Each rule works on a subset. That is Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*
- **Figure support for the isomer claim:** Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :124, *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** show one mechanism accounting for reservoir and collimation across all jet classes with one anchor and no residual; or show either binding needs an atom the other cannot be written with (kills the strict isomer claim, leaves the functional-group claim); or show both use the same bond (they collapse to one term).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that "the answer is Blandford-Znajek". The figures return a shape, not a name.
- Not that spin, the disc, or frame dragging is ruled out. Each is a binding with residual-relative valence (Fig 11 :49).
- Not a derivation of jet luminosity, Lorentz factor, or collimation angle. No number is returned.
- Not a theory of the emission chain. The synchrotron and inverse-Compton chain has no anchor in this folder and is excluded from the verdict.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an observation taken by this author.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess (They're Scanning Books, 2026).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source", and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

1. Does the reservoir signature track jet power at fixed accretion state, or not? Either answer fires on the union.
2. Does a single mechanism return the same collimation and power signature across radio galaxies, blazars, gamma-ray bursts and microquasars? If yes, the union dissolves; if no, per-slot verdicts stand.
3. Is there any observable that distinguishes a spent spin store from a spent disc store when both give the same bright, narrow flow? If no instrument can, the `gap` becomes unreachable in principle rather than unreached, and the verdict must be restated.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :14, :22, :46-47, :75-76 and Symbols table :91, :97-98. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: does the McKinney/Livio split track a real divide in the literature, or has one side since won? If one side has won at the ground, report the measurement, not the citation count.
3. Name one instrument that would return the reservoir signature at fixed accretion state, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
