## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :132-133 adds that "a system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the distinction is concrete:

> `POWER(???)JET` is a shape. "The Blandford-Znajek process" is a name. "A spendable gradient on a finite energy store producing a directed flow" is a shape. "Magnetic field lines threading the ergosphere" is a name.

A shape constrains: any answer must account for directedness from a directionless store. It does not fill the slot with a reservoir.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap from the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from the ordinary terms "power", "jet", "bright", "spin", "field" and narrow toward "flow", "gradient", "finite store".
- The same essay gives the split rule that governs F2 of `01`: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is Fig 9 :4-5 in narrative form, and it is why the question "what physical process" is answered as a union, not a single name.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In effect terms the residual is: something spends a finite store and supplies directedness, producing a bright bipolar outflow. "Blandford-Znajek" is a cause word imported from the astrophysics frame, so it cannot be used as the description of the residual.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the governing rule for `02_outreach_email.md`: send what an answer would predict, not the mechanism name.

## 3. The stopping rule and the paradox

*Library of Babel*: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And: "by the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Here the loop stops when two conditions both hold: the composition shape is stated (energy plus gradient to flow), and the name is identified as a `gap` owed to a ground instrument. That is a genuine stop, not a premature one. The alternative, naming Blandford-Znajek as the answer, would be treating the search vocabulary as the answer, which is the paradox the essay warns about.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For jets, that means a source-discriminating observation at fixed accretion rate, not a well-cited mechanism.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Blandford-Znajek name has both in the world literature, so it is admissible as a *candidate binding*, not as a figure result.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*, opened, sharpens what an import can be: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. A solution that used the figures to "prove" that black holes must extract spin energy would be turning a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 5. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes can produce the same reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's resolution: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists", and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Mapped to this question:

| reading | candidate sources that give the same reading |
|---|---|
| a bright, narrow, relativistic outflow | spin extraction (Blandford-Znajek), disc extraction (Blandford-Payne), frame dragging (Penrose), combinations |

The brightness reading does not determine the source. Naming one source from the reading is exactly the metamerism error the essay names. What is owed is the analogue of the spectrometer: a measurement that discriminates among sources at fixed accretion state. The world sources show that such a discriminator is not yet paying: McKinney 2005 argues only BZ can produce an ultrarelativistic jet, Livio et al. 1999 argue the hole's spin is "probably irrelevant". Two frames ranging against the same imperfect readings still disagree, which is the signature of a projection that cannot comment on its source, not of a settled mechanism.

## 6. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids using any named mechanism. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Gradient, network, spectrum, energy, space and time are all already held. Naming a binding from the astrophysics frame onto held atoms (spin to energy-over-time, magnetic topology to gradient-over-space, outflow to flow) is allowed as provenance. What is forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh primitive, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall, and (c) presenting the name as a figure derivation.

My §5 metamerism point is **not** retracted. It does not rest on a ban on names. It rests on the absence of a discriminator between sources that give the same reading. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction is the direct analogue of `QMGR-0037` `05` section 5b and `06` Q1 in the exemplar.

## 7. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The most cited mechanism, or the most famous jet, is a budget for trying a claim, not a verdict about it. `03_public_contact_points.csv` therefore contains institutional venues, not famous-name lists, and `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a failure, not an endorsement.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any jet mechanism and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. One-sentence answer to "what shape is this question's answer?"

A shape that constrains any answer to spend a directed, spendable difference on a finite, directionless energy store to make a collimated flow, while leaving the reservoir name open until a source-discriminating instrument closes it.
