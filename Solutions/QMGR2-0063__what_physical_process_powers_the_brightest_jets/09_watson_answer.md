## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. A jet is a flow, and a flow requires a finite, directionless energy store composed with a spendable, directed difference. "Powers" names that production bond, not an ingredient list. The literal question is a union of reservoir and collimation conditions plus an unanchored emission chain, so verdicts stay per jet class and are never averaged. Spin and disc bindings are isomers over the full shared atom set and members of one functional group as usually written; the bipolar jet is second order over a settled flow. Which named reservoir wins in a given jet is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, owed to a source-discriminating instrument, not to a citation count. The shape verdict is `specified` and triangulated; the structural split is `general`; the name is `gap`; nothing is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the mode, the refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a famous mechanism name instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is hiding the `gap`, which would replace an honest stop with a manufactured consensus.
- **undoable:** yes. The `gap` remains a slot until an instrument pays for a name; no primitive has to be withdrawn to revise it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and the union split can be refined into a partition if one mechanism is shown to cover every slot. What is not undoable without cost is a recipient who has already absorbed the framework before recording a verdict on stage 1.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement that returns the reservoir signature at fixed accretion state would name the slot; a directed relativistic outflow with no spendable difference, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five, would break the shape; a single mechanism covering every jet class with one anchor would dissolve the union; a figure re-census that admits a figure line naming the reservoir would close the `gap` figure-side. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
