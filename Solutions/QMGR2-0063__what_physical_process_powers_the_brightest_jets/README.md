## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before running the loop, the sought-for shape is written down and bound so it can accrue a residual like anything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"). Here the bound term is:

> `POWER(???)JET` : a jet is a directed flow, so whatever powers it must spend a directed difference (a spendable gradient) on a finite energy store, and must supply collimation (a relation among the outflow parts).

A name such as "the Blandford-Znajek process" is a binding of that shape to the astrophysics frame, not the shape itself. The derivation records which is which.

## Question restated as an effect

Two options are being decided. Option A: a single named physical process powers the brightest jets, and the task is to name it. Option B: the figures fix only the shape any answer must have (a spendable gradient on a finite energy store producing a directed flow), while the name of the process is a `gap` that a source-discriminating ground must close. Option A sends a `general`-mode verdict in a `specified` slot, imports a name without a source gate, and turns agreement among researchers into a pseudo-anchor. Option B sends a checkable shape, a written refuter, an honest `gap`, and an instrument condition.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No assertion that the figures name Blandford-Znajek, Blandford-Payne, the Penrose process, or any other mechanism. The figures fix a shape; the name is a world-source binding with zero verdict weight from the figures.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for people who already favour a mechanism manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Agreement is not settlement.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts an outside frame into a member of the frame being checked.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
