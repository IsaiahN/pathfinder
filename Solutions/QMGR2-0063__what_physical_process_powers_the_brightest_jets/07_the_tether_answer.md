# The Tether's answer on what powers the brightest jets

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and on each per-domain verdict, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the named process. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe a narrow, bright, bipolar outflow moving close to the speed of light from some black hole systems. The residual is: what spends a finite energy store and supplies the directedness that makes a directed flow, and which reservoir does the spending in a given jet. The question bundles at least two conditions (reservoir and collimation) plus a third contact-only chain (emission), so it is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5). Verdicts stay per jet class.

## 2. The shape

1. **A jet is a flow, and a flow is a composition, not an atom.** Fig 13 :75-76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* The energy store is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14); the gradient is *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22) and carries directedness (Fig 13 :46-47). So the process must spend a directed difference on a finite store to produce a directed outflow. "Brightest" is the magnitude of that flow, and finiteness is what makes "powers" a real question.
2. **"Powers" is a production bond, not an ingredient list.** Operators table: "A produces B; B did not exist before". Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A name that lists spin, field, disc and plasma without the bond has recorded half of the answer.
3. **No single name is licensed.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Two censuses found no figure line selecting spin over disc or field extraction over frame dragging. The specific process is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind. The figures return `POWER(GRADIENT)JET` and stop.
4. **The question is a union, so the answer is not one mechanism.** Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The world disagreement between McKinney 2005 (only BZ reaches ultrarelativistic speed) and Livio et al. 1999 (the hole's spin is "probably irrelevant" when the field threading the hole is not stronger than the field threading the disc) is exactly that signature. It is evidence for the union, not a problem to be smoothed away.
5. **Per-slot indexing.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A radio galaxy, a blazar, a gamma-ray burst and a microquasar are different slots. Actor valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so spin can dominate one slot and be irrelevant in another. No average mechanism.
6. **The bipolar jet is second order over a settled flow.** Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. The collimated pair composes over a settled energy-to-flow molecule plus a field relation. No seventh primitive. The number of levels beyond two is a live `gap`.
7. **Spin extraction and disc extraction are isomers over a shared atom set.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* As written in the literature they share only the functional group (Fig 12 :124). Full derivation and falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the answer is Blandford-Znajek". That is a name from the astrophysics frame with zero figure verdict weight, however well sourced.
- Not "spin is irrelevant" or "the disc is irrelevant". Both are bindings whose valence depends on the jet slot.
- Not a derivation of jet power from the six-term set. The framework returns a shape, not a number, not a reservoir, not a mechanism.
- Not a theory of collimation. The magnetosphere loop is a structural hint (Fig 12 :121), not a plasma calculation.
- Not a claim about the emission chain. That domain has no anchor in this folder and is excluded from the verdict.
- Not grounded. No observation was taken by this author, and the existing instruments do not discriminate source at fixed accretion rate.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

The observable (bright, narrow jet) cannot comment on its source, in the words of *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." What is owed is the analogue of a spectrometer.

- **Source discriminator:** a measurement that returns the reservoir signature at fixed accretion state. Example shape: a correlation of jet power with black-hole spin at fixed accretion rate and fixed field topology, or its absence. Either result would fire on the union and name the slot.
- **Real falsifiers from `01` §4:** a directed relativistic outflow with no spendable difference; a derivation of one of the six from the other five; a single mechanism that accounts for reservoir and collimation across all jet classes; a figure line that names the reservoir directly; a measurement contradicting the energy-plus-gradient shape; a demonstration that the bipolar structure is first order or needs a new primitive.
- **Instruments are already returning something that fails to resolve.** Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* VLBI, the Event Horizon Telescope, X-ray timing and GRMHD simulations all return readings; the Livio/McKinney disagreement is the failure to resolve. So this is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **Threshold on "brightest":** set by instrument flux limits and selection, not by the substrate. A filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

What we offer is the shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument condition. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
