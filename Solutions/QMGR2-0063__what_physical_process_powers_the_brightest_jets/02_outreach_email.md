# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the mechanism

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until a reader can check the shape claim without holding the framework. If stage 1 does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about relativistic jets before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a relativistic jet is a flow. Under the minimum set the framework assumes, a flow is a finite energy store composed with a spendable, directed difference. Energy is conserved, finite and directionless on its own; a gradient is a difference that can be spent, and it is the gradient that carries directedness. So whatever powers a jet must spend a directed difference on a finite store and supply the directionality. "Brightest" is the magnitude of that flow. This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement, and it does not name a reservoir.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the answer is the Blandford-Znajek process, or the Blandford-Payne disc wind, or the Penrose process. Those are bindings from astrophysics onto the shape, and in the opened literature they disagree. McKinney 2005 argues only BZ can produce an ultrarelativistic jet; Livio, Ogilvie and Pringle 1999 argue the spin of the hole is probably irrelevant to the electromagnetic power output. I read that as a union of conditions rather than one unresolved mechanism, so I am not asking you to pick a side. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a directed relativistic outflow with no spendable difference anywhere in the chain;
> 2. a derivation of any one of the six assumed terms from the other five;
> 3. a single mechanism that accounts for the reservoir and the collimation of every jet class with one anchor and no residual left over;
> 4. a working instrument that returns the reservoir signature at fixed accretion rate and contradicts the energy-plus-gradient shape.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **The question I actually want your help with:** what observation would discriminate a spin store from a disc store when both produce a bright, narrow flow? The jet reading does not by itself comment on its source. If you know of a measurement that already does this, or can say why none can, that is the answer I am looking for.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Blandford and Znajek 1977 bibliographic record and mechanism summary: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blandford%E2%80%93Znajek_process
> - Livio, Ogilvie and Pringle 1999, the opposing reservoir: https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9809093
> - Current high-energy astrophysics listings, to see who is working on the residual: https://arxiv.org/list/astro-ph.HE/recent
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the framework material. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## What is deliberately absent

- No "this is the true mechanism" sentence.
- No famous-name endorsement, no citation-count argument, no appeal to authority.
- No request for belief, trust, or a share.
- No claim that the framework has settled the question it reports on. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.
