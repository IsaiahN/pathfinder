# Expert verification packet: the Tether on separating institutional effects

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in causal identification, institutional economics or political methodology can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape of a valid separation, `specified` on any claim about a particular institution or design. Not `grounded`.** No institutional effect was measured in this derivation.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A raw association between an institution and an outcome cannot be a verdict when both draw on the same historical, economic and cultural evidence pool. The separation problem is a union, not a list of variables, so it must be unbundled before a method is chosen. Separation then requires an import of variation from a frame whose closure differs from the institution's own habitat, with independence bought and tested against an anchor that does not move, an enumeration of the surrounding habitat outward, per-slot reporting instead of an average, and a separate test for transport. The institution is a molecule whose bonds may be cultural, so the level at which culture is an atom or a bond must be declared with an instrument. What is offered is a shape with four refuters written in advance, not an estimate.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 9

- **Statement:** the raw institution-outcome association is disqualified as a verdict, and the separation problem is a union that must be unbundled before searching.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **What to check:** does the figure actually disqualify the association, or only warn about misreading it? Does a single control lever leave an intersection standing, as Fig 9 :55 claims?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case in which the institutional effect is additive and invariant across every stratum of the accompanying conditions, with a pre-registered interaction test, so the union is a partition there; or exhibit an anchor that disqualifies none of the association.

### C2: identification-facing; checkable against any design-based study

- **Statement:** separation inside the observed joint distribution of institution and conditions is impossible without an import from a closure that differs; independence must be bought and tested, not declared.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **What to check:** is the claim about the figure's closure operator, not a claim that regression is useless? Does the proposed source of variation fail to move when the background conditions move, so that it qualifies as an anchor under Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"?
- **Refuter:** derive the separation from inside the same observational distribution with no new variation, and show it is invariant to functional form and to unmeasured confounding of the kind Bulbulia (2024) and Byrnes (2025) describe; or produce a balance, placebo or over-identification test that fails and show the estimate survives it.

### C3: structure and transport; checkable against a multi-site or multi-context study

- **Statement:** the habitat must be enumerated outward and effects reported per slot; transport to another habitat is a separate claim; culture may be a bond rather than an atom, and which it is depends on the level of analysis.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** does the study enumerate second-order contact, or stop at first-order covariates? Does it report heterogeneity per unit? Does it test transport rather than infer it from internal validity? Is the informal norm treated as a separate variable when it is functioning as the bond?
- **Refuter:** show a first-order enumeration exhausts the cascade for that case; or show a transplanted institution produces the identical effect where the original conditions are absent; or show the written rule and the informal norm vary independently with separately identified effects, which would make them two atoms at that level.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an estimate of any institutional effect, and not a new dataset.
- Not that randomised trials are the only admissible import. Fig 6 :14 names the operator and does not rank its instances.
- Not that culture is always a confounder separate from the institution. Whether it is atom or bond is level-relative and is recorded as a live strain in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 8.
- Not that a within-sample effect transports.
- Not that institutional effects are unidentifiable in principle. Fig 8 :41-42 distinguishes underivable in principle from unreachable at this budget.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an outcome.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered design with independent variation, a transport test in a second habitat, a source-discriminating instrument, or a pre-registered interaction test showing the union is a partition for a given case. The standing falsifiers are in section 2 of this packet and section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :40, Fig 9 :54-55, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :32 and Fig 11 :44, :66. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one study you know: was the source of variation independent of the historical, economic and cultural conditions, and was that tested rather than asserted?
3. Check C3 against the same study: were second-order conditions enumerated, were effects reported per unit, and was transport tested?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish an institution effect from the background configurations producing the same reading, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no later agreement will be pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
