## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

An answer is not a single coefficient. It is a *shape for a separation procedure*: a description of the residual to be explained per unit, an enumeration of the conditions in contact with it, an import of variation from a frame whose closure differs from the institution's own habitat, a check that the imported source does not draw on the same evidence as the accompanying conditions, a per-slot report, and a refuter written before the run. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. Agreement is not a verdict. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (when quoted with its leading clause). Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :32: "Independence is what makes agreement mean something."
3. Asking for trust before a check spends independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
