## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" (usage rule). Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form of a defensible separation that can be stated before a particular institution, dataset or instrument is named. It constrains: any acceptable answer must unbundle the compound, import variation from elsewhere, buy independence, enumerate the habitat, and report per unit. It does not fill: it does not say which institution matters, how large the effect is, or which instrument will work in a given case.

For INS-0097 specifically:

> A shape = *per-slot residual + unbundled compound + imported variation + bought independence + enumerated habitat + declared refuter.*

That is a procedure with a testable form, not a coefficient.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "with each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Recursive proximity, not a leap to a name.
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay form of Fig 9 :54 and it applies directly to the question in this folder, which is a bundle.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." This is why the residual in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 is written as a prediction gap per slot rather than as a variable name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason the outreach in `02_outreach_email.md` asks what a reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before sending the sources.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The loop stops at the limit of what can discriminate, not at a comforting answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument version: the colour instrument "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source", and the metamerism case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." An institutional effect is likewise a reading from an instrument; several background configurations can produce the same reading, so the reading does not name its source without a discriminator.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :53), with a source gate and a shadow test. In this field that is a randomised rollout, a discontinuity, a natural experiment or a historical shock, offered with the reasons it is independent of the accompanying conditions.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second item: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A new control variable is a new term; a source of variation that breaks the joint distribution of institution and conditions is a new dimension.

And its limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The variable list a researcher declares is a visible choice, not nature's own list.

## 4. Correction to my own section 3 (keep both readings)

In the first draft of this folder I wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming a research design. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing", a primitive not held in the frame. An instrumental variable or a difference-in-differences design is not a missing primitive of the Tether; it is an import from the identification frame, and naming it is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong in the first draft is the opposite error: treating a named design as if naming it settled independence. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A design name buys nothing until the independence test is run. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost:

- naming a design is permitted and must be provenanced;
- naming a design does not settle whether it separates the institution from its conditions.

## 5. Why contact and independence decide the answer

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." And: "The group's agreement is never the verdict." Ten studies that all cite the same identification strategy are one witness wearing ten names. This is Fig 2 :31-32 in the language of research communities: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the drift mechanism: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." A literature that checks its institutional-effect estimates only against other estimates in the same literature drifts in one direction. The stop is a ground that does not move: an outcome, a register, an experiment.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the same point at the level of representation: "the mapping is not a tree with roots and branches, it is a web, with no canonical order and no single path through." A single estimate is a path through that web, not the web itself, which is the round-trip argument in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." An influential journal, a famous author or a large grant can give a separation design a budget to be tried. None of them can make the instrument independent of the conditions. The ask in `02_outreach_email.md` is therefore for a check, not an endorsement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And on LLM output specifically: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that an LLM simulation "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any institutional effect and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* closes with "This essay is my provenance record", which is the right standard for this folder too: the lineage is the checkable object, not the confidence of the prose.

## 8. The honest stop for INS-0097

This derivation can say what a separation must look like. It cannot say whether any particular institution has been validly separated from its accompanying conditions, because no dataset, instrument or outcome was read here. That verdict is `unreachable at this budget`, not silence, and not a universal claim that institutional effects are unidentifiable. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor in this field is an outcome that pays or does not, not the agreement of readers.
