# Outreach draft: check the separation claim before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 has been re-read. The offer collapses on contact if the refuter is not stated first.

---

**Subject:** One claim about identifying institutional effects, with a test that could break it

> Hello [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against your own work. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`general` shape, not `grounded`):** when a researcher estimates the effect of an institution on trust, growth, corruption or conflict, the institution almost never appears alone. Historical, economic and cultural conditions travel with it. The question is how much of the observed association belongs to the institution and how much to the conditions that accompany it. Stated per unit, the thing to be explained is the part of the outcome the institution term fails to predict, unit by unit. If that residual collapses when the surrounding conditions are entered, the association was carried by a shared background source. If it persists and does not trend down, something is owed to the institution itself.
>
> **The shape claim I want checked:** a raw institution-outcome association cannot be a verdict when both draw on the same evidence pool, because the agreement is the pool talking. Separating the two requires unbundling the bundle before searching it, importing variation from a setting whose closure differs from the institution's own habitat, and showing that the imported source does not move with the accompanying conditions. The residual must then be reported unit by unit, not folded into one average, and the habitat must be enumerated outward because the condition that matters can be several steps away.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular institution has a particular effect size; that randomised trials are the only acceptable source of variation; that culture is always a separate confounder rather than partly the bond that makes the institution what it is; and that a clean within-sample estimate transports to another setting. Those are separate questions with separate tests.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. a design that fully separates the institution from its accompanying conditions inside one closure, with no new variation and no import, and with an effect that is invariant across every stratum of the conditions;
> 2. an association that survives every enumerated habitat condition and every placebo, balance or over-identification test, against an anchor that does not move when the researcher's reading changes;
> 3. an institutional transplant that produces the identical effect where the original accompanying conditions are demonstrably absent;
> 4. a single estimate that predicts outcomes in every target population without further information.
>
> If any of those holds, I will retract the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - The figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The architecture paper, including the section on mappings that were offered and declined: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the method:** write down what would refute your own result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework's own limit is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so a preference for a preferred answer is invisible unless it is made an ordinary term first. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, recorded explicitly

- **Stage 1:** the recipient checks the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters using their own knowledge and data. Their verdict is recorded before any framework material is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, the three links are sent, with the note that the figure text is the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a reader who has seen the interiors shares the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** the formula is offered as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Nothing here asks for trust, agreement or a public endorsement. Nothing is sent by this agent.
