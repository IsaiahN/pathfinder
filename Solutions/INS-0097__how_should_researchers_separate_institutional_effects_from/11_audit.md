## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the part of an outcome the institution term fails to predict, per unit. Arity: the treatment as observed is a compound of the institution and its conditions, so a union (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: the observed association is symmetric in the data while the causal ordering is not, which is the signal of a common cause. Scale: the operative condition can be first order or several steps out.
- **Domain 1, identification and design.** Actors: researcher, source of variation, registers. Currency: independence bought from variation uncorrelated with the conditions. Anchor: a measurement that does not update. Question type: partition after import, since only a different closure adds the missing atom.
- **Domain 2, transport and habitat.** Actors: study and target populations, informal rules, geography. Currency: what survives a substituted habitat. Anchor: the target ground. Question type: a separate downstream claim.
- **Domain 3, verification and reporting.** Actors: readers and replications. Currency: residuals and refuters. Anchor: the ground. A checking arrangement, not a causal domain.
- **Rejected candidate: raw association.** No anchor for the causal claim. It is the collapse, not a domain.

Order by dependency: describe R per slot, measure the units, enumerate the habitat, unbundle the union, characterise R in effect terms, import variation, test independence, report per slot, test transport, declare the refuter. Refuse a held false term before minting (formula step 2, a formula rule rather than a figure line).

My census matches the primary's four positions, including the rejected candidate. Where the primary derived from Fig 2's common-cause panel and Fig 6's import operator, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 11's enumeration rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
