## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules, stated before the practical advice:

- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a method and can be given away. A conclusion is not a method and should not be pushed.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what a reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A reader who has absorbed the framework before recording a verdict shares the pool. Their later agreement is not a second witness.
- **Fig 7 :35:** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Do not select venues by how likely they are to agree.
- **Fig 9 :60:** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Prestige and reach are contact-cost filters, never evidence.

**Recommended order in any reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the four refuters and the request "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) the verdict recorded in writing; (4) only then, if invited, the three links, with the note that the figure text rather than this summary is the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the recipient checks the claim using their own ground. No framework material is shown. Their verdict is recorded first.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the framework sources are offered. Agreement after this point is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** the formula is offered as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it. A failure is more useful than a success.

## Hard precondition

Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are still live because none has been run against a real dataset in this simulation. Send the claim as a claim, not as a result.
