## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a defensible separation of an institution I from its accompanying historical, economic and cultural conditions C looks like. Option A: treat the observed institution-outcome association as the effect, adjusting for C as nuisance covariates. Option B: treat the association as a compound, unbundle it, import variation from a closure that differs, buy independence against an anchor, enumerate the habitat and report per unit. What each leads to: A produces a coefficient that may be the pool talking, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a shape with refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where no source can discriminate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The part of an outcome Y that the institution term fails to predict, per unit s, before and after the conditions C enter. Arity: the observed treatment is a compound of I and C, which co-occur. Symmetry: the observed I-Y association is symmetric in the data while the causal ordering is not, and that mismatch is the warning. Scale: the operative condition may be first order or several steps out (Fig 11's DDT case). It is a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, identification and design.** Actors: the researcher seat, the imported source of variation, outcome registers. Currency: independence bought from variation uncorrelated with C. Anchor: a measurement, experiment or register that does not move when the researcher's reading changes. This is a real domain.
- **Domain 2, transport and habitat.** Actors: study population, target population, informal rules, geographic and cultural conditions. Currency: whether the effect survives a change of habitat. Anchor: the ground in the target setting, not the imported estimate. Downstream of Domain 1.
- **Domain 3, verification and reporting.** Actors: outside readers, reviewers, replications. Currency: residuals and refuters, not agreement. Anchor: the ground. A checking arrangement rather than a causal domain.
- **Rejected candidate: raw association.** No anchor for the causal claim, only measurements that fix numbers but not meaning. It is the collapse to be escaped, not a domain to solve inside.
- **Partition or union.** Union, because I and C are overlapping conditions of the same cases. Unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R per slot, measure the units, enumerate the habitat, unbundle the union, characterise R in effect terms, import variation, test independence, report per slot, test transport, declare the stake and the refuter. Optimising a later link cannot rescue one that was never reached.

Strain test applied: Fig 9's "split rather than search" and Fig 8's "import is the only wall-mover" are sequential, not rival. Fig 11's "isolation is substitution" and Cartwright's result that RCTs establish causal claims concern transport and internal validity respectively, different objects. One live strain remains: whether an informal norm is a bond or an atom is level-relative, and which level a study is at needs an instrument.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any particular institutional-effect estimate is undecided here. A raw association is disqualified as a verdict when institution and outcome draw on one evidence pool; the separation problem is a union that must be unbundled; separation requires an import from a closure that differs and independence bought and tested against an anchor; the habitat must be enumerated outward; effects must be reported per slot; and transport is a separate claim. Culture is sometimes a bond rather than an atom, and which it is at a given level needs an instrument. This is a `general` structural result with `specified` sub-claims, ungrounded and candidate. No dataset was read.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three checkable sub-claims and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an estimate instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices when it says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The sub-claims can be narrowed individually without touching the census: a case can turn out to be a partition, a design can be replaced, a bond can turn out to be an atom at the level studied. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no estimate is being retracted because none was produced.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered design with independent variation and passing placebo and balance tests would settle the identification clause; a transport test in a second habitat with conditions enumerated in advance would settle the habitat clause; a source-discriminating instrument of the kind described in *The Hard Problem is About Source* would separate an institution effect from the background configurations producing the same reading; a pre-registered interaction test showing a partition would narrow the union clause; and a falsifier firing as listed in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate and the status is unreachable at this budget.
