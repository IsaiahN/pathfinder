# Expert verification packet — the Tether on cultural division of time, space, nature and social relationships

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cultural-cognition researcher, linguistic anthropologist or linguistic relativist can check. Mode, derivation, world conflicts and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the culture-specific name. Not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The four domains in the question are not four independent dimensions along which cultures carry values. They are windows onto one fixed six-term set: states space, time and energy, and structures spectrum, gradient and network, with composition running across the two kinds and never within one. Time and space are held states; social relationships are the held network structure; nature is a compound over energy plus ordering and directedness. What varies across cultures is the typed bonds over those axes, not the axes themselves. Continuity, discreteness, spatial-axis choice, color-boundary placement and kin-term merger are arrangement facts or composition remainders. The four slots overlap, so verdicts are kept per slot and never averaged into a cultural style score. A disagreement is settled by behavior that does not move when an informant or analyst relabels the division. The framework returns that shape and refuses to name which division a given culture uses; that name is a gap owed to measurement.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** no new kind is introduced by the four domains. Time and space are two of the six states; social relations are the network structure; nature is a compound over energy, spectrum and gradient. Cultural variation cannot be variation in the kinds.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 13 :35 — "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity. Neither are spectrum, gradient and network."
  - Fig 13 :41 — "Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."
  - Fig 13 :115 — "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a division of time, space, nature or social relationships that cannot be composed from the six held terms and is not a compound of them; or derive one of the six from the other five; or show the set admits a seventh member.

### C2 — framework-internal plus world-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** variation lives in typed bonds, and the four slots form a union whose verdicts must be kept separate.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :5 — "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
  - Fig 12 :117 — "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 13 :124 — "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."
  - Fig 9 :54 — "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
  - Fig 1 :19 — "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check in the world:** is the observed cross-cultural difference concentrated in relations over the axes (axis choice, boundary placement, which kin positions merge) rather than in the existence of new axes? Does a single lever leave the other intersections standing? Focal-color universality with boundary variability is the cleanest test case; kin-feature dimensions are the second.
- **Refuter:** show a division requiring a direct bond of two states or two structures with no intervening opposite-kind term; or show that the four slots form a clean partition so one lever clears all of them; or show a behavioral measure that pools the slots without losing a live signal.

### C3 — framework-internal plus verification theory; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 9

- **Statement:** a claim about a division is settled by a non-updating behavioral anchor, not by agreement inside a shared narrative; and the framework returns a shape, not the culture-specific name.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :29 — "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
  - Fig 2 :35 — "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
  - Fig 9 :32 — "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
  - Fig 13 :134 — "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"
- **What to check:** is the informant's own account being used as the anchor when it is only an instrument? Do two "independent" informants or fieldworkers actually share one elicitation protocol and one translated stimulus set? If yes, their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Refuter:** show a division settled by self-report that does not survive a behavioral task; or show two frames drawing on genuinely independent evidence that reach incompatible division claims and that the ground cannot discriminate; or supply the culture-specific name from the figure set alone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named culture divides time vertically, uses an absolute frame, has N color terms, or merges cousins. Those are names, and the framework does not supply names (`gap`).
- Not a cultural style score or a ranking of cultures. That is an aggregate over slots (Fig 1 :22) and a budget filter, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that language determines thought. The world evidence includes a published failure to replicate (Chen 2001 against Boroditsky 2001); the conflict is recorded in `01` section 8, and the figure verdict does not depend on resolving it.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess at compression axes.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
- Not a claim about which cross-domain mapping is a bond versus an import. That is live strain S4 in `01` section 7.

## 5. What would settle it

Ground readings, not agreement:

1. **Axis test:** a preregistered replication of the time-metaphor priming task with a non-spatial target, resolved against the analyst's narrative; this adjudicates the recorded Boroditsky/Chen conflict.
2. **Frame test:** compass-checked pointing plus rotated-array memory coding for absolute versus relative frame speakers.
3. **Foci versus boundary test:** color-chip focusing and boundary placement across languages.
4. **Kin-grid test:** independent coders mapping terminologies to a genealogical grid with declared feature dimensions.
5. **Standing failures:** a seventh kind; an invariant division under a non-updating anchor; a direct state-state or structure-structure bond; a mapping shown to be a post hoc gloss; two "independent" frames shown to share one protocol.

5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
