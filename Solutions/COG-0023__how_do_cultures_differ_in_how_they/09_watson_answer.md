## QUESTION

Option A: treat the four as four independent dimensions on which cultures take values and seek a style profile. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-slot gap between the divisions inherited from the analyst's language and institutions and the divisions that govern reasoning and conduct in an unfamiliar community. Arity: four named slots plus their overlaps, so at least five live slots. Symmetry: "divide up" reads as symmetric, but the cross-domain mappings are ordered, so the compound carries ordered bonds (Fig 12 :46). Scale: individual learner, linguistic community, institution (calendar, taxonomy, kinship law).
- **Domain 1, time.** Actors: speakers, calendars, temporal metaphors, bilinguals. Currency: distinctions that change non-linguistic ordering and timing judgments. Anchor: behavioral priming and ordering tasks that do not move when the informant relabels them.
- **Domain 2, space.** Actors: speakers, wayfinders, gesture. Currency: navigation, memory coding, direction accuracy. Anchor: compass-checked pointing and rotated-array memory.
- **Domain 3, nature.** Actors: speakers, folk taxonomies, color lexicons. Currency: category foci and boundaries. Anchor: color-chip focusing and sorting; expert taxonomy.
- **Domain 4, social relationships.** Actors: speakers, kinship terminologies, descent groups. Currency: which relations merge or split and the obligations that follow. Anchor: genealogical-grid term mapping and observed conduct.
- **Domain 5, overlaps.** Actors: metaphor mappings, hierarchies, developmental sequences. Currency: the cross-domain bond itself. Anchor: tasks that discriminate a mapping from its source domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Time is divided through space, social relations through spatial and hierarchical terms, nature through temporal order. One lever clears its intersection and leaves the others standing (Fig 9 :54-55).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, probe, carry (Fig 3 :20). The chain breaks at the objective link where a dictionary is treated as a grammar (Fig 12 :115). Describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Settle each division against the ground before comparing two of them (Fig 12 :104).

Strain test applied: "nature" strained as a seventh kind and was re-censed as a compound over energy, spectrum and gradient. Symmetric "divide up" strained against ordered metaphor mapping and was re-censed as ordered bonds. Human judgment as anchor strained against agreement as disqualifying and was dissolved by separating the question's object from the pool of agreeing frames. Two strains remain live and are named below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the culture-specific name does not. The four domains are windows onto one fixed, indivisible six-term set: time and space are held states, social relationships the held network structure, nature a compound over energy plus ordering and directedness. Cross-cultural variation is carried by typed bonds over that set, not by new kinds; continuity, discreteness, axis choice, boundary placement and kin-term merger are arrangement facts or composition remainders. The four slots form a union, so verdicts stay per slot and are never averaged into a style score. A claim about a division is settled by a non-updating behavioral anchor, not by agreement inside a shared narrative. The framework returns a shape and refuses to name which division a named culture uses; that name is a declared `gap` owed to measurement. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `gap` on the name, not `grounded`. This is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle which division any particular culture actually uses.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a global cultural-style convention instead of a per-slot generator (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The global-style reading and any named-culture binding are removable without touching the substrate derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A preregistered axis test resolving the Boroditsky/Chen conflict, compass-checked pointing and rotated-array coding for frame-of-reference speakers, color-chip focusing separating foci from boundaries, or independent kin-grid codings would name bindings. A demonstrated seventh kind, an invariant division under a non-updating anchor, or a direct state-state or structure-structure bond would break the shape claim. A result showing that a cross-domain mapping adds an atom with a source gate and shadow test would resolve strain S4 as an import. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
