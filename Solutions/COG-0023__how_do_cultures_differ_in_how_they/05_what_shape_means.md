## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

*The Library of Babel* gives the operational version: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Elsewhere in the same essay: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of".

For COG-0023 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer about cultural division that can be stated before any particular culture is named. It constrains: variation lives in bonds over a fixed axis set, verdicts are per slot, the anchor is non-updating behavior. It does not fill: it cannot say that a named people divides time vertically or merges cousins. That shape versus name distinction is the central methodological result of this folder.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, note recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the exact instrument problem in cross-cultural comparison: an inventory of a culture's own words is context and subtext without the pretext, so an effect description ("what you would see in a non-linguistic task") is the only portable claim.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place," and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is the six-term substrate: once the four domains are mapped onto held terms, further searching for a seventh kind is a search inside a closed set (Fig 13 :115-118).

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question the instrument is behavioral (priming, pointing, focusing, term-to-grid mapping), and it must not move when the informant or analyst relabels the division (Fig 2 :29-30).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A folk-biology taxonomy imported into the figure frame is such a binding. It must be recorded as coming from that frame, not derived from the six.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this and is unusually relevant to the "nature" slot. A spectrometer reads green but "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An instrument that cannot distinguish these cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the instrument, not an argument, that resolved it. Applied here: a color term or a kin term is a reading. Naming the underlying division from that reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating task.

## 4. Why a "cultural cognition style" is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." And: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."

A global style label ("holistic," "analytic," "high-context") is an aggregate over slots (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average") and a convention built from shared pretext. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources." Two fieldworkers with one manual and one translated stimulus set are "one frame wearing two names" (Fig 8 :48-49).

## 5. Shape for this question: the exact content

The shape returned by the derivation is a four-part constraint:

1. **Closed axis set.** The four domains are windows onto the held six; no culture contributes a seventh kind (Fig 13 :41, :115). Time and space are states, social relations the network structure, nature a compound over energy plus ordering and directedness.
2. **Bond-level variation.** What differs across cultures is which typed relation is used over the held axes (Fig 12 :5, :46, :94). Continuity, discreteness, axis choice, boundary placement and kin merger are arrangement facts or composition remainders (Fig 13 :124).
3. **Union, not partition.** The four slots overlap, so verdicts are per slot and never averaged (Fig 9 :54-55; Fig 1 :19).
4. **Behavioral anchor.** A claim about a division is settled by something that does not move when the frame relabels it (Fig 2 :29, :37-38).

Everything beyond these four items is a name, and the framework does not supply names.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §5 (kept visible)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any cultural naming whatsoever. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here nothing is missing from the six: space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network are all held. Naming a culture's specific arrangement is therefore a **binding** from the field frame onto held atoms, which is provenance plus measurement, not the invention of a seventh kind and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

What remains forbidden is stronger and narrower: the framework cannot *derive* which binding a culture uses, and calling the binding a result of the framework would be the metamerism error of section 3. Both statements are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A famous ethnography or a high-citation time-metaphor paper gives a claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether a culture divides a domain in a given way. The Chen 2001 replication failure is the concrete case: a widely cited result did not survive a ground test, and the correct response is to record the conflict, not to weigh the names.
