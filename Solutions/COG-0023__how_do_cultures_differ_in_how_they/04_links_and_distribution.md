## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** — "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the whole figure set before someone has described what they would count as a failure sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot residual, the shape claim and the five refuters.
- **Fig 10 :42** — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering any source link.
- **Fig 2 :40** — "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for likely agreement. Two people using the same elicitation manual count as one witness.
- **Fig 2 :42** — "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Spend the budget on a frame that did not read this folder and does not share the analyst's field training.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the shape claim with its mode; (2) the five refuters, with the request "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then, if invited, the framework links with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any framework interior is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the local framework links. Their agreement after stage 2 is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A reply that identifies a failure is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The Medium/Substack record itself warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula," so a method without a local ground is not a result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only make sense if stage 1 survives. The shape claim here is general and the culture-specific name is a declared gap. Do not send a claim that a particular culture divides time in a particular way; that is the part the world literature itself contests and the part the framework cannot name.
