## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, stated in full in `02` and `04`:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict across a shared pool.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
2. **An anchor must not update.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." For a question about people, the legitimate ground is behavior that does not move because an informant or analyst relabels it (Fig 2 :37-38).
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

## The one-line result

The four domains are not four independent dimensions along which "culture" has a value. Time and space are two of the six held states, social relationships are the held network structure, and nature is a compound over energy and the ordering and directedness structures. Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Cultural variation therefore lives in the bonds and arrangements over a fixed, indivisible six-term set, never in new kinds. The framework returns that shape and refuses to name which division a given culture uses; that name is owed to a non-updating behavioral ground.
