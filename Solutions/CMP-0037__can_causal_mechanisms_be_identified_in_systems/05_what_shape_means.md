## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For CMP-0037 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of a mechanism claim that can be stated before the specific mechanism is named. It constrains: the answer must be a bonded arrangement with a lineage, indexed per slot, over an enumerated habitat, checked by low-overlap frames against an anchor that does not update. It does not fill: it does not say which arrangement is the one that fired.

`MECHANISM(???)`, with the bond type and the route still open, is a shape. `PART-A ⇒ PART-B` is a name. The difference matters because the question asks *whether mechanisms can be identified*, and the honest answer distinguishes shape-identification from name-identification (see section 5).

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* For this question the closest held vocabulary is `R` per slot, `atoms(Γ)`, bonds, habitat, anchor and lineage. We describe the effect (a vector of residuals after one action) and bootstrap from these.
- Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* We do not begin with the word "cause" from any one discipline; we begin with the effect vector.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email in `02` asks what a recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before showing any framework material.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* For CMP-0037 the stop is reached when the remaining gap is a missing instrument, not a missing arrangement.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :54-57 in narrative form and is the source of the union verdict in F3.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only three things, all outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles a minted term.** `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README adds that the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). *Arrival of the Fittest* tags its borrowed results as *"borrowed machinery: a result this framework uses (an import), developed elsewhere and unchanged"*, and tags its corroborating cases as *"an instance: a case the framework describes, arrived at independently by someone else."* The distinction is the record, not the contents.
3. **A discriminator that changes contact.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"* and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* An instrument that cannot distinguish competing sources *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the third point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* If a mechanism cannot be named because the measurement scheme cannot represent the distinction, the owed thing is a new axis, not a new label.

## 4. Why mechanism identification is a source question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the exact mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The central sentence is: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."*

That is CMP-0037 in one line. Several typed arrangements can create the same per-slot outcome vector. The vector is a projection. Naming the mechanism from it is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading. *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the general form twice: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it"*, and *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* It adds that *"The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."*

So the answer to "can the mechanism be identified?" depends on which of two cases holds, and the essays name both. Where a trace exists, depth can recover it: the lab-grown diamond case, where isotope ratios distinguish mined from grown once a tool can read them. Where no trace survives the transformation, only provenance (the intervention record) can distinguish. *How Intelligence Actually Works* names the collective form: a projection sinks below the **union horizon** when *"no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin"*, and warns that *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* That is why this solution records a `gap`, not a denial.

## 5. Q: does the shape license naming a mechanism in a system?

**No, not by itself.** What the shape licenses is a refusal of the unqualified question and a set of conditions under which a name becomes legitimate.

1. The framework returns a shape, so it cannot select which arrangement occupied the slot (Fig 9 :32; canonical README).
2. Held atoms may be bound from the system's own vocabulary as provenance, but the binding is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such. *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*
3. A name is licensed only by a ground settlement or a provenanced import with a shadow test, and for a mechanism in a complex system that means an instrument that discriminates among the arrangements that would give the same reading (section 3).
4. The intervention record is not optional. It is the lineage, and the lineage *is* the term (Fig 13 :130). To discard it and keep only the outcome vector is to keep the projection and throw away the thing that could distinguish its source.

**Does any part of this folder commit the forbidden error?** It would if it asserted a named mechanism for a specific system. It does not: `01` section 4 records the specific mechanism as a `gap`, `07` section 5 keeps the slot open, and `08` section 3 lists "the mechanism in system X" as a non-claim. Both readings are kept visible in the retraction log.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any naming at all, which would have made the whole question unanswerable. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in `Γ`. The causal vocabulary here (atoms, bonds, habitat, anchor, lineage) is already held; naming a binding of a measured quantity onto a held atom is a provenance act, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is (i) claiming the framework selected the binding, (ii) claiming the binding moves the wall as an import that adds an atom, and (iii) naming a source off a projection with no discriminator. My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted; its reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on names. The earlier and corrected readings are both recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequence for the derivation's status

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* supplies two consequences:

- **Prestige is not action.** *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A response selected for people likely to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.
- **This audit is a simulation.** *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* records the same caveat: generated outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and **cannot** settle it. The only settlement is a ground reading: an intervention with a non-updating anchor and a discriminator that separates the candidate arrangements.
