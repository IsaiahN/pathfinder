# Expert verification packet: the Tether on CMP-0037

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist in causal inference or complex systems can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the structural skeleton. Not `grounded`.** No intervention on a real system was run for this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

One action that changes several connected parts at once does not, by itself, identify a mechanism. It produces a vector of per-slot residuals and a bundle of overlapping conditions. A mechanism is a typed arrangement plus a lineage, and the route cannot be read off the outcome. Identification is possible only as a shape: per-slot residual, unbundled union, habitat enumerated outward, a bond vocabulary that can express cycles and catalysts, and two frames with different closures ranged against an anchor that does not update. Over a shared part set, two mechanisms differing only in bond placement are isomers and the same reading cannot separate them. The specific mechanism in a specific system stays a gap until a discriminator exists.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12, 13 and 1

- **Statement:** the outcome vector is the artefact, the intervention record is the lineage, and the route is not recoverable from the artefact. The residual is indexed per part, never averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :129, **"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."** Fig 12 :5, **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 1 :19, **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
- **What to check:** is the distinction between a part-set and a bonded arrangement real for the systems you know? Does a pooled effect size ever locate the live part when the global residual is near zero?
- **Refuter:** show that two different typed arrangements with the same parts and the same per-slot outcome vector are always distinguishable from the vector alone, or that a global aggregate reliably locates the live slot.

### C2 — method-facing; checkable by a causal-inference specialist

- **Statement:** the multi-part change is a union of overlapping conditions, and identification is conditional on structure, assumptions and independent frames. Association is not production; production `⇒` has the test **"is there a B before A fires? No."**
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 2 :35, **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :40, **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."**
- **What to check:** does joint intervention identifiability require the structural assumptions this claim says it requires? Are the world-side results (Jeunen et al. 2022; Panayiotou and Şimşek 2026; Hitchcock, SEP) correctly characterized as conditional rather than unconditional?
- **Refuter:** exhibit two frames with high evidence overlap producing an independent verdict by the figure's own test, or show that one rule covers every subset of a multi-part response with an error that keeps shrinking.

### C3 — structure-facing; checkable against Figure 11 and the habitat cases

- **Statement:** contact cascades several steps out, and isolation substitutes a habitat rather than removing one. A test harness proves wiring, not capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52, **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** Fig 11 :66, **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
- **What to check:** in your system, does the mechanism claimed sit at one step or several? Was the control an unchanged habitat or a substituted one? Does the intervention import actors the analyst did not choose?
- **Refuter:** show a real intervention that removes a part while leaving the habitat unchanged in every respect that bears on the residual, or that second- and third-order contact terms carry no residual mass.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a single intervention names a mechanism in a specific system. That is a `gap` here.
- Not that the framework can select which arrangement occupies the slot. It returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that agreement among readers or among frames sharing a source is corroboration.
- Not that a synthetic or simplified setting transfers to the real habitat.
- Not that failure to identify proves absence (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any ground is claimed.

## 5. What would settle it

1. An instrument that discriminates among the typed arrangements producing the same per-slot vector.
2. A second low-overlap frame ranged separately against a non-updating anchor, with the verdict recorded before any framework material is shown.
3. A fully enumerated habitat with second- and third-order contact terms measured.
4. A bond test separating production from sequence at the ground.
5. Any of the eight refuters in `01` section 6 firing, which would withdraw the corresponding finding.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, Fig 13 :129, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :55 (four short passages).
2. Name one system you know where the same outcome vector could come from two different arrangements. Say what would discriminate them, or why nothing can.
3. Check C2 against your own practice: when do two analyses of the same intervention count as independent frames?
4. Name one second- or third-order contact term that a direct-contact scoping would miss in your system.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
