## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific mechanism does not. In Domain 1 a mechanism is a bonded arrangement with a lineage, the residual is per part, and a multi-part reading cannot recover the route, so mechanisms are identifiable in shape and not in name. In Domain 2 the multi-part change is a union of overlapping conditions with an outward cascade and a substituted habitat, so the conditions are identifiable and the partition into mechanisms is a gap until a system is enumerated. In Domain 3 identification requires low-overlap frames and a non-updating anchor and is bounded by instruments already returning something, so the specific mechanism is unreached at this budget. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified` on domains, `general` on the skeleton, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named mechanism instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap is local. Refuters are written before the run, cuts are ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :41), and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A superseding packet can remove a wrong binding without touching the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates the candidate arrangements producing the same per-slot vector would name the mechanism. A real intervention with a non-updating anchor and a second low-overlap frame would settle the Domain 2 partition. A fully enumerated habitat with measured second- and third-order contact would close the cascade claim. Any of the eight pre-registered refuters firing would reverse the corresponding finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
