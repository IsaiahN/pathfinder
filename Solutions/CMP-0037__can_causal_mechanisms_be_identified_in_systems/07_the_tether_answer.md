# The Tether's answer: causal mechanisms under multi-part interventions

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the structural skeleton. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

One action is applied and several connected parts change at once. The residual is `R(s) = |Γ(b,a)(s) − o′(s)|`, indexed by object slot. The question asks whether the mechanism can be identified: the typed arrangement and the route by which the action produced the vector, not just the size of the change. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: where different rules work on different subsets, the honest output is several well-formed questions (Fig 9 :4, **"Split it rather than search."**).

## 2. The shape, per domain

### D1. Identification logic: IDENTIFIABLE AS A SHAPE, NOT AS A NAME

1. A mechanism is a bonded molecule with a lineage, not the set of parts that moved. Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 13 :129: **"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."** The outcome vector is the artefact; the intervention record is the lineage.
2. The residual is indexed per part, never averaged. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** A global effect near zero with one live part is a legal state.
3. The arrangement must be expressible. Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."** A causal claim must state which bond it asserts; production `⇒` is not sequence `→`, and a chain cannot close a loop (Fig 12 :121).
4. Two mechanisms over the same part set that differ only in bond placement are isomers (Fig 12 :117): **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** The same reading does not determine the source.
5. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32). The specific mechanism in a specific system is a `gap` at this budget, not a negative answer.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 12 plus Fig 13 plus Fig 1, low overlap). Identifiable in shape; name identification is owed a discriminator.

### D2. Intervention and habitat: CONDITIONAL, UNION BEFORE SINGLE MECHANISM

1. A multi-part intervention is a union of overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. Enumerate outward. Fig 11 :52: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** The mechanism is often three steps from the changed part.
3. Valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49), so there is no standing list of causal parts that survives a change of question.
4. Isolation substitutes a habitat. Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** A test harness proves wiring, never capability (Fig 11 :70). Transfer inherits *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"* (Fig 11 :29).

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 plus Fig 11 plus Fig 1). Conditions are named; the specific partition into mechanisms is a `gap` until a system is enumerated.

### D3. Measurement and frames: REACHABLE ONLY AT THE INSTRUMENT LIMIT

1. Identification needs a non-updating anchor and low-overlap frames. Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."**
2. One intervention plus its outcome is one frame. A second frame must differ in closure and draw on different evidence (Fig 8 :45, **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**). Provenance separates convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :50).
3. An instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32). Where nothing at any resolution is failing to resolve the candidate arrangements, there is no edge to extend. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."**

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 plus Fig 8 plus Fig 6). The conditions for identification are `specified`; the name of the mechanism in any system is a `gap`.

The three verdicts are kept separate. They are **not averaged** into a single yes or no.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a single intervention identifies a mechanism. It identifies a vector of per-slot residuals and a bundle of conditions.
- Not that the framework can name the mechanism in a system. It returns a shape.
- Not that a mechanism found in a test harness transfers. A substituted habitat proves wiring, not capability.
- Not that more agreement helps. Frames sharing an evidence pool are one witness.
- Not that a failure to identify proves absence. *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. No intervention was run and no measurement was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

1. **A discriminator.** An instrument that separates the candidate arrangements that give the same per-slot vector. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the green case.
2. **A second low-overlap frame** ranged separately against an anchor that does not update. The intervention record, the control and the measurement must be stitched provenantly, not pooled.
3. **An enumerable habitat** with second- and third-order contact terms measured, so "three steps out" is a checked fact rather than a metaphor.
4. **A bond test.** Does the evidence distinguish `⇒` from `→`? Is there a B before A fires?
5. **Standing refuters from `01` section 6:** route recoverable from the artefact; an adequate global aggregate; one rule covering every subset; no cascade; a self-anchored frame; subtractive isolation; a flat vocabulary sufficing; a discriminator built from nothing.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The gap is local and named: no instrument in this derivation discriminates among the typed arrangements that would produce the observed per-slot vector, and no second low-overlap frame was consulted about a real system. *They're Scanning Books* warns that a minimum viable set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* and that *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Fig 4 :30 requires the round-trip gap `R_T` to be measured, not assumed zero; `11_audit.md` records it qualitatively. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Yes, but only in a restricted and checkable sense. One intervention that changes several parts at once does not by itself identify a mechanism: it produces a vector of per-slot residuals and a bundle of overlapping conditions. A mechanism is a typed arrangement plus the record of what fired in what order, and the route is not recoverable from the outcome alone. So the framework returns a shape. Identification requires that the residual be indexed per part, that the union be unbundled before searching, that the habitat be enumerated outward to the second and third order, that a chain vocabulary be replaced by one that can express cycles and catalysts, and that two frames with different closures be ranged separately against an anchor that does not update. Over a shared part set, two mechanisms differing only in bond placement are isomers, and the same reading cannot tell them apart. The specific mechanism stays a gap until an instrument discriminates the candidates. That is a shape, a refuter written in advance, and an honest stop, not a name and not a verdict.
