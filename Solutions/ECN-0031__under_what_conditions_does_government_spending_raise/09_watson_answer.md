# WATSON answer: when does government spending raise total output rather than displace private activity?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal multiplier or sector list. Not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. ## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the relation between government spending and total output. Option A: treat the question as a two-way choice and answer with a multiplier above or below one. Option B: treat it as a union of overlapping conditions and return a per-slot separator with a declared gap. What each leads to: A hands a ratio the job of choosing an ordered bond, which it cannot do, and hides the live signal in an aggregate; B hands the reader checkable guards, per-domain verdicts and four refuters, and says plainly that no number or sector list is available. The question as posed prefers a list of conditions. That preference is bound as an ordinary term in `README.md` so it can be refused, and it is refused as a universal list in `01` section 4.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The discrepancy between predicted and realized output per slot (sector, region, household class, capacity type) when the fiscal authority acts. Arity: two operands, public spending and private activity, in one accounting. Symmetry: not symmetric, the bond is ordered (production `⇒` or subtraction `−`), so a correlation cannot choose the arrow (Fig 2 :39). Scale: per slot and per period versus an aggregate over both. The aggregate is a filter, not the verdict object.
- **Domain 1, fiscal authority frame.** Actors: treasury, legislature, finance ministry. Currency: budget authority, bond issue, currency. Anchor: realized receipts and realized output, which do not move when the authority changes its forecast. Question type: composition inside the closure, or import of an atom the private closure lacks.
- **Domain 2, private production frame.** Actors: firms, households, savers, investors. Currency: private expenditure, investment, sales, saving. Anchor: realized sales and cash flow. Question type: bond assignment over a shared atom set; addition and displacement are isomers (Fig 12 :117).
- **Domain 3, real-resource habitat.** Actors: workers, machines, materials, land, time. Currency: physical capacity, utilisation, inventories. Anchor: physical feasibility at a moment. Question type: support, whether some slot carries positive mass.
- **Domain 4, monetary and credit channel.** Actors: central bank, banks, bond market. Currency: loanable funds, liquidity, the interest rate. Anchor: the observed rate and observed credit creation, provided the authority cannot declare them into existence. Question type: a channel that can break while the ground does not.
- **Domain 5, verification frame.** Actors: statistical agencies, fiscal researchers, narrative coders. Currency: per-slot residual estimates and shock series. Anchor: an exogenous or narrative shock and a non-updating outcome record. Question type: verdict weight, never receptiveness.
- **Union, not partition.** "Raises output" and "displaces private activity" overlap; both can hold in different slots of one episode, and the intersection is a specific compound (Fig 9 :55, :57). One lever leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into one multiplier.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Perceive the live slot; have terms for the two bonds; state the objective in a declared currency; direct the spend; carry only what settled. The likely break is the middle link, where a vocabulary of ratios exists without a term for the bond.

Strain test applied: the Fig 6 statement that mint cannot add an atom was initially read as forbidding a multiplier above one. Re-census assigns Fig 6 to primitives and the multiplier to settled molecules and to a different currency (Fig 12 :125), so the contradiction dissolves without changing either figure. It remains recorded as S1 in `01` section 7.

- **Economics / reference synthesis.** Wikipedia, "Fiscal multiplier" (2026, opened 2026-11-23). Quote: "the spare capacity available". Predicts that support is the pivot and that MPC heterogeneity splits the slots. Corroborates D1 and D3. One pool with the next two references; counted once.
- **Economics / crowding out.** Wikipedia, "Crowding out (economics)" (2026, opened 2026-11-23). Quote: "if the economy is at capacity or full employment, then the government suddenly increasing its budget deficit ... could create competition with the private sector for scarce resources". Predicts displacement where atoms are already held. Corroborates D2 and D3.
- **Economics / liquidity trap.** Wikipedia, "Liquidity trap" (2026, opened 2026-11-23), quoting Keynes 1936 and Krugman 1998. - **Heterodox macroeconomics.** Wikipedia, "Modern Monetary Theory" (2026, opened 2026-11-23). Quote: "money-financed spending is sustainable only while the economy has spare capacity". Predicts the same support condition, with a different policy conclusion. Corroborates the shape, not the politics; heavily criticised, recorded as a claim not an authority.
- **Public finance / narrative data.** Hites Ahir, Miyoko Asai, Matthieu Bellon and others, "Inside fiscal policy: A new dataset on government actions", VoxEU, 23 November 2026; opened 2026-11-23. Quote: "multipliers vary more within income groups than between them." Predicts per-slot variation larger than group averages; also supplies a source gate through direction, instrument, motivation and implementation status. Corroborates D1, D3 and D5. This is the strongest world witness because it reads actions rather than inferring them from outcomes.
- **Human cognition, low overlap.** Wikipedia, "Overjustification effect" (2026, opened 2026-11-23), summarizing Deci, Koestner and Ryan 1999 and Cameron et al. 2001. Quote: "rewards can increase intrinsic motivation for tasks that initially hold little intrinsic interest". Predicts that the sign of an external input depends on the initial state of the slot. Corroborates the shape only, not the economic mechanism.

The economics sources are one broad evidence pool and count as one witness; the psychology source is the only genuinely low-overlap corroborator and it supports shape only. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is a union of conditions with different anchors, so it does not admit one averaged answer. The verdict object is the per-slot residual. Over a fixed atom set, addition and displacement are isomers, distinguished by a production bond or a subtraction bond rather than by an amount. A spending is a candidate for raising output only when some slot carries positive residual, the new activity composes from what the economy holds, any missing atom is genuinely imported with provenance and a shadow test, and the channel to a non-updating outcome stays open; any zero factor forces inertness and the spending reallocates. The pooled multiplier is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. No figure line returns a number, a slack threshold or a standing sector list, so those are a declared `gap` of the unreached and shape-not-a-name kind. The whole folder is `specified` and `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, guards and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is handing a ratio the job of an ordered bond, which would send a program to the wrong slot, and spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The gap is declared rather than filled, so no number has to be withdrawn. An import claim can be refused without deleting anything from the literature; its standing to be composed over as a verdict falls (Operators :58). A program wrongly classified can be re-read against a per-slot outcome without changing the separator.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A narrative action record with implementation status plus a per-slot non-updating outcome could assign a single spending's change to addition or displacement in every slot and would turn the `gap` into a `grounded` verdict for that case. A controlled episode at full capacity with an intact channel and no import that still raises output one for one would break the support condition. A multiplier stable across regimes with a pinned anchor would collapse the union into one condition. A figure re-census that admits a ratio can choose an ordered bond would clear the C2 type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
