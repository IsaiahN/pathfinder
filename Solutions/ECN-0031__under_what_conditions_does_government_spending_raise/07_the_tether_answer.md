# The Tether's answer: when does government spending raise output?

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal multiplier or standing sector list. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7). No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is posed as a choice: spending raises total output, or it displaces private activity. In effect terms these are two bonds over the same atom set, not two values of one quantity. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The verdict object is per slot, not the national total: Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

The question is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition (Fig 9 :55). Addition and displacement can both occur in one episode in different slots, and the case where both bind is categorically different from either alone (Fig 9 :57). So the answer is a **separator**, and the verdicts stay per domain.

## 2. The shape: four conditions, a product, and a declared gap

A spending action is a **candidate** for raising output when all of the following hold together. THE_FORMULA step 3 makes the guards a product: *"gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness."*

1. **Support, a live slot.** Some slot carries positive residual: idle capacity, unmet demand, hoarded saving, an output gap. Fig 5 :21: *"the gap has real mass somewhere"*. If every slot is saturated, addition cannot be minted; only reallocation is composable.
2. **Reachability and novelty, a produced activity.** The activity must compose from what the economy already holds and must not already be in the private library. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Operators table :37: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*.
3. **Import where the private closure lacks an atom.** If the spending brings a demand floor, a public good, a coordination point or a source of finance the private frame cannot create, that is an import and it is the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). An import owes a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-13).
4. **An open channel to a ground that does not update.** The action must reach the live slot while the outcome stays readable. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. At a zero lower bound the private channel is saturated and the ground has not moved (Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*). If the monetary authority offsets the spending, it is a frame ranging against the same ground, not the ground itself.

Where condition 1 or 2 fails while the atoms are already held by private actors, the bond is subtraction (Operators table :43, *"A with B removed or absent"*) and the spending displaces private activity. Where condition 3 holds, the two outcomes are not isomers at all; the wall has moved (Fig 6 :23).

The multiplier is **not** the answer. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A ratio cannot choose an ordered bond.

**No figure line returns a number, a slack threshold, or a standing list of qualifying sectors.** That is a declared `gap`: unreached at this budget and of the shape-not-a-name kind, not silence (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a multiplier value. No figure line governs one.
- Not "deficits never matter" and not "deficits always crowd out". Both are verdicts in `general` mode with no ground reading.
- Not a sector list. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not a claim that MMT is right or that Ricardian equivalence is right. Both are frames; the separator is offered in effect terms so either can be checked.
- Not a claim that the framework names the qualifying atom. Naming a held economics term is a provenanced binding, not a derivation (see the correction in `05` section 5).
- Not `grounded`. Every substantive claim is candidate until an outcome settles it.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not a larger regression. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the corresponding instruments are:

- **A narrative action record with implementation status and motivation**, so the actual action is known rather than inferred from the outcome. The Ahir et al. 2026 Global Fiscal Actions dataset is the closest available example (VoxEU column, opened 2026-11-23).
- **A per-slot outcome that does not update on the forecast**, so addition and displacement can be read separately. The outcome must persist after the spending ends if a catalyst is claimed.
- **A source gate and a shadow test for any import claim**: where did the new atom come from, and which gap does it explain (Fig 8 :12-13)?
- **The pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4.** In particular, a controlled episode at full capacity with an intact channel and no import that still raises output one for one would break the support condition; a stable multiplier across regimes with a pinned anchor would break the union reading.

## 6. The honest stop

The stop is **unreached, not unreachable**. The separator is a shape whose guards can be checked; the number and the list are not available. The framework reports what is missing in effect terms and records the lineage (Fig 13 :136-138). It cannot name the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it. Indiscernibility is not identity.

The round trip from the figures to a concrete policy verdict and back is lossy (`R_T` nonzero; `01` section 9). Four things drop out: the identity of the imported atom, the source of the anchor, the belief channel, and the conversion between physical capacity and national income. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would be dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a fiscal multiplier, and it does not pick a side between Keynes and crowding out. It says that "total output" is the wrong object: the live signal is per sector and per period (Fig 1 :19). Public spending and private activity use the same atoms but can join them with different bonds, production or subtraction, so over a fixed atom set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117). Spending is a candidate for adding output only when a slot has positive residual, the new activity composes from what the economy already holds, any missing atom is genuinely imported with provenance, and the channel to an outcome that does not move with the forecast stays open; any zero factor forces inertness. Where the atoms are already held, the bond is subtraction and the spending displaces. The pooled multiplier is a filter for the budget, not a witness for a verdict (Fig 9 :60), and no figure line returns a sector list or a number, so that is a declared gap. What is offered is the separator, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open until the ground settles it.
