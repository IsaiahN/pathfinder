# Expert verification packet - the Tether on government spending and output

**What this is:** a claim in a form a fiscal-policy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal multiplier and sector list. Not `grounded`.** No per-slot measurement was taken by this author.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Does government spending raise output or displace private activity?" is a union of overlapping conditions, not one question with two answers. The verdict object is the per-slot residual, not total output. Over a fixed atom set, addition and displacement are different bonds over the same atoms: production (`⇒`) versus subtraction (`−`). A spending is a candidate for adding output only when a slot carries positive residual, the resulting activity composes from what the economy already holds, any atom the private closure lacks is genuinely imported with provenance and a shadow test, and the channel to an outcome that does not update on the forecast stays open. Any zero factor forces inertness, and the spending reallocates. The pooled multiplier is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. No number or standing sector list is returned; that is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 12
- **Statement:** the verdict must be per slot; the two outcomes are different bonds over the same atoms; a live positive residual is a necessary support condition.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
  - Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."**
  - Fig 5 :21: **"the gap has real mass somewhere"**.
  - Operators table :37: **"A produces B; B did not exist before"**; :43: **"A with B removed or absent"**.
- **What to check:** are the quoted lines present and do they say what the claim says? Is the bond assignment consistent with the operator definitions?
- **Refuter:** a controlled episode with every slot at capacity, no atom imported and the private channel intact, in which total output still rises persistently one for one. Also: a case where addition and displacement require different atom sets while the atom set is fixed. Either breaks C1.

### C2 - Economics facing, checkable by a fiscal-policy researcher against the literature
- **Statement:** the multiplier literature is an ensemble statement and cannot decide a per-slot bond. Cross-regime and cross-country variation is large enough that a pooled ratio is a budget filter, not a verdict. Spare capacity and the state of the credit channel are the two pivots.
- **Source, verbatim (figure side):** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 2 :39: **"Correlation has valence."**
- **World support (secondary, opened 2026-11-23):** Auerbach and Gorodnichenko, NBER WP 16311 / AEJ: Economic Policy 2012: *"fiscal policy being considerably more effective in recessions than in expansions"*; Ramey, JEL 2011: *"the multiplier for this type of spending is probably between 0.8 and 1.5"*; Ahir et al., VoxEU 2026: *"multipliers vary more within income groups than between them."* These are three records in one economics evidence pool, not three independent witnesses.
- **What to check:** is the characterization of the literature faithful? Do these estimates really describe ensembles, and do they fail to identify an ordered bond for a single program?
- **Refuter:** a multiplier estimate with a confidence interval narrow enough that every individual slot's effect lies on the same side of one, read against an outcome that does not move when the forecast changes. That would collapse the union into one condition and break C2.

### C3 - Verification facing, checkable against Figures 2, 8 and 10
- **Statement:** an import claim needs provenance and a shadow test; an offsetting central bank is a frame, not an anchor; agreement among readers who share an outcome series is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"**.
  - Fig 8 :12-13: **"source gate: provenance"** and **"shadow test: explains a gap"**.
  - Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**.
  - Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
  - Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**
- **What to check:** is the difference between an import and a composition inside the closure drawn correctly for a given program? Is the proposed outcome series non-updating?
- **Refuter:** a spending episode that imported no atom (same goods, same finance, same actors) yet permanently expanded the reach into private activity that had previously been idle. Or a realized outcome that moves when the authority or the researchers change the forecast. Either breaks C3.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **A narrative action record** with direction, instrument, motivation and implementation status, so the action is known rather than inferred from outcomes. The Ahir et al. 2026 dataset is the closest available example (VoxEU column, opened 2026-11-23).
2. **A per-slot outcome that does not update on the forecast**, with persistence measured after the spending ends.
3. **A source gate and shadow test for each import claim**: which atom was missing, where did it come from, and which gap does it explain.
4. **The four refuters in `01` section 4**, run before any further theory is shown.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 12 :117, Fig 5 :21 and Fig 9 :60. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: do the three cited estimates really describe ensembles rather than per-program verdicts?
3. Name one instrument that would assign a single spending's output change to addition or displacement in every slot, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. No reply is needed if engagement is not wanted.
