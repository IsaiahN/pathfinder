## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0031 the shape is the **separator**: a slot-level relation between a spending action and a private closure that decides whether the bond is production or subtraction. It constrains any answer: the answer must be per slot, must name the bond, must pass the three guards, must keep the channel open, and must not average across regimes. It does not fill the slot with a number or a sector name.

| not a shape | a shape |
|---|---|
| "the multiplier is 1.6" | "some slot must carry positive residual before an addition can be minted" |
| "spending on infrastructure always works" | "a sector's valence flips with the residual" |
| "crowding out happens at full employment" | "when the atoms are already held, the bond is subtraction" |
| "the answer is Keynes / the answer is Ricardian" | "describe the effect first, then see which frame predicts it" |

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the bootstrap terms were residual, bond, atom, import, channel, anchor, habitat.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the shape is written as idle slot, produced activity, subtracted activity, open channel, not as MPC, IS-LM slope or natural rate.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* (opened) on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it"*. Here the process narrowed the question from a two-way choice to a union of conditions; that narrowing is the result, not a prelude to a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means an outcome that persists after the spending ends and that does not move when the forecast moves.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Ahir et al. 2026 dataset is an example of a source gate: it records what the action actually was, rather than inferring discretion from the outcome.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* [carried]: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. So the figure vocabulary is a visible guess, and importing an economics term into it is a binding that must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why a multiplier reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is the exact error the question invites. GDP is a reading. The same rise in GDP is compatible with:

- spending that produced activity that would not otherwise have happened;
- spending that pulled forward activity that would have happened later;
- spending that displaced private investment one for one while the public component rose;
- a statistical revision that changed the reading without changing the economy.

The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator (a narrative action record, a per-slot outcome, an exogenous shock), not a larger multiplier regression. Fig 2 :37-38: an instrument *"can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So a multiplier estimated from the same outcome series it claims to explain is not a check.

## 5. Correction to my own earlier reading

**I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in section 1 and in `01` F7.** The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in the library. In this question, the candidate bindings (idle capacity, hoarded saving, an open credit channel, a demand floor) are **not missing primitives**. They are already-held economics terms bound onto the framework's slots. Naming one is **provenance**, not an invented primitive, and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :14; Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

So the correct position is:

- It is **not forbidden** to say which slot is live in a particular case, provided the binding is sourced and the source is recorded.
- It **is forbidden** to promote that binding into a universal standing list, because Fig 11 :51 says *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- It **is forbidden** to call the binding an import that moves the wall when it adds no atom.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not retracted**. The multiplier reading still cannot name its source without a discriminator. What was wrong was treating every act of naming as a forbidden invention. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A panel of famous economists selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried and lets an outcome settle it.

**Two frames, not one.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* [carried] and Fig 8 :45 (*"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"*) support the demand for independent readers: one reader from fiscal econometrics, one from a different closure (economic history or public finance administration), each ranging against the same outcome and not against each other. Their agreement is evidence only if they did not share the evidence pool. In this simulated run, all seats share one pool, so agreement here is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and every other file in this folder are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the separator and cannot settle the question. The correct label on every substantive claim is `general` or `specified`, never `grounded`.

## 8. Q&A answered here

**Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the qualifying condition?**
No. It governs a missing primitive. Naming a held binding is provenance. Promoting it to a standing list is forbidden for a different reason (Fig 11 :51).

**Q: What would make the shape a name?**
A source-discriminating instrument that assigns a given spending's output change to addition or displacement in every slot, read against an outcome that does not update on the forecast. Then the slot can be named for that case and the gap narrows.

**Q: Is the multiplier a shape?**
No. It is a reading in one currency. Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* The shape is the guard structure and the bond; the multiplier is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
