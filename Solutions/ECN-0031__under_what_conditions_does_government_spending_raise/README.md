## The residual in effect terms

A fiscal authority takes an action (spend, finance, direct). The observation is realized output per slot (sector, region, household class, capacity type). The residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s`, never a single global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Two effects are in question:

1. **Addition:** the spending composes over atoms the private closure did not hold or could not reach, so output rises. Bond reading: production (`⇒`, *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*, Operators table :37).
2. **Displacement:** the spending draws on the same scarce atoms (savings, real resources, customers) that private activity would have used. Bond reading: subtraction (`−`, *"A with B removed or absent"*, Operators table :43).

An answer would look like a **separator**, a set of conditions that decides, for a given spending action in a given slot, which bond holds. That is the sought-for shape, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"), so it can accrue a residual and be refused. The package does not return the separator as a universal list. Fig 11 :51 says *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* It returns the **conditions under which a separator can be built**, and a declared gap where no figure line governs.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numerical fiscal multiplier presented as the answer. A multiplier is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of economists selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **The aggregate hides the live signal.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A pooled multiplier cannot decide a particular spending's verdict.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in channels.
