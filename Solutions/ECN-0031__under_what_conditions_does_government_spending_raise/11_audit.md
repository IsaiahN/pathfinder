## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the question and the residual in effect terms, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The discrepancy between predicted and realized output when the fiscal authority acts. Arity: two operands in one accounting, public spending and private activity. Symmetry: ordered, because the relation is production (`⇒`) or subtraction (`−`); a symmetric reading would be a correlation, and Fig 2 :39 *"Correlation has valence."* Scale: per slot and period versus an aggregate over both. I therefore refuse "total output" as the verdict object before choosing any figure line.
- **Domain 1, fiscal authority.** Actors: treasury, legislature. Currency: budget authority, bonds. Anchor: realized receipts and output, which do not update on the authority's forecast. Without a nameable anchor this would not yet be a domain; it has one.
- **Domain 2, private production.** Actors: firms, households, savers. Currency: private outlay, saving, investment. Anchor: realized sales and cash flow.
- **Domain 3, real-resource habitat.** Actors: labour, capital, materials, time. Currency: capacity and utilisation. Anchor: physical feasibility, which no announcement changes.
- **Domain 4, monetary channel.** Actors: central bank, banks, bond market. Currency: liquidity and interest rate. Anchor: observed rate and credit creation, provided no frame can declare them. The qualifying clause matters: an authority that sets the rate to hit a target is a frame, not an anchor.
- **Domain 5, verification.** Actors: statistical agencies, researchers. Currency: per-slot estimates, shock series. Anchor: an exogenous or narrative action record and an outcome that does not move with the forecast.
- **Union, not partition.** The two named outcomes overlap in a single episode across slots; the intersection is a compound with its own reading. A lever that raises output in a slack slot can displace investment in a tight one. This matches the primary's union reading.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the live slot; have a vocabulary of bonds; state the objective in a declared currency; direct the spend; carry only settled terms. I expect the break at the objective link, because the literature has ratios but no term for the bond.

My census matches the primary's five domains, its union reading and its dependency order. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 :55 and :57, I derived it from Fig 12 :40 (*"same two atoms, three substances"*) and the orderedness of the operators. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. Low overlap in route, but one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak internal consistency, not corroboration.
