## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the divergence between a regional water and climate prediction with no agricultural actors and observation. Arity: three interventions on two receivers plus couplings. Symmetry: the interventions are not interchangeable; water quantity, reactive nutrient mass and a transport relation are different kinds of thing, so their spellings are not related by a symmetric bond. Scale: field to coast, season to decade, with the residual often two or three steps downstream of the application. Union rather than partition: the same land is irrigated, fertilized and runoff-generating, so a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, managed field.** Actors: irrigation water, N and P fertilizer, crop, soil microbes, water table. Currency: water flux, nutrient mass, yield, N2O flux. Anchor: field instruments. Type: maintained habitat with a seat.
- **Domain 2, receiving basin.** Actors: runoff, streams, aquifers, algae, decomposers, oxygen, sediments, estuary. Currency: load, oxygen, flow. Anchor: gauges, monitoring, whole-lake manipulations, hypoxia surveys. Type: contact cascade and cycle.
- **Domain 3, regional atmosphere.** Actors: ET, albedo, N2O, ammonia, aerosol. Currency: heat, moisture, radiative forcing. Anchor: towers, remote sensing, atmospheric records. Type: separate slots with opposite signs.
- **Domain 4, coupled region.** Actors: the cross-scale fluxes. Currency: coupled state. Anchor: integrated observation. Type: union compound with its own loop.

Order by dependency: source term before load, load before coastal effect, first-order molecules before the compound, and describe the residual before choosing a frame. A link with no instrument is unmeasured, not zero.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54-58 (union compound), Fig 1 :19-22 (per slot), Fig 11 :52-53 (cascade), Fig 4 :30 (round trip).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` and `07`: split the question, keep slots separate, enumerate contacts, measure cross-scale gaps, refuse magnitudes.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete regional assessment naming irrigation, nitrogen and phosphorus, runoff, ET, N2O, hypoxia and salinity.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **mechanism inventory** (nitrification, denitrification, stratification, internal sediment loading, ET partitioning) is supplied by world sources, not by the shape;
- the **ground selection** (which gauge, tower or manipulation settles which slot) is an import with a source gate, not a figure line;
- the **magnitude** of every slot, which the shape explicitly refuses to name.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the requirement to split, keep slots separate, enumerate outward and measure the cross-scale gap. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
