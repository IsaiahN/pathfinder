# The Tether's answer on irrigation, fertilizer, runoff, water and climate

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A regional water and climate prediction made without agricultural actors diverges from observation in several distinct slots. The question asks for *"the effect"*, but the effect is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58), not one quantity. The answer below keeps the four domains separate and never averages their verdicts.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before you search.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Field source, basin receiver, atmospheric receiver and their coupling are different loops. A single lever clears only the region where one condition binds.
2. **The residual is per slot, never an average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Irrigation evapotranspiration cools the surface locally; fertilizer-derived N2O warms the global atmosphere; they are different currencies and opposite signs. Averaging them discards the only signal that says where to act.
3. **Enumerate contacts out to the cascade horizon.** Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* For this question: application, soil loss, transport, load, bloom, decomposition, oxygen depletion, fish and water users. The effect that matters is usually not at the point of application.
4. **Valence follows the residual.** Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Nitrogen serves yield and harms oxygen. Irrigation serves crop water and can harm aquifer level and salinity. No standing good/bad list survives a change of target.
5. **Management is permanent work with asymmetric power.** Fig 11 :61-62: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Nutrients and water can be added; the load that has entered the basin or the N2O already emitted cannot be subtracted by a later act of management.
6. **The cross-scale gaps are measurements, not assumptions.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Field efficiency does not map unchanged to basin water saving where return flows exist.

## 3. One loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

### Loop D1: the managed field

- **Actors / currency / anchor:** water, N and P fertilizer, crop, soil microbes, water table; water flux, nutrient mass, yield, N2O flux; lysimeters, flux chambers, soil probes, yield cuts.
- **Residual:** applied nutrient and water not accounted for by crop uptake, plus the N2O that escapes the soil. Nitrification and denitrification are the mechanisms; high solubility and low anion retention let nitrate leave the root zone.
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** the field is a maintained habitat, presence of a seat with asymmetric power. A field-level efficiency gain is not a basin-level saving.
- **Refuter:** an intervention that genuinely subtracts an actor from the habitat, or a field saving that maps to the basin with zero gap where return flows exist.

### Loop D2: the receiving basin

- **Actors / currency / anchor:** runoff, streams, aquifers, algae, decomposers, dissolved oxygen, sediments, estuary; water quantity and quality, nutrient load, oxygen, flow; stream gauges, water-quality monitoring, whole-lake experiments, hypoxia surveys.
- **Residual:** oxygen and water-quality slots diverge from the prediction that ignored upstream nutrient loading. The causal chain is load, bloom, sinking, decomposition, oxygen depletion. NOAA calls nutrient pollution *"the primary cause of those zones created by humans"*; EPA links the recurring Gulf hypoxia to a basin draining 31 states.
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** contact cascades at least three steps out; nutrient load and dissolved oxygen are separate slots; internal nutrient cycling makes this a cycle rather than a one-pass chain.
- **Refuter:** measured second and third-order contacts contributing nothing while first-order contacts explain the residual.

### Loop D3: the regional atmosphere

- **Actors / currency / anchor:** irrigated land, evapotranspiration, albedo, soil N2O, ammonia, secondary aerosol; heat flux, moisture flux, radiative forcing; flux towers, remote sensing, atmospheric monitoring.
- **Residual:** two slots with opposite signs. Irrigation increases evapotranspiration and can lower daytime surface temperature locally; nitrogen fertilizer drives N2O, a long-lived greenhouse gas (about 40% of human-caused emissions from agriculture per the fetched source), and ammonia contributes to aerosols.
- **Verdict (`triangulated` for the two-slot structure, `single-source` for magnitudes):** the two slots must not be averaged; Fig 13 :76 gives the structural reason, *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*
- **Refuter:** one mechanism and one evidence pool driving both slots with residuals that trend together, or a source-discriminating instrument showing the apparent cooling is an artifact of the reading.

### Loop D4: the coupled region

- **Actors / currency / anchor:** the cross-scale fluxes between D1, D2 and D3; coupled water, energy and nutrient state; integrated observation read together.
- **Residual:** the question as asked. It is a union compound and must be unbundled before the loops above are composed.
- **Verdict (`general` shape, `specified` route; numeric coupling `gap` at figure level):** the framework returns the shape and the reference, not the magnitudes. *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- **Refuter:** a figure line that yields a numeric regional magnitude without a world import, or one lever clearing every slot.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number for regional cooling, nutrient load, N2O flux, hypoxia area or salinity. Those are `gap` at figure level and are owed to instruments, not to the framework.
- Not that nitrogen, irrigation or runoff is intrinsically harmful. Valence is residual-relative.
- Not that the field, basin and atmospheric results can be averaged into one agricultural effect.
- Not that a calibrated regional model is the ground. A ground that updates on the claim has become a frame (Fig 2 :47).
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that these four loops are the only loops. The habitat is enumerated, never composed, and the enumeration stops when the cascade stops mattering for this residual.
- Not a claim that the four Wikipedia and two government pages are six independent witnesses. They are two pools.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Source attribution:** does a measured local temperature change partition into evapotranspiration, albedo and radiative forcing? Instrument: paired flux towers over irrigated and non-irrigated comparators, with radiation budget and stable-isotope or compositional attribution where possible.
- **Load attribution:** does the downstream oxygen residual track the nutrient load from the field, after controlling for weather and stratification? Instrument: paired gauges, load monitoring and whole-basin or whole-lake manipulation where feasible.
- **Cascade horizon:** at what step does the effect stop mattering? Instrument: the same enumeration repeated with second and third-order contacts measured, then the refuter in D2 fired or not.
- **Cross-scale round trip:** does a field water saving survive the trip down to the basin with return-flow users? Instrument: paired field and basin water balances, with R_T reported as the gap rather than set to zero.
- **Standing refuters:** one lever clearing all slots; zero cascade contribution; zero round-trip gap; a ground that moves when frames push on it.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** A *"human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* is a visible choice, not a neutral one; declaring it makes the choice visible (They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature). The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; this is the numeric part's honest status.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how irrigation, fertilizer and runoff change a region. It gives the shape any defensible answer must have. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be split into field, basin and atmosphere before it is searched. The residual is indexed by slot and must never be averaged: irrigation cooling and N2O warming are different currencies with opposite signs. Actors are read for what is in contact with the residual out to the third step, because the effect that matters is usually the bloom and the oxygen loss, not the application. Valence follows the target: nitrogen helps yield and harms oxygen, and management can introduce actors but cannot subtract what has already left the field. Every field-to-basin handoff carries a round-trip gap that must be measured. The numeric remainder is recorded as a gap, not filled with a guess, and the instruments that would close it are named. What is offered is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and a request to report a failure rather than an endorsement.
