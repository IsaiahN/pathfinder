## 5. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; a list selected for people likely to agree manufactures exactly that pool.
- **Fig 9 :60:** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue tier, journal impact and audience size are budget filters, never evidence that the shape is right.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three sub-claims and the four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) only then the method.

## 6. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, validate the output.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what would fail. Record the verdict before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If they are curious, offer the three figure links plus the earth-system context pages. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter, names their own ground and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The shape claim here has four refuters written in advance; if one fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and nothing is sent until the audit is redone.
