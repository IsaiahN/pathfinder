The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"a shape, not a name"*; Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* For this question that means the deliverable is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the magnitudes are known.

## 1. A shape constrains; it does not fill

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For EAR-0065 the shape is: split the question into field, basin and atmosphere; keep per-slot residuals separate; enumerate contacts out to the cascade horizon; measure the cross-scale gaps; refuse to name a numeric net effect. It does not include the number of degrees of cooling or the tonnes of nitrogen. Those are names, and a name here would be a magnitude the framework does not hold.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: describe the effect, then find the frame

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. The answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

Applied here: describe the residual as *"stream oxygen declines after nitrogen application in a basin with these soil and drainage conditions"* before choosing which discipline's instrument to import. If the discipline is named first, every discipline will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name or a magnitude

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here: a lysimeter, a flux chamber, a stream gauge, a whole-lake manipulation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 6 :12 *"source gate: provenance"*; Fig 6 :13 *"shadow test: explains a gap"*).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible choice, not nature's own list; importing a physical magnitude into it must be recorded as an import, not presented as a derivation. The same essay adds: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* and notes that a reasoner *"can only be surprised along axes its primitives can already represent."* An instrument that cannot distinguish irrigation cooling from N2O warming is an inadequate instrument, not proof that the effects cancel.

## 4. Why the metamerism point matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The green reading does not determine whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB or CMYK. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

The earth-system analogue is direct. A regional mean temperature is a projection. It can be produced by irrigation evapotranspiration, by a change in albedo, by N2O forcing, by aerosol effects, by circulation, or by several at once. Naming the source from the mean reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (flux partitioning, isotopic or compositional attribution), not a verbal choice of culprit. This is the ground for finding F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (first pass)

I first wrote that the figures forbid naming any source or magnitude for this question. That overstated the rule. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*: a primitive not held in the library. Irrigation water, reactive nitrogen and transport are not missing primitives in this frame; they are holdable atoms from the earth-system frame. Naming them is a **binding**, and what is forbidden is claiming the binding is a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall.

The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted. A mean temperature reading still does not determine its source without a discriminating instrument. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I initially treated the four Wikipedia and two government pages as six independent corroborations. They are two pools. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that *"You may all agree and be wrong"*; Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The ledger now declares overlap per row.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. Journal scope, impact factor and audience size are budget filters (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; the outputs must be seen as *"simulacra."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the earth-system claim and cannot settle it. *How Intelligence Actually Works* closes the loop: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. What survives for this question

- A shape (split, per-slot, enumerate, measure the gap, no net magnitude).
- A refuter written before the run.
- An explicit `gap` for the empirical remainder, with the instruments that would close it.
- No claim that the framework's author, the essays, or the figure set is a ground for whether a given region actually cooled.
