## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the effects of irrigation, fertilizer use and agricultural runoff on regional water and climate systems. Option A: answer as one agricultural effect with a net sign and a single magnitude. Option B: unbundle the residual into field, basin, atmosphere and their coupling, keep the per-slot residuals separate, enumerate the cascade, measure the cross-scale gaps, and record the numeric part as a gap owed to instruments. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A regional water and climate prediction made without agricultural actors returns errors in stream nutrient loads, dissolved oxygen, groundwater nitrate, aquifer level and salinity, evapotranspiration and near-surface temperature, and atmospheric N2O and ammonia. Arity: three interventions acting on two receivers plus couplings, a union compound rather than one hard question. Symmetry: irrigation changes water quantity and surface energy flux, fertilizer adds reactive mass, runoff is the transport relation; they are distinct atoms under distinct bonds, not interchangeable spellings. Scale: field to coast, season to decade, with the residual often three steps from the application.
- **Domain 1, managed field.** Actors: irrigation water, N and P fertilizer, crop, soil microbes, water table. Currency: water flux, nutrient mass, yield, N2O flux. Anchor: lysimeters, flux chambers, soil probes, yield cuts. Question type: maintained habitat, a seat acts.
- **Domain 2, receiving basin.** Actors: runoff, streams, aquifers, algae, decomposers, dissolved oxygen, sediments, estuary. Currency: water quantity and quality, nutrient load, oxygen, flow. Anchor: stream gauges, water-quality monitoring, whole-lake experiments, hypoxia surveys. Question type: mostly unmaintained contact, cascade matters, internal cycling makes it a cycle.
- **Domain 3, regional atmosphere.** Actors: irrigated land, evapotranspiration, albedo, soil N2O, ammonia, secondary aerosol. Currency: heat flux, moisture flux, radiative forcing. Anchor: flux towers, remote sensing, atmospheric monitoring. Question type: partial overlap with D1 and D2, separate slots.
- **Domain 4, coupled region.** Actors: the cross-scale fluxes between D1, D2 and D3. Currency: coupled water, energy and nutrient state. Anchor: integrated observation read together. Question type: union, not partition, its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Field source before basin load; basin load before coastal effect; first-order molecules before the coupled region; describe the residual before picking a frame. A link with no instrument is unmeasured, not zero.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question as asked does not. The residual is a union compound that must be unbundled into field, basin, atmosphere and coupling. The residual is per slot and must never be averaged, because the irrigation moisture/heat slot and the fertilizer radiative slot have opposite signs and different currencies. Contacts must be enumerated to the cascade horizon, where the load, bloom and oxygen loss are the effect that matters. Valence is relative to the target residual, and management can introduce but not subtract. Cross-scale gaps are measurements, not assumptions. The numeric magnitudes are `gap` at figure level, owed to instruments with a source gate and a shadow test. This is a `general` shape with `specified` per-domain predictions, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework summary before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters. A second cost is making a management decision from an averaged net effect, which hides the slot that actually needs action.
- **undoable:** yes. The partition reading, the good/bad actor list and any implied net magnitude are all removable without touching the shape. The corrections are already recorded in `01` section 6 and `05` section 5. No atom has to be withdrawn, and no source has to be silently deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. One lever clearing all slots across seasons would refute the union compound; measured second and third-order contacts contributing nothing would refute the cascade finding; a zero field-to-basin gap with return-flow users would refute the round-trip claim; a source-discriminating instrument partitioning the temperature response would either name the climate slot or show the averaging was legitimate. Prestige, venue rank and pooled agreement are not such instruments.
