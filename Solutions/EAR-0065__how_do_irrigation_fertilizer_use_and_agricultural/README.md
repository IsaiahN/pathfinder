## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not become an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Showing the interior before asking for a verdict spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
