## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit, local figures README: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have
to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
Fig 13 (local extract): *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It
cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (essay, opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and
deepen your vocabulary."

For ECO-0077 the shape is:

> The sign of a biodiversity change for disease transmission is not carried by the change itself.
> It is carried by a relation between the change and a named residual, read at the changed contact
> link, in a habitat enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering.

That is a constraint on any acceptable answer. It is not a list of species or interventions and it
does not name which pathogen will move in which direction here.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* (essay): bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, "connect and correlate combinations
  of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms
  that you are unfamiliar with." Here the known words were "contact", "habitat", "residual" and
  "valence"; the newly found terms were "reservoir competence", "density-dependent transmission",
  "mixing network" and "component effect".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* The residual was described as a sign that fails to resolve, not as a framework
  term, before any discipline was chosen (Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second."*).
- Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and
  outward until the cascade stops mattering."* This is the search procedure the shape prescribes.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the
  answer better." Here the stop is a shape plus a refuter, not a universal list.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 (local): *"the ground settles it. Verified only
   by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is measured infection in a
   defined host population after a defined community change. It is not a meta-analysis, a review
   verdict or a policy consensus.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. The four dilution conditions from the Cary page and the contact
   mechanisms from Luis et al. are such imports, recorded with provenance in
   `10_source_ledger.csv`.

So a claim of the form "adding predator X reduces disease Y in place Z" is licensed only by a
measurement in that system, or by an import with provenance and a shadow test. It is not licensed by
the six-term set, which returns a shape.

## 4. Why a universal list is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* is listed in the exemplar's canonical mapping but was not
re-opened this session, so its metamerism quote is not used as authority here. The figure-level
version of the same warning is enough:

- Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual; no standing list of good actors survives a
  change of residual.
- Fig 12 :46-47: a bond carries information the operands do not, so a recipe that names only its
  ingredients has recorded half of itself. A species list is the ingredients; the interaction
  structure is the bond.
- Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single
  "biodiversity" index is that average.

A universal "biodiversity reduces disease" list commits all three at once: it treats valence as
ingredient, drops the bond and averages the slots.

## 5. Prestige is not action (consequence for outreach)

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to
validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be
tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be
blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well-known ecologists to agree that biodiversity protects health is the
named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and
let measured infection settle it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being
wrong."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found
in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real
architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator.
The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against
the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot
settle which biodiversity changes increase transmission anywhere. That is the honest stop.

## 7. Correction to my own framing (kept visible)

I initially wrote the shape as "biodiversity change has no sign." That was too strong. Fig 11 :49
says valence is relative to the residual, not that valence is absent. A change can have a sign once
the residual and the contact link are named. Earlier wording is preserved here as evidence of the
correction rather than silently erased. The narrower, surviving form is the one in section 1.
