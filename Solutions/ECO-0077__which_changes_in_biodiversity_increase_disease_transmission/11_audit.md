## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity,
symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by
dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of a community change to determine a
  transmission sign. Arity: change, pathogen and target host, with context as a fourth operand, so a
  union compound rather than one hard question (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: not a symmetric join, because
  the operands cannot be swapped without changing the term; the same actor carries opposite valence
  for different residuals. Scale: local, landscape and regional, with a decisive link sometimes three
  steps out (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Domain 1, the pathogen's habitat.** Actors: reservoirs, vectors, non-competent hosts, predators,
  competitors, people. Currency: contact rate, transmissibility and host density, observed as
  infection prevalence. Anchor: an instrument reading infection in the target host. Question type:
  union of component mechanisms, enumerated rather than composed.
- **Domain 2, the management seat.** Actors: managers and agencies. Currency: introductions,
  substitutions, vaccination, culling. Anchor: disease risk after intervention, the same ground as D1.
  Question type: partition of levers by the contact mechanism each acts on.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: ecologists, meta-analysts, modellers, methodologists.
  Currency: effect sizes, mechanism paths, refuters. Anchor: experiment or measurement, never
  agreement. Question type: union of component hypotheses; a net null is not a mechanism null.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); enumerate direct
contact before assigning valence (Fig 11 :43-44); follow the cascade before decomposing the changed
link; measure the net effect before stating management (Fig 3 :20-21).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from
Fig 1 slot indexing and Fig 11 valence, I derived from Fig 9 union/partition and Fig 3 dependency
order. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes inside the framework,
so the agreement is weakly non-circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured
rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-69, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 9 :26,
   :32 and :60.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: valence is relative to the
   residual; enumerate the habitat and cascade; read sign at the changed link; add, do not subtract.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claims: four dilution conditions from the Cary page;
   component mechanisms from Luis et al.; contact-network structure from Keeling and Eames and Silk
   et al.; context-dependent policy from Hopkins et al.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **asymmetry of management** (introduction available, exclusion not) is compressed away if the
  rule is read as "design a better community";
- the **cascade depth** (third-order effects, Fig 11 :52-53) is lost if "enumerate contacts" is read
  as "list direct contacts";
- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19) is lost if the rule is read as a property of a diversity
  index rather than of a pathogen x host x place slot.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: valence is relative to the residual and the habitat must be
enumerated from the world. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically,
because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped
constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set
R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
