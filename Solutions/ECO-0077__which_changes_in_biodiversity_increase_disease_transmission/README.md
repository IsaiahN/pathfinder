## The question restated as an effect

We are asked to decide what follows, for the transmission of a named pathogen to a named host, from
a change in the living community. Option A: "biodiversity change" carries its own sign, so a list of
transmission-increasing and transmission-reducing changes can be stated context free. Option B: the
sign is not carried by the aggregate; it is carried by which contacts changed, for which residual,
at which scale. A leads to a portable rule but one that breaks on known reversals. B leads to a
shape that must be filled per system by measurement. The answer bound here is the ordinary term
"the valence of a biodiversity change is relative to the residual, not a property of the change",
so it can accrue a residual like anything else (Formula step "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that biodiversity protects health.
- No bulk-send plan, no purchased list, no harvested addresses.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for a preferred conclusion manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A larger audience changes the vocabulary, not the ground.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public
opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground
reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, read 2026-11-23): "LLMs can quickly
simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the
truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula
yourself." Nothing here can settle which changes in biodiversity increase transmission in any
particular place. It can say what an answer must look like and what would refute it. That is the
limit it stops at.
