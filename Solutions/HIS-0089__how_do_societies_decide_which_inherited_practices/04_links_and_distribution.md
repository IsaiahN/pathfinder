## Wider literature that bears on the shape claim

1. **Fidelity is not method.** Whiten et al. 2009 report *"a relative conservatism and conformity in chimpanzees' learning, contrasting with cumulative cultural learning in young children."* They also describe children copying causally irrelevant actions at over 90 percent in some tasks despite explicit instructions not to, and explain it as *"copy-all, refine/correct-later."* This is a ground-level instance of Fig 4's distinction between a head start and a recording: high transmission fidelity can coexist with blind replay. It corroborates F5, but it is a different field, so the overlap with the figures is low.

2. **The ground still decides.** van Schaik & Burkart 2011: *"Social learning is not necessarily adaptive."* Their account makes the adaptiveness of a transmitted practice contingent on the environment and on the learning pathway (vertical and oblique transmission of demanding skills is most likely adaptive). This corroborates F1 and F6: retention is not proof of fit, and maintenance is work.

3. **Nobody decides in the unmaintained case.** Krützen et al. 2005 show a foraging tradition in dolphins maintained by vertical transmission and exclude genetic and ecological explanations. There is no seat in the account; the practice persists because it is copied within a matriline and works in deep-water channels. This is the closest real-world instance of Fig 11's unmaintained mode, corroborating F1.

4. **Internal reform has a mechanism.** Richerson, Boyd & Henrich 2010 describe culture as *"a system of descent with modification"* and name *"decision-making forces"* by which people *"pick and choose from among the different cultural variants they observe."* They also name drift, mutation and migration as non-selective sources of change. This corroborates F4 (modification has more than one mechanism) and supplies the drift term that F1's refuter asks for.

5. **Agreement has a bias problem.** The Dual inheritance theory reference page lists prestige bias, success bias, and conformist bias. Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement that shares an evidence pool; these biases are the mechanism by which a pool produces agreement without adding evidence. Corroborates F2. Tertiary source, so weight is low.

No world source was read that conflicts with the figure verdict. If one is found, the rule is to return to the domain census, not to flip the verdict (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).
