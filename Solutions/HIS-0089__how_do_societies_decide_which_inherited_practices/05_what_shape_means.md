## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of a decision rule that can be stated before a particular society has been examined. It constrains: it says the rule must be indexed per practice, must name a domain and an anchor, and must keep the three effects separate. It does not fill: it does not say which practice a given society retained, or why in that year.

`SPACE(???)TIME` was the exemplar's shape. The corresponding shape here is:

> **PRACTICE(slot) x DOMAIN(anchor) -> {retain, modify, reject}**, with the slot-indexed residual and the pre-registered refuter attached.

## 2. How the shape was obtained, including a correction to my own start

I began by trying to find a single decision rule. *The Library of Babel* names that error: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Re-census then produced three domains, and the bundle was unbundled into them.

The vocabulary for the shape came from proximity, as the essay describes: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The terms that narrowed this question were **slot** (Fig 1), **ground** (Fig 2), **head start vs recording** (Fig 4), **refusal** (Fig 5/6), **seat** (Fig 10/11), **convention** (Fig 2/10), and **w** (Symbols table).

**Correction to my own first pass.** I initially treated Fig 2 :40 (*"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*) as applying to all inherited practices. It does not. Fig 2 :41 separates conventions, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the reason in plain terms: *"Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* The finding is now scoped to verdicts, and the convention domain has its own rule. The correction is kept visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things license naming a particular mechanism or practice, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The test that environment applies is what we call fitness."* A practice's fate in D1 is settled this way, with no seat.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). From *Arrival of the Fittest*: an entry tagged *an instance* is *"a case the framework describes, arrived at independently by someone else."* The world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` are instances of this kind, not derivations.

The essays add a third caution specific to this question. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Observing that a practice was retained does not tell you which mechanism retained it: selection, reweighting, deliberate maintenance, and pure convention can all produce the same observable persistence. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. The essays that bear most directly on societies and transmission

Four essays are unusually close to this question, and they are the ones that turned the abstract shape into a usable one.

### 4.1 *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (the transmission channel)

- *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* This is the essay-level statement of Fig 4's recording/head-start distinction. A transmitted practice that depends on pretext degrades into a recording.
- *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself."* Exact corroboration of Fig 4 :22.
- *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* This is Fig 8 :29's low-overlap requirement stated for a society: transmission itself consumes the independence that would make variation useful.
- *"Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice."* This is Fig 2 :34 applied to the history of knowledge, and it is the mechanism behind F2.
- On Bartlett: *"each participant's report of the story mirrored his or her own culture."* A ground-level case of retention-by-reconstruction, where what survives is what fits the receiver's prior. This is a real case of modification by reweighting (F4) running without a seat.
- *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* The clearest statement of the D3/D1-D2 boundary in the whole corpus.

### 4.2 *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (the decision rule, in plain terms)

- *"Each agent then has to decide how far to trust its own experience and how far to trust the crowd."* This is the Symbols table's `w`, and it is the answer to "modify" in the least technical terms available.
- *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :39. A society's ability to decide is a property of the society and its habitat, not of its institutions alone.
- *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* F8 and F2.
- *"The group shares methods, never recordings."* Fig 4 :22.
- *"If every agent's 'w' drifts to the average, the spread is gone and the gradients are spent."* This is the failure mode of a conformist tradition: it can still change, but it has spent the variation needed to select.
- *"Things fade, so they can be tried again... The record is never deleted."* This is the framework's answer to over-rigid rejection, stated as a design rule. It is why the refusal must have decay, and why "reject" cannot mean erase.
- *"A carefully chosen room is still a chosen room."* Fig 10's selection test, relevant whenever a reform committee is hand-picked.

### 4.3 *How Intelligence Actually Works* (the ground)

- *"intelligence is the emergent result of extensive mapping of a complex system against unknown and unforgiving environments."* Retention is not a property of a practice; it is a property of a lineage's contact.
- *"It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it."* This is the head start, and it explains why a practice can be retained without the current generation understanding it.
- *"the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted."* The question "which practices should this society keep?" has no answer until a specific ground is named.
- *"Variation is a natural hedge."* F8.
- On group failure: *"if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift."* The reason a society's deliberations cannot substitute for a ground.

### 4.4 *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (prestige and the working boundary)

- *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* This is Fig 11's unmaintained mode, and it is the cleanest one-sentence statement of F1.
- *"The same code runs when there is a Ground Maintainer. It runs in cultivation, when humans select which plants to breed. It runs in habitat formation, when beavers build dams and change the selection pressures. It runs in your own mind, when you decide which ideas to keep."* This is the reason one shape applies to all three domains: the loop does not change, only the seat does.
- *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A society's most prestigious inherited practice is not thereby its best grounded one.
- *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* The reason this package must carry a refuter and cannot certify itself.

## 5. Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanism?

No, and this corrects my own initial reading. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the relevant mechanisms (w, refusal, head start, convention, seat) are already in the figure set. Naming one as the mechanism in a given case is a **binding** from the world frame onto a held term, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is (a) claiming the framework derived which specific practice survived, and (b) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). From *They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** A list of prestigious societies whose traditions agree with the shape claim would be a convention, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *Arrival of the Fittest* warns that the demo's plausibility *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
