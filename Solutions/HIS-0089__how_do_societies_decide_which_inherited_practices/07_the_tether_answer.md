# The Tether's answer: how societies decide which inherited practices to retain, modify, or reject

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a set of refuters, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading of any society.

## 1. The residual it answers

A society holds Γ, a library of inherited practices. For each practice there is a residual R between what the inherited repertoire predicts and what the ground returns. The question asks what produces three effects: retain, modify, reject. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The answer is a vector over practices, not a verdict over a society.

## 2. The shape: three domains, one loop, three verdicts kept separate

**D1, unmaintained transmission. Nobody decides.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 11 :56: *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Here "retain" means persistence and "reject" means extinction. There is no seat, no vote, and no verdict. The word "decide" does no work. Drift is an unmodelled third possibility at this level; the refuter names it.

**D2, maintained reform. Reform is asymmetric permanent work.** A seat can act, but Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* You can add a practice; you cannot subtract the habitat. A ban adds a refusal and leaves the conditions that produced the practice intact, so reform is permanent maintenance, not a solved state. Modification itself splits into three: reweighting w between inherited prior and private experience (Symbols table), refitting a mis-attached practice, or minting a new term when the mechanism is broken (Fig 5). These have different costs and must not be averaged.

**D3, convention. Agreement is the product.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* For greetings, dress, and etiquette, there is no external ground and the decision *is* agreement; the framework's anti-agreement rule does not apply. The boundary is sharp: Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Once a convention allocates resources or determines truth, it owes an answer to a ground.

**The cross-cutting rules.** What is transmitted is a head start, not a recording: Fig 4 :11 *"a head start, never a recording."* Handed-down agreement adds no evidence: Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Rejection is a refusal added to the record: Fig 6 :42 *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* and Fig 5 :55 *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* A vocabulary without negation can only accumulate. Selection among practices needs low-overlap variation (Fig 8 :29, :32); variation inside one frame is just agreement (Fig 7 :35).

The question is a **union compound**, not a single question (Fig 9 :54): a real practice is usually in two or three domains at once, so unbundle before asking which lever applies.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single decision procedure. There is no rule that covers all three domains without averaging them.
- Not a claim that handed-down practices are worthless. They are head starts when transmitted as methods (Fig 4 :22).
- Not a claim that a ban erases a practice. The record never deletes; what falls is the reach (Fig 5 :55).
- Not a claim that agreement is disqualified in conventions. Fig 2 :41 is the exception, and it is constitutive.
- Not a derivation of which practice a particular society kept. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not `grounded`. No society was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it

A ground reading, domain by domain.

- **D1:** a transmission-chain experiment that separates selection from drift and from reweighting of w. The Whiten et al. diffusion designs are the right instrument type.
- **D2:** a reform case where the old practice's standing was refused before the replacement was minted, versus one where it was not. Measure whether the reach built on the old term fell when the refusal was added. First-hand outcomes, not committee agreement, are the votes.
- **D3:** a convention observed at the moment it starts deciding an outcome. If it remains legitimate with no ground contact, Fig 10 :47 fails.
- **Cross-cutting:** an instrument that discriminates a head start from a recording at the point of transmission. Overimitation data are a candidate ground, not a derivation.
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3: six numbered conditions. Any one fires a retraction of the corresponding branch.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework returns a shape: the residual must be per slot, the domain must be named, the anchor must be nameable, and the three effects must stay separate. What it cannot return is the name of the practice. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself."* The record stops where the instruments stop, and the lineage is kept so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give one rule for deciding which inherited practices to keep. It splits the question into three. Where nobody maintains anything, nobody decides: selection ran, and what survives is what the ground kept. Where a seat exists, reform is asymmetric permanent work: you can introduce a practice but cannot subtract the habitat, and rejecting one means adding a refusal rather than erasing it. Where a practice is a pure convention, agreement is the product, not a corruption, until the convention starts deciding an outcome and owes an answer to a ground. Across all three, what is transmitted is a head start or a recording, handed-down agreement is not evidence, and selection needs variants that do not draw on one evidence pool. The framework returns this shape and its refuters, not the name of any practice. The center stays open until a ground settles it.
