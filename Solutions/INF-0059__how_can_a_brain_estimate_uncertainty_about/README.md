## The question restated as an effect

"What does it mean to estimate uncertainty about your own belief or decision?" is answered here in effect terms, before any vocabulary of confidence is chosen. A brain holds a belief b (what it takes to be the case before it acts; Symbols table :20, *"What the system takes to be the case before it acts"*, and :21, *"Not the observation"*). It acts a, the world returns o′, and the per-slot residual is R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| (Formula step 1; Fig 1 :11, *"residual R indexed by object slot"*).

The residual R is *"the aim, not only the error signal"* (Symbols table :39). So "uncertainty about a belief or decision" is not a feeling attached afterwards to a finished thought. In effect it is a **second-order read of the reliability of R**, and it has to answer a discriminating question that the first-order readout cannot answer by itself: did R stay small because the prediction met its ground, or because the channel stopped delivering a discriminating observation (Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Formula, Closure: from inside, *"a perfect prediction or a closed channel... look the same"*).

- Option A, "uncertainty is one number the brain attaches to a belief": leads to the prediction that confidence is a global scalar, scoreable by the same frame that produces it. Fig 1 :22 refuses this: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :27 refuses the self-score: *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- Option B, "uncertainty is a typed, per-slot, second-order read of the gap, ranged against an immovable ground and a second frame": leads to the prediction that an uncertainty estimate has kinds (held, new, mis-attached, broken, refuted; Fig 5 :10-14, :53), that it is refusable (Fig 12 :140, *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate"*), and that it reports *"unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134) where the answer is not in reach rather than manufacturing a number.

**What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65, *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*): a shape with five properties, (1) per-slot, never a global average, (2) typed by the kind of gap, not graded only by magnitude, (3) second-order, a read of the reader, (4) channel-aware, distinguishing ground-met from channel-decayed, (5) ground-ranged and refusable, ending in a clear `unreached` or `gap` where the instruments cannot discriminate. Until that shape is bound as a term, it is a preference, predicts nothing, and no check inside the loop can see it (Fig 5 :62-63).

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. D1, D2 and D3 are `triangulated`; D4, the implementation frame, is a `gap` after a genuine re-census: no figure names a circuit, and the world sources explicitly lack a consensus implementation.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the cognitive-science question, no claim that the figures name a brain circuit or a single correct measure of confidence.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
