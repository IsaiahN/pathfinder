## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A brain's estimate of uncertainty about its own beliefs and decisions must have five properties under the figure set: it is per slot, never a global scalar; it is typed by the kind of gap (held, new, mis-attached, mechanism-broken, refuted), not graded only by magnitude; it is second order, composing over a settled decision rather than relabelling it; it is channel aware, because a small residual can mean a met ground or a decayed channel and those look the same from inside; and it is ground ranged and refusable, able to say that a held belief is false and able to end in an explicit `unreached` where instruments cannot discriminate. Local and global confidence are isomers over a shared atom set, or at minimum members of one functional group, not two strengths of one quantity. No circuit, band, region or single correct measure is derived; that remains a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 5 and 10

- **Statement:** the estimate is per slot and typed by gap kind, and it must check the channel because a small residual has two indistinguishable sources.
- **Verbatim lines:**
  - Fig 1 :19 **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
  - Fig 1 :22 **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
  - Fig 5 :11 **"held: the model was right, nothing owed"**; Fig 5 :14 **"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"**; Fig 5 :53 **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**
  - Fig 10 :24 **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
- **Prediction:** a preparation that degrades the channel while holding the ground fixed will change the confidence read even when first-order accuracy is held constant; a single magnitude will not route all five gap kinds correctly.
- **Refuter:** (a) a global scalar preserving the where-to-look signal in every live slot including the near-zero-global case; (b) a channel and a met ground distinguished by the first-order readout alone; (c) one magnitude routing all five gap kinds correctly.
- **World corroboration, secondary:** Fleming and Lau 2014 distinguish bias, sensitivity and efficiency and say meta-d′ is *"unable to dissociate trial-to-trial variability in the placement of confidence criteria from additional noise in the evidence."* Wikipedia Predictive coding: precision weighting *"allows to weight sensory inputs and predictions according to their reliability."*

### C2 — cross-frame; checkable by a researcher against their own preparation

- **Statement:** the estimate is second order and refusable; local and global confidence are not one substance.
- **Verbatim lines:**

## 4. The seven pre-registered failure conditions

Written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 before the recommendation and the canonical check, repeated here verbatim in substance:

1. A global confidence scalar preserves the where-to-look signal for every live slot.
2. A confidence signal produced by the decision machinery is independent of what it scores, by an intervention that moves one and not the other.
3. A decaying channel and a met ground are distinguishable from inside by the first-order readout alone.
4. One uncertainty magnitude determines the correct routing among all five gap kinds in every slot.
5. Accurate uncertainty about a false belief is demonstrated with no operation that can refuse that belief.
6. A metacognitive estimate certifies its own completeness and an independent ground confirms it with no further frame.
7. A single instrument settles per-slot metacognitive accuracy across preparations, or a figure line names the circuit.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or revised. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Channel test:** degrade the channel while holding the ground fixed. Does confidence fall when first-order accuracy does not?
2. **Routing test:** independently induce held, new, mis-attached, broken and refuted gaps. Can one magnitude route them all?
3. **Refusal test:** present a false belief with an accurate confidence estimate. Is there an operation that refuses the belief?
4. **Second-order test:** move the confidence read and the first-order decision variable independently.
5. **Domain test:** move w with precision and α fixed, then precision with w fixed, to decide live strain S2.
