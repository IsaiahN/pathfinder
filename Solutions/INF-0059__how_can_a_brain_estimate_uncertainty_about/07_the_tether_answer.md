# The Tether's answer: how a brain can estimate uncertainty about its own beliefs and decisions

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain determinants, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A brain holds a belief b, acts a, observes o′, and forms a per-slot residual R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|. The question is how the brain reads the reliability of that readout when the same machinery that makes the decision also produces the apparent confidence in it. This is a **union of four overlapping asks**, not one scalar (Fig 9 :54, **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"**): uncertainty about beliefs, uncertainty about decisions, the "how can" mechanism question, and the "estimate" measurement question. Each gets its own loop; verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Per slot, never a global number.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A global confidence near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state.
2. **Typed by gap kind, not graded only by magnitude.** Fig 5 :11-14 names held, new, mis-attached and mechanism-broken; Fig 5 :53 adds refuted. These route differently: aim the probe, refit, mint, or refuse. A single magnitude cannot decide the route.
3. **Second order, a read of the reader.** Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."** A first-order decision settled against the ground becomes the atom for the confidence estimate. It is not a relabelling of the decision.
4. **Channel aware.** Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**. A small R means the prediction met the ground or the channel stopped carrying a discriminating observation; from inside, Formula's Closure says those *"look the same."* The estimate must include a check that the instrument is still returning something (Fig 6 :34).
5. **Ground ranged and refusable.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** The estimate ranges against an immovable anchor and a low-overlap second frame (Fig 2 :29, :31). It can say a held belief is false, because *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate"* (Fig 12 :140; Operators :58). And it can end in an explicit stop: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** (Fig 13 :134).
6. **It distinguishes depth, absence and resolution.** Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, keeping the instructions distinct. "Low confidence" alone issues no instruction.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that this is how the brain actually computes confidence. No measurement here settles that.
- Not a name for the estimator. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**; Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** No circuit, band or region is derived.
- Not that meta-d′, a confidence rating, a wager or a decoding accuracy is the answer. Those are budget filters and measurement procedures, never witnesses for the shape (Fig 9 :60, **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**).
- Not that the Symbols table's w is the precision weight or the Behrens learning rate. Different operands, different bonds; strain S2 is live and named in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3.
- Not that local and global confidence are one quantity with two strengths. They are isomers over a shared atom set, or at minimum members of one functional group (`06` Q2).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened): a colorimeter reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Channel test:** an intervention that degrades the channel while holding the ground fixed. Does the confidence read fall when only the channel falls? If not, the channel-awareness property fails.
- **Routing test:** a preparation in which held, new, mis-attached, broken and refuted gaps can be independently induced. Does one magnitude route all five correctly? If so, the typed-gap property fails.
- **Refusal test:** a false belief with an accurate confidence estimate. Can the system refuse the belief? If it has no such operation, the estimate is accumulation, not estimation (Fig 12 :140).
- **Second-order test:** does the confidence read compose over a settled first-order decision, or is it a relabelling of it? An intervention that moves one and not the other settles this.
- **Domain test:** an intervention that moves w while holding precision and α fixed, and one that moves precision while holding w fixed. These decide live strain S2.

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 apply unchanged: seven conditions written before the recommendation and the canonical check.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, and not silence.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (attributed essay, *They're Scanning Books*), not nature's own list. The implementation frame D4 has actors and an anchor but no governing figure line, so it stays a `gap`. The framework states what an estimator must look like, states which instrument would close the stop, and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (Figures README) No number is manufactured where the instruments cannot discriminate.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a confidence circuit and does not claim a single correct measure of metacognition. It describes the shape any estimator of uncertainty about a belief or decision must have: per slot, because a global error hides the one live slot; typed by the kind of gap, because held, new, mis-attached, broken and refuted gaps need different responses; second order, because confidence composes over a settled decision rather than relabelling it; channel aware, because a small error can mean either a met ground or a dead channel and those look the same from inside; and ground ranged and refusable, because a mirror cannot correct you and a vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate. What it offers is that shape, seven failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which brain circuit implements it is a declared gap, not a hidden answer. The stop is recorded so it can be checked.
