## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The Figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel* (re-opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an uncertainty estimator that can be stated before a circuit or a measure is named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the five properties in the README: per-slot, typed by gap kind, second order, channel aware, ground ranged and refusable. A specific answer, such as "confidence is the decoded activity of region X at frequency Y", is a name.

The distinction matters because the question is a *"how can"* ask. A shape answers a generative question by stating necessary properties; a name answers it by pointing at an implementation. Only the first is available from the figures (Fig 13 :133, *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*).

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (re-opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the held vocabulary is R, b, w, o′, the gap kinds and the bonds; the sought-for vocabulary is the reliability read.
- Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"**. So the residual was described as a self-score problem and a channel problem before any field was named as the frame to import from.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (attributed, not re-opened): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a failure would look like before showing the sources.
- *Library of Babel* (re-opened): the stop is where *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In this question the stop is where the instrument that would discriminate a met ground from a decayed channel is named, or declared unreached.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The Figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, an instrument that discriminates ground-met from channel-decayed in a preparation would license a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a region or a measure from the neuroscience frame is such an import; it is owed provenance and a shadow test, and it adds no atom to the figure set.

*They're Scanning Books* (attributed, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list; treating a neural binding as a figure result would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: source named from a reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened) gives the mechanism. A colorimeter reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* (quote as recorded in the exemplar; the re-opened essay states the same case in prose). And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question the projection is the confidence reading. A high-confidence signal could come from a well-grounded decision, from a mis-attached but coherent model, from a saturated metric, or from a channel that stopped carrying disconfirming evidence. Naming the source as "certainty" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"** names exactly why the reading underdetermines the source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A confidence signal that only validates the decision that produced it is not a validator.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid a computational account of confidence?

**No.** The rule governs a *missing* primitive (Figures README: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*). A reliability read, a boundary diff and a learnable weighting w are not missing primitives: w is already in the Symbols table (:23-24, *"w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings"*), and the gap kinds are already in Fig 5. What is missing is an instrument that discriminates sources. So stating the shape is allowed, and so is importing a candidate mechanism with provenance. What is forbidden is presenting the import as a figure derivation, or naming it as *the* answer before the ground pays.

**Does the record commit the source error?** Yes. Any account that reads "confidence" off a single projection (an accuracy curve, a firing rate, a decoded probability) and names the source as certainty is doing what the spectrometer was needed to prevent. The correct form keeps the source open and reports which instrument would close it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let an outcome or an experiment settle it.

**The record is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (re-opened): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the cognitive-science claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Honest standing of the five-property shape

The five properties are a shape, not a result. They are necessary conditions for an uncertainty estimate under the figure set, derived before the world sources were read. They are `general` as structural constraints and `specified` when applied to a particular slot or preparation. Nothing in this folder claims they are the mechanism the brain actually uses. The implementation remains a declared `gap` (D4); which property is measurable first in a given preparation is a question for the ground, not for this derivation.
