## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Bias, sensitivity and efficiency are different things** (Fleming and Lau 2014). A confidence level and the ability to discriminate correct from incorrect trials are not one quantity. This corroborates the typed-gap finding F5 and warns against a single scalar.
2. **Meta-uncertainty is itself noisy and imposes a ceiling** (Fleming 2026, defining the CASANDRE parameter from Boundy-Singer et al. 2023). This is the world counterpart of Fig 8 :37, *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
3. **Precision is a reliability weight, not a belief-source weight** (Wikipedia Predictive coding; Behrens 2007). It corroborates F4 and F9 but must not be conflated with the Symbols table's w (live strain S2).
4. **The measures are not empirically settled** (Rahnev 2025). Seventeen measures of metacognition were assessed and most had been assumed rather than established. This corroborates the declared gap D4 and forbids naming a single correct measure.
5. **Decision theory prices information rather than measuring doubt** (Wikipedia Value of information). It corroborates F9: what matters is what information would change, and the value of two pieces of evidence is not additive.
6. **Animal metacognition supplies a low-overlap witness** (Wikipedia Metacognition, Foote and Crystal 2007 rats declining difficult trials; Morgan et al. 2014 macaques staking tokens). A decline or wager option is a per-trial, ground-ranged readout, not a verbal report. It corroborates the per-slot and ground-ranged shape without sharing the human self-report evidence pool.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered.
