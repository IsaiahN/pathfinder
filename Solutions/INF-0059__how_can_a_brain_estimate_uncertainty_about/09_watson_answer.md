## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a brain estimating uncertainty about its own beliefs and decisions. Option A: uncertainty is one scalar confidence value the brain attaches to a belief or decision, measurable by a standard metacognitive index. Option B: uncertainty is a per-slot, typed, second-order, channel-aware and refusable read of the residual, ranging against an immovable ground, ending in a stated stop where instruments cannot discriminate. What each leads to: A sends a self-score that discards the where-to-look signal, collapses five gap kinds into one magnitude, and cannot distinguish a met ground from a dead channel. B sends a checkable shape, seven refuters written in advance, and a declared implementation gap rather than a named circuit.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B survives in `general` and `specified` mode; Option A is refused. An uncertainty estimate must be per slot, typed by gap kind, second order, channel aware, ground ranged and refusable, and it must end in an explicit stop where the instruments cannot discriminate. Local and global confidence are isomers over a shared atom set or, more weakly, members of one functional group, not two strengths of one quantity. The Symbols table's w is not the precision weight; strain S2 stays live. No circuit, region, band or single correct measure is derived: D4 is a `gap`, and the framework returns the shape and the discriminating instrument, not a name. All verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Seven refuters were written before the run, the modality is stated on every substantive claim, and the implementation is declared a gap rather than filled by a name, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a confidence scalar or a named circuit instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices this: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The refused elements are removable without touching the derivation. A scalar report can be replaced by a per-slot typed report; a named circuit can be withdrawn and left as a gap; strain S2 can be resolved by an intervention that moves one weight and not the other. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and nothing is deleted from the record (Fig 6 :41-46: what is recorded only grows; what is reachable can fall).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An intervention that distinguishes a decayed channel from a met ground, a routing test that lets one magnitude handle all five gap kinds, a refusal test with an accurate estimate of a false belief, a domain test separating w from precision, or an instrument that settles per-slot metacognitive accuracy across preparations would move the relevant claim to `grounded` or reverse it. A consensus among readers sharing one evidence pool is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :40). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
