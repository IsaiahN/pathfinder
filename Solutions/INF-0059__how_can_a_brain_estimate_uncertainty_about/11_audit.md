## Round trip gap R_T

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

One thing survives cleanly: the estimate is a read over a settled first-order decision, and it must be ranged against a ground rather than negotiated. That is the residue worth sending.
