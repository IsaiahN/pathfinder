## QUESTION

Option B: the structural shape any such answer must have, with the ranking left open. What each leads to: A presents a `general`-mode verdict that the figures do not license and spends the independence of the people checking it; B presents a checkable conjunction, written refuters and an open slot until a ground settles it. The files take B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The false-minus-true difference in cascade reach and persistence. Arity: two content classes on one topology, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55-58), not one hard question. Symmetry: a threshold comparison of two quantities, reach and persistence (Operators table :46-52), not a symmetric join. Scale: microscopic node and edge, mesoscopic community and bridge, macroscopic cascade. The governing effect is three steps out (Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 1, topology.** Actors: nodes, edges, communities, bridge nodes. Currency: adjacency and paths. Anchor: measured adjacency, who actually connects to whom. Question type: partition boundary (which paths exist). Verdict source: Fig 13 and Fig 8.
- **Domain 2, diffusion.** Actors: cascades, adopters, repost handoffs. Currency: transmissions. Anchor: observed cascade size, depth and time. Question type: union with content; a claim travels only if both structure and content allow it. Verdict source: Fig 2 and Fig 7.
- **Domain 3, truth and correction.** Actors: claims, fact-checkers, ground truth. Currency: truth value and corrections. Anchor: the claim's ground truth, which does not move when the network pushes on it. Question type: partition boundary (anchor versus frame). Verdict source: Fig 2 and Fig 10.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero and its agreement is not a witness.

Order by dependency: enumerate adjacency, then handoff, then anchor, then measured differential (Fig 3 :20-21; Fig 11 :43-48). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: homophily was initially mapped only onto Fig 2 panel 2, which says "they never talk, and still agree." Homophilous nodes do talk. Re-census separated Fig 2 panel 1 (mutual update) from the Fig 8 and Fig 13 overlap reading (shared evidence). Two collapses, one compound. No strain remains after that re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure frame decides a shape, not a ranking. The conditions under which a claim outruns correction are: correlated evidence pools, no non-updating anchor, same-closure handoff, catalytic crossings between closures, and multi-hop contact. They form one union compound and must be reported separately, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Bridges are catalytic and valence-free; the false-specific reason is an import from psychology, not a figure result. Which condition dominates, and the effect sizes, is a `gap` at the figure level. Status: `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded but real. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The content-side binding is removable without touching the structural derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold it. A sent claim can be superseded; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A matched-seed cascade instrument that separates structure from content, a fixed-topology anchor test, a hop test, or a first-order-only result would settle the empirical ranking; a figure re-census that ranks the conditions would change the shape verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
