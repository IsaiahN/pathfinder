# The Tether's answer: which network features let misinformation outrun truth

**Mode: `general` on the figure laws, `specified` on their application to diffusion networks. Nothing is `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two claims, one true and one false, enter the same network. We observe a difference in reach (cascade size and depth) and persistence (time until circulation stops). The question asks which structural features produce that difference. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: structure and content are two conditions, and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :55-58). It has its own loop and its own ground. The differential is per slot (Fig 1 :19), never an average over content classes.

## 2. The shape

1. **A social network is already the relevant structure.** Fig 13 :24 defines network as "that entities relate, and influence travels." The application is an instance, not an analogy, so the figures bear directly on the question.
2. **Correlated evidence pools are the first suspect.** Fig 8 :47-49: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 13 :38-39 adds that six overlapping members would be fewer members wearing six names. A dense like-minded cluster has no surplus to correct anything.
3. **No non-updating anchor is the second suspect.** Fig 2 :13-15: with "no anchor; each updates on the others," the network converges on "whoever had the most influence." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." A fact-check that updates on the crowd is not an anchor; it is panel 1 again (Fig 2 :34).
4. **The two are one compound, not two levers.** Echo chambers have both mutual update and a shared pool. Fig 9 :54-58 requires unbundling a union. A bridge added to an unanchored cluster moves content but supplies no ground.
5. **Handoff down a same-closure chain gives motion without progress.** Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room." Persistence is the time the chain takes to run out of nodes, not evidence accumulating.
6. **Bridges are catalytic and valence-free.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same bridge carries correction as readily as falsehood. The structural claim is about reach, not about a node being pro-false.
7. **The effect is multi-hop.** Fig 11 :52: "first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Degree alone is incomplete.
8. **The figures do not say why *false* specifically wins, and do not rank the features.** The content-side reason (novelty, identity, emotion) is an import from the psychology frame, debited against independence (Fig 6 :23-24). Formula "Direction": every check runs on the mechanism, none on the direction of the question. That is a `gap` at the figure level, reported plainly.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranked list. There is no figure line that orders the five conditions by effect size.
- Not a claim that any node is inherently a misinformation node. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not a claim that adding cross-cutting ties alone fixes the problem. That treats only one half of a union compound (F3).
- Not a derivation of the content-side mechanism. Novelty and identity are imported, provenanced as such.
- Not a measurement. No cascade data was analysed here. Nothing is `grounded`.
- Not a claim that the figures have settled the empirical question. They return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the method: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and it was the spectrometer that resolved it. Here the corresponding instruments are:

- a cascade measurement that separates structural position from content properties under matched seeds, so a reading can no longer be attributed to an unknown source;
- an anchor test: insert a non-updating correction node at fixed topology and measure the change in false-over-true persistence;
- a hop test: measure the differential at first, second and third order and check whether first-order degree accounts for all of it;
- a bridge test: compare the same bridge carrying true and false content, to separate catalytic reach from content bias.
- Standing falsifiers from `01`: no differential in a high-overlap unanchored network; no anchor effect; same-closure handoffs that increase ground contact; a fully first-order differential; a figure line naming a content property as a network feature.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books* reminds us that "a minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the vocabulary makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). A model that reproduces observed cascades is not thereby the generator of them.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name one winning feature, and it does not claim to have measured anything. It says that a social network is already the structure whose meaning is that influence travels, and that from the figure laws it follows that a claim outruns correction where evidence pools are correlated, no anchor refuses to update, handoffs stay inside one closure, catalytic bridges carry content across closures, and the effect sits two or three hops out. It also says that these are one union compound, so a single lever should not be expected to clear it, and that bridges are valence-free: the same bridge carries corrections. The framework deliberately does not say which feature dominates or why false content specifically wins, because the content-side reason is an import from psychology and the figures return a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
