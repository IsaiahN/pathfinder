## Question in effect terms

Two content classes, true and false, are released into one network. The residual is the observed difference in reach (cascade size and depth) and persistence (time before the claim stops circulating). The question asks which structural features of the network produce or amplify that difference. Option A: a ranked list of features with effect sizes. Option B: the shape of the structural conditions under which any claim outruns the ground, with the ranking left open. The files below take B and say plainly what the figures cannot decide.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settled the question. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`), not a ground reading.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: agreement among correlated frames is "disqualifying for a verdict" (Fig 2 :40); a list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless; and "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42).
