## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For CMP-0064 a **shape** is therefore: a set of structural predicates that any answer must satisfy, stated before we can rank them or attach effect sizes. "A network in which evidence pools are correlated and no non-updating anchor is reachable" is a shape. "Degree centrality above 0.7 is the cause" is a name, and the figures do not license it. The shape constrains; it does not fill.

The same essay names the exact structural condition of this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is Fig 9 :54-58 in prose, and it is why the answer here is a conjunction, not a ranked list.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary bootstrapping: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer." The known terms here were homophily, cluster, bridge, cascade, anchor; the recurring new terms were structural isolation, shared evidence pool, mirror chain, catalytic node.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality." The tether for this question is the measured cascade, not the theory.
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** So the residual was written as a difference in reach and persistence, not as "virality."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the outreach ordering in `02_outreach_email.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here, that means a measured cascade under matched content, which this derivation did not run.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The content-side reason false wins (novelty, identity) is such an import, from psychology. It is recorded as an import and debited against independence, not presented as a figure derivation.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "a minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So naming "network" as the relevant structure is a visible choice among the six, not a discovery about social media specifically.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that bears hardest here: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." A cascade size is a reading. It does not say whether the claim spread because of topology, because of content, or because the measurement pooled the two. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." What is owed is an instrument that discriminates structure from content, not a larger corpus of cascade observations.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the central social mechanism in essay form, and it is nearly a restatement of Fig 2 and Fig 8:

- "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."
- "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources (Fig 2 :40–41)."
- "High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."
- "Two frames that overlap almost completely are 'one frame wearing two names' (Fig 8 :48–49)."
- "Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained (Fig 10 :80)."

This is the essay-level reason a dense, like-minded cluster is the structural suspect: the very contact that makes the cluster communicate also consumes the independence that would let it correct itself. It also flags the intervention limit: adding cross-cutting contact helps only if it renews *separate* experience, not merely more of the same pool.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the arithmetic: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different. Figure 2 defines independence as 'how little they draw on the same evidence.' Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." And on individual-level fixes, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1), which is why one average virality score destroys the true/false differential the question is about.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names the failure mode: "if you are just going off of what is present in your heads, then eventually, the room will be swayed by negotiation which is persuasion, or by coherent drift (both of you found a middle ground to agree on)." It also gives the honesty limit: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A model that reproduces observed cascades is not thereby the generator of those cascades.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**For this question, "shape" means a conjunction of structural conditions that a measured cascade must survive before any single feature can be named as the cause.** Concretely, any candidate answer must be stated in effect terms as:

> A claim outruns correction when the network gives it correlated evidence pools, no non-updating anchor, same-closure handoff, catalytic crossings between closures, and multi-hop reach; and when the claim itself imports a content property the pool rewards.

That is a shape. It does not rank the five conditions, does not say which is necessary, and does not attach numbers. The figures cannot do those things, and the world sources, being mostly one computational-social-science pool, do not give them independent authority either.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A well-cited paper is a filter for what to read, not a witness for the verdict.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* records the demo caveat: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figure text. It is not a test of the claim, it is not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on. That limitation is stated on every substantive file in this folder.

## 7. Correction to my own first reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific structural feature. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Network, gradient and spectrum are already among the six (Fig 13), and "homophily" and "bridge" are bindings onto held structure, not new primitives. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh structural primitive or claiming the binding moves the wall. The real limit in this question is the other one: the figures cannot rank or measure the features, and they cannot see the content-side direction of the question. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
