# The Tether's answer on balancing priced benefits and hard-to-quantify ecological values

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the procedure, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a decision for any particular place. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A conservation choice produces outcomes of two kinds: those that register in one monetary accounting and those that do not. A procedure holding only the monetary accounting cannot register the second kind, so it returns an answer that is coherent inside its frame and wrong at the ground, silently. Fig 9 :54 says "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a union compound, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57 says "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either".

## 2. The shape

1. **Do not sum.** Fig 12 :125 says "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." And Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Keep the priced and the unpriced readings as separate accounts and compose them only at the decision. (`general` rule, `specified` application to conservation, corroborated by IPBES and Daw et al.)

2. **Keep the per-slot signal.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." And Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single total erases the place or species or group that carries the loss. Keep residuals at the slot level.

3. **Enumerate the habitat.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." List what is actually in contact with the decision, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. Do not start from a list of priceable services.

4. **Two independent anchors, not agreement.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Use measured ecological state for 'did it work' and documented human judgment for 'what is valued', each legitimate for its own question (Fig 2 :36). A market price is an instrument for both, not an anchor for either. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Ecological loss is often irreversible, so the cost is high.

5. **Conventions must be visible and violable.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A discount rate or criterion weight that alone flips the decision has become an un-authorised verdict. Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Declare the stake.

6. **Protected values get a threshold, not a weight.** The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities". A sacred or protected value enters as a constraint or a refusal, not as a term in a sum. A vocabulary with only the symmetric bond can accumulate and cannot refuse.

7. **Measure the gap of any index.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Publish R_T for any condensation of the value set.

8. **Pre-register the refuter.** Seat Map :287: 'And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device.' The five refuters in `01` section 5 were written before this conclusion.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that ecology is priceless or that all trade-offs are forbidden. Commensurable services can be summed inside their own domain.
- Not a universal weight, discount rate, or dollar value per species. Those are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that more reviewers settle it. A room does not add an anchor (Seat Map :163; Fig 2 :35).
- Not that economics is wrong. It is one necessary reading that cannot be the verdict.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles a general 'how should'.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

Not a further argument, but a discriminator or a case set.

1. **Commensuration test:** exhibit one accounting that preserves every per-slot residual and round-trips with R_T = 0.
2. **Anchor test:** find an anchor that settles a conservation question while moving when a frame pushes on it.
3. **Case test:** compare a single-number procedure with the plural procedure across real decisions; if the single number protects the hard-to-quantify values at least as reliably, 'do not sum' fails.
4. **Operator test:** show a protected value is compensable at the margin without altering what the community protects.
5. **Re-census test:** show the two-currency rule of Fig 12 does not recurse to valuation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a shape, not a name. The seven steps above constrain any defensible balance without supplying the weights, the species, or the place. Another instrument or a case set could move the verdict toward `grounded`; agreement cannot. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give conservation a single number for balancing people's measurable benefits against ecological values that resist quantification. It says the two sides are different currencies that cannot be added, so summing them into one total is the error rather than the fix. The question is a union of two conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched. A defensible procedure keeps residuals at the level of each species, place, and group, enumerates the habitat and its cascades rather than starting from a list of priceable services, ranges ecological measurement and documented human judgment as two independent anchors, treats discount rates and weights as visible conventions, gives protected values threshold standing rather than a weight, and publishes what any single index leaves out. It carries refuters written in advance. What it offers is the shape any answer must have and the instruments that would close it, not a decision for any particular place. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
