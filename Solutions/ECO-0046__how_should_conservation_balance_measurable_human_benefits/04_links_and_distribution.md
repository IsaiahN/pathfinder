## Two things in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The aggregation failure is empirical, not only structural.** Daw et al. (2015) found an apparent conservation-profit win-win at the aggregate scale that concealed losses to food production and to marginalised women traders. That is F2 in the audit: the per-slot residual disappeared into an average. Because the paper is from a different field and uses a different method, this is genuine low-overlap corroboration, not a framework author agreeing with the framework.
2. **The incommensurability is already a policy finding.** The IPBES Values Assessment found that 74 percent of valuation studies used instrumental values, 20 percent intrinsic and 6 percent relational, and that fewer than 5 percent of valuation studies reach policy. IPBES explicitly calls for plural methods and notes that valuation tools act as 'value articulating institutions'. That is F1 and F5 from an independent institutional source. It corroborates the figure verdict; it does not produce it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, refuter. Ask what would refute it. No theory shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, offer the three or four source links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once the recipient holds the framework's interior their agreement is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (formula 'Direction'; Library of Babel essay on bundled problems).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the shape claim is `general`/`specified`, not `grounded`, and its refuter is written in advance. Repair any finding in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 that fires before sending.
