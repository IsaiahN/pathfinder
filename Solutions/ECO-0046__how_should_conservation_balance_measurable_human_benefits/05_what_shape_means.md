## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can fill it

The canonical README says the framework returns 'what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)' and stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is honest. Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From the Library of Babel essay: 'you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question.'

For ECO-0046, the shape is **not** a particular weight, discount rate, or species list. It is the form a legitimate balancing procedure must have before any of those are chosen: keep the currencies distinguishable, range each against its own anchor, enumerate the habitat and its cascades, make conventions visible and violable, protect what is protected with a threshold or a refusal, publish the round-trip gap of any index, and pre-register a refuter. A filled answer would be 'protect 62 percent of the watershed and spend USD 4.1m'. A shape is 'any defensible decision here must keep the unpriced residual visible at the moment of choice'.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- **Proximity bootstrapping.** The Library of Babel essay: 'use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of', then 'With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary.' Applied here: start from the words the question already gives ('measurable', 'difficult to quantify', 'balance') and let the figure text refine them into 'per-slot residual', 'incommensurable currency', 'anchor', 'union compound', 'habitat'.
- **Residual first, frame second.** Fig 9 :27 to :28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". The essay agrees that the anchor is the question: 'When traversing through an infinite library, you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing.' If the residual is described as 'how do we price the unpriced', any pricing frame will seem to fit. Described as 'a union of two conditions where one cannot generate a signal in the other's accounting', the frame set narrows.
- **Bundle detection.** The essay: 'Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually.' Fig 9 :54 says the same thing in the framework's terms: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is the single most direct transfer from the essays to this question.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles a term.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: 'the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground.' For conservation, a monitoring result, a recovery, a confirmed loss.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here, importing the plural-values frame into a cost-benefit frame is legitimate only if the import is recorded as coming from IPBES, Daw, or a community's own deliberation, and only if it explains a gap the cost-benefit frame left.

The canonical README adds the limit: 'Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible' (quoted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` as the corresponding essay claim). Declaring that a species has intrinsic value does not make the declaration a measurement; it makes the valuation stance visible. That is the correct use of the concept here.

## 4. The failure mode this question is prone to

The unpriced side is a **projection loss**. The canonical framing from the Hard Problem essay (as carried in the exemplar's local `05` file) is that an instrument reads green and 'cannot comment on the source'; several sources can give the same reading, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. A monetary total is a reading of a bundle of sources. Naming one source (the value) from that reading is the same error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

For this question that means: an aggregate benefit figure cannot tell you whether the underlying value came from use, existence, bequest, or identity. Treating the aggregate as if it did is the metamerism error. The round-trip gap R_T in Fig 4 :30 measures exactly what the aggregate dropped: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

## 5. Answer to the question 'what shape?'

**Shape:** a decision procedure that (a) splits the union before searching, (b) holds at least two incommensurable currencies apart and composes them only at the decision, (c) enumerates the habitat and its second- and third-order cascades, (d) ranges each frame against its own non-updating anchor, (e) treats discount rates and weights as visible, violable conventions and declares the stakeholder's stake, (f) gives protected values threshold or refusal standing, (g) publishes R_T for any index, and (h) carries a pre-registered refuter.

**Not a shape:** a universal weight, a fixed dollar value per species, a single 'correct' discount rate. Those are names where the framework returns a shape, and they are exactly what the world sources warn against.

## 6. Simulation status

The canonical README quotes the validation limit, and the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay (via the exemplar's local copy) states it plainly: 'LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself'; LLM output is 'simulacra'. So this whole folder, including this file and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on, and no verdict here is `grounded`.
