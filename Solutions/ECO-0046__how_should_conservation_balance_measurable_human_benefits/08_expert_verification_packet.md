# Expert verification packet: the Tether on conservation valuation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a conservation researcher or valuation specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the procedure, not `grounded`.** No case set or instrument is claimed to have settled the general 'how should'. This is a shape and a route, not a decision for a particular place.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A conservation decision that holds only one monetary accounting cannot register an ecological value with no unit in that accounting, so its failure is silent. 'Balance' is therefore a union of two conditions, not one scalar to be optimised. A defensible procedure keeps the currencies apart and composes them only at the decision; keeps residuals per species, place and group; enumerates the habitat and its cascades; ranges measured ecological state and documented human judgment as two independent anchors; treats discount rates and weights as visible, violable conventions; gives protected values threshold or refusal standing; publishes the round-trip gap of any index; and carries a refuter written in advance. The framework returns this shape, not a name or a number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Fig 12 and Fig 1

- **Statement:** two incommensurable currencies must not be summed; aggregation destroys the live per-slot signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130, "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** are these strings present in the verbatim figure extract, and do they say what the claim says? Note the honest boundary: the figure names bits and actions, not money and ecology; the application is level-recursive and `specified`, corroborated by Daw et al. 2015 and IPBES, not asserted as a verbatim figure statement about money.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single accounting that preserves every per-slot residual and round-trips with R_T = 0.

### C2: ecology-facing, checkable by a conservation practitioner

- **Statement:** enumerate the habitat and its cascades before composing a value list; there is no standing list of 'good' services, because valence is relative to the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43, "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :49, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :44, "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did a valuation that started from a fixed service list miss a second- or third-order effect that only appeared when the habitat was enumerated? Naidoo and Ricketts (2006) found that which forests passed a cost-benefit test depended on how many services were counted, and that carbon storage dominated and swamped other values. Is that an enumeration failure or a legitimate weighting?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a composition-derived service list that misses no second- or third-order effect for a changed residual.

### C3: decision-facing, checkable by a valuation specialist or regulator

- **Statement:** conventions must be visible and violable; protected values are thresholds, not weights; any index has a measurable round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47, "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Operators table: ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities". Fig 4 :30, "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **What to check:** in a decision you know, did a discount rate or a criterion weight alone flip the outcome? Did a protected value get treated as compensable when the community treated it as sacred? Was the gap between the index and the underlying value set reported? Daw et al. (2015) found that monetary framing of taboo trade-offs hid incommensurable conflicts and could worsen compliance.
- **Refuter:** show a convention that decides an outcome while remaining checkable and non-verdict-like; or show an index whose T_E composed with T_A returns the original set with zero gap; or show a protected value compensable at the margin without altering what it protects.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that ecological values are infinite or that trade-offs are always forbidden.
- Not a universal weight, shadow price, discount rate, or species value.
- Not that economics is wrong. It is a necessary reading that cannot be the verdict.
- Not that more expert reviewers improve the verdict. A room does not add an anchor (Seat Map :163).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the general 'how should'.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A commensuration that preserves all per-slot residuals with zero round-trip gap.
2. An anchor that settles a conservation question while updating under a frame's opinion.
3. A case set in which a single-number procedure protects hard-to-quantify values at least as well as a plural one.
4. A protected value that is compensable at the margin without changing what it protects.
5. A re-census showing the two-currency rule does not recurse to valuation.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :125 and :130, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 11 :43 and :49, Fig 10 :47, and Fig 4 :30. Eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: was the value list composed from a typology or enumerated from contact? Did a cascade get missed?
3. Check C3 against one decision you know: did a convention decide the outcome, and was the index gap reported?
4. Name one instrument or case set that would distinguish a single-total procedure from a plural procedure.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
