## 0. The residual in effect terms

A conservation decision selects an action. Each action produces outcomes of two broad kinds.

- **Kind P (priced).** Outcomes that register in one market-denominated accounting: timber volume, catch, tonnes of carbon, avoided flood damage, willingness-to-pay. They share a unit, so they can be summed, ranked, and traded against cost.
- **Kind U (unpriced or hard to quantify).** Outcomes that do not register in that accounting: the continued existence of a species, the integrity of an ecosystem, cultural identity, bequest to future generations, intrinsic value. They have no shared unit with Kind P and often no agreed ordering among themselves.

The residual is not 'which number is bigger'. It is this: **a decision procedure that holds only the priced accounting cannot register a Kind U outcome at all, so it returns an answer that is self-consistent inside its frame and systematically wrong at the ground.** The failure is silent, because the missing side produces no error signal in the frame that omits it. The question asks for the form of a procedure that brings both sides to a decision without pretending they share a unit.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step 'Direction'): *a decision rule that keeps the currencies distinguishable, ranges each against its own anchor, enumerates the habitat and its cascades, makes its conventions visible and violable, and states in advance what would refute it.* This shape is bound here so the loop can accrue a residual against it.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** two conditions, a commensurable condition P and an incommensurable condition U. A decision lands in their intersection, so this is a **union compound**, not a partition (Fig 9 :54 to :58). Split it rather than search it as one hard question.
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. Kind P supports a total order in money; Kind U resists that order. They are not swappable operands of a symmetric bond. Their relation is threshold-like (a limit, a constraint, a protected value), not additive.
- **Scale:** first order inside each value domain (a valuation, a monitoring survey); second order at the decision that composes them; contact reaches three steps out (Fig 11 :52 to :53, the DDT cascade).

One line per domain. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Market valuation** | analysts, prices, WTP surveys, discount rates | money, a single common denominator | the measured biophysical flow being priced, not the price itself (a price updates, so it is an instrument) | domain; anchor nameable |
| **D2 Ecological state** | species, populations, processes, habitats, cascades | biophysical state and trajectory | a survey, plot, or experiment: the measured state of the system, which does not move when a valuation frame objects | domain; anchor nameable |
| **D3 Plural and held values** | communities, indigenous and local knowledge, ethicists, cultural identity | reasons, narratives, principles, protected values | documented, deliberated human judgment, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36 to :37) | domain; anchor nameable but incommensurable with D1 |
| **D4 Decision and governance** | boards, regulators, managers, conventions (discount rate, threshold, protected-area rule) | a choice among alternatives, with reversibility and cost of being wrong | follow-up monitoring of whether the action conserved what it claimed | domain; anchor nameable |

D3 initially looked anchorless and was nearly dropped. Re-census fixed it: Fig 2 :36 says "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." For 'what do people hold valuable', human judgment is the right anchor; for 'is this code correct' it would be only an instrument (Fig 2 :37 to :38). This correction is kept visible in the retraction log.

**Partition or union.** Union. D1, D2 and D3 overlap in the same decision and cannot be separated into independent problems; D4 composes them. Per Fig 9 :54, unbundle before searching again. A single lever (for example, a higher carbon price) clears the region where only P holds and leaves the intersection standing because U still binds.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, can the frame register U at all; vocabulary, does it have terms for what the situation affords (existence, bequest, intrinsic value); objective, can each domain say what counts as progress in its own terms; planning, a route under incomplete information; learn and carry. The middle link (vocabulary to objective) is where conservation valuation usually breaks, because it has no instrument (Fig 3 :24 to :27). Optimising D1 cannot rescue a break at D3 (Fig 3 :19).

## 3. Findings

### F1: The two sides are two currencies and do not add (governing finding)

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
Fig 12 :126: "They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"
Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."

This is the decisive structural point. A single monetary total is one accounting trying to price a quantity that the other accounting cannot represent. The shortfall is not noticed from inside the accounting, exactly as the figure says at :127: "and will not notice the shortfall." The legitimate move is to hold two readings apart and compose them at the decision, not to force a common denominator.

**Mode and strain, stated honestly.** Fig 12 names its two currencies as bits (cost of description) and actions (cost of finding out whether a composition holds). Applying the rule to money versus ecological value is a **level-recursive application**, licensed by Fig 12 :103 ("Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible") and by the canonical README's recursivity claim. It is `general` as a structural rule and `specified` as an application to conservation. It is not a verbatim statement about money. The world sources (IPBES, Daw, Rea and Munns) independently name the same incommensurability, so the application is corroborated outside the framework. Recorded as a level shift, not hidden as a strain.

**Refuter:** show a commensuration that preserves every per-slot residual and round-trips with zero gap R_T. If one exists, 'do not sum' fails.

**Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 12 (two currencies), Fig 1 (aggregation hides signal) and Fig 9 (split a union), which are low-overlap figure lines.

### F2: Aggregating across slots throws away the live signal

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A total economic value or a single hectares-protected headline is an average over slots. The species, place, or group that carries the live loss vanishes into it. This is not a side objection: Daw et al. (2015) found that aggregate conservation-profit 'win-wins' concealed losses to food production and to marginalised stakeholders, and that monetary valuation "can mask individual winners and losers". The figure and the world source agree from different fields.

**Refuter:** show that the aggregate preserving the decision is sufficient, that is, no slot carries a residual that would change the decision if it were visible.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 1 plus the Daw PNAS result, low overlap).

### F3: This is a union compound, so split it before searching

Fig 9 :4 to :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

'Balance P against U' treats a union as one scalar. It is not one problem made harder; it is a specific compound (Fig 9 :58). Unbundle first. After unbundling, the residue is smaller and, more importantly, finally describable. Searching for the optimal weight on a single scale is searching inside the wrong closure.

**Refuter:** show the two conditions collapse to one measurable condition with a shared unit and no residual left in the intersection.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 9 split rule plus Fig 12 two currencies).

### F4: Enumerate the habitat; do not compose a list of priced things

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :61 to :62: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

A conservation valuation that starts from 'the services we can price' has composed its list inside its own closure. The figures require the opposite order: read off actors, conditions and relations in contact with the residual, then move outward until the cascade stops mattering. An actor that is a benefactor to one residual is a malfactor to another, so no standing list of 'good' services survives a change of the question. And because introduction is available but exclusion is not, a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state (Fig 11 :63).

**Refuter:** exhibit a complete valuation list derived by composition from a fixed service typology that misses no second-order or third-order effect for a changed residual.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 9 'describe it before you go looking').

### F5: Conventions must be visible and violable

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."

Discount rates, shadow prices, and criterion weights are conventions. They make different value domains statable on one page; they do not settle which action is right. If a discount rate alone flips the decision, it has stopped being a convention and has become an un-authorised verdict. Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. This is the framework's own rule, not an external ethical preference.

**Refuter:** show a convention that decides an outcome while remaining checkable and non-verdict-like, or a decision process in which no convention carries any outcome weight.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 10 plus the IPBES observation that valuation methods are 'value articulating institutions').

### F6: Anchors, not agreement, settle whether conservation worked

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

D2 (measured ecological state) and D3 (documented human judgment) are two low-overlap anchors, each legitimate for its own question. D1's market price is not an anchor for either: it updates on what people are willing to pay, which is exactly what a frame thinks. Because ecological loss is often irreversible, the cost of being wrong is high, so the framework says to buy independence in proportion to that cost. Aggreement among many economists in one evidence pool is not a second witness.

**Refuter:** show a market price that does not move when willingness-to-pay changes and still settles the ecological question.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor rule plus Fig 11 habitat/contact cascade).

### F7: Protected values are thresholds, not weights

Operators table: comparison ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities"; Fig 12 :86 names the test, "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."
Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

Some Kind U values are sacred or protected: communities profess not to trade them for money. Daw et al. (2015), building on Tetlock, call these 'taboo trade-offs' and show that forcing them into monetary compensation hides the conflict and worsens compliance. The figure's grammar has the right operator: such a value enters as ⋛ (a threshold or constraint) or as a refusal, not as + (a weight in a sum). A vocabulary with only + can accumulate and cannot refuse.

**Refuter:** show a protected value that is meaningfully compensable at the margin without changing what the community protects.

**Triangulation:** single-source for the operator mapping (Operators table / Fig 12), corroborated by the Daw PNAS result. Marked single-source on the figure side.

### F8: Any single index needs a measured round-trip gap

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
Fig 4 :31: "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"

A headline indicator (total economic value, a single biodiversity index) is a condensation T_A of the full value set. Bringing it back to a concrete decision (T_E) loses something. That loss is R_T, and the figures require it to be measured rather than assumed zero. Reporting an index without its round-trip gap is the dead-reckoning error the figure names.

**Refuter:** exhibit an index whose T_E composed with T_A returns the original value set with zero gap.

**Triangulation:** triangulated (Fig 4 plus Fig 1 per-slot rule).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach (Seat Map :287: 'And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device.'). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single accounting is exhibited that preserves every per-slot residual and round-trips with R_T = 0, so nothing is lost by commensuration.
2. An anchor is found that settles a conservation question while moving when a frame pushes on it (breaks the anchor test, Fig 2).
3. Across a case set, a decision procedure that sums P and U into one number protects the hard-to-quantify values at least as reliably as a plural procedure that keeps them apart.
4. A figure re-census shows the two-currency rule of Fig 12 governs only bits and actions and does not recurse to valuation at a higher stratum.
5. A protected value is shown to be compensable at the margin without altering what the protecting community protects.

If any fires, the procedure is withdrawn or repaired. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Unbundle the question into D1 through D4 before searching for any weight or trade-off rule (F3).
2. Keep the currencies apart; compose them at the decision, never in a single total (F1).
3. Enumerate the habitat and its cascades rather than starting from a list of priceable services (F4).
4. Treat discount rates and weights as visible, violable conventions; declare the analyst's stake (F5).
5. Use measured ecological state and documented human judgment as two independent anchors; do not substitute reviewer agreement (F6).
6. Give protected values threshold or refusal standing, not a weight (F7).
7. Publish the round-trip gap of any index you report (F8).
8. Send the generator (the procedure and its refuter), not a recording of one decision (Fig 4 :22, 'Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.').
