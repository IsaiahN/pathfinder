# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` sections 3 and 5 have been re-checked against the verbatim figure text. The offer collapses on contact if the output does not survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about conservation valuation, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I work on conservation priority methods. I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` procedure, not `grounded`):** a conservation decision that holds only one monetary accounting cannot register an ecological value that has no unit in that accounting. The failure is silent, because the omitted side produces no error signal inside the frame that omits it. So a 'balance' between measurable human benefits and hard-to-quantify ecological values is not one number to be found. It is a union of two overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched. The defensible shape is a procedure that keeps the currencies apart, ranges each against its own anchor, enumerates the habitat and its cascades, makes its conventions visible and violable, and states in advance what would refute it. That is a shape and a route, not a result.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that ecological values are infinitely valuable, that no trade-off is ever legitimate, or that economics has no role. Those are straws. The claim is narrower: summing the two sides into one total is the error, not the fix, and a weighting that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this email:**
> 1. a single accounting that preserves every per-slot residual and round-trips with zero gap, so nothing is lost by commensuration;
> 2. an anchor that settles a conservation question while moving when a frame pushes on it;
> 3. a case set in which a single-number procedure protects the hard-to-quantify values at least as reliably as a plural procedure;
> 4. a re-census showing the two-currency rule governs only bits and actions and does not recurse to valuation.
>
> I wrote these down before contacting anyone. If any holds, I will retract or repair the claim. A one-line 'this fails because ...' is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I would value from you:** tell me what you would expect to see if the shape claim were true, and what you would count as a failure. Your verdict on this paragraph is the whole point of the message.
>
> **If it survives, and only then, here are the sources.** The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The taboo-trade-offs result in conservation: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4460479
> - IPBES on value monism versus pluralism: https://www.ipbes.net/contrasting-approaches-values-valuation
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the loop on a conservation problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

## Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the framework's interior their agreement is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
