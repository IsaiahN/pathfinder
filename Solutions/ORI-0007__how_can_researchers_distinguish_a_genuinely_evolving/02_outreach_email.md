# Outreach draft - check the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter survive the reader's own check. The output must stand without the framework or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One output to check: repetition versus evolution in a chemical flask

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual.** A growth curve, an oscillation trace or a mass spectrum can be produced by a reaction that merely repeats and by a chemical system that is genuinely evolving. The reading does not comment on its source. So the question is not "is it moving?" It is "which source produced this reading?"
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A discriminator between the two must have four properties. It must be **per species**, not an aggregate, because averaging hides the live signal. It must **track lineage**, because variation without heritable carry-forward is just noise. It must be **reach-sensitive**, because genuine evolution settles new arrangements that become inputs to further chemistry while a cycle revisits the same repertoire. And it must **resolve two sources that give the same reading**, because a null at one resolution is not proof of repetition. This is a shape an instrument must have, not a device I am naming.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a particular biosignature threshold settles the question; that the framework names the instrument; that lack of observed novelty proves absence. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Published criticism of single-threshold biosignatures is real and I am not asking you to set it aside.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. an aggregate observable that provably separates a confirmed repeating system from a confirmed evolving one across chemical networks, with no lineage information;
> 2. a confirmed evolving system whose set of composable settled species does not grow, where the null is not a budget limit;
> 3. a confirmed repeating cycle whose side products are heritable and accumulate differentially;
> 4. an instrument whose reading of the two systems is provably distinct at the same resolution with no lineage assumption.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will retract or rewrite the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - Isaiah
