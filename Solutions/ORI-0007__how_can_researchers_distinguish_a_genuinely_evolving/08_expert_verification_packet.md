# Expert verification packet - the Tether on chemical evolution versus repetition

**What this is:** a claim in a form a chemist, origins-of-life researcher or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A repeating chemical reaction is a closed cycle: its output re-enters as input and the repertoire does not grow, however long it runs. A genuinely evolving chemical system grows its reach by settling new molecular arrangements that become inputs to further chemistry, with heritable variation and differential persistence. The two can produce the same coarse reading, so a discriminator must be per species, lineage-bearing, reach-sensitive and source-discriminating; it must report a null as either compositional closure or unreachability at the current resolution, not as proof of absence. The framework returns this shape, not a named instrument and not a verdict about any particular flask.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13

- **Statement:** repetition is a closed cycle and closure adds no new atom; evolution grows the reach by settling new arrangements.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* Fig 13 :115 - *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **What to check:** is it true within the framework that a new molecule species is a new arrangement over held kinds rather than a new primitive, and that the open-endedness of arrangements is enough for reach growth without an import?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a repeating cycle whose repertoire of settled, composable species grows monotonically, or a confirmed evolving system whose reach does not grow where the null is not a budget limit, or a new settled species requiring a new primitive kind with no import.

### C2 - chemistry-facing; checkable against a real network or dataset

- **Statement:** activity, oscillation, autocatalysis and chaotic patterning do not discriminate. The discriminating signal is per species and requires lineage and settlement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 5 :49 - *"Machinery firing is not the milestone. A running system can mint and still return nothing new, which is exactly what the third guard exists to record."* Fig 7 :33 - *"variation inside, selection between"* Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **What to check:** in a known oscillatory or autocatalytic system such as the Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction, does any aggregate observable track a growing set of settled species? In a known evolving system such as serial-transfer RNA evolution, is the signal visible only in the per-species trajectory over rounds? Does an instrument that reads only the current state lose the distinction?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an aggregate observable that provably separates a confirmed repeating system from a confirmed evolving one with no lineage information, or a repeating cycle whose side products are heritable and accumulate differentially.

### C3 - measurement-facing; the source problem

- **Statement:** the same reading can come from two different sources, so a source-discriminating instrument is owed and a null has two meanings.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*
- **What to check:** is there a published instrument whose reading of a confirmed repeating system is provably distinct from its reading of a confirmed evolving system at the same resolution, with no prior lineage assumption? The candidate examined in WORLD, assembly-index mass spectrometry, has a published dispute attached: a 2024 Royal Society Interface paper reports abiotic mineral structures exceeding the proposed MA = 15 divide.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate such an instrument. That closes the residual at the ground and moves this claim to `grounded`. Equivalently, show that a null observation at finite resolution licenses a proof of absence, which voids the two-meanings claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any particular chemical system is alive or evolving.
- Not that assembly theory, mass spectrometry or serial transfer is the answer. Each is a candidate with provenance, not a figure-derived device.
- Not that a single threshold, including MA = 15, separates life from abiotic chemistry.
- Not that oscillation, autocatalysis, complexity or self-organisation is enough.
- Not that absence of observed novelty proves absence of evolution.
- Not a derivation of life from chemistry, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A per-species measurement resolving descendants across rounds in a network whose repetition status is independently known.
2. A source test: the same instrument yields provably different readings for a known repeating system and a known evolving system at the same resolution.
3. A reach test: the set of settled, composable species grows in one case and returns to a repertoire in the other, with side products excluded by a stated rule.
4. A demonstrated aggregate discriminator (a refuter of C2).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :121, Fig 8 :32, Fig 13 :115 and Fig 1 :22. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against one system you know well: does an aggregate reading lose the distinction between a cycle and a lineage?
3. Name one instrument that would give different readings for the two sources at the same resolution, or say why none is possible at current resolution.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
