## 0. The residual in effect terms

A chemical reaction network is observed over time. The same coarse readings can be produced by two different sources:

- **Option A, genuine evolution:** new molecular arrangements appear, are retained, are copied with variation, and the variants differ in persistence under the same conditions. The set of composable, settled species grows.
- **Option B, mere repetition:** a cycle or oscillator returns to the same repertoire. Concentrations rise and fall, autocatalysis amplifies, even chaos appears, but no new heritable arrangement is admitted and the trajectory revisits its states.

The residual is not "is it moving?" Both options move. The residual is **which source produced the reading**. This is the metamerism problem: a reading that cannot comment on its source. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states it as *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and warns that *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"): a **source-discriminating observable** that takes one value for a closed loop and a different value for an open lineage. The rest of this audit derives the required shape of that observable. It does not name the instrument; that is owed from the ground.

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two competing accounts of one reading, so a discrimination, not a measurement of a single quantity. Symmetry: a repeating cycle is symmetric under time translation over its period (the state at t returns at t plus period); an evolutionary lineage is not, because earlier states are not recovered. Scale: the reading is taken at one scale (concentrations over time), the source operates at another (which species are composable and heritable). The round trip between them is lossy and needs a measured gap (Fig 4 :30).

## 2. Findings

### F1 - A repeating reaction is a cycle, and closure adds no new atom

Fig 12 :121:

> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Fig 8 :32:

> **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

Fig 13 :118:

> **"So a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach."**

An autocatalytic cycle or a Belousov-Zhabotinsky oscillator is the figure's cycle. Its output re-enters as input and the loop closes. However long it runs, composition inside the closed set adds no new member. Unbounded activity is compatible with an unchanged reach. Fig 7 :32 names the result: *"one long room: motion, no progress."*

This is the first half of the discriminator: **repetition is compatible with unbounded time and a fixed repertoire**. Duration alone cannot distinguish A from B.

- **Mode:** `general` (structural) for the closure claim; `specified` for its application to chemical oscillators.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a repeating chemical cycle whose repertoire of settled, composable species grows monotonically across cycles without any import. That would break the identification of repetition with a fixed reach.

### F2 - Evolution grows the reach by settling new arrangements

Fig 12 :103 and :104:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**
> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

Fig 13 :115:

> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

At the chemical level, a new molecule is a new **arrangement** over held building blocks, not a new primitive kind. Once settled against the ground it becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. Because the arrangements do not run out while the kinds do, open-ended novelty is possible without any new kind being introduced. The reach grows by accepted and composable terms (Formula step 8: *"IT IS THE REACH THAT DRIVES THE LOOP, NOT THE LIBRARY"*).

This is the second half of the discriminator: **genuine evolution shows a growing reach, a widening set of settled species that serve as inputs to further composition**. A system whose library grows while its reach does not move is counting the denominator, not evolving in the sense the question asks about.

- **Mode:** `general` for the recursion; `specified` for the chemical reading.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a system with confirmed heritable variation and differential persistence whose set of composable settled species does not grow over the observation window, and show that this is not a budget limit (Fig 8 :39-43: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*, and underivable-in-principle differs from unreachable-at-this-budget).

### F3 - Novelty alone is not evolution; variation must be heritable and selected

Fig 5 :49:

> **"Machinery firing is not the milestone. A running system can mint and still return nothing new, which is exactly what the third guard exists to record."**

Fig 7 :33:

> **"variation inside, selection between"**

Fig 11 :36:

> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**

A reaction that throws off many side products has variation inside. That is not enough. Evolution needs the second factor: selection between, with the variant carried forward. Fig 11 :56: *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* In a chemical system there is no seat choosing the winner; the ground keeps what persists. So the discriminator must include **lineage**: does a variant appear, persist differentially under fixed conditions, and seed the next round?

The three Darwinian conditions named in the world literature (variation, differential fitness, heritability) are corroboration, not the source of this verdict. They arrive in WORLD as a secondary witness.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Refuter:** show a repeating cycle whose side products are heritable and accumulate differentially across rounds. Then repetition and evolution collapse into one category and this finding fails.

### F4 - The measurement must be per species, not aggregate

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

A global observable (total mass, total rate, mean molecular weight, overall colour) is a single slot average. Both Option A and Option B can hold it steady or drive it in the same direction. The signal that separates them lives in individual molecular species and their descendants. So the required observable is **per slot**: identity and abundance of each species across rounds, with lineage recorded. A source-discriminating instrument reads each slot against an anchor that does not move when the researcher changes their interpretation.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Refuter:** exhibit an aggregate observable that provably separates the two sources for all chemical networks of interest. That would make the per-slot requirement unnecessary.

### F5 - The source is not in the reading; an instrument is owed

Fig 9 :27-28:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Fig 6 :34:

> **"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."**

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The growth curve, the oscillation trace and the mass spectrum of a repeating system can be indiscernible from those of an evolving one at the current resolution. Indiscernibility is not identity. The framework's own limit: it returns *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). So the honest output of this derivation is the **shape of the discriminator**, not a named device. Assembly-index spectroscopy and serial-transfer lineage tracking are candidate instruments from WORLD; the figures do not select among them.

- **Mode:** `general` for the limit; `specified` for the chemical case.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate an instrument, at any resolution, whose reading of a confirmed repeating system is provably distinct from its reading of a confirmed evolving system, with no lineage assumption. That would close the residual at the ground and move this file to `grounded`.

### F6 - False positives: activity is not the milestone

Fig 5 :49 (above) and Fig 4 :25-26:

> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."**

A system can replay a past success indefinitely and look like it is maintaining itself. A catalyst that is regenerated unchanged (Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*) is the repeating case. A replicator whose differences are carried forward is the evolving case. Because "nothing records which a term is", the observer must add the record; the system will not supply it.

- **Mode:** `specified`.
- **Refuter:** show that a spent and regenerating part and a heritable variant are discriminable without an external lineage record. Then the "nothing records which" clause is wrong for chemistry.

### F7 - The anchor is the ground, not agreement among readers

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Fig 2 :40:

> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 11 :40:

> **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Ten chemists agreeing that a flask "looks alive" is one evidence pool speaking ten times. The anchor for this question is the experiment that can fire: a measurement of per-species identity across rounds, with a perturbation that can refuse the hypothesis. Buying more agreement does not buy independence (Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*).

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Refuter:** show that consensus among independent instruments with disjoint evidence pools can settle the question without a discriminating measurement. Then the anchor requirement is relaxed for this case.

### F8 - Unreachable here is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 13 :134:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

If no new species is observed in a window, two different facts remain: the system may be compositionally closed (underivable in principle without import), or the new species may be in the reach and unfound at this resolution (unreachable at this budget). Fig 8 :41-43: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* The first calls for OUTWARD, the second for more SEARCHED, not an import. The report must say which, and if it cannot, it must say so.

- **Mode:** `general`.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where a null observation at finite resolution licenses a proof of absence. Then this distinction is void for that case.

## 2b. The three routes kept distinct (Formula step 7)

When no line closes the residual directly, the formula asks three questions, not one. They are reported separately.

- **SEARCHED, asked first and cheapest.** Is the discriminating observable already in the current reach and unfound? Same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited. Concretely: are existing time-series methods (per-species abundance, lineage tracking, assembly-index mass spectrometry) already sufficient at current resolution? This is where the WORLD search lands, and it is the only one of the three that has been attempted here.
- **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. Sharper mass spectrometry, longer observation windows, single-molecule tracking. Built from a worse instrument already returning a reading (Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*). Not attempted here.
- **OUTWARD.** Import a frame whose closure predicts the shape of R. Characterise R in effect terms first (done in section 0), then look up a frame. The frame must pass a source gate and a shadow test and be debited against independence (Fig 6 :11-14, Fig 8 :9-13). The evolutionary-biology frame (variation, selection, heredity) and the assembly-theory frame are the two candidates examined in WORLD. Neither is asserted as the answer.

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and were never read against each other.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single coarse, aggregate observable is demonstrated to separate a confirmed repeating system from a confirmed evolving system across chemical networks of interest, with no lineage or per-species information (breaks F4 and F5).
2. A confirmed evolving chemical system is shown to have a non-growing reach over the observation window, where the null is not a budget limit (breaks F2).
3. A confirmed repeating cycle is shown to produce heritable, differentially retained variations that accumulate (breaks F1 and F3, collapses the distinction).
4. A new settled species appears that is not a composition over held atoms and is not an import from a frame with a different closure (breaks Fig 12 :104 and Fig 13 :115).
5. An instrument is demonstrated whose reading of a repeating system is provably distinct from its reading of an evolving system at the same resolution, with no prior lineage assumption (closes F5 at the ground and moves the result to `grounded`).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or rewritten. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual, not the framework: the same reading can come from a closed loop or an open lineage (F5).
2. State the required shape of the discriminator before naming any instrument: per-slot, lineage-bearing, reach-sensitive, anchored to a measurement (F1 to F4).
3. Treat any single-threshold biosignature claim as a candidate, not a verdict. The world literature records a real dispute about abiotic mimics (F6, F5).
4. Report a null as "unreachable at this resolution" or "compositionally closed", with the distinction stated, not as "no life" (F8).
5. Ask a checker what they would count as a failure before showing them the theory (F7, Fig 10 :42).
6. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, the shape claim and the refuter, not a conclusion.

## 6. Verdict

**The shape survives; the instrument is not named.** A repeating reaction is a closed cycle over a fixed repertoire, and no amount of duration adds a new atom (F1). A genuinely evolving chemical system grows the reach by settling new arrangements that become atoms for further composition (F2). The discriminator must detect heritable variation with differential persistence (F3), read per species rather than as an aggregate (F4), and resolve two sources that produce the same reading (F5). Activity is not the milestone (F6), agreement is not the anchor (F7), and a null is a verdict with two different meanings, not silence (F8).

This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. It is triangulated across Fig 12, Fig 13, Fig 8, Fig 1, Fig 5, Fig 7 and Fig 11 for the structural claims; single-source where only one figure line governs. One strain was found and resolved by re-census (atoms versus arrangements). No figure line was forced.
