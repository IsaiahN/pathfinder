## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what would discriminate option A (a chemical system undergoing cumulative, heritable, selectable change) from option B (a reaction that repeats a fixed repertoire), given that both can produce the same coarse reading of growth, oscillation, autocatalysis and patterned behaviour. What each leads to: treating activity as the discriminator yields repeated false positives, because a nonlinear oscillator is active and not evolving; treating the source as the discriminator yields a shape for an instrument, an explicit refuter, and an honest stop when no instrument is available.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union. Order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two competing accounts of one reading, so a discrimination rather than a single quantity. Symmetry: a repeating cycle is symmetric under time translation over its period, because its states recur; a lineage is not, because earlier states are not recovered. Scale: the reading is taken over time at one scale, the source concerns which arrangements are composable and heritable at another. Fig 9 names a union compound: compositional openness and dynamical return are independent conditions.
- **Domain 1, chemical composition.** Actors: molecules, bonds, catalysts, food molecules. Currency: which arrangements compose and settle. Anchor: the experiment that admits a species. Union member, not a partition cell.
- **Domain 2, temporal dynamics.** Actors: concentrations, variants, oscillations, perturbations. Currency: persistence across rounds and return to prior states. Anchor: per-species measurement over time. Union member.
- **Domain 3, observation and instruments.** Actors: instrument, reading, source, lineage record. Currency: what the reading can discriminate. Anchor: the ground, which does not move with a frame. Dependency: perception before vocabulary before objective (Fig 3).
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers, claim, refuter. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: detect the cycle and refuse the repetition claim before minting a novelty claim (Formula step 2); settle species before counting them (Formula step 5); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the instrument is not named. Repetition is a closed cycle over a fixed repertoire, and duration adds no atom. Genuine evolution grows the reach by settling new arrangements that become atoms for further composition, with heritable variation and differential persistence. The discriminator must read per species, track lineage, count only settled and composable species, resolve two sources that produce the same reading, and report a null as either compositional closure or unreachability at the current resolution. Activity, oscillation, autocatalysis, complexity and self-organisation are false positives. Assembly theory is a candidate instrument with a published dispute, not an answer. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result at a gap: the figures return the shape of the instrument and stop.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states three sub-claims with refuters and names the false-positive class explicitly, so an error is findable. The real cost is sending a named instrument or a single-threshold verdict that fails on abiotic mimics; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate instruments are removable without touching the derivation, and the refusal of single-threshold claims is already in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can replace this one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement giving provably different readings for a confirmed repeating system and a confirmed evolving system at the same resolution would close the residual and move the result to `grounded`; a confirmed evolving system with a non-growing reach, or a repeating cycle with accumulating heritable variants, would break the shape; an aggregate discriminator would void the per-slot requirement. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
