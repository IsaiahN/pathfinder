## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* - the framework can narrow what the missing thing must be like without naming it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is: a discriminator that is per species, lineage-bearing, reach-sensitive and source-discriminating. The name of the instrument is not in that list.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* That is the effect term used to bring the chemistry case into the framework. Nothing was assumed about which molecules or which flask.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is at the shape, not at a device.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measurement that actually separates a confirmed repeating system from a confirmed evolving one would name the discriminator.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Darwinian triplet and assembly theory are candidates examined in `09_watson_answer.md`; neither is asserted as the answer.

*The Library of Babel* adds a warning about *"Just In Time Learning"*: keeping a respectable distance from the details lets you see connections without losing fidelity. That is a reason to derive the shape before reading the instrument literature closely, and a reason to check the shape afterwards.

## 4. The central essay for this question: source, not behaviour

*The Hard Problem is About Source* is directly on point. An instrument that detects green *"cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."* Then:

> **"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"**
> **"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"**
> **"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."**

Applied here: a growth curve is the green reading. A closed autocatalytic cycle and an open evolutionary lineage are two of the possible sources. The essay's own resolution was empirical, not argumentative: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* That is why this solution stops at the shape of the better instrument and refuses to name one from derivation alone.

## 5. What agreement is worth here

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* In this folder the second seat reads the same figure text and the same essays as the primary. Its agreement is one evidence pool talking. It is recorded as internal consistency in `11_audit.md`, never as corroboration.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige is not equal to actions." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question the practical form is: a well-known group asserting that a flask is alive does not settle the discrimination. A lesser-known lab with a source-discriminating measurement does. The contact list in `03_public_contact_points.csv` is therefore built from public, opt-in institutional venues, not from famous names, and no recipient is ranked by likely agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*Arrival of the Fittest*: outputs produced by prompting an LLM with the formula "must be seen as simulacra" and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the discrimination question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction to an earlier reading

An earlier draft of this file treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any molecule. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming an arrangement already in the reach, or a candidate instrument found in another field, is a binding or an import with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the derivation itself produced the name. Both the narrowed and the original reading are kept here so neither is quietly lost.
