## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The Darwinian triplet is the field's own operational test.** Directed evolution states variation, differential fitness and heritability explicitly. Natural selection gives the same three processes in Darwin's formulation. Both corroborate F3 of the audit. They do not decide the case, because a repeating autocatalytic set can show variation without heritable carry-forward.

2. **Serial transfer makes a lineage visible.** Spiegelman's Monster is the cleanest public demonstration that the discriminator is a trajectory, not a snapshot: the RNA changed across rounds in a direction set by the selection regime. A single time point would not have shown it. This corroborates the per-slot, lineage-bearing requirement (F3, F4).

3. **Oscillation is a genuine false positive.** The BZ reaction is far from equilibrium, autocatalytic, patterned and chaotic, and it is not evolving. Activity, complexity of behaviour and self-organisation are not sufficient. This corroborates F6 and the *"Machinery firing is not the milestone"* line.

4. **Assembly index is a candidate instrument with a live dispute.** It is measurable by mass spectrometry and published claims connect it to selection and evolution. The 2024 criticism (abiotic minerals above the threshold; the contention that only life reaches MA >= 15 is an error) is exactly the Fig 8 :32 warning applied to a real instrument: closure over abiotic chemistry can reach readings that a naive threshold reads as biological. Do not present it as a settled discriminator. Present it as a SEARCHED candidate whose source gate is still open.

5. **The definition of life used by the field is already evolutionary.** NASA's "self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution" makes this question prior to any life-detection claim. That is an anchor from the field's own vocabulary, not from the framework.
