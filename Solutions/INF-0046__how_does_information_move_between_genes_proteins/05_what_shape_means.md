## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INF-0046 the **shape** is the per-boundary structure that any checkable answer must have: a bonded carrier, a lossy crossing, an ordered arrow, a non-updating anchor, a cycle, and an enumerated habitat. A **name** would be a specific molecular pathway presented as figure-derived. `BOUNDARY(CARRIER, CURRENCY, LOSS, ANCHOR)` is a shape; `gene → RNA → protein → phenotype` is a name borrowed from biology.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here this means an input-output measurement at the cellular boundary (Lan and Tu 2016), or a fitness measurement at the population boundary.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming transcription or methylation is like that: the biology frame holds the atom; the framework returns the cross-kind composition shape into which it can be bound.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the cross-kind composition rule is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning it into "the mechanism by which genes instruct proteins" converts a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a source-naming reading is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

In INF-0046 the parallel is exact. A mutual-information number between an environmental cue and a behavioural output is a reading of green: many molecular mechanisms can produce the same reading. Naming one mechanism from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming transcription, translation, selection? CORRECTION TO MY OWN §1 AND §4

**Answer: no. Correction kept visible.**

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive **not held in Γ**.
- Cross-kind composition, the bond, the cycle and the membrane are already among the framework's held atoms and rules (Fig 12, Fig 13, Fig 4). Transcription, translation and selection are atoms held by the **biology frame**, available as bindings.
- ⇒ Naming them is **not** forbidden. It is a binding from the biology frame onto a held rule. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh operator, or claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- **I overstated this earlier** by treating any biological name as a forbidden filling of the slot. Retracted as applied to held bindings. Triangulated (README + Fig 12 + Fig 6).
- My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: naming the molecular source of a mutual-information reading without a discriminator is still the error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous biology labs that agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any biological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The one-paragraph shape

> The shape is relational, lossy, cyclic and grounded. What crosses a biological boundary is a bonded carrier, not a substance; the bond carries the correspondence the operands do not. Each crossing throws information away, so what arrives is a method or a head start rather than a replay. The four crossings form a cycle with feedback, and the feedback that runs environment to lineage is selection, not sequence information flowing backwards. Which cue matters is relative to a residual and must be enumerated from the habitat, not read off a standing list. Whether any transfer was functional is settled by a ground that does not update on the description of it. No figure line names a molecular pathway; those names are bindings from the biology frame and are recorded as provenance, not as derivations. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
