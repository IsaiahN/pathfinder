# The Tether's answer: how information moves across biological scales

**Mode: `specified` on the per-boundary shape and verdicts, `general` on the structural topology, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a biological result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how "information" moves between five stops. In effect terms: a change at one scale produces a change at another that the receiving scale's prediction did not account for, and we need to know what crossed, in what currency, what was lost, and what settles whether the crossing was functional. That residual is indexed per boundary (Fig 1 :19, :22; Symbols :43), not pooled into one flow rate. It is a union of overlapping conditions with feedback, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57), so each boundary gets its own loop and its own verdict.

## 2. The shape

1. **What crosses is a bonded carrier, not a substance.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The sequence-to-product correspondence, the receptor-to-response correspondence and the cue-to-behaviour correspondence are typed relations. Ingredient lists do not record them. `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 + Symbols table).

2. **Every crossing is lossy and directional.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* What arrives at the next scale is a re-applicable method or a head start, never a replay of the occasion. The round-trip gap `R_T` must be measured, not assumed zero (Fig 4 :30). `general` on the topology.

3. **The arrows are typed and ordered, not one sequence.** Production ⇒ for sequence to product, sequence plus feedback → for sensing, comparison ⋛ for thresholds, negation ¬ for refusal of a cue (Operators table). Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Dependency order: correspondence, then cellular input-output, then multicellular coordination, then the population loop. `specified`.

4. **The topology is a cycle, not a five-stop line.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* Selection carries environment to lineage; niche construction carries organism back to environment. These are **not** sequence information running protein to nucleic acid. Cobb 2017: *"Once information has got into a protein it can't get out again."* Sequence order and selection pressure are different quantities with different anchors. `general` on the cycle, `specified` per arrow.

5. **The anchor does not update.** Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* For the cell the anchor is the measured input-output relation and survival; for the population it is fitness. Lan and Tu 2016: information theory *"does not reveal the underlying mechanism"* nor *"which information is important for the organism."* Reliability is a channel property: Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* `specified`.

6. **Which cues matter is residual-relative and enumerated from the habitat.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* `general` on the rule, `specified` per habitat.

7. **At the origin boundary, interpretation does not need an interpreter.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* `specified`; no prebiotic measurement exists.

8. **The molecular mechanism list is a gap.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* Naming transcription, translation, phosphorylation, synaptic transmission or niche construction is a binding from the biology frame onto held atoms, provenance required, not a figure derivation. `gap`, unreached at this budget and reported as a verdict rather than silence (Fig 8 :39).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that information is a substance that flows unchanged. What crosses is a bonded carrier across a lossy membrane.
- Not that a single "information flow" number covers all five stops. Per-slot indexing is mandatory.
- Not that channel capacity or mutual information tells an organism which information matters. Those price the description, not the action (Fig 12 :125-126).
- Not that the central dogma is contradicted by niche construction. Different quantities, different bonds, different anchors.
- Not that the framework derives any molecular pathway. Those names are bindings with provenance.
- Not `grounded`. No biological measurement was taken by this agent.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Bond test:** recover the full message at the receiving scale after destroying the typed relation and supplying no new one.
- **Round-trip test:** measure a cross-scale transform with `R_T = 0` in both directions.
- **Cycle test:** show the crossings form an acyclic chain with no feedback in any regime.
- **Anchor test:** show the ground changes its answer when a frame's description changes, or that it lists one of the parties.
- **Slot test:** show all boundaries share one residual that trends down under a single intervention.
- **Instrument test:** show with an instrument that a specific molecular pathway or interpreter is required by the framework rather than borrowed from biology.

Standing refuters from `01` §4 are the same set, written before the conclusion.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not add a fifth stop to the central dogma and does not claim a single fluid called information. It says that what crosses a biological boundary is a physical carrier joined to a receiving process by a typed relation, and that the relation, not the ingredient list, is where the correspondence lives. Each crossing is lossy, so what arrives is a method or a head start rather than a replay. The four crossings are ordered by dependency and form a cycle: sequence information runs to protein, selection runs from environment to lineage, and niche construction runs from organism back to environment. Which cues matter is relative to a residual and is enumerated from the habitat, not read off a standing list; the anchor that decides whether any of it was functional does not move when a description of it changes. No figure line names a molecular pathway, so those names are recorded as bindings from biology rather than as derivations. What the framework offers is the shape any checkable answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The mechanism stops open until the ground settles it.
