Arity: four boundaries, not five stops, and the boundaries are slots to be indexed separately. Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric, because the production arrow (sequence to product) and the selection arrow (environment to lineage) are different kinds of arrow. Scale: molecular to cellular to organismal to environmental/population, each a membrane crossing. Question type: union with feedback, not partition.

- **Domain 2, cellular.** Actors: receptor, kinase, regulatory RNA. Currency: concentration and information measures. Anchor: the input-output measurement and survival. Signal/noise partition with a channel loop.

Order by dependency: type the correspondence bond before measuring the input-output relation; settle the cell before composing over it at the organism level; run the population loop last because its operands are settled organismal terms.
