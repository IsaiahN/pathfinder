## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that information moves along a five-stop line from genes to proteins to cells to organisms to environments. Option A: accept the ordinary picture of a substance flowing one way and offer a single flow account. Option B: refuse the substance metaphor, treat the crossings as a union of bonded, lossy, typed and cyclic boundaries with per-slot residuals and non-updating anchors, and report the molecular mechanism list as an unreached gap. What each leads to: A aggregates away the live signal (Fig 1 :22), mistakes a recording for a generator (Fig 4 :25) and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A change at scale s produces a change at scale s+1 that the receiving scale's prediction did not account for. Arity: four boundaries, indexed per slot, never pooled. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric; sequence information runs to protein, selection runs from environment to lineage, niche construction runs from organism to environment. Scale: molecular, cellular, organismal, environmental and generational crossings of the membrane between sizes.
- **Domain 1, gene to protein.** Actors: DNA, RNA, ribosome, tRNA, amino acids. Currency: sequence order carried by a production bond. Anchor: the observed sequence correspondence and the cell that survives on the product. Question type: a binding from the biology frame onto a held cross-kind composition, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 2, protein to cell.** Actors: receptors, kinases, methylation enzymes, regulatory RNAs. Currency: concentration, mutual information, channel capacity, transfer entropy. Anchor: the input-output measurement and cell survival. Question type: signal versus noise, a channel-maintenance loop.
- **Domain 3, cell to organism.** Actors: tissues, endocrine and neural signals, behaviour. Currency: coordinated signals and actions. Anchor: organismal survival and reproduction. Question type: level crossing; a settled molecule becomes an atom.
- **Domain 4, organism to environment.** Actors: conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency), other organisms. Currency: selection pressure, ecological inheritance, niche construction. Anchor: the environment, which does not update on the organism's belief. Question type: union of conditions, habitat enumerated.
- **Domain 5, cross-generation.** Actors: lineages, germ line, small RNAs, chromatin state. Currency: heritable variation and transmitted state. Anchor: fitness, what the ground kept. Question type: unmaintained habitat, selection ran.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Protein is a product in D1, a signal in D2, a trait in D4 and a transmitted cause in D5. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle the correspondence bond before the cellular input-output relation; settle the cell before the organism; the environmental ground anchors all. Refuse the one-substance line before minting a mechanism (Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: mapping genes and proteins directly onto the six physical terms of Fig 13 strains the text. Re-census: Fig 13 governs the physical substrate; at the biological scale only actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :20). No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The ordinary five-stop line is refused. What crosses a biological boundary is a bonded carrier; the bond carries the correspondence the operands do not. Every crossing is lossy, so a method or head start arrives, never a replay. The crossings are typed and ordered by dependency, and they form a cycle in which sequence information, selection pressure and niche construction are distinct arrows. The ground does not update, cue valence is residual-relative, and the habitat is enumerated rather than composed. At the origin boundary selection ran without an interpreter. No figure line names a molecular pathway, so the mechanism list is a `gap`, unreached at this budget and reported as a verdict rather than silence. Per-domain verdicts are `specified`, the cycle topology is `general`, nothing is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, records five refuters before the conclusion and keeps the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording of one conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The substance metaphor, the single flow rate and the standing cue list are removable without touching the cross-scale composition rule. A retracted claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no framework primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be corrected because the record holds the refuter (Fig 10 :62, *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."*).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A bond-destruction experiment that still recovers the message; a measured round-trip gap of zero; a demonstration that the crossings are acyclic; a ground that moves when the frame's description changes; one residual shared by all boundaries under a single intervention; or an instrument that discriminates among molecular sources of the same reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` (Fig 2 :29 anchors the test).
