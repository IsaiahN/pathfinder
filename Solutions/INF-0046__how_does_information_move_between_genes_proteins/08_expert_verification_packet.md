# Expert verification packet - the Tether on cross-scale biological information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a biologist, biophysicist or information theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-boundary shape, `general` on the cycle topology, `gap` on the molecular mechanism list. Not `grounded`.** No biological measurement is claimed by this agent. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Information does not move between genes, proteins, cells, organisms and environments as a substance along a line. What crosses each boundary is a physical carrier joined to a receiving process by a typed relation, and the relation carries the correspondence the operands do not. Each crossing is a lossy transform, so what arrives is a method or a head start, not a replay. The four crossings are ordered by dependency (correspondence, cellular input-output, multicellular coordination, population loop) and form a cycle: sequence information runs to protein, selection runs from environment to lineage, and niche construction runs from organism back to environment. Which cues matter is relative to a residual and enumerated from a habitat; the anchor that settles whether a transfer was functional does not update on any frame's description of it. The framework returns this shape and refuses to name a molecular pathway; those names are bindings from the biology frame, recorded as provenance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 4

- **Statement:** the bond carries the cross-scale information, and the transform is lossy, so a recording of one occasion cannot cross as knowledge.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 4 :27 - *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- **What to check:** is the typed relation, rather than the molecular ingredient list, what actually carries the sequence-to-product or cue-to-response correspondence in the systems you know?
- **Refuter:** recover the full message at the receiving scale after destroying the typed relation and supplying no new one; or measure a cross-scale round trip with zero loss in both directions.

### C2 - biology-facing; checkable against the molecular and cellular literature

- **Statement:** the crossings are typed and ordered, and the topology is a cycle in which sequence-order information and selection pressure are distinct quantities.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Operators table :50 defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 11 :36 - *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*
- **What to check:** is sequence information genuinely one-way (protein to nucleic acid transfer of sequence order is not established), while niche construction and ecological inheritance give a separate environment-to-organism feedback? Does treating them as different quantities dissolve the apparent conflict?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a well-documented protein-to-nucleic-acid transfer of sequence-order information; or show the four crossings are an acyclic chain with no feedback in any regime. Then the cycle reading falls.

### C3 - framework-internal with a biology anchor; checkable against input-output measurements

- **Statement:** reliability is a channel-maintenance problem and the ground does not update. Per-slot residuals must not be averaged, and a standing list of useful cues is impossible.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*; Fig 2 :33 - *"The anchor must not update."*
- **What to check:** in a system you know, does a cue's valence flip when the residual changes, and is the failure attributable to the channel rather than to the ground? Would a pooled flow rate hide which boundary is live?
- **Refuter:** show a ground that changes its answer when the describing frame changes, or that all four boundaries share one residual trending down under a single intervention.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that information is a substance, or that there is one universal flow rate.
- Not that channel capacity, mutual information or transfer entropy tells an organism which information matters. Bits price the description; actions price finding out whether it holds (Fig 12 :125-126).
- Not that the central dogma is refuted by epigenetics or niche construction. Those are different currencies.
- Not that the framework derives transcription, translation, phosphorylation, synaptic transmission or niche construction. They are bindings with provenance, not figure results.
- Not `grounded`. This agent took no measurement.
- Not a claim that the shape is unique or final. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here: (1) the bond-destruction test; (2) a measured round-trip gap, zero or nonzero; (3) a test for feedback that would show the cycle closing; (4) an anchor test, does the ground move; (5) an instrument that discriminates among candidate molecular sources of the same mutual-information reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
