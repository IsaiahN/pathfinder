# Expert verification packet: the Tether on computation and observers

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in foundations of computation can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating instrument is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is computation a property of the system or of the observer's mapping?" is a union compound, not a partition. Any ascription needs three terms: a system with causal organization, a mapping that is a pose-dependent frame transform, and a ground that settles the mapping. Pure intrinsic accounts drop the pose; pure observer-relative accounts drop the ground and trivialize, since an unrestricted mapping makes a rock under the sun a NOT gate. The bond carries the information the states do not, so the rock and the gate stand as isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The framework returns this shape and five falsifiers; it does not name which of the two traditional positions is correct, and the binary stays `unreached` until a discriminator is applied.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 2 and the Symbols table
- **Statement:** the observer's mapping is real and requires a pose, but it cannot be the ground. A flip of the mapping with all physical facts fixed cannot flip the ascription.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Symbols table :82: *"Cannot be run without a pose."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
- **Refuter:** a case where redrawing the mapping changes the computational status while every physical fact and intervention outcome stays fixed; or a description of states that yields a unique input/output partition with no vantage.

### C2: philosophy-of-computation-facing; checkable against the accounts of concrete computation
- **Statement:** an unrestricted mapping is insufficient. A genuine ascription needs a restriction to counterfactual or causal support, which is the figure's typed bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*
- **What to check:** does the simple mapping account really make the rock implement a NOT gate under an unrestricted mapping, and do the restricted accounts (counterfactual, causal, mechanistic) block it? Is the figure's typed bond a faithful structural analogue of those restrictions, or does it add something they lack?
- **Refuter:** an unrestricted mapping with no counterfactual or causal support that is nevertheless sufficient for a genuine computation ascription; or two systems with identical states and identical typed bonds that differ in computational status.

### C3: framework-internal derivation with a world-facing nose
- **Statement:** the three terms are ordered by dependency (states, mapping, ground), and the missing discriminator is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."* Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."* Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **Refuter:** an argument that discriminates the two accounts from a shared behavioural trace with no instrument and no residual to sharpen against; or a derivation of the discriminator purely inside one frame.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that computation is purely intrinsic or purely observer-relative. Both drop a term.
- Not that the framework settles the philosophical debate. Fig 8 :37: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
- Not that a particular mapping is the correct one. The figure returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that the rock does not compute in any sense. The claim is narrower: under an unrestricted mapping it does, and that is the reduction the figures forbid.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument has settled the binary.
- Not that agreement among readers is corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not an argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. **Intervention test:** does the ascription track intervention-invariant behaviour rather than the observer's redescription?
2. **Bond test:** can two systems with the same state sequence be shown to differ in the typed bond, as the rock and the gate differ in whether transitions are self-produced or externally driven?
3. **Counterfactual test:** does the physical transition support the counterfactuals the claimed computation requires?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29, Fig 12 :5 and :46, and Symbols table :79-82: four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the accounts of concrete computation you know: is the typed bond a faithful analogue of the causal or counterfactual restriction, or a restatement of it?
3. Name one instrument or intervention that would distinguish an imposed mapping from a causal organization that supports counterfactuals, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
