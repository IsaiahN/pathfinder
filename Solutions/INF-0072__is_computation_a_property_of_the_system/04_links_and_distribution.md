## What in this literature bears directly on the question

1. **The simple mapping account and its trivialization.** The SEP on physical computation defines the simple mapping account and reports that it attributes many computations to many systems, so that every physical system of enough complexity implements every computation from a broad class. The Wikipedia page attributes the same objection to Putnam and Searle. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 10 :49, *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
2. **The restricted accounts are the "better instrument."** Counterfactual, causal and mechanistic accounts restrict acceptable mappings. The SEP's rock case shows the restriction doing work: the rock's transitions are not due to the rock's own causal powers. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 12 :5, *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information."*
3. **The observer-relative claim and its critics.** The SEP Chinese Room entry quotes Searle: *"Computation exists only relative to some agent or observer who imposes a computational interpretation on some phenomenon."* The same entry reports Block and Haugeland replying that a wall is not a computer because the implementation must support counterfactuals and right causal powers. Both sides are needed for the union reading. Cite this entry when explaining why the framework refuses to pick a winner.

- **Seat rule:** an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying. Declare the stake in any cover note.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, with a third stage

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. Send the residual, the shape and the refuter. No theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded, because once they hold the theory their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* So each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the output is a shape plus falsifiers, not a verdict between the two camps. If a reader shows that one of the five falsifiers fires, stop and retract before offering anything else.
