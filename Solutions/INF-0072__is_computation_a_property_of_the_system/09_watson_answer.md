## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether computation belongs to a system itself or to how an observer maps its states to inputs and outputs. Option A (intrinsic) leads to a property with no role for the pose and no account of why one mapping rather than another is correct. Option B (observer-relative) leads to a property with no resistor to an arbitrary mapping and therefore to the trivialization result documented in the world literature (a rock under the sun implements a NOT gate under the simple mapping account). What each option omits: A omits the mapping, B omits the ground.

- **R (the residual).** The disputed predicate "computation." Arity: three terms, a system with states and transitions, a mapping to inputs and outputs, and a ground that settles the mapping. Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric. The mapping is a pose-dependent transform and the frame ranges against the ground, never the reverse (Fig 2 :45). Scale: system and frame at one scale, ground at a different logical type (it does not update, Fig 10 :22).
- **Domain 1, the physical system.** Actors: states, transitions, physical components. Currency: physical state change. Anchor: an instrument reading what the system does under intervention (Fig 2 :29). Nameable, yes.
- **Domain 2, the observer / mapping frame.** Actors: the observer, the mapping F, the pose. Currency: mapping states to inputs and outputs. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows Domain 1's ground. Nameable, no independent anchor. F is "the frame transform" and "Cannot be run without a pose" (Symbols table :80-82).
- **Domain 3, the ground / verification frame.** Actors: instruments, experiments, the researcher. Currency: residuals settled against the anchor. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not update. Nameable, yes.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound, not a partition. Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"; Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A single lever, declaring a mapping, clears the region where only Domain 2 holds and leaves the intersections standing because Domains 1 and 3 still bind.
- **Order by dependency.** Track states, then map them, then settle the mapping against the ground (Fig 3 :20). A claim of computation before the ground link exists is a reading below the break (Fig 3 :22-23).

Strain test applied: the anchor rule (Fig 2) and the pose-dependent F (Symbols table) seem to strain. - **Philosophy of mind / computation.** Michael Rescorla, "The Computational Theory of Mind", Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, first published 2015, substantive revision 2024. Predicts: the simple mapping account is exactly Option B, and the page reports Putnam and Searle arguing that it trivializes computational descriptions. Corroborates F3.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The dichotomy is the wrong cut. Computation is a three-term union compound: a system with causal organization, a pose-dependent frame transform, and a ground that settles the mapping. Pure intrinsic accounts drop the pose; pure observer-relative accounts drop the ground and make every rock a computer under some description. The bond carries the information the states do not, so the rock and the NOT gate stand as isomers. The framework returns this shape with five falsifiers written in advance. It does not name a winner between "intrinsic" and "observer-relative", and the binary stays `unreached` rather than `unreachable` until a discriminator is applied. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape does not depend on choosing one camp, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A mis-drawn bond in a particular ascription can be corrected without touching the three-term structure, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold the naming that would have been the error.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates intervention-invariant causal organization from imposed description would settle the binary; a case where redrawing a mapping with all physical facts fixed changes the ascription would break F1; a unique input/output partition with no pose would break F2; an unrestricted mapping sufficing would break F3; identical states and typed bonds differing in status would break F4. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
