## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Adding readers to a list selected for agreement changes the vocabulary, not the anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
