# The Tether's answer on computation and observers

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether computation belongs to the system or to the observer's mapping. In effect terms the predicate relates three things: a system with states and transitions, a mapping of those states to inputs and outputs, and a ground that settles whether the mapping is faithful. Option A drops the mapping. Option B drops the ground. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* This is a union compound with its own loop, not a single question and not two separate questions.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor test bars pure observer-relativity.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* If flipping a mapping changed the ascription while all physical facts and intervention outcomes stayed fixed, the anchor would have become another frame. That is coherent drift, panel 1 of Fig 2.
2. **The mapping is real but bounded.** Symbols table :79-82: F is *"the frame transform"*, *"The map between allocentric and egocentric at one scale. Distinct from T_A and T_E, which run between scales. Cannot be run without a pose."* The observer contributes a vantage, not the property. Fig 10 :68: *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."*
3. **A declared mapping is not a grounded mapping.** Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* The restriction on mappings is not a ban on interpretation; it is the requirement that the interpretation pay against the ground.
4. **Computation is in the bond, not in either term.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The rock and the NOT gate share state ingredients and differ in the bond, which is why one computes and the other does not under restricted accounts. `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 treats them as isomers.
5. **Capability is relational, not arbitrary.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a verdict between the names.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The intrinsic/observer-relative binary is `unreached`, not `unreachable`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that computation is purely intrinsic. That drops the pose and cannot explain why one mapping is correct.
- Not that computation is purely observer-relative. That drops the ground and trivializes.
- Not that the framework settles the philosophical debate. Fig 8 :37: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*
- Not that a particular mapping is correct. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Not `grounded`. No instrument that discriminates causal organization from imposed description has been applied.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer settled it, and *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Intervention test:** does an ascription of computation track what the system does under intervention, remaining invariant to irrelevant redescription? This is Fig 2 :29's anchor applied to the case.
- **Bond test:** can two systems with the same state sequence be shown to differ in the typed bond, as the rock and the gate differ in whether transitions are self-produced or externally driven?
- **Counterfactual test:** does the physical state transition support the counterfactuals the claimed computation requires? This is the restriction the SEP's counterfactual and causal accounts impose on the simple mapping account.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a status change with all physical facts fixed; a unique input/output partition with no pose; an unrestricted mapping sufficing; identical states and bonds differing in status; an experiment showing computation tracking ascription rather than causal organization.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The three-term shape is stated, the refuters are written in advance, and the stop is recorded so a later reader can locate the error. *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* The terms used here are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between "the system computes" and "the observer maps." It says the question is the wrong cut. Any real ascription needs three things: a system with the right causal organization, a mapping that is a frame transform requiring a pose, and a ground that settles whether the mapping is faithful. Pure intrinsic accounts drop the pose; pure observer-relative accounts drop the ground and make every rock a computer under some description. The figures say a declared meaning is not a grounded one, that the bond carries the information the ingredients do not, and that capability is a property of the system and its contact, never of the system alone. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The binary stays open until that instrument exists.
