## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The ambiguity in "changed": a new reading can fill a held slot, mint a new term, refuse a held term, or change contact so that the classified answer back and the reading changes. Arity: three operations across three named domains, so a **union** with overlaps, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57). Symmetry: a measurement returns an ordering over a state and a category is a threshold cut, so the governing bond is ordered/comparative, not a symmetric join. Scale: perception link, vocabulary link, habitat link, in that dependency order (Fig 3).
- **Domain 1, nature (physical kinds).** Actors: instruments, standards, physical states, observers. Currency: readings in a declared scale, paid in calibration actions. Anchor: a fixed point that does not move when a frame changes its mind. Nameable, so a domain. Question type: partition boundary, which compositions are legal over held atoms.
- **Domain 2, people (human kinds).** Actors: classified people, classifiers, tests, censuses, institutions. Currency: assigned kinds and the consequences that follow. Anchor: human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-38). Nameable, so a domain. Question type: union of mint, refusal, and contact change.
- **Domain 3, social life (collections and institutions).** Actors: agencies, counts, reporting rules, programs consuming the counts. Currency: counts and their categories. Anchor: conditional on a non-updating ground being reachable; where absent, the count becomes a convention that decides an outcome and stops being one (Fig 10 :47). Recorded as a union condition with an anchor test.
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument must already return something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35); then vocabulary must have terms for what the reading affords (Fig 3 :7); then the society must say what counts as progress (Fig 3 :10); then planning; then learn and carry (Fig 3 :15). The break is usually between vocabulary and objective, where there are fewest instruments (Fig 3 :27).
- **Partition or union.** Union. A medical thermometer, a census used as a population measurement, and an index used to allocate funds are compounds with their own loops and their own anchors. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

Strain test: the apparent conflict between Fig 6 (instruments extend representation) and Fig 13 (composition rearranges held kinds) dissolves on re-census because they govern different operations. No figure strain after re-census. One declared gap on the enumeration.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The separator survives; the single-list framing does not. A new measurement extends what can be represented, and the category change that follows is a mint under three guards and a cost bargain, a refusal that adds a negation, or a contact change that alters the classified and the reading. The anchor is set by what the question is constitutively about: a fixed point for physical states, human judgment for people, and a conditional non-updating ground for social counts. Over a fixed atom set old and new categories are isomers; where a new ordering was added the relation is composition plus import. The effect is indexed per slot, the three verdicts are kept separate, and a cross-time comparison is valid only with the lineage. The specific categories a future instrument will produce, and a complete historical ranking, are a declared `gap`. This is `general` / `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, and simulated throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a historical list instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The averaged verdict, the instrument-as-anchor reading for people, and the subtraction reading of reclassification are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A support test showing the live slot and the failed reading; a mint test showing the guards and the bargain; an anchor test showing human judgment settled a people case; a contact test showing no behavioral or institutional change; a round-trip measurement of `R_T` across a category change; or any of the ten pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 would move the verdict. Prestige, number of confirming cases, and a longer list of agreeing historians are filters, not such an instrument.
