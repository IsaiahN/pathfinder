## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is whether a new measurement system merely fills slots in the category vocabulary a society already holds, or changes the categories themselves. Each option leads somewhere different. If it only fills slots, then older categories survive and comparison across time is safe. If it changes categories, then a new term was minted or a held term was refused, and comparison across time breaks until the lineage is recorded. The question names three domains (people, nature, social life), and the anchor for a verdict is not the same in all three, so the answer must be three verdicts kept separate, not one averaged claim.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): a separator that says which of the three domains a measurement changes by supplying support for a mint, which by changing contact so that the classified answer back, and which by replacing a reachable ground with a convention that decides an outcome. The specific list of categories a future instrument will produce is explicitly not part of the answer; the figures return a shape, not a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle the historical record on their own.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Gathering more historians to agree does not create an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the failure mode of unsolicited bulk contact, as well as the structural one. Nothing in this folder sends anything.
