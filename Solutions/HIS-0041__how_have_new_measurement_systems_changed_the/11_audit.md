## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :26 and :21 (instrument extension versus mint), Fig 2 :36-38 (anchor by domain), Fig 13 :20 and :75 (ordering and cross-kind structure), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), and Fig 10 :47 (convention becomes verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: measurement extends representation, then mint or refusal or contact change; anchor by domain; old and new categories are isomers; lineage is required; no future category name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical claim of the ordinary kind, for example "the census created new racial categories" or "the thermometer invented hot and cold."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **guard set** on a mint (support, reachability, novelty, and the cost bargain), which a loose historical reading drops in favor of "the instrument produced the category";
- the **anchor test**, which a loose reading replaces with "the measurement settled it," the exact inversion Fig 2 :37 forbids for people;
- the **negation operator**, which a loose reading turns into subtraction, so dropped categories look deleted rather than refused (Operators :58, Fig 6 :42);
- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim, which fails as soon as the instrument added a new ordering and the relation becomes composition plus import.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a measurement supplies support and an ordering, and a category change must be a mint, a refusal, or a contact change, with the anchor set by the question. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
