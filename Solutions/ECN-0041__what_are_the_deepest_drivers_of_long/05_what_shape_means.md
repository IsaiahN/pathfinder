## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0041 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the single driver. The shape has four parts: a union of overlapping conditions, a closure limit on accumulation, an import-or-habitat requirement, and per-slot measurement. A **name** would be the sentence "technology is the deepest driver" or "institutions are the deepest driver". The first is supported as a shape-derived binding; the second is the same kind of binding from a different frame; neither is delivered by the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer. This brings your search of unlimited books to a finite sum."*
- The same essay on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The residual was described in effect terms (persistent growth after a push, failure not repairable by other pushes) before any of the four fields was chosen.
- Residual first, frame second (Fig 8 :53): the domains were censused from the effect, not from a favourite literature.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a measurement that identifies which link binds in a given place and era, per slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Calling an idea nonrival (Jones, 2004) is a description that travels; calling it "the deepest driver" adds a verdict the description does not carry.

*The Library of Babel* also gives the failure mode for a bad single-root question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54-57 in the vocabulary of the essays, and it is the reason §5 reports `gap` rather than a winner.

## 4. Metamerism: why an aggregate reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The essay's conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A pooled cross-country growth coefficient is the same kind of reading. Capital deepening, idea diffusion and institutional contact can all produce a similar measured association with per-capita growth, and the reading does not determine which source was present. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* What is owed is a per-slot discriminator, not a name.

## 5. What licenses the names "technology" and "institutions" here

Nothing in the framework licenses either as *the* answer. Both are bindings from the economics frame onto shapes the figures license:

- "technology" binds onto **an import from a different closure** (Fig 6 :23), supported in the world by Solow's growth accounting and Jones's nonrivalry.
- "institutions" binds onto **a habitat condition that changes contact** (Fig 11 :40), supported in the world by AJR's natural experiments and Solow's institutional-peculiarities passage.

Both bindings are recorded with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* The record is the whole difference between a derivation and a preference here.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the shape-not-a-name rule as if it forbade naming any driver. It does not. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing thing, a primitive not held in the library. ECN-0041 is not asking for a new primitive; it is asking which held candidate is deepest. What Fig 9 :32 forbids is treating the framework as if it returns the answer itself. Naming a constraint (union, closure, contact) is exactly what the derivation does. Naming a single winner is the forbidden step, not naming the shape.

Both readings are kept visible: the over-broad one here in §3 and its narrowing in this section, so a reader can see the correction rather than only the corrected claim.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige is not equal to actions."* *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A shortlist of famous growth economists assembled to endorse this shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against slot-level data, and let the ground settle it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the growth question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
