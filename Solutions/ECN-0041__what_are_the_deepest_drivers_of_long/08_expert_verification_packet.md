# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the deepest drivers of growth

**What this is:** a claim in a form an economic-growth researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the shape-not-a-name rule, `gap` on the literal single-driver question. No slot-level growth measurement is claimed.** This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The candidates named in the question form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition with one deepest member, so the instruction is split rather than search. Capital and education-as-embodied-skill are accumulation inside a closed frame: they change levels and transition speeds but cannot, on their own, set the long-run rate. Anything that could set that rate must have one of two shapes: an import from a frame whose closure differs, which is the shape ideas have because using one does not consume it, or a habitat condition that changes which actors are in contact with the ground, which is the shape rules and incentives have. The framework does not name a single deepest driver; it returns a kind of thing. What it offers is the shape any driver must have, refuters written in advance, and the slot-level instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and conceptually checkable

- **Statement:** the candidates form a union, so a single-lever "deepest driver" search is mis-posed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* And Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- **Refuter:** show that one candidate carries the persistent residual across independent subsets and closures, so the union collapses to a partition.

### C2 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 11

- **Statement:** accumulation inside a closure cannot set the long-run rate; the only things that can have the shape of an import or a contact-changing habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **Refuter:** a measured sustained rise in per-capita growth generated entirely inside the closure of a reproducible factor, with no import and no change in contact; or a demonstration that rules and incentives are just another reproducible input, so habitat adds no distinct kind.

### C3 - the limit claim, checkable by reading Figure 9 and the canonical README

- **Statement:** the framework returns a shape and not a name, so the single-driver slot is `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The canonical figures README states the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops where instruments can no longer distinguish.
- **Refuter:** a figure re-census that licenses a single-root derivation, or a ground reading that names the binding driver per slot. Until one fires, naming a winner is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "technology is the deepest driver", and not "institutions are the deepest driver". Both are bindings from the economics frame onto licensed shapes, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not that capital and education are irrelevant. They set levels and transitions; Solow's embodiment discussion and Denison's education share are retained.
- Not that all four candidates are mutually exclusive. They are a union.
- Not a pooled regression coefficient as a verdict. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a slot-level measurement.
- Not a new primitive, not an import that moves a wall, and not a derivation of one school from another.

## 5. What would settle it

A slot-level discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Binding test:** in a given country and era, which domain has a residual that persists and does not trend down when the others are pushed?
2. **Closure test:** does sustained per-capita growth occur with no import and no change in contact?
3. **Union test:** does one candidate carry the residual across independent subsets and closures?
4. **Aggregation test:** is the signal recoverable from a single pooled aggregate, or only per slot?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

2. Check C1 against your own sense of the growth-drivers literature: do the candidates work on different subsets, or is there a single root?

4. Name one slot-level instrument that would distinguish these, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
