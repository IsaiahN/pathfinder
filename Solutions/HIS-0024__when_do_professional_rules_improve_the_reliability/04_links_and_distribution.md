## What the wider literature bears on this question

Three findings matter, and none of them decides the verdict (the figures do):

1. **The reliability side is real and measurable.** Ioannidis argues that adherence to common standards increases the proportion of true findings, and COS documents a concrete rule (registered reports) that removes outcome-contingent incentives. Both fit column A of the partition: they change contact or carry provenance. Both are drawn from the same modern metascience pool, so they count as one witness with two names.
2. **The interest-protection side is also real and documented historically.** Moxham and Fyfe, via JSTOR Daily, show that refereeing at the Royal Society was built for institution-building, finance and prestige before it became a quality filter. Merton's counter-norms describe the same move at the level of individual careers. Occupational licensing supplies the economic version: entrance rules create monopoly rents for incumbents. These are low-overlap with the metascience pool (history of science, sociology of professions, economics), so they are genuinely independent corroboration.
3. **The two effects coexist in one institution.** The licensing page records both rent-seeking and a maternal-mortality improvement from midwifery licensing; the peer-review page records both quality maintenance and role-duality bias. This is why the union reading in `01` F1 is not a philosophical preference: the world sources show the same unbundling problem.

No world source contradicts the figure verdict. The conflict rule was never triggered.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the two-column test first; the framework comes only after a recorded verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the figure set before answering has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the partition were true before showing them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is modest, so a small set of independent venues is enough. A mass send would buy spelling, not search.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two columns and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only if curious, the local figure links, with the note that the figure text is the authority; (4) only if they ask, the formula as a reusable method with their own refuter written first.

## Two-stage offer, plus the optional third stage

1. **Stage 1.** Residual and refuter first, with mode stated. No theory, no links, no request for trust or agreement.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** Offer the three framework links. Their verdict on stage 1 is already recorded and is never pooled with later agreement.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

The hard precondition is unchanged from `02_outreach_email.md`: if a stage-1 test fails, stages 2 and 3 do not happen.
