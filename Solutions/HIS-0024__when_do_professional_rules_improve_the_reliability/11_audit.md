Symmetry: the rule-to-claim relation is ordered (production ⇒, sequence →, comparison ⋛), so swapping rule and claim changes the object. Scale: first order at one claim and its checker, second order at the institution that sets the rule.
- **Domain 1, knowledge production.** Actors: researchers, claims, instruments. Currency: per-claim residuals. Anchor: experiment and outcome, nameable and unmoved.
- **Domain 2, the institution.** Actors: editors, referees, licensers. Currency: conventions, rulings, allocation. Anchor: the ground, but the channel can die, and the anchor can be captured by becoming a party.

- **Domain 4, habitat.** Actors: funders, journals, instruments, careers. Currency: introduction, not exclusion. Anchor: the ground, which is not the habitat.

Order by dependency: refuse the false regularity before minting a new standard (formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle a first-order molecule before composing a field-wide rule over it (Fig 12 :104).
