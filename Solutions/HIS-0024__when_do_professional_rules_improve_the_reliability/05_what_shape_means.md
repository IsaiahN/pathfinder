## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (read 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle a case. For this question the shape is the two-column partition test: it constrains what any answer must say about a rule (does it change contact? does it become the ground? does it protect standing? does it raise rho?), and it does not name a particular profession as good or bad. "Professional rules are good" is a name. "A rule must be checked for channel contact, outcome-deciding, standing and correlation before it can be called reliable" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: "credential", "peer review", "standard", "licence" were bootstrapped into "anchor", "convention", "standing", "rho", "channel".
- The same essay contains the decisive move for this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54's union rule. It is why the answer is per rule, not per profession.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the rule is described by what it does to contact and independence, not by the label the institution gives itself ("quality assurance", "professional standards").
- *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A rule that merely looks like quality assurance is a projection; several institutional arrangements (ground-keeping and rent-protection) can produce the same visible label. What is owed is a discriminator, not a new name.

## 3. What licenses a name (and why this question stops short of one)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a named profession, that means a held-out measurement of claim reliability before and after the rule, with an independent ground. This run did not make one.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The history-of-peer-review source (ledger row 4) is such an import: it brings a finding from The Historical Journal into the question, and its provenance is recorded.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* states the limit cleanly: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* Applied here: a metric that counts publications, citations or credentials cannot distinguish a rule that keeps the channel open from one that closes it. It demonstrates the metric's inadequacy, not the rule's virtue.

## 4. Why the two-column reading is the specific forbidden error avoided

A single verdict on "professional rules" would read a projection (the institution's own description of its standards) and name its source (reliability, or capture) without a discriminator. That is the metamerism error named in section 3. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23) gives the institutional form: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* The credential is a record of past success; letting it settle whether a present claim is reliable is exactly the substitution the essay names. The two columns are the discriminator: they ask what the rule does to contact and independence, not who carries it.

## 5. Q: what does "shape" mean for HIS-0024, concretely?

**Answer.** It means a per-rule partition grammar with five checkable properties: contact, independence, provenance, convention-versus-outcome, and per-slot measurement. The framework cannot name a historical profession as the answer, and this run does not try. A particular rule is placed in column A or B only after a case-level ground reading, which is `specified` and which this run did not perform. Where no reading exists, the honest report is `unreachable` (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*), not silence and not a guessed verdict.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prestigious referees selected for likely agreement is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the partition test against a ground, plus an invitation to refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
