## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the occupant

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

For EAR-0015 the shape is this: **reliability is per slot and per regime; the anchor exists only where an instrument returns one; the object is a class and not a single draw; every import from a model frame needs provenance; and the source slot stays open where no discriminator exists.** That is a constraint on any acceptable answer. It does not name greenhouse gases, aerosols, ENSO or any other occupant. Naming one is a binding from the climate-science frame and must be recorded as such.

Concretely, the shape rules out three tempting answers before any science is done:

- "Yes, reliably", as a single global verdict, because it aggregates across slots (Fig 1 :22).
- "No, never", as a single global verdict, because it treats an `unreached` single-event question as a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
- "The IPCC says high confidence, so the figures would call it grounded", because a confidence label is a filter (Fig 9 :60), not the ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: shrink the space, keep the question as tether

*The Library of Babel* describes the method: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The tether is the question itself, ranged against every returned candidate: "you can ask: Are we getting warmer, or are we getting colder? Are these answers getting closer to solving the general answer or diverging?"

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms first (a positive per-slot gap between an observation and a counterfactual), and only then were frames (observational records, model ensembles, statistical methods) brought in. Had the frames been named first, any of them would have seemed to fit. The question acted as tether when the temptation arose to read "reliability" off an IPCC confidence label instead of off the anchor condition.

The essay also notes what to do when the bundle refuses to close: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is exactly the Fig 9 :54 union rule that produced the domain split in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1.

## 3. What licenses a name in a slot

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For EAR-0015 this is the measurement anchor in D1.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, "the only operator that moves the wall"; Fig 8 :51-52, "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"). For EAR-0015 this is a model-derived counterfactual, licensed only when its provenance and its independence from other frames are on the record.

Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."

So the question "was this class changed" is constitutively about the measurement, and the measurement is a legitimate anchor. The question "what would have happened without humans" is not constitutively about any instrument; its baseline is imported from a model frame. Conflating the two is the error F3 records.

## 4. The metamerism point is the single-event case

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And the remedy: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Also: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

The observed magnitude of one extreme event is that green reading. Forced thermodynamic change, a natural circulation state, and their interaction can all produce it. Fig 6 :32 says an instrument "is improved from a worse instrument already returning something"; at the single-event source slot nothing is returning a discrimination, so there is no edge to extend (Fig 6 :35). What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. The honest word is "unreached", and the status is held open until a discriminator exists.

This essay shares an author and an evidence pool with the figures. Its agreement with Fig 6 is therefore internal consistency, not a second witness. The low-overlap world corroboration for the same point comes from Bellprat et al. (2019) and Perkins-Kirkpatrick et al. (2022), both read this session and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 5. Simulation notice and the prestige rule

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a **simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any climate claim and cannot settle the question it reports on.

The same essay states the rule that shapes outreach: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." And: "You may all agree and be wrong." A list of prestigious climatologists selected for likely agreement would be the named failure. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried, by asking for a refutation, and lets the ground settle it.

## 6. Correction kept visible

An earlier draft of this file treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any mechanism. That was overstated. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Greenhouse forcing, aerosols and internal modes are already held in the climate-science frame; naming them in a slot is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is pretending the framework derived the name, or treating the binding as an import that moves a wall it does not move. This correction is the analogue of the exemplar's own §5b correction and is kept here rather than silently erased.

## 7. One-paragraph answer to "what shape means here"

For this question, a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill the source slot: reliability is per slot; the anchor is the observation, not the counterfactual; the reliable object is a class, not one event; agreement counts only across low-overlap frames with recorded provenance; and where no source-discriminating instrument exists the report is "unreached" with the lineage kept. It constrains, it does not fill. Filling the slot with a specific forcing or mode is a provenanced binding from climate science, not a result the figures produced.
