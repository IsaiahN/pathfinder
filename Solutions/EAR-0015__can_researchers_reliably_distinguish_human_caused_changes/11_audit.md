## Independent domain census (done before reading the primary's conclusions)

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between observed extreme-event statistics and statistics under a natural-only hypothesis. Arity: three terms, one of which (the counterfactual) is never observed. Symmetry: ordered comparison, not a symmetric join; exchanging factual and counterfactual changes "made more likely" into "made less likely". Scale: one draw versus a distribution, local versus global, event time versus baseline time. So no single scalar verdict can carry R.
- **Domain 1, observed record.** Actors: instruments, reanalyses, catalogues. Currency: measured magnitude and frequency. Anchor: the measurement, nameable and non-updating. Question type: one condition of a union.
- **Domain 2, counterfactual model frame.** Actors: GCMs, ensembles, ALL/NAT experiments. Currency: `P_ALL`, `P_NAT`, risk ratio, FAR. Anchor: none for the NAT world; the observed anchor is borrowed only for evaluation. Question type: a frame, not anchor-bearing ground, and another condition of the union.
- **Domain 3, internal variability.** Actors: ENSO, NAO, chaotic weather. Currency: unforced variance. Anchor: partial and too short for rare tails. Third union condition.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: perception, event-definition vocabulary, comparison, verdict. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The event-definition link is the instrument-poor middle link; a risk ratio computed before it is settled is a reading of nothing.

**Partition or union:** union. A lever that lengthens observations clears only the measurement-error condition; a better model clears representation error and leaves internal variability. I reached the union reading from Fig 9's "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again", whereas the primary reached it from the same figure plus Fig 3. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires measuring it rather than assuming it zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 and :31 (anchor and independence), Fig 6 :32 (instrument rule), Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`: reliability is per slot; measurement is anchor; counterfactual is frame; class not singleton; single event unreached; agreement is not a witness.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a working attribution claim, for example "a named heatwave class was made at least N times more likely by anthropogenic greenhouse gases, high confidence".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down to a concrete claim:

- the **provenance of the import**: the shorthand says "counterfactual is a frame", but a concrete claim must say which model, which ensemble, which forcing set and which evaluation against observations; otherwise the import is unrecorded and Fig 8 :51-52 applies;
- the **event definition**: the shorthand says "per slot", but a concrete claim must fix threshold, region and season before the comparison; if it does not, the Fig 3 middle link has no instrument;
- the **direction of the bond**: the shorthand says "risk ratio", but a concrete claim must orient factual over counterfactual; a symmetric report loses which world is more likely.

Two things survive cleanly: a structure must exist in the event-definition slot before a comparison can be trusted, and the observation is the only anchor in the chain. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not measured numerically**, because no ground reading exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating a condensation as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap.
