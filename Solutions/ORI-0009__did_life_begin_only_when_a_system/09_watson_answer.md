## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, where a discrete predicate ("alive") first attaches to a continuous physical process. Option A attaches it to a system that can reproduce independently, giving a late, sharp threshold and a nameable first living individual. Option B attaches it to the start of chemical selection, giving an earlier, process-based boundary and no clean first individual. What each leads to: A makes the question a single threshold and imports a capability that is downstream of the process it is meant to explain; B makes the question a union, keeps the process before the label, and leaves the boundary instant a convention. The figures decide the shape. World sources corroborate only.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A binary predicate applied to a temporal series of states, with two named candidate anchors. Arity: two candidate anchors, but the underlying conditions (selection, replication, energetic coupling, compartmentalisation) overlap, so the residual is a **union compound**, not a partition. Symmetry: selection is a population-level relation extended in time, reproduction is a capability of a bounded individual at a moment, so they are not symmetric alternatives at one level. Scale: chemical and molecular versus organismal, so the boundary crosses a membrane. Fig 9 :4-5 says split a disagreement that does not shrink with effort.
- **Domain 1, chemical selection (unmaintained habitat).** Actors: replicators, autocatalytic sets, monomers, gradients, compartments. Currency: differential persistence, what the ground kept. Anchor: the ground read as physical persistence. Nameable anchor: yes, "nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept" (Fig 11 :14-15). Question type: partition boundary within a population.
- **Domain 2, independent reproduction (closed individual cycle).** Actors: a bounded system, its boundary, its replication loop. Currency: closure of the loop. Anchor: the same ground at a different scale, namely whether the cycle closes. Nameable anchor: yes, a measurement of a closed reproductive cycle. Question type: capability of system and habitat.
- **Domain 3, the definition and naming frame.** Actors: researchers, working definitions, conventions. Currency: agreement about meaning. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows from D1 and D2. Nameable anchor: **no**, so this is a convention, not a ground-bearing domain. Question type: convention, and the main generator of the dispute.
- **Domain 4, the historic record.** Actors: fossils, isotopes, stromatolites, phylogenies. Currency: surviving traces. Anchor: the ground through a partly dead channel. Nameable anchor: yes in principle, but weak for prebiotic stages (Fig 10 :24). Question type: measurement availability.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :54-55: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again. A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Order by dependency.** Split the union before searching (Fig 9 :4-5); settle selection before closure (Fig 3 :19-20); choose the naming convention last and label it a convention (Fig 2 :43); read the record last against the split questions (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: Fig 11 :56 says "nothing selects" of the unmaintained habitat, while Fig 11 :36 says "selection ran". Re-census shows one denies a selecting seat, the other affirms ground-driven persistence. Different objects, no strain. All quotes were re-opened in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

**Domain 2, independent reproduction.**

- Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." -> reproduction is a closed cycle.

- **Verdict:** convention, not a verdict. **triangulated** across Fig 2 and the Seat Map, two figures with different offices. Not a gap: the figures govern it.

**Domain 4, historic record.**

- Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." -> no record is not proof of no transition.
- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> the weak prebiotic channel is a channel failure.

- Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." -> the boundary is level-relative.
- **Verdict:** unreached for the precise path, reached for the ahistoric shape. **triangulated** across Fig 8, Fig 10 and Fig 13.

**Overall.** The ordering leans B without naming a date: the process precedes the label, selection is the legitimate anchor, reproduction is a later closed cycle, the word is a convention, and the date is unreached. No verdict is averaged across domains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The process precedes the label. Chemical selection is the earlier anchor for a question about the origin of the capacity to evolve; independent reproduction is a later closed cycle, downstream of the process it consumes. The "A or B" form is a union that must be split, and the naming frame has no ground, so "life" remains a convention until a ground settles it. The precise historic boundary is unreached rather than absent, and any boundary drawn is level-relative. This is a `general` shape plus a `specified`, ungrounded ordering. No date is asserted. A single-threshold answer (A) is refused as a type error over different bonds and scales; B is accepted only in the weak, non-dated sense.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, splits the union and records three refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a finished theory before the residual; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The ordering claim can be withdrawn without surrendering a primitive or the derivation, and a sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A measurement showing a sharp reproductive onset with no prior differential persistence; a demonstration that chemical-scale persistence requires a bounded individual; a source-discriminating instrument that names the process behind the reading; a dated, checkable signature resolving a specific transition; or a derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
