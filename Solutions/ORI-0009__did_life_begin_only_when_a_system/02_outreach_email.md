# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the framing

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the union split in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 is understood. If the email still reads as "A or B," it re-bundles the very question it is asking the reader to check.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the "first life" boundary, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the ordering, not `grounded`):** the question "did life begin at independent reproduction or at the start of chemical selection?" is not one well-formed question. Reproduction and selection overlap, so the "or" bundles several questions. Unbundled, there are three: when differential persistence began, when a reproductive loop closed, and which word we will use for the boundary. Only the first two have grounds; the third is a convention.
>
> The figure-grounded result is an ordering, not a date: the process runs before the label. Differential persistence among chemical replicators is the process that the word "life" is applied to afterwards. Independent reproduction is a later milestone, a closed cycle, downstream of the process that supplies it. So the honest form of the answer is "earlier when chemical selection began, with no single boundary instant," not a named first living individual.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that we know when this happened, that there was one sharp transition, or that any working definition (including "a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution") settles the question. Definitions are useful filters for what to look for. They are not witnesses for when the world changed. The historic path is unreached rather than silent.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A measurement showing a sharp onset of a closed reproductive cycle with no prior differential persistence among replicators.
> 2. A demonstration that differential persistence cannot occur at chemical scale without a bounded, independently reproducing individual.
> 3. A figure-level re-census showing that the "label applied afterwards" rule governs only self-maintained habitats and not unmaintained chemical ones.
>
> I wrote these down before writing the conclusion. If any holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, especially Fig 11 :34-36, Fig 11 :41, Fig 2 :36-38, Fig 9 :4-5, Fig 12 :121.
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - Peer-reviewed context: Pross and Pascal, "The origin of life: what we know, what we can know and what we will never know," Open Biology 3(3), 2013, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3718341/
> - Public Q&A context: NASA Astrobiology, "Ask an Astrobiologist," https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/ask-an-astrobiologist/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the eight-step loop is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the source essays warn that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, staged explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the union split and the three refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a method, with the instruction that each user states their own mode and writes their own refuter before the run.

**Stage 1 cannot work unless the email still fits on one screen.** The union split is the whole payload. Anything longer sends a recording instead of a generator.
