- **D5 Separability.** Actors: levers and the two conditions. Currency: which condition clears. Anchor: an experiment moving one lever and reading the other. Admitted.
- **Question type and order.** D1 and D2 are a union with intersection, not a partition. D2 is upstream of D1 by dependency (Fig 3); D5 consumes both; D4 waits for an anchor that has not arrived, so the chain stops there.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The ranking half is `gap`. No figure and no world source supplies an anchor that settles "matters more" and does not move when either side pushes on it; a chosen welfare function is a declared convention, and a cross-country correlation is a filter. The separability half is `specified`: opportunity is the generator and sits upstream of the outcome readout (Fig 10 :68, Fig 11 :40); the two form a union with intersection, so a single lever clears only the non-overlap region (Fig 9 :55, :57); the overlap is asymmetric under the standard decomposition, with the opportunity component inside the outcome total; and a single aggregate index hides slot-level signal (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). The comparison itself is well-typed but ungrounded. The ranker's stake in choosing a priority is structural and declared (Fig 10 :59). This is a shape and a route, not a verdict, and nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by treating a correlation or an agreed priority as a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The ranking claim is withdrawn rather than repaired, and no primitive is removed. The union and dependency findings stand on their own in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, so a failed ranking leaves a usable result. A superseding packet costs one revision, not a re-derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An invariant non-authored anchor for priority would close the `gap`; a lever experiment moving circumstances with outcomes held fixed, or measuring zero intersection, would reverse the dependency or union findings; a source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes equal outcomes from a fair process from equal outcomes produced by compensation would name what the outcome reading alone cannot. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` with the ranking reported as `gap`.
