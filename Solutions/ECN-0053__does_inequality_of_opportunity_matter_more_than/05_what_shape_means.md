## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the form any answer must have before the ranking can be computed: opportunity is upstream of outcome, the two form a union with an intersection, and a single aggregate outcome index hides slot-level signal. It constrains; it does not fill. "EOp matters more" is a verdict, not a shape. "The generator precedes the readout, and the two overlap" is a shape.

*The Library of Babel* also gives the stopping rule: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on proximity: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the terms the question already supplies (distribution, inequality, opportunity, outcome), then let the figure lines narrow which relations are legal.
- *Library of Babel*, on bundles: "If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed. Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay-side warrant for F4 in `01`: the question bundles a ranking and a separability problem, and they must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again").
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The shape is the plain description of effects: what would have to be true for one lever to move the other.
- Fig 6 :30, quoted in that essay: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So say "the condition that produces outcomes varies across slots", not a discipline-specific jargon label that only one school of inequality research can read.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why this question cannot name the winner

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md`: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52).

For ECN-0053 neither is available for the ranking. No instrument in the sources read returns an invariant ordering of "matters". No import supplies one; the Roemer decomposition and the Gatsby curve are imports that describe structure, not a common currency of priority. So the honest output is `gap` (Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").

*Arrival of the Fittest* is explicit about the framework's own limit: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." Its own "Three mappings offered and declined" section is the precedent for declining a mapping that resembles the framework without aligning with it. The Gatsby correlation is such a near miss: it couples the two distributions without supplying a ranking.

## 4. Outcomes cannot reveal their opportunity source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that makes F3 and F1 substantive. Metamerism: an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source". And: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay adds that the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists, and that a case of this kind "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument".

Applied here: an outcome distribution is a reading. Several opportunity configurations can produce the same outcome distribution (equal outcomes from a fair race, or from a heavily compensated one). Naming the opportunity structure from the outcome reading is the metamerism error. Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state" is the figure form of the same point. An instrument that discriminates sources, not a further argument, is what would name it.

## 5. Outputs are not generators

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "a corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator." And: "You cannot accumulate your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of thing, and no quantity of the first sums to the second."

Applied here: opportunity is the generator, outcomes are the output. Fig 10 :68: "a generator crosses, an instance does not". Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." So an outcome-only lever is an attempt to accumulate generators from outputs. It cannot add the thing that produces the distribution. This is the essay-side warrant for F2, still `specified` and not a moral ranking.

The same essay adds: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." That is the honest limit on any framework claim here: the shape is a guess made visible, not nature's own list.

## 6. A correction to my own reading

**Earlier reading:** the phrase "shape, not a name" forbids naming the generator in this question at all, so even saying "opportunity produces outcomes" would be illegitimate.

**Corrected reading:** the canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The opportunity field is not a seventh primitive; Fig 11 already names the habitat and Fig 10 already distinguishes generator from instance. Naming "opportunity is the generator, outcomes the readout" is a **binding** onto held terms, not an invention, and it is `specified` rather than forbidden. What remains genuinely unnamed is the *winner of the ranking*, because that requires an anchor nobody holds. Both claims are now recorded so neither is silently lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own §5b correction.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of respected economists agreeing that one inequality matters more is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape claim. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs from an attached formula must be seen as "simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test and must be labelled as such.
