## QUESTION

Did the universe undergo a very brief period of extremely rapid expansion called inflation? Restated in effect terms: does the residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` between a family of early-universe generators and the instrument readings close on any slot? Option A: treat "consistent with inflation" as a settled yes. Option B: treat it as a shape constraint, keep the slots open, and name the instrument that would close them. What each leads to: A delivers a `general`-mode verdict without a ground reading and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B delivers a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot. The figures support B and refuse A.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between early-universe model predictions and instrument readings. Arity: one model class against many observable slots, so many residuals, not one. Symmetry: model-to-data agreement is a threshold comparison ⋛, not identity ≡ (Operators table). Scale: generator at energies beyond terrestrial accelerators, observation a cold 2.7 K glow today, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :30). Fig 9 names the question a union compound, not a single hard question.
- **D1 Instrument and sky.** Actors: CMB temperature and polarization maps, B-mode polarization, lensing, BAO, detector arrays. Currency: measured spectra and upper limits. Anchor: the sky, a reading that does not update on what any theorist thinks (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 Model frame.** Actors: inflaton potential, slow roll, monomial potentials, attractors, non-inflationary alternatives. Currency: predicted `n_s`, `r`, consistency relation. Anchor: none that settles alone; a model is a frame, not an anchor. Admitted, zero verdict weight on internal coherence.
- **D3 Verification and aggregation.** Actors: collaborations, reviewers, community. Currency: agreement, shared data products. Anchor: the ground only; agreement is disqualified when frames share a pool (Fig 2 :40). Admitted, verdict weight on agreement = 0.
- **D4 Foreground and contact habitat.** Actors: Milky Way dust, synchrotron, lensing, atmosphere, instrument noise. Currency: contamination and its removal. Anchor: multi-frequency cross-checks. Admitted; enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43).
- **X1 "Consensus proves inflation true".** Actors: the community. Currency: prestige. Anchor: none nameable, because consensus updates on the frames it aggregates. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "A non-detection proves inflation false".** Absorbed into D1 and D4; a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

Union, not partition. D1 and D4 form one compound: the sky cannot be read until foregrounds are separated. D2 and D3 form another: models are judged by a community that shares data products. Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: "consistent with" was first read as identity, which strained because many generators pass the same comparison; re-census keeps it as a ⋛ threshold relation. Different objects, no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not answer whether inflation happened. They constrain the shape of any answer: the sky settles each slot, never agreement; the residual is per slot and never averaged; "consistent with" is a threshold comparison that underdetermines the generator; a null is unreached rather than false; foregrounds must be enumerated before any slot is read; shared pools are one witness; and only a new channel moves the wall. The factual claim is `gap` for the figures. Four domains are triangulated, none is a ground reading, and their verdicts are kept separate and never averaged into a yes or no. Everything here is `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-domain verdicts and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim can be narrowed slot by slot without touching the census. A source-discriminating measurement closes one slot; a figure re-census withdraws a structural claim; no primitive has to be abandoned. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one with the refuter kept visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A primordial B-mode spectrum with `r` inconsistent at high confidence and foregrounds independently separated would close the tensor slot; a non-Gaussianity measurement discriminating among mechanisms sharing `n_s` and `r` would name a source; a demonstration that no instrument at any resolution could return a discriminating reading would move the question from unreached at this budget to genuine unreachability; a figure re-census forbidding the composition would withdraw the structural claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate` and the factual row stays `gap`.
