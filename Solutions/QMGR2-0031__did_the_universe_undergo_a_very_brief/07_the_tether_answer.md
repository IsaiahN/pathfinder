# The Tether's answer on "did inflation happen?"

**Mode: `general` on the structural rules, `specified` on the application to inflation evidence. Not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. The figures cannot answer the factual question, and this solution says so plainly.

## 1. The residual it answers

A family of early-universe models predicts values; instruments return maps. `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). The slots are `n_s`, `r`, curvature, Gaussianity, and the foreground channel that must be cleared before any of them can be read. The question "did inflation happen" is a **union compound** of "the instrument closes a slot" and "the model class predicts that slot", not one hard question (Fig 9 :54 to 57). It has its own ground and its own loop.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that inflation did or did not happen. That is a ground question and this run took no ground reading.
- Not that a particular inflaton, potential or energy scale is the driver. That is a name where the framework returns a shape, and it belongs to question `QMGR2-0032`, not this one.
- Not that `r_0.05 < 0.036` is a detection. It is an upper limit reported as such by the collaboration.
- Not that a non-detection would prove inflation false. Fig 8 :39 forbids that inference.
- Not that "consistent with the predictions of inflation" means the predictions are identical to the observations. ⋛ is a threshold relation, not ≡.
- Not that the framework's structural rules are a measurement. They are `general`, and applying them to this case is `specified`.

## 4. What would settle it, and how the stop is recorded

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The specific questions are:

- Does the tensor channel return a primordial B-mode spectrum with `r` inconsistent with zero, with foregrounds independently separated at several frequencies? That closes the slot.
- Does non-Gaussianity (`f_NL`) discriminate among mechanisms that share `n_s` and `r`? That is the analogue of the spectrometer in the metamerism example.
- Are two analyses independent once an effective-sample-size correction is applied (Fig 2 :44)? If yes, their agreement counts as two witnesses; if not, it counts as one.
- Standing falsifiers from `01`: a genuine in-principle underivation, a non-inflationary mechanism closing the same slots, or a figure re-census forbidding the composition.

The honest stop: **unreached, not unreachable**. The answer stops at what the instruments can currently distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 5. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer whether inflation happened, and it does not claim a new measurement. It says the question closes per slot, never as one average: the scalar tilt, the tensor ratio, curvature, non-Gaussianity and the foreground channel each carry their own residual against the sky. The published `r` limits are upper bounds, not detections, and a null is unreached rather than false. "Consistent with inflation" is a comparison, not an identity: one CMB reading is compatible with many generators, so the data constrain a shape without naming a source. The ground is the instrument, not agreement, and analyses that share a sky and a dust model are one witness wearing several names. What the framework offers is that shape, the order in which the habitat must be enumerated, the refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the open slot. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
