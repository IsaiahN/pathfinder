## What this folder is

A derive-first, figure-grounded treatment of the question. It does **not** answer whether inflation happened. It states the residual in effect terms, censuses the domains before choosing any figure line, quotes figure lines verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, gives a verdict per domain kept separate and never averaged, writes a pre-registered refuter before the conclusion, and records world sources that were actually opened with author, venue, year and a verbatim quote.

It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-23) an LLM can only *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So this folder cannot settle the question it reports on. The empirical claims it cites belong to the instruments that made them, not to this derivation.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a conclusion about inflation.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No verdict that inflation did or did not happen. The figures are metalaws about composition, ground and reach. They constrain the **shape** any answer must have and they refuse to settle the fact. Saying they settle it would be the output-layer failure the framework names: an unmeasured quantity delivered as a verdict.
- No claim that a null result proves inflation false, and no claim that "consistent with inflation" proves it true. Both are barred by one figure line each (Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :32), quoted in `01`.
