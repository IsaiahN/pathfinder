## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual

The question is not "is the inflationary paradigm popular." It is a contact question between a **generator** (a family of early-universe models) and an **observation** (the sky as returned by instruments). Stated in effect terms with the Symbols table notation:

> `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` evaluated per slot `s`, where `Γ` is the model family, `a` is the act of pointing an instrument, `o′` is the returned map, and `b` is the inherited prior about the early universe.

The slots are not one number. They are at least: the scalar spectral index `n_s`, the tensor-to-scalar ratio `r`, spatial curvature `Ω_K`, Gaussianity and isocurvature, the B-mode polarization spectrum, and the foreground channel that must be removed before any of them can be read. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single headline "inflation confirmed" or "inflation refuted" would aggregate the slots and throw away the only signal that says where to look.

**Arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: a relation between one model class and many observable slots, so many residuals, not one. Symmetry: model-to-data agreement is a **comparison** bond, not an identity. The Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; identity ≡ would say two names, one referent, which no dataset supplies here. Scale: the generator is posited at energies far beyond terrestrial accelerators; the observation is a cold 2.7 K glow today. The transform between them is lossy, so Fig 4 :30 applies: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run).** An answer is either (A) a ground reading that closes a named slot with a source-discriminating instrument, or (B) a characterisation of what such an instrument would have to return, with the remaining slots left open. Option A is a measurement claim; option B is the shape the framework can actually deliver. This binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual like anything else, per the "Direction" note in `THE_FORMULA.md`.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The ground settles this question, and the ground here is the sky, not the paradigm (decisive, `general`)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

The question "did inflation happen" is about a historical physical event. The only thing that can settle it is a reading that does not move when theorists revise their priors: a spectrum, a polarization pattern, a foreground-separated map. A model family cannot be its own anchor, because it is exactly the frame under test. The 2014 BICEP2 claim is the case in point: an excess B-mode signal was reported, then a joint Planck and BICEP/Keck analysis attributed most of it to Milky Way dust (NASA JPL, 2015; NASA, 2016). The reading moved, the paradigm did not, and the anchor did its job.

- Mode: `general` for the anchor rule; `specified` for naming the sky as the anchor of this question.
- Triangulation: triangulated, low overlap between the anchor definition in Fig 2 and the verifier non-access rule in Fig 10 and the instrument history in the world sources.
- Refuter: a settlement of the question by vote, prestige, or the internal coherence of a model family with no new instrument reading. If that is accepted as decisive, F1 fails and the framework's anchor rule is wrong.

### F2 — The residual is per slot, never averaged into one verdict (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :6: *"the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"*

The opened sources return several distinct slots, not one: Planck Collaboration (2020) reports `n_s = 0.9649 ± 0.0042` at 68% CL and a 95% upper limit `r_0.002 < 0.10`, tightened to `r_0.002 < 0.056` with BK15; BICEP/Keck XIII (2021) reports `r_0.05 < 0.036` at 95% confidence and calls these *"the strongest constraints to date on primordial gravitational waves"*; the same Planck paper confirms spatial flatness to 0.4% at 95% CL with BAO. These are different slots with different residuals and different instruments. Collapsing them into "inflation: yes" loses exactly the signal that would tell an observer where to look next.

- Mode: `specified` for the slot list; `general` for the no-aggregation rule.
- Triangulation: triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 9 (different routes, same per-slot conclusion).
- Refuter: a single scalar evidence score that claims to settle the question while at least one of the slots above remains live. If such a score is admitted as a verdict, F2 fails.

This is the framework's shape-not-a-name limit applied to an empirical question, and it is the honest boundary of what the figures can contribute.

Then the slot moves to `grounded` and the shape-not-a-name limit is satisfied for that slot, not broken. F3 would fail only if the framework claimed uniqueness was settled by agreement alone.

### F4 — A non-detection is not a proof of absence; budget and principle are different facts (`general`)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 8 :41 to 42: *"Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*

`r_0.05 < 0.036` is an **upper limit**, not a non-existence proof, and the collaborations describe their own result as a constraint that *"shrink[s] this limit by combining data at three frequencies"* with archival WMAP and Planck maps (BICEP/Keck XIII, 2021). NASA JPL (2015) says plainly: *"As for signs of the universe's inflationary period, the question remains open"* and *"The gravitational wave signal could still be there, and the search is definitely on."* NASA (2016) records the other side of the same rule: failure to detect is still informative, because *"It means that we need to come up with a different model of what happened in the early universe."* So the correct verdict on a null is **unreached**, not **unreachable** and not **false**. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* is reserved for a genuine closure limit, not for this budget.

- Mode: `general` for the logical rule; `specified` for the current limit being a budget state.
- Triangulation: triangulated across Fig 8 and Fig 9 (two different arguments, one from closure, one from the unsealed room), plus the instrument history.
- Refuter: a demonstrated underivation in principle, showing no instrument at any resolution could return a source-discriminating reading for this epoch. That would move the generator question to genuine `gap`, and F4 would be narrowed, not silently kept.

### F5 — The habitat must be enumerated, not composed: foregrounds are in contact with the residual (`specified`)

### F6 — Correlated observations are not independent witnesses (`general`)

Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full line opens with "disqualifying for a verdict:"). Fig 2 :44: *"The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (line 12: *"same closure, handed along;"*).

Planck and BICEP/Keck are not independent in the strong sense: the joint analysis explicitly combines the satellite's multi-frequency foreground maps with the ground experiment's sensitivity, and the BICEP/Keck likelihood fixes lensing B-modes to the Planck 2018 cosmology. Wikipedia's survey-statistics entry (read 2026-11-23) defines the design effect and states: *"When Deff>1, then inference from the data collected is not as accurate as it could have been if people were picked randomly."* The effective sample size `n_eff = n / Deff` is the number of independent observations that would carry the same information. Ten analyses sharing one sky and one dust model are one witness with ten names, not ten witnesses. This cross-field corroboration is deliberately from survey statistics, a field with low overlap with cosmology, and it is the one place where a non-framework source explains the figure's arithmetic rather than merely agreeing with it.

- Mode: `general`.
- Triangulation: triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 7 inside the framework, and corroborated by an independent field (survey statistics) whose evidence pool does not overlap cosmology's sky data. This is the strongest independence in the whole audit.
- Refuter: two frames with demonstrably independent instruments and independent foreground models whose agreement on the same slot survives an effective-sample-size correction. If it does, the disqualification does not apply to that pair.

### F7 — The wall moves only on import; more model building inside one class adds no atom (`general`)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. ## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written in advance is a design instrument. The shape claim in this folder is refuted or narrowed if any of the following fires:

1. **Ground closure of a slot.** An instrument returns a primordial B-mode spectrum with `r` inconsistent with zero and with all named alternatives at high confidence, with foregrounds independently discriminated at multiple frequencies. That names the slot, moves it to `grounded`, and removes the shape-not-a-name limit for that slot.
2. **Genuine underivation in principle.** A demonstration that no instrument at any resolution could ever return a source-discriminating reading for this epoch. That moves the generator question from `gap` at this budget to genuine unreachability, and F4 is rewritten.
3. **A non-inflationary mechanism closes the same slots.** An instrument that returns a cyclic or ekpyrotic signature at the `n_s`, `r` and non-Gaussianity slots would reverse the paradigm-level preference without any vote or consensus being taken.
4. **A figure re-census forbids the composition.** If a genuine re-census shows that the figure structure forbids composing an early-universe state with the observable structure used here, the per-domain verdicts are withdrawn and the census is redone.
5. **A dependence correction.** If two supposedly independent instruments are shown to draw on one fully shared calibration and foreground pool, the F6 triangulation is downgraded to single-source.

None of these is a request for agreement. Until one fires, the verdict on the factual question stays `candidate` and the figure-derived shape stays `specified` and ungrounded.
