## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the set of properties any answer must have before we can name the cause:

- a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic spectrum with `n_s` measurably below one;
- a tensor channel with `r` bounded, not yet detected;
- a flat geometry to the quoted precision;
- foregrounds separated by multi-frequency reading;
- no unique generator selected by the data alone.

That is a shape. "It was a scalar field with potential V(φ)" is a name. The distinction is not pedantry: the shape can be checked by an instrument now, while the name is a binding from a different frame that needs its own provenance.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then bootstrap. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is `n_s`, `r`, B-modes, dust, lensing; each instrument refinement narrows the space of viable early-universe stories.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to 31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe what an instrument would return, not which field supposedly acted.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email in `02` asks what the recipient would expect to see before showing any framework material.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For inflation this means the stop is at the instrument limit, and the instrument limit is itself part of the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For inflation this would be a source-discriminating measurement of the tensor channel or of non-Gaussianity with foregrounds independently removed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An inflaton potential imported from particle physics must show where it came from and which gap it explains.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens why an instrument is required. Metamerism: a colour instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can all give the reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. The CMB `n_s` and `r` readings are green readings; they do not comment on the source. Kallosh and Linde (2021) make the same point in cosmology: attractor models are *"compatible with any value of the tensor to scalar ratio r, all the way down to r = 0."*

## 4. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an inflaton? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework licenses naming the driver.** The framework returns a shape. A particular field or potential is licensed only by a ground measurement that discriminates among sources, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this solution commit the forbidden error?** No, because it does not name a driver. It names *slots* and *instruments*, which are observables read off the world (Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*). It leaves the generator unnamed, which is exactly the shape limit.

### Correction to my own §3

I overstated the rule in a first draft by writing that the framework forbids naming any term it does not derive. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Here the missing thing is a **source-discriminating instrument**, not a seventh model term. Naming `n_s`, `r` or B-mode polarization is naming observables already in the instrument frame, not inventing a new primitive. The corrected claim is narrower: the framework cannot name the cause, and it cannot turn "consistent with" into identity. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that inflation is true (or false) is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a dish, a detector, a foreground model, settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional and opt-in venues, and no receptiveness rank.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of inflation and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Consequence for this question

The honest output is a stopped search, not an answer. *Library of Babel*: *"If you don't have the answer to a question, it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."* Here the missing thing is an instrument that discriminates sources at the tensor channel. Until it returns something, the answer is a shape with an open slot. That is a verdict, and it is recorded with its lineage rather than filled with a name.
