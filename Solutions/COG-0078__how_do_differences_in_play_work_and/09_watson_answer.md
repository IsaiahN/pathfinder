## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how play, work and storytelling shape a child's learning and social imagination. Option A: treat them as interchangeable doses of one enrichment variable and report one average effect on one development score. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping habitat substitutions with different anchors, keep per-slot verdicts separate, and leave the slot assignment open until a ground settles it. What each leads to: A produces a pooled number that discards the live slot and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B produces a checkable shape, six written refuters, and an honest account of the instrument that is owed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what we predict about a child after an activity and what is observed at the ground. Arity: three activity conditions crossed with two outcome families. Symmetry: the activities are not interchangeable under a symmetric bond, so the arrangement carries the information (Fig 12 :94). Scale: one child's learning loop at first order, and a population's shared library crossed by imagination at second order. Fig 9 :54 and :57 name this a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the child's learning loop.** Actors: child, materials, affordances, more knowledgeable other. Currency: residual per object slot, terms minted and settled against the ground. Anchor: the ground, what the child can actually do. There is no nameable anchor inside the framework for "which activity is best", so that is not a domain verdict; it is a ground question.
- **Domain 2, the activity forms as habitats.** Actors: play, work and storytelling as assemblies of actors, conditions and relations. Currency: contact. Anchor: none for ranking; the habitat is enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43). Question type: union with overlap, because play and work overlap in the world and storytelling is itself a play pattern.
- **Domain 3, social imagination and cultural transmission.** Actors: other minds, narratives, conventions, the shared library. Currency: methods crossing the membrane. Anchor: the ground for what a narrative predicts; structured human judgment for whether a shared meaning is good, because that question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36). Question type: union; a convention is legitimate until it decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the affordance, have terms for what it affords, say what counts as progress, plan a route, carry what worked. Refuse before mint (Formula step 2), settle each first-order term before composing over it (Fig 12 :104), and describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: applying Fig 13's six-term physical substrate directly to the three activities strains, because that set governs a physical system while an activity form is a habitat. Re-census assigns Fig 11 to D2, Fig 3 to the dependency chain and Fig 4 to D3. Fig 13's network line is an analogy only and is not load-bearing (strain S1 in `01` section 6).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the slot assignment and magnitudes do not. The three activities are overlapping habitat substitutions, not a partition, not a ladder and not three doses of enrichment. Their effect lives in child-and-habitat and is per slot, so a global development score is the aggregation failure Fig 1 :22 names. They differ at the objective and vocabulary link, which is the link with no instrument (Fig 3 :27). Over a shared atom set they are isomers with a common functional group (Fig 12 :117, :123). Storytelling transmits a method, not a recording (Fig 4 :22, :25), and a shared story is a convention until it decides an outcome (Fig 2 :41, Fig 10 :47). Which named activity fills which slot, and every effect size, are a declared `gap` of the unreached-at-this-budget kind. Verdicts are kept per domain and are never averaged. This is `specified` and ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, six refuters and the lineage before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot claims and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The bond assignment and any provisional slot language are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the overstatements and label the rest. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates a contact change from a within-child change would test Fig 11 :40; an instrument for the objective link would test Fig 3 :27; a method-versus-recording test would test Fig 4 :22 and :25; a global score preserving every live slot would break R1; and two genuinely low-overlap frames, sharing no data, ranged against the same ground would break the verification reading. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
