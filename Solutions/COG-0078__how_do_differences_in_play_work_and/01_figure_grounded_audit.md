## 0. The question under audit, restated in effect terms

> How do differences in play, work, and storytelling shape children's learning and social imagination?

**Option A:** treat the three activities as interchangeable doses of one "enrichment" variable and
report one average effect on one development score.

**Option B:** treat them as a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, keep verdicts
per slot, and refuse to name which activity fills which slot until a ground settles it.

**What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term):** a statement of (i) what in
the child-and-habitat system each activity changes contact with, (ii) what method the child carries
forward, (iii) which per-slot residuals are settled, and (iv) what instrument would discriminate the
three. Until that statement is bound, the sought-for shape is a preference, not a term, and Fig 5
:61 says nothing here can see it. This audit binds it in section 3.

**Residual R in effect terms:** the gap between what we predict about a child after an activity and
what is observed at the ground. Arity: three activity conditions crossed with two outcome families.
Symmetry: the activities are not interchangeable, so the arrangement, not the ingredient list,
carries the information (Fig 12 :94). Scale: the learning loop runs inside one child; social
imagination crosses the membrane to a population's shared library (Fig 4 :4-5).

**Partition or union.** D2 is a union, not a partition. Play and work overlap in the world:
*Many communities use play to emulate work* (Wikipedia, Play (activity), opened 2026-11-22), and
Morelli, Rogoff and Angelillo's title alone, *Cultural variation in young children's access to work
or involvement in specialised child-focused activities* (2003), names two conditions that co-occur.
Storytelling overlaps both: the National Institute for Play listed *storytelling play* as one of its
seven play patterns. Under Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping
conditions, not a list of separate ones"*, unbundling is required before search. A single lever that
clears "play only" leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance.
Each link consumes what the one above produces"*. The chain here is: perceive the affordance, have
terms for what the activity affords, say what counts as progress, plan a route, carry what worked.
Refuse before mint (Formula step 2), settle a first-order term before composing over it (Fig 12
:104), and describe the residual before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. Findings F1 to F7

### F1 : This is a union compound, not one mechanism (triangulated)

Fig 9 :54 and :57 govern: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle
before you search again"* and *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19
and :22 govern independently: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* and
*"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Three activities crossed with two outcome families is a union with overlap (section 1). A single
"effect of enrichment" number is precisely the average that throws away the live slot. **Verdict:
`triangulated`**, Fig 9 (union logic) and Fig 1 (per-slot indexing) having low overlap. **Refuter:**
show a single scalar that preserves every per-slot residual a per-slot measurement preserves. Until
that is run, no pooled effect size may be reported as the answer.

### F2 : An activity shapes the child by changing contact, not by improving the child in isolation (triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40:
*"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 6
:23, from a different figure: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact
with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is
substitution of one habitat for another."*

Play, work and storytelling are therefore three habitat substitutions, and the effect lives in the
child-and-habitat system. Fig 11 :49 adds: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a
property it carries"*, so no activity is inherently good or bad. **Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 11
and Fig 6 with different quantities (contact and closure). **Refuter:** a child's performance
improves with no measurable change in what the child is in contact with; that would break the
capability claim. World corroboration is strong and low-overlap: Gibb et al. 2021 describe a
play-based program whose first step was *"to build adult capability"* and whose second was to build
delivery skill in the school setting, that is, a habitat change, not an isolated child change.

### F3 : The three activities differ at the objective link, the link with no instrument (specified, single-source)

Fig 3 :24 names *"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."* Fig 3 :26: *"Perception has
obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests."* The link
between them, having terms for what a situation affords and saying what counts as progress, has
neither, and Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has
looked at."*

Reading each activity at that link, with bond vocabulary from Fig 12 :112 (*"A goal is a comparison"*)
and Fig 12 :77 (*"A produces B; B did not exist before"*):

- **Work** typically arrives with an external standard supplied by the habitat. Progress is a
  comparison against a standard the child did not set (Fig 12 :112; Operators table :51, "a goal is
  a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one").
- **Play** typically supplies a child-generated objective inside a network of affordances. Free-form
  play lets the child change the rules and the objective at any time (Wikipedia, Play (activity):
  *"Both the activity and the rules are subject to change in this form, and children can make any
  changes to the rules or objectives of the play at any time."*).
- **Storytelling** supplies a comparison of possible worlds and produces a representation not
  present before (Fig 12 :77). Narrative psychology describes *"how human beings construct stories
  to deal with experiences"* (Wikipedia, Narrative psychology).

So the measured differences among activities are most likely at the objective/vocabulary link, which
is exactly the unmeasured link. Work on perception or planning will find activity differences that
are real but not the ones the question asks about. **Verdict: `specified`, single-source** for the
bond assignment: Fig 12 defines the bonds but never assigns them to play, work or storytelling, so
this is a structural reading, not a figure derivation. **Refuter:** show two activities with the same
objective/vocabulary structure differing only at perception or planning; the assignment then fails.

**Retraction, kept visible.** An earlier draft of this audit asserted "storytelling is a production
bond, work is a comparison bond" as figure-derived. That is retracted. The figures license the bond
definitions and the dependency chain; the mapping onto named activities is our binding and must be
carried as `specified`, ungrounded, like the Gradient binding in the exemplar.

### F4 : Learning is per-slot settlement, not a global maturity score (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19 and :22 govern (section F1). Fig 5 :32 closes the loop: *"then check it against the ground,
then carry it up."* A term is settled only when the ground pays; a gate is not the ground
(`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5). Adornetti et al. 2024 supply a low-overlap empirical dissociation:
autistic and typically developing children, matched on age, IQ, working memory, attention, theory of
mind and phonological short-term memory, still differed separately at micro-linguistic and
macro-linguistic levels, with *"more errors of global coherence and a fewer number of visible events
and inferred events"* in the narrative domain. One global language score would have hidden which
slot was live. **Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 1 (per-slot) and Fig 5 (settle then carry). **Refuter:**
a global score that preserves all live slots and correctly predicts where to look next.

### F5 : Social imagination is cross-scale, and storytelling carries a method, not a recording (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried
upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Storytelling that hands a child
a fixed script transmits a recording. Storytelling that hands a child a way to simulate another mind
transmits a generator. Theory of mind research names the second directly: simulation theory argues
that individuals *"simulate the internal states of others to build mental models for their cognitive
processes"* (Wikipedia, Theory of mind).

Fig 2 :41: *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* makes
convention legitimate in D3. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being
one"* bounds it: a shared story may constitute a shared identity, but it may not decide who counts
as learned. **Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 4 (membrane) and Fig 2 (convention/verdict split),
different quantities. **Refuter:** a story transmitted as a verbatim recording produces the same
reusable perspective-taking effect as one transmitted as a method; then the distinction collapses.

### F6 : Verification frame: agreement here is one pool talking (general structural)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2
:35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40:
*"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* In this
simulated run the primary seat, the second seat and the world sources are read by one agent in one
session. Their agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. This is stated again in
`09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md`. **Verdict: `general`**, structural, not a world finding.
**Refuter:** two frames with genuinely low overlap, for example an instrument reading and an
independent field report, brought into agreement without sharing data.

### F7 : The honest gap (unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle)

No figure line governs which named activity fills which per-slot effect, or any effect magnitude.
The framework returns Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*, and Fig 13
:134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, with Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds
nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The world literature independently confirms the gap rather than filling it. Cankaya et al. 2023
write: *"no previous research has explored the direct relationship between preschool-age children's
indoor loose parts play experiences and cognitive development."* That is a stated absence of an
instrument, not evidence that no effect exists. What is owed: (i) an instrument that discriminates a
contact change from a within-child change, (ii) per-slot measurement instead of a global score, and
(iii) a source-discriminating test for method versus recording in storytelling. Under Fig 8 :40-43,
this is `unreached at this budget`, not `underivable in principle`, so the instruction is more search
with independent searchers, not an import.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. Seat Map :287, local file: *"a falsifier written before the run is a
design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

R1. A single global development score preserves every per-slot residual a per-slot measurement
preserves and correctly predicts where to look next. (Breaks F1, F4.)
R2. A child's performance improves with no measurable change in what the child is in contact with.
(Breaks F2, Fig 11 :40.)
R3. Two of the three activities are shown to have the same objective/vocabulary structure, differing
only at perception or planning. (Breaks F3, Fig 3 :24-27.)
R4. A story transmitted as a verbatim recording produces the same reusable perspective-taking effect
as one transmitted as a method. (Breaks F5, Fig 4 :22 and :25.)
R5. A figure re-census shows the six-term physical substrate governs the three activity forms
directly, rather than the habitat and dependency-chain figures governing them. (Changes the strain
status in section 6 and the bond assignment in F3.)
R6. Two genuinely low-overlap frames, for example an instrument reading and an independent field
report, disagree about the same per-slot residual after both are ranged against the same ground.
(Breaks the verification reading in F6.)

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled
`grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Report three per-slot verdicts, never one pooled "enrichment effect" (F1, F4).
2. Describe each activity as a habitat substitution and state what contact it changes (F2).
3. Aim the next instrument at the objective/vocabulary link, not at perception or planning, and say
   that this link currently has no instrument (F3, Fig 3 :27).
4. Treat shared stories as conventions for shared meaning, never as verdicts about who learned (F5).
5. State the gap plainly and owe the instrument rather than inventing an effect size (F7).
6. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
   Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not a conclusion.

## 6. Canonical usage check and strains

Read after sections 0 to 5: `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `THE_FORMULA.md` and the seat map. The
figures README says *"They outrank everything else in the repo... the quotes are the authority, not
this summary"* and requires that *"a reading that makes one contradict another has misidentified its
domain, not found a flaw."*

**Strain S1, re-census performed.** An early draft applied Fig 13's six-term physical substrate
(space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) directly to play, work and storytelling and read
the three activities as compositions of those six. That strains: Fig 13's set is the substrate of a
physical system, while an activity form is a habitat of actors, conditions and relations. Re-census:
Fig 11 governs D2, Fig 3 governs the dependency chain, Fig 4 governs D3. Fig 13 :24, *"that entities
relate, and influence travels"*, is used only as a structural analogy for the network shape of
social relations and is explicitly **not** load-bearing. Strain named, not hidden; refuter R5 stands.

**Strain S2, dissolved by re-census.** Gibb et al. 2021 report that *"play has been shown to be a
powerful means to enhance learning and brain development"*, which can be read as "the child improves
in isolation" and so conflict with Fig 11 :40. Re-census: the program changed adult capability and
school delivery, that is, the habitat. Once ranged against the same residual there is no conflict.
The world source corroborates F2; it does not override it.

**Strain S3, dissolved as one witness, not two.** Theory of mind cultural-variation evidence (Iranian
and Chinese children reach knowledge access before diverse belief, reversing the Western order) and
Wikipedia's cultural-differences account of play both come from the same encyclopaedic pool. Their
agreement with Fig 1 :19 (per-slot) is one witness, declared, never two.

**Strain S4, narrowed.** *"Shape, not a name"* (Fig 9 :32) was initially read as forbidding us to
name play, work or storytelling at all. Corrected: the rule governs a primitive not held in the
library. Naming a habitat arrangement is a provenanced reading off the world, legitimate if recorded
as our binding. The forbidden move is presenting that binding as a figure derivation.
