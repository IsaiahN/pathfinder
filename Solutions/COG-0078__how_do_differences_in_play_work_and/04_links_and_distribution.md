## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three findings from the sources actually read are worth stating plainly to a checker:

1. **Play and work overlap rather than partition.** Play (activity) reports that many communities use
   play to emulate work and that Mayan children are encouraged to play while watching parents do
   household work. This is why Fig 9 :55 governs: a single lever for play leaves the work overlap
   standing.
2. **The link with no instrument is measurable in principle but not yet measured.** Cankaya et al.
   2023 explicitly name the absence of direct research on loose parts play and cognitive development,
   while Gibb et al. 2021 report improvement after a program that changed the adult and school
   habitat. These are compatible: the second changes contact, the first says the discriminating
   instrument is missing.
3. **Culture changes which slot is valued, not whether the slot exists.** Theory of mind reports a
   reversal in the order in which Iranian and Chinese children pass knowledge-access and
   diverse-belief tasks relative to Western children. Cultural psychology reports the Whiting model's
   "cultural learning environment" with an activity in progress, a physical space, a characteristic
   group and norms of behaviour. Both corroborate Fig 1 :19 (per slot) and Fig 11 :43 (the habitat is
   enumerated).

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the per-slot claims without
holding the framework's interior. Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*;
Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

**Stage 2, opt in only.** If curious, read the theory that produced it. Their stage-1 verdict is
recorded first and is never pooled as corroboration later (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction to state a ground and write a
refuter before running it. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its
own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the bond
assignment in F3 is a `specified` binding, not a figure derivation, and the slot assignment is a
declared gap. Both must be stated before sending.
