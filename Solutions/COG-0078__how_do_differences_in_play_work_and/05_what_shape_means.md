## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would
have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is
small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For this question, "an activity substitutes a habitat and changes which slots are
live" is a shape. "Play builds attention, work builds independence, storytelling builds imagination"
is a name, and nothing in the figures licenses that specific assignment (see F3 and F7 of `01`).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring
  new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the
  space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a
  plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
  This is why the outreach in `02` leads with refuters rather than with theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be
  the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the
  process of locating it."*

For this question, describing the residual in effect terms means saying "what changes in the child's
contact and what is carried forward", not "what enrichment dose does each activity deliver". The
distinction matters because any frame picked after the word "enrichment" will seem to fit (Fig 9
:27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can
   distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing
space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's
careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."*
Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The activity vocabulary (play, work, storytelling) is a reading off the human cultural habitat, not
a seventh primitive and not a derivation from the six-term physical substrate. Declaring it makes a
choice visible; it does not make it neutral. Section 6 of `01` records the strain this created and
the re-census that resolved it.

## 4. Why the source question matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism (carried). Metamerism: an instrument reads
green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, whether one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or
CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths
create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An
instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the
spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: an observed improvement in a child after storytelling does not identify the source of
that improvement. It could be a changed habitat, a changed objective, a changed method carried
forward, or a convention about shared meaning. A single post-test reading cannot comment on the
source. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, which is exactly the gap in F7 of `01`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The
agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q2: what licenses a particular activity in the slot?

**Answer:** nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. Naming play or work or
storytelling as *the* cause of a per-slot effect is licensed only by (i) the ground settling a minted
term, or (ii) a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's
own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the error?** It names the three activities throughout, so the question is
fair. The answer is no, because the naming is the object of study read off the world (the activities
are what the question gives us), not a name assigned to a framework slot. What would be the error is
assigning one of them to the objective link as if Fig 3 derived it. F3 explicitly retracts that and
marks the assignment `specified`, ungrounded. The distinction is the one the exemplar discovered in
its own §5b: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a primitive not held in the library, not a binding
onto something already in view.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must
never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof...
Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an
action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of
well-known developmental psychologists assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse,
not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground to settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly
simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived
from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is
a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of
the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
