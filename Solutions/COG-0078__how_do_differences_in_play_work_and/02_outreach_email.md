**Subject:** One checkable claim about play, work and storytelling, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking
> you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not
> survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified` on the shape, `general` on the structure):** we want to know how
> differences among play, work and storytelling change what a child is in contact with and what the
> child carries forward into learning and into representing other minds. The three activities are not
> three doses of one enrichment variable. They overlap, and they differ most at the step where a
> child has terms for what a situation affords and says what would count as progress. That is the
> step in the dependency chain that has no instrument, so most measurement lands one link too early
> or one link too late.
>
> **The shape claim, with mode attached:** an activity shapes a child by substituting a habitat, not
> by improving the child in isolation. Effects are per slot, so a single development score will hide
> the live signal. A shared story is a convention about shared meaning, which is legitimate, until it
> is used to decide an outcome such as who counts as learned, at which point it has stopped being a
> convention. Storytelling transmits a reusable way of modelling another mind when it transmits a
> method, and transmits only one occasion when it transmits a fixed script.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not claiming which named activity fills which slot, or any effect
> size. Which activity does what for which child, and by how much, is an open `gap` in this material,
> not a result. I am not claiming the framework names the activity. It returns a shape. Assigning
> play, work or storytelling to that shape is my binding from the field's own vocabulary and is
> recorded as such.
>
> **What would refute it (written down before this message):**
> 1. a single global score preserves every per-slot residual and predicts where to look next;
> 2. a child improves with no measurable change in what the child is in contact with;
> 3. two of the three activities share the same objective and vocabulary structure, differing only
>    at perception or planning;
> 4. a verbatim recording of a story produces the same reusable perspective-taking effect as a
>    method;
> 5. two genuinely low-overlap frames disagree about the same per-slot residual after both are
>    ranged against the same ground.
>
> If any holds, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more
> useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are
> the authority.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md

> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported
