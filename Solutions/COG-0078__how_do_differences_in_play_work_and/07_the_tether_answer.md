# The Tether's answer on play, work, storytelling, learning and social imagination

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-domain shape claims, `general` on the cross-domain structure,
`gap` on which activity occupies which slot and on all magnitudes. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape
and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We want to know how three different activity forms change a child's learning and social imagination.
In effect terms the residual is the gap between what we predict after an activity and what is observed
at the ground. It is a union compound with three activity conditions crossed with two outcome
families (Fig 9 :54, :57), not one hard question. Arity: several conditions. Symmetry: the activities
are not interchangeable, so the arrangement carries the information (Fig 12 :94). Scale: one child's
learning loop, and a population's shared library crossed by imagination (Fig 4 :4-5).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, the learning loop.**
1. Effects are per slot, never one maturity score. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object
   slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into
   an average."*
2. An activity changes what the child is in contact with. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not
   change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property
   of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
3. A term is learned when the ground pays for it, not when the activity is completed. Fig 5 :32:
   *"then check it against the ground, then carry it up."*
4. The activities differ where the chain most often breaks: having terms for what a situation affords
   and saying what counts as progress. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that
   works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**D2, the activity forms as habitats.**
5. Play, work and storytelling are three habitat substitutions (Fig 11 :66). No activity has fixed
   valence: Fig 11 :49, *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
6. They are not a partition. Play and work overlap in the world, and storytelling is itself a play
   pattern. Fig 9 :55 governs: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not
   a list of separate ones."*
7. Over a shared atom set they are isomers (Fig 12 :117): *"Same atoms, different bond, different
   substance."* Their common functional group (Fig 12 :123) is why a coarse measure finds no
   difference between them.

**D3, social imagination and cultural transmission.**
8. Storytelling transmits a method when it hands over a reusable way to model another mind, and a
   recording when it hands over a fixed script. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts
   come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of
   one occasion."*
9. A shared story is a convention about shared meaning, where agreement is the product (Fig 2 :41),
   until it decides an outcome such as who counts as learned, at which point Fig 10 :47 applies:
   *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
10. The slot is not named by the framework. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer
    itself"*. Naming which activity does what is our binding from the field's vocabulary, recorded as
    such, not a derivation.

Verdicts are kept separate per domain and are never averaged into a single "enrichment effect".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which activity fills which slot, and not any effect size. That is a declared `gap`.
- Not that play is better than work, or that storytelling is a higher stage. The activities are not
  seniority ordered (`06` Q1).
- Not that the framework derives the bond assignment. Fig 12 defines bonds; assigning them to named
  activities is `specified`, ungrounded, and the earlier overstatement is retracted in `01` F3.
- Not that the six-term physical substrate governs activity forms. That was a strain, re-censused to
  the habitat and dependency figures (`01` section 6, S1).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground settles `left(R,φ)` here.

## 4. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in
`05`) notes that an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer
resolved it. Here:

1. **Contact discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes a change in what the child is in
   contact with from a change internal to the child. Without it, Fig 11 :40 cannot be tested.
2. **Objective-link instrument:** a measure of what counts as progress from the child's own terms in
   each activity. Fig 3 :27 says this link currently has no instrument.
3. **Method-versus-recording test:** does a child who received a fixed script produce a reusable
   model of another mind, or only a replay of one occasion? Fig 4 :22 and :25 predict a difference.
4. **Per-slot replication:** a single global score that preserves every live slot would break R1.
5. **Low-overlap frames:** an instrument reading and an independent field report, sharing no data,
   ranged against the same ground. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they
   draw on the same evidence."*

Standing refuters from `01` section 4, written before the conclusion, are R1 to R6.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, not unreachable in principle.** No figure line governs which named
activity produces which effect, and the empirical literature states the absence directly: Cankaya
et al. 2023 report *"no previous research has explored the direct relationship between preschool-age
children's indoor loose parts play experiences and cognitive development."* The framework returns a
shape, not the answer (Fig 9 :32), and Fig 8 :39 says *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof
of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so
the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README, carried).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank play, work and storytelling as three doses of enrichment. It says they are
> three overlapping habitat substitutions, not a partition and not a ladder; that their effect lives
> in the child-and-habitat system, so an improvement with no change in contact changes nothing; that
> effects must be read per slot, because a single development score is exactly the average that hides
> the live signal; that the three differ most at the step where a child says what counts as progress,
> which is the step in the dependency chain with no instrument; and that storytelling carries a
> reusable method for modelling other minds when it transmits a method, and only one occasion when it
> transmits a script. Shared stories are conventions about meaning, legitimate until they decide an
> outcome. The framework refuses to name which activity fills which slot or to give effect sizes. What
> it offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments
> that would close it. The slots stay open until the ground settles them.
