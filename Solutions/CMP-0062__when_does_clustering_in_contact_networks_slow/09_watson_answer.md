## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the two reported effects of clustering in contact networks. Option A: treat clustering as one structural factor with a universal sign and ask which sign wins. Option B: treat the two outcomes as a union of conditions with different anchors and derive a separator. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The failure of a scalar clustering reading to predict observed infection outcomes across clusters and regimes. Arity: clustering is a three-place relation (a triangle); transmission is an ordered sequence; persistence is a threshold comparison. Symmetry: the contact edge may be undirected, but transmission is directed in time, so the relation is not symmetric. Scale: node and edge, triangle/clique/community, whole network. Fig 9 names the question a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, contact structure.** Actors: individuals, edges, triangles, cliques, communities; the network structure on which "entities relate, and influence travels" (Fig 13 :24). Currency: connectivity, transitivity, arrangement, contact duration. Anchor: measured contact pattern from diaries, proximity loggers or contact tracing. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, transmission dynamics.** Actors: susceptible, exposed, infected, removed hosts; pathogen. Currency: infection events, growth rate, final size, peak timing. Anchor: observed incidence and serology. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, persistence and threshold.** Actors: pathogen, host population, reinfection or waning immunity, reservoir group. Currency: reproduction number against a threshold, disease lifetime, endemic state. Anchor: observed fade-out versus endemicity. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, measurement and model frame.** Actors: clustering coefficient, closure terms, generating functions, model assumptions. Currency: predictions, residuals, biases. Anchor: the ground, never model agreement. Admitted.
- **Excluded:** "clustering" as a free-floating scalar cause with one universal sign. No anchor independent of the outcome, so not a domain yet. Group size is absorbed into Domain 1 as a habitat condition.
- **Order by dependency.** Measure the contact pattern before simulating spread; decide which quantity (speed or persistence) and which immunity regime before choosing a model; discriminate arrangement before using a coefficient; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's six physical terms to nodes and edges fit only by renaming social quantities as physical ones. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Clustering slows transmission when it spends a contact on an already-reached susceptible slot, so local pools are depleted and the chain cannot open new ground; it creates a persistent reservoir when the same closed path re-enters infection through reinfection or waning immunity, so the triangle becomes a cycle. These are two members of one functional group with different anchors, kept separate and never averaged. The controlling object is the arrangement (bond), not the scalar clustering coefficient. Degree heterogeneity can move speed and final size in opposite directions. The flip is a threshold comparison whose operand type is a live strain; no figure line returns a number, and no instrument here identifies the arrangement in an observed network. Mode `specified` on the separator, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number and the arrangement, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the regime, the strain and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a blended "clustering effect" that sends an intervention in the wrong direction, for example breaking up clusters to shorten an outbreak when the system is SIS-like and the same clusters are the reservoir. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That means holding degree sequence and immunity regime fixed and buying a second, low-overlap frame.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar claim is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` can be closed by an instrument without overturning the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment or natural comparison that holds degree sequence and transmissibility fixed and shows the same arrangement failing to slow SIR and failing to speed SIS would reverse the separator; a matched-network study showing no difference between arrangements would reverse the isomer claim; an instrument that recovers arrangement from outbreak data with calibrated error would close the gap; a figure re-census showing that ⋛'s operands are quantities at every level would close S2 against this reading; deriving one of the six substrate terms from the other five would break the figure side. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
