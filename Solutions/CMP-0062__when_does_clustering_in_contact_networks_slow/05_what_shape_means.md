## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it or measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0062 the shape is a **separator with two anchors**, not a number:

- `clustering + permanent removal -> local depletion, slower and smaller outbreak`
- `clustering + re-entry of infection -> cycle, reservoir, lower threshold and longer lifetime`

"One coefficient with one sign" is a name, and a false one. "A separator that asks which quantity and which immunity regime before assigning a sign" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: "use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the bootstrapped vocabulary runs from the loose word "clustering" to the discriminated terms triangle, clique, community, arrangement, depletion, cycle, re-entry, reservoir.

The effect terms here are "fewer new susceptible slots per infection" and "infection re-enters the same cluster".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried, not re-opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."

- *The Library of Babel* on stopping, opened directly: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that would be an experiment or a natural experiment that holds degree sequence and immunity regime fixed and pays for the separator with observed incidence and fade-out.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The badger and communal-nursery studies are such an import from disease ecology, and their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried, not re-opened): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The "network" substrate term in Fig 13 :24 is a visible choice, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why "the coefficient is the cause" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried, not re-opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The clustering coefficient is a reading of a contact pattern. From that reading alone you cannot tell whether the source is disjoint triangles, shared-edge motifs, cliques, or communities with bridges. Rolls et al. 2015 and Volz et al. 2011 both report that different arrangements with the same coefficient give different epidemic dynamics. Welch 2011 reports that even a full transmission tree does not recover the coefficient independently of the degree sequence. That is metamerism: a source named from a lossy projection, where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This package is therefore a candidate shape, not a settled result.

## 5. Correction to an overstatement (kept visible)

An early pass of this reading said the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any structure in the answer. That is too broad. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive or instrument not held in the library. Here, "network" is already a held structure (Fig 13 :24), and "arrangement", "cycle" and "threshold comparison" are already in the bond and operator vocabularies (Fig 12, Operators table). Naming them is a binding from held vocabulary onto the contact-network frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (i) inventing a seventh substrate term, (ii) claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"), and (iii) naming a numerical flip point or a specific arrangement in an observed network without an instrument. The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried against incidence and persistence data.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the epidemiological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
