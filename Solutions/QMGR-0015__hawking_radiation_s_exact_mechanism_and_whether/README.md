## The question as an effect

Two outcomes are being weighed. If the radiation's form is fixed by the horizon alone, then two collapses with the same mass, charge and spin produce the same coarse-grained spectrum, and the collapse history is not recoverable from that spectrum. If the radiation's form carries collapse detail, then the spectrum (or its correlations) is a channel back to the initial state, and the coarse-grained thermal description is incomplete. The framework decides which of these is reachable from the figures and which is owed to a ground we do not hold.

An answer would look like: a shape for the mechanism, a verdict per domain on collapse-dependence, a named instrument that would close the open part, and a pre-registered refuter. Those are bound as ordinary terms in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 and `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 5.
