## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "does Hawking radiation's spectrum depend on the details of collapse?" is two conditions bundled together. At the coarse-grained thermal level the answer is no by construction: a blackbody spectrum is one parameter, temperature, and aggregation across modes is exactly what discards per-history detail. At the fine-grained level the answer is unreached: no gravitational instrument returns the relevant state, and a search that finds nothing would not prove absence. The emission mechanism has a legal composition shape, energy reaches a thermal ordering only through a structure at the horizon, but the framework returns the shape and not the name of the structure. The analogue BEC observation is a substituted habitat whose round trip gap to gravity must be enumerated. What is offered is the level split, the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the open part.
