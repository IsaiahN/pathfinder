## What the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Universality is a coarse-grained statement.** The information-paradox article records that the form of the radiation depends on a single temperature parameter, while the initial state cannot be characterized by one parameter. That is the figure-1 aggregation finding in physics language, not a separate witness for it.
2. **The level split is already present in the field.** The no-hair theorem constrains the relaxation of the exterior geometry; the information paradox concerns the fine-grained state of the radiation. They are different conditions, matching the union reading in `01` F5.
3. **The analogue result is real but substituted.** Steinhauer's experiment gives an approximately thermal distribution and entanglement in a BEC. Under Fig 11 :70 it proves wiring, not gravitational capability. The round trip to gravity carries a gap that includes the absence of dynamical curvature, different short-distance dispersion and absent gravitational back-reaction.
4. **The 1976 and 2005 positions differ.** Hawking's 2005 abstract says elementary quantum gravity interactions do not lose information. So "Hawking's view" is not a single stable witness; citing it as one would be a provenance error.
5. **Soft hair is an isomer claim in figure terms.** Different soft hair with otherwise identical black holes yields exact relations between evaporation products. That is "same atoms, different arrangement, different evaporation", which `06_isomers_and_order.md` tests against Fig 12 :117.
