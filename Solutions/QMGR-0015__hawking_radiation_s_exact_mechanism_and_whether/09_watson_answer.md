## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question must be split by level. At the coarse-grained thermal level the spectrum does not depend on collapse details beyond M, Q, J; this is `triangulated` (Fig 1 plus Fig 4) and conditional on the settled exterior. At the fine-grained level the dependence question is a `gap`, unreachable at this budget, and non-detection is not proof of absence. The mechanism has a legal shape, energy reaches a thermal ordering through a structure at the horizon, but the framework returns that shape and not the name of the mechanism; surface gravity is a provenanced binding with one live type strain. The analogue experiment is a substituted habitat with a non-zero round trip gap. No part is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the two-stage offer is used. The corrected packet states mode, level split and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single universal verdict instead of a level-indexed shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal verdict, the named mechanism and the analogue-as-ground reading are removable without touching the shape derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A gravitational measurement of a history-dependent coarse spectrum at fixed parameters would reverse D2. A fine-grained instrument returning radiation correlations that settle the information question would move D3 from `gap`. An instrument discriminating the horizon derivative's type would clear the D1 strain. A derivation of a direct energy-to-spectrum composition would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
