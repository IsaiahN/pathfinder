## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the mechanism

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel* (first-hand): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is not a mechanism story about virtual pairs. It is the pair of constraints:

1. a structure must mediate between energy and a thermal ordering at the horizon;
2. a coarse-grained, aggregated channel cannot display per-slot collapse detail.

Both are statable without naming which structure or which fine-grained variable. `Energy -> (???) -> Spectrum` is a shape. `Energy -> surface gravity -> Hawking temperature` is a name, and a binding from the physics frame, not a derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, then narrowing

From *The Library of Babel* (first-hand): use the vocabulary you already hold that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms."* The essay states the recursive step: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the known vocabulary is spectrum, gradient, energy, aggregation, anchor. The nearest physics terms are surface gravity, Bogoliubov transformation, greybody factors, fine-grained entropy. The overlap narrows the search to the two levels described in `01` F1, and stops there because the next term (a fine-grained gravitational spectrum) has never registered at any resolution.

Fig 6 :30 (*local figure text*): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual is stated as "does any level of the radiation carry collapse detail?" rather than "is the Bogoliubov coefficient history-dependent?"

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground pays for it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For the gravitational mechanism no ground pays. The analogue BEC ground pays for analogue kinematics only.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming surface gravity as the mediating structure is such an import. It is legitimate once provenanced, and it does not move the wall because Gradient is already among the six.

From *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried via the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. The source-discrimination lesson is the core of this question

From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (first-hand): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

This is exactly the situation at the coarse-grained Hawking spectrum. A thermal reading does not determine its source. Different collapse histories, and different candidate microstructures, can project to the same temperature. The honest response is not to name the source from the reading; it is to say what a source-discriminating instrument would have to resolve. *The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the general form: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 5. Why the validator is external here

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (first-hand): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay is explicit: *"I am not claiming that the Tether is a self-validating axiom system. Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulation, not a test. It cannot settle whether the exact mechanism is pair separation, Bogoliubov mixing or tunnelling, and it cannot settle whether collapse information is preserved. It can only say what shape a settling answer must have and which instruments would close the gap.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this question is prestige-loaded

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (first-hand): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

Hawking radiation research is unusually loaded with famous names. That is exactly the channel Fig 2 :40 disqualifies: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The outreach artifacts therefore ask for a refutation, never an endorsement, and the source ledger records overlap rather than hiding it.

## 7. Correction to an earlier reading in this run

I initially used the phrase "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as if it forbade any identification of the mediating structure. That is an over-reading. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive. Gradient and Spectrum are both among the six held atoms (Fig 13), so selecting one is a binding from the physics frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding was derived from the six alone, or that it moves the wall. Both readings are preserved in `01` section 7 so the correction is visible rather than silent.
