## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Settling comes before emission, and that is figure-derived, not an assumption

- Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* (the short form "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." is the portion used elsewhere).
- The world source on the no-hair theorem records that an isolated unstable black hole *"decays rapidly to a stable black hole"*, and that all other information disappears behind the horizon *"after the black hole 'settles down' (by emitting gravitational wave and electromagnetic waves)."*
- The temperature and the thermal spectrum are defined for the settled, stationary exterior. The radiation is not emitted by the collapsing matter; it is emitted by the settled horizon state.
- ⇒ The chain is: collapse -> settling (D2) -> horizon as a term (Fig 12 :104) -> thermal emission (D1). Settling is the dependency link that makes the thermal description possible. This is Fig 3's dependency order, not a claim about temporal priority alone: effort spent describing the radiation before the settled object exists arrives at a step that cannot use it.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** exhibit a thermal Hawking spectrum from a horizon that has not settled, or show that gravitational-wave ringdown is not a precondition for defining the temperature. If either holds, Q1 falls and the order must be re-censused.
- Status: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 :104 and the no-hair source (low overlap: level-relative irreducibility versus exterior relaxation).

### Q2 - Which link in the chain is instrumentless?

- Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- Link 1 (perceive): an analogue thermal flux returns something. Instrumented.
- Link 2 (vocabulary): spectrum, gradient, energy, aggregation, anchor. Held.
- Link 3 (objective): what would count as collapse-dependence? Not instrumented at the gravitational fine-grained level.
- Link 4 (planning): which instrument? Blocked by link 3.
- ⇒ The chain stops between vocabulary and objective in D3. Fig 3 :22-23: *"a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."* Any claim about the fine-grained spectrum made from this position is a reading of nothing, however confident its prose.
- **Falsifier:** name and operate a gravitational instrument that returns a fine-grained observable of the radiation. Then link 3 is instrumented and the chain can proceed.
- Status: `general`, single-source for the figure (Fig 3), with the literature's unresolved information paradox as corroboration of the break.

### Q3 - Are two different collapse histories isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Weak reading (exterior only).** Two black holes with the same M, Q, J are described by the same exterior term. They are not isomers at that level; they are the *same* substance. A difference invisible in the exterior formula is not an isomer difference until it is shown to be decisive in behaviour.
- **Strong reading (interior or soft hair).** The soft-hair source reports *"an infinite number of exact relations between the evaporation products of black holes which have different soft hair but are otherwise identical."* If the collapse history is encoded in soft degrees of freedom that change the evaporation products, then the two are Fig 12's isomers: same exterior atoms, different hidden arrangement, different evaporation behaviour. The difference is invisible in the coarse-grained thermal formula and decisive in the fine-grained products.
- **Which survives?** Both, at different levels. At the settled exterior level the weak reading holds; the thermal spectrum collapses the history into one term. At the fine-grained level the strong reading is a live isomer claim with a stated falsifier. This is level-relative irreducibility (Fig 12 :104) again: a settled molecule becomes an atom at the next level, and a difference erased at one level can be decisive at the next.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** prove that two black holes with different collapse histories but identical M, Q, J have identical complete fine-grained states, so that no arrangement difference exists at any level. Then the isomer claim fails and the weak reading is all that remains.
- Status: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 :117 and the soft-hair source (low overlap: bond-relative identity versus conservation relations in evaporation).

### Q4 - The analogue system is a substituted habitat, with a measured round trip gap

- Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*
- Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- The analogue BEC experiment observes an *"approximately thermal distribution"* and partner entanglement. That is a real ground for the kinematics of horizon-induced pair creation. It is not a ground for gravitational Hawking radiation. Calling it an *instance* of the framework (as the exemplar did for black-hole thermodynamics) is acceptable only if the provenance is recorded; calling it a *derivation* of the gravitational result is the flat-formula error.
- **Round trip gap R_T, enumerated rather than computed numerically** (no ground reading exists for the gravitational side, so this is a qualitative enumeration):

| item sent up from gravity | what the analogue returns | what is lost on the trip back |
|---|---|---|
| dynamical curved spacetime with back-reaction | fixed or effectively fixed analogue background | gravitational back-reaction and the evolution of the horizon |
| Lorentz-invariant dispersion at all scales | atomic dispersion with a microscope scale | the trans-Planckian behaviour of modes |
| surface gravity set by M, Q, J | effective surface gravity set by flow gradients | the parameter map from exterior state to temperature |
| fine-grained gravitational state of the radiation | BEC partner correlations | the information question entirely |

- The gap is non-zero. Treating the analogue result as identical to the gravitational prediction would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
- **Falsifier:** a controlled analogue experiment in which changing the collapse-like initial conditions changes the thermal form itself, or a proof that dispersion and back-reaction differences cannot affect the spectrum. The first widens R_T, the second narrows it. Neither is a figure conflict.
- Status: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 4.

### Q5 - The strain: is surface gravity the Gradient atom?

- Fig 13 :22 calls a gradient *"a difference that can be spent"* and Fig 13 :76 says *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*
- Surface gravity is a radial (space-like) gradient redshifted to infinity. It plays the role of a difference that can be spent, and the Unruh source notes that the Hawking temperature has the same form as the Unruh temperature with surface gravity in place of acceleration.
- But role identity is not atom identity. Whether surface gravity is literally the Gradient among the six, or a distinct derivative structure that composes the same way, is a type question. The six-term set cannot decide it, and no instrument at this budget discriminates the two at a horizon.
- Named as `strained-for-now`, not corrected away. Matches `01` section 7 and `11_audit.md`.

## CONCLUSION

The chain is collapse -> settling -> horizon as a settled term -> thermal emission, with the instrumentless break located between vocabulary and objective for the fine-grained state. Different collapse histories are identical at the settled exterior level and a real isomer claim at the fine-grained level, which is exactly the level-relativity Fig 12 :104 states. The analogue BEC is a substituted habitat with an enumerated, non-zero round trip gap. One strain remains live: the identification of surface gravity with the Gradient atom is a binding whose type has not been settled by an instrument.

What survives without any new primitive: the order, the isomer test, the gap enumeration and the strain. What does not survive: the idea that the analogue result derives the gravitational spectrum, or that the thermal formula displays the collapse history.
