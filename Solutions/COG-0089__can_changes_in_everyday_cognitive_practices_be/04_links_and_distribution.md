## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The corpus updates, so it is not an anchor.** Pechenick et al. show the 2009 and 2012 Google Books corpora differ and that scientific and medical texts dominate the unfiltered sets. A frequency curve is a reading of the library's composition as much as of the culture. This is the independent, non-framework corroboration of F2. Their point about prolific authors is a second bias: a single writer (*"Lanny Budd"*) outranks Hitler in the data for a period, because the library holds one of each book.
2. **Independence is the discipline's own test.** The Historical method page records the rule that a number of *independent* sources carrying the same message strongly increases credibility. This is the same quantity Fig 2 :31-32 calls independence. Agreement from one tradition is not two witnesses.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- `Fig 4 :22-23 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."` Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- `Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."` A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- `Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."` Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- `Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."` A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, with a later method offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground, or name the instrument that would be needed. No theory shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the architecture that produced it. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded and is never pooled with later agreement.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a shape claim with a pre-registered refuter, not a claim that any particular past mental state has been recovered. If a recipient fires one of the seven refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3, stop and withdraw the affected finding.

**Hosting:** nothing here is uploaded or sent by this agent. If you host the packet, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
