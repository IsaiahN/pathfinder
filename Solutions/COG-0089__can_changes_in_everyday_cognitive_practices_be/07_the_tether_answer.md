# The Tether's answer on reconstructing everyday cognitive practices

**Mode: `specified` on the limits and the shape, `general` on the structural rule, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a report of a past mind. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We can read records. We want to know how everyday cognition changed in the people who made them, and what stops the reading from being a faithful reconstruction. The residual splits into a union compound: the past practice, the process that made the record, and the present frame doing the reconstruction. `Fig 9 :54-56 "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."` Each overlap gets its own loop and its own verdict. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single answer about "the past mind".

## 2. Verdicts per domain, kept separate

**Domain A, the past practice itself: `gap` at this budget.** No live anchor answers for the specific past instance. `Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."` The practice is the residual that drives the search, not a domain that can be settled from a present instrument. `Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."` This is a verdict, not a silence, and it is not the same as saying the practice never existed.

**Domain B, the record and archive: `triangulated`, reconstructable as artefact.** The artefact is what it is, but the archive updates with corpus versions, cataloguing and OCR, so it is a habitat, not an anchor. `Fig 2 :29-30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."` Its contents can be established per slot; its warrant about a past mind cannot.

**Domain C, the reconstruction frame: `triangulated`, checkable from outside.** The frame's categories are present-day compressions. `Fig 5 :62-63 "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."` The remedy is to bind the shape as an ordinary term and commit a refuter before reading the records. `Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."`

**Domain D, the production link: `triangulated` for the limit, `single-source` for the exact figure statement.** The order of the route is not in the artefact. `Fig 13 :129 "The order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."` Two candidate practices with the same artefact stand as isomers under `Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."` The limit is case dependent: a trace can recover the route in some cases, as isotope ratios distinguish a mined from a lab grown diamond.

## 3. The shape that survives

1. **A change in everyday cognitive practice can be reconstructed as a set of constraints, never as a name of a past mental state.** `Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."` What a record licenses is a shape: what must have been in contact for this artefact to be as it is.
2. **The habitat must be enumerated before the record is read.** `Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."` Tools, media, institutions, other people and material conditions are read off the world, not invented from the text. `Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."`
3. **The archive is a habitat, not the ground.** Pechenick et al. (2015) show corpus composition moving while the question stays fixed. A frequency curve is a reading of the library as much as of the culture.
4. **Agreement is worth only what independence buys.** `Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."` `Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."`
5. **Absence is a search report.** `Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."` Report the bucket: SEARCHED, INWARD or OUTWARD. Do not report a past void.
6. **Verdicts are per slot.** `Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."` A global rate such as "literacy rose" is frame-internal and can hide the live local signal.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that historians can never say anything true about the past. The artefact constrains a shape, and a provenanced import or a surviving trace can name a slot.
- Not that no source is better than another. Independence and provenance decide; document count does not.
- Not that the record is the practice, or that the sequence in a chronicle is the sequence in the act.
- Not that a present category is forbidden. It is allowed as a binding, recorded as such, and it adds no atom to the past.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of a specific past cognitive practice was taken, and none exists in this package.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

The instruments named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 are the answer to this. In particular:

- a discriminating trace that identifies which of two candidate practices produced one artefact, the spectrometer move;
- an archive whose composition is invariant under the interventions that move real corpora, so it can act as an anchor;
- two records with demonstrated independent provenance whose agreement survives a third ground that influenced neither;
- an instrument on the middle link of Fig 3's chain, returning a reading when the practice changes, built from a worse instrument already returning something.

None of these is agreement with a theory. Prestige cannot substitute for them. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The shape stops where the available instruments cannot discriminate among candidate sources. *How Intelligence Actually Works* calls this the union horizon: *"being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* A later instrument may sharpen it. The lineage is kept so the stop is checkable. `Fig 13 :133 "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."`

This stop is itself simulated. LLM output is *"simulacra"*; the genuinely testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or the formula worked by a person against a ground. This answer cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not recover past minds from documents. It says that a record is an artefact whose producing route is not written on it: the order is recoverable from the lineage, not from the thing itself. What can be reconstructed is a shape, a set of constraints on everyday practice that the record and its habitat can support, not a name of a past mental state. The archive is a habitat and updates when corpora and catalogues change, so it is not an anchor; records that agree from a shared source are one witness, not several; a global average can hide the live local change; and an absent record is a search report, not proof that a practice never existed. The biases are structural and checkable from outside. What would close the question is a discriminating trace, an invariant anchor, or an instrument on the link between practice and record. None of those is agreement with a theory. The answer stops at the union horizon and records the lineage so the stop can be audited.
