## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* `Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."` Fig 13 :132-133 says a system with a closure can answer whether something could have been reached at all, and *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the set of constraints on a past cognitive practice that the records and their habitat can support, stated before we can name the practice. It is a narrowed form of an answer, not a filled-in portrait of a past mind. "A structure sat between the act and the record" is a shape. "People in this period thought in this exact way" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- `Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."` `Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."`
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on stopping: the operation ends when *"nothing you have registers a difference from it."* It is operational, and it is not the same as reaching the truth. *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

**Consequence for this question.** The search is over effect descriptions of past practice, not over names of mental states. The vocabulary of the present frame is itself a compression shaped by present sensory and institutional priors. *They're Scanning Books*: *"Compression is shaped by the sensory priors of the compressor... there is no neutral representation to aim at."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For a past cognitive practice there is no live ground that answers for the specific instance. What exists are stand ins: material traces, independent dating, cross-cultural or experimental replication. Each is a frame that can be corrected separately; none is the original ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Importing a category from present cognitive science into a reading of a medieval ledger is an import. It must be recorded as such, debited against independence, and it adds no atom to the past.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

## 4. Why reading a practice off an artefact is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet known can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the exact historical-record analogue: a frame extracted from a video and a frame photographed are *"indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* That is provenance loss. The same essay adds the crucial qualification: *"Some processes leave traces and depth recovers them; some produce identical output and no depth helps."* Isotope ratios distinguish a mined diamond from a lab grown one. A historical record may or may not have its equivalent trace.

So the error is not "infer past cognition." It is naming the source off a projection when the projection does not discriminate sources, and then treating the name as if the record had settled it. What is owed is a lineage or a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any cognitive category in a reconstruction. That was too strong. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive: a thing not held in the library. Reading, memory and counting are categories we already hold. Naming one of them as a candidate description of a past practice is a **binding** from the present frame onto a pattern observed in the record, not the invention of a seventh primitive. It is allowed, provided it is provenanced as a binding and not presented as a derivation from the artefact.

What remains wrong in the naive reconstruction is calling that binding a ground licensed reading. Fig 13 :129 says *"The order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* The correct form is a constraint plus its provenance, not a name presented as recovered. Both the original claim and this narrowing are recorded here so neither is silently lost.

## 6. Why this shape is checkable from outside

- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The shape claim is stated as what one would expect to see in a record if a practice had changed: a new distribution of marginal marks, a changed ledger format, a different pronoun balance in a corpus.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Independent discoveries count most"* and *"the past self is a vantage point, never the judge. The ground stays the anchor."* Two frames drawn from different habitats can check a shape claim; a single tradition cannot check itself.
- The eight essays all share an author and an evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. The independent world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv` (Pechenick et al. 2015; Wikipedia on historical method and presentism) carry the external corroboration.

## 7. Simulation, not ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this reading are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
