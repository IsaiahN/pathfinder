## 0. The claim under audit, stated as a residual

The question asks two things that must be kept separate:

1. Can changes in everyday cognitive practices be reconstructed from historical records?
2. What biases limit that reconstruction?

Residual in effect terms. The present frame holds categories for everyday cognition (reading, remembering, counting, attending, keeping time, telling a story) and a set of records (texts, ledgers, diaries, marginalia, objects, digitised corpora). It wants to predict the shape of past practice from those records. `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot: Γ predicts what the records imply about the practice, `o′` is what the records actually are, plus whatever material and comparative traces survive. The gap is not a single global number. `Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."` and `Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."` So the verdict is given per domain and per practice, never averaged into a single answer about "the past mind".

**Arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at least three conditions, the past practice, the record production process, and the present reconstruction frame. That is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-58): a case in two or more conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: the time arrow makes this asymmetric. A practice can produce a record; a record does not uniquely produce the practice. Scale: individual cognitive acts, institutional recording habits, and long run cultural change are different strata with a lossy transform between them. `Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."`

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** A defensible answer is a *shape*: a set of constraints on past practice that any adequate record reading must satisfy, with the limits stated as named biases and a refuter written in advance. The answer must not be a name of one past mental state licensed by the artefact alone. Binding the shape now, before the run, is the discipline the formula requires (*Direction*: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so make the sought-for shape an ordinary term).

### Domain census before choosing any figure line

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain that can be settled.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, the past practice itself | historical persons doing everyday cognition | cognitive acts and their material traces | no live anchor answers for the specific past instance; the ground is dead | residual, not yet a settleable domain |
| B, the record and archive | scribes, printers, institutions, archivists, digitizers, OCR pipelines | surviving inscriptions, books, ledgers, corpora | the artefact is what it is, but the archive updates when corpus composition changes, so it is a habitat, not the ground | settleable as artefact, not as practice |
| C, the reconstruction frame | historians, cognitive scientists, their categories and instruments | hypotheses, models, n-gram frequencies, interpretations | the artefact plus, where available, a live ground (experiment, cross-cultural observation, material constraint) | settleable per slot |
| D, the production link (practice to record) | the same persons as practitioners and record makers; the medium, writing, print, oral tradition | what transfers from a cognitive act into a record | none direct; this is where source underdetermination lives | union compound with its own loop |

**Partition or union.** It is a **union**, not a partition. Domains A, B and D overlap: the people who made the records are the people whose cognition is being inferred. Fig 9 :54-58 says to unbundle a union before searching again, because a case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in one. Each overlapping compound gets its own loop.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** 1. Establish what the record is, with provenance (perception). 2. Have terms for what a record affords (vocabulary). 3. State what would count as progress: a shape of change, refusable (objective). 4. Plan a route: triangulation and imports from low-overlap frames. 5. Learn and carry methods, not recordings. `Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."` The chain usually breaks at link 2 or 3, the middle link with no instrument of its own. `Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."`

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The record is an artefact, not the practice, and the route that produced it is not recoverable from the artefact (decisive)

`Fig 13 :128-129 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."`
`Fig 13 :129 "The order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."`
`Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."`

A historical record is a composed term. It carries the atoms that survived and the arrangement that survived, but the cognitive route that produced it is not written on it. The lineage, the chain of production and custody, is the only place the route can be read. Where the lineage is lost, the artefact cannot be made to speak. *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the exact case: a frame extracted from a video and a frame photographed are *"indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants,"* and *"nothing in the image records which."*

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 13 (lineage is the proof), Fig 12 (arrangement is the substance) and the essay on projection and perception. The three have low structural overlap: composition law, bonding law, and an instrument-style thought experiment.

**Refuter:** a record whose features uniquely determine the cognitive practice that produced it, checked against a ground that discriminates two candidate practices. The diamond case in the essay is the positive form: isotope ratios do distinguish mined from lab grown, because a trace existed. If a historical case behaves that way, F1 is refuted for that case.

### F2 - The archive is a habitat, not an anchor: it updates, so it cannot settle the question

`Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."`
`Fig 2 :29-30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."`

The archive changes when a new corpus is digitised, when OCR improves, when a collection is re-catalogued, when a war removes volumes. Pechenick, Danforth and Dodds (PLOS ONE, 2015) show this directly for Google Books: the 2009 and 2012 corpora differ, scientific journals increasingly dominate the unfiltered set, and the fiction set labelled fiction was *"saturated with medical literature."* Their abstract says the corpus is *"an obscure mask of cultural popularity."* The same authors note its library-like sampling: *"we have one seed for each tree but no view of the real forest that will emerge."* A corpus that changes its answer when its composition changes is a frame output, not a ground.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 habitat, Fig 2 anchor test, independent bibliometrics with no shared author with the framework). Strongest world corroboration in the package.

**Refuter:** exhibit a record set whose composition is invariant under the interventions that changed the corpus versions, or a ground that returns the same answer under every admissible change to the archive. Until then, corpus frequency is a reading of the habitat.

### F3 - Agreement among records from a shared pool is not corroboration

`Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"`
`Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"`
`Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"`

A hundred chronicles that copy one another are one witness wearing a hundred names. The discipline of history states the same condition in its own terms: *"If a number of independent sources contain the same message, the credibility of the message is strongly increased"* (Wikipedia, Historical method, accessed 2026-11-22). Independence is the quantity that decides whether agreement means anything, and it is not the number of documents.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 (ground and collapses), Fig 7 (room chain) and Fig 8 (union surplus), three figures with different subject matter, plus an independent methodological source.

**Refuter:** two records with demonstrated independent provenance, neither drawing on the other's evidence, whose agreement survives a check against a third ground that influenced neither. That would be corroboration at maximum independence and would refute the shared-pool diagnosis for that case.

### F4 - Source underdetermination: the reading cannot comment on the source

`Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"`
Symbols table :38-40: *"it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*
`Fig 6 :34-35 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."`

*The Hard Problem is About Source* names the mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A green reading does not say whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet known. A written record is the same kind of reading. Several cognitive routes can produce the same diary entry, the same ledger, the same marginal note. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the important qualification: some projections leave recoverable traces and some do not, so this is a case dependent finding, not a universal ban on inference.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 borrowed words, Symbols slice, Fig 6 instrument edge, plus the metamerism essay). The world source shares authorship with the figures, so its agreement is expected; the figure lines decide.

**Refuter:** a discriminating instrument for the historical case, the spectrometer move, that distinguishes among the candidate cognitive sources for one artefact. If it works, the underdetermination narrows for that case.

### F5 - Presentism: the sought shape is held as a preference, not a term, so the loop cannot see it

`Fig 5 :62-63 "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."`
`Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"`

Presentism is defined as *"the introduction of present-day ideas and perspectives into depictions or interpretations of the past"* (Wikipedia, Presentism in historical analysis, accessed 2026-11-22). In figure terms, the historian holds a picture of what the answer should look like as a preference. It is unbound, predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so none of the loop's checks can see it. The remedy is structural, not moral: write the sought shape down as an ordinary term, let it accrue a residual, and make it refusable by the same test as everything else. Committing a prediction before the run is this, already working (Fig 5 :66).

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 5 direction limit, Fig 9 borrowed words, independent historiographical source).

**Refuter:** a reconstruction that states its sought-for shape as a checkable term before examining the records, and whose stated shape then fails or survives a ground check. If a frame can certify its own direction, the limit is wrong.

### F6 - Capability is agent-and-habitat: the practice is not carried by the text

`Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."`
`Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."`
`Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."`
`Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."`

Everyday cognitive practice is embodied and situated. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the transfer limit: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* And: *"text alone can't hand it over."* The essay's sharpest line for this question is: *"knowledge can survive in writing while the people who could understand it die out. A manual outlives the craft when no one is left who can read it as it was meant."* The habitat of a practice is enumerated from contact (tools, media, institutions, seasons, other people), not composed from the text. The record is one actor in that habitat, not the whole.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 capability and enumeration, tacit knowledge essay, and the essay on outputs versus generators: *"The text is the frozen output of an ongoing process, and inheriting the output is not inheriting the process."*)

**Refuter:** a cognitive practice fully reconstructable from a document alone, with a ground check that confirms the reconstruction, in a case where nothing outside the document contributed to the capability.

### F7 - The reconstruction is a round trip with a nonzero gap

`Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."`
`Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."`

The trip is from a past practice, up through a record, into a present reconstruction, and back down to a claim about what the past practice was like. The transform loses information in both directions. Fig 4 :27-29 requires the gap to be measured, not assumed zero. The gap is the honest report of what the record cannot hold. Its size is enumerated qualitatively in `11_audit.md`, because no ground reading exists to measure it numerically.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 4 round trip, Fig 10 :68 lossy transform between scales, tacit knowledge essay on the written record as a lossy encoding).

**Refuter:** a closed round trip in which the reconstruction returned to the past case matches the original practice on every slot the record touches, verified at a ground. For a specific past instance that would set `R_T` to zero.

### F8 - Per-slot verdicts: aggregation hides the live signal

`Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."`
`Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."`

An aggregate such as "literacy rose in this period" is a frame-internal score computed from the same records it is meant to explain. It can rise while a particular everyday practice is lost, or fall while a new one appears in a slot the aggregate does not track. The lesson from the multi-agent essay is the same: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average,"* so the rate per kind of situation must be kept, not the total.

**Status:** single-source for the figure law (Fig 1), corroborated by the framework's own methodological essay, which shares the evidence pool. Marked `single-source` for the verdict because no independent discipline was read that licenses this exact quantity.

**Refuter:** show a case where an aggregate statistic predicts every per-slot change with no residual, so the per-slot restriction buys nothing.

### F9 - Missing records are residual, not proof of absence

`Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."`
`Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."`

Archival silence is a reading of the archive, not a fact about the past. *How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the asymmetry plainly: a case with no trace can only ever be reported as *"nothing has been found yet,"* while a case with a trace may close when an instrument improves. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The honest report is "no record found at this resolution", with the search bucket named.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 8 metaframe limit, Fig 9 sealed room, Symbols slice, and the distinction horizon essay).

**Refuter:** a genuinely sealed archive, fully enumerated and closed, in which absence can be proven. The figures say no frame is a sealed room, so the refuter would require a new kind of object.

### F10 - The middle link of the chain has no instrument

`Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."`

Historians can measure texts. Cognitive scientists can measure present cognition. The link between them, "what change in everyday practice would register as this change in the record", has neither an obvious failure nor an obvious test. Fig 3 :24-26 says the middle link attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments because it has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests. Fig 6 :32-34 requires an instrument to be built from a worse instrument already returning something, not from a description.

**Status:** single-source for the figure law (Fig 3), with an independent corroboration in the Google Books case where version changes and OCR errors were detected only after better instruments and metadata analysis were applied. Marked `triangulated` for the existence of the gap, `single-source` for its exact figure statement.

**Refuter:** present an instrument that returns a reading on this middle link, improved from a worse instrument already returning something, and show that the reading changes when the practice changes.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The verdict is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A record is shown to uniquely determine the cognitive practice that produced it, with a ground that discriminates at least two candidate practices (breaks F1 and F4).
2. A corpus or archive is exhibited whose composition is invariant under the interventions that move real corpora, so it can serve as an anchor that does not update (breaks F2).
3. Two historically independent records, with provenance showing no shared evidence pool, agree and their agreement survives a check against a third ground that influenced neither (breaks F3 for that case).
4. A discriminating instrument distinguishes the cognitive sources that yield one artefact, in the way a spectrometer distinguishes the sources of green (breaks F4 for that case).
5. A practice is fully reconstructed from a document alone and confirmed at a ground, with nothing outside the document contributing to the capability (breaks F6).
6. An aggregate statistic predicts every per-slot change with no residual (breaks F8).
7. An instrument returns a reading on the middle link of Fig 3's chain, built from a worse instrument already returning something (breaks F10).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict is re-run from the residual. Until one is run, no part of this answer is `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Report per practice and per stratum, never as a single verdict on "the past mind" (F8, Fig 1 :19).
2. Enumerate the habitat of each practice from contact before reading the record: tools, media, institutions, other people, material constraints (F6, Fig 11 :43-48).
3. Write the sought shape as an ordinary term and commit the refuter before examining the records (F5, Fig 5 :66).
4. Require provenance for every record: which route produced it, which route merely resembles it, and which frame imported the category now being used to read it (F3, F7).
5. Treat archival silence as a search report with a named bucket, not as an absence (F9).
6. Measure the round trip gap `R_T` qualitatively and keep it visible alongside the reconstruction (F7, Fig 4 :30).
7. Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. `Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."`

### Re-census after the canonical usage check

No figure line was found that governs a full reconstruction of a past cognitive practice. After a genuine re-census the "full reconstruction" branch is `gap` at this budget, not silence: `Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."` The reachable residue is the shape, the bias list and the refuters. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, and the search buckets (SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD) remain distinct: SEARCHED for records already in the archive, INWARD for better instruments on the same material, OUTWARD for imports from cognitive science, archaeology and comparative work, each debited against independence.
