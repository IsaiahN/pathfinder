# Tether outreach and derivation package: COG-0089

**Question:** Can changes in everyday cognitive practices be reconstructed from historical records, and what biases limit that reconstruction?

**Source:** `cognitive science.txt` line 108. **Section:** 9. History, institutions, and cultural change. **Field for public contact points:** cognitive practices and cultural change research.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims about what reconstruction can and cannot do, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. No present instrument settles a claim about a past person's cognitive practice, so nothing here is recorded as settled by the ground.

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending (registry is owned by the parent session).

**Boundary.** All work is inside this folder. Nothing outside it was edited. Figure quotes are taken from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and cited as `Fig N :line`. World sources were opened on 2026-11-22 and are listed with author, venue, year, accessed date and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

**Honest standing.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This package cannot settle the question it reports on. It records the shape a defensible answer must have, the biases the figures license, and the instruments that would close the gap.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** `Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."` A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless.
2. **The ground, not a room, decides.** `Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."`
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** `Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."` The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional, public or opt-in.
