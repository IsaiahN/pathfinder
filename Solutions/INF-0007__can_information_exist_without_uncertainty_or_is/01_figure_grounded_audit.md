## 0. The question under audit, stated as an effect

> Can information exist without uncertainty, or is uncertainty essential to the concept?

Effect terms: **information** is whatever makes a difference to what a frame can predict or state; **uncertainty** is the spread of possibilities still open for that frame before a signal arrives. Reading A says the second is a necessary condition of the first, so uncertainty at zero drives information to zero. Reading B says information is a pattern, a truth, an arrangement or a physical state, so it can be present with no observer uncertain. The consequence of A is that a certain message is empty. The consequence of B is that a certain message is empty *only for that observer*, while the arrangement still carries information.

Per the Formula's Direction discipline, the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run: **either one definition governs all domains, or the question is a union of frames with a different verdict in each.** The run then tests that shape.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two terms in a relation, information and uncertainty, so the relation is binary and ordered (information reduces uncertainty, not the reverse). Symmetry: the relation is asymmetric, so the comparison or sequence bonds are candidates and a symmetric join is not. Scale: definitional at the level of a frame's library Γ, and measurable only *inside* a frame (a probability distribution is assigned by someone).

**One line per domain, actors / currency / anchor.** A domain needs a nameable anchor or it is not yet a domain.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union, so it must be split (decisive for method)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

The four meanings of information identified in the world literature (probability, computation, truth, physical state) overlap in the word but not in anchor. Asking "is uncertainty essential to information?" without naming the domain is asking whether a key fits a lock before naming either. World corroboration: the SEP *Information* entry notes that three influential proposals, Shannon/probability, Kolmogorov/computation and Floridi/truth, *"have next to nothing in common"* and some conflict. This is a union, not a single question.

**Refuter:** show by a grounded derivation that all four domains reduce to one quantity with one anchor. If that is shown, the union verdict fails and the rest of this audit must be re-run.

### F2 — In the figures, information is the bond, not the entropy

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands."*
Fig 12 :47: *"recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*

The framework already uses the word "information" for the arrangement that the operands do not carry. That is a structural use. It is not Shannon entropy. A strain test confirms the two are different quantities: forcing Fig 12 :5 to govern a probability spread fails, because a typed relation between operands is not a measure of surprise. Per the strain rule, the line governs another quantity, so this is a separate domain (D5), not a reading of D1.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a bond's typed-relation information is the same quantity as Shannon uncertainty, with a shared unit and a shared anchor. Until then the two domains stay split.

### F3 — A zero-information case exists inside the figures

Fig 12 :100: *"With one bond type the term is zero, and it rises as the vocabulary of bonds grows, and only then."*
Fig 12 :101: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."*

The figures admit an arrangement that carries no information (the flat or free-bond case). This is the closest figure-side analogue of "zero uncertainty gives zero information," but it is a fact about the *vocabulary of bonds*, not about an observer's uncertainty. So the figures do contain a zero-information limit; they do not attribute it to uncertainty. World corroboration: entropy is zero exactly when the outcome is certain, so D1's limit is a theorem inside D1, not a law across domains.

**Refuter:** show that the flat case in Fig 12 is driven by an observer's uncertainty rather than by the number of bond types. That would merge D5 and D1 and falsify the separation.

### F4 — Uncertainty is frame-internal; a global zero does not erase a live slot

Symbols table :38: *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it. It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

R is a gap between a prediction made by Γ and an observation. Entropy likewise is assigned from a distribution inside a frame. Fig 1 :27 disqualifies a quantity a frame scores with something it also produces. So "uncertainty" is a property of a frame's state, not of the world as such. This supports the frame-relativity part of the verdict. It also warns that an aggregate zero is not the absence of a local signal: R is *"always a slice,"* so "no uncertainty" at one resolution need not mean no information at another.

**Refuter:** exhibit a frame-independent measure of uncertainty that does not depend on any Γ, and show that the ground settles it. That would break the frame-relativity claim.

### F5 — From inside a frame, "unresolved" and "no fact" look the same

Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

When a receiver says "there is no uncertainty here, so no information," they may be reporting either a settled fact (genuine absence of alternatives) or the limit of their own instrument (depth). The framework cannot tell these apart from inside. This is why the question cannot be answered by inspecting a single frame's entropy value.

**Refuter:** provide an instrument that discriminates settled certainty from unresolved depth inside one frame. The figures say such an instrument would have to come from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), so this refuter is about importing a new instrument, not about re-reading the same measure.

### F6 — Information is a derivative, not one of the six primitives

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
Fig 13 :124: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

"Information" is not among the six terms (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network). Like smoothness or chaos, it is something a composition leaves behind. That is why asking whether it is "essential" in the primitive sense is a category error: derivatives are not essential or inessential in the way a primitive is, they are what a particular arrangement produces.

**Refuter:** derive information as a seventh member from the six, or show it is one of the six under a different name. Either would break Fig 13's minimality and reopen the census.

### F7 — The definitional question is a convention, not a ground verdict

Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*
Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*
Fig 10 :46: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."*
Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*

"Which definition of information is the correct one" is constitutively about a shared meaning, so agreement there is a convention, not a verdict. Fig 10 :47 disqualifies a convention that decides an outcome, so the definition may not be smuggled in as a premise and then used to settle the question. The legitimate output is the split plus each domain's verdict, not a single winner.

**Refuter:** show a measurement that settles which meaning of "information" is correct, where the question is constitutively about that measurement. If none can be named, the convention verdict stands.

### F9 — What actually survives

1. The question is a union of frames, not one question (Fig 9 :54-57). `general`, triangulated.
2. In the entropy frame, uncertainty is essential by definition and by theorem. `grounded` inside that frame.
3. In the semantic, algorithmic and physical frames, information exists without an observer's uncertainty. `specified` (physical: `grounded`).
4. In the figures, information is the typed bond and the derivative a composition leaves behind, not a primitive and not Shannon entropy. `general`, triangulated.
5. Which definition "wins" is a convention the ground does not settle. `general`.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A grounded derivation reduces D1 to D4 to one quantity with one anchor, collapsing the union.
2. Fig 12's bond-information is shown to be the same quantity as Shannon uncertainty (same unit and anchor), collapsing the re-census and proving a line was strained.
3. A frame-independent measure of uncertainty is exhibited that does not depend on any Γ.
4. A true, meaningful proposition, a determined string or a physical bit is shown to carry zero information in every frame, which would refute "information exists without uncertainty."
5. A measurement settles which definition of information is correct, making the convention verdict false.

If any fires, the verdict is withdrawn and the census re-run. Until one is run, nothing beyond the in-frame theorems and the measured Landauer bound may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the split (F1). Do not answer "yes" or "no" until the domain is named.
2. In the entropy frame, say the theorem plainly: information reduces uncertainty, and at zero uncertainty the message carries no new information.
3. In every other frame, say information persists without uncertainty, with the SEP, Landauer and algorithmic sources as witnesses.
4. State the framework's own use of "information" (bond, derivative, frame-relative) and do not confuse it with entropy (F2, F6).

6. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Send the split and the refuter, not a one-word answer.
