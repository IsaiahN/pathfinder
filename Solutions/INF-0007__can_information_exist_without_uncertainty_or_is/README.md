# Tether outreach — figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can information exist without uncertainty, or is uncertainty essential to the concept?

**Source:** Information Theory.txt line 10

**Section:** 1. What information is

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. The question is definitional in part, so no measurement settles the whole of it. Nothing here is `grounded` on the definitional question itself.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## The question restated as an effect

Two readings are in play. Reading A: uncertainty is a necessary condition for information, so a receiver who already knows the outcome receives zero information (the entropy frame). Reading B: information is a pattern, a truth, an arrangement or a physical state, any of which can be present when no observer is uncertain. Each reading leads somewhere different. A makes "no uncertainty" equivalent to "no information." B makes it equivalent to "no new information for that observer, while the arrangement still carries information."

What an answer would look like is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the Formula's "Direction" rule: either one definition settles all domains, or the question is a union of frames with a different verdict in each. That shape is written down, then tested against the figures.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 7 :12-13 says *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Gathering agreement does not add an anchor.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. Asking for trust before checking spends independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
