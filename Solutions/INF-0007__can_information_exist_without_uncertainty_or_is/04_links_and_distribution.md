## Wider literature that bears on this question

- Claude Shannon, *A Mathematical Theory of Communication*, Bell System Technical Journal, 1948. The entropy frame (D1). The direct PDF could not be rendered by the fetch tool, so the SEP entries are used as the opened source for Shannon's claim, with the original cited here for provenance only, not as a read source.
- Rolf Landauer, *Irreversibility and heat generation in the computing process*, IBM Journal of Research and Development, 1961, and *Information is physical*, Physics Today, 1991. The physical frame (D4), read through the fetched Landauer's principle page and its reference list.
- Luciano Floridi, *strongly semantic information*, 2004 to 2005, reported in the SEP Semantic entry. The semantic frame (D2).
- Andrei Kolmogorov and Gregory Chaitin, algorithmic information, reported in the SEP Information entry. The algorithmic frame (D3).
- Bar-Hillel and Carnap, *An Outline of a Theory of Semantic Information*, 1952, reported in the SEP Semantic entry. Historical source of the semantic/inverse-range tradition and also of the puzzle that a tautology carries minimal semantic information.

No claim in this folder rests on a source that was not opened, except where the line above explicitly marks a citation as provenance only.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :52, bring a characterised residual, never the source. Sending the full literature before anyone has named a domain sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask which frame the reader is using before giving them our answer.
- Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A mass send chosen for agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Later agreement is never pooled as a second witness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the union shape and its five refuters; (2) ask which definition the reader is using and what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not our summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1: the residual (four frames, one word), the shape (a union with separate verdicts) and the pre-registered refuter. Record the reader's verdict **before** any theory is shown. Fig 2 :45, frames range against the ground, they do not negotiate with it.

Stage 2, opt in only: the three source links and the figures text. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so their stage-1 verdict is the one that counts and their later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).

Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22, only methods cross up, only head starts come down. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
