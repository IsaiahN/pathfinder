# The Tether's answer: information and uncertainty

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the union shape, not `grounded` on the definitional question.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

"Information" is used in four frames that share a word but not an anchor: entropy reduction, truth-bearing data, shortest-description length and physical state. Asking whether uncertainty is essential to information without naming the frame is asking whether a key fits before naming the lock. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The residual is a **union compound** with its own loop, not one hard question.

## 2. The shape and the per-domain verdicts

1. **The question must be split.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. `general`, triangulated (Fig 9 + the SEP's observation that the leading definitions *"have next to nothing in common"*).
2. **D1, syntactic:** uncertainty is essential *inside the entropy frame*. Information there is defined as a reduction of entropy; at zero uncertainty a message carries no new information. `grounded` within that frame by theorem. Corroborated by the SEP Information entry and the general Information page.
3. **D2, semantic:** uncertainty is not essential. Strongly semantic information is *"well-formed, meaningful, and truthful data"* (SEP Semantic). A true, meaningful datum can be certain and still carry information. `specified`, triangulated.
4. **D3, algorithmic:** uncertainty is not essential. The information in a string is the length of the shortest program that produces it, so a fully determined string has information. `specified`, single-source in the SEP Information entry.
5. **D4, physical:** uncertainty is not essential. A bit is a physical state, and erasing it has a measured minimum heat cost (Landauer). `grounded` in the physical sense, corroborated by the Landauer principle page and its primary references, low overlap with the philosophy sources.
6. **D5, the framework's own use:** information is the typed bond, the part a list of ingredients does not record (Fig 12 :5), and a derivative a composition leaves behind rather than one of the six primitives (Fig 13 :124). It is frame-relative: uncertainty is a gap between prediction and observation (Symbols table :38), and a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27). `general`, triangulated (Fig 12 + Fig 13 + Fig 1).
7. **The definitional residue is a convention.** Which definition "wins" is constitutively about a shared meaning (Fig 2 :36), so it is not a verdict the ground settles. Fig 10 :46: *"A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."* `general`.

**Answer in one sentence:** uncertainty is essential to information only in the entropy frame; in the semantic, algorithmic, physical and framework-internal frames information exists without an observer's uncertainty; and the choice among those meanings is a convention, not a measurement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Shannon was wrong. Inside D1 the theorem stands.
- Not that uncertainty is irrelevant. It is the central quantity of D1 and a frame-relative residual in D5.
- Not that there is one true definition of information. That is exactly the claim Fig 10 :47 disqualifies.
- Not that the four frames are isomers as written. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2.
- Not `grounded` on the whole question. Only the in-frame theorem and the measured Landauer bound carry that label.

## 4. What would settle it, and the ground

- Within D1: the theorem is already settled (entropy is zero exactly at certainty). No further argument is owed there.
- Within D4: the Landauer bound has been measured; the ground has paid.
- Across frames: a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here the equivalent would be an instrument that shows which frame's quantity a given physical arrangement instantiates, or a grounded derivation collapsing the frames to one anchor.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** a derivation reducing D1 to D4 to one quantity; a demonstration that bond-information equals Shannon uncertainty; a frame-independent uncertainty measure; a case with zero information in every frame; a measurement settling the correct definition.

## 5. The honest stop

**Split, not unreachable.** The framework does hand a shape here: a union of frames, separate verdicts and a convention at the seam. It does not hand a single yes or no, and saying so is the correct output rather than a failure. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Here the verdict is "split and label," not "unreachable." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

Indiscernibility is not identity. From inside one frame, "no uncertainty" may mean a settled fact or the limit of the instrument (Fig 6 :27). The record keeps those apart.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "yes" or "no" to whether information needs uncertainty, because the question is a bundle of four frames wearing one word. In the communication frame uncertainty is essential by theorem: a message about an outcome already known carries no new information. In the semantic, algorithmic and physical frames information exists without any observer being uncertain: a true datum, a shortest program and a physical bit all carry information at certainty. Inside the figures, information is the typed bond that a list of ingredients fails to record, and it is a derivative that a composition leaves behind, not one of the six primitives. Which of these meanings is "the" meaning is a convention about shared words, not something a measurement settles. What the framework offers is the split, a verdict for each frame, an explicit convention at the seam, and five refuters written in advance.
