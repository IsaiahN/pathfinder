## QUESTION

Do loyalty programs create durable relationships, or mostly shift purchases and discounts among competing firms?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small for years in some customer-slots and grow quickly in others, and is that small gap maintained by contact with what the customer values or by the program's own points, tiers and discounts? R is indexed per customer, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: revealed choice under a discriminator, with a market named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: a loyalty program creates a durable relationship only where the low residual is maintained by contact with a ground that still pays when the program instrument is removed; where the low residual is maintained instead by points, tiers and discounts, the program mostly shifts purchases and discounts among competing firms, with the shift tracking reward qualification and competitor matching rather than any change in what the customer values. The two conditions are a union, kept separate per customer, never averaged. The retention device can change sign when the residual changes. Which program, which customer and what fraction is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The marketing pool corroborates after re-mapping its names to Γ and the channel; the low-overlap fields corroborate mechanism, not a program-level verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A customer failing C2 would be sold a deeper contact study, or a customer failing C1 would be given more points, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :65).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular a program-internal metric that predicts per-customer survival of removal, an instrument that discriminates the sources of a repeat purchase, evidence that purchases shift while the channel is fresh and unmatched, or evidence that a program renews reach purely internally yet survives removal across a habitat change. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-customer durability is fully predicted by averaged program effects would also reverse it.
