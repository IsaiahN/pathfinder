## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Leenheer, Liu and Khodakarami are all in marketing. They agree that program effects are conditional and that the naive reading overstates loyalty, but they draw on the same conferences, the same citation chain and the same retailer-panel lineage. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not three.

- Leenheer et al. 2007 start from the observation that *"Loyalty program members may have a much higher share-of-wallet at the firm with the loyalty program than non-members have, but this does not necessarily imply that loyalty programs are effective."* They find a *"small positive yet significant effect"* that is *"seven times smaller than is suggested by a naïve model that ignores the endogeneity of program membership."* This is the numerical form of F1 and F9: the frame scores itself, and the pooled reading overstates the effect.
- Liu 2007 reports that *"consumers who were heavy buyers at the beginning of a loyalty program were most likely to claim their qualified rewards, but the program did not prompt them to change their purchase behavior"*, while light and moderate buyers *"gradually purchased more and became more loyal to the firm."* That is the union finding F2 at the customer level: same program, different starting Γ, different result. It is a filter for where to look, not a verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Khodakarami et al. 2024 find that *"a customer’s purchase probability at the focal firm decreases after they qualify for a reward independent of redemption at a competing firm"* and that *"customer progress toward a goal in a loyalty program is influenced by competing loyalty programs."* This is the sharpest world corroboration of F4 and F6: purchases track the bracket, and the bracket lives across firms.

**Low-overlap witnesses**, each from a different field and each read directly:

1. **Economics and industrial organisation.** Klemperer 1995: *"In a market with switching costs (or 'brand loyalty'), a firm's current market share is an important determinant of its future profitability."* He sets the low-price capture choice against *"setting a high price to Harvest profits by exploiting its current locked-in customers"* and discusses *"introductory offers and price wars"*. This corroborates the "discounts shift among firms" half of the question without sharing marketing's evidence pool. It is a binding onto Fig 10's channel and Fig 6's closure, recorded as an import with no source gate for the firm-level claim, so it carries no verdict weight on its own.
2. **Cognitive science.** Wood and Neal 2007: *"Once a habit is formed, perception of contexts triggers the associated response without a mediating goal."* This corroborates F3 and F7: a repeat purchase can be produced without the goal that a "relationship" reading would infer. It is mechanism corroboration at the individual stratum, with the anchor re-named per Fig 4 :20.
3. **Behavioural public health.** Delfabbro and King 2020: *"At least 40% of problem gamblers reported loyalty card use compared with only around 10% of gamblers in general."* They also warn that *"it is not clear whether loyalty program membership is necessarily causally related to problem gambling"*, noting self-selection. A different field, a different anchor, the same self-selection and valence-flip structure as F1 and F6.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

**Venue pages opened, no verdict bearing (silent on the question).** AMA homepage, JAMS submission guidelines, EMAC homepage, SSRN homepage. These establish public opt-in routes only. They are not evidence about loyalty.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The JAMS submission guidelines require authors to disclose competing interests and to include a data-availability statement, and the broader journals in this field expect work authored by the submitting researchers. This solution is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with disclosure as the venue requires.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
