## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among firm-library Γ, program action a and customer response o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. It has at least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (program, repeat purchase) would lose the action and the prediction, so it is rejected. It carries an ordered sequence bond and a comparison bond, because "durable" is a threshold in time and "loyal" a threshold against alternatives (Fig 12 :87).
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor is a market and does not update on the firm's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Loyal" is a threshold comparison, so one operand is a quantity compared against a second, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a customer, but the comparison "shifts among competing firms" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the customer's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4), so the residual is intrinsically cross-scale. The lossy transform requires an R_T measurement (Fig 4 :30).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *loyalty programs work when they keep a relational loop between the customer and what the customer values.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific program situation. Result: *listen to customers and keep giving them rewards.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a firm failing channel maintenance merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Relational loop" does not specify that revealed choice under a discriminator, not the program ledger, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :27). Coming back down, it permits a program to measure its success with a quantity it produces.
3. It loses the source limit. "Keep giving rewards" has no place for the possibility that a repeat purchase came from habit or a lack of alternatives, with no mediating goal (Fig 9 :30-32; Wood and Neal 2007).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep giving rewards" has no place for the possibility that the same reward releases the customer after qualification (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
