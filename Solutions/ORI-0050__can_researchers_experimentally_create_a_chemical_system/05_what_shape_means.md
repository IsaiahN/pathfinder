## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. And Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is the four-condition separator in `01` section 0: any candidate answer must locate growth, maintenance, heritable change and the enzyme boundary as composable conditions with distinct anchors, and must say where the information needed to evolve lives. "A chemical system that grows, maintains itself and evolves without enzymes" is a shape. "RNA cross-replicating ribozymes are the answer to the origin of life" is a name, and the second does not follow from the first.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describing the residual in effect terms is what lets a chemistry frame and a framework frame meet without either importing the other's vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the plain description is: expect a system whose copy number grows logistically, whose catalysts are regenerated from within the reaction set, and whose variants differ in inherited fitness.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the instrument that discriminates "capable of evolving new functions" from "has evolved in a test tube for a while", which does not yet exist (Joyce, Q&A source).
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The field "synthetic life research" was named after the residual was described in effect terms, not before, so that any chosen venue would not automatically seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For F2 of `01` this happened: the measured growth curve and recombinant takeover pay for "grows and undergoes heritable change without proteins".
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Calling the RNA system a genetic system is an import from molecular biology onto a held structural description, and it must carry its provenance.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming RNA, RAF networks or lipids as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. That is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` names the chemistries only as instances, each with the experiment that licenses it.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

This bears directly on the question. A flask that shows exponential growth and takeover is a reading of "Darwinian evolution in a test tube". It cannot comment on the source of the capability: a self-contained system whose catalysts evolve with it, or a rubric-dependent harness whose protein polymerases and activated substrates were supplied by the experimenter. Joyce's "puppeteer" sentence is the metamerism warning in domain language: "there's not a puppeteer that handles all the information offstage, and then the molecules are just the puppet show." What is owed is a discriminator that reads the source, not a further interpretation of the reading. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is why the present derivation is labelled simulated and why the narrow `grounded` claim is credited to the experiment, not to the framework.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What must any answer constrain?** It must (a) assign growth, maintenance and heritable change to distinct figure-governed compositions with distinct anchors, (b) treat "without modern enzymes" as a boundary/provenance condition about where the information lives, (c) keep per-domain verdicts separate, and (d) name the instrument that would close the gap.

**What does the framework not give?** It does not name the chemistry that satisfies the slot. Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :116-117, *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*

### Correction to my own section 3 (parallel to exemplar `05` section 5b)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. RNA, peptides, lipids and autocatalytic reaction networks are **not** missing primitives of this framework; they are chemistries in the world, already named by chemistry, and citing them is a binding from the laboratory frame onto structural descriptions. What remains wrong is treating such a binding as if the framework derived it or as if it added an atom to the closure (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - this one adds none to the framework). Both the original limit and this narrowing are kept visible, so neither is quietly lost.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reading "evolves in a test tube" still does not determine the source of the capability, and the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming laboratory chemistries.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree that "synthetic life has been made" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The JCVI minimal cell is the sharpest trap: a celebrated result whose relevance to an enzymes-free system is nil without a provenance check. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let the ground, an experiment, an algorithm or a discriminator, settle it, and record the boundary stipulation so a later reader can see where the information was assumed to live.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The one `grounded` sub-claim is grounded by Lincoln and Joyce's measurement, not by this folder.
