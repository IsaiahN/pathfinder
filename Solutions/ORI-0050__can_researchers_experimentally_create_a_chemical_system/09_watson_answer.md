## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that researchers can experimentally create a chemical system that grows, maintains itself and undergoes heritable change without modern enzymes. Option A: answer yes, synthetic life has been made. Option B: answer no, enzymes are necessary. Option C: unbundle the question into its conditions and return a per-condition verdict with the boundary stipulation and the owed instrument. What each leads to: A delivers a `general`-mode verdict on an object the figures say is a label applied afterwards and spends the independence of readers; B refuses an experiment that genuinely achieved growth and heritable change without proteins; C returns a checkable shape, separate verdicts and a pre-registered refuter, and leaves the literal yes or no marked `unreached` rather than silence. This run takes C.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a shape claim (three properties composable without modern enzymes) and what the experiments show (two properties in one closure, the third in others, capability unmeasured). Arity: four conditions, three positive and one exclusion, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-55). Symmetry: growth and maintenance are homeostatic, heritable change is ordered across generations (Fig 12 :72), and the exclusion is a unary negation (Operators :53-58), so the conditions are not interchangeable. Scale: molecular ribozyme, protocell compartment, evolving population.
- **Domain 1, the genetic / informational frame.** Actors: ribozymes, oligonucleotide substrates, recombinant variants. Currency: templated ligation, exponential amplification, inherited fitness. Anchor: the measured growth curve and recombinant takeover (Fig 2 :29). Nameable anchor, so a domain.
- **Domain 2, the compartment / protocell frame.** Actors: vesicles, encapsulated nucleic acids, Mg2+-citrate, lipid and nucleotide feed. Currency: growth, fission, content retention, template copying. Anchor: observed coupled growth and division with retained contents. Nameable anchor, so a domain.
- **Domain 3, the metabolic / network frame.** Actors: reaction networks, food set, catalysts, RAF sets. Currency: reflexive catalysis and food generation. Anchor: the RAF algorithm's decision and laboratory autocatalytic networks. Nameable anchor, so a domain.
- **Domain 4, the verification / boundary frame.** Actors: experimenter, instrument, definition of life, supplied tools. Currency: agreement versus residual, generator versus recording. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). The boundary stipulation lives here.
- **Partition or union:** union, not partition (Fig 9 :55, :57). The exclusion is a boundary on the closure, not a fourth property.
- **Order by dependency:** food and gradient, then network, then catalytic closure, then a genetic polymer, then compartment, then population selection (Fig 3 :20). Genetics consumes metabolism; optimising the later link cannot rescue a missing earlier one (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: reading "maintains itself" as thermodynamic self-sufficiency strains against both Fig 11 and the open-system chemistry literature. Re-census assigns it to food-generated catalytic closure, a cycle (Fig 12 :121), a different quantity, so the strain dissolves without flipping the verdict (see `01` F3).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The union survives; the literal yes or no does not. Growth and heritable change without modern protein enzymes are achieved and `grounded` by the 2009 experiment (Fig 12 :121). Food-generated self-maintenance has a decidable anchor in RAF theory and is a different condition with a different currency (Fig 12 :119, Fig 9 :57). The phrase *without modern enzymes* is a boundary and provenance condition about where the information lives, satisfied inside a ribozyme closure and stipulated, not satisfied, when the experimenter supplies polymerases (Fig 8 :51). All three together in a complete, open-endedly evolvable system in an unmaintained habitat are not demonstrated. *Life* is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, and a harness proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :36, :70). The framework returns a shape, not a name, so it does not select RNA, peptides or RAF networks as the unique answer (Fig 9 :32). The literal *create life* question is `unreached`, and unreached is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The verdicts are per domain and modes are stated, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by presenting a recording of one celebrated experiment as an answer, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-condition verdicts and the refuter. A second cost, on the other side, is refusing the 2009 result outright and losing the one genuine yes.
- **undoable:** yes. A novel-function demonstration or a source discriminator moves a single domain without touching the union shape; no primitive has to be withdrawn. The boundary stipulation is recorded explicitly, so it can be redrawn visibly rather than silently. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the overclaims.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A chemical system without modern enzymes that evolves a new function the experimenter did not supply would fire refuter 1 and make the answer a clean yes. Overturning the 2009 result, or proving heritable change impossible without proteins, would remove the grounded sub-claim. A source-discriminating instrument showing the test-tube systems were never capable of open-ended change would narrow D1 to "exhibited evolution". A figure re-census admitting a seventh member, or showing the cycle/catalyst lines govern other quantities, would break the shape. Prestige, publication venue and agreement from a shared pool are not such instruments.
