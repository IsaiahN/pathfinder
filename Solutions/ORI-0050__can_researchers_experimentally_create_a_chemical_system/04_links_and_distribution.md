## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The headline question is nearly verbatim the NASA working definition.** NASA Astrobiology gives *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"* and states the three prerequisites Joyce enumerates: self-replication, mutability/heritability/variation, and a flux of higher-energy starting materials to lower-energy products (metabolism implicit). The question's "grows, maintains itself, undergoes heritable change" is a lay restatement of that cluster, and "without modern enzymes" is a boundary stipulation the definition itself does not contain. Cite this when explaining why no single yes/no is available: the definition is a signpost, not an instrument, and even its co-author refuses to call the 2009 system a life-form.
2. **The strongest positive result is narrow and should be stated narrowly.** Lincoln and Joyce achieved growth and heritable change without proteins. Joyce's own framing in the CSHL review is "the first example, outside of biology, of evolutionary adaptation in a molecular genetic system". But the same source says the system has not evolved any new functions and lacks information capacity. Do not let "Darwinian evolution in a test tube" be heard as "synthetic life achieved".
3. **Maintenance has a formal anchor that replication lacks in the metabolism-first camp.** RAF theory gives an algorithm that decides whether a self-sustaining, food-generated catalytic closure exists. That is a genuine ground for the "maintains itself" condition, unlike the "alive" label, for which there is no instrument (Fig 3 :27).
4. **Growth, maintenance and heredity are demonstrated in different closures and are not yet one system.** Coupled vesicle growth/division, nonenzymatic template copying inside vesicles, and free RNA replicators each exist; the 2023 ACS Nano paper says there is "no plausible path" to a self-replicating protocell under prevalent formulations. This is the empirical content of the union reading.
5. **The minimal-cell result is a trap for this question.** JCVI-syn3.0 is a famous "synthetic life" result, but it is a chemically synthesised genome transplanted into a modern recipient cell with a full enzymatic apparatus. It answers a different question and comes with 149 genes of unknown function. It is exactly the kind of prestige proxy the figures exclude from verdict weight.
