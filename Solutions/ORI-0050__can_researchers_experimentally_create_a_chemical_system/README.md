# Tether outreach - figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can researchers experimentally create a chemical system that grows, maintains itself, and undergoes heritable change without modern enzymes?

**Source:** `origins of life.txt` line 68, section 5 "Metabolism-first and chemical networks".

**Mode:** `grounded` on the one narrow experimental sub-claim the literature has actually settled (a chemical system can grow and undergo heritable change without protein enzymes: the Lincoln and Joyce cross-replicating ribozymes). `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and on the union reading of the question. `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded` on the headline question "can researchers create life", which the figures refuse as the wrong object. Every substantive file says so where the claim appears.

**Status:** done.

## The question restated in effect terms

The question asks for a yes or no about a conjunction. "Grows", "maintains itself", and "undergoes heritable change" are three properties to be composed at once, and "without modern enzymes" is an exclusion on the components allowed. Each option leads somewhere different. Answering "yes, life has been made" delivers a `general`-mode verdict on an object the figures say is a label applied afterwards and spends the independence of the people checking it. Answering "no, impossible" refuses the experiments that did demonstrate heritable change without proteins. What survives is a union reading: three conditions with different actors, currencies and anchors, plus a boundary condition about where the information lives. The answer is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that life has been or can be made.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283). A list of sympathetic readers adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42); the outside frame "must not hold the interiors" (Seat Map, Seat 4).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
