# Outreach draft: check the output before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive validation. The output must stand on its own or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about valuing diffuse, long-term, shared ecosystem benefits

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a valuation of an ecosystem function whose benefits are diffuse, long-term or shared across borders is admissible only if it does five things. (1) It reports value **per slot** (beneficiary class, place, time cohort), not as one headline number, because a global total near zero is compatible with one live slot in serious loss. (2) It keeps the **ecological ground as an independent anchor**, reported as an undiscounted biophysical trajectory beside any discounted money value, because discounting is a change in the channel, not in the ground. (3) It **unbundles** the three conditions and their intersections before searching, because a case in two conditions is categorically different from a harder case of either. (4) It holds **money use-value and ecological integrity as two readings**, not one summed figure. (5) Where no available frame can hold the value, it says **out of reach of current instruments** rather than zero.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not that any particular number is the right value, not that monetary valuation is useless, not that a new institution is needed now, and not that this is settled. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The idea of a frame that could hold all three conditions is offered as a shape, not as a named agency or policy.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. a single scalar aggregate that preserves a live per-slot signal that a disaggregated report loses;
> 2. a market or willingness-to-pay instrument that registers non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without systematic directional error;
> 3. an instrument that makes "what counts as progress" work here without a new frame or convention;
> 4. evidence that the ecological ground itself moves when the discount rate changes;
> 5. a single unit in which money use-value and ecological integrity are commensurable without loss, demonstrated by an instrument rather than asserted by a survey design.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures are the authority, not my summary of them. I can send the three source links in `04_links_and_distribution.md` if you are curious.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a valuation problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
