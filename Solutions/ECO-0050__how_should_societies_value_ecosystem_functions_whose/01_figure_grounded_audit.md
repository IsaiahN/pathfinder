## 0. The residual in effect terms

A society must choose how much to protect or restore an ecosystem function. The benefit has three features named in the question: it is **diffuse**, **long-term**, and **shared across borders**. In effect terms, none of these is a difficulty of arithmetic. Each is a different break in the channel between a valuation instrument and the ground:

- **Diffuse** means the benefit lands in many slots (beneficiary classes, places, species, future people) with no single slot loud enough to drive a decision. A reported total can be large while the live signal that should steer action is invisible inside the sum.
- **Long-term** means the benefit arrives after the horizon over which the current decision maker, the current budget and the discount factor operate. The ground does not change because a discount rate was applied; the channel simply stops carrying the late arrivals.
- **Shared across borders** means the benefit is non-excludable and non-rival, so no jurisdiction can charge for it and no single authority can collect. The instrument has no payer to range against, and its anchor becomes a convention rather than a ground.

The residual is therefore **the gap between what the valuation returns and what is at stake**, described as three overlapping conditions, not one hard question. What is owed is a rule for making the valuation's readings answerable to the ground under all three conditions.

**Bound sought-for shape (formula step "Direction"):** per-slot reporting; an independently maintained ecological anchor; unbundled union; two readings held apart; unreachable stated as a verdict, not as zero.

## 1. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** three conditions (diffuse, long-term, transboundary). They overlap, so this is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54, :55, :57), not three independent questions and not one question with three degrees of difficulty.
- **Symmetry:** the benefit is a threshold and public-good relation, not a symmetric conjunction of two payers. The market's `+` bond (both hold) does not describe a non-excludable benefit. The value is **relational to the residual**, not carried by the function as a property (see Fig 11 :49 and :50). A question of the form: what is this function worth, is ill-posed until it becomes: worth to whom, over what horizon, under which jurisdiction?
- **Scale:** from a single beneficiary or hectare to a global commons, from the current budget cycle to intergenerational time. A jurisdiction-bound, present-day price cannot be the anchor that holds across all three ranges.

## 4. Findings

### F1: Diffuse benefits vanish into the aggregate (decisive for the reporting rule)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**
Symbols table :41 to :43: **"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."**

A benefit that is diffuse is, by definition, spread over many slots. Reporting one total converts the per-slot signal into a presentation. A society can then hold a large headline number and still have no reading that tells it which beneficiary class, place or time cohort is actually losing ground. Fig 1 :13 makes the inverse legal: a global number near zero is compatible with one live slot in distress. That is exactly the case of a small minority or a distant place carrying the whole loss.

- **Refuter:** show a single scalar aggregate that preserves a live per-slot residual which a disaggregated report loses. Fails if any per-slot signal is recoverable only from the sum.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 and the Symbols table, low overlap between the Agent figure and the notation table). Mode `specified`.

### F2: Money/WTP is an instrument wrong on the axes that matter here

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 2 :37 to :38: **"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."**

A market price is listed as a possible anchor, and legitimately so for a question constitutively about exchange. This question is constitutively about what happens to ecosystem functions and to people across time and borders, not about what a market will pay. For a non-excludable benefit there is no payer to range against; for an intergenerational benefit there is no present market at all. So WTP, revealed preference and hedonic pricing are instruments that do not register on the three axes named in the question. The world literature agrees: contingent valuation was invented precisely because "Existence values are unable to be assessed through market pricing mechanisms," and the NOAA panel concluded that "the survey operator has a high burden of proof to satisfy before the results can be seen as meaningful." That is a corroboration from a low-overlap field, not the source of the verdict.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a market or WTP instrument that registers non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without systematic directional error, under a source-discriminating test.
- **Status:** `single-source` for the figure rule (Fig 2); world corroboration from contingent valuation and social discount rate. Mode `specified`.

### F3: The objective link is the un-instrumented middle of the chain

Fig 3 :25 to :26: **"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

The chain runs: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learning. The link between perception (we can measure a wetland) and planning (we can build a payment scheme) is the one that says what would count as progress. For ecosystem functions with diffuse, long-term, shared benefits, that link is where the difficulty sits, and it is the link with the fewest instruments. Effort spent refining a price link that already works for timber or water cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19). The world analogue is direct: IPBES commissioned a "Methodological assessment regarding the diverse conceptualization of multiple values of nature and its benefits, including biodiversity and ecosystem functions and services," which is an instrument-building move at this exact link.

- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument that makes the objective link (what counts as progress in valuing these functions) work without importing a new frame or constructing a new convention.
- **Status:** `single-source` (Fig 3), corroborated by the existence of the IPBES Values Assessment. Mode `specified`.

### F4: Discounting is channel decay, not a change in the ground

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :25 to :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :29: **"keep the anchor reachable"**

A high social discount rate does not alter an ecosystem; it alters what the valuation channel carries. Late benefits become "dropped transients," diffuse benefits become "pooled readings," and benefits in another jurisdiction become "stale percepts" at the receiving end. Fig 10 names the resulting output honestly: not a verdict but "you have lost touch." The remedy is to keep the anchor reachable by reporting the undiscounted biophysical trajectory alongside the discounted money value, so a reader can see what the channel dropped. The world literature supplies the mechanism and the conflict: hyperbolic discounting shows "humans show a preference for one that arrives in a more prompt timeframe," and the social-discount-rate literature records that "There is no consensus among economists" and "no 'one-size-fits-all' solution." Derek Parfit's line that no one would accept a "Spatial Discount Rate" a certain percent per yard is the transboundary version of the same point.

- **Refuter:** show that the ecological ground itself moves when the discount rate changes, so that the rate is measuring a real change rather than discarding a late signal.
- **Status:** `single-source` (Fig 10); corroborated by hyperbolic discounting and social discount rate (two different fields). Mode `specified`.

### F5: The residual that matters is three steps out

Fig 11 :52 to :53: **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."**
Fig 11 :49: **"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**

Diffuse and long-term benefits are almost by definition second- and third-order effects. A first-order enumeration that prices only direct, local, present use will miss the very function at issue. Fig 11 requires enumerating the habitat (actors, conditions, relations) and following the cascade until it stops mattering, and it forbids a standing list of "good" actors because valence depends on which residual is being tracked. So a species or process is not "worth X" in itself; its value is relative to the decision and the beneficiary class. The world analogue: supporting services "have indirect impacts on humans that last over a long period of time," and cultural values are "not easily quantifiable in monetary terms."

- **Refuter:** show that the benefit in question is fully captured by first-order direct-contact enumeration, with no second- or third-order cascade carrying any live residual.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 11's cascade rule and its worked DDT case; low overlap with the per-slot rule in F1). Mode `specified`.

### F6: Two currencies, and do not sum them

Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"**
Fig 12 :130: **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."**

Applied to valuation, a money-denominated use value and the biophysical integrity of the function are two readings in different units. Contingent valuation tries to express the second in the first's unit; the figure says a system holding one accounting "will not notice the shortfall." The world literature is in open conflict here, and the conflict is recorded rather than smoothed: one peer-reviewed estimate puts the total at "between US$16 and $54 trillion per year," while Salles is reported as saying "The total value of biodiversity is infinite, so having debate about what is the total value of nature is actually pointless because we can't live without it." One number and an infinite number cannot both be the value being decided; the figure's instruction is to hold the readings apart. MCDA makes the same structural move from an engineering frame: "typically, there does not exist a unique optimal solution" and "A solution is called nondominated if it is not possible to improve it in any criterion without sacrificing it in another."

- **Refuter:** exhibit a single unit in which money use-value and ecological integrity are commensurable without loss, demonstrated by an instrument that discriminates sources rather than by a survey instruction.
- **Status:** `single-source` for the figure rule; world sources **conflict** with each other on commensurability, so no world corroboration is claimed. Mode `specified`.

### F7: The three conditions form a union; unbundle before searching

Fig 9 :54: **"When the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

Diffuse, long-term and transboundary overlap. A benefit can be diffuse and long-term, long-term and transboundary, or all three. In each intersection the second condition still binds, so a single lever (for example, raising a discount rate) clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the compound standing. The correct move is to value each condition and each intersection separately, then search each with the ground appropriate to it. The governance literature corroborates from a different frame: management of the global commons "requires pluralistic legal entities, usually international and supranational, public and private, structured to match the diversity of interests and the type of resource to be managed."

- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions are one condition whose subcases are harder instances of the same thing, so that no unbundling changes the valuation rule.
- **Status:** `single-source` (Fig 9); world corroboration from global commons governance. Mode `specified`.

### F8: Describe the gap before choosing the method; filters are not witnesses

Fig 9 :27 to :28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
Fig 9 :30 to :31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

If we begin by choosing contingent valuation, or cost-benefit analysis, or MCDA, any of them can be made to fit a vaguely stated "valuation problem." The residual must first be described in effect terms (per-slot signal lost, time channel dropped, jurisdiction without a payer) and only then matched to a frame whose closure predicts that shape. Tier labels, cost scores and tractability scores are filters for the budget. They must never hand us the verdict. Fig 2 :42 prices the independence this requires: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." For irreversible ecosystem loss the cost is high, so independent frames are worth buying.

- **Refuter:** show a valuation frame chosen before the residual description that still returns an unbiased reading across all three conditions and their intersections.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 9's describe-first rule and Fig 2's independence/correlation rule; different figures, low overlap). Mode `specified`.

### F9: Unreachable is a verdict, not zero

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**
Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**
Fig 13 :140: **"what could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ."**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"**

When no available valuation frame can hold a transboundary, intergenerational, non-use value, the honest output is not "the value is zero" and not silence. It is the verdict that the value is out of reach of the current instruments, together with a characterisation in effect terms of what a frame that could hold it would have to look like. Fig 13 forbids naming the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it; only the shape can be exported. Fig 6 then supplies the only legal exit: import a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap?). The world analogue is the appearance of new institutional forms, such as the global commons governance arrangements and the IPBES Values Assessment, which are attempts to build a frame that can hold these values rather than insisting the old frame already does.

- **Refuter:** produce a valuation inside the current market/WTP/CBA closure that holds all three conditions without an import, and that survives the per-slot test in F1 and the two-currency test in F6.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 13, Fig 8 and Fig 6, three different figures). Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the exit procedure.

### F10: Declare the stake; do not let a reputation protect the ground

Fig 10 :60: **"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."**
Fig 10 :93: **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Every assessor of ecosystem value has a stake: a conservation agency, a developer, a local community, a future generation that cannot speak. The figures do not demand a stake-free assessor, which would be an idealisation no real occupant could meet; they demand declaration. They also disqualify a ground whose keeper benefits from the answer, and they disqualify agreement among frames that share an evidence pool. So a consensus of economists using the same survey manuals is one witness, not many, and it cannot substitute for the ecological ground.

- **Refuter:** exhibit an assessor and an anchor with no stake in the valuation outcome and no shared evidence pool with the other frames, verifiable from public records.
- **Status:** `single-source` (Fig 10) plus Fig 2's correlation rule. Mode `specified`.

### F11: Status check: this is not a mint

Fig 5 :33: **"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."**
`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s`; REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)`; NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`; "gated by a product; any factor at zero forces inertness."

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single scalar aggregate preserves a live per-slot residual that a disaggregated report loses (breaks Fig 1 :19 and :22 and the Symbols table per-slot rule).
2. A market or WTP instrument registers non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without systematic directional error under a source-discriminating test (breaks Fig 2 :37 to :38).
3. An instrument makes the objective link (what counts as progress in this valuation) work without a new frame or convention (breaks Fig 3 :27).
4. The ecological ground is shown to move when the valuation frame or the discount rate changes (breaks Fig 10 :24).
5. Money use-value and biophysical integrity are commensurable in one unit without loss, shown by an instrument rather than asserted by a survey design (breaks Fig 12 :125 to :130).
6. The three conditions are shown to be one condition whose subcases are harder instances of the same thing, so unbundling changes nothing (breaks Fig 9 :54 to :57).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed to the surviving findings. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. **Report per slot.** Publish value by beneficiary class, place and time cohort; let any total be a sum of displayed slots, never the decision object (F1).
2. **Keep the anchor outside the frame.** Maintain an independent biophysical record of the function (persistence, integrity, trajectory) and report it undiscounted beside any discounted money value (F2, F4, F10).
3. **Unbundle the union.** Treat diffuse, long-term and transboundary as overlapping conditions; value each and each intersection with its own ground (F7).
4. **Build the objective link.** Spend instrument effort on "what counts as progress," using deliberative and multi-criteria procedures, rather than refining a price link that already works for excludable local goods (F3, F6).
5. **Do not sum the currencies.** Hold money value and ecological integrity as two readings apart; record trade-offs as nondominated alternatives, not as a single net present value (F6).
6. **Buy independence in proportion to irreversibility.** Declare every stake; never pool agreement from a shared evidence pool as corroboration (F8, F10).
7. **Name unreachability.** Where no frame holds the value, say so, characterise the missing frame in effect terms, and import it with provenance and a shadow test (F9).
8. **Send the generator, not the number.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Outreach carries the reporting rule and its refuter first; links come after a verdict is recorded.

## 8. Search and exit questions (formula step 7, kept distinct)

- **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest):** is an instrument that holds all three conditions already inside the current valuation closure and merely unfound? The world literature contains many methods (avoided cost, replacement cost, contingent valuation, choice experiments, hedonic pricing, MCDA). A genuine search must test each against the per-slot and two-currency refuters before concluding. This derivation records that the search was conducted across the six field sources in the ledger and that none passed F1 and F6 simultaneously; it does not claim an exhaustive census of all valuation practice.
- **INWARD:** extend the instrument from one already returning something. Biophysical monitoring, undiscounted trajectory reporting and per-slot disaggregation are sharpenings in our own terms; they do not add a new frame.
- **OUTWARD (import):** characterise R in effect terms, then find the frame whose closure predicts its shape. Candidates in the world record are pluralistic global-commons governance and deliberative multi-value assessment (IPBES). Each would need a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains which gap), and would be debited against the independence of the frames that adopt it.

INWARD and OUTWARD were not read against each other. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget remain different facts with different instructions.

## 9. Updated verdict

The shape survives; every specific money number does not. A valuation of these functions is admissible only if it is per-slot, keeps an independently maintained ecological anchor, unbundles the three overlapping conditions, holds money and integrity as two readings rather than one sum, declares its stakes, and states unreachability where no frame can hold the value. The missing frame is a shape, not a name. No part of this is `grounded`: a source-discriminating instrument that could hold non-use, intergenerational and transboundary value on one axis would be required, and none is claimed here. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; one world-literature conflict on commensurability, recorded in F6.
