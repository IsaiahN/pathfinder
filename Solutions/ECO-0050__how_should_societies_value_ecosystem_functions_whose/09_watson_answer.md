## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what an admissible valuation of an ecosystem function looks like when its benefits are diffuse, long-term or shared across borders. Option A: report one monetary total and treat it as the value. Option B: report a per-slot, independently anchored, unbundled set of two readings, with unreachability stated where no frame holds the value. What each leads to: A hides the live slot, drops the late arrivals and loses the jurisdiction at the point of decision; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an open number until a ground settles it. This is a union compound (diffuse and long-term and transboundary overlap), not one hard question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a valuation returns and what is at stake. Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a union compound of seven non-empty regions (Fig 9 :55, :57). Symmetry: a non-excludable benefit is not a symmetric conjunction of two payers; value is relational to the residual, not carried by the function (Fig 11 :49-50). Scale: local to global, present to intergenerational.
- **Domain 1, measurement / valuation frame.** Actors: analysts, markets, surveys (contingent valuation, choice experiments), discount rates, CBA, natural capital accounts. Currency: money (WTP/WTA), discounted NPV, utility. Anchor: claimed market price or WTP, but that is an instrument wrong on the axes this question names (Fig 2 :37-38). It is the frame under examination, not yet a ground.
- **Domain 2, ecological / biophysical ground.** Actors: ecosystem functions, species, processes, cascades, conditions and relations. Currency: material flows, resilience, integrity, persistence. Anchor: the ecological ground, what actually persists, independent of any frame. Partition boundary: which compositions cross kinds.
- **Domain 3, governance / decision frame.** Actors: societies, states, institutions, future generations, other countries, user communities. Currency: policy decisions, agreements, legitimacy, compliance. Anchor: real ecological and human outcomes plus procedural legitimacy, never agreement alone (Fig 2 :35). Union: local/global, present/future, excludable/non-excludable overlap.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); keep the anchor reachable before aggregating (Fig 10 :29); enumerate the cascade before pricing (Fig 11 :52-53); unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); only then consider an import (Fig 6 :23). The conditions order diffuse, then long-term, then transboundary (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).

Strain test applied: Fig 1's per-slot rule versus global total valuation is not strained, because signal location and presentation are different quantities. Fig 12's two-currency rule versus contingent valuation is not a figure strain: the conflict is inside the world literature and is recorded in WORLD below. No line was softened to fit.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single number does not. An admissible valuation reports value per slot, keeps an ecological anchor independent of the valuation frame with undiscounted trajectories beside discounted money, unbundles the seven overlapping regions in dependency order, holds money use-value and ecological integrity as two readings rather than one sum, refuses the held terms "the value is the number returned" and "the aggregate is the decision object," declares its stakes, and states unreachability where no frame can hold the value. The missing frame is a shape, not a name, and any binding of it requires a source gate and a shadow test. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest outreach point is the per-slot rule: a global reading near zero is a legal state even while one slot carries the whole loss.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished number instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-total framing is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A published number can be superseded by a per-slot report, and no held valuation method has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing that one aggregate preserves a per-slot signal, a market or WTP instrument that registers non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values without directional error, evidence that the ecological ground moves with the discount rate, a lossless common unit for money and integrity, or a valuation inside the current closure that holds all three conditions would each break the shape. A figure re-census that permits summing the two currencies would clear the F6 finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
