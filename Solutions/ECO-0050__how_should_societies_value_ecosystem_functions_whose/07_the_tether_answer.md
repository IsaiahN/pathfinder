# The Tether's answer on valuing diffuse, long-term, transboundary ecosystem functions

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape: not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a number. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Societies must decide how much to protect or restore ecosystem functions whose benefits are diffuse, long-term or shared across borders. The available valuation instruments were built for benefits that are local, near-term and tied to a payer inside a jurisdiction. When the benefit has these three features, the instrument returns a small, zero or highly contingent number exactly where the live stake sits.

The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57). Three overlapping conditions break three different links: diffuse breaks the per-slot reading, long-term breaks the time channel, transboundary breaks the jurisdictional anchor. A case in two conditions is "categorically different" from a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Report per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Symbols table: "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." A headline total may be displayed as a sum of shown slots; it must not be the decision object.

2. **Keep an independent ecological anchor.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." This question is constitutively about what happens to ecosystem functions and to people, not about what a market pays. Fig 2 :37-38: an instrument "can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Market prices, WTP and revealed preference do not register non-use, intergenerational or transboundary value. So the ecological ground is maintained outside the valuation frame and reported undiscounted beside the discounted money value.

3. **Discounting is channel decay.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." A high discount rate drops late arrivals from the channel; it does not change the ecosystem. Fig 10 :29: "keep the anchor reachable." Undiscounted trajectories are the way to keep it reachable.

4. **Follow the cascade.** Fig 11 :52-53: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Enumerate actors, conditions and relations until the cascade stops mattering. Fig 11 :49-50:valence is relative to the residual; a species or process is not worth X in itself.

5. **Unbundle the union.** Fig 9 :54: "When the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Value each of the seven non-empty regions separately, in dependency order: diffuse, then long-term, then transboundary (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).

6. **Hold two readings, not one sum.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Money use-value and biophysical integrity are two readings. Trade-offs are ranked as nondominated alternatives, not summed into one net present value.

7. **Refuse the false held terms.** Formula step 2 REFUTED: "a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation." Operators table :57: "a refutation is a negation." Refuse "the value is the number returned" and "the aggregate is the decision object." Nothing is deleted; their standing to be composed over these conditions falls.

8. **Say unreachable rather than zero.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Where no available frame holds the value, report that it is out of reach of current instruments, characterise the missing frame in effect terms (a shape, not a name), and import only with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, :23).

9. **Declare stakes and refuse pooled agreement.** Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Irreversible loss is high cost, so independent frames are worth buying.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number. The framework returns a shape, not a name; this answer returns no valuation figure.
- Not that monetary valuation is useless. Markets and WTP are legitimate anchors for exchangeable local goods; the claim is only about the axes they do not measure (Fig 2 :36-38).
- Not that the two currencies can never be compared. They cannot be **summed**; they can be held apart as nondominated alternatives (Fig 12 :130).
- Not that a new institution is required now. A missing frame is a shape; naming one would require an import with provenance and a shadow test.
- Not that Fig 11's habitat is the ecological niche. The essay declines that mapping; the figure's habitat is contact with a residual, enumerated, not composed.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation decides how a society should value anything.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per-slot test:** does any single aggregate preserve a live per-slot signal that a disaggregated report loses? (Would break claim 1.)
- **Anchor test:** does a market or WTP instrument register non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without systematic directional error? (Would break claim 2.)
- **Channel test:** does the ecological ground itself move when the discount rate changes? (Would break claim 3.)
- **Cascade test:** is the relevant benefit fully captured by first-order direct-contact enumeration? (Would break claim 4.)
- **Commensurability test:** is there one unit in which money use-value and ecological integrity are commensurable without loss, demonstrated by an instrument? (Would break claim 6.)
- **Ground-maintainer check:** can an assessor and an anchor be shown to have no stake and no shared evidence pool? (Would break claim 9.)

These are written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 and repeated in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The held methods are a visible choice, not nature's own list: *They're Scanning Books* says "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). This simulation cannot settle the question it reports on: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself" (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for a diffuse, long-term or shared ecosystem benefit, and it does not claim the valuation question is settled. It says that such a benefit must be reported per slot, because an aggregate hides the live signal; that the ecological ground must be kept as an anchor outside the valuation frame, with undiscounted trajectories beside discounted money, because discounting decays the channel and not the ground; that the three conditions overlap and must be unbundled in dependency order; that money use-value and ecological integrity are two readings that must not be summed; that the held ideas "the value is the number" and "the aggregate is the decision object" are to be refused, not explained away; and that where no frame can hold the value, the honest word is out of reach of current instruments, not zero. What it offers is the shape any admissible valuation must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
