## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The same essay makes the point that matters most here: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union compound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §1. A shape for this question is the set of constraints an admissible valuation must satisfy; it is not the number the valuation returns.

Concretely, the shape is: **per-slot, independently anchored, unbundled, two readings held apart, unreachable stated as a verdict**. "Valuation = one number" is a filler, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Stop when "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with effects (signal lost per slot, late arrivals dropped, no payer) rather than with the framework's terms.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: the framework "cuts down to the shape of a thing, problem or question based on the given information available at this point in time." The recursion continues until "the shape of the answers sinks beneath the union horizon and becomes indiscernible from the answers themselves." But the same essay warns: "indiscernibility is not identity." So a perfectly narrowed shape is still not the ground.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."

For this question, "the missing valuation frame" is a shape. It is not named as a specific agency, policy or institution in `01`. Any such naming would be a binding from the governance frame, recorded as such, not a derivation.

## 4. Why the single-number answer is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; several sources can give the same reading. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here, a money total is a reading, not a source. It cannot tell us whether the value came from a use that could be charged, a non-use that could not, a present beneficiary or a future one, this jurisdiction or another. Naming the total as "the value" is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a better total. This is the structural reason behind finding F6 in `01`: two readings must be held apart.

The same essay's caution applies to this derivation itself: if a valuation method is said to be "settled" merely because it fits the shape, that is projection. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any valuation method? CORRECTION

**I overstated this on first reading.** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Market prices, contingent valuation, discount rates, MCDA and biophysical indicators are **already held** in the world's library. Naming them is a **binding** from another frame onto held methods, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden:

- claiming that a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" is false here; the method already exists);
- naming the missing frame that could hold all three conditions as if we already held it;
- presenting a bound method as figure-derived rather than as a provenanced import.

What is permitted:

- ranking held methods against the shape (per-slot, anchor, unbundled, two readings) and reporting which pass;
- saying a frame that could hold non-use, intergenerational, transboundary value on one axis would have a specified **shape**, with a source gate and shadow test before any adoption (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52).

This correction is retained alongside the earlier overstatement rather than silently erasing it. It follows the exemplar's correction pattern on held atoms.

## 6. A correction specific to ecology: habitat is not an ecological niche

This question is in ecology, where "habitat" and "niche" are live technical terms. *Arrival of the Fittest* has a section "Three mappings offered and declined" and explicitly declines this one: "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a *residual*, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes." So Fig 11's habitat must **not** be read as the ecological niche, and the figure's rule "the habitat is enumerated, never composed" must not be reported as an ecological theorem. It is a rule this framework imposes on itself. The ecological facts in `01` F5 (DDT cascade, biomagnification) come from the world literature, not from the figure's term.

This is a genuine near-miss for an ecology question and is recorded here so a reader does not assume the figure is restating ecology.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prestigious economists who agree that a valuation is correct is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the reporting rule and a ground to settle it.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." And on the demo: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**. It is not a test of the claim. It cannot settle the question it reports on, and every substantive claim in this folder is labelled `specified` or `general`, never `grounded`.
