## Restatement as an effect

A society must decide how much to protect or restore an ecosystem function. Its benefits are (a) **diffuse**: spread over many beneficiaries, places and species, with no single payer who can be asked; (b) **long-term**: accruing beyond the budget horizon of the current decision maker and beyond the discount horizon; (c) **shared across borders**: non-excludable and non-rival, crossing jurisdictions with no single authority that can charge or collect.

The valuation instruments available (market prices, revealed preference, contingent valuation with a social discount rate, national cost-benefit analysis) were built for benefits that are local, near-term and tied to a payer inside a jurisdiction. When the benefit has these three features, the instrument returns a small, zero or highly contingent number exactly where the live stake sits. In effect terms the residual is a **channel failure** between the valuation instrument and the ground, which is what actually happens to ecosystem functions and to the people who depend on them. The three features are three overlapping conditions that break three different links: diffuse breaks the per-slot reading, long-term breaks the time channel, transboundary breaks the jurisdictional anchor.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, per THE_FORMULA.md "Direction"):** a rule for reporting value that (i) keeps the signal per slot, (ii) keeps an ecological anchor independent of the valuation frame, (iii) unbundles the union before searching, (iv) holds money and biophysical integrity as two readings rather than one sum, and (v) says "out of reach of current instruments" where no frame can hold the value. That is the sought-for shape, written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused.
