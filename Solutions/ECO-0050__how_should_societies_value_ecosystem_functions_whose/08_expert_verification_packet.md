# Expert verification packet: valuing diffuse, long-term, transboundary ecosystem functions

**What this is:** a claim in a form an environmental valuation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape: not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a number.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A valuation of an ecosystem function whose benefits are diffuse, long-term or shared across borders is admissible only if it reports value per slot rather than as one headline number; keeps an independently maintained ecological anchor, with undiscounted biophysical trajectories beside discounted money values; unbundles the three overlapping conditions and their intersections before searching; holds money use-value and ecological integrity as two readings rather than one sum; refuses the held terms "the value is the number returned" and "the aggregate is the decision object"; declares the assessor's stake; and says "out of reach of current instruments" where no available frame can hold the value. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and the missing frame is offered as a shape only.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2

- **Statement:** the live residual is per slot, and the market price or WTP reading is an instrument that does not register the three named axes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37-38 "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."
- **What to check:** is the per-slot rule a claim about where the decision signal lives, distinct from the claim that a total can be computed? Does the anchor clause license a market anchor for exchangeable local goods while withholding it for non-use, intergenerational and transboundary benefits?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single scalar aggregate that preserves a live per-slot residual which a disaggregated report loses; or exhibit a market or WTP instrument that registers non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without systematic directional error under a source-discriminating test.

### C2: valuation-facing; checkable by an environmental economist against current practice

- **Statement:** the three conditions form a union of seven regions, and money use-value and biophysical integrity are two readings that cannot be summed into one figure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."
- **What to check:** do the pairwise and triple intersections of diffuse, long-term and transboundary actually occur in current valuation practice? Does an instrument that produces one monetary total lose information that a set of nondominated alternatives would keep? The world literature is in open conflict here: one estimate gives a finite global total and another source is reported as saying the total is infinite. That conflict is recorded, not resolved by the figures.
- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions are one condition whose subcases are harder instances of the same thing; or exhibit one unit in which money use-value and ecological integrity are commensurable without loss, demonstrated by an instrument rather than asserted by a survey design.

### C3: framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 10, 11 and 13

- **Statement:** discounting is channel decay rather than a change in the ground; the relevant signal can sit three steps out in a cascade; and unreachability is a verdict rather than zero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 11 :52-53 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** does a discount rate change the ecosystem, or only what the valuation channel carries? Is there a first-order-only case in which no second- or third-order effect carries a live residual? Can a frame be exhibited that holds all three conditions inside the current closure, which would make the "out of reach" verdict false?
- **Refuter:** show that the ecological ground moves when the discount rate changes; show that the benefit is fully captured by first-order direct-contact enumeration; or produce a valuation inside the current closure that holds all three conditions and passes C1 and C2.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a number, and no claim that a particular valuation figure is right.
- Not that monetary valuation is useless. It is legitimate for exchangeable local goods; the claim is confined to the three named axes.
- Not that the two currencies can never be compared. They cannot be summed; nondominated alternatives are still rankable.
- Not that a new institution is required. The missing frame is a shape, not a name; any binding would need a source gate and a shadow test.
- Not that Fig 11's habitat is the ecological niche. *Arrival of the Fittest* declines that mapping explicitly.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not corroborated by this packet's author's stake. Assessors have stakes; the requirement is to declare them (Fig 10 :60), not to deny them.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a consensus. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source" was resolved by the spectrometer. Here:

1. **Per-slot test:** does any single aggregate preserve a live per-slot signal that a disaggregated report loses?
2. **Anchor test:** does a market or WTP instrument register non-use, intergenerational and transboundary values on the same axis as local use values, without directional error?
3. **Channel test:** does the ecological ground itself move when the discount rate changes?
4. **Cascade test:** is the relevant benefit fully captured by first-order direct-contact enumeration?
5. **Commensurability test:** is there a single lossless unit for money use-value and ecological integrity, demonstrated by an instrument?
6. **Ground-maintainer check:** can an assessor and anchor with no stake and no shared evidence pool be exhibited?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 2 :36-38, Fig 9 :55 and :57, Fig 10 :24, Fig 12 :125 and :130, Fig 13 :134. Nine short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: do the three conditions overlap in practice, and does a single total lose information a nondominated set would keep?
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a per-slot signal from an aggregate reading, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
