## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:28 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29-30/:41 (anchor and convention), Fig 3 :10/:19 (objective link), Fig 5 :53 and Fig 12 :112 (refusal), Fig 11 :44/:52-53/:61-62 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: agenda changes per slot through contact; proof has two layers; proof is upstream of agenda; honest change is a refusal; useful controversy selects rooms.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical claim about a named controversy, for example "public campaigning forced the field to raise its standard of proof."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which layer moved.** The shorthand says "two layers"; a concrete case supplies a changed threshold that may be convention, ground, or a moved anchor. The discriminator is not in the shorthand. Fig 10 :47 names the failure but does not supply the case-level instrument.
- **Which slots and what contact.** "Per slot" is a constraint, not a list. The concrete case must enumerate the actors and the cascade depth (first, second or third order) that Fig 11 :52-53 exemplifies.
- **Convergent derivation versus adopted import.** "Rooms selected against the residual" is a rule; the case-level record must show which rooms were selected before the residual was described and which were chosen after (Fig 8 :50-52).
- **The proof-to-agenda dependency.** The shorthand orders the two; a case may show them co-occurring, and the round trip cannot tell dependency from correlation without case-level evidence.

One thing survives cleanly: a public controversy cannot legitimately move the ground itself, because an anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no case was adjudicated; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
