### How the wider literature was used

World sources are **corroboration only**; the figures decide. Two things were checked before treating any agreement as support:

1. **Overlap.** Shwed and Bearman (network instrument), post-normal science (policy epistemology), Oreskes and Conway (history), and the politicization literature (sociology) are different fields with different methods. Two pages recounting the same Oreskes and Conway result were counted as one witness. Silent Spring is high overlap because Fig 11 already cites it.
2. **Prediction before reading.** Each source was asked what it would predict about agenda change and proof standards before its quote was recorded. All predicted a distinction between public heat and ground settlement; none overturned a figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Prefer recipients whose work touches the residual from a different closure; do not rank by likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A later expression of agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual in effect terms and the two-layer claim; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Hosting and sending

This agent does not host or send anything. If the packet is hosted, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject lines, and an opt-out line in every message. GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions, which is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
