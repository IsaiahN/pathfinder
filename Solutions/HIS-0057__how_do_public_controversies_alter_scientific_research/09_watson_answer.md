## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a public controversy's two reported effects. Option A: treat it as one global pressure that moves both research agendas and standards of proof. Option B: split the residual, hold agenda change to per-slot contact, hold proof change to two layers with an immovable ground, and order proof upstream of agenda. What each leads to: A averages the signal away and licenses the very anchor movement the figures forbid; B yields a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance, and an open case until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A public controversy is a change in the habitat in contact with R plus a change in the frames bearing on R. Arity: two outputs, agenda and proof standard, so a union compound at the mechanism level (Fig 9 :54-58: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either). Symmetry: asymmetric, introduction available and exclusion not (Fig 11 :61-62). Scale: bench, institution, public and policy strata, with a membrane between them (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, research agenda (where to look).** Actors: researchers, funders, institutions, journals, publics, media, regulators, litigants. Currency: attention, funding, object-slot residuals. Anchor: yes, the live residual per slot and the ground that fails to resolve. Question type: partition boundary, which slots are live.
- **Domain 2, standards of proof (what settles).** Actors: the scientific frame, the public frame, measurements, experiments, markets, patient outcomes, courts. Currency: what pays, refutations, conventions about shared meaning. Anchor: yes, the ground, which must not update when frames push it. Question type: partition, convention layer versus ground layer.
- **Domain 3, controversy mechanism (how the change propagates).** Actors: interested groups, common-cause sponsors, mirror chains, extended peer communities. Currency: agreement, influence, prestige. Anchor: yes, the ground still decides and independence decides whether agreement means anything. Question type: union, D1+D3 overlap when publicity changes funding and D2+D3 overlap when a convention is mistaken for an anchor.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame (us and the checker).** Actors: primary seat, second seat, outside expert. Currency: internal consistency and residuals. Anchor: yes, the verbatim figures and the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Habitat contact and vocabulary first, objective or standard of proof second, planning or agenda third, learning and carrying last. Proof sits upstream of agenda. The proof link is Fig 3's least-instrumented middle link (Fig 3 :26-27).

Strain test applied: Fig 2 (anchor) and Fig 10 (convention) initially seemed to strain. Re-census resolved it: Fig 2 governs what settles, Fig 10 governs what a seat may author. Different objects, no strain. Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and world "public mood" measures also do not strain: a mood scalar is frame-internal, not R.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the bundled question is split. A public controversy changes the agenda per slot through changed contact and cascades, never as one global scalar (D1, triangulated). It changes a standard of proof only by renegotiating a convention or recording a refusal; if the settler moves when frames push it, it was never the ground (D2, triangulated). Proof is the objective link upstream of the agenda-planning link, so the 'and' in the question is a dependency, not a parallel effect (D3, single-source). Manufactured controversy is diagnosed as mutual update or common cause, and useful controversy as selection between low-overlap rooms. The verdict is `specified` and ungrounded. F1 and F2 are triangulated across low-overlap figures; F3, F6 and F7 are single-source and labelled as such; there is one declared high-overlap world source and one genuinely independent instrument.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet carries a mode, four concrete refuters and a lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters. A second cost is moral rather than epistemic: a wrong verdict that treats every public challenge as manufactured doubt would dismiss genuine refutations. The packet explicitly refuses that reading.
- **undoable:** yes. The two-layer claim and the dependency claim are removable without touching the figure text. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state non-claims that make the retreat clean. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no historical case has been asserted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A case where a controversy moved the measurement itself, or shifted attention by a global scalar with no slot change and no new contact, or raised a proof standard by voices alone with no refusal, or reversed the Fig 3 dependency while the objective link stayed unmeasured, or subtracted an actor from a habitat, would break the corresponding finding. A re-census that cannot separate convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52) would void the collapse diagnosis. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
