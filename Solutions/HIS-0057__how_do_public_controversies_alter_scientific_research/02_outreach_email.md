# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding whether the frame is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two-layer proof claim in F2 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has been checked against a historical case you actually know. The offer is a checkable shape, not a conclusion.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about public controversy and research standards

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question: what a public controversy can and cannot change in a scientific field. I have one specific output I would like you to check against a case you know well. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. A controversy changes a research *agenda* by changing which specific questions carry live residual: a new funder, a new regulator, a new instrument, a new audience puts a previously unwatched slot under measurement. The effect is per question, not a single global "public pressure" number. Contact cascades: the DDT case moved the residual three steps out, from spraying to biomagnification to eggshell thinning.
> 2. A standard of *proof* has two layers. One is convention: what format counts as evidence, who evaluates quality, what threshold a panel uses. That layer is shared meaning and can be renegotiated. The other is the ground: the measurement, experiment, counter, market or patient outcome. That layer does not change its answer because frames push on it. If it does, it was never the ground.
> 3. The legitimate way a proof standard changes is a **refutation**: a claim we held is recorded as wrong, and the reach falls because something was added, not because something was deleted. Adding voices does not do this. Agreement is a bond operation; a refusal is a different kind of operator entirely.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that public pressure settles scientific questions; that consensus is the same as evidence; that every controversy is manufactured doubt; that this framework settles any actual historical case. I have no ground reading. This is a shape and a route, not a verdict about DDT, tobacco, climate or vaccines.
>
> **What would refute the output:**
> 1. A case where a controversy moved the ground itself, meaning a measurement or outcome changed its answer because people pushed on it.
> 2. A well-instrumented case where attention shifted by a single global pressure scalar with no change in any specific question's residual and no new contact.
> 3. A case where a proof threshold was raised purely by adding voices, with no new evidence and no refutation recorded.
> 4. A case where people who merely agreed with each other produced a genuine refusal of a held claim.
>
> I wrote these down before approaching anyone. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. **Please record what you would count as a failure before reading any theory below.**
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source links are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the eight-step formula is in the figures link. Run it on a controversy you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. The framework cannot check the direction of its own question, so the write-down is the only protection against fitting the answer to the method. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual in effect terms, the four refuters, and the request for a failure. Record the recipient's verdict *before* showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, send the three source links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A later expression of agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A returned refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
