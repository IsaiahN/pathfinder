## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the per-slot shape to one sentence. Result: *essentiality is the response of a declared function to a declared intervention at one interaction slot, checked against a ground that does not move.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that sentence into a specific field situation. Result: *remove the interaction in a replicated plot and measure the function.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** What the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It loses the union. "One interaction slot" reads as a single lever. Coming back down, it tells a team to keep removing interactions one at a time while the channel is still dead, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the declaration of function. "A declared function" is a placeholder until someone names it; the one-sentence form does not force the name, so a team can return with a ranking and no anchor.
3. It loses the channel discipline. "Checked against a ground" does not say that an empty cell is a channel reading. Coming back down, a null detection is silently recorded as a true zero.
4. It loses the cascade radius. Nothing in the abstraction says how far to enumerate, so the field team stops at direct partners (Fig 11 :52).
5. It loses the typed bond. "Interaction" is untyped; two networks with the same species set come back identical (Fig 12 :5).
6. It loses the independence requirement. One team measuring the same plots twice reads as two checks (Fig 2 :31).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version. The paragraph in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
