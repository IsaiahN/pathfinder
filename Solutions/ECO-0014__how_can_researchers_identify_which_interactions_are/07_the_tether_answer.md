# The Tether's answer on essential interactions under partial observation

**Mode: `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Researchers hold an interaction map that predicts a system's response to perturbation. Under partial observation the observed response diverges from the prediction in ways they cannot localise, because an empty cell can be a true non-interaction or a dead channel. The residual is R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| indexed **per interaction slot** s (species pair plus interaction type plus focal function). The question asks which slots carry live mass. This is a union compound of channel maintenance (D1, D2) and reach renewal (D3, D4), not one hard problem (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).

## 2. The shape

1. **Essentiality is per slot, never a network average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global statistic near zero with one live interaction is a legal state. Keep the per-slot table.
2. **Essentiality is relative to a declared function and intervention.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Without the function and the intervention there is no anchor (Fig 2 :29). The comparison is ⋛, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table :48), not a symmetric join.
3. **Enumerate the habitat outward, never compose it.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Conditions count, and the relevant effect can be three steps out (Fig 11 :52).
4. **An empty cell is a channel reading, not a verdict.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Run Formula step 7 in order: SEARCHED first, then INWARD (Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*), then OUTWARD (cast the residual in effect terms and import a frame, with source gate and shadow test). Never read INWARD and OUTWARD against each other.
5. **Corroboration needs low overlap.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Two datasets from one campaign are one witness. Each frame ranges against the intervention ground separately (Fig 2 :45).
6. **The interaction type is a bond, not an ingredient.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Same species plus different bond types is an isomer claim with a stated falsifier (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
7. **Cross-scale claims carry a measured gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Description cost and action cost do not add (Fig 12 :125).
8. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. A particular essential interaction requires an intervention outcome or a provenanced import. Until then the slot stays `???`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking of species or interactions in any real ecosystem.
- Not "centrality equals essentiality." Centrality is a reading from the same adjacency matrix the method is meant to test (Fig 1 :27 *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"*).
- Not "absence equals non-interaction."
- Not "keystone species is the framework's answer." That is a binding from the ecology frame with no source gate.
- Not a replacement for field experiments. Nothing here is `grounded`; no intervention outcome was read.
- Not a single lever. The two conditions (contact maintenance and reach renewal) are unbundled and kept separate.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An intervention and a discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Function test:** for a declared F, does the per-slot removal ranking predict the observed change in F better than any aggregate at the same action cost? This uses the ground directly.
- **Source-discriminator test:** does a method exist that distinguishes a true non-interaction from an undetected one for the slot in question (replicated sampling, multiple detection channels, detection-corrected inference)? If not, report `unreachable at this budget`, not `no interaction`.
- **Cascade test:** does adding second- and third-order contacts change the per-slot ranking for F?
- **Independence test:** do two low-overlap frames, ranged separately against the same intervention outcome, agree beyond the effective sample size?
- **Standing refuters** from `01` section 3, all six. If one fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The species set and the chosen interaction vocabulary are visible choices, not nature's list (*They're Scanning Books*: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. Where nothing at any resolution is returning a reading, the method characterises the missing instrument in effect terms and stops. *Indiscernibility is not identity.* The answer stops at what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which interactions are essential in your ecosystem, and it does not claim to. It says that "essential" has no meaning until you name the function and the intervention; that the signal lives in individual interaction slots, not in a network average; that an empty cell is a reading of your sampling channel, not proof that the interaction is absent; that you must enumerate contact outward past direct partners because the effect that matters can be three steps away; that two checks drawn from one dataset are one witness; and that when no method at any resolution returns a reading, the honest answer is an unresolved slot plus a description of the instrument that would settle it. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and a stop recorded at the limit of the instruments. It can be refuted. That is the point.
