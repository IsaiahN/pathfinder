# Expert verification packet: the Tether on essential ecological interactions

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecological-methods researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the method, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Essential interaction" cannot be a property of a species pair by itself. It is a per-slot residual, indexed to a species pair plus interaction type plus a declared focal function, measured against a declared intervention and settled by an outcome that does not move when researchers disagree. Because the channel is degraded, an empty cell is a reading of the sampling channel until a discriminator distinguishes true non-interaction from undetected interaction. The habitat must be enumerated outward to cascading order, the typed interaction relation must be kept (same species with different bond types is a different substance), two checking frames must draw on low-overlap evidence, and the cross-scale step must carry a measured round-trip gap. Where nothing at any resolution returns a reading, the honest output is an unresolved slot plus the shape of the instrument that would close it, never a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal and per-slot; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 11
- **Statement:** essentiality must be indexed per interaction slot and relative to a declared function and intervention; it is not a network-wide average and not an intrinsic property of a pair.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50 *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Anchor rule, Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit (a) a single network aggregate that preserves the ordering of per-slot intervention effects at equal cost, or (b) an interaction whose essentiality ranking is invariant across focal functions and interventions. Either breaks C1.

### C2: methods-facing; checkable against an ecological dataset you know
- **Statement:** under partial observation, empty cells and unclassified links are channel readings; the order of investigation is SEARCHED, then INWARD (sharpen an instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import a frame that predicts the shape, with source gate and shadow test). Corroboration requires low overlap between frames.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*; Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*; Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*; Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
- **What to check:** in a dataset you know, can an empty cell be classified, with an independent detection channel, as true non-interaction rather than missed sampling? If yes, was that discriminating channel already returning a weaker reading before it was sharpened? Are the two "independent" datasets actually independent, or do they share sampling effort or detection bias?
- **Refuter:** build and validate an instrument from a description alone, with no prior reading at any resolution, that resolves a slot no existing method registered; or show that two frames drawing on one evidence pool corroborate as well as two low-overlap frames at equal cost.

### C3: framework-internal derivation, with an ecological consequence
- **Statement:** interaction type is a bond, so two networks over the same species set with different typed relations are isomers (Fig 12 :117), and effects must be enumerated outward because the decisive residual can be three steps away. A cross-scale claim carries a measured round-trip gap `R_T`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*; Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 11 :52 *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*; Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **Refuter:** show two networks with the same species set but different typed interaction relations are behaviourally identical under every intervention on the declared function; or show an essentiality ranking is unchanged when all second- and higher-order contacts are removed; or show `R_T = 0` for the stated scale transform.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranked list of essential interactions in any real ecosystem.
- Not "centrality equals essentiality", and not a diversity-index proxy for interaction importance.
- Not "absence in a matrix means no interaction".
- Not "keystone species is the framework's answer". That is a binding from the ecology frame with no source gate.
- Not a replacement for removal, exclusion or addition experiments.
- Not a single lever: the contact-maintenance and reach-renewal conditions are unbundled and kept separate.
- Not `grounded`. No intervention outcome was read in producing this packet.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement:

1. **Function test:** for a declared F, does the per-slot removal ranking predict the observed change in F better than any aggregate at the same action cost?
2. **Source-discriminator test:** can a method distinguish a true non-interaction from an undetected one for the slot, and was there a weaker prior reading to sharpen?
3. **Cascade test:** does adding second- and third-order contacts change the ranking for F?
4. **Independence test:** do two low-overlap frames, ranged separately against the same intervention outcome, agree beyond the effective sample size?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 11 :49, :50, :52 and Fig 4 :30. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one dataset you know: name an empty cell and say what would discriminate non-interaction from missed detection.
3. Name the strongest aggregate alternative to per-slot ranking, and say what it would cost to test.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
