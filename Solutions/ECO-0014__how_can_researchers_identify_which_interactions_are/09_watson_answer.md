Option A: report a ranked list of essential interactions from an aggregate network statistic or expert agreement. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| indexed per interaction slot s = (species pair, interaction type, focal function). Arity: at least four operands (interaction, function, intervention, outcome). Symmetry: not symmetric, essentiality is the threshold comparison ⋛ of with versus without against a declared function, and removal is the ordered bond −, not a symmetric join. Scale: pairwise interaction, network, ecosystem function, crossing the membrane with a measured `R_T`.
- **Domain 1, the interaction habitat.** Actors: species, typed relations, conditions (season, temperature, terrain). Currency: contact. Anchor: the actual contact structure, read off the world and updated. Enumeration is outward to cascading order (Fig 11).
- **Domain 2, the observation channel.** Actors: sampling methods, detection probabilities, instruments already returning something, inference frames. Currency: readings and residual. Anchor: whatever at some resolution is already returning a reading; the ground persists while the channel decays (Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 3, the essentiality verdict.** Actors: interaction slots, interventions, checking frames. Currency: per-slot residual mass and independence between frames. Anchor: the intervention outcome on the declared function, which does not move when researchers disagree (Fig 2 :29).
- **Domain 4, the cross-scale composition.** Actors: local interactions, networks, functions, typed bonds. Currency: cost in bits and cost in actions, held apart (Fig 12 :125). Anchor: the function at the upper scale.
- **Excluded:** "most connected species is essential" (no anchor independent of function), "expert consensus settles it" (no anchor outside the pool), "empty cell means no interaction" (the anchor is the channel, which moves).
- **Partition or union:** union. D1∩D2, D2∩D3 and D3∩D4 are compounds with their own loops (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Under poor observation the chain breaks at perception. Effort above the break cannot rescue it.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 governs contact enumeration and valence; the ecology literature's "keystone" concept is a binding from another frame and carries zero verdict weight without a source gate. Fig 6 :34-35 is scoped by its own :36-39 note (theory precedes instruments), so it does not forbid new instruments in general. No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Keep the shape, refuse the list. Essentiality is a per-slot residual against a declared focal function and intervention, anchored to an intervention outcome that does not update on researcher agreement, found by enumerating the habitat outward past direct partners, blocked at the channel when nothing is returning a reading, and corroborated only across low-overlap frames. Same species with different typed interaction relations is an isomer claim with a stated falsifier; cross-scale transfer carries a measured `R_T`. The framework does not name which interaction is essential, does not equate centrality with essentiality, and does not treat an empty cell as a true zero. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point for outreach is the honest stop: unresolved is a verdict, not a silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished species list instead of a method, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its six refuters. A second cost is a wrong conservation priority if a channel reading is mistaken for a true non-interaction.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective bindings (a ranked list, centrality, keystone-as-answer) are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating detection method for a slot, a removal or addition outcome that contradicts the per-slot ranking, a demonstration that a single aggregate preserves per-slot ordering at equal cost, a demonstration that essentiality is invariant across functions, or a frame re-census that admits a description-only instrument would each reverse or narrow the verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
