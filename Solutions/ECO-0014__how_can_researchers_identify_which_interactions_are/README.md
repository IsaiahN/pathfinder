## The residual, restated as an effect

For a focal ecosystem and a focal function F (persistence, stability, biomass, pollination service), researchers hold an interaction map Γ that predicts what happens when a species or an interaction is perturbed. The residual R is the gap between that prediction and the observed outcome o′, indexed **per interaction slot** s (a species pair together with an interaction type), never as one network-wide number. "Poorly observed" means the channel from the ground is degraded: an empty cell in the interaction matrix can mean *no interaction* or *no reading*. The question asks which slots carry positive residual mass when the system is perturbed. An answer would look like: a per-slot ranking with a stated focal function, a stated intervention, an anchor that does not move when researchers disagree, a route for extending instruments where nothing yet resolves, and an explicit stop where the frame cannot decide. This is bound as an ordinary term in `01` and `07`, so the sought-for shape can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula step "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of ecologists selected for receptiveness would manufacture the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"*).
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence."* Adding more readers of an interaction map raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
