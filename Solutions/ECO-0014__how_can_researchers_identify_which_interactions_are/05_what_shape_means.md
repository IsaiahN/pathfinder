## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* From the substrate section of Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form of a method that can be stated before any ecosystem is named. It constrains; it does not fill:

- *A shape:* essentiality is a per-slot residual against a declared function and intervention, anchored to an intervention outcome, habitat-enumerated outward, checked across low-overlap frames, with an explicit stop.
- *A name:* "the sea otter interaction is essential in kelp forests."
- *A list:* a ranked table of the ten most important interactions in a given web.

The framework licenses the first. The second and third require a ground reading or a provenanced import.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, then recurse on recurring new terms. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* For ECO-0014 this means describing the residual as "an empty cell may be a dead channel or a true non-interaction" rather than as "missing link", because the latter already assumes the frame.
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An observed absence of interaction can come from several sources; the reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Applied here: once you can state the function and intervention precisely, the "essential interaction" question has largely dissolved into a measurement question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For ECO-0014 the ground is the intervention outcome on the declared function (Paine's removal, exclusion cages, addition trials), not the opinion of the reading community.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing a centrality metric or a link-prediction score is permitted only if its provenance is shown and it is shown to explain a gap the ecological frame cannot.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For ecology, a "species set" is a visible choice of primitives; declaring it does not make it nature's list. Calling a slot essential is a binding onto that choice, and the binding is the researcher's contribution. It must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why "keystone species" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

The ecological analogue: a diversity drop after a removal is a reading. Several mechanisms can produce that reading (direct predation, competition release, habitat modification, a third species three steps out). Naming "keystone" from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating intervention, not a label.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A species list that grades itself with a quantity it produces (centrality computed from the same adjacency matrix) is the named failure.

## 5. Q: does the framework license naming *which* interactions are essential? ANSWERED

**No.** The framework returns a shape: a procedure with a declared function, a declared intervention, per-slot residuals, habitat enumeration, channel discipline and low-overlap checking. A particular interaction is licensed only by the ground settling a term (an intervention outcome in a specified system) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The correct form for this solution remains `interaction(???) -> function`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected ecologists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the intervention ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The article adds: *"This will require a different architecture or arrangement of machine learning models entirely."* And on the demo: *"due to the black-box nature could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the method and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What the Society of Agents essay adds for this question

From *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened): *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And, on Figure 1, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The essay also notes Kish's correction: *"N samples that resemble each other by an amount ρ count as only N / (1 + (N−1)ρ) independent ones"*. That is the arithmetic behind Fig 2 :44, and it is the reason two observational datasets from one campaign are one witness. A reply from a checked expert is a conversation, never corroboration, because the first contact does not share the framework and the later one does.
