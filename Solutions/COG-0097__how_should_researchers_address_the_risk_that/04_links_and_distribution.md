## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Under-determination of a reading.** The unobtrusive-research entry requires triangulation because each method carries its own usually unknown biases. This is the methodological twin of the figure rule that one frame cannot correct itself. It is also why a single "reactivity-corrected" score is not a ground.
2. **The effect is not a single mechanism.** The methodology review and the clinical RCT both weaken the single-cause story. The RCT's own conclusion (small effect, perhaps due to learning or familiarity) is a corroboration of the union reading, not a refutation of the figure verdict.
3. **Culture modulates the channel.** Response-bias work on acquiescence and extreme responding, and the 100-culture correction study, show that the way a participant responds to being measured varies by culture. This lands in D2 and D3: the awareness cue and the response style are different conditions with different instruments.
4. **Ethics is not an afterthought.** The Belmont principles and Shore's critique show that consent and selection are part of the design, not a compliance step appended afterwards. Fig 12 :125-130 supplies the rule that these costs do not add to the validity gain.
5. **A low-overlap pointer from animal behaviour.** The talk page cites a 2024 *Cell* report that audience presence influences cognitive task performance in chimpanzees. I did not open the *Cell* article itself, so it is recorded as a pointer, not as a read source. If it holds, it corroborates that the awareness channel is not a purely human, language-mediated artefact, which would widen D2. It does not change the verdict.
6. **A provenance trap.** The physics sense of "observer effect" and the social-science sense share a name but not an anchor. The public discussion page warns against conflating them. This is an echo, not a shadow.

## Distribution rules

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the diagnosis, the union claim and the six refuters. Offer the links only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement or write the message so that agreement is the easy reply.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people chosen because they are likely to agree would manufacture the disqualifying correlation.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the channel diagnosis were true before giving them the figures.
- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 offers the formula as a generator, not the conclusion as a recording.
- **Fig 10 :59-60** - "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." State that I have an interest in the method being found useful, and that this interest is declared rather than hidden.

## The two-stage offer (and a third stage)

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the channel diagnosis, the union claim, and the six pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the three local links. The figures, not this summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, names their own ground and states their own mode. A reported failure is more useful than a reported success.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 and 2 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the artifact to send; it carries the mode, the three checkable sub-claims, the verbatim lines, the refuters, the non-claims and the provenance before the conclusion.
