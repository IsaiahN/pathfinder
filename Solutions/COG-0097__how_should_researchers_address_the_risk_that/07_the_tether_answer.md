# The Tether's answer on participant reactivity

**Mode: `specified` on the diagnosis and the remedy, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a protocol and not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 5), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A researcher wants a claim about a participant's cognition or behaviour. The only access is an observation. If awareness of being observed changes the response, then the observation is not a passive reading of the ground; the channel carries the frame's own belief back into the thing measured. The residual is the gap between a response under study and the response that would have occurred without awareness of study. The question asks how to address the risk. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Different participants, items, cultures and settings carry different live mass.

## 2. The shape

1. **Reactivity is a channel failure, not a ground failure.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." When the participant's response moves with what they think the study wants, the anchor has been replaced by a frame that updates on the hypothesis. Fig 2 :34 warns that the resulting drift is coherent, "which is why nobody notices." The output of this state is not a verdict but "you have lost touch" (Fig 10 :28).
2. **The naïve reading is refused, not corrected.** "The observed response is the participant's natural response" is a false held term. It owes a refusal (Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"), not a reactivity covariate appended to the original interpretation. Nothing is deleted; the reach falls because something was added.
3. **The "effect" is a union of overlapping conditions.** Attention and demand, evaluation apprehension, social desirability, experimenter expectancy, habitat substitution and cultural response style are different conditions with different instruments. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Verdicts are kept per condition and never averaged.
4. **The lab is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." The study introduces awareness of observation and cannot subtract it by an act of will. It can only substitute a habitat and enumerate what it failed to reproduce and what it unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :66-70).
5. **One reactive frame cannot corroborate itself.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." More participants or more items of the same self-report kind produce "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). The remedy needs low-overlap instruments, bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42), and sharpened from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-34).
6. **Validity gain and ethical cost are two currencies that do not add.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Deception, covert measurement and public-trace research reduce the awareness residual but cost consent. "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it" (Fig 12 :130). Report them separately.
7. **The stake must be declared and the lineage recorded.** Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." Fig 13 :127: "The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable." Say which method was chosen before the result was seen, who knew the hypothesis, what was refused, what was imported and from where, and what would reverse the verdict.
8. **The remedy is a shape, not a technique name.** The methods form one functional group (Fig 12 :123: "the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"), and pairs with the same atoms under different bonds are isomers (Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance"). No single method is sufficient in all habitats and cultures, and no figure line supplies a magnitude.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a protocol. The framework returns a shape, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a claim that reactivity does not exist, and not a claim that it is universal. The original illumination result was weak, and a randomised trial found only a small effect. Those are findings about particular studies, not a verdict from the figures.
- Not a name. This is not "the Hawthorne effect" as one mechanism, and it does not borrow the physicist's "observer effect." Those are different frames with different anchors (Fig 9 :30-31).
- Not a sum of validity and consent into one utility score.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument has been run here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the form: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the ambiguity. Here the corresponding tests are:

- Does a measure discriminate the aware from the unaware response independently of what the participant believes about the study, and is the gap non-zero? (refuter 1).
- Does one mechanism account for all reactive variance across settings, cultures and measures with a stable sign and no remainder? (refuter 2).
- Can awareness be subtracted without substituting a habitat, with cross-cultural invariance after a provenanced translation round trip? (refuter 3).
- Do the supposedly independent methods share one evidence source? (refuter 4).
- Is there an accepted scale on which validity gain and consent cost are commensurable with no remainder? (refuter 5).
- Is one technique sufficient in all habitats and cultures with a fixed magnitude? (refuter 6).

These were written in `01` section 4 before the conclusion and are carried into `08` and `09`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** No verbatim figure line names a magnitude for reactivity, and no line names a single sufficient technique. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The framework reports that something is owed (a source-discriminating instrument, a provenanced import, an enumerated habitat) and stops at the limit of the available instruments, recording the lineage. One live design strain remains visible: the methodological gain versus consent cost trade-off cannot be dissolved by re-census, because the two currencies genuinely do not add.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not offer a de-biasing recipe and does not claim that reactivity has a fixed size. It says that when a participant changes a response because they know they are studied, the failure is in the measuring channel, not in the participant, because an anchor that moves with what the frame thinks cannot settle the question. It refuses the naive reading that an observed response is a natural response, splits the single "effect" into overlapping conditions with different instruments, treats the lab as a substituted habitat whose introduced awareness cannot be subtracted, and requires low-overlap methods bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. It keeps the validity gain and the consent cost in separate columns, declares the researcher's stake, and records the lineage. What it offers is the shape any acceptable remedy must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The magnitude stays open until a study settles it.
