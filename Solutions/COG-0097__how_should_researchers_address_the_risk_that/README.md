# Solution COG-0097

**Question:** How should researchers address the risk that study participants change their responses because they know they are being studied?

**Source:** cognitive science.txt line 118

**Section:** 10. Methods, comparison, and theory

**Mode:** `specified` on the channel diagnosis and the design remedy, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. Every substantive claim in this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What this solution answers, in effect terms

A researcher predicts a participant's response from a hypothesis. The prediction is tested by observing the participant. If awareness of being observed changes the response, then the measuring channel is not passive: the ground the researcher wanted to range against moves when the frame pushes on it. The residual is the gap between a response under study and the response that would have occurred without awareness of study. The question asks what to do about that gap.

The answer derived below is structural. Reactivity is a **channel failure**, not a ground failure. The remedy is not one technique but a re-arrangement of the checking arrangement: keep an anchor that does not update on the participant's belief, enumerate the habitat before choosing a method, split the bundled "effect" into its conditions, buy low-overlap methods in proportion to the cost of being wrong, declare the stake that cannot be removed, and record the lineage. No single de-biasing device is sufficient, and no magnitude is claimed.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The IACCP contact page publishes role addresses; this folder does not copy them into a send list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map equivalent, Fig 2 :40 in the figure text: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- No bulk-send plan. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- No claim that the figures settle the empirical question. They constrain the shape of a remedy; a source-discriminating instrument in a real study settles a particular instance.
