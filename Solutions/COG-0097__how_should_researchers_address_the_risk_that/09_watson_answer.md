## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the risk that participants change responses because they know they are being studied. Option A: treat it as one named bias with a removable magnitude and apply a standard correction. Option B: treat it as a channel failure in a union of overlapping conditions and deliver a shape of a remedy with refuters. What each leads to: A averages live slots, clears only one condition, spends the independence of the people checking it and asserts a magnitude no figure supplies; B keeps verdicts per condition, buys low-overlap instruments, keeps the two currencies apart, declares the stake and reports the magnitude as unreached.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a response under study and the response that would have occurred without awareness of study. Arity: at least four overlapping conditions (awareness/demand, evaluation apprehension, social desirability, experimenter expectancy) plus habitat substitution and cultural response style. Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-57). Symmetry: directional, the response moves toward pleasing, toward avoiding a bad score, toward suspicion or toward a peer norm, so "Correlation has valence" (Fig 2 :39). Scale: per response slot (Fig 1 :19) and at the lab-versus-field transform (Fig 4 :22).
- **Domain 1, channel and observation.** Actors: researcher, participant, hypothesis, the participant's interpretation. Currency: attention, trust, interpreted demand. Anchor: the response absent awareness, approached but never held. Verdict: the aware response is a frame that updates on the hypothesis, not an anchor.
- **Domain 2, habitat and transfer.** Actors: participant, setting, cultural norms, observer, peer group. Currency: ecological transfer, valence relative to the residual. Anchor: behaviour under ordinary contact in the unmodified habitat. Verdict: the lab is a substituted habitat and introduction is asymmetric.
- **Domain 3, frames and corroboration.** Actors: self-report, unobtrusive, implicit, physiological, archival methods and multiple experimenters. Currency: low-overlap agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: one reactive frame cannot corroborate itself.
- **Domain 4, ethics and consent.** Actors: participant, ethics committee, researcher. Currency: consent, minimized harm, declared interest. Anchor: participant autonomy and the declared stake of the checker. Verdict: validity gain and consent cost are two currencies.
- **Domain 5, naming and reporting.** Actors: the literature's "Hawthorne effect", reviewer, editor, reader. Currency: verdict and lineage. Anchor: the ground for a study, the record for a claim about methods. Verdict: the single named mechanism and the fixed magnitude are a `gap`.
- **Domain 6, the remedy.** Actors: the design moves, each a frame with a closure. Currency: reach of what can be corrected. Anchor: an instrument that discriminates the reactive from the non-reactive response. Verdict: a shape, not a name.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the naïve reading before minting (formula step 2); enumerate habitat before choosing a method (Fig 11 :43); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); establish a non-updating anchor before buying independence (Fig 2 :33, :42); declare the stake and record (Fig 10 :60, Fig 13 :127).
- **Union.** D1 to D4 overlap. A single lever clears one condition and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). D5 and D6 are second-order loops over the others.

Strain test: "the anchor must not update" appeared to conflict with human judgment as a ground for a question about people. Re-census assigned the aware response to the channel domain and human judgment, when blind to the hypothesis, to the anchor domain (Fig 2 :36-38). No line was bent.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reactivity is a channel failure in a union of overlapping conditions. The naïve reading that an observed response is a natural response is refused, not covariate-corrected. The lab is a substituted habitat whose introduced awareness cannot be subtracted, so the remedy must enumerate the habitat and import low-overlap instruments with a source gate and a shadow test. Validity gain and consent cost are two currencies held apart, the interest in the outcome is declared, and the lineage is recorded. The methods form a functional group, not a single interchangeable technique. No figure line supplies a magnitude or a single sufficient method, so that part is reported as `unreached`, not as silence. The result is `specified`, ungrounded and a candidate. The strongest single corroborator from an independent field is the clinical randomised trial describing the label as a catch-all for uncontrolled factors; the strongest figure chain is Fig 10 :24 followed by Fig 2 :33-34 and Fig 12 :125-130.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a protocol as if it were a settled result, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). A second cost is hiding the consent trade-off inside a single utility score, which Fig 12 :130 names as the error rather than the fix. Both are avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective claims (a single mechanism, a fixed magnitude, one sufficient technique, a summed utility score) are removable without touching the channel diagnosis or the union rule. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows a zero gap would break the channel diagnosis; a low-overlap demonstration of one mechanism with no remainder would break the union; invariance across cultures after a provenanced translation round trip and without habitat substitution would break the habitat claim; identification of a single shared evidence source would break the independence claim; an accepted commensurable scale for validity and consent would break the two-currency claim; one technique sufficient everywhere with a fixed magnitude would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
