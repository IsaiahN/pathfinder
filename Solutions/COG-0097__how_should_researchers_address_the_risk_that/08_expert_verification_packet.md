# Expert verification packet: the Tether on participant reactivity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a research-methods researcher or research ethicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the diagnosis and the remedy, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No magnitude is claimed and no single technique is asserted.

**Status: simulated derivation, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When a participant changes a response because they know they are being studied, the failure is in the measuring channel: the response moves with the frame's own hypothesis, so it cannot serve as its own anchor. The naïve reading "observed response equals natural response" must be refused rather than corrected and retained. The single named "effect" is a union of overlapping conditions (demand, evaluation apprehension, social desirability, experimenter expectancy, habitat substitution, cultural response style), so verdicts are per condition and never averaged. The remedy is a shape, not a technique: a non-updating anchor, an enumerated habitat, low-overlap instruments sharpened from something already returning a signal, validity gain and consent cost reported in separate columns, a declared stake and a recorded lineage. No magnitude and no single sufficient method are claimed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 2

- **Statement:** reactivity is a channel failure. An anchor that changes with what a frame thinks cannot settle the question.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33, "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34, "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."
- **What to check:** does the aware response in the case you know best behave like a frame that updates on the hypothesis, or like a ground that stays put? Is the drift coherent, that is, does the dataset look clean while the interpretation has moved?
- **Refuter:** an instrument that discriminates observed from unobserved response independently of the participant's belief about being studied, with a zero gap across all tested slots. That breaks the channel diagnosis.

### C2. Methods-facing; checkable by a methods researcher against the literature

- **Statement:** the "effect" is a bundle, and more of the same paradigm cannot correct it. Remedy requires low-overlap instruments.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 7 :13, "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 6 :32, "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **What to check:** in a study you know, list the distinct conditions that could have produced the response shift and name an instrument for each. Do the instruments draw on separate evidence, or do they all depend on the participant's awareness of the hypothesis? Is there a proto-instrument already returning something that fails to resolve, from which a sharper one could be built?
- **Refuter:** one mechanism accounts for all reactive variance across settings, cultures and measures with a stable sign and no named remainder; or all supposed independent methods share one evidence source and the corroboration vanishes once the source is named. Either breaks this sub-claim.

### C3. Ethics-facing; checkable by a research ethicist or IRB member

- **Statement:** the validity gain from deception and covert measurement and the consent cost are two currencies that do not add. The interest in the outcome must be declared.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126, "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :130, "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Fig 10 :60, "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."
- **What to check:** does the study protocol report the gain and the cost in separate terms? Does it state who knew the hypothesis, when the method was chosen, and what the researcher's interest in the result was? Is there a single accepted scale on which the two have been summed?
- **Refuter:** a single accepted scale on which validity and consent are commensurable, with no remainder and general agreement on the weights. Until then the two-reading rule stands.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a protocol, and not a claim that any named technique is the answer.
- Not a magnitude, a confidence interval, or a universal direction of change.
- Not "reactivity does not exist." Weak original data and small trial effects are findings about particular studies, not a verdict from the figures.
- Not the physicist's "observer effect." Different frame, different anchor. Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Not a single utility score combining validity and ethics.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an experiment.

## 5. What the six refuters are, in one line each

1. Zero-gap discriminating instrument breaks the channel diagnosis.
2. One mechanism with no remainder breaks the union claim.
3. Awareness subtracted without habitat substitution breaks the habitat claim.
4. Shared evidence source breaks the independence claim.
5. A single commensurable scale breaks the two-currency claim.
6. One technique sufficient everywhere with a fixed magnitude breaks the shape claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :24, Fig 2 :29/:33/:34, Fig 9 :54/:57, Fig 12 :125/:130, Fig 6 :32. Six short passages.
2. For a study you know, list the distinct reactive conditions and say which one your usual method actually clears.
3. Name one instrument already returning something that could be sharpened into a low-overlap check, or say why none exists (Fig 6 :34).
4. Say whether your ethics review treats the validity gain and the consent cost as two readings or one score.
5. Send the refutation, not the endorsement. A failure is more useful than agreement.
