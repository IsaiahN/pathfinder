## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I did this before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R.** The residual is a gap between a response obtained under observation and the response that would have occurred without it. Arity: more than one operand, because a response can be shifted by attention, by a guessed hypothesis, by a desire to look good, by the experimenter's manner, by the setting and by a culturally patterned response style. This is a **union** (Fig 9 :54-57), not one hard question. Symmetry: the shift is directional, so a single signed magnitude is already lossy (Fig 2 :39). Scale: it registers at the single slot and at the habitat transform (Fig 1 :19, Fig 4 :22).
- **D1 channel.** Actors: researcher, participant, hypothesis, interpretation. Currency: attention and trust. Anchor: a response that does not move with the frame's belief, approached but never held. This is a channel problem: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).
- **D2 habitat.** Actors: participant, setting, culture, observer. Currency: ecological transfer. Anchor: behaviour under ordinary contact. Lab is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66, :70) and introduction is asymmetric (Fig 11 :61).
- **D3 corroboration.** Actors: methods as frames. Currency: low-overlap agreement. Anchor: ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :40).
- **D4 ethics.** Actors: participant, committee, researcher. Currency: consent and harm. Anchor: autonomy and declared stake (Fig 10 :60). Two currencies, no sum (Fig 12 :125).
- **D5 naming and reporting.** Actors: the literature label, reviewer, reader. Currency: verdict and lineage. Anchor: ground for a study, record for a method claim. Single mechanism and fixed magnitude are a `gap`.
- **D6 remedy.** Actors: design moves. Currency: reach. Anchor: a source-discriminating instrument. Shape, not a name.

Order by dependency: refuse "observed equals natural" before minting (formula step 2); enumerate habitat before choosing a method (Fig 11 :43); describe the residual before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); anchor before independence (Fig 2 :33, :42); record last (Fig 13 :127).

My census matches the primary's six domains and its union reading. I started from Fig 9's union rule and Fig 2's anchor geometry; the primary started from the channel line in Fig 10 and the habitat rules in Fig 11. Different starting figures, same boundaries. That is low overlap between routes within one framework, but still one evidence pool, so the agreement carries no independent weight.
