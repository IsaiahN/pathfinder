**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the six refuter conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are written into whatever stage-1 artifact you hand over. The offer collapses on contact if the claim cannot survive a one-line refutation.

**Subject:** Check one claim about participant reactivity before you decide whether the method is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about research methods. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a participant changes a response because they know they are being studied, the failure is in the **measuring channel**, not in the participant and not in the hypothesis. The framework I am using puts it this way: "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24), and an anchor is "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). Aware responding moves when the frame pushes on it, so it cannot serve as its own anchor. This is a structural diagnosis of a shape, not a measurement of an effect size, and I make no claim about how large the effect is in any given study.
>
> Three consequences follow, and these are the part I would most value you checking:
>
> 1. The phrase "the Hawthorne effect" names a **bundle** of overlapping conditions, not one mechanism. Attention, demand interpretation, evaluation apprehension, social desirability, experimenter expectancy and the study setting are different conditions with different instruments. Averaging them into one number discards exactly the signal that says which one is live (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54-57).
> 2. More participants in the same paradigm do not fix it. "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). The remedy requires an instrument whose evidence does not depend on the participant's awareness of the hypothesis, sharpened from something already returning a signal (Fig 6 :32-34).
> 3. Methodological gain and ethical cost are two currencies that "do not add" (Fig 12 :125). Deception and covert measures lower the awareness residual but cost consent. I report them in separate columns and declare the stake rather than summing them into one score.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not that any single technique is the answer; not that a fixed magnitude exists; not that the physicist's "observer effect" transfers here (it is a different frame with a different anchor); not that the framework has settled anything. This is `specified`, ungrounded, and a candidate.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. an instrument that discriminates observed from unobserved response independent of the participant's belief about being studied, with a zero gap;
> 2. one mechanism accounting for all reactive variance across settings, cultures and measures with a stable sign and no named remainder;
> 3. awareness subtracted without substituting a habitat, with cross-cultural invariance after a provenanced translation round trip;
> 4. all supposedly independent methods shown to share one evidence source, with the corroboration vanishing once the source is named;
> 5. a single accepted scale on which validity gain and consent cost are commensurable with no remainder;
> 6. one technique sufficient in all habitats and cultures with a fixed magnitude.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local in this repository): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - The full derivation and refuter (local): `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` in this folder
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the link above. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
