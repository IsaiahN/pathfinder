## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0097, the shape is a set of constraints on any acceptable answer to the reactivity problem, not the name of a technique:

- the anchor must not move with the participant's belief about the study;
- the residual must be read per slot, not averaged into one "effect";
- the bundle must be split before a method is chosen;
- a low-overlap instrument must exist for each live condition;
- the gain and the ethical cost must be reported in separate currencies;
- the lineage must be recorded, including what was unreachable.

A specific technique (double blind, ballot box, randomised response, unobtrusive observation) satisfies these constraints or fails them. It is not itself the answer. "We can only speak from our own perspectives" is not in scope here; the point is structural.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with "participant changes response", then acquire the discriminating vocabulary from the methods literature (demand, apprehension, desirability, expectancy, habitat, response style) until the one phrase splits into named conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the diagnosis in F3 of `01`. The question as asked is a bundle; part of the answer is to say so.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The shape stops the search when it is discriminating enough, not when a technique name feels familiar.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect is "response changes with awareness of observation." "Observer effect" is a borrowed word from a different frame with a different anchor.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." A shape must be cast so a methods researcher can recognise it without adopting the framework's vocabulary first. That is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with the effect description.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means an instrument that discriminates the reactive from the non-reactive response in a real study. A method that is merely plausible has not been settled.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An unobtrusive measure imported from the social-sciences methods literature is licensed by its provenance (Webb, Campbell, Schwartz and Sechrest 1966, via the public encyclopaedia entry) and by whether it explains *this* residual, not by its popularity.

The canonical README makes the boundary explicit: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For COG-0097 that limit is reached quickly: no figure line supplies a magnitude, and no figure line names a sufficient technique. The lineage is what is delivered.

## 4. The metamerism point applies directly

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colorimeter reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes produce the same reading. The essay's ground statement: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And the stop condition: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a self-report score reads "green" (a response). The researcher names the source as "the participant's cognition." But the same reading could come from natural cognition, from a good-participant role, from social desirability, from experimenter expectancy, or from a culturally patterned acquiescence style. "The full set of ways to produce a reading is not known to be closed or determined." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name assigned off the projection. This is the world-side corroboration of F1 and F3 in `01`.

## 5. Simulation, not ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also states plainly, "I learned that this metatheory could not validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the reactivity remedy and cannot settle it. A real study, with a source-discriminating instrument and a declared stake, settles an instance.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question: a method's prestige (the famous name attached to a bias technique, the number of citations, the eminence of the lab) can get it tried. It cannot make the method correct for a particular population. A campaign that collects prestigious endorsements of a de-biasing technique is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the technique against a ground, including in a culture or habitat where it has not been tried before.

## 7. Radioactive trace correction: "shape not a name" is narrower than it first appeared

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbids naming any method. The canonical README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The missing primitive is the case governed. Naming an already-held atom or an already-held method is a **binding** with provenance owed, not a forbidden act. What remains forbidden is assigning a source off a projection (the metamerism error in section 4) and inventing a new primitive or a new sufficient method the figures do not contain.

Both readings are kept visible here, the over-broad one and its narrowing, so a reader can see the correction rather than only the final form. The same narrowing appears in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and is applied in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 8. What this means for the answer

The Tether's contribution to COG-0097 is not a protocol. It is a shape plus a refusal:

- **Shape:** keep the anchor non-updating, split the union, buy low-overlap instruments, keep the two currencies apart, declare the stake, record the lineage.
- **Refusal:** "the observed response is the natural response" is refused, not corrected and retained.
- **Stop:** no magnitude and no single sufficient technique are claimed. Unreached is reported, not filled.

The researcher still has to do the empirical work. That is the point of a barrier theorem: it tells you what your current frame cannot compose and what would have to be imported, then it stops.
