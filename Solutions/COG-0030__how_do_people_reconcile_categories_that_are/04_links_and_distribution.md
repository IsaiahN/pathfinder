## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The same item can be in two classes for a reason, not by error.** The IEP article on classification notes that a mathematics book is shelved differently depending on whether it is a teachers' monograph or a children's textbook. That is a scope-indexed binding, not a failed partition. It corroborates F4 without being a figure line.
2. **A rule can decide an outcome without consulting the thing it is about.** The ketchup episode is the clearest instance: a condiment could be credited as a vegetable under a cost-cutting rule, while the rule's purpose was nutrition. That corroborates F3. Neither case is a derivation from the figures; both are world instances with provenance.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the stage-one verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* Agreement about what the everyday term means is useful. It just is not evidence about the world.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot residual and the shape claim, (2) the four refuters, (3) ask which ground they would use for each of the three domain questions, (4) only after their verdict is recorded, the links, with the note that the figures and not the summary are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** send the residual and the shape claim with its refuters. Ask what would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any theory is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because the shared evidence pool is the thing that disqualifies it.
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-one claim survives. In this folder the stage-one claim is a shape plus four refuters, not a mechanism. Do not upgrade it into a claim about how people actually reconcile categories; no such measurement was taken here.
