## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not supply the missing anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :39-40: "Correlation has valence." and "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are opt-in or submission routes.
