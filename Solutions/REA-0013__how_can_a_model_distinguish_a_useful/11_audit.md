## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before using the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; union or partition; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a training-fit report for a candidate term and what would hold at contact the frame did not produce. Arity two (term, ground), with an optional peer frame. Symmetry: asymmetric. The frame ranges against the ground; a correlation inside the corpus is symmetric but the verdict is not. Scale: one closure inside the model versus two closures at contact. Fig 9 :57 makes the comparand a union, not a single hard question.
- **Domain 1, the model frame.** Actors: candidate term, terms, bonds, object slots, residual. Currency: bits, compression. Anchor: none. By the census rule this is not a verdict domain; it is a gate domain.
- **Domain 2, the ground.** Actors: new observations, instruments, habitat. Currency: settlement at new contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update.
- **Domain 3, independent frames.** Actors: peer observers with different closures. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame; price per slot; refuse before mint; settle at the ground; promote a method, never a recording.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27 (per-slot residual, no self-scoring), Fig 5 :32 and :43-44 (bargain and one-occasion failure), Fig 2 :40 (same-pool agreement), Fig 11 :70 (habitat substitution).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, "price per slot, seek new contact, record provenance, keep the currencies apart".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete proposed test, for example a particular codelength plus a particular held-out protocol plus a named reviewer.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the trip back down:

- the **gate/ground separation** (a concrete protocol tends to present its score as the verdict, collapsing formula step 5 into step 3);
- the **closure-difference requirement** on a confirming frame (a named reviewer is often selected from the same pool, which Fig 2 :40 disqualifies);
- the **two-currency rule** (a concrete scalar usually sums bits and actions, which Fig 12 :126 forbids).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a term that fits one occasion fails the bargain when priced over the whole corpus and read per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
