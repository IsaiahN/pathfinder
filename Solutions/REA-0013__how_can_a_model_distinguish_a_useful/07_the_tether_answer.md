# The Tether's answer: useful regularity versus coincidence

**Mode: `specified` on the three-condition shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A model holds a pattern `phi` learned from a corpus. The question is not "is `phi` true" in the abstract. It is "what would tell us that `phi` will still pay when the frame that produced it is no longer the frame being graded". The answer is a **union compound** of a gate condition, a contact condition, and a provenance condition. It is not one score (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate)

**Domain 1, the model frame (gate only).** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." So training fit cannot be the verdict metric. It remains a gate input. Fig 5 :43-44: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly." A coincidence is precisely a term that fits one occasion perfectly and fails the bargain `cost(phi) + left(R,phi) < cost(R)` when the whole corpus and the per-slot leftovers are counted. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Verdict for this domain: **gate passed or failed, never settled**.

**Domain 2, the ground (settlement).** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A held-out split from the same corpus is a substituted habitat. It tests portability within a pool; it cannot by itself establish that the pattern is a feature of the world. Verdict for this domain: **unsettled until new contact is named and the anchor is shown to be reachable** (Fig 10 :29).

**Domain 3, independent frames (independence and provenance).** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Two models trained on the same data are one witness. A peer whose closure differs, approached from a described residual, is a second witness. Verdict for this domain: **not corroboration until closure difference and provenance are recorded**.

The loop runs in that order: describe the residual (Fig 9 :27-28), price per slot, refuse a held wrong term before minting a new one, settle at the ground, then promote a method and never a recording (Fig 4 :22). The reach, not the library, drives the loop.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a particular regulariser, codelength, or validation scheme is *the* discriminator. That would be a binding from the statistics frame, provenanced as such, and unproven.
- Not that clearing the MDL bargain settles the question. A gate is not the ground.
- Not that a large validation score, a committee of readers, or a famous reviewer adds evidence. Agreement from one pool is the pool talking.
- Not that this is `grounded`. Nothing here has been measured.
- Not that a search inside the same corpus can prove the pattern is not a coincidence. "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." (Fig 9 :44)
- Not that every fit is suspicious. Most regularities are useful precisely because they compress across slots and survive contact; the shape is a filter for which ones to distrust, not an accusation.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

**To settle a particular candidate:** expose it to contact not produced by the frame (a genuinely new habitat, an instrument, or an independent frame with a different closure), report the per-slot residual there, and record the provenance of every confirmation. The world literature adds a concrete instrument shape: swap sampling with blinded modelers, where models are developed across independent samples by teams who cannot see one another's work, predicted poor external validity better than traditional cross-validation (W7). That is the close practical analogue of Fig 2 :31 independence.

**Pre-registered refuters (already in `01` section 5):**

1. A recorded ground reading in which a frame-internal quantity alone settles the distinction.
2. A one-occasion fit that pays the full-corpus bargain and survives ground contact without drop.
3. Two same-pool frames shown to add evidentiary weight to a world claim.
4. A substituted habitat that reproduces every goal condition, with no imports, whose synthetic solve transfers to capability.
5. A finite closed library that certifies from inside that one of its own terms is not a coincidence.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or re-censused.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," More epochs on the same corpus visit more of the same closure; they do not move the wall (Fig 8 :32). What moves it is an instrument refined from one already returning something (Fig 6 :32) or a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23). The framework returns the shape of the discriminator, not its name, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A useful regularity and a coincidence can return the same in-sample score, because a training-fit number is produced by the frame it is asked to grade. The Tether does not name a single magic test. It gives the shape any discriminator must have: price the candidate against the whole corpus per object slot, so a term that merely explains one occasion fails the compression bargain; expose it to contact the frame did not produce, because clearing the internal gates is necessary and not sufficient and a held-out split is a substituted habitat that proves wiring, not capability; and record where any confirming frame came from, because two models trained on one pool are one witness. These are a union of conditions, not a score to average, and description cost and testing cost are two currencies that do not add. What would settle a particular candidate is a ground reading, not more agreement. What would refute the shape was written down before this paragraph. The answer stops there, with the lineage intact.
