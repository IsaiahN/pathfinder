## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: how can a model distinguish a useful regularity from a coincidence in its training data? Option A: treat a training-fit statistic as the discriminator. Option B: treat the distinction as a union of a compression gate at the frame, a settlement at the ground, and a provenance check across independent frames. What each leads to: A grades the frame with a quantity the frame produces, so any pattern from that pool can confirm itself; B returns a shape that can be refuted in advance and leaves a named slot open until the ground pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, and partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the training fit reports for a candidate term `phi` and what would still hold at contact the frame did not produce. Arity: two, one term and one ground, plus a possible peer frame. Symmetry: asymmetric, the frame ranges against the ground and not the reverse (Fig 2 :45). A correlation inside the corpus is symmetric; the verdict is not. Scale: one closure inside the model versus two closures at contact. Fig 9 :55 makes the comparand a union, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the model frame.** Actors: candidate `phi`, stored terms, bonds, object slots, residual `R`. Currency: description length in bits and compression achieved. Anchor: none inside the frame. **No nameable anchor, so not yet a verdict domain.** It is a gate domain only.
- **Domain 2, the ground.** Actors: new observations, instruments, experiments, the habitat. Currency: whether `phi` pays when composed over new contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 3, independent frames.** Actors: peer observers with different closures. Currency: independence, overlap, convergent derivation. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 8 :47).
- **Partition or union.** D1 is partitioned off from D2 and D3 by a closure boundary (Fig 6 :23). The answer is a union of the three conditions, not an average (Fig 9 :57).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the residual in effect terms (Fig 9 :27-28), price per slot, refuse a held wrong term before minting, settle at the ground (Fig 5 :32), promote a method not a recording (Fig 4 :22).

Strain test applied: "the ground enters as the only metric" (Fig 1 :23) seems to conflict with a codelength computed on training data. Re-census resolves it: Fig 1 governs the verdict metric, the MDL bargain governs the mint gate (formula steps 3 and 5). Different quantities, so no strain. Same for Fig 2 :40 versus :41: valence differs for a world claim and for a convention.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A useful regularity and a coincidence can return the same training score, so a training-fit metric cannot be the discriminator. The figure-grounded shape is a union of three conditions: the candidate pays the compression bargain over the whole corpus with the residual read per slot, so a one-occasion fit fails; it is exposed to contact the frame did not produce, because a gate is not the ground and a held-out split is a substituted habitat; and every confirming frame carries provenance, because same-pool agreement is the pool talking. MDL and testing cost are two currencies that do not add, and the conditions must not be averaged into one score. A particular candidate is settled by a ground reading, not by further agreement. This is `specified` and ungrounded; no part is settled by measurement.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the mode, the three conditions and the refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is sending a fitted conclusion instead of a test, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. If a refuter fires, the affected condition is withdrawn without disturbing the others. No primitive must be retracted, and a superseding packet can be issued. The shape is a test procedure, not a deployed model.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A named habitat not produced by the frame, a per-slot residual at that habitat, and a recorded closure difference for each confirming frame would move a candidate from `specified` to `grounded`. A candidate that fits one occasion and pays the full-corpus bargain and survives such contact would fire refuter 2 and break the shape. A demonstration that same-pool frames add evidentiary weight would force a re-census of Fig 2 :31. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
