## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From The Library of Babel (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question, a **shape** is the form of a discriminator you can write down before you have the discriminator. It is not a named regulariser, not a p-value threshold, not a particular codelength. The shape here is: price the candidate against the whole corpus, expose it to contact the frame did not produce, and record where any confirming frame came from. "A useful regularity" is the filler; the shape is the empty slot plus the tests it must pass.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Bootstrap vocabulary by proximity.** Library of Babel: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question, the near vocabulary is already in the world literature: compression, out-of-sample fit, independence, provenance. The shape narrows as each is added.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is "the pattern stops paying when the frame changes", not "the pattern was caused by noise".
- **Range the candidate against the question.** Library of Babel: the books must be "tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." This is Fig 2 :45 in the search register.
- **Unbundle a conflation.** Library of Babel: "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one". The regularity/coincidence question is a union of a gate condition and a contact condition, so it must be searched as two conditions and recombined (Fig 9 :55).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

Until one of those, the name of the discriminator is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why the answer file names conditions, not products.

## 4. The instrument point (source discrimination)

The Hard Problem is About Source (opened) gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". Several underlying causes can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". It was the spectrometer, and "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", that resolved it.

Applied here: a high training score is a projection. It does not comment on its source. A stable regularity and a one-occasion coincidence can return the same in-sample number. What is owed is a discriminator that separates sources, not another argument about thresholds. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy".

## 5. Why this is not a naming licence for a favourite metric

There is a real risk of reading "shape" too loosely. The figures README's phrase governs a **missing** thing. Here the missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, not a new primitive in the model's library. Naming a specific metric as *the* discriminator, before ground settlement, would be a binding from the statistics frame, not a derivation. It must be recorded as a binding and carry zero verdict weight until it pays.

## 6. Correction to section 4 of my own earlier draft

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific test. That was an overstatement in the other direction. The rule forbids inventing a seventh primitive or claiming a binding moves the closure wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). Selecting a known metric as a binding is allowed, with provenance. What remains wrong in such a binding is treating it as settled by the framework. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened): "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Prestige is not action.** Same essay: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A well-cited regulariser, a famous reviewer, or a large venue gives a claim a budget to be tried. It does not make the distinction true. The ground does that, and the refuter is how we keep the question honest.
