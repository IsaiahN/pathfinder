## What this folder is

A figure-grounded derivation and WATSON answer for one open question, plus the four outreach artifacts and two audit artifacts. It derives the shape any such answer would have to take, records what would license the stronger claim, and writes the refuter before the conclusion.

## Residual stated as an effect

The word "topological" in matter names a property that survives local deformation: a global pattern, an invariant, a ground-state degeneracy, a long-range entanglement structure (Ball 2018; Wen, quoted in Ball 2018). The word "spacetime" in gravity names the arena whose structure the question asks about. The residual is the gap between these two uses of "structure" and the unlicensed step that would connect them. Option A asserts a connection directly (spacetime is a topological phase, or geometry is entanglement). Option B keeps the figure-derived shape, requires a provenanced import and a ground settlement before any binding, and leaves the slot open until then. A sends a `general`-mode verdict with no source gate and spends the independence of the people asked to check it. B sends a checkable shape, a written refuter, and an honest stop.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, formula step "Direction")

A candidate answer would have to state, in effect terms: (1) which structure sits between the states being composed; (2) what invariant survives the composition and what measurement reads it; (3) which import, if any, moved the wall between the condensed-matter frame and the spacetime frame; and (4) what observation would refuse the result. Until all four are written down, the sought-for connection is a preference, not a term, and Fig 5 :62-63 says nothing in the check can see it.
