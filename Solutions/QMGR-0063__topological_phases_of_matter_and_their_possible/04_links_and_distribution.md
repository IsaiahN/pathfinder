## Wider literature that bears on the residual, in effect terms

1. **Topology as robustness, not as a substance.** Ball 2018 and Chen, Gu and Wen 2010 give the ground-visible version of Fig 12's arrangement thesis: same local degrees of freedom, different global pattern, different measurable behaviour. This supports F1 without needing the framework.
2. **The bundle is genuinely three questions.** Jaksland 2026 is the strongest outside corroboration in this folder. It reaches Fig 9's union/unbundle conclusion from philosophy of physics with no reference to the Tether, using different evidence. Le, Lee and Yang 2026 is a concrete case: it treats anyonic entanglement and emergent AdS-like geometry in one system, while explicitly naming an open question about non-Abelian anyons, so even there the connection is partial.
3. **The entanglement-geometry claim is real but conditioned.** Van Raamsdonk 2010 and the Cambridge/Annual Reviews literature state the result in its own frame. Under Fig 8 :50-52, that it is real in its frame does not make it a figure derivation; the provenance has to be recorded. The Jaksland result sharpens this: the metric case needs boundary data beyond entanglement, which is exactly the "import needs a source gate" warning.

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the shape against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on a problem the reader already knows how to check.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.

The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."
