## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. "A structure sits between space and time, and the connection is a union that must be unbundled" is a shape. "Spacetime is a Z2 topological order" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundle detection: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level match for Fig 9 :54-56 and for Jaksland 2026.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to the structure slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one unless the binding is provenanced.

## 4. Why naming the slot from a reading is the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be green, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: reading "geometry" off an entanglement measurement and naming the framework's network atom is a source named from a projection. Several underlying arrangements could yield the same entanglement reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. Jaksland 2026 makes the same point in physics language: the bulk metric case needs boundary data beyond entanglement, so the emergence claim may fail the novelty condition.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular structure in the spacetime slot?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular structure is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the folder commit the forbidden error?** No, but it is tempting. The correct form is "a structure sits in the slot; which one is open." Writing "spacetime is the network atom" would name a held atom as if the framework derived it, and would read the source off a projection (metamerism). Both errors are recorded here so neither is quietly made later.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Network, spectrum and gradient are **already among the six** (Fig 13). Naming one is a **binding from the physics frame** onto an atom already held, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in a specific binding is calling it an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 says "IMPORT adds an atom", and this binding adds none.

§4's metamerism point is **not retracted**: naming the source of an entanglement reading (geometry = network) without an instrument that discriminates among sources is still the projection error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"; LLM outputs in this mode are "simulacra." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
