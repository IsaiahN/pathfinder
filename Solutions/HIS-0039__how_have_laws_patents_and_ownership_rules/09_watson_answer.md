## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that legal rules are a lever that adds or removes knowledge, so that the answer is one signed number (option A), against the claim that legal rules add exclusions but no atoms, so that the answer is a two-output shape with an instrument list and the sign left to the ground (option B). What each leads to: A delivers a single "impact of IP" verdict that averages two different slots and mistakes the number of rules for the reach; B delivers the shape any historical answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question is a union compound, not one hard question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Systematic variation in access to knowledge and control over its use that tracks changes in legal rules. Arity: two outputs plus the habitat, so a union compound. Symmetry: control is an ordered exclusion, access is availability, so they are not the same quantity under relabelling. Scale: individual, firm, institution, society and international regime, which makes this a membrane question (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, legal and conventional frame.** Actors: legislatures, courts, patent and copyright offices, licensors. Currency: rights, exclusions, licences, term lengths. Anchor: the statute, ruling or grant as issued in a public register. Question type: a convention that may or may not decide an outcome.
- **Domain 2, knowledge composition frame (the reach).** Actors: researchers, texts, data, prior art, instruments. Currency: derivations, citations, compositions. Anchor: reproducibility, a working artefact, the prior-art record. Question type: partition, what composes across kinds.
- **Domain 3, access and use outcome frame.** Actors: users, firms, patients, the public. Currency: actual use, prices, denials, diffusion. Anchor: measured outcomes. Question type: union compound, access and control have different anchors.
- **Domain 4, historical verification frame.** Actors: historians, IP scholars, archivists. Currency: archival evidence and citations. Anchor: the dated archive. In this simulated run all four frames draw on one evidence pool, so agreement is one witness, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Order by dependency.** The reach must exist before it can be enclosed; the convention must be installed before it can gate; the outcome is downstream of both; verification is last. Optimising patent examination cannot rescue an outcome whose access link has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

No anchor here is a reputation, a famous case or an agreement count. Those are movable frames (Fig 2 :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reading B stands, narrowed. Legal rules add exclusions to the record, not primitives to the reach, so the dependent variable is reach and who may compose over it. Access and control are two outputs with two anchors and must never be averaged. A convention that decides who may use knowledge has stopped being a convention and owes a ground check. Enclosure substitutes a habitat with two silent failure modes, while provenance, citation and prior art are the legitimate, constitutive function of ownership. The sign of the effect is not fixed by the framework: disclosure can raise reach and thickets can lower it, and the world literature appropriately conflicts on magnitude. No historical magnitude is claimed. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest checkable point is Fig 10 :47, a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, lists four refuters before the conclusion and carries the figure lines, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no regime is condemned. The claim can be narrowed to one domain if a refuter fires in another; the four findings are separable. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured denial rate or diffusion curve showing that a rule added a reachable composition with no gate change would break the atom claim; a single lossless anchor for access and control would break the two-slot claim; a convention that decides an outcome and stays checkable against an unmoved ground would break the convention-to-verdict claim; a source-discriminating instrument showing that a disclosure record determines the lineage of a capability uniquely would break the source-discrimination claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
