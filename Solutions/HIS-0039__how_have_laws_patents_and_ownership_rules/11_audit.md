Order by dependency: reach before enclosure, convention before gate, outcome downstream, verification last (Fig 3 :19).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :21, :25 and :42 (atom addition and reach), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 10 :47 (convention versus verdict), Fig 12 :125 and :126 (two currencies), Fig 13 :130 (lineage).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: rules gate reach without adding knowledge; access and control are separate; the sign is not fixed; enclosure substitutes a habitat; provenance is the legitimate function.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete historical claim of the kind the question asks for, for example "the 1980 decision changed access to genetically engineered organisms by X."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the two slots** in a given case, which the shorthand labels but does not measure;
- the **anchor for each slot**, which the shorthand names as "measured outcomes" but does not instantiate;
- the **sign of the effect**, which the shorthand explicitly refuses to fix and which any concrete claim must supply;
- the **source discrimination**, since a disclosure record does not uniquely identify the lineage that produced a capability.

One thing survives cleanly: a rule adds an exclusion, not an atom, so the measure is reach. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
