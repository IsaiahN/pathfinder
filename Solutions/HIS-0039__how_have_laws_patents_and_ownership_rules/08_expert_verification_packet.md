# Expert verification packet — the Tether on knowledge ownership

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scholar of knowledge ownership can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No historical measurement is claimed. This is a shape and an instrument list, not a history.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Ownership rules are conventions that gate reach without adding knowledge. A legal rule adds an exclusion to the record, not a primitive to what a community can compose, so the effect to measure is the change in which compositions are reachable and by whom. Access and control are two distinct outputs with distinct anchors and must be measured separately. A convention that decides who may access or use knowledge has stopped being a convention and needs a ground check. The direction of the effect is not fixed: a disclosure mandate can lower the action cost of composing while a licence thicket raises it. Enclosure substitutes a habitat with two silent failure modes, while provenance, citation and prior art are the legitimate, constitutive function. What is offered is this shape, its refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The historical magnitude is left open.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 6 and 13

- **Statement:** a legal rule gates reach; it cannot add a compositional atom.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :25, *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 13 :115, *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*
- **Refuter:** show a legal rule that adds a new primitive to what a community can compose, moving the epistemic wall rather than only gating reach. Or show that increasing the number of rules increases the reachable set with no compensating import.

### C2 — empirical-facing, checkable by a scholar of IP or science policy

- **Statement:** access and control are separate quantities with separate anchors; averaging them loses signal. A convention that decides an access or use outcome stops being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22, *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 2 :30, *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
- **What to check:** find a regime where access rose while control tightened, or the reverse. If such cases exist, the two slots are not one quantity and any single-score study is lossy. Then ask what ground would show the resulting allocation to be wrong.
- **Refuter:** show a single measure of access that tracks denials, prices and the set of reachable compositions with no loss, and that remains a convention while deciding outcomes.

### C3 — structural, checkable by reading Figures 12 and 11

- **Statement:** the effect is not signed; it is paid in two currencies that do not add, and enclosure is habitat substitution.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126, *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 11 :66, *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68 to :69, *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"*
- **What to check:** compare a disclosure mandate with a thicket on the two currencies separately. The world sources conflict on the sign (Heller and Eisenberg 1998 versus Lesser 2012 and Hegde et al. 2023), which is what the two-currency claim predicts.
- **Refuter:** show that the two currencies reduce to one measure without loss, or that excluding composition increases reach with no import and no habitat change.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that patents are harmful, or that all enclosure is wrong, or that the public domain is always the answer.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating outcome instrument, not a further argument.

1. **Access slot:** a measured count of who can actually compose, not a count of licences.
2. **Control slot:** measured denials and the set of derivatives that are reachable.
3. **Reach:** which compositions became reachable, and whether an atom was added or a gate moved.
4. **Two currencies:** bits for describing the right, actions for checking whether it holds, reported separately.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** add a primitive; show one lossless anchor for both slots; show a convention that decides outcomes and stays checkable; show exclusion that raises reach with no import or habitat change.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21 and :25, Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 10 :47, Fig 12 :125 and :126. Six short passages.
2. Name one case where access and control moved in opposite directions. If you can, C2 stands and the averaging error is real.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate "rule added an atom" from "rule moved a gate" in your field, or say why none is possible.
4. Say whether you think the two currencies can be summed. If they can, C3 fails.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
