# The Tether's answer on laws, patents and ownership rules

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and an instrument list, not a history. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Access to knowledge and control over its use vary with legal rules. The residual is that variation. Arity: two outputs, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54, *"unbundle before you search again"*). Symmetry: control is an ordered exclusion, access is availability, so they cannot be averaged. Scale: individual, firm, institution, society and international regime (Fig 4).

## 2. The shape

1. **Rules gate reach; they do not create knowledge.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* A patent adds an exclusion to the record, not a primitive to what can be composed. The dependent variable is reach, not rule volume.

2. **Access and control are separate slots.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A regime can raise access while tightening control. Any single "impact of IP" score throws away the only signal that says where to look.

3. **A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.** Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Ownership is efficient as a shared meaning about attribution and priority. It becomes a defect when it decides who may access and use knowledge without a ground check. Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

4. **The direction of the effect is not fixed.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Disclosure mandates lower the action cost of composing; thickets raise it. The framework does not say property is good or bad. It says the two currencies must be held apart and the ground must decide which dominates.

5. **Enclosure is habitat substitution.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* The two failure modes are silent: what you failed to reproduce and what you brought with you (Fig 11 :68 to :69). The scarcity that enclosure claims to manage is often manufactured by the exclusion, because knowledge is nonrival (Fig 10 :18, *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*).

6. **Provenance is the legitimate function.** Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* Prior art, citation and attribution are constitutive of knowledge, not overhead around it. The standing to block composition is the part that needs a refuter.

7. **No last room.** Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room."* A regime that tries to close the search space converts a chain into *"one long room: motion, no progress"* (Fig 7 :32). Agreement among lawyers, economists or historians is not ground: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that patents are harmful, or that the public domain is always the answer. The sign is not fixed by the figures.
- Not a historical magnitude for any statute, country or period. That is a ground question, and this run has no measurement.
- Not that a named institution or a famous case settles anything. Prestige funds a try; it cannot settle an action.
- Not that exclusion is always substitution. For rival resources the analysis differs, and that case governs another domain.
- Not `grounded`. No denial rate, price or diffusion curve was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating outcome instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Access slot:** does the rule change measured access, that is a count of people or organisations who can actually compose, not a count of licences issued? Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **Control slot:** does the rule change who can exclude whom, measured by denials and by which derivatives are reachable?
- **Reach:** which compositions became reachable that were not before, and did the rule add an atom or only move a gate?
- **Two currencies (Fig 12 :126):** report bits (cost of describing the right) and actions (cost of checking whether it holds) separately. Summing them is the named error.
- **Standing falsifiers:** add a primitive to the closure; show access and control share one lossless anchor; show a convention that decides an outcome and stays checkable; show exclusion that increases reach with no import or habitat change.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures give the shape any historical answer must have and the set of instruments that would close it. They do not give the history. This stop is recorded with its lineage so it is checkable: the domains, the figure lines, the refuters and the conflicting world sources are all on the record.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that patents are bad or that the public domain is always right. It says that ownership rules add exclusions, not knowledge: they gate which compositions are reachable and by whom, so the thing to measure is reach rather than the number of rules. It says access and control are two different outputs with two different anchors and must never be averaged into one "impact of IP" score. It says a convention that decides who may use knowledge has stopped being a convention and has become a verdict that needs a ground check, and that the direction of the effect is fixed only by measurement: disclosure rules can lower the cost of composing while thickets raise it. It says enclosure substitutes a habitat and hides what it failed to reproduce and what it brought with you, while provenance, citation and prior art are the legitimate, constitutive part of the whole arrangement. What it offers is this shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The history stays open until the ground settles it.
