## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label"* (Seat Map :283) and Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list of people selected for agreement manufactures a convention, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Asking for receptiveness as a selection criterion buys exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
