## 0. The claim under audit

The question asks how laws, patents and ownership rules have shaped (a) access to knowledge and (b) control over its use. Stated as an effect: a legal convention is installed into a habitat of knowers; some compositions become reachable only through a licence, some become unreachable, and some become cheaper. The residual is the systematic variation in access and control that tracks changes in those rules.

Two readings are possible and they are not equivalent:

- **A:** rules are an external lever that simply adds or removes knowledge, so the answer is a single signed number, more IP means more or less knowledge.
- **B:** rules are conventions that gate reach without adding atoms, so the answer is a shape with two separately measured outputs and a defined set of instruments that could close it.

This audit decides between A and B. It does not decide whether any particular patent statute was good or bad. That is a ground question for historians, and this simulated run cannot answer it.

## 0b. Residual and domain census, before any figure line was chosen

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two outputs, access and control, plus the habitat that carries them. Symmetry: control is an ordered exclusion relation (one party may block another); access is an availability relation. They are not the same quantity under a relabelling, so averaging them is a type error. Scale: individual knower, firm, institution, society and international regime. That is a membrane question, not one flat scale (Fig 4).

**Domain census, one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor.** A nameable anchor is required; without one the item is not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable, does not move with opinion) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, legal and conventional frame | legislatures, courts, patent and copyright offices, licensors | rights, exclusions, licences, term lengths | the statute, ruling or grant as issued and recorded in a public register | convention that may or may not decide an outcome |
| D2, knowledge composition frame (the reach) | researchers, texts, data, prior art, instruments | derivations, citations, compositions | reproducibility, a working artefact, the prior-art record | partition: which compositions are reachable across kinds |
| D3, access and use outcome frame | users, firms, patients, public | actual use, prices, diffusion, denial | measured outcomes: access counts, time to diffusion, prices, denials | union compound: access and control have different anchors |
| D4, historical verification frame | historians, IP scholars, archivists | archival evidence, citations, revision | the archive and the dated record | checking frame; in this run all frames share one evidence pool |

**Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a partition boundary question: what may cross from the legal frame into the composition frame. D3 is a **union**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Access and control must be searched separately and then recombined. D4 is downstream of all three.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The reach (D2) must exist before it can be enclosed; the convention (D1) must be installed before it can gate; the outcome (D3) is downstream of both; verification (D4) comes last. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* A reform of patent examination cannot rescue a broken access outcome if the reach is already enclosed.

No anchor in this census is a person's reputation, a famous institution, or an agreement count. Those would be movable frames, not anchors (Fig 2 :30).

## 2. Findings

### F1 — Rules gate reach; they add no atoms (decisive)

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."**
Fig 6 :14 names **"the only operator that moves the wall"**, and Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3: *"Nothing is ever deleted, and the reach falls because something was ADDED."*

A patent, a copyright term or a trade-secret rule adds a legal exclusion to the record. It does not add a primitive to what a community can compose. Therefore:

- rules cannot create knowledge; they can only change which compositions are reachable and by whom;
- the correct measure of a rule's effect is the change in **reach**, not the change in the number of rules on the books. Fig 5 :49 warns that *"Machinery firing is not the milestone. A running system can mint and still return nothing new."* A busier patent office is not a larger reach.

**Strain test, sent back to the census before use.** The obvious analogy is "a patent is an import." That reading strains: an import adds an atom and moves the wall (Fig 6 :14), while a patent adds no knowledge atom. Re-census shows the patent governs **reach**, not the **wall**. It is a gate on composition, not an import of a primitive. Similarly, "a licence is a bond" strains, because the seven bonds are compositional relations, not legal permissions. The legal frame has its own vocabulary; the analogy is a mapping, not an identity. After re-census, no strain remains in F1. **Triangulated** across Fig 6, Fig 12 and Fig 13 (three figures governing atom addition, composition and set closure); note all are framework sources, so this is internal consistency, not independent corroboration.

### F2 — A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention

Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
Fig 2 :43: **"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**
Fig 2 :41: **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**
Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

Ownership is a convention: a community agrees on a shared meaning (this result is attributable to X; this use requires a licence). Conventions are efficient for coordination. The named failure is when the convention is promoted to a verdict about the world, that is, when it decides who may access and use knowledge without a ground check. Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."** A grant states a meaning; it does not demonstrate that the resulting allocation is the one the ground would pay for.

**Refuter:** show a regime in which an ownership convention determines an access or use outcome and remains checkable against a ground that it cannot move. If that is possible, F2 is false. **Triangulated** across Fig 10 and Fig 2 plus the Seat Map, at low conceptual overlap (one governs the checking arrangement, one the anchor).

### F3 — Knowledge reified as a record; the generator is lost

Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**
Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

A patent specification, a published paper and a licence are recordings. Treating the recording as the knowledge is the named reversal: a description of one occasion is promoted to a generator. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* A disclosure record tells you what was claimed, not which of several possible sources produced the capability.

This is why "patents promote disclosure" and "patents enable enclosure" are not a contradiction in the framework. Disclosure sends a recording outward; the right to exclude governs what may be composed from it. Different quantities, different loops. **Single-source** for the figure line (Fig 4), corroborated by the source-discrimination essay, which shares the framework's evidence pool.

### F4 — Access and control are separate slots; never average them

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 9 :54 and :57 as quoted in §0b.

A regime can raise access while tightening control (a free-to-read article with all reuse rights reserved), or lower access while loosening control (a paywalled work in the public domain after term). A single "impact of IP on knowledge" score hides both. Fig 9 :29: *"matching is a one-pass check, not a search"*, and :27 to :28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** The two outputs must be described separately before a legal frame is chosen to explain them.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a measure of knowledge access that is invariant under changes in who may exclude others, or vice versa, with one anchor for both. If such a measure exists and loses no signal, F4 fails. **Triangulated** across Fig 1 and Fig 9.

### F5 — Enclosure is habitat substitution, and the manufactured scarcity is legal

Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**
Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
Fig 11 :68 to :69: **"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"**
Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**

The commons literature calls patented research inputs "scarce" (Heller and Eisenberg, 1998). Knowledge is nonrival; the scarcity is created by the exclusion. The declaration manufactures the condition it then claims to manage. Fig 11 adds the two silent failure modes of moving a residual into a substituted habitat: what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails, and what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts. Applied here: a closed regime may retain the appearance of a knowledge habitat while dropping the cross-license and informal-sharing conditions that made composition work.

**Refuter:** produce a case where exclusion is applied to a genuinely rival, non-substitutable resource and the two silent failure modes do not apply. That case would govern another quantity, not this one. **Single-source** for the habitat law (Fig 11); the "declared meaning" line from Fig 10 corroborates at low overlap.

### F6 — Provenance is the legitimate function: the lineage is the term

Fig 13 :126: **"WHY THE LINEAGE IS THE PROOF"**
Fig 13 :127: **"The array cannot be checked by inspection, so the record is what makes it verifiable."**
Fig 13 :130: **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."**

Prior art, citation, attribution and priority are not administrative overhead around knowledge; under Fig 13 they are constitutive of the term. This is the part of ownership rules the figures affirm. The illegitimate part is converting provenance into a standing to block composition. Boyle's *"second enclosure movement"* is the world-side name for that conversion, but the figure verdict does not depend on his framing. Merton (1942) records the counter-norm inside science itself: *"The substantive findings of science are a product of social collaboration and are assigned to the community."*

**Refuter:** show a composable knowledge term whose lineage can be discarded with no loss of verifiability. **Single-source** for the figure law (Fig 13), with two low-overlap world sources corroborating.

### F7 — No last room: enclosure that closes the room stalls the chain

Fig 7 :6: **"the limit: no last room"**
Fig 7 :32: **"one long room: motion, no progress"**
Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."**

A regime that tries to close the search space into a final, owned room converts a chain into one long room. Filing, licensing and renewal continue (motion) while the reachable set does not grow (no progress). This is the same reason the framework refuses a status claim based on how many people endorse a rule. **Triangulated** across Fig 7 and Fig 13's *"infinite effort inside a finite reach"*, with the open-source "many eyes" result below as a low-overlap world corroboration.

### F8 — The direction of the effect is not fixed by the figures

Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**

A disclosure mandate lowers the action cost of composing (fewer steps to find prior art). A thicket raises it. Both are legal rules. The framework does not say "property is bad" or "property is good"; it says the two currencies must be held apart and the ground must decide which dominates in a given case. Summing them into one welfare number is the error Fig 12 names. The world literature confirms the split empirically and conflicts on the sign: Hegde, Herkenhoff and Zhu (2023) find accelerated publication increased diffusion, while Lesser (2012) finds the predicted anticommons *"has not materialized significantly"* and Sampat (2018) calls the net effect on cumulative innovation *"theoretically ambiguous."* Conflict sends us back to the census, never to a flipped verdict; here the census already predicted a split. **Triangulated** at the level of "two currencies, sign not fixed" across Fig 12 and the two opposed empirical camps.

### F9 — The ground is measured outcomes, not agreement or prestige

Fig 2 :30: **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**
Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

The anchor for D3 is a measured outcome: a denial rate, a time to diffusion, a price, a count of compositions actually made. Agreement among economists, lawyers or historians is not that anchor. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A publication's prestige, an institution's authority and a citation count are filters for where to look; they are not witnesses for whether access changed. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

**Refuter:** show that a consensus measure of "fairness of access" tracks measured denials and prices with no loss on either axis. Until then this remains `specified`, not `grounded`. **Triangulated** across Fig 2, Fig 3 and Fig 9.

### Honest gap at the ground

The figures give the shape and the instrument list, not the historical magnitudes. This simulated run has no measurement of denial rates, prices or diffusion for any regime. On the question "how much did rule X change access in year Y," the honest status is **gap at the ground for this run**: unreached, not unreachable. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any world source was used to support a finding, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim (B above) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A legal rule is shown to add a new primitive to what a community can compose, that is, to move the epistemic wall rather than only gate reach (breaks Fig 6 :14, :21, :25).
2. Access and control are shown to be one quantity with one anchor such that averaging them loses no signal (breaks Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 9 :54).
3. A regime is shown in which an ownership convention decides an access or use outcome and still remains checkable against a ground it cannot move (breaks Fig 10 :47).
4. A case is produced in which excluding composition increases the reachable set with no compensating import and no change of habitat (breaks Fig 6 :42 and Fig 11 :66).
5. A source-discriminating instrument shows that the disclosure record determines the source of a capability uniquely, so that naming the owner from the record is not a metamerism error (breaks F3 and the source essay).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Unbundle the question into access and control, each with its own anchor, before searching for a legal frame (F4). A single "impact of IP" study repeats the aggregation error.
2. Measure reach, not rule volume. The dependent variable is which compositions became reachable to whom, not how many patents were granted (F1).
3. Treat ownership as a convention under test, not as an answer. Ask what ground would show that the allocation it produces is wrong (F2, F9).
4. Keep provenance and exclusion apart. Citation, prior art and attribution are the legitimate function; the standing to block composition is the claim that needs a refuter (F6).
5. Report the two currencies separately: cost in bits of the description and cost in actions of checking whether it holds (F8).
6. Fig 4 :25 again: send the generator, that is the residual, the shape and the refuter, not the conclusion. This is what `08_expert_verification_packet.md` does.
