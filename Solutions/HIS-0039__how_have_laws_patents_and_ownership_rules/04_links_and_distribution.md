## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the interior before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong answer about who may use knowledge, so independence is worth paying for.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with their own ground and their own pre-registered refuter.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual and the shape claim, with the refuter written in advance. The recipient checks it without holding the framework's interior. Their verdict is recorded before anything else is shown.

**Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method. This is permitted (Fig 4 :22), but each user must state a ground and write a refuter before running it. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test. Nothing here has been sent by this agent; hosting and sending are yours to do.
