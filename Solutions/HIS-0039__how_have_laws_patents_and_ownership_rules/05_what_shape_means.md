## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the right legal instrument or the right historical case. It constrains; it does not fill. "A rule changes which compositions are reachable and by whom" is a shape. "The 1980 Chakrabarty decision caused X" is a name, and a causal claim that needs a ground.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "access", "control", "reach", "exclusion", "cost of checking". We do not begin with "patents" or "the public domain" as explanations, because those are borrowed frames. We begin with what changed in reach and who could act on it.

## 3. When a bundle must be split before it can be searched

*The Library of Babel* names the failure directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :54 says the same thing in the canonical register: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

This is the central "shape" result for HIS-0039. The question looks like one question about "laws, patents and ownership rules", but access and control are two conditions with different anchors. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. The shape is therefore a two-output shape, never a single signed effect.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measured denial rate, a price, a time to diffusion, a count of compositions actually made.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The ten world sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md` are imports of this kind. Each carries its field, year and quote so the record shows where it came from.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."*

## 5. Why "who owns it" is the forbidden shortcut

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered: the reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a public disclosure record tells you that a capability was disclosed and who filed it. It does not tell you which of several possible lineages produced the capability. Naming the owner as the source of the knowledge is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a confident attribution.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous case, a famous court or a famous reviewer gives a claim a budget to be tried. It does not make the claim true.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And *"You may all agree and be wrong."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** ground readings and cannot settle the historical question.

## 7. Correction to my own §4

I originally wrote that the framework's *"shape, not a name"* rule forbids naming any legal instrument. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in the library. Patent, copyright, licence and public domain are all already terms in the world frame. Naming one as the candidate that occupies a slot is a binding from the world frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework itself derives which one, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :25, *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
