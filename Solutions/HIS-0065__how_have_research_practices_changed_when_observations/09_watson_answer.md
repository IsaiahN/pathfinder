## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the change in research practice when observations become large-scale, automated or remote. Option A: treat the three changes as a new epistemology in which volume of observation replaces the old loop. Option B: keep the invariant loop and locate the change in the cast, the currency, the channel, the habitat and the provenance. What each leads to: A states a `general` verdict about the history of science with no ground reading, treats volume as evidence and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance and an explicit `gap` where the historical narrative should be.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Research practice is the loop that keeps frames ranging against the ground; the observation term changes in three ways. Arity: three-place (practice, observation mode, ground) and a union compound across four domains. Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric; scale, automation and distance act on different atoms (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`). Scale: one loop at one scale, the same kernel at population scale, a lossy transform between.
- **D1 Observation channel.** Actors: sensors, pipelines, remote platforms. Currency: observations, per-slot residuals, bandwidth. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Status admitted.
- **D2 Inference and settlement.** Actors: frames, teams, models, reviewers. Currency: agreement, residuals, effective sample size. Anchor: the ground through triangulation. Status admitted.
- **D3 Method and membrane.** Actors: methods, communities, habitats. Currency: methods crossing up, head starts down, contact. Anchor: the ground through contact. Status admitted.
- **D4 Provenance and source.** Actors: records, lineages, instruments. Currency: provenance, shadow test, round-trip gap. Anchor: the ground plus the record. Status admitted.
- **X1 Big data as a new kind of evidence.** No nameable anchor independent of D1 and D2; frame-internal. Not a domain yet, absorbed.
- **X2 Automation as a smarter researcher.** No nameable anchor independent of D3; absorbed.
- **Union, not partition.** C1 contact maintained = D1 + D4; C2 reach renewed = D2 + D3. Overlaps are compounds with their own loops; unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry. The likely break is the middle link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a historical narrative does not. The loop is invariant under scale, automation and distance. What changes is the cast, the currency, the channel, the habitat and the provenance. Instruments extend representation, not settlement. The channel decays while the ground does not, with saturation and pooling as the named failures. Volume of agreement is not independent evidence. Capability belongs to researcher and habitat together, so remote observation is a habitat substitution with two invisible failures, and only methods cross scales. Provenance becomes part of the claim. Which practice changed first, where and when, is a `gap` for this simulation and is owed to the ground. This is a `general` / `specified`, ungrounded result: candidate, not accepted.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework instead of a residual. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective moves are removable without touching the census. Claiming a new epistemology, treating n as evidence, and naming a first practice can all be deleted; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already delete them. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no historical claim is staked.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Dated records from two low-overlap closures that show independent ground contact rising with volume, an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, a channel that never drops or pools, or a reading whose source is recoverable without lineage would break the corresponding sub-claim. A demonstration that the loop stops and a different loop produces checkable ground contact would break the kernel claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and the historical `gap` stays open.
