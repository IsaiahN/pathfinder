## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and then stops "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for HIS-0065 a **shape** is the set of invariants that any account of the change in research practice must satisfy, plus the location of the break, plus the instrument that would settle it. It is not a list of dates, not a manifesto about a new epistemology, and not a name for the first practice that changed.

- Shape: "the loop is invariant; the cast, currency and anchor access change; the channel decays; volume of agreement is not independent evidence; remote observation substitutes a habitat."
- Not a shape: "large-scale observation began in astronomy in year X" or "science moved from hypothesis-driven to data-driven in decade Y." Those are historical names, and the ground must supply them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The initial vocabulary is the residual described in effect terms (§1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`), not the vocabulary of "big data" or "automation" taken as givens.
- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." This is why the census starts with actor, currency and anchor, not with a literature review.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Library of Babel*: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The stop in this solution is earlier and more honest: a shape plus an explicit `gap` where the history should be.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a history-of-science question the ground is dated, checkable records: instrument logs, data statements, protocol changes, replication outcomes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A term borrowed from survey statistics, such as effective sample size, is an import of this kind. It must be marked as borrowed, not presented as figure-derived.

Fig 2 :29 names the anchor: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For this question the anchor is the historical record, not the agreement of people who already accept a story about big data.

## 4. Source discrimination is the reason the shape stops where it does

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". A reading of "more data" cannot tell you whether the channel improved, the ground moved, or the same pool was counted many times. The essay continues: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy".

Applied here: a claim that practice improved because automation raised objectivity is a source named from a reading. The figure-grounded response is not a counter-claim about which source is right; it is a request for an instrument that discriminates. Fig 10 :24 puts it in one line: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".

This is why F3 and F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are `specified` refusable claims and why the historical narrative is a `gap`. The instrument is owed, not invented.

## 5. What would be an error here

Two errors are easy and both are recorded:

1. **Naming the change as a new epistemology.** That turns an observable question about instruments and records into a verdict about the whole history of science. Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." It also breaches the loop rule: the kernel does not change with size (Fig 4 :19-20).
2. **Treating the figure shape as the historical answer.** Fig 13 :134 says "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The shape says what any answer must look like. It does not say what happened.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous methodologists who agree with the shape is a budget, not an anchor.

Same essay, on the standing of this document: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not ground readings. They cannot settle the question they report on. Prestige, agreement and a larger citation list do not change that.
