## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks what changes in research practice when the observation term in the loop changes in three ways: many slots per step (large-scale), the action and observation pair executed by an instrument rather than by a person (automated), and contact with the object mediated by distance (remote).

Stated as a residual: research practice is the loop that keeps a community's frames ranging against a ground. The old loop assumed observation was scarce, local, human-read and person-present. The new observation changes the actors, the currency and the access to the anchor. What is owed is an account of which links move, which are invariant, and where the residual goes when the old instruments stop returning a resolvable reading.

- **Arity.** Three-place: practice, observation mode, ground. The three modes (scale, automation, distance) are not interchangeable labels.
- **Symmetry.** Ordered, not symmetric. Automation and distance are changes to the channel (Fig 10 :24), and scale is a change of size with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :30).
- **Scale.** One loop at one scale, the same kernel at population scale, and a round trip between them with a gap R_T (Fig 4 :19-20, :30).

**What an answer would look like (Direction bound as an ordinary term).** A list of invariants that any historical account must satisfy, plus the named link at which the loop breaks when the observation changes, plus the instrument that would settle each break. Not a narrative of dates, which the figures do not supply. This is a shape, not a name (Fig 13 :133).

## 4. Findings (one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate)

### F1: The loop does not change. The actors, currency and anchor access do. (D1, D3; `general`)

Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

So "research practice changed" cannot mean the loop was replaced. It means the cast changed: instruments now act, data is the currency, and access to the anchor is mediated. Anything offered as a brand new loop that supersedes observation, hypothesis and test is a misdescription. This is `general` and structural. **Triangulated** with Fig 11 :39-40 (capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of the agent alone). Refuter: demonstrate a scale at which the perceive/plan/act/predict loop stops running and a different loop produces checkable ground contact.

### F2: Scale extends what can be represented, not what can be settled. (D1; `specified`)

Fig 6 :26: "instruments extend what can be represented at all."

Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall".

A larger, automated, remote instrument enlarges closure(Γ): more slots, finer resolution, previously unrepresentable states. It does not add an atom to the world (Fig 6 :25) and it does not by itself settle a claim. The mint still needs support, reachability, novelty and settlement. **Triangulated** across Fig 6 (closure and reach) and Fig 5 (the three guards, a product). Refuter: a claim settled purely by volume of observation, with no guard and no ground payment, that survives a re-census.

### F3: The channel, not the ground, is what decays at scale and at distance. (D1; `specified`)

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".

Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".

Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch".

This is the figure that governs automated and remote observation most directly. The world does not change because the sensor is distant; the link to it does. Saturated metrics and pooled readings are exactly the failures a large automated pipeline produces. The honest output of a channel check is not a verdict about the world, it is "you have lost touch." **Triangulated** with Fig 1 :22 (aggregation throws away the signal) and, in the world literature, with automation bias (Wikipedia, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23), where automated aids produce errors of omission: "Cancers diagnosed in 46% of cases without automated aids were discovered in only 21% of cases with automated aids that failed to identify the cancer." Refuter: a channel at scale that never drops a reading, never saturates and never pools, with no new instrument and no provenance cost.

### F4: Volume of agreement is not volume of evidence. (D2; `general`, with a `specified` prediction about effective sample size)

Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not".

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Fig 2 :44: "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."

Ten thousand sensors drawing on one calibration, one pipeline and one reduction are one frame wearing many names. Reporting n = 10,000 as independent evidence is the pooled-reading failure named in F3, restated in the language of statistics. Ioannidis (PLoS Medicine, 2005) gives the world-side version: with ten independent teams chasing a low prior, "hardly any higher than the probability we had before any of this extensive research was undertaken!" **Triangulated** across Fig 7, Fig 2 and Fig 1 (three distinct figures, low overlap between room-handoff, anchor and per-slot residual). Refuter: show that effective sample size grows one-for-one with observation volume because each added observation carries independent ground contact, not a shared pool.

### F5: What changes in practice is the method that crosses the membrane and the habitat that supplies contact. (D3; `specified`)

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

Fig 4 :25-26: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The portable object is the method (the pipeline design, the calibration protocol, the refuter), never the recording of a particular successful dataset. A laboratory that buys a faster sequencer but does not change contact has improved the agent in isolation, which the figure says changes nothing. **Triangulated** across Fig 4 (membrane) and Fig 11 (habitat). Refuter, taken from Fig 11 :72 itself: an agent-only improvement at scale that moves the ground would refute the capability claim.

### F6: Remote observation is a substituted habitat, and two failures are invisible. (D3; `specified`)

Fig 11 :67: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails".

Fig 11 :69: "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts".

Remote observation is not observation with the distance subtracted. It is a habitat substitution: the field survey's conditions, season and local relations are replaced by a platform's sampling, a sensor's bandpass and a reduction pipeline. What was left out appears only when the conclusion fails; what rode in with the new platform appears only when it acts. World-side, remote sensing (Wikipedia, 2026, accessed 2026-11-23) states the mechanism plainly: "Generally speaking, remote sensing works on the principle of the inverse problem: while the object or phenomenon of interest (the state) may not be directly measured, there exists some other variable that can be detected and measured (the observation) which may be related to the object of interest through a calculation." The same page notes that ground truth still requires a field survey for validation. **Single-source in the figures** (Fig 11), corroborated from a low-overlap field (earth observation). Refuter: a remote observation whose habitat substitution preserves every condition the goal required and introduces no unchosen actor, demonstrated rather than assumed.

### F7: At scale, provenance stops being documentation and becomes part of the claim. (D4; `specified`)

Fig 10 :62: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom."

Fig 10 :65-66: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

A large dataset with no lineage cannot tell convergent derivation from adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52). The record must carry the residual it answered, the reasoning and the date, and at scale that record must be machine-actionable. The FAIR principles (Wilkinson et al., Scientific Data, 2016) are the world-side instance: "It also necessitates that the machines keep an exquisite record of provenance such that the data they are collecting can be accurately and adequately cited." **Triangulated** across Fig 10 (provenance), Fig 9 (residual first) and Fig 13 (lineage is the proof). Refuter: a large-scale reading whose source is recoverable from the reading alone, with no lineage and no discriminating instrument.

### F8: The middle link is where the new observation breaks the chain. (D1 to D3; `specified`)

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Fig 3 :25-26: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."

A pipeline can be fast (perception link), produce a schema (vocabulary link) and still not say what would count as progress. Optimising the instrument above the break cannot rescue that (Fig 3 :19). This is the strongest single figure-grounded point for the field named in the question: research practice changes where the objective link is rebuilt for n = all. **Triangulated** with Fig 12 :111-113 (a vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one). Refuter: a large-scale automated practice that states what would count as progress in its own terms and has an instrument for it, with the instrument named and its failure mode recorded.

## 5. Strain tests and re-census

**Apparent strain A: Fig 6 :26 versus Fig 10 :24.** One says instruments extend what can be represented; the other says the channel decays. Re-census: Fig 6 governs representation, the size of closure(Γ). Fig 10 governs contact, whether R still arrives from the ground. Different quantities. A sensor can enlarge what a frame can hold while simultaneously degrading the channel that feeds it. Not strained.

**Apparent strain B: Fig 7 :13 versus the statistical value of large n.** More observations do increase precision in ordinary survey statistics. Re-census: Fig 7 governs handoff between rooms with the same closure (frames), and Fig 2 :44 states the effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations. Increasing n inside one frame is "more search at the same reach" (Fig 6 :49-50), not a new room, and correlated observations are one witness wearing many names. Not strained; the statistical result corroborates the figure when independence is honoured.

**Apparent strain C: "shape, not a name" versus a history-of-science question.** The question asks how practices changed, which sounds like a request for a historical name and date. Re-census: the figures return a shape (Fig 13 :133) and stop at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. The name of a particular change belongs to the ground, that is, to historical evidence. The derivation returns the constraints and stops.

**After re-census: no strain hidden. One open item remains:** no figure line settles *which* practice changed first in *which* discipline. That is a `gap` for this derivation, recorded as such, not smoothed over. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A large-scale or automated observation is demonstrated to increase independent ground contact one-for-one with its volume, with no shared evidence pool (breaks F4).
2. An agent-only improvement at scale is demonstrated to move the ground (breaks F5 and F6; this refuter is taken from Fig 11 :72).
3. A channel is demonstrated that never drops, pools or saturates a reading at scale, with no new instrument and no provenance cost (breaks F3).
4. A large-scale reading is demonstrated whose source is recoverable from the reading alone, with no lineage and no source-discriminating instrument (breaks F7).
5. A scale is demonstrated at which the perceive/plan/act/predict loop stops running and a different loop produces checkable ground contact (breaks F1).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the shape is redrawn from the census. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Recommendation

1. Lead with the invariant and the break, not with a claim that science has entered a new epistemology. Fig 1 :27: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.
2. Report residuals per slot and keep the aggregation as an explicit choice, not a default (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42). For a claim about research practice, the cheap independence is a reader from a different closure who has not seen the framework's interior.
4. Send the method and the refuter, not the recording (Fig 4 :22, :25-26).
5. State what would count as progress in the new observation's own terms before choosing instruments (Fig 3 :27, Fig 9 :27-28).
6. Use the public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` and ask for a failure, not agreement.

## 9. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. The historical question needs dated, checkable records of practice (instrument logs, data statements, protocol changes, replication outcomes) from at least two closures with low overlap. In the figures' terms: an instrument that discriminates whether the channel or the ground moved (Fig 10 :24), and a reader who does not hold the framework's interior (Fig 10 :42). Until then the result is a `specified` shape with a `gap` where the history should be. "A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39); this is a search that found a shape and then stopped honestly.
