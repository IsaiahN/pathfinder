# The Tether's answer: research practice under large-scale, automated and remote observation

**Mode: `general` on the structural invariants, `specified` on the per-domain predictions. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading of the historical record.

## 1. The residual it answers

Research practice is the loop that keeps a community's frames ranging against a ground. Observation changes in three ways: more slots per step, the action and observation pair executed by an instrument, and contact mediated by distance. The question asks what changes in practice. Fig 9 :60 says: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Observation volume is a filter. It is not the witness.

The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54): the three changes land on four different domains, and the overlaps are compounds with their own loops.

## 2. The shape

1. **The loop is invariant; the cast changes.** Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." So "practice changed" means instruments act, data is the currency and access to the anchor is mediated. It does not mean a new epistemology replaced observation and testing.

2. **Scale extends representation, not settlement.** Fig 6 :26: "instruments extend what can be represented at all." A larger closure is not a larger reach, and no amount of arranging produces a new atom (Fig 6 :21). The mint still needs support, reachability, novelty and settlement.

3. **The failure moves into the channel.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". The honest output of a channel check is not a verdict about the world; it is "you have lost touch." In automation-biased human factors this is an error of omission, reported from a low-overlap field.

4. **Volume of agreement is not volume of evidence.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Ten thousand sensors on one calibration are one frame wearing many names. Fig 2 :44 gives the statistical version: "The effective-sample-size correction for correlated observations is standard in survey statistics and meta-analysis; the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."

5. **Capability lives in agent and habitat, and only methods cross scales.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What is portable is the method and its refuter, never the recording of one successful dataset.

6. **Remote observation substitutes a habitat.** Fig 11 :67: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails". Fig 11 :69: "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts". Distance is the mechanism; substitution is the effect.

7. **Provenance becomes part of the claim.** Fig 10 :62: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". At scale the lineage must be machine-actionable, and the residual must be described before the frame is chosen.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that science entered a new epistemology that replaces observation and test. The kernel does not change with size.
- Not that automated observation is more objective because it is automated. Automation changes the channel; it does not exempt it from decay.
- Not that large n is independent evidence. It is a budget filter until independence is shown.
- Not a historical narrative. Which practice changed first, in which field, on which date, is a `gap` here. Fifteen figure lines do not supply a date.
- Not `grounded`. No historical record was read to settle the claims above.
- Not a claim that this derivation is a ground reading. A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

Historical evidence from at least two closures with low overlap: instrument logs, data statements, protocol changes, preregistrations, replication outcomes. The questions an instrument must answer:

1. Did the channel degrade while the ground stayed fixed, or did the ground move? An instrument that discriminates these would name the failure (Fig 10 :24).
2. Did effective sample size grow one-for-one with volume, or did the readings share a pool? The test is independence, not n (Fig 2 :44).
3. Did the method cross the membrane, or only the recording? The test is reusability across a habitat substitution (Fig 4 :22, Fig 11 :67-69).
4. Did an agent-only improvement change contact? If not, the capability claim stands; if yes, it is refuted (Fig 11 :40, and the refuter at Fig 11 :72).
5. Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6: one-for-one ground contact with volume; agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a channel that never drops, pools or saturates; a reading whose source is recoverable without lineage; a scale at which the loop stops running.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The derivation stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The historical record is owed, and is not manufactured here. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not announce a new epistemology, and it does not claim to write the history of science. It says that the observation loop is invariant, and that what changes when observations scale, automate and move remote is the cast, the currency and the access to the ground. Bigger instruments extend what can be represented, not what can be settled. The failure moves into the channel, where stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings live; the ground does not decay, the channel does. More agreement is not more independent evidence, because "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Capability belongs to the researcher and their habitat together, so remote observation is a habitat substitution with failures that stay invisible until the conclusion fails. Provenance stops being documentation and becomes part of the claim. What is offered is the shape any historical account must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which practice changed first is left to the ground.
