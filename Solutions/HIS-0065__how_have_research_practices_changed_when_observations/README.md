## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation reports the actual historical record. It reports the shape any such account must have, and it stops where the instruments stop.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** A larger audience raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :29).
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
