## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the size of a monetary aggregate but the **location of the lever**. In effect terms: the central bank's library Γ contains its instruments (policy rate, reserve remuneration, asset purchases, reserve requirements). It predicts an outcome at a slot s, Γ(b,a), and the published statistics return o'. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is measured at each monetary slot (M0, M1, M2, M3, per jurisdiction), never as one pooled number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "the central bank controls the money supply": the money stock is in the closure of the central bank's own instruments, so acting on the quantity closes R directly.
- Option B, "the central bank mainly influences interest rates and credit conditions": the price of reserves and the terms of credit are in its closure, while the quantity of broad money is composed in a different closure, so the lever reaches the former and only conditions the latter.
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape** stating (i) which quantities are reachable from the policy frame's instruments, (ii) which are composed by another frame, (iii) what non-updating anchor settles the claim, and (iv) that verdicts are returned per slot, not averaged into one yes or no. Until that shape is bound it is a preference, predicts nothing, and no internal check can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is a qualified Option B in `specified` mode, with per-domain verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the economics question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of monetary policy.

Four reasons, three from the figures and one practical:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
