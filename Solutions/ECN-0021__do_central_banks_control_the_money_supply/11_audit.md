## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between the policy frame's prediction at a monetary slot and the released series. Arity: at least three conditions (the policy instrument, the bank's lending decision, the public's currency choice) plus a measurement convention, so a **union compound**, not a binary partition. Symmetry: "control" is ordered, while the observed rate-money co-movement is symmetric, so the arrow cannot be read off the data. Scale: base money at the policy frame's scale, broad money at the composed scale, with a lossy transform between.
- **Domain 1, policy frame.** Actors: committee and balance sheet desk. Currency: reserves and the policy rate. Anchor: the published policy rate and balance sheet, which do not update on an opinion.
- **Domain 2, banking and public habitat.** Actors: commercial banks, borrowers, depositors. Currency: loans and deposits. Anchor: released aggregates.
- **Domain 3, measurement.** Actors: statistical agencies and researchers. Currency: readings and revisions. Anchor: the released series and its revision history.
- **Domain 4, discourse.** Actors: commentators and committees. Currency: agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; ranges legitimately only when it consults domains 2 and 3.

Order by dependency: perception of the aggregate, vocabulary for what each frame affords, objective at the slot, lever, carry. The break is at vocabulary: the policy frame can name its own tools and has no term for deposit creation.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. **R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** is flattened when the answer is heard as "rates, not money", even though both conditions bind at different slots;
- the **per-slot indexing** is lost when "the money supply" is treated as one object, which is exactly the Simpson reversal risk;
- the **provenance of the missing atom** (commercial bank deposit creation) is lost, so the economics claim looks figure-derived when it is an import;
- the **ordered-versus-symmetric distinction** is lost when a correlation is heard as evidence of control.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a lever cannot compose atoms outside its closure, so the policy frame cannot directly mint broad money. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
