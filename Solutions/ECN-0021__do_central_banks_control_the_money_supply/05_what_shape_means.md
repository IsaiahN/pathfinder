## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `CONTROL(???)MONEY` is a shape: a relation whose slot and anchor are still open.
- `the central bank controls M2` is a name, and the framework cannot return a name of that kind without a ground reading or a provenanced import.
- `the lever reaches the price of reserves inside its own closure; broad money is composed with another frame` is a shape with a typed structure, and is what the derivation actually returns.

The question as posed asks for a name (which side wins). The honest answer reports the shape and declines the name until an anchor settles it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start from "control", "money supply", "interest rate", "credit conditions"; find the proximate terms "closure", "reachability", "anchor", "per-slot residual", "union surplus"; narrow.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Hence the outreach email asks what a refuter would expect to see at each slot.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: a final book that does not answer the question means the question was *"unanswerable, or malformed"*, possibly *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. This is the direct essay-level warrant for F1.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, that would be a jurisdiction-specific, aggregate-specific, identified measurement with a non-updating series.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Bank of England 2014 article is such an import: source gate (published institutional research), shadow test (it explains why a quantity target did not compose the intended outcome). It is debited against independence because it is one economics witness.

*They're Scanning Books*, opened this session, sharpens the distinction: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Source discrimination is the operative limit

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a rise in M2 is a reading. It is consistent with central bank asset purchases, with commercial bank lending, with the public shifting out of currency, or with several at once. Naming "central bank control" as the source of the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a stronger assertion.

## 5. Q - what licenses the verb "control" here?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape: which quantities are reachable from which closure, what the non-updating anchor is, and that readings are per slot. A verb is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the verb is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. In this solution the verb is recorded as a provenanced binding from the economics frame, `specified` and ungrounded, never as a figure derivation.

### 5b. Correction to my own overstatement (kept visible)

An earlier draft applied *"shape, not a name"* as a ban on saying anything concrete about monetary mechanisms. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. The economic actors here (a central bank, commercial banks, a published aggregate) are already available in the world frame. Naming a binding onto them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains wrong in the first draft is calling that binding an import that moves the figure wall. Fig 6 says *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; the Bank of England article adds an atom to the **policy frame's** reach, not to the figure's library. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The essay's own precedent is a PrestigeFirewall separating trust from action budget. A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, let the published series settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the monetary claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
