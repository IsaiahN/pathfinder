## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The usage rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (2026-10-02): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0034, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an adequate attribution rule that can be stated before the psychological mechanism is known. It constrains; it does not fill. "Attribution must be per factor, not a global verdict" is a shape. "People use Kelley's covariation model" is a name from the world frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay names the stopping condition: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the `06_isomers_and_order.md` union reading.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The same essay says two people who only check against each other "drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16): "Contact is Distinction Resolver". The missing factor is found by contact with an instrument or a different frame, not by searching harder inside the same vignette.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a study in which factors are varied independently and the attribution is scored against the manipulation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming Kelley's covariation model is such a binding from the social-psychology frame onto the held idea of per-factor attribution. It is recorded as provenance, not presented as a figure derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the reason names are expensive here: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The essay's conclusion: "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." With many interacting factors the outcome alone cannot name its source; the spectrometer, not the argument, settled the analogous colour case.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06) adds the limit on the observer: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

## 4. The shape for this question

The narrowed form has four constraints:

1. **Per factor.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
2. **Relative valence.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The Knobe effect is the world illustration of what happens when the reading loses its anchor and the outcome's valence leaks backwards.
3. **Arrangement-sensitive.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
4. **Cascade-aware.** Fig 11 :52 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

What the shape does not fill: which rule people actually use, how often they use it, and whether it differs by culture. Those are empirical, and this derivation did not look.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (2026-10-22) states the per-factor rule in its own words and ties it to Fig 1: "Keep the rate per kind of situation. A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them." The same essay records the asymmetry that governs responsibility: "Bad news travels in the same lane as good news."

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a world attribution model? CORRECTION TO MY OWN SECTION 3

Initially I wrote that the framework "returns a shape, not a name", so no specific attribution rule may be mentioned. That overstates the rule. The canonical sentence governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like". It governs a primitive or mechanism not held in the frame. Kelley's covariation model, the Knobe effect and the Swiss cheese model are not missing primitives in this derivation; they are world bindings onto constraints the figures already hold (per-factor signal, relative valence, arrangement). Naming them is permitted as provenance, and forbidden as a claim that the figures derived them.

I keep the earlier reading visible: it was right that the framework cannot *derive* a specific psychological mechanism and that such a mechanism must be recorded as a world import. It was wrong to extend that to a ban on naming held ideas from another frame. The corrected form is used in `09_watson_answer.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A venue's prestige is a filter for where to try the claim. It is never a witness. A checkable failure from an unknown reader is worth more than an endorsement from a famous one.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture post calls LLM outputs "simulacra" and says the solutions "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
