## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** `R_s = |Γ(b,a)_s - o'_s|` per candidate factor slot. Arity: `k > 2`, with both symmetric bonds (conjunction, disjunction) and ordered bonds (sequence, production, comparison, subtraction). Symmetry: the overall relation is ordered; actor and observer are not interchangeable. Scale: first-order direct contact, second-order cascades, and population scale, with the same loop at each level. Fig 9 names cause and responsibility a union compound, not one hard question.
- **D1, lay causal cognition.** Actors: reasoners, candidate factors in a vignette. Currency: per-factor causal judgments. Anchor: a controlled factor manipulation, which was not run here. Question type: the empirical answer, currently `gap`.
- **D2, responsibility attribution.** Actors: observer, actor, harmed party. Currency: blame and culpability. Anchor: an explicit norm or court decision. Question type: a compound with D1, with its own loop.
- **D3, scientific causal inference.** Actors: researchers, variables, instruments. Currency: effect estimates. Anchor: randomized experiment. Question type: the frame that holds the instrument D1 lacks.
- **D4, safety engineering.** Actors: barriers, latent conditions, active failures. Currency: accident trajectories. Anchor: incident record. Low overlap with D1 to D3.
- **D5, comparative cognition.** Actors: crows, hidden agents. Currency: causal-inference behaviour. Anchor: controlled hidden-agent test. Low overlap.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive factors, have terms for what they afford (Fig 3 :7), say what counts as progress (a per-slot residual that trends down), infer the arrangement, carry the result (Fig 3 :20). A link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27).
- **Partition or union.** Union. With Fig 9 :55, unbundle before search; a lever that clears only cause detection leaves the responsibility intersection standing.

Strain test applied: an early reading made responsibility a bond between actor and outcome. Re-census: bonds compose factors in the world; an attribution is a term in the reasoner's library. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. The Knobe/Fig 11 apparent conflict was also re-censused in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: ecological valence versus instrument bias.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not answer the empirical question. They constrain its shape. An adequate attribution is per factor rather than global; responsibility is relative to the outcome explained rather than a carried trait; the same factors under a different bond are a different explanation; a standing condition differs from a trigger across uses; the responsible factor can be three steps out; and agreement among reasoners sharing a vignette is not a verdict. Each domain verdict is stated above and is not averaged with the others. The named psychological mechanism is `gap`, and the instrument that would close it is a controlled factor manipulation. This is `specified` and `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape, its four refuters and the provenance are written down in front of the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before their stage-1 verdict; Fig 2 :42 prices that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). It is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no real case is decided. A later study can add a mechanism by import with provenance, and a failed refuter narrows the shape without destroying it. The outreach packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled vignette that varies factors independently and scores global versus per-factor attribution would settle D1; invariance across necessary and sufficient readings would break Fig 12 :117 here; responsibility tracking a fixed actor trait across outcomes would break Fig 11 :49; an independent anchor that fails to remove the shared-source attribution would break Fig 2 :29. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
