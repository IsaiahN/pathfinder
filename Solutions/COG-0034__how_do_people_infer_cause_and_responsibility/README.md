## The question in effect terms

An outcome occurs, and a person must say which of several interacting factors produced it and who is responsible. The effect to be explained is per factor: which candidate carries live residual mass, which carries none, and why responsibility attaches to an actor rather than to a condition. An answer would be a rule that (a) picks out the live factor or factors, (b) still works when the same factors are arranged differently, and (c) is checkable where factors are varied independently. That sought-for rule is bound as an ordinary term in `07_the_tether_answer.md` so it can accrue a residual rather than remaining an invisible preference.

## What the figures decide and what they do not

The figures give the shape any adequate answer must have: per factor rather than a global verdict, factors enumerated from the world, valence relative to the outcome rather than a fixed property of a person, causal contact several steps out, and arrangement carrying information the factor list does not. They do not give the empirical mechanism. That is recorded as `gap`, not as silence. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).
