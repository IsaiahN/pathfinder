## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, in WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I did this before relying on the primary's conclusions.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** `R_s = |Γ(b,a)_s - o'_s|` per candidate factor. Arity `k > 2`, so several operands; both symmetric (`+`, `∥`) and ordered (`→`, `⇒`, `⋛`, `-`) bonds are licensed. Symmetry: the attribution is ordered, since cause precedes outcome and actor and observer are not interchangeable. Scale: first-order contact, second-order cascade, population scale, same loop at each.
- **D1, lay causal cognition.** Actors: reasoners and candidate factors. Currency: per-factor judgments. Anchor: a factor manipulation, not run here. Question type: the empirical target, `gap`.
- **D2, responsibility.** Actors: observer, actor, harmed party. Currency: blame. Anchor: norm or decision. Compound with D1; different anchor, own loop.
- **D3, scientific causal inference.** Actors: researchers, variables, instruments. Currency: effect estimates. Anchor: randomized experiment. Holds the instrument D1 lacks.
- **D4, safety engineering.** Actors: barriers, latent and active failures. Currency: accident trajectories. Anchor: incident record. Low overlap.
- **D5, comparative cognition.** Actors: crows, hidden agents. Currency: behaviour. Anchor: controlled hidden-agent test. Low overlap.
- **Union, not partition.** Cause and responsibility share atoms but differ in anchor. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive factors, terms for what they afford, what counts as a cause, infer arrangement, carry result (Fig 3 :20). A link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :43-52 (habitat, valence, cascade) and Fig 12 :117-119 (isomer, catalyst).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: cause is per factor; responsibility is relative to the residual; arrangement matters; indirect factors matter.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim a researcher might hear, namely "people attribute causes by weighing factors in proportion to their covariation with the outcome, and they blame the actor who scores highest."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand does not carry survive badly in the trip back down:

- the **bond type** collapses. "Weighing factors in proportion to covariation" is a flat formula; it cannot distinguish necessary from sufficient from compensatory causes, which Fig 12 :117 and the Kelley schemata separate;
- the **cascade** is lost. A single-episode weighting cannot represent the factor three steps out (Fig 11 :52) or the catalyst that survives its own use (Fig 12 :119);
- the **anchor condition** is lost. The T_E claim is stated as a mechanism, while the figure shape says only what an adequate attribution must look like. Turning the shape into a mechanism would set R_T to zero and is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names.

One thing survives cleanly: the requirement that the attribution be indexed per factor and the factor list be read off the world. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
