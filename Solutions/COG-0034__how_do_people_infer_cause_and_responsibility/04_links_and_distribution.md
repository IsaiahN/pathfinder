## Relevant wider literature, by field

- **Social psychology of attribution.** Kelley's covariation model and the three causal schemata are the nearest world frame to the per-factor shape. The fundamental attribution error and actor-observer difference are the named failure modes where an observer over-reads disposition and under-reads situation.
- **Experimental philosophy.** The Knobe effect shows the outcome's moral valence changing a non-moral judgment, which is the drift Fig 2 warns about when no anchor holds the reading still.
- **Statistics and epidemiology.** Randomized assignment is the anchor that holds other factors constant; common-cause fallacy and multicollinearity are the instrument-level names for Fig 2's hidden shared source.
- **Safety engineering.** The Swiss cheese model gives the world vocabulary for latent conditions, active failures and aligned holes, which is Fig 12's arrangement claim in a different closure.
- **Comparative cognition.** Taylor et al. (2012) show that a non-human animal infers an unseen causal agent, and that the inference changes behaviour. This is low-overlap corroboration for Fig 11's enumerated habitat and hidden causes.

Agreement across these fields is not pooled as corroboration of the figures. Each is a world binding onto a held shape, and all are secondary.
