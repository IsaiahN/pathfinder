## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a corruption report reaching a body able to settle it and one destroyed or absorbed before arrival. Arity: three parties plus a habitat of contacts. Symmetry: asymmetric, power flows one way from the corrupt actor to the reporter, and capture is absorption. Scale: individual, organizational, institutional, so recursion applies. **Union**, not a single question: two conditions with a non-empty intersection (Fig 9 :54).
- **Domain 1, detection.** Actors: insider, document, anomaly. Currency: evidence. Anchor: the corrupt act. Per slot, not aggregated. Settling domain.
- **Domain 2, the channel.** Actors: reporter, journalist, editor, intake, counsel. Currency: transmission and protection. Anchor: intact arrival. The decays here, not the ground.
- **Domain 3, adjudication.** Actors: court, regulator, auditor, public record. Currency: verdicts and corrections. Anchor: the settled allegation, which must not move with opinion. Settling domain.

- **Order by dependency.** Allegation, vocabulary, a defined settlement event, a route chosen after describing R, a carried method. Refuse before mint (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 8 :53).

My census matches the primary's three settling domains, its union reading and its exclusion of D4. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: a maintained channel to an unmoved ground, a different frame imported, declared stakes, bought independence, habitat enumerated.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete institutional reading a reader will naturally make: "pass a comprehensive whistleblower protection statute and create an independent commission."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **asymmetry of introduction** (Fig 11 :61), which a statute cannot convert into exclusion of the corrupt habitat;
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** (Fig 9 :32), which the concrete reading breaches by treating an institution as the derived answer.

One thing survives cleanly: a specific residual needs a maintained channel and a keeper who does not benefit from the answer. That is the residue worth sending.
