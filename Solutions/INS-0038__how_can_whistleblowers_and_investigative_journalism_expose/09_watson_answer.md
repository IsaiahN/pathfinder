## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a corruption report that reaches a body able to settle it and one destroyed or absorbed before arrival. Arity: three parties (reporter, corrupt actor, settling body) plus a habitat of second-order contacts. Symmetry: asymmetric; power flows from the corrupt actor to the reporter, and capture is absorption in one direction. Scale: individual, organizational, institutional; recursion applies. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, detection.** Actors: insider, document, anomaly. Currency: evidence. Anchor: the corrupt act, recoverable from records. Per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 2, the channel.** Actors: whistleblower, journalist, editor, secure intake, counsel. Currency: transmission and protection of the source. Anchor: intact arrival at a body that can act. The channel decays; the ground does not (Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 3, adjudication.** Actors: court, regulator, inspector general, auditor, published record, public. Currency: verdicts, corrections, sanctions. Anchor: the settled allegation, which must not update on any frame's opinion. A keeper who benefits is disqualified (Fig 10 :93).
- **D4, the habitat.** No nameable settling anchor, so not a verdict-bearing domain. It is the contact structure around D1 to D3, enumerated not composed. Effects are recorded in the findings, not given a separate verdict.
- **Union, not partition.** Retaliation and capture are two conditions with a non-empty intersection. Unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency.** Specific allegation, vocabulary for the failure, a defined settlement event, a route chosen after describing R, then a method carried forward. Post-report retaliation monitoring is the currently uninstrumented link.

Strain test applied: the "independent court" is a role description, not a named primitive. It is derived from the keeper test. Naming an office would be an import, not a strain. No strain remains after re-census except the journalist-as-outside-frame approximation recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q4.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the naming does not. Retaliation and capture are channel failures, so the remedy must change contact. The corrupt system cannot be its own adjudicator, because a keeper who benefits from the answer is not an anchor. The only route out of the closed frame is an import from a frame whose closure differs, described in effect terms first and paid for with independence in proportion to a cost measured in lives and careers. Stakes are declared, not denied. The habitat is enumerated three steps out. Retaliation and capture are a union compound whose intersection needs its own loop. No figure line names a statute, office or jurisdiction, so that part is `gap`. This is a `general` shape with `specified` failure-mode findings, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** the direct cost is a false shape that sends a reporter into a protection regime that shields the source but leaves the editor captured, which Fig 3 would call effort above the break. The wider cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named institution and asking for endorsement before a verdict. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" prices that. The cost is bounded and locatable because the packet carries five refuters and a dependency order, so an error has an address.
- **undoable:** yes. No statute or institution was committed to, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A case record that settles one lever can be added to `01` without touching the shape. The outreach materials can be superseded by a corrected packet. A sent claim with its refuter can be corrected in public; a sent claim that asked for trust cannot recover the spent independence.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An arrival count with the source safe would settle whether the channel held; a keeper test would show whether the adjudicator was an anchor; an evidence-pool audit would settle whether two venues were independent; a cascade test would settle whether third-order protection worked; and a single lever clearing the intersection would falsify the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
