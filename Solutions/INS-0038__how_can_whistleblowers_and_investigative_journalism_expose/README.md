## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list built from people likely to agree is a mirror chain, not a check.
2. **Selection for agreement disqualifies the verdict.** Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." In this field the correlation is already structural: most people in a professional network share the same evidence pool.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally, scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
