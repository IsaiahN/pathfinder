# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the reader can see the stage-1 claim in isolation. The two-stage offer collapses if the theory arrives before the verdict.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about corruption exposure, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question: what would an arrangement have to look like for a corruption report to reach a body that can settle it, without the reporter being punished or absorbed? I am not asking you to read a framework, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the claim below does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the failure modes):**
>
> 1. Retaliation and capture are failures of the *channel*, not failures of the ground. The corrupt act does not change when a reporter is fired or bought; the connection to the body that could settle it does.
> 2. The system under investigation cannot be its own adjudicator when its keeper benefits from the answer. A supervisor who profits from the corruption is not an anchor.
> 3. The only route out of a closed frame is contact with a frame whose closure differs: an independent court, a regulator in another jurisdiction, a press outlet with a different evidence pool. I wrote this down from the figures before searching the field.
> 4. Retaliation and capture are two conditions, not one. A shield for the source does not fix a captured editor. They have to be unbundled and then rejoined.
> 5. The cost of being wrong here is a life or a career, so independence has to be bought in proportion to that cost. Ten outlets repeating one dossier are one witness.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular statute, country or ombudsman office *is* the answer; that whistleblowers are disinterested (they have an unavoidable interest in the outcome); or that this derivation is a measurement. It is a shape. I am not asking you to trust anything.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a report settles against an unmoved ground with no different frame entering the corrupt system;
> 2. a reporter or adjudicator with a declared, independently modelled interest still tracks the corrupt actor's interest;
> 3. one lever clears both retaliation and capture in their overlap;
> 4. two low-overlap venues agree and the ground later settles the allegation as false;
> 5. a dead channel revives without the missing link being restored.
>
> If any of those holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because ..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the source links. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Full architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Why this order

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The reader checks the claim without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Refuter stated before the claim's reception | Seat Map :287 "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." |
| Mode stated on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule. No measurement here, so nothing is `grounded`. |
| No claim that the reporter is disinterested | Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." |
| No named institution as the answer | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". |
| Independence priced by cost of being wrong | Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the formula as a method, not a belief | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask each user to write their own refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |

## One-to-one only

No bulk send, no mail merge, no ranking by likely agreement. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional or opt-in submission points. A reply at stage 1 is a verdict to record, not corroboration to pool (Fig 2 :40).
