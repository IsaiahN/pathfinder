## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A report about hidden wrongdoing has to travel from a person inside or near a corrupt system to a body that can settle the allegation. A court, a regulator, an auditor, a published record, an electorate. Two failure modes are named in the question:

- **Retaliation.** The channel to the reporter is attacked, so the report stops arriving or the reporter is silenced.
- **Capture.** The reporter, the editor or the adjudicating body is absorbed into the corrupt frame, so the report is neutralised, reframed or rewarded into silence.

The residual is the gap between a report that reaches an unmovable ground and a report that is destroyed or absorbed before arrival. It is not "how do we find corruption" and it is not "how do we pass a law." Those are two different links in a chain (Fig 3).

**Sought shape, bound as an ordinary term before the run** (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction"). An answer would look like this:

> An arrangement in which a specific allegation reaches a body that (i) can settle that allegation without updating on the corrupt actor's opinion, (ii) cannot be moved by the corrupt actor, and (iii) cannot be reconstructed or absorbed by the corrupt actor, while the reporter's continued contact with the ground does not depend on the corrupt actor's consent.

This shape is written down and bound now so it can accrue a residual like any other term. It is a shape, not a name: it does not name a statute, a country or a newsroom.

## 1. Domain census

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three parties. Reporter, corrupt actor, adjudicating or publishing body. Plus a habitat of second-order contacts (employer, legal counsel, editor, family, professional network, media owner, regulator).
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. Power flows one way from the corrupt actor to the reporter; the reporter cannot retaliate in kind. Capture is also asymmetric: the corrupt frame absorbs the reporter, not the reverse.
- **Scale:** individual (one allegation, one insider), organizational (newsroom, agency, compliance unit), institutional/societal (court, regulator, public). Multiple scales, so recursion applies (Fig 4 :19).

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Detection / residual production | insider, document, anomaly, auditor | evidence, documentation | **yes:** the corrupt act itself, recoverable from records. Per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). |
| D2 The channel | whistleblower, journalist, editor, secure drop, hotline, counsel | transmission and protection of the source | **yes:** intact arrival at a body that can act. The channel, not the ground, is what decays (Fig 10 :24). |
| D3 Adjudication / settlement | court, regulator, inspector general, auditor, legislature, published record, public | verdicts, corrections, sanctions, publication | **yes:** the settled allegation. Must not update on any frame's opinion (Fig 2 :33). |
| D4 Habitat / power field | corrupt network, management, SLAPP litigation, patronage, blacklist, media ownership | retaliation capacity, capture capacity | **no nameable settling anchor.** It is the environment, enumerated not composed (Fig 11 :43). Not a verdict-bearing domain. |

**Domain rule applied.** D4 has no anchor that settles anything, so by the census rule it is not a fourth domain with its own verdict. It is the contact structure around D1 to D3 and is treated as an overlay. This is a correction I made mid-run; see §6.

### Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** The question bundles two conditions (retaliation, capture). Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. Anonymity protects the source against the boss but does nothing about an editor who kills the story for access. The union and its consequence are derived in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

### Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. The allegation must exist and be specific enough to name (perception, vocabulary). Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
2. The channel must be reachable and monitored (objective, planning).
3. A body must be able to settle it (learning, carrying).

Effort spent on protection law after the channel has been killed is effort above the break. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The Wikipedia account of retaliation monitoring reports that fewer than half of employees were monitored for retaliation after an investigation, which is exactly an uninstrumented link.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Both named harms are channel failures, not ground failures

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :27-28 "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch" and :29 "keep the anchor reachable".

Retaliation severs the reporter from the channel. Capture turns the reporter or the adjudicator into a frame that updates on the corrupt system. Neither event changes the corrupt act. The remedy is therefore a change in contact, not a change in courage. Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

**Refuter:** show a case where the channel to an unmoved ground stays open and the allegation settles, while an identical channel without the protective arrangement settles it too, with report-arrival measured in both. If protection leaves arrival unchanged, F1 fails.

### F2 - A corrupt system cannot be its own adjudicator

Fig 2 :34 "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices". Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."

Internal-only reporting to a supervisor who benefits from the corruption is one frame checking itself. The Wikipedia account of US federal appeals reports that agency "attorney examiners" deny 98 percent of whistleblower appeals, with high affirmance rates upstream. That is a keeper with a reputation to protect, not an anchor.

**Refuter:** exhibit an internal body whose keeper demonstrably benefits from the outcome and which nonetheless returns verdicts against its own interest, with the incentive removed shown rather than assumed.

### F3 - The remedy requires an import from a closure that differs

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

The shape therefore requires a different frame: an independent court, a regulator in another jurisdiction, a press outlet whose funding and evidence pool do not overlap the corrupt network. UNODC states that "The protection of whistle-blowers and reporting persons is frequently referred to as one of the most effective tools to prevent, detect and ultimately prosecute corruption cases," which is a world-source description of an added frame, not a derivation of one. The import has gates: source gate (provenance) and shadow test (does it explain the gap?). Importing a frame because it sounds independent is the failure Fig 9 :27-28 names.

**Refuter:** show a corrupt system that becomes reliably self-correcting with no different closure entering, with the books showing no import.

### F4 - Capture is the anchor moving; declare the stake, do not demand its absence

Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 10 :61 "standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on". Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying". Fig 10 :60 "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."

A reporter who wants the corrupt actor removed has an interest in the outcome. That is unavoidable and not disqualifying. A reporter whose reading of the evidence changes with career prospect, payment or access has an interest in the reading. That is disqualifying. The honest rule is declaration plus an independent check, not a fantasy of a stake-free observer.

**Refuter:** exhibit a reporter or adjudicator whose declared interest was independently modelled and whose verdict nevertheless tracked the corrupt actor's interest on the ground; or show a captured body whose correct verdict was traceable to a ground it could not move.

### F5 - Independence must be bought; agreement is not corroboration

Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Ten outlets repeating one leaked dossier are one frame wearing ten names. The SEC whistleblower program pays for original information that leads to an enforcement action over $1 million, which prices the atom rather than the echo. CPJ's documented counts (130 journalists and media workers killed in 2025, 340 imprisoned as of December 1 2025) price the cost of being wrong in the highest possible currency, so independence is worth buying at scale here.

**Refuter:** exhibit two venues with audited disjoint evidence pools whose agreement survives that audit, and a ground reading that settles the allegation. Agreement without settlement is still one witness.

### F6 - Unbundle before searching; one lever does not clear the intersection

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Seat Map :211 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Anonymity and legal shields address retaliation. Blind editing, declared interests and independent adjudication address capture. Neither clears the other's region, and the intersection (a protected source whose story is killed by a captured editor) is a specific compound requiring its own loop.

**Refuter:** exhibit a single lever that demonstrably clears both conditions in the intersection, with the intersection measured rather than assumed.

### F7 - Enumerate the habitat; contact cascades outward

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52-53 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Retaliation is third order: dismissal, then blacklist, then family and health. Capture is second order: a promotion, a silent editor, a dead story. Wikipedia reports a University of Pennsylvania study in which 69 percent of 300 whistleblowers were fired or forced to retire, and describes blacklisting and prolonged campaigns to "silence, discredit, isolate, and erode" well-being. The revenge literature reports crows scolding 26 percent of mask-wearing researchers within two weeks and 66 percent three years later, a transmitted, multi-year response. Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Protection adds shields; it cannot subtract the corrupt habitat.

**Refuter:** enumerate a habitat in which the cascade terminates after one link, with the termination shown; or measure a protection at one node that stops the whole chain.

### F8 - The protecting seat must not see inside the frame it protects

Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Seat 4 of the seat map "must not hold the interiors, the records, the anchor, any stake, or any authority."

The journalist reading a report without the source's private circumstances is the outside frame: they produce corrections on shape and the question "could this have been anything else?" The protection institution is closer to the ground maintainer: it routes, shields and issues rulings with provenance, but it may not author the verdict. If the body that grants protection also decides what counts as corruption, it has become a participant.

**Refuter:** exhibit a body that both shields sources and adjudicates truth, with a record showing the adjudication did not move with the shielding.

### F9 - The answer is a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".

No figure line names a statute, a country or an ombudsman office. The framework returns the constraints any working arrangement must satisfy. Naming a specific institution as *the* answer requires a provenanced import with a shadow test, not a derivation.

**Refuter:** produce a figure-derived derivation that uniquely names one institution as the answer without an import; that would breach Fig 9 :32.

### F10 - Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence".

Lines govern the channel, the ground, the import and independence, so the verdict is not `gap`. But no line governs which statute works, so that part is `gap` and is reported as such. The `gap` is a boundary of the derivation, not a failure to search.

**Refuter:** show a figure line that does govern a specific institutional design after a genuine re-census; then the `gap` closes.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287 "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A report reaches an unmoved ground and settles the allegation with no different closure entering the corrupt system (breaks F3).
2. A reporter or adjudicator with a declared interest, independently modelled, still returns verdicts that track the corrupt actor's interest (breaks F4).
3. A single lever demonstrably clears both retaliation and capture in their intersection (breaks F6).
4. Two frames with audited low overlap agree and the ground later settles the allegation as false (breaks F5).
5. A dead channel is revived by effort applied above the break without restoring the missing link (breaks F1 and Fig 3).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Treat each allegation as a separate slot; document it before naming a venue (Fig 1 :19, Fig 8 :53).
2. Route it into a body whose keeper does not benefit from the answer (F2) and whose closure differs from the corrupt system (F3).
3. Maintain the channel: source protection, secure intake, legal counsel, and post-investigation retaliation monitoring, which is the uninstrumented link the world sources expose (F1, Fig 3 :27).
4. Buy independence in proportion to the cost: low-overlap venues and an audit of shared evidence, not a syndicated echo (F5).
5. Declare the stake; keep the reading independent (F4).
6. Enumerate two or three steps out for retaliation and capture cascades (F7).
7. Offer the formula only after the outside reader has recorded a verdict on the shape (F8, `02_outreach_email.md`).
8. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the generator: the shape, the dependency order and the refuter, not a list of conclusions.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a fact here, or a bundle?** A union: retaliation and capture are two conditions, so unbundle (Fig 9 :54).
2. **Where does the signal live?** Per slot (Fig 1 :19). Each allegation gets its own loop; no aggregate "culture of corruption" score.
3. **What fails?** The channel (Fig 10 :24), not the ground. Retaliation and capture are both failures of contact.
4. **Who can settle it?** A keeper who does not benefit (Fig 10 :93). The corrupt actor's own oversight body is a frame, not an anchor.
5. **What moves the wall?** An import from a different closure (Fig 6 :23), with source gate and shadow test. Describe the residual before naming the venue (Fig 8 :53).
6. **What is settled?** Nothing in this run. UNODC, OSC, SEC, CPJ and the cognitive sources are world corroboration, not settlement.
7. **What is the route when no atom closes R?** SEARCHED first: is an independent venue already inside the available closure and unfound (Fig 6 :49)? INWARD: instrument the channel, since an unmonitored link is nobody's link (Fig 3 :27). OUTWARD: import a different frame, debited against independence. These are three questions, never one (Fig 6 :31).
