# Expert verification packet - exposing corruption without retaliation or capture

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in whistleblower protection, institutional design or investigative journalism can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. Send this one, not the whole folder.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the failure-mode findings, not `grounded`.** No case record or instrument is claimed as settled here.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Retaliation and capture are failures of the channel between a corruption report and the body that could settle it, not failures of the ground. The corrupt act does not change when a reporter is fired or absorbed; the connection to a settling body does. From that it follows that a system under investigation cannot be its own adjudicator when its keeper benefits from the answer; that the only route out of a closed frame is an import from a frame whose closure differs; that retaliation and capture are a union compound whose intersection needs its own loop; that independence must be bought in proportion to a cost measured in lives and careers; and that the habitat must be enumerated to at least three orders because contact cascades. The framework returns this as a shape, not a named institution. Five refuters are written below, before any theory is shown.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 2
- **Statement:** retaliation and capture are channel failures, so the relevant remedy changes contact, not courage; and a keeper who benefits from the answer is not an anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 2 :34 "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
- **Refuter:** show a case where an identical report settles with and without the protective arrangement, with arrival measured; or exhibit an internal keeper who benefits and reliably returns verdicts against its own interest with the incentive removed demonstrated.

### C2 - institutional design; checkable against case records and the world literature
- **Statement:** the two harms are a union with a non-empty intersection. Source shields address retaliation; blind editing and independent adjudication address capture; neither clears the other's region. Second- and third-order contact (blacklist, legal attack, family) must be enumerated because contact cascades.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
- **What to check:** does a protection regime that only shields the source leave a captured-editor failure standing? Are second- and third-order effects actually monitored in the cases you know? The world literature reports that fewer than half of surveyed employees were monitored for retaliation after an investigation, and that 69 percent of 300 studied whistleblowers were fired or forced to retire.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a single lever that demonstrably clears both conditions in the intersection, with the intersection measured; or enumerate a habitat whose cascade terminates at first order with the termination shown.

### C3 - verification arrangement; checkable by an outside reader
- **Statement:** the protecting seat must not author the verdict, independence must be bought in proportion to the cost, and stakes must be declared rather than denied.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a body that both shields sources and adjudicates truth with a record showing the adjudication did not move with the shielding; or two low-overlap venues whose agreement survives an evidence-pool audit and whose allegation the ground later settles as false.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a guarantee of safety for any reporter or journalist.
- Not a named statute, country, ombudsman office or newsroom as *the* answer. That is an import requiring a source gate and a shadow test.
- Not a claim that reporters are disinterested. Their interest in the outcome is declared, not denied.
- Not a claim that publication alone settles corruption. Publication is a channel event; settlement is a ground event.
- Not a derivation of one field from another. The figures are domain-agnostic.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment, a court or a case record in this run.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Arrival test:** a measured count of allegations that reach a body able to act, with the source safe and unabsorbed, under the arrangement and without it.
2. **Keeper test:** whether the adjudicating keeper benefits from the answer (Fig 10 :93).
3. **Overlap test:** an audit of whether the allegedly independent venues draw on disjoint evidence (Fig 2 :40).
4. **Cascade test:** whether protection holds at second and third order, not only at the employer (Fig 11 :44).
5. **Split test:** whether the retaliation and capture conditions remain separate in the intersection after a lever is applied (Fig 9 :54).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 10 :24 and :42, Fig 2 :34 and :42, Fig 9 :54, Fig 11 :44 and :61. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against a case you know: was the failure retaliation, capture, or the intersection, and was the second-order effect monitored?

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
