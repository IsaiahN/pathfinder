## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. "A report reaches an unmoved ground through a maintained channel" is a shape. "Country X's whistleblower statute is the answer" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

For this question the bootstrap vocabulary is deliberately plain: report, channel, ground, keeper, import, stake, cascade. None of those names a statute. They are the closest terms we already have to the effect described.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Applying them to whistleblower protection does not make the institutional conclusions neutral. It makes the choice of vocabulary visible, which is why §6 of `01` records the retractions.

## 4. Why the single-lever answer is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a fallen conviction rate, a quiet newsroom, a compliant inspector general are all green readings. They do not tell you whether the source was retaliation, capture, or an honest absence of wrongdoing. Naming "weak enforcement" as the source from the green reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources: post-report retaliation monitoring, a protected intake, a court with no stake in the keeper's reputation.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q2 - what licenses a particular protection institution?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular institution is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term (does the allegation actually get adjudicated, with the reporter still safe?) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does recommending an independent court commit the error the README forbids?** No, with a qualification. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. "Independent adjudicator" is a role description, a shape, not a named institution. The moment it becomes "Court X in Country Y," it is an import from the world frame and needs provenance and a shadow test. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Someone let prestige stand in for proof ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous whistleblowers or award-winning outlets to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let a court, an audit or a published record settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional and opt-in venues, not famous individuals.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "outputs generated must be seen as simulacra." So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. The society-of-agents warning

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." In this field that is the default state of a professional network: the same leaked dossier, the same press release, the same hostile commentary. Fig 2 :40 makes the arithmetic explicit. A shape that says "buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" is a direct consequence, not a rhetorical preference.

## 8. Honest stop

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The figure-derived shape can tell you what a working arrangement must look like. It cannot tell you that a particular jurisdiction has one, or that a particular reporter is safe. The record of the route is what makes that checkable, and the check belongs to a ground, not to agreement.
