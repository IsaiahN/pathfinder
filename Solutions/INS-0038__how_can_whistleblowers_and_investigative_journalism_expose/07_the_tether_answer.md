# The Tether's answer: exposing corruption without retaliation or capture

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the failure-mode findings, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A report about hidden wrongdoing has to get from someone near it to a body that can settle it. The two named harms are not two versions of the same problem: retaliation attacks the carrier, capture changes the reader. They form a union compound, each with its own loop and its own instrument (Fig 9 :54). The residual is the gap between a report that reaches an unmoved ground and a report destroyed or absorbed before arrival.

## 2. The shape

1. **The ground does not decay; the channel does.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Retaliation and capture are both channel failures. Courage, exhortation and "awareness" do not change contact; Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
2. **The corrupt system cannot adjudicate itself.** Fig 10 :93 "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified." An internal keeper who profits from the answer is a frame, not an anchor (Fig 2 :34).
3. **The wall moves only on contact with a different closure.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The remedy is an import: an independent court, a regulator in another jurisdiction, a press outlet with a distinct evidence pool. It needs a source gate and a shadow test, and the residual must be described before the frame is picked (Fig 8 :53).
4. **Unbundle the union.** A source shield clears retaliation; blind editing and independent adjudication clear capture. Neither clears the intersection, so the compound needs its own loop (Fig 9 :54).
5. **Buy independence in proportion to the cost.** Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Here the cost is a life or a career. Low-overlap venues and an audit of shared evidence are required; ten outlets on one dossier are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
6. **Declare the stake; do not pretend it away.** Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." The reporter's interest is not a defect. The interest that moves the reading is.
7. **Enumerate the habitat three steps out.** Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Protection is introduction, not exclusion (Fig 11 :61).
8. **The answer is a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". No statute, country or office is derived. Choosing one is an import and must be provenanced.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a guarantee of safety. The shape constrains an arrangement; it does not promise an outcome.
- Not a named institution, law or jurisdiction. That is an import with a source gate, not a derivation.
- Not a claim that reporters are disinterested. Their interest in the outcome is structural and declared.
- Not a claim that a free press alone settles corruption. Publication is a channel event; settlement is a ground event.
- Not a derivation of one field from another. The figures are domain-agnostic: only actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :19-20).
- Not grounded. No case record or instrument was measured here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Arrival test:** does the allegation reach a body that can act, with the source still safe and unabsorbed? This is a count, not an impression.
- **Keeper test:** does the adjudicating keeper benefit from the answer? If yes, it is not an anchor (Fig 10 :93).
- **Overlap test:** do the supposedly independent venues draw on disjoint evidence? Audit the pools (Fig 2 :40).
- **Cascade test:** did protection hold at second and third order (blacklist, legal attack, family), or only at the employer? (Fig 11 :44).
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: settlement with no different frame entering; a declared interest that still tracks the corrupt actor; one lever clearing the intersection; low-overlap agreement that the ground later refutes; a dead channel revived without its missing link.

The world sources supply partial instruments: OSC's confidential intake and public case file, the SEC's awards and retaliation rules, CPJ's killed and imprisoned counts, UNODC's country reviews. They corroborate the need for these tests; they do not run them here.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached on the shape, `gap` on the institution.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The channel, ground, import, independence and habitat lines govern, so the verdict is not `gap` overall. But no figure line names a working statute or office, so that part is `gap` and is reported as such. *They're Scanning Books*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a law or promise safety. It says that both harms in this question, retaliation and capture, are failures of the channel rather than of the ground: the corrupt act does not change when a reporter is fired or bought, only the connection to the body that could settle it does. So the corrupt system cannot be its own judge, a different frame must be imported, independence must be bought in proportion to a cost measured in lives and careers, stakes must be declared, and the habitat must be enumerated three steps out because retaliation and capture cascade. Anonymity clears the source but not the captured editor; the intersection needs its own loop. What the derivation offers is the shape any working arrangement must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The choice of institution stays `gap` until a ground settles it.
