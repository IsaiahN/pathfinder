# Union, order and cascade: the supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The question names two harms in one sentence. Are they one problem with two faces, two separate problems, or a compound with its own loop? And in what order must the remedies arrive? Three questions are owed:

1. Is the pair a partition (two independent cases) or a union (overlapping conditions with a specific intersection)?
2. What is the dependency order of the links, so effort is not spent above a break?
3. How far out does the contact cascade reach before protection can be called complete?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: report, channel, ground, keeper, import, stake, habitat, refusal. Bonds held: the seven plus negation. The two conditions (retaliation, capture) are not atoms; they are descriptions of how contact fails. No new primitive is available without import.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- Seat Map :211 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."
- Let R mean retaliation, C mean capture. A source shield clears R. A blind editing rule plus an independent adjudicator clears C. Does the shield clear C? No: a protected source whose story is killed by a captured editor still produces no settlement. Does blind editing clear R? No: an independent editor cannot stop an employer from firing the source.
- Therefore the pair is a **union with a non-empty intersection**, not a partition and not one hard problem.
- **Isomer test, done properly.** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Retaliation and capture share the report and the ground but not the failing relation: retaliation attacks the carrier, capture changes the reader. They are not isomers in the sense the exemplar used for GR and QM, because they do not share a full atom set arranged differently. They are closer to Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Both are *channel failures* (the group); the qualifier says which one.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a single lever demonstrably clears both conditions in the intersection, with the intersection measured, the union claim fails and the pair collapses to one problem with one loop.

### Q2 - What is the order of links?

- Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." (quoted from the figure text, which says the chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance)
- Ordered chain for this question:

| link | product | what breaks it | instrument that reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 perception | a specific allegation with a slot | vagueness, aggregation into "culture" | per-slot documentation (Fig 1 :19) |
| 2 vocabulary | terms for what the report affords | no language for the failure | describe the effect, not the cause (Fig 9 :30-31) |
| 3 objective | what counts as progress here | "awareness raised" instead of allegation settled | a defined settlement event |
| 4 planning | a route to that objective | naming a venue before describing R | residual-first lookup (Fig 8 :53) |
| 5 learn and carry | a method that survives the case | the story published, the method lost | method carried up, head start down (Fig 4 :22) |

- Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." In the world sources, the uninstrumented link is post-investigation retaliation monitoring: fewer than half of employees were monitored for retaliation after an investigation. Effort aimed at louder anti-corruption messaging lands above that break.
- **Falsifier:** if a case is settled with link 3 absent (no defined settlement event) and the reporter remains safe, the dependency order is wrong for that case and must be re-censused.

### Q3 - How far out does the cascade reach?

- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- Fig 11 :52-53 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- Enumeration for a whistleblower:

| order | actors in contact | failure | protection introduced |
|---|---|---|---|
| first | employer, immediate supervisor | dismissal, demotion, workload | legal shield, confidential intake |
| second | industry network, professional regulator, editor | blacklist, killed story, captured review | independent venue, blind editing, declared stake |
| third | family, health, legal costs, media owner | harassment, SLAPP suits, financial ruin | legal defence fund, emergency safety channel, cross-jurisdiction publication |

- Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A shield is an introduction. It does not remove the corrupt network from the reporter's habitat, which is why protection is "permanent work rather than a solved state" (Fig 11 :63-64).
- The world sources corroborate the second and third orders: Wikipedia describes "extensive, coordinated and prolonged efforts that institutions can deploy to silence, discredit, isolate, and erode their financial and mental well-being," and reports that 69 percent of 300 studied whistleblowers were fired or forced to retire. The revenge source reports crows transmitting a response to a dangerous mask across years (26 percent scolding within two weeks, 66 percent three years later), a non-human instance of a cascade that outlives the original encounter.
- **Falsifier:** enumerate a habitat where the cascade terminates at first order, with the termination shown, and the three-order requirement is narrowed to that case.

### Q4 - Is the journalist the outside frame?

- Seat Map Seat 4: "Whoever reads the reports without seeing what they describe." It "must not hold the interiors, the records, the anchor, any stake, or any authority."
- A journalist working a source *does* hold some interior (the source's account, sometimes the documents). So the journalist is not literally Seat 4. The **audience** and the outside expert reader are Seat 4: they read the shape without the referent and can catch failures of shape that insiders read straight past.
- The protection institution is closer to Seat 3, the ground maintainer: it keeps the channel reachable, routes material with provenance, and issues rulings. It "may author what has no truth value and nothing that does" (Seat Map :46). If it authors the verdict on corruption, it has left its seat.
- **Falsifier:** exhibit a protection body that both shields sources and adjudicates truth, with a record demonstrating the adjudication did not move with the shielding. F8 in `01` carries the same test; both are recorded so it is not lost.

### Q5 - What about negation?

- Operators table :57-58: negation "this does not hold", and :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."
- A reporting system that can log complaints but cannot return "this finding is false" can only accumulate. It can be corrected in practice and cannot carry the correction in its records. This is why the pre-registered refuter in `01` §4 is part of the output, not an appendix.
- **Falsifier:** show a system with no negation operator that withdraws a settled finding while preserving the record of why. That would refute the operator claim at this level.

## CONCLUSION

Retaliation and capture form a union compound with a specific intersection, not a partition and not an isomer pair. They are two members of the same functional group, channel failures, with different qualifiers. The dependency order runs perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to carry, and the currently uninstrumented link is post-report retaliation monitoring. The habitat must be enumerated to at least three orders because contact cascades, and protection is introduction, not exclusion. The outside frame is the reader of the shape, not necessarily the reporter who holds the source. A vocabulary without negation cannot carry its own corrections.

This framing rescues what is checkable without claiming a named institution: the union, the order, the cascade and the refuter are all figure-derived; the choice of venue remains an import with a source gate.
