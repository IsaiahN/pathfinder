## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has characterised a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** In this field most professional networks share sources. A mass send selected for sympathy produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Record the stage-1 verdict first and never pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").
- **Hard precondition:** the output must survive stage 1. This folder offers a shape and a refuter, not an institutional design or a promise of safety.
