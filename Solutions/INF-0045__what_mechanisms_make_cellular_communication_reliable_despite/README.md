## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures derive a specific molecule. They return a shape; the molecular names are bindings from the biology frame, provenanced as such.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. **Do not show the interior before the verdict.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
