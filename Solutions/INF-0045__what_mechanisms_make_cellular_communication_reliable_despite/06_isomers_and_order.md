## DERIVATION

### Q1: Are refusal, maintenance and independence isomers?

- Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Refusal is **not a bond**. Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." It is a unary operator and does not enter the bond count.
- Maintenance is **not a single bond either**. It is a set of structures: Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."; :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."; :122 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- Independence **is** a bond at the abstract level: disjunction, "either suffices; not both required" (Fig 12 :60-64), plus the triangulation rule of Fig 2.

So the three shapes are **not one isomer family**. They span three different categories of the framework's notation: a unary operator, a set of structures, and a symmetric bond. That is the structural reason they form a union (F8 of `01`) and why a single lever cannot clear all three conditions. The earlier reading, that they were isomers of one reliability molecule, is **retracted here and kept visible**.

- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if all three shapes can be rewritten within one bond type (for example all as disjunction over the same operand set), then they are isomers and the union claim fails. Equivalently, if ¬ can be shown to be a bond under the six tests of Fig 12, the category split collapses.

### Q2: What is the order of the three loops?

Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." The dependency order derived in `01` §1 is:

1. **Perception (D1):** distinguish the signal from a near-match. Refusal is owed first.
2. **Vocabulary and objective:** state what counts as the correct response. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." This is the middle link Fig 3 names as the least instrumented.
3. **Maintenance (D2):** carry the state while the channel decays.
4. **Carry and select (D3):** independent paths within a cell, selection between cells.

**Refuse before mint.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 puts REFUTED ahead of the other bins because the other bins assume its answer. In biology terms: do not build a stronger response pathway before checking that the pathway is responding to the right molecule.

### Q3: Is the population loop the same structure or a new one?

- Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
- Fig 7 :33 "variation inside, selection between"; :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."

The population loop is the same rule at another stratum, not a fourth mechanism. Where a maintainer exists (the cell with chaperones and feedback), the channel is actively kept; where none exists (the population), selection runs. Fig 12 :103 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."

### Q4: Does the biology frame offer an analogue of the isomer claim?

Edelman and Gally 2001 (opened 2026-11-23, `10_source_ledger.csv`) define degeneracy as structurally different elements performing the same function. Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." That is the figure's version of the same idea: different chains can compose functionally identical terms. So *degenerate signalling paths* are a genuine instance of the figure's route-to-substance claim, and the analogy is licensed by the figure line rather than imported from biology into the framework.

This is **corroboration of a figure shape by a world frame**, not a derivation. It carries the weight of one witness from a different field, and it does not settle which molecules occupy the shape.

If "robustness" is used as a single label for all three, the abstraction `T_A` drops at least three constraints: the refusal operator, the per-slot indexing of the residual, and the fact that noise is structural rather than removable. Bringing that word back down (`T_E`) produces a claim that sounds like an answer but cannot fire any of the six refuters. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. ## CONCLUSION

The three mechanisms are not isomers of one molecule. They are a union spanning a unary operator, a set of structures, and a symmetric bond, which is the notational reason a single lever cannot clear the residual. Their dependency order is refuse, then state the objective, then maintain, then select. The population loop is the same rule at the next stratum. One biology analogue, degeneracy, is licensed by Fig 13 :121 and corroborates the route-to-substance shape.

**What would reverse this:** all three shapes expressible in one bond type; ¬ admitted as a bond under the six tests; a dependency ordering in which maintenance can succeed while perception is broken; or a measurement showing that within-room consensus, not selection between rooms, is what keeps a population reliable.
