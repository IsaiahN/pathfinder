## What the wider literature adds, and what it does not

- **Hopfield** is a molecular-kinetics frame. It corroborates the refusal shape (F3) but does not derive it from the figures. The energy cost of the delay step is a real physical price, which the figures also price in actions rather than bits (Fig 12 :126 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add").
- **Elowitz** is a measurement frame. It establishes that noise is real and that low copy number limits precision, which corroborates F1. It does not tell us the shape of a mechanism.
- **Barkai and Leibler** is a control-and-network frame. Robust adaptation from connectivity rather than fine tuning corroborates F5. Kitano 2004 cites this paper, so those two are partly one evidence pool and are not counted as two independent witnesses.
- **Edelman and Gally** is an evolutionary frame. Degeneracy versus identical redundancy corroborates F4 and F6 from a field that does not use the Tether vocabulary.
- **Hartl and colleagues** is a protein-homeostasis frame. Constant chaperone surveillance corroborates F2 as a maintenance process.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict. If one did, the rule is to return to the domain census, not to flip the verdict.
