# Expert verification packet: the Tether on reliable cellular communication

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cell biologist or biophysicist can check. Mode, shapes and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the union structure, `specified` on the three mechanism shapes. Not `grounded`.** No biological measurement is claimed to settle any sub-claim. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Reliability under molecular noise and component turnover is a union of three conditions, not one problem. Noise is the discarded detail of a lossy cross between scales and therefore cannot be engineered to zero; the residual has to be measured per component and the round-trip gap tracked. Component turnover is decay of the signalling channel while the ground it reports on does not decay, so keeping contact is active work. Near-match molecules must be refused by a unary operation, not merely out-competed by stronger binding, and identical duplicate paths share the same error, so reliability needs structurally different paths that triangulate one ground. At the population stratum the same loop appears as variation inside and selection between. These are shapes. Molecular instances such as kinetic proofreading, chaperone surveillance, and feedback control are bindings from outside the framework and are not claimed as derivations.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Biophysics facing: refusal is a required shape

- **Statement:** reliable discrimination against a near-match requires an operation that refuses a held wrong candidate, not just a stronger first binding event.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 "A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Also Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." The Operators table :58 states: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."
- **What to check:** does high-fidelity recognition in the system you know use an energy-dissipating delay or rejection step, and does blocking it raise the error rate to the equilibrium binding limit? Hopfield 1974 (opened 2026-11-23) is the reference instance, not a figure result.
- **Refuter:** the observed error rate is reached at equilibrium with no delay or rejection step. If that is shown, C1 is withdrawn.

### C2: Cell biology facing: turnover is channel decay and needs maintenance

- **Statement:** the signal's meaning does not decay when a receptor is replaced; the channel that carries it does. Maintaining contact is work, not a property of a part.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Also Fig 10 :29 "keep the anchor reachable." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." and :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** across a full turnover interval, what actually preserves the response? Replacement, chaperone surveillance, feedback, or a substitutable functional group? Hartl, Bracher and Hayer-Hartl 2011 (opened 2026-11-23) reports that "Because protein molecules are highly dynamic, constant chaperone surveillance is required to ensure protein homeostasis (proteostasis)."
- **Refuter:** exact signal-to-response contact persists across full component replacement with no maintenance process of any kind. If shown, C2 is withdrawn.

### C3: Networks facing: independent difference, not averaged duplication

- **Statement:** two identical noisy copies share the same error; structurally different paths ranging against one ground provide a surplus. Averaging copies is the failure mode.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** in the pathway you know, is reliability carried by duplicate copies of one module or by structurally different inputs, scaffold arrangements, or feedback motifs? Edelman and Gally 2001 (opened 2026-11-23) distinguish "degeneracy, which involves structurally different elements" from "redundancy, which occurs when the same function is performed by identical elements."
- **Refuter:** reliability comes from averaging identical correlated copies and degenerate paths contribute nothing to the error rate. If shown, C3 is withdrawn.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating intervention, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The six refuters in `01` §4 are written so that each can fire. The three sharpest: block the delay step in a high-fidelity recognition reaction; hold the signal fixed and replace the whole molecular complement; compare duplicated identical paths against structurally different paths under the same noise load.
