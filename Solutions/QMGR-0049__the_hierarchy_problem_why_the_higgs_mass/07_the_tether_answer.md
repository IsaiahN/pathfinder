# The Tether's answer on the hierarchy problem

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive shape, `general` on the structural claim. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Not "why is one number small" but "why can the Standard Model not calculate the weak scale, and why is the parameter that sets it quadratically sensitive to the cutoff?" Wikipedia: "The problem cannot even be formulated in the strict context of the Standard Model, for the Higgs mass cannot be calculated." Strassler: "This is incorrect. The problem is with how big the non-zero Higgs field is." The residual bundles three conditions: radiative instability, a 10¹⁷ scale ordering, and the absence of an in-frame derivation. It is a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **The weak scale is out of reach inside the Standard Model closure.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." The mass is an input, not a derived term. This is `specified` for this frame, not a proof that no frame could ever derive it (Fig 9 :44).
2. **SEARCHED comes first, and it is answered.** The term is not in the closure unfound; the world source says it cannot be calculated. The route therefore goes OUTWARD to import, with INWARD (a better instrument) held as the opposed track (Formula step 7).
3. **A resolution has the shape of an import, not a bigger calculation.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The import must explain the gap (shadow test), arrive with provenance (source gate), be debited against independence, and be settled by an instrument. It may need a new axis of representation, not merely a new term.
4. **The framework does not name the import.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Supersymmetry, extra dimensions, compositeness and anthropic selection are candidate bindings from the physics frame, not derivations. Their identity is a **gap** after re-census, which is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
5. **A second branch is live.** The residual has persisted and not trended down (Fig 5 :59). Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". The candidate for refusal is naturalness as a demand on theory choice. This does not assert that nature is unnatural. It records that the prior is refusable and attaches a test to it.
6. **Naturalness is a filter, never a witness.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Calling the hierarchy a problem nature must solve converts the filter into a verdict. The verdict belongs to the ground.
7. **The problem is a union and must be unbundled.** Fig 9 :55. Radiative instability, scale ordering and non-derivability are overlapping conditions; one lever does not clear all three.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a solution to the hierarchy problem. No mechanism is named and no scale is predicted.
- Not "naturalness is false". The refusal branch is live, with a falsifier, not settled.
- Not "supersymmetry is wrong" or "extra dimensions are right". All candidate bindings are withheld pending provenance and ground settlement.
- Not `grounded`. No collider result, no precision measurement and no expert verdict was used as an input.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess at compression axes; another sensorium's set need not be these six.

## 4. What would settle it, and the standing tests

A ground reading, not further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Mechanism test:** does any imported primitive remove the quadratic sensitivity at the scale where the theory becomes strongly coupled, and does an instrument see it? A null result at accessible scales that is not a bound on the mechanism does not settle anything (Strassler: "the jury is out").
2. **Operand-type test (from `06` Q2):** is the renormalised mass-squared parameter a well-defined quantity comparable to M_Pl at the cutoff order? A derivation either way resolves the one live strain.
3. **Refusal test:** does the residual start to trend down under new data? If yes, the refusal branch weakens and the import branch strengthens. If the prior is instead settled by an instrument, the filter/verdict distinction at F7 changes.
4. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** derive the weak scale from SM atoms with no import and no fine-tuned input; accept an import with no source gate or shadow test; show agreement among models can settle the question.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The import is owed; its name is not available. "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. Treating a candidate as settled because it is aesthetically satisfying is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not solve the hierarchy problem. It restates it: the Standard Model cannot calculate the weak scale, and the mass-squared parameter that sets it is quadratically sensitive to the highest scale the theory knows. Because a frame cannot reach outside its own closure by more composition, a resolution has the shape of an import from a different frame, one that explains the gap, arrives with provenance, and is settled by an instrument. The framework refuses to name that import: supersymmetry, extra dimensions and compositeness are candidates from physics, not outputs of the derivation. A second branch stays open: the residual has persisted without trending down, so naturalness itself may be a term owed a refusal rather than another theory to be minted. What is offered is the shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name stays open until the ground settles it.
