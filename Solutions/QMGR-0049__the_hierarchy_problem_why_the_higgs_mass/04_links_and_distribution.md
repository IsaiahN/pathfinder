## Two things in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The question is misstated in the common form.** Strassler's correction ("This is incorrect. The problem is with how big the non-zero Higgs field is") and Wikipedia's "cannot even be formulated in the strict context of the Standard Model" together change the residual from "a small number is weird" to "a parameter is not derivable and is radiatively unstable". This is exactly the difference between a hard search and a reachability failure in the figures. Cite it when explaining why the import verdict is not just a rhetorical preference.
2. **The null results are not yet a verdict.** Strassler's 2024 update ("the jury is out") and Wikipedia's "no scientific consensus" are the world-level reason the REFUTED branch at `01` F6 is *live*, not *settled*. Do not present the persistence of the residual as proof that naturalness is wrong. It is a candidate for refusal with a refuter attached.

## Wider literature, relevant but not read as authority

The Wikipedia entry points to primary work that a reader may want to check before engaging with the shape claim: Arkani-Hamed, Dimopoulos and Dvali (1998) on large extra dimensions; Barbieri and Giudice (1988) on upper bounds on supersymmetric particle masses; Meissner and Nicolai (2007) on conformal symmetry and the Standard Model; and the Bayesian-naturalness papers by Fowlie and others (2014 to 2016). These are listed so a reader can locate the field's own record. They were not opened for this derivation and are therefore **not** in `10_source_ledger.csv`, in keeping with the rule never to cite what was not read.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. But each user needs a ground per problem and must write down what would refute their result before running it.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The checkable form is `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. If a reader refutes it, the refutation goes into `11_audit.md` with provenance and is re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
