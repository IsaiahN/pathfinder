## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms before any figure line is chosen

**Question verbatim:** "The hierarchy problem: why the Higgs mass is so much smaller than the Planck mass"

**Correction to the question (recorded, not silently applied).** The wording is a common but imprecise shorthand. Matt Strassler, *The Hierarchy Problem* (2011, revised 2024): "By the way, you will often read the hierarchy problem stated as a problem with the Higgs particle mass. This is incorrect. The problem is with how big the non-zero Higgs field is." For experts he adds that quantum mechanics corrects "not the Higgs particle mass but the Higgs mass-squared parameter", making the field value zero or immense. Wikipedia states: "The problem cannot even be formulated in the strict context of the Standard Model, for the Higgs mass cannot be calculated." So the residual is not a measured mass being anomalously small in a theory that predicts it. It is that the theory cannot predict it, and the parameter that sets the weak scale is radiatively unstable against the highest scale the theory knows.

**R, in effect terms.** A frame (the Standard Model as an effective field theory up to a cutoff Λ, with gravity entering at the Planck mass M_Pl) predicts that the Higgs mass-squared parameter receives corrections of order Λ². When Λ is taken near M_Pl, the natural value of the weak scale is near M_Pl. An instrument returns a weak scale near 10² GeV, roughly 10¹⁷ times smaller. R = |predicted natural scale, observed scale|. It is a **persistent, non-trending residual**: decades of experiment have not reduced the gap, and no new stabilising physics has appeared at the scales where it was expected.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**
- Arity: three coupled conditions, not one. (a) The mass-squared parameter is quadratically sensitive to the cutoff. (b) The weak scale and the Planck scale are wildly ordered. (c) The Standard Model cannot calculate the mass, so there is no in-frame derivation to compare with. This is a **union compound**, not a single hard question (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones").
- Symmetry: the governing relation is a threshold comparison (⋛), not a symmetric join. The two operands are ordered, "more and less can be said" (Fig 13 :20).
- Scale: first order inside the effective theory (radiative corrections to one parameter), second order at the contact between the electroweak frame and the quantum gravity frame.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run.** Per Fig 5 :65: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Bound answer shape A1: *an imported primitive, from a frame whose closure differs from the Standard Model's, that removes the quadratic sensitivity of the mass-squared parameter, arrives with provenance, explains an observed gap, and is settled by an instrument.* Bound answer shape A2: *a refusal of the naturalness prior itself, if the residual is really that a reigning regularity is false rather than that a term is missing.* Both are ordinary terms here and each accrues a residual below.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The Standard Model closure cannot reach the weak scale; this is a reachability failure, not a hard search (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
Fig 9 :6: "the verdict: is φ* in here at all?"
Fig 9 :18: "φ* out of reach: nothing inside can prove that"

The Standard Model's closure contains the Higgs field, its mass-squared parameter, the counterterm and the radiative corrections. It can compose corrections to any order. What it cannot compose is a derivation of the small parameter from its own atoms, because the parameter is an input. Wikipedia (2026): "The problem cannot even be formulated in the strict context of the Standard Model, for the Higgs mass cannot be calculated." That is not a statement about budget. It is a statement about the closure.

So the verdict for D1 is **out of reach from inside**, not "unfound yet". The triangulating pair is Fig 6 (closure geometry) and Fig 8 (the idempotence theorem), with Fig 9 supplying the question form. Low overlap between figures, so `triangulated`. World corroboration is a second, independent field (particle physics), but it is secondary and cannot overrule the figures.

**Refuter:** if the weak scale can be derived by composing Standard Model atoms, with no imported primitive and no fine-tuned input, F1 is refuted. A merely better fit to the measured mass does not refute it; a derivation does.

### F2: SEARCHED comes first; underivable and unreachable-at-budget issue different instructions (triangulated)

Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first because it is cheapest and the other two assume its answer. Here the world source answers it: the mass "cannot be calculated" in the SM, so the term is not in the closure waiting to be found. That points OUTWARD (import), not to a bigger perturbative computation. But Fig 9 :44 forbids turning this into a proof that no frame could ever derive it. We record **underivable from this frame**, not **unreachable by any frame**.

**Refuter:** show that the small parameter is in the SM closure and was merely not computed. Then the remedy is more search, and importing a new atom would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :42: "already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom").

### F3: The comparison of two scales is real, but the operand is strained; this strain governs the question's miswording (single-source, named)

Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"
Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."
Operators table :51 (not a Fig N line): "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"

The hierarchy problem is a ⋛ threshold comparison: M_Pl ⋛ m_weak. The structure doing the ordering is the Spectrum. So far clean. The strain is the operand. The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities." The Planck mass is a derived scale from G, ħ, c. The "Higgs mass" in the question is not a stable quantity at this order: it is a bare parameter plus a quadratically divergent correction, and what is physically measured is the electroweak vacuum expectation value. Strassler: "This is incorrect. The problem is with how big the non-zero Higgs field is." So we are comparing a scheme-dependent parameter to a derived scale, not two like quantities.

Per the strain rule, a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so we re-census: Fig 13 :20 governs the **ordering** (which is larger), which is sound. The type question about the operand is a separate matter and remains open. It is recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2 and in `11_audit.md` as a live strain. It is not hidden.

**Refuter:** a derivation showing that the renormalised mass-squared parameter is a well-defined quantity at the cutoff scale, comparable to M_Pl at that order, removes the strain and strengthens the comparison framing. A derivation showing it is not removes the comparison framing but leaves F1 standing.

### F4: The problem is a union compound; unbundle before searching (single-source for the figure rule, corroborated by the essays)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

Isaiah N, *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (2026): "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The residual bundles radiative instability (a technical question about one parameter), a numerical ratio (an ordering question about two scales), and the absence of a calculable mass (a closure question about one frame). A single lever that removes the quadratic divergence, say, leaves the ordering question standing. After unbundling, D1 is the loop that governs the other two. This is why the audit runs D1 first.

**Refuter:** show that one lever closes all three conditions simultaneously, so the union reading is unnecessary. Until then, verdicts stay separate and are never averaged.

So the shape owed is: an import that (i) is reachable by a frame with a different closure, (ii) passes a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains an observed gap, Fig 8 :9-13), (iii) is debited against the independence of the proposing frames, and (iv) is settled by a ground reading, not by agreement. The specific names, supersymmetry, large extra dimensions, compositeness, relaxion, anthropic selection, are bindings from the physics frame onto this shape. Isaiah N, *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026) sharpens the shape: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A resolution may require a new axis of representation (a new symmetry structure or a new spatial dimension), not merely a new field written in the same 4D effective-theory vocabulary. This is a shape claim, `general`, not a prediction of which axis.

**Refuter:** produce a candidate import with no provenance, no shadow test, and no ground settlement, and show that it is being treated as an answer. That would be a recording passed off as a generator (Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion") and would refute the claim that the gates are being applied.

### F6: The REFUTED branch is live: the naturalness prior may owe a refusal, not another mint (single-source for the mechanism, world-corroborated)

Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2: "REFUTED: a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation"

The residual has persisted and not trended down. Strassler (2024 update): "As of 2024, it is no longer early days... Many classes of possible new phenomena, including many of those that could have addressed the hierarchy puzzle, would have turned up by now. Others would not have, so the jury is out." Wikipedia: "no evidence has been found so far for supersymmetry." By Fig 5 :59, the term to point a refusal at is the one with the persistent flat residual. The candidate for refusal is **naturalness as a demand**, not the Higgs field value. This does not say naturalness is false. It says the frame holds it as a regularity, and the residual's content may be that the regularity is wrong, which is the fifth kind the four bins cannot sort.

A refusal is a negation, not a bond: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate" (Operators table :58). The correct output, if this branch fires, is to remove the standing of "naturalness must be explained" as a constraint on theory choice, not to mint another theory to satisfy it.

**Refuter:** a ground measurement that finds a stabilising mechanism at accessible scales, so the residual begins to trend down. Then the refusal branch is weakened and the import branch is strengthened. This was written before the conclusion.

### F7: Naturalness has been used as a verdict; the figures permit it only as a filter (single-source)

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
Seat Map :281: "An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."

Naturalness is a heuristic for which theories to try first, a budget filter. It is not a witness for the verdict about nature. Calling the hierarchy a "problem" that nature must solve converts the filter into a verdict, the output-layer failure genus the Seat Map names. The verdict belongs to the ground: an instrument, an experiment, a measurement. This is the same failure mode the exemplar found in `QMGR-0037` at its F4, and it is named here rather than left implicit.

**Refuter:** show a derivation in which naturalness is settled by an instrument rather than assumed by a community. Then it is no longer merely a filter, and F7 is refuted.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The weak scale is derived by composing Standard Model atoms, with no imported primitive and no fine-tuned input.
2. A proposed import passes no source gate and no shadow test yet is accepted as an answer.
3. A ground measurement finds a stabilising mechanism at accessible scales, so the residual trends down (this would weaken the refusal branch).
4. A derivation shows that the scheme-dependent mass-squared parameter cannot be compared to M_Pl at the relevant order (this removes the comparison framing, not the reachability claim).
5. Naturalness is settled by an instrument rather than assumed by a community (this removes F7).

Until at least one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
