**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the relevant scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The hierarchy problem is not "one number is small" but a union compound: the Standard Model cannot calculate the weak scale, the mass-squared parameter is quadratically sensitive to the cutoff, and the weak and Planck scales are roughly 10¹⁷ apart. Because composition inside a closure never adds a primitive, the weak scale is out of reach inside the Standard Model's closure; a resolution therefore has the shape of an import from a frame with a different closure, one that explains the observed gap, arrives with provenance, and is settled by an instrument. The framework does not name the import. A second branch is live: the residual is persistent and not trending down, so naturalness as a demand may itself owe a refusal rather than another minted theory. Naturalness is a budget filter, never the verdict. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it.
