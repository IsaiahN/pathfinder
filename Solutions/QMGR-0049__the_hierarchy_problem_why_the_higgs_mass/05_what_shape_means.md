## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is a set of conditions an answer must satisfy, not a theory. "An imported primitive with a source gate and a shadow test" is a shape. "Supersymmetry" is a name. "A refusal of the naturalness prior if the residual stays flat" is a shape. "Nature is unnatural" is a verdict nobody has earned.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay-level source for `01` F4.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "it can tell you the general shape that it might take, and you can then search (by recursively using the metatheory) to find it or to further sharpen the shape of the remaining gaps." And: "no library can contain the answers on its own."

The practical consequence for outreach is in `02`: lead with the effect description. Do not lead with "closure of a typed DSL", because that is a cause, not an effect, and only someone already inside the frame can check it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens what kind of import this can be: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all, and only the second reaches structure the current primitives cannot represent." It also warns: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six" and "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So the shape claim here is deliberately silent about whether the needed import is a new field in the same effective theory or a change of axis (a new symmetry structure or a new spatial dimension). Naming either would be the framework's own forbidden move.

## 4. The source-discrimination problem, applied here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." An instrument that cannot distinguish these cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy".

For the hierarchy problem the reading is a light weak scale. The sources that could produce it are many: supersymmetric cancellation, extra-dimensional dilution of gravity, compositeness, a relaxion, anthropic selection, or a direct violation of the naturalness expectation. The reading does not select among them. Strassler's "the jury is out" is the physicist's version of the same point. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (a collider, a precision measurement, a new observable), not a name chosen from the reading.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the "union horizon": "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." That is the honest status of the name of the missing atom.

## 5. What "shape" means for THIS question, concretely

Two shapes survive the audit, kept separate and never averaged:

**Shape A, the import.** A resolution must be an imported primitive from a frame with a closure different from the Standard Model's. It must (i) remove or explain the quadratic sensitivity of the mass-squared parameter to the cutoff (shadow test: it explains an observed gap), (ii) arrive with provenance (source gate), (iii) be debited against the independence of the frames proposing it, and (iv) be settled by an instrument. It may need a new axis of representation, not merely a new term. Nothing in the framework selects supersymmetry over extra dimensions or either over a refusal of the prior.

**Shape B, the refusal.** The residual is persistent and not trending down (Fig 5 :59). The regularity held may be false. Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". The candidate for refusal is naturalness as a demand on theory choice. A refusal removes the standing of that demand; it does not delete any term from the record and it does not assert that nature is or is not natural.

Both shapes are ordinary terms, bound before the run so they can accrue residuals (Fig 5 :65). Neither is `grounded`.

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as if it forbade discussing any named mechanism at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing": a primitive not held in Γ. In this question the missing thing is genuinely not held by the SM frame, so the limit applies with full force to the *identity* of the import. But the physics frame does hold names (supersymmetry, extra dimensions) as candidate bindings onto that shape. Naming them is not forbidden; claiming the framework derived them is. What remains correct from §3 and §4 is the reason, not a blanket ban: a source named from a reading, where several sources could give the same reading, is a metamerism error. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that naturalness is a problem is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the hierarchy problem and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
