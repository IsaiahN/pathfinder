## 2. What the wider literature adds, and what it does not decide

1. **The channel is a real, measurable object.** Shannon's framework separates source, transmitter,
   channel, receiver and destination, and defines channel capacity. This corroborates Domain 1 at low
   overlap: speed and reach are properties of the channel, not of the claim. It does not decide the
   figure verdict.
2. **Repetition in a closed system raises felt credibility without adding evidence.** The echo chamber
   and Woozle effect sources describe the mechanism in social terms: repetition, citation accretion,
   and a shift from qualified to absolute language. This is the world-facing version of Fig 7 :13.
   The sources disagree about how widespread the effect is, which is an honest limit on the empirical
   claim but does not change the figure verdict.
3. **Reach is not the same as independent contact.** The filter bubble and misinformation sources
   record that algorithms select content and that more sources can be available while independence
   falls. This is the empirical counterpart of Fig 2 :40. One source records the opposite finding,
   that some users are exposed to more diverse sources online. That conflict sends the derivation
   back to the census, where Domain 3 already separates reach from independence; it does not flip the
   verdict.
4. **History gives a contact case, not a credibility case.** The printing press source reports faster
   circulation, wider geographical range, and the formation of communities with shared methods. It
   also reports contemporary complaints about accuracy. That is exactly the distinction F1 and F5
   draw: a technology changes contact and throughput; whether a claim is settled is a separate question.
5. **Framework-author essays are high overlap.** The two Substack essays agree with the figures
   because they come from the same author and evidence pool. Their agreement is one witness, not two.
   This is declared in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the shape claim and
  its refuter. Send links only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :45:** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what they
  would expect to see if the claim were true, before showing them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence
  pool."* Do not pool later agreement as corroboration, and do not select recipients for likely
  agreement.
- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator;
  giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Fig 9 :60:** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand
  you a verdict."* A journal's prestige is a filter on where to send, never a reason to believe the
  claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the refuter; (2) the question "what would you
count as a failure?"; (3) record the answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures,
not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the three domain verdicts and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a
failure, not an endorsement. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the three source links (the figures text
local extract, the canonical figures README, and the metalaws note), plus the two framework essays
clearly labelled as the author's own writing. Their agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states
their own mode before running it. Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own
question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. Nothing in this folder is a ground
reading; the package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.
It cannot settle the question it reports on.
