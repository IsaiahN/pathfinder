## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have
to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do
know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And the stopping
rule: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and
descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the mechanism. It
constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> A change in a communication technology alters the channel and the distribution of contacts. It does
> not alter the ground. Credibility that accrues inside a shared closure is a frame-internal signal,
> not a witness. The only way the technology bears on which claim is settled is by changing which
> frames contact the claim, or by cheapening independent checking.

That is a shape. "Social media makes claims less trustworthy" is a name, and a verdict, and is not
derived here.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary closest to the effect, then *"With each new term
  gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For this
  question the starting vocabulary is speed, reach, credibility, channel, ground, contact and
  independence.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* and Fig 6
  :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in
  effect terms."* So "credibility" is described as *the quantity a frame produces about a claim*, not
  as "trust" or "authority", which import a theory of mind.
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same
  way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing."* The question itself is the anchor:
  if the three-effect description is not answered, the search continues.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several
  problems masquerading as one?"* This licenses the union reading in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means a controlled transmission study, a
   measurement of independence before and after, and a ground check of the claim's standing.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. A historical case, such as the printing press and the formation of
   astronomical communities, is such an import: it is admitted with its provenance, not treated as a
   derivation from the figures.

The six independent world sources opened this session are of the second kind. They corroborate the
shape at low overlap but do not license naming a particular technology as the cause.

## 4. Why "credibility" is the forbidden self-score

Fig 1 :26-27: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"* and *"They are frame-internal. A frame
cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* This is the central move. A community that
credits a claim because it has been widely transmitted is using a quantity it produced to grade its
own output. That is the named failure, not a matter of degree.

The opened *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* sharpens the mechanism: *"Prestige must never be allowed
to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to
be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be
blind to prestige."* It names the exact illusion behind this question: *"the prestige loop looks like
agreement, and agreement feels like truth."* Reach buys a claim a budget to be tried. It does not pay
for the claim.

This is why the shape refuses to treat credibility as evidence, while still admitting that credibility
is a real social quantity. Fig 1 disqualifies it as a verdict, not as a fact about people.

## 5. Contact is the load-bearing term

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose
closure differs."*

*The Library of Babel* adds a practical constraint on the multi-frame case: *"Convergence towards the
answer is faster with multiple helpers, but communication between them is paramount and would have to
be virtually 1:1 to be effective."* That is a shape for the legitimate use of a communication
technology: not broadcast reach, but near one-to-one contact between independent searchers. It is a
corroboration from a framework-author essay, high overlap, not an independent witness.

## 6. Two consequences for the public-facing work

**Prestige is not action.** The opened essay gives the rule; Fig 2 :42 prices the repair: *"Buy
independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The legitimate ask in outreach is a budget to
try the claim, not an endorsement, and a request for a refutation rather than a list of believers.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The opened essay states: *"the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM
output is described in the same essay as simulacra. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this document
are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests
of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to an earlier framing

An earlier draft of this question was framed as "technology changes what is credible, therefore
technology changes what is true." That conflation is retracted. The figures separate the channel from
the ground (Fig 10 :24), and the essays separate prestige from action. What technology changes is the
channel and the contact distribution; what it can fund is a trial; what settles the claim is the
ground. The retracted wording is kept here so the correction is visible.
