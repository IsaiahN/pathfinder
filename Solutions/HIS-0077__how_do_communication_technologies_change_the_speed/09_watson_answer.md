## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says happens when a communication technology enters a field
of competing claims. Option A: treat speed, reach and credibility as three ways claims become better
supported, and rank technologies by how much credibility they produce. Option B: treat speed and reach
as channel properties, credibility as a frame-internal signal the channel amplifies, and contact as the
only ground-bearing route. What each leads to: A ends in a verdict made from a quantity a frame
produces about itself, disqualified by Fig 1 :26-27 and Fig 2 :40; B ends in a checkable shape, six
pre-registered refuters, and a named instrument for the one case that would change the verdict. The
figures license B. No technology is named, ranked or blamed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or
union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The observed delta when a technology enters: claims move faster, reach more
  actors and places, and credibility shifts. Arity: three effects, so a **union compound**, not one
  hard question (Fig 9 :5: split rather than search). Symmetry: speed and reach are symmetric under a
  change of message content; credibility is a directed relation between a claim and a frame.
  Scale: first order inside one frame (arrival and credit), second order across frames (many
  credit the same claim), third order at the ground (settled or not).
- **Domain 1, the transmission channel.** Actors: emitter, signal, medium, receiver. Currency:
  throughput and coverage. Anchor: channel fidelity, a measurable engineering quantity that does not
  change because a claim is popular. A real domain.
- **Domain 2, the credit frame.** Actors: competing claims and the frames that rank them. Currency:
  credibility accrued and agreement. Anchor: the ground, which does not update on agreement. A real
  domain whose own currency is disqualified as its own verdict (Fig 1 :27).
- **Domain 3, the habitat of receivers.** Actors: audiences, institutions, gatekeepers, peer frames.
  Currency: attention, trust and independence between frames. Anchor: the ground for truth; human
  judgment for the separate question whether an answer is good for a person (Fig 2 :36-37). A real
  domain: contact is nameable and can be enumerated.
- **Union, not partition.** A lever on speed clears the speed region and leaves the credibility
  intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-56). Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency.** Transmission, then vocabulary, then a named objective and settlement,
  then credit, then cross-frame comparison (Fig 3 :20-21). A credibility reading taken before
  settlement is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

Strain test applied: the everyday claim that wide belief is good support strains against Fig 2 :31-32
and Fig 7 :13. Re-census resolves it: those figures govern the verdict question; "wide belief" is a
social-credibility quantity. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. The reading is
recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 rather than erased.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any ranking of named technologies does not. A communication technology changes
the channel (speed and reach) and the volume of the frame-internal credibility signal. It does not
change the ground. Credibility produced inside a shared closure compounds agreement without adding
evidence, so it is inadmissible as a witness for the claim it credits. The only way such a technology
bears on a settled claim is by changing contact with frames whose evidence differs, or by making
independent checking cheaper. Speed, reach and credibility are a union and must be unbundled; speed
and reach are isomers of one transmission event, and credibility is second order. This is a
`specified`, ungrounded result. No figure line governs the comparative historical question, so that
question ends in `gap` here rather than being answered by preference.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the
  conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is
  spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict about a named technology instead
  of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
  The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The channel conclusion is separable from the contact conclusion; a revised account
  of which technologies change independence can be substituted without touching the ground claim or
  withdrawing any primitive. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled medium-only experiment showing a
  settled answer moves; a credibility score that predicts settlement after independence and ground
  contact are controlled for; retransmissions that improve accuracy with independence fixed; reach
  that moves a settled verdict with independence unchanged; or one mechanism that moves all three
  effects with no residual. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
