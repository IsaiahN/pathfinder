# Structure and order: are speed and reach one effect, and in what order do the loops run?

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground
reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

The audit left two structural questions open. (1) Speed and reach are both produced by the same
technology. Are they one term under two names, or different terms whose notation hides the
difference? (2) The three effects and the ground form several loops. In what order do they run, and
which can be skipped?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held for this derivation: a signal, a medium, a time, a receiver set, a claim, a frame, a ground.
Bonds held: the seven plus negation. No new primitive is available without import. The three effects
are a union (Fig 9 :54-56), so the derivation must unbundle rather than average.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Speed and reach are different terms, not two names for one

- Fig 13 :76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**
- Speed is a rate: a difference bonded to **time**. Reach is an extent: an ordering over a receiver
  set bonded to **space or network**. Same underlying signal and medium, different state bonded, so
  different term under the figure's own rule.
- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- So speed and reach are **isomers** of the transmission event: identical ingredients (signal, medium,
  receivers), different bond (time versus space/network), decisive difference in behaviour. A
  technology can double speed without doubling reach, and vice versa. That is why treating "faster and
  wider" as one effect is a notation error, not a simplification.
- Fig 12 :95-96: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**

### Q2 - Credibility is second order, not a third isomer

- Credibility is not a property of the signal. It is a bond between a claim and a frame, and it is
  built on the first-order event of the claim arriving.
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**
- A claim that has been transmitted (first order) can be credited (second order). A claim that has been
  credited by a frame can only then be compared across frames (third order). Credibility is not an
  isomer of speed or reach; it sits at the next level and needs a different bond, comparison, not rate
  or extent.
- Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."** A claim's
  standing relative to the ground is a comparison; reach is a sequence of handoffs; neither can be
  converted into the other by subtraction.

### Q3 - Order by dependency

- Fig 3 :20-21: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
- The order the figures require:
  1. **Transmission (perception).** Does a signal arrive and is it distinguished from what was there
     before? Fig 3 :4-5.
  2. **Vocabulary.** Do receiving frames have terms for the claim? Fig 3 :6-7. Without terms, a wider
     reach delivers noise.
  3. **Objective / settlement.** What would count as progress here, in the frame's own terms? Fig 3
     :9-10. This is where the ground is named.
  4. **Credit.** Only after 1 to 3 can a frame assign credibility without self-scoring.
  5. **Cross-frame comparison.** Fig 12 :104; only settled molecules become operands.
- A credibility reading taken at step 4 when step 3 has not run is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23:
  *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."*). This is the
  structural reason "more reach, therefore more support" fails before any empirical check.

### Q4 - A chain of retransmissions cannot close into the ground

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Reach is a chain: one handoff after another. It cannot close on itself into a verdict. To get from
  repetition to settlement you need a different bond, a comparison against the ground, and that is a
  seat's office, not a property of more handoffs.
- Fig 7 :40: **"The room is summoned by the residual rather than composed."** The next check is picked
  by the residual the last one surfaced, not by how many people have already repeated the claim.
- This is the isomer distinction again at a higher level: the same messages can be arranged as a chain
  (reach) or as a cycle with a ground comparator (checking). Same atoms, different bond, different
  substance.

### Q5 - What the union residue looks like after unbundling

- Fig 9 :57-59: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**
- After the speed lever and the reach lever are applied, what remains is the intersection: a claim that
  has travelled fast and wide into frames that already share its evidence pool. That residue is not a
  harder speed problem or a harder reach problem. It is a credibility compound with its own loop and
  its own ground.
- Fig 9 :59: **"Which is the second-order value of a big lever, and usually the larger one: not that the pile is smaller, but that the residue is finally describable."**

## FALSIFIER, WRITTEN BEFORE THE RUN

If any of the following holds, this supporting derivation is refuted and the corresponding claim is
withdrawn:

1. A technology is found for which doubling speed necessarily doubles reach through one mechanism,
   with no residual and no separate bond. That collapses the isomer distinction in Q1.
2. Credibility is shown to be first order, a direct property of a signal rather than a bond between a
   claim and a frame. That breaks Q2 and the dependency order in Q3.
3. A chain of retransmissions is shown to close into a ground verdict without a comparator, contrary
   to Fig 12 :121. That breaks Q4.
4. After unbundling speed and reach, the credibility residue is shown to be a harder case of one of
   them rather than a categorically different compound. That breaks Fig 9 :57-59 and the union reading.

## CONCLUSION

Speed and reach are **isomers** of the transmission event: same atoms, different bond, decisive
behavioural difference. Credibility is **second order**, a bond between a claim and a frame that can
only be assigned after transmission, vocabulary and a named objective. The loops run in dependency
order, and a chain of retransmissions cannot close into a ground verdict. The residue after unbundling
is a specific credibility compound, not a mixture. This derivation rescues the structural content of
the question without naming a technology or asserting a measurement.
