# The Tether's answer on communication technologies and competing claims

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the three domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a
shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

When a communication technology enters a field of competing claims, three effects are observed:
claims move faster, they reach more actors, and the credibility assigned to them shifts. The question
asks what the technology changes. The residual is whether each effect is a change in the **channel** or
a change in the **ground**, and what would settle each. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-56), not one
hard question.

## 2. The shape, with verdicts kept separate

**Domain 1, the channel. Verdict: technology changes speed and reach. `general`, triangulated.**

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles
the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* A communication
technology is a channel instrument. Speed (how fast a signal moves) and reach (how many receivers it
reaches) are channel quantities, measurable in the channel's own terms. They are isomers of one
transmission event, bonded differently to time and to space or network (Fig 13 :76;
`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1). The ground, which settles which claim holds, does not move with them.

**Domain 2, credit. Verdict: credibility is amplified but disqualified as evidence. `general`,
triangulated.**

Fig 1 :26-27: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued... They are frame-internal. A frame cannot
score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement
compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection
between rooms is where the ground votes."* A technology changes credibility in the sense that it
produces more of the frame-internal signal. It does not thereby produce evidence. More retransmission
inside a shared closure is motion, not progress. This verdict is not averaged with Domain 1.

**Domain 3, the habitat. Verdict: the only ground-bearing effect is a change of contact. `specified`,
single-source with low-overlap corroboration.**

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose
closure differs."* Fig 6 :50: *"Distribute the search, and keep the searchers independent."* A
technology can matter to which claim is settled, but only by changing which frames contact the claim,
by making independent checking cheaper, or by carrying a provenanced import (Fig 8 :50-52). Broadcast
reach into a closed pool is the opposite case: Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless
when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

**The union.** The three verdicts are not one verdict with three faces. A lever on speed does not
clear the credibility intersection (Fig 9 :55-56). After unbundling, the residue is a specific
compound: a claim that travelled fast and wide into frames that already share its evidence pool. That
residue needs its own loop and its own ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named technology (print, telegraph, radio, television, internet, social media) is good
  or bad. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the
  answer itself"*).
- Not that widespread belief makes a claim true, or that credibility is worthless. Credibility is a
  real social quantity; it is disqualified as a verdict, not as a fact about people.
- Not that faster transmission is harmful. A contact change can be beneficial; the verdict is
  conditional on independence, not on speed.
- Not that the three effects can be averaged into a single "technology makes claims more trustworthy"
  score. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Not a derivation of any historical outcome. The printing press and the echo-chamber studies are
  imports with provenance, not results produced by the figures.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The needed readings are:

1. **Channel-only test.** Change the medium while holding the claim and the receiving frames fixed.
   Does the ground's answer to an already-settled question move? If it does, F1 falls.
2. **Credibility test.** Regress ground settlement on credibility after controlling for independence
   and ground contact. Does credibility still predict above chance? If it does, F2 falls.
3. **Repetition test.** Increase retransmissions of an unchanged claim with independence fixed. Does
   verdict accuracy improve? If it does, F3 falls.
4. **Contact test.** Measure the independence distribution of receivers before and after the
   technology. Reach that leaves independence unchanged cannot bear on the ground; reach that raises
   independence can. This is the discriminating instrument for Domain 3.
5. **Union test.** Look for one mechanism that moves speed, reach and credibility with no residual in
   any intersection. If found, the union reading and Fig 9 :57-59 fall.

The full pre-registered list, written before the conclusion, is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`
section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instruments that would discriminate channel change from contact
change are named but not run here. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of
absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the lineage is recorded
so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity. Prestige, reach and agreement are not
substitutes for the instrument: the opened essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states that
*"Prestige can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank printing, radio or social media, and it does not claim that new media make
> claims truer or falser. It says three things, kept separate. Speed and reach are channel properties:
> a technology changes how fast a signal moves and how many people receive it, but the ground that
> settles which claim holds does not move with the channel. Credibility is the quantity a community
> produces about a claim, so it is frame-internal and cannot witness the claim it credits; faster
> retransmission inside a shared pool compounds agreement, not evidence. The one way a technology can
> bear on a settled claim is by changing contact, by bringing the claim to frames whose evidence
> differs or by making independent checking cheaper. What the package offers is that shape, the
> refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground remains open until
> those instruments run.

## 7. Falsifiers, restated for the sender

The shape claim is withdrawn if any of these is observed:

1. A medium-only change alters the ground's answer to an already-settled question.
2. Credibility in a closed pool predicts ground settlement above chance after independence and ground
   contact are controlled for.
3. More retransmissions of an unchanged claim, with independence fixed, improve verdict accuracy.
4. Reach rises, independence is unchanged, and a settled verdict moves.
5. One mechanism moves speed, reach and credibility with no residual in any intersection.

A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Send the failure, not the agreement.
