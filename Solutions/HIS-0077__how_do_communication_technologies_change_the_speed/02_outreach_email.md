# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have checked the three sub-claims in
`08_expert_verification_packet.md` against the verbatim figure text yourself. The output must survive
validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One claim about communication technology that you could refute in twenty minutes

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a question from the history of science and knowledge: how do communication
> technologies change the speed, reach, and credibility of competing claims? I have one specific
> output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the
> framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is
> nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` domain verdicts, not `grounded`):** a communication
> technology changes the **channel**, not the **ground**. Speed and reach are channel quantities: they
> describe how fast a signal moves and how many receivers it reaches. The ground is what settles which
> claim holds: a measurement, an experiment, an instrument, a board. The two do not move together.
> The figure I would ask you to check says, verbatim, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
> (Fig 10 :24).
>
> The second claim is that **credibility is a frame-internal quantity**. It is produced by the same
> community that would use it to decide, so it cannot witness the truth of the claim it credits. The
> relevant line is *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued... They are frame-internal. A frame
> cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"* (Fig 1 :26-27). What follows from this is
> that more transmission in a shared community increases agreement without increasing evidence:
> *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :12-13).
>
> The one legitimate way a technology can matter to a settled claim is by changing **contact**: by
> delivering the claim to a frame whose evidence base differs, or by making independent checking
> cheaper. The figure for that is *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing,
> however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40). This is a conditional claim, not a claim that any given
> technology helped or harmed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a particular medium (print, radio, the internet, social media) is
> good or bad; that widespread belief makes a claim true; that credibility is worthless as a social
> fact (it is a real and important quantity, just not a verdict); or that any of this is measured.
> I am not claiming the framework settles the historical question. It returns a shape, and it stops
> where the instruments stop.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. A change only in the transmission medium alters the ground's answer to an already-settled
>    question.
> 2. Credibility in a closed community predicts ground settlement above chance after independence
>    and ground contact are controlled for.
> 3. More retransmissions of an unchanged claim, with independence held fixed, improve the accuracy
>    of the resulting verdict.
> 4. Reach rises, the independence distribution of receivers does not change, and a settled verdict
>    moves anyway.
>
> If any of these holds, the corresponding claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this
> fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is
> needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the source links. The figures are the authority, not
> my summary of them, so you should check the quoted lines yourself before reading anything else I
> have written.
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can
> check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the
> mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | The framework's discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode on every substantive claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Links only after a verdict is recorded | Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| No claim that a named technology is good or bad | Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; the framework returns a shape. |
| Credibility kept as a real quantity but not a verdict | Fig 1 :27: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. |
| Contact named as the only ground-bearing route | Fig 11 :40 and Fig 6 :23: the wall moves only on contact with a differing closure. |
| No request for prestige or trust | The opened essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action... but it cannot settle an action."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask each user to state their own ground and refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |

## Distribution rules

- One recipient at a time. No mailing list, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** sending any stage-2 link. Later agreement is
  never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- A reply that is a refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the
  auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
- Include an opt-out line in every message. Public institutional and opt-in channels only; no
  personal emails harvested from private posts.
