I redid the census before comparing with the primary conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity,
symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by
dependency.

  enters a field of competing claims. Arity: three effects, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :5, split
  rather than search), not one hard question. Symmetry: speed and reach are content-symmetric channel
  quantities; credibility is directed from a frame to a claim. Scale: first order within a frame,
  second order across frames, third order at the ground.
- **Domain 1, the channel.** Actors: emitter, signal, medium, receiver. Currency: throughput, coverage.
  Anchor: channel fidelity. Real domain, nameable anchor.
- **Domain 2, credit.** Actors: claims and ranking frames. Currency: credibility accrued and agreement.
  Anchor: the ground. Real domain, and its currency is disqualified as its own verdict.
- **Domain 3, habitat.** Actors: audiences, institutions, gatekeepers, peer frames. Currency: attention,
  trust, independence. Anchor: the ground for truth. Real domain, contact enumerable.
- **Union, not partition.** A speed lever leaves the credibility intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-56).
- **Order by dependency.** Transmission, vocabulary, objective and settlement, credit, cross-frame
  comparison (Fig 3 :20-23). Fig 12 :121 makes a chain of retransmissions unable to close into the
  ground.

My census matches the primary's three domain boundaries and its union reading. Where the primary

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to
be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 10 :24 (channel versus ground), Fig 1
   :26-27 (credibility as self-score), Fig 7 :12-13 (agreement compounds), Fig 11 :40 (contact) and
   Fig 9 :54-56 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: a technology changes the channel,
   not the ground; credibility is frame-internal; contact is the only ground-bearing route; the three
   effects are a union.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a verdict about a named technology, for example "social media makes
   claims less credible" or "the printing press made knowledge more trustworthy."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **name ban** (Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*), which the
  concrete verdict breaches by naming a technology and ranking it;
- the **admissibility rule** (Fig 1 :27), which a reach-based verdict breaches by using the frame's
  own credibility signal as evidence;
- the **union structure** (Fig 9 :55-56), which a single-cause verdict breaches by treating three
  overlapping conditions as one lever.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a technology changes throughput and coverage, and the ground
does not move with it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because
no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and
would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a
measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
