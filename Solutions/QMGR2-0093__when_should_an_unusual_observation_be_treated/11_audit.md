## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a live per-slot mass R⁺_s, the positive part of |Γ(b,a) - o'|. Arity: one per tracked slot, therefore a vector; a scalar R would lose the address. Symmetry: unsigned magnitude but one-sided live mass; an anomaly that could have appeared anywhere is exchangeable and carries no address, one that recurs in the same slot does. Scale: promotion is within-scale, law-making is cross-scale with R_T as the error. I initially read this as a partition with two outcomes and had to re-census after the bin table forced itself in.
- **Domain 1, inference frame.** Actors: slots, b, a, o', Γ, R, φ, code. Currency: residual plus price in bits. Anchor: the ground for settlement. Question type: partition of R into bins.
- **Domain 2, significance frame.** Actors: p, sigma, trials, priors, Bayes factors, stopping rules. Currency: probability or evidence. Anchor: none independent; the frame's test statistic is generated by the model it tests. Convention-making only.
- **Domain 3, ground frame.** Actors: independent instruments, pipelines, channel audits. Currency: settled contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Supplies the verdict.
- **Domain 4, announcement frame.** Actors: journals, reviewers, community, prestige. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none. Not a domain for a verdict.
- **Order by dependency:** perceive before vocabulary before objective before planning before carry (Fig 3); SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD (Formula step 7); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before frame selection (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading, and its order. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 5's bin table and Fig 9 :54, I reached it from the arity of R: a vector residual cannot be decided by a single binary cut without discarding the index, which is the same place the primary arrived via Fig 1 :19. Different starting figures, same boundary. Low overlap between routes within the framework, but one evidence pool, so the agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :27 (per-slot residual, frame-internal scores), Fig 5 :10-14 and :42-60 (bins, bargain, persistence, refusal), Fig 9 :54 and :60 (union, filter versus verdict), Fig 2 :29-31 (anchor, independence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: "a residual must be per-slot, sorted, priced, persistent, ground-confirmed, channel-audited; thresholds are filters; search before import."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an operational lab or analysis protocol with a declared code, a scan count, a stopping rule, an independence plan, and a channel audit.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **code** in which cost(φ) and left(R,φ) are measured; without a declared code the bargain is not a number (Fig 5 :45 ties it to minimum description length);
- the **independence plan**: "two instruments pointed at the same thing" is a design requirement, not a rhetorical preference, and two readers of the same report are one frame (Fig 2 :31);
- the **refusal operator**: the shorthand says "refuse a false term" but the Operators table records that ¬ is unary, that a vocabulary without it can only accumulate, and that its price is open. The operational protocol must name an actual refusal mechanism, which the abstraction does not supply.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a live residual must remain indexed by slot and must not be scored by the frame that selected it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
