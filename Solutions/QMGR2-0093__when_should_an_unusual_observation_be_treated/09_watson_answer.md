## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the promotion rule for an unusual observation. An observer holds Γ(b,a) per slot s, receives o', and sees a live residual R⁺_s > 0. Option A: promote it to a candidate discovery, offer a term φ, pay the mint price, then seek settlement. Option B: keep it as a fluctuation, keep Γ, extend perception and aim the probe. What each leads to: A imports an atom and can shrink the reach if wrong; B leaves a live signal unfollowed and the reach below what it could be. A third reading, refused here, is that a numeric threshold itself answers the question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A live per-slot mass R⁺_s = the part of |Γ(b,a) - o'| that is positive on slot s. Arity: one residual per tracked slot, a vector not a scalar. Symmetry: the magnitude is unsigned but the live mass is one-sided; a fluctuation is exchangeable across slots and time, a discovery is anchored and recurs. Scale: the promotion decision is within-scale (steps 1 to 5); whether the term becomes a law is cross-scale (step 6, with R_T as error). Fig 9 names the decision a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the inference frame.** Actors: slots, b, a, o', Γ, R, candidate φ, declared code. Currency: residual plus price in bits. Anchor: the ground for any settlement. Question type: partition of R into the figure's five bins.
- **Domain 2, the significance frame.** Actors: p-values, sigma levels, trials factors, priors, Bayes factors, stopping rules. Currency: probability or evidence strength. Anchor: none fully independent; the sampling distribution is produced by the model being tested (Fig 1 :27). This frame makes conventions, not verdicts.
- **Domain 3, the ground frame.** Actors: independent instruments, datasets, pipelines, channel audits. Currency: settled contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Supplies the verdict.
- **Domain 4, the announcement frame.** Actors: journals, reviewers, community, prestige. Currency: agreement and credibility. Anchor: none nameable and independent. Convention-making only, zero verdict weight; not a domain in which a verdict can be grounded.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception before vocabulary before objective before planning before carry (Fig 3). SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD (Formula step 7). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Treat an unusual observation as a **possible discovery** only when it is a live per-slot residual that survives unbundling into the five bins, is not already reachable by independent search inside the current closure, admits a candidate whose declared-code price plus leftover is below the confusion it removes, persists and does not trend down under accumulation, survives a channel audit, and is confirmed by a low-overlap ground contact. Thresholds buy the follow-up; they are never the verdict. Where the residual shows a held term is false, the correct output is a refusal, not another positive explanation. The significance frame is a filter only; the announcement frame has zero verdict weight. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded result, not a number and not a decision about any particular anomaly.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A false promotion imports an atom priced above starting from primitives and can shrink the reach; a missed discovery leaves a live residual unfollowed. Sending a recording instead of a checkable rule spends the independence of outside readers. All are recoverable if the lineage and the refuter are kept.
- **undoable:** yes, in the record. A refusal is added rather than a term deleted, so a false promotion can be corrected without silent erasure, and a missed signal can be recovered by more independent search. Reach and independence take time to rebuild, but no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled benchmark on cost-plus-leftover and persistence, an arrangement test on the isomer model, a channel test, or any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
