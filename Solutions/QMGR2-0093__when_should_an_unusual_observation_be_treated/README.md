## The residual in effect terms

An observer holds a prediction Γ(b,a) for each tracked slot s and receives an observation o'. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| per slot is the only signal. An unusual observation is a live per-slot mass R⁺_s: something happened there that the prediction missed. Two options follow, and this package states what each leads to before choosing:

- **Option A, mint:** treat the residual as a discovery, offer a new term φ into the library, pay the bargain cost(φ) + left(R,φ) < cost(R), pass the three guards, then wait for the ground. If wrong, a wrong atom has been imported and the reach can fall (Formula step 3; Fig 6 :24).
- **Option B, no mint:** treat it as a fluctuation, keep the current library, leave the residual live, extend perception and aim the probe (Fig 5 :12). If wrong, a genuine signal stays unfollowed and the reach stays smaller than it could be.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run:** a decision rule that (i) reads the residual per slot and never as an aggregate, (ii) sorts it into the figure's bins rather than into a two-way discovery/fluctuation binary, (iii) prices a candidate term against the confusion it removes, (iv) checks whether the residual persists and does not trend down under accumulating data, and (v) defers the verdict to an independent ground, using any threshold only as a budget filter. This is the shape the derivation tests. It is written down here so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the rule.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No numeric significance threshold presented as the answer. The figures explicitly separate a budget filter from a verdict (Fig 9 :60) and call the acceptance test a bargain, not a threshold (Fig 5 :42).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the exclusions:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Prestige cannot settle a method.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." A famous name on a methodology claim gives it a budget to be tried, not a verdict.
