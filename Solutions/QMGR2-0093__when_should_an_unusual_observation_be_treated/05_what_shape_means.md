## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler and not a number

The canonical README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the set of constraints any acceptable promotion rule must satisfy: per-slot, sorted into the figure's bins, costed under a declared code, persistent under accumulation, ground-settled, channel-audited, search-before-import, refusal-capable. It is not a cutoff, a p-value, a Bayes factor threshold, or a list of famous anomalies that happened to fit. Those are fillers. A shape narrows; it does not decide a particular case.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer." Use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest, then recurse on the new terms that correlate with it, *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The question is described in effect terms (live per-slot residual, scan, stopping rule, independent instrument), not in the jargon of one testing tradition.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason the outreach in `02` sends a checkable rule, not a manifesto.
- *Library of Babel* on a bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly F2 of `01`: the anomaly decision is a union, and the shape is obtained by unbundling it.

## 3. What licenses a name, and what is forbidden here

Only two things license naming a cause (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52): the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened 2026-11-23) sharpens the distinction: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all, and only the second reaches structure the current primitives cannot represent." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring primitives "does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the framework cannot, from the shape alone, name *what* a given anomaly is. It can say what a candidate explanation must look like if it is to be admissible. Naming the cause of a particular anomaly is a binding from the physics frame (or the statistical frame) onto held atoms, provenanced as such.

**Correction to a possible overstatement.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. It does not forbid using an ordinary term to describe a decision procedure we already hold. `01` §0 binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, which is the recommended discipline (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66), not a breach of the limit. The line is: state the constraints, refuse to name the unseen cause.

## 4. The recognizer: the essay that governs this question most directly

*They're Scanning Books* gives the minimal mechanism of discovery in effect terms, and it is the closest world-level statement of the figure-derived shape:

- "Discovery under consequence = a random walk plus a model that can be surprised. ... Science is another: experiment is the walk, theory is the model, and the anomaly-chaser is the recognizer."
- "But the walk alone discovers nothing. Radioactivity was not discovered when the plate fogged; it was discovered when someone recognized the fogging as anomalous and did not stop."
- "A walk with no model has no anomalies, only noise, you can only be surprised relative to an expectation."
- "Novelty is a kind of failure, not a kind of success." A human facing novel structure "fails by searching"; a corpus model "fails by guessing." General intelligence is "the right kind of failure: making progress through search-and-adaptation rather than confabulation."
- "The recognizer can only register an anomaly it has the primitives to represent." A snake cannot be surprised by a color.

This corroborates the figure reading at several points. An anomaly is *relative to a model* (Fig 1 : R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|); without a prediction there is no residual, only events. Recognizing an anomaly is a disposition to invest in the violation rather than discard it, which is F3's compressibility test and F4's persistence test in narrative form. And the limits of representability are the source-discrimination limit below: a frame cannot be surprised along an axis it cannot encode, which is why "no search, however long, adds an atom" (Fig 8 :32).

## 5. Why more of the same reading does not settle it

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the instrument argument:

- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."
- An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." Yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce green.
- "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
- "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a high-sigma deviation is a reading, not a source. Several causes (a real signal, a trials artifact, a channel fault, a mis-specified null) can produce the same reading. What is owed is a discriminating instrument or an independent frame, not an argument about how surprising the reading feels. This is F5 and F9 of `01` in a different field's vocabulary. It is corroboration, not derivation: the figure lines decide.

## 6. Effect terms, projection, and the question as tether

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the failure of a claim that cannot be checked by anyone outside the frame:

- "a measurement with no question is noise, and a reading with no encounter is projection."
- Fig 9 :27-28 is cited there: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- On independent frames: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone" (Fig 8 :45), and "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it" (Fig 2 :45).
- On drift: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." The reference point must stay put.

For this question, the tether is the residual in effect terms: a single slot's prediction failure, with the scan and stopping rule declared. Without that, "is this a discovery?" is a measurement with no question, and any framework you pick will seem to fit.

## 7. Prestige and simulation, the two standing disqualifiers

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23):

- "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."
- "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."
- "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."
- "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So the present audit is a simulation. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures and the opened sources, and it cannot settle whether the rule is good. A famous anomaly, a famous referee, or a large number of agreeing readers cannot either. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

## 8. Q2 answered: what does "shape" mean *for this question*?

**Answer:** it means a reusable decision *form* whose slots are constrained but whose filling is left to the ground. Concretely, the shape is: R must remain per-slot; the residual must be sorted before it is judged; a candidate must be priced against the confusion it removes; persistence must be checked; the verdict must be an independent ground contact; the channel must be audited; the closure must be searched before an atom is imported; and the vocabulary must be able to refuse. What it does not supply is the number, the instrument, or the name of the cause in any particular case.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** It would if it presented a specific sigma value, a specific anomaly, or a specific causal binding as *the* answer. It does not. `07_the_tether_answer.md` keeps the shape and lists the instruments that could close it, and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks a reader to test the form, not to endorse a conclusion.

## 9. What would move the shape

A source-discriminating instrument that separates a real signal from a trials artifact and a channel fault at the same residual magnitude, or a controlled benchmark in which the figure-derived form outperforms and underperforms fixed thresholds exactly as predicted. Fig 13's honest rule applies: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Until such a test runs, the status is `candidate`, not `grounded`.
