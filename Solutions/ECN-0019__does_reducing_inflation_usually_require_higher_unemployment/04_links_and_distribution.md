## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the answer before any framework material is shown (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figures and formula links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output is a shape claim, not a number. Do not slip a sacrifice-ratio estimate into stage 1, because that number belongs to the ground and cannot be settled by the figures.
