## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this run: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** the cost is indexed by regime slots; the anchor must not update; the compound must be unbundled; the regime change must be an import with provenance; capability is agent-and-habitat.
- **Not a shape, and therefore not supplied by the framework:** the sacrifice ratio, the NAIRU estimate, the choice of inflation targeting versus another framework, the name of any central bank, and the answer to "usually".

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this run: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The method is proximity to what you already know, then recursion.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" (joined across the two lines).
- Applied here: describe the residual as "the cost varies with the regime and the binding condition" rather than "the Phillips curve slope is beta". The first is an effect description that travels to another frame; the second is a parameter in one frame's language.

## 3. The bundle warning is this question exactly

*Library of Babel*, opened this run: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the word "usually" in the question. It bundles four conditions. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." gives the figure-level version. Neither the essay nor the figure says the bundle has no answer; both say the answer must be assembled from the separated parts.

## 4. What licenses a number

Only the ground settles a number. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this run: *"The Tether ties alignment to the ground. Not to human preferences. Not to a reward model trained on human feedback. To the thing that settles a question regardless of what any frame thinks. An outcome. A measurement. A board that pays or does not."*

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." is the figure-level statement of the same rule. In this question the data on inflation and unemployment across episodes is the ground. A model's internal consistency is not.

The second licensing route is a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. The world sources about credibility and institutions are such imports into the policy frame, and are recorded as such in `09_watson_answer.md`.

## 5. Why the framework cannot answer "usually"

Three separate limits converge:

1. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global average discards the live signal.
2. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The framework returns the form the answer must have.
3. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this run: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator."*

So the honest answer is a shape plus a measurement instruction, not a verdict on the magnitude. Naming a magnitude as "the Tether's answer" would be the forbidden move: a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this run: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous economists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional venues, not a ranked list of believers.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this run: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
