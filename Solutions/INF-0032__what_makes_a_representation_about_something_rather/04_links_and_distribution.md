## Wider literature that bears on the separator

1. **Information carriage is not representation.** SEP *Mental Representation* section 6 is the cleanest statement of the residual: tree trunks, smoke, thermostats and telephones carry information but do not represent. Causal-informational theories alone are judged insufficient; asymmetric dependency and teleological theories are the main attempts to add the extra. This corroborates the figure finding that a bond and a refusal path, not more correlation, are owed.
2. **Aboutness is a directed, possibly non-existent object.** SEP *Intentionality* gives Brentano's "direction toward an object", the intentional inexistence puzzle, and the Frege/Russell/Kripke attempts to fix what the object is. This is the world-side version of Fig 12's point that the relation is not recoverable from the operands.
3. **Derived versus original intentionality.** SEP *Externalism About the Mind* says a notebook sentence is mere shapes until interpreted, and that original intentionality is the traditional mark of the mental. This corroborates F6: a declared "about" is a convention until a ground can violate it.
4. **Animal signalling.** The vervet alarm-call case in the public animal communication page shows distinct calls for distinct predators with appropriate listener responses, while the same page warns that animal signals are easier to describe than interpret and that the same gesture can mean different things by context. It corroborates the per-slot and discriminator conditions at low overlap with the philosophical sources. It does not by itself settle whether the calls are "about" predators; that is the open discriminator question.
5. **Source discrimination.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives metamerism: one green reading, many possible sources, and a spectrometer that resolves what the eye cannot. It is the framework author's own essay, so it shares the figure evidence pool and carries no independent witness weight.

Overlap is declared rather than hidden: the three SEP entries are one scholarly pool (and all are reference works, not independent experiments), the animal behaviour page is genuinely lower overlap, and the four essays share the framework author completely.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a source, not a checkable claim.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before offering any theory.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record that verdict; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter before running it.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual, the three conditions and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, offer the links. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*).
- **Stage 3, optional method transfer.** Offer the formula as a reusable generator (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state a ground and a refuter before running it, because the loop cannot check the direction of its own question (formula "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. Run the five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 before sending anything.
