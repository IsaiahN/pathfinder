## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :46-47 and :117 (typed bond, isomer), Fig 2 :29 and :45 (anchor, ranging), Fig 12 :141 (refusal), Fig 6 :32 (instrument).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: correlation is co-presence, aboutness is a typed bond to a non-updating ground with a refusal path, plus a discriminator; the two are isomers.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete case, for example a thermometer reading said to be about the temperature of a specific room at a specific time.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the discriminator** for the case. The shorthand says one is owed; nothing in the figures says which instrument would separate the rival sources here;
- the **per-slot indexing**. A pooled corpus-level claim about "representation" loses the room and the time that make the thermometer token about that temperature;
- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim. The shorthand says correlation and aboutness share the same atom set; a concrete case may need an atom (a selected function, an evolved role) that the correlation case does not have, which drops the claim to a functional group.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: co-presence alone does not carry the typed relation, and without a refusal path the system cannot mark the token false of the object. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
