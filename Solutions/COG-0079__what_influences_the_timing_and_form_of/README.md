## What this solution decides

The question is a **union**, not one thing. Timing and form are two different quantities with different instruments, and agency, intention and responsibility are three sub-residuals that share atoms but differ in their bonds. Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The figures return a shape, never the age or the list: a capability that is child-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), a channel that decays while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24), a form carried by bonds rather than ingredients (Fig 12 :5), and a dependency order in which the refusal operator is needed for responsibility (Operators :58). The specific ages and weights are a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
