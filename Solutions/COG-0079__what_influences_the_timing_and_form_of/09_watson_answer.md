## QUESTION

What influences the timing and form of children's understanding of agency, intention, and responsibility?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| stay small at different points in the ordered succession (timing) and take different bonded structures (form) across child slots and across the three concepts? b is the child's belief about a social event, a is the attribution, Γ is the child's social-cognitive library, o′ is the independently measured state of the event, and s is the child and the concept. Anchor: the independently measured social state, legitimate here because Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Option A: a single list of influences with ages and weights. Option B: a union-shaped answer with a dependency order, a channel distinction and a declared gap. Option A sends a pooled reading that discards the per-slot signal; Option B sends a checkable shape with refuters and an open slot where a discriminator is owed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Multi-place: child, social event, attribution, time. Arity is a sequence carrying ordered `→`, production `⇒`, comparison `⋛`, subtraction `−` and negation `¬`. Symmetry: understanding is a threshold against the independently measured ground, not an absolute quantity; timing is asymmetric in time. Scale: child loop, proximal dyad, distal culture and cohort, life-course span. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
- **D1 Child loop.** Actors: child, Γ, beliefs about events. Currency: attributions and residual reduction. Anchor: the independently measured social state. Admitted.
- **D2 Proximal habitat.** Actors: caregivers, siblings, peers, mental-state talk, joint attention. Currency: contact and imported terms. Anchor: the same ground reached through contact. Admitted.
- **D3 Distal habitat.** Actors: culture, schooling, media and technology, historical cohort. Currency: imported practices and room changes. Anchor: the same ground reached through differing closures. Admitted.
- **D4 Composition.** Actors: atoms (agent, action, goal, outcome, belief) and bonds. Currency: reach and |φ|. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **D5 Measurement channel.** Actors: task, language, experimenter, scoring rule. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Pure maturation as sufficient cause.** No anchor independent of contact; not a domain yet, re-scoped into D4/D5. X2 culture as sole cause and X3 chronological age as cause are absorbed into D2/D3 and D5.
- **Union, not partition.** D1/D2, D2/D3 and D4/D5 overlap; each overlap is a compound. Unbundle timing from form and the three concepts from each other before searching (Fig 9 :54, :57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the event, have terms for what it affords, say what counts as progress, plan the attribution, learn and carry (Fig 3). Responsibility additionally requires the refusal operator.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ages and the weights do not exist in this derivation. The question is a union: timing and form are different quantities, and agency, intention and responsibility are bond configurations over shared atoms, so they are isomers rather than rungs of one ladder. Capability is child-and-habitat with residual-relative valence, so there is no standing list of influences. New social atoms enter only from a closure that differs. Measured timing is a compound of genuine contact change and channel decay, because the ground does not decay while the task does. The order is a dependency, agency then intention then responsibility, gated by the middle link and by the refusal operator, with life-course handoffs depending on bracket fidelity and ground pressure. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. The specific ages and causal weights are a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** a misdirected decade. A child whose problem is a channel failure would be given more contact-rich input, while a child whose problem is a missing import would be given a better task; a single lever would be applied to a union and leave the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). The real cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its eight refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A channel discriminator that separates ground-contact change from task change; a contact discriminator showing invariant timing across a documented habitat substitution; an operator discriminator showing responsibility without the refusal operator; a handoff discriminator across cohorts with different transition timing; or a figure re-census that returns a numeric age or ranked influence list. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
