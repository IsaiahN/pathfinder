# Expert verification packet: the Tether on COG-0079

**What this is:** a claim in a form a developmental psychologist or life-course researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the specific ages and weights. Not `grounded`.** No child-level measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union, not a single list of causes. Timing and form are different quantities with different instruments, and agency, intention and responsibility share atoms but differ in the bond that joins them, so they stand as isomers over a shared atom set. The understanding is a property of the child and the child's habitat, never of the child alone, and no standing list of influences keeps its valence when the residual changes. New social atoms enter only from a frame whose closure differs. Part of every measured timing difference is a channel effect, because the ground does not decay while the measuring task does. The order is a dependency, agency then intention then responsibility, gated by the middle link of vocabulary and objective and by the availability of a refusal operator. The ages and causal weights are a declared gap. What is offered is the shape, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The union and the per-slot rule

- **Statement:** timing and form must be reported separately, per child and per concept; a single aggregate score or a single age has already discarded the only signal that says where to look.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Also Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."
- **Refuter:** one prospectively validated number that predicts timing and form across all three concepts per child, with no task or country term and no leftover residual.

### C2: Child-and-habitat, with residual-relative valence

- **Statement:** the capability is not located in the child alone; it depends on contact with caregivers, language, peers, schooling, technology and cohort, and the valence of each is relative to the residual, so no standing list of good influences holds across all three concepts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Also Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." and Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Plus the import rule, Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
- **What to check against your own knowledge:** is there any documented case of a genuinely new social-cognitive atom acquired with no differing frame (no caregiver, peer, language or institutional input), while ground contact still pays? Is there a habitat influence whose measured valence is invariant across a change of residual?
- **Refuter:** either case above. The figure states its own version: an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim (Fig 11 :72).

### C3: Bond-differentiated form and a decaying channel

- **Statement:** the three concepts are distinguished by typed bonds, not by ingredient counts; responsibility needs the refusal operator; and measured timing can move because the channel decays while the ground does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does".
- **What to check:** does Piaget's documented coexistence of consequence-based and intention-based judgment in the same child fit a bond switch better than an atom addition? Do task-language and paradigm changes in the false-belief literature move the apparent timing without any change in the child?
- **Refuter:** the three concepts shown to differ only by added atoms with no bond difference; or a child passing an independently measured responsibility judgment before mastering the refusal operator; or a task manipulation that leaves timing invariant while ground truth is held constant, with no paradigm dependence or publication bias.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an age at which any concept is mastered, and not a weight for any named influence.
- Not a ranking of parenting against schooling, peers, technology or culture.
- Not a causal claim from correlational data. "Influence" is ordered, while most field evidence is correlational (live strain S3).
- Not a denial that biology matters; only a denial that an agent-only change with no contact change can move the ground (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a theory of everything, and not a final answer. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a child-level measurement.
- Not a claim that the structure in the form slot is a particular named module. Naming a held habitat or bond binding is provenance and a candidate, not a new primitive (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Four discriminators are specified in `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 4: channel, contact, operator and handoff. Until one is run, the ages and weights stay a `gap` and the shape stays `candidate`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :4, Fig 11 :39 and :51, Fig 12 :5 and :117, and Fig 10 :24, six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of the field: is any named influence valence-invariant across a change of residual? If yes, send it.
3. Check C3 against Piaget's intention/consequence data and against the task-factor findings in Wellman et al. 2001 and Barone et al. 2020: does the bond-versus-atom and ground-versus-channel reading fit better than the alternatives?
4. Name one instrument that would separate a ground-contact change from a measuring-channel change, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
