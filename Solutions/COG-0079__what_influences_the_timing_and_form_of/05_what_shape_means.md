## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "→ you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a union of timing and form, contact-dependent capability with residual-relative valence, a measuring channel that can decay independently of the ground, bond-differentiated concepts, and a dependency order in which refusal precedes responsibility. The shape is not "four years old", not "parental talk wins", not "Piaget was right".

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" and :31 "rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (a gap between a child's attribution and the independently measured social state, indexed per child and per concept) before any figure line was selected. The field labels "theory of mind", "mental-state talk" and "false belief" were withheld until the census and then used as bindings, not as answers.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question that means a reported age or a reported causal weight is a name. It is licensed only by a ground measurement that pays for it or by a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (for example, life-course longitudinal method imported into developmental psychology). Until then it is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why a task score cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." An instrument that cannot distinguish these cases "demonstrates its own inadequacy". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here a child's score on a false-belief task is a reading. Several sources can produce the same reading: a change in competence, a change in task language, a change in motivation, a publication filter. Naming the source from the reading alone is exactly the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (an instrument that separates ground contact from channel change), not a name. F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is this point in figure form.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming parental talk, schooling or culture?

**No, and the earlier overstatement is corrected here.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. Caregiver talk, peers, schooling, technology and cohort are not missing primitives in this question; they are already-available bindings from the developmental-psychology and life-course frames onto the habitat and channel terms the figures already hold (Fig 11 :45-47, Fig 10 :24-26).

So naming them is a **binding**, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall: Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom", and Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." A study that shows parental mental-state talk correlates with earlier false-belief performance has added a reading, not a primitive. It still needs a source gate and a shadow test before it can be treated as an import that explains the residual.

Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost: the metamerism point stands (a score cannot name its source without a discriminator), and the "shape, not a name" ban applies to missing primitives, not to naming held habitat bindings.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous developmentalists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, let an independently measured social ground settle it. That is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutional opt-in venues and no personal emails.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any developmental claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on; only a child-level ground measurement could do that.

## 7. The stop for COG-0079

The framework gives the shape and stops. It does not give the age, the list of winning influences, or the causal weight. The stop is honest because the lineage is recorded: which figure governed which finding, which world source was read, which conflict was returned to the census, which strain remains live (S3 on the order of "influence"), and which eight refuters would break it. Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." `11_audit.md` measures the round-trip gap qualitatively as nonzero and enumerates what the shorthand drops.
