# The Tether's answer: COG-0079

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the specific ages and weights. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a developmental result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No child was measured by this agent.

## 1. The residual it answers

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s, where b is the child's belief about a social event, a is the child's attribution, Γ is the child's current social-cognitive library, o′ is the independently measured state of the event, and s is the child and the concept. The phenomenon is not a level but two quantities: when the residual first stays small (timing) and what structure the attribution takes (form). Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 2. The shape

1. **It is a union, not one question.** Timing and form are different quantities with different instruments; agency, intention and responsibility are three sub-residuals sharing atoms but differing in bonds. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

2. **The understanding is child-and-habitat, not child alone, and no standing list of influences survives a change of residual.** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The influences are contact conditions: caregivers, language, peers, schooling, technology and cohort, read off the habitat rather than invented (Fig 11 :48). Their valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). A practice that speeds intention understanding can slow responsibility understanding, and nothing about the practice records which.

3. **New social atoms enter only from a closure that differs.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The child cannot derive a genuinely new social concept by arranging what it already holds forever. The differing frame (a caregiver who labels a mental state, a peer who contradicts a reading, a story with another perspective, an institution demanding a new distinction) is the import, and imports need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).

4. **Part of every measured timing difference is a channel effect.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Task language, single trials, pooled scores and publication filters move the reading without moving the child. Fig 2 :37 "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." The social ground is the anchor; the task score is an instrument.

5. **Form is carried by bonds, not by an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Agency uses production (`A produces B; B did not exist before`), intention uses comparison (Operators :51 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"), responsibility adds the refusal of the accidental reading (Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"). Over a shared atom set the three concepts are isomers: Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

6. **The order is a dependency, not a clock.** Fig 3 :25 "Perception has obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests. The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Agency precedes intention precedes responsibility because responsibility needs the refusal operator; the break is usually at vocabulary and objective, not planning. Timing in the large is a life-course chain whose handoffs depend on bracket fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39), and on when transitions occur (Elder 1998), not on calendar age alone.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not an age. No figure line returns the age at which a named child masters a named concept.
- Not a list of winning influences with weights. No figure ranks parental talk against schooling or culture; the valence of each is residual-relative (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a causal claim from correlational evidence. "Influence" is ordered, while most world evidence is correlational; a correlation cannot choose the arrow (live strain S3).
- Not a claim that biology is absent. An agent-only improvement that leaves contact unchanged "changes nothing, however much it improves" (Fig 11 :40); the figure's own refuter is an agent-only improvement that moved the ground (Fig 11 :72).
- Not `grounded`. No social-ground measurement in this run pays for the shape.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

- **Channel discriminator:** does the same child's timing and form stay invariant across a task-language or paradigm change that leaves the ground truth of the event fixed? If not, part of the timing is channel.
- **Contact discriminator:** do timing and form move when a documented habitat substitution changes contact, with valence flipping for one concept but not another?
- **Operator discriminator:** can the child assert the refusal of the accidental reading before the comparison bond is in place, measured independently?
- **Handoff discriminator:** do two cohorts with different transition timing and historical context differ in the way Fig 7 :37-39 predicts, with no channel compensation?

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The specific ages and weights are a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39). The shape stops where the available instruments can no longer discriminate a change of ground contact from a change of measuring channel. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable: which figure governed which finding, which world source was read, which conflict was returned to the census, which strain is live. ## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say when children understand agency, intention and responsibility, and it does not rank the influences. It says the question is a union: timing and form are different quantities, and the three concepts are three different bond configurations over shared atoms, so they are isomers rather than three rungs of one ladder. The understanding is a property of the child and the child's habitat, with no standing list of influences whose valence survives a change of residual; new social atoms enter only through contact with a frame whose closure differs. Part of every measured timing shift is a channel effect, because the ground does not decay while the measuring task does. The order is a dependency, agency then intention then responsibility, with the break usually at the middle link of vocabulary and objective and with the refusal operator needed last. What the framework offers is that shape, eight refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ages and the weights stay open until a ground measurement pays for them.
