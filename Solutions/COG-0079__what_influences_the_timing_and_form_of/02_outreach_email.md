# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive review. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one output about children's social understanding before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about children's social understanding. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the child-facing claims, `general` on the structural split, not `grounded`):** the question "what influences the timing and form of children's understanding of agency, intention and responsibility" is a union, not one question. Timing and form are two different quantities with different instruments, and the three concepts share atoms but differ in the bond that joins them. From the framework's habitat rule it follows that the understanding is a property of the child and the child's habitat, never of the child alone, and that no standing list of influences keeps its valence when the residual changes. From its ground-maintainer rule it follows that part of every measured timing difference is a channel effect: the ground does not decay, the measuring channel does. From its bond rule it follows that agency, intention and responsibility are distinguished by the kind of bond, not by having more concepts, and that responsibility needs a refusal operator the other two do not.
>
> What the output does **not** say: it does not give the age at which any of this happens, it does not rank parenting against schooling or culture, and it does not claim a causal weight for any named influence. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Those specific values are a declared gap, recorded as unreached rather than silence.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. One number that prospectively predicts both timing and form across all three concepts, per child, with no task or country term left over.
> 2. Timing and form unchanged across a habitat substitution that demonstrably changes contact, or an agent-only biological improvement that moves the ground.
> 3. A task manipulation that leaves measured timing invariant while the ground truth of the event is held constant, and no paradigm dependence or publication bias across studies.
> 4. Responsibility shown to require no refusal operator, or the three concepts shown to differ only by adding atoms.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture article: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a developmental problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it: the framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and the write-up warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]

## Practical constraint

The Society for Research in Child Development publishes a Generative Artificial Intelligence Policy on its Child Development journal page, and Child Development has Registered Reports author guidelines. Because this packet is a simulated derivation, any submission through those venues should be checked against that policy and framed, if at all, as a methods or Registered Report style note with a pre-registered refuter, not as an empirical finding. See `04_links_and_distribution.md`. Nothing is sent by this agent; you host and send.
