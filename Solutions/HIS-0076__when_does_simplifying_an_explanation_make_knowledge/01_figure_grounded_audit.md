## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question is not about length, readability, or style. It is about what happens to a **per-slot residual at the receiver** when an explanation is passed through a compressing transform.

- A source frame holds Γ_S: atoms, bonds, and a structure of uncertainty, that is, which predictions are solid and which slots still carry live residual.
- A simplification is a lossy transform T across a membrane from Γ_S to a receiver frame Γ_R (Fig 4).
- The receiver acts: Γ_R(b,a) predicts, the ground returns o′, and R_s = |Γ_R(b,a) - o′| is measured **per slot**, never as one global comprehension score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).
- A second quantity is the round-trip gap R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)): what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :30, Fig 4 :31).

**Accessibility** is the case where receiver per-slot R falls against the ground **and** R_T is reported. **Distortion** is the case where R_T is not reported, or where the bonds that carried decisive difference were dropped without any record of the drop, so a low measured R coexists with unseen live slots.

**Arity, symmetry, scale.** The residual is at least a four-place relation (source explanation, simplified explanation, receiver state, ground), with an ordered transform, a comparison against the anchor, and a threshold deciding which slots are live. It is not symmetric: direction of travel matters, because *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). It is cross-scale: the explainer's loop, the transmission, and the receiver's loop are separated by membranes, and the transform between them is lossy (Fig 4 :27).

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists: the ground that settles the receiver's prediction. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* So this is a well-formed question and not yet a `gap`. If the chosen test is "did the audience say they understood", that is not an anchor, because it is produced by the same frame being checked (Fig 1 :27).

## 3. Findings

### F1 - Accessibility and distortion are two quantities, not two ends of one scale (primary)

Fig 4 :30:

> **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

Fig 4 :31:

> **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"**

Reading: the transform necessarily loses information (Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*), so loss is not itself the defect. The defect is an unreported loss. Accessibility is receiver per-slot R falling while R_T is recorded; distortion is a low reported R with R_T unmeasured. Collapsing them into one "comprehension" scalar is the named failure, because *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22).

**Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a single scalar measure accounts for both the benefit and the distortion with zero per-slot residual and zero round-trip gap across a documented set of cases.

### F2 - A method may cross; a recording of one occasion may not (primary)

Fig 4 :22 and Fig 4 :23:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**

Fig 4 :25:

> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

Reading: a simplification that hands the receiver a reusable way to check and re-derive is accessible, because it makes the next search cheaper. A simplification that hands over only the concluded sentence is a recording. It can be repeated and cannot produce a new prediction, which is exactly the distortion case when conditions change. Fig 5 :43 adds the MDL side: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing"* fails the bargain, and so does one that explains one occasion perfectly.

**Mode `general` for the structural rule, `specified` for explanations. Refuter:** a verbatim simplified conclusion that reliably produces correct predictions on new cases for a receiver who holds no method and for whom the unexplained remainder measures zero.

### F3 - Dropping the bonds hides the difference that matters (primary)

Fig 12 :5:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :14 and Fig 12 :16:

> **"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not"**
> **"and nothing signals that anything was lost"**

Fig 12 :117:

> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Reading: a simplification that keeps the list of facts but drops the relations has recorded the atoms and lost the molecule. Two accounts with the same facts but different arrangement are isomers: the difference is invisible in the simplified notation and decisive in what the receiver predicts. This is the mechanism behind "distorts the underlying uncertainty": the uncertainty is not merely smaller, it is restructured into something the notation cannot distinguish. Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*

**Mode `general`. Refuter:** a documented case set where removing every bond between atoms leaves all predictions and all uncertainty judgments invariant, measured on the ground and not by self-report.

### F4 - A frame cannot score the transmission with a quantity it produces

Fig 1 :27:

> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Fig 9 :60:

> **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Reading: compression achieved, coverage, clarity ratings, and credibility accrued are frame-internal (Fig 1 :25, Fig 1 :26). They are useful for deciding how much budget to spend on a communication, and they are disqualified from deciding whether accessibility occurred. Only a receiver's per-slot prediction against the ground can decide that. Selecting an audience for how likely they are to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).

**Mode `general`. Refuter:** an independently ground-referenced measurement showing that a frame-internal clarity or credibility metric tracks receiver R per slot with no unexplained residual.

### F5 - A mean comprehension score is how a live signal disappears

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Reading: "the audience understood the explanation" is an aggregate over receivers, claims, and decisions. It can be high while one claim in one decision context has a large live residual. The correct report is a vector of per-slot residuals, each with its own uncertainty. This is a direct instance of the framework's per-slot rule, not an addition to it.

**Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a mean measure with zero measured variance across slots in a case where at least one slot is demonstrably wrong at the ground.

### F6 - The channel decays; the ground does not

Fig 10 :24:

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Fig 10 :26:

> **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"**

Reading: distortion is often a channel failure rather than a source failure. The source explanation was fine; the medium dropped transients, saturated a metric, or pooled readings. The output of that state is not a verdict but *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :28). The remedy is to keep the anchor reachable (Fig 10 :29) and to declare any interest that cannot be removed (Fig 10 :60). Receiver agreement with the explainer does not restore contact (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).

**Mode `specified`. Refuter:** receiver predictions stay calibrated on a fresh ground reading after the explainer's framing is removed and the channel is shown to have dropped transients.

### F7 - Accessibility is relative to the receiver's frame

Fig 4 :20:

> **"it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."**

Fig 11 :39:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

Reading: the same simplified text can lower R for a novice and raise it for an expert, because Γ_R differs. "Is this explanation accessible?" is under-specified until the receiver's prior terms and the decision they face are named. The world literature calls the same phenomenon the expertise reversal effect (see `09_watson_answer.md`, World). This is a property of the receiver-and-habitat, not of the text alone.

**Mode `specified`. Refuter:** an identical simplification produces identical per-slot R changes across receivers whose prior knowledge was measured to differ.

### F8 - The best simplification for a case is a `gap`, not a hidden answer

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

Reading: after a genuine re-census no figure line selects the optimal compression for a given audience. That is not a failure of search. The framework returns a shape, not a name: the two conditions and the instrument that would close them. What is missing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms and not yet held. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Mode `general` for the limit. Refuter:** a figure line that determines the unique best simplification for a named audience, produced by re-census rather than by binding a name from a world field.

### F9 - The source must be reported, not inferred from the reading

Fig 8 :50:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**

Fig 10 :49:

> **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

Reading: a simplified explanation looks the same whether it was derived and checked or merely adopted from a prestigious source. The record of provenance is the whole difference. Calling a simplified sentence "accurate" because its author is trusted is a prestige leak; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (essay source, `10_source_ledger.csv`, not a figure quote).

**Mode `specified`. Refuter:** a claim whose source is unknown still produces ground-verified per-slot predictions with the same residual as a claim whose derivation is fully recorded.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach, per the seat-map rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One scalar measure of accessibility accounts for both the benefit and the distortion, with no per-slot residual and no round-trip gap (breaks F1 and F5).
2. A simplified conclusion reliably yields correct novel predictions for a receiver who holds no method and whose unexplained remainder measures zero (breaks F2).
3. Removing all bonds between atoms leaves predictions and uncertainty judgments invariant across a documented case set (breaks F3).
4. A frame-internal metric is shown to be the anchor that settles receiver R at the ground (breaks F4).
5. An identical simplification produces identical per-slot R change across receivers whose prior knowledge measurably differs (breaks F7).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Report R_T with the simplified explanation: what the coarser description cannot hold. Make the gap part of the artefact, not an appendix (F1).
2. Send a generator, not a recording. Give the receiver a way to re-derive and check, and say what would count as a failure (F2).
3. Keep the bonds. If a relation is dropped, mark the slot and say what difference it would make (F3).
4. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Count clarity and clicks as contact-cost estimates only (F4).
5. Report per-slot residuals, never one comprehension average (F5).
6. Name the receiver and the decision. The same text is not the same explanation for two different Γ_R (F7).
7. Use the public submission and contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, and ask for a refutation, not belief. Record the verdict before showing any theory.

## 7. Three questions at the limit

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7, when no line closes the residual, ask SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, and keep them distinct.

- **SEARCHED:** is the optimum in here and unfound? The closure over the figures was swept for a selection rule for a best simplification. None appeared. More search inside the same set does not change the wall (Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*).
- **INWARD:** extend the instrument from one already returning something. The per-slot residual and the round-trip gap are already readable in the framework; sharpening them into a ground-referenced receiver test is the inward move. It refines in our own terms.
- **OUTWARD:** import. Characterise the missing thing in effect terms: an instrument that discriminates a dropped bond from a legitimately absent one, at the receiver, against the ground. Search low-overlap fields for frames whose closure predicts that shape (education, risk communication, cognitive science). This is what `09_watson_answer.md` does. Importing against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed, so no new primitive is claimed.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: a simplification is accessible when it carries a reusable method, keeps the arrangement that decides behaviour, reports the round-trip gap, and lowers receiver residual per slot against a reachable ground; it distorts when any of those is missing while a frame-internal metric reports success. Which compression is best for a named audience is a declared `gap`. Strains after re-census: **none unresolved**; both apparent strains were sent back to the census and resolved on different objects.
