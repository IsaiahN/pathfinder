### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** at least four operands (source explanation, transform, receiver, ground), with an ordered transform and a comparison. A two-place reading (text, comprehension score) loses the transform and the ground, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. Direction of travel is load-bearing (Fig 4 :22). The ground is an anchor and does not update on either frame's opinion (Fig 2 :25).

The transform between scales is lossy, so the residual is intrinsically cross-scale (Fig 4 :27).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to one sentence. Result: *a good simplification keeps what matters and is checked against the world.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that one sentence into a specific science communication decision. Result: *use plain language and ask the audience whether it makes sense.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. "What matters" does not say **bond**. Coming back down, it permits a summary that keeps every fact and drops every relation, which is the isomer failure (Fig 12 :117).
2. "Checked against the world" does not say **per slot**. Coming back down, it permits one mean comprehension score to stand in for a vector of residuals (Fig 1 :22).
3. It loses the **method versus recording** distinction. "Keep what matters" tells a communicator to keep a conclusion, not to hand over a reusable check (Fig 4 :22).
4. It loses the **round trip itself**. Asking whether the audience "makes sense" of the text is a frame-internal metric, not a ground reading (Fig 1 :27).
5. It loses the **convention versus verdict** separation. A shared meaning is a legitimate product, but it is not accuracy (Fig 2 :41).
6. It loses **receiver dependence**. The one sentence implies one audience. The same text has different residuals for different Γ_R (Fig 4 :20).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version. The paragraph in `07` section 6 is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
