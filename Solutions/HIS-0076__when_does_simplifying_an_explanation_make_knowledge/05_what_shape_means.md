## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns a shape for a **missing** thing, not a name for it. From *The Library of Babel*: you can stop when a returned set is *"small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the recursive cut puts *"real material in"* only when the practitioner supplies measurements; the framework alone narrows by constraint.

So for this question a **shape** is the pair of conditions that separates accessible simplification from distorting simplification, before anyone has measured a particular audience. It constrains; it does not pick the wording, the reading level, or the medium. The two conditions are: the round trip is reported, and the receiver's residual is measured per slot against a reachable ground. A one-sentence summary is not the shape; it is an instance of a transmission that the shape would grade.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the receiver-side instrument this question needs: a prediction anyone can check.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has composed the answer through the search. For outreach this is a warning: a shape that feels finished is often just a familiar vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens both: *"Outputs are not generators"*, and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A simplified text is an output; it does not contain the generator that produced or checked it. That is why F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` requires a method to cross, not a recording.

## 4. Why a smooth simplification can be the specific forbidden error

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the mechanism with a video-frame example: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants."* And: *"nothing in the image records which."* This is Fig 12 :16 (*"and nothing signals that anything was lost"*) at the level of a public explanation. The short version looks complete; the provenance that would distinguish a checked derivation from an adopted slogan is gone.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* names the corresponding instrument error: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Several different uncertainties can produce the same smooth sentence. The sentence cannot say which source produced it. What is owed is a discriminating test at the receiver, not a better-sounding sentence.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the receiver's side: *"we can know more than we can tell"*, so a compressed expert message only unpacks for someone with the right history. The essay proposes a measurable proxy: *"The size of a correction tells you how much of the knowledge is still tacit for that learner."* That is a concrete candidate for the missing instrument named as a `gap` in F8: correction size per slot, trending down, instead of a one-off satisfaction rating.

## 5. Two corrections to my own reading

### 5a. "Shape, not a name" does not forbid simplification

I initially read the canonical phrase as a ban on naming any part of an explanation. **Retracted.** The phrase governs a *missing primitive*, one not held in Γ. A simplified explanation is a transform, not an attempt to mint a seventh primitive. Naming an atom already held is a binding, not an invention. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none). Both readings are kept so the correction is visible.

### 5b. Agreement is a convention here, not a verdict

*Arrival of the Fittest* says *"You may all agree and be wrong."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds that a group is only worth having while its members stay different, and that the group's agreement is never the verdict. Fig 2 :41 makes the distinction canonical: agreement is *"efficient for a convention"* and *"disqualifying for a verdict"*. So an audience that agrees a simplified explanation is clear has settled the shared meaning, which is a legitimate product. It has not settled whether the explanation is true at the ground. F4 and F5 depend on keeping those two offices apart.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well-known communicator can get a simplification tried. They cannot make it correct. Selecting reviewers for how likely they are to agree with a simplification is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the caveat that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` through `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any science communication claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle HIS-0076.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The right output of this solution is not a recommended reading level, a template, or a list of best practices. It is a shape with a measuring instrument attached: report the round-trip gap, keep the bonds that decide behaviour, measure the receiver's residual per slot against a ground the receiver can reach, and refuse to let a self-produced clarity metric decide the verdict. The essays supply the reasoning for why that is the honest stop; the figures supply the authority for the shape itself.
