# WATSON answer, HIS-0076

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

When does simplifying an explanation make knowledge accessible, and when does it distort the underlying uncertainty?

Restated as an effect: what happens to R_s = |Γ_R(b,a) - o'| at the receiver, per claim and per decision, when the source explanation is passed through a compressing transform, and what happens to R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), the gap the coarser description cannot hold? R is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The anchor is the ground that settles the receiver's prediction, named in Fig 2 :29. The two outcomes are accessibility (receiver R falls and the gap is reported) and distortion (a low reported R coexists with an unreported gap or a dropped bond).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a four-place relation among source explanation, simplified explanation, receiver state and ground, carrying an ordered transform, a comparison against the anchor and a threshold for live slots. Symmetry: not symmetric, because direction of travel matters (Fig 4 :22). Scale: the explainer loop, the transmission and the receiver loop are separated by lossy membranes (Fig 4 :27).

|---|---|---|---|---|

Union, not partition: D1, D2 and D3 form one compound transmission; accessibility and distortion overlap in D2 (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). Simplification sits at the vocabulary and objective links.

> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)
> *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"* (Fig 4 :31)

Reading: loss is necessary and neutral; unreported loss and dropped bonds are the distortion mechanism. Two short accounts over the same facts with different relations are isomers. Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for explanations. Refuter: a documented case where removing a relation changes neither prediction nor uncertainty judgment at the ground.

**D1 Source frame, status triangulated (Fig 4 and Fig 5, methods vs bargains).**

Reading: the accessible artefact is a generator the receiver can reuse, not a recording of one occasion. Mode `general`. Refuter: a recorded conclusion that works on novel cases without re-derivation and with zero unexplained remainder.

Reading: the same simplification is not the same explanation for two receivers. Accessibility is a property of receiver and habitat. Mode `specified`. Refuter: identical per-slot R changes across receivers whose prior knowledge measurably differs.

Reading: clarity, coverage and engagement are budget filters. The verdict is receiver R against a ground the receiver can reach. Mode `general` for the office rule, `specified` for this application. Refuter: a frame-internal metric shown to be the ground that settles per-slot R.

Reading: the two conditions are separate and overlapping. Audience agreement settles a convention, not the truth of the account.

**Naming the best simplification for a named audience, status `gap`.** No figure line governs this after a genuine re-census. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Figure-derived verdict:** a simplification makes knowledge accessible when a method crosses rather than a recording, the bonds that decide behaviour are kept, the round-trip gap is reported, and the receiver's residual falls per slot against a ground the receiver can reach. It distorts the underlying uncertainty when any of these is missing while a frame-internal metric reports success. The two conditions are a union, kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The deficit-model and measurement literature corroborates the receiver-side condition; cognition and education sources corroborate the representation and receiver-dependence claims; they are secondary and carry no verdict weight. The best simplification for a named audience remains a declared `gap`.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A distortion case caused by a dropped bond would be treated as a clarity problem and given more graphics, while an accessible case would be distrusted and made denser. Frame-internal metrics would be allowed to decide a verdict they cannot settle. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, except where a public decision has already been made on a distortion.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: (1) one scalar accounts for both outcomes with no per-slot residual and no gap; (2) a recorded conclusion works on novel cases with zero unexplained remainder; (3) removing relations changes no prediction; (4) a frame-internal metric is the ground that settles receiver R; (5) identical simplifications produce identical per-slot changes across receivers with differing prior knowledge. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing the same results would also reverse it.
