## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are" (Fig 2 :31) and "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration.
