# What "shape" means for this question

Canonical reading from the eight essays, checked against the actual pages on 2026-11-23. The essays are by Isaiah N (Nwukor), 2026, mostly on adventuresinml.substack.com, plus the Medium architecture article. The figures themselves are primary; the essays are the philosophical grounding for the word "shape".

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (the line continues that it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it).

From *The Library of Babel: Locating The Book of Books* (opened 2026-11-23): "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is: *a representation is a frame whose bonds fix reachable inference; the effect is per slot; it adds no atom and does not settle the ground; surplus requires differing closures.* Any specific claim that "a scatterplot always beats a table" is a name, not a shape, and is not delivered by the framework.

## 2. How a shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: proximity and recursion. Start from the vocabulary you have, correlate it with recurring new terms, narrow. "This is a bootstrapping convergence process."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 to :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context* (as quoted and read in the exemplar `05`, and listed here as a source actually opened in the exemplar's run): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." I did not re-open this essay in this run; it is recorded with that honest qualification in `10_source_ledger.csv` and is used only for the outreach ordering, not for a figure verdict.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (same data, different reasoning) before any field was chosen. That ordering matters, Fig 9 :27 to :28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

For this question, the map/diagram/table examples are imports from the history of science and cognitive science frames. Their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They corroborate the figure shape; they do not name a new primitive, and convergence from a different field is "corroboration at maximum independence" (Fig 8 :50) only because the evidence pools really are different.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the discriminator rule: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The resolution in the color case was "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Two informationally equivalent representations are the same kind of projection: a reading that does not determine the source.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The question asks how representations shape reasoning "rather than merely display results." The source problem shows why that opposition is not settled by inspection. A reading (the display) does not determine its source (the arrangement that produced it, or the theory the reader brought to it). Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." A legend declares; an instrument settles.

In the Snow case this is checkable. The Science History Institute (opened 2026-11-23) reports that the ghost map "could be used to argue that living near a pump that emitted a foul odor caused cholera", so the same arrangement supported a miasmatic reading until Snow "needed to prove that the water had to be ingested for cholera to spread." Brody et al. (*The Lancet*, 2000, first-page text read via the indexed excerpt; publisher fetch returned 400) state: "The map did not give rise to the insight, but rather it tended to confirm theories already held by the various investigators." The display was a projection; the ground required a separate instrument (the cesspit excavation, the water-company comparison table).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular representation as the cause of a reasoning change?** Not the framework. The framework returns a shape: a representation is a frame; the bond carries the difference; the effect is per slot; reach extends without new atoms; the ground is not moved; surplus needs differing closures. A specific causal claim about a specific visualization in a specific task is licensed only by a controlled experiment (ground) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the investigator's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the "display only" view commit an error the README forbids?** It commits the flat-form error of Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." It also aggregates away the per-slot residual (Fig 1 :22). What remains correct in it is the humbler half: a representation adds no atom to the world (Fig 6 :26), so it cannot manufacture truth. Both halves are recorded; neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that visualization matters is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried by people who can refute it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on. And: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."
