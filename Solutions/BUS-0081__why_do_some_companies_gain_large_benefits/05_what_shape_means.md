## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135 says it *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0081 the shape is the five-condition conjunction in `01` section 1.3, that is, a kind of thing any high-benefit slot must be. A named winner (a specific platform, a specific operating model, a specific percentage of productivity gain) is a name, and names are withheld.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* The essay also names the bundle failure directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the governing line for treating this question as a union (Fig 9 :54-57).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual was stated as `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` and not as "failed digital transformation".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The same essay warns that writing carries context and some subtext but almost never pretext, so a best-practice document cannot carry the history that made the practice work. That is the shape of F6 in plain language.
- Fig 9 :32 again: the search returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The stop is where instruments can no longer distinguish conditions, with the lineage recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question that means a per-slot market measurement after the tool is in place, not an adoption count.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

*They're Scanning Books* (opened this session) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A firm's declared tool stack is such a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's list.

## 4. Why a benefit reading cannot settle its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the same reading could come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And the conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A firm that reports "productivity went up after the tool" has read the projection. The source could be the bond arrangement, a changed market, a channel that happened to stay reachable, an import from a differing closure, or a habitat that was reproduced by accident. Several paths create the same reading. What is owed is a per-slot discriminator, not a name for the tool.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And the prestige rule that governs outreach: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

## 5. ANSWERED: what does the framework return here?

**A conjunction of conditions and a refuter, not a winner.** The framework can say that arrangement, contact, reach and habitat reproduction must be examined per slot, and it can say what each failure would look like. It cannot say which platform wins, which operating model is best, or what fraction of benefit is attributable to technology. The source-discriminating instrument is not held here. That is a `gap`, recorded as a verdict and not as silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).

**Does naming "bonds", "channel" or "habitat" commit the forbidden error?** No, and this is the correction in section 6. These are held atoms or held relations of the framework, not missing primitives invented to fill a slot. What remains forbidden is claiming that naming them settles the source of a particular firm's gain, or that naming a bought tool moves the wall without provenance and a shadow test.

## 6. Correction to my own §3/§4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in `Gamma`. "Bond", "channel", "anchor" and "habitat" are already held in the framework and in the figure vocabulary. Naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in §4 if read strongly is the claim that naming a source from a benefit reading settles anything. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The same correction applies to the business literature. Calling complementarity, absorptive capacity or dynamic capabilities by name is a binding from the economics/IS frame onto held bonds and reach. It is not an invented seventh term and it is not a wall-moving import. It carries zero verdict weight until a source gate and a shadow test supply the per-slot provenance (Fig 8 :50-52).

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** A study with famous authors, a vendor with a famous customer list, or a journal with a high impact factor can fund an action by giving it a budget to be tried. It cannot settle the action. The legitimate ask is a per-slot refutation, not an endorsement (see `02_outreach_email.md`).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
