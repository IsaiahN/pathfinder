## 1. Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. The residual line I worked from was the effect statement only: *why does the gap between what a firm's routines predict a newly bought technology will return and what its market actually returns remain small in some slots and grow in others when the technology atom is the same?*

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A three-place relation, firm `Gamma`, action `a`, market return `o'`, indexed by slot `s` and ordered by time. It carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not a symmetric one (Fig 12 :72, :87). The benefit is a threshold, not an absolute. Scale: one firm loop, a population ranking, and a time channel across adoption. The membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).
- **Domain 1, adoption loop.** Actors: managers, routines, the tool, `Gamma`. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized market return per slot (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **Domain 2, market habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, entrants, suppliers, regulators. Currency: survival, share, willingness to pay. Anchor: customer choice. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, composition library.** Actors: tool, data, skills, routines, decision rights and the bonds among them. Currency: reach and `|phi|`. Anchor: settlement against the ground (Fig 5 :32). Admitted.
- **Domain 4, measurement channel.** Actors: metrics, instruments, percepts. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **Rejected candidate, technology as a free-floating cause.** No anchor independent of the firm's realized outcome. Not a domain yet. This matches the primary's X1.
- **Absorbed candidate, industry averages.** Already inside D2 and D4; also an average dissolves the slot signal (Fig 1 :22). Matches the primary's X2.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D3 overlap because `Gamma` lives inside the loop; D2/D4 overlap because contact is exercised inside the habitat. Each overlap is a compound with its own loop. The whole is a union of arrangement, vocabulary/objective, channel, reach and habitat reproduction (Fig 9 :54-57). Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its rejection of X1 and its union reading. Where the primary derived D3 from the bond and isomer lines of Fig 12, I derived it from Fig 8 :19-21 (an undirected union with no described residual is mute) and Fig 6 :32 (an instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is low overlap **between routes**, not two evidence pools; the agreement is still one pool talking.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5, :46, :117 (ingredients, bonds, isomer), Fig 11 :39-40, :66-70 (capability and habitat), Fig 10 :24-26 (channel), Fig 6 :23 (reach), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` section 8 and `07` section 2: same tools are same atoms, benefit is the arrangement, keep the channel reachable, import from a differing closure, reproduce the pilot habitat, keep verdicts separate.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete consulting answer of the form: buy platform X, set up a centre of excellence, run an agile rollout and track utilisation.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim (real buyers never hold identical sets, so the strong form needs a controlled comparison and the weak form is a functional group);
- the **source gate and shadow test** on imports (Fig 8 :50-52), which the term centre of excellence silently drops, turning a provenanced binding into a named success factor;
- the **non-updating anchor** (Fig 2 :29, :33-34), which the metric track utilisation replaces with an instrument that moves when the firm pushes it;
- the **union structure** (Fig 9 :55, :57), which a single rollout lever flattens into one cause, leaving the intersections standing.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the per-slot indexing of the residual, and the refusal to treat the tool alone as the cause. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
