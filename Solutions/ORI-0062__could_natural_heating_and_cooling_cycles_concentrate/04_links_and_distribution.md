## 3. Distribution rules, figure-grounded

- **Send a generator, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A package containing the shape claim and its refuter is a method a reader can run. A package containing a favourite hot-spring story is a recording of one occasion.
- **Verdict before theory.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Send the three refuters first. Show the figures only after the reader has recorded what they would count as a failure.
- **Describe before choosing.** Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what an answer would have to look like before offering a setting.
- **Do not select for agreement.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list of people already inclined to agree is a convention, not corroboration.
- **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Journal scope, article charges and abstract deadlines decide where a claim gets tried, not whether it is true.
- **Opt-in only.** Public institutional submission and contact routes are listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. The legal failure and the structural failure have the same remedy: use channels where the recipient has opted in.

## 4. The two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the three conditions and the three refuters from `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the framework links in section 1, with the note that the figures, not our summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer THE FORMULA as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives a check without the framework in view. That is what `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is for. Do not attach the architecture to the first message.

## 5. Hosting and sending

The files in this folder are local. Nothing here uploads, hosts or sends anything. If the package is hosted, prefer an opt-in download page over an attachment pushed to a list, so a recipient can decline contact. The arrow points from the refuter to the claim, never from a claimed result to a search for agreement.
