# Solution ORI-0062: Tether outreach, figure-grounded package

**Question:** Could natural heating and cooling cycles concentrate molecules and encourage reactions?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 88 (question 62, section 7 "Energy, environment, and chemical cycles").

**Mode:** `specified` on the structural conditions (necessary shape of any working cycle), `general` on the union/unbundling claim. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement reported in this package settles the question.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

## Question restated as an effect

In a dilute prebiotic mixture, molecules stay dispersed and reactions are too slow to build the molecules life needs. Natural heating and cooling (day/night, geothermal, wet-dry, freeze-thaw) are proposed as the mechanism that closes that gap. Two options: (A) answer yes or no to thermal cycling in general; (B) describe the shape any thermal cycle must have in order to concentrate and to promote reactions, and leave the naming of a setting to the ground. This package takes B. Taking A would state a verdict in `general` mode without a ground reading, and it would hide that concentration (transport) and reaction promotion (kinetics) are two conditions joined by a third, retention between cycles.

**What an answer would look like:** a checkable conjunction of necessary conditions, each with a refuter, not a single yes/no. Bound that as the ordinary term "the shape of a working cycle" (Fig 5 :65-66, make the sought-for shape an ordinary term so it can accrue residual).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
