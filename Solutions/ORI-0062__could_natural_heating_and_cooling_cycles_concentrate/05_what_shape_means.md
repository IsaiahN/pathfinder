## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and stops where instruments can no longer distinguish, with the lineage recorded. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". The Library of Babel essay states the same thing in search terms: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the set of necessary conditions any working thermal cycle must satisfy, statable before anyone names a hot spring, a tidal pool, snow, or a geothermal field. It constrains; it does not fill. The sought-for shape is the ordinary term "the shape of a working cycle". `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" requires making it an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, then let it be refuted. Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 2 :29 defines the anchor as whatever settles the question without moving when a frame pushes on it. Here that is a measured concentration factor and yield under a declared cycling regime, not expert assent.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). They're Scanning Books: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." The Brownian ratchet is such an import: physics says a cycle at uniform temperature gives no net transport, which explains the Fig 12 :121 sign condition in effect terms. It adds no new primitive.

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap states the limit on the whole exercise: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This reading is therefore a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 4. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I first read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any specific mechanism at all, and nearly wrote the answer as a purely formal statement with no chemistry in it. That was an overreading, and it is retracted here rather than silently dropped.

The canonical phrase governs **a missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. Its subject is "a missing thing". Applied to this question:

- The six primitives (Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds.") include gradient as a held structure. Naming a spendable gradient as one condition is a **binding** from the chemistry frame onto a held atom, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall.
- The retention substrate and the regenerating surface come from Fig 12 :107-110 and :119 as structural conditions. They are figure-derived shape, not new members of the six.
- What is genuinely not held in Γ is the **setting**. Hot spring, tidal pool, ice, geothermal field and pore geometry are actors read off the world (Fig 11 :46 "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain" and Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."). None is a held atom here. Naming one as the answer is exactly the forbidden move: an atom introduced without import and without a ground reading.

So the corrected rule is sharper than my first one. The necessary conditions are shape and may be named as bindings onto held atoms, with provenance. The setting is a name and is refused until the ground settles it or a frame is imported with a shadow test. This matches the QMGR-0037 correction, where naming Gradient was allowed as a binding onto a held atom but claiming it was an import that moved the wall was not.

My §3 metamerism-style point is **not** retracted: the Hard Problem is About Source says "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A measured yield alone does not tell you which path concentrated the reactants. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates paths, not a name guessed from a yield. Both the corrected §4 and §3 now stand.

## 5. Why the answer stops where it does

Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." No experiment in this package failed; none was run. The absence of a demonstrated pathway in a setting is not evidence that no pathway exists there. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap is explicit about the status of this kind of output: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." The testable version would have to be derived from the real architecture or by working the formula directly. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and the rest of the folder are internal-consistency checks against the figures. **They cannot settle the question they report on.**

## 6. Consequence for the outreach

The ask is not agreement with a preferred setting. It is a check of the shape: are the three refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` the right ones, and is there a measurement that would separate concentration from reaction promotion from retention? Prestige cannot substitute for that. From the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
