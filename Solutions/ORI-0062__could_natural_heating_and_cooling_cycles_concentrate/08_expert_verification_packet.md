## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Could natural heating and cooling cycles concentrate molecules and encourage reactions?" is not one question. It is three conditions joined: concentration (a transport and separation effect), reaction promotion (a kinetics effect), and retention of the product between cycles (the record that lets the next cycle start from what the last one built). A single lever can clear one slot and leave the others standing. From that split, four necessary structural conditions follow for any cycle that actually concentrates: the loop must not close; the gradient must be spendable; a substrate must record the product across the cycle boundary; and a surface or catalyst must regenerate. Heating alone does not change contact, concentration does. The framework returns this shape, not the name of a setting. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the measurements that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. The residual is a union compound, not one question

- **Statement:** concentration, reaction promotion and retention are three conditions with different currencies and different instruments. No single intervention moves all three in every tested setting.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Mode:** `general`. Triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 3 (union rule versus dependency chain, low overlap).
- **Refuter:** show that concentration, reaction promotion and retention are one condition with one lever, so that a single intervention moves all three together in every tested setting.

### C2. A cycle concentrates only if its sign is non-closing and its gradient is spendable

- **Statement:** a perfectly reversible thermal cycle returns to its start and nets zero accumulation. Concentration requires an unreturned asymmetry between the hot and cold halves, and a temperature difference with directedness that can be spent. A bare ordering is not a driver.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 13 :31: "each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next". Fig 13 :46: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain".
- **Mode:** `specified` on the non-closing and spendable clauses, `general` on the habitat clause. Triangulated across Fig 12, Fig 13 and Fig 11 within the framework; the Brownian ratchet is one low-overlap world witness, not a second figure.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a perfectly symmetric thermal cycle with no retained asymmetry that still produces net accumulation per cycle; or show concentration and reaction proceeding at uniform temperature with no surface or phase asymmetry and no external source beyond thermal noise at equilibrium.

### C3. Accumulation requires a substrate that records the product and a surface that regenerates

- **Statement:** if the product survives the cycle boundary (oligomer on a mineral surface, brine in a pore, vesicle in a film), the next cycle starts from a head start and the effect compounds. If the record is dropped and the product diffuses back to bulk, every cycle re-pays the full cost. A part that survives its own use behaves differently from a part that is spent.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :107: "Whether that cost must be paid again depends on the substrate: where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once." Fig 12 :109: "Which boundary matters is the one the record does not cross. Inside a run the arrangement persists and nothing is re-earned; across a boundary that drops it, everything is." Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Mode:** `specified`, single-source within the framework, corroborated and qualified by world evidence (Ross and Deamer 2016 found Le Chatelier shifts insufficient; Hassenkam and Deamer 2022 found an 80 °C regimen produced polymer-like products where room temperature gave irregular strings; Šimonis et al 2026 found one Icelandic hot-spring water degraded 95% of AMP over sixteen cycles).
- **Refuter:** show a cycling system in which the record is deliberately dropped between cycles (product fully dispersed, no surface or pore retention) that accumulates product across cycles at the same rate as a retention-enabled control; or show sustained accumulation with no surface, catalyst or regenerating structure where homogeneous kinetics alone account for the yield.

The three sub-claims are conjunctive. Failing any one withdraws the shape claim; it does not merely weaken it.

## 5. What would settle it

A measurement, not a further argument. Specifically:

1. Net concentration factor and product yield per cycle under a declared amplitude, period, surface and retention regime.
2. The same run with the record deliberately dropped between cycles. This is the decisive comparison for C3.
3. A fixed-chemistry comparison across temperature regimens (room temperature versus 80 °C), which separates C2 and C3.
4. A uniform-temperature control with no surface or phase asymmetry.

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. If any refuter in section 2 fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or re-censused, and the correction is kept visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54 and :57, Fig 12 :121 and :109, and Fig 13 :46. Five short passages, verbatim in section 2.
2. Check C1 against your own field: are concentration, yield and retention actually measured with different instruments in the cycling literature, or is one usually assumed from another?

4. Name one instrument that would separate a retained product from a re-paid cost per cycle, or say why none is available.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
