# The Tether's answer on natural heating and cooling cycles

**Mode: `specified` on the structural conditions, `general` on the unbundling claim. Nothing here is `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No experiment was run for this package.

## 1. The residual it answers

In a dilute, ambient, well-mixed prebiotic mixture, molecules stay below the concentration and yield needed to build the molecules life needs. Thermal cycling is proposed to close that gap. The question does not name a setting, a period or a molecule; it asks about a mechanism class.

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Three slots are kept separate and never averaged (Fig 2 :29):

- **Slot 1, concentration.** A transport and separation effect. Local density per cycle.
- **Slot 2, reaction promotion.** A kinetics effect. Yield per cycle.
- **Slot 3, retention.** A record across the cycle boundary, which decides whether the next cycle starts from what the last one built.

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A single lever (raise the amplitude, lengthen the hot half) can clear one slot while leaving the others standing. A global yes or no is not an answer to this question; it is a verdict that hides a union.

## 2. The shape: four necessary conditions

Verdicts are kept per domain, never averaged. Triangulation is between figures with low overlap in subject matter and is internal consistency, not external corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**C1, union unbundling. `general`. Triangulated (Fig 9 union rule plus Fig 3 dependency chain).**
The question is a union compound of concentration, kinetics and retention. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The likely unmeasured link is retention. Refuter: one lever moves all three together in every tested setting.

**C2, the loop must not close. `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 12 cycle structure plus Fig 13 recursive remainder).**
Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 13 :31: "each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next". A perfectly reversible thermal cycle returns to its start and nets zero accumulation. Concentration needs an asymmetry between halves: solvent removed but solute kept, ions excluded into brine, one species adsorbed and another released. Heating and cooling are ordered, not interchangeable (Operators table :31, :33). Refuter: a symmetric cycle with no retained asymmetry still produces net accumulation per cycle.

**C3, the gradient must be spendable and the habitat supplies it. `general` on the habitat clause, `specified` on the spendable clause. Single-source on the gradient, triangulated on the habitat (Fig 13 plus Fig 11, low overlap).**
Fig 13 :46: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain". A bare temperature difference is an ordering; it becomes a driver only when it can be spent through evaporation, phase separation or a surface that holds one species. Temperature is a habitat condition read off the world, not a seventh primitive (Fig 13 :43). Refuter: concentration and reaction proceed at uniform temperature with no surface or phase asymmetry and no external source beyond thermal noise at equilibrium.

**C4, a substrate records the product, and a surface regenerates. `specified`. Single-source on the figure, corroborated in the world.**
Fig 12 :107: "Whether that cost must be paid again depends on the substrate: where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once." Fig 12 :109: "Which boundary matters is the one the record does not cross. Inside a run the arrangement persists and nothing is re-earned; across a boundary that drops it, everything is." Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

If the product (an oligomer on a mineral surface, a concentrated brine in a pore, a vesicle in a film) survives into the next cycle, the effect compounds. If it diffuses back to bulk, every cycle starts from zero and re-pays. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Concentration changes contact; heating by itself does not. World corroboration is real but qualified: Ross and Deamer 2016 found simple Le Chatelier shifts insufficient, Hassenkam and Deamer 2022 found an 80 °C regimen produced polymer-like products where room temperature gave irregular strings, and Šimonis et al 2026 found one Icelandic hot-spring water destroyed 95% of AMP over sixteen cycles. The surface and water chemistry decide the sign. Refuter: a regime that drops its record between cycles accumulates at the same rate as a retention-enabled control.

**Governing condition on all four: no maintainer, so the ground decides. `general`. Triangulated (Fig 11 unmaintained mode plus Fig 2 anchor definition).**
Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Prebiotic chemistry has no seat and no designer. What persists is what the ground kept, so the verdict is a measurement of concentration factor and yield under a declared cycling regime, not agreement among researchers and not the elegance of a setting.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a global yes or no. That would be a `general`-mode verdict with no ground and with three conditions hidden.
- Not a name of a setting. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Hot spring, tidal pool, snow and geothermal field are bindings from D4, provenanced as such, not derivations from the six-term set.
- Not a sixth or seventh primitive. Temperature is a habitat condition and an ordering over energy; the gradient is already held.
- Not that heating alone increases contact. Concentration increases contact; heating alone does not (Fig 11 :40).
- Not a proof of absence. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No experiment in this package failed; none was run.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement reported here settles the question. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two (Fig 2 :40). Funding, tractability and venue are filters, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

- Run a cycling experiment with declared amplitude, period, surface and retention; measure net concentration factor and product yield per cycle.
- Compare a retention-enabled regime with a regime that deliberately drops the record between cycles. This is the measurement that separates C4 from its refuter.
- Vary temperature regimen at fixed chemistry (room temperature versus 80 °C) and check whether the predicted sign change in product appears. This separates C3 from its refuter.
- Test a uniform-temperature control with no surface or phase asymmetry. This separates C2 and C3 from their refuters.
- Report failures as data. Fig 5 :48: "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

If any pre-registered refuter fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or re-censused. The full list is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 and the checkable subset in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say yes or no to thermal cycling in general, and it does not name a setting. It says the question is three conditions joined: concentration, reaction promotion and retention of the product between cycles. Any working cycle must have an ordered hot and cold half whose loop does not close, a temperature difference that can actually be spent, and a substrate that records the product so the next cycle starts from a head start. Heating alone changes no contact; concentration does. The framework returns this shape, not a hot spring, and it stops where the ground must speak. It offers the shape, the refuters written in advance and the measurements that would close it. A simulated derivation checked for internal consistency is not a ground reading, so the setting stays open until an experiment settles it.
