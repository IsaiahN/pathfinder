## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** In a dilute, ambient, well-mixed prebiotic mixture, molecules stay below the concentration and yield needed to build the molecules life needs. Arity: three conditions, concentration (transport), reaction promotion (kinetics) and retention across the cycle boundary (a record). Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Symmetry: heating and cooling are ordered and non-interchangeable, so the cycle has a sign. Scale: macroscopic forcing driving molecular contact, a cross-scale contact to be checked against Fig 4 and not forced.
- **Domain 1, thermal forcing.** Actors: heat, cold, amplitude, period, geothermal flux, season. Currency: energy flow and gradient magnitude. Anchor: temperature time series and calorimetry. Real physical anchor, so it is a domain.
- **Domain 2, transport and concentration.** Actors: solvent, solute, evaporation, freezing, brine, pores, surfaces. Currency: concentration factor and chemical potential. Anchor: measured concentration and phase diagrams.
- **Domain 3, prebiotic kinetics.** Actors: monomers, oligomers, condensation and hydrolysis, mineral surfaces. Currency: rate and yield. Anchor: laboratory product distributions.
- **Domain 4, habitat and setting.** Actors: hot springs, ice, tidal pools, volcanic ponds. Currency: cycling regularity and stability. Anchor: field measurements and the geological record.
- **Domain 5, framework-internal composition.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, negation. Currency: composition in closure(Γ) and settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. This is the derivation frame, not a sixth world domain.

Union, not partition: D1 through D4 are world domains that must all hold; D5 is applied to the residual. Dependency order: forcing -> spendable gradient -> transport -> concentration -> molecular contact -> reaction -> retention -> the next cycle starts from the accumulated product. Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not.

Strain test applied before concluding. My first instinct put retention under the Fig 4 membrane. It strains: Fig 4 governs crossings between sizes, while retention is a record within one scale. Re-census sends retention to Fig 12 :109 and demotes Fig 4 to an analogue. My second instinct made temperature a seventh primitive. It strains: temperature is a habitat condition and an ordering over energy, while energy and gradient are already held. Re-census keeps the six unchanged. Both first readings are preserved in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives as a `specified`, ungrounded candidate; a global yes or no is refused. The question is a union compound of concentration, reaction promotion and retention. Any working cycle must have an ordered hot and cold half whose loop does not close, a spendable temperature gradient, a substrate that records the product across the cycle boundary, and a surface that regenerates. Heating alone changes no contact; concentration does. No setting is named, because none is held in Γ and no ground reading was taken here. Verdicts per domain: D1 forcing, triangulated and `general`; D2 concentration, triangulated and `specified`; D3 kinetics, corroborated and world-qualified, `specified`; D4 setting, `gap`, since no figure line governs which setting is the answer; D5 composition, triangulated on the union and single-source on the retention clause. The second seat agrees, but shares the evidence pool, so that agreement is internal consistency only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is stated with three refuters before any theory, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a favourite setting or a global verdict instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by using opt-in public venues.
- **undoable:** yes. The setting slot is already open, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refuter that fires can be recorded and the shape narrowed or withdrawn without touching the six-term substrate. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state the corrected form. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A controlled cycling experiment with declared amplitude, period, surface and retention that measures net concentration and yield would settle the claim. Specifically: a symmetric cycle with no retained asymmetry that still accumulates product per cycle breaks the non-closing condition; a regime that drops the record between cycles accumulating at the same rate as a retention-enabled control breaks the retention condition; concentration and reaction at uniform temperature with no surface or phase asymmetry breaks the spendable-gradient condition; one lever moving all three conditions together breaks the union reading. A source-discriminating instrument showing which path produced a measured yield would name the slot. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
