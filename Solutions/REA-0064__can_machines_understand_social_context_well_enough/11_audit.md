## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: three places plus a field, speaker, utterance, hearer, shared context. A two-place "text in, meaning out" census drops the field and is the wrong domain. Symmetry: intended versus literal is a comparison, and the three named phenomena use different typed bonds, so they are not interchangeable. Scale: step, turn, relationship, seat, lineage. Union: the three conditions overlap in the habitat and form a compound, not three separate questions.
- **Domain 1, the signal.** Actors: tokens, literal semantics. Currency: description bits. Anchor: none for intent. On its own this is not a verdict-bearing domain; it is a sub-frame of Domain 3. I agree with the primary's demotion.
- **Domain 2, the machine's record.** Actors: library Γ, priors, world knowledge. Currency: per-slot residual and description length. Anchor: a benchmark the frame also produces, so frame-internal.
- **Domain 3, the social habitat.** Actors: speaker, hearer, shared attention, relationship, convention, correction. Currency: coordination success. Anchor: the outcome the hearer acted on. Capability is agent-and-habitat here.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: outside readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Refuse the bundle before minting; bind the sought-for shape; describe the ground before choosing a frame; settle link 3 before links 4 and 5; only then decide on import.
