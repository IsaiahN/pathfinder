## 1. The residual, in effect terms

A machine is handed an utterance plus context and must produce the meaning the speaker expected a hearer to recover. "It is cold in here" is not a report about temperature but a request to close the window. "Nice job" after a failure is not praise. A punchline violates the literal expectation and is resolved by a reinterpretation. What fails is not the token string. What fails is the required interpretation, and the gap is on a specific object slot: the intended meaning, as distinct from the literal composition.

Stated as the loop's error: `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot, where Γ composes the literal reading from the utterance and the machine's library, and `o'` is the hearer's recovered meaning. The question asks whether R can be driven to zero. It does not say which slot, which ground, or which system. That omission is itself the first finding. `[general]`

**Arity, symmetry, scale** (Fig 9 :22 names these three ranges):

- **Arity:** three places plus a field. Speaker, utterance, hearer, with a shared context that the utterance leans on. A two-place reading (text in, meaning out) drops the shared field and is the wrong census.
- **Symmetry:** speaker and hearer are not interchangeable in the direction of composition, but the intended meaning is a comparison between literal and intended, and the recovery is a production from context. Indirect speech, humor, and implied meaning are not one symmetric relation; Fig 12's bond table separates sequence, comparison, subtraction, and negation.
- **Scale:** step (one utterance), cycle (a conversational turn), level (a relationship with history), seat (the hearer's stance), lineage (a cultural convention). A verdict at one scale does not transfer to another without a stated transform.

**Domain census** (one line per domain: actors / currency / anchor). No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the signal | tokens, literal semantics, syntax | bits of description | the text itself plus corpus; no anchor for intent here |
| D2, the machine's record | library Γ, priors, world knowledge | residual R and description length | benchmark loss, which the machine also produces, so frame-internal |
| D3, the social habitat | speaker, hearer, shared attention, conventions, culture | coordination success (window closed, laugh returned) | the outcome that settles whether the interpretation was right |
| D4, verification | expert readers outside the frame | residual and agreement | the ground, never agreement; in this simulated run independence is zero |

**Partition or union.** D2 and D3 form a seam (Fig 10: two frames, one field, no shared reading). The three named phenomena form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not three separate questions and not one hard question. A single lever that clears literal decoding leaves the intersections standing, because a second condition still binds (Fig 9 :54-57). D1 on its own is not a domain for this question: it has no anchor for intended meaning, so it is a sub-frame of D3, not a verdict-bearing domain.

**Order by dependency** (Fig 3): perception of utterance and context, then vocabulary for what an utterance affords (request, irony, implicature), then an objective stated in the system's own terms (coordination success), then a route to interpretation, then carry and verify. The chain typically breaks between vocabulary and objective, the middle link that has neither obvious failures nor obvious instruments (Fig 3 :25-27). Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (decisive for the form of the answer)

`Fig 9 :54-57` "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
`Fig 9 :57` "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

`Fig 12` separates the bonds: indirect speech is a production or sequence, humor is a comparison whose resolution is the point, implied meaning is a subtraction of the literal from the intended. Under `Fig 12 :117` "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The Library of Babel essay says the same in plain words: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."

So "can machines understand social context" is not one yes/no question. It is three conditions that overlap in the habitat. One lever that makes a model better at literal paraphrase clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. `[general]`

- **Mode:** `general`, structural.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 unbundle plus Fig 12 bond table plus an essay with a different evidence route but high author overlap).
- **Refuter:** exhibit one bond, one operand pair, and one ground that governs all three named phenomena with no loss, and show that a single per-slot residual is unnecessary.

### F2 - The anchor is the coordination outcome, not the benchmark score

`Fig 2 :29-30` "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
`Fig 2 :35` "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
`Fig 1 :23` "The ground enters as the only metric."
`Fig 1 :27` "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

A benchmark for pragmatic interpretation is produced by the same community that optimises models against it. If it moves when the frame pushes on it, `Fig 2 :30` says it is not an anchor. The anchor for "did the hearer recover the intended meaning" is the outcome: the window was closed, the reply was a fitting laugh, the false implicature was corrected. `[specified]`

- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 1 (low overlap: one is the checking arrangement, one is the agent's metric rule).
- **Refuter:** name an instrument for intended meaning that does not move when a model or a lab pushes on its score, and show it settling a disputed case.

### F3 - Per-slot verdicts, never an average

`Fig 1 :19` "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
`Fig 1 :22` "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A single accuracy figure on "social context understanding" throws away the only signal that says where to look. A system can score well on indirect requests and fail completely on irony while the average looks healthy. This is a legal state under Fig 1 :13 and :20, and it is the state an average conceals. `[general]`

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 plus the Fig 9 unbundle for the same reason at a different scale).
- **Refuter:** give a scalar summary that preserves the per-slot ordering and predicts which slot will fail next without reading the slots.

### F4 - Text-only closure cannot mint the social primitive by more search

`Fig 6 :21` "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
`Fig 6 :23` "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
`Fig 8 :32` "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
`Fig 8 :39` "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

The Decoding Tacit Knowledge essay, read this session, is direct: "text is mostly context with some subtext and almost no pretext"; "If intelligence is something each mind rebuilds through contact, then text alone can't hand it over"; and "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy." Under the figures this is a reachability claim, not a claim of impossibility in principle. `[specified]`

**Strain test.** The scope note at Fig 8 :40-43 separates underivable-in-principle from unreachable-at-this-budget. Reading F4 as "no machine can ever understand humor" strains the line: it governs the machine's closure with the primitives it actually holds, not all possible closures. Re-census: D2 is the machine's Γ as it stands, D3 is the frame whose closure differs. The verdict is "not reachable from this closure without contact", which is different from "unreachable at this budget" (more search) and from "underivable in principle" (go outside). This is the third question Fig 6 :48-53 names. No strain remains after the re-census.

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 plus Fig 8, with Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists.").
- **Refuter:** compose an out-of-closure pragmatic primitive from a text-only library and show it changing a per-slot coordination outcome.

### F5 - Capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone

`Fig 11 :39` "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
`Fig 11 :40` "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
`Fig 11 :70` "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

The Theory of mind source opened this session records the exact analogue: "very simple robots that only react by reflexes and are not built to have any complex cognition at all can pass the tests for having theory of mind abilities", and asks whether such tests measure cognition at all. That is Fig 11 :70 in another field: a substituted habitat producing a synthetic solve. The habitat must be enumerated, never composed (`Fig 11 :43`: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."): speaker, hearer, shared attention, relationship, convention, correction, and the cascade out to culture. `[specified]`

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 plus the Fig 2 anchor rule: an outcome, not an internal score).
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the coordination ground without changing contact, or a demonstration that a synthetic solve transfers to an unscripted human interaction.

### F6 - "Well enough" is a preference held as a shape, not a term

`THE_FORMULA.md`, step 2: "a frame searching for an answer of a given SHAPE holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not a term. Unbound, predicting nothing, no residual - so nothing above can see it."
`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."
`Fig 13 :134-135` "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
`Fig 13 :138-139` "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."

"Understand social context well enough" has no bound threshold, predicts nothing, and accrues no residual. The loop can check the mechanism and cannot check the direction of its own question. We therefore bind the four-part shape in `README.md` before the run and let it be refusable. What the figures return is the kind of thing an answer must be, not the answer and not a name for the missing primitive. `[general]`

- **Status:** triangulated (THE_FORMULA plus Fig 13, with the canonical README's "a shape, not a name").
- **Refuter:** state a threshold for "well enough" as an ordinary bound term with its own residual, and show the ground settling it.

### F7 - The objective link is where the chain breaks

`Fig 3 :25-27` "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."
`Fig 3 :27` "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
`Fig 3 :19` "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

The field has strong instruments for perception (token accuracy) and for planning (task completion), and weak instruments for "what counts as progress in interpreting an indirect request". BIG-bench, read this session, reports that "tasks that exhibit 'breakthrough' behavior at a critical scale often involve multiple steps or components, or brittle metrics", and that "social bias typically increases with scale in settings with ambiguous context". Brittle metrics are a symptom of an unstated objective, not a solved one. `[specified]`

- **Status:** single-source for the figure claim (Fig 3), corroborated from a low-overlap engineering field (BIG-bench).
- **Refuter:** exhibit an instrument for pragmatic coordination progress, show the chain passing link 3, and show improvement above the break actually arriving there.

### F8 - The three phenomena are not interchangeable, and the ground can be mistyped

`Fig 12 :5` "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
`Fig 11 :36` "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."

Theories of humour, read this session, say there is "no consensus about which of these three theories of humor is most valid" and that incongruity, relief, and superiority each explain different aspects. Treating "humor" as one slot with one ground repeats the flat-formula error: the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not. A machine that detects incongruity and a machine that returns a fitting laugh are answering different questions. `[general]`

- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 bond rule plus Fig 11 ground rule).
- **Refuter:** show that the three bonds reduce to one typed relation with the same ground and the same falsifier.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Fig 10's design-instrument rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single typed relation, one ground, and one falsifier are exhibited that govern indirect speech, humor, and implied meaning without loss, so the union reading (F1) is false.
2. An instrument for intended meaning is produced that does not move when a model or a lab pushes on its score, and it settles a disputed case (breaks F2).
3. A text-only library composes a pragmatic primitive that was not in its atoms and that changes a per-slot coordination outcome (breaks F4).
4. An agent-only change moves the social coordination ground without changing contact (breaks F5, Fig 11 :72's standing refuter).

If any fires, the relevant finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Split the question into three loops before answering it. Keep the verdicts separate and never average them (F1, F3).
2. State the ground for each slot in effect terms: what would have to happen in the world for us to say the interpretation was recovered (F2).
3. Report contact, not just accuracy. A benchmark win is a synthetic solve unless it transfers to an unscripted interaction (F5).
4. Record the closure question separately from the budget question. Do not answer "unreachable" with more of the same corpus (F4 strain test).
5. Lead outreach with the shape and the refuter, not with the framework (F9). Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is this one question?** No. Three conditions, different bonds (Fig 9 :54, Fig 12 :117).
2. **What is the ground?** The coordination outcome, not the score (Fig 2 :29-30).
3. **What is the currency?** Per-slot residual and description length in D2, coordination success in D3 (Fig 1 :19; Fig 12 :126).
4. **Can text alone reach it?** Not from a closure that lacks the social primitive; more search inside the closure does not add an atom (Fig 8 :32).
5. **What moves the wall?** Contact with a frame whose closure differs; import with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23).
6. **Does a benchmark settle it?** No. A harness is a substituted habitat and proves wiring (Fig 11 :70).
7. **What closes the loop?** Refuse the blanket yes/no, bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, and stop at the instrument limit with a recorded lineage (Fig 13 :134-139; THE_FORMULA Direction).

## 7. Updated verdict

The figures do not return "yes" or "no" for machines in general. They return the shape any sufficient answer must have: unbundled into three slots, anchored on coordination outcomes rather than scores, reporting a per-slot residual, and stating whether the wall moved by contact with a different closure. A model whose library lacks social-contact primitives cannot reach pragmatic interpretation by more text of the same kind; that is a reachability verdict about a closure, not a proof of impossibility, and it is reversible by an import whose provenance is recorded. The blanket "machines cannot understand social context" is refused as a verdict stated in `general` mode without a ground. Strains after re-census: none hidden; the one live item is the shape of the imported primitive, which the framework explicitly cannot name.
