## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A machine composes a literal reading from an utterance and context; the hearer's recovered intended meaning is what was owed. Arity: three places plus a field (speaker, utterance, hearer, shared context). Symmetry: non-symmetric in composition direction; intended versus literal is a comparison, and the three named phenomena use different typed bonds. Scale: step, turn, relationship, seat, lineage. Fig 9 names the bundle a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the signal.** Actors: tokens, literal semantics, syntax. Currency: bits of description. Anchor: the text and a corpus, with no anchor for intent. Not a verdict-bearing domain on its own; a sub-frame of Domain 3.
- **Domain 2, the machine's record.** Actors: library Γ, priors, world knowledge. Currency: per-slot residual R and description length. Anchor: a benchmark loss produced by the frame itself, so frame-internal, not ground.
- **Domain 3, the social habitat.** Actors: speaker, hearer, shared attention, relationship history, convention, correction. Currency: coordination success, the outcome the hearer acted on. Anchor: that outcome. Capability here is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers outside the frame. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Domains 2 and 3 form a seam: two frames, one field, no shared reading. The three phenomena form a union of overlapping conditions, not three separate questions and not one hard question.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Refuse the bundle before minting; bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term; describe the ground in effect terms before choosing a theory; settle link 3 (the objective) before links 4 and 5; decide on import only after the objective is stated.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures do not return yes or no. They return the shape any sufficient answer must have. The question is a union of three conditions with different bonds, so it must be split before search and the verdicts kept separate, never averaged. The ground is the coordination outcome, not a benchmark a frame produces and optimises. A library built only from text cannot compose a social primitive it does not hold by searching that same library; this is a reachability verdict about a closure, not a proof of impossibility and not a budget verdict. Capability belongs to agent-and-habitat, so a synthetic solve proves wiring only. A search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence. The missing thing is characterised as contact with a frame whose closure differs and is not named. All of this is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so a wrong reading is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the whole framework before their verdict (Fig 10 :42), and importing an expensive named primitive against a budget limit when the actual defect is an unstated objective (Fig 8 :40-43). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The four refuters are written down, the bindings are recorded as provenance rather than derived fact, and no primitive has to be withdrawn to correct the answer. A superseding packet can replace this one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A blinded instrument that distinguishes a fluent guess from a recovered intention and does not move when pushed would name the slot; a scripted result that transfers unchanged to unscripted interaction would break the harness finding; a text-only composition that adds a pragmatic atom, or an agent-only change that moves the coordination ground without changing contact, would break the closure and capability findings. Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
