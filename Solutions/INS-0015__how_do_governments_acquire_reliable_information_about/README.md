# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package

**Question:** How do governments acquire reliable information about citizens, firms, and local conditions?

**Source:** `institutional questions.txt` line 20. **Section:** 2. State capacity and public administration.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the concrete institutional recipe. Nothing here is `grounded`. Every substantive file is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. By its own rule it cannot settle the question it reports on (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending.

**Field for public contact points:** government information gathering research (official statistics, survey methodology, public administration).

## What an answer would look like (the sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term)

Restate the question in effect terms. A government must choose actions (policy, enforcement, allocation) over a world it cannot see directly. It holds a belief `b` about citizens, firms and local conditions, predicts `Γ(b,a)`, acts, then observes `o'`. The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot `s`, where a slot is a citizen, a firm or a locality. "Reliable information" would be information that (i) keeps `R` small at each slot, (ii) is anchored to something that does not change its answer when the government pushes on it, and (iii) reaches the decision maker while the channel to that anchor stays reachable. An answer with those three properties is what the term is bound to. The binding makes the preference refusable (Formula, "Direction": write down what an answer would look like, bind it, let it accrue a residual). What no figure returns is which census design, register, sample size or agency chart achieves it; that is a declared `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade the label: "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Third, asking for trust before checking spends the only thing an outside frame contributes, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute them, not a purchased list of believers. Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are yours to do.
