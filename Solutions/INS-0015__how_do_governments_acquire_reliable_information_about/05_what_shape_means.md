## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :138: "And it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "keep the residual indexed per citizen, firm and locality, range it against a reference that does not update, keep the channel reachable, and install a checker that cannot be the producer" is a shape. "Adopt continuous registers every three years with a 5 percent audit sample" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then look for "recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it", and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30–31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

The method for this question was exactly that: describe the residual in effect terms (a gap between prediction and observation, per slot) before choosing any figure line or any world source. Choosing "official statistics" first would have made any literature seem to fit (Fig 9 :27–28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: saying "the ground is the price recorded in a transaction" is a binding from the market frame, chosen because the question is about prices. It must be recorded as a choice, not presented as a discovery of nature's own list. Saying "use this vendor's dashboard" without provenance and a shadow test is an import that moves no wall and may import a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).

## 4. Source, projection and the missing discriminator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most for government information. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

This is the exact failure mode of a published rate. A single number (an inflation rate, an unemployment rate, a poverty headcount) is a projection. Several sources can produce it: honest price collection, a changed sampling frame, an instruction to record a benefit as work, or an adjustment made to please a minister. Reading the source off the number is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (an independent series, a reinterview, a provenance record), not a confident attribution.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A statistical agency certifying its own figure is the named failure.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an instrument? ANSWERED, with a correction

**First pass (kept visible).** I read the rule as forbidding any concrete institutional name. On that reading the answer would be that no census, register or survey may be mentioned.

**Correction (§5b).** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in the library. Household surveys, administrative registers, price collectors and independent indices are **not missing primitives**; they are bindings already available in the institutional vocabulary. Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming that such a name is what the framework derived, or that adding it moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 : "IMPORT adds an atom" is about primitives). The correction narrows the gap in F9 of `01`: the recipe, ratio and institutional layout are still a `gap`, but the mention of real instruments is allowed and must be provenanced.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous statisticians or respected agencies to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let the ground settle it, and declare the unavoidable interest of whoever holds the seat (Fig 10 :59–60).

## 8. The three drift-stoppers, restated for this question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names three safeguards and each maps to a figure line:

1. **A ground both frames are corrected by, separately.** "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it" (Fig 2 :45). For this question: an independent measurement, not a committee agreement.
2. **Separate experience.** "Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained" (Fig 10 :80). For this question: a reading collected by a different instrument with a different incentive.
3. **Claims in effect terms.** An effect description "can be checked against the ground by anyone" (Fig 6 :30). For this question: "a price rose by X" is checkable; "confidence improved" is not, until its instrument is named.

The essay also gives the failure: "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice" (Fig 2 :34, "the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"). A reporting chain that ranges only against its own prior publications is that failure, and it is invisible from inside the chain.
