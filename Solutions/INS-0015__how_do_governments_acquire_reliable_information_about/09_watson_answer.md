## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about acquiring reliable information about citizens, firms and local conditions. Option A: treat "information quality" as one property of a report and look for the best reporting design. Option B: treat the question as a union of overlapping conditions and state the shape each must satisfy. What each leads to: A averages away the per-slot signal, invites a metric that updates on the policy it is used to steer, and pools agreement from one evidence pool; B returns a four-condition shape, five refuters written in advance, and a declared gap where no figure line governs the recipe.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what the government's model predicts and what it observes, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot, where a slot is a citizen, a firm or a locality. Arity: three object classes, each a set of slots. Symmetry: the ground corrects the report, the report cannot correct the ground (Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."). Scale: first order at slot, second order at published series (Fig 12 :104 permits a settled molecule to become an atom at the next level). It is a union compound: Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Domain 1, measurement and perception (D1).** Actors: households, firms, enumerators, instruments, registers. Currency: observations `o'`, per-slot residual `R_s`. Anchor: the ground, a measurement, a counter, a market (Fig 2 :29). Question type: partition boundary for which compositions cross from state to structure to reading.
- **Domain 2, the government's reporting frame (D2).** Actors: ministries, statistical offices, field offices. Currency: reports and agreed figures. Anchor: should be the same ground, but is at risk of mutual update (Fig 2 :13–15) or a common pool (Fig 2 :18–21). Question type: an anchor-integrity question, not a data-volume question.
- **Domain 3, outside frames (D3).** Actors: households' expectations, firms, markets, peer institutions, press. Currency: self-reports, behavioural traces, transactions, alternative series. Anchor: partial, imported with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :52). Question type: import and independence.
- **Domain 4, verification and the seat (D4).** Actors: auditors, courts, comptrollers, researchers. Currency: residuals surfaced, conventions authored. Anchor: the ground, never agreement, and the checker must be outside the slice it grades (Fig 10 :86). Question type: seat design.
- **Union and order.** Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54–58). Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); the middle links are the least instrumented (Fig 3 :27). Strain test applied: Fig 2's non-updating anchor governs references, Fig 11's residual-relative valence governs habitat actors, so no contradiction (re-census S1 in `01` §4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of four conditions, not one quality dial. The residual must stay indexed per citizen, firm and locality; the reference must not update on the report; readings must come from frames whose evidence pools do not overlap; and the checker must be outside the slice it grades, unable to produce the claim, with its unavoidable interest declared. The chain breaks between perception and planning, at vocabulary and objective, where there is no instrument. Over a fixed atom set the familiar instruments are isomers differing by typed bond, so type, not quantity, is the variable. The recipe (sample size, interval, disclosure rule, agency chart) is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as `unreachable at this budget` rather than as silence. Everything here is `specified` or `general`, ungrounded, and a candidate. Prestige and agreement are not instruments that can move it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is used as designed. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, and spending public budget on a metric that updates on the policy it is meant to steer. Fig 2 :42 prices the first: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The Argentina case prices the second in credibility and in a suspended series. Neither cost is unavoidable; both are avoided by leading with the shape and its refuters and by naming the discriminator.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions are properties of a measurement arrangement, not a fixed regime; an independent reading, a provenance record, a source gate and a refusal seat can be added without replacing a statistical system. A published wrong claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a refutation adds a term that refuses while removing nothing from the record (Fig 6 :42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent reinterview showing that a national aggregate hides a live per-locality residual; a moving-ground instrument showing that a target metric stayed non-updating; a design-effect test showing two readings share no pool; a protocol in which a producer verifies no better than an outsider; an instrument that selects among concrete census designs and disclosure rules for a stated residual; or a figure re-census that produces a line naming the recipe without strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. A source-discriminating instrument is owed for the metamerism problem: a published rate alone cannot comment on its source.
