# The Tether's answer: how governments acquire reliable information

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the institutional recipe. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A government must act on a world it cannot see. It predicts what it will observe, acts, and compares. "Reliable information" is the name we give the case where that comparison stays small at each citizen, firm and locality, where the reference does not move when the government pushes on it, and where the channel bringing the reading back stays reachable. The residual is per slot, not a national average: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The question is a **union compound**, not one problem with several labels. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." One lever clears the region where a single condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 2. The shape: four conditions, verdicts kept separate

**D1, measurement and perception: `specified`, triangulated.**
The reading must stay indexed per slot. A national figure near zero with one live locality is a legal state (Fig 1 :13), not a success. Systematic error accumulates rather than washing out at scale: the Census Bureau's *Measures of Nonsampling Error* says "bias caused by systematic errors cannot be reduced by increasing the sample size." Aggregation can reverse the slot-level result (Simpson's paradox). The verdict is about the object of measurement, not about whether aggregates are ever useful.

**D2, the reporting frame: `specified`, triangulated.**
The reference must not update on what the government wants to hear. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30 spans two lines: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :34: "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." A metric used to decide an outcome stops being a convention-free measurement (Fig 2 :43). The OECD Argentina review and Goodhart's law supply the case and the mechanism from lower-overlap fields. The channel also decays: Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does", with "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" (Fig 10 :25–26) as the named failure modes.

**D3, outside frames: `specified`, triangulated.**
Independence is a quantity that can be bought. Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The same parameter decides whether agreement means anything and whether a union has surplus (Fig 8 :48). Two frames sharing one evidence pool are "one frame wearing two names" (Fig 8 :49), so a larger number of agreeing agencies adds spelling, not evidence (Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not"). Outside data are imports needing a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :51), not free additions to a dashboard. Kish's effective sample size and engineering sensor fusion corroborate the arithmetic from different fields.

**D4, the verification seat: `specified` with the impossibility clause `general`, single-source on the figure side.**
Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :43: "Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it." The checker must be outside the slice it grades (Fig 10 :86), must author conventions but not verdicts, and must declare the interest it cannot remove (Fig 10 :59–60). A body that publishes both the number and the judgment on the number is a party in the ground's seat (Fig 2 :47).

**Order and type (supporting).** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry (Fig 3 :20); the middle links are the least instrumented (Fig 3 :27). Over a fixed atom set, survey, register, market trace and independent index are isomers (Fig 12 :117), so the question is which typed bond is present, not how much of each instrument to buy. Description cost and action cost do not add (Fig 12 :125).

**The recipe: `gap`.** No figure line returns a census interval, sample size, disclosure rule, agency chart or weighting scheme. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." This is `unreachable at this budget`, not shown to be underivable in principle (Fig 8 :39). The honest replacement is the shape plus the instrument owed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "more data is better". Correlated observations are fewer observations (Fig 2 :44, Kish's design effect).
- Not "aggregation is always wrong". A settled slot reading can become an atom at the next level (Fig 12 :104); the claim is about what gets composed over.
- Not "experts cannot be trusted". Prestige is a budget filter, not a verdict: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
- Not "human judgment is irrelevant". It is the right anchor for questions constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37); it is an instrument, not the anchor, for questions constitutively about measured conditions.
- Not "any named institution is the framework's answer". Naming an available binding is provenance, not a derivation (§5b of `05`).
- Not `grounded`. No government measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Slot discriminator.** An independent reinterview or administrative match showing whether a national aggregate hides a live per-locality residual.
2. **Anchor discriminator.** A moving ground plus an instrument that shows whether a published metric still tracks it after the metric became a policy target. This is the refuter that Buenos Aires' alternative inflation indices and household expectations actually ran.
3. **Independence discriminator.** A test of whether two readings draw on the same underlying pool, using the design-effect or correlation machinery rather than a claim of independence.
4. **Seat discriminator.** A protocol in which the ground can move independently of both claim and checker, and where a checker that produced the claim is compared with one that did not.
5. **Instrument for the gap.** A measurement that selects among census designs, registers, sample sizes and disclosure rules for a stated residual. Until one exists the recipe stays a `gap`.

Standing refuters from `01` §5: a pooled series retaining the per-slot signal; a target metric staying non-updating; same-pool agreement settling the world; a producer verifying its own claim; the four conditions composing as a partition; a figure line naming the recipe without strain.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four-condition shape is as far as the figures go. The institutional vocabulary used to describe it (households, registers, surveys, markets) is a visible choice drawn from a particular administrative tradition: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the choice does not make it neutral; it makes it auditable. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return an institutional recipe and does not claim that any country's statistics office is doing it right. It says that reliable government information has a shape with four overlapping conditions: the residual, the gap between prediction and observation, must stay indexed per citizen, firm and locality, because a national average can be settled while one locality is live; the reference it ranges against must not move when the government pushes on it, because a target metric stops being a measurement; the readings must come from frames whose evidence does not overlap, because agreement from one pool is one witness wearing many names; and the checker must be outside the slice it grades and unable to produce the claim it checks. The chain breaks at vocabulary and objective (what a local condition affords, and what would count as progress), not at perception. Survey, register, market trace and independent index are isomers over a fixed atom set, so the variable is the typed bond, not the quantity of data. No figure selects a sample size or agency chart; that is a declared gap and the instrument that would close it is owed. What is offered is the shape, five refuters written in advance, and the discriminators that would move it from candidate to settled. The slot stays open until the ground pays.
