## Wider literature that actually bears on this question

These are secondary corroborations only. The figures decide; none of these sources is used to overrule a figure line. Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Official statistics as a case of an updating anchor.** OECD, *Review of the Statistical System and Official Statistics of Argentina* (2019): "methods and data were distorted in order to produce figures which downplayed problems of inflation and poverty, and reduced debt service obligations... Argentina's official statistics steadily lost credibility." Also records the 2016 restoration and the instruction that 2007 to 2015 series be "used with caution." This is Fig 2 :29–35 and Fig 10 :24–28 in a real statistical system.
2. **The target corrupts the measure.** Charles Goodhart, "Goodhart's Law", *Le Libellio d'AEGIS* 9(4), 2013: "any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." Rodamar 2018 in *Significance* records the related Campbell's law and Strathern's phrasing "When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure." These are Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47 from economics and social measurement. Goodhart, Rodamar and the CNA report are one evidence pool, not three witnesses.
3. **Policy changes the relation being measured.** The Lucas critique, as recorded in the public secondary literature: "any change in policy will systematically alter the structure of econometric models." A relationship measured under one regime is not invariant under the regime it is used to steer. Partly overlaps the Goodhart pool (macroeconomics), so counted as the same witness for the anchor verdict.
4. **Citizens keep a channel the official series lost.** Cavallo, Cruces and Perez-Truglia, reported by UCLA Anderson Review (2018): "household inflation expectations aligned neatly with the alternative, unofficial inflation indices, which clocked in approximately 10 percentage points higher than the official data." This is independence and triangulation (Fig 2 :31, :35) with a different instrument.
5. **Systematic error does not wash out with scale.** US Census Bureau, *Measures of Nonsampling Error* (2020): "systematic errors tend to accumulate over the entire sample" and "bias caused by systematic errors cannot be reduced by increasing the sample size." This is Fig 1 :22 from survey statistics.
6. **Correlated observations are fewer observations.** Kish's design effect, as presented by Zipf and Valliant (2026): "clustering increase the design effect (and decrease the effective sample size)". This is the standard arithmetic that Fig 2 :44 names as a warning.
7. **Aggregation can reverse the slot-level result.** *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*, Simpson's Paradox (2021, revised 2026): "an association between two variables in a population emerges, disappears or reverses when the population is divided into subpopulations." This is Fig 1 :19/:22 from mathematics and philosophy, a genuinely low-overlap corroborator.
8. **Independent sensors reduce uncertainty.** Stanford AA203 sensor-fusion notes (2021): "Using measurements from multiple sensors (potentially different types of sensors) is an effective technique for reducing the uncertainty in downstream perception and estimation tasks." The notes name redundancy and sensor diversity as the mechanisms, and conditional independence as the assumption. This is Fig 2 :31–32, :44 and Fig 8 :48 from engineering, a second low-overlap corroborator.
9. **Statistical capacity is about use and sources, not only output.** World Bank SPI: "Statistics have value only if they are used" and data collection "goes beyond the typical censuses and surveys to include administrative and geospatial data as well as data generated by private firms and citizens." This is the union and import finding (Fig 9 :54, Fig 6 :23) from public administration.

The two genuinely low-overlap corroborators are the statistics/mathematics stream (Simpson, Kish, Census) and the engineering stream (sensor fusion). The economics and public-administration sources partly share one evidence pool. All essay sources share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52**: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the shape and the refuter first; send the links only after the recipient has recorded a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27–28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator; giving it away gives away no answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-condition shape claim and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which refuter is easiest to run; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only if they are curious, the three local links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 (before theory).** Residual, shape and pre-registered refuter. The recipient's verdict is recorded here.
- **Stage 2 (opt-in).** Sources, only if curiosity is expressed. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 (method).** The formula as a reusable generator (Fig 4 :22). Each user supplies their own ground and refuter; the author's warning that outputs can be "contrived to fit the formula" applies to them too.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If a refuter fires, publish the retraction first. Nothing here is `grounded`, and the whole folder is labelled a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency.
