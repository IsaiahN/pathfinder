## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual statistical system: sample frames, questionnaires, published indices, revision policies, audit offices, disclosure rules.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which instrument discriminates a non-updating anchor.** The shorthand says "do not let the anchor update." Fig 2 :29 supplies the definition but no instrument that detects an updating anchor in a particular series. The Argentina case is a retrospective identification, not a forward discriminator.
- **The union structure.** The shorthand lists four conditions; T_E tends to turn them into four departments, which is a partition reading. Fig 9 :54–58 requires the intersections to be handled as specific compounds.
- **The method versus recording distinction.** A published series is a recording of one occasion unless the method and the lineage cross with it (Fig 4 :22–25). T_E routinely ships the series without the method.
- **The unavoidable stake.** The shorthand says "outside checker"; Fig 10 :58–59 requires a real position with an unavoidable interest declared, not an idealised disinterested seat. A rule no real occupant could meet is "a prohibition stated wrong" (Fig 10 :53).

One thing survives cleanly: a structure between the report and the world must not itself update on the report, and the residual must stay per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no government measurement exists in this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
