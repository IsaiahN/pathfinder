# Expert verification packet: the Tether on government information gathering

**What this is:** a claim in a form a statistician, survey methodologist or public-administration researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the institutional recipe. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Reliable information about citizens, firms and local conditions has a four-condition shape, not a single quality score. (1) The residual, the gap between prediction and observation, must stay indexed per slot; a national average that looks settled while one locality is live is a legal state, not a success. (2) The reference the report ranges against must not change its answer when the government pushes on it; a metric used to decide an outcome has stopped being a convention-free measurement. (3) Readings must come from frames whose evidence pools do not overlap; independence is a quantity that can be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong. (4) The checker must be outside the slice it grades, unable to produce the claim it checks, and must declare the interest it cannot remove. The four conditions form a union with overlapping intersections, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. The dependency order is perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry, and the middle links are the least instrumented. Over a fixed atom set, survey, register, market trace and independent index are isomers, differing by typed bond rather than by quantity of data. No figure selects a sample size, census interval or agency chart; that is a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The per-slot claim (framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 1)

- **Statement:** the residual must stay indexed per citizen, firm and locality. Aggregating across slots discards the only signal that says where to look.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". Symbols table :38–39: "The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it."
- **World check:** the US Census Bureau's *Measures of Nonsampling Error* (2020) says "bias caused by systematic errors cannot be reduced by increasing the sample size"; Simpson's paradox is that "an association between two variables in a population emerges, disappears or reverses when the population is divided into subpopulations" (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, 2021, revised 2026).
- **Refuter:** exhibit a pooled national series that retains the per-locality signal aggregation is said to destroy, measured by an instrument independent of the series. If one exists, the per-slot claim is narrowed to a caution rather than a structural requirement.

### C2: The non-updating anchor and independence claim (framework-internal plus institutional case)

- **Statement:** the reference must not update on the report; a reported metric used as a policy target stops being a measurement; agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool is not corroboration.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30: "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 2 :34: "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :43: "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- **World check:** OECD 2019 on Argentina: "methods and data were distorted in order to produce figures which downplayed problems of inflation and poverty, and reduced debt service obligations." Goodhart 2013: "any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." Kish's design effect (Zipf and Valliant 2026): "clustering increase the design effect (and decrease the effective sample size)". Sensor fusion (Stanford AA203, 2021) assumes conditional independence for its uncertainty reduction.
- **Refuter:** (a) show with a moving ground and a discriminator that a target metric remained a non-updating anchor over repeated policy changes; or (b) show two frames drawing on the same evidence whose agreement still settles a question about the world, with a discriminator demonstrating it is not the pool talking. Either breaks the corresponding half of C2.

### C3: The refusal-seat and gap claim (framework-internal)

- **Statement:** a checker that produced the claim cannot verify it; the checker must be outside the slice it grades; no figure line returns the institutional recipe.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :43: "Soundness requires the checker to be unable to produce what it is checking." Fig 10 :86: "Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".
- **What to check:** is the seat described by its access (what it may see) rather than by a named office? Does it author only conventions, not verdicts (Fig 10 :47)? Is the interest declared rather than denied (Fig 10 :59–60)?
- **Refuter:** show a checker that produced the claim and still verifies it soundly under a protocol where the ground could move independently of both; or produce a figure line that names a concrete institution, ratio or sample size for this residual, quoted verbatim and governing without strain.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of countries, agencies or statistical systems. No measurement was taken.
- Not "more data is better"; correlated observations are fewer observations.
- Not "aggregation is always wrong"; a settled slot reading can become an atom at the next level (Fig 12 :104).
- Not that prestige or institutional reputation settles anything: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
- Not that human judgment is irrelevant; it is the anchor for questions constitutively about people (Fig 2 :37).
- Not that a named survey, register or index is the framework's answer. Naming an available binding is provenance (see `05` §5b).
- Not `grounded`. Five refuters are written before the run and any one that fires produces a visible retraction.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. An independent reinterview or administrative match showing whether a national aggregate hides a live per-locality residual.
2. A moving ground plus an instrument showing whether a published metric still tracks it after it became a policy target.
3. A correlation/design-effect test of whether two readings share an evidence pool.
4. A protocol where the ground can move independently of both claim and checker.
5. An instrument that selects among concrete census designs, registers and disclosure rules for a stated residual. This is the owed instrument for the declared gap.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 2 :29–31/:42–43 and Fig 10 :42/:43/:86. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one case you know: did a published target metric stay a non-updating anchor, and how was that demonstrated rather than asserted?
3. Name one discriminator in your field that would separate a slot-level signal from a pooled average, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
