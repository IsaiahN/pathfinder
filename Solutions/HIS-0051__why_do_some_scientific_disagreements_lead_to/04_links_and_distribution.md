## 2. What the wider literature contributes

The world sources are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide.

- **Science studies and HPS (the field itself).** The Wikipedia scientific-controversy page and the SEP entry agree on the central difficulty: progress needs a criterion that is not just "the community moved". SEP says *"movement in the wrong direction does not constitute progress"* and that there is no single concept covering all aspects of progress. That corroborates the union reading and the refusal to average domain verdicts. Overlap between these two pages is low (one STS summary, one analytic philosophy survey).
- **Human cognition (low overlap).** Confirmation bias corroborates the coherent-drift failure: people set higher standards of evidence for views they already reject and return to prior belief when given mixed evidence. It predicts entrenchment when frames are not forced to range against a separate, non-updating anchor. It does **not** license a personality explanation, because Fig 11 :51 says *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- **History of science, productive case (low overlap).** Continental drift predicts that a missing mechanism and a new instrument, not more of the same evidence, are what move a stalled controversy. This corroborates F5 and F6.
- **History of science, entrenched case (low overlap).** Cold fusion predicts that early announcement without a published protocol, failed replications, no accepted model, and a renamed in-group with no internal criticism produce a mirror room. This corroborates F1, F7, and F8.
- **Engineering and safety (low overlap).** Normalization of deviance predicts that early warning signs are misinterpreted or missed during a long incubation period and that a code of silence maintains the drift. This corroborates Fig 10 :24-28, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, and Fig 2 :34.
- **Framework author essays (maximal overlap).** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap is by the framework's author, so its agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent witness weight. It is used for its explicit mode and prestige rules, not as corroboration.

Overlap declaration: the two Wikipedia case studies and the cognition page are genuinely different evidence pools. The two society homepages and the Berkeley page are venue verification, not witnesses. The essay shares the figure author's pool completely. No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the six-condition separator and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 2 :40**, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60**, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue prestige is a budget filter.

Recommended order in a reply: (1) the separator and its seven refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interiors their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, which is permitted because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write down what would refute their own result and state the mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The separator is currently `specified`, with one live type question (S5 in `01`) and one declared gap (F10). Keep both visible when sending.
