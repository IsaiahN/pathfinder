## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's findings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry, and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency, and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between the observed outcome class of a controversy and the outcome predicted by its conditions. Arity: multi-term, at least two frames, one ground, and one checking arrangement. Symmetry: the labels are symmetric, the process is ordered, so "entrenched" is a missing link, not an opposite cause. Scale: one scientist, a room, and crossings between rooms and scales. Question type: union compound (Fig 9 :4-5), not a two-list partition.
- **Domain 1, frames.** Actors: research programs as frames. Currency: predictions, residuals, evidence pool. Anchor: a ground each frame can range against separately. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 2, ground and instruments.** Actors: instruments, replications, panels. Currency: contact; bits and actions. Anchor: the non-updating ground. Question type: discriminator owed.
- **Domain 3, community.** Actors: reviewers, rooms, funding panels. Currency: agreement, prestige, conventions. Anchor: ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation versus mirror.
- **Domain 4, method channel.** Actors: methods, recordings, imports, next rooms. Currency: method versus recording. Anchor: settlement before composition. Question type: import versus re-arrangement.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle before composing (Fig 12 :106); carry a method up only after it pays (Fig 4 :22); book a differing room (Fig 7 :36).
