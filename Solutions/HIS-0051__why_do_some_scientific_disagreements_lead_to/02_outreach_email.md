# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the six-condition separator in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is stated with its mode and its refuter intact. If the output cannot survive the first check, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one separator before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about scientific controversy. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a scientific disagreement becomes productive when six conditions hold jointly across four domains. Two of them concern the disputing frames: they draw on genuinely different evidence, and they range separately against something that does not change its answer when either frame pushes on it. Two concern the ground: the residual is stated per slot rather than as a pooled grievance, and there is a discriminator, an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. One concerns the method channel: a reapplicable method crosses in, and if a new primitive is needed it is imported from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test. One concerns the community: it can refuse one of its own held terms, and the room changes when the question changes. An entrenched controversy is one where at least one of those conditions is absent, and which one differs from case to case. This is a shape, not a recipe, and it returns no productivity score.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this explains every controversy, that the six conditions can be ranked, that "productive" and "entrenched" are two kinds of controversy rather than two outcomes of one union of conditions, or that I can name the missing condition in any particular historical case. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them, so I am not presenting the historical cases as derivations the framework produced.
>
> **What would refute the separator:**
> 1. a controversy settled by ground while both sides shared one evidence pool and the reference moved when they pushed on it;
> 2. a controversy that stays entrenched while all six conditions are present and reachable;
> 3. a bundled controversy settled by one lever across every overlapping condition, with no unbundling;
> 4. a controversy settled by replaying a recording of one occasion, with no method and no import;
> 5. a community with no way to refuse its own held terms that still settles a disagreement by ground;
> 6. a chain of identical rooms that produces ground-settled progress;
> 7. a figure line that names the missing primitive in a specific controversy or returns a per-controversy productivity number.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the separator is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures README and figure text with line numbers: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Scientific Progress: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/scientific-progress/
> - Understanding Science, What controversy: https://undsci.berkeley.edu/understanding-science-101/a-scientific-approach-to-life-a-science-toolkit/what-controversy-is-a-controversy-misrepresented-or-blown-out-of-proportion/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a controversy you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
