## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the observed variance in outcomes for scientific disagreement. Option A: treat productive and entrenched as two kinds of controversy and look for the property that distinguishes them. Option B: treat the outcome split as a union of overlapping conditions and return a separator with per-domain verdicts and a declared gap. What each leads to: A sends a pooled, ranked cause list that averages away per-slot residuals and manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot until a discriminator settles each case. The effect to explain is the outcome class per controversy, not an average amount of disagreement.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency, and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the observed outcome class of a controversy (productive or entrenched) and the outcome predicted by the conditions present in it. Arity: multi-term, at least two frames plus a ground plus a checking arrangement. Symmetry: the labels are symmetric but the process is ordered, so "entrenched" is an absence of links, not a mirror cause. Scale: one scientist, a research community as a room, and crossings between rooms and scales. Fig 9 names it a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the frames in disagreement.** Actors: two or more research programs, each a frame with a library, atoms, bonds, and a weighting. Currency: predictions, residuals, and the evidence pool drawn on. Anchor: a ground each frame can range against separately. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 2, the ground and instruments.** Actors: instruments, replications, panels, discriminators. Currency: contact with a non-updating ground; description cost in bits and action cost in settling. Anchor: the ground itself. Question type: a discriminator is owed when several sources could give the same reading.
- **Domain 3, the community and checking arrangement.** Actors: reviewers, editors, rooms, funding panels. Currency: agreement, prestige, residuals, conventions. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation versus mirror chain; prestige is a budget filter, not a witness.
- **Domain 4, the temporal and method channel.** Actors: methods (generators), recordings, head starts, imported atoms, the next room. Currency: reapplicable method versus one-occasion recording; bits and actions. Anchor: settlement of a term before it can be composed over. Question type: import across a wall versus re-arrangement inside a closure.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse a held false term before minting (Formula step 2); settle before composing (Fig 12 :106); carry a method up only after it pays (Fig 4 :22); book a room that differs from the last and matches the residual it surfaced (Fig 7 :36).

Strain test applied: the six physical terms of Fig 13 were first mapped onto "stages of a controversy" and produced a strain. Re-census assigns Fig 13 to physical composition and keeps only its recursive cross-kind alternation here. The comparison bond ⋛ was considered as the main operator and declined as a strain: it governs a threshold relation between two quantities, not the separator. Both re-censuses are recorded in `01` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any case-specific name does not. A scientific disagreement becomes productive when six conditions hold jointly across four domains: evidence-level independence and a non-updating anchor in the frames, a per-slot residual and a working discriminator in the ground domain, a method crossing and a provenanced import in the channel, and a refusal-capable, question-responsive community. It remains entrenched when at least one condition is absent, with the absent condition differing per case. The outcome classes are isomers over a fixed atom set, same atoms and different bond, and the four public kinds of controversy are different bonds over partly shared atoms. No figure line returns the missing primitive of a specific controversy or a productivity number. Mode `specified` on the separator, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the case-specific missing condition, not `grounded`, `candidate`. The whole is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle any historical controversy it reports on.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, seven refuters, and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranked cause list instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter. A second cost is reifying the six conditions as a standing checklist, which Fig 11 :51 forbids: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- **undoable:** yes. The case-specific bindings are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no figure term has to be withdrawn. The live fixed-atom strain on the isomer reading (`06`) can be narrowed further without changing the separator.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that reports the seven conditions per controversy slot and returns a per-slot verdict would move a case toward settled; a controversy settled while its reference demonstrably updates on its own frames would break Fig 2 :29-35; a bundled controversy settled by one lever would break Fig 9 :4-5; a figure line naming the missing primitive of a specific controversy would close the F10 gap; a controversy settled by replaying a recording with no import would break Fig 4 :22-25. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
