## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question reports a variance we want to explain. Two outcome classes are observed for scientific disagreement:

- **Productive:** the disagreement terminates in a new method, a sharper instrument, a discriminating measurement, or a stronger explanation, and the claim is settled or refined against a ground.
- **Entrenched:** the disagreement persists, often for decades, with no new ground contact, with each side's rule continuing to work well on a different subset of the cases, and with resolution coming only from a convention, a vote, an institutional decision, or exhaustion.

The effect to explain is this difference in outcome, **per controversy**. It is not an average amount of disagreement and it is not a personality trait of the scientists. Stated as an effect, the residual is:

> R = the gap between the outcome class we observe for a controversy (productive or entrenched) and the outcome the conditions present in that controversy predict.

What is being decided: which conditions separate the two classes, and what would have to be true for a given entrenched controversy to become productive. What an answer would look like, bound now as an ordinary term so it can be refuted: **a union separator**, a set of jointly necessary conditions drawn from the census below, each with a checkable ground and a failure condition, with verdicts kept separate per domain and never averaged into a single recipe or percentage. The figures return a shape of this kind, never the identity of the missing condition in a particular case (Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).

**Strain guard.** The ordinary word "productive" is a symmetric label for an ordered process. Productive controversies have a direction (residual stated, frame chosen, ground contacted, discriminator built, method crossed, term refused, new term settled). Entrenched controversies are not its symmetric mirror image; they are the absence of one or more of those ordered links. The census therefore uses the dependency language of Fig 3, not a two-list partition.

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** multi-term. A disagreement involves at least two frames, a ground they might range against, and a checking arrangement (a community, a review panel, a journal, a funding body). The outcome depends on the relations among these four, not on any one of them. Single-variable answers ("diversity", "evidence", "personality", "status") are already too small.
- **Symmetry:** the outcome *labels* are symmetric but the *process* is ordered. Productive requires a sequence of links (Fig 3); entrenched is a break in one of them. There is also a threshold reading available (Fig 12 comparison ⋛, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), but forcing it here would be a strain. It governs a different quantity, namely the point at which a community's bracket fidelity and ground pressure are jointly sufficient for a handoff to progress (Fig 7 :37-39). It is noted, not used as the main operator.
- **Scale:** three strata are in play. One scientist running a loop (Fig 4, the one-agent panel). A research community as a room with a shared library and something that ranks what it produces (Fig 4, the population panel). Crossings between rooms and between scales (Fig 4 membrane, Fig 6 wall, Fig 8 union). The same loop applies at each, only actors, currency, and anchor change (Fig 4 :19-20).

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, the frames in disagreement** | two or more research programs, each a frame with its own library Γ, atoms, bonds, and weighting w | predictions, residuals, imports, and the evidential pool they draw on | the ground each frame can range against separately | union compound, not a partition (several kinds of controversy overlap) |
| **D2, the ground and instruments** | instruments, experiments, replications, review panels, discriminators | contact with a non-updating ground; R relative to that ground; description cost in bits and action cost in settling | the ground, which must not move when a frame pushes on it | discriminator owed when several sources could give the same reading |
| **D3, the community and checking arrangement** | reviewers, editors, conference rooms, funding panels, the room as a whole | agreement, prestige, residuals, conventions | should be the ground, never agreement; when the reference updates on the room's own reports it drifts | mirror chain versus triangulation |
| **D4, the temporal and method channel** | methods (generators), recordings, head starts, imported atoms, the next room | reapplicable method versus one-occasion recording; bits and actions | settlement of a term before it can be composed over | import across a wall versus re-arrangement inside a closure |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. Each of D1 to D4 has one: D1's anchor is the ground each frame ranges against; D2's is the non-updating ground itself; D3's is the ground rather than agreement; D4's is settlement before composition. A fifth candidate, "the social status of the disputants", is **not** admitted as a separate domain here. It has no anchor of its own, it is a currency inside D3 (prestige), and Fig 2 :42 prices it as a budget filter rather than a witness. It is the subject of the sibling question HIS-0052.

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union, not partition. A disagreement over how data should be interpreted, a disagreement over which ideas are best supported, a disagreement over which ideas are worth pursuing, and a disagreement over the ethics or applications of a method can all be present in one public controversy. The UC Berkeley Understanding Science page *"What controversy: Is a controversy misrepresented or blown out of proportion?"*, opened 2026-11-23, distinguishes fundamental scientific controversy, secondary scientific controversy, conflict over ethicality of methods, conflict over applications, and conflict between a scientific idea and a non-scientific viewpoint. It says *"mistaking one form of controversy for another could easily lead one astray about the science at stake."* That is exactly the union warning in Fig 9 :54-58: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

Consequence: the answer is a **conjunction of necessary conditions**, not a weighted recipe. Each controversy must be assessed per slot. Verdicts per domain stay separate and are never averaged.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. Can the disagreement be stated as a residual in effect terms (perception and vocabulary, Fig 3 :4-8)?
2. Can the community say what would count as progress here, in its own terms (the objective, Fig 3 :9-11)?
3. Are the frames independent, and is there a ground that does not update (Fig 2 :29-35)?
4. Is there a discriminator, an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :32-35, Fig 3 :27)?
5. Can a method cross the membrane and can a new atom be imported with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 4 :22, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52)?
6. Can the community refuse a held term, and does the room change when the question changes (Operators :58, Fig 10 :72)?

The chain is ordered by dependency. *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* (Fig 3 :19). The middle link, having terms for what a situation affords and being able to say what would count as progress, is where chains usually break, and *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27).

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1. The ground must not update (necessary, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :33-35:

> **"The anchor must not update."**
> **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

Productive controversies range against something that does not move because a frame pushes on it: a measurement, a replication, a GPS reading, a panel verdict, a patient outcome. Entrenched controversies are the coherent-drift case: the reference updates on the disputants' own reports, so convergence is the most influential frame talking, and nobody notices because the drift is coherent. Fig 2 :47 closes the escape route: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."*

**World corroboration (low overlap).** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened 2026-11-23: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* That is the same coherent-drift law from human validation practice, not from the figure diagrams.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor test, Fig 11 contact claim, cognition essay). Low overlap between the geometric anchor argument and the clock analogy. **Refuter:** a controversy that is settled by ground while the reference demonstrably moved when a frame pushed on it, with no new non-updating instrument introduced.

### F2. Independence is one quantity read twice (necessary, triangulated)

Fig 2 :31-32:

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."**

Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*

The same parameter decides whether agreement means anything and whether the union of two frames has a surplus. Productive controversies have low-overlap frames, so their agreement, when it comes, is triangulation. Entrenched controversies often have high overlap: the two camps read the same reports, cite the same authorities, and train in the same tradition, so their persistent disagreement is one pool talking under two names, and their occasional agreement is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :41). Independence must be bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42), which is a budget rule, not a verdict rule.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 8 (two different diagrams, one parameter). **Refuter:** a controversy resolved by a new ground reading while both frames drew on demonstrably the same evidence pool throughout and no new low-overlap frame entered.

### F3. A union compound must be split before it is searched (necessary, triangulated)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54-58:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

An entrenched controversy often stops shrinking because it is several well-formed questions bundled into one name. Applying one lever to the bundle clears the region where a single condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Productive controversies are the ones where the compound is unbundled: the evidential question, the interpretive question, the methodological question, and the applications question get separate slots and separate grounds. The UC Berkeley page corroborates the unbundling from science education: it says true controversy involves disagreements over how data should be interpreted, over which ideas are best supported, and over which ideas are worth investigating, and that mistaking one form for another leads one astray.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union split, Fig 1 per-slot indexing, science education corroboration). **Refuter:** a bundled controversy in which one lever settled every overlapping condition at once, with no unbundling and no per-slot residual.

### F4. The broken link is usually the objective link, and it has no instrument (single-source on the figure side)

Fig 3 :24-27:

> **"The middle link is where chains usually break."**
> **"Perception has obvious failures and obvious tests. Planning has obvious failures and obvious tests. The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

A controversy stays entrenched when the community cannot say, in its own terms, what would count as progress in the dispute. Much effort then lands above the break, where the measurements already exist (Fig 3 :29-30: *"Most improvement effort in practice lands above the break, because that is where the measurements already exist."*). Productive controversies are the ones where somebody builds the missing objective link: a criterion, a protocol, a registered prediction, a discrimination test.

**Status:** `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 3 alone), corroborated directionally by the UC Berkeley page's distinction between a genuine evidential controversy and a conflict over applications. No second figure independently governs this exact link, so the verdict is not upgraded. **Refuter:** a controversy that remained entrenched while a well-instrumented, accepted objective criterion for progress was continuously available and used by both sides.

### F5. What crosses must be a method, not a recording (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22-26:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."**

Productive controversies spread a generator: a method that can be reapplied, verified, and compressed to its essentials. Entrenched controversies replay a recording of one occasion, so the newcomer repeats a past success and cannot produce a new result. Fig 12 :107-110 adds why this matters across lineages: where the arrangement is not recorded, *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*

**World corroboration (low overlap, history of science).** The Wikipedia page *"Continental drift"*, opened 2026-11-23, reports that Wegener presented much evidence but *"was unable to provide a convincing explanation for the physical processes which might have caused this drift"* and that *"One problem was that a plausible driving force was missing."* The controversy became productive only when a new method and a new instrument crossed in: bathymetry, magnetometry, and the Vine-Matthews-Morley interpretation, after which *"Without workable alternatives to explain the stripes, geophysicists were forced to conclude that Holmes had been right."* The original evidence was not replayed; a method arrived.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 4 membrane, Fig 12 lineage rule, geological case). **Refuter:** a controversy settled by replaying a single successful result into a substituted habitat, with no reapplicable method and no instrument, and with the settlement paying against the ground.

### F6. Import moves the wall; re-arrangement inside the closure does not (triangulated)

Fig 6 :21, :23-24:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**
> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
> **"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."**

Fig 8 :32: *"no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 8 :45-46: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone. Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."* Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

Productive controversies import the missing atom from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (explains a gap). The mechanism for continental drift came from oceanography and geophysics into a debate that had stalled on fossils and coastline fit. Entrenched controversies try to derive from inside what is underivable, or import a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives. Cold fusion is the contrasting case: the Wikipedia page *"Cold fusion"*, opened 2026-11-23, reports that the claim was announced before the protocol was published, that *"physicists in several countries attempted, and failed, to replicate the excess heat phenomenon,"* and that there remains *"no accepted theoretical model that describes how cold fusion could occur."* A missing mechanism and a missing discriminator were never imported with a source gate; the community renamed itself LENR and CMNS rather than moving the wall.

Fig 6 :32-34 supplies the instrument rule: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* This is why the question cannot be answered by saying "they needed more evidence". An instrument is owed, and it must be built from a worse instrument already returning something.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 wall, Fig 8 surplus, two low-overlap historical cases). **Refuter:** a controversy resolved purely by re-arranging the same atoms inside one closure, with no new instrument and no import from a differing frame, and with the settlement paying against the ground.

### F7. Refusal is required: a vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (triangulated)

Fig 5 :50-53:

> **"the gap has a fifth kind, and the pipeline has no branch for it."**
> **"A residual whose content is that a regularity the system holds is false owes a term, and the term it owes refuses another rather than explaining a gap."**
> **"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"**

Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* Operators :73-74: *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

A productive controversy can refuse a held term. That is why the Goodstein quotation in the cold fusion page is diagnostic: *"Between cold fusion and respectable science there is virtually no communication at all. Cold fusion papers are almost never published in refereed scientific journals, with the result that those works don't receive the normal critical scrutiny that science requires... because the Cold-Fusioners see themselves as a community under siege, there is little internal criticism."* No internal criticism is a vocabulary without negation. It can only accumulate.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 5 amendment, Operators table, Fig 10 seat rules). **Refuter:** a controversy settled against the ground in a community that demonstrably had no operator for refusing its own held terms and no external seat able to refuse for it.

### F8. The room must change when the question changes (triangulated)

Fig 7 :12-13:

> **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Fig 7 :35-36:

> **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
> **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

Fig 7 :42: *"The chain approaches the truth only as the world runs out of ways to refuse it."* Fig 10 :71-73: *"The room composition is a function of the question. When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."*

Entrenchment has a temporal signature: the same community, the same instruments, the same journals, and the same arguments are handed along from year to year. Agreement compounds inside the room while evidence does not. Productivity has the opposite signature: each handoff books a room that differs from the last and matches the residual the last one surfaced. In continental drift the rooms were mineralogy, paleontology, oceanography, magnetometry, seismology, and finally GPS. In cold fusion the room stayed fixed and its members renamed it.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 7 room chain, Fig 10 room selection, two historical cases). **Refuter:** a chain of demonstrably identical rooms, same closure and same instruments, that produced a new ground-settled method and a refined explanation without importing a differing room.

### F9. Prestige and status are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."*

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, opened 2026-11-23, states the rule in its strongest form: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* And: *"the prestige loop looks like agreement, and agreement feels like truth."*

So a controversy can be entrenched by status without the status being a separate domain. Status is a currency inside D3: it buys a budget to try, and when it is allowed to settle, the channel to the ground is broken and a broken channel looks exactly like a working one until the drift is large. This is why the sibling question HIS-0052 (status and institutional rivalry) shares D3 but has its own residual. This question's verdict on status is narrow: it is a necessary check on the checking arrangement, not an independent cause.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 :42, Fig 9 :60, cognition essay). **Refuter:** a controversy whose claim was accepted or rejected by ground while the verdict weight of prestige was demonstrably nonzero and no seat was blind to it.

### F10. The gap: no figure line names the missing condition of a particular controversy

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

No figure line returns the name of the condition whose absence made a given historical controversy entrenched, nor a numerical productivity score, nor a universal recipe. That is not a failure of the search; it is the shape the figures return. The ground for each controversy (a protocol, a discriminator, an instrument, a panel decision) has to be read in that case. This simulated derivation cannot do that reading, so it reports `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind rather than silence or a fabricated name.

**Status:** `gap`, triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 13, and Fig 8. **Refuter:** a figure line that, after re-census, does name a missing primitive for a specific controversy or returns a per-controversy productivity number. Until one is shown, the gap stands.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The union separator is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A controversy is settled by ground while two frames share one evidence pool and the reference demonstrably updates on their own reports, with no new non-updating anchor (breaks F1 and F2).
2. A controversy remains entrenched while a non-updating anchor, independent frames, a stated per-slot residual, a working discriminator, a method crossing, a refusal seat, and a changing room are all present and reachable (breaks the joint necessity of the union).
3. A bundled controversy is settled by one lever across all overlapping conditions, with no unbundling and no per-slot residuals (breaks F3).
4. A controversy is settled by replaying a recording of one occasion into a substituted habitat, with no method and no import, and with the settlement paying against ground (breaks F5 and F6).
5. A community with no operator for refusing its own held terms settles a disagreement by ground without any external seat able to refuse for it (breaks F7).
6. A chain of demonstrably identical rooms produces ground-settled progress (breaks F8).
7. A figure line is produced that names the missing primitive of a specific controversy or returns a per-controversy productivity number (breaks F10).

**Instrument condition.** Even if none of these fires, nothing here becomes `grounded`. To move a case from candidate to settled would require a discriminator that reports, per controversy slot, whether the anchor is non-updating, whether the frames are independent at the evidence level, whether a residual is stated per slot, whether a discriminator exists, whether a method crossed, and whether refusal is possible. That instrument is owed; this derivation does not build it.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the answer as a **union separator**, not a recipe. Productive = the joint presence of the six conditions; entrenched = at least one necessary condition absent, and which one differs per case.
2. Keep the four domain verdicts separate and never average them into a single "amount of disagreement", a productivity percentage, or a personality account.
3. Index the residual per controversy slot (Fig 1 :19), not per field or per era. Aggregating across slots is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
4. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*). Do not start from "scientists are biased" or "institutions are conservative" and then pick cases.
5. Lead with the refuter. *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25). Send the generator, not the conclusion.
6. Report the gap as a verdict. The figures return a kind of thing, never the name of the missing condition in a case.
