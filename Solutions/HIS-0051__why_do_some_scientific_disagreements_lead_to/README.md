## The question restated as an effect

Two outcome classes are observed for scientific disagreement. Productive disagreement terminates in new methods, sharper instruments, and stronger explanations. Entrenched disagreement persists with no new ground contact, often with each side's rule working well on a different subset. What is being decided is which conditions are present in the first class and absent in the second. An answer would look like a separator stated in effect terms, per domain, with a ground that can settle each case and a failure condition written in advance. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that productive controversy has a separable structure.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Adding agreeable recipients raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground (Seat Map; Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
