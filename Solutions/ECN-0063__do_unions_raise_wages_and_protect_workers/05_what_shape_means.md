## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not decide. For this question the shape is: a union compound, four slots with separate anchors, a functional group of two faces, a comparison bond for power, and no composable net scalar. The shape does **not** include whether unions are good or bad. Writing down "unions raise wages by about 9 log points" is a name, not a shape; writing down "the wage slot has a positive direction conditional on skill and career stage, with an anchor in wage data" is a shape.

*The Library of Babel* also gives the bundling test: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That sentence is the closest essay-level statement of the Fig 9 :54 finding that governs F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect description is "an institution that collectively organizes workers acts on four different measured outcomes; ask what you would see in each." That is checkable by anyone with the data, whatever their view of unions.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The four slots in this solution are imports from the labor-economics frame. Recording that is what keeps them from being mistaken for figure-derived primitives (F7 in `01`).

## 4. Why "net effect" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

A reported union wage premium is a reading. Several sources generate it: rent extraction, productivity selection, skill composition, career-stage effects (NBER Digest 2025; IZA DP 16185). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator in the instrument, not a name for the source. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A single literature that validates its own two-face model against its own pooled data is the named collapse.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any of the outcome slots. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Wage, voice and income share are not missing primitives in the figure frame at all; they are world-side quantities imported with provenance from labor economics and organizational psychology. Naming them is not forbidden, but claiming the figures derived them is false.

What remains forbidden: claiming the figure frame selects which slot matters, or claiming that naming a slot settles its direction. Both claims are retracted here. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the wage reading still has several possible sources, and the discriminator is an instrument, not an argument. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers respected labor economists to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the unbundling against each slot's data, and let the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The Medium article's demo section calls such outputs "simulacra" and warns they "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the union question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
