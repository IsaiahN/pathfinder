# The Tether's answer on unions, wages and worker protection

**Mode: `specified` : not `grounded` by this derivation.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. The empirical directions below are the published findings of the named authors, attributed to them.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which of two options is correct: unions raise wages and protect workers at the cost of employment, or unions improve productivity and counterbalance employer power. The residual is the gap between that either/or and what the evidence actually shows in four different outcome slots. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own anchors per slot.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before answering.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The four slots are wage and employment, voice and productivity, distribution and power, and the conditions that flip each sign. A single net verdict is "aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22).
2. **Each slot has its own anchor.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Wages and headcount for D1; quits, tenure and productivity for D2; wage shares and law for D3. Agreement among economists is not an anchor: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35)
3. **The two faces are a functional group, not a demonstrated isomer.** Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." The shared substructure is collective worker organization; the differing bonds are monopoly power and voice. The stronger isomer form is a real, falsifiable structural claim, not yet a result (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
4. **"At the cost of employment" is a subtraction inside the wage slot, not a verdict about the whole.** Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." Brändle (2024) supplies the empirical witness: employment growth is two to four percent lower in firms with union bargaining. The subtraction is local; it does not license a claim about productivity or power.
5. **"Counterbalance employer power" is a comparison bond.** Operators table :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one." The DOL review reports a shift in income from capital to labor (Doucouliagos, Freeman and Laroche 2017), and Kochan and Kimball (2019) report negative effects on profits. These are two readings of one comparison, not a contradiction to be averaged away.
6. **The slot directions are conditional, not universal.** Dodini et al. (2023): "the career effect of union membership differs greatly depending on the age at which workers enroll." Kochan and Kimball (2019): effects on performance depend "on the quality of the labor-management relationship." The NBER Digest (2025) reports a wage floor that helps low-skill workers and a ceiling that can hurt higher-skilled workers. Conditions are part of the answer, not noise around it.
7. **The figure frame gives the shape, not the institutional detail.** Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." The slot names are imports from labor economics and organizational psychology, provenanced as such. The figures do not say which outcomes a society ought to care about.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that unions are net good or net bad. No net scalar is composable from the figure frame (F1).
- Not that "at the cost of employment" is the only cost, or that productivity is the only benefit. There are four slots, each with conditions.
- Not that the monopoly and voice faces are isomers. They are a functional group; isomer is a falsifiable stronger claim.
- Not that the empirical signs were derived here. They are the authors' `grounded` reports. This derivation has no dataset.
- Not that a consensus of labor economists settles any slot. "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35)
- Not `grounded`. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The net-scalar answer is unreachable at this budget and from this frame.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or a new anchor, not a further review inside the same pool.

- **Discriminate the wage premium's sources.** An instrument that separates rent extraction from productivity selection at the worker level, beyond the decomposition already reported by Beauregard et al. (2025). *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the analogous case.
- **Test the isomer form.** Write both faces over the same full atom set and look for an atom one requires and the other does not. The falsifier in `06` decides it.
- **Type the comparison.** At the workplace scale, specify quantities for both operands of ⋛, or withdraw the comparison there.
- **Find a low-overlap witness.** The OSHwiki organizational-psychology article is a start: it corroborates the voice mechanism from a different field but explicitly declines a causal verdict. One independent replication of a slot with a new dataset would be worth more than ten more reviews in the same lineage (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **Fire the pre-registered refuter.** If any of the four clauses in `01` section 4 holds, the shape claim is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The union compound is describable, the slots are nameable with provenance, and the anchors are public. What this derivation cannot do is return the net direction, because the figure frame never held that quantity and the empirical pool is correlated. "A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39), so this is not a claim that no net effect exists. It is a claim that the question as posed cannot be answered by one scalar from this frame. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether unions are good or bad, and it does not pick a side in the question as written. It says the question is a bundle posing as one choice: wage and employment, voice and productivity, and countervailing power are separate slots with separate data anchors, so a single net verdict is an average that throws away the signal (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54). The monopoly and voice faces are a functional group, not demonstrated isomers; "at the cost of employment" is a subtraction inside the wage slot; and countervailing power is a comparison bond, not a point on the wage scale. The directions for each slot come from the empirical literature and are attributed to their authors, not derived here. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, three refuters written before the sources were read, and the instruments that would close it. It stops at "unreachable is a verdict, not a silence," and records the route so the stop can be checked.
