# Outreach draft : check the unbundling before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the reader can see the four slot definitions and the refuter in one screen. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 artifact has to be explained.

---

**Subject:** One structural check on a labor-unions question, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know from your own work. I am not asking you to read any framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "do unions raise wages at the cost of employment, or improve productivity and counterbalance employer power?" is a bundle presented as a single choice. The figures I am testing say that a residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated, and that when a split lands on a union rather than a partition, you unbundle it before you search again. So the answer shape is not one verdict but four readings against four different anchors:
> 1. wage level and employment level, anchored in wage and headcount data;
> 2. voice and productivity, anchored in quit, tenure and productivity data;
> 3. distribution and countervailing power, anchored in wage-share and institutional data;
> 4. the conditions that flip each sign, anchored in the studies' own subgroup results.
>
> On this reading, "at the cost of employment" is a subtraction across two slots, and "counterbalance employer power" is a comparison bond, not a point on the wage scale. The shape does not pick a side between the pro-union and anti-union answers; it says neither is composable as a single net scalar.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the figures tell us the sign of any slot. They do not. The directions come from the empirical literature and are attributed to its authors. I am also not claiming that the monopoly and voice faces are isomers; the defensible form is weaker, they are a functional group. And I am not claiming a new synthesis of the labor evidence. I have not run a study. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. one outcome measure, consistently applied, dominates every other slot with the same sign in every institutional context, with no conditional reversal;
> 2. institutional context makes no difference to the sign of any slot, so the compound is really one condition;
> 3. a fully independent evidence pool, not drawing on the same literature lineage, finds all four signs reversed and the original pattern fails re-analysis.
>
> I wrote these down before looking at the empirical sources. If any one holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figure text, not my summary, is the authority.
> - Verbatim figures with line numbers: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The two-faces paper (Freeman and Medoff, NBER, 1979): https://www.nber.org/papers/w0364
> - A recent decomposition of the wage premium (NBER Digest, 2025): https://www.nber.org/digest/202508/unpacking-union-wage-premium
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the unbundling procedure is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name / role]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter before the verdict | The refuter is written before the world search (section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`), so it cannot be tuned to the sources. |
| Verdict recorded before theory | Each slot verdict is attributed to its author before any figure line is shown in stage 2. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`, "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| Directions withheld from the figures | The figures give the shape; the authors give the signs. Fig 8 :51, "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." |
| No "net good" or "net bad" framing | Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| Offer the method as reusable | Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask for a ground and a refuter per problem | *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." |
| Explicit simulated-status line | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Hiding this would let the reader mistake a simulation for a measurement. |

## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** the four slots, the shape claim, the three refuters, and the explicit non-claim about directions. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record the answer before showing anything else.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the three links. The figures are the authority, not the summary. The reader's later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the unbundling method for the reader's own problem, with the instruction to state a ground and a refuter in advance.
