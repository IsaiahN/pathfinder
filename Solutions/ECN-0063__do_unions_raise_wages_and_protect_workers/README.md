## The question restated as an effect

An institution U, the collective organization of workers, acts on a set of outcome slots S = {wage level, employment level, job protection and tenure, productivity, distribution of income and bargaining power}. Option A says U raises one slot (wages) at the cost of another (employment). Option B says U raises other slots (productivity, countervailing power). What an answer would look like: a per-slot direction with the conditions under which each direction holds, bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual, rather than a single net scalar (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 1 :19).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe an answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35)
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, on top of the structural failure. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid both.
