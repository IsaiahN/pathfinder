## 5. Legal and channel constraints

Public institutional and opt-in channels only. No personal emails, no purchased lists, no scraped addresses, no bulk send, no misleading subject lines, and an opt-out line in every one-to-one message. GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions. A public competition or submission portal avoids both the legal failure and the structural one: it selects for ground-visible work, not for likely agreement.
