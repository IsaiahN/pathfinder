## VERDICT (from the figures)

Split the question. Universality at the kernel level is affirmed as `general`: the same cross-kind composition recurs across scales while actors, currency and anchor change. "Mirror" is not a bond and is refused where the evidence pool is shared; it is accepted as corroboration only where separate instruments converge, and that provenance test has not been run. No specific universal law is derived, the continuum is not a shared primitive, and the two cores are a functional group over the six-term set with a real but untested isomer reading. Whether a named law actually holds in both domains is **gap** from the figures and is owed a ground reading. This is `specified`, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode labeled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending an analogy as if it were evidence. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split, the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific-law bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. If a provenance test shows low overlap, the mirror refusal can be narrowed without changing the kernel verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A provenance audit showing independent instruments behind a shared law would convert the mirror refusal into a triangulation verdict; a measurement of a regularity not recoverable as cross-kind composition, a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or the admission of a continuum as a seventh primitive would break the shape claim; a source-discriminating instrument that settles a named law in both domains would move that sub-claim from gap toward grounded. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
