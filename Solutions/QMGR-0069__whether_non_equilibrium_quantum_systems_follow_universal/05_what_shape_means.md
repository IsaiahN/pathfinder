## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *a cross-kind composition rule that recurs across scales, with only actors, currency and anchor changing*. The name of a specific physical law is not part of the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle case, which governs this question directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question the six are a visible choice, not nature's published list. Naming the fluctuation theorem or the chaos bound as *the* universal law would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the "mirror" reading is the specific failure here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; the reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applying that to this question: two domains whose laws *look* the same may share a source (one mathematical culture, one review pool) or may have arrived separately at the same form. The appearance is a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating check on provenance, not a declaration that the laws are the same.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* A shared equation that no one has discriminated is not thereby a shared ground.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular law in the slot?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular law is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4 (as in the exemplar's §5b)

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket ban on naming physical terms. That is an overstatement. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Gradient, Spectrum and Network are already among the six (Fig 13), and the fluctuation theorem, chaos bound and Einstein equation are held terms of the physics frame. Naming them is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming that such a binding is a figure derivation or that it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: naming the source of a reading without a discriminator is still the error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Why the six are not the answer

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* says capability is *"a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* (Fig 11). *They're Scanning Books* says *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess."* *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says *"The Tether is like a human. The kernel is fixed. The operating system grows."*

So the six-term set is the fixed kernel of this framework, not a discovery of nature's own list. It is the vocabulary in which the shape is stated, not the physics the answer is about. This is why the answer file keeps the shape claim and refuses the specific-law claim.

## 7. Two consequences for this audit

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous physicists who agree that universality exists is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim and an instrument that can refuse it.

**The audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And from *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula"* is the standing worry. So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the physics and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. One limit I kept explicit

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* records the framework's own admission: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* Applied here, that means the figures cannot certify that this use of them is the right use. The round trip must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*). `11_audit.md` records that gap as R_T.
