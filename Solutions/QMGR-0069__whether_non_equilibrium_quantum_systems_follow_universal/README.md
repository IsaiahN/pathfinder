## What this question decides, in effect terms

Two effects are bundled in one sentence. (1) A within-domain claim: far-from-equilibrium quantum dynamics is governed by universal laws at all, not only by system-specific detail. (2) A cross-domain claim: those laws *mirror* the laws of gravity. Affirming the bundle as written buys a "same law" story whose evidence is often one shared mathematical pool, which is a mirror in the figures' sense, not a corroboration. Refusing the bundle's wording keeps a real comparison open: same kernel, different actors and anchors, with the specific empirical question left to a measurement.

Figure 9 :54 governs the structure: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* So this solution answers the two conditions separately and never averages their verdicts.
