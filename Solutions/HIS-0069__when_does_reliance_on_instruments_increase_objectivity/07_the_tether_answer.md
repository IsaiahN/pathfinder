# The Tether's answer: instruments, objectivity and new dependence

**Mode: `specified` on the six conditions, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks what happens when a mediator is inserted between a knower and a ground. The answer is not one threshold but a union of conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :54-55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Verdicts are per measurement slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and they are never averaged into "instruments help by X percent."

## 2. The shape: six conditions, two branches

**Branch A, where reliance increases objectivity:**

1. **The instrument stays an instrument.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The device helps while the question is ranged against something that does not move. The same device is the anchor for a question about its own scale and only an instrument for a question about the world (Fig 2 :37-38).
2. **Readings are independent.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :31-32: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Two instruments help only if they do not share the same evidence.
3. **A previously unread residual becomes readable.** Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access. Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."* Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Branch B, where reliance creates dependence and uncertainty:**

4. **The channel decays while the ground does not.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. The correct response is to restore contact with the anchor, not to read the instrument more carefully.
5. **The instrument becomes the anchor, or a convention decides the outcome.** Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Agreement then compounds while evidence does not (Fig 7 :13).
6. **The reading underdetermines its source, or the habitat is substituted.** Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* What is owed in the first case is a discriminator; in the second, a reproduction of the required contact.

The same device can sit in either branch depending on its bond to the ground (`06` Q1). Over a fixed atom set the two regimes are isomers: Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* The general condition that fixes the good branch is a functional group, `reading → ground`: Fig 12 :124, *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single "when" threshold, a universal amount of instrumentation, or a historical date at which instruments begin to help. That object is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*).
- Not that objectivity is the absence of human judgment. Trained judgment is part of the practice (world source W1), and the framework's own rule is that a frame cannot validate itself: *"the output is only as good as the validator"* (framework essay, opened 2026-11-23).
- Not that more instruments help. Fig 2 :31-32 makes independence, not count, the variable. Adding a reading that shares the evidence of an existing reading adds spelling, not search (Fig 8 :48-49).
- Not that a stable reading is a grounded one. Fig 5 :48: *"Its own output is never a measure of success."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken, and no specific instrument is named as the winner.
- Not a claim that world sources corroborate the verdict independently. Most share an author or an evidence pool; they are secondary.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

- **Anchor test.** Exhibit the reference for a measurement and ask whether its answer changes when a frame pushes on it. If it does, it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30).
- **Independence test.** Ask whether two readings draw on the same evidence, same calibration chain or same reduction. If they do, their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
- **Channel test.** Ask what would show that contact has been lost while the instrument still returns a value (Fig 10 :24-28). An uncertainty statement is necessary but not sufficient; it must accompany the reading (world source W5).
- **Discriminator test.** Enumerate the sources that could produce the reading. If several remain and no instrument discriminates them, report `unreached` (Fig 6 :27).
- **Habitat test.** For a simulated or bench result, name what was failed to reproduce and what was unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** an updating anchor that still increases objectivity; a gain from a single evidence pool; a decayed channel that still settles a verdict; a decision-fixing convention that remains a convention; a substituted habitat with no loss or addition.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The framework returns a shape and a discriminator requirement, not a name for the instrument and not the universal threshold. Six conditions were enumerated; where the figure set runs out, the answer stops and records the lineage (Fig 13 :129-130: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*). Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The gap is reported as a verdict. This is a simulated derivation, so it cannot settle anything it reports on; a ground reading, an instrument intercomparison, or a decisive historical case would be needed to move any condition to `grounded`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that instruments are objectivity, and it does not give a single crossover point. It says objectivity is a relation to something that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, so an instrument helps while it is ranged against such a ground and stops helping when it becomes the ground itself. It helps when two instruments are genuinely independent, two instruments pointed at the same thing being two frames, and when it makes a previously unread residual readable. It creates new dependence when the channel decays while the ground does not, when a convention quietly decides the outcome it was built to measure, when a reading cannot discriminate among the sources that could produce it, or when a test habitat is substituted for the real one. Over a fixed set of ingredients the good and bad regimes are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The framework returns that shape, the per-slot verdicts and five refuters written in advance. It refuses to name the winning instrument or the universal threshold; those are bindings from the world, or a declared gap. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
