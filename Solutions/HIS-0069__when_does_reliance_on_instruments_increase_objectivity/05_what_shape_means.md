## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried, not re-opened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For HIS-0069 the shape is a **six-condition separator with per-slot verdicts**: name the actor, currency and anchor for each condition, say whether the instrument is ranged against a non-updating ground, keep the verdicts separate, and refuse the universal threshold. The thing the question asks for, a single "when," is not a shape the figures return. It is a `gap` (Fig 13 :134).

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was described in effect terms (a mediator between knower and ground) before any field was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The effect description travelled to history of science, philosophy of science, human factors and metrology.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, the paradox being that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the separator is the finish line: once each condition has its anchor, the search for a single threshold stops making sense.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So the six-condition separator in `01` is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming a particular instrument or a particular historical turning point as *the* answer would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the "objectivity comes from instruments" reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several causes can create the same reading; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The case was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The naive historical reading treats the self-registering instrument as the source of objectivity. That is the metamerism error in structural form: a reading (the instrument's output) is treated as determining its source (the ground). What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 6 :27: *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."* This is Fig 2 :35 in essay form: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 5. What "shape" means specifically for HIS-0069

The answer the figures support is a shape with four fixed features and one open slot:

- **Fixed:** objectivity is a relation to an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29, :33); independence between readings is the currency of the gain (Fig 2 :31-32, :42); the channel can decay while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24); a reading can underdetermine its source (Fig 6 :27).
- **Fixed:** verdicts are per slot (Fig 1 :19), and the question is a union (Fig 9 :54-58), so no verdict is averaged into a single percentage of instrumentation.
- **Open:** which particular instrument, calibration, or historical intervention occupies a slot in a given case. That is a binding from the world, provenanced as such, never a derivation from the figures.
- **Declared gap:** the universal "when" threshold. Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* is the honest stop.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3/§4, kept visible

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any instrument. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not in Γ. Naming an instrument that is already available is a binding from a frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is (a) deriving a specific instrument from the six-condition shape alone, and (b) claiming that naming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*). The metamerism point in §4 is not retracted: the reason it stands is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous historians to agree about objectivity is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
