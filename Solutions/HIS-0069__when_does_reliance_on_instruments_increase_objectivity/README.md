## The question restated as an effect

The question asks what an inserted instrument does to the relation between a knower and a ground. Option A: the instrument extends perception, gives an independent reading, and increases objectivity, meaning it improves contact with something that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. Option B: the instrument becomes a frame in its own right, a channel that decays, a source that underdetermines its cause, or a habitat substituted for the real one, and the result is new dependence and uncertainty. What each option leads to: A leads to triangulation and a reachable anchor; B leads to coherent drift that looks like agreement, or to a reading whose source cannot be discriminated.

## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

The sought-for shape is **a condition set with per-slot verdicts**: for each measurement situation, name the actors, the currency, and the anchor; say whether the instrument is ranged against a non-updating ground or has become the ground; say whether two instruments are independent frames or one evidence pool wearing two names; then give the refuter. Written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run, this shape becomes refusable by the same test as any other claim (Formula, "Direction"). The one shape the figures refuse to fill is a single universal "when" in the abstract, because the question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one threshold (Fig 9 :4-5, :54-57). That refusal is reported as a `gap`, not as silence (Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade the label: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Second, correlation disqualifies a verdict: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Third, asking people to trust the framework before they check the output spends the only thing they contribute: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, harvested personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and would get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional and opt-in venues.
