## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the cosmology verdicts do not. The question is a union compound: tensor generation, amplitude threshold and instrument resolution get separate verdicts and are never averaged. Inflation licenses a tensor background whose amplitude is set by the energy scale, but the framework supplies neither a value of `r` nor a detection. Detectability is `r ⋛ sigma(r)`, a threshold comparison between amplitude and sensitivity at a stated pivot scale and sky fraction. The published bound `r < 0.036` excludes some large-field models and leaves the rest unreached, not absent: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The channel, not the sky, is the current limiter, and primordial and lensing B modes are isomers under the observed BB atoms. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is mode-labelled, the refuters precede the conclusion and the lineage is recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost would be spending an outside reader's independence by sending a verdict instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The union split and the threshold shape stand independently of any particular inflation model or forecast. A claimed detection or absence can be superseded without withdrawing a primitive. A sent claim can be replaced by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A 5-sigma B-mode spectrum with `r > 0` surviving null tests and foreground separation would settle the threshold in favour of detection; a demonstrated identity of primordial and lensing sources at every accessible frequency would fire the isomer falsifier; a proof that inflation cannot generate tensors would break D1; a bound from a sealed instrument with a maintained channel would convert "unreached" into "absent"; a re-census showing detection is not a `⋛` comparison would break the shape. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
