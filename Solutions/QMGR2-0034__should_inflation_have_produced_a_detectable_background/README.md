## Restatement in effect terms

The question splits into three conditions, not one:

1. Did inflation generate tensor perturbations at all? (theory frame)
2. Is their amplitude, set by the energy scale of inflation and parameterised by `r`, above the threshold of an instrument? (threshold comparison)
3. Can an instrument resolve that amplitude against foregrounds, lensing and systematics, so that a reading is returned rather than an upper limit? (instrument frame)

An answer that merges the three into one verdict is the named union error (Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). The shape any answer must have is a threshold relation between a tensor spectrum amplitude and an instrument sensitivity, written `r ⋛ σ(r)`. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and not a number for `r`.
