## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the answer can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- The shape is `r ⋛ sigma(r)`, a threshold comparison between a tensor spectrum amplitude and an instrument sensitivity, together with the union split into three conditions.
- A "name" in this context would be a measured amplitude, a detected B-mode spectrum, or a verdict that inflation did or did not occur. The framework supplies none of these. It supplies the form any such answer must have.

`r < 0.036` is a world-sourced upper limit, not a shape the figures produced. The figure-derived shape is the comparison itself (Fig 12 :87, "a threshold relation between two quantities").

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction to my own first reading

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A 5-sigma B-mode spectrum that survives null tests would name the amplitude.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

**Correction to my first reading.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming `r`. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The tensor background is not a missing primitive: spectrum is already one of the six (Fig 13 :19-20), energy is one of the six (Fig 13 :13-14), and comparison `⋛` is one of the seven bonds (Fig 12 :85-89). So naming `r` as the ordering quantity is a **binding from the physics frame onto held atoms**, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming that binding moves the wall or that it is a derivation. Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" adds none here. I keep the correction visible rather than erasing the first reading. This is the same correction the exemplar makes in `QMGR-0037/05_what_shape_means.md` §5b for Gradient and Spectrum.

## 4. The "union horizon" is the precise reading of a non-detection

*How Intelligence Actually Works* introduces the term that fits this question exactly: "There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another." And, at the collective level: "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." Also: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

A tensor signal below the sensitivity of every existing instrument is below the union horizon. It is not therefore absent. This is the essay-level restatement of Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence", and Fig 6 :27, "some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." The instrument report must say "not reached yet", with the reach stated.

The same essay separates two cases: "Case one is not learnable in the same way. 'No trace exists' can never be confirmed by better instruments." and "Case two is learnable. A trace exists, instruments improve, someone detects it." Applied here: if the tensor background exists, more search and better channels can reach it; if it does not, no amount of instrument improvement will produce a detection, and the absence itself remains unprovable from inside. This asymmetry is why the honest verdict is `candidate`, not `grounded` absence.

## 5. Metamerism: why the source cannot be named from one BB spectrum

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The colour example: a green reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a B-mode power spectrum has several possible sources. Primordial tensor modes, gravitational lensing of E modes, Galactic dust and synchrotron all produce curl polarization. A single BB bandpower cannot name which source wrote it. Multi-frequency component separation and delensing are the spectrometer, not a new theory. This is the supporting derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md` and the reason C3 in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks for a source-discriminating instrument.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

A list of famous cosmologists who agree that inflation should have produced a detectable background is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground (bandpowers, null tests, a measured `r`) settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` has no personal emails and no receptiveness rank. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds the budget discipline in figure language: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A forecast sensitivity is a filter.

## 8. The one-paragraph canonical reading

For this question, "shape" means the constraint that any answer about detectability must satisfy before it can be named: a threshold comparison between a tensor spectrum amplitude and an instrument sensitivity, embedded in a union of three separately anchored conditions, with non-detection reported as unreached rather than absent.
