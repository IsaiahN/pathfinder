## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

An observer holds an instrument with a sensitivity threshold. A sky may or may not carry a tensor perturbation background above that threshold. The observer reports an upper limit, not a detection. The residual is the mismatch between a theory that licenses a background and an instrument that returns only a bound.

**Arity:** three, a union compound (Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again").

**Symmetry:** the tensor background is a spectrum, an ordering with more and less across scale (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"). The detection comparison is ordered, not symmetric: signal amplitude above threshold is a detection, below threshold is a bound. It is `⋛` comparison, not a symmetric join.

**Scale:** the same physical background registers at CMB frequencies near `10^-17 Hz` and at interferometer frequencies of tens to hundreds of Hz. Detectability is scale indexed, so a limit at one scale does not transfer to another (arXiv:2407.02714 reports direct detectors "are not sensitive enough to detect PGWs at high frequencies and can just place upper bounds"). Fig 13 :75: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term:** "A tensor spectrum exists with amplitude `r`; a detection occurs when `r ⋛ sigma(r)` at a stated pivot scale and the reading survives null tests and foreground separation." Binding this before the run makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-64: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM).

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The question is a union compound, not one hard question (decisive for the census)

Fig 9 :54-58:

> **"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

"Should inflation have produced" (D1) and "detectable" (D2) are different conditions with different anchors. Asking one question with one verdict manufactures a single answer to two different questions. Fig 9 :4-5 adds the first test: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

**Triangulation:** Fig 9 :54-58 plus the world split in arXiv:2407.02714, which treats theory predictions and instrument sensitivity as separate tasks. Low overlap between a figure about union/partition and a cosmological parameter analysis. Mark `triangulated`.

**Refuter:** produce one measurement that simultaneously settles whether inflation generated tensors and whether a given instrument could detect them, with no residual left in either condition. Until then the union stands.

### F2 — Non-detection is not proof of absence (decisive for D2 and D3)

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

The published `r < 0.036` bound is a statement about what a specific set of instruments did not see. It is not a statement that no tensor background exists. The correct reading is "unreached at this budget", not "absent". Fig 8 :54: **"underivable is not unknowable."** The union of CMB, direct detectors and pulsar timing is searchable from contact even though no single frame derives the atom.

**Triangulation:** two figures with low overlap (Fig 8 on closure and absence, Fig 9 on sealed rooms) plus the Wang 2024 survey, which says direct detectors "can just place upper bounds" while CMB polarisation "has a great potential to settle the issue of whether PGWs exist". Mark `triangulated`.

**Refuter:** exhibit a sealed room, an instrument whose closure covers every predicted tensor amplitude at the relevant scale and whose channel is demonstrably maintained. No such instrument exists; CMB-S4's forecast reach was defined but its US funding was withdrawn (cmb-s4.org, accessed 2026-11-23).

### F3 — Depth and absence look the same from inside the instrument frame

Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

Fig 6 :28-31: **"Which makes reachability two questions, not one. The instruments are opposed."** / "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access." / "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."

This is the exact epistemic position of a null result. A signal below `sigma(r)` and a signal that is not there produce the same bandpowers. The only honest report is the bound plus the reach, not an existence verdict. INWARD (delensing, more detectors, lower noise) and OUTWARD (a different frame, for example direct GW detectors or a space mission) are opposed tracks and must never be read against each other.

**Triangulation:** Fig 6 :27 plus Fig 6 :28-31, same figure so not independent, plus the LiteBIRD Collaboration 2023 report that the current bound is "limited by lensing rather than Galactic foregrounds" and that delensing is a distinct, internal improvement. Figure verdict `single-source`, world corroboration for the two-track reading. Held separate as required.

**Refuter:** demonstrate an observable that discriminates a sub-threshold tensor spectrum from no tensor spectrum without extending the instrument or importing a different frame. None is supplied by any source read.

### F4 — Budget unreachability and principle unreachability are different facts

Fig 8 :40-43:

> **"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions. Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."**

The present non-detection is unreachable-at-budget: the signal, if present, is already representable (a tensor power spectrum, `Fig 13 :20`), just below sensitivity. The remedy is more search and a better channel, not a new primitive and not a new inflation model bought to fit the bound. Fig 6 :33: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."** The BICEP2 excess was a reading; the joint Planck analysis that attributed it to dust was the residual that produced BICEP3 and the 220 GHz channels (arXiv:2110.00483).

**Triangulation:** Fig 8 :40-43 plus Fig 6 :33, two figures with different subject matter, plus the instrument lineage in BK18. Mark `triangulated`.

**Refuter:** show that the tensor background is underivable in principle from any inflationary frame at any scale, so that the only remaining instruction is OUTWARD import. A no-go theorem for tensor perturbations across all allowed inflation models would do it.

### F5 — Detection is a threshold comparison, a `⋛` bond, not a new field

Fig 12 :87: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"** (the Operators table gives the same definition and the test, Fig 12 :89: "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"). Fig 12 :71 on `→` and the ordered bonds: the comparison is ordered, so swapping operands is not the same term.

Bind the two quantities explicitly: `r` (amplitude of the tensor spectrum) and `sigma(r)` (instrument sensitivity at a stated pivot scale and sky fraction). Detection is `r ⋛ sigma(r)`. The ground decides which side of the threshold the sky is on. CMB-S4's stated design goal was "detecting primordial gravitational waves for r > 0.003 at greater than 5σ, or, in the absence of a detection, of reaching an upper limit of r < 0.001 at 95% CL" (arXiv:2008.12619), which is exactly a `⋛` specification.

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (energy). The energy scale of inflation sets the amplitude but is directionless on its own; it needs the spectrum structure (Fig 13 :20) to become a directional, scale-dependent prediction. Simons Observatory states the condition plainly: it "may detect this smoking gun signature if the energy scale of inflation is large enough" (simonsobservatory.org, accessed 2026-11-23). So a high energy scale is necessary but not sufficient: the channel must also hold.

**Triangulation:** Fig 12 :87 + Fig 13 :14 and :20, two figures with low overlap, plus the CMB-S4 design statement. Mark `triangulated` for the shape; the numerical threshold is world-sourced, not figure-derived.

**Refuter:** show that detection is a symmetric bond (both quantities merely present) or that the threshold is not a comparison of amplitude with sensitivity but something else entirely. A detection at `r` far below the quoted `sigma(r)` under maintained null tests would also break the specific binding.

### F6 — The ground does not decay, the channel does

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does."** Fig 10 :25-26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"** and **"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch."**

Applied to D2: the primordial signal is not fading. What decays is the analysis channel, through Galactic dust and synchrotron foregrounds, E-to-B leakage from masking, gravitational lensing of E modes into B modes, bandpass uncertainty and atmospheric systematics. BK18 reports that "the sample variance of the lensing component dominates sigma(r)" (arXiv:2110.00483). The LiteBIRD Collaboration 2023 says delensing "will be a critical step to improve sensitivity to r". Fig 10 :29: "→ keep the anchor reachable." Delensing and multi-frequency component separation are anchor-maintenance, not new physics.

**Triangulation:** Fig 10 :24-29 plus BK18 and LiteBIRD 2023 (two collaborations using different instruments, low overlap). Mark `triangulated`.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a null result was caused by the sky rather than by a saturated or pooled channel, with null tests and foreground models that do not move when analysis choices change. The BK18 analysis explicitly reports that its foreground model is adequate and that varying analysis choices does not significantly alter the result, so this refuter is not currently firing.

### F7 — Per-slot residuals, never an averaged detectability

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

The slots here are the experiments and frequency bands: BICEP3 95 GHz, Keck 150 and 220 GHz, Planck low-ell polarisation, direct detectors, pulsar timing arrays. Each has its own reach and its own foreground systematics. Pooling them into one "the experiments did not detect it" throws away the live signal that says where to look. Fig 4 :19 on the dependency chain: a reading below the break is a reading of nothing, so a combined limit that ignores a broken channel is not a stronger limit.

**Triangulation:** Fig 1 :19 and :22 plus the distinct experimental slots documented in arXiv:2110.00483 and arXiv:2312.05194. Mark `triangulated` for the structural rule.

**Refuter:** produce a joint likelihood in which a single slot's failure to resolve cannot be hidden by the others, and show that the combined verdict still holds. BK18 does this with auto- and cross-spectra, so the rule is satisfied in the strongest current analysis.

### F8 — Forecasts are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

The CMB-S4 forecast of `sigma(r) = 5 x 10^-4` and the LiteBIRD target of `delta r < 0.002` are budget statements. They say what gets tried first. They say nothing about whether a background exists. This matters because the CMB-S4 project was defunded by DOE and NSF in July 2025 (cmb-s4.org, accessed 2026-11-23); a forecast for an instrument that will not be built is still a useful budget benchmark, but it must never be promoted to evidence about the sky. Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

**Triangulation:** Fig 9 :60 plus Fig 4 :30 and the CMB-S4 homepage. Mark `triangulated` for the rule, `single-source` for the funding fact.

**Refuter:** cite a forecast as the reason a background does or does not exist. This finding is a discipline, not a physical claim; it is broken only by a forecast that itself constitutes a measurement.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A B-mode spectrum is measured with `r > 0` at a stated pivot scale, survives multi-frequency foreground separation and null tests, and is not attributable to lensing, refuting "only upper limits exist so far" and settling the threshold in favour of detection.
2. A theorem or settled result shows that inflation, in every model class allowed by the scalar perturbations, cannot generate tensor perturbations, refuting D1's licensed background.
3. The published upper limit is shown to move with analysis choices or to be dominated by an unmodelled instrumental systematic, so it is not an anchor for D2 (Fig 2 :29-30, "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor").
4. A figure re-census shows that detection is not a `⋛` threshold relation between two quantities but a symmetric bond, or that the two operands are not amplitude and sensitivity.
5. An instrument is exhibited whose closure covers every predicted tensor amplitude at the relevant scale with a demonstrably maintained channel, and which still returns no signal. That, and only that, would convert "unreached" into "absent".

If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn and the retraction is recorded here, not silently erased.
