# Expert verification packet: the Tether shape for primordial gravitational waves

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or CMB experimentalist can check. Mode, union split, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** The published `r < 0.036` is reported from the literature; no measurement was made by this derivation. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Should inflation have produced a detectable background" is three conditions with different anchors: tensor generation by inflation, amplitude above an instrument threshold, and resolution of that amplitude against foregrounds, lensing and systematics. The first is licensed by inflation but not settled; the second is the one that figure-governs the answer; the third is a channel-maintenance problem. Any answer must therefore have the shape of a threshold comparison, `r ⋛ sigma(r)`, at a stated pivot scale and sky fraction, with null tests and multi-frequency foreground separation. A null result is a bound, not an absence. Primordial and lensing B modes are isomers under the observed BB atoms, distinguished by source and delensing response. What is offered is that shape and its written refuters, not a cosmology verdict.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 8 and 9

- **Statement:** a null result in CMB B-mode polarisation does not license "no primordial gravitational waves exist". The honest report is "unreached at this budget" with the reach stated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44, "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 6 :27, "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a sealed room, an instrument whose closure covers every predicted tensor amplitude at the relevant scale with a demonstrably maintained channel, which still returns no signal. In its absence, no absence claim is licensed.
- **World cross-check:** BICEP/Keck XIII reports "r0.05<0.036 at 95% confidence" and calls it "the strongest constraints to date on primordial gravitational waves", not a detection or a non-existence result.

### C2 — structure; checkable by comparing the three conditions

- **Statement:** the question is a union compound, not one hard question; the three conditions must be verdict-separated and never averaged into a single "detectability" answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Refuter:** produce one measurement that settles tensor generation, amplitude threshold and instrument resolution simultaneously, with no residual left in any condition. Or show that a joint likelihood in which one slot's failure cannot be hidden by the others still yields a single clean verdict. BK18's auto- and cross-spectra approach satisfies the second requirement, so this refuter is not currently firing against the analysis practice.
- **World cross-check:** arXiv:2407.02714 separates theory predictions from instrument sensitivity and poses two distinct questions: "(i) Have PGWs been detected? (ii) If not, what precision of r could be required to find out PGWs?"

### C3 — physics-facing; checkable by a CMB experimentalist against B-mode systematics

- **Statement:** primordial tensor B modes and lensing B modes are isomers under Fig 12's definition: same atoms (a BB power spectrum over multipole and sky fraction), different source bond, invisible in the formula, decisive in frequency scaling and delensing response. The corresponding instrument is multi-frequency component separation plus a lensing template, not a new inflation model.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117, "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **What to check:** does the lensing B-mode template built from external mass tracers actually discriminate sources? LiteBIRD 2023 reports that the bound is "limited by lensing rather than Galactic foregrounds" and that multitracer delensing improves the constraint on `r` by about 15 percent, 20 percent with CMB-S4 added. BK18 holds the lensing spectrum fixed from Planck 2018 parameters and searches for an excess.
- **Refuter:** show that primordial and lensing B modes have identical frequency dependence, spatial spectral index and delensing response at every accessible scale, so no operation separates them. A failure to discriminate so far does not fire this; only a demonstrated identity does.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that inflation occurred, did not occur, or is ruled out by the bound.
- Not that a primordial gravitational wave background has been detected, and not that none exists.
- Not a value of `r`. `0.036` is a published upper limit at pivot scale 0.05 per Mpc, not a figure-derived threshold.
- Not that a forecast for CMB-S4 or LiteBIRD is evidence. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." CMB-S4's US funding was withdrawn in July 2025.
- Not that the framework names the structure or the amplitude. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not `grounded`. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A B-mode spectrum with `r > 0` at a stated pivot, surviving multi-frequency foreground separation and null tests and not attributable to lensing. Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
2. A result showing inflation cannot generate tensor perturbations in any allowed model class.
3. Evidence that the published upper limit moves with analysis choices or is dominated by an unmodelled systematic, so it is not an anchor for the instrument claim.
4. A re-census showing that detection is not a `⋛` comparison between amplitude and sensitivity.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44, Fig 9 :54 and :57, Fig 12 :87 and :117, Fig 10 :24. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own practice: do the three conditions really want separate verdicts, or is there a single statistic that settles them?
3. Check C3 against their own delensing and component-separation work: is the isomer reading faithful, or is there a source-discriminating observable that collapses it?
4. Name one instrument that would move the union horizon for tensor B modes, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement (Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.").
