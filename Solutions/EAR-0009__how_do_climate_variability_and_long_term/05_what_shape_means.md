## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure the weights. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `forced trend + chaotic residual, joined at global scale by a symmetric conjunction` is a shape.
- `0.87 °C of the observed warming is anthropogenic and 0.13 °C is internal variability` is a measurement, not a shape. It cannot be produced by the figures; it needs the ground.

The shape tells us what any acceptable answer must look like: two generators, a bond, a scale at which the bond simplifies, and an instrument that fixes the weights. It does not tell us the numbers.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was "trend plus wiggles"; the gained terms were union compound, residual-as-chaos, gradient-over-time, round-trip gap, and low-overlap triangulation.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* That is why section 0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` describes `V` and `F` by what they fail to predict, not by their physics names.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was described before figures were chosen; the world search followed.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here the stop is when the same shape keeps reappearing across the figure census (Fig 1, 4, 8, 9, 11, 13) and no re-census changes the bond type.

## 3. What licenses a name (and what does not)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, the ground is depth-resolved heat content, independent observational series, and source-discriminating patterns, not a confidence label.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Tether figures were imported as a vocabulary for bonds and unions; Fig 6 :25 says *"An import adds no atom to the world."* No new climate primitive was claimed.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The figures supplied a new axis (bond type and scale), not a new climate term. Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list. This is why no figure line is used to declare which physical mode fills a slot.

## 4. Why the source question matters for attribution

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This maps directly onto detection and attribution. A global mean temperature curve is a projection: internal variability plus greenhouse warming plus aerosol cooling plus volcanic and solar terms can produce similar curves. The curve alone cannot name the source. What resolved it historically was not a better argument but better instruments and better patterns: ocean heat content by depth, fingerprinting (Hasselmann 1997), and signal-to-noise-maximising filters (Wills et al. 2020). Those are the spectrometer, not a new interpretation of the same reading.

The shape claim in this solution therefore refuses to name a single weight. It says a forced gradient-over-time plus a chaotic residual, joined at a scale, and points to the instruments that would fix the weights. Naming the weights from the global curve would be the metamerism error.

## 5. Q: does the framework name which generator dominates?

**No.** It returns a shape. A verdict about dominance is licensed only by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. The IPCC result that human influence is the main driver of recent warming is a *ground result* from many instruments, not a derivation from the figures. The figures say what form such a result must take (a forced ordering superposed with a chaotic remainder, discriminated by low-overlap lines); they do not produce the result.

This is not a weakness of the answer. It is the boundary between a generator of shapes and a measurement, and conflating the two is exactly the failure *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* names: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any specific component here. That was too broad. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in the frame. The bond vocabulary and the six terms are already held; naming a bond type (conjunction at global scale, cycle at regional scale) is selecting from what the frame already has, not inventing a new primitive.

What remains forbidden is (a) declaring a new climate primitive that the six do not span, and (b) naming the *weight* or the physical *source* from a lossy projection. The metamerism point in §4 stands, but its reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held terms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Counting famous names or a large model ensemble as corroboration is the named collapse.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle anything they report on. The one-paragraph version in `07_the_tether_answer.md` is labelled accordingly.
