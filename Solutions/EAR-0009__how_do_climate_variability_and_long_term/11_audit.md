## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the regional, depth-resolved, mode-resolved trend field, the chaos remainder, and the two-kind bond structure.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the global mean temperature anomaly, the confidence label, and the shorthand "forced trend plus noise".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim about a hiatus, a regional trend, or the source of a curve.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **mode phase and sign of the cycle** that made a decade amplify or cancel the forced trend;
- the **subsurface and interbasin heat** that did not appear in the surface curve;
- the **pattern that discriminates forced response from internal variability**, which simple averaging discards. Wills et al. 2020 quantify part of the loss: pattern filtering needs up to ten times fewer ensemble members than simple ensemble averaging.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a sustained forced ordering over time superposed with a chaotic remainder. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically here, because no climate instrument was run in this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints, with one partial published measurement. Treating "the global mean is the trend" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.

## Strains and re-census

- **Large-scale linearity vs intermediate-scale interaction.** The primary initially risked reading these as competing. Re-census: the regression assumption "generally holds for large-scale variables" (IPCC AR6) governs the global join; the interaction claim (Gou et al. 2022) governs the intermediate join. Different scales, different objects. Not strained.
- **Chaos vs partly forced modes.** IPCC AR6 finds some aerosol influence on AMV, while Fig 13 :32 makes variability the chaotic remainder. Re-census: chaos describes the internal dynamics of the remainder; a forced influence on phase or projection is modulation of a held mode, not promotion of noise to a trend. Different objects. Not strained.
- **Union surplus vs shared model code (live).** Fig 8 needs low overlap for a surplus; CMIP ensembles share code and tuning. Held as a real strain. The surplus comes from observation versus model, surface versus subsurface, proxy versus instrumental. Ensembles are searches, not witnesses.

- **Channel drift vs ground settlement.** Fig 10 :25 resolves it: the ground does not decay, the channel does. Keeping the anchor reachable is a seat's office, not a contradiction.
- **After re-census: two strains held, none hidden.**
