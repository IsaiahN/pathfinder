## What this package is, in effect terms

Observed climate trends are the output of two asserted generators: internal climate variability (the coupled atmosphere-ocean system running without external forcing) and long-term human-driven change (the forced response to anthropogenic radiative forcing). Each alone leaves a residual. Internal variability cannot carry a sustained centennial rise. A smooth forced trend cannot produce the observed interannual and decadal structure, including episodes such as 1998-2012 where the observed surface trend departs from the forced trend. The residual in effect terms is: what is the composition rule `C(V, F)` that turns a chaotic variability term and a forced trend term into the observed time series, at what scale, and what would settle which weights held?

An answer would look like a bond type plus a scale plus a discriminating instrument. Under the figures, the result is a scale-dependent union compound: a forced gradient-over-time (an ordering of energy through the climate system) superposed with a chaotic residual that re-enters each turn, joined by a symmetric conjunction at global scale and by ordered interaction at regional scale, with the ground settling the weights through low-overlap triangulation.
