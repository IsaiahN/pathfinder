# Expert verification packet: the Tether on variability plus forced change

**What this is:** a claim in a form a climate scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No percentage attribution is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Observed climate trends are a scale-dependent union of internal variability and the human-driven forced response. At global scale the join behaves as a symmetric conjunction: a sustained forced gradient-over-time (an ordering of energy through the climate system) plus a chaotic residual that re-enters each turn. At regional and decadal scale the forced pattern projects onto held internal modes and the mode phase modulates the surface expression of the forced trend, so the join is a cycle whose sign decides amplification or cancellation. The global mean is a lossy compression of the regional, depth-resolved field, so the round trip from global to regional carries a measurable gap. The figures supply the bond types, the dependency order and the refuters; the weights come from low-overlap measurement lines, not from the figures and not from the number of models that agree.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** internal variability is the chaotic remainder, not a second sustained trend generator; the forced trend is a gradient over time, a composition of energy and ordering. At global scale the join is the symmetric conjunction; at regional scale it is a cycle.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 13 :32 *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."*
  - Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."*
  - Operators table :15 *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."* (the test for the symmetric conjunction).
  - Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **Refuter:** an unforced control integration, reproduced across independent models, producing a persistent centennial global trend of the observed magnitude; or a demonstration that swapping forced and internal terms changes the large-scale fit; or a regime in which forced pattern and internal mode never re-enter each other's input.

### C2: earth-system facing; checkable against the observational record

- **Statement:** the surface trend is not the whole system trend. The energy that explains a surface slowdown is two or three steps out in contact: subsurface uptake, interbasin redistribution, storage below 700 m and 2000 m. A global mean is therefore a lossy compression of the deciding field.
- **What to check:** IPCC AR6 WG1 reports 58% of industrial-era heat uptake in 0-700 m, 21% in 700-2000 m and 22% below 2000 m, and calls the 1998-2012 slowdown a temporary event followed by strong warming with ocean heat content rising throughout. Gou, Liu and Wang (Tellus A, 2022) detail Pacific-to-Indian and Pacific-to-Southern-Ocean redistribution. NOAA Climate.gov states: *"Heat doesn't disappear; it just doesn't all show up at the surface."*
  - Fig 11 :52-53 *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
  - Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*
- **Refuter:** show that the observed surface trend is fully explained by first-order surface contact with no role for subsurface storage or basin redistribution, or that a global-to-regional-to-global round trip preserves the discriminating pattern (`R_T = 0` for this question).

### C3: instrumentation; checkable against detection-and-attribution practice

- **Statement:** the weights are fixed by low-overlap measurement lines, not by counting agreeing models. An ensemble mean is a subtraction that removes internal variability, so it cannot witness what variability did.
- **What to check:** Hasselmann (Climate Dynamics, 1997) requires the noise covariance of natural internal variability, not just agreement among runs. Wills et al. (Journal of Climate, 2020) show that spatially coherent internal modes "would otherwise alias into estimates of regional responses to forcing" and that pattern filtering finds the forced response "with up to ten times fewer ensemble members than simple ensemble averaging". Gou et al. (2022): an ensemble average "will, by definition, average out the internal variability leaving only the externally forced response."
  - Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
  - Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
  - Fig 10 :25 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
- **Refuter:** exhibit two measurement lines with genuinely different instruments and error structures that, after honest correction, disagree about whether a forced component exists; or show that a confidence label or ensemble size, by itself, settles a physical composition question.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a percentage attribution, and not a claim that natural variability caused the centennial warming.
- Not that a single global mean series settles regional attribution or depth-resolved heat uptake.
- Not that a large multi-model ensemble is independent evidence because its members agree. Shared code and tuning are one evidence pool.
- Not that the interaction term is measured. The instrument exists; it was not run here.
- Not that the 1998-2012 hiatus refuted forced warming.
- Not that CMIP models are useless. They are searches and instruments; they are not witnesses by virtue of agreement.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement taken in this folder.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Depth-resolved heat budget:** does the full water column close the energy imbalance when the surface trend does not?
2. **Pattern discrimination:** does signal-to-noise-maximising filtering separate the forced pattern from ENSO/IPO/AMV in the observed record, better than linear trend analysis?
3. **Sign of the cycle:** in a given regime, does the forced pattern project positively or negatively onto the held mode?
4. **Independent series:** do proxy and instrumental reconstructions with different error structures agree on the centennial shape?
5. **Standing falsifiers:** unforced centennial trend; zero interaction at all scales; an anchor that updates; a lossless global round trip; a swap that changes the large-scale fit.
