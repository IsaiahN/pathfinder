# Bond type, order and analogues for the variability / trend union

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

The shape claim says the join between internal variability `V` and forced change `F` is a symmetric conjunction at global scale and an ordered compound at regional scale. Three questions are owed: (1) is that bond assignment licensed by the figure's bond tests; (2) what is the dependency order of the composition; (3) are the two generators isomers, a functional group, or something else under Fig 12's definitions?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held in the climate frame: the six substrate terms (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network) plus the measured climate objects (forcing, heat content, modes, trend) as compositions over them. Bonds held: the seven, especially `+` conjunction, `→` sequence, `⇒` production, `−` subtraction, `⋛` comparison, and the `cycle` structure. `V` and `F` are not new primitives; they are molecules composed from held atoms. No new primitive is available without import.

## DERIVATION

### Q1: What bond holds at global scale? The swap test

- Operators table :15, the test for `+`: *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."* Fig 12 :57-59 gives the same test and the symmetric column "yes".
- The standard detection-and-attribution model (IPCC AR6 3.2.1, quoting Hasselmann 1997) writes the observation as a linear combination of externally forced signals plus internal variability, with the residuals representing internal variability.
- Swap test: relabel the forced term as residual and the residual as forced. At global scale the fit statistic is unchanged; what changes is only the interpretation, which is exactly what a symmetric bond means.
- ⇒ The global join behaves as **conjunction `+`**: both hold at once, order irrelevant. Fig 12 :55-57: *"both hold at once"*, symmetric, test *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*
- **Falsifier:** show that swapping the terms changes the large-scale fit. Then the bond is ordered and this assignment fails.

### Q2: What bond holds at regional and decadal scale?

- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- The forced response projects onto internal modes (for example a forced trend in the North Atlantic Oscillation), and the phase of those modes modulates the surface expression of the forced trend (Wills et al. 2020; Gou et al. 2022: "the trend between these two timescales is primarily the result of the interaction"). Output re-enters as input: a cycle.
- The **sign** decides whether it closes. A positive projection amplifies the regional trend; a negative projection produces a hiatus-like cancellation. This is why the same forced trend yields different observed regional trends depending on the mode phase.
- `⇒` production is refused for the modes themselves: production means *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* (Fig 12 :77-79), and ENSO, PDO/IPO and AMV exist in unforced control runs (Deser et al. 2012; IPCC AR6). Forcing modulates a held mode; it does not bring it into existence.
- ⇒ The regional join is a **cycle**, with the sign as the live question. Triangulated (Fig 12 cycle definition + the world pattern-filtering result). The sign's magnitude is unmeasured here.
- **Falsifier:** exhibit a regime where the forced component and the internal mode never re-enter each other's input, so no cycle closes. Then the regional join is a plain conjunction after all.

### Q3: Which term is subtracted?

- Fig 12 :82: subtraction is *"A with B removed or absent"*, with the test *"is the second operand's absence the point? Yes."*
- The ensemble mean is exactly a subtraction in effect: it removes internal variability to leave the forced response. Gou et al. (2022): an ensemble average *"will, by definition, average out the internal variability leaving only the externally forced response."*
- This is a legitimate instrument operation, not a claim that variability is absent from the world. Confusing the two is the error Fig 5 warns about when a mechanism fires but produces nothing new.
- **Falsifier:** show that the ensemble mean retains a specified fraction of internal variability as signal. Then it is not `−` and the filter reading must be redrawn.

### Q4: Are `V` and `F` isomers, a functional group, or neither?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Over the shared substrate `{space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network}`, both `V` and `F` are `state -> structure -> state` derivatives (F1 of `01`): internal variability is a gradient over space/time in the coupled ocean-atmosphere system; the forced trend is a gradient over time driven by energy. Same atoms, different bond and different operands, different behaviour. Under Fig 12's definition they are **isomers** over the full shared atom set.
- Read narrowly as climate objects (`F` = radiative forcing plus ensemble mean; `V` = ENSO/IPO/AMV), they do **not** share every atom: forcing is not a mode and a mode is not a forcing. They then share a **functional group** (a state-structure-state crossing) with different members, not full isomerhood.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either generator can be written without an atom the other requires (for example, if the forced trend can be composed with no spectrum/ordering over time, or a mode with no network of influence), the isomer claim fails and only the functional-group reading stands. This mirrors the exemplar's use of the same test.

### Q5: What is the order of the union?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- Dependency order for this question: reach the ground record (perception) -> hold terms for what forced and internal change afford (vocabulary) -> state what counts as a composition and what would refute it (objective) -> compose and attribute (planning) -> carry the method, not the recording, to the next question (learn and carry).
- The middle link is exactly where the bond `C` sits. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The instrument is pattern filtering plus depth-resolved heat content, which is why `04_links_and_distribution.md` leads with those sources.
- **Falsifier:** show that composition can proceed without stating an objective and refuter, and that a derived split is still checkable. Then the dependency order is wrong for this domain.

### Q6: The round trip `R_T`

- Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** Symbols table :77-78 defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`.
- Sent up (`x`): the regional, depth-resolved, mode-resolved trend field.
- Abstraction (`T_A`): the global mean temperature anomaly and the confidence label.
- Concretisation (`T_E`): the claim about what a regional trend or a hiatus means for the forced response.
- `R_T` is nonzero. What is lost: the mode phase that produced the hiatus, the subsurface heat that did not appear at the surface, and the pattern that discriminates forcing from internal variability. Wills et al. (2020) put a number on part of it: pattern filtering needs "up to ten times fewer ensemble members than simple ensemble averaging", so simple averaging throws away a measurable factor of information.
- **Falsifier:** a global-to-regional-to-global round trip that preserves the discriminating pattern. Then `R_T = 0` for this question and the global mean is sufficient.

## CONCLUSION

The union is **not one bond**. At global scale the join is the symmetric conjunction `+`, licensed by the swap test and the standard regression form of detection and attribution. At regional and decadal scale it is a **cycle** whose sign decides whether the join amplifies or cancels, licensed by Fig 12's cycle structure and the observed interaction between forced patterns and internal modes. Production `⇒` is refused for the modes because they exist in control runs. `V` and `F` are isomers over the full shared substrate and a functional group when read as narrow climate objects. The dependency order puts the bond at the unmeasured middle link of Fig 3, and the round trip from global to regional carries a nonzero, partly measured `R_T`.

This rescues the defensible part of the question without inventing a primitive: the answer is a bond type plus a scale plus an instrument. It does not supply a percentage split. `metric curve = forced source` remains a metamerism error (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §4); `forced trend and internal variability are scale-dependent isomers in the six-term set` is a checkable structural claim with a stated falsifier.
