## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described the residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A reader who has absorbed the whole architecture before answering has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Low-overlap measurement lines are worth more than a large pile of agreeing models.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and shape claim; (2) the five refuters, and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the sources, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method, each user writing their own refuter and mode.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. Record the verdict before showing any theory (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the links above. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they have the framework they share the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state their own ground and refuter: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* warns that simulated outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

**No attachments pushed, no bulk send.** Prefer a public submission portal or an opt-in download page to an unsolicited attachment. Legal constraints on unsolicited outreach apply in many jurisdictions; public institutional and opt-in venues avoid the practical failure as well as the structural one.
