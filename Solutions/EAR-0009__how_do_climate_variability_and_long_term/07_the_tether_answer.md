# The Tether's answer: how variability and human-driven change combine

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken here.

## 1. The residual it answers

Observed climate trends are produced by two asserted generators: internal variability `V` and the human-driven forced response `F`. `V` alone cannot carry a sustained centennial rise; `F` alone cannot produce the interannual and decadal structure, including the 1998-2012 surface hiatus while ocean heat content kept rising. The residual is the composition rule `C(V, F)`, the scale at which it holds, and the instrument that fixes the weights. It is a union compound, not one hard problem (Fig 9: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The join is scale-dependent.** At global scale it behaves as the symmetric conjunction `+` (Fig 12 :55-57, *"both hold at once"*; Operators table :15, *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No."*): a sustained forced gradient-over-time plus a chaotic residual. At regional and decadal scale it is a **cycle** (Fig 12 :121, *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes"*): the forced pattern projects onto internal modes and the mode phase modulates the surface expression of the forced trend. The two readings are not averaged.

2. **Internal variability is the chaotic remainder, not a second trend generator.** Fig 13 :32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* That is the figure's account of ENSO, the annular modes and weather noise. The sustained centennial direction belongs to the forced term.

3. **The forced trend is a gradient over time, a composition, not a bare state.** Fig 13 :76: *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Radiative forcing (energy) through a gradient over time composes a flow of warming. This is why the forced trend has a direction and why ocean heat uptake can oppose its surface expression.

4. **The global mean is a lossy compression.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* The round-trip gap `R_T` between the regional, depth-resolved field and the global mean must be measured. The hiatus was largely a story about that gap: the surface was flat while heat moved into the deeper ocean and between basins (Fig 11 :52-53, the residual three steps out).

5. **The ground settles the weights by low-overlap triangulation.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Model agreement is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60). The witnesses are independent measurement lines: surface versus subsurface heat content, instrumental versus proxy records, pattern filters versus simple averaging.

6. **The figures return a shape, not a number.** They say what any answer must look like: two generators, a bond, a scale, a discriminator. They do not say what fraction of a given trend is attributable. That fraction is a ground result, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that natural variability caused the centennial warming. The figures give variability the role of chaos and noise, not of a sustained directed trend.
- Not that a single global mean temperature series settles regional attribution or depth-resolved heat uptake. Aggregation is how a live residual disappears into an average (Fig 1 :22).
- Not that a large multi-model ensemble is independent evidence because its members agree. Shared code and tuning are one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the interaction term is measured. It is named; the instrument exists but was not run here.
- Not that the surface hiatus refuted forced warming. IPCC AR6 calls it a temporary event followed by strong warming, with ocean heat content rising throughout.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R, φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A better discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

- **Depth-resolved heat budget:** does the full water column close the energy imbalance when the surface trend does not? This is the measurement that turned the hiatus from a refutation into a cross-scale effect.
- **Pattern discrimination:** does signal-to-noise-maximising filtering separate the forced pattern from ENSO/IPO/AMV in the observed record (Wills et al. 2020) better than linear trend analysis? A positive result fixes the regional bond.
- **Sign of the cycle:** in a given regime, does the forced pattern project positively or negatively onto the held mode? This decides amplification versus cancellation.
- **Independent series:** do proxy and instrumental reconstructions, with different error structures, agree on the centennial shape? That is triangulation; agreement within one dataset family is not.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** an unforced control producing the observed centennial trend; a demonstrably zero interaction at all scales; an anchor that updates with the frame; a lossless global round trip; a swap of forced and internal terms that changes the large-scale fit.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The six-term substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

Two strains remain open and are not hidden: whether CMIP ensemble agreement counts as independent evidence (it mostly does not), and whether forced modulation of a held mode is a production bond or a cycle (cycle is the current fit; the discriminator is unrun).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a percentage split between natural variability and human influence, and it does not claim to settle attribution. It says that the two combine as a scale-dependent union: at global scale the observed trend is a sustained forced flow, an ordering of energy over time, superposed with a chaotic residual that re-enters each turn; at regional and decadal scale the forced pattern and the internal modes re-enter each other's input, so the join is a cycle whose sign decides whether a decade amplifies or cancels the trend. The global mean is a lossy compression of that field, which is why a surface hiatus can coexist with a rising ocean heat content. The figures supply the bond vocabulary and the refuters, not the weights; the weights come from low-overlap measurement lines, and the answer stops where those instruments stop discriminating. This is a `specified` shape from a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
