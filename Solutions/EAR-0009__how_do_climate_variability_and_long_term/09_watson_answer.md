## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how two asserted generators combine to produce observed climate trends: internal variability `V` (the coupled climate system running without forced change) and long-term human-driven change `F` (the forced response to anthropogenic radiative forcing). Option A: treat the question as a single additive partition and report a percentage split. Option B: treat it as a scale-dependent union compound and report a bond type, a scale, and a discriminating instrument. What each leads to: A hides the interaction, the cross-scale gap and the evidence-pool problem, and can mistake a confidence label for a verdict; B states a checkable shape with refuters written in advance and leaves the weights to the ground.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity two (`V`, `F`). Symmetry: the candidate bonds are the symmetric conjunction and the ordered cycle or production; comparison is a possible threshold operator between them. Scale: interannual to centennial, cross-scale by construction. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." names it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the observational ground.** Actors: thermometers, satellites, Argo floats, ocean heat content, paleo proxies, reanalyses. Currency: temperature anomaly, heat content, sea level. Anchor: the ground itself, which does not change its answer because a frame thinks something (Fig 2 :29). Contains a real partition sub-question: real change versus observational artifact.
- **Domain 2, the forced-response frame.** Actors: greenhouse gases, aerosols, solar and volcanic forcing, CMIP models, energy imbalance. Currency: radiative forcing and ensemble-mean response. Anchor: the top-of-atmosphere energy budget and ocean heat uptake.
- **Domain 3, the internal-variability frame.** Actors: ENSO, PDO/IPO, AMO/AMV, annular modes, weather noise. Currency: variance, covariance, control-run spread. Anchor: unforced control integrations and the residual after forced-signal removal.
- **Domain 4, the detection-and-attribution frame.** Actors: optimal fingerprinting, signal-to-noise-maximising pattern filters, single-model large ensembles. Currency: signal-to-noise ratio, scaling factors. Anchor: the ground through tests a frame cannot grade itself.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Reach the ground record, then hold terms for what each generator affords, then state what counts as a composition and what would refute it, then compose and attribute, then carry the method to the next question. More models land above the middle link if the bond is never stated.

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between a large-scale linear combination and an intermediate-scale interaction is a scale distinction, not a contradiction. Re-census by scale dissolves it. Two genuine strains remain: ensemble independence and the production-versus-cycle distinction. Both are named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 and in the verdict below.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate but never overrule the figures. Fields were chosen for low overlap with the framework and with each other: assessment, climate dynamics, statistical detection, signal processing, public communication, and regional mechanisms.

- **Assessment / earth systems.** Eyring, Gillett et al., IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 3 (IPCC / Cambridge University Press, 2021, https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-3): "the observed change consists of a linear combination of externally forced signals plus internal variability, which generally holds for large-scale variables" and "the residuals represent internal variability". Also: internal variability and solar and volcanic forcing "partly offset the anthropogenic surface warming trend over the 1998-2012 period". Predicts a conjunction at large scale and a compound at smaller scale; corroborates F1 and F2. Overlap: independent of the figures; shares the general climate literature with the other climate sources.
- **Climate dynamics.** Deser, Phillips, Bourdette and Teng, Climate Dynamics 38:527-546 (2012, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00382-010-0977-x): "Internal variability is estimated to account for at least half of the inter-model spread in projected climate trends"; forced temperature changes are "detected earlier and with fewer ensemble members than those in atmospheric circulation and precipitation". Predicts that variability is spread, not trend, and that the coarse global field loses information; corroborates the chaos and scale findings. Overlap: distinct author group and method from the assessment source.
- **Statistical detection.** K. - **Signal processing / methods.** Wills, Battisti, Armour, Schneider and Deser, Journal of Climate 33(20):8693-8719 (AMS, 2020, https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/mqh7j-b8e38): spatially coherent internal modes "would otherwise alias into estimates of regional responses to forcing"; pattern filtering finds the forced response "with up to ten times fewer ensemble members than simple ensemble averaging". ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the requested percentage split does not, because the figures cannot produce weights. The two generators combine as a scale-dependent union. At global scale the observed trend is a sustained forced flow, an ordering of energy over time, superposed with a chaotic residual that re-enters each turn; the join behaves as the symmetric conjunction and D&A regression is its ordinary form. At regional and decadal scale the forced pattern projects onto held internal modes and the mode phase modulates the surface expression of the forced trend, so the join is a cycle whose sign decides amplification or cancellation. Production is refused for the modes because they exist in control runs. The global mean is a lossy compression of the regional, depth-resolved field, and the round-trip gap is partly measured. The weights are settled by low-overlap measurement lines, not by model agreement or confidence labels. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result from a simulated derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim states its bond, its scale, its strains and five falsifiers before any evidence, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is presenting a shape as a number, or presenting model agreement as corroboration, which spends the independence of outside readers. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bond assignment and the scale qualifier can be revised without touching the domain census or withdrawing the residual. If the interaction term is measured to be zero at all scales, the union narrows to a partition and the cycle reading is dropped; no primitive has to be removed. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An unforced control integration producing the observed centennial trend would promote variability to a trend generator; a demonstration that the interaction term is zero at all scales would collapse the union to a partition; a demonstration that observational series move with the attribution frame would break the anchor; a lossless global round trip would remove the cross-scale gap; a swap of forced and internal terms that changes the large-scale fit would make the bond ordered at global scale. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
