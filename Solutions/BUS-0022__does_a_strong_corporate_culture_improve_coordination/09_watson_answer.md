## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether a widely shared and strongly held set of meanings in a firm improves coordination or suppresses dissent and independent judgment. Option A: treat it as one dial with one best setting and return a single verdict. Option B: treat it as a union of conditions with different anchors and return separate verdicts. What each leads to: A returns a `general`-mode verdict that hides a per-slot residual, mistakes a convention for a verdict, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a shape, five refuters written in advance, a structural separator, and a declared gap where no instrument yet discriminates. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union. Order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unchosen sign of shared meaning: does `R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` shrink at a decision slot while the capacity to refuse a wrong term stays intact? Arity: at least two conditions, each indexed per slot. Symmetry: "coordination" is a symmetric agreement about a code (Fig 12 :59), while "improve" and "suppress" are ordered claims, so a pooled correlation cannot choose the arrow. Scale: member loop, firm pool, time channel; the transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4).
- **D1 Coordination / convention.** Actors: members, shared code, conventions, roles. Currency: description length, coordination cost, transfer of methods and head starts. Anchor: the product, the shared meaning itself (Fig 2 :41). Domain type: convention.
- **D2 Judgment / verdict.** Actors: members as frames, the evidence pool, a decision seat. Currency: independence and residual. Anchor: an external ground that does not update on what the frames think (Fig 2 :29, :33). Domain type: verdict.
- **D3 Convention promoted to verdict.** Actors: the convention, the decision seat, the shared report. Currency: verdict versus convention. Anchor: the ground, which a convention cannot decide (Fig 10 :47). Domain type: named failure genus.
- **D4 Measurement of "strong."** Actors: the culture score, decision slots, instruments. Currency: per-slot live residual versus a pooled average. Anchor: ground readings at the slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). Domain type: channel failure.
- **D5 Preservation of dissent as a seat.** Actors: a checker or dissenter, the ground maintainer. Currency: refusal, non-authorship. Anchor: the non-updating ground (Fig 10 :42, :52). Domain type: checking arrangement.
- **X1 Culture strength as a free-floating property with one sign** has no nameable anchor once the anchor is renamed per domain, so it is not a domain. It dissolves into D1 to D4.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 overlap: the same shared code that makes coordination cheap is the same overlap that destroys independence. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Read the per-slot residual before naming a frame, and refuse before mint.
- **Strain test applied.** Fig 2 :41 (agreement efficient for a convention) and Fig 2 :40 (agreement disqualifying for a verdict) seemed to contradict. Re-census: Fig 2 :39 names valence as the rule and the two lines govern different objects. No strain after census. One live type question remains in D5 (is a dissent seat an instance of unary negation or a binary relation?) and one world conflict is recorded in WORLD below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is refused and replaced by five separate verdicts, never averaged. D1: a shared meaning is cheap for a convention and transfers as a method or head start, `specified`, triangulated. D2: the same sharing suppresses judgment when evidence overlap is high and the anchor updates or disappears, `specified`, triangulated. D3: a convention promoted into an outcome stops being a convention, `general`, triangulated. D4: one culture score hides per-slot live residuals and lets the frame score itself, `specified`, triangulated. D5: preserve judgment with a non-updating ground and a refusing seat, not a standing list of contrarians, `specified`, triangulated. Culture's valence is relative to the residual, so no standing pro-or-anti verdict survives. The numerical strength and universal intervention are a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. The business evidence pool corroborates valence flipping after Sorensen is re-mapped to Fig 11 :49-51; the Aldag conflict was returned to census and narrowed the mechanism claim without flipping the verdict; Asch and Condorcet corroborate the seat and independence shape from genuinely different fields.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected intervention. A firm failing D2 would be sold more culture programs, and a firm failing D1 would be told to add dissent without a shared code, so a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is real, bounded and avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if a finding fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The five verdicts, five refuters, two strains and the gap are recorded with provenance (Fig 10 :62-64), so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4, in particular (1) a measured convention that fails to lower coordination cost, (2) low-overlap frames sharing the code producing no accuracy surplus where the ground pays, (3) an updating anchor still settling the verdict correctly across a change of residual, (4) a single score shown to preserve a per-slot live residual across a change of ground, or (5) a figure line that selects a numerical strength. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that the sign of shared meaning does not flip with the habitat would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. No endorsement or prestige is such an instrument (Fig 2 :40).
