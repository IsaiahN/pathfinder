## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: at least two conditions, each indexed per decision slot, so a single global score is the wrong object (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the agreement relation is symmetric while "improve" and "suppress" are ordered, so a pooled correlation cannot choose the direction. Scale: member loop, firm pool, time channel, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, convention / coordination.** Actors: members, shared code, roles. Currency: description cost, transfer of method or recording. Anchor: the shared meaning as product. Question type: convention.
- **Domain 2, verdict / judgment.** Actors: members as frames, evidence pool, decision seat. Currency: independence. Anchor: a ground that does not update. Question type: verdict.
- **Domain 3, failure genus.** Actors: convention, seat, report. Currency: convention versus verdict. Anchor: the ground. Question type: category error, a convention promoted to an outcome.
- **Domain 4, measurement.** Actors: culture score, slots, instruments. Currency: live residual versus pooled average. Anchor: ground reading at the slot. Question type: channel failure.
- **Domain 5, checking arrangement.** Actors: checker, ground maintainer. Currency: refusal. Anchor: non-updating ground. Question type: seat design.
- **X1, culture strength as a one-sign property.** No nameable anchor once the anchor is renamed per domain, so not yet a domain. Dissolved.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :39-41 (valence), Fig 2 :31-35 (anchor and independence), Fig 10 :47 (convention promoted to verdict), Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot), Fig 8 :47-49 (one parameter read twice).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`, `08` and `09`: the question is a union of two conditions; sharing is cheap for conventions and dangerous for verdicts with an updating anchor; a score hides the slot; keep a seat and a ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete organizational claim, for example "increase cultural homogeneity to improve coordination" or "add a devil's advocate to fix judgment."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot condition**, which a firm-level score or a firm-wide intervention drops;
- the **method-versus-recording distinction** (Fig 4 :23, :25), which a culture program can silently erase;
- the **anchor test**, namely that the reference must not move when the frames push on it (Fig 2 :29-30), which an internal-sentiment anchor fails;
- the **union structure**, which a single lever cannot clear (Fig 9 :55-57), so a single intervention leaves the intersection standing.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the sign of shared meaning flips with the kind of anchor and the residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
