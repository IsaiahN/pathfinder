## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The Sorensen result is the closest empirical match to the figure verdict.** Strong culture improves reliability in stable environments and loses that benefit in volatile ones. That is exactly Fig 11 :49-51 valence relative to the residual, with the environment as the habitat. It does not settle the figure claim, because the figure claim is structural and Sorensen's is a sample-level correlation. It corroborates the shape from a different field, with one caveat: it is the same broad business evidence pool as the management sources, so it is one witness, not several.
2. **Aldag's critique is a real conflict and is recorded as one.** It targets the empirical cohesion-to-groupthink mechanism. The figures' claim is about evidence overlap and a non-updating anchor, not emotional cohesion, so the conflict sends the derivation back to the census and the verdict survives with a narrower wording. It is not smoothed over.
3. **Asch and Condorcet are the low-overlap witnesses.** One is an experimental human-cognition result showing that a single dissenter breaks unanimity; the other is a mathematical theorem showing that independence is what makes majority accuracy possible. Different currencies, same directional constraint: keep a frame that can range independently against a ground. Neither is business literature, so their agreement is not a second reading of the same pool.

## The two-stage offer, restated

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The reader checks the output without holding the derivation's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first and never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3, if offered, sends a method.** Fig 4 :22 permits it. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because Formula "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a simulated derivation and says so; that is not the same as surviving.
