# Expert verification packet — the Tether on computation in non-digital substrates

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in analog or unconventional computation can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain casts, `gap` on the literal per-substrate definition. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle the definition. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The substrate question "how should computation be defined in analog, chemical, biological and quantum systems" dissolves under the figures; the contact question does not. One invariant loop runs in all four: a bounded frame whose prediction is compared with an observation, whose residual is indexed per slot, whose new terms are gated by support, reachability and novelty, and whose settlement is paid by a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks. The four named systems are casts of actors, currency and anchor, not four definitions. Discreteness and continuity are remainders of composition, so the four classes form a union with leaking boundaries. Within a substrate, the arrangement (bond), not the ingredient list, is the object of description. The framework does not return a unique substrate-specific name; that is a declared gap, reported as unreached rather than silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 4 and 2

- **Statement:** the substrate is not where the verdict lives. Define the invariant loop; cast actors, currency and anchor per substrate.
- **Sources, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :41 — "The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not."
  - Fig 4 :19-20 — "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
  - Fig 2 :29 — "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a substrate property that changes whether a process can compute while ground contact and the per-slot residual are held fixed; or show two substrate classes that need different loop structure while both settle at the ground.

### C2 — interface with the substrate literatures; checkable by a researcher in the field

- **Statement:** the four classes are a union, not a partition, because discreteness and continuity are remainders rather than primitives, and hybrids and mixed descriptions are the rule.
- **Sources, verbatim:**
  - Fig 13 :124-125 — "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."
  - Fig 9 :55 — "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
  - Fig 1 :19 — "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** is the union claim faithful to the literature? Bournez and Pouly (2018) say even discrete versus continuous is not a clear dichotomy and most historical analog machines were hybrid; Chen, Doty and Soloveichik (2013) say chemical rates are analog while stoichiometry is naturally digital; Flamm, Merkle and Stadler (2025) say chemical computation may refer to two fundamentally different aspects, concentrations and molecular structures. Are these three findings evidence of a union, or of three unrelated uses of one word?
- **Refuter:** produce a clean partition of the four substrates with no hybrid, no shared atom and no mixed discrete/continuous process; or show that a global computes bit predicts failure locations as well as per-slot residuals do.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a live type question

- **Statement:** within one substrate, the bond carries information the ingredient list does not, so two processes with the same components under different bonds are different computations. The quantum case leaves one type question open: the measured state updates, so the recorded outcome is offered as the anchor.
- **Sources, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :5 — "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
  - Fig 12 :117 — "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 12 :125-126 — "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."
- **Refuter:** show that two processes with the same atoms under different bonds behave identically under every instrument that can discriminate the residual, so the bond carries nothing; or show that a recorded measurement outcome does not satisfy the anchor condition in Fig 2 :29. The second would change D4's verdict and leave C1 and C2 standing. It is stated here as a live question, not buried.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not four definitions, one per substrate, and not a superset definition that simply lists all four.
- Not that computation is whatever an observer maps onto a physical process. The mapping selects what is checked; the ground settles the check.
- Not that every physical process computes, and not that a rock implements a NOT gate because it can be described by one.
- Not that analog and digital are two kinds, or that discreteness is a primitive.
- Not that a substrate literature corroborates the figures. Each is an import with a source gate and shadow test.
- Not that the quantum anchor question is settled. It is live (S1 in `01` §6).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not another definition.

1. An instrument that returns which slot is live in a physical process, so the residual is readable per slot rather than aggregated.
2. Two low-overlap frames with different casts, each ranging against an anchor neither can move, with the same verdict returned. Absence of such an anchor for a substrate is a `gap`, not silence.
3. The quantum type test: does a recorded measurement outcome satisfy the non-updating anchor condition, given that the state updates? A correction here changes D4, not the shape.
4. The standing refuters in `01` §4, written before the derivation.
