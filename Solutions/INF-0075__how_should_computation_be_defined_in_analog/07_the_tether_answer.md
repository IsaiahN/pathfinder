# The Tether's answer: how computation should be defined

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain casts, `gap` on the literal per-substrate definition. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a definition keyed to substrate. The effect that needs settling is: what would make the verdict "this process computes" answerable by something other than the observer's choice of description? The four substrate literatures each work well on a different subset and the disagreement does not shrink with more description, so this is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5, :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **The substrate question is refused; the contact question is answered.** Fig 11 :41: "The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not." The bird/lift move replaces "which substrate is computation" with "what makes a physical process capable of being checked as computing". Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone (Fig 11 :39).
2. **The invariant is the loop; the four systems are casts.** Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Define computation by: a bounded frame with Γ, a prediction Γ(b,a) against an observation o′, a residual indexed per slot, gated minting (support, reachability, novelty), and settlement by a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks.
3. **A global computes bit is disqualified.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." In D2 this is literal: the two aspects, concentrations and molecular structures, are different computations, not one chemical computation with two views.
4. **Frame-internal yardsticks are disqualified.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." "Symbols manipulated", "information processed" and "states traversed" score the frame with what the frame produces.
5. **The observer supplies the cast; the ground supplies the verdict.** Fig 2 :29: an anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Neither "computation is observer-relative" nor "computation is observer-independent" is the answer. The mapping selects what is checked; it cannot be what settles the check.
6. **Discrete and continuous are remainders, not primitives.** Fig 13 :124-125: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member." The four classes form a union with leaking boundaries, not a partition.
7. **Within a substrate, the arrangement is the object.** Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Processes with the same components under different bonds are isomers; catalysts and cycles are structures a flat ingredient list cannot record.
8. **Two currencies, not one power score.** Fig 12 :125-126: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

## 3. Per-domain verdicts (kept separate, never averaged)

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 analog | continuous variables, integrators, trajectories | trajectory residual; bits and actions held apart | finite-precision instrument | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4, Fig 13; Bournez and Pouly 2018) |
| D2 chemical | species, counts, catalysts, reaction networks | digital counts and analog rates; product yield | counter or measured yield | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 :19; Chen et al. 2013; Flamm et al. 2025) |
| D3 biological | proteins, cells, lineages, habitat | contact and survival; agent-plus-habitat capability | outcome that does not update on the organism's report | `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 :36/:39; Bray 1995) |
| D4 quantum | states, qubits, gates, outcomes | prediction versus Born outcome; accessible versus specification information | recorded measurement outcome, with a live type strain | `specified`, single-source on the figure side plus SEP; strain S1 named |
| D0 definitional | frames with Γ, b, a, o′, R | per-slot residual; settled versus candidate | the ground, never agreement | `specified` on the shape, `gap` on the name |

No row is promoted to `grounded`. The `gap` in D0 is the literal request for a definition in each substrate; it is unreached, not silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that computation is a fixed feature list of symbols, states or spikes.
- Not that analog and digital are two kinds of computation, or that one substrate is the real one.
- Not that any physical process is computing because it can be described computationally. The rock example in the SEP is exactly what the shape refuses.
- Not that the observer decides the verdict. The observer names the slot; the ground settles it.
- Not that a substrate literature is corroboration for the figures. Each is an import with a provenance record and a shadow test.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument returned a verdict on which substrate defines computation.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator, not another definition. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot discriminator:** an instrument that returns which slot is live in a process, so the residual can be read per slot instead of being aggregated.
- **Anchor test:** a case where two low-overlap frames, each with its own cast, range against an anchor that neither can move, and the anchor returns the same verdict. If no such anchor can be named for a substrate, that substrate's definition is `gap`.
- **Quantum type test (live strain S1):** does a recorded measurement outcome count as something that does not update in Fig 2 :29's sense, given that the state itself updates on measurement? A correction here changes D4, not the shape.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §4: a substrate property that flips computational status at fixed contact; two classes needing different loop structure; a clean partition; a mapping-only verdict with a non-updating anchor; a global bit that predicts failure as well as per-slot residuals; a definition that clears all four mint gates.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four substrate vocabularies are "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*, opened this session); declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six (Fig 13). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README). Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give four definitions of computation, one per substrate. It refuses the substrate question the way Cayley refused the bird question: the substrate is not where the verdict lives. What survives is one loop, whatever it runs in: a bounded system whose prediction is compared with an observation, whose mismatch is read per slot instead of averaged, whose new terms are licensed only when they are supported, reachable and new, and whose settlement is paid by something that does not change its answer because of what any observer thinks. Analog, chemical, biological and quantum systems are different casts of actors, currency and anchor for that loop, and because hybrid and mixed cases are common they form a union with leaking boundaries, not a partition. Discreteness and smoothness are what a composition leaves behind, not primitive kinds. The observer chooses the cast; the ground chooses the verdict. What the framework does not return is a unique substrate-specific name for computation, and it says so rather than inventing one. The falsifiers were written before the derivation, and the one open type question about the quantum anchor is stated rather than hidden.
