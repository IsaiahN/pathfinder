## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what an answer to "how should computation be defined in analog, chemical, biological and quantum systems" must look like. Option A: produce four substrate-keyed definitions and treat the substrate as the ground of the verdict. Option B: refuse the substrate question, define the invariant contact loop, cast the four systems as different actors, currency and anchor, and report the literal per-substrate name as a `gap`. What each leads to: A gives a list whose boundaries leak, lets a description manufacture the verdict, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives one checkable shape, four separate verdicts and a stated stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A verdict about a physical process that does not shrink when more descriptions are added. Arity: at least four conditions bundled (invariant kernel, substrate cast, observer/verdict, granularity). Symmetry: the verdict is asymmetric between process and ground, but the mapping from physical states to computational states is symmetric under relabeling, which is why mapping-only accounts trivialize. Scale: first order within a substrate, second order when a settled process becomes an atom for another frame. Fig 9 :4-5 makes this several well-formed questions, not one hard one.
- **D0, the definitional frame.** Actors: bounded frames with Γ, b, a, o′, R. Currency: per-slot residual, settled versus candidate terms. Anchor: the ground, a measurement or outcome that does not update on any frame's report. Type: a union of four conditions.
- **D1, analog systems.** Actors: continuous variables, integrators, trajectories. Currency: trajectory residual; bits and actions held apart. Anchor: a finite-precision instrument. Type: compound with D2 and D4.
- **D2, chemical systems.** Actors: species, counts, catalysts, reaction networks. Currency: digital stoichiometry and analog rates; product yield. Anchor: a counter or measured yield. Type: compound with D1 and D3.
- **D3, biological systems.** Actors: proteins, cells, lineages, habitat actors. Currency: contact and survival; agent-plus-habitat capability. Anchor: an outcome that does not update on the organism's own report. Type: compound with D2.
- **D4, quantum systems.** Actors: states, qubits, gates, measurement outcomes. Currency: prediction versus Born outcome; accessible versus specification information. Anchor: the recorded outcome, with a live type strain because the state updates. Type: compound with D1.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :55 states that a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions. Hybrid analog machines, chemical systems with both digital counts and analog rates, biological systems implemented in chemistry and quantum models that may be analog all block a partition.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the substrate question before minting (Formula step 2); describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); fix the invariant before casting; settle each first-order process before second-order composition (Fig 12 :104); import only with provenance and a shadow test.

Re-census assigns Fig 13 to the physical substrate the loop assumes, Fig 4 to invariance across casts and Fig 12 to arrangement within a cast. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains: the quantum anchor type question (S1), named in `01` §6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The substrate question dissolves; the contact question does not. Computation should be defined as one invariant loop with a per-slot residual, gated minting and a ground that does not update on any frame's report, with analog, chemical, biological and quantum systems serving as casts of actors, currency and anchor. Discreteness and continuity are remainders of composition, so the four classes form a union with leaking boundaries, not a partition. Within a substrate, the bond and arrangement carry the information the ingredient list misses. The observer supplies the cast; the ground supplies the verdict. The literal request for a definition in each substrate is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached rather than silence. `general` on the shape, `specified` on the casts, not `grounded`. One live strain remains: the quantum anchor type question.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent as written, because it states mode, refuters, provenance and the live strain before the conclusion. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished definition instead of a checkable shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The per-substrate list was never asserted, so no primitive has to be withdrawn. If the quantum anchor test fires, only D4 changes; C1 and C2 stand. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that returns a per-slot residual, two low-overlap frames ranging against an anchor neither can move, a clean partition of the four substrates, a substrate property that flips computational status at fixed contact, or a definition clearing all four mint gates would reverse the verdict. A derivation of discreteness from the other five members would reverse Q2 of `06`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
