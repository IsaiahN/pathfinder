## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The rock is the refuter, not a curiosity.** The SEP's simple mapping account makes a rock under the sun implement a NOT gate with feedback; counterfactual, causal, dispositional, robust-mapping and mechanistic accounts were invented to block that result. This is the world-side version of Fig 2 :29 and Fig 1 :27. Cite §2.1 when explaining why "the observer maps states to outputs" cannot be the whole definition.
2. **Substrate differences are differences of vehicle, not of kind.** The SEP's mechanistic account says digital, analog and quantum computation are "species of generic computation" and calls the relevant descriptions substrate neutral or medium flexible. Bournez and Pouly add that discrete versus continuous is not a clear dichotomy and that most historical analog machines were hybrid. This is why the four substrate classes are a union, not a partition.
3. **Precision and identity are different currencies.** Chen, Doty and Soloveichik write that in chemical reaction networks the rates are analog quantities while identity and stoichiometry are naturally digital. The SEP's quantum entry distinguishes accessible information (at most one bit) from specification information (conserved under unitary evolution). Neither reduces to the other, which is Fig 12 :125 in two separate literatures.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interiors.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and their later agreement never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
