## The question restated as an effect

The question asks for one word, "computation", to be made precise in four substrates. The effect to be decided is not the word. It is this: what would have to be observed, in a physical process, for the claim "this process computes" to be settled by something other than the observer's choice of description? Two options and what each leads to:

- **Option A:** define computation by substrate signature (discrete symbols, continuous trajectories, molecular counts, neural circuits, qubits). This leads to a list of four definitions whose boundaries leak into one another, and to a verdict that a description can manufacture by relabeling the same process. The Stanford Encyclopedia's simple mapping account is exactly this failure: under it a rock under the sun implements a NOT gate with feedback.
- **Option B:** define computation by the substrate-invariant contact loop, and treat the four substrate classes as different casts of actors, currency and anchor. This leads to one checkable shape, per-domain verdicts kept separate, and an honest stop where no instrument discriminates.

The answer binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual (Formula, "Direction"; Fig 5 :65). The shape is: *a bounded frame whose prediction ranges against an observation, whose residual is indexed per slot, whose new terms are gated by support, reach and novelty, and whose settlement is paid by a ground that does not update on the frame's own report.* That shape is what an answer would look like before it is named.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies its own agreement (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.
3. **Use filters for the budget, not the verdict.** Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Contact cost can decide who is approached first. It cannot decide whether the shape is right.

Legally: cold-harvested personal email lists breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03` are public, institutional and opt-in.
