## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :137: *"it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape is: conversion closes when the political anchor moves, an import crosses the boundary, overlapping frames negate independence, a catalyst stock is reused, and a feedback loop closes.
- A name would be: "campaign finance caused it", "Citizens United is the cause", "the threshold is X dollars" or "country Y is captured".

The first is a conjunction of conditions with an instrument attached. The second is a binding from political science onto the vocabulary, not a derivation from the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :54-57 applied to a question about wealth and government: the conversion and the institutional prevention are overlapping conditions, not two topics in one sentence.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence "the outcome moves when wealth pushes it" rather than "the regulator was bribed".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the reason the first contact carries a refuter, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox, *"By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the instrument would have to return an outcome that stays pinned when wealth is varied, and would itself have to be unmovable by wealth.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "revolving door" or "dark money" as the mechanism is such an import: legitimate if the source is recorded and it explains a described gap, illegitimate if it is smuggled in as a figure-derived result.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So a political-science vocabulary is a visible guess about the axes along which influence varies. Turning it into "the framework's answer" turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the empirical record cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a correlation between donations and roll-call votes reads the same green for at least three sources - direct exchange, shared social habitat, and reverse causation where expected influence attracts wealth. Naming one of them from the correlation is exactly the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A study of influence that uses the same elite interviews, the same donor records and the same expert pool as the claim it is asked to settle cannot validate that claim.

## 5. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"*, *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And the operational sentence: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

The essay also gives the instance that maps closest to this question: *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."* Replace "prestige" with "wealth" and "compute" with "political agenda" and that is F1 plus F5 of the audit, arrived at in an agent system, not in political science. Because it is the framework author's own essay, it corroborates rather than decides.

The legitimate ask is therefore the opposite of gathering prestigious endorsers: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a pinned outcome with a discriminator, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 6. Correction to an earlier reading of mine

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific mechanism in this question. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Terms such as "revolving door", "disclosure rule" and "judicial review" are already in the political-science library; naming them is a **binding** onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the binding was derived from the figures when it was imported, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall when Fig 6 says only an import adds an atom (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*; the political-science binding adds none to the figure library), and (c) claiming a numerical threshold follows from the shape. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. What shape means here, in one paragraph

For INS-0042 a shape is the checkable conjunction that closes the conversion in a named institutional slot: an anchor that moves under a wealth push, an import that crosses from the economic closure into the political one and is debited against the political frame's independence, near-total overlap between the two frames, a wealth stock that survives its uses, and a feedback loop in which influence returns as wealth. Institutions prevent the conversion only while they remain conventions that constrain rather than decide outcomes, and that maintenance is permanent work in a habitat whose actors were not chosen. The shape names none of those conditions as a culprit, gives no dollar threshold and no country ranking, and stops at the limit of the available instruments with the lineage recorded. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
