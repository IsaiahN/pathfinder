## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when a stock of wealth becomes a standing capacity to determine political outcomes in a named institutional slot, and when an institution prevents that conversion. Option A: conversion is a smooth function of wealth size and institutions are barriers of greater or lesser strength. Option B: conversion is the event of a political anchor becoming movable, requiring an import across the frame boundary and closed by a feedback loop, while prevention is the maintenance of a convention that constrains rather than decides outcomes. What each leads to: A predicts a national correlation and a reform programme of stronger rules; B predicts per-slot categorical events, three separate failure conditions, and permanent maintenance work. R is indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19).

- **D2, the political slot (political frame).** Actors: legislators, regulators, judges, election administrators. Currency: votes, rules, rulings, budgets, enforcement. Anchor: a recorded vote, a published judgment or allocation, conditionally on not moving under a wealth push. Admitted.
- **D3, the institutional ground maintainer.** Actors: constitutions, oversight bodies, disclosure rules, independent judiciaries. Currency: constraint and legitimacy. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24); the office is to keep the anchor reachable. Admitted with a live failure mode.
- **D4, the habitat of contact.** Actors: lobbyists, revolving-door staff, think tanks, platforms, party finance channels. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the habitat is read off the world, not composed (Fig 11 :48). Admitted.
- **D5, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers, journalists, oversight researchers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **X1, money is power as a free-floating property.** No anchor independent of the slot verdict. Not a domain yet.
- **X2, culture explains everything.** Measurable only as agreement inside a frame, which is a room, not an anchor. Absorbed into D3 and D4.
- **Partition or union.** D1/D4, D2/D3 and D4/D5 overlap, so the whole is a union of C1 anchor failure, C2 import crossing and C3 feedback closure (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The middle link, terms for what the situation affords and a stated objective, has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; every specific empirical claim is withheld. Wealth translates into influence in a named slot when the political anchor moves under a wealth push, when a resource crosses from the economic frame into the political one and is debited against the political frame's independence, and when influence returns as wealth so the loop closes. Wealth is a catalyst, so a bounded stock composes many influence molecules and annual flow is the wrong unit. Institutions prevent the conversion only while they remain conventions that constrain the frame rather than decide the outcome; declaring independence does not ground it, a keeper with a reputation to protect is disqualified, and isolation substitutes a habitat rather than removing contact, so prevention is permanent work. Actor valence is relative to the residual. No threshold, country, named culprit or dollar figure follows. This is `specified` and ungrounded, with three conditions kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states three refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a national-average correlation instead of a per-slot generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter. A second cost is misdiagnosing a union as a partition and applying a single lever that leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The three conditions can be tested and discarded separately, the imported political-science bindings are provenanced and removable, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected per-slot packet. A reform that introduced unchosen actors cannot be simply reversed, because exclusion is not available (Fig 11 :61), so the practical policy is undoable only by further introduction and maintenance.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pinned-outcome measurement in a named slot with an instrument that does not itself move under a wealth push would close C1; a habitat census showing no cross-frame contact would close C2; a measured loop sign of zero or negative would close C3; a rule shown to decide the outcome while remaining an anchor under independent measurement would break the convention reading. Agreement among agreeable readers, a larger correlation from the same evidence pool, or a list of prestigious endorsers is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :35, Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :35). Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
