**The residual in effect terms.** `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot `s`, where `s` is crop x region x season x management regime. `Γ` is the production frame's predicted yield; `o′` is realised yield or nutrition. The residual is the part of a yield change the production frame did not predict, traced to changes in the three organism groups. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The anchor is the measured harvest, a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market (Fig 2 :29). The question is constitutively about that anchor (Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it.").

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** A per-slot habitat census and cascade map: actors, conditions and relations in contact with the yield residual, second and third-order effects named, actor valence recorded as relative to the residual, the management seat's asymmetric power stated, and a pre-registered refuter. That is a shape, not a quantitative forecast. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A pool of pollination-believers is one witness wearing many names.
3. **Sending the interior spends the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the theory.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
