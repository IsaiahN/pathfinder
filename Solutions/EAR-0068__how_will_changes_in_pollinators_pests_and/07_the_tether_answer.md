# The Tether's answer on pollinators, pests, soil organisms and food production

**Mode: `specified` on the field application, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a yield forecast. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how changes in three organism groups affect food production. Stated as an effect: which part of the yield residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` becomes visible when those groups change, and in which slots? R is per crop x region x season x management regime. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The anchor is the measured harvest (Fig 2 :29). The three groups are the habitat, not the ground (Fig 11 :73). The question is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of three independent inputs (Fig 9 :55).

## 2. The shape

1. **Enumerate the habitat before attributing effects.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :47: "relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency." The figure's own list contains the question's subject. For each slot, list actors, conditions and relations in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, until the cascade stops mattering.

2. **Valence is relative to the residual, not to the organism's name.** Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." A pollinator can be a competitor elsewhere; a pest can be food for a natural enemy; a soil microbe can suppress or cause disease. No standing good/bad list survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51). Mode `specified`.

3. **Effects cascade beyond direct contact.** Fig 11 :52: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Pollination, pest control and soil processes interact across at least two links. The world literature corroborates: Lundin et al. 2013 found the joint pollination-plus-pest-control gain "outweighed the sum of seed set gains obtained when increasing each service separately." Mode `specified`, `grounded` only for that one crop experiment.

4. **The union is non-additive and must be unbundled.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single lever clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing. Control is a per-slot comparison threshold (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"), matching IPM's site-specific action thresholds. Mode `specified`.

5. **Management can add and cannot subtract.** Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." Introducing a pollinator, a parasitoid or an inoculant without enumerating its cascade is the cane-toad and kudzu failure (Fig 11 :65). Mode `specified`.

6. **Yield is the anchor; aggregation can hide a live slot.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A rising global yield series does not refute a per-slot pollination or soil service deficit. They are different quantities. Mode `specified`.

7. **The channel decays; keep it reachable.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Vivid pollinator collapses are over-weighted and diffuse soil change under-weighted, as the availability-heuristic source predicts. The cure is per-slot measurement, not more expert agreement (Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"). Mode `general` for the rule.

8. **No last room, no point forecast.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns the narrowed form of an answer. A global magnitude, a universal ranking of the three groups, and a named winning intervention are not in the closure at this budget. That is a declared `gap`, not a silence (Fig 13 :134). Mode `general`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any group is universally beneficial or harmful. Valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that adding pollinators, parasitoids or soil inoculants always raises yield. Introduction is asymmetric and cascade-dependent.
- Not a global yield forecast, not a percentage, not a ranking of the three groups. Those need a ground reading per slot.
- Not that the euro valuation of pollination settles the effect. A valuation prices the description, not the actions (Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add.").
- Not a derivation from the figures of any specific organism's role. Pollination and predation are named in the figure's relation list; the particular species are world imports with provenance.
- Not `grounded`. No harvest measurement was taken in this run, and the derivation is a simulation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument and a reading, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched this session): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Per-slot yield series**, not global aggregates, with pollination service, pest pressure and soil condition measured in the same slots over a full season.
- **Interaction test:** does an interaction term between pollination and pest control (and between soil condition and both) reduce the per-slot residual relative to an additive model, on held-out slots? Lundin et al. 2013 provide the prototype experiment.
- **Threshold test:** does a site-specific action threshold (IPM) fire before a cascade closes, and can early-warning indicators (Scheffer et al. 2009) detect the approach?
- **Cascade census:** were second and third-order effects enumerated before each introduction, and did any unenumerated cascade appear?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:** a three-variable surface that predicts held-out slots with no habitat census; invariant organism valence; additive model as good as interaction model; a one-off introduction needing no maintenance; aggregate decline with no live slot; agreement changing the yield relationship without a ground change.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The habitat list is read off the world and "what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual" (Fig 11 :48). More search inside the current closure is the right first move: SEARCHED, then INWARD (sharpen IPM thresholds, pollination-deficit protocols and per-slot monitoring), then OUTWARD only for the low-overlap frames already declared. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and this is the second: Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a global yield forecast, and it does not rank pollinators above pests or soil organisms. It says that yield change has to be read per crop, region, season and management regime, and that the three organism groups are actors in a habitat in contact with that residual, alongside light, water, temperature, season and terrain, joined by pollination, predation, competition and dependency relations. An organism's sign is relative to the residual, so these are roles rather than fixed good or bad kinds. Effects cascade beyond direct contact, pollination and pest control interact non-additively, soil processes condition both, management can add an actor and cannot subtract the habitat, and a managed ecosystem is permanent work. The anchor is the measured harvest, not an average and not expert agreement. What the framework offers is that shape, the interaction and threshold instruments that would close it, and four refuters written in advance. The magnitude stays open until the ground settles it.
