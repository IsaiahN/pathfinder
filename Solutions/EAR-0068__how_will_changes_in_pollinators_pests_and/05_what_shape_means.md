## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a shape is the narrowed form of the answer you can state before you can name a winning organism or a yield magnitude. It constrains: the answer must be per-slot, cascade-aware, valence-relative, union-unbundled, seat-maintained and ground-settled. It does not fill: it does not say that bees matter more than soil microbes everywhere, or that yields will fall by a given percentage.

Concretely:

- A **shape** for EAR-0068: "yield change is a per-slot residual whose habitat includes pollination, predation, competition and dependency relations; effects cascade beyond direct contact; organism valence is relative to the residual; the union of conditions is non-additive; management is an asymmetric, permanent act; the anchor is the measured harvest."
- A **name** would be: "bees are the limiting factor" or "soil biodiversity adds 4 percent to yield." Neither is licensed by the figures for the general case.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the finish line "will also be the starting place," because by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: I describe the residual as a per-slot yield gap, then enumerate what is in contact with it, rather than picking "pollinator ecology" as the frame and mapping everything into it. Frame second, residual first.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, that means a harvest reading on a named slot, not a review or a valuation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Lundin 2013 synergy result is such an import: source gate (peer-reviewed, full text read), shadow test (explains why additive three-variable models leave an intersection standing). It licenses the *bond*, not a universal name.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So the habitat list is a visible enumeration, not a discovery of nature's own list.

**Correction to my own §3 and §4.** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming a mechanism at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Pollination, predation and competition are already held atoms in the figure's own relation list (Fig 11 :47), and the world literature holds the interaction terms. Naming them is a binding from the ecology frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: import adds an atom; this one adds none) and naming a per-slot winner as if the framework derived it. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. The metamerism caution, which is not retracted

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." It was "the arrival of a better instrument" that resolved the case, "not by argument."

For EAR-0068 this is load-bearing. Aggregate yield is a reading. Several sources can create the same reading: higher pollinator service, cultivar replacement, irrigation, fertiliser, area expansion, or a change in reporting. A rising aggregate cannot name its source. The metamerism error here would be to read "yields are up" and name "pollinator decline does not matter" as the source. What is owed is a better, per-slot instrument, not a name. This point survives the correction in §3, because its subject is source discrimination, not primitive naming.

## 5. Q: what does "shape" license us to answer here?

**Answered.** The framework licenses a shape: the per-slot habitat-and-cascade form of any answer, with per-domain verdicts kept separate. It does not license a global magnitude, a universal ranking of the three organism groups, or a named winning intervention. Those are bound only by a ground reading (a harvest measurement on a named slot) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test (the Lundin interaction bond).

**Does the derivation commit the forbidden error?** Not as written. It names the three groups because the figure itself names the relations pollination and predation (Fig 11 :47), and it records the world literature's interaction term as an import with provenance. It explicitly refuses to name a winner or a magnitude. The one place it comes close is the word "soil organisms," which is a world-category, not a figure term; it is mapped to the habitat's actors and conditions (Fig 11 :45-46), not promoted to a primitive.

## 6. Two consequences for this project

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous pollination researchers assembled to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let the harvest settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
