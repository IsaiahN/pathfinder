## QUESTION

How will changes in pollinators, pests, and soil organisms affect food production? Restated as an effect: which part of the yield residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, indexed per crop x region x season x management regime, becomes visible when those groups change, and through which relations and cascades? Option A treats the three groups as additive inputs with fixed signs and forecasts a global yield change. Option B treats them as habitat actors with valence relative to the residual, joined by non-additive relations, enumerated per slot and settled by the harvest. A leads to an averaged coefficient that misses intersections and cascades. B leads to a checkable shape with a refuter, and leaves the magnitude open until the ground pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unexplained part of per-slot yield change after a change in the three organism groups. Arity: multi-place, organism group x condition x relation x slot x time. Symmetry: the bonds are ordered, production `⇒`, sequence `→`, threshold comparison `⋛`, subtraction `−`; no symmetric sum preserves the effect. Scale: field, landscape and food system, with first, second and third-order contact. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

- **D2 The habitat.** Actors: pollinators, pests, soil organisms, natural enemies, crops; conditions light, water, temperature, season, terrain; relations pollination, predation, competition, dependency. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground settles while the habitat updates (Fig 11 :73). Admitted; enumerated, never composed.

- **D4 Observation channel.** Actors: monitoring, indicators, surveys, models. Currency: residual signal. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **D5 Cross-scale food system.** Actors: farms, regions, supply chains, trade. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground at each scale. Admitted as a compound with a lossy membrane.
- **X1 One lever fixes it.** No nameable anchor beyond D3 and D5. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Fixed good and bad organism kinds.** Contradicted by Fig 11 :50. Absorbed into D2 as relations.

Union, not partition; unbundle before searching again (Fig 9 :54). Dependency order: perception of the slot residual, vocabulary for what organisms afford, objective (yield), planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived shape survives; the three-variable input framing does not. Yield change must be read per slot. Pollinators, pests and soil organisms are habitat actors whose valence is relative to the residual, joined by ordered and non-additive relations, with effects cascading to third order. The union of conditions must be unbundled before a lever is proposed; management adds and cannot subtract, so it is permanent work. Yield is the anchor and the channel must be kept reachable. Global magnitudes, a universal ranking of the three groups and a named winning intervention are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted field effort. A union of overlapping conditions would be treated as three independent inputs, so a manager would apply one lever to single-condition regions and leave the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). The second cost is spending an expert's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 10 :42), which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance (Fig 10 :62), so a later reader can locate the error. A sent packet can be superseded.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot yield series with pollination, pest and soil measurements together over a season would close the residual. Specifically: a three-variable surface predicting held-out slots without a habitat census; invariant organism valence; an additive model as good as an interaction model; a maintenance-free cascade-free introduction; aggregate decline with no live slot; or agreement changing the yield relationship without a ground change. Any of these withdraws the sub-claim it targets. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
