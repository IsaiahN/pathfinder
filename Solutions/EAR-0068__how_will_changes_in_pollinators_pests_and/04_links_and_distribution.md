## Relevant wider literature

Two findings bear directly on the outreach claim and should be offered after the stage-1 verdict, not before:

1. **The interaction is synergistic, not additive.** Lundin et al. 2013 measured pollination and pest control together in red clover and found that the joint gain "outweighed the sum of seed set gains obtained when increasing each service separately." They also warn that "benefit transfer functions" extrapolating one service from one area to another "may give misleading results if interactions between ecosystem services are not taken into account." That is the union reading at the ground, not a figure analogy.
2. **The aggregate and the slot disagree, and both are real.** The Pollinator decline entry reports rising aggregate yields over the period of supposed pollinator decline, while IPBES and Gallai et al. document service value and vulnerability that "varied considerably among crop categories." Once the quantity is re-censused as aggregate output versus per-slot service, these are not a conflict. Cite this only after asking the recipient which quantity they were checking.

Both sources are secondary corroboration. They do not decide the figure-derived verdict.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** A frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds" (THE_FORMULA.md, Direction). Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and state the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are the test. If one fires, withdraw that part before sending anything further.
