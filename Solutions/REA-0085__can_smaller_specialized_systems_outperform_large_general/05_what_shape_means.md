# What "shape" means for this question

**Sources.** The canonical local files were read this session: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`. Two of the eight essays were opened in full on 2026-11-24: *The Library of Babel: Locating the Book of Books* (https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-library-of-babel-locating-the) and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-recursive-self-improvement-trap). The other six essays by Isaiah N are catalogued in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` but were not re-opened this session, so no quote from them is recorded here. That limit is stated rather than hidden.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

> "For habitat H and non-updating anchor A, architecture X has smaller residual than Y on axis g at equal contact."

is a shape. "Small models always win" is a name, and a name the framework cannot license.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." And: the books returned "need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."
- Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :28: "any frame you pick will seem to fit" if you describe the residual after choosing the frame. This is why habitat and anchor come before architecture in this solution.
- The essay on Babel states the stop condition: "you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." It also states the warning that a bundle masquerading as one question must be searched as several: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

Here the search narrowed to: union, not one question; size is frame-internal; a specialist can win only where closures differ and the anchor holds; adaptation needs maintained contact. That is the shape. No single architecture is named as the winner.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a winning architecture would require a controlled comparison on a named habitat with a non-updating anchor, reported separately for reliability and adaptability. The empirical papers in `04_links_and_distribution.md` are close but not sufficient: most compare on benchmarks whose leakage status is not established, and ConStat shows why that matters.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

A benchmark that has leaked into a model's training data has become another frame. It cannot settle a comparison. The concrete instrument owed here is a dynamic or freshly held-out evaluation whose non-leakage is demonstrated, not asserted. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." ConStat and the dynamic-benchmark literature are that worse instrument already returning something, so the inward route is available.

The source problem also appears in reading the empirical results. Two papers quoting the same benchmark suite are one witness. The source ledger records overlap so a pool is not mistaken for corroboration.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does the Tether return here?** A conditional inequality over four cells (two axes x two habitat conditions), with size explicitly excluded as a deciding variable, and with an honest `gap` on the absolute ranking. Not a verdict of "yes" and not a verdict of "no".

**Does the empirical literature name the missing thing?** Not reliably. It reports many specialist wins, but under benchmark conditions whose anchor integrity is contested, and it reports generalist advantages under human evaluation and under habitat variability. Those are the two currencies of Fig 12 :125 appearing in the data. The missing thing is not a new architecture. It is a non-updating anchor plus an enumerated habitat. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous researchers who agree that small models are better is the named collapse, not evidence. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the conditional claim against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**First contact carries the residual, not the theory.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outreach order in `02_outreach_email.md` follows from these two lines.
