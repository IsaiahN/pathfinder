## Independent domain census, redone from the residual line alone

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I started from the raw question and the no-mechanism prediction, not from the primary's finding headings.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of the prediction that unrelated strangers will not be helped, plus the observed variation across populations. Arity: multiple overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57), not one hard question. Symmetry: the conditions are ordered. Knowledge precedes help, a breach precedes sanction, a boundary precedes exclusion. So `→` and `⋛` and `−`, not a symmetric `+` pile. Scale: encounter, group, society, cross-society comparison, each with its own actors, currency and anchor (Fig 10 :68). Slot: the specific problem in the specific place (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 1, the encounter.** Actors: donor, recipient, observer, stranger. Currency: cost, benefit, reputation, payoff. Anchor: the measured cooperation rate in that interaction. Type: union of mechanisms.
- **Domain 2, the habitat and institution.** Actors: resource users, monitors, sanctioners, norm carriers, plus conditions and relations. Currency: rule enforcement, conflict resolution, information. Anchor: the field outcome. Type: habitat, enumerated (Fig 11 :43), not composed.
- **Domain 3, cross-society comparison.** Actors: populations, field sites, labs. Currency: measured magnitude of punishment, altruism, cooperation. Anchor: the cross-population measurement. Type: per-slot residuals, never averaged.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: experimenters, replicators, preregistration venues, outside readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.

Order by dependency: perceive who is a stranger and what counts as cooperation, build vocabulary for what conditions afford, say what counts as progress in that society, plan the intervention, carry the method onward (Fig 3 :4-15). I reached the same four domains from a different starting point: the primary derived them from the actor/currency/anchor test applied to the cooperation literature, whereas I derived them from the arity/symmetry/scale of R and the Fig 9 union/partition split. Different starting figures, same boundaries. Low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39, :43, :49, :51 (habitat, enumeration, relative valence, no standing list), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 1 :19, :22 (per slot), Fig 2 :40-41 (two valences of correlation) and Fig 12 :5, :117 (bond carries information).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: conditions are the habitat of a cooperation residual; valence is relative; no list transfers; union not list; report per slot; ground decides the name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim a reader might try to build from that shorthand, for example: "reputation and punishment are the conditions that support cooperation among strangers, and cross-society differences are differences in how much of each is present."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive that trip back down:

- the **union structure**. The concrete claim turns conditions into additive quantities, dropping Fig 9 :56's intersections and Fig 12 :5's bond information;
- the **bond distinction** between a cycle (direct reciprocity), a catalyst (a surviving norm) and a functional group (mechanism kind plus local qualifier), all collapsed into "how much";
- the **convention/verdict distinction** of Fig 2 :40-41, which a concrete causal claim may quietly drop by treating agreement among researchers as support.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: cooperation is agent-and-habitat so conditions must be enumerated, and valence is relative so no list transfers. Those are the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensation of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.

**On the claim: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.** The shape survives (conditions as habitat, relative valence, union not list, per-slot not averaged, two valences of correlation, bond structure). A universal list does not. The empirical naming is owed to per-slot measurement, and nothing here is settled. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

**Recommendation.** Send the packet with its mode and refuters attached, ask what would refute it, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration. Keep the shape claim and the refuter in front of the links. Report each society separately.
