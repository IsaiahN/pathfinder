## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a rock reading and the source process that produced it. Arity: one reading, several candidate sources. Symmetry: the source-to-reading map is many-to-one and has no inverse; different sources can leave the reading unchanged. Scale: the reading is present, the source is deep time, and the transform discards the lineage. Per Fig 9 this is a union of four conditions, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the rock and the reading.** Actors: stromatolites, δ13C in graphite and apatite, sulfur isotopes in barite, microfossils, deformation fabrics. Currency: preserved morphological and chemical signatures. Anchor: the dated rock and the instrument, which do not move when a frame changes its mind. Question type: partition (biological or abiotic for a named feature), settled first.
- **Domain 2, the origin process.** Actors: the first evolving system, its pathway, timing, location, uniqueness. Currency: the process that produced the signatures. Anchor: none currently nameable; no source read reports an instrument that discriminates processes from the ancient reading alone. Not yet a domain with a verdict; this is the residual, reported as unreached at this budget.
- **Domain 3, verification and interpretation.** Actors: field geologists, paleobiologists, astrobiologists, synchrotron and PIXL instruments. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence between frames is the parameter; the four Wikipedia pages used here share one encyclopedia pool and count as one witness.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (measure and date) precedes vocabulary (biological or abiotic terms), which precedes the objective (state the discriminator). The break sits at the vocabulary-to-objective link: there are terms for the reading and no instrument that fixes the source. Effort spent above the break cannot arrive. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The oldest surviving evidence establishes a lower bound on biological-grade organisation and a shape constraint on any account of the origin. It does not establish the source process, the exact origin time, the pathway, the identity of the first organism, or whether there was one origin or several. *Oldest evidence* is a four-condition union that must be unbundled. Biogenicity is decided by elimination of abiotic causes under a stated model, not by resemblance; the 2016 and 2018 Isua readings stand as isomers of the same rock inventory. The source slot stays open as `unreached at this budget`, with an instrument-shaped remedy. This is a `specified` shape and a `general` structural limit, not a `grounded` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, unbundles the four questions and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a conclusion instead of a residual and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A wrong lower bound would misdirect the search for older rocks; a wrong reading that the source is open would either close a live question prematurely or keep an instrument unfunded.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim is revisable without touching the geological record. A closed abiotic-elimination result for one feature, a new radiometric date with the overprint stated, or an instrument that discriminates sources would each move a specific sub-claim, and the packet records which one. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that recovers the origin process from the ancient reading alone on a real specimen would fill the source slot and reverse C3. A closed elimination of all plausible abiotic causes for one named feature would move that feature from contested to established and reverse C2 for that feature. A demonstration that every claimed oldest instance is abiotic would collapse the lower bound and reverse C1. A measurement fixing the origin event would reverse the bound-only claim. Agreement among readers, however numerous, is not such an instrument.
