## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The 2016 and 2018 Isua papers are a genuine low-overlap disagreement.** *"Here we report new research that shows a non-biological, post-depositional origin for the structures."* The 2018 paper used three-dimensional fabric analysis and synchrotron X-ray fluorescence on new samples. This is the strongest public evidence that a single reading does not determine its source. Cite both sides; do not present only the 2016 claim or only the 2018 correction.
2. **The conditional wording in the primary literature is the shape claim, not a caveat.** Mojzsis et al. wrote *"Unless some unknown abiotic process exists which is able both to create such isotopically light carbon and then selectively incorporate it into apatite grains, our results provide evidence for the emergence of life on Earth by at least 3,800 Myr before present."* The "unless" is what makes it a lower bound with an escape clause, which is exactly what the Tether derives.
3. **The biosignature definition is an operational instrument, not a confidence feeling.** *"A biosignature is a phenomenon that can be explained by biological processes where all possible abiotic causes of this phenomenon have been eliminated."* This gives a checkable task: name the environmental model and enumerate what was excluded. It is the world's version of Fig 5's acceptance bargain.
