## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

> What does the oldest surviving evidence of life actually establish, and what does it leave unknown?

The residual is the gap between a **reading** (a laminated rock, an isotopically light carbon signature, a filament in chert) and the **source** that produced it (an origin process billions of years earlier). Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": an answer here would be a statement of the form *[what is licensed] + [what is not licensed] + [what would close the gap]*. That binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual like any other term.

- **Option A:** the oldest evidence establishes when and how life began. This requires reading the source off the projection.
- **Option B:** the oldest evidence establishes a lower bound and constrains the shape of any origin account, while the source process stays unreached until a discriminating instrument arrives.

What each leads to: A issues a verdict where the figure set licenses only a bound, and it spends the independence of any reader asked to confirm it. B issues a checkable shape, a stated limit, and refuters written in advance.

A pilot strain appears immediately and is recorded, not smoothed: the phrase "the oldest evidence tells us when life began" fits only if a many-to-one map can be inverted from a single reading. That is a strain, so it governs another quantity (existence-by-a-date, not origin-time). Re-census below.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: one reading, multiple candidate sources. Symmetry: the source-to-reading map is many-to-one and lossy, so it has no inverse; swapping a source for another source can leave the reading unchanged. Scale: the reading is a present observation, the source is a process at the origin of the Earth, and the transform between them crosses a deep-time boundary that discards the lineage.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, the rock and the reading** | stromatolites, δ13C in graphite and apatite, sulfur isotopes in barite, microfossils, deformation fabrics | morphological and chemical signatures preserved in rock | the rock and the instrument that measures it; the interpreter's channel can decay | **partition** (biological or abiotic for this feature), ordered first |
| **D2, the origin process** | the actual first evolving system, its pathway, timing, location, uniqueness | the process that produced the signatures | **no nameable anchor currently available**: no instrument discriminates sources from the ancient reading alone | not yet a domain with a verdict; this is the residual |
| **D3, verification and interpretation** | field geologists, paleobiologists, astrobiologists, synchrotron and PIXL instruments | agreement and residuals | the ground, never agreement; independence between frames is the parameter | **union of two conditions** (biogenicity AND age), split before search |

**No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.** D2 is therefore reported as unreached, not as a smaller domain with a guessed answer. Naming a source (RNA world, metabolism first, hydrothermal vents, panspermia) would be binding a term from another frame onto an empty slot, and per Fig 13 :135 a primitive cannot be named by a frame that does not already hold it.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception (the reading exists) precedes vocabulary (is there a term for biological versus abiotic here), which precedes the objective (what would count as progress: a source-discriminating instrument), which precedes planning. The break sits at the vocabulary-to-objective link: there are good terms for morphology and isotopes, but no instrument that fixes the source from the rock alone. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Effort spent above that break cannot rescue it.

**Partition or union.** "Oldest evidence" bundles at least four questions: (a) is this feature biological at all, (b) how old is it, (c) what process produced it, (d) was it the only origin. By Fig 9 these are a **union**, not one hard question: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* They must be unbundled before search, or a single lever will clear one condition and leave the intersections standing.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** A line that says "no source can ever be determined" strains, because it would assert an absence where the figure set only reports an unreached state. That line governs a *different* quantity (underivability in principle, Fig 8 :40), so it is re-censused and kept out of the verdict. The D2 verdict is `unreached at this budget`, not `unreachable in principle`. This matters: the two are different facts with different instructions (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7).

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - The evidence is a reading, not the source (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The world source for this is exact. Isaiah N, *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The same structure appears in *How Intelligence Actually Works* (2026-09-16): *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."*

A stromatolite-like lamination, a light carbon signature, or a filamentous microfossil is the green reading. The origin process is the unknown source. Reading the source off the projection is the metamerism error: one reading, several sources, no discriminator.

**Refuter:** publish an instrument that distinguishes the candidate source processes from the ancient reading itself, and show it on a real specimen. Then the reading determines the source and this finding falls.

### F2 - What the reading does establish: a lower bound and an anchor (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The rock, dated by radiometric methods, is such an anchor for the question "was biological-grade organisation present by this date in this setting."

The primary literature phrases the claim conditionally. Mojzsis et al. (1996), quoted in the Wikipedia *Isua Greenstone Belt* article (accessed 2026-11-23): *"Unless some unknown abiotic process exists which is able both to create such isotopically light carbon and then selectively incorporate it into apatite grains, our results provide evidence for the emergence of life on Earth by at least 3,800 Myr before present."* The word **unless** is the whole epistemic content: a lower bound with an explicit escape clause.

The Wikipedia *Earliest known life forms* article (accessed 2026-11-23) states: *"The earliest undisputed evidence of life on Earth dates from at least 3.5 billion years ago according to the stromatolite record."* That is a bound, not an origin time. A bound is a legitimate `specified` output. Fig 2 :36 adds the legitimacy condition: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

**Refuter:** a demonstration that every claimed oldest instance is demonstrably abiotic would collapse the lower bound. A closed argument eliminating all plausible abiotic causes for one feature would instead move that feature from contested to established, without touching the origin question.

### F3 - "Oldest evidence" is a union of four questions, not one hard problem (triangulated)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The same article: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The four: biogenicity (is the feature biological), age (when did it form), source (which process produced it), uniqueness (one origin or several). A single lever, for example "find an older rock," clears the age condition and leaves the others standing. *The Library of Babel* essay (2026-10-02) names the failure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

**Refuter:** show that all four conditions are decided by one measurement. Until then, an answer that does not say which of the four it is answering is not checkable.

### F4 - Biogenicity is settled by elimination, not by resemblance (triangulated)

Fig 5 :43: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."* The bargain is paid by eliminating alternatives, not by saying "it looks like a modern mat."

The Wikipedia *Biosignature* article (accessed 2026-11-23) gives the operational criterion: *"A biosignature is a phenomenon that can be explained by biological processes where all possible abiotic causes of this phenomenon have been eliminated."* And: *"No single compound will prove life once existed. Rather, it will be distinctive patterns present in any organic compounds showing a process of selection."*

The literature supplies the test case. The Wikipedia *Isua Greenstone Belt* article: *"The identity of the ISB features as stromatolites is controversial, because similar features may form through non-biological processes."* The primary source, Allwood et al., *Nature* 563 (2018): *"Here we report new research that shows a non-biological, post-depositional origin for the structures."* A 2016 claim and a 2018 reassessment of the same rocks. Resemblance lost; a better instrument won.

**Refuter:** show that the elimination is closed for a feature (all plausible abiotic causes considered and excluded under a stated environmental model), or show that the 2018 reassessment was itself wrong by a further measurement. Both are ground readings; neither is an argument from analogy.

### F5 - The channel decays across deep time; the ground does not (triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :25-27 name the failure modes as *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."*

The rock is the ground; our reading of it is the channel. Metamorphism, metasomatism, deformation, and contamination are channel failures. The Wikipedia *Earliest known life forms* article: *"The primary issue with isotopic evidence of life is that abiotic processes can fractionate isotopes and produce similar signatures to biotic processes."* It also reports that claimed 2.7 Ga lipids *"were contaminants."* The channel, not the ground, had failed.

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round trip here is: from the rock up to a general statement ("life existed by 3.5 Ga"), then back down to the specific specimen. The gap is what the general statement cannot carry.

**Refuter:** produce a specimen and a transform whose round trip returns the same feature, with the lost detail enumerated. If the gap is zero, the shorthand carries the whole claim; so far it does not.

### F6 - The oldest evidence is a bound on life, not the time of the origin (triangulated)

Per the Wikipedia *Earliest known life forms* article: *"LUCA is not thought to be the first life on Earth, but rather the only type of organism of its time to still have living descendants."* The NASA Astrobiology *Ask an Astrobiologist* FAQ (accessed 2026-11-23) is blunter: *"we don't know what information might be missing about LUCA from all of the lineages that went extinct in the 3+ billion years since LUCA lived... We may have lost too much information over the course of evolutionary history to ever know exactly what LUCA looked like."*

There is a lineage gap between the first living system and the last universal common ancestor, and another between LUCA and any surviving rock. The fossil date is a floor, not the event. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

**Refuter:** a molecular-clock or geological result that fixes the origin event with a ground-backed interval, and shows the oldest rock sits at that event rather than after it, would reverse this finding for the timing question.

### F7 - No name for the source is licensed by the framework (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32 governs: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The figures README (canonical local copy) states the limit as *"a shape, not a name."*

Choosing RNA world, metabolism first, hydrothermal vents, clay, or panspermia is a binding from a different frame onto an unheld slot. Per Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."* A binding is not a new atom, and it does not move the wall. It must be provenanced as the searcher's contribution. *They're Scanning Books When They Should* (2026-09-06) adds: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

**Refuter:** an import with a source gate and a shadow test, where the imported primitive explains a specific residual the rock reading leaves, would license the name. Until then, the slot stays open.

### F8 - The 2016/2018 disagreement is triangulation, not noise (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* The two Isua papers used different instruments (field morphology and petrography versus 3D fabric analysis and synchrotron X-ray fluorescence) and returned different readings of the same rocks.

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The conflict is the selection event. Per the method rule, a conflict sends us back to the census, never to a flipped verdict. Re-census at F1 gives the resolution: the reading is a projection, so two instruments can legitimately disagree until a third discriminates.

Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Four Wikipedia articles on the same dispute are one pool, not four witnesses. The two primary papers are closer to two frames, though they share field area and some authors.

**Refuter:** show that both teams drew on the same evidence and the same instruments, so the disagreement is a spelling difference rather than an independent reading. Then the triangulation claim drops.

### F9 - Age numbers are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The dates (3.5 Ga, 3.7 Ga, 3.8 Ga, 4.1 Ga) decide which rocks get instrument time first. They do not decide whether the origin is understood.

Seat Map :163 (local file): *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list of oldest claims ranked by how much people already believe them is a convention, not corroboration.

**Refuter:** show a case where a date, by itself, settled a source question. None is recorded in the sources read; the claim is therefore `specified` and ungrounded.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287 (local file): *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if:

1. An instrument discriminates the origin process from the ancient reading alone, demonstrated on a real specimen (breaks F1).
2. A closed argument eliminates all plausible abiotic causes for a specific claimed feature under a stated environmental model (breaks the open scope of F4 for that feature).
3. Every claimed oldest instance is shown to be abiotic, collapsing the lower bound (breaks F2).
4. A measurement fixes the exact origin event, showing the oldest rock coincides with it (breaks F6).
5. A single measurement is shown to settle biogenicity, age, source, and uniqueness together (breaks F3).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is labelled `grounded`.

4. Unbundle the four questions before searching. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).
