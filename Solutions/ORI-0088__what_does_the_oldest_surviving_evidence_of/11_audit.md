## Independent domain census from the residual line alone

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a reading in a rock and the process that produced it. Arity: one reading, several candidate sources. Symmetry: the source-to-reading map is many-to-one and non-invertible; two sources can leave the same reading. Scale: the reading is present, the source is deep time, and the transform discards the lineage. This is a **union** of at least four conditions (biogenicity, age, source, uniqueness), not one hard question: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57).
- **Domain 1, the rock and the reading.** Actors: dated mineral, isotope ratio, lamination, microfossil, deformation fabric. Currency: measured signature. Anchor: the rock and the instrument, which do not move when a frame changes its mind. Question type: partition, settled first.
- **Domain 2, the origin process.** Actors: first evolving system, pathway, timing, uniqueness. Currency: the producing process. Anchor: **none nameable in the sources read**. No source reports an instrument that recovers the process from the ancient reading. Under the rule that no nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet, this is the residual, reported as unreached, not answered.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: geologists, paleobiologists, astrobiologists, instruments. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is near zero.

Order by dependency: measure and date (perception); name biological versus abiotic (vocabulary); state what would count as a discriminator (objective); then plan. The break is at objective, because the instrument that fixes the source is not reported. A second possible break is the union itself: searching "oldest evidence" as one question spends effort above the split.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 9 (union split) and Fig 2 (anchor), I derived from Fig 6 :34 (an instrument must already be returning something) and Fig 12 :117 (same inventory, different arrangement, different substance). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
