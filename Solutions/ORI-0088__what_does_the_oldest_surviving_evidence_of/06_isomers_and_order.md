# Structure, order, and analogues for the oldest-evidence question

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

The reading (a rock signature) and the source (an origin process) are not the same object. Three questions are owed: (1) what is the structure of "oldest evidence" as a claim, (2) in what order must its four sub-questions be settled, (3) what analogue makes the reading-to-source gap checkable rather than merely asserted.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: the rock, the dated mineral, the measured signature, the candidate abiotic processes (metamorphism, metasomatism, Fischer-Tropsch synthesis, abiogenic precipitation, contamination), and the candidate biological processes (carbon fixation, sulfate reduction, microbial mat accretion). Bonds: → sequence (a process leaves a signature), ⇒ production (the signature did not exist before the process), ≡ identity (two names for one reading), ¬ negation (a candidate source is refused). No new primitive is available without import.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - What is the structure of the claim?

- Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**
- "Oldest evidence of life" names one object but bundles four claims: biogenicity, age, source, uniqueness.
- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** And :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
- ⇒ The claim is a **compound**, not a single measurement. Each condition has its own loop and its own ground. Reporting one number (a date) for the compound is the flat-formula error of Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
- This is the load-bearing structural point: the date is an ingredient; the bonds between biogenicity, age, source and uniqueness are what the answer is made of.

### Q2 - Are the 2016 and 2018 readings isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- The two Isua papers address the **same rocks** (same atoms in the plain sense), using field morphology and petrography (2016) versus three-dimensional fabric analysis plus synchrotron X-ray fluorescence (2018). They arrive at different substances: microbial mat structure versus post-depositional deformation assemblage.
- Under Fig 12 this is an **isomer relation**: identical inventory, different arrangement, decisive behavioural difference. The formula "Isua structures" cannot tell them apart; the bond (which process arranged the atoms) can.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if both teams are shown to have measured the same fabric with the same instrument and only differed in wording, the isomer claim fails and the disagreement is a spelling difference, not two substances.

### Q3 - What is the order of settlement?

Fig 3 :4-16 gives the dependency chain. Mapped here:

1. **Perception:** a signature is measured and dated. Ground: the instrument and the radiometric clock.
2. **Vocabulary:** there are terms for biological and abiotic origin of the signature. Ground: the published criteria (the Wikipedia *Biosignature* elimination criterion).
3. **Objective:** what would count as progress here? A stated discriminator between candidate sources. **This is the break.** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
4. **Planning:** choose which rock, which instrument, which candidate source to test.
5. **Learn and carry:** record the lineage so the next reader pays less.

Fig 3 :22: **"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."** Effort spent on step 4 before step 3 is spent above the break and arrives nowhere. This is why the honest output stops after steps 1 and 2, with step 3 named as the owed instrument.

### Q4 - What analogue makes the gap checkable?

- *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, read 2026-11-23): the green reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths give one reading; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* This is **metamerism**, not scepticism. It is checkable: the spectrometer resolved the colour case.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: **"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."** And **"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."**
- *They're Scanning Books*: **"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."** The owed thing is a new discriminating axis, not another candidate story.
- Fig 6 :34: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."** The proto-instrument must already be returning something. Here it is: X-ray fluorescence on the Isua rocks was already returning element maps, and a sharper use of it changed the 2016 reading. That is an inward extension from an instrument already returning something, not a telescope specified from an account of Mars.
- Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Hence the source is `unreached`, not `absent`.

### Q5 - Does any figure line license the origin time?

- Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**; :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**
- The Wikipedia *Earliest known life forms* article: **"LUCA is not thought to be the first life on Earth, but rather the only type of organism of its time to still have living descendants."** The NASA *Ask an Astrobiologist* FAQ: **"We may have lost too much information over the course of evolutionary history to ever know exactly what LUCA looked like."**
- ⇒ No line licenses a date-as-origin. The figure-grounded residue is a bound over a shape constraint. The date is a **filter for the budget**, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**).

## CONCLUSION

"Oldest evidence" is a four-condition union whose bonds, not its ingredients, carry the claim. The 2016 and 2018 Isua readings stand to each other as isomers: same rocks, different arrangement, different substance. The dependency chain breaks at the objective link, where a source-discriminating instrument is owed. The metamerism analogue makes the gap checkable and stops it from collapsing into either "we know the origin" or "we can never know." The origin time, pathway and uniqueness are unreached at this budget, not proven absent.

This framing keeps what is defensible (a lower bound, a shape constraint, a live experimental programme) without the part that fails (a source or a date named from a projection). No new primitive, no import that moves the wall, and no average across the four conditions.
