## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0088, a **shape** is the pair of statements you can license before you can name the source: *there was biological-grade organisation by this date in this setting*, and *any origin account must be capable of producing the observed signature by then*. `The oldest evidence marks the origin event` is a name, not a shape. `RNA world` or `hydrothermal vents` as *the* source is also a name. Neither is licensed by the reading.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the residual, look for recurring terms that overlap it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the four-question split in `01` F3 and Fig 9 :54.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Describe the reading and the missing discriminator, not a preferred origin story.
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The residual is the source gap; RNA world, vents, clay and panspermia are frames, so they are considered only after the residual is described.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In geobiology, this is an instrument result on a specimen, not an argument from resemblance. Allwood et al. (2018) did this for the Isua structures.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A candidate pathway may be imported, but the record must show where it came from and which gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* And: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The missing thing here is not another candidate pathway; it is a discriminating dimension that would separate pathways from the same reading.

A note against overreach: the rule governs a primitive not held in Γ. A candidate source from the literature is a binding onto an unheld slot, not the invention of a seventh term. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as a derivation or as a wall-moving import (Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*).

## 4. Why the question is a metamerism case

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Apply to ORI-0088: a light carbon signature or a laminated structure is the green reading. Biological carbon fixation, Fischer-Tropsch synthesis in hydrothermal settings, metamorphic redistribution, and abiogenic precipitation are candidate sources. The Wikipedia *Earliest known life forms* article confirms the live ambiguity: *"Reassessment of the Akilia graphite show that metamorphism, Fischer-Tropsch mechanisms in hydrothermal environments, and volcanic processes may be responsible for enrichment lighter carbon isotopes."* An instrument that cannot distinguish these demonstrates its own inadequacy; it does not demonstrate that no distinction exists.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the second half: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* And the provenance statement: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* The lineage, not the artefact, carries the answer. For a 3.5 Ga rock, the lineage has been mostly transformed away, which is why the source stays open.

## 5. The distinction horizon, stated honestly

*How Intelligence Actually Works* defines a *"distinction horizon"* for an observer and a *"union horizon"* for a group: *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* Crucially: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

This is the precise status of D2 in this solution: the origin source is below the current union horizon, so the verdict is `unreached at this budget`, not `unreachable in principle`. The two are different facts with different instructions (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7). Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I first wrote "the framework never lets you name an origin process." That overstates the rule. The correct form is: the framework does not *derive* a source from the reading, and it cannot name a primitive it does not hold. It does not forbid an expert from importing a candidate pathway with provenance. What remains wrong in a claim like "the oldest rock shows life began then" is the source named from a projection, not the act of naming as such. Both statements are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

Status: `general` for the structural limit, `specified` for the geological application, `grounded` only where a cited instrument result was actually read.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous 2016 claim is not settled by the fame of its authors; a 2018 correction is not settled by the journal. Both are ranged against the rock.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question they report on. What they can do is state the shape, the limit, and the refuters, so a geobiologist can check them quickly.

**A falsifier for this reading.** If an instrument is demonstrated that recovers the source process from the ancient reading alone, with provenance recorded, then "shape, not name" still holds as a general rule but its application to this question fails: the slot would be filled by a ground result. That falsifier is written here before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287 (local file).
