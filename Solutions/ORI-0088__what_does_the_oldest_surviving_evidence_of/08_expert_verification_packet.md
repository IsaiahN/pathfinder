# Expert verification packet: the oldest surviving evidence of life

**What this is:** a claim in a form a geobiologist or paleobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural limit, not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed in this packet.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The oldest surviving evidence of life establishes a lower bound: biological-grade organisation was present by the date of the oldest accepted signature, in that setting, and any account of the origin must be able to produce that signature by then. It does not establish the source process, the exact time of the origin, whether there was one origin or several, or the identity of the first organism. A biosignature is a reading, and a reading cannot comment on its source; several processes can yield the same light carbon or the same lamination. "Oldest evidence" is a union of four questions (biogenicity, age, source, uniqueness), not one hard problem. Biogenicity itself is decided by elimination of abiotic causes under a stated environmental model, not by resemblance. The gap is unreached at this budget, not proven unreachable in principle, and the remedy is an instrument rather than another argument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 9 and Figure 2
- **Statement:** the question is a four-condition union, and the dated rock is an anchor for the existence-by-a-date question only.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Also Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." And Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **Refuter:** show one measurement that settles biogenicity, age, source and uniqueness together; or show an anchor that moves when a frame pushes on it.

### C2 - geology-facing; checkable against the Isua record and the biosignature literature
- **Statement:** biogenicity is decided by elimination, and the 2016/2018 Isua sequence is the worked case. The reading does not determine the source.
- **What to check:** the 2016 Nature paper reported 3.7 Ga microbial structures; the 2018 Nature paper reinterpreted the same rocks as post-depositional deformation using 3D fabric analysis and synchrotron XRF. The Wikipedia *Biosignature* article gives the operational criterion: "A biosignature is a phenomenon that can be explained by biological processes where all possible abiotic causes of this phenomenon have been eliminated."
- **Refuter:** show that both teams used the same evidence and the same instruments (which would make the disagreement a spelling difference), or produce a closed elimination of all plausible abiotic causes for one named feature under a stated environmental model (which moves that feature from contested to established), or show the 2018 reinterpretation is wrong by a further measurement.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a world test
- **Statement:** the source is underdetermined by the reading; the verdict is `unreached at this budget`, and the remedy is a source-discriminating instrument extending an instrument already returning something.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 6 :34 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
- **Refuter:** demonstrate an instrument that recovers the origin process from the ancient reading alone on a real specimen. Then the slot is filled and this sub-claim falls.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the oldest rock is the moment life began. The fossil is a floor, not the event; LUCA is not the first life.
- Not that RNA world, hydrothermal vents, clay or panspermia is the source. These are bindings from other frames, provenanced as such, not derivations.
- Not that biogenicity is settled for every famous specimen. The Isua record is the counterexample.
- Not that abiotic causes can never be excluded. The *Biosignature* criterion is exactly their elimination.
- Not that the source is unknowable in principle. It is unreached at this budget; the two are different facts.
- Not `grounded`. No new measurement is asserted here.

## 5. What would settle it

The ground, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument on a real specimen would settle the source question. A closed abiotic-elimination argument would settle a specific feature's biogenicity. A radiometric date with the metamorphic overprint stated would settle the age of the hosting unit. None of these is supplied here; the first and third are reported in the sources read, the second is an operational standard rather than a completed result for every claimed instance.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54, :57, :32 and Fig 2 :29. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the two Isua papers (2016 and 2018): did the later study genuinely use different instruments and reach a different reading?
3. Name one measurement that would distinguish a biological from an abiotic origin for a single named signature, or say why none is possible on the ancient record.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Record the verdict before reading any framework material.
