Symmetry: the anchor is asymmetric to the frames, it can move them and cannot be moved by them (Fig 2 :24-27). Scale: contact, not survey volume. Question type: union compound. - **Domain 1, observational ground.** Actors: CMB polarization, BAO spectroscopy, lensing imaging, GW sirens, 21 cm arrays. Currency: readings and precision. Anchor: the sky as measured by an instrument that does not update on a theory frame. This is the ground domain.
- **Domain 2, theory frames.** Actors: ΛCDM, early dark energy, modified gravity, w0waCDM, inflation classes, particle dark matter. Currency: predictions and fits. Anchor: the same ground, ranged against separately. Binds onto held shapes; adds no primitive.
- **Domain 3, inference and verification.** Actors: pipelines, combined fits, tension metrics, committees. Currency: agreement and σ. Anchor: should be the ground, often replaced by agreement. Independence here is zero in this simulated run because every seat draws on one pool.

Order by dependency: split the union (Fig 9 :54), describe the slot in effect terms before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28), find the frame whose closure predicts the shape (Fig 8 :27-28), sharpen an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35), let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **reach-versus-volume distinction** (Fig 8 :32, Fig 13 :113), which a mission ranking silently replaces with a bit-volume proxy.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a resolving observation must be a pinned, low-overlap anchor, and it must be sharpened from an instrument already returning something. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
