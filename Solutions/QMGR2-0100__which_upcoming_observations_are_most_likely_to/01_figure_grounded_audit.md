## 0. The question stated as a residual in effect terms

The question asks for a selection over *future observations*, ranked by whether they will **resolve** a debate or **merely add more data**. That is itself a two-frame disagreement, and the frames use different metrics:

| frame | what it counts as progress | figure status |
|---|---|---|
| an accumulation frame | more photons, more galaxies, smaller error bars, more combined datasets | frame-internal coverage (Fig 1 :25) |
| a resolution frame | a held term becomes refusable, or a new term is settled, so the reach changes | ground contact (Fig 1 :23, Fig 8 :32) |

For each debate the residual R_s is a *disagreement between frames at a named slot*, not a single global number: the H0 slot, the S8/growth slot, the dark-energy w0-wa slot, the inflation-model-class slot, the dark-matter and neutrino-mass slots. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A combined "tension significance" that folds H0, S8 and w0-wa into one score is exactly the aggregation the figure forbids.

**R in effect terms:** which candidate observation, when performed, changes the ground metric at a live slot (so a term is refused or settled), as opposed to increasing coverage, compression, credibility or predicate count inside a closure that is already returning the same disagreement. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

**What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term).** Before searching, bind the sought shape: *a resolving observation is a pinned anchor at which at least two low-overlap theory frames make different predictions, it is sharpened from an instrument already returning a failing reading, and it can refuse a held term; an accumulating observation spends description bits inside one closure and cannot move the ground.* That binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as anything else.

**Residual, one line per slot:**

- H0: distance-ladder calibration versus early-relic sound horizon. Disagreement does not shrink with more same-kind data (over 6σ by 2025). Union compound.
- S8 and growth: lensing, clustering and CMB predictions disagree; survey-dependent; possibly a single fσ8(z) mismatch rather than one tension. Union compound.
- Dark energy: ΛCDM versus w0waCDM, 2.8 to 4.2σ preference for evolution, not yet discovery. A tension reduction is not a model comparison.
- Inflation: model classes differ in predicted tensor amplitude r. A detection *or* a sufficiently low upper limit refuses whole classes. This is a refusal-shaped slot.
- Dark matter and neutrino mass: parameter degeneracies couple the mass question to the expansion model. Union compound.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two or more theory frames plus one candidate anchor per slot; the question is second order because it ranks observations by their effect on first-order residuals. Symmetry: the anchor is asymmetric with respect to the frames, it can move them and they cannot move it (Fig 2 :24-27). Scale: the deciding scale is contact, not survey volume. A bigger survey at the same closure changes bits; a smaller independent anchor changes reach.

**One line per domain. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable anchor? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 observational ground | CMB polarization, BAO spectroscopy, weak lensing imaging, GW standard sirens, 21 cm arrays | readings, precision, systematics control | the sky as measured by an instrument that does not update on a theory frame | yes, each instrument returns a specific failing reading |
| D2 theory frames | ΛCDM, early dark energy, modified gravity, w0waCDM, inflation model classes, particle DM models | predictions, evidence, fits | the same observational ground, ranged against separately | yes, each predicts a different value at a named slot |
| D3 inference and verification frame | analysis pipelines, combined fits, tension metrics, review committees | agreement, σ, credibility | **should be** the ground; in practice often agreement | partially, this is where the "more data" collapse lives |

**Partition or union.** The set of major cosmology debates is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition. The Hubble tension bundles at least a calibration condition, a sound-horizon condition and a new-physics condition; the neutrino-mass tension bundles a mass condition and a dark-energy-model condition. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Verdicts must therefore be kept per domain and never averaged into a single "most promising mission".

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Order: (1) ask whether there is a fact at the slot, and split the union (Fig 9 :4-5); (2) describe R in effect terms before naming a frame or a mission (Fig 9 :27-28); (3) find the observation whose closure predicts that shape (Fig 8 :27-28); (4) sharpen from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35); (5) let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42). A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed (Fig 3 :22-23), so a mission with no prior reading is not a weak lever, it is a reading of nothing.

## 3. Findings

### F1 : "More data" and "resolve" are different quantities (decisive for the selection rule)

Fig 1 :25: *"coverage, and compression achieved"*. Fig 1 :26: *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"*. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

An observation whose entire effect is to increase coverage, compression or credibility is scored with a quantity the frame itself produces. It cannot move the ground metric, so by the figure it cannot be the thing that resolves. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* This is the exact failure of a combined-tension score: it averages away the slot that is still live.

**Refuter:** show a coverage-only observation (same closure, same frames, more of the same readings) settling a debate by the ground without refusing a held term or changing contact.

### F2 : Debates that do not shrink with effort are union compounds, not hard questions

Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :58: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

World corroboration, low overlap with the framework: Di Valentino, Said and Saridakis (2025, arXiv:2509.25288) report that "the Hubble constant H0 discrepancy has now exceeded 6σ, and new high-precision data from DESI, JWST, ACT, and other facilities have made this tension more robust". Siegel (2024, Big Think) writes: "The Hubble tension is still very real, and is no closer to being resolved by these new studies." The same report notes that S8 "may not be a single phenomenon, but rather a manifestation of a more general mismatch in the late-time growth rate fσ8(z)", which is the figure's union language used by cosmologists.

**Refuter:** show H0 or S8 is one well-formed question whose disagreement shrinks monotonically as same-kind data accumulates.

### F3 : A resolving observation is a pinned anchor at which low-overlap frames differ

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*

Effect: the highest-leverage H0 observation is not a more precise Cepheid or a more precise CMB fit. It is an anchor from a frame that does not draw on either evidence pool. The LIGO Scientific Collaboration's standard-siren analysis states: "our gravitational-wave distance estimate has the great virtue of being completely independent of the Cosmic Distance Ladder derived from electromagnetic observations." Siegel (2024) identifies the same structure in ordinary language: "This is where the tension comes from: whether you choose your anchor point to be nearby, and then extend it out into the ever-more-distant Universe, or whether you choose your anchor point to be close in time to the Big Bang, and then come forward to measure our cosmic expansion history since that time."

**Verdict status:** triangulated for the shape (Fig 2 plus Fig 8, low overlap between the anchor rule and the surplus rule, corroborated by two independent world sources). Single-source for the specific ranking of standard sirens first among H0 anchors, since that is a world-frame binding.

**Refuter:** show that the siren H0 value moves when the distance-ladder prior is changed, or that siren and ladder draw on one evidence pool. Either breaks the anchor claim.

### F4 : Resolution is often a refusal, not a detection

Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 5 :54: *"A term that refuses another pays the same bargain."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*

Effect: a sufficiently deep B-mode upper limit refuses whole inflation model classes and changes the reach. The CMB-S4 collaboration states: "For the foreseeable future, precise measurements of CMB B-modes are our only way to detect these primordial gravitational waves" and "Even an upper limit from CMB-S4 would provide invaluable insights into the first moment of our Universe." This is the cleanest instance in the field of an observation whose resolving power does not depend on a positive detection.

**Verdict status:** triangulated (Fig 5 amendment plus the Operators table plus Fig 12, corroborated by the CMB-S4 source). `specified`, ungrounded.

**Refuter:** show that a deep upper limit leaves the admissible model set unchanged, so no term is refused and the reach does not move.

### F5 : Instruments are sharpened from readings that already fail to resolve

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

Effect: SO and CMB-S4 sharpen a B-mode foreground and lensing signal that is already measured; DESI, Euclid and Rubin sharpen BAO and lensing signals already returning; LIGO sharpens a siren signal already detected; HERA and SKA-Low sharpen a 21 cm limit already returning. By contrast Mondal and Barkana (2023, Nature Astronomy) write: "The 21 cm signal from the dark ages provides a potential new probe of fundamental cosmology" and their paper concludes that only a space- or lunar-based telescope can detect that signal. The dark-ages window has not registered anywhere at any resolution, so under Fig 6 :34-35 it is **unreachable at this budget**, not a top pick. That is a verdict, not a silence: Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

**Verdict status:** triangulated for the sharpening rule. Single-source, `specified`, for placing dark-ages 21 cm outside the shortlist at this budget, since that depends on the current instrument record.

**Refuter:** show a dark-ages 21 cm instrument already returning a failing reading at some resolution, or show a never-registered sensor outranking an already-returning one.

### F6 : Precision and settlement are two currencies that do not add

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :127: *"They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them and will not notice the shortfall."* Fig 12 :128-129: *"A long chain of cheap parts is expensive to describe and may cost one action to refute. A short arrangement over a costly operand is cheap to state and may take a whole budget to settle."*

Effect: survey volume, galaxy counts and error-bar shrinkage are bits. Deciding between ΛCDM and w0waCDM, or between inflation classes, is an action at the ground. A mission that is spectacular in bits can be inert in actions. Fig 5 :26: *"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"* The MDL bargain is about confusion removed, not data acquired.

**Refuter:** show a bit-only increase settling a debate at the ground without a change of contact. This is the same failure mode as F1 measured in a different currency, so it is recorded separately rather than merged.

### F7 : Systematics work maintains the channel; it is not settlement

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*.

Effect: foreground modelling in B-mode searches, shear calibration in lensing, and Cepheid crowding checks in the distance ladder are channel maintenance. They are necessary for an anchor to be reachable, but "the channel works" is not the same claim as "the ground settled it". The DESI DR2 guide states: "We've also performed a lot of extra tests this time which make us confident that the result isn't driven by some unknown effect in any of the data." That is channel maintenance reported honestly, and its own wording stops short of settlement.

**Refuter:** show a combined fit that changed the ground rather than the error bar, with the change surviving a blind re-analysis.

### F8 : "Most likely" is a budget filter, never a witness

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Effect: the figures do not and cannot rank named missions. They return the shape a resolving observation must have. Naming CMB-S4, Euclid or a standard-siren program is a **binding from the world frame** onto that shape, and it carries provenance, not figure authority. The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)".

**Refuter:** exhibit a figure line that ranks specific instruments or missions. None was found after a full census.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before outreach and before the conclusion was written. The seat map (line 287) states: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An observation that only increases coverage, precision or credibility within one closure demonstrably settles a debate at the ground without changing contact or refusing a term (breaks F1, Fig 1 :25-27 and Fig 11 :40).
2. A debate whose disagreement does not shrink with added data is shown to be one well-formed question rather than a union compound (breaks F2, Fig 9 :4-5).
3. An observation credited with resolving a debate is shown to move when a theory frame pushes on it, so it was not a pinned anchor (breaks F3, Fig 2 :29).
4. Two observations credited with triangulating are shown to draw on one evidence pool (breaks F3, Fig 2 :40 and Fig 8 :48-49).
5. An upper limit or null result is shown to leave the held model set unchanged (breaks F4, Fig 5 :53-54).
6. A sensor that has never registered anything at any resolution is shown to be the highest-leverage next step (breaks F5, Fig 6 :34-35).
7. A figure line is produced that ranks specific upcoming missions (breaks F8).

If any fires, the corresponding verdict is withdrawn or narrowed and the retraction is added below. Until a ground reading of "resolved" is specified, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape, not a mission shortlist: an anchor, low overlap, already returning a failing reading, capable of refusal (F1 to F5).
2. Split each debate before ranking anything (F2). Do not fold H0, S8, w0-wa and neutrino mass into one significance score.
3. Treat tension reduction as a filter, not a witness (F8). A drop from 6σ to 2σ is a budget signal, not a settlement.
4. Keep channel maintenance visible and separate from ground contact (F7).
5. State mode on every claim, including the claim that a named mission is promising (F9).
6. Offer the derivation and the refuter, not the conclusion. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

## 7. Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a fact at each slot?** Yes for H0, S8, w0-wa, inflation class and neutrino mass, but each is a union compound, so split first (Fig 9 :4-5, :55).
2. **What is the residual in effect terms?** A live per-slot disagreement that does not shrink with same-kind data; the question asks which observation changes the ground metric at that slot (Fig 1 :19, :23).
3. **Can a frame see the sought shape?** No. "Most likely" is a preference, not a bound term. It was bound as an ordinary term in §0 so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Fig 5 :62-66 in the verbatim extract).
4. **Which operator resolves?** Refusal (¬) or settlement, not accumulation. An upper limit can refuse a class (Fig 5 :53-54; Operators :58).
5. **Where does the instrument come from?** From a worse instrument already returning something. New never-registered sensors are a separate, unreachable-at-budget track (Fig 6 :32-35).
6. **Can search inside one closure settle it?** No. Closure is idempotent (Fig 8 :32), so volume cannot buy reach. A third low-overlap frame is the only way to move the wall (Fig 6 :23).
7. **What would reverse the result?** A coverage-only settlement, a shrinking union, a movable anchor, a shared evidence pool, an inert upper limit, or a figure line ranking missions. All seven are written in §4 before this conclusion.

**Final verdict of the audit.** The shape survives: resolving means pinned anchor, low overlap, already returning, capable of refusal, per slot. Every specific ranking is `specified` and ungrounded. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; one honest stop recorded for dark-ages 21 cm and one refusal recorded for mission ranking. This is a simulated derivation and cannot settle which observation will actually resolve a debate. The ground will.
