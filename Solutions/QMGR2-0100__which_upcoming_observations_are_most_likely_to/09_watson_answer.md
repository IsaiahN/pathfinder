## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** Arity: two or more theory frames plus one candidate anchor per slot. Symmetry: the anchor is asymmetric with respect to the frames, it can move them and they cannot move it. Scale: contact, not survey volume. Question type: union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :58 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Domain 1, observational ground.** Actors: CMB polarization experiments, BAO spectroscopy, weak-lensing imaging, GW standard sirens, 21 cm arrays. Currency: readings, precision, systematics control. Anchor: the sky as measured by an instrument that does not update on a theory frame. This is the ground domain.
- **Domain 2, theory frames.** Actors: ΛCDM, early dark energy, modified gravity, w0waCDM, inflation model classes, particle dark matter models. Currency: predictions, evidence, fits. Anchor: the same observational ground, ranged against separately. Question type: a binding onto held shapes, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 3, inference and verification frame.** Actors: analysis pipelines, combined fits, tension metrics, review committees. Currency: agreement, σ, credibility. Anchor: should be the ground, but is often substituted with agreement. This is where the "more data" collapse lives. Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **Order by dependency.** Split the union first (Fig 9 :4-5), describe the slot residual in effect terms before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28), find the frame whose closure predicts the shape (Fig 8 :27-28), sharpen from an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35), let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mission ranking does not. A resolving observation is a pinned anchor at which low-overlap frames differ, sharpened from an instrument already returning a failing reading, capable of refusing a held term, applied per slot after the union is split. Coverage, compression, credibility and tension reduction are frame-internal filters and cannot be witnesses. Standard sirens are an independent H0 anchor candidate; a deep B-mode upper limit can refuse inflation model classes; BAO, lensing and supernovae gain reach only where their closures differ; dark-ages 21 cm is unreachable at this budget; a figure-level mission ranking is a gap by design. Everything substantive is `specified` and ungrounded. The structural rule is `general`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the shape, the per-slot verdicts and seven refuters before any theory, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is handing a budget filter the authority of a witness, which selects a shortlist for likely agreement and spends the independence of the people asked to check it. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. Each named mission binding is removable without touching the shape derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already refuse the ranking. A superseded shortlist harms no primitive and can be replaced when a ground settles a slot.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A coverage-only observation settling a debate at the ground would break the shape claim; a siren anchor that moves with the ladder prior, or two "independent" probes sharing an evidence pool, would break the anchor claim; a deep upper limit that leaves the model set unchanged would break the refusal claim; a dark-ages 21 cm instrument already returning a failing reading would move the honest stop; a figure line ranking missions would break the gap verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
