# Classifying observations by bond, currency and order

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6). No em dashes are used.

## RESIDUAL

The question asks "which observations", which is a ranking claim. A ranking presumes that observations can be treated as interchangeable items with a score. The figures say the opposite: arrangement carries meaning the ingredients do not, and two currencies price a composition without adding. A second derivation is owed, one that classifies observations by their **bond to a debate**, their **currency**, and their **order in the loop**, rather than by mission name or data volume.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: instrument readings, theory frames, named residual slots. Bonds held: the seven (conjunction, disjunction, identity, sequence, production, subtraction, comparison) plus the unary negation. No new primitive is available without import. ## DERIVATION

### Q1 : Two observations with the same ingredients are not the same observation

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."**

A CMB polarization map and a BAO measurement both consist of a sky, a detector and a statistic. What differs is the bond to the frame in question. Under a **sequence** bond (further measurements of the same kind) the result is accumulation. Under a **comparison** bond (two frames' predictions ranged against one pinned reading) the result is triangulation. Under a **subtraction** bond (a held calibration assumption removed) the result is a cleaner contact. Under **negation** (an upper limit refusing a model class) the result is a change of reach.

So an observation is not resolved by its ingredients. Fig 12 :40: **"same two atoms, three substances"**. The mission name is the formula; the bond is the thing being asked about.

### Q2 : Precision and settlement are different currencies

Fig 12 :125: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Fig 12 :126: **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Fig 12 :128-129: **"A long chain of cheap parts is expensive to describe and may cost one action to refute. A short arrangement over a costly operand is cheap to state and may take a whole budget to settle."**

Bits: galaxy counts, survey area, error-bar shrinkage, σ. Actions: performing the anchor observation that decides between two frames. A mission can be enormous in bits and inert in actions. Because the units differ, "we collected more data" cannot be converted into "we resolved the debate" by addition. Fig 5 :26: **"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"** The bargain is confusion removed, not data acquired.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** show a bit-only increase that settles a debate at the ground without a change of contact. If that is demonstrated, this currency split is idle and should be dropped.

### Q3 : Order is not optional

Fig 9 :43: **"The verdict tells you to leave. The lookup tells you where to go. Elimination is what you do after you arrive."** Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

The order is: (1) describe the slot residual in effect terms; (2) decide whether it is one question or a union (Fig 9 :54); (3) find the frame whose closure predicts that shape (Fig 8 :27-28); (4) sharpen the instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :33); (5) let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42). Formula step 7 keeps SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD distinct: SEARCHED asks whether it is in the closure and unfound; INWARD extends an instrument already returning something; OUTWARD imports a frame whose closure differs. A never-registered sensor is not an INWARD step, and confusing the two is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

### Q4 : Catalyst and functional group analogues

Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."** A method that produces an independent anchor (standard sirens) survives across many H0 slots and crosses up as a method, not as a recording: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Weak lensing is a functional group appearing in Euclid, Rubin and Roman. The group names a kind of contact; it does not by itself resolve anything. The qualifier and the bond decide. This is why "Stage IV surveys will resolve the tensions" is too coarse even as a world-frame claim.

### Q5 : A union is not a harder single case

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Fig 9 :58: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

A single observation can clear the calibration condition of the H0 tension while leaving the sound-horizon condition standing. The remaining case is not a weaker H0 tension; it is a different compound. Per-slot verdicts are therefore mandatory, and a single ranked list is a category error.

## CONCLUSION

An observation resolves when its **bond** to a live slot is comparison, subtraction or negation, when it pays in **actions** as well as bits, and when it enters the loop **after** the residual is described and **through** an instrument already returning something. An observation that adds only sequence-bonded precision is an isomer of the same substance under a different arrangement: it is real data and it is not a resolution. This rescues the defensible part of "upcoming observations matter" without pretending the figures rank telescopes.

**FALSIFIER (pre-registered):** if a bond-blind ranking of observations predicts which debates get resolved as well as a bond-aware ranking over a ground-tested set, the bond classification is idle. If bit volume alone predicts settlement, Q2 is refuted. Both failures are recorded here rather than smoothed away.
