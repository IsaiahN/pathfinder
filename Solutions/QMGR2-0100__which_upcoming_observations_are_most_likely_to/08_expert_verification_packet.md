# Expert verification packet : the Tether on resolving cosmology observations

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or instrument scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts; `general` on the structural rule. Not `grounded`.** No claim is made that any observation has settled a debate.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A cosmology observation resolves a debate only if it acts as a pinned anchor at which at least two low-overlap theory frames make different predictions, is sharpened from an instrument already returning a failing reading, and can refuse a held term or settle a new one at a named slot. An observation that only increases coverage, compression, credibility or combined-fit significance is frame-internal and cannot be the verdict. Because the live debates are union compounds, verdicts must be given per slot: standard sirens are an independent H0 anchor candidate; a deep B-mode upper limit can refuse inflation model classes; BAO, lensing and supernova probes gain reach only where their closures differ; dark-ages 21 cm is unreachable at this budget. The framework returns this shape, not a mission ranking.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 8

- **Statement:** coverage inside one closure cannot be a verdict; only a pinned, low-overlap anchor can change the reach.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :27 : *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :29 : *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 8 :32 : *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
- **Refuter:** show a coverage-only observation settling a debate at the ground without a change of contact or a refused term; or show an allegedly pinned anchor moving when a theory frame pushes on it; or show two "independent" probes drawing on one evidence pool.

### C2 : cosmology-facing; checkable against the current H0 and B-mode literature

- **Statement:** the highest-leverage H0 observation is a third anchor independent of both distance ladder and early-relic evidence; a deep B-mode upper limit is a resolving observation even without a detection.
- **What to check:** the LIGO Scientific Collaboration states that the standard-siren distance is "completely independent of the Cosmic Distance Ladder derived from electromagnetic observations". The CMB-S4 collaboration states that "Even an upper limit from CMB-S4 would provide invaluable insights into the first moment of our Universe". Is the independence claim accurate at the level of analysis systematics, and does an upper limit near the quoted r = 0.001 threshold actually remove a model class rather than merely tighten a bound?
- **Refuter:** a siren H0 that shifts materially when the ladder prior changes, or an upper limit that leaves the admissible inflation model set unchanged.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation with a named strain

- **Statement:** the live debates are union compounds, so single-mission rankings are a category error, and observations must be classified by bond and currency.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 : *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 12 :125 : *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 : *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
- **What to check:** is the H0 tension plausibly a bundle of calibration, sound-horizon and new-physics conditions, as the 2025 tensions report and Big Think analysis both describe? Does the two-currency distinction match how survey teams actually report precision versus model discrimination?
- **Refuter:** show that the disagreement shrinks monotonically under same-kind data, or that bit volume alone predicts settlement. **Named strain:** the Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, so comparing two theory frames must be cashed out as comparing their predicted readings, not the frames. Whether a settled molecule counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. This is recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of cosmology and not a forecast of which mission will succeed.
- Not that bigger surveys are useless. They are necessary channel maintenance and bit accumulation; they are just not a verdict on their own (Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*).
- Not that a 5σ detection is required. A refusal can resolve (Fig 5 :53).
- Not that any current tension is evidence of new physics. Tension reduction is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that dark-ages 21 cm is unimportant. It is unreachable **at this budget** under Fig 6 :34-35, which is a verdict rather than a silence.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by the sky.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. A third-anchor H0 measurement whose value is stable under a blinded re-analysis and lands outside, or decisively inside, one camp.
2. A B-mode result that either detects r or pushes an upper limit through a published model-class boundary.
3. Lensing, BAO and supernova probes that either still disagree after independent systematics characterisation (compound survives) or converge (pooled reading).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :25-27, Fig 2 :29, :31, Fig 8 :32, Fig 9 :55 and Fig 12 :125-126. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is the siren truly independent, and does the quoted upper limit refuse a model class?
3. Name one observation in your field that you think resolves a debate while adding almost no data, and say which bond it uses: comparison, subtraction or negation.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
