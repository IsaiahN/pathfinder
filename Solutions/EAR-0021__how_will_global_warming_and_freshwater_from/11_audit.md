## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among two forcing channels, a circulation state, and a measurement, indexed by slot and ordered in time. At least four operands once the measurement is included. A two-place reading (warming, circulation) loses the freshwater channel and is rejected. A three-place reading that treats the measurement as identical to the state is also rejected: the channel is not the ground.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. Forcing drives response; "weaker" is a threshold comparison against a measured baseline (Fig 12 :87), not a symmetric joining of two equals.
- **Scale:** the slot is a local convection site, the currency changes at the basin scale, and the consequence is planetary. The residual is intrinsically cross-scale. Fig 4 :19 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand."

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the per-slot shape to one sentence: *a warming, freshening ocean loses the density contrast that drives its overturning, and the response must be watched per system because an average hides where it is happening.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific observing decision: *keep both the subtropical and subpolar arrays running, report wind and buoyancy contributions separately, and state what the scalar index loses.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It collapses the union into one lever. Coming back down, it would tell a reader to "reduce warming" as if the freshwater condition disappeared. It loses Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
2. It loses the sign of the loop. "Loses the density contrast" does not say whether the feedback damps or reinforces, which is the operative question in Fig 12 :121.
3. It loses the anchor rule. "Watch per system" does not say that the ground is the measured circulation and that the observing channel, not the circulation, can fail (Fig 10 :24).
4. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would separate a density-driven weakening from a wind-driven redistribution or a channel artifact, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.
5. It loses the refusal of a schedule. "The response must be watched" can be misread as "a collapse is coming", which is the dated claim the solution withdraws.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
