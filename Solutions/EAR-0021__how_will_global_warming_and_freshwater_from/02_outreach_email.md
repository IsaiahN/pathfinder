**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** Check one circulation claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about ocean circulation. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the framework's minimum set composes a flow from an energy state and a gradient structure, "a difference that can be spent" (Fig 13 :22). Surface warming and freshwater from melting ice both reduce the surface-to-deep density contrast, so both erode the gradient that drives deep convection and the overturning. Because they act on the same water, they form one compound, not two independent problems, and because a spatial gradient and a temporal gradient are different terms (Fig 13 :76), they cannot be averaged into one scalar "conveyor strength".
>
> So the claim I want checked is narrow: **track the response per circulation system, not as one global number, and treat the thermal and freshwater channels as a compound whose interaction has to be measured.** The overturning is a loop, not a chain, and the sign of that loop decides whether a perturbation damps or reinforces (Fig 12 :121). A scalar index cannot tell you the sign.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not predicting a collapse in a named year. The public assessment is that the AMOC will very likely decline this century, with medium confidence that it will not collapse abruptly before 2100 and low confidence in the quantitative rate. I have no instrument that beats that, and the figures forbid naming a schedule where only a shape is available. I am also not claiming that "the global conveyor belt is weakening" as a single sentence: the observing record shows the subpolar signal concentrated in one leg of the array and shows wind forcing dominating parts of the variability, so a single belt is a metaphor with no anchor.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a sustained direct measurement in which the density gradient rises or holds in a convection region while both warming and local freshwater flux increase there, with no compensating wind or heat-flux change;
> 2. a well-observed basin where a freshwater anomaly produces the opposite sign of response to its feedback structure, or repeated same-sign anomalies produce opposite responses with no change in background state;
> 3. independent subtropical and subpolar observing systems whose indices move together after noise removal, so that one global scalar predicts both;
> 4. a controlled result in which the thermal and freshwater channels combine additively, with no interaction term, across the observed range.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I will withdraw the affected claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers, local to this package: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule, local to this package: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The loop and the mode rule, local to this package: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 9 (Ocean, Cryosphere and Sea Level Change): https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-9/
> - RAPID 26N observing array, National Oceanography Centre: https://rapid.ac.uk/
> - OSNAP, Overturning in the Subpolar North Atlantic Program: https://www.o-snap.org/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a circulation problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own limit is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, so a reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Each reader ranges the claim against the ground separately. |
| No request for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter before the conclusion | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No collapse date | Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." |
| Per-slot, not scalar | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| Two channels as compound | Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." |
| Observation channel named separately | Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" |
| Links only after a verdict is recorded | Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A reader who has absorbed the framework before checking has joined the frame being checked. |
| Prestige excluded | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A generator can be given away without giving away an answer. |

## Distribution constraint

One to one only. No list of likely believers, no bulk send, and no later pooling of agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). A refusal is recorded as data; an endorsement is not counted as evidence.
