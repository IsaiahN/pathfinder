## What this folder claims, in effect terms

The residual is the gap between the circulation state our vocabulary predicts under two forcing channels and what the observing channels actually return, indexed per circulation system and never as one global number. The two channels are thermal warming and freshwater from melting ice. They are a **union of two conditions on the same water**, not two independent problems, because warm fresh surface water is buoyant for both reasons at once. The answer the figures license is a shape: the circulation is a gradient-driven flow organised as a loop; both channels erode the density gradient that drives it; the response is tracked per slot, and a single scalar "conveyor strength" is a frame-internal average that hides the slots. The framework cannot name the year of a threshold crossing or a single global number. It stops at the limit of the observing instruments and records the lineage.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No dated prediction of an AMOC collapse, and no single global circulation number presented as the answer.

Three reasons, from the figures themselves:

1. A list selected for agreement is a convention, not corroboration. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and Fig 7 :13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not".
2. A scalar average destroys the only usable signal. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average".
3. Sending the whole framework before a check is recorded spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it".

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure and the structural one together.
