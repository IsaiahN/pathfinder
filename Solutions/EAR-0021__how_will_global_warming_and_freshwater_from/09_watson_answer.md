## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when global warming and freshwater from melting ice act on major ocean circulation systems. Option A: answer with one global "conveyor strength" and a dated collapse. Option B: answer with a per-system shape, the two forcings as a union compound, and an open sign of the loop until the arrays settle it. What each leads to: A delivers a schedule and a scalar that the observation channel does not return, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B delivers a checkable shape, four refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the limit of the instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For each circulation system s (subtropical Atlantic, subpolar Atlantic, Southern Ocean, gyres), R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| where b is the warming and freshwater belief, a the forcing trajectory, and o' the observed transport, convection site and mixed-layer depth. Arity: two forcing channels plus a circulation state, indexed by slot and ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric, the channels drive the response and the comparison is a threshold against a measured baseline. Scale: convection local, overturning basin, heat redistribution planetary. Fig 9 :22 names arity, symmetry and scale as the descriptors. This is a union compound: warm fresh surface water is buoyant for both reasons, so the channels overlap.
- **Domain 1, surface buoyancy and convection.** Actors: temperature, salinity, freshwater flux, sea ice, wind. Currency: surface buoyancy flux and density contrast. Anchor: measured density profiles, convection site, mixed-layer depth. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, basin overturning and gyres.** Actors: water masses, currents, mooring and float arrays. Currency: volume transport in Sverdrups, heat and freshwater transport. Anchor: RAPID 26.5 N and OSNAP subpolar arrays. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the observation channel.** Actors: moorings, floats, reanalyses, proxies. Currency: contact with the circulation state and the round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: the circulation itself, which persists while the channel decays. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, paleo regime history.** Actors: ice cores, sediments, Dansgaard-Oeschger events. Currency: reconstructed overturning state over millennia. Anchor: physical proxy archives. Admitted.
- **D5, "the global conveyor belt" as one object.** No nameable anchor, it is a metaphor. Excluded as a frame, not admitted as a domain.
- **D6, "collapse by year X".** A candidate claim inside Domains 2 and 3, not a domain.
- **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). The historic break sat at the objective and planning seam, because no instrument returned continuous transport. RAPID and OSNAP mend it, so the question is reachable at this budget.

Strain test applied: the apparent contradiction of calling a changing circulation an anchor was returned to census and resolved. Fig 2 :29 says the anchor does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, not that it is static. No strain remains after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the scalar and the schedule do not. The circulation is a flow composed from an energy state and a density gradient, so warming and meltwater erode the gradient that drives the overturning. The overturning is a loop whose feedback sign decides whether a perturbation damps or reinforces. The response must be indexed per system, the two forcing channels form a union compound and a functional group, and a global scalar plus a collapse date are refused. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest world-grounded point is that the conveyor-belt scalar was overturned by observation itself: RAPID reports wind dominance and imperfect meridional coherence, and OSNAP reports a sixfold East versus West difference.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states four refuters before the conclusion and keeps the domains separate, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a scalar forecast instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** partly. A published wrong structural claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, since no primitive has to be withdrawn. A physical regime shift, if the loop proves reinforcing, is not undoable on a human timescale. The distinction between an undoable claim and a non-undoable ground is itself figure-derived (Fig 12 :121, the sign of the loop decides whether it closes).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A sustained direct record showing the density gradient rising while warming and freshwater rise would break Domain 1; a freshwater anomaly producing the opposite sign of response would break Domain 2; independent subtropical and subpolar indices moving together after noise removal would break Domain 3; an additive combination of the two channels with no interaction term would break the union finding; and a re-census that removes the nameable anchor of any admitted domain would break the census. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
