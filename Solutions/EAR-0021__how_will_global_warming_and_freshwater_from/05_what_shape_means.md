## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0021 the shape is: *a warming, freshening ocean erodes a density gradient that drives a looping flow, and the response must be indexed per circulation system while the two forcing channels remain a compound.* A date ("collapse in 2057") and a scalar ("the conveyor is 15 Sv") are names or schedules, not shapes.

The same essay supplies the union rule directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is the Fig 9 :54 instruction in philosophical vocabulary, and it is why the thermal and freshwater channels are unbundled before they are recombined.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". We therefore describe "the surface-to-deep density contrast is reduced" rather than leading with a model name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (inherited from the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Here contact *is* possible, which is exactly why a shape is not the end of the work.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").

*They're Scanning Books* (opened) sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question, that means the six-term substrate is a visible guess used to organise the composition, not a discovery that the ocean has exactly six ingredients. Naming heat as the energy slot and density contrast as the gradient slot is a binding from the physical-oceanography frame onto held atoms, provenanced as such. It is not a derivation of a new primitive and it does not move the wall.

## 4. Why a reading cannot settle its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Metamerism: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The instrument that cannot distinguish cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: an apparent slowdown in a single scalar index cannot tell us whether the source is a density-driven weakening, a wind-driven redistribution, or a change in the observing channel. Caesar et al. (2021) itself attracted a Matters Arising (2022), which is a source-dispute in miniature. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name. This is the honest stop in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §5.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid mentioning the AMOC at all?

No, and this is a correction to an overreading that is easy to make.

- The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Energy and gradient are both already in the six (Fig 13).
- Fig 13 :76 "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." The figure itself distinguishes the spatial and temporal cases; it does not select which basin or which array.
- So naming measured systems (RAPID at 26.5 N, OSNAP subpolar) is reading the habitat off the world (Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world"). It is not inventing a seventh term.
- What remains forbidden is naming a **collapse date** and inventing a **single global scalar** as if it were a measured object. Those are a schedule and an average, respectively, and neither is a held atom with a ground.

This correction is recorded rather than hidden, because the first draft of this solution did overread the rule and refused all measurement-language. The re-census restored it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the arrays settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
