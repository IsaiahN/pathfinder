## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **The world assessment already refuses a date.** The IPCC statement combines a very likely decline with medium confidence in no abrupt collapse before 2100 and low confidence in the quantitative rate. Our structural claim adds a per-slot instruction, not a competing forecast. Do not present the shape as a better forecast than the assessment; it is a different kind of statement.
2. **The scalar belt was overturned by observation, not by argument.** RAPID's own account lists the "great ocean conveyor" expectation among the things the array changed: wind forcing dominates parts of the variability and meridional coherence is more complicated than expected. And OSNAP's East and West numbers differ by a factor of about six (16.35 Sv versus 2.72 Sv). These two facts are the ground for refusing a single global circulation number. They are the strongest non-framework material to lead with.
