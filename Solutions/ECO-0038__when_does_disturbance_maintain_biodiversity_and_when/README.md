## The residual in effect terms

The same named actor, a disturbance, is observed to do opposite things. It can raise local species richness after a windthrow or a grazing event, and it can push a community toward a degraded, lower-diversity state. The decision is not "is disturbance good or bad." It is: what determines the sign of a disturbance's effect on a particular community's diversity residual? The two options lead to different actions. Treating disturbance as intrinsically beneficial licenses blanket "let nature burn" policy; treating it as intrinsically harmful licenses blanket suppression. The figure-grounded derivation below refuses both and gives a relational shape instead.

## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Before deriving, I write down the sought-for shape so it can accrue a residual like any other term (Formula "Direction"). The answer shape is: disturbance's valence is relative to a residual, measured per species slot, with effects cascading outward from first contact, decided by a threshold comparison between two quantities, and settled by measurement rather than by agreement among ecologists. This is a shape and a route, not a threshold number. Binding it in advance lets it be refuted (see the pre-registered refuter in `01`, written before the findings were concluded).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that disturbance is beneficial.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons. First, a room does not upgrade a label: agreement among agreeing frames is a weighted average of influence, not an anchor. Second, selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Third, asking someone to trust the framework before they check the output spends the only independence they contribute. A refutation is more useful than an endorsement.
