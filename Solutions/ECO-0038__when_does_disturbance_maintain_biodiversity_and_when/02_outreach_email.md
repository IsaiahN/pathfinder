# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have checked the shape claim against your own data. If it does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** One shape claim about disturbance and diversity, and what would refute it

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of a question you know better than I do: when does disturbance maintain biodiversity, and when does it push an ecosystem toward degradation? I am not asking you to agree with anything, and I am not asking you to read a theory first. I have one output and one refuter. If the output fails your check, please just say so.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the disturbance is not the bearer of the sign. Its effect on diversity is relative to the residual of the community it contacts. The same windthrow that opens substrate for beetles can remove the seed bank the herbs needed. That is a structural claim, not an opinion about fire or grazing.
>
> From that follow four checkable parts. (1) The residual has to be read per species slot, because a single Shannon number averages away the one species that is actually disappearing. (2) The question is a bundle of overlapping conditions, disturbance aspect, diversity measure, scale and environmental context, so it must be split before it is searched. (3) The sign is decided by a threshold comparison between two quantities, such as return interval against recovery time, which is a relation, not a fixed universal number. (4) Consequences cascade, so a first-order richness gain can sit on top of a second- or third-order loss of carrying capacity or a mutualist.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that there is a single intermediate disturbance level that maximizes diversity everywhere. That curve is a candidate from the ecological literature, not something the framework derives, and its own reviews find it holds only on subsets. I am also not claiming that expert agreement about the intermediate disturbance hypothesis settles anything. A measured community trajectory settles it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before I ran this:**
> 1. a disturbance class whose sign on diversity is invariant across residuals, systems, scales and measures, with no case where it helps one residual and harms another;
> 2. a single diversity index that tracks degradation unambiguously, so per-slot enumeration adds no checkable information;
> 3. one diversity-disturbance relation that predicts outcomes across disturbance types, taxa, scales and productivity contexts out of sample;
> 4. a demonstration that the deciding relation is a linear addition, not a threshold between two quantities.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is worth far more to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this project.
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - The disturbance-diversity literature I actually read: Svensson et al. 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3321721 ; Dornelas 2010, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2982012 ; Miller et al. 2011, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3078405 ; Meyer et al. 2021, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8462171 .
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the method is in the figures and the formula. Run it on a disturbance problem you can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, because a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]
