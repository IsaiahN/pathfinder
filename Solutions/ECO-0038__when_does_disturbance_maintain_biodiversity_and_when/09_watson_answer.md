## QUESTION

Option A: assert a single universal rule, either that intermediate disturbance maximizes diversity or that disturbance degrades ecosystems. Option B: refuse the intrinsic-sign framing and give a relational shape with its refuters. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: one disturbance actor against a multiset of species slots. Symmetry: not symmetric, it is an ordered threshold comparison between two quantities, so which side is larger decides the sign. Scale: the event is local and brief; the deciding residual may be second or third order and may sit at a different scale from the measurement (Fig 11 :52-53). Fig 9 names the whole a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, disturbance as habitat actor.** Actors: disturbance events, species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (competition, predation, pollination). Currency: demographic shifts and resource release. Anchor: measured community trajectory. Question type: enumerate contact, never compose it.
- **Domain 2, the diversity measurement.** Actors: species slots, richness, evenness, compound indices. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: counts and persistence. Question type: per-slot partition, never an aggregate verdict.
- **Domain 3, the threshold.** Actors: disturbance return interval and community recovery or competitive-exclusion time. Currency: a comparison between two quantities. Anchor: observed coexistence boundary. Question type: threshold shape, not a named constant.
- **Domain 4, the management seat.** Actors: manager, restoration acts, introduced or suppressed disturbances. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: permanent, asymmetric work.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate habitat, then measure per slot, then split the union, then identify the comparison, then let the ground settle the binding, then act (Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*).

Strain test: assigning Fig 12's comparison to disturbance-as-event strained. Re-census placed it in Domain 3 as a timing relation between two quantities. Recorded in `01` section 6; no live strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; every universal curve asserted as a figure result fails. Disturbance has no intrinsic sign: its valence is relative to a community residual, so the same event can help one component and harm another. Diversity must be read per species slot, not as one compound index. The question is a union compound and must be unbundled by disturbance aspect, diversity measure, scale and context. The sign is decided by an ordered threshold comparison between the return interval and the recovery time of the slowest limiting slot, a relation whose number is local and must be measured rather than named by the framework. Effects cascade, so a first-order gain can sit on a third-order loss; alpha, beta and gamma are lossy transforms. Where a manager acts, introduction is available and exclusion is not, making this permanent work. Status: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, five refuters and lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a universal curve instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no curve is entrenched. The five refuters and the provenance rules let the claim be corrected without rebuilding the derivation; a circulated curve can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot before/after control showing a disturbance class with invariant sign would break Domain 1; a single index that tracks degradation unambiguously would break Domain 2; one relation predicting all subsets out of sample would break the union claim; a demonstration that the deciding relation is linear rather than a threshold would break Domain 3; a measure showing alpha, beta and gamma move together would break the scale clause. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
