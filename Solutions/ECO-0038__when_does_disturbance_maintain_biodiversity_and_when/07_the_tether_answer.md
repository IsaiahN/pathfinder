# The Tether's answer on disturbance and biodiversity

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The same class of event, a disturbance, is reported to raise local diversity in one system and drive degradation in another. The question "when does disturbance maintain biodiversity and when does it degrade it?" is therefore a question about a sign flip, not about a thing. In effect terms: what determines the valence of a disturbance actor relative to a community's diversity residual? Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that does not shrink with effort, where each rule works on a different subset, is not one hard question. The literature fits that description: support for a single peaked curve ranges from 16 percent to 46 percent depending on metric and ecosystem (Mackey and Currie 2001; Svensson et al. 2012; Kershaw and Mallik 2013, as summarized in Meyer et al. 2021).

## 2. The shape (verdicts kept per domain, never averaged)

**Domain 1, disturbance as habitat actor. Verdict: valence is relative, triangulated.**
A disturbance carries no fixed sign. Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries. The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another."* The Formula habitat rule says *"Valence is relative to R, not carried."* Ecologically: a windthrow can open substrate for beetles and remove the seed bank for herbs; Thom et al. 2016 report carbon down 38.5 percent and richness up 35.6 percent for the same disturbances. The "when" is a relation between the event and the residual of the community it contacts, not a property of the event.

**Domain 2, the diversity measurement. Verdict: per slot, triangulated.**
Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single Shannon index can stay flat while one species declines. Svensson et al. 2012 found richness and evenness disagree about the IDH in over 75 percent of studies using both. Report richness, evenness and a compound index separately, with an a priori argument for each.

**Domain 3, the threshold comparison. Verdict: a threshold between two quantities, shape only, triangulated for the form and `specified` for the binding.**
Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Operators table :49: comparison is *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* The two quantities are the disturbance return interval and the community's recovery or competitive-exclusion time; which is larger decides the sign. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework does not return the number. Dornelas 2010 shows the unimodal pattern appears only when mortality, birth and carrying-capacity shifts are negatively correlated; Janousek et al. 2020 shows the threshold moves with latitude and productivity. The relation is real; the constant is local and must be provenanced.

**Domain 4, management. Verdict: permanent asymmetric work, single-source for the figure rule, corroborated in practice.**
Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :63: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* A manager can add a disturbance (prescribed burn, managed grazing) but cannot subtract the habitat. Yuan et al. 2016 find richness peaks at intermediate grazing and conclude that preservation depends on both encouraging and limiting disturbance within thresholds. That is permanent calibration, not a one-off fix.

Across domains: the question is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-56), so these four verdicts must not be averaged into one disturbance-diversity curve. Each has its own loop and its own ground.

## 3. Cascades and scale, cross-cutting

Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A first-order richness gain can sit on a second- or third-order loss of carrying capacity or a mutualist. Dornelas 2010 finds carrying-capacity disturbance has the most lasting consequences because *"there is no recovery if community capacity is reduced."* Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Meyer et al. 2021 found the hump in alpha and gamma but barely in beta. Alpha, beta and gamma are not interchangeable readings of one quantity.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal intermediate-disturbance curve. That is a world candidate (Connell 1978) that fits the shape but is not figure-derived, and its own literature finds it subset-dependent.
- Not a number. No return interval, intensity or canopy-removal percentage is asserted.
- Not "disturbance is good" or "disturbance is bad." Both are the intrinsic-valence error F1 refuses.
- Not that expert agreement settles the IDH debate. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken; nothing here is settled by an instrument.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

The anchor is a measured community trajectory (Fig 2 :29-30: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"*), not a vote. The decisive measurements are:

1. **Per-slot trajectory:** for each species slot in a disturbed and an undisturbed control, does abundance recover, decline or stay flat? This discriminates a real gain from a stable aggregate over a dying species.
2. **Slow-variable test:** did carrying capacity, the seed bank, soil carbon or a required mutualist recover? Dornelas 2010 predicts this decides the long-run sign where first-order richness does not.
3. **Threshold test:** does the sign flip where return interval crosses recovery time for the slowest limiting slot, and does that threshold move with context as Janousek et al. 2020 and Gao and Carmel 2020 find?
4. **Scale round trip:** measure alpha, beta and gamma separately and report the gap; a local gain that is a regional homogenization must be visible (Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap."*).
5. **Standing falsifiers:** the five in `01` section 4, written before the world sources were interpreted.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure-derived result stops at a shape and a route. The specific threshold is unreached because the relevant instrument here is a system-specific measurement, and no such measurement was taken in this derivation. It is not unreachable in principle: before/after controls, diversity-distribution metrics and slow-variable monitoring already return readings (Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not declare disturbance good or bad for biodiversity, and it does not hand down a universal intermediate-disturbance curve. It says that disturbance has no fixed sign: the sign belongs to the relation between the event and a particular community's residual, so the same windthrow can help beetles and hurt herbs. The residual must be read per species slot, because a single diversity index averages away the species that is actually disappearing. The question is a bundle of overlapping conditions, disturbance type, diversity measure, scale and environmental context, and has to be split before it can be answered. What decides the sign is a threshold comparison between the disturbance return interval and the community's recovery time, a relation whose number is local and must be measured, not a constant the framework names. Effects cascade, so a first-order richness gain can sit on a third-order loss of soil or a mutualist, and local and regional diversity are lossy transforms of one another. Where a manager acts, introduction is available and exclusion is not, so maintaining diversity under disturbance is permanent work. What this offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. The threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
