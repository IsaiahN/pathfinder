## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand actually carried in the folder: disturbance has no fixed sign; measure per species slot; split the union; the sign is a threshold comparison; effects cascade; scales are lossy; the ground settles it.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management or research answer for a specific system, for example "moderate grazing maximizes plant richness in this grassland, so set stocking at X."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **value of the threshold**, which the figures refuse to name and which the concrete answer must supply from a measured trajectory;
- the **identity of the limiting slot**, which the figure says is per-slot but does not select;
- the **order of the cascade**, which determines whether a first-order gain is real or sits on a third-order loss;
- the **metric choice**, since richness and evenness support different answers in most studies that use both.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the demand to enumerate residual-relative valence per slot and to split overlapping conditions before answering. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
