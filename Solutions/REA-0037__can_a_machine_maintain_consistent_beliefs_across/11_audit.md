- **Domain 2, the ground and evidence frame.** Actors: observations, instruments, changing world. Currency: per-slot residual. - **Domain 3, the channel across time.** Actors: successive states, transcript, channel maintainer. Currency: bracket fidelity and round trip gap. - **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: an outside checker. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is the parameter that decides whether agreement means anything (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Union, not partition, so one lever leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :24-27 and :19 (frame-internal disqualification, per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29 and :34 (anchor, coherent drift), Fig 10 :24 (channel decay), Fig 5 :53 (refusal), Fig 7 :36 (mirror chain).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: the cheap quantity is disqualified; the ground must not update; the channel decays; a refusal is owed; the threshold is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ordinary engineering reading, for example "add a longer context, a retrieval step and a confidence score and the machine will be consistent."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of the residual, which the engineering reading flattens into a global consistency score;
- the **unary refusal**, which a confidence score does not supply, because a lower weight is an addition to a competition and not a negation of a held term;
- the **non-updating anchor**, which a retrieval step that reads the machine's own report turns into another frame;
- the **open item** in Fig 6 :47, which an engineering reading tends to close by declaring a scope rule without a ground reading.

One thing survives cleanly: a machine's self-score cannot settle its own contact. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no machine reading was taken. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
