# The Tether's answer: can a machine maintain consistent beliefs across long conversations and changing evidence?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on a numerical threshold. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No machine was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles at least three conditions. Fig 9 :55-56: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* The conditions are: carry a term across many turns, revise it when evidence changes, and avoid the machine's own report of coherence being taken as evidence. A longer context clears the first region and leaves the other two standing. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The answer, in four parts

**1. "Consistent beliefs" is not one quantity and the cheap one is disqualified.** Internal coherence is a symmetric relation among held terms. The figures classify it as frame-internal: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* (Fig 1 :24-27). The meaningful quantity is stable contact with an anchor that does not move when the frame thinks so: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A machine can be perfectly self-consistent and still have lost contact. Fig 2 :34 names the case: *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* `general` on the split, `specified` on the prediction that a high self-consistency score and a lost channel coexist.

**2. Across a long conversation the channel decays, not the ground.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics and pooled readings are reported as *"you have lost touch"*, not as a false verdict (Fig 10 :25-28). A conversation whose turns all agree is a mirror chain: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :12-13), *"A chain of mirrors is one long room"* (Fig 7 :36). Progress per handoff needs both bracket fidelity and the ground's pressure; zero either and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39). What may be carried forward is a method, not a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22-25), and the round trip gap must be measured (Fig 4 :30).

**3. Under changing evidence, accumulation is not revision.** Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 12 :112-113: *"A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* A system that only adds terms keeps the old belief and the new one together. Fig 6 :42-43: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* Where the world moved, a term and its refusal can both have been settled, and the record alone cannot decide between two scopes, a ground error and a changed world (Fig 6 :47). That open item belongs to the framework, not to this derivation.

**4. The threshold is a gap, not an approximation.** No figure line fixes a number of turns, a context length, a drift rate or a confidence threshold. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. This is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`: a real machine measurement could set it, and none was taken here.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named model does or does not maintain contact. No architecture is licensed by the shape.
- Not that a longer context, a bigger memory or a refinement of confidence scores is sufficient. Those can all grow the dictionary without writing the grammar (Fig 12 :115).
- Not that "consistency" is worthless in every sense. It is *"efficient for a convention"* when the product is a shared meaning (Fig 2 :41). It is disqualifying only as a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a number. No turn count, drift rate or confidence threshold is asserted.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.
- Not that the framework settles its own open item in Fig 6 :47. It records it and stops.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried in `05`): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a high consistency reading come from genuine contact with a non-updating anchor, from coherent drift, from a saturated metric, or from a shared evidence pool? An instrument that discriminates these would settle the source.
- **Handoff test:** measure `R_T` per turn. Does the belief re-derive against the anchor at the next turn, or is the transcript being replayed (Fig 4 :30)?
- **Refusal test:** when the ground moves, does the held term's standing to be composed over fall, and is the correction carried in the record (Fig 5 :55)?
- **Per-slot test:** is the residual truly indexed per belief, with one live slot hiding inside a good global score (Fig 1 :13, :19)?
- Standing refuters from `01` section 4: an anchor that updates on the frame; a refusal implemented without a refusal operator; a genuinely global residual; a room chain with measured fidelity and ground pressure that still cannot revise; a figure line that fixes the threshold.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return the conditions a maintainer must satisfy and the tests that would break them. They do not return an architecture or a number. The record stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and keeps the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :141: *"not only what was concluded, but what could have been concluded, and what would have had to be true for the answer to differ."* Prestige or agreement is not an instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, carried in `05`).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that machines can or cannot be consistent, because the word names two different quantities. One is internal coherence, and the figures disqualify it as a self-score: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. The other is stable contact with an anchor that does not move when the machine reports on it, and that is the one worth settling. Across a long conversation the channel decays while the ground does not, so a chain of turns that all agree is a mirror, motion without progress. When the evidence changes, adding new terms is not revision: a held belief and its contradiction can both accumulate unless the vocabulary has a refusal operator, which is not the same as deletion. Where the world moved, the record may hold a term and its refusal, and the framework says it cannot decide from inside whether that is two scopes, a bad instrument or a changed world. What it offers is the shape any maintainer must have, the tests that would break it, and the instruments that would close it. The threshold is left open until a measurement sets it.
