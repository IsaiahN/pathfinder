## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that a machine can maintain consistent beliefs across long conversations and changing evidence. Option A: treat "consistent" as one quantity and answer yes or no by a score. Option B: split the two quantities, keep the one that can bear evidence, and report the shape a maintainer must have with the refuters that would break it. What each leads to: A produces a self-score from a quantity the frame also produces, which is disqualified, and it hides a drifting channel behind an average. B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open slot where a measurement is owed. The verdict comes from the figures; world sources corroborate only.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, then partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The problem of keeping a belief in contact with what settles it while the conversation lengthens and the evidence changes. Arity: at least three overlapping conditions (length, evidence change, the machine's own coherence report), so a union compound, not one dial (Fig 9 :54-56). Symmetry: internal coherence is symmetric; correction against an anchor is ordered. Scale: per slot, and across scales with turns as rooms and `R_T` as the between-scale error signal (Fig 1 :19, Fig 7).
- **Domain 1, the belief frame.** Actors: held terms, candidate phi, the unary refusal. Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma) and cost in bits and actions. Anchor: none of its own, so it is not a domain until paired with Domain 2. Question type: partition boundary between held and refused.
- **Domain 2, the ground and evidence frame.** Actors: observations, instruments, the world that changes. Currency: the per-slot residual. Anchor: whatever settles the slot and does not move because a frame thinks so (Fig 2 :29). Question type: correction, plus the open case where a term and its refusal were both settled (Fig 6 :47).
- **Domain 3, the channel across time.** Actors: successive states, transcript, channel maintainer. Currency: bracket fidelity and round trip gap. Anchor: the ground must stay reachable; the channel decays (Fig 10 :24). Question type: room chain, selection between rooms (Fig 7).
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: an outside checker. Currency: agreement and residual, with independence as the parameter. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: triangulation. A checker from the same evidence pool is one witness under two names.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). The middle link, vocabulary and objective, is where the chain usually breaks (Fig 3 :24-27).

Strain test applied before choosing lines: Fig 7 was first read as governing within-turn consistency. Re-census assigns it to the between-turn chain and keeps the per-slot residual with Fig 1. No strain after the split. Two narrow live strains remain and are named in the verdict section.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

"Can a machine maintain consistent beliefs across long conversations and changing evidence?" The figures return a separator and a shape, not a yes or a no. Internal coherence is disqualified as a self-score, so it cannot be the thing that is maintained in any evidential sense. Stable contact with a non-updating anchor per slot is the thing worth maintaining. Across a long conversation the channel decays while the ground does not, so a chain of agreeing turns is a mirror. Under changing evidence, addition is not revision: a refusal is owed, and where the world moved the record cannot decide from inside between two scopes, a ground error and a changed world. No figure line fixes a turn count, a context length, a drift rate or a confidence threshold, so the threshold is a declared gap, unreached rather than unreachable in principle. Which machine instantiates the shape is a ground question this simulated derivation cannot settle.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, binds the sought-for shape in advance and writes four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a self-consistency score as if it were evidence, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The gap stays open, the two strains stay named, and a machine measurement can replace the shape claim without touching the figure derivation. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates genuine anchor contact from coherent drift would settle the source. A measured per-turn round trip gap of zero without re-derivation would break the handoff requirement. A refusal implemented with no refusal operator and no new competing addition would break Fig 5 :53. An anchor that updates on the frame would break Fig 2 :29. A figure line that fixes the numerical threshold would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
