## What the wider literature does and does not do

- The AI sources (Xu et al., Bonnet et al., Kirkpatrick et al.) are one broad machine-learning evidence pool, not three independent witnesses. Their agreement with the figure shape is expected and carries limited independent weight.
- The mathematics and philosophy sources (SEP Gödel, SEP coherence theory) are genuinely low overlap with the framework and corroborate the **shape** only: internal consistency does not certify itself, and coherence is not correspondence.
- The engineering analogue (CAP) corroborates that "consistency" is a term internal to a system's own bookkeeping. It does not establish anything about belief or mind.
- The animal behaviour source corroborates the contact-and-update shape at the widest possible distance from the framework. It does not bear on machine architecture.
- No world source was treated as deciding a verdict. The verdicts come from the figures. No world source conflicted with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered; if one had, it would have sent the audit back to the domain census, not flipped the verdict.
