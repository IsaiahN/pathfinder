## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupant. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is not "use retrieval" or "use a longer context." It is the four-part condition set in `01` section 0: a non-updating anchor per slot, a measurable channel, a refusal operator, and a lineage. That is what any candidate answer must have, and it is checkable before an architecture is chosen.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here, the effect description is: a system keeps contact when the slot's residual shrinks against something that does not move when the system reports on it, and it loses contact when the evidence changes and no refusal fires. That description travels to a cognitive scientist, an animal-behaviour researcher or a distributed-systems engineer without importing the framework's vocabulary. The world sources in `04` were chosen that way: by the effect, not by a search for "Tether consistency."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Therefore a machine-specific threshold, a named architecture and a drift rate are not licensed by the shape. They need a measurement or a provenanced import. This is why F12 of `01` is a `gap`, not an invention.

## 4. Why a self-report of consistency is the forbidden move here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A machine's report "my beliefs are consistent" is a reading of the projection. Several very different sources can produce it: genuine contact with a ground, a coherent drift with no anchor (Fig 2 :34), a saturated metric that has stopped resolving differences (Fig 10 :25-28), or a shared evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Fig 8 :34 states the mathematical version, and SEP (read this session, `04`) corroborates the shape: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency from inside.

## 5. Q: does the framework name a mechanism for maintaining consistency?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework returns a mechanism name. It returns the shape a mechanism must have. A particular operator or architecture is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the reason `02_outreach_email.md` never says which model does or does not maintain contact.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an already-available binding, a refusal operator, a provenance record, a channel check, is not inventing a missing primitive. It is a binding from the engineering frame onto something the loop already assumes. What remains wrong would be claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 : *"IMPORT adds an atom"* it adds none) or that the framework itself selected it.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a machine's consistency reading still does not determine its source, and several sources can produce the same reading. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held bindings. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The exemplar made the same correction in its own `05` section 5b; the re-census is therefore triangulated across two solutions as well as the README.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree that machines can be consistent is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any machine and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
