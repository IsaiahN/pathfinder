**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **foreground-removal assumptions**, which decide whether the projected sigma is achievable (Escudero et al found that with 1 percent foreground removal the future mission "may no disentangle among the different models in the (n s ,r) plane");
- **the convergence-versus-collapse distinction** of Fig 8 :49 versus :50, which the shorthand "overlap means one class" flattens;
- **the habitat change**, since the original forecast pool assumed CMB-S4, which its own site removed in 2025.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the criterion itself, per model and per slot against a fixed ground at a named instrument, with overlap collapse and a union compound. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the criterion is the same thing as the figure set" would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
