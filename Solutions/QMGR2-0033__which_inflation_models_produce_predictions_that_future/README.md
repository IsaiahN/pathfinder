## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Following Formula step "Direction", the sought-for shape is written down and bound before the run so it can accrue a residual like anything else. Call it **distinguishability class**: a partition of a stated set of inflation models such that two models are in different classes if and only if their predicted distributions on at least one observable slot (for example `n_s`, `r`, `alpha_s`, `beta_s`, `f_NL`, `n_t`, or a feature template) are separated by more than the projected resolution of a *named* future instrument. Without the instrument and its resolution, the claim is unbound and predicts nothing.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No list of "the right inflation model" asserted from the figures. The figures decide the criterion for distinguishability; the ground decides which model, if any, survives it.

The figure reasons are stated in `02_outreach_email.md` and `04_links_and_distribution.md`. Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions; public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary and honesty

This package is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** By *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. Figure citations are checked character by character against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and cited as `Fig N :line`. World sources were opened on 2026-11-23 and are listed with quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
