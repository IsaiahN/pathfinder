# The Tether's answer: can plural knowledge systems cooperate when they disagree?

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the case-specific ground and convention. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A joint decision must be made while the participating knowledge systems disagree about causes, evidence and success. The three disagreements are a **union**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." It has its own ground, kept per frame and per slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the object of contact. A non-updating ground is required.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :46: "The anchor is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote." Each frame ranges against it separately (Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). If no such anchor can be named, the arrangement is not yet a cooperation domain. What looks like cooperation is then mutual update (Fig 2 :13-15) or a shared evidence pool (Fig 2 :18-21), and neither is triangulation.

**D2, the frames. Disagreement about causes is the surplus, not the obstacle.** Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :47-49: "Independence is one quantity read twice." "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Retaining the causal difference, with provenance, is what makes the union productive. Do not average the residuals (Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.").

**D3, the cooperation arrangement. Success needs a convention, not a shared metric.** The success disagreement is the objective link, where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24, :27). Fig 10 :14: "a disagreement nothing can state". Fig 10 :17: "it makes the bug statable". Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one". Fig 10 :46: "a convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do." Fig 2 :41: "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". The guard is Fig 2 :43 (and Fig 10 :47): "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A convention that is declared, violatable and truth-value-free makes the disagreement statable; an outcome-fixing metric becomes another frame.

**D4, evidence and transmission. Admissibility on the residual; methods, not recordings.** Fig 9 :51: "evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

**Answer to the literal question.** Yes, conditionally: plural knowledge systems can cooperate while disagreeing, provided the three conditions in D1 to D3 hold and the D4 discipline is kept. The condition is not that the disagreements be resolved. It is that they be kept distinct, ranged against a shared non-updating ground, and made statable by a convention that does not decide the outcome. If those conditions fail, the honest verdict is no for that case, and the failure is locatable in a named domain.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that agreement on causes is a prerequisite. That would destroy the surplus (Fig 8 :49).
- Not that a single success metric should be imposed. That is a convention that stopped being one (Fig 2 :43).
- Not that every knowledge system may be averaged into one cooperation score (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not that one system should validate another by its own standards. Fig 10 :46 separates a convention from a verdict; Fig 2 :47: "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."
- Not that the framework can name the ground, the convention or who authors it. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not `grounded`. No case measurement was taken and no instrument was run.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the questions are:

- Does the proposed ground stay fixed when each participating frame pushes on it, measured by a record of its outputs rather than by the frames' reports? (Fig 2 :29-30)
- Does the joint result survive when the frames' evidence sources are made separate, and does it fail when they share one pool? (Fig 8 :47-49, Fig 2 :40)
- Can the success convention be violated without the outcome changing, and is it recorded who authored it and with what stake? (Fig 10 :18, :47, :59)
- Does each frame retain a refusal operator, so a held cause term can be corrected rather than merely added? (Fig 5 :55, Operators :58)
- Does what crosses between frames regenerate new ground-contacted results in the receiving habitat, or is it a recording that only replays one occasion? (Fig 4 :22-25, Fig 11 :70)

The six pre-registered failures in `01` section 3 are the standing falsifiers.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable for the shape; `gap` for the binding.** The ground and the convention for a particular case are not in the library. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 10 :58: "A seat with no stake at all does not exist." so the stake of whoever authors the convention is declared, not hidden. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that disagreeing knowledge systems must first agree. It says that agreement on causes is the useful difference, not the obstacle: two frames with genuinely separate evidence reach something neither reaches alone. What cooperation requires is a ground that does not move when any participant pushes on it, residuals kept per system instead of averaged into one score, and a declared convention about success that makes the disagreement statable without deciding the result. Evidence is admitted when it bears on the shared residual, not on a system's standing; what crosses between systems is a method or a head start, not a recording of one success. The framework cannot name your ground or your convention; it returns the shape they must have and the failures that would refute the claim. The rest is owed to an instrument, not to another vote.
