## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether plural knowledge systems can cooperate while disagreeing about causes, evidence and success. Option A: they must first converge on one causal account, one evidence rule and one success metric. Option B: without that convergence, cooperation is impossible and one system must absorb the others. Option C: cooperation is triangulation of independent frames against a shared non-updating ground through a convention that makes the success disagreement statable without deciding the outcome. What each leads to: A is either mutual update (converge on whoever had the most influence) or a shared evidence pool (the pool talking), both named collapses in Fig 2; B is not forced because frames can range against a ground separately; C keeps the disagreement as surplus and yields a checkable shape plus a refuter.

- **D3 Cooperation arrangement.** Actors: the frames plus a ground-maintainer seat that authors conventions only (Fig 10 :45-46). Currency: agreement and independence, residual surfaced per handoff (Fig 7 :26). Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). This is where the answer lives.
- **D4 Transmission habitat.** Actors: knowledge keepers, apprentices, conditions and relations (Fig 11 :43-48). Currency: contact and introduction (Fig 11 :61). Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the habitat updates (Fig 11 :73).

- **X2 Agree on causes as a single lever.** Already inside D2 and D3. Absorbed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** conditionally yes. Cooperation is possible while the systems disagree, but not by resolving the disagreements. A non-updating ground must exist and be named locally (D1); frames must be independent at the evidence level so their union has a surplus (D2); the success disagreement must be made stateable by a declared, violatable, truth-value-free convention that does not decide the outcome (D3); and evidence must be admitted on the residual while methods, not recordings, cross between frames (D4). Consensus-first and absorption are named collapses, not alternatives. The three verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. Which ground, which convention and who authors it is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the binding, `unreached` overall rather than `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** a cooperation that is really coherent drift or one shared pool gets a checkable label it has not earned, and participants are asked to converge on a success metric that quietly decides outcomes for them. The cost is locatable because the three domains carry separate refuters. It is bounded but real: a false yes here licenses a whole class of cross-system projects whose joint result is the pool talking. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices the remedy.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). A convention that turns out to decide an outcome can be withdrawn and re-authored with a declaration of stake (Fig 10 :59-63). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the six pre-registered failures in `01` section 3 would reverse it: a movable anchor with a still-sound joint result; a surplus that depends on a shared pool; a convention that decided the outcome with both frames still corrected; pooled residuals preserving the live signal; a recording regenerating results with no method; or a cause term corrected by addition alone. A source-discriminating instrument at the case level, or a genuine world source from a different evidence pool showing that independent frames produce no surplus, would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
