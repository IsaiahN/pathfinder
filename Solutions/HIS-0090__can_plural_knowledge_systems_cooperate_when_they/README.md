## The answer in one line

Conditionally yes, but not by settling the disagreement. Cooperation is triangulation of independent frames against a non-updating ground through a truth-value-free convention that makes the success disagreement statable. Disagreement about causes is retained as the union surplus, not averaged away.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not the verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
3. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
