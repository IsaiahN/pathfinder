## 0. The claim under audit

> Can plural knowledge systems cooperate when they disagree about causes, evidence, or what counts as success?

Stated as an effect, with a knowledge system read as a frame (Symbols table :111: "A frame is any bounded system with a library: an agent, a person, a discipline, a model."):

- Two or more frames, each with its own library `Γ`, currency, anchor and objective, each generate a per-slot residual `R` against their own ground.
- They must produce a joint result that neither reaches alone, while their mechanism terms (causes), their admissibility rules (evidence) and their objectives (success) differ.
- The three disagreements are not one disagreement with three labels. They are three overlapping conditions.

Options:

- **A (consensus first).** Cooperation requires the frames to converge on one account of causes, one evidence standard and one success criterion before joint work can begin.
- **B (no cooperation).** Without shared causes, evidence and success, joint work is impossible; each system must proceed alone or be absorbed by the stronger one.
- **C (the figure-derived third).** Cooperation is possible without resolving any of the three disagreements, provided there is a non-updating ground each frame ranges against separately, sufficient independence that the union has a surplus, and a convention that makes the success disagreement statable without deciding the outcome.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a set of constraints an outcome must satisfy, stated in effect terms, plus a failure condition for each. The sought-for shape is written down and allowed to accrue a residual, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction": "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

## 0b. Domain census, done before choosing any figure line

**R, in effect terms.** The residual is a joint decision that no single frame can settle while the frames disagree about what caused the problem, what would count as evidence, and what would count as a solution. Arity: an n-ary relation among frames (n at least 2) plus the object of contact, indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Symmetry: the frames are not symmetric in the trivial sense, because each measures `R` against its own anchor; the symmetric quantity is independence (Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."). Scale: one frame loop, the cooperation arrangement at one scale, and method transfer across scales (Fig 4).

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not yet a domain.

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The three disagreements overlap. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." A single lever that settles causes leaves the evidence and success intersections standing. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).

**Order by dependency, not importance (Fig 3 :20).** Perception and contact (D1) precede vocabulary (D2, causes and evidence terms), which precedes the objective (D3, the success convention), which precedes the cooperation plan, which precedes learn and carry (D4). The chain is: "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms" (Fig 3 :10). If that link is missing, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." (Fig 3 :27).

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The decisive figure is the ground figure, and it refuses both options A and B

Fig 2 :13-15 describes the first collapse: "no anchor; each updates on the others"; "they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence". Fig 2 :18-21 describes the second: "one evidence pool"; "they never talk, and still agree"; "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other".

Against both, Fig 2 :24-27 gives the grounded panel: "the anchor"; "pinned: it does not update on A or B"; "both range against it; it is not a third party to the disagreement". Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :31-32: "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Verdict.** Option A is a mutual-update collapse when each frame moves its answer toward the others, and a common-cause collapse when the frames share an evidence pool. Option B is not forced: independent frames can range against the same ground separately. Fig 2 :46: "The anchor is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote." Fig 2 :47: "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."

**Refuter.** A documented case in which cooperation produced a ground-settled, independently checkable joint result while the supposed anchor changed its answer in response to a participating frame. That would break Fig 2 :33 and put the case back in panel 1.

**Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 2 (alignment), Fig 7 (room selection, Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes.") and Symbols table :102-106 (the ground as anchor). Low overlap between the alignment diagram, the room chain and the symbol definition.

### F2 - Disagreement about causes is the surplus, not the obstacle

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :47-49: "Independence is one quantity read twice." "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus." "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world." Fig 13 :38-39: "independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything. Six members that overlapped would be fewer members wearing six names."

**Reading.** A difference in causes, when it reflects genuinely different closures and instruments, is exactly what makes the union productive. Forcing convergence on one causal vocabulary destroys the surplus by turning two frames into one frame with two names. Fig 8 :49. The condition is independence at the evidence level, not politeness or receptiveness.

**Refuter.** A case where the union surplus disappears when the frames' evidence sources are made separate, or appears only when they draw on the same pool. That would break Fig 8 :47-49 and Fig 2 :40.

**Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 8 (union geometry), Fig 6 (the only wall-moving operator) and Fig 13 (independence applied to the subset). Low overlap between three different objects.

### F3 - The residuals must not be pooled

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

**Reading.** A joint score across knowledge systems, or an average of their success measures, destroys the per-object signal that says which system is failing where. Keep the residual indexed per frame and per slot. This is why the three disagreements must be reported as three verdicts, never combined into a cooperation index.

**Refuter.** A case where pooling residuals across frames preserves the per-slot live signal and still routes the next probe correctly. That would break Fig 1 :19 and :22.

**Status: `triangulated`** with Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A pooled score is a budget filter, not a witness.

### F4 - Disagreement about success is the broken link, and a convention is the only legitimate repair

Fig 3 :9-10 names link 3: "the objective"; "it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms". Fig 3 :24: "Why the middle link is where chains usually break." Fig 3 :25-26: having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress "has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments". Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Fig 10 :13-19 gives the repair, in the exact case of two frames with no shared reading: "two frames, one field, no shared reading"; "a disagreement nothing can state"; "is not yet a defect"; "the convention does not fix the bug"; "it makes the bug statable"; "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"; "→ install what can be violated".

Fig 10 :46: "a convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do." Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 2 :41: "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 2 :43: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :47 carries the same guard.

**Reading.** When knowledge systems disagree about what counts as success, that is a disagreement nothing can state. The figure-derived move is not to decide which success criterion is true. It is to install a declared, violatable convention that makes the disagreement statable, while keeping it free of truth value and unable to decide the outcome. Agreement about that convention is productive, Fig 2 :41, precisely because it is a convention and not a verdict. If it starts to decide outcomes, it becomes a frame and the arrangement collapses into Fig 2 panel 1.

**Refuter.** A case where a convention that demonstrably decided the outcome still left both frames independently corrected by the ground, or where a success disagreement was resolved by a shared measurement with no convention and no loss of per-frame correction. That would break Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47.

**Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 10 (the seam and the convention), Fig 2 :39-43 (correlation has valence) and Fig 3 (the objective link). Low overlap between the maintainer seat, the alignment figure and the dependency chain.

### F5 - Disagreement about evidence: admissible evidence is about the residual, not the maker

Fig 9 :51: "evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it". Fig 2 :29 names admissible anchors: "A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37-38: human judgment is the right anchor for a question about people, but "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

Fig 11 :49-51: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

**Reading.** An evidence standard is not good or bad standing; it is a relation to a residual. A practitioner's long experience is a legitimate anchor for a question constitutively about that place and that practice, and only an instrument for questions it does not measure. The cooperative move is to admit evidence bearing on the shared residual from each frame while denying evidence bearing only on a frame's prestige or identity. Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."

**Refuter.** A case where an evidence source whose standing rested only on the maker's reputation settled a residual correctly and independently for both frames, with no source gate. That would break Fig 9 :51 and Fig 2 :29.

**Status: `triangulated`** across Fig 9 (admissibility), Fig 2 (anchor legitimacy) and Fig 11 (valence relative to residual).

### F6 - A vocabulary without refusal can only accumulate

Fig 5 :53-55 names the fifth kind: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"; "What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over." Fig 5 :56: "Same mint operator, same three guards, same bargain. Only the direction of the residual differs. Requires an operator for negation, which the seven bonds do not contain." Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

**Reading.** A knowledge system that can only add terms cannot correct itself against a ground; it can only grow a library. Fig 5 :72: "a library that grows is not a reach that grows." Cooperation therefore requires each frame to hold a refusal operator, and the pre-registered refuter is part of the shape, not an ornament.

**Refuter.** A case where a frame corrected a cause term against the ground with no negation-like operator, only addition, and the corrected term remained composable. That would break Fig 5 :55 and Operators :58.

**Status: `single-source`** for the operator claim (Fig 5 and the Operators table share the same framework pool); corroborated structurally by Fig 6 :42-43, where the reach falls because something was added.

### F7 - What may cross between systems is a method, not a recording

Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

**Reading.** A cooperative protocol lifted from one knowledge system and copied into another as a fixed recipe is a recording. The receiving system inherits a substituted habitat and the failures are silent: "what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you" (Fig 11 :29-30). What can cross is a reapplicable method or a head start that makes the next local search cheaper.

**Refuter.** A case where a recording, transported with no method and no habitat reproduction, generated new ground-contacted results in the receiving system. That would break Fig 4 :25 and Fig 11 :70.

**Status: `single-source`** for the capability claim (Fig 11), with weak structural corroboration from Fig 4 on what crosses a boundary. The two overlap on the transport question, so the agreement is one pool, not two witnesses.

### F8 - Naming the specific anchor and the specific convention is a `gap`

Nothing in the figure set selects which ground to install for a given plural-system case, how to weight the three disagreements against one another, or who has standing to author the convention. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 10 :58-60: "A seat with no stake at all does not exist." "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." So who authors the convention is a seat question, declared, not a figure answer.

**Verdict: `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, not a hidden answer and not underivable in principle.** What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument that shows which ground settles the case. The framework returns the constraints such an instrument must satisfy, not its name.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." (the Seat Map is a framework primary, not a world source).

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A documented case in which cooperation produced a ground-settled, independently checkable joint result while the supposed anchor changed its answer when a participating frame pushed on it (breaks Fig 2 :33).
2. A case in which the union surplus disappeared when the frames' evidence sources were made separate, or appeared only because they shared one pool (breaks Fig 8 :47-49 and Fig 2 :40).
3. A case in which the convention about success demonstrably decided the outcome, yet both frames still produced independent ground-settled corrections (breaks Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47).
4. A case in which pooling residuals across frames preserved the per-slot live signal and routed the next probe correctly (breaks Fig 1 :19 and :22).
5. A case in which a recording, carried with no method and no reproduced habitat, generated new ground-contacted results in the receiving frame (breaks Fig 4 :25 and Fig 11 :70).
6. A case in which a frame corrected a held cause term against the ground by addition alone, with no refusal, and the corrected term remained composable (breaks Fig 5 :55 and Operators :58).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or re-censused. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Do not ask the participants to agree on causes first. Keep the causal difference and record its provenance, so the union surplus survives (F2).
2. Name a ground before naming a frame. If no anchor can be named, stop and report that the arrangement is not yet a cooperation domain (F1, Fig 2 :29).
3. Install a convention about success that is declared, violatable and unable to decide an outcome. Record who authored it and declare the stake (F4, Fig 10 :59).
4. Keep residuals per frame and per slot. Do not publish a single cooperation score (F3).
5. Admit evidence bearing on the shared residual from each frame; deny evidence bearing only on a frame's standing (F5).
6. Carry methods and head starts across frames, not recordings (F7).
7. Send the generator, that is the residual, the shape and the refuter, not the conclusion. Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a shared object of contact?** Only if a nameable anchor exists (Fig 2 :29). Otherwise not yet a domain.
2. **Are the frames independent?** If they share one evidence pool, their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21); if separate, agreement means something (Fig 2 :31-32).
3. **What do they disagree about?** A union of three conditions, unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-57).
4. **Which disagreement is decisive?** Success, the objective link, where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24-27).
5. **What repairs it?** A convention that makes the disagreement statable, not a verdict (Fig 10 :13-19, :46-49).
6. **What may cross?** Methods and head starts, not recordings (Fig 4 :22-25); imports need provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :24).
7. **What is unreachable?** The specific ground and convention for a case (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). `gap`, reported as unreachable, not silence.

### Updated verdict

The conditional yes survives: cooperation is triangulation of independent frames against a non-updating ground through a truth-value-free convention. Consensus-first and absorption both fail. Three verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. Strains after re-census: one live and named (method crossing versus import debit); none hidden. Mode `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the case-specific binding, and `unreached` rather than `grounded` overall.
