## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape of cooperation is a set of constraints on any outcome that would count as cooperation between disagreeing systems. It is not a named institution, a protocol, or a percentage of agreement.

Concretely, the shape returned here is: a non-updating ground, independent frames, per-slot residuals, a truth-value-free convention that makes the success disagreement statable, and methods rather than recordings crossing between frames. That is a shape. "Hold a joint workshop with a shared KPI" is a name, and the figures do not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened directly: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the same move as Fig 9 :54-57. The three disagreements were unbundled before any figure was chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence "knowledge system" was cashed out as a frame with a library and an anchor (Symbols :111), not treated as a self-explanatory term.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, carried from the exemplar: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality". That is the question-as-tether, not a licence to pick a convenient frame.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."

*They're Scanning Books*, carried from the exemplar: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: naming a specific ground, such as a particular patient outcome or a particular harvest measure, is a binding from a local frame, not a discovery by the framework. It needs provenance and a shadow test. Naming a specific convention (a reporting standard, a consent protocol) is likewise a binding, and must be declared as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The reading does not determine the source. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question: two knowledge systems can produce the same joint outcome from different causes, different evidence rules and different success criteria. A shared outcome is a projection, not a determination of which system supplied the cause. The framework owes a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms, not a vote about which source to prefer. This is why the answer keeps the per-frame residuals separate (Fig 1 :19) and why an agreed success metric that decides the outcome is forbidden (Fig 2 :43).

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "You may all agree and be wrong." Both are the exact failure the ground figure warns against (Fig 2 :35).

## 5. Correction to my own §3: "shape, not a name" governs missing primitives

The exemplar records this correction and it applies here too. The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`. A convention, a ground measure or a cooperation protocol that is already available in a participating frame is not a missing primitive. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto an ordinary term, which Fig 6 :22 allows, provided provenance is recorded. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh primitive, or claiming that the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world.").

I keep both claims visible: (a) the framework cannot name a missing ground or a missing discriminator; (b) it can name an already-held binding, as long as that binding is provenanced and not mistaken for a derivation. The earlier, broader statement of the rule was an overstatement and is retracted here rather than quietly dropped.

A second correction: *Two-Eyed Seeing* (opened directly) is often read as "combine the two systems into one". Albert Marshall's actual wording is "from one eye with the strengths of Indigenous ways of knowing, and to see from the other eye with the strengths of Western ways of knowing, and to use both of these eyes together". That is the shape of independent frames ranging toward a joint result, not a merger. The Broadhead and Howard critique recorded on the same page warns that Western categorisation can miss knowledge that is "fluid, adaptable and continuously evolving", which is a concrete instance of Fig 9 :30-31 ("describe the effect rather than the cause").

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of distinguished people who already agree is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**Agreement is about meaning here, not about the world.** Fig 2 :41: "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". The one thing the systems may legitimately agree on is the convention that makes their disagreement statable. They may not treat that agreement as a verdict that one causal account won.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"; "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 7. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "can plural knowledge systems cooperate"?** Not a yes or no, and not a procedure. It is this constraint set:

1. A ground exists that the question is constitutively about and that does not move when any frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29, :33, :36).
2. The frames draw on separate evidence, so their union has a surplus (Fig 8 :47-49).
3. Residuals stay indexed per frame and per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22).
4. The success disagreement is made statable by a declared, violatable convention that carries no truth value and does not decide the outcome (Fig 10 :13-19, :46-49; Fig 2 :43).
5. Each frame holds a refusal, not just an accumulation (Fig 5 :55; Operators :58).
6. What crosses between frames is a method or a head start, not a recording (Fig 4 :22-25).

No figure line names the ground, the convention or the person who authors it. That remains a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."
