# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no source-discriminating measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about plankton and the ocean carbon sink before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about marine carbon that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the ocean's role in the carbon cycle is not one lever that plankton push. It is a union of conditions held by the biological community and the physical habitat together. Two quantities have to be kept apart:
>
> 1. the natural surface-to-deep carbon gradient, which is mostly set by biology, and
> 2. the contemporary human-caused carbon sink, which is mostly set by physics and chemistry, with the biological perturbation more than an order of magnitude smaller.
>
> The claim is that these are different questions with different instruments, not two degrees of one question. Plankton changes do propagate through an enumerated food web: the relations that matter are predation, competition and dependency, and the effect that matters can be several steps from the cell that changed. But the sign is not fixed. The same plankton group can increase export in one state and reduce it in another, and a community with the same species but different coupling is a different substance for carbon, even though a species inventory cannot tell them apart. The soft-tissue pump is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes, not a one-way chain.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which species wins, that any particular basin is a case in point, that a particular number counts as "substantial", that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of the ocean carbon cycle. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name and it is not a number.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a plankton change whose cascade stops at the first trophic step, with no downstream change in export or remineralisation;
> 2. a plankton-only change that moves the measured sink while temperature, stratification and nutrient supply are held fixed;
> 3. a plankton group whose measured effect on export never changes sign across a documented change of community and residual;
> 4. a basin where the biological pump is demonstrably a one-way chain with no return flux;
> 5. two communities with identical species and identical coupling whose export diverges with no difference in habitat;
> 6. one intervention that changes both the natural gradient and the human-caused sink by the same factor everywhere.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two quantities kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended "plankton effect" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map :287) |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning species | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51) |
| Cascade stated as a checkable reach | *"And contact cascades... the residual that mattered was three steps out."* (Fig 11 :52) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A direct-outreach reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
