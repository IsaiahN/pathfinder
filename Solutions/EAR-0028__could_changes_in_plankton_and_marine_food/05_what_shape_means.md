## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for EAR-0028 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about the ocean's carbon role that can be stated before any particular species, pump or number is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a union of conditions held by a community and a habitat, with a cascade through named relations, a sign that depends on the residual, a cycle whose loop can flip, and two differently weighted quantities (natural gradient and anthropogenic sink).*
- Name, withheld: *diatoms are the decisive group*, *krill control the pump*, *the biological pump is 30 percent of the sink*, *a change above X percent is substantial*.

The distinction matters here because the public narrative about marine carbon is almost entirely in the naming business. It names whales, diatoms, krill, mangroves or "blue carbon" as the answer. Those are bindings from other frames onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "biological pump", "solubility pump", "transfer efficiency", "food-web restructuring", "co-occurrence network", "valence" and "carbon feedback". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (a threshold comparison between two differently weighted quantities, indexed per region and pool) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating EAR-0028 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), and for separating the natural gradient from the anthropogenic sink, rather than as one hard question.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two quantities are separated, the cascade and sign rules are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the decisive species is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For EAR-0028, neither has happened. There is no source-discriminating measurement here that attributes a flux change to a named plankton group rather than to physics, and the marine names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the two `gap` rows in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The IPCC's *"low confidence in how changes in the biological pump will influence the magnitude and direction of the ocean carbon feedback"* is the ground's own statement of the same stop.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error the question invites. A change in measured air-sea CO2 flux is a reading. It is consistent with warming-driven solubility loss, with stratification reducing nutrient supply, with a plankton community shift, with a change in remineralisation depth, or with several of these at once. The reading does not determine the source. Ostle et al. 2022 say so in their own conclusion: *"our correlation analysis does not permit us to further untangle the non-thermal drivers and quantify the relative contributions of biology and physics separately"*. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a confident name. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes the relevant part: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory, and why the magnitude row is a `gap`.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to marine ecology

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*
> *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

The last lines are the author's own design instance, in an agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about oceans.

For outreach this means: a list of famous marine scientists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. The public contact venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` were selected as opt-in institutional routes, not as a list of likely believers.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular plankton group is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Diatoms, coccolithophores, grazers and mixotrophs are already in the marine literature's library. Naming one as a candidate is a binding from a world frame onto an atom already held, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), or treating the binding as the framework's answer. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about the ocean. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For EAR-0028 the canonical shape is: **a union of conditions held by a community and its habitat, enumerated outward through named relations, with a sign that depends on the residual, a cycle whose loop can flip, and two differently weighted quantities that must not be averaged.** It is not a species list, not a single pump, not a carbon number, and not a ranking of marine actors. The names that appear in the marine-carbon literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
