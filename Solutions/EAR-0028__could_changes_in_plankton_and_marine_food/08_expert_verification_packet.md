# Expert verification packet - the Tether on plankton, food webs and the ocean carbon cycle

**What this is:** a claim in a form a marine ecologist or biogeochemist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The ocean's carbon role is not one lever that plankton push. It is a union of conditions held by a biological community and a physical habitat together, and two quantities must be kept apart: the natural surface-to-deep carbon gradient, which is mostly set by biology, and the contemporary human-caused sink, which is mostly set by physics and chemistry with the biological perturbation more than an order of magnitude smaller. Plankton changes propagate through an enumerated food web whose relations are predation, competition and dependency, and the effect that matters can be several steps from the cell that changed. The sign is not fixed: the same change can strengthen export and weaken the sink, because the pump is a cycle whose loop can flip and because two communities with the same species but different coupling are different substances for carbon, even though a species inventory cannot tell them apart. The measured carbon inventory, not model agreement, is the anchor. The claim is a shape that any candidate answer must have; it does not name a decisive species or a substantial percentage.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 9 and 2

- **Statement:** the habitat is enumerated and contact cascades, so the effect of a plankton change cannot be scoped to the first trophic step; the question is a union of two differently weighted quantities; the anchor is the measured carbon inventory, not agreement.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
  - Fig 11 :47 - *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"*
  - Fig 11 :52 - *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
- **Refuter:** a plankton change whose cascade stops at the first trophic step with no downstream change in export or remineralisation; or a single intervention that changes the natural gradient and the anthropogenic sink by the same factor everywhere; or a demonstration that a model consensus settles the sink without a measurement. Any one fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one ocean-facing check

- **Statement:** viewed from the ground, the present sink is set mostly by warming, carbonate chemistry and circulation, not by plankton; viewed from the ground, export and sink can move in opposite directions.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
  - Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
  - Fig 12 :121 - *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
  - Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Resplandy, Lévy and Bopp 2026 state that *"The biological carbon pumps explain ~90% of the present-day surface-to-deep increase in carbon, while the solubility pump only explains the remaining 10%"* and that human-driven biological change is *"more than an order of magnitude smaller than the anthropogenic carbon sink (+2.70 GtC/year)."* Armstrong McKay et al. 2021 report *"opposite sign responses for the carbon sink"* where temperature-dependent remineralisation increased export but reduced the sink. Do those two statements contradict each other, or do they name different quantities? A "they contradict" answer is a real refutation of the mapping. A "they name different quantities" answer is the shape being checked.
- **Refuter:** a plankton group whose measured valence on export is invariant across a documented change of residual and community context; or a basin where the pump is demonstrably a one-way chain with no return flux. Either breaks C2.

### C3 - Structure and competing explanations

- **Statement:** bonds carry information the species list does not, so two communities with the same atoms but different coupling stand as isomers; the framework returns a shape, not a name or a number.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 12 :124 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
  - Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **Refuter:** two communities with identical atoms and identical measured bonds whose export diverges with no difference in habitat contact or imported atoms, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone; or a figure line governing the numeric magnitude of a substantial change after a genuine re-census, which would close the gap.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation pays for any part of this.
- Not a theory of everything about the ocean, and no final or unique theory claim.
- Not a name. No species, functional type or pump is asserted as the decisive one.
- Not a number. No percentage or gigatonne threshold is asserted as "substantial".
- Not an average. No claim about a global "plankton fraction of the sink".
- Not that plankton are irrelevant. They set most of the natural gradient. The claim is that the natural gradient and the anthropogenic sink are different quantities.
- Not that the marine-carbon literature independently corroborates this as several witnesses. Resplandy, Ostle, Benedetti, Lin, Armstrong McKay and Carroll share a disciplinary frame and synthesis lineage; they corroborate different parts of the mechanism, not a basin-level number.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-region study that measures Q1 and Q2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, follows the cascade beyond the first trophic step, and attributes an observed flux change to a named source. Concretely:

1. **Cascade test:** did the change at the plankton base alter export, remineralisation depth or flux attenuation more than one trophic step away? Fig 11 :52 says the residual that matters can be three steps out.
2. **Two-quantity test:** does the intervention move the natural surface-to-deep gradient, the contemporary anthropogenic sink, or both, and by how much? A pooled answer fails the test (Fig 9 :57).
3. **Sign test:** under a documented change of community and residual, does the valence of a group stay fixed or flip? Fig 11 :49-51 and Fig 12 :121 predict a flip is possible.
4. **Bond test:** do two communities with the same species but different coupling export different amounts? Fig 12 :5 and :117 predict yes; Lin et al. 2017 report a version of this.
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish warming-driven solubility loss, stratification, community change and remineralisation depth when all produce the same flux reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.

## 7. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :43, :47, :49, :52; Fig 9 :57; Fig 2 :29; Fig 12 :5, :104, :117, :121; Fig 9 :32. Twelve short passages, about eight minutes.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: do the ~90 percent natural-gradient result and the <0.15 GtC per year sink result name different quantities, or is the distinction an artifact? About four minutes.
3. Name one measurement that would distinguish a community-driven export change from a solubility-driven flux change in the same basin, or say why none exists. About five minutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
