Arity: two conditions, selection (S) and adjudication (A), plus their intersection, so a **union compound** (Fig 9: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either). Independence is bought. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: perceive cases, then bind terms for like case and irrelevant feature, then define progress as per-slot residual shrinking, then refuse the sufficiency of agent-only change before minting the habitat route, then describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Conversely, the core survives cleanly: a non-updating standard, a grader outside the slice, and habitat contact rather than agent-only change.
