## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0017 the shape is therefore not "an ombudsman", "a merit exam", "tenure protection" or "a code of ethics". Those are names. The shape is:

> a non-updating standard that grades cases, a grading seat outside the slice being graded, independence bought from low-overlap frames, habitat change instead of agent-only change, and selection separated from adjudication, together with an instrument that would show whether the shape is realised.

It constrains any candidate answer. It does not fill the slot with an institution.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, correlate it with recurring new terms, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Here the starting vocabulary was the question's own: "impartial", "political network", "employment or promotion". The nearest figure terms were the anchor, the seat, independence, habitat and comparison. Each new term narrowed the description until the two-condition residual appeared.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the direct source for the union reading in F7.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

In this question that means: a particular civil service commission or oversight design is licensed only by a case-level measurement showing that per-slot R shrank, or by a documented import with provenance and a stated shadow test. The world sources supply candidate atoms (anonymous examinations, an outside ombudsman, published indicators), but they are recorded as imports with their own limits, not as figure results.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the warning: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one institutional design into *the* answer would hide a choice that should remain visible.

## 4. Source, not appearance

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a declared code is not enough. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source": the same reading can come from one green light, from yellow plus blue, or from a source scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: an agency can publish an impartiality code, an equal-opportunity statement and a clean aggregate satisfaction score. All three are readings of the same green. They do not discriminate the source, which might be merit-based case handling, or a network that rewrites the standard, or a mixture of both. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The missing instrument is the case-level comparator, not another values statement. This is the reason F5 and F6 are not rhetorical preferences.

## 5. Why agreement cannot close the question

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

In an administration where every official depends on the same network, agreement among officials is the pool talking. A review panel drawn from the same closure is a mirror with a different name. This is Fig 2 :31-32 and :40 in narrative form.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For outreach this means the sign-off on a claim is worth nothing. A prestigious journal, a well-known society or a respected office gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not make the claim true. The first contact therefore asks for a refutation of the shape, and records the verdict before any theory is shown. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves... You need an external validator. You need both the concept and the percept... You need the seed and the ground."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not a test of whether any state actually provides services impartially, and they cannot settle the question they report on. No case-level measurement was taken. Status is `candidate`, not `grounded`.

## 8. One correction recorded

An earlier reading of §3 treated the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket prohibition on naming any institution. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive: a thing not held in Γ. The rule that governs this question is narrower and comes from Fig 9 :32 and the import gates in Fig 6 and Fig 8: the figures withhold the specific institutional design because it is an import that must be provenanced and paid for, not because names are forbidden in general. Both readings are recorded here so the correction is visible rather than silent.
