## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that impartial public service is secured by the character of officials, against the claim that it is secured by the structure in which officials act. Option A: character first, hire and train good people and publish a values code. Option B: habitat first, make the grading standard a non-updating anchor, move the grading seat outside the slice, buy independent frames, change appointment, promotion, assignment, publication and oversight, and unbundle selection from adjudication. What each leads to: A predicts that agent-only change moves the per-case residual and leaves the structure intact; B predicts that A leaves the residual where it is unless refuter R1 fires, and returns a shape rather than a named institution.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Per-case deviation between treatment on the merits and treatment received, indexed by case slot. Arity: two conditions plus their intersection, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: impartiality is a comparison between like cases, not a quantity held by one case. Scale: case-level at the street, system-level at the career and reward structure.
- **Domain 1, street-level adjudication.** Actors: officials and citizens. Currency: service decisions and discretion. Anchor: the merits of the case as a case-level reading. This is a partition-style boundary question at first order: which compositions of case features and decision rules are admissible.
- **Domain 2, career and appointment.** Actors: officials, political networks, appointing authorities. Currency: employment and promotion. Anchor: **none as described**. A standard the network can rewrite moves when pushed, so this is a cavity, not yet a domain. It becomes checkable only when an imported atom supplies a non-updating rule.
- **Domain 3, verification and oversight.** Actors: ombudsman, courts, audit office, legislature, press, citizens. Currency: complaints, findings, corrections. Anchor: case-level outcome data, never agreement. Independence is bought, not assumed.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive cases before defining like cases; define the objective as per-slot residual before planning levers; refuse the sufficiency of agent-only change before minting the habitat route (Formula step 2); describe R before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the habitat rule (Fig 11) and merit-exam reform were initially read as conflicting. Re-census shows a merit exam changes the habitat, not the agent in isolation, so there is no figure conflict. The one live strain is the ⋛ operand type in F9, recorded below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The habitat-first shape survives; the character-first claim does not, pending R1. The standard that grades public services must be an anchor that does not update when a political network pushes on it; the official whose career depends on that network cannot own the reading that grades them; independence must be bought from low-overlap frames; habitat contact must change, not just the person; and selection must be unbundled from adjudication. Impartiality is a comparison goal, so a headcount-and-budget vocabulary cannot state it. The specific institutional design is an import that must be provenanced and paid for. Domain 2 remains a **gap** at the anchor level until a non-updating standard is imported. One strain is live: the ⋛ operand type at case comparison. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a design copied without its method) instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as the cost of being wrong. Leading with the shape claim and its refuters avoids it.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings (a code treated as a fix, a single lever applied to a union, a design presented as derived) are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No primitive is withdrawn and no institution must be dismantled to revise the claim.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A case-level comparator showing connected and unconnected like cases treated alike would settle whether the shape is realised; an agent-only improvement that moved that comparator would fire R1 and break Fig 11 :40; a standard that survives being rewritten by the network would fire R2 and break Fig 2 :33; one lever clearing S, A and the intersection would fire R3 and break Fig 9 :54. A figure re-census admitting structured cases as ⋛ operands without demotion would clear the F9 strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
