# The Tether's answer on impartial public service

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No case-level measurement of service allocation was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A state allocates services case by case. The residual is the per-case deviation between treatment on the merits and treatment actually received, indexed by case slot and not averaged (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question adds a specific coupling: the official's employment and promotion depend on a political network, so the currency paid for a decision is career advancement rather than the merits of the case.

The failure is a union compound, not one hard question: selection of who serves (S), adjudication under the live reward structure (A), and their intersection (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The standard that grades cases must be an anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :29-30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." A values statement that the network may reinterpret is not the ground; a case-level record of comparable treatment is the candidate ground.
2. **The official cannot own the reading that grades them.** Fig 10 :86 "Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it." Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." The stake is structural, not a claim of individual corruption; where it cannot be removed, declare it (Fig 10 :60).
3. **Independence must be bought from low-overlap frames.** Fig 2 :31-32 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Ombudsman, courts, audit office, legislature, press and affected citizens count only if they do not draw on the network's evidence pool. More officials in one network is one long room (Fig 7 :36).
4. **Change the habitat, not the agent in isolation.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The habitat is appointment, promotion, case assignment, publication of decisions and the oversight channel.
5. **A declared meaning makes the failure statable; it does not ground the correction.** Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :16-17 "the convention does not fix the bug it makes the bug statable." A code of ethics is a precondition for correction, not the correction. Install what can be violated (Fig 10 :19).
6. **Unbundle selection from adjudication.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Merit reform clears S, oversight clears A, and the intersection needs both.
7. **Impartiality is a comparison, not a stock.** Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." Two agencies with the same staff and budget are different substances if the arrangement differs (Fig 12 :117).
8. **The specific institution is not named by the figures.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." A commission, exam, ombudsman model or indicator is an import to be provenanced and paid for (Fig 6 :24), not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named country or agency is impartial or corrupt. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that virtue is irrelevant. It says agent-only change is insufficient unless refuter R1 fires; it does not say character never matters.
- Not that a code of ethics is useless. It makes the failure statable; it is not the correction.
- Not that one lever fixes both conditions. The union reading says otherwise.
- Not that a particular civil service design is the answer. That is a binding, not a figure result.
- Not grounded. No measurement at the case level settles `left(R,phi)` here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A case-level instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **The comparator.** For pairs of materially identical cases, does outcome differ with political connection after the relevant features are held constant? An instrument that answers this per slot would name whether the shape is realised.
- **The anchor test.** Can the published impartiality standard be rewritten by the network? If yes, it fails Fig 2 :33.
- **The seat test.** Is the grader outside the slice being graded, with the stake declared? If no, it fails Fig 10 :86.
- **The habitat test (R1).** Does an agent-only change move the comparator? If yes, Fig 11 :40 falls.
- **The union test (R3).** Does one lever clear S, A and the intersection? If yes, Fig 9 :54-58 falls.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instrument that would discriminate connected from unconnected treatment at the case level is owed and was not built here. The framework can describe the shape such an instrument must have and can record the lineage, but it cannot supply the measurement. A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). The record stops where the instruments stop, and says so. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a bureaucracy and does not claim to settle whether any state is impartial. It says that the standard grading public services must be an anchor that does not move when a political network pushes on it (Fig 2 :33); that an official whose career depends on that network cannot also own the reading that grades them (Fig 10 :86); that independence has to be bought from frames that do not share the network's evidence (Fig 2 :42); that changing the person without changing the habitat changes no contact and therefore no allocation (Fig 11 :40); and that selecting officials and adjudicating cases are two conditions that must be unbundled before repair (Fig 9 :54). It returns this as a shape, not a design, because the specific institution is an import that must be provenanced and paid for. What would close it is a case-level comparator showing whether like cases are treated alike, plus the five refuters written in advance. Until that instrument runs, the answer is `specified`, ungrounded, and a candidate.
