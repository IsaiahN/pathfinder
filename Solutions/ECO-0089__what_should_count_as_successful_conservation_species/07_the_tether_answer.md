# The Tether's answer: what should count as successful conservation?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape and verdicts, `general` on the barrier rules, `gap` on the choice of one universal criterion, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading. No dataset was analysed and no real project was settled.

## 1. The residual it answers

A conservation actor predicts an outcome under a chosen success term; the world returns an observation. The gap has to be read per object slot, never as a single number: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19), and "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22). The four named criteria are not four answers to one question. Each works well on a different subset, so the question is a union that must be split: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search" (Fig 9 :4-5).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

**D1, species persistence.** The anchor is a counter of organisms, legitimate when the question is constitutively about continued existence. "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome" (Fig 2 :29). But a persistence slot read only at direct contact is insufficient: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles" (Fig 11 :52), and the habitat "is enumerated, never composed" (Fig 11 :43). Status: `specified`, `triangulated` across Fig 2 and Fig 11. The criterion is legitimate for its constitutive question and never a universal winner.

**D2, ecosystem function.** The anchor is a measurement of a process rate, legitimate when the constitutive question is about what the system does. The same cross-kind discipline applies: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change" (Fig 4 :20). Function cannot be read off a species count alone; the world literature corroborates with Tobias et al. 2025 and Capmourteres and Anand 2016. Status: `specified`, `triangulated` across Fig 4 and Fig 9.

**D3, evolutionary potential.** The anchor is a measurement across generations, on a timescale the action can reach. In an unmaintained system the ground still settles: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground" (Fig 11 :36). The timescale and the relevant vehicles must be declared, which Milot et al. 2020 also require. Status: `specified`, `triangulated` across Fig 11 and the formula's gate product.

**D4, human well-being.** Human judgment is a legitimate anchor but only under a strict condition: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it" (Fig 2 :36), and "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people" (Fig 2 :37). If the constitutive question is about a person, D4 governs. If it is not, importing D4 as the winner would move an anchor that should not move. Status: `specified`, `triangulated` across Fig 2 :36-37 and :29-30.

**D5, the reporting frame.** The indicator producer must be outside the frame it grades: "Its own output is never a measure of success" (Fig 5 :48) and "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). Agreement counts only at measured low overlap, because "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40) and "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13). Status: `general` for the ban, `specified` for the overlap prediction, `triangulated` across Fig 5, Fig 10, Fig 2 and Fig 7.

**The universal criterion.** No figure line governs it. The closest looking lines govern other quantities after re-census: Fig 2 :36 is conditional on what the question is about; Fig 1 governs how success is measured; Fig 9 governs question form; Fig 2 :43 governs the selection procedure. The honest report is "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134), with the limit "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). Status: `gap` after a genuine re-census, not a failure and not silence.

## 3. The six rules any defensible success report obeys

1. **Vector, not scalar.** Four per-slot residuals, each with its own baseline and threshold. A composite deletes the live slot.
2. **Split before search.** The four criteria are a union with compound intersections. "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again" (Fig 9 :54).
3. **Declare the constitutive question.** Derive the anchor from what the question is about. Do not appoint a permanent winner.
4. **Keep the criterion a revisable convention.** "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 2 :43). A declared meaning is not a grounded one (Fig 10 :18).
5. **Enumerate the habitat outward** to the cascade horizon and declare any substituted habitat (Fig 11 :43, :67). Management is "permanent work rather than a solved state" (Fig 11 :63).
6. **Carry provenance and a refuter.** Agreement is a witness only at low overlap with a record of where each indicator came from, because "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :51).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one of the four criteria is the correct one. That slot stays `gap`.
- Not that species persistence is primary because it is measurable, or that well-being wins because people are involved.
- Not a new composite index, weighting scheme or universal threshold.
- Not that the four criteria are interchangeable. Their currencies do not commute.
- Not that criteria agreeing is corroboration. It is one witness unless overlap is measured and low.
- Not that a fenced or captive population is simply "persisted." It is a substituted habitat and must be declared (Fig 11 :67).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

A real project settles its own report when a ground pays for each slot: a census for D1, a process measurement for D2, a cross-generational genetic reading for D3, and a person's own judgment for D4, each with a declared baseline and an instrument outside the producing frame. "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground" (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5).

The general question would move only if one of these fires:

1. A verbatim figure line naming a universal anchor.
2. A constitutive restatement of "conservation success" that the whole field accepts, which would let Fig 2 :36 supply an anchor legitimately.
3. A ground reading showing one slot's residual is the only one that persists and does not trend down, giving the selector something to point at.
4. A validated composite that matches the slot vector's decisions in all cases, including one-live-slot cases.
5. A figure re-census that admits the four as isomers over a shared atom set and gives a rule for choosing among them.

Until one fires, the answer is unreached, not shown to be unreachable in principle: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39). INWARD (sharpen the constitutive-question test) and OUTWARD (import a normative frame stating which values conservation serves) are opposed tracks and were not read against one another. Importing against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :24).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner among species persistence, ecosystem function, evolutionary potential and human well-being. It says the question is four well-formed questions bundled into one, so it must be split before it is searched. Success has to be reported as a vector of per-slot residuals, never a composite score, because an average hides the one thing still failing. Each criterion is the right anchor only when the question is constitutively about it: continued existence, a process, adaptive capacity, or a person's life. A universal winner is a `gap` in the framework, reported as a verdict and not a silence; what the framework does return is the shape any defensible report must have. That shape also requires an enumerated habitat, a declared baseline, a reported convention that never decides outcomes, independent indicators with provenance, and a refuter written before the run. The slot stays open until a ground settles it. Agreement among people who already agree is not the thing that closes it.
