## 3. What the wider literature actually bears on

Four findings in the world literature change what a careful reader should look for. None of them decides the verdict, which comes from the figures.

1. **The criteria are not measuring the same thing.** Tobias et al. 2025 argue that a species-only, pattern-based approach falls short of long-term viability and ecosystem function, and call for integration with process-based strategies. Capmourteres and Anand 2016 show that "conservation value" is used with multiple meanings and metrics across 139 papers. This is world corroboration of the figure-driven split (Fig 9 :4-5), not the source of it.
2. **The choice encodes values, not just precision.** Milot et al. 2020 insist that advocates of evolutionary potential state which vehicles, timescale, measurability and degree of human influence they assume, because different objectives express different and sometimes conflicting values. That is the world analogue of F4: a declared meaning is not a grounded one.
3. **Recovery is rarer than persistence.** Simkins et al. 2025 found that improvement in Red List category was uncommon and full recovery very rare, and Akcakaya et al. 2025 found assessed species less than halfway to full recovery and moving farther away since the mid-20th century. This supports F5 (permanent work) and the warning against treating a single good news slot as a solved state.
4. **Multi-criteria protocols already exist and keep the axes apart.** Brichieri-Colombi et al. 2018 score biological and socioeconomic benefits and resilience separately and report them as distinct numbers. The IUCN Green Status treats viability as necessary but not sufficient, requiring ecological functionality and representation. These are the closest world analogues to the per-slot vector shape, but they remain secondary: they are practices, not figure lines.

**Overlap honest report.** The ecology sources cite one another and share review and monitoring pools. Tobias, Dicks, Grace and the IUCN Green Status framework appear across several of them, so they are one witness family, not eight independent witnesses. Naeem et al. 2016 (framework analysis), Milot et al. 2020 (environmental philosophy) and Capmourteres and Anand 2016 (measurement review) are lower-overlap fields relative to the figure set, but all are still world sources. Corroboration from a shared evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the residual, the shape and the refuter first. The full derivation is stage 2.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not select recipients for likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 7 :35).
- **Fig 10 :52:** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

**Recommended order in a reply.** (1) The shape claim and its pre-registered refuter. (2) Ask what they would count as a failure and which constitutive question they think the project was answering. (3) Record that answer. (4) Only then, if they opt in, offer the three framework links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. (5) Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 5. The two-stage offer, and why the stages stay separate

The proposition is (1) validate the output against the ground, (2) only if curious, read the derivation that produced it, (3) use the formula recursively on other problems.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** follows Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A recipient's verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** under Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The full shape claim is in `01`; the compact version and its refuter are in `02` and `08`. Repair before sending.

## 6. Hosting

This agent can create files locally. It cannot host them or send them to anyone. Hosting (a repo release, an institutional page, an opt-in download) and sending are the user's to do. Prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact. An opt-in download page is closer to the figure's rule than an attachment pushed to a list.
