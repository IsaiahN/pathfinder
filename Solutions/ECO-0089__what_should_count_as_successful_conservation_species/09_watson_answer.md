## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the record's four candidate criteria for successful conservation: species persistence, ecosystem function, evolutionary potential and human well-being. Option A: treat them as four answers to one question and select a winner. Option B: treat them as four domains of a union, report a per-slot vector, and record the universal winner as `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a single composite or a declared winner, aggregates away the live slot, turns a convention into a thing that decides outcomes and spends the independence of anyone asked to check it. B produces a checkable shape, separate per-domain verdicts, a declared anchor for each constitutive question, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a predicted conservation outcome under a chosen success term and the observed state of the world. Arity: four conditions, so a union with overlapping compound cases. Symmetry: not symmetric under exchange, because abundance, process rate, heritable variance and well-being are different currencies. Scale: first order within a site or taxon, second order at the union, third order when habitat cascades are included.
- **Domain 1, species persistence.** Actors: populations, individuals, metapopulations. Currency: abundance, occupancy, extinction risk, viability. Anchor: a counter of organisms. Legitimate when the constitutive question is about continued existence.
- **Domain 2, ecosystem function.** Actors: processes, trophic links, functional groups. Currency: rates, fluxes, interaction strengths. Anchor: a process measurement. Legitimate when the constitutive question is about what the system does.
- **Domain 3, evolutionary potential.** Actors: genomes, alleles, lineages. Currency: heritable variance, adaptive capacity. Anchor: a measurement across generations. Legitimate when the constitutive question is about adaptive capacity.
- **Domain 4, human well-being.** Actors: people, communities, institutions. Currency: health, livelihood, security, subjective and objective well-being. Anchor: human judgment, but only when the constitutive question is about a person.
- **Domain 5, verification and reporting.** Actors: indicators, monitors, auditors. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero by construction.
- **Union, not partition.** The four conditions overlap. A viable population can contribute little to process; an ecosystem can function while losing adaptive options; well-being can rise while biodiversity falls. Compound cases need their own loops.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning and carrying. The question under audit sits at the objective link. Describe the residual before choosing a frame.

Strain test: the line about human judgment being the right anchor looks like it selects Domain 4. It is scoped to a question about a person, so applying it universally is a strain. Re-census returns it to Domain 4 only. No strain remains after that re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the winner slot does not. The question is a union of four conditions with compound intersections, so split it before search. Success is a vector of per-slot residuals against declared baselines, never a composite. Each criterion is the legitimate anchor of a different constitutive question, and none is the anchor of conservation in general. A criterion chosen by agreement is a convention, not a ground, and must not be allowed to decide outcomes. The habitat is enumerated outward to the cascade, substitution is declared, and management is permanent work. Indicators are counted as witnesses only at measured low overlap with a provenance record. The universal winner is `gap`, reported as a reach verdict and not a silence. All substantive modes are `specified` or `general`, none `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, derivation and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices with "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is the slide from a declared convention into a criterion that decides funding and reporting, which Fig 2 :43 names directly. Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by keeping the criterion revisable.
- **undoable:** yes. The `gap` is a reach verdict in a simulated run, not a withdrawn primitive. A constitutive restatement of the question, a figure re-census naming a general anchor, or a ground reading showing one slot's residual persists and stays flat would all close it without destroying the rest of the derivation. A sent packet can be superseded. The per-slot vector and the refuter remain valid regardless of which criterion a community eventually adopts for its own question.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a verbatim figure line naming one criterion as the general anchor; a constitutive restatement of "conservation success" accepted across the field so that Fig 2 :36 supplies an anchor legitimately; a measurement showing one slot's residual is the only one that persists and does not trend down; a validated composite that matches the slot vector in all cases including one-live-slot cases; or a demonstration that two criteria share a full atom set and differ only in bonds, which would upgrade the isomer claim. Agreement among people selected for likely agreement is not such a reversal.
