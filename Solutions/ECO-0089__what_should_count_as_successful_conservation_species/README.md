## What this package answers, in effect terms

The question asks which of four candidate criteria is the ground that settles whether a conservation action worked. Stated as a residual, a conservation intervention predicts an outcome under a chosen success term, and the world returns an observation. `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is measured per object slot, never as a single global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The four candidates are not four answers to one question. They are four domains with different actors, currencies and anchors, so this is a union of overlapping conditions and must be split before it is searched (Fig 9 :4-5, Fig 9 :54).

The figure-grounded verdict has two halves. On the question "which single criterion is the answer," the honest result is `gap`: no figure line elevates one of the four to the general anchor, and "unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). On the question "what form must any defensible success report take," the result is a shape: a per-slot vector across four domains, each with its own declared anchor, each verdict kept separate and never averaged, the union unbundled before search, the habitat enumerated outward to the cascade, the metric producer kept outside the frame, and a refuter written before the conclusion. The figures decide. The conservation literature read for this package corroborates the shape and is never allowed to override it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons. First, a room does not add an anchor: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict" (Fig 7 :35). Second, selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Third, asking for trust before a check spends the only independence a reader brings: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid the practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a simulated derivation. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a measurement, not a meta-analysis, and not a ground reading. It cannot tell a conservation practitioner which criterion actually settled a real project, because no dataset was analysed here. The world sources are read and quoted, but they corroborate a shape derived from the figures; they are not the source of the verdict. Where the figures leave a slot open, this package says `gap` and records what would have to be imported to close it. That is a verdict, not a silence.
