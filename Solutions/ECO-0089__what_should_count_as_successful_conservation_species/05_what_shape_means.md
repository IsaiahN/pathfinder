## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 puts it in the figures' own words:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 adds that the system can report what is out of reach without naming the missing primitive:

> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

A **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be filled. It constrains; it does not fill. In this question, the shape is not "the answer is ecosystem function." It is the set of conditions any acceptable success report must satisfy:

1. Success is a vector of per-slot residuals, not a composite scalar (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).
2. The four criteria are four domains with different actors, currencies and anchors (Fig 4 :20).
3. Each criterion is the anchor only for a question constitutively about it (Fig 2 :36).
4. The union is unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54).
5. The habitat is enumerated outward to the cascade (Fig 11 :43, Fig 11 :52).
6. Management is permanent work, not a solved state (Fig 11 :63).
7. The report carries a pre-registered refuter and declares the mode (formula, Direction section).

That is a kind of thing. It is not a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

The derive-first order matters. The residual is stated in effect terms first (gap between predicted and observed outcome per slot). Only then are figure lines quoted. Only after that is any world literature consulted.

- **Describe the effect before choosing a frame.** Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Had we started with the four names, any one of them would have seemed to fit.
- **Describe in effect terms, not borrowed words.** Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The phrase "what would count as progress here" is the effect term for a success criterion (Fig 3 :10).
- **Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term.** `THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because "the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." The remedy is to write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual like anything else. Section 0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` does exactly that.
- **Stop at the instrument limit.** The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here there is no single instrument that settles which criterion is the universal anchor, so the run stops at `gap` and records the route.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For a real project, a measurement of what actually persisted, functioned, adapted or improved would settle that project's report. It would not settle the general question, because the general question is about which constitutive question is being asked.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :33). A normative frame that states which values conservation serves could supply a general anchor, but it would be an import, and Fig 6 :24 warns that "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."

## 4. Why the world literature is corroboration and never the anchor

Tobias et al. 2025 argue that species-only pattern approaches fall short of long-term viability and ecosystem function. Capmourteres and Anand 2016 show that "conservation value" carries many meanings and metrics. Milot et al. 2020 show that the choice of evolutionary potential as a target encodes normative commitments that are rarely made explicit. Akcakaya et al. 2025 and the IUCN Green Status guidelines show that viability is necessary but not sufficient for recovery.

Each of these could be mistaken for an answer. Under the figures they are not. They are world frames ranging against the same residual. They corroborate the figure-derived claims that the criteria are distinct and that a single scalar is inadequate. They cannot override Fig 2 :36, which says an anchor is legitimate only when the question is constitutively about it, and they cannot fill the `gap` with a universal winner. A conflict would send the derivation back to the census; there was no conflict in this run.

The same applies to prestige. A widely used index or an authoritative society standard can give a criterion a budget to be tried. It cannot settle which criterion is right. That is the distinction the exemplar's essay mapping records as prestige versus action: an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, but an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59). A verifier who has already accepted the authority of the index cannot then check it.

## 5. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any of the four criteria as the answer. That overstates the rule.

- The canonical README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- The four criteria are not missing primitives of the figure set. They are already-held frames in the conservation literature, with published instruments and anchors. Naming one as the anchor of a specifically stated constitutive question is a **declared convention** (F4 of `01`), not the invention of a fifth primitive.
- What remains forbidden is inventing a fifth universal criterion and claiming the framework derived it, or claiming that a ground settled a convention.

So the correct statement is: the framework returns a shape for the *general* question, and it is silent on the universal winner. It does not forbid a practitioner from declaring which constitutive question they are asking and using the corresponding criterion as that question's anchor, provided the declaration is recorded as a convention and kept revisable. Both the overstatement and its narrowing are preserved here so neither is quietly erased. The same narrowing is recorded in `01` section 6, `06` Q1 and `11_audit.md`.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

The essay tradition warns that an approximate answer can be simulated, while a genuinely testable output would have to be derived from the real architecture or obtained by working the formula against a ground. LLM output is a simulacrum of a derivation in this sense.

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not measurements. They cannot tell a practitioner which criterion actually settled a real project, because no dataset was analysed. The second seat in `11_audit.md` reads the same evidence pool and is a mirror with a different name, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, Fig 2 :40).

## 7. Two consequences for how the shape is used

**Consequence one: the report, not the ranking.** Because the shape is a vector with a declared anchor per slot, the useful output of this package is a reporting form, not a league table of the four criteria. Asking "which one wins" is asking the general question that the figures leave open. Asking "which constitutive question did this project answer, and what does each slot show" is answerable.

**Consequence two: the honest stop is unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The `gap` recorded here is a reach verdict for this figure set and this simulated run. A normative import, a settled constitutive question or a ground reading naming the one slot that persists and stays flat would all move it. Until one of those fires, the shape stands and the winner slot stays empty.
