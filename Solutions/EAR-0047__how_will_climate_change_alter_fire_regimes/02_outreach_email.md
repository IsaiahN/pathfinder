# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before deciding whether the framework is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape and its refuter survive a fire ecologist's stage-1 check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the first-stage output does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about fire-regime residuals, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am not asking you to read a theory, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I have one
> specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about fire regimes. If it
> does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` sub-claims, `gap` on the empirical numbers):** a fire
> regime is not one trend line. The residual, the gap between what a model predicts and what the
> ground returns, is indexed by slot: region, biome and metric (frequency, severity, intensity,
> seasonality, burned extent, reburn interval). A global average near zero is a legal state even
> when one slot is live. So any claim of the form "fire regimes are intensifying" is incomplete
> until it says which slot and which metric.
>
> Three further things follow structurally. First, the relevant field is enumerated from contact,
> not composed: climate, fuel, ignition, terrain, people, and the cascades several steps out into
> vegetation, carbon and smoke. Second, the question is a union of climate and land-use conditions,
> not two separate piles, so the intersections need their own treatment. Third, the land-management
> seat can introduce actors (treatments, prescribed and cultural burning, suppression capacity) but
> cannot exclude a condition such as fire weather. Its leverage is real inside footprints and over
> finite times, it decays as fuels rebuild, and it is permanent work rather than a solved state.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that the framework predicts the sign or size of any
> biome-specific change, or the percentage effect of a treatment. It returns a shape, not a name and
> not a number. Those numbers are owed by observation products and experiments. I am also not
> claiming that the framework's "habitat" is the ecologists' habitat or niche. The source declines
> that mapping. What transfers is the rule about enumerating contact, not the ecological term.
>
> **What would refute this, written down before sending:**
> 1. Showing that one global fire-regime metric predicts every per-slot trend without residual loss.
> 2. Showing a management action that removes a habitat condition rather than only adding an actor,
>    or a treatment effect that does not decay as fuels rebuild.
> 3. Showing that treatment effect is invariant to the upstream fire-weather condition.
> 4. Showing that climate and land-use effects are additive and non-overlapping across major biomes.
>
> If any one holds, the shape claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails
> because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is
> needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my
> summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. The source warns that outputs *"could never be
> verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* A reported failure is
> more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thank you for your time.

## Two-stage order, restated

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures and the observations, not this summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
