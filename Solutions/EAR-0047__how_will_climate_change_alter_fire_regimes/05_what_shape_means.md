## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can fill it

The canonical figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0047 the shape is: *per slot, per metric, enumerated from contact, a union of conditions, asymmetrically and permanently movable, settled only by observation.* A single global trend line is a name without a shape. A 62 to 72 percent severity reduction is a number without a shape until its slot is stated.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened directly: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the expected observations are per-slot fire records, severity maps and before/after treatment measurements.
- *Library of Babel*, opened directly, on the bundle failure: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* and *"You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level statement of Fig 9 :54 and it governs the climate/land-use split in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F5.

## 3. What licenses a number here

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For fire regimes the ground is the observation product and the experiment, not a review's agreement with another review.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. The fire-science literature is such a frame. But two reviews that cite the same underlying studies are one source wearing two names (Fig 2 :40), so the ledger records overlap.

*They're Scanning Books* (as recorded in the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The per-slot decision in this solution is a visible choice about which dimensions to measure, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why "habitat" must not be borrowed from fire ecology wholesale

This is the specific trap for this question, and the source names it. *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture*, section "Three mappings offered and declined", opened directly:

> *"The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says enumerated, never composed rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."*

So Fig 11's *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* is a list of contact categories read from the world, not the ecologists' niche concept. When this solution says a fire regime is a habitat to enumerate, it means: list everything in contact with the fire-regime residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). It does **not** mean importing the niche concept and its disciplinary baggage. The same section of the essay also notes that *"introduction is available to a maintainer and exclusion is not"* is one of the claims with no prior citation found, recorded as *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Correction to my own first reading.** My initial audit treated Fig 11 as if it licensed an ecological-habitat analysis directly. That was the declined mapping. It is retracted here and in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6. The structural rules of Fig 11 still transfer; the ecological term does not. This is a genuine retraction, kept visible rather than silently replaced.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a verdict

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers respected fire ecologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried (a pilot check, a review submission, a reanalysis of an existing dataset), and let the ground, an observation or an experiment, settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains public submission and opt-in venues and no personal emails, and why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what would refute the claim rather than asking for agreement.

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened directly:

> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."*
> *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

And the architecture article's own caveat: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any fire-regime claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The four refuters are the design instrument that keeps this honest.

## 7. What the shape is for this question, stated once

> Fire-regime change is a per-slot, per-metric residual over an enumerated field of contact; climate and land use form an overlapping union that must be unbundled before attribution; land management can introduce but cannot exclude, acts downstream of fire weather, and is permanent work rather than a solved state; the sign, size and effect estimates are owed by observation, not by the figures.

That is a shape. It is not an answer to "how much", and it does not pretend to be.
