## Wider literature that bears on the residual

These were read through the sources above; where the full text was not fetched, that is stated in `10_source_ledger.csv` and no verbatim quote is attributed to them here.

- **Flannigan et al. 2009**, *Implications of changing climate for global wildland fire*, International Journal of Wildland Fire 18(5), 483 to 507. A general increase in area burned and occurrence, with substantial spatial variability including areas of no change or decrease. Bears on Fig 1 per-slot reporting.
- **Davis et al. 2024**, *Tamm review: a meta-analysis of thinning, prescribed fire, and wildfire effects on subsequent wildfire severity*, Forest Ecology and Management 561, 121885. Stand-level severity reductions of 62 to 72 percent, declining with treatment age. Bears on Fig 11 permanent work.
- **Jain et al. 2021**, *Effectiveness of fuel treatments at the landscape scale*, USDA Forest Service Rocky Mountain Research Station. Treatment effectiveness often limited during extreme fire weather. Bears on Fig 3 dependency order.
- **Bowman et al. 2020**, *Vegetation fires in the Anthropocene*, Nature Reviews Earth and Environment 1, 500 to 515. Cited by Haas et al. 2024 as the earth-system framing of fire. Not independently fetched; used only as a signpost.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the observations, not this summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This folder states its own refuters in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. Send those, not the conclusion.
