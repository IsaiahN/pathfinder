## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a two-part question. Option A: report one global direction for fire regimes and treat land management as a lever that offsets it. Option B: treat the residual as per slot, enumerate the field of contact, unbundle the climate/land-use union, and report a shape with bounded, asymmetric management leverage and an explicit gap where the empirical numbers belong. Option A hides live local signals in an average and overstates management. Option B keeps the residual visible and states what must be measured before any number is believed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicted and observed fire-regime behaviour, indexed by region, biome and metric (frequency, severity, intensity, seasonality, burned extent, reburn interval). Arity: multi. Symmetry: broken, because management can introduce but not exclude. Scale: landscape to regional, with cascades several steps out. Fig 9 :55 names it a union compound: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Domain 1, fire-climate habitat.** Actors: temperature, humidity, drought, wind, season, terrain, vegetation and fuel, ignition, people. Currency: per-slot fire-regime quantities. Anchor: observation (fire records, MODIS, VIIRS, GFED, FIRMS). Question type: a field of contact to enumerate, then a union of overlapping conditions.
- **Domain 2, land-management seat.** Actors: managers, thinning, prescribed and cultural burning, suppression, policy. Currency: before/after differences in fire behaviour. Anchor: experiments and BACI style measurements. Question type: a downstream link with asymmetric power; permanent work, not a solved state.
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach.** Actors: fire ecologists, reviewers, data stewards. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so the third domain's independence is near zero.

**Order by dependency:** observe the upstream condition (fire weather, fuel state), then state a measurable objective, then evaluate a treatment. Optimising a downstream link cannot rescue a broken upstream one (Fig 3 :19). **Strain test applied:** the figure's habitat is a residual-relative enumeration, not the ecologists' ecological niche; the source declines that mapping, so the term was re-censused rather than borrowed. No other strain survived re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical numbers do not exist in the figure set. Fire-regime change is a per-slot, per-metric residual over an enumerated field of contact; climate and land use form an overlapping union that must be unbundled before attribution; land management can introduce but cannot exclude, acts downstream of fire weather, and is permanent work rather than a solved state. The sign, size and effect estimates are owed by observation and experiment. No figure line governs them, and the honest verdict is `gap` for that part. Four refuters were written before the run: a global metric that predicts every slot; an action that excludes a habitat condition; a treatment effect invariant to fire weather; additive climate and land-use effects. The habitat-as-ecological-niche mapping was declined and retracted, not smoothed over. This is a `general` and `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is presenting a figure-derived shape as an empirical number, which would mislead land-management decisions.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical gap is declared rather than filled, so no number has to be withdrawn. The one over-read (habitat as niche) is already retracted in `05_what_shape_means.md` and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, with the original reading preserved. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one without touching the derivation.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot observation products with stated uncertainty, or before/after/control/impact measurements recording treatment age and fire-weather percentile, would move a `specified` sub-claim toward `grounded`. Any of the four refuters firing would break the shape. A verbatim figure line that directly predicts a biome-specific sign would move F7 from `gap` to governed and supersede this derivation. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
