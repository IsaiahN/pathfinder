# Expert verification packet: the Tether on climate, fire regimes and land management

**What this is:** a claim in a form a fire ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the checkable sub-claims, `gap` on the empirical central question. Not `grounded`.** No biome-specific sign or treatment effect size is asserted.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Fire-regime change is not one trend line. The residual, the gap between predicted and observed fire-regime behaviour, is indexed by slot: region, biome and metric (frequency, severity, intensity, seasonality, burned extent, reburn interval). A global average near zero is a legal state even when one slot is live. The field of contact is enumerated outward, not composed: climate, fuel, ignition, terrain, people, and cascades into vegetation, carbon and smoke. Climate and land use form an overlapping union, not two separate piles. Land management can introduce actors (treatments, prescribed and cultural burning, suppression capacity) but cannot exclude a condition such as fire weather; it acts downstream of that condition, its effects decay as fuels rebuild, and it is permanent work rather than a solved state. The sign, size and effect numbers are owed by observation and experiment. The framework returns a shape, not a number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** R is per slot; the contact field is enumerated; the climate/land-use split is a union.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
  - Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
  - Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
  - Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
- **Refuter:** show that one global fire-regime metric predicts every per-slot trend without residual loss; or that the relevant contact set is closed under composition; or that climate and land-use effects are additive and non-overlapping across major biomes.
- **Scope note:** the figure's habitat is defined by contact with a residual, not by an organism's relation to its environment. The source's "Three mappings offered and declined" section explicitly declines *"The habitat as an ecological niche"*. Do not read C1 as importing the ecological niche concept.

### C2 - management-facing; checkable against treatment studies

- **Statement:** management leverage is asymmetric, downstream and temporary. It can add fuel treatments, prescribed and cultural burning and suppression capacity; it cannot remove drought or fire weather; effects are bounded by footprint, decay with fuel regrowth, and require maintenance.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :61 - *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
  - Fig 11 :63 - *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."*
  - Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 3 :19 - *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- **What to check:** in the studies you know, is the treatment effect conditioned on fire-weather percentile and on treatment age? Does any treatment remove the fire-weather condition itself? Davis et al. 2024 report 62 to 72 percent stand-level severity reductions that decline with treatment age; McKinney et al. 2022 report that at some extents fire weather mattered more than area treated, and that strong landscape-level operational conclusions are not yet possible.
- **Refuter:** a treatment effect invariant to fire weather; a one-off treatment with permanent effect; or a management action that excludes a habitat condition.

### C3 - framework-internal limit; checkable by reading the figures

- **Statement:** no figure line governs the empirical sign or magnitude, so that part is `gap`, not a result.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
  - Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
  - Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **Refuter:** produce a figure line that directly predicts a biome-specific sign or a numeric treatment effect, with a verbatim citation. If one exists, this sub-claim is superseded and the derivation should be redone.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a global trend prediction, and not a number for any biome.
- Not that land management can cancel climate-driven change.
- Not that the figure's habitat is an ecological niche. That mapping is declined by the source.
- Not that climate and land use are additive and separable.
- Not that agreement among reviews settles anything. Reviews sharing underlying studies are one evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No fire measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

1. Per-slot observation products (MODIS, VIIRS, GFED, FIRMS), reported per region, biome and metric with uncertainty.
2. Before/after/control/impact or post-fire severity measurements, recording treatment age and fire-weather percentile.
3. Attribution analyses that treat climate and land use as overlapping conditions.
4. Any of the four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` firing against the shape claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 11 :43, :61, :63 and Fig 9 :55 - six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own field: is the effect of a fuel treatment conditioned on fire weather and treatment age? Does any treatment remove the fire-weather condition?
3. Name one per-slot observation or experiment that would change your mind about the union claim, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
