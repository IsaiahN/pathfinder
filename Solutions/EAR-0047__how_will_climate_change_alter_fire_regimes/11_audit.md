## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the gap between predicted and observed fire-regime behaviour. Arity: multi, because the regime is a bundle of metrics and the drivers are a bundle of conditions. Symmetry: broken, because a management seat can introduce but not exclude. Scale: landscape to regional, with cascades several steps out. This is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, fire-climate habitat.** Actors: temperature, humidity, drought, wind, season, terrain, fuel, ignition, people. Currency: per-slot fire metrics. Anchor: observation products and field measurement. Question type: enumerate a field of contact, then unbundle an overlap.
- **Domain 2, land-management seat.** Actors: treatments, prescribed and cultural burning, suppression, policy. Currency: before/after differences in fire behaviour. Anchor: experiment and BACI measurement. Question type: downstream link with asymmetric power; permanent work.
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach.** Actors: fire ecologists, reviewers, data stewards. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence near zero in this simulated run.

Order by dependency: observe the upstream condition and state a measurable objective before evaluating a treatment (Fig 3 :19). My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 11, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's plan/goal/progress distinction. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :43, :61, :63 (enumeration, asymmetry, permanence), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 8 :39 and Fig 13 :134 (gap as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-line shape in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: per slot and per metric; enumerated contact; overlapping union; asymmetric, downstream, permanent management; empirical numbers owed by observation.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete research or management report: a global "fire regimes are intensifying" headline with a single treatment-effectiveness percentage and no slot, no fire-weather conditioning and no treatment age.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which a global headline drops;
- the **union structure**, which an additive climate-plus-land-use decomposition drops;
- the **asymmetry and decay of management leverage**, which a single percentage with no treatment age or fire-weather percentile drops;
- the **gap status**, which a number presented as settled drops.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must be enumerated from contact before a management effect can be read, and the seat cannot exclude a condition. That is the residue worth sending. **R_T was not measured numerically**, because no ground reading exists for the shape claim itself; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a stated gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now says.

## 5. Strains and re-census

- **Habitat versus ecological niche.** The primary's first reading treated Fig 11 as licensing an ecological-habitat analysis. The source article's "Three mappings offered and declined" section explicitly declines *"The habitat as an ecological niche"*, saying the figure's habitat is defined by contact with a residual and the residual moves. Re-census resolves it: the structural rules transfer, the ecological term does not. Retraction kept visible in `05_what_shape_means.md` section 4 and `01` section 6. Matches the canonical rule that a reading which makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.
- **Davis et al. versus McKinney et al. on treatment confidence.** Not a conflict after re-census. Davis measures stand-level severity; McKinney assesses landscape-level empirical evidence. Different objects and scales, so both can be true. The primary records both rather than averaging them.
- **Fig 11 :46 names conditions that look like the ecologists' list.** Resolved by the same re-census: the figure lists light, water, temperature, season and terrain as *conditions in contact*, not as a niche definition.
- **The `gap` verdict and the empirical world literature.** Not a contradiction. The world literature has real measurements; the figure set has no fire model. The gap is about what the figures can derive, not about whether fire science knows anything.
- **After re-census: one strain resolved by retraction, no live strains hidden.**
