## What is deliberately not here and why

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* A list of fire ecologists selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01`, `08` and `02` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape claim. Record that verdict before showing any theory. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
