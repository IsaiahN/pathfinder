## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons for the order in `02_outreach_email.md`:

- **Fig 10 :52**, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual and the shape claim before the framework. A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
- **Fig 2 :35**, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. Ask what would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the figures.
- **Fig 9 :60**, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* An importance ranking is a filter for which question gets tried first. It is never evidence for the verdict.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the four-part shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method to reuse with their own refuter and mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it, and labels the mode (THE_FORMULA "Direction").
