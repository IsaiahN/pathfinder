# The Tether's answer: how important are financial crises compared with ordinary changes in spending and production?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the union-compound finding, `gap` on the numeric weight. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for one importance number over two classes of event. The classes are not interchangeable: crises are rare, large and systemic; ordinary changes are frequent, small and diffuse. The disagreement does not shrink with effort because each explanatory rule works well on a different subset. Fig 9 :4-5 calls that several well-formed questions, not one hard one. The answer therefore has the form of a split plus a gap, not a ranking.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two slots, not one score.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Keep a per-class verdict for ordinary changes and a per-class verdict for crises. A national importance average throws away the live signal.
2. **A union compound, not a tail of one distribution.** Fig 9 :4-5 and Fig 9 :54-58 require the split. The overlap, ordinary fragility that becomes a crisis under credit stress, is a specific compound with its own loop and a signed cycle (Fig 12 :121). It is not a harder case of either class.
3. **Two currencies that do not add.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Frequency prices one thing, consequence and persistence price another. "Crises matter more" is a goal statement until a currency is declared (Operators table :51).
4. **The number is a gap, reported as a verdict.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* The figures return a shape, not a name and not a number. The numeric weight is unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle, because the field has nameable anchors.
5. **The anchor is the outcome, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29-30 defines the anchor as what settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. National accounts, crisis-dating datasets, credit spreads and persistence studies are anchors. Expert consensus is not (Fig 2 :35, :40).
6. **Importance is a budget filter.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* An importance ranking decides what gets tried first. It is never evidence for the truth of a claim.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that crises are just the fat tail of ordinary fluctuations. That is a world hypothesis (Taleb), not a figure verdict, and it fails Fig 9's single-question test.
- Not that ordinary changes are unimportant because they are frequent. Frequency is a filter, and the two currencies do not add.
- Not that one general model ranks the two classes. The union needs two loops and a compound.
- Not a derivation of the crisis generator from the six-term substrate. No seventh atom was invented.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no stable numeric weight was obtained.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that cannot distinguish cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"* and that the discriminator was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The relevant questions:

- Does a single distribution reproduce both the frequency of ordinary changes and the depth and persistence of crisis losses, or is a residual left that persists and does not trend down?
- Are crisis and non-crisis recessions distinguishable in depth and persistence conditional on the same shock? (Cerra and Saxena; Jordà, Schularick and Taylor, already point one way, but their agreement is one evidence pool.)
- Can a currency be declared that prices frequency and consequence without loss? If yes, the two-currency claim fails.
- Does the cross-scale round trip preserve the per-slot signal, or does the firm-level story lose what matters at the systemic level? Measure R_T, do not assume it is zero (Fig 4 :30).
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3: single-process reproduction, indistinguishable depth and persistence, a common currency, preservation of the live signal under aggregation, absence of the overlap compound, or a stable out-of-sample numeric ranking.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four domains in `01` are a visible choice of axes, not nature's own list. *They're Scanning Books When They Should* says *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how important financial crises are relative to ordinary changes in spending and production. It says the question is malformed as a single comparison. The two classes are separate slots, and aggregating them is how a live signal disappears into an average, so the answer must be two per-slot verdicts kept apart. The disagreement persists because each rule works on a different subset, which makes this a union compound: ordinary fluctuations, crises, and a specific overlap where credit stress turns ordinary fragility into a systemic event. Frequency and consequence are two currencies that do not add, so a ranking needs a declared currency before it can even be stated. The numeric weight is a gap, not a silence, and it is owed to a ground reading: national accounts, crisis-dating datasets, credit spreads and persistence studies. Importance is a budget filter for what gets tried first, never a witness for what is true. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop until the ground pays.
