## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: two classes, one union compound, per-slot verdicts, no aggregate importance number without a declared currency, numeric weight owed to the ground. The number itself is not the shape; it is the gap the shape points at.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the bundling failure directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay-level companion to Fig 9 :54-58 and is the sharpest canonical support for the union split in this solution.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual was stated as a comparison of two event classes and their consequences, not as a dispute between named economic schools.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name, and what licenses a number

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument rule: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* A reading of aggregate variance cannot say whether the source was credit-driven crisis dynamics or ordinary demand fluctuation. The essay adds: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* So the numeric importance weight is owed to an instrument, not to a further argument or a vote among economists.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the primitive point: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the four domains in `01` are a visible choice of axes, not a discovery of nature's own list. That choice is recorded, not hidden.

## 4. The specific forbidden error here: a number named from a pool

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the valence rule that governs the crisis/ordinary comparison: *"the same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another"* and *"A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them. Figure 1: 'Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.'"* Low ordinary volatility is good for a short-horizon performance residual and bad for a systemic-fragility residual. There is no context-free importance weight to read off.

The same essay on correlation: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* The three long-run macro-finance programs used as world sources share authors and datasets, so they are one pool for those parts, not three witnesses. Their agreement cannot supply the number.

## 5. Q: what would make the shape refusable?

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **A ground reading.** A measurement that returns a stable numeric weight across anchors and holds out of sample, or one that shows crisis and ordinary recessions have the same depth and persistence conditional on the same shock. Either closes or breaks the gap.
2. **A currency that prices both classes.** If frequency and consequence become commensurable without loss, the "two currencies" claim (Fig 12 :125-127) fails and the classes can be ranked on one scale.

Until one of these, the rank is a goal statement (Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*), not a measurement. The shape itself is refusable by the six conditions in `01` section 3, written before the conclusion.

## 6. Correction to my own first reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read the canonical phrase as forbidding the naming of any economic term in the answer: credit, leverage, output, crisis. That was too strong. The README's full phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*; the subject is a primitive not held in the library. Credit, leverage, output and dated crisis episodes are terms already held in the economics frame. Naming them is a binding from that frame, provenanced as such, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh substrate term called "crisis" or "importance" and claiming the framework derived it (Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*).

The correction does not weaken the main claim. The number is still a gap, because no held term in either frame prices both frequency and consequence. Both readings are kept visible: the over-strong one as retracted, the narrowed one as operative.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering famous economists to agree that crises matter more is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground, an instrument or a held-out dataset settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture essay adds the warning: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the economic claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. The canonical limit, stated plainly

*Arrival of the Fittest* (the architecture essay) says: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So this solution offers a shape, a refuter and an honest stop. It does not offer the last word on how important financial crises are. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The record of the route is what keeps the result checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the same limit as *"indiscernibility is not identity"*: a shape that fits every known case is still a projection until the ground pays.
