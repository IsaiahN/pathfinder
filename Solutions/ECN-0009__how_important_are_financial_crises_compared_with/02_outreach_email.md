# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two-stage order below is understood. The stage-1 claim must survive validation or the offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about comparing financial crises with ordinary fluctuations

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** The question "how important are financial crises compared with ordinary changes in spending and production" asks for one importance number over two classes of event. The two classes are not interchangeable: one is rare, large and systemic, the other frequent, small and diffuse. The output I want checked is a claim about the *form* of the answer, not a number.
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on the form, `specified` on the union finding, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. The two classes are separate slots. A residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated, and aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average. So keep two verdicts and do not average them into a national importance score.
> 2. The disagreement is a union compound, not one hard question. Each rule works well on a different subset of episodes, so the right move is to split before searching. The overlap, ordinary fragility that becomes a crisis under credit stress, is a specific compound with its own loop.
> 3. Frequency and consequence are two currencies that do not add. "Crises matter more" is a goal statement until a currency is declared that prices both.
> 4. The numeric weight is a gap at this budget, not a silence and not a consensus estimate. It is owed to a ground reading: national accounts, crisis-dating datasets, credit spreads and persistence studies.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that financial crises are simply the fat tail of ordinary fluctuations; that one general equilibrium model ranks the two; that experts should be selected for likely agreement; or that I have measured the relative weight. I have not.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim. I wrote these down before sending this:**
>
> 1. A single generating process with a single distribution reproduces both the frequency of ordinary changes and the depth and persistence of crisis losses, with no residual that persists and does not trend down.
> 2. Crisis and non-crisis recessions have statistically indistinguishable depth and persistence once conditioned on the same shock.
> 3. A declared common currency prices both frequency and consequence so the two classes collapse to one scalar without loss.
> 4. Aggregating across slots preserves the live crisis signal rather than erasing it.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is far more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
>
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README, canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The Tether architecture and the formula: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the formula on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the four-part shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links and note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (Fig 4 :22; `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending is yours to do. Prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
