## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** importance is not one number. It is a per-galaxy, union-structured attribution whose channel ranking flips with the residual, embedded in an enumerated gas habitat, and the field's own sources corroborate the union and the mass dependence. A single cosmic importance coefficient is refused (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :55, Fig 11 :49). The numerical magnitude is a declared `gap` at the current instrument budget (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134), characterized by the missing source-discriminating instrument (Fig 6 :32) and not upgraded to `grounded`. Verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged. Mode `specified`. World sources corroborate the shape after the mass-dependence conflict was returned to the census; they cannot decide it.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected instruments. A researcher who believes in one universal dominant channel will optimise for the wrong halo mass, spend telescope time on a scaling relation that cannot discriminate sources, and treat the union's interaction term as noise. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43, quoted as a figure line). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a single importance coefficient that predicts removal after slot stratification, (2) zero interaction residual across halo masses, (3) invariant channel ranking across a change of residual, (4) a direct-contact-only census matching a cascaded one, or (5) a source-discriminating observable with demonstrated resolution, which would close the gap and require an update to `grounded` rather than a defence of the current shape.
