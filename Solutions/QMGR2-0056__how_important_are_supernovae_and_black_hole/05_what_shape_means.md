## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Not a shape:** "supernovae are 60 percent responsible."
- **A shape:** *gas removal is indexed per galaxy, is a union of two overlapping channels whose ranking flips with the residual, and attribution is owed a source-discriminating instrument that is not yet held.*

The first is a name where the framework returns a constraint. The second is what can be stated before any single number is licensed.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and Fig 9 :31: *"rather than the cause"* (both quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks what a failure would look like before sending the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5, *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A measurement that discriminates SN from AGN as the source of an outflow would name the channel.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They’re Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human’s careful guess at reality’s compression axes; another sensorium’s minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

So "SN feedback" and "AGN feedback" are bindings from the astrophysics frame onto channels already admitted in the census. Naming one as the answer is a choice made visible, not a discovery of nature's own ranking. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*

## 4. Why the attribution gap is the central error the framework forbids naming around

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

An outflow spectrum is the green reading. Supernovae, AGN winds, radio jets and cosmic rays can all produce it. Buchner (2024) says the same in the field's words: *"It is observationally difficult to distinguish the proposed feedback mechanisms and establishing causality for most systems."* Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a preference.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q answered

**What licenses a particular channel as the dominant one?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape: per-slot indexing, union, valence flip, instrument gap. A particular channel is licensed only by a ground that discriminates sources, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the literature commit the error the README forbids?** Often, yes. When a scaling relation between AGN luminosity and outflow velocity is read as "AGN causes the outflow", the source is named from a projection that several channels can produce. The correct form keeps the shape and marks the binding as `specified`, ungrounded.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if the framework forbade naming SN or AGN at all. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, here the missing source-discriminating instrument, not the channels already in the census. SN and AGN are already admitted domains D1 and D2 with nameable anchors. Naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is calling that binding a derivation, or treating it as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :7, *"mint moves only in here"*; an import adds an atom, this adds none).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the source of an outflow reading is still named without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held channels. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous feedback researchers to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, a survey, a simulation with a discriminating observable, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
