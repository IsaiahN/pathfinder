## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *existence* of supernova (SN) or active galactic nucleus (AGN) driven outflows (both are observed) but the *attributable share of gas removal* in each galaxy-slot. In effect terms: a galaxy's library Γ predicts its own gas reservoir and outflow given feedback actions a; the observed gas mass, star-formation rate (SFR), metallicity and molecular outflow rate are o′; the residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per galaxy-slot, indexed by halo mass, redshift and gas geometry, never averaged into a single cosmic "importance" number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "one channel is universally dominant": leads to the prediction that a single importance coefficient transfers across all galaxy-slots.
- Option B, "importance is a slot-relative union of two overlapping channels": leads to the prediction that the attributable share changes with the residual (halo mass, redshift, gas distribution) and that the two channels couple non-additively (Fig 11 :49, Fig 9 :55).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape** naming (i) the per-slot conditions under which each channel dominates, (ii) the union structure of their overlap, and (iii) an instrument that would discriminate the source of an observed outflow. Until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. The numerical magnitude of "how important" is declared a `gap` (unreachable at this instrument budget), not a hidden answer and not declared underivable in principle.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the astrophysical question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of galactic feedback.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names. Most published feedback reviews draw on the same survey and simulation pool, so their agreement is one witness.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
