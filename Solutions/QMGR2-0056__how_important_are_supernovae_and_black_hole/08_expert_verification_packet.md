# Expert verification packet - the Tether on supernovae, black holes and gas removal

**What this is:** a claim in a form a galactic-feedback researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No source-discriminating measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "how important are supernovae and black-hole activity in pushing gas out of galaxies?" does not have one transferable answer. The residual, the gap between predicted and observed gas state, is indexed per galaxy by halo mass, redshift and gas geometry, not averaged (Fig 1 :19). Supernova and AGN feedback are a union of overlapping conditions, not two independent causes to be weighted (Fig 9 :55): SN changes metal cooling and inflow, inflow feeds the SMBH, and AGN entrains the SN fountain. The ranking of the two channels flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49), which is why stellar feedback dominates the low-mass end and AGN feedback the high-mass end. The habitat must be enumerated out to the circumgalactic medium (Fig 11 :43). A single importance coefficient is therefore a category error, and the outstanding need is an instrument that discriminates the source of an observed outflow. The figures return that shape and its refuters; they return no number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 11

- **Statement:** importance is slot-relative and union-structured; no single coefficient transfers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- **What to check:** are the three lines present in the figure text, and do they together rule out a weighted-sum model of two independent feedback channels?
- **Refuter:** a single importance coefficient that predicts gas removal after stratifying galaxies by halo mass, redshift and gas geometry; or an invariant channel ranking across a documented change of residual.

### C2 - astrophysics-facing; checkable against the feedback literature

- **Statement:** SN and AGN couple non-additively and their ranking changes with halo mass.
- **What to check:** Biernacki and Teyssier (2018, arXiv:1712.02794) report *"a non-linear coupling"* when both feedback modes act together and that in massive haloes *"the escape velocity is too high for SN feedback to play a significant role ... while AGN feedback is believed to take over."* Heckman and Best (2014) state *"Stellar feedback is generic in these objects, and strong AGN feedback is seen only in the most powerful AGNs."* Buchner (2024) asks the *"absolute and relative importance"* of the channels while noting it is *"observationally difficult to distinguish the proposed feedback mechanisms and establishing causality for most systems."* Are those characterisations faithful?
- **Refuter:** a dataset in which removing one channel reduces total gas removal by exactly its independent contribution with zero interaction residual across halo masses; or a measured invariant ranking across a halo-mass change.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; the instrument gap

- **Statement:** attribution needs a source-discriminating instrument that is not yet held; the numerical magnitude is a `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 - *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- **What to check:** is the gap characterised in effect terms (a discriminator with known resolution across halo mass) rather than named as a preferred channel? Is the distinction between unreachable-at-budget and underivable-in-principle preserved (Fig 8 :40)?
- **Refuter:** a single observable that uniquely assigns an outflow to SN or AGN across halo masses, with the discriminating resolution demonstrated. If it fires, the numerical verdict becomes reachable and the solution is updated, not quietly defended.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a cosmic importance percentage, and not a winner between the two channels.
- Not that either channel is unimportant under all conditions.
- Not a derivation of the mass dependence. That is world corroboration, ranged against the ground separately.
- Not a claim that the two channels are isomers as usually written. They are members of a functional group (Fig 12 :124); the isomer reading requires the full shared atom set and carries its own falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a discriminating measurement.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of galactic feedback.

## 5. What would settle it

1. Does an observable uniquely assign an outflow to SN or AGN across halo masses, with demonstrated resolution? Closing this closes the gap.
2. Does total gas removal show a non-additive interaction term when both channels are active?
3. Does the channel ranking invert across a documented change of halo mass or gas geometry?
4. Does an enumerated circumgalactic census outperform a direct-contact-only census at predicting removal?
