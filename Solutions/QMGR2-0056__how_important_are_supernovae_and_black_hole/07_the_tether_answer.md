# The Tether's answer on supernovae, black holes and gas removal

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Both channels are observed. What fails to resolve is the attribution: given an outflow, what share of the gas removal is due to supernovae and what share to black-hole activity? The residual is indexed per galaxy, by halo mass, redshift and gas geometry (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). It is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55), and the anchor is the measured gas reservoir and baryon inventory that settle whether gas was actually removed (Fig 2 :29), not the outflow reading itself.

## 2. The shape

1. **There is no single cosmic importance coefficient.** Fig 1 :19 forbids the aggregation; Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Any answer must be a function of the slot.
2. **The two channels form a union, not a weighted list.** SN and AGN overlap: SN changes metal cooling, cooling changes inflow, inflow feeds the SMBH, and AGN entrains the SN fountain. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever leaves the intersection.
3. **Their ranking flips with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The same supernovae clear a dwarf and fail in a massive halo; strong AGN feedback appears only in the most powerful systems. This is corroborated by Biernacki and Teyssier (2018) and Heckman and Best (2014), field sources with zero verdict weight until they are ranged against the ground.
4. **The habitat must be enumerated.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Contact cascades outward through the circumgalactic medium and inflow; a first-order "explosion hits gas" story is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :27).
5. **Attribution is owed an instrument.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The outflow spectrum is a metameric reading; several sources produce it. Buchner (2024): *"It is observationally difficult to distinguish the proposed feedback mechanisms and establishing causality for most systems."* The missing thing is a source discriminator, characterised in effect terms, not a preferred channel.
6. **The numerical magnitude is a declared `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* where the unreachability is at the current instrument budget, not a claim of underivability in principle (Fig 8 :40).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that supernovae are always dominant, and not that black-hole activity always wins.
- Not that either channel is unimportant. Both are observed to drive gas out under different conditions.
- Not a number. No fraction such as "60 percent supernovae" is figure-derived.
- Not that the framework has discovered the mass dependence. That is world corroboration, not a figure line.
- Not `grounded`. No discriminating measurement of channel attribution is claimed.
- Not a claim that the channels are isomers as usually written. They are members of a functional group (Fig 12 :124); the isomer reading needs the full shared atom set and has a falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further scaling relation. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the questions are:

- Is there an observable that uniquely assigns a given outflow to SN or AGN across halo masses, with demonstrated resolution rather than asserted sensitivity? Closing this closes the `gap` and turns the verdict from `specified` to `grounded`.
- Does total gas removal show an interaction term when both channels are active, as Biernacki and Teyssier (2018) report, or is it additive? This tests the union shape.
- Does the channel ranking invert across a documented change of halo mass or gas geometry? This tests the valence rule.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3: a transferable importance coefficient; zero interaction residual; invariant ranking; direct-contact-only census as good as cascaded enumeration; a working discriminator (which would update rather than refute the shape).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is characterised: a source discriminator with known resolution across halo mass. It is not named, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). The remedy order is SEARCHED first, then INWARD (extend an instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import a frame with a differing closure); importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :52).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a cosmic percentage for supernovae versus black holes, and it does not pick a winner. It says the attribution has to be made per galaxy, because averaging across slots erases the signal; that the two channels are a union of overlapping conditions, so removing one leaves a coupled intersection standing; that their ranking flips with the residual, which is why supernovae matter in dwarfs and AGN activity in massive haloes; and that the gas habitat has to be enumerated out to the circumgalactic medium because contact cascades. What it says is owed, but does not yet hold, is an instrument that can tell from an outflow spectrum which source produced it. The framework returns that shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the route to the missing instrument. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
