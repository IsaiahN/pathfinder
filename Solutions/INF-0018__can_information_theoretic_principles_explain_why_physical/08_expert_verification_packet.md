# Expert verification packet, the Tether on information and physical law

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in information-theoretic physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. **Simulated derivation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Information-theoretic principles constrain the *shape* of physical law as the price rule for admitting descriptions and as a gated import between frames; entropy-type measures are compositions out of a six-term substrate and are therefore downstream of it, not a seventh primitive. Because a frame cannot certify its own metaframe, the explanatory "why" of law-form cannot be settled from inside physics by information theory alone; it is owed to a ground. The two effects, description and ground, form a union and keep separate verdicts. Three falsifiers are written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 5, 1 and 13

- **Statement:** information theory occupies the price role inside the framework, and entropy measures are downstream of a six-term substrate, not primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :45, *"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."* Fig 1 :27, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 13 :116, *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."*
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six terms from an information measure; or exhibit a seventh member that survives the indivisibility and minimality tests of Fig 13; or show the framework's admission test is not MDL-shaped.

### C2, framework/metaframe; checkable by anyone who knows Gödel/Tarski/Chaitin

- **Statement:** a physical frame cannot derive its own law-form from an information principle inside its own closure; that would be self-certification.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :34, *"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency."* Fig 8 :37, *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."* Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a formal derivation, inside a physical theory, of that theory's own law-form from an information principle, with no import and no external validator.

### C3, cross-frame; checkable against the entropic-gravity and max-entropy literature

- **Statement:** an information principle can travel between the information frame and the physics frame as a gated import, with a source gate and a shadow test, but it adds no physical atom and does not by itself settle the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :25, *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 8 :45, *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :50, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 9 :42, *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."*
- **What to check:** does Verlinde's derivation (arXiv:1001.0785) carry a source gate and a shadow test, and does it add a physical primitive? Does Hardy's axiom reconstruction (arXiv:quant-ph/0101012) derive law-form *after* axioms are imported rather than from nothing?
- **Refuter:** a source-gated, shadow-tested import from the information frame that demonstrably adds an atom to the physical world, or a measurement in which law-form varies with an information parameter the six-term substrate cannot compose.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that information is a seventh primitive, nor that physics is "just computation."
- Not that Wheeler's *"all things physical are information-theoretic in origin"* is established; it is a programmatic source claim without a source-discriminating instrument.
- Not that Verlinde's entropic gravity or Jaynes's maximum entropy is true; they are contested world-side instances of the description effect.
- Not that the framework's entropy-like composition is identical to thermodynamic entropy. That is a live strain, not an identity claim.
- Not a theory of everything; no last room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
