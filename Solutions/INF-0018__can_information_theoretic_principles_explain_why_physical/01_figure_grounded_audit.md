## 0. The question restated as an effect

The question *"Can information-theoretic principles explain why physical laws take their observed form?"* is not one question. It bundles three effects:

1. **A description effect.** Information-theoretic quantities (entropy, description length, channel capacity) successfully describe physical law, especially thermodynamics and statistical mechanics. This is observed and not in dispute.
2. **A derivation effect.** A principle from information theory could generate the *form* of a physical law, so the law follows from the principle rather than the principle merely redescribing it.
3. **A ground effect.** Something settles *why* the law has this form and not another. The word "why" in the question asks for this third effect.

If option A ("yes, information explains law") is asserted without separating these, it promotes a frame-internal description to a verdict. If option B ("no, information has nothing to do with law") is asserted, it ignores the observed description effect. The residual R is the gap between (1) and (3): a successful description is being asked to carry a ground it does not hold.

**Bound ordinary term (Formula step "Direction").** An answer will be written as a placement: *an information principle occupies the role of price / import / ground in a named frame, and a figure line governs that placement.* This shape is bound now, so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8 (quote first, conclude second)

### F1, Information theory is already inside the framework, as the acceptance price (decisive for D2)

Fig 5 :45, verbatim:

> **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."**

And Fig 5 :26: *"and then: does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"*

The framework's own test for admitting a term is a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and **Shannon coding**. Information theory is not an outside candidate waiting to be imported into physics; it is the *price rule* the framework already uses to decide whether any term is worth having. Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**

Consequence, stated as a shape: an information-theoretic principle can constrain the *description* of a law (the bits side), and it cannot by itself pay the *action* side (settling the law against the ground). Promoting the bits price to the ground is exactly the error Fig 1 :27 names:

> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

**Per domain:** D2, triangulated (Fig 5 :45 and Fig 1 :27 and Fig 12 :126 are three low-overlap routes: the price rule, the disqualification rule, the two-currency rule). Mode `specified`. Refuter: show a derivation in which the MDL/Shannon price is *not* frame-internal to the system applying it, or the framework's admission test is not MDL-shaped.

### F2, A frame cannot be its own ground (decisive for D3)

Fig 8 :34 and :37, verbatim:

> **"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency."**
> **"A frame cannot certify its own limit."**

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**

Physics, expressed in information-theoretic terms, is still a frame with a closure. To claim from inside physics that information theory is *the* explanation of law-form is to have physics certify its own metaframe. The figures forbid that move categorically, for any frame at any level. The honest report is Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**, not a gap and not a refusal to answer.

**Per domain:** D3, triangulated (Fig 8 :34/:37 plus Fig 9 :44 plus Fig 13 :134). Mode `specified` structural. Refuter: exhibit a formal derivation, inside a physical theory, of that theory's own law-form from an information principle, with no import and no external validator.

### F3, Information is downstream of the six-term substrate, not upstream (decisive for D1)

Fig 13 :49-50, verbatim:

> **"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard. Each member is load-bearing in at least one of them, and the continuum in none."**

The framework derives thermodynamics from the six-term set, with entropy's dependencies named per member: network supplies *"no relation between systems, and no configurations to count"* (:59), energy supplies *"finiteness in a finite region is what makes the counting terminate"* (:68), gradient supplies *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"* (:71). Counting configurations, finiteness and direction are the ingredients of an entropy or information measure. On this reading, information is a **composition of the substrate**, not its source.

And Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."** Fig 13 :118: **"infinite effort inside a finite reach."** If someone proposes "information" as the primitive that explains the six, they are proposing a seventh kind, which composition cannot produce and which the framework excludes at every level.

**Per domain:** D1, triangulated (Fig 13 :49-71 and :116-118, with the essay's Rule 1 at low overlap: *"You can never compose within the same kind. They must alternate."*). Mode `specified`. Refuter: derive one of the six from information measures, or add a seventh member that survives the indivisibility and minimality tests of Fig 13 :143.

### F5, An import is possible, but it is gated, recorded, and adds no atom (for D2 to D3)

Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (import). Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :25, verbatim:

> **"An import adds no atom to the world."**

Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** But Fig 8 :50: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. **Per domain:** D2 to D3, triangulated (Fig 6 :14/:21/:25 plus Fig 8 :45/:50). Mode `specified`. Refuter: show an import that adds a real atom to the physical world, or that needs no source gate and no shadow test.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"*, the shape claim of this audit is: **information-theoretic principles occupy the role of price and gated import, are downstream of the six-term substrate, and cannot be the ground that settles why law has its form.** That claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Primitive refutation.** A derivation shows that an information measure is a seventh primitive not derivable from the six terms, and that law-form derives from it inside physics closure. Breaks Fig 13 :116-118 and F3.
2. **Ground refutation.** A physical measurement shows law-form varying with an information-theoretic parameter in a way the six-term substrate cannot compose, so the information frame pays as an anchor. Breaks Fig 1 :23 and F2.
3. **Import refutation.** A source-gated, shadow-tested import from the information frame demonstrably adds an atom to the physical world. Breaks Fig 6 :25 and F5.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this package may be labelled `grounded`. These refuters are not rhetorical: each maps to a specific verbatim line above.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there a fact here?** Yes, but two: a description effect and a ground effect. Fig 9 :54 says split (F4).
2. **Is information a primitive?** No. Fig 13 :116 and the essay's Rule 1 (F3).
3. **Where does information sit in the framework?** In the admission price: Fig 5 :45 MDL = Kolmogorov + Shannon (F1).
4. **Can the price be the ground?** No. Fig 1 :27 disqualifies a frame scoring itself with what it produces (F1).

7. **What settles the "why"?** The ground, not agreement: Fig 9 :42, Fig 7 :35. The answer stops at a shape.
