# Outreach draft, check the shape first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its three refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are written down and checked. The output must survive stage 1 or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about information and physical law, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Information-theoretic principles can constrain the *shape* of a physical law, but they cannot be the ground that settles *why* the law has that form. In the framework I am using, Shannon coding and Kolmogorov complexity appear as the **price rule** for admitting any term at all, and entropy-type quantities compose out of a six-term physical substrate (space, time, energy; spectrum, gradient, network). They are downstream of the substrate, not upstream of it. A physical frame cannot certify its own metaframe, so an information-theoretic derivation of law-form from inside physics would be the frame certifying itself. What I am offering is that shape, with its refuters, not a new unified theory.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not saying information is a new fundamental primitive, that physics is "just computation", that a code exists behind reality, or that this settles the interpretation of quantum mechanics. Those are bindings onto held terms, not derivations. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Where an information principle crosses between frames it is a gated import with a source and a shadow test, and it adds no atom to the world. Until that provenance is shown it carries zero evidentiary weight.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before contacting anyone:
> 1. A derivation showing that an information measure is a seventh primitive not derivable from the six terms, and that law-form derives from it inside physics closure.
> 2. A measurement showing law-form varying with an information-theoretic parameter in a way the six-term substrate cannot compose, so the information frame actually pays as an anchor.
> 3. A source-gated, shadow-tested import from the information frame that demonstrably adds an atom to the physical world.
>
> If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The 13 figures, Operators and Symbols tables, and the formula (public repository): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule for how the figures may be cited: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked derivation of the six-term substrate and its five rules: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and its author warns that outputs could be contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
