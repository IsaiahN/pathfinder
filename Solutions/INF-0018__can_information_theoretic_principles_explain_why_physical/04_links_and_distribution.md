## 4. Distribution rules

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the shape claim and the refuter. Do not send a recording of a conclusion before anyone has described a residual.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**, agreement from a shared pool is not corroboration. Do not pool later agreement as a witness.
- **Seat Map :163**, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their mode.
