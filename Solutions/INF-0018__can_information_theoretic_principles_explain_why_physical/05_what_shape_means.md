## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**, and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the ground. For INF-0018 the shape is: *an information principle can price a law and can travel between frames as a gated import, but the "why" of law-form is owed to a ground and stays open.* `information = the primitive of physics` is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or recur, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** We therefore describe the effect ("what would I observe if information were the ground of law-form?") rather than borrowing the word "information" from a particular program.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks for a failure class before showing the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this question that happens when the information frame and the physics frame, each ranged separately against a ground, converge on the same shape without either certifying the other.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 5 step 5 (formula): *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). In this question that is the route by which Verlinde's entropic-force result or Jaynes's max-entropy inference can bear on the physics frame, each recorded as imported, not derived.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why the field's favourite claim is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a successful information-theoretic description of thermodynamics is a reading of a projection. Naming information as the *source* of the law-form from that reading is the metamerism error. Several different grounds could yield the same entropy reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. Wheeler's *"all things physical are information-theoretic in origin"* is exactly this kind of source claim, offered without such an instrument.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* closes the loop: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The specific answer to "what shape does this question permit?"

**Shape permitted:** information-theoretic principles place as the admission price inside a frame and as a gated import across frames; the explanatory "why" of law-form stays a residual owed to the ground. The relationship between the information frame and the physics frame is a **union of overlapping conditions**, so it must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54).

**Names refused, with the reason attached:**

- "Information is the primitive from which the six terms derive", refused, Fig 13 :116 and the essay's Rule 1.
- "Physics is computation" / "it from bit" as a settled result, refused as a verdict; retained as a programmatic claim that needs a ground.
- "Entropy IS the framework's ordering/configuration/finiteness composition", held as a strain, not an identity (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8). Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of §3

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade using any information concept in an explanation. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Shannon coding and Kolmogorov complexity are already held inside the framework's admission test (Fig 5 :45), so using them is a price rule, not an invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is promoting that price to the ground and claiming a new primitive. Both the correction and the original claim are kept visible here: the first was about what the framework can *say*, the second is about what it can *settle*.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous information-physics names assembled to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question. Every file says so in its mode line; no file claims otherwise.
