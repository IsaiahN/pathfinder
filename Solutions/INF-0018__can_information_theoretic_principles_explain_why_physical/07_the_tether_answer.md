# The Tether's answer: can information-theoretic principles explain physical law-form?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles a description effect and a ground effect. We can describe physical law with information-theoretic quantities; the question asks whether that description explains *why* the law has its form. Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** The two effects are a **union compound** and must have separate verdicts, never an average.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**Domain 1, framework price (triangulated).** Fig 5 :45: **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."** Information theory is already inside the framework as the rule that prices a term. Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** So the price cannot be promoted to the ground of the frame it prices. Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 5, Fig 1, Fig 12).

**Domain 2, substrate and primitives (triangulated).** Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."** Entropy-type measures compose out of the six terms and their cross-kind arrangements, especially counting configurations, finiteness in a finite region and directedness (Fig 13 :49-71). An information measure is downstream of the substrate, not a seventh primitive above it. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 and the derivation record's Rule 1, low overlap).

**Domain 3, the metaframe limit (triangulated).** Fig 8 :34: **"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe. Godel's second theorem: a consistent system cannot prove its own consistency."** Fig 8 :37: **"A frame cannot certify its own limit."** Physics expressed in information-theoretic terms remains a frame; a derivation of its own law-form from inside that frame would be self-certification. Verdict: `specified` structural, triangulated (Fig 8 and Fig 9).

**Domain 4, cross-frame transport (triangulated).** An import is the only operator that moves the wall, but Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** It carries a description, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14, Fig 8 :45-52). A successful information-theoretic derivation of a physical law is a legitimate gated import; it is not the generation of a physical primitive. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8).

**The union's own verdict.** The "why" of law-form is not cleared by any of the above. Fig 9 :42: **"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. So the honest report for the explanatory half of the question is `unreachable`, not `no` and not silence. Verdict: `specified`, `candidate`, `unreached`.

**Per-domain verdicts kept separate:** D1 yes (information is the price); D2 yes (information is derivative of the substrate); D3 yes (no self-grounding); D4 yes (gated import only); union "why" unreached. Nothing is averaged into a single "yes" or "no".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that information is a new fundamental primitive, nor that physics is "just computation".
- Not that a code exists behind reality, and not a resolution of the interpretation of quantum mechanics.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 names *"the limit: no last room"*; no final information-theoretic frame closes all questions.
- Not that Verlinde's entropic gravity or Jaynes's max-entropy inference is *true*. They are world-side corroborations of the description effect, and both are contested.
- Not that the framework's entropy-like composition is identical to thermodynamic entropy. That is a named strain, not an identity claim.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating experiment was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. **Primitive test.** Derive one of the six terms from an information measure, or exhibit a seventh that survives Fig 13's indivisibility test. This would break the derivative verdict.
2. **Ground test.** Measure a law-form varying with an information parameter that the six-term substrate cannot compose, so the information frame actually pays as an anchor. This would break the price-only verdict.
3. **Import test.** Produce a source-gated, shadow-tested import that demonstrably adds an atom to the physical world. This would break the no-added-atom verdict.
4. **Standing refuters** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, written before this conclusion: the same three, stated as falsifiers of the shape.

An agreement among information physicists is none of these. Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Information-theoretic principles do constrain the shape of physical law, but they do not supply the ground that explains why law has that form. In the Tether framework, Shannon coding and Kolmogorov complexity appear as the price rule for admitting any term, and entropy-type measures compose out of a six-term physical substrate, so information is downstream of the substrate rather than a seventh primitive. A physical frame cannot certify its own metaframe, which rules out deriving law-form from inside the frame; an information principle can cross into physics only as a source-gated import that adds no atom to the world. The "why" therefore stays open: it is owed to a source-discriminating instrument, not to agreement among researchers. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, three refuters written in advance, and the exact tests that would close it. Mode `specified`, not `grounded`.
