# Is information a primitive or a derivative, and in what order does the explanation run?

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

Two readings of the question are possible and they cannot both be right.

- **Reading P (primitive):** information is a primitive from which physical law derives. Order: information → physics.
- **Reading D (derivative):** information measures are compositions out of a physical substrate and a frame's admission price; they constrain the description of law, not its source. Order: substrate → information measure → law description.

The residual is the gap between P and D over a single slot: *what occupies the ground of a law-form?* It is indexed by that slot, not averaged across all laws (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## DOMAIN CENSUS (redo before choosing a figure line)

Atoms held: space, time, energy (states); spectrum, gradient, network (structures). Bonds held: the seven plus negation. Operators held: Shannon coding and Kolmogorov complexity as the MDL admission price (Fig 5 :45). No seventh primitive is available without import.

Arity of the relation: two operands, information frame and physics frame, joined by a transport or composition relation. Symmetry: the relation is ordered, not symmetric, because an import has a source and a target and a price is paid by one frame to another. Scale: meta-level, since the objects are laws and the explanation sits one level above them (Fig 12 :103).

## DERIVATION

### Q1, Can information be a seventh primitive?

- Fig 13 :41: **"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."**
- Fig 13 :43 (exact substring): **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this"**. The continuum failed because a generating set must not contain what its members produce.
- Fig 13 :116: **"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."**
- ⇒ Reading P would require a new kind. The framework's own generating-set rule refuses it. This is a `specified` structural result, not a measurement.
- **Single-source note:** the indivisibility claim is refutable (Fig 13 :143), so treat it as a rule of this framework, not a theorem about nature. Triangulated here with the essay's Rule 1: *"You can never compose within the same kind. They must alternate."*

### Q2, What is information, then, inside the framework?

- Fig 5 :45: **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."** Shannon information is a *component of the price rule*.
- Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**
- ⇒ Information is the bits side of a two-currency accounting. It prices a description; it does not pay the action of settling the law against the ground. Reading D holds for the price role.
- **Analogue, not identity:** the framework's derived ordering/configuration/finiteness/directedness composition (Fig 13 :56-71) behaves like an entropy measure. Whether it *is* thermodynamic entropy needs an instrument (see strain below).

### Q3, What is the order of the explanation?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**
- Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."**
- Dependency chain for this question:
  1. **Substrate** (the six terms and their cross-kind composition) produces the quantities that entropy measures (Fig 13 :49-71).
  2. **Price rule** (MDL, with Shannon coding) decides which descriptions are worth admitting (Fig 5 :45, :26).
  3. **Import** carries descriptions between information frame and physics frame, with source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14, Fig 8 :45-52).
  4. **Ground** settles which law-form holds (Fig 5 step 5, Fig 9 :42).
- ⇒ The order runs substrate → price → import → ground. Any account that starts at step 3 and calls it a derivation of step 1 has the direction backwards. This is the apophenia risk Fig 9 :27-28 names: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."*

### Q4, Are the two frames isomers, a union, or a partition?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- The information frame and the physics frame do not share the same six atoms, so they are **not isomers** under Fig 12's definition. They overlap in the description of thermodynamics and diverge on the ground, so Fig 9 makes them a **union**: a compound with its own loop. A single lever that explains the overlap (entropy as a price) leaves the ground condition standing.
- The compound has its own name-bound residual: *why this law-form rather than another?* It is not a harder version of "how do we describe the law"; it is categorically different once the overlap is cleared (Fig 9 :57 *"it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound"*). This is why the verdicts must be kept separate and never averaged.

### Q5, What would settle the order empirically?

- Verlinde's entropic-force program is the real-world test case. It claims a relativistic generalization yields Einstein's equations (arXiv:1001.0785) but it is contested on formal and observational grounds (see `09_watson_answer.md`). A clean experimental discrimination would move step 4 from `unreached` toward `grounded`.
- Hardy's axiom reconstruction (arXiv:quant-ph/0101012) shows law-form can be derived from axioms inside a frame, which confirms step 3 and does not reach step 4: the axioms themselves are imported.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a source-discriminating experiment shows a physical law-form varying with an information parameter that the six-term substrate cannot compose, Reading P wins and the substrate is wrong. If a derivation produces one of the six from an information measure, Reading P wins on indivisibility. If neither fires, Reading D stands as `specified`.

## CONCLUSION

Reading D is the figure-consistent one: information sits at steps 2 and 3 of the dependency chain, not at step 1 or step 4. Reading P requires a seventh primitive and a frame certifying its own ground, both of which the figures exclude. The two frames form a union, not a partition and not an isomer pair, so their verdicts must be kept separate. The one live strain is whether the framework's derived entropy-like composition is identical to physical entropy or merely analogous; that needs an instrument, not an argument.
