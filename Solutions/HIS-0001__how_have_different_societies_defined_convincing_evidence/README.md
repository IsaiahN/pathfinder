## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks for the shape of an explanation of observed variation: communities do not converge on one definition of "convincing evidence" (trial by ordeal, oath, witness testimony, documents, probability thresholds, intime conviction). Option A treats the variation as one hard question with one correct answer still to be found. Option B treats it as a union of several well-formed questions whose standards are conventions anchored in human judgment. A leads to a search that cannot measure its own anchor. B leads to a checkable shape with a stated refuter. The verdict below is B, at `general`/`specified` mode, not `grounded`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **A checker given the interior stops being a checker.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
