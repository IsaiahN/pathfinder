## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the observed variation in standards of convincing evidence plus the request for its explanation. Arity: two-place (reasons E to claim H for a community) plus a second-order cross-community comparison, so a **union compound**, not one hard question. Symmetry: not symmetric; the relation is an ordered threshold comparison. Scale: individual, institution, historical comparison.
- **Domain 1, the fact-finding frame.** Actors: jurors, judges, elders, oracles, parties, witnesses. Currency: what counts as a reason. Anchor: whatever the community treats as final. Question type: partition member, one room per community.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: institutions, case pool, religion, literacy, technology, political stakes. Currency: conventions transmitted. Anchor: the ground filtered through the habitat (not the habitat itself). Question type: union, overlaps D1 and D3.
- **Domain 3, the comparing frame.** Actors: historian and sources. Currency: corroboration and residuals. Anchor: the historical record plus human judgment, legitimate because the question is about people. Question type: union, overlaps both.

Order by dependency: habitat supplies vocabulary, frame applies it, comparator compares. I derived this from Fig 3's dependency chain plus Fig 9's "describe the effect before the cause," while the primary derived it from Fig 4's scale rule plus Fig 3. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`, six features: union, threshold convention, habitat census, updating anchor, lineage, separate verdicts.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete historical claim a reader might expect: "religion caused high thresholds," "the jury system caused reasonable doubt," "society X was more rational."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **four-way structure** collapses if a reader blends admissibility, sufficiency, seat and anchor into one "standard";
- the **updating-anchor consequence** is lost if a reader treats "what people find convincing" as a fixed measuring rod rather than the moving thing being explained;
- the **lineage requirement** is lost if two similar thresholds are read as the same standard rather than as convergence or import.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: variation is located in actors, currency, anchor and habitat at each scale, and the verdicts stay per domain. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
