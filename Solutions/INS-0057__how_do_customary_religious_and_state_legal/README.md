## What an answer would look like (the sought-for shape bound as an ordinary term)

Before deriving, write down what would count as an answer so the preference can accrue a residual like any other term (Formula, Direction). An answer here would say, for a given conduct and a given person: which order's rule actually governs, why that order rather than the other two, what happens when two orders claim the same slot, and what would have to change for the governing order to change. It would also say what "influence" consists of, in effect terms: a rule migrating from one order into another, a convention becoming enforceable, or a frame's closure changing through contact. Anything that merely lists the three orders and asserts "they interact" is not an answer; it is the question restated.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map, failure genera: "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." A list of agreeable pluralism scholars is a room, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map, the laws: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." An outside frame that has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
