## Four findings in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **Overlap is the normal state, not a deviation.** GSDRC: "Legal pluralism produces hybrid or mixed legal environments where state, local and non-state actors are linked and the lines between them are blurred." This is the union census of Fig 9, reached independently in a development-policy source.
2. **Forum choice is actor-level ranging.** Wikipedia, legal pluralism: "individuals would choose to bring their legal claims under the system that they thought would offer them the best advantage." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The source corroborates the shape; it does not confirm the figure.
3. **Transplants are not smooth diffusion.** Teubner's "legal irritation" (1998), as reported in the legal transplant entry: a foreign rule "disrupts established norms and societal arrangements" and is redefined in the receiving context. This is the same shape as Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." It is an independent witness from comparative law.
4. **A declared rule is not an enforced rule.** GSDRC, South Sudan case: "Prohibiting discrimination in customary law can produce a law that looks good on paper, but that is unlikely to be enforced or to change social norms." Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Again, independent corroboration of the figure's distinction.

Overlap warning: items 1 and 4 come from the same GSDRC guide and are one source, not two. Items 2 and 3 come from two different Wikipedia entries that share an encyclopedia evidence pool. They are still independent of the framework author, which is the overlap that matters for this derivation. The two Substack essays share the framework's author and evidence pool, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

## The two-stage offer, with the third stage

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against a ground they know (a court record, an enforcement dataset, an ethnographic case). They check it without holding the framework's interior. This is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the links. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method. Fig 4 :22 permits this because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The empirical ranking is explicitly a `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9, so do not present a named winning order as the output. The output is the shape and its refuter.
