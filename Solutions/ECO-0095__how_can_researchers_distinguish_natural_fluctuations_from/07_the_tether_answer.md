# The Tether's answer on natural fluctuation versus long-term ecosystem change

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the limit. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a discipline, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A monitored indicator produces an observation a stationary-belief model did not predict. The same observation is compatible with "normal variation" and with "early sign of long-term change". The question is which bin of residual this is, and bins imply different acts. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." The answer therefore cannot be produced by agreeing on a statistic; it has to be produced by a procedure that ranges against the ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the bundle first.** Fluctuation, critical transition, step change, noise-induced transition, and linear tracking of a driver are different compounds with overlapping signatures. Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

2. **Read residuals per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A flat ecosystem-level average can coexist with a live signal in one population or one site (Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state").

3. **Commit the expected signal before the run.** Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Fig 5 :63: "Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION". A search for a signal the analyst never wrote down cannot see its own preference.

4. **Buy independence, not volume.** Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Four statistics from one series are one witness. Remote sensing, in situ sampling, Indigenous observation, and a mechanistic experiment are separate frames only where their evidence pools differ.

5. **Maintain the channel.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch." A saturated sensor or a reading pooled across a change produces a loss-of-contact report, not a stability verdict.

6. **Leave the verdict to the ground.** Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Here the ground is the ecosystem's future trajectory, an experiment, or a manipulation. A finite series that shows no trend is not proof of no change: Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

7. **Thresholds are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A p-value, an AIC weight, or a false-alarm rate decides what gets tried next. It never decides what is true.

8. **Carry mechanism knowledge with provenance.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". A mechanism model or a whole-lake experiment is an import from a differing closure; it must carry its source, its date, and a shadow test.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not a named statistic.** No variance, AR(1), skewness, density ratio, spectral ratio, or machine-learning classifier is asserted as the answer. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- **Not a universal method.** The world literature is explicit that "not all systems that show regime shifts exhibit critical slowing down, or vice versa" (Boettiger et al. 2013) and that "no single technique fits all situations" (Thygesen 2025).
- **Not that a non-significant trend means stability.** Fig 8 :39 (above) and Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- **Not that a significant trend means a critical transition.** Gsell et al. 2016: false positives and false negatives occur, and "agreement among the four indicators was low."
- **Not grounded.** No measurement in this derivation settles any ecological claim.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Threshold crossing:** does the ecosystem's own trajectory cross and persist beyond the predicted boundary, or return? That is the ground for "real change" versus "fluctuation".
- **Hysteresis test:** if the driver is relaxed, does the system return along a different path? Gsell et al. 2016 note that single step changes "cannot provide direct evidence of a critical transition" and that the forward and backward thresholds may differ.
- **Source discrimination:** does a mechanism model or a whole-ecosystem manipulation distinguish a bifurcation from a step change, a noise-induced transition, or linear tracking for the specific state variable?
- **Channel audit:** has the monitoring channel lost contact through saturation, pooling, or dropped transients? If so, the correct output is "contact lost", not either of the two original options.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a pooled indicator with no pre-registered shape that works across independent ecosystems; two analyses of the same series shown to corroborate independently; a finite series shown to settle the question without a ground; invariance of detection to channel degradation; a single indicator that discriminates across all union members.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." A short, noisy, coarsely sampled series is a budget problem: the remedy is longer or higher-resolution monitoring, not a new theory. A signal that has never registered at any resolution is a different matter, and the framework stops there and records the lineage. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Indiscernibility is not identity. This answer stops at the limit of the available instruments. It cannot tell a researcher which indicator to use for a named lake or forest; it can tell them what the procedure must look like and what would refute it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name an early warning statistic, and it does not claim that a rising variance or autocorrelation settles anything. It says that "fluctuation versus long-term change" is a bundle of different mechanisms and must be split before it is searched; that residuals must be read per indicator and per population, never pooled into a reassuring average; that what "early change" would look like has to be written down before the run, or the search cannot see its own preference; that agreement among statistics drawn from the same time series is one witness, not several; that the monitoring channel decays and a saturated or pooled reading is a loss of contact, not a verdict of stability; and that only the ecosystem's own future trajectory or an experiment settles the question. Thresholds are filters for what to try next, never witnesses for what is true. What the framework offers is the shape any defensible distinction must have, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where the instruments run out.
