## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about distinguishing natural fluctuations from early signs of long-term ecosystem change. Option A: treat it as one binary classification problem and name a universal early-warning statistic. Option B: treat it as a union of mechanisms and state the shape a defensible distinction must have, with the verdict deferred to the ground. What each leads to: A sends a name where the framework returns a shape, mistakes a threshold for a witness, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable discipline, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop where the instruments run out.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A monitored observation that a stationary-belief model did not predict, compatible with several mechanisms. Arity: at least two conditions (stationary variation, step change, noise-induced transition, approaching bifurcation, linear driver tracking). Symmetry: the operative relation is a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order inside one indicator series, second order at the ecosystem level. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a union compound.
- **Domain 1, observation and monitoring.** Actors: sensors, field samples, remote sensing, Indigenous observation, the indicator series. Currency: observations, sampling interval, resolution. Anchor: the ecosystem's actual trajectory, reachable but unsettled at stage 1. Contributes slots to the union.
- **Domain 2, statistical detection (the analyst's Γ).** Actors: indicators (variance, AR(1), skewness, density ratio), null and surrogate series, rolling windows, thresholds. Currency: residuals, trend significance, error rates, description length. Anchor: the ground, meaning whether the indicator actually predicts a transition for this system. Union with domain 3.
- **Domain 3, mechanism and causal frame.** Actors: drivers, feedback loops, alternative stable states, hysteresis, trophic cascades. Currency: causal explanation and provenance. Anchor: whole-ecosystem experiment, manipulation, mechanistic model. This is the differing closure that supplies imports.
- **Domain 4, verification and decision.** Actors: managers, reviewers, outside readers, decision thresholds. Currency: agreement, residual, cost of missed versus false alarm. Anchor: the ground, never agreement.
- **Not a domain yet:** "the universal early warning signal." No nameable anchor exists; four world sources report mechanism dependence and low agreement.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

- **Freshwater ecology / predictive ecology.** O'Brien et al., "Early warning signals have limited applicability to empirical lake data", Nature Communications 14:7942, 2023, opened 2026-11-23: "We find few instances of critical transitions, with different trophic levels often expressing different forms of abrupt change." - **Theoretical ecology / mathematics.** Boettiger, Ross and Hastings, "Early warning signals: the charted and uncharted territories", Theoretical Ecology 6:255-264, 2013, opened 2026-11-23: "not all systems that show regime shifts exhibit critical slowing down, or vice versa. - **Ecology / decision theory commentary.** Thygesen, "Predicting regime shifts and beyond", PNAS 122(29) e2513605122, 2025, public record via search 2026-11-23: "Thresholds for raising alarms involve a trade-off between missed and false alarms" and "no single technique fits all situations." ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; every offered universal statistic is refused as a name. The distinction is a union of mechanisms and must be split before search. Residuals are read per slot, never pooled. The expected mechanism-specific signal is written down before the run. Corroboration requires low evidence overlap, not more of the same series. The monitoring channel is maintained and its failure reported as loss of contact. The verdict belongs to the ecosystem's future trajectory or to an experiment, not to a threshold or to agreement; and absence of a detected trend is not proof of no change. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is the union/split requirement; the strongest world corroboration is the low agreement among indicators and the mechanism dependence reported by Gsell et al. 2016 and O'Brien et al. 2023.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the discipline is followed. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded success instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Operationally, the cost of a wrong call is asymmetric: a missed alarm can lose the window to avert a regime shift, a false alarm spends management resources. Thygesen 2025 names that as the alarm trade-off. Both costs are avoidable only by a ground check, not by tuning a threshold.
- **undoable:** yes, methodologically. The five-part discipline (split, per-slot, pre-register, low overlap, maintain channel) can be added to any monitoring program without deleting existing data, and a false alarm can be superseded by continued observation. The ecological outcome itself may not be undoable: some regime shifts show hysteresis, and Fig 11 :63 "a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state". A missed shift in the world is not recovered by a corrected report.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A trajectory that crosses and persists beyond the predicted boundary, or a relaxation test showing hysteresis, would settle a real change; a return to the prior state over a long, well-resolved series would settle a fluctuation for that interval. A source-discriminating experiment distinguishing a bifurcation from a step change for the named state variable would name the mechanism. A pooled indicator with no pre-registered shape that works across independent ecosystems, or a finite-series rule with a correctness proof, would break the shape claim. A short series is a budget problem (more search), not a reason to import a new atom (Fig 8 :40-41). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
