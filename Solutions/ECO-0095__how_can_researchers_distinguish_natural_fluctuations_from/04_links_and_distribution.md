## Four things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Agreement among indicators is not corroboration.** Gsell et al. 2016 and O'Brien et al. 2023 independently report low agreement among early-warning indicators on the same systems. In figure terms that is Fig 2 :40 ("agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"), not a reason to average them.
2. **The same reading has several sources.** Boettiger et al. 2013 show that regime shifts need not exhibit critical slowing down and vice versa; O'Brien et al. 2023 show different trophic levels in one lake can undergo different forms of change. That is the metamerism case of *The Hard Problem is About Source* applied to a time series, and it is why the source essay's remedy is a better instrument rather than a better argument.
3. **Preprocessing chooses the answer.** O'Brien et al. 2023: "Each EWS method was strongly influenced by the data pre-processing performed with no single indicator being reliable and no coherence across indicators." That is Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" arriving through the data-preparation channel.
4. **The alarm threshold is a cost decision, not a discovery.** Thygesen 2025: "Thresholds for raising alarms involve a trade-off between missed and false alarms." That is Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." in operational clothing.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the five-part shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".** Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Do not ask for agreement. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
- **Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."** A mass send selected for people already sympathetic produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Stage 3 offers it only after stage 1 is recorded.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter, and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the stage-1 verdict; (4) only if curious, the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) only on request, the formula as a method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). But each user still needs a ground and a pre-registered refuter, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** the shape claim must survive the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. As written it is internally consistent with the verbatim figures, but it has not been checked against an ecological ground. That check is what stage 1 is for.
