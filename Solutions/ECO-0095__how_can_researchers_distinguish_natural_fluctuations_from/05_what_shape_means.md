## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

*The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECO-0095 the shape is therefore **not** a statistic. It is the narrowed form of a method you can state before you know which indicator will work: read residuals per slot, commit the expected signal in advance, require low-overlap corroboration, keep the channel to the ground reachable, and let the ground decide. A named universal test (variance alone, AR(1) alone, EWSNet) is a name where the framework returns a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity and recursive narrowing

*The Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." It also names the failure mode this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

So the ecological residual is described in effect terms: *an observation that a stationary model did not predict, compatible with several mechanisms*, rather than in the borrowed vocabulary of one statistical school. Only then is a frame chosen. F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is this move applied to the bundle.

## 3. Why the pattern is not the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism with metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Its general statement is: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The resolution is not a better argument but a better discriminator: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

For ecological time series, rising variance and AR(1) are a reading, not a source. A bifurcation, a step change in a driver, a seasonal artifact, and a noise-induced transition can produce the same reading (Boettiger, Ross and Hastings 2013; O'Brien et al. 2023). Naming "critical slowing down" as the cause of a particular rising variance is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which here means a mechanism model, an experiment, or a longer and better-resolved series. The framework's rule is instrumental: Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34-35 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

## 4. Why the expected signal must be written down before the run

Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Fig 5 :63: "Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION".

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the practical case: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A plain description is checkable by a reader who does not share the analyst's jargon. It is also the only form in which the analyst's preference can be refused by someone else.

The outreach consequence: stage 1 sends the residual, the shape, and the pre-registered refuter. It does not send the framework. Once a reader has absorbed the interiors, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 10 :42; Seat Map :163).

## 5. Q: what licenses a name in this question?

**Nothing in the figures.** A particular mechanism is licensed only when the ground settles it (a whole-ecosystem experiment, a manipulation, a future trajectory that crosses or does not cross a threshold) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52). Until one of those, the name is the analyst's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This is not a ban on naming held atoms. The six substrate terms are already in the library; the ban is on pretending that a figure derived which one fills an ecological slot, or that a finite series named the source of a reading.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the framework cannot name the source at all. That overstates it. The correct claim is narrower: the framework cannot name the source **from the reading alone**. A provenanced import can name it, and the world literature shows exactly that move: Gsell et al. 2016 required "a priori knowledge of the underlying mechanisms driving ecosystem transitions" to choose relevant state variables. So §3 stands as a metamerism warning about pattern-only inference, not as a prohibition on mechanism knowledge. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (source essay + Fig 6 + world source).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of well-known ecologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." The truly testable output "would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
