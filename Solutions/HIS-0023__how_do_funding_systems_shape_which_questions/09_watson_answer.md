## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that funding systems shape which questions researchers ask and which findings receive attention. Option A: answer with a single funding rule, a percentage, a rank of agencies, or a claim that funding determines which findings are true. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, refuse the rule and the verdict claim, and leave the specific binding open until a ground settles it. Each leads somewhere different. A sends a preference where a term is owed, spends the independence of the people asked to check it, and confuses reach with settlement. B sends a checkable shape with refuters written in advance and an instrument request. The figures decide for B. The residual to be explained is the gap between a ground-pressured allocation and the allocation the funding-and-attention system actually produces.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, then partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the allocation a ground would keep and the allocation actually kept. Arity: at least three live quantities, the ex ante selection of questions, the ex post selection of findings, and the habitat conditions coupling them. Symmetry: ordered, since money enables a question, the question produces a finding, and the finding's attention feeds later criteria. Swapping the order gives a different process. Scale: per grant, per paper, per career slot, then field and portfolio. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, the allocation frame (ex ante).** Actors: funders, programme officers, panel reviewers, applicants. Currency: grant money and review scores. Anchor: the eventual ground contact of the funded question, an experiment or dataset that does not move because a panel liked the proposal. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **D2, the attention and circulation frame (ex post).** Actors: editors, reviewers, indexers, citers, communicators. Currency: citations, visibility, journal prestige. Anchor: replication or measurement of the finding. The operational metric is an instrument for that anchor and can be wrong on axes it does not measure. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **D3, the compounding frame across time.** Actors: past winners, past nonwinners, later panels reading past awards. Currency: cumulative funding and status. Anchor: an external ground introduced by a room that differs from the last one. Nameable as what selection between rooms would reach, so this is a domain.
- **D4, the funder's seat and stake.** Actors: the funding organisation and its governance. Currency: reputation, mission, accountability. Anchor: consequences for knowledge read by a checker who cannot author those consequences. A ground with a reputation to protect is disqualified, so this anchor must be sought outside the funder. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Rejected as a domain.** Public opinion about science. No anchor can be named that does not itself update on the funding system's reports. It is habitat, not a verdict-bearing domain.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. Past attention is an input to future funding, and funding constrains which findings can exist. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. Verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged into one effect of funding.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning, then learn and carry (Fig 3). The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. The suspected break is between vocabulary and objective: funding systems have terms for inputs and outputs but typically no instrument for whether a funded question changed contact with a ground.

Strain test: a line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity. Fig 2 :40 and Fig 2 :41 were read as a contradiction and re-censused as one independence parameter on two quantities, verdict and convention. No figure line was found to have drifted; two live strains remain named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the funding rule and the verdict claim do not. Funding sets reach, not settlement. Ex ante selection and ex post attention are a union that must be unbundled and read per slot, never averaged. A criterion that draws on its own past decisions is a mirror, and its agreement is variation rather than a verdict. A metric that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention; counts price the budget and are not witnesses. No standing list of good funding rules survives a change of residual, and the funder cannot certify its own importance. The audience's stake and this derivation's simulated status are declared rather than hidden. Mode: `specified` on the four domain mechanisms, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. The specific funding mechanism that wins in a field, its magnitude and its cost are a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached, not as silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable but not trivial. If reach and settlement are not distinct, a funding body could certify a finding by funding it, which no instrument in the ledger supports; if the union is actually a partition, the separate per-domain verdicts collapse into one average and the intersection goes unexamined; if actor valence is fixed rather than residual-relative, a standing rule becomes licit and the no-list verdict fails. The avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the full framework before the check. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the shape and its refuter avoids that cost.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no institution is committed by this derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state the shape, the non-claims and the refuters. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and a named binding can be provenanced or retracted without touching the figure reading. Fig 9 :29-31 requires every cut to stay ranked and reversible, which this verdict does.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. The following would reverse or refine it: a funding criterion shown to settle a slot without moving when the funding body is pushed, with per-slot residuals trending down (breaks D1 and D2); a single lever shown to clear both selections and their intersection (breaks the union); a metric shown to decide an outcome while staying checkable against a non-updating ground with no coherent drift (breaks the convention verdict); a rule shown to improve per-slot choice across several changed residuals without changing valence or adding contact (breaks the no-list verdict); a global attention average that predicts per-slot choice as well as per-slot residuals at the ground (breaks per-slot indexing); or an instrument that measures whether a funded question changed contact with a ground, which would move the objective link from unreached to reached. A body of agreeing readers, or a list of prestigious names selected for likely agreement, is not such a reversal. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
