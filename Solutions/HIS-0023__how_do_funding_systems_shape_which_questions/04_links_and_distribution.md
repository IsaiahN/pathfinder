## 3. What bears on the question from the wider literature

1. **The ex ante selection is not a measurement of quality.** Bol et al. 2018 use a hard threshold and show that near-identical proposals diverge in later funding, with no discontinuity in measurable output. The interpretation offered by the authors is that early funding itself is an asset in later competitions, plus a participation effect on the supply side. This is exactly the mirror-chain shape in Fig 7 :35-36: agreement compounds, evidence does not.
2. **The ex post selection has the same shape.** The Google Scholar entry describes a ranking that feeds on its own output, and the publication-bias entry describes a publication decision that feeds on the sign of the result. Both are selection criteria promoted into verdicts (Fig 10 :47).
3. **The two are one pool in part.** Citation counts are used both to allocate attention and, through prior-achievement criteria, to allocate funding. Two frames drawing on the same evidence are one frame wearing two names (Fig 8 :48-49). Their agreement cannot certify a question's importance.
4. **A genuine second frame is available in the world.** Independent replication, registered reports and a funding body's funded-grants database are public, checkable records that do not depend on the panel's preference. They are candidate anchors (Fig 2 :29) with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14).
5. **The agenda-setting analogue is low weight.** The Wikipedia article carries a maintenance notice saying it appears to incorporate large-language-model text (June 2026). It is used only for the Cohen 1963 distinction between what to think and what to think about, attributed to Cohen, and is not counted as an independent witness.

## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Leading with the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the theory.
- **Seat Map :163** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Prefer venues and readers whose evidence pool differs from the funding-policy pool.

**Reply order:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters, (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it, (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figure text, not the summary, is the authority.
