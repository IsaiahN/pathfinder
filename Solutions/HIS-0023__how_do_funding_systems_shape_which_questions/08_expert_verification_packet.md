## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Funding systems shape the reach of inquiry, not its verdict, and they do so through two overlapping selections that must not be averaged. Before the work, a panel allocates a budget; after the work, editors, indexers and citers allocate visibility. These are a union of conditions, not a partition, because past attention re-enters as evidence for future funding and funding constrains which findings can exist to be attended to. Money gives a question a budget to be tried, so it sets how much of the arrangement space can be searched; it cannot decide which finding is true or which question mattered, because only a ground that does not move when a frame pushes it can settle that. A selection criterion that uses its own past awards as evidence of merit is a mirror rather than an anchor, so its internal agreement is variation and not a verdict, and a chain that rebooks the same kind of room travels in place. A metric that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention, counts of grants and citations price the budget but do not witness a finding, no standing list of good funding rules survives a change of the residual, and the effect is per slot (field, career stage, question type), never an aggregate. The honest output is that shape plus the refuters written in advance, with the specific funding rule left unspoken until a ground settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Funding sets reach, not settlement (framework-internal and measure-facing)

- **Statement:** a budget determines how much of the space of attempts is visited; it does not settle truth or importance. The residual is per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 12 :127 "the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all." Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** is there any instrument in the funding system whose reading changes the per-slot residual when the reading is pushed, rather than only counting what the system itself produced? Fig 1 :22 says "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a funding criterion that settles question choice, does not move when the funding body is pushed, and whose per-slot residual trends down as funding rises while attention tracks that same non-updating measurement rather than prior attention. If this holds, C1 fails.

### C2 - The two selections form a union whose reused criteria become mirrors (framework-internal and empirically checkable)

- **Statement:** ex ante funding selection and ex post attention selection are overlapping conditions; when a criterion draws on its own past decisions, agreement compounds and evidence does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **What to check:** in a real panel, does a prior award enter the next merit judgement as evidence? In a ranking, does past citation count enter the visibility rule? Bol, de Vaan and van de Rijt 2018 report a hard-threshold divergence with "No discontinuity in publications, citations or h-index at the cut." Perc 2014 gives the general preferential-attachment shape.
- **Refuter:** show a single lever that clears both the funding-only and attention-only regions and their intersection, so the compound is a partition rather than a union; or exhibit a sequence of funding rooms with low overlap whose next room is chosen against the residual surfaced by the previous one and whose per-slot residuals trend down. Either result breaks C2.

### C3 - Valence, filters and the absence of a standing rule (framework-internal)

- **Statement:** a metric used as a verdict has been promoted past its office; counts are filters for the budget; actor value flips with the residual, so no funding rule is good across changed residuals.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence."
- **What to check:** does a single score both decide an award and claim to report importance? Fig 2 :40 warns that "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", while Fig 2 :41 says agreement is "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". The two are different offices, and Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Refuter:** show a metric that decides an outcome, remains checkable against a non-updating ground at the slot, and produces no coherent drift across repeated use; or exhibit a funding rule that improves per-slot question choice across several changed residuals without changing valence and without adding contact. Either result breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a funding rule, a percentage, an optimal peer-review design, or a rank order of agencies. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that funding determines which findings are true or which questions mattered. Only the ground settles (Fig 2 :29).
- Not that all funding systems converge, or that any specific one does. The mirror claim is conditional on criteria drawing on their own past decisions.
- Not that double-blind review, lotteries, registered reports or independent panels are the answer. They are bindings from the world frame, provenanced as such, and candidates at a fixed residual.
- Not that a funding body can certify its own importance. Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :93: "A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."
- Not `grounded`. The source ledger records what was read; no measurement was taken here.

## 5. What would settle it

A better instrument, not a further argument. A per-slot measure of whether a funded question changed contact with a ground would instrument the broken objective link (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). Two instruments already returning something are the starting points for INWARD work (Fig 6 :32): the regression-discontinuity design around a hard funding threshold, and the registered-report protocol review. An OUTWARD anchor, such as a replication registry or a held-out dataset, would need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14) and would be debited if it shared the panel's evidence pool. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
