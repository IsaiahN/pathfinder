# The Tether's answer: how funding systems shape questions and attention

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain mechanisms, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a policy result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken here.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two selections are observed. Before the work, a panel decides which questions get a budget. After the work, editors, indexers and citers decide which findings are visible. Their allocations do not track a ground reading of importance or truth: a hard threshold near which review scores are almost identical produces a large later funding divergence (Bol et al. 2018), ranking by past citations strengthens the advantage of already-cited work (Wikipedia, Google Scholar), and publication favours positive results at equal quality (Wikipedia, Publication bias). The residual is the gap between a ground-pressured allocation and the allocation actually produced. It is per slot, never an average (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## 2. The shape

1. **Funding sets reach, not settlement.** An anchor "does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). Money gives a question a budget to be tried, and "the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all" (Fig 12 :127). It cannot decide which finding is true or which question mattered. The essay's prestige/action rule is the same claim in other terms: prestige can fund an action but only the ground can settle it.

2. **The two selections are a union, not a partition.** "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones" (Fig 9 :55), and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). Past attention re-enters as evidence for future funding, and funding constrains which findings can exist. The verdicts stay separate per domain; no average between them is licit. The coupling is an ordered bond, not a co-occurrence: "A then B; B needs A to have happened" (Operators table :30).

3. **A criterion that uses its own past decisions is a mirror.** "The anchor must not update" (Fig 2 :33). When it does, "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34). Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." The room's agreement is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35). "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :12-13). The world data are consistent with this: near-threshold winners diverge, and a substantial part of the divergence comes from who keeps applying.

4. **A metric that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.** Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 2 :41 keeps the other half: agreement is "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". The distinction is valence, not degree (Fig 2 :39). A citation count is an instrument for a ground it does not measure on every axis (Fig 2 :37-38), and the ground does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24).

5. **No standing list of good funding rules.** "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries" (Fig 11 :49); "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" (Fig 11 :51). What a seat can do is introduce, not subtract: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not" (Fig 11 :61). The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43). A mechanism that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves (Fig 11 :40).

6. **Counts are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Independence is bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42); two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names (Fig 8 :48-49), while "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone" (Fig 8 :45) only where their closures differ.

7. **The funder's stake is structural.** "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying" (Fig 10 :59). Where the stake cannot be removed, declare it (Fig 10 :60). A ground with a reputation to protect is not a ground (Fig 10 :93).

8. **The effect is per slot.** Index the residual by field, career stage and question type (Fig 1 :19). Aggregating across slots throws away the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1 :22).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a funding rule, a percentage, a rank of agencies, or an optimal peer-review design. "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32). The set is closed and the arrangements are not (Fig 13 :115).
- Not that funding determines which findings are true. Only the ground settles (Fig 2 :29).
- Not that all funding systems converge, or that any specific one does. The mirror claim is conditional on criteria drawing on their own past decisions.
- Not that double-blind review, lotteries, registered reports or independent panels are *the* answer. They are bindings from the world frame, provenanced as such, and candidates at a fixed residual (see the correction in `05` section 3).
- Not that the funding body can certify its own importance. Its reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A better instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; several paths can create the same reading; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Objective link.** Is there a per-slot measure of whether a funded question changed contact with a ground? The middle link of Fig 3 has "no instrument" in most systems (Fig 3 :27); instrument it before spending more effort above the break (Fig 3 :19).
- **Anchor test.** Does the criterion move when the funding body is pushed? If it moves, it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Cycle test.** Does past attention re-enter with a positive or negative sign, and do the successive rooms differ and match the residual surfaced (Fig 7 :36, :38-39)?
- **Convention test.** Does the metric decide an outcome while remaining checkable, or has it become a constant the seat authored (Fig 10 :47-48)?
- **Standing falsifiers.** A global attention average that predicts per-slot choice as well as per-slot residuals; a lever that clears the union's intersection; a rule that improves choice across changed residuals without adding contact.

Two instruments already returning something are the starting points for INWARD work (Fig 6 :32): the regression-discontinuity design around a hard threshold (Bol et al. 2018) and the registered-report protocol review (COS, opened this session). Neither is imported here as an atom; both are candidate refinements of our own measurement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39). "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). Indiscernibility is not identity. The claim that one funding design wins is a preference held as a shape, not a term bound and accruing residual; until it is bound and tested, the loop cannot see it (Fig 5 :62-63).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not name a best funding system. They give the shape any answer must have. Funding sets the reach of what can be tried, not the verdict on what is true. Asking which questions get funded and asking which findings get attention are two overlapping selections, not one, so their verdicts must be kept separate and read per field and career stage. A criterion that uses its own past decisions as evidence is a mirror rather than an anchor, and its agreement is variation rather than a verdict; a metric that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. There is no standing list of good funding rules, because a reviewer's value flips with the question being asked. Counts of grants and citations price the budget; they do not witness a finding unless separately ranged against a ground that does not move. What the framework offers is that shape, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
