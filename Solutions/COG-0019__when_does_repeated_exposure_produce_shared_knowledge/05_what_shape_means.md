## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135, *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, a shape is not "seven exposures" or "a critical mass." It is the list of conditions under which convergence is knowledge rather than drift, together with the carve-out under which divergence is correct. That shape can be checked. A number cannot be derived from these sources at all.

From the exemplar's mapping of *The Library of Babel* (canonical reading, not independently reopened this session): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- Describe the effect, not the cause. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Describe before choosing a frame. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* In this solution the residual was written in effect terms before the figure census was chosen.
- Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term. `THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The README does this; §3 of `01` is the resulting refuter.
- Separate the three routes. `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD are *"three questions, not one."* More exposure is SEARCHED. It is the cheapest and is asked first, but it cannot add a primitive.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* In this question that would be an experiment that changes contact, for example a design in which one group ranges against a stable external test and another only against its own estimates, with accuracy measured at the end.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

Until one of these, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are imports of this kind. Lorenz et al. (2011) is not Tether-derived; it is a shadow that explains a gap, and it is labelled as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most here. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists,"* and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied to this question: agreement is a reading, not a source. The same observed agreement can come from (i) independent triangulation against a ground, (ii) mutual updating, or (iii) a shared hidden source. An observer who sees only agreement *"cannot comment on the source."* This is exactly Fig 2's two collapses. What is owed is a discriminator, not a verdict about the meaning of agreement. That is why the packet asks for the provenance of the convergence, not just its magnitude.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator."* And, operationally, *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* A famous researcher agreeing with the shape adds no ground contact. Prestige can fund the test; it cannot settle it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**The shape of the answer to COG-0019 is a two-condition union with a convention carve-out.**

1. **Independence of frames.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."*
2. **A ground that does not update.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation."*
3. **Methods, not recordings, across the membrane.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
4. **Divergence is admissible while the residual is live.** Symbols table :25: *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
5. **Conventions are real but weightless as world verdicts.** Fig 2 :41: *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

That is a shape: it tells you what any candidate answer must look like and what would reverse it. It does not tell you how many exposures are enough. Naming such a number would require a measurement this package does not contain, and the figures explicitly refuse to name a missing primitive.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Keep the outside frame outside.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the source. The recipient's verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40). That is what `02_outreach_email.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` implement.
