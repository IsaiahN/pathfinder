## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about repeated exposure to the same observation by two or more frames. Option A: accept the common claim that more exposure produces more agreement and that agreement is shared knowledge. Option B: split agreement into verdict, method and convention, and state the conditions under which each is produced. What each leads to: A sends a single-lever answer that mistakes coherent drift and a shared source for knowledge, and it hands a filter a verdict. B sends a two-condition shape with a convention carve-out, four written refuters, and a named discriminator. The residual is per slot: R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|, with the comparison across frames indexed by exposure n. Fig 9 :57 names this a union, not one hard question: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** What drives the per-slot prediction errors of two or more frames toward the same value after repeated exposure to o′, and what drives them apart? Arity: at least two frames, one observation, one exposure index, read per slot. Symmetry: the input is symmetric, the update is not, because histories and w differ (Symbols table :25). Scale: within one frame at n = 1, across the membrane between people at n > 1, population scale for the word "shared." Intrinsically cross scale.
- **Domain 1, contact inside one frame.** Actors: one frame, its percepts and Γ. Currency: residual R per slot. Anchor: the observation o′, only if it is stable. Admitted: yes, conditionally.
- **Domain 2, transmission across the membrane.** Actors: sender, receiver, the artifact or utterance. Currency: method versus recording. Anchor: none by itself, it is a filter with direction. Admitted: yes.
- **Domain 3, social alignment in the room.** Actors: the frames, their estimates, influence. Currency: agreement and confidence. Anchor: absent in the two collapses, present in the triangulation case. Admitted: yes, with two opposite verdicts.
- **Domain 4, ground and habitat.** Actors: the world, conditions, relations. Currency: settlement, what pays. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Admitted: yes, this is the only thing that makes shared agreement into knowledge.
- **Domain 5, convention and shared meaning.** Actors: the group, a symbol or practice. Currency: coordination value. Anchor: a declared meaning. Admitted: yes, as a convention with zero world-verdict weight.
- **Excluded.** "The influential person" has no anchor independent of influence. "Culture" as a superorganic entity has no anchor and is absorbed into D3 and D4. Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
- **Partition or union:** union of independent frames, non-updating ground, effect-term transmission, with a convention carve-out. The overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Exposure count sits in planning, below the objective link, so it cannot rescue a broken objective.
- **Strain test:** the Fig 12 isomer line initially seemed to fit "same exposure, different interpretation." It governs composition inside a library, not transmission, so it was returned to the census and kept only as a declared analogue with a falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B. Repeated exposure does not produce shared knowledge by itself. Knowledge requires a union of independent frames and a non-updating ground, with transmission in effect terms. Without those conditions, exposure produces coherent drift or a shared-source agreement, both of which look like knowledge from inside. Where the conditions hold and the residual is live, divergence is the correct reading, not a failed intervention. Repeated exposure also produces a genuine shared convention, but that is agreement about meaning and carries zero world-verdict weight. Exposure count is a budget filter and never a witness. Mode `specified`, ungrounded. The common claim in the question is refused as written.

- **cost if wrong:** the corrected packet states its mode, writes four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is handing a filter a verdict, that is, telling a group that more exposure will fix a broken objective link (Fig 3 :19). That cost is avoidable by asking what would count as a failure before designing the exposure. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The single-lever claim, the threshold number and the promotion of convention to verdict are removable without touching the two-condition shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A design that separates private estimates from estimates after feedback and shows accuracy rising with diversity unchanged would break the D3 verdict; a case of mutual updating whose agreement survives an independent instrument would break it outright. An exposure that adds a primitive inside a fixed closure would break Fig 8 :32. A group with identical histories and w that diverges on the same residual would break the Symbols :25 mechanism. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
