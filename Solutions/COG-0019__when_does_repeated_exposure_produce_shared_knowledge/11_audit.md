## 1. The residual line, taken alone

> After n exposures of the same observation o′ to two or more frames, what makes their per-slot prediction errors converge in a way that deserves to be called shared knowledge, and what makes them diverge? Nothing in this line says "culture", "social learning", "conformity", "norms", "polarization" or "bias". Any answer that begins with one of those words has named a candidate before describing the residual, against Fig 9 :28 (*"any frame you pick will seem to fit"*) and Fig 8 :53 (*"Residual first, frame second"*).

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. The object of explanation is the *status* of convergence, not its magnitude. A small gap between two people is not yet knowledge; it is a reading that could have several sources.

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists in the abstract: the ground, explicitly listed in Fig 2 :29, and the question is constitutively about what settles a shared claim. But the anchor is not guaranteed to be present in a given exposure design. So the question is well formed and not a `gap`, while the most common empirical configuration (people hearing the same story from each other) may have no anchor at all. That distinction is where the answer lives.

## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among at least two frames, one observation o′, and an exposure index n, indexed by slot s. A two-place reading (person, opinion) drops the observation and the other frame, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** the input is symmetric, the update is not. Symbols table :25 says *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* So identical input does not imply identical output. This is the single most important fact in the residual and it is easy to miss.
- **Scale:** one frame at n = 1, across the membrane between people at n > 1, population scale for the word "shared." The residual is cross scale, so a single-scale answer is already the wrong shape (Fig 4).

### 2.2 Domains, one line each

**Divergence from the primary census, recorded.** This seat at first proposed splitting C into "mutual update" and "shared source" as two domains. On re-census they share an anchor condition (absent in both) and a currency (agreement without new evidence), so they are two verdicts inside one domain, not two domains. The primary kept them inside D3 for the same reason without stating the test. This seat also tested merging E into C, because both produce agreement. It declined: E's anchor is a declared meaning and C's is either a ground or the frames themselves, and the two have opposite verdict weights. Both censuses arrive at five admitted domains.

**Partition or union:** union of independent frames and a non-updating ground, with effect-term transmission as the crossing condition and a convention carve-out. The overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :57).

**Dependency order:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). This seat agrees with the primary ordering and adds one constraint the primary did not spell out: exposure count n lives under *planning*, which is below the *objective* link. Fig 3 :19 (*"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not"*) therefore rules out more exposure as a remedy for a group that cannot say what would count as a failure. The primary states this in F6 but does not tie it to the dependency order. Refinement, not conflict.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the two-condition shape to one sentence. Result: *shared knowledge requires independent frames ranging against a ground that does not move with them.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that sentence into a specific design. Result: *collect private estimates first, then let people see only what they need, and keep an external check.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** What the one sentence cannot hold:

1. It loses the method-versus-recording condition. "Independent frames" says nothing about what passes between them, so coming back down it permits a design in which people independently read the same recording. That is collapse 2, not triangulation (Fig 2 :21).
2. It loses the convention carve-out. The sentence makes "shared knowledge" sound like one kind. Coming back down, it licenses a group to treat a shared label as a world verdict (Fig 10 :47).
3. It loses the admissibility of divergence. The sentence is about convergence, so a design built from it will keep pushing toward agreement even when the residual is live and the correct output is different estimates (Symbols :25).
4. It loses the budget rule. "Independent frames" does not say that exposure count is a filter, so a builder can read it as "more independent frames always better," which ignores the coherence cost and the idempotence law (Fig 8 :32).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The sentence cannot say what instrument would distinguish triangulation from a shared source, which is the actual open gap.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version in `07`. That paragraph is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.

3. **Annotation A, independence.** This second seat is simulated and shares an evidence pool with the primary. The solution has not received an independent human check. Its status must remain `specified`, and it must not be presented as corroborated by this audit.
4. **Annotation B, round trip.** The one-paragraph version in `07` is lossy in five named ways (section 4). It is suitable only as an invitation to check the full packet, never as a substitute for it.
5. **Annotation C, reach.** The answer stops at a shape. No threshold number of exposures is given and none is derivable from these sources. That is an honest stop at the limit of the instruments, not a silence. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).

**Final:** the solution is internally consistent with the figures, honest about its simulation status, and correctly stopped at a `specified` two-condition shape with an open discriminator. It is not a ground reading and cannot settle COG-0019. What would settle it is a design that separates independent ranging from mutual updating and measures accuracy after each, which is exactly what the packet asks an outside frame to check.
