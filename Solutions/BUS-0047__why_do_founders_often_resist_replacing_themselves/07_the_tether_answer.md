# The Tether's answer on founder succession

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a business finding. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two conditions, split before the search (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."):

- **R1, why founders resist.** The founder's prediction that they must stay persists and does not trend down even where the market returns a residual against it. The frame doing the grading is part of what is being graded.
- **R2, when transition is necessary.** The organisation's capability no longer composes over the founder in isolation, because the binding link has moved to a slot that frame cannot close.

They get separate verdicts. They are never averaged.

## 2. The shape, by domain

### D1, the founder frame: resistance is a no-anchor, standing effect

1. **There is no nameable anchor inside the founder's self-assessment.** Fig 2 :13 "no anchor; each updates on the others". A frame that grades itself with a quantity it also produces is disqualified: Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." So resistance is coherent drift (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."), not evidence the founder is right and not a character defect.
2. **Standing is a stake that survives the outcome that earned it.** Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." The interest in the company's outcome is unavoidable; the interest in the reading of whether the founder should stay is disqualifying for a verdict. Declare it (Fig 10 :60 "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged.").
3. **The live slot is hidden in the aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". A good aggregate quarter is compatible with one failing executive slot.

**Verdict D1 (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 10, Fig 1):** resistance is a structural effect of self-grading with no outside anchor plus a surviving stake. It is not a verdict on whether the founder should stay.

### D2, the organisation as habitat: replacing the person is not a capability change

4. **Capability is joint.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Changing the CEO while holding the contacts fixed changes nothing.
5. **A handover is substitution, not subtraction.** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails". The founder's tacit context is part of what the goal required; the successor's own actors were not chosen.
6. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same founder can be a benefactor to the startup residual and a malfactor to the scale residual. Asking "is the founder valuable?" is malformed; ask "valuable to which residual?"

**Verdict D2 (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 11, Fig 3, Fig 4):** transition is necessary when the habitat has moved such that the founder's contact is no longer the binding one, not at a size threshold. No figure gives a threshold; that is a recorded `gap`.

### D3, verification and succession: follow the moved link, describe before looking

7. **Follow the dependency order.** Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Ask which link the company stops at, and whether that was measured (Fig 3 :29-30 "Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."). Size is a filter for starting the search, never the verdict: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
8. **The successor's frame must differ and match.** Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." A clone stops travel; so does an outsider chosen for contrast only. Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."
9. **Describe the residual before the shortlist.** Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". The output is a kind of successor, not a name: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
10. **Hand over a method, not the playbook.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The early playbook is a recording of one occasion; replayed at scale it "produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one" (Fig 4 :26).
11. **Keep the checker outside.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A founder-selected, founder-briefed, founder-rewarded board cannot check the founder's necessity. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" applies to an agreement-heavy board.

**Verdict D3 (`specified`, triangulated across Fig 9, Fig 7, Fig 10):** the transition is owed when a named live slot persists against a ground the founder frame cannot move, and the incoming frame differs from the founder's in the dimension that slot names.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a size, revenue or headcount threshold. No figure supplies one, and inventing one would be a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not "hire an outsider" and not "promote an insider". Both are names selected before the residual is described.
- Not a character judgement on the founder, and not a claim that resistance is irrational. Fig 2 calls it coherent drift.
- Not that the founder's playbook is worthless. It becomes a method only when distilled from a recording into something reapplicable.
- Not a claim about a particular company. No ground reading was taken.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R, phi)` for any live slot.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot instrument:** can the board measure the candidate live slot (for example executive decision latency, escalation handling, key-customer retention) separately from the aggregate, before naming a successor? If not, Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **Contact test:** does the company's ground return change when the founder's contacts are redistributed, with the person held constant? That separates agent from habitat (F4).
- **Room test:** does a successor whose closure matches the founder's on the named slot close the residual? If yes, the "must differ" claim fails.
- **Standing test:** do founders with no public standing attached to the early success resist at the same rate? If yes, the standing mechanism (F2) fails.
- **Catalyst test:** is the founder regenerated or spent by each new stage, measured on a slot named in advance? That closes F13.
- Standing falsifiers from `01`: the five pre-registered conditions in §3. Any one fires a withdrawal or a narrowing.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of what the instruments in this folder can distinguish. From *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The rubric that names the live slot is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Indiscernibility is not identity: a successor who looks like the founder on paper is not thereby the same frame.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a company size at which a founder must step down, and it does not name a successor. It says three things. Founder resistance is a no-anchor effect plus standing that survived the early success, so the founder's own assessment of their necessity cannot settle the question. Capability belongs to the leader and the habitat together, so changing only the person changes nothing; a handover is a substitution of habitat, and what the founder silently supplied is invisible until the goal fails. A transition is therefore owed when a named live slot persists against a market the founder's frame cannot move and the binding link has moved past that frame. The next leader must differ from the founder in the dimension the residual names and match the residual the founder surfaced; the early playbook is a recording, not a method, and a founder-selected board cannot check the claim. What the framework offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
