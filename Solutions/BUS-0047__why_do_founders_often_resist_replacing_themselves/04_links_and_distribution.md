## What the wider literature bears on, and what it does not

Three low-overlap fields were opened and each predicts part of the shape:

1. **Founder succession and entrepreneurship research (business).** The board is the natural owner of the decision, but there is no standard framework for grading the CEO. This corroborates the need to describe the live slot before selecting a candidate. It does not decide the verdict.
2. **Human cognition and social psychology.** Motivated reasoning, escalation of commitment and social identity theory independently predict that a person will defend an identity-relevant reading and resist a bet against it. They corroborate F1 and F2. They do not by themselves show that the founder is wrong.
3. **Animal behaviour.** Honeybee supersedure is a case where the maintainers, not the queen, replace a failing queen, and introducing a replacement meets resistance ("balling"). This corroborates the board-as-maintainer shape and the substitution hazard in F5. It is an analogue, not evidence about companies.
4. **Software engineering.** The bus factor names a contact concentration as a project risk, which corroborates F4: the problem is where contact lives, not the person's talent.

Overlap is declared. The eight essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement is one evidence pool talking, not independent corroboration (Fig 2 :40). The Wikipedia sources are tertiary; they are used as pointers to the primary literature and as qualitative corroboration only.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. If a source had conflicted, the instruction is to go back to the domain census, not to flip the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two shape claims and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for the recipient's failure criterion. Record their verdict before showing any theory. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a reusable method. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method. The author's own caveat applies: outputs "must be seen as simulacra" unless derived from the real architecture or the formula worked directly; each user writes a refuter and states a mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in §3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are live. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
