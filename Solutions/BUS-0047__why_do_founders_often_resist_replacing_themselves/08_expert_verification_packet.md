## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question splits into two conditions. Founder resistance (R1) is a no-anchor, standing effect: the founder's own assessment of their necessity has no independent anchor inside it, and the standing earned on the early success survives into a stage where the question has changed. Leadership transition (R2) is governed by agent-and-habitat: capability is a property of both together, so changing only the person, with the company's contacts held fixed, changes nothing. A transition is owed when a named live slot residual persists against a market the founder's frame cannot move and the binding link has moved past that frame. The next leader must differ from the founder in the dimension the residual names and match the residual the founder surfaced; the founder's recorded playbook is not the same as a method, and a founder-selected, founder-briefed, founder-rewarded board cannot check the claim. No size threshold and no named successor are claimed.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus cognition-facing; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 10

- **Statement:** founder resistance is coherent drift, not a measurement. There is no nameable anchor inside the founder's self-assessment, and standing is a stake that survives the outcome that earned it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :13 "no anchor; each updates on the others". Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." Fig 10 :60 "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."
- **What to check:** is the founder's necessity claim graded by a metric the founder helped design, and is the board's verdict independent of the founder's framing? A globally healthy P and L is not a refutation: Fig 1 :13 "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state".
- **Refuter:** a founder self-assessment instrument predicts the market return without being moved by the founder's standing or by team agreement; or founders with no public standing attached to the early success resist at the same rate. Either fires the withdrawal of this sub-claim.

### C2 - organisation-facing; checkable against a real succession

- **Statement:** capability belongs to the leader and the habitat together. A handover is a substitution of habitat, not a subtraction of a person. Transition is necessary when the binding link has moved, not at a size threshold.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails". Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- **What to check:** in a succession you know, list what the founder silently supplied (relationships, judgement under ambiguity, trust of early employees) and what the newcomer introduced that was not chosen. Did the goal fail on the first list or act on the second? Was the transition triggered by a measured moved link or by a revenue chart?
- **Refuter:** a controlled succession in which only the leader changes, with habitat and contact held fixed, persistently moves the ground return. That breaks the decisive claim. Note also that no figure supplies a size threshold; if a checker produces one from outside, it is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

### C3 - successor-shape; checkable by a board or search committee before a shortlist exists

- **Statement:** describe the residual before naming candidates. The next leader's frame must differ from the founder's in the dimension the residual names and match the residual the founder surfaced. Hand over methods, not the recorded playbook.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
- **What to check:** was the live slot written down before the shortlist? Does the chosen candidate differ on that slot specifically, rather than on style, tenure or celebrity? Is the founder's early playbook being handed over as a script or distilled into a reusable method?
- **Refuter:** a candidate whose frame matches the founder's on every residual-relevant axis closes the live slot at the new stage; or candidates chosen before the residual was written down outperform candidates chosen after it in a controlled comparison. Either breaks the successor shape.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a size, revenue or headcount threshold. That is a recorded `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- Not "hire an outsider" and not "promote an insider".
- Not that the founder is irrational, spent, or worthless. Whether the founder is a catalyst is an open item: Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
- Not a derivation of one particular company's decision from the figures.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement.
- Not an endorsement of any candidate or board. The claim is about the shape of the decision.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Slot instrument:** can the board measure the live slot separately from the aggregate before naming anyone? If not, Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
2. **Contact test:** does the ground return change when the founder's contacts are redistributed while the person is held constant? This separates agent from habitat.
3. **Room test:** does a successor whose frame matches the founder's on the named slot close the residual?
4. **Standing test:** do founders without public standing resist at the same rate?
5. **Catalyst test:** is the founder regenerated or spent by each stage, measured on a slot named in advance?
