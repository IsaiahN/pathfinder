## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: resistance is a no-anchor/standing effect; capability is agent-and-habitat; transition follows a moved link with a differing successor; no size threshold.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a practical succession policy: describe a live slot, choose a candidate whose frame differs on that slot, keep the board outside the founder's briefing, hand over methods.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the live slot**, which no figure supplies and which an organisation must measure with an instrument it may not have (Fig 3 :27);
- the **catalyst distinction** of Fig 12 :119, which the shorthand carries as an open item but which a practical policy silently resolves by assuming the founder is spent;
- the **constraints on the board's independence**, which the shorthand compresses into "outside the founder's framing" and which in practice requires a selection mechanism the figures do not specify (Fig 10 :73-79).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: capability is joint, a handover substitutes a habitat, and the successor must differ and match the surfaced residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no company ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
