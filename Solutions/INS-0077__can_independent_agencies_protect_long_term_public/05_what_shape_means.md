## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133 "reached at all. A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel* (read directly): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0077 the shape is therefore *the separator*, not *the institution*: a reference that does not update on the political frame, separate correction of agency and principals by that reference, and an outside seat maintaining the channel with its stake declared. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape; here the analogue is `INDEPENDENCE(???)CONTROL`, with the `???` occupied by "a channel to a non-updating outcome maintained from an outside seat," not by fixed terms or a budget rule.

A shape constrains any admissible answer; it does not fill the slot. Any proposed design that updates its reference on the current majority, or that makes control the agency's compliance with the executive, fails the shape regardless of how well it scores on an independence index.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (read directly): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from "commitment problem," "capture," "auxiliary precautions," and let the residual narrow which of them belongs.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe "a reference that does not move when pushed," not "a politically independent commission."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read directly): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email sends a failure list rather than a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here: the answer is reached when the separator and its six refuters are one object, not when an institution is named.

## 3. What licenses a name

## 4. Why an independence index commits the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read directly) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

An independence index reads the same green: a fixed term, a budget rule, and a removal protection can all produce the same score while arising from different sources (genuine commitment, capture, or sheer irrelevance). The reading does not determine the source. Naming the source from the score is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator that separates commitment from capture, not a finer index.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A framework's own independence score cannot validate the framework's reading of independence.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a verdict

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read directly): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question, a well-known institute agreeing that independence works gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether any agency's channel to the long-term outcome stayed reachable. The legitimate ask is for a refutation from a different closure. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read directly) adds the drift warning: two frames that only check against each other "drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices," and "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another." A community of independence researchers shares pretext; that makes communication cheap and its agreement worthless as a check of the claim.

## 6. Capability is contact, not title

From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (carried from the exemplar mapping): intelligence is a mapping built against an unforgiving environment. Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." So "independent agency" is not a capability; the capability lies in agency plus the channel to a non-updating outcome and the outside seat that maintains it. This is finding F4 in `01`.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* (carried) and the *Arrival of the Fittest* essay (carried) supply the multi-seat background: rooms per problem, a commons of methods only, ground under all. Applied here: one checking room per residual, not one permanent super-committee with a fixed view of "the public interest."
