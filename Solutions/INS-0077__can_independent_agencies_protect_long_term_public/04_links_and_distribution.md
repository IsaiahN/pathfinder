## Wider literature: what bears on the claim

1. **Madison's auxiliary precautions** is the closest low-overlap analogue to Fig 7 and Fig 10: control comes from arranged opposing interests and independent means of defence, not from trusting motives. This is why the packet asks for a checking arrangement rather than a better class of agency head.
2. **Kydland and Prescott and the Odysseus device** are the canonical statement of the residual's first half: a decision maker binds its future self because the future self will otherwise prefer the immediate option. The framework's refusal operator (¬) and the anchor rule (Fig 2) describe the same shape in its own vocabulary. This is corroboration from a different field, not the source of the verdict.
3. **Regulatory capture** is the strongest conflict in the set. It says insulation from politics can simply exchange one principal for another. The derivation did not answer it by adding a caveat; it returned to the domain census and produced F5 (insulation substitutes a habitat, with two silent failures). The conflict strengthened the union reading.
4. **Central-bank independence** supplies the two-role distinction: legal independence is a commitment device for an outcome question, while democratic accountability is a legitimacy question with a different proper anchor. Used carefully, because one public summary carries a maintenance warning about AI-generated text.
5. **The four framework essays** share the author and evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement is expected and carries no independent witness weight. They are used only to fix the canonical meaning of "shape" and the outreach order.
