## The question restated as an effect

An elected governance system discounts outcomes that arrive after the next election. The long-term public interests in question (stable regulation, environmental protection, financial stability, rights of minorities and future persons) are exactly those discounted outcomes. The proposed lever is an independent agency: fixed terms, removal protection, an own budget, expert staff, a mandate. That lever refuses short-term political pressure. The worry is that the same insulation that keeps the long-term outcome reachable also removes the channels by which citizens, through elected representatives, correct the agency.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, per the formula's "Direction"):** a separator that says which conditions must hold for insulation to keep a non-updating anchor reachable while the correction channel from the polity stays intact, and which conditions turn insulation into a veto and control into interference. The sought-for shape is written down and made refutable before the derivation runs (see `01` section 4).

## Boundary and deliberate omissions

Sources were the local canonical framework files under `FRAMEWORK/`, the public figure set, and the public pages listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. No file outside this solution folder was edited. The validator's own report at `Logs/validation_report.csv` is a side effect of the required validation step, and the completion log append is required by the standing instructions.

Deliberately absent:

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figure-grounded reasons are recorded in `01` finding F8 and in `04`: selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they have checked the output spends the only independence they contribute.
