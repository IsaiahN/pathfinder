## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the AMOC stability question and the observation-selection question that is bundled with it. Option A: treat stability as one scalar and rank observations by their correlation with it. Option B: treat the question as a union of a trend condition, a variability condition and a threshold condition, indexed per observing slot, with channels classified as anchors or instruments by their relation to a ground that does not update, and a declared stop where no instrument can read a numerical tipping distance. What each leads to: A sends a pooled number and a ranked list that can be manufactured by selecting agreeable indices, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, an anchor test, and an explicit gap where the figures return no number.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a frame predicts about overturning and what the Atlantic returns. Arity: three-place at the threshold (transport, critical value, freshwater control), two-place elsewhere, indexed per slot. Symmetry: ordered and hysteretic, not interchangeable; adding freshwater is not the reverse of removing it (Fig 12 :121). Scale: days to decades at 26°N, 1900 to present in reconstructions, millennia in paleo records, up to 4,400 model years in hosing runs. Fig 9 :4-5 says split rather than search when each rule works on a different subset.
- **Domain 1, direct observing arrays.** Actors: RAPID at 26°N, OSNAP in the subpolar North Atlantic, SAMBA at 34°S, Florida Current cable, Argo. Currency: volume, heat and freshwater transport in Sverdrups. Anchor: measured transport, a counter that does not update on a model's opinion (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **Domain 2, indirect indices and fingerprints.** Actors: subpolar SST cold blob, gyre index, North Atlantic carbon sink, dynamic sea level, CERES and Argo heat products. Currency: correlation with overturning state. Anchor: none on its own; must be ranged against Domain 1. Instrument that reads green cannot comment on its source. Admitted as instrument, not anchor.
- **Domain 3, models and projections.** Actors: CMIP5/CMIP6 GCMs, EMICs, CESM hosing. Currency: percentage weakening and freshwater thresholds. Anchor: the ground pays only where a model reproduces observed freshwater transport; CMIP3/5/6 have a known sign bias. Admitted with the bias recorded.
- **Domain 4, paleo and reconstruction records.** Actors: Dansgaard-Oeschger and Heinrich records, corals, sediments, ice cores. Currency: inferred AMOC strength over centuries to millennia. Anchor: geological archive does not update, but dating and transfer functions are instruments with limits. Admitted.
- **Domain 5, assessment and expert judgment.** Actors: assessors, elicitation participants, confidence language. Currency: "very likely", "medium confidence". Anchor: the underlying ground, never the consensus. Admitted as budget filter only (Fig 9 :60).
- **Rejected candidates.** A single global stability index has no nameable anchor and is not a domain yet. The subpolar gyre is a separate slot, not the whole AMOC.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Domain 1 overlaps Domain 2 at validation, Domain 1 overlaps Domain 3 at observational constraints, Domain 3 overlaps Domain 4 at paleo calibration, Domain 2 overlaps Domain 5 at confidence language (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (can the array resolve transport?), vocabulary (do we hold FovS as well as volume transport?), objective (what counts as stable?), planning, learn and carry. The middle link is where the chain breaks, because "stable" has no instrument (Fig 3 :24-27).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's six-term physical substrate to salinity and overturning only fit with a strain, so Fig 13 governs the honest stop here, not the physical decomposition. One live strain remains: the operand type of the comparison at the threshold level (S4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

AMOC stability is not one scalar. The figures return a shape with three separable conditions (trend, variability, threshold), each indexed per observing slot and each with its own instrument and refuter. Direct transport arrays are the anchors; fingerprints, gyre indices, carbon-sink anomalies and confidence labels are instruments carrying zero verdict weight until provenance separates them. The three conditions are isomers over a shared atom set, so a single stability index loses the bond that carries the information. Climate models are too stable inside their own closure, and the only operator that moves that wall is observation-constrained import with a source gate and a shadow test. The distance to a tipping point is reachable as a shape, the freshwater-transport minimum at 34°S, but not as a number at this budget. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the domain claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a consensus or a collapse date instead of a refutable shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A wrong imported atom, for example a single index treated as an anchor, costs more than starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24).
- **undoable:** yes. The numerical claims are removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. Sustained 34°S and 26°N records can close the gap. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating observation that indexes distance to threshold with validated uncertainty; an unconstrained model that reproduces the observed 34°S freshwater-transport sign and still projects only small weakening; a demonstration that all Atlantic channels return one state vector so the union collapses; or convergent derivation by two frames with genuinely disjoint evidence. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
