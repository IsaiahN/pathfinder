## Independent domain census

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I deliberately started from different figures than the primary (Fig 9 union split and Fig 1 per-slot indexing rather than Fig 13 honest stop and Fig 6 import) to test whether the same domain boundaries emerge from a low-overlap route inside the framework.

Arity: the threshold verdict is three-place (transport, critical value, freshwater control), the other two are two-place, and all are indexed per slot. Symmetry: ordered and hysteretic; adding freshwater is not the reverse of removing it. Scale: days to decades, 2004 to present, 1900 to present, millennia, and multi-millennial model runs. Fig 9 :4-5 applies: several well-formed questions, not one hard one.
- **Domain 1, direct observing arrays.** Actors: RAPID, OSNAP, SAMBA, Florida Current cable, Argo. Currency: transports in Sverdrups. Anchor: measured transport, a counter that does not update on a model's opinion. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, indirect indices.** Actors: SST cold blob, gyre index, carbon sink, sea level, heat-flux products. Currency: correlation with state. Anchor: none on its own. Admitted as instrument only.
- **Domain 3, models.** Actors: CMIP5/6, EMICs, CESM hosing. Currency: weakening percentage and threshold in Sv. Anchor: biased but nameable, the observed freshwater transport. Admitted with the bias recorded.
- **Domain 4, paleo and reconstructions.** Actors: ice-core, coral, sediment records. Currency: inferred strength over centuries to millennia. Anchor: archive does not update; transfer function is an instrument. Admitted.
- **Domain 5, assessment.** Actors: assessors and confidence language. Currency: likelihood labels. Anchor: none independent; the ground is the underlying record. Admitted as budget filter only.
- **Rejected:** a single global stability index, no nameable anchor; the subpolar gyre as the whole AMOC, re-slotted as a separate domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1 and D2 overlap at validation, D1 and D3 at constraints, D3 and D4 at paleo calibration, D2 and D5 at confidence language (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The objective link breaks first because "stable" has no instrument (Fig 3 :24-27).

My census matches the primary's five domains, its rejection of the scalar, and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 13's honest stop and Fig 6's import, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 1's per-slot indexing. Different starting figures, same boundaries. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: stability is three separable conditions; direct transport is the anchor; models are too stable; the threshold distance is a shape, not a number.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claims: a collapse date near 2065 with 95% confidence 2037 to 2109, or a "the AMOC is 15% weaker and collapsing" headline.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test** (Fig 2 :29-30), replaced by a confidence label or a proxy correlation that moves when a model changes;
- the **evidence-pool provenance** (Fig 8 :50-52), erased when two CMIP6-based groups are counted as two witnesses;
- the **gap itself** (Fig 13 :134), overwritten by a number the figures do not return.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: direct transport arrays are counters and the three stability conditions are different bonds over a shared atom set. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this work; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
