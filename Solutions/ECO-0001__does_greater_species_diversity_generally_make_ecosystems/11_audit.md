## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. My starting figure was Fig 11, not the primary's Fig 9, so the route differs even though the evidence pool does not.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the non-shrinking disagreement over whether a diversity count predicts stability. Arity: three conditions, namely the stability property and perturbation, the cast and its bonds, and the habitat with its conditions and cascades. Symmetry: the count is permutation-invariant (the symmetry of a conjunction under Fig 12), while the mechanism is ordered (who eats whom). Scale: one global number versus per-species and per-interaction signals, with effects second and third order out. This is a **union compound** by Fig 9 :5 and must be unbundled.
- **Domain 1, empirical stability frame.** Actors: species, populations, perturbations, experimenters. Currency: variance, resistance, resilience, return rate. Anchor: the time series and the experiment. Union of sub-conditions.
- **Domain 2, interaction frame.** Actors: species pairs and interaction signs and strengths. Currency: stability criteria. Anchor: the matrix criterion plus experiment. Union with Domain 1 through shared data.
- **Domain 3, habitat frame.** Actors, conditions and relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground. Union with both above, which it contains.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Expert readers and public venues. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence zero in this simulated run.
- **Quantity not domain:** species richness S. No anchor, so it cannot settle anything.

Order by dependency: name the anchor before comparing counts; enumerate the habitat before judging identity; evaluate S third; verify against the ground fourth.

My census matches the primary's three ecological domains, one verification domain, and the union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9's split rule and non-shrinking disagreement, I derived it from Fig 11's enumeration rule: if the habitat contains conditions and relations that a count cannot represent, the question cannot be one question about a count. Different starting figures, same boundary. That is low overlap between routes inside one framework, which is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.
