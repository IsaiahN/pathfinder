## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the final term. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is not a yes or a no. It is a three-part decomposition: name the anchor (which stability property and perturbation), enumerate the habitat (actors, conditions, relations), then evaluate the count inside that habitat. A single number cannot occupy that shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is exactly the ecology question. The shape is obtained by splitting the bundle, not by searching harder for a single general rule.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question that means: state what you would expect to observe if the union verdict were true, before naming the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical rule says the framework stops where instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage so the stop is honest.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For ECO-0001 this matters because the figure term "capability" is being bound to the ecology term "stability". That binding is a choice made visible, not a discovery, and it is recorded as `specified` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F4. No essay licenses calling the binding `grounded`.

## 4. Metamerism: why a single number cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a diversity count is a reading. The same count can arise from communities with entirely different interaction structures, conditions and cascade pathways. Naming "stability" from the count alone is the metamerism error: several sources, one projection. What is owed is a better instrument, that is, a measured stability property and an enumerated habitat, not a name. Different mechanisms (asynchrony, overyielding, reduced competition, interaction sign, ecosystem engineering) can produce the same aggregate stability reading.

## 5. Q: what shape does the answer to ECO-0001 have?

**Answered.** The shape is a decomposition with three required slots and a prepublished refuter:

1. **Anchor slot:** which stability property, which perturbation, which timescale. Until filled, no ground can settle the question (Fig 2 :29, Fig 9 :27-28).
2. **Habitat slot:** actors, conditions and relations, enumerated outward until cascades stop mattering (Fig 11 :43-52). This is the slot the diversity count leaves empty.
3. **Bond slot:** the interaction structure among the species present. Same species set with different bonds is a different substance (Fig 12 :117). Identity alone is the ingredient list, not the molecule.

The verdict then takes the form: "under anchor A and habitat H, varying S changes property P by M, for as long as V is held fixed". It is a `specified` shape. It does not say which sign M has until the ground pays, and it does not claim that a general law is impossible (Fig 8 :39).

## 5b. Correction to my own reading

I initially considered writing that the framework *forbids* naming diversity or stability as the answer. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Species richness, interaction and habitat are already in the ecology frame; naming them is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong in the pooled literature is treating the binding as if it were a derivation, and reading the source off a single projection. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This matches correction §5b of the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous ecologists to agree with the decomposition is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a measurement settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
