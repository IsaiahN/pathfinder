## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The positive empirical result, with its mechanism named.** Tilman et al. 2006 find greater temporal stability at higher plant diversity and attribute it to *"portfolio (statistical averaging) and overyielding effects"*, with no covariance effect. Read through the figures, this is a within-condition result: it holds for annual aboveground plant production in one experimental grassland system under the climate variation observed there. It is not a general law, and the statistical-averaging mechanism is a different quantity from the Fig 1 search signal. Both are kept.
2. **The sign depends on interaction type.** Allesina and Tang 2012 extend May's random-network result to defined interaction types and find predator-prey stabilizing and mutualistic and competitive destabilizing. They also find that stability *"decreases when a realistic food web structure is imposed"*. This is the strongest low-overlap corroboration of the figure claim that valence is relative to the residual and that the bond is not recoverable from the ingredient list.
3. **The anchor is not one thing.** Ives and Carpenter 2007 say the notion of stability is multifaceted and that diversity-stability relationships *"cannot be understood outside the context of the environmental drivers affecting both"*. The NSF summary of that work goes further: *"stable isn't necessarily good"*. This corroborates Fig 2 :29 and Fig 9 :27-28.
4. **More than one facet of biodiversity matters.** Craven et al. 2018 find that richness, phylogenetic diversity and functional-trait composition act through different pathways and conclude that the relationship is *"more multicausal"* than acknowledged. This corroborates the union reading.
5. **Habitat modification is a real mechanism.** Jones et al. 1994 define engineers as organisms that modulate resource availability by causing physical state changes and thereby *"modify, maintain and create habitats"*. This is the source the figures cite at Fig 11 :65, and it is why cascade order belongs in the answer.
6. **Mechanisms are still contested.** Loreau and de Mazancourt 2013 identify asynchrony, differential response speed and reduced competition as the three main stabilising mechanisms, and call for trait-based approaches. This corroborates F3 and F7 without settling the verdict.

No source conflicts with the figure verdict. A conflict would send the derivation back to the domain census; none was triggered. The essays in `05_what_shape_means.md` share an author with the framework, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight. The ecology sources are from a different pool and are used only as corroboration.
