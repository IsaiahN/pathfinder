## Headline verdict

The figures do not license either option as the question is posed. The question is a **union compound** of at least three conditions: which stability property and perturbation is the anchor, which species and interaction bonds are present, and the habitat of conditions and relations that carries contact and cascades. A species count is a symmetric aggregate over slots, and Fig 1 :22 says aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so no species is stabilising in general. Within a specified habitat and metric, more species often stabilises an aggregate property, but that is a within-condition result, not a general law.
