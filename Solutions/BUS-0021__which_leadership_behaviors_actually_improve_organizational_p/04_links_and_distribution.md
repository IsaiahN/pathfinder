## What in the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The field's own methods literature already refuses the standing list.** Antonakis et al. (2010) treat common-method variance and endogeneity as making an estimate uninterpretable, not merely imprecise, and review six designs that recover a counterfactual in field settings. This is the import that moves the wall for this question (Fig 6 :23).
2. **The positive correlations are real but bounded, and are not verdicts.** Judge and Piccolo (2004) find a .44 overall validity and simultaneously that transformational leadership failed to predict leader job performance and correlates .80 with contingent reward. High overlap among the "behaviors" is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **The measurement link is the one without an instrument.** The 2025 meta-analysis reports 83 percent cross-sectional designs and one experimental study among working populations. This is Fig 3 :27 in the field's own words.
4. **Context dependence is corroborated outside management.** The animal-behaviour sources find that who leads changes with the current need, the time scale and the decision context, and that dominance is independent of leadership in pigeons. That is the world-level counterpart of Fig 11 :49-51. It is corroboration, not authority; the figure decides.
5. **No world source overrides the figure verdict.** The statistics and design sources explain *why* a shared reading cannot be a verdict; they do not supply a behavior name. The conflict rule was not triggered.

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and the refuter; offer links after.
