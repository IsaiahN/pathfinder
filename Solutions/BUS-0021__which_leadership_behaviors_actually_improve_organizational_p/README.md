## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not a list of behaviors but the difference between two relations. Let a unit (a team, a store, a firm at a time) be a slot s. The research frame's library Γ predicts a performance return Γ(b,a); the world returns o'. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is measured per slot, never averaged across slots (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "rank behaviors by their measured correlation with performance and publish the winners": leads to a convention whose metrics are frame-internal, because the same instruments that propose the behavior also certify the performance (Fig 1 :27).
- Option B, "a behavior counts as improving performance only when the outcome moves under exogenous variation of the behavior and the outcome was read by a source that could not see the behavior": leads to a per-slot, habitat-relative claim with an instrument and a stated refuter (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :39).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* stating the two conditions that must hold before "improves" can be said (independent readings and an anchor that does not update), plus an instrument that discriminates a real effect from a shared cause. Until bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` are kept per domain and never averaged. The standing list is a `gap`; the separator is `specified` and triangulated.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles which leadership behaviors work, and no claim that the framework is the only account of leadership and performance.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
