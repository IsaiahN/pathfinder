# Outreach draft - validate the shape first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 is stated in the recipient's own terms. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one shape claim about resilience before you decide whether the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm working on a question in ecological theory and have one specific output I'd like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** asked whether a general theory can explain when ecological networks are resilient, the derivation refuses the either/or. The question is a union of two conditions, not a partition into "general" and "context":
>
> 1. a channel to a ground that does not update on the network's own report, so recovery can be read per species or function slot rather than as one pooled score;
> 2. contact with a closure that differs from the network's own, so the network has terms for a residual that changed.
>
> Both must hold. One lever clears the region where only one holds and leaves the intersection standing. The capacity to absorb a shock is a property of network-and-habitat, never of the network alone, and an actor's valence is relative to the residual: the same predator or mutualist changes sign when the perturbation or the measured function changes. So there is no standing list of resilience-conferring traits. Which structural feature occupies the slot is left open, with provenance owed to the frame that supplies it.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this settles the diversity-stability debate, that a particular network metric is the general predictor, or that context dependence means nothing general can be said. The figures return a shape and a route, not a name and not a result. The specific trait inventory is recorded as a gap, with the instrument that would close it characterized in effect terms but not held.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a structural invariant that predicts slot-level recovery across two documented habitat changes and two different residuals, with the habitat contribution measured separately and found to be zero;
> 2. one trait whose sign is invariant across two independently measured residuals;
> 3. a single lever that clears both conditions with no categorically different residue, or evidence that the two conditions are independent with no overlap;
> 4. an aggregated resilience index that predicts a slot-level failure earlier than the slot readings themselves, with those readings withheld from the fit;
> 5. a failure that persists after the indicator is replaced by one resolving the previously saturated range and all readings are kept unpooled.
>
> I wrote these down before running this. If any one holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md`
> - Eight-step formula and mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a network you know well and can check against monitoring data. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Shape claim and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Standing instruction: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No network metric named as the predictor | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The canonical README limits the framework to *"a shape, not a name."* |
| Per-slot framing rather than a global index | Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| Union, not either/or | Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* |
| Valence declared residual-relative | Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask anyone to endorse it. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. |

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A method may cross up; a recording of one success may not.

A Stage 1 failure is the desired outcome if the shape is wrong. It is more informative than an endorsement, and Fig 2 :42 says to *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
