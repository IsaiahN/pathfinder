# The Tether's answer on ecological network resilience

**Mode: `specified` on the ecological claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the trait inventory. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether a general theory can explain when ecological networks are resilient, or whether resilience depends too strongly on context. Restated in effect terms: after a perturbation a, the network's prediction Γ(b,a) for each tracked slot s is compared with the habitat's return o′, giving R_s(a,t) = |Γ(b,a)_s(t) − o′_s(t)|. Resilience is the keeping and shrinking of R_s across slots, not a single pooled number (Fig 1 :19, :22).

The two offered answers are a bundled union, not a partition (Fig 9 :55, :57). Asking which one is right is like asking whether a machine fails because of its wiring or because of its power supply when both conditions must hold and they overlap.

## 2. The shape

1. **A general theory is possible, but only as a shape of conditions, not as a theory of the network alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Substituting the ecological cast, a network's capacity to absorb a shock is a property of network-and-habitat. Adding an interaction that does not change contact with the ground changes nothing the ground pays for (Fig 11 :40).

2. **Two conditions must both hold.** C1: a maintained channel to a ground that does not update on the network's own report, so recovery is readable per slot. The anchor is the measurement, not the model (Fig 2 :29). C2: contact with a closure that differs from the network's own, so the network has terms for a residual that changed (Fig 6 :23). One lever clears the region where only one holds and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55-58).

3. **There is no standing list of resilience-conferring traits.** An actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The same predator, mutualist, module or diversity level changes sign when the perturbation or the measured function changes. Allesina and Tang 2012 supply a low-overlap witness: predator-prey interactions stabilizing and mutualistic or competitive interactions destabilizing under one assumption set, with realistic structure sometimes decreasing stability.

4. **Context dependence has a specific mechanism: truncated contact.** Fig 11 :52: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43), and it is not the ground: it updates, while the ground settles and does not (Fig 11 :73).

5. **A large part of what is called context is a channel failure with a different remedy.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 10 :26 lists *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* Those are instrument problems, not limits on what can be general. The test in `01` F6 separates them from genuine context dependence.

6. **The specific trait inventory is a declared gap.** Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 13 :118: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."* A general shape is reachable; an exhaustive catalogue of which trait confers resilience in which network is not reachable by rearranging the current library. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The gap is unreached with the library as it stands, not proved impossible and not hidden.

7. **The framework does not name the slot.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Modularity, connectance, redundancy and response diversity are already ecology terms. Naming one as *the* mechanism is a binding from the ecology frame, owed provenance and a source-discriminating instrument, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall.

8. **Over a fixed species set, differently wired networks are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* When the species set changes, the relation is composition plus import, not isomerism. The fixed-atom condition is a live strain and is named in `06` and `11`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one side of the either/or wins. The union is the verdict.
- Not that diversity, connectance, modularity or any other metric is *the* general predictor. No figure line names one, and Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list.
- Not that context dependence is merely bad measurement. Sometimes it is (F6), and the refuter distinguishes the cases.
- Not that an aggregated resilience index is the ground. It is a budget filter at best (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the habitat is the ground. The habitat updates; the ground does not (Fig 11 :73).
- Not `grounded`. No monitoring campaign was run to settle left(R,φ) for any proposed term.
- Not a claim that this framework is the only true account of resilience. Fig 8 :37: *"A frame cannot certify its own limit."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Recovery of what, to what?** State the slot and the perturbation before measuring. Carpenter et al. 2001: *"To assess a system's resilience, one must specify which system configuration and which disturbances are of interest."*
- **Source discrimination.** Can the measurement distinguish a redundant group taking over, a slow variable that never crossed its threshold, a refuge maintaining contact, and a transient that was simply missed? If not, the instrument is inadequate, not the phenomenon unexplainable.
- **Channel test.** Does the failure persist after replacing the indicator with one that resolves the previously saturated range and keeping all readings unpooled?
- **Union test.** Are C1 and C2 independent with no overlap in a documented case, or does one lever clear both with no categorically different residue?
- **Valence test.** Is there one trait whose sign is invariant across two independently measured residuals?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §5: the seven conditions written before the run.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The ecological terms used in this folder are a visible guess at the field's compression axes (see *They're Scanning Books* in `05` §3). Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the instruments cannot discriminate among sources and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

What remains open is not a hole to be filled with a plausible metric. It is owed to a frame with a differing closure, brought in with a source gate and a shadow test, and then paid against the ground.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side between a general theory of resilience and irreducible context dependence. It says the question is a union of two conditions that overlap: a channel to a measurement that does not update on the network's own report, so recovery is read per species or function slot, and contact with a closure that differs from the network's own, so the network has terms for a changed residual. Capacity is a property of network-and-habitat, never of the network alone, and no standing list of resilience-conferring traits survives a change of residual: the same predator or mutualist changes sign when the perturbation or the measured function changes. Much of what looks like context dependence is a channel failure, stale or pooled readings, and has an instrument remedy; the rest is a genuine cascade that was scoped too narrowly. A general shape is available, but the specific trait inventory is not derivable from it and is recorded as a gap with an instrument characterized in effect terms. What the account offers is that shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the conditions under which the question would have to be rebuilt. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
