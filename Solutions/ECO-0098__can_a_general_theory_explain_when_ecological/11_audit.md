## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before relying on the primary's conclusions. The line given was: can a general theory explain when ecological networks are resilient, or does resilience depend too strongly on context?

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity is four-place: network structure, perturbation, habitat conditions, observed response, indexed by slot and ordered in time. Symmetry: "resilient" is a threshold comparison against a baseline and a shock, not an intrinsic quantity; a pooled score discards the operands (Fig 12 :87). Scale: population slot, network, ecosystem and the lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :19-20, :27). So the residual cannot be a single global number, and the question cannot be settled at one scale without measuring the round trip.
- **D1 Network composition.** Actors: species, links, strengths, modules. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ), flows. Anchor: the measured recovery of each slot, outside the graph. Status admitted, because an anchor is nameable.
- **D2 Habitat and context.** Actors: conditions and relations read off the world (Fig 11 :45-48). Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not update. Status admitted.
- **D3 Perturbation and channel.** Actors: the shock, indicators, sampling design. Currency: R per slot. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does (Fig 10 :24). Status admitted.
- **D4 Theory and verification.** Actors: modellers, meta-analyses, peer frames. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: experiment and monitoring data. Status admitted. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so D4's independence is zero.
- **X1 Resilience as a property of the network alone.** No nameable anchor independent of measurement. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 Context alone.** Already inside D2. Absorbed.

Question type: union, not partition. The two operative conditions C1 and C2 overlap, because a monitoring programme is itself part of the habitat and can change reach, and a new interaction can change what the ground pays for. Dependency order: perceive R per slot, acquire terms for what the shock affords and what recovery would count as, state the objective, choose a route, name what worked and carry it. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as *"gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))"*, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39, :49, :51, :52, :73 (habitat, valence, cascade), Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 13 :115 (closed set, open arrangements).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2: two conditions must hold; capacity is network-and-habitat; no standing trait list; trait inventory is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field claim, for example "this reef network is resilient because it has high functional redundancy."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The field claim names a network-level property; the shorthand requires recovery of what, to what, per slot. A redundancy score has thrown away the operand.
- **Residual-relative valence.** The shorthand says redundancy changes sign with the residual; the field claim treats it as a property carried by the network. The source instrument that would discriminate a redundancy takeover from a slow variable that never crossed is not present.
- **The union.** The shorthand requires both C1 and C2; the field claim has one lever. The intersection is left standing.
- **The gap.** The shorthand reports the trait inventory as unreached; the field claim fills the slot with a name and loses the provenance and the refuter.

One thing survives cleanly: capacity is network-and-habitat, so an intervention that changes the graph without checking contact has not bought the result. That is the residue worth sending.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
