# WATSON answer, ECO-0098

**Mode: `specified` on the ecological claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the trait inventory. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No ecological measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Can a general theory explain when ecological networks are resilient, or does resilience depend too strongly on context?

Restated as an effect: why does R_s(a,t) = |Γ(b,a)_s(t) − o′_s(t)| shrink back toward zero after a perturbation in some slots and keep growing in others, and is that difference predictable from network structure alone? R is indexed per species, function or patch, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). The anchor is measured recovery, which does not update on what any model thinks (Fig 2 :29).

Option A predicts a small structural term set that works across habitats. Option B predicts that only case descriptions survive. What the derivation binds as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction") is a shape: the conditions that must both hold for a slot's residual to shrink, plus an instrument that distinguishes a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a multi-place relation among network structure, perturbation, habitat conditions and observed response, indexed by slot and ordered in time. Symmetry: "resilient" is a threshold relative to a shock and a baseline, not an intrinsic quantity (Fig 12 :87). Scale: population slot, network, ecosystem and cross-scale membrane, with a lossy transform between scales (Fig 4).

Union, not partition. D1 and D2 form a compound, and D3 and D4 form another. The two operative conditions are C1 (maintained channel to a non-updating ground) and C2 (contact with a differing closure). They overlap, so a single lever leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55, :57). Dependency order: perceive R per slot, acquire terms for what the perturbation affords and what recovery would count as, state the objective, choose a route, name what worked and carry it (Fig 3 :19-21).

Strain test: Fig 11 governs contact and valence, Fig 2 governs the anchor, Fig 13 governs the minimal generative set. An apparent conflict between them was returned to census and dissolved as three different objects. No figure line was stretched.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The verdict is neither option as stated. A general theory of ecological network resilience is possible as a two-condition shape: a maintained channel to a non-updating ground with recovery indexed per slot, plus contact with a differing closure so the network has terms for a changed residual. Capacity is network-and-habitat, never network alone; no standing trait list survives a change of residual; and a large part of context dependence is a channel failure with an instrument remedy, while the rest is a cascade that was scoped too narrowly. The specific trait inventory is a declared `gap`, unreached with the current library, not hidden and not proved impossible. Over a fixed species set the differently wired networks are isomers; when the set changes they are a composition plus import. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** a union treated as a partition gets one lever aimed at one condition while the intersection stands (Fig 9 :55-58), so a recovery intervention is bought that does not pay at the ground. A pooled index can hide which slot is about to fail (Fig 1 :22). In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape, and independence is spent by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices this: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is real but bounded and reversible if the packet leads with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The conditions, the seven falsifiers and the provenance are recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). The live fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim can be narrowed without withdrawing the union verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A structural invariant that predicts slot recovery across two habitat changes and two residuals with the habitat contribution measured at zero would break F2. An invariant trait sign across two independently measured residuals would break F3. Evidence that C1 and C2 are independent with no overlap, or one lever clearing both, would break the union. A pooled index predicting slot failure with those readings withheld would break F5. A source-discriminating instrument that closes the gap would supersede F8, and a derivation of one condition from the other would collapse the union. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
