## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *level* of resilience but whether the network's residual, after a perturbation, keeps shrinking toward zero for each tracked function or species slot. In effect terms: a network frame Γ predicts a response Γ(b,a) to perturbation a; the habitat returns o′; R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is measured per slot s, not averaged across species or functions (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). A network is resilient when R stays small and trends back down across those slots after a shock. The anchor is the measured recovery, not the model's own report (Fig 2 :29).

- Option A, "a general theory explains resilience": leads to the prediction that a small set of structural terms predicts the return of R across habitats, so the shape of recovery is derivable from network composition alone.
- Option B, "resilience depends too strongly on context": leads to the prediction that resilience is a property of network-and-habitat, and that the same structural feature changes sign when the residual changes (Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :49, Fig 11 :51).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape* naming the conditions that must both hold for a slot's R to shrink after a shock, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. That shape is the sought-for term; until it is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` refuses the either/or as a bundled union and keeps per-domain verdicts separate, never averaged. The structural shape is `general`; the ecological application is `specified`; the inventory of resilience-conferring traits is a declared `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the ecological question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of resilience.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
