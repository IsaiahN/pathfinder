## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The complexity-stability result is conditional, not universal.** May 1972 derives a sharp stability-instability transition in random networks; Cohen and Newman 1985 show that simple counter-examples falsify the unconditional claim. This is the model of a general statement in ecology that is real but domain-indexed, which is exactly the shape returned in `07` and `09`. It corroborates F7, not Option B's "no general statement."
2. **Interaction type changes the sign.** Allesina and Tang 2012 find predator-prey stabilizing and mutualistic or competitive destabilizing under one assumption set, and report that realistic food-web structure can *decrease* stability. This is valence relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51) from an independent evidence pool. It corroborates F3.
3. **Resilience needs a specified object and disturbance.** Carpenter et al. 2001 and Holling and Walker 2003 both make "resilience of what to what" and an empirical context-dependent definition the condition for measurement. This corroborates F5 and the channel diagnosis in F6, and it explains why a pooled resilience index is the wrong ground.
4. **The review literature records partial success, not a settled theory.** MathisonSlee et al. 2022 found all fourteen Walker et al. propositions mentioned, some tested, some critiqued, and concluded that the source article is *"no longer the authority on this topic."* That is the room-chain rule in practice (Fig 7 :12-13, agreement compounds across a field while the ground decides). It corroborates F9's filter rule.
5. **"Beyond panaceas" is the institutional form of the same result.** Ostrom 2007 refuses universal prescriptions and supplies a multitier diagnostic framework. The framework author's own provenance note in *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* records that Ostrom's work was encountered from a multi-agent systems direction after the structural rhyming was found. That is a convergent derivation, not an adopted import, but only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-52), so the record is stated here rather than the convergence being claimed as corroboration.
