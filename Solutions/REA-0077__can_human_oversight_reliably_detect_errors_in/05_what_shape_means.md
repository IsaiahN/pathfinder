They are used here only to fix what the framework's own word "shape" means, not to decide the oversight question. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` come from outside that pool.

## 1. A shape is a constraint stated before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."

For this question a shape is therefore **not** a reliability percentage and **not** a yes or no. It is the set of constraints that any settled answer must satisfy: legitimate ground, independent checker, per-slot residuals, a refusal operator, a maintained channel, and an unbundled union. It constrains; it does not fill.

The essay also names the stopping condition: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." That is why the pre-registered refuter in `01` is written first: it is the only part of this package that is not a flattering preference.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the target, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. The problem determines what counts as relevant: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not." So the residual selects the frame, not the other way round.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the direct warrant for the two-stage outreach: send the expected failure, not the framework.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "Contact is Distinction Resolver". A measurement answers more than one question at once because several questions are undetermined at the same joint. For oversight, one experiment can settle the anchor question, the independence question and the vocabulary question together.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual in `01` is described before the field of human oversight research is entered.

## 3. Why a scalar "reliability" is the forbidden form here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Several distinct sources can produce one reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that settled it.

An oversight log that reports "95 percent of errors caught" is a reading of green. It cannot say whether the errors caught were the ones that matter, whether the reviewer was above or below the system on those slots, or whether the ground was ever reachable. Several very different arrangements (independent frame with a ground; single shared pool with a rubric; lucky sample) give the same aggregate. Naming one of them from the aggregate is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a coefficient.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the same point: "A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator." An oversight record is an output. It does not contain the generator that produced the detection, so the record cannot certify the detector. The essay's closing warning is directly applicable: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Declaring a detection metric makes the metric's choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. What licenses a name, and why this solution does not name a person or a number

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The essays add three cautions that matter here:

- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." Adding reviewers who draw on the same evidence does not add a frame. It also says "Votes come from outcomes, not popularity" and "Use the success rate per try, not the total", which is the per-slot rule in another vocabulary.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." That is the shelf life of oversight independence in psychological language, and it is why Fig 10 :24 says the channel decays.
- *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A better rubric is a new term; an independent instrument that can see an error class the reviewer's primitives cannot represent is a new dimension. Only the second can close the superhuman-task gap.

## 5. Correction to my own §3: the "shape, not a name" rule is narrower than I first wrote it

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming anything concrete. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in the library. Naming a ground (a verified dataset, a counter, an experiment) is not inventing a missing primitive; it is a binding from the task frame onto an ordinary piece of the arrangement. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework itself selects that ground, or that the binding moves the wall.

My §3 metamerism argument is **not** retracted. An aggregate oversight reading still does not determine which arrangement produced it. The corrected reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming. Both readings are kept so neither is quietly lost, matching the exemplar's own correction practice.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous reviewers who agree with the shape claim is therefore the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture post is blunter: LLM outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not tests of the oversight claim and cannot settle it. No measurement was taken in producing them.

## 7. What shape means, in one sentence for this question

A shape here is the pre-stated, per-slot, ground-anchored form of an oversight arrangement that could be reliable, stated before any number is computed, refusable by a written criterion, and stopped honestly where the available instruments can no longer distinguish one cause of a reading from another.
