# Solution REA-0077: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Can human oversight reliably detect errors in systems that perform better than humans on some tasks but worse on others?

**Source:** `Questions/reasoning.txt` line 94 (question 77 in the source file).

**Section:** 8. Human and machine intelligence.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No claim here is `grounded`, because no measurement in this package settles the question.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (registry is maintained outside this folder).

**Field for public contact points:** human oversight research.

## What is being decided, in effect terms

Option A: human oversight can reliably detect the errors of a system that is superhuman on some tasks and subhuman on others. Option B: that claim cannot be sustained as stated, because "human oversight" pools several conditions that have different grounds, and because human judgment is an instrument for correctness rather than the anchor for it. What each leads to: A leads to a deployment rule that treats a person's review as the check, and it spends the independence of the reviewer at exactly the tasks where the reviewer's reading is weakest. B leads to a decomposed requirement, a shape for an oversight arrangement, and a named ground that could still reverse it.

An answer would look like a per-slot detection claim with a stated ground, a stated checker independence, a stated refuter, and a mode. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## Boundary and honesty

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. The framework's own essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is "simulacra" and that "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Consequently this package cannot settle the question it reports on. It can say what shape a settled answer would have and what would reverse the shape. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6.

Nothing in this folder was written outside the folder. Figure citations are against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`; quoted strings are verbatim from that extract, and citations use `Fig N :line`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional submission or engagement routes.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement. Agreement is a convention, not a witness (Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan. Contact is one to one and opt in.
- No claim that this simulation is a ground reading. It is not a test of the claim.
