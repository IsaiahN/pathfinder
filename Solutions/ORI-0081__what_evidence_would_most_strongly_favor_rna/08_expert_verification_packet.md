# Expert verification packet: the Tether on RNA-first vs metabolism-first

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** No discriminating experiment is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a verdict for any closure.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three-way comparison (RNA-first, metabolism-first, hybrid) is not one question and the hybrid is not a third peer alternative; it is a union of the other two conditions. The evidence that could decide among them must be a ground anchor rather than agreement, and it must have a specific shape: a closed loop, a part that survives its own use, and heritable variation selection can act on, together with the capacity to discriminate historical sources from a shared modern reading. Rendered as per-closure predictions: an evolvable RNA-only system favors RNA-first; an evolvable autocatalytic network without a genetic polymer favors metabolism-first; a system that loses evolvability when either polymer or network is removed favors the hybrid. No closure is selected and the historical verdict is `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 2

- **Statement:** the three-way question must be split, and hybrid is a union compound, not a midpoint. The discriminating evidence must be an anchor, not agreement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
- **Refuter:** sustained work on one subset makes the three-way disagreement shrink (then it was one hard question); or an anchor that moves with frame preference, such as consensus or prestige, is accepted as decisive.

### C2: science-facing; checkable against the origins-of-life literature

- **Statement:** modern molecular features do not discriminate the three histories. The ribosome's RNA core, nucleotide cofactors (ATP, NAD, FAD) and the universal genetic code are readings that RNA-first, metabolism-first-with-later-adoption, and hybrid can each retrodict. Therefore another retrodiction favors nobody; a source-discriminating measurement is required.
- **What to check:** can you name, from your own field, an observation that distinguishes an RNA-first history from a metabolism-first history that later adopted RNA, using only a modern feature? If yes, that is the discriminator this claim says is missing.
- **World sources, read:** RNA world review (Wikipedia, 2026): *"even proponents agree that there is still not conclusive evidence to completely falsify other paradigms and hypotheses"* and *"Opinions differ... as to whether RNA constituted the first autonomous self-replicating system or was a derivative of a still-earlier system."* Nogal et al. 2023, *Chem Soc Rev*: *"extant life features/signatures could also be misleading in certain aspects."*
- **Refuter:** name the discriminating instrument or observation and show it is falsifiable. If one exists, this sub-claim narrows from "missing" to "available but unbuilt."

### C3: framework-internal derivation with a world check

- **Statement:** the shape of the discriminating observation is a closed loop plus a surviving catalyst plus heritable variation, and the hybrid must be priced in both description bits and experimental actions.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :125-126 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
- **World check:** the abiogenesis review reports NASA's definition of life as *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution"* and notes *"The transition from non-life to life has not been observed experimentally."* Hordijk and Steel 2018 (*Life*) state *"Autocatalytic sets are a necessary (although not sufficient) condition for life-like behavior"* and ask whether autocatalytic sets can be made with both RNA and peptides together. Proto-metabolism review: *"There is limited experimental evidence for these theories"* (metabolism-first).
- **Refuter:** define "self-sustaining evolvable" so loosely that a plain autocatalytic reaction, a single replicator and a modern cell all qualify, or show that the hybrid's union adds no surplus over one pure closure. Then the shape loses discriminating power and is withdrawn.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating ground reading, not another argument:

1. An RNA-only system undergoing Darwinian evolution with no metabolic network (favors A).
2. An autocatalytic network with heritable, selectable variation and no genetic polymer (favors B).
3. A system whose evolvability vanishes when either polymer or network is removed (favors C).
4. A discriminating reading of the ancient record that separates the three histories from a shared modern feature.
