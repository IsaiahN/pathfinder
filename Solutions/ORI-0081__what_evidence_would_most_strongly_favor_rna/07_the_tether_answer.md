# The Tether's answer: what evidence would favor RNA-first, metabolism-first, or a hybrid

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Three closures claim the transition from prebiotic chemistry to the first self-sustaining, heritable, evolvable system. The question asks which evidence would favor one. The figure-grounded answer is a **shape for the discriminating evidence**, not a verdict for a closure: a description of the observation that would range against the ground and separate the three, written before we can name the exact instrument that delivers it.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the question; the hybrid is a union compound.** Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* RNA-first and metabolism-first each keep working on a different subset; hybrid is their intersection, to be tested where both conditions bind, not pursued as a midpoint.

2. **The discriminating evidence must be an anchor, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Consensus, publication counts, and "most favored paradigm" are filters for the budget (Fig 9 :60), not witnesses for the verdict.

3. **It must register a closed loop, a surviving catalyst, and heritable variation.** Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* This is the shape; the framework does not name the molecule.

4. **It must discriminate historical sources.** Modern readings (the ribosome's RNA core, nucleotide cofactors, the universal code) are compatible with all three closures. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Another retrodiction favors nobody; what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument.

5. **Per-closure predictions, kept separate and never averaged.**
   - RNA-first favored by: an RNA-only system that self-replicates with heritable variation and Darwinian evolution, with no metabolism-like network required.
   - Metabolism-first favored by: an autocatalytic network that self-sustains and shows heritable, selectable variation without any genetic polymer.
   - Hybrid favored by: an evolvable system requiring both in mutual dependence, losing evolvability if either is removed, with a surplus neither alone has.
   All three are `specified`, ungrounded. None is asserted as demonstrated.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a winner. No closure is selected; the historical verdict is `gap`.
- Not a named experiment. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not that more reviews will decide it. The literature has more reviews and still no discriminating observation.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not a claim that the three closures are symmetric options. Hybrid is a union compound (§2.1).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument or experiment, not a further argument. Concretely:

- An RNA-only system that undergoes Darwinian evolution with no metabolic network: favors A.
- An autocatalytic network with heritable, selectable variation and no genetic polymer: favors B.
- A system whose evolvability disappears when either the polymer or the network is removed: favors C.
- A discriminating reading of the ancient record that separates the three histories from a shared modern feature: favors whichever it separates.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a single non-discriminating body of evidence shown to settle priority; an anchor accepted that moves with preference; a system where all three are true without a union surplus; work on one subset that shrinks the three-way disagreement.

Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The transition from non-life to life has not been observed, and the field's own reviews say no paradigm can yet be conclusively falsified. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. `Indiscernibility is not identity.` The missing middle-link instrument (Fig 3 :27) is named, not hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick RNA-first, metabolism-first, or a hybrid, and it does not claim the final answer. It says the three-way comparison is not one question: each of the first two keeps working on a different subset of the evidence, and the hybrid is a union of the two conditions that must be tested at their intersection. It says the evidence that could decide the matter must be a ground anchor, not a consensus, and must have a specific shape: a closed loop, a part that survives its own use, and heritable variation that selection can act on. It says modern molecular features are lossy readings that all three histories can retrodict, so the strongest evidence is a source-discriminating instrument, not another retrodiction. What it offers is the shape any decisive experiment must have, the per-closure predictions written in advance, and the falsifiers. The historical verdict stays open until the ground settles it.
