## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Which closure describes the transition from prebiotic chemistry to the first self-sustaining, heritable, evolvable system. Arity: three candidates, of which hybrid is a union of the other two conditions, not a peer. Symmetry: RNA-first and metabolism-first are ordered priority claims; hybrid is a conjunction. Scale: molecular (ribozymes, metabolites, minerals), systemic (an evolvable protocell), geological (the rock and molecular record). Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, prebiotic-chemistry lab.** Actors: activated nucleotides, ribozymes, peptides, metabolites, mineral surfaces, wet-dry and freeze-thaw cycles, lipid vesicles. Currency: yields, selectivity, replication fidelity, catalytic rate, compatibility of conditions. Anchor: a reproducible bench reconstruction that either yields an evolvable self-sustaining system or does not. Nameable anchor: yes, the experiment.
- **Domain 2, geological / molecular record.** Actors: ancient rocks, isotopic fractionations, microfossils, LUCA reconstructions, universal cofactors, the ribosome. Currency: preserved signals, phylogenetic inference. Anchor: the record, but a lossy reading that all three histories can fit. Nameable anchor: partial.
- **Domain 3, theory-comparison frame.** Actors: the three hypotheses and their literature. Currency: explanatory coverage, publication counts. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows the ground from D1 and D2. Nameable anchor: no, so it is a frame-set, not a ground-bearing domain.
- **Domain 4, verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (a discriminating instrument) to vocabulary (terms for what a system affords) to objective (what counts as progress) to planning (which experiment) to learn and carry. The middle link has no instrument. Refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Split the comparison; hybrid is a union compound, not a midpoint. The evidence that could decide must be a ground anchor with the shape of a closed loop, a surviving catalyst and heritable variation, and must discriminate historical sources from a shared modern reading. Per-closure predictions are RNA-only evolvability for A, autocatalytic evolvability without a polymer for B, and mutually dependent evolvability for C. No closure is selected. The historical verdict is `gap`, `specified` ungrounded, candidate. The strongest outreach point is structural: a union cannot be searched as if it were a list, and an anchor cannot be replaced by agreement.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled and states refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict or a theory before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the split, the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The per-closure predictions and the union reading are removable without touching the figure derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. If a discriminating experiment contradicts the shape, the shape is narrowed and the record keeps the earlier version visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment showing an evolvable RNA-only system, or an autocatalytic evolvable network without a polymer, or a system that loses evolvability when one component is removed, would move the relevant closure from `candidate` to `grounded`. A discriminating reading of the ancient record that separates the three histories would do the same. A single non-discriminating body of evidence shown to settle priority, or an anchor that moves with preference, would refute the shape claim itself. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
