**Question:** What evidence would most strongly favor RNA-first over metabolism-first, or a hybrid model?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 113

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. ## The residual in effect terms

Three answers are on offer: (A) genetics came first, as an RNA world; (B) metabolism came first, as an autocatalytic chemical network; (C) both co-emerged in a hybrid. What is being decided is which of these the ground would select. Each option leads to a different experimental program and a different reading of the same modern molecular features. An answer would look like a **shape for the discriminating evidence**: a description of the observation that would range against an anchor and favor one closure over another before we can name the exact experiment that delivers it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe one model.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words, recorded here and developed in `01`:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So stage 1 sends the residual and the refuter, never the framework.

The people who can help are those with a public, checkable record of contact with the ground (experiments, datasets, reviews, rock records), approached one-to-one through an opt-in channel, and asked what would refute the shape claim, not whether they agree with it.
