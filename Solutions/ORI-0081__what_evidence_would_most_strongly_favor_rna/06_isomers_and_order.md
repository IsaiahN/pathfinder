## DERIVATION

### Q1: Is "hybrid" an isomer, a functional group, or a union compound?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Fig 9 :55-57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

RNA-first and metabolism-first, as pure claims, do **not** share the same atom set: one is built around an informational polymer, the other around a network of small-molecule reactions. So they are not isomers under Fig 12 :117. They are better read as two members of a **functional group**: systems that achieve a self-sustaining evolvable whole, with different atoms in the "what closes the loop first" slot (Fig 12 :123). The hybrid is then not a third member of that group; it is the **union compound** where both conditions bind at once (Fig 9 :55-57). A lever that clears RNA-first does not clear the intersection, because the network condition still binds there.

**Falsifier, written first:** if the hybrid's atom set is identical to one of the pure closures and differs only in a relabeling, then Fig 8 :13-14 (*"same atoms, different names"*, *"their union adds nothing"*) applies and the union claim fails; the hybrid is a blend and is dropped.

### Q2: Order: which link does each closure bear on?

Fig 3 orders the chain by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

- RNA-first is a claim about the **vocabulary/objective** link: what system first had terms for heritable variation and could say what counted as progress. Template replication with modifications that breed true gives that directly (world: proto-metabolism review).
- Metabolism-first is a claim about the **perception/energy** link: geochemical gradients and catalytic networks existed and could be perceived before any genetic vocabulary. Its weakness is the next link, heredity (world: *"limited experimental evidence"*, needs high-fidelity replication).
- Hybrid is a claim that the chain **cannot close** with one kind alone; the objective link requires both.

The middle link is where chains usually break (Fig 3 :24-27): having terms for what a situation affords and being able to say what would count as progress has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests. In origins of life this is exactly the gap: there is no instrument that registers "this mixture has become a self-sustaining evolvable whole." Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

**Order verdict:** the discriminating evidence bears first on the middle link, not on the chemistry of monomers. Experiments that synthesize more building blocks optimise a link that already works (Fig 3 :19).

### Q3: The two currencies

Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."**

- A shared atom set (RNA, peptides, metabolites, compartment, gradient) is cheap in **bits** to describe for all three closures. Description alone discriminates nothing.
- The **action** cost differs: demonstrating an evolvable RNA-only system, an evolvable autocatalytic network, and a mutually dependent union are three different experimental budgets. A closure that is cheap to describe and expensive to settle is precisely the case Fig 12 warns about: *"a system holding one accounting cannot compare them."*
- Therefore the hybrid claim must be priced in actions, not just asserted as a conceptually tidy middle. Its extra condition is an extra experiment, not an extra adjective.

### Q4: Analogues, with provenance

- **Metabolism-first analogue: autocatalytic / RAF sets.** Hordijk and Steel 2018 (read this session) define a reflexively autocatalytic, food-generated set and state *"Autocatalytic sets are a necessary (although not sufficient) condition for life-like behavior."* They also describe compositional inheritance: mutations are the gain or loss of a closed subRAF. This is a real source-discriminating structure, not a metaphor, but it is a world import with a source gate (Fig 6 :12-14: provenance and shadow test). It corroborates the *shape* of the metabolism-first prediction; it does not prove the historical claim.
- **RNA-first analogue: template replication with variation.** The RNA-world review (read this session) reports ribozyme polymerases and 10,000-fold amplification, and the proto-metabolism review notes that template replication *"can evolve readily if modifications breed true."* This is an instance of Fig 12 :119 catalyst plus :121 cycle. It corroborates the shape of the RNA-first prediction.
- **Hybrid analogue: two-polymer coevolution.** Hordijk and Steel: *"It would be interesting to see if they can be made with both types of polymers together."* The RNA-world review notes *"As some cofactors contain both nucleotide and amino-acid characteristics, it may be that amino acids, peptides, and finally proteins initially were cofactors for ribozymes."* These are genuine low-overlap corroborations of the union shape, each from published literature, but they are **retrodictions and suggestions**, not demonstrations.
- **Shared-pool warning.** All three reviews overlap in the prebiotic-chemistry literature they cite. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Agreement among the reviews is one pool talking, not three witnesses.

### Q5: The round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

- **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set (Fig 9 split and union rules; Fig 12 catalyst, cycle, functional group; Fig 2 anchor).
- **Abstraction (T_A):** the folder's shorthand (three closures; hybrid is a union; discriminating evidence is an anchor with the closed-loop shape).
- **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete demand such as "demonstrate an evolvable RNA-only system" or "build a two-polymer autocatalytic set."

**R_T is nonzero.** What the shorthand holds that the concrete demand drops:
- the requirement that the evidence **discriminate historical sources**, not merely retrodict a modern feature;
- the requirement that the hybrid be priced in **both** currencies (Q3);
- the **refusal** step (Fig 5 amendment: a term that refuses another; the missing middle-link instrument in Q2).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a closed loop plus a surviving catalyst plus heritable variation, and the union reading of the hybrid. That is the residue worth sending. R_T is recorded qualitatively, not numerically, because no ground reading exists.

## CONCLUSION

The hybrid is a union compound of the RNA-first and metabolism-first conditions, not an isomer and not a midpoint. The evidence that would discriminate the three bears first on Fig 3's middle link, for which no instrument currently exists. The three closures are priced differently in actions even when their descriptions are cheap in bits. World analogues (RAF autocatalytic sets, ribozyme polymerases, two-polymer coevolution) corroborate the *shape* of each prediction from low-overlap literature, but are retrodictions, not demonstrations, and share an evidence pool. Nothing here selects a historical winner.
