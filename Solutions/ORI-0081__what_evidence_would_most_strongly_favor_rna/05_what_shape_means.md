## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :131-135: the system can report what it lacks and can narrow the question, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the Library of Babel essay, fetched this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the specific instance. For ORI-0081 the shape is the form of the discriminating evidence (an anchor that registers a closed loop, a surviving catalyst, and heritable variation, and that separates historical sources). Naming a particular ribozyme or a particular rock signature as "the answer" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question, describe the effect (which system self-sustains and evolves, and which component is required) rather than the cause (which molecule happened to come first).
- Library of Babel on steering: books must be *"tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* That is Fig 2's ground: frames range against the ground; they do not negotiate with it.
- On bundling, the same essay is unusually direct: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level source for F1 and for `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here, a reproducible experiment or a discriminating measurement in the record.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A result adopted because it fits a preferred narrative is indistinguishable in content from one derived independently; only the record separates them.

Until one of these occurs, any name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why retrodiction is not discrimination here

The metamerism point from *The Hard Problem is About Source* (via the exemplar's canonical mapping): an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several distinct sources give the same reading, and the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. For ORI-0081 the ribosome's RNA core and the nucleotide cofactors are the green reading. An RNA world, a metabolism-first world with later RNA adoption, and a hybrid all predict them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a cleverer retrodiction. The fresh Nogal et al. review corroborates from the chemistry side: *"extant life features/signatures could also be misleading in certain aspects."*

## 5. Correction to an overstatement (kept visible)

I initially wrote this section as if the framework forbade naming any component of an answer. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. In this question the relevant entities (RNA, peptides, metabolites, catalytic networks) are already in the scientific library; selecting among them is a binding from the world frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), or presenting a binding as a derivation. The correction is recorded rather than erased, matching the exemplar's own §5b retraction.

## 6. HONEST STOP: what this shape does and does not do

- It does **not** pick RNA-first, metabolism-first, or hybrid.
- It does **not** supply a specific experiment to run. It constrains the form of any experiment that could discriminate.
- It **does** say the three-way comparison must be split, that the hybrid is a union compound, that the evidence must be a ground anchor, and that modern molecular features are lossy readings requiring source discrimination.
- It **does** stop where the ground is silent: no transition-from-non-life observation exists, so the historical verdict is `gap`. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (via the exemplar's canonical mapping): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any origin hypothesis and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 8. Two consequences for outreach

- **Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A "most favored paradigm" is a budget fact, not a witness (Fig 9 :60).
- **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So stage 1 sends the residual, the shape, and the refuter, never the architecture.
