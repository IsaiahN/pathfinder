## 0. The question under audit, stated as a residual

> What evidence would most strongly favor RNA-first over metabolism-first, or a hybrid model?

Restated in effect terms. Three closures compete to describe the same transition: from prebiotic chemistry to the first self-sustaining, heritable, evolvable system.

- **Option A, RNA-first / genetics-first.** The first autonomous system was a self-replicating informational polymer (RNA) that also catalyzed reactions. Metabolism and proteins came later. Evidence it would predict: an RNA-only system that replicates with heritable variation and undergoes Darwinian evolution without a metabolism-like network.
- **Option B, metabolism-first.** The first autonomous system was an autocatalytic reaction network driven by geochemistry; genetic molecules and membranes emerged later. Evidence it would predict: a self-sustaining autocatalytic network that shows heritable, evolvable behavior without any genetic polymer.
- **Option C, hybrid / co-emergence.** Information and metabolism arose together in mutual dependence. Evidence it would predict: the first evolvable system requires both and loses evolvability if either is removed.

An answer would look like a **shape for the discriminating evidence**: a stated form the observation must have, with a refuter, before we can name the particular experiment that delivers it. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 1. Residual description before any figure line

- **Arity.** Three candidate explanations, but not three peers. A and B are ordered opposites (each claims priority); C is a union of the two conditions. The residual is therefore a **union compound**, not a three-way menu.
- **Symmetry.** A and B are asymmetric claims about which came first; they are not interchangeable. C is a conjunction, not a third point on a line. Fig 12 :117 distinguishes *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"* from a blend.
- **Scale.** Molecular (nucleotides, ribozymes, metabolites, mineral surfaces), systemic (a self-sustaining evolvable protocell), geological (the ancient rock and molecular record). Three scales, so a transform between them is lossy and must be bracketed (Fig 4).

Strain test to apply after the census: a line that only fits by pretending C is a third peer governs a different quantity. Re-census, do not soften the line.

## 4. Findings F1 to F9

### F1: The three-way question is a union compound, not one hard question (decisive shape finding)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."**
> **"Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

RNA-first keeps working on one subset (ribozymes, the ribosome's RNA core, nucleotide cofactors, template replication that breeds true). Metabolism-first keeps working on another (geochemical energy, autocatalytic cycles, the ancient core pathways reconstructed in LUCA). More evidence of the same kind does not shrink the disagreement, which is exactly the Fig 9 signature of several well-formed questions rather than one hard one. World check: the RNA-world review says *"even proponents agree that there is still not conclusive evidence to completely falsify other paradigms and hypotheses."* And the hybrid case is not a third peer; it is the intersection of A and B, so Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling before searching.

**Refuter:** if sustained work on one subset does make the three-way disagreement shrink, then it was one hard question and this split is wrong. Also refuted if the intersection has no surplus and is just a relabeling of one side.

### F2: The discriminating evidence must be an anchor, not agreement

Fig 2 :29-30:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Fig 2 :35 and :40:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
> **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

The strongest evidence is a ground-visible measurement, not a head count of papers or a consensus statement. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* World check: the abiogenesis review states *"The transition from non-life to life has not been observed experimentally,"* so the anchor is currently missing, not merely underused. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Refuter:** if a frame-internal quantity (publication count, elegance, number of adherents, "most favored paradigm") is offered as decisive, the anchor test fails and this finding stands refuted.

### F3: The shape of the discriminating observation: a self-sustaining evolvable whole

The anchor, once built, must register the crossing from chemistry to a system. World anchor, NASA's definition as reported in the abiogenesis review: life is *"a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution."* Figure support is structural, not a name:

- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."**
- Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

So the discriminating observation must show a closed loop plus a part that survives its own use plus heritable variation that selection can act on. The three closures differ in which component closes that loop first.

**Refuter:** define "self-sustaining evolvable" so loosely that a plain autocatalytic reaction, a single replicating molecule, and a modern cell all qualify. Then the shape has no discriminating power and is withdrawn.

### F4: Modern molecular features are lossy readings; the evidence must discriminate sources

Fig 9 :27-28:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

Fig 6 :32:

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

The ribosome's RNA core, the nucleotide cofactors ATP/NAD/FAD, and the universal genetic code are *readings*. All three closures can retrodict them: an RNA world leaves an RNA core; a metabolism-first world that later adopted RNA also leaves one; a hybrid leaves one by construction. This is the metamerism point: a reading does not determine the source. World checks: the RNA-world review says *"Opinions differ, however, as to whether RNA constituted the first autonomous self-replicating system or was a derivative of a still-earlier system,"* and Nogal et al. 2023 warn that *"extant life features/signatures could also be misleading in certain aspects, as chemical pathways to the first protobiological systems may not need to be identical to those of current biochemistry."* Therefore the evidence that most strongly favors one model is a **source-discriminating instrument**, not another retrodiction from a modern feature.

**Refuter:** name an instrument, at any resolution, that already distinguishes the three historical sources from a modern feature and is falsifiable. If none can be described, this finding is a limit, not yet a method, and is recorded as such. Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

### F5: Per-domain verdicts on what would favor each closure (kept separate, never averaged)

**D1 lab frame, RNA-first.** Favored by an RNA-only system that self-replicates with heritable variation and Darwinian evolution with no metabolism-like network required. World corroboration: the RNA-world review reports a polymerase ribozyme (24-3) that could copy almost any other RNA, with RNAs *"amplified up to 10,000 times, a first RNA version of the polymerase chain reaction (PCR)."* The proto-metabolism review notes the gene-first advantage: *"a single molecule capable of template-directed replication can evolve readily if modifications breed true, whereas metabolic networks are fundamentally resistant to evolutionary change."* Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure shape plus world corroboration; not `grounded`, because a full evolvable RNA-only system has not been demonstrated.

**D1 lab frame, metabolism-first.** Favored by an autocatalytic network that self-sustains and shows heritable, selectable variation without a genetic polymer. World corroboration: Hordijk and Steel 2018 state *"Autocatalytic sets are a necessary (although not sufficient) condition for life-like behavior,"* and describe compositional inheritance in RAF sets. The counterweight is explicit in the proto-metabolism review: metabolism-first *"would also need self-replicating abilities with a high degree of fidelity,"* and *"There is limited experimental evidence for these theories."* Nogal et al. 2023 locate the bottleneck: *"The major bottleneck then seems to be the self-organization of those building blocks into systems that can self-sustain."* Verdict: `specified`, the gap is named, not `grounded`.

**D2 record frame, hybrid.** Favored if the only route to evolvability requires both a genetic polymer and a network, with mutual dependence and a surplus neither alone has. Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* World corroboration: Hordijk and Steel write *"It would be interesting to see if they can be made with both types of polymers together,"* and the RNA-world review notes *"As some cofactors contain both nucleotide and amino-acid characteristics, it may be that amino acids, peptides, and finally proteins initially were cofactors for ribozymes."* Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* The hybrid must be priced in both currencies, not just asserted as a blend. Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure surplus claim; the record cannot yet discriminate.

**D3 theory-comparison frame.** No anchor of its own. The claim that RNA-world is *"the most favored abiogenesis paradigm"* is a budget/FILTER fact about where effort goes. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Verdict: no verdict; this frame is excluded from witness standing until it borrows D1 or D2 ground.

### F6: The hybrid is a union compound, not a compromise position

Fig 9 :55-57:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Calling C "the middle ground" is the blend error. If A and B are conditions that overlap, C is the compound where both bind; a single lever that clears A leaves the intersection standing because B still binds. The hybrid must be decomposed into its two conditions and tested at the intersection. Supported further in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Refuter:** show that the hybrid has no condition that the other two lack, that removing either component leaves evolvability unchanged, and that its union adds no surplus: Fig 8 :13 *"same atoms, different names"* and Fig 8 :14 *"their union adds nothing"*. Then it is a blend and should be dropped.

### F7: Records and lineage: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical

Fig 8 :50-52:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"**

A result that RNA and metabolism coevolved, reached by a lab reconstruction and a result adopted because it fits a preferred narrative look the same in the conclusion. The dates, the order of derivation, and the provenance are the whole difference. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Refuter:** any claim offered without the order of derivation and the source of each component gets zero evidentiary weight until the record is produced.

### F8: The honest stop: unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

No discriminating ground reading currently exists. That is an honest `gap` for the historical answer, not a defeat and not a licence to invent a winner. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*

**Refuter:** produce the discriminating measurement. It moves the verdict from `candidate` to `grounded`; it does not change the shape.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Fig 10 :42 and the formula mode rule, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single body of evidence that does not discriminate historical sources is shown to conclusively settle priority among A, B and C.
2. An anchor that moves with frame preference (consensus, publication count, prestige) is accepted as decisive.
3. A system is demonstrated in which all three closures are simultaneously true in a way that is not a union compound with a surplus.
4. Sustained work on one subset makes the three-way disagreement shrink, showing it was one hard question after all.
5. "Self-sustaining evolvable" is defined so loosely that all three qualify, giving the shape no discriminating power.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
