# The Tether's answer on within-culture variation

**Mode: `specified` on the method shape, `general` on the structural claims, `gap` at the method-name level. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A study predicts one observation per society and observes a distribution inside it. The residual R = |Γ(b, a) − o'| lives at the person and subgroup slots. A society mean zeroes those slots before the ground is consulted. The task is to keep R where it carries mass. "Variation within a culture" is not one clean variance: it is a union of overlapping conditions (language, schooling, migration, media, subgroup norm, ecology). Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **Index the residual per slot, never per aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Symbols table :43-44: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." Operationally: nest persons in subgroups in societies; report within-society dispersion as a result, not as error.

2. **Treat pooling as a channel failure until shown otherwise.** Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". A mean is a saturated metric with a pooled reading. Statistical partial pooling that retains per-slot residuals is a different object and is not condemned.

3. **Unbundle the union before choosing an instrument.** Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

4. **Combine methods as low-overlap frames, not as consensus.** Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population records count as separate frames only if they draw on different evidence.

5. **Enumerate the habitat before picking the frame.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

6. **Carry the method across scales, not the recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

7. **No figure line names a best method.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Multilevel decomposition, measurement invariance testing, behavioural reaction norms and ethnography are provenanced imports from the world frame, each with a source gate and a shadow test. The verdict at the method-name level is `gap`, not a ranking.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that multilevel modeling, or any other named method, is *the* answer. Those names are imports; the figures return constraints.
- Not that aggregation is always wrong. It is wrong when it discards live per-slot mass; a study whose target is a policy at society level may legitimately use aggregate data. The world source on ecological fallacy records that aggregate regressions can be valid "when the target of inference is the aggregate model".
- Not that qualitative and quantitative methods are rivals. They are candidate frames whose independence has to be shown, not assumed.
- Not that every within-society difference is real. Measurement non-invariance is a real failure mode (Van De Schoot et al., 2015), and the world pool conflicts on how to diagnose it. The figures do not settle that statistical dispute.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

An instrument that discriminates sources, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Per-slot test:** does a decomposition return live residual mass at the person or subgroup level after the society mean is fitted? If not, F1 is spent.
- **Union test:** do the within-society conditions partition with no intersections, and does one dimension predict R with no residue? If yes, F3 fails.
- **Independence test:** do the methods in the battery draw on different evidence pools? If not, their agreement is a convention (Fig 2 :40).
- **Cross-scale test:** does a method carried from person to society survive a round trip with a measured, nonzero but reportable gap, or does it silently become a recording?
- Standing refuters from `01` §4, including the production of a figure line that selects a unique method name without importing from a world frame.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, and partly underivable by construction.** The figures narrow the class of admissible methods and refuse to select a member. That is a barrier result: no search inside the figure closure can manufacture a method name that was not imported (Fig 6 :14, "the only operator that moves the wall"). The answer stops where the instruments stop: with a shape, a refuter and a source ledger, not with a winner. Indiscernibility is not identity. A recorded route is what keeps the stop checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a best method for studying variation within a culture, and it does not claim that every within-society difference is signal. It says that the residual is indexed per object slot, not aggregated, so a society mean zeroes the very variation the question is about; that pooling a channel is how contact with the ground is lost; that "variation within a culture" is a union of overlapping conditions which must be unbundled before an instrument is chosen; that laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population records are usable together only when they draw on independent evidence; and that a method crossing from person to society scale must be carried as a generator with a measured round trip, not as a recording of one occasion. The figures return that shape and no method name, so specific estimators are offered as provenanced imports and the ground, not agreement, must settle which one pays.

## 7. Cost, undoability and reversal (summary)

- **Cost if wrong:** reporting per-slot dispersion that is actually measurement error spends effort and can reify subgroups. The packet states this refuter first, so the error is findable.
- **Undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawing it; method imports can be swapped without touching the figure derivation.
- **Reversed by:** a per-slot decomposition with no live mass, a partition of conditions with no intersections, a single-evidence-pool battery, a zero-gap round trip, or a verbatim figure line that selects a unique method. Not by agreement or by the prestige of a method's author.
