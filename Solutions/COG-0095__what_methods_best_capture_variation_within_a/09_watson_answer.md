## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about methods for within-culture variation. Option A: report one society-level mean and treat within-society dispersion as noise. Option B: keep the residual indexed at the person and subgroup slot, unbundle overlapping conditions, range independent frames against a ground, and carry the method across scales. What each leads to: A produces a tidy number and zeroes the live residual before the ground is consulted; B keeps the signal and pays for more instruments, more independence and a measured round trip. The residual decides, not a preference for fine-grained data.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: many nested slots (person in subgroup in society). Symmetry: same slot form, different valence, because "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries" (Fig 11 :49). Scale: person to society, at least two. R is a vector over slots, never a scalar group mean.
- **D1 Measurement instruments.** Actors: items, scales, respondents, latent constructs. Currency: invariance, loadings, thresholds, unexplained variance. Anchor: a discriminating instrument that settles whether readings are the same construct. Type: partition (within versus between at a declared level).
- **D2 The population as habitat.** Actors: persons, subgroups, schools, media, migration, institutions. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: behaviour and response, the ground for the cognitive claim. Type: union compound of overlapping conditions, not one variance.
- **D3 The frame set.** Actors: laboratory, ethnography, historical record, population data. Currency: independence, how little the frames draw on the same evidence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Type: union of low-overlap frames.
- **D4 Verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, journals, societies. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Resolve per slot, then acquire vocabulary for slot and condition, then state the objective (R reduced per slot), then choose the instruments, then carry the method across scale. The middle link is where such studies break: a link with no instrument is not a link that works.

Strain test applied: "pooled readings" in Fig 10 is not partial pooling in the statistical sense. Re-census separates a channel that cannot return per-slot data from an estimator that retains per-slot residuals. No figure-to-figure strain remains.

- **Psychometrics / measurement.** Wikipedia, *Measurement invariance*, 2026, accessed 2026-11-22. Quote: "Measurement invariance or measurement equivalence is a statistical property of measurement that indicates that the same construct is being measured across some specified groups." Predicts that the instrument itself can be the failure; corroborates D1. Also records a live conflict: Welzel and colleagues argue invariance criteria "favor constructs with low between-group variance, while constructs with high between-group variance fail these tests", while proponents reply that nomological validation assumes comparability. That conflict **conflicts** with a simple "always test invariance first" rule; it does not conflict with the figure verdict. Recorded, not resolved by the figures.
- **Statistics / mathematics.** Wikipedia, *Multilevel model*, 2026, accessed 2026-11-22. Quote: aggregation "discards all within-group information (because it takes the average of the individual level variables)" and "as much as 80–90% of the variance could be wasted". - **Statistics / mathematics.** Wikipedia, *Ecological fallacy*, 2026, accessed 2026-11-22. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the method-name ranking does not. Keep R at the person and subgroup slot and report within-society dispersion as a result. Treat a society mean as a pooled reading that can sever contact with the ground. Unbundle the overlapping conditions inside a society instead of scaling them on one dimension. Combine laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population records only at demonstrated low overlap, and enumerate the habitat before choosing any frame. Carry a method across person and society scales as a generator with a measured round trip. The figures return these constraints and no estimator name, so specific methods are provenanced imports whose fate the ground decides. This is `specified`, ungrounded; the method-name level is `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** reporting per-slot dispersion that is actually measurement error spends effort and can reify subgroups. The packet states this refuter first, so the failure is findable rather than invisible. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawal, and method imports can be swapped without touching the figure derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot decomposition showing no live residual mass, a partition of conditions with no intersections, a battery shown to draw on one evidence pool, a zero-gap scale round trip, or a verbatim figure line selecting one method name without import. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
