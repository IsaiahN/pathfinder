# Expert verification packet: the Tether on within-culture variation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a methods researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the method shape, `general` on the structural claims, `gap` at the method-name level. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A society mean zeroes the residual at the person and subgroup slots, where the variation the question asks about actually lives. The figures index R per object slot, treat a pooled reading as a channel failure, and require overlapping conditions to be unbundled before an instrument is chosen. The method shape that follows has four parts: per-slot residuals retained and reported; conditions treated as a union, not one scale; laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population frames kept independent until each has ranged against the ground; and a method carried across person and society scales as a generator with a measured round trip. The framework returns this shape and no method name, so every named estimator is a provenanced import the ground still has to pay for.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 10

- **Statement:** the residual must be read per slot; a society-level mean is a pooled reading that loses contact with the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Symbols table :43-44: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."
- **Refuter:** a complete per-slot decomposition that returns no live mass at the person or subgroup level for this question, so the variation is demonstrably measurement error; or a study whose target is genuinely the aggregate model, where a mean is the correct estimand.

### C2 : methods-facing; checkable against the methods literature

- **Statement:** "variation within a culture" is a union of overlapping conditions, and named methods form a functional group sharing `per-slot R + ground + low-overlap frame`, not one uniquely best estimator.
- **What to check:** do language, schooling, migration, media, subgroup norm and local ecology overlap within one society? Does any single dimension predict the within-society residual with no residue? Do multilevel decomposition, reaction norms and invariance testing share the stated substructure, or does one lack a slot or a ground? Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 12 :124: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **Refuter:** a condition census showing a partition with no intersections and one dimension with no residual residue; or a method in the family shown to lack a slot or a ground. Either breaks the union or functional-group claim.

### C3 : verification-facing; checkable against Fig 2 and the invariance literature

- **Statement:** combining laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population records is evidence only at low overlap; agreement from one evidence pool is a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **Refuter:** show that the proposed battery draws on one pool (same items, same respondents, same sampling frame), so its agreement carries no more information than one instrument repeated. The world literature supplies the operational checks: multi-trait multi-method designs and measurement-invariance tests (Van De Schoot et al., 2015). Those are instruments, not verdicts, and the figures do not adjudicate the dispute between invariance testing and nomological validity.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named method is the answer. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that aggregation is always wrong. Aggregate inference can be valid when the target of inference is the aggregate model.
- Not that qualitative methods are anecdotal or that quantitative methods are sufficient. They are candidate frames requiring demonstrated independence.
- Not that measurement invariance must hold in the strict form before any comparison. The world pool conflicts on this and the conflict is recorded, not resolved by the figures.
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not a claim that this second seat corroborates the first. It shares the same evidence pool and is a check of internal consistency only.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Per-slot test:** after fitting a society mean, is there live residual mass at person or subgroup level?
2. **Union test:** do the conditions partition with no intersections, and does one dimension predict R with no residue?
3. **Independence test:** do the frames in the battery draw on different evidence pools?
4. **Cross-scale test:** carry a method from person to society and back. What is the round-trip gap, measured rather than assumed?
5. **Name test:** produce a verbatim figure line that selects one method name over its rivals without importing from a world frame.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 10 :25-26, Fig 9 :54, Fig 2 :31-32 (four short passages).
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: do the within-society conditions overlap, and does one dimension leave residue?
3. Name one instrument that would measure the round-trip gap between person and society scale, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
