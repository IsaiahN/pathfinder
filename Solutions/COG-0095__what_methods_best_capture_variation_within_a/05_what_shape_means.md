## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again. It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (re-fetched): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the final member of the class. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a method that returns R per slot, unbundles overlapping conditions, ranges independent frames against a ground, and enumerates the habitat before choosing its instruments.
- **Not a shape:** "use multilevel modeling", "use measurement invariance", "use ethnography". Each is a member of the class, selected by a world import with a source gate.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (re-fetched): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that "overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms", recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- From the exemplar's inherited mapping of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling, directly relevant to F3: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The inherited reading of *They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: multilevel modeling, invariance testing and reaction norms are imported dimensions from the world frame. They are licensed by their sources (recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`), not by the figures. Calling one of them *the* answer would turn a visible import into an invisible one.

## 4. Source discrimination: why a single group mean is a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A society mean is exactly such a projection. Several within-society arrangements (a bimodal split, a small extreme subgroup, a uniform distribution) produce the same mean. Reading the source off the mean is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, that is, a method retaining per-slot information, not a more confident name for the mean.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A method that grades itself by how tidy its group means look is the named failure.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and to the exemplar's blanket phrasing

The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. The figure terms used here (slot, residual, ground, frame, habitat) are already held. Naming an everyday statistical method is therefore not inventing a seventh figure term, and the "shape, not a name" rule does not forbid it.

What it **does** forbid is claiming the figures derived that name or that importing the name moves the wall (Fig 6 : "IMPORT adds an atom" would be false here, since no figure atom is added). In `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F7 this is recorded as a `gap` at the method-name level, not as a ban on method names. Both the earlier blanket reading and its narrowing are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-fetched): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous methods or famous endorsers is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (re-fetched): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What this means for the question asked

The shape is checkable in ordinary terms before any theory is shown: ask a methods researcher what they would expect to see if within-society variation were live signal rather than noise, and what would count against it. If they name a method, that is stage 1 data. Only after their verdict is recorded do we offer the figures and explain that the framework returns the constraints, while the method name is a provenanced import the ground still has to pay for.
