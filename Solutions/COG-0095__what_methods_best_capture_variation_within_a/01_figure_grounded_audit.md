## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Let a study hold a prediction Γ(b, a) for a unit it labels "a culture", and let the observation o' arrive as a distribution over persons, subgroups and contexts inside that unit. The residual is R = |Γ(b, a) − o'| per slot s. The failure the question names is a **slot error**: the prediction is made at the society slot while the live mass of R sits at the person or subgroup slot. Reporting a society mean sets the person-slot residual to zero by construction, so the signal that says where to look is discarded before the ground is consulted.

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."
Symbols table :43-44: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."

So the residual is not "culture A differs from culture B". It is "the prediction fails at these slots inside the supposed unit, and the method has no term for them". What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): **a method that returns R at the finest slot where it carries mass, that keeps separate frames ranging against a ground, and that can say which within-society conditions are compounds rather than noise.** This is the sought-for shape written down so it can be refused.

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity:** many slots, nested (person in subgroup in society in region). R is a vector over slots, not a scalar. A scalar group mean is a different object with less information.
- **Symmetry:** the slots have the same form but not the same valence. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A standing list of which subgroups matter is therefore not licensed by the figures.
- **Scale:** at least two. The method must cross the membrane between person and society without treating a recording of one occasion as a method (Fig 4 :19-25).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. Partition or union stated. Order by dependency (Fig 3).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Measurement instruments | items, scales, respondents, latent constructs | invariance, loadings, thresholds, unexplained variance | a discriminating instrument, a measurement that settles whether two readings are the same construct (Fig 2 :29-30) | partition: within versus between variance; which parameters are group invariant |
| D2 The studied population as habitat | persons, subgroups, schools, media, migration, institutions | contact and cascade | behaviour and response, the ground for the cognitive claim | union compound: several overlapping conditions, not one variance to average (Fig 9 :54-57) |
| D3 The frame set | laboratory experiment, ethnography, historical record, population data | independence, how little the frames draw on the same evidence | the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35, :40) | union: low-overlap frames, each ranging separately |
| D4 Verification and outreach | expert readers, journals, societies | agreement and residual | the ground; in this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so independence is zero | partition of roles, zero verdict weight for receptiveness |

**Partition or union.** D1 is a partition (a quantity is either within or between at a declared level, and an item either holds its meaning across groups or does not). D2 and D3 are unions: "variation within a culture" is a bundle of conditions that overlap, and the local frames are not interchangeable spellings of one instrument. Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A method aimed at the mean clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** 1 perception: can the instrument resolve within-society variation at all? 2 vocabulary: does the method have terms for within/between, for subgroups, for context? 3 objective: what counts as progress, residual reduced per slot rather than group mean made tidy? 4 planning: choose the instruments (multilevel decomposition, reaction norms, invariance checks, ethnography). 5 learn and carry: carry the method up, never the recording (Fig 4 :22). The chain usually breaks between 2 and 3: having a word for "culture" is not having a term for what the situation affords, and a link with no instrument is not a link that works (Fig 3 :27).

## 3. Findings

### F1 : Keep R per slot. Never report a society mean as the observation (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."
Symbols table :43-44: "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."

A method that returns one number per society has already answered "internally uniform" before any observation arrived. The figure verdict is not a preference for fine-grained data. It is a claim about where the residual lives: at the slot. The operational reading is a variance decomposition with persons nested in groups, subgroup-level residuals retained, and within-group dispersion reported as a result rather than as error.

**Triangulation status:** `triangulated`. Fig 1 governs the agent loop (actors: object slots, currency: residual, anchor: ground). Fig 7 :35 governs the room chain (actors: rooms, currency: variation versus verdict, anchor: ground): "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :14 names the exact failure: "blending: novelty swamped to the mean." Two figures with different actors and different currency converge. They still share one framework evidence pool, so this is internal triangulation, not two independent witnesses.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the residual for this question is genuinely scalar at the society level, meaning within-society dispersion is measurement error with no live mass and no downstream consequence. If that is shown, per-slot indexing is spent effort. Until then, averaging is a choice, not a result.

### F2 : Pooled readings are a channel failure, not a finding (single-source figure, world corroborated)

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
Fig 10 :28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"

Treating a society as internally uniform is a saturated metric with a pooled reading. The ground (behaviour, response) did not change; the channel lost the within-society variation. The figure's instruction is "keep the anchor reachable" (Fig 10 :29).

**Triangulation status:** `single-source` for the figure (Fig 10 alone). World corroboration is real but low independence: the multilevel-model literature says aggregation "discards all within-group information (because it takes the average of the individual level variables)" and that "as much as 80–90% of the variance could be wasted" (Wikipedia, *Multilevel model*, 2026, read 2026-11-22).

**Distinction, not a strain:** statistical *partial pooling* (shrinking subgroup estimates toward a mean while retaining subgroup residuals) is not the "pooled readings" Fig 10 forbids. Fig 10 governs a channel that cannot return a per-slot reading at all. A partial-pooling estimator can. Naming the difference prevents a false conflict between the figure and the standard method.

**Refuter:** show that the study's channel returns per-slot residuals that discriminate subgroups at the ground, so nothing was lost by the summary. A mean alone cannot do this; a variance decomposition with retained slots can.

### F3 : "Variation within a culture" is a union compound. Unbundle before searching (single-source figure)

Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

Within-society variation is not one variance to be estimated. It is language, schooling, migration, media exposure, subgroup norm and local ecology, overlapping. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single lever (a group mean, a single dimension such as individualism-collectivism) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

World corroboration: cross-cultural psychology records that "strong individualistic and collectivistic orientations may coexist in the same culture" and that "Cultures are much more complex and contextually based than represent in these inflexible dimensional representations" (Wikipedia, *Cross-cultural psychology*, 2026, read 2026-11-22). This is corroboration at low independence from the other Wikipedia sources and is recorded as such.

**Refuter:** a census showing the within-society conditions are a partition, each person in exactly one condition with no overlap, and that a single dimension predicts the residual without residue. Then unbundling is overhead.

### F4 : Combine methods as low-overlap frames, not as a consensus (triangulated)

Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 13 :38-39: "independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence, and it is what makes their agreement mean anything."

Laboratory experiment, ethnography, historical record and population data are candidates for D3 only if they draw on different evidence. Two survey instruments built from the same item pool are one frame wearing two names. The world literature supplies the operational version: multi-trait multi-method designs and measurement-invariance tests exist precisely to detect method effects and item non-invariance (Van De Schoot et al., 2015; Wikipedia, *Measurement invariance*, 2026). The figures decide the rule; the world supplies the instruments.

**Triangulation status:** `triangulated` across Fig 2 and Fig 13. Fig 2 governs the checking arrangement; Fig 13 governs independence applied to the minimum set itself. Different actors, different currency, low overlap, one framework pool.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the methods in a proposed battery draw on a single evidence pool, so their agreement carries no more information than one instrument repeated. Then the battery is a convention, not corroboration, and the recommendation to combine them is spent effort.

### F5 : Enumerate the habitat within the culture before naming a method (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

The residual selects the actors. If the study starts by naming "culture" as the cause and then picks a method, any method will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). The enumeration step is the habitat: who acts on the cognitive residual, under what conditions (school, media, migration, season, terrain), through what relations (competition, dependency, instruction). The method follows from that list, not the other way round. Animal-behaviour work provides a low-overlap analogue: behavioural reaction norms "measure the behaviour of an individual over an environmental gradient, therefore providing information on how an animal behaves on average and how their behaviour changes across an environmental gradient" (Wikipedia, *Personality in animals*, 2026, read 2026-11-22). That is the same per-slot-plus-context shape in a different field.

**Triangulation status:** `triangulated` across Fig 11 and Fig 9. Fig 11 enumerates contact; Fig 9 governs the order of description before frame choice. Different objects, low overlap.

**Refuter:** a complete habitat enumeration (closed list of actors in contact with the residual, with no second-order cascade) that still fails to select a method. Then enumeration is not the expensive step the figures claim.

### F6 : Carry the method across scales, not the recording (single-source)

Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

A finding from one society is a recording of one occasion. What crosses up is the method for resolving within-society variation, verified and compressed; what comes down is a head start, not a replay. This rules out shipping a group mean as a reusable result.

**Refuter:** show a result that is genuinely a generator (reapplicable method with stated refuter) rather than a description of one sample, so the membrane rule adds nothing here.

### F7 : No figure line names a statistical method. This is a `gap` at the method-name level

The figures return the shape: per-slot residual, unbundled union, independent frames, enumerated habitat, method across scale. They do **not** return "multilevel model", "measurement invariance test", "behavioural reaction norm", "ethnography", or any specific estimator. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 6 :14 names import as "the only operator that moves the wall", and Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Therefore every method name in this folder is a **provenanced import from the world frame**, bought with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :11-14), not a derivation from the figures. The world sources actually read are in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The verdict is `general`/`specified` on the shape and `gap` on the unique best method. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence": the honest answer to "which method best" is that the figures narrow the class and do not select a member.

**Refuter for the gap:** derive a unique method name from the figures alone, with a verbatim line that picks it over its rivals and a source gate that does not import from a world frame. If that is produced, F7 is withdrawn and the verdict moves to `grounded` on the method name.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A complete per-slot residual decomposition returns no live mass at the person or subgroup level for this question, so the within-society variation is demonstrably measurement error (breaks F1, F2).
2. The within-society conditions form a partition with no intersections, and one dimension predicts the residual with no residue (breaks F3).
3. The proposed method battery is shown to draw on a single evidence pool, so its agreement is a convention rather than triangulation (breaks F4).
4. A habitat enumeration assembled before frame choice fails to constrain which frames are relevant (breaks F5).
5. A figure line is produced that selects a unique statistical method over its rivals without importing from a world frame (breaks F7).

If any fires, the recommendation is withdrawn in the part it breaks. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Report the residual per slot. Nest persons in subgroups in societies. Publish within-society dispersion as a result, not as error (F1, F2).
2. Unbundle the union. Treat language, schooling, migration, media, subgroup norm and local ecology as overlapping conditions with their own residuals, not as items on one scale (F3).
3. Combine instruments only at low overlap. Keep laboratory, ethnographic, historical and population frames separate until each has ranged against the ground; record their provenance so convergent derivation is distinguishable from adopted import (F4, F5).
4. Enumerate the habitat before choosing the method, and carry the method across scales as a generator with a stated refuter (F5, F6).
5. Do not claim the figures name a best method. Mark the method level `gap`, take the method names as provenanced imports, and let the ground settle which one pays (F7).

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A cheap method is a filter for what gets tried first, never a witness for what is true.

### Round trip note

Compressing the figures into the shorthand "measure per slot, unbundle, triangulate" and then concretising it into a specific study design loses three constraints: the anchor must not update, the frames must be low overlap, and the method must cross scale as a generator. R_T is therefore nonzero. It is recorded qualitatively as that enumerated set of dropped constraints, not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
