## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what a frame predicts about a claim and what a verifiable record returns, both during spread and after a correction. Arity: three-place (frame, action, ground return), indexed per claim, so Fig 1 :19 forbids averaging. Symmetry: ordered in time, with a threshold comparison for "more effectively"; not symmetric, since a correction does not restore the pre-exposure state. Scale: person, passing chain, attention habitat. Union compound under Fig 9 :54-57, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, individual belief-and-sharing loop.** Actors: person, Γ, b, w. Currency: residual reduction, fluency and novelty reward. Anchor: a verifiable record of truth and consequence. Question type: partition inside one frame's library.
- **Domain 2, transmission chain.** Actors: tellers, audiences, claim as content. Currency: exposure, repetition, agreement. Anchor: per-handoff ground contact. Question type: a chain of rooms, Fig 7.
- **Domain 3, correction and refusal frame.** Actors: corrections, ¬, fact-checkers. Currency: reach, refusal, standing to compose over. Anchor: the ground. Question type: operator availability, Fig 5 and the Operators table.
- **Domain 4, attention habitat.** Actors: ranking systems, incentives, novelty, emotion, cohort. Currency: contact, cascade, supply. Anchor: the ground, which does not move with the habitat. Question type: habitat enumeration, Fig 11.
- **Excluded X1 and X2.** "Intrinsic stickiness" and "gullibility" fail the anchor test and are absorbed into D4 and D1.

Order by dependency: describe the residual (Fig 9 :27-28); install ¬ at the middle link (Fig 3 :24-27); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); restore ground pressure in the chain (Fig 7 :37-39).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 7 :12-13, :35-39 (room chain and two factors), Fig 5 :53-55 and Operators table :58 (refusal), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :39-40 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`: two conditions, a union, an instrument gap, no named mechanism, and a dependency order.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete intervention, for example a redesigned correction delivered to a specific audience in a specific platform habitat with specific measurements.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which observable distinguishes bracket fidelity from ground pressure in a live cascade (Fig 7 names two factors but supplies no instrument for either);
- whether a given correction intervention is a ¬ refusal or an added frame-internal cue (the operator distinction is clear in the framework, the operational test is not);
- the boundary between the two conditions in real data, where exposure, agreement and belief are usually pooled into one metric (Fig 10 :26);
- the source of a spread advantage, since the reading cannot discriminate novelty from arousal from identity (the metamerism limit).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a claim-and-habitat chain with low ground pressure compounds agreement without evidence, and a correction that cannot carry ¬ cannot remove standing. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
