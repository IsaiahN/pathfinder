## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape in this solution is: *two conditions, a union, and an instrument gap*. The names "novelty", "arousal", "partisan identity", "age" and "digital literacy" are fillers that the framework does not supply.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is exactly the two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md`: send what would be seen if the claim were true, let the recipient check it, then offer the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the sharpest version for this question. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Applied here: a retweet count reads a behavior. It cannot tell you whether the cause was novelty, identity, arousal, or a source the audience already trusted. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 4. Correction is a refusal, not a fourth term (essay-specific)

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the closest philosophical source to INF-0092. Four passages bear directly:

1. "who drift away from the truth together if they only ever check against each other." This is Fig 2 panel 1, mutual update, stated in ordinary language.
2. "Nobody lied. Each person heard it through the understanding they had, and the errors compounded in one direction (also happens in the 'telephone game')". This is Fig 7 :12-13, same closure handed along, agreement compounding without evidence.
3. "Context, subtext and pretext decide what a message means to you. None of them decides whether it is true. Only the ground does that." This is the anchor rule of Fig 2 :29-30 applied to a message rather than an instrument.
4. "A correction isn't a fourth thing to add to the list. When a master says 'no, like this,' the words carry almost nothing. The meaning is in the unstated gap between what you did and what they show you, which makes it subtext." This is Fig 5 :53-55 and Operators table :58 in human terms: a refusal removes standing rather than adding content, and it needs two-way contact to land.

The essay also names the three drift-stoppers, which are the practical form of C1 and C2: "A ground that both are corrected by, separately", "Separate experience", and "Claims in effect terms". Its warning about high contact is exactly Fig 8 :49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."

## 5. Q for this question: what licenses a particular mechanism as the cause?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. In `09_watson_answer.md` every world source is marked as corroboration or conflict, and none is allowed to name the slot.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I overstated "shape, not a name" in section 3. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. Novelty, arousal, identity and age are not missing primitives in this derivation; they are bindings from the world frame onto conditions already describable in Γ (attention habitat, gradient, comparison, network). Naming one as a candidate is not forbidden. What remains forbidden is presenting it as a framework output, or claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - this one adds none). Both the original claim and this narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Why the source-discriminator matters here

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* notes that "an understanding that's never checked feels exactly as certain as one that has been." This is the round trip of Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." For INF-0092 the round trip would be: abstract the figures into the two-condition shape, concretise it back into a concrete intervention (a specific correction design in a specific habitat), and measure what the shape dropped. That dropped set is R_T, recorded in `11_audit.md`. It is not zero, and pretending it is zero is the dead-reckoning error.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a claim spreads because of identity is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a verifiable record, settle it.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "the catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the misinformation question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
