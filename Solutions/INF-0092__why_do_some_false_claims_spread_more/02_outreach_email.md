# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two-stage order below is kept. The shape claim and its refuter must survive contact on their own, without the framework behind them.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about misinformation spread before you decide whether the derivation is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "why do some false claims spread more than accurate information, even when corrections are available?" is not one question. It unpacks into two conditions with different instruments:
>
> 1. **Transmission.** A false claim travels far when the chain of tellers keeps the ground's pressure near zero and keeps high fidelity to whatever earns social reward (a new angle, a surprise, a quick retweet). Each retelling raises the appearance of confirmation without adding a new bearing on whether the claim is true. Agreement compounds; evidence does not.
> 2. **Correction.** A correction is not one more item placed beside the claim. It is a refusal that removes the claim's standing to be built on. If the receiving frame has no term for "this is false", or if the verifiable record is no longer reachable, the correction becomes just another claim and the original keeps its standing.
>
> This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement. It does not name novelty, arousal, partisan identity or age as the cause. Those are bindings from the world frame, and until their provenance is shown they carry no evidentiary weight here.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A claim's propagation distance is unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the ground's payment for it.
> 2. A chain of low-overlap tellers, each selected against what the previous one surfaced, still shows spread tracking agreement rather than ground contact.
> 3. A cascade with ground pressure and bracket fidelity both measured high still out-runs the accurate claim.
> 4. A frame demonstrably lacking a term for refusal still drops a false claim's standing to be composed over, without anything new being added.
> 5. One lever restores both conditions in all failure types and leaves no categorically different residue.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Full architecture (Medium, 19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative and the shape derivation (Substack): https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Best,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, explicitly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. **Record their verdict before showing any theory.**
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
