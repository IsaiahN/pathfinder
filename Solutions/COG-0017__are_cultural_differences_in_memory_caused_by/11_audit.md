## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54 and :30-31, Fig 6 :32, Fig 11 :39 and Fig 12 :123.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source underdetermination**, which a concrete attribution silently drops by naming one source from the reading;
- the **anchor requirement**, which a concrete attribution bypasses when test familiarity and sampling are not checked;
- the **union and independence structure**, which a single-cause attribution collapses into one witness wearing three names.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: memory machinery is a shared functional group with variable members and bonds, and experience is the habitat. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09` states.
