# The Tether's answer on cultural differences in memory

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain, `gap` on which cause produced a given observed difference. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe a cross-cultural difference in memory and want to attribute it to a different strategy, a different social goal, or a different form of experience. The residual is the unassigned source of that difference. It is indexed per memory phenomenon, cultural group and candidate source (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), it is non-symmetric because a source produces a reading and not the reverse, and it spans scales from an encoding operation to a developmental habitat.

The three candidates are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54). This answer fixes the shape a source attribution must have and refuses to supply the attribution for a particular case.

## 2. The shape

1. **A reading cannot name its source.** An observed recall difference is a projection with lossy information. Several sources can produce the same reading. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The source-discrimination essay gives the instrument-level reason: the green meter *"cannot comment on the source"* and the case closed only when a spectrometer arrived.
2. **The three candidates form a union, not an either/or.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A social goal can recruit a strategy; experience can supply both; the intersections are specific compounds (Fig 9 :57).
3. **Every verdict is per slot.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single pooled cause for "cultural memory differences" is the named averaging error.
4. **A non-updating anchor is upstream of the cause.** Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Test familiarity, translation and sampling are checked before attribution; a difference that fails the check is refused, not explained. The empirical literature supplies the same caution through the education confound in Dujmic et al. 2026 and the familiarity check in Millar et al. 2013.
5. **Independence, not a bigger pool.** Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Strategy, goal and experience measured in one session from one dataset are one witness.
6. **Memory is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Strategies and goals are not separable levers; the habitat supplies what is available and what pays. There is no standing list of memory strategies that survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
7. **The invariant item is a functional group.** Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Encoding, consolidation and retrieval are the group; the member and the bond vary. With a fixed atom set, two cultural arrangements are isomers (Fig 12 :117).
8. **A discriminating instrument is owed.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The source question breaks where no instrument separates the three candidates. Sharpen an instrument that already returns something; do not invent one from a description.
9. **Which cause, in a given case, is `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The gap is not an omission in the answer. It is the answer's honest limit.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one of the three causes is correct in general, and not that they form a clean partition.
- Not that a particular cultural group uses a particular strategy or holds a particular goal. Those are bindings from the empirical frame, provenance-stamped, not derivations.
- Not that "culture" is a fourth cognitive primitive. Experience is the habitat (Fig 11 :39).
- Not that a correlation between strategy and recall fixes the arrow. The ordered bond `→` and the symmetric bond `+` are different terms (Fig 12).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the green-reading instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Anchor test.** Does the memory difference survive translation, test familiarity and sampling checks, with the instrument shown not to move when the group changes? If not, the difference is refused (Fig 2 :33).
- **Discriminator test.** Is there an intervention that returns strategy, goal and experience effects separately, with independent readings that do not share participants or tests? Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
- **Bond test.** Is an observed strategy-recall association an ordered sequence `→`, a production `⇒`, or a symmetric conjunction `+`? A manipulation, not a correlation, decides this (Operators table).
- **Standing refuters.** A clean partition; a single reading that uniquely names a source; an anchor that moves; a variation requiring a new cognitive primitive. Any of these narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The source of a given difference is unreached at this budget because no discriminating instrument is in hand. It is not unreachable in principle: the empirical literature is actively building the instruments, as the common-garden designs in Pravosudov et al. 2015 and the encoding manipulations in Dujmic et al. 2026 show. The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing sources, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity: a shape that fits the known studies is not proof that the source list is complete.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not choose between strategies, social goals and forms of experience as the cause of a cultural memory difference. It says that the question is a union of overlapping conditions, not an either/or, that the observed difference is a reading which cannot identify its own source, and that verdicts must be indexed per memory phenomenon and group rather than pooled. Before naming a cause, you need an anchor that does not move when the researcher changes groups, independent readings that do not all come from one dataset, and an instrument that discriminates among the three sources, because several sources can produce the same recall score. Across cultures the memory machinery is a shared functional group whose members and bonds vary, with experience acting as the habitat rather than a fourth primitive. The framework returns that shape, a pre-registered refuter and the instruments that would close it. It refuses to name the source of any particular difference until a ground pays for the name.
