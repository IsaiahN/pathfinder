## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of the answer that can be stated before the specific question, actor or weight is known. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape for HIS-0031 is: a per-slot residual, a habitat written by introduction, a convention that becomes a verdict, a decaying channel, an interested seat, and a declared `gap` where the source-discriminating instrument is missing. A list of named villains or a single causal percentage is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Applied here: describe "an actor was introduced into the habitat of this question" before saying "a patron", "a censor" or "a funder", because the borrowed word imports a whole theory.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Applied to the union in `01` section 1.3.
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The habitat is enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a per-slot archive decision: the grant, the ban, the dismissal, the textbook withdrawal. Consensus about the period is not such a decision.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Polanyi's 1962 "you cannot shape it" and Borinskaya's 2019 archival record are imports from the history-of-science frame. They are recorded as bindings onto held atoms, not as figure derivations.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the limit. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Applied here: a publication record cannot tell whether silence came from a patron, a censor or a lost channel. Naming the source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator.

## 4. Why the historical question cannot be settled by this derivation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

This audit is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a reading of the archive. It cannot settle which question was suppressed, by whom, or with what weight. Its own mode line says so (Formula: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate."*). Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid using the word "patron" or "market"?

**No, with a condition.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Patrons, censors, funders and markets are already atoms in the questioner's own vocabulary; naming them is a binding from the history-and-science-of-science frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh kind of cause and calling it framework-derived (Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*);
- claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*);
- naming the specific question or computing the causal weight, which is the `gap` in F8.

Every such binding is recorded as provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`, with the source, year and quote. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-51).

## 6. Two consequences for this outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of eminent historians who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape on one question-slot against the archive.

**The gatekeeper's stake must be declared.** Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."* Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* This applies to the project author as much as to any historical censor: the interest in the framework being useful is declared in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` rather than hidden.

## 7. Honest stop

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops where the instruments stop. What would move it to `grounded` is an archive reading that pays per slot, not another argument and not a larger agreement.
