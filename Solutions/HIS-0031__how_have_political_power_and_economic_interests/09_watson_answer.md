Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small for some question-slots and grow without the question being asked for others? ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the name does not. Which questions could safely be asked is a per-slot property of question-and-habitat: power and interest introduce actors whose contact cannot be subtracted; a safety convention becomes a verdict when it decides outcomes and its keeper's interest disqualifies agreement with the gate; unasked questions are refusals and channel losses, so silence is not proof of no suppression; prestige funds asking but cannot settle safety; and agreement among historians is one evidence pool. The three conditions are kept separate and never averaged. Which question, which actor and what weight is a declared gap that a per-slot archive reading would close. Mode `specified`. No world source flipped a verdict; the one scope strain (Fig 13 applied to political actors) was returned to the census and resolved.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is flattening a union of three conditions into one lever, which leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :55).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by the corrected one, and no actor class has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a per-slot archive reading, not agreement. Any of the six pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 0b would reverse the part it names, in particular (1) askability restored by subtraction with no introduction, (2) a gate with no stake and no effect on outcomes, (3) a complete sealed archive with no silent slots, or (4) two genuinely independent instruments converging per slot. The structural falsifiers in `06_isomers_and_order.md` would reverse the isomer, catalyst and order claims. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot askability is fully predicted by a period-level average would also reverse the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
