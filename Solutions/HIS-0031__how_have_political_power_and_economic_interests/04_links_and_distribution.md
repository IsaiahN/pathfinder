## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described one question-slot sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see in a named case before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :60**: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."* Declare that this derivation is authored by a project with an interest in the framework being useful.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask which of the three conditions is doing the work in a case they know; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the two links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and never pooled later as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per question (the archive, per Fig 2 :29) and must write their own refuter before running it.
