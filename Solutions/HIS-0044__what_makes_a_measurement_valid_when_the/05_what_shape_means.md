## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is: an anchor that does not move when frames push on it, an effect description before a frame is chosen, independence bought in proportion to consequences, a validation argument with a pre-registered refuter, and a recorded lineage. A specific instrument, index, poverty line, or diagnostic threshold is a **name**. The difference matters because a contested construct is exactly a case where the name is what is in dispute.

The exemplar's correction applies here too: "shape, not a name" governs a **missing** primitive, not the selection of an atom already held. For measurement validity, all the needed figure atoms are already present (anchor, ground, channel, residual, frame). Choosing a specific indicator is a binding from a world frame onto an available term, requiring provenance (Fig 8 :50-52), not the invention of a new primitive. What is genuinely missing, and reported as a gap, is the correct operationalisation of the contested construct.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns that a hard question may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one", which is the union-compound finding in `01` §1.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the operational rule for a contested construct: state what you would observe if the construct were present and absent, before selecting the indicator.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* also gives the cleanest statement of the two-part measurement found in the figures: "Every measurement works this way: something has to name what's being measured (Fig 13 :16), and something has to decide what the reading would mean (Fig 13 :26). Neither does it alone." Contested constructs are contested precisely at the second term.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

"They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature": "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And, on declared primitives: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot." That is exactly the honest status of any chosen operationalisation of intelligence, health, or poverty: a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why a reading cannot settle its own dispute (the metamerism case)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the strongest essay-level support for this question. An instrument reads green. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: an IQ score, a health index, or a headcount is a reading. Several rival constructs can produce the same reading. A measure that cannot discriminate among those sources is inadequate to settle which construct is correct. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument or a provenance record, not a name assigned from the reading.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the matching line: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A scale whose output is indiscernible from the construct is still a scale, and the lineage has to be kept even when the copy looks perfect.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid choosing a measure? ANSWERED

No. It forbids pretending the choice was derived. The framework returns a shape; a specific indicator is licensed only by a ground reading or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The figures do not name a correct operationalisation of intelligence, health, or poverty, and reporting that as `gap` is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Two consequences for this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous endorsees for a contested measure is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the measure against a ground, not agreement with it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
