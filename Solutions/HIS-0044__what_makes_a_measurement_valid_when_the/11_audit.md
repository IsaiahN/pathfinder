## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the standing disagreement about what a contested construct is. Arity: at least two contesting frames, and at least two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"), not one hard question. Symmetry: the frames are symmetric claimants; the anchor is asymmetric and pinned (Fig 2 :25). Scale: first order for the reading, arrangement-level for validity (Fig 11 :39).
- **Domain 1, reading and instrument.** Actors: instrument, operation, reading, case. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground. Question type: anchor test. Nameable, so a domain.
- **Domain 2, construct definition.** Actors: researchers, standards bodies, communities, definitions, nets. Currency: meanings, conventions. Anchor: **absent at the start**. By the census rule it is not yet a domain; it becomes one through constitutive legitimacy or a net reaching observables. The specific operationalisation is therefore a `gap` verdict, not a silence.
- **Domain 3, verification and use.** Actors: reviewers, users, affected persons, institutions. Currency: agreement, residuals, consequences. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable, so a domain. In this simulated run its independence is zero: all three domains draw on one evidence pool.

Order by dependency: describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse a declared meaning before minting a measure (formula step 2); settle each condition's ground before composing the compound verdict (Fig 12 :104 logic); buy independence in proportion to consequences (Fig 2 :42).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 2 and Fig 10, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 11's capability claim. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the level of mapping. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30 and :36-38 (anchor and constitutive legitimacy), Fig 9 :54 (union compound), Fig 10 :47-49 (declared meaning), Fig 11 :39-40 (capability).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` §5 and `07` §2: an anchor that does not move, a constitutive test, effect description before indicators, bought independence, a refuter, a gap for the specific operationalisation.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer to "is this test of intelligence, this health index, this poverty line valid?"

**R_T is nonzero and this is the honest result.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **classification of the construct**, which the constitutive rule demands and the shorthand leaves unperformed;
- the **source-discrimination requirement**, which the shorthand compresses into "range against the ground" but which needs an instrument that distinguishes rival constructs producing the same score;
- the **independence audit**, which the shorthand states as a rule but cannot execute without knowing the evidence pools of the actual corroborating frames.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a reading that moves with frame preferences is not an anchor, so agreement and reliability cannot be the verdict. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
