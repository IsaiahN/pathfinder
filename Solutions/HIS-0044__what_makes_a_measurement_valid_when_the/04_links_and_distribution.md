## Wider literature, read through search result excerpts

These are real pages whose text was returned by the search tool on 2026-11-23. The PDF fetcher could not open them directly; the excerpts are quoted in the ledger with that status.

- **Messick 1995, *Validity of psychological assessment***, American Psychologist 50(9), 741 to 749. Landing page at https://psycnet.apa.org/doiLanding?doi=10.1037%2F0003-066X.50.9.741. Abstract: "The traditional conception of validity divides it into three separate and substitutable types: content, criterion, and construct validities. This view is fragmented and incomplete, especially because it fails to take into account both evidence of the value implications of score meaning as a basis for action and the social consequences of score use." The six aspects are content, substantive, structural, generalizability, external and consequential.
- **Messick, consequential validity definition**, https://econtent.hogrefe.com/doi/10.1027/1015-5759/a000664 (European Journal of Psychological Assessment, 2021): consequential validity "appraises the value implications of score interpretation as a basis for action as well as the actual and potential consequences of test use, especially regarding sources of invalidity related to issues of bias, fairness, and distributive justice."
- **Sen 1981, *Poverty and Famines***, ILO/Clarendon text: "For the person studying and measuring poverty, the conventions of society are matters of fact (what are the contemporary standards?), and not issues of morality or of subjective search." This directly supports the constitutive-anchor finding: describing a prevailing convention is description, not prescription.
- **UN DESA, *Multidimensional Poverty*, Development Issues No. 3** (2015), https://policy.desa.un.org/sites/default/files/inline-images/eapd2023/publication/dsp_policy_03.pdf: "The measurement of poverty is composed of two fundamental steps, according to Amartya Sen (1976): determining who is poor (identification) and building an index to reflect the extent of poverty (aggregation)." Both steps "have been sources of debate over time among academics and practitioners." This is a real-world union compound, matching Fig 9 :54.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** : "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
