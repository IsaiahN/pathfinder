# Expert verification packet : measurement validity under contestation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a measurement specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A valid measurement of a contested construct is a reading that ranges against an anchor which does not move when frames push on it, where the anchor is legitimate because the question is constitutively about it, where the construct is described in effect terms before an indicator set is chosen, where independence between corroborating frames was bought in proportion to the consequences of being wrong, and where a validation argument names in advance what would refute it. Reliability and expert agreement are frame-internal and cannot by themselves be the verdict. The specific operationalisation of intelligence, health, or poverty is not named by the framework; it is recorded as an honest gap until a source-discriminating ground settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 1
- **Statement:** a reading is an anchor only if it does not update on frames, and a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it produces.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 : "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 1 :27 : "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **What to check:** is the anchor condition stated as a property of the thing that settles the question, rather than as a property of the measuring community? Can reliability, item count or agreement be smuggled in as the anchor?
- **Refuter:** show a case where a reading that moves when frames push on it nevertheless settles the contested construct, with no independent anchor.

### C2 : framework-internal plus world-facing; checkable by a measurement specialist
- **Statement:** a declared meaning is not a grounded one, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. Operationalism alone cannot validate a construct measure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :49 : "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :47 : "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :18 : "a declared meaning is not a grounded one."
- **World-facing check:** Cronbach and Meehl 1955 require that a construct occur in a nomological net whose laws reach observables through "explicit, public steps of inference"; they state "Rationalization is not construct validation" and "The construct is at best adopted, never demonstrated to be 'correct.'" SEP *Operationalism* reports that if the measurement method defines the concept, the method is "automatically valid, as a matter of convention or even tautology", and that metrological validity "becomes an interesting question only if the concept possesses a broader meaning than the specification of the method of its measurement."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a contested construct whose operational definition genuinely grounds itself, with no anchor outside the definition, and where a convention that decides an outcome remains a legitimate convention.

### C3 : verification arrangement; checkable against Figures 2, 7, 8 and 11
- **Statement:** agreement is not corroboration, and validity belongs to the arrangement rather than the instrument.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :35 : "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40, leading clause included : "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 7 :35 : "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 11 :40 : "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** did the corroborating frames draw on different evidence, and did the instrument change contact with ground and habitat, or only precision?
- **Refuter:** show two frames with a fully shared evidence pool supplying independent corroboration, or an instrument improvement that changes validity while leaving contact unchanged.

## 3. Standing falsifiers (pre-registered, written before the run)

1. A measurement settles a contested construct with no anchor independent of the frames doing the measuring.
2. An operational definition grounds itself, with no anchor outside the definition.
3. Two frames with a fully shared evidence pool supply independent corroboration.
4. An instrument improvement changes validity without changing contact with ground or habitat.
5. A single coefficient, with no nomological network, no rivals and no account of consequences, settles construct validity.
6. A unique correct operationalisation of a contested construct is derived from the figure set alone, with no world import and no ground reading.

If any fires, withdraw the corresponding claim. Until one is run, no part is `grounded`.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a verdict on any existing test, index, diagnostic threshold or poverty line.
- Not that consensus is useless. It is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict.
- Not that reliability is irrelevant. It is a necessary channel property, not a sufficient condition for validity.
- Not that the figures classify intelligence, health, or poverty, or name a correct operationalisation.
- Not a ground reading. The packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures and cannot settle the question it reports on.
- Not a claim that the second-seat audit is corroboration. It shares this derivation's evidence pool.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29-30, :36-38, and Fig 10 :47-49. Five short passages.
2. Say whether the constitutive-legitimacy test is a genuine criterion for choosing an anchor, or a relabelled preference. If it is the latter, C1 fails.
3. Name one real contested construct where an operational definition currently decides an outcome, and say whether Cronbach and Meehl's nomological-net requirement is met. If it is, C2 weakens.
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate among rival constructs producing the same score, or say why none is possible. This is the source-discrimination test in `07` §4.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
