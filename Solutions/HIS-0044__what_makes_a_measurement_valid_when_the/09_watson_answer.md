## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the validity of a measurement whose construct is contested (intelligence, health, poverty). Option A: a measurement is valid when qualified people agree on it and a coefficient is published, so consensus settles the matter. Option B: agreement is a convention until the reading ranges against an anchor that does not move when frames push on it, the construct is described in effect terms before an indicator is chosen, independence is bought in proportion to consequences, and a validation argument survives a pre-registered refuter. What each leads to: A buys a convention that can drift invisibly; B buys a checkable shape and leaves the specific operationalisation open until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unresolved disagreement about what the construct is, showing up as readings that do not settle it. Arity: two or more contesting frames, with at least two overlapping conditions (ground contact and construct meaning), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: the frames are symmetric claimants, swappable without changing the dispute; the anchor is asymmetric and "pinned: it does not update on A or B" (Fig 2 :25). Scale: the reading is first order, validity is a property of the whole arrangement (Fig 11 :39).
- **Domain 1, the reading and instrument.** Actors: instrument, operation, reading, case. Currency: observations. Anchor: the ground, "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, the construct definition.** Actors: researchers, standards bodies, communities, operational definitions, nomological nets. Currency: meanings, conventions. Anchor: **initially none**, which is why the construct is contested. It becomes a domain only through constitutive legitimacy (Fig 2 :36) or a net contacting observations. Recorded as provisional with an absent anchor. Verdict for the specific operationalisation: `gap`.
- **Domain 3, verification and use.** Actors: reviewers, users, affected persons, institutions. Currency: agreement, residuals, consequences. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Partition or union.** R is a union compound, not a partition. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). Each condition has its own loop and ground; a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn. The middle vocabulary link is where contested constructs break (Fig 3 :27, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at"). Finer instruments (link 1) cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: constitutive legitimacy (Fig 2 :36) appears to conflict with the evidence-pool rule (Fig 2 :40). Re-census resolves it: the constitutive rule picks the *kind* of anchor; the evidence-pool rule judges the *independence* of the particular frames. Different quantities, no contradiction. Reported, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A valid measurement of a contested construct requires an anchor that does not move when frames push on it; a constitutive determination of what may serve as that anchor; an effect description before an indicator is chosen; independence bought in proportion to consequences; a validation argument with a pre-registered refuter; and a lineage that records imported bindings as bindings. Reliability and agreement are frame-internal and cannot by themselves be the verdict. The specific operationalisation of intelligence, health, or poverty is not derivable here and is reported as `gap`. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. This verdict comes from the figures; world sources only corroborate it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is handing a reader a recording instead of a generator, which spends their independence; Fig 2 :42 prices that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong"). It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter. A second, larger cost is mistaking a convention for a verdict, which Fig 10 :47 names directly.
- **undoable:** yes. No named measure is asserted, so no primitive is withdrawn if the verdict falls. The three checkable sub-claims in `08` can be removed or repaired independently; the falsifiers are written down and the lineage is recorded. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes rival constructs producing the same score would name the slot; a demonstration that a reading moving with frame preferences still settles a construct would break the anchor claim; a showing that an operational definition grounds itself with no outside anchor would break the declared-meaning claim; independent frames with genuinely separate evidence are required for corroboration, not frames sharing a pool. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
