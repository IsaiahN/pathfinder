## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"You should then be able to connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with and grasp the shape of."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: *reversal is asymmetric where a refusal was added or a habitat substituted, because the record grows while reach can fall, and recovery requires growth rather than deletion.* A return time of 14 years for a particular lake is a name; the shape is the ordered comparison plus the growth requirement.

The distinction matters because the question asks "when can a system recover," which invites a threshold value. The figures return a condition structure instead: which domain is in play, what the anchor is, which of the three questions (SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD) is owed, and what would refute the claim.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends a failure criterion, not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, because by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question, the stop is the four-domain census in `01`: there is no fifth domain with a nameable anchor, so the search stops there.
- Fig 9 :32 again: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The kind of thing here is a recovery route, not a recovery date.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A threshold crossing is a gate, not a settlement. A measured return trajectory against a non-updating reference would settle a particular system's recovery; no such measurement was taken here.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. World sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are used this way only: ecology and physics supply effect-shape corroboration, never the verdict.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all. It also warns: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The choice of "recover" as return time versus disturbance absorbed is such a declared primitive; declaring it makes it auditable, not neutral.

## 4. The instrument question, not the word question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the governing test for this folder. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: before and after a crossing the same state variables can be recorded while the arrangement of reinforcing loops differs (Fig 12 :13-17, :117). An instrument that reads only the variables cannot comment on the arrangement, so it cannot tell recovery from a different state that happens to read the same. What is owed is a discriminator, not a new definition. This is the reason F8 in `01` reports a gap of the right kind rather than forcing an answer.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a mechanism, such as a feedback loop or a habitat substitution?

**No, with a condition.** The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`. The figures already hold the network atom (Fig 13 :23-24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*) and the comparison bond (Fig 12 :85-89). Naming a feedback arrangement or a habitat substitution is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What is forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"* (Fig 6 :22). The binding adds no atom.

**Correction to my own first reading.** In an earlier pass I wrote that "shape, not a name" bars the derivation from saying anything about mechanism at all. That overstates the rule. The correct form is: the framework returns the shape of the recovery, and a particular mechanism is licensed only by a ground reading or a provenanced import. The correction is kept here rather than silently erased. It does not change the verdict, because no mechanism is asserted as `grounded` anywhere in this folder.

## 6. Why the question's own word is a preference, not a term

Formula step 2: *"the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* A frame searching for "recovery" carries that shape as a preference: unbound, predicting nothing, accruing no residual, so no internal check can see it. The remedy is a discipline: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else. That is why the README binds recovery as *per-slot reach restored against a non-updating ground* before any derivation, and why the outreach email states the refuter before the theory. *The Library of Babel*: the question is the anchor that lets a searcher know whether to stop or turn.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of well-known resilience researchers assembled to agree with this shape would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let a measurement settle it.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So every substantive file in this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle whether any real system will recover after crossing. Saying otherwise would be a mode stated as a verdict (Seat Map :281).

## 8. One-paragraph reading of "shape" for CMP-0045

For this question a shape is the condition structure that any answer must have, stated before a number exists: a threshold is an ordered comparison, a crossing that adds a refusal lowers reach while the record grows, a refused term cannot cycle back without new growth, the recovery questions are ordered SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD, a habitat substitution is permanent work, and the whole thing must be read per slot against a ground that does not update. The framework does not return a recovery time, a critical value, or the name of the intervention that will work in a named system. A particular value is licensed only by the ground settling it or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Nothing in this folder has either, so the shape is `specified` and the question of any real system's fate stays open.
