## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the failure of the prediction that the current control preserves the regime after a crossing. Arity: a binary comparison plus an action and an observation, so a compound, not a scalar. Symmetry: broken, because the threshold operator `⋛` is ordered and its symmetry column is `no`. Scale: local crossing, with propagation across levels through settled molecules (Fig 12 :104). Per slot: a global average can be near zero while one slot still carries positive live mass (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). Anchor: a measurement against a ground that does not update.
- **Domain 1, the threshold.** Actors: two quantities and the comparison. Currency: which is larger. Anchor: the measurement. Partition. Nameable anchor, so a genuine domain.
- **Domain 2, closure and reach.** Actors: library, atoms, closure, refusals, candidate terms. Currency: reach, non-monotone. Anchor: derivability under everything held plus ground settlement. Partition, becoming a union when two refusals bind.
- **Domain 3, membrane and habitat.** Actors: methods up, head starts down, habitats, contact actors. Currency: contact and `R_T`. Anchor: the measured gap and the non-updating ground, distinct from the updating habitat. Substitution rather than removal.
- **Domain 4, ground and channel.** Actors: ground, channel, checker, outside frames. Currency: whether a reading arrives. Anchor: the ground.
- **No fifth domain.** A recovery percentage has no anchor and aggregates away the per-slot signal, so it is refused rather than admitted.
- **Union.** The four conditions overlap; a single lever (reverse the control) leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Restore or verify the channel (D4) before reading the comparison (D1); enumerate the remaining reach (D2) before choosing the route (D3). The vocabulary/objective link is the one that lacks a single instrument (Fig 3 :27).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 6's reach law and Fig 11's habitat law, I derived from Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 3's dependency ordering. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 6 :42-46 (record grows, reach falls, no cycle back), Fig 4 :21/:28-30 (directional membrane), Fig 11 :61-67 (asymmetric introduction, habitat substitution), Operators :49/:51 (ordered comparison).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: threshold is ordered; crossing can lower reach; reversal requires growth; SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD; exclusion unavailable.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an intervention claim about a real system, for example a lake flipped to a turbid state, with a proposed nutrient load reduction and a predicted return trajectory.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the objective**, return time versus disturbance absorbed. The shorthand says "recover"; the concrete claim must pick one, with a different anchor and a different instrument (Gunderson 2000, Allen et al. 2016);
- the **per-slot indexing**, which a system-level average destroys (Fig 1 :22);
- the **discriminator**, an instrument that distinguishes the before and after arrangements while variables read equal. Without it the concretisation cannot tell recovery from a different state with the same reading;
- the **habitat and channel state**, whether a substitution occurred and whether the checker's channel is live. The shorthand states the rule; the concrete case must measure both, and two habitat failures are silent (Fig 11 :68-69).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: if a term lost standing in the closure, the reverse requires growth, not deletion. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
