## 2. Wider literature relevant to this question

The four substantive sources opened from outside the framework form two low-overlap pools. Ecology and resilience science (Beisner 2012; Scheffer et al. 2001 via the reproduction; Gunderson 2000; Allen et al. 2016) is one pool: they cite each other and share the alternative-stable-states literature, so their agreement is one witness, not four. Physics and engineering (Wikipedia's hysteresis overview, which covers control systems, ferromagnetism, phase transitions and cell biology) is a second, genuinely lower-overlap pool. The framework essays are a third pool with maximal overlap with the figures, because they share the author; their agreement with the figures carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40-41).

What the pools agree on, in effect terms, is the shape: the state depends on history, the reverse threshold differs from the forward one, more work is needed to return than to leave, and the change is nonlinear. The figures add the mechanism that the pools do not supply: the record grows while reach can fall, a refusal does not delete, and recovery therefore requires growth rather than an un-crossing.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** : *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full framework before a reader has described the residual is a recording, not a generator (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Record the verdict on stage 1 first.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering any figure text.
- **Seat Map :163** : *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :61-63** : introduction is available, exclusion is not. An unsolicited push cannot be un-sent, so prefer opt-in venues and an opt-in download page.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the three local figure links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the bound meaning of "recover", and the five refuters. Ask for the reader's own failure criterion. Do not send the derivation or the figures. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the formula and the canonical usage rule. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user must write down what would refute their own result before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If a checker fires one of the five refuters, stop and re-census rather than showing the framework.
