## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:28 (slot indexing), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 12 :87/:121 (threshold and cycle), Fig 10 :24 (channel), Fig 4 :30 (lossy crossing).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a price spell is a reading with several sources; the either/or is a union of conditions with different bonds; the load-bearing condition changes with the shock; no universal side is returned until an instrument pays.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete economics claim: "firms are slow because of menu costs and contracts" or "firms are slow because they act strategically."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** of the residual, which a single economy-wide frequency destroys;
- the **bond distinction** between a threshold and a cycle, which a two-share decomposition erases;
- the **source-discriminating requirement**, which an argument from the price path cannot satisfy;
- the **lossy crossing** from a per-slot classification to an index frequency, which changes the answer when temporary sales are removed.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: stillness at a slot is a reading with several sources, and the slot-level market rather than the pooled frequency is what settles any claim about it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no firm-level source-discriminating instrument exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap.
