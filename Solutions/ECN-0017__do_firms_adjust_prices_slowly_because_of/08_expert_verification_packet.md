**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No firm-level source-discriminating measurement is claimed. ## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A price that stays fixed at one firm while cost, demand, information or rivals' prices move is a reading with several possible sources; the reading cannot name its own cause. The either/or "costs and contracts versus strategy" is a union of five conditions with different bonds and different anchors, not a two-share partition: an adjustment-cost threshold, a strategic feedback whose return path is the rival's price, an information channel that can be stale while the price is not sticky, a cost and contract state reaching the rule, and a measurement channel that pools the spells into a frequency. The load-bearing condition changes with the size and sign of the shock, so no standing coefficient or universal side is licensed. What is offered is the shape any settleable answer must have, ten falsifiers written in advance, and the design that would close the question.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the question is a union of overlapping conditions, and a per-slot residual is never an average. A universal choice of one side is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"
- **Refuter:** a single adjustment speed that predicts prospectively and per named firm-slot which price will next reset across a change in index composition and after removing temporary sales; or a verbatim figure line that types and settles the source. Either breaks C1.

### C2 — economics-facing; checkable by an economist against the price-setting literature

- **Statement:** the two candidate causes carry different bonds. The cost-and-contract case is a threshold comparison (adjust only if the gain exceeds the adjustment cost). The strategic case is a feedback cycle whose return path is the rival's anticipated response. They are not opposite ends of one dial.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities" and Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? In the menu-cost model the firm compares the gain B − A with the cost Z, and Z acts as a buffer for small shifts. In the kinked-demand model the rival response creates a discontinuity in marginal revenue, so marginal cost can move without price moving. Stigler's criticism is that the kink's mechanism is not specified. Does a single coefficient capture both, or are the mechanisms categorically different?
- **Refuter:** a zero-width threshold band with smooth response to arbitrarily small shocks, or a measured rival response function without a discontinuity at the incumbent price. Either breaks C2.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a world-facing instrument

- **Statement:** the fixed price does not determine its source, and the firm-to-index crossing is lossy. Sticky information is a separate channel condition. The round-trip gap must be measured.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **What to check:** the CPi frequency studies share one evidence pool; the US mean spell more than doubles to 11 months when temporary sales are removed; sticky-information models have flexible prices and motivated "dual stickiness" models. Can a per-slot source classification be carried up to an index and back without loss?
- **Refuter:** an instrument that discriminates all four sources at the decision moment, or an invertible firm-to-index aggregation. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the framework decides between menu costs and strategy. It returns a shape, not a universal cause.
- Not a two-share decomposition of stickiness. A variance share is a budget filter, not a witness (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
- Not that the published frequency of price change is the ground. It is a pooled reading; the slot-level market is the ground.
- Not that information stickiness is a renamed repricing cost. It is a distinct channel condition with a different anchor.
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating measurement was run here.
- Not that naming a repricing cost, contract schedule or kink is forbidden. Naming a held binding in a named slot is provenance from the economics frame; claiming the framework derived it, or that it moves the wall, is forbidden (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at the firm-slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here:

1. At the decision moment, was the repricing cost below the gain from changing price? (threshold test)
2. Was a contract term binding on that date? (contract condition)
3. Did a rival's recent or anticipated price change alter the gain? (cycle test)
4. Had the relevant information arrived and been attended to? (channel test)
5. Does the per-slot classification survive aggregation to the index, or is the result a pooled reading with a nonzero `R_T`?

A design that records all five, drawn from a frame that does not reuse the CPI microdata estimator, would be the lower-overlap instrument the question owes.
