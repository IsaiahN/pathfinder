## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

A shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0017 the shape is: *a price spell is governed by a set of per-slot conditions with typed bonds, and the observed stillness does not by itself name which condition is load-bearing.* The specific answer "it is menu costs" or "it is strategic complementarity" is a name, not the shape.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed." That is the figure's union rule in essay form: a question that will not close under search may be a bundle, not a hard single problem.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote carried from exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23, on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For ECN-0017 this means a firm-level design that records repricing cost, contract term, rival price and information set at the decision moment can name the load-bearing condition in that slot. Until such a design pays, "menu cost" and "strategy" are bindings from the economics frame onto conditions already available, not new primitives the framework discovered.

## 4. Why the either/or is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several paths, one reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The reading does not determine the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was "the arrival of a better instrument," not an argument, that resolved the color case.

A price that did not move is the green reading. Repricing cost, contract, rival response and stale information are the candidate sources. Asking the either/or to choose between two of them from the reading alone repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is the discriminator, not a better argument for one side.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming menu costs, contracts or a strategic kink?

**No, and this corrects an overstatement I initially made.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Adjustment cost, a contract schedule, a rival's price and an information set are all already available in the economics frame and in the firm's own library. Naming one as the binding condition in a named slot is **provenance from a frame whose closure differs**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (i) claiming the framework derives which condition must govern from six abstract terms alone, and (ii) claiming that a binding moves the wall, when Fig 6 :21 says "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The earlier, stronger reading was retracted in `01` section 7.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prominent economists assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the design a budget to be tried, and let the slot-level market settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened 2026-11-23: "However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the price-setting claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. The one-paragraph shape

A price spell is stillness at a slot while the world moves. The framework says that stillness is a reading with several possible sources, so the reading cannot choose among them; that the candidate conditions form a union with different bonds and different anchors, not a two-share partition; and that what the slot returns, not what a taxonomy says, is the anchor. It refuses to name a universal cause because the instrument that would discriminate the sources at the decision moment is not held. That refusal is the shape, and it is checkable: a refuter is written in advance, and a source-discriminating design would close it.
