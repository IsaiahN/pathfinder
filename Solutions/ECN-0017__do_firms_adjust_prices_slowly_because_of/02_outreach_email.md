# Outreach draft — validate the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and the ten refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated in the message. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one output on price stickiness before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about price setting. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a price that stays fixed at one firm while cost, demand or rivals' prices move is a *reading*, and the reading does not name its own source. The same stillness is consistent with a repricing cost that is smaller than the gain from changing the price, with a contract that binds on that date, with a rival whose response would cancel the gain, and with information that has not arrived. So the question "is it costs and contracts, or is it strategy?" does not have one answer across firms. It is a union of conditions with different structures: an adjustment-cost threshold; a strategic feedback whose return path is the rival's price; an information channel; and a measurement channel that pools the spells into a frequency. Which condition binds changes with the size and sign of the shock, so there is no standing coefficient and no two-share split.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework decides between menu costs and strategy. It returns a shape, not a universal cause. Naming a repricing cost or a kinked demand curve in a named slot is a binding from the economics literature onto a condition already available, provenanced as such, not a derivation from the framework. A variance share is a filter for where to look, not a verdict on the cause.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. one adjustment speed that predicts, prospectively and per named firm-slot, which price will next reset across a change in index composition and after removing temporary sales;
> 2. an instrument that discriminates repricing-cost stillness from contract stillness from rival-response stillness from information stillness at the decision moment, stable under a change of contract classification;
> 3. a single intervention that restores all five conditions and leaves no categorically different residue;
> 4. a zero-width threshold band in which prices respond smoothly to arbitrarily small shocks.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures are the authority, not my summary of them. I can send the verbatim figure lines and the derivation on request.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a price-setting problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The write-up warns that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula, so each run needs its own ground and its own mode label. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual (a price spell with several possible sources), the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the verbatim figure lines and the links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently written it is a shape plus refuters, not a universal cause. Repair any breach of the ten refuters in `01` before sending.
