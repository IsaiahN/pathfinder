## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the question

1. **Criteria of refutation must be laid down beforehand.** Popper's SEP entry says tests need *"criteria of refutation... laid down beforehand"*, and that a single conflicting instance is methodologically insufficient. This is a separate field reaching the same shape as Fig 5 :59 (persistent residual) and Fig 5 :65-66 (write the sought-for shape as an ordinary term). Overlap is low: philosophy of science versus a domain-agnostic composition theory. It is corroboration, not a second derivation of the figures.
2. **Failed synthesis is not the same as falsification.** The RNA-world and abiogenesis sources both describe a leading paradigm whose predicted steps have not been completed in the lab, while explicitly declining to call it refuted. That matches Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*, and the underivable/unreachable-at-budget distinction at Fig 8 :39-40. Do not present a failed synthesis as a killing finding; present it as budget information until a positive, persistent, discriminating residual is shown.

A surface conflict exists between Popper's *"single counter-instance falsifies"* logical criterion and the framework's persistence requirement. Re-census resolves it: Popper's logical criterion is about what a universal statement excludes, while his own applied-methodology caveat immediately restricts actual falsification. Fig 5 :59 governs the community-level cause of abandonment, which is the applied question here. Different objects, no strain after re-census. Kuhn's *"accumulation of particularly troublesome anomalies"* is in the same applied register and agrees with persistence.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the question and the refuter; offer links only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed this framework before answering has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would count as a discriminating finding before giving them the RNA-world or Popper links.
- **Fig 2 :35** — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not select recipients for receptiveness and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends the loop as a reusable method, not an answer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-property shape and the six pre-registered refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure for a model they know; (3) record that answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Residual and refuter first, theory second (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
- **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a reader holds the framework's interiors, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40, Fig 10 :42).
- **Stage 3 sends a method.** The loop is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user still needs a ground and a written refuter for their own problem (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").
- **Nothing is sent by this agent.** Hosting and sending are the user's work. Prefer an opt-in download page or a public submission venue over a pushed attachment.

## Hard precondition

Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter list in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 must be checked by an outside reader before any send. If one fires, revise the shape first.
