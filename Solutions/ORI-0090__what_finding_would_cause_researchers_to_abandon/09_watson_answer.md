## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a leading model M's prediction Gamma(b,a) and what the ground returns o' on a named slot s. Arity: two relata (M and the finding f), plus a routing rule that assigns the community decision. Symmetry: not symmetric. A refusal is negation, a unary operator that joins nothing; the order perceive, route, re-fit or mint or refuse, settle, is ordered. Scale: three scales in one chain, bench and field, model, community. The question is a **union**, not one hard question: abandon is REFUTED, revise is BROKEN rebinding or BROKEN mechanism (Formula step 2). Unbundle before search.
- **Domain 1, ground and instrument.** Actors: experiments, instruments, rock and isotope record, field sites, meteorites. Currency: observations o', measurements, residual mass on slot s. Anchor: the ground, which *"does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"* (Fig 2 :29). Question type: partition, what is readable at all (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Domain 2, model closure.** Actors: competing models (RNA-first, metabolism-first, lipid-first, coevolution), their bound predicting terms and bonds, description length. Currency: residual R, cost of a term, reach of the closure. Anchor: the ground; models range against it and do not negotiate (Fig 2 :45). Question type: union of three route bins.
- **Domain 3, verification and community.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, editors, funders, public venues. Currency: verdicts, agreement, prestige, residuals. Anchor: must be the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35, :40). Question type: partition, a verdict is anchored or it is a convention. If its anchor were consensus it would not qualify as a checking domain.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the finding on an instrument (Fig 3 :27); route it (Formula step 2); describe its shape before naming a rival frame (Fig 9 :27-28); re-fit, mint or refuse; settle against the ground (Formula step 5); record the lineage. A reading below the break, such as counting papers that stopped citing a model, is a reading of nothing.

Strain test applied: Fig 5 :59 persistence and Kuhnian accumulation are the same applied criterion in different vocabularies; Popper's logical single-instance falsifiability is about what a universal statement excludes, not about community-level causes. Re-census resolves both. The map of actual models onto a shared atom set is unmeasured and is labelled single-source, not smoothed into a result.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure set governs this question and returns a shape, not a name. A finding causes abandonment or substantial revision when it has four properties: it is an anchor reading that does not update on model commitment; it carries positive residual mass on a named slot; its residual persists under re-fitting and it reads the arrangement that distinguishes competing models; and refusing the failing term is cheaper than keeping it, in a vocabulary containing negation. "Abandon" routes to REFUTED and mints a refusal, which removes the model's standing to be composed over without deleting it from the record. "Revise" routes to re-fit or mint and keeps the term. A failed synthesis is budget information, not a refutation. A consensus is a convention, not an anchor. The name of the finding is withheld until a ground pays for it. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The answer is written as four refusable properties with six refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is asking outside readers for agreement instead of a finding, which spends their independence; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter and recording the stage-1 verdict first.
- **undoable:** yes. Each property can be withdrawn independently without touching the others, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state them separately. No model or primitive has to be withdrawn, because a refusal adds a term and removes standing, not content.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot-discriminating instrument that reads arrangement rather than ingredients would settle P3; a demonstration that a missing step is underivable rather than unreachable would settle the budget clause in P2; a documented case of legitimate abandonment with no ground reading moving would break P1; a vocabulary without negation that nevertheless abandons would break P4; a derivation of a specific finding from the figures alone would break the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
