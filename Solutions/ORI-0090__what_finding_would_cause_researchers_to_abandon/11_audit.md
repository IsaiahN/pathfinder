## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I started from the residual as stated in `01` §0 and redid the census before reading the primary's §1 conclusions. WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a model's prediction and a ground reading on a slot. Arity: two relata plus a routing rule. The community decision is not a third independent relatum; it is the route assignment once the residual is described. So this is a two-relatum residual with a classifier. Symmetry: not symmetric. "Abandon" is ¬, a unary operator; the route has an ordered structure (perceive, route, re-fit or mint or refuse, settle). Scale: bench and field, model, community, with a lossy transform between each pair. Question type: **union**, because the question names two outcomes that the route separates. A case in two route bins is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, ground and instrument.** Actors: experiments, instruments, rock and isotope record, field sites. Currency: observations and residual mass on a named slot. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Partition: what is readable at all.
- **Domain 2, model closure.** Actors: competing models and their bound predicting terms, plus the operator set including ¬. Currency: residual, description length, reach. Anchor: the ground. Union: three route bins.
- **Domain 3, verification and community.** Actors: researchers, reviewers, venues. Currency: verdicts and agreement. Anchor: must be the ground; agreement is the forbidden anchor. Partition: anchored verdict or convention.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the finding before naming a rival frame; route before mint; re-fit before refusing unless persistence points at refusal; settle only against the ground; record the lineage. A publication count is a reading below the break.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I reached it from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's negation-is-not-a-bond amendment, whereas the primary reached it from Formula step 2 and Fig 5's refuted kind.
