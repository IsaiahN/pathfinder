# The Tether's answer: what finding would make researchers abandon or revise an origin-of-life model?

**Mode: `specified` on P1 to P4, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No origin-of-life model was tested.

## 1. The residual it answers

A leading origin-of-life model M predicts outcomes through its bound terms. On some object slot s the ground returns o'. The residual R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| on that slot either shrinks under re-fitting or does not. Question 90 asks what property of o' makes a community route that residual to "abandon" or "revise". Abandon and revise are not one outcome: revise is re-fit or mint (Formula step 2), abandon is a refusal (¬) of a bound term (Fig 5 :53). The question is a union to be unbundled before search (Fig 9).

## 2. The shape: four properties a finding must have

1. **Anchored (P1).** It is a ground reading from an instrument, experiment, or record that does not change its answer because of what model adherents think. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* A reinterpretation of existing data that appears only after a community changes its mind is not a finding of this kind.
2. **Local and positive (P2).** It lands on a named slot and carries positive residual mass there, not a global impression. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 5 :21: *"the gap has real mass somewhere"*. "The field no longer finds the model convincing" has no slot and no live mass.
3. **Persistent and discriminating (P3).** It is a pattern whose residual persists and does not trend down under the model's best re-fitting, and it reads the **arrangement** that distinguishes the models, not the ingredient list they share. Fig 5 :59: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
4. **Payable as a refusal (P4).** Refusing the failing term costs less than holding it (Fig 5 :43-44: *"A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before"*), and the community's vocabulary contains negation, so it can say "this term is false" and not only "this term is incomplete" (Operators table :58).

A finding with all four is sufficient, in this derivation, to cause abandonment. P1 and P2 without P3 give an anomaly or a budget shortfall, not a refutation. P1 to P3 without P4 change the science without changing what the community can say.

## 3. Verdicts per domain, kept separate

- **D1, ground and instrument — `triangulated`.** A finding must be an anchor reading with positive mass on a named slot. Fig 2 :29 (anchor rule) and Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual) are low-overlap and agree. World corroboration from the abiogenesis literature, which states *"The transition of non-life to life has not been observed experimentally"* (Wikipedia, 2026) and locates open problems on named slots such as protocell evolvability. No conflict.
- **D2, model closure — `triangulated`.** Persistence plus bond-discrimination is the operational content of "cause". Fig 5 :59 (shrink test read with opposite sign) and Fig 12 :117 (isomer definition) are low-overlap and agree. Popper's SEP entry independently requires *"criteria of refutation... laid down beforehand"* and warns that *"a single conflicting or counter-instance is never sufficient methodologically for falsification"*; Kuhn's SEP entry says *"It is only the accumulation of particularly troublesome anomalies that poses a serious problem for the existing disciplinary matrix."* Both corroborate persistence from a separate field. The mapping of actual models onto a shared atom set is single-source and unmeasured: a figure-licensed shape, not a chemical result.
- **D3, verification and community — `single-source` for the anchor requirement within the figures, with separate-field corroboration.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"* and Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* forbid a poll of researchers as an anchor. Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* forbids first contact that carries this framework. The prestige rule from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened 2026-11-23) corroborates from a separate essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Prestige and agreement are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

The three verdicts are **not averaged**. A finding that is anchored but not persistent is a D1 result, not a partial abandonment. A community that abandons without a ground is a D3 failure, not a successful D2 refutation.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not that a failed experiment refutes a model.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :39-40 separates *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget"*. A synthesis that did not work is budget information until a positive, persistent, discriminating residual is shown.
- **Not that consensus, fashion, or authority causes legitimate abandonment.** That is a convention (Fig 2 :35, :40), and the correlation it selects for is disqualifying for a verdict.
- **Not the name of the finding.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The canonical README says the framework returns *"a shape, not a name"*. Any specific isotope ratio or molecule is an import from the abiogenesis frame, provenanced as such.
- **Not that abandonment deletes the model.** Fig 5 :55: *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* The record keeps the failed model; only its standing to be used as a premise falls.
- **Not grounded.** No measurement here settles `left(R,phi)` for any model.

## 5. What would settle it — the ground, not more agreement

A source-discriminating instrument or record, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the color case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Slot discriminator:** does an instrument read the arrangement (which molecular species must exist before heredity starts) rather than only the ingredient list? If not, no finding can discriminate the models, and P3 is untestable here.
2. **Persistence test:** does the anomalous result stay flat on its slot under the model's best re-fitting across independent labs and settings? If it trends down, point the re-fit at it, not a refusal.
3. **Budget test:** is the missing product underivable in principle, or merely unreachable at this budget? Fig 8 :40-43 issues different instructions for the two.
4. **Standing test:** after a community abandons a model, does the ground still refuse it when a new lineage composes over the record? A refusal that cannot cycle back without new growth is the safe computation Fig 6 :44-46 describes.

## 6. The honest stop

**Shape reached, name unreached.** The figure set closes R at four properties. What it cannot do is tell us which finding will appear, because the figures are domain-agnostic and return a shape. The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (canonical README). This is a `candidate` result: a ground reading that fires one of the six refuters in `01` §4 would move it, or move the relevant property out of the answer. Agreement from readers, including agreement from the outside experts in `08`, is never such a reading.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the finding that will kill an origin-of-life model. It gives the shape such a finding must have. It must come from an anchor, a measurement or record that does not move when model adherents push on it. It must land on a named slot with positive residual mass, not a vague sense that the field has moved on. Its residual must persist and not trend down under the model's best re-fitting, and it must read the arrangement of prebiotic ingredients that distinguishes the models, not the ingredient list they share. And refusing the failing term must cost less than keeping it, in a vocabulary that can say "this is false" and not only "this needs adjusting." A failed lab synthesis is not such a finding: a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence. A consensus is not such a finding either: agreement is a convention, not an anchor. What the framework offers is the shape any decisive finding must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The name stays open until the ground pays for it.
