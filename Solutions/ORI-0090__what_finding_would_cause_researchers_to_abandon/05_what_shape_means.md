## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (FIGURES_README_canonical.md). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (re-opened): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a ground-anchored, local, persistent, discriminating, payable finding" is a shape. "The finding is a 13C/12C anomaly in a specific formation" is a name. The figure set licenses the first and not the second.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay names the precise failure this question is prone to: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union reading in `01` §0. "Abandon" and "substantially revise" are two problems masquerading as one; searching for a single finding before splitting them is how a search gets lost.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For this question the effect description is P1 to P4 in `01` §3, written before the world search. The world search then tests them rather than choosing them.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. If an abiogenesis specialist names a specific discriminating experiment, that is an import from their frame; it is recorded as such and carries no figure authority by itself.

## 4. Why "abandon" cannot be read off a population-level projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-opened) gives the discriminator mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. Its color case: an instrument reads green *"it cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. The resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a community's behaviour (papers stop citing a model, a consensus forms) is a projection. Several underlying sources can produce the same projection: a genuine ground refutation, a funding shift, a prestige cascade, or a fashion. Reading "abandonment" off the projection alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, which in D3 means an anchor that does not update on the community's commitments (Fig 2 :29).

And from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened): *"You may all agree and be wrong."* Agreement is not a discriminator.

## 5. The answer to "what licenses a specific finding?" — nothing in the framework

Nothing in the figure set names a finding. A specific finding is licensed only by the ground settling a term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the honest content of Fig 9 :32 and the canonical README's *"a shape, not a name"*.

The record in `01` does **not** commit the forbidden error of naming. It commits the opposite, useful error risk: it can be read as saying no finding ever could cause abandonment. That reading is also wrong. The shape is reachable; the name is withheld until a ground pays for it.

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote §3 as a flat prohibition: "a specific finding is never licensed by the framework." That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Gamma. The relevant object here is not a missing primitive but an unobserved **finding** on a slot the models already predict. Naming such a finding is not inventing a seventh term; it is a binding from the abiogenesis frame onto a held slot, provenanced as such. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding is figure-derived, or that it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none).

Both claims are now recorded: a name needs a ground or a provenanced import (§3), and the ban applies to figure-derived naming, not to honest, sourced bindings (this section). Neither is silently erased.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (re-opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of eminent researchers asked whether they agree that a model should be abandoned is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any origin-of-life model and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.
