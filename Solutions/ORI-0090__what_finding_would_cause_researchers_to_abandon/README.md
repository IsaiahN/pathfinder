**Status:** done.

## What this package answers, in effect terms

The question asks for the property a finding F must have such that a leading origin-of-life model M is abandoned or substantially revised. Restated as an effect: what observation o' drives the residual R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| on some object slot s to persist and not trend down, so that a refusal of a bound predicting term of M becomes cheaper than continuing to hold M, and so that the community verdict is anchored to the ground rather than to agreement?

An answer would look like this: a characterisation of F in effect terms (its arity, symmetry, scale, anchor, and cost), not the name of a specific isotope ratio or molecule. That characterisation is bound as an ordinary term, written down before the run, and made refusable (Formula "Direction", Fig 5 :65-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes them; they are not reproduced here.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that a model should be abandoned.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A poll of researchers is a convention, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
