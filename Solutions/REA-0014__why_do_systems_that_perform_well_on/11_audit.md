## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :70 and :39 (substituted habitat, capability as pair), Fig 10 :72 (room tracks question), Fig 9 :60 (filter not witness), Fig 4 :30 (measure the gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: the score is a substituted-habitat reading; capability is agent-and-habitat; the fixed room makes the score a symptom; more search in the same closure does not reach the new term; the honest report is `R_T`.
3. **Concretization (T_E):** a claim about a specific system: "this model's 95 percent in-domain accuracy means it will work when we deploy it in the new hospital / camera trap / user population."

**`R_T` is nonzero in kind and unmeasured in magnitude.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **which** of the five union conditions, which the shorthand deliberately leaves open but a deployment claim must name;
- the **value** of the gap for this system and this habitat change, which the shorthand requires be measured and the claim above simply assumes away;
- the **independence of the test habitat**, which the shorthand requires but the concrete claim treats as already satisfied by "more evaluation".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a score obtained in a substituted habitat is not by itself a verdict about capability in another habitat. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this folder. It is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints. Treating "the score is the capability" would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a required gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
