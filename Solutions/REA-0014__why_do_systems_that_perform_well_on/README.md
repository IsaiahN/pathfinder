## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own terms:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list chosen for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A benchmark score is itself a correlated in-frame reading; stacking more agreeing readers does not add a ground.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the venues in `03`. The people who can help are the ones with a public, checkable record of working on distribution shift and robustness, reached through an opt-in submission route, and asked for a refutation rather than belief.

## Honest standing of this package

Every derivation here is a **simulation**, checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. Per *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, LLM output is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This package cannot settle the question it reports on.
