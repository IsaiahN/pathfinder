## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 states: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the constrained answer to "why does the score and the contact come apart?" It is not a named culprit. The shape we derived is: the benchmark is a substituted habitat; capability is a property of agent and habitat; when the question changes and the room does not, the score becomes a symptom; and the honest report is the round-trip gap between the score and deployment. Any particular cause (a texture cue, a source tag, an underspecified predictor, a saturated metric) is an **instance** of that shape, not the shape itself. The shape constrains which causes are admissible; it does not select one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* gives the method of proximity: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." We used the vocabulary already closest to the residual (habitat, harness, room, channel, closure, round trip) and let the figure lines narrow it.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual was stated as an effect (score stays reportable while success collapses), not as a cause ("the model overfit").
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the form of the stage-1 email: a description of what would be seen if the shape were true, checkable without the theory.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is at the figure-verdict shape plus the pre-registered refuter, not at a named mechanism.

## 3. The union clue in the essays

*The Library of Babel* names the same compound the census found: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is Fig 9 :55 written in essay form. It is why we did not force a single cause. Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." The shape of the answer must be a union shape: several overlapping conditions, with the intersection becoming a specifically describable compound.

## 4. Why the score cannot name the source (the metamerism point)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source." And: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay adds: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

A benchmark score is exactly such an instrument. Many internal states and many habitats can produce the same high reading: a genuine capability, a shortcut cue, a memorized item, a saturated metric. The reading does not determine the source. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the deployment consequence explicit: "Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy." A benchmark can be a test of in-habitat fluency without being a test of capability under habitat change.

What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name. In this solution the discriminator is the out-of-habitat round trip and the independent test habitat, not a claim about which cue was used.

## 5. Contact, and why agreement exhausts reach

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the figure law in plain language: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (quoting Fig 11 :40). It also states the subtle cost that bears directly on benchmark saturation: "Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach." A model and its benchmark, trained and tuned together until the score is perfect, are two frames pulled together by long close contact. Their agreement is smooth and looks like success, while the extra reach that would let either see the changed context has been spent. Fig 8 :48: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."

So the answer's shape includes a warning about its own verification: a robustness evaluation built too close to the training frame has the same defect as the benchmark it replaces.

## 6. Prestige and the status of this derivation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A well-known benchmark, a famous leaderboard, or a prestigious reviewer can give a claim a budget to be tried. None of them settles whether capability survives a habitat change.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also warns: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim, they cannot settle it, and no part of them is labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Q: what licenses a particular cause in the slot?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular cause is licensed only by a discriminating measurement in a changed habitat, or by a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test. Until one of those, the named cause is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is the same limit the exemplar reached on naming Gradient: "a shape, not a name" governs what the framework can return, not what a researcher may hypothesize with provenance attached.
