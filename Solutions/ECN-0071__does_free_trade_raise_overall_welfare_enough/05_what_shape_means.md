## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the missing thing

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be asserted before the missing term is available. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: any admissible answer must keep the per-slot residual live and must not let a global total discharge it; the "enough to justify" term is missing and stays open.
- Not a shape but a name: a particular welfare weight, a particular compensation scheme, a particular policy verdict. Those would fill the slot, which the framework says it cannot do.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on new terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually". This is the essay-level twin of Fig 9 :5's split rule.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach sends the slot residual and its refuter, not the whole theory.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". The residual was described before the trade-economics frame was chosen.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here that means the final claim is the initial effect statement, refined: the slot is live and the anchor is missing.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a reading cannot name its source: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." Applied here: a positive aggregate surplus is a reading that several underlying distributions could produce. Naming from it a settled welfare conclusion is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument at the slot, not a name for the total.

## 4. The specific question: is this a gap or a hidden name?

**Answer: a gap, correctly characterised.** The missing thing is the interpersonal weighting rule that would let one person's loss be offset by another's gain. No figure line supplies it, and the canonical README says the framework returns a shape for exactly such a missing thing. So the honest output is the shape of that rule (it would have to be a figure line that states a distributional weight or a settlement rule) and an open slot, not the rule itself.

This is not an evasion. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The verdict is "unreachable here", which is a verdict, not a silence.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (recorded, not erased)

**Overstatement retracted.** In an earlier reading I treated "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any of the quantities in this question, including "aggregate surplus" and "concentrated loss". That is wrong. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the library. Both the aggregate and the slots are already nameable and are named in the economics frame; they are not missing. What is missing is the normative rule that would connect them.

What remains standing: the metamerism point in §3. The aggregate reading does not determine the distribution, and a conclusion naming a settled welfare gain from that reading alone is an instrument pretending to comment on its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held quantities.

Both the earlier overstatement and its narrowing are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers prestigious economists to endorse a position on trade is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a local measurement or a refutation, settle it. Also from the same essay: "You may all agree and be wrong."
