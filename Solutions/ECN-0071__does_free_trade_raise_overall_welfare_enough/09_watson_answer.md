## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that free trade raises overall welfare enough to justify concentrated losses among workers and regions. Option A: accept the aggregate comparison as a verdict and treat the concentrated losses as a price already paid. Option B: keep the aggregate and the per-slot residuals as separate verdicts, refuse the type error in "enough to justify", and record the missing normative anchor as a gap. What each leads to: A lets an average discharge a live slot without a slot measurement or a ground settlement, and asks readers to accept a weighting rule no figure supplies; B sends a checkable shape, three written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The contact failure between an aggregate-welfare reading and the slot-level losses. Arity: two conditions plus N slots, so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :5). Symmetry: the aggregate is symmetric under permutation of persons and regions; the slot residuals are not, and the question assumes they are commensurable. Scale: first-order price and production effects versus second and third-order community and health effects (Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 1, the aggregate welfare frame.** Actors: consumers, producers, firms, national accounts, the representative agent. Currency: surplus, GDP, efficiency. Anchor claimed: the measured market total. It fails anchor status because the frame produces the metric it scores itself with (Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."). Verdict: single-source for the frame-internal finding, but triangulated with Fig 2 on the anchor requirement.
- **Domain 2, the per-slot distributional frame.** Actors: displaced workers, households, regions, communities. Currency: income, employment, health, community continuity. Anchor: the ground for each slot, meaning observable local outcomes. This is where the live residual sits. Verdict: triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 11, two figures with low overlap.
- **Domain 3, the justification frame.** Actors: the polity, policymakers, ethicists. Currency: legitimacy. Anchor: none nameable in the figures. Fig 2 :37 gives human judgment as an anchor only for a question about a person, not for aggregating across persons. Verdict: gap.
- **Order by dependency.** Slot ground first, aggregate second, normative comparison third and separate. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

Union, not partition: the aggregate is built from the same people whose slot residuals it proposes to override, so the domains intersect (Fig 9 :54). Strain test applied: Fig 12 :126 was tried as a two-currency statement and retracted as the wrong quantity; Fig 2 :37 was narrowed from an anchor for "justify" to an anchor only for person-level questions. Both corrections are visible in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the question as posed does not. A national total cannot discharge a live per-slot residual, hypothetical compensation is not settlement, concentrated losses may sit several steps out in a community cascade, and "enough to justify" is a mistyped threshold comparison for which no figure supplies an anchor. The three domains keep separate verdicts and are never averaged into one yes/no on trade. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with a `gap` at the normative core. The honest output is the shape an answer must have, the refuters, and the open slot.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending a reader's independence by sending a verdict instead of a residual; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the slot residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The aggregate framing and the gap statement are separable from the figures themselves. A refutation of C3 would remove the cascade claim without touching the no-aggregation shape, and a figure line supplying a weighting rule would close the gap. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot instrument showing that a region with a positive national total has no live residual; a ground reading showing concentrated losses are fully captured at first order; or a verbatim figure line that states a distributional weight or a settlement rule between slots. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
