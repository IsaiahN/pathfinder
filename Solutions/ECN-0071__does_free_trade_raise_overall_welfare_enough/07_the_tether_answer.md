# The Tether's answer on aggregate gain and concentrated loss

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on "enough to justify". Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question joins a claim about a total to a claim about particular people and places, then asks whether the first is "enough" for the second. The residual is the contact failure between those two ways of reading the same population. It is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **A global total does not discharge a live slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state". A positive national figure is compatible with a live loss in a named region. The question's move from "overall" to "justifies" is the averaging operation the figures disqualify.
2. **Possible compensation is not settlement.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." A counterfactual transfer does not pay, so the slot residual stays live.
3. **The losses are a habitat cascade, not a first-order line item.** Fig 11 :52: "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." An instrument that reads only the first order will miss the residual that matters.
4. **The "enough" comparison is mistyped.** The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities". The operands offered are a sum over slots and a set of slot residuals. They are not two quantities of a kind, and flattening the set into one number destroys the arrangement (Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance.").
5. **The normative anchor is missing, so the verdict is gap.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The figures give an anchor for judging an answer about a person, but no rule for weighing one person's loss against another's gain. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that free trade is good or bad. No policy verdict is returned.
- Not that any estimate of gains or losses is right. No welfare data were opened.
- Not that concentrated losses are necessarily larger than aggregate gains. That is an empirical question for the ground, and the figures do not answer it.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final room. The center stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A reading at the slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Slot instrument:** does a named region with a positive national total still carry a live residual in employment, health and community continuity? That is measured at the slot, not read off the total.
- **Cascade test:** does the Slot residual have second and third-order effects, as Fig 11 :52 predicts, or is it fully captured at first order?
- **Anchor test:** is any figure line produced that states a distributional weight or a settlement rule between slots? If yes, F4 closes.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line under which a global total discharges a live slot (breaks F1); a ground reading showing no beyond-first-order effect (breaks F3).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable at any price.** The six-term set and the eight operators are a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing a total from a distribution, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Importing a welfare-weighting rule from a peer frame would add an atom the figures do not license and would debit that frame's independence (Fig 6).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether free trade is worth it. It says that the question mixes two different things: a total over everyone, and a loss that lives in particular people and places. Its residual is indexed by slot, so a healthy national number is fully compatible with a live loss in one region, and averaging the slots into the total is exactly how the signal about where to look disappears. A gain that could compensate a loss is not the same as a gain that pays it. The concentrated losses may sit several steps out in a community, where a first-order aggregate instrument never reads them. And "enough to justify" needs a rule for weighing one person's loss against another's gain that the figures simply do not contain, so the honest answer records that as a gap rather than inventing a number. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, three written refuters, and the instruments that would close the open slot. That is a checkable stop, not an evasion.
