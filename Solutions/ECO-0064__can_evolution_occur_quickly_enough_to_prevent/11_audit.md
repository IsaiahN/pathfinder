## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between a moving adaptive optimum and a population's current trait distribution, coupled across populations. Arity: two quantities in a threshold relation plus a cascade. Symmetry: comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: generational within a population, decadal at the community. Union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, evolutionary ground.** Actors: alleles, standing variation, selection, demography. Currency: variance, heritability, population size. Anchor: measured persistence or extinction. Question type: rate comparison.
- **Domain 2, ecological ground.** Actors: species and interactions, climate, habitat. Currency: abundances and functions. Anchor: measured ecosystem state. Question type: union compound with a per-slot cascade.
- **Domain 3, research frame.** Actors: researchers, experiments, models. Currency: data and predictions. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence zero in this simulated run.
- **Domain 4, application frame.** Actors: managers. Currency: introductions and contact. Anchor: the ground. Maintained mode.

Order by dependency: measure the residual per slot before choosing vocabulary; refuse the universal question before minting an answer (Formula step 2); settle population-level outcomes before composing the community claim (Fig 12 :104).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried through the folder: score per population, compare two rates, require transmission and selection, evolvability is habitat-bound, manage by introduction, the ground decides, universal is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example "population P in habitat H will be rescued if the adaptive response rate exceeds the warming-driven optimum shift before the critical population size is reached."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the anchor** at slot level (which measurement, which time series, which error structure); the shorthand says "the ground decides" but cannot name the instrument, and Fig 6 :34 says *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
- the **operand type of the comparison** at concretisation (Fig 12 :126, two prices in different units);
- the **source of persistence** (plasticity versus range shift versus genetic adaptation), which the shorthand collapses into "adaptive response" and the source-discrimination essay says needs a better instrument.

Two things survive cleanly: the per-slot rule and the two-factor requirement. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
