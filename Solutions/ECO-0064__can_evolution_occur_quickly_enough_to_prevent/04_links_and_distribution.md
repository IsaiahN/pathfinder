## Wider literature that bears on the residual

- **Bell and Gonzalez 2009**, "Evolutionary rescue can prevent extinction following environmental change", Ecology Letters 12(9):942-948. Referenced in both Wikipedia articles and in Schiffers et al.; the foundational experimental result that spatial structure and dispersal can rescue yeast populations. Cited by provenance, not as a source opened this session.
- **Carlson, Cunningham and Westley 2014**, "Evolutionary rescue in a changing world", Trends in Ecology and Evolution 29(9):521-530. Referenced in the opened sources; frames the field as a race under a changing world.
- **Gomulkiewicz and Holt 1995**, "When does natural selection save a population from extinction?", Evolution 49(1):201-207. The origin of the term; cited in the opened sources.
- **Chevin, Lande and Mace 2010**, "Adaptation, plasticity, and extinction in a changing environment: towards a predictive theory", PLoS Biology 8:e1000357. Gives the critical rate of change beyond which a population must decline, the closest world analogue to the figure-derived comparison. Cited in Schiffers et al.

Overlap warning: these papers and the opened PMC reviews draw on the same modelling lineage. Agreement among them is one evidence pool, not independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
