# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` have survived a ground-visible check. The first stage must stand on its own.

**Subject:** Check one prediction about evolutionary rescue before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a population facing a moving climate optimum should be scored per population, not per ecosystem average. The deciding quantity is a threshold comparison between two rates, the rate at which the optimum moves and the rate at which the population's trait distribution tracks it. Adaptive progress per generation has two factors, and either one at zero stops it: fidelity of transmission (heritability, effective additive genetic variance) and the selection pressure actually imposed by the changed environment. Evolvability belongs to the population together with its habitat, not to the species alone. In the managed case a seat can add head starts and contact, but cannot subtract the habitat, so the work is permanent.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that there is a single critical rate of warming that decides this for all species; that short generation time guarantees rescue; that a global biodiversity average tells us which population is in trouble; or that the framework names which intervention to use. The framework returns a shape and a route, not a number and not an answer. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents, and only the record separates them, so the biology I cite carries its own provenance and no more.
>
> **What would refute this shape:**
> 1. showing that one rate comparison decides all slots at once, with no per-population residual and no cascade through interactions;
> 2. demonstrating adaptive progress with transmission zero or selection zero;
> 3. making a population more capable by an improvement that leaves its contact with the habitat unchanged;
> 4. a ground reading for a named species under a named warming rate that the shape cannot accommodate.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I will withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - The worked evolutionary-rescue literature that bears on stage 1: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4275089/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3538450/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim and refuter. Record the recipient's verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, the three links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A Stage 2 or Stage 3 agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
