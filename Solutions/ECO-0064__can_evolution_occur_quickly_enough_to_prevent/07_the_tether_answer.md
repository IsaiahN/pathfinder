# The Tether's answer on evolutionary rescue and collapse

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the universal empirical question. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A changing climate moves the adaptive optimum away from a population's current trait distribution. The question asks whether the population's adaptive response closes that gap before its absolute fitness goes negative long enough for the population to be lost, with knock-on effects through interactions. The residual is indexed per population (Fig 1 :19), and the question bundles two conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **Score per population, not per ecosystem average.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* One species can be at the brink while the aggregate looks stable.
2. **The deciding quantity is a threshold comparison of two rates.** The Operators table defines comparison as *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Here the operands are the rate at which the optimum moves and the rate at which the trait distribution tracks it. "Fast enough" means the second exceeds the first for long enough, per slot.
3. **Adaptive progress per generation has two factors.** Fig 7 :37: *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* Fig 7 :38: *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and Fig 7 :39: *"the ground's pressure on the room."* Transmission fidelity and selection pressure. Zero either, and no amount of time closes the gap.
4. **Evolvability belongs to population and habitat together.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Short generation time is a filter, not a witness.
5. **Evolution is the unmaintained mode.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* There is no built-in guarantee of sufficiency.
6. **The managed lever is contact, and the work is permanent.** Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :63: *"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."* The framework gives the shape of the lever, not the name of the actor to introduce.
7. **The community claim is second order.** A settled per-population outcome becomes an atom for the community composition (Fig 12 :104). Collapse is not a first-order property of a species list.
8. **The ground decides, and this derivation cannot.** Fig 2 :29 names the anchor as whatever settles the question independently of what any frame thinks. No figure line supplies a persistence measurement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal yes or no. The question as posed is a union compound and is `gap` as a single question.
- Not a critical rate of warming. Any number from any model is a parameter and a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that a short generation time or high heritability guarantees rescue.
- Not a claim that any species actually was or will be rescued here. No measurement was taken.
- Not a name for the source of a persistence signal. Plasticity, range shift, demographic rescue and genetic adaptation can give the same reading; the discriminator is an instrument, not an argument (`05_what_shape_means.md` §4).
- Not a prescription for which conservation actor to introduce. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a per-slot time series, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a spectrometer resolved the green-light case, not a better argument about green.

- **Rate test:** measured movement of the local optimum versus measured change in the population's trait mean, with both error bars reported separately.
- **Two-factor test:** measured effective additive genetic variance (transmission fidelity) and measured selection differential (ground pressure) per generation.
- **Source test:** genomic or common-garden evidence separating genetic adaptation from plasticity, range shift and demographic rescue.
- **Cascade test:** for a population that crosses the threshold, which interaction partners change residual, and at what order of contact.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a single rate comparison deciding all slots; progress with a factor at zero; a capability gain with no contact change; commensurability of the two rates in one currency.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached at this budget, and unreachable as posed.** The universal question asks for one answer over a union of per-slot, per-habitat compounds; there is no sealed room in which that single answer could be searched to the end (Fig 9 :44). Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* A search that found no rescue in a few systems proves no impossibility (Fig 8 :39). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether evolution can outrun climate change, because it scores the residual per population rather than per ecosystem and because the question bundles two conditions that must be separated first. What it gives is the shape any answer must have: a threshold comparison between the rate at which the adaptive optimum moves and the rate at which a population's trait distribution tracks it; two per-generation factors, transmission fidelity and selection pressure, with either at zero stopping progress; evolvability as a property of population and habitat together, not of a species alone; evolution as the unmaintained mode, with no guarantee of sufficiency; and, where people intervene, an asymmetric power to add head starts but not to subtract the habitat, so the work is permanent. A community collapse claim is second order, built over per-population outcomes that have each settled against the ground. The framework supplies neither a critical warming rate nor a name for the actor that saves a population; it supplies the comparison, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
