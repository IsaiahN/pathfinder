# What "shape" means - canonical reading from the eight essays

Sources read 2026-11-23, all by Isaiah N, at the URLs in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The figures README is the canonical usage rule and is cited as a local file.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECO-0064 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can settle it: a threshold comparison of two rates, a two-factor per-generation progress condition, a per-slot cascade rule, and the identity of the anchor that must decide. It is not a critical warming rate, not a species name, and not a yes or no.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity to the thing you are trying to describe, then recurse: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach draft asks what a reader would expect to observe if the shape were true before offering the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual as a rate comparison in effect terms first (an optimum moving faster than a trait distribution can track, a transmission or selection term at zero, a live signal in one species hidden by an ecosystem average), and only then choose which literature to read. Naming the field "evolutionary rescue" first would make any paper seem to fit (Fig 9 :27).

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measured persistence, recovery or collapse of a named population under a named rate of change.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the two-factor shape is a visible choice of axes (transmission fidelity and selection pressure), not a discovery of nature's own list. Turning it into a universal constant would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a source-discriminating instrument is owed, not a better argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several underlying causes create identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Here, observing that a population persisted tells us nothing about the source of that persistence: genetic adaptation, phenotypic plasticity, range shift, demographic rescue, or some combination. The opened rescue literature says exactly this. Schiffers et al. list *"three key types of response may ameliorate the threat of extinction: buffering against negative effects of deteriorating habitat by phenotypic plasticity, tracking suitable climate through range shifting and adapting to changing conditions by rapid evolution."* Question 70 in the source file asks how to distinguish genetic adaptation from behavioural flexibility or movement; that is the source-discrimination question. What is owed is a better instrument (genomic time series, reciprocal transplants, common gardens), not a name for the source.

## 5. Q: what does "shape" licence here? ANSWERED

**What licenses a particular critical rate?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape and the identity of the comparison. A particular rate is licensed only by the ground settling a measured term for a named population, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the world literature commit the forbidden error?** In part. A single warming value from one simulation is a source named from a projection: many different biological mechanisms could produce the same survival curve. That is the metamerism error. The correct form keeps the comparison and the two factors, and leaves the source to an instrument.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any concrete quantity in the slot. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Rate, variance, heritability, selection and contact are all already available in the biological frame. Naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* - it adds none) or that a model parameter is the answer. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README plus Fig 13).

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous evolutionary biologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to check the output, and let the ground, a measurement, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And from *Arrival of the Fittest*: the outputs *"generated must be seen as simulacra."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*
