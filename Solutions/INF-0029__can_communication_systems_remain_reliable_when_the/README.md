## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, stated once here and developed in `01`:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* An adversary who adapts to the code is precisely a mirror: it reads what you send and returns a jamming distribution shaped to it.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **A verifier who holds the interior cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So stage 1 carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, and it is a practical delivery failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in venues avoid both.
