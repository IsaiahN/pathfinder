## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. The residual line I used was exactly: "An instrument is inserted between a frame and its ground; two changes follow, new readings arrive and some readings get settled into composable terms."

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity two, plus an interface condition. The two conditions are not the same kind of change: one is a change in what can be registered, the other a change in what can be composed over. Union compound, not partition: Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Symmetry asymmetric, the instrument produces the reading, the reading does not rebuild the instrument. Scale intra-frame and inter-frame, recursive because a settled term becomes an operand at the next level (Fig 12 :104).
- **Domain 1, access.** Actors: instrument, worse predecessor instrument, reading, per-slot residual. Currency: representable states. Anchor: the ground through something already returning a failing reading. Nameable anchor, so this is a domain. Union member.
- **Domain 2, settlement.** Actors: frame, library, settled molecules, community. Currency: composability. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Nameable, so this is a domain. Union member.
- **Domain 3, source discrimination.** Actors: instrument, candidate sources, record. Currency: discriminating readings. Anchor: the ground via a better instrument. Nameable, so this is a domain. It is the interface between the other two, not their intersection, because it decides what an already-arrived reading licenses.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual first (Fig 9 :27-28). A reading must precede settlement (Fig 12 :106 "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."). Source discrimination follows settlement because it asks what a settled reading means. Methods carry across generations (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading.
