## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "How have instruments changed what people could observe and what they considered real?" Option A: treat the two halves as one change, in which better instruments steadily produce a more objective picture and the word "real" simply tracks that picture. Option B: treat them as a union of two conditions with different anchors, in which instruments extend a frame's access while settlement decides what can be treated as real, and the world gains no primitives. What each leads to: A averages away the per-slot signal and credits agreement with grounding terms; B yields separate verdicts, written refuters, and a recorded stop at the limit of the available instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** An instrument is inserted between a frame and its ground. Two changes follow: new readings arrive, and some readings get settled into composable terms. Arity: two conditions plus the source-discrimination interface. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either", so this is a union compound, not one question. Symmetry: asymmetric, an instrument produces a reading and a reading does not rebuild the instrument. Scale: intra-frame and inter-frame, recursive because a settled term becomes an atom for the next generation.
- **Domain 1, access.** Actors: instrument, worse predecessor, reading, per-slot residual. Currency: representable states. Anchor: the ground, through a predecessor already returning a failing reading. Union, not partition. First in dependency order, because a reading must exist before it can be settled.
- **Domain 2, settlement and the real.** Actors: frame, library, settled molecules, community. Currency: settled terms and composability. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Second in dependency order.
- **Domain 3, source discrimination.** Actors: instrument, candidate sources, record. Currency: discriminating readings. Anchor: the ground via a better instrument. Third in dependency order, because it asks what an existing reading means.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"). Settle a reading before composing over it (Fig 12 :106 "Nothing composes over what was never admitted as a part."). Carry methods upward, not recordings (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").

Strain test applied: reading "what people considered real" as "what people agreed to" makes Fig 2 :35 and Seat Map line 163 contradict the historical record. A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so the census was re-run: agreement is a convention, settlement is a verdict, and the two are separate ledgers. No strain remains after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; Option A does not. An instrument extends a frame's access and never adds a primitive to the world. What a frame treats as real is its settled reach, not its agreed list, and a reading cannot name its source without a discriminator. Access and settlement are an ordered pair with different anchors, and the two must never be averaged. The stop is a report about the current horizon, not proof of absence. Across generations methods and records carry, not lists of observations. This is a `specified`, ungrounded candidate with the per-domain verdicts kept separate and never averaged. Option A's averaged "objectivity" story has no figure line after the census: it is the Fig 1 aggregation error and the Fig 4 flat-formula error at once.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is sent with its mode and refuters. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside checker by sending a finished history instead of a characterised residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter. A second cost is crediting agreement with grounding, which Seat Map line 163 disqualifies.
- **undoable:** yes. The world-side detail is absent rather than wrong, and can be added without touching the shape. The two-ledger distinction can be withdrawn without withdrawing any primitive, and the four refuters are listed in `01` section 4 and `07` section 4. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A documented case of an instrument adding a world primitive, a sensor built from a description with no predecessor reading, a term settled with no ground contact that the framework must still compose over, or a metamer resolved without a discriminator would reverse the relevant verdict. A historian's record showing the spectroscope case is misdescribed would reverse the single-source case verdict. A figure re-census showing that settlement and agreement are the same quantity would reverse the central distinction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
