## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Core knowledge and the shared sequence.** Spelke 2024 and Spelke and Kinzler 2009 describe a small set of early, domain-specific systems shared with other animals, which scaffold later learning and reach widely differing endpoints. This corroborates the *shape* of the universal half (small set, early, ordered) but is **not** the framework's six-term substrate. Corroboration of a shape, not an identity. Overlap between the two Spelke sources is high because they share an author and research programme; they are one witness, not two.
2. **Pace versus pattern.** Rosati et al. 2014 separates differences in the rate of acquisition from differences in the order and causal structure of acquisition. This is the world-facing counterpart of Fig 3 :20 and Fig 4 :34 to :35: the order can be shared while the pace and endpoint differ. Independent of the framework's author, so low overlap.
3. **Individual-level cross-cultural work.** Bohn et al. 2024 argues that group-level co-variation does not identify the cultural or ecological factor that moves a child's development. This is the methodological half of the answer and corroborates Fig 2's independence rule.
4. **The critique that must be faced.** Singh 2026 argues that infant research confounds universality with innateness and presence at birth, and that samples are skewed toward White infants from North America and Western Europe. This **conflicts** with a strong universal-inventory reading. Per the figures' conflict rule, the conflict sent the derivation back to the census; the result is the narrowed, shape-only verdict in F2 and F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, not a flip of the figure verdict.

- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before giving them any framework material.
- **Fig 2 :31:** "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter, (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?", (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Residual and refuter, with the mode stated. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, the three links. The verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** The formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As written, the output is a shape with a withheld inventory, not a universal-capacity claim. Keep it that way.
