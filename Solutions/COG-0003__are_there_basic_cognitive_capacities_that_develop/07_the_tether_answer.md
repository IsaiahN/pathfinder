# The Tether's answer on basic cognitive capacities and cultural variation

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`README.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md` §7). It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two well-supported observations pull apart: a stable, widely shared sequence in early development, and large cross-cultural variation in which capacities are elaborated and what they are used for. One mechanism cannot absorb both without dissolving one side. Fig 9 :58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The residual is a union compound with its own ground.

## 2. The answer, per domain (verdicts kept separate, never averaged)

1. **Shared form, not shared inventory.** What recurs is a kernel, the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, and a dependency order. Fig 4 :19 to :20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The seed carries what will grow; the conditions decide what it becomes (Fig 4 :34 to :35). `general`, structural.
2. **Development is ordered, not calendared.** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry. A later link whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. "Develops similarly everywhere" is this order, not identical milestones. `specified`, falsifiable.
3. **A minimum substrate is required, but it is not named as cognitive.** Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." The six are states and structures, not a psychological inventory. Calling them "the basic cognitive capacities" is a binding from this frame, not a derivation. `general` for existence; the identity is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
4. **Use varies by cast and arrangement.** A culture is a different frame: different actors, currency and anchor (Fig 4 :20), a lossy transform between levels (Fig 10 :68). One settled capacity composed into counting in one community and wayfinding in another is Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." `specified`.
5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." This is why the same kernel yields different endpoints: changing contact changes what the capacity becomes. `specified`.
6. **Universal versus widespread is decided by independence, not agreement.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A widespread pattern produced by similar environments is a shared habitat condition; a universal is a frame invariant. They are separated by two low-overlap instruments ranging against an anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :33). `general`, method.

## 3. Verdict on the question as asked

**Yes, in a qualified structural sense; no, in the inventory sense.** Yes: there is an invariant kernel and dependency order that develop the same way everywhere, and a varying cast that explains different cultural uses. No: the figures do not identify which specific capacities are universal, and they forbid naming a slot they leave open. The specific inventory is `gap` at this budget and with these instruments, not silence and not failure: Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a particular list of core knowledge systems is the framework's six-term substrate. The two agree on a shape, not on an inventory.
- Not that every observed cross-cultural regularity is a universal. Similar habitats produce similar patterns (Fig 11 habitat; Singh 2026).
- Not that the developmental sequence is a fixed schedule of milestones. Only the dependency order is claimed.
- Not that agreement among experts settles the question. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not `grounded`. No cross-cultural measurement was taken by this agent.

## 5. What would settle it, and what would break it

**Settle it (the ground):** an instrument that discriminates sources. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here the instrument is a cross-cultural, individually resolved design with two frames in genuinely different closures, an anchor that does not update, and a record of which community's conditions moved which link.

**Break it (the pre-registered refuters, in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4):**
1. a later dependency link acquired without the earlier one across two independent instruments;
2. a community requiring a different kernel or loop, not a different cast;
3. two low-overlap instruments finding no common early sequence;
4. the two comparison frames found to share one evidence pool (withdraw the triangulation label).

## 6. The honest stop

The answer stops where the instruments stop: a shape is available, a name is not. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). Two of the three Formula step 7 routes remain open: INWARD (sharpen cross-cultural instruments that discriminate shared kernel from shared habitat), and OUTWARD (import a candidate inventory with provenance and a shadow test, while recording that it adds no atom to the framework's closure). SEARCHED was asked first and did not produce a figure-derived selection. No claim is accepted; all are candidates.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not hand over a list of universal cognitive capacities. They give the shape such a list would have to fit. What develops the same way everywhere is an ordered dependency chain and a loop that names what worked; what varies across cultures is the cast, the actors, the currency and the anchor that a community composes over the settled capacity. So "develops similarly" means the same order, not the same milestones, and "used differently" means the same atom under a different arrangement. A widespread pattern caused by similar environments is not thereby a universal; the two are separated by independent instruments ranging against a ground that does not move. The framework stops there: it returns a shape, not a name, and records the refuters that would break the shape. No measurement here settles the question.
