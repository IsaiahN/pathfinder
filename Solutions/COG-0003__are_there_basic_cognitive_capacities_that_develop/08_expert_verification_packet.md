# Expert verification packet: COG-0003

**What this is:** a claim in a form a developmental cognitive scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union compound: a shared developmental sequence and variable cultural deployment are two conditions with their own ground. The invariant part is a kernel, the same dependency order and the same loop for naming what worked; the variable part is the cast, the actors, currency and anchor that different communities compose over a settled capacity. "Develops similarly" means the same order, not the same milestones. "Used differently" means the same atom under a different arrangement. The figures do not name the universal inventory; that remains a `gap` until an instrument discriminates a shared kernel from a shared habitat. An expert should check whether the dependency-order claim and the cast claim are faithful, and whether the withheld inventory is the right stop.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal plus field-facing; checkable by reading Fig 3 and by developmental data

- **Statement:** the universal part is a dependency order, not a milestone list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Also Fig 3 :22 to :23 "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can." Also Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** do the documented sequences (object tracking, number, gaze following, theory-of-mind steps) show a stable order with variable pace and terminal additions, as Rosati et al. 2014 report? Does any population reliably acquire a later link without the earlier one, stably across two instruments?
- **Refuter:** such a population, replicated in two frames with low overlap, breaks the dependency-order claim.

### C2. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Fig 4 and Fig 12

- **Statement:** cultural difference is a different cast or arrangement over the same kernel, not a different kernel. One settled capacity under two different bonds is Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Also Fig 4 :35 "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree" (full line Fig 4 :34 to :35: "The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."). Also Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."
- **What to check:** can a documented cross-cultural difference (for example, counting versus wayfinding uses of object tracking) be written as the same atom in different compounds? Or does it require a different capacity altogether, which would break the isomer reading and leave only a functional-group claim (Fig 12 :124: "The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member")?
- **Refuter:** a community whose practices require a different kernel or loop, not a different cast, breaks the cast claim.

### C3. Method; checkable by reading Fig 2 and the sampling record

- **Statement:** a universal is separated from a widespread pattern by independence between frames, not by agreement. Two pages quoting the same review are one witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Also Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Also Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
- **What to check:** do the two comparison instruments differ in sample, task and analyst, and does each range separately against a ground that neither can move? If the samples are narrow or shared, the triangulation label must be withdrawn. Singh 2026 is a published critique on exactly this point and is recorded as a conflict in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F6.
- **Refuter:** evidence that the two frames draw on one evidence pool forces the verdict to be relabelled `single-source`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named list of universal cognitive capacities. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The specific inventory is `gap`.
- Not that core knowledge systems are the six-term substrate. They agree on a shape, not on an inventory.
- Not that every cross-cultural regularity is a universal. Shared habitats produce shared patterns.
- Not that developmental milestones are identical everywhere. Only the dependency order is claimed.
- Not that expert agreement settles the question. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured by this agent.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here that means a cross-cultural design with individually resolved measures, two low-overlap frames, and an anchor neither can move, which can tell a shared kernel from a shared habitat.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 3 :20, Fig 4 :19 to :20, Fig 2 :31 and Fig 9 :32. Four short passages.
2. Check C1 against the developmental literature you know: is the order stable and the pace variable, or is the order itself variable?
3. Check C2 against one cross-cultural case you know: same atom in different compounds, or a different atom?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate a shared kernel from a shared habitat, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
