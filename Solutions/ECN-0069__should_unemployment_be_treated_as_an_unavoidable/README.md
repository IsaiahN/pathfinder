## The answer in one line

Both options as posed are refused. The figure-derived shape is that this is a **union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question**: a per-slot residual that must be unbundled before any lever is searched; three domains with separate verdicts; a measured anchor that never moves because an economist changes their mind; and a numerical share that the figures cannot supply, which is a `gap` owed to the ground.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own language:

1. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Agreement among economists who read the same reviews is one pool talking, not independent corroboration.
3. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside reader sees the residual and its refuter first, never the framework's interiors.
