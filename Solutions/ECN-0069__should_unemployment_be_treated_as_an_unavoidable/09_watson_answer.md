## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that unemployment is either an unavoidable feature of market adjustment (Option A) or a policy failure that can be substantially reduced (Option B). What each option leads to: A directs attention to smoothing and compensation and treats the level as a constraint; B directs attention to instruments and treats the level as a defect. A third possible output, taken here, is that the pair is a bundled question and must be split before either can be assessed. The sought-for shape was written down as an ordinary term before the run: a per-slot union decomposition, a reachability verdict per domain, a measured anchor, and a gap on the share.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A frictionless clearing prediction says everyone willing to work at the prevailing wage has a job; the observation is persistent positive unemployment in particular slots plus cyclical swings. Arity: several conditions, so a **union compound**. Fig 9 :4-5 says disagreements that do not shrink with effort and where each rule works on a different subset "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Symmetry: asymmetric and ordered, worker to vacancy to match takes time. Scale: individual match, local labour market, national cycle.
- **Domain 1, matching and friction.** Actors: workers, firms, vacancies, employment services. Currency: matches, search time, information. Anchor: the Beveridge curve and vacancy/unemployment flows, a market measurement. Question type: one condition of the union; reachability inside a closure.
- **Domain 2, demand and the cycle.** Actors: households, firms, central bank, fiscal authority. Currency: aggregate demand, layoffs, vacancies. Anchor: the measured unemployment rate and the output gap. Question type: one condition of the union; direct policy reach.
- **Domain 3, skills, institutions and wage setting.** Actors: training providers, regulators, unions, benefit agencies. Currency: skills, wage floors, benefit design, mobility. Anchor: job-finding rates and unemployment duration by group. Question type: one condition of the union; import-dependent reach.
- **Partition or union.** Union. The conditions overlap; a long-term unemployed low-skilled worker in a region with vacancies is a specific compound, not a harder case of any one condition.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive per slot, get terms for what each slot affords, state what counts as progress in that slot, then choose a lever, then evaluate and carry. The middle link is uninstrumented. "Substantially reduced" is a direction until a threshold is written down.
- **Strain test applied.** The natural-rate literature calls its quantity unobservable while the figures require an anchor that settles. Re-census resolves it: measured flows are the anchor; the natural rate is an estimated comparison, a goal-shaped term. Different objects, so no figure conflict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Both options as posed are refused. The question is a union compound indexed by slot, and must be unbundled before a lever is searched. D1: friction is unavoidable relative to the present closure, and the wall moves on import. D2: demand unemployment is directly reachable, and the measured flows say where the boundary is. D3: skills and institutions unemployment is import-dependent and lever-specific, so the intersections survive a single lever. The three verdicts are never averaged. The anchor is measured flows and durations by group, not the unobservable natural rate and not agreement. The reducible share is a `gap` at this instrument. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the method is followed. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records its lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A wrong verdict also misdirects policy attention toward levers that cannot touch the intersections. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the union split and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The union split and the per-domain verdicts can be superseded without withdrawing the method. The natural-rate misidentification and the U3-as-ground reading are removed in `01` section 6 and `05` section 3. A sent claim can be corrected by a revised packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Flow and duration data by group that decompose the union; an identified Beveridge shift following an imported institution; a credible policy evaluation by lever and group that returns the D3 share; a written slot-specific threshold that turns "substantially reduced" into a comparison; or a source-discriminating measurement that returns the reducible share far from the shape claimed. Prestige and citation volume are not such instruments.
