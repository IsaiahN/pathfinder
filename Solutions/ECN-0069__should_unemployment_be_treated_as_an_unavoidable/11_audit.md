## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is clearing-prediction minus observed joblessness, per slot. Arity: several conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4-5). Symmetry: asymmetric and ordered; worker, vacancy and filled job are different states and the transition consumes time. Scale: individual match, local labour market, national cycle.
- **Domain 1, matching and friction.** Actors: workers, firms, vacancies. Currency: matches and information. Anchor: vacancy and unemployment flows. Question type: reachability inside a closure.
- **Domain 2, demand and the cycle.** Actors: firms, households, monetary authority. Currency: demand and layoffs. Anchor: unemployment rate and output gap. Question type: direct policy reach.
- **Domain 3, skills, institutions and wage setting.** Actors: training systems, regulators, benefit agencies. Currency: skills, wage floors, mobility. Anchor: job-finding rate and duration by group. Question type: import-dependent reach.
- **Partition or union.** Union. The conditions overlap; the intersection case is a compound.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive per slot, acquire terms for what the slot affords, state the objective, then plan the lever, then evaluate. The objective link is uninstrumented.

**Where my route differs from the primary's.** I reached the union split from Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-56 first, then used the CRS decomposition as corroboration. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-56 (union), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 8 :32 and Fig 6 :23 (closure and import), Fig 11 :40 (contact).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: split the union; three domains; unavoidable relative to closure; D2 reachable; D3 lever-specific; share is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question, "should unemployment be treated as unavoidable or as a policy failure that can be substantially reduced?"

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot index** is dropped when the answer is compressed into a yes or no about "unemployment";
- the **level-relativity of "unavoidable"** is dropped when the term is moved from a closure to a national economy, where it becomes an absolute claim the figures do not license;
- the **anchor definition** is dropped when the natural rate is substituted for measured flows, an error the CBO's own observability statement makes visible.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union split and the three separate reachability verdicts. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09` now states.
