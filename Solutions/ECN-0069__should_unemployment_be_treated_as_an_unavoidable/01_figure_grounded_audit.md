## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question offers two options. Option A: unemployment is an unavoidable feature of market adjustment. Option B: it is a policy failure that can be substantially reduced. Each leads somewhere different. A directs attention to smoothing and compensation and treats the level as a constraint. B directs attention to instruments and treats the level as a defect to be repaired.

Stated as a residual, not as a position: a frictionless clearing prediction says that everyone willing to work at the prevailing wage has a job. The observation is persistent positive unemployment, including at cycle peaks, plus large swings around it. `R` is the gap between that prediction and what the labour-force survey returns, per slot. The slots are not one number. They are the long-term unemployed, the young, the low-skilled, the displaced, the region with vacancies and joblessness side by side.

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

So "the unemployment rate" is a useful headline and a bad diagnostic. The live signal is in the slots. Any answer that averages them into a single verdict about "unemployment" has already thrown away the thing that would tell us which lever to use.

Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term, per the formula's "Direction" step: an answer would look like (i) a list of the conditions that produce the residual, (ii) for each condition, whether the lever that removes it is already inside the labour-market closure or must be imported, (iii) a measured statement of how much of the observed residual each condition accounts for in each slot, and (iv) a written refuter. I hold that shape as a term from here, so it can accrue a residual and be refused rather than silently steering the search.

## 1. Domain census

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: several conditions, so a **union compound**, not a single hard question. Fig 9 :4-5: **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."** Symmetry: strongly asymmetric. A worker, a vacancy and a filled job are different states, and the transition is ordered and time-consuming. Scale: the individual match, the local labour market, the national cycle. The same residual appears at all three, but the lever that moves it differs by scale, so they are separate loops, not one.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, matching and friction | workers, firms, vacancies, employment services | matches, search time, information | the Beveridge curve and vacancy/unemployment flows, a market measurement | one condition of the union; reachability inside a closure |
| D2, demand and the cycle | households, firms, central bank, fiscal authority | aggregate demand, layoffs, vacancies | the measured unemployment rate and the output gap | one condition of the union; direct policy reach |
| D3, skills, institutions and wage setting | training providers, regulators, unions, benefit agencies | skills, wage floors, benefit design, mobility | job-finding rates and unemployment duration by group | one condition of the union; import-dependent reach |

All three anchors are nameable. The measured unemployment rate, the vacancy rate and the flow into and out of unemployment are counters that do not change their answer because a frame thinks about them.

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :29: **"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

**Partition or union.** Union. The conditions overlap. A worker can be long-term unemployed, low-skilled and in a region with vacancies at the same time. That case is not a harder case of any one condition. It is a specific compound (Fig 9 :57-58). A single lever, for example demand stimulus, clears the region where only the demand condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

Fig 9 :55-56: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the residual per slot first (Fig 3 :4). Get terms for what each slot affords second (Fig 3 :6). State what would count as progress, in the slot's own terms, third (Fig 3 :9). Only then choose a lever and evaluate it. The middle link is the one with no instrument: "full employment" is a comparison the field has not agreed how to measure, and a goal is a comparison (Operators table :52: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*). Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** This is the link this question skips when it asks for a single yes or no.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The either/or question is a union, and the union must be split before search (decisive)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :55-56: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

Fig 9 :57-58: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

The world's own vocabulary matches this. The Congressional Research Service records that when the economy is at a sustainable level, *"cyclical unemployment is zero and the unemployment rate is roughly equal to the sum of structural and frictional unemployment"* (Weinstock, CRS, 2025). That is a union decomposition, not a single rate with two interpretations. The search-and-matching literature distinguishes frictional from structural by what a downward movement in the matching function does, and Diamond notes that *"Structural unemployment is then analyzed in terms of downward movements in the matching function."* (Diamond, NBER w18761, 2013). Different conditions, different diagnostics.

The Library of Babel essay names the same failure from the search side: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

So Option A and Option B are not exhaustive alternatives. Each is true of part of the union and false of other parts. **Refuter:** if unemployment were shown to be one well-formed question whose disagreements shrink with effort, rather than several that each keep working on a different subset, F1 fails and the union verdict is withdrawn.

### F2 - The anchor is measured flows, not agreement between schools of thought

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**

Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

A natural-rate frame and a policy-failure frame can agree or disagree, and the agreement carries no verdict weight. Fig 8 :50: **"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."** Two economists reading the same BLS release are one witness with two names. Two instruments, one a vacancy series and one a flow series, are two frames pointed at the same thing.

Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cost of being wrong here is large: a mistaken "unavoidable" verdict leaves millions of people in reducible joblessness, and a mistaken "policy failure" verdict spends a budget on levers that cannot touch the intersection. Independence is worth buying.

**Refuter:** if measured unemployment and vacancy flows moved when a frame changed its opinion but did not move when a policy instrument changed, the anchor claim fails.

### F3 - Friction is unavoidable relative to a closure, not absolutely

Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Inside a matching closure with a given information structure and a given set of actors, searching harder does not create matches that the closure cannot represent. The residual that persists is structural to that closure. It is the thing Option A is pointing at. But the wall moves on import: a new employment service, a vacancy-information system, a training programme, a mobility scheme. The Federal Reserve's own derivation makes the same point in a different vocabulary: the steady state is *"U = s/(s+f)"*, and matching efficiency is a parameter that *"represents factors that can increase (or decrease) hires without a change in labor market tightness"* (Figura and Waller, FEDS Note, 2022). Change the parameter and the residual moves. That is an import, not more search.

The honest form of Option A is therefore conditional: **unavoidable given the present closure**, not unavoidable in principle. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 8 :40-41 distinguishes *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget"* as different facts with different instructions. Treating a budget-limited residual as a law of nature is the dead-reckoning error.

**Refuter:** if the frictional residual trends down monotonically under more search inside an unchanged closure, or if no import has ever shifted a Beveridge curve, F3 fails.

### F4 - The cyclical domain is directly reachable, and the ground says so

Figura and Waller's estimates show how much the unemployment rate moves with vacancy and separation rates, and they conclude that *"a soft landing is a plausible outcome for the labor market going forward."* The RBA's NAIRU explainer states the reachability boundary plainly: *"monetary policy can influence fluctuations in the unemployment rate around the NAIRU in the short run, but it cannot easily change the long-run level of the unemployment rate. To do this, policymakers must look beyond monetary policy to the government policies that more directly influence the NAIRU."*

That is the world corroborating the domain split with a low-overlap route: a macro policy instrument reaches D2 and does not, by itself, reach D3. The figures govern the shape; the institution supplies the measurement. Fig 2 :45: **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."**

**Refuter:** if demand instruments demonstrably leave measured employment unchanged across cycles while other frames say they should move it, the D2 verdict is withdrawn.

### F5 - A large literature is not evidence that the residual is shrinking

Fig 5 :49: **"Machinery firing is not the milestone. A running system can mint and still return nothing new, which is exactly what the third guard exists to record."**

Fig 5 :41: **"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."**

There is an enormous literature on unemployment. That is a fact about the library, not about the reach. Fig 4 :31: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."** The count of papers is a filter for the budget. It is not a witness for the verdict.

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

**Refuter:** if citation volume trackeded reductions in measured long-term unemployment per slot across countries, the filter-to-verdict distinction would be less important here. It does not, so the distinction stands.

### F6 - Measurement decay is a channel failure, not permission to redefine the ground

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Fig 10 :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**

The official U3 rate omits discouraged workers who have stopped looking and people working part time for economic reasons. The CRS report lists alternative measures for exactly this reason (Weinstock, 2025). That is the channel degrading: the metric saturates and pooled readings hide the slots. It does not license replacing the ground with a preferred definition. Fig 10 :29: **"→ keep the anchor reachable."**

The remedy is a better instrument, not a better argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

**Refuter:** if the headline rate and the broader measures moved in opposite directions systematically with no change in the underlying flow data, a single anchor would be impossible and this finding would need a re-census.

### F7 - Policy reach is asymmetric and levers are condition-specific

Fig 11 :61-62: **"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

A policy can introduce a training provider, a vacancy database or a mobility scheme. It cannot subtract the habitat: the worker still lives where they live, the local demand still exists or does not, and contact is a fact about the world that does not consult the policy. This is why a managed labour market is permanent work rather than a solved state, and why a one-off lever leaves the intersections of the union standing.

The world's mixed evaluation record is the same shape read from data. The IZA Journal of Labor Policy review concludes that *"the weight of evidence from the macroeconometric cross-country literature is that ALMP intensity, which is a key component in an activation strategy, can help cut unemployment"*, while the World Bank review finds that *"many active labor market policies are much less effective than policymakers typically assume"* and that *"Many of these evaluations find no significant impacts on either employment or earnings."* These two sources conflict at the level of a blanket verdict. They agree at the level of the union: the lever has to match the condition. Fig 9 :53: **"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."**

**Refuter:** if one lever cleared all three conditions including their intersections, consistently across countries, F7 fails.

### F8 - The three conditions are a functional group, with a stronger isomer claim still open

Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

As usually written, frictional, structural and cyclical unemployment share the substructure "a flow imbalance between job seekers and vacancies" with different qualifiers. That is a functional group, not Fig 12's isomer.

Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

If all three are written over one shared atom set (workers, vacancies, wages, time, information, institutions) and differ only in which bond connects which atoms, they are isomers in the figure's sense. That is a testable structural claim, not a metaphor. The supporting derivation is in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Refuter:** if any condition requires an atom the others do not, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands. This refuter is written before the conclusion.

### F9 - The refusal is a negation of the dichotomy, not a compromise between the options

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 names a fifth routing class: *"REFUTED - a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

Both options are terms held in the question. The shape claim refuses their standing as an exhaustive pair. It does not split the difference and say "partly both", which would be the averaging error. Fig 12 :126-127: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Bits price the description; actions price finding out whether it holds. "Partly both" adds the two currencies and reports a single number, which is exactly what the figure forbids.

**Refuter:** if the two options were re-specified so that their union was a partition and each had a single measurable anchor, the refusal would be unnecessary and would be withdrawn.

### F10 - The numerical share is a `gap`, and that is a verdict

Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

Fig 13 :134-135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

The figures return the shape: split the union, name the anchors, decide reachability per domain, import what the closure lacks. They do not return a percentage. To produce one would require the ground reading that this derivation explicitly does not have: flow data by group, credible identification of policy effects, and a measured counterfactual. The honest output is `gap` on that specific quantity.

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** A `gap` is not a claim that the share is unknowable. It is a claim that this instrument does not return it.

### F11 - Agreement cannot settle it, including this audit's agreement

Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

Fig 7 :36: **"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."**

`11_audit.md` is a simulated second seat that reads the same figure text and the same essays as this file. By Fig 2 :40 its agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict"*, not corroboration. The only admissible witness is a ground reading: a measurement, a flow series, a policy experiment. Prestige is no help either. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument"*. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union refuter.** Unemployment is one well-formed question whose disagreements shrink with effort, rather than a union of overlapping conditions (breaks F1, Fig 9 :4-5, :54-56).
2. **Anchor refuter.** The measured unemployment and vacancy flows move when a frame changes its opinion but do not move when a policy instrument changes (breaks F2 and F4, Fig 2 :29, :33).
3. **Single-lever refuter.** One lever clears all conditions including their intersections, consistently (breaks F1 and F7, Fig 9 :55-56, Fig 11 :40).
4. **Closure refuter.** The frictional residual trends down under more search inside an unchanged closure, or no import has ever shifted a Beveridge curve (breaks F3, Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23).
5. **Isomer refuter.** Any one of the three conditions requires an atom the others do not (breaks F8, Fig 12 :117).
6. **Ground refuter.** A source-discriminating measurement returns the reducible share per slot with credible identification, and it is far from the shape the figure-derived verdict predicts (breaks F10).

If any fires, the corresponding domain verdict is withdrawn and the census is rerun. Until one is run, nothing in this folder is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union split and the per-slot residual, not with a verdict on either option (F1).
2. State the mode on each domain verdict and keep the three verdicts separate. Never average them (F9).
3. Say "unavoidable relative to the present closure" and never "unavoidable in market economies" (F3).
4. Route the "how much" question to the ground: flow data by group, Beveridge shifts, and policy evaluations with credible identification (F4, F6, F10).
5. Send the unbundling method and the refuter, not a conclusion. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
6. Use the public opt-in venues in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape. Do not ask for agreement.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :4-5 and :54-56. Union of overlapping conditions. Union verdict.
2. **What is the anchor?** Measured vacancy and unemployment flows and group-level durations, not a natural-rate estimate and not agreement (Fig 2 :29).
3. **What is the residual's unit?** The slot, not the national average (Fig 1 :19, :22).
4. **Is friction mintable away?** No inside the closure (Fig 8 :32). Import moves the wall (Fig 6 :23).
5. **Can policy reach demand unemployment?** Yes, and the ground says where the boundary is (RBA, FEDS Note). D2 verdict is direct reach.
6. **Can policy reach skill and institutional unemployment?** Only with the matching lever and with permanent contact (Fig 11 :40), and the world's mixed evaluations are the corroboration.
7. **What does the framework return?** The shape and the refuters, not the percentage (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). `gap` on the share.

## 7. Strains and honest gaps

- **Strain, recorded not hidden: "unavoidable" is level-relative.** Option A means something different at the individual-match scale (a job search takes time) and at the national scale (a measured rate persists). Fig 12 :104 makes irreducibility a fact about a level. The verdicts are kept per domain and per scale, never merged.
- **Strain, recorded: the natural-rate literature calls its quantity "unobservable", while the figures require an anchor that settles.** Re-census resolves it: the anchor is the measured flow series, and the natural rate is an estimated comparison, a goal-shaped term. Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."* No contradiction between figures.
- **Gap: the reducible share.** The figures do not return a number. The world sources do not agree on a single evaluation. This is `gap`, not `unreachable` and not silence.
- **Conflict handled by re-census, not by flipping the verdict:** the IZA review is broadly positive about active labour market policy and the World Bank review is sceptical. Re-census shows they are reading different levers and different countries, which is exactly the union the figure predicted. The verdict is unchanged; the description of the union is improved.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: a per-slot union that must be unbundled, three domains with separate verdicts, an anchor in measured flows, and a wall that moves only on import. Both options as posed are refused. The specific claim that the figures return a reducible percentage is retracted. After re-census: two level and definition strains, both named; one gap, stated; no hidden figure conflict.
