# Expert verification packet - the Tether on unemployment

**What this is:** a claim in a form a labour economist or policy evaluator can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on each domain verdict, `gap` on the reducible share. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken by this derivation. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The either/or question is a bundled question and must be split. Unemployment is a union of overlapping conditions indexed by slot, not one national rate with two interpretations. Three domains carry separate verdicts: matching and friction is unavoidable relative to the present closure but reachable by import; demand and the cycle is directly reachable by policy instruments; skills and institutions is import-dependent and lever-specific, which is why the programme-evaluation literature is mixed. The anchor is measured vacancy and unemployment flows plus group-level durations, not the unobservable natural rate and not agreement between schools of thought. The verdicts are never averaged. The share that is actually reducible is a `gap` at this instrument, not a number the figures return.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the question is a union of overlapping conditions; the residual is indexed by slot; a single lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55-56 - **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Also Fig 9 :54 - **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** And Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
- **What to check against the world:** the CRS decomposition into cyclical, structural and frictional unemployment, and Diamond's treatment of structural unemployment as downward movements in the matching function, both describe a union. Does the group-level data show the same overlapping conditions, or one well-formed rate?
- **Refuter:** unemployment is one question whose disagreements shrink with effort; or one lever clears all conditions including the intersections consistently across countries.

### C2 - economics-facing; checkable against measured flows

- **Statement:** the anchor is measured flows and durations, not the natural rate. Friction is unavoidable relative to a closure; demand unemployment is directly reachable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 8 :32 - **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 6 :23 - **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**
- **What to check:** the Fed's FEDS Note derives `U = s/(s+f)` and reports that the actual rate tracks the steady state closely; the CBO says the natural rate *"is not observable"*; the RBA says monetary policy moves unemployment around the NAIRU in the short run but *"cannot easily change the long-run level"*. Are those three statements consistent with the per-domain reachability verdict, and does a Beveridge shift ever follow an imported institution?
- **Refuter:** measured flows move when a frame changes its opinion but not when a policy instrument changes; or the frictional residual trends down under more search inside an unchanged closure; or no import has ever shifted a Beveridge curve.

### C3 - policy-evaluation-facing; checkable against programme evidence

- **Statement:** the skills and institutions domain is import-dependent and lever-specific. A positive effect for one programme or group is not a verdict for the union. A persistent stock is not itself evidence of failure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 - **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** Fig 12 :119 - **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** Fig 9 :60 - **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**
- **What to check:** the IZA review concludes that ALMP intensity *"can help cut unemployment"* while the World Bank review finds *"Many of these evaluations find no significant impacts on either employment or earnings."* Is the disagreement explained by lever and group, as the union predicts? Do the evaluation datasets record whether a programme is spent or regenerating? Little's law `L = lambda W` licenses a persistent stock at steady state; does any cited study treat that arithmetic as a failure?
- **Refuter:** any condition requires an atom the others do not, so the functional-group reading fails; or one lever works across all groups and countries; or a source-discriminating measurement returns the reducible share with credible identification and it is far from the shape claimed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything. No final verdict on unemployment.
- Not that the natural rate is observable, real, or a target.
- Not "unemployment is zero at full employment" and not "a positive rate is a failure by itself".
- Not that policy can substantially reduce every component. The intersections stay standing.
- Not a derivation of any percentage, decomposition weight, or country ranking.
- Not a claim that U3 is the ground. It is a channel that saturates and pools readings.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

1. Flow and duration data by group and region that decomposes the union where it lives.
2. Beveridge-shift tests with identified shocks, to fire or clear the closure refuter.
3. Policy evaluations with credible identification, reported by group, to return the D3 share.
4. A written objective per slot, so "substantially reduced" becomes a comparison rather than a direction.
5. A channel check against alternative unemployment measures using the same underlying flows.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54-56, Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29, Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :23 and Fig 11 :40. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against the CRS decomposition and the CBO's observability statement: is the anchor claim defensible?
3. Check C3 against one programme evaluation you know well: does the effect depend on lever and group in the way the union predicts?
4. Name one instrument that would return the reducible share per slot, or say why none exists at this budget.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
