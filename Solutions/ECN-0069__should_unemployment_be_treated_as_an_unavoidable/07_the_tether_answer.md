# The Tether's answer: unavoidable or policy failure?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on each of the three domain verdicts, `gap` on the reducible share. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A frictionless clearing prediction says everyone willing to work at the prevailing wage has a job. We observe persistent positive unemployment in particular groups and places, plus large cyclical swings. The residual is indexed by slot: the long-term unemployed, the young, the low-skilled, the displaced, the region with vacancies and joblessness together.

## 2. The shape

1. **Neither option survives as an exhaustive answer.** This is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard question. Fig 9 :55-56: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** A case in two conditions is a specific compound, not a harder case of either.

2. **The anchor is measured flows, not a natural-rate estimate and not agreement.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** The CBO states that the natural rate *"is not observable"*. An unobserved comparison cannot settle anything. The ground is the vacancy series, the flow into and out of unemployment, and duration by group.

3. **Friction is unavoidable relative to a closure, not absolutely.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Little's law supplies the arithmetic: a stock can persist at a steady state with finite search time. That is what Option A is really pointing at. The wall moves on import, so the honest form is conditional.

4. **The demand domain is directly reachable.** The Fed's Beveridge derivation and the RBA's NAIRU explainer both show demand instruments moving measured unemployment in the short run, with the RBA marking the boundary where they stop working. That is the ground reporting on the D2 verdict, not a frame negotiating with it.

5. **The skills and institutions domain is import-dependent and lever-specific.** The IZA and World Bank reviews conflict at the level of a blanket verdict and agree at the level of the union: which programme, which group, which country. Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

6. **The three verdicts are never averaged.** "Partly unavoidable and partly policy failure" is not a compromise; it is the averaging error. Fig 12 :126-127: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** Each domain keeps its own verdict, its own anchor and its own refuter.

7. **The reducible share is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** with the qualification that this is unreached at the available instruments, not underivable in principle. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that unemployment is zero at full employment, and not that a positive rate is evidence of failure by itself.
- Not that the natural rate is real, observable, or a policy target. It is an estimated comparison and the CBO says so.
- Not that policy can substantially reduce every component. The intersections of the union stay standing after a single lever.
- Not that the framework supplies a percentage, a decomposition weight, or a country ranking. None of those is in the closure.
- Not that the headline U3 rate is the ground. It is a channel that saturates and pools readings (Fig 10 :26).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): a reading that cannot comment on its source *"demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the colour case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. **Flow discrimination by slot.** Job-finding rates, separation rates and durations by group and region, so the union can be decomposed where it lives rather than in a national average.
2. **Beveridge-shift tests with identified shocks.** Does the curve move when an information or training institution is imported, and does it stay put under more search inside the same frame? This fires F3.
3. **Policy effects with credible identification.** The endogeneity caveat in the IZA review is the real obstacle. Randomised or quasi-experimental programme evaluations, reported by group, would return the D3 share.
4. **A stated objective per slot.** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** "Substantially reduced" is a direction until a threshold is written down.
5. **A channel check.** Compare U3 with broader measures against the same underlying flows, so a saturated metric is not mistaken for a changed ground.

## 5. The honest stop

The shape is reached; the magnitude is not. The figure set returns a union split, three separate reachability verdicts, an anchor in measured flows and a written refuter, then stops. It cannot say which primitive a ground-reading instrument would need to add, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :134-135). What remains is `candidate` until the ground pays.

This second seat shares the primary's evidence pool. By Fig 2 :40 its agreement is *"disqualifying for a verdict"*, not corroboration. Internal consistency is all that has been checked.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "unavoidable or policy failure" with a yes. It says the question is bundled: unemployment is a union of overlapping conditions, and a single lever clears the part where only one condition binds while leaving the intersections standing. So it splits into three domains. Friction is unavoidable relative to the present matching closure, but the wall moves when an institution is imported; demand unemployment is reachable by policy instruments and the measured flows say so; skills and institutions unemployment is lever-specific and the evaluation literature is mixed for exactly that reason. The anchor is not the natural rate, which is unobservable, and not agreement between economists, but the measured flow between work and joblessness by group and place. The three verdicts are kept separate, never averaged, and the share that is actually reducible is declared a gap owed to a better instrument, not filled with a number the framework does not have. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
