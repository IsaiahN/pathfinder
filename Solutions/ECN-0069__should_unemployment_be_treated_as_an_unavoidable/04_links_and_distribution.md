## Wider literature, by field and overlap

- **Economics, labour and macro (primary field, high internal overlap).** Diamond 2013, Figura and Waller 2022, Consolo and Dias da Silva 2019, CBO 2008, RBA 2021, CRS 2025, IZA 2015, World Bank 2017, IZA World of Labor 2017. These nine sources share data traditions and often cite each other. Their agreement is one pool talking. It is recorded as corroboration of the *shape* only, never as a verdict on the reducible share.
- **Mathematics and operations research (low overlap).** Little's law. It licenses the stock-flow identity that the search models use without supplying any labour-market claim. It corroborates the structural fact that a stock of unemployment can persist with positive flows even at a steady state, which is the formal content of "unavoidable relative to a closure".
- **Animal behaviour and ecology (low overlap).** The marginal value theorem and its great-tit evidence. It corroborates the search-effort rule in effect terms: an agent leaves a patch when the marginal capture rate falls to the habitat average. That is a residual-driven loop in a different substrate. It is an analogue, not evidence about labour markets, and is labelled as such in `09_watson_answer.md`.
- **Metatheory (framework author, high overlap with the figures).** The three essays above. They govern how the figures may be read. Their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight.

**Conflict handled.** The IZA review and the World Bank review conflict at the level of "does policy reduce unemployment". Re-census (section 7 of `01`) shows they read different programmes and different country settings, which is the union the figure predicted. The conflict sent the derivation back to the census and improved the description of the union. It did not flip the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 4 :22-25: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Sending the full architecture before someone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the union split, the three domain verdicts and the refuter. Offer the links after their verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the union claim were true before giving them the figure text.
- Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** The cost of being wrong is a large misdirection of policy attention, so seek readers whose ground contact is different from ours, not readers most likely to nod.

**Recommended reply order:** (1) the per-slot residual and the union split; (2) the three domain verdicts with their modes; (3) the pre-registered refuter; (4) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (5) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Check the shape claim against the ground and record a verdict. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Only if useful, take the formula as a method to reuse, writing a fresh refuter and stating a fresh mode.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Stage 3 sends a generator, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, but it still needs a ground per problem, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*
