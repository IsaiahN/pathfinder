## 1. A shape is the answer form you can state before you can name its contents

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134-135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the shape is:

1. the residual is indexed by slot, not by a national average;
2. the question is a union of overlapping conditions and must be split before any lever is searched;
3. each domain has its own anchor and its own verdict, which are never averaged;
4. the wall friction sits against moves only on import, so "unavoidable" is level- and closure-relative;
5. the reducible share is a `gap` at this instrument.

That is a form. It constrains any acceptable answer before it supplies one. A verdict of "yes, unavoidable" or "no, policy failure" has the wrong form: it fills the slot the shape says to keep open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the first vocabulary was "frictional, structural, cyclical"; the second was "closure, wall, import, anchor, slot"; the narrowing is the derivation.
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** The effect description is "a stock of joblessness that persists with positive flows", not a preferred doctrine about its cause.
- Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Both the natural-rate frame and the policy-failure frame seem to fit until the residual is described per slot.
- *Library of Babel* names the exact failure this question presents: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That sentence is the single closest world-source corroboration of the figure-derived union split.

## 3. What would license a name, and why the natural rate is not one

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

The "natural rate" fails test 1 on the world's own admission: the CBO writes that *"The natural rate of unemployment is not observable and its somewhat broad definition... makes it hard to estimate"* (CBO working paper 2008-06). An unobserved, model-dependent estimate is a comparison, not an anchor. Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one."**

Likewise a specific percentage, for example "two thirds of unemployment is structural", would be a name where the framework returns a shape. It is licensed only by a ground reading with identified policy effects. Until then it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The metamerism test, applied to a single unemployment rate

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, or CMYK can all produce the reading.

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*

The headline unemployment rate is the same kind of projection. A persistent 6 per cent can come from search friction, from skill mismatch, from demand shortfall, from benefit design, or from several of these at once. The reading does not determine the source. *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the better instruments are vacancy flows, job-finding rates by group, duration distributions and credible policy evaluations. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

This is also why the U3 headline must be treated as a channel rather than the ground. Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; Fig 10 :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid saying which domain is reachable?

**No, and this is a correction to an easy overreading.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Reachability in a domain is not a missing primitive. It is a structural fact about what the closure composes, and the figures do give it: Fig 6 :23 on what moves the wall, Fig 8 :32 on closure idempotence, Fig 11 :40 on contact. So the three domain verdicts are `specified` statements that the figures license. What they do not license is a magnitude or a single merged verdict.

The exemplar's own `06` Q1 records the same narrowing for a different question: naming a held atom is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. I apply it here without claiming a new figure line.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A claim backed by famous names or by the number of citations is funded, not settled. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets measured flows decide.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on. That is why the verdict field reads `candidate`, not `accepted`.
