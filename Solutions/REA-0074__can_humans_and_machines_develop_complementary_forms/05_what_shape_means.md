# What "shape" means for this question

**Sources read 2026-11-24.** Framework-internal, maximal overlap with the figures, no independent corroboration weight: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` (local file), *The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, 2 Oct 2026, https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-library-of-babel-locating-the), *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (Isaiah N, 19 Nov 2026, https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-recursive-self-improvement-trap). The other six essays in the set are mapped in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md`; I did not re-open them in this run and do not cite them here as fresh evidence.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is in the figures README: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and then stops at *"the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the form of the human-machine relation that can be stated before any particular pair is measured: *two frames with low evidence overlap, differing closures, and a shared unmoved ground, whose union reaches a term neither reaches alone.* That is a constraint. It does not fill in which model, which person, or which reasoning primitive. "Machines imitate humans" is a regime of the same relation (high overlap), not a different kind of object.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The residual, not a field name, selects the search. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Here the residual is stated in effect terms as an overlap quantity plus a task ground, so the fields that can bear on it (HCI, management, mathematics, philosophy of mind) are read after the residual, not before.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* "Complementary" is a term from the question; "low evidence overlap and a task ground" is the effect description that travels to another field.
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: *"the anchor is provided by your own question."* Here the question is a tether that returns a stop condition when no figure line governs the particular forms.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question, a task ground: a checked proof, a correct diagnosis, a paid price. Agreement between the frames is not that ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

Calling a model's hidden states "the machine's intuition," or a person's habit "the human's gradient," would be naming from a reading without a discriminator. The figures README calls that move indiscernibility treated as identity and forbids it: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the mechanism. A system that samples human text gets *"A copy of a copy of a copy. You can only do that so many times and retain fidelity."* The essay's alignment argument is the same shape as Fig 6 :21: a closure copied from a source cannot manufacture what the source did not contain. And the validator rule is explicit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

The consequence for this question is that a claim of complementarity cannot be validated by the human-machine pair talking to itself. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The meta-analysis in `04_links_and_distribution.md` shows the practical version: human-AI combinations beat humans alone but not the best of either alone on average. That is a ground reading on a task, not a verdict about "intelligence."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "complementary forms of reasoning" mean here?** It means a union surplus between two reasoning frames under a specific overlap condition, not a difference of labels and not a claim that either frame is generally superior. The shape has three load-bearing parts:

1. `closure(H)` and `closure(M)` differ, and neither draws wholly on the other's evidence.
2. There is a task ground both range against separately and cannot move.
3. On some slot, the union reaches a term neither reaches alone.

**Is imitation excluded?** Only for a verdict question. Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* If the task is to produce human-sounding text, matching human output is the product. If the task is to reach a result the ground settles, a shared evidence pool disqualifies the agreement (Fig 2 :40). Both parts are recorded; they are not averaged.

**Does the shape name the forms?** No. Which reasoning primitive sits on the human side and which on the machine side is a `gap` (F7 in `01`). An instrument that measures evidence overlap is owed, not named.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-known reviewer's agreement is a budget to be tried, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle whether any real human-machine pair is complementary.

**Contact follows the residual.** Send the shape and its refuter first, ask for a failure, and show sources only after the verdict is recorded. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* That ordering is what keeps a responder outside the frame being checked.
