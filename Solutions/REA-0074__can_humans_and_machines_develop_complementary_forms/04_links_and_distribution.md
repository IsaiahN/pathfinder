## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The meta-analysis gives the shape, not a blank cheque.** Vaccaro and colleagues find human augmentation on average but no human-AI synergy on average, with task type and relative baseline performance as the significant moderators. This is exactly the Fig 8 requirement that the union surplus depends on what each frame already reaches: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"* holds only where the frames differ in what they can do. The 2024 review and the MIT press release share an evidence pool, so they are one witness, not two (Fig 8 :49).
2. **The field experiment supplies a task partition.** Dell'Acqua and colleagues show gains inside a "jagged frontier" and losses outside it, with Centaurs delegating by task and Cyborgs integrating workflows. That is Fig 3 territory: a division of labour is an ordered dependency chain, not a label on a model. It corroborates the domain census, at a cost: it is a business field with its own measures, so it does not settle a reasoning claim, only the shape of one.
3. **Mathematics supplies a genuinely different closure.** Shulman describes proof assistants that verify steps and may train human intuition, while the human retains ideas and understanding. Here the machine and the human do not compete to produce the same output; they do different things against a proof-checking ground. This is the strongest low-overlap corroboration of F3 and F5, and it comes from a field with almost no overlap with the management literature.
4. **The philosophy of extended cognition is a check, not a cheerleader.** Drayson's argument that complementarity claims can be deflated by internal surrogates is the exact reason the solution refuses to name a human or machine reasoning primitive without a source gate. It *conflicts* with a naive "tools are part of mind" reading and sent the derivation back to the census, where the claim was narrowed to differing closures plus a ground. The verdict did not flip.
5. **Distributed cognition corroborates the habitat claim.** Sutton and colleagues' review gives the broad setting: cognition can be distributed across bodies, artifacts and social groups. It corroborates Fig 11 :39 at the individual-artifact stratum and adds no task-ground measurement.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if low overlap were the deciding variable, before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60 - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A venue's prestige is a contact-cost estimate. It never enters the verdict.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure condition. No theory, no links, no request for agreement. This is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links above. Their stage 1 verdict must already be recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Fig 2 :40 says shared-pool agreement is worthless for a verdict.

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode, and names their own ground. The essays warn that a simulated output *"would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

**Hard precondition:** the stage 1 output must survive in the repaired form, with imitation and complementarity separated by question type (F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). The mirror reading as originally drafted does not survive.
