## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for people who already agree is a mirror, not a check.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients by receptiveness manufactures the shared pool that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Show the framework before the verdict and the outside frame has joined the frame it was there to check.

The distinction between imitation and complementarity is the whole question, so it is worth stating the respect in which agreement is legitimate. Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* If the task is to write in a human style, imitation is the product. If the task is to reach a result that a ground settles, imitation is a mirror. The solution keeps those two questions separate and never averages them.
