# WATSON answer, REA-0074

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can humans and machines develop complementary forms of reasoning rather than machines simply imitating human thought?

Restated as an effect: for a task slot s, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` for each frame, and the question is whether the union of a human frame H and a machine frame M reaches a term neither reaches alone, or whether M merely returns H's outputs from H's evidence pool. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). Anchor: the task ground, named in Fig 2 :29 as a measurement, experiment, counter, market or patient outcome. "Complementary" is a relation between frames relative to R, not a property carried by either frame.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: three-place (H, M, ground), indexed by task slot and ordered in time as the loop repeats; carries a comparison bond (which frame pays on a slot), a sequence bond (delegation), and an import relation (atoms crossing closures), none symmetric. Symmetry: the valence of each frame is relative to the residual, so no standing list of helpful frames survives a change of task (Fig 11 :51). Scale: one frame loop, the union of two frames, and the habitat of tasks and instruments; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :20).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** complementarity is possible and is exactly the Fig 8 union surplus, but only under two conditions: `closure(H)` and `closure(M)` differ and both range against a task ground that neither can move. Imitation is the high-overlap mirror regime, which is the correct product for a convention about shared meaning and disqualifying for a verdict about the world. New reasoning primitives enter only by import from a differing closure, so training a machine on more human output is depth, not novelty. Capability lies in the pair plus its habitat, not in either engine alone. The particular human and machine forms are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`; no domain verdict is averaged with another. The business pool corroborates the task-partition shape after re-census; the philosophy conflict was returned to the census and narrowed the claim, not reversed it.

- **cost if wrong:** a team would apply one lever to a union of three conditions and spend its budget on the wrong one. A model scaled up for contact that never changed, or a mirror accepted as corroboration because it agreed, wastes an expert's twenty minutes and can entrench a wrong regime. The error is bounded and mostly reversible because nothing is deleted.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a refusing term and removes nothing; the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 amendment :42). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, in particular (1) a high-overlap union with a genuine surplus, (2) a primitive entering the model with no differing-closure source, (3) a gain that survives an agreement-only metric, (4) an agent-only improvement that moves the task ground with contact fixed, or (5) two forms with identical atoms and bonds behaving differently on the same ground. A genuinely independent source measuring overlap directly would also reverse the `gap` into a measurement.
