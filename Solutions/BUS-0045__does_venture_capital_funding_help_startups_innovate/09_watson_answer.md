## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when an external funding frame enters a startup's loop. Option A: funding adds the capacity to innovate, so novelty is a function of resources and more capital should produce more ground-settled new compositions per startup. Option B: funding imposes narrow growth targets and premature scaling, so the investor's objective replaces the startup's objective, the metric becomes frame-internal to the fund, and the behavioural stage outruns the actual stage. What each leads to: A sends a resource-determinist story that averages across slots and ignores which ground pays; B sends a channel story that can be checked per startup but that, if stated alone, misses the legitimate import channel. The recorded answer keeps both conditions separate and never averages them.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The per-startup gap between what the startup's library predicts and what the customer market returns, indexed by startup slot and by time, when a funding frame enters with capital and a return objective. Arity: three-place (startup Gamma, action, customer return), ordered by financing rounds. Symmetry: not symmetric, funder and startup are not interchangeable; "innovation" and "premature" are threshold comparisons. Scale: first order inside the startup loop, second order at the fund/portfolio membrane. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the startup loop.** Actors: founders, product, team, the startup's Gamma. Currency: actions and residual reduction. Anchor: realized customer payment, retention and survival. Question type: one-agent loop, per slot.
- **Domain 2, the funding frame.** Actors: partners, fund lifecycle, milestones, board. Currency: capital, governance, the return objective. Anchor: realized fund return and exit, a different ground from Domain 1. Question type: a different closure, so an import is possible here.
- **Domain 3, the capability library.** Actors: atoms (technology, data, people, cash, network, customer access) and bonds. Currency: reach and cost of a term. Anchor: settlement, *"then check it against the ground, then carry it up"* (Fig 5 :32). Question type: partition (which compositions cross the two kinds in this frame's terms).
- **Domain 4, the scaling channel.** Actors: metrics, growth targets, reviews, dashboards. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: channel fidelity, the place premature scaling lives.
- **Excluded, X1 "innovation as a property of money".** No anchor independent of the customer and citation instruments. Not a domain yet.
- **Absorbed, X2 "growth as sole cause".** This is Domain 2's objective instrument and Domain 4's metric, not a separate ground.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry. The funding frame arrives at the objective link before the vocabulary link has an instrument. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second").

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no single-sided verdict survives. Funding helps innovation only when it imports a differing closure and a new composition is settled at the customer ground. Money alone is a currency inside an existing closure and cannot move reach. Funding pushes toward narrow growth targets and premature scaling when the fund's growth comparison replaces the startup's objective and the behavioural stage outruns the actual stage; those are bracket/channel failures with different instruments. Capital's valence is relative to the residual, so the same money can be a benefactor or a malfactor depending on what ground pays. Investor agreement is not evidence. The two conditions are kept separate and never averaged. Which named term sheet, milestone or growth rate fills the slot is a `gap` under Fig 9 :32. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the two conditions and their refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective single-sided readings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-startup discriminator showing a new settled composition with no differing closure would break the C1 reading; a demonstration that the fund's growth metric is identical to the customer signal per slot, or that bracket fidelity and ground pressure stay high when behavioural stage outruns actual stage, would break the C2 reading; a derivation of one of Fig 13's six terms from the other five, or a failure of Fig 6 idempotence inside a closed set, would break the figures themselves. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
