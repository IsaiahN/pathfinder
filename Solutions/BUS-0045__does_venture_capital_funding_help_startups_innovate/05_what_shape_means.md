## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the pair of conditions in `01` section 0: (C1) a differing closure was imported and a new composition was settled at the customer ground, and (C2) the objective and scale stayed faithful to that ground. "VC helps innovate" is a verdict, not a shape. "Premature scaling" is practice-pool vocabulary, not a figure-derived primitive.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Describing the residual before choosing a frame is not a courtesy here. The empirical literature already supplies the frame names (venture capital, growth targets, scaling). Starting from them is how the union got bundled in the first place (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second").

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a startup slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. This is why the solution declines to name "product market fit", "blitzscaling" or "unit economics" as figure-derived primitives. They are bindings from the practice frame.

## 4. The metamerism error in this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the most directly relevant essay here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply that to the world sources:

- A patent count reads "innovation" and cannot comment on the source. The same count can come from a genuine new composition settled at a customer ground, from a defensive filing, from a subsidy, or from rationalising an existing portfolio (Fig 8 :50 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents.").
- A growth rate reads "progress" and cannot comment on the source. The same rate can come from a faithful bracket meeting real demand, or from a behavioural stage that outran the actual stage.
- A fund's return multiple reads "value created" and cannot comment on which ground paid.

What is owed is a discriminator, not a louder verdict. The essay is explicit: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* This is the reason the refuter in section 4 of `01` is written as an instrument test rather than as a request for agreement.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q - what licenses a particular mechanism in the slot?

**ANSWERED, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework licenses "growth target" or "closure import" as *the* mechanism in a given startup. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

Does the record commit the error the README forbids? It does not name a single mechanism as the answer. It names two conditions and leaves the instrument open. The one place it comes close is F3 of `01`, where the Startup Genome vocabulary of "actual stage" and "behavioural stage" is used; that is a practice-pool binding recorded as such, and the figures carry the mechanism (Fig 10 :24-26, Fig 7 :37-39), not the vocabulary.

## 6. Prestige is not action - the special relevance here

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

This is not a side point in venture capital. A famous fund backing a startup is exactly the case where prestige can fund an action and cannot settle it. The high-prestige investor is believed because they are high-prestige; the growth rate is assumed to be real because the investor is believed; the customer ground is never consulted. The essay names the collapse: *"the root cause is almost always the same: someone let prestige stand in for proof."* A campaign that gathers prestigious investors to agree that funding helps innovation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the customer ground settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own section 3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any component of the answer. It does not. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a thing not held in Gamma. The two conditions here are a shape because they are constraints, not because every word in them is unnameable. What remains wrong would be to promote a practice-pool binding ("blitzscaling", "unit economics") into a figure-derived primitive, or to apply Fig 13's six physical terms to startup resources (strain S1 in `01`).
