## Round trip gap R_T

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: money alone cannot move reach; premature scaling is bracket failure; growth targets are a comparison that can replace the objective.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: premature scaling is a bracket/channel failure and capital's valence depends on the residual. That is the residue worth sending.
