## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Two grounds are in the sentence.** The economics papers (NBER 27150, NBER 26196, Lahr and Mina) measure patenting, employment and exit; the practice studies (Startup Genome, HBR, SMJ scaling) measure customer-facing scale-stage mismatch. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The customer market and the capital market are two different anchors. A study that uses one cannot settle a claim made about the other.
2. **Both sides are reported inside single studies.** NBER 27150 reports higher quality and simultaneously more pro-cyclical, with a shift toward less innovative firms closer to profitability in downturns. Bernstein reports rate up and quality down. This is why the union reading in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F7 matters: the findings are different slots, not a contradiction to be averaged.
3. **The strongest mechanistic corroboration comes from outside business.** The ecology source on delayed feedback and overshoot is the only witness with a genuinely different evidence pool. It corroborates the shape of the premature-scaling mechanism, not the business conclusion. Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
4. **A verifier must not be handed the interior.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Ask for the refutation of the stage-1 shape before sending the framework links.

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording of the conclusion sent before anyone has described a residual is not a generator. Lead with the two conditions and the refuter; offer the links after.

- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Do not report agreement counts. Report which named refuter fired.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two conditions and their refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure in their own data; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) record the verdict before any theory is shown.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration: Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter.
4. **Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01` section 4 are written in advance so that survival can be checked.

## AI-policy caveat

Several of these venues, including the Academy of Management through its publishing guidance, state that submitted work must be authored by the authors and not be a product of AI tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited. This package is a simulated derivation. Before any submission, check the current policy of the chosen venue and disclose the method honestly, or treat the packet as a private question to a refuter rather than a submission. That constraint is recorded here rather than hidden.
