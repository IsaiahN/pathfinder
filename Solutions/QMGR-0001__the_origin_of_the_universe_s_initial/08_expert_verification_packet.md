# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the universe's initial state

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of cosmology can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the limits, not `grounded`.** No measurement of the initial state is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The gravitational dynamics does not select a unique initial state, so a boundary condition is owed and is an import rather than something derived from the dynamics. The Hartle-Hawking and Vilenkin proposals are two arrangements over the same formal atoms (action, wave function of the universe, minisuperspace variables, path integral or canonical constraint, semiclassical approximation), differing in the boundary bond; under Fig 12 they are isomers, not two independent frames. The origin itself is unreached: nothing currently returns a discriminating reading, and both proposals can be made compatible with the observed universe. The framework returns this shape, not a name, and refuses any claim that a boundary condition ends inquiry.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal plus mathematics-facing; checkable by reading Fig 13 and any boundary value problem

- **Statement:** the dynamics supplies a general solution; the boundary condition supplies the unique solution. The missing piece is an import with a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :14, *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 13 :115, *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*
- **What to check:** is a boundary condition in quantum cosmology genuinely external to the dynamics in the relevant sense (it is chosen, not derived by the constraint), and does the boundary-value analogy hold at the level claimed?
- **Refuter:** a derivation inside the dynamics alone that yields a unique initial state with no boundary condition added, or a demonstration that the missing selection is internal composition rather than import.

### C2. Physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the two proposals

- **Statement:** HH and V share the formal atom set and differ in the boundary bond, so they are Fig 12 isomers rather than two independent frames. Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 8 :49, *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*
- **What to check:** is the atom set genuinely shared? Does one proposal require a primitive the other does not (a landscape, a different measure, a different underlying theory)? Do they draw on independent evidence at a resolvable scale? If yes to either, the isomer claim fails and the two are different frames. If they yield identical weights over identical histories with no structural remainder, the difference is not a bond carrying information and the claim also fails.
- **Refuter:** show a primitive asymmetry between the proposals, or exact observational equivalence with no structural remainder. The weaker fallback is the functional-group reading, Fig 12 :123, *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

### C3. Framework-internal limit; checkable by reading Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 9 and Fig 13

- **Statement:** the origin is unreached, not absent and not silence; a terminal "origin theory" is a last room and is refused.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :40, *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."* Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134, *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 7 :6, *"the limit: no last room."*
- **What to check:** is there an instrument, at any resolution, already returning something that fails to resolve the boundary condition? Is the claim of unreachability a budget claim or an in-principle claim? This packet reports **unreached at this budget** and does not assert underivability in principle (Fig 8 :40-43).
- **Refuter:** a discriminating instrument, or a proof that the choice is underivable from any frame. The first moves the verdict toward `grounded`; the second narrows it. Either is preferable to continued argument.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a choice between Hartle-Hawking and Vilenkin. No preference is expressed.
- Not a derivation of a probability measure, contour prescription or classicality condition.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room. A boundary condition is a handoff, not an endpoint.
- Not "the universe came from nothing." A vacuum state is a state with structure, not nothing.
- Not that the low-entropy arrow and the perturbation realization follow from the boundary condition alone. Those are separate slots.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a ground reading. Internal consistency against the figures is the only check performed.
