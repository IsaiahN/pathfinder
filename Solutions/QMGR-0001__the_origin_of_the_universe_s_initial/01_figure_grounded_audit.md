## 4. Findings F1 to F9

### F1. The initial-state question is a boundary-condition question, and one is owed

Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

The dynamics of the formalism does not select a unique solution. In the language of the figures, the set of kinds is closed and the arrangements over it are not. What the dynamics supplies is the equivalent of a general solution; what the initial-state question asks for is the specific solution. Those are different objects. The boundary condition is the import that supplies the missing selection, and it is owed before the question can close.

Low-overlap corroboration (mathematics / engineering, **not** a figure line): the *Boundary value problem* reference page states that *"Without the boundary conditions, the general solution to this equation is"* a form with free constants, and that *"imposing boundary conditions allowed one to determine a unique solution."* This is the same structure in a field that shares no evidence pool with the Tether. Lehners' 2023 review [*Review of the No-Boundary Wave Function*] states that *"given a dynamical theory, the no-boundary proposal provides probabilities for various solutions of the theory. In this sense it provides a quantum theory of initial conditions."* Both predict that a boundary condition is what a theory of initial conditions adds.

- **Mode:** `specified` (structural), with the physics reading `specified` and ungrounded.
- **Status:** **triangulated** across Fig 6 and Fig 13 (two figures with low overlap: closure/import versus the six-term set), plus one low-overlap world witness. The world witness corroborates; it does not decide.
- **Refuter:** show that the dynamics alone yields a unique initial state with no added boundary condition, or that the formalism can add a primitive by internal composition. Either breaks F1.

### F2. The two proposals are isomers within one closure, not two independent frames

Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

Both proposals operate over the same atoms: a gravitational action, a wave function of the universe, minisuperspace variables (scale factor and matter fields), a path integral or a canonical constraint, and a semiclassical approximation. They differ in the arrangement: HH sums over compact Euclidean geometries with no past boundary and regular matter configurations; Vilenkin imposes an outgoing-wave condition at the nucleation point. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. That is Fig 12's isomer, not two convergent derivations from independent closures.

The world record agrees on the shared atoms. The Hartle-Hawking reference page describes the *"transformation to Euclidean space-time"* and says *"this rotation is applied to every path ... and therefore the sum of histories becomes a quantum superposition of spacetimes."* Lehners' review describes the same framework, noting the *"canonical and path integral approaches, and their interpretations"* and explicit implementations *"in minisuperspace."* These are one evidence pool, so their agreement with each other is not corroboration of the proposals; it is one witness describing one formalism.

This matters for the verdict: a preference for HH over V, or the reverse, cannot be triangulated by finding more papers in the same literature. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*), and the cost here is high.

- **Mode:** `specified` for the isomer reading, `general` for the no-surplus limit.
- **Status:** **triangulated** across Fig 12 (composition) and Fig 8 (frame overlap), two figures governing different objects. The physics-facing check that the atoms are genuinely shared is single-source (one formalism, one evidence pool) and is labelled as such.
- **Refuter:** show that one proposal requires a primitive the other lacks, such that their closures genuinely differ, or that they draw on independent evidence. That would move them from isomers to two frames and reopen triangulation.

Three of those are world witnesses, but they are low-overlap with the framework and they predict the same thing the figures predict: the channel to the origin is dark at present. ## 5. Pre-registered refuter, written before the conclusion

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

- **R1 (import owed).** A derivation inside the dynamics alone yields a unique initial state with no boundary condition added. Breaks F1 and the import reading (Fig 6 :14).
- **R2 (isomer / one closure).** One proposal requires a primitive the other lacks, or they draw on genuinely independent evidence. Breaks F2 and reopens triangulation (Fig 12 :117, Fig 8 :49).
- **R3 (unreachable).** An instrument at any resolution returns a discriminating reading of the boundary condition. Breaks F3 and moves the verdict toward `grounded` (Fig 6 :40).
- **R4 (no last room).** A terminal frame is demonstrated with no outside left to import from, or one of the six terms is derived from the other five. Breaks F4 (Fig 7 :6, Fig 12 :104).
