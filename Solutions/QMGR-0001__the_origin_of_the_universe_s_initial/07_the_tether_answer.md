# The Tether's answer on the universe's initial state

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the limit statements, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gravitational dynamics does not select a unique initial state. Two named proposals try to supply what the dynamics leaves open: Hartle-Hawking removes the past boundary; Vilenkin imposes a tunneling, outgoing-wave boundary condition. The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question: (dynamics underdetermines the boundary condition) union (two arrangements share one closure). Fig 9 :55-57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* It must be unbundled before it is searched.

The residual is indexed by six slots, not by one score (Fig 1 :19): origin of time, of space, of energy, the arrow, the perturbation spectrum, and relations. Four of the six have no ground reading at all.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**Domain 1, the formalism.** A boundary condition is owed and is an import. Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* The dynamics supplies the general solution; the boundary condition supplies the selection. Low-overlap corroboration: the mathematics of boundary value problems, where free constants become a unique solution once a boundary condition is imposed. `specified`, triangulated.

**Domain 2, the proposals.** Hartle-Hawking and Vilenkin are **isomers over a shared atom set**, not two independent frames. Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. **Domain 3, the ground.** The origin is **unreached**, not absent. Underivable-in-principle and unreached-at-this-budget are kept distinct (Fig 8 :40-43). This solution reports the latter.

**Domain 4, the frame.** An "ultimate origin" that ends inquiry is a terminal room and is refused. **Across domains.** The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). Selecting Hartle-Hawking or Vilenkin is a binding from the physics frame, recorded with provenance, not a figure derivation. The per-slot residual must not be averaged into one "origin" score (Fig 1 :22).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a winner. The figures do not choose between HH and V, and this answer does not smuggle a choice in.
- Not a derivation of a probability measure, a contour prescription or a classicality condition. Those live in the physics frame and were not recomputed here.
- Not a theory of everything. No last room; the center stays a compound with its own loop.
- Not "nothing comes from nothing." The SEP entry warns that a vacuum state *"is not nothing"* and that treating a creation account as answering why there is something rather than nothing *"is a mistake."*
- Not that the arrow and the perturbation spectrum follow from the boundary condition alone. They are separate slots.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the origin settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency only.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a better argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not reasoning, resolved the source. Here:

- **Inward (sharpen an instrument already returning something):** primordial non-Gaussianity, the tensor-to-scalar ratio, and other statistics that bear on the initial perturbation condition. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* These instruments exist and return readings; they do not currently separate the boundary conditions at the origin.
- **Outward (import a frame with a different closure):** the mathematics of boundary value problems and the philosophy of cosmology, both actually read, both with source gate and shadow test declared, both debited against independence (Fig 6 :24).
- **R1:** a derivation inside the dynamics alone that yields a unique initial state with no boundary condition added.
- **R2:** a demonstration that the proposals require different primitives or independent evidence.
- **R3:** an instrument, at any resolution, that returns a discriminating reading of the boundary condition.
- **R4:** a derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or a demonstrated terminal frame.
- **R5:** a single boundary condition shown to determine the arrow and the perturbation realization with no extra assumption.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, and not silence.** The stop is at the limit of the available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* The canonical README adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Two readings that cannot currently be distinguished are not thereby the same. This answer does not claim to know which boundary condition nature used. It claims to know what any answer must look like and what would reverse the claim.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between the Hartle-Hawking and Vilenkin proposals, and it does not offer a final theory of the origin. It says that the gravitational dynamics underdetermines the initial state, so a boundary condition is owed and is an import; that the two named proposals are isomers over one shared formalism, same atoms arranged differently, so their disagreement cannot be settled by counting adherents or accumulating more papers in the same literature; and that the origin itself is unreached, because nothing at any resolution currently returns a discriminating reading. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, six failure conditions written in advance, and the inward and outward tracks that would close it. The boundary-condition slot stays open until a ground settles it.
