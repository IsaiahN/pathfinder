A boundary condition is owed and is an import; Hartle-Hawking and Vilenkin are isomers over one shared formalism, so their disagreement is not triangulation; the origin is unreached at this budget, with both proposals compatible with the observed universe; and an origin theory that ends inquiry is a last room, which the figures forbid.
