## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among method Γ, action a, target observation o′ and a transform from source conditions to target conditions. At least four operands. A two-place reading (method, works or fails) loses the action and the transform, so it is rejected. Because the carrying is ordered, it uses a sequence bond; because "still pays" is a threshold, it uses a comparison bond.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The target ground does not update on the method's reputation (Fig 2 :25). Portability from A to B is not portability from B to A. The transform is lossy, so the round trip does not close (Fig 4 :27-30).
- **Scale:** the slot is a place, but the "works" comparison exists only across a population of practices (Fig 4 :5). The carrying crosses the membrane between the practitioner loop and the population selection loop (Fig 4), so the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *portability requires a relational loop between a method and its receiving place*.
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific transfer. Result: *keep the method's core and adapt the rest to local conditions*.

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a failing practitioner to adapt harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the generator versus recording distinction. "Relational loop" does not say whether what crossed was a reapplicable method or a recipe (Fig 4 :22-25). Coming back down, it permits a replayed recipe to count as portable.
3. It loses the two silent habitat failures. "Adapt to local conditions" has no place for what you failed to reproduce or the actors you unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).
4. It loses the R_T requirement itself. "Core plus adaptation" sounds lossless; the coarser description cannot hold what the round trip dropped, and treating it as identity is the dead-reckoning error.
5. It loses the anchor rule. "Relational loop" does not specify that the receiving place's ground, not agreement, settles the result (Fig 2 :29, :35).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a generator must cross, and the habitat must be reproduced. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no target-ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
