# The Tether's answer on method portability

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A method that keeps the gap between prediction and outcome small in one place is carried to another, and the gap either stays small or grows. The question is not whether the method is good but why `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` stays small after transport in some slots and grows in others. R is indexed per place and population, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The anchor is the receiving place's ground, named in Fig 2 :29. This is a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57).

## 2. The shape

1. **What crosses must be a generator, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* A fixed recipe replayed elsewhere repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one (Fig 4 :25). The receiving place remakes what lands there: *"The level a method lands on remakes it."* (Fig 4 :34).
2. **Portability is a property of method-and-habitat, not of the method alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Moving a method is a habitat substitution with two silent failures: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :68, :69). A pilot site is a substituted habitat and *"proves wiring and never capability"* (Fig 11 :70).
3. **The description must travel in effect terms, with its lineage.** Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* A recipe without its bonds has recorded half of itself.
4. **The round-trip gap must be measured.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The coarser description cannot hold everything the source held; the loss is a quantity, not an embarrassment to hide.
5. **Renewal of reach needs contact with a differing closure.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* A transplanted method optimised only at home never learns what the new place requires.
6. **The receiving place's ground settles it.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Adoption counts, practitioner agreement and method prestige are not anchors. Fig 2 :29 names the candidates: a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome.
7. **The framework does not name which method will work where.** It returns a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* A binding of a named method onto a slot is provenance owed, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any method is universally portable. Portability is a relation, not a property owned.
- Not that local context is everything. The set is closed and arrangements are not; renewal needs an import (Fig 13 :115).
- Not that fidelity to the original protocol is always the right test. FRAME's own function-over-form reading agrees that which elements are core can change with the population.
- Not that prestige, standardisation or number of adopters settle a transfer. They can fund a trial; they cannot pay for it.
- Not `grounded`. No target-place measurement settles `left(R,φ)` here.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A measuring instrument in the receiving place, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Generator Test:** does the carried thing remain reapplicable in the target place, or is it only a replayed recipe? A protocol that cannot generate a new success when the ground changes has failed.
- **Habitat Test:** enumerate what the method's goal required in the source and check what was reproduced in the target. Record the unchosen actors that entered. The two failures of Fig 11 :68-69 must be measured, not assumed absent.
- **Round-Trip Test:** measure R_T for the compression. If a compressed method reapplied in the target returns the source prediction exactly across a real place change, F4 is false.
- **Closure Test:** did the target practitioners contact a differing closure, and did the method's reach move? If reach never moved while the method kept paying, F5 is false.
- **Discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes generator-crossing from lucky habitat match, and required-condition failure from unchosen-actor interference. That instrument is not held here; this is the declared `gap`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The conditions are stateable, the two failure modes are named, and the falsifiers are written. Which specific method will work in a named place is not derivable from the shape. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a universally portable method. It says that portability is a conjunction of two conditions: what crosses must be a generator and a head start rather than a recording of one occasion, and the receiving place must reproduce what the method's goal required while accounting for the actors it did not choose. The description has to travel in effect terms with its lineage, the loss on the round trip has to be measured, and the ground of the receiving place, not adoption or prestige, settles whether the method paid there. The two conditions have different failure modes and different instruments, so they must never be averaged into a portability score. What the framework offers is the shape any portable method must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It stops there, without naming the method that will work in a given place, until a ground reading there pays for the name.
