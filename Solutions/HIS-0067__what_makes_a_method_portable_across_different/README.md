## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether a method is good, but why the gap between what a method predicts and what a place returns stays small after the method is carried there. In effect terms, using the loop of `THE_FORMULA.md`:

- **b** is the practitioner's belief in the source place, formed from local history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the use of the method: the procedure, instrument, protocol, or practice.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the method's library predicts will happen when it is used.
- **o′** is what the place actually returns: a measurement, an experiment outcome, a patient outcome, a market, a yield.
- **s** is the place and population. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed by place, never averaged across places (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The phenomenon to explain: a method that keeps R small in slot A is carried to slot B, and R either stays small (portable) or grows (not portable). The anchor is the ground of the receiving place, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29 (a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome). The method's own reputation is not an anchor.

- Option A, "portability is a property a method owns": leads to the prediction that a good method works anywhere and failure is local incompetence.
- Option B, "portability is a property of method-and-habitat plus what crosses": leads to the prediction that portability fails when a recording is carried instead of a generator, or when the receiving habitat lacks what the goal required, or when unchosen actors act.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula, "Direction") before the run: the answer would be a conjunction of conditions whose holding keeps R small after transport, plus an instrument that discriminates a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the historical question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of method transfer.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
