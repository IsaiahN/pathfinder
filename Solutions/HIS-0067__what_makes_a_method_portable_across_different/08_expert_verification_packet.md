# Expert verification packet — the Tether on method portability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a historian of science, implementation researcher or methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement in a named receiving place is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Portability is not a property a method owns. It is a conjunction of two conditions. What crosses from source to target must be a generator and a head start, not a recording of one occasion, because the receiving place remakes whatever lands there. And the receiving habitat must reproduce everything the method's goal required while the actors it did not choose are enumerated, because isolation is habitat substitution with two silent failures. The description must travel in effect terms with its lineage, the loss on the round trip must be measured, and the receiving place's ground, not adoption or prestige, settles whether the method paid. The framework returns a shape and will not name which method will work in a given place.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 10

- **Statement:** a carried method must be a reapplicable generator and a head start, never the recording of a particular success; the transform is lossy, so the round-trip gap must be measured.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23 — *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 4 :25 — *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 4 :30 — *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 10 :68 — *"a generator crosses, an instance does not"*.
- **Refuter:** a method keeps paying in a new place while only a fixed recipe crossed, with no reapplicable generator and no head start, and a measured round trip shows zero loss across the real place change.

### C2 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** moving a method is a habitat substitution, and capability is a property of method-and-habitat, never of the method alone. Two failures are silent: what was not reproduced and what was unintentionally introduced.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 — *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 — *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :66 — *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68 — *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; :69 — *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*; :70 — *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **Refuter:** a documented transport in which performance survives a habitat substitution that removes a condition the goal required, and the new habitat's unchosen actors measurably do not affect the outcome.

### C3 — cross-frame; checkable by a methodologist against the transfer literature

- **Statement:** what travels must be the bond and the lineage, not the ingredient list; reach in the target place renews only through contact with a differing closure; the target ground settles the result.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :30 — *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 12 :5 — *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :130 — *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* Fig 6 :23 — *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **What to check:** does FRAME's requirement to record why and when a modification was made, and Pearl and Bareinboim's requirement to represent differences between populations, match the shape? Or do they describe something else? Deaton and Cartwright's *"credibility in estimation can lead to incredibility in use"* is the same warning in the language of evidence policy.
- **Refuter:** a method transported in source-only terms with no lineage recorded that still keeps paying against a measured target ground, with no contact with a differing closure and no new atom entering the target library.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any method is universally portable, or that a good method's failure elsewhere is local incompetence.
- Not that local context is everything. The set is closed and the arrangements are not; renewal needs an import (Fig 13 :115).
- Not that fidelity to the original protocol is always the right test. Which elements are core can change with the population; this is a world-source finding from FRAME, not a figure line.
- Not that prestige, standardisation, or number of adopting institutions settle a transfer. Prestige can fund a trial and cannot pay for it.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement in a named place.
- Not a claim that the structure of a method is named by the framework. The name is a binding from another frame, provenance owed.

## 5. What would settle it

A measuring instrument in the receiving place, not a further argument.

1. **Generator Test:** does the carried thing remain reapplicable when the ground changes, or is it only a replayed recipe?
2. **Habitat Test:** enumerate what the goal required in the source, check what was reproduced in the target, and record the unchosen actors that entered.
3. **Round-Trip Test:** measure R_T for the compression across a real place change. Zero loss falsifies the claim that the transform is necessarily lossy.
4. **Closure Test:** did target practitioners contact a differing closure, and did the method's reach move?
5. **Discriminator:** an instrument that distinguishes generator-crossing from lucky habitat match, and required-condition failure from unchosen-actor interference. Not held here; declared `gap`.
