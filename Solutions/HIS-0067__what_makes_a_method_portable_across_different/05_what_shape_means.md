## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for HIS-0067 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about portability that can be stated before any particular method, protocol, discipline or place is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *a method is portable when a generator crosses and the receiving habitat reproduces what the goal required, with the ground of the receiving place settling the result.*
- Name, withheld: *this method is portable because it is evidence based / standardised / empirically validated / universally true.*

The distinction matters here because the literature on method transfer is almost entirely in the naming business. "Best practice", "evidence-based", "standardised protocol" and "local adaptation" are bindings from other frames onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure, read directly:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a bootstrapping convergence process."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "transportability", "external validity", "fidelity and adaptation", "embodied knowledge" and "circulation of knowledge". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (persistence of a low-residual slot after carrying, indexed per place) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating HIS-0067 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question. The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. The essay's own portability rule

*The Library of Babel* contains a direct statement about why analogies carry knowledge across domains, read directly:

> *"The reason why analogies work so well to explain concepts: they are translating structural relationships and problems through language and scenarios that you are already familiar with, so that the knowledge transfers."*

This is the shape of Fig 6 :30 (*"Cast it in effect terms"*) in the essay's own vocabulary: what transfers is the structural relationship, not the source domain's nouns. Fig 9 :30-31 adds *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and *"rather than the cause"* for the same reason. A method described in the source place's local nouns fails this test; a method described as the relation it holds to its ground can be recognised elsewhere.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For HIS-0067 neither has happened for a specific method. There is no ground reading here that pays for "this protocol will work in that place", and the names in the transfer literature were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F9 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a portability claim makes when it reads a method's success in a new place and names its source. A method that pays is a reading. It is consistent with the generator having crossed, with a lucky habitat match, with an unmeasured local condition that the goal required, with an unchosen actor that happened to help, or with a measurement that does not discriminate these cases. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; what is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 6 :30 again: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes with the point that decides the honest stop: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to method transfer

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is the author's own agent-system instance of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 7 :13 (*"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; recorded as an illustration of the rule.

For this question: a list of famous methods, or of prestigious adopting institutions, would be a monoculture, not a check. A method's reputation can give it a budget to be tried in a new place; it cannot establish that it paid there. The legitimate ask is for the receiving place's ground to settle it.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about methods and places. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular method is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement is too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a method is already in the receiving frame's library, naming it is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), and naming the source of a reading without a discriminator. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For HIS-0067 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, a lossy carrying transform whose gap must be measured, and a valve that changes the valence of the method's parts when the residual changes.** It is not a list of best practices, not the name of a universally portable method, and not a ranking of methods by adoption. The names that appear in the transfer literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
