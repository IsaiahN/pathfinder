## What the wider literature bears on the question

Four fields, four different evidence pools. None was allowed to flip a figure verdict.

1. **Statistics and causal inference.** Pearl and Bareinboim make transportability a formal question: a license to transfer effects, decided by representing the *differences and commonalities* between populations before seeing data. Predicts that portability depends on the relation between source and target, not on the method alone. **Corroborates** F5 and F3.
2. **Economics and philosophy of science.** Deaton and Cartwright argue that a trial's estimate applies only to its sample and that extension needs justification and mechanism knowledge: not "what works" but "why things work". Predicts that a recorded result is not a portable method. **Corroborates** F1.
3. **Implementation science.** FRAME requires recording what was changed, when, by whom and why, and separates fidelity-consistent from fidelity-inconsistent modification, with function prioritised over form. Predicts that the lineage and the reason for adaptation travel with the method. **Corroborates** F3 and Fig 12's bond-over-ingredients claim.
4. **History of science.** Roberts refuses the diffusionist picture: practices are shaped by and shape the local context. Sibum's group treats techniques as embodied *"knowledge in action"*. Predicts that a method is not an object moving into an empty room. **Corroborates** F2.

Agreement from these four fields is four witnesses only if their evidence pools do not overlap. They are drawn from distinct literatures, so they are counted separately. They remain secondary; the verdict comes from the figures.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :30-31** — *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*, *"rather than the cause"*. Ask what a portable method would look like in their field before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Later agreement is never pooled as confirmation of stage 1.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-condition shape and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure in a transfer they know; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* — and Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If a reader fires any of the five refuters, stop and correct the shape before offering the links.
