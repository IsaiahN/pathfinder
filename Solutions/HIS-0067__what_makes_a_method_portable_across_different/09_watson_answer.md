# WATSON answer: what makes a method portable across different places and populations?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

What makes a method portable across different places and populations? Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay small in some place-slots after a method is carried there and grow in others? R is indexed per place and population, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The anchor is the receiving place's ground, named in Fig 2 :29. Option A, portability is a property a method owns, predicts a good method works anywhere and failure is local incompetence. Option B, portability is a conjunction of generator-crossing and habitat-reproduction, predicts distinct failure modes and distinct instruments. What each leads to: A sends a recording and blames the receiver; B sends a shape with six falsifiers and stops at the instrument boundary.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale: a relation among method-library, action, target observation and source-to-target transform, at least four operands, carrying an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric, and lossy so not reversible. Scale spans the practitioner loop, the population of practices, and the place across which the method travels, with a lossy membrane between scales (Fig 4).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Method as generator | method Γ, atoms and bonds, carrying loop | reach, |φ| | settlement in source ground | admitted |
| D2 Receiving habitat | local actors, conditions, relations | contact, survival of practice | target ground | admitted |
| D3 Carrying transform | what crosses up, head start down, description | information lost, R_T | measured round-trip gap | admitted |
| D4 Transmission channel | record, lineage, instruments | fidelity of bracket | the ground that does not decay | admitted |
| X1 Universal validity because it worked at home | method alone | truth | none independent of source ground | not a domain yet |
| X2 Prestige or adoption count | reputation, adopters | influence | not a ground, cannot settle | excluded from verdict |

Union, not partition: D1/D3 and D2/D4 form two compounds. The unbundled conditions are C1, a generator crosses, and C2, the habitat reproduces what the goal required (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception to vocabulary to objective to planning to learn and carry (Fig 3). The middle link breaks first.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain)

**D1 Method as generator, status triangulated (Fig 4 membrane and Fig 10 cross-scale rule, low overlap).**

> *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22)
> *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* (Fig 4 :23)
> *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* (Fig 4 :25)
> *"a generator crosses, an instance does not"* (Fig 10 :68)

Reading: a recipe replayed elsewhere repeats a success and cannot produce a new one; what crosses is a generator. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a method keeps paying with only a recipe crossing and no head start.

**D2 Receiving habitat, status triangulated (Fig 11 habitat and Fig 4 landing rule, low overlap).**

> *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* (Fig 11 :39)
> *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* (Fig 11 :66)
> *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* (Fig 11 :68)
> *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :69)

Reading: transport is habitat substitution with two silent failures; a pilot proves wiring, not capability. Mode `specified`. Refuter: performance survives removal of a required condition with no effect from unchosen actors.

**D3 Carrying transform, status triangulated (Fig 4, Symbols table, Formula step 6, distinct roles).**

> *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* (Fig 4 :27)
> *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30)

Reading: R_T is a quantity to measure, not to assume away. Mode `general` for the requirement, `specified` for this carrying. Refuter: zero measured loss across a real place change.

**D4 Transmission channel, status triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 9, Fig 12 and Fig 13, distinct roles).**

> *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* (Fig 6 :30)
> *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* (Fig 9 :30)
> *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* (Fig 12 :5)
> *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* (Fig 13 :130)

Reading: the lineage and the bond travel, not the ingredient list. Mode `general`. Refuter: a source-only description with no lineage still pays in the target.

**Renewal and anchor, status triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 13, Fig 2, low overlap).**

> *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* (Fig 6 :23)
> *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* (Fig 8 :32)
> *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* (Fig 13 :115)
> *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35)

Reading: renewal needs a differing closure; adoption and agreement do not settle the result. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a method keeps paying with no contact with a differing closure, or agreement prospectively predicts the next failure.

**Middle link, status single-source (Fig 3).**

> *"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."* (Fig 3 :24)
> *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27)

Reading: vocabulary and objective, not execution, are the usual break. Mode `specified`. Refuter: failure located at planning with the first three links measured as working.

**Naming which method will work where, status `gap`.** No figure line governs this after a genuine re-census. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*; Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held.

## WORLD (secondary, each source read; field, author and venue and year, quote, prediction, corroborates or conflicts)

- **Statistics and causal inference.** Judea Pearl and Elias Bareinboim, *External Validity: From Do-Calculus to Transportability Across Populations*, Statistical Science 29(4):579-595, 2014, read via arxiv:1503.01603 on 2026-11-23. Quote: *"a license to transfer causal effects learned in experimental studies to a new population"* and *"selection diagrams for expressing knowledge about differences and commonalities between populations of interest"*. Prediction: transfer is licensed only when differences between populations are explicitly represented. **Corroborates** F5 and F3. Overlap: low, distinct statistics pool.
- **Economics and philosophy of science.** Angus Deaton and Nancy Cartwright, *Understanding and misunderstanding randomized controlled trials*, Social Science & Medicine 210:2-21, 2018, read via PMC6019115 on 2026-11-23. Quote: *"credibility in estimation can lead to incredibility in use"*; trials contribute only *"as part of a cumulative program... to discover not 'what works', but 'why things work'"*. Prediction: a recorded estimate is not a portable method. **Corroborates** F1. Overlap: low, distinct economics and philosophy pool.
- **Implementation science.** Shannon Wiltsey Stirman, Ana A. Baumann and Christopher J. Miller, *The FRAME*, Implementation Science 14:58, 2019, read via Springer Nature on 2026-11-23. Quote: reporting must cover *"when and how in the implementation process the modification was made"* and *"the reasons for the modification"*; *"the function of intervention elements"* can be prioritised over form. Prediction: lineage and reason for adaptation travel with the method. **Corroborates** F3 and Fig 12. Overlap: low, distinct health-implementation pool.
- **History of science, embodied knowledge.** Otto Sibum, *Experimental History of Science* research group, Max Planck Institute for the History of Science, 1999-2007, read via MPIWG on 2026-11-23. Quote: *"Knowledge governs but does not determine practice; and practices, as they are enacted, may constitute a source of new information and open prior knowledge to reproduction or transformation"*; practices as *"knowledge in action"*. Prediction: a method is embodied practice changed by enactment, not a disembodied recipe. **Corroborates** F1 and F2. Overlap: low, distinct history pool.
- **History of science, global exchange.** Lissa Roberts, *Situating Science in Global History: Local Exchanges and Networks of Circulation*, Itinerario 33(1):9-30, 2009, read via docslib on 2026-11-23. Quote: *"the achievements of modern science must not blind us to the complex history whereby its institutions, practices and visions of nature came both to be shaped by, and to have a formative impact on, the global context in which they developed"*. Prediction: methods are shaped by and shape the receiving context; diffusion is not automatic. **Corroborates** F2. Overlap: low relative to the other four, though both history sources read practices at a similar stratum, so they are counted as one historical pool with two views.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: a method is portable when a generator and a head start cross and the receiving habitat reproduces what the method's goal required with its unchosen actors enumerated. It fails when a recording is replayed, when a required condition was not reproduced, when unchosen actors change the method's valence, or when the target library never meets a differing closure. The description must travel in effect terms with its lineage, the round-trip gap must be measured, and the receiving place's ground, not agreement, settles the result. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`. Which specific method will work in a named place is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A method failing C1 would be sent more training in the recipe, or a method failing C2 would be told to improvise more, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular a method paying with only a recipe crossing, performance surviving removal of a required condition, zero measured round-trip loss, or agreement prospectively predicting the next transfer success. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that portability is fully predicted by method properties alone, with habitat measured as irrelevant, would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
