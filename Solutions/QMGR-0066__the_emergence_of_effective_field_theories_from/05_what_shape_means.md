## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and immediately, *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the form any candidate account of EFT emergence must have, stateable before the specific EFT is named. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape here is: a directed, lossy cross-scale map; settled composites promoted to atoms at the next level; emergent properties as what a composition leaves behind; the continuum as a derivative; a round-trip gap reported rather than hidden. "A specific EFT with fields X, Y and coefficients Z" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the direct warrant for the unbundling in `01` section 1: derivability, universality, loss and irreducibility are searched separately.
- Fig 9 :30-31 (via the tacit-knowledge essay): *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was cast in effect terms (a many-to-one, non-invertible cross-scale map), not in field-theory jargon.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the process narrowed the question to a shape and a refuter; that is the stop.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For EFTs, the ground is low-energy measurement, Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 provenance discussion). The physics frame already holds specific EFTs; borrowing one is an import with provenance, not a derivation from the six-term set.

The tacit-knowledge essay sharpens the transfer problem: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* An effect description of the shape travels; a private shorthand for a specific EFT does not.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters for the round-trip gap. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The reading does not determine the source. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Also: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

This is exactly the EFT situation. The IR reading (the EFT) does not determine its source (the UV completion), because several UV paths give the same IR behavior. Recovering the UV from the IR is a metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminating instrument at higher energy, not a name asserted from the low-energy reading. R_T in `01` F6 is the formal record of that loss.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the self-validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And on drift: *"On every level of a system, data is lost."* Which is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 4 :27, *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" require of an account of EFT emergence?** Four constraints and one limit, none of which names a specific theory:

1. It must be a cross-scale map with a direction, not a same-scale substitution. (Fig 4 membrane)
2. It must throw away detail, and that loss must be what makes the result universal. (Fig 4 :27-29)
3. It must treat what is fundamental as level relative: settled effective composites become atoms at the next level. (Fig 12 :103-104)
4. It must report the round-trip gap R_T rather than pretend the IR determines the UV. (Fig 4 :30-32; source essay)
5. **Limit:** it cannot select the specific EFT, field content or coefficients. Those are bindings from the physics frame and from the ground, provenanced as such. (Fig 9 :32)

The continuum deserves a separate note because the question sits in Condensed Matter and Analogue Gravity. Fig 13 :43-44 removes the continuum from the minimum set: it is a spectrum without gaps, a composition, not a primitive. So an emergent continuum is not an anomaly needing a special explanation; it is what this framework expects a composition to leave behind.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (recorded, not erased)

I initially wrote the shape-not-a-name rule as if it forbade naming any effective degree of freedom. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Effective fields such as phonons are compositions already held in the effective closure; naming one is a binding from the physics frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming the framework *derives* that binding: it does not, because coarse graining adds no atom (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the IR reading still does not determine its UV source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
