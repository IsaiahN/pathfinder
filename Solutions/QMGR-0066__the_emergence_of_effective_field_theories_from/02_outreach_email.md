**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated cleanly. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one cross-scale claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about one specific output, not about the theory that produced it. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read the framework first. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the emergence of an effective field theory from a microscopic quantum system is a lossy, directed cross-scale map. The framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with composition running across kinds and never within one. Across a scale boundary, detail is thrown away. That is what makes the resulting long-wavelength description reapplicable across many different microscopic systems, and it is why the trip back is not reversible. A settled effective description becomes an atom for the theory at the next level, so what counts as fundamental is a fact about a level rather than about the world. Emergent properties such as smoothness, symmetry and discreteness are what a composition leaves behind, not extra fundamental members. The continuum is explicitly treated as a derivative, not a primitive.
>
> **The limit I am stating:** the framework returns the shape of the effective theory, not a specific effective theory. It does not select field content, gauge group or operator coefficients, and it does not recover microscopic information from the infrared. Analogue gravity systems are offered as a different frame that can triangulate, not as the ground; analogy is not identity.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. an information-preserving cross-scale map in the relevant sector, where the framework says detail is thrown away;
> 2. a proof that irreducibility is absolute, or that a settled effective description cannot serve as an atom at the next level;
> 3. a derivation of the continuum as a primitive member of a minimum set rather than as a composition;
> 4. an effective theory whose form depends essentially on microscopic details the framework says are washed out;
> 5. a final, cutoff-free effective description that closes all residuals.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, the shape is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
