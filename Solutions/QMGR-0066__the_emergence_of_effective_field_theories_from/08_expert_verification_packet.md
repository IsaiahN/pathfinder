# Expert verification packet: emergence of effective field theories

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle which degrees of freedom are fundamental at a given scale. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An effective field theory emerges from a microscopic quantum system as a directed, lossy cross-scale map. Detail is thrown away, and that loss is what makes the long-wavelength description universal and reapplicable across many microscopic systems; the trip back is not reversible, and the round-trip gap R_T is the honest measure of what the infrared cannot say about the ultraviolet. What counts as fundamental is a fact about a level, because a settled effective description becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. Emergent properties such as smoothness and symmetry are what a composition leaves behind; the continuum is a derivative, not a primitive. The framework returns the form any candidate EFT must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It does not name a specific EFT, its field content or its coefficients.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and physics-facing; checkable by reading Fig 4 and comparing with the RG

- **Statement:** the UV to IR passage is lossy and directed, so universality and non-invertibility are two faces of one transform.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :27 *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :28-29 *"Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent."*
- **What to check:** does a real coarse-graining map discard detail in the relevant sector, and do many UV completions yield one IR fixed point? Wilczek (PNAS 2013): *"we will be satisfied to find an effective theory that averages or 'integrates out' the influence of fluctuations smaller than some cut-off."* Nakayama (arXiv 2014): *"the renormalization group flow is accompanied with a kind of coarse graining. We lose information along the flow."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit an information-preserving cross-scale map with zero R_T in that sector.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Fig 12 and Fig 13

- **Statement:** irreducible is level relative; emergent properties are what a composition leaves behind; the continuum is a derivative.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :103 *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."* Fig 13 :124-125 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Fig 13 :43-44 *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."*
- **What to check:** is there any compositional derivation that makes a set of degrees of fundamental at every scale, or makes the continuum a primitive member of a minimum set?
- **Refuter:** prove absolute irreducibility, or derive the continuum as a primitive. Either breaks the claim.

### C3 - framework limit; checkable by reading Fig 9 and Fig 6

- **Statement:** the framework returns the form of an EFT, not the EFT; coarse graining adds no atom, so apparent new degrees of freedom are promoted composites.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 12 :104 (above) for the promotion mechanism.
- **What to check:** can the six-term substrate uniquely select a specific EFT with no import from the physics frame? If yes, the limit is wrong.
- **Refuter:** produce such a unique selection, fully provenanced, or show that coarse graining adds a primitive absent from the microscopic closure.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the EFT is the microscopic theory in fewer words, and not that the UV can be recovered from the IR. R_T is nonzero.
- Not that the framework names the EFT, its field content, its gauge group or its operator coefficients. Those are bindings from the physics frame, provenanced as such.
- Not a final, cutoff-free effective theory, and not a theory of everything (Fig 7 no last room).
- Not that analogue gravity is gravity or that an analogue measurement is the anchor. It is a different closure that can triangulate; analogy is not identity.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

1. **Invertibility test:** a cross-scale map with zero round-trip gap where detail is supposedly discarded.
2. **Level test:** absolute irreducibility, or a settled effective theory that cannot be an atom at the next level.
3. **Continuum test:** the continuum derived as a primitive rather than a composition.
4. **Universality test:** two same-symmetry microscopic theories flowing to different IR fixed points.
5. **Analogue test:** a BEC or fluid analogue that measures which effective structure mediates which state relation, with the record showing convergent derivation rather than adopted import.
