## 0. The residual under audit, stated in effect terms

> **Question:** The emergence of effective field theories from microscopic quantum systems.
>
> **Residual R:** Given a microscopic quantum system (actors: fundamental fields or lattice spins or quanta, plus a Hamiltonian at a UV cutoff Λ0), a low-energy, long-wavelength description is available whose form is largely independent of the microscopic detail. What is the relation between the two descriptions, and what is lost between them?

**Arity, symmetry, scale (Fig 9 :21-22: *"R, described / arity · symmetry · scale"*).**

- **Arity:** two descriptions joined by a scale-directed map, UV and IR. This is not a union of two independent questions. It is a membrane crossing (Fig 4). The verification frame is a separate third domain, so the overall situation is a chain plus one external frame, not a flat list.
- **Symmetry:** the map is directional and many-to-one. Many microscopic theories flow to the same long-wavelength theory (universality), so the crossing is not invertible. Its inversion is not a symmetry of the situation.
- **Scale:** explicitly cross-scale. The relevant comparison is between what holds at Λ0 and what holds at Λ_IR, with a round-trip gap R_T between them (Fig 4 :30).

**What each option leads to.** Option A (literal derivation, information preserving, effective degrees of freedom are new fundamentals) sends a claim that contradicts the lossy-transform law and cannot account for universality. Option B (membrane crossing, lossy, level-relative irreducibility, nonzero R_T) sends a checkable shape with a stated refuter and an open slot where the physics frame must bind the particulars. This audit derives B before concluding, then states what would overturn it.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term.** The sought-for shape is written down here before the run: *a cross-scale description formed by throwing away detail, in which settled composites become atoms for the next level, and in which the discarded information is reported as a gap rather than hidden.* Binding it lets it accrue a residual like any other term, which is the remedy formula step "Direction" prescribes for a frame that otherwise cannot check its own aim.

## 3. Findings, each with verbatim figure lines and a refuter

### F1 - Emergence is a Fig 4 membrane crossing, not a same-scale composition (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 4 :27:

> **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."**

Fig 4 :28-29:

> **"Going up throws away detail; that is what makes the result reapplicable. But it means the trip is not reversible, and something carried up and brought back down will not match what was sent."**

Fig 4 :22:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

The passage from a microscopic quantum system to an EFT is exactly this membrane. Throwing away short-distance detail is what makes the long-wavelength description reapplicable across many microscopic realizations, and it is also why the trip is not reversible. Universality and irreducibility are two faces of one transform, not two independent facts. This is triangulated across Fig 4 (the transform) and Fig 12 :103-104 (level-relative irreducibility), two figures with low overlap: one describes the crossing, the other describes what a level may call irreducible.

**Mode:** `general` (structural), applied `specified` to EFT emergence. **Refuter:** exhibit a cross-scale map in which the relevant sector is information preserving, so the round-trip gap is zero where the figure says detail is thrown away.

### F2 - Irreducibility is a fact about a level, never about the world (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 12 :103:

> **"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."**

Fig 12 :104:

> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"**

The modern EFT picture is this line applied to physics. What counts as fundamental at Λ0 (quarks and gluons) is not what counts at Λ_IR (pions, nucleons, phonons). Each settled effective theory becomes an atom for the theory composed over it. The framework does not say there is no microscopic world; it says "fundamental" is indexed to a level. This is triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 4 :33, *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."*

**Mode:** `general` structural; the specific physics mapping (which composites at which scale) is `specified` and ungrounded. **Refuter:** prove that a particular set of degrees of freedom is fundamental at every scale, or show a settled effective molecule cannot serve as an atom at the next level.

### F3 - Universality is "different routes can arrive at the same substance" (single-source figure, low-overlap world corroboration)

Fig 13 :121:

> **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**

Fig 13 :124-125:

> **"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."**

Different microscopic chains may compose functionally identical effective terms, which is what a universality class is. The emergent properties (smoothness, symmetry, chaos, discreteness) are what a composition leaves behind, not additional fundamental members. The world literature is explicit and low overlap: Wilczek writes that *"the same value of the critical exponent is observed in many different materials with vastly different Curie temperatures, a property we call 'universality'"*; Nakayama writes that *"we can then integrate out the short-range degrees of freedom that might show non-universal dynamics. Afterwards we can talk about the effective theory of the long range degrees of freedom by keeping only relevant degrees of freedom"*. These corroborate the figure verdict but do not decide it. Triangulation status: single-source for the figure definition, with corroboration from two independent physics sources; the verdict comes from the figures.

**Mode:** `general` structural. **Refuter:** exhibit two microscopic theories with the same symmetries, dimensionality and range that flow to different IR fixed points, so the "same substance" claim fails.

### F4 - The continuum is a derivative, not a primitive (single-source, directly relevant to analogue gravity)

Fig 13 :43-44:

> **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."**

The framework explicitly removes the continuum from its minimum set because a generating set must not contain what its own members produce. Smoothness is a spectrum without gaps, a composition. So an effective spacetime continuum arising from a discrete microscopic substrate is figure-licensed: it is a composition leaving behind smoothness, not a seventh fundamental member. This bears directly on the Condensed Matter and Analogue Gravity section. The analogue gravity literature reports the same shape experimentally, for example Belenchia, Liberati and Mohd (2014) report *"a Lorentz invariant, geometric theory of semiclassical gravity emerges from an underlying quantum theory of matter in flat spacetime"*.

**Mode:** `general` structural; the specific analogue systems are `specified`. **Refuter:** derive the continuum as a primitive member of a minimum set, so it is no longer a composition.

### F5 - Coarse graining adds no atom, so apparent new degrees of freedom are promoted composites (triangulated)

Fig 6 :21:

> **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Coarse graining stays inside closure(Γ_UV). Phonons and magnons are not imported primitives; they are compositions within the microscopic closure that become atoms at the IR level by Fig 12 :104. This dissolves the apparent conflict noted in the census re-census. Fig 6 governs one closure; Fig 12 governs the level-relative promotion. The EFT's form is reachable from the microscopic closure; the specific operator coefficients are matched to the ground (D4), not minted from nothing. Fig 5 :30 names the failure to avoid: *"the machinery worked; the answer was already known"* if one claims the framework produced a specific EFT it did not derive.

**Mode:** `general` structural; coefficient matching is `specified`, ungrounded here. **Refuter:** show that an EFT requires a primitive absent from the microscopic closure and that no settled composite can fill the slot.

### F6 - The round-trip gap R_T is nonzero and is the honest report of what is discarded (triangulated)

Fig 4 :30:

> **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."**

Fig 4 :31-32:

> **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."**

Carry the microscopic theory up to the EFT and back down: the reconstruction does not match what was sent. Infinitely many UV completions give the same IR EFT, so the EFT cannot determine its own ultraviolet. R_T is the enumerated set of discarded details (irrelevant operators, microscopic couplings), recorded qualitatively here because no measurement was taken. Nakayama's independent physics statement corroborates: *"the renormalization group flow is accompanied with a kind of coarse graining. We lose information along the flow."* This corroborates but does not decide. Treating "the EFT is the microscopic theory in fewer words" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

**Mode:** `general` structural; the size of R_T for a given theory is `specified`, unmeasured. **Refuter:** demonstrate an invertible round trip for a sector where detail is supposedly thrown away.

### F7 - The framework returns a shape, not a name (limit, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 9 :27-28:

> **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**

What the Tether can say for this question: any candidate EFT must be a local, symmetry-respecting, cross-kind composition, level relative and valid only up to a cutoff. What it cannot say: which field content, which gauge group, which operator coefficients. Those are bindings from the physics frame (D3) and from matching to the ground (D4), and they carry zero evidentiary weight until provenance is shown. The canonical figures README states the same limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The Wikipedia EFT article independently describes the binding: *"An effective field theory includes the appropriate degrees of freedom to describe physical phenomena occurring at a chosen length scale or energy scale, while ignoring substructure and degrees of freedom at shorter distances"*.

**Mode:** `general` limit. **Refuter:** produce a derivation from the six-term substrate that uniquely selects a specific EFT without importing anything from the physics frame.

### F8 - The ground, not agreement, settles the EFT (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."**

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

An EFT is settled by low-energy experiment, not by agreement with the microscopic theory or with other theorists. Analogue gravity is useful precisely because it is a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23) and can therefore triangulate: Barceló, Liberati and Visser describe the programme as one that *"explores analogues of general relativistic gravitational fields within other physical systems, particularly but not exclusively in condensed matter systems"*, while warning in the body of the same review that *"analogy is not identity"*. The analogue is a witness, not the anchor; the ground remains measurement.

**Mode:** `general` for the verdict structure; the specific experiments are `specified`. **Refuter:** settle an EFT by consensus of frames sharing one evidence pool and show the ground does not move.

### F9 - No last EFT (single-source, triangulated with Fig 4)

Fig 7 :6:

> **"the limit: no last room"**

Fig 7 :30:

> **"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"**

An EFT is valid up to a cutoff. A cutoff-free, final effective theory would be the last room, which the framework forbids. This is the same limit as Fig 4 :33, *"Which is why there is no top and no infinite regress."* It is not a claim that there is no ultraviolet physics; it is a claim that no frame's description is terminal.

**Mode:** `general` structural. **Refuter:** exhibit a final effective description whose residual no further question can reopen.

### F10 - The residual is per operator sector, not a global score (single-source)

Fig 1 :28:

For EFTs this means tracking which operator or which observable carries the failure (the sector where the EFT breaks down), not averaging into a single "the theory works to 5 percent" number. A global near-zero residual with one live sector is a legal state (Fig 1 SVG line 13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*). Aggregating is how the breakdown signal disappears.

**Mode:** `general` structural. **Refuter:** show that a single global residual determines where the EFT fails without per-sector indexing.

## 3b. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A cross-scale map between a microscopic quantum system and its long-wavelength description is exhibited that is information preserving in the relevant sector, so R_T is zero where Fig 4 :27-29 says detail is thrown away.
2. Irreducibility is shown to be absolute: a set of degrees of freedom is proved fundamental at every scale, or a settled effective molecule cannot become an atom for the next level (breaks Fig 12 :103-104).
3. The continuum is derived as a primitive member of a minimum set rather than as a composition (breaks Fig 13 :43-44).
4. An EFT is produced whose form depends essentially on microscopic details that Fig 13 :121, :124-125 says are washed out (breaks universality).
5. A final, cutoff-free effective description is exhibited that closes all residuals (breaks Fig 7 no last room).
6. An EFT is uniquely selected from the six-term substrate with no binding from the physics frame (breaks the shape-not-a-name limit, Fig 9 :32).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the EFT in the microscopic closure?** Its form is (Fig 6 :21 composes inside the closure); its coefficients are matched to the ground, not minted.
2. **Which structure mediates a crossing?** A structure is required between states if states compose at all (Fig 13 :75); which one is not given by the six alone (shape, not a name).
3. **Why universality?** Different routes to one substance (Fig 13 :121), emergent properties are what a composition leaves behind (Fig 13 :124-125).
4. **Is the crossing reversible?** No. Detail is thrown away (Fig 4 :28-29); R_T measures what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31-32).
5. **Import?** No new atom is added by coarse graining (Fig 6 :21). The physics frame is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence.
6. **Settlement?** Experiment at accessible energies, never agreement (Fig 2 :29, :35). Status `candidate`.
7. **Route?** Describe the residual before picking a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). The physics frame was not named first here; the residual was.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: an EFT emerges from a microscopic quantum system as a lossy, directed membrane crossing in which detail is discarded, settled composites become atoms at the new level, emergent properties are what the composition leaves behind, and the round-trip gap is the honest measure of what cannot be recovered. No final EFT, no unique naming of field content from the substrate. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**; one open item remains (the numerical size of R_T for a given theory), which is a job for the ground, not for an argument.
