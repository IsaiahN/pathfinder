## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific EFT and any claim of lossless derivation do not. An EFT emerges from a microscopic quantum system as a directed, lossy membrane crossing: detail is discarded, which is what makes the long-wavelength theory universal and reapplicable; settled effective descriptions become atoms at the next level, so irreducibility is level relative; emergent properties are what a composition leaves behind and the continuum is a derivative; the round-trip gap R_T is nonzero and is the honest report of what the IR cannot say about the UV; universality classes are functional groups, or isomers only under the identical-atom-set rewrite; no final, cutoff-free EFT exists. The framework returns the form any candidate must have, not a specific theory. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result; a source-discriminating instrument at high energy, a proof of absolute irreducibility, a primitive continuum, or a counterexample to universality would each move it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states five refuters before the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings (which EFT, which coefficients, which analogue implementation) are withheld, so nothing structural has to be withdrawn if one is later shown wrong. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive is repudiated.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An invertible cross-scale map with zero R_T in the relevant sector would break the lossy-transform claim; a proof of absolute irreducibility, or a settled effective theory that cannot become an atom, would break level relativity; a derivation of the continuum as a primitive would break the derivative claim; two same-symmetry microscopic theories flowing to different IR fixed points would break universality; a final cutoff-free EFT would break the no-last-room limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
