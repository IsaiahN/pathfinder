## What this package decides, in effect terms

Given a microscopic quantum system (degrees of freedom plus a Hamiltonian at a UV cutoff), what accounts for the fact that long-wavelength, low-energy physics is captured by a local effective field theory whose form is largely independent of the microscopic detail, and what is lost in the passage?

Option A: the EFT is a literal derivation that preserves all microscopic information, and the effective degrees of freedom are new fundamental entities.
Option B: the EFT is a membrane crossing, a lossy cross-scale transform in which detail is thrown away, settled effective composites become atoms for the next level, and the round-trip gap is the honest measure of what cannot be recovered.

The figure-derived shape is option B. Option A is refused (see findings F1, F5, F6 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). The two are not averaged into a middle position.

## What an answer would look like

A shape, not a name: the form any candidate EFT must have (composed across states and structures, level relative, local, symmetry respecting, valid up to a cutoff), together with a pre-registered refuter. The framework does not select field content, gauge group, or operator coefficients. Those are bindings from the physics frame and must be provenanced as such. Committing to that shape before the run is the discipline formula step "Direction" calls for, so the sought-for form accrues a residual like any other term.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make the agreement worthless.
2. **A shared pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Two readers of the same review are one frame with two names.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's full architecture.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
