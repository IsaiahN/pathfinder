## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is reframed, not answered yes or no. Algorithmic character is a level-relative remainder of composition, not a primitive: the continuum was removed for being a derivative, and discreteness joins it as a derivative. Any algorithmic description is a closure with finite kinds and unbounded arrangements, so it can search without end and there is no last room. Four barriers bind it, held apart: self-limit (no internal proof of completeness), closure limit (only import moves the wall), combinatorial limit (infinite effort inside a finite reach), and settlement limit (two currencies, output is not success). The ground-level question of whether physical law is in itself algorithmic is `gap`, recorded as a verdict rather than a silence. This is a `specified` and ungrounded result. The strongest outreach point is the reframing with its four pre-registered refuters, not an assertion about any particular algorithm.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Every substantive claim is mode-labelled and carries its own refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or by forcing a yes or no where the figures require a gap. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four refuters are separate, so any one can fire without collapsing the others. The reframing can be withdrawn and the coverage question reopened without touching the barrier derivations. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive must be withdrawn, because none was claimed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates a discrete algorithmic source from a continuum-limit source at the relevant scale would name the level and settle D2; a formal system that certifies its own completeness under Fig 8's conditions would break the self-limit; a composition that adds an atom, a last room, a seventh primitive, a single additive price, or a round trip with `R_T = 0` would break the corresponding finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
