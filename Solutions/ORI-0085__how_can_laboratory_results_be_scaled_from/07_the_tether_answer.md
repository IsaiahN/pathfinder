# The Tether's answer: scaling a lab result to a geological habitat

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a field result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A controlled microcosm yields a result; the geological setting is a different habitat. The residual is the gap between what the lab frame predicts when it extrapolates and what the ground would return there. It is indexed per condition, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). It is a union of conditions (Fig 9 :55), not one hard problem. The two options lead to different places: treat the result as a property of the chemistry and you send a recording to be replayed at larger scale; treat it as a property of agent and habitat and you send a method, a measured gap, and a refuter.

## 2. The shape

1. **The lab result is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A lab success licenses a mechanism claim under the conditions supplied. It does not license a geological capability claim. Mode `general` for the structure, `specified` here.

2. **The move is a substitution, not an enlargement.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Two silent failure modes follow: what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you (Fig 11 :68).

3. **The kernel is invariant; the cast is not.** Fig 4 :19-20: *"The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* So a method can transfer, but the actors (minerals, cycling, dilution, time) must be re-enumerated in the field.

4. **Carry the method, never the recording.** Fig 4 :22-23: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* The exportable item is a protocol for varying conditions until failure (Cleaves 2013), not a single optimised yield.

5. **Measure `R_T`; do not assume it.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*

6. **Enumerate the habitat from the world.** Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Second-order contact matters: a conditioning film forms on a mineral surface within minutes (Westall et al. 2023), so the field surface is not the flask surface (Fig 11 :52-53).

7. **Unbundle the union.** Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever (bigger vessel, longer run, added mineral) clears only the single-condition regions.

8. **The ground is the rock record, not lab agreement.** Fig 2 :29 names the anchor as *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Twenty labs reproducing a yield are one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "prebiotic lab results are irrelevant." The wiring is real and the method is useful.
- Not a named mineral, cycling regime, concentration mechanism or duration. The framework returns a shape, not a name; those are bindings from the geology frame, provenanced as such.
- Not "it scales" and not "it fails." Both are verdicts without a measured `R_T`.
- Not a general field-equivalence criterion. That is a declared `gap` (F7), not a hidden answer.
- Not `grounded`. No rock or field measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not a claim that the search for such a criterion has proved it absent. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

The ground: a field or rock measurement that changes its answer only when the pathway really changes, not when labs disagree. A source-discriminating instrument in the sense of *The Hard Problem is About Source*: something that distinguishes among geological settings that would produce the same lab reading. Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

The four pre-registered refuters (from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3):

1. habitat invariance: the result survives a documented substitution that removes a required condition and still pays at the ground;
2. recording success: a replayed recording, with no method carried, pays at the ground;
3. zero gap: a measured `R_T = 0` across all required conditions;
4. single lever: one intervention restores all four union conditions with no categorically different residue.

A fifth event would close a gap rather than refute the shape: publication and use of a general field-equivalence instrument.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set supplies the shape of a scaled result and the rules for moving it. It does not supply the geological habitat, the specific conditions, or the equivalence instrument. Those must be read off the world (Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*). The derivation stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage: the shape, the two gaps, the four refuters, and the sources. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README), so a condition that fits the lab reading is not thereby the source of that reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a prebiotic lab result will or will not scale. It says that "scaling" is the wrong picture. A tiny controlled system is a substituted habitat, a test harness, and what it proves is wiring under supplied conditions, not capability in a geological setting. The move to the field is a habitat substitution: you reproduce what the result required, you inherit actors you did not choose, and there are two silent failure modes, what you failed to reproduce and what you brought with you. What can cross the boundary is the method, never the recording of the one success, and the transfer is lossy, so the honest report is the measured round-trip gap rather than a verdict. The habitat has to be enumerated from the field, including second-order contact such as the conditioning film that coats a mineral within minutes. The claim is a shape, not a protocol; it names no mineral, cycling regime or duration. Four refuters were written in advance. The answer stops at the missing instrument that would tell us what "the same result happened in the field" would even look like, and the rock record, not agreement among labs, is what would close it.
