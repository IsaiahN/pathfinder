## The question as an effect

What is being decided is whether a result purchased in a controlled microcosm survives a move into a geological habitat. Option A says the result is a property of the chemistry and scales with volume or time. Option B says the result is a property of agent and habitat, so the move is a habitat substitution that must be enumerated and whose round-trip gap must be measured. A leads to a recording replayed at larger scale and a claim the ground has not settled. B leads to a method, a stated gap, and a refuter. The framework picks B in effect terms, then stops where the instruments stop.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a method that can be reapplied in a geological habitat, with a measured round-trip gap, settled by a rock or field measurement rather than by agreement among labs.* That is the sought-for shape. It is refusable by the criteria in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40). Asking a checker to trust the framework before they have tested the output spends the only independence they contribute (Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*). Public opt-in channels also avoid the GDPR/CAN-SPAM failure that personal-email harvesting creates.
