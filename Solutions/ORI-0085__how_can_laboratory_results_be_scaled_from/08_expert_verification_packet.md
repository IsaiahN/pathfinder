# Expert verification packet: scaling lab results to a geological habitat

**What this is:** a claim in a form a field geochemist, prebiotic chemist or astrobiologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No field or rock measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A result obtained in a tiny, controlled lab system does not scale by magnification. The lab system is a substituted habitat, a test harness; what it proves is wiring under supplied conditions, not capability in a geological habitat. Moving the result to the field is a habitat substitution: you must reproduce what the result required and you inherit actors you did not choose, with two silent failure modes, what you failed to reproduce and what you brought with you. Only a method can cross the scale boundary, never the recording of a single success; the transform is lossy and the honest report is the round-trip gap `R_T`. The geological habitat must be enumerated from the field, including second-order contact, and the whole is a union of conditions that one lever cannot clear. The framework returns this shape; it does not name the mineral, cycling regime or duration, and it does not say whether any particular result scaled.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** a lab result is a synthetic solve in a substituted habitat. Moving it is a substitution, not an enlargement, and a field-equivalence criterion is not supplied by the figures.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
- **What to check:** is the geological setting being treated as a bigger flask anywhere in the claim? It is not. Does the figure actually say a lab result cannot be used? It does not; it says what kind of thing is proved.
- **Refuter:** a protocol whose result is unchanged across a documented habitat substitution removing a required condition, and still paid by a field or rock measurement. Also: a published, instrument-backed general criterion for field equivalence, which closes the gap.

### C2  -  Method over recording; checkable by reading Figure 4

- **Statement:** what crosses the scale boundary is the method, never the recorded outcome, and the loss must be reported as a measured `R_T`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :23 *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31 *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*
- **What to check:** is "the reaction works in the lab" being used as if it were the exportable object? The claim says the exportable object is the protocol for testing to failure, as in Cleaves (2013).
- **Refuter:** a recorded outcome replayed at field scale with no method carried and no condition varied, still paid by the ground; or a measured `R_T = 0` for every required condition.

### C3  -  Union and ground; checkable by reading Figures 9, 2 and 11

- **Statement:** scaling is a union of conditions, not a list of independent ones; the habitat is enumerated from the world; the ground, not agreement, settles it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **What to check:** does a single intervention in the literature restore all four conditions at once and leave no categorically different residue? The claim predicts it will not.
- **Refuter:** such a single lever; or a field measurement that changes its answer when labs disagree (then it is a frame, not an anchor).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that prebiotic chemistry is irrelevant, or that lab wiring is false.
- Not that any named mineral, cycling regime, concentration mechanism or duration is the answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not "it scales" and not "it fails" for any particular result.
- Not a general field-equivalence instrument. That is a declared `gap` (F7).
- Not a derivation of the geological habitat from lab terms.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
- Not that the missing instrument has been proved absent. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Source-discriminating instrument:** a measurement that distinguishes geological settings producing the same lab reading. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
2. **A measured round trip:** the difference between lab prediction and field observation for every condition the result required, reported as `R_T` rather than suppressed.
3. **A field analogue:** a natural or field system where the pathway is observed end to end, not a single step. Westall et al. (2023): *"no experiment has yet been run end to end, i.e. from the initial ingredients... to the emergence of a protocell."*

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :66-70 and Fig 4 :22-25, :30-31 (six short passages).
2. Name one condition your own field would insist on reproducing before calling a lab result field-relevant. Check whether the claim forbids naming it (it does not; it only refuses to derive it from the figures).
3. Name one actor a field system introduces that a clean lab system does not, and say whether it was enumerated or assumed.
4. Say what you would count as a failure of the shape claim, or name an existing instrument for field equivalence.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
