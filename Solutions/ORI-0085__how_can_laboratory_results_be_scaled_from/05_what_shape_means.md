## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0085:

- **`LAB(???)GEOLOGY` is a shape.** A method survives a habitat substitution, with a measured round-trip gap, settled by the geological ground.
- **`LAB(montmorillonite, wet-dry, 10^4 years)GEOLOGY` is a name.** It selects a particular mineral, cycling regime and duration. The figure set does not supply those; field measurement does.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the nearest held vocabulary is "screening matrix", "conditioning film", "natural analogue", "round-trip gap".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A scaling claim should therefore be sent as: what you would expect to see in the field if the result had transferred, and what would count as a failure.
- Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."* The shape includes the gap as a term.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a field or rock measurement, not a reproducible lab yield. Fig 2 :29 lists *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"* as anchors; the geological analogue is a rock record or field observation that does not move when labs disagree.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The geochemistry literature is such a frame. Westall et al. (2023) import the conditioning film as an actor the lab frame did not hold; it explains a gap (clean-surface catalysis versus field-surface catalysis), so it passes the shadow test in effect terms. Its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

The eight-essay set warns against turning a visible choice into an invisible one. *Arrival of the Fittest*, "A note on what is not cited", lists claims with no prior source found, including *"isolation is substitution of one habitat for another rather than removal"* and *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Absence of a citation is not a claim of originality; Fig 8 :39 says *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 4. Metamerism: why a lab reading cannot name its geological source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a measured lab yield is a reading, not a source. Several geological histories (different mineralogies, cycling regimes, concentration mechanisms, durations) could produce the same yield. Naming one as "the reason it scales" is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name. Fig 6 :33-34: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

This is why F7 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a declared gap. The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: something that discriminates among settings giving the same reading. It is not named.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated "shape, not a name" in an earlier reading as a ban on naming any condition at all. The canonical README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Mineral surfaces, pH, concentration, temperature and cycling are all already in the geochemistry library; naming them is a **binding** from the geology frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derives which specific mineral, which cycling regime or which duration governs this result, or that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this binding adds none).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The lab reading still cannot name its geological source without a discriminator. The reason is the missing instrument, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, "Prestige Is Not Action": *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous laboratories that agree with a scaling claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.

Same essay, on simulation: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And, in the architecture article: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Consequence for this question

The honest deliverable is not "the protocol that scales." It is: the shape any scaled result must have (a method, an enumerated habitat, a measured `R_T`, a ground that decides), the four refuters written in advance, and the name of the missing instrument. The specific mineral, cycling and duration stay open until a field measurement settles them, or until a provenanced import from geology passes a shadow test. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README), so a label that fits the lab reading is not the source of that reading.
