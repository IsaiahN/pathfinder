## QUESTION

How can laboratory results be scaled from tiny, carefully controlled systems to a geologically realistic environment? Restated as an effect: a result is settled in a controlled microcosm; the question asks what would have to be true for it to still hold in a geological habitat. Option A treats the result as a property of the chemistry and scales it by volume or time. Option B treats it as a property of agent and habitat, so the move is a habitat substitution across a lossy membrane, with a measured round-trip gap and a ground that decides. A sends a recording to be replayed at larger scale and a claim no ground has settled. B sends a method, a stated gap, four refuters, and an honest stop at the missing instrument.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, and status.

- **R (residual).** Arity: a two-frame relation (lab frame, geology frame) joined by a scale transform, indexed per condition (pH, concentration, temperature, mineral, cycling, time), never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: asymmetric, since methods cross up and head starts come down (Fig 4 :22), and introduction is available but exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). Scale: the governing axis, one agent's loop versus a population-and-deep-time regime across a membrane (Fig 4). Union, not partition: reproduce conditions, enumerate introduced actors, survive time, be checkable at the ground (Fig 9 :55-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3), with the break at the objective (Fig 3 :25-27).
- **D1 Lab system (substituted habitat, test harness).** Actors: purified reagents, fixed conditions, instrument, experimenter in the maintainer seat. Currency: yield under fixed conditions, reproducibility. Anchor: the lab measurement, which pays or does not. Admitted.
- **D2 Geological habitat (field of contact).** Actors: minerals, wet-dry cycling, hydrothermal flux, dilution, dissolved organic matter, time, contaminants. Currency: persistence of a pathway under real contact. Anchor: the rock record, field measurement, geochemical signature. Admitted.
- **D3 Scale membrane (transform).** Actors: method carried up, head start sent down, recording, `R_T`. Currency: methods up, head starts down, gap measured. Anchor: the round trip (Fig 4 :30). Admitted.
- **D4 Verification / ground-maintainer frame.** Actors: independent instruments, natural analogues, field studies, other frames. Currency: ground contact, not agreement. Anchor: the anchor, which does not update (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **X1 "The result itself scales" as a free-floating property.** Actors: the result. Currency: truth. Anchor: none nameable; it depends on the habitat. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "Just run a bigger version."** Actors: volume, throughput. Currency: scale. Anchor: already inside D3, because scale changes the actors (Fig 4 :19-20). Absorbed.

Strain test applied: Fig 4 (kernel invariant across size) and Fig 11 (habitat must be reproduced) initially strained. Re-census resolves it: Fig 4 governs the transform and what may cross; Fig 11 governs the source and target habitats and what is in contact with the residual. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the magnification picture does not. A lab result is a wiring claim under a substituted habitat. Scaling is a habitat substitution across a lossy membrane: reproduce what the result required, enumerate what the field introduces including second-order contact, carry the method rather than the recording, measure `R_T`, unbundle the four conditions, and let a ground that does not update decide. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so no mineral, cycling regime or duration is asserted, and no general field-equivalence instrument is claimed. Two gaps are declared (F4 unmeasured `R_T`, F7 missing instrument) and four refuters are pre-registered. Mode `specified` on the application, `general` on the structure, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, gaps and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is asking a field-facing checker to engage with a recording instead of a method, which spends their independence (Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was struck out (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3). The shape, gaps and refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** habitat invariance, a replayed recording paying at the ground, a measured `R_T = 0`, or a single lever clearing all four conditions with no categorically different residue. A source-discriminating field instrument would close the F7 gap; it would not refute the shape. Agreement among labs is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :35).
