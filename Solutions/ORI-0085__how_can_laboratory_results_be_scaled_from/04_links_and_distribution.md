## Two things in the literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **Cleaves (2013) is a method, not a verdict.** Its proposal to test each reaction to failure across a small screening matrix is the exportable "method crosses the membrane" item (Fig 4 :22-23). It is a low-overlap corroboration of the figure's rule, from geochemistry rather than from the framework.
2. **Westall et al. (2023) supplies second-order contact.** The conditioning film that coats a mineral surface within minutes is exactly the kind of actor that is absent from a clean lab vessel and that a first-order enumeration misses (Fig 11 :52-53). This is corroboration, not a figure-derived name for the missing thing.
