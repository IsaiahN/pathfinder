## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28** - "any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the framework.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people already sympathetic to the framework produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat Map :163** - "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) the question, what would they count as a failure, and at which scale and species slot; (3) only after their verdict is recorded, the figures link, with the note that the figures and not this summary are the authority; (4) the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, output before theory.** Send the separator, its mode and its pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not an endorsement. Record the verdict before any framework material is shown (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the three framework links. Their stage-1 verdict stays on record and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.
3. **Stage 3, a method not an answer.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away a result (Fig 4 :22). Each user must name their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because THE_FORMULA.md, Direction, says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 separator survives. Do not present the verdict as a universal fragmentation percentage; do not average alpha loss into gamma gain; do not cite the two opposed reviews as two independent witnesses. If any of those happen, the packet fails on contact.

## 6. Legal and venue cautions

Public institutional and opt-in channels are used throughout `03_public_contact_points.csv`. No personal emails of named individuals, no scraped lists, no bulk send, no receptiveness ranking. ESA ECOLOG-L and the SCB listserves are opt-in with user-chosen delivery. Journal and congress routes are public submission systems. Where a page was not fetched cleanly (IALE, Biological Conservation author guide), that is stated in the CSV notes rather than hidden. An unsolicited message to a public institutional mailbox should carry an opt-out line and identify itself plainly; GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules apply to unsolicited outreach in many jurisdictions.
