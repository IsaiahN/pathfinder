## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the split of continuous habitat into smaller and more isolated patches. Option A: treat "isolate vulnerable species" and "create opportunities for diversity" as opposite answers to one dial and pick a winner or a percentage. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping conditions and return a separator that is indexed per species slot and per scale, with a refuter and an owed instrument. What each leads to: A averages a local persistence loss with a landscape richness gain and produces a `general`-mode verdict the figures forbid; B produces a checkable shape, keeps verdicts separate, and leaves the number open until the ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A habitat configuration change followed by two readings: a population loses goal-required contact, or another slot gains richness, turnover or edge habitat. Arity: two overlapping conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"). Symmetry: valence is assigned by the residual, not carried by the fragment. Scale: patch, local landscape and region, with alpha, beta and gamma as different slots.
- **Domain 1, the substituted habitat.** Actors: the species and everything in contact with it, food, mates, predators, competitors, microclimate, matrix, edges, corridors, disturbance. Currency: contact. Anchor: measured persistence and extinction in the world. Union with D2 and D3; isolation is substitution, not subtraction.
- **Domain 2, the species slot.** Actors: one population holding a goal. Currency: the gap between required and supplied contact. Anchor: that population's persistence at the ground. Partition across slots; never aggregated.
- **Domain 3, the diversity opportunity.** Actors: other slots, heterogeneity, edges, matrix, and a maintaining seat where one exists. Currency: new settled terms and surplus from closures that differ. Anchor: measured richness or turnover at a declared scale. Union surplus; unmaintained and maintained modes are different loops.
- **Domain 4, the selection frame.** Actors: analyst or manager choosing slot and scale. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. The sought-for shape is a preference held as a term and is made refusable by writing it down before the run.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate contact, index the slot's goal, compare supplied with required contact, decide substitution failure, then evaluate the surplus at another slot or scale. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :28 "any frame you pick will seem to fit").

Strain test applied: per-slot residual versus alpha/beta/gamma is not a contradiction but two sizes with a directional filter; Fig 11 asymmetry versus real invasive removal resolves by re-census (removing an actor is an introduction of a different actor, not a subtraction of contact). Strains are named in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound. Fragmentation isolates a species when the substituted habitat fails to reproduce the contact its persistence required, with two silent failure modes; it opens opportunity where the new configuration introduces contact that a different residual can settle into at another scale. "Vulnerable" is residual-relative and indexed per slot, so no standing list and no averaged verdict survive. In the unmaintained mode what survives is what the ground kept; in the maintained mode introduction is available and exclusion is not. The "when" is a comparison against a measured ground, and no figure line returns a universal number. The number is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable in the shape, expensive in the field. If a landscape diversity gain is reported as compensation for a local persistence loss, the isolated population's only operative quantity, contact with what its goal required, is left untouched while the report looks successful. Fig 11 :40 states the failure: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" sets the check budget. The cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter and by measuring at the right slot and scale.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdicts are per slot and reversible in principle. A corridor can be introduced, a management regime can be changed, and a refused claim stays in the record while losing its standing to be composed over. What cannot be undone is contact once a population is gone, which is why the silent failure modes matter and why the ground, not agreement, decides.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A replicated manipulation at a declared scale that separates habitat amount, configuration and matrix quality with an independent frame reading the same ground could return the number the figures withhold. A measured preservation of required contact across substitution would break Fig 11 :66. An invariant vulnerable-species list would break Fig 11 :51. A demonstrated restoration of the same population's own residual by a landscape gain would break the union reading. A derivation from the figures that respects Fig 13 :135 and still returns a universal threshold would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
