## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity, symmetry, scale).** The residual is the opposed-reading event after a habitat configuration change: a population loses goal-required contact, or another slot gains richness or turnover. Arity: two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound**, not a two-way partition (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: valence is assigned by the residual, not carried by the fragment, and the seat's power is asymmetric (Fig 11 :49, :61). Scale: patch, local landscape, region, with alpha, beta and gamma as different slots.
- **Domain 1, substituted habitat.** Actors: species plus everything in contact with it, matrix, edge, microclimate, disturbance. Currency: contact. Anchor: measured persistence and extinction. Union with D2 and D3.
- **Domain 2, species slot.** Actors: one population with a goal. Currency: gap between required and supplied contact. Anchor: that population's persistence. Partition across slots.
- **Domain 3, diversity opportunity.** Actors: other slots, heterogeneity, edges, a maintaining seat where one exists. Currency: settled terms and surplus from differing closures. Anchor: richness or turnover at a declared scale. Union surplus; two modes.
- **Domain 4, selection frame.** Actors: analyst or manager. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground. The sought-for shape is a preference that must be bound and refused.

Order by dependency: enumerate contact, index the slot's goal, compare supplied with required contact, decide substitution failure, then evaluate the surplus. This order is taken from the habitat paragraph of THE_FORMULA.md and Fig 3 :20, not from the primary's ordering of findings. I then read the primary's census and compared.

My census matches on all four domains and the union reading. I derived the union from Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 2 :39-40, where the primary derived it from Fig 11 isolation law and the habitat paragraph of the formula. Different starting lines, same boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, but still one evidence pool. The agreement is expected and carries no independent weight.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured, not assumed. I ran the round trip on the shorthand this folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**, which the concrete reading replaces with a single landscape verdict;
- the **union structure**, which the concrete reading treats as cancellation rather than two conditions whose intersection still binds;
- the **silence of the failure modes**, which the concrete reading turns into an observable immediate decline;
- the **asymmetry of introduction and exclusion**, which the concrete reading converts into a claim that contact was subtracted.

One thing survives cleanly: the configuration is a substitution, and a species is isolated when its goal-required contact was not reproduced in it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
