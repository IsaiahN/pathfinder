## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECO-0052 a **shape** is the separator that any answer must satisfy before it can be tested: fragmentation is a habitat substitution; valence is relative to a residual; the residual is indexed per species slot; opportunity is a union surplus at a different slot or scale; the two habitat modes have asymmetric power; contact cascades; and no universal number is returned. Those are constraints. They do not say which reserve, which species or which corridor width.

A concrete contrast from this question:

- Shape: "fragmentation isolates a species when the substituted habitat fails to reproduce the contact its persistence required."
- Name or number: "reserves below 100 ha isolate specialist butterflies" or "20% edge habitat maximises gamma diversity."

The first is checkable in principle by anyone with a population record. The second imports a threshold from one dataset and presents it as a law.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms". "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the starting vocabulary was the source terms (fragmentation, isolation, diversity, edge, corridor, matrix) and the recurring new terms were substitution, residual-relative valence, union, slot and introduction-asymmetry.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The outreach email in `02` is written this way: it says what a failure would look like, not what to believe.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here that means the separator returns to the question it started from: when the goal-required contact is reproduced, it is not isolation; when it is not, it is; and the diversity question is a different slot.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md, step 5: "SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that means a replicated manipulation with a measured population outcome and a measured richness outcome at a declared scale.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. A threshold borrowed from a meta-analysis is an import. It must carry its provenance (which species, which biome, which scale, which inclusion rule) and pass a shadow test (does it explain a gap in our own residual?). Otherwise it is a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried quote, not re-opened): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A universal 40% habitat-cover rule is a number from a space whose dimensions (taxon, matrix quality, scale) differ from a new landscape; importing it without those dimensions is the failure the essay names.

## 4. Source discrimination: why "which species is vulnerable" needs an instrument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; green might be one light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

The fragmentation analogue: a low population count after fragmentation is a reading. Its source could be failed reproduction of required contact, an introduced predator at the edge, a disease, a matrix effect three steps out, or a time-lagged extinction debt. The count cannot comment on the source. Haddad et al. 2015 report that losses grow over two decades, so a single early count is a projection with a large loss. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error; what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a confident attribution. That is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks an ecologist to name the discriminating measurement, not to endorse the separator.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This derivation cannot certify its own answer.

## 5. Q answered: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an edge, a corridor or a heterogeneity mechanism?

**No.** See the correction in section 6. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Edge, corridor, matrix, heterogeneity and contact are already in the ecology frame's vocabulary and map onto held framework atoms (space and network, with gradient and spectrum as the structures that decide what a distance or an ordering means). Naming them is a **binding** from the ecology frame onto held atoms, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a seventh primitive and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world."). What remains forbidden is presenting a binding as a derivation, or presenting a threshold from one landscape as a universal number.

## 6. Correction to my own section 1 and to the first draft of audit finding F6

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name an edge or a corridor" and that F6 followed from a ban on naming. That overstated the rule and is retracted here, visibly, rather than erased.

What the README actually says is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive absent from the library. Edge and corridor are not missing; they are bindings onto space and network, which the six-term set already holds. The correct consequence for ECO-0052 is narrower: the framework cannot derive *which* binding occupies the slot or *what number* it has, but it can constrain the shape that any binding must have (substitution, per-slot valence, union surplus, asymmetry, cascade). The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: a population count still does not determine its source, and the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well-cited reviewers to agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a field measurement or an experiment, settle it. In particular, the standing of Fahrig or Haddad is not a witness weight; the evidence is their measurements, read at the right scale.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** ground readings and cannot settle which species in a real landscape is isolated.

## 8. Shape applied to the question, in one line

The separator is a shape (a relation among substitution, residual and scale), not a name (a reserve, a species) and not a number (a patch size, an edge distance, a habitat fraction). A ground reading at a declared slot and scale is owed before any of it is called settled. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README).
