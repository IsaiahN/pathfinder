**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist or landscape researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One to one, not bulk.

**Mode: `specified` on the boundary claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal number. Not `grounded`.** No field measurement is claimed. This is a separator and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Fragmentation is a habitat substitution, not a subtraction. It isolates a species when the substituted habitat fails to reproduce the contact that species' persistence required; the failure is silent until the population declines, and the introduced actors are silent until they act. "Vulnerable" is a relation to that residual, so verdicts are indexed per species and per scale and no standing list is licensed. Opportunity is a union surplus where new contact and heterogeneity are usable by a different residual at another scale, so a local alpha loss and a landscape gamma gain can co-occur without either compensating for the other. Where no seat maintains the habitat, what survives is what the ground kept; where a seat exists, introduction is available and exclusion is not. The word "when" is a comparison against a measured ground, and no figure line supplies a universal number for it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** isolation is substitution of one habitat for another, and the two failure modes (unreproduced goal contact, unchosen introduced actors) are silent until the goal fails or the actor acts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Also Fig 11 :67 "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose." And Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"; Fig 11 :69 "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts".
- **Refuter:** a measured case where goal-required contact is preserved unchanged across a substitution, the mechanism is observed, and the population persists. Also a case where an introduced actor acts with no effect on the residual and the lack of effect is measured, not assumed.

### C2 - ecology-facing, checkable against fragmentation studies

- **Statement:** the sign of a fragmentation effect is residual-relative and scale dependent, so an averaged "fragmentation is good or bad" verdict is the wrong object. Alpha loss at the patch and gamma gain at the landscape are different slots, not a contradiction or a compensation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." For the scale split, Fig 4 :21 "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."
- **What to check:** in the studies you know, does the sign flip with taxon, traits, habitat amount or scale? Perrin et al. 2025 report "habitat fragmentation negatively affects α-diversity at the patch scale, it can enhance overall β- and γ-diversity at the landscape scale". Rybicki et al. 2020 report "When the total amount of habitat is large, fragmentation per se tends to increase species diversity, but if the total amount of habitat is small, the situation is reversed". Are those two results different slots, or is one of them an artifact of a different definition of fragmentation?
- **Refuter:** a standing list of vulnerable species whose membership is invariant across residuals and scales, measured; or a ground reading in which a landscape gain demonstrably restores the same population's local residual; or a single-scale experiment in which isolation and opportunity co-occur for the same residual with no union explanation and the effect does not shrink when amount and scale are separated.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation, with a type caveat named

- **Statement:** the "when" is a comparison (⋛) between goal-required contact and supplied contact at a declared scale, not a symmetric conjunction of fragmentation and diversity. The maintained and unmaintained modes have asymmetric power: introduction is available, exclusion is not. No universal threshold is returned.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :48 "a threshold relation between two quantities". Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".
- **Refuter:** a derivation from the figures that returns a universal patch-size, edge-distance or habitat-fraction number while respecting Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"; or a demonstrated maintained-seat operation that subtracts contact rather than adding an actor, with the subtraction measured rather than renamed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal patch size, edge distance, corridor width or habitat fraction. The number is a declared `gap`, not silence and not an invitation to invent a threshold from one landscape.
- Not a standing list of vulnerable species. A list bound to a stated residual and scale is a candidate with provenance, not a law.
- Not that a gamma gain compensates for an alpha loss. They have different anchors and are never averaged.
- Not that Fahrig 2017 and Haddad et al. 2015 are two independent witnesses. They largely draw on the same studies; that is one evidence pool with an internal scale and measure dispute (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- Not a conclusion about any named reserve, species or corridor. No field data were taken.
- Not `grounded`. All claims are `specified` or `general`, and the whole packet is a simulated derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at a declared slot and scale, not another review. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does the substituted habitat reproduce the goal-required contact, and which introduced actor acts? Measured, not inferred from a population count.
2. Does the same species keep the same valence across residuals and scales? Measured across at least two residuals.
3. Does a landscape-scale richness gain restore the same population's own residual? A causal path must be shown, not assumed from co-occurrence.
4. A replicated manipulation that separates habitat amount, configuration and matrix quality at one scale, read by a frame that did not author the separator. This is the only route to a number.
