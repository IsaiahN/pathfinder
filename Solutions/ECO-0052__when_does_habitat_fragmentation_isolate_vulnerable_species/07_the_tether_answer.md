# The Tether's answer: when fragmentation isolates, when it opens opportunity

**Mode: `specified` on the boundary claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on any universal number. Not `grounded`.** This is a separator and a route, not a field result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The split of continuous habitat into smaller, more isolated patches is followed by two opposed readings: a population loses the contact its persistence required, or a landscape gains richness, turnover or edge habitat. The question asks for the boundary condition. It is a union compound, not one dial with a right setting (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). One lever will clear the region where only one condition holds and leave the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56).

## 2. The shape

1. **Fragmentation substitutes a habitat; it does not remove one.** Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." A patch brings edge, matrix, altered microclimate and inherited actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :67). The two failures are silent: what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails, and what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts (Fig 11 :68, :69).
2. **"Vulnerable" is a relation to a residual, not a property of a species.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Verdicts are indexed per species slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). A global richness near zero is compatible with one live population residual (Fig 1 :13).
3. **Opportunity is a union surplus at a different slot or scale, not compensation paid to the isolated population.** Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :21: "nothing was aimed; the union is mute." The surplus exists only where closures differ (new edge, new matrix, a maintained corridor) and a residual has been described. A landscape gamma gain and a local alpha loss can both be true at once; the gain does not repair the loss.
4. **The two habitat modes differ in who acts and what is available.** Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Where a seat exists, arrival is worked on; where none exists, what survives is what the ground kept. Managed habitat is permanent work, not a solved state (Fig 11 :63).
5. **Contact cascades, so first-order scoping is how the residual is lost.** Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44). What cannot yet be perceived is residual, not absence.
6. **The "when" is a comparison against a ground, not a symmetric choice.** Operators table :48: "a threshold relation between two quantities"; :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one." The operands are required contact and supplied contact at a declared scale. No figure returns the number at which the threshold fires. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal patch size, edge distance, corridor width or habitat fraction. That is a declared `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name and unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not silence (Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").
- Not a standing list of vulnerable species. Fig 11 :51 refuses it as a law. A list bound to a stated residual and scale is a provenanced candidate, not a finding.
- Not that a landscape-scale diversity gain compensates for a local extinction. They are different slots with different anchors and are never averaged.
- Not that the two opposed reviews corroborate one another. They draw on the same evidence pool; their disagreement is a fact about scale and measure (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the framework can name which actor in a real landscape caused a decline. A population count cannot comment on its source; a source-discriminating instrument is owed.
- Not `grounded`. No field measurement, experiment or population count was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument at a declared slot and scale, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): a color instrument "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

- **Substitution test:** measure whether the goal-required contact was reproduced in the substituted habitat, and whether introduced actors acted. A measured preservation of required contact across substitution with persistence would refute finding F1.
- **Standing-list test:** measure whether the same species keeps the same valence across residuals and scales. Invariance would refute F2.
- **Compensation test:** measure whether a landscape richness gain restores the same population's per-slot residual. A causal path would refute F3.
- **Threshold test:** a replicated manipulation at one declared scale that separates habitat amount, configuration and matrix quality, with an independent frame reading the same ground. It could return the number the figures withhold.
- **Standing refuters:** the seven conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6, written before the conclusion and repeated in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The boundary number is out of reach with the library as it stands; an instrument is owed. "A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39). The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). If a source-discriminating instrument arrives, this verdict is revised; until then it is `candidate`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Fragmentation does not take habitat away; it substitutes a new one, with new edge, matrix and actors, and both outcomes the question asks about follow from that substitution. It isolates a species when the new habitat fails to reproduce the contact that species' persistence required, and those failures are silent until the population declines. It opens opportunity when the new configuration introduces contact that a different species can settle into, usually at a different scale, so a local loss and a landscape gain can both be true at once. "Vulnerable" is therefore a relation to a residual, not a property a species carries, and there is no standing list and no universal patch size. What the framework offers is the separator, the scale at which each verdict must be read, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the remaining number. The threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
