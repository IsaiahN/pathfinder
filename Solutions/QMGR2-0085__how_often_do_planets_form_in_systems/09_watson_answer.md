## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the number does not exist in this derivation. The answer is a per-system distribution, not an average; a union of three conditions, not one question; a survivorship-corrected statistic, because we see the survivors; a threshold declared before the cut; and a result from detection frames with low overlap. The mechanism is chaos in a gravitational network, so same ingredients in a different arrangement can give different outcomes. The numerical frequency is `gap`, owed by a source-discriminating instrument. This is `specified` where the figures are applied and `general` where they govern structure. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, six refuters and the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's twenty minutes on a shape that was never right, or presenting a bare percentage that hides survivor bias and per-slot structure. Fig 2 :42 prices the safeguard: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the shape fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A figure line yielding the number; an instrument showing no dynamical contribution to the age decline; a survey showing born and detected samples are indistinguishable in instability history; a threshold-invariant fraction combined with a declaration that the threshold was chosen after the cut; or a demonstration that per-slot indexing is inapplicable to population rates. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
