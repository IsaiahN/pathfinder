## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 gives the figure form: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says the system "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands", and :135 that "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (2026): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For QMGR2-0085 the shape is therefore not a frequency. It is the set of constraints any frequency answer must satisfy: per-system indexing, union of conditions, declared instability threshold, survivorship correction, and low-overlap detection frames. Concretely, `SYSTEMS(???)` rather than `f = 0.4`. The `???` is the number, and it is owed by instruments.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the thing and *"find these new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"*, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Here that means describing the residual as a count over system slots and a threshold on timescales, not as "instability" alone.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So we do not begin by naming planet-planet scattering; we begin with "a fraction of systems changes architecture after formation" and let the frame be found.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as recorded in the canonical exemplar): when contact is not possible, the best sent item is a plain description of what you would expect to see, because anyone can check it against the world. For this question the expected sight is a decline in multiplicity with age at fixed host fraction, which is exactly what Yang et al. (2023) report.

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the number is not one

Only two things: the ground settling a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). Frequency numbers come from surveys, so they are ground readings made by a different frame. The figures cannot produce them, and pretending otherwise is the Flat Formula error: naming the ingredients and forgetting the bond.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (as recorded in the canonical exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." A survey result imported into this derivation adds a dimension (time and detection completeness) that the figures' six terms do not carry. It must be recorded as such, with a source gate and a shadow test.

## 4. The instrument question, and the survivor question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026) gives the decisive image. An instrument that detects green "cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes create identical readings, so "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists", and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: observing 1.8 planets per old star is a reading. The sources could be formation-only (fewer planets born), dynamical instability (planets removed later), or detection bias (the same planets no longer seen), and a mixture of all three. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the source. This is the metamerism point, and it is why F3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a `specified` prediction about the necessity of a survivorship correction, not a claim about the value of the correction.

## 5. Selection ran

*Arrival of the Fittest* and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* both make the survivor point in the framework's own words. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :15: "nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept."

The detected planetary population is a lineage that met the detection ground. It is not the born population. Any "how often" measured from it is a post-selection statistic. The survivor shape is therefore not a caveat added by this author; it is the figure's account of where actors come from.

## 6. Standing of this document, repeated honestly

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* (2026) records the same limitation: because the library behind the simulation is a black box, "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra", and they "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `05`, `01`, `06` and `07` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests. They cannot settle the frequency they report on.

## 7. What the shape is, in one line

**`SYSTEMS(s) -> {formation at t0, instability threshold at t1, survival into the detected sample}, reported per slot with an independently anchored detection frame and a declared threshold, with the number left as `???`.** That is the shape. The number is a ground reading owed by survey and simulation, not by this framework.
