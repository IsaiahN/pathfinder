# The Tether's answer: how often do planets form in systems that later become unstable?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on two predictions, `gap` on the number. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a frequency over systems ordered in time: planets form at t0, some systems become dynamically unstable at t1 > t0. The figures do not contain that frequency. What they contain are the constraints on what a correct frequency report must look like, and the reasons the raw observed count is not the quantity asked for. The number is `gap`, not silence (Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

## 2. The shape

1. **Index per system, never average away the live slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The answer is a distribution of instability times over system slots, from which a rate can be derived, not a bare percentage.
2. **It is a union of conditions, not one hard question.** Formation, later instability, and survival into the detected sample are three conditions with their own anchors (Fig 9 :54-57). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
3. **The detected population is survivor-shaped.** Fig 11 :15 "nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Any observed rate is post-selection and must be corrected. Mode `specified` for the prediction that the correction is necessary; its size is a ground question.
4. **"Unstable" is a comparison threshold that must be declared before the cut.** Fig 12 :87 "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes." The Operators table calls comparison "a threshold relation between two quantities." An undeclared threshold is a convention that decides the outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids.
5. **The mechanism is chaos in a network, not clockwork.** Fig 13 :32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise." A planetary system is a network over states; influence travels. Marzari (2024) finds that a significant fraction of systems on the verge of instability underwent instabilities in the past, and Yang et al. (2023) find multiplicity declining with age at nearly constant host fraction. Mode `specified` for the application; `general` for the figure's mechanism claim.
6. **Detection frames must be independently anchored.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Transit, radial velocity, microlensing and astrometry are useful together only if they do not share a completeness model (Fig 2 :40).
7. **The number is owed by an instrument, not an argument.** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The needed instrument is a source discriminator that separates formation-only, dynamical-instability and detection-bias explanations of the same multiplicity reading. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a frequency value. No fraction, percentage or rate is asserted.
- Not "every system becomes unstable." Chaos is a property of an amplifying chain, not a universal timescale, and the mathematical proof reported by Cepelewicz (2023) is for a specific three-planet model with changes possibly acting over hundreds of millions of years.
- Not that the survivorship correction has a known size. Only that it is owed.
- Not that the figures settle the astronomy. They govern the shape; the ground settles the number.
- Not grounded. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green reading "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the equivalent tests are:

- **Source test:** at fixed host fraction, does the age-dependent multiplicity decline disappear when detection completeness is held fixed and ejections are counted directly? If yes, formation-only wins; if no, instability contributes.
- **Threshold test:** does the reported fraction stay stable when the declared instability threshold is moved over the survey baseline range? If it flips, the convention was authored after the cut.
- **Independence test:** do transit and radial velocity samples share a target list and completeness model? If they do, their agreement is one witness, not two.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a figure line yielding the number directly; a survey showing the detected sample is born-indistinguishable; an instrument showing no dynamical contribution to age decline; a demonstration that per-slot indexing is inapplicable to population rates.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set is a generator of shape, not a survey. It stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The observed multiplicity reading does not tell us which source produced it, and differences that matter here (formation, instability, detection) are indiscernible at the current resolution of a single snapshot. A better instrument would change the answer; another argument would not.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how often planets form in systems that later become unstable. It says the number is not in its library and is owed by instruments. What it can give is the shape any answer must have: a per-system distribution rather than a global average, because averaging hides the live signal; a union of three conditions, formation, later instability and survival into the detected sample, rather than one question; a survivorship correction, because we observe the systems that survived; an instability threshold declared before the cut, because otherwise the convention decides the result; and detection frames that do not share a completeness model, because agreement from one evidence pool is one witness. The mechanism is chaos in a gravitational network, so same ingredients in a different arrangement can give different outcomes. The figures return a shape, not a name and not a frequency. The number stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles which of formation, instability and detection produced the observed decline.
