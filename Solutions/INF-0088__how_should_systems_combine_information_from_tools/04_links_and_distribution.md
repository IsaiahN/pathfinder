## What bears on this question

1. **The field already has the version of this question that the figures would ask first.** Castanedo's JDL sources are sensors, a priori information, databases and human inputs. That is tools, learned parameters and users. The engineering literature therefore corroborates the domain census at Fig 2 :31-32: these are different frames, and "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."

2. **Causal inference comes before fusion in an independent literature.** Kayser and Shams and Seilheimer et al. both report that the system first decides whether the signals share a source, then integrates or segregates. Roach et al. show that mandatory averaging fails as discrepancy grows. This is low-overlap corroboration of Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

3. **Correlation is priced in three independent fields.** Faes et al. give effective sample size, the forecasting literature gives correlated-forecaster degradation, and Castanedo gives data association. All three corroborate Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Agreement from a shared pool is one witness, not two.

4. **One apparent conflict, recorded and resolved by re-census.** Ungar et al. report that uniform averaging of forecasts is extremely hard to beat. Read alone this looks like a refutation of F1. Re-census dissolves it: those forecasts are scored by an outcome ground and the forecasters are selected for independence, so the average is a convention whose weights the ground later prices (Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"). It does not license treating agreement as a verdict about the world when frames share evidence. The verdict did not flip; the scope was narrowed.

5. **The numeric weights remain a gap.** None of the world sources is a figure line, and the figures do not hand down a coefficient. Importing a formula from any of them would need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6). That is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` reports a shape and not a number.
