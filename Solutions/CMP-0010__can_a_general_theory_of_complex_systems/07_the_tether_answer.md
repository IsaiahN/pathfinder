# The Tether's answer: can a general theory of complex systems predict across domains without erasing differences?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the application to complex systems theory, `gap` on the factual claim of universal success. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A candidate general theory predicts in a domain it was not built in. The question asks whether the residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| can stay small there without the theory flattening what makes that domain different. R is indexed per domain-slot, never aggregated: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The apparent tension is a union of two conditions, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Both conditions must hold and they overlap; a single lever leaves the intersection standing.

## 2. The verdict

**Yes in shape, conditional in substance, ungrounded in fact.**

1. **What is general is the kernel; what is particular is the cast.** Fig 4 :19-20 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 10 :68 "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it." So "without erasing differences" is not a cost paid for generality. Differences live in the actors, currency and anchor, and are precisely what a correct general theory leaves where they are.
2. **Testability is local.** Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :46 "The anchor is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote. Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it." A prediction is testable in a domain only if that domain has an anchor that can refuse it.
3. **The failure mode is replay, not transfer.** Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." A general theory that spreads by replaying one domain's recorded success does not predict in the next domain; it erases it.
4. **Erasure is measurable, not promised.** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." The round-trip gap R_T is the instrument for the "without erasing" clause.
5. **Structure transfers; semantics do not.** Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The target domain's meaning stays in the target. Marks, Gerrits and Marx 2019 find the same thing empirically: syntax transfers, semantics require modification.
6. **Failure in a domain owes an import, not more internal search.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
7. **No final theory.** Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room" and Fig 13 :90 "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route." A general theory that ends inquiry is excluded.
8. **The framework cannot witness for its own possibility.** Fig 8 :34 "A frame cannot reach its own metaframe." *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator." The Tether is a candidate instance of the class the question asks about, so its agreement with the shape is internal consistency, not corroboration.
9. **The factual universal-success claim is `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." No figure line says whether some actual theory fills the shape in every domain. That is a ground question about particular theories.

Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. D1 (kernel) admits the kernel/cast split, D2 and D3 (source and target domains) each require their own anchor and their own residual report, and D4 (verification) requires low-overlap frames ranging against those anchors. The union of these is the answer; no single domain's verdict stands in for the whole.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any existing framework, including this one, has succeeded in every domain. That is the declared `gap`.
- Not that domain differences are unimportant or reducible. They are carried by the cast and are what the ground checks.
- Not that a general theory can replace domain-specific instruments. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- Not that cross-domain agreement is evidence. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not a theory of everything. No last room, no final frame.
- Not `grounded`. No domain-by-domain residual table or measured round-trip gap was produced in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Per-domain residual table.** Publish R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| indexed by domain-slot for a candidate theory in at least three low-overlap domains (Fig 1 :19). A single averaged score is a filter, not a witness: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
2. **Independent second frames.** In each domain, a reader whose evidence pool does not overlap the theory authors', ranging against that domain's anchor separately (Fig 2 :31, :45; Fig 10 :80).
3. **Measured round trip.** Report R_T for each kernel transfer, as the list of domain-specific readings the shared kernel could not hold (Fig 4 :30-32).
4. **Provenance record.** Separate convergent derivation from adopted import, because Fig 8 :51-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4.** Any of the first nine firing conditions refutes the corresponding finding; the tenth narrows the gap.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is stated and the instruments are named. What is not available in this derivation is a candidate theory's actual per-domain record, an independent second frame in each domain, and a measured R_T. The essay on tacit knowledge says "the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" is the correct report where a projection cannot discriminate sources. The stop is recorded here so it is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A general theory of complex systems can make testable predictions across domains without erasing domain differences, but only on a specific condition: what transfers is the method, not the source domain's cast. The loop and the rule for naming what worked are shared; the actors, the currency and the anchor belong to each domain, and each domain's ground must be able to refuse the prediction. If a theory carries one domain's recorded success into another instead of a re-applicable method, it replays rather than predicts, and "agreement across domains" is a chain of mirrors. What was given up in any transfer is measured by the round-trip gap, reported per domain and never averaged. The figures also forbid a final theory and forbid this framework from certifying its own possibility: it is a candidate instance, not a neutral witness. The result is a shape, a set of refuters written in advance and a list of instruments that would close it. Whether any actual theory fills the shape in all domains is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

## 7. Pre-registered refuter (repeated from `01` section 4)

The shape claim is refuted if: (1) a source cast transferred as kernel still gets an accepted target ground reading; (2) user agreement suffices for a test where the ground cannot refuse; (3) replaying a recorded success is independently confirmed in the target with no re-specification; (4) a transfer's round trip is shown to have zero gap; (5) an averaged score detects the failing domain without a per-slot instrument; (6) reach is extended by pure internal composition with no new atom; (7) source semantics carried unchanged are confirmed by the target's ground; (8) the framework certifies its own universal applicability; (9) a terminal general theory is proved complete from inside itself. The gap closes, rather than the shape failing, if (10) a published theory supplies per-slot residuals and measured R_T in three low-overlap domains with separate grounds.
