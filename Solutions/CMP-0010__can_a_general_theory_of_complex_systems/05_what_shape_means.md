## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of the answer that can be stated before any particular general theory is named. It is two conditions in a union (a kernel that crosses, a per-domain ground that refuses) plus a measuring instrument (the round trip). A concrete theory that claims to satisfy them, for example a specific renormalisation-group or network-science framework, is a name, not a shape.

The distinction matters here because the question itself asks after a *general* theory, and the obvious move is to name one. The figures refuse that move. Naming a candidate is a binding with provenance; mistaking the binding for the derivation is the error F7 in `01` tracks.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on distance from detail: "Keeping a respectable distance from the details allows you to see the connections and their meaning, without losing fidelity and perspective." This is the precise warrant for a cross-domain theory: it keeps structure and lets the local detail stay local.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach artifacts describe what would refute the shape, not what the framework asserts.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 import rules). Fig 8 :51-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

For this question, a named general theory earns its name in a domain only when that domain's ground pays for a prediction and the provenance record shows whether the theory derived the prediction or imported it. A cross-domain score does not do this (Fig 1 :22).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the import point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The kernel is a visible guess. Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. A case of convergent derivation at maximum independence

The most relevant passage for this exact question comes from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, where the author describes deriving a governance structure from agent systems and then encountering Elinor Ostrom's work on polycentric governance:

"I had arrived at the same conclusions from bacteria and biology and game agents. She had arrived at them from committees and fisheries and forests. I could prove I had not copied her because my paper trail was different, and my vocabulary was different. But the structures matched. And because we came from different fields, I was able to explain things she left vague, and vice versa."

This is the framework's own instance of the thing the question asks about: the same structure reached in low-overlap domains, with separate evidence pools, so the agreement carries information. Fig 8 :50 "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

It is also a warning. The author later says "I learned that this metatheory could not validate itself. It could not be fully mapped. I accepted that." Convergence across fields corroborates a structure; it does not certify the metaframe that notices the convergence.

## 5. The source/provenance error, and a correction

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied here: a general theory that fits domain B's aggregate readings has not identified what in domain B produced them. Several different mechanisms could give the same fit. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument in B, not a name imported from A.

### 5b. Correction to my own reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any particular condition in the answer. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Naming the two held conditions C1 and C2, and the instrument M, is a binding from the framework's own vocabulary, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework names a particular *theory* that fills the shape in every domain. That is a metaframe certification and is refused by section 6.

The metamerism point is not retracted. It now states: the fit of a general theory to a domain's readings does not determine which mechanism in that domain produced them, so a source-discriminating instrument is still owed.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A general theory that gathers famous endorsements across domains has bought itself a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The legitimate ask is a refutation from a ground-visible reader in a domain that has not been shown the framework's interior.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of whether a general theory of complex systems is possible and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The self-application limit, stated once more

Because the Tether calls itself a "Recursive, Domain-agnostic, Self-consistent Metatheory", it is an instance of the class this question asks about. Using it to prove that such theories are possible would be circular. The essay is explicit: "Metatheories cannot validate themselves. This is Godel's insight, and it still holds here. You need an external validator." So the shape in this folder is offered as a constraint on any candidate, with its own refuter, and not as a proof that the candidate which produced it succeeds.
