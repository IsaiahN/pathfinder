# WATSON answer, CMP-0010

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the application to complex systems theory, `gap` on the factual claim of universal success. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can a general theory of complex systems make testable predictions across domains without erasing important domain-specific differences?

Restated as an effect: can R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| stay small in each domain while one kernel is shared? Option A, generality as a shared cast (same actors, currency and anchor everywhere), leads to predictions fitted to the source domain and failing at the target's ground, erasing differences by construction. Option B, generality as a shared kernel with a per-domain cast and a per-domain anchor, leads to testable predictions whose residual is reported per domain and whose round-trip gap is measured. R is indexed per domain-slot and never aggregated: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: one kernel related to N domains, each with its own ground, so a family of predictions indexed by domain-slot. Symmetry: ordered, the kernel applies to a domain and not conversely; the cross-domain claim is a threshold comparison (⋛), not a symmetric join. Scale: two levels with a lossy membrane, the method level and the instance level. Fig 9 :22 "arity · symmetry · scale".
- **D1 Kernel / method level.** Actors: the loop, the seven bonds and negation, the six-term substrate, the rule for naming what worked. Currency: reach and settlement. Anchor: the figures for design questions, each instance's ground for measurement. Admitted.
- **D2 Source domain.** Actors: the entities the kernel was distilled from. Currency: its predictions. Anchor: its instrument, experiment or dataset. Admitted.
- **D3 Target domain.** Actors: different entities. Currency: different predictions. Anchor: its own ground, which the general theory must not be able to move. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification / comparison frame.** Actors: expert readers in both domains. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement; frames must be at low overlap. Admitted.
- **X1 Theory with no ground.** No nameable anchor, so not a domain yet. X2 Averaged differences: the disqualified operation at Fig 1 :22, not a domain. X3 Terminal theory: excluded at Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room".

**Union, not partition.** C1 (a kernel that crosses) and C2 (a per-domain ground that refuses) overlap; one lever leaves the intersection standing. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Dependency order: describe R in effect terms, name the target anchor, state what counts as progress there, plan the transfer, then accept or refuse the term and measure the round trip (Fig 3). The middle links are where it breaks: Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

## WORLD (secondary, each source read; field, author/venue/year, quote, prediction, corroborates or conflicts)

World sources corroborate but never overrule. No conflict with the figure verdict was left standing; two apparent conflicts were returned to the census and resolved without flipping the verdict.

- **Statistical physics / renormalisation group.** H. Nishimori and G. Ortiz, *Elements of Phase Transitions and Critical Phenomena*, Oxford University Press, 2011, chapter "Phase transitions and critical phenomena" read via Oxford Academic, 2026-11-22. Quote: "an important consequence of universality is that quantities that describe the essential features of critical phenomena, typically the critical exponents, do not depend on the system details but are specified only by a few basic factors such as the symmetry of the system, range of the interactions... and its spatial dimensionality". Also: "There are, of course, many properties that are non-universal, the critical temperature itself being a relevant example that depends on the microscopic details of the model." Prediction: a shared coarse description predicts across very different systems while the non-universal residue stays in each system. **Corroborates** the kernel/cast split at maximum independence (different field and era). This is an existence proof for the shape in one branch of physics, not for every domain.
- **Condensed matter / emergence.** P. W. Anderson, "More Is Different", Science 177(4047), 1972, full text read via a university reprint, 2026-11-22. Quote: "at each level of complexity entirely new properties appear, and the understanding of the new behaviors requires research which I think is as fundamental in its nature as any other." Also: "Psychology is not applied biology, nor is biology applied chemistry." Prediction: a general theory cannot reconstruct a higher level from lower rules; each level needs its own ground and instruments. **Corroborates** Fig 10 :68 and Fig 4 :27, and **conflicts** with any naive reductionist reading of Option A; the conflict was returned to the census and resolved by restricting the claim to kernel transfer.
- **Mathematics / optimisation.** D. H. Wolpert and W. G. Macready, "No Free Lunch Theorems for Optimization", IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation 1(1), 1997, abstract and theorem statement read via the journal page and the Wikipedia entry, 2026-11-22. Quote: "if an algorithm performs well on a certain class of problems then it necessarily pays for that with degraded performance on the set of all remaining problems." Also: "any two algorithms are equivalent when their performance is averaged across all possible problems." Prediction: a fixed general method has no advantage averaged uniformly over all domains; advantage comes from problem-specific knowledge. **Conflicts** with the claim that generality alone predicts, **corroborates** Fig 1 :22 and the requirement C2 after re-census. One witness in one field.
- **Machine learning / transfer.** W. Zhang, L. Deng, L. Zhang and D. Wu, "A Survey on Negative Transfer", IEEE/CAA Journal of Automatica Sinica 10(2), 2023, abstract read via DOI page, 2026-11-22. Quote: "leveraging source domain data/knowledge undesirably reduces learning performance in the target domain." Prediction: carrying a source model into a target domain can erase local structure and make predictions worse; the remedies are domain-similarity estimation and safe transfer. **Corroborates** the import source gate and Fig 4 :25. Shares an optimisation/learning pool with Wolpert and Macready, so these two count as one broad field witness, not two independent ones.
- **Complexity science / philosophy of science.** Peter Marks, Lasse Gerrits and Johannes Marx, "How to use fitness landscape models for the analysis of collective decision-making: a case of theory-transfer and its limitations", Biology & Philosophy 34(1):7, 2019, full text read via PMC, 2026-11-22. Quote: "the more the model is tailored towards the target domain, the more exploratory and explanatory power it will have, the less it will resemble the original model." Also: "the properties of the target domain allow for a transfer of the syntactic structure but don't tolerate the semantic transfer." Prediction: structure transfers, meaning requires re-specification. **Corroborates** Fig 9 :30-31 and Fig 4 :19-20. Genuinely low overlap with physics and optimisation, so this is the strongest independent world witness for the kernel/cast split.
- **Framework essays, canonical reading only.** Isaiah N, *The Library of Babel*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, and others, adventuresinml Substack, 2026, opened 2026-11-22. Wolpert and Macready plus Zhang et al share an optimisation and learning pool and are counted as one witness. The essays share the framework author and carry no witness weight. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Yes in shape, conditional in substance, ungrounded in fact.** What is general is the kernel and the rule for naming what worked; the actors, currency and anchor belong to each domain, and a prediction is testable only where that domain's ground can refuse it. Erasure is not traded against generality: it occurs when a recording is replayed instead of a method being re-applied, and its size is measured by the round-trip gap reported per domain and never averaged. Failure in a domain owes an import from a differing closure, not more search inside the theory. A terminal general theory and a self-certifying general theory are both excluded. Whether any actual theory fills the shape in every domain is a declared `gap`, reported as a verdict rather than a silence. The verdict is the union of D1 to D4 kept separate per domain, never averaged.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the two conditions, the instrument and ten refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than hidden by an averaged score. The real cost is the erasure the question names: sending a recording of one domain's success as if it were a general method, which Fig 4 :25 says produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape, the per-domain residual requirement and the refuter.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 amendment). The shape, its refuters and its provenance are recorded, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A wrong general theory can be superseded without withdrawing the kernel/cast distinction.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A published theory with per-slot residuals and measured R_T in three low-overlap domains, each with an independent second frame and a separate anchor, would move the factual claim from `gap` toward `grounded`. Any of the first nine refuters in `01` section 4 would break the corresponding finding: a source cast that works as kernel, user agreement that suffices for a test, replay confirmed in the target, a zero round-trip gap, an averaged score that detects the failing domain, reach extended without import, source semantics confirmed unchanged, self-certification, or a terminal theory proved complete. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
