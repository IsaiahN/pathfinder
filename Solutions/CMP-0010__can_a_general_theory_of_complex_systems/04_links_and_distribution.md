## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Universality is the strongest existence proof for the shape.** Nishimori and Ortiz describe a shared coarse description (symmetry, interaction range, dimensionality) that yields identical critical exponents in a magnet and a fluid. The non-universal residue, for example the critical temperature, stays in each system. This is exactly the kernel/cast split: prediction transfers, differences are not erased. Its scope is limited to critical phenomena, so it corroborates the shape, not universal success.
2. **Anderson is the strongest limit.** "at each level of complexity entirely new properties appear." A general theory cannot reconstruct the higher level from the lower rules, so a target domain's own instruments are not optional. Anderson corroborates Fig 10 :68 and Fig 3 :27.
3. **No free lunch is the strongest refusal of Option A.** "any two algorithms are equivalent when their performance is averaged across all possible problems." A general method gains nothing by being general alone; it needs problem-specific knowledge. This is the mathematical form of the warning against averaged cross-domain scores.
4. **Negative transfer is the engineering form of the failure.** Source knowledge "undesirably reduces learning performance in the target domain." The remedies in that literature, domain-similarity estimation and safe transfer, are the practical analogue of the source gate and shadow test in Fig 8.
5. **Marks, Gerrits and Marx is the closest analogue of the kernel/cast claim.** "the more the model is tailored towards the target domain, the more exploratory and explanatory power it will have, the less it will resemble the original model." Syntax transfers, semantics do not. This is world corroboration for Fig 9 :30-31, not a second figure.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :23 "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the two conditions, the per-domain residual requirement and the refuter.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Choose readers from domains that do not share the framework author's evidence pool; do not select for receptiveness.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list of agreeable readers is a room, not an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the two conditions; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The Formula's "Direction" section says a loop "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and state the mode of what they get.
