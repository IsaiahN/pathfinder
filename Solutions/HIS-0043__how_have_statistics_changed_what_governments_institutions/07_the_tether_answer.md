# The Tether's answer on statistics and social problems, HIS-0043

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the counterfactual category list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading of the history of statistics.

## 1. The residual it answers

Why did a condition that was once a private trouble, a moral failing or unremarked become a public object with a rate, a norm, a name and a budget? In effect terms: an act of counting changed the observation side of `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot, and the new readings were composed into comparisons that could become goals (Fig 1 :19, Fig 12 :87, Operators table). The question bundles three conditions, so it has three loops and three verdicts.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **D1, the instrument condition.** Enumeration extends what can register. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** The parish bill of mortality, the registry and the early census were already returning counts; the rate was an improvement on those readings. This is an instrument change, not the arrival of a new atom in the world (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

2. **D2, the category and comparison condition.** A reading becomes a problem through a comparison, not through being present. Fig 13 :20: **"ordering, so more and less can be said"**; Fig 12 :87: **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."** A category is a convention, and Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** Because the question is constitutively about people, public regard is legitimately part of the subject (Fig 2 :36), but it is an instrument for reality, not the anchor (Fig 2 :29). Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

3. **D3, the attention and channel condition.** The channel from condition to public regard can decay. Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; Fig 10 :26: **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"**; Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not."** When ministries, press and publics read the rate off other readings of the rate, the arrangement becomes a mirror chain (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*). Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

4. **Cross-cutting, the double effect of aggregation.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The same rate that makes a condition publicly visible can erase the slot that is in trouble. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.

5. **The gap.** No figure line returns the list of problems that would have been recognised without enumeration, and no instrument held here discriminates a problem before categorisation registers it. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."** Verdict `gap`, unreached at this budget, not shown underivable in principle.

Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a single historical verdict. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that statistics invented social problems out of nothing. Fig 6 :25: an import adds no atom to the world.
- Not that statistics merely discovered pre-existing problems. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A rate does not say what produced it.
- Not that any particular category (poverty, unemployment, normality, BMI) is the correct name returned by the framework. These are provenanced bindings from the history-of-statistics frame, candidates only.
- Not that counting is good or bad in itself. Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**
- Not `grounded`. No instrument here settled which source produced a given historical rate.
- Not a theory of everything about measurement. There is no last room (Fig 7), and the framework returns a generator of shape, not a final history.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not a debate. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Per-slot test.** A published rate that preserves every slot's live mass, checked against a case where the aggregate currently hides a live slot.
2. **Source test.** An instrument that discriminates, from one rate reading, among condition change, code change, reporting change and population change.
3. **Channel test.** A reporting regime in which the rate moves only when an independently measured condition moves, over two or more cycles, with the measuring instrument outside the reporting frame.
4. **Category test.** A category that cannot be quietly redefined when its reported outcome becomes inconvenient (Fig 10 :47).
5. **Standing refuters from `01` section 4**, all seven, written before the run.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The gap in section 2 is recorded with the three Formula step 7 questions kept distinct. SEARCHED was asked first: within D1 to D3 there is no pre-categorisation list. INWARD would need an instrument that detects a problem before any category registers it; OUTWARD would need a frame whose closure predicts that counterfactual. Neither is held, and the two are never read against each other. Importing a ready-made list against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* (canonical figures README)

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the social problems that statistics created, and it does not claim counting simply revealed problems that were already there. It says that counting extends what can register, that a comparison turns a reading into an object a government can act on, and that a category becomes a verdict the moment it starts deciding the outcome it was built to report. It also says the same rate that makes a hidden condition visible can dissolve the individual case into an average, and that the channel from the condition to public regard decays into mirrors of itself when ministries and publics read the rate off other readings of the rate. The ground does not decay; the channel does. What it offers is the shape any historical answer must have, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The list of problems that would have been recognised without counting stays open, because the framework returns a shape, not a name.
