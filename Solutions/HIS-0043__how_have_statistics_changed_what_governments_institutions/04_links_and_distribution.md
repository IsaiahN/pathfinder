## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends the interior, not the shape. Lead with the refuter.
- **Fig 2 :45** - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record it, before offering any theory.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the history-of-statistics literature.
- **Fig 7 :13** - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (quoted in `09`). A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :51** - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Do not rank recipients by receptiveness. Receptiveness is a contact-cost estimate at best, never an evidence score.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method for their own problem, with a request that they state their own ground and refuter.

## Two-stage offer, restated

- **Stage 1:** the residual in effect terms, the three-condition shape, and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient's verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the figure extract, the canonical usage rule and the five public literature links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a reader who has absorbed the frame has joined it.
- **Stage 3:** the formula as a generator to reuse on a problem with a checkable ground, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits this: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. That is what the refuter is for. Nothing here is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are yours to do, preferably through an opt-in page that lets a recipient decline contact.
