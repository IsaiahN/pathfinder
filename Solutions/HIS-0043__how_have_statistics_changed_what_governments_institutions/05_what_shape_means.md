## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question the distinction is concrete. The shape is: an instrument extends what registers, a comparison turns a reading into an object, a convention must not decide the outcome it reports, and the channel to the non-updating ground can decay. The **name** would be a list of the particular problems that statistics supposedly created (poverty, unemployment, abnormality, crime). The framework does not return that list. It constrains any such list: whatever is on it must have become visible through an instrument, must have been made actionable through a comparison, and must not owe its reality solely to the code that names it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "counting," "rate," "category" and "regard," then let the history-of-statistics literature supply "classes of equivalence," "normal people," "biopower" and "things that hold."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was stated in effect terms in `01` section 0, not as "the invention of the welfare state."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the repeated convergence of three domains on the same three conditions and one gap.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied here: the names "average man," "unemployment," "poverty line" and "normality" are imports from the history-of-statistics frame. They are real historical objects, but their status in this derivation is that of bindings onto held structures (the spectrum/ordering atom and the threshold bond), not primitives returned by the framework. Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** The distinction matters because the ordinary history question asks for names, and the framework returns only the shape those names must have.

## 4. The source problem is the governing error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A social statistic has the same structure. A rising suicide rate, a falling crime rate or a rising unemployment figure can be produced by a change in the condition, a change in the code, a change in reporting, or a change in the population. The reading does not determine the source. Grue and Heiberg (2006, opened this session) supply a historical instance: the tabulation of causes of death involved *"a controversial history that opposed aetiology to symptoms"* (Daston, LRB 2000), and the choice of code altered what the statistics could say. So the honest form of any historical claim here is *"the reading changed, and a discriminator is owed,"* not *"the problem was created by X."*

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming poverty, unemployment or normality?

**No, with the same narrowing the exemplar reached.** The README phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The structures used here (ordering, comparison, threshold, network) are already in the six-term set and the bond set (Fig 13, Fig 12, Operators table). Naming a historical category is a binding from the history-of-statistics frame onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What is forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall, or treating the name as the framework's answer to which problems are real.

Correction kept visible: an earlier reading of mine would have said "the framework cannot name any social problem." That is too strong. It cannot name the *pre-categorisation* problems (F7 of `01`). It can constrain any named category, and it can accept a provenanced binding as a candidate. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous historians to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground (an independently measured condition, a discriminating instrument, a checkable code history) settle it.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the historical question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. The shape in one sentence for this question

> Statistics change what is regarded as a social problem by extending what can register, by supplying comparisons that turn readings into objects with budgets, and by opening a channel to a non-updating ground that can silently decay into mirrors of itself; which particular problems result is a name, not a shape, and is left as a declared gap.
