## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How statistics changed what is regarded as a social problem" is a union of three overlapping conditions, not one lever. (1) A counting instrument extends what can register, and it is improved from a worse instrument already returning something: the parish bill of mortality came before the census rate. (2) A category is a comparison over an ordering, and a convention that starts deciding the outcome it was built to report has stopped being a convention. (3) The channel from the condition to public regard can decay, so that institutions and publics read the rate off other readings of the rate instead of off the condition. Cross-cutting: the same rate that makes a hidden condition visible can erase the individual case into an average. The specific list of problems that would have been recognised without counting is a declared `gap`, not an answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal and instrument-facing; checkable by reading Fig 6 and Fig 1

- **Statement:** an instrument extends what can register and is improved from a worse instrument already returning something; the per-slot signal must not be dissolved into the aggregate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Fig 6 :34-35 - **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22 - **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
- **What to check against the historical record:** were the new rates introduced on the back of already-existing counts (bills of mortality, registries, censuses), rather than specified in full from a description? Did any published rate hide a slot that a case-level record kept visible?
- **Refuter:** a counting instrument specified in full from a description that returned discriminable readings where nothing at any resolution had returned anything; or a published rate that preserves every slot's live mass with no loss against a case where the aggregate currently hides a live slot.

### C2. Category-facing; checkable by a historian of quantification or a sociologist of classification

- **Statement:** a category becomes actionable through a comparison, and a convention that decides the outcome it was built to report has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :20 - **"ordering, so more and less can be said"**; Fig 12 :87 - **"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."**; Fig 10 :47 - **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."** Operators table: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."*
- **What to check:** did the category in the case you know (unemployment, poverty line, normality, crime rate) acquire a budget or a legal consequence, and did its operating definition then become a matter of dispute? Daston (LRB, 2000) records the question directly: *"Why did the category of 'unemployment' supersede that of 'poverty' around the turn of this century?"* Wikipedia's synopsis of Desrosières records categorisation as *"creating new entities as poverty or unemployment."*
- **Refuter:** a category whose definition can be changed every time its reported outcome becomes inconvenient, with no loss of function, showing it was a convention throughout; or a case where a category became a policy object with no comparison of any kind.

### C3. Channel-facing; checkable against any reporting regime

- **Statement:** the ground does not decay, the channel does; when a regime ranges its readings against itself, agreement compounds while evidence does not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**; Fig 10 :26 - **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"**; Fig 7 :13 - **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**; Fig 2 :35 - **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
- **What to check:** can you produce a case where the published rate moved while an independently measured condition did not, or did not move while the condition did? Hacking, quoted by Tremain (2021): *"Enumeration demands kinds of things or people to count. Counting is hungry for categories."*
- **Refuter:** a reporting regime in which the rate moves only when an independently measured condition moves, over two or more cycles, with the measuring instrument outside the reporting frame.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that statistics invented problems out of nothing (Fig 6 :25, *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).
- Not that statistics merely discovered pre-existing problems (Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
- Not a list of which problems statistics created. That is the declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not that counting is good or bad in itself (Fig 11 :51, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*).
- Not a claim that the framework settles a historical dispute. It returns a shape; the ground settles the record.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument was run.
