## QUESTION

How have statistics changed what governments, institutions, and publics regard as social problems? Restated as an effect: why does a condition in a population move from being a private trouble, a moral failing or unremarked, to being a rate, a deviation from a norm, a named category and a budgeted object? Option A: statistics discovered pre-existing problems and society finally saw them. Option B: statistics invented problems from nothing. What each leads to: A makes a source claim from a reading (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."), B makes the convention decide the outcome it reports (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"). The figures govern a third answer: an instrument extends what can register, a comparison turns the reading into an object, and a channel to a non-updating ground can decay into mirrors of itself. The residual is indexed per slot and per time, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A three-place relation among a condition in a population, a counting instrument, and a public or institutional reading. Arity: three, carrying an ordered bond (enumerate, classify, attend, act) and a comparison bond (above or below a norm, rising or falling rate). Symmetry: not symmetric; a problem is a threshold over a distribution (Fig 13 :20 "ordering, so more and less can be said"; Fig 12 :87 "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."). Scale: individual case, population distribution, institution or policy, public discourse, with a lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point.").
- **Domain 1, the instrument.** Actors: censuses, bills of mortality, registries, bureaux, survey instruments, statisticians. Currency: counts, rates, distributions. Anchor: the actual condition in the population, which does not move when a frame re-describes it. Nameable anchor yes. Status admitted.
- **Domain 2, the category and ordering.** Actors: equivalence classes, codes, the normal curve, the average man, the poverty line, the unemployment definition. Currency: equivalence and ordering, the spectrum and threshold structures. Anchor: contested, because a category is a convention. Legitimacy comes from the question being constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."), but the reference must not update on frames (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."). Nameable anchor yes, as a two-part test. Status admitted as a compound.
- **Domain 3, the attention and policy channel.** Actors: ministries, press, reformers, publics, budgets. Currency: attention, policy, budget, regard. Anchor: outcomes in the world that do not move when regard changes, provided the channel is kept reachable. Nameable anchor yes. Status admitted.
- **X1, the social problem in itself before any counting.** Actors none nameable, currency none nameable, anchor none: any candidate updates when a frame changes its mind about what counts. Not a domain yet. This is a residual that cannot be cashed, not an answer.
- **Union, not partition.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap: instrument produces category, category directs attention, attention funds instrument (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.").
- **Order by dependency.** Registry or perception, then rate and code or vocabulary, then what counts as progress or objective, then policy or planning, then revised code or learn and carry (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."). The objective link has the weakest instrumentation (Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :36 and Fig 2 :29 appear to strain. Re-census shows two different quantities, legitimacy of subject matter versus non-updating of the reference. Public regard is legitimate evidence for usability and an instrument for reality; when it decides an outcome it has become a frame (Fig 10 :47). No strain after re-census; the distinction is kept visible because it decides D2.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures return a shape and three per-domain verdicts, never a list. Counting changed regard by extending what could register, by supplying comparisons that turned readings into budgeted objects, and by opening a channel to a non-updating ground that can silently decay into mirrors of itself. The same rate that made a hidden condition visible could dissolve the individual case into an average. The specific categories that resulted are bindings from the history-of-statistics frame onto held structures, provenanced as candidates, not names returned by the framework. The pre-categorisation problem list is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on domain claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the list, not `grounded`. The world sources corroborate the mechanism, but the verdict comes from the figures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, seven refuters and the provenance trail, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the whole architecture before they have described a residual (Fig 10 :52), and the cost of treating a pooled agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a refuter fires; a refusal is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Formula step 3, Fig 6 :42-43). The defective bindings can be withdrawn without touching the shape, and the lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that names which source produced a rate; a published rate preserving every slot's live mass where the aggregate hides a live slot; a reporting regime where the rate moves only when an independently measured condition moves over two or more cycles; a category shown to have been a convention throughout because it was redefined whenever its outcome became inconvenient; or a frame returning a pre-categorisation problem list that survives the anchor test in Fig 2 :29. Any of the seven pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4 would reverse the corresponding row. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
