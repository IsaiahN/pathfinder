## The finding in one line

The figures do not return a list of social problems and do not say which category was the right one. They return a shape: a counting instrument extends what can register, a category is a convention that must not decide the outcome it was built to describe, the per-slot signal must not be dissolved into the average, and the channel to the non-updating ground decays into a mirror chain whenever public regard reads itself instead of the world. Statistics change what is regarded as a social problem by changing which slots carry live residual, which comparisons become stateable, and which conventions acquire a budget. Which specific problems result is a `gap` at this budget, reported as unreached rather than as silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no receptiveness rank. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless (Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention.").
- No bulk-send plan. A room does not add an anchor (Seat Map :283).
- No claim that the framework settles the historical question. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and the ground settles the rest.
