## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks how statistics changed what is *regarded* as a social problem. Stated as an effect, not as a history:

> Before a counting instrument is introduced, a condition in a population is a private trouble, a moral failing, a visitation, or simply unremarked. After enumeration, the same condition can appear as a rate, a distribution, a deviation from a norm, a category with a budget attached, and a public object. Something changed in what registers, in what can be compared, and in what a government or public will treat as actionable.

Let `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot (Formula step 1). The action `a` is an act of counting, coding, tabulating or publishing a rate. The observation `o′` is the condition in the population. The claim is not that the condition changed, but that the **observation side** changed: enumeration extended what could be observed at all, and the new readings were then composed into categories and comparisons that became objects of attention. The residual is indexed per slot and per time; it is not a single number for a whole society.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula "Direction"): *an account that names (i) what the instrument made registerable, (ii) what comparison turned a reading into a problem, and (iii) how the channel from the condition to public regard was kept open or was allowed to decay.* That is the sought-for shape. Binding it as a term lets it accrue a residual like anything else, so it can be refused by the same test as the rest.

Two readings are refused at the outset:

- **Not** "statistics discovered pre-existing problems and society finally saw them." That is a source claim made from a reading. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The instrument reads a projection; which source produced it is a separate question.
- **Not** "statistics invented problems out of nothing." That would make the convention decide the outcome, which is the named failure in Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*

The honest residual sits between those two, and the figures are what locate it.

## 1. Domain census, before choosing any figure line

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** A three-place relation: a condition in a population, a counting instrument, and a public or institutional reading of the resulting category. It carries an **ordered** bond (enumerate, then classify, then attend, then act) and a **comparison** bond (above or below the norm, rising or falling rate). It is not a symmetric conjunction of three independent causes.
- **Symmetry.** "A social problem" is a threshold over a distribution, not a symmetric join of two held quantities. Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"* (the spectrum structure). Fig 12 :87: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."* The Operators table puts it plainly: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."* A rate only becomes a problem through a comparison, not through being present.
- **Scale.** Four scales are in contact: the individual case, the population distribution, the institution or policy, and public discourse. The membrane between them is lossy, Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* A registry row carries up into a rate and a rate carries back down into a policy; neither trip preserves the case.

### 1.2 One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable anchor? | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 The instrument domain** | censuses, bills of mortality, registries, bureaux, survey instruments, the statisticians who improve them | counts, rates, distributions, "things that hold" | the actual condition in the population (deaths, births, disease, employment), which does not move when a frame re-describes it | yes: the condition, a counter in the world | admitted |
| **D2 The category and ordering domain** | equivalence classes, codes, the normal curve, the average man, the poverty line, the unemployment definition | equivalence and ordering; the spectrum and threshold structures | contested. A category is a convention. Its legitimacy comes from the question being constitutively about people, but it must not decide the outcome it was built to report | yes, but only as a two-part test (see F4) | admitted as a compound |
| **D3 The attention and policy domain** | ministries, press, reformers, publics, budgets, charities | attention, policy, budget, "regard" | outcomes in the world (mortality, incidence, employment) that do not move when public regard changes | yes: the outcome, provided the channel to it is kept reachable | admitted |
| **X1 The social problem in itself, independent of any counting** | none nameable | none nameable | none: any candidate updates when a frame changes its mind about what counts | no | not a domain yet |

X1 fails the anchor test in Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* If the thing we call a social problem changes whenever a bureau changes its code, it is a frame, not an anchor. So "the problem itself before statistics" is not yet a domain; it is a residual that cannot be cashed. That is a finding, not a silence.

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap: the instrument produces the category, the category directs the attention, and the attention funds the next instrument. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

The question is also two questions bundled into one phrasing: (a) what did enumeration make visible, and (b) what got named, compared and acted on? It must be unbundled before search, Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

1. **Perception,** counting registers a discriminable signal from a worse instrument already returning something (Fig 3 :4-5, Fig 6 :32).
2. **Vocabulary,** a category and a rate exist: the bill of mortality, the cause-of-death code, the unemployment definition (Fig 3 :6-8).
3. **Objective,** the frame can say what progress on the new object would be: a lower suicide rate, a falling death rate (Fig 3 :9-11). This is the link with the weakest instrumentation. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
4. **Planning,** a policy route chosen under incomplete information (Fig 3 :12-13).
5. **Learn and carry,** the category is revised and the revision is recorded (Fig 3 :14-17).

The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not"* (Fig 3 :19). Effort spent on step 4 while step 3 has no instrument arrives at a step that cannot use it.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1. The change is a union of at least three conditions, not one lever

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

"How have statistics changed regard?" bundles (D1) an instrument change, (D2) a category and comparison change, and (D3) an attention and policy change. A single lever (say, publishing more data) clears the region where only D1 holds and leaves D2 and D3 standing. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.

**Mode:** `general` for the union structure, `specified` for its application here. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 plus Fig 3 dependency order, low overlap between the union rule and the chain rule). **Refuter:** one intervention that restores visible signal, correct category and a reachable outcome channel in one move, with no categorically different residue left behind.

### F2. The instrument extended what could register, and was built from a worse instrument already returning something

Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
Fig 6 :34-35: **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

The weekly bills of mortality, the parish registers and the early censuses were already returning something: counts of deaths, births and causes. The census, the cause-of-death code and the rate were incremental improvements on those readings, not devices specified from an account of "social problems." This is why the history is intelligible in Fig 6's terms: enumeration did not add an atom to the world. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*

The world source agrees at low overlap. Daniel Little, reading Ian Hacking's *The Taming of Chance* (Understanding Society, 2013-09-01, opened 2026-11-23): *"the act of conceptualizing and measuring is also often the act of constituting a particular slice of human reality"*, and Hacking's own line quoted there: *"By 1830 innumerable regularities about crime and suicide seemed visible to the naked eye... No one would have imagined such statistical stabilities had it not been for an avalanche of printed and public tables."*

**Mode:** `general` for the instrument rule, `specified` for the historical case. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 6 and the Hacking source, different fields). **Refuter:** a documented case where a counting instrument was specified in full from a pure description and produced discriminable readings where nothing at any resolution had previously returned anything.

### F3. Statistics can reveal a live slot and can dissolve it into an average

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**
Fig 13 :20: **"ordering, so more and less can be said"**

This is the double effect at the centre of the answer. A rate makes a condition visible as a public object, and the same rate can hide the very slot that is in trouble. Quetelet's *l'homme moyen*, read through Lorraine Daston's review of Desrosières (LRB, 2000-04-13, opened 2026-11-23), is described as *"an arithmetical fiction, the 'average man', who embodied the regularities that operated at the level of whole societies rather than of individuals"*. Daston also records that this required *"classes of equivalence"* established by *"some 'sacrifice' of fine-grained local information"*. Grue and Heiberg (Scandinavian Journal of Disability Research, 2006, opened 2026-11-23) record the other half: *"Quetelet discovered that this law of error or error curve also described the distribution of human features, such as height and weight"*, and that *"Large and small deviations from the mean constituted ugliness in body and dissipation in moral virtue."*

So the average is not a neutral representation. It is a structure (ordering) bonded to a state (the population), and the bond decides what counts as deviation.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 and Fig 13 plus two low-overlap world fields, history of science and disability studies). **Refuter:** a published rate that preserves every slot's live mass with no loss, demonstrated against a case where the aggregate currently hides a live slot.

### F4. A category is a convention that must not decide the outcome it was built to report

Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
Fig 2 :36: **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."**
Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Here is the strain that had to be re-censed rather than smoothed. The question is constitutively about people and what they *regard*, so by Fig 2 :36 human judgment is legitimately part of the subject matter. But Fig 2 :29 requires an anchor not to update on what any frame thinks. These are two different quantities: legitimacy (whether the subject matter includes people) and non-updating (whether the reference moves when frames push on it). Public regard is legitimate evidence for "is this category usable for people," and it is only an instrument for "is this the condition that is actually present." When a category acquires a budget and the budget decides who is unemployed, the category has stopped being a convention and has become a verdict (Fig 10 :47).

The world literature converges on exactly this. Wikipedia's synopsis of Desrosières (opened 2026-11-23) records that categorisation and allocation *"thus creating new entities as poverty or unemployment"*, following Durkheim's motto to *"treat social facts as things"*. Daston (LRB 2000): *"Why did the category of 'unemployment' supersede that of 'poverty' around the turn of this century?"* and *"they do not just describe the world, they change it."*

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 10 and Fig 2, low overlap with the history-of-statistics source). **Refuter:** a category whose operating definition is changed each time the outcome it reports becomes inconvenient, with no loss of function, which would show that it was a convention all along and never an anchor.

### F5. Naming the source of a reading is a metamerism error

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."**

A rate reads the projection, not its cause. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28, opened 2026-11-23): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* So a rising crime rate does not by itself say whether crime rose, reporting rose, the code widened, or the population changed. To name one of those from the reading alone is the metamerism error the essay names.

**Mode:** `general` for the shape limit, `specified` for the statistics case. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 and Fig 10 plus the essay, with the overlap declared: the essay shares the framework author, so this is weak independent corroboration). **Refuter:** an instrument that, from the same rate reading, discriminates among the sources that produced it without additional information.

### F6. The attention channel decays into a mirror chain

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :26: **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

D3 is where the failure lives. When ministries, press and publics read the rate off other readings of the rate, rather than ranging separately against the condition, the arrangement becomes panel 1 of Fig 2: mutual update, coherent drift, nobody notices. The ground does not decay; the channel does. The figure that describes the consequence is Fig 7: handing the same closure along a chain produces motion, not progress.

The Hacking source quoted by Shelley Tremain (Biopolitical Philosophy, 2021-10-24, opened 2026-11-23) supplies the low-overlap corroboration, and a warning: Hacking writes that *"Enumeration demands kinds of things or people to count. Counting is hungry for categories. Many of the categories we now use to describe people are byproducts of the needs of enumeration."* The same page quotes Hacking's *"information and control"* being *"more truly 'disinformation and mismanagement'"*, which is the channel failure named in economic language.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 10 and Fig 7 plus a biopolitics source). **Refuter:** a regime in which the published rate moves only when an independently measured condition moves, across at least two reporting cycles, with the measuring instrument outside the reporting frame.

### F7. The counterfactual list of "real" problems is a declared gap

Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

No figure line returns the list of social problems that would have been recognised if nothing had ever been counted. To produce such a list is to pick a frame first and then claim the residual found it, which is the apophenia Fig 9 forbids. After a genuine re-census of D1 to D3 and X1, no line governs that list. The answer is `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreached at this budget. X1 has no nameable anchor (section 1.2), which is why the gap is not an invitation to invent one.

Note the instruction distinction (Fig 6 :49-53 and Formula step 7): this is not shown to be underivable in principle, only unreachable from the frames held here. SEARCHED was asked first: within the three admitted domains there is no such list. INWARD would need an instrument that detects a problem before any categorisation registers it; OUTWARD would need a frame whose closure predicts that counterfactual. Neither is held. Importing a ready-made list against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed and would be priced above starting from primitives.

**Mode:** `general` for the gap verdict. **Status:** `gap`. **Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument or an independent frame that returns a pre-categorisation problem list and survives the anchor test in Fig 2 :29.

### F8. The valence of counting is relative to the residual, not carried by counting

Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Enumeration is a benefactor to a residual that is hidden by invisibility (a hidden harm becomes countable) and a malfactor to a residual that is a convention deciding an outcome (a code hardens into a verdict). There is no figure-licensed verdict "statistics are good" or "statistics are bad." The same census helps one residual and harms another. Fig 4 :20: **"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."** The kernel does not change with the field.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 11 and Fig 4). **Refuter:** a metric whose valence is invariant across two residuals with opposite ground readings.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A single lever is demonstrated that restores per-slot visibility, a correct category and a reachable outcome channel in one move, with no categorically different residue (breaks F1).
2. A counting instrument is documented that was specified in full from a description and returned discriminable readings where nothing at any resolution had returned anything (breaks F2).
3. A rate is published that preserves every slot's live mass with no loss against a case where the aggregate currently hides a live slot (breaks F3).
4. A category is shown whose operating definition can be changed every time its reported outcome becomes inconvenient, with no loss of function, proving it was a convention throughout (breaks F4).
5. An instrument is shown that discriminates among the sources of one rate reading without additional information (breaks F5).
6. A reporting regime is shown in which the rate moves only when an independently measured condition moves, over two or more cycles, with the measuring instrument outside the reporting frame (breaks F6).
7. A source or instrument returns a pre-categorisation problem list that survives the anchor test in Fig 2 :29 (breaks F7).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the folder is revised with the failure recorded here. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the union and the refuter, not with a theory of statistics. Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."**
2. Keep the per-slot verdict separate from the aggregate. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A rate is a filter for where to look; it is not a witness for which problem is real.
3. Never let a category decide the outcome it reports. Fig 10 :47. Where it has, name that as a finding, not as a neutral fact.
4. Record the provenance of every code change and every definition change. Fig 10 :62-64 requires a ruling to carry the residual it answered, the reasoning and the date, so a later reader can locate the error.
5. Ask a checker what they would count as a failure before showing them any of this. Seat 4 reads shape without referent and produces corrections on shape, not endorsements.

## 7. Final verdict of the audit

The shape survives. The counterfactual list does not, and is reported as a `gap`. Statistics changed regard by making new slots registerable, by supplying comparisons that turned readings into problems, and by building an attention channel whose ground can be replaced by mirrors of itself. The specific categories that resulted (poverty, unemployment, normality, crime rate, suicide rate) are bindings from the history-of-statistics frame onto held structures, provenanced as such, not names returned by the framework. Mode `specified` on per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. A source-discriminating instrument, an independent reporting channel and a pre-categorisation problem list would be required to move any row to `grounded`. Agreement and prestige are not such instruments.
