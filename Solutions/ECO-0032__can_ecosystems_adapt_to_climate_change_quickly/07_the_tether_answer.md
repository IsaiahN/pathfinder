# The Tether's answer on ecosystem adaptation rates

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-slot verdict structure, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. The empirical yes or no is `gap` at this budget.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question compares two moving quantities: the rate at which the climatic ground moves and the rate at which an ecosystem can respond through migration, evolution or reassembly of species composition. It is a union of overlapping loops, not one scalar. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". The residual is per slot: a population, a range edge, an interaction, a management unit. A global average can be near zero with one live slot (Fig 1 :20).

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is unbundled before it is searched.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Migration, evolution and composition shift get separate loops, and the pairwise overlaps are named as compounds.
2. **"Quickly enough" is an ordered threshold comparison, not a property.** Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison." Operators table :48 "a threshold relation between two quantities". The form is `rate_of_ground ⋛ rate_of_response`. The figures can state the bond; they cannot supply either rate or the threshold.
3. **The residual is per slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single adaptation percentage answers a different question.
4. **Unmaintained adaptation is selection running, not an agent adapting.** Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Persistence is the ground's reading; it does not identify the mechanism that produced it.
5. **Migration substitutes habitat; it does not remove it.** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." You inherit actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :67), and two failure modes stay invisible until they act (:68, :69).
6. **Managed composition work is permanent and asymmetric.** Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." There is no finished adapted state to declare.
7. **The likely break is the objective link.** Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break." Ecology has measurements and vocabulary; what is often missing is an instrumented definition of "enough" for a named slot.
8. **The verdict stays open at the ground.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The answer is unreached by figures alone, not "no" and not silence.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "yes, ecosystems will keep up" and not "no, they will not". Both are verdicts without a ground in this folder.
- Not that the figures contain an ecological rate, a climate velocity or a threshold value.
- Not that a persistence reading identifies a mechanism. That is the metamerism error (`05` §4).
- Not that the framework's habitat is the ecological niche. That mapping is retracted (`01` §6, `05` §6).
- Not that managed adaptation reaches a solved state. Fig 11 :63 says the opposite.
- Not a theory of everything. This is a threshold question with a ground.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument per slot, not a further argument. The questions to put to the ground:

- **Rate test:** for a named slot, is the ground velocity greater than or less than the response velocity, under one measurement protocol? An instrument that reports both in comparable units would close the ⋛ comparison.
- **Source test:** does the taxon, genetic or turnover evidence discriminate migration from selection from replacement as the source of a persistence reading? A spectrometer analogue, not an argument.
- **Introduction test:** in a managed case, was the new actor added to a habitat that remained, or was a habitat substituted? Which actors arrived unchosen?
- **Threshold test:** who is entitled to declare "enough", and on what measured criterion? Without this, the objective link stays broken (Fig 3 :27).
- Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: one global rate that preserves live slots under reweighting; migration as removal rather than substitution; exclusion in a maintained habitat; fitness increase with zero variance and unchanged contact; a numeric rate derived from the figures alone.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures narrow the shape of any answer and say what kind of measurement would close it. The canonical article warns that a metatheory *"cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself"*, and the trap essay warns that LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So this derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Measurements are that anchor. Agreement among models is not.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether ecosystems can keep up with climate change, because the figures contain no ecological rates. It says the question is a union of loops, not one number: migration, evolution and composition change overlap, and the residual must be measured per slot or the live signal disappears into an average. It says "quickly enough" is an ordered threshold comparison between the velocity of the climatic ground and the velocity of one response, that unmaintained persistence is selection's output rather than an agent's achievement, that migration substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose, and that managed composition work is permanent because introduction is available and exclusion is not. The likely break is not the data but the objective: nobody has stated an instrumented threshold for "enough" per slot. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. The answer stays open until the ground settles it.
