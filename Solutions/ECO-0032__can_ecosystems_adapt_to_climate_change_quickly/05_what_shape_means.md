## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

*The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not supply. For this question the shape is not "yes" or "no". It is: a per-slot threshold comparison between two velocities, with enumerated contact, identified introductions, and the persistence reading kept separate from its claimed mechanism. `yes` and `no` are fillers. The bond type, the slot structure and the ground separation are constraints.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms overlapping it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So "adapt" is the wrong starting word; "persistence after the ground moved" is an effect description anyone can check.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, because by then you have composed the answer through locating it.

Here the recursion ran: "adapt" was replaced by "persistence", then by "persistence per slot", then by "persistence compared against a ground velocity", then by the three mechanisms unbundled. Each step narrowed without naming a number. That is a shape.

## 3. What licenses a name, or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral.

For ECO-0032, a rate is not a missing primitive. It is a measurement. Importing it from monitoring data is legitimate if provenance and shadow test are supplied (does the imported rate explain the residual it was brought in for?). Claiming that the figures produced it is not legitimate.

## 4. Why persistence does not identify the mechanism (the metamerism error)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply this to ecology without forcing it: an assemblage that persists at time t+1 may have persisted because populations tracked climate by moving, because selection shifted allele frequencies, because other species replaced them, or because the manager intervened. The persistence reading is a projection. It cannot comment on the source. A claim that "the ecosystem adapted by migration" from persistence alone is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (genetics, telemetry, turnover partitions, before/after controls), not a name.

This is why the packet in `08` asks for three separate checkable sub-claims instead of one verdict. Each is a different discriminator.

## 5. Q2 answered: what licenses "quickly enough"?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A threshold is licensed only by the ground settling it for a named slot, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the threshold is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The honest form is: `rate_of_ground ⋛ rate_of_response` with the threshold marked as unfilled.

## 6. The mapping that must not be made

*Arrival of the Fittest*, in "Three mappings offered and declined", records: "The habitat as an ecological niche. A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."

So reading Fig 11's habitat as the ecologists' habitat or niche is the named error: a structure found elsewhere and given somewhere to live without a shadow test. Fig 11 still governs the *shape* of an ecology answer: enumerate actors, conditions and relations; distinguish maintained from unmaintained; introduction is available and exclusion is not; isolation substitutes rather than removes. It does not hand us the ecological niche. This retraction is carried in `01` §6 and is visible in the outreach text.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous ecologists who agree with the shape is a prestige loop, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction kept visible

An earlier reading treated "shape, not a name" as banning any specific claim about mechanism. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the mechanisms are not missing primitives; they are arrangements of actors and relations already enumerated (Fig 11 :45-47). Naming "migration" as a candidate mechanism is a binding from the ecology frame onto a described contact pattern, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is deriving a rate from the binding, or treating the binding as a derivation. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
