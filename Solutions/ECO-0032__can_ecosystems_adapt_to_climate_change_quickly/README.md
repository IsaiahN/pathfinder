## The residual in effect terms

Two quantities move: the climatic ground (temperature, rainfall, extremes, season) and an ecosystem's responses (migration, evolution, reassembly of species composition). "Quickly enough" is a comparison between their rates, not a property of either alone. The residual is per slot: a population, a range edge, an interaction, a management unit closes or fails to close the gap between predicted and observed persistence as the ground moves. Option A answers yes or no globally by averaging rates. Option B refuses the global answer, states the shape any answer must have, and names the measurements that would settle it per slot. A hides the live signal in an average (Fig 1 :22). B yields a checkable claim with a refuter attached.

An answer is bound in advance as an ordinary term: a per-slot rate comparison between the velocity of the climatic ground and the velocity of a named response mechanism, with the habitat contact enumerated, introduced actors identified, and the mechanism that produced persistence kept separate from the reading of persistence itself.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no ranking of people by likely agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures contain an ecological rate, and no claim that this simulated run is a measurement.
- No mapping of the framework's "habitat" onto the ecological niche. That mapping is explicitly declined by the canonical article and is recorded as a retraction in `01` and `05`.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Correlation disqualifies.** Seat Map :163 "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Do not spend the checker's independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.
