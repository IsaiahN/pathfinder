## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: one moving driver plus three overlapping responses, so a union compound (Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."). Symmetry: the comparison is ordered, ground velocity versus response velocity, so it is ⋛ rather than the symmetric conjunction +. Scale: the ground moves regionally over decades; the responses happen per population, per generation, per interaction. Different scales, so a membrane and a round trip are involved.
- **Domain 1, unmaintained habitat.** Actors: populations, species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain) and relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: persistence and demographic counts. Anchor: the environmental filter, measured persistence. The ground does not update on what any frame thinks. Question type: pure contact, no seat.
- **Domain 2, migration.** Actors: dispersers, corridors, range edges, recipient ecosystems. Currency: distance per time against climate velocity per time. Anchor: observed range boundaries and measured climate velocity. Question type: habitat substitution with inherited actors.
- **Domain 3, evolution.** Actors: heritable variants, selection pressures, generation time. Currency: change in mean fitness and additive variance. Anchor: allele-frequency and fitness measurements. Question type: selection between rooms, a two-factor handoff.
- **Domain 4, composition shift.** Actors: species pool, introductions, disturbances, and a manager only in the maintained mode. Currency: turnover and cascade effects. Anchor: measured composition and function. Question type: union member with a maintained/unmaintained split.
- **Domain 5, measurement and verification.** Actors: ecologists, monitoring networks, models, instruments. Currency: per-slot residuals between predicted and observed rates. Anchor: observed persistence and rates, never agreement among models. This is a frame over the other four, not a fifth mechanism.
- **Partition or union, and order.** A union of domains 1 to 4 under domain 5; the pairwise overlaps are compounds with their own loops. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Dependency order: perceive rates, acquire vocabulary, state the objective, plan, carry. The suspected break is the objective link (Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break.").

Strain test applied: reading Fig 11's habitat as the ecological niche strains the figure. Re-census gives two different objects, contact-with-residual versus a set of ecological conditions, so the line governs contact structure and the ecology reading is a provenanced import. The naive mapping is retracted, not smoothed over.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the empirical answer does not, at this budget. The question is a union of loops, not one scalar, and the residual must be per slot or the live signal is averaged away. "Quickly enough" is an ordered threshold comparison between ground velocity and response velocity; the figures give the bond and refuse to fill either rate. Unmaintained persistence is selection's output and does not identify its mechanism. Migration substitutes habitat and inherits unchosen actors. Managed composition work is permanent because introduction is available and exclusion is not. The likely break is the objective link: an instrumented definition of "enough" per slot is usually missing. The empirical yes or no is `gap`, unreached by figures alone. This is `specified` and ungrounded. The strongest outreach point is the refuter set, not a verdict.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a global answer with no ground, or by mapping the framework's habitat onto the ecological niche when the canonical article declines that mapping. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted niche mapping, the global-rate claim and the named-mechanism-from-persistence claim are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no atom has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrumented per-slot comparison of the two velocities would close the ⋛ comparison; source-discriminating evidence (telemetry, genetics, turnover partitioning) would name the mechanism behind persistence; a demonstrated exclusion in a maintained habitat would break the asymmetry claim; a maintained intervention with no substituted habitat would break the substitution claim; an instrumented threshold with a named declarer would repair the objective link. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
