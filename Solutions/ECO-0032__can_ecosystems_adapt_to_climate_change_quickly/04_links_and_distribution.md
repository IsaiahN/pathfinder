## Wider literature that bears on the question

Open access sources actually read. These are secondary corroboration only. They do not overrule a figure line, and their agreement with each other is not independence if they draw on the same evidence pool.

1. **Rubenstein, Weiskopf, Bertrand, Carter, Comte, Eaton, et al. (2023)**, "Climate change and the global redistribution of biodiversity: substantial variation in empirical support for expected range shifts", *Environmental Evidence* 12:7. https://environmentalevidencejournal.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13750-023-00296-0 . Systematic review of 315 studies and more than 12,000 species. Quote: "most species are not shifting in expected directions". Predicts a per-slot answer rather than a global yes. Corroborates F1 and F7. Overlap with the other ecology sources: medium, it is cited by later syntheses.
2. **Ibáñez, Gornish, Buckley, Debinski, Hellmann, Helmuth, et al. (2013)**, "Moving forward in global-change ecology: capitalizing on natural variability", *Ecology and Evolution* 3(1):170-181. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3568852/ . Quote: "for most organisms, particularly long-live species, their evolutionary rates will be too low to keep up with the pace of climate change". Distinguishes acclimation (5 to 50 years) from adaptation (multigenerational). Corroborates F5 and F8. Overlap: low with the range-shift review, which studies distribution rather than mechanism.
3. **Fisher, R. A. (1930)**, theorem as quoted at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher%27s_fundamental_theorem_of_natural_selection (page accessed 2026-11-23). Quote: "The rate of increase in fitness of any organism at any time is equal to its genetic variance in fitness at that time." Corroborates the two-factor shape in F5. The page notes the theorem is about a partial change, which is why this is a rate shape and not a guarantee. Overlap: distinct field and era.
4. **Acosta Alamo, M. and Manne, L. L. (2025)**, "The speed of change in climate and land cover is associated with the speed of biodiversity changes in avian assemblages of the United States", *PLOS ONE* 20(8):e0330153. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0330153 . Quote: "Rates of change in biodiversity were more strongly predicted by rates of change in land use land cover than by rates of change in climate." Corroborates F10: the driver matters as much as the response, and the answer changes by ecoregion. Overlap: low with the global range-shift review, different response variable.

No world source conflicts with a figure verdict. The apparent conflict between sources 1 and 2 was sent back to the census and resolved as a difference of slot and scale, not flipped by editorial preference (F10).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Leading with the full architecture sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual, the shape and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :54** "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Ask which mechanism and which slot they would check first, before offering the theory.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for endorsement.
- **Fig 9 :60** "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A journal tier or a citation count is a budget filter, never evidence for the shape.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the unbundled shape and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which slot they would check first; (3) record the verdict; (4) only if they are curious, the two public links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## Two-stage offer, restated

Stage 1 is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient holds the interiors, their agreement stops being evidence, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). Stage 3 sends a generator, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22), but each use still needs a ground and a pre-registered falsifier, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question".

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The shape as written here is `specified`, not `grounded`, and it does not carry a numeric answer. If a recipient supplies the rate that closes a slot, that result belongs to their ground, not to this derivation.
