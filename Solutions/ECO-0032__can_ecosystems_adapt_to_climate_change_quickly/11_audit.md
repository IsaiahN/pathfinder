## Independent domain census

Using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a comparison of two rates under a moving ground. Arity: one driver plus three overlapping responses, so a union compound rather than a single question (Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."). Symmetry: ordered comparison, ground velocity against response velocity, so ⋛ and not +. Scale: regional and decadal for the ground, per population and per generation for the responses. Different scales, so a membrane and a round trip are involved.
- **Domain 1, unmaintained contact.** Actors: populations, conditions, relations. Currency: persistence. Anchor: the environmental filter, which does not update on any frame's opinion. Question type: pure contact, no seat.
- **Domain 2, movement.** Actors: dispersers, corridors, range edges, recipient systems. Currency: distance per time versus climate velocity. Anchor: observed boundaries and measured velocity. Question type: habitat substitution, with two silent failure modes.
- **Domain 3, inheritance.** Actors: heritable variants, selection pressures, generation time. Currency: rate of change in fitness. Anchor: allele-frequency and fitness measurements. Question type: handoff with two factors, fidelity and ground pressure.
- **Domain 4, reassembly.** Actors: species pool, introductions, disturbances, manager only in maintained mode. Currency: turnover and cascade. Anchor: measured composition and function. Question type: union member with a maintained/unmaintained split.
- **Domain 5, verification.** Actors: ecologists, instruments, models. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: observed persistence and rates, never agreement. This is a frame over the first four.
- **Order.** Perceive rates, acquire terms, state the objective, plan, carry. The break is at the objective link (Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break."), because the threshold for "enough" is usually assumed, not instrumented.
