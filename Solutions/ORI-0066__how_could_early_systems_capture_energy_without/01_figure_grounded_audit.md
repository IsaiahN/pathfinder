## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks how an early system could "capture energy" when it lacks two modern pieces: enzymes (protein catalysts specified by genes) and membranes (the lipid bilayer that holds an ion gradient in chemiosmotic coupling). Stated as an effect, the residual is this:

> Energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14). A system that "captures" it must turn that directionless quantity into a *"difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22), hold the difference somewhere long enough to do work, have something that survives its own use while the work happens, and sit in contact with a drive that keeps the difference from running down. Modern biology solves this with enzymes plus a lipid membrane holding a proton gradient. Remove those two and ask what must still be present.

The residual is **not** "which molecule did it". It is "what shape must any answer have". Three things follow immediately:

1. The word "capture" silently imports directedness that energy does not carry on its own. So the first thing owed is a gradient, not a source of energy.
2. "Without membranes" removes a *binding*, not a *requirement*. Fig 13 :75-76 requires a state to bond the gradient to, and a membrane is one way to supply the state (space). The question does not remove the requirement; it asks for a different binding.
3. "Without enzymes" removes a *molecular kind*, not a *functional requirement*. Fig 12 :119 requires a part that survives its own use; an enzyme is one way to be such a part.

I bound the sought-for shape in the README before the run, so these three are refusable terms rather than a preference smuggled in after the fact (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** "Capture energy" is at least four conditions conjoined: a spendable difference, a state to bond it to, a part that survives its own use, and a maintained habitat. A fifth condition, a coupling that makes a copy of itself, is what converts capture into an evolving system. Arity greater than one means a **union**, not a partition: one lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56).
- **Symmetry.** Energy alone is directionless (Fig 13 :14); a gradient has *"directedness"* (Symbols table :97). Capture is therefore an **ordered** relation, not a symmetric conjunction. Inside-outside, reduced-oxidised, high-low are orientations, and swapping the operands changes the thing. This rules out treating "energy plus a container" as a flat formula in which the arrangement is free (Fig 12 :5).
- **Scale.** At first order a state and a structure compose into a flow (Fig 13 :76). The energy currency, whether thioester, acetyl phosphate, polyphosphate or ATP, is a settled molecule that becomes an atom for whatever composes over it (Fig 12 :104). The habitat that maintains the drive is a larger-scale room (Fig 11). So the question spans at least two levels and the transform between them is lossy (Fig 4 :27-29).

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** "Without enzymes and without membranes" is a bundle of overlapping conditions, not two boxes. A system can lack a lipid bilayer yet have a mineral wall; it can lack a gene-encoded enzyme yet have a metal-sulfide surface; it can lack both and still have a thermal cycle. Conversely, a mineral pore supplies the spatial condition while leaving the catalytic condition binding. By Fig 9 :55-56, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* So verdicts will be kept per domain and never averaged into one "the answer".

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance (Fig 3 :20-21). The order used here is:

1. **D1** establishes what is spendable (perception of the residual: what failed to resolve?).
2. **D2** establishes how the difference is held (vocabulary: what does a barrier or a cycle afford?).
3. **D3** establishes what survives use (catalyst versus spent part).
4. **D4** establishes where the drive comes from and what the habitat contains.
5. **D5** checks each of the above against a ground that does not update.

The middle link of Fig 3 is the fragile one here: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"* (Fig 3 :25-26). In origins-of-life work the nearest thing to an objective definition is Sousa et al. 2013: *"Life is the harnessing of chemical energy in such a way that the energy-harnessing device makes a copy of itself."* That is a testable objective, not a feeling, and it is worth making explicit before asking whether any candidate mechanism "works".

## 3. Findings

### F1 — "Capture energy" owes a gradient; energy alone cannot be captured (decisive, `general`)

Fig 13 :14: **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (the line defining energy).
Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"** (the line defining gradient).
Symbols table :97: *"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."*
Fig 13 :45-47: **"An ordering varying across space gives a distribution, and a distribution is a fact that stays put. What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."** and **"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."**

The word "capture" in the question is where the directedness is smuggled in. A container full of energy at equilibrium has no spendable difference, so it captures nothing in the operative sense. The framework therefore refuses to answer "where did the energy come from" as if that were the question. The answer must name a gradient: a redox tension, a pH difference, a temperature difference, a photochemical charge separation. This is a structural claim, `general`, and it is **triangulated** across Fig 13 (the derivation of the gradient as a whole with a property neither part has) and the Symbols table (the definition that adds directedness). The two figures share an author, so this is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness. World corroboration comes from a genuinely different field: Wolchover 2014 reports that living things are *"much better at capturing energy from their environment and dissipating that energy as heat"* and quotes England's drive toward configurations that resonate with an external source. That is a physics reading of the same shape. Refuter: a demonstrated case of directed, copy-making work with no spendable difference anywhere in the system; that would break the definition and the verdict with it.

### F2 — "Without membranes" removes a binding, not the requirement for a state (`specified`)

Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**
Fig 13 :76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**
Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

The modern answer bonds a proton gradient to **space**: the lipid bilayer is the spatial state that holds inside and outside apart. The question removes the modern lipid bilayer. It does not remove the need for a state. Two figure-legal substitutions remain: (a) a spatial substitute, an inorganic wall or pore that holds a pH or redox difference (Herschy et al. 2014 precipitated thin Fe(Ni)S barriers and measured formate and formaldehyde across them; thermophoresis concentrated a tracer up to about 5,000 fold in a microporous matrix); or (b) a temporal substitute, a thermal or redox cycle in which the output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**). Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** applies at the habitat level; the same move applies at the binding level here, and I record it as a re-census rather than a new primitive. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 13 :74-76 and Fig 12 :121. Refuter: a demonstration of a spendable gradient with no state bonded to it at any scale. I initially read "without membranes" as "without any structure" and retracted that in §7.

### F3 — "Without enzymes" removes a molecular kind, not the catalyst requirement (`specified`)

Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

The functional requirement is a part that survives its own use. A gene-encoded protein enzyme is one member of that functional group; an Fe(Ni)S mineral cluster, awaruite, magnetite or green rust is another. Sousa et al. 2013 describe modern enzymes of core carbon and energy pathways as *"replete with FeS, FeNiS and MoS (WS) centres"* and note that *"the catalysis in CODH/ACS is performed by transition metals (Fe and Ni) coordinated as sulfide clusters"*, adding that with the methyl group supplied, *"the reaction works in vitro, without enzymes, using either Fe2+ or Ni2+ as catalysts"*. Gaudu et al. 2025 show that nickel and amino acids make green rust nanometric and hydrophobic, so it *"could have functioned as a primitive, inorganic precursor to modern chemiosmotic metalloenzymes"*. This is a binding of a held function onto an inorganic actor, not a new term in the six-term set. Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 12 :119 is the governing line) with world corroboration from two fields: bioenergetics (Sousa 2013) and geomicrobiology (Gaudu 2025). Refuter: a case where capture persists while the putative catalyst is consumed one-for-one and never regenerated, so that nothing survives its own use.

### F4 — Capability belongs to system and habitat together (`general` shape, `specified` per habitat)

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**
Fig 11 :43-44: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** and **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
Fig 11 :46: **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**
Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

An early system did not "decide" to capture energy. In the unmaintained mode (Fig 11 :56: **"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."**), what survived is what the geochemical ground kept. The enumerable habitat around this residual includes serpentinizing fluid (H2, CH4), acidic CO2-rich ocean water, Fe(Ni)S precipitates, brucite and carbonate chimneys, thermal gradients, UV light, wet-dry cycles and mineral surfaces. Schwander et al. 2023 list serpentinization as a source of *"energy, electrons, organics, catalysts, nutrients and pH gradients"* and emphasise inorganic microcompartments. Sousa et al. 2013 add that the vent–ocean interface is *"rich in gradients of redox, pH and temperature"* and that the microcompartments unite *"two very important properties for early chemical evolution: catalysis and compartmentation."* Verdict: `general` on the agent-and-habitat shape, `specified` on each habitat actor. Triangulated across Fig 11 (habitat enumeration and capability) and Fig 6 :23 (**"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**) because the geochemical actors are imports into a chemical frame. Refuter: an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, which Fig 11 :72 names as the refutation of the capability claim.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A directed, copy-making capture occurs with no spendable difference anywhere in the system (breaks Fig 13 :14, :22 and Symbols :97).
2. A spendable gradient is demonstrated with no state, spatial or temporal, bonded to it (breaks Fig 13 :75-76).
3. Capture persists while the putative catalyst is consumed one-for-one and never regenerated (breaks Fig 12 :119).
4. An improvement to the isolated system, with no change of contact, moves the ground (breaks Fig 11 :39-40; this is Fig 11 :72's stated refutation of the capability claim).
5. One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh term is derived from the six (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility and minimality).
6. A ground measurement establishes that a single named prebiotic pathway was necessary and sufficient, which closes F6's `gap` into a name.

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the correction is appended here rather than silently erased. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Two world conflicts returned to census (the verdict was not flipped)

**Conflict A: natural pH gradients, useful or not?** Jackson 2016 (*Journal of Molecular Evolution*) concludes that natural pH gradients across inorganic membranes *"are unlikely to have played a part in life's origin"*, on the grounds that the membranes described are thick, *">200 times greater than those of the lipid bilayers"*, and that no thin inorganic membrane holding a sharp gradient has been observed at Lost City. Lane 2017 (*BioEssays*) replies that Jackson criticised his own interpretations, that alkaline vents harbour *"pH gradients of similar polarity and magnitude to those employed by modern cells"*, and that the geochemical and biochemical congruence is at the level of broad-brush stroke.

**Re-census.** The two papers govern different quantities at different scales. Jackson evaluates whether a thick mineral wall can substitute for a 5 nm dielectric in a rotor-stator ATP synthase (a D2 binding question at the molecular scale). Lane evaluates whether a natural gradient of the right polarity and magnitude exists in a vent habitat (a D4 habitat question at the metre-to-micrometre scale). Fig 13 :76 says a gradient over space is a term whose bonded state is part of what it is; the two papers bond the gradient to different states. No contradiction, no flipped verdict. The figure requirement (a spendable difference bonded to a state) survives both.

**Conflict B: is a gradient required at all?** Herschy et al. 2014 built a reactor in which natural proton gradients across thin Fe(Ni)S barriers *"appear to be capable of reducing CO2 to form low yields of simple 1C organics"*. Schwander et al. 2023 review experiments showing *"no gradient or compartmentation is required for CO2 reduction"* and conclude that *"the key to organic synthesis in serpentinizing systems are not gradients but catalysts"*.

**Re-census.** These are again different objects: Herschy tests gradient-assisted reduction across a barrier (D2); Schwander tests catalyst sufficiency in free solution (D3). Fig 12 :123 separates a functional group from its qualifier: the group (a spendable difference) fixes the kind of outcome; the qualifier (gradient across a wall versus redox tension in solution) says which member. F1 is narrowed by this: what is owed is a spendable difference, which a redox disequilibrium supplies even where no pH gradient exists. The verdict survives; the binding is left open. This correction is kept visible in §7.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census, so the conflict rule (back to census, never flip) was triggered twice and resolved twice without changing the shape.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape, not a named environment. The defensible answer is five conditions, not "it was the vents". Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.
2. State the mode on every sentence. "Capture" is not an observation; it is an effect term with a refuter.
3. Keep the four domains separate. A mineral pore, a metal sulfide, a thermal cycle and a habitat are not one lever (Fig 9 :55-56).
4. Put provenance ahead of any mineral name. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :50-52).
5. Make the objective explicit: capture becomes an answer to this question only when the energy-harnessing device makes a copy of itself (Sousa et al. 2013, world source).
6. Offer the shape and its refuter to the venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what would count as a failure, not whether the reader agrees. Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Send the generator, not a summary of one paper.
