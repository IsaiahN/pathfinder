# The Tether's answer: early energy capture without modern enzymes and membranes

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which historical pathway ran. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It returns a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The residual it answers

Energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14). The question's verb "capture" quietly supplies the directedness that energy lacks. So the residual is not "where did the energy come from" but "what must be present for a directionless quantity to become a spendable one, held long enough to drive work, in a world with no protein enzymes and no lipid bilayer". The question is a union of overlapping conditions, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55-56): remove the membrane and a different spatial binding may still hold; remove the enzyme and a different regenerating catalyst may still work. One lever clears only the region where one condition binds.

## 2. The shape

1. **A spendable difference is owed before any capture can occur.** Fig 13 :22: **"a difference that can be spent"**. Symbols table :97 adds that what makes a gradient a gradient is *"directedness"*. Fig 13 :45-47: **"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."** The candidate differences named in the world literature are redox tension, pH difference, temperature difference and photochemical charge separation. Which one is a binding, not part of the shape.

2. **The difference must be bonded to a state, and "without membranes" removes a binding, not the requirement.** Fig 13 :75: **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."** Fig 13 :76: **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."** A lipid bilayer is the modern spatial binding. An inorganic pore or wall is a spatial substitute (Herschy et al. 2014 precipitated thin Fe(Ni)S barriers); a thermal or redox cycle is a temporal substitute (Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**). Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** The two bindings are isomers over the fixed six-term atom set (`06` Q1).

3. **A part that survives its own use is owed, and an enzyme is one kind of it.** Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."** Fe(Ni)S precipitates, awaruite, magnetite and green rust are members of the same functional group as the enzyme active site; Sousa et al. 2013 note that CODH/ACS catalysis is performed by *"transition metals (Fe and Ni) coordinated as sulfide clusters"* and that the reaction works *"in vitro, without enzymes, using either Fe2+ or Ni2+ as catalysts"* once the methyl group is supplied. The requirement is regeneration, not protein.

4. **The capability belongs to system and habitat together.** Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :48: **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."** Serpentinization, acidic CO2-rich ocean water, thermal gradients, mineral pores and UV light are read off the world, not composed. In the unmaintained mode *"nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept"* (Fig 11 :56). A how-could-it question is therefore a question about a habitat, not about a lone mechanism.

5. **Capture becomes an answer to this question only when it makes a copy.** The objective link of Fig 3 is the fragile one: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"* (Fig 3 :25-26). The nearest instrument in the literature is Sousa et al. 2013's definition: *"Life is the harnessing of chemical energy in such a way that the energy-harnessing device makes a copy of itself."* Once an energy currency settles, Fig 12 :104 lets it become an atom for the next composition: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**. Thioesters, acetyl phosphate and polyphosphates are candidate currencies; which one is historical is open.

6. **Which pathway ran is a `gap`, not a silence and not a name.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The world literature is in live conflict about the gradient itself (Jackson 2016 against Lane 2017; Schwander et al. 2023 against Herschy et al. 2014), and Fig 9 :27-28 warns: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**. The shape is reported; the name is withheld.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that alkaline vents, hot springs, ice or wet-dry cycles were *the* site. Those are provenanced bindings from geochemistry, not derivations (Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*).
- Not that a pH gradient was required in every case. Schwander et al. 2023 report CO2 reduction with *"no gradient or compartmentation"*; what is required is a spendable difference, which a redox tension supplies.
- Not that any named mineral has been shown to be the historical catalyst, or that the reactor yields amount to a demonstration of life's origin. Herschy et al. 2014 call their work *"a preliminary proof of concept"* with *"low yields"*.
- Not that the four conditions are a recipe. They are a union, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged (Fig 9 :55-56; Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement by this agent, and no experiment cited settles which pathway ran.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

1. **Regeneration test.** Does a candidate inorganic actor survive its own use across multiple cycles, or is it consumed one-for-one? Fig 12 :119 does not supply the instrument; reactor re-use runs do.
2. **Barrier test.** Can a spendable difference be held across the relevant barrier at the relevant thickness, and is the polarity the one the coupling needs? This is the quantity Jackson 2016 and Lane 2017 dispute; a measurement, not a review, decides it.
3. **Copy-making test.** Does the candidate system produce more of itself while maintaining contact with the drive? That is the objective definition from Sousa et al. 2013, stated so it can fire.
4. **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** a directed capture with no spendable difference; a gradient with no bonded state; capture with a consumed catalyst; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a derived substrate term; a measurement that closes the pathway `gap` into a name.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The figure stops where the instruments cannot distinguish among the candidate pathways and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. No claim is made that the stop is the same thing as the world's answer.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the place or the molecule where early life captured energy, and it does not claim to settle the question. It says that energy is directionless on its own, so capture owes a spendable difference; that the difference must be bonded to a state, so removing modern membranes removes a spatial binding and not the requirement, with a mineral pore or a thermal cycle as the figure-legal substitutes; that removing enzymes removes a molecular kind and not the need for a part that survives its own use, which Fe(Ni)S or green rust can supply; and that capability belongs to the system and its habitat together. The four conditions are a union, not a recipe, and their verdicts are kept separate. Which historical pathway actually ran is reported as a `gap`: the framework returns a shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would decide it, and it stops there.
