## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is five conditions: a spendable difference, a bonded state, a part that survives its use, a maintaining habitat, and a copy-making coupling. Naming alkaline vents, green rust or a particular thermal cycle is a name. The shape is what survives when those names are removed.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened this session): use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"*. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"**. This is why the residual was written as "energy is directionless, so a spendable difference is owed", and not as "the answer is a proton gradient".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (quote carried from the exemplar's mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This governs the outreach order in `02` and `04`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it"*. Applied here: once the five conditions are stated, the search for "the mechanism" stops looking for a sixth thing and starts checking which held binding the world paid for.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question the relevant instruments are redox and pH measurements, reactor runs, and ultimately a demonstration of copy-making under prebiotic conditions.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fe(Ni)S minerals, green rust, serpentinization and thermophoresis enter from geochemistry this way. They are real imports and are recorded with author, venue and year.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (quote carried from the exemplar's mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. The essay's conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case.

This question invites the same error in a historical key. A present-day measurement of a pH gradient across a vent wall, or of formate in a reactor, is a reading. Naming that reading as *the source* of early energy capture assumes several paths could not give the same reading. Hot springs, vents, ice and wet-dry cycles can each supply a spendable difference and a maintained habitat. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this audit cannot certify its own shape; a ground must.

## 5. Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a mineral actor? No, and here is the narrowed rule

This is a correction to the naive reading, and it is kept visible rather than erased.

- The figures README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- Gradient, catalyst-as-functional-group, space and time are **already held** (Fig 13; Fig 12 :119). None is missing.
- Fig 13 :75 requires *a* state but does not select which binding; Fig 12 :123: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Therefore naming Fe(Ni)S, green rust or a mineral pore is **not** forbidden. It is a binding from the geochemistry frame onto atoms already held. What is forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh substrate member, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21), and (c) declaring one *historical pathway* to be the answer when the world evidence is in conflict.
- The exemplar made the same correction for the Gradient/Spectrum binding. I overstated the rule in the first pass of this folder and narrowed it here. The narrowed form governs F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: the `gap` is about which pathway ran, not about whether an actor may be named.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous origins-of-life researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let a measurement settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened this session): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim, cannot settle it, and must be labelled as such. No prebiotic experiment was run by this agent; the experiments cited were read and are attributed to their authors.
