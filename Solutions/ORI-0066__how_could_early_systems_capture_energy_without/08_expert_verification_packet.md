# Expert verification packet: the Tether on early energy capture

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in early energy capture research can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical pathway. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No prebiotic measurement was taken by this agent.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Energy is conserved, finite and directionless on its own, so "capture" owes a spendable difference before it owes a name (Fig 13 :14, :22). That difference must be bonded to a state: a lipid bilayer is the modern spatial binding, and a mineral pore or a thermal cycle is a figure-legal substitute, so "without membranes" removes a binding and not the requirement (Fig 13 :75-76; Fig 12 :121). A gene-encoded enzyme is one way to have a part that survives its own use; an Fe(Ni)S cluster, awaruite or green rust is another (Fig 12 :119, :123). Capability belongs to the system and its habitat together (Fig 11 :39-40). These are four conditions in a union, not a recipe, and which historical pathway satisfied them is a `gap`: the framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12
- **Statement:** directed capture requires a spendable difference, and the difference must be bonded to a state; removing a membrane removes one spatial binding, not the cross-kind requirement. Spatial and temporal bindings are isomers over the fixed six-term atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 **"a difference that can be spent"**; Fig 13 :75 **"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."**; Fig 13 :76 **"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."**; Fig 12 :117 **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a directed, copy-making capture with no spendable difference anywhere, or a spendable gradient with no state bonded to it, or show the spatial and temporal cases require different atoms (which kills the isomer claim and leaves a functional-group claim standing).

### C2 — geochemistry-facing; checkable by an origins researcher against the reactor and mineral literature
- **Statement:** the catalyst requirement is a part that survives its own use, not a protein. Fe(Ni)S clusters, awaruite, magnetite and green rust are members of the same functional group as an enzyme active site; the mineral species are provenanced imports from geochemistry, not derivations of the six-term set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**; Fig 12 :123 **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**; Fig 8 :50-52 **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**
- **What to check:** Sousa et al. 2013 report that CODH/ACS catalysis uses *"transition metals (Fe and Ni) coordinated as sulfide clusters"* and that the thioester-forming reaction works *"in vitro, without enzymes, using either Fe2+ or Ni2+ as catalysts"*. Gaudu et al. 2025 report that nickel and amino acids make green rust nanometric and hydrophobic so it could act as *"a primitive, inorganic precursor to modern chemiosmotic metalloenzymes"*. Herschy et al. 2014 call their reactor result *"a preliminary proof of concept"* with *"low yields"*. Are those characterisations faithful, and is the regenerating-versus-spent standing of each actor recorded?
- **Refuter:** capture that persists while the putative catalyst is consumed one-for-one and never regenerated; or an imported mineral whose provenance cannot be produced or which does not explain the residual it was imported for.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a world-facing objective
- **Statement:** an energy capture only answers this question when the coupling makes a copy; an energy currency that settles becomes an atom for the next composition. The four conditions are a dependency chain, so a paper that measures a mineral surface without naming the difference it holds is working above the break.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**; Fig 12 :104 **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**; Fig 12 :121 **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
- **What to check:** Sousa et al. 2013 define life as *"the harnessing of chemical energy in such a way that the energy-harnessing device makes a copy of itself"* and propose acetyl phosphate as *"the first universal currency of high-energy bonds"*. Is the copy-making objective measurable in the candidate system, or is it assumed?
- **Refuter:** a settled energy currency that cannot become an atom for a copy-making composition, or a copy-making capture sustained by a strictly open chain with no re-entry of output.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that alkaline vents, hot springs, ice or wet-dry cycles were the site. The literature disagrees, and Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not that a pH gradient was required in every case. Schwander et al. 2023 report CO2 reduction with *"no gradient or compartmentation"*; what is required is a spendable difference, which a redox tension supplies.
- Not that any named mineral was the historical catalyst, or that reactor yields demonstrate an origin.
- Not that the four conditions form a recipe or a ranking. They are a union; verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged (Fig 1 :19; Fig 9 :55-56).
- Not `grounded`. No part of this packet is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Regeneration test:** does a candidate inorganic actor survive its own use across cycles?
2. **Barrier test:** can the difference be held at the relevant thickness and polarity? This is exactly what Jackson 2016 and Lane 2017 dispute; a measurement decides it.
3. **Copy-making test:** does the system produce more of itself while maintaining contact with the drive?
4. **Ground closure:** a measurement that shows one named pathway was necessary and sufficient would close the `gap` into a name. Agreement among reviews would not. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

1. Read Fig 13 :14, :22, :75-76 and Fig 12 :119, :121 (five short passages).

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
