## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's four-domain table, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the directionlessness of energy relative to the verb "capture". Arity: at least four conditions (spendable difference, bonded state, regenerating actor, maintaining habitat) plus a copy-making coupling. Four or five conditions mean a **union compound**, not a single question (Fig 9 :55-56). Symmetry: ordered, because a difference has a sign and swapping inside and outside changes the term; a flat symmetric formula would lose the arrangement (Fig 12 :5). Scale: first order within a state-structure-state molecule, second order when a settled currency becomes an atom (Fig 12 :104), habitat scale above that (Fig 11).
- **Domain 1, thermodynamic.** Actors: energy, gradient, space, time. Currency: spendable free energy. Anchor: measured ΔG, redox potential, pH, temperature. Question type: which compositions cross the two substrate kinds.
- **Domain 2, structural.** Actors: gradient, barrier or pore, cycle, flow. Currency: cross-kind composition. Anchor: an instrument showing a difference held or renewed. Question type: binding of the gradient to a state.
- **Domain 3, catalytic.** Actors: protein enzymes and inorganic clusters and minerals. Currency: survival across uses. Anchor: re-use experiments. Question type: functional group with members; mineral species are imports.
- **Domain 4, habitat and selection.** Actors: serpentinizing fluid, ocean, pores, gradients, light. Currency: contact and maintained disequilibrium. Anchor: the ground, which keeps what survives. Question type: enumeration, not composition.
- **Domain 5, checking.** Actors: readers and venues. Currency: residuals versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: establish what is spendable before how it is held, before what survives use, before where the drive is maintained, then check each against a ground (Fig 3 :20). The copy-making objective is the link most likely to be assumed rather than measured (Fig 3 :27).
