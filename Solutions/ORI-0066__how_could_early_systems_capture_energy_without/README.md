## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run, bind the sought-for shape so it can accrue a residual like any other term (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction"). An answer to this question must do five things: (1) name a spendable difference, because energy alone is directionless; (2) name the state, space or time, that the difference is bonded to; (3) name a part that survives its own use; (4) name the habitat that keeps the difference far from equilibrium; and (5) say how the coupling makes a copy of itself. A proposal that names a specific pathway without these five is a candidate name, not an answer under this framework. This binding is written down before the conclusion so it is refusable by the same test as everything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map). Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
