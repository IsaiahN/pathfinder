## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** Energy is *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (Fig 13 :14); "capture" imports the directedness that energy lacks. Arity: at least four conjoined conditions (a spendable difference, a bonded state, a part that survives its own use, a maintained habitat) plus a fifth coupling that makes a copy. Symmetry: ordered, not symmetric, because inside-outside and reduced-oxidised swap to a different thing. Scale: first order inside a state-structure-state molecule, second order when a settled energy currency becomes an atom (Fig 12 :104). Fig 9 :55-56 names this a union compound, not one hard question: a single lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Domain 1, thermodynamic and physical.** Actors: energy, gradient, space and time. Currency: spendable free energy. Anchor: measured redox potentials, ΔG, pH, temperature; an electrode or calorimeter does not update on what a frame thinks. Question type: partition boundary (what composes across the two kinds).
- **Domain 2, structural and spatial-temporal.** Actors: gradient, barrier or pore, cycle, flow. Currency: cross-kind composition, state plus structure yields flow. Anchor: an instrument that shows a difference held across a barrier or sustained through a cycle. Question type: binding (which state the gradient bonds to).
- **Domain 3, catalytic and kinetic.** Actors: enzymes, Fe(Ni)S minerals, green rust, awaruite, magnetite. Currency: a part that survives its own use. Anchor: re-use experiments showing regeneration. Question type: functional group with several members; the mineral species are provenanced imports.
- **Domain 4, habitat and selection.** Actors: serpentinizing fluid, ocean, thermal gradients, mineral pores, UV light. Currency: contact and maintained disequilibrium. Anchor: the habitat read off the world; what survives is what the ground kept where no seat maintains. Question type: enumeration, never composition.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers and venues. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so agreement here is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle what is spendable (D1) before how it is held (D2), before what survives use (D3), before where the drive comes from (D4), then check each against the ground (D5). Fig 3 :20 governs; the objective link is the fragile one (Fig 3 :25-27).

Different objects, so no strain after re-census (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5). The verdict was not flipped.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the pathway name does not. A directionless quantity cannot be captured until it is bonded to a spendable difference (D1, `general`). Removing modern membranes removes a spatial binding while leaving the cross-kind requirement intact, with a mineral pore or a temporal cycle as figure-legal substitutes (D2, `specified`). Removing enzymes removes a molecular kind while leaving the requirement for a part that survives its own use (D3, `specified`, single-source on the figure side). Capability belongs to the system and its habitat together (D4, `specified`). The four conditions form a union, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged, and which historical pathway satisfied them is a `gap` reported as unreached rather than silence (D5). Mode overall: `specified` on substantive conditions, `general` on structural shape, `gap` on the pathway, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named pathway as if it were a derivation. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its four refuters, and by disclosing both literature conflicts.
- **undoable:** yes. The named bindings (vent, mineral species, candidate currency) are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. No substrate primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A regeneration test showing a candidate actor is consumed one-for-one would break D3; a barrier measurement at the relevant thickness and polarity would settle the Jackson/Lane dispute and might break or confirm D2; a copy-making demonstration under prebiotic conditions would close the objective link; a measurement establishing one named pathway as necessary and sufficient would close D5's `gap` into a name; a derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or a seventh term, would break the substrate. Prestige or a show of hands is not such an instrument.
