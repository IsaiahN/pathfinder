## The residual in one line

The question asks for a ranking over slots. Figure 1 indexes the residual per slot and forbids aggregation (Fig 1 :19, :22), and Figure 11 says an actor's valence is relative to the residual and that no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :49, :51). So the honest figure-derived output is not "Mars first, Europa second." It is a per-slot separator plus the shape a supporting environment must have, with the ranking declared as a `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no receptiveness rank.
- No bulk-send plan and no request for agreement or trust.
- No claim that this derivation settles which planetary environment actually hosts prebiotic chemistry. That would require a measurement at the ground, which this run did not take.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list of people chosen because they already favour a particular environment is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). The public astrobiology literature largely shares one prebiotic-chemistry evidence pool; that agreement is one witness, not many.
3. **Do not hand the recipient the interior first.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: public institutional contact pages and abstract submission portals are used, not scraped personal addresses. Unsolicited bulk outreach would breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, a practical failure on top of the structural one.
