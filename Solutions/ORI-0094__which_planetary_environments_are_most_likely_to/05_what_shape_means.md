## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ORI-0094 a shape is therefore not "Mars, then Europa, then Enceladus." It is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before a name is licensed: a per-slot separator (a spendable difference, a network, a catalyst, a cycle, a functional group), plus the requirement that the pathway be named before the place. "Mars today, under pathway P, measured by instrument I" is a named term; "a slot where a spendable gradient and a network are in contact with the residual" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The searcher starts with the vocabulary they have, bootstraps by proximity, and lets the question tether the search.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is the failure of a reaction step at a place, not the name of the place.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That sentence governs ORI-0094 directly. The question bundles a habitat question, a chemistry question and an instrument question. Fig 9 :55 agrees: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this solution the stop is the declared `gap`, not a discovery.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For ORI-0094, a measurement at a slot that discriminates among pathways. The canonical README adds that the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing a laboratory pathway into a planetary setting is such a move; it must carry its provenance and explain a gap it was chosen to fill.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A list of candidate environments is a visible choice of habitat axes, not nature's own list.

## 4. The specific error the question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

ORI-0094 invites the same metamerism. A "favourable environment" reading can be produced by a UV-driven cyanide pathway, by a vent proton gradient, by ice-eutectic concentration, or by several in sequence. Naming Mars or Europa from that single reading would name a source off a projection. What is owed is a discriminator: a measurement at the slot that tells which path produced the reading.

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A null detection today is not a verdict that a slot cannot support prebiotic chemistry; it may be a channel failure (Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does").

## 5. Answering the question's own "what would an answer look like?"

Written down so it can accrue a residual like any other term (Formula, "Direction"):

- **An answer would name the pathway first**, then the slot, then the instrument, and would keep the three indices separate.
- **An answer would be per slot**, not a global score, because the residual is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- **An answer would state a spendable difference and a network** in each slot, plus a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group (Fig 13 :22, :24; Fig 12 :119, :121, :123).
- **An answer would distinguish wiring from capability**: laboratory success is not planetary capability (Fig 11 :70).
- **An answer would carry a refuter written in advance** and a declared mode.

If an answer cannot do these things, the framework's output is a shape plus a `gap`, not a ranking. That is what this solution reports.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially overstated the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Naming a binding onto an atom already held is not forbidden; it is provenance. Gradient and Network are already among the six (Fig 13). So naming a spendable gradient in a vent slot is a binding from the chemistry frame onto a held structure, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework itself selects a planetary name, or that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

Both the narrowed rule and the original overstatement are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. The exemplar's `05` section 5b makes the same correction.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly", and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous astrobiology names agreeing with a ranking is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any planetary environment and cannot settle the question it reports on.
