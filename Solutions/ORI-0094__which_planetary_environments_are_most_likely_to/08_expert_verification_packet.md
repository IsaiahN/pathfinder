# Expert verification packet: the Tether on planetary prebiotic environments

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astrobiologist or prebiotic-chemist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal ranking. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at a planetary slot is claimed. This is a separator and a shape, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The framework cannot answer which planetary environments are most likely to support prebiotic chemistry today as posed, because that question asks for one global standing ranking. Favourability is relative to a named prebiotic residual, the residual is indexed per slot and must not be aggregated, and the question is a union of overlapping habitat, chemistry and instrument conditions. What the framework returns is a per-slot separator and the shape a supporting environment must have: a spendable difference, a network over which influence travels, and a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group, with laboratory success read as wiring rather than planetary capability. The literal ranked list is declared a gap, to be closed by a measurement at the slot. Falsifiers were written before the conclusion and are listed below.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 1 and 9

- **Statement:** there is no standing ranking. Valence is relative to the residual, the residual is per slot, and the question is a union that must be split before it is searched.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a ranking over environments that stays invariant across two pathway residuals with demonstrably different required conditions (for example a UV-dependent photochemical pathway and a UV-shielded vent pathway), measured at the ground in both cases; or show a per-slot residual can be aggregated without losing the live signal. If either holds, C1 fails.

### C2. Chemistry-facing; checkable by an astrobiologist against named pathways

- **Statement:** a supporting slot must contain a spendable difference and a network, plus a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group. An inventory of water, carbon and energy is a state, not a route.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent." Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input." Fig 12 :123 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome."
- **What to check:** for a pathway the recipient knows well, is the environment's contribution a gradient that can be spent (redox, pH, thermal or photochemical), a network over which influence travels, and are there regenerated parts, re-entrant products and an outcome-fixing substructure? Sasselov et al. 2020 give a comparable access list; Saha et al. 2022 enumerate the micro-scale conditions; Herschy et al. 2014 give a vent example. These corroborate, they do not decide.
- **Refuter:** a prebiotic pathway demonstrated at the ground with no spendable gradient and no network, confirmed by an independent ground reading; or two slots with identical catalysts, cycles and functional groups over identical histories that yield different products with no unenumerated actor. If either holds, C2 fails.

### C3. Framework-internal derivation; checkable against Figures 11, 13, 9 and 8

- **Statement:** a laboratory reconstruction is a substituted habitat that proves wiring, not capability, and the literal ranking is a declared gap rather than a hidden answer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **Refuter:** a laboratory reconstruction that reproduces the full planetary capability, including unchosen actors and the second-order cascade, with the planet-side measurement paying for the same prediction; or a verbatim figure line selecting a specific environment at a specific time for a named residual, checked in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. If either holds, the gap is withdrawn.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranked list of named environments, and not a habitability score that adds the cost of describing a slot to the cost of testing it.
- Not that a null detection today proves a slot cannot support prebiotic chemistry. The channel fails while the ground does not.
- Not that the chemistry bindings (proton gradient, wet-dry cycle, ice eutectic) are figures selecting a planet. They are bindings from the chemistry frame, provenanced as such, and they move no wall.
- Not a theory of everything and no claim to have settled which environment actually hosts prebiotic chemistry. That requires a ground measurement this run did not take.
- Not `grounded`. Every substantive claim is `specified` or `general`, and the ranking is a `gap`.
- Not corroborated by reader agreement. The essays and figures share one author's evidence pool, and this packet's simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` shares the primary's pool.

## 5. What would settle it

A measurement at a slot, not a further argument and not a count of agreeing papers. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong before pooling agreement; a list of readers selected because they already favour a ranking is a mirror. The refuters in section 2 are the concrete settlements. A source-discriminating measurement that tells which pathway produced a reading at a slot would name the slot and close the gap; a ranking invariant across two different pathway residuals would break the shape claim.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :49 and :51, Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :54 and :55, four short passages, and confirm that no standing ranking is licensed.
2. Check C2 against one pathway you know: name the spendable difference, the network, the catalyst, the cycle and the functional group, or say which is missing.
3. Name one measurement at a slot that would distinguish a UV-driven pathway from a UV-shielded vent pathway, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no reply is needed if you would rather not engage.
