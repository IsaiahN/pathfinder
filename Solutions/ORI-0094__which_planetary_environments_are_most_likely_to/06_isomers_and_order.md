# Isomers, order and analogues: supporting derivation for ORI-0094

**Mode: `specified` on the astrobiology bindings, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6). No assay, spectrum or sample measurement was taken by this run.

## RESIDUAL

The question asks which planetary environment is most likely to support prebiotic chemistry today. Before any ranking three structural questions are owed, and a rank cannot be issued until they are answered. (1) Are two settings with the same chemical inventory but a different internal arrangement different substances for this purpose, that is, are they isomers under the figure definition? (2) What order does a supporting setting supply: an unordered ingredient list, or a route with sequence, production, a catalyst and a cycle? (3) Which analogues from chemistry transfer to the figure structures, and which transfers are strained bindings rather than derivations? None of these is answered by naming Mars, Europa or Enceladus.

## DERIVATION

### Q1. Same inventory, different outcome: an isomer claim

Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Fig 12 :95: "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing." Fig 12 :96: "Two entries can share every ingredient and be inverses of one another."

Applied to ORI-0094: a UV-lit shallow lake and a UV-shielded alkaline vent can carry the same inventory of water, C N O P S and an energy source, yet differ in which bond joins them. A photochemical cyanide pathway is driven by the UV flux; a vent proton-gradient pathway is destroyed by it. Under the figure definition they are different substances, not two spellings of one favourable environment. This is why an ingredient list cannot rank environments. The figure's own chemistry precedent makes the point: Fig 12 :97, "Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties", and Fig 12 :98, "structure into the notation. Liebig and Wohler, 1824 to 1826; named isomerism by Berzelius, 1830."

**Strain, named.** An environment is not a molecule in the library Gamma. Calling two settings isomers is a binding from the chemistry frame onto a figure structure, not a derivation of a planetary fact. The transfer is licensed as an analogue because the figure itself uses chemistry as its example; it is not licensed as a measurement. No strain is hidden here.

### Q2. What order a supporting setting supplies

Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

An inventory names a state. A pathway names a route, and a route is ordered. Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing." Under a symmetric bond, reordering the operands changes nothing; under an ordered bond, it changes the substance. Applied here: listing light, water and minerals in any order is one state, but the step order of a prebiotic pathway is not interchangeable. A setting that supplies repeated wet-dry or freeze-thaw cycling supplies an ordered bond (output re-entering input), not merely a set of conditions. This is the structural reason the question's word "support" must be indexed to a named pathway: support is a route, and routes are ordered.

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." A laboratory route composed from the six cannot manufacture a seventh environmental primitive; importing a pathway from the chemistry frame into a planetary setting moves the wall for the frame, not for the world (Fig 6 :21, Fig 8 provenance rule). The order of the route is preserved or it is not a route.

### Q3. The three structures inside a slot

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input." Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome."

These three, not the ingredient list, are the checkable content of "supports prebiotic chemistry." A mineral surface or UV-driven redox shuttle is a catalyst if it is regenerated. Wet-dry concentration repeated over cycles is a cycle if a product re-enters as input. Phosphate availability, borate or a pH window is a functional group if its presence fixes the outcome. The world literature supplies bindings: Herschy et al. 2014 describe natural proton gradients across thin Fe(Ni)S barriers and thermophoretic concentration (catalyst and gradient), and Cleaves 2013 and Saha et al. 2022 enumerate the conditions in which such structures appear. Those bindings are provenance from the chemistry frame, not figures selecting a planet.

Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent" and Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels" are the two structures a slot must contain before the three Fig 12 structures can do any work. Fig 13 :76: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." A thermal gradient over space and a cycling regimen over time are different terms, so a slot must state which it supplies.

### Q4. Two currencies, not one habitability score

Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

Describing an environment (how many parameters the model needs) and testing it (how many assays are spent) are different quantities in different units. A global habitability index that sums them is the error this figure names, not the fix. It proposes arrangements it cannot afford to test and reports the sum as knowledge. For ORI-0094 this is a direct reason to refuse a single ranking number: the description of a slot and the measurement at that slot must be reported separately, and the budget decides how much of the space is reached.

## FALSIFIER, WRITTEN BEFORE THE CONCLUSION

The isomer and order reading is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Two settings with identical catalysts, cycles and functional groups over identical histories yield different prebiotic products at the ground with no unenumerated actor (breaks Q1 and Q3).
2. A prebiotic pathway is demonstrated at the ground that is an unordered set of ingredients, with no sequence, no production step and no cycle (breaks Q2).
3. A single score in one unit correctly prices both the description of a slot and the actions needed to test it, with the units shown to be the same (breaks Q4).
4. The chemistry-to-environment binding produces a new environmental primitive not composed from the six and not imported with provenance (breaks Q1 strain).

If any fires, the analogue is withdrawn or re-censused. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## CONCLUSION

The supporting derivation yields three checkable structural results. Environments with the same inventory but different arrangement are different substances for this purpose, so an ingredient list cannot rank them. A supporting setting supplies an ordered route, not a state, so the pathway must be named before the place. Inside a slot the checkable content is a spendable difference, a network, a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group, priced in two currencies that do not add. The chemistry terms are bindings from the chemistry frame, honestly provenanced, not figures selecting a planet and not new primitives.
