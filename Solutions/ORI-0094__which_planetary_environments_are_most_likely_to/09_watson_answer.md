## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, which planetary environments are most likely to support prebiotic chemistry today, using the Tether figures as primary and world sources as secondary corroboration only. Option A: assert a ranked list of named environments. Option B: return a per-slot separator and the shape a supporting environment must have, and declare the ranking a gap. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The failure of a prediction about prebiotic products to survive an observation at a place and time. Arity: at least three (an environment slot, a pathway residual, an observing instrument); the word most silently reduces this to one. Symmetry: support is ordered (an environment enables a pathway, not the reverse), while a similarity ranking of environments is symmetric; the question mixes the two. Scale: chemistry at microns to metres, environment at metres to kilometres, comparison across planets and geological time. Union compound, not one question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :54, Fig 9 :55).
- **Domain 1, environment and habitat.** Actors: conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), mineral surfaces, gradients, wet-dry and freeze-thaw cycles. Currency: contact, first and second order. Anchor: an assay at that slot. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, chemistry closure.** Actors: atoms C H N O P S, the seven bonds, negation, reaction sets. Currency: reach inside closure and description cost. Anchor: settlement of a term against an experiment. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, scale membrane.** Actors: methods going up, head starts coming down. Currency: reapplicability versus one-occasion recording. Anchor: the round-trip gap. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, instrument and ground.** Actors: instruments, readings, per-slot residuals, independent frames. Currency: residual reduction. Anchor: the ground, which does not update when a frame changes its mind. Admitted.
- **X1, intrinsically favourable environment.** A supposed standing property of a place. No nameable anchor, because valence changes with the residual. Not a domain yet; absorbed into Domain 1 as a relation.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition. A wet-dry lake is at once a habitat, a chemistry closure and an observing problem, so the intersections form compounds with their own loops (Fig 9 :54, Fig 9 :55).
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual in effect terms, enumerate the habitat, assess the chemistry closure, check the scale transform, read the ground per slot, then issue a verdict with provenance. Optimising a later link cannot rescue an earlier break (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: the Fig 11 actor language applied to planetary environments is resolved by re-census (the cast changes, the residual-relative rule transfers); gradient as figure structure versus redox or UV gradient in chemistry is one object at two strata; the two currencies cannot be added. Strains are named in `11_audit.md`, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the ranked list does not. Favourability is relative to a named pathway, so the question is asked once per pathway. Verdicts are per slot and never averaged. The question is a union of habitat, chemistry and instrument conditions. A supporting slot needs a spendable difference, a network, a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group, and a laboratory reconstruction proves wiring rather than planetary capability. The literal ranking is a declared gap, to be closed by a measurement at the slot. This is a `specified` and ungrounded result. Agreement from readers is never pooled as corroboration, and the simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` is internal consistency only.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet records the mode, the refuters and the lineage in front of the claim, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a ranked list instead of a generator; the figures price that cost and require it to be bought in proportion to being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is naming a planet from a single reading and thereby fixing a standing list that later measurements must overturn.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective reading (a ranked list, a global average, a null-detection absence claim) is removable without touching the shape derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent ranking can be superseded by the corrected separator. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the gap can be declared at any point before a ground measurement arrives.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A ranking invariant across two pathway residuals with demonstrably different requirements, measured at the ground, would break the shape claim; an averaged multi-environment score that predicts detections while per-slot scores fail would break the per-slot rule; a pathway demonstrated with no spendable gradient and no network would break the separator; a laboratory reconstruction reproducing full planetary capability with the planet-side measurement paying for the same prediction would collapse wiring into capability; and a verbatim figure line selecting a specific environment at a specific time for a named residual would withdraw the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
