# The Tether's answer: which planetary environments are most likely to support prebiotic chemistry today

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain structural claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the literal ranked list. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a separator, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a ranking over slots. Stated as an effect: at which slot s (a specific environment, at a specific time, under a specific prebiotic pathway) is the residual small enough that a prediction about prebiotic chemistry survives contact with an observation? Three defects are visible before any figure is read. The word "most" aggregates, and the framework indexes the residual per slot (Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The word "support" is a two-place relation, and favourability is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."). The word "today" changes the ground at the slot, and the channel fails while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does"). The question therefore splits into one loop per pathway and is a union compound, not one ranking problem (Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").

## 2. The shape

1. **Name the pathway before the place.** No standing ranking survives a change of residual: Fig 11 :51, "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." A UV-lit shallow lake favours a cyanide photochemical pathway and destroys a UV-shielded vent pathway. The ranking flips when the residual changes, so "which environment" must be asked once per named pathway.

2. **The answer is per slot, not a global score.** Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The live signal is the pathway step that failed to resolve at one place and time, not an average over places. A global habitability index is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.

3. **The question is a union of overlapping conditions.** Habitat contact, chemistry closure and instrument reach overlap; a single lever leaves the intersections standing. Verdicts are kept separate per pathway and never averaged (Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.").

4. **A supporting slot must contain a difference that can be spent and a network over which influence travels.** Fig 13 :22, "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 13 :24, "that entities relate, and influence travels." An ingredient list of water, carbon and energy is a state, not a route. Fig 12 :119, "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 12 :121, "cycle. The output re-enters as input." Fig 12 :123, "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome." These three structures are the checkable content of support.

5. **A laboratory success is a substituted habitat, not a planetary verdict.** Fig 11 :70, "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A bench vent pore, drying lake or ice eutectic proves that a mechanism can work, not that a planet supplies the unchosen actors and the duration.

6. **The literal ranking is a declared gap, not silence.** Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"; Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." No figure line returns "present-day Mars outranks Enceladus." That needs a ground measurement and a criterion the question does not supply. This is unreachable at this budget with an unstated pathway, not underivable in principle. Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranked list of named environments. That would be a standing list, a global aggregate and a name where the framework returns a shape.
- Not "Mars first, Europa second," and not a habitability index that sums the cost of describing a slot and the cost of testing it. Those are two currencies that do not add (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q4).
- Not that a null detection today proves a slot cannot support prebiotic chemistry. A null result may be a channel failure, not an absence.
- Not that the chemistry bindings (proton gradient, wet-dry cycle, eutectic concentration) are figures selecting a planet. They are provenanced bindings from the chemistry frame.
- Not `grounded`. No spacecraft measurement, laboratory assay or observation was taken by this run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating measurement at a slot, not more literature agreement. Agreement from a shared pool is one witness (Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). What would move the verdict toward `grounded`:

- A ranking over environments that stays invariant across two pathway residuals with demonstrably different requirements, measured at the ground.
- An averaged multi-environment score that predicts detections correctly while all per-slot scores fail.
- A pathway demonstrated at the ground with no spendable gradient and no network.
- A laboratory reconstruction that reproduces the full planetary capability, including unchosen actors and the second-order cascade, with the planet-side measurement paying for the same prediction.
- A figure re-census that yields a verbatim line selecting a specific environment at a specific time for a named residual. That would withdraw the `gap`.

Until one of these fires, the ranking stays a `gap` and the structural claims stay `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops at the limit of what the available figures and instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Any condensation of the figures into a shorthand carries a round-trip gap R_T that must be measured rather than assumed zero (Fig 4 :30, "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."). The second-seat re-census in `11_audit.md` is simulated and shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration. Buying independence before pooling agreement is priced by the framework (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank planetary environments, and it does not claim that any place is intrinsically favourable. It says that favourability is relative to a named prebiotic pathway, so the question must be asked once per pathway and the answer kept per slot, because the residual is indexed per slot and a global average hides the live signal. It says the question is a union of habitat, chemistry and instrument conditions, not a list of separate environments. It says a supporting slot must contain a difference that can be spent, a network over which influence travels, and a catalyst, a cycle and a functional group, and that a laboratory reconstruction proves wiring rather than planetary capability. What it offers is that separator, the shape any winning candidate must have, and refuters written in advance. The literal ranking is reported as a gap, to be closed by a measurement at the slot rather than by agreement between readers.
