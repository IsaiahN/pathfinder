## Wider literature that bears on the answer

1. **The "access" frame, not the "place" frame.** Sasselov et al. 2020 write: "When we discuss surface conditions here, we want to think in terms of access: (i) to long-lived liquid H2O; (ii) to C, N, S, P, and Fe; (iii) to mid-range UV light; (iv) to thermal and chemical gradients (e.g., redox gradients, vents, and volcanoes); and (v) to stable climate..." This is the closest world-source statement to the figure-derived separator in `01` F5. It corroborates, it does not decide.
2. **Reaction failure as the discriminating test.** Cleaves 2013: "it may be instructive to explore the extreme regions of known and future investigations of prebiotic chemistry, where reactions fail". This is the world-side analogue of minting after a residual, rather than accumulating successful demonstrations.
3. **Scale of the habitat.** Saha et al. 2022 make the case that "these geological settings, while informative about constraining the potential reaction environments, conditions, and chemicals, are mostly still at large length scales of centimeters, meters, or kilometers" and argue for microenvironments. That is the inward instrument track in `01` F7.
4. **Substituted habitat caution.** Herschy et al. 2014 describe a reactor and report low yields, variable formaldehyde, and a proof of concept needing optimisation. Read with Fig 11 :70, this is wiring, not planetary capability.
5. **Union, not single winner.** Ansari 2026: "no single setting fully satisfies all requirements for life's emergence" and "complementary processes operating across interconnected and evolving environments collectively enabled the assembly, concentration, stabilization, and integration of prebiotic molecules." This is the strongest low-overlap corroboration of the figure verdict, though it is still inside the astrobiology evidence pool.

**Overlap declaration.** Sources 1 to 5 are one broad astrobiology/prebiotic-chemistry pool. Ansari 2026 cites much of the same literature as Sasselov et al. and Herschy et al. Their agreement is one witness talking, not five independent confirmations (Fig 2 :40). The framework essays share the figure author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight. No world source was found that conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered; the field's state of uncertainty was kept as evidence for the union reading rather than used to flip it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the paper.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send to people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Recursive Self-Improvement Trap:** "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask for endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter, (2) the question of what the recipient would count as a failure, (3) their verdict recorded, (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority, (5) optionally the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## Two venue cautions

- Do not submit to OLEB. Its own society page records termination in 2023; the successor journal was listed as "TBD" when fetched. Discover Life is the live open-access journal in this space.
- ISSOL's contact page publishes named institutional emails. They are not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Use the public page and the conference and society routes, one to one.
