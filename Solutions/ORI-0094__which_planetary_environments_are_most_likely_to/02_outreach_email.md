# Outreach draft: check one separator before deciding which environment to study

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive the recipient's own check. If stage 1 fails, there is nothing else to look at.

---

**Subject:** A per-environment test, before anyone argues about Mars versus Europa

> Hello [name],
>
> I have one specific structural output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as posed:** which planetary environments are most likely to support prebiotic chemistry today? My claim is that the framework cannot answer that question as posed. It can return a per-environment separator and the shape a supporting environment must have. It cannot return a ranked list of names, and it does not try.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the astrobiology claims, `general` on the structure, not `grounded`):**
>
> 1. Favourability is not carried by a place. It is relative to a named prebiotic residual. The same UV flux that drives one pathway destroys another. So "which environment" must be asked once per pathway, with the pathway written down.
> 2. A global "most likely" score aggregates across slots, and the framework indexes the residual per slot. The live signal is the step that failed to resolve at one place and time, not an average.
> 3. The question is a union of overlapping conditions (habitat contact, chemistry closure, instrument reach), not a list of separate environments. One lever leaves the intersections standing.
> 4. Inside a slot, what matters is a difference that can be spent, a network over which influence travels, and the presence of catalysts, cycles and a functional group. An ingredient list of water, carbon and energy is not enough.
> 5. A laboratory reconstruction of a vent pore, a drying lake or an ice eutectic proves that a mechanism can work. It does not prove that a planet supplies that mechanism with the right actors and duration. A test harness is a substituted habitat.
>
> **What would refute the output, written before I sent this:**
> 1. a ranking of environments that stays invariant across two pathway residuals with demonstrably different requirements, measured at the ground;
> 2. an averaged multi-environment score that predicts detections correctly while per-slot scores fail;
> 3. a demonstrated pathway at the ground with no spendable gradient and no network;
> 4. a laboratory reconstruction that reproduces full planetary capability, including unchosen actors and second-order effects, with the planet-side measurement paying for the same prediction;
> 5. two slots with identical catalysts, cycles and functional groups over identical histories that yield different products with no unenumerated actor.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The loop in compact form (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - A recent open review of the field: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11084-026-09753-5
> - The comparative-planet paper this reasoning was checked against: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7002131/
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an environment you know well and can check against a measurement. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage 2 and stage 3

Stage 2 is opt-in. If the recipient returns a verdict on stage 1, record it **before** offering any framework material. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interior they are no longer an outside frame (Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.").

Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, not as an answer. Each user supplies their own ground and writes their own refuter first. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap warning applies to them as much as to me: the testable output "would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."
