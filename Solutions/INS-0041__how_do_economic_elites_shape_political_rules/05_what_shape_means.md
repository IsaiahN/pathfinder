## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INS-0041 the shape is: **a rule slot can move while the seat is fixed if an upstream variable moves, and there are at least three distinct kinds of upstream variable (habitat, convention, import).** That constrains any answer; it does not tell us which variable fired in a given case. "Elites shape rules by shaping the rules" is a filler, because it does not constrain anything.

The distinction matters because of the question's own structure. *The Library of Babel* again: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :54 agrees: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The shape is therefore a union of three mechanisms, not one mechanism with three names.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (OPENED): use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then *"connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with"*, and *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is how the three domains were found: start from "campaign finance", "lobbying", "media ownership" (words already in the question set, see question 43 in the same section), then notice that habitat, convention, and import are the recurring new terms they correlate with.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* This is why the world search used niche construction, Goodhart's law, regulatory capture, and agenda-setting rather than starting from a preferred theory of elites.
- Fig 9 :30 to :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect here is "the rule changed while the office holder did not", not "hegemony", "cultural capture", or "discourse coalition".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (INHERITED): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the rationale for the outreach email's stage 1.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (OPENED): *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The stop here is the shape plus refuter, not a named culprit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A discriminating instrument on a single rule slot would name which of habitat, convention, or import fired.
2. **A provenanced import with a shadow test** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51 to :52). *They're Scanning Books* (INHERITED): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Naming "cognitive capture" from regulatory-capture research is such an import; it must be recorded as coming from that frame, with its source and date, not presented as a figure derivation.

**Correction to my own §1.** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming any mechanism here. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Habitat, convention, and import are not primitives of the six-term set either, so naming them is a binding from an institutional-science frame, not a derivation. What is owed is provenance and a shadow test, not silence. Both the original claim and this correction are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why naming the mechanism from a policy reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (OPENED) gives the mechanism, metamerism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Apply this directly. Observing that a tax rule tracks donor preferences is a reading. Three sources could produce it:

| source | what would discriminate it |
|---|---|
| habitat introduction | new actors or conditions entered the rule's environment before the change, and the change persists after spending stops |
| convention authorship | the rule was previously a coordination device and became outcome-deciding; the change sits in what counts, not in who spoke |
| import into the decision frame | the decision frame's consider-able options changed on contact with a differing closure, with a provenanced source for the new measure |

The policy reading cannot tell these apart. Naming one is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the core methodological point of `09_watson_answer.md` section "What would settle it".

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED) adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A derivation that names its own mechanism from its own reading would be exactly that failure.

## 5. Convergence at low overlap is the legitimate corroboration

The RSI essay (OPENED) records the author's own precedent: of Ostrom's work on polycentric governance, *"The paper I derived and hers were not a carbon copy. They rhymed. I had arrived at the same conclusions from bacteria and biology and game agents. She had arrived at them from committees and fisheries and forests. ... Because we came from different fields, I was able to explain things she left vague, and vice versa."*

That is Fig 8 :50, *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* It is also the model for this solution's WORLD section: the ecology and metrology sources did not read the figure set, yet they predict the same shape. Their agreement is weak corroboration, not a verdict, and it is recorded with its field and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From the RSI essay (OPENED): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous scholars agreeing that elites rule is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried and let a ground settle it.

**Names carry standing.** Fig 10 :61: *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."* A prominent named theorist invoked to settle the mechanism is a stake, not an instrument. The outreach material therefore carries no named authority list.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the RSI essay (OPENED): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md`, and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the institutional question. They cannot settle it, and no agreement with them should be recorded as evidence about political rules.
