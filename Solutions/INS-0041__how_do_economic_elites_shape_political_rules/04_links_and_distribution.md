## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The field has a ground, and it is not the seat.** Gilens and Page test four theories in one model over 1,779 policy issues and find independent impacts for economic elites and business groups. That is evidence that influence enters policy without the elite holding every office. It corroborates the figure shape but does not decide it, because it shares no evidence pool with the figures and the figures are primary.
2. **A convention that allocates is no longer a convention.** Goodhart's law is the same arithmetic from metrology and accountability: once a measure is used to control, the regularity collapses. This is strong corroboration of Fig 10 :47, but it is one witness from economics and systems, not a second figure.
3. **The habitat is modified, not merely occupied.** Niche construction gives the biological version of Fig 11: organisms change the selection pressures they face, and the modification persists as ecological inheritance. It is why "introduction is available, exclusion is not" is a structural claim and not a metaphor.
4. **Capture can be cognitive before it is material.** Regulatory capture distinguishes bribery and revolving doors from the adoption of the industry's own representation by the regulator. That is Fig 6's import: the frame's wall moves on contact with a closure it did not author.
5. **Agenda control is upstream of the vote.** Agenda-setting research shows media influence on what is thought about and on the definitions used in future discussion. That is Fig 7's room selection. It does not prove capture; it supplies a low-overlap field in which the same shape has been measured.

**Overlap warning.** These five sources come from five different fields (political science, metrology and economics, evolutionary biology, economics of regulation, communication studies). They are deliberately low overlap. The three framework essays, by contrast, share an author with the figures and carry little independent weight; their agreement is expected.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 to :25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essays.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A rule that allocates public resources is high cost, so spend the effort on low-overlap frames, not on more names in one pool.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown and never pooled later as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula, "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, the output is a `specified` shape with one live strain (seat power versus capital-authored convention across strata), recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3b and `11_audit.md`. Do not present the strain as resolved; ask the recipient to test it.
