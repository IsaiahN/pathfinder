# The Tether's answer on elite influence over political rules

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A rule slot changes while the office that writes the rule stays in the same hands. The residual is registered per rule domain, never as one aggregate score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The question is not how much power elites have, but where the change enters the system. The contact failure is a **union compound**, not one hard lever: habitat, convention, and import overlap, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :56 to :58).

## 2. The shape

1. **The seat is downstream of what it ranges against.** A political seat predicts and rules from a habitat, a convention layer, and imported descriptions it did not author. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* The moment a coordinating convention systematically allocates outcomes, it is an outcome-deciding device wearing convention's clothes. This is why direct control of government is not needed: the rule is pre-authorised upstream.
2. **The available lever is introduction, not exclusion.** Fig 11 :61: *"The seat’s power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Wealth buys candidates, lobbyists, media outlets, expert bodies, and metrics into the habitat. It cannot subtract the public, because contact is a fact about the world. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so the phrase media ownership names a relation to a rule slot, not a fixed kind.
3. **Improving the seat in isolation changes nothing.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Replacing office holders while leaving habitat, convention, and imports fixed is an agent-only improvement. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
4. **Imports move the wall of what can be considered.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Expert definitions, technical metrics, and think-tank proposals are imports that add no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25) but give the frame access. Their provenance is the whole issue, because *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents"* (Fig 8 :51).
5. **Money is a filter until it becomes a verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Wealth legitimately decides what gets tried. It becomes the captured mechanism when it decides what counts as evidence, what is measurable, or which frame is credible, which is item 1 again at a different stratum.
6. **The three mechanisms are isomers, not one lever.** Over a shared institutional atom set they are *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"* (Fig 12 :117). They have distinct anchors (persistence, violability, shadow test) and a dependency order: habitat → convention → import → seat ruling (`06_isomers_and_order.md`). They form a union, so a single lever leaves the intersections standing.
7. **The mechanism is not named by the framework.** The shape names three upstream variables and the discriminators between them. Naming "culture", "discourse", or "cognitive capture" as *the* mechanism is a binding from a social-science frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation. Until a discriminator fires, the reading is a projection with lossy information (Hard Problem essay).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that economic elites are one actor with one preference. Residual per slot, not aggregated (Fig 1 :19).
- Not that wealth buys office directly. The shape says the office need not change hands.
- Not that every rule is captured, or that capture is always successful. A negative cycle (Fig 12 :120) is possible and is an empirical question.
- Not a conspiracy theory. Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."* The derivation is about upstream conditions and conventions, not hidden intent.
- Not a claim that regulation is always bad or that public interest theory is false. The derivation is structural, not normative.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here discriminates among the three isomers for any actual rule slot.
- Not that the framework is the only account of elite influence. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Habitat test:** did new actors or conditions enter the rule's environment before the change, and does the change persist after the spending stops? If yes, the habitat isomer fired.
- **Convention test:** was the rule previously a coordination device that became outcome-deciding, with a keeper who has an interest in its reading? Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
- **Import test:** did the decision frame's consider-able options change on contact with a differing closure, with a provenanced source and a shadow test (does the import explain a gap the frame could not state)?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4:** rule moves with all three upstream variables held fixed; an outcome-deciding convention with a disinterested keeper; an office-only improvement that moves the ground; one aggregate elite preference that predicts every slot.

The instrument owed is one that can run these three tests on the **same** rule slot, so that the projection is separated into its sources. Without it, "elites shaped the rule" is a green reading with an unstated source.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The frameworks' institutional vocabulary is a visible choice, not nature's own list. Another discipline's habitat need not be enumerated with these three categories. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. One live strain is kept visible: the seat's asymmetric power of introduction (Fig 11 :61) versus capital's power to author conventions (Fig 10 :47) may be the same power at two strata or two different powers. An instrument is owed, not an argument.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say economic elites run the government, and it does not name a culprit. It says the office that writes a rule is downstream of the habitat, the conventions, and the imported descriptions it ranges against, so a rule can move while the office holder stays the same. Wealth acts there: it adds actors and conditions to the habitat, it turns coordinating conventions into outcome-deciding rules, and it imports expert frames that change what a legislature can consider. Money is a budget filter until it is promoted into a verdict about what counts as evidence. These three moves are isomers over the same institutional atoms and must be examined in that dependency order, not averaged into one score. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, the instruments that would separate the three sources, and falsifiers written in advance. The mechanism in any particular case stays open until the ground settles it.
