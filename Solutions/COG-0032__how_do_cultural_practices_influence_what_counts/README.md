## Restatement as an effect

Two things are being decided and they are often run together. (1) For a given event E and candidate explanation phi, does phi range against the ground (a verdict question about the world)? (2) Is phi legible and acceptable to the people judging it (a convention question about shared meaning)? Each option leads somewhere different: option 1 leads to a measurement that neither judging community can move; option 2 leads to agreement within a community that is the product, not a corruption, of that community. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run, is a two-condition shape with two different anchors, not a single standard of convincingness. That binding is written down in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 and left to accrue a residual like any other term.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. (1) A room of agreeing readers is not an anchor: Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (2) Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". (3) Showing the framework before the verdict spends the reader's independence: Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
