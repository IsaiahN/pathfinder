## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before comparing with the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the systematic variation of "phi is convincing" with the judging community. Arity: at least three-place (phi, event, community), indexed per community. The primary's Fig 1 :19 prevents averaging, and I reach that independently from the slot rule. Symmetry: the judgment is a comparison against a standard and an alternative, so a threshold relation, not a symmetric join. Scale: individual, community, cross-community; the cross-community transform is lossy.
- **Domain 1, explanation as a term.** Actors: atoms, bonds, candidate term. Currency: cost plus leftover against prior residual. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, community convention.** Actors: members, shared vocabulary, legibility standards. Currency: agreement about shared meaning. Anchor: human judgment, because the question is constitutively about people. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, world verdict.** Actors: frames and instruments. Currency: contact and residual. Anchor: an outcome no frame can move. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, cross-community contact.** Actors: frames with differing closures. Currency: imports and head starts. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **Candidate X, one universal standard.** No anchor governs both senses without updating on frames. Not yet a domain.
- **Candidate Y, culture determines truth.** Anchor would update on frames. Refused.

**Union, not partition.** D2 and D3 overlap: an explanation can be legible and false, true and illegible, both, or neither. A case in two conditions is categorically different from either. **Order by dependency:** compose, then price, then judge legibility, then range against the ground. Refuse the false fusion before minting; describe the residual before choosing a frame.

My census matches the primary's four domains and both rejected candidates. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :36-41 (two anchors, correlation valence), Fig 5 :43 (the bargain), Fig 6 :23 (closure), Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 11 :39 (habitat).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: culture changes cost and closure, not the ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a specific claim about a specific community's explanatory standards.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **which sense of "convincing" a community is actually using** in any observed judgment, so cost effects and convention effects stay confounded at the point of measurement;
- **whether a convention has started deciding outcomes** (Fig 10 :47), which can flip a legitimate convention into a ground violation without changing its wording;
- **the `gap` on which practice moves which standard**, which the shorthand can appear to answer simply by naming a practice.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the two-anchor union and the rule that agreement has opposite valence in the two halves. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
