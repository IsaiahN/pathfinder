# The union, the order, and the analogues: supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5).

## RESIDUAL

The main audit found a two-condition union. Three supporting questions remain. (1) What is the *structure* of the compound, a partition or a union, and what does that decide? (2) What is the *order* of the two conditions, does legibility come before truth or the reverse? (3) What analogues does the figure set license, and which analogues must be declined? Each has its own falsifier.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: the two senses of "convincing" (V verdict, C convention), cost and leftover from Fig 5, closure from Fig 6, anchors from Fig 2. Bonds held: comparison (threshold between two quantities), sequence, and negation (refusal). No new primitive is available without import. The operative distinction is Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence": the same agreement relation carries opposite valence in D2 and D3.

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either".
- The two conditions overlap: an explanation can be legible and false, true and illegible, both, or neither. A single lever aimed at "convincingness" clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersection standing. A case that is both illegible and false is not a harder case of either; it is a compound.
- ⇒ **Union.** One loop per condition, never an average. Refuter: show that legibility and truth-for-the-world are the same measurement under a single anchor, so a change in one is a change in the other.

### Q2: What is the order?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- The dependency here runs cost and closure first, legibility second, verdict third. An explanation that cannot be composed in the community's Γ (D4) cannot be weighed for cost (D1), cannot be judged legible (D2), and cannot be tested against the ground (D3). Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A community without the terms cannot even formulate the candidate whose truth is in question.
- So legibility is *upstream* of verdict in practice, but **not** upstream in authority. A widely shared convention gives an explanation a hearing (a budget to be tried); it does not settle the verdict. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (verified): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
- The order is therefore a **composition order**, not a rank: compose the candidate first, then check its price, then its legibility, then send it to the ground. Handing a community the verdict before it can compose the explanation is a reading below the break (Fig 3 :23: "a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing.").
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a community reliably settles a world-directed verdict without first being able to compose the candidate explanation, the dependency order is wrong and Q2 fails.

### Q3: Which analogues are licensed, and which are declined?

Three analogues are licensed because the figure governing them is the same figure, applied at a different stratum.

1. **The court.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (verified) notes that courts rule on evidence "which neither side can move". This is Fig 2's anchor at the institutional stratum. Licensed.
2. **The emic/etic distinction.** The cross-cultural psychology entry defines the emic approach as studying behavior "from within the culture" and the etic approach "from outside the culture system, and is based on many cultures". This is Fig 9 :30-31 ("your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" "rather than the cause") at the methodological stratum. Licensed, with the pseudoetic failure as its named failure mode.
3. **Van Fraassen's contrast class.** The SEP entry on 20th century explanation records that an explanation answers a "why P rather than the alternatives X" question, where the contrast class X and the relevance relation R are fixed by context. This is Fig 2 :39's valence and Fig 11 :49's residual-relative valence at the philosophy-of-science stratum. Licensed as corroboration, not as a figure line.

Two analogues are declined.

1. **"Isomers" of explanation styles.** Fig 12 :117 defines an isomer as "same atoms, different bond, different substance". Two explanation traditions that share no atoms (different vocabularies, different closures) are not isomers; they are different substances with different atom sets. Calling them isomers would smuggle a shared Γ that was never established. Declined.
2. **"A single best explanation style."** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (verified): culture is a backdrop, not a set of beliefs, and "an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (Fig 11 :40). A universal style ranked by inherent quality has no anchor, so it is not a domain yet (census X1). Declined.

### Q4: What is the round-trip gap on this derivation?

- Sent up (x): the figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :36-41 (two anchors, correlation valence), Fig 5 :43 (the bargain), Fig 6 :23 (closure), Fig 9 :55 (union).
- Abstraction (T_A): "culture changes cost and closure, not the ground."
- Concretisation (T_E): a specific claim about a specific community's explanatory standards.

Three constraints do not survive the trip back down: which of the two senses a given community is actually using; whether a convention in that community has started deciding outcomes (Fig 10 :47); and whether the cost term is being confused with truth. The shorthand is therefore lossy, and `11_audit.md` records the gap `R_T` rather than assuming it is zero (Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").

## CONCLUSION

The compound is a **union**, not a partition. Its order is a **dependency order** (compose, price, judge legibility, then range against the ground) with legibility upstream in practice but zero authority over the verdict. Three analogues are licensed (court, emic/etic, contrast class) and two are declined (isomers of style, a single best style). The round trip is lossy in three named places. All four claims carry falsifiers above and are `specified`, not `grounded`.
