## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle the normative "should" by measurement. They constrain the shape of a defensible protocol; the ground settles any instance of it.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies their agreement.
2. **The checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never this derivation.
3. **A status is not a filler.** Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The honest output of an evidence-free question is a recorded status, not a guess dressed as an answer.
