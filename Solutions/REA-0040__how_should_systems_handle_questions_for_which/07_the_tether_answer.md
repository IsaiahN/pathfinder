# The Tether's answer: questions for which no evidence supports a definite answer

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the operational protocol, not `grounded`.** No measurement settles an "ought", so nothing here is grounded. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system is asked Q. Nothing in Γ or closure(Γ) settles Q against a ground. The system nevertheless emits a definite-looking answer, or a bare "unknown" that erases why nothing settled. The residual is the mismatch between the demand for an answer and the absence of a settling anchor. In effect terms: the system must return a **state of the world** (settled, unfound, unreachable, refused) and instead returns a sentence.

Fig 13 :134 makes the missing output explicit: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 9 :6 names it the first question: "the verdict: is φ* in here at all?".

## 2. The shape

1. **"No evidence" is a union, not one question.** Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The three conditions are: unfound at this reach, unreachable from any instrument, or a false premise. Their outputs are not isomers (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1), so one slot cannot hold them.

2. **Run SEARCHED first.** Formula step 7 names three questions, not one. Fig 6 :49: "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." SEARCHED is cheapest and the other two assume its answer. Distribute searchers independently: "searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names" (Fig 6 :52-53).

3. **Keep unreachable-at-budget and underivable-in-principle apart.** Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Fig 6 :26-27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." The first owes more search; the second owes an import or an honest stop. Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Formula step 7).

4. **INWARD needs an edge; OUTWARD needs effect terms.** Fig 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 6 :31: "Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other." OUTWARD describes the residual first (Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit") and carries a source gate and a shadow test.

5. **A false premise gets a refusal, not an explanation.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Fig 5 :55: "What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach." Refusal is negation ¬, a unary operator and "not a bond" (Fig 12 amendment). A vocabulary without it "can only accumulate" (Operators table :57-58).

6. **The output is a status with a mode, a refuter and a lineage.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A room of agreeing frames cannot supply the missing evidence. The report says which condition fired, what was tried, what would settle it, and what would reverse it.

7. **Do not mint a filler.** Fig 5 :41: "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting." Fig 13 :134-135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The framework returns a shape. A name needs a ground reading or a provenanced import.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "say something plausible". Confidence is not evidence; a fluent output is not a settled term.
- Not "always say unknown". A bare status is a lossy condensation (F10) that throws away the operation owed next.
- Not "search harder". Idempotence (Fig 8 :32) and the proto-instrument rule (Fig 6 :34-35) forbid treating persistence as a remedy by default.
- Not "import any frame that answers". A frame with no off-switch and no anchor is not a ground; Fig 6 :23 requires differing closure and Fig 8 requires provenance.
- Not "agreement settles it". Fig 7 :13, "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not".
- Not `grounded`. This is a `specified` protocol; its validity as an "ought" is not a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The ground here is an instance-level check, not a general measurement of an "ought". For a given question Q:

1. Is there a ground that settles Q and does not move when frames change (Fig 2 :29)? If yes, Q was not evidence-free.
2. Does the answer lie in closure(Γ) and simply remain unfound? Distribute independent searchers; if one finds a settled term, SEARCHED was the right first move.
3. Is any instrument already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35)? If yes, INWARD is owed; sharpen from that reading.
4. Does a frame with differing closure predict the residual's shape, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :12-13)? If yes, OUTWARD is owed.
5. Is the premise false? If yes, write the refusal and remove the premise's standing to be composed over.
6. Is the channel stale (Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does")? Then report "lost touch", not a verdict about the world.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: a ground that settles the supposedly evidence-free question; a closure that changes under repeated search with the same atoms; an instrument built from a description alone; a false premise withdrawn without negation; agreement among overlapping frames that predicts the ground as well as an anchored instrument; a one-word status with zero round-trip gap.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The protocol stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. From inside a frame a closed channel and a perfect prediction look the same (Formula "Closure"), so channel maintenance is part of the deliverable, not an afterthought. "A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39), and the limit recorded in the canonical figures README that indiscernibility is not identity. No ruling was requested and none is claimed: the figures are the ground for this design question (`60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`).

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not tell a system to invent an answer, and it does not reduce every hard question to "unknown". It says that "no evidence" is a union of at least three conditions, so split it before searching; that the cheapest check comes first, namely more independent search at the same reach, because closure is idempotent and no amount of it adds an atom; that unreachable-at-this-budget and underivable-in-principle are different facts with different instructions, and that a new instrument must grow from one already returning something that fails to resolve; that a false premise is owed a refusal rather than an explanation, since negation is a unary operator and not a bond; and that the honest output is a mode-labelled status with a refuter, a lineage and a measured round-trip gap, never a filler name. Agreement among frames cannot supply the missing evidence. The status is a verdict, not a silence, and the answer stops at the limit of the instruments with the lineage recorded so a checker can locate the error.
