## 0. The claim under audit

> The observed distribution of income and wealth can be divided into a share that reflects differences in skill and effort and a share that reflects inheritance, bargaining power, discrimination and market structure. The shares are additive and a policy can target the larger one.

The first pass wanted to find the right percentages. That reading is retracted in §6 and replaced. The claim actually under audit is the **form** of the question: that the six named sources form a partition of one outcome with an assignable shared fraction.

**Residual stated in effect terms.** A reader wants a number that tells them which lever to pull. The effect they need is: for a given person or household, which changes to the conditions around them would change their outcome, and by how much, measured against an anchor that does not move when the reader changes their mind about which source is important. What each option leads to:

- **Partition with shares (Option A).** Produces a decision rule, but must assign a shared region to one name. The assignment is a convention, not a measurement.
- **Union with per-slot verdicts (Option B).** Produces a set of lever-specific tests and no universal percentage. The shared region stays visible and unassigned.

**Shape bound before the run (Formula "Direction"):** per-slot residual, union not partition, per-domain anchor, an instrument condition for source discrimination, and a declared `gap` where no figure returns a share. Bound here so the preference is refusable at the end.

## 1. Figure census

### 1.1 The residual R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** One outcome per slot (person or household), six named candidate sources (skill, effort, inheritance, bargaining power, discrimination, market structure). This is a many-to-one map, not a two-term ratio. Symbols :43: *"One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."*
- **Symmetry.** The question is written with a symmetric conjunction: "how much X, and how much Y". Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The six sources are not interchangeable operands. Inheritance is a head start sent down (Fig 4 :11-12), bargaining power is a relation (Fig 11 :47), skill and effort belong to the agent. Operators :16 describes the flat formula: *"the only bond in a flat formula, which is why a formula records ingredients and not arrangement"*. The symmetric phrasing is a notation error, not a finding about the world.
- **Scale.** First order is the slot. Second order is the distribution built over slots (a Gini, a variance share). Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* Aggregating the two scales is the move Fig 1 :22 forbids: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A person can be low-skilled **and** inherit little **and** face a discriminating employer **and** live in a thin local labour market. The intersections are the common case, not an edge case. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step"*.

1. Perception: measure the outcome per slot (Fig 1 :19).
2. Vocabulary: have terms for what each condition affords, including the bond between them (Fig 12 :5).
3. Objective: say what would count as progress, as a lever-specific counterfactual (Fig 3 :10).
4. Planning: identify an instrument that changes one source while holding the others fixed (D4).
5. Learn and carry: settle a verdict per slot and carry the method, not the recording (Fig 4 :22).

The question as asked jumps from step 1 to a percentage. The middle link, step 3, is the one with no instrument in most of this literature. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The six sources are a union, not a partition (decisive for the form)

Fig 9 :54-57, quoted above, and Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset,"**
> **"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

A skill-only regression and a structure-only regression can each work well on a different subset. That is the signature of several well-formed questions, not one hard allocation problem. The shared fraction cannot be assigned by looking at the outcome, because the outcome is one reading produced by several paths. This is the metamerism point below (F4).

World corroboration at low overlap: the Great Gatsby Curve literature records that the country is often taken as the unit of analysis and that the resulting correlation may be an artifact of that aggregation. Durlauf, Kourtellos and Tan 2022 (*Annual Review of Economics*) is the cited review; the public summary records the Mankiw objection that grouping persistently rich Connecticut with persistently poor Mississippi, or Germany with Greece, manufactures the pattern. That is a world witness for "aggregation creates artifacts", not a ground reading of this question.

**Status: triangulated** across Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 1 :19/:22/:28, Fig 12 :5/:94 and the low-overlap aggregation objection. The figures have low overlap: one governs union structure, one governs per-slot indexing, one governs the bond. Agreement is not from one pool.

### F2 - The residual must stay per slot, not per distribution

Fig 1 :19 and :22 quoted above. Fig 1 :28:

> **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**

A national Gini is a useful filter for deciding where to spend research budget. It is not a witness about any person. Symbols :38-40 describes `R` as *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

The practical consequence: a verdict about inheritance must be a verdict about a slot or a class of slots, not a coefficient on a national aggregate. The Great Gatsby Curve's intergenerational elasticity, reported as 0.47 for the United States in the public summary, is an average over a unit (the country) chosen before the question was asked. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The unit of analysis is a frame choice, and it must be described, not assumed.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 1, Fig 4 :19-20 on scale, Symbols table).

### F3 - Skill and effort are a generator; inheritance is a head start, not a method

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :23-24:

> **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**
> **"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."**

Skill acquired and carried is a method. Effort is an action. Inherited wealth and inherited rank are head starts that make the next search cheaper, and they are not methods, because the receiver did not re-derive them. Conflating the two is the concrete error behind the phrase "skill and effort explain X percent, inheritance explains Y percent": it puts a generator and an initial condition in the same additive list.

Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

So even the generator half is not an agent-only quantity. A training program that raises measured skill without changing the person's contact with a paying market has changed the agent and not the outcome. That is a `specified` prediction with a clear refuter (F10 below), not a ground reading.

World corroboration at low overlap: the ethology entry on dominance hierarchies distinguishes *"Dominance is an individual's preferential access to resources over another based on coercive capacity based on strength, threat, and intimidation, compared to prestige (persuasive capacity based on skills, abilities, and knowledge)."* That is a non-economics field separating coercive power from skill-based standing, and adding that *"Rank may also be acquired from maternal dominance rank"*. It corroborates the shape (bargaining power is a relation, inheritance is a head start) without measuring human income.

**Status: single-source on the figure side (Fig 4), with low-overlap world corroboration.**

### F4 - Naming a source from the outcome reading is a metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*
> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Color metamerism is the physical instance: in colorimetry *"metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions"*, and the way to resolve it in industry is a spectrometer or microspectroscopy, not an argument about which pigment was probably used. Identifiability in statistics states the same limit formally: *"A model is identifiable if it is theoretically possible to learn the true values of this model's underlying parameters after obtaining an infinite number of observations from it"*; when it fails, *"two or more parametrizations are observationally equivalent"*.

An income observation is a tristimulus reading. Skill-led, inheritance-led, discrimination-led and structure-led histories can produce the same reading. Without a source-discriminating instrument, assigning the reading to one source is a projection error. The instrument is **owed**, which is why D4 has no anchor yet.

Fig 6 :34 quoted above and Fig 3 :27 (*"A link with no instrument is not a link that works"*) govern this. Instrumental variables is the economics-frame attempt at such an instrument: it requires a variable that changes the explanatory variable but *"has no independent effect on the dependent variable"* (the exclusion restriction), and *"Since U is unobserved, the requirement that Z be independent of U cannot be inferred from data and must instead be determined from the model structure"*. That last sentence is exactly the import's source gate (Fig 8 :52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*).

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 6 :34, Fig 3 :27, Fig 8 :52, plus three low-overlap fields: colour science, statistics, econometrics).

### F5 - A variance share is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the cause

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 1 :23-27:

> **"The ground enters as the only metric."**
> **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A regression's R-squared, a Shapley decomposition of explained variance, and a between-group variance share are all quantities the analyst's own frame produces. They can rank which slot to investigate next. They cannot settle which cause produced the slot's outcome.

Shapley value regression is the most principled version of this allocation: *"Shapley value regression is a statistical method used to measure the contribution of individual predictors in a regression model... the 'gain' is the total explained variance or predictive power of the model."* Its four axioms are *"widely accepted as defining a fair distribution"*. That is the point. Fairness of an allocation rule is a convention. Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**. Once the Shapley share is used to decide who receives a transfer, it is a verdict wearing a convention's clothes, and it needs an anchor the frame cannot move.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 9 :60, Fig 1 :23-27, Fig 10 :47, with a mathematics witness for the convention reading).

### F6 - Discrimination and market structure are habitat relations, not actor properties

Fig 11 :45-48:

> **"actors: what acts on it, and what it acts on"**
> **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**
> **"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"**
> **"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."**

A discriminating employer, a segregated school district, a thin local labour market, and an occupational licensing rule are relations and conditions in the habitat, enumerated rather than composed. Fig 11 :49-51:

> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**
> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

This is why the question's standing list of six sources cannot carry fixed signs. A union rule can be a head start for an insider and a barrier to an outsider. The same condition changes valence when the residual changes.

World corroboration, low overlap: the ethology entry records that *"Dominance varies, too, depending on the context or resource, and on group size"* and that in vervet monkeys *"high-ranking females have higher foraging success when the food resources are clumped, but when food is distributed throughout an area they lose their advantage"*. That is residual-relative valence observed in another field.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 11 :45-51 plus animal behaviour).

### F7 - The human attribution of outcomes is biased toward the dispositional source

Fig 2 :39-41:

> **"Correlation has valence."**
> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
> **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**

Within-economics agreement that "skill and effort are the main driver" is efficient for a convention (a shared way of talking about merit) and worthless as a verdict about the world, because the agreeing frames share the same training and the same outcome data. This is not a claim that the skill explanation is false. It is a claim that agreement cannot make it true.

Human cognition supplies a mechanism for the one-sided reading. The fundamental attribution error is *"a cognitive attribution bias in which observers underemphasize situational and environmental factors for the behavior of an actor while overemphasizing dispositional or personality factors"*. The entry also records a real criticism: a 2006 meta-analysis found the weight of evidence does not support the first form, though it does not contradict the second. That conflict is recorded honestly, does not flip the figure verdict, and is returned to the census as evidence that attribution is a measurement problem, not a settled cognitive law.

The Great Gatsby Curve entry records the matching cultural split as a political-economy equilibrium: in an "American Dream" equilibrium *"the society believes that income is determined by effort and social mobility is high"*, while in a "Euro-pessimism" equilibrium *"the society believes that luck, birth, connections, and corruption are the major determinants of income"*. Beliefs about source are part of the habitat, and they feed back into behaviour. Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

**Status: single-source on the figure side (Fig 2), with low-overlap cognition witness and an economics witness conflict recorded.**

### F8 - "How much" is a comparison, and six shares are a composition whose bond is unrecorded

Operators table :51: **"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"**. Fig 12 :89: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."* A "how much X, how much Y" question is a `⋛` comparison between two quantities, not a sum over six.

Fig 12 :5 and :94:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
> **"The arrangement is the substance."**

A list of six sources with six percentages is a flat formula. It records the ingredients and not the arrangement (skill enables an action; inheritance changes the starting slot; bargaining power changes the split of an existing surplus; discrimination changes the reception of the same action). Same six ingredients, different bond, different substance. Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

The fixed-atom condition is not met by the question as written, because the list mixes agent properties, head starts, relations and conditions. `06_isomers_and_order.md` states the stronger, checkable form and its falsifier.

**Status: triangulated** (Operators, Fig 12 :5/:94/:117, Fig 9 :60).

### F9 - Two currencies: the description cost and the action cost of a share do not add

Fig 12 :125-127:

> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."**
> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"**
> **"and will not notice the shortfall. It proposes arrangements it cannot afford to test, and the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all."**

A decomposition that is cheap to state (one number per source) can take a whole research budget to settle, and a decomposition that is expensive to describe may be cheap to refute. The question asks for the cheap description as if it were also a settled result. Fig 5 :26: *"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"* The six shares do not, because the confusion they remove is the shared region they cannot assign.

**Status: single-source on the figure side (Fig 12), triangulated with Fig 5 and Fig 9 :60.**

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*. The author's stake is a preferred structural reading; the refuter is the instrument that makes that stake harmless.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is produced that returns a numerical share for a named source at a given slot, with the shared fraction uniquely assigned (breaks the `gap`).
2. A source-discriminating instrument is demonstrated that maps an outcome difference to one source while holding the other five fixed at the slot, with a non-updating anchor and a stated exclusion restriction (breaks F4).
3. The six sources are shown to be a partition whose intersection terms have zero mass across the population of interest (breaks F1).
4. An anchor is shown to update on the analyst's preferred source and still settle a verdict about the source (breaks Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."*).
5. The per-slot residual can be aggregated into one global number without losing the signal that says where to look (breaks Fig 1 :22).
6. Two of the named conditions can be written over the same fixed atom set with only a difference of bond and yet produce identical outcome distributions, so the isomer caution in `06` is withdrawn (breaks F8's live strain).

Until one fires, no part of this may be called `grounded`. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The `gap` is a verdict about what this closure can return, not a claim that the percentage does not exist in the world.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Drop the partition question in its current form. Replace it with a per-slot union question: which condition, changed at this slot, moves the outcome against a ground that does not update on the analyst's preference (F1, F2, F7).
2. State the mode on every claim (F10). The honest form of the headline is: *"Under this closure, the sources form a union with an unassignable shared fraction; a universal allocation ratio is a `gap`, not a discovered number."*
3. Price the instrument before the decomposition. Ask what observation would discriminate inheritance from discrimination at one slot, and refuse to name a source until it exists (F4, Fig 3 :27).
4. Treat Shapley or variance shares as budget filters only, and record that use explicitly (F5).
5. Enumerate the habitat for each slot rather than composing a list of six causes (Fig 11 :43-48).
6. Send the generator, not the recording. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter.
