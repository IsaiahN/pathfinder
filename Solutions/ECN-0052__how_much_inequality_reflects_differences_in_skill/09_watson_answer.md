## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when asked to split an observed inequality distribution among skill and effort, inheritance, bargaining power, discrimination and market structure. Option A: return additive shares and let a reader act on the larger one. Option B: treat the request as a union-of-overlapping-conditions question, keep the residual per person or household slot, give a separate verdict and anchor per condition, and report the universal percentage as a `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a decision rule that silently assigns an unmeasurable shared region to one name and promotes a convention into a verdict; B produces a checkable shape, lever-specific refuters, and an honest description of the instrument still owed. This run derives B. The shape was bound before the run in `README.md`, so it can be refused by the same test as anything else.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** One outcome per slot, six named candidate sources. Arity: many-to-one, not a two-term ratio. Symmetry: the question phrases the sources as interchangeable operands (a flat `+`), while the actual relations are typed and ordered (head start, method, action, coercive relation, reception relation, condition); the symmetric phrasing is a notation error. Scale: first order is the person or household slot; second order is the distribution (Gini, variance share) built over slots. Symbols :43 "One object, region or entity being tracked. R is measured per slot, never as a single global number." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Domain 1, measured outcome.** Actors: persons and households as slots. Currency: income, consumption, wealth. Anchor: tax records, surveys, market transactions, which settle what happened but not why. Type: per-slot measurement, not a domain of causes.
- **Domain 2, generator.** Actors: the agent, with skill as method and effort as action. Currency: a method that crosses, an action that fires. Anchor: a performance that pays against a task or market ground. Type: partition boundary question about what crosses the membrane.
- **Domain 3, habitat.** Actors: conditions and relations, family, schools, neighbourhoods, firms, employers, rules. Currency: contact, enumeration, cascades. Anchor: the habitat read off the world, which updates. Type: union of overlapping conditions with residual-relative valence.
- **Domain 4, attribution and identification.** Actors: analysts, instruments, excluded sources. Currency: residual and exclusion. Anchor: a source-discriminating instrument, which is **not held**, so this is not yet a domain with a nameable anchor. Fig 6 :34 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Recorded as `gap`.
- **Domain 5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Measure per slot; have terms for the bonds; state a lever-specific objective; identify an instrument; settle and carry a method. The question as posed jumps from measurement to a percentage and skips the objective and instrument links.

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :51 (no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual) initially seemed to forbid naming any source. Re-census: Fig 11 governs actor valence relative to a residual, not the prohibition of naming conditions. Kept as a constraint on fixed signs. The fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim remains one live strain (`06` Q1 and Q3).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The requested object is refused; the shape survives. Inequality's residual belongs to a person or household slot, so an aggregate share is a filter for the budget and never a witness for a source. Skill is a generator and inheritance a head start, so they are different bond types, not comparable entries in one additive list. Bargaining power, discrimination and market structure are habitat relations and conditions whose valence is relative to the residual. The six sources form a union of overlapping conditions with an unassignable shared fraction; the intersection is a specific compound with its own loop. Attribution needs a source-discriminating instrument with a non-updating anchor, an exclusion restriction justified from the model structure, and provenance; without it, naming a source from an income reading reads a projection as a source. No figure returns a universal percentage, so the share is a declared `gap` of the unreached kind. Mode: `specified` on those per-domain claims, `general` on the union and per-slot structure, `gap` on the share, ungrounded throughout. One live strain (the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim) is named in `06` and refuter 6 of `01`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are used. The real cost is promoting a convention into a verdict: a Shapley or variance share used to decide a transfer without a source-discriminating anchor. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong", so the remedy is to buy a discriminator before deciding, not to gather more agreement. A second cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework before the residual; that is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn if the separator is wrong. The allocation convention can be demoted back to a filter, the unit of analysis can be re-declared, and the instrument condition can be met or left open. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with the correction visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a figure line returning a slot-level share with the shared fraction assigned; an instrument that changes one source at one slot while holding the others fixed against an anchor that does not update on the analyst's preference and with an exclusion restriction from the model structure; a demonstration that the six conditions are a partition with zero-mass intersections; an anchor that updates on preference and still settles a source verdict; or a collapse of the per-slot residual into a global number with no signal loss. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
