## 2. Wider literature actually opened, by low-overlap field

These are corroboration only. They are ordered by how little they share with the economics evidence pool.

1. **Perception and measurement, colour science.** *Metamerism (color)*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. *"In colorimetry, metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions."* The entry records that industrial matches are resolved with a spectrometer or microspectroscopy, not with argument. This is the physical instance of the source-discrimination problem. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metamerism_(color)
2. **Statistics and system identification.** *Identifiability*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. *"A model is identifiable if it is theoretically possible to learn the true values of this model's underlying parameters after obtaining an infinite number of observations from it."* When it fails, *"two or more parametrizations are observationally equivalent"*. This is the formal version of the projection error. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identifiability
3. **Animal behaviour and ethology.** *Dominance hierarchy*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. Separates *"Dominance is an individual's preferential access to resources over another based on coercive capacity based on strength, threat, and intimidation, compared to prestige (persuasive capacity based on skills, abilities, and knowledge)"*, records maternal rank inheritance, and shows rank advantage changing with resource distribution. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominance_hierarchy
4. **Human cognition.** *Fundamental attribution error*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. *"observers underemphasize situational and environmental factors for the behavior of an actor while overemphasizing dispositional or personality factors"*. The entry's own criticism of the first form of the bias is recorded in `01` F7. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamental_attribution_error
5. **Mathematics and game theory.** *Shapley value*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. The allocation rule with four axioms, and *"Shapley value regression is a statistical method used to measure the contribution of individual predictors in a regression model"*. Corroborates the reading that a fair allocation is a convention. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shapley_value
6. **Econometrics.** *Instrumental variables*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. *"Since U is unobserved, the requirement that Z be independent of U cannot be inferred from data and must instead be determined from the model structure"*. Corroborates the source gate. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instrumental_variables
7. **Economics of inequality and mobility.** *Great Gatsby Curve*, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23. Durlauf, Kourtellos and Tan 2022 is the cited review. The entry records the aggregation objection and the "American Dream" versus "Euro-pessimism" belief equilibria. Same broad evidence pool as the question, so it is not an independent witness. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Gatsby_curve

**Overlap declared.** Sources 1 to 4 are genuinely low overlap with economics; 5 and 6 are mathematical and statistical method sources with partial overlap; 7 is the economics pool itself. The two framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight. No world source was taken to overrule a figure line.
