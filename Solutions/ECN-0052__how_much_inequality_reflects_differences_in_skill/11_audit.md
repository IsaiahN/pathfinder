## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 4 :22 (method versus head start), Fig 10 :47 (convention versus verdict), Fig 13 :134 (gap as verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: "union, not partition; per slot; generator versus head start; filter not witness; source discrimination owed; percentage is a gap".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the policy-facing form this shorthand would take if a reader tried to turn it into an allocation rule: a percentage per source applied to a national distribution.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim (Fig 12 :117 requires the same atoms; the question mixes kinds, so the strong claim is not well formed as written);
- the **source discriminator** (Fig 3 :27 and Fig 6 :34); the shorthand names the instrument's shape but cannot build it, and a reader can easily hear "gap" as "leave it blank" rather than "instrument owed";
- the **non-updating anchor** (Fig 2 :33); a reader can substitute an endogenous outcome and still call it an anchor, which turns the whole separator into coherent drift;
- the **convention/verdict boundary** (Fig 10 :47); a reader can take the filter and use it to decide transfers, which is exactly the collapse the shorthand warns against.

One thing survives cleanly: the residual is per slot and the sources are a union of overlapping conditions with residual-relative valence. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
