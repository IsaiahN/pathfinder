# The Tether's answer to "how much inequality reflects skill versus inheritance, power and structure?"

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal percentage. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A reader observes an unequal distribution and wants a number that tells them which lever to pull. The figure-grounded response is that the number being requested is the wrong object. The residual is not "what fraction of the Gini is skill?" It is: for a given person or household slot, which change to which condition would move that slot's outcome against an anchor that does not move when the reader changes their preferred explanation.

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

The six named sources are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors and residual-relative valence, not a partition with an assignable shared fraction. That is the whole structural answer. There is no universal percentage, and reporting that absence is a verdict rather than a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

Verdicts are kept separate. They are never averaged.

**D1, the measured outcome.** The outcome is measurable (tax records, surveys, market transactions) and can serve as an anchor for *what happened*. It cannot serve as an anchor for *why*, because one outcome reading can be produced by several source histories. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Verdict: `specified`, anchored for the outcome, unanchored for the source.

**D2, the generator.** Skill is a method that can cross a membrane; effort is an action. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* But Fig 11 :39-40: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* So a skill improvement that does not change contact with a paying market is not a lever. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4 plus Fig 11 plus the ethology witness), ungrounded.

**D3, the habitat.** Inheritance is a head start, not a method. Bargaining power is a coercive relation; discrimination is a reception relation; market structure is a condition. Fig 11 :45-47 enumerates actors, conditions and relations, and :49-51 makes valence relative to the residual. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 4, Fig 11, ethology), ungrounded.

**D4, attribution and identification.** This is the bottleneck. The instrument that would change one source while holding the others fixed at a slot, with an anchor that does not update on the analyst's preference, is not held. Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** Naming a source from the outcome reading is a metamerism error: the reading reports the projection, not the source. Verdict: `gap`, with the instrument's shape described. Triangulated (Fig 3, Fig 6, colour science, identifiability, instrumental variables).

**D5, verification.** Agreement among economists is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict (Fig 2 :39-41). A larger literature is a room, not an anchor (Fig 7 :35). Verdict: `general`, structural.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not a percentage.** No figure line returns an allocation ratio, and no variance decomposition or Shapley share is promoted into one.
- **Not that skill and effort are unimportant.** A generator that cannot reach a ground changes nothing; that is a falsifiable prediction, not a dismissal.
- **Not that discrimination or market structure are the hidden main cause.** Their valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49-51).
- **Not that the six sources are six of anything.** They mix methods, actions, head starts, relations and conditions (Fig 4, Fig 11, Fig 12 :46).
- **Not that a decomposition is wrong.** A decomposition is a fair and useful convention until it decides an outcome, at which point it needs an anchor (Fig 10 :47).
- **Not that public agreement would settle anything.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
- **Not grounded.** Nothing here was measured. Every substantive claim is `specified` or `general`, and the share is a `gap`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not more decomposition

The missing object is a source-discriminating instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the colour case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The instrument's shape, in effect terms:

1. **Slot-specific.** It changes one condition at one person or household slot and observes that slot's outcome (Fig 1 :19).
2. **Non-updating anchor.** The outcome it ranges against is not affected by what the analyst believes about the source (Fig 2 :33, *"The anchor must not update."*).
3. **Exclusion.** It has no independent path to the outcome other than through the source it moves. The instrumental-variables entry states this as the exclusion restriction and adds that it *"cannot be inferred from data and must instead be determined from the model structure"*.
4. **Provenance and shadow test.** If it is imported from another frame, its source and the gap it explains are recorded (Fig 8 :52).
5. **Refutable.** Its failure condition is written down before the run (Fig 10 :59).

Standing falsifiers from `01` §3: a figure line that returns a share; an instrument meeting the five conditions above; a demonstration that the six sources are a partition with zero-mass intersections; an anchor that updates on preference and still settles a source verdict; a collapse of the per-slot residual into a global number with no signal loss.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The discriminator is absent from this closure, not proved impossible. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :136: **"It can say the question is not one question and must be split before it is searched again."** The split is owed before the search: one loop per condition, one anchor per loop, and the intersections treated as their own compound (Fig 9 :57).

One live strain remains: the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim in `06` Q1 and Q3. It is named, with a falsifier, not smoothed. The author's stake in the structural reading is declared under Fig 10 :59 (interest in the outcome is unavoidable; interest in the reading is disqualifying).

## 6. What would have to be true for the answer to differ

- A figure line governing allocation shares exists and was missed. This is SEARCHED first (Formula step 7): the closure has been swept, and the sweep is recorded in `01` §1.5.
- INWARD: the instrument can be sharpened from something already returning a reading. The variance decomposition and the local identified effect are already returning something, so this route is live and is the cheapest next step.
- OUTWARD: another frame already holds the discriminator. Colour science (spectrometry), statistics (identifiability) and econometrics (instrumental variables) each hold a piece. Each import needs a source gate and a shadow test, and is debited against independence. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and were not read against each other. Importing a percentage against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :42-43).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a percentage split of inequality into skill, inheritance, bargaining power, discrimination and market structure. It returns the shape any admissible answer must have. The residual belongs to a person or household, not to a national aggregate, so a Gini or a variance share is a filter for where to look, not a witness for what caused it. The named sources form a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a partition with an assignable shared fraction: the same person can be short of skill, short of inheritance, face a discrimination barrier and sit in a thin labour market at once, and that intersection is its own compound. Skill is a generator and inheritance is a head start, so they are different bond types and cannot be added as comparable shares. The bottleneck is an instrument that changes one source while holding the others fixed against an anchor that does not update on the analyst's preference; without it, naming a source from an income reading reads a projection as if it were the source. What the answer offers is that separator, its four refuters written in advance, and the description of the missing instrument. The universal percentage stays a declared gap until the ground settles it.
