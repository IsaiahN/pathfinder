## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the missing part. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0052 the shape is not a list of six percentages. It is a set of constraints that any admissible allocation answer must satisfy:

- the residual is per slot, not per national aggregate (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the sources form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57);
- the verdicts are per domain and are never averaged (Fig 12 :125-127 says the currencies do not add);
- an allocation that decides an outcome is a verdict, not a convention (Fig 10 :47);
- an attribution without a discriminator is a projection read as a source (essay 7);
- the universal share is a `gap`, reported as unreached rather than filled (Fig 13 :134).

A percentage is a number. The shape is the reason no number is returned yet.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So we describe the residual as "an outcome reading produced by several paths", not as "a Gini coefficient caused by skill". The words "skill" and "structure" are borrowed from the economics frame; the effect is source-indistinguishability.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Translated here: say what you would expect to observe if a source-specific lever existed, before asserting the lever.
- *Library of Babel* (carried) on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the separator is the finish line: once you can say which condition to change at which slot, the question of a universal share dissolves.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, that means a discriminator that changes one source while holding the others fixed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The ethology distinction between coercive dominance and skill-based prestige is such an import. Its provenance is recorded, so it is a binding with a source, not an invented primitive.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six sources named in the question are a visible choice of vocabulary, not nature's own list. Naming one of them as *the* source turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The governing error for this question: source read off a projection

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly 2026-11-23:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."*
> *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

An income number is the green reading. Skill-led, inheritance-led, discrimination-led and market-structure-led histories are the yellow plus blue, the RGB, the CMYK and the scheme not yet discovered. An instrument that reports the income and then names the source has exceeded its measurement. This is not an analogy imported from colour science for decoration: the physical case is the same structure, and the physical remedy is the same kind of remedy, a spectrometer rather than a further argument. The colour-science entry confirms that industrial metamerism is resolved by a measuring device, and the identifiability entry states the general condition under which sources are *"observationally equivalent"*.

Consequently: the universal percentage is not the missing primitive. The **missing thing** is a discriminator. Its shape is "something that changes one source without moving the others at one slot, read against an anchor that does not update". We can describe that shape. We cannot name it, and no figure supplies one.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first drafted §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming skill, inheritance or market structure at all. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Skill, effort, inheritance, bargaining power, discrimination and market structure are all already available terms in the economics frame. Naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto the residual's habitat, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains wrong is two other moves:

- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*);
- naming the source of an income reading without an instrument that discriminates sources (the metamerism error of §4).

Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The correction does not restore the percentage. It narrows the reason the percentage is refused.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly 2026-11-23:

> *"Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)"*
> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A decomposition produced by a famous economist gets a budget to be tested; it does not get to be true. A list of prestigious signatories to a statement about the "true drivers of inequality" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a discriminator and a refuter, not endorsements.

## 7. My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any inequality claim, and it must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on. The Journal of Economic Inequality's own LLM policy (see `04`) is a world-side instance of the same rule: the tool can assist, it cannot be the accountable author of a verdict.

## 8. One-paragraph consequence

For this question, "shape" means: the residual of an outcome is read per person or household; the named sources form a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors and residual-relative valence; verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into a percentage; an allocation of shared variance is a convention until an instrument with a source gate settles it; and the universal share is a declared `gap`. The framework returns the form any admissible answer must have, plus the instrument owed to close it. It does not return the number, and it does not ask to be trusted before being checked.
