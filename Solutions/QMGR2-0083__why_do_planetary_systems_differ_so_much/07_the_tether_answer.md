## 1. The residual it answers

The spread of planetary architectures and compositions is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5: "Split it rather than search."). It has at least three overlapping conditions: initial-condition variance, in-disk stochastic processing, and selection by dynamical survival and detection. A single lever clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57). Each condition gets its own loop and its own ground; verdicts below are kept separate and never averaged.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one cause. No single variable is named as deciding the whole spread, and Option A in `README.md` is retained only as a refuter target.
- Not a derivation of astrophysics. Core accretion, migration and chaos in planetary dynamics are physics results arrived at independently; the framework describes their shape, it did not produce them.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room." A final answer to planetary diversity would be a terminal room, and the framework rules those out.
- Not an isotropic random draw. The variation being arranged is biased by the generator; the framework's own randomness is a remainder compounding through an amplifying chain, not a uniform sample.
- Not `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument was run here, and `left(R,φ)` at the population level was not measured.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Invertibility test:** a mapping from observed architecture and composition back to unique initial disk conditions, validated across more than one system. If it succeeds, the "cannot name the cause" claim falls.
- **Union test:** one variable that accounts for both arrangement and composition across the full population including the intersections, with no compound left. If it succeeds, the split instruction falls.
- **Selection test:** a population whose generated and observed distributions are identical with no selection or detection work. If it succeeds, selection-between stops doing work.
- **Habitat test:** a residual whose causal habitat closes at direct contact with no second-order effect mattering. If it succeeds, the cascade enumeration requirement falls.
- **Standing figure refuters:** derive one of the six terms from the other five; admit a composition that cannot be refused; show a remainder that is discarded rather than compounding.
