## The question restated as an effect

The question asks what decides the spread of planetary system architectures and bulk compositions we observe, from resonant super-Earth chains and hot Jupiters to the Solar System's separated rocky and giant planets. It is a "why" question about a distribution, not about one object.

State the options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, one lever.** Diversity is the output of one dominant variable, for example disk mass or metallicity. This leads to a prediction of a narrow family of outcomes and a search for the missing variable.
- **Option B, a compounding union.** Diversity is the expected output of a lossy, non-invertible mapping from many initial disk conditions through in-disk stochasticity and subsequent selection. This leads to a prediction of a structured but broad distribution, a shape with no single named cause, and a search for the instruments that discriminate among sources.

What an answer would look like: a **shape** that any physical mechanism must satisfy, stated before any mechanism is named, plus the physically named mechanisms recorded as imports or bindings with provenance, and the instruments that would close the residual. Bind that description as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (formula step "Direction"): *the answer is a shape constraining a union compound, not a name for one cause.*

2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the source.
