The shape survives; no single cause is licensed. The spread is a union compound that must be split; divergence is structural because a compounding remainder is what the next turn composes against; the disk to system mapping is lossy and non-invertible so an architecture cannot name its source; variation is generated inside and pruned by selection; arrangement is the bond layer and composition the atom layer; the habitat must be enumerated through cascades; and detection functions are budget filters, never verdicts. Every physical mechanism (core accretion, migration, impacts, photoevaporation, chaos in celestial mechanics) is a provenanced binding from the astronomy frame, not a figure derivation. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest outreach point is the mode limit: the framework returns a shape, not a name, and records its stop.
