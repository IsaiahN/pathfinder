# Is planetary diversity an isomer problem? Structure, order and analogues

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The question asks why systems differ in **arrangement** and **composition**. Two sub-questions are owed: (1) can two systems share their ingredients and still be different substances in Fig 12's sense, and (2) does the order of the formation sequence carry information the ingredient list does not? A third, cross-field question is whether the variation being arranged is isotropic or biased.

## DERIVATION

### Q1  -  Arrangement versus composition is the isomer distinction

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :97-98: **"This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties, which is what forced structure into the notation."**
- Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

Two disks with the same mass, metallicity and angular momentum can end in a resonant chain or a single hot Jupiter plus separated giants. By Fig 12 those are not variants of one thing with different parameters; they are different substances whose ingredient lists cannot tell them apart. The difference is carried by the bond (the ordering and the dynamical relation), not by the atoms.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if the full ingredient list plus scalar parameters predicts the architecture uniquely, with no additional bond information required, the isomer reading fails and "arrangement" collapses into "composition."

**Status:** `specified`. Triangulated for the figure definition (Fig 12 :117 and :5); the physical instance is a world claim, not a figure output.

### Q2  -  The order of the sequence is a bond, not a list

- Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."**
- Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."
- Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

The standard formation narrative is a sequence: grain growth → pebble concentration → planetesimal formation → core growth → gas accretion → migration and instability. Under Fig 12 that is a → chain, and swapping two steps changes the term. The chain is also a cross-kind alternation under Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one," where disk states and dynamical structures alternate. So "arrangement" has two layers: the order of the sequence (a bond) and the cross-kind crossings (a composition rule).

**Important guard.** Fig 13 :75 was tried in `01` as a direct explanation of composition and retracted; it governs the six-term substrate. Here it is used only for the structural claim that a sequence is a bond and that crossings run between kinds. That is a different object, so no strain after re-census.

**Falsifier:** if two different orderings of the same formation stages produce indistinguishable architectures across the population, then → is not carrying information here and the sequence claim is empty.

### Q3  -  The analogue is developmental bias, not isotropic variation

This is the low-overlap witness. Uller et al. 2018 (Genetics), opened this session: "Phenotypic variation is generated by the processes of development, with some variants arising more readily than others"; "even simple networks have multiple attractors that can lead minor differences in the dynamic parameters to have large phenotypic consequences"; "only a small part of the phenotypic space can be reached by a given developmental system, while the remainder is inaccessible." Lala 2024 (Palaeontology), text returned in search: developmental systems "create the landscape for selection, and the admissible pathways through it."

Mapped as an analogue, not as a figure line: the disk is a generator with a biased propensity, not a sampler of all possible architectures. Multiple attractors let small initial differences have large consequences (F1), and only a small part of the architecture space is reachable from any one disk (F2). This corroborates the shape from a field with genuinely different evidence. It does not decide the figure verdict.

**Falsifier:** show that the distribution of generated architectures is isotropic over the accessible architecture space, with no bias toward some outcomes, so that the developmental-bias analogue has nothing to corroborate.

### Q4  -  What is irreducible at this level?

- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**
- Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

A settled theory (for example, a well-tested migration mechanism) becomes an atom for the next level of explanation. That is why the diversity question is not answered by deriving one mechanism from another: at the next level the mechanisms are operands. What is irreducible is a fact about the level at which the diversity residual is being read.

**Falsifier:** show that a term held as an atom at the population level must be opened up and re-derived at that same level, so that no settled molecule is available as an operand.

## CONCLUSION

Arrangement and composition stand to each other as bond to atoms: same ingredients, different substance (Fig 12 :117). The formation sequence is a → bond whose order carries information the ingredient list loses (Fig 12 :112), and it is constrained to cross between states and structures (Fig 13 :74). The variation being arranged is biased rather than isotropic, corroborated from a low-overlap field whose agreement is weak because it is read through the same residual description. Every mechanism stays a provenanced binding; none is promoted to a figure derivation.
