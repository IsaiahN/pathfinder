# Expert verification packet  -  the Tether on planetary system diversity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a planetary scientist or exoplanet researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed; this is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The spread of planetary architectures and compositions is a union compound, not one hard question, so it must be split before it is searched. Divergence is structural: each disk's turn leaves a remainder that the next turn composes against, and where the chain amplifies a compounding remainder is chaos, so no two systems begin from the same place. The mapping from disk conditions to a mature system is lossy and non-invertible, so an observed system cannot name its own source. Variation is generated inside the disk and then pruned by dynamical survival and by detection; arrangement and composition stand as bond to atoms; and the causal habitat must be enumerated outward through second- and third-order effects. Detection selection functions are budget filters, never verdicts. The framework does not name a single cause and does not claim a final answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 10

- **Statement:** divergence is structural and the disk to system mapping is non-invertible, so no unique initial condition is recoverable from one observed architecture.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :31-32 "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise"; Fig 13 :88-89 "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."; Fig 10 :70 "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. - **Triangulation:** two figures with low overlap (substrate recursion versus levels across a lossy transform), so `triangulated`.
- **Refuter:** exhibit an invertible mapping from observed architecture and composition back to unique initial disk conditions, validated across more than one system; or show a process where the remainder is discarded so two different histories reach the same next turn with identical state.

### C2  -  physics-facing; checkable by an exoplanet researcher against demographics and selection functions

- **Statement:** the residual is a union requiring both an internal generator and selection; detection corrections are budget filters and cannot decide the underlying population.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."; Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."; Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** does the observed architecture distribution require both generation and selection, or is the generated distribution already the observed one once completeness is corrected? Do completeness and reliability corrections change the inferred distribution enough that an uncorrected catalog cannot decide it?
- **Refuter:** show a population where generated and observed distributions are identical with no selection or detection work; or show a survey whose selection function equals the population over the region of interest, so corrections change nothing.
- **Status:** single-source in figures for the selection claim (Fig 7), triangulated for the filter claim (Fig 6, Fig 9, Fig 2). World corroboration only.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not one cause. No variable is named as deciding the whole spread.
- Not a derivation of astrophysics. Core accretion, migration, giant impacts, photoevaporation and chaos in celestial mechanics are physics results arrived at independently; the framework describes their shape and did not produce them.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room."
- Not an isotropic random draw. The generated variation is biased by the generator.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument in this run.
- Not a claim that the evo-devo analogue decides the verdict. It is a witness from a different evidence pool, read through the same residual description, so its agreement is weak.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

1. **Invertibility test:** unique initial disk conditions recovered from observed architecture and composition across more than one system.
2. **Union test:** one variable covering both arrangement and composition including the intersections, with no compound left.
3. **Selection test:** generated and observed distributions identical with no selection or detection work.
4. **Habitat test:** a causal habitat closed at direct contact with no second-order effect mattering.
5. **Standing figure refuters:** derive one of the six terms from the other five; admit a composition that cannot be refused; show a remainder discarded rather than compounding.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :28, :31-32, :88-89 and Fig 10 :70 (four short passages). Does the non-invertibility claim follow from them without importing astrophysics?
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: is the exoplanet architecture distribution better read as a union of generation plus selection, or as one lever? Name one completeness correction that changes a demographic conclusion.
3. Check C3 against one system you know well: do two disks with the same bulk ingredients plausibly end as different substances in the Fig 12 sense, or is the bond layer redundant?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate among histories that give the same architecture, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
