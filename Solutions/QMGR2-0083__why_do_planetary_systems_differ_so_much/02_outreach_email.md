**Subject:** Check one shape claim about planetary system diversity before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about planetary system diversity. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to read the framework first. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the residual is the spread of planetary system architectures and compositions. Before naming any cause, the framework constrains the shape any answer must have:
>
> 1. Divergence is structural. Each disk's turn leaves a remainder, and the remainder is what the next turn composes against. Where the chain amplifies, a compounding remainder is chaos. No two systems begin their next turn from the same place.
> 2. The mapping from initial disk conditions to a mature system is lossy and non-invertible. Many initial states give one observed architecture, so a single system cannot name its own cause.
> 3. Diversity needs both variation generated inside each disk and selection at the ground. The surviving population is not the generated population.
> 4. "Arrangement" and "composition" are a union of overlapping conditions, not one list. One lever clears the single-condition regions and leaves the intersections standing.
> 5. The causal habitat must be enumerated outward, including second- and third-order effects such as metallicity changing cooling, which changes fragmentation, which changes architecture. A factor's sign is relative to the residual it is being used to explain.
> 6. Detection selection functions are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict.
>
> This is a prediction of a **shape**, not a measurement, and not a name for one cause.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the framework derives core accretion, migration, giant impacts or photoevaporation. Those are physical mechanisms from your field. In the framework's terms they are bindings with provenance, admitted only after a source gate and a shadow test. Until that record is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight. I am also not claiming that one variable explains the whole spread, and I am not claiming a settled answer.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
>
> 1. An invertible mapping from observed architecture and composition back to unique initial disk conditions, validated on more than one system.
> 2. One variable accounting for both arrangement and composition across the full population including the intersections, with no compound left.
> 3. A population where generated and observed distributions are identical, with no selection or detection work.
> 4. A residual whose causal habitat closes at direct contact, with no second-order effect mattering.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the checker's own knowledge of exoplanet populations. Record the verdict before any framework is shown.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the framework that produced it. Once a recipient has absorbed the interior, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40, evidence-pool rule).
- **Stage 3:** use the formula recursively on another problem with its own ground and its own pre-registered refuter. Sending a method is permitted (Fig 4 :22); sending a conclusion is not.

A stage 1 verdict is a conversation, never corroboration. Do not pool it with other replies as if independent witnesses had agreed.
