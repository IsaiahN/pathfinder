## Wider literature that bears on the residual

- **Disk initial conditions remain poorly determined.** Armitage 2025: choices of surface density profile, gas-to-dust ratio and disk mass are "more a matter of art and convention than anything based on solid data." This is the habitat-enumeration problem (F5) from the astronomy side.
- **Chaos is structural in the Solar System.** Laskar's result (Lyapunov time about 5 Myr) and the NSR interview's marginal-stability picture corroborate F1 and F3: systems are full, marginal, and rearrange into fewer, longer-lived bodies.
- **Detection is a filter, not the population.** The Kepler completeness and reliability literature and the MNRAS bias paper corroborate F6: corrections change inferred occurrence rates by factors of two or more, so an uncorrected catalog cannot hand a verdict.
- **Variation is structured, not isotropic.** The evo-devo literature (Uller et al. 2018; Lala 2024) is a genuinely low-overlap field. It corroborates the shape's claim that outcomes are channelled by the generator (a bias toward some variants), that multiple attractors let small differences in initial conditions have large consequences, and that only a small part of phenotype space is reachable. It does not dictate any figure reading; it is a witness from a different pool.
