## 0. The residual under audit

Restate the question in effect terms before choosing any figure line.

> Planetary systems differ in **arrangement** (number of planets, orbital spacing, resonant chains, hot Jupiters, intra-system uniformity or anti-ordering) and in **composition** (rocky, volatile-rich, ice and gas giants, water fraction gradients). What decides the spread?

- **Arity:** the residual is many-to-one. A distribution of outcomes is given, and the mapping back from an outcome to a unique initial disk is not. This is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, not one hard question (Fig 9 :4-5, :54).
- **Symmetry:** broken. No outcome is privileged by the figures; the observed skew is a product of generation plus selection, not a default state. A symmetric reading ("all architectures equally likely") is not licensed.
- **Scale:** the generating process runs at disk scales (tens to hundreds of au, roughly 0.1 to 10 Myr); the selected outcomes are read at system scales (au, Gyr). Two frames, a lossy transform between (Fig 10 :68-69).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term:** a shape constraining a union compound (divergence from a compounding remainder; variation inside and selection at the ground; habitat enumerated including cascades; filters kept apart from verdicts), with physical mechanisms admitted only as provenanced bindings and with the instruments that would close it. This is the sought-for shape made refusable (formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).

## 3. Findings

### F1  -  Divergence is structural, not a deviation (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :28: "They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."

Fig 13 :31-32 (the quote is a substring of the wrapped text): "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."

Fig 13 :88-89: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."

**Derivation.** Each disk's turn leaves a remainder. The remainder is what the next turn composes against. Where the chain amplifies (self-gravity, resonances, runaway growth), a compounding remainder is chaos. Therefore no two systems begin their next turn from the same place, and divergence is the expected output of the loop rather than a failure of a common blueprint.

**Triangulation.** The randomness claim comes from Fig 13 (substrate recursion). The branching claim comes from Fig 10 :70 (a different figure, different quantity: levels across a lossy transform). Low overlap, so triangulated and not one passage counted twice.

**Refuter.** Show a process in which the remainder is discarded rather than compounding, so that two different histories reach the same next turn with identical state; or show the chain is damping rather than amplifying at the relevant scale. Either would remove the structural expectation of divergence.

### F2  -  The mapping is non-invertible; the source cannot be read off the artefact (`general`, single-source in figures, world-corroborated)

Fig 10 :68-69: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."

Fig 10 :70: "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."

**Derivation.** From disk conditions to mature architecture the transform is lossy and non-invertible. Many initial states map to one observed architecture (the same final spacing can be produced by different migration and merger histories). So a single observed system cannot name its own cause. This is the metamerism / source-discrimination failure from *The Hard Problem is About Source* applied at system scale.

**Mode and status.** `general` for the structural claim. Single-source within the figures (Fig 10 only); the cross-field analogue is corroboration from a different evidence pool, not a second figure.

**Refuter.** Exhibit an invertible mapping from observed architecture and composition back to unique initial disk conditions, validated against more than one system. That would make "the cause" nameable from the artefact and break this finding.

### F3  -  Variation inside, selection between (`specified`, single-source in figures, world-corroborated)

Fig 7 :33: "→ variation inside, selection between"

Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

**Derivation.** Diversity needs both a generator inside each disk (many embryos, stochastic giant impacts, migration pathways, photoevaporation timing) and a selector at the ground (long-term dynamical stability, survival of embryos, and what the instruments can actually see). The surviving population is not the generated population; selection removes configurations. Which selector dominates in a given regime is a physical question, so the finding is `specified`, not `general`.

**Strain test and re-census.** Read biologically, "selection" strains: planets do not reproduce. Re-census: in Fig 7 the phrase governs selection between rooms against a residual, a structural selection principle. Its application to planetary survival is an analogue, corroborated by world sources (marginal stability, repeated rearrangement into fewer bodies), so it is recorded as `specified` with the mechanism named by the world frame, not dictated by the figure. No strain remains after re-census.

**Refuter.** Show a population in which the generated distribution and the observed distribution are identical, with no removal by dynamical instability or detection, so that selection does no work.

### F4  -  The "why" is a union compound and must be split before searching (`general`, single-source)

Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :55-57: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

**Derivation.** "Arrangement" and "composition" overlap. Initial conditions, stochastic processing and selection are each individually sufficient over part of the population and all bind at the intersections. One lever (disk mass, metallicity) clears only the single-condition regions. The residue left by the big lever is smaller and categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, it is a compound. So the honest answer splits into at least three loops before any mechanism search.

**Refuter.** Exhibit one variable whose variation accounts for both the arrangement and the composition residuals across the full population, including the intersections, with no residual compound left over.

### F5  -  The habitat must be enumerated, including cascades (`general`, methodological, single-source)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

**Derivation.** The causal habitat of planetary diversity is not just "disk mass." It includes host metallicity, opacity, cooling time, irradiation, photoevaporation, binary companions, and the second- and third-order contacts among them (for example, metallicity changes cooling, which changes fragmentation, which changes architecture). An actor's sign is relative to the residual: the same metallicity promotes giants for a gas-giant residual and is inert for a water-fraction residual at a different scale.

**Strain test.** The DDT example is ecological. Applying it to disks is a world analogue, corroborated by a formation model in which disk lifetimes correlate with disk masses and dynamical events shape final architecture (A&A 2023, see ledger). Marked as a methodological rule from the figure plus a world analogue, not a claim that Fig 11 discovered metallicity cascades.

**Refuter.** Show a residual whose causal habitat closes at direct contact, with no second-order effect mattering, so that enumeration outward changes nothing.

### F6  -  Filters are for the budget, not the verdict (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

**Derivation.** Detection selection functions (transit geometry, period sensitivity, radial-velocity completeness, reliability corrections) are instruments improved from worse instruments already returning something. They are budget filters. They must not hand us a verdict about the underlying population. This is triangulated across three figures with low overlap (instrument enhancement, filter/verdict separation, and ground/anchor rule).

**Refuter.** Show a survey whose selection function is identical to the underlying population over the region of interest, so that no completeness or reliability correction changes the inferred architecture distribution.

### F7  -  The answer is a shape, not a name; the stop is honest (`general`)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

**Derivation.** The figures license the shape (divergence, union, variation plus selection, cascades, filter/verdict separation). They do not license "the cause is disk mass." Naming a cause turns a visible choice into an invisible one. Where nothing registers at any resolution, there is no edge to extend from; the honest report is `unreachable`, not a produced answer. This is confirmed by the canonical figures README, which says the framework returns "a shape, not a name."

**Refuter.** Derive a unique cause for the spread from the six-term set alone, with no world binding, and show it predicts the observed architecture and composition distributions.

### F8  -  The field's bottleneck is the middle link, not observation (`general`, single-source)

Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Fig 3 :26-27: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." / "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

**Derivation.** Perception is strong (ALMA disk substructure, large transit and RV catalogs) and planning is strong (N-body and population synthesis). The weak link is vocabulary and objective: terms for what a given disk affords, and an instrument for what would count as progress on "explaining diversity." Most effort lands above the break because the measurements already exist there.

**Refuter.** Point to a working instrument that measures "what this disk affords" or "how far along we are in explaining the spread" and show it is already constraining models rather than being assumed.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A mapping from observed architecture and composition back to unique initial disk conditions is validated across more than one system (breaks F2, invertible mapping).
2. A single variable accounts for both arrangement and composition residuals across the full population including the intersections, with no compound left (breaks F4, union).
3. The generated and observed distributions are shown to be identical with no selection or detection work (breaks F3, selection between).
4. A residual is found whose causal habitat closes at direct contact with no second-order effect mattering (breaks F5, cascade).
5. A unique cause of the spread is derived from the six-term set alone with no world binding (breaks F7, shape not a name).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a physical cause (F7). The Tether returns a constraint on any answer, not the answer.
2. Split the question into at least three loops before searching for mechanisms: initial-condition variance, in-disk stochasticity, selection (F4).
3. Enumerate the habitat outward, including metallicity / opacity / cooling / irradiation cascades, and mark each actor's valence as relative to the residual (F5).
4. Keep instruments and selection functions as budget filters, and use independent ground readings as witnesses (F6).
5. Admit physical mechanisms only as bindings with provenance: source gate and shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13). Do not present a world result as a framework derivation.
6. Fig 4 :23: "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success." Send the generator (the shape and the refuter), not a conclusion.
