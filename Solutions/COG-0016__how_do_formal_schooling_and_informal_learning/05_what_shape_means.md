## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For COG-0016 it is: the set of constraints that any answer about the two learning regimes and E must satisfy, plus a description of the instrument that would name which constraint fires. A name would be "schooling produces analytic reasoning." A shape is "E is a learnable term; each regime imports a vocabulary and a weighting; the failures are symmetric; the compound needs two low-overlap instruments."

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union/partition finding F7: the question as asked is a bundle of at least five slots.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: when contact is not possible, *"the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the instrument description in the shape.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual is stated as a variation in expectations, not as a cause named "schooling."

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). Scribner and Cole's 1973 observations and Kuhn's 2001 results are such imports: they are named with authors, venues and years in `10_source_ledger.csv`, and they are corroboration, not the verdict.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the validator limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* That is why this folder cannot name which expectation a given person holds.

## 4. Why the "capacity" framing is the specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* Several sources (one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, something not yet discovered) produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a test score is a projection. It can register the same output for a criterion-bonded expectation and an authority-bonded one. Naming the source ("this child reasons formally because of schooling") from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. That is the substance of F8.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" mean for COG-0016? (answered)

It means the five-part constraint set in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and §3, plus:

- **Not a capacity claim.** The child is not a blank slate on the capacity side either: *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says *"The blank slate was a half-truth. The child is a lineage."* The inherited priors and primitives are real; what the two regimes change is the vocabulary, the weighting and the bond to the ground.
- **Not a regime ranking.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* describes the apprentice: much of what is picked up never comes as instructions, learned *"not only through language but also by observation, imitation, and practice."* It also says *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* Each medium carries and loses something. The result is the symmetric table in F6.
- **Not a stable trait.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: two people who only check against each other *"drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* A shared evidentiary standard can be a convention that holds a community together and simultaneously make its members unable to see its error. This is Fig 2 :40-41 restated for classrooms.
- **An instrument description.** The shape owes a description of what would discriminate: an observable that separates source-standing, warrant-type, explanation-form, threshold and transfer in the same person across both settings. Until such an instrument returns something, the slot stays open.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket ban on naming anything in this domain. That is too broad. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive or a **source read off a projection**. The concepts named here (vocabulary, weighting, bond, habitat, instrument) are not missing from Γ; they are canonical terms. What is forbidden is (a) presenting a discriminating instrument's reading as if it named the source, and (b) inventing a new cognitive capacity to fill a slot. Naming a *constraint* is allowed; naming a *person's expectation* from a projection is not. Both readings are now recorded so neither is silently lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own correction in `05` §5b.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of sympathetic education researchers cannot settle the shape claim. Give the claim a budget to be tried: ask for an instrument or a refutation.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The canonical article adds that LLM outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on. The four domain verdicts are internal-consistency verdicts. The empirical direction and magnitude remain a `gap`.
