## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Different instruments, different claims.** Wilde et al. (2001) date a zircon and read oxygen isotopes to infer crust and oceans at 4.4 Ga. Zellner (2017) re-dates lunar impact melts and counts craters to infer a protracted bombardment. They are low-overlap frames. Their agreement supports a punctuated-but-habitable shape, but agreement from different archives is still world corroboration, not a figure verdict.
2. **The impact timing is a live dispute, not a settled backdrop.** The Wikipedia synthesis says the LHB *"is now debated"* and that *"definitive evidence either way remains elusive"*; Zellner argues for protracted bombardment and against a cataclysmic spike. Any answer that treats a single terminal cataclysm as established fact is out of date.
