## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"a shape, not a name"* for a **missing** thing. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name or date it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0013 the shape is: a per-slot, contact-based, habitat-substituting, time-indexed record, with continuity treated as a derivative to be paid for. "Continuously habitable" and "repeatedly disrupted" are both **names for a completed history**, not shapes. The shape is what any acceptable history must look like.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* (the line continues *"rather than the cause"*).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Applied here: describe what you would expect to see in a zircon oxygen-isotope profile or an impact spherule bed if the shape were true, before naming an epoch or a paper.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name or a date

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is the rock record: zircons, spherule beds, isotope systems. No argument in this folder settles what those returned.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. The binary options offered by the question are imports from geology, not figures.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Applying the six-term substrate to Hadean history makes our vocabulary visible; it does not make the rock speak.

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. This is why the answer records the shape and refuses to fill the slot with "impacts" as if the framework had dated a crater.

## 4. The metamerism problem applied to the early Earth

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a carbon-isotope excursion, a shock feature, or an Ir anomaly is a reading. Several histories can produce the same reading. A hiatus in sedimentation and a global sterilization event can both leave a gap. What is owed is a discriminator, not a story. *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the matching limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* The lineage must be kept even when two histories read the same.

This is the deepest reason the figures cannot settle ORI-0013: the relevant discriminators (zircon oxygen isotopes, impact spherule mineralogy, lunar Ar/Ar standards) are instruments, not figure lines. Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."*

## 5. The avoided error: echo without shadow

*Arrival of the Fittest* uses the marker `SPACE(???)TIME` and immediately adds *"we cannot say which state composed first, and we don't know if the order mattered."* The `(???)` is deliberately left open. An outreach draft that deletes the `(???)` and writes a completed history has committed the same error as deleting it from the spacetime chain: a name where the framework returns a shape.

For ORI-0013 the equivalent honest marker is **HABITABLE(? windows?)DISRUPTED**. The shape says what the bracket must be: per-slot, contact-defined, with habitat substitution. It does not say how many windows, or when.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* The figures cannot validate the geological reading; only the ground can.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous advocates for either "continuously habitable" or "cataclysm" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried against the rock record.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* with the formula, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` through `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any geological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own §1

I initially wrote that *"a shape, not a name"* forbids naming any historical condition. Narrowed after re-census: the README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Continuity is precisely such a derived, non-primitive quantity, so the correction bites hardest on the word "continuously" in the question. Naming an observed zircon population or an impact horizon is a provenanced import from the geological frame, which is permitted provided the provenance is recorded. Both the original claim and the correction are kept visible here.
