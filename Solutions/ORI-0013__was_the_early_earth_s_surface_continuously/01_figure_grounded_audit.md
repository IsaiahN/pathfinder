## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Two candidate histories of the early Earth surface are offered:

- **A, continuously habitable:** a gapless stretch of clement surface conditions including liquid water, from roughly 4.4 Ga into the Archean.
- **B, repeatedly disrupted:** large impacts reset or sterilize surface environments, so habitability is punctuated.

What is at stake: whether prebiotic chemistry had an uninterrupted window (A) or a window divided by destructive episodes with refugia and recoveries (B). This residual touches ORI-0011 (first environments capable of prebiotic chemistry), ORI-0012 (how soon life could appear), ORI-0014 (atmosphere), ORI-0015 (oxygen), and ORI-0016 (ocean chemistry).

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction"): an acceptable answer is a *time-indexed record of which surface slots were in contact with which conditions, what the anchor reading was, and whether apparent gaps are missing data or real absences*. Writing this down makes the preference refusable by the same test as any other term.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale:**
- Arity: two named histories, but the correct form is a **union compound**, because a baseline habitable state and episodic impacts can both be true. Fig 9 :55: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- Symmetry: not symmetric under time reversal. Impacts are a **sequence** (the ordered bond `→`). Operators table :30: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* Continuity presumes a gapless **continuum**, which is a derived quantity, not a member of the six (Fig 13 :43-44).
- Scale: planetary surface over roughly 500 Myr, but the residual is **per slot** (time windows, regions), never a single global average. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. Figure census and findings F1 to F9

Every quote below is verbatim from `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. The citation number is the line inside the figure's code block.

### F1: The question is a union compound, not a binary (structural, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :55: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

The binary "continuously OR disrupted" bundles a state (habitable conditions persisting) with a process (impacts occurring). Both can hold at different slots. Averaging them into one global verdict is the exact failure Fig 1 :22 names: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Refuter: a ground measurement that resolves the entire window as a single homogeneous state at the relevant resolution (for example, a continuous, gapless proxy record across the whole Hadean-Archean surface) would dissolve the union and restore the binary. Until then, refuse the binary.

### F2: "Continuously" presumes a continuum the framework removed (structural, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :43: **"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or"** Fig 13 :44: **"smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."**

`THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section agrees: the derived quantities *"(symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."* Continuity is therefore not a primitive that the early Earth either has or lacks. It is a derivative that a record must compose and pay for. Asking "was it continuous" puts the cart before the instrument. The figure-governed question is: at what resolution do the readings resolve into slots, and what fills the gaps?

Refuter: a derivation of a continuum from the other five members alone, so that it could be restored to the minimal set, would break this finding. None is supplied.

### F3: Habitability is contact, not a property of the planet (shape, `general`, single-source in the figures)

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Apply the same move to "habitable": it is a property of an organism-and-habitat pair, not of the substrate alone. The question must name what is being supported and which habitat is in contact. Fig 11 :46 lists the actors to enumerate: **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"**.

Fig 11 :49: **"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** An impact is a malfactor to surface prebiotic chemistry and a benefactor to subsurface thermophiles. Calling it simply "disruption" smuggles in a residual.

Refuter: a demonstration that habitability is an intrinsic scalar of a planet, independent of what is being supported, would break this. No such demonstration exists in the figures.

### F4: An impact substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one (shape, `specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**

This is the strongest single figure line for this question, and it is triangulated across two low-overlap world sources. Sleep, Zahnle and Neuhoff (2001, PNAS): *"subsurface high-temperature organisms are the likely survivors of an ocean boiling asteroid impact that otherwise sterilized the planet."* Zellner (2017, Origins of Life and Evolution of Biospheres), reporting 3-D models: the surface *"would have been sterilized"* while *"hydrothermal vents below Earth's surface could have incubated life by providing a sanctuary for thermophile microbes"* (as quoted in the Wikipedia Late Heavy Bombardment page, accessed 2026-11-23).

Consequence: option B is true at the surface and false as total sterilization. "Repeatedly disrupted" describes a change of habitat, not the absence of one.

Refuter: a ground demonstration that a specific impact removed all available habitats at once, with no substituted refuge anywhere in the system, would break this finding.

### F5: The anchor is the rock record, and absence is not proof (method, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when"** Fig 2 :30: **"you push on it, it is not an anchor."**

For this question the anchor is the mineral and isotope record, not agreement among models. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

The absence of an early biosignature or of an impact horizon in a given section is therefore *depth* (instrument or section not good enough) or *genuine absence*, and from inside the current record the two look identical. Zellner (2017) records exactly this: terrestrial evidence for a 3.9 Ga cataclysm is *"elusive"*, with studies returning both low Ir signatures and slight elevations in the same Greenland metasediments.

Instruments are improved from worse instruments already returning something. Fig 6 :33: **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."** Zircons were already returning a reading before anyone designed the ion microprobe that sharpened it.

Refuter: an anchor shown to move when frames push on it (a proxy that changes answer depending on which lab reads it or which prior is applied) would disqualify it as an anchor.

### F6: The impact-chronology dispute is one evidence pool, not two witnesses (verification, `general`, triangulated)

Zellner (2017): *"most evidence supports a prolonged lunar (and thus, terrestrial) bombardment from ~4.2 to 3.4 Ga and not a cataclysmic spike at ~3.9 Ga."* The Wikipedia Late Heavy Bombardment page (accessed 2026-11-23): *"The existence of the LHB was once widely accepted by the academic community but is now debated; definitive evidence either way remains elusive."* Both the cataclysm and protracted views draw on the same Apollo samples, re-dated with recalibrated standards. By Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** and Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

So the timing of the bombardment is a genuine `gap` at this budget, and a `gap` is a verdict, not a silence. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

Refuter: two chronologies built on demonstrably independent evidence (different samples, different instruments, different dating systems) that converge would upgrade this from one pool to corroboration and move the chronology verdict.

### F7: Two low-overlap frames are available and must be kept separate (method, `specified`, triangulated)

Fig 8 :53: **"Residual first, frame second."** Fig 8 :45: **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."** Fig 2 :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence"**

D1 (zircon geochemistry: Wilde et al. 2001, Nature) and D3 (lunar and planetary impact chronology: Zellner 2017) use different instruments and different archives. Wilde et al.: *"This zircon thus represents the earliest evidence for continental crust and oceans on the Earth."* Both frames support the shape "habitable windows existed and were interrupted", but they are world sources, secondary to the figures, and their agreement is corroboration of the shape only, not a figure verdict.

Refuter: showing that both frames in fact draw on the same dating standard or the same sample pool would reduce them to one frame and remove the surplus.

### F8: Filters are not witnesses (method, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The ranking of which epoch to sample first is a budget decision. So is the choice of which journal or meeting to approach. Neither says anything about whether A or B is true.

Refuter: any step in this solution where a budget filter is used as evidence would be a violation to strike.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing instruction and the exemplar's discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A ground measurement resolves the whole Hadean to early Archean window as a single homogeneous habitable state at the resolution in question, so the residual no longer splits into slots.
2. A ground demonstration shows that impacts never crossed a surface-sterilizing threshold anywhere in that window.
3. An anchor is found that changes its answer depending on which frame pushes on it, disqualifying the rock record as anchor.
4. A case is shown where an impact removed all habitats with no substituted refuge anywhere, breaking the substitution claim.
5. A continuum is derived from the other five members of the six, so it could be restored to the set.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or amended. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
