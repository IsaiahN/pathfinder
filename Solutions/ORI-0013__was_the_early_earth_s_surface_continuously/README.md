## What this solution decides, in effect terms

The question asks us to choose between two stories of the Hadean and early Archean surface. Option A says conditions were continuously habitable, a gapless stretch of liquid water and clement temperature. Option B says impacts repeatedly disrupted that surface, resetting or sterilizing environments. What each leads to: A gives prebiotic chemistry an uninterrupted window and pushes the origin-of-life questions ORI-0011 and ORI-0012 toward an early, calm start; B gives it a punctuated window and pushes those questions toward refugia, recovery, and the intervals between impacts.

The derived answer refuses the binary as posed. The figures decide the **shape** of any acceptable answer, not the empirical timeline. They say habitability is a time-indexed contact relation, not a global property; continuity is a derived quantity, not one of the six primitives; an impact substitutes a habitat rather than removing one; and the anchor is the rock record, which must be read per slot. The figures do not say what the rock record returned. That is a `gap` on the historical fact, reported honestly, and the ground (geology) supplies the empirical content. World sources read here, all opened, point to punctuated surface conditions with long quiescent intervals and subsurface or hydrothermal refugia, with the impact-chronology itself still contested.
