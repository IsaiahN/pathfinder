# The Tether's answer on early Earth habitability

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints, `gap` on the empirical timeline. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers a binary: was the early Earth's surface continuously habitable, or did impacts repeatedly disrupt it? In effect terms this asks whether prebiotic chemistry had an uninterrupted window (option A) or a punctuated one with refugia and recoveries (option B). The two options are a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). They have different loops and must be unbundled before being searched (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **A global binary is the wrong form.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The answer is a time-indexed record of which slots registered against the ground, at what resolution, and what filled the gaps.

2. **"Continuously" is not a primitive.** Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or"* / *"smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Continuity is a derived quantity that must be composed. The question's own word begs the thing that needs to be shown.

3. **Habitability is contact, not a planet property.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* An impact is a malfactor to surface chemistry and a benefactor to subsurface thermophiles. The answer must name what is supported and which habitat is in contact.

4. **An impact substitutes a habitat, it does not remove one.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* So option B is true at the surface and false as total sterilization. World sources read here (Sleep et al. 2001; Zellner 2017) corroborate the substitution shape: surface sterilization with subsurface and hydrothermal refugia.

5. **The anchor is the rock record.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A gap in a section is depth or genuine absence, and from inside the record the two look the same (Fig 6 :27).

6. **The bombardment chronology is a `gap`, reported.** Zellner (2017) argues for protracted bombardment from about 4.2 to 3.4 Ga rather than a 3.9 Ga cataclysm; the Wikipedia synthesis says the LHB *"is now debated"* and *"definitive evidence either way remains elusive."* The figures do not decide this. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures decide A or B. The figures govern shape; the ground settles history.
- Not that the early Earth was continuously habitable, and not that a single terminal cataclysm is established.
- Not a date, an impactor size, or an atmosphere composition. Those are readings from instruments this derivation did not run.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken here; no part of this is a geological result.
- Not that an impact left no habitat. The substitution claim is the opposite.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The discriminators are instruments, not arguments (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it).

- A zircon or spherule record that resolves the window per slot at the relevant resolution (does the residual split, or stay flat?).
- A proxy that discriminates a sedimentation hiatus from a sterilizing impact in the same section.
- An impact chronology built on independent samples and dating systems, so the two frames are no longer one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40).
- A case where an impact removed all habitats with no substituted refuge (would break the shape).
- A derivation of a continuum from the other five members of the six (would break F2).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the vocabulary makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the opened sources can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the "continuously habitable or repeatedly disrupted" binary, because that binary is a union of two conditions that can both hold at different times and places. What the figures decide is the shape any acceptable answer must have: habitability is a per-slot contact relation between an organism and a habitat, not a global property of a planet; "continuously" is a derived quantity, not one of the six primitives, so it has to be paid for rather than assumed; and an impact substitutes one habitat for another rather than removing habitat, which makes surface sterilization compatible with subsurface and hydrothermal refugia. The rock record is the anchor and it settles the history, not agreement among frames. The impact chronology is still contested, and the figures report that as a gap rather than a silence. What is offered is the shape, five written refuters, and the instruments that would close it.

## 7. Cost if wrong, undoability, reversal

- **Cost if wrong:** a wrong shape would send researchers to the wrong archive (for example, to a single global proxy instead of per-slot records), and would mistake a sedimentation gap for an absence of life. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **Undoable:** yes. The shape is a claim about form, not a dated history. It can be amended without withdrawing the figure set.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot record that stays homogeneous across the whole window, a demonstration that no impact crossed a sterilizing threshold, a movable anchor, a case with no substituted refuge, or a derived continuum would each reverse a specific part of the shape.
