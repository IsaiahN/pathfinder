# Expert verification packet: the Tether on early Earth habitability

**What this is:** a claim in a form an early Earth researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints, `gap` on the historical timeline. Not `grounded`.** No measurement was taken in this derivation.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The binary "continuously habitable or repeatedly disrupted" is a union compound, not one hard question, and a global answer hides the per-slot signal. The Tether's shape says: habitability is a time-indexed contact relation, not a property of the planet alone; "continuous" presumes a continuum that the six-term substrate explicitly removed as a derivative, so continuity must be composed and paid for; and an impact substitutes one habitat for another rather than removing habitat, so surface sterilization is compatible with subsurface and hydrothermal refugia. The figures do not date the bombardment or read the rock record. That is a reported `gap`, not a silence. What is offered is the shape, written refuters, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework internal, checkable by reading Figure 13
- **Statement:** a continuum is a derived quantity, not a member of the six-term minimum set. The word "continuously" in the question therefore smuggles in a derivative that must be composed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43 - *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or"* and Fig 13 :44 - *"smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."* Also `THE_FORMULA.md` substrate section: *"the derived quantities (symmetry, smoothness, chaos, a continuum) are what a turn leaves rather than members of the set."*
- **Refuter:** derive a continuum from the other five members alone so it could be restored to the set.

### C2: framework internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 11
- **Statement:** habitability is a contact relation indexed per slot, and an impact substitutes a habitat rather than removing one. The binary is a union, not a partition, and averaging across slots destroys the signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 - *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :39 - *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :55 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*
- **Refuter:** show a case where an impact removed all habitats with no substituted refuge anywhere; or show habitability is an intrinsic scalar of a planet independent of what is supported; or resolve the whole window as one homogeneous state at the relevant resolution.

### C3: world-facing, checkable against the literature (secondary corroboration only)
- **Statement:** the figure shape is compatible with the current literature, which points to punctuated surface conditions with refugia and a contested bombardment chronology. The figures do not decide the history.
- **What to check:** Sleep, Zahnle and Neuhoff (2001, PNAS): *"subsurface high-temperature organisms are the likely survivors of an ocean boiling asteroid impact that otherwise sterilized the planet."* Zellner (2017, OLEB): *"most evidence supports a prolonged lunar (and thus, terrestrial) bombardment from ~4.2 to 3.4 Ga and not a cataclysmic spike at ~3.9 Ga."* Wikipedia Late Heavy Bombardment synthesis: *"The existence of the LHB was once widely accepted by the academic community but is now debated; definitive evidence either way remains elusive."*
- **Refuter:** a chronology from independent samples and dating systems that establishes a single cataclysm, or a per-slot record showing no surface sterilization anywhere. Note the overlap: these sources share archives, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not two witnesses.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures decide which option is historically true. They govern shape; the rock record decides history.
- Not that the early Earth was continuously habitable, and not that a single terminal cataclysm is established.
- Not a date, impactor size, atmosphere composition, ocean pH, or oxygen level.
- Not that an impact left no habitat. The substitution claim is the opposite.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument in this run.
- Not that the two histories are isomers as posed. That claim failed the strain test; it is recorded as retracted in `01` §5 and re-specified in `06`.
- Not a claim from a personal email or a purchased list. Public venues only.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. The discriminators are instruments: per-slot zircon and spherule records; a proxy that separates a hiatus from a sterilizing event; an impact chronology built on independent samples and standards. Also decisive would be any of the five refuters in §2 firing.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :43-44, Fig 11 :66, Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :55. Four short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: does an impact leave a substituted habitat in every case, or can it remove all of them?
3. Name one measurement that would discriminate a sedimentation hiatus from a surface-sterilizing impact in a Hadean section, or say why none exists.
4. Say whether the bombardment chronology is settled enough to date the shape. If yes, name the independent evidence.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
