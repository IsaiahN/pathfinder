## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the surrounding text says it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: the next arrangement must be a method rather than a recording, keep a channel to a non-updating ground, keep a seat able to refuse, enumerate the new habitat, and hold its cuts reversible. The next org chart is a name, and it is withheld.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on precision: *"At some point the recursion of the 'shape' will have carved away at so many aspects, that it will fall out of perceptible range and be indistinguishable, in your frame, from the answer."* Then the guard: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

A shape is tightened by repeated cuts that add content, not just constraint. This is why `01` enumerates six required properties of a next structure rather than stopping at "the room must change."

## 3. Why a bundle must be split before a shape can be stated

*Library of Babel* names the failure directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :55-57 makes the same move. For this question, "why does the structure fail" is a bundle: question change, method/recording crossing, channel decay, habitat substitution, and convention-to-verdict drift. The shape is stated per domain before any combined answer is attempted, and the verdicts are never averaged.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one. In this solution the six-term substrate is used as a generator for the shape; it is not offered as a discovery about organizations.

## 5. What the essays say about the exact failure this question names

Three passages bear directly on "the thing that worked stops working":

- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"a decay on old knowledge so the system did not calcify around what worked in the past."* Calcification around past success is treated as a failure mode to be engineered against, not a sign of health. Compare Fig 4 :25-26, where a recording replayed produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Brains did not become intelligent by aggregating more neurons... Brains became intelligent by structural reorganization under selection: the architecture changed, not merely the count."* (This is in *They're Scanning Books*; the same point about structure over scale recurs in the essays.) Growth in headcount is not growth in capability unless the arrangement changes; compare Fig 11 :40 and Fig 12 :94.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"The room, one per problem. Agents share everything, recordings included, to solve that one problem faster."* and *"When the room closes, only the general method moves up into the shared library that newborn agents inherit."* This is the cleanest canonical statement of why the stage transition is the dangerous moment: a room that does not close, or that promotes its recordings rather than its method, is the failure. Fig 10 :71-72 governs it.

## 6. Source discrimination: why an outcome reading cannot name its cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a stage in which "the structure stopped working" is a reading, not a source. Question change, channel decay, habitat substitution and a promoted convention can all produce the same reading. The framework returns the set of shapes that could produce it and the discriminator that would separate them. It does not name the cause from the reading alone. That is the F8 gap in `01`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the lineage guard: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* Hence the record, not the artifact, is what makes the result checkable (Fig 13 :129: *"It is recoverable from the lineage."*).

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known organizations that agree with this claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the architecture essay is blunter: LLM outputs generated by prompting the framework are *"simulacra"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any company and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any organizational element. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in `Γ`. Network, spectrum, gradient, space, time and energy are all already held (Fig 13). Naming a coordination mechanism or a decision right is a **binding** from the business frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What stays forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"*; this binding adds none). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: naming the source of an outcome reading without a discriminator is still the error. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
