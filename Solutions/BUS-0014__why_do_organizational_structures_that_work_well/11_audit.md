## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The gap between what a structure predicts and what the ground returns. Arity: library, belief and action, observation, plus a slot index, a network edge and a time index. Symmetry: stage and growth are orderings; failure is a threshold comparison; structure is a network arrangement; the governing bonds are ordered, so a rearrangement is a different substance. Scale: decision loop, membrane between scales, room chain, habitat cascade, with a lossy transform between. This matches the primary's census.
- **Domain 1, per-slot decision loop.** Actors: decision makers, library, actions, observations. Currency: residual reduction. Anchor: the paying outcome, which does not update. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, scale membrane.** Actors: cast, methods, head starts. Currency: method versus recording. Anchor: whether a generator crosses. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, room chain.** Actors: rooms, surfaced residuals, next room. Currency: progress per handoff. Anchor: ground pressure on the room. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, habitat.** Actors: customers, competitors, regulators, conditions, relations. Currency: payment, survival, contact. Anchor: the ground that settles and does not update. Admitted.
- **Domain 5, verification seat and channel.** Actors: seat, verifiers, conventions. Currency: verdicts versus conventions, channel fidelity. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **Excluded:** "the structure" as a free-floating object (no nameable anchor); "culture" as sole cause (absorbed into domains 4 and 5). Matches the primary's X1 and X2.
- **Partition or union.** Union. A transition changes the room, the crossing, the habitat and the channel at once. Fig 9 :55 and :57 govern.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive, vocabulary, objective, plan, learn and carry. The room follows the question, so the question is upstream of the chart. Matches the primary's order.
