# Expert verification packet: the Tether on apprenticeship-transmitted practical knowledge

**What this is:** a claim in a form an apprenticeship, expertise or VET researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the five conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the practice list and coefficient, not `grounded`.** No measurement of an apprentice, a craft or a programme is claimed.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Practical knowledge transmitted by apprenticeship stays reliable when five conditions hold: a re-applicable method and head start cross the handoff rather than a recording of one occasion; the arrangement that carries the practice is preserved or re-derived rather than left in the ingredient list; a non-updating ground stays reachable at each case so agreement between master and learner cannot stand in for the outcome; successive cases differ and are chosen against the residual the last one surfaced; and the training habitat is reproduced rather than substituted with unexamined silent failures. Reliability is per case and per slot, never a programme average. The specific named practices and any numerical coefficient are a declared gap. The packet offers the shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the description of an instrument that would close the gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12

- **Statement:** a generator crosses a handoff; a recording does not. The arrangement is the part a proposition drops.
- **Verbatim sources:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*; Fig 4 :26 *"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*; Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*; Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **What to check:** are these four lines present verbatim in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, and do they govern what crosses a transmission boundary rather than a different quantity? (Re-census in `01` §3 S1 assigns Fig 4 to the scale membrane and Fig 12 to the record boundary; they are not merged.)
- **Refuter:** show that both transmission modes can be written over the same bond with no difference in behaviour on a changed question (the isomer claim in `06` Q1 fails), or that the arrangement is fully recoverable from the stated ingredient list.

### C2: ground-facing, checkable by an apprenticeship or expertise researcher

- **Statement:** the anchor is the ground's return, not master-learner agreement; the residual must be indexed per case.
- **Verbatim sources:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*; Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** in a real programme, does reliability measured by agreement with the master's report diverge from reliability measured by an outcome the master cannot move? Does a programme average hide a live per-case failure?
- **Refuter:** per-case reliability unchanged when the ground return is replaced by a master's report; or a reported average that preserves and displays the live per-case signal.

### C3: lineage-facing, checkable by a VET or learning-sciences researcher

- **Statement:** a chain of identical rooms is motion without progress; the next room must differ and be selected by the surfaced residual; a substituted habitat has two silent failures.
- **Verbatim sources:** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*; Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*; Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*; Fig 11 :68 *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **What to check:** in a training lineage, is the next case chosen because it surfaced a residual, or because it resembles the last one? When a workshop is replaced by a simulation, are the two silent failures explicitly enumerated?
- **Refuter:** an identical-rooms lineage that keeps per-case R low on a changed question; or a substituted habitat shown to reproduce the goal with no silent failures.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of named practices, a teaching-method ranking, an optimal duration, or a reliability coefficient.
- Not that explicit theory is useless. It is a head start when a ground and a differing master remain in the loop.
- Not that apprenticeship always works, or that any standing list of teacher traits predicts reliability across a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a stage model of skill acquisition. No figure governs stages, and the staged reading was retracted in `04`.
- Not a derived identity between a manual and a craft. The round-trip gap R_T is nonzero; `11_audit.md` enumerates what drops.
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that this simulation settles the question.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths give one reading; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the reading is low per-case R. The discriminator must vary one condition at a time: channel, bond visibility, anchor, room difference, habitat fidelity. A partial candidate from *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is to *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given"*; it reads how much remains tacit but does not by itself separate the five conditions.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Open `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and verify the 13 quoted strings in C1 to C3 character by character. (Five minutes.)
2. Name one real programme in which reliability was measured by master agreement rather than by an outcome the master could not move, and say what would change if the anchor were swapped. (Five minutes.)
3. Name one case where a training habitat was substituted for a workplace and write down what was probably not reproduced and what was probably introduced. (Five minutes.)
4. Send the refutation, or say which of the six pre-registered falsifiers you would try first. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. (Five minutes.)

Do not ask for agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration. The figures are the authority, not the summary in this packet.
