## 0. The residual in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s (Symbols table :38-44; Formula step 1).

- Γ is what the practitioner can currently predict and do: their library of moves, discriminations, timings and adjustments.
- b is the belief formed from private history and inherited prior (Symbols :20-22). In apprenticeship the inherited prior is the master's practice; the private history is the apprentice's own repetitions.
- a is the action: the cut, the weld, the diagnosis, the navigation decision.
- o′ is what the ground returns: does the joint hold, does the patient recover, does the boat arrive, does the crop yield.
- s is the slot: one object, one case, one apprentice, one occasion. R is *"indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19), and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22).

**The residual to explain.** Why does R stay small across learners and across a lineage when the transmission channel is demonstration, imitation, correction and practice, and why does R tend to grow when the same content is transmitted as explicit propositions? Stated as an effect, not a cause: the question asks which conditions keep prediction aligned with the ground's return when the currency of transmission is not a written code.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction").** An answer is a *separator*: a set of conditions such that, when they hold, a low per-slot R is maintained across a handoff and a changed question, and when one is absent, R drifts upward in a locatable slot. The sought-for shape is written down here before the run so it can accrue a residual like anything else. It is not a list of named practices (shadowing, storytelling, guided practice), because no figure line returns such a list. That list is a declared `gap`.

**Two options and what each leads to.** Option A: reliability lives in the content, so a sufficiently complete explicit theory can reproduce it. Option B: reliability lives in the contact structure, so what crosses a handoff must be a generator plus a reachable non-updating ground, and a proposition-only channel drops the arrangement. A leads to better manuals. B leads to maintaining generators, differing closures and ground contact, with manuals used as head starts rather than recordings. The figures decide between these in F1 to F6 below.

## 1. DOMAIN CENSUS, written before any figure line was chosen

### 1a. R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three-place (master's practice, learner's action, ground's return), plus a fourth term when the handoff is repeated across a lineage (the next learner). The bond structure is ordered (→ sequence, master then learner) and comparative (⋛ threshold: does the outcome count), not symmetric. "Reliability" is a threshold over per-slot residuals, not a symmetric join of two frames (Fig 12 :87, the comparison bond and its test).
- **Symmetry:** master and learner are not interchangeable, so the transmission bond is ordered. Two apprentices reading the same manual are *one* evidence pool, not two frames. Two apprentices each ranging against the ground are two frames (Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence"*). Agreement between the master and the apprentice is symmetric and, for a verdict about the world, disqualifying (Fig 2 :40).
- **Scale:** (i) the individual practice loop, one action and one return; (ii) the master-apprentice membrane (Fig 4); (iii) the lineage or room chain across generations (Fig 7); (iv) the habitat or community of practice, the field of contact from which actors come (Fig 11). The transform between scales is lossy, so the round trip must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30).

### 1b. One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 The practice loop | apprentice, materials, tools, action | residual reduction per slot | the ground's return (joint holds, patient recovers, boat arrives) | yes |
| D2 The transmission membrane | master, apprentice, demonstration, correction | methods and head starts crossing, or a recording crossing | whether what crossed is a generator or a one-occasion description | yes |
| D3 The representation seam | atoms (ingredients, states) and bonds (arrangement, timing, feel) | cost in bits and cost in actions (Fig 12 :125-127) | whether the arrangement is recorded and can travel | yes |
| D4 The habitat and lineage | practitioners, instruments, conditions, relations, successive rooms | survival, arrival, variation and selection | the ground, which does not update; selection between rooms (Fig 7 :35) | yes |
| D5 The explicit-theory frame | propositions, manuals, curricula, checklists | compression, codifiability, repeatability | experiment and reproducibility when used as a generator; agreement with the text when used as a recording | yes, and it is a compound with D2 and D3 |
| X1 "Talent" or "natural aptitude" as a free-floating property | the gifted individual | talent | none that does not update on the outcome | not a domain yet |

X1 is excluded by the anchor test (Fig 2 :29): if "talent" is inferred from the very performance it is meant to explain, it moves when pushed and is not an anchor. It is retained here as a visible exclusion, not silently dropped.

### 1c. Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** Reliability is a union of overlapping conditions: (C1) a generator rather than a recording crosses the membrane (D2), (C2) the bond arrangement is preserved or re-derived (D3), (C3) a non-updating ground stays reachable at the slot (D1), (C4) successive rooms differ and are selected by the residual (D4), (C5) the habitat is reproduced without silent failures (D4). Overlaps are compounds with their own loops: a manual used by an apprentice who also has a master is C2 ∩ C3 ∩ D5, not a harder case of either. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* One lever (write a better manual) clears the region where only C2 binds and leaves the intersections standing.

### 1d. Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Perception of the ground's return → vocabulary for what the situation affords → objective (what counts as progress) → planning (a route) → learn and carry (name what worked, verify, carry to the next problem). The chain typically breaks at the middle link: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither"* obvious failures nor obvious tests (Fig 3 :25-26). Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* A manual can name ingredients (affordances) without naming progress (bonds and routes), which is exactly where apprenticeship supplies a head start. Dependency order: settle D1 before D2; settle C1 and C2 before C4; describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 2. Findings F1 to F9 (verbatim figure lines before conclusions)

### F1: Explicit propositions are a recording; apprenticeship carries a method and a head start (decisive for Option B)

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
Fig 4 :26: *"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*
Fig 4 :11: *"a head start, never a recording"*; Fig 4 :12: *"it makes the next agent's search cheaper"*

Explicit theory, as a transmission artefact, is a compressed description of one occasion or of many collapsed into one formula. It crosses a handoff as a recording. Apprenticeship crosses the same handoff as a method (a re-applicable way of perceiving and acting) and as a head start (a cheaper search for the next learner). The difference is not verbosity; a long manual is still a recording. It is whether the receiving frame can re-apply the method to a new case or only replay a past success. Mode `specified`. Status **triangulated**: Fig 4 (membrane direction) and Fig 12 :107-109 (record boundary) have low overlap, one governs scale, the other governs what a record preserves. Refuter: a proposition-only channel, with no ground contact and no master, producing the same novel-case per-slot R as a guided apprenticeship with ground contact.

### F2: The bond is the lost half, and a vocabulary without bonds cannot name a route (decisive for the representation seam)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :47: *"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*

A craft manual names the ingredients and the states: the cut angle, the temperature, the sequence of steps. It does not, and cannot in propositions alone, carry the timed relation, the pressure, the adjustment to a particular piece of material, the sense of when the state has arrived. That is the bond. Fig 12 :112 names the consequence: a vocabulary without bonds can state what is in a situation and cannot state how to get somewhere from it. Apprenticeship is the channel that transmits bonds through guided perception and correction. Mode `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for craft transmission. Status **triangulated**: Fig 12 (bond invisibility) and Fig 3 :25-27 (the middle link with no instrument) are different figures with low overlap. Refuter: the arrangement shown to be fully recoverable from the stated ingredient list, so a recipe records the whole of the practice.

### F3: Where the arrangement is not recorded, every lineage pays again; where it is, re-deriving is a lookup

Fig 12 :107: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."*
Fig 12 :108: *"Where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once."*
Fig 12 :109: *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*

This is the mechanism behind F1 and F2, stated in its own terms. Settling a term compresses its description. Whether the cost of establishing it must be paid again depends on the substrate: where the arrangement is recorded (a method, a discrimination, a timed relation carried in a body and a community), re-deriving is a lookup; where it is not, each new practitioner re-pays the actions. This explains the familiar observation that a craft can be lost when the lineage breaks even while its written descriptions survive: the descriptions are the compressed result, not the recorded arrangement. Mode `specified`. Status **single-source** on the figure side (Fig 12 only), corroborated at low overlap by world sources on the transmission of tacit skill (see `09` WORLD, Polanyi/Bessemer and Lave and Wenger). Refuter: a lineage that re-derives the arrangement from the written result with no re-payment of actions.

### F4: Reliability is per slot; a global average hides the live signal

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Symbols :39: *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice"*

A question "is apprenticeship reliable?" asked of a programme average is already malformed. R must be read per apprentice, per case, per change of question. A programme can show a low mean R and one live slot trending upward, which is exactly the signal the average destroys. This governs the verdict and the outreach: ask what fails in which slot, not whether the method works in general. Mode `general` for the indexing rule, `specified` for the training application. Status **triangulated**: Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and Symbols table (R as a slice) are distinct but low-overlap statements of the same measurement rule. Refuter: a reliability report that preserves per-slot residuals and still hides a live failure.

### F5: The ground must not update; agreement with the master is the wrong anchor

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Reliability is not master-apprentice agreement. A master and an apprentice who share one evidence pool (the same teacher, the same manual, the same local lore) are one frame wearing two names; their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21). The anchor is the ground's return: the joint, the patient outcome, the arrived boat, the yield. Those do not change because the apprentice believes the master. If the reference becomes "the master says it is right," panel 3 turns into panel 1: everything drifts together coherently and nobody notices (Fig 2 :34). Mode `specified`. Status **triangulated**: Fig 2 (anchor and correlation) and Fig 7 :35 (selection between rooms is where the ground votes) are distinct low-overlap sources. Refuter: reliable performance measured by agreement with a master's report rather than by an outcome the master cannot move.

### F6: A single lineage is one room; reliability needs differing rooms selected by the residual

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
Fig 7 :12: *"same closure, handed along;"* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
Fig 10 :72: *"When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."*

An apprentice who only copies the master is a mirror chain: motion without progress. Reliability across generations requires low overlap between successive rooms (a new problem, a different site, a changed question) and selection of the next room against the residual the last one surfaced. The ground votes between rooms; the master does not adjudicate from inside one. Mode `specified`. Status **triangulated**: Fig 7 (room chain) and Fig 10 :72 (room function of question) are different figures with low overlap. Refuter: a lineage whose rooms all share the same closure and the same question, maintained over a changed question with R still low.

### F7: Transmission must keep the ground reachable; the channel decays, the ground does not

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :25: *"stale percepts, dropped transients,"* Fig 10 :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Apprenticeship keeps a channel open to the ground: the master points at what failed in this particular instance, the instrument is a worse instrument already returning something and is sharpened from that. Codified training substitutes a description for the channel, and the ways it fails are exactly the named ones: stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings. Fig 10 :52 also governs the outside checker: send the characterised residual and its refuter, not the full framework, or the reader's independence is spent. Mode `specified`. Status **single-source** for the channel-decay line (Fig 10), triangulated with Fig 6 :32 for instrument improvement (low overlap: grounding versus reach). Refuter: a proposition-only channel that keeps a non-updating ground reachable at each slot without a live discriminator.

### F8: A substituted habitat has two silent failures

Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
Fig 11 :68: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*
Fig 11 :69: *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*
Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Replacing a workshop with a classroom, or a live master with a simulation, does not remove the habitat; it substitutes one. Two things then become invisible: what the substitute failed to reproduce (the weather, the material variability, the consequence of error) and what it unintentionally introduced (a grade, a rubric, a simulated success). So reliability demonstrated in a substituted habitat proves wiring, not capability. This is the figure-grounded reason a purely propositional or simulated training result cannot establish practical reliability. Mode `specified`. Status **triangulated**: Fig 11 (habitat substitution) and Fig 10 :24 (channel decay) are distinct low-overlap figures. Refuter: a substituted habitat reproducing the goal with no silent failures on a changed question.

- **S1, Fig 4 (between-scale membrane) versus Fig 12 (record boundary).** Both appear to say "what crosses is a method, not a recording." Re-census: Fig 4 governs the direction across a size change (one agent to a population); Fig 12 governs what a record preserves at one level. Different quantities, so no strain after re-census. They are kept as two low-overlap witnesses for F1, not merged.
- **S2, Fig 2 (anchor must not update) versus Fig 11 (actor valence is relative to the residual).** Apparent conflict: if master and ground are both "anchors," how can valence flip? Re-census: Fig 2 governs what settles a verdict (the ground's return), Fig 11 governs which actors help or harm a given residual. A master can be a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :49-51) while the ground remains unmoved. No strain.

Naming an already-available binding (a method, a head start, a discriminator) is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. The gap is the specific list and the number, not the naming of held atoms.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach, per the seat map: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The separator shape is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Method/recording:** a proposition-only channel, with no ground contact and no master, yields the same low per-slot R on a changed question as a guided apprenticeship with ground contact (breaks Fig 4 :22-26).
2. **Bond visibility:** the arrangement is shown to be fully recoverable from the stated ingredient list in a domain where the binder claims it is not (breaks Fig 12 :5, :47, :111).
3. **Anchor:** per-slot reliability is unchanged when the ground's return is replaced by agreement with a master's report (breaks Fig 2 :29, :35, :40).
4. **Rooms:** a lineage whose rooms all share one closure and one question keeps R low on a changed question (breaks Fig 7 :36, Fig 10 :72).
5. **Habitat:** a substituted training habitat reproduces the goal with no silent failures on a changed question (breaks Fig 11 :66-70).
6. **Slots:** a reported programme average hides a per-slot residual that is positive and not trending down (breaks Fig 1 :19, :22).

If any fires, the separator is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the separator, not with a list of practices. Ask what fails in which slot and what the ground returns there (F4, F5).
2. Treat manuals and explicit theory as head starts and recordings, not as the generator. The generator is the method plus the arrangement (F1, F2).
3. Keep the ground reachable per slot and buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (F5, F7, Fig 2 :42).
4. Keep successive rooms different and selected by the residual; do not let a fixed curriculum stay fixed while the question moves (F6).
5. Do not claim capability from a substituted habitat (F8). Say what the substitute failed to reproduce and what it introduced.
6. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not a conclusion and a reading list.

## 7. Updated verdict

The separator survives: practical knowledge transmitted by apprenticeship stays reliable when the channel carries a re-applicable method and a head start rather than a recording of one occasion, when the bond arrangement is preserved or re-derived, when the ground's return stays reachable at each slot and cannot be moved by master or apprentice agreement, when successive rooms differ and are selected by the surfaced residual, and when the habitat is reproduced rather than substituted with unexamined silent failures. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged into a single "apprenticeship works" claim. Which named practices and what numerical coefficient occupy the slots is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence. Mode `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`. Strains after re-census: one live type question (can a proposition compose an ordinarily somatic bond), named and refutable; nothing hidden.
