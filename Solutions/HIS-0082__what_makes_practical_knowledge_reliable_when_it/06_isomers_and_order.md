# Isomers, catalysts and order in practical-knowledge transmission

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

The main audit (`01`, F1 and F2) established that what crosses a handoff can be a generator (method plus arrangement) or a recording (a description of one occasion), and that the bond arrangement is the half a proposition does not record. Two supporting questions are owed: (1) are these two transmission modes **isomers** under Fig 12's definition, or merely different amounts of the same thing, and (2) what is the dependency order of a transmission chain, and where does it break?

## DOMAIN CENSUS (supporting)

Atoms held: method, head start, recording, atom (ingredient/state), bond (arrangement/timing), ground (non-updating return), room (a bounded frame), habitat (field of contact), correction (the return signal). Bonds held: → sequence, ⇒ production, ⋛ comparison, ¬ refusal. No new primitive is needed; every term above is named in the figures or the Symbols table. Currency: bits (description cost) and actions (cost of finding out whether it holds), which do not add (Fig 12 :125-127). Anchor: the ground's return, which does not update.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are method transfer and recording replay isomers? CORRECTION TO MY OWN §5 of `05`

- Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- Both modes share the same content atoms: the ingredients, the steps, the stated temperatures, the same manual on the same shelf. They differ in the bond. Method transfer bonds the content to a re-applicable practice and a reachable ground; recording replay bonds it to one past occasion and a report. Same atoms, different bond, different behaviour on a changed question. So they are **isomers**, not quantities of one dial.
- This is *not* a ban on naming the modes. The README's "shape, not a name" governs a missing primitive; both modes are bindings onto held atoms. **I overstated that rule in `05` §5 and retract the overstatement here.** What is forbidden is claiming the framework derives which mode a particular programme uses. That needs a discriminator (Fig 12 :117 says the difference is *"invisible in the formula"*).
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if both modes can be written over the same bond with no change in behaviour on a changed question, the isomer claim fails and the degree claim stands instead.
- Status: **triangulated** for the structure (Fig 12 isomer rule plus Fig 4 membrane direction, low overlap), **single-source** for the figure definition of isomer itself.

### Q2 — Where does a transmission chain break?

- Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- Ordered links for this question: perception of the ground's return → vocabulary for what the situation affords → objective (what counts as progress here) → planning (a route through the material) → learn and carry (name what worked, verify, carry to the next problem).
- Fig 3 :25-26: *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither"* obvious tests. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- A manual supplies affordance vocabulary (context-free features in the situated-cognition essay: *"the objective measurements of ingredients and cooking times in a recipe"*) and can even supply a route (→). It does not supply the objective discriminator *for this particular piece of material now*. That is the middle link, and it is what a master's correction supplies. Optimising the written route above the break cannot rescue a missing objective below it (Fig 3 :19).
- **Falsifier:** a proposition-only channel that supplies a working objective discriminator on a changed case, measured by per-slot outcomes, breaks this claim.

### Q3 — What is the master, and what is the head start?

- Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- The master is a **catalyst**: they survive their own use, and a correction does not spend them. A worn-out instruction or a copied demonstration is a **spent part**: it behaves differently across uses, and a manual that records only the last success does not mark which it holds. Fig 12 :119 names exactly this as something nothing records.
- "A good teacher" is a **functional group**, not a standing list: *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* A master is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :49-50). The question's search for "what makes it reliable" must not be answered with a standing list of teacher traits.
- **Falsifier:** a standing list of teacher traits that predicts per-slot reliability across a documented change of residual.

### Q4 — What order is the lineage?

- Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
- The order is: one loop closes at one scale (perception to carry), the method is abstracted up with a lossy transform (drop the one-occasion detail), the next room is chosen against the surfaced residual, and the method is brought back down as a head start rather than a replay. The round-trip gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31-32). In apprenticeship terms: a master cannot hand over the experience, only a compressed method and a head start, and the learner re-derives the rest against the ground.
- The gap R_T is measured qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. It is nonzero. Treating the written manual as identical to the craft would set it to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
- **Falsifier:** a lineage in which the next room is chosen by similarity to the last rather than by the surfaced residual, and R stays low on a changed question.

## CONCLUSION

Method transfer and recording replay are **isomers** over a shared content atom set: same atoms, different bond, different behaviour on a changed question. The master is a catalyst and the head start is a compressed method, not a replay. The chain breaks at the middle link, where a written affordance vocabulary has no instrument for what counts as progress on this material now. Lineage order is governed by the round trip and by selection between differing rooms, not by copying likeness. No new primitive is required for any of this. What remains ungoverned is which named practice occupies a slot and what coefficient measures it; those stay `gap`.
