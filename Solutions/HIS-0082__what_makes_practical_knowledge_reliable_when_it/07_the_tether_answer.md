# The Tether's answer: what makes apprenticeship-transmitted practical knowledge reliable

**Mode: `specified` on the five conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the concrete practice list and any reliability coefficient, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| stays small across learners and across a lineage under one transmission regime and grows under another. The question asks which conditions produce that difference. It is a **union** of overlapping conditions, not one dial with a right setting: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). The answer is a separator of five conditions with per-slot verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape: five conditions

1. **A generator crosses, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :26: *"Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."* What the apprentice receives must be re-applicable to a new case, not a description of one occasion. A long manual is still a recording.
2. **The arrangement is preserved or re-derived.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* The timed relation, the pressure, the sense of arrival live in the bond, not the ingredient list. Where the arrangement is not recorded, *"every lineage pays again"* (Fig 12 :109).
3. **A non-updating ground stays reachable at each slot.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* The joint that holds, the patient who recovers, the boat that arrives. Master-apprentice agreement is not the anchor; if they share one evidence pool, they are one frame with two names (Fig 2 :40).
4. **Successive rooms differ and are selected by the surfaced residual.** Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 10 :72: *"When the question changes, the room should change."* A lineage of identical cases with one closure is motion without progress.
5. **The habitat is reproduced, not substituted unknowingly.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Two silent failures follow: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :68-69). A simulated workshop proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70).

**Per-domain verdicts, separate, not averaged:** D1 practice loop `specified` (triangulated); D2 transmission membrane `specified` (triangulated); D3 representation seam `general` structure plus `specified` application (triangulated); D4 habitat and lineage `specified` (triangulated); D5 explicit-theory frame is a compound with D2/D3, `specified`; X1 talent excluded by the anchor test. The specific practice list and the coefficient: `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of named practices. The figures return a shape; Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not a reliability coefficient, an optimal apprenticeship duration, or a ranking of teaching methods. Those are gaps, reported as unreached rather than as silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).
- Not "explicit theory is useless." It is a head start and a recording carrier; used with a reachable ground and a differing master it helps. The claim is about substituting the proposition for contact.
- Not "apprenticeship always works." Reliability is per slot (Fig 1 :19); the master can be a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :49-51).
- Not a standing list of good teachers (Fig 11 :51) or a stage model of skill acquisition. No figure line governs stages, and the staged reading is retracted in `04` under the Dreyfus criticism.
- Not `grounded`. No apprentice, craft or programme was measured.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

The question is currently `unreached`. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths give one reading; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A low per-slot R after training is such a reading. It does not say whether the source was method transfer, a recording replay, a substituted habitat, or agreement with the master's report.

The instrument, characterised in effect terms:

1. Vary the **channel** (generator versus recording) while holding ground contact fixed; read per-slot outcomes on a changed question.
2. Vary **bond visibility** (arrangement present versus ingredient list only) in a domain where the arrangement is claimed to be transmissible.
3. Replace the **anchor** (ground return versus master's report) and see whether per-slot reliability moves.
4. Change the **room** while holding method constant, and check whether the next room was chosen against the surfaced residual.
5. Compare a **reproduced habitat** with a substituted one and look specifically for the two silent failures.
6. Preserve **per-slot** residuals; refuse to report a programme average that hides a live failure (Fig 1 :22).

One candidate partial instrument comes from *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* A correction that shrinks as experience builds is a reading of how much is still tacit for that learner. It does not by itself separate the five conditions, so it is partial.

## 5. Standing falsifiers (pre-registered in `01` §4)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds: a proposition-only channel with no ground contact matches guided apprenticeship on a changed question; the arrangement is fully recoverable from the ingredient list; reliability is unchanged when the ground return is replaced by a master's report; an identical-rooms lineage keeps R low on a changed question; a substituted habitat reproduces the goal with no silent failures; or a programme average hides a live per-slot failure. Any one fires a withdrawal or narrowing.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five-condition shape is what the figures license. The concrete practice list and the coefficient are not in the closure; an instrument is owed before they can be asked again. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). Here the limit is the budget and the instruments, not a proof that no practice could ever be named. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost of being wrong is misdirecting how a craft is taught, so buying a genuinely independent check is worth it. Indiscernibility is not identity; the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a list of teaching tricks or a reliability percentage. It returns a separator. Practical knowledge stays reliable through apprenticeship when what crosses the handoff is a re-applicable method and a head start rather than a recording of one occasion; when the arrangement the master can see is preserved or re-derived, because a recipe names ingredients and not bonds; when the ground's return stays reachable at each case and cannot be moved by agreement between master and apprentice; when successive cases differ and are chosen against what the last one surfaced; and when the training habitat is reproduced rather than substituted with failures nobody has looked for. Verdicts are per case and never averaged. The specific practices and the number are a declared gap. What would close it is a discriminator that varies one condition at a time and reads outcomes on a changed question, not a longer argument. This is a `specified` shape from a simulated derivation, not a ground measurement.

## 8. Carried method for the derivation log

Describe the transmission residual in effect terms; census the membrane and the record boundary separately; refuse any anchor that updates on agreement; hold verdicts per slot; and ask what a discriminator must distinguish before naming a practice. Only methods cross up (Fig 4 :22).
