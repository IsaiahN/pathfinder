## World sources actually opened (secondary corroboration only)

| what | URL | verified content and use |
|---|---|---|
| Tacit knowledge | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tacit_knowledge | Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"*; transfer of tacit knowledge requires *"extensive personal contact, regular interaction, and trust"*; the Bessemer patent failure (a written process the purchasers could not get to work); the Matsushita bread-machine "twisting stretch" learned by apprenticing with a baker. Corroborates F1 and F3. |
| Community of practice | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Community_of_practice | Lave and Wenger 1991, *Situated Learning*; newcomers *"observe and perform simple tasks in basic roles while they learn community norms and practices"*; workers are *"five times more likely to turn to a co-worker than an explicit source of information (book, manual, or database)"*; the Xerox repair reps' informal tips. Corroborates F1, F6, F8. |
| Practice and deliberate practice | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practice_(learning_method) | Ericsson, Krampe and Tesch-Romer 1993; criteria include *"immediate feedback about their performance, so they can make the changes needed to improve"*; deliberate practice is *"highly structured activities that (a) are most relevant for improving performance, (b) are cognitively effortful, and (c) have no immediate rewards"*; the meta-analyses that limit the 10,000-hour claim. Corroborates F5, F7 and the gap on coefficients. |
| Situated cognition | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situated_cognition | *"knowing is inseparable from doing"*; novices rely on *"context-free features"* such as *"the objective measurements of ingredients and cooking times in a recipe"*; legitimate peripheral participation; Lave and Wenger's five apprenticeship settings. Corroborates F2 and F8. |
| Dreyfus model of skill acquisition | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreyfus_model_of_skill_acquisition | *"when things are proceeding normally, experts don't solve problems and don't make decisions; they do what normally works"*; stages from context-free rules to intuitive action; the recorded criticism that there is no empirical evidence for stages. Corroborates F2 and is a partial conflict, handled in the census (below). |
| Animal culture | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_culture | Teaching defined as requiring that *"a teacher must change its behavior when interacting with a naïve individual and incur an initial cost from teaching, while an observer must acquire skills rapidly as a direct consequence"*; meerkat and orca examples; *"alternative foraging techniques seeded in different groups of chimpanzees spread differentially... with substantial fidelity"*; the "zone of latent solutions" hypothesis. Genuinely low overlap with the management and education pool. Corroborates F1, F6. |

**Overlap declaration.** The six world sources span human cognition, education, animal behaviour and the philosophy of skill, but five of them are Wikipedia syntheses of overlapping literatures (Polanyi, Lave and Wenger, Ericsson, Dreyfus recur). They are one broad evidence pool, not six independent witnesses. The animal-behaviour source is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator. No world source overrules a figure line. The Dreyfus "no empirical evidence for stages" criticism conflicts with a staged reading of skill acquisition; it was returned to the census and resolved by dropping the stage claim entirely, since no figure line governs stages. The verdict was not flipped.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22-26** (membrane): sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. No receptiveness rank, no bulk send.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is that a wrong separator would misdirect how a craft is taught, so buying a genuinely independent checker is worth the effort.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the separator and the six refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure and in which slot. Record their verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the audit. The figures are the authority, not the summary.
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :18-21 and :40). A Track B reply, if one ever exists, is a conversation, not a witness (Seat Map :163).
