# Outreach draft: check one separator before you decide whether the account is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. Replace `[name]` and the bracketed sentence with a real, specific question about their work.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the figure lines in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 to F6 have been checked against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in this folder. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** What would count as a failure of reliability in your apprentice's first changed case?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim about how practical knowledge survives transmission, and I would like you to check it against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** practical knowledge stays reliable through apprenticeship when the transmission channel carries a re-applicable method and a head start rather than a recording of one occasion, when the arrangement the master can see is preserved or re-derived, when the ground's return stays reachable at each case and cannot be moved by agreement between master and apprentice, when successive cases differ and are selected against what the last one surfaced, and when the training habitat is reproduced rather than substituted with silent failures. This is a separator of conditions, not a list of named practices and not a reliability coefficient.
>
> I am deliberately **not** claiming that any particular practice (shadowing, storytelling, guided practice, a fixed duration) is the cause. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The specific list is a gap I am reporting, not a result.
>
> **What I take to be the anchor:** the outcome the master cannot move. A joint that holds, a patient who recovers, a boat that arrives, a yield. Agreement between master and apprentice is not evidence for a verdict about the world; if they draw on the same evidence, they are one frame with two names.
>
> **What would refute the claim, written before I sent this:**
> 1. a proposition-only channel, with no ground contact and no master, producing the same per-case outcome on a changed question as a guided apprenticeship with ground contact;
> 2. the arrangement shown to be fully recoverable from the written ingredient list in a domain where I say it is not;
> 3. per-case reliability unchanged when the ground's return is replaced by agreement with a master's report;
> 4. a lineage of identical cases, all with the same closure and the same question, keeping reliability on a changed question;
> 5. a substituted training habitat reproducing the goal with no silent failures on a changed question;
> 6. a programme average that hides a live per-case failure.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figure text and the derivation are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework file): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - The full audit and its retraction log: `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` in this folder
> - The expert verification packet: `08_expert_verification_packet.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the loop is in `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result **before** you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Contact discipline

One recipient at a time. No list ranked by likely agreement, because selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40). No personal emails. Use only the public, opt-in, institutional channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Where a venue publishes an AI policy, comply with it: the Academy of Management, for example, states that submitted work must be created by the authors and not be a product of AI tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited. This packet is a simulated derivation and must be disclosed as such if it is ever submitted.
