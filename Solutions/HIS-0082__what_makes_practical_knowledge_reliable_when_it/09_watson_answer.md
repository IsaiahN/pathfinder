## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the reliability of practical knowledge transmitted by apprenticeship rather than explicit theory. Option A: reliability lives in the content, so a sufficiently complete explicit theory can reproduce it. Option B: reliability lives in the contact structure, so what crosses a handoff must be a generator and a non-updating ground must stay reachable at each slot. What each leads to: A leads to better manuals; B leads to maintaining generators, differing rooms and ground contact, with manuals used as head starts rather than as substitutes for contact. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Why R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| stays small across learners and a lineage under one transmission regime and grows under another. Arity: at least three-place (master's practice, learner's action, ground's return), plus a fourth term across a lineage; ordered bond (→ sequence) and a comparison (⋛ threshold), not symmetric. Symmetry: master and learner are not interchangeable; two apprentices reading the same manual are one evidence pool, two apprentices ranging against the ground are two frames (Fig 2 :31). Scale: individual loop, master-apprentice membrane, room chain across generations, habitat.
- **D1 The practice loop.** Actors: apprentice, materials, tools, action. Currency: residual reduction per slot. Anchor: the ground's return (joint holds, patient recovers, boat arrives). Admitted.
- **D2 The transmission membrane.** Actors: master, apprentice, demonstration, correction. Currency: methods and head starts crossing, or a recording crossing. Anchor: whether what crossed is a generator or a one-occasion description. Admitted.
- **D3 The representation seam.** Actors: atoms (ingredients, states) and bonds (arrangement, timing, feel). Currency: bits and actions, which do not add (Fig 12 :125-127). Anchor: whether the arrangement is recorded and can travel. Admitted.
- **D4 The habitat and lineage.** Actors: practitioners, instruments, conditions, relations, successive rooms. Currency: survival, arrival, variation and selection. Anchor: the ground, which does not update; selection between rooms. Admitted.
- **D5 The explicit-theory frame.** Actors: propositions, manuals, curricula. Currency: compression and codifiability. Anchor: experiment when used as a generator, agreement with the text when used as a recording. Admitted as a compound with D2 and D3, not a separate answer.
- **X1 "Talent" as free-floating property.** Actors: the gifted individual. Currency: talent. Anchor: none that does not update on the outcome. Not a domain yet; excluded by the anchor test.
- **Union, not partition.** Five overlapping conditions (generator, arrangement, anchor, differing rooms, reproduced habitat), each with its own loop. Overlaps are compounds, so one lever (write a better manual) leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, :57).
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception of the return → vocabulary for what the situation affords → objective (what counts as progress) → planning (a route) → learn and carry. The chain breaks at the middle link, which has no instrument (Fig 3 :27). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 4 (scale membrane) and Fig 12 (record boundary) both say what crosses is a method rather than a recording, but they govern different quantities, so no strain after re-census. Fig 2 (non-updating anchor) and Fig 11 (valence relative to residual) govern settling versus helping, so no strain. See `01` §3.

**D4 The habitat and lineage, status triangulated (Fig 7, Fig 10 and Fig 11, room selection plus channel decay plus habitat substitution).**

- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> the transmission channel can fail while the ground stays put.
- Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." -> a classroom substitutes a habitat.
- Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" -> the first silent failure.
- Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." -> a simulation proves wiring only.
Mode `specified`. Refuter: an identical-rooms lineage keeping R low on a changed question, or a substituted habitat with no silent failures.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reliability is a union of conditions, not a property of content and not a single dial. Practical knowledge transmitted by apprenticeship stays reliable when a re-applicable method and head start cross the handoff rather than a recording of one occasion; when the bond arrangement is preserved or re-derived; when a non-updating ground stays reachable at each slot so master-learner agreement cannot stand in for the outcome; when successive cases differ and are chosen against the surfaced residual; and when the habitat is reproduced with its silent failures enumerated. Verdicts are per domain and per slot, kept separate and never averaged. Which named practices occupy the slots and what coefficient measures them is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The figures decide; the world sources corroborate the mechanism shape but carry no verdict weight.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A wrong separator would misdirect how a craft is taught, for example supplying more documentation where the failure is a decayed channel or a substituted habitat. Because the packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42-43). The five conditions and six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet without withdrawing a primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the six pre-registered falsifiers: a proposition-only channel matching guided apprenticeship on a changed question; the arrangement fully recoverable from the ingredient list; reliability unchanged when the ground return is replaced by a master's report; an identical-rooms lineage keeping R low on a changed question; a substituted habitat reproducing the goal with no silent failures; or an average hiding a live per-case failure. A source-discriminating instrument that names the practice list and the coefficient would close the `gap` and move the verdict toward `grounded`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
