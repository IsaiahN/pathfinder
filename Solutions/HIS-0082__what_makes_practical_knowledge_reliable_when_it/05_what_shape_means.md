## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the five-condition separator in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F9: generator across a membrane, arrangement preserved, non-updating ground reachable, differing rooms, habitat reproduced. A list of named practices (shadowing, storytelling, a four-year term) is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* The residual was described in effect terms (Section 0 of `01`) before any field was named.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is exactly why the question is treated as a union, not as one dial (Fig 9 :55-57).
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* The answer here stops at a shape, not an exhaustive practice inventory.

## 3. The governing essay: what "shape" means for apprenticeship specifically

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the closest thing in the corpus to a direct treatment of this exact question. Its claims map cleanly onto the figure findings:

- **What writing drops.** *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The pretext is the learner's rebuilt history; the bond is the arrangement. Same object, two vocabularies. Supports F2.
- **What contact does.** *"the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."* Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Contact is the channel that carries the method. Supports F1.
- **The learner is not a receiver.** *"An apprentice isn't receiving messages; they're slowly building the history that makes the messages make sense."* This is Fig 11 :39-40 at the individual stratum: capability is agent-and-habitat; an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. Supports F8.
- **Correction is the return trip.** *"Correction is subtext on the return trip... When a master says 'no, like this', the words carry almost nothing. The meaning is in the unstated gap between what you did and what they show you."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The essay adds a usable instrument: *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* This is a genuine measurement proposal, recorded in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §5 as something that would move the result toward `grounded`.
- **The loop must run both ways.** *"The loop only closes if the learner acts; the highlighted step is where the correction arrives, unstated."* And: *"One-way subtext can't correct anything, because the teacher never sees how it was understood."* A proposition-only channel is one-way; this is the mechanism behind F1's refuter.
- **Agreement is not the check.** *"that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* The essay's three safeguards are *"a ground that both are corrected by, separately,"* *"separate experience"* (*"The apprentice needs time alone at the bench as well as time beside the master"*), and *"claims in effect terms."* These are F5, F6 and the Fig 9 :30-31 description rule.
- **Drift.** *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
- **The manual outliving the craft.** *"A manual outlives the craft when no one is left who can read it as it was meant."* Fig 12 :109: *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."* Supports F3.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a specific practice as *the* cause is a binding from the education or craft frame onto held atoms. It is allowed as a candidate with provenance; it is not a derivation. What is forbidden is inventing a seventh condition or claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

## 5. Source discrimination: the missing instrument, characterised

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the shape of what would close the remaining gap. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And the key instruction: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved it.

Applied here: a low per-slot R after training is a reading. It does not tell you whether the source was method transfer, a recording replay, a substituted habitat, or agreement with the master's report. Several paths give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a further argument. Fig 8 :54: *"underivable is not unknowable."* The discriminator, in effect terms: an instrument or design that varies one condition at a time (channel, bond visibility, anchor, room difference, habitat fidelity) and reads per-slot outcomes on a changed question. Decoding Tacit Knowledge's correction-size trend is one candidate for part of it.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading (kept visible)

I initially wrote F9 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any practice at all. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Method, head start, bond, ground, room and habitat are all already available atoms. Naming one as a condition is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework selects one named practice as *the* answer, and (b) claiming the binding adds an atom or moves the wall. The genuine gap is narrower than I first said: the concrete practice list and the numerical coefficient, not the naming of held atoms. Same correction is recorded in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 and in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6.

A second overstatement is also corrected here and kept visible: I treated *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* as independent corroboration. It is by the framework's author, so its overlap with the figures is maximal. It explains and extends the figure claims; it does not corroborate them from a separate evidence pool. The genuinely low-overlap world witness is the animal-behaviour material in `09_watson_answer.md`.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A roster of famous apprenticeship advocates cannot settle whether this separator is right. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried against per-slot outcomes and lets the ground settle it. The essay's own warning applies: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
