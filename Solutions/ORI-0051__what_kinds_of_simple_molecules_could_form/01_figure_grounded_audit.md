## 0. The residual in effect terms

The residual is not "which molecule?" asked in the abstract. It is the gap between what a prebiotic setting could supply and what a boundary must do for the word *cell-like* to apply. Stated per slot, because aggregation is how the signal dies (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22):

- **Slot A, separation:** an inside must be distinguishable from an outside.
- **Slot B, permeability:** the separation must be selective. Nutrients in, waste out, useful polymers retained.
- **Slot C, persistence:** the enclosure must survive the actual pH, salt, temperature and feedstock of its setting.
- **Slot D, propagation:** it must grow and divide, so differential persistence can compound.
- **Slot E, composition:** the behaviour must depend on arrangement as well as ingredients.

The ground for slots B to E is an experiment or a geochemical record, not agreement among readers. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

The bound shape from the README is carried into every finding: a selectively permeable, physically self-assembling enclosure, built from molecules available without enzymes, that maintains a distinguishable interior, grows and divides by intrinsic physical processes, and whose behaviour is not recoverable from its ingredient list.

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A molecule that passes slot B may fail slot C; that is not a contradiction to be averaged, it is two verdicts.
- **Symmetry:** the boundary relation is **ordered**, not symmetric. It has an inside and an outside, and what crosses in one direction is not what crosses in the other. Fig 4 :21 names the shape: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. That is a **different stratum**, and the reading is an analogue, not a chemical claim. The strain test in §4 makes this explicit rather than smoothing it over.

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 — The question is a union of conditions, not one chemical fact (decisive for structure)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The world literature confirms the union rather than replacing it. Deamer 2017 lists *"What amphiphilic compounds might have formed the permeability barrier of primitive cell membranes?"* as only one of three questions, alongside polymer encapsulation and the marine-versus-freshwater question. Milshteyn et al. 2018 list five. Ekosso et al. 2026 vary chain length, headgroup, pH and ionic strength simultaneously. These are conditions on one another, not alternative answers to one question.

**Refuter:** if all five slots were shown to be governed by one variable such that passing one entails passing all, the union reading collapses to a partition. A single-phase diagram for all conditions would do it.

### F2 — A boundary is a directed filter, not a wall

Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."*

**Mode `general`**, because Figure 4's own subject is the boundary between one agent and a population, not a lipid bilayer. The structural shape transfers across strata under the framework's own domain-agnostic rule, but the figure does not decide any chemical fact. The corroboration is empirical and independent: Deamer 2017 writes that *"the barrier is not absolute, but instead permeability varies over eight orders of magnitude for different solutes"*; Budin and Szostak 2011 show that fatty-acid membranes are permeable to polar nutrients while phospholipid membranes retain metabolites, and that the transition imposes new selective pressures for transport. A wall that retains everything is a failure of slot B, not a stricter version of a boundary.

**Refuter:** a compartment that is impermeable to every solute and still supports growth, division and an internal chemistry would break the directed-filter claim.

### F3 — The molecule is a bonded arrangement; the formula is not the substance

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :48: *"A molecule is a bonded arrangement. A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."* Fig 12 :44: *"the arrangement is priced or it is free"*

An amphiphile has a polar head and a nonpolar tail. Deamer 2017: *"An amphiphile is defined as a molecule having both a non-polar hydrocarbon moiety and a polar head group."* Knowing that a sample contains C12 acid and C12 monoglyceride is not knowing that a 1:1 mixture forms stable vesicles across acidic and alkaline hot-spring water while the pure acid crystallises (Milshteyn et al. 2018). The arrangement, including the mixing ratio, carries the behaviour.

**Refuter:** if the ingredient list alone predicted vesicle formation, permeability and pH tolerance over the tested range, with no loss from omitting arrangement and ratio, Fig 12 :5 would be refuted as applied here.

### F4 — Which molecule is not derivable; the framework returns a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

No figure line yields "decanoic acid" or "dodecyl phosphate". Those are bindings from the chemistry frame onto the composition slot, arrived at by experiment. The framework can say *what a candidate must be able to do* and can refuse candidates that fail the shape. It cannot, from the six terms and seven bonds, name the molecule that actually did it on the early Earth. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Refuter:** a derivation of a specific molecular identity from the figure set alone, with no experimental or geochemical import and with the source gate shown, would break this refusal. Note the asymmetry: the identity is not unanswerable in principle, it is answered by another frame (the ground), so this is unreached, not unreachable (Fig 8 :39 and the scope note at Fig 8 :40-43).

### F5 — Capability is agent-and-habitat; a membrane alone is not a protocell

Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* Fig 11 :46: *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*

The habitat is read off the world. Deamer 2017 finds self-assembly favoured in *"low ionic strength solutions with minimal content of salt and divalent cations"* and infers freshwater pools on volcanic islands; Milshteyn et al. 2018 report *"None of the amphiphiles that were tested formed stable vesicles in sea water because the high ionic concentrations disrupted membrane stability."* The same molecule passes in one habitat and fails in another. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A better amphiphile in the wrong water is not progress.

**Refuter:** a membrane that forms, persists, grows and divides identically in every tested habitat, independent of pH and ionic strength, would dissolve the habitat condition. Ekosso et al. 2026 report the opposite: vesicle formation, CVC and permeability *"vary systematically with amphiphile composition and environmental conditions."*

### F6 — Growth and division is a cycle and a competition, not a design

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 11 :15: *"nothing selects; what survives is what the ground kept"* Fig 11 :36: *"Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*

Budin and Szostak 2011 report that low levels of phospholipid drive fatty-acid vesicle growth by reducing fatty-acid efflux, and that growth into filamentous vesicles followed by gentle shear gives division *"without the need for preexisting cellular machinery."* That is the cycle closing: the output (a grown vesicle) re-enters as input (a dividing vesicle), and differential persistence is supplied by contact, not by an author. Selection needs no maintainer here.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated growth-and-division mechanism that requires an encoded catalyst to be present at the start, with no physical pathway before it, would break the claim that propagation is intrinsic to the boundary. The harness caveat matters: Fig 11 :70 — *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A robot that assembles liposomes proves the wiring, not that the first boundary did it itself.

### F7 — Candidates are a functional group, not one substance; the mixture is the unit

Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*

Fatty acids, fatty alcohols, monoglycerides, alkylamines, alkyl sulfates and alkyl phosphates are members of a functional group in the figure's sense: a head that interacts with water and a tail that excludes it. They are not isomers of one another, because they differ in composition, not just arrangement. Within a headgroup family, chain length and headgroup identity shift behaviour enough that Ekosso et al. 2026 find *"DDS and DDP agree in approximately 61% of cases, with disagreement observed in 39% of samples."* The unit of selection may be the mixture, not the pure species: Milshteyn et al. 2018 find the 1:1 lauric-acid/monoglyceride mixture *"less temperature-sensitive and assembled into relatively stable membranes at both acidic and alkaline pH ranges."*

**Refuter:** if all candidate families behaved identically across the tested conditions, the functional-group distinction and the mixture advantage both fail.

### F8 — Two currencies, and the budget decides how much of the space is reached

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126 (substring): *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*

A candidate membrane can be cheap to state (one headgroup, one chain length) and expensive to settle (does it encapsulate RNA at pH 3.3 in real hot-spring water?). The reverse also occurs. Ekosso et al. 2026 needed automated sample preparation and confocal microscopy over hundreds of condition combinations to answer a question that is one line to write down. A literature list of "possible membrane molecules" prices only the description and then proposes arrangements it cannot afford to test. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Refuter:** a single currency that predicted both description cost and experimental settle cost with no residual would break this finding. It is named as a structural constraint, not measured here.

### F10 — What survives after a genuine re-census

The defensible residue, each with its mode:

1. A first boundary must be a directed filter, not a seal. `general` / `specified`.
2. It must be a bonded arrangement whose head-tail structure and mixing ratio carry behaviour the ingredient list does not. `specified`.
3. It must be formable and persistent in an enumerated habitat, with low divalent cation concentration and a temperature above the gel-fluid transition as reported conditions. `grounded` on reports, `specified` as a condition.
4. Growth and division can be intrinsic physical properties of fatty-acid vesicles, so no machinery is owed at that step. `grounded` on the report, single-source.
5. Which molecular kind actually formed the first boundary, and where, is a `gap` reported as a verdict, not as silence.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is:

> A first cell-like boundary must be a selectively permeable, composition-bearing enclosure that persists in an enumerated habitat and can grow and divide by intrinsic physical processes. The chemical identity of the first such boundary is a ground fact, not a figure output.

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Filter condition fails:** a boundary impermeable to all solutes still supports an interior chemistry, growth and division (breaks Fig 4 :21 and the permeability contrast reported by Deamer 2017).
2. **Arrangement condition fails:** ingredient lists predict formation, stability, permeability and pH tolerance with no information lost by omitting head-tail structure and mixing ratio (breaks Fig 12 :5 and the mixture result of Milshteyn et al. 2018).
3. **Habitat condition fails:** the same molecular kind has the same formation, persistence and permeability in every tested pH and ionic regime (breaks Fig 11 :39 and the condition-dependence reported by Ekosso et al. 2026).
4. **Intrinsic propagation fails:** growth and division require an encoded catalyst present at the start, with no physical pathway before it (breaks Fig 12 :121 and the reported shear division).
5. **Figure identity claim fires:** a specific molecular identity is derived from the figure set alone, with source gate and shadow test shown (breaks Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134-135). This would *strengthen* the framework and simultaneously refute this folder's refusal.
6. **Per-slot rule fails:** one variable governs all five slots such that passing one entails passing all (breaks Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :55).

If any fires, the affected claim is withdrawn and the re-census is redone from the residual line. Until one is run, no part of the figure-governed shape may be labelled settled by the figures alone.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :55 gives a union of overlapping conditions; unbundle before searching. Five slots survive.
2. **What form must a boundary have?** A directed filter, not a wall (Fig 4 :21, at `general` mode), with arrangement-bearing behaviour (Fig 12 :5).
3. **Which molecules are candidates?** Head-and-tail amphiphiles and their mixtures (Deamer 2017; Milshteyn et al. 2018; Ekosso et al. 2026). This is a world binding, not a figure output.
4. **Which habitat?** Enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43): dilute ionic strength, pH near the headgroup pK, temperature above the gel-fluid transition. Read off the world.
5. **Can it grow and divide?** Yes under reported conditions (Budin and Szostak 2011): a cycle closes (Fig 12 :121) and selection runs without a maintainer (Fig 11 :36).
6. **Is the identity derivable?** No. Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134-135. Route to the ground; record `gap` on the historical case.
7. **What would reverse the verdict?** A source-discriminating experiment or rock record naming the first boundary, a refuter in §5 firing, or a figure re-census admitting a chemical derivation. Until then, candidate.

### Updated verdict

The figure-governed shape survives: a first boundary is a directed, selective, arrangement-bearing filter, formable in an enumerated habitat and capable of intrinsic growth and division, with its chemical identity left to the ground. The candidate set from the world sources is real and checkable, dominated by single-chain amphiphiles and their mixtures rather than by modern phospholipids, and strongly habitat-dependent. The historical question, which kind came first and where, is a declared `gap` reported as a verdict. Strains after re-census: one analogue labelled, none hidden.
