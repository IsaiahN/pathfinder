## 1. The question restated as an effect

"Which simple molecules could form the first cell-like boundaries?" is not one question with one answer. In effect terms it asks which molecular kinds can satisfy four separable conditions at once:

1. **Separate an inside from an outside** without being a perfect seal. A boundary that retains everything also starves the interior; a boundary that retains nothing is not a boundary.
2. **Persist in a real habitat** of given pH, ionic strength, temperature and available feedstock, while remaining composed of molecules the setting could supply without modern metabolism.
3. **Grow and divide** by physical processes, so that differential persistence can compound without pre-existing cellular machinery.
4. **Be a composition, not an ingredient list.** The same atoms in a different arrangement behave differently; the head-and-tail arrangement is the substance, not the formula.

The options lead to different consequences. A pure single species in seawater gives crystals, not vesicles (Milshteyn et al. 2018). A mixture in dilute freshwater gives metastable vesicles that encapsulate polymers (Deamer 2017; Milshteyn et al. 2018). A highly permeable fatty-acid membrane admits nutrients but leaks ions; a diacyl phospholipid membrane retains metabolites but needs transport machinery (Budin and Szostak 2011). So the answer is a **union of conditions with different anchors**, and verdicts must be kept per condition and never averaged (Fig 1 :19; Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", the sought-for shape is written down and bound before the run so it can accrue a residual like anything else:

> **First cell-like boundary (bound term):** a selectively permeable, physically self-assembling enclosure, built from molecules available without enzymes, that maintains a distinguishable interior in contact with a habitat, can grow and divide by intrinsic physical processes, and is a bonded arrangement whose behaviour is not recoverable from its ingredient list.

This is a shape, not a name. Any candidate molecule is checked against it, and the check is refusable.
