## 3. Two things in the world literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The site question is live, not settled.** Deamer 2017 infers freshwater pools on volcanic islands because divalent cations in seawater precipitate fatty acids; Milshteyn et al. 2018 report that none of their amphiphiles formed stable vesicles in seawater. The papers by Russell and Hall (1997) and Lane and Martin (2012) cited inside these articles argue for submarine hydrothermal settings. This folder does not average the dispute away. It records the habitat as an enumerated condition and leaves the historical site as a `gap`. The conflict rule sent the derivation back to the census and narrowed D3 from a site claim to a condition claim; it did not flip the figure verdict.

2. **The candidate unit may be the mixture, not the pure molecule.** Milshteyn et al. 2018 find the lauric-acid/monoglyceride mixture stable across acidic and alkaline hot-spring water where the pure acid is temperature- and pH-sensitive; Ekosso et al. 2026 find headgroup and chain-length changes shift the environmental boundaries in roughly a third to two-fifths of tested conditions. This supports Fig 12 :5 at the chemical level: the arrangement, including the mixing ratio, carries information the ingredient list does not. It is independent corroboration from a different experimental tradition, but the origins papers share authors and a research programme, so they are one evidence pool, not four witnesses.
