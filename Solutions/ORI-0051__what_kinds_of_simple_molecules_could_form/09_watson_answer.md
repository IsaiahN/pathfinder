## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a prebiotic setting can supply and what a boundary must do for "cell-like" to apply. Arity: five slots (separation, permeability, persistence, propagation, composition). Symmetry: ordered, inside versus outside, with different traffic in each direction; a boundary is a filter, not a wall. Scale: one compartment, with Figure 4's membrane as a cross-stratum analogue between one agent and a population. Fig 9 :55 gives a union of overlapping conditions; Fig 9 :57 says a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1, boundary form.** Actors: candidate amphiphiles, water, ions; the six substrate terms as a structural check only. Currency: composition across kinds, then settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for the shape, an experiment for the chemistry. Partition question: what may cross, and what composes.
- **D2, chemical identity.** Actors: fatty acids and their alcohol, monoglyceride and amine mixtures, decyl sulfate, dodecyl sulfate, dodecyl phosphate, polycyclic aromatic admixtures. Currency: physical prediction. Anchor: the experiment. The candidate names are bindings from this frame onto the composition slot, not new framework primitives.
- **D3, habitat and persistence.** Actors: hot spring water, seawater, wet-dry cycles, pH, divalent cations, temperature. Currency: contact, survival of the compartment. Anchor: what the setting kept (Fig 11 :15). Union question: conditions combine, they do not add.
- **D4, growth and division.** Actors: vesicles, monomers, shear, osmotic swelling, encapsulated polymers. Currency: differential persistence and surface growth. Anchor: an experiment that can show growth and division. Cycle, not chain.
- **D5, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers in membrane formation research. Currency: residual and refuter, never agreement. Anchor: the ground. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence here is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle the boundary form before the candidate; settle supply, concentration, medium and temperature before encapsulation and division (Fig 3 :20); identity is last and goes to the ground.

Strain test applied: Figure 4's membrane as a lipid claim was re-censused into a cross-stratum analogue at `general` mode; Fig 13 :75 as a derivation of head-tail composition was declined because it governs the six substrate kinds, not molecular chemistry. Both corrections stay visible in `01` §4.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the identity does not come from the figures. A first cell-like boundary must be a directed filter rather than a seal, a bonded composition whose headgroup, chain length and mixing ratio carry behaviour the ingredient list does not, formable and persistent in an enumerated habitat, and capable of growth and division by intrinsic physical processes. The candidate families from the world sources are members of a functional group rather than isomers, and the robust reported unit is often a mixture, not a pure species. Which composition actually formed the first boundary, and where, is a declared `gap` of the unreached-at-this-budget kind: the ground exists, and this derivation did not read it. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged: D1 `specified` plus `general` analogue, triangulated; D2 `grounded` on reports and `gap` on identity, single-source per experiment; D3 `grounded` on reports and `specified` as a condition, one pooled witness; D4 `grounded` on one reported mechanism, single-source; D5 `specified`, triangulated. The Figure 4 analogue is labelled; no other strain remains hidden.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each sub-claim has a refuter written before the run, and the candidate names carry provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a candidate list before the shape and its refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the shape and the refuter avoids it.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are removable without touching the figure-governed shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove any winner claim. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no framework primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating measurement or rock record naming the first boundary would fill the identity slot; an impermeable boundary that still supports growth and division, a pure species matching mixture stability across habitats, a division mechanism requiring an encoded catalyst at the start, or one variable governing all five slots would break the shape claim; a figure re-census admitting a chemical derivation of a molecular identity would reverse the refusal. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
