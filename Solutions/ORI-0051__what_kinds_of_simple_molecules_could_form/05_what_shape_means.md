## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0051 the distinction is concrete:

- **Shape:** a directed, selectively permeable, arrangement-bearing enclosure, formable in an enumerated habitat, capable of growth and division by intrinsic physical processes.
- **Name:** decanoic acid, dodecyl sulfate, dodecyl phosphate, a 1:1 lauric-acid/monoglyceride mixture.

The first constrains candidates and can be checked. The second is a claim about early Earth that must be settled by experiment or the rock record. A shape can reject a candidate ("a perfect seal fails"); it cannot select the winner among candidates that pass.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*
- Fig 6 :30 (read in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened 2026-11-23): *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is exactly the stage-1 email in `02_outreach_email.md`: a description of what a first boundary would look like, checkable without handing over the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the direct warrant for the union reading in `01` F1.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For ORI-0051, a vesicle-formation experiment, a permeability measurement or a geochemical assay pays or does not. Deamer 2017 and Milshteyn et al. 2018 report such payments for particular mixtures in particular waters. Those reports are `grounded` **as reported**; this derivation did not run them.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming dodecyl phosphate is an import from the physical-chemistry frame into the shape slot. Its provenance is the paper and the measurement; its shadow test is whether it explains a gap the shape left open (for example, stability at low pH in sea salt, which Ekosso et al. 2026 report).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A candidate molecule is not a new primitive of the framework; it is a world binding onto the composition slot. Saying otherwise would be claiming the wall moved when it did not (Fig 6 :21 — *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

## 4. Why "which molecule" is a metamerism problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it can only speak about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's conclusion: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and the case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here, several molecular kinds produce the same observable endpoint: a vesicle under a microscope. Deamer 2017 lists fatty acids, fatty-acid/alcohol mixtures, fatty-acid/monoglyceride mixtures, fatty-acid/alkylamine mixtures, fatty-acid/PAH mixtures, sodium dodecyl sulfate with dodecyl alcohol, and chlorosulfolipids as membrane-forming candidates. Milshteyn et al. 2018 add a 1:1 acid/monoglyceride mixture. Ekosso et al. 2026 compare decyl sulfate, dodecyl sulfate and dodecyl phosphate and find overlapping but non-identical outcomes. A microscopy image does not comment on which source produced it. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument or a geochemical record, not an argument from the image.

This is not a claim that the question is forever open. It is a claim that the *observation* underdetermines the *identity* until the instrument discriminates. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5. Q2 — What licenses a particular candidate? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Which simple molecule formed the first boundary?** Nothing in the figure set answers this. A particular candidate is licensed only by (a) a ground reading that discriminates among candidates, or (b) a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the candidate name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The honest output is the shape plus the candidate set with sources, not a winner.

**Does the folder commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the corrections in §6 hold. Naming decanoic acid is a binding from the chemistry frame, recorded with its source and habitat. It is not presented as a figure derivation, and it does not fill a missing primitive with a name the framework does not hold.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I first wrote §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any candidate. That overstates it. The README's phrase is about a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The composition slot is held; candidate molecules are world bindings onto it, not invented primitives of the framework. What remains wrong when a candidate is named without provenance is the metamerism error of §4: a source named from a projection. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on names. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction. I first read Figure 4's "filter with a direction" as a direct claim about lipid bilayers. It is not. Figure 4's subject is the membrane between one agent and a population. The use here is a cross-stratum structural analogue at `general` mode, and the chemical claim is carried by Deamer 2017's measured permeability contrast. The re-census is recorded in `01` §4 and `11_audit.md`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous origin-of-life researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried in a real membrane assay, and lets the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the chemistry and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
