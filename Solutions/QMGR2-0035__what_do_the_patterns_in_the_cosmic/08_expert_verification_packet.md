## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The CMB pattern is a per-slot residual against a sky anchor; the monopole is a pooled reading that hides it. The pattern constrains a hot, near-flat, highly uniform expansion whose perturbations are nearly scale-invariant, Gaussian and adiabatic, with the physical information carried by bonds between pixels (acoustic peak locations and ratios, E/B parity) rather than by pixels alone. Foregrounds, lensing and reionization are in the habitat of the residual and cannot be scoped away. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of channel that would close the open slot.
