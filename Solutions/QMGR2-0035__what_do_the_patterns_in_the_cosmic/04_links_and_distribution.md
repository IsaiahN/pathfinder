## What the wider literature contributes, and what it does not

The world sources are secondary corroboration only. Figures decide.

1. **Instrument and data records.** The NASA LAMBDA pages and the Planck 2018 abstract are the ground-visible record: COBE found the anisotropy, Planck measured the acoustic scale to 0.03 percent and the spectral index to n_s = 0.965 ± 0.004, and found no compelling evidence for extensions to the base model. They corroborate the per-slot shape constraints in F1 and F3. They do not select a mechanism: the same abstract explicitly reports no compelling extension, and the inflation record states the particle-physics mechanism is unknown.
2. **One cosmology evidence pool.** The three Wikipedia cosmology records plus the instrument pages all draw on the same experiments (COBE, WMAP, Planck, BICEP). Their agreement is one witness, not several (Fig 2 :40). Overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
3. **Low-overlap witness, statistics.** Effective sample size and design effect (survey statistics) corroborate Fig 2 :44 and F6: correlated observations reduce the information carried by a larger sample. This is a genuinely different field, so it is a witness for the *shape* of the independence claim, not for any cosmological fact.
4. **Low-overlap witness, philosophy of science.** Underdetermination corroborates F3 and F10 at the level of method: evidence can leave rival theories equally supported, and any phenomenon admits a multiplicity of hypotheses. It does not corroborate inflation; it corroborates the refusal to name a mechanism from a shape.
5. **Live-field check.** The arXiv listing shows many competing mechanisms still being tested (axion monodromy, warm inflation, CMB-S4 forecasts). That is corroboration for the F10 gap: if a figure line or a single dataset had already selected the mechanism, this diversity would not be the active state of the field.
6. **Conflict rule.** No world source conflicts with the figure verdict after re-census. The one apparent tension, "Planck confirms inflation" versus F10 gap, was returned to census and dissolved: confirming a *shape* (flat, Gaussian, adiabatic, nearly scale-invariant) is not naming a *mechanism*. The inflation record itself says the detailed mechanism is unknown. A conflict sends the derivation back to the census; it never flips the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - agreement disqualifies a verdict when frames share a pool. A send list selected for people likely to agree that inflation happened produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A claim about the first instant is high-stakes; independent readers are worth more here than a large agreeing audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before any theory is shown; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter per problem. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
