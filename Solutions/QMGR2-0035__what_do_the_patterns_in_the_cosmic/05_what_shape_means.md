## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, a shape rather than a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; the surrounding text says the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *a hot, near-flat, uniform expansion with nearly scale-invariant, Gaussian, adiabatic seeds* is a shape;
- *inflation driven by a specific inflaton potential, followed by a specific reheating history* is a name;
- *a channel that discriminates tensor from scalar perturbations at last scattering without foreground degeneracy* is a shape;
- *BICEP2 detected inflation* is a name, and it was withdrawn after foreground analysis.

The whole package stays on the left side of that line. F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records the right side as a gap.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then bootstrapping: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The starting vocabulary here is residual, slot, anchor, import, independence, bond, threshold. Each source (COBE anisotropy, acoustic peaks, peak ratios, polarization modes, effective sample size) narrows what a discriminating channel would have to be.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "a pattern whose per-slot statistics a model cannot predict without importing a mechanism" before choosing between particle physics, statistics, or philosophy of science.
- The exemplar's canonical reading of *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: a plain description of what you would expect to see is sendable because anyone can check it against the world. Used here only as the canonical reading already recorded in the exemplar, not as an independently opened source this session.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place. This shape stops when the vocabulary returns to the residual it started from: per-slot, anchored, imported only with provenance, independent before agreement counts. It does not stop with a named inflaton.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question an instrument or a channel that discriminates two mechanisms pays; an argument does not. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. n_s and r are imports of this kind: licensed by the measurement record, not minted by the figures. Jumping from those imports to a specific potential is an unprovenanced binding.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"the spectrometer, not an argument"* that resolved the case. Applied here: the angular power spectrum is a projection. It cannot say whether the source was slow-roll inflation, a bounce, or an ekpyrotic onset. Naming the source from the projection is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating channel, not a name. This matters historically: the BICEP2 B-mode claim was attributed to cosmic dust after new Planck results, a textbook case of a source named from a reading that a better discriminator overturned.

## 4. Why a mechanism name is the specific forbidden error

A list of "the inflation model that caused the CMB" reads the artifact (the pattern) and names its source (the cause) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. Several models generate the same green: slow-roll potentials, Starobinsky gravity, warm inflation, and alternatives can all produce nearly scale-invariant Gaussian adiabatic perturbations. The behaviour is the same; the source is not. Fig 2 :40 says agreement is worthless when the frames share an evidence pool. The arXiv astro-ph.CO listing opened this session shows many such models still under active test, which is what underdetermination looks like from inside a live field.

This is not a claim that inflation research is useless. It is a claim about what the framework can and cannot deliver. The framework returns the constraint a discriminating channel must satisfy; a measurement with a ground can name the mechanism.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid mentioning n_s, r, or inflation at all? ANSWERED

**No, and the scope matters.** The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in the frame. n_s, r, flatness and adiabaticity are already in the cosmology frame's vocabulary; they are bindings from the measurement onto available terms, not inventions of missing primitives. Naming one is not forbidden. What is forbidden is claiming that the binding is a figure derivation, or that it moves the wall without provenance. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."*

Inflation is a frame in D3 whose closure predicts the shape. It is admissible as a candidate frame. What is forbidden is promoting it to a figure result or a final room. So the honest form is: *"A candidate frame is admissible if it predicts the observed shape with provenance and survives an independent channel; which frame is correct is a ground question."* It is not *"inflation is the answer."*

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to this question

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A famous satellite, a large collaboration, a Nobel Prize, or a widely cited model is prestige in this question. It can give a claim a budget to be tried: it gets telescope time and data release. It cannot settle whether the claim is true. A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a mechanism is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any cosmological claim and must be labelled as such. The CMB measurements it discusses were made by other people and are cited with provenance; this author reduced no map and fitted no spectrum. The package cannot settle anything it reports on. The two-stage offer in `02_outreach_email.md` exists so that a real outside ground can be consulted before the framework is shown.
