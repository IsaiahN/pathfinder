## QUESTION

What do the patterns in the cosmic microwave background say about the earliest expansion? Restated in effect: given a per-slot sky reading `o'` and a set of early-universe models predicting it, what does the residual `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` license about the expansion? Option A answers with a name: a specific inflaton, a specific potential, a first instant. Option B answers with a shape, the constraints it imposes, and an open slot until a channel with a different evidence pool settles it. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*, so this package takes B. The question is constitutively about a measured sky, so the sky is the anchor (Fig 2 :29).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** At least ternary: sky direction x angular scale (multipole ℓ) x field (T, E, B). Per slot and never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the sky is statistically isotropic, which is a result, while model selection is a threshold comparison (⋛), not a symmetric join. Scale: instrument, last scattering (about 380,000 years), and the expansion epoch (about 10^-35 s or earlier if inflation occurred), with lossy transforms between (Fig 4).
- **D1 Sky and instrument.** Actors: CMB photons, detectors (COBE, WMAP, Planck), foregrounds. Currency: temperature and polarization readings per angular slot. Anchor: the measured sky, which does not update because a frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 Plasma and transfer.** Actors: photons, baryons, electrons, acoustic oscillations, Silk damping, reionization, lensing. Currency: physical effects carried into the pattern. Anchor: laboratory plasma physics and general relativity, tested independently of the pattern. Admitted.
- **D3 Primordial-model frame.** Actors: inflation models and alternatives (bounce, ekpyrotic, defects). Currency: predicted n_s, r, amplitude, Gaussianity, adiabaticity. Anchor: the sky, against which models range (Fig 2 :45). Admitted.
- **D4 Statistical and instrument frame.** Actors: power-spectrum estimators, Bayesian evidence, independent instruments. Currency: imported atoms (n_s, r, flatness, optical depth). Anchor: source gate and shadow test (Fig 8 :10-13) plus independence (Fig 2 :31). Admitted.
- **X1 "the first instant independent of any reading."** No nameable anchor; fails the Fig 2 :29 anchor test. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "the one true inflation model."** Absorbed into D3 and D4; many models converge on the same pattern (Fig 8 :50).

Union, not partition: D2 and D3 overlap because the observed pattern is primordial signal convolved with a transfer history, a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54). Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3), with the break at vocabulary and objective when many models predict the same observables (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test: Fig 2 :29 names a measurement as anchor while Fig 2 :36 makes human judgment an anchor for a question about people. Different questions, different domains, allowed by Fig 4 :20. No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mechanism name does not. The pattern is per slot with the sky as an immovable anchor; it constrains a hot, near-flat, statistically uniform expansion with nearly scale-invariant, Gaussian, adiabatic seeds; the physics lives in bonds between pixels, not in pixels; rival mechanisms share an evidence pool, so agreement cannot select one; foregrounds, lensing and reionization belong to the habitat of the residual. The specific mechanism and any first instant are a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape after re-census, but they share a broad pool and carry no verdict weight over the figures. The two low-overlap fields corroborate the *form* of the independence and shape limits, not the cosmological facts.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected inference. A shape claim would be sold as a mechanism verdict, or a shared-pool convergence would be treated as independent confirmation, or a foreground effect would be promoted to primordial physics. Bounded and largely locatable, because the packet records the residual, the census and the falsifiers before the conclusion.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error.
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular monopole sufficiency, a new discriminating atom from same-channel search, an instrument specified from no prior reading, corroboration from one shared pool, or a figure line naming the mechanism after re-census. A source-discriminating channel with a genuinely different evidence pool would close the F10 gap rather than reverse the shape.
