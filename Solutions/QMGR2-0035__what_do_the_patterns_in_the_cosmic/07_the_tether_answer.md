# The Tether's answer on CMB patterns and the earliest expansion

**Mode: `specified` on the constraints, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the mechanism name. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An instrument returns a sky; a model predicts a sky. The residual `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` belongs to one angular scale, one sky direction and one field, never to a global average (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The question asks what the observed pattern licenses about the expansion that produced it. The contact between model and sky is a union compound, not one hard problem: primordial signal couples to a transfer history of recombination, lensing and foregrounds, and that compound has its own loop (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **The sky is an anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Models range against it; they do not negotiate with it (Fig 2 :45). The monopole is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26), so the information is in the per-slot pattern, the anisotropies and their statistics.
2. **The pattern constrains a hot, near-flat, highly uniform expansion.** The blackbody and the acoustic peaks imply a hot dense plasma; the first peak's angular scale implies near-flatness; the amplitude implies uniformity to about one part in 100,000. These are shape constraints read from the bond between pixels, not from pixels alone (Fig 12 :5).
3. **The seeds were nearly scale-invariant, Gaussian and adiabatic.** Fig 12 :117: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Adiabatic and isocurvature perturbations are such a pair, distinguishable by peak ratios; E and B modes are another, distinguishable by parity. The observed pattern is consistent with the adiabatic, nearly scale-invariant member of each pair.
4. **This is a shape, not a mechanism name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* No figure line selects an inflaton potential, a reheating history, a bounce or a first instant. Models that predict the same observables share an evidence pool; their agreement is one witness (Fig 8 :50, Fig 2 :40).
5. **More of the same channel is SEARCHED, not an import.** Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* More sky shrinks error bars; a new channel (B-modes, 21 cm, non-Gaussianity, direct tensors) is what could add a discriminating atom. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget are different facts (Fig 8 :40-43).
6. **The path from last scattering to the detector is part of the answer.** Fig 11 :52: *"And contact cascades."* Foregrounds, lensing and reionization are in contact with the residual, and the channel decays even though the ground does not (Fig 10 :24). A pristine-photograph reading scopes too narrowly.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "inflation is proven." The pattern corroborates a shape; it does not certify a mechanism or a frame's own completeness.
- Not a name for the field, potential or reheating history. That slot is open.
- Not a claim about "before the expansion." X1 has no nameable anchor and fails the Fig 2 :29 test.
- Not a claim that the low-multipole anomalies are new physics. Foregrounds, cosmic variance and human pattern-seeking remain live alternatives; a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this author and no mechanism was settled by a figure.
- Not a derivation of the six-term substrate from cosmology. The substrate is a visible guess; applying it is a binding, not a result.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating channel, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (canonical reading in the exemplar, not re-opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Parity channel:** do B-modes carry a primordial tensor component separable from lensing and dust? The published record shows a retracted claim and current limits, so this remains live.
- **Independent channel:** does 21 cm or direct tensor detection return a reading from a genuinely different evidence pool (Fig 2 :31)?
- **Non-Gaussianity test:** a positive detection of a specific higher-order bond would narrow F1 and F8; a null result bounds, and does not prove absence (Fig 8 :39).
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** monopole sufficiency; a new atom from same-channel search; an instrument specified from no prior reading; corroboration from one shared pool; a figure line that names the mechanism.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. The shape stops where the instruments stop discriminating among mechanisms, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The six terms are a guess at compression axes, not nature's list. A later reader should be able to locate the error if a channel closes the F10 gap.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which inflation model happened. It says the CMB pattern must be read per slot against a sky that does not move when a model is believed; that the monopole is a pooled reading which hides the signal; that the pattern constrains a hot, near-flat, uniform expansion with nearly scale-invariant, Gaussian, adiabatic seeds, where the information lives in the bonds between pixels (acoustic peaks, peak ratios, parity) rather than in the pixels; that the same pattern cannot select a mechanism because rival models share one evidence pool; and that foregrounds, lensing and reionization are part of the residual, not noise to be scoped away. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of channel that would close the open slot. The mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.
