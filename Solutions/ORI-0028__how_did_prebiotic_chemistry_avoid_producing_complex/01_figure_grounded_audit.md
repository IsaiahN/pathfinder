## 0. The residual under audit, stated in effect terms

> "How did prebiotic chemistry avoid producing complex mixtures dominated by unwanted or harmful compounds?"

Restated as an effect, before any figure line is chosen:

- Prebiotic chemistry held a feedstock of small molecules and free energy. The set of compositions reachable from those atoms and bonds is large. Most reachable arrangements are not useful to a later living system.
- `R` is the gap between the unselective distribution chemistry tends toward (tars, non-proteinogenic analogues, isomer clutter) and the narrow functional subset life would later require.
- The question presupposes an agent that "avoided" an outcome and a standard by which some compounds are "unwanted or harmful." Neither presupposition is available before life exists. The first move is therefore a **refusal**, not an explanation (Formula step 2: a REFUTED term owes a refusal, and no arrangement of the other four reaches it).

`R` by arity, symmetry, scale:

- **Arity:** per molecular species, a distribution over product slots, not one global number. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Symmetry:** isomerism means many arrangements share the same atoms. Fig 12 :92 "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing." Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings but one term. The product space has huge spelling multiplicity.
- **Scale:** first order inside the molecular closure; second order at the habitat and its cycles. The membrane between them is Fig 4.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The complex mixture is the expected arrangement surplus, not an accident (governs D2)

Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Fig 13 :117 to :118: **"a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach."**

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**

Fig 8 :32: **"no search, however long, adds an atom."**

A small feedstock gives a closed set of atom kinds and an unbounded space of arrangements. Chemistry explores the arrangements. The tar is what that exploration looks like from inside. Fig 13 says the surplus is structural: a closed set with unbounded arrangements necessarily produces more combinations than the narrow subset that is useful. The classic Miller-Urey observation matches: after a week the trap solution was "a deep red and turbid" organic matter (world source, Wikipedia Miller-Urey). So the answer to "how did it avoid producing them" begins: **it did not avoid them.** The mixture is the default; the puzzle is how anything selective emerged from it.

**Mode:** `general` (structural). **Status:** triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 6 and Fig 8 (low overlap: minimum-set argument, closure operator, idempotence theorem).

**Refuter:** a figure re-census showing the arrangement space of the held atoms is finite and exhaustible, or a measurement that prebiotic mixtures were in fact dominated by life-useful compounds without any selective persistence. Either breaks this finding.

### F2 — There was no planner: selection was ground contact in the unmaintained habitat (governs D1)

Fig 11 :56: **"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."**

Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."**

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Before a living system exists there is no seat, no maintainer, no forward-looking avoidance. Selectivity is differential persistence: molecules that survive hydrolysis, dilution, UV, and cycling are the ones the ground kept. The world literature names the same process. Ball and Brindley (2025) call their surface oscillator "a very basic sorting and winnowing function, effectively a primitive form of selection." Asche et al. (2021) designed recursive dilution to "dilute out any product compounds that are not robust during the reaction cycle."

**Mode:** `specified` (the mechanism class is named, the instance is not). **Status:** triangulated across Fig 11 and two low-overlap world sources (mathematical biology; systems chemistry).

**Refuter:** show a maintainer or live frame operating prebiotically (a forward-looking selector), or show that differential persistence does not depend on contact with a ground. Either breaks this finding.

### F3 — "Unwanted or harmful" is frame-relative valence; the question is retrospective (governs D3)

Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**

Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

A compound is not harmful in itself. It is harmful relative to a residual, and the relevant residual here (a living system that would be harmed) does not yet exist. "Unwanted" is a projection backward from the biosphere. Ball and Brindley (2025) state the same point in world terms: "a pre-biotic world may have been perfectly satisfied with itself; it was not striving to ultimately produce us." Wikipedia's definition of life as "a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution" makes the dependence explicit: no such system, no standard of harm.

This is a **refusal**, not an explanation. Formula step 2: "REFUTED a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation." The term held is "chemistry avoided an outcome by a standard." What is owed is the removal of its standing to be composed over, not a new story about a planner.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 11 (valence) and Fig 2 (anchor), with a direct world corroboration.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a molecule carries "harm" as an intrinsic property independent of any residual, or that a prebiotic system had a standard of value before life. Either breaks the reframe.

### F4 — The mechanisms of narrowing are the figure's own structures (governs D4)

Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :119: **"A part that survives its own use."**

Fig 12 :121: **"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."**

The figure gives three structures that narrow a mixture and one rule that makes narrowing composable:

1. **Isomer separation.** Same atoms, different bond, different substance. Islam, Bučar and Powner (2017) report that "only the aminal of glyceraldehyde separates from the equilibrating mixture," beating the thermodynamically favoured isomerisation into dihydroxyacetone. This is Fig 12 :117 at the bench.
2. **Catalytic survival.** A part that survives its own use persists across cycles. Mineral surfaces and sequestering agents play this role.
3. **Cycle.** The output re-enters as input; a closing loop can amplify a selected product. Autocatalytic and thermochemical cycles are the world-side instances.
4. **Settlement.** Only what the ground settles becomes an atom for the next level. Persistence alone is not enough; it must be composable over.

**Mode:** `general` for the structures, `specified` for any binding of a particular structure to a particular molecule. **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 and three world sources, but the specific molecule is a `gap` (F6).

**Refuter:** show a narrowing mechanism in prebiotic chemistry that is not an isomer separation, a surviving part, a closing loop, or a ground settlement. That would send us back to the census.

### F5 — The mixture must be read per species, not averaged (method constraint on D1)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

A question about a "mixture dominated by unwanted compounds" invites a global statistic. The figure forbids averaging: the live signal is the per-species residual. In the robotic-chemist data (Asche et al. 2021), a global Mass Index can rise while thousands of individual species change; the per-slot reading is what carries the selectivity.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 1), corroborated by a world measurement but not triangulated across two figure lines.

**Refuter:** a figure re-census admitting a global mixture residual as the operative quantity.

### F6 — The specific chemical pathway is a gap after re-census (governs all domains)

The figures README: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 8 :39: **"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

After the genuine re-census, no figure line governs *which* molecule did the selecting or *which* setting hosted it. Reporting a single named pathway would be a name where the framework returns a shape.

**Mode:** `gap` on the instance, `general` on the shape. **Status:** gap, stated plainly. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.

**Refuter:** a figure line that derives a specific selector from the six terms, or a ground measurement that settles one pathway. Until one fires, no named mechanism is licensed.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives, the instance does not. Prebiotic chemistry did not "avoid" complex mixtures; the mixture is the structural default of a closed atom set with unbounded arrangements. Selectivity was differential ground contact in an unmaintained habitat, expressed through isomer separation, surviving parts and closing cycles, and it only became composable once a ground settled it. "Unwanted or harmful" is valence relative to a residual (life) that did not exist yet. The specific chemical pathway is a `gap`, reported as such. No strains remain hidden after re-census; the one vocabulary strain is named in section 1 and section 7.
