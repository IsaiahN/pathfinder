## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Prebiotic chemistry did not "avoid" complex mixtures; the mixture is the structural default of a closed set of atom kinds with unbounded arrangements. Selectivity was differential persistence under ground contact in an unmaintained habitat, no planner and no forward-looking avoidance, expressed through four structures the figure already names: isomer separation, a part that survives its own use, a loop whose output re-enters as input, and settlement that makes a product composable over. "Unwanted or harmful" is valence relative to a residual, life, that did not exist yet, so the question is retrospective and its framing owes a refusal. The framework does not name the selecting molecule or the setting; it returns a shape, not a name, and the specific pathway is recorded as a `gap`. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13, 6 and 8

- **Statement:** the mixture is an expected arrangement surplus. A closed set of atom kinds with a rich bond vocabulary yields more arrangements than the narrow functional subset. The tar is the default state, not an accident to be explained away.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :117-118 "a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach." Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."
- **Ground, secondary:** after a week the Miller-Urey trap solution was "a deep red and turbid" organic matter (Wikipedia, Miller-Urey experiment, ledger row 5).
- **Refuter:** show that the arrangement space of the held atoms is finite and exhaustible, or measure prebiotic mixtures dominated by life-useful compounds without any selective persistence. Either breaks C1.

### C2. Framework-internal plus chemistry-facing; checkable against Figure 12 and the literature

- **Statement:** the narrowing mechanisms are structures the figure already names, not imports that move the wall. Isomer separation, a surviving part, a closing loop, and a settled product that becomes an atom for the next level.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." Fig 5 :70 "A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful to the bench? Islam, Bučar and Powner (2017) report that "only the aminal of glyceraldehyde separates from the equilibrating mixture" (ledger row 1). Ball and Brindley (2025) call their surface oscillator "a very basic sorting and winnowing function, effectively a primitive form of selection" (ledger row 2). Asche et al. (2021) describe cycles "designed to dilute out any product compounds that are not robust during the reaction cycle" (ledger row 3). These are three different instances of the four structures, not one proved pathway.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a prebiotic narrowing mechanism that is not isomer separation, a surviving part, a closing loop, a functional group, or a ground settlement; or show that a settled product cannot become an atom for a later composition. Either sends the census back to the start.

### C3. Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 2 and 1

- **Statement:** the question's own framing is retrospective. There was no maintainer, selection was ground contact, valence was relative to a residual that did not exist, and the residual must be read per species.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :36 "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Ground, secondary:** Ball and Brindley (2025) state that "a pre-biotic world may have been perfectly satisfied with itself; it was not striving to ultimately produce us" (ledger row 2). NASA's definition of life, quoted on Wikipedia's Abiogenesis page, is "a self-sustaining chemical system capable of Darwinian evolution" (ledger row 4); no such system, no standard of harm.
- **Refuter:** show a maintainer or forward-looking selector operating prebiotically; or show that a molecule carries "harm" as an intrinsic property independent of any residual; or show that a global mixture residual, not the per-species one, is the operative quantity. Any one breaks C3.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument:

1. A bench experiment in which a sequestering agent purifies one isomer from an equilibrating mixture and the purified product persists across repeated dilution.
2. A surface experiment in which stochastic thermal oscillation fractionates refractory carbon from releasable monomers, with the released monomer shown to be composable downstream.
3. A measurement that reads the per-species residual through several cycles, so that a global Mass Index rise can be checked against individual species trajectories.
4. Any ground reading in which a compound carries harm before a living residual exists. This would break the reframe outright.
