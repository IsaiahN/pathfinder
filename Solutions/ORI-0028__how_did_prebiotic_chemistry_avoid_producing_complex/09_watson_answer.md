## QUESTION

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the instance does not. Prebiotic chemistry did not avoid complex mixtures: the mixture is the structural default of a closed atom set with unbounded arrangements, observed at the bench as tar. Selectivity was differential ground contact in an unmaintained habitat, no planner and no forward-looking avoidance, expressed through isomer separation, a surviving part, a closing loop and settlement that makes a product composable over. "Unwanted or harmful" is valence relative to a residual, life, that did not exist yet, so the question is retrospective and owes a refusal of its framing. The specific pathway is a `gap`, reported as such. D2 and D1 and D4 are triangulated across low-overlap figure lines; the per-slot constraint and the round trip are single-source; the instance is a gap.
