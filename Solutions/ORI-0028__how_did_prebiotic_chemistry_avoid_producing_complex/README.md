## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
