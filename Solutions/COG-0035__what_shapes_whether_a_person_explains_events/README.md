## The question restated as an effect

An event happens. A person owes an explanation that closes the gap between what the event predicted and what was observed. Four candidate bindings are on offer: this happened because of an individual choice (a person-scale agent), because of a social relationship (a bond between people), because of an institution (a convention over many agents), or because of a spiritual force (an agent-like cause not available to the senses). The question asks what selects among these bindings. Option A: a stored preference, personality trait, or culture label selects. Option B: the structure of the residual and the reach of the person's library at that slot select, with the four options forming a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition. Each option leads somewhere different: A produces a ranking of people or cultures and treats agreement as corroboration; B produces a four-condition selector test, per-domain verdicts kept separate, refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where no figure line names the individual attribution.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a shape with four discriminators, (i) which cause-atoms are held and reachable in closure(Γ), (ii) which atom the habitat makes salient for this residual, (iii) whether the cause crossed from a differing closure with provenance and a shadow test, and (iv) whether an anchor that does not update is reachable. That shape is written down here before the derivation and is refusable by the same test as everything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people or cultures by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No verdict on whether any of the four explanations is true. The figures decide the shape of the selector, not the metaphysical status of spirits, institutions, relationships or choices.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **An interest in the reading is disqualifying.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :52).
3. **The target is per slot, not a population average.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A cross-cultural average is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
