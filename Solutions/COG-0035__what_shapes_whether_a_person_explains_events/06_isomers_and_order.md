# Are the four explanations isomers? Structure, order and analogues

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the bond vocabulary, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

The audit showed that individual choice, social relationship, institution and spiritual force form a union of overlapping conditions (F1) and that the variable selecting among them is reachability plus salience (F2, F3). Two questions remain: (1) when these four are written over the same atoms, what is their structural relation, and (2) in what order do they become available? Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held for this derivation: Space, Time, Energy (states) and Gradient, Spectrum, Network (structures), plus one person-slot s from Fig 1. Bonds held: the seven, including → sequence, ⋛ comparison and − subtraction, plus ¬ negation as the eighth operator that is not a bond. Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."

No new primitive is admitted. The agent-shaped atom in the spiritual case is treated as either a composition of held atoms or an import, and the strain between those readings is kept open (audit §6).

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Written over the full shared atom set, are they isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Write each explanation as a molecule over the full shared set `{Space, Time, Energy, Gradient, Spectrum, Network, person-slot}`:
  - **Individual choice:** a gradient over a person's time slot, `Time → Gradient → person-slot`. The directedness is the choice.
  - **Social relationship:** a network bond between two person-slots, `person-slot + Network + person-slot` with influence travelling (Fig 13 :24, "that entities relate, and influence travels").
  - **Institution:** a spectrum over many person-slots composed with a convention, `person-slot × n → Spectrum → convention`. Fig 13 :20: "Ordering, so more and less can be said." Fig 10 :47 governs the convention.
  - **Spiritual force:** an agent-shaped atom bonded to the event-slot. If the atom is composed from Network plus Gradient, this is a held-atom molecule; if it is imported, it is an import and owes provenance and a shadow test.
- All four draw on the same atom vocabulary. They differ in which bond is charged and which state the structure is bonded to. Under Fig 12 :117 they are **isomers**: same atoms, different bond, different substance.
- Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Two informants who list the same four causes have not said the same thing if the bonds differ.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if any one of the four requires an atom that none of the others holds and cannot be shown to be an import with a source gate and a shadow test, the isomer claim fails and the four are not over a shared atom set.

### Q2: As the question writes them, are they a functional group instead?

- Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- The question writes the four with disjoint ingredient lists: a person, a relationship, an institution, a spirit. On that writing they share only the event-slot. By Fig 12's definition they are then **not isomers**; they are members of a functional group of "cause → effect" explanations with different atoms in the slots.
- The stronger isomer claim of Q1 needs the explicit re-description over the full shared set. That re-description is the contribution this file makes: it is a checkable structural claim, not a restatement of the question.

**Falsifier:** show that the shared-atom re-description loses a distinction the informants actually make, so that two different explanations compose to the same molecule. Then the functional-group reading stands and the isomer reading is withdrawn.

### Q3: Symmetry: why the order of operands matters for three of the four and not the fourth

- Fig 12 :92: "Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing." Operators table: the symmetric bonds are +, ∥ and ≡; →, ⇒, − and ⋛ are ordered.
- Social relationship under + is symmetric: swapping the two people yields one term. Fig 12 :23 covers the k! spellings of a symmetric bond.
- Individual choice under → is ordered: the choice must precede the act. Reversing them changes the explanation.
- Institution under ⋛ is ordered in a different way: a threshold relation between a quantity and a rule, "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?" (Operators table). A convention that is merely present is not yet a verdict.
- The spiritual case depends on the open strain. If it is an import, the operator is not a bond at all; import moves the wall (Fig 6 :14, "the only operator that moves the wall"). If it is composed of Network plus Gradient, the bond is ordered and the person-slot it lands on is part of the term (Fig 13 :76, "a gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is").
- **Consequence:** the four cannot be scored on one scale of "how much cause." Three are ordered compositions and one is either a symmetric composition or an import. They differ in kind, so their verdicts cannot be averaged. This is the structural reason for keeping per-domain verdicts separate.

### Q4: Order: in what sequence do the four become available?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- The dependency order for an attribution is:
  1. **Perception** of the event and its slot (Fig 3 :4-5). Without a registered difference there is no R.
  2. **Vocabulary** of cause-atoms in Γ (Fig 3 :6-8). What the person can name as a possible cause. This is where culture and profession change the reach.
  3. **Objective** of the explanation, what would count as progress here (Fig 3 :9-11). Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Without ⋛ and ¬ the person can name causes but cannot say when an explanation is sufficient or wrong.
  4. **Planning** the attribution under incomplete information.
  5. **Learning and carrying** it to the next event (Fig 3 :15-16).
- Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Training people to name more institutions (link 2) cannot rescue a missing criterion for sufficiency (link 3).
- Fig 3 :25-27 names why the middle link is the usual break: perception and planning have obvious tests, the vocabulary-to-objective link has neither. This is where an instrument is owed.

**Falsifier:** a case where a person states a clear criterion for a sufficient explanation before having any vocabulary for the cause-atoms, and the criterion still selects a correct attribution. That would invert the dependency order and require a re-census of Fig 3.

### Q5: Analogy check: what the psychological four-I model does and does not license

The *Cultural psychology* record (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-22) describes Markus and Conner's 4 I's culture cycle: Individuals, Interactions, Institutions, Ideas. Four layers, and the first three line up strikingly with the question's individual choice, social relationships and institutions; "Ideas" is the layer that answers the big questions of why we are here and where we are going, which is where spiritual forces would sit.

This is an **analogue, not an import**. Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." The residual was described in effect terms before this frame was brought in, so the resemblance is not doing the selecting. Fig 8 :50-52 warns that convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them; the record here says the four-I model was read after the figure derivation and corroborates the union shape, while the figures govern if they disagree.

where they disagree is instructive: the four-I model is a cycle in which the layers mutually constitute each other, while the figures treat these as overlapping conditions with different bonds and different anchors. The analogy supports "union" and supports "no standing cause list." It does not support treating Ideas as a seventh primitive or as a cause that moves the wall.

### Q6: The membrane: teaching an attribution between people and cultures

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
- Teaching someone "in this culture we blame the institution" is a recording. It transfers one occasion's answer. Teaching them how to enumerate the habitat and check which atom carries positive mass is a method, and it crosses as a head start.
- Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The round trip here is: describe the cause-selection method in effect terms, hand it to a person in another closure, ask them to apply it to their own event, and compare what came back. The gap R_T is the measurement of what the coarser description could not hold, not an error to erase.

## CONCLUSION

Over the full shared atom set the four explanations are isomers under Fig 12 :117: same atoms, different bonds, different substances. As the question writes them, with disjoint ingredients, they are a functional group (Fig 12 :123). Three of the four are ordered compositions and one is either a symmetric composition or an import, which is why they cannot be averaged into one score. They become available in the dependency order of Fig 3, and the usual break is between having terms for cause-atoms and saying what would count as a sufficient explanation. The teaching channel carries methods and head starts, never the recording of one culture's answer. One strain stays live: whether the agent-shaped atom is composed from held atoms or imported, with an instrument still owed to discriminate the two.
