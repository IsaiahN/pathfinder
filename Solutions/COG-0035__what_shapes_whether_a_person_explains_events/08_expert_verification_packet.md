# Expert verification packet: the Tether on attribution choice

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cultural psychologist or explanation researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the individual attribution. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

What shapes whether a person explains an event through individual choice, social relationship, institution or spiritual force is not a stored preference or a culture label. It is a four-condition discriminator at the event slot: which cause-terms are held and reachable in the person's library, which cause carries positive mass on this event, which cause the habitat makes salient, and whether a reference that does not move when people update on it is available to settle the attribution. The four candidates are overlapping conditions, not exclusive boxes; over a shared atom set they are isomers and their verdicts cannot be averaged. The framework returns the selector and its refuters. It does not name the cause a given person selects, and records that as a gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The four explanations are a union of overlapping conditions, not a four-way partition

- **Statement:** an event can carry positive mass on several cause-types at once; one lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Also Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :4 adds that disagreements that do not shrink with effort where each rule works on a different subset "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- **What to check:** in the literature you know, do multi-cause attributions for a single event survive controls that force a single answer, or do they collapse into one? If forced-choice designs produce the apparent partition, that is an instrument artefact, not a finding about causes.
- **Refuter:** a controlled case where three causes are ruled out by construction, the person still shows positive residual on the ruled-out slots, and a single-cause model predicts new events better than the union model.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 plus the low-overlap developmental record of coexisting natural and supernatural frameworks).

### C2: Reachability and salience, not a standing preference, select the cause

- **Statement:** a person can only offer a cause-term that has positive mass at the live slot and composes from atoms the library holds; which atom wins depends on what the habitat makes salient for this residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
- **What to check:** does a residual-independent ranking of the four causes predict attributions across unrelated event types better than a model using event structure and the vocabulary available to the person? The socioecological literature on relational mobility predicts that coordination demands make external forces and powerful others salient, which is the effect side of this claim.
- **Refuter:** a cause-term minted with zero positive mass on every slot, or outside closure(Γ) with no contact with a differing frame and no new atom; or a standing ranking that outperforms the structural model.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 5 and Fig 6 are two figures with different objects; the low-overlap corroborator is the relational-mobility record, not the framework essays, which share the author's evidence pool).

### C3: The measurement reference must not update on what informants say, and the effect is per slot

- **Statement:** a vignette or rating scale is an instrument, not a ground. If its scoring moves with responses, the comparison is coherent drift; and a culture-level mean cannot decide a single person's event attribution.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** is the instrument invariant under informant response, and do two genuinely low-overlap instruments agree? The literature records both a self-report methodology critique and a debate over domain-specific versus global measurement, which are instrument questions, not figure echoes.
- **Refuter:** a measurement shown to move when frames update on it whose verdict is still counted, or a global score that predicts a single new event's attribution as well as a per-event instrument across multiple event types.
- **Status:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 1 plus independent methodology records).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any cause is true or false, and not that spiritual forces do not exist. The import claim is structural.
- Not that institutions are illegitimate. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." The failure is only when a convention decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not a cultural ranking, a personality typology, or a universal percentage.
- Not grounded. No measurement, no dataset, no experiment was run for this packet.
- Not a name for the cause any particular person selects. That is a declared `gap`.
- Not a claim that the agent-shaped atom must be imported. The composed reading remains live; an instrument to discriminate them is owed.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-event instrument that enumerates the available cause-atoms, registers which carries positive mass at the slot, states a sufficiency criterion before collecting attributions, and ranges two low-overlap frames against a reference that does not update. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The existing questionnaire and vignette instruments are the ones to improve, not replace by stipulation. A discriminator that separates an imported agent-atom from a composed agent-shaped network would close the live strain, and a case where a spiritual attribution beats the natural alternative against a non-updating anchor would move that term from imported candidate to settled term.

2. Check C2 against a study you know: is there evidence that available vocabulary and event structure beat a standing cause-preference in prediction?

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no later agreement will be pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
