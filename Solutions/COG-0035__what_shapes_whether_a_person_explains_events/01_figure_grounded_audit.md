## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question is not "which of the four causes is correct." It is: what is the variable that selects which candidate is offered when an event fails prediction?

Per slot s: a person acts, observes o', and compares it with Γ(b,a). R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|. The Symbols table calls R "The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it." An explanation is a candidate term φ offered against the bin that owes one (Formula step 3). The four candidates are four bindings of the cause-slot:

| candidate | effect reading | figure type |
|---|---|---|
| individual choice | a directedness over one person's time slot | a gradient over a state |
| social relationship | a bond between two person-slots | the network atom, a typed bond |
| institution | a ranking over many agents that enforces a convention | spectrum over a population plus a convention |
| spiritual force | an agent-shaped cause not available to the senses | an atom imported from a closure that differs |

The residual to be explained is the **selection** itself: why one binding rather than another, for this event, for this person, in this habitat. The sought-for shape was bound in the README as a four-discriminator selector. That binding is what the findings below test.

**Arity / symmetry / scale of R.** Arity: one live event-slot, but the candidate causes are a union of overlapping conditions, so more than one can carry positive mass at the same slot. Symmetry: the four are not interchangeable under swap. They are different molecules over different bonds, and one of them (spiritual force) may require an import rather than a composition. Scale: the loop runs at the person scale, the network and institution scales are different membrane crossings, and the research frame that compares cultures is a further closure that does not itself explain events.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. All four explanatory domains have anchors, but the anchors are of different kinds, which is why their verdicts must be kept separate and never averaged.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 the individual reasoner** | the person, belief b, weighting w, library Γ, one event-slot s | residual R per slot; cost(φ) plus left(R,φ) | what actually happened at that slot | loop; partition boundary of self versus not-self |
| **D2 the immediate habitat** | other people, roles, face-to-face correction, relationships | contact; influence travel; reproduced practice | whether the relationship actually obtains and keeps contact | union condition; the network atom |
| **D3 the institutional frame** | rules, courts, schools, markets, official categories | conventions and whether they decide outcomes | the ground, never the agreement of the institution | union condition; convention that must not become a verdict |
| **D4 the spiritual or cosmological frame** | unseen agents, spirits, gods | causal mass assigned to an agent not available to the senses | none at the human sensorium; what cannot be perceived is residual | import from a differing closure |
| **D5 the research / measurement frame** | researchers, vignettes, scales, informants | agreement versus independence | an anchor that does not update on what informants say | separate closure; ranges against R, not a fifth cause |

**Partition or union (Fig 9 :55).** D1 through D4 form a **union of overlapping conditions**, not a partition. An illness, a job loss, or a crop failure can be attributed at once to a choice, a relationship, an institution, and a spiritual cause. The four conditions bind different fractions of the same residual and can co-fire. D5 is not a fifth member of that union: it is a different closure sitting outside it and trying to range against it. Treating D5 as another cause is the category error that makes cross-cultural comparisons look like explanations.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** The chain runs: perception of the event (link 1) → having terms for what the event affords as a cause (link 2) → being able to say what would count as an explanation here (link 3) → planning the attribution (link 4) → carrying it to the next event (link 5). Fig 3 :19 says "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." The usual break is between link 2 and link 3: a person can name the actors (a person, a relative, a boss, a spirit) without a term for what would count as a sufficient explanation. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

**Strain test applied before findings.** An early reading tried to put all four candidates onto the six-term substrate of Fig 13 as if they were states and structures. That strains: "spiritual force" is not a seventh kind, and "institution" is a convention over agents rather than a structure bonded to a state. The re-census assigns Fig 13 to the atoms available for composition and assigns Fig 10 to conventions, Fig 11 to the habitat, and Fig 6 to imports. Different objects, no strain after re-census. The one live strain is recorded in §6.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The four explanations are a union, not a four-way partition

Fig 9 :55 states: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 adds: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :4 says that disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule works well on a different subset, "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

The question's grammar invites a partition: individual, relationship, institution, or spirit, pick one. The figures refuse that grammar. A single event has positive mass on several cause-slots at once, and one lever (for example, teaching a person to name institutions) clears the region where only institution holds and leaves the intersections standing. The intersections are not harder cases of the same choice; they are a specific compound with its own loop.

**World corroboration.** The *Cognitive science of religion* page (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-22) lists Legare and Gelman's paper "Bewitchment, Biology, or Both: The Co-existence of Natural and Supernatural Explanatory Frameworks Across Development." The title itself records coexistence rather than exclusive choice. *Attribution (psychology)* records that the external/internal distinction was supplemented by stability and globality dimensions, which is a union of conditions, not one axis.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** across Fig 9 and the low-overlap developmental-cognition record. **Refuter:** a controlled case where exactly one of the four causes can be attributed by construction and the other three are ruled out before the person answers, with the person still showing positive residual on the ruled-out slots. That would reinstate a partition.

### F2: There is no standing list of correct cause-types; valence is relative to the residual

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :45-47 enumerates the habitat as actors, conditions and relations, and Fig 11 :48 says "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."

So "culture makes people explain through relationships" is a reification. What changes with the residual is which atom is salient and which has positive mass. If the event is a sudden death, the salient atom may be a person, a spirit, or a rule, depending on what failed to be predicted, not on a stored cultural trait.

**World corroboration.** *Relational mobility* (Wikipedia, 2026-11-22) states: "Individuals' need to coordinate their actions and avoid conflict makes salient the influence of external forces, including powerful others in the environment, on their own situation." This is a socioecological condition making an atom salient, not a belief installed in a person, and it is a genuinely low-overlap witness. *Fundamental attribution error* (Wikipedia) records that "people from individualistic (Western) cultures are reportedly more prone to the error while people from collectivistic cultures are less prone," and also that the effect is contested, with situation strength interacting with disposition. The contest is evidence for the union reading, not against it.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 11 plus low-overlap socioecology). **Refuter:** a stable, residual-independent ranking of the four causes that predicts attributions across unrelated event types better than a model using event structure and available vocabulary.

### F3: Reachability and salience, not preference, select the cause

Formula step 3 gates a minted term with three factors in product: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s` before the search, REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)` during, and NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)` after. Fig 5 :33: "The three guards are a product, not a checklist." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

Applied here: a person can only offer a cause-term that (i) has positive mass on the live event-slot, (ii) is reachable by composition from the cause-atoms their Γ already holds, and (iii) is not merely a restatement of an atom already there. What differs across upbringings, professions and institutions is the **library and its closure**, not a dial labelled "individualism." A person who has never had the institutionAtom available cannot mint an institutional explanation; a person whose Γ includes the network atom can compose a relational one cheaply.

Fig 5 :62-64 also names the limit that matters most for this question: a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE, not as a term, so nothing in the mechanism can see it. The remedy is the discipline already applied in the README: write the sought-for shape down as an ordinary term and let it accrue a residual.

**World corroboration.** *Cultural psychology* (Wikipedia) quotes Gerd Baumann: "Culture is not a real thing, but an abstract analytical notion. In itself, it does not cause behavior but abstracts from it. It is thus neither normative nor predictive but a heuristic means towards explaining how people understand and act upon the world." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-10) says "Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against." Both support Γ-and-closure as the variable rather than a culture label. The essay shares the framework author's evidence pool with the figures, so it is not an independent witness; the Baumann quote is.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 5 gates plus Fig 6 closure, two figures with different objects, plus an independent world quote). **Refuter:** a demonstrated cause-attribution minted from outside closure(Γ) with no contact with a differing frame and no new atom, or a case where SUPPORT is zero on every slot and a cause-term is still admitted.

### F4: Spiritual force is an import from a differing closure, not a seventh primitive

Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Fig 6 :23 again: the wall moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

An agent-shaped cause that no instrument registers is, structurally, an atom imported from a closure that differs from the perceiver's measurement closure. It fills the slot where nothing currently registers, and it needs a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap?) before it can be composed over (Fig 6 :12-13). This is a structural claim about where the atom came from. It is emphatically **not** a claim that spirits do not exist, nor that they do. The figures do not rule on that; they rule on reachability and on what would have to be imported.

**World corroboration.** *Agent detection* (Wikipedia) quotes Gray and Wegner: "The high cost of failing to detect agents and the low cost of wrongly detecting them has led researchers to suggest that people possess a Hyperactive Agent Detection Device, a cognitive module that readily ascribes events in the environment to the behavior of agents." *Cognitive science of religion* (Wikipedia) says scholars "seek to explain how human minds acquire, generate, and transmit religious thoughts, practices, and schemas by means of ordinary cognitive capacities." Both predict agent-shaped causes where the signal is ambiguous, which is the effect-side reading of the import.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 6, Fig 8 and Fig 11, three figures with low overlap, plus two low-overlap world records). **Refuter:** a verified case where a spiritual-attribution term settles a checkable physical prediction better than the natural alternative against an anchor that does not update. That would move the term from imported candidate to settled term and change the verdict.

### F5: Institutions are conventions, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one

Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 10 :49 restates the failure as "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 2 :41 is careful to give convention its due: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 2 :34 warns that when the reference updates on the frames, "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

An institutional explanation is legitimate as shared meaning. It becomes a failure when the institution's own agreement is used as the answer, so that the reference moves when the frame pushes on it. This is why D3 has an anchor (the ground) and why its verdict must be kept separate from D2's: a relationship can be real and a convention can be useful while neither has settled the event.

**Verdict: `single-source` on the figure side** (Fig 10 is the governing line; Fig 2 is closely related to it rather than an independent frame). No world source in this run tests the convention-versus-verdict distinction directly, so world corroboration is absent, not negative. **Refuter:** a case where an institution's ruling is used as the ground for the very question about the institution and no drift is detectable with an independent instrument.

### F6: The cross-cultural anchor must not update on what informants say

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."

A cross-cultural vignette or rating scale is an instrument, not a ground. If its scoring is adjusted until respondents produce the expected cultural contrast, it has become panel 1 of Fig 2: mutual update, coherent drift, nobody notices. Independent frames are bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42), and their agreement means something only when they draw on different evidence (Fig 2 :31).

**World corroboration.** *Cultural psychology* (Wikipedia) records the criticism that "assigning characteristics to a culture promotes a disregard for heterogeneity and minimizes the role of the individual" (Gjerde) and that overemphasising self-report "can lead to relatively unstable and ultimately misleading data." *Locus of control* (Wikipedia) records that "Debate continues whether domain-specific or more global measures of locus of control will prove to be more useful in practical application." These are instrument critiques, not figure echoes.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 2 plus independent measurement-method literature). **Refuter:** a comparison where the instrument is shown to be invariant under informants' responses and two genuinely low-overlap instruments still disagree about the cultural effect, with no third anchor to range against.

### F7: The attribution is per slot; an averaged "explanatory style" is a filter, not a witness

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :20-21: a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state and not an inert one. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

A culture-level mean attribution score tells you where to look. It cannot tell you what closed R for this person at this event. The Symbols table defines w as "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior," learnable from outcomes of past weightings. A person's tendency is a learnable parameter of the loop, not a term naming a cause, and it still has to be ranged against the ground per slot.

**World corroboration.** *Explanatory style* (Wikipedia) states that "an individual does not necessarily show a uniform explanatory style in all aspects of life, but may exhibit varying responses to different types of events," and records evidence for domain specificity (work-related versus interpersonal attributions). *Locus of control* (Wikipedia) likewise distinguishes domain-specific from global measures.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 1 plus two independent psychology records). **Refuter:** a global score that predicts a single new event's attribution as well as a per-event instrument, across multiple event types, with no loss of live slots.

### F8: Which cause a given person selects is a declared `gap`, not a silence

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 13 :129: "the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

No figure line maps a person, a culture, or an event type to one of the four causes. The figures return the shape of the selector and the conditions a candidate must pass. Naming the actual cause for a given person would be a binding from the research frame, provenanced as such, and a candidate awaiting a ground reading, not a derivation. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Master Plan §1).

**Verdict: `gap` declared.** **What would close it:** a per-event enumerating instrument that lists the available cause-atoms, measures which carries positive mass on the live slot, checks reachability, and ranges against an anchor that does not update, run by two low-overlap frames with a pre-registered refuter. Until then the answer stays `candidate`.

### F9: The broken link is vocabulary-to-objective, which is why "explain" is undefined in most studies

Fig 3 :25-27: perception and planning "have obvious failures and obvious tests. The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 12 :112: "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."

Most attribution research gives informants the actors (a person, a group, a rule, a spirit) but never asks what would count as a sufficient explanation for that event. Without a comparison term (⋛) and a refusal operator (¬), the study can record which label is chosen and cannot record what would have refuted it. The result is a list of ingredients with no route.

**World corroboration.** *Cultural psychology* (Wikipedia) notes the field's move to non-self-report methods precisely because self-report "can lead to relatively unstable and ultimately misleading data," and lists cognitive tasks, behavioural data and natural language as alternatives. Those are attempts to install an instrument at the middle link.

**Verdict: `triangulated`** (Fig 3 plus Fig 12 plus the methodology record). **Refuter:** a study that states a ground-level criterion for "sufficient explanation" before collecting attributions and shows the criterion is invariant across the cultures compared.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument (Seat Map :287). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Union refuter (F1).** In a controlled case where three of the four causes are ruled out by construction, the person still shows positive residual on the ruled-out slots and a single-cause model predicts new events better than the union model.
2. **Reachability refuter (F3).** A cause-term is minted outside closure(Γ) with no imported atom and no contact with a differing frame, or a term is admitted with zero positive mass on every slot.
3. **Salience refuter (F2).** A residual-independent standing ranking of the four causes predicts attributions across unrelated event types better than a model using event structure and the available cause-atoms.
4. **Anchor refuter (F6).** A measurement is shown to move when frames update on it and its verdict is still counted as corroboration by two or more frames, with no independent anchor available.
5. **Import refuter (F4).** A spiritual-attribution term settles a checkable physical prediction better than the natural alternative against an anchor that does not update, so the term moves from imported candidate to settled term.
6. **Per-slot refuter (F7).** A global culture-level score predicts a single new event's attribution as well as a per-event instrument across multiple event types.
7. **Figure re-census refuter.** A re-census shows that the four candidates are a partition over the six-term set with mutually exclusive atoms, so the union finding was a misreading of Fig 9.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the shape claim is revised. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape: the four discriminators in the README, not a claim about which cause people use.
2. Split the question before answering it. Treat the four options as a union of overlapping conditions and keep D1 through D5 verdicts separate (Fig 9 :54-57).
3. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing any comparison frame (Fig 9 :27-28, :30-31).
4. State the mode on every sentence (Formula mode rule). The person-level attribution stays `gap`; the selector shape is `general`; per-domain findings are `specified`.
5. Ask for a refutation, not agreement. Send the pre-registered refuter with the shape claim (Fig 10 :52; Fig 2 :35).
6. Do not rank cultures or people. That manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40) and averages away the live slot (Fig 1 :22).

## 8. Verdict of the primary audit

The shape survives. What selects an attribution is a four-condition discriminator: reachability of the cause-term in closure(Γ), positive mass on the live slot, salience supplied by the habitat, and an anchor that does not update, with the four explanations forming a union of overlapping conditions and any import carrying provenance and a shadow test. The specific cause for a specific person is a declared `gap`. Mode: `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the individual attribution, not `grounded`. After re-census, one strain remains live (imported versus composed agent-atom) and is named with the instrument that would settle it. No figure line was smoothed to fit.
