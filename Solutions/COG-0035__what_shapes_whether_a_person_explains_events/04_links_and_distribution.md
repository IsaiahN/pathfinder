## 3. Which world findings bear on this question

1. **Coexistence, not exclusive choice.** The Legare and Gelman title in the *Cognitive science of religion* references is the cleanest independent support for Fig 9's union reading. Natural and supernatural frameworks coexist across development; they are not a switch with one position.
2. **Socioecological salience.** *Relational mobility* gives the low-overlap mechanism: coordination demands make external forces and powerful others salient, and high mobility is associated with an internal locus. This is the effect-side counterpart of Fig 11 :49 (valence relative to the residual). It is not a culture label.
3. **Instrument instability.** *Explanatory style* and *Locus of control* both record the debate over domain-specific versus global measurement, and *Cultural psychology* records the self-report critique. Together they support Fig 1 :19 and Fig 2 :29: measure per slot against a reference that does not move.
4. **Agent-shaped causes from ordinary capacities.** *Agent detection* and *Cognitive science of religion* predict agent attributions without invoking any special faculty. This corroborates the structural import reading in F4, but it does not settle whether the agent-atom is imported or composed, which remains the live strain.
5. **A contest, not a consensus.** The *Fundamental attribution error* page records substantial contestation of the effect. That is kept as evidence for the union reading rather than smoothed into agreement (Fig 2 :40, agreement from one evidence pool is one witness).

## 4. Overlap declaration

The eight essays and the architecture article share the framework author's evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement with the figure verdicts is expected and carries no independent witness weight. The seven psychology and religion encyclopedia records draw partly on overlapping literature (attribution, attributional style and locus of control cite many of the same studies), so they count as one broad evidence pool, not seven witnesses. *Relational mobility* is the one genuinely low-overlap corroborator, with its roots in socioecology, agriculture and animal behaviour. The venue pages are not evidence at all.

## 5. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the shape and the refuter (a generator) first. Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording of one occasion.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the four-discriminator shape claim; (2) the four refuters; (3) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (4) record their verdict; (5) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 6. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Do not show the formula, the essays or the figures. Record the verdict before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in.** If the reader is curious, offer the three links. The figures are the authority.

**Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user states their own ground and writes their own refuter before the run. The author's own warning applies: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The live strain on the agent-atom must be declared, not hidden, and the `gap` on the individual attribution must be stated, not filled.
