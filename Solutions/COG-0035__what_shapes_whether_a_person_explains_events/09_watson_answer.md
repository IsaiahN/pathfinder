## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what selects the cause-binding when a person explains an event. Option A: a stored preference, personality trait or culture label selects among four exclusive boxes. Option B: four conditions at the event slot select, and the four candidates form a union of overlapping conditions rather than a partition. What each leads to: A produces a ranking of people or cultures, treats agreement as corroboration, and averages away the live slot; B produces a four-discriminator selector, per-domain verdicts kept separate, refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where no figure line names the individual attribution.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A person's prediction fails at an event slot, so an explanation is owed. Arity: one live event-slot, but the candidate causes are overlapping conditions, so several can carry mass at once. Symmetry: the four candidates are not interchangeable; three are ordered compositions and one is either a symmetric composition or an import. Scale: the loop runs at the person scale, network and institution are different membrane crossings, and the research frame that compares cultures is a further closure. Fig 9 names this a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the individual reasoner.** Actors: person, belief b, weighting w, library Γ, one slot s. Currency: residual R per slot and the cost of a candidate term. Anchor: what actually happened at that slot. Question type: the loop itself, partition boundary of self versus not-self.
- **Domain 2, the immediate habitat.** Actors: other people, roles, face-to-face correction, relationships. Currency: contact and influence travel. Anchor: whether the relationship obtains and keeps contact. Question type: union condition, the network atom.
- **Domain 3, the institutional frame.** Actors: rules, courts, schools, markets, official categories. Currency: conventions and whether they decide outcomes. Anchor: the ground, never the agreement of the institution. Question type: union condition, a convention that must not become a verdict.
- **Domain 4, the spiritual or cosmological frame.** Actors: unseen agents, spirits, gods. Currency: causal mass assigned to an agent not available to the senses. Anchor: none at the human sensorium; what cannot be perceived is residual. Question type: import from a differing closure, with a live strain against the composed reading.
- **Domain 5, the research and measurement frame.** Actors: researchers, vignettes, scales, informants. Currency: agreement versus independence. Anchor: an anchor that does not update on informant responses. Question type: a separate closure ranging against R, not a fifth cause.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception of the event, then vocabulary of available cause-atoms, then a criterion for what counts as a sufficient explanation, then planning the attribution, then carrying it. The usual break is the vocabulary-to-objective link, which has no obvious instrument.
- **Partition or union.** Domains 1 to 4 are a union of overlapping conditions. Domain 5 is outside that union. Treating Domain 5 as a fifth cause is the category error that turns cross-cultural comparison into an explanation.

Strain test applied: an early reading forced all four candidates onto the six-term substrate and strained. Re-census assigns Fig 13 to available atoms, Fig 10 to conventions, Fig 11 to the habitat and Fig 6 to imports. Different objects, no strain after re-census. One strain stays live and is named in the VERDICT.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

What selects an attribution is a four-condition discriminator: reachability of the cause-term in closure(Γ), positive mass at the live event-slot, salience supplied by the habitat, and a reference that does not move when frames update on it. Individual choice, social relationship, institution and spiritual force are overlapping conditions rather than exclusive boxes; over a shared atom set they differ by arrangement and cannot be averaged. A spiritual attribution is structural import from a closure that differs, carrying provenance and a shadow test, and this is not a claim that it is false. Institutional explanations are conventions until they decide an outcome. Which cause a given person actually selects is a declared `gap`. Mode: `specified` per domain, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the individual attribution, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before its conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording of one culture's answer instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is the metamerism error: naming a cause from one questionnaire reading when several causes can produce that reading, which asks for a discriminator and gets a story.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding from the research frame onto any particular cause is removable without touching the selector shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet. The `gap` is reversible by a source-discriminating instrument, not by more agreement.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not consensus. A per-event instrument that enumerates available cause-atoms, registers positive mass at the slot and states a sufficiency criterion before collecting attributions would name the selector; two low-overlap frames ranging against a non-updating anchor would decide the cross-cultural question. A cause-term admitted with zero mass or outside closure(Γ) would break C2. A standing cause ranking that outperforms event structure plus vocabulary would break F2. A measurement shown to move with informants and still counted would break C3. An instrument that discriminates an imported agent-atom from a composed agent-shaped network would close the live strain, and a spiritual attribution that beats the natural alternative against a non-updating anchor would move that term from candidate to settled. Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
