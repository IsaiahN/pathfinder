## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0035 a **shape** is the four-discriminator selector that any answer must pass: reachability in closure(Γ), positive mass at the slot, habitat salience, and a non-updating anchor. It is not the name of the cause a given person selects. The distinction is the whole point here. "This person attributes the event to fate" is a name. "An agent-shaped cause with no instrument registered at the slot can only have arrived by import from a closure that differs, and owes provenance and a shadow test" is a shape.

The Library essay also names the failure this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is Fig 9 :55 in narrative form, and it is why F1 of the audit refuses the four-way partition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place," because by then "you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Described in effect terms, the residual is not "why do people believe in spirits?" (a cause-shaped question that already assumes an answer) but "what makes one cause-term carry more predictive mass than another at a given event slot?" (an effect-shaped question whose words are not borrowed from any one culture's ontology).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test and a debit against independence.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And it adds the caveat that governs all of §6 of the audit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So the four candidate causes are not nature's own list. They are a visible guess supplied by the question. Naming one as the winner would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the "explanatory style" answer commits the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" (one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered). "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same structure applies here. A response on an attribution questionnaire is a reading of a projection. Several different cause-structures can produce the same response: a person who has never heard of institutions and a person who has heard of them but dismisses them can give the same "not an institution" answer. Naming the source from the reading is a metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." An answer that says "people in culture X explain through relationships" is an output of a measurement instrument that has not been validated against anything that can refuse it.

## 6. The correction that must stay visible

In an earlier reading I treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any of the four causes. That was an overstatement, and it is corrected here rather than erased.

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. An institution, a relationship, a person and an agent-shaped unseen cause are all already available as terms in the human frame; naming one of them as a candidate is a **binding from the research frame onto a held atom**, not the invention of a new primitive. What the figures forbid is (i) inventing a seventh cause-type and calling it derived, and (ii) claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom").

What remains forbidden by the source argument of §4 is reading the cause off a single projection without a discriminator. That is a different error from naming a held atom, and both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar records in its own `05` §5b, applied to a different field.

## 7. Consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not the verdict.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A well-known researcher agreeing that "culture shapes explanation" gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle it, and collecting such agreements would manufacture the disqualifying correlation of Fig 2 :40.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim, cannot settle which cause a person selects, and must be labelled as such everywhere.

**Films of one occasion.** Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A single cross-cultural dataset is a recording. The transferable object is the four-discriminator selector plus its refuter, which is what `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sends.
