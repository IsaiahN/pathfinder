## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 5 :33 and Fig 6 :23 (guards and closure), Fig 11 :49 and :51 (residual-relative valence), Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 1 :19 (per slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `README.md` and `07`: a four-discriminator selector; the four candidates are overlapping; the individual cause is a `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim an informant would actually use: "this person explains events through spiritual forces because that is what people like them believe."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure**: the concrete sentence picks one cause, while the abstraction says several carry mass (Fig 9 :55);
- the **per-slot indexing**: the concrete sentence is a person-level or culture-level label, while the abstraction measures R at an event slot (Fig 1 :19, :22);
- the **provenance and shadow test on imports**: the concrete sentence treats an agent-shaped cause as a belief held, while the abstraction requires a source gate and a gap it explains (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :53);
- the **non-updating anchor**: the concrete sentence is confirmed by asking more people from the same pool, while the abstraction requires a reference none of them can move (Fig 2 :29, :40).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a cause-term must be reachable in closure(Γ) and carry positive mass at the slot, and which atom is salient depends on the residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no per-event instrument exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
