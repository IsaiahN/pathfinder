## 1. Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the effect of non-interchangeable components on collective contact and joint action. Arity: at least two components, one collective outcome, one ground, and the residual itself as the aim. It is one field with two readers whenever two components draw on the same evidence, so it is a seam before it is a quantity (Fig 10 :13). Symmetry: not interchangeable by assumption; where they are interchangeable, k operands have k! spellings and one term (Fig 12 :92). Scale: step (per-slot residual), population (membrane), lineage (selection between rooms).
- **Domain 1, the single component and its library.** Actors: Γ_i, b_i, w_i, per-slot residual. Currency: prediction minus observation. Anchor: the ground as the only metric. Diversity is undefined here.
- **Domain 2, the population across the membrane.** Actors: many components, a shared library, a ranking. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground via selection.
- **Domain 3, independence and overlap.** Actors: two frames with closures. Currency: independence over evidence. Anchor: a ground that does not update. This is the hinge and it governs both outcomes.
- **Domain 4, the union and the aim.** Actors: R and the frames predicting its shape. Currency: described residual, conventions. Anchor: the ground. Failure state: undirected union, mute.
- **Domain 5, the habitat and selection.** Actors: components, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, not the habitat. Valence is assigned relative to R.

**Union, not partition.** Domains 3 and 4 overlap in two different ways: independence without aim (unaimed variety) and aim without independence (one frame with several names). Domain 5 supplies the actors that Domain 3 measures. Each overlap is a compound with its own loop, so the verdicts stay separate and are never averaged (Fig 1 :22).

**Order by dependency.** Per-slot residual, describe R, measure independence of R-selected frames, enumerate the habitat and let the ground vote, then ask what the population can compose. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

**Where my census differs from the primary's.** The primary derived Domain 3 from Fig 8 :47 (one quantity read twice) and Domain 5 from Fig 11 :49 (valence relative to R). ## 4. Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete advice a practitioner would take away: "measure independence, add a convention, report two curves, do not publish a single optimum."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The shorthand says "report two curves." The figure requires per-slot R (Fig 1 :19, :22). Two global curves can hide the live slot.

One thing survives cleanly: the governing quantity is evidence independence, not roster variety, and the two effects run through different operations. That is the residue worth sending.
