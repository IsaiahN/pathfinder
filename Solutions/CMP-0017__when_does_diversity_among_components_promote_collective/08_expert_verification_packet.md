## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Diversity among components" is not one quantity. The governing quantity is independence over evidence, and one quantity is read twice. Low overlap between frames lets them be ranged separately against a non-updating ground, which is what makes a population more than one frame. The same low overlap removes the shared reading a joint action needs, so coordination must be supplied by an authored, revisable convention that carries no verdict weight. Neither effect works without a described residual, valence is relative to that residual, and the resilience benefit and the coordination cost are in different currencies, so no single optimal-diversity number is licensed. The specific winning mixture is reported as a `gap`, not named.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and conceptual; checkable by reading Figures 2, 7 and 8

- **Statement:** Independence, not roster variety, is the quantity that governs the resilience effect. Correlated frames are one frame with several names.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 6 :53 "searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names."
- **What to check:** is the governing quantity in your own field independence of evidence, or is it instead a variance over observable traits? If the latter, can you exhibit a case where trait variance is high and evidence independence is zero, yet a genuine per-slot surplus survives a change in the ground?
- **Refuter:** R1 and R2 from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: a high trait-variance score with zero independence produces a real, per-slot surplus after the ground changes; or frames correlated at origin retain a per-slot resilience advantage after the ground changes.

### C2 - framework-internal plus world-facing; checkable against the coordination literature

- **Statement:** The coordination cost is structural. Independent frames have no shared reading, so a convention must be authored across the seam; it makes the disagreement statable, carries no world claim, and must stay revisable.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :13 "two frames, one field, no shared reading." Fig 10 :17 "it makes the bug statable." Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 2 :43 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- **What to check:** in your own system, what performs the bridging? Is it a shared protocol, a common metric, a vocabulary, a governance rule? Can you point to the information it discards, and to the condition under which it should be revised? If removing it changes nothing, it may not be doing the bridging work.
- **Refuter:** R3: a maximally independent set coordinates a joint action with no convention and no described residual and still improves its ground reading.

### C3 - world-facing; checkable against the invertibility problem in your data

- **Statement:** Convergent derivation and adopted import are identical in their contents. A study that measures only the distribution of answers, traits or heuristics cannot tell an independent population from one frame wearing many names; the lineage is the only discriminator.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents." Fig 8 :52 "Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."
- **What to check:** does your dataset record, per contribution, whether it was derived against the ground or copied from a peer? Lorenz et al. 2011, as reported on the opened Wikipedia page, found that when people could see each other's guesses, "their answers narrowed without getting any more accurate." Does your setting reproduce that narrowing without accuracy?
- **Refuter:** F-A in `06_isomers_and_order.md`: a population whose contributions are all adopted imports, with correlation near one, shows the same per-slot resilience gain as a population of independent discoveries, with lineage recorded in both cases.

### Cross-cutting refuter on the currencies

- **Statement:** The resilience benefit and the coordination cost are not additive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."
- **Refuter:** R7: a single additive payoff over heterogeneity predicts both curves across two residuals with declared units and no residual-specific term.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a scalar optimal diversity level, and not an inverted-U law. Inverted-U results in the world literature are outcome curves, not figure lines.
- Not that more components always help. Ten correlated components are one component (Fig 6 :53).
- Not that coordination costs are removable engineering friction.
- Not that a convention is a ground, a third frame, or a tie-breaker (Fig 2 :47).
- Not that independence can be read from contents (Fig 8 :51).
- Not a claim to name the winning component mixture for a given system. That is the `gap` from F9.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Lineage test:** record origin (derived or adopted) for every contribution. Without that column, independence is not observable.
2. **Perturbation test:** change the ground's reading and measure per-slot contact, not a global average (Fig 1 :19, :22).
3. **Convention test:** remove the bridging convention and check whether joint contact still improves; check that it never decides which frame was right (Fig 2 :43).
4. **Two-currency test:** report bits and actions separately (Fig 12 :130).
5. **Gap closure:** a figure line naming the mixture, or a ground measurement settling it, would close F9.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47-52 and Fig 10 :13-18. Six short passages. Decide whether "independence over evidence" and "authored convention" name things that exist in your field.
2. Check C1 against your own system: can you exhibit a case with high trait variance and zero independence?
3. Check C2: name the thing that supplies a shared reading across independent members, and name one piece of information it discards.
4. Check C3: does your data record whether a contribution was derived or adopted? If not, say so; that is the most useful answer you can give.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
