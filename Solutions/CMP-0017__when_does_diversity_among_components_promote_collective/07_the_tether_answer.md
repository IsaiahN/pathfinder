# The Tether's answer: when does diversity promote resilience and when does it impede coordination?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the four operational conditions. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

---

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for one effect of one input on two outcomes. The figures refuse the framing. There is no single "diversity" quantity: there is a governing quantity, independence over evidence, read in two operations (Fig 8 :47). The residual is a union of two conditions, so "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57). Each condition gets its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Independence, not roster variety, is the governing quantity.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Two components reading the same report are one component with two names; "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names" (Fig 8 :49). A trait-variance score can be large while independence is zero, so it cannot be the witness.

2. **Independence promotes resilience when the population is comparing verdicts against a ground that does not update.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :33: "variation inside, selection between" and "Selection between rooms is where the ground votes" (Fig 7 :35). Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The operational form is low ρ between rooms, with the next room "picked against the residual the last one surfaced" (Fig 7 :24) and progress R = h² × S (Fig 7 :26). Correlated frames add spelling, not reach: "searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names" (Fig 6 :53).

3. **The same independence impedes coordination, because coordination requires a shared reading that independence removes.** Fig 10 :13: "two frames, one field, no shared reading." The cost is structural, not friction: "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point" (Fig 4 :27). What supplies the shared reading is an authored convention that makes the disagreement statable (Fig 10 :17) while carrying no world claim, because "a declared meaning is not a grounded one" (Fig 10 :18). It must stay revisable: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 2 :43).

4. **Without a described residual, difference is unaimed and the union is mute.** Fig 8 :21: "nothing was aimed; the union is mute." This is why the first operational step is Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Residual first, frame second (Fig 8 :53).

5. **Valence is relative to the residual, not a property of the component.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same difference that buys resilience for one task is the coordination cost for another. Any standing list of "good" component types is forbidden.

6. **The benefit and the cost are in different currencies and cannot be added.** Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." There is no single optimal-diversity number. There are two curves, a named residual, a named ground, and a budget that decides how much of the space is reached (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").

7. **The specific winning mixture is an honest `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," A search that finds no figure line naming the mixture is not a proof that none exists (Fig 8 :39), and the gap is of the unreachable-at-this-instrument kind, not the underivable-in-principle kind.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a scalar optimal diversity level, and not an inverted-U law. The inverted-U appears in world sources as an outcome curve; it is not a figure line and does not replace the mechanism.
- Not that more components always help. Ten correlated components are one component with ten names (Fig 6 :53).
- Not that coordination costs are removable. They are the price of the lossy transform and are paid in a different currency.
- Not that a convention is a ground, a third frame, or a tie-breaker (Fig 2 :47).
- Not that independence can be read off contents. Convergent derivation and adopted import are identical in the contents (Fig 8 :51).
- Not grounded. No measurement at the system's ground has settled the per-slot claim, and the mode rule forbids writing it as a verdict (`THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which.").

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The metamerism case in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green "cannot comment on the source," and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here the equivalent questions are:

- **Lineage test.** For each component contribution, was it derived against the ground from its own evidence, or adopted from another component? Without that column the independence measure is unobservable, because "Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :52).
- **Perturbation test.** Change the ground's reading, then measure resilience per slot, never as a global average (Fig 1 :19). A global diversity score is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22).
- **Convention test.** Remove the coordination convention and ask whether the joint action still improves contact. If removing it changes nothing, it was not doing the bridging work. If it decides which frame was right, it has already stopped being a convention (Fig 2 :43).
- **Two-currency test.** Report description cost and action cost separately (Fig 12 :130). Any single additive payoff that claims to predict both is R7 and fails the figure.
- The standing refuters R1 to R9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, written before the conclusion, are carried unchanged into `08` and `09`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The framework returns the shape of any viable answer: low evidence overlap, a described residual, a non-updating ground, selection between rooms, a revisable convention, per-slot reporting, two currencies. It does not return the name of the mixture for a given system. Naming one would mean already holding it. The record stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

The user's stated field for this question is diversity and collective performance research. The public, opt-in venues for that field are in `03_public_contact_points.csv`; the first contact carries the residual and the refuter, not this theory.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give one optimal amount of diversity. It says that the governing quantity is how little the components draw on the same evidence, and that this one quantity is read twice. When the collective is comparing separate readings against a ground that does not move, low overlap buys resilience, because correlated frames are one frame with several names and only a ground can correct them. When the collective is acting as one, the same low overlap is a cost, because independent frames share no reading and the convention that supplies one must be authored, revisable, and never allowed to decide which frame was right. Neither effect works without a described residual, and the two costs are in different currencies, so they cannot be added into a single number. The framework returns that shape and a pre-registered refuter. It does not name the winning mixture for a particular system; that stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it at the ground.
