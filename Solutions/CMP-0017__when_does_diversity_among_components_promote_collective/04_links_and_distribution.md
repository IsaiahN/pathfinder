## 5. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before Stage 2.** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user writes a refuter before running it and labels the mode of the result.
