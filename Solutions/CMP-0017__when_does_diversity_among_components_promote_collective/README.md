## The question, restated as an effect

A collective is a set of components that compose into something the components alone are not. "Diversity among components" names a relation among those components: they are not interchangeable. The question asks when that non-interchangeability buys two different things:

1. **Collective resilience.** The collective keeps meeting its ground under perturbation, loss, or a change in conditions.
2. **Coordination.** The components can compose a coherent collective term, agree on a route, and act as one where the task requires it.

A single answer of the form "diversity is good" or "diversity is bad" cannot be right, because the two outcomes are governed by different figure lines and pull in opposite directions on the same underlying quantity. The residual is a union compound: two conditions bind at once, and a case in both is categorically different from a case in either (Fig 9 :57).

**What an answer would look like.** Write the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before searching, per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a bounded rule that says, for a given residual and a given ground, which sort of component difference buys independence and which sort only adds unaimed variety. Binding that shape lets it accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else.

```
DECIDES:    If the component differences are differences of closure and evidence such that the
            components can be ranged separately against a non-updating ground, we keep them and
            expect resilience (A). If they are only different names over one shared evidence pool,
            or unshared variation with no described residual, we expect coordination failure (B).
            A = B? No: they answer different questions, independence-of-evidence versus aim.

            the HOURS entry is a budget, not evidence about the verdict (Fig 12 :126).
PRIOR CASE: CMP-0004 (emergence rename test), CMP-0006 (irreducibility), CMP-0009 (model
            explanation): all treat a union of conditions with per-domain verdicts, never averaged.
ANALOGUE:   Hong and Page 2004 (functional diversity of perspectives and heuristics) and the
            diversity-stability literature (May 1972, McCann 2000): actors / currency / anchor
            map to problem solvers / solutions found / the value function and its optimum.
LIMIT:      At 1,300 components, does the coordination cost stay flat? No. The coherence cost of
            keeping every member in sync grows faster than the independence benefit past a peak;
            this is the price Fig 12 :126 assigns to the second currency.
```

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the result.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283). A list of agreeable readers is a bigger room, not a second frame.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163; Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the reader contributes.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42); the outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
