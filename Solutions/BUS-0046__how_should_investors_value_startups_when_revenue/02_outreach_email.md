# Outreach draft — validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the stage-1 shape below survives the reader's own check. The offer collapses on contact if the output does not survive.

---

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide whether the valuation method is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about startup valuation I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when revenue, user growth and future-market signals conflict, the figures' per-slot rule says the residual must be indexed by slot and never averaged. The quoted line is: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19) and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22). A blended valuation can therefore look sound while one live slot carries the whole error. The conflict is not one hard number to compute; by the union rule it is several well-formed questions with different instruments: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* (Fig 9 :5)
>
> The anchor is the realised clearing event, not today's comparable multiple. *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A comparable multiple moves with the investing pool, so it is a frame, not an anchor. Investor consensus built on one shared data room is a convention for closing a round, not evidence that the price is right: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40).
>
> The price and the settlement are two currencies. *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* (Fig 12 :125-126). So the answer is a shape, not a number: a per-slot valuation, a named non-updating anchor, independent readings bought where the loss is largest, a target price written down as a refusable term, and a staged commitment whose tranches buy the readings that are still missing.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this returns the right price for any named startup, that it returns a weighting, a discount rate or a multiple, or that it settles the question before a realised exit. The framework returns a shape. The company-specific number is a declared gap: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32), and *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134).
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A prospective test showing a blended score preserves the discriminating information of the per-slot residuals when two companies share the blend but differ in a live slot.
> 2. A demonstration that the three readings converge under more measurement of the same kind, with no instrument or anchor change, leaving one residual.
> 3. A realised clearing price that moves systematically with the first investor's private model revision, with no change in the company's cash, customers or conditions.
> 4. Equal realised accuracy from investor panels drawing on disjoint evidence and panels drawing on one shared evidence pool.
> 5. A showing that description cost and action cost are commensurable in one unit and summing them changes the recommendation.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figures and formula (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - A public valuation-method overview that disagrees with the figures about the word "triangulation", useful as a check: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_(finance)
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a valuation decision you can check against a realised outcome. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 — *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 — *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287 — *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` — *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No company-specific number | Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 — *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* |
| Target price bound as a term | Formula, "Direction"; Fig 9 :27 — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* |
| No request for agreement or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Staging offered as a method, not a conclusion | Fig 4 :22 — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Links after the verdict, opt-in only | Fig 10 :42 — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown. |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check what it is looking for; the essays warn that outputs can be contrived to fit the formula. |
