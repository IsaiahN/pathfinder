# The Tether's answer on valuation under conflicting signals

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the company-specific number, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An investor holds a prediction Γ(b,a) (a valuation), takes an action a (a price, a tranche structure, a reserve), and observes o′ (a realised clearing event or an audited cash count). The gap R is indexed by slot, never blended:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

When three signals conflict, the disagreement does not shrink with more measurement of the same kind, and each rule works well on a different subset. By Fig 9 :4-5 it is therefore not one hard question but several well-formed ones. The question is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), with its own loop and its own ground, not a number to be reconciled.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain (verdicts kept separate)

**D1, the signal frames. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 plus Fig 10, low overlap).** Keep a per-slot residual for revenue, user growth and future market. The named decay mode *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26) describes what a blend does to the channel. Signal valence is relative to the residual: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* (Fig 11 :49). The same growth number helps a company whose problem is "can it sell" and hides the problem of one whose cash burn outruns conversion. No standing weight survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).

**D2, the valuation act. Verdict: `general` on the two-currency rule, `specified` on staging, triangulated (Fig 12 plus Formula step 5).** The description and the settlement are two prices:

> **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** (Fig 12 :125)
> **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** (Fig 12 :126)

A DCF or a comparable multiple prices the description. Diligence, tranches, information rights and time price the settlement. Collapsing them into one expected value is the named error. The target price is a held preference, not a term (Formula, "Direction"), so write it down before the analysis and let it accrue a residual.

**D3, the settlement frame. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 7, low overlap).** The anchor is the realised, arms-length clearing event or audited counter:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** (Fig 2 :29)
> **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."** (Fig 2 :30)

A live comparable multiple moves with the investing pool, so it is a frame, not an anchor. Syndicate agreement built on one data room is a convention, not corroboration: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40) and *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).

**D4, the temporal channel. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 10 plus Fig 4).** Staging is the legitimate form of reversibility. *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24), and *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). A tranche tied to a missing instrument buys the next reading; it is a head start, not a replay of this deal.

**Cross-domain.** The three methods (DCF, comparables, market narrative) are a functional group as usually written, with a real isomer claim only over a fixed shared atom set (Fig 12 :117, :123; see `06`). No figure line returns a weight, multiple or price: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a price, a weighting, a discount rate, or a universal multiple. The company-specific number is a `gap` (Fig 13 :134).
- Not that the three signals should be given equal weight, or any weight. Weighting is an arrangement inside the investor's existing method set, and *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* (Fig 8 :32).
- Not that consensus is useless. It is efficient for a convention: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* (Fig 2 :41). It simply is not a witness for the world.
- Not that the current comparable multiple is the market's verdict. It is the market's ongoing conversation; the anchor is the realised event.
- Not grounded. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a realised outcome in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A realised clearing event, not a further argument. The questions that would pay for the shape:

- **Per-slot test:** do two companies with the same blended score but different live slots realise different outcomes, in the direction the live slot predicts?
- **Independence test:** do panels drawing on disjoint evidence sets outperform panels sharing one data room, in realised outcomes?
- **Staging test:** does a tranche tied to the currently missing instrument change realised loss, compared with a single upfront payment at the same headline price?
- **Anchor test:** does the realised clearing price stay fixed when the first investor privately revises their own model, with no change in the company?
- **Standing refuters from `01`:** convergence of the three readings with no instrument change; commensurability of the two currencies; a figure line that returns a numeric valuation rule; a pre-realisation measurement that pays for a price.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134) and *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* (Fig 8 :39). The missing thing is a discriminating ground reading, not a cleverer weighting. The six-term substrate is not used as a valuation ontology; it is retained for the honest-stop role only, and no attempt to map revenue/users/market onto states and structures survived the strain test (re-census R3 in `01`).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a price for a startup and does not claim to. It says that a valuation residual must be indexed by revenue, user growth, future market and company separately, because blending hides the live slot that tells you where the error is. The conflict between the signals is a union of well-formed questions, not one hard number, so split it before you search. The anchor is the realised, arms-length clearing event or an audited cash count, not today's comparable multiple, which moves when investors move; agreement among investors who share one data room is a convention for closing a round, not evidence that the price is right. The description and the settlement are two currencies that do not add, so price the model and price the actions separately, write the target number down as a refusable term, and stage the commitment so each tranche buys the reading that is still missing. What it offers is the shape any defensible answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
