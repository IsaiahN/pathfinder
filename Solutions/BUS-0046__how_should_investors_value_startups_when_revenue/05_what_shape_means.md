## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the preceding line says a system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: per-slot residuals instead of a blend; a union split into well-formed questions; a non-updating realised anchor; independence bought in proportion to the cost of being wrong; two prices kept in two currencies; and a staged commitment that buys readings. A specific price, a discount rate, a multiple or a universal weighting is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The word "valuation" is borrowed from finance; the effect description is: a prediction held by an investor, an action, a realised payment, and a gap indexed by slot.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question: two companies with the same blended score and different live slots will realise different outcomes. That is checkable without agreeing on valuation theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In practice: a valuation method is finished when the residual it predicts is the residual it set out to explain, which is also why self-validation is forbidden.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the ground is a realised clearing event or an audited counter, not an internal rate of return computed from the same inputs as the price.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A new instrument (cohort retention, signed design partners, regulatory approval) is such an import. A new weighting of the same three numbers is not.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Choosing revenue, users and TAM as the three slots is a visible choice, not nature's list.

## 4. The specific "shape" issue in this question: the word triangulation

The valuation literature uses the word "triangulation" for a weighted average of methods (Wikipedia, *Valuation (finance)*, opened this session: *"used in combination, providing a 'triangulation' or (weighted) average."*). The figures use the same word for the opposite discipline: Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Shape reading: the two are different objects. A blend combines readings that share an evidence pool; it is efficient for producing a shared number, which Fig 2 :41 calls *"efficient for a convention."* Independent ranging against a non-updating anchor is what the figures call triangulation, and it is the only version that can carry a verdict. The shape claim therefore refuses the blend as evidence while allowing it as a convention for closing a round. This is a re-census (R1 in `01`), not a semantic preference, and it is recorded in the retraction log.

## 5. Q: What licenses a particular weight or multiple?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular weight or multiple is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, it is the investor's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The world-side record is Investopedia's opened page: *"Different valuation methods will produce different values for the same underlying asset or company, which can lead analysts to employ the technique that provides the most favorable output."* That sentence is what the Formula's "Direction" limit predicts when the sought-for shape has not been bound as an ordinary term.

**Does the answer commit the error the README forbids?** No, because it names no number. It names conditions and instruments, which are already held terms or imports with declared provenance. It explicitly declares the company-specific number a `gap` (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Asking a well-known investor to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a check against a realised outcome.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction kept visible

An earlier reading of the "shape, not a name" rule would forbid naming any valuation method at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an already-held method (a cohort analysis, a comparison bond, a staged tranche) is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is returning a price the framework does not hold and pretending the gap was closed. Both claims are kept here so neither is silently lost, matching the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.
