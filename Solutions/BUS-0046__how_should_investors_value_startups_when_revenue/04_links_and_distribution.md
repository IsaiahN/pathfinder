## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **A genuine conflict about the word "triangulation."** The valuation overview says the three approaches are *"used in combination, providing a 'triangulation' or (weighted) average."* Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The two uses are different objects. In the figures, triangulation means frames ranged separately against a ground; a weighted average of three readings from one data room is a convention, not a witness. The conflict was returned to the census (R1 in `01` section 6) and did not flip the verdict. It is the most useful single thing to put in front of a practitioner, because it exposes the exact word they use in a different sense.
2. **Method choice can follow the desired output.** Investopedia's line about the *"most favorable output"* is a world-side statement of the Formula's "Direction" limit: the sought-for shape is held as a preference, not a term, so no check inside the model can see it. Predicts that a target price smuggled in as a method assumption will survive every internal consistency check.
3. **Independence is the active parameter, not the count of signals.** The crowd and ensemble sources both say averaging helps only when errors differ and systematic error is not shared. This corroborates Fig 2 :31 and :40: three metrics fed by one customer story are one frame wearing three names. Ensemble forecasting adds the practical warning that a spread that is too small produces overconfidence, which is the point-estimate version of Fig 1 :22.
4. **Vivid case information beats base rates.** The base-rate source corroborates Fig 1 :27 (*"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces"*) in shape: a compelling founder narrative is individuating information, and startup survival base rates are the general information that gets ignored.
5. **The number depends on the share count and the count depends on the convention.** Damodaran's public worked example shows that a single company yields a wide range of market caps depending on the share-count convention and that there is no single service following the option-adjusted practice. This is a business-side corroboration of per-slot indexing (Fig 1 :19) and of the two-currency rule (Fig 12 :125): the description is not the settlement. Note that Damodaran is a well-known practitioner; his public record is used as a source, not as a verdict, and his fame carries zero weight under the figures.

No world source overrules a figure line. The three Wikipedia articles, the Investopedia page, and Damodaran's practitioner blog overlap in the broad business/finance pool: they count as one witness, not five. The meteorological and cognitive articles are lower-overlap corroborations of mechanism shape only.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* — ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any theory.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* — a mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's prestige is a filter for where to spend the check, never a reason to accept it.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before showing anything else; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before theory.** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the five refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. This is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious after recording a verdict, offer the three source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator with the instruction that each reader writes their own refuter and declares their own mode. What crosses is a method, which Fig 4 :22 permits.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The current stage-1 claim is deliberately narrow (per-slot indexing, union split, realised anchor, two currencies, bounded gap); it does not assert a price. If a reader refutes any sub-claim, the corresponding finding in `01` is withdrawn and the packet is corrected before reuse.
