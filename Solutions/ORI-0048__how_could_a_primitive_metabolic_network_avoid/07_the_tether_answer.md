# The Tether's answer on primitive metabolic robustness (ORI-0048)

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a chemical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A primitive network keeps its self-maintaining function only while the reactions that compose that function remain within its closure and its habitat. When conditions change, the food set, gradients, mineral catalysts and retention structures shift, and the reach of what can actually be composed can fall even while the reaction list stays the same. The residual is the gap between the function that must persist and the arrangement still reachable after the shift. It is a union compound (network composition and habitat supply), not one hard problem, and it is indexed by reaction function, not by an average robustness score (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a chemical pathway. No named cycle, mineral, cofactor or setting is asserted as the actual carrier. Those are bindings from the chemistry frame and need a ground reading and a provenance record (Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them.").
- Not a claim that autocatalytic sets have been demonstrated in a prebiotic setting. RAF theory is relevant prior work, not something this derivation establishes.
- Not a claim that any laboratory network is robust. Fig 11 :70: a synthetic solve proves wiring, never capability.
- Not a claim that a network anticipates change. Fig 11 :36: selection ran.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the binding, and Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not a theory of everything. The union of network and habitat is a compound with its own loop.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The needed instruments are source-discriminating, not more arguments. Three checks, each stated so it can fire:

1. **Regeneration test:** after a catalyst is catastrophically lost in a steady-state network, does the same closing state return without external replenishment of that catalyst? This discriminates cycle-regeneration from external supply.
2. **Boundary test:** does the same closing arrangement re-assemble after a full habitat substitution and a boundary drop, with no carried template? Fig 12 :107-110 predicts the cost is paid again where the record does not cross.
3. **Overlap test:** under two different food sets, do at least two distinct arrangements over the same molecular inventory close the function? Fig 12 :117 predicts yes; Soyer and Pfeiffer 2010 report the analogous independent fluxes and multifunctional enzymes.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: open chains only with externally replenished catalysts; regeneration of an atom absent from closure and food set with no external supply; robustness by a single fixed list under arbitrary substitution; cost-free re-assembly after a full boundary drop.

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops where the instruments stop. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The framework reports a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32). What is unreachable at this budget (a discriminating measurement on a prebiotic network) is different from what is underivable in principle, and importing against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a first metabolic pathway. It says what shape any robust primitive network must have. The function that survives a change of conditions is the closing arrangement, not the reaction list: essential steps sit in cycles that regenerate their own catalysts, redundancy is different arrangements over the same molecules, and a network that has lost an atom can only recover it by import from outside its closure. Robustness belongs to the network together with its habitat, so changing conditions is a substituted habitat, and a laboratory result under one fixed condition set proves wiring, not capability. Variation plus selection between variants is enough; no foresight is required. The framework names no molecule and claims no measurement. What it offers is the shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The chemical slot stays open until the ground settles it.
