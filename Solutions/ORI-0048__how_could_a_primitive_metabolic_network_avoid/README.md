# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package for ORI-0048

**Question:** How could a primitive metabolic network avoid losing essential reactions when conditions changed?

**Source:** `Questions/origins of life.txt` line 66 (question 48), section 5, Metabolism-first and chemical networks.

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive structural claims, `general` on the shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no experiment on a primitive network was read as settling the binding. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. See `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

**Field for public contact points:** metabolic robustness research (origins of life, autocatalytic networks, systems chemistry, evolutionary systems biology).

## Restating the question as an effect

What is being decided is not which chemical pathway is correct, but what shape any answer must have. Option A: a network keeps a fixed list of reactions and "buffers" the environment until the list fails. Option B: a network keeps the *function* (self-maintenance, energy harvesting, catalyst regeneration) reachable by arranging its reactions as closure, overlap, and import. A leads to a brittle list that cannot name what to preserve. B leads to a checkable shape with a written refuter and an open chemical slot. This binds the sought-for shape as an ordinary term per THE_FORMULA.md "Direction", so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

What an answer would look like: a structural claim of the form "essential function survives perturbation iff the reactions that compose it are closed, overlapping, and open to import", with the chemical identities left as bindings from the world frame, provenanced as such.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of believing the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not replace an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises vocabulary and does not add a measurement.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A list chosen for likely agreement is a list chosen to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
