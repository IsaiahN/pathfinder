## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about preserving the self-maintaining function of a primitive metabolic network under a change of conditions. Option A: the network preserves a fixed reaction list by buffering the environment. Option B: the network preserves a closing arrangement (cycle, regenerated catalyst, isomeric overlap, import), with robustness belonging to network plus habitat. What each leads to: A gives a brittle list that cannot say which reaction is essential or what to import when a catalyst vanishes; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open chemical slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the function that must persist (self-maintenance, energy harvesting, catalyst regeneration) and the arrangement still reachable after the habitat shifts (food set, gradients, mineral catalysts, retention structures, temperature, wet-dry cycles). Arity: two conditions, so a union compound; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: ordered and graded, not symmetric; catalysis is directed, a gradient is a spendable difference, and a perturbation has a direction. Scale: first order inside the network, second order across variants and habitats. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The slots are reaction functions, not a global robustness score.
- **Domain 1, reaction network composition.** Actors: metabolites, reaction steps, catalysts that survive their own use. Currency: closure and reachability from the food set; bits to describe versus actions to test. Anchor: the kinetic and thermodynamic ground, namely whether the reaction runs and sustains flux. Question type: partition inside the frame, overlapping with habitat supply. Order: upstream of D2 and D3.
- **Domain 2, habitat and import.** Actors: food molecules, mineral surfaces, spendable gradients, wet-dry cycles, other compartments. Currency: availability of difference, supply of atoms, perturbation. Anchor: the ground settles persistence; the habitat updates (Fig 11 :73). Question type: union with D1, and enumerated rather than composed. Order: read first, because the food set bounds closure.
- **Domain 3, variation and selection.** Actors: protocompartments, network variants, lineages. Currency: heritable variation and differential persistence. Anchor: the ground via selection between rooms. Question type: union with D2. Order: downstream, needs a settled first-order whole to be a variant of.
- **Domain 4, verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Dependency order.** Refuse before mint (THE_FORMULA.md step 2); settle each first-order whole before the second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the import barrier (Fig 8 :32) and the import operator (Fig 6 :14) were read as strained at first. Re-census resolved it: Fig 8 governs search inside a closure, Fig 6 governs contact with a frame whose closure differs. Different quantities and opposed instruments, so no strain after re-census. One genuine strain remains: the figures price arrangements in bits while chemistry also prices them in free energy, and Fig 12 :125-126 forbids adding the two. It is named, not hidden.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim)

Cited against `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. Quotes are verbatim from the figure text.

### Domain 1: reaction network composition, triangulated

- Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." -> preserve the function, not the member reaction.
- Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." -> essential reactions sit in closing loops; chains cannot self-maintain.
- Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." -> the catalyst must be regenerated, not spent.
- Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." -> redundancy is rearrangement over shared molecules.
- Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish." -> where the record does not cross a boundary, the cost is paid again; this is why loss happens.
- Verdict: **triangulated** across four independent figure quantities, with the fifth supplying the boundary mechanism.

### Domain 2: habitat and import, triangulated

- Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" and Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> robustness is not a property of the network in isolation.
- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." -> read off food, minerals, gradients and conditions; do not design them.
- Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." -> changed conditions are a substituted habitat.
- Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." -> keep the changing habitat distinct from the settling ground.
- Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." and Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom" -> an absent atom cannot be recovered by internal search.
- Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall" and Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." -> import is the only repair, and addition is available where subtraction is not.
- Fig 11 :70 "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." -> a lab result under fixed conditions proves wiring.
- Verdict: **triangulated** across the habitat law, the import barrier, and the introduction asymmetry.

### Domain 3: variation and selection, single-source

- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." -> variation inside, selection between.
- Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." -> no foresight is needed.
- Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" -> agreement among variants is not corroboration.
- Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." -> the honest stop.
- Verdict: **single-source** (three figure quantities, one evidence pool). The biological carrier of variation is not named by the figures and stays open.

No domain is averaged with another. There is no `gap` here: the figure lines close the residual as a shape, not as a chemistry.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate only; they never overrule the figures. Unlike the exemplar, these sources are not written by the framework author. Their overlap is declared rather than hidden.

- **Theoretical biochemistry.** Gabriel Piedrafita, Francisco Montero, Federico Moran, Maria Luz Cardenas, Athel Cornish-Bowden, "A Simple Self-Maintaining Metabolic System: Robustness, Autocatalysis, Bistability", PLOS Computational Biology, 2010. Quote: "A living organism must not only organize itself from within; it must also maintain its organization in the face of changes in its environment and degradation of its components." Also: "a catastrophic disappearance of a catalyst" from which the system recovers. Prediction: closure and catalyst regeneration preserve function under perturbation. **corroborates** D1. Overlap: distinct authors and an ODE model, low overlap with the figures; shares the broad autocatalysis literature with Soyer and Pfeiffer, so the two world sources are one partial pool, not two independent witnesses.
- **Evolutionary systems biology.** Orkun S. Soyer, Thomas Pfeiffer, "Evolution under Fluctuating Environments Explains Observed Robustness in Metabolic Networks", PLOS Computational Biology, 2010. Quote: "fluctuating environments can be a major evolutionary force leading to the emergence of robustness as a side effect." Also: "an increased number of independent fluxes and multifunctional enzymes" and robustness "should decrease upon switching to more stable environments." Prediction: overlap over shared metabolites, not a fixed list, carries robustness. **corroborates** D1 and D2. Overlap: different method (evolutionary simulation) from Piedrafita, but both are computational network models; recorded as low to moderate.
- **Systems biology / origins of metabolism.** Rogier Braakman, Eric Smith, "The compositional and evolutionary logic of metabolism", Physical Biology 10 011001, 2013. Quote: metabolism shows "striking and robust regularities in the forms of modularity and hierarchy, which may be described compactly in terms of relatively few principles of composition." Also adaptations to "simple chemical or energetic differences of environment". Prediction: the unit of analysis is network plus environment, with conserved modules. **corroborates** D2. Overlap: review of metabolism architecture, low overlap with the two PLOS modelling papers.
- **Origin-of-life review.** Arif Husain Ansari, "Progress and persistent questions in understanding the origin of life on Earth", Discover Life 56 article 35, 2026. Quote: "complementary processes operating across interconnected and evolving environments collectively enabled the assembly, concentration, stabilization, and integration of prebiotic molecules." Also: "the transition from geochemistry to biology remains unresolved, underscoring the need for integrative frameworks." Prediction: no single habitat or isolated reaction is sufficient; enumerate the habitat. **corroborates** D2 and the honest stop. Overlap: broad review, shares general origin-of-life literature with the other reviews, so corroboration weight is modest.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives as a `specified`, ungrounded result. A primitive metabolic network preserves its self-maintaining function under changed conditions by keeping the *arrangement* rather than the reaction list: closing cycles regenerate their catalysts, redundancy is isomeric overlap over shared molecules, an atom absent from closure and food set can only be restored by import, and an unrecorded arrangement is paid for again at a boundary. Robustness belongs to network plus habitat, not to the network alone; variation plus selection between variants is sufficient, with no foresight. Domain 1 and Domain 2 are triangulated; Domain 3 is single-source, with the carrier of variation left open. No molecule, pathway or setting is named, and no measurement is claimed. The honest stop is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle, and importing an atom against a budget limit would add what was not needed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, carries four refuters and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The chemical bindings are simply not asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct them. A refutation narrows one domain or removes the shape claim; the other domains can still be checked. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating regeneration test showing whether recovery is internal or externally supplied; a boundary test showing cost-free re-assembly after a full habitat substitution; an overlap test showing that only one arrangement closes over the shared inventory; or a closed system regenerating an atom absent from its closure and food set. Any of these would move the verdict. Prestige, a larger expert room, or repeated agreement would not.
