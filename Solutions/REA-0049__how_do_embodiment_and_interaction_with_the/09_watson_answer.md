## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two options. Option A: embodiment changes representation by adding representational content to an inner store, so a richer body means a richer store. Option B: embodiment changes the reach, the settlement channel and the instruments, so contact, habitat and sensorimotor coupling are the operative variables and the agent alone is not the bearer of capability. What each leads to: A gives a plausible story with no figure-governed quantity and no instrument for deciding which terms are added; B gives a checkable shape, five pre-registered refuters and a set of discriminators, while refusing to name which atoms a given body imports. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a frame predicts from its own library and what registers when it acts in and is coupled to the physical world. Arity: at least two frames, a system and the world; one frame cannot move its own wall. Symmetry: asymmetric, the world is the anchor and does not update on what the frame thinks (Fig 2 :33). Scale: intra-loop settlement, cross-scale instrumentation with `R_T`, and cross-frame import. Fig 9 makes it a union compound: reach-change, settlement and instrumentation can co-occur and a lever clearing one leaves the intersections standing.
- **Domain 1, the representational frame.** Actors: six terms, seven bonds, negation, candidate terms. Currency: composition in `closure(Γ)`, price in bits, reach. Anchor: the ground for settlement. Question type: partition (what composes across the two kinds; what is in reach).
- **Domain 2, the physical world as habitat and anchor.** Actors: objects, affordances, conditions, other agents, forces, selector. Currency: contact, residuals, physical payment. Anchor: the ground, never updates. Question type: union compound, enumerated not composed, with its own loop.
- **Domain 3, the sensorimotor interface / instruments.** Actors: sensors, effectors, pose, frame transform F, prior proto-instruments. Currency: observations, per-slot residuals, `R_T`. Anchor: something already returning something. Question type: dependency link, perception then vocabulary then objective.
- **Domain 4, the checking arrangement.** Actors: outside expert readers, the ground maintainer seat. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool for the framework side, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
- **Order by dependency.** Instrument returning something first; then affordance vocabulary; then objective; then planning; then learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse before mint (FORMULA step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Habitat enumerated before capability claims (Fig 11 :43).

Strain test applied: the content-addition reading strains against Fig 6 :25-26, since the world gains no primitives and the frame gains access. Re-census moves the governed quantity from library size to reach and access. A second strain, behavior-based robotics denying internal world models, dissolves on re-census: representability is not the use of representations. Both kept visible in `01` §6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-governed shape survives as `specified` and ungrounded. Interaction changes what a system can represent by changing its reach and access (Domain 1), by keeping the settlement channel live (Domain 4), and by supplying instruments sharpened from prior instruments at the affordance vocabulary link (Domain 3). Capability belongs to agent and habitat together, not to the agent alone (Domain 2). It does not do this by enlarging an inner store while contact stays fixed, the world does not gain primitives, the anchor does not update, and the framework does not name which atom a given body imports. The three mechanisms are a functional group, not an averaged single cause; the contact bond is ordered, not symmetric. This is a candidate shape, not a measurement.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Every claim is mode-labelled and paired with a refuter and a provenance record, so an error can be found rather than hidden. The avoidable cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the architecture before the shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Keep the shape and refuter in front of the links and that cost is not paid.
- **undoable:** yes. The content-addition reading and the symmetric-coupling phrase are removable without touching the figure text; `01` preserves them as retractions rather than deleting them. A superseding packet can be sent, no primitive has to be withdrawn, and nothing was sent by this agent.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A reach-versus-library instrument that holds contact fixed and shows reach still growing would break F1; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground would break F3 (the figure's own stated refuter); a sensor specified from a description with no returning predecessor would break F4; a term settled with no live ground contact would break F2; a measured, interaction-invariant affordance vocabulary link would break F5. A figure re-census that admits a wall-mover other than import would also reverse the verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
