## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essays.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The public-venue list is a contact-cost filter only.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
