## 0. The residual, in effect terms

The question asks how embodiment and physical interaction change "what a system can represent." The phrase must be bound before any figure is chosen, or every figure will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*).

Binding: **"what a system can represent" = the reach, `closure(Γ)`**, everything expressible by combining the primitives currently held. This is not the library `Γ` itself. Fig 6 :42 separates them: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."* `THE_FORMULA.md` step 8 is blunter: "IT IS THE REACH THAT DRIVES THE LOOP, NOT THE LIBRARY."

R by arity, symmetry, scale:

- **Arity:** at least two frames. A frame and the physical world it is coupled to. One frame cannot change its own wall from inside (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"*).
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. The world is the anchor, and the anchor does not update on what a frame thinks (Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update"*). So an "interaction is mutual updating" reading strains immediately.
- **Scale:** three scales in one residual. Intra-loop settlement (FORMULA steps 1-5), cross-scale instrumentation (step 6, `R_T`), and cross-frame import (Fig 6, Fig 8).

The effect claim under audit:

> Physical interaction does not enlarge an inner store while leaving the agent otherwise the same. It (i) imports atoms into a frame and expands its reach, (ii) keeps the channel to the ground reachable so a term can be settled at all, and (iii) supplies the sensorimotor instrument, which is improved from a worse instrument already returning something. What changes is contact and reach, not the agent in isolation.

**Status:** `specified`, candidate, ungrounded. No measurement in this run distinguishes which atoms a given body imports.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — Interaction changes the reach by import, not by internally enumerating more compositions (D1, decisive)

Fig 6 :14 names **"the only operator that moves the wall"** = IMPORT. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :25-26:

> **"An import adds no atom to the world."**
> **"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access. Composition explores what the operators reach; instruments extend what can be represented at all."**

Fig 8 :45-46:

> **"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."**
> **"Composition crosses the boundary between them, and that surplus is where imports live."**

So when a system interacts with the physical world, the distinguishable effect is not that the world acquires new primitives or that the system rearranges what it already has more thoroughly. The effect is that a frame gains access to an atom it did not hold, its closure expands, and things become representable that were previously unreachable. Note the strict consequence: because only import moves the wall, a system whose interaction never brings it into contact with a closure that differs can compose forever and still not gain a new representational primitive. Fig 13 :118: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

**Strain test.** "The body gives the system grounded concepts" is the ordinary reading. It strains because it confuses the frame's gain with a property of the body. Re-census in D1/D2: the atom is added to the frame, the body is the contact surface. The reading is preserved as a binding, not as a derivation.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 6 (closure/reachability) and Fig 8 (union surplus) with low conceptual overlap, each governing a different question (what moves the wall vs where the atom comes from). Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** show a frame's reach growing while its contact with any differing closure is held fixed, or show a new atom entering the library by composition alone. Either breaks F1.

### F2 — The second job of interaction is to keep the ground reachable (D2, D4)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does."** Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

A term is representable in two different senses: expressible, and settleable. Fig 5 step 5 and `THE_FORMULA.md` "SETTLE" make settlement a separate condition: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Physical interaction supplies the payment channel. A frame with a large internal library but no live channel holds candidates, not settled terms. This is the figure-grounded reason embodiment matters for representation even when no new atom is imported: without contact, nothing in the library can move from `candidate` to `accepted`.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 10 (channel) and Fig 2 (anchor), low overlap. Mode `general` for the architectural claim, `specified` for the prediction that a no-contact system cannot settle new terms.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a term accepted as settled by a frame that has had no contact with the ground, where "settled" means able to be composed over and to pay on a measurement nobody can move.

### F3 — Capability is agent-and-habitat, not an inner enrichment (D2, decisive)

Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

This is the figure that most directly answers the question. The sentence "interaction changes what a system can represent" is true only if "interaction" names a change in contact. If it names an internal redesign that leaves the agent's contact with the habitat the same, the figures say nothing changes, however impressive the redesign looks. The habitat is read off the world, not invented: actors, conditions, relations, then the cascade outward (Fig 11 :43-48). And isolation is substitution, not removal: moving a residual into a clean test environment changes the habitat, inheriting actors you did not choose, which is why a synthetic success proves the wiring and not the capability.

The figure even supplies its own refuter, Fig 11 :72: **"an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."** This is adopted wholesale as pre-registered refuter R2 below.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 11 (capability and isolation) and Fig 6 (import requires a differing closure), different routes. The figure itself labels the isolation law and capability claim as fitted to known cases and not tested forward, so they remain `candidate`. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground, or a demonstration that capability is a property of the agent alone with the habitat held fixed.

### F4 — The sensorimotor interface is an instrument, and instruments are grown not specified (D3)

Fig 6 :26 (above): *"instruments extend what can be represented at all."* Fig 6 :32-33:

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**
> **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."**

Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

Applied to embodiment: a sensorium is not derived from a theory of what a body should sense. It is an instrument inherited and sharpened from a cruder instrument that was already returning a failing reading. The Symbols table makes this literal rather than poetic: the frame transform `F` between allocentric and egocentric "Cannot be run without a pose." A body supplies the pose as a precondition of the transform, not as an extra detail.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 6 (instrument rule) and Symbols table (pose precondition), plus Fig 3 (below). Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** exhibit an instrument specified from a description alone, with no prior returning instrument and no residual to sharpen against.

### F5 — The body-facing link is the affordance vocabulary link, and it is the least measured (D1-D3 boundary)

Fig 3 :7: **"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked."** Fig 3 :25-27:

> **"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Fig 12 :115: *"having terms for what a situation affords was taken for a dictionary and it is a grammar."*

Fig 3 orders perception before vocabulary before objective. The affordance terms are the middle link, the one with no obvious failure and no obvious test. This is where embodied interaction does its specific representational work: it supplies the residuals from which terms for what situations afford can be minted. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* effort spent decorating an inner model upstream of a missing affordance vocabulary cannot fix the missing vocabulary.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 3 (dependency and instrumentation) and Fig 12 (dictionary vs grammar), low overlap. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** show a system that has the affordance vocabulary link working, measured, and invariant under changes in its physical interaction, so that the body contributes nothing to that link.

### F6 — The "body adds content" reading is a flat-formula collapse, and 4E labels are a functional group unless the shared atom set is stated

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Fig 12 :124: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

A sentence of the form "embodiment, extension and situatedness all change representation" records the ingredients and not the bond. Under Fig 12, embodied, embedded, enactive and extended cognition (labels the SEP entry and the Wikipedia pages distinguish) are a **functional group** over a shared kind (`state -> structure -> state` style coupling), not automatically **isomers**. They become a real isomer claim only when written over a full shared atom set with a different arrangement, and then the difference is decisive and invisible in the flat formula. The worked derivation, with its falsifier, is in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Verdict: `single-source` for the figure definition (Fig 12), with the world-level distinctions as corroboration only, and the final type question unmeasured. Mode `specified`.**

**Refuter:** show that the 4E labels can be written with no shared atoms at all, or that their differences are recoverable from the ingredient list alone (which would make the flat formula sufficient and the bond free).

### F7 — What would settle this is a discriminator, not more argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-23): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The colour case was closed by a spectrometer, not by a better argument. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Here the corresponding discriminators are: an instrument that shows whether a reach grew by import or by internal composition while contact is held fixed; a measurement in which an agent-only change moves the ground; an instrument that resolves whether a new affordance term was minted or merely relabelled. Until one of these fires, the verdict is `candidate`, not `grounded`.

**Verdict: `triangulated`**, Fig 8 (proof of absence) and the source essay (discriminator), low overlap because one is a metalaw and one is an instrument case. Mode `general` for the principle, `specified` for the three proposed discriminators.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **R1 (F1):** a frame's reach grows while its contact with every differing closure is held fixed, or a new atom enters by composition alone. Breaks the import-only rule.
2. **R2 (F3):** an agent-only improvement moves the ground, or capability is demonstrated as a property of the agent with habitat fixed. This is the figure's own stated refuter (Fig 11 :72).
3. **R3 (F4):** an instrument is specified from a description alone with no prior returning instrument and no residual to sharpen against. Breaks the proto-instrument rule.
4. **R4 (F2):** a term is shown settled, and composable over, for a frame with no live contact with the ground. Breaks the settlement-channel rule.
5. **R5 (F5):** the affordance vocabulary link is measured, working, and invariant under changes in physical interaction. Breaks the dependency-order claim.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the shape is re-censused from the residual line. Until one is run, no part may be labelled `grounded`.

### SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD

- **SEARCHED:** is the answer already in the frame's closure and merely unfound? The reach-not-library distinction is already stated in Fig 6 and FORMULA step 8. Distribute the search; keep searchers independent (Fig 6 :50).
- **INWARD:** extend the instrument from one already returning something. Proposed discriminators in F7 are of this kind. They sharpen in our own terms and are opposed to OUTWARD.
- **OUTWARD:** find the frame whose closure already predicts the shape. The embodied cognition literature (SEP, ecological psychology, niche construction, situated cognition, behavior-based robotics) is the import track. Characterise R in effect terms first: a reach that changes only on contact. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

### Updated verdict

The shape survives as `specified` and ungrounded: interaction changes what a system can represent by changing its reach and its access, by keeping the settlement channel live, and by supplying instruments grown from prior instruments. It does not do so by enlarging an inner store while contact stays fixed, and the agent alone is not the bearer of the capability. Verdicts per domain: D1 triangulated, D2 triangulated (candidate per the figure's own forward-test caveat), D3 triangulated, D4 triangulated. Strains after re-census: **none hidden**, two named and dissolved in the retraction table. The honest stop is at the instruments: which atoms a given body imports is unreached here, not declared unreachable.
