# What "shape" means — canonical reading from the essays

**Honesty note on sources.** Four of the eight essays were opened and read this session (2026-11-23): *The Library of Babel*, *The Hard Problem is About Source*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*. The other four (*How Intelligence Actually Works*, *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, *They're Scanning Books When They Should*, *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether*) were **not** re-opened in this run and are not quoted here as if they had been. The canonical usage rule and the figure text are local files and were read in full. All essays are by Isaiah N, so their agreement with the figures is one evidence pool, not corroboration.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule, from `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: "interaction changes the reach, not the store; capability is agent-and-habitat; instruments are grown from instruments."
- A name: "the body installs the concept of up, therefore bodies produce grounded symbols." That is a specific binding, not what the figures return.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The anchor is the question itself: "This anchor is provided by your own question."
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, fetched this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` describes effects and withholds the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was bound in effect terms first ("what can represent" = reach), then the figures were censed, then the three mechanisms were separated. The shape is the narrowed set, not a chosen favourite.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

This is why the external literature in `09_watson_answer.md` is recorded as secondary and with overlap. Naming "affordance" or "niche construction" as *the* mechanism would turn a low-overlap corroboration into an import with no source gate.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: metamerism

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, fetched this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." The colour case was closed "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," the spectrometer.

For this question the metamerism error would be: observing that a better behaved system also has a richer body, then naming the body's richness as the source of the representational gain. Same reading, several possible sources: more contact, a different habitat, a sharper instrument, or a larger library. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name (F7 in `01`).

## 5. What the framework does not license here

- It does not license "the body grounds concepts" as a verdict. That is a `general` research-programme thesis in the SEP entry, with live criticisms recorded there. The figures license a capability-and-contact claim, not a metaphysical constitution thesis.
- It does not license a ranking of mechanisms by importance. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- It does not license naming which atoms a given body imports. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

The same essay supplies the prestige rule used in outreach: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers important names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration.
