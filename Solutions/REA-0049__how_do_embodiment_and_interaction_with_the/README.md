## The question restated as an effect

"What a system can represent" is not a warehouse of pictures. In figure terms it is the **reach**, `closure(Γ)`: everything expressible by combining the primitives a frame holds. The residual is the gap between what a frame predicts from its own library and what actually registers when the frame acts in and is coupled to the physical world.

Two options, stated by what each leads to:

- **A (content-addition reading):** embodiment adds representational content. A body supplies grounded concepts, so a richer body means a richer inner store. This leads to a claim about library size and to no figure-governed instrument for deciding which terms are added.
- **B (contact-and-reach reading):** embodiment changes the reach and the conditions under which a term can be settled, not the agent in isolation. Physical interaction is contact with a frame whose closure differs, which is the only operator that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14); it extends instruments, which is what extends "what can be represented at all" (Fig 6 :26); and capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone (Fig 11 :39). This leads to checkable refuters and to a shape that stops where the instruments stop.

The solution argues for B from the figures, keeps A visible as the reading that was re-censed away, and does not claim the run settles the empirical question.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run):** a three-part shape, (i) interaction imports atoms into a frame and expands its reach, (ii) interaction maintains the channel to the ground so terms can be settled, (iii) the sensorimotor interface is an instrument improved from a worse instrument already returning something. If any part fails the pre-registered refuter in `01`, the shape is withdrawn.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this run is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless.
2. **A checker who holds the interior cannot check.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Public-venue choice is a contact cost, never an evidence weight.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
