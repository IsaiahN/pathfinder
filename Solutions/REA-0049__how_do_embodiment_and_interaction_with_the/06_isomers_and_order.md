# Isomers and order: three mechanisms, one compound, and the 4E question

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The question "how does interaction change what a system can represent" arrives as one sentence. The figure census in `01` found three different quantities being changed: the reach (Fig 6), the settlement channel (Fig 10, Fig 2), and the instrument (Fig 6, Symbols table). Two questions are owed:

1. Are these three mechanisms **isomers** or a **functional group** under Fig 12, and what would make the stronger claim true?
2. What is the **dependency order** among them, and which orderings are forbidden by the figures?

A third, world-facing version: are embodied, embedded, enactive and extended cognition different things or different spellings of one thing? That is the same question at a different stratum.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are the three mechanisms isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- As stated in `01`, the three mechanisms do **not** share an atom set. Import operates on `closure(Γ)`; the channel operates on settlement; the instrument operates on the perception-to-vocabulary link. They share no atoms in the figure's sense, so under Fig 12 they are **not isomers** of one another. They are three members of a **functional group**: "contact changes what a system can represent," with the qualifier naming which contact.
- The stronger form: write each mechanism over the full shared atom set `{space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network}` plus the bonds, differing only in which structure mediates which state pair and which operator crosses the wall. Then they **are** isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the claim worth testing, because the difference would be decisive in behaviour while invisible in a flat formula.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if any one of the three mechanisms can be written without an atom that another requires (for example, an instrument account with no `R` or no slot, or a channel account with no ground), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands. Triangulated for the definition (Fig 12 plus the FORMULA composition section); the behavioural discrimination is unmeasured.

### Q2 — What is the dependency order?

Fig 3 names the chain: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry. Applied:

1. **Perception:** an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35). Without a returning reading there is no residual to route.
2. **Vocabulary:** terms for what situations afford (Fig 3 :7: *"it has terms for what things afford: what can be moved, opened, joined, blocked"*). This is the body-facing link.
3. **Objective:** what would count as progress, in the system's own terms (Fig 3 :9-10).
4. **Planning**, then **learn and carry**.

Two further orderings bind:

- **Refuse before mint.** FORMULA step 2 routes REFUTED before MINT; "a refusing term pays the same bargain. What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over." A frame that has been carrying a flat "body adds content" term owes a refusal first, not a new synonym.
- **Describe before import.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The residual was bound as reach before the embodied cognition literature was opened.

Forbidden orderings, named so they can be checked:

- Optimising the inner model while the vocabulary link is missing. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."*
- Specifying an instrument from a description. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- Treating the test harness as the habitat. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

### Q3 — The 4E question at the world stratum

The SEP entry and the Wikipedia entries distinguish embodied, embedded, enactive and extended cognition, and explicitly warn that the labels overlap. Under Fig 12:

- If the four are written with a shared atom set (an agent, a body, an environment, a coupling, a residual) and differ only in the bond between them, they are **isomers**, and their behavioural differences are decisive.
- If they merely list ingredients ("body matters, context matters, action matters"), they are a **flat formula**: Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* and :14-16: *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not / nothing downstream can tell them apart, / and nothing signals that anything was lost."*
- The world sources supply the distinctions but do not choose the bond. The SEP records "the constitution debate" as live; the situated cognition page records the Suchman versus Vera and Simon debate as live. Fig 12 :90: *"Six tests, and they separate the seven bonds."* No test here separates the 4E bonds, so the isomer claim stays `specified` and unmeasured.
- **Falsifier:** if a single symmetric bond (say conjunction) is shown sufficient to write all four with no loss of behavioural discrimination, the isomer claim fails and they collapse to one term under a free bond.

### Q4 — Symmetry of the contact bond

Fig 12 :92-93: *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing. Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term."*

Contact here is **ordered**, not symmetric. Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update"* and :34: the alternative is *"everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* The world ranges against the frame; the frame does not range against a world that changes to match it. So "body and world mutually constitute representation" is an unordered spelling of an ordered relation. Under Fig 12 it is the wrong bond, and charging for its permutations would overcharge by log₂(k!). The ordered form is: the frame ranges, the anchor stays.

**Falsifier:** show a case where the physical anchor changes its answer because of what the frame thinks, and where that change is the mechanism of representational gain rather than drift. That would break the ordered-bond reading.

### Q5 — Is this one compound or several questions?

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The contact question is a **union compound**: reach-change, settlement-channel, and instrument-sharpening can all be present at once, and a single lever that clears the region where only one holds leaves the intersections standing. That is why `01` keeps three separate verdicts and does not average them. The residue after unbundling is not "a harder body question"; it is three specific compounds, each with its own refuter.

## CONCLUSION

The three mechanisms are a **functional group** as currently written, not isomers. They become a real isomer claim only when written over the full shared atom set with the arrangement stated, and that claim has a stated falsifier. Their dependency order is fixed by Fig 3: instrument first, then affordance vocabulary, then objective, with refusal before mint and description before import. The contact bond is ordered because the anchor must not update, so a symmetric "mutual constitution" spelling is the wrong bond. The union reading requires three separate loops and three separate verdicts, which `01` and `09` supply.

This rescues what is defensible without inventing a primitive: no seventh term, no import that moves the wall by decree, and no naming of which atom a body supplies.
