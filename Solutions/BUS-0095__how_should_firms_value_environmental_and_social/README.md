## The residual in effect terms

A firm's prices and accounting books register what the firm pays. They do not register what other people, other species, or future states of the world bear because of the firm's activity. The gap is a set of live object slots (a specific emission, a specific injury, a specific lost habitat), not one global number. The question asks what an answer must look like given that the price system cannot see those slots. Option A: keep searching inside the price and accounting frame until a number appears. Option B: characterise the harm in effect terms, import what the frame lacks from frames whose closure differs, keep the slots separate, and stop at a shape until a ground settles a number. The derivation below arrives at B and states precisely where it stops.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not settle a harm.** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* (Fig 7 :13). Gathering more signatories to a valuation does not add a physical measurement.
2. **Selecting for agreement disqualifies the verdict.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list of people chosen because they already accept a single-number approach manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A firm cannot be the blind verifier of its own harm.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42); *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59). The stake must be declared, not pooled as evidence.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
