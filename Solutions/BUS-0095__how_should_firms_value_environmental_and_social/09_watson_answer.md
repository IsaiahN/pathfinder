# WATSON answer: how should firms value environmental and social harms not reflected in prices or accounting?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is a simulated approximation and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself") it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement of any harm was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says a firm's valuation of unpriced environmental and social harms must look like. Option A: keep searching inside the price and accounting frame until a number appears. Option B: characterise the harm in effect terms, import what the frame lacks from a frame whose closure differs, keep the slots separate, declare the stake, and stop at a shape until a ground settles a number. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode number produced by the same frame that omitted the harm, hides live slots in an average, and spends the independence of the people asked to check it. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an open number until an instrument closes it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Positive mass in object slots where harm occurs and the firm's books show zero. Arity: many slots, one per harm, party, place and time. Symmetry: low, a tonne of emissions, a worker exposure and a lost habitat are not interchangeable. Scale: first order at the direct operation, second order at cascades (Fig 11 :52-54). Kind: a union of overlapping conditions, not one pile: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57).
- **Domain 1, the firm's price and accounting frame.** Actors: management, prices, ledgers, budgets, KPIs. Currency: money, private cost. Anchor: the market transaction, which is frame-internal and produced by the frame, so it cannot settle the harm. Standing: real domain, wrong anchor for this residual.
- **Domain 2, the valuation and natural/social science frame.** Actors: ecologists, epidemiologists, economists, standard setters. Currency: physical measures and monetary estimates. Anchor: physical measurement, dose-response, outcome data. Standing: real domain, anchor available through import.
- **Domain 3, the assurance and business-ethics seat.** Actors: auditors, regulators, affected parties. Currency: independence and declared stake. Anchor: the ground, measured outcomes, never agreement. Standing: real domain, stake must be declared. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Domain 4, the aggregation compound.** Actors: a vector of slots versus a single total. Currency: bits for description, actions for settlement. Anchor: none for a cross-slot sum. Standing: a compound of D1 and D2 plus a notation choice, not a domain until split.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the harm, acquire vocabulary for it, state what counts as progress on that slot, then plan and learn (Fig 3 :19-27). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the comparison bond (Fig 12 :87) governs comparison at a level; the two-currency rule (Fig 12 :125-130) governs summation across description and action. Different objects, so no strain after re-census (see `01` F9 and §7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape closes in four domains; the number does not. Keep the per-slot vector of physical and social measures and treat a total as a budget filter, not a witness. Anchor to a measurement that does not move when the firm changes its reported number. Import the missing atoms from science and public health with provenance and a shadow test, describe the effect before choosing a method, declare the firm's stake, and place the check in a seat that cannot reconstruct the valuation. Hold description and settlement in two accounts and report the round-trip gap of any summary. The specific dollar value for any named harm is `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable at this budget. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters. A second cost is operational: if a single total is treated as a witness, a live slot can worsen unnoticed, which is the exact failure Fig 1 :22 names.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-score claim is removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already refuse it. A published vector can supersede a published total, and no term has to be withdrawn from the figure set. A firm can also add a discriminator and a blind assurance step without importing a new primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument showing two different slot vectors behind the same total would make the total untenable as a witness in the specific case; an instrument showing the physical harm moves when the reported number moves would change the contact test; a demonstration that a price quantity is an unmoving anchor would break Fig 1 :27; a derivation of a physical harm from price atoms alone would break Fig 6 :21; and a figure-derived dollar value settled against a measurement would move that value from `gap` to `grounded`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
