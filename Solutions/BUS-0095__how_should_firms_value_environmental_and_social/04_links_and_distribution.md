## What the wider literature contributes, and how it is weighted

The field given for this question is social impact measurement and business ethics research. The genuinely low-overlap witnesses are ecology and biodiversity valuation (IPBES), climate economics (the SCC record), and the general economics of externalities. Human cognition and instrument discrimination come from the framework author's essays, which are high-overlap and carry no independent witness weight.

1. **IPBES, multiple values of nature.** IPBES hosts a methodological assessment on *"the diverse conceptualization of multiple values of nature and its benefits"* and a business and biodiversity assessment. This predicts that environmental harm is assessed through several value concepts, not one price. It corroborates the per-slot reading in `01` F1 and F7, and the refusal of a single aggregate. It does not decide the verdict; the figures do.
2. **The social cost of carbon record.** The SCC is a dollar estimate of marginal damage, and the record documents that it is *"highly sensitive to a small number of assumptions"* and that different administrations produced *"wildly divergent SCC values"*. This predicts that a number produced inside a frame with a stake in the outcome will move with the frame. It corroborates the anchor rule (Fig 2 :33) and the stake rule (Fig 10 :59). It is one witness from climate economics, not a second figure.
3. **Externality definition.** An externality is *"a cost or benefit to an uninvolved third party"* and often *"cannot reflect the true costs or benefits"* in a private price. This is the residual in effect terms, corroborating Fig 1 :19 and Fig 6 :21. The same record notes that true monetary values often cannot be determined, which corroborates *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).
4. **Impact measurement institutions.** ISV, GRI and the Impact Management Platform are standard setters and professional bodies in the requested field. They predict that impact measurement is an established practice with public, opt-in venues. They corroborate the contact strategy, not the shape claim. The Impact Management Platform's partners explicitly share one evidence pool by design, so their consensus is a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
5. **Framework essays (high overlap).** *The Hard Problem is About Source* predicts that a single reading cannot name its source and that only a better instrument closes the question. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* predicts that prestige and self-validation cannot settle an action. Both corroborate the figure verdicts but share the framework author's evidence pool completely.

No world source overrode a figure. The two apparent tensions were returned to census and narrowed rather than used to flip a verdict (see `01` §7 and `11_audit.md`).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a harm sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essay links.
- **Fig 7 :13** *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* A mass send selected for people already sympathetic to non-financial accounting produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* The standard-setting bodies in the contact CSV consult one another by design. Record their agreement as a convention.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what instrument would discriminate a real change in harm from a change in reported number; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Record their stage-1 verdict before showing any theory, and never pool their later agreement as corroboration.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask for a failure. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure text, the canonical README and the two public economic records. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded, because once they hold the interior their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because the loop cannot check the direction of its own question and the framework's author warns that outputs can be simulated approximations.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If any refuter in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` fires, repair or withdraw the shape claim before sending.
