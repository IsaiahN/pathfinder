# Expert verification packet: the Tether on unpriced environmental and social harms

**What this is:** a claim in a form a social-impact measurement researcher or business ethicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement of any harm is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An unpriced harm is a live object slot in the residual, not a missing entry in a total, so valuation must be per slot and the anchor must be a physical or social measurement that does not move when the firm changes its reported number. A price, provision or budget is produced by the same frame that omitted the harm and cannot score it. What the price frame lacks must be imported from frames whose closure differs (ecology, epidemiology, public health) with provenance and a shadow test; the firm's stake must be declared and the check placed in a seat that cannot reconstruct the valuation. Description and the actions needed to settle it are two currencies that do not add, so a single monetary score is a budget filter with a nonzero round-trip gap, never a witness. The framework returns that shape; the number for any named harm stays `gap` until a ground settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2
- **Statement:** the residual is per object slot, and the anchor for a harm is a measurement that does not change when the firm pushes on it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 — "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 — "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :27 — "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29 — "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 — "The anchor must not update."
- **Refuter:** a price or accounting quantity shown to be an unmoving anchor for an unpriced harm, or a single aggregate shown to retain every per-slot residual. If either holds, C1 falls.

### C2 — valuation-facing; checkable by an impact-measurement researcher
- **Statement:** the firm's closure cannot reach the harm by more accounting; physical measures must be imported with provenance, and the two currencies (description and settlement actions) must be held apart.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 — "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 — "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :51 — "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 12 :125 — "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130 — "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."
- **What to check:** is there a real case where a firm derived a physical harm measure entirely from price and ledger atoms? Is there a case where the two currencies share a unit, so a single figure preserves both? Are there published valuations where provenance of the physical inputs is recorded and a shadow test applied?
- **Refuter:** a derivation of a physical harm from price atoms alone, or a demonstration that description and settlement share one unit. Either kills C2.

### C3 — ethics and assurance; checkable by a business ethicist or assurance provider
- **Statement:** the stake is disqualifying unless declared, the checker must be outside the firm's reconstructed claim, and agreement among peers is a convention, not a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :59 — "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying". Fig 10 :60 — "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged." Fig 10 :42 — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 7 :13 — "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 2 :40 — "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **What to check:** in a real assurance arrangement, can the checker return a verdict without holding the firm's valuation model? Do the standard-setting bodies invited to comment draw on a shared evidence pool? If the firm authors the check, does the arrangement still produce evidence?
- **Refuter:** a case where the valuing firm is also the blind verifier and its agreement counts as evidence without producing the correlation Fig 2 :40 disqualifies. If shown, C3 falls.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- No dollar value, social cost of carbon, SROI ratio or discount rate is derived or endorsed.
- Not a claim that monetary estimates are worthless. They are legitimate budget filters (Fig 9 :60), not anchors and not witnesses.
- Not a claim that a convention is useless. A convention may allocate a budget; it may not decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not a claim that stakeholder lists are fixed. Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."
- Not a derivation of one domain verdict from another. D1 through D4 are kept separate and never averaged.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

1. **Contact test:** does the physical measure change when the reported number changes? If not, the valuation is a recording.
2. **Discriminator:** can two firms with the same aggregate total be shown to have different slot vectors? If no instrument can separate them, the total is not a witness.
3. **Compound test:** does a lever that clears the environmental condition alone leave a residue in the social condition?
4. **Assurance test:** can a checker who cannot reconstruct the valuation still range against the physical measurement and return a verdict?
