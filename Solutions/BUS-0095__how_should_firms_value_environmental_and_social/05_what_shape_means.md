## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: "keep the harm slots separate; anchor to a measurement that does not move when the reported number moves; import the physical atoms with provenance; put the check outside the firm's reconstructed claim."
- A name: "the social cost of carbon is 190 dollars per tonne." That number is an import from an external frame, not something the figures return.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on the limit of convergence: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage must be kept even when a copy is perfect.

Applied here: describe the harm as an effect (what physical or social state changes, for whom, over what scale), then choose the frame whose closure predicts that shape. Starting with "shadow price" or "SROI ratio" and mapping harms onto it is the inversion Fig 9 :27-28 forbids.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For firms, a published shadow price is a declared primitive. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, but it does not ground the harm. The six-term set is a visible guess; a corporate valuation taxonomy is another visible guess. Naming either as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the single aggregate is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A single monetary total is a projection of many distinct harms. It cannot comment on the source. Two firms with the same total can have entirely different underlying slot vectors, and the total cannot tell you which. What is owed is a better instrument (a per-slot physical vector with a source-discriminating measurement), not a single name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point for provenance: "very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." The number cannot carry the arrangement that produced it.

## 5. Q2: what licenses a particular harm's value? ANSWERED

**What licenses the number?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular number is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test and a source-discriminating instrument. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the corporate practice commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, when it reads the number off a projection and names its source. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." A number with no provenance is indistinguishable from a derived one and carries no evidentiary weight.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. A measured harm is not a new primitive of the six-term substrate; it is an imported atom from a scientific or social frame. Naming it with its measured value is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong is calling the binding a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall without provenance. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own correction in its `05` §5b.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious names to endorse a valuation is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**Consensus is a convention.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work* records that a group is only worth having while its members stay different, that "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average", and that "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The standard-setting bodies in the contact CSV consult one another by design. Their converged method is a convention, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture's own demo note says the outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on, and it returns a shape rather than a number. The eight essays agree with the figures because they share an author and evidence pool; that agreement is one witness, not eight.
