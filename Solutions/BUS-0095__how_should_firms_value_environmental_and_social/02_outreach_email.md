# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the number

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been re-checked against the verbatim figure text. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One check on how an unpriced harm becomes visible

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a firm's price and accounting frame registers what the firm pays. It does not register a worker's exposure, a river's state, or a future person's loss. In the figures' terms, the residual is indexed by object slot, never aggregated: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19). A global total is how a live signal disappears into an average (Fig 1 :22).
>
> **The output:** any acceptable valuation must have five features. It keeps the slots separate. Its anchor is a physical or social measurement that does not move when the firm changes its reported number, because *"The ground enters as the only metric."* (Fig 1 :23) and *"The anchor must not update."* (Fig 2 :33). It imports what the price frame lacks from a frame whose closure differs, with the source recorded (Fig 6 :23). It declares the firm's stake and puts the check outside the firm's own reconstructed claim (Fig 10 :42, :59). And it keeps two currencies apart: a description of the harm and the actions needed to find out whether the valuation holds (Fig 12 :125-130).
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not giving you a dollar value for a ton of carbon or a statistical life. The framework returns *"a shape rather than a name"* (Fig 13 :133), so the number is an import from your field, provenanced as such, not a result I derived. I am not claiming that a single monetised total is useless as a budget filter. I am claiming it cannot be a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. A price or accounting quantity is shown to be an unmoving anchor for an unpriced harm, one that does not change when the firm pushes on it (breaks Fig 1 :27 and Fig 2 :33).
> 2. A single aggregate is shown to retain every per-slot residual, so that summing the slots loses nothing (breaks Fig 1 :22 and Fig 12 :130).
> 3. The person who sets the valuation can also reconstruct the claim and check it, and that verdict is counted as corroboration (breaks Fig 10 :42 and Fig 2 :40).
> 4. A source-discriminating instrument shows that moving the reported number never moves the physical harm and that no independent physical measurement is used (breaks Fig 2 :29 and Fig 11 :40).
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - The figure text with line numbers, canonical local copy (Figures 1 to 13, Operators and Symbols tables): `local: C:\Users\Admin\Desktop\Project Pathfinder\FRAMEWORK\reference\03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and its author warns that LLM outputs can be simulated approximations rather than testable derivations. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`, *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No dollar value asserted | Fig 13 :133, *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*; Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* |
| Physical measurement as anchor | Fig 2 :29, an anchor is *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"*; Fig 1 :27, *"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* |
| Stake declared, verifier outside | Fig 10 :59 and :60; Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* |
| No request for prestige or consensus | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| One-to-one, no bulk list | Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 2 :40 on shared evidence pools |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A method is a generator, not a recording of one valuation. |

**No ask for trust.** The email asks for a failure. It does not ask the recipient to believe the framework, to endorse a number, or to join a consensus. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration.
