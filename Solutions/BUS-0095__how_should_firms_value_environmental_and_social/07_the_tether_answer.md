# The Tether's answer: how firms should value unpriced environmental and social harms

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No dollar value for any harm is derived or claimed.

## 1. The residual it answers

A firm's prices and accounting books register private costs. They leave positive mass in object slots where real harm occurs and the books show zero. The residual is per slot, not per firm total: Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." And Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

The pair of environmental and social harms is a union of overlapping conditions, not one pile and not two tidily separate questions (Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep the slots separate.** Value each harm, each harmed party and each place as its own slot. A total may be computed as a budget filter, but Fig 9 :60 says "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The vector is the witness.

2. **Anchor to a measurement that does not move with the firm.** Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." The anchor is a physical or social measurement, tonnes, cases, exposure, hectares, not a share price, provision, or reported estimate.

3. **Import what the price frame lacks, with provenance.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The physical atom comes from ecology, epidemiology and public health, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13). Describe the effect before choosing the frame: Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

4. **Declare the stake and put the check outside the firm.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying". Fig 10 :60: "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The firm supplies data; it does not supply the blind verdict.

5. **Hold two currencies apart.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." Compare at a level, do not add across currencies.

6. **Report the round-trip gap of any summary.** Any single number is a lossy condensation of the vector. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Publish what the number dropped.

7. **Stop at a shape, not a name.** Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The specific dollar value is an import from the valuation science frame, and at this budget it is `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a dollar value, a social cost, an SROI ratio or a universal discount rate. Those are imports with provenance, not figure-derived results.
- Not that monetary estimates are worthless. They are frame products and legitimate budget filters (Fig 9 :60); they are simply not anchors and not witnesses.
- Not that the environmental and social pair is one question or two. It is a union with a compound at the overlap.
- Not that consensus among firms or standard setters settles anything. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not".
- Not that a published shadow price grounds a harm. Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no figure line was allowed to be overruled by a world source.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Contact test:** does the physical measure change when the firm changes its reported number? If the number moves and the harm does not, the valuation is a recording. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Discriminator:** can two firms with the same aggregate total be shown to have different slot vectors? If no instrument can tell them apart, both are below the current distinction horizon and the total is not a witness.
- **Compound test:** does a lever that clears the environmental condition alone leave a residue in the social condition? If yes, the union reading is confirmed for that case (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Assurance test:** can a checker who cannot reconstruct the valuation still range against the physical measurement and return a verdict? If the checker must hold the firm's model to produce its answer, Fig 10 :42 fires.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is a human choice made visible, not nature's own list, and the bodies in the contact CSV share one evidence pool by design. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. What remains open is the number for any named harm, and only a ground reading closes it.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, repeated from `01` §5 and `08`:

1. A price or accounting quantity is an unmoving anchor for an unpriced harm (breaks Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :33).
2. A single aggregate retains every per-slot residual, losing no live signal (breaks Fig 1 :22, Fig 12 :130).
3. The valuer can reconstruct the claim and check it, and that agreement is counted as corroboration (breaks Fig 10 :42, Fig 2 :40).
4. No independent physical measurement is used anywhere and moving the reported number never moves the harm (breaks Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :40).
5. A figure-derived dollar value for a named harm is settled against a ground reading (breaks the stated `gap` and changes the mode).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a firm a number for an unpriced harm, and it does not claim that a single social cost can settle the question. It says the residual is per slot, not per total, so a combined figure hides the very harms it was built to report. The anchor must be a physical or social measurement that does not move when the reported number moves, which a firm's own price, provision or budget cannot be. What the accounting frame lacks has to be imported from ecology, epidemiology and public health with provenance, the firm's stake has to be declared, and the check has to sit in a seat that cannot reconstruct the claim. Description and the actions needed to settle it are two currencies and must be held apart; summing them into one score is the error the figures name. Any single-number summary is a lossy condensation with a round-trip gap that has to be reported. What the framework offers is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
