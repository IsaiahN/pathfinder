## Independent domain census (from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is cross-society variation in category boundaries and groupings. Arity: many slots, one per domain and society, so Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symmetry: two bond types are mixed in the ordinary phrase "categorize differently", symmetric similarity grouping and ordered membership, inference and threshold comparison. Scale: learner, language community, ecology, with a lossy cross-scale transform. A global difference score would discard the only signal that says where to look.
- **Domain 1, the mechanism.** Actors: learners, with other animals as comparison cases. Currency: residual-driven learning, prototype abstraction, priced minting. Anchor: common-task performance under a fixed instrument. Question type: shared-kernel claim.
- **Domain 2, the binding.** Actors: speakers and communities. Currency: labels, generics, classifiers, spatial frames, learned boundaries. Anchor: the local discrimination that the category pays for. Question type: variable-binding claim.
- **Domain 3, the measurement frame.** Actors: researchers and instruments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: a non-updating anchor only if constructed; otherwise the frame's own WEIRD norms. Question type: can the difference be believed?
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The two offered answers are conditions, not opposites.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle D1 before judging D2; describe R before choosing a frame; build the anchor before weighting agreement.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 4 and Fig 5, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's bond definition. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak rather than circular, but it is still one evidence pool.
