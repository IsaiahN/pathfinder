## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For EAR-0078 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before any specific link is named. The shape is: cascade as contact enumeration; per-link status ordered by dependency; chain or cycle named as a notation choice; global scale as a lossy transform with a measured round-trip gap. Naming "Greenland ice loss causes AMOC collapse" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" and "rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach email asks which link would fail first rather than presenting a conclusion.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question that means the shape stops where the per-link list is complete and each link has an instrument, not where a global probability is produced.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A link becomes supported when a measurement or instrument pays for it, not when a model reproduces it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 8). A coupling coefficient taken from a different instrument is such an import; a coefficient inherited from the same expert elicitation is not.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The practical bearing: the global aggregate is a visible choice. Naming it "the planetary cascade" turns a visible choice into an invisible one. That is the same error the exemplar identified in binding metric tensor to Gradient.

## 4. Why "the models agree" is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the fix was the spectrometer, an instrument, not an argument. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Here the reading is "cascade risk is high in the model ensemble". Several underlying causes can produce that reading: shared thresholds, shared expert elicitation, shared coupling signs, or a real physical cascade. The reading does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another agreeing ensemble. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* applies the same rule to groups: "Use the success rate per try, not the total." A cascade claim that has been tried in many correlated models piles up votes without adding ground contact (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. Shape for this question, stated in effect terms

The answerable question is not "is a global tipping cascade real?" as a single verdict. It is: what would have to be true of the contact structure, the link instruments, the bond types and the scale transform for a global cascade claim to be checkable? That is a question about the shape of the claim, and it can be answered without a ground reading. The companion question, which specific links are supported, stays `gap` until an instrument pays for a link.

## 5b. Correction to my own §4

I initially wrote that model agreement "proves nothing" and left it there. That overstates it. Agreement inside one evidence pool is not worthless, it is *efficient for a convention* (Fig 2 :41): a shared vocabulary for what counts as a tipping element is the product of that agreement, not a corruption of it. What is forbidden is promoting that agreement to a verdict about the world (Fig 2 :40). Both readings are retained here so the correction is visible rather than silently applied.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous recipients selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture* records the caveat directly: "outputs generated must be seen as simulacra", and the demo "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The canonically correct stop

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." *Arrival of the Fittest*: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." The honest end of this solution is therefore a shape with named refuters and a recorded lineage, not a verdict on whether global cascades happen. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," (Fig 13 :134).
