# The Tether's answer on EAR-0078

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two effects. First: can a threshold change in one element compose through physical contact into a cascade at global scale? Second: which links in a proposed chain are well supported? The first is a composition question; the second is a per-link ground-status question. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The bundle must be split before it is searched (Fig 9 :54-57), because a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **A cascade is contact enumeration, not a single number.** Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." So the cascade shape is licensed by the figures as a compositional form. Its physical occurrence in any named chain is not asserted. `general` for the shape, `specified` for Earth-system application.
2. **Each link carries its own status.** Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A chain has only the support of its weakest link; a global average discards the signal that says where to look. The honest answer to "which links" is a list, each marked reached or not assessable (Fig 3 :16-17). `specified`.
3. **A chain is a lossy notation.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Where the physical mechanism has a return path, the arrow diagram is missing that bond. Redrawing is a notation correction, not a discovery about ice or forests. `general`.
4. **Global scale needs a bracketed transform.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The loop is the same at both scales; only actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :20, "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."). A global cascade probability quoted without `R_T` is dead reckoning. `specified`.
5. **Agreement is not a ground reading.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Stylized network models and ensembles that inherit the same expert-elicited thresholds are one witness. `general`.
6. **Which specific link is supported is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". No figure line names a physical link, and none should: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The world literature agrees that link strengths are uncertain and that empirical cascade size distributions are lacking. `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that global tipping cascades are physically established. The figures license a shape, not an occurrence.
- Not that any named link (Greenland to AMOC, AMOC to Amazon) is well supported. That is a ground question.
- Not that more models agreeing raises support. One evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the chain diagram is the mechanism. It is one bond type over a habitat with feedback.
- Not that a global aggregate is a new domain. It has no nameable anchor independent of model output and is treated as a lossy aggregate (X1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument that discriminates propagation from shared forcing on a named link, not another ensemble. The corresponding questions are:

- **Contact test:** can a nameable actor and currency be given for the link? If not, F1 fires.
- **Instrument test:** is anything, at any resolution, already returning a failing measurement on the link? Fig 6 :34-35: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve. Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
- **Bond test:** does the link close a loop? If so, the chain must be redrawn as a cycle with a sign.
- **Round-trip test:** does the global aggregate reproduce element-level residuals? Report `R_T`, do not assume it zero.
- **Independence test:** are the two agreeing frames drawing on different evidence and each ranging against the ground? If not, their agreement is one pool.

The six pre-registered refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. The first four break the shape; the last two would improve it or close the `gap`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." A source-discriminating instrument at the ground would close the link question. Until one exists, the answer stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that global tipping cascades are real, and it does not claim to know which links are supported. It says that a cascade is a contact effect, so the habitat must be enumerated outward until the effect stops mattering; that each proposed link needs its own instrument and ground status, because a chain has only the support of its weakest link; that a chain diagram with a feedback return path is missing a bond, because a chain cannot close by construction; and that the move from an element to a global aggregate must carry a measured round-trip gap. It refuses to name a supported link, because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any checkable cascade claim must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The link list stays open until the ground settles it.
