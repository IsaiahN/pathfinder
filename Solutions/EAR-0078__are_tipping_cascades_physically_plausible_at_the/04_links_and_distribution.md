## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Sending the full derivation before anyone has described a link status sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the link-status shape and its refuter; offer sources after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask which link they would expect to fail before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** — "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people already inclined to accept global cascades manufactures the correlation that voids their agreement.
- **Fig 2 :42** — "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong global-risk headline, so buy a low-overlap checker, for example an ecologist or network scientist, not a fifth climate modeller using the same thresholds.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the link-status shape and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask which link would fail first and what they would count as a ground reading; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1** sends the residual and its refuter, never the source (Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source"). The recipient checks the shape against their own ground contact. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2 is opt-in.** Only if curious, send the three source links. Later agreement is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40). A Stage 2 recipient who now holds the interiors can no longer serve as an outside frame for Stage 1.

**Stage 3 sends a method.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state the mode of their result and write their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. If a checker fires any of the six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, stop and revise; do not proceed to Stage 2 with the same claim.
