## QUESTION

How do mistakes by respected institutions change public trust in knowledge more broadly? Restated as an effect: a local, ground-confirmed error at one institution is followed by trust changes in slots that had no direct contact with the error. Option A: treat this as one stock of trust that falls by some amount. Option B: treat it as a per-slot reclassification whose spread follows evidence-pool overlap, with the ground unchanged. What each leads to: A aggregates away the only signal that could explain the spread and invites a coefficient no figure supplies; B keeps four domains separate, names the governing variable, states refuters in advance and records a `gap` where no instrument exists.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: institution, ground-refuted claim, and a trust reading per knowledge slot. Symmetry: "trusted" is a threshold reading (Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities"), while "error changes trust" is ordered, not symmetric. Scale: first order inside the institution's loop, second order across the peer field and the public narrative, third order in the time channel. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, institution loop.** Actors: institution, statements, staff, reward system, reputation. Currency: verdicts and credibility. Anchor: the outcome that does not update on what the institution thinks. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
- **D2, peer verification field.** Actors: other institutions, journals, replication teams, funders. Currency: independence versus overlap. Anchor: the same ground, reached separately. Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice."
- **D3, public trust channel.** Actors: public, media narrators, survey instruments. Currency: contact and framing. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".
- **D4, correction and refusal.** Actors: the mint/refuse machinery, outside seats able to refuse. Currency: terms added and reach. Anchor: the ground settles; refusal is a unary operator, not a bond.
- **X1, one global trust stock.** No nameable anchor and no single instrument. Not a domain; absorbed into D3 as many slots.
- **X2, "science is broken" as a free-floating cause.** No nameable actors. Absorbed into D3 as a narrative frame.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." One lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual, refuse the held term, import a new atom with provenance and shadow test, settle against the ground, then compose. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :34 (coherent drift when an anchor updates) and Fig 10 :24 (the ground does not decay) govern different objects, a frame in the anchor position and the ground itself, so no strain after re-census. Likewise Fig 2 :47 (a ground listed as a party has become a frame) and Fig 2 :29 (a patient outcome can be an anchor) govern an institution entering a disagreement and the outcome itself.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question is a union of four conditions, not one stock. A ground-confirmed error reclassifies an institution from granted-anchor to frame; the breadth of the trust change follows evidence-pool overlap; what falls is contact with a non-updating ground, not knowledge; the owed response is a refusal plus an imported discriminating atom, not an explanation; and the public must remain outside the frame it checks. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on magnitude and sign. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape but do not decide it. A source-discriminating instrument for contact and overlap is owed; until one exists, the result is `candidate`, not `grounded`.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but real. If the union is mistaken for one stock, a single lever (an apology, a retraction, a new mission statement) is applied to a compound and leaves the intersections standing, while a public that already distrusts the frame is asked to trust more messaging. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The avoidable part is spending the independence of outside readers by leading with the theory instead of the residual (Fig 10 :52).
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing; "What falls is the reach" (Fig 5 :55), so nothing has to be deleted to reverse course. Rulings carry provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected shape without withdrawing any figure.
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 5, in particular uniform per-slot movement, no separation of verdicts under visibly independent correction, refusal without reach loss, or internal repair that works without import. A figure line returning a magnitude, or a ground instrument that settles the coefficient across slots, would close the declared gap. Agreement, respect and prestige are not such instruments (Fig 1 :26-27).
