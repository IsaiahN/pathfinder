# The Tether's answer: HIS-0019

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the magnitude and universal sign. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8). No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A ground-confirmed mistake by a respected institution is local. The reported change in public trust is broad. The gap between those two facts is the residual, and it must be indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The question is a union of four conditions, not one problem with one answer: the institution's own loop, the overlap of the peer verification field, the public trust channel, and the correction or refusal mechanism (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.").

## 2. The shape, by domain

**D1, the institution itself.** A respected institution is a frame that the public has granted anchor standing. It is not the ground. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." The error reclassifies the frame: it moves from the anchor column back to the frame column. Trust in the specific institution should fall to the level its maintained contact with a non-updating ground warrants. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10).

**D2, the peer field.** The breadth of the change is the overlap radius of the evidence pools that had been treated as independent. Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 8 :48-49: "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Where pools are shared, one error reads as a failure of the whole set and trust falls together. Where frames range separately against the same ground, the error stays local and the method's standing can hold or rise, because a differing frame was visibly selected against the residual (Fig 7 :35). Verdict `general` on the structure, `specified` for publics, triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 8, Fig 7).

**D3, the public trust channel.** What drops is contact, not knowledge. Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch". A trust decline is a lost-touch signal: the public can no longer tell which frame is ranging against the ground and which is reading from a shared pool. "Pooled readings" is named as a channel failure in Fig 10 :26. Verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 2).

**D4, the correction mechanism.** The error is a REFUTED gap and owes a refusal, not an explanation. Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Fig 5 :55: "What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach." Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". A genuine repair imports a new discriminating atom from a differing closure (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.") with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13). Internal re-arrangement adds no atom (Fig 8 :32). The public must remain outside the frame it checks: Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Verdict `specified` and `general`, triangulated (Fig 5, Fig 6, Fig 10).

**Magnitude and universal sign.** Declared `gap`. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". No figure line returns a trust coefficient. A trust percentage is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. There is no "net effect on public trust" to report.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that knowledge changed, that science is broken, or that the ground moved. The ground does not decay.
- Not that trust always falls or falls by a measurable amount. The sign and size are a `gap`.
- Not that skepticism is good in itself. "Skepticism" is a frame reading, not an anchor.
- Not that a particular institution is corrupt. A competence error and an integrity failure are different qualifiers of the same functional group; this derivation does not decide which occurred.
- Not that an apology, a retraction or a new mission statement repairs anything by itself. A retraction is a refusal only if it actually refuses the held term; an import is required to move the wall.
- Not that one institution's error always spreads. The spread follows overlap, and can be local.
- Not `grounded`. No survey, experiment or outcome was read to settle the shape.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the owed instruments are:

1. **A per-slot trust reading that separates the five slots** (institution, field, peers, method, ground) and separates ability, integrity and benevolence within each. A single global trust item cannot register the shape.
2. **An overlap map** of the evidence pools the trusted frames actually draw on, independent of their public claims of independence. This is the variable the shape predicts will govern the spread.
3. **A contact reading** of the ground that does not update on what any frame thinks: replication outcomes, predictive success, patient outcomes. This distinguishes a lost channel from a degraded ground.
4. **A refusal audit**: does the response actually add ¬ against the load-bearing claim, or only add a justification term? Fig 5 :43: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."
5. **A catalyst test**: does the correction survive its own use and keep returning readings on later errors (Fig 12 :119), or is it a spent part?

Standing refuters from `01` section 5 apply: uniform per-slot movement; no separation of verdicts under independent correction; refusal without reach loss; internal repair that works without import; a figure line returning the number; contact inverse to trust.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The figures fix the shape and the order; they do not fix the magnitude or the sign in a given culture. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument for contact and overlap, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Report the stop and keep the lineage (Fig 10 :62-64: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom."). "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a mistaken institution destroys trust in knowledge, and it does not produce a number for how much trust falls. It says that trust is read per slot, not as one stock: the institution, its field, its peers, the method and the ground are different quantities. A respected institution is a frame that the public had put in the anchor position; the error reclassifies it. How far the change spreads is governed by evidence-pool overlap, so shared pools fall together and independent frames keep an error local, where it can even strengthen trust in the method. What drops is contact, not knowledge, because the ground does not decay and the channel does. The response owed to a refuted claim is a refusal, not an explanation, and a repair that adds no atom from a differing closure is only re-arrangement. The framework returns that shape, five refuters written in advance and the instruments that would close it. The magnitude stays open until a ground settles it.
