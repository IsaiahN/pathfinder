## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before adopting the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a local, ground-confirmed error and a reported broad trust change. Arity: institution, claim, ground, and a trust reading per slot. Symmetry: "trusted" is a threshold (Fig 12 :87), "changes" is ordered; a symmetric join would erase the direction of the effect. Scale: first order in the institution's loop, second order across peers and public narrative, third order in the time channel. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, institution frame.** Actors: institution, staff, reward system, reputation. Currency: verdicts and credibility. Anchor: the outcome that does not update on the institution's opinion. Question type: reclassification of a frame granted anchor standing.
- **D2, peer field.** Actors: other frames, journals, replicators, funders. Currency: independence versus overlap. Anchor: the same ground reached separately. Question type: a compound whose shared fraction cannot be assigned by a single lever.
- **D3, public channel.** Actors: public, narrators, survey instruments. Currency: contact and framing. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type: channel state, not knowledge state.
- **D4, correction mechanism.** Actors: mint/refuse machinery and outside seats. Currency: reach. Anchor: the ground settles; negation is unary, not a bond. Question type: operator availability and import.
- **X1, one global trust stock.** No nameable anchor and no discriminating instrument. Not a domain; absorbed into D3.
- **X2, "science is broken" as a cause.** No nameable actors; a narrative inside D3.

Order by dependency: describe the residual, refuse the held term, import with provenance and shadow test, settle, compose. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

My census matches the primary's four domains and its X candidates. I derived D2 and D3 from Fig 8's independence parameter and Fig 10's channel rule, whereas the primary derived D2 from Fig 2's collapses and D3 from Fig 10 alone. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 says "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per slot), Fig 2 :29 and :33 (anchor), Fig 5 :53 and :55 (refusal lowers reach), Fig 6 :12-14 and :23 (import gates), Fig 8 :47 (independence), Fig 10 :24 (channel), Fig 13 :134 (gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-line shape in `01` section 1: per-slot union; spread follows overlap; standing and contact fall, not the ground; refusal plus import, not explanation.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a real case: a journal retracts a paper, and public trust in science falls by some amount.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **five-slot split** collapses to one trust percentage in most public reporting, losing the differential by standing and overlap;
- the **unary operator** ¬ becomes an ordinary "we apologise" sentence, which is a convention and makes no claim about the world (Fig 10 :46);
- the **source gate and shadow test** on the import are dropped, so an internal review is presented as equivalent to an outside check (Fig 6 :12-14);
- the **gap itself** is erased when a coefficient is quoted, turning a filter into a witness (Fig 9 :60).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a ground-confirmed error does not change the ground, and the correction must be ranged against something that does not update. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground instrument was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the four-line shape as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
