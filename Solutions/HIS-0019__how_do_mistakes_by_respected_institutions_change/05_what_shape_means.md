## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the missing primitive. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> Trust change is per slot. It is a union of at least four conditions. Its spread follows evidence-pool overlap. What falls is standing and contact, not the ground. The owed response is a refusal, not an explanation. No figure returns a magnitude.

A single number, "public trust in knowledge fell by X points", is not a shape. It is a filled slot with no constraint on which slot it occupies.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on direction: the returned books "need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." This is Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.", applied to search.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question the checkable expectation is: trust changes should differ by prior standing and pool overlap, not be uniform.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For trust, an anchor would need to be a measurement that does not update on what the surveyed public or the institution thinks: an outcome, a replication rate, a patient result.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :12-13: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap."

They're Scanning Books (carried mapping) sharpens this: the real import is a new dimension of the space, not a new term inside it; declaring primitives makes a choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. Why one global "trust in knowledge" number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". Several underlying causes can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists," and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a single trust percentage is a reading of one green light. It cannot say whether the source was a loss of contact, a shared evidence pool revealed, a reputation protecting itself, a framing narrative, or a genuine change in the ground. Naming one of those from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a larger survey.

World corroboration, not a figure line: Besley and Tiffany 2023 find that "direct measures of trust are a poor substitute for differentiating between discrete trustworthiness perceptions and behavioral trust"; the Wellcome/RAND review states that "Trust relationships are not simply characterised by the amount of trust placed, but by how well it is placed."

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, the section titled "Prestige Is Not Action":

> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

This is decisive for the framing of HIS-0019. "Respected institution" is a prestige description. It can explain why an error is noticed widely and why a correction gets a budget, but it cannot enter the verdict about what the error did to trust. The verdict hinges on contact and overlap: Fig 11 :40, "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The same essay gives the rule for this whole folder: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This document is such a simulation.

## 6. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any concrete mechanism in this question. That was an overstatement. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Naming an available binding, for example a public replication record or an outside verification seat, is a binding from the world frame onto atoms the library may already hold. It is provenance work, not the invention of a new primitive, and it does not by itself move the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.").

What remains wrong when a name is attached too early is the metamerism point of section 4: naming the source of a single trust reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I initially treated "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24) as if the public's trust reading were itself the ground. It is not. The ground is the outcome that settles the knowledge claim. The trust reading is a channel property. The error changes the channel reading; it does not change the corpus.

## 7. Consequences for this solution

1. **`01` states a shape, not a coefficient.** Five refuters are written in advance; no magnitude is claimed.
2. **`07` and `08` keep verdicts per domain and never average them.**
3. **Outreach leads with the residual and the refuter**, because *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says an effect description is the one message that travels without shared history, and Fig 10 :52 says "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
4. **No prestige weighting anywhere.** A respected institution is a subject of study here, not a source of verdict weight.
5. **The honest stop is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`.** The missing thing is a discriminating instrument for contact and pool overlap, characterized in effect terms but not held.
