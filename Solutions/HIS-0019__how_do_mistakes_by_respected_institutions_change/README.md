## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan, no scraped contact list, no receptiveness column.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the very correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts an outside frame into a member of the frame being checked.
3. **Standing is a stake.** Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." Recruiting by prestige imports exactly the failure this question is about, so the outreach carries no prestige weight at all.

Legally, unsolicited bulk outreach to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
