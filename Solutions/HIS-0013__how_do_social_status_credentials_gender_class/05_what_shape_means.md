## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is therefore:

> a per-slot separator between attributes that settle claims and attributes that only change contact, with the ranking of the six attributes left unfilled because no instrument at this budget discriminates them.

The shape is not the name "credentials matter most", nor "gender is the main driver", nor any effect size. Those are bindings from a world frame, recorded as such, not outputs of the derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- **Describe the residual before choosing a frame.** Fig 9 :28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". The residual here is the per-slot gap between belief and what the claim's ground would pay without the maker's markers. If I had started from "sociology of knowledge" or from "bias" as a frame, any attribute would have seemed to fit.
- **Cast the description in effect terms.** Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The effect is differential belief under marker variation, not "prejudice" as a cause.
- **Enumerate the habitat.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The six attributes are read off the question itself; the reader is told to enumerate other contact actors (institution, discipline, venue, timing) rather than compose them from the physical six.
- **Keep the three searches separate.** Formula step 7. SEARCHED: the world literature already searched this space and returned mixed effects, so more of the same search adds no atom (Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."). INWARD: build the blind-ground vignette instrument from an instrument already returning something, namely existing audit studies that vary names (Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from"). OUTWARD: import an identification frame from experimental economics or psychometrics with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23). Never read INWARD against OUTWARD.

## 3. What licenses a name in this question

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A factorial vignette with independently varied markers and a checker blind to those markers would pay for a per-slot binding.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). The Bertrand and Mullainathan field experiment is such an import from economics: it varies a name while holding the resume fixed and reads callbacks, an actual ground for "who gets a hearing". It licenses the binding for that slot, in that labor market, at that time. It does not license a universal binding for scientific claims.

From *They're Scanning Books* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six attributes in this question are a declared list inherited from the question, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why the ranking is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the projection is a measured association between a marker and belief. Several paths produce it: the marker changes the audience's prior; the marker changes who reaches the audience; the marker is correlated with a third variable that does the work; the audience's prior belief changes the inferred marker. An association cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a ranking.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why the audience's own report of why it believed is not the discriminator. It is produced by the frame being checked.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an attribute at all? ANSWERED, with a correction

**Answer.** No. The canonical phrase governs a primitive not held in the library, a thing that is missing. Gender, class, race, ethnicity, status and credentials are not primitives of the Tether library at all; they are habitat actors (Fig 11 :45), already held in the world frame. Naming one as a candidate channel is a binding from that frame, provenanced as such. The framework neither supplies nor forbids the name.

**Correction to my own first pass.** I initially wrote that the framework "returns a shape, not a name, therefore no attribute may be named". That overstated the rule. The exemplar's own `05` section 5b corrected the same overstatement for physical atoms: the shape rule governs a missing primitive, and naming a held atom is a binding. The same narrowing applies here, with the additional point that these six are not Tether primitives in the first place. What remains forbidden is (i) claiming the framework derived which attribute dominates, and (ii) claiming that naming one moves the closure wall. Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Naming a habitat actor adds no atom to the framework's library.

Both the first reading and this correction are kept visible. Nothing was silently erased.

## 6. Why the answer cannot rank the six

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

The missing thing is a discriminator, not a number. A number without a discriminator aggregates across slots and then asks which slot won, which is the reverse of the method. The honest stop is: the shape is stated, the ranking is `gap`, and the instrument that would fill it is described. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious names to endorse the separator is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the output a budget to be tried by people outside the frame and let a blind ground settle it.

Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost of being wrong here is high, because a false separator would distribute authority wrongly. So independence is bought, not requested as agreement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
