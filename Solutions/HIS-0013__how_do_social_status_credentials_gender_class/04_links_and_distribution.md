## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on the residual

1. **The effect is a union, not one dial.** The Matilda effect page records both attribution reversals and studies finding no difference in citations by author sex. That is not a contradiction to be averaged away. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The mixed record is the signature of a union with unmeasured intersections.
2. **The arrow is not given by the association.** The halo effect literature shows that attractiveness changes ratings of competence, and impression-formation work shows that a central trait radiates into other ratings. Both are symmetric associations unless the marker is varied experimentally. Fig 12 :59 gives the symmetric-bond test; the Operators table gives the ordered sequence bond with the test "swap them. Does it become a different thing? Yes." The word "influence" asserts the ordered form, so an identification frame is owed.
3. **A marker can be a proxy for quality or a substitute for it.** The credentialism source describes both a method-certifying function and a "rough, rule-of-thumb screening mechanism". Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Only the first adds a term; the second reweights existing terms. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before anyone has described what they would count as a failure sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 9 :28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask the recipient what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the figures.
- **Fig 2 :35** - "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :25** - "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Lead with the shape and its refuters.
- **Seat Map :163** - "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." No receptiveness rank exists in this folder.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot separator and its eight refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record that answer before any framework material; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual and the refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** Only if the recipient is curious after recording a stage-1 verdict, offer the canonical README, the verbatim figure text and the figures folder. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because their verdict on stage 1 was recorded first and their evidence pool now overlaps the framework's.
3. **Stage 3, method reuse.** Offer the formula as a generator with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula "Direction"), so each user needs a ground and a failure condition for their own problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `01` and `08` carries two live type questions, S3 on testimony and S4 on the direction of influence. Both are declared in the message. If a recipient resolves either against the separator, the separator is withdrawn at that slot rather than defended.
