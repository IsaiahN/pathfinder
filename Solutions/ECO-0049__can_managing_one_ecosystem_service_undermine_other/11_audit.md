## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what a service intervention predicts and what the habitat does on functions outside the metric. Arity: many slots, since the habitat is a set of actors, conditions and relations. Symmetry: asymmetric; valence is relative to the residual, so the same actor can help one function and harm another. Scale: cascading, with the material effect several steps out and on a longer time horizon. Not a single hard question.
- **Domain 1, the managed service.** Actors: manager, instrument, target process, metric. Currency: service output. Anchor: none internal, because the metric moves when pushed. This is an instrument, not a ground.
- **Domain 2, the unmonitored functions.** Actors: soil, water, nutrients, pollinators, predators and the slow processes they carry. Currency: function state. Anchor: the ground, meaning long-run measurement of those functions. This anchor is absent from Domain 1's frame.
- **Domain 3, the habitat and contact field.** Actors: everything in contact with R and then with those, outward. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. This is where the union lives.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Service and function share conditions (water, season, organisms, relations). A lever on one condition leaves the shared region bound by the other.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat outward, then stabilise the slow slots and their instruments, then choose the lever. Fig 3 :20 governs; effort above the break cannot rescue a link below it.

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 11 (habitat contact) and Fig 1 (per-slot residual), I derived from Fig 9 (union unbundling) and Fig 3 (dependency order). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54/:56 (union), Fig 11 :44/:52/:61 (habitat, cascade, asymmetry).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: "service metric is one slot among many, contact cascades, single lever leaves intersections, management is permanent work."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete management claim, for example "intensifying crop production will degrade water quality and soil organic matter at this watershed."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **which slots are live.** The figures require enumeration from the world; the shorthand does not carry the actor and condition list, so a user can re-import only the services they already measure (Fig 11 :48).
- **the time horizon.** The figures say cascades run outward and the residual can be three steps out; the shorthand does not carry the multi-year monitoring requirement, which is why the ecology sources (Rodriguez et al. 2006) had to be added as a separate atom (Fig 3 :27).
- **the "can" modality.** The shorthand can be read as "always undermines." Raudsepp-Hearne et al. 2010 shows high-output cases with no crossed thresholds, so the universal reading is a lossy artefact of compression.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a single lever on a union leaves intersections standing, and a good average can hide a live slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
