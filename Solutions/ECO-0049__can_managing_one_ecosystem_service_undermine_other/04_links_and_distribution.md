## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Bennett et al. 2009** supplies the typology: trade-offs arise from direct interactions between services or from common drivers. This is the world-language version of Fig 11's relations and Fig 9's union.
- **Raudsepp-Hearne et al. 2010** supplies the measurement: 34 of 66 service pairs were significantly correlated, crop and pork production were negatively correlated with all regulating services measured, and some high-crop municipalities crossed no thresholds. The last point is the honest limit on the "inevitable trade-off" reading.
- **Rodriguez et al. 2006** supplies the scale and time axes: spatial scale, temporal scale and reversibility, plus the instruction to monitor slowly changing variables.
- **Simons and Chabris 1999** supplies the low-overlap mechanism for "less visible": attention, not distance, decides what is noticed. A focused monitoring task makes an unexpected change in an unmonitored function easy to miss.
- **IPBES** supplies the institutional response: a standing science-policy body for services that markets do not price.

These are corroboration only. The verdict in `07` and `09` comes from the figures.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* The ecology papers cite each other and share authors; sending all four as "four independent confirmations" would be the named collapse.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the figure text and the source titles, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** check the structural output against the ground. The recipient sees the residual, the shape and the refuters, and nothing about the framework's interiors.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the output survived and they are curious, offer the three framework links and the source titles. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the loop as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output stands. If a cascade-bound or aggregation refuter fires, stop and retract before sending anything.
