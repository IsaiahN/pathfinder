## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: `S_visible -> (cascade over unmonitored slots) -> F_slow`, with the cascade held as an unknown.
- Name (not supplied): "the decline of eggshell integrity in eagles under DDT in 1962."

The shape says a cascade must occupy the middle term. It does not say which function, which place, or which chemical.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then find *"new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"* and recurse: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* This is the ecology answer to the Babel searcher's problem: the vocabulary comes from the world, not from the framework.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why stage 1 of the outreach carries an effect description and a refuter, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here that means the residual stops arriving only when the slow functions have instruments; until then, the shape is what is offered and the list stays open.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a multi-year measurement of a named function under a named intervention.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* sharpens the first: *"The reference point both sides check against has to stay put. If it moves, 'everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices.'"* The managed service metric moves when pushed, so it cannot be that reference point. The slow function's measured state can.

## 4. Why "less visible" is a shape term, not a vague adjective

The question's phrase has a precise reading here. It does not mean "spiritually important but unmeasurable." It means:

- **Not in the slot list of the managed metric.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **Attended to or not.** Simons and Chabris 1999 (opened, `10_source_ledger.csv`): *"we perceive and remember only those objects and details that receive focused attention."* A manager watching one service is exactly the focused-monitoring condition that misses an unexpected change elsewhere.
- **Registered only when the cascade reaches it.** Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

So "less visible" is an operational condition: no slot, no instrument, no attention. It is checkable, which is what makes it a usable term rather than a rhetorical one.

## 5. Q: Does 'shape, not a name' forbid naming pollination or water quality as the undermined function?

**No, with a distinction.** The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Pollination, nutrient cycling, water regulation and predation are not missing primitives of the framework: Fig 11 :47 lists *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"* and :46 lists *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain."* They are already in the habitat register.

What the framework cannot do is select, from inside, **which** of them is live in a particular intervention. That requires the ground. So naming pollination as a possible undermined function is a binding from the ecology frame onto a held register, provenanced as such. Naming it as *the* answer to a specific question, without measurement, would be the metamerism error: reading a source off a projection.

**Correction to my own first pass (kept visible):** I initially read "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any ecological function. That was overstated, for the reason above. The narrowed form stands: the register is held, the selection is not.

## 6. Why my audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** an ecological test, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From the same essay: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

Applied here: a list of well-known ecosystem-services researchers selected for their agreement with the trade-off claim would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite. Give the claim a budget to be tried by people who can refute it, and let the ground: a monitoring dataset, a before and after measurement, an experiment: settle it.

## 8. Consequence for this folder

Every substantive claim in `01`, `07`, `08` and `09` carries a mode. The structural claim is `general`, the measured ecology patterns are `specified`, and nothing is `grounded`. The shape is offered with three refuters written in advance. The list of unmonitored slots stays open because, per Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
