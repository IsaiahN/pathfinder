# Outreach draft — check the shape before you decide about the ecology

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive your own check. The output must stand before the two-stage offer is made.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about single-service management, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` ecology, not `grounded`):** when a manager raises one ecosystem service, the other ecological functions are not a side condition that follows along. They are live slots in the same habitat. The structure is that the residual is indexed per function and never aggregated, so a service metric near zero can sit beside a live residual on a slow function. Contact cascades outward past first order, and a single lever on a union of conditions clears only the region where the target condition holds, leaving the intersections standing. The claim is that managing one service **can** undermine less visible or slower functions. It is not a claim that every intervention does, or that a named service in a named place is being damaged right now.
>
> **What would refute it:**
> 1. A measured lever whose effect on every other slot is zero at first, second and third order.
> 2. A demonstration that a global service residual is sufficient for all slots, so a healthy average entails no live function residuals.
> 3. A demonstration that the service and the other functions form a partition, not a union of overlapping conditions.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and the mode rule: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
> - Public ecology sources on service trade-offs are listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`; I will send titles on request so you can check them independently.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a habitat you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name / role]
