## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The neutron EDM result is a bound, not a measurement of zero: `d_n = (0.0 +/- 1.1_stat +/- 0.2_sys) x 10^-26 e cm`, implying `theta_bar < 10^-10`. QCD permits theta_bar anywhere in [0, 2 pi], so its closure cannot derive the small value; setting it to zero by hand is a stipulation. The weak CKM phase gives order-one CP violation in K and B decays but only about `10^-32 e cm` to the neutron EDM, so "CP is conserved" is refused. Whatever solves the residual must (a) relax theta_bar_eff dynamically, (b) coexist with the large weak CP phase, and (c) be falsifiable by an EDM measurement or an axion signal. Those three constraints are the shape. The framework does not name the mechanism; the specific answer is unreachable until a ground settles it.
