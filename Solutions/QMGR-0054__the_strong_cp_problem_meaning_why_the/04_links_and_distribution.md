## Two findings that bear directly on the strong CP pitch

1. **The weak frame kills the cheap answer.** The NC State / TUNL page states that the CKM phase produces observed CP violation in B and K meson decays but contributes only about `10^-32 e cm` to the neutron EDM, whereas the QCD theta angle is free in [0, 2 pi] and allows as much as `10^-16 e cm`. So the residual is a comparison of two CP conditions, not a demand for global CP conservation. This is the cheapest ground fact in the pitch and it should lead.
2. **The null result is a bound, not a zero.** The measured value is `0.0 +/- 1.1 +/- 0.2` in units of `10^-26 e cm`. Pawel et al. (eLife 2024) explain why a null result can be evidence of a negligible effect within an interval without being evidence of an exact zero. Cite this when explaining why "theta_bar = 0" is not what the experiment says.
