## Restatement of the question in effect terms

QCD admits a CP-violating term proportional to the vacuum angle theta_bar. Generically that term gives the neutron an electric dipole moment of order `d_n = (5.2e-16 e cm) theta_bar`. The ground returns `d_n = (0.0 +/- 1.1_stat +/- 0.2_sys) x 10^-26 e cm`, an upper bound `|d_n| < 1.8 x 10^-26 e cm` at 90 percent confidence, hence `theta_bar < 10^-10`. Because theta_bar is a periodic parameter free to take any value in [0, 2 pi], the theory's own closure accommodates order-one values and cannot derive the small one. Two options. Option A: assert that theta_bar is simply near zero, a raw fact or a stipulation. Option B: treat the smallness as a residual owed a mechanism from a frame whose closure differs, and leave the slot open until a ground reading settles which mechanism. This package takes B and records why A fails the figures.
