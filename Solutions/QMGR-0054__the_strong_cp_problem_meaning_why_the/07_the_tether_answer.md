# The Tether's answer on the strong CP problem

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claims, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

QCD permits a CP-violating vacuum angle theta_bar that would give the neutron an electric dipole moment of order `d_n approximately 5.2 x 10^-16 e cm times theta_bar`. The ground returns `d_n = (0.0 +/- 1.1_stat +/- 0.2_sys) x 10^-26 e cm`, a bound implying `theta_bar < 10^-10`, while the angle is free in [0, 2 pi]. The weak CKM phase supplies order-one CP violation but only about `10^-32 e cm` to the neutron EDM. So the residual is not "why is CP conserved"; it is "why is the strong CP angle roughly ten orders below the weak one."

It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). It has its own slot and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The EDM result is a bound, not a zero.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Say `theta_bar < 10^-10`, never `theta_bar = 0` measured.
2. **The ground is the measurement, not theory's preference.** Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The Abel et al. value does not move when a theorist prefers zero.
3. **QCD's closure cannot fix theta_bar.** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* theta_bar is an input, not a derived quantity. Setting it to zero by hand is a stipulation, not a derivation.
4. **The easy answer is refused.** The weak frame shows order-one CP violation with an EDM contribution of about `10^-32 e cm`, so "CP is conserved" cannot close the residual. Refuse that term before minting any mechanism.
5. **A solving mechanism must satisfy three constraints**, each checkable: (a) relax theta_bar_eff dynamically rather than stipulate it; (b) coexist with the large weak CP phase; (c) be falsifiable by a ground reading, namely a nonzero EDM at the next sensitivity or an axion signal with the predicted relaxation. These come from describing the residual first (Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*).
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The axion, a massless up quark, Nelson-Barr models and anthropic selection are names from the physics frame, provenanced as such. None is selected by the figures. The axion import passes a shadow test (it explains the tiny EDM) but has no completed source gate (no detection; Fig 6 :12-13).
7. **Which mechanism is correct is unreachable at this budget.** Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Unreachable at this budget differs from underivable in principle (Fig 8 :40-43): the remedy is more search, not an extra primitive.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the axion exists, or that PQ symmetry is realised in nature.
- Not that theta_bar has been measured to be zero. It has been bounded.
- Not that CP is conserved. The weak frame forbids that answer.
- Not a derivation of the strong CP angle from the six-term substrate.
- Not a new primitive, and not a wall-moving import that has completed its source gate.
- Not `grounded`. No EDM or axion measurement settles `left(R, phi)` for any candidate mechanism.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **EDM threshold test:** does the next generation of nEDM experiments (n2EDM, TUCAN, LANL, PanEDM, nEDMSF) return a nonzero `d_n` with theta_bar extracted, or push the bound further down? The NC State / TUNL page lists the experiments and the `10^-27 e cm` target.
- **Axion test:** does a haloscope or helioscope detect an axion with the coupling implied by the theta_bar relaxation, with a mass and coupling that exclude the alternatives? The ADMX, CAST and IAXO programs are the public instruments, listed through the sources in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
- **In-closure test:** does a lattice result remove the need for an import by showing the effective CP angle is automatically negligible? That would fire refuter 1 in `01` section 3 and withdraw the shape claim.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** (1) an in-closure derivation fixing theta_bar; (2) a measured EDM near `10^-16 e cm`; (3) a restoration of global CP compatible with K/B CP violation.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The framework stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The specific mechanism is not named because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). Agreement among readers is not an instrument: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not solve the strong CP problem and does not claim a final answer. It says the neutron EDM result is a bound, not a zero; that QCD's closure cannot derive the tiny vacuum angle theta_bar, so setting it to zero by hand is a stipulation; and that the weak CP phase is order one while its own contribution to the neutron EDM is about `10^-32 e cm`, so "CP is conserved" is not the answer. What any solution must do is relax theta_bar_eff dynamically, coexist with that large weak phase, and be testable by an EDM measurement or an axion signal. Those three constraints are the shape. The framework refuses to name the mechanism, because it returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Which mechanism is right is unreachable at this budget, and that is a verdict, not a silence. The falsifiers were written before this run, and the instruments that would close it are named.
