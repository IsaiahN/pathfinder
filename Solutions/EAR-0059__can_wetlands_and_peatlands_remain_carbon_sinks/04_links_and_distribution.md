## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4: only methods cross up; a recording carried upward "looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see at a named site before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Agreement gathered from a list selected for receptiveness is a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Journal prestige and citation count choose where to send, never whether the shape is right.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a site they know; (3) record their verdict and date; (4) only then offer the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4: methods cross up, head starts come down. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per site.** THE_FORMULA.md "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 residual survives. As drafted it does, but only if the five refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are sent unchanged and before the links. Do not replace them with a request for endorsement.
