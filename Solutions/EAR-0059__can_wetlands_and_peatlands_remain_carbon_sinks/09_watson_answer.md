## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** a peatland remains a carbon sink where hydrological contact is maintained, the loop sign stays on the accumulation side with CH4 and combustion included, and the landscape does not push the site across a state threshold. These three conditions are kept separate and indexed per site and microform, never averaged into a global yes or no. The same water position changes valence with the gas and the horizon. Quick loss of sink status is a channel failure, not decay of the ground. The universal flip point is a `gap`: what is owed is a per-slot source-discriminating instrument. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the mechanism shape but share a broad evidence pool, so they are one witness, and the low-overlap source that appeared to conflict was resolved by re-census rather than ignored.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. If the union is treated as one question, a manager rewets a site whose binding condition is temperature or fire, and a carbon budget counts a sink that has already crossed at the pool microform. The packet records the shape, the falsifiers and the lineage, so the error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a pooled conclusion instead of a per-slot generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing, so the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted. No condition has to be withdrawn to correct the shape; a failed refuter is recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot net carbon instrument that names a flip point and pays prospectively across sites would close the `gap`; sink unchanged across removal of water-table contact, a single lever restoring all three conditions, a pooled average predicting named slots, a monotonic water table across gases and horizons, or a figure re-census deriving a universal threshold would each break the corresponding finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
