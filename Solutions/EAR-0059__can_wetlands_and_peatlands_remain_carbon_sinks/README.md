## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single global yes or no verdict. The residual is indexed by site and by microform, and the figures forbid averaging it into one signal (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.
3. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A publication record or a prestigious affiliation is a contact-cost filter, not evidence about the carbon balance.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
