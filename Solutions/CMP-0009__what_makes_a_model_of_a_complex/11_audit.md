## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete verdict about a specific modelling practice, for example "a high-accuracy agent-based model of a city is explanatory if it uses the right variables and has been validated on one dataset".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **isomer condition** (Fig 12 :117), which collapses to the functional-group claim (Fig 12 :123) as soon as the explanatory model needs a variable the fit lacks;
- the **name-versus-shape limit** (Fig 9 :32), which a concrete request for "the right model" breaches by asking the framework to name what it returns as a shape.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a fit cannot score itself, and a typed relation plus a refusing ground is what the standing requires. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
