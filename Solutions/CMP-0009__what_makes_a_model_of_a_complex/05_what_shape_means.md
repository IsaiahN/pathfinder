## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0009 the shape is the two-condition standing in `01` (C1 compositional structure plus C2 ground contact). Naming the one right model for a given complex system would be a name, not a shape, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual (a fitted model's self-score) in effect terms before choosing any figure line. That is what section 1 of `01` does. Choosing a favourite figure first would be the inversion Fig 9 :27-28 warns against.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the two-condition shape is a visible guess about what explanation requires, not a discovery of explanation's own list. Turning it into a name (a specific model architecture declared explanatory by fiat) would convert a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source/provenance rule, and why fit cannot see the generator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is exactly the pattern-reproduction case. A fit is a reading of the projection. It does not carry the source. Many different causal arrangements can produce the same observed patterns, and the fit cannot distinguish them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the source guessed off the reading. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

*They're Scanning Books* states the general form: *"A corpus is the compressed output of human minds; the capacity that produced it is a generator, and outputs do not contain their generator."* A fitted model is an output-shaped object. Explanatory standing belongs to the generator side, which is why C1 asks for a re-applicable method and C2 asks for a ground that can refuse it.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. In every stable system, the two are separated... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous modellers to agree with a shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried: an intervention test, a habitat substitution, a second frame with a different evidence pool. That is also why `03_public_contact_points.csv` lists public institutional venues rather than personal addresses, and why no receptiveness ranking appears anywhere in this folder.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule at first, taking it to forbid naming any structure at all. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It does not forbid binding a term already held in Γ. Applied here: the two-condition standing is the shape of a missing status (explanatory standing not yet settled by a ground), so naming that standing as C1 and C2 is legitimate; naming a specific model architecture as *the* explanation would be a name where the figure returns a shape.

I also overstated §4 as if fit could never carry information about source. *They're Scanning Books* gives the counterexample of lab-grown versus mined diamonds: isotope ratios are a trace, and when a tool can read them, provenance becomes recoverable. The correct claim is narrower: a fit **without a discriminating trace or instrument** cannot comment on source. The metamerism point stands; the absolute version does not. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. None of them can settle anything they report on. The explicit author's warning in *Arrival of the Fittest* is relevant: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* That is precisely why the refuter in `01` section 5 was written before the conclusion.

## 8. What the essays are and are not used for

All eight essays share an author and an evidence pool with the figures (maximal overlap). Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight. They are used here for three things only: the canonical reading of "shape", the source/provenance and prestige/action rules, and the honest statement of simulated status. The WATSON verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` comes from the figures.
