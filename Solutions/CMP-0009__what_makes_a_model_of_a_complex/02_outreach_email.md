# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two conditions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated as a shape and the refuters below are written down. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one output before you decide what "explanatory" means

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about modelling complex systems. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the application, not `grounded`):** a model earns "explanatory" only when two conditions hold, and they must be kept separate.
>
> 1. **Internal structure (C1).** The model carries typed relations between its variables, not just the variables. The relation is the part a flat formula leaves out: two models with the same ingredients under different relations are different things, and the difference is invisible in the pattern fit but decisive in behaviour. What crosses to a new problem is the re-applicable method, not the recording of one successful fit.
> 2. **Ground contact (C2).** The residual is reported per object slot, never as a single accuracy number. A global fit near zero with one live slot is a legal state, and averaging is how that live signal disappears. The claim must be rangeable against something that does not change because the model thinks otherwise, and it must be checked by a second frame that does not share the model's evidence pool.
>
> Fitting observed patterns satisfies neither condition by itself. Coverage and compression are quantities the model produces about itself, so they cannot be the verdict. A term that explains one occasion perfectly fails the acceptance bargain: stating it plus what it leaves unexplained must cost less than the confusion it removes, and a one-occasion fit does not pay that across occasions.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the model is the seat of explanation. Explanation is a property of model and habitat together, the way capability is a property of agent and habitat. I am also not claiming to name the one right structure for your system. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am not claiming this is a theory of everything, and I am not asking anyone to take the framework on trust.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a criterion independent of the model's own coverage and compression settles explanatory standing in favour of a high-fit, ungrounded model;
> 2. a model declared explanatory hides a live per-slot residual that changes a prediction under intervention;
> 3. two models sharing every variable but differing in a typed relation turn out to be interchangeable under intervention;
> 4. a pure recording, with no re-applicable method, generates novel correct predictions after the habitat changes;
> 5. explanatory standing is established by agreement within one evidence pool, with no second closure and no ground that can refuse the claim;
> 6. the standing survives a habitat substitution that removes the ground's payment.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong in that place and I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README, including the "shape, not a name" limit: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Full architecture (19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question, and its author warns that outputs could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]

## Stage 1 before stage 2

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other problems. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
