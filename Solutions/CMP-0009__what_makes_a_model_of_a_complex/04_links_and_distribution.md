## What the wider literature bears on the question

These are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide; a conflict would send the derivation back to the domain census, never flip the verdict.

1. **Statistics, low overlap with the framework.** Shmueli (2010) separates explanatory from predictive modelling on the grounds that the two pursue different goals, and notes that *"the 'wrong' model can sometimes predict better than the correct one."* This corroborates F1 and F2 at the level of statistical practice: fit and explanation are different standings. It does not corroborate the bond claim (F4), which is figure-internal.
2. **Causal inference, low overlap.** Pearl (2018 interview) puts passive pattern learning on the first rung and intervention and counterfactuals above it. This corroborates the seeing/doing asymmetry read in section 1 and the refutability requirement (F7). His *"model-free curve-fitting"* phrase is the world's name for what Fig 1 :25 disqualifies.
3. **Philosophy of science and complex systems, low overlap.** Strevens (Depth, 2011; Bigger Than Chaos interview) argues that explanation keeps only the causal difference-makers and abstracts the rest away. This corroborates F4 and F5: what survives crossing a scale is not the full recording but the part that makes a difference. His warning that a bare "something killed them" is not an explanation corroborates the requirement that the bond be typed, not merely present.
4. **Physics of complex systems, low overlap.** Anderson (1972) argues that *"at each level of complexity entirely new properties appear"* and that reconstruction from lower-level laws is not the same as reduction. This corroborates the level-relativity of Fig 12 :104 and the closure result in F9. It does not license importing a specific new primitive; the figures still decide that.
5. **Framework-internal essays, maximal overlap.** The three essays fetched above share an author and an evidence pool with the figures. Their agreement with the verdict is expected and carries no independent corroboration weight. They are used for the canonical reading of "shape" and for the simulated-output caveat, not as witnesses.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered. Overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv` so that one pool is not mistaken for two witnesses (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* The next venue should differ from the last one and match the residual surfaced.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its two conditions, (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure here?", (3) record the verdict, (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems, each with its own ground and its own refuter written in advance.

Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient holds the interiors, their agreement stops being evidence. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted by Fig 4 :22, but `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" requires each user to write down what would refute their own result, since a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
