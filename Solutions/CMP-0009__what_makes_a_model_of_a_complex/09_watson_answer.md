# WATSON answer: what makes a model of a complex system explanatory

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the application, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

What makes a model of a complex system explanatory rather than simply capable of reproducing observed patterns? Restated as an effect: why does a model with R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| near zero on observed slots still not earn explanatory standing, and what would have to hold for it to do so? Option A: fit is enough. Option B: two conditions, compositional structure (C1) and maintained ground contact (C2), must both hold and be kept separate. A leads to a self-score the frame produces about itself; B leads to a checkable shape with stated refuters. R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unaccounted standing of a fitted model. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57), not one hard question. Symmetry: the standing is an ordered threshold, not a symmetric join; explanatory is above fitted, not a second name for it (Operators :51). Scale: single-model loop per slot, population of models selected between rooms (Fig 7 :35), and the membrane between a recording and a method (Fig 4 :22).
- **D1 Model-internal frame.** Actors: atoms, bonds, Γ, candidate terms. Currency: |φ|, residual R, settlement. Anchor: the ground for a term and the MDL bargain for acceptance. Admitted.
- **D2 Ground and observation frame.** Actors: measurements, interventions, o′, slots. Currency: contact, R. Anchor: the anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D3 Verification frame.** Actors: verifiers, competing models, rooms. Currency: agreement versus residual, independence. Anchor: the ground via triangulation, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat and deployment frame.** Actors: environment, conditions, relations. Currency: capability as model-and-habitat contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 Explanation as a property of the model alone.** No nameable anchor; the standing floats free of any ground. Not a domain yet.

Union, not partition: C1 and C2 overlap and the whole is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-57). Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, refusal, planning and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test: the apparent compression conflict between Fig 1 :25 and Fig 5 :42 was reconciled by re-census, not smoothed. Fig 1 disqualifies compression as a self-scored verdict; Fig 5 prices a term under a declared code. Different objects.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A model is explanatory when C1 (typed bonds carrying a re-applicable method, with the one-occasion bargain refusing a flat fit) and C2 (per-slot residual, an unmoving ground, low-overlap triangulation, and a stated failure condition) both hold, in a habitat where contact can actually change. Fit is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. The model alone is not the seat of the standing. The specific model that earns it is a declared `gap`. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** a misdiagnosed model. A model failing C2 would be given more architecture, and a model failing C1 would be given more data, so a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. The cost is bounded and locatable because the refuters are written before the run, so an error is findable rather than invisible. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting the wrong shape.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its nine falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A superseding packet can replace a sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An intervention showing a bonded model and its flat-fit twin predicting alike; a habitat substitution in which a pure recording nonetheless generates novel correct predictions; a live per-slot residual that changes nothing under intervention; a standing established by one evidence pool with no refusing ground; or a source with genuinely different evidence showing that fit alone predicts explanatory standing. Any of these moves the named finding, not the whole verdict by averaging.
