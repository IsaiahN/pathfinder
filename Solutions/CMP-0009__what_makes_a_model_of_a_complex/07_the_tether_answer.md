# The Tether's answer: what makes a model explanatory

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on its application to complex-system modelling, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A model Γ can drive R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| near zero on the slots already observed and still not be explanatory. The residual is the unaccounted standing: what would have to be true for the same fit to be a stand-in for the world rather than a summary of one occasion? It is a union compound, not one hard question: C1 (right internal structure) and C2 (maintained ground contact) must both hold, and *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Fit is not a verdict.** Coverage and compression are frame-internal. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* A model cannot earn explanatory standing by a quantity it produces about itself.
2. **One-occasion accuracy fails the bargain.** Fig 5 :44: *"fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Explanatory standing requires |φ| + |R|φ| to pay across occasions under a declared code, not merely to memorize one dataset.
3. **C1: bonds, not just variables.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* A model with the right variables under the wrong relation is a different substance, and the fit cannot tell the two apart. What crosses a scale is the method: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
4. **C2: a ground that can refuse, reported per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A second low-overlap frame, ranged separately against the same ground, is what makes the standing mean something.
5. **Explanation is model-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A more elaborate model that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :40).
6. **The two conditions are never averaged.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A single accuracy number is a filter; the per-domain verdicts are the witnesses.
7. **No name is returned.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The figures give the shape of explanation, not the name of the right model for a given complex system. That name is a declared gap (F10).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that prediction is worthless. Prediction is a legitimate goal; it is a different goal. The claim is only that predictive success cannot certify explanatory standing.
- Not that every model must be complex, or that a small model cannot explain. A minimal model with the right bond is fine; a maximal model with only ingredients is not (Fig 12 :5).
- Not that agreement among modellers settles anything. Agreement in one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the framework can certify its own account of explanation. *"A frame cannot certify its own limit"* (Fig 8 :37).
- Not `grounded`. No intervention test, habitat substitution or second-frame check has been run here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument or intervention, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the answer *"is unreached until a better instrument exists"*; the spectrometer, not a dispute, resolved it.

- **Intervention test:** does the model's bond structure predict the result of an intervention that its flat-fit twin gets wrong? This discriminates the isomer case (Q2 of `06`).
- **Habitat substitution:** does the method cross to a new habitat and generate novel correct predictions, or does the model repeat a past success (Fig 4 :25)?
- **Per-slot exposure:** does the model declare a live slot residual that changes a prediction, or does its aggregate hide one (Fig 1 :13, :22)?
- **Second-frame check:** does a low-overlap frame, ranged separately against the same ground, converge or conflict (Fig 2 :31)?
- **Standing falsifiers:** the nine conditions in `01` section 5. Any one fires against the named finding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The two-condition shape stops at the limit of what these instruments can distinguish. The specific model that earns the standing is not named; naming it would be a name where the figure returns a shape. *They're Scanning Books* warns that *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The record of the route is what keeps the result checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that a model explains because it fits. Fit is a self-score: coverage and compression are frame-internal, and a term that explains one occasion perfectly fails the acceptance bargain. What earns explanatory standing is a pair of conditions kept separate. First, the model carries typed relations, not just variables, so it can say what would follow if the arrangement changed; two models with the same ingredients under different bonds are different substances, and only the re-applicable method crosses to a new scale. Second, the model keeps a channel to a ground that can refuse it: the residual is reported per slot, never averaged, and a second frame with a different evidence pool checks it separately. Explanation is a property of model and habitat together, not of the model alone. The framework returns that shape, not the name of the right model. It writes down refuters before the run and stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish, recording the lineage so the stop is checkable.
