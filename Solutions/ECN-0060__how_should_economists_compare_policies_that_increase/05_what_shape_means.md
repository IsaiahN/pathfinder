## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (verbatim in `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`). Fig 13 records that the system *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, as quoted in the exemplar's mapping: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Not a shape:** "Policy A is better than Policy B."
- **A shape:** "The comparison has two operands in different currencies, is ordered rather than symmetric, requires a named threshold, must be reported per slot, and must record the provenance of its objective."

The shape is checkable without knowing the winner. That is the whole point of sending it first.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the residual in `01` §0 is stated in effect terms (two currencies, a thing that moves when pushed), not in the vocabulary of any welfare doctrine.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describing the residual (two currencies, no anchor) already narrows the field of admissible comparison procedures before any welfare theory is named. That is the work `01` does.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

Choosing a welfare function in policy evaluation is exactly this kind of visible choice. Declaring it does not ground it (Fig 10 :49).

## 4. The source problem: why a summary is not an instrument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, as quoted in the exemplar's mapping. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

For ECN-0060 the parallel is direct. A national income statistic and an inequality index are two readings. Many different welfare objectives can generate the same ranking of those readings. The ranking does not identify the objective. What is owed is a discriminator that can tell objectives apart by their ground consequences, not a further argument about which objective is elegant. This is why `01` F4 puts the missing instrument at the objective link and why the ranking stays `gap`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A policy index that certifies its own fairness is a frame certifying itself.

## 5. Scope correction: what "shape, not a name" does and does not forbid

I initially overstated the rule. The README phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. In this question:

- Total income and the distribution are **already available, measured terms**. Naming them and reporting them separately is not forbidden. It is a binding onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive.
- The **winning policy** is not derivable from the figures. The framework returns the shape of the comparison procedure, not the winner. Asserting a winner would name something the derivation does not hold.
- The **objective anchor** is genuinely missing. The framework can characterise what it would have to look like (invariant under admissible reweighting, ground-settled, not authored by the ranker) but cannot name one. That is the `gap` in `01` F1.

The earlier overstatement is kept visible here rather than silently erased. The correction narrows the rule; it does not remove it.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, as quoted in the exemplar: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the procedure against real policy data, and a ground that settles it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
