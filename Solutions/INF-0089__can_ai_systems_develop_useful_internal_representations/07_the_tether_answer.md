# The Tether's answer: can AI systems develop useful internal representations of causal structure from observational data alone?

**Mode: `specified` on the domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a boundary, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| per object slot, where o' is a stream of passive observations and Γ is the model's library. The question bundles three conditions, which are a union compound rather than one hard problem (Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."):

- **D1 representation:** can Γ hold a directional, refusable term at all?
- **D2 identification:** is that term reachable in closure(Γ) from passive o' alone?
- **D3 usefulness:** does the ground pay for it?
- **D4 import:** what would have to cross the wall, and with what provenance?

The load-bearing word is "alone". It removes D4's import and D3's ground contact, leaving a closed frame with no anchor. Refuse that term before minting anything (formula step 2).

## 2. The shape

1. **Causal direction is a bond, not an ingredient.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A model that fits the joint distribution has the ingredients; it does not thereby have the ordered bond that makes one slot a cause of another. Refusal additionally needs the unary `¬` (Operators table :57). `general`, triangulated across Fig 12 and the Operators table.

2. **A closed observational closure cannot add the direction atom.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." If the atom is absent, more data, more parameters and more search do not supply it. This is why observationally equivalent DAGs fit the same data and diverge only under intervention, Fig 12's isomer. `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8.

3. **The verdict on D2 is `gap`, not "impossible".** Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," The framework can characterize what is owed in effect terms, a channel that ranges against an unmovable ground, but cannot name the missing primitive (Fig 9 :32). This is a structural barrier theorem inside the framework, not an empirical proof about all systems.

4. **Usefulness is settled at the ground.** Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." Observations are the habitat; interventions and downstream outcomes are the ground. A disentanglement score or a reconstruction loss is a filter for which term to mint (Fig 5 :26), never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 11.

5. **The wall moves only on import.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall" and Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Remove "alone" and the literature's positive results fall into place: auxiliary variables (Khemakhem et al. 2020), multiple environments and interventions (Schölkopf et al. 2021), goal-directed interventions and time order (Meltzoff et al. 2012; Schulz et al. 2007). Each is an import with a source gate and a shadow test, debited against independence. `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 13.

6. **Per-domain verdicts, never averaged.** D1: yes in principle, if Γ has the bonds (`general`). D2: `gap`, no direction atom reachable in a closed observational closure (`specified`). D3: no observational-only claim can be `grounded` (`specified`). D4: yes under conditions after import (`specified`). Averaging these into "partly yes" would destroy exactly the structure the question needs.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "impossible in the world". Fig 8 :39 forbids that reading, and the barrier is conditional on closed Γ, no import, no ground contact.
- Not a name for any causal variable. Which latent is causal is paid for by the ground, not by the framework (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that reconstruction loss, disentanglement scores or compression are evidence. They are frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).
- Not that more models help. Agreement compounds, evidence does not (Fig 7 :13).
- Not a proof that agents generally cannot learn causality from observation. Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The claim is about contact, not about intelligence.
- Not `grounded`. No intervention reading was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Intervention test:** present two observationally equivalent graphs to the learned representation and intervene on the putative cause. Does the representation's predicted effect move with the intervention outcome? That is the ground paying.
- **Temporal discriminator:** does the representation exploit time order, and does performance fall to chance when cause and effect are reversed, as in Meltzoff et al.? That is a discriminating instrument, not a name.
- **Import audit:** for each positive identifiability result, record which assumption was imported, its provenance and its shadow test. A result with no recorded import and no ground contact would reverse D2.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** reachability of the direction atom with no import; settlement by a self-produced metric stable across ground change; a symmetric-only vocabulary carrying a refusable directional claim; two disjoint frames agreeing with later interventional confirmation; one lever clearing all three conditions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four slots are reported, the lineage is recorded, and the stop is at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. Fig 4 :30 requires the round-trip gap R_T to be measured rather than assumed; here it is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints in `01` §6 (the closed-frame condition, the underivable/unreachable-at-budget distinction, the shape-not-a-name limit). *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The figure text is metalaw for design questions, the author is the anchor only for what it genuinely cannot settle (WATSON 60, 2026-11-22). Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer yes or no to whether AI systems can learn causal structure from observations alone. It splits the question into four conditions and refuses the word "alone" before minting anything. Causal direction is an ordered bond, not an ingredient of the joint distribution, so a model that fits the data has not thereby represented a cause. A frame searching inside a closed library adds no atom, so a direction atom that is not already held cannot be found by more passive data; the honest verdict there is a gap, not an impossibility proof. Usefulness is settled at the ground by an intervention or a downstream outcome, never by a score the model produces for itself. The wall moves only on import, and the positive results in the literature all pay that price: auxiliary variables, environment labels, time order, interventions. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which latent variable is causal stays open until the ground pays.
