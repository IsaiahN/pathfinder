# WATSON answer: causal representation from observational data alone

**Mode: `specified` on the domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can AI systems develop useful internal representations of causal structure from observational data alone?

Restated as an effect: can a frame Γ whose only channel is passive o' mint and settle a term φ that carries causal direction, where R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19)? Option A (yes) accepts a correlation-shaped term as causal with no anchor that could refuse it and spends the independence of anyone asked to check it. Option B (a shape and a boundary) keeps what the figures derive, refuses the word "alone", and states the import or instrument that would close the gap. The load-bearing word is "alone": it removes both the import and the ground contact.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Multi-slot: causal structure is at least binary and typically a DAG, so R is a vector over slot pairs. Symmetry: causal direction is asymmetric, while the observational distribution over two variables is symmetric under swapping them. The bonds that could carry direction are the ordered `→`, `⇒`, `⋛` plus unary `¬`. Scale: one model loop, a population of frames across environments, and the channel to the ground. Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **D1 Representation frame.** Actors: variables, latents, ordered bonds, negation. Currency: description length |φ| and the MDL bargain. Anchor: the figures as metalaws for what composes, the ground for settlement. Admitted, mode `general`.
- **D2 Identification channel.** Actors: passive o', Γ, the search over closure(Γ). Currency: search budget, atoms, reach. Anchor: none nameable inside the frame. Admitted, verdict `gap`.
- **D3 Ground and usefulness.** Actors: interventions, experiments, downstream decisions, verifier. Currency: settlement by what pays. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. Admitted, mode `specified`.
- **D4 Import compound.** Actors: inductive biases, causal sufficiency, ICM, auxiliary variables, environment labels, time order. Currency: imported atoms with source gate and shadow test, debited against independence. Anchor: the provenance record. Admitted, mode `specified`.
- **X1 "useful" as a frame-internal score.** Actors: the model's own reconstruction or compression metric. Currency: loss. Anchor: none that does not move with the frame. Not a domain yet, absorbed into D3.
- **X2 "causal structure" as a free-floating property.** No nameable actors, currency or anchor apart from D1 to D4. Not a domain; it is the union of them.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D2 overlap (a frame cannot search for a term its vocabulary cannot express) and D2/D4 overlap (an import changes what the search can reach). One lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). **Order by dependency:** perception → bond vocabulary → intervention-sensitive objective → search or mint → settlement at the ground, with a refusal of "alone" before any mint (Fig 3, formula step 2).

Strain test: Fig 8 :54 (underivable is not unknowable) does not strain against the closed-frame barrier. The first is a frame that can import; the second is a frame forbidden to import. Different closures, different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is a shape and a boundary, not a yes or a no. D1: a causal representation needs ordered bonds and a refusal operator; a joint-distribution fit does not supply them (`general`, triangulated). D2: in a closed frame with no import and no ground contact, the direction atom is unreachable in closure(Γ); the verdict is `gap`, not impossibility (`specified`, triangulated). D3: usefulness is settled at the ground by an intervention or downstream outcome, never by a self-produced score (`specified`, triangulated). D4: with a provenanced import and an intervention ground, identification is possible under conditions (`specified`, triangulated). Naming the concrete causal latent is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32). Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. The word "alone" decides which side of the boundary a claim falls on. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **Cost if wrong:** two symmetric errors, both bounded. If the boundary is drawn too loosely, a correlation-shaped term is accepted as causal and the verifier's independence is spent by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). If it is drawn too tightly, legitimate import-based identification is blocked. Because mode and refuters are stated before the claim, an error is locatable rather than invisible.
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing; the reach falls because something was added (formula step 3). No primitive has to be withdrawn, and the verdict can be superseded by the corrected packet. The lineage stays in the record so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 provenance rule).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Reachability of a direction term from passive data with no import and no new instrument, validated against unseen interventions, reverses D2. A self-produced score that stays stable across a ground-changing intervention while external outcomes disagree reverses D3. A directional, refusable claim in a symmetric-only vocabulary reverses C1. Two disjoint frames agreeing with later interventional confirmation reverses the independence finding. A figure re-census admitting that the shape-not-a-name limit governs held atoms as well as missing ones would change the naming verdict. Precisely these are pre-registered in `01` §4.
