## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 requires it be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

- the D1 representability verdict, which is a yes and is lost when the four domain verdicts are compressed into one no.

What survives cleanly: no closed observational closure adds a direction atom, and usefulness is settled at the ground. R_T was not measured numerically because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
