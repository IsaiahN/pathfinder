## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes institutional officer addresses; those addresses are not reproduced here. Use the public channels listed in `03` only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan. Contact is one-to-one and opt-in.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Contact cost decides how we approach someone, never whether their answer counts.
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence before the check.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Headline result

The figures give a **shape**, not a date and not a chemical name. Naked RNA replication is a legal cycle (Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input."), so the figures do not require a compartment for replication as such. But capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), a compartment is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66), and the replicator plus its boundary is a union compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55). So a boundary of some kind becomes constitutive for Darwinian evolution once cooperation and parasites matter, while *which* boundary and *when* it appeared are ground questions. The honest answer to "from the start" is `gap`: unreachable is a verdict, not a silence.
