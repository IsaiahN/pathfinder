# The Tether's answer on RNA evolution and compartments

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `gap` on the historical timing claim. Nothing here is `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether RNA-based evolution required a membrane or some other compartment "from the start". It bundles a functional question (is a boundary required for Darwinian evolution at all?) with a historical question (was one present at the origin of heritable replication?). Fig 9 :55 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". The replication condition and the boundary condition are co-present and form a union; the timing question is a separate, downstream partition. The verdicts stay separate and are never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Naked replication is a legal cycle.** Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input." Fig 12 :119 "A part that survives its own use." A self-replicating RNA is exactly a cycle plus a catalyst, and the figure says a loop closes when its sign is right. It does not say the loop needs a wall. So for replication as such the answer is no compartment (D1, `general`).

2. **Capability belongs to the replicator together with its habitat.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A compartment is a substituted habitat: Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." It changes local concentration, keeps a catalyst near its products, and makes a lineage something a parasite cannot freely enter. For higher-level Darwinian evolution the answer is yes, a boundary of some kind (D2 and D3, `specified`).

3. **The compound is a union with its own loop.** Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Bounded differential reproduction is reached by the compound and by neither condition alone. A lever that only improves replication leaves the boundary condition binding (Fig 9 :55). The unit of selection is the bounded lineage once cooperation and parasitism matter.

4. **The framework returns a shape, not a chemical name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" Fig 13 :10 "near, far, between, contained" already licenses containment as a state of space, and Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." So no seventh primitive called membrane is minted. Vesicle, coacervate and mineral pore are bindings from the chemistry frame, provenanced as such, not figure-derived names.

5. **The figures do not date the event.** Fig 6 :34-35 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The rock record and chronological experiment are the ground for timing, and the figures carry no temporal index for it. The answer to "from the start" is `gap`: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

6. **Do not mistake the figure called "The Membrane" for a lipid bilayer.** Fig 4 :21 "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." That figure filters methods upward and head starts downward between an individual and a population. It governs the outreach transfer question, not prebiotic chemistry (retracted reading in `01` F1).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a lipid membrane was required from the first replicating molecule. The functional claim is about a boundary that changes contact, and the chemistry is withheld.
- Not that the figures settle geological order. D4 is `gap`; the world sources conflict on timing and the conflict is recorded, not voted away (Ma 2017, Le Vay and Mutschler 2019, Slootbeek et al. 2022).
- Not that naked RNA cannot evolve at all. Simple Darwinian evolution in solution is real and is corroborated by Joyce and Szostak 2018 and Slootbeek et al. 2022. The claim is about the compound where cooperation and parasites bind.
- Not a new primitive, not a theory of everything, and not `grounded`. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement cannot convert a candidate into a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, and already recorded in `01` §4 and `06`:

1. A multi-function, parasite-resistant, open-ended RNA evolutionary system in well-mixed solution with no boundary of any kind would refute the functional shape.
2. A demonstration that containment is not expressible by the space term, or that a membrane must be admitted as a seventh primitive, would refute the atom inventory.
3. A ground reading that dates encapsulation unambiguously relative to the origin of heritable replication would close the timing gap and convert `gap` to `grounded`. It would not refute the structural shape.

The ground is an experiment or a rock reading, not further argument. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
