## 3. Two things in the world literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The "no" side is real, not a straw man.** Slootbeek et al. 2022 and Joyce and Szostak 2018 both state plainly that simple Darwinian evolution can occur without compartmentalization. The shape claim in this folder does not deny that; it says the boundary becomes part of the unit once cooperation and parasites bind. Anyone checking this should test the compound claim, not the crude "compartment always required" claim.
2. **The "other form of compartment" phrase is already answered by the field.** Membraneless coacervates, fatty acid vesicles, porous rocks and mineral surfaces are all live candidates (Slootbeek et al. 2022; Joyce and Szostak 2018). This is exactly why the framework returns a shape and not a name: the evidence does not discriminate among these sources from a single reading.
