## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0040 the shape is: *differential reproduction of a bounded heritable unit*. The chemical slot stays open: `boundary(???)`. Naming fatty acid vesicle, coacervate or mineral pore is a **name**, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on bootstrapping vocabulary by proximity: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 9 :30-31 says describe the effect rather than the cause, because our words are borrowed.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the effect description: what would you observe if bounded differential reproduction were the operative shape? A lineage whose beneficial products stay with the lineage, and a parasite that cannot freely enter it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question the stop is not a fused answer but a disciplined split: functional necessity is shaped, chemical identity and timing are `gap`.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** An experiment or a rock reading that discriminates among lipid vesicle, coacervate and mineral pore as the historical boundary, or an experiment that fixes the order of encapsulation and replication.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. A result from coacervate chemistry may be imported into the shape, but the import must be recorded as adopted, not passed off as derived.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason names are dangerous: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several compartments (vesicle, coacervate, pore, mineral surface) can produce the same reading, "bounded differential reproduction". A single reading does not determine which source produced it. The essay's lesson is that the answer stays unreached until a better instrument arrives: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The error is to read the shape "a boundary is required for higher-level evolution" and conclude "therefore a membrane existed from the start". That commits two faults at once:

- It **names** a slot the framework leaves open (Fig 9 :32).
- It **dates** an event the figures carry no index for (`01` F7, `gap`), turning a functional claim into a historical verdict.

The two faults are visible in the world literature as a real conflict: Ma 2017 says higher-level Darwinian evolution shows clearly only after enclosure in lipid membranes; Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 say encapsulation may have occurred at any stage; Slootbeek et al. 2022 say compartmentalization is not strictly required. Agreement among these sources is not available, and the framework does not pick a side. The honest move is to keep the shape and mark the rest `gap`.

## 5. What "compartment" means in the field, in effect terms

The question says "a membrane or some other form of compartment". In the literature fetched this session the class contains at least:

- fatty acid and phospholipid vesicles (membrane-enclosed);
- coacervate droplets formed by liquid-liquid phase separation (membraneless);
- porous rocks, mineral surfaces and ice matrices (spatial localisation without a self-made boundary).

All of these change the same thing in effect terms: contact. They concentrate reagents, keep a catalyst near its products, and make a group of relatives a unit that parasites cannot freely enter. That is why the framework can license the **effect** without licensing a **name**. The class is defined by what it does, not by what it is made of.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names agreeing that "the membrane came first" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be tried and let the ground, an experiment or a measurement, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." plus "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Answer to "what does shape mean here"

For ORI-0040, **shape** means: a bounded unit of differential reproduction, with the chemical identity of the boundary left as `???` and the timing left as `gap`. It is enough to tell a checker what to look for (does the system keep a benefit with its own lineage? can a parasite cross the boundary?) and what would refute it. It is not enough to answer "which compartment, and when". That is the framework's honest limit, not a gap in the writing.
