## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the name and the date do not. Naked RNA replication is a legal cycle and needs no boundary as such (D1, `general`). A compartment is a substituted habitat that changes contact, so capability for higher-level Darwinian evolution belongs to replicator and boundary together, and the two form a union compound with its own ground (D2 and D3, `specified`, triangulated). Containment is already a space state, so no membrane primitive is licensed, and the framework returns a shape while withholding the chemistry (Fig 9 :32). Whether a boundary was there at the very start is `gap`: the figures carry no temporal index, and the world sources conflict (D4). This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result from a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the discipline is kept. The shape is stated with its mode, its figure lines and its refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than smuggled in. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator: a claim that a lipid membrane was required from the first replicator would be both unlicensed and easy to refute, and would waste a reviewer's budget. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The two overclaims, a chemical name and a geological date, are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, no primitive has to be withdrawn, and the retraction stays visible in `01` §6.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment demonstrating multi-function, parasite-resistant, open-ended RNA evolution with no boundary would break the functional shape; a derivation of containment from something other than the space term, or a forced seventh primitive, would break the atom inventory; a rock-record marker or chronological experiment dating encapsulation relative to heritable replication would close D4 and move that verdict from `gap` to `grounded`. A figure re-census showing that Fig 4's membrane is a lipid bilayer would reopen F1. None of these has fired, so the verdict stands as `candidate`.
