# Expert verification packet: the Tether on RNA evolution and compartments

**What this is:** a claim in a form an origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, verbatim figure lines and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `gap` on geological timing. Not `grounded`.** No experiment or rock reading is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6). It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A self-replicating RNA is a legal cycle whose output re-enters as input and whose catalyst survives its own use, so nothing in the framework requires a compartment for replication as such. Capability nevertheless belongs to the replicator together with its habitat, and a compartment is a substituted habitat that changes contact: it concentrates reagents, keeps a catalyst near its products, and makes a lineage something a parasite cannot freely enter. The replicator plus its boundary is therefore a union compound with its own loop and ground, and a boundary of some kind becomes part of the unit of selection once cooperation and parasites bind. The framework returns that shape, not a chemical name, and carries no instrument to date the event. It offers the shape any candidate answer must have, refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where the figures stop.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework internal; checkable by reading Fig 12

- **Statement:** a self-replicating RNA is a legal cycle plus catalyst, and no enclosing boundary is required for the cycle as such (D1, `general`).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Also Fig 12 :119 "A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
- **What to check:** do these two lines say anything about containment, or only about loop closure and catalyst identity? A cycle closes by its sign; the figure gives no wall term.
- **Refuter:** derive from the figures a rule that every cycle closing under a catalyst requires an enclosing boundary. No such rule exists in the text; if one is produced, C1 falls. A well-mixed, multi-function, parasite-resistant, open-ended system with no boundary would also break the compound claim in C3 at the ground.

### C2 - capability and habitat; checkable against agent-and-habitat reasoning

- **Statement:** a compartment is a substituted habitat that changes contact, and capability belongs to replicator plus habitat, never to the replicator alone (D2, `specified`).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
- **What to check:** does a boundary change the contact that selects, or only decorate a system whose differential reproduction is already fixed? If the latter, the figure's unit of capability is not implicated.
- **Refuter:** the figure itself supplies one: Fig 11 :72 "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim." Exhibit an RNA-only improvement that increases differential reproduction in a closed system with no boundary change. A real counterexample would refute C2.

### C3 - union, chemical name withheld, timing not dated

- **Statement:** replication and boundedness are a union with its own loop; containment is an existing space state, so no seventh primitive is licensed; and whether the boundary was present "from the start" is `gap` (D3 and D4).
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 13 :10 "near, far, between, contained" Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." - **What to check:** is the compound categorically different from more replication, rather than a linear sum? Is the residue a shape (bounded differential reproduction) rather than a name (fatty acid vesicle, coacervate, mineral pore)? Is any figure line a temporal index? There is none.
- **Refuter:** show that boundedness and replication add linearly so one lever clears both; or derive a seventh primitive called membrane; or produce a ground reading that dates encapsulation relative to the origin of heritable replication. The third closes the timing gap and converts it to `grounded`; it does not refute the structural shape.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that a lipid membrane was required from the first replicating molecule.
- Not that naked RNA cannot evolve in solution. The "no" side in the literature is real: Joyce and Szostak 2018 state that simple natural selection can occur in solution, and Slootbeek et al. 2022 state that compartmentalization is not required for Darwinian evolution in theory. Test the compound claim, not a crude "compartment always required" claim.
- Not which compartment did the work. Vesicles, coacervates and mineral pores are functional-group variants of one boundary effect, and the figures do not discriminate among them.
- Not a geological date. Ma 2017, Le Vay and Mutschler 2019 and Slootbeek et al. 2022 disagree about order and strict necessity. Their conflict is recorded; it is not pooled into a verdict.
- Not the figure called "The Membrane": Fig 4 :21 "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." That line governs individual-to-population transfer, not a lipid bilayer (retraction in `01` F1).
- Not a new primitive, not a theory of everything, and not `grounded`. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement. The three concrete instruments are the three refuters in §2 plus the timing reading in C3. A rock-record marker or a chronological experiment that fixes the order of encapsulation and heritable replication closes D4. An experiment that sustains open-ended, parasite-resistant evolution with no boundary touches C1 and C3. A derivation of containment from something other than the space term touches C3. None of these has been run here.
