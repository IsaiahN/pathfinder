# The Tether's answer: when does "emergent" explain?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four conditions, explicitly not `grounded`.** This is a test and a route, not a classification of any system. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A macro regularity P is observed at stratum L, and the model at L-1 does not derive it. The practice inserts the word "emergent" at that contact. The residual is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a micro description with a non-shrinking residual, and a macro pattern stable enough to be named (Fig 9 :54-57). It has its own loop and its own ground. The question is not "is this system emergent?" but "which of four conditions does this macro term pass, and what is owed where it fails?"

## 2. The four conditions (the shape)

1. **C1, bargain.** Stating the macro term, plus what remains unexplained after it, must cost less than what was unexplained before. Fig 5 :43: "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly." A word applied to every pattern has said nothing.
2. **C2, contact.** The term must change a prediction or point to an instrument that could be built. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A retrospective label fails.
3. **C3, bond.** The term must record the arrangement the micro ingredients omitted. Fig 12 :5: "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." An ingredient list plus a pattern name is a flat formula.
4. **C4, composition.** Once the term is settled against the ground, something must be able to compose over it at the next level. Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world". Fig 4 :30 requires the round-trip gap to be measured.

Pass all four and "emergent" is an abbreviation for a hard-won result. Fail any one and it is a rename: C1 failure means the term is not worth keeping; C2 failure means a label was applied after the fact; C3 failure means the route was never written; C4 failure leaves a settled but unusable term, since "a library that grows is not a reach that grows" (Fig 5 :72).

No "degree of emergence" is computed. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that emergence is always empty. A macro term can pass all four conditions and do real work (Fig 12 :104).
- Not that any particular system has been shown to be emergent. This folder supplies a test, not a classification.
- Not that a macro property is a new primitive. Derived quantities are "what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member" (Fig 13 :124).
- Not that agreement or prestige settles anything. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the four conditions in this simulated derivation.
- Not that a failed search proves absence. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding checks are:

1. **Prediction check.** Does asserting the macro term change any prediction that the micro description alone does not? If yes, C2 is satisfied at the ground.
2. **Shortcut check.** Is there an analytic shortcut to the macro state, or must the microdynamic be iterated? Baker 2010 and Carroll and Parola 2024 both make this the operational content of the timing claim. A shortcut removes the "unpredictable" reason for the word.
3. **Bond check.** Can the arrangement be written and tested, or is only the ingredient list available? A false macro regularity owes a refusal, not another label (Fig 5 :53).
4. **Round trip.** Carry the macro claim down to the micro frame and back and measure the gap. Fig 4 :30: "Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
5. **Standing pre-registered refuters.** The seven conditions in `01` section 4. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the test.

## 6. What is owed when a condition fails

Keep the three questions of formula step 7 distinct. **SEARCHED** first: is the term in the closure and merely unfound? **INWARD** second: extend an instrument that is already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35). **OUTWARD** third: find a frame whose closure already predicts the shape, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :11-14). Do not answer a missing instrument by minting a word. Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."

## 7. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Which particular regularity passes the test in any given field is a question for that field's ground. The framework returns the shape of the test and records the lineage. It stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish. "Indiscernibility is not identity." If no case is found in a domain, that is `unreachable` at this budget, reported as a verdict and not as silence (Fig 13 :134).

## 8. One-paragraph version for outreach

> "Emergent" explains only when the macro term pays its way. It must cost less to state than the confusion it removes; it must change a prediction or point to an instrument; it must record the bond that the micro ingredients omitted; and once settled, it must become something you can compose over at the next level. If it does those four things, the word is an abbreviation for a result. If it does not, it is a name applied afterwards to a gap, an alias for one frame with two labels, or a route nobody wrote. The Tether offers the four-condition test and its refuters, not a list of systems that pass. Which regularity passes is for a ground in a specific field to decide, and agreement that a pattern is emergent is not that ground.
