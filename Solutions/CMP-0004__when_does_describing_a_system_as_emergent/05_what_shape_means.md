## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question the shape is the **four-condition test** in `01` section 5 and `07` section 2. It constrains what any successful use of "emergent" must look like. It does not fill in which system is emergent. `SYSTEM X IS EMERGENT` is a name. `A MACRO TERM THAT PASSES C1 TO C4 EXPLAINS` is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The method is proximity: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then recurse on recurring new terms.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the four conditions are written as checks anyone can run, not as a theory of emergence to be believed.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question: calling a particular regularity emergent is a name, and a name is licensed only by a ground that pays (C2 and C4) or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The word "emergent" itself is not a name of a new primitive; it is a comment about the status of a term in Γ.

## 4. Why the rename is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Metamerism: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing a macro pattern is a reading. Naming the pattern "emergent" names a source (a new level, a new causal power, an irreducible whole) from that reading, when several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a word. This is C2 in the essay's vocabulary.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a macro property as emergent? ANSWERED

**It does not forbid the naming by itself.** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. A macro property observed in the world is already nameable; it is not a missing primitive. What the rule forbids is:

- inventing a new primitive to fill the gap (a seventh member, Fig 13 :43-44);
- claiming that the word moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom");
- treating the name as the answer rather than as a claim whose residual must be measured.

So saying "this regularity is emergent" is permitted as a **binding** with provenance owed. Saying "emergence explains it" is a verdict that must pass C1 to C4. My earlier reading, which treated any naming as forbidden, was too strong. Correction recorded here and not silently erased.

The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources remains the failure. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous people who agree that a pattern is emergent is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the four-condition test and a ground to settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of emergence and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph canonical statement

For this question, a shape is the form an answer must have before the answer can be named. The figures return a four-condition test, not a classification of systems: a macro term must pay its bargain, change contact, record the bond its ingredients omitted, and compose at the next level. Which regularity passes is a matter for a ground in a specific domain, and the names of those regularities are bindings, not deliveries from the framework. The record stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and says what would have to be imported to continue. Indiscernibility is not identity.
