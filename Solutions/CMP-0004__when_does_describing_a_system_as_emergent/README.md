## The answer in one line

"Emergent" explains only when the macro term pays the figure's bargain: it is settled against the ground, it compresses the residual, it records the bond the micro ingredients omitted, and it becomes an atom that composes at the next level. It renames when it is a label applied afterwards to a gap nobody has yet given an instrument. Fig 5 :43, Fig 11 :36, Fig 12 :104.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that emergence is always empty, and no claim that any particular system has been shown to be emergent. The verdict is a test, not a classification of a system.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade a label. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The CSV therefore contains only institutional submission portals and public office contact pages.
