## QUESTION

Option A: insert the word "emergent" and treat the naming as an explanation. Option B: treat the macro description as a candidate term and run a four-condition test. What each leads to: A adds a word to Γ without adding an atom, a bond or a prediction, and names the gap so that no instrument is built for it; B either licenses the macro term as a settled, composable molecule, or reports precisely what is owed (SEARCHED, INWARD or OUTWARD) and refuses the word as a rename. The question is a union compound, not one hard question: a non-shrinking micro residual and a stable macro pattern.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

"Emergent" explains exactly where the macro term passes C1 to C4: it pays its bargain, changes contact, records the omitted bond, and becomes composable at the next level with a measured round trip. It merely renames where it fails any of them. The word has zero verdict weight; the test has. Which regularity passes is a matter for a ground in a specific domain, and agreement that a pattern is emergent is not that ground. The framework returns the shape of the test, not a classification of systems. This is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the conditions, ungrounded and candidate until a ground pays.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending a reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a test. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the test and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The four conditions are removable or narrowable one at a time as counterexamples arrive, without withdrawing the others. No primitive has to be retracted, because the word "emergent" was never admitted as one. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A prediction check showing the macro term changes nothing the micro description does not already predict breaks C2. An analytic shortcut to the macro state removes the timing reason for the word. A complete explanation with no bond breaks C3. A derivation preserving all micro detail across a crossing breaks C4. A field-accepted explanatory case failing any condition (Batterman universality, Anderson levels, Baker or Carroll simulations) triggers the pre-registered refuter. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
