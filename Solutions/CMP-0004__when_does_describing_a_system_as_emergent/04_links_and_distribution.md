## What the wider literature adds

No world source decides the verdict. The figures are primary. The world sources are read as checks on the shape and on the choice of domain.

1. **Physics, level structure.** Anderson 1972 and Carroll and Parola 2024 both support level-relative novelty while rejecting mystery. Carroll and Parola also supply the exact distinction F6 needs: novelty as individual judgment, and "maybe it simply hasn't been derived yet." Their Type 0 to Type 2 categories give a vocabulary for a macro theory that commutes with the micro dynamics without any new ontology.
2. **Philosophy of science, the rename worry.** The SEP entry records Alexander's "It admits no explanation", a historical case of a name marking a stop. It also records Broad's structural criterion: the whole R(A,B,C) is not deducible from the parts in isolation, where R is the arrangement. That is Fig 12 :5 in another vocabulary, which is why F4 is triangulated rather than single-source.
3. **Complexity and simulation.** Baker 2010 shows that "not all derivations are also explanations" and gives the trivial derivation D* as a counterfeit. His non-vacuousness and non-triviality requirements are the world-facing twin of Fig 5 :43: a definition that makes everything emergent has said nothing.
4. **Explanation in physics.** Hooker's review of Batterman gives the positive case: asymptotic explanation works by showing which micro information can be deleted and why. An unexplicated appeal to a higher organizing principle is not that. This is the world-facing twin of C3.

## Overlap declaration

Carroll and Parola cite Bedau; Baker is entirely about Bedau; the SEP entry works through Broad, O'Connor and Wilson. These four therefore share part of one literature pool and their agreement is not four independent witnesses. Anderson (condensed matter) and Hooker (philosophy of physics) are the genuinely separate frames. The four Substack essays are by the framework's author and carry maximal overlap with the figures; they are used for the canonical reading of "shape" only, never as corroboration of the verdict. No world source conflicted with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered.
