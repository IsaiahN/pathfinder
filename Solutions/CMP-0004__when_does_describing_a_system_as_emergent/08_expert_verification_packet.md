# Expert verification packet: when does "emergent" explain?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher of science or a complexity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the four conditions, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a test and a route, not a classification of any system. **Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.**

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Emergent" explains only when the macro term pays its way. Four conditions: (C1) stating the term plus what remains unexplained costs less than the confusion it removes; (C2) the term changes a prediction or points to a buildable instrument; (C3) the term records the arrangement the micro ingredients omitted, so it names a route and not just a state; (C4) once settled against the ground, it becomes an atom over which something composes at the next level, with a measured round trip gap. A macro description passing all four makes the word an abbreviation for a result. Failing any one makes it a rename: a gap (C2), an alias (C3), or a flat ingredient list (C3). The test applies per domain and per slot; no degree of emergence is computed, and no system is classified by this packet.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C-A — The bargain and the contact test decide the rename case

- **Statement:** a macro term that explains nothing beyond its own occasion, and a label applied afterwards to a pattern that already met its ground, fail as explanations. The operational test is whether left(R, φ) falls and whether a prediction changes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :43 "A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly." Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a purely retrospective macro label with no added composition that nevertheless shrinks the residual against a ground, or a macro term that passes the bargain while adding no reach at any level.

### C-B — The bond is the difference between an alias and a new substance

- **Statement:** two descriptions over the same atoms that differ only in arrangement are different substances, and the difference is decisive in behaviour. Two descriptions that differ only in name are one frame and have no surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :5 "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 8 :13 "same atoms, different names". Fig 8 :15 "two names for one frame".
- **Refuter:** exhibit two descriptions with identical atoms and bonds whose union moves the reach, or an explanation that is complete with no bond written and no route named.

### C-C — Novelty is level-relative, and the crossing is lossy

- **Statement:** a settled macro term can become an atom for the next level, so irreducibility is a fact about a level and not a declaration about the world. The crossing must be bracketed by a round trip gap measurement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world". Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 5 :69 "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a derivation that preserves all micro detail across the crossing with no lossy transform, or a case where a settled term composes over nothing and yet the field counts the explanation as complete.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that emergence is always a rename. A term passing all four conditions does work.
- Not that any particular system, field or research programme has been shown to be emergent.
- Not that the word names a new primitive. Fig 13 :124: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."
- Not that agreement, authority or a literature consensus settles a naming claim. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."
- Not that a failed search proves absence. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not `grounded`. The derivation is simulated and checked only for internal consistency.

## 4. Pre-registered refuters

Written before this packet was compiled and carried unchanged from `01` section 4. The test is refuted or narrowed if:

1. a term passing all four conditions is still only an alias with no added reach;
2. a retrospective label with no composition shrinks the residual at the ground;
3. a complete explanation writes no bond and names no route;
4. a derivation preserves all micro detail across a level crossing;
5. a substrate member is derived from the others, or a seventh is found;
6. a field-accepted explanatory case fails one of the four conditions (check Batterman's universality case, Anderson's level case, and the Baker and Carroll simulation cases);
7. a frame proves its own absence from a closure.

## 6. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Operationally:

1. **Prediction check:** does the macro term change a prediction the micro description alone does not make?
2. **Shortcut check:** is there an analytic shortcut to the macro state, or must the microdynamic be iterated? See Baker 2010 and Carroll and Parola 2024.
3. **Bond check:** can the arrangement be written and tested, or only the ingredients listed? A false macro term owes a refusal, not another label (Fig 5 :53).
4. **Round trip:** carry the macro claim down and back and measure the gap (Fig 4 :30).

## 7. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 5 :43, Fig 11 :36 and :40, Fig 12 :5 and :104, Fig 4 :30. Six short passages.
2. Apply C1 and C2 to one macro regularity you know well. Does the word change anything measurable?
3. Apply the `≡` substitution test. If nothing is lost, the two descriptions are one frame (Operators :27).
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish a genuine new bond from an ingredient list in that case, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
