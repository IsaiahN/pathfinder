## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Performance-based pay has three conditions, not one verdict. First, a metric stays a reading of the organization only while employees cannot move it; once the number determines pay it stops being an anchor and becomes another frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces. Second, a metric is cheap to state and expensive to verify against the real outcome, and those two prices are in different units and do not add, so a scheme that funds only the description drains effort toward the measured atom while the unmeasured task degrades. Third, the channel to the long-run outcome must stay reachable; short-termism is saturated metrics and pooled readings while the ground keeps paying elsewhere, and internal competition is the same ranking room handed around until collaborators become rivals. These are three different failure modes with three different instruments, not degrees of one problem. The claim is a shape any candidate answer must have. It does not name which pay scheme wins.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 1 and 10

- **Statement:** a convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention; the long-run outcome is the anchor, not the metric; the metric cannot be scored by the frame that produces it; short-termism is channel decay.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :43 - *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Also Fig 10 :47 - *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
  - Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :30 - *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."*
  - Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
- **Refuter:** a pay metric demonstrated to remain an unpushable reading of the ground, with employees unable to move the reading without moving the outcome; or short-term behaviour appearing while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one business-facing check

- **Statement:** the two currencies of description and verification do not add; a closed set is idempotent, so optimizing inside the measured task cannot add to the unmeasured one; effort direction therefore needs a second frame and a budgeted verification action.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
  - Fig 12 :126 - *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*
  - Fig 12 :127 - *"They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them"*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** the Surrogation page reports that the extra tendency to surrogate under incentives *"is reduced when managers are compensated based on multiple measures of a strategy rather than on a single measure"* (Choi, Hecht and Tayler 2012 as reported there). Does that remedy price the verification actions, or does it only add more cheap descriptions? A showing that it prices both is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** effort allocations under a single measurable proxy shown to be identical to allocations under a full multi-task ground measurement, with no drift to the measured atom; or a demonstration that a firm renewed effort direction purely by internal composition with no new atom from a differing closure.

### C3 - Structure and competing explanations

- **Statement:** the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; two schemes with identical atoms but different bonds are isomers; the same bonus can be a catalyst or a spent part depending on the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 11 :49 - *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
  - Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores all three conditions in all failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or two schemes with identical atoms and identical bonds whose long-run results diverge with no difference in habitat or ground contact.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-team study that measures C1, C2 and C3 separately, records bonds as well as ingredients, and reports which condition failed before the decline. Concretely:

1. **Push test:** can employees move the metric without moving the long-run outcome? If yes, C1 is refuted for that scheme.
2. **Two-currency test:** are the actions required to verify the metric against the ground budgeted, and are they in a different accounting from the description cost? If not, C2 predicts drift toward the measured atom.
3. **Fidelity and pressure test:** are bracket fidelity between metric and outcome, and the ground's pressure on the team, both nonzero at each review handoff? Fig 7 :37-39 says zero either stops the chain.
4. **Repeated-room test:** across two review cycles, is the ranking closure different and are new ground-verified readings arriving without information withholding? If not, C3 predicts internal competition.
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish genuine improvement from a gamed measure from a paying habitat from luck when all produce the same score reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.
