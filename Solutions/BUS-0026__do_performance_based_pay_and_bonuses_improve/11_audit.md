- **Arity:** a relation among belief b, action a, metric reading m and outcome o', indexed by slot and ordered by time. There are at least four operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (pay, performance) loses the metric and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The outcome is an anchor and does not update on the employee's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Superior" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), and a forced ranking is a comparison among employees, which is a different bond from a threshold against a standard.
- **Scale:** the slot is an employee or team, but "superior" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the employee's step loop and the organization's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's three-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a pay scheme works while its metric stays honest and its channel to the long run stays open.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific team situation. Result: *use a metric people cannot game and keep listening to customers.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a team failing C2 merely to pick a harder-to-game metric, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Honest metric" does not specify that the outcome, not the score, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :30). Coming back down, it permits a team to grade itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the two currencies. "Cannot game" is a property of the description, with no place for the actions required to verify it against the ground (Fig 12 :125-127).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep listening to customers" has no place for a colleague turning into a rival when the comparison bond changes (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 7 :36).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish genuine improvement from a gamed measure, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
