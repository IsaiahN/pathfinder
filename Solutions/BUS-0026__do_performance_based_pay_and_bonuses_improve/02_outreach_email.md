# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no organizational-ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about performance pay before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about incentives that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** tying pay to a measured result does not have one effect. It is three conditions, and which one fails decides what you see.
>
> 1. **Metric fidelity.** A measure is a convention until it decides an outcome. Once employees can move the number that sets their pay, that number stops being a reading of the ground and becomes another frame trying to grade itself. The cure is not a more precise metric; it is a second reading from a frame that does not draw on the same evidence.
> 2. **Effort direction.** A metric is cheap to state and expensive to verify against the real outcome. Those are two different currencies and they do not add. If only the description is priced, effort drains toward the measured atom and the unmeasured task quietly degrades. Multiple measures help, but only if the verification actions are actually budgeted.
> 3. **Horizon contact.** Short-termism is a failure of the channel, not a failure of the market. Metrics saturate and distinct signals get pooled into an average while the ground keeps paying someone else. Internal competition is the same mechanism at the team scale: the same ranking room handed around, with collaborators turning into rivals when the residual changes.
>
> So the answer to "do bonuses improve results or cause gaming" is: both, in different slots, depending on which of the three conditions holds. A blended score hides the diagnostic.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which scheme wins in your setting, that incentives are always corrosive, that intrinsic motivation is a primitive I have identified, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of incentives. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a pay metric that is shown to remain an unpushable reading of the ground, with employees unable to move the number without moving the outcome;
> 2. effort allocations under a single proxy shown to be identical to allocations under a full multi-task measurement, with no drift to the measured atom;
> 3. short-term behaviour appearing while both reading fidelity and the ground's pressure on the team measure high and non-declining;
> 4. a team keeping the same ranking closure across two review cycles while continuing to produce new, ground-verified readings with no withholding of information;
> 5. a pay change improving long-run results while every habitat condition and channel to the ground is held constant.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape, and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Three conditions kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Push test before precision | *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 2 :43) |
| Two currencies named | *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* (Fig 12 :126) |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map line 287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the winning scheme | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
