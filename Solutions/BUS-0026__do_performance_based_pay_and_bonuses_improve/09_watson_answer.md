# WATSON answer, BUS-0026

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Do performance-based pay and bonuses improve results, or encourage gaming, short-termism, and internal competition?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| sometimes narrow and sometimes widen when a measured result is attached to pay? R is indexed per employee, per team and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). Anchor: the long-run organizational outcome, named in effect terms as the thing that settles survival and customer value, not the metric, which moves when pushed (Fig 2 :29, :30).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among employee belief b, action a, metric reading m and outcome o', indexed by slot and ordered by time; carries a sequence bond, a production bond and a comparison bond for "superior" and for ranking. It is not symmetric. Symmetry: "superior" is a threshold relative to peers (Fig 12 :87); a relative scheme changes the bond, hence the term (Fig 12 :92). Scale: single employee loop, population ranking, organization outcome and the time channel across quarters; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).

- **Social science methodology.** Wikipedia contributors, *Campbell's law*, Wikipedia, 2026, opened 2026-11-22. Quote: *"The more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it will be to corruption pressures and the more apt it will be to distort and corrupt the social processes it is intended to monitor."* Prediction: corruption pressure scales with decision weight. **Corroborates** F1. Overlap: different field, partly shared mechanism shape with Goodhart.
- **Management accounting / cognition.** Wikipedia contributors, *Surrogation*, Wikipedia, 2026, opened 2026-11-22, reporting Choi, Hecht and Tayler 2012. Quote: managers act *"as though the measures are the constructs"*; *"This appears to occur even if a measure-maximizing choice ultimately works against the strategy"*; the effect *"is reduced when managers are compensated based on multiple measures of a strategy rather than on a single measure."* Prediction: multi-measure pay reduces but does not remove surrogate behaviour. **Corroborates** F3. Overlap: same pool.
- **Labour economics.** Wikipedia contributors, *Piece work*, Wikipedia, 2026, opened 2026-11-22. Quote: *"Incentivizes productivity: piece rate pay encourages workers to increase their output as they directly benefit from producing more"*; *"Quality concerns: workers may sacrifice quality for quantity to maximize their earnings."* Prediction: measured output rises and unmeasured quality falls. **Corroborates** F3. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** performance-based pay narrows R where the metric remains a convention ranged against an independent ground, where the two currencies of description and verification are both budgeted, and where the channel to the long-run outcome stays reachable. It widens R into gaming, short-termism and internal competition where the metric becomes its own ground, where the measured atom drains effort from the unmeasured task, or where repeated review cycles hand the team the same room. The three conditions are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The management evidence pool corroborates both halves but is one witness; the low-overlap fields corroborate mechanism shape, not an organizational ground fact. Which scheme wins is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A team failing C2 would be sold a better dashboard, or a team failing C3 would be told to add more measures, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs three. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a pay metric shown to be unpushable against the ground, (2) identical effort allocations under a single proxy and a full multi-task measurement, (3) short-term behaviour with both bracket fidelity and ground pressure high, (4) two review cycles of same closure with new ground-verified readings and no withholding, or (5) a pay change improving long-run results with every habitat condition held constant. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing per-slot results fully predicted by a single average incentive score would also reverse it.
