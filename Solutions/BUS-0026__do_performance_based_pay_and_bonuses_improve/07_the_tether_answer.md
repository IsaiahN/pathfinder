# The Tether's answer on performance-based pay and bonuses

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No organizational measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a pay scheme predicts will be rewarded and what the organization actually gets sometimes narrows and sometimes widens into gaming, short-termism, and internal competition. R is indexed per employee, per team and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **the behaviour of a measured reward under contact**, not about the size of the bonus.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is three conditions, not one hard question with a yes/no answer:

- **C1:** the metric still ranges against an independent ground after it decides pay.
- **C2:** the two currencies, description and verification, are both budgeted so effort does not drain to the measured atom.
- **C3:** the channel to the long-run outcome stays reachable across review cycles.

## 2. The shape

1. **A meter that decides pay stops being a meter.** Fig 2 :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* The anchor is the long-run outcome; the metric is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. Precision does not fix this: it increases leverage on the same pushable reading.
2. **The scoring frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* An employee whose effort produces the reading and whose pay is computed from that reading is graded by the frame's own output. The cure is a second reading from a frame that does not draw on the same evidence (Fig 8 :50).
3. **Bits and actions do not add.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A metric is cheap to state and expensive to settle against the ground. If only the description is priced, effort flows to the measured atom and the unmeasured task degrades. Inside a closed set, idempotence applies: *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* (Fig 8 :32).
4. **Short-termism is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :28: *"you have lost touch"*. The market keeps paying someone; the team stops hearing it. Fig 7 :37-39 gives the two-factor form: bracket fidelity times ground pressure, and zero either stops the chain.
5. **Internal competition is a repeated room with a flipping sign.** Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A colleague becomes a rival when the comparison bond changes, without anything about the colleague changing.
6. **Results are agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A bonus gain cannot be credited to the employee alone; the metric, peers, rater, market and horizon all changed contact.
7. **The metric is a dictionary, not a grammar.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* A KPI names a state and a comparison. It cannot name the route to the outcome, which is why more precise goals do not repair the missing grammar.
8. **Two schemes with the same ingredients can still differ.** Under Fig 12 :117 they are isomers if the bonds differ: *"same atoms, different bond, different substance."* A compensation ingredient list cannot predict results on its own.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective organizational measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a single verdict. It does not say "bonuses work" or "bonuses corrupt." It says which condition fails decides which happens, per slot.
- Not a name. It does not say which pay scheme wins. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of performance attributable to incentives, culture, or industry. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that motivation, talent, or leadership are irrelevant. They are Γ. The claim is that Γ does not settle results on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The accessible management pages share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap fields from economics of regulation, social science methodology, AI engineering and animal behaviour corroborate the mechanism shape, not an organizational ground fact.
- Not a theory of everything about organizations. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are metric scores, engagement surveys and quarterly returns; the candidate sources are a genuine improvement, a gamed measure, a paying habitat, or luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. a pay metric shown to remain an unpushable reading of the ground;
2. effort allocations under a single proxy identical to a full multi-task measurement, with no drift;
3. short-term behaviour appearing while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high and non-declining;
4. the same ranking closure held across two review cycles with new ground-verified readings and no information withholding;
5. a pay change improving long-run results while every habitat condition and channel to the ground is held constant;
6. a metric system that encodes routes rather than states still failing to improve outcomes.

A prospective, per-team study that measures C1, C2 and C3 separately, records the bonds as well as the ingredients, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`. The AOM Registered Reports call described in `04` is the natural venue shape for such a pre-registered design, subject to the AI-policy caveat there.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The three conditions in section 2 are a shape; the fourth thing, which scheme actually pays off in a given firm, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank pay schemes or name the bonus that wins. It says that performance pay has three conditions, not one verdict. A metric stays useful only while it still ranges against an outcome employees cannot move; once the number sets pay, it stops being a meter and becomes another frame grading itself. A metric is cheap to state and expensive to verify against the real result, and those two prices do not add, so if only the description is funded, effort drains to the measured task and the unmeasured one quietly degrades. Short-termism is the channel going stale, saturated and pooled into an average while the market keeps paying someone else. Internal competition is the same ranking room handed around, with collaborators turning into rivals when the comparison changes. Results belong to the employee-and-habitat, not the employee alone. What is offered is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which scheme occupies the slots stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
