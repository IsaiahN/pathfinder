# Expert verification packet: the Tether on recession prevention versus softening

**What this is:** a claim in a form a macroeconomic policy researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the instrument and the magnitude. Not `grounded`.** No national counterfactual is claimed to have been measured. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question as posed is a false partition. The recession residual is a union of overlapping conditions (demand, supply, financial, slow adjustment, tipping points) indexed per slot, and "prevent" and "soften" invoke different operators: `¬` refuses a claim, while `⋛` and `−` compare quantities and subtract progress. A particular held fragility can be refused and that condition's reach falls, but universal prevention is a final room, which the figures forbid. What policy can do in general is maintain contact with a non-updating, per-slot ground and close part of the gap each round, which is softening in the strong sense of real per-handoff progress with no arrival. No instrument, multiplier or amplitude follows, so that is recorded as a `gap` and as `unreachable`, a verdict rather than silence. Verdicts are per condition and per slot, never averaged into one national yes or no.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - union, not partition; checkable by reading Fig 9 and the source question list

- **Statement:** the condition classes overlap, so one lever clears only the single-condition region and leaves the intersections standing. A single national prevention verdict aggregates away the per-slot signal.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :56 "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **What to check:** does the standard taxonomy in your field treat these as disjoint explanations, or as overlapping conditions? Does a single policy instrument close all intersections?
- **Refuter:** one instrument demonstrably closes `left(R,φ) = 0` for all named classes and their intersections, at the ground. Then the union finding fails.

### C2 - refusal versus comparison; checkable by reading the Operators table and Fig 12

- **Statement:** "prevent" is the unary operator `¬`, which refuses a held term and adds no atom; "soften" is the comparison `⋛` of two quantities plus the subtraction `−` of progress. They are not two values of one dial.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Fig 6 :42 "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
- **What to check:** is the prevention claim in your literature a true claim of non-occurrence (a refusal) or a claim of reduced frequency and severity (a comparison)? Eichengreen 2023 says economists made headway "if not necessarily in preventing" crises; BIS 2011 says crises "will not disappear, but their frequency and severity can be reduced". Those are comparison claims, not refusals.
- **Refuter:** show `¬` composes with another operand under one of the seven bond tests, or that a refusal adds an atom to `atoms(Γ)`. Either breaks the operator claim.

### C3 - the anchor and the final room; checkable by reading Fig 7, Fig 2 and Fig 10

- **Statement:** universal prevention requires a terminal handoff after which no further question about the residual can arise, and the figures forbid that room. The legitimate anchor is realized activity and employment, not the target series; the ground does not decay while the channel does.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room". Fig 7 :30 "every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives". Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one".
- **What to check:** does the policy target move when the authority announces it? If so, it cannot anchor the verdict. Does any policy framework in your field claim a terminal state of the business cycle?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a finite policy sequence after which the residual never rearrives and the maintaining work ends; or show the target series settles the question while moving with announcements. Either reverses C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "recessions cannot be prevented" in an empirical or predictive sense. Particular fragilities can be refused; the universal claim has the wrong shape.
- Not "policy is useless". World sources document reductions in frequency and severity; the figures license per-handoff progress.
- Not a named instrument, multiplier, target rate or numerical amplitude. No figure line returns one; that is a `gap`.
- Not that the national aggregate is the right unit of verdict (Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average.").
- Not `grounded`, not a self-validating theory, and not a claim that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

Not agreement. The ground, through instruments that discriminate sources:

1. A per-slot, source-discriminating reading that separates demand, supply, financial and tipping-point conditions instead of averaging them.
2. A counterfactual with an anchor that does not update on announcements, so amplitude reduction can be measured.
3. A bracket-fidelity measure across a policy handoff, so `R = h² × S` can be evaluated.
4. A demonstration that maintaining work ends after a finite sequence, which would reverse C3.
