## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, between Option A: "recessions can be prevented", meaning a policy term `φ` exists with `left(R,φ) = 0` so the event does not occur; and Option B: "policy can only soften them", meaning `0 < left(R,φ) < |R|` so the event occurs with reduced amplitude or duration. What each leads to: A commits to a final room in which no further question about the residual can arise, and to a refusal operator applied to an unheld universal term; B commits to maintained contact, per-handoff progress and no arrival, but supplies neither an instrument nor a magnitude. A third possibility, developed in the census, is that the two options are different operators on different quantities and cannot be averaged into one national verdict.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A recession event indexed per slot (sector, household, balance sheet, region). Arity: at least four overlapping condition classes named in the source file itself (demand, supply, financial and slow adjustment, plus tipping points). Symmetry: asymmetric, because "prevent" is unary `¬` while "soften" is the ordered bonds `⋛` and `−`. Scale: first order inside a condition, second order at their union, third order at the policy seat. Fig 9 makes it a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the economy as habitat.** Actors: households, firms, banks, credit chains. Currency: spending, credit, confidence. Anchor: realized activity and employment, which do not change when a forecast is announced. Question type: union of overlapping conditions, enumerated, never composed.
- **Domain 2, the policy seat.** Actors: monetary authority, fiscal authority, regulator. Currency: rates, liquidity, spending, guarantees, capital rules. Anchor: the same ground, reached through a maintained channel. Question type: maintained seat inside the habitat; introduction is available, exclusion is not.
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: statistical agencies, dating committees, modelers. Currency: revisions, identification, counterfactual estimates. Anchor: realized outcomes, not the target series. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so none is an independent witness.
- **Domain 4, the instrument and the magnitude.** Actors: none holdable. Currency: none. Anchor: none nameable by a figure line. Reported as `gap`, not as a silent omission.
- **Order by dependency.** Split the union and describe the residual per slot before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Settle what counts as an event at the ground before asking whether policy changed it (Fig 3 chain). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Choose instruments last. Effort above the break cannot rescue a missing objective link.

Strain test applied: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a missing primitive, not the naming of a binding onto an atom already held; re-census resolves the apparent conflict with the policy-frame bindings. Two live strains remain and are named in the VERDICT, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's either/or is refused. The residual is a union of overlapping conditions indexed per slot, so there is no single national prevention verdict: verdicts are per condition and per slot and are never averaged. Universal prevention is structurally the final room (Fig 7 :6, :30), so it is refused; a particular held fragility can be refused (Operators :58, Fig 6 :42), so no claim of empirical inevitability is made. What policy can do in general is maintain contact with a per-slot anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :61, :63) and close part of the gap each round (Fig 7 :30), which is softening in the strong sense. No figure line names an instrument or a magnitude; D4 is `gap` and `unreachable`, a verdict and not silence. Every claim is `specified` or `general`, none `grounded`, and this whole run is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a framework instead of a characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is policy-relevant: treating a target series as an anchor can make a convention decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47), so a wrong verdict here is not merely academic.
- **undoable:** yes. The false partition is removable without touching the figures; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected framing, and no term has to be withdrawn from the framework. By contrast, a policy built on a wrong identification is harder to undo, which is why the independence buy-in is worth its cost.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates the overlapping conditions per slot, a counterfactual with an anchor that does not move on announcements, or a bracket-fidelity measure across a policy handoff would close the gap. A finite policy sequence after which the residual never rearrives and the work ends would reverse the final-room finding; a target series shown to settle the question while moving with announcements would reverse D3; showing `¬` to be a bond would reverse the operator claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, with two live strains named: the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim, and the absence of an instrument for handoff fidelity.
