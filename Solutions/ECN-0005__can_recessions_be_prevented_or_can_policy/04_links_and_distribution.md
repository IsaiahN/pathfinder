## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The prevention claim has a date and a counterexample.** Lucas 2003 said depression prevention was solved for practical purposes; Eichengreen 2023 reports that 2008-10 and the European debt crisis exposed that conclusion as premature. This is a world-level conflict with a claim, not with the figure verdict. It is returned to the domain census in `09_watson_answer.md` and does not flip the verdict.
2. **The mitigation language is the policy literature's own language.** Bernanke's "less frequent and milder", BIS's "frequency and severity can be reduced", and Eichengreen's "understanding... if not necessarily in preventing" all describe amplitude reduction without arrival. That is the same shape Fig 7 :30 gives structurally, reached from a different evidence pool. It is corroboration, not authority.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them anything else.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong answer about recession policy, so independence is worth buying with genuinely different instruments and fields.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) only then the three local links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the framework that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems, each with its own refuter and mode.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are the test; a failure is more useful than an endorsement.
