## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union), Fig 7 :6/:30 (no last room), Fig 12 :112 (operator arity), Fig 2 :29 (anchor test), Fig 11 :61/:63 (seat and permanent work).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the question is a false partition; per-slot union verdicts; refusal for a named fragility, universal prevention refused; softening is maintained contact with no arrival; the anchor is realized outcomes; the instrument is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a policy prescription in ordinary macro language, for example "use a countercyclical capital buffer plus monetary easing to prevent recessions."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** is flattened into one policy lever, so the intersections are cleared by assertion rather than by a second loop (Fig 9 :56);
- the **operator distinction** between `¬` and `⋛`/`−` is lost, so a reduced-amplitude result is reported as prevention (Fig 12 :112);
- the **non-updating anchor** is replaced by the target series, so the verdict is read from a convention that has decided the outcome (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :47);
- the **permanent-work condition** is dropped, so the policy is treated as a solved state (Fig 11 :63).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the need to split the bundle and report per slot, and the prohibition on a final room. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because there is no ground reading in this simulation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
