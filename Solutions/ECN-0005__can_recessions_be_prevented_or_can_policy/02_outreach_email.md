# Outreach draft - check the shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 is written into the message below, unchanged in substance. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one structural claim about recession policy before you decide whether the framing is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I'd like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as posed is a false partition (`specified`, not `grounded`).** "Can recessions be prevented, or can policy only soften them?" treats two different operators as two answers to one question. Prevention is a refusal of a claim: the event does not occur. Softening is a comparison of two quantities (output with and without the policy) and a subtraction of progress. The figures I am working from separate these: Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate".
>
> **The output, stated in effect terms.** The residual is indexed per slot, not per headline aggregate. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The candidate conditions (demand shortfall, supply disruption, financial shock, slow adjustment, tipping points) overlap, so the question is a union, not a list. Fig 9 :56 "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." A single national yes or no therefore throws away the signal.
>
> **What follows structurally.** Universal "prevention" is a terminal handoff after which no further question about the residual can arise. The framework forbids that room: Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"; Fig 7 :30 "every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives". What policy can do in general is maintain contact with an anchor that does not move when policy is announced (Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."), and keep doing so, because Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." A *specific* held fragility can still be refused, so I am not claiming that no particular event was ever averted. I am claiming the universal version has the wrong shape.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that a named instrument, multiplier, or percentage follows. No figure line returns one, so that is recorded as a `gap` and as `unreachable`, which in this framework is a verdict rather than silence: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." I am also not claiming that policy is useless, that recessions are inevitable in an empirical sense, or that any country's actual policy record is settled by this.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. One instrument closes the residual to zero for all named condition classes and their intersections, measured at the ground.
> 2. A finite policy sequence is exhibited after which the residual never rearrives and the maintaining work ends.
> 3. The target series is shown to settle the question while moving systematically with policy announcements.
> 4. An aggregate success is shown to leave zero positive residual on every slot-resolving instrument.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

## Two-stage discipline

**Stage 1:** the recipient checks the shape claim and its refuter against their own knowledge, with no framework interior in view. Record their verdict before showing anything else.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

Their stage-1 verdict is never pooled as corroboration. If they later agree after reading the framework, that agreement is one more frame in the same evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). If they return a refutation, it is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.
