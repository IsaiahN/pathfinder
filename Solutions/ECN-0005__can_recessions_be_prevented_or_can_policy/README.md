## What this package decides, in effect terms

The question offers two options, but they are not two values of one variable. "Prevent" commits to a policy term `φ` that closes the recession residual at the ground (`left(R,φ) = 0`, no event occurs). "Soften" commits to reducing the amplitude or duration of an event that still occurs (`0 < left(R,φ) < |R|`). The first is a **refusal** (the operator `¬`, "this does not hold"), the second is a **comparison** and a **subtraction** (`⋛`, `−`). They use different operators on different quantities, so averaging them is a category error.

The figures return three things, kept separate: (1) the presented either/or is a **union** of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not a partition, so one lever clears only the single-condition region and leaves the intersections standing; (2) universal "prevention" is structurally the **final room**, which the figures forbid, while a **particular** held fragility can be refused and that condition's reach can fall; (3) what policy can do in general is maintain contact with a non-updating ground and close some of the gap each round, which is softening in the strong sense of "motion toward the ground that never arrives." No figure line names an instrument, a multiplier, or a numerical amplitude. That is a declared `gap`, not silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness score.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make its agreement worthless, and sending the full architecture before a verdict is recorded spends the independence of the people whose check is wanted.

**Legally:** no scraped contact lists, no purchased lists. Public institutional submission and contact channels only, with the verification status of each recorded in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
