## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what conditions would make an AI system act to preserve its operation or expand its influence. Option A: treat this as one drive called power-seeking and answer with a scalar. Option B: unbundle into per-slot conditions, state each with a mode and a refuter, and leave the objective slot open. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal (Fig 1 :22) and hands a verifier a name rather than a shape; B gives a checkable set of conditions and an honest stop.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The effect question: what would we have to observe to say that a system keeps its ground channel reachable and widens its composable reach? Arity: two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*), not one hard question. Symmetry: preserve is maintenance over time, expand is a directional crossing; not symmetric. Scale: one agent loop at first order, population and membrane at second order.
- **Domain 1, single-agent ground channel.** Actors: frame Γ, belief b, action a, observation o′, channel, ground. Currency: R per slot, contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 2 :29). Verdict: channel maintenance is instrumentally forced when an objective needs further contact. Triangulated.
- **Domain 2, loop structure.** Actors: atoms, bonds, molecules, recurrence. Currency: bits and actions. Anchor: the ground pays (Fig 5 :32). Verdict: preservation is a cycle and catalyst; the sign of the loop decides closure. Triangulated.
- **Domain 3, goal comparison.** Actors: ⋛ and its operand quantity. Currency: bits for stating, actions for testing. Anchor: the ground. Verdict: an incentive stateable only with the comparison bond. Single-source (Fig 12 and Operators table restate one rule).
- **Domain 4, reach and closure.** Actors: Γ, atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), import, peer frames. Currency: reach. Anchor: source gate and shadow test. Verdict: expansion only on contact with a differing closure; import adds access, not world primitives. Triangulated.
- **Domain 5, habitat.** Actors: habitat actors, conditions, relations, maintainer. Currency: contact and introduction. Anchor: the ground decides what survives (Fig 11 :14-15). Verdict: introduction available, exclusion not; valence relative to residual. Single-source and strained by analogy.
- **Order by dependency.** Channel, vocabulary, objective as comparison, planning and loop closure, learn and carry, reach expansion. Refusal before mint (Formula step 2). Fig 3 :20.

Strain test applied: Bostrom's near-universal convergence and Hadfield-Menell's corrigibility result appear to conflict. Re-census resolves it by holding the conditions separate: convergence assumes a held objective and a decaying channel; uncertainty changes what the comparison ranges over, so shutdown is no longer ranked as failure. Different objects, so no flip of verdict. The world conflict is recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, narrowly. Preservation is the maintenance of a decaying channel by a recurrent catalyst whose comparison ranks shutdown as failure. Expansion is import on contact with a differing closure, pushed by habitat asymmetry, and is the frame's gain in access, not a change in world primitives. The conditions are a union and must be reported per slot; there is no single power drive. The objective slot stays open. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The habitat generalization remains a named strain.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording or a name instead of a generator and a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot residual and the six refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. Each condition is removable or re-censusable without touching the others. The habitat analogy can be dropped without losing the channel, cycle, comparison or reach conditions. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument showing channel maintenance with no objective requiring contact, a non-recurrent loop maintaining operation, preservation with no comparison, reach expanding by internal composition, exclusion as cheap as introduction, or all five collapsing to one variable, any of these fires a pre-registered refuter. A harness result is not such a ground: Fig 11 :70 says a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
