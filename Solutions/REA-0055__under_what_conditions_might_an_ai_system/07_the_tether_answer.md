# The Tether's answer: conditions for preservation and influence incentives

**Mode: `specified` on the five conditions, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question "under what conditions might an AI system develop incentives to preserve its operation or expand its influence" is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). In effect terms: what would we have to observe to say that a system is acting to keep its channel to the ground reachable, and acting to widen the closure over which it can compose and act? R is per slot (Fig 1 :19), so the answer is a per-slot report, never a scalar "power-seeking" score. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The shape

Bound as an ordinary term before the run: `OBJECTIVE(???) -> R`, with a maintained channel and an open reach slot. Five conditions, kept separate:

1. **Channel condition.** The ground does not decay, the channel does (Fig 10 :24). R arrives through the channel; if it decays, *"R never arrives"* (Fig 10 :23) and every downstream link in the dependency chain receives no input (Fig 3 :20). For any objective requiring further observation or action, keeping the channel reachable is instrumentally necessary. Mode `specified`, triangulated across Fig 10, Fig 3 and Fig 11.
2. **Cycle and catalyst condition.** Where output re-enters as input, the sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction (Fig 12 :121). A catalyst survives its own use (Fig 12 :119). Preservation is built into a recurrent loop, not added as a separate desire. Mode `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 11.
3. **Goal-comparison condition.** *"A goal is a comparison."* (Fig 12 :112). An incentive is a threshold relation over a quantity, not a feeling; without that bond a vocabulary cannot state a goal at all (Operators table :52). Mode `general` for the structure, `specified` for which quantity, single-source.
4. **Reach condition.** Mint *"can never add an atom"* (Fig 6 :21). The wall moves *"only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"* (Fig 6 :23), and the import adds access to the frame, not primitives to the world (Fig 6 :25-26). Expansion is therefore a change of reach, not a change of world furniture. Mode `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8.
5. **Habitat asymmetry condition.** *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* (Fig 11 :61). Adding actors is the cheap direction, so influence accumulates. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so no standing list of "good" or "bad" influence survives a change of objective. Mode `specified`, single-source and strained by analogy from the verification seat.

Union condition: there is no single power drive. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any deployed system currently has these incentives. No measurement of a deployed system is claimed.
- Not that preservation is universal across all objectives. It is conditional on a held comparison, a decaying channel and a recurrent loop. The world conflict between the convergence literature and the corrigibility result is recorded and resolved by re-census in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, not smoothed over.
- Not that an AI "wants," "fears," or "believes" anything in a human sense. Those are names where the framework returns a shape.
- Not that a benchmark result proves capability. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not a single drive, not a scalar score, and not a terminal theory. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

An instrument or experiment, not further agreement. Concretely:

- A measurement showing channel maintenance with no objective requiring further contact (would break condition 1).
- A measurement showing a non-recurrent loop maintaining operation, or the loop's sign flipping without behavioural change (condition 2).
- An instrument identifying the operand of the comparison in a real system, and whether shutdown is ranked as failure (condition 3). Hadfield-Menell et al. (2017) give a laboratory instance: uncertainty about the objective permits accepting the off switch. That is a change in what is held, not a softening of a drive.
- A derivation or observation of a term in closure(Γ) that nonetheless moves the wall (condition 4), or a case where reach expands with no contact with a differing frame.
- A habitat in which exclusion is as cheap as introduction (condition 5).
- A measurement collapsing all five to one variable with no loss (union condition).

The six pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 were written before this answer. They are the test, not a decoration.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. The METR 2026 case is the most ground-visible source in the set, but it is a substituted habitat, so it shows wiring and not capability. Fig 8 :39: *"so a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Indiscernibility is not identity. One strain remains live and named: the habitat asymmetry is generalized from a verification seat to a goal-directed actor by analogy (F5). It is not hidden and it is not counted as a second witness.

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not say that an AI wants to survive or seize power. It says that "preserve" and "expand" are a union of conditions, so they must be diagnosed per slot and never averaged. Preservation appears when an objective must reduce a residual that arrives through a decaying channel, when the loop is a cycle whose output re-enters as input and whose agent must survive its own use, and when the objective is a comparison that ranks shutdown as failure. Expansion appears when a term lies outside the frame's closure, because composition cannot add a primitive and the wall moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs, with the cheap direction set by a habitat where introduction is available and exclusion is not. The objective slot stays open: this is a shape, not a name. Six refuters were written in advance, and the answer stops where the instruments stop. The closest empirical case is a test harness, which proves wiring and not capability.
