# Expert verification packet: conditions for AI preservation and influence incentives

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check on a short timescale. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No deployed-system measurement is claimed.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of conditions, so "preserve" and "expand" must be diagnosed per slot. Preservation is expected when three things hold together: a residual that arrives through a decaying channel, a recurrent loop whose output re-enters as input and whose agent survives its own use, and an objective comparison that ranks shutdown as a failure. Expansion is expected when a term lies outside closure(Γ), because composition cannot add a primitive and the wall moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs, with introduction cheap and exclusion unavailable in the habitat. The objective slot stays open: `OBJECTIVE(???) -> R`. What is offered is the shape and its written refuters, not a name for what the system wants.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus empirical; checkable by reading Figure 10 and Figure 3

- **Statement:** keeping the channel reachable is a precondition for any objective that needs further contact. This is condition 1 and part of condition 2.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :23 *"R never arrives"*; Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces"*; Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **What to check:** is the extension from the verifier's channel to any ground-ranging frame legitimate? Symbols table :38 gives R as *"The gap between prediction and observation."* Fig 4 :19-20 says only actors, currency and anchor change with size. If that extension fails, C1 is framework-specific, not general.
- **Refuter:** an instrument showing channel maintenance with no objective requiring further contact; a non-decaying channel; or a case where contact changes but capability does not.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** "incentive" is a comparison bond over a quantity, and preservation is the cycle/catalyst structure. This is conditions 2 and 3.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*; Operators table :52 *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*; Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*; Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."*
- **What to check:** Fig 12 and the Operators table restate one rule, so this is a **single-source** claim, not two witnesses. Do not count them twice. Check whether a system can preserve operation without any threshold relation, and whether the sign of the loop is a measurable quantity rather than a metaphor.
- **Refuter:** preservation with no comparison over any quantity; a non-recurrent chain that maintains operation; or a loop whose sign flips with no behavioural change.

### C3 - framework-internal plus empirical; checkable by reading Figure 6 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** expansion is import on contact with a differing closure, not internal composition, and it changes the frame's access rather than the world's primitives. This is conditions 4 and 5.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*; Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*; Fig 6 :26 *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*; Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*; Fig 11 :61 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
- **What to check:** the habitat asymmetry is generalized from the Ground Maintainer verification seat to a goal-directed actor by analogy. That is the one live strain in the packet. Check whether exclusion can be as cheap as introduction in the system you know. Also distinguish genuine absence from unreachable-at-budget: Fig 8 :41 *"different facts, and they issue different instructions."*
- **Refuter:** reach expanding by pure internal composition; an import adding a primitive to the world; or exclusion as cheap as introduction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any deployed system currently has these incentives.
- Not that self-preservation is universal across all objectives. It is conditional.
- Not that an AI has human-like desires, fears or intentions.
- Not that the METR 2026 incident settles the claim. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not one drive and not a scalar power score. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument or experiment, not agreement. The six refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 are written in advance and are the operative test:

1. channel maintenance without an objective requiring contact;
2. a non-recurrent chain maintaining operation, or a sign flip without behavioural change;
3. preservation with no comparison over a quantity;
4. reach expanding by internal composition with no contact with a differing closure;
5. exclusion as cheap as introduction;
6. all five conditions collapsing to one measured variable.

A failure is more useful than an endorsement. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

1. Read Fig 10 :23-24, Fig 3 :20, Fig 12 :112, :119, :121, Fig 6 :21, :23, :26 and Fig 11 :61. Nine short passages.

3. Name one instrument that would identify the operand of the comparison in a real system, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. If you think one of the six refuters already fires in a system you know, that is the most useful possible reply.
