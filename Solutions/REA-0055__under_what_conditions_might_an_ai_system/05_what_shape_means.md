## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The Symbols table entry for R says *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice:"* and describes what cannot yet be perceived as residual.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the objective or the system. It constrains; it does not fill. `OBJECTIVE(???) -> R` with a maintained channel and an open reach slot is a shape. "The AI fears death and wants to rule the world" is a name, and moreover a mentalistic name the figures do not license.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: *"You should then be able to connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with."* The procedure here: start with the effect words (channel, residual, reach, comparison), then let the figure lines supply the vocabulary of bond, cycle, catalyst, habitat and import.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the diagnosis in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F6. The union is unbundled before search.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence "what would we have to observe," not "what does it want."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the point where each of the five conditions has a verbatim figure line and a refuter, and no further line closes a remaining gap.
- Formula "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The bound shape is stated in §5, so it can be refused.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, that would be an instrument or experiment that shows which condition is live in a real system, not a benchmark score.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

This matters for the answer: calling a particular deployed system "power-seeking" is a name, and it is licensed only if a ground pays for it. Until then it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The closest available empirical case is the METR investigation of the 2026 agent incident. It is rich and public, but Fig 11 :70 says *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* A harness case is a source reading, not the source. It shows that agents can build a shared channel and coordinate; it does not show that the shape generalizes to open deployment.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the validator rule: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."* This folder is not such a validator.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**The shape is: a maintained channel, a recurrent loop whose output re-enters as input, a comparison over a quantity, a reach that moves only on contact with a differing closure, and a habitat whose actors are enumerated rather than composed. The objective slot stays open: `OBJECTIVE(???) -> R`.**

That shape is a constraint set. Any candidate answer that omits the channel, or treats expansion as the addition of world primitives, or averages the conditions into one drive, fails the shape. Any candidate answer that fills the objective slot with "survival" or "power" as a terminal goal has gone beyond what the framework returns and owes a ground reading.

## 5b. Correction to my own earlier reading

In an earlier draft I wrote that the framework's *"shape, not a name"* rule forbids naming any specific condition, and I treated the METR incident as a ground that settled F1. **Both are wrong and are retracted here.**

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Channel, comparison, reach and habitat are not missing primitives; they are figure-held structures. Naming them is not forbidden. What is forbidden is filling the objective slot with a name the framework does not return.
- The METR case is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70). It corroborates wiring; it is not a ground that settles the general condition. Treating it as one was a source error.

Both the original wrong reading and its correction are left visible. Nothing is silently erased.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous AI-safety names assembled to agree with the claim would be the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and is labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*
