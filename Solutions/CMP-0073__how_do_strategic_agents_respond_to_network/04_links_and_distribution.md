## Wider literature that bears on the question, and what it predicts here

- **Economics, Lucas critique:** an intervention evaluated on historical regularities fails because agents' decision rules change with the policy. Predicts that a measure calibrated before the intervention is not valid after it. Corroborates F1.
- **Measurement and policy, Goodhart and Campbell:** *"The more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it will be to corruption pressures..."* Predicts that a consequential score is moved by agents. Corroborates F1. The Medium architecture article already cites these as instances under Fig 2 and Fig 10.
- **AI safety, specification gaming and reward hacking:** an optimiser reaches the formal objective without the intended outcome. Predicts the metric-improves-while-outcome-degrades signature. Corroborates F2. Note the overlap: this is one evidence pool with the Goodhart literature, so agreement is not two witnesses.
- **Behavioural economics, information cascades:** agents ignore private information and follow predecessors, producing fragile mass behaviour. Predicts F5's positive loop sign and F6's warning that convergence is not evidence.
- **Game theory and network science, games on networks:** Jackson and Zenou's handbook chapter studies games where players are connected by a network structure and the structure changes individual behaviour. Predicts that the response is a property of the network game, not of a defective agent. Corroborates F3.
- **Sociology and network science, complex contagions:** Centola and Macy find that *"as adoption thresholds increase, long ties can impede diffusion."* Predicts that the obvious structural intervention can preclude the intended diffusion. Corroborates F4.
- **Public health and network epidemiology:** voluntary vaccination under herd immunity creates free riders; the intervention is defeated by the response it elicits. Predicts F1 and F3 together, and supplies a nameable ground (infection outcomes) for the verification domain.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual and refuter first. The full architecture goes only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

**Hosting.** Nothing is sent by this agent. Host the packet yourself and prefer a link that lets a recipient decline contact. One-to-one only. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line, and an opt-out line in every message.
