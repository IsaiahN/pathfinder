## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *"the response defeats the effect when the consequential measure is agent-producible and the feedback sign is positive."*
- A name: *"the engagement ranking on platform X is gamed by bot networks in week 3."*

The shape can travel to a frame that has not seen this network. The name cannot.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *They're Scanning Books*: borrow structure from a field whose primitives differ. *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Here the borrowed dimensions are the Lucas critique (policy changes alter agents' decision rules), Goodhart and Campbell (a measure becomes corrupt when consequential), specification gaming (an optimiser reaches the formal objective without the intended outcome), information cascades (agents follow predecessors against private information), and policy resistance in vaccination games. These are low-overlap fields, so their agreement is not one pool talking.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach in `02` does exactly this: it states what a failure would look like.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"If your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* This is the essay-level source for the union finding F8.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An independent outcome measurement, not produced by the measured agents, would name which intervention in which network was defeated.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A field's measured cascade data could be imported with its provenance intact.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the discriminator requirement: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A model that reproduces the observed pattern of agent behaviour is not thereby the source of that pattern. *The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens it: an instrument reading green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, the spectrometer.

For this question, the projection is the clean metric (high uptake, high engagement, high compliance). Its source may be genuine adoption, gaming, coercion, or a cascade. The clean reading does not discriminate. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The intervener reads the intended effect off the agents' behaviour: if the score rose, the intervention worked. That is the metamerism error in world terms, and it is exactly what the specification-gaming and policy-resistance literatures document. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* names the operational fix: *"Votes come from outcomes, not popularity"*, and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."*

Two further cautions from the essays:

- *They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The choice of which network quantity to optimise is a visible choice, not a discovery.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* This is Fig 11 :52-54 in essay form, and it is the reason first-order enumeration is insufficient.

## 5. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous intervention, a large platform, or a well cited field gives a claim a budget to be tried; none of them settles whether the response defeated the effect.

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 6. Consequence for the two-stage offer

Because the shape is a constraint and not a name, stage 1 carries the condition and its refuter, not a story about a particular network. Because a name needs a ground, stage 2 is opt-in and the recipient's verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown. Because agreement is a convention when it only coordinates meaning (Fig 2 :41) and disqualifying when it decides an outcome (Fig 2 :43), the outreach never asks whether the recipient agrees. It asks what they would count as a failure. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the reason: *"Two navigators don't find their position by agreeing with each other. Each takes a bearing on the same fixed landmark... and nobody can talk the landmark into moving."*
