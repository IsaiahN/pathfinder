## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README in `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0039 a **shape** is the separator that any usable answer must have, before the wage, the buffer ratio or the programme size is known. It constrains: two slots, separate anchors, a habitat introduction, an external price anchor, a real-capacity test, two silent failures, a declared gap. It does not fill: it does not return "yes" or "no", a percentage of GDP, or a wage multiple of the minimum.

`SPLIT(employment, inflation; resource, habitat)` is the shape. `a job at 60 percent of the median wage with a 4 percent buffer ratio` is a name, and the figures do not license it without a ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: the ordinary terms "stabilization", "buffer", "anchor" and "slack" are proximity vocabulary. The figure text narrows them to per-slot residuals, a non-updating anchor, and an import into the employment habitat.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect" and Fig 9 :31 "rather than the cause". So we describe the effect (the employment reading does not move when the government claims success; the price reading does not move when the wage is announced) rather than importing "NAIBER" or "NAIRU" as if those names settled anything.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the pre-registered refuter in `01` is written as observable failures, not as school commitments.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that place is the separator plus its refuter, not a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Choosing the employment and inflation slots is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

**Correction to my own §3 (kept visible, see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).** I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming the wage rule or the buffer. That overstates it. The README phrase governs a **missing** primitive. A buffer rule and a wage floor are already available bindings from the policy frame onto held slots, so naming them is provenance, not the invention of a new primitive. What is genuinely missing is a source-discriminating instrument and a regime-valid tuning rule. Those are characterized in effect terms in `01` F4 and are a declared gap. The earlier sentence is retracted as applied to wage and buffer; the gap claim is not retracted.

## 4. Why the question is a union and not a bundle to be averaged

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session) asks: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The employment slot and the inflation slot overlap (the wage floor enters both) but do not share an anchor. So the correct operation is split, derive per domain, then recombine as a union with the intersections named. It is not "average the two into an overall effect on stabilization".

## 5. Why the source lesson matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied to ECN-0039: a falling unemployment rate and a stable price index can be produced by many underlying arrangements (a genuine buffer expansion, a statistical reclassification, a suppressed labour supply, a commodity windfall). The reading does not name the source. Claiming that the job guarantee produced the reading from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a name attached to a projection. The MGNREGA record in `01` F7 is the concrete case: the same programme is a "stellar example of rural development" in one reading and a set of "serious lapses" in another, because the readings do not discriminate the same things.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers well-known economists to agree that job guarantees are good (or bad) is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried against employment and price readings, and lets the ground settle it. The same essay says *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* while *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figure text. They are **not** tests of whether a job guarantee stabilizes employment or inflation. They are labelled as such everywhere. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 8. One-paragraph shape for outreach

> The figures do not return a yes or no on job guarantees. They return the shape any answer must have: employment and inflation are two slots with different anchors and must not be averaged; the job guarantee is a habitat introduction whose power is asymmetric, with two silent failures; the inflation anchor must be external to the policy because a declared wage is a convention, not a ground; the resource test is real capacity; and the wage, buffer ratio and programme size are a declared gap awaiting a source-discriminating instrument. That shape comes with seven refuters written in advance. It is a simulation checked against figures, not a measurement.
