## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot s. Γ is the government's policy belief about how the economy responds, a is the instrument (a standing job guarantee at a fixed wage), o′ is what actually happens. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Symbols table :38: "The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it. It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."

The question names two slots: employment and inflation. They have different currencies (jobs and hours versus a price level), different instruments for reading them, and different anchors (Fig 2 :29). A verdict that averages them into one "stabilization works" number would do exactly what Fig 1 :22 forbids. So the residual is a **union of at least two live slots**, each with its own loop, plus two conditioning domains (a fiscal/resource domain and an implementation/habitat domain).

**What an answer would look like (the sought-for shape bound as an ordinary term before the run):** not a yes or a no, but a separator of the form: a job guarantee closes the employment slot under conditions A; it does not by itself close the inflation slot, which needs an anchor that does not update; the resource slot supplies the real-capacity test; the habitat slot supplies the silent failures. Each clause carries a mode and a refuter. This paragraph is the pre-committed prediction. It is written before the findings below, so it can be refused by the same test as everything else (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66 on making the sought-for shape an ordinary term).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at least four-place (employment slot, inflation slot, resource slot, habitat slot), indexed per slot, never summed. Symmetry: "stabilize" is a threshold relation, a comparison against a target, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"; Operators table :51 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"). The instrument's action is ordered: the buffer expands in a downturn and contracts in a boom. Scale: one program loop, a national macro loop, and a time channel across regimes; the membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Employment slot | workers, employers, the JG authority, local job banks | jobs, hours, wage floor, employment rate | payroll and employment readings that do not move because the government claims success | admitted |
| D2 Inflation and price slot | firms, price setters, workers, the central bank | price level, inflation rate, wage demands | an independent price index that does not update on the policy announcement | admitted |
| D3 Fiscal and resource slot | treasury, central bank, bond markets, real capacity | public funds, real resources, output space | real capacity and observed resource use, not a self-declared budget rule | admitted |

| D5 Verification frame | researchers, institutions, journals, reviewers | studies, residuals, agreement | the ground (data), never agreement | admitted |

| X2 "One stabilization verdict" | none | aggregate | aggregates Fig 1 slots, so there is nothing to anchor | refused |

**Partition or union.** Union. D1 and D2 overlap (the wage floor enters both), D3 and D4 condition both. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." One lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception of each slot → vocabulary for what the instrument affords → objective stated as a comparison → planning the buffer rule → learn and carry. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Most published JG argument lands above the break (whether public employment is desirable); the middle link (what would count as progress in each slot, measured how) is the one with the fewest instruments.

**Strain test applied to the census.** My first pass wanted to map "employment and inflation" onto the six physical terms of Fig 13 (states and structures). That is a strain: the schedule of a price level is not a physical spectrum and a wage floor is not a physical gradient in the sense Fig 13 :45-47 argues. Re-census: the figures that bear are the slot/anchor figures (1, 2), the union/split figures (3, 9), the habitat figure (11), and the bond/cycle/comparison figures (12, Operators). Fig 13 is used only for the discipline that composition runs across kinds and that the framework returns a shape (Fig 13 :115, :134), not as a physical mapping. The strain is dissolved by assigning each figure its proper object, per the canonical re-census rule. The earlier reading is kept visible here rather than silently erased.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 The single-verdict framing is refused (decisive, structural)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort"
Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The employment slot and the inflation slot do not share a currency or an anchor. A job guarantee can close the first while leaving the second live; the reverse is also possible. A single "yes, use it for stabilization" would average two different residuals. The honest form is a per-slot verdict in a union.

**Mode:** `general` for the refusal of aggregation, `specified` for the two named slots. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 plus Fig 9 plus Fig 12 :125 on two currencies that do not add; low overlap between per-slot indexing, union splitting, and pricing). **Refuter:** a demonstration that employment and inflation residuals are one slot with one anchor, such that one lever provably clears both together. If that is shown, F1 is withdrawn.

### F2 The job guarantee is a habitat introduction, with asymmetric power (per-domain, D1 and D4)

Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."
Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"
Fig 11 :69 "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"

A job guarantee does not improve a worker in isolation. It adds an actor (an employer of last resort) to the employment habitat, so the relevant capability is worker-and-habitat. The seat's power is asymmetric: it can introduce a wage floor, it cannot subtract the private sector or the skills structure from the habitat. Two failures are silent until late: the skills and matching functions the programme failed to reproduce, and the actors it unintentionally introduced (administrative dependency, substitution of regular public jobs, displacement effects).

World corroboration, job guarantee field: Bill Mitchell's 2013 explanation says the programme offers "a job at a living (minimum) wage to anyone who wants to work but cannot find employment" and that the jobs "hire off the bottom", so "minimum wages are not in competition with the market-sector wage structure" (Mitchell, billmitchell.org, opened 2026-11-23). Malcolm Sawyer's criticism, recorded in the Wikipedia job guarantee article, includes that "the programme would require productive capacity in the right quantities and places to employ people who would otherwise be unemployed, which Sawyer regards as a significant practical constraint." Both describe a habitat change, not an agent fix.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 habitat and asymmetry plus Fig 6 :21 on closure). **Refuter:** show that the JG is a pure relabelling of existing unemployment benefits with no new composable term in D1, or that implementation has no silent failures over a full cycle. Either fires F2.

### F3 The inflation anchor cannot be the declared wage (D2)

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."
Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."

The JG wage is a convention: a number a government declares. Stable inflation requires a measure that does not change because the government announced the number. If the fixed wage is promoted into the price anchor by declaration, a convention decides an outcome and stops being a convention. Fig 2 :34 names the failure: the anchor becomes another frame and the drift is coherent, so nobody notices.

World corroboration: the Wikipedia job guarantee article states "There is an issue about the validity of an unchanging nominal anchor in an inflationary environment" and "Its viability as a nominal anchor relies on the fiscal authorities reining in any private wage-price pressures." The Mitchell piece says "in general, there cannot be inflationary pressures arising from a policy that sees the Government offering a fixed wage to any labour that is unwanted by other employers." That is a claim about the design's intended insulation from wage competition, not a measurement of an anchor. The distinction is exactly F3.

A low-overlap corroborator from human cognition: the Wikipedia anchoring effect article defines the effect as "a psychological phenomenon in which an individual's judgments or decisions are influenced by a reference point or 'anchor' which can be completely irrelevant" and notes that "anchoring is very difficult to avoid." A declared number that institutions then range against is a cognitive anchor as well as a policy convention, which is why the ground check cannot be skipped.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 anchor rule and Fig 10 convention rule are different figures with low overlap; world sources in macroeconomic policy and cognitive science are different fields but share the "anchor" vocabulary, so they count as one weak witness on the word and two on the mechanism). **Refuter:** a measurement in which the inflation anchor updates on the government's own policy announcement and still predicts inflation out of sample, or a demonstration that a declared wage settles the price level by declaration alone. Either breaks F3.

### F4 The controller shape is returned; the tuning is not (D2, D3, gap)

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"
Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

A job guarantee is a feedback controller: it reads a gap between a target and a measured variable and adjusts the buffer. Mitchell's account gives the shape: "Government employment and spending automatically increases (decreases) as jobs are lost (gained) in the private sector" and the relevant ratio is the "Non-Accelerating-Inflation-Buffer Employment Ratio (NAIBER)", which is "a full employment steady state JG level, which is dependent on a range of factors including the path of the economy." That last clause is the gap: the steady-state level depends on the path, and the figures do not return the path.

Low-overlap engineering corroborator: the Wikipedia PID controller article says "The PID controller automatically compares the desired target value (setpoint or SP) with the actual value of the system (process variable or PV). The difference between these two values is called the error value", and that "The balance of these effects is achieved by loop tuning to produce the optimal control function" with constants that "must be derived for each control application, as they depend on the response characteristics of the physical system, external to the controller." It also warns "The use of the PID algorithm does not guarantee optimal control of the system or its control stability." The engineering witness says the same thing the figures say: shape is portable, tuning is ground-bound.

The numerical wage, the buffer ratio, and the programme size are therefore a declared **gap**, reported as unreached, not silence and not a licence to invent a number.

**Mode:** `general` for the shape, `gap` for the tuning. **Status:** single-source on the figure side for the instrument rule (Fig 6), triangulated with the engineering witness on the structure of the claim. **Refuter:** a ground-validated tuning rule that holds across regimes, or a figure re-census that returns a numerical wage. Until one fires, the gap stands.

### F5 Job guarantee versus unemployment benefits is a bond choice, not a degree (D1, D3)

Fig 12 :46 "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."
Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."
Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Both instruments move public funds to households. The bond differs: wage-for-work against unconditional transfer. Over the shared fiscal atom set they are Fig 12 isomers, same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. Likewise NAIBER and NAIRU are isomers over the employment-status atom set: an employed buffer against an unemployed buffer as the disciplining stock. The choice is a type decision, and the valence of either instrument flips with the residual. Sawyer's second and third criticisms (downward pressure on comparable public-sector wages, and substitution of regular public jobs) are precisely claims about the bond, not about the amount spent.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 bond definition plus Fig 11 valence). **Refuter:** a demonstration that the two programmes differ only in degree of payment and have identical behavioural effects on wage demands, job search and public-sector staffing. Then the isomer claim fails and the degree reading stands. Developed in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

### F6 The loop sign is a ground question (D2, D3)

Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

The wage bill becomes demand, which becomes employment, which changes the wage bill. Whether that cycle damps or amplifies depends on real capacity, which is the D3 anchor. Mitchell's design claim is that hiring "off the bottom" avoids "the inflationary tendencies of old-fashioned Keynesianism, which attempted to maintain full capacity utilisation by 'hiring off the top' (i.e. making purchases at market prices and competing for resources with all other demand elements)." Sawyer's constraint and the Wikipedia buffer-stock article's observation that a scheme can produce "huge stockpiles, or in other cases, destruction of commodities" point the other way: a buffer only stabilizes while there is somewhere to put it. The sign is not decided by the wage declaration; it is decided at the ground.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** single-source on the figure side (Fig 12 cycle), corroborated by two positions inside the same job-guarantee evidence pool, so one witness, not three. **Refuter:** a controlled demonstration that the loop sign is fixed by the nominal wage alone regardless of real capacity, in either direction.

### F7 Program-level ground is not macro ground (D4, D5)

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"

The Indian MGNREGA programme is the largest real-world reading available in the sources opened. Its own accounting reports "1200 crore (12 billion) person-days of employment has been generated" and that "The average wage per person-day has gone up by 81 per cent since the Scheme's inception", and independent research found "the key benefit of the scheme lies in the reduction of wage volatility". The constitutional auditor found that "only 30.3 per cent of planned works had been completed" and "serious lapses arising mainly due to lack of public awareness, mismanagement and institutional incapacity." The same source records the World Bank chiding the act in 2009 and calling it a "stellar example of rural development" in its 2014 World Development Report. These are not contradictions to average away. They are two different slots: the programme paid wages (D4 ground) while the macro and implementation channels degraded (D2 and D4 channel). Fig 10 :24 says the ground does not decay; the channel does.

Mitchell's own blog comment thread supplies the same lesson at the design level: a commenter asks "what if the person given the job refuses to do it, or fails to work in a normal manner", and another observes "if there is no INDUCEMENT OR INCENTIVE to find regular work, then labour supply to the regular jobs market is constrained, which is inflationary." These are habitat objections, counted here as part of the Mitchell witness, not as separate sources.

**Mode:** `specified`. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 enumeration plus Fig 1 per-slot plus Fig 10 channel). **Refuter:** a full-cycle implementation in which neither silent failure appears and every planned work is completed while the wage floor holds.

### F8 The MMT versus mainstream disagreement is a non-shrinking union, not one debate (D5)

Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort"
Fig 7 :12 "same closure, handed along;"
Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"
Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

The Wikipedia NAIRU article records that "some economists have questioned whether there is any real empirical evidence for it at all", that "any estimates of the NAIRU at any point in time have a relatively wide margin for error, which limits its practical value as a policymaking tool", and that "the NAIRU theory was mainly intended as an argument against active Keynesian demand management". Mitchell's piece asserts the government "has no financial constraint as the issuer of the currency" and that the JG's microeconomic foundations "bear no resemblance to those underpinning the neoclassical NAIRU." The schools are not answering one question with opposite answers; they range against different anchors (real capacity versus nominal ratio), so the disagreement does not shrink with effort. Fig 9 :4 says split it. Agreement inside a school is a room's variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).

**Mode:** `general` for the splitting rule, `specified` for this literature. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 plus Fig 7 plus Fig 2). **Refuter:** a source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing both schools are ranging against the same anchor and the disagreement shrinks with additional data.

### F9 Cost and multiplier figures are filters for the budget, never witnesses (D3, D5)

Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."
Fig 5 :43 "A new term is worth having when stating it, plus what remains unexplained after it, costs less than what was unexplained before."

Cost estimates for a JG, fiscal multipliers, and buffer ratios tell us how much of the space is reached. They do not tell us whether the employment slot closes at an anchor that does not update. The "should" in the question is a verdict about the union, not a threshold on a cost table.

**Mode:** `general`. **Status:** single-source (Fig 9, canonically reinforced in `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md`). **Refuter:** a demonstration that a single cost threshold determines the per-slot verdicts. If that holds, F9 is withdrawn.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device" (Seat Map :287, quoted in the exemplar). The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single aggregate measure of "stabilization" is admitted that treats employment and inflation as one slot with one anchor (breaks F1, Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. The inflation anchor is shown to update on the government's own policy announcement and still predict out of sample (breaks F3, Fig 2 :33-34).
3. A declared wage is shown by the ground to settle the price level by declaration alone (breaks Fig 10 :47).
4. The job guarantee is shown to add no new composable term to the employment slot (a pure relabel of unemployment benefits), **or** shown to add a new atom to the inflation mechanism itself (breaks F2 and F4 in either direction, Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 6 :21).
5. The JG-versus-benefits distinction is shown to be degree-only with identical behavioural effects (breaks F5, Fig 12 :117).
6. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool shows that the two schools range against the same anchor and the disagreement shrinks with more data (breaks F8, Fig 9 :4).
7. A ground-validated controller tuning rule holds across regimes (closes the F4 gap, Fig 6 :32).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the separator is re-censused. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Updated verdict

The shape survives; the numerical answer does not. A job guarantee is a habitat introduction that can close the employment slot under a non-updating employment reading, provided its wage does not compete with the market wage structure. It does not by itself close the inflation slot, which needs an anchor that does not update on the policy; the declared wage is a convention and cannot settle an outcome. The resource slot supplies the real-capacity test, the habitat slot supplies two silent failures, and the verification frame must keep school agreement out of the verdict. The choice against conventional benefits is a bond choice (isomer), not a degree. The numerical wage, buffer ratio and programme size are a declared gap of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind.
