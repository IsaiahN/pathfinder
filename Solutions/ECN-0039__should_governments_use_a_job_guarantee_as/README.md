## The question restated as an effect

A government holds a policy belief Γ about how employment and inflation respond to its actions. It is considering one action: a standing public offer to employ anyone willing and able to work at a fixed wage. R is the gap between what that policy predicts and what happens, indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19). The two named slots, employment and inflation, have different currencies and different anchors, so the residual is a union, not one number. The question asks for a verdict on the instrument. What an answer would look like, written down as an ordinary term before the run: a separator that says for which slot-conditions the instrument closes R, which anchor each slot must range against, and what would refute the claim. That shape is bound below and allowed to accrue a residual.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the separator.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not replace a ground: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Third, showing the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence: Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

## Standing of this derivation

Every substantive file says so in its own header: this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. No employment or inflation measurement was taken in producing this folder.
