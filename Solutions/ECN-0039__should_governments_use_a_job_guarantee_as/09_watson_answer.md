## QUESTION

Should governments use a job guarantee as a tool for employment and inflation stabilization? Restated in effect terms: a government considers one instrument, a standing public job at a fixed wage, to close R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| in two slots. Option A: treat this as one question and answer yes or no. Option B: split the union, derive a verdict per slot, and declare the numerical parts a gap. What each leads to: A averages two different anchors and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The figures decide B.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four slots, employment, inflation, fiscal and resource, habitat and implementation, indexed per slot and never summed. Symmetry: "stabilize" is a threshold comparison against a target, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"). The buffer action is ordered: expand in a downturn, contract in a boom. Scale: one programme loop, one national macro loop, and a time channel across regimes. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1 Employment slot.** Actors: workers, employers, JG authority, local job banks. Currency: jobs, hours, wage floor. Anchor: employment readings that do not move because the government claims success. Admitted.
- **D2 Inflation and price slot.** Actors: firms, price setters, workers, central bank. Currency: price level, wage demands. Anchor: an independent price index that does not update on the policy announcement. Admitted.
- **D3 Fiscal and resource slot.** Actors: treasury, central bank, bond markets, real capacity. Currency: public funds and real resources. Anchor: observed resource use, not a self-declared budget rule. Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat and implementation.** Actors: local labour markets, skills, administrators, other programmes. Currency: contact and job matches. Anchor: actual placement and completed work. Admitted.
- **D5 Verification frame.** Actors: researchers, institutions, reviewers. Currency: studies, residuals, agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted. In this simulated run all five domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **X1 "The government can always afford it".** No nameable anchor if read as unlimited; absorbed into D3 real capacity. X2 "One stabilization verdict" aggregates slots, so there is nothing to anchor; refused.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 overlap through the wage floor; D3 and D4 condition both. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." One lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive each slot, find terms for what the buffer affords, state the objective as a comparison, plan the buffer rule, carry the verified result (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."). Describe the residual before naming a school (Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second.").

## VERDICT (from the figures)

A job guarantee can close the employment slot under a non-updating employment reading and a wage that does not compete with the private wage structure. It does not by itself close the inflation slot, because the fixed wage is a convention and a convention that decides an outcome stops being one; that slot needs an anchor external to the policy. The resource slot is tested by real capacity, the habitat slot contributes two silent failures, JG versus benefits is a bond choice rather than a degree, and the loop sign is decided at the ground. The numerical wage, buffer ratio and programme size are a declared gap. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified`, `general` on structure, `gap` on tuning, ungrounded, candidate. This is a shape and a route, not a policy result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, per-domain verdicts and seven refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than hidden. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a school argument or a cost table instead of the residual and its refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The separator and its falsifiers are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one without withdrawing the whole derivation.
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered refuters: (1) one aggregate stabilization slot with one anchor; (2) an inflation anchor that updates on the policy and still predicts out of sample; (3) a declared wage that settles prices by declaration; (4) a JG that adds no new term to employment or adds a new atom to the inflation mechanism; (5) identical JG and benefit behaviour at equal spend; (6) one shared anchor for both schools with a shrinking disagreement; (7) a ground-validated tuning rule across regimes. Also reversed by a source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing real capacity is irrelevant to the loop sign. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
