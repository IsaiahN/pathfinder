# The Tether's answer on a job guarantee for employment and inflation stabilization

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural separator, `gap` on the numerical tuning. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## 1. The residual it answers

A government asks whether one instrument, a standing public job at a fixed wage, should stabilize two things. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Employment and inflation have different currencies and different anchors, so the question is a union of conditions, not one problem with a yes/no answer (Fig 9 :55). The answer is a separator with a verdict per slot, never an average.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split before answering.** Fig 9 :4 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort" and Fig 9 :5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The MMT versus mainstream argument does not shrink because the two schools range against different anchors. Keep the school labels out of stage 1.
2. **Employment slot, conditionally yes.** A job guarantee is a habitat introduction: it adds an employer of last resort rather than improving a worker in isolation (Fig 11 :39, :40). It can close the employment residual when the employment reading does not move because the government claims success, and when the wage floor does not compete with the market wage structure (world: Mitchell's "hire off the bottom"). Verdict `specified`, triangulated, candidate.
3. **Inflation slot, not by the instrument alone.** The JG wage is a convention. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47). Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." An independent price reading, not the declared wage, must settle this slot. The buffer rule changes an input to the inflation process; it does not supply a new atom to the inflation mechanism. Verdict `specified`, triangulated, candidate.
4. **Resource slot is the real-capacity test.** "The government can always afford it" has no nameable anchor if read as unlimited; the real anchor is capacity and observed resource use (Fig 2 :29). Sawyer's constraint that "the programme would require productive capacity in the right quantities and places" is the objection that bites here, not the treasury balance sheet. Verdict `specified`, single-source on figure side, live strain.
5. **Habitat slot: introduction without exclusion.** Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Two failures are silent: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts" (Fig 11 :68, :69). MGNREGA supplies both readings: a "reduction of wage volatility" and "only 30.3 per cent of planned works had been completed". Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
6. **The choice is a bond choice.** JG and unemployment benefits are isomers over the fiscal atom set (Fig 12 :117); NAIBER and NAIRU are a functional group of buffer thresholds. Type, not degree, is the variable. Verdict `specified`, triangulated for the figure definition, unmeasured for the economic identification.
7. **The loop sign is decided at the ground.** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The wage bill re-enters as demand. Whether that damps or amplifies depends on spare capacity, not on the wage declaration. Verdict `specified`, single-source on figure side.
8. **The numbers are a gap.** No figure line returns the wage, the buffer ratio, or the programme size. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument plus a regime-valid tuning rule, characterized in effect terms but not held.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a yes or a no on job guarantees in the abstract. The figures return a separator, not a policy verdict.
- Not that a job guarantee controls inflation by wage declaration. That is the F3 failure (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that MMT is right and mainstream macro is wrong, or the reverse. That is the Fig 9 :4 split.
- Not a wage multiple, a buffer percentage or a programme size. Those are a gap.
- Not that a cost table decides the question. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not that a successful pilot is a macro verdict. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- Not `grounded`. No employment or price measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it, and what would refute it

The ground, not agreement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The instruments owed are:

- An employment reading that does not update on the government's own claim of success.
- A price reading that does not update on the policy announcement, with a discriminator that can tell a genuine buffer shift from a statistical reclassification or a suppressed labour supply.
- A real-capacity measurement that fixes the sign of the wage bill to demand loop.
- A regime-valid tuning rule for the wage and the buffer ratio.

Seven pre-registered refuters are in `01` §4 and repeated in `08` and `09`. The decisive ones: a single aggregate stabilization slot; an updating inflation anchor; a declared wage that settles prices; a JG that adds no new term to employment or adds a new atom to inflation; identical JG and benefit behaviour; one shared anchor for both schools; a validated tuning rule.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The separator stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish sources. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable; "Indiscernibility is not identity" (canonical README). One live strain is visible: MMT's claim that the currency issuer "has no financial constraint" versus the real-capacity constraint. It was returned to the census and did not flip the verdict.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not answer yes or no to a job guarantee. They say the question is two slots, not one: employment and inflation have different currencies and different anchors, so a single "stabilization" verdict would average away the live signal. A job guarantee is a habitat introduction that can close the employment slot when the employment reading does not update on the government's own claim and the wage does not compete with the market. It cannot by itself close the inflation slot, because the declared wage is a convention and a convention that decides an outcome stops being one; that slot needs an anchor external to the policy. The real test is productive capacity, implementation has two silent failures, and the choice against unemployment benefits is a bond choice rather than a matter of degree. The wage, the buffer ratio and the programme size are a declared gap awaiting a better instrument. What this offers is the shape, seven refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It is a simulation checked against figures, not a measurement.
