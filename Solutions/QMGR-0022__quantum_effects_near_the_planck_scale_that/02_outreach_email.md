**Subject:** One checkable output on Planck-scale causal order

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the rule, `specified` on the prediction, not `grounded`):** the framework's minimum set has six terms in two kinds, states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network), with the rule that composition runs across kinds and never within one. From that rule, smooth continuum causal order is not a primitive. It is a residue of composition, along with discreteness, symmetry and chaos. So a question about quantum effects that change causal structure near the Planck scale is about what a composition leaves behind when the scale changes, not about adding a seventh thing. The framework returns a shape, not the name of a specific effect.
>
> Two further consequences follow from the same rule. A causal ordering between space and time needs a structure in the slot, because two states cannot bond directly. And the slot stays marked `???`: the six do not select which structure, and no Planck-scale instrument is available to settle it. I am explicitly not claiming to know which quantum effect does this.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that causal sets are the answer, that Lorentz invariance is violated at the Planck scale, that spacetime foam is real, or that any of these follows from the figures. Those are bindings from physics frames onto held atoms. They need provenance and a ground, and neither has been supplied here. Agreement with any of them would not corroborate this output, because we would be drawing on the same evidence pool.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. deriving one of the six from the other five, or a seventh from the six;
> 2. showing the framework must admit a state-state or structure-structure composition at first order and cannot refuse it;
> 3. a Planck-scale ground reading returning a change in causal order that cannot be written as any composition of the six under the seven bonds plus negation;
> 4. showing the two conditions in the residual have independent grounds, so the union reading is false.
>
> I wrote these before sending this. If any holds, I will narrow or withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures are the authority, not my summary of them. Public sources I actually read:
