## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Which bond holds between the two conditions?

Fig 12 :46:
> **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."**

The question's wording, "quantum effects near the Planck scale that change the causal structure", commits to a **production** reading: the effect produces a change in order. That is an ordered bond with a before and after. It is not a symmetric conjunction. But the world evidence does not support one bond across all candidates. Causal set theory keeps causal order as the fundamental relation and lets the continuum emerge from order plus number (Surya 2019). Lorentz-violation phenomenology modifies propagation in a fixed continuum (Mattingly 2005, Desai 2023). Those are different bond/atom arrangements, not two spellings of one term.

⇒ The bond is **not settled by the six-term set**. It is a binding from a physics frame, and the union must stay unbundled under Fig 9 :54. **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a single ordered bond can be shown to generate both candidates from the same atoms under the same scale rule, the union reading fails and one question replaces two.

### Q2 - Isomers or functional group?

Fig 12 :117:
> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :123:
> **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Write the two first-order pictures as the record would:

- smooth relativistic order: `Space - (structure) - Time`;
- discrete order with continuum limit: `(ordering) + (number)`, then a continuum as a derivative.

As written, the two do **not** share all atoms. They share the ordered relation and the space/time states at most, so by Fig 12 :117 they are **not isomers**. They are two members of the same **functional group**: a cross-kind composition whose output is an ordering. The group says what kind (an order-producing composition); the qualifier says which member (smooth continuation or discrete sprinkling).

The stronger version: if both are rewritten over the full shared atom set `{Space, Time, Energy, Gradient, Spectrum, Network}`, differing only in which structure mediates which state pair, then they **are** isomers, same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the claim worth testing, and it is still `specified`.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either picture can be written without an atom the other requires, or if no arrangement over the shared set reproduces both, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands.

### Q3 - Does the figure license a closed causal loop?

Fig 12 :121:
> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

A closed timelike curve is the physics name for a causal order that returns to its own past. The figure licenses a cycle as a bond-defined structure and puts the decision in the sign of the loop, not in the atoms. It does not say that a physical closed causal curve exists at the Planck scale, and no instrument in this derivation decides it. Mattingly 2005 notes that Lorentz violation can break microcausality but need not break local causality in a concordant frame, and that general relativity itself admits closed timelike curves. That is corroboration from a different field, not a figure ruling.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** a ground reading showing that a cycle bond cannot be realised as a causal order at any scale, or a figure re-census that forbids cycles under the ordered bonds, would remove this candidate. Until then the verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, and the strain is named rather than hidden.

### Q4 - What is the order of the comparison?

Fig 12 :104:
> **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Both first-order pictures are settled against the ground in their own domains (relativistic causal structure at accessible scales; discrete spectra in analogue and model systems). Each may therefore serve as an atom at the next level. Their union is **second order**, licensed by the settled-molecule rule rather than by a new primitive. This is the recursion the figure names, not an import that moves a wall (Fig 6 :25-26: *"An import adds no atom to the world. The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*).

**Falsifier:** show that a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom, or that the low-energy settlement does not carry across the scale transform. The second is the live risk: the round trip is lossy, and Fig 4 :30 requires its gap to be measured rather than assumed.

### Q5 - Does the scale transform preserve the ordering?

Fig 13 :88:
> **"The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."**

The low-energy ordering and the Planck-scale ordering are not two readings of one slot. Each turn begins from the residue the previous turn left. So extrapolation of a light-cone structure upward in energy is a composition against a residue, not a simple rescaling. This is the structural reason the question cannot be answered by repeating the same measurement at smaller distance.

**Falsifier:** a single scale-invariant composition that carries the low-energy causal order unchanged to the Planck scale without residue would collapse the distinction. That would be a `grounded` result if measured, and the shape claim would narrow accordingly.

## CONCLUSION

The two conditions are not yet one term. The bond that joins them is not settled by the six-term set, so the union stays unbundled. As the record writes them, the smooth and discrete pictures share a functional group rather than being isomers; the stronger isomer claim over the full shared atom set is real but unmeasured. A closed causal loop is licensed by the cycle bond, with its sign left to the ground. The union is second order, licensed by settled molecules becoming atoms, and the scale transform carries a residue that must be measured by a round trip rather than assumed zero.

This rescues what is defensible in the question without adding a seventh term or a name: there is a shape any candidate must have, and there are four stated falsifiers. No specific effect is derived.
