## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essay links.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list ranked for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Planck-scale claims are expensive to get wrong, so independent checking matters more than a large audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record that answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) only if asked, the formula as a method to reuse with their own ground and their own refuter written first.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim, the mode and the pre-registered refuter. Ask the recipient what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict before sending any theory. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the links above. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the framework's interior (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, on request.** Offer the figures and formula as a generator. Require each user to state their own ground and write their own refuter before running it, because formula step "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question and the Arrival essay warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The shape here is deliberately modest: it claims a residue and a union, not a named effect. If that modest claim fails a check, stop.
