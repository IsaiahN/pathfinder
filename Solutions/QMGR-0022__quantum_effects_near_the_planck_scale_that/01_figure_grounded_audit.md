## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question names an effect, "quantum effects near the Planck scale that change the causal structure of spacetime." Stated as a residual R:

> The continuum frame predicts a smooth causal order at every scale. At the Planck scale nothing in the frame registers, so the prediction and the observation cannot be compared. What is owed is a term that says what a causal order is made of and what would have to change for it to be observed to change.

R is not "what is the true theory of quantum gravity." That is a whole field's library, not a residual. R is the gap at a specific contact: smooth ordering carried down to a scale where smoothness is not licensed by anything held.

- **Arity.** Two conditions in contact: quantum fluctuation (discreteness or indeterminacy of geometry) and Lorentzian causal order (an asymmetric before/after relation). Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* This is a **union compound**, not one hard question.
- **Symmetry.** Causal order is an ordering, not a symmetric conjunction. Fig 12 :49 *"The seven bonds, and whether the order of the operands matters. Negation is the eighth operator and is not a bond"*; Operators table: sequence `→` is ordered, comparison `⋛` is ordered. A "change in causal structure" is therefore a change in an ordered relation, not the addition or removal of an ingredient.
- **Scale.** First order inside each theory (a smooth light-cone structure inside GR, a fixed causal background inside ordinary QFT), second order at their contact. Fig 13 :88 *"The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."*

**What an answer would look like, committed before the run:** a composition residue whose bond type and scale decide whether an ordering survives. A name of a specific effect (causal set sprinkling, Lorentz violation, spacetime foam, doubly special relativity) is not that shape; it is a binding from a physics frame, owed provenance. This binding is written down so it can be refused by the same test as everything else, per formula step "Direction".

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before any conclusion, per the seat map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim in section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh member is derived from the six. This breaks Fig 13 indivisibility and minimality.
2. The framework must admit a state-state or structure-structure composition at first order and cannot refuse it. This breaks Fig 13 :74-75.
3. A Planck-scale ground reading returns a change in causal order that cannot be written as any composition of the six under the seven bonds plus negation. This breaks the shape claim at the ground, not inside the frame.
4. The two conditions in the residual turn out to be separate questions with independent grounds, so the union reading is false and Fig 9 :54 was misapplied.
5. Causal order is shown to be a primitive member of a revised minimum set derived under the figure rules. This would remove the very residue the question names.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. A failed refutation attempt is not a confirmation; it is a search that has not yet found a refusal (Fig 8 :39).

## 5. SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD, kept distinct

Formula step 7 asks three questions in a fixed order. They are not read against one another.

**SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest).** Is the specific effect already in the closure and unfound? The figure set holds causality only as an ordered relation (sequence, comparison) and as a cycle; it holds no named Planck effect. Searching the six-term closure for "causal set" or "LIV" cannot manufacture them. The answer is no; nothing owed was missed inside the frame.

**INWARD (extend an instrument from one already returning something).** GRB arrival-time lags, polarisation birefringence, gravitational-wave dispersion, clock comparisons and threshold reactions already return readings at accessible energies. They extrapolate to a Planck scale many orders above their reach. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* INWARD says the channel is thin, not that no channel can ever exist.

**OUTWARD (import from a frame whose closure predicts the shape).** Causal set theory predicts order plus number instead of a continuum; loop gravity gives discrete area and volume spectra; LIV phenomenology predicts modified dispersion. Each was described in effect terms before being named, and each carries provenance. Import is debited against independence and supplies a shape, not a name. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
