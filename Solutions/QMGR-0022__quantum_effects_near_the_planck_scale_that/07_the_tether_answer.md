# The Tether's answer on Planck-scale causal structure

**Mode: `general` on the composition rule, `specified` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The continuum frame carries a smooth causal order down to scales where smoothness is not licensed. Nothing registers there. The residual is the uncompared gap between that prediction and the ground, with two conditions in contact: quantum discreteness of geometry and Lorentzian causal order. Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* This is a **union compound**, not one hard problem.

## 2. The shape

1. **Causal structure is a residue, not a member of the minimum set.** Fig 13 :43-44: *"A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time."* Fig 13 :124-125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Both sides of the question's imagined change (smooth order and discrete order) are derivatives. The effect being asked about is a change in what a composition leaves behind at a scale.
2. **A structure must sit between space and time.** Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* A light cone is not a primitive. The slot stays marked `???`, because the six do not select which structure (Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*).
3. **The two conditions must be unbundled before search.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Substrate discreteness and propagation symmetry are separate questions with different instruments. Mixing them manufactures a false single answer.
4. **The union is second order.** Both first-order pictures are settled in their own domains, so each becomes an atom for the next level: Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"*. The comparison is a structural relation at a threshold, not a unified field. As written, the pictures share a **functional group** rather than being isomers; the stronger isomer claim over the full atom set is testable but unmeasured (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).
5. **A closed causal loop is licensed but unsigned.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* The figure names the structure; the ground must decide the sign.
6. **The stop is a verdict, not silence.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The state is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything, and no final room. Fig 7 :6 *"the limit: no last room"*.
- Not a derivation of causal set theory, Lorentz violation, doubly special relativity, spacetime foam or any discrete spectrum. Those are bindings from physics frames, provenance owed, not outputs of the six-term set.
- Not a claim that Lorentz invariance is violated at the Planck scale. The world sources disagree about whether discreteness requires it (Surya 2019 versus Mattingly 2005), and the figure does not adjudicate.
- Not a measurement at the Planck scale, and not a claim that the extrapolation of low-energy causal order is valid there.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no seventh primitive.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument is needed, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Ordering versus directedness test.** Does the metric behave as ordering (spectrum-like) or directedness (gradient-like) at the Planck scale? An instrument that discriminates these would name the slot.
2. **Propagation test.** Does an energy-dependent arrival time survive a joint analysis across redshifts that separates intrinsic lag from propagation? Desai 2023 records that claims of LIV from GRB spectral lags conflict with the most stringent limits from other searches; the conflict is a measurement problem, not a verdict either way.
3. **Substrate test.** Does a discrete order reproduce the low-energy conformal geometry with a measured round-trip gap `R_T`, rather than only by assertion? Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
4. **Standing refuters.** Derive one of the six from the other five; admit a first-order state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; measure a change in causal order not writable as any composition of the six; show the union has independent grounds. Any one fires against the shape claim, not just against a detail.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six"* (They're Scanning Books). Declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

It says that smooth causal order is a composition residue, not a primitive: both smoothness and discreteness are things a composition leaves behind, so the question is about what changes in a residue when the scale changes. It says a structure must sit between space and time, that the slot stays open because the six do not select a member, and that substrate discreteness and propagation symmetry must be searched separately rather than merged into one answer. The union is second order, built from already-settled first-order pictures, and a closed causal loop is licensed by a cycle bond whose sign only a ground can decide. What it offers is the shape any candidate must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The stop is `unreached`, not silence, and the slot remains open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
