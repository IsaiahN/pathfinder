## What the question decides, in effect terms

Neutrinos are known to be massive only through flavour oscillations, which measure mass-squared differences and not the absolute mass scale. The open effects are: (1) the absolute mass scale, (2) whether the mass term conserves total lepton number (Dirac, neutrino distinct from antineutrino, requiring a right-handed state) or violates it by two units (Majorana, neutrino identical to antineutrino), and (3) the mechanism that generates the mass. The experimental discriminator for (2) is neutrinoless double beta decay (0νββ): a positive signal establishes lepton-number violation and, by the black box theorem, a Majorana mass term; a null result at any finite sensitivity does not establish Dirac.

What an answer would look like: a ground reading from an instrument that discriminates lepton-number-conserving from lepton-number-violating mass terms, stated as an ordinary term with a mode and a refuter written in advance. Until that reading exists, the honest output is a shape and a route, not a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a Dirac or a Majorana reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283)
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163)
3. Showing the source before the verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
