## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can implement it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> an instrument that discriminates a lepton-number-conserving (ΔL=0) mass term from a lepton-number-violating (ΔL=2) mass term.

That is a shape. "Neutrinos are Majorana" and "neutrinos are Dirac" are names. The shape can be stated today; the name awaits a ground reading.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the near vocabulary is lepton number, ΔL=2, half-life limits, and the effective Majorana mass.
- The same essay names the union error directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: the three neutrino-mass slots are usually bundled.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So describe the effect (a ΔL=2 process producing two electrons with no missing energy), not the gauge-theory cause.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it" and "the finish line will also be the starting place". For this question the loop stops when an instrument no longer registers a difference between the ΔL=0 and ΔL=2 hypotheses at the relevant mass scale. It has not stopped.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." This is the metamerism point applied here: a half-life limit cannot comment on the mechanism that produced it, and a mass value cannot comment on the lepton-number character. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved green; a source-discriminating experiment, not a preference, resolves Dirac versus Majorana.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** *They're Scanning Books*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." The 0νββ programme is such an import into the neutrino-mass question: it adds an axis (lepton-number violation) that oscillation data did not represent at all. It carries a source gate (Schechter and Valle 1982, via the reviews) and a shadow test (it explains why oscillations were silent on s2).

*They're Scanning Books* also warns: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Using it to route a neutrino question is a choice, and recording it is what keeps it checkable.

## 4. Why the current evidence cannot fill the slot

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: a spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source". The equivalent here are three distinct projections:

- **Mass-squared differences** read Δm² and cannot comment on the absolute scale or on L character.
- **A 0νββ half-life limit** reads a lifetime and cannot comment uniquely on m_beta-beta, because the nuclear matrix elements carry a spread (the PDG review notes "a factor ∼2-3 spread in the calculated nuclear matrix elements").
- **A positive 0νββ signal** reads ΔL=2 and cannot comment on which mechanism dominated, because the black box generated mass is far too small to explain the observed splittings.

Each is a lossy projection. The instrument owed is one that discriminates among sources, not another argument about which source is more elegant. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid saying Dirac or Majorana? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own first reading

My first reading of the rule was too broad, in the same way the exemplar's first reading was too broad. Correction:

- The README's phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like." The subject is a missing primitive or instrument, not a term already held in the physics frame.
- Dirac and Majorana are **already in the physics library** as two candidate bindings of the neutrino field. Neither is missing from Γ. Naming either is a binding from the physics frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework *derives* which binding nature chose (Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
- What **is** missing here is the source-discriminating instrument. For that missing thing, the rule bites exactly: we can characterise its shape (discriminates ΔL=0 from ΔL=2, reaches the relevant mass scale), and we cannot name its implementation from inside the framework. 0νββ is the current best candidate (Springer review: "The most sensitive known experimental method to verify whether neutrinos are Majorana particles is the search for neutrinoless double-beta decay"), and non-relativistic cosmic neutrino background capture is another (same review: a Majorana rate "up to a factor of two larger"). The framework selects neither.

Both claims are now recorded: naming is not forbidden for held bindings, and the shape-not-a-name limit is strict for the missing instrument. Neither is silently erased. **Verdict: `triangulated`** (README + Fig 9 :32 + two reviews). Refuter: a verbatim figure line that selects one implementation, or a measurement that makes two implementations observationally identical for all future instruments (which would turn the shape into a mere preference).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The fact that "most, if not all, theorists believe that neutrinos are Majorana particles" (Rodejohann) gives the Majorana reading a budget to be tried. It does not settle it. A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with either reading is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**A room is not an anchor.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." Ten neutrino physicists who all read the same PDG review are one evidence pool. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, and ask each reader for a stage-1 verdict before showing the framework.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture essay adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any neutrino hypothesis and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
