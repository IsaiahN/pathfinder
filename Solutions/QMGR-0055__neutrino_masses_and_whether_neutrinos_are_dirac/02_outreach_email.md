**Subject:** Check one output on neutrino mass before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "neutrino mass" is three slots that are usually averaged into one. (1) Oscillations give mass-squared differences but no absolute mass scale and no information on lepton-number character. (2) Whether the mass term conserves total lepton number (Dirac, neutrino distinct from antineutrino, a right-handed state needed) or violates it by two units (Majorana, neutrino identical to antineutrino). (3) The mechanism that generates the mass. These have different grounds, so one lever does not clear them all.
>
> **The output:** a shape and a route, not a name. The shape is an instrument that discriminates a ΔL=0 mass term from a ΔL=2 one. I am not claiming the framework selects Dirac or Majorana; it returns a shape, not a name. Neutrinoless double beta decay is the anchor for slot (2) because a positive signal would show total lepton number is violated, and the black box theorem connects that to a Majorana mass term regardless of the mediating mechanism. A null result at any finite sensitivity is depth-or-absence: it narrows the space, it does not establish Dirac.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that theory preference for Majorana is evidence, that a null 0νββ result proves a Dirac neutrino, that the absolute mass scale would settle the nature question, or that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against a fixed figure set. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written before this message):**
> 1. a figure line that actually selects Dirac or selects Majorana, where I claim none exists;
> 2. one ground reading that fixes the absolute mass scale and the lepton-number character at once, so the three slots should be merged;
> 3. a confirmed 0νββ signal compatible with a strictly Dirac neutrino and no Majorana mass term, which would break the black box connection as I have used it;
> 4. a null result at a sensitivity excluding a Majorana contribution for every ordering and mechanism, which would make a Dirac verdict reachable.
>
> If any holds, I retract the relevant part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The verbatim figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

> - Canonical figures usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
