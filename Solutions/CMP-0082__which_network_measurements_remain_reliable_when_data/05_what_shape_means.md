## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". `Fig 9 :32`: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The Symbols table adds that the residual "is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of a reliable measurement that can be stated before any named measure has been checked against a ground. It constrains. It does not fill. The shape here is four conditions: per-slot residual, independent non-updating anchor, union of the three defects with separate loops, and a measured round-trip gap. A ranked list such as "degree is reliable, betweenness is not" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the terms you already have that are closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- `Fig 9 :27-28`: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The residual was described in effect terms in `01` §0 before any measure was considered.
- `Fig 6 :30`: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The shape is stated as a property of (measure, channel, ground), not as software-specific vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle branch: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union finding F4. The question looks like one list of reliable measures; it is three overlapping conditions plus their intersections.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the shape stops when the residual against an independent ground stops arriving, not when a list of measures has been produced.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means an independently enumerated `G` and measured per-slot residuals for the named measure under each defect.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :50-52`), with a source gate and a shadow test. A sampling design borrowed from survey statistics is such an import when its provenance and its shadow (which gap it explains) are recorded.

A claim that "betweenness centrality is unreliable" is a name. Without a ground reading it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The figures do not license it.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that governs biased-platform data. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Several underlying causes create the same reading, so "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The paper's answer is not a better argument but a better instrument: the case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Apply that to network measurement. An observed degree distribution or clustering coefficient is a projection. Several true networks under several collection channels can produce the same observed statistic. Reading the source, that is, declaring that the observed statistic is the true network's statistic, is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources: a second independent channel or an enumeration. This is F3 and F5 stated in the source vocabulary. `Fig 10 :24`: "the ground does not decay, the channel does".

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean here?** A set of constraints on any measurement that could be called reliable under the three defects, stated before the ranking is attempted:

1. The error is reported per slot, because "R is measured per slot, never as a single global number" (Symbols table) and "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (`Fig 1 :22`).
2. The measurement is ranged against a ground that does not move when the collection frame changes (`Fig 2 :29-30`, :33).
3. The three defects are treated as a union of overlapping conditions, not severities of one condition (`Fig 9 :57`), and each intersection gets its own loop.
4. The round-trip gap between observed and true values is measured, because "the transform between scales loses information" (`Fig 4 :27`) and the gap must be taken and measured (`Fig 4 :30`).
5. Agreement counts only across low-overlap channels (`Fig 2 :31-32`, :40).

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No. It does not name a measure as reliable or unreliable without a ground. It names the conditions. If a later reader supplies an enumerated ground and per-slot residuals, the shape can be filled and a ranking can be attempted; that is a different document with a different mode.

**Correction to my own framing.** I initially wrote the shape as "keep the measure local." That was too strong. `Fig 1 :19` governs the indexing of the residual, not the choice of statistic. A global statistic can be computed and reported, but it cannot certify the per-slot claim. The correct form is "report the per-slot residual alongside whatever global statistic you publish," not "never use global statistics." The earlier phrasing is preserved here rather than silently erased.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known network scientists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an independent enumeration settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
