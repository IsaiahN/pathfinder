## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"** and the following line says it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From the *Library of Babel* essay, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ECN-0058 a **shape** is therefore: conditions that must be present, stated before any one cause is named. The shape here is *per-slot union of four compounding conditions with an instrument that would discriminate which one is load-bearing*. That constrains an acceptable answer. It does not supply the answer "returns" or "inheritance" or a variance share.

Concretely:
- "Large wealth differences come from a compounding loop, a flow into it, a transferred head start, and a closure of scarce claims" is a shape.
- "Large wealth differences are 60 percent returns" is a name plus a share, not a shape, and is not licensed by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is nearest the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* This is the bootstrap by proximity. For this question the starting vocabulary is ordinary economics (return, saving, bequest, asset); the figure vocabulary added is slot, residual, cycle, membrane, closure, ground.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence the effect restatement in `README.md`: a persistent positive per-slot residual, not a cause list.
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is almost verbatim the union finding F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, reached from an essay rather than from Fig 9.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the stop is not a share but the shape plus its refuter; the stop is honest because the route is recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name or a share

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here the instrument is administrative wealth and inheritance data plus natural experiments, not a further decomposition of the same survey.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fagereng et al.'s registry estimates enter this folder as such an import, declared with author, venue, year and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

A variance share that ranks the four conditions is neither of these. It is computed inside one closure by the estimator that also uses it to score itself, which Fig 1 :27 calls frame-internal.

## 4. Why "mainly" is the specific forbidden error

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* Applied here: a single workshop, dataset, or model that announces which cause dominates is a frame certifying itself.

The essay names the precise failure mode of demanding endorsement: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* It continues: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of prominent economists who agree about the dominant cause is prestige standing in for proof. That is why the outreach artifacts in this folder ask for a refutation, not an endorsement.

The question's "mainly" also commits the bundling error named by Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A ranking presupposes the partition that the figure refuses.

## 5. Q2 - ANSWERED: what does the framework give us here?

**It gives the union shape, the per-slot discipline, the compounding cycle, the head-start transfer, the closure condition, and the filter-versus-witness distinction. It does not give a ranking.** A ranking would require an independent instrument that discriminates load-bearing conditions in the same case, plus provenance for any import used to name one. Until that instrument exists, the share is a `gap` in the strict sense of Fig 13 :134, not an omission.

**Does the question commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, in its framing. It asks for a *name* (which one) where the framework returns a *shape* (the compound and the discriminator). The honest answer records the reframing instead of supplying the requested name.

## 6. Correction to my own §1 wording

I initially wrote that the framework "cannot name any of the four." That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing: *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Returns, saving, inheritance and scarce assets are all already held concepts in the economics frame; naming them is a **binding** onto described effects, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is treating one binding as *the* answer, or converting it into a share, without an independent anchor. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own §5b correction, reached independently from the README wording.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach and for this folder's own status

**Prestige is not action.** Quoted in section 4 above from the essay actually opened. A campaign that gathers names to agree with a dominant-cause claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets administrative ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From the same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any economics claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
