## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that large wealth differences are mainly caused by one of four things: unequal returns, unequal saving, inheritance, or ownership of scarce assets. Option A: accept the either/or and rank the four. Option B: keep the per-slot residual, treat the four as a union of overlapping conditions with separate anchors, and label the ranking a budget filter. What each leads to: A sends a frame-internal share as if it were a verdict and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, four written refuters, and an open ranking until an instrument closes it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Persistent positive wealth residuals at particular slots that a population average erases. Arity: four conditions in contact with one signal, not four values of one variable. Symmetry: the roles are asymmetric; returns and saving are per-period flows, inheritance is a generation-crossing transfer, scarce ownership is a stock/closure condition. Scale: within lifetime for flows, across cohorts for inheritance, closure boundary for assets, meta-level for measurement. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." and Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." together name it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, within-lifetime compounding.** Actors: savers, asset holders, rates. Currency: wealth as a spendable difference. Anchor: administrative wealth and tax records. Verdict: returns and saving are factors of a product in a cycle, not rival answers.
- **Domain 2, intergenerational transfer.** Actors: donors, heirs, cohorts. Currency: bequests and head starts. Anchor: estate and inheritance records. Verdict: a real distinct mechanism crossing a membrane; not a replay of a past fortune.
- **Domain 3, scarce-asset closure.** Actors: asset owners, asset supply, legal titles. Currency: claims on hard-to-replicate atoms. Anchor: asset supply, property and valuation records. Verdict: gates reachability for composition; the legal-title-to-atom mapping is a binding, not a derivation.
- **Domain 4, measurement and verdict.** Actors: researchers, datasets, estimators. Currency: variance shares and residuals. Anchor: independent fiscal and administrative data, which must not update on any model. Verdict: "mainly" is a filter, not a witness; ranking is a `gap`.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." D3 closure -> D2 transfer -> D1 flows -> D4 measurement. The temporal reading of this order is refused (see strain below).
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Intersections are real: Fagereng et al. find returns correlated with wealth and the permanent component mildly correlated across generations.

Strain test applied: the figure's "gradient" governs a spendable difference in a state/structure chain; using it for a wealth stock is a binding from the economics frame, declared as such in `01` and not promoted to a new primitive. No figure line was forced to fit. The temporal reading of the dependency order was refused after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Large wealth differences are a per-slot residual, not a population average. The four candidate sources are a union of overlapping conditions with separate anchors, never averaged: returns are a compounding cycle whose sign decides concentration, saving is the flow into the stock, inheritance is a head start across a generation membrane, and scarce-asset ownership is the closure that gates composition. The demand for a single "mainly" answer is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict; the figures return a shape and a discriminator, not a share. The ranking is a `gap` of the unreachable kind, recorded explicitly rather than left as silence. Mode `general` on the union, `specified` per domain, `grounded` on nothing here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a share as if it were a verdict; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The union verdict can be withdrawn without touching the figure census if a partition, a single-lever result, a ranking figure line, or a negative feedback sign is produced. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the four refuters. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** an instrument, not agreement. Independent fiscal, estate and wealth-tax records, or a natural experiment on transfers or asset supply that moves one condition while the others are observed, would name the load-bearing condition in a given case; a demonstration of empty intersections, a single-lever null across two independent datasets, a verbatim figure line ranking the four, or a zero/negative feedback sign would break the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
