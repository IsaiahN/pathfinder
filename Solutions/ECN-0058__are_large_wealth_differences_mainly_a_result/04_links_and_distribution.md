## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Returns are heterogeneous, persistent and correlated with the stock.** Fagereng et al. is the strongest single corroboration for F3: the 10th to 90th percentile return spread and the permanent individual component mean that a representative-agent return cannot settle a per-slot residual. It also intersects F5, because the permanent component is mildly correlated across generations.
2. **The conditions are explicitly non-exclusive.** The wealth overview's *"Because these mechanisms are non-exclusive, all three explanations could work together to compound the effect"* corroborates the union verdict from a different frame. Its land concentration and rent-seeking material bears on the scarce-asset condition.
3. **Cumulative advantage is not an economics-only shape.** The Matthew effect (sociology) and preferential attachment (network science) independently produce rich-get-richer dynamics and power-law tails. These are low-overlap witnesses for the compounding cycle. They are analogues: they corroborate the shape, they do not measure wealth, and their agreement with the figures is not evidence that the figures derived it.
4. **The anchor is administrative data.** WID and the Saez/Zucman provenance chain both point to fiscal and tax records as the instrument that can see the top tail. This is a measurement claim, not a causal one. It corroborates F8 without deciding which of the four conditions dominates.
5. **No source read supplies a figure ranking.** After reading all ten pages, none was found that could turn "mainly" into a dataset-free verdict. The slot stays a `gap` (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134). This is consistent with the figures and is recorded as such.

### Overlap warning

Fagereng et al., the Saez page, WID and the wealth overview draw on overlapping economics literature. They are at most a small number of witnesses, not four independent confirmations. The Matthew effect and preferential attachment pages are genuinely low overlap with the figures and with the economics pool, but they are encyclopedic summaries of their fields, so they corroborate a shape, not a measurement.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the complete framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot shape and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the union shape were true before giving them the economics literature.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A survey of expert opinion is a room, not an anchor.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot union shape and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first and is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 allows methods to cross. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a self-written refuter, because the loop cannot check the direction of its own question.
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The four refuters are therefore printed on the same page as the claim, before any links.
