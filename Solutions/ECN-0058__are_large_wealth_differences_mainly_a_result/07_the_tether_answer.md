# The Tether's answer on the sources of large wealth differences

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Large wealth differences are a persistent positive residual at particular wealth slots, not a population statistic. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The question asks which of four conditions mainly produces them. Fig 9 :4-5 requires that a bundle which does not shrink with effort be split rather than searched as one question, and Fig 9 :55 says such a bundle is usually a union with overlaps. The residual is therefore four conditions in contact with one per-slot signal, each with its own anchor, kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The four are a union, not a multiple choice.** Fig 9 :56: **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** A case with inheritance and scarce assets and high returns is a specific compound (Fig 9 :57-58), not a harder case of any one. No verdict is computed by averaging across the four.
2. **Unequal returns operate as a compounding cycle.** Fig 12 :121: **"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Wealth is a spendable difference (Fig 13 :22). A one-shot return on a fixed stock cannot close the top tail; the loop must re-enter and its sign must amplify.
3. **Unequal saving is a distinct flow link, not a rival answer.** Saving supplies the input to the stock; returns supply the rate. They are factors in a product. Their readings do not add into one "cause" (Fig 12 :125).
4. **Inheritance is a head start crossing a generation membrane.** Fig 4 :10: **"a head start, never a recording"**. Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** What crosses is something that makes the next search cheaper; a replay of a past success carries no new capability. Its anchor is estate and inheritance records, separate from the returns anchor.
5. **Ownership of scarce assets is the closure that gates composition.** Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** You can only compose returns over a claim you hold. A settled fortune becomes an atom for the next round (Fig 12 :104), which is the structural form of wealth begetting wealth. The mapping from "legal title" to "atom" is a binding, not a derivation (strain recorded in `01`, section 7).
6. **"Mainly" is a filter, not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** A variance share computed inside one closure is a device for allocating research attention. Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** The framework returns a shape and an instrument that would discriminate which condition is load-bearing; it does not return a ranking.
7. **The ranking slot is a `gap`, not silence.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. After a genuine re-census no figure line selects one of the four. That is reported as unreachable from the figures, and what is owed is an instrument, not another argument.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any one of the four is the main cause. The question's either/or is refused as a union.
- Not that a Gini coefficient or a top-one-percent share settles the per-slot residual. Those are aggregates (F1).
- Not that the framework derives Piketty's r > g or any particular elasticity. Those are world findings, imported with provenance, not figure outputs.
- Not that inheritance is a replay of a past fortune, or that scarce-asset ownership adds a primitive to the world (both readings retracted in `01`, section 6).
- Not that expert agreement can settle the share. A room is not an anchor (Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :35).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)` for the union claim.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument, not a further decomposition. Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** For this question the candidates are:

- **Independent administrative readings.** Fiscal, estate and wealth-tax records, used as anchors that do not update on the researcher's model. WID notes that surveys are *"based upon self-reporting and are well known to underestimate top incomes and top wealth shares"*, which is why the survey-only frame is insufficient.
- **Natural experiments on transfers and assets.** A change in inheritance taxation or asset supply that shifts one condition while the others are observed, giving the discriminating reading the shape requires.
- **Falsifier 1 (partition):** showing empty intersections among the four.
- **Falsifier 2 (single lever):** variation in one condition leaving the top-tail residual unchanged across two independent administrative datasets.
- **Falsifier 3 (figure):** producing a verbatim figure line that ranks the four.
- **Falsifier 4 (sign):** showing zero or negative feedback in the relevant wealth process.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, at the level of measurement.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :139: **"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"**. The six-term substrate and the figure rules are a candidate generator, not a final explanation of wealth inequality. Fig 7 names *"the limit: no last room"*; there is no terminal decomposition after which nothing further can be asked. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The figures README states *"Indiscernibility is not identity"*. Agreement among the sources read here is one pool talking, not corroboration, because they share the framework and much of the economics literature (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single main cause of large wealth differences, and it does not claim a variance share. It says that the residual must be read per wealth slot, because an average erases the live top-tail signal; that the four candidate causes are a union of overlapping conditions, not a multiple choice; that returns act as a compounding cycle whose sign decides concentration, saving feeds the stock, inheritance is a head start across a generation membrane, and scarce-asset ownership is the closure that decides what can be composed at all. Each condition has its own anchor and none is averaged with another. The demand for a single "mainly" answer is a filter for research budget, not a witness for a verdict, and the figures return a shape plus an instrument that would discriminate which condition is load-bearing. The ranking slot stays open as a declared `gap` until independent administrative data and natural experiments settle it.
