# Expert verification packet - the Tether on the sources of wealth inequality

**What this is:** a claim in a form a wealth-inequality researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the union/shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`.** No dataset result is claimed as settled by this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Large wealth differences must be read as a per-slot residual, not a population average. The four candidate sources are a union of overlapping conditions, not rival answers: returns are a compounding cycle over a stock, saving is the flow that feeds it, inheritance is a head start across a generation membrane, and ownership of scarce assets is the closure that decides what can be composed at all. Each has its own anchor, and no verdict is averaged across them. A variance share ranking the four is a filter for research budget, not a witness for a verdict; the figures return the shape and the instrument that would discriminate which condition is load-bearing, and they leave the ranking as a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 1

- **Statement:** the four options in the question form a union with nonempty intersections; splitting them into a multiple choice is the wrong operation, and "mainly" is a budget filter.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Fig 9 :56 - **"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** Fig 9 :60 - **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Fig 1 :19 - **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
- **What to check:** are the four conditions genuinely non-exclusive in the world you know, and does a single-lever decomposition leave an intersection standing? Fagereng et al. find returns positively correlated with wealth and the permanent return component mildly correlated across generations, which is an returns/inheritance intersection; the wealth overview states the mechanisms are *"non-exclusive"* and *"could work together to compound the effect"*.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a population in which each top-tail slot has exactly one active condition and all intersections are empty; or exhibit a variance share invariant across independent closures and anchored to a non-updating measurement.

### C2 - framework-internal plus physics-free structural check; checkable by reading Figures 12, 13, 4 and 6

- **Statement:** the returns account is a cycle whose sign decides concentration; inheritance is a head start across a membrane rather than a replay; scarce-asset ownership gates composition as a closure condition, and a settled fortune becomes an atom for the next round.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 - **"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** Fig 4 :10 - **"a head start, never a recording"**. Fig 6 :23 - **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 12 :104 - **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."**
- **What to check:** is a one-shot return on a fixed stock insufficient to sustain a top tail in your data, and do the intergenerational and asset-supply readings behave like separate conditions with separate anchors? The figure mapping of a legal title onto an atom is a **binding**, not a derivation; if you think that binding is wrong, say so. It is already recorded as a live strain.
- **Refuter:** a zero or negative feedback sign in the wealth process; or a case with no intergenerational transfer and no scarce-atom closure in which the top-tail residual is unchanged.

### C3 - on the verdict itself; checkable by reading Figures 8, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the demand for a single "mainly" answer is unreachable from the figures. The honest output is the union shape, its four refuters, and the kind of instrument that would close it. This is reported as `gap`, not silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134 - **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. Fig 9 :32 - **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.
- **What to check:** after reading the figure census in `01`, can you produce a verbatim figure line that selects one of the four conditions as the main cause? The primary seat could not.
- **Refuter:** the existence of such a line. It falsifies C3 immediately.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any one of the four is the main cause, and not that a Gini or top-share statistic settles the per-slot residual.
- Not that the framework derives r > g, any specific elasticity, or any empirical variance share. Those are world findings, imported with provenance where used.
- Not that inheritance is a replay of a past fortune, or that acquiring a scarce asset adds a primitive to the world. Both readings were retracted in `01` section 6 and the corrections are preserved.
- Not that agreement among researchers, or a panel of experts, can settle the share. Fig 7 :35 - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not a further argument. Fig 2 :29 - **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Concretely: independent fiscal, estate and wealth-tax records, and natural experiments on transfers or asset supply, used so that one condition moves while the others are observed. The three affirmative refuters in section 2 are written so that data can fire them. If none fires and the union shape survives, the remaining share question is still a `gap` until an instrument discriminates load-bearing conditions.
