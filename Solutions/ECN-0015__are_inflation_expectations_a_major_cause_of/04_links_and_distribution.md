## What the wider literature does with this residual

Three findings bear directly, and they do not agree:

1. **Cause leg supported, conditionally.** Moessner (2025) finds a significantly positive effect of professional survey expectations on inflation across 36 economies, larger when inflation is high. This is the per-slot result F5 in world form.
2. **Response leg supported.** Fair (2024) finds household expectations "mostly affected by current and past inflation" and treats the expectation variable in the price equation as possibly non-causal. This is F4 in world form.
3. **The identification problem is acknowledged.** Rudd (2021) reviews the same literature and finds the belief in a strong causal role rests on "extremely shaky foundations". Moessner writes there is "no consensus". This is F6 and F7 in world form.

Because these sources cite overlapping literatures, their disagreement is one pool with two readings, not two independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40). The figure verdict comes from the figures; the world sources are corroboration only.
