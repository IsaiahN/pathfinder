# The Tether's answer on inflation expectations and inflation

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the per-slot rule, `gap` on the magnitude. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We observe two series: stated inflation expectations `pi^e` and realised inflation `pi`. The question asks which one drives which, and whether the driving is "major". In loop terms `pi^e` is the belief `b`, "What the system takes to be the case before it acts." (Symbols table :20), "mixed by w. Not the observation." (Symbols table :21), and `pi` is the observation `o'`, "What actually happened." (Symbols table :32). The residual is the forecast error `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|`, measured per slot (Symbols table :38, :39; Fig 1 :19).

## 2. The shape

1. **The forced choice is malformed.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Both conditions hold: outturns update expectations, and expectations enter price setting. This is a union compound with its own loop, not one hard question (Fig 9 :5).
2. **The coupling is a signed cycle, not a one-way chain.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The cause leg is `b → a ⇒ o'` (belief precedes action, action produces outturn). The response leg is `o' → b` (outturn feeds the next belief). Operators table :36: "A produces B; B did not exist before".
3. **The two directional readings are isomers over the shared atoms.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." A flat list of ingredients cannot tell them apart; the bond carries the difference (Fig 12 :94, "The arrangement is the substance").
4. **The residual must be read per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Country, horizon, forecaster type and inflation regime are separate slots. The world literature agrees that the coupling is stronger in high-inflation slots (Moessner, 2025), but that is corroboration, not the figure verdict.
5. **"Major" is a magnitude, and the framework returns a shape.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The magnitude is a `gap` here: unreached, not answered "no" (Fig 8 :39).
6. **What would close it is an instrument, not another argument.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Existing surveys, panels, announcements and breakevens already return something that fails to resolve direction. Sharpen them with exogenous variation. Do not import a new theory of inflation (Fig 6 :24).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "expectations matter" as a verdict. That is a `specified` structural claim about a bond, with the magnitude unmeasured.
- Not "expectations do not matter". The response leg is not a denial of the cause leg; both are present in the cycle.
- Not a single coefficient, a share of variance, or a threshold for "major". Those are ground questions.
- Not that survey expectations, professional forecasts and market breakevens are interchangeable readings of `b`. They are different instruments and the question of which one reads the belief is a source-discrimination problem.
- Not that more agreement among economists settles anything. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not settled by this run. No instrument was operated.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not a better argument, resolved it. Here the projection is the correlation between expectations and later inflation, which can be produced by at least three paths (expectations cause prices, prices cause expectations, a common driver causes both). The corresponding questions are:

- **Exogenous shift test:** if beliefs are moved by randomised information or an unexpected announcement while costs and demand are held, do prices and wages move? This fires refuter R1 in `01` §4 if they do not.
- **Surprise update test:** if realised inflation surprises respondents, do stated expectations move by the predicted amount? This fires R2 if they do not.
- **Slot test:** does the coupling differ by country, horizon, forecaster type and inflation regime? This fires R3 if it does not.
- **Sign test:** does the loop damp in anchored regimes and reinforce in high-inflation regimes? A single-sign model would be refuted by a sign flip at fixed belief dynamics.
- **Source-discrimination test:** can one instrument distinguish a risk-premium shift from an expectations shift in market breakevens? The Cleveland Fed method page (2026) says its estimate uses yields, inflation data, swaps and surveys; the mixing is the problem.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instruments exist and return something; what they fail to resolve is direction and magnitude at this budget. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." So this answer stops at the shape, records the lineage, and hands the instrument question to the outside frame. The one live strain is recorded in `01` §6: whether a secondary report of a ground reading can count as a ground reading for this derivation. It cannot, because this seat did not operate the instrument.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in "expectations cause inflation" versus "expectations follow inflation", and it does not claim a magnitude. It says the question is a union compound that must be split: expectations are a belief held before price and wage setting, so they enter the action that produces realised inflation, and the belief is formed from past outcomes, so realised inflation feeds back. The coupling is a signed cycle, not a one-way chain, and the two directional readings are isomers over the same three atoms, different arrangements, different behaviour. The error signal has to be read per slot, by country, horizon, forecaster type and regime, because averaging across slots is how the live signal disappears. What the framework cannot supply is the word "major": that is a magnitude owed to an instrument that moves expectations exogenously and measures the response. The shape, the refuters written in advance and the instrument specification are offered; the number stays open until the ground settles it.
