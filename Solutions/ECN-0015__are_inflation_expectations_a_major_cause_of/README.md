## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before running the derivation I wrote down what a candidate answer must contain, so the sought-for shape could accrue a residual like any other term (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-64). A satisfactory answer must: (1) say whether the directed coupling between expected inflation and realised inflation is one-way or a loop; (2) say at what scale the coupling is to be read (aggregate or per slot); (3) name what would have to be measured before the word "major" could be asserted; (4) state what would refute it. Anything that supplies only (1) as a bare A-or-B verdict is incomplete by this standard.

## The answer in one line

The forced choice is refused as written. Expectations and realised inflation stand in a **signed cycle** (Fig 12 :121), the question is a **union compound** that must be unbundled (Fig 9 :54), the residual must be read **per slot** (Fig 1 :19, :22), and the magnitude question is a **gap** here because it needs an instrument that exogenously moves expectations, not a further argument (Fig 6 :32; Fig 9 :32).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict when the frames share a pool.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list of economists selected for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **An anchor cannot be a party.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The CPI print, not the number of endorsements, is the anchor for "what actually happened".
3. **Send the generator, not the recording.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Recipients get the shape claim and its refuter first. The theory and the links come only after their verdict on stage 1 is recorded.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional submission venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
