## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not supply the number. For ECN-0015 the shape is:

- a **signed cycle** rather than a one-way chain;
- a **union compound** that must be split into D1, D2 and D3;
- a residual that is **per slot**, not averaged;
- an **open slot** where the magnitude and its identifying instrument would go.

`pi^e = 0.6 * pi + ...` is a filled number. "The coupling is a signed cycle read per slot, and the magnitude awaits an exogenous instrument" is a shape. Only the second is licensed by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- The same essay names the failure mode this question exhibits: "You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." A question bundled from "cause" and "response" must be split before it is searched.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." For this question the effect description is: shift expectations exogenously and watch prices; surprise people with an inflation print and watch their expectations.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place": and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here that means: once you have specified the instrument, the shape and the instrument coincide.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. For economics this matters: a macro model's state variables are a chosen compression, and declaring them does not make them neutral.

The same essay: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

## 4. Why "major" is the forbidden kind of question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the projection is a correlation between survey expectations and later inflation. Several paths produce it: expectations cause prices; past prices cause expectations; a common driver (demand, money, supply shocks) causes both. The correlation cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger coefficient. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and **indiscernibility is not identity.**"

## 5. The per-slot rule in the essays

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* cites the figure directly: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." It then reports that in group tasks "The arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'" For this question: the arrangement of belief, action and observation matters more than which clever forecaster is asked. The same essay notes that "Selection between rooms is where the ground votes", which is why the three domains D1, D2 and D3 must be checked separately rather than blended.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the stopping rule: "This process isn't about reaching the truth. It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it. That's operational, and it's the only version that doesn't require knowing the answer in advance." Applied here: the derivation stops at the shape because no instrument in this run registers a difference between the cause and response readings.

## 6. Q answered: what is the shape for ECN-0015?

**Nothing in the framework returns the magnitude.** The shape is the signed cycle, the union split, the per-slot residual and the open instrument slot. A particular number is licensed only by a ground reading with exogenous variation, or by a provenanced import with a stated source gate and shadow test. Until then, any "major" claim is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Not if it stays at the shape. If it were to name a coefficient as *the* answer of the framework, it would be naming a quantity the framework does not return, and doing so from a projection that cannot discriminate among sources (the metamerism error of §4).

## 7. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first drafted §3 as "the framework returns a shape, so it cannot name any quantity at all". That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive or quantity not held in the library. Beliefs, actions and price observations are all already in the library of this question. Naming them is a binding from the economics frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is treating that binding as a derivation, or claiming the magnitude follows from it. Both versions are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected names to agree with the cycle claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried by people who can refute it, and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. The architecture article adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the economic claim, must be labelled as such, and cannot settle anything it reports on.
