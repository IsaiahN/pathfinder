## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about two quantities: stated inflation expectations `pi^e` and realised inflation `pi`. Option A: expectations are a major cause of inflation, so moving beliefs moves prices and wages. Option B: expectations are mostly a response to price changes, so moving prices moves beliefs. What each leads to: A licenses a policy story in which anchoring expectations controls inflation but supplies no magnitude here; B licenses a story in which expectations are a lagging indicator and supplies no magnitude here. The word "major" asks for a number. The prior question is whether the either/or framing is well formed at all. Answer shape committed before the run, bound as an ordinary term: a directed coupling plus a scale plus an instrument plus a refuter (Formula "Direction").

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The forecast error `R_s = |Gamma(b,a) - o'|` between expected and realised inflation, per slot. Arity: the question frames two terms but the mechanism has three (belief, action, observation), with feedback. Symmetry: the bonds are ordered, sequence, production and comparison; the coupling need not be symmetric. Scale: first order inside a period (belief to action to outturn), second order across periods (outturn re-enters the belief). Fig 9 :54 names a union compound, so this is not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, belief formation.** Actors: households, firms, professional forecasters, survey respondents. Currency: stated or formed expectations. Anchor: the survey response is a legitimate anchor for *stated* expectations only; for the true belief there is no direct anchor in this run. Question type: measurement, not causality.
- **Domain 2, price and wage setting.** Actors: firms, unions, workers. Currency: prices and wages, the action. Anchor: realised inflation from a price index, which does not move when a frame changes its mind. Question type: production.
- **Domain 3, verification and identification.** Actors: econometricians, central banks, survey designers. Currency: residuals and identification strategies. Anchor: an exogenous shift in expectations that moves the belief without moving costs or demand. This anchor is specified in shape but was not operated here, so the domain is `candidate`.
- **Partition or union:** union. Both conditions are present and overlap (Fig 9 :54).
- **Order by dependency:** distinctness of terms, then per-slot residual, then bond and sign, then magnitude (Fig 3 :19). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced choice is refused as written. The coupling between inflation expectations and realised inflation is a **signed cycle** over a **union compound**: the belief enters the price-setting action that produces the outturn, and the outturn re-enters to update the belief. The two directional readings are isomers over the shared atoms. The error signal must be read per slot, by country, horizon, forecaster type and inflation regime. The word "major" is a magnitude, and no figure line returns a magnitude, so it is a `gap` here, unreached rather than answered "no". What would close it is an exogenous instrument, not a further argument or more agreement. All structural claims are `specified` or `general` and none is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the union reading is wrong, the three domains were separable and the refuter R3 (same sign and magnitude in every slot) would detect it. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a number or a request for agreement instead of a shape and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong" prices that cost. It is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the three refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No atom was imported and no primitive was withdrawn. The shape can be refined per slot, the sign can be filled by an instrument, and a magnitude can be added later, without retracting the cycle. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An exogenous expectations shift with no price response kills the cause leg; inflation surprises with no expectation update kill the response leg; identical sign and magnitude in every slot kill the per-slot rule; a figure line governing magnitude, or an instrument reporting the attributable share, closes the gap. A source-discriminating instrument separating risk premia from beliefs would also change the D1 anchor. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
