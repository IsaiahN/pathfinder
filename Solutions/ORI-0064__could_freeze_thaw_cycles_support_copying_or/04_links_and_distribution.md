## 3. What the wider literature actually does and does not establish

The fetched sources support three laboratory facts and no field fact.

1. Freezing concentrates solutes in the eutectic brine, and the concentration can be measured (Budin and Szostak 2010; Attwater et al. 2010; Kanavarioti et al. 2001).
2. Activation, bridged-intermediate formation and nonenzymatic copying can be made compatible in one cycling mixture (Zhang et al. 2022; Ding et al. 2023).
3. Ribozyme assembly and strand propagation can be driven by repeated cycling in vesicles (Salibi et al. 2023; Attwater et al. 2025).

They do **not** establish that any particular early Earth site ran the required amplitude and chemistry. The only geological bound read in this pass is Root-Bernstein and Brown's remark that cycles *"may have begun soon after the Earth cooled sufficiently to support prebiotic chemistry some 4 billion years ago or more"*. That is a bound from geology and astronomy, not a site measurement. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 9 :42 says *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. The honest report for the site remains `gap`.

## 5. Distribution rules

The figure-grounded reasons are already recorded in `02_outreach_email.md`. They are repeated here as operating rules.

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the four-condition split and the refuter. Do not attach the framework until the recipient has recorded a verdict on the shape claim.
2. **No request for agreement.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A reply that says *"I agree"* is not evidence, and a reply that says *"this fails because..."* is.
3. **Methods cross up.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* If stage 3 happens, hand over the formula as a method with the recipient's own refuter written first, not a recording of this run.
4. **One to one.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* No bulk send. A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement (Fig 2 :40).
5. **Venues, not people.** Use only the institutional and opt-in channels in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. No personal emails, no prestige ranking, nofollow-up pressure. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
6. **Keep the chemistry and the shape apart.** Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Any named nucleotide or yield travels with its author, venue and year. It is never presented as a figure derivation.
7. **Never send `08` without `01`.** The verification packet depends on the retraction log and the pre-registered refuter in the figure-grounded audit. Sending it alone invites a reader to grade the framework's interior rather than the claim (Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*).

## 6. The two-stage offer, in the order it must be sent

**Stage 1 (claim and refuter).** Send the three-line structural claim from `02` plus the four refuters. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer before any theory is shown.

**Stage 2 (opt-in theory).** Only if the recipient asks, send the local figure extract and the canonical usage rule, not a summary. Tell them the quotes, not the audit, are the authority. A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3 (method).** Offer the eight-step loop for the recipient's own problem, with their own pre-registered falsifier. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

**Hard precondition.** Do not send anything until the four conditions remain separate in the text. If the offer collapses concentration, assembly, copying and site into one *"freeze-thaw helps"*, stage 1 has already failed and the rest of the distribution plan is void.
