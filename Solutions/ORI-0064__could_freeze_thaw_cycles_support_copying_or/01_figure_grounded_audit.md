## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks whether a freeze-thaw cycle can do work on genetic molecules. Stated as an effect, the residual is: dilute, chemically reluctant building blocks do not by themselves form long, information-bearing polymers, and a polymer that forms does not by itself make another copy of itself. What is being decided is whether an environmental temperature cycle supplies the missing work. Four distinct effects hide inside that one sentence:

1. **Concentration.** Solutes excluded from growing ice crystals collect in a thin liquid brine, raising local concentration by a large factor.
2. **Assembly.** Activated monomers or short oligomers form phosphodiester or analogous bonds without an evolved enzyme (non-templated polymerisation, splinted or templated ligation).
3. **Copying.** A template directs the ordered addition of complementary units, so the product can serve as a template on a later cycle.
4. **Loop closure and environment.** The product must survive the thaw, re-enter as input, and the cycle must be a real environmental regime rather than a laboratory convenience.

These are not one problem with one answer. Fig 9 :54 says *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57 says *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. A single lever that clears concentration leaves the copying condition bound.

**The sought-for shape, bound in advance as an ordinary term** (Formula, "Direction": write down what an answer would look like and let it accrue a residual). The shape we are looking for is: *a cycle is a structure whose output re-enters as input; it closes only if the product survives the thaw; a directed difference is spent to concentrate solutes; and a habitat condition enumerated from the world, not composed, supplies the cycling.* This is a shape, not a chemistry. It is written here before the search so it can be refused by the same test as everything else.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** four conditions (concentration, assembly, copying, loop closure/environment). This is a **union compound**, not a partition. The overlap is not a rounding error: concentration is what makes assembly cheap, assembly is what supplies the oligomers that catalyse copying, and copying is what closes the loop.
- **Symmetry:** the cycle is an **ordered** alternation, freeze then thaw then freeze. It is not a symmetric join. Templated copying is also ordered: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :72, sequence). The concentration step has directedness, which is a gradient, not a mere distribution (Fig 13 :22, *"a difference that can be spent"*).
- **Scale:** molecular (monomers, oligomers, brine veins) up to environmental (seasonal or diurnal temperature cycles). This is a cross-scale claim, so Fig 4 requires a measured round trip rather than an assumed identity: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 Eutectic physical chemistry** | water, ice crystals, solutes, ions, brine channels | concentration factor, water activity, temperature | yes: measured phase behaviour, microscopy, conductivity, freezing-point depression | partition (which solutes enter the brine) |

### 1.3 Partition or union

Union. The four conditions overlap. Verdicts will be kept per domain and never averaged into a single yes or no.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

D1 concentration precedes D2 assembly, which precedes D3 copying, which precedes D4 closure in a real environment. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :22: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can."* A reading of "did copying happen" taken before asking whether solutes concentrated is a reading of nothing. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 3. Findings F1 to F10

### F1: The bundle is a union, not one yes/no question (decisive for the framing)

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A single answer to "could freeze-thaw support copying or assembly" would aggregate four slots. The world literature itself shows the split: eutectic concentration is a physical effect measured by phase behaviour (Budin and Szostak 2010), nonenzymatic copying is a chemical yield measured by gels (Zhang et al. 2022; Ding et al. 2023), and ribozyme replication over repeated cycles is yet another anchor (Attwater et al. 2010; Attwater et al. 2025). These have different anchors and cannot be averaged.

**Refuter:** show that all four conditions share one anchor and one currency, so a single measurement settles them together. Until then, verdicts stay separate.

### F2: The figure's cycle governs loop closure, not the temperature oscillation

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 13 :31: *"each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next"*. Fig 13 :28: *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."*

The first-pass reading, "the figures license freeze-thaw as a cycle, therefore freeze-thaw can replicate molecules", strains. The figure line is about a **loop whose output re-enters as input**, not about any physical oscillation. A freezer that is switched on and off is an alternation (Fig 13 :28) but closes no chemical loop unless the product survives the thaw and can serve again. The sign condition in Fig 12 :121 is the real test: the loop closes only if the product is retained rather than degraded or lost. Root-Bernstein and Brown (2022) report exactly this asymmetry: cycles *"preferentially degrade some RNA and DNA sequences in preference to others"*. Salibi et al. (2023) show that in their vesicle system the ribozyme is nearly inactive during frozen and thawing conditions and replication occurs predominantly in the aqueous interval, and that repeated cycling causes content loss. So the closure condition is a live experimental quantity, not a metaphor.

**Re-census result:** the *cycle* line governs D3 and D4 (loop closure), not D1. Re-census done; strain recorded as S1 in section 7 and kept visible.

**Refuter:** a controlled experiment in which the product of one freeze-thaw round is demonstrably the input template of the next, with the product isolated and sequenced, would **confirm** the figure reading; a demonstration that no molecule survives the thaw in a cycling regime would break the closure claim at the ground.

### F3: Concentration is a spendable directed difference, not a name of a molecule

Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"* (gradient). Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"* (spectrum). Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

Ice exclusion creates a directed chemical-potential difference that can be spent on condensation. The figures license that shape. They do **not** select which molecule occupies the slot. The canonical reading is explicit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* (figures README, canonical local copy). Naming phosphorimidazolides, imidazolium-bridged dinucleotides or triphosphate triplets is a binding from the chemistry frame onto a held structural slot, provenanced as such (Fig 8 :50, *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*). It is not a figure derivation and it adds no atom: Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*

**Refuter:** derive a specific nucleotide activation chemistry from the six-term set alone, with no chemistry-frame import. If that succeeds, the shape-not-a-name limit was too strong here. It has not been done in this simulated run.

### F4: Temperature and season are habitat conditions enumerated from the world

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :46: *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"*. Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*

Freeze-thaw is not composed out of the six terms. It is read off the world as a condition acting on the residual. Its valence is relative to the residual: cold protects RNA from hydrolysis and concentrates solutes in D1 and D2 (Attwater et al. 2010; Budin and Szostak 2010), while the same cycling degrades selected sequences and ruptures vesicles in D3 and D4 (Root-Bernstein and Brown 2022; Ding et al. 2023). There is no standing list of good conditions. This is why a blanket "freeze-thaw helps" is disqualified: it fixes a valence that the framework says is relative.

Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A better tube protocol that never reaches a real cycling environment changes no contact and therefore settles nothing about the environmental question.

**Refuter:** show that a freeze-thaw regime has the same valence for concentration, assembly, copying and compartment retention, independent of the residual. The cited experiments contradict that, so the refuter is live and currently fired against the blanket claim.

### F5: The loop needs a retained product and a comeback path

Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*

A ribozyme that survives its own use is a catalyst in the figure's sense. A template that is consumed in copying is a spent part. Attwater et al. (2010) show the R18 polymerase ribozyme retains activity in ice for weeks and its half-life rises from under 52 hours at 17 °C to over 16 days at −7 °C, which is a catalyst-survival result. Attwater et al. (2025) show product strands from one cycle being used as templates in the next, which is the settled-molecule-becomes-atom rule at work. Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* The precursor pool and the copied pool are not variants of one thing under the figure's definition; the bond arrangement changed.

**Refuter:** if no settled product can serve as a template in a subsequent cycle, or if the catalyst is spent rather than regenerated, the loop is a chain and cannot close (Fig 12 :121).

### F6: The ground for this question is experiment, not agreement among papers

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

The world sources here are experiments with gels, HPLC traces and sequencing. That is the right kind of anchor. But they share an evidence pool: Attwater et al. 2010, Attwater et al. 2025, Zhang et al. 2022, Ding et al. 2023 and Salibi et al. 2023 are linked by authors, institutions, reference lists and the same round of RNA-world experimentation. Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Two papers quoting the same review are one witness, not two. The genuinely lower-overlap corroborator is the physical-chemistry and habitat framing in Budin and Szostak 2010 and the critical apparatus review in Root-Bernstein and Brown 2022, which is not from the Szostak or Holliger groups.

**Refuter:** show two independent groups using different readout chemistry, different starting materials and no shared review chain reaching the same closure result. Until then, the world verdict is one pool with internal consistency, not multiple corroboration.

### F7: Instruments, not more arguments, extend what can be represented

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

The specific prebiotic site is the gap. No instrument in this folder can read the temperature cycle at a particular Hadean pond. The honest move is to say what a discriminator would have to be, not to invent a site. Root-Bernstein and Brown (2022) note that freeze-thaw cycles *"may have begun soon after the Earth cooled sufficiently to support prebiotic chemistry some 4 billion years ago or more"*, which is a bound from geology and astronomy, not a measurement of a site.

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A failure to find a cycling site in the rock record is not proof that none existed. This is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, distinct from underivable in principle (Fig 8 :40-43).

**Refuter:** an instrument or geological reconstruction that returns the cycle regime at a named site would close the gap. None was read in this run.

### F8: Filters are for the budget, witnesses are for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The number of papers cited, the impact factor of the journal, and the number of cycles run in a lab protocol are budget filters. They decide what gets tried first; they do not decide whether a template copied. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

**Refuter:** if a verdict in this folder is found to rest on citation count, author prestige or the number of agreeing reviews rather than on a measured yield, finding or refuter, it is withdrawn.

### F10: The cross-kind composition rule is not violated by "replicate"

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."*

A first pass might try to force "replicate" onto the six-term set as a direct bond between two states or two structures. That would strain. ## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before the run, per the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim in this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Closure fails at the ground.** A controlled freeze-thaw experiment shows that the product of one round cannot serve as template or catalyst in the next, across independent labs and readouts (breaks F2, F5 and Fig 12 :121).
2. **No directed concentration step.** A measurement shows that the relevant solutes are not enriched in the interstitial brine under any cycling regime that also permits bonding (breaks F3 and the D1 anchor).
3. **Valence is fixed, not relative.** A freeze-thaw regime is shown to have identical valence for concentration, assembly, copying and compartment retention regardless of the residual (breaks F4 and Fig 11 :49).
4. **Arrangement is not the difference.** Precursor and copied pools are shown to differ only by ingredients, not by typed bond arrangement (breaks F5 and Fig 12 :117).
5. **The substrate rule breaks.** A direct state-state or structure-structure bond is admitted and cannot be refused, or a seventh primitive is derived from the other six (breaks F10 and Fig 13 :74-75, :41).
6. **The figure frame claims a chemistry verdict.** Any sentence in this folder asserts a specific molecule or yield as settled by the figures rather than by an experiment (breaks F3 and the mode rule).
7. **The site gap is filled by invention.** A specific Hadean site is named without an instrument or geological reading (breaks F7).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is retracted in this log rather than quietly edited. Until one is run, no figure-derived part of this answer is labelled `grounded`. The world claims are grounded only for the lab protocols actually measured by the cited authors, and that grounding is theirs, not the framework's.

## 8. Summary verdict and the honest stop

The figures return a **shape**, not a chemistry. That shape is stable across this simulated run: a cycling temperature condition enumerated from the habitat; a directed difference spent on concentration before bonding; a loop that closes only when the product survives the thaw and re-enters as input; and catalysts that survive their own use. The six-term composition rule is not violated and no seventh primitive is owed. All of this is `general` or `specified` and ungrounded by the figures.

The world experiments supply the chemistry grounding for the **lab protocols**: concentration factors above 200-fold (Attwater et al. 2010; Budin and Szostak 2010), activation and nonenzymatic copying in one frozen mixture (Zhang et al. 2022; Ding et al. 2023), ribozyme replication and partial self-replication under cycling (Attwater et al. 2010; Attwater et al. 2025), and ribozyme assembly plus content exchange in vesicles (Salibi et al. 2023). That grounding is the authors', measured with their instruments; this simulated derivation did not replicate it and does not claim to.

The specific prebiotic site, and the question of whether these cycles actually ran with the right amplitude and chemistry at the origin of life, is a **`gap`**. Unreachable at this budget is the honest report (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. The ground here is a measurement, not another argument and not an increase in the number of agreeing papers.
