## VERDICT (from the figures)

The bundle is a union, so the verdict has four parts and they are not averaged. A directed difference can be spent to concentrate dilute solutes before bonding becomes cheap (`general`, triangulated). Assembly is production and copying is ordered production over a template; a chemical loop closes only if the product survives the thaw and re-enters as input (`specified`, triangulated for closure, single-source for the catalyst and isomer claims). The valence of cold is relative to the residual, so the same cycling that protects and concentrates in one condition degrades and ruptures in another (`specified`, single-source on the figure side, world-corroborated by measurement). The specific prebiotic site and the specific reagent are a `gap` and an import respectively: the figures return a shape and provenance rules, not a molecule or a pond. The fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim is live. Nothing here is `grounded` by the framework. This is a candidate, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its four refuters and its overlap before any theory is shown, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a protocol with no refuter) instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is collapsing the four conditions and publishing a single "freeze-thaw helps" sentence, which would discard the destructive results that are already measured.
- **undoable:** yes. The site gap is reported rather than filled, and no primitive has to be withdrawn if a refuter fires. A superseded packet carries its retraction in `01` section 6 rather than silently erasing the earlier reading. The D4 gap can be closed by a measurement without changing the D1 to D3 shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A geological or astronomical reconstruction that returns the cycling regime at a named site would close D4. A controlled experiment showing no product retention across rounds would break the closure claim. A measurement showing no solute enrichment under any bonding-permitting regime would break the concentration claim. A demonstration that valence is fixed rather than residual-relative would break Fig 11 :49. A source-discriminating instrument that names the activation route would convert an import into a grounded binding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
