# Expert verification packet: the Tether on freeze-thaw cycles and genetic molecules

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist, astrobiologist or origins-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific prebiotic site. Not `grounded` by this derivation.** No new measurement is claimed here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "could freeze-thaw cycles support copying or assembly of genetic molecules?" is a union of four conditions with different anchors: concentration of dilute solutes, chemical assembly of oligomers, templated copying whose product re-enters as input, and the environmental reality of a cycling site. The framework returns a shape, not a chemistry and not a site. The shape is: a directed difference can be spent on concentration before bonding becomes cheap; a cycle closes only if its product survives the thaw and can serve again; a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, while a spent part is not; and the valence of cold is relative to the residual, so the same cycling that protects and concentrates in one condition degrades and ruptures in another. The laboratory literature has measured the first three under specific protocols. No figure line governs the fourth, which is reported as `unreachable at this budget`, not as silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The union framing and the dependency order (framework-internal; checkable against the figures)

- **Statement:** concentration, assembly, copying and site are four conditions with different anchors; effort spent above a broken link cannot rescue one whose input never arrives. A single averaged verdict is a category error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 3 :19, *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- **What to check:** are the four anchors genuinely different objects (phase behaviour, product yield, copied sequence, geological setting)? If they collapse to one measurement, the framing fails.
- **Refuter:** show a single instrument that settles all four at once, or show that one condition's verdict determines the others. If that holds, the union claim is withdrawn.

### C2: Loop closure is the figure's cycle, not the temperature oscillation (framework-internal plus ground-facing)

- **Statement:** a freeze-thaw oscillation is an alternation. It becomes a replicating cycle only if the product survives the thaw and re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121, *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 13 :31, *"each turn leaves a remainder (the residual) that re-enters the next"*. Fig 13 :12, *"the ordered succession of differences"*. Fig 12 :119, *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*
- **What to check:** Attwater et al. 2010 (ribozyme half-life over 16 days in ice), Attwater et al. 2025 (products of one cycle used as templates in the next), Salibi et al. 2023 (content exchange and serial dilution) are offered as **world corroboration only**. Do their readouts actually show product re-entry, or only accumulation in one pot? Root-Bernstein and Brown 2022 and Ding et al. 2023 report the destructive side: preferential degradation and vesicle rupture.
- **Refuter:** a controlled experiment in which the product of one round cannot serve as template or catalyst in the next, across independent labs and readouts; or a demonstration that a one-pot accumulation was misreported as iterative replication. Either breaks C2.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Closure test:** product of one round isolated and shown to serve in the next, across independent labs and readouts.
2. **Source-discriminating readout:** a measurement that separates bridged-dinucleotide, monomer-bridged-oligonucleotide and triplet-invasion mechanisms rather than reading one gel band as three causes.
3. **Compartment compatibility:** product retention and membrane integrity measured through the same cycles that drive activation.
4. **Site reconstruction:** a geological or astronomical reading returning the cycle regime at a named setting. That closes the D4 gap.
