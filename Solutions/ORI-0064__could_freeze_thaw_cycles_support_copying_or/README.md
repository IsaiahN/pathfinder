# Solution ORI-0064

**Question:** Could freeze-thaw cycles support copying or assembly of genetic molecules?

**Source:** origins of life.txt line 90

**Section:** 7. Energy, environment, and chemical cycles

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific prebiotic site and on a figure-selected chemistry. Not `grounded` by this derivation. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What is being decided, in effect terms

The question bundles four conditions that do not share one anchor: (1) concentration of dilute monomers by ice exclusion, (2) chemical bond formation or assembly of oligomers, (3) templated copying whose product re-enters as a template on the next cycle, and (4) the environmental reality of a cycling site on early Earth. Option A says yes across the bundle; option B says the bundle must be split and the verdicts kept separate, with the figure frame returning only a shape and the ground (laboratory and geological measurement) returning the chemistry verdict. This solution takes B. What a figure-derived answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term in advance: a cycle is a structure whose output re-enters as input; it closes only if the product survives the thaw; a directed difference (a gradient) is spent to concentrate solutes; and a habitat condition (temperature, season) enumerates the cycle from the world rather than composing it. That bound term is stated in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 and is allowed to accrue a residual like anything else.

## Boundary

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle a chemistry question. They return a shape; the ground settles the chemistry.
- No universal cycle frequency, temperature, concentration factor or yield. Those are instruments and measurements, not figure derivations.

The reason for the first three is structural, not merely legal: Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :42 says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting people for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies their agreement (Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
