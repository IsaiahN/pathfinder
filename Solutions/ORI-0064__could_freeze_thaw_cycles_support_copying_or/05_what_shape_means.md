## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

*The Library of Babel* states the search-side version: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the recursion: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For ORI-0064 this means the framework can return: a cycle whose output re-enters as input, a directed difference spent on concentration, a habitat condition enumerated from the world, a catalyst that survives its own use. It cannot return: a particular activating agent, a particular temperature, a particular yield, a particular pond. Those are names from the chemistry and geology frames.

A **shape** here is therefore the answer's constraint set, not a filler. `SPACE(???)TIME` is the exemplar's shape. Ours is `DILUTE ---(directed concentration)---> BONDABLE ---(loop closure)---> COPIED`, with the slots for molecule, temperature and site left open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the *"closest"* known vocabulary, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse; the problem *"determines what makes a book relevant to you or not"*.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the two-stage outreach in `02_outreach_email.md`: state what you would expect to see if the shape claim were true, before showing the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the shape is stable, but the site is not reached, so the stop is an honest gap rather than a finish line.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is HPLC, gel and sequencing data from the cited labs. Their names (2-aminoimidazole, methyl isocyanide, trinucleotide triphosphates) are grounded for their protocols by their measurements, not by the framework.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). Naming a molecule from the chemistry frame is an import of a binding onto a held structural slot. It adds no atom to the world and does not move the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also warns that *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So even the six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Applying it to a chemical question is an act of framing, and the framing must be recorded as such.

## 4. Source discrimination: why the figure frame cannot name the chemistry

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The essay's formal statement: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. And the remedy: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

This is directly relevant. A gel band reading "extended primer" is a projection. Several mechanistic routes (bridged dinucleotide, monomer-bridged oligonucleotide, triphosphate triplet invasion) can produce similar bands. The figure frame reads the band and cannot name the source. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, which the cited authors built for their own systems, not a name from the framework.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the lineage condition: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* This is why `10_source_ledger.csv` records who read what and when, and why `09_watson_answer.md` declares overlap instead of hiding it.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a molecule here? ANSWERED, with a correction kept visible

**My first-pass reading was too strong.** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming a specific molecule in this answer. That is wrong. The README's phrase governs *"a missing thing"*, a primitive not held in Γ. Naming something that already exists in the chemistry frame is a binding from another frame onto a structural slot, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a seventh primitive and it is not banned. What remains banned is claiming the figure frame derived it or that the binding moves the wall.

Visible correction, not erased: the overstatement appeared in the preliminary outline of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section F3 and was narrowed after the canonical check. The narrowed form stands in F3 and in the retraction log of that file.

**Does this folder commit the error?** No molecule is named as a figure output. Every named chemical is attributed to an author, a venue and a year in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`, and is marked as a binding or an import. The D4 site is left as a `gap`.

## 6. Capability, habitat and the unit of verdict

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* quotes Fig 11 directly: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* For ORI-0064 the analogue is that a chemical system's capacity to copy is a property of the molecule-and-habitat. The same activating chemistry behaves differently in a dilute bulk solution and in a eutectic brine; the same ice matrix protects a ribozyme and ruptures a vesicle. This is the structural reason the four conditions of the census cannot be averaged.

The same essay gives the method rule: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A lab protocol is a method when the binding and the refuter travel with it; it is a recording when only the temperature and duration travel. The latter produces a system that repeats one occasion and cannot produce a new one.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the governing rule: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a ground that pays or does not.

The same essay refuses self-validation: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."* And it names the status of this folder exactly: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*; the architecture article adds that such solutions *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

Therefore: `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file and `06_isomers_and_order.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not tests of the claim, they did not run an experiment, and they cannot settle the question they report on. Their value is the shape, the refuters and the lineage.

## 8. What the shape reading yields for this question

| level | what the essays and figures license | mode |
|---|---|---|
| structural | a cycle closes only if output re-enters as input; a directed difference can be spent on concentration; habitat conditions are enumerated | `general` |
| per domain | concentration is real but physical; assembly and copying have different anchors; compartments conflict with cycling; site is unread | `specified` |
| naming | specific chemistries are imported bindings with provenance, never figure outputs | `specified`, ungrounded by the framework |
| site and yield | no figure line governs them; instruments and measurements are owed | `gap` / unreached |

The shape is refusable. If a reader can show a cycle with no retained product, or a valence that does not depend on the residual, the shape fails on the terms written in `01` section 4. A reported failure is more useful to me than an endorsement.
