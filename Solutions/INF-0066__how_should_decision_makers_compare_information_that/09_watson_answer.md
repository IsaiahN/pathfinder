## QUESTION

How should decision-makers compare information that reduces uncertainty with information that changes what actions are possible? Restated as an effect: given `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot, what is lost when an item that keeps the same atoms and wall is priced on the same scale as an item that moves the wall, changes contact or removes a term's standing to be composed over? Option A: add both into one value-of-information number. Option B: classify by effect on the reach, keep two ledgers with no exchange rate, report per slot, and order SEARCHED before INWARD and OUTWARD. A gives a budget tool the standing of a verdict and hides the shortfall; B gives a checkable shape with refuters written in advance and an open `???` where the common scale would be.

Anchor: the ground, *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"* (Fig 2 :29). Status: admitted. Question type: partition (which compositions stay inside the closure).
- **D2 Reach and action-space change.** Actors: frames with differing closures, imported primitives, instruments, habitat actors and relations. Currency: atoms added or standing removed, contact changed, actions priced in actions. Anchor: the ground plus provenance and a shadow test for imports (Fig 6 :12-13). Status: admitted. Question type: union compound, since import, instrument extension and refusal are overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones.
- **D3 Valuation and comparison.** Actors: the decision-maker, two ledgers, the verifier. Currency: bits for the description, actions for finding out whether it holds. Anchor: the ground, never an aggregate or an audience's agreement. Status: admitted. Question type: compound accounting layer.
- **X1 one market price independent of users and uses.** No nameable anchor once users and context are removed. Not a domain yet. Glynn et al. 2022 explicitly doubt it.
- **X2 audience receptiveness as a proxy for value.** No anchor: selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation it would measure (Fig 2 :40). Not a domain, forbidden as a verdict input.
- **Order by dependency:** characterize the residual in effect terms (Fig 9 :27-28), refuse before mint (Formula step 2), search before import (Formula step 7), settle before composing (Fig 12 :104).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The two-ledger shape survives; the single-scalar answer is refused. U is within-closure search with a definitional ceiling (Fig 8 :32), reported per slot (Fig 1 :19). A is import, instrument extension or refusal and is the only kind that changes the reach (Fig 6 :14, Fig 5 :55). Bits and actions are held apart with no exchange rate (Fig 12 :125-130), SEARCHED precedes INWARD and OUTWARD (Formula step 7), refusal precedes mint (Formula step 2), and any computed VoI number is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60). The exact weights and any exchange rate are a declared `gap`: an instrument that discriminates the sources behind one value reading would be required. This is a `general` structural result and a `specified` decision-making application, both ungrounded and therefore `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** if the two-ledger shape is wrong, decision-makers keep a needless accounting split and under-use a legitimate common metric; real budgets would be spent on classification instead of measurement. The cost is bounded and locatable because the claim states its own refuters and its lineage. The larger avoidable cost runs the other way: presenting a single VoI number as a verdict spends the independence of the checker, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. By Fig 5 :55, *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* A refutation is added, the reach falls safely, and the provenance trail lets a later reader locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, in particular a common unit with a measured round trip (Fig 4 :30), within-closure search adding an atom (Fig 8 :32), an import changing the ground rather than access (Fig 6 :25), an item creating a new action with no change of reach, contact or standing (Fig 11 :40), or an aggregate preserving the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22). Agreement among users of the same method is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :35, :40).
