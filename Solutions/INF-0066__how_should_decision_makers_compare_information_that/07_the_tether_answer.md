# The Tether's answer: how to compare the two kinds of information

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on its application to decision-makers. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The residual it answers

A decision-maker holds Γ, belief `b`, actions and a residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` indexed per slot (Fig 1 :11, :19). Two items are offered. U lowers `R` without changing the wall. A changes which actions are possible. The question is not which is more valuable but whether they can be compared on one scale at all. The ordinary practice says yes: both become a number, EVPI or EVSI or a money equivalent, and the numbers are added. The figures say the units differ (Fig 12 :125-127).

## 2. The shape

1. **Classify by effect on the reach, not by topic.** Same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited is U (Fig 6 :49). A moved wall, a new atom, changed contact, or a term stripped of its standing to be composed over is A (Fig 6 :14, :23; Fig 11 :40; Fig 5 :55). A price forecast that only updates belief is U even if it is very accurate; a rough signal that reveals an instrument you can use is A.
2. **Hold two ledgers and no exchange rate.** *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* (Fig 12 :125-126). *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."* (Fig 12 :130). Both prices attach to every item; only the effect decides the class.
3. **U has a definitional ceiling.** *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* (Fig 8 :32). No amount of U creates a new action. A is the only route past the wall.
4. **A includes refusal.** Information that invalidates a model is not U with a negative sign on accuracy; it is a fifth kind of gap that *"sort[s] a gap by what is wrong"* (Fig 5 :51). It removes standing, and *"What falls is the reach"* (Fig 5 :55). Negation is unary, not a bond (Operators :70-78).
5. **Order is load-bearing.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); run SEARCHED before INWARD and OUTWARD (Formula step 7); settle each change before composing over it (Fig 12 :104). An import bought against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed (Fig 8 :41-43).
6. **Report per slot, never as one aggregate.** *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19) and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22). Overlaps, where an item both lowers `R` and opens an action, are compounds with their own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).
7. **A computed number is a filter, not a witness.** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* (Fig 9 :60). EVPI and EVSI rank which research to buy. They do not settle which action is right, and agreement among users of the same method is not a second witness (Fig 2 :35, :40).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a single value-of-information number, and no exchange rate between bits and actions. The common scale is declared `gap`, which is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134; Fig 8 :39).
- Not that the field's VoI methods are wrong. They are budget tools; the shape says what they cannot do.
- Not that A is always "more options." Refusal removes options and still changes what actions are possible.
- Not that information has no value unless it opens an action. U has value per slot, bounded by the wall.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement compares the two ledgers here.
- Not a ranking of decision-makers by likely agreement. That would manufacture the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, re-opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A single expected-value reading can be produced by several action spaces; the reading does not name its source. The corresponding tests are:

- **Common-scale test:** exhibit a unit that prices bits and actions together for one decision, with a measured round trip `R_T` (Fig 4 :30) rather than an asserted equivalence.
- **Wall test:** show within-closure search adding an atom under a fixed atom set, or an import changing the ground rather than the frame's access (Fig 6 :25).
- **Order test:** reverse refuse/search or search/import and show cost and reach unchanged for every slot.
- **Aggregate test:** show an organization-wide number preserving the per-slot "where to look" signal and the compound structure.
- **Standing test:** show an item creating a genuinely new possible action with no change of reach, contact or standing.

These are the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, written before the conclusion. If any fires, the two-ledger shape is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The two currencies are held apart because the figures name them and forbid the sum. Whether a third instrument can discriminate their sources is a question about the available instruments, not about this derivation. The framework returns a shape: a classifier, two ledgers, an order and a list of refuters. It does not return a number, and *"naming it would mean already holding it"* (Fig 13 :135). The canonical figures README states: Indiscernibility is not identity. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give you one value for information. It says that two things commonly called valuable information are different in kind. One lowers prediction error inside the set of actions you already have, and no amount of it can create a new action, because search inside a closed set never adds an atom. The other moves the wall: an import, an instrument, a new affordance, or information that removes a term you were relying on. Those two are priced in different units, bits for the description and actions for finding out whether it holds, and the framework says they do not add. So classify by effect on the reach, keep two ledgers, report per case rather than as one average, refuse and search before importing, and treat any computed value of information as a budget filter rather than a verdict. The common scale stays marked `???` until an instrument that discriminates its sources exists. That is a shape and a set of refuters written in advance, not a number and not a settled theory.
