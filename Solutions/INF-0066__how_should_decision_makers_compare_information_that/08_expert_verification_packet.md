# Expert verification packet — the Tether on comparing two kinds of information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a decision analyst, information theorist or risk researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural claim, `specified` on the application to decision-makers. Not `grounded`.** No measurement compares the two ledgers. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Information that lowers prediction error inside a fixed action set (U) and information that changes which actions are possible (A) are different in kind, not two points on one scale. U is within-closure search: same atoms, same wall, more of the space visited, with a definitional ceiling because no search adds an atom. A moves the wall by import or instrument extension, changes contact, or strips a held term of its standing to be composed over, including refutation. Bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds; the two are in different units and do not add. The shape is therefore: classify by effect on the reach, keep two ledgers with no exchange rate, report per slot, refuse and search before importing, and treat any computed value-of-information number as a budget filter rather than a verdict. The common scale stays open.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 12

- **Statement:** U and A are separated by effect on the reach, and their two prices do not add.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :49 — *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change."* Fig 8 :32 — *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Fig 12 :125-126 — *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130 — *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a common unit that ranks both kinds for one decision and preserves both currencies, with a measured round trip rather than an asserted equivalence; or show within-closure search adding an atom under a fixed atom set.

### C2 — decision-analysis facing; checkable against the field's own VoI methods

- **Statement:** EVPI, EVSI and expected net gain are budget tools, not verdicts, and even inside one currency the joint value of two pieces of evidence is not their sum. A single expected-value reading cannot tell you which action space produced it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60 — *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 1 :22 — *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** Howard 1966, *Information Value Theory*, IEEE Trans. Systems Science and Cybernetics 2(1):22-26, abstract: *"the joint elimination of the uncertainty about a number of even independent factors in a problem can have a value that differs from the sum of the values of eliminating the uncertainty in each factor separately."* Glynn et al. 2022, Frontiers in Environmental Science 10:805245: *"How appropriate is it to offer an absolute single estimate of VOI? We argue not very."* These are secondary witnesses, not figure lines.
- **Refuter:** a decision where a computed VoI number, not the ground, settles which action is correct; or two U items whose joint value equals their separate sum for every state while the per-slot ordering is preserved.

### C3 — action-space facing; checkable by a researcher in risk, real options or ecological psychology

- **Statement:** "what actions are possible" is a property of agent-and-habitat, and it can change by import, by instrument extension, or by refusal, where the reach falls rather than rises.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 — *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 — *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 5 :55 — *"What it removes is not a term but a term's standing to be composed over. Nothing leaves the library. What falls is the reach."* Fig 6 :23 — *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Operators table :58 — *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*
- **What to check:** Chemero 2024, *Affordances*, MIT Press: *"Affordances are features of the environment that enable actions for a perceiver."* Glynn et al. 2022 VE3 reports that information invalidating a model is valuable but rarely counted. Engelen and Cassimon 2019 define real options as flexibility under uncertainty with *"upside potential while insulating from downside risk"*. Do these describe the same A branch?
- **Refuter:** an item that creates a genuinely new possible action with no change of reach, contact or standing; or a refusal that leaves the reach unchanged yet genuinely changes what actions are possible.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not one value of information, no exchange rate, and no weights for the two ledgers. The common scale is `gap`, a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).
- Not that EVPI or EVSI are wrong; they are filters for research budgets.
- Not that A always means more options. Refusal removes options and is still A.
- Not that U is worthless. It is valuable per slot and bounded by the wall.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured.
- Not that agreement among users of the same method is corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Five pre-registered refuters, written before the conclusion:

1. a common unit with a measured round trip that ranks U and A without losing a constraint;
2. within-closure search adding an atom or moving the wall under a fixed library;
3. an import changing the ground rather than the frame's access to it (Fig 6 :25 — *"An import adds no atom to the world"*);
4. an item creating a new possible action with no change of reach, contact or standing;
5. an aggregate preserving the per-slot "where to look" signal and the compound structure.

Any one fires, the two-ledger shape is withdrawn.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :49, Fig 8 :32 and Fig 12 :125-130, three short passages. Is the two-ledger shape what they say?
2. Check C2 against your own field: does a VoI number decide an action, or only rank which research to buy? Can two U items be added safely?
3. Check C3: name one case where information changed what actions were possible without moving a wall, changing contact, or removing a held term. If you can, that is the refutation.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no reply is needed if you would rather not engage.
