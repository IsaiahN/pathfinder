## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** verbatim figure text, chiefly Fig 4 :27-32 (lossy transform and round trip), Fig 12 :5, :94, :117 (bond, arrangement, isomer), Fig 5 :42-45 (MDL bargain), Fig 2 :29 and Fig 1 :27 (anchor and frame-internal disqualification).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: memory is lossy; keep the bond; use an MDL bargain; anchor usefulness outside the frame; report the gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete memory claim, for example: the hippocampus rapidly stores separated episodes and the neocortex slowly extracts semantic structure; forgetting is adaptive compression.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **topology**, cycle rather than chain. The shorthand says "memory is lossy and selective"; the concrete two-system account says nothing by itself about retrieval re-entering as encoding. That constraint comes from Fig 12 :121 and is dropped in the concretisation.
- the **per-slot requirement**. A concrete compression result is usually reported as a global distortion or reconstruction error. Fig 1 :19 forbids aggregating the live signal, and that constraint is lost.
- the **bond-versus-inventory distinction**. A concrete "semantic structure" claim can be read as an item list with associations, which is not the priced typed relation of Fig 12. The bond price is dropped.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: loss is the condition of reuse, and the retained part must be a relation with a ground-defined relevance variable. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
