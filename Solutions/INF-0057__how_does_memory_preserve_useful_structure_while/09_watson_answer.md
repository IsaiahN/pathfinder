## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure verdict is the shape, not a mechanism. Memory preserves a typed bond structure, not an item list; retains a detail only when stating it plus what remains unexplained costs less than what was unexplained before; anchors usefulness in a relevance variable external to the frame; describes the cut per slot; reports the round-trip gap as the honest measure of what was given up; and has cycle topology because retrieval re-enters as encoding. The two halves are a union compound with different anchors, so a single compression objective does not close the residual. No brain region, synapse or algorithm is named by the figures. Every substantive claim is `specified` or `general`, and `candidate` until a ground pays for it. World sources corroborate the shape from several fields; they do not decide it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape states non-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording, a list of remembered occasions or a neural story, instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is modular and the neural bindings are withheld, so a failing sub-claim can be withdrawn without disturbing the rest. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the bindings. Nothing has to be deleted: a refutation adds a term that refuses and the reach falls because something was added.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An isomer test showing that bond type makes no difference to later inference would break the structure claim; a bargain test showing retention tracks raw capacity rather than what remains unexplained would break Fig 5 :43; an anchor test showing improvement with unchanged task contact would break Fig 11 :40; a round-trip measurement returning zero gap for every input would break Fig 4 :28; a cycle test in which blocking retrieval-induced change costs nothing would break Fig 12 :121. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
