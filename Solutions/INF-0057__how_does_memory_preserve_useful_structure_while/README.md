## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary, not the anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 9 :60, *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission routes avoid that failure and the structural one together.
