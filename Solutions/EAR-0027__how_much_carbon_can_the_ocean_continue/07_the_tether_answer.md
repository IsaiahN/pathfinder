# The Tether's answer: how much carbon can the ocean keep absorbing?

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a remaining capacity. The residual is the gap between what the projection frame predicts and what the ocean, as a warming, acidifying, stratifying system, actually absorbs. It is indexed by slot (basin, biome, mechanism), it is a union compound of at least four overlapping conditions, and it runs from seasonal to millennial scale. Fig 9 :4-5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **Not one number.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The 2023 record-SST year is the proof by counterexample: the non-polar sink weakened about 10% while the tropical Pacific took up more than expected. A scalar loses that sign reversal.

2. **A union compound, not a single lever.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Solubility, carbonate buffering, ventilation and biology must be reported separately and their intersections named as specific compounds.

3. **The flux is a gradient-driven flow, with two distinct gradient terms.** Fig 13 :76: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." The air-sea fugacity difference and the multidecadal trend are not interchangeable. Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

4. **The objective before the capacity.** "Continue to absorb" has no referent until pathway, horizon and threshold are bound. A threshold such as 4.5 PgC per year is a `specified` objective from one model study, not a universal constant. A capacity without a stated objective is a link whose input never arrives (Fig 3).

5. **Measurement is the anchor; agreement is a filter.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." An ensemble mean, a tier or a "25% of emissions" headline allocates a budget; it does not settle the quantity.

6. **The round-trip gap is part of the answer.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The difference between observation-based fCO2 products and global ocean biogeochemistry models (about 3.1 versus 2.6 GtC per year over 2014 to 2023, with a roughly 10% model underestimate against interior constraints) is reported as a measured gap, not averaged away.

7. **Enumerate the habitat, including second-order cascades.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52-53: "first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Warming alters stratification and the surface DIC reservoir; the compensation can reverse the sign of the flux anomaly regionally.

8. **The framework returns a shape, not a number.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." What is returned is an admissible form for any answer, with the value left to the instruments.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a capacity value in GtC per year. No figure line yields one.
- Not that the ocean sink has already flipped sign globally. The world literature says the fraction taken up has stayed near 25% while the absolute sink has grown; the weakening is a projected tendency and a regional anomaly, not a global observation of decline.
- Not that any model ensemble is the anchor. Models range against the measurement.
- Not that 4.5 PgC per year is a wall of nature. It is one pathway-conditioned model threshold.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by experiment.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* records that an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot test:** does a per-basin, per-mechanism report predict the next year's regional flux anomalies better than a global scalar? The fCO2 observing network and repeat hydrography already return the readings needed.
- **Threshold test:** under a given pathway, does the proportional relation between atmospheric CO2 and uptake break at a definable sink strength, and is that break reproduced by an independent instrument rather than by the same model family?
- **Cascade test:** can an enumerated contact web including DIC depletion and stratification predict the sign reversal that a first-order thermal model missed in 2023?
- **Standing falsifiers from §5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`:** a lossless global scalar, one monotonic law across all pathways, a non-gradient flux, a moving anchor, a pathway-independent capacity, or a first-order-only contact list that predicts the reversal. Any one fires the refuter.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The literature itself says the loss of natural CO2 cannot yet be quantified conclusively with observations, and that the observational networks must be sustained and expanded (Gruber et al. 2023, read 2026-11-22). The answer stops at the limit of what the instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Indiscernibility is not identity; a missing capacity measurement is not proof that capacity does not exist. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a number for how much carbon the ocean can still absorb, and it does not claim the sink has already failed. It says the question is asked in the wrong form: the residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated, so a single global capacity scalar throws away the regional sign reversals that carry the live signal. Uptake is a union compound of solubility, carbonate buffering, ventilation and biology, not one lever; the air-sea flux is a gradient-driven flow in which the spatial gradient and the long-term trend are distinct terms. Any admissible answer must name its pathway, horizon and threshold before calling anything a capacity, must treat measurement as the anchor and model agreement as a budget filter, must report the measured gap between projection and observation, and must enumerate the second-order cascades that can reverse the sign of a regional anomaly. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The number stays open until the ground settles it.
