## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between projected and observed air-sea CO2 uptake. Arity: four overlapping conditions (solubility, carbonate buffering, ventilation, biology), so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: asymmetric, drivers have opposing valences (warming lowers solubility; DIC depletion can compensate; weaker upwelling cuts tropical natural outgassing while stronger stratification cuts extratropical ventilation). Scale: seasonal to millennial, biome to globe. Fig 9 :4-5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- **Domain 1, the ocean carbon system.** Actors: carbonate system, heat, circulation, winds, biology. Currency: CO2 flux, DIC, alkalinity, pH, omega. Anchor: measurements, fCO2 observations, interior inventories, autonomous floats. Type: union compound of pumps. Dependency: first.
- **Domain 2, the projection frame.** Actors: global ocean biogeochemistry models, fCO2 mapping products, Earth system models, neural-net maps. Currency: projected uptake and uncertainty. Anchor: the ground in Domain 1, which the frame must range against. Type: a frame, not an anchor. Dependency: second.
- **Domain 3, the verification and outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero. Dependency: third.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat, perceive the slot-indexed residual, bind the objective (pathway, horizon, threshold), only then compare with a threshold relation, then settle against measurement. ## WORLD (secondary sources read)

- **Assessment / carbon budget.** Global Carbon Project, Global Carbon Budget 2024 FAQs (globalcarbonbudget.org, 2024): "Climate conditions reduced the ocean sink by an estimated 6% in the past decade, likely dominated by altered winds that perturb the ocean circulation with a smaller contribution from the reduced solubility of CO2 in warming waters." Predicts a slot-indexed, circulation-dominated weakening; corroborates F1 and F10. Overlap: high with other GCB products.
- **Assessment / physical science.** Canadell et al. Predicts carbonate chemistry as one slot, not the whole answer; corroborates F2. Overlap: high with the assessment family.
- **Observational biogeochemistry.** Muller et al., "Unexpected decline in the ocean carbon sink under record-high SSTs in 2023", Nature Climate Change (2025): "the global non-polar ocean absorbed about 10% less CO2 than expected". Predicts opposing regional signs and a second-order DIC compensation; corroborates F1 and F10. Overlap: medium, shares authors and fCO2 products with the reviews.
- **Review / oceanography.** Gruber et al., "Trends and variability in the ocean carbon sink", Nature Reviews Earth and Environment (2023): "The net oceanic uptake rate of CO2 will likely decrease in the future owing to several converging trends: reduced emissions of CO2 leading to reduced atmospheric CO2 growth rates in response to climate policy; reduced storage capacity owing to continuing ocean acidification; and enhanced outgassing of natural CO2 owing to ocean warming and changes in ocean circulation and biology." Predicts a union compound with sign-different terms; corroborates F2. Overlap: high, shares the same community.
- **Modelling / earth system science.** Terhaar, "Drivers of decadal trends in the ocean carbon sink", Biogeosciences (2024): "80 % of decadal trends in the ocean carbon sink can be explained by changes in decadal trends in atmospheric CO2 as long as the ocean carbon sink remains smaller than 4.5 Pg C yr-1." ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's form is refused. The capacity is not one number but a per-slot, pathway-conditional residual against measurement, with a union compound of mechanisms, a gradient-driven flux with distinct spatial and temporal terms, a named objective before any threshold comparison, a measured projection-to-observation gap, and an enumerated habitat that includes second-order cascades. The figures return the shape and the refuters; the instruments own the value. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. A single global capacity scalar, a monotonic law across all pathways, a non-gradient flux, a model ensemble that moves the anchor, a pathway-independent capacity, or a first-order-only contact list that predicts the 2023 reversal would each break the shape claim.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its six refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The scalar framing is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that predicts regional flux anomalies better than a global scalar would collapse the slots; an independent instrument reproducing a threshold break under a stated pathway would name the capacity; a lossless global scalar, a monotonic law across all pathways, or a first-order contact list that predicts the 2023 sign reversal would break the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
