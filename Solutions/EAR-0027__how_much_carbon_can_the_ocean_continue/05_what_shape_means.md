## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* [direct]: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill its numbers. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "per-slot, pathway-conditional residual with an operationalized threshold and a named instrument" is a shape. "3.4 GtC per year" is a filling, and it belongs to the measurements.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* [direct]: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Bootstrapping by proximity: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then follow recurring terms that overlap it.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* [exemplar]: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* [direct] on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the exact situation of the ocean capacity question: solubility, buffering, ventilation and biology masquerade as one number.

## 3. What licenses a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* [exemplar] sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

A reported ocean sink is a genuine measurement import. It is legitimate content, but it is licensed by the instrument, not by the loop, and its provenance (which product, which period, which bias correction) is the whole difference between a witness and a pool.

## 4. Why a single number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* [exemplar] gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can produce one reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a global sink number is one green reading over many sources. In 2023 the same warm SST produced a weaker sink in the North Atlantic and a stronger one in the tropical Pacific (Muller et al. 2025 [direct URL source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41558-025-02380-4]). A single number cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a larger average.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* [direct]: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what would license a particular capacity value? ANSWERED

Nothing in the framework. A capacity value is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The correct form here is a per-slot residual with the slot left open until an instrument closes it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids reporting any numeric capacity at all. That overstates it. The README phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. A capacity is not a primitive; it is a derived comparison (a threshold relation, ⋛) over quantities the framework already holds. So reporting a measured capacity is not forbidden. What is forbidden is presenting the loop's own output as if the ground had settled it. The error is a mode error, not a category error. I record both readings so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 13 :115).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* [direct]: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets fCO2 observations, inventories and floats settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay [direct]: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ocean capacity question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
