## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks for a remaining capacity. Each candidate answer leads somewhere different: a single number feeds a carbon budget directly; a range feeds a risk envelope; a pathway-conditional functional form feeds scenario planning; a refusal says the question is under-specified until an objective is named. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula step "Direction"): a per-slot, pathway-conditional residual between projected and observed uptake, with an operationalized threshold and a stated instrument that would move it. That bound is the sought-for shape, written down so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan, no receptiveness rank used as evidence.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." An ensemble of models that share a forcing and a model generation is one room, not several witnesses.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (the leading clause "disqualifying for a verdict:" belongs to the full line). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the agreement that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

All substantive files state that this is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading**. By the framework's own rule (essay, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This package therefore cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns the shape any answer must have and the instruments that would close it.
