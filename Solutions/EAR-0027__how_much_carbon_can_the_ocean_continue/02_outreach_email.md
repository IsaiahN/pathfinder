# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide whether the question has an answer

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 are checked against your own knowledge. The stage-1 output must survive or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output about ocean carbon uptake, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as I found it:** how much carbon can the ocean keep absorbing as it warms and its chemistry changes? As written it asks for one number.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the figures I am using say that the residual is indexed by slot, not aggregated ("The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"; "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"). So a single global capacity number is the wrong container. What the derivation returns is a shape any answer must have:
>
> 1. a **per-slot** residual, by basin and by mechanism, not a global scalar, because the regional signs can oppose each other;
> 2. a **union compound** of solubility, carbonate buffering, ventilation and biology, not one hard problem, so one lever does not clear it;
> 3. the flux treated as a **gradient-driven flow**, with the spatial gradient and the long-term trend kept as separate terms;
> 4. an **objective named before the capacity**: pathway, horizon, threshold and the instrument that would move it;
> 5. the **measurement as anchor** and model agreement as a budget filter, never as a verdict;
> 6. a **measured round-trip gap** between projection and observation, and an enumerated contact web that includes second-order cascades.
>
> What I am explicitly **not** claiming: that this derivation produces a GtC per year value, that a particular model ensemble mean is the answer, that a published threshold such as 4.5 PgC per year is a universal ceiling, or that any of this is settled by measurement. The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself. The number is owned by the instruments.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before writing the conclusion:
> 1. a single global capacity scalar predicts the per-basin and per-mechanism residuals without losing sign or rank;
> 2. the four mechanisms reduce to one variable under one monotonic law across all emissions pathways;
> 3. a measurement shows the air-sea flux is not a gradient-driven flow, or that spatial and temporal gradients are the same term;
> 4. a model ensemble moves the measurement rather than ranging against it;
> 5. a pathway-independent capacity number with a stated objective and instrument is produced;
> 6. a first-order-only contact list predicts the 2023 regional sign reversal in the ocean sink.
>
> If any holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** links to the public sources are below. The figures and the published literature, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Surface Ocean CO2 Atlas (public observations): https://www.socat.info/
> - Global Carbon Budget FAQs (public assessment): https://globalcarbonbudget.org/gcb-2024/faqs
> - IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 5 (carbon and biogeochemical cycles): https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-5
>
> **If you want to use the method:** it is an eight-step loop that any field can run. Write down what an answer would look like before you run it, write down what would refute it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]
