## Wider literature that bears on the shape claim

Read this session and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`:

- The 2023 record-SST decline (Muller et al. 2025) is the clearest case of a second-order cascade. First-order thermal reasoning predicted a stronger sink; the non-thermal DIC response overcompensated in the tropics and failed in the North Atlantic. This is Fig 11 :52-53 applied to a real ground.
- The consolidated budget (Friedlingstein et al., Nature, published 12 November 2025, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09802-5, opened and read 2026-11-22) reports fCO2 products at 3.1 +/- 0.3 and GOBMs at 2.6 +/- 0.4 GtC per year over 2014 to 2023, and an approximately 10% model underestimate against independent constraints. This is the measured round-trip gap of Fig 4 :30.
- Terhaar (2024) converts "capacity" into an operationalized threshold, which is the object-binding discipline of Formula step "Direction". It is a `specified` objective, not a figure constant.
- Gruber et al. (2023) shows the annual sink varies about +/- 20% around its trend. A single number without an uncertainty envelope is not an answer in this field.
