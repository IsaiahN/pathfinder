## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to accept the claim.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that agreement from a receptive audience counts as corroboration.

The reason is structural, from the figures: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163, carried in `04`). *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless. The recipient is asked what would refute the shape claim, not whether they agree with it.

## Key verdict in one line

The four identity axes are a **union of overlapping conditions**, not four independent levers and not one additive score; an identity becomes politically mobilized not by being a category but by (a) contact with a frame whose closure differs, (b) residence in a habitat that supplies resources and an opening, (c) the arrival of bonds a bare category lacks, namely a comparison (goal), a sequence (plan), a subtraction (progress) and a negation (grievance), and (d) an anchor, an outcome that does not update on the group's own account. No figure line names which identity wins in a given case, so that part is a declared `gap`, a verdict and not a silence.
