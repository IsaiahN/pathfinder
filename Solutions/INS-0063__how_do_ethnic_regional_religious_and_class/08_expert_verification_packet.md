# Expert verification packet: the Tether on identity mobilization

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scholar of political behaviour, social movements or identity politics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which identity wins in a given case. Not `grounded`.** No survey, experiment, election result or dataset is claimed here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Ethnic, regional, religious and class identities are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition and not one additive score. An identity does not become politically mobilized by virtue of being a category; it mobilizes when its frame is brought into contact with a differing closure inside a habitat that supplies resources and an opening, when bonds arrive that the bare category lacks (a goal as a comparison, a plan as a sequence, progress as a subtraction, a grievance as a negation), and when an outcome anchor settles the claim without updating on the group's own account. The valence of any axis is relative to the question being asked, so no standing list of mobilizing identities survives a change of question. The framework does not name which identity wins in a given place; that is a declared gap awaiting a discriminator.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: union versus partition; checkable by reading Figure 9

- **Statement:** the four axes form a union of overlapping conditions. A single-axis lever clears the region where only that axis binds and leaves the intersections standing. There is no additive salience score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*
- **What to check:** are ethnic, regional, religious and class positions overlapping in the same individuals in the cases you know? Is there a single measured lever that clears all four intersections?
- **Refuter:** a mutually exclusive and exhaustive partition, or one lever with a measured zero residue across intersections.

### C2: the mechanism; checkable against the mobilization literature

- **Statement:** a category alone does not mobilize. Contact with a differing closure, a habitat with resources and an opening, and four bonds are required. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*. Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **What to check:** resource mobilization says *"grievances are not sufficient to explain creation of social movements"*; political opportunity says *"When there are no political opportunities, simply having grievances and resources is not enough."* Do these agree with the claim, and does the minimal group result (categorization alone produces in-group favouritism) actually contradict it? My answer is no: bias is not mobilization. Say if you disagree.
- **Refuter:** an identity that mobilizes with no contact change, no resource and no opening; or a mobilization with no comparison, no sequence, no subtraction and no refusal.

### C3: the anchor and the refusal to name a winner; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** group agreement is a convention, not a verdict; success is settled by an outcome that does not update on the group's account; which axis wins is a gap, not a silence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :41 *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **What to check:** in a case you know, did the measure of success change when the group's preferred story changed? If so, the measure was a convention. Is there an instrument that would discriminate among the four axes on a held-out case?
- **Refuter:** an anchor that demonstrably updated on the frame; or a working discriminator, which closes the gap and requires the claim to be revised.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a standing list of mobilizing identities, and no ranking of the four axes by inherent power (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a single summed salience variable.
- Not that ethnic identity, or class, is the hidden real cause of the other three.
- Not that the group's self-report is evidence about the world.
- Not that the six-term physical substrate makes an identity a "state" or a party a "structure". That first reading was retracted and preserved in `01` §6 and `06` Q5.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A discriminator that predicts which axis becomes salient from the described residual alone, tested on a held-out case.
2. A contact test: a case with a differing closure and no mobilization, or mobilization with no contact change.
3. A bond test: a mobilization lacking a comparison, a sequence, a subtraction and a negation.
4. An anchor test: a measure that moved when a frame pushed on it.
5. A partition test: one lever that clears every intersection with zero residue.
