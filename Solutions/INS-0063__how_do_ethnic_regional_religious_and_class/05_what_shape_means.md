## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

For this question, a shape is the form that any answer about identity mobilization must have, stated before we can say which identity will mobilize where. The shape has four parts: a union of overlapping conditions (Fig 9 :55), a differing-closure contact in a habitat (Fig 6 :23, Fig 11 :39), bonds that turn a category into a route (Fig 12 :111-112), and an outcome anchor that does not update (Fig 2 :29). It constrains an answer without filling in the local name.

From "The Library of Babel", opened directly: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And the decisive line for this question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :55 in the vocabulary of search.

Contrast: "class is the real driver and the others are masks" is a name. "Which axis dominates in this election" is a name. The shape is what must hold before either name is licensed.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* In this run the residual was described in effect terms (a category that predicts nothing political becoming a force) before the world fields were chosen. Otherwise any field, from political sociology to social psychology, would have seemed to fit.
- "Decoding Tacit Knowledge" (carried mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Library of Babel on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, an instrument or outcome that discriminates among the identity axes in a held-out case would license a name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :50-51).

"They're Scanning Books" (carried mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Nothing in this folder declares ethnic, regional, religious or class identity to be nature's own categories. They are the question's own terms, and we treat them as a visible choice.

## 4. Why the metamerism point matters here

"The Hard Problem is About Source", opened directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: an observed surge in votes along an ethnic line is a reading. The source could be ethnic grievance, class positioning, religious organization, regional exclusion, or a combination that produces the same reading. Naming the ethnic source from the reading alone repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the reason F8 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a `gap` rather than a guessed winner.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming ethnic or class identity in the shape?

**No, with a correction to my own first reading.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Ethnic, regional, religious and class identities are terms the question already supplies; they are held atoms in the research frame, not missing primitives to be invented. Using them is a binding from the question's vocabulary, which is allowed, provided it is recorded as a binding rather than presented as a derivation of nature's categories. What remains forbidden is promoting one of them to *the* answer without a discriminator.

**Correction preserved:** I initially wrote that the framework's shape limit forbids naming any identity axis at all. That overstates it. The limit forbids naming a missing primitive and forbids naming the winner without a ground. It does not forbid using the terms the question gives us. Both the overstatement and its narrowing are kept here so the correction is visible.

## 6. Prestige is not action

"The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap", opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A well-known scholar of ethnic conflict gives the claim a budget to be tried, not a verdict. *"The high-prestige person is believed because they are high-prestige. The action is assumed to work because the person is believed. The ground is never consulted."* This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues and public calls, not a list of famous names, and why no recipient is selected for likely agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The essay also says plainly: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves... You need an external validator. You need both the concept and the percept."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim. They cannot settle anything they report on. Fig 10 :59 applies to this author as much as to any reader: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*, so the interest is declared here and a refuter was written in `01` before the conclusion.
