# Expert verification packet - the Tether on predicting future capabilities

**What this is:** a claim in a form a strategic foresight or dynamic capabilities researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` for firms, `general` for reachability. Not `grounded`.** No future market is claimed to have paid for this shape. The figures are primary and decide; world sources are secondary and corroborate but never overrule. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

At the moment a firm chooses which capabilities to build, the capability that will matter in the future market is unreachable from its current library: from inside that library, "already in here and unfound" and "genuinely absent" look the same, and composing inside a closed set never adds an atom. Foresight can return only a shape, a capability whose value survives a change in what the market rewards and whose reach is renewed through contact with a closure that differs. Reliable strategy therefore treats "predict or adapt" as a union of two conditions with different instruments, reaching the shape before the market settles and keeping the channel and reach alive so the firm can rebind, refit, or import when it does. A same-closure scenario process is a mirror chain, a capability's value is relative to the residual rather than a property it carries, and a forecast carries a round-trip gap that must be measured.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 13

- **Statement:** at decision time the specific capability name is unreachable; only a shape is. This is `unreachable`, not silence and not a claim that more search never helps at a fixed reach.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :27 - *"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 13 :133 - *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** do the reachability definitions in the Symbols table (`closure(Γ)` = *"Everything expressible by combining the primitives, at any depth. What the system could say if it searched forever"*) support reading the capability as the φ* of Fig 6, so that the idempotence argument applies? Is the time crossing a genuine membrane (Fig 4) rather than a same-scale composition?
- **Refuter:** a prospective, per-firm, per-capability prediction issued at t, with a source gate and a shadow test, confirmed by the market at t+Δ across repeated slots. If it holds, C1 is false.

### C2 - world-facing; checkable against the foresight and dynamic capabilities literature

- **Statement:** the field's own vocabulary already treats capability choice as conjectural and foresight as partly reactive, so the shape answer is corroborated at the level of mechanism, not settled at the level of firm fact.
- **World evidence:** Teece 2007: *"These conjectures become working hypotheses that can be updated as evidence emerges."* Kleiner 2013 reports Teece: *"dynamic capabilities theory is not tightly predictive"* and *"It's very hard to figure out which capabilities are most important."* Semke and Tiberius 2020: *"Sensing constitutes a reactive action of the firm, as it perceives changes after they have already occurred."* Scoblic 2020: *"managers cannot watch for things they have not thought of."* These four are one evidence pool, so one witness (Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Tetlock (human cognition) and MathisonSlee et al. (ecology) are low-overlap witnesses for the mechanism only: *"subject-matter experts don't know as much as they think they do about the future. They are miscalibrated."* and *"Phase transitions were easier to identify after the fact."*
- **What to check:** are those characterizations faithful? Is the pointer to "working hypotheses" a fair reading of Teece rather than a selective quote? Does the Semke and Tiberius finding actually support the union reading, or only a gap between sensing and implementation?
- **Refuter:** a single prospective study from a genuinely different evidence pool showing that a named capability's future value is predicted at t by the firm's existing library with calibration confirmed at t+Δ. That would move the claim from corroborated mechanism to refuted firm-level verdict.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation about the union and the channel

- **Statement:** the two conditions must be kept separate, and foresight quality is selection between differing rooms rather than agreement within one. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
- **What to check:** does Fig 9 :57 (*"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*) license separate instruments, or is the union being over-read? Are the two factors of Fig 7 :37-39 (*"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"*) a product structure rather than a calibrated equation?
- **Refuter:** a single intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types with no categorically different residue left over (breaks the union), or a same-closure process that keeps producing ground-verified new readings for two decades without import (breaks the mirror-chain claim).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that firms cannot forecast, that experts are useless, or that scenario planning is worthless.
- Not that dynamic capabilities theory names the winning capability, and not that this analysis is a new theory of strategy.
- Not a single lever, not a blended foresight score, and not a `grounded` result.
- Not that the future is unknowable in general. The verdict is limited to the name at decision time under the closure the firm holds.
- Not that the figures are a theory of everything or the only true account of foresight. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and stops where instruments can no longer distinguish.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source"*; what is owed is a discriminator.

1. **Prospective name test:** a per-firm prediction at t that names the capability, with source gate and shadow test, confirmed at t+Δ across repeated slots.
2. **Valence test:** an instrument that discriminates a regenerating capability from a spent one (Fig 12 :119) when the residual changes.
3. **Closure test:** a firm renewing reach across a habitat change with no new atom from a differing closure (Fig 6 :23).
4. **Round-trip test:** R_T measured for a forecast carried from t to t+Δ and back (Fig 4 :30).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :32, Fig 13 :133-134, Fig 7 :36 and Fig 9 :55-57. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own reading of Teece, Scoblic and Semke and Tiberius. Are those characterizations faithful, or cherry-picked?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate depth from genuine absence at t, or say why none could exist. That is the `gap` this packet declares.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
