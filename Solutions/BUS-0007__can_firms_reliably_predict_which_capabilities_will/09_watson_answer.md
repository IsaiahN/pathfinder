## QUESTION

Can firms reliably predict which capabilities will matter in future markets, or must strategy mostly adapt after conditions change?

Restated as an effect: at decision time t, can a firm's library Γ close the gap between what it predicts about which capability the market will reward at t+Δ and what that market actually pays, or does the gap close only after o′(t+Δ) arrives? R is indexed per firm and per capability slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Anchor: the future market, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The anchor exists but is not reachable at t. That is the crux of the question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among firm-library Γ, belief b about the future, action a, and future return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered from t to t+Δ. It carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones, because the bet precedes settlement and "which matters" is a threshold (Fig 12 :87, "a threshold relation between two quantities"). Symmetry: the future market does not update on the firm's forecast. Scale: the firm loop at t, the population market at t+Δ, and the lossy time membrane between them (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** at decision time the specific capability that will matter is unreachable from the firm's current library, because depth and genuine absence look the same from inside that closure. Foresight can return only the shape a winning capability must have, plus a pre-registered refuter. Reliable strategy therefore keeps the union's two conditions separate and never averaged: maintain a channel to a ground that still pays, and keep moving reach outward through contact with closures that differ, so that when the future market settles the firm can rebind, refit, or import rather than discover that a former strength changed sign. Mode `specified` for firms, `general` for the reachability structure. The business pool corroborates only after Teece's "conjectures" are re-mapped to Γ; all four business sources are one witness. The two low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism, not the firm-level fact. Which named capability will win is a declared `gap` of the `unreachable` kind, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** a firm commits to a predicted capability and builds a capability-anchored strategy while the future residual has shifted, so the strength becomes a rigidity (Fig 12 :119, Fig 11 :50). The mirror-chain failure is invisible from inside the scenario room that produced it (Fig 7 :36). In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape, and a firm would apply one lever to a union that needs two. The cost is real, asymmetric in time, and only partly reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). A capability bet already made is less undoable, which is why the verdict is stated before commitment rather than after.
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered refuters, in particular (1) a prospective per-firm prediction that names the capability with source gate and shadow test and is confirmed at t+Δ across repeated slots, (2) a firm renewing reach across a habitat change with no new atom from a differing closure, (3) measured valence invariant across a change of residual, (4) a same-closure process producing ground-verified new readings for two decades without import, (5) a single lever restoring both union conditions with no categorically different residue, (6) a round trip from t to t+Δ with no measurable gap while valence changed, or (7) a failure of idempotence inside a genuinely closed set. A source from a genuinely different evidence pool showing that named capabilities are reliably predictable at t with calibration confirmed at t+Δ would also reverse it.
