## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a capability whose valence survives a change in what the market rewards and whose reach is renewed by contact with a differing closure" is a shape. "Build capability X" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on the bootstrap: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* rather than *"the cause"*. Asking "which capability will win?" in the vocabulary of the current market is sharpening the question into terms only the current frame can read.
- *Library of Babel*, on the stop: *"Maybe your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the canonical reading of the question's "predict or adapt" wording: a union that must be unbundled before a lever is chosen (Fig 9 :55).
- *Library of Babel*, on the paradox: *"By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For firms, this is why the shape becomes usable only after the market has settled and the firm has rebounded against it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question, the market at t+Δ pays or does not pay for the capability.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50). Naming the business-pool terms dynamic capabilities or sensing is a binding from the business frame onto an atom already held, not the discovery of a new primitive. It carries zero verdict weight until provenance is shown.

## 4. Why "predict the exact capability" is the source error the essay names

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A forecast of t+Δ is such an instrument. It reads a projection of market reward and cannot comment on which capability produced that reward. The figure-grounded response is not to name the source from the projection. It is to characterize the missing discriminator in effect terms and record the stop.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds the validation limit: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A foresight method that scores its own accuracy with its own scenario set is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces (Fig 1 :27).

## 5. ANSWERED: what does the framework license for this question?

**Nothing in the framework names the capability that will matter.** The framework returns a shape (Fig 13 :133) with a reachability verdict (Fig 6 :27). A particular capability is licensed only by the ground settling a term at t+Δ, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the reachability verdict change with the corrected reading?** No. The correction in the exemplar said that "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, not an atom already in Γ; Gradient and Spectrum are already among the six, so naming them was a binding, not an invention. Here the capability that will matter at t+Δ is precisely not held in Γ at t. If it were held, the question would reduce to more search. So the ban applies in its strict form: the name is unreachable at decision time, and even a business-pool name like "dynamic capabilities" earns only the status of a binding with zero verdict weight. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this question's field, that means a famous futurist's forecast gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not make the forecast true. A list of agreeable strategy professors is a prestige pool, not an anchor. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried by a ground, and let the market at t+Δ settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the business claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle BUS-0007. This is why the mode is `specified` and the honest stop in `07_the_tether_answer.md` remains open.
