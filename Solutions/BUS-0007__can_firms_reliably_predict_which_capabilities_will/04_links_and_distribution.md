## What the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The business pool is one witness, not many.** Teece 2007, Kleiner 2013, Scoblic 2020 and Semke and Tiberius 2020 all touch the same claim from the same management-and-foresight evidence pool. Their agreement is *"the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"* (Fig 2 :21). Their value here is to show that the field's own vocabulary ("working hypotheses", "process break", "not tightly predictive") already leans toward the shape answer, not to settle it.
2. **Teece's own caveat is the sharpest business-source line.** *"dynamic capabilities theory is not tightly predictive"* and *"It's very hard to figure out which capabilities are most important"* (Kleiner 2013) are exactly the figure-derived reachability verdict in management language. Use them as corroboration, never as authority.
3. **Foresight's own practitioners report a process break.** Semke and Tiberius found that four of seven foresight experts saw a break after sensing, and that *"Sensing constitutes a reactive action of the firm, as it perceives changes after they have already occurred."* That supports the union reading: reaching a shape and carrying it into reconfiguration are two conditions, not one.
4. **Two low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism only.** Tetlock (human cognition) supplies calibration and active open-mindedness; MathisonSlee et al. (ecology) supplies the rigidity trap and the observation that *"Phase transitions were easier to identify after the fact."* Neither measures firm capability renewal, so neither is a firm-level witness.
5. **The source-discriminator point is not a business point.** *The Hard Problem is About Source* argues that an instrument that reads a value *"cannot comment on the source."* A forecast reads a projection of t+Δ and cannot name the capability that produced it. What is owed is a discriminator instrument, which is the declared `gap` in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 and Fig 4 :25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the reachability claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the reachability claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what they would expect to see if it were true; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the stage-1 claim against the ground. The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the sources. The verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If one of the seven refuters in `01` section 4 fires, repair or withdraw before sending.
