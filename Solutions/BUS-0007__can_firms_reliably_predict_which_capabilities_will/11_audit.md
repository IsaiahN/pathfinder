## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among Γ, belief b about t+Δ, action a at t, and o′ at t+Δ, indexed by slot s. At least three operands plus an ordering and a comparison. A two-place reading (firm, future performance) would drop the bet and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The future market is an anchor and does not update on the firm's forecast (Fig 2 :25). The phrase which will matter is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared with another, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a capability inside a firm at t, but the ranking "matters" exists only at the population market at t+Δ (Fig 4 :5). The time index crosses the membrane between the firm's step loop and the market's selection loop (Fig 4). So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale and the transform is lossy.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's verdict to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *the future capability cannot be named now; keep the channel open and let the ground decide.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific firm situation. Result: *do not commit to a named capability; keep listening to the market and keep bringing in outside practices.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. It collapses the union into one lever. "Keep listening and bring in outside practices" reads as two tips, not as two conditions with different instruments and a categorically different intersection (Fig 9 :57). Coming back down, it tells a firm failing C2 merely to listen harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the reachability argument. The one-sentence form says "cannot be named now" without carrying idempotence or depth-versus-absence (Fig 6 :21, :27). Coming back down, it becomes mere pessimism about forecasting rather than a decision rule about what to do before t+Δ.
3. It loses the anchor rule. "Let the ground decide" does not specify that the future market, not scenario consensus, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits a firm to score its foresight with a quantity its own scenarios produce (Fig 1 :27).
4. It loses the valence flip. "Keep bringing in outside practices" has no place for the possibility that the old good capability changed sign (Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 12 :119).
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish depth from genuine absence at t, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :23-25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
