## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says when several tipping points interact. Option A: the additive reading, in which likelihood rises with the count and consequences add, so the answer is a multiplier and a sum. Option B: the compound reading, in which coupling is a signed bond, the arrangement decides loop closure, consequences are indexed per slot, and the universal number is a gap. What each leads to: A returns a coefficient the figures do not license, treats a union as a list, and averages away the live slot; B returns a checkable shape, a set of per-domain verdicts, falsifiers written in advance, and an explicit statement of what an instrument would have to discriminate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Several thresholds with coupling between them; outputs are likelihood and consequences of systemic change. Arity: each crossing is binary (`⋛` between a quantity and a threshold), but N crossings give N comparisons and up to N(N-1)/2 couplings, so the arrangement is the object. Symmetry: the comparison is ordered and couplings carry sign, so operands are not interchangeable. Scale: first order inside one element, second order at the cascade, because a settled molecule becomes an atom for what composes over it. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, threshold and crossing.** Actors: control quantity, state variable, critical value. Currency: comparison and distance to threshold. Anchor: a measured bifurcation. Type: partition (does the comparison hold?). Order: first.
- **Domain 2, coupling and interaction.** Actors: tipping elements, directed couplings, sign of each. Currency: influence travelling, the network structure. Anchor: an observed cascade. Type: union compound with a possible cycle. Order: second; a baseline element cannot transmit.
- **Domain 3, consequences and systemic outcome.** Actors: all slots, the habitat, the aftermath state. Currency: per-slot residual and reach. Anchor: a measured aftermath. Type: union, per-slot, third-order effects. Order: third.
- **Domain 4, verification and instruments.** Actors: modellers, models, observations, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency:** describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27); settle each crossing before composing over it (Fig 12 :104); refuse before mint (Formula step 2). The weak link is the vocabulary for coupling and sign, not perception of a threshold (Fig 3 :19, :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The compound shape survives; the additive reading does not. Coupled tipping points form a union of overlapping conditions whose couplings are signed bonds, so the sign of the loop rather than the count decides whether a cascade closes. Over a fixed roster the arrangements are isomers, same atoms and different substance. Consequences are a per-slot vector with effects several steps out, and crossing removes standing to compose over the prior regime. No universal likelihood multiplier, critical coupling strength or named missing atom is returned; that is a declared gap, of the shape-not-a-name kind for the universal number and the unreachable-at-this-budget kind for a named system. The two live strains are the operand type of the comparison at second order and the status of coupling strength as weight or new kind. Status: `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the skeleton, `gap` on the number, candidate and ungrounded throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and strains before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The additive reading and the universal number are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The two live strains can be closed by a measurement without changing the skeleton.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A sign-and-arrangement discriminator at a named system would close the gap; a proof that the threshold conditions form a disjoint partition would break the union verdict; a proof that couplings are acyclic would break the loop verdict; a demonstration that consequences are additive with no live-slot asymmetry would break the per-slot verdict; a measurement that restoring the driver restores the prior composition with no new term would break the reach-fall claim. A figure re-census that admits a numerical multiplier would close the gap from inside. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
