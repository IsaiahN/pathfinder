## 2. Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on the shape claim

1. **Sign is a property of the bond, not of the count.** Fong et al. (2018) found additive, synergistic and antagonistic interactions in equal frequency with the same three stressors. Buma (2015) distinguishes a disturbance chain from a disturbance network and names the loop explicitly. This is the world-facing version of Fig 12's `The arrangement is the substance`: a roster-level theory cannot generate three signs from one set. Use these two sources when explaining why the answer is an arrangement and not a multiplier.
2. **The climate-tipping cluster is one witness, not several.** Wunderling 2021, Wunderling 2024 and Lenton 2024 share authors, institutions and a conceptual-network modelling tradition. Their agreement that interactions tend to destabilise is expected, and by Fig 2 :40 it is not corroboration of the figure verdict. The genuine low-overlap corroboration comes from network physics, multiple-stressor ecology and the engineered-systems review. Keep the pools separate in any reply.

- **Fig 4 :22** `Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.` Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the compound shape and the refuter; offer links after.

## 5. The two-stage offer, with its formal reason

**(1)** Validate the output against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems, writing a fresh refuter each time.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 `Bring a characterised residual, never the source` and Fig 2 :45 `Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.` The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- Stage 3 sends a method, which Fig 4 :22 permits. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; each user must write down what would refute their result and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The two live strains (the operand type of the comparison at second order, and whether coupling strength is an atom or a bond weight) are stated in `01` and `08`. If a recipient resolves either against the claim, the shape is narrowed in public rather than repaired in private.

No archive is created or sent by this agent. If you host the material, prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
