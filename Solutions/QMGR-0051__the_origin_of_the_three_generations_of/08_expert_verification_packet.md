# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the three generations

**What this is:** a claim in a form a particle physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the content verdict, not `grounded`.** No figure line yields an integer or a mass ratio, and no discriminating measurement is claimed here.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles the number of fermion generations with their mass and mixing pattern, and these must be split before search. The figures cannot produce the integer 3 or the mass ratios, so the content answer is a `gap`. What they constrain is the shape of any explanation: a generation is not a new member of the closed six-term set, so the repetition must be an arrangement over held terms or an import; the three copies are one term with three gauge spellings whose ordering is a comparison over slots, not a chemical-style isomerism; every candidate from outside the Standard Model is an import requiring provenance and a shadow test; and only a measurement, not agreement among models, settles which candidate survives. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the question is a union to be split, and a generation cannot be a new primitive.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5, "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. - **Refuter:** show that count and hierarchy are one condition that shrinks with single-track effort; or derive a seventh primitive from the six; or show the six-term set is not closed.

### C2 - framework-internal with a physics-facing check

- **Statement:** the three generations are one term with three spellings under a symmetric bond, ordered by a comparison; they are not Fig 12 isomers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :92, "Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term, so a code that charges for the sequence overcharges by log₂(k!) while the bond term undercharges." Fig 12 :117, "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities".
- **What to check:** do the three generations of a given species indeed share all gauge quantum numbers and differ only in the Yukawa coupling? If yes, the bond is the same and the isomer definition fails. If a model gives them different bond structure over the held atom set, the copy reading fails.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a model in which two generations differ by a bond, not a parameter, over the same atom set; or show that a mass hierarchy is not a comparison over ordered slots.

### C3 - provenance and settlement

- **Statement:** candidate mechanisms are imports with gates, and the measured count is an anchor that no frame may move.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23, "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." - **What to check:** the experimental anchor is real. ATLAS reports a Z invisible width "in agreement with the most precise determination from LEP and the Standard Model prediction based on three neutrino generations." So any explanation must range against a measured integer.
- **Refuter:** show a candidate admitted with no provenance and no gap it explains; or produce a measurement of the count that changes when a theorist changes their mind.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating observation, not another fit. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The same three masses are reproduced by several mechanisms, so the reading does not determine the source. Concretely:

1. **Neutrinoless double-beta decay:** does it occur at the level predicted for Majorana neutrinos?
2. **Sterile-neutrino X-ray line:** present or absent at the predicted energy?
3. **Mass-ratio sum rules and precision Higgs Yukawa couplings:** do the parameter-free relations reported by Singh survive an apples-to-apples renormalisation-scale comparison?
4. **Flavour violation:** meson splittings, rare decays and mu-e conversion at the levels predicted by the dualized model?

A result in any of these separates frames. Agreement in commentary does not.
