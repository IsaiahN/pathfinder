## Wider literature that bears on the question

The four physics sources are deliberately from different closures: a duality-based model, a Jordan-algebra mass-ratio derivation, a division-algebra classification, an anthropic cosmological argument, plus an experimental measurement as the ground. Their agreement that the puzzle exists is expected, because they all read the same measured masses. That is one evidence pool, not five witnesses (Fig 2 :40). Two points worth carrying into outreach:

1. **A measured anchor already exists.** ATLAS and LEP settle the number of light neutrino species near three. So a correct answer must explain a measured integer; it is not free to predict a different one without confronting that ground.
2. **The candidate mechanisms make different further predictions.** The anthropic paper predictions concern X-ray observations and neutrinoless double-beta decay; Singh's construction predicts Majorana neutrinos and a leptonic CP phase; the dualized model predicts correlated flavour-violation effects. That is the shape of a discriminator: look for the observation that separates frames, not the paper that sounds most complete.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the census, the shape claim and the refuter. Do not send a 200-page fit as the first contact.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed a whole candidate model before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before naming any flavour symmetry.
- **Fig 2 :40**, agreement from a shared evidence pool is worthless for a verdict. Never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35**, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict." No bulk send, no receptiveness rank.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the shape claim and the refuter against the ground. Record the verdict before any candidate model is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the figure text and the two puzzle-statement links. Their later agreement is never counted as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. The content answer is a `gap` from the figures; that is reported openly, not dressed as a result.
