## 1. The residual it answers

The Standard Model fits three generations and their masses but derives neither. The question bundles at least two conditions, the integer count and the mass and mixing pattern (plus a third, the quark versus lepton mixing contrast). The count is already a ground reading from the Z invisible width. What is owed is an explanation of a measured integer and a measured ordering. Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **Split before search.** Count, hierarchy and mixing are three well-formed questions, not one. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The Library of Babel essay: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"
2. **Report per slot, never as an average.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The hierarchy lives in the differences between generation slots; an average deletes it.
3. **No seventh primitive.** A generation cannot be a new member of the closed six-term set. Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh." So the repetition operation is either an arrangement over held terms or an import; it is not a new atom.
4. **Copies plus ordering, not isomers.** Under the gauge symmetry the three generations are one term with three spellings (Fig 12 :92). They are not Fig 12 isomers, because the bond is the same and only the Yukawa parameter differs. The ordering is a comparison over slot-quantities (`⋛` in the Operators table: "a threshold relation between two quantities"), not a derived pattern.
5. **A shape, not a name.** The integer 3 and the mass ratios are not figure outputs. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Any specific flavour symmetry, algebra or anthropic argument is a binding from the physics frame, admitted only with provenance and a shadow test.
6. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Agreement among models that read the same masses is one evidence pool, not several witnesses.
7. **No final theory of flavour.** Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room". A terminal explanation would be the forbidden final room.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not the number 3 from the figures. That number is measured.
- Not a mass ratio, a Yukawa coupling, a mixing angle, or a choice among flavour symmetries, Jordan algebras, trialities or anthropic selection. Those are imports with gates, not derivations.
- Not that the puzzle is unsolvable. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The verdict is `gap` for what the figures can supply, not a claim about nature.
- Not a theory of everything, and not a unified flavour force.
- Not `grounded`. No figure line and no discriminating measurement is claimed here as settling a candidate mechanism.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating observation, not another fit. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Several candidate mechanisms reproduce the same three masses and mixings, so the reading does not determine the source. The essay continues: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

Concrete discriminators named in the literature actually read:

- **Neutrinoless double-beta decay.** If neutrinos are Majorana, Singh's construction predicts it; the anthropic frame predicts a related signal; a Dirac-neutrino alternative would be excluded. A result here separates frames.
- **X-ray line from sterile-neutrino decay.** Predicted in the anthropic frame under its stated assumptions about the third right-handed neutrino.
- **Precision Higgs Yukawa measurements and mass-ratio sum rules.** Singh's paper lists concrete mass-ratio tests against PDG-2024 values; a deviation would refute that ladder.
- **Flavour-violation observables.** The dualized model predicts correlated meson splittings, rare decays and mu-e conversion. Absence at the predicted level would remove that frame.
- **Standing falsifiers of the shape claim:** a generation derived as a seventh primitive; count and hierarchy shown to be one condition; the count settled by agreement rather than measurement; a candidate admitted with no provenance; averaging across slots with the ordering residual intact.
