A generation cannot be a seventh primitive, so any repetition is an arrangement over held terms or an import. The three copies are one term with three gauge spellings, ordered by a comparison, not isomers. Every candidate from domain 2 is an import requiring provenance and a shadow test, and only a measurement settles it. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," is the honest stop. This is `specified` on shape and `general` on content, ungrounded throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a candidate model instead of the residual and its refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." - **undoable:** yes. The shape claim carries no specific binding, so no primitive and no physical claim has to be withdrawn if a candidate fails. A rejected candidate is recorded as provenance, not erased. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminating result in neutrinoless double-beta decay, a sterile-neutrino X-ray line, precision Yukawa sum rules or flavour-violation observables would name a frame; a derivation of a generation as a seventh primitive, a demonstration that count and hierarchy are one condition, or a measurement in which the count moves with theoretical fashion would break the shape claim; a figure re-census admitting a figure line that yields N=3 would dissolve the content gap.
