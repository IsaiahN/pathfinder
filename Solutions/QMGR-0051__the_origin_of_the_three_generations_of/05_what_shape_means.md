## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", and "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an explanation we can state before we can name its mechanism. "A repetition operation over held terms plus an ordering operation, reported per generation slot" is a shape. "The exceptional Jordan algebra J3(O) fixes it" is a name, and it is a binding from the physics frame, not an output of the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here, the terms gained are "count", "hierarchy", "mixing", "slot", "import", "anchor".
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level twin of Fig 9 :4-5 and governs F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." So the shape is stated as what one would expect to see: three slots, a strong ordering across them, a quark versus lepton mixing contrast, and a new observation that would discriminate candidate frames.
- *Library of Babel* on the anchor: "you need something to tether and anchor you to reality, the same way a mountaineer needs a tether when rappelling or climbing." Here the anchor is the measured Z invisible width and the measured masses.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here a name would be licensed by an observation that separates the dual-colour, Jordan-algebra, triality and anthropic frames, for example a result in neutrinoless double-beta decay or a precision Yukawa measurement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a specific flavour group is such an import, with provenance recorded, not a derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator rule: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Several candidate mechanisms reproduce the same three masses and mixings, so the reading does not determine the source. The essay adds: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not another fit.

Note an important scope correction: the "shape, not a name" rule governs a **missing primitive**, not every act of naming. Here the question is not about a missing member of the six-term substrate; it is about a physical count and a parameter pattern. So the rule applies with full force: neither the count nor the values are figure outputs. This is narrower than saying "the framework can never name anything", which would overstate the canonical sentence.

## 4. Why the measured integer is not a figure output

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :116: "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh." The six-term set cannot yield the number 3 as one of its members, and it does not contain a counting operator over physical generations. The count enters from domain A as a measurement. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." So the integer is an anchor, not a derivation, and any explanatory frame must range against it rather than negotiate with it (Fig 2 :45).

## 5. Question answered

**What licenses a particular mechanism for the three generations?** Nothing in the figures. A mechanism is licensed by the ground settling a candidate, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the mechanism is the searcher's own contribution and is recorded as such.

**Does the question need splitting before it is searched?** Yes. Fig 9 :4-5 and the *Library of Babel* bundle passage both say so. Count, hierarchy and mixing are three well-formed questions that must not be searched as one.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous flavour theorists who agree with a shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.
