## Restating the question as an effect

The Standard Model accommodates three copies of each fermion species and fits their masses and mixings, but it takes the number of copies and the Yukawa couplings as free inputs. So the residual is: observed {three generations, hierarchical masses and mixing} minus what the theory derives {no derivation of the count, no derivation of the pattern}. Option A is to assert that a candidate model explains it. Option B is to report the shape any explanation must have and name what would settle it. Option B is what this folder does.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe a candidate model.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35). Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Honest standing of this package

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This folder cannot settle the question it reports on.
