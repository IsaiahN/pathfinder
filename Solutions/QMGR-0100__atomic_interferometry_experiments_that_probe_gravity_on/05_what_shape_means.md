## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (as quoted in the exemplar): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For QMGR-0100:

- "A gravitational phase is measured on a quantum matter wave" is a measurement, not a shape.
- "The phase does not identify its source" is a gap verdict, not a shape.
- "What is missing is an observable on which classical-channel and quantum-channel predictions separate while phase readings agree" **is** a shape. It constrains the search without naming the theory or the platform.

Naming "gravitationally induced entanglement" as *the* answer would be a name, not a shape; the GIE program is one candidate that instantiates the shape, and the Tobar et al result shows why the name must not be conflated with the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (as quoted in the exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place" and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: start from the phase that is already returning a reading (Fig 6 :32) and describe the missing discriminator in effect terms: two predictions, one ground reading, phase agreement. Only then look for the frame that holds it (entanglement protocols, clocks, levitated masses). Residual first, frame second (Fig 8 :53).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (as quoted in the exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question: the six terms are a visible guess. Saying that the interferometer composes space, gradient, and time is a binding from the physics frame onto held atoms, not a discovery of nature's own list. That binding is allowed; claiming it settles quantum gravity is not.

## 4. The decisive essay for this question: source, not reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched 2026-11-23) supplies the exact mechanism this question needs. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

The interferometer fringe is the green reading. Classical, semiclassical, and quantum-gravitational couplings can all produce the same phase. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a statement that the phase "shows" quantum gravity. This is independently corroborated by the experimental literature (Dobkowski et al: "such models may also predict the same phase"; Tino: "the same experiment can have different interpretations"; Carney et al: an open question about Newton-potential entanglement), which is why F5 in `01` is marked `gap` rather than a framework-only opinion.

## 5. Q: Does the framework name the missing thing here?

**No.** It returns the shape: an observable whose value separates the channels. It does not say whether that observable will be entanglement of atom interferometers, clock-energy entanglement, decoherence of a levitated mass, or something not yet proposed. Each of those is a binding from a world frame onto the shape, provenanced as such. The Tobar et al result ("neither required nor implied") is a direct warning that even a successfully observed entanglement signal does not automatically name the source: the instrument question returns.

**Does any file in this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the two verdicts stay separate. `01` F1 is `grounded` on the fringe; `01` F5 is `gap` on the source. Blending them into "atom interferometry has tested quantum gravity" would be the source-named-from-a-reading error the essay names.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (fetched 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous groups who agree that interferometry tests quantum gravity is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the physics claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
