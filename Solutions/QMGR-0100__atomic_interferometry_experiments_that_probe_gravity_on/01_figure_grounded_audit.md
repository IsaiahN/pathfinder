## 0. The residual in effect terms

What is an atom interferometer doing when it "probes gravity on quantum states," and what does the resulting fringe settle?

In effect terms: a matter wave is split at a beam splitter into two spatially separated arms; gravity acts with a different potential on the two arms; the arms are recombined at a second beam splitter; the output population difference is a **relative phase** attributable to gravity. The measuring instrument is the interferometer; the measured quantity is a phase; the thing being probed is the coupling of gravity to quantum matter.

The residual R is the gap between what that phase licenses and what the question asks. It has two conditions:

- **Condition A (measurement):** is the phase a real, non-updating reading of gravity acting on a quantum state?
- **Condition B (inference):** does that phase decide whether gravity itself is quantum, rather than a classical background in which quantum matter evolves?

These are not one hard question. Fig 9 names the shape: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :55-57, capacity text under the union/partition panel). One loop measures a phase; a different loop decides what the phase's source is. Averaging them produces a sentence like "atom interferometry has tested quantum gravity," which is the error this audit refuses.

**What an answer would look like, bound before the run (formula step "Direction").** A **source-discriminating interferometric observable**: a measured quantity whose predicted value differs between a classical gravitational channel and a quantum gravitational channel while the phase-only readings agree. Examples actually under development are gravitationally induced entanglement between two interferometers, and clock-based proposals in which internal energy contributes to active gravitational mass. Binding this term now makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else, instead of an invisible preference.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**
- Arity: two conditions (A and B), hence a union compound, not one residual.
- Symmetry: the interferometer is a comparison between two arms. The measured phase is a difference, and the bond that records it is ordered (sequence → for the pulse order, comparison ⋛ for the arm contrast). It is not a symmetric join of two equal operands.
- Scale: tabletop and mesoscopic. The experiment composes the quantum micro scale with the gravitational macro scale, so it sits exactly on the membrane Fig 4 describes.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The phase is a genuine anchor (grounded)

Fig 2 :29 quotes the anchor rule above. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." The fringe counter in a cold-atom interferometer is an instrument that returns a number independent of anyone's theoretical commitment. The 2025/2026 Quantum Galileo Interferometer measured the free-fall gauge phase at the predicted prefactor with residuals of about 2.5 percent (Dobkowski et al, arXiv:2502.14535), and dual-species Rb interferometers have pushed the Eötvös parameter toward 10 to the minus 12 (Tino 2021 review, section 5). These are settled measurements.

**Verdict:** `grounded`, domain D1. The honest sentence is: these experiments measure a gravitational phase on quantum matter. "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence" (Fig 2 :31), so their agreement with classical gravimetry is triangulation, not a shared pool trick.

**Strain:** none at this level.

### F2 — The residual is a union compound, not one hard problem (specified)

Fig 9 :55-58 records the union rule: unbundle before searching. The phase-measurement loop and the source-identification loop have different grounds: the first closes on a fringe; the second closes only on an observable that discriminates channels. Herrmann, Dittus and Lämmerzahl (2012) put the conceptual half directly: "Since in the quantum domain all objects are extended, it is not clear whether the notion of a WEP in the quantum domain makes sense at all." That is a question about the term, not about the fringe.

**Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 9 (union/partition) and the independent 2012 foundations paper (low overlap: relativity/foundations group, not this simulation).

**Refuter:** show that one and the same observable both measures the phase and identifies its source; then the two conditions collapse to one and the union reading is wrong. As of the sources read, no such observable is reported.

### F3 — The interferometer is a legal cross-kind composition (general structural)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

The interferometer parses as: space (arm separation) bonded through a structure (the gravitational potential difference, a gradient) to time (the pulse sequence); the atom's internal energy (a state) is read through the laser frequency (a spectrum structure); the beam-splitter and mirror pulses form a network structure that holds the arms in relation. No state bonds directly to another state, and no structure bonds directly to another structure without a state between. The composition is legal. Fig 13 :76 adds a useful discrimination: "A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." A gravimeter and a clock interferometer are different molecules, not two names for one.

**Verdict:** `general`, structural, triangulated (Fig 13 and the formula substrate section). The claim is about the shape of the composition, not about any experimental number.

**Refuter:** exhibit an interferometer geometry admitted by the framework in which two states or two structures bond with no intervening opposite-kind term, and which cannot be refused. Then Fig 13 does not govern this domain and re-census is owed.

### F4 — Independent instruments corroborate the equivalence-principle result (specified)

Fig 2 :31-32 and Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Atomic tests of the weak equivalence principle (85Rb against 87Rb, K against Rb, Sr against Sr, bosons against fermions, different spin orientations) use quantum matter waves, while torsion balances and MICROSCOPE use macroscopic test masses. They reach the same term by different closures. Tino 2021 records the comparison explicitly and the convergence of precision.

**Verdict:** `specified`. The corroboration is real but bounded: it is evidence for the equivalence principle on quantum matter, not evidence that gravity is quantum. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Strain:** these instruments share Earth's gravity as a common cause. That is not the common-cause collapse of Fig 2 panel 2, because the ground is supposed to be common; the collapse is when what they agree on is a hidden *source* rather than a ranged measurement. Named, not hidden.

### F5 — The phase cannot comment on its source (the decisive finding, gap for condition B)

This is the load-bearing point, and it has three independent witnesses.

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28): "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." And: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's color case is exactly the interferometer case: a green reading does not select among one green source, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Independent corroboration, from the actual experimental literature (low overlap with the framework essays):

- Dobkowski et al (arXiv:2502.14535, 2025/2026), the QGI measurement: "we will not be excluding all possible models for which the EP does not hold, as such models may also predict the same phase." The experimenters themselves refuse the inference from phase to theory.
- Tino (2021): "the same experiment can have different interpretations and physical implications depending on the results and on the underlying theoretical model."
- Carney et al (Snowmass 2021 white paper, arXiv:2203.11846): "If we observe entanglement generation through the Newton potential, is there a precise sense in which this actually demonstrates that the gravitational field is quantized?" A number of authors argue yes; the paper lists dissenting opinions.

**Verdict:** `gap` for condition B. This is not silence. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figures return the shape of the missing discriminator, not its name: an observable on which classical-channel and quantum-channel predictions separate while the phase agrees.

**Refuter:** publish a source-discriminating observable, measured, with two predictions that differ and one ground reading that selects between them. Then condition B moves from `gap` to `grounded` and this finding is withdrawn.

### F6 — The discriminator is reachable, not unreachable (specified)

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."

Atom interferometry already returns a phase that fails to resolve the source. That is a proto-instrument with an edge to extend from. The extension is visible in the literature: Howl, Cooper and Hackermüller (arXiv:2304.00734) place two atom interferometers in parallel and look for correlations in atom-number statistics; the required total mass is near the Planck scale, reduced by squeezing. Carney et al describe the second class of proposal: rule out models of gravity that cannot generate entanglement between massive objects. Tobar, Aspelmeyer and Zych (arXiv:2611.08313, 2026) propose a levitated particle entangled with an array of nuclear clocks in a regime where internal energy contributes to active gravitational mass.

**Verdict:** `specified`, single-source for the figure rule (Fig 6), corroborated by three independent physics groups. Reachable but not reached. The costs are explicit in the sources: Planck-scale mass, long coherence, shielding of Casimir and magnetic backgrounds, and the interpretation debate recorded in Carney et al.

**Round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Sending up "we measure a gravitational phase on a quantum state" and bringing down "therefore we test quantum gravity" drops three constraints: the source-discrimination requirement (F5), the two-condition split (F2), and the distinction between a method that crosses scales and a recording that does not (F4, Fig 4 :22). R_T is nonzero and is enumerated in `11_audit.md`.

### F7 — The chain stops at the vocabulary/objective link (specified)

Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Precision improvements at link 1 or 2 cannot rescue a missing link 3, which is the ability to say what would count as progress on condition B. Precision is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict: Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Verdict:** `specified`. More sensitivity is bought by better instruments; it does not by itself buy a quantum-gravity result.

### F8 — Prestige and agreement are not instruments (general)

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." Fig 2 :35 and Fig 7 :13 do the same work structurally. A famous group's endorsement of "atom interferometry probes quantum gravity" is a budget event, not a ground event.

**Verdict:** `general`, triangulated across the essay and Fig 2/Fig 7 (low overlap between the prose and the figure text, but same author and evidence pool, so declared as internal consistency rather than independent corroboration).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the same discipline the exemplar cites from the seat guide: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Source discrimination achieved:** a measured observable separates a classical gravitational channel from a quantum one while phase-only readings agree, with the two predictions stated in advance. Then F5's `gap` is closed and condition B moves toward `grounded`.
2. **Quantum universality of free fall fails:** two interferometers with different quantum internal states but identical trajectories return different gravitational phase per unit mass. Then F1's equivalence-principle reading is refuted.
3. **Substrate rule fails:** an interferometer composition is admitted by the framework in which two states or two structures bond with no intervening opposite-kind term and cannot be refused. Then F3 is refuted and re-census is owed.
4. **The discriminator shape fails:** a non-entangling local classical model is shown to reproduce every proposed GIE observable, including clock-energy proposals. Then F6's reachable shape collapses and the honest verdict becomes unreachable-at-this-budget, not merely unreached.
5. **Union reading fails:** one observable is shown to both measure the phase and identify the source. Then F2 is withdrawn and the two conditions merge.

Until one fires, no part of condition B may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Strains, honestly recorded

1. **⋛ operand type at the readout.** The Operators table defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities." In the interferometer the operands are two path phases, which are quantities, so the type is clean at first order. It becomes strained if one tries to compare two settled molecules-now-atoms at a higher level, as the exemplar also records. Named, not hidden; it does not bear on this question's verdict.
2. **Common cause.** D1 and classical gravimetry share Earth's gravity. This is a common ground, not a common hidden source, but the distinction is a reading and is recorded.
3. **Overlap of world sources.** The physics papers cite one another, so they are moderately overlapping witnesses, not fully independent ones. They are still independent of the framework essays, which share one author with the figures. Overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
4. **Interference measurement is theory-laden at the source level but not at the fringe level.** This is not a contradiction; it is exactly the metamerism point the source essay makes. The fringe is ground; the source is unreached.

**After re-census: four strains named, none hidden, no verdict flipped.**
