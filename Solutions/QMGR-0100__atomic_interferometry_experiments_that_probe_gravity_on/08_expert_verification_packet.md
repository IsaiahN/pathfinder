# Expert verification packet — the Tether on atom interferometry and gravity

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimental physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `grounded` on the phase measurement, `specified` on the structural shape, `gap` on the quantum-gravity inference.** No measurement of a source-discriminating observable is claimed. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An atom interferometer measures a gravitational phase on quantum matter, and that fringe is a real anchor: the measurement half of the question is grounded. But the phase does not identify its own source; classical, semiclassical and quantum gravitational couplings can predict the same phase, so the inference "therefore gravity is quantum" is a gap, not a result. Structurally, the interferometer is a legal cross-kind composition of the six-term set (`Space → Gradient → Time` for the arms, `Energy → Spectrum → Time` for the internal-state readout, a Network geometry and a ⋛ comparison of two phases). What is missing is a source-discriminating observable, a shape the framework can state but not name. That instrument is reachable from existing interferometry, and current entanglement and clock proposals are attempts to build it. The claim stops there with falsifiers written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading the figure text

- **Statement:** the interferometer is a cross-kind composition; no state bonds directly to another state, and the readout is a comparison of two quantities.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :74 — "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Also Fig 13 :75 — "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." And Fig 13 :76 — "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."
- **What to check:** does the parse `Space (arm separation) → Gradient (potential difference) → Time (free-fall interval)` and `Energy (internal state) → Spectrum (laser frequency) → Time (pulse timing)` hold under those lines? Does the ⋛ readout operate on two phase quantities, which is what the Operators table requires?
- **Refuter:** exhibit an interferometer composition admitted by the framework in which two states or two structures bond with no intervening opposite-kind term and cannot be refused; or show that the readout's operands are not quantities at that level.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by an experimental physicist against the literature

- **Statement:** a measured gravitational phase on a quantum matter wave does not, by itself, identify the source of the coupling. This is the physics claim behind the `gap` verdict.
- **Corroborating sources actually read:** Dobkowski et al (arXiv:2502.14535, 2025/2026), the Quantum Galileo Interferometer: "we will not be excluding all possible models for which the EP does not hold, as such models may also predict the same phase." Tino (2021, *Quantum Science and Technology* 6 024014): "the same experiment can have different interpretations and physical implications depending on the results and on the underlying theoretical model." Carney et al (Snowmass white paper, arXiv:2203.11846): "If we observe entanglement generation through the Newton potential, is there a precise sense in which this actually demonstrates that the gravitational field is quantized?"
- **What to check:** is there any published atom-interferometry result whose phase reading, on its own, discriminates a classical from a quantum gravitational channel? If yes, C2 is false.
- **Refuter:** publish such an observable, measured, with both channel predictions stated in advance and one ground reading selecting between them. The `gap` closes and the claim is withdrawn.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a physics consequence

- **Statement:** the discriminator is reachable, not unreachable, because the instrument is improved from one already returning a signal; and the existing proposals are members of one functional group, not isomers as written.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 — "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34 — "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." Fig 12 :117 — "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :124 — "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."
- **What to check:** do the atom-interferometer GIE proposal (Howl et al, arXiv:2304.00734), the massive-object proposals reviewed by Carney et al, and the clock proposal (Tobar et al, arXiv:2611.08313) share one atom set? They do not as written, which is why the functional-group reading is used.
- **Refuter:** show a non-entangling local classical model that reproduces every proposed entanglement observable, including clock-energy proposals, or show two proposals with identical atoms and bonds giving different source-discrimination predictions.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that atom interferometry has already demonstrated quantum gravity. The measurement is grounded; the inference is a gap.
- Not that gravitationally induced entanglement alone proves quantised gravity. Tobar et al: "introducing quantized gravitational degrees of freedom is neither required nor implied by gravity induced entanglement."
- Not that the equivalence principle is violated. Current atomic tests are consistent with it.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room.
- Not a derivation of general relativity from quantum mechanics, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded` for condition B. Only the phase readings themselves are grounded.
- Not a ground reading of this derivation: it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Channel test:** a measured observable on which classical and quantum gravitational channels predict different values while phase-only readings agree. Closes the gap.
2. **Quantum universality of free fall:** two interferometers with different internal quantum states and identical trajectories returning different gravitational phase per unit mass. Refutes the grounded equivalence-principle reading.
3. **Entanglement interpretation:** an entanglement signal that survives the non-entangling classical-channel accounts kept open by Carney et al and Tobar et al.
4. **Substrate test:** derive one of the six terms from the other five, or admit an unrefusable state-state or structure-structure composition.
