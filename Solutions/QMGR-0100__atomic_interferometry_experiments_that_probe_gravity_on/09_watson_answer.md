## QUESTION

Option B: keep the two conditions separate, treat the phase as a grounded measurement, and report the quantum-gravity inference as a gap with the shape of a source-discriminating observable. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Two verdicts, kept separate. **The phase measurement is grounded:** atom interferometers measure gravity acting on quantum matter, and the fringe is a genuine anchor. **The quantum-gravity inference is a gap:** the phase cannot comment on its source; classical and quantum channels can predict the same reading. The structural shape is legal and `general`: the interferometer composes across the two kinds, the readout is a comparison of two phase quantities, and no new primitive is needed. What is missing is an observable on which the channels separate while phase-only readings agree. It is reachable from existing interferometry, and the current GIE and clock programs are attempts to build it, but none has settled the source. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result for the shape and a `grounded` result for the measurement. The strongest outreach point is the source principle: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on its source, and the answer stays unreached until a better instrument exists.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, separates the two verdicts, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by presenting a phase as a quantum-gravity result, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the grounded/gap split and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The naive inference is removable without touching the measurement claim; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured source-discriminating observable with two channel predictions stated in advance would close the gap; two interferometers with different internal states returning different gravitational phase per unit mass would break the grounded equivalence-principle reading; a non-entangling local classical model reproducing every GIE and clock observable would collapse the discriminator shape; a derivation of one of the six from the other five, or an unrefusable state-state composition, would break the structural shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
