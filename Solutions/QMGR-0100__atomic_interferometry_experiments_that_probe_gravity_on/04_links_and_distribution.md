## Two findings from the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The phase does not identify its source.** The QGI experimenters write that they are "not excluding all possible models for which the EP does not hold, as such models may also predict the same phase." Tino records that "the same experiment can have different interpretations and physical implications depending on the results and on the underlying theoretical model." These are independent of the framework essays and corroborate Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Do not present a phase measurement as a quantum-gravity result.
2. **The discriminator is reachable but costly and contested.** Carney et al frame the goal as ruling out non-entangling models and record a live interpretive dispute. Howl et al put the requirement near the Planck mass scale, reducible by squeezing. Tobar et al argue that entanglement alone does not imply quantised gravitational degrees of freedom, and propose a clock-energy regime. State the cost and the dispute together; omitting either produces a misleading pitch.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the two verdicts and the refuter. Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** — "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if a source-discriminating observable existed before giving them the GIE papers.
- **Fig 2 :35** — "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement, and do not select recipients for likely agreement (Seat Map :163 as quoted in the exemplar).
- **Fig 9 :60** — "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Journal prestige and institutional affiliation are contact-cost filters only.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the grounded/gap split and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and labels the mode of what they get.
