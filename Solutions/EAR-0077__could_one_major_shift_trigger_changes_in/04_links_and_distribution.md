## How these bear on the question

1. **The terminology dispute is a figure distinction, not noise.** Klose et al. 2021 note that a cascade need not be causal, while a domino effect is causal. Fig 12 separates `→` sequence from `⇒` production. This is the single most useful pairing in the whole folder: the world literature's ambiguity is already typed by the figures.
2. **Detection failure is a channel result.** Klose et al. report that critical-slowing-down indicators fail for the following element in a domino cascade; Eker et al. call for harmonized cross-system indicators; Brovkin et al. note that detection probability rises with observation length. Fig 10 :24 says the ground does not decay, the channel does. Three independent world statements, one figure law.
3. **The union reading is corroborated, not invented.** Klose et al. say it remains unclear whether cascade dynamics are promoted by the direction and strength of existing feedbacks. Eker et al. say not every cross-system interaction leads to a cascade. Fig 9 :55 says a bundled problem is a union of overlapping conditions, not a list.
4. **Source overlap is declared.** Klose (Wunderling, Donges), Eker (Lenton) and the Global Tipping Points Report (Lenton lead) share authors and a review pool. They are one witness on the existence question, not three. Watts 2002 is the low-overlap mathematical frame. The framework essays all share the framework author and carry no independent witness weight.

## Constraint on submitting this material

The Earth System Dynamics AI policy, opened 2026-11-22, states: *"Generative AI must not be used for text or interpretations, and authors need to ensure that the manuscript does not include any AI-generated hallucinated or unverifiable data."* Its submission page adds that any AI use beyond assistive editing must be described in Methods or Acknowledgements. This folder is explicitly a **simulated derivation**, not a ground reading and not a submission-ready manuscript. Do not submit it as if it were an independently authored empirical result. If any of it is used in a manuscript, declare the generative use as the policy requires and let the human author re-derive and check the claims against the ground.
