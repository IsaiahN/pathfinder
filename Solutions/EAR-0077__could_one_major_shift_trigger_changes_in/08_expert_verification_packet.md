**What this is:** a claim in a form an Earth-system researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural sub-claims, `general` on the union framing, `gap` on the named chain. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Contact propagates outward beyond first-order enumeration, so a shift in one Earth-system element can change a receiver that is several links away; the residual is indexed per receiver slot, not averaged into a global mean; a "trigger" is a bond-type claim, where a sequence and a production are different molecules; amplification requires a re-entrant loop with a positive sign, because a chain cannot close by construction; and a failed early-warning indicator is evidence about the observing channel, not about the receiver's safety. Reversal is asymmetric, and the conditions form a union. The named chain is `gap`: the figures return a shape and a refuter, not an inventory of which element tips which.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11, 1 and 12

- **Statement:** cross-element propagation is a figure law; the residual must be per slot; the link must be typed before the word trigger is used.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52 to :53, *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :44, *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 1 :19, *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 12 :72, *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*; Fig 12 :77, *"A produces B; B did not exist before."*
- **Refuter:** a measured case with no positive residual two or more links out while the channel stays reachable; or a global scalar that predicts per-element crossings with no per-slot residual left over.

### C2 — physics-facing, checkable against a cascade record or model

- **Statement:** a domino cascade is the degenerate chain, and a two-phase or joint cascade is a common-cause or loop case; they are isomers over a fixed atom set, with the fixed-atom condition stated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :48, *"A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."* Fig 12 :121, *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 2 :6 to :7, *"collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)."*
- **What to check:** does the empirical configuration have re-entrant feedback, or is it a one-way transfer into an element already near threshold? Klose et al. 2021 report that critical-slowing-down indicators fail for the following element in a domino cascade but are partly sensitive in a two-phase cascade; does that hold in the record the expert knows?
- **Refuter:** a cascade with no re-entrant loop and amplification fully explained by linear superposition; or a pattern that requires an atom another pattern does not, which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim standing (Fig 12 :123).

### C3 — detection and decision, checkable against an observing record

- **Statement:** the ground does not decay, the channel does; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :25, *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 2 :42, *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **What to check:** in the expert's own case, was the absence of a warning due to the receiver being safe, or to a saturated or pooled reading? Was the decision rule a convention for what counts as evidence, or did it silently decide the outcome?
- **Refuter:** a detector that reliably signals the second element before the first fires, repeatedly, in the domino configuration; or a decision rule demonstrably tracking the ground without deciding an outcome.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named element tips another, and not a threshold, date, probability or timescale. That is `gap`.
- Not that the figures settle the Earth-system question. They decide the shape; the ground decides the instance.
- Not that the DDT case supplies the physical medium. It supplies the shape of contact propagation.
- Not that all cascades are causal. Fig 12 separates sequence from production; the field's own terminological dispute is recorded, not overruled.
- Not that agreement among assessments corroborates the claim. They share authors and a review pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.
- Not a final theory. Fig 13 :90, *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*

## 6. What would settle it

1. A source-discriminating measurement of a coupling medium, separating a common driver from direct production.
2. A receiver-side early-warning record that stays reachable through the first element's transition.
3. A return experiment distinguishing subtraction of a condition from substitution of a habitat (Fig 11 :66).
4. A per-element residual vector across three or more elements (Fig 1 :19).
