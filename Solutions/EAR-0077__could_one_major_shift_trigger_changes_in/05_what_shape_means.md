## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *if one element crosses a threshold, receiver slots in contact through a carrying medium may acquire positive residual, and the chain amplifies only where a positive feedback loop closes.*
- A name: *the AMOC will tip the Amazon within a decade at 2 °C.* The figures do not return that, and the world sources read here do not settle it.

The distinction is not pedantry. It is the difference between a checkable constraint with a refuter and an ungrounded forecast with no failure condition.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :27 to :28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* In this question, "cascade" is the borrowed word; "per receiver slot, medium, bond type, loop sign" is the effect description that replaces it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundle: if the final exhaustive search does not answer, one possibility is *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"* and the remedy is to search each individually and combine them. Fig 9 :55 makes the same move: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The cascade question is such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that forbids short-circuiting this. Metamerism: a color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources could give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The essay's resolution is explicit: the case *"was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: observing that element B changed after element A tipped does not identify the coupling source. A common driver, a shared medium, or a third element could produce the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the coupling. This is why F9 is `gap` and why the email refuses to name a chain.

## 4. Correction to my own reading of the "shape, not a name" rule

**I initially overstated it.** The README phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The coupling media and network structures invoked here (moisture transport, ocean circulation, food-web and river connections) are not new primitives proposed by this derivation; they are bindings read off the world frame onto structures the figures already hold, chiefly the network atom, *"that entities relate, and influence travels"* (Fig 13 :24).

- Naming a coupling is therefore **provenance work**, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
- It is also **not an import that moves the wall**: Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The binding adds no atom; it attaches a world name to a held atom.
- What *is* forbidden is claiming the figure returns that name, or smuggling in a genuinely new mechanism without a source gate and a shadow test.

The metamerism point in §3 is **not retracted**. Naming the source of a cross-element reading from the reading alone remains the error. Both claims are kept visible: the limit applies to sources not discriminated by an instrument, not to every use of a world name. This is the same correction the exemplar records in its own §5b, carried forward rather than silently applied.

## 5. Q — Does the shape rule forbid saying which elements are connected?

**No, with a condition.** It forbids deriving the connection from the figures. It does not forbid reporting a connection that the ground has already settled and provenancing it. Report it as: *"The world literature reports a coupling from A to B through medium M, with such-and-such support, as a binding from that frame; the figures neither produce nor certify it."* Until the source-discriminating instrument exists, the specific chain stays a candidate.

**Does this package commit the forbidden error?** No. It states the shape, types the bonds, and records `gap` for the named chain. The one place where world names enter (AMOC, Amazon, ice sheets, coral) is as provenanced bindings from sources opened 2026-11-22, not as figure outputs.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a cascade claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground decide.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the Earth-system claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this evidence pool and is a mirror with a different name, not a witness.

## 7. What the shape is for this question, in one line

The shape is: **per receiver slot, contact enumerated outward, bond typed before the word trigger is used, loop sign checked for closure, channel checked for reachability, and reversal treated as substitution rather than subtraction.** Everything else in this folder is a refuter, a provenance record, or an honest `gap`.
