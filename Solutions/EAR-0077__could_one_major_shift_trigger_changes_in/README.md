# Solution EAR-0077 — figure-grounded package

**Question:** Could one major shift trigger changes in other parts of the Earth system?

**Source:** `earth systems.txt` line 102.

**Section:** 8. Tipping points, thresholds, and early warning.

**Mode:** `specified` on the structural shape (contact propagation, per-slot indexing, bond type, closure of feedback loops, channel decay, asymmetry). `general` on the stopping rule and the union framing. `gap` on which named element tips which named element, at what threshold, on what timescale. Nothing here is `grounded`: no measurement was taken, and the figures do not return an empirical cascade inventory.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

**Standing of this derivation.** Every substantive file below is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22) says LLM output is an approximate simulation and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package therefore cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures decide which tipping element actually triggers which. That is a `gap`, recorded as a verdict and not as silence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A shared evidence pool is one witness.** Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Four assessments that cite the same reviews and the same lead authors are one pool, not four corroborations.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels are used throughout `03`. Personal emails harvested for unsolicited outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and would get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
