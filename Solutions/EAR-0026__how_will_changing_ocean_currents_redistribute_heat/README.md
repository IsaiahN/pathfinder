## What an answer would look like (the bound ordinary term)

An answer would say, for each transported quantity and each basin and depth slot: the sign of the change, the magnitude, the time window, the mechanism, and what measurement would falsify it. That shape is written down here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused (formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66). The figures return only part of it. They return the grammar of the redistribution (a flow composed from energy and a gradient, a habitat enumerated by contact, verdicts indexed per slot, agreement that does not substitute for an anchor). They do not return the numbers. The honest answer is the shape plus the instrument specification plus a pre-registered refuter, with the numerical slots left open as a `gap`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals and no receptiveness ranking of people.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that a model-ensemble agreement is corroboration.
- No numerical forecast of heat, nutrient or carbon redistribution. The figures do not contain one and no measurement was taken. Reporting one would be a verdict in `general` mode, which is the named output-layer defect.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement is not an anchor.
2. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". CMIP-class models and reanalyses largely share observations and model ancestry.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.
