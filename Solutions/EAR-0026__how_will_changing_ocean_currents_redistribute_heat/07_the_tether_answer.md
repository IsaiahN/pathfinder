# The Tether's answer on changing currents and redistribution

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain shape, `general` on the structural split, explicit `gap` on the numbers. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No ocean measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a direction and magnitude of redistribution. Stated in effect terms, the residual is the gap between what a held picture of ocean transport predicts about where heat, nutrients and carbon end up and what the observing system reads under a changed circulation. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Heat, nutrients and carbon have different currencies and different anchors, so one lever does not clear their intersections.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before answering.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." There is no single redistribution percentage. There are per-quantity, per-basin, per-depth and per-window verdicts, kept separate and never averaged.

2. **Heat transport is a flow, and the gradient is bonded to a state.** Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." So a meridional heat transport and a seasonal heat flux are different terms, not spellings. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The physical carrier is a legal cross-kind composition; no new primitive is needed.

3. **The residual is per slot, not per globe.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A global heat content trend can be near zero while one basin carries a live residual (Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"). The slots are basin x depth x window for heat, region x nutrient x trophic level for nutrients, and pump x depth x basin for carbon.

4. **Nutrient and carbon effects are cascades.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." A current change is first order; the consequences for productivity, community composition, carbon export and fisheries are three steps out. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same weakening cools one basin and warms another, so there is no standing list of beneficial or harmful circulation changes.

5. **Observation and enumeration come before model judgement.** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." The anchors here are RAPID, OSNAP, Argo and BGC-Argo. They do not update on what a model concludes (Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update.").

6. **Agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Model families and reanalyses share observations and ancestry; their agreement is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict about magnitudes.

7. **Threshold change is a different object.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." The overturning is a cycle with a salt-advection feedback, so smooth weakening and a regime transition need separate loops and separate instruments.

8. **No last forecast.** Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures return the shape, the refuters and the instrument specification. They do not return a transport number. That slot is a `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a basin-specific heat, nutrient or carbon forecast, and not a date for a threshold crossing.
- Not that model consensus settles magnitude. Consensus is a budget filter, never a witness (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
- Not that the global mean heat content trend answers the question. It is the aggregate that hides the live slot.
- Not that a binding of a held atom (current, gradient, pump) is a new primitive or a wall-moving import.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any mooring or float was made for this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Trend versus variability:** continue RAPID to at least 2034 so a 30-year record separates an anthropogenic trend from decadal variability. The NOC's Moat states the requirement directly.
- **Source of a heat content change:** distinguish AMOC redistribution from surface forcing and internal oscillations using wave-propagation timing, basin heat budgets and reanalysis intercomparison, with the shared-evidence caveat recorded.
- **Nutrient and carbon slots:** sustained BGC-Argo nitrate, oxygen, pH and chlorophyll profiles plus sediment traps; resolve submesoscale fronts, which Guo et al. 2024 show can change carbon export enough to bias basin estimates.
- **Cycle sign:** a measurement of present-day freshwater transport and mixed-layer depth that fixes the sign of the overturning feedback.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5: union refuter, flow refuter, slot refuter, cascade refuter, anchor refuter, gap refuter, kind refuter.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The numerical redistribution is out of reach for this derivation at this budget; it is reachable with a longer observing record and finer biogeochemical coverage. Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." The stop is recorded with its lineage so a reader can locate the error. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not forecast how much heat, nutrients or carbon a changing current will move, and it does not claim that model agreement settles the question. It says that the three are a union of overlapping conditions with different currencies and anchors, so verdicts must be kept per quantity, per basin, per depth and per window rather than averaged into one number; that heat transport is a flow of energy bonded to a gradient, where a gradient over space and one over time are different terms; that the residual must be indexed per slot because a global mean hides the live signal; that nutrient and carbon consequences are contact cascades whose sign flips with the region; and that the observing arrays, not model consensus, are the anchor. It treats a threshold crossing as a different kind of object from a smooth trend, with its own loop. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument specification that would close the gap. The numerical slots stay open until the ground settles them.
