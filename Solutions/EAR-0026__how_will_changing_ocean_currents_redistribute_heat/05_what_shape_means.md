## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form any redistribution answer must take before we can name the numbers: three transported quantities with different currencies, a flow composed from energy and a gradient, slots indexed per basin and depth, cascades three steps out, and an anchor that is an observing system. A numerical forecast is a name, not a shape.

Concretely: "the subpolar North Atlantic loses heat while the Indo-Pacific gains it on a centurial window, with the Southern Ocean as mediator" is already a world result, not a figure result. The figure-derived shape is weaker and more portable: *the residual is per-slot, the transport is a flow, the biological consequence is a cascade, and agreement is not an anchor.*

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Applied here: describe the effect (a per-slot sign reversal in heat content, a cascade into productivity and export, a model agreement that does not settle magnitude) before picking any figure line or any field. That is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` starts with the residual, not with AMOC.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a transport number is licensed by the RAPID, OSNAP, Argo and BGC-Argo records analysed with an explicit model, not by a figure. Until then, the number is the analyst's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The 173.09 TW and 133.79 TW figures in the Li and Liu paper are world measurements derived from a model experiment; they are not figure outputs, and they are cited as secondary corroboration only.

## 4. Source discrimination: why a reading is not the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects green "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The resolution "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." And: "An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy".

Applied here: a global ocean heat content trend is a reading. Several sources can produce it: a weakened overturning redistributing heat, a change in surface forcing, an internal climate oscillation, or a combination. The reading does not name the source. Liu et al. 2024 report that OHC trend patterns are "sensitive to the selected time period" and that the Pacific pattern reverses between 2006-2013 and 2006-2020. Li and Liu 2026 show that AMOC-induced signals propagate as Kelvin and Rossby waves with a decade lag, so the same basin reading at two different times has different sources. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (longer mooring records, BGC-Argo coverage, tracer inventories), not a name assigned from the projection.

This is the deepest reason the question's "how will" cannot be answered from the figures. The figures can tell us what a discriminator would have to distinguish; they cannot be the discriminator.

## 5. Q  -  what licenses a transport number for a named basin?

**ANSWERED, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework. A number for a named basin and period is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution. The figures return the shape and the refuters, and Fig 9 :32 refuses the rest.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, by construction: it reports the numerical slots as a `gap`. It does not name a source for a reading where several sources could produce it.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any component of an answer. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the frame. Energy, gradient, network, space, time and spectrum are already among the six; a current, a temperature contrast and a biological pump are bindings from the ocean frame onto held atoms. Naming them is a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").

My §4 metamerism point is **not retracted**: a reading still does not determine its source, and a source-discriminating instrument is still owed. What was wrong was treating the "shape, not a name" rule as a blanket prohibition on naming held atoms. Both statements are kept so neither is silently lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous modelling groups whose agreement is quoted as the answer is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the shape a budget to be checked by an instrument.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figure text. It is not a test of any ocean forecast and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
