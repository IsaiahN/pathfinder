## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Heat, nutrients and carbon are not one transported stock. They form a union of overlapping conditions with different currencies and anchors, so verdicts must be kept per quantity, per basin, per depth and per window and never averaged into a single redistribution number. Heat transport is a legal cross-kind flow: energy bonded to a gradient over a state, with a gradient over space and one over time being different terms. The residual is indexed per slot, because a global mean hides the live signal. Nutrient and carbon consequences are contact cascades with residual-relative valence, not first-order transport. The observing arrays are the anchor; model agreement is a budget filter, not a witness. A threshold crossing is a different kind of object from a smooth trend, with its own loop. The numerical slots are reported as a `gap`, with the instrument specification that would close them.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1  -  framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 13

- **Statement:** transport is energy bonded to a gradient over a state; a gradient over space and one over time are different terms; the residual is per slot, not a global mean.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76: "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." Also Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." And Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** does the figure text say this, and does the physical reading of a meridional ocean heat transport as a flow down a density or temperature contrast over space fit the bond without straining it?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a legal heat transport term in which energy is not bonded to a gradient over a state, or show that a gradient over space and one over time are the same term; or show that for the decision at hand the global mean is sufficient and regional sign reversals carry no information.

### C2  -  ocean-facing; checkable by an oceanographer against the literature

- **Statement:** the nutrient and carbon response is a contact cascade whose sign is relative to the residual, and model agreement does not settle magnitudes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **What to check:** is it true in this field that a circulation change propagates into productivity, community composition, export and oxygen several steps out and with opposite signs in different basins? Do the cited model families and reanalyses draw on shared observations and shared model ancestry? Li and Liu 2026 report a 173.09 TW Atlantic heat loss to the Southern Ocean and a 133.79 TW onward transport into the Indo-Pacific; Liu et al. 2024 report that OHC trend patterns are period-sensitive and that reanalyses disagree in places. Does that pattern support the union reading rather than a single consensus number?
- **Refuter:** show that the nutrient and carbon response is strictly first-order in the current anomaly with no trophic or depth cascade; or exhibit two model families with independent instruments and independent assimilation whose agreement survives an effective-sample-size correction.

### C3  -  threshold and gap; framework-internal plus a world check

- **Statement:** smooth weakening and a regime transition are different objects, and no figure line returns a transport magnitude.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** is the overturning's salt-advection feedback a loop whose sign determines closure? Is the present-day sign a measurement question rather than a figure question? Does the cited literature distinguish a "very likely" smooth weakening from a debated threshold event with different definitions of collapse?
- **Refuter:** show the threshold case is continuous with the trend case so one loop and one instrument cover both; or produce a figure line that returns a basin-specific transport magnitude, or a ground reading that settles one.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a numerical forecast of heat, nutrient or carbon redistribution for any basin or period. That is a `gap`, not a withheld answer.
- Not a date for an AMOC tipping or collapse event.
- Not that model consensus settles magnitude (Fig 2 :40; Fig 7 :35).
- Not that the global mean heat content trend answers the question (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- Not a new primitive, not a wall-moving import, and not a derivation of ocean physics from the six-term set. Currents, contrasts and pumps are bindings from the ocean frame onto held atoms, provenanced as such.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement taken for this derivation.
- Not a standing list of beneficial or harmful circulation changes (Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.").

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Trend versus variability:** a RAPID record long enough (to at least 2034) to separate an anthropogenic trend from decadal variability.
2. **Source of a heat change:** wave-propagation timing, basin heat budgets and reanalysis intercomparison with the shared-evidence caveat recorded.
3. **Nutrient and carbon slots:** sustained BGC-Argo nitrate, oxygen, pH and chlorophyll plus sediment traps, resolving submesoscale fronts.
4. **Cycle sign:** a measurement of present-day freshwater transport and mixed-layer depth fixing the sign of the overturning feedback.
5. **Falsifiers:** the seven refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.
