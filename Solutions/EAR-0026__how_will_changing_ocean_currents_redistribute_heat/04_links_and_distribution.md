## What the literature bears on the shape

1. **The world has no single number.** Different groups report different things: Song et al.-style reanalyses disagree about where heat goes; Li and Liu find a mediator role and enhanced uptake efficiency; the IPCC separates qualitative confidence from quantitative confidence. This is evidence for the union reading, not against it. Fig 9 :4-5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
2. **The instrument is the limit.** RAPID needs a longer record to separate trend from variability; BGC-Argo is what makes the nutrient and carbon slots measurable. Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
3. **Fine scales reach basin budgets.** The Lévy review and the Guo result show that 1 to 100 km fronts alter carbon export enough to matter at basin scale. This corroborates the cascade shape and the chaos remainder, at low overlap with the physical heat-transport pool.
4. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Model families share data and ancestry. The IPCC's own qualitative/quantitative distinction matches Fig 2 :40.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would count as a failure before giving them the sources.
- **Open Science / journal AI policies:** the Ocean Science journal page lists an AI policy; the simulated status of this derivation must be disclosed on submission, not discovered by a reviewer.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then send the three public source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) if asked, offer the formula as a method and require each user to write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the shape claim against the reader's own ground. No theory, no framework, no links. Their verdict is recorded before anything else is shown.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they have now seen the frame (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3, on request.** Offer the formula as a method. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write a refuter before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. This folder is a simulated derivation. It is offered for checking, not for belief.
