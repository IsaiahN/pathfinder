## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a held picture of ocean transport predicts about where heat, nutrients and carbon end up and what the observing system reads under a changed circulation. Arity: four, one carrier plus three transported quantities. Symmetry: broken; heat is energy moved by a gradient and is nearly conservative in a basin budget, nutrients are matter transformed by biology, carbon has both a solubility and a biological pump and crosses the air-sea boundary. Scale: basin to global, seasonal to centennial, surface to abyssal, 1 to 100 km fronts upscaling to basin budgets. Fig 9 :57 says a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, so this is a union compound with its own loop each.
- **D1, physical heat transport.** Actors: currents, temperature and salinity gradients, Kelvin and Rossby waves, eddies. Currency: ocean heat content, meridional heat transport, heat uptake efficiency. Anchor: RAPID, OSNAP, Argo temperature profiles, altimetry. Question type: partition boundary, what composes across the two kinds. Dependency: first, fix the anchor and the slot.
- **D2, nutrient and carbon transport.** Actors: nitrate, iron, silicate, phytoplankton, grazers, solubility and biological pumps, fronts. Currency: mass flux, stoichiometric ratios, PgC/yr, export efficiency. Anchor: BGC-Argo nitrate, oxygen, pH and chlorophyll, sediment traps, tracer inventories. Question type: union compound with a contact cascade. Dependency: second, after the carrier is fixed, enumerate the habitat.
- **D3, modelling and assessment frame.** Actors: CMIP-class models, reanalyses, assessment panels. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the observing system, never agreement. Question type: a frame ranging against the ground (Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). Dependency: third, after D1 and D2 are anchored.
- **D4, threshold and route change.** Actors: deep-water formation, salt-advection feedback, convection collapse, bistability. Currency: regime state, threshold distance. Anchor: moorings plus mixed-layer depth records. Question type: a different object from a smooth trend, its own loop. Dependency: parallel, not averaged into D1 to D3.

**Order by dependency.** Observe and enumerate before judging models (Fig 3 :20); settle each first-order budget before second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104); refuse the forecast before minting a number (formula step 2); describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the per-slot rule (Fig 1) and basin-integrated heat budgets (Li and Liu 2026) seemed to conflict. Re-census resolved it by level: a basin is a slot at the next level, and Fig 12 :104 permits a settled molecule to become an atom. Different objects, no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the forecast does not. The three transported quantities form a union with different currencies and anchors, so verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Heat transport is a cross-kind flow with the gradient bonded to a state; the residual is per slot; nutrient and carbon effects are contact cascades with residual-relative valence; observation and enumeration precede model judgement; model-ensemble agreement is a filter, not a witness; and smooth weakening and a regime transition are different objects. The numerical redistribution is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not a silence and not an invitation to invent a number. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording or a numerical forecast before they have described a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The numerical slots are empty and the bindings are provenanced, so a corrected measurement can be inserted without withdrawing the shape. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive has to be removed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A longer RAPID record separating trend from variability, sustained BGC-Argo coverage resolving the nutrient and carbon slots, a source-discriminating instrument distinguishing redistribution from surface forcing, or a measurement fixing the sign of the overturning feedback would move those slots to `grounded`. A figure re-census that returns a transport magnitude would also reverse the `gap`. A union refuter, flow refuter, slot refuter, cascade refuter, anchor refuter, gap refuter or kind refuter firing would break the shape. Prestige and consensus are not such instruments.
