## The question restated as an effect

Inflationary dynamics predict what an observer should see given an already prepared patch: a nearly uniform region, an inflaton displaced onto a flat part of its potential, and a Hubble time long enough for e-folds. The residual is the contact failure between that prepared patch and whatever supplied it. Option A, taking inflation to be self-starting from generic initial data, leaves the past boundary inside the inflationary closure and owes an explanation of why the patch existed. Option B, taking the boundary condition to come from a different closure (quantum cosmology, a pre-inflationary kinetic or contracting phase, or a statistical measure), owes provenance and a shadow test. What an answer would look like is bindable in advance: a shape for the missing item (an imported boundary condition with stated source and a discriminating observation), not necessarily a name.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation, and sending the theory before asking for a verdict spends the only independence an outside reader has.

## Legal note

No emails are harvested or stored. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional and public. Unsolicited bulk email would breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and is not part of this package.
