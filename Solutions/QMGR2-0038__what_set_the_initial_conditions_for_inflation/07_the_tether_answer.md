# The Tether's answer: what set the initial conditions for inflation

**Mode: `general` on the closure and union structure, `specified` on the shape of the missing item, nothing `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

---

## 1. The residual it answers

Inflation predicts the CMB from an already prepared patch. We observe a universe consistent with those predictions: near flatness, n_s near 0.965, no detected tensors, adiabatic perturbations. The contact failure is between the prepared patch and whatever supplied it. It bundles three overlapping conditions: a smooth patch, a field value that lands on the flat part, and a regular past boundary. This is a union compound, not one hard question. A case in three conditions is not a harder case of one.

## 2. One loop per domain

### Loop A. Inflationary effective field theory (`grounded` for predictions, silent on the boundary)

**Actors:** inflaton, potential, expansion, perturbation modes. **Currency:** e-folds and spectra. **Anchor:** CMB and BAO. **Route:** the loop runs normally and predicts observables from assumed data. It cannot run backwards to its own start, because the start is not in its library.

**Verdict for A:** cannot supply its own initial data. `general`, triangulated. Refuter: a checkable internal derivation of the prepared patch from A's own atoms.

### Loop B. Quantum cosmology and Planck-scale frames (open anchor slot)

**Actors:** wave function of the universe, tunneling proposals, landscape vacua, Planck density. **Currency:** weights over initial field configurations. **Anchor:** none settled. This is a candidate frame, not yet a domain with a nameable anchor.

Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall" and Fig 9 :19 "the witness is always imported". Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

**Verdict for B:** a legitimate source of candidate imports, provided each carries provenance and passes a shadow test. `general` for the permission, `specified` for any particular proposal. Refuter: a candidate that closes the residual without adding an atom or explaining the gap.

### Loop C. Measure and typicality (`specified`, no ground)

**Actors:** phase-space volume, attractor basins, Eisenhart lift, Laplace indifference. **Currency:** fractions of initial-condition space. **Anchor:** internal mathematical proof plus a future observational hook.

The world results disagree. Finn 2020 concludes finely tuned initial conditions under his measure; Carrasco, Kallosh and Linde 2015 find natural initial conditions in alpha-attractors. Under Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", their disagreement is not one fact contradicted but a union to split. The figures do not supply a measure.

**Verdict for C:** measure-conditional, not a settlement. `specified`. Refuter: a measure-independent result that selects one verdict.

### Loop D. Pre-inflationary dynamics (`model-dependent`, no ground)

**Actors:** kinetic dominance, anisotropy, contracting or bouncing phase, Planck surface. **Currency:** dynamical evolution toward or away from slow roll. **Anchor:** numerical relativity and Planck-surface analysis within each model class.

Hergt et al 2019 provide a concrete candidate import: for plateau potentials the Planck surface is kinetically dominated for almost all field values of interest. This has a clear shadow test, namely whether that surface actually feeds enough e-folds for the observed potential amplitudes.

**Verdict for D:** a real candidate import with a stated shadow test, not the answer. `specified`. Refuter: a model class in which the Planck surface does not feed slow roll.

### Loop E. Verification frame (this session shares one evidence pool)

**Actors:** expert readers, instruments, public venues. **Currency:** residuals and agreement. **Anchor:** the ground, never agreement.

**Verdict for E:** no corroboration from this simulated run. All domains here draw on one evidence pool. `general`. Refuter: an outside reader who checks stage 1 before seeing the framework.

## 3. The shape

1. **Inflation cannot derive its own prepared patch.** The missing item is not deeper inside the model space.
2. **What is owed is an import.** It must come from a frame whose closure differs, carry provenance and pass a shadow test.
3. **The answer is a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The figures do not choose between no-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, a bounce or a measure.
4. **The question is a union.** Split the smooth patch, the field value and the past boundary before searching again. Clear one condition and the intersections remain.
5. **The habitat is enumerated.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." List the actors in contact with the residual, then their contacts outward.
6. **Unreachable is a verdict.** Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." The name is unreached, not refuted and not absent.
7. **Measure the round trip.** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The Planck-to-CMB compression drops at least three constraints, listed in `01` F8.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that inflation did or did not occur. The question is conditional, and the derivation stays conditional.
- Not that a singular beginning is proved. BGV is a world corroboration, not a figure line, and past incompleteness is not the same claim as a singularity.
- Not that fine-tuning is established. It is measure-conditional and the two world verdicts conflict.
- Not that the CMB reads the past boundary. It reads a projection; several sources can give the same reading.
- Not a new primitive, not a unified theory, and not `grounded`. No observation at the past boundary exists.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. Concretely:

- a finite-onset signature in the large-scale power spectrum with the predicted decaying oscillations;
- a specific non-thermal initial-state signature in the bispectrum or in tensor modes;
- a bounce or pre-inflationary contracting-phase signature that distinguishes it from a no-boundary weight;
- a measure-independent typicality result that survives a change of model class.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: an internal derivation of the prepared patch; a discriminating observation; a reviewed past-completeness result; a failure of idempotent closure; a proof that the three conditions form a partition.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle, and not answered.** Domain B has no nameable anchor, so the slot stays open. Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The derivation stops where the instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. If a later reader supplies a discriminating observation, the shape can be filled and this file superseded. Until then the verdict is `candidate`, `specified`.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say what set the initial conditions for inflation, and it does not claim inflation is false. It says that the inflationary frame can predict the CMB from an already prepared patch but cannot derive that patch from inside its own closure, so what is owed is a boundary condition imported from a different frame with a stated source and a shadow test. The question is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, a smooth patch, a field value that lands on the flat potential, and a regular past boundary, so no single lever clears it. The candidate imports, no-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, a bounce and a measure, are isomers over a shared atom set: same ingredients arranged differently, with different observable behaviour. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, not a name. The slot stays open until a source-discriminating observation closes it, and the refuters are written in advance so a failure is findable.
