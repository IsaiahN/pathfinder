## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the filler

The canonical README says the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, a shape and not a name, and stops where instruments stop distinguishing while recording the lineage. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 states that the system can report it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway, and cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From the Library of Babel essay: you can cut a search short if the set of returned books is small and descriptive enough to provide a general shape of the answer. The essay also says the problem may be a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one, and that such a bundle has to be searched problem by problem and then combined.

Applied here: the shape is *an imported past-boundary condition with a stated source frame, a shadow test, and a discriminating observation*. The name is *no-boundary*, *tunneling*, *kinetic dominance*, *bounce* or *measure*. The framework licenses the first, not the second. The bundle is the three conditions in `01` section 0: smooth patch, field value, regular past boundary.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Proximity bootstrapping.** The Library of Babel essay says to use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then connect and correlate it with recurring new terms until the space narrows. Here the starting vocabulary is residual, closure, import, anchor, habitat and bond. The recurring new terms in the world literature are Planck surface, measure, attractor basin and decoherence.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 9, your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause. In effect terms the missing item is *whatever supplies a smooth, displaced patch from a closure that inflation does not contain*. Calling it a wave function of the universe is already a cause, and is therefore a binding, not a derivation.
- **Range against the residual.** The essay says returned books must be tested against the question to steer the next search. Here a candidate passes only if it explains why the patch existed, not merely why the predictions fit.
- **Stop rule.** The essay says the finish line will also be the starting place, and that by the time you have the exact search terms you have composed the answer through locating it. In this solution the start was the residual and the finish is the same residual with a narrowed class of answers. That is a shape, not a final book.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground pays for it.** The formula says the ground settles a term and a gate is not the ground. Here that means an observation that discriminates among candidate boundary conditions, for example a finite-onset signature or a specific non-thermal initial-state signal. Planck 2018 constrains the consequences but has not delivered this.
2. **A provenanced import with a shadow test.** Fig 6 gives a source gate and a shadow test, and Fig 8 says the surplus lives where closures differ. A candidate from quantum cosmology or phase-space measure theory is licensed only when its origin is recorded and it explains the residual.

The Hard Problem is About Source gives the failure mode. An instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source; several distinct sources provide the same reading. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. With inflation, the CMB reads a projection: spectral index, tensor bound, adiabaticity. Those readings do not determine whether the source was a no-boundary wave function, a tunneling event, a kinetic-dominated Planck surface, a bounce or a measure selection. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." More agreement among readings is not a better source discriminator.

## 4. Why the question is especially prone to a false name

- **Retrodiction risk.** A boundary-condition proposal postdicts the observed uniformity. The exemplar warns that a case the framework describes after the fact is an instance, not a result the framework produced. So a no-boundary or attractor result that explains why we see a patch is not thereby a derivation of the patch.
- **Measure dependence.** Finn 2020 and Carrasco et al 2015 disagree because their measures and model classes differ. Under Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" the reverse also holds: disagreement between frames with different pools is not one fact refuted but a union to split.
- **Theory before instrument.** Fig 6 explicitly allows theory to precede its instrument. So quantum cosmology proposals are not disqualified for lacking a direct observation. They are disqualified only if presented as settled names rather than candidate imports.
- **Prestige leak.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap states that prestige can fund an action but cannot settle it, and that only the ground can settle an action. A famous proposal's authority is a budget to be tried, not a verdict.

## 5. Corrected reading of my own §3

I initially wrote that naming any candidate is forbidden by the shape-not-a-name rule. That overstates it. The rule governs a primitive not held in the frame's library. No-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, bounce and measure are not primitives of the figure set; they are bindings from world frames onto the open boundary slot. Discussing them is allowed, and required for the shadow test. What is forbidden is presenting one as the figure's answer or as an import that moves the wall without provenance. Both the earlier and the narrowed readings are preserved here; nothing is silently erased.

## 6. Simulation warning

From the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: an LLM can quickly simulate an approximate answer with the formula, but the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or by working the formula yourself. That essay also says the output is only as good as the validator and that a frame or theory cannot validate itself. This file is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency. It cannot settle what set the initial conditions for inflation.
