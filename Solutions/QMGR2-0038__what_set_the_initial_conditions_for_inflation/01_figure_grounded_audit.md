## 0. The residual in effect terms

We see a universe consistent with an inflationary past: flat to about 0.4 percent with BAO, scalar index near 0.965, no detected tensor signal, adiabatic perturbations. Inflationary dynamics convert an assumed prepared patch into those observables. The residual is not the fit. It is the contact failure between the prepared patch and whatever supplied it.

- **Arity.** At least three conditions are bundled in the phrase "initial conditions": (i) a kinematic patch, a region large and smooth enough that vacuum energy can dominate locally; (ii) a dynamical entry, a field value and kinetic fraction that actually land on a flat part of the potential and give enough e-folds; (iii) a past boundary, whatever replaces the singular surface when the spacetime is run backwards. A case in three conditions is not a harder case of one. It is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9, the union rule).
- **Symmetry.** The question is about a past boundary, so it is time-asymmetric in the sense that matters here: the arrow of time runs from the prepared patch to us, not the other way. Any proposed answer that is time-symmetric at ultra-large scales must say how the asymmetry appears locally.
- **Scale.** The requested cause lives at the Planck scale or at a past boundary; the evidence lives at CMB and large-scale-structure scales. There is a lossy transform between them. The round trip has to be measured, not assumed zero (Fig 4 :30).

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the run.** An answer would look like this: a characterisation of a boundary condition, stated in effect terms, with a named source frame, a shadow test that shows it explains the residual, and at least one observation that would discriminate it from rivals. It is a shape (an imported atom with provenance), not yet a name. This binding is written down before the derivation so it can accrue a residual like anything else rather than steer the search invisibly.

## 3. Findings

### F1. Inflation cannot supply its own past boundary from inside its closure (decisive, `general`)

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Domain A holds atoms: inflaton, potential, expansion, slow roll, perturbations. Its closure contains predictions conditional on a prepared patch. The prepared patch itself is not in that closure. Running the equations backwards produces a past-incomplete boundary under broad conditions, which is the content of the Borde, Guth and Vilenkin result read as a world corroboration, not as the authority. The figure line decides the structural point: a frame cannot compose an atom it does not hold.

Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."

**Refuter:** exhibit a derivation inside inflationary effective field theory that produces the prepared patch from its own atoms with no boundary condition imported, or show the closure is not idempotent. Either would break F1.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 8. Low overlap: one line states what moves a wall, the other states why search cannot. Two different figures, two different questions.

### F2. The missing item must be an import, and imports carry gates (`general`)

Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall" and Fig 9 :19 "the witness is always imported". The import is not a free pass. Fig 6 gives a provenance source gate and a shadow test: the imported frame must actually explain the gap, and its origin must be recorded. Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."

Applied here: a no-boundary proposal, a tunneling proposal, a kinetic-dominance surface, a bounce, or a landscape measure are candidate imports. Each must say what atom it adds, where it came from, and which feature of the residual it explains. A proposal that merely re-describes the prepared patch adds no atom and fails the shadow test.

**Refuter:** a candidate frame that closes the residual without adding any atom to A, with the derivation shown.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 9 and Fig 13, but the three share the import vocabulary, so the overlap is higher than F1. Marked triangulated on the structural claim only.

### F3. The answer is a shape, not a name (`general`, with a `specified` corollary)

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13, the framework can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway, and it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

So the figures license the shape: an imported past-boundary condition with provenance and a discriminating observation. They do not license the name: no-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, bounce or measure is selected by the derivation. Each is a binding from a world frame, provenanced as such.

The corollary at `specified` mode: any claim that one of those proposals is *the* answer is a mint that has not passed the three guards. Fig 5 :23 "it composes from what we already have" and Fig 5 :28 "keep the gap, look for an import" are the two branches; the world literature supplies candidates for the second, not a settlement.

**Refuter:** a figure-licensed derivation that names one candidate and shows the others fail the shadow test.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 13. Low overlap between the leave-and-arrive figure and the minimum-set figure.

### F4. The question is a union compound, and must be unbundled before search (`general`)

Fig 9's union rule states that a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones, and that a case in two conditions is categorically different from a harder case of either. Here the three conditions in section 0 overlap. A smooth patch can come from a dynamical attractor while the regular past boundary comes from quantum cosmology. A kinetic-dominated start can supply the field value but not the arrow of time.

Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The ranking of questions by tractability is a filter for where to spend effort, never a witness that the boundary condition exists.

**Refuter:** a proof that the three conditions are independent and separately sufficient, so no intersection term remains.

**Status:** single-source for the union rule (Fig 9), triangulated with the world result that different model classes solve different parts of the problem.

### F5. The habitat must be enumerated, not composed (`general`)

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."

Enumerated habitat for this residual, first cascade outward from the patch:

- **Direct contact:** inflaton field value and velocity, potential shape, local curvature, Hubble radius, anisotropy, quantum fluctuations of the field.
- **Second cascade:** Planck density surface, decoherence and coarse graining, the state of scalar modes (thermal vacuum versus alternatives), the measure used to say typical.
- **Third cascade:** a prior contracting or bouncing phase, a landscape of vacua, a wave function of the universe, selection effects and the arrow of time.

Same actor, different valence: an anisotropic shear is a malfactor for the smooth-patch residual but can be a benefactor for a bouncing model's residual. Valence is relative to the residual, not carried (Fig 11).

**Refuter:** exhibit an actor in the habitat that was composed from a vocabulary rather than read off the world, and show the residual changes when it is removed.

**Status:** single-source (Fig 11). The enumeration itself is checkable by any reader against the published literature.

### F6. The two instruments are opposed and must be run as two tracks (`general`)

Fig 6 :29 "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms." Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

**Inward:** sharpen CMB observables. Planck already tests the adiabaticity of initial conditions and constrains features, r and n_s. A finite-onset signal, a large-scale power suppression with decaying oscillations, or a bispectrum shape would be an edge to extend. This track cannot, by sharpening alone, manufacture a reading of the past boundary.

**Outward:** describe the residual in effect terms and find the frame whose closure predicts its shape. Quantum cosmology and phase-space measure theory are candidate frames. They must be described in effect terms, not in jargon only they can read.

Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." There is something returning and failing to resolve, so this is not a search for a proto-instrument that does not exist. Fig 6 :37 permits theory to precede its instrument. Neither track may be read against the other.

**Refuter:** show that no existing observation fails to resolve the boundary, in which case the inward track has no edge and the outward track becomes unmotivated.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 6 :29-32 and Fig 3 :27 (a link with no instrument).

### F7. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (`general`)

Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

Distinguish three statuses carefully:

- **Underivable in principle** from A alone: the boundary atom is not in A's closure. This follows definitionally from F1.
- **Unreachable at this budget**: the atom may be in a frame we already hold but the search did not find it. Remedy is more search, not a new import (Fig 8, the scope rule).
- **Wrong question**: the three bundled conditions have not been split. Remedy is unbundling (F4).

Importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed, and a wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives. So the honest report is: the name is unreached, not the question answered or refused.

**Refuter:** a completed search inside a frame we already hold that finds the boundary condition, showing the wrong import was proposed.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 8, Fig 9 and Fig 13, with attention to their different objects.

### F8. The round trip gap is nonzero (`specified`)

Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **Sent up:** the verbatim figure set, chiefly closure, import, union, habitat and the shape-not-a-name limit.
- **Abstracted:** inflation cannot supply its own initial data; the missing item is an imported boundary condition with provenance; the three conditions must be split.
- **Back down:** the concrete world proposals (no-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, bounce, measure).

Three constraints do not survive the trip back down: (i) which proposal is correct is not licensed by the figures; (ii) the fine-tuning verdict depends on the measure, which the figures do not supply; (iii) the time-asymmetry of our local arrow is not derived by any of the candidate boundary conditions without extra assumptions. R_T is recorded qualitatively as this dropped set. It is not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists at the past boundary.

**Refuter:** a measurement that discriminates the candidate boundary conditions and leaves no dropped constraint.

**Status:** single-source for the requirement to measure the gap (Fig 4), with the gap itself enumerated from the derivation.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter, written before the conclusion

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. These were written before any verdict above was finalised.

1. **Internal derivation refuter.** If a derivation within inflationary effective field theory produces the prepared patch from its own atoms with no imported boundary condition, and the derivation is checkable, F1 and F2 fall.
2. **Discriminating observation refuter.** If a ground-visible observation (finite-onset power suppression with decaying oscillations, a specific non-thermal initial-state signature, a bounce signature, or a measure-independent prediction) selects one candidate and excludes the others, the slot may be named and F3 is downgraded at that level.
3. **Past-completeness refuter.** If an institutionally reviewed result shows past-incompleteness is evaded by a regular past boundary inside a closed frame, the import owed verdict must be re-censed, not flipped by argument.
4. **Framework refuter.** If the import operator is shown to compose from within, or closure is not idempotent, Fig 6 and Fig 8 fail and this entire derivation falls.
5. **Union refuter.** If the three conditions are proved independent and separately sufficient, F4 falls and the loops must be split and rerun.

Until one of these fires, the verdict remains `candidate`, `specified`.
