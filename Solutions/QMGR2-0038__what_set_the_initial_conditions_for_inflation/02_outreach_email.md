# Outreach draft: check the residual before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and all five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are stated in the message. If the stage-1 output cannot survive a twenty-minute check, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One output about inflation initial conditions, and what would break it

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question in effect terms.** Inflationary dynamics predict what we see given an already prepared patch: a smooth region, an inflaton displaced onto a flat potential, and enough e-folds. The residual is the contact failure between that patch and whatever supplied it. Three conditions are bundled in the phrase initial conditions: a smooth patch, a field value that lands on the flat part, and a regular past boundary. They overlap, so a single lever will not clear all three.
>
> **The output (`general` on the structural claim, `specified` on the shape, not `grounded`):** the inflationary frame cannot derive its own prepared patch from inside its own closure. Searching more inflation models cannot add the missing item, because composing inside a closed set never leaves it. What is owed is a boundary condition imported from a frame whose closure differs, with a stated source and an observation that would discriminate it from rivals. That is a shape, not a name. The derivation does not select between no-boundary, tunneling, kinetic dominance, a bounce or a measure.
>
> **What would refute it. I wrote these down before running the derivation:**
> 1. a checkable derivation inside inflationary effective field theory that produces the prepared patch from its own atoms, with no imported boundary condition;
> 2. a ground-visible observation that selects one candidate boundary condition and excludes the others;
> 3. an institutionally reviewed result showing past-incompleteness is evaded by a regular past boundary inside a closed frame;
> 4. a demonstration that the import operator composes from within, or that closure is not idempotent;
> 5. a proof that the three conditions are independent and separately sufficient, so the union reading is wrong.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line reply naming the failure is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that inflation did or did not occur; that fine-tuning is established independent of a measure; that the Borde-Guth-Vilenkin result decides a framework question; that any named proposal is the answer. Those are bindings from world frames and they are recorded as bindings, not derivations.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/docs/01-FOUNDATIONS/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md
> - Canonical figures README and usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The compressed formula: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/THE_FORMULA.md
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is at the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output before theory | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." |
| Ask what would fail, do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | The framework's own discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. |
| Mode stated on every claim | The formula's mode rule: general, specified, grounded; accepted means the ground settled it. |
| Three conditions kept separate | Fig 9 union rule: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. |
| Union and partition distinguished | Fig 9 unbundling rule: clear one condition and the intersections remain. |
| No receptiveness ranking, no bulk send | Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" |
| Links only after a recorded verdict | Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | Fig 4, only methods cross up; head starts go down, never recordings. |
| No trust request, no prestige appeal | The framework separates prestige from action budget: prestige can fund an action, it cannot settle one. |

## Stage boundaries

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the five refuters. Record the reader's verdict on stage 1 before showing anything else.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If and only if the reader is curious after stage 1, offer the three links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they hold the interiors.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a reusable method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Nothing in this package sends anything.** Hosting and sending are the operator's job. Prefer an opt-in download page over a pushed attachment, so a recipient can decline contact.
