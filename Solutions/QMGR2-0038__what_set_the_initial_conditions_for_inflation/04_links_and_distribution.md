## Wider literature that bears on the residual

Grouped by what each source contributes, with overlap declared.

1. **Past-boundary theorem.** BGV supplies a model-independent kinematic reason the inflationary patch is not self-explaining. It corroborates the shape of the residual, but it is a world result, not the figure line that decides.
2. **Naturalness claims in both directions.** Carrasco/Kallosh/Linde and Finn reach opposite verdicts because they use different model classes and different measures. This is the central world conflict. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" applies in reverse too: disagreement across different closures is not a single fact contradicted, it is a union that must be split.
3. **Planck-surface work.** Hergt et al and Handley et al supply the concrete pre-inflationary dynamical frame. Their result that the Planck surface is kinetically dominated for plateau potentials is a candidate import with a clear shadow test.
4. **State selection.** Kaloper et al constrain the quantum state of the inflaton. Carroll/Chen and Linde 2002 supply thermodynamic and anthropic frames. These are different closures, so their agreement is weak corroboration at best.
5. **Observational anchor.** Planck 2018 tests the consequences of initial conditions and constrains features, but does not read the past boundary. This is why Domain B has an open anchor slot and the verdict stops at a shape.
