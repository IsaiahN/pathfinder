## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For COG-0013, "attention change is a change in agent-and-habitat contact, per slot, with a functional-group machinery whose members and bindings vary" is a shape. "Environment E trains feature F in N trials" is a name and a number, and neither is delivered by the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The same essay on bundles: when an exhaustive search fails, *"perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*, and then *"you will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is the union reading of `01` section 1.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the practical version of the effect-term rule, and it is directly about practice: *"An apprentice isn't receiving messages; they're slowly building the history that makes the messages make sense."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* From *How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened 2026-11-22): *"the answer doesn't exist until the question is contacted."* The essay's governing claim is that *"the mapping is the intelligence"*, and that it *"can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it."* That is the transmission shape this question asks about: a head start is inherited, but the bill was paid somewhere.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* Fit is not completeness, so a fluent account of how a practice trains attention is not a measurement of it.

The six-term substrate is a visible guess. Naming a member as the answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one, which is why the feature list in `01` section 5 is `gap`.

## 4. Contact is the governing idea for this question

*How Intelligence Actually Works* names the central term: *"Contact Is the Distinction Resolver."* It then adds that *"the seed is not the recording of a tree, it is instead the encoding of something that will grow one, wherever it lands in conditions that are hospitable."* That is Fig 4 :22 and Fig 11 :39 in another vocabulary, and it is the cleanest statement of why "training" cannot be a transfer of a recording.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the mechanism at human scale. Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"* is quoted there, and the essay adds that *"Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge."* Learning happens *"not only through language but also by observation, imitation, and practice"*, and what gets built *"has to be checked against something nobody can move: the ground."* The habit of attention is exactly the kind of knowledge that travels badly as text and well through contact, which is why the environment-and-practice question is not answered by listing what a culture says it attends to.

## 5. Why an attentional reading cannot identify its training source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the discriminator argument in the shape this question needs. An instrument that detects green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: an eye-tracking pattern, a discrimination threshold, or a change-detection score is a reading. It cannot identify which practice produced it, because immersion, explicit instruction, and a saturated rubric can yield the same reading. The claim "this environment trained that attention" is a source claim made from a projection unless a discriminator exists. What is owed is an instrument, not a story. This is the governing reason for F9 in `01`.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading of "shape, not a name"

Overstatement to avoid: the phrase *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* governs a primitive **not held** in Γ. It does not forbid selecting among atoms already held.

- Goldstone's "attentional weighting", the object-versus-context distinction, and gaze following are **bindings from the world literature onto mechanisms the derivation already holds**, not new primitives invented to make the answer fit.
- What is forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh cognitive primitive or an eighth "culture bond" and (b) claiming that a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* it adds none).
- The correction is recorded here and in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1 rather than silently applied. Earlier drafts of this question treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any mechanism. It is narrower than that.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous laboratories whose researchers agree that practice shapes attention is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against a ground, and the ground is a measurement of a specific observer-regime-feature slot.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This essay also says of the framework *"Because a Metatheory is recursive, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself"* (as carried in *How Intelligence Actually Works*). So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
