## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the change in attentional weighting attributed to an environment or practice. Arity: three-indexed, `(observer, regime, information kind)`, so a one- or two-index residual drops the comparison. Symmetry: ordered, because exposure precedes the change and "train" commits to a produced term rather than a co-occurrence (Fig 12 :77). Scale: practice and developmental timescales, from one lab task to a lifetime, which invites the aggregation failure named in Fig 1 :22.
- **Domain 1, perceptual learning.** Actors: observers, stimuli, task demands. Currency: thresholds, weighting, transfer. Anchor: psychophysics. Question type: mechanism and slot specificity.
- **Domain 2, cultural attention to scenes.** Actors: observers in regimes, scenes. Currency: fixation and object-versus-context weighting. Anchor: cross-cultural eye tracking. Question type: binding of a shared group.
- **Domain 3, social attention.** Actors: observers, social partners, faces. Currency: gaze following and face selectivity. Anchor: gaze-cueing experiments. Question type: habitat-relative valence.
- **Domain 4, development.** Actors: children, caregivers, habitat. Currency: looking time and novelty. Anchor: developmental studies. Question type: the uninstrumented objective link.
- **Domain 5, professional vision.** Actors: experts, novices, domain. Currency: accuracy, scan paths. Anchor: expert-novice studies. Question type: method versus recording.
- **Domain 6, animal ecological attention.** Actors: species, habitat. Currency: vigilance and salience. Anchor: ethological exposure. Question type: capability as agent-and-habitat, low-overlap comparator.

**Union, not partition.** Exposure and task training, environment and seat work, visual and social information all overlap. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The break sits at vocabulary and objective, where emic/etic comparability and the threshold for "attending appropriately" have no instrument (Fig 3 :27). No domain without a nameable anchor was counted, and no seventh appeared.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a change in contact between observer and regime, with shared machinery arranged differently. That is the residue worth sending.
