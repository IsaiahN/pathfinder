# Expert verification packet : the Tether on training attention

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in perceptual learning or cultural practices can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. One to one, not for bulk send.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain, `gap` on the feature list, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any observer-regime-feature slot is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Training attention" is a change in the contact between an observer and their environment or practice regime, not a change in an isolated observer. The effect is indexed per observer, regime and information kind, never pooled into one score. Unmaintained exposure and maintained practice are two different conditions, and only the second has a seat that can introduce an instrument or a head start. What transfers across regimes is a method, not a recording of one occasion. The machinery of orienting, discriminating and comparing is a functional group shared across regimes; over a fixed atom set two regimes stand as isomers, same atoms arranged differently. Practice rearranges what is already reachable, and a new information kind appears only on contact with a differing closure. A practice can also sever the channel to the ground. The framework does not name which feature a regime trains, because it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 1
- **Statement:** an attentional habit is agent-and-habitat and per slot; training is a contact change, not an isolated-agent change. Two training modes are distinct.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 11 :56 *"Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* Fig 11 :58 *"Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there. That is the garden, and it is how arrival is worked on rather than waited for."*
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change that moves the ground with habitat held fixed (the figure's own standing falsifier at Fig 11 :72); or a pooled verdict reproducing every per-slot verdict; or a regime with no selector and no seat in which attention moves systematically toward a non-updating ground.

### C2 : world-facing; checkable by a perceptual-learning or cross-cultural researcher
- **Statement:** in the world literature, what changes with practice is attentional weighting and the discriminations an observer can make, the change is often specific to the trained slot, and mere exposure and task-relevant training are separate conditions. These are the method-versus-recording and two-mode claims at the ground.
- **What to check:** Goldstone 1998 (Annual Review of Psychology) defines perceptual learning as environment-caused change and names attentional weighting as one mechanism; Sagi 2011 (Vision Research) *"Training on a restricted stimulus set may lead to perceptual overfitting and over-specificity"*; Tsushima and Watanabe 2009 (Learning & Behavior) reports that psychophysicists and animal-learning researchers reached opposite conclusions about attention because one line studied mere exposure and the other task-relevant training; Nisbett and Miyamoto 2005 (Trends in Cognitive Sciences) reports that *"participating in different social practices leads to both chronic as well as temporary shifts in perception."* Is that reading of those sources faithful? The framework does not depend on them being true; they are corroboration only.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that one pooled effect size reproduces every per-slot result, or that exposure and task training produce the same shape with no separable mechanism. That would remove the ground support for C2, though it would not change the figure verdict in C1.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation, with a live scope question
- **Statement:** what crosses a practice boundary is a method/head start, not a recording; the shared machinery is a functional group and two regimes over a fixed atom set are isomers; practice cannot add an information kind, and only contact with a differing closure moves that wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **Refuter:** a one-occasion recording with no reapplicable method that transfers as a new capability; or a regime that adds a genuinely new information kind with provenance showing no import; or a practice variation requiring an atom the other regime does not have (which kills the isomer claim and leaves the functional-group claim standing).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a feature list. "Attend to context", "attend to gaze" and "attend to focal objects" are bindings from the world literature, provenanced as such. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*
- Not a number of trials, a time course, a critical period, or a universal effect size. All are `gap`.
- Not that all practice helps. Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, and a scoring rubric that decides the outcome stops being a convention (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that every attentional difference is learned. Maturation and species-typical capacities are outside what this derivation settles.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the framework named the mechanisms. Attention weighting and the analytic/holistic distinction are world-literature bindings onto held slots; the correction is visible in `05` section 6 and `06` Q1.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot, source-discriminating instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and the color case was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

1. **Slot test:** for observer `s`, regime `p`, feature `k`, does measured weighting differ from baseline in the predicted direction, with an anchor that does not update on the trainer's rating?
2. **Method test:** does the weighting transfer to a new setting as a method, or only replay one occasion?
3. **Mode test:** do mere exposure and maintained practice produce distinguishable shapes?
4. **Discriminator test:** can any instrument tell which of two practices produced the same reading? If not, the source claim stays `gap`.

1. Read Fig 11 :39, :40, :56, :58 and Fig 1 :19. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of perceptual learning or cross-cultural attention: are those four characterizations faithful, and is the exposure-versus-task distinction real in your field?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate which practice produced a given attentional reading, or say why none is possible.
