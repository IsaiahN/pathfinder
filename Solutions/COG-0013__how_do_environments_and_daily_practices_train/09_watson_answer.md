## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide that the answer has this shape: attention training is a change in agent-and-habitat contact, per observer, regime and slot; environment and practice form a union of two modes (one maintained by a seat, one unmaintained); what transfers is a method and a head start, not a recording; the machinery is a functional group with variable members and bindings, and two regimes over a fixed atom set are isomers; practice rearranges the reachable and cannot add an information kind; a practice can sever the channel to the ground; and the trained system's own output cannot certify the training. They do not decide which features a given environment trains, or the time course. That is `gap`, stated plainly, not silence. Per-domain verdicts are in the table above and are never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** locatable. If a universally trained feature is asserted and is actually habitat-bound, instruments and interventions built on one population are exported to regimes where the ground does not pay. If a habitat-bound effect is treated as universal, effort is spent hunting variation that is not there. The shape claim itself costs little to be wrong, because it withdraws cleanly. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a named answer before a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim is a generator plus a refuter, not a settled list. Withdrawing it removes standing to compose over it, not a term from the record (Fig 6 amendment on reach). Each domain verdict can be rewritten independently.
- **reversed by:** (1) an agent-only change that moves the ground with habitat fixed defeats the contact claim; (2) pooled verdicts reproducing every per-slot verdict defeat the slot rule; (3) a clean partition of the union or a shared-machinery counterexample defeats the two-mode and functional-group claims; (4) an alleged anchor that moves when pushed removes that domain's ground; (5) two frames marked independent and found to share one evidence pool lose the triangulation mark; (6) a source-discriminating instrument that names a trained feature and pays for it promotes that slot from `gap` to `specified`, then to `grounded` only when the ground pays.
