## DERIVATION

### Q1 : Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the mechanisms? CORRECTION TO MY OWN §6 of `05`

- The figures README: *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ.
- Attention weighting, differentiation and orienting are already in the library of the world literature and are bound onto mechanisms the derivation holds. None is a new member of the six.
- Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* ⇒ Naming a mechanism is a **binding**, not an import that adds an atom (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
- **I overstated this earlier.** The rule governs missing primitives, not the selection of atoms already in Γ. Retracted as applied to these mechanism names, kept as applied to the feature list. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 :115).

### Q2 : Which bond is "train"?

- Fig 12 :57 *"both hold at once"* is conjunction `+`, symmetric. Training is not symmetric co-occurrence: swapping environment and information kind gives a different measurement (Fig 12 :92 *"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."*).
- Fig 12 :72 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* is sequence `→`. A practice schedule is a sequence, so training has a `→` component.
- Fig 12 :77 *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* is production `⇒`. A new discriminative response, one the observer could not make before, is exactly a B that did not exist before. This is the governing bond for "train". Goldstone's differentiation case in SEP 2024 (Japanese speakers learning to distinguish /r/ and /l/) is a produced perceptual capacity.
- Fig 12 :87 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* is comparison `⋛`. "Different kinds of information" invites `⋛`, but the comparison is between two quantities at a level; where levels differ, Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* licenses comparison only after both operands settle.
- Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* So a practice plan is `→`, the target is a `⋛` comparison, and the change from baseline is a `−` subtraction. A vocabulary with only `+` could name all the ingredients and still not state the route. Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **Falsifier:** if a training effect is fully describable as symmetric co-occurrence (`+`) with no produced term, no schedule and no threshold, then Q2 is wrong and the directed reading of "train" is withdrawn.

### Q3 : Functional group or isomer?

- Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- The shared machinery across D1 to D6 (orient, select, discriminate, compare) is a **functional group**: its presence fixes the kind of thing an attentional regime is. Analytic and holistic scene attention, or expert and novice scan paths, differ in which member fills the slot and how it is bonded. That is the weaker, safer claim.
- The stronger claim is **isomerism**: hold the full atom set fixed (observer, regime, information kind, differentiating, unitizing, weighting, orienting, comparing) and let two regimes differ only in arrangement. Then they are *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"* (Fig 12 :117), and the difference is *"decisive in the behaviour"*, which is what cross-regime eye-tracking measures.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either regime requires an atom the other does not (for example a social-attention regime that cannot be written without a primitive absent from a scene-attention regime), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group claim stands instead. Recorded, not hidden.

### Q4 : Where is the dependency break?

- Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*
- Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- Chain: perception, vocabulary (comparable terms for what a regime affords), objective (what counts as attending appropriately), planning (a practice regime), learn and carry (a disposition checkable in a new setting).
- The break is at vocabulary and objective. "Attends more to context" is not yet an emic-comparable term, and "attending appropriately" is not yet an operational threshold. Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The regime and the observer are a union, so a single lever cannot clear the overlap.
- **Falsifier:** a working instrument that fixes comparability and the threshold across two low-overlap regimes would move the break and require a fresh census.

### Q5 : Catalyst and cycle in a practice regime

- Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* A head start or a reusable weighting method survives its own use; a spent practice trial does not. Nisbett and Miyamoto 2005 describe chronic and temporary shifts from social practice, which are the spent and regenerating parts distinguished here.
- Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input."* The correction loop in an apprenticeship (act, be corrected, act again) is a cycle, and its sign decides whether it closes toward a ground or drifts (Fig 2 :34 *"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*).
- A linear chain cannot close by construction, so a training account that lists exposures without a return path cannot explain how a habit stabilizes or drifts. This is the structural reason `01` F9 requires an external reading rather than the system's own output.
- **Falsifier:** a practice regime whose correction loop demonstrably cannot re-enter its own input, yet still produces a stable attentional habit against a ground, would refute the cycle reading.

## CONCLUSION

"Train" commits to production `⇒` with a `→` schedule, a `⋛` target and a `−` progress measure, not to symmetric co-occurrence. Across D1 to D6 the machinery is a functional group; over a fixed atom set two regimes are isomers with a stated falsifier. The dependency chain breaks at comparability and the objective, not at perception or planning. A practice contains catalysts (methods that survive use) and a cycle whose sign decides drift. These are structural readings from the figures, not measurements of any particular regime.
