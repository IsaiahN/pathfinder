# The Tether's answer on environments, practices and attention

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, `gap` on the feature list and numerical time course. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The word "train" hides a causal claim. Stated in effect terms, the question asks how an observer's attentional weighting changes when the environment or daily practice changes, for visual and for social information. The residual is three-indexed (observer, regime, information kind), non-symmetric, and cross-scale (Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). Environment and practice are a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Six domains were censused, each with a nameable anchor, and their verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Training changes contact, not an isolated agent.** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* An attentional habit is a property of observer-and-regime. "Trained" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground (Fig 11 :36).

2. **The effect is per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A global "attention increased" figure throws away where the change happened. A near-zero global effect with one live feature is a legal state (Fig 1 :13).

3. **There are two training modes, and only one has a seat.** Unmaintained immersion, where nothing selects and what survives is what the ground kept (Fig 11 :56), and maintained practice, where a teacher or institution can introduce instruments and head starts (Fig 11 :58). The seat's power is asymmetric: introduction is available, exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61). Mere exposure and task-relevant training are different conditions, not degrees of one dial.

4. **What transfers is a method, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A transferable attentional weighting is a generator; a memorised scan path is one occasion. What the expert's label compresses is the result, not the cost of establishing it (Fig 12 :107).

5. **The machinery is a functional group; the variation is the binding.** Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Orienting, selecting, discriminating and comparing are present across regimes; which member fills the slot varies. Over a fixed atom set two regimes are Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* No standing list of target features survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).

6. **Practice rearranges the reachable; it does not add a kind.** Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 8 :32 *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* A new information kind appears only on contact with a closure that differs (Fig 6 :23), which is why the search crossed into cross-cultural work and animal behaviour.

7. **A practice can also sever the channel.** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Saturated metrics, pooled readings and a rubric that decides the outcome (Fig 10 :47) train attention away from the ground. Valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49), so the same practice can help one residual and harm another.

8. **The shaped system's own output cannot certify the training.** Fig 5 :47 *"So a system that starts with nothing can still be shaped, by seeking the gap that is large and compressible, which is the same quantity the acceptance test scores."* But Fig 5 :48 *"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."* Confidence, ratings and practice volume are frame-internal (Fig 1 :27). A discriminator is owed, because a reading cannot comment on its source.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal training effect size. The effect is per slot and per regime.
- Not a named feature list. Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* "Attend to context" and "attend to gaze" are bindings from the world literature, provenanced as such, not figure derivations.
- Not a number of trials, a critical period, or a time course. Those are owed from the ground.
- Not a claim that all practice is beneficial (point 7) or that every attentional difference is learned (Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* leaves maturation and species-typical capacity outside what this derivation settles).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any observer-regime-feature slot was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A per-slot, source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*, and it was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The concrete questions are:

- **Slot test:** for observer `s`, regime `p`, feature `k`, does the measured weighting differ from baseline in the direction predicted, with the anchor not updating on the trainer's rating?
- **Method test:** does the trained weighting transfer to a new setting as a reapplicable method, or only replay a recording of one scene (Fig 4 :22, :25)?
- **Mode test:** does mere exposure and maintained practice produce distinguishable shapes, as Fig 11 :56 and :58 predict?
- **Discriminator test:** can any instrument tell which of two practices produced the same attentional reading? If not, the source claim stays `gap`.
- **Standing falsifiers:** an agent-only change that moves the ground; pooled verdicts reproducing every per-slot verdict; a clean partition of the union; an anchor that moves when pushed; a variation requiring a new primitive.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32). The feature list and time course are `gap`, which is a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (*How Intelligence Actually Works*, opened 2026-11-22).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the features that a given environment teaches anyone to attend to, and it does not give a number of trials. It says that attention training is a change in the contact between a person and their habitat, not a change in an isolated person; that the effect must be indexed per observer, regime and feature rather than pooled; that unmaintained exposure and maintained practice are two different conditions; that what transfers is a method and a head start, not a recording of one occasion; that the machinery of orienting and discriminating is shared while its members and arrangements vary; and that practice can sever the channel to the ground as easily as open it. It refuses to name the trained feature because the framework returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The feature list stays open until the ground settles it.
