## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The environment is in the definition.** Goldstone's definition, quoted in SEP 2024, says perceptual learning changes are *"caused by this environment."* That is the same shape as Fig 11 :39 (capability is agent-and-habitat) and Fig 11 :40 (*"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing"*), arrived at in a different field. Use it as corroboration of the contact claim, not as a figure quote.
2. **Mere exposure and task training are different conditions.** Tsushima and Watanabe 2009 report that psychophysicists and animal-learning psychologists reached literally opposite conclusions about whether attention is needed, because one line studied mere exposure and the other studied task-relevant training. That is the two-mode union of Fig 11 :56 and :58. It is a low-overlap corroborator (animal behaviour) from a genuinely different evidence pool.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :51** *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*: do not send citations of the framework's author as evidence for the shape claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.
