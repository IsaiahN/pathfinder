## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the set of constraints any adequate ecosystem-climate-land-use model must satisfy, statable before anyone names a particular model architecture, grid resolution, or coupling scheme. "Report the round-trip gap against the ground" is a shape. "Use a coupled DGVM at 0.25 degrees" is a name. The seven-part claim in `README.md` is a shape; the specific models in the world-source section are instances, not answers derived from the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." So start from the residual in effect terms ("I cannot tell which dropped detail changed the decision") and let the figure vocabulary narrow it.
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." The anchor "is provided by your own question." That is the ground discipline in search vocabulary.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." In this solution the residual was described before the world sources were chosen, so the papers on emulation error, FLUXNET fusion and emergent constraints were found because they touch that residual, not because they were already favourites.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Concretely here: measure R_T against decision-relevant observations, and name a detail as useful only when its removal changes the outcome. Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. If a particular emulator architecture is adopted, it arrives from the modeling literature with a record, not from the six-term substrate. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world," so it does not move the wall; it only gives this frame access.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the difference between a reading and its source: an instrument that detects green "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." A model's global skill score is such a reading. It cannot tell you which structural choice produced it, because "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution was not another argument: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." In this question, the better instrument is an observation that discriminates among the dropped details, not a finer grid by itself.

## 4. Why "useful" is part of the shape and not a preference

If "useful detail" were left as a preference, no check could run on it. The discipline from Fig 5 : "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." That is why `README.md` binds the seven-part claim explicitly and `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` writes five refuters before the conclusion. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A modeller's taste for higher resolution is a filter; the ground is the witness.

## 5. Answer to the question this file exists to settle

**What licenses a particular coupled model as "the" answer?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the shape: measure the loss against an anchor, keep per-slot residuals, close the feedback cycles, enumerate the habitat, triangulate independent observers, treat resolution and cost as filters. A particular model is licensed only by the ground settling it or by a provenanced import. Until then, naming it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the world-source section commit a forbidden naming error?** No, with a qualification. Calling Womack et al.'s emulator-error framework or Lu and Ricciuto's SVD surrogate "the answer" would be the error. They are instances of the shape in a different closure, recorded with their authors, venues, years, and quotes so the record shows where each came from (Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"). The qualification: the framework cannot certify that these instances are complete or that no better one exists. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous modellers to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried against data.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" and "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any Earth-system feedback, and they cannot settle the question they report on. No measurement was taken.
