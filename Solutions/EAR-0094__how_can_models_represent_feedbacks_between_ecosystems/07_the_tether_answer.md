# The Tether's answer on representing feedbacks without losing useful detail

**Mode: `specified` on the verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A coupled model of ecosystems, climate, and land use must stand in for the world so a land-use decision can be made. The residual is the per-slot gap between model prediction and observation, and the failure named by the question is that coarsening the model discards a term that was load-bearing for that decision without any instrument registering the loss. The coupling is a **union compound** with overlapping conditions (land use is both decision and forcing; ecosystems are both sink and climate responder), so it needs its own loop and its own ground, not a single merged model (Fig 9 :54-58).

## 2. The shape (seven parts, verdicts kept separate and never averaged)

1. **Loss is structural; report it as a round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." So "without losing useful detail" is re-stated as: coarsen, carry the result back down, measure the gap against the ground, and report it. `general` structural, triangulated with Fig 13 :32.

2. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single global skill score can be near zero while one region or season carries the feedback that matters. `specified`, single-source within the figures.

3. **Wire feedbacks as cycles, not chains.** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels." The return path is the feedback; a one-way forcing chain does not model it at all. `general`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13.

4. **Triangulate independent observers.** Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Models fed the same reanalysis and built on the same parameterizations are one witness wearing several names; agreement among them is not evidence. `general` on the rule, `specified` on which frames are independent in a given study, triangulated across Fig 8 and Fig 2.

5. **Enumerate the habitat out to where the cascade stops.** Fig 11 :43-44 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52-53 "Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Useful detail is detail in the contact cascade of the decision-relevant residual, even three steps out. `general`, single-source within the figures.

6. **Resolution and cost are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 6 :29 "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms." Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." `specified`, triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 6.

7. **The framework returns the shape, not a model.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It cannot select a grid spacing, an architecture, or a coupling scheme. Those are settled by the ground or imported with provenance. `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 and Fig 13 :134.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any lossless coupled model exists, or that maximum resolution is the answer.
- Not that a particular model, emulator, DGVM, or coupling scheme is the one the framework derives. Those appear in `04_links_and_distribution.md` as instances from other closures, with provenance.
- Not that agreement among models validates the representation. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that the shape is `grounded`. No Earth-system measurement was taken here.
- Not that the three subsystem frames are one problem. They are a union compound with overlapping conditions and separate loops (Fig 9 :54-58).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A measurement and a round trip, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source," and the color case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here:

- **Round-trip test:** pick a land-use decision, coarsen the coupled model, carry the result back, and measure R_T against decision-relevant observations. A nonzero gap on a live slot is the loss made visible (Fig 4 :30).
- **Slot test:** show whether the per-slot residual carries a signal the global average destroys, for that decision.
- **Cycle test:** intervene in one subsystem and observe whether the return path actually closes with the predicted sign.
- **Independence test:** check whether the observing frames draw on the same reanalysis or systematic bias; if they do, they are one witness (Cox 2019, opened).
- **Standing falsifiers:** any of the five in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4. If one fires, the corresponding part of the shape is withdrawn.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish: no measurement of R_T for a specific coarsening and decision was made in this derivation. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Naming a particular model would exceed what the framework returns, so the slot stays open. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which coupled model to build, and it does not promise a representation with no loss. It says that every pass from the coupled ecosystem-climate-land-use system to a coarser model is lossy, so the honest question is how to report the loss: measure the round trip against observations instead of assuming the gap is zero; keep the residual per place, season, and ecosystem rather than averaging it into one score; wire the feedbacks as cycles with a sign, not one-way forcing chains; enumerate each feedback's habitat out to where the third-order cascade stops mattering; triangulate observations that do not share a bias; and treat resolution and computing cost as filters for what gets tried, never as witnesses for what is true. What it offers is the shape any adequate model must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot for a particular architecture stays open until the ground settles it.
