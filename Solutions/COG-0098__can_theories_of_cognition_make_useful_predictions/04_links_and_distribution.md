## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The pseudoetic failure is named in the field.** Cross-cultural psychology describes a "pseudoetic approach [that] is actually an emic based approach developed in a Western culture while being designed to work as an etic approach." That is reduction in the question's own terms, and it corroborates the requirement that the route of a category be recorded.
2. **Prediction is residual-driven in cognitive theory itself.** Predictive coding describes a model used to predict signals that are "then compared with the actual input signals", with a residual driving updates. This corroborates the shape of R. Its own "no consensus" note is honest evidence that a mechanism is not settled here.
3. **A model must be lossy.** The good regulator theorem corroborates that prediction requires a model while the mapping "can lose information about the entity that is modeled." This is the round-trip gap Fig 4 :30 names, and it is why recording the route matters.
4. **A concrete partial failure.** The Müller-Lyer literature first reported large cross-cultural differences and a carpentered-environment explanation, then reported contrary findings and, by 2025, a review challenging cultural determination. That history fits the union verdict: a prediction can be habitat-relative without the category attribution being settled. The conflict was returned to census in `01` section 6, not used to flip the verdict.
5. **Comparability and prediction pull in different directions.** Measurement invariance asks whether the same construct is measured across groups, while Welzel and colleagues argue that the criteria favour low between-group variance. Fig 2 :41 says agreement is efficient for a convention; this is a convention problem, not a refutation of per-slot prediction.

Overlap is declared in `10_source_ledger.csv`. The six cross-cultural and psychometric sources share a broad Western psychology evidence pool and count as fewer witnesses than their number suggests. The good regulator theorem (cybernetics), predictive coding (neuroscience) and the animal results reported inside the Müller-Lyer page are lower-overlap corroborators. The eight essays share the framework author completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before a residual is described sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim and offer links later.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: describe the residual before choosing a frame. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the article.
- Fig 2 :35 and :40: selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
- Fig 8 :51: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Always ask for the route, never only the result.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only if they ask, the source links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method only after stage 1 is recorded, and ask each user to write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1 before stage 2** follows Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks an effect description without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Stage 1 verdicts are recorded before any theory is shown and are never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** by Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. THE_FORMULA "Direction" still applies: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so every user writes down a refuter before running it and labels the mode of the result.
