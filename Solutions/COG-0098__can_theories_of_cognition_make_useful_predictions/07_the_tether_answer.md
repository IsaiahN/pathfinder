# The Tether's answer on prediction and unfamiliar societies

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, `gap` on the category list and the discriminator, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7). It cannot settle COG-0098.

## 1. The residual it answers

A cognitive theory predicts by comparing what its terms say should happen with what is observed, separately for each practice in each society. R is that gap per slot. The question asks whether R can close on a society the theory was not built on while the society's own distinctions survive. The hard part is not accuracy. It is that a theory can appear to become more accurate simply by recoding the practice into a familiar category, and the record will not show that the recoding happened (Fig 8 :51). The question is therefore a provenance question disguised as a prediction question.

## 2. The shape

1. **A theory is a frame with a closure.** It predicts inside its reach, and no amount of searching inside that reach adds a new primitive. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." More WEIRD subjects cannot import a local category.
2. **The unfamiliar society is a differing frame, not a harder sample of the same one.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :27 says some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same. The theory cannot tell which it has without going outside.
3. **"Useful" must be settled by an anchor that does not move when the theory recodes.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Here that is the local outcome or the society's own judgment, because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-38). Agreement between the researcher's coding and the theory's categories is a mirror, not an anchor (Fig 2 :35).
4. **The residual must stay indexed per practice and society.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A pooled cross-cultural score can be near zero with one live slot still wrong.
5. **There are exactly two legitimate ways to close R without reduction.** Either the practices are isomers over a shared atom inventory, in which case the changed bond must be written down and shown to matter behaviourally (Fig 12 :117), or the practice needs an atom from the society's frame, in which case it crosses with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :14, :22; Fig 8 :51). Calling it just another case of category X is neither. Fig 12 :5 says the bond is the half nothing was recording.
6. **The prediction is a shape, not the local name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." The theory can say what a closing category must do and where it could be found; it cannot name it from its own library.
7. **The chain breaks at vocabulary and objective.** Researchers can record behaviour and name familiar categories, but have no instrument for what the practice affords locally and for what counts as a successful prediction there (Fig 3 :27). Effort spent on better sampling and better statistics lands above the break (Fig 3 :19).
8. **The room must change when the society changes.** Fig 10 :72: "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." Fig 7 :36: "The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

## 4. What this explicitly does not say

- Not that no cognitive theory can predict across societies. The shape is a condition on how prediction is done, not a refusal of prediction.
- Not that all practices are unique, or that no cognitive universals exist. Fig 12 :123 allows a functional group with different members. What is refused is a standing list whose members survive every change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not that any particular cognitive atom is universal. No list is returned.
- Not that a local category is a new biological primitive. An import adds an atom to a theory's library, not to the world (Fig 6 :25).
- Not that the local ground is the habitat. Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."
- Not `grounded`. No society was measured, no held-out prediction was run, and no instrument was built.
- Not a claim that the Müller-Lyer or WEIRD literature settles this. Those are secondary corroboration with their own conflicts, recorded in `09` and `10`.

## 5. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a held-out prediction, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the pattern: a reading cannot name its source, and the spectrometer settled the colour case. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Local anchor test.** Does the prediction's residual trend down on a held-out practice when scored against a local outcome that does not move when the coding scheme changes? If the only anchor is the researcher's category fit, it is a mirror (Fig 2 :33-35).
- **Route test.** For every category that closes a residual, can the team state whether it was a re-arrangement of held atoms or an import with a named source and a shadow test? Fig 8 :51 says the contents alone cannot answer this.
- **Isomer test.** For a claimed isomer pair, are the atom sets identical, is the changed bond named, and does the change matter on held-out slots? `06` Q1 gives the three-part test and its falsifier.
- **Per-slot test.** Does the result survive when residuals are indexed per practice and society rather than pooled? Fig 1 :22 says aggregation is how the live signal disappears.
- **Standing-falsifiers.** The six conditions in `01` section 4 were written before this conclusion and remain live. Any one firing withdraws or narrows the shape claim.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not shown unreachable.** The missing discriminator between "re-arrangement suffices" and "a new atom is owed" is not supplied by any figure line. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The theory can characterise what the missing thing would do, which is a shape, and it can point to the frame likely to hold it, which is the society's own practice. It cannot name it. The eight essays add the standing caveat that "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring a theory's atoms makes the choice visible; it does not make them neutral. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable, and "indiscernibility is not identity."

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that cross-cultural prediction is impossible, and it does not offer a universal list of mental categories. It says that a cognitive theory is a frame with a finite reach, that an unfamiliar society is a different frame rather than a harder sample, and that more home-population data cannot import a category the theory does not hold. A prediction stays useful without reduction in one of two ways: the two practices are the same atoms in a different arrangement, with the bond written down and shown to matter, or a category crosses from the local frame with its source and gap-explaining role recorded. Recoding the practice into a familiar category is neither. The result must be scored per practice and society against a local outcome that does not move when the researcher changes the coding, and the theory can return only the shape of the missing category, not its name. What is offered here is that shape, six refuters written in advance, and an honest report that the instrument to decide between the two success modes is still owed. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
