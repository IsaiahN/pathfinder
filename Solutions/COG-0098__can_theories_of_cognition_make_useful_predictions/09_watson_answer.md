## QUESTION

Can theories of cognition make useful predictions about unfamiliar societies without reducing their practices to familiar categories? Restated in effect terms: given a cognitive theory's library Γ, a researcher belief b, a prediction protocol a and an observed practice o' in a society outside the theory's home population, can the residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| be closed against a ground on each practice slot while the society's own distinction survives the prediction as a distinction? Option A says yes by mapping the practice onto a familiar category. Option B says yes by treating the society as a frame whose closure differs and either re-arranging held atoms with the bond stated or importing the category with provenance. Option C says no. What each leads to: A gives an apparent accuracy gain with the route unrecorded, so the error becomes invisible. B gives a checkable prediction with a stated route. C gives an honest `gap` where no instrument can discriminate. Source: `cognitive science.txt` line 119, section 10.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least four-place, Γ, b, a and o', indexed by slot s = (practice, society). Symmetry: not symmetric. The local ground is an anchor and does not update on the theory's categories, and "without reducing" is an ordered constraint on the route of the closing term. Scale: the slot is a practice, while the anchor that settles it lives at the population or ecological scale, so the residual crosses the membrane between an individual cognitive step and a society's selection loop.
- **D1, the theory frame.** Actors: the cognitive theory's atoms and bonds, the researcher. Currency: prediction residual and description length. Anchor: home-population experiments, legitimate only for questions constitutively about that population. Admitted.
- **D2, the society as a differing frame.** Actors: practitioners, their categories, language, local ecology. Currency: local distinctions, what works and fails locally. Anchor: local consequences and the society's own classificatory judgments. Admitted.
- **D3, the measurement and coding channel.** Actors: instruments, coding schemes, ethnographer. Currency: observations converted into the theory's terms. Anchor: the recorded behaviour and outcome. Admitted; this is the decaying channel.
- **D4, comparison and import.** Actors: atoms and bonds of the two frames, the described residual. Currency: provenance, shadow test, surplus. Anchor: whether the imported category explains a gap described before the search. Admitted.
- **D5, the verdict on "useful".** Actors: prediction and held-out observation. Currency: residual on a held-out slot. Anchor: a measurement that does not move when the category assignment changes. Admitted.
- **Excluded X1 "culture" as a single cause** and **X2 "universal mechanism" as a standing list**: neither has an anchor independent of the outcomes to be explained. Fig 11 :51 says "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 9 :4-5 says to split a bundle rather than search it.
- **Union, not a partition.** Prediction validity, category preservation and anchor independence are overlapping conditions. Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57), so one lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Dependency order.** Describe R in effect terms, choose the frame, decide isomer versus import, predict against the local ground, settle, then carry. The chain in Fig 3 breaks at vocabulary and objective: there is no instrument for local affordances or a local definition of progress.

Strain test: Fig 13's six physical substrate terms were tried as a cognitive category list and sent back to census because they fit only by renaming mental terms as spectrum or gradient. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide the shape, not the category list. Yes, a theory of cognition can make useful predictions about an unfamiliar society without reduction, if the prediction keeps the residual indexed per practice and society, ranges it against a local anchor that does not update on the coding scheme, and states the route of every closing category. The route has exactly two legitimate forms: an isomer over held atoms with the bond named and behaviourally decisive, or a category imported from the local frame with provenance and a shadow test. Recoding the practice as a familiar category is neither, and more home-population data cannot manufacture the missing category. No figure line returns a universal category list or the instrument that discriminates isomer from new atom. Both are `gap`, stated plainly. The answer is `general` on structure, `specified` on the four admitted domains, and not `grounded`. Per-domain verdicts in the table above are never averaged into a single yes or no.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, route rules and refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a hidden recoding that makes a theory look general while erasing a local distinction, which Fig 6 :24 prices as a wrong atom costing more than starting from primitives. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by contacting a genuinely differing frame and recording the route.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim is a generator plus refuters, not a settled list. Withdrawing it removes a term's standing to be composed over, not a term from the record. Each domain verdict can be rewritten independently; no primitive has to be withdrawn and no society has to be re-described.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Six refuters are pre-registered in `01` section 4: prediction without any sourced local input; two independent frames failing to converge; a claimed import shown to be an isomer; accuracy driven by the researcher's own category fit; the result vanishing under per-slot indexing; a standing list surviving every residual change. A source-discriminating instrument that names a local category and pays for it would promote that slot from `gap`. A figure re-census that supplies the isomer versus new-atom discriminator would clear the live strain. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
