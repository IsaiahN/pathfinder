## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of a prediction you can state before you can name the society's category. It constrains a candidate prediction, it does not supply one. For COG-0098 the shape is the three-part separator in `01` section 0: per-slot residual, non-updating local anchor, and a stated route for the category that closes the residual. A list of cognitive universals is a name. A claim that any closing category must be either an isomer over held atoms or a provenanced import is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." That is exactly what an ethnographic vocabulary does to a cognitive theory, if the new terms are allowed to stand as new terms.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The researcher's words are borrowed from the home frame, so the effect description must come first.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the stage-1 email: describe what a successful prediction would look like locally and let the reader check it.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Fig 9 :4-5 says to split rather than search. The universal-versus-culture question is such a bundle.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done when "the finish line will also be the starting place." The same essay gives the reason: "you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author." The practitioner supplies the content, not the framework. This bears directly on who gets to name a local category.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." A local category becomes nameable when a source-discriminating instrument pays for it in that society.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. The category arrives with a record of where it came from and which gap it explains.

"They're Scanning Books" adds the standing caveat: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So a cognitive theory's atom list is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Treating it as neutral is the first step toward reduction. The same essay states the physical version of the limit: "Change the sensors, and not just the data changes, the shape of the compression changes: the categories, the primitives, what counts as similar to what." A society is not a different sensorium in the literal sense, but the structural point carries: different contact, different compression axes.

## 4. Why "reduce to a familiar category" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several sources can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Apply that to COG-0098. The observed practice o' is a reading. Naming its source as "an instance of working memory" or "a local version of analytic reasoning" reads the source off the projection. There may be several sources for the same reading, and some of them are not in the theory's library at all. What is owed is a discriminating instrument or a provenanced import, not a name.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the social version. In Bartlett's retellings, "details that didn't fit the listener's schema were simply 'omitted from the recollection, or transformed into more familiar forms.'" That is reduction in one sentence: missing and substituted, with nobody lying. A researcher who codes an unfamiliar practice into familiar categories is running Bartlett's experiment on a society.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the provenance version: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." A prediction that fits after recoding carries no visible record of the recoding. "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."

## 5. Q answered: what licenses a particular category in a slot?

**Nothing in the framework licenses it.** The framework returns a shape. A particular local category is licensed only by the ground settling it in that society, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the category is the researcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the reduction move the wall?** No. Fig 6 :22 says "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Renaming an unfamiliar practice as a familiar atom adds nothing to Γ, so nothing moves. The researcher feels they have learned something because Γ now has a new case, but Fig 12 :5 warns that "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." A new case filed under an old name is a recorded ingredient with the lost half unrecorded.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I overstated the rule in an earlier pass. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. A familiar atom used as a binding onto an already-held term is not a missing primitive. Naming that binding is provenance, not a forbidden invention. What remains wrong is claiming that the binding is a derivation from the theory alone, or claiming that it moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22). The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: naming the source of a reading without a discriminator is still the error. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." The same essay adds: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand." A list of distinguished cross-cultural psychologists who endorse the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a trial against a local outcome.

**A society of readers is a habitat, not a second ground.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "The group shares methods, never recordings." It quotes Fig 11 as "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A room of researchers who all learned the same coding manual shares one pool and drifts together. What helps is a frame with different contact, a local anchor, and the ability to refuse the verdict.

*Arrival of the Fittest* states the same limit at the level of the framework itself: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." Its own demo note warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." That is why `02` asks for a failure first.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." It will often be better than a generic answer, but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The same essay calls such outputs "simulacra."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any cognitive theory and they are not an ethnography. They cannot settle COG-0098. The only thing that can is a held-out prediction against a local ground, with the route of every closing category recorded. That is what the expert packet in `08` asks for.
