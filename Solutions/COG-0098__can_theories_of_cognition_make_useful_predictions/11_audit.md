That makes Fig 8 :51 the likely governing line rather than a side remark. This seat flags it before reading the primary's findings.

## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among theory Γ, belief b, action a and observation o', indexed by slot s = (practice, society). That is at least four operands plus a two-part index. A two-place reading (theory, society) would drop the prediction and the protocol, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. Two asymmetries are visible in the line itself. First, the local ground is an anchor that does not update on the theory's opinion (Fig 2 :25). Second, "survives the prediction" is ordered: the practice's distinction is the thing preserved, the theory's vocabulary is the thing that must accommodate it, not the reverse. A symmetric bond such as `+` would mis-state this; an ordered bond or a comparison is required.
- **Scale:** the slot is a practice at the individual or small-group scale, while the anchor that settles whether the prediction was useful lives at the population or ecological scale. The residual therefore crosses the membrane between the step loop and the selection loop (Fig 4 :3-16). This is not a single-scale measurement problem.

### 2.2 Domains, one line each

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | admitted? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: the theory as controller | theory Γ, researcher, atoms and bonds | predicted versus observed practice, description length | home-population measurements | yes, but as an instrument for a question about another population |
| B: the local frame | practitioners, local categories, ecology | local distinctions, what works locally | local consequences and local judgments | yes, the differing closure |

**Divergence from the primary census, recorded.** This seat at first proposed two changes. (1) Merge C (channel) and E (verdict) into one measurement domain, since both involve instruments. On re-census they were kept separate: C's anchor is whether the observation preserved the distinction, E's anchor is whether the residual settled against something that does not move. A saturated coding scheme can pass E's test trivially while failing C. (2) Treat D (route operation) as a sub-part of A (the theory frame), since imports end up inside Γ. On re-census D was kept separate: D's anchor is the source gate and shadow test, A's anchor is home measurement. Conflating them is exactly the failure the residual line warns about, because the import would then be reported as a home derivation. The primary also kept five domains, for the same reasons, though it named them differently. Both censuses arrive at five admitted domains.

**Partition or union:** union. Prediction validity (A, C, E), frame difference (B) and route integrity (D) overlap. The overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57), not a harder case of any one condition. A lever such as "improve the coding manual" clears only the region where C alone binds.

**Dependency order:** describe R in effect terms, choose the frame, decide re-arrangement or import, predict against the local ground, settle, carry. This seat agrees with the primary's ordering and adds one sharpening: the route decision (D) must precede the prediction, not follow it, because a prediction made before the route is stated cannot later have its route audited. Fig 8 :51 supports this: the contents alone do not separate convergence from adoption.

**Chain order, separately:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The break is between vocabulary and objective. This seat agrees with the primary and notes that the residual line itself only forces the first three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by the frame, not forced by the residual.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the five-domain shape to one sentence. Result: *a cognitive theory predicts an unfamiliar society when it keeps the residual local and per-slot, ranges it against a ground that does not move, and states whether the closing category was a re-arrangement or a provenanced import.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that sentence into a specific society. Result: *use the same theory, collect local data, and report which categories are familiar and which are new.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Coming back down, it tells a team to collect more local data when the actual failure was an unstated route.
2. It loses the order of operations. "States whether" sounds like a report written afterwards; the figure requires the route to be fixed before the prediction so it can be audited (Fig 8 :51).
3. It loses the bond-level test. "Re-arrangement" has no content without a named typed bond and a behavioural difference on a held-out slot (Fig 12 :117).
4. It loses the anchor's identity. "A ground that does not move" does not say that the local outcome, not the coding manual, is the anchor. Coming back down, a team can score accuracy against their own category fit and believe they complied.
5. It loses the channel. The abstraction has no place for Fig 10 :26, the four ways a coding channel decays while the ground does not.
6. It erases the gap itself. The one-sentence form sounds complete. It does not say that the discriminator between isomer and new atom is still owed, so a reader can mistake a shape for a result.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator and not the one-paragraph version. The paragraph in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.

Check the target venue's current policy on AI-assisted submissions before sending anything; no such policy is quoted in this folder, so none is asserted here.
