# The Tether's answer: what would count as evidence that a machine understands information?

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific discriminating instrument. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No machine was tested.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a discriminator between a machine that understands information and one that merely processes its structure. Both states can produce the same output, so the observation cannot decide between them. In effect terms the residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot (Fig 1 :11, Symbols table :36-40), and the question asks what would make the difference between two sources of that residual visible.

The question is a **union**, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57). It bundles: (C1) internal structure processing, (C2) maintained contact with a non-updating ground, and (C3) a verifier positioned outside the slice being graded. Understanding requires all three; "merely processing" is C1 with C2 absent. One lever cannot clear the compound, and the verdicts stay separate per domain.

The question also splits on what the word "understanding" is about. If it is about a person's judgment of an answer, human judgment is the legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :36-38). If it is about the machine's contact with a ground, human judgment is only an instrument and can be wrong on axes it does not measure. These are two verdicts, never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question asks for the wrong object as posed.** Fig 11 :34-36 calls "What is intelligence?" "The wrong question, and this figure is the replacement for it," and says "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." The answerable replacement is Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Apply the same move to "understanding": evidence of it is evidence of maintained contact, not of an inner state.
2. **Contact must be per slot, not an average.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A global score near zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). Any evidence offered as a single number has already thrown away the signal the question asks for.
3. **Internal structure cannot supply the atom.** Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." More parameters and more search inside one closure cannot move the wall.
4. **The difference is a bond, not an ingredient.** Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Over a fixed atom set, understanding and structure processing are isomers. A reading of the operands, whether it is an output transcript or an internal activation, cannot recover which bond holds.
5. **A test harness proves wiring, never capability.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Fig 11 :68: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails," and :69: "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." A benchmark is a substituted habitat with two silent failure modes.
6. **No self-report or internal inspection settles it.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 9 :51 denies the verifier evidence bearing only on the maker. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened 2026-11-23, names the practical version: "I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."
7. **Multiple agreeing instances are one room.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." The next room must differ in closure and be selected by the residual, not by a shared corpus.
8. **The discriminator is a `gap`.** The figures give its shape: a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29), per-slot residual (Fig 1 :19), reproduced habitat (Fig 11 :66-70), independent frames (Fig 2 :31-32), bond rather than ingredient (Fig 12 :46), and a verifier who cannot reconstruct the claim (Fig 10 :42). They do not name a test, a threshold, or a number. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms and not built here.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- **Not** that any particular machine understands or does not understand. No machine was tested.
- **Not** that fluency is worthless. Under the observer sense of the question, human judgment is the legitimate anchor (Fig 2 :36-38). It answers a question about people, and that verdict is kept separate.
- **Not** that a benchmark score is evidence of capability. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60), and a harness is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70).
- **Not** that a named test has been found. The discriminator is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- **Not** a derivation of consciousness. The framework stops at contact and capability; the hard problem of subjective experience is not treated here.
- **Not** `grounded`. No source-discriminating instrument paid for any claim in this folder. Status is `candidate`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* reads: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." In color science the case was closed "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." The corresponding requirements here are:

1. **Per-slot reading.** Does the instrument report a residual per slot, so that a global near-zero with one live slot remains visible? (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22.)
2. **Anchor test.** Does the reference move when the system pushes on it? "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor" (Fig 2 :30). A benchmark the system can train on is not such a reference.
3. **Habitat reproduction.** Has the harness reproduced what the goal required, and does it declare what it introduced? (Fig 11 :66-69.)
4. **Independence.** Do at least two frames draw on different evidence and each range against the anchor separately? (Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 8 :48.)
5. **Refusal.** Can the verifier write a negation, so it can report that a claim is wrong rather than only that it stopped paying? (Operators :58.)
6. **Provenance.** Is the record of which route produced an answer preserved, given that "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :50-52)?
7. **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 5: contact explained without import; a source-discriminating instrument demonstrated; a pooled score shown to track contact; shared-pool frames shown to corroborate; understanding shown to survive habitat substitution; or a re-census that admits human judgment as the anchor for the contact sense.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework's answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating source. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." This solution records the shape of the missing instrument, the route by which it would have to be imported, and the five refuters written in advance. It does not claim that no machine can ever understand, and it does not claim that none does. It claims that the proposed observation does not settle the question, and that an instrument of a particular shape would be needed.

The record is the proof: "The seed was always the seedline" (Fig 13 :142). A later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). One live strain is kept visible: the Apple versus Lawsen dispute about whether benchmark collapse is a reasoning limit or an output ceiling (F5 of `01`). It is a dispute about the instrument, and it does not flip the figure verdict.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether any particular machine understands. It says that the question as posed asks for the wrong object: understanding is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, and the answerable question is what makes a system capable, which is a property of the system and its habitat together. So the evidence cannot be a fluent answer, a self-report, or an aggregate score, because a global residual near zero can hide a live slot, a harness substitutes a habitat, and a checker that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. Over a fixed atom set, a system in ground contact and one processing only structure are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance, with the difference invisible in the output. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument that reads the same behavior and separates the two at the per-slot level against a non-updating anchor, with independent frames and a recorded lineage. No figure line names that instrument, so this folder reports its shape, its falsifiers written in advance, and an honest stop until such an instrument pays for a name.
