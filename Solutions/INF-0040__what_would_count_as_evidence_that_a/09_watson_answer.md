## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what would count as evidence that a machine understands information rather than merely processing its structure. Restate as an effect: `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), and the question asks what would make visible the difference between a system whose residual tracks a ground that does not update and one whose residual is only frame-internal. Option A: treat understanding as a one-place property of the machine and look for a behavioral or internal signature. Option B: re-ground the question as maintained agent-and-habitat contact, require a per-slot discriminator against a non-updating anchor, and report the specific instrument as a `gap` until one pays. What each leads to: A spends the independence of the verifier on a reading that cannot decide the source; B yields a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the current instrument horizon.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: a three-place relation among belief, action and observation, indexed per slot (Fig 1 :10-11). Understanding is therefore a two-place relation between a system and a habitat-plus-ground, not a one-place property. Symmetry: outputs of the two candidate states can be identical, so the difference is carried by an ordered bond, not by a symmetric feature of the operands (Fig 12 :46). Scale: single-system loop, population of eval rooms, and the habitat including observer and instrument; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).
- **D1 Internal frame.** Actors: `Γ`, atoms, seven bonds, negation, loss. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)` and residual reduction. Anchor: none nameable; loss is a frame-internal metric. Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric," and Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Admitted as a mechanism, refused as a verdict domain.
- **D2 Habitat and ground contact.** Actors: the world, users, instruments, other agents. Currency: maintained contact and per-slot residual. Anchor: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." (Fig 2 :29). Admitted, primary.
- **D3 Verification and observer frame.** Actors: expert readers, test designers, interrogators. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement, except where the question is constitutively about a person's judgment, in which case human judgment is legitimate (Fig 2 :36-38). Admitted, split into two verdicts.
- **D4 Test harness and benchmark.** Actors: synthetic tasks, held-out sets, prompts, leaderboards. Currency: scores, coverage, speed. Anchor: the harness itself, a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70). Admitted as a wiring test only.
- **D5 Provenance and lineage.** Actors: the record. Currency: source gate, shadow test, `R_T`. Anchor: the checkable record (Fig 12 :126-130). Admitted.
- **X1 Understanding as a free-floating inner property.** No nameable anchor; self-report is a mirror (Fig 2 :35) and "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Not a domain yet.
- **Union, not partition.** C1 internal processing, C2 ground contact, C3 outside verifier. "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." (Fig 9 :54). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Verdicts kept per domain, never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse before mint (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 2), describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28), import with provenance and shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52), settle, then carry up with a measured round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30). Inside a frame: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :19-20).

Strain test applied: the Fig 6 contact claim and Harnad's bottom-up grounding read as one requirement in two vocabularies, so they count as one witness, not a conflict. The Apple collapse result and the Lawsen token-ceiling rebuttal are a live dispute about the instrument, preserved as F5 of `01` and not used to flip the figure verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is re-grounded, not answered as posed. Understanding as a one-place property is a label applied after a lineage met its ground; the answerable question is capability, and capability is a property of agent and habitat together, indexed per slot (Fig 11 :36, :39; Fig 1 :19). Internal processing cannot add the atom of contact (Fig 6 :23), the bond carrying contact is not recoverable from the operands or the output (Fig 12 :46), harnesses prove wiring not capability (Fig 11 :70), and self-report and shared-pool agreement are disqualified (Fig 10 :42; Fig 2 :40). Over a fixed atom set the two states are isomers; without that condition they are a functional group. The observer sense and the contact sense of the question have different anchors and remain two verdicts, never averaged. The specific discriminator is a `gap`, reported as unreached rather than silence. Mode `specified` per domain, `general` on the bond and closure rules, `gap` on the instrument, not `grounded`; status `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** a verifier is spent on a reading that cannot decide the source. The concrete cost is treating a pooled score or a fluent interrogation as evidence of contact, which Fig 9 :60 prices as a filter handed a verdict and Fig 2 :42 prices as an independence loss: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is bounded and largely avoidable by leading with the refuter and the per-slot requirement.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out. The shape, five refuters and the strain are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A superseded packet can replace a sent one without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates contact from internal composition per slot against a non-updating anchor would close the `gap`; a demonstration that capability survives habitat substitution with no reproduced contact would break Fig 11 :39-40; a pooled score shown to track per-slot contact would break Fig 1 :19-22; a bond to a ground recovered from the operands would break Fig 12 :46; a figure re-census admitting human judgment as the anchor for the contact sense would collapse the union in F7. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
