## 2. Wider literature that bears on the residual

Three findings matter for the outreach, and all are secondary to the figures.

1. **Output cannot comment on source.** Harnad's Chinese/Chinese dictionary merry-go-round and Searle's room operator are the same structural claim as metamerism: several paths produce the same reading, so the reading does not determine the source. Bender and Koller sharpen it for current systems with the octopus thought experiment: a perfect predictor of conversations need not be a participant in the world those conversations are about. These sources are from three fields (cognitive science, philosophy of mind, computational linguistics) and do not share an author, so their agreement is stronger than the framework essays' agreement with the figures. They corroborate Fig 12 :46 and Fig 11 :39; they do not overrule them.
2. **The observable test is under dispute, not settled.** Apple's controlled puzzles report collapse beyond a complexity threshold; Lawsen's rebuttal attributes the collapse to output token ceilings. This is a live disagreement about the instrument. It is kept as a strain (F5 of `01`) and was returned to the census rather than used to flip a verdict. Fig 10 :26 names the failure mode: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."
3. **Extension is not metaphor.** Clark and Chalmers argue that cognitive processes can be constituted partly by features of the environment when those features play the right role. That is the low-overlap philosophical corroborator for Fig 11 :39. Searle's robot reply is the opposing move from within the same debate, so the two are kept as one conversation, not two independent witnesses.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Sending the framework before anyone has described what they would count as a failure sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if ground contact were present before giving them anything to read.
- **Fig 2 :40** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :70** "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." Do not present a leaderboard position as evidence of the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the five pre-registered refuters; (3) the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (4) record their answer; (5) only then, if asked, the links, with the note that the figures and the opened sources, not this summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory:** send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what would count as a failure. Record the verdict before any framework material is shown. This is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if the recipient is curious, offer the source links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Fig 2 :40 governs.
3. **Stage 3, method only:** offer the formula as something to reuse on a problem the recipient knows and can check. Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question."

## 5. Practical constraints on submission

- **IJCAI-ECAI 2026** states in its main-track call that "Generative AI models do not satisfy the criteria for authorship" and that an author using an LLM in any part of the writing process assumes full responsibility for all content, including checking correctness. A simulated derivation like this folder cannot be submitted as if it were a ground reading. Disclose the method, or submit only the checkable sub-claims with their refuters.
- **ACL 2026** uses ACL Rolling Review with two-stage review and requires AI-use disclosure under the ARR ethics policy. The call explicitly welcomes "negative findings" and "argue a position" papers, which is the honest genre for this material.
- Several deadlines on the venue pages have passed as of 2026-11-23. `03_public_contact_points.csv` records them as verified snapshots, with a note to check the current cycle. Sending to an expired call is a contact-cost failure, not an evidence failure.
