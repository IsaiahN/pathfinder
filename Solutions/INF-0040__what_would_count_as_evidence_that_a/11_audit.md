## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I started from the residual line alone, without using the primary's domain labels.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unsolved discriminator between two sources of the same output. Arity: a three-place prediction relation, indexed per slot, and the sought discriminator is a two-place relation between a system and a habitat-plus-ground. Not one place. Symmetry: two states with identical outputs cannot be separated by any symmetric feature of the output, so the discriminator must rest on an ordered bond. Scale: system loop, eval population, and the observer-plus-instrument habitat, with a lossy membrane between them.
- **Domain A, the closed computer.** Actors: library, tokens, weights, loss. Currency: composition and internal error reduction. Anchor: none nameable. A score the system helps produce updates on the system, so it fails the anchor test. Mechanism only.
- **Domain B, the contacting system.** Actors: the world, the operator, the instrument, the user who pays for an outcome. Currency: contact and per-slot residual against a reference. Anchor: a measurement that does not move on being pushed. Admitted.
- **Domain C, the checking room.** Actors: reader, interrogator, reviewer. Currency: agreement and refusal. Anchor: ground first; human judgment only when the question is constitutively about a person. Splits into two verdicts.
- **Domain D, the substituted arena.** Actors: harness, held-out set, leaderboard. Currency: scores. Anchor: the arena itself, which is not stable under the goal. Wiring only.
- **Domain E, the record.** Actors: provenance, dates, route. Currency: source gate and shadow test. Anchor: the checkable record. Admitted.
- **Excluded candidate:** understanding as a standalone inner essence. No anchor can be named for it, so it is not yet a domain.

**Union.** A (processing), B (contact) and C (outside check) overlap; they are not a partition. Removing A leaves nothing to test; removing B leaves a harness solve; removing C leaves a self-report. Dependency order: refuse, describe the residual, find the differing frame, import with provenance, settle, carry with a measured gap. Inside a frame: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry. The break, if any, is at the objective link, which has the fewest instruments.

My census matches the primary's structure, but the routes differ. The primary reached the union through Fig 9's union/partition split; I reached it through the anchor test in Fig 2 applied to each candidate domain. The primary named the objective break from Fig 3; I named it from the absence of a nameable anchor for a self-report. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes within one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak but not circular in the derivation. It is still one pool, so it carries no independent weight.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))` (Symbols :75-78). Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :36-40 (label and capability), Fig 1 :13/:19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 6 :21-23 (closure), Fig 12 :46/:117 (bond and isomer), Fig 11 :66-70 (habitat substitution), Fig 10 :42 (verifier non-access), Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134-135 (shape and gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` section 2 and `07` section 2: understanding is contact, contact is per slot, harnesses substitute habitats, the difference is a bond, the discriminator is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a reader might form after reading only the shorthand: "benchmarks do not measure understanding, so build a better benchmark that does," or "a model that passes a harder test understands."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor test**: a reference that moves when the system pushes on it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :30), and a harder benchmark can still be one the system trained on;
- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim: without it the claim degrades into a functional-group statement (Fig 12 :124);
- the **two-senses split**: shorthand collapses the observer and contact senses into one verdict, which Fig 2 :36-38 forbids;
- the **provenance and refusal requirements**: shorthand omits the source gate and the unary negation a verifier needs (Fig 8 :50-52, Operators :58).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a structure must sit between a system and an unmoving ground if capability is to be more than a label, and the residual must be indexed per slot. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading and no instrument exist; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
