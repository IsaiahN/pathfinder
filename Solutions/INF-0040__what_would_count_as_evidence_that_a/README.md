# Solution INF-0040: figure-grounded package

**Question:** What would count as evidence that a machine understands information rather than merely processing its structure?

**Source:** Information Theory.txt line 49 (question 40 in section 4, Information, meaning, and interpretation).

**Section:** 4. Information, meaning, and interpretation.

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the specific discriminating instrument, not `grounded`. Every substantive file states that this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (registry not yet built at time of writing; the solution folder is complete).

## What this is

A complete Pathfinder Solution following the nine-file exemplar at `Solutions/QMGR-0037__spacetime_continuum_or_emergent/`, with the three additions required by the current contract (`09_watson_answer.md`, `10_source_ledger.csv`, `11_audit.md`).

The question is restated in effect terms before any figure is chosen. "Understanding" is treated as the label a verifier would attach to a system whose residual stays small against a ground that does not move when the system pushes on it, indexed per slot. "Merely processing structure" is the same system inside a substituted harness where the residual is frame-internal. The two are not read as a two-item list; they are a union of conditions with different anchors, so the verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

The residual, the pre-registered refuter, and the domain census precede the conclusion in every file that carries a verdict. Links to theory are withheld until after a reader has recorded a verdict on the stage-1 shape claim.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. `03` contains institutional submission portals and public contact pages only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer. Fig 2 :40 disqualifies a verdict whose frames share an evidence pool, and the Seat Map (:163) makes high correlation efficient for a convention but disqualifying for a verdict.
- No bulk-send plan. The offer is one-to-one, and a reply is a conversation, never corroboration.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question. Fig 8 :34-37 forbids a frame from certifying its own limit, and the exemplar's shape essay names LLM output as a simulation. The verdict is `candidate` until a source-discriminating instrument pays for it.
