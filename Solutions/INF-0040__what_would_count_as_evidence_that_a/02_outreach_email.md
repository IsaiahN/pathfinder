# Outreach draft: check one claim before you decide whether the frame is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has been checked against the verbatim figure text on your own machine. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 claim does not survive.

---

**Subject:** What would you count as a failure of this claim about machine understanding?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual:** we can observe a machine's output, but we cannot observe from that output whether it is in contact with the world or only processing the structure of its inputs. Two states produce indistinguishable outputs: a system whose prediction error stays small against a measurement that does not move when the system pushes on it, and a system whose error is only internal to its own test set. I want to know what would discriminate them.
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** understanding, if the word is to do work, is maintained per-slot contact with a ground that does not update on the system's own output. It is a property of the system and its habitat together, not of the system alone. Three things follow: a pooled benchmark score can hide a live failure on one slot; a synthetic harness proves wiring and never capability because it substitutes a habitat; and a system's own report about whether it understands cannot count, because a checker that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it.
>
> **What this claim deliberately does not say:** it does not name a particular machine, benchmark, or threshold; it does not say that a fluent interrogator's judgment is worthless, only that it answers a question about people rather than a question about contact; it does not say that current systems lack something forever, only that no source-discriminating instrument has been shown here; and it does not say this derivation is a measurement. It is a shape and a route, not a result.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these down before this message and before any framework material was shown:
>
> 1. A system shows capability against a non-updating ground in a reproduced habitat, and that capability is fully explained by internal composition with no contact with a differing closure.
> 2. An instrument is demonstrated that reads the same behavior and separates the two states at the per-slot level without any provenance supplied from outside the reading.
> 3. A pooled score is shown to track per-slot contact with no hidden live slot across a habitat substitution.
> 4. Two frames with a shared evidence pool are shown to give independent corroboration.
> 5. Understanding is shown to be a property of the system alone that survives habitat substitution with every goal-required contact removed.
>
> If any of these holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before I show you anything else:** which of those five would you point at, and what would you count as evidence that a machine understands rather than merely processes structure? Please record your answer first. I am asking because a later agreement from someone who has already seen my framing is not evidence; it is the framing talking back.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send three public sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority. Ask and I will send the links.
>
> **If you want the method rather than my conclusion:** the eight-step loop is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Distribution rule

The message is not a survey of opinions. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If the recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat treat it as if it came from any other source. If the recipient returns agreement after seeing the framing, record it as one witness from a shared pool and give it zero verdict weight (Fig 2 :40).
