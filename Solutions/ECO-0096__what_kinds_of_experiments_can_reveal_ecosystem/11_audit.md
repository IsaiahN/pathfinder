## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **reference-atom provenance** (covariate-selected versus parallel-trends versus minted), which the shorthand compresses into "low-overlap readings" but which changes behaviour in the field;
- the **capability versus mechanism distinction** at the substituted habitat, which the shorthand compresses into "mesocosm is substitution" and which the mesocosm literature keeps separate;
- the **instrument precondition** from Fig 6 :32-35, which the shorthand compresses into "extend the instrument" and which actually requires something already returning something at some resolution;
- the **gap itself**, which a reader can easily turn into "no design is known" (a budget statement) rather than "no figure line names one" (a structural statement).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the action-price reading and the four-condition separator. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the metalaws would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
