# Expert verification packet: the Tether on constrained ecosystem experiments

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimental ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural claim, `gap` on the named design, not `grounded`.** No ecosystem measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. The whole packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When controlled manipulation is impractical or unethical, the figures do not return a ranked list of experimental designs. They return a four-condition separator that any qualifying design must satisfy: a non-updating anchor, genuinely low-overlap readings, a habitat cascade enumerated past direct contact, and a residual indexed per slot rather than averaged into one ecosystem score. An exogenous perturbation can be the anchor; a counterfactual model tuned to agreement cannot. A mesocosm is a substituted habitat, valid for mechanism shape and silent about field capability. Named designs are bindings from the ecology frame onto this separator, recorded as provenance and held `candidate` until a ground settles them per slot. The framework returns a shape plus failure conditions written in advance, not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — The anchor condition (checkable by reading Figure 2)

- **Statement:** the settling thing need not be a randomized treatment. It must be something that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. An exogenous event qualifies; a model tuned until it agrees is a convention that has decided an outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Also Fig 2 :33 — *"The anchor must not update."* And Fig 2 :43 — *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
- **What to check:** can a reader name the perturbation's assignment process in your study, and show that the process did not respond to the analyst's model? For BACI and its relatives, is the reference site a covariate deliberately chosen (Stewart-Oaten, Murdoch and Parker 1986) or a population-drawn control under a parallel-trends assumption (Craig et al. 2017)? Both can be anchors; the difference must be declared.
- **Refuter:** exhibit an anchor that updates on the frame's own report and still settles the question; or show that only randomized assignment can ever qualify.

### C2 — The cascade condition (checkable by reading Figure 11 and one case)

- **Statement:** an ecosystem-level effect can sit several steps out from direct contact. A design scoped to first-order contact can return a clean null while the residual lives at the third order. The habitat is enumerated from the world, not composed from a list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43-44 — *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52-53 — *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **What to check:** in a study you know, was the response variable chosen at the point of direct contact, or after enumerating outward? The US EPA CADDIS case is a documented instance: *"extensive toxicity testing of DDT on adult bird mortality revealed no relationship"* because *"lethality was not the impairment; decline of birds-of prey was the impairment"*. The conclusion came from field observation, laboratory experiment and field stressor-response evidence together.
- **Refuter:** show an ecosystem-level effect whose causal chain is demonstrably first-order and fully captured by direct-contact enumeration; that narrows Fig 11 :52 to a special case.

### C3 — The substitution and per-slot conditions (checkable by reading Figures 11 and 1)

- **Statement:** a mesocosm, microcosm or test harness substitutes a habitat; it proves mechanism wiring and never field capability. Two failures are silent until they act: what you failed to reproduce and what you unintentionally introduced. The residual must remain indexed per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 — *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68-69 — *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*. Fig 11 :70 — *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** does the mesocosm literature you know support mechanism isolation while leaving capability transfer open? Macaulay et al. 2025 report enclosure artifacts and divergence from natural communities; Petersen and Kemp 2008 call scale a crucial extrapolation issue. Both are consistent with C3 as narrowed, not with a blanket ban on mesocosms.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a substituted habitat that reproduces field capability rather than mechanism wiring, with the field result arriving afterwards and matching per slot; or show a pooled ecosystem average that carries a live effect vanishing under every per-slot decomposition.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named design is figure-derived. BACI, RD, DiD, synthetic control, paleo records and mesocosms are bindings from the ecology frame, provenanced as candidates.
- Not that non-detection proves absence (Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44).
- Not that a pooled ecosystem index is the effect (Fig 1 :19).
- Not that a well-fitting model is an anchor (Fig 2 :43).
- Not that mesocosms are useless. The limit is capability transfer, not mechanism isolation.
- Not `grounded`, and not a measurement of any ecosystem by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

1. For a named study, show that the anchor's assignment process was independent of the analyst's model (C1).
2. Show that the two readings draw on genuinely different evidence and that agreement is not one pool talking (Fig 2 :31, :40).
3. Enumerate the habitat outward and show where the residual actually sits, with a source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes no effect, displaced effect, lagged effect and saturated metric.
4. Test the substitution limit: does a constructed habitat reproduce field capability per slot, or only mechanism?
5. Run the seven pre-registered refuters in `01` §3. A failure is a result.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29-33 and :40-43, Fig 11 :43-44 and :52-54, and Fig 11 :66-70, Fig 1 :19. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one impact study you know: was the response variable chosen at direct contact, or after a cascade enumeration?
3. Name one discrimination instrument that would separate no effect from displaced or lagged effect at the slot you care about, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
