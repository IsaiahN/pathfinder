## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

*The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the four-condition separator a design must satisfy before it can count as revealing an ecosystem-level effect. It constrains; it does not fill. "Use a BACI design" is a name. "Name an anchor that did not update, buy low-overlap readings, enumerate the cascade past direct contact, and keep `R` indexed per slot" is a shape. The separator can be refuted condition by condition; a preferred design cannot, because a preference is not a bound term (Fig 5 :62-63).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. Here: describe "a perturbation not under researcher control that divides the system into exposure groups", not "difference-in-differences", because the latter is a term only econometricians can read.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The four-condition separator is exactly that: plain enough that a field ecologist can check it against a real study without adopting the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the start was the effect statement of the residual and the stop is the same separator, now bound as an ordinary term.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: a named design earns a name when a per-slot ground reading pays for it in a specific ecosystem.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. BACI enters from impact assessment, RD and DiD from econometrics, time series from policy evaluation, cores from paleoecology. Each explains a gap the ecology frame could not close alone, and each carries its provenance.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar's mapping): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one design as *the* answer to an ecosystem turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly: metamerism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several paths can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, so *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The case was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, the spectrometer.

Applied here, two distinct failures are named:

- **Source named from a projection.** A before/after signal, a significant BACI interaction, or a fitted counterfactual is a reading. It does not by itself identify the source. Several mechanisms can produce the same per-slot contrast. The figure's DDT case is the canonical example: the early toxicity tests read mortality and named the source as acute toxicity, while the actual source was eggshell thinning several steps out (Fig 11 :52-54; EPA CADDIS case opened 2026-11-23).
- **One instrument standing for all sources.** A single monitoring index that returns "no change" cannot discriminate among no effect, a displaced effect, a lagged effect, and a saturated metric. Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. What is owed is a discriminator (a second low-overlap instrument, a longer channel, a cascade enumeration), not a stronger claim from the same reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A design that validates itself by its own significance test is the named failure.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape does an experiment need in order to reveal ecosystem-level effects when manipulation is impractical or unethical?** Four conditions, plus the refusal of the standing list:

1. **Non-updating anchor.** The perturbation or measurement settles the question and did not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. An exogenous event qualifies; a model tuned until it agrees does not. Fig 2 :29-30, :33, :43.
2. **Low-overlap readings.** Independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence (Fig 2 :31). Agreement from one pool is the pool talking (Fig 2 :40). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
3. **Enumerated cascade.** List actors, conditions and relations outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-48). The residual may be three steps out (Fig 11 :52-53). A first-order-scoped design can return a clean, wrong null.
4. **Per-slot residual.** `R` is indexed by object slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19, :22). Ecosystem level is a scale, not a pooled average.

Plus the substitution rule: a mesocosm or harness is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66) with two silent failures (Fig 11 :68-69); it speaks to mechanism shape, not field capability (Fig 11 :70).

**Does this derivation commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the fourth condition is not silently promoted into a name. It does not say "the answer is BACI" or "the answer is a mesocosm." It says what any answer must satisfy and records the chosen design as a provenanced binding. If a later reader takes the separator itself as a final theory of ecological design, that reader has made the metamerism error in reverse: a shape has been read as a source.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I initially treated *"shape, not a name"* as a ban on naming any design. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. BACI, RD, mesocosms and paleo records are all already in the ecology library. Naming one as a candidate binding onto the separator is **provenance**, not the invention of a new primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"* adds none here). What remains forbidden is claiming the framework derives the name. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 6).

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ecologists who agree with the separator is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a real residual, and a ground that pays or does not.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
