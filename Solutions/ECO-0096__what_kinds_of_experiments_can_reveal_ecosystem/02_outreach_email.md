# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the list

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been re-checked against the verbatim figure text in this folder. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Check one design-shape claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about experimental ecology. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a manipulative experiment is impractical or unethical, the figures do not return a ranked list of designs. They return a four-condition separator that any qualifying design must satisfy. (1) There must be an anchor that settles the question and did not change its answer because of what any frame thinks; an exogenous event can be an anchor, a model tuned to agree cannot. (2) The readings must be genuinely low-overlap; two observers of the same data set are one witness, not two. (3) The habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; the figure's own example is DDT reaching the eagles, where the residual that mattered was three steps out. (4) The residual stays indexed per species, site, and time; a pooled ecosystem average near zero with one live slot is a legal state, not a clean null. A substituted habitat such as a mesocosm is allowed for mechanism shape, but it proves wiring and never field capability.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named design, BACI, regression discontinuity, difference-in-differences, synthetic control, paleo record, or mesocosm, is figure-derived. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Which design occupies a slot is a binding from your field, provenanced as such, and a candidate until the ground settles it. I am also not claiming that non-detection proves absence, or that an average response captures an ecosystem-level effect.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. A design with a non-updating anchor, two low-overlap readings, and a cascade enumerated at least three steps out whose per-slot residual disagrees with the ground when the ground is later measured.
> 2. A demonstration that the DDT and eagle cascade is first-order, so it does not govern what "ecosystem-level" means.
> 3. A substituted habitat that reproduces field capability rather than mechanism wiring, with the field result arriving afterwards and matching per slot.
> 4. A figure line that names a specific design for a stated ecosystem effect, which would close the gap I am reporting.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the affected condition. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The figures themselves (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on an ecosystem problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [name]

## Two contact rules that are easy to get wrong

1. **Do not lead with the DDT example as if it were proof.** It is the figure's own illustration and a corroborating world case, not a measurement this derivation made. Present it as the shape the figures predict, then let the reader check it.
2. **Do not pool later agreement.** If a recipient ends up agreeing after stage 2, that agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration. Record their verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown, and keep the two separate (Fig 2 :40).
