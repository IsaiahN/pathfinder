# Solution ECO-0096

**Question:** What kinds of experiments can reveal ecosystem-level effects when controlled manipulation is impractical or unethical?

**Source:** ecology.txt line 117

**Section:** 10. Measurement, prediction, and ecological theory

**Mode:** `specified` on the shape of a qualifying design and on the per-domain verdicts; `general` on the structural claim that design classes are bonds to an anchor; `gap` on which named design fits which ecosystem and effect. Nothing here is `grounded`: no ecosystem measurement was taken by this derivation.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## The residual in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is indexed per ecosystem slot: per species, site, trophic position, region, and time (Fig 1 :19). The anchor that normally settles `R` is a controlled manipulative experiment, because a randomized treatment/control contrast does not update on what the researcher thinks (Fig 2 :29-30). The question removes that anchor by premise: manipulation is impractical (scale, duration, irreversibility, cost) or unethical (one may not randomize harm). The residual is therefore not "which experiment is best" but "which arrangements can still range against a non-updating ground and return a per-slot residual when the direct action is unavailable." Answered in effect terms, the candidate must (i) name an anchor that did not update on the frame, (ii) buy independence between readings, (iii) enumerate the habitat cascade outward until the cascade stops mattering, and (iv) keep verdicts per slot rather than averaging them into one ecosystem number.

## What an answer would look like (Direction, bound as an ordinary term)

Before searching, the sought-for shape is written down as a checkable term: a **design-shape separator**, four conditions any qualifying design must satisfy and a falsifier that would break it. The separator, not a list of named designs, is the deliverable. Naming BACI, regression discontinuity, a mesocosm, or a paleo record is a binding from the ecology frame onto the shape, recorded as provenance and held as `candidate`, never as a figure-derived name.

## Key verdict, stated once

No figure line selects a named experimental design for an ecosystem. The figures fix the **shape** any qualifying design must have and refuse the standing list (Fig 11 :51). The residue that survives re-census: unusable action cost forces designs that price description and independence instead (Fig 12 :125-127); an exogenous perturbation can be an anchor provided it did not update (Fig 2 :29-30, :33); two readings sharing one evidence pool are one witness (Fig 2 :40, Fig 8 :48); the effect may be three steps out, so the habitat must be enumerated, not composed (Fig 11 :43-44, :52-53); a substituted habitat proves wiring and never field capability (Fig 11 :66-70); failure to detect is not proof of absence (Fig 8 :39, Fig 9 :44). Per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
