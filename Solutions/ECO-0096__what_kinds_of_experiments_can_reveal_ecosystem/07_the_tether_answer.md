# The Tether's answer: experiments for ecosystem-level effects under constraint

**Mode: `specified` on the separator and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the named design, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An ecosystem-level effect is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` indexed per species, site, trophic position, region and time (Fig 1 :19). The usual anchor is a randomized manipulative experiment, which does not update on what the analyst thinks (Fig 2 :29-30). The question removes that anchor: manipulation is impractical because the system's scale, duration, connectivity or irreversibility makes the action price prohibitive, or unethical because harm cannot be randomized. The residual is not "which design is best" but "which arrangements can still range against a non-updating ground, with independent readings, an enumerated cascade and per-slot verdicts." This is a union compound: several conditions overlap, so one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape: a four-condition design-shape separator

1. **A non-updating anchor.** Name the perturbation or measurement and show it did not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* An exogenous event qualifies. A counterfactual model tuned until it agrees does not: Fig 2 :43, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."*
2. **Low-overlap readings.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Three observers of one data set are one witness. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Irreversible ecosystem decisions justify real cost here.
3. **An enumerated cascade.** Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :52-53: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A design scoped to direct contact can report a clean, wrong null.
4. **A per-slot residual.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Ecosystem level is a scale of description, not permission to pool. A global mean near zero with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13).

**The substitution rule.** A mesocosm, microcosm or harness is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :66), with two silent failures, *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"* (Fig 11 :68-69). Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Substituted habitats speak to mechanism shape, not field capability. Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61), so a "control" is never a habitat-free state.

**The instrument rule.** Instruments are not specified from descriptions; they are improved from worse instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32-35). INWARD means sharpening the long-view record, monitoring, cores and remote sensing. OUTWARD means importing a design frame whose closure differs, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52). Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."* More regressions on the same data set are SEARCHED, not a new instrument (Fig 6 :49).

**The design classes are bindings, not answers.** BACI and difference-in-differences are the same written term over `before`, `after`, `impact`, `reference` with different reference qualifiers; RD and interrupted time series are different molecules over partly overlapping atoms; synthetic control mints a reference atom; a mesocosm substitutes the habitat. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The same is true of designs.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named design is figure-derived. A design is a provenanced binding onto the separator and remains `candidate`.
- Not that a non-detection is a non-effect. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- Not that a pooled ecosystem index captures the effect. It is an observer summary, not the ground.
- Not that a model with a good fit is an anchor. It is a frame (Fig 2 :43).
- Not that mesocosms are useless. They are valid for mechanism isolation; the limit is capability transfer.
- Not `grounded`. No ecosystem measurement was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A ground reading, not agreement. The specific discriminators:

1. **Anchor test.** For a candidate study, exhibit the perturbation's assignment process and show it did not move in response to the analyst's model (Fig 2 :29-33).
2. **Independence test.** Show that the two readings draw on different evidence: different instruments, different observers, different closures (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :48). Shared data means one witness.
3. **Cascade test.** Enumerate outward at least three steps and show where the residual actually sits. The EPA CADDIS DDT case is the standing illustration: the endpoint first tested, adult mortality, was not the impairment, which was reproductive failure through eggshell thinning (opened 2026-11-23).
4. **Source-discriminating instrument.** From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer settled it, not an argument. Here: an instrument that distinguishes no effect from displaced effect from lagged effect from saturated metric would move a slot from `candidate` toward `grounded`.
5. **Standing falsifiers.** The seven conditions in `01` §3, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** No figure line names a design for a stated ecosystem and effect. That is a declared `gap`, and a verdict rather than silence (Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*). The six-term substrate and the figure set are a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; another frame's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a best experiment for ecosystem-level effects, and it does not claim a final answer. It says that when manipulation becomes too costly or too unethical, the price shifts from actions to description and independence: any design that reveals an ecosystem-level effect must name an anchor that did not update on the analyst's own model, buy readings that do not share one evidence pool, enumerate the habitat cascade outward until the residual stops moving, and keep the result indexed per species and site instead of averaging it into a single ecosystem score. An exogenous event can be an anchor; a model tuned to agree cannot. A mesocosm is a substituted habitat, useful for mechanism and silent about field capability. What the framework returns is this shape plus failure conditions written in advance, not a ranked list of methods. The slot stays open until the ground settles it per species and per site.
