## QUESTION

What kinds of experiments can reveal ecosystem-level effects when controlled manipulation is impractical or unethical? Restated as an effect: how can `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` be reduced at each ecosystem slot when the anchor that normally settles it (a randomized treatment/control experiment) is unavailable because the action price is prohibitive? Option A: return a ranked list of named designs. Option B: return a four-condition separator that any qualifying design must satisfy, with a pre-registered refuter and the design names held as provenanced bindings. A leads to a standing list whose valence flips when the residual changes (Fig 11 :51); B leads to a checkable shape whose slot stays open until the ground pays (Fig 9 :32). The answer below is B, with the named-design question declared `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (residual).** Arity: multi-actor and multi-relational, including actors, conditions and relations (Fig 11 :45-47). Symmetry: asymmetric, introduction available and exclusion not (Fig 11 :61); valence relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-50); comparison is a threshold, not a symmetric join (Fig 9 :22). Scale: per slot across nested scales linked by a lossy membrane (Fig 1 :19, Fig 4).
- **D1 Manipulative experiment.** Actors: experimenter, randomized plots. Currency: assigned action and action cost. Anchor: measured response to assignment. Status: admitted as reference only, unavailable by the premise. Fig 12 :125-127 prices it in actions.
- **D2 Exogenous-perturbation design.** Actors: an event outside researcher control, impact and reference sites, repeated observations. Currency: before/after and impact/reference contrast. Anchor: the event, if it did not update. Status: admitted, triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 6). BACI, BACIPS, DiD, RD, ITS and synthetic control are ecology/econometrics bindings here.
- **D3 Observational and gradient study.** Actors: covariates, multiple instruments or observers. Currency: covariance, spatial or temporal gradient. Anchor: present only when readings are low-overlap. Status: conditionally admitted, single-source on the figure side.
- **D4 Substituted-habitat experiment.** Actors: enclosed community, enclosure artifacts. Currency: control, replication, mechanism isolation. Anchor: the real habitat approached through substitution. Status: mechanism only, triangulated with strain S1 (Fig 11 :66-70).
- **D5 Long-view record.** Actors: monitoring, archives, cores, remote sensors. Currency: temporal depth. Anchor: ground that does not decay while the channel does. Status: admitted, triangulated (Fig 10, Fig 6).
- **D6 Model and counterfactual frame.** Actors: model terms, donor pool, simulation. Currency: description bits, counterfactual. Anchor: none by itself. Status: admitted as a shape-predicting frame with source gate and shadow test, never a verdict alone.
- **X1 "The ecosystem" as one pooled outcome.** No nameable anchor per slot. Not a domain yet; refused.
- **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :54-57: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Dependency order: perceive slots and cascade, acquire the objective term, choose route, carry method (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test applied before conclusion: Fig 11 :70 versus mesocosm utility re-census separates capability transfer from mechanism isolation; Fig 2 :40 versus multiple lines of evidence is not strained because low-overlap readings are the permitted case. Both recorded in `01` §5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's premise is a prohibitive action price, so the bargain shifts the design spend from actions to description and independence (Fig 12 :125-127). What survives is a four-condition separator: a non-updating anchor (exogenous event allowed, tuned model refused), low-overlap readings, a habitat cascade enumerated past direct contact, and `R` indexed per slot. A substituted habitat serves mechanism isolation, not field capability. The design classes are bindings onto the shape, not figure-derived answers: BACI and DiD are functionally identical over a shared atom set with different reference qualifiers; RD and ITS are different molecules; synthetic control mints a reference atom; a mesocosm substitutes the habitat. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. No figure line names a design for a stated ecosystem effect; that is a declared `gap`, and a verdict rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the shape and per-domain claims, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the name, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, separates filters from witnesses and keeps verdicts per slot, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded design list instead of a checkable generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The named design bindings are removable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A circulated claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that returns per-slot contact for a candidate design; a demonstrated first-order ecosystem effect that narrows Fig 11 :52; a substituted habitat that reproduces field capability per slot (refutes Fig 11 :70); an anchor shown to update yet settle (refutes Fig 2 :33); or a figure re-census that names a design (closes the gap). Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
