## What is being decided, in effect terms

Two readings are on the table. **A:** define quantum information as the outcome of a measurement (a Shannon-style entropy of the distribution the chosen context yields), and accept that the definition changes with the context. **B:** define quantum information as the quantity that does not change when the context changes, and treat the measurement context as the pose that renders one outcome from it. A leads to a definition with no anchor, where the chosen measurement decides what the word means. B leads to a shape with an open slot and a stated refuter. The derivation in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` records which figure lines govern each.

What an answer would look like, written down before the run (formula step "Direction"): a definition that (1) does not move when the measurement context moves, (2) names the context as a pose rather than as the thing being defined, (3) places the information carried by the system and context together in the relation rather than in the system alone, and (4) stays open about which physical quantity occupies the invariant slot until a ground settles it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the framework settles which physical quantity is "the" definition of quantum information.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283, *"A room does not upgrade the label... Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the architecture.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
