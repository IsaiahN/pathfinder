## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly

1. **Context is a set of compatible observables, not a vague background.** Budroni et al. define a context operationally and distinguish the observables perspective from the effects perspective. This sharpens what the "pose" in the Symbols table amounts to in a real experiment. It does not derive the figure line; it corroborates its shape.
2. **Invariance under basis change is the operational content of the anchor.** Witten shows that `S(ρ)` is invariant under `ρ → UρU⁻¹`, while ordinary Shannon entropy requires a specified distribution first. That is exactly the distinction between the anchor and the read-out. It is a physics-frame binding, not a figure derivation, and it is marked as such.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union split, the anchor test and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Two-stage offer.** Stage 1: send the residual, the shape claim and the four pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record that verdict before any theory is shown. Stage 2, opt-in only: if they are curious, offer the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Stage 3: offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is stated as `general` and `specified`, with the specific entropy binding marked as a provenanced import. Do not upgrade it to `grounded` before a ground reading exists.
