# The Tether's answer on information in quantum systems

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the binding to a context-invariant state quantity, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For one prepared system, different compatible-measurement sets return different outcome values, and no assignment of noncontextual definite values reproduces all of them. The question asks what "information in the system" is when the read-out depends on that choice. Fig 9 :4-5 makes this a **union compound**, not one hard problem: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The definition loop and the context loop have separate grounds.

## 2. The shape

1. **A definition that moves with the measurement context has no anchor.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The chosen context is a frame. If it decides what the word denotes, the definition is panel 1 of Fig 2: convergent drift toward whoever had the most influence. The read-out is a legitimate operational quantity; it is not the anchor.
2. **The context is the pose, not the thing being defined.** Symbols table `F`: *"The map between allocentric and egocentric at one scale... Cannot be run without a pose."* The invariant quantity is allocentric; the outcome is egocentric. The same state returns different outcomes because the pose changed. This is the figure's structural reading of contextuality.
3. **The bond carries the information the operands do not.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Dropping the context from the description drops information, not noise.
4. **The map is lossy and the round trip must be measured.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* One context is a projection; a state estimate composed from settled read-outs is the return trip. `R_T` is what the coarser description cannot hold.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The slot is open. Quantum information theory offers `S(ρ) = −Tr(ρ log ρ)` as the occupant, invariant under unitary change of basis. That is recorded as a binding from the physics frame, `specified` and ungrounded. It is not derived from the six-term set and it does not move the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none).
6. **A noncontextual definite-value definition is refused.** Kochen-Specker (Held, SEP; Budroni et al., RMP 2022) rules it out in dimension three and above. Re-census against Fig 2: the anchor is the context-invariant state quantity, not a value map over observables. Value definiteness and state invariance are different objects (Fig 13 :38-39).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that Shannon entropy of a read-out is the definition. It is indexed by a specified random variable, so it is context-relative.
- Not that the framework settles which functional of the state is "the" definition. That name is owed to a ground or a provenanced import.
- Not that contextuality is a defect to be removed by better bookkeeping. The context is a typed relation in the molecule.
- Not that this resolves any interpretation of quantum mechanics. It states a shape any candidate definition must have.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at this level settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Invariant test:** does the proposed quantity stay fixed under every change of measurement context (unitary change of basis) for the same preparation? A measurement that shows it moving breaks the anchor claim.
- **Pose test:** can an outcome be registered without any compatible-measurement set being specified? If yes, the pose requirement fails.
- **Bond test:** does removing the context leave the outcome distribution unchanged for every state? If yes, the bond carries nothing and Fig 12 :5 is broken.
- **KS test, restated so it can fire:** a noncontextual definite-value model reproducing all predictions in dimension at least three. That would collapse the union into one question.
- **Standing framework falsifiers:** derive one of the six from the other five; admit a state-state or structure-structure composition that cannot be refused; show a lossless round trip for all states.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner among definitions of quantum information and does not claim a final answer. It says that a definition whose value changes with the measurement context has no anchor, so information in the system must be the quantity that does not move when the context moves; that the context is the pose required to render an outcome; and that the information is carried by the system-context relation, not by the system alone. It refuses to name the occupant of the invariant slot, returning a shape rather than a name. Quantum information theory offers the von Neumann entropy, invariant under change of basis, and that is recorded as a binding from the physics frame, not as a derivation. The question is a union of a definition loop and a context loop with separate grounds, so it must be split before it is searched. What is offered is the shape any candidate definition must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
