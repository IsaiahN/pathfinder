## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how information should be defined in a quantum system when the read-out depends on the measurement context. Option A: identify information with the read-out of the chosen context and accept that the definition moves with the context. Option B: identify it with the quantity that does not move when the context moves, demote the context to a pose, and keep the occupant of the invariant slot open until a ground settles it. What each leads to: A gives a definition with no anchor, where a convention decides an outcome; B gives a checkable shape, four written refuters and an honest stop. A third reading, a noncontextual assignment of definite values to all observables, is refused before either by the Kochen-Specker result.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question must be split before it is searched. A definition whose value moves with the measurement context has no anchor, so information in the system should be defined as the quantity that does not move when the context moves; the context is the pose that renders a read-out; and the information is carried by the system-context bond, not by the system alone. The framework returns this shape and refuses to name the occupant of the invariant slot. Quantum information theory offers `S(ρ) = −Tr(ρ log ρ)`, invariant under change of basis, recorded as a `specified`, ungrounded binding from the physics frame. Shannon outcome entropy is refused as the anchor; a noncontextual definite-value definition is refused by Kochen-Specker. Status: `candidate`. `triangulated` for the shape and the union split, `single-source` for the pose line and the entropy binding, one named strain on the pricing of `F`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the binding as provenance, so an error is findable. The real cost is presenting a context-relative read-out as a context-independent definition, which lets a convention decide an outcome (Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The binding to `S(ρ)` can be replaced without touching the shape: the anchor requirement, the pose structure and the bond claim stand independently. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument showing that the proposed invariant moves under a change of measurement context would break the anchor claim; a context-free outcome would break the pose claim; a context removable with no change in outcomes would break the bond claim; a noncontextual definite-value model reproducing all predictions in dimension three or above would collapse the union and remove the need for a pose; a lossless round trip for all states would break the lossy-transform claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
