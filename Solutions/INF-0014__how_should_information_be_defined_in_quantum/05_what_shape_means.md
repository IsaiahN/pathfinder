## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *information in the system is the quantity that does not move when the measurement context moves, and the context is the pose that renders a read-out.*
- A name: *information in the system is the von Neumann entropy* asserted as the framework's answer.

The first is figure-derived. The second is a binding from the physics frame that must be provenanced, not a derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question the effect description is "does not change when the basis changes", not a formula for entropy.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: the room is one problem's work spread across many bodies, but only methods cross up. A shape is a method, not a recording of one successful measurement.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming the von Neumann entropy as *the* definition would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. Recording it as a binding from the physics frame leaves the choice visible.

## 4. Why "the read-out is the information" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

A measurement read-out is exactly such a projection. Several hidden decompositions can produce the same distribution, and the read-out cannot say which. Naming the read-out as "the information in the system" is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the measurement-side version: *"Measurement doesn't reveal the value. It creates the value by collapsing the space of values."* This is why the outcome is not a pre-existing item to be read off and called information; it is produced at the joint where the pose is applied.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular context-invariant quantity? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape: the anchor must not move with the frame, and the pose is required to render an outcome. A particular functional is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. The von Neumann entropy satisfies the invariance condition and is the standard physics candidate, but that is a binding recorded from the physics frame. Until a ground settles the choice, it is `candidate`.

**Does the audit commit the error the README forbids?** No, after the correction in §5b. `01` states the shape, records `S(ρ)` as a binding from the physics frame, and refuses to call it the framework's answer. The earlier draft did call it the answer; that reading is withdrawn below.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §5

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. The state and the entropy are already held in the physics frame. Naming them is a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is deriving the binding from the six-term set without physics input, or claiming that it moves the wall (Fig 6: *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; this one adds none to the figure set).

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a read-out still cannot comment on its source, and the read-out is still a projection with loss. What changes is the reason: the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers reputable names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground (an instrument, an experiment, a board) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
