## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether customers are sincere. It is why a *reading taken in words* and a *reading taken at the till* of the same slot diverge. In effect terms:

- **b** is the customer's belief about what a product affords, formed from history and inherited priors.
- **a** is the act available to the customer: pay the premium, decline the data disclosure, choose the cheaper alternative.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the stated answer predicts will happen: "I value sustainability, so I will buy the certified one."
- **o′** is what the market returns: the actual choice, the price actually paid, the data actually disclosed.
- **s** is the customer and the purchase occasion. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per customer, per attribute, per occasion. It is never aggregated into "consumers care, but do not buy" (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)

- Option A, "the stated answer is the true preference and the purchase is a failure to enact it": predicts that the gap is a single preference problem and that better information is the lever.
- Option B, "the two readings are different instruments with different anchors, read in different habitats": predicts a union of at least two conditions, one about measurement and one about contact, with a categorically different residue where both bind.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a shape naming the conditions that must both hold for the two readings to come apart, plus an instrument that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is bound in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. The specific driver of any one customer's choice is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the marketing question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of consumer choice.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
