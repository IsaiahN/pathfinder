# The Tether's answer: stated values versus revealed choice

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No purchase data was collected.

## 1. The residual it answers

The customer's avowal and the customer's choice are two readings of the same slot that disagree. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per customer, per attribute, per occasion, and never aggregated into "consumers care but do not buy".

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)

The divergence is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57). It has its own loop and its own ground: an instrument-and-anchor condition and a contact condition, with a categorically different residue where both bind.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two readings, two anchors, two questions.** The avowal is the legitimate anchor for "what did this person say"; the market is the legitimate anchor for "what did this person choose".

   > **"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."** (Fig 2 :36)
   > **"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."** (Fig 2 :37-38)

   The avowal is an instrument for the choice question. Promoting it to an anchor for that question is the category error at the center of the "gap".

2. **A survey is a substituted habitat.** It proves wiring, never capability.

   > **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."** (Fig 11 :70)

   In the survey the action costs words; at the shelf it costs money and disclosure. These are not different strengths of one reading.

3. **Words and actions are two currencies and must be held apart.**

   > **"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart."** (Fig 12 :130)

   "They say they value it but will not pay for it" sums the two and reports the difference as a weak preference. That is the named error.

4. **Capability is customer-and-habitat; valence is relative to the residual.**

   > **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** (Fig 11 :39)
   > **"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** (Fig 11 :49)

   Sustainability, quality, and privacy are not standing goods. They pay when the habitat makes them available, affordable, legible, and trusted at the point of choice, and they do not otherwise. More consumer education is an agent-only improvement (Fig 11 :40).

5. **Contact can fail while the ground keeps paying.**

   > **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"** (Fig 10 :24)
   > **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"** (Fig 10 :26)

   A fast reversal of stated value at the shelf is a channel failure, not proof that the value never existed.

6. **The habitat is enumerated out to the second and third order.**

   > **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** (Fig 11 :52)

   Price premium, availability, default, label trust, peer norm, and time pressure are read off the world, not composed from an attitude score.

7. **The specific driver is not named.**

   > **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"** (Fig 9 :32)
   > **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** (Fig 13 :134)

   The framework returns the union, the two-currency rule, and an instrument described in effect terms. It does not say which segment buys for which reason.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that customers lie, are hypocritical, or lack willpower. "Hypocrisy" fails the anchor test and is excluded before figure lines (`01` section 1.2, X1).
- Not that price is the sole driver. Price is a habitat condition and a comparison bond, not a separate domain.
- Not that information or nudging closes the gap. That is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a single-preference theory with a strength parameter. The readings are isomers under Fig 12 :117, not points on one scale.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the purchase occasion settles `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not a claim that the framework is the only true account of consumer choice. A uniqueness claim over all frames would need a census nobody has run (Fig 9 :44).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating design, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"it cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Two-reading test:** record the avowal and the realized choice on the same customer, product, attribute, and occasion. Does the word-reading predict the market-reading above its own instrument error with habitat held constant?
- **Two-currency test:** can a common unit make stated value and payment commensurable without loss on the same slot? If yes, F3 dies.
- **Contact test:** does stated value vanish while contact with price and consequence measures fresh, unpooled, and non-declining? If yes, F4's mechanism is not the cause.
- **Habitat substitution:** does the attribute keep invariant positive valence after payment, availability, or trust is removed at the point of choice? If yes, F5 dies.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4, all written before the run.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is an instrument that discriminates among the sources of the same word-reading. It is characterized in effect terms but not held. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 makes unreachable a verdict, not a silence. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

The world literatures corroborate the shape at low overlap: green consumption, privacy research, stated-preference economics, and health psychology each find the same two-reading structure with a renamed anchor. They do not name the customer-level driver, and where they share the intention-behaviour label they are one pool, not four witnesses.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say customers are hypocrites, and it does not name the driver of any one purchase. It says that "I value sustainability" and the choice at the till are two readings of the same customer by two instruments with two different anchors: the avowal settles what was said, the market settles what was chosen, and neither settles the other's question. Words cost bits and purchases cost actions, so they are two currencies that must not be summed into one preference strength. The capability to choose well is a property of customer and habitat together, not of the customer alone; a value changes sign when the habitat stops paying for it, and contact with that fact can decay in the channel while the market itself keeps paying. The question is a union of an instrument condition and a contact condition, a case in two conditions rather than a harder case of one. What is offered is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the design of an instrument that would separate the readings. The specific driver stays open until the ground settles it.
