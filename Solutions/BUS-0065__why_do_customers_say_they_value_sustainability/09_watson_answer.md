# WATSON answer: BUS-0065, stated values versus revealed choice

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No purchase data was collected.

## QUESTION

Why do customers say they value sustainability, quality, or privacy but sometimes choose cheaper or less responsible products? Restated as an effect: why do the word-reading and the market-reading of the same customer-attribute-occasion diverge, with R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| indexed per slot and never aggregated? Option A: treat the avowal as the true preference and the purchase as a failure to enact it, which predicts one preference problem and an information lever. Option B: treat the readings as two instruments with different anchors read in different habitats, which predicts a union of an instrument condition and a contact condition with a categorically different residue where both bind. The verdict below is Option B in `specified` mode, and the specific driver is a declared `gap`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-preference framing does not. The avowal and the purchase are two readings of one slot by two instruments with different anchors: the avowal settles "what was said", the market settles "what was chosen", and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure (Fig 2 :36-38). Words and actions are two currencies held apart; summing them into a preference strength is the named error (Fig 12 :130). Capability is customer-and-habitat, valence is relative to the residual, and contact can fail while the ground keeps paying (Fig 11 :39, Fig 10 :24). The divergence is a union of an instrument condition and a contact condition, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :57). The specific driver is a declared `gap`, since the framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32). Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument.

- **cost if wrong:** a misdirected diagnosis. A customer failing the contact condition would be sold an instrument-condition lever (more information, another survey), and a customer failing the instrument condition would be sold a habitat lever (a price cut), and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and reversible, and it is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if a finding fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out. The shape and all five refuters are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A preregistered two-reading test in which the survey moves the purchase with habitat held constant (breaks C1); a lossless common unit for stated value and payment (breaks C2); stated value vanishing while contact measures fresh (breaks F4); invariant attribute valence across habitat substitution (breaks F5); one intervention restoring both union conditions with no categorically different residue (breaks C3); or a figure re-census that admits the physical six as customer valuations without strain (breaks the S1 strain record). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
