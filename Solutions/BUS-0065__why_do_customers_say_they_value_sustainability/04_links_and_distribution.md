## What in the wider literature bears on this question

The world sources are secondary. They corroborate a shape; they do not derive it, and they never overrule the figures.

1. **The primary field names the two readings.** ElHaffar, Durif and Dubé (2020) describe the green gap as the discrepancy between what consumers say about environmental concern and what they truly do, and note that *"the research focus must shift from exploring the reasons behind the gap to implementing solutions to it."* That last sentence is a budget instruction in world vocabulary, not a verdict; Fig 9 :60 keeps filters and witnesses apart. Blas (2022) names the same object *"the conundrum between positive attitude and apparent lack of corresponding behavior"* and uses choice-based conjoint, an instrument that puts the action price back into the reading. Both corroborate F1 and F2. Same broad field, so one witness, not two (Fig 2 :31).
2. **Economics prices the action.** Penn and Hu (2018) show that stated-preference methods carry hypothetical bias and that incentive-compatible designs mitigate it. That is the world-level name for Fig 12's two currencies. Low overlap with the figures, so a genuine corroboration of the shape.
3. **Health psychology finds the same shape at another stratum.** Rhodes and de Bruijn (2013) measure an intention-behaviour gap of 46%; Feil, Fritsch and Rhodes (2023) measure 47.6% and conclude that translation of positive intention into behaviour is nearly at chance. This is the Fig 4 kernel at a different scale, with actors, currency, and anchor renamed. It corroborates the two-reading shape, not the specific customer-level cause. The two health papers share an evidence pool and a vocabulary, so they count as one witness.
4. **Information systems finds it under privacy.** Norberg, Horne and Horne (2007) and Dong (2024) show the same divergence between disclosure concern and disclosure behaviour. Norberg's paper is an especially clean instance: the avowal and the action are both about the same data field. Corroborates F1.
5. **A mechanism candidate, not a verdict.** Gollwitzer (1999) on implementation intentions suggests that binding an intention to a specific situation can close part of the gap. This is a method that changes the bracket across the handoff (Fig 7 :38), not a new term that moves a wall (Fig 6 :21). The Crossref record is all that was opened; the claim is held at title level.
6. **Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap gives the outreach rule directly. Gathering well-known names to agree with the two-reading claim would spend exactly the independence the check needs.

No source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. Where sources share the intention-behaviour label they are recorded as overlapping at the label level; overlap is in `10_source_ledger.csv`, never averaged into the verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full derivation before anyone has described what they would count as a failure sends the source, not the residual. Lead with the two-reading claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the two-reading claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a misdiagnosed intervention applied to a union that needs two, so independence is worth buying.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the two-reading shape and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which instrument would discriminate the two conditions; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, with a third stage for method reuse

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration later.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem and must write down what would refute their result before running it (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The five refuters in `02_outreach_email.md` are written down precisely so that a failure can be reported in one line.
