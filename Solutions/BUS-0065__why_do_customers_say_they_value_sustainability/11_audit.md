Symmetry: asymmetric between word and market, because the avowal is an instrument for the choice question while the market is its anchor; "cheaper" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). - **D2 The stated-report instrument.** Actors: respondent, questionnaire, interviewer. Currency: words. Anchor: the avowal for the question about what was said (Fig 2 :36), not for the question about what was chosen. Admitted with restricted competence.
- **D3 The market habitat.** Actors: firms, price, availability, labels, defaults, peers. Currency: survival and trust. Anchor: customer choice. Admitted.
- **D4 The temporal channel.** Actors: habits, percepts, metrics. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 Hypocrisy as a free-floating property.** No anchor independent of the market. Not a domain yet. I reach the same exclusion as the primary, by a different route: I excluded it because a property with no instrument to read it cannot enter a slot-wise R at all, rather than because the market already anchors the choice question.
- **X2 Price as sole cause.** Already inside D3 and the comparison bond. Absorbed.

**Union, not partition.** D1/D2, D2/D3, D3/D4 overlap, each overlap a compound. **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); the middle link has no instrument (Fig 3 :27).

My census matches the primary's four domains and two exclusions. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29, :36-38 (two anchors), Fig 11 :70 (substituted habitat), Fig 12 :125-126, :130 (two currencies), Fig 9 :54-57 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in the outreach email: "two readings, two anchors, two currencies, a habitat that must pay, and a union of two conditions".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the customer-level claim: "the customer says sustainability matters but buys the cheaper item because the habitat does not pay for the attribute and the channel has gone stale".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19). The concrete sentence aggregates customers into "the customer", which is exactly the averaging the figure forbids;
- the **restricted competence of D2**. The shorthand "two anchors" can be heard as two grounds for the same question, whereas Fig 2 :36 gives the avowal competence only for "what was said";
- the **`gap` on the specific driver**. The concrete sentence smuggles in "because the habitat does not pay" as if the driver were named, while the derivation withholds it (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: two readings of one slot, different bonds and different prices, with the market settling the choice question. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no purchase data exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
