## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: two readings of one slot, different anchors, different currencies, a habitat that must pay, and a channel that can lose contact. The names "price", "habit", "distrust of the label", or "income constraint" are candidate fillers and are withheld until an instrument discriminates among them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The effect description here is: a word-reading and a market-reading of the same customer on the same occasion come apart. That description traveled to four low-overlap fields (green consumption, privacy research, stated-preference economics, health psychology), and each returned a version of the same shape. The return is corroboration of the shape only; each field named its own atom and none derived the figure claim.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union reading of section 1.3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, arrived at independently in another vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question: a realized purchase, a realized disclosure, a field experiment in which the price of the action is actually paid.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A world finding may be imported only after it is stated in effect terms and its provenance is recorded. Importing the word "habit" because it is familiar would be an adopted import masquerading as a convergence; the contents look identical and only the record separates them.

Gollwitzer's implementation intentions are the closest thing in the opened world sources to a candidate mechanism, but the Crossref record read this session gives the title only. Naming it as *the* driver would be a name bought on prestige, not on a source-discriminating instrument.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source.

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*

Applied here: the reading "I value sustainability" is a projection. Several sources can produce it - a genuine preference, a social-desirability reading in a survey context, a habit of answering positively, a belief that the interviewer expects it, or a value that is real but unaffordable at the shelf. The word-reading cannot comment on the source. Fig 11 :70 supplies the structural version: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

The source-discriminating instrument here is not a better argument. It is a design that separates the word-reading from the market-reading on the same slot and lets the ground pay. *The Hard Problem is About Source* states the rule: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* And Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The current folder has not found the driver; that is a report of the instrument's limit, not a proof that no driver exists.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework say the answer will look like?** Not a ranked list of barriers. It will be:

- a union of an instrument condition and a contact condition, kept separate per customer and per occasion (Fig 9 :54-57, Fig 1 :19);
- a statement that words and actions are two currencies and must not be summed (Fig 12 :130);
- a statement that the capability is customer-and-habitat and that valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :39, :49);
- an instrument, characterized in effect terms, that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of the other, with the price of the action actually paid;
- an open slot for the specific driver, reported as `gap` until the ground settles it (Fig 13 :134).

**Does the common framing commit the error the README forbids?** Yes. When it treats "they say they value it but choose cheaper" as one preference with a strength deficit, it fills the slot with a name before the discriminating instrument exists. The word-reading is then used as the ground for the choice question, which Fig 2 :37-38 forbids: *"instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers impressive consumer-research names to agree with the two-reading claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a purchase, a disclosure, a field experiment settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains public opt-in venues and no personal emails, and why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what would refute the shape rather than asking for agreement.

**Second consequence.** *Library of Babel*: *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."* A reader who checks the stage-1 shape and supplies their own discriminating instrument has authored part of the answer. That is the intended outcome. Their verdict is still recorded before they see the framework, so the authorship does not corrupt the check.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulation. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the marketing claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

This is not a disclaimer to be tucked away. It is the reason the verdict is `specified` and `candidate`, and the reason the five refuters in section 4 of `01` are written down before any outreach. A simulation with a pre-registered refuter is a design instrument; a simulation without one is an opinion wearing the vocabulary of a derivation.
