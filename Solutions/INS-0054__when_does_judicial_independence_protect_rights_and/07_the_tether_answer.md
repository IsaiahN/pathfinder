## 2. The shape

1. **The discriminating quantity is the placement of the insulation, not its magnitude.** Insulation protects rights while it runs between the judge and political pressure, because the judge is then an anchor that ranges against law, facts and the affected person. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." If the judge updates on majority will, "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34).

2. **The harm begins when insulation moves onto the ground channel.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"; Fig 10 :27: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch". At that point the seat is not independent but self-referential. Fig 2 :47: "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."

3. **Independence is a priced guard, not a verdict.** Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :43: "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A guard that fixes outcomes has changed kind. The bond is an ordered threshold between two placements, not a symmetric balance (Operators table :49: "a threshold relation between two quantities").

4. **Accountability correction is selection between rooms, not negotiation with a sitting judge.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :46: "The anchor is not a third party to the disagreement and it is not the tie-breaker in a vote." Elections and retention mechanisms are legitimate when they transmit a ground-bearing reason and select the next room against the residual the last one surfaced.

5. **The two effects are two currencies, not one scale.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." A single independence-versus-accountability score commits the averaging error Fig 1 :22 names: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

6. **Tenure is a structural stake that must be declared.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." Fig 10 :61: "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." Permanent tenure is exactly that standing. Denying it does not remove it; declaring it makes it checkable.

7. **The framework returns the shape, not the design value.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular country's courts are independent or not. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that a specific tenure, removal rule or election design is best. That is the `gap` in item 7.
- Not that rights and democracy trade off on one scale. They are two currencies (item 5).
- Not that agreement between judges and public settles either effect. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not that a clean provenance record makes a ruling right. Fig 10 :65: "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately."
- Not `grounded`. Verdict status is `candidate` until a ground pays.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, a reading "cannot comment on the source", and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists". Here the questions are:

- Does the ruling track law, facts and the affected person, rather than the majority's preference? An instrument that discriminates those sources would name the rights effect.
- Does the review or reason-giving channel actually reduce error? An instrument that measures correction rather than the existence of a rule would name the accountability effect.
- Does insulation move from the political relation onto the ground channel at a measurable point? V-Dem's high court independence index and comparable measures are instruments already returning readings, so INWARD is available (Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms.").

Standing falsifiers, written before the run: rights improving while the judge updated on majority preference; an intact ground channel with political correction absent and no error correction following; a single commensurable score for both effects; duration alone predicting outcomes; agreement from a shared evidence pool substituting for a ground reading.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for judicial independence. It gives the relation that decides the case. Independence protects rights while it insulates the judge from political pressure, because the judge is then an anchor ranging against law, facts and the person affected. The same insulation insulates from democratic accountability when it moves onto the judge's channel to that ground: the reasons stop tracking evidence, the correction channel goes quiet, and the seat becomes a self-referential frame. The two effects are isomers over the same atoms, with the insulation on different bonds, and they are priced in two currencies that do not add, so no balance score can hold both. Elections are legitimate as selection between rooms, not as negotiation with a sitting judge, and permanent tenure is a standing that must be declared. Which specific rule achieves this is a declared gap, not a hidden answer. What is offered is the shape, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground decides; agreement does not.
