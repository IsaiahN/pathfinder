## Restatement as an effect

The setting under examination is the insulation `I` of the adjudicative frame from the political and electoral frames. The question asks for the condition that separates two downstream effects:

- `E1` rights protection: the judge ranges against law, facts and the person affected without being moved by majority pressure;
- `E2` accountability insulation: the judge stops ranging against that ground, the channel decays, and no electoral or reason-giving correction reaches the seat.

An answer would look like a discriminating quantity `D`, stated in effect terms, such that `D` holds when the insulation runs between the judge and political pressure, and `D` fails when the insulation has moved onto the judge's channel to the ground. `D` is bound as an ordinary term before the run so it can accrue a residual and be refused (THE_FORMULA, "Direction"). The verdict is kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement, and no bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures settle the empirical threshold (how long a tenure, which removal rule). They return a shape and an honest stop.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the framework before the recipient has recorded a verdict spends their independence.
3. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Contact cost is a filter, never a witness.
