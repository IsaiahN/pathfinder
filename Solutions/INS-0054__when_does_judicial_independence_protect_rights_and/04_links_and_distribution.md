## What in the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Federalist No. 78 is an effect description, not a verdict.** Hamilton ties tenure to a function: keeping the legislature "within the limits assigned to their authority" and protecting "the minor party". That is the rights effect `E1` in the language of the audit. He also says the judiciary has "neither FORCE nor WILL, but merely judgment", which is the anchor rule: it ranges against a ground rather than negotiating with a branch. Both are cited as corroboration only; the figure lines decide.
2. **Counter-majoritarianism states the other slot without resolving it.** Bickel's difficulty is that unelected judges overrule elected representatives; the standard response is that substantive rights must be protected even when they check majoritarianism. This is a world-facing restatement of the union compound, not a figure line. It supplies no threshold value.
3. **Separation of duties supplies a low-overlap analogue.** In accounting and information systems, role separation is a control that prevents abuse, "must be balanced with the increased cost/effort required", and needs compensating controls when roles cannot be separated. This corroborates the figure claim that independence is a priced guard (Fig 2 :42) that can change kind when it fixes an outcome. It is one witness from a different field, not a second reading of the same evidence pool.
4. **Principal-agent supplies a second low-overlap analogue.** Accountability is a channel problem: the principal lacks the means to punish the agent and interests diverge. With multiple principals the problem intensifies. This corroborates the channel-decay account of `E2` without supplying a design value.
5. **V-Dem is an instrument, not an authority.** Its high court independence index is a ground-facing measure that could turn the shape into a reading. It is cited under INWARD, not OUTWARD: the instrument already returns something that fails to resolve, which is exactly the condition THE_FORMULA step 7 places on extending an instrument.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The four essays are by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is one evidence pool and carries no independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the discriminating condition and its refuter.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the condition were true before showing the figures.
- Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Do not select recipients by likelihood of agreement.
- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Stage 3 offers a reusable method, never a recording of this particular result.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask for endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the discriminating condition and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer the figure links, with the note that the figures, not my summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method they can run on a problem they can check, with their own refuter written first.
