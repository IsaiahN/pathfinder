# Expert verification packet: the Tether on judicial independence and accountability

**What this is:** a claim in a form a specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the application to courts, not `grounded`.** No measurement of a court system is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Standing:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Judicial independence is a relation, not a magnitude. It protects rights while the insulation runs between the judge and political pressure, so the judge ranges against law, facts and the affected person without updating on a majority's will. The same insulation insulates from democratic accountability once it moves onto the judge's channel to that ground: reasons stop tracking evidence, correction channels go quiet, and the seat becomes self-referential. The two effects are isomers over a shared atom set, differing in which bond carries the insulation, and they are priced in two currencies that do not add, so no single balance score can hold both. Elections are legitimate as selection between rooms, not as negotiation with a sitting judge. Permanent tenure is a standing that survives the outcome and must be declared. The framework returns this shape and no specific tenure length or removal rule; that value is a declared `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the rights effect and the accountability effect must be kept as separate slots in a union, and they are priced in two currencies that do not add.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."
- **Refuter:** show a commensurable measure of both effects that predicts outcomes without residual on either slot, or show that the two effects are a partition rather than a union with an overlap compound.

### C2 - adjudication-facing; checkable by a legal scholar or judge against the anchor and channel rules

- **Statement:** insulation protects rights while the judge stays an anchor ranging against law and facts, and becomes insulation from accountability when the ground channel decays.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".
- **What to check:** is the discriminator really the placement of insulation, or is duration or selection method doing all the work? Would a judge who defies a majority but whose reasons track evidence count, under this claim, as rights-protecting? Would a judge with a perfect record but no correcting channel count as insulated?
- **Refuter:** rights improving while the adjudicative frame demonstrably updated on majority preference with no separate correction against law and facts; or a fully intact ground channel where political correction cannot reach the seat and no rights loss or error correction follows.

### C3 - design-facing; checkable by an institutional designer against the guard, room and stake rules

- **Statement:** independence is a priced guard, electoral correction is selection between rooms, tenure is a declared standing, and the numeric design value is a `gap`.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :43 "Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 10 :61 "And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- **What to check:** does the framework anywhere supply a tenure length, removal threshold or electoral rule? If it does, item 7 of `07_the_tether_answer.md` is wrong. If a fixed rule can decide outcomes without being refusable and still be called a guard, Fig 2 :43 is being misread.
- **Refuter:** a figure line giving a specific design value; or a fixed insulation rule admitted as an outcome-deciding convention that cannot be refused, still classified as a guard.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named country's judiciary is independent or unaccountable. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that tenure length, a removal rule or an electoral system is prescribed.
- Not that rights and democratic legitimacy are commensurable or trade on one scale.
- Not that agreement between judges and the public settles either effect: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." and Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- Not that a clean provenance record makes a ruling correct: Fig 10 :65 "Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately."
- Not `grounded`. Status is `candidate`.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reads green but "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists". Here:

1. An instrument that distinguishes a ruling tracking law, facts and the person from a ruling tracking majority preference.
2. An instrument that measures whether review and reason-giving actually reduce error, not whether a review body exists.
3. A measurement of where insulation stops protecting the anchor and starts severing the channel, using an already-returning instrument such as the V-Dem high court independence index rather than a newly invented one.
