## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework reports "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And the same essay warns that a question may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one".

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be given a value. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- shape: insulation protects rights while it runs between the judge and political pressure, and insulates from accountability once it moves onto the judge's channel to the ground;
- not a shape: "judges should serve 12 years and be removable by a two-thirds vote." That is a value, and no figure line carries it.

The shape is a relation between named figure terms (anchor, channel, independence, threshold), so it is nameable. The tenure value is a missing primitive or instrument, so it is not. This is the distinction that keeps "shape not a name" from forbidding the derivation.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on steering: "the books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search." And on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here, the effect description is: name which relation the insulation is between, and say what you would observe if it were the rights-preserving relation (reasons track evidence and law; a majority's preference does not move the ruling) versus the accountability-insulating relation (reasons stop tracking evidence; correction channels return nothing).

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: "SETTLE - the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." An instrument that discriminates ground contact would name a specific rule's effect.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several paths can create the same behavior, so "behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

For this question the corresponding error is naming a cause from a single reading. A high independence index is a reading; it does not tell you whether the judge is anchored to law or disconnected from the public. A court that defies the majority is a reading; it does not tell you whether the deflection came from a ground or from the judge's own frame. Several sources produce the same reading, so the discriminator is missing. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* gives the structural statement directly: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." And the drift warning: "Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices." The document names three drift-stoppers: "A ground that both are corrected by, separately", "Separate experience", and "Claims in effect terms". It adds that "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice" and that "An experiment, an outcome, a measurement" is the reference point.

That is the whole judicial-independence question in the essay's terms. Independence is valuable exactly when it preserves the court's separate bearing on the fixed landmark. It becomes harmful when the court and its own constituency check only against each other, because the drift is then coherent and invisible. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :46: "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework return here?** A discriminating relation, not a constitutional design. It says:

- the two effects are slots in a union, never a single score (Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 9 :54);
- the rights slot is governed by the anchor rule: the judge ranges against law, facts and persons and does not update on political frames (Fig 2 :33, :45);
- the accountability slot is governed by channel maintenance: the ground does not decay, the channel does, and a guard that decides an outcome has stopped being a guard (Fig 10 :24, :47);
- accountability correction is selection between rooms, not negotiation with a sitting judge (Fig 7 :35);
- permanent tenure is standing that survives the outcome, a structural stake to be declared (Fig 10 :61);
- the value of a safe threshold is `gap`: no figure line supplies a duration, a removal rule or an electoral design (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :134).

**Does the framework commit the forbidden error?** No, provided the answer stays at the relation. Naming a specific rule as *the* answer would be a name where the framework returns a shape, and it would be a source named from a projection, which is the metamerism error.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of distinguished judges or prestigious journals assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle what they report on.
