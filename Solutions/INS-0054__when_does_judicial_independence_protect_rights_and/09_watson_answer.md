## QUESTION

When does judicial independence protect rights, and when can it insulate judges from democratic accountability? Restated as an effect: why does the same insulation setting `I` sometimes keep the adjudicative frame ranging against law, facts and the person (`E1`), and sometimes sever the channel by which correction reaches the seat (`E2`)? `R` is indexed per slot and never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). An answer would look like a discriminating quantity `D`, bound as an ordinary term before the run, stating which relation the insulation is between.

Symmetry: the insulation is directional (from the political frames) and the bond is an ordered threshold, not a symmetric join (Operators table :49). Scale: first order is a case, second order is the institutional rule and the review compound; a settled ruling becomes an atom at the next level (Fig 12 :104).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict, kept per domain and never averaged: judicial independence protects rights while it insulates the adjudicative anchor from political frames, so the judge ranges against law, facts and the person; it insulates from accountability when the same insulation moves onto the ground channel, so reasons stop tracking evidence and the correction channel goes quiet. The two effects are isomers over a shared atom set, priced in two currencies that do not add. Electoral correction is selection between rooms, not negotiation with a sitting judge; tenure is a declared standing; independence is a priced, refusable guard. The numeric design value is a `gap`. Status `specified`, `candidate`, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected reform. A court failing the channel condition would be given more tenure, and a court failing the anchor condition would be given more electoral correction. Each lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the compound standing (Fig 9). In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and locatable because the five refuters are written down.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn if the shape fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added, not because anything was deleted (Fig 6). The shape and its refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that distinguishes a ruling tracking law, facts and the person from one tracking majority preference; a measurement showing review actually reduces error; a demonstration that duration alone predicts outcomes with no dependence on which relation the insulation is between; a commensurable score for both effects; or a figure re-census supplying a specific design value. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
