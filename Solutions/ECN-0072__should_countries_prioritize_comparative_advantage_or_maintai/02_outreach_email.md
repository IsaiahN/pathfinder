# Outreach draft: check one shape claim before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the pre-registered refuter in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is intact and your own version of it is written down first. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One concrete claim about trade and strategic capacity, with its refuter attached

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a figure-grounded derivation of the question: should countries prioritize comparative advantage, or maintain domestic capacity in strategically important industries? I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question is not one trade-off but two conditions bundled into one sentence. One condition is allocation, where the anchor is a market price and relative opportunity cost. The other is continuity under a cut in supply, where the anchor has to be a named disruption with a cost. These have different anchors, so their verdicts cannot be averaged. "Strategic" is not a property an industry carries; it is a designation relative to a named cut, and the list changes when the cut scenario changes.
>
> Three further claims follow: (1) "maintain domestic capacity" is not removing dependence on the world, it is substituting a domestic habitat for a global one, so you must reproduce everything the goal required and you inherit domestic actors you did not choose; (2) a capability absent from the national library cannot be composed into existence by policy alone, it needs an import from somewhere whose knowledge base differs; (3) the measurable object is the supplier bond map, not the list of domestic industries, because the same industries arranged differently behave differently under the same cut.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that free trade is always right, that autarky is ever right, that any particular industry is strategic, or that my side has won. I have not measured any country. This is a shape and a route, not a result.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this run:**
> 1. show that cheapness and supply exposure are mutually exclusive for every industry, so one scalar ranking is well defined;
> 2. name an industry that is strategic under every conceivable disruption and every change of circumstances;
> 3. show a case where external dependence was removed rather than substituted, with no unchosen domestic actors inherited, nothing required left unreproduced, and no continuing maintenance needed;
> 4. show national capability rising while the relevant firms' pattern of contact with their supplier habitat stayed unchanged;
> 5. show two countries with identical industry lists and identical supplier topologies behaving decisively differently under the same cut.
>
> If any one holds, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. What would you count as a failure? If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight-step loop: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - WTO World Trade Report 2023, chapter C on security and trade: https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/booksp_e/wtr23_e/wtr23_ch3_e.pdf
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a trade question you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter before conclusion | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` mode rule: general / specified / grounded, state which. |
| No "which side wins" framing | Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." |
| "Strategic" is scenario-relative | Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." |
| Habitat substitution, not exclusion | Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." |
| Independence priced by cost of being wrong | Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." |
| No request for trust or prestige | Prestige can fund an action and cannot settle it; only the ground settles it. |
| Links only after a verdict is recorded | Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |

## Two-stage offer, explicit

- **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim, the absence of a measurement, and the five refuters. Ask what they would count as a failure. **Record their verdict before showing any framework material.**
- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the four links, with the note that the figures, not the email, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because by then they have seen the interior (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Nothing is sent by this agent. One to one only, no bulk send, opt-out line in every message.
