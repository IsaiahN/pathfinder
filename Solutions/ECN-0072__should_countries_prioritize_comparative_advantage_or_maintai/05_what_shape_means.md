## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 records that a system with a closure "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands" and "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: *the problem is a union of an allocation condition and a continuity condition; the second has an anchor only when a cut is named; the capacity that survives is a substituted habitat, measured on a bond map, with independence bought against the cost of being wrong.* That is a shape. "Country X should do Y" is a name, and the framework does not supply it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, fetched: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Start with the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual was stated in effect terms: not "efficiency versus security" but "where capacity goes and what survives a cut".
- *Library of Babel*, fetched, on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."

## 3. The bundle diagnosis is already in the essay

This is the most directly relevant sentence in the eight essays for ECN-0072. *The Library of Babel*, fetched: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

That is the same instruction as Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again", reached from a different starting point (search practice rather than closure theory). Two low-overlap routes to the same instruction constitute weak triangulation inside one evidence pool, not two independent witnesses. The essay is by the framework's author; overlap is high at the author level and low at the route level. Both facts are recorded.

The essay continues: "You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." That is exactly the per-domain verdict structure of `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`, and the refusal to average the two verdicts.

## 4. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a supplier graph plus a named cut with a measured cost, or an actual disruption with observed continuity.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The ecology, finance, engineering and WTO sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are such imports. They characterize the shape; they do not pick a country's policy.

The name that is refused is *"strategic industry"* as an intrinsic category. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Naming an industry strategic without a cut scenario is a name where the framework returns a shape relative to a residual.

## 5. Why the scalar "national interest" score is the forbidden move

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Also: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A single weighted score that trades efficiency against security is a convention, not a ground. It has no instrument that can be violated; any result can be produced by reweighting. Fig 1 :27: "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." The score is a quantity the policy frame produces, so it cannot grade the policy frame. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the continuity claim a budget to be tried against a named cut, and let the ground, not the score, settle it.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched: "You may all agree and be wrong." A room of trade-policy experts agreeing on a weighting is the named failure, not corroboration (Fig 7 :35).

## 6. Correction to my own earlier framing

In section 4 above I wrote that the framework "does not supply" a name. That needs narrowing, in the same way the exemplar narrowed its own rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Market prices, supplier graphs and industrial habitats are not missing primitives; they are ordinary terms already in the policy frame. What the framework refuses is not talking about them. It refuses *EITHER* declaring one of the two domains the whole question, *OR* treating a scenario-relative designation as a property carried by an industry. Both claims are now recorded; neither is quietly lost. This correction does not change the shape in section 1.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any policy and cannot settle the question it reports on. Its mode is `specified`, never `grounded`, and the pre-registered refuter in `01` section 4 is the only mechanism that can make it withdraw a claim.
