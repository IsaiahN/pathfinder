## The residual in effect terms

A policy maker must commit scarce national resources, capital, labour, skills and supplier relationships, under two different descriptions of what those resources are for. One description allocates them by relative opportunity cost and buys the rest from the world. The other retains a domestic productive base in industries labelled strategic, at a cost above the market price. The question asks which commitment leaves the country better able to meet its ground. The derivation below finds that these are not two answers to one question. They are two domains with two different anchors that have been bundled into one sentence. The honest output is an unbundling and a per-domain verdict, not a single scalar ranking.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Before the run, the sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: an answer would (a) name the anchor that settles each half of the question, (b) say which half is a partition with a ground and which half is a union compound needing unbundling, (c) give a per-domain verdict without averaging them, and (d) state a refuter that would make the answer withdrawn. This is the `Direction` discipline from `THE_FORMULA.md`: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question unless it writes the sought-for shape down first.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Only institutional, public, opt-in or role-based venues in `03`.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan. One-to-one contact only.
- No claim to have measured any country, industry, supply chain or welfare effect. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for people who already prefer one answer manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
3. **Sending the whole frame before asking for a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the architecture.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.
