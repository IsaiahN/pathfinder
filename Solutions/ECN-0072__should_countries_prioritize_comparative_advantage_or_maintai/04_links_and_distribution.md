## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The institutional source agrees with the unbundling, not with a side.** The WTO World Trade Report 2023 says that when the source of future shocks is unknown, the safest strategy is many suppliers across the world, and that cutting partner count to reduce geopolitical exposure raises exposure to natural disasters. That is a two-condition compound in the field's own vocabulary, and it matches Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". It corroborates the shape, not the framework's authority.
2. **Efficiency and resilience pull in opposite directions in ecology.** The ecological resilience article states that specialisation, required for efficiency, weakens resilience by creating dependence on prevailing conditions. This is a low-overlap corroboration of the filter/witness distinction (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict"). It is a corroboration from a shared structural pattern, not an independent proof of the figure line.
3. **Independence is the load-bearing quantity in finance and engineering.** The finance article reports that correlations rise in market stress, so diversification benefits shrink exactly when needed. The engineering article says the redundancy arithmetic assumes independent failure events and fails for two supplies on the same socket. Both corroborate Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" and Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong". Agreement about shared failure sources is not independence.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the unbundling and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded first.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** A mass send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not evidence.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** The formula is a generator. Giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a refuter of their own.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim, its mode and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the email summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, explicit

- **Stage 1:** residual, shape, mode, refuter. No framework material. This is the invoice that must be paid before anything else is sent.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the four world links plus the canonical figure links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

## Practical constraints

Nothing is uploaded or sent by this agent. Hosting a packet and sending the link is a human action. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list. Personal emails of named individuals are deliberately absent from `03_public_contact_points.csv`; only role-based institutional inboxes and public submission portals appear there. One to one contact only, no automation, no bulk send, opt-out line in every message.
