## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The residual is real but frame-dependent.** DESI DR1 found a preference for w0 > -1, wa < 0 in combinations with CMB and supernovae, yet DESI BAO alone are consistent with flat ΛCDM and constant w alone is consistent with ΛCDM. DR2 reports the preference growing to 2.8 to 4.2 sigma depending on the supernova sample, and simultaneously reports a 2.3 sigma tension between BAO and CMB parameters. That is a per-epoch residual with a frame dependency, not a settled refutation (Fig 5 :59).
2. **The prior is part of the frame.** Cortes and Liddle locate the signal in the derivative of w rather than its mean offset from -1 and argue the result is strongly prior-dependent. This is world-side corroboration for Fig 9 :60: the sigma level budgets effort, it does not witness the verdict.
3. **Independence is scarce.** DESI constrains using Planck and ACT CMB plus three overlapping supernova compilations. Agreement among these combinations is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). The model-agnostic reconstructions that find less significant evidence use the same public data, so they are a re-analysis of the pool, not a second witness.
4. **The name is underdetermined.** Wolf and Ferreira argue that precision surveys will at best give a phenomenological description of dark-energy microphysics. This corroborates the `gap` in `01` F7 and Fig 9 :32.
5. **The symmetry framing is a structure, not an answer.** Baez's statement of Noether's theorem connects time-translation symmetry to energy conservation. It licenses saying that "constant versus evolving" is a symmetry distinction, but it does not say which holds in the cosmos. It is a low-overlap mathematical witness for the shape, not for the verdict.
6. **Honest search limit.** Searches in human cognition and animal behaviour for a low-overlap source that bears on this residual returned nothing usable. That is a recorded search limit, not a proof of absence: Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
