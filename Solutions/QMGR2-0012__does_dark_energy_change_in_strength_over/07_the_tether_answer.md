# The Tether's answer on dark energy changing strength

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The cosmological library Γ predicts an expansion history. The observations return one. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is per redshift epoch, not a global average (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :28). The question asks whether the term holding the "strength" of dark energy should be kept as constant or refused in favour of a time-varying one.

It is a union compound, not one hard question: constant-term adequacy, time-channel resolution and source availability overlap, and a single lever does not clear the intersections (Fig 9 :55, :57).

## 2. The shape

1. **Read the residual per epoch.** A single exclusion significance is a budget filter. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
2. **Refuse before mint.** The held term is Λ with w = -1. Formula step 2. It becomes REFUTED only if its residual persists and does not trend down under changes of frame (Fig 5 :59). At this budget the residual is real in some frames and shrinks in others, so Λ remains `candidate`.
3. **The frames are not independent.** DESI constraints use Planck and ACT CMB and overlapping supernova compilations. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Agreement among these combinations is one witness.
4. **Constant versus evolving is a bond-to-Time distinction.** Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Both compositions are legal cross-kind compositions (Fig 13 :74, :115), so the framework cannot select one. That is why this is a ground question.
5. **The time channel can fail on its own.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. At high redshift current data are admittedly insufficient; an instrument must be improved from one already returning something (Fig 6 :32, :34).
6. **The microphysical name is a `gap`.** Quintessence, phantom energy, coupled black holes and modified gravity are imports from differing closures and need a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23). Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "dark energy evolves". Not "dark energy is constant". Both options are legal and the ground has not settled the case.
- Not that the measured sigma level is a discovery. It is a filter for effort.
- Not that DESI, Planck and the supernova teams are independent confirmations of one another. They share data.
- Not that quintessence, phantom crossing or coupled black holes is the source. Those are names, and the framework returns a shape.
- Not that a better instrument must exist. Fig 6 :34 requires one already returning something before an instrument can be improved.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles left(R,phi).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not another fit to the same pool:

- **Per-epoch discriminator:** two or more analyses with disjoint instruments and disjoint supernova samples returning the same per-epoch w(z) shape. That would be triangulation rather than one pool (Fig 2 :35).
- **Persistence test:** the evolution residual does not trend down under defensible changes of prior and dataset. That would license REFUTED and a mint (Fig 5 :59).
- **Channel test:** a high-z probe already returning something that resolves or removes the low-z variation (Fig 6 :34).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** flat per-epoch residuals with a large global statistic; the residual trending down under prior changes in independent frames; one of the two compositions shown illegal under the six-term set; a single lever clearing all three union conditions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The world literature gives a real residual and a real prior dependence, and it gives admitted insensitivity at high redshift. The framework stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* refers to a declared gap in the closure, not to this open ground question. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say whether dark energy changes. It says what a checkable answer would have to look like. The residual must be read epoch by epoch, not collapsed into one sigma number, because a global statistic budgets effort rather than deciding the case. The cosmological constant is a held term and stays a candidate until its residual is shown to persist and not trend down when the analysis frame changes. Today's preference for evolution is real but moves with priors and with which supernova and BAO datasets are combined, and the frames that report it share an evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness. Structurally, constant and evolving dark energy differ by whether a gradient is bonded to Time; both are legal, so the framework cannot choose between them and does not name quintessence, phantom energy or modified gravity as the winner. What would settle it is a discriminator built on an instrument already returning per-epoch data, plus a residual that survives independent frames. Until then the answer is a shape, a set of refuters written in advance, and an honest stop.
