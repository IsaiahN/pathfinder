## 0. The residual in effect terms

The library Γ holds a cosmological model. Its prediction Γ(b,a) is an expansion history: a distance-redshift relation and a growth rate. The observations o' are BAO distance ratios, CMB angular scales and supernova luminosity distances. The residual R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is the gap between them.

The question "does dark energy change in strength" asks a routing question about one term in Γ. The term currently held is Λ, with w = -1 and constant density. Two effects are possible:

- The gap is HELD or MIS-ATTACHED (Fig 5 :11, :13). Nothing is owed. The apparent preference for evolution is a property of the priors or of a particular dataset combination, and the correct move is to re-fit, not to mint.
- The gap is REFUTED (Fig 5 :53): *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. The constant term is refused and a new term is owed. The candidate terms are ENUMERABLE: wCDM, w0waCDM, thawing quintessence, emerging dark energy, mirage models, coupled black holes, modified gravity. They rank by the residual that persists and does not trend down (Fig 5 :59).

The sought-for shape, written down and bound before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a time-varying w whose variation survives changes of prior and frame, plus a discriminator that separates it from a constant term plus dataset tension. The shape is made an ordinary term so that it can later be refused by the same test as everything else.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The residual is per epoch, not a global average

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :28 *"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."*

DESI DR1 measures distances in seven redshift bins over z = 0.1 to 4.2, and Cortês and Liddle locate the signal near a pivot epoch, with the strongest deviation at z about 0.51. That is exactly a per-slot phenomenon. A single "is Lambda excluded at N sigma" number is a budget filter, not the residual itself. The figure requires the residual to be reported per epoch.

**Refuter:** a reconstruction in which the per-epoch residuals are flat at zero while a global significance statistic is large, with no single bin carrying the signal. That would show the slot indexing was unnecessary here.

### F2 - The routing is held versus refuted, and the test is persistence

Fig 5 :14 *"mechanism broken: a new term is owed"*. Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 5 :59 *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*.

The held term is Λ. Formula step 2 refuses before it mints: the refusal of "constant strength" must be earned first. The world evidence shows a real residual that has grown from DR1 to DR2, but also shows that its size depends on priors and on which supernova compilation and BAO dataset are combined. The figure's test is therefore exactly the right one: does the residual persist and not trend down when the frame is changed? At this budget the answer is "it persists in some frames, shrinks in others", so the refusal is not yet licensed. Λ remains `candidate`.

**Refuter:** show the w0-wa residual trending down toward zero under a sequence of defensible prior changes in at least two frames that do not share a dataset pool. Then the gap was mis-attached (Fig 5 :13) and no new term is owed.

### F3 - The anchor is the measurement, not the agreement of analyses

Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

DESI DR2, Planck, Pantheon+, Union3 and DES-SN5YR do not form independent witnesses for the conclusion. DESI's constraint uses Planck CMB and ACT lensing; the supernova compilations overlap in their light curves; Cortês and Liddle reuse the DESI chains. Agreement among these is one evidence pool talking. The anchor is the measured distance-redshift relation, and each frame must range against it separately.

**Refuter:** two analyses with disjoint instruments, disjoint supernova samples and different reconstruction methods yielding the same per-epoch w(z) shape. That would be triangulation, not a pool.

### F4 - Significance levels are filters, not witnesses

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 12 :86 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*.

The reported significances (2.5, 3.5, 3.9 sigma in DR1; 2.8 to 4.2 sigma in DR2) are likelihood-ratio thresholds. They decide which analysis gets more observing time. They do not decide whether the strength of dark energy changes. "Stronger" is a comparison bond between two epoch values, and a comparison does not become a verdict because its number is large. Wolf and Ferreira's underdetermination result is the world-side corroboration: even with precision surveys, at best a phenomenological description is available.

**Refuter:** a direct, source-discriminating measurement of rho_DE(z) at two epochs whose difference is resolvable without a likelihood-ratio scan over a parametrization. That would be a witness.

### F5 - The time channel can fail independently of the ground

Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

"Over time" is a contact question across epochs. Where the channel returns something (z below about 1), we can ask whether it resolves a change. At higher redshift the current data are admittedly insufficient to constrain evolution. There is no edge to extend from there: one cannot specify a high-z instrument from an account of evolving dark energy, only improve an instrument already returning something, such as the Ly-alpha forest measurement that DESI DR2 reports at z above 2.

**Refuter:** a resolved per-epoch change at high redshift from an instrument that was not built by sharpening an existing reading, together with a demonstration that the earlier apparent change was a channel artefact. Then the channel diagnosis is wrong.

### F6 - Constant versus evolving is a bond-to-Time question, and both are legal

Fig 13 :76 *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."*

Read against the six-term substrate, a constant Λ is energy with no gradient bonded to Time. An evolving component is a gradient bonded to Time, which Fig 13 explicitly licenses as a different term. Both are cross-kind compositions, so both are legal. The framework can state the difference in structure, but it cannot select one: Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* This is why the answer is a ground question, not a theorem.

**Refuter:** show that one of the two compositions is not legal under the six-term set, for example that a gradient cannot bond to Time. That would let the framework decide the question a priori and would break this shape claim.

### F7 - The microphysical name is a gap, not a hidden answer

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 13 :141 *"what would have had to be true for the answer to differ"*. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Quintessence, phantom energy, coupled black holes and modified gravity are candidate imports. Each would have to pass a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (it explains the per-epoch gap), and each is debited against the independence of the frame that supplies it. Under a genuine re-census no figure line governs which of them is correct. This is declared `gap`, not filled by the most popular review. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Refuter:** a provenanced import from a differing closure that predicts the observed per-epoch residual shape before it is fitted, and is then settled by a ground measurement. Until one exists, any name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

### F8 - The question is a union compound

Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The conditions are: constant-term adequacy (D2), channel resolution (D4) and source availability (D3). A single lever, for example more supernovae, addresses D2 only. Cortês and Liddle show that changing the prior addresses D2 differently; the high-z limitations noted in the model-agnostic reconstruction literature address D4; the microphysical underdetermination result addresses D3. One lever cannot clear the intersections.

**Refuter:** a single intervention that removes the per-epoch residual in all three conditions and leaves no categorically different residue behind.

### F9 - A strain: Fig 13 :64 does NOT decide this question

Fig 13 :64 reads *"no time-translation symmetry, and no finite operations to leave a remainder"*. It is tempting to use this to conclude that dark energy must change with time. That is a strain. In context the line is in the "REMOVE time" row of the substrate table: it says what is lost from established thermodynamics if the substrate member Time is removed (first and third laws). It governs the derivation of laws, not the fitted constancy of a parameter.

Re-census: the line governs a different quantity, so it is sent to the strain log and not used as a verdict, exactly as the canonical usage rule requires (a reading that makes figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain). After re-census, F6 carries the structural claim instead.

**Refuter:** a derivation showing that w(z) constancy follows from the presence or absence of Time as a substrate member. Until then, using Fig 13 :64 here would be apophenia.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim (per-epoch residual, REFUTED before MINT, union compound, gap on the name) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The per-epoch residuals are flat while a global statistic carries the entire signal, with no slot showing positive mass (breaks F1 and Fig 1 :19).
2. The apparent evolution residual trends down to zero under defensible prior changes in independent frames, showing it was mis-attached rather than refuted (breaks F2 and Fig 5 :59).
3. Two analyses with disjoint instruments and disjoint data show the same per-epoch w(z) shape, proving genuine triangulation rather than one pool (breaks F3; this would strengthen the world verdict, not break the figure shape, so it is recorded as a strengthening trigger).
4. One of the two compositions, constant or evolving, is shown not to be legal under the six-term set (breaks F6).
5. A single lever removes the residual in all three union conditions and leaves no categorically different residue (breaks F8).

If 1, 2, 4 or 5 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
