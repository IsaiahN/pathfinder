# Expert verification packet - the Tether on dark energy changing strength

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement at the relevant epochs is claimed to settle this. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The residual for dark energy is indexed by redshift epoch, not by a single global exclusion significance. The cosmological constant with w = -1 is a held term and remains a `candidate` until its residual is shown to persist and not trend down under changes of prior and dataset. The reported preference for w0 > -1 and wa < 0 is a real per-epoch signal, but its magnitude is frame-dependent and the frames share an evidence pool, so it has not yet licensed a refusal. Structurally, a constant term and an evolving term differ by whether a gradient is bonded to Time, and both compositions are legal under the six-term substrate; the framework therefore gives the shape of the distinction, not its truth value, and does not name a microphysical source. The union of constant-term adequacy, channel resolution and source availability must be split before it is searched.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - observation-facing; checkable against the published per-epoch constraints

- **Statement:** the signal lives in particular redshift epochs and in particular dataset combinations, not in a single uniform offset. A global sigma is a filter for effort, not the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **World anchor to check:** DESI DR1 reports seven BAO bins over z = 0.1 to 4.2, DESI BAO alone consistent with flat LCDM, and a preference for evolution only in combinations with CMB and supernovae. DR2 reports 2.8 to 4.2 sigma depending on the supernova sample. Check whether any single bin or pivot epoch carries the signal.
- **Refuter:** per-epoch residuals flat at zero while a global statistic carries the whole signal, with no slot showing positive mass.

### C2 - frame-facing; checkable against the prior and dataset analyses

- **Statement:** the constant term is not yet REFUTED because the evolution residual has not been shown to persist across frames that do not share an evidence pool.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :59 *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"*. Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
- **World anchor to check:** Cortes and Liddle (JCAP 12, 2024) find the evidence concentrated in the derivative of w and argue it is strongly prior-dependent; model-agnostic reconstructions find weaker evidence than the parametric w0wa fit; Wolf and Ferreira (PRD 108, 103519, 2023) argue that at best a phenomenological description is available.
- **Refuter:** the residual trends down to zero under defensible prior changes in two independent frames; or two analyses with disjoint instruments and disjoint data yield the same per-epoch w(z) shape (the latter strengthens the world verdict but does not break the figure shape; record it as such).

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** constant and evolving dark energy are a bond-to-Time distinction over the same substrate, both legal, hence not decidable by the framework alone. Over the full shared atom set they satisfy the isomer definition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :76 *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 13 :115 *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** are both compositions cross-kind and legal under Fig 13 :74? If yes, the framework cannot choose between them, and any claim that it does is a category error.
- **Refuter:** show that one of the two compositions is not legal under the six-term set, or that the two models require different substrate atoms rather than different bonds.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that dark energy evolves, and not that it is constant.
- Not a discovery at any sigma level. Significances are filters for the budget.
- Not that DESI, Planck, Pantheon+, Union3 and DES-SN5YR are independent witnesses. They share data and chains.
- Not that quintessence, phantom energy, coupled black holes or modified gravity is the answer. Those are imports with no source gate in this packet.
- Not that a source-discriminating instrument must exist. Fig 6 :34 requires an instrument already returning something.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final frame. The microphysical name is a declared `gap`.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Per-epoch discriminator:** disjoint instruments and disjoint data returning the same per-epoch w(z) shape.
2. **Persistence test:** residual not trending down across defensible frame changes, licensing REFUTED and a mint.
3. **Channel test:** a high-z probe already returning something that resolves or removes the low-z variation.
4. **Union test:** one lever clearing all three conditions and leaving no categorically different residue.
