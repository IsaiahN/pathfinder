## The question restated as an effect

"Strength" is not a literary term here. In the model library Γ it is the dark-energy equation-of-state parameter w(z) = p/(rho c^2), equivalently the evolution of the density rho_DE(z). The cosmological constant Λ holds w = -1 and a constant density. An evolving term holds w = w(z) and a density that changes with expansion.

R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is the gap between the expansion history the library predicts and what the observations return. The residual is indexed by redshift epoch, not averaged into a single global number (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22, Fig 1 :28).

- Option A, "the strength is constant": the Λ term is held and the residual is HELD or MIS-ATTACHED. Nothing is owed. Leading consequence: no new term, and the prior/dataset dependence of the apparent signal is a property of the analysis frames.
- Option B, "the strength changes": the Λ term is REFUTED (the fifth kind, Fig 5 :53) because its residual persists and does not trend down under changes of frame (Fig 5 :59). A new term is owed. Leading consequence: an import from a microphysical closure, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :53).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term BEFORE the run (Formula, "Direction"): a *shape*, namely a time-dependent term in w whose variation survives changes of prior, dataset and parametrization, together with an instrument that discriminates that variation from a constant term plus dataset tension. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check inside the loop can see it. This is why the pre-registered refuter in `01` is written first.

The verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` is neither a bare Yes nor a bare No. From the figures the question is a union compound, the constant term is currently `candidate` rather than `refused`, and which microphysical name would fill an evolving slot is a declared `gap`. Kept per domain, never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the cosmology question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of cosmic acceleration.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. Significance thresholds are filters for the budget, not witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60). A list selected for receptiveness or for agreement with a sigma level is a filter handing you a verdict.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and can get the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
