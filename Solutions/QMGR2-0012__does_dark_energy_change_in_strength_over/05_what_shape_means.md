## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question:

- A **shape** is the constraint that a time-dependent w must survive changes of prior and frame, and that the residual must be read per epoch.
- A **name** is quintessence, phantom energy, coupled black holes or modified gravity.

The first is checkable before the ground settles anything. The second is an import and carries zero evidentiary weight until it has provenance and a shadow test.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* In effect terms, the question is not "is there a scalar field" but "does the per-epoch residual persist under frame changes".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` therefore sends an expectation (flat per-epoch residuals would kill the claim), not a theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A source-discriminating instrument would have to show that the per-epoch variation is a feature of the cosmos rather than of the prior and the dataset combination.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :53). Wolf and Ferreira's underdetermination result is the world-side warning that precision fitting alone does not deliver this (see `09`).

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The same distance-redshift reading can be produced by a constant term plus dataset tension, or by an evolving term plus a compatible prior. The reading does not determine the source. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the analogue is not a claim that a better instrument definitely exists. It is that a sigma level from a likelihood-ratio comparison is a projection, and that the thing which would resolve the source is an instrument, not a further argument. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 4. The union is part of the shape

*Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Our union has three conditions: constant-term adequacy, channel resolution and source availability. The shape must say which condition each observation bears on. A single global exclusion significance does not, which is why F1 in `01` insists on per-epoch residuals.

## 5. Q: what licenses "the strength changes"? ANSWERED at shape level

Nothing in the framework licenses the claim that it does. The framework licenses only this: if there is a change, it is a gradient bonded to Time (Fig 13 :76), and both the bonded and unbonded compositions are legal (Fig 13 :74, :115). The distinction is therefore a ground question and not a theorem. The world literature supplies a residual in some frames and a substantial prior dependence in others, which is exactly why the honest label is `candidate`, not `refused`. Any name for the source is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this field the prestige proxy is the famous collaboration or the large sigma number. Either can buy observing time. Neither can settle whether w varies. A packet that leads with "4.2 sigma from DESI" hands the reader a filter as if it were a witness (Fig 9 :60).

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the cosmology claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The canonical README closes the same point: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
