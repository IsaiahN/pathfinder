**Residual in effect terms.** Epidemic spread is the effect to be explained (incidence curve, peak height, persistence, geographic arrival time). Four named conditions are offered as its causes. The word "jointly" is the whole question: does each condition contribute a separate additive slice, or do the four overlap into intersections that are categorically different from any one condition? The derivation decides the shape of that composition and records which coefficients are owed from measurement.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term.** A candidate answer is: the four conditions form a union compound whose per-slot residuals must be kept separate, whose habitat must be enumerated out to second-order contact cascades, and whose information-to-behavior-to-contact link is a cycle that can close. Binding this shape as a term lets it accrue a residual like anything else (formula step "Direction"). The pre-registered refuter is written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3, before the conclusion.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room of agreeing readers does not add a ground. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies their agreement. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
3. Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
