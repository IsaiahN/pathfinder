# The Tether's answer: how four conditions jointly shape epidemic spread

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the composition rule, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles four conditions into one "jointly." The residual is the gap between what a model using those conditions predicts and what the ground returns (case data, serology, arrival times). The first task is to decide whether the four form a partition (additive contributions) or a union compound (overlapping conditions whose intersections are categorically different). Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The figure-derived verdict is **union compound**.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before you search.** Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Mobility x household, information x contact, and institution x both are specific compounds with their own loops and their own grounds. A single lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.
2. **Keep the residual per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Report residuals per condition in context. One pooled score hides the live slot and is how "a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22).
3. **Enumerate the habitat to second order.** Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Online information is the second-order actor: virtual contact changes behavior, behavior changes physical contact, contact changes infection. Fig 11 :52 names the first-order failure: "contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles". Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so mobility and information can damp or amplify depending on the case.
4. **The information link is a cycle.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Cases generate information, which generates behavior and contact, which generate cases. A one-way chain cannot represent this.
5. **The conditions are arrangements, not ingredients.** Over a shared atom set they are Fig 12 isomers: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Split into disjoint blocks they are a functional group (Fig 12 :124). `06_isomers_and_order.md` carries the falsifier.
6. **The ground is measured outcomes.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Mobility, household, information, and institutional models are frames ranging against one ground; they do not settle anything by agreeing. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
7. **The figures return the shape, not the coefficients.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." No reproduction number or interaction coefficient is derivable from the figures. Those are owed from measurement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not one additive "joint effect" number. That is the blend collapse Fig 8 names.
- Not that any one of the four is *the* cause. Attribution requires a source-discriminating instrument, not a curve.
- Not a coefficient, a reproduction ratio, or a ranked importance list. Filters are for the budget, never the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that online connections are a physical contact layer. They act through behavior and couple to physical contact (§2.4).
- Not a physical six-term mapping. Only the network structure and cross-kind composition rule generalize; the energy/spectrum mapping was retracted as strain (audit §1).
- Not grounded. No measurement settles the coefficients here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading "cannot comment on the source," and "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." Here the discriminators are:

- **Interaction terms:** do measured outcomes show non-additive cross-terms between mobility and household, or between information and contact? (R1)
- **Per-slot check:** does a per-slot model beat one pooled score on held-out data? (R2)
- **Feedback test:** do rising cases measurably change information and behavior, closing the cycle? (R3)
- **Mixing test:** are within-condition contacts clustered, with a measurable second-order information effect? (R4)
- **Source instruments:** stratified contact surveys, mobility traces, serology by subgroup, and timing of policy and information events, so a curve can be attributed to a combination rather than named from a projection.

The standing falsifiers R1 to R6 are written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 and were recorded before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The coefficients are unreachable at this budget and owed from a ground reading. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." A simulated derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not add four factors into one "joint effect." It says the four conditions form a union compound: their overlaps are different kinds of case, so the answer must keep a separate residual for each one. It says the habitat must be enumerated out to the second-order cascade where online information changes behavior, behavior changes contact, and cases feed back into information, which makes the link a cycle and not a one-way chain. Writing the four conditions over one shared set of persons, places, contacts, information and rules makes them isomers, same atoms arranged differently, with different behaviour. The ground for all of this is measured outcomes, not agreement between models, and the observed curve cannot name its own source. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have and the refuters written in advance, not a reproduction number. The coefficients stay open until a discriminating instrument settles them.
