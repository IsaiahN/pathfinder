## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For CMP-0061 the shape is: a union compound, per-slot residual, enumerated habitat with second-order cascades, a cyclic information link, and coefficients owed from measurement. A specific R0 value or a ranked list of "the most important factor" is a name and a number, not a shape. The shape constrains which answers are admissible; it does not fill the slot.

The same essay gives the precise failure mode for a bundled question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly what "jointly" risks being here. Fig 9 :55 says the same thing in framework terms: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with." Each new term "narrow[s] the space by definition and deepen[s] your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- The residual is described in effect terms before a frame is chosen (Fig 9 :27-28). For this question that means describing the effect (which outcome fails to be predicted, in which subgroup and place) before deciding whether "mobility" or "institutions" is the relevant frame.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." The stop is recorded as a lineage, not hidden.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For CMP-0061 the ground is case data, serology, contact surveys, and arrival times.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and shadow test. The multiplex and coupled-behavior models in `10_source_ledger.csv` are such imports.

So a coefficient like "mobility reduces peak by X percent" is licensed only by a measurement, and a claim like "the information layer is the dominant driver" is licensed only by a source-discriminating instrument. Until then, naming one of the four conditions as *the* cause is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Why the observed curve cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Apply this to an epidemic curve. A single peak can be produced by high mobility with strong household mixing, by weak institutions with a slow information response, or by dense online connectivity that failed to change behavior. The curve is the green reading. It cannot comment on which combination produced it. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: stratified contact data, mobility traces, serology by subgroup, and timing of policy and information events. This is a direct, non-analogical application of the essay's rule.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

## 5. The specific error this question invites

The invited error is to read "jointly" as "add the four contributions and report one number." By Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated," and by the curve/source argument in §4, an aggregate both hides the live slot and pretends to name a source from a projection. The correct form keeps the slots, enumerates the habitat, and records which discriminator would be needed to attribute the curve.

A second invited error is treating the four conditions as four states that bond directly. By Fig 13 :75, "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." The generalizable point is that a structure must mediate: here a contact network and an information network mediate between the conditions and the outcome. No new physical primitive is imported.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let an instrument settle it.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on.
