# Outreach draft: check the shape claim before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until a ground-visible checker has stated what they would count as a failure of stage 1. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the shape claim is presented as a conclusion rather than a falsifiable prediction.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about how four conditions combine in epidemic spread

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow, checkable claim and I would like your verdict on it before I show you any theory. I am not asking you to agree with it, and I am not asking you to trust a framework. If the claim fails, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "how do mobility, household structure, institutions, and online connections jointly shape spread" is a **union compound**, not four additive contributions. The four conditions overlap, so the intersections, such as mobility inside households or online information changing physical contact, are different kinds of case, not harder versions of one case. Three consequences follow:
>
> 1. The residual must be reported **per slot** (per condition in context), not as one averaged score. A single pooled number hides the live slot.
> 2. The habitat must be enumerated out to **second-order cascades**. Online information changes behavior, behavior changes contact, contact changes infection, and cases then feed back into information. That is a cycle, not a one-way chain.
> 3. Attributing one observed epidemic curve to a single condition is a **source error**. Several different mixtures of the four conditions can produce the same curve, so the curve alone cannot name its cause.
>
> This is a claim about the **shape** any answer must have. It does not supply a reproduction number or a set of coefficients. Those are owed from measurement, and I am not claiming them here.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I wrote to you:**
> 1. the four effects are separable and additive on a measured outcome, with no interaction terms and no cross-cascade;
> 2. one scalar summary predicts held-out outcomes as well as per-slot residuals, with no live slot hidden by the average;
> 3. the information effect is demonstrably one-way, with no measurable feedback from cases to behavior;
> 4. within-condition contacts are random-mixed and no second-order information effect is measurable.
>
> If any of these holds, I will withdraw the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Local figure text extract (verbatim, with line numbers): available in the Tether framework folder, cited as Fig N :line.
> - Full architecture (19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
> - Worked narrative and the six-term derivation: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The framework's own rule is that a loop cannot check the direction of its own question. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Stage discipline

- **Stage 1:** the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the reader would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory.
- **Stage 2 (opt-in):** if they are curious, offer the three links. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

No stage asks for trust, belief, or endorsement.
