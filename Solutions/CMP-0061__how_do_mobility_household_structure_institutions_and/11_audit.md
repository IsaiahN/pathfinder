- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a model's prediction using four conditions and the measured epidemic outcome. Arity: four conditions, so the residual is four-dimensional, never a scalar. Symmetry: the conditions are not interchangeable; mobility is spatial, household is small-group, institutions are rule-based, online is informational. Scale: between-place, within-place, meso/macro, and overlaid information layer. This is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, physical habitat.** Actors: persons, households, schools, workplaces, transport nodes, pathogen. Currency: contacts and infections. Anchor: case data, serology, arrival times. Question type: union of overlapping conditions.

- **Domain 3, institutional / policy.** Actors: institutions, regulators, policies. Currency: rules, closures, mandates. Anchor: the dated decision plus its measured effect. Question type: modifier of Domains 1 and 2.
- **Order by dependency.** Slot-wise perception, habitat enumeration, ground pinning, coupling identification, coefficient fitting. Refuse the one-number reading before any coefficient; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).
