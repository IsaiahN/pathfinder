## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that societies have decided who is qualified to produce or interpret knowledge by granting labels such as degrees, titles and reputations. Option A: treat the question as asking for a roster or a universal criterion, and answer with a list of qualities or a historical periodization. Option B: treat it as a union of three conditions with different anchors, return the shape that any adequate qualification decision must have, and record the roster as a `gap`. What each leads to: A scores a frame with a quantity the frame produces (Fig 1 :26-27) and manufactures the disqualifying correlation by selecting for agreement (Fig 2 :40); B sends a checkable shape, pre-registered refuters, and an open roster until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a credential predicts (this person's claims bear on the world) and what the ground returns. Arity: three terms, a claimant, an issuing institution and a checking frame. Symmetry: the ordinary label reads as symmetric (qualified people are peers), while the underlying relation is ordered (frames range against the ground, the ground does not range against frames, Fig 2 :45). Scale: individual knower, institution, whole society. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Verdicts are per slot, never a societal average.
- **Domain 1, the knower in the habitat.** Actors: practitioners, elders, witnesses, apprentices, local knowers. Currency: claims and whether the world keeps them. Anchor: the ground. Unmaintained mode, so qualification is a post-hoc label.
- **Domain 2, the credentialing institution.** Actors: academies, universities, licensing boards, editors, reviewers. Currency: conventions, credentials, standing. Anchor: ought to be the ground; in practice frequently the institution's own agreement. Maintained mode, so a seat exists and can act.
- **Domain 3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: rival specialists, later generations, instruments, other fields, the public. Currency: residuals and refutations. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Pool must be unauthored and must change with the question.
- **Partition or union.** A union, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-57). The conditions overlap and have different anchors. One lever clears the region where only one condition binds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Ground contact, then vocabulary for what a claim affords, then the objective, then the checking arrangement, then carrying methods rather than recordings. Refusal precedes mint (Formula step 2). The residual is described before any frame is chosen (Fig 8 :53).

Strain test applied: Fig 11 :36 (post-hoc label in an unmaintained habitat) and Fig 10 (maintained seats that issue credentials) first looked contradictory. Re-census assigns them to different modes, different objects, so no strain remains. Both are kept because both are needed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the roster does not. *Qualified* is a label applied afterwards to a lineage whose claims the world kept, not a property carried by a person. The credential is a convention about who may act, legitimate for coordination and disqualifying as a verdict about the world the moment it decides one, and it becomes standing that outlives its occasion unless the record shows a re-test at each use. A claim is settled only where a verifier ranges against a non-updating ground, is denied the interiors of the claim, and is drawn from an unauthored pool that changes when the question changes. The three conditions have different anchors, so the question is a union and the verdicts stay separate. Credentialing filters who gets tried and never supplies the verdict. Credentialed and local arrangements are isomers over the full shared atom set if that atom set genuinely matches; otherwise they are a functional group. The literal list of who was qualified is a `gap`, reported as a verdict rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the three domains, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, its refuters and its lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a roster or a prestige list instead of a generator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the six refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective readings (qualification as a carried stock, the credential as a verdict, the roster as an answer) are removable without touching the shape. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A re-test record showing whether a credential regenerates; a role-duality audit showing whether a verifier was denied the interiors; a change-of-question test showing whether the room changed; a standing list that remained correct across a change of residual; or a controlled comparison showing that qualification travels with the individual across habitats. Any of these fires a pre-registered refuter in `01` §4 and withdraws the claim. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
