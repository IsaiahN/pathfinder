## Four findings in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **A credential is a proxy, not a discrimination instrument.** The credentialism page reports that employers use the bachelor's degree as *"a proxy for quality employees - a rough, rule-of-thumb screening mechanism."* This is F2 in world terms: a frame-internal score used to decide who may speak. It does not show that the proxy never correlates with contact; it shows that correlation is not discrimination.
2. **Role duality is the named failure of peer review.** The peer review page: *"A particular concern in peer review is 'role duality' as people are in parallel in the role of being an evaluator and being evaluated."* This is F5 at the institutional scale, the same shape as Fig 10 :86 (*"Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it"*).
3. **Unauthored pools exist and have their own failure.** Traditional knowledge is transmitted orally, Elders are employed as knowledge holders, and customary law governs who may use which knowledge. Critics demand that such claims face the same scrutiny as other claims. Both halves matter: enumeration gives independence, but a habitat without a ground drifts (Fig 2 :34-35).
4. **Self-assessment is not a ground.** The Dunning-Kruger page records both the effect and a substantial statistical critique. I use only the narrow claim that a low-skill person lacks the ability to recognize the qualitative difference between performances, which is Fig 3's broken middle link, and leave the size of the effect open.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule was not triggered. The three history and sociology sources (credentialism, peer review, sociology of scientific knowledge) are one broad science-studies evidence pool, not three witnesses. Traditional knowledge and Dunning-Kruger are genuinely lower-overlap fields. The seven essays share the framework author's pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 and :25** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* and *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the source links.
- **Fig 7 :35** — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 1 :26-27** — *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued... They are frame-internal."* Do not count positive replies as evidence. Count them as contact, and keep them separate from the verdict column.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. But `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*, so each user must write what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape survives. The pre-registered refuter in `01` §4 is the test. If it fires, stop and retract before sending anything.
