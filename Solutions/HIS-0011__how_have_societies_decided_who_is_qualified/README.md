**Mode:** `specified` on the three per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal standing list of who is qualified. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* A list of people selected for receptiveness is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting correspondents for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **The outside frame must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
