## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README (local, read this session) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and the same passage says the system *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the form of the answer you can state before you can fill it: a table indexed by star kind, with definitions and instruments attached and each cell left open until a measurement fills it. The shape constrains; it does not fill. "One rate per star-kind slot" is a shape. "η⊕ = 0.24" is a filled cell, and the figures did not fill it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is "occurrence rate per star", "rocky", "habitable zone", "star kind"; narrowing adds the slot index and the definition columns.
- Fig 9 :27-28 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing Kepler, TESS, HARPS or direct imaging as the frame that will fill a cell.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is not independently verified this session, so the only version used here is the figure route: Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* and Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the parallel is that once the slot definitions and instruments are right, the numbers are a lookup, not a derivation.

## 3. What licenses a cell value

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For η⊕ this is a reported measurement with its completeness correction and credible interval.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The measurement literature is such an import. It adds an atom to our frame (a rate) without adding an atom to the world (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

The 2025 review's own refusal to provide a new estimate is the correct behaviour at this boundary: it consolidates the shape and the failure modes and leaves the cells to measurements.

## 4. The source/instrument discrimination point

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a single η⊕ number is a projection. It cannot tell you whether the rate came from a separable power-law extrapolation, from a short-period population contaminated by stripped cores, or from a sample whose star kinds shifted after Gaia. Those are three different sources with one reading. What is owed is a better instrument and an explicit provenance, not a more confident single number. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 5. What the shape is NOT for this question

- Not a star-kind ranking. The figures contain no principle that ranks FGK above M or vice versa; the world literature currently disagrees about any trend (F6 of `01`).
- Not a single mission-planning number. That is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60), not a witness.
- Not a proof that the rate is small because few planets were detected. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not a derivation of climate science from the six-term substrate. The habitable-zone boundaries are imported, with their own anchors.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A consensus η⊕ from a high-prestige working group can fund a mission; it cannot settle the rate.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, this file and `09_watson_answer.md` are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not tests of the question and cannot settle it. Every substantive claim in this folder is labelled `general`, `specified` or `grounded` accordingly, and the numeric question is explicitly recorded as a `gap` from the figures.
