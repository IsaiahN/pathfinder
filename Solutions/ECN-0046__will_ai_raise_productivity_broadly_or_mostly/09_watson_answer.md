## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: will AI raise productivity broadly, or mostly benefit a narrow set of firms and workers? Option A: answer the empirical question from the figures. Option B: state the shape a checkable answer must have, name the ground that would settle it, and refuse the binary as a verdict. What each leads to: A delivers a `general`-mode claim with no ground and spends the independence of the people checking it; B delivers a per-slot measurement design, five pre-registered refuters, and an open empirical slot until instruments settle it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between capability signals and measured productivity, plus unobserved dispersion across firms and workers. Arity: a distribution over many slots, not one number. Symmetry: breadth is a threshold relation over a distribution, not a symmetric join of two aggregates. Scale: first order inside a firm or task, second order at the economy aggregate. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, production and measurement.** Actors: firms, workers, tasks, statistical offices. Currency: output per hour, total factor productivity. Anchor: measured, revisable productivity data plus rollout designs. Legitimate because the question is constitutively about measured productivity.
- **Domain 2, AI capability.** Actors: models, labs, compute, benchmarks. Currency: benchmark scores. Anchor: none *for this question*. Benchmarks are produced by the frame that reports them, so they cannot settle productivity here.
- **Domain 3, diffusion habitat.** Actors: adopters, managers, data, process redesign, complements. Currency: contact. Anchor: the measured productivity of Domain 1 reached through adoption. Capability lives in agent-and-habitat, not in the model alone.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: researchers, expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Partition or union:** union (capability breadth and diffusion breadth). Unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency:** capability → contact → per-slot effect → threshold comparison → verdict; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: Fig 1's per-slot rule and the BLS aggregate are not in conflict. Fig 1 governs the residual the question is a residual of; the aggregate is a lossy projection of it. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The binary is refused as a verdict and kept as a measurement question. A checkable answer is a distribution over firm, worker and task slots with a breadth threshold fixed before measurement, unbundled into capability and diffusion conditions, with capability and worker-level versus firm-level breadth treated as different terms. Capability alone does not move measured productivity unless contact changes. The figures return this shape and five pre-registered refuters; they do not return an empirical answer. Status `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single point is Fig 1 :19-22: an average is the wrong instrument for a question about dispersion.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but real. If the shape is wrong, the loss is a wasted measurement budget on per-slot data that add nothing, and the spending of outside readers' independence by sending a generator they cannot use. Fig 2 :42 prices the latter: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by asking for a refutation of the shape before offering anything else.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape and its refuters can be replaced without touching the figure text. No primitive is withdrawn. A published packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and the correction stays visible in the retraction log.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot-resolving dataset showing that decomposition adds nothing over the mean would fire refuter 1; a study in which one condition explains all dispersion would fire refuter 2; a contact-free capability improvement that moves output would fire refuter 3 (Fig 11 :40); a benchmark that settles productivity without a ground reading would fire refuter 4; a verdict that flips across unreported cuts would fire refuter 5. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
