## VERDICT (from the figures)

The division is a chemistry-forced shape delivered in a particular historical order. The two halves are both true and are never averaged. A long-term archive needs a substrate in which the arrangement is recorded and a flag for damage; working roles need a carrier whose deriving is not carried forward and whose reactivity is the price of its usefulness. Deoxyribose and thymine are a `grounded` binding from chemistry that supplies the first; the 2'-OH chemistry supplies the second. RNA precedes DNA in biosynthesis and probably in history, and selection fixed the union. The molecule names are provenance-recorded bindings, not figure derivations. The first genetic polymer remains unreached. The chemistry claims are `grounded` in the opened literature; the shape and the history are `specified`, and nothing here is a ground reading taken by this author.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, four refuters and the provenance split before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending the framing before the residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with C1 to C3 and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes, domain by domain. The chemical binding can be replaced if a better instrument discriminates sources differently; the history reading can be withdrawn if a DNA-first lineage is found; the shape claim can be withdrawn if a cell is shown to keep a heritable archive with no recorded substrate. None of these require rewriting the six-term set. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A comparative measurement showing the 2'-OH does not materially change persistence or that uracil repair does not reduce mutational load would move the chemistry; a cell with no recorded archive would break the shape; a DNA-first lineage would break the arrival order; a re-census showing Fig 12 :107-109 governs only cross-boundary settlement would send F1 back to census. A source-discriminating instrument on alternative backbones would name what the archive slot can hold. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
