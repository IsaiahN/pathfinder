## 0. The residual in effect terms

The thing to be explained is a **persistent asymmetry between two carriers of the same kind of information inside one cell**.

- DNA is retained across divisions and across generations, is copied with high fidelity, is chemically long-lived, and is repaired.
- RNA is made when needed, acts as a message, an adapter, a structural and catalytic component of the ribosome, and a regulator, and is degraded on a timescale suited to the job.

The residual is not "which molecule is better". It is: **what property must a carrier have to serve as a record, and what property must a carrier have to serve as an intervention, such that one polymer is repeatedly kept for the first role and the other for the second.**

Bound term for the run: **record stability** means the arrangement persists and re-deriving it is a lookup; **work plasticity** means the arrangement is spent in the doing and the deriving is not carried forward. These are ordinary terms, bound now so they can accrue a residual and be refused (Fig 5 :62-66: make the sought-for shape an ordinary term).

Two options with their consequences:

| option | what it asserts | where it leads |
|---|---|---|
| Necessity | The asymmetry is a shape forced by the chemistry of the two backbones | convergence is expected; the specific polymer is a binding from chemistry, not a free choice |
| Contingency | The asymmetry is a frozen arrival order (RNA first, DNA later) with no chemical compulsion | alternatives remain live; the present division is a lineage record, not a law |

The question as written already contains a partial answer ("for long-term information storage" versus "for many intermediate roles"). The audit does not treat the premise as settled. §8 records the live exception: RNA-genome viruses and laboratory deoxyribozymes show that neither role is exclusive to one polymer.

## 3. Findings

### F1: the figure that governs the split is the recorded arrangement, not the molecule's name (decisive, `specified`)

Fig 12 :107: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."* The sentence continues into the substrate rule. Fig 12 :108-109: *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."* The complementary half, from Fig 12 :107-108: *"where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once."*

The mapping is exact at the effect level:

- **DNA is the substrate where the arrangement is recorded.** The cost of establishing the sequence was paid once in a lineage; each cell that inherits it performs a lookup, not a fresh derivation. That is what long-term storage *is* in these terms.
- **RNA is where the result carries forward and the deriving does not.** A messenger carries the working copy; it is read and degraded. Each round of expression pays again. That is what an intermediate role *is*.

Fig 4 :22 gives the same rule from a different figure with low overlap: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 adds: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Two figures, different subject matter (composition cost across a boundary versus cross-scale transform), same governed quantity: what persists as a record versus what must be re-derived. **Triangulated.** Low overlap because Fig 12 prices an arrangement and Fig 4 prices a transform between sizes.

- **Refuter:** show a cell that maintains a long-lived archive with no substrate in which the arrangement is recorded, paying the derivation cost every generation at no loss; or show that Fig 12 :107-108 governs only cross-boundary settlement and not molecular persistence (that sends us back to census, it does not patch the reading).

### F2: the two roles are priced in different currencies and a single figure cannot hold both (`specified`)

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*

The archive role is priced in bits: density, fidelity, how much can be held and copied. The work role is priced in actions: catalytic turnover, how fast a message is made and read, how quickly a ribozyme acts, how cheaply a wrong copy is replaced. A system that holds only one accounting will propose an arrangement it cannot afford to test. The question's own phrasing already separates the two ("storage" versus "roles"), and the figures say the separation is structural, not rhetorical.

**Single-source** for the figure definition (Fig 12 :125-127 only). The world source on DNA storage density corroborates the bits side but shares no evidence pool with the figure, so it is a separate witness for the *grounded* claim, not for the figure's taxonomy.

- **Refuter:** derive the two prices from one another, or show that a single scalar can price both persistence and intervention without loss. Fig 12 :129-130 says they come apart in both directions, so a successful derivation would break the figure, not refine it.

### F3: the chemical ground settles which polymer can be the record (`grounded` for the chemistry)

The figure gives the shape (a record needs a substrate in which the arrangement persists). The chemistry frame supplies the name, with a source gate and a provenance record. World sources read 2026-11-23:

- Wikipedia, *RNA world*: *"RNA is less stable, being more prone to hydrolysis due to the presence of a hydroxyl group at the ribose 2' position."*
- Same source: *"This group makes the molecule less stable because, when not constrained in a double helix, the 2' hydroxyl can chemically attack the adjacent phosphodiester bond to cleave the phosphodiester backbone."*
- Same source: *"DNA has greater stability and durability than RNA, which may explain why it became the predominant information storage molecule."*

The 2'-OH is a chemical handle that makes RNA reactive and therefore short-lived; deoxyribose lacks it. This is the ground reading that licenses the binding of "archive" to DNA. It is **not** a figure line and is not claimed as one.

A second, independent chemical reason concerns the bases. *RNA world*: *"Uracil is, however, one product of damage to cytosine that makes RNA particularly susceptible to mutations"*. In DNA, uracil is abnormal, so damage is detectable. Wikipedia, *Uracil-DNA glycosylase*: *"Its most important function is to prevent mutagenesis by eliminating uracil from DNA molecules by cleaving the N-glycosidic bond and initiating the base-excision repair (BER) pathway."* And: *"Uracil bases occur from cytosine deamination or misincorporation of dUMP residues."* A record needs not only stability but a flag for damage. Thymine supplies the flag because it is methylated uracil; the repair system can then remove deaminated cytosine without destroying correct bases.

**Verdict:** the *chemistry* is `grounded` by the opened sources (hydrolysis mechanism, deamination, repair pathway). The *figure reading* that this is what a record requires remains `specified`.

- **Refuter:** a measurement showing that the 2'-OH does not materially change strand persistence under cellular conditions, or that uracil removal does not reduce mutational load, would break the chemical half. It would not by itself break F1, which is a structural claim about records.

### F4: RNA's work roles are the reactive half of the same trade-off (`grounded` on function, `specified` on the figure reading)

Wikipedia, *Ribozyme*: *"are RNA molecules that have the ability to catalyze specific biochemical reactions, including RNA splicing in gene expression, similar to the action of protein enzymes."* And: *"The 1982 discovery of ribozymes demonstrated that RNA can be both genetic material (like DNA) and a biological catalyst (like proteins)"*. *RNA world*: *"In general, DNA is much more chemically inert than RNA and hence much more resistant to obtaining catalytic properties. If in vitro evolution works for DNA it will happen much more easily with RNA."*

The same 2'-OH that disqualifies RNA as an archive is what gives it catalytic reach. Fig 12 :119 governs the structural side: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Ribozymes are parts that survive their own use; the archive is the part whose *arrangement* is recorded. These are different structures, and the figure says nothing records which a term is, which is exactly the provenance problem the field has with assigning roles from chemistry alone.

**Single-source** for the figure line (Fig 12 :119). The catalytic function is corroborated by two opened world pages with partly different evidence (ribozyme discovery and in-vitro evolution), but neither shares the figure's evidence pool. `grounded` on function.

- **Refuter:** show a working catalyst whose catalytic power does not come from a reactive group that also shortens its persistence, and F4's chemistry half fails.

### F5: the residual must be kept per role, not averaged (`specified`)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Applying one score such as "chemical stability" to both roles erases the signal. The archive slot wants persistence; the work slot wants turnover. An average of the two predicts nothing about either. This is why §7 keeps verdicts per domain and never averages them.

**Triangulated** with Fig 9 :54 (union unbundle) and Fig 2 :31 (independence). Different figures, low overlap: Fig 1 is about slot index, Fig 9 about question type.

- **Refuter:** produce a single scalar that predicts both persistence and turnover in the same cell without losing either. Until then the per-role treatment stands.

### F6: the arrival history is a second condition, not a footnote (`specified`)

The chemistry does not say which polymer appeared first. The history frame does. *RNA world*: *"RNA is thought to have preceded DNA, because of their ordering in the biosynthetic pathways."* And: *"a cell must have the ability to make RNA before it can make DNA."* From *Ribozyme*: *"RNA can be both the chicken and the egg."* Fig 11 :36 states the structural reading: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."* Selection ran; no one designed the assignment. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Adding DNA changed contact with time, because a stable record reaches descendants that a transient copy does not.

The arrival order is therefore a genuine second condition (union, not partition). A chemical necessity claim that ignores it is Answer A with the middle link missing.

**Single-source** for the figure reading (Fig 11 :36, :40). The historical sequence is corroborated by the biosynthetic-ordering statement in the opened world source. `specified` overall, because no direct measurement of the first genetic polymer exists.

- **Refuter:** a lineage found whose heritable polymer precedes RNA in its own biosynthetic pathway, or evidence that DNA-like molecules were the first genetic material, would break the arrival-order half.

### F7: the shape is bounded; the polymer names are bindings (`specified`)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

The figures return the shape: a record needs a persistent substrate, and working roles need a reactive one. They do not return "deoxyribose" or "thymine". Those come from the chemistry frame, and the record must say so. The canonical README phrase is *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. DNA and RNA are **not** missing primitives, so naming them is not forbidden; but calling the naming a *derivation* would be the metamerism error described in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. A stability reading does not by itself disclose its source.

**Triangulated** for the shape limit (Fig 9 :32 plus the canonical README, low overlap between a figure and the usage rule). `specified`.

- **Refuter:** derive the specific sugar and base from the six-term set alone, with no chemistry import, and F7 is broken.

### F8: the six-term set is not challenged by this question (`general`, re-censused)

Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* DNA and RNA are arrangements of phosphate, sugar and base inside a closed chemistry; they are not candidates for seventh substrate members. The question therefore does not license any revision to the substrate. The earlier attempt to force Fig 13 :75 onto the pair is retracted in §9.

**Single-source** (Fig 13 :115). `general`.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a polymer role that requires a new substrate member rather than a new arrangement of held ones.

### F9: what survives the ground check, and what does not

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The two science pages for stability and repair corroborate the chemistry half; the ribozyme and RNA-world pages corroborate the work half; the biosynthetic-ordering statement corroborates the arrival half. All of them are secondary. The framework essays are by the framework's author and carry high overlap with the figures, so their agreement is one pool, not an independent witness (Fig 2 :40).

- **Refuter:** a source from a genuinely different evidence pool (an independent measurement, not a review of the same literature) that contradicts the 2'-OH hydrolysis account or the uracil-repair account would trigger a re-census. It would not flip the figure verdict by itself.

### F10: filters cannot supply the verdict (`general`)

Ranking the venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, or counting how many sources agree, are budget decisions. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The verdict in §7 comes from the figures plus the chemical ground, not from the number of pages opened.

### F11: the honest stop is a measurement limit, not a silence (`general`)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No search of the modern-biochemistry literature settles the prebiotic question of the first genetic polymer. The result is recorded as unreached with the present instruments and as a union that must be unbundled, not as a missing answer manufactured by argument.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is: **a long-term archive requires a substrate in which the arrangement is recorded; working roles require a carrier whose deriving is not carried forward; the two are held apart by backbone reactivity and damage-flag chemistry, and were joined historically in an RNA-first order.**

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Chemical refuter.** A measurement under cellular conditions shows that the ribose 2'-OH does not materially reduce strand persistence, or that uracil-directed repair does not reduce mutational load. This breaks F3 and the chemical half of F4 at the ground.
2. **Structural refuter.** A cell is found that maintains a long-lived heritable archive without any substrate in which the arrangement is recorded, paying the full derivation cost each generation at no loss. This breaks F1.
3. **Census refuter.** A re-census shows Fig 12 :107-108 governs cross-boundary settlement only and not molecular persistence. Then the line was aimed at another quantity, F1 is withdrawn, and a new line is owed. This is a re-census trigger, not a patch.
4. **History refuter.** A lineage is found whose heritable polymer is not downstream of RNA in its own biosynthesis, or direct evidence places DNA-like polymers first. This breaks F6.
5. **Exclusivity refuter, stated against the question's own premise.** A modern organism is shown using RNA as its sole long-term archive with DNA absent and with fidelity and persistence equal to DNA-based lineages. This weakens the necessity reading to a local fact about one lineage. RNA viruses are noted as a live partial case in §8 and are not silently excluded.

If any fires, the corresponding domain verdict is withdrawn and the record is updated without erasing this list.

## 8. Where the search stops, and the live exceptions

Formula step 7 asks SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, and keeps them distinct.

- **SEARCHED (modern biochemistry).** Well swept. The 2'-OH hydrolysis mechanism, the repair pathway, the catalytic versatility of RNA and the biosynthetic ordering are all in the opened sources. More searching here will not settle the *first* polymer, because all extant life already has DNA.
- **INWARD (sharper instruments on extant systems).** Available. Compare persistence and mutational load across RNA-genome and DNA-genome lineages under matched conditions. This would move the chemical claim toward ground.
- **OUTWARD (a different frame).** Prebiotic chemistry and synthetic XNA work. *Ask an Astrobiologist* (NASA) states the live question directly: researchers have synthesized DNA and RNA analogs with simpler backbones, and *"The challenge for these molecules, however, has been for them to maintain their stability, required for undergoing Darwinian evolution and remaining biologically active."* That is an import opportunity with a source gate and a shadow test, not a conclusion.

**Live exceptions, not hidden:** RNA viruses use RNA as genetic material; laboratory deoxyribozymes show DNA can catalyze when selected. Neither overturns the predominant division in cellular life, but both prevent the claim from being stated as a categorical law. Fig 8 :39 applies: absence of a counter-case in the pages read is not proof that none exists.

**Round-trip gap.** The condensation carried into `09_watson_answer.md` drops three things that the verbatim set holds: the per-slot residual rule, the union/unbundle rule, and the warning that a shape is not a name. `11_audit.md` measures R_T qualitatively as that dropped set. Treating the condensed answer as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
