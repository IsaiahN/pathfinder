## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures state the limit. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The canonical README gives the compact form: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the answer can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: a long-term archive requires a substrate in which the arrangement persists and a flag for damage; working roles require a carrier whose deriving is not carried forward.
- Name: deoxyribose, thymine, uracil-DNA glycosylase, the 2'-OH.

The shape is what the figures return. The names are a binding from the chemistry frame with provenance. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F7 keeps the two apart.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* The method is proximity: start with the vocabulary you have that is closest to what you are trying to describe, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms (record persistence versus working plasticity) before any figure line was selected. Only then was Fig 12 chosen. That order is what prevents the chemistry frame from being picked first and then mapped onto whatever figure looks closest.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the chemical measurements are the ground.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The sugar and base names are such an import, from nucleic-acid chemistry.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The instrument that resolved it was a spectrometer: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Here, "DNA is stable, therefore it is the archive" is a reading off a projection. Several chemical sources could produce a stable archive (deoxyribose, thymine, double-stranding, repair enzymes, encapsulation). The reading does not disclose which one is doing the work. The honest move is to name the measurement that discriminates, not to declare the source from the reading.

## 4. Correction to the usual "shape, not a name" reading (carried into this solution)

The phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. DNA and RNA are not missing primitives of the chemistry frame. They are compositions of phosphate, sugar and base, and they are already named in the frame that supplies the ground. Naming them is therefore not the forbidden act.

What **is** forbidden is claiming that the framework derived those names from the six-term set, or that binding them moves the wall of the closure (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). Two claims are kept side by side, neither erased:

- The shape (a persistent record plus a reagent working set) is figure-derived and `specified`.
- The specific molecules are a provenanced binding from chemistry, `grounded` in the opened pages, and are recorded as such.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question, the named researchers associated with the RNA world are a budget reason to open the literature, never a reason to accept the conclusion. The four refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are the ground-facing part. A reply that says "this agrees with what [famous author] says" is not a verdict.

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures and against pages this author opened. It is not an experiment, not a measurement, and not a settlement of the question. The chemistry claims are `grounded` in the sense that they report what the opened pages report; they are not independently reproduced here.

## 7. What shape means, stated once for this question

A **shape** here is the constraint that any answer to the DNA/RNA division must satisfy: an archive is a substrate where the arrangement is recorded so that re-derivation becomes a lookup, while working roles are carriers whose deriving is not carried forward and whose reactivity is the price of their usefulness. The shape does not select deoxyribose or thymine. Those come from measurement, are recorded as bindings, and can be replaced if a better instrument discriminates differently. The parts that are unreached (the first genetic polymer, the full set of alternative archives) are recorded as unreached, not filled in. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
