## Wider literature that bears on the question, and how it was used

1. **Hydrolysis and backbone chemistry.** The RNA-world page reports that the 2'-OH can attack the adjacent phosphodiester bond when the strand is not constrained in a double helix. This is the ground for preferring deoxyribose in an archive. It is a chemical measurement reported in a secondary page, not a figure line and not a measurement by this author.
2. **Damage flagging.** The uracil-DNA glycosylase page supplies a second, independent reason: deaminated cytosine becomes uracil, and a repair enzyme removes it in DNA but could not flag it in RNA where uracil is normal. This is why "stable" is not enough for a record; the record also needs a damage discriminator.
3. **Catalysis and folding.** The ribozyme page reports that the same 2'-OH group acts as a nucleophile in self-cleaving ribozymes, and that RNA can be both genetic material and catalyst. One chemical feature, two opposite consequences. This is the concrete instance of the two-currency rule in Fig 12 :125-126.
4. **Engineering corroboration.** The DNA data storage page is from a different field (computer storage), shares no evidence pool with the origin-of-life pages, and independently treats DNA as an archival medium priced by density and error correction. It is the lowest-overlap world witness in this set. It does not decide the figure verdict; it corroborates the bits side of the two-currency reading.
5. **History and the open prebiotic question.** The NASA Astrobiology FAQ states the stability problem for synthetic genetic polymers. That is an outward-search target, not an answer.

Overlap note: sources 1 to 3 quote overlapping chemistry literature (Wikipedia pages cross-link heavily). They are treated as one evidence pool for the hydrolysis and repair claims, not as three independent witnesses. Source 4 is the only genuinely low-overlap witness. The three framework essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement is expected and is recorded as high overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv` (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framing before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35**: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. A failure is the useful return.
- **Seat Map :163**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list ranked by likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42**: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* One-to-one contact through opt-in venues buys more independence than a broadcast.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the four science links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) on request, the formula as a method, with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## Two-stage offer

**Stage 1, before any theory.** Send the residual and the refuter in `02_outreach_email.md`. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, send the links above. The stage-1 verdict is already recorded, so later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Stage 3, on request.** Send the formula as a generator. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must therefore write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The RNA-virus and deoxyribozyme exceptions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §8 must be stated in the first message. A recipient who has to supply the exception themselves has already refuted the framing.
