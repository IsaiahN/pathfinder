## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For baryogenesis, a **shape** is the set of properties any mechanism must have before we know which mechanism it is. It is:

- a refusal of held symmetries (B, C, CP cannot all be exact);
- a spendable directedness (the process must leave equilibrium);
- a carrier outside the Standard Model's closure;
- a compound, since each condition alone is insufficient.

That is a shape. "Leptogenesis" is a name. "Affleck-Dine" is a name. "CPT-symmetric universe" is a name. The framework cannot select among them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "it cannot comment on the source" and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Describe what would discriminate sources, then let whoever can check it against the world do so.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms first (a signed net excess on one slot), and only then were figure lines and world fields consulted. That order guards against the apophenia Fig 9 :27-28 names: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Baryogenesis is a field where it would be easy to pick leptogenesis first and then find figure lines to fit it. That inversion was refused.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For baryogenesis, a collider signature of new CP violation, a measured electric dipole moment, a neutrinoless double beta result, or a cosmological non-gaussianity signal could discriminate among the candidate atoms.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A leptogenesis calculation imported into the shape must record where it came from and which gap it explains.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the color case.

Here the reading is eta. Several candidate mechanisms produce the same eta. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a vote among models.

## 4. Why "the thermal average is zero" is the shape's sharpest everyday form

Wikipedia ("Baryogenesis", 2026): "the interactions must occur before thermal equilibrium because the thermal average of the standard model baryon asymmetry is zero." Wikipedia ("Baryon asymmetry", 2026): "the interactions must be out of thermal equilibrium, since otherwise CPT symmetry would assure compensation between processes increasing and decreasing the baryon number."

This is F1 in the world's own vocabulary. A directionless background returns zero on the slot. A directed, out-of-equilibrium process can return a small positive residual. Fig 13 :14 says energy is "conserved, finite, directionless alone"; Fig 13 :22 calls a gradient "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 13 :47 says "neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." The world rate condition and the figure directedness rule are two descriptions of the same requirement from different evidence pools. Agreement here is corroboration, not a second derivation.

## 5. The shape's standing after the check

**The deliverable is the shape plus its refuters, not a model.** From the README: "It can say what is reachable from what you hold, what is unreachable and therefore owed, and what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name) - then it stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." The word "Indiscernibility is not identity" is the guard against claiming that because several models fit eta they are the same answer.

**Correction to my earlier reading.** I initially treated "shape, not a name" as if the framework could never bind any concrete physics term. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a missing thing": a primitive not held in Gamma. The four shape properties are constraints, and binding a measured quantity (eta) or a measured symmetry violation (neutral-kaon CP) onto them is a binding from the physics frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the framework *derives* which model is right. Both the narrower and the broader point are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and a refutation from the ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of baryogenesis and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
