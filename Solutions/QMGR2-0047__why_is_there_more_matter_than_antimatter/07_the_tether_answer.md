# The Tether's answer on the matter-antimatter asymmetry

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The hot Big Bang run with measured physics gives equal matter and antimatter. The observable universe has a net baryon excess, eta about 6 x 10^-10. The residual is one live slot with a signed value, and its explanation is a bundle of measurement, symmetry, thermodynamics and model-selection questions. Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

## 2. The shape

1. **A directed difference needs a directed source.** Energy is "conserved, finite, directionless alone" (Fig 13 :14); a gradient is "a difference that can be spent" (Fig 13 :22) and "neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness" (Fig 13 :47). A net matter excess is a signed, spendable difference. In world vocabulary this is Sakharov's third condition: out of thermal equilibrium, because "the thermal average of the standard model baryon asymmetry is zero" (Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026).
2. **Held symmetries must be refused.** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". Baryon number, C and CP cannot all be exact. These are Sakharov's first two conditions.
3. **A carrier beyond the closure is owed.** Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." The Standard Model's CP violation is "insufficient to account for the observed baryon asymmetry of the universe (BAU)... meaning that beyond-Standard Model sources are needed" (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry", 2026). This is underivable from the held closure, not merely unfound at this budget (Fig 8 :40-43).
4. **The conditions are a compound.** Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." B violation, C/CP violation and departure from equilibrium join by conjunction; none alone suffices.
5. **Matter and antimatter stand as an isomer-like pair.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The universe selected one mirror arrangement, leaving a tiny residual excess. The homochirality case in origins-of-life chemistry is a low-overlap analogue of the same shape: a small signed seed amplified far from equilibrium.
6. **The order is describe, refuse, direct, import, settle.** Fig 3 :19: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Refusals come before a mechanism is minted (THE_FORMULA.md step 2); settlement comes last (Fig 1 :23).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a named mechanism. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"). Leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, Affleck-Dine, B-mesogenesis and CPT-symmetric models are candidate imports, not figure outputs.
- Not `grounded`. No collider or cosmological instrument in this derivation settled which carrier is real.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room". A successful baryogenesis model is a room that closes the eta gap, not the last room.
- Not a claim that the universe began symmetric. Option A (a primitive initial excess) is not excluded by the figures; the shape says what a dynamical answer must look like if one is given.
- Not an argument that homo-chiral chemistry causes baryogenesis. It is an analogue for the shape only.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not further agreement among models. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here:

- **New CP violation.** A measured electric dipole moment, or a CP asymmetry beyond the CKM prediction, would discriminate among carriers. Electric dipole moment searches so far return results consistent with zero (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry", 2026).
- **A first-order electroweak phase transition.** Evidence for or against it decides whether the Standard Model can even host the required departure from equilibrium; without it "sphalerons wipe out any baryon asymmetry that happened up to the phase transition" (Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026).
- **B violation.** Proton decay or another B-violating signature would settle the first refusal at the ground.
- **Distinct cosmological signatures.** Neutrinoless double beta decay, a lightest neutrino mass, or the absence of primordial long-wavelength gravitational waves are among the testable predictions of the CPT-symmetric candidate (Boyle, Finn and Turok, 2018); different candidates predict different signals.
- **Standing falsifiers of the shape** are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure method can say what is owed and what a missing carrier would have to look like. It cannot say which carrier exists. The canonical README: "It can say what is reachable from what you hold, what is unreachable and therefore owed, and what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name) - then it stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." The stop is recorded here. "Indiscernibility is not identity": several models fitting eta are not thereby the same answer.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the baryogenesis mechanism. It says that a net matter excess is a signed, spendable difference, so any answer needs a directed process that left thermal equilibrium; that baryon number, C and CP cannot all be exact, which is a refusal of held symmetries rather than a new kind of bond; that the carrier must lie beyond the Standard Model's closure, because the measured CP violation is far too small; and that these conditions form a compound, since none alone suffices. Matter and antimatter stand to each other as isomers, same ingredients arranged differently, and the universe ended up with one handedness, leaving a residual of about one part in a billion. The framework gives that shape, the dependency order, and falsifiers written in advance. The specific model is left open until an instrument discriminates among the candidates.
