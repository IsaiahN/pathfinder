# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the matter-antimatter asymmetry

**What this is:** a claim in a form a baryogenesis researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural constraints. Not `grounded`.** No collider or cosmological result is claimed to have settled the carrier. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The observed baryon excess is a signed, spendable difference. Any answer must therefore have four parts: refusals of held symmetries (baryon number, C and CP cannot all be exact); a directed, out-of-equilibrium process; a carrier outside the Standard Model's closure; and the three acting together as a compound, since none alone suffices. Matter and antimatter stand as an isomer-like pair, same ingredients arranged differently, with the universe selecting one handedness and leaving a residual eta of order 6 x 10^-10. The framework returns that shape, the dependency order and pre-registered falsifiers. It does not name the mechanism.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** a signed difference requires a directed source; a conserved, directionless background cannot produce it; symmetry breaking is a unary refusal (negation), not an eighth bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14 "conserved, finite, directionless alone". Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :47 "neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness". - **Refuter:** reproduce the excess in exact thermal equilibrium with B, C and CP all conserved; or show baryogenesis can proceed with no refusal of a held regularity. If either holds, C1 fails.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by a baryogenesis researcher against the Standard Model

- **Statement:** the Standard Model's closure cannot reach the observed eta; an import (new physics) is owed. This is underivable from the held closure, not merely unfound at this budget.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :14 "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence". Fig 8 :41 "Underivable: go outside."
- **What to check:** is the insufficiency real? World sources: "Despite the allowance for CP violation in the Standard Model, it is insufficient to account for the observed baryon asymmetry of the universe (BAU) given the limits on baryon number violation, meaning that beyond-Standard Model sources are needed" (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry", 2026); "the thermal average of the standard model baryon asymmetry is zero" (Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026); and Riotto and Trodden (1999) survey the candidate imports. Does a strongly first-order electroweak phase transition rescue the Standard Model, or do sphalerons erase the asymmetry?
- **Refuter:** a calculation wholly inside the Standard Model, using measured CKM CP violation and a first-order transition, that reproduces eta of order 6 x 10^-10. If that lands, no import is owed and C2 fails.

### C3: compound and measurement; checkable by reading Figure 9 and against cosmological data

- **Statement:** the Sakharov conditions are a union compound (each necessary, none sufficient), and eta is fixed by two low-overlap instruments (CMB and BBN), not by model agreement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones". Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."
- **What to check:** are B violation, C/CP violation and departure from equilibrium jointly necessary with none sufficient? Are CMB and BBN eta determinations genuinely independent instruments?
- **Refuter:** one condition alone suffices to produce eta; or CMB and BBN determinations share a single evidence pool. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a named mechanism. Leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, Affleck-Dine, B-mesogenesis and CPT-symmetric models are candidate imports, not figure outputs (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this packet settled the carrier.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room".
- Not a claim that the universe began symmetric. A primitive initial excess is not excluded; the shape constrains a dynamical answer if one is offered.
- Not a chemical claim. The homochirality case is a cross-field analogue of the shape, not evidence about particle physics.
- Not a claim that the figures derive the measured value of eta. They constrain the form of an answer; eta is a ground reading.
