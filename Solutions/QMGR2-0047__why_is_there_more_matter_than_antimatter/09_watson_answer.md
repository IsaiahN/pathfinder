## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape any answer must have to the question "Why is there more matter than antimatter in the observable universe?" Option A: treat the excess as a brute initial condition and stop. Option B: derive the constraints a dynamical answer must satisfy, mark which are settled and which are owed, and bind a mechanism only where a ground pays. What each leads to: A gives up the residual before describing it; B gives a checkable shape, pre-registered falsifiers, and a route to the instrument that would close it. This run takes B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures govern the shape and the route in every domain and do not name the mechanism. Any answer must refuse B, C and CP as exact symmetries (triangulated), identify a directed out-of-equilibrium process (single-source in the figures, corroborated by the world rate condition), import a carrier beyond the Standard Model's closure (triangulated), treat the three conditions as a compound (triangulated), and bind the anchor to measured eta from two independent instruments (triangulated). Matter and antimatter stand as an isomer-like pair, same ingredients arranged differently, with the universe selecting one handedness. The specific carrier is a `gap` the figures cannot fill; a source-discriminating instrument is owed. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate` result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed without withdrawing the whole derivation. If the Standard Model is shown sufficient, the import finding is dropped and the refusals and directedness findings remain. If eta's two instruments are shown correlated, the anchor finding is replaced. No six-term primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument (electric dipole moment, non-CKM CP asymmetry, proton decay, or a discriminating neutrino signal) would name the carrier; a Standard Model calculation reproducing eta would remove the import; a proof that the excess can arise in equilibrium with all three conditions absent would break the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
