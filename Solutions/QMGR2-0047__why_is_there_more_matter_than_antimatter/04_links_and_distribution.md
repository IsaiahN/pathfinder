## What the world sources bear on

They are secondary corroboration only. The figures decide.

1. **The residual is real and measured.** Two independent Wikipedia pages, a 2012 review and a 1999 review all report eta of order 6 x 10^-10 and the absence of a consensus mechanism. This corroborates the F5 anchor reading: eta is a ground reading, not a theoretical preference.
2. **The closure is open.** "beyond-Standard Model sources are needed" (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry") corroborates F3: an import is owed. Riotto and Trodden list four mutually incompatible candidate atoms, and Boyle, Finn and Turok add a fifth. That proliferation is what an unclosed import looks like.
3. **The three conditions behave as a union.** The Sakharov set is jointly necessary in both background pages, with each condition covering a distinct failure. This corroborates F4.
4. **A low-overlap analogue corroborates the shape, not the specifics.** Homochirality (origins-of-life chemistry) shows a small initial enantiomeric imbalance amplified far from equilibrium by autocatalysis and mutual antagonism, with "the racemic state ee=0 is unstable". It is not evidence for any baryogenesis model. It corroborates the abstract shape: a tiny signed seed plus a far-from-equilibrium amplifier yields a large persistent imbalance. Different field, different evidence pool, so it is a genuine second witness for the shape only.
5. **No conflict triggered.** Nothing in the world sources contradicts the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not needed.
