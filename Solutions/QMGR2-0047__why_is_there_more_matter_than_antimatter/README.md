## The question restated as an effect

The hot Big Bang, run with the physics we measure today, predicts matter and antimatter in near equal amounts. The observable universe instead carries a net baryon excess of order one part in a billion (eta about 6 x 10^-10). The residual is the gap between the symmetric prediction and the measured excess. Option A: read the excess as a primitive initial condition and stop. Option B: derive the shape any dynamical answer must have, name what the figures say is owed (refusals of held symmetries, a spendable directedness, an imported atom beyond the Standard Model), and bind the term only where a ground settles it. This package takes B. It delivers a shape, a route, and pre-registered refuters, not a named mechanism.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim to have named the baryogenesis mechanism.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Adding more agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29, :35).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** A list ranked by likelihood of agreement is a list selected to agree. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **Asking for trust spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors before giving a verdict.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The routes in `03` are public and opt-in.
