## 0. The residual under audit

**Effect statement.** Running the hot Big Bang with the physics we measure today gives matter and antimatter in near equal amounts. The observable universe instead has a net baryon excess, with eta = (n_B - n_anti-B)/n_gamma of order 6 x 10^-10 (Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026; Canetti, Drewes and Shaposhnikov, 2012). The residual R is the gap between the symmetric prediction and the measured excess. The question asks what produced the excess. Two readings: (A) the universe began with a small preference for matter and that is a brute initial condition; (B) a dynamical process in the early universe generated the imbalance from a symmetric start. The figure method can only say what shape B must have. It cannot name the process.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** one live residual slot, the cosmic baryon number, but its explanation is a conjunction of three necessary conditions. So it is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-58).
- **Symmetry:** the whole content of the residual is a broken symmetry. Matter and antimatter are nearly mirror images; the observed asymmetry is a directed difference (more of one than the other). Symmetry and its violation are the subject matter, not a metaphor.
- **Scale:** cross-scale. The cause is microphysical (particle interactions at the electroweak or GUT scale), the effect is the entire observable universe's matter content. The round trip between those scales is exactly the kind Fig 4 handles, with a gap R_T that must be measured rather than assumed (Fig 4 :30).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term.** Following THE_FORMULA.md "Direction", the sought-for shape is made an ordinary term before the run: *a spendable directedness, acting on a background that no longer conserves the relevant charge, out of thermal equilibrium, whose net effect is a small persistent positive residual on the baryon slot, and whose carrier is not in the Standard Model's closure.* Binding it lets it accrue a residual like anything else instead of remaining an unseen preference.

## 3. Findings

### F1: the excess is a directed difference, not a property of a conserved background (governs D2)

Fig 13 :14: "conserved, finite, directionless alone". Fig 13 :22: "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :47: "neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness". Fig 13 :36: "adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction".

In the six-term set, a background that merely has a magnitude (ordering, spectrum-like) or merely persists (conserved, energy-like) carries no direction. A net matter excess is a signed, spendable difference. So the answer must identify a source of directedness that acted, not just a quantity that was present. In the world literature this is Sakharov's third condition: interactions out of thermal equilibrium, "since otherwise CPT symmetry would assure compensation between processes increasing and decreasing the baryon number" (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry", 2026), or "the thermal average of the standard model baryon asymmetry is zero" (Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026). The out-of-equilibrium decay review states it as a rate comparison: the reaction rate must be less than the expansion rate (Wikipedia, "Baryon asymmetry", 2026).

- **Mode:** `general` on the structural requirement (a directed difference needs a directed source), `specified` on the mapping onto thermal equilibrium.
- **Triangulation:** single-source for the directedness claim within the figures (Fig 13 and the Symbols table are one witness restated). It is corroborated, not decided, by the rate condition in the world literature, which is a different field.
- **Refuter:** an asymmetry generated and preserved while the process stayed in exact thermal equilibrium with all charges conserved. Such a result would break the directedness requirement and F1 fails.

### F2: refusals of held symmetries are owed (governs D1)

Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". THE_FORMULA.md step 2 sorts a residual whose content is that a held regularity is false into REFUTED, and says the term owed is a refusal, not an explanation.

A hot universe symmetric under C and CP and conserving baryon number returns net zero on the baryon slot. To get a nonzero slot, three held regularities must be false: baryon number conservation, C symmetry, and CP symmetry. These are exactly Sakharov's first two conditions plus the B constraint ("Baryon number B violation. C-symmetry and CP-symmetry violation. Interactions out of thermal equilibrium." Wikipedia, "Baryogenesis", 2026). The figures' operator for this is negation, which "takes a term and yields a term, and joins nothing" (Fig 12 amendment). It is not a bond and it is not a new atom in the world.

- **Mode:** `general` that refusals are structurally owed when a held regularity is false; `specified` that the three Sakharov conditions are those refusals.
- **Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 5 and the Operators table, two figures with low overlap (pipeline sorting versus operator inventory).
- **Refuter:** a verified baryogenesis mechanism that leaves B, C and CP exactly conserved and stays in equilibrium. If the excess can arise with no refusal, this finding fails.

### F3: the Standard Model's closure cannot reach the observed eta, so an import is owed (governs D1 and D2)

Fig 5 :58: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN". Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall". Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 8 :41: "Underivable: go outside."

The world sources agree on the measurement and disagree on the cause. Wikipedia ("Baryon asymmetry", 2026): "Despite the allowance for CP violation in the Standard Model, it is insufficient to account for the observed baryon asymmetry of the universe (BAU) given the limits on baryon number violation, meaning that beyond-Standard Model sources are needed." Wikipedia ("Baryogenesis", 2026): "This insufficiency has not yet been explained, theoretically or otherwise." Riotto and Trodden (1999) survey the candidate imports: "GUT baryogenesis, leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis and the Affleck-Dine mechanism". Boyle, Finn and Turok (2018) offer a competing one: "a new interpretation of the cosmological baryon asymmetry" from a CPT-symmetric universe. Multiple incompatible mechanisms exist, which is what an unclosed import looks like from inside.

- **Mode:** `specified` that an atom beyond the Standard Model's closure is owed. It is not `grounded`: no collider result has named it.
- **Triangulation:** triangulated across Fig 6, Fig 8 and THE_FORMULA.md step 7. The world reviews corroborate the insufficiency but are secondary.
- **Refuter:** a calculation purely inside the Standard Model, using the measured CKM CP phase and a strongly first-order electroweak phase transition, that reproduces eta of order 6 x 10^-10. If that lands, the import is not owed and F3 fails. Note the distinction from budget: this is underivable from the held closure, not merely unfound at this budget (Fig 8 :40-43).

### F4: the Sakharov conditions form a union compound, not a single lever (governs D1 and D2 together)

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction: "A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The observed excess is reproduced exactly in thermal equilibrium with all three Sakharov conditions absent (breaks F1 and F2).

3. One Sakharov condition alone is shown sufficient (breaks F4).
4. CMB and BBN eta determinations are shown to share one evidence pool (breaks F5).
5. A figure line is produced that names the specific mechanism (breaks F6), or the no-last-room rule is violated by a terminal answer (breaks F7).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
