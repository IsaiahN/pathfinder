## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (inherited quote): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the form an answer must have, statable before the answer itself is available. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0011, the shape is: *an earliest local slot, with an environment (a structure) composing water, a spendable gradient, feedstock and duration, bounded by an instrument reading, with the sparse record stated as a limit.* The date, and the identity of the winning setting, are not the shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained for a time question

- *Library of Babel* (inherited): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (inherited): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The effect description used in `01` is exactly this: not "when did life start" (a cause question) but "at what earliest time does a mineral reading return positive mass on water, gradient, feedstock and duration" (an effect question with a checkable observation).

## 3. What would license a name, here a date

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For this question: a U-Pb age, a δ18O or δ13C measurement, or an equivalent mineral or experimental reading that discriminates among sources.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question: a laboratory prebiotic chemistry result or a geological model imported into the framework must be labelled as such, not presented as a figure derivation.

The world lower bound (water by roughly 4.4 to 4.3 Ga, rock record only to about 4.0 Ga) is type 2 evidence: a world import with its provenance stated. It is not the framework naming the answer.

*They're Scanning Books* (inherited): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set is a visible choice, not nature's own list; treating it as a clock would turn that choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source problem in a dating question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (inherited) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a zircon δ18O reading is an observation of a mineral, not a direct reading of "an environment capable of prebiotic chemistry." Several settings can produce the same isotope signature, and one signature can be produced by processes that are not a stable surface environment. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name for the setting and not a spurious precision in the date. The Bell et al. (2015) carbon result is a good example: the authors explicitly note several abiotic processes that could produce light carbon and call for "a large database of Hadean carbon isotope measurements." That is an instrument request, not a verdict.

## 5. Answer to the inherited "what licenses a particular structure" question

The exemplar corrected an earlier overstatement of the "shape, not a name" rule. The same correction applies here, in a different form:

- The phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- Water, gradient, network and duration are not extra primitives of the six-term set. Water is a physical state and a condition; gradient and network are already two of the three structures; duration is time. So describing the capable slot is a **binding** of world conditions onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh term.
- What the figures forbid is claiming that this binding moves the wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - this one adds none) or that the framework itself selects which setting won.

So the honest position has two halves, and both are recorded so neither is quietly lost:

1. The shape is figure-derived: earliest slot, structure between space and time, absence of record not absence of environment, anchor is a measurement.
2. The specific lower bound and the ranking of settings are world-derived, provenanced as such, and never presented as figure outputs.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (inherited): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous astrobiologists who agree with a Hadean narrative is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let an instrument settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (inherited): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is simulacra. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against verbatim figure text. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
