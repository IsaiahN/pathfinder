## What this package decides in effect terms

The question asks for the earliest time at which a local environment had the conditions prebiotic chemistry needs (liquid water, a spendable energy gradient, feedstock, and duration). Two answers are compared:

- **Option A:** a single planetary "date of habitability", averaged over all environments and reported as a number.
- **Option B:** an earliest live slot, anchored to a mineral measurement, with the date bounded rather than fixed and the sparse Hadean record stated as a limit rather than hidden.

What each leads to: A aggregates away the exact signal the question asks for (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22) and turns an absence of old rock into an absence of old environments. B keeps the per-slot shape, refuses to name a number the figures do not contain, and records what instrument would move the bound.

The verdict below is B, with mode `specified`. This package does not settle when life began, and it does not claim the framework can read a date off the rock record.
