# The Tether's answer on the first prebiotic environments

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the structural constraints, `general` on the cross-kind habitat scaffold. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a date. Simulated derivation checked against verbatim figure text (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the earliest time at which a local environment carried the conditions prebiotic chemistry needs: liquid water, a spendable energy gradient, feedstock, and duration. The competing readings are (A) one planetary habitability date, obtained by aggregating settings, or (B) the earliest live slot, anchored to a mineral reading, with the sparse record stated as a limit. Option B is the figure-grounded reading. The source itself asks about "environments" and about which settings "lasted long enough", so the slot object is the question's own object, not an imposed frame.

## 2. The shape

1. **The answer is a slot, not an average.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." If one setting was capable while the planet mostly was not, the correct "first" is that setting. A global habitability date answers a different question.

2. **"Where and when" requires a structure between the two states.** Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Space and time are states, so a place-and-date pair is not a legal composition. The environment, with its conditions, is the structure that sits between them. Fig 11 :39: "And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." "Capable" belongs to a setting and what happens in it, not to chemistry in isolation and not to the planet in isolation.

3. **The conditions are enumerated from the world, not composed from the six terms.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :46: "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain". Candidate settings (vents, ponds, ice, volcanic land, crust interior) are world imports with a source gate owed. The framework returns the kind of thing an answer must be, not the winner.

4. **Absence of old rock is not absence of old environments.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Zircons already return ages and isotope ratios, so there is an edge to extend from. The honest output is a lower bound plus a stated limit, not a first date.

5. **The number is an anchor reading, never an agreement.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." No number of agreeing narratives moves a U-Pb age. The world lower bound (surface water by roughly 4.4 to 4.3 Ga, rock record only to about 4.0 Ga) is secondary corroboration from sources actually read, not a figure verdict.

6. **The framework returns a shape, not a name or a date.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The settings are compared only after the residual is described (Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"), so a preferred setting cannot hand us the answer.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a date. No figure line contains a number, and no number is asserted as a Tether result.
- Not a claim that life existed at 4.1 Ga or 3.8 Ga. Those are world claims about biosignatures, secondary and contested, and they concern the origin of life, not the first environment capable of prebiotic chemistry.
- Not a winner among vents, ponds, ice, volcanic land or crust interiors. That requires a source-discriminating instrument.
- Not a derivation of the conditions from the six-term set. The conditions are read off the world (Fig 11 :48).
- Not a global habitability state. The question is plural and the residual is per slot.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (inherited, see `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. The corresponding questions here are:

- Do the oxygen and carbon isotope signatures in Hadean zircons discriminate among a stable surface environment, a transient hydrothermal setting, and an abiotic carbon source? A measurement that does so would move the bound.
- Are there Hadean zircon localities beyond Jack Hills with the same signatures? More localities would test whether the signal is local or planetary without changing the per-slot logic.
- Is there direct mineral evidence of prebiotic products in grains older than 4.0 Ga? That would convert a lower bound on conditions into a lower bound on chemistry.

**Standing falsifiers:** show that the object is a global state so aggregation is legitimate; show a direct space-time composition with no structure; show the pre-4.0 Ga record complete in the relevant sense or produce a positive measurement setting a later bound; show a date settled by agreement rather than an anchor. If any fires, the shape is withdrawn and the answer becomes an open gap.

## 5. The honest stop

**Bounded, not dated, and not unreachable.** The six-term set is a visible guess at the compression axes, not nature's own clock. The answer stops where the mineral record stops and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Indiscernibility is not identity: a rock record that cannot distinguish settings is not a world that had no settings.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a date for the first environments capable of prebiotic chemistry, and it does not pick a winning setting. It says that the question is about the earliest local setting, not a planetary average, because a residual is indexed per slot and averaging throws away the signal. It says that "where and when" cannot be answered by a place and a date, because space and time are both states and a structure must sit between them; that structure is the environment with water, a spendable gradient, feedstock and duration. It says that the scarcity of rock older than four billion years cannot be read as a scarcity of environments, and that the number, when it comes, will be an anchor reading from minerals rather than an agreement among researchers. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would move the bound. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
