## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 13 :74-75 (structure between states), Fig 8 :39 (absence is not absence), Fig 2 :29 (anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the first capable environment is an earliest local slot with a structure between space and time, bounded by a mineral reading, not a date.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a reader's likely concrete output: "life's ingredients were present on Earth by about 4.4 billion years ago in hydrothermal vents".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing**. The concrete sentence names a setting and a planet-wide time, collapsing the slot distinction the shape insists on;
- the **absence-not-absence rule**. A concrete date invites the reading that nothing capable existed earlier, which Fig 8 :39 forbids;
- the **anchor rule**. A concrete number invites the reading that the number is a figure output, when it is a world import with provenance owed.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure with water, gradient, feedstock and duration must sit between "where" and "when". That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
