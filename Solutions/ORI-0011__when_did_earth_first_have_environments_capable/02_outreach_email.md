**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the earliest prebiotic environments on Earth

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about the early Earth record. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with me. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "when did Earth first have environments capable of supporting prebiotic chemistry?" is a question about the earliest local setting, not a planetary average. Three figure-derived constraints follow:
>
> 1. A residual is indexed per object slot, not aggregated. If one pond or vent was capable while the planet mostly was not, the correct "first" is that slot. Averaging across settings is how the signal disappears.
> 2. "Where and when" cannot be answered by pairing a place with a date. Space and time are both states, and the framework's minimum set does not allow two states to bond directly. An environment with conditions (water, a spendable energy gradient, feedstock, and duration) is the structure between them.
> 3. An absence of ancient rock is not an absence of ancient environments. The pre-4.0 Ga record is sparse, so the honest output is a lower bound plus a stated limit, not a first date.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** I am not claiming that the framework returns a date. It returns a shape. I am not naming a winning setting (vents versus ponds versus ice versus crust interiors); those are candidate frames selected after the residual is described. I am not claiming this derivation is a measurement. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. Show that the question's object is a single global habitability state, so that averaging across environments is legitimate.
> 2. Show a composition of space and time with no intervening structure under the six-term set, or show that "capable" is a property of chemistry alone rather than of a setting and its contents.
> 3. Show that the pre-4.0 Ga record is complete in the relevant sense, or produce a positive measurement that no capable slot existed before a later bound.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I withdraw the shape claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source links are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.

>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the local figures folder. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order for any reply

1. **Stage 1:** send only the shape claim and its three refuters. Record the reader's verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local source paths. The figures are the authority, not the summary.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. A refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat as if it came from any other source.
