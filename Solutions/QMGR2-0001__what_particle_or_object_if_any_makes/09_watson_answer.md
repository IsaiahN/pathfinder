## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the identity is `gap`. The mass discrepancy is a set of live, per-slot residuals, and the reading is many-to-one, so the framework returns the shape any answer must have (gravitating, non-baryonic, cold, stable, discriminating signature) and cannot select a constituent. Null results and candidate popularity are budget filters, not witnesses. The candidate set is a union of overlapping conditions with a negation branch for the no-substance case, so the question is three loops, not one. A discriminating instrument, not an argument or a vote, settles it. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged; the identity row is `gap`, not silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a favourite candidate instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No candidate is named, so nothing has to be withdrawn if a detection arrives: a recoil, conversion, lensing or gravitational-wave result would close the residual and move the identity row from `gap` to `grounded`. The unbundled loops and the shape claim are unaffected. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet without touching any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates sources (a recoil spectrum, an axion conversion signal, a PBH mass distribution no particle model can produce, or one modified-gravity law fitting all observables) would name the source. A derivation of the identity from the six-term set alone, a proof that the forward map is injective, a proof that the candidates form a partition, or a demonstration that a prestige ranking settled a particle identity without measurement, would each fire one of the pre-registered refuters in `01` section 3b and reverse the shape claim. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
