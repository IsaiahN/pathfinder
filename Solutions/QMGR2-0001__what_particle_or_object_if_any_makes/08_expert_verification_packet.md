# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the dark-matter identity question

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the meta-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** The specific identity is a `gap`. No measurement is asserted. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The gravitational mass residual is indexed by object slot, not by one global average, and it is many-to-one: the same lensing, rotation and CMB readings can be produced by several different sources. The framework therefore returns a shape, not a name. The shape is a gravitating, non-baryonic, cold, stable constituent or set of constituents, or a modification of gravity, that closes the residual and leaves a signature one instrument can discriminate from the alternatives. Null results narrow the allowed closure; they do not prove absence, and a majority preference for a candidate is a budget signal, not a verdict. The candidate conditions overlap, so the problem is three loops (existence, identity, no-substance branch), not one. The specific identity is reported as `gap`, with refuters written in advance and a list of instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 13

- **Statement:** the framework cannot name the source of a missing primitive; it returns a shape. The identity question therefore ends in `gap`, not in a candidate name.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 - "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :135 - "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"; Fig 13 :134 - "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The canonical figures README (local) states the limit as "a shape, not a name".
- **Refuter:** derive the identity of the constituent from the six-term set and the figures alone, with no imported particle physics. If that succeeds, the `gap` verdict is false.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a cosmologist against the observations

- **Statement:** the residual is a many-to-one source-discrimination (inverse) problem. The gravitational reading does not determine the source, so a discriminating instrument is owed, not another argument.
- **What to check:** are the rotation-curve, lensing (Bullet Cluster), CMB and structure readings genuinely degenerate across WIMP, axion, PBH, multi-component and modified-gravity models? If yes, naming a source from those readings alone is a metamerism error. World corroboration (opened 2026-11-23): CERN says "researchers have been able to infer the existence of dark matter only from the gravitational effect it seems to have on visible matter"; Wikipedia "Inverse problem" says "It is called an inverse problem because it starts with the effects and then calculates the causes" and records non-uniqueness as a named difficulty.
- **Figure line:** Fig 6 :32 - "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
- **Refuter:** show the gravitational data alone force a unique source, so the forward map is injective and no discriminating instrument is owed. That breaks the metamerism reading.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a physics-facing consequence

- **Statement:** null results narrow the closure but do not prove absence, and the candidate set is a union (`+` over overlapping components, with a `¬` branch for the no-substance case), so the question must be unbundled before further search.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 - "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"; Fig 9 :44 - "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing"; Fig 9 :55 - "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"; Fig 9 :60 - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The bond structure (`+`, `∥`, `¬`) is set out in `06`.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a sealed frame in which a finite search exhausts the candidate set (breaks the null-result claim), or show the candidate conditions are mutually exclusive and exhaustive (breaks the union claim, so unbundling is not owed).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that WIMPs, axions, primordial black holes or modified gravity are right or wrong. No candidate is selected.
- Not that null results prove any candidate absent, nor that they prove one exists.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own list. Declaring the primitives makes a choice visible; it does not make them neutral.
- Not that the majority view in the field is evidence. Prestige can fund a trial but cannot settle it; only the ground settles it.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not one question. The three loops (existence, identity, no-substance) are kept separate and never averaged.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Recoil / conversion test:** an event whose rate, spectrum and target dependence single out one candidate and survive an independent instrument.
2. **Compact-object test:** a lensing or gravitational-wave mass distribution that particle and baryonic models cannot produce and that fits PBH constraints.
3. **Modified-gravity test:** one law fitting rotation curves, cluster lensing, CMB peaks and structure simultaneously without unseen mass. Current world sources report that no proposed modified-gravity theory does all of these at once.
4. **Refutation of the shape claim:** any of the four pre-registered refuters in `01` section 3b fires.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135 and Fig 13 :134, three short passages (C1).
2. Say whether the gravitational observables are degenerate across the candidate classes. One sentence suffices (C2).
3. Say whether the candidate conditions overlap (so union and unbundling) or are mutually exclusive (so partition). One sentence suffices (C3).
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate sources, or say why none could.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
