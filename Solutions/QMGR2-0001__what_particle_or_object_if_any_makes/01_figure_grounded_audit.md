## 0. The residual under audit, in effect terms

The prediction `Γ(b,a)` from known physics (GR, the Standard Model, measured baryonic matter) minus observation `o'` is a positive mass discrepancy. It is indexed by object slot, not averaged:

- galaxy rotation curves: flat where a Keplerian decline is predicted;
- cluster velocity dispersions: Zwicky's unseen mass;
- gravitational lensing: mass maps separated from the baryonic gas (the Bullet Cluster);
- cosmic microwave background acoustic peaks and large-scale structure;
- baryon acoustic oscillations and structure formation.

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." So the residual is not one global number for "dark matter"; it is a set of live slots, each with `R⁺_s > 0`. Support for a mint is satisfied (Formula step 3: `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s`), but support alone does not license a name.

The question asks for the *source* of this reading. The reading does not determine the source: many underlying constituents give the same gravitational projection. That is the structure this audit tests.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, Formula "Direction"):** a constituent that gravitates at the measured strength, is non-baryonic and cold enough to match the CMB and structure, is stable over a Hubble time, and leaves a discriminating signature in at least one instrument that the alternatives do not.

## 1. Domain census

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** one effect (excess gravitation) with many candidate sources (WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino, primordial black hole, other compact object, modified gravity, several components, none). This is a many-to-one mapping, not a one-to-one one.
- **Symmetry:** the gravitational reading is insensitive to which constituent produces it. Swap the source and the lensing/rotation/CMB reading is unchanged. That is the metamerism structure: same reading, different source.
- **Scale:** spans the particle scale (cross-sections, couplings) to the cosmological scale (CMB, clusters, structure). Cross-scale, so the loop must be run at more than one level (Fig 4).

### One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

1. **Observational cosmology / astrophysics.** Actors: galaxies, clusters, CMB, lensing maps, BAO. Currency: gravitational measurements. Anchor: instruments and surveys (optical/radio telescopes, CMB experiments). Nameable anchor exists. This is where R is measured.
2. **Particle physics frame.** Actors: WIMP, axion, sterile neutrino, dark photon. Currency: cross-sections and couplings. Anchor: detectors (XENON/LZ, ADMX, CAST, LHC). Nameable anchor exists but has so far returned null in the standard mass windows.
3. **Compact-object frame.** Actors: primordial black holes, MACHOs. Currency: lensing rates, merger mass spectra, Hawking-radiation constraints. Anchor: LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA, Fermi, microlensing surveys. Nameable anchor exists; constraints are live and debated.
4. **Modified-gravity frame.** Actors: MOND, TeVeS, entropic gravity. Currency: fits to rotation curves without a particle. Anchor: the same observations as domain 1, so overlap with domain 1 is high.
5. **Framework-internal (Tether) frame.** Actors: the six terms, the bonds, Γ, closure(Γ). Currency: composition and settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement.

Every empirical domain has a nameable anchor, so all five are domains. There is no "not yet a domain" placeholder here.

### Partition or union

Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The question is a **union compound**, not a partition. The candidate conditions overlap: dark matter can be partly particle and partly compact object; modified gravity can coexist with some unseen mass; "if any" leaves the no-substance branch open. Three distinct questions are bundled: (a) is there a mass discrepancy (settled by domain 1), (b) what is its microphysical source (open), (c) could gravity itself be modified (open). Keep three loops. Never average their verdicts.

### Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. perception: can the discrepancy be measured? Yes (domain 1 instruments).
2. vocabulary: do we have terms for what candidates afford (cross-sections, lensing signatures)? Yes, well developed.
3. objective: what counts as progress here, in the frame's own terms? A discriminating measurement that closes R. This is the weakest link: a null result is often reported as progress, but it does not identify a source.
4. planning: routes exist (direct detection, haloscopes, PBH searches, lensing surveys).
5. learn and carry: each null narrows the allowed parameter space and is carried forward.

The chain is intact. What has not arrived is the ground reading at the end of it. This is an unreached ground, not a broken middle link.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The framework cannot name the constituent (decisive for the question as asked)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The canonical figures README (local) states the limit as "a shape, not a name" and says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."

The identity of dark matter is exactly a missing primitive: a constituent not held in Γ. The framework returns the shape any answer must have (gravitating, non-baryonic, cold, stable, discriminating signature) and stops. It cannot select WIMP over axion over PBH from the figure set. Naming one would mean already holding it.

**Mode: `general`** (structural limit). **Status: triangulated** across Fig 9 (leave/arrive), Fig 13 (minimum set) and the canonical README, three low-overlap routes. **Refuter:** derive the identity of the constituent from the six-term set and the figures alone, with no imported particle physics. If that succeeds, F1 is false and the `gap` verdict collapses.

### F2 - The residual is a source-discrimination (inverse) problem, not a source-bearing reading

CERN (world source, opened 2026-11-23) says: "researchers have been able to infer the existence of dark matter only from the gravitational effect it seems to have on visible matter." Wikipedia "Inverse problem" (opened 2026-11-23) defines the structure: "It is called an inverse problem because it starts with the effects and then calculates the causes," and notes that a rank-deficient forward map makes "the solution of the inverse problem will be undetermined." Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" (opened 2026-11-23): "metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions." The framework's own essay (Isaiah N, "The Hard Problem is About Source", opened 2026-11-23) puts it in the instrument's own words: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." The existing gravitational readings are the "wandering point": an edge to extend from. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not another argument.

**Mode: `specified`** (the structure of R is asserted, not measured). **Status: triangulated** across the figure set (Fig 6) and three low-overlap world fields (particle-physics outreach, mathematics/geophysics, perception science). **Refuter:** show that the gravitational data alone force a unique source, so the forward map is injective and no discriminating instrument is owed. That would break the metamerism reading.

### F3 - Null results narrow the closure; they do not prove absence

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Wikipedia "Dark matter" (opened 2026-11-23) reports "all reported null results for WIMPs across the standard GeV–TeV mass range." Wikipedia "Primordial black hole" (opened 2026-11-23): "evidence that primordial black holes may constitute dark matter is inconclusive as of 2026." Wikipedia "Axion" (opened 2026-11-23) records that an XENON1T excess was discarded by XENONnT, an instance of a better instrument resolving a source question.

So the WIMP null results and the PBH constraints are filters on the budget, not witnesses for the verdict. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Mode: `general`** (logical structure of null results). **Status: triangulated** across Fig 8 and Fig 9, corroborated by three independent experimental summaries. **Refuter:** exhibit a sealed frame in which a finite search exhausts the candidate set and a null result is a proof of absence. No such frame was found; if one is produced, F3 is withdrawn.

### F4 - Underivable and unreachable-at-budget are different facts

Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

Which applies here? The candidate names (WIMP, axion, PBH) are already in the closure Γ: physics can state them and predict their signatures. What is missing is a ground reading that selects one. That is a SEARCHED / INWARD situation first (more distributed, independent search, and better instruments), not an OUTWARD import of a new primitive. The true constituent, if it is a new particle outside the Standard Model, is not in Γ; but the framework forbids naming it (F1), so importing a name without a source gate is not available either.

**Mode: `specified`.** **Status: triangulated** across Fig 8 and Fig 6. **Refuter:** show that the candidate set is not in closure Γ and that a specific frame already predicts its shape, which would make this an OUTWARD import with a source gate rather than a search. **Strain named:** from inside the frame, depth and genuine absence look the same (Fig 6 :27); this solution cannot decide between them and says so.

### F5 - The question is a union and must be unbundled

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :5: "Split it rather than search." Wikipedia "Dark matter" (opened 2026-11-23): "So far, none of the proposed modified gravity theories can describe every piece of observational evidence at the same time, suggesting that even if gravity has to be modified, some form of dark matter would still be required." That sentence is itself a union report: the modified-gravity branch and the unseen-mass branch overlap.

The "if any" in the question is not a rhetorical hedge. It opens the branch in which there is no new particle, and the branch in which there are several components. Collapsing these into one hard question is the bundling error.

**Mode: `general`.** **Status: triangulated** (Fig 9 union rule plus the world source's explicit overlap statement). **Refuter:** demonstrate that the candidate conditions are mutually exclusive and exhaustive, so a partition is correct and no unbundling is owed.

### F6 - The verdict belongs to the ground, never to agreement or popularity

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides."

WIMP dominance in the literature is a budget and attention filter, not a verdict. A crowd of papers agreeing on a candidate is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40). What counts is low-overlap instruments ranging against the same residual.

**Mode: `general`.** **Status: triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 9. **Refuter:** show a case in this field where a vote or a popularity ranking correctly settled a particle identity without a discriminating measurement. None is recorded here.

### F7 - The instrument discipline: refine from something already returning a reading

Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Fig 6 :37-39: "Neptune was calculated before it was seen, and the prediction is what told anyone where to point". The inverse-problem source (opened 2026-11-23) records the same case: "the calculations of Adams and Le Verrier which led to the discovery of Neptune from the perturbed trajectory of Uranus." Theory preceded the instrument, and the reading told observers where to point.

In dark matter the edges already exist: recoil detectors, haloscopes, lensing surveys, gravitational-wave detectors. Refinement is INWARD on those instruments, not the invention of a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere.

**Mode: `specified`.** **Status: triangulated** (Fig 6 plus an independent mathematics source). **Refuter:** identify a candidate that has never produced any reading at any resolution and for which no proto-instrument exists. That would move it from INWARD to a genuine proto-instrument search.

## 3b. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per the seat rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device" (Seat Map, cited in the exemplar). The shape claim is: the framework cannot name the constituent, the residual is a many-to-one source-discrimination union, and the ground must settle it. It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. The identity of the constituent is derived from the six-term set and the figures alone, with no imported particle physics (breaks F1).
2. The gravitational data alone force a unique source, so the forward map is injective and no source-discriminating instrument is owed (breaks F2).
3. The candidate conditions are shown to be a partition (mutually exclusive and exhaustive), so no unbundling is owed (breaks F5).
4. An agreement-based or prestige-based ranking is shown to have correctly settled a particle identity without a discriminating measurement (breaks F6).

A direct detection event that identifies the constituent would **close** the residual rather than refute the shape claim. It would move the answer from `gap` to `grounded` for that constituent, and it is listed under "what would settle it" in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.
