## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README (local) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", and "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the conjunction of properties an answer must have, not the label of a particle.

For dark matter the closest known vocabulary is gravitational mass, lensing, CMB and structure. Bootstrapping from those yields non-baryonic, cold, stable, gravitating.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape (Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays"):

1. **The ground settles it.** A detector event, a lensing/merger signature, or a discrimination that survives independent instruments. The README says the framework stops where instruments stop.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. Correction to my own first reading (kept visible)

I first wrote as if "shape, not a name" forbade mentioning WIMP, axion or PBH at all. **That was too strong.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. WIMP, axion and PBH are already in the physics library: they are candidate arrangements over atoms the physics frame holds (see `06`). Listing them is an enumeration of bound terms, which the loop can do (Formula step 2). What the framework forbids is:

- deriving the *true* identity from the six-term set alone (no figure line does this);
- asserting a name as settled without a ground reading or a provenanced import.

So the candidates are legitimate terms to test. The `gap` verdict attaches to selecting among them, not to mentioning them. This correction mirrors the exemplar's `05` section 5b and is left here rather than erased.

## 5. The specific error this question invites

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow+blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Wikipedia "Metamerism (color)" records the general case: "metamerism is a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions." Wikipedia "Inverse problem" adds the mathematical form: an underdetermined forward map makes "the solution of the inverse problem will be undetermined."

Naming a constituent from the gravitational reading alone is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection, where several sources could give the same projection. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name. This is why the shape claim in `01` F2 is `specified` and why the identity row is `gap`.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01` and this file are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the dark-matter claim and cannot settle it. No measurement was taken for this solution.
