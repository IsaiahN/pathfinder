# The Tether's answer: what makes up most dark matter?

**Mode: `specified` on the meta-claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** The specific identity is reported as `gap`. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Known physics plus measured baryons misses the observed gravitational mass in several distinct slots (rotation curves, clusters, lensing, CMB, structure, BAO). Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." It persists and does not trend down under more observation, so it is a live residual with positive mass on multiple slots, not a closed one.

The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: (a) is there a mass discrepancy (settled), (b) what is its source (open), (c) could gravity itself be modified (open). Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Keep three loops; never average their verdicts.

## 2. The shape

1. **The reading does not determine the source.** Gravitational lensing, rotation and CMB peaks are many-to-one. The framework cannot select a constituent from them. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
2. **The answer must have a shape, not a name.** It must gravitate at the measured strength, be non-baryonic, cold enough to match CMB and structure, stable over a Hubble time, and leave at least one signature an instrument can discriminate from the alternatives. That description is bound here so it can accrue a residual (Formula "Direction").
3. **The candidate set is a conjunction of overlapping conditions with a negation branch.** Mixed models are not excluded, so `+` rather than `∥`; the "if any" branch is `¬` (refusal of the substance claim), not `−` (a component removed). See `06`.
4. **Null results narrow, they do not refute.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." WIMP nulls and PBH constraints are filters on the budget.
5. **The ground decides.** Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
6. **The instruments already exist; refine inward.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." Recoil detectors, haloscopes, lensing surveys and gravitational-wave detectors are the wandering point.
7. **The specific identity is `gap`.** Not silence, not a guess, not a leading candidate. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". SEARCHED first (distributed independent searches), then INWARD (better instruments); OUTWARD only with a source gate, and the framework forbids naming a missing primitive anyway (Fig 13 :135).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "WIMPs are most dark matter", nor axions, nor primordial black holes, nor modified gravity. No selection is made.
- Not that the null results prove any candidate absent, nor that they prove one exists.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess whose declaration makes the choice visible, not neutral (canonical README).
- Not `grounded`. No detector event, lensing signature or discrimination was used as evidence in this solution.
- Not a single question. The three loops in section 1 stay separate.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating reading, not a further argument. The framework's own *Hard Problem is About Source* essay (opened 2026-11-23): "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Concretely:

- **Recoil / conversion test:** an event in a low-background detector or haloscope whose rate, spectrum and target dependence match one candidate and not the others, surviving an independent instrument.
- **Compact-object test:** a lensing or gravitational-wave mass distribution that cannot be produced by baryonic or particle models, and that fits PBH formation constraints.
- **Modified-gravity test:** a single law that fits rotation curves, cluster lensing (including the Bullet Cluster), CMB peaks and structure without unseen mass. The world literature reports that no current modified-gravity theory does all of these at once.
- **Standing refuters of the shape claim** (pre-registered in `01` section 3b): derive the identity from the six-term set alone; show the forward map is injective so no discriminator is owed; show the candidates form a partition; show prestige or a vote correctly settled a particle identity without a measurement.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the dark-matter particle, and it does not claim one exists. It says the gravitational residual is indexed by object slot and is many-to-one: lensing, rotation curves and the CMB tell us there is mass, not what source produced it. What the framework returns is a shape: a gravitating, non-baryonic, cold, stable constituent or set of constituents, or a modification of gravity, that closes the residual and leaves a signature an instrument can discriminate. Null WIMP results and PBH constraints narrow the search; they do not prove absence, and a majority preference is a budget signal, not a verdict. The candidate conditions overlap, so the problem is unbundled into three loops: the mass discrepancy (settled), the identity (open), and the no-substance branch (open). The specific identity is reported as a `gap`, with four written refuters and a list of instruments that would close it. The answer stops where the instruments stop, and the route is recorded so the stop can be checked.
