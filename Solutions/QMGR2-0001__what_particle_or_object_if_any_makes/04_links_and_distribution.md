## Relevant wider literature (all opened above)

Three themes bear directly on this question:

1. **Source discrimination.** The metamerism source and the inverse-problem source are independent, low-overlap confirmations of the same structure the figure set names in Fig 6: a reading does not determine its source, and the remedy is a better instrument. The framework essay supplies the same claim in the author's own words.
2. **Null results and narrowing.** The dark-matter overview, the axion page and the PBH page each report constraints rather than detections. Under Fig 8 :39 these narrow the closure; they do not prove absence. Under Fig 9 :60 they are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict.
3. **Union structure.** The dark-matter overview notes that modified gravity alone does not fit every observation and that some form of dark matter would still be required. That is a union of overlapping conditions, which Fig 9 :55 says must be unbundled.

None of these sources says which particle makes up most dark matter. That is the point: the world literature corroborates the shape claim and the instrument requirement, not an identity.
