## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 13 :31-32 (amplifying chain), Fig 12 :121 (cycle with sign), Fig 12 :86-89 (threshold comparison), Fig 1 :13 and :22 (per-slot residual, aggregation loss).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a small shock is amplified by a positive loop, crosses a threshold, cascades through contacts, and is hidden by aggregation.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world reading that a reader might take away: the financial accelerator is the loop, a leverage ratio is the threshold, and a named recession is the case.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **distinction between tip and push**. The shorthand says "tipping point causes the recession"; the figures say a comparison names a crossing and does not supply the dynamics that reach it (Operators :51);
- the **sign and gain condition**. "Feedback loop" alone does not amplify; the loop must re-enter with a positive sign and sufficient gain (Fig 12 :121);
- the **frequency claim**. Nothing in the figure set returns "sometimes", so the step from shape to empirical frequency is an addition, not a derivation;
- the **slot indexing**. "Aggregate output fell" loses the fact that the residual was live in one slot before it propagated (Fig 1 :19).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: an amplifying chain is required for a small remainder to dominate, and averaging across slots destroys the live signal. That residue is worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
