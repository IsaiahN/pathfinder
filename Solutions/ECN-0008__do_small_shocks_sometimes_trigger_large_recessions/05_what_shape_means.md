## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0008 the shape is: a chain that compounds a remainder rather than damping it, a threshold comparison that switches regime, a loop whose output re-enters as input with a sign that decides amplification, and a live residual held in a slot that aggregation erases. It is not a named recession, not a named leverage ratio, and not a measured loop gain. A shape constrains an answer; it does not fill one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained for this question

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you have that is closest to the thing, then look for new terms that overlap it, recursively. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the starting vocabulary was "small shock" and "large recession"; the proximate new terms were threshold, loop gain, cascade, slot, aggregation.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the verification packet asks what they would expect to see if the shape were true, before showing the figure lines.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." So the claim is stated as a loop with a gain and a threshold, not in the framework's private vocabulary of bonds and slots alone.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that point is not reached. The remaining gap is empirical, so the search stops at the instrument, not at the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For this question that means a measured loop gain, a dated threshold crossing, an impulse response. The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :9-13). Saying "the financial accelerator is the loop" is an import from macroeconomics into the figure vocabulary, and it carries provenance and a shadow test or it carries nothing.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens the second point with the replicator thought experiment: each pass "adds content, not just constraint", and the content comes from the practitioner. "The content result is theirs, not the framework's." An expert who names a threshold variable is supplying the content. The figure supplies the shape of the slot the variable goes in.

## 4. Why the empirical reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing a large recession after a small shock does not tell you which mechanism produced it. A financial accelerator, a threshold in confidence, an inventory cycle, and a supply-network cascade can all read as "disproportionate response". Reading the mechanism off the aggregate output is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, such as a measured loop gain or a dated threshold variable, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the horizon language: "indiscernibility is not identity." A model that reproduces the aggregate path below the union horizon has not thereby recovered the cause. The lineage has to be kept anyway.

## 5. Q - Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the financial accelerator?

**No, but it forbids presenting the name as a figure output.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. The things this derivation uses, the cycle, the threshold comparison, the amplifying chain, the per-slot residual, are already in the figure set (Fig 12, Fig 13, Fig 1). Naming the financial accelerator is a **binding** from the economics frame onto held bonds, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs") or claiming the framework derived it.

So the correct form is: the figure gives the shape of an amplifying loop with a threshold; the financial accelerator is a candidate binding from the macro frame, provenanced as such and still unmeasured by this package. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them (Fig 8 :50-52).

### Correction to my own first pass

I initially wrote that the framework returns a shape and therefore cannot say anything about which mechanism is at work. That overstates the limit. The limit is about naming a primitive not held in the library. Here the relevant bonds are held, so the framework can say a great deal about the *arrangement* a candidate mechanism must have. What it cannot do is select among economic mechanisms without a ground reading or a provenanced import. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A survey of famous economists agreeing that small shocks matter is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an impulse response or a measured threshold, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the economic claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The bundle limit

*The Library of Babel* names the exact hazard in this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." Tipping point and feedback loop are such a bundle. Fig 9 :54-57 gives the figure-side rule. The two conditions are kept in separate verdicts throughout this folder.
