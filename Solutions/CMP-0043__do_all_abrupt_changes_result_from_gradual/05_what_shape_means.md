## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the case it applies to. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: the residual is a union of conditions, a global warning statistic cannot carry a slot-level signal, detectability is a property of the observing arrangement, and no failed search proves absence. Naming a particular indicator as the universal warning would be a name, not a shape.

The same essay states the unbundling rule that governs this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :55.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. The search is steered by the question, which acts as a tether.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The shape is reached when further search returns no new distinction.

Applied here: describing the effect means saying "an abrupt qualitative change with no registered precursor at a stated resolution", not naming variance or autocorrelation as the cause. The cause is what the mechanism search is for.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." An instrument that cannot discriminate sources demonstrates its own inadequacy.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The essay *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the sharpest form of the limit for this question: "A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach. You can only ever say nothing has been found yet." And: "Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet." A "no detectable warning" finding is exactly this kind of report.

## 4. Why the universal is the forbidden error

Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The universal "all abrupt changes" aggregates across mechanisms, systems and instruments. Under Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated", so the universal is not a well-formed single observation.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* gives the social form of the same error: "You may all agree and be wrong." A field that agrees on one warning law has a convention, not a ground result. Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it", while Fig 2 :40 makes the same agreement "disqualifying for a verdict". Agreement about a warning law is useful for coordination; it is not evidence that the law covers every branch.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I initially drafted section 3 to say that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any indicator for any slot. That overstated the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The mechanism names here are not primitives of the framework; they come from the dynamical-systems frame and would be bindings or imports if brought across, subject to a source gate and a shadow test. What remains wrong in a claim that names one universal indicator is not the naming itself but the missing provenance and the missing instrument that discriminates among sources. Both versions are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." Asking a well-known reviewer to endorse the shape would convert a check into a convention.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."
