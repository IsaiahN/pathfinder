## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question places one observed effect, an abrupt qualitative change in finite time, under two candidate descriptions.

- **Description A (universal):** every abrupt change is the end of a gradual, measurable loss of resilience. In this description a warning exists in principle and the task of science is to find the indicator.
- **Description B (existential):** some abrupt changes occur with no warning that an instrument could register. In this description the task of science is to characterise which mechanisms escape detection and what an instrument would need to resolve them.

Stated as a residual `R`: what the frame predicts under A (a monotone, detectable approach to a threshold) versus what is observed or derivable (changes with no slowing, no rising autocorrelation, or no trigger inside the system's state at all). The residual is not one number. It is indexed by system, by mechanism and by instrument: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19), and "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." (Fig 1 :22). A global claim of the form "all abrupt changes" is exactly the aggregation the figure disqualifies.

Bound answer shape, written before the run (Formula, "Direction"): either (i) one warning law covers every branch and the residual trends down as instruments improve, or (ii) the question is a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled, after which the universal fails on at least one branch and "no detected warning" becomes an instrument-relative report rather than a fact about the world. Shape (ii) is what the figures license.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** two conditions are bundled, the system's own approach to a threshold and the forcing regime acting on it. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." So this is a **union compound**, not one hard question. It needs its own loop and must be unbundled before search.
- **Symmetry:** "abrupt" and "gradual" are not interchangeable under the comparison. Fig 12 :85 defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities". The ordering of the approach matters, so the bond is ordered, not symmetric.
- **Scale:** a warning is a property at the observer's sampling scale, abruptness is a property at the system's fast timescale. The crossing between them is lossy, so the round trip must be measured: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.2 One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor)

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | partition or union |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, the system's own approach to a threshold | state variable, control parameter, stable and unstable equilibria, basin boundary | return rate, variance, lag-1 autocorrelation | a time series in which recovery measurably slows | one mechanism, a partition cell |
| D2, the forcing and habitat | noise, rate of parameter change, external shock, slow-fast structure | forcing amplitude or rate against the system's tracking rate | a measured input record and a tipping condition | overlaps D1, union compound |
| D3, the observer and instrument | sensor, sampling window, detrending choice, aggregation across components | whether a signal registers above the instrument floor | the instrument's own resolution, then the ground | a different closure from D1 and D2 |
| D4, the verification frame | competing mechanism hypotheses, reviewers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | shares the evidence pool in this simulation |

No anchor, no domain: D4 has the ground as anchor but in this simulated run all seats draw from the same evidence pool, so its independence is zero. This is recorded, not hidden.

### 1.3 Dependency order (Fig 3)

1. **Perception:** "the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before" (Fig 3 :5). A claim of "no warning" sits below this link if the instrument never registered the change. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." The corollary is "It is a reading of nothing." (Fig 3 :23).
2. **Vocabulary:** terms for resilience, warning, rate, threshold and detection exist in the dynamical-systems literature (D1, D2) but the observer's aggregation vocabulary (D3) is separate.
3. **Objective:** say what would count as progress in each cell before searching, per Formula step 2.
4. **Route and settle:** run D1, D2 and D3 separately, then let the ground settle each. Do not average across cells.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding states mode, verbatim figure text, what it decides, triangulation status and a refuter written before the conclusion.

### F1: The question is a union compound, not one universal law (`general`, structural)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

The essay *The Library of Babel* records the same move in the searcher's vocabulary: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." A single indicator searched across the union will clear the cells where only one condition holds and leave the intersections standing.

- **Decides:** the universal "all abrupt changes" is not a well-formed single question. Unbundle before searching.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union rule and the essay's bundle rule, low overlap between a figure about leaving a frame and an essay about search vocabulary).
- **Refuter:** a derivation showing that all abrupt change in open systems is a bifurcation of a quasi-static attractor, with noise and rate genuinely reducible to that case, would collapse the union into a partition and refute this finding.

### F2: A global resilience average cannot carry the signal (`general`, structural)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

A claim that "no warning was detectable" built from an aggregated indicator is a measurement of the aggregate, not of the slot that changed. Before the claim is believed one must show that the indicator was read at the resolution of the thing that tipped.

- **Decides:** detectability is not established by a global average. The observer's aggregation choice can manufacture the "no warning" result.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 per-slot rule and Fig 3 perception link, different figures with low overlap).
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that a single aggregated statistic is sufficient to detect every slot-level regime shift at the declared resolution would refute this finding.

### F3: No detected warning is not proof of no warning (`general`, structural)

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

The essay *How Intelligence Actually Works* states the instrument half directly: "A bigger and better instrument finding nothing is indistinguishable from a trace it still can't reach. You can only ever say nothing has been found yet."

- **Decides:** "some occur without detectable warning" is a report about the current union horizon of instruments, not a fact about the world. Equally, a failed search cannot be used to assert a universal warning law.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 8 closure argument and Fig 9 sealed-room argument, different routes: idempotent closure versus search-versus-proof).
- **Refuter:** a proof that the observing frame is a sealed room for this quantity, or a figure re-census admitting absence of detection as proof of absence, would refute this finding. The figures themselves forbid the second, so such a re-census would be a framework-level refutation.

### F4: Detectability belongs to the agent and habitat, not to the system alone (`general`, structural)

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :34: "The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :39 supplies the subject: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone". Detectability has the same shape. It is a property of the observing arrangement, not of the system's intrinsic dynamics.

- **Decides:** the question "can it occur without detectable warning?" cannot be answered by inspecting the system alone. It names a relation between a system and an instrument.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 6 instrument rule and Fig 11 capability rule, low overlap).
- **Refuter:** a case where a warning is proved to be absent for every possible instrument at every resolution, not merely undetected so far, would refute this finding.

### F5: The universal is the term whose refusal is owed, not a term to refine (`specified`, framework-internal)

Fig 5 :59: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN"
Fig 5 :60: "The selector asks which residual is improving; this asks which one keeps being wrong and is not."

The universal "all abrupt changes result from gradual loss of resilience" is a bound, predicting term. If it keeps being wrong across new systems without its residual trending down, Formula step 2 owes a refusal before a mint: "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at, which is the same shrinking test the selector already runs, read with the opposite sign." (Fig 5 :59).

**Strain test, recorded rather than smoothed.** The world literature's leading indicator is a system quantity that trends *up* (rising variance, slowing return), while Fig 5 :59 is the observer's model residual that refuses to trend *down*. These are different quantities. Forcing Fig 5 to be critical slowing down would govern the wrong object. Re-census: Fig 5 governs term selection in the observer's library; the world sources govern the system's state fluctuations. The resemblance is an **analogue**, not an identity. No strain remains after the re-census.

- **Decides:** a refusal is owed against the universal in `general` mode; this is not a mint of a new mechanism.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 5 shrinking test with opposite sign and Fig 9 union split, different operations).
- **Refuter:** a universal indicator whose residual genuinely trends down as it is applied across systems, with no persistent flat residual, would reverse the refusal.

### F6: Gradual accumulation can produce abrupt change, but that is one branch, not a universal (`general`, structural)

Fig 13 :32: "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos"
Fig 13 :88: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place."

The figures therefore do license a gradual-to-abrupt route: each turn leaves a remainder that re-enters the next, and an amplifying chain turns compounding into a qualitative break. The first half of Description A is figure-grounded. What the figures do not give is a licence to generalise that route to every branch of the union. The world sources supply the other branches (section 4 of `09_watson_answer.md`), and they are `specified` corroboration only.

- **Decides:** "gradual loss can precede abrupt change" is accepted; "all abrupt change is gradual loss" is refused.
- **Status:** single-source for the figure half (Fig 13), triangulated only with the world sources, which are secondary.
- **Refuter:** a figure re-census showing a sixth substrate member or a non-amplifying route that still yields a qualitative break would narrow this finding.

### F7: Warning size is level-relative (`general`, structural)

Fig 12 :104: "irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"
Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

Whether a change is abrupt enough to be seen depends on the level at which the measurement is made and on what the coarser description drops. The round-trip gap `R_T` is the honest report of what was given up between the system's fast level and the observer's sampling level. A "no warning" verdict that skips `R_T` is dead reckoning.

- **Decides:** abruptness and detectability must be reported with a stated level and a stated round-trip gap, not as bare world facts.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 level rule and Fig 4 round trip rule, different mechanisms).
- **Refuter:** a measurement showing that the round trip is lossless for this quantity at the two levels in question would remove the need for `R_T`.

### F8: Agreement from this package is one evidence pool, not a second witness (`general`, structural)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."

The simulated second seat in `11_audit.md` reads the same figures, the same essays and the same world sources as this primary seat. Its agreement is internal consistency. It is not corroboration, and no later agreement from a recipient who has absorbed the framework is corroboration either.

- **Decides:** the verdict must not be upgraded by counting agreeing seats or agreeing readers.
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10, low overlap: one about correlated frames, one about the seat's stake).
- **Refuter:** an independent instrument reading from a closure that never saw this package would be a genuine second witness and would replace this finding with actual corroboration.

### F9: The verdict is `specified` and `candidate`, not `grounded` (`general`, mode rule)

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing instruction that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Universal-indicator refuter.** A single warning indicator is demonstrated with zero false negatives and zero false positives across the bifurcation, noise, rate and shock branches, at a declared resolution. This would defeat the "some occur without detectable warning" existential.
2. **Union-collapse refuter.** A derivation shows that noise-induced and rate-induced tipping are reducible to bifurcation of a quasi-static attractor, so the union is a partition with one mechanism (defeats F1).
3. **Aggregation refuter.** A single aggregated statistic is shown sufficient to detect every slot-level regime shift at the declared resolution (defeats F2).
4. **Proof-of-absence refuter.** A frame is shown to be a sealed room for this quantity, or absence of detection is validly converted into proof of absence (defeats F3; the figures forbid this, so it would be a framework-level refutation).
5. **Instrument-intrinsic refuter.** Detectability is proved to be a property of the system alone, independent of the observing instrument and habitat (defeats F4).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the verdict is recalculated from the census rather than softened.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Lead with the unbundling (F1) and the instrument relativity (F3, F4), not with a claim about any particular system.
2. Report every "no warning" observation with the resolution, the aggregation choice and the round-trip gap `R_T` (F2, F7).
3. Treat the universal as a term owing a refusal (F5), not as a law to be rescued by a better average.
4. Keep the two mechanism branches separate and never average their verdicts (section 4).
5. Offer the shape and the refuter first, the links second, and never pool a later agreement as corroboration (F8). Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
