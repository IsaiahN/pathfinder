# Solution CMP-0043

**Question:** Do all abrupt changes result from gradual loss of resilience, or can some occur without detectable warning?

**Source:** complexity science.txt line 54

**Section:** 5. Critical transitions, tipping points, and resilience

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive verdict, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: no instrument reading settles the question in this package, and the derivation is simulated, checked for internal consistency against the figures rather than against a system.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Boundary.** Work was confined to this folder. Figure quotes are taken from the local canonical extract `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and cited as `Fig N :line`. World sources were opened over HTTP(S) on 2026-11-22 and are recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date, quote and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`. No personal emails and no receptiveness ranking appear anywhere in this folder.

## What an answer would look like

Stated as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): an answer binds the effect "abrupt change" to one of two shapes, a universal gradual-resilience-loss with a registered warning, or a union of mechanisms in which some branches carry no warning at the resolution of the observing instrument. The first shape leads to a search for one indicator with no false negatives. The second leads to a search for the instrument that could resolve the branch, and to the claim that an undetected warning is not proof of an absent one. The second shape is the one the figures license.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40), and "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make their agreement worthless. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## Status of this derivation

This package is a **simulated derivation**. The source essay states that "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This audit is checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
