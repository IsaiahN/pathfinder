## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that all abrupt changes result from gradual loss of resilience, versus the claim that some occur without detectable warning. Option A: assert the universal gradual-loss law with a single warning indicator. Option B: unbundle the residual into mechanism cells, accept the warning law only in the threshold cell, and treat undetected warning as an instrument-relative report. What each leads to: A sends a `general`-mode universal across a union it has not split, so it clears single-condition cases and leaves the intersections standing; B sends a checkable shape, separate per-cell verdicts, pre-registered refuters and a stated instrument horizon.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The universal is refused as a universal and kept for one cell. Not all abrupt changes are established to result from gradual loss of resilience: the forcing branches can produce an abrupt change with no change in the system's own return rate. Some abrupt changes can occur without a warning detectable at a stated resolution, but that is a report about the observing instrument and its aggregation choice, not proof that no gradual loss occurred. A global average can hide the component that tipped, no failed search proves absence, and detectability is agent-and-habitat. The three mechanisms are isomers over a shared atom set at `specified` status, with the functional-group claim as the weaker alternative. Every verdict is `specified` on the substantive half and `general` on the structural shape; none is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is asserting the universal over a union: it sends researchers down a single-indicator search that clears only the threshold cell and leaves the intersections standing. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the unbundling and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape is a constraint and a refusal, not a primitive and not a withdrawal of the threshold-cell warning law. A ground reading can reinstate a narrower universal for one cell, and a better instrument can move the union horizon, without removing any figure line.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. One indicator with zero false negatives and zero false positives across all branches at a declared resolution would settle the existential; an instrument discriminating a falling return rate from constant return rate under increasing forcing variance would name the cell; a union collapse reducing noise and rate to bifurcation would dissolve the compound; a demonstration that a global average suffices would defeat the slot-level claim; a proof that absence of detection holds for every possible instrument would defeat the no-proof-of-absence line. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
