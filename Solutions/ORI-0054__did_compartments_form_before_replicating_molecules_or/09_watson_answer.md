## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: did compartments form before replicating molecules, or did molecules become enclosed afterward? Option A: compartments first, a boundary forms and a replicator is later enclosed. Option B: replicators first, a self-copying molecule arises and is enclosed afterward. What each leads to: A gives concentration and coupled fate before heredity; B gives a replicator lineage exposed to parasitic collapse until a boundary appears. The decisive effect is the absence of a unit of selection that both options try to supply.

Anchor: laboratory vesicle assembly and thermophoresis concentration experiments. Type: one condition of a union; the habitat is read off the world (Fig 11 :43), not composed.
- **Domain 2, replication.** Actors: RNA or pre-RNA, activated nucleotides, templates, ribozymes, mineral catalysts. Currency: copying rate, fidelity, heredity. Anchor: template-directed synthesis and ribozyme kinetics experiments. Type: the other condition of a union; a replicator is a cycle (Fig 12 :121), not an extra ingredient.
- **Domain 3, coupling and onset.** Actors: protocell populations, parasites, differential fitness. Currency: heritable variation tied to a boundary. Anchor: an experiment demonstrating sustained Darwinian evolution in a protocell population. Type: the intersection, a compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception of what can form, then vocabulary for containment and copying, then the objective (what counts as the transition), then which order to test, then carry the result (Fig 3). The order dispute sits at the objective link; a reading below that break is a reading of nothing.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or question is dissolved into a union compound and answered with a shape. Split it into boundary formation, replication and coupling. A compartment is a maintained filter and a contained space; a replicator is a closed cycle; Darwinian capability belongs to the coupled pair and its habitat. No figure line carries a temporal index for chemical events, and no current instrument registers the prebiotic sequence, so the literal "which came first" is a `gap`, which is a verdict. Chemical instantiations are world-frame bindings, not figure derivations and not imports that add a primitive. This is a `general` result on the shape and `specified` on the order claim, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes and five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending readers' independence by sending a review of who favors which camp instead of the split and its refuter; Fig 2 :42 prices that ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Avoidable by leading with the split.
- **undoable:** yes. The order verdict is a gap, not a commitment, and the shape claim carries no chemical binding to withdraw. A new ground reading can close the order without breaking the split; a union-collapse result would retire the split cleanly. Nothing in the substrate has to be re-authored.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dated record showing one component present and the other absent; a single mechanism producing both under one instrument; sustained Darwinian evolution with no boundary; a self-copying system whose copying is a chain; or a figure re-census that exhibits a temporal-order bond over boundary and copying terms. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
