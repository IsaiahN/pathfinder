# The Tether's answer on ORI-0054

**Mode: `general` on the split-and-coupling shape, `specified` on the order claim, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question offers two sequences for the origin of the first Darwinian entity: a compartment forms and a replicator is later enclosed, or a replicator forms and is enclosed afterward. Stated as an effect, the residual is the **absence of a unit of selection**: heritable variation must stay tied to something long enough for selection to act. Both options try to supply that unit. The Tether does not pick one; it shows the question is a compound and returns the shape any answer must have.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the compound.** Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Three domains: boundary formation, replication, and their coupling. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
2. **A compartment is a maintained filter and a space relation.** Fig 4 :21: *"The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction."* Fig 13 :10 lists containment as a space state. It has no temporal index in the bond vocabulary.
3. **A replicator is a closed cycle, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121: *"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :119: *"A part that survives its own use."* Replication is a bond structure over the same atoms, not an extra ingredient.
4. **The capability is in the coupling.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A better membrane alone gives no lineage; a faster replicator alone gives no unit of selection. The second-order union is where Darwinian evolution appears (Fig 12 :104).
5. **No figure line returns a historical order.** Fig 6 :40: *"What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The literal which-came-first question is a `gap`, and that is a verdict.
6. **"Before" is a bond-type question, and the licensed bond is co-presence.** Operators :59: *"Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term"*. Within the closure the two cycles are co-present; a production reading was retracted after the strain test (`01` F5). A real temporal order lives only in geological world-frame scenarios, which need a source gate and shadow test and cannot be derived from the six terms.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that compartments came first, and not that replicators came first. Both are refused as figure verdicts.
- Not that the framework names fatty acids, RNA, clay or coacervates. Those are world-frame bindings onto the shape, provenanced as such.
- Not a dated sequence for the transition. No instrument in this run returns one.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` for the union claim.
- Not that the two literatures agree and therefore confirm each other. They share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40), so their agreement is one witness.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another review. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (exemplar mapping): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding observations are:

- **Dated-order test:** a temporally resolved record (microfossils, isotopic clocks, or a laboratory reconstruction with an observed sequence) in which one component is demonstrably present and the other absent.
- **Union-collapse test:** a single mechanism, under one instrument, that produces boundary and heredity together. That would collapse the union to a partition and invalidate the split.
- **No-boundary test:** sustained Darwinian evolution in a replicator population with no boundary at any scale. That would move capability back to the component alone.
- **Cycle test:** a self-copying system whose copying is a chain across generations, breaking the cycle reading.
- **Bond test:** a figure line that returns a temporal order between a boundary term and a copying term without an import.

The five refuters are written in advance in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and repeated in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

## 5. The honest stop

The endpoint (a compartment containing a replicator) is reachable by several paths, and the observation cannot discriminate among them. ## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not decide whether compartments or replicators came first. It says the either/or question is a union of two conditions that both had to hold before evolution could begin, so it must be split: a maintained boundary that keeps related molecules together, and a copying cycle whose output re-enters as input. In the figures a boundary is a filter with a direction and a contained space, a replicator is a cycle rather than a chain, and Darwinian capability belongs to the coupled pair and its habitat, not to either component alone. The framework returns that shape and stops; it carries no bond that dates one component before the other, and no current instrument registers the prebiotic sequence. Five refuters are written down in advance, including a single mechanism that produces both components at once and a dated measurement that licenses a sequence bond. Until one of those fires, the order stays open and the coupling shape stands as a `general` result, not a `grounded` one.
