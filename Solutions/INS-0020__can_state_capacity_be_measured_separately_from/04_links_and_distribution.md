## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The literature confirms the dimension problem but not the separator.** Wikipedia and Suryanarayan both insist that state capacity is multidimensional. That supports per-slot and per-dimension reading, but neither supplies an instrument that ranges capacity while leaving fairness unread. Suryanarayan's decomposition into informational and infrastructural inputs is a budget filter, not the discriminator the question asks for.
2. **The strongest dual-use evidence is concrete, not conceptual.** ICTD's Robodebt case is a case in two conditions: the data system worked as capacity and failed as fair administration, and the error travelled across institutions. It is the world-level instance of the figure claim that an atom's valence is relative to the residual.
3. **The index builders themselves warn against verdict use.** The WGI page says treat scores as estimates, not definitive ratings. A composite index used as a verdict is exactly the Fig 1 :27 error, a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces.
4. **Principal-agent theory corroborates the checker condition from a different pool.** Its warning that targeting measurable variables makes others suffer, and that complex jobs resist complete specification, is the economics version of Fig 9 :60 and Fig 12 :125. It is secondary corroboration and never overrules a figure.
5. **No world source flipped a figure verdict.** The only apparent tension, WGI's broad use in ratings versus its own usage advisory, was dissolved at the instrument level: a filter for budget is not a witness for a verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients by likely agreement manufactures that pool.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it is searching for is not a term it holds. Each user writes a refuter before running it and labels the mode of the result.
