## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among administrative library Γ, action a and citizen outcome o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time. At least three operands, one ordering, one threshold. A two-place reading (state, capacity score) loses the action, the citizen and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The citizen outcome is anchor-side and the agency is a frame. "Fairly" is a comparison across cases (Fig 12 :87), and a wrong is not reversed by undoing the action that caused it, so one operand is compared against a threshold rather than merely conjoined.
- **Scale:** the slot is a case, the ranking of "fairly" exists only across a population of cases, and the time index crosses the membrane between a case loop and a population loop (Fig 4 :5). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, so a national index is a transformation that loses information (Fig 4 :30).

On re-census they are separate: B is settled by what happened to this person at a moment, D by the persistence of a refusal-and-correction channel across cases and time. The anchors differ in kind, so they cannot merge. This seat also tested merging the administrative loop with the administrative library, since Γ sits inside the agency; it declined, because C is about reach and cost while A is about action and residual, and their overlap is a compound with its own loop. The two named conditions are a maintained channel to a settling ground (A and D) and a non-authoring checker that can refuse (C, with B supplying the differing closure). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57). A case in both conditions is not a harder case of either; it is a specific compound.

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's two-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *separate capacity from fairness by keeping a working measurement channel and an independent checker.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific case. Result: *collect better case data and hire an auditor.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into two generic nouns. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, "better case data" is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30) when the real failure is vocabulary or objective.
2. It loses the anchor identity. "Working measurement channel" does not say that the per-case outcome, and not self-reported fairness or a national index, settles the case (Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :27). Coming back down, it permits an agency to score itself with a quantity it produces.
3. It loses the checker non-access rule. "Independent checker" can be read as any internal review unit. The figure requires soundness through non-access (Fig 10 :42-43), not organisational distance alone.
4. It loses the dual-use finding. "Collect better data" is precisely the intervention that raises capacity for unfair use when valence flips (Fig 11 :49-51). The abstraction has no place for that sign change.
5. It loses the source-discriminator requirement. The abstraction cannot say what instrument would distinguish a lawful from an unlawful use of the same record, which is the actual `gap` in the answer.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
