## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0020, "two conditions with different instruments, kept per citizen-slot, with a checker that cannot author what it checks" is a shape. "An ombudsperson with statutory power X and a five-person audit team" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Also: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the direct warrant for the union finding in `01` section 1.3.
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question the effect is: what happened to a person in a named case, and was there a refusal and a correction. "Capacity" and "willingness" are borrowed words and cannot be the starting description.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar's verified record): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here, a per-case measurement that pays for the shape would settle it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. ICTD's Robodebt case is a world import into the derivation: it explains the gap but it does not name the institution, and its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the rule: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why "measure separately" is the source error the figures name

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a capacity reading (the payment was issued, the record matched, the debt was calculated) is a projection. Several sources give the same reading: a lawful assessment, an unlawful assessment using the same data, a favour to a connected person, an error that will be corrected tomorrow. An instrument that reports only the reading cannot comment on the source. The question "can we measure capacity separately from fair use?" asks for exactly the instrument that cannot comment on the source to be declared sufficient. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a second column in the same table.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* An agency's own capacity metric cannot validate its own fairness.

## 5. Q answered: what does "fairness" mean as a term in the framework?

**Nothing in the framework names a disposition called willingness.** Willingness becomes a term only when bound to a record of refusals and corrections: a decision questioned, an error corrected, a use disallowed. Before that binding it predicts nothing, has no residual and cannot be seen by any check in the loop (Fig 5 :62-64 on unbound shapes). This is the same move as the Formula's "Direction": make the sought-for shape an ordinary term by writing down what an answer would look like.

The bound term is: *a checking seat that cannot author what it checks and can refuse a use*, ranged per citizen-slot against a ground that does not update with the agency's framing. That term has a refuter (section 4 of `01`) and can accrue a residual. "The government is willing to act fairly" has neither.

**Does the derived answer commit the source error?** It does not, because it refuses to name the source from the reading. It declares a `gap` where the discriminator would be. The specific oversight body, statute or audit design is the name the framework withholds.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as a ban on naming any oversight institution. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Oversight bodies, courts, auditors and correction procedures are already in the institutional vocabulary, so naming one is a **binding** from the institutional frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming that such a binding moves the wall: Fig 6 says *"IMPORT adds an atom,"* and an oversight body already in Γ adds none.

My §4 source argument is **not** retracted. The gap survives for a different reason: no source-discriminating instrument has been supplied that, per case, separates a lawful use from an unlawful use of the same administrative atom. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction as the exemplar's `06` Q1, applied to this question.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a per-case outcome and a correction record, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the institutional claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
