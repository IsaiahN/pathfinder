# WATSON answer, INS-0020

**Mode: `specified` on the application, `general` on the structural two-condition shape, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Can state capacity be measured separately from a government's willingness to use that capacity fairly?

Restated as an effect: can an instrument return a low residual for "the state brought about the outcome" while remaining silent on "the person was treated fairly," and can the two readings be kept separate per citizen-slot? R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per citizen and per case, never averaged into a national capacity score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). Anchor: the per-case outcome and its correction record, a measurement that does not move when the agency changes its own description. Fig 2 :29 names the anchor type: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* "Willingness" is not an anchor until bound to a refusal-and-correction record.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a three-place relation among administrative library Γ, action a and citizen outcome o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones (Fig 12 :72, :87). Symmetry: "fairly" is a threshold relative to other cases (Fig 12 :87), and a wrongly raised debt is not undone by reversing the data flow. Scale: the single case, the citizen population, and the time channel across repeated decisions and appeals; the membrane is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :22, :30).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** "measured separately" is refused as a partition of one instrument. State capacity cannot be read apart from fair use as two independent columns; what can be delivered separately are two conditions of one state-and-habitat relation, kept per citizen-slot and never averaged. C1 is a maintained channel to a ground that settles each case from a source the measuring frame cannot move. C2 is a checking seat that cannot author what it checks and can refuse a use, with its stake declared. The same administrative atoms have no fixed sign; their fair and unfair arrangements are isomers over a shared atom set at the strongest reading and one functional group at the safest. Quick failure of fair use is channel failure, not ground decay; the chain usually stops at vocabulary or objective, so capacity investment above the break does not close it. The specific institution that fills the seat is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified` on the application, `general` on the two-condition shape. The state-capacity pool corroborates the dimensional and dual-use shape; the principal-agent source corroborates the checker rule from low overlap; no world source flipped a verdict, and the one apparent conflict was dissolved at the instrument level.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected reform. A fairness failure would be sold as a capacity investment (more data, more linkage, more automation), which increases the capacity available for unfair use; a C1 failure would be met with more oversight that cannot reach the per-case ground. Real people continue to carry errors that they cannot contest, while a composite score reports improvement. A high capacity score also becomes a prestige proxy: *"Prestige can fund an action... But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* The cost is serious but bounded and mostly reversible at the level of analysis.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the eight pre-registered falsifiers in `01` section 4, in particular (1) a national index that prospectively predicts a named slot's unfair case with no per-slot input, (2) an atom with invariant valence across a documented residual change, (3) an agency's own metric that predicts its own fairness failures at the per-case ground, or (4) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool that supplies a source-discriminating instrument and pays for it per case would also reverse the `gap`.
