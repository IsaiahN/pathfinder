## The question restated as an effect

Option A, respond: the bank tightens or deploys macroprudential tools while the consumer-price slot is quiet. This buys a chance of stopping an imbalance early and risks a false-positive recession and higher interest-rate volatility in the stability indicators themselves.

Option B, do not respond: the bank treats a quiet consumer-price slot as sufficient. This avoids false positives and lets the conventional anchor stay legible, and risks that credit and leverage build a residual the aggregate never registers until the bust.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step, Direction): *a decision rule that keeps the price slot and the credit/asset slot as separate slots, states which slot the chosen instrument actually contacts, and names the ground reading that would settle whether the response helped.* This is the sought-for shape written down so it can accrue a residual like anything else.
