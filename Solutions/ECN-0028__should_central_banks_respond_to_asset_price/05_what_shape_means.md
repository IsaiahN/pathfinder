## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the answer

The canonical figures README says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0028 the shape is: *a decision rule that keeps the price slot and the credit/asset slot separate, states which slot the chosen instrument contacts, and names the measurement that would settle the response.* The forbidden filler is a single word, "tighten" or "hold," delivered as if it closed the question. `R = separate slots + named contact + named ground` is a shape. `raise rates 50 basis points` is a name, and the framework cannot produce it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the held vocabulary is "inflation target," "policy rate," "output gap." The recurring nearby terms in the opened sources are "credit growth," "leverage," "financial imbalance," "macroprudential," "benign neglect." Those narrow the space without naming an answer.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as "a quiet consumer-price reading while balance-sheet risk rises," not as "a deviation from the Taylor rule," because the latter is vocabulary borrowed from one frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the two-stage outreach in `02_outreach_email.md`: send the effect description first.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." In this question the finish line is not a rule; it is a ground reading that stops registering a residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, neither of them inside the shape.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the operational stop: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A rule that survives every test is still a projection until the ground pays.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Here that would be an empirical result from a credit-cycle model imported into the policy rule, recorded as an import, not passed off as a derivation from the figures.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the limit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So the six-term substrate is a visible guess. It cannot be used to declare which of the two mandates, price stability or financial stability, is the real one. The mandate is a choice made by a legislature and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: naming the source of a reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The reading is the same for one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or "a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered that can comprise the entire color set." The paper's conclusion: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a low consumer-price index is a reading. It does not comment on its source. The same quiet CPI print can come from a balanced expansion or from a credit-fuelled boom that has not yet reached goods prices. Calling the first source "no problem" from the CPI reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (credit aggregates, leverage, valuation gaps), not a verdict read off the projection.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point for artifacts: "often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it." A quiet CPI print is an artifact of many possible routes.

## 5. Why no answer of the form "always respond" or "never respond" is a shape

- *Arrival of the Fittest* states the mode rule: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which. Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate." A universal "should" delivered without a ground reading is a verdict in `general` mode, the named output-layer failure.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the arithmetic of why a room cannot decide this: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." And: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average" (Fig 1 :22, quoted in that essay). A committee of inflation watchers is one frame wearing many names if all of them read the same CPI series.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* separates prestige from the ground: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A famous central banker's prior is a budget to try a rule, not a reason the rule is right.
- *They're Scanning Books* adds the representation limit: "A built agent cannot be surprised along an axis its type system cannot express." A decision rule whose type system contains only CPI cannot be surprised by a credit boom. That is F1 in a different vocabulary, and it is a second, independent reason for the multi-slot shape.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

And from *Arrival of the Fittest*, on prompts that attach the formula: "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. They are not measurements, they cannot settle whether a specific bank should tighten in a specific boom, and no part of them is labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Consequence for this question

The eight essays converge on one operational instruction. Do not send a verdict. Send the shape (separate slots, named contact, named ground), the branch condition on the mandate, and the refuter written before the run. Then let a reader check those against the world, and record their verdict before showing them the framework. That is the whole of `02_outreach_email.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.

**Falsifier for this reading:** if a decision rule can be shown to discriminate boom sources using only the CPI slot at the decision horizon, then the metamerism argument (section 4) and the per-slot rule (Fig 1 :19) are both wrong for this question, and the single-slot verdict is restored. This is written here, before the conclusion, and is carried into `01` section 5 and `09`.
