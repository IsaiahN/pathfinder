# Expert verification packet: the Tether on required biodiversity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a biodiversity or ecosystem-services researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, not `grounded`.** No ecosystem measurement is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a number.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A single portable quantity of biodiversity cannot carry the answer to "how much is enough under rapid environmental change". The residual is indexed per function, place and change, so a global average discards the signal; an actor's value is relative to the function being tracked, so a roster does not survive a change of residual; and the habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering. The acceptable answer form is four parts: name a function and its instrument, name the change scenario, enumerate contact out to the cascade limit, and state what would reverse the requirement. The ecology literature on insurance effects and the analogues in portfolio theory and reliability engineering support the shape but carry no universal threshold. This is a structural result, not an ecological measurement.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1

- **Statement:** the residual that drives the loop is indexed by object slot and is destroyed by aggregation. A single global diversity number is therefore not a metric of this residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Also Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **What to check:** is the quoted figure text present and does it say what is claimed? Does the number produced by the measurement frame stand inside that frame?
- **Refuter:** show a single number that predicts maintenance of every named function under every change at the same threshold, independent of which actors are present.

### C2: ecology-facing; checkable by a biodiversity researcher against their own work

- **Statement:** an actor's sign is relative to the function and the change, so a fixed "essential species" list cannot be transferred across scenarios; and the relevant habitat is read off the world by enumeration, including indirect contact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
- **What to check:** in your own system, does a species or functional group change sign between functions or between change scenarios? Does a count restricted to direct actors miss an effect that appears two or three steps out?
- **Refuter:** a controlled before/after comparison in which a fixed roster keeps its signs across a change of residual and explains the maintained function better than asynchronous response diversity.

### C3: cross-field; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 4

- **Statement:** ecology's insurance claim, financial portfolio theory and the engineering n-1 rule share a functional group (asynchronous components under a named failure) but not their atoms, so the analogy supports the shape and carries no number. Compressing the four-part shape into a one-line target loses the function, the change and the cascade, so the round-trip gap is nonzero.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :123 "A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **What to check:** do the three frames hold the same atoms or only the same arrangement? What is lost when a four-part requirement becomes a single policy number?
- **Refuter:** show that species, assets and grid components are the same atom under one closure, or that a one-line target preserves the function, the change and the cascade without loss.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that biodiversity is unimportant. The ecology sources report strong positive effects on functioning and stability.
- Not that a universal number is false. It is unreached from these figures and unmeasured in this simulation. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not a species roster, not a functional-group shortlist, and not a diversity index as an anchor.
- Not a derivation of an ecological number from portfolio theory or engineering.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measuring an ecosystem.
- Not an assertion that the eight essays were re-opened this session. Their quotations are carried from the exemplar and are not counted as world sources here.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A ground test of the compiled requirement at a new place, under a new change, for a new function.
2. A controlled sign-change test of the roster under a change of residual.
3. An instrument that measures the cascade extent at which further contact stops changing the function.
4. A measurement of the round-trip gap between the four-part requirement and a one-line target.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22 and Fig 11 :43, :44, :49, :51. Six short passages.
2. Name one case from your own system where an actor's sign changed between functions or scenarios, or say why none exists.
3. Name one effect in your system that would be missed by counting direct actors only.
4. Say whether the portfolio and n-1 analogues share atoms with your species, or only an arrangement.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
