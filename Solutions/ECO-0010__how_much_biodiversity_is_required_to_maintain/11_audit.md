## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (slot-indexed residual), Fig 11 :43, :44, :49, :51 (enumeration and residual-relative valence), and Fig 9 :54 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-part answer form in `07` §3: function and instrument, change scenario, enumerated contact to cascade limit, reversal condition.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the policy artifact the question asks for, a single universal biodiversity number.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the four-part shape holds do not survive the trip back down to a scalar:

- the function that was measured;
- the change scenario;
- the cascade extent at which enumeration stopped;
- the reversal condition.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the claim that the requirement is per residual and that a roster is not portable. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this simulation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "a diversity target summarises the shape" as an identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `06` and `09` now state.

## Strains and re-census

- **Saturating diversity curves versus the refusal of a universal number.** The primary initially treated the ecology curves as a candidate conflict. Re-census: Yachi and Loreau's threshold is computed within one trophic level or functional group under a declared response distribution, and moves with the variance of species responses. It is a per-slot threshold, not a cross-context number. Different objects, so no strain. Matches the canonical rule that a seeming contradiction means the domain was misidentified.
- **The cull objection against Fig 11 :61.** Re-census: the figure forbids subtracting the habitat, not removing an actor. A cull changes contact and is an introduction of a controlling action. Resolved, and kept visible in `01` F5.
- **Diversity indices against "enumerated, never composed".** Re-census: enumeration governs the habitat; an index is a recording of an enumerated list. Allowed as a recording, refused as an anchor. No strain.
- **Standing at the end: no live strain.** Three apparent strains were named, re-censused and recorded rather than smoothed over.
