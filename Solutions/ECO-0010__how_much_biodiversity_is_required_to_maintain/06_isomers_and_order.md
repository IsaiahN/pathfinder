# Structure, order and analogues for the biodiversity question

**Mode: `specified` on the analogues, `general` on the order.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The phrase "essential ecosystem functions" may be a partition (a list of separable functions, each with its own diversity requirement) or a union (overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before a requirement can be stated). The question also asks for an order: must the function be measured before the diversity target is set, or can the target be computed first? Finally, the ecology claim about asynchronous responses has analogues in portfolio theory and reliability engineering. Are those analogues the same claim under a different name, or a different claim that only looks similar?

## DERIVATION

### Q1: Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :4 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Bundling pollination, nutrient cycling, primary production and stability under one "essential functions" label produces a mixture, not a harder single function. Each function has its own actor set and its own sign convention (Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."). The correct operation is therefore unbundle first. The bundled number fails even to describe the compound it is meant to serve.

**Result:** union. **Mode: `general`.** Falsifier: show that one measurement instrument reads all named functions without residual-relative sign changes; then it is a partition and a single target becomes composable.

### Q2: What is the order?

- Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."
- Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- Fig 3 :23 "A reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."

Order: perceive and measure the function, acquire terms for what the habitat affords, state what counts as progress, then plan, then carry and verify. A universal target is a step-four artifact produced before step one. Formula step 7 keeps three questions distinct, and the order matters: SEARCHED first (is the per-context answer already in the closure and unfound?), then INWARD (sharpen the instrument already returning something), then OUTWARD (import a frame whose closure predicts the residual's shape). Importing before SEARCHED adds an atom that was not needed.

**Result:** measure first, target second; searched, inward, outward. **Mode: `general`.**

### Q3: Are the portfolio and engineering analogues isomers?

- Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Fig 12 :123 "A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

The three frames hold a shared substructure: *several components whose responses are not perfectly synchronized, evaluated against a named failure*. This is Fig 12's functional group: it fixes the kind of arrangement. The members differ in what they call the currency.

| frame | actors | currency | what the count misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| ecology (Yachi and Loreau 1999) | species in a functional group | temporal mean and variance of productivity | the richness at saturation depends on the response variance; it is a per-slot threshold |
| finance (Medo, Yeung and Zhang 2009) | assets | portfolio variance | effective size is much smaller than the number of holdings and falls in crises |
| engineering (n-1 criterion) | lines and transformers | continuity of supply after one named failure | the requirement is failure-relative; critical networks may need n-2 |

They are **not** the same substance. Under Fig 12 :117 they are Fig 12's isomers if, and only if, the atom set is held fixed and only the bond and currency change. Here the atoms are not fixed: a species, an asset and a transformer are different primitives. So these are members of a **functional group** (Fig 12 :123), not isomers. The analogy licenses the shape (asynchronous components under a named failure); it does not license the ecological number.

**Falsifier, written before this conclusion:** if one could show that a species, an asset and a transformer are the same atom under a single closure (that is, that the currency and the failure definition are identical), the isomer claim would hold and the analogy would carry a number. It does not. Single-source for each frame's own literature; triangulated for the functional-group reading across Fig 12 and Fig 11.

### Q4: What is lost when the shape becomes a number?

- Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."

Carry the shape up to a one-line target (abstraction), then bring the target back down to a habitat (concretisation). Three constraints do not survive the trip: (i) the function that was measured, (ii) the change scenario, and (iii) the cascade extent. What survives is a number with no referent. The round-trip gap `R_T` is therefore nonzero and must be recorded. Treating "a diversity target summarises the shape" as an identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 names.

## CONCLUSION

The bundle is a union and must be unbundled. The order is measure first, target second, with SEARCHED before INWARD before OUTWARD. Portfolio theory and reliability engineering are members of the same functional group as the ecological insurance claim, not isomers of it: they share the arrangement (asynchronous components under a named failure) but not the atoms. The analogy supports the shape and carries no number. The round trip from shape to universal target drops the function, the change and the cascade, so its gap is nonzero and is recorded rather than assumed away.
