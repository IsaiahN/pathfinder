# The Tether's answer on required biodiversity

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a number. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A policy reader wants a portable quantity of biodiversity sufficient to keep essential ecosystem functions running under rapid environmental change. In effect terms, the question bundles several functions, places and change scenarios into one slot. Option A (a universal number) is cheap to state and untestable at the ground. Option B (a per-function, per-place, per-change enumeration) is expensive and checkable. The figures decide for B as a shape and refuse A as a framing.

## 2. Pre-registered refuter (written before this answer was synthesized)

The shape claim is refuted if:

1. one diversity number predicts maintenance of every named function under every rapid-change scenario at the same threshold, regardless of which actors are present;
2. a fixed roster of species keeps the same signs across a change of residual;
3. a ground measurement under change shows that a fixed roster, not response diversity in contact, maintained the function;
4. a universal target computed before any function measurement predicts the outcome at the ground;
5. a genuine figure re-census admits a cross-context universal threshold as a direct figure conclusion.

Until one fires, the verdict is `candidate`. These are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 with their figure grounds.

## 3. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot, not per aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single global number averages away the function, the place and the change that make the question answerable.
2. **An actor's sign is relative to the function being tracked.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Since rapid change is a change of residual, the roster does not carry over.
3. **The habitat is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." The figure's own case puts the material residual three steps out.
4. **The bundle is a union and must be unbundled.** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." One "essential functions" phrase is a mixture of specific compounds, not one harder function.
5. **Management is permanent work.** Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." There is no reached-and-left threshold: the channel decay (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does") requires renewed contact.
6. **The answer is a shape, and the missing number is a verdict.** Fig 13 :133 "A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The framework does not name the universal number because none is in its closure. That is a report, not an omission.

**The four-part answer form:** name the function and its instrument; name the change scenario; enumerate contact out to the cascade limit; state the reversal condition. Anything that cannot be written in those four parts has not answered the question.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that biodiversity is unimportant. Four ecology sources report strong positive effects of diversity on ecosystem functioning and stability; the figure verdict concerns the *form* of the required quantity, not its significance.
- Not that a universal number is false. It is **unreached from these figures** and **unmeasured at this ground**. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not a species roster. Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
- Not that portfolio theory or engineering gives the ecological number. They are functional-group analogues (Fig 12 :123), not isomers.
- Not grounded. No ecosystem was measured in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

The anchor is the named function under the named change. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

- Does the compiled requirement predict the measured function under a new change, at a new place, for a new function? A failure here fires refuter 1 or 4.
- Do the signs of the enumerated actors change with the residual in a controlled before/after comparison? A failure fires refuter 2.
- Does a fixed roster, rather than asynchronous response diversity, explain the maintained function in a ground measurement? A fire here triggers refuter 3.
- What is the cascade extent at which additional contact stops changing the function? This determines the enumeration's stopping rule and is currently unmeasured here.
- What is the round-trip gap between the four-part requirement and any one-line policy summary? It is nonzero by Fig 4 :30 and must be measured, not assumed away.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** Per-function requirements exist in the literature; what is absent is one figure line, or one world measurement in this simulation, that closes the cross-context universal number. The stop is recorded with its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). Agreement among seats is one evidence pool and is not a witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a universal number for how much biodiversity is enough, and it does not claim that diversity is unimportant. It says that the residual lives in specific functions, places and changes, so a global average throws away the signal; that an actor's value is relative to the function being tracked, so a roster does not survive environmental change; that the habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; and that managed diversity is permanent work, not a solved threshold. The shape it returns is four parts: name a function and its instrument, name the change, enumerate contact out to the cascade limit, and state what would reverse the requirement. The literature on insurance effects and the analogues in finance and engineering support that shape, but they carry no universal number: their thresholds are per function, per response distribution, per named failure. A universal target is refused as a framing, and the missing number is reported as a verdict rather than left as a silence. Four refuters were written before this answer. Until a ground measurement fires one of them, the result stands as `candidate`, `general` in shape and `specified` in its predictions.
