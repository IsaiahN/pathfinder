## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a question that bundles two conditions: (A) what role an observer plays in quantum measurement, and (B) whether consciousness plays any part in producing a definite outcome. Within B, separate "necessary for a definite record", "sufficient to produce one", and "the privileged perspective from which facts are definite". Option A: assert the strong claim that consciousness causes collapse. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, refuse the strong claim, and leave universal absence open. What each leads to: asserting the strong claim delivers a term that at present predicts no distinct number, makes consciousness an anchor for a question whose ground is the record, and spends the independence of the people checking it; keeping the shape delivers a per-context residual, a union split, a bound candidate term, four refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where the figures cannot decide.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Linear evolution of system plus apparatus yields an entangled superposition of apparatus-record states; observation yields one definite record. Arity: two conditions bundled, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4 *"not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*). Symmetry: the von Neumann cut is movable, so "observer" is not a role fixed by the formalism; the question nevertheless treats it as one. Scale: microscopic system, macroscopic record, then second-order multi-observer scenarios (Wigner's friend and its extensions). Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Domain 1, physics of measurement.** Actors: system, apparatus, environment, physical record, multiple observers. Currency: unitary evolution, decoherence, Born probabilities, collapse postulate. Anchor: the experimental record (interference visibility, detector counts, Bell-Wigner S), which does not move when an interpretation pushes on it. Question type: per-context composition and settlement.
- **Domain 2, consciousness / mind.** Actors: first-person experiences, self-reports, neural correlates. Currency: first-person versus third-person data, psychophysical correlations. Anchor: none nameable for the causal-collapse claim; reports and neural data are instruments for the correlation question. Question type: candidate physical mechanism with a mental trigger.
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers, survey respondents. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54). The structural observer question and the consciousness causal question overlap at the word "observer", but their grounds differ. "Necessary", "sufficient" and "privileged perspective" are further distinct conditions with distinct refuters.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27); refuse the ambiguous "collapse" term before minting a new one (Formula step 2); bind the sought shape as an ordinary term (Fig 5 :65); introduce an instrument before asking for a settlement (Fig 6 :32); let the ground decide (Fig 9 :42).

Strain test: the framework's "observer" (a checking seat) and the physicist's "observer" (a physical information-storing system) strain when mapped onto each other. Re-census resolves it as a homonym across D1 and D3, not a figure contradiction (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the strong consciousness claim does not, and the positive absence claim is not established. The residual is per measurement context, not a global "observer"; the question is a union of a structural and a causal condition and must be split; the ground is the public record, so consciousness is an instrument here and cannot be the anchor; the claim "a definite record requires a conscious observer" is refused by automated detectors and by the Bell-Wigner record, while "consciousness plays no part" is `gap` because failing to find a role is not proof of no role; and "observer" names a physical record-holder in D1 and a checking seat in D3, which must not be moved between. The figures decide this shape and the ground. They do not select an interpretation of quantum mechanics, add a physical primitive, or settle the question. Verdict status: `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`. Per-domain verdicts remain separate: D1 `triangulated`, D2 strong refusal `triangulated` with positive absence `gap`, D3 `triangulated` on method and `single-source` on poll numbers.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, split conditions and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a preferred interpretation instead of a residual and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The strong claim is removable without touching the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No physical primitive has to be withdrawn, and a superseding packet can be sent. The `gap` is not undoable at will, because it records an instrument limit rather than a preference.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A registered trigger-variation experiment, a quantitative mental-trigger model with a distinct prediction, or a source-discriminating instrument at the level of the record would move the D2 verdict toward `grounded`. A term closing both union conditions, a derivation that the D1 record-holder and the D3 seat are one closure, or a figure re-census predicting a consciousness signature would reverse the structural findings. Prestige, polls and later agreement are not such instruments.
