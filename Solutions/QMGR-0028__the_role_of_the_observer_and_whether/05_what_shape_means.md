## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and the same passage says it cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (essay 5, read this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a per-context residual between a unitary prediction and a definite record; a candidate term with a specifiable trigger and a changed observable prediction; a ground that is the record; a union of two conditions that must be split. The name "consciousness" is not licensed by that shape. The name of a mechanism is licensed only by a ground reading or a provenanced import (§3).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* (essay 5): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* This is the bootstrapping convergence process.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. For this question the effect is "a definite record appears"; the cause "consciousness" is exactly what is in dispute, so it cannot be used to describe the residual.
- *The Library of Babel* also names the union hazard directly: the answer may be *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*, in which case *"You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. That is F4 in `01`.
- **Stop when the shape stops registering.** The essay's paradox: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the stop is the bound term in `01` §0 together with the pre-registered refuter: a design instrument, not an answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* (the SETTLE step, quoted without its leading step label). Here that is an experiment that varies the candidate trigger at fixed physical conditions (refuter 1 in `01` §3).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing "consciousness" from philosophy of mind into the physics frame is an import. It adds no physical primitive, and it must be recorded as an import; it does not move the wall by itself (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

The six substrates are a visible guess, not nature's own list. `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md` states the figures are metalaws and that the seat reads the ground rather than asking the anchor to decide what the figures already settle. So the framework cannot be used to declare which interpretation of QM is correct, and this solution does not try.

## 4. The instrument/source error, applied to the observer question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay 7, read this session) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a definite record is a reading. Several explanations (collapse, branching, hidden variables, epistemic update, consciousness) produce the same reading. Naming consciousness as the source of the record from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a preferred interpretation. This is the substantive reason behind refuter 1 and behind F8's honest stop.

The same essay's criticism of Chalmers is relevant to how this question must **not** be framed: *"Chalmers takes that frame and makes it absolute and unfalsifiable."* If "consciousness" is defined so that no observation could tell whether it is involved, then it is a preference (Fig 5 :62), not a term, and it cannot be tested. The bound term in `01` §0 is the discipline that prevents that.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid saying anything about the observer?

**No.** It governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the primitives of the physics frame (system, apparatus, record, unitary evolution, decoherence) are already held. The framework can state constraints on how they compose and what would settle the residual; it cannot name a seventh physical primitive called consciousness. So:

- Allowed and figure-derived: the residual is per context; the candidate term must predict; the ground is the record; the question is a union; the strong necessity claim is refused; universal absence is `gap`.
- Not allowed: deriving which interpretation is correct; asserting a new mental primitive; claiming a poll settles the issue; treating Wigner's provenance as a measurement.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document's own status

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (essay 2, read this session): prestige can fund an action, *"But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering famous names to agree that consciousness does or does not matter is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLM output can simulate an approximate answer, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and cannot settle it. This is stated on mode lines throughout the folder and is not a disclaimer to be removed at the end.
