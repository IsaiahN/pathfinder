## 0. The claim under audit, stated in effect terms

The question is not "is consciousness real" and not "which interpretation is fashionable". It is a residual with two distinguishable conditions that the question bundles together.

**The residual R.** Standard quantum theory gives two rules: linear unitary evolution of the state, and a collapse or definite-outcome rule at measurement. Apply only the first to system plus apparatus and one obtains an entangled superposition of apparatus-record states, not one definite record. The observation is one definite record. The gap between those is R. In the symbols of the framework, R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| per slot: what the library predicts for the record versus what the observation returns.

**Option A, consciousness causes collapse.** Add a term whose trigger is conscious awareness; the definite record appears only when a conscious observer registers the result. This leads to a distinguished physical role for mind, a sharp observer/observed cut, and the prediction that a definite record requires consciousness.

**Option B, the observer is an ordinary physical system.** The definite record arises from decoherence plus an interpretive choice (Everett, GRW, Bohm, QBism, relational, consistent histories); consciousness is not an ingredient of the formalism. This leads to the observer being any system that extracts and stores information, with no special mental term.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** The sought shape is a *definite-outcome term*: a term (i) absent from unitary-only evolution, (ii) with a specifiable trigger stated in physical vocabulary, (iii) that changes an observable prediction relative to a no-term baseline, and (iv) whose admission is settled by a ground reading. Writing this down binds the preference as a term so it can accrue a residual and be refused, instead of remaining an invisible direction (Fig 5 :62, quoted in F2). The pre-registered refuter in §3 is the test applied to this bound term.

**What is at stake.** Option A is not refutable by "most physicists disagree" (that is a room, F6). It is refutable only by an instrument that varies consciousness while holding the physical apparatus fixed, or by showing the claim predicts nothing distinct (F2). Option B is not provable by the same absence: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39). This asymmetry is carried into the verdict.

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: at least two conditions bundled into one question, (1) the structural role of the observer, (2) the specific causal claim about consciousness. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :4 *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Symmetry: the von Neumann cut is movable, so "observer" is not a distinguished role fixed by the formalism; the question nevertheless treats it as one. Scale: microscopic system, macroscopic apparatus and record, then second-order multi-observer scenarios (Wigner's friend and its extensions). The residual is indexed per measurement context, not aggregated: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

**Partition or union.** A union, not a partition. D1's structural question and D2's causal claim overlap at the term "observer". Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever that answers "what is an observer" leaves the consciousness condition standing, because the second condition still binds. The compound has its own ground and needs its own loop. A further union sits inside condition (2): "consciousness is necessary for a definite record" and "consciousness is sufficient or privileged" are different claims with different refuters (F5).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance: (a) can the system tell what counts as a record and tell it apart from what was there before (perception/vocabulary link); (b) does it have terms for what a measurement affords, i.e. what would count as progress toward a definite outcome (the middle link where chains usually break, Fig 3 :24-27); (c) what would count as a progress measure, i.e. a prediction that changes; (d) can it plan an instrument that varies the candidate trigger; (e) can it settle and carry. The question as posed jumps from (a) to "consciousness" at (b), which is exactly the offer of a term for an affordance without an instrument. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Refusal precedes mint (formula step 2): the existing "collapse" term is ambiguous between a knowledge update and a physical process, so it is refused/re-fitted before any new term is minted.

**Strain test applied before choosing a line.** The figures use "observer" for a checking seat (Fig 9 :51, Fig 10), while physics uses "observer" for any information-storing physical system (Proietti et al. 2019, read this session). Forcing one onto the other strains both. Re-census: D1 and D3 are different closures with different anchors, and "observer" is a homonym across them. The strain is named in F7 and in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, not smoothed over.

### F1: The residual is per slot; "the observer" aggregates it (specified)

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :13 *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*.

The live slot here is the apparatus-record correlation in one measurement context. "The role of the observer" gathers many contexts under one global label, and "consciousness" then inherits the aggregate. Wigner's friend and its extensions matter precisely because they show that different observers assign different state descriptions to the same record, so the slot is per observer-context, not global. The figure licenses asking *which* interaction yields a definite record for *which* context; it does not license a single global term named "observer".

**Refuter:** a derivation showing that the record residual is genuinely global, so that a per-context decomposition loses the only live signal, would collapse F1. This is testable in the extended Wigner's friend scenarios, where the disagreement is between contexts.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 1 plus the multi-observer results in D1). Mode `specified`.

### F2: The sought shape is held as a preference, not a term (specified, decisive for the framing)

Fig 5 :62 *"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term."* The line continues: *"It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."* Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

"Consciousness causes collapse" is, in the broad literature, a sought shape rather than a bound predicting term. The von Neumann/Wigner proposal in its classical form does not yield a numerical prediction that differs from a no-consciousness baseline in a registered experiment; it relocates the cut. Where a version does predict a difference (for example, that interference visibility depends on whether a conscious observer is present), it becomes an ordinary term and F2 lapses for that version. This is why the refuter below is written before the run and why the bound term in §0 requires a changed observable prediction.

**Refuter:** exhibit a specific consciousness-collapse model with a registered, quantitatively distinct prediction and a trigger specifiable without mental vocabulary. Then it is a term, accrues a residual, and must be tested at the ground, not dismissed as a preference.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 5 :62-65 plus formula "Direction" plus Seat Map :190 *"the claim most worth refusing may not be held at all"*). Mode `specified`.

### F3: The anchor is the record, not consciousness and not agreement (specified)

Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* The line continues: *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Seat Map :18: *"Where it estimates a fact outside itself it is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*

The question "does consciousness play a part in measurement" is constitutively about physical records and their definiteness, so the legitimate anchor is the record: interference visibility, detector counts, the Bell-Wigner parameter. Consciousness and conscious reports are instruments for that question, not its ground. Putting consciousness in the anchor position would make the ground update on a frame's opinion, which Fig 2 :25 calls *"pinned: it does not update on A or B"* and forbids. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

**Refuter:** a reproducible experiment in which varying conscious awareness, with all physical variables held fixed, changes the record. That would make consciousness a legitimate anchor for this question and move the relevant sub-claim toward `grounded`.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 2 plus Proietti et al. 2019, who define the observer operationally as any information-storing system, and plus the historical record that Wigner later repudiated the view, SEP notes 2024). Mode `specified`.

### F4: This is a union; unbundle before searching (general, structural)

Fig 9 :4 *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :27 *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (the full line reads *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*).

Two conditions must be separated: (1) what role an observer plays in the formalism, which is a structural question about the cut and about records; (2) whether consciousness has a causal role, which is a question about a candidate physical mechanism. They overlap in the word "observer", but the ground for (1) is the formalism plus record, while the ground for (2) would require an instrument that varies consciousness. Within (2), "necessary" and "privileged perspective" are further distinct conditions. Picking a frame first (for example, a favoured interpretation) and then reading the residual into it is the inversion Fig 9 :27 forbids.

**Refuter:** one term that closes both conditions at once, with a single specifiable ground and a stated falsifier, would show the split was artificial.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 :4, :27, :54 plus Seat Map :211 on unions). Mode `general` on the structure.

### F5: The strong necessity claim is refused; the positive absence claim is a gap (specified / gap)

Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."*

An automated detector produces a definite record with no conscious observer present; the extended Wigner's friend experiments go further and show that the conflict is between recorded facts, not inside anyone's consciousness. Proietti et al. 2019 write: *"the lack of objectivity revealed by a Bell-Wigner test does not arise in anyone's consciousness, but between the recorded facts."* That refuses the strong claim "a definite record requires a conscious observer". It does **not** establish the positive claim "consciousness plays no part whatsoever", because a failure to find a role is not a proof of absence; the figures say so explicitly (Fig 8 :39). The honest verdict is asymmetric: strong necessity refused, universal absence is `gap`.

A second refusal is historical: Wigner himself later repudiated the view, stating it was *"outside the realm of quantum mechanics"* (SEP notes to *Quantum Approaches to Consciousness*, reporting Wigner 1977). That is a provenance fact about the originator, not a ground measurement, and it is recorded as such.

**Refuter:** an experiment with a registered analysis in which the presence or absence of a conscious observer, at fixed physical conditions, changes the record would re-open the strong claim. Conversely, a theorem that the formalism forbids any mental trigger would close the gap in the positive direction; none is cited here.

**Status:** refusal triangulated (Fig 5 :53, Proietti, SEP); positive claim `gap` (Fig 8 :39). Mode `specified` for the refusal.

### F6: Polls and agreement cannot decide an interpretation (specified)

Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

The well-known surveys (Schlosshauer, Kofler and Zeilinger 2013, 33 respondents; Norsen and Nelson 2013, 76 respondents) report that very few participants endorse a distinguished physical role for consciousness (6 percent and 1 percent on the relevant item). Those numbers describe the community, not the world. By Fig 7 :13 they are a room, and by Fig 9 :60 they are a filter for where to look, not a witness for the verdict. The two polls share the same instrument and overlapping community, so their agreement is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40), not corroboration.

**Refuter:** a poll would become evidence about the world only if the question were constitutively about the community's opinion (Fig 2 :36). It is not; the question is about physical measurement.

**Status:** triangulated (Fig 7, Fig 2, Fig 9). Mode `specified`.

### F7: Strain: the framework's "observer" and the physicist's "observer" are different closures (named, re-censused)

The framework's seat language uses "observer" for a checking position: Fig 9 :51 *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Physics uses "observer" for any system that extracts and stores information. Mapping one onto the other strains both: a physical observer is inside the system being measured, while a checking seat must be outside the slice it grades.

Re-census: D1 and D3 are different domains with different actors, currencies and anchors. The physicist's observer belongs to D1 and is an actor in the experiment. The framework's seat belongs to D3 and is a position in a checking arrangement. The homonym is the source of the strain, not a contradiction between figures. After re-census the strain dissolves; it is recorded here so that a later reader does not silently collapse the two.

**Refuter:** a derivation showing that the verification seat and the physical record-holder are one and the same object at one level would require a new census and would change this finding.

**Status:** single-source for the physics definition (Proietti et al. 2019) and triangulated for the seat definition (Fig 9, Fig 10, Seat Map). Mode `specified`.

### F8: What the figures actually decide, and what they do not (general)

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 11 :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

The figures are a domain-agnostic metatheory. They govern the shape of any answer, the location of the ground, and the conditions under which a claim could be settled. They do not contain a physical model of collapse, decoherence or consciousness, and they license no seventh primitive (Fig 13). So they decide: the residual is per slot (F1), the candidate term must be bound and predict (F2), the ground is the record (F3), the question is a union (F4), the strong necessity claim is refused while universal absence is a gap (F5), agreement is not a witness (F6), and the two senses of observer are different closures (F7). They do **not** decide which interpretation of quantum mechanics is correct. Saying otherwise would be a mode error: Seat Map :281 *"An unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*

**Refuter:** a figure line that directly predicts an experimental signature of consciousness in measurement, or one that derives the measurement problem away, would overturn F8. A genuine re-census that finds such a line should be recorded, not forced.

**Status:** triangulated across Fig 6, 9, 11, 13 and the canonical figures README. Mode `general`.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The verdict of this audit is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Instrument firing.** A reproducibly registered experiment shows that varying conscious awareness, with apparatus, environment and detector held fixed, changes the definiteness or statistics of the record. This makes consciousness a legitimate anchor (F3) and re-opens the strong claim (F5).
2. **Bound term firing.** A specific consciousness-collapse model is exhibited with a quantitatively distinct, testable prediction and a trigger specifiable in physical vocabulary, so the claim is a term rather than a preference (F2).
3. **Union collapse.** One term closes both the structural observer question and the consciousness causal question against one ground, so the unbundling in F4 was unnecessary.
4. **Census collapse.** The verification seat and the physical record-holder are derived to be the same closure at one level, dissolving the F7 strain in the opposite direction.
5. **Figure re-census.** A figure line is found that directly predicts a consciousness signature in measurement or derives the measurement problem away, overturning F8.

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict re-run. Until one is run, no part of this audit is labelled `grounded`. Note that item 1 is the only one that is a ground reading; items 2 to 5 are structural re-census events.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Split the question before searching: "what is an observer in the formalism" and "does consciousness have a causal role" (F4). Keep "necessary", "sufficient" and "privileged perspective" apart (F5).
2. Bind the sought term in advance: write down the observable that would change, the trigger, and the baseline (§0, F2). Do not send a claim that predicts nothing distinct.
3. Name the ground as the record, not consciousness and not agreement (F3, F6). A poll is a budget filter, never a witness.
4. Refuse the strong necessity claim, state universal absence as `gap`, and refuse to treat "no role found" as proof of no role (F5).
5. Keep the two senses of "observer" in separate columns (F7). This is the most common silent error in this literature.
6. Lead outreach with the residual and the pre-registered refuter. Offer the framework only after a verdict on stage 1 is recorded (Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
7. Record the lineage: what was unreachable, what was imported, what would reverse it (F8).

Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the method and the refuter, not a recording of a preferred interpretation.
