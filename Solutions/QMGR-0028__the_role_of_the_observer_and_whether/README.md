## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that consciousness is or is not involved.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* Polls of foundational attitudes (see `09_watson_answer.md`) are rooms, not anchors.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier that holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in submission venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary and honesty

Nothing in this folder is a ground reading. The derivation is a simulation checked for internal consistency against the figures. The figures are a domain-agnostic metatheory, not a physical theory of measurement, so they decide the **shape** of an answer and the **ground that would settle it**, not which interpretation of quantum mechanics is correct. This solution cannot settle the question it reports on. It says what a settled answer would have to look like and what would move the verdict from `candidate` to `grounded`.
