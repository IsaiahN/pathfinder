# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the observer and consciousness in measurement

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural ordering: not `grounded`.** No experiment varying a mental trigger at fixed physical conditions is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The measurement residual is per measurement context, not a global property of "the observer". The question is a union of a structural question (what role an observer plays in the formalism) and a causal question (whether consciousness produces a definite outcome), and must be unbundled before either is searched. The strong claim that a definite record requires a conscious observer is refused by existing records and by the Bell-Wigner experiments; the positive claim that consciousness plays no part is not established, because failing to find a role is not proof of no role. The ground for this question is the public record, so consciousness is at most an instrument, not an anchor. "Observer" names two different objects in the two domains, a physical record-holder and a checking seat, and arguments must not move between them. The framework returns this shape, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. It does not select an interpretation of quantum mechanics.
