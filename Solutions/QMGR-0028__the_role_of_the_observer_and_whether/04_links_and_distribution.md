## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The observer is operationally defined, not mental.** Proietti et al. 2019 define an observer as any physical system that extracts and stores information. The experiment uses photonic memories as the friends. This is a ground-visible reason to treat consciousness as an additional claim rather than as part of the definition of "observer". It corroborates the F3 anchor finding and the D1/D3 separation in F7.
2. **The no-go results are neutral about which assumption to drop.** Frauchiger and Renner 2018 state that the theorem does not say which of universal validity, consistency, or single outcomes is wrong. So an extended Wigner's friend result does not by itself select an interpretation, and certainly does not select consciousness. It corroborates F8: the figures give a shape, and a ground decides.
3. **The poll numbers are a trap.** The 6 percent and 1 percent figures are widely cited as if they settled the matter. By Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* they are a budget filter and nothing more. Both polls use the same instrument and overlapping conference communities, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the split question, the bound term and the four refuters; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed a framework before being asked for a stage-1 verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if a consciousness role were real, before giving them the articles.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement. Ask for a failure.
- **Seat Map :163** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people likely to agree manufactures the disqualifying correlation.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the split residual and the bound term; (2) the four pre-registered refuters and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the stage-1 verdict; (4) only then, if the recipient opts in, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 is a method, which is permitted to cross.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away gives away no answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** the offer only works if the stage-1 output survives. That output is the F5 asymmetry (strong necessity refused, universal absence `gap`) and the F2 bound term. If a recipient supplies a quantitatively distinct model, stage 1 has failed on purpose and the claim is updated. That is the desired outcome, not a problem to argue around.
