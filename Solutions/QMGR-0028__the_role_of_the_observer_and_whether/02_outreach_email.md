# Outreach draft: check the residual and its refuter before the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the bound term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and the refuter in §3 are stated in the message. The message must survive a stage-1 check on its own, without the framework.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the observer in quantum measurement

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow question in quantum foundations: what an observer does in a measurement, and whether consciousness has any part in producing a definite outcome. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with a conclusion. I have one checkable output and a failure condition written in advance. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Linear evolution of system plus apparatus gives an entangled superposition of apparatus-record states. Observation gives one definite record. The gap between those is the residual. In the literature this question arrives bundled: (1) what role the observer plays in the formalism, and (2) whether consciousness has a causal role. Those are two conditions with different grounds. "Necessary for a definite record", "sufficient to produce one", and "the privileged perspective from which facts are definite" are three more claims that are usually conflated. I treat them separately.
>
> **The output I want checked.** The strong version, that a definite record requires a conscious observer, is refused by existing records: automated detectors register definite outcomes with no conscious observer present, and the extended Wigner's friend experiments locate the conflict between recorded facts rather than in anyone's consciousness. The positive version, that consciousness plays no part whatsoever, I do **not** claim. Finding no role is not proof of no role. That part is left open.
>
> **The bound term, written before the run.** A candidate consciousness-collapse mechanism would have to (i) be absent from unitary-only evolution, (ii) name a trigger specifiable in physical vocabulary, (iii) change an observable prediction against a no-term baseline, and (iv) be settleable by an experiment. As usually stated, the claim predicts no distinct number and so is a shape, not a term. I am asking whether you know of a version that does meet those four conditions.
>
> **What would refute my output (written in advance):**
> 1. A registered experiment in which varying conscious awareness, with apparatus, environment and detector held fixed, changes the definiteness or statistics of the record.
> 2. A specific model with a quantitatively distinct prediction and a non-mental trigger, so that the claim becomes a testable term.
> 3. One mechanism that closes both the structural observer question and the consciousness question against a single ground, so that my split was artificial.
> 4. A derivation showing that the checking seat and the physical record-holder are the same closure at one level.
>
> If any holds I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this settles the interpretation of quantum mechanics; that a poll of physicists decides anything about the world; that Wigner's later change of mind is evidence (it is provenance, not a measurement); or that a framework I am using contains a physical model of collapse. It does not. It constrains the shape of an answer and identifies the ground that would settle it.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Philosophical Issues in Quantum Theory" (Wayne Myrvold), section 4.3 on extended Wigner's friend scenarios: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qt-issues/
> - Frauchiger and Renner, "Quantum theory cannot consistently describe the use of itself", Nature Communications 9, 3711 (2018): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6143649
> - Proietti et al., "Experimental test of local observer independence", Science Advances 5, eaaw9832 (2019): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6754223
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is public at `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md` in this package's repository tree. Run it on a problem you can check against a ground, write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
>: [name]

| the move | figure-grounded reason |

| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Frames range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |

| Bind the sought shape as a term | Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* |
| Refuse the strong claim but do not assert absence | Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* |

| Provenance not treated as measurement | Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that."* (full line opens with that sentence) |
| No request for prestige or trust | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: Prestige can fund an action, *"But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Keep the two senses of observer apart | F7 in `01`: D1 actor versus D3 seat, different closures. Forcing them together is the silent error in this literature. |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* |

**Stage rule.** Stage 1 is the residual, the bound term and the four refuters. Stage 2, the three source links, is opt-in and only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
