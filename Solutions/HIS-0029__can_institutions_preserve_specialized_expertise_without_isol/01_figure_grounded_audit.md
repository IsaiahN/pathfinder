## 0. The claim under audit

> Institutions can preserve specialized expertise, that is, scarce, hard-won, ground-checked competence, without isolating the experts who hold it from public needs and public criticism.

Restated as a residual: an institution predicts that insulation protects expertise, while what actually arrives includes drift from the discipline's ground, loss of legitimacy, and failure to register unmet needs. The question is whether the two conditions (depth of expertise and contact with public needs and criticism) can be held at once, and if so by what structure.

The first pass tempted a trade-off reading: more insulation buys more expertise and costs more public contact, so pick a point on the line. That reading is refused at the census below. It assumes the two conditions share one currency and one anchor. They do not (Fig 2 :37). It is a union, not a dial (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 1. Residual, stated in effect terms

From the Symbols table :38-40: *"The gap between prediction and observation. The central quantity: everything the system does is driven by it. It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."*

- **R (arity).** Two conditions in tension plus the grounds they range against. A **union compound**, not one hard question with opposite answers (Fig 9 :54-57: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*).
- **R (symmetry).** Asymmetric and directed. Criticism flows toward the expert; expertise is answerable to need. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Public opinion is not symmetric with technical validity.
- **R (scale).** Three scales: individual expert, institution, public. The boundary between them is a filter with a direction, not a wall (Fig 4 :21).
- **R is indexed per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Here a slot is a specific specialty, expert, need or criticism, not an institution-wide "trust in science" average. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **R cannot be scored by the institution itself.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* An "engagement metric" the institution also produces is such a quantity.

**Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a **union with an unassignable overlap** (they can share facts and even people, but their anchors differ), and D3 is a compound that composes over both. It is not a partition of one question. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The overlap is a compound with its own loop and its own ground.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** The chain runs: (1) perception: can the institution tell what the need is and what the expert knows? (2) vocabulary: does it have terms for what expertise affords and what public criticism affords? (3) objective: can it say what would count as progress? (4) planning: is there a governance route? (5) learn and carry: name what worked, verify it, carry it. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The likely break is between (2) and (3). Fig 3 :25-27 explains why: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments. A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Most "public engagement" effort lands at (2) (a new office, a new vocabulary) and never reaches an instrument at (3).

**Strain test.** Fig 2 :37 makes human judgment the right anchor for a question about people and *"only an instrument"* for a technical question. Fig 11 :40 makes contact necessary for capability. Read together they might seem to strain: if public contact is required and public judgment is not the technical anchor, what does the contact do? Re-census resolves it: D1's ground settles technical validity, D2's ground settles legitimacy and need, and D3's job is to keep both channels reachable. Different objects, no strain. The apparent conflict is two anchors, not one anchor with two opinions.

## 4. Findings, one per domain, verdicts kept separate

### F1. Isolation changes the habitat, it does not preserve the agent (D1, decisive)

Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."*
Fig 11 :68-69: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*
Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

An institution that keeps experts competent by walling them off does not keep the same capability. It substitutes a new habitat (internal reviews, funders, administrative incentives, a homogeneous peer room) and inherits actors it did not choose. The contact with the phenomena and with public need that the expertise required is not removed; it is dropped from the reproduction, and its loss is invisible until the goal fails. Even on the institution's own terms, the "improvement" changes nothing because it does not change contact.

This does not say expertise can be improved by maximizing exposure. It says capability is agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39), so the unit of preservation is the contact arrangement, not the expert in isolation. **Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 11 :40/:66-69 plus Fig 2 :29 on the non-updating anchor; world corroboration from Granovetter 1973 and Polanyi 1962, low overlap with the figures).

**Refuter:** show a competence improvement that changes no contact with either ground and still increases capability at the slot. That breaks Fig 11 :40.

### F2. The two grounds must stay distinct; the either/or is a false union (D1 and D2)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*
Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
Fig 2 :37: *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Option A fails by cutting the channel to the technical ground and to need. Option B fails by letting public approval update the technical anchor: that is panel 1, coherent drift, invisible from inside. The right form is two anchors, each non-updating, with the frames ranging against them separately (Fig 2 :45). Public judgment legitimately settles "is this answer good for people"; it is an instrument, and an instrument can be wrong on axes it does not measure, for "is this result correct."

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 2 :29/:33-34/:37/:45; the two-anchor split is also carried by the world sources Polanyi 1962 and Wynne 1992, but they disagree about who counts as a peer, recorded as a world conflict in `09`).

**Refuter:** show that public need or human judgment is the proper anchor for technical correctness, or that the anchor can update on frame opinion and still settle anything. That breaks Fig 2 :33/:37.

### F3. A closed expert room is a mirror chain (D1)

Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*
Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."*

A guild that validates only itself is one long room. Tenure, prizes and internal citation are changes in the room's agreement, not new evidence. The corrective room must differ from the expert room (low overlap) and must be selected against the residual the expert room surfaced, not chosen first. Fig 7 :42: *"The chain approaches the truth only as the world runs out of ways to refuse it."*

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 7 :35-36 plus Fig 8 :32, two figures with different objects; world corroboration from Janis's groupthink literature and Granovetter).

**Refuter:** show a homogeneous expert room drawing only on its own evidence producing a genuine surplus against the ground. That breaks Fig 8 :32 and Fig 7 :12-13.

### F4. Public engagement needs low overlap and a residual described first (D2 and D3)

Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*
Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*
Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*
Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 6 :24: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

The surplus is real but conditional. If the "public" channel is the institution's own communications office, a friendly advisory panel, or a handpicked set that reads the same reports, the two frames overlap almost completely and the union adds nothing (Fig 8 :49). If the residual (this unmet need, this specific criticism) is not described before the engagement mechanism is chosen, any mechanism will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). And a single loud voice imported as "the public" is a wrong atom, priced above starting from primitives (Fig 6 :24). Convergence reached separately and a claim adopted from a peer look identical in the content; only the record separates them (Fig 8 :51), so provenance of every criticism is part of the design.

Also note Fig 8's undirected union collapse: putting experts and publics in a room with no described residual is a mute union, not a mint. The figure *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"* is conditional on low overlap and an aimed residual.

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 8 :45/:49/:53 plus Fig 9 :27-28 and Fig 6 :23-24; world corroboration from Funtowicz and Ravetz's extended peer community and Wynne's lay knowledge).

**Refuter:** show that high-overlap frames (institution and its own PR) produce a surplus at the slot, or that a mechanism chosen before the residual was described is still checkable. That breaks Fig 8 :49 and Fig 9 :27-28.

### F5. The ground does not decay; the channel does (D3)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
Fig 10 :28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*
Fig 10 :29: *"keep the anchor reachable."*
Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

The failure of an expert institution is usually not that the phenomena stopped settling questions or that public need vanished. It is that the channel carrying them went stale: pooled readings, saturated metrics, dropped transients. The honest reading of a failed channel is not "the public was wrong" but *"you have lost touch."* The design task is therefore channel maintenance, not the production of a participation report. And the first contact must carry the characterised residual, not the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :52).

**Verdict: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 10 :24/:26/:28 plus Fig 2 :29; world corroboration from Granovetter on bridging ties and Janis on group insulation).

**Refuter:** show that channel decay is really ground decay, that is, the anchor itself moved because frames pushed on it. That breaks Fig 2 :33 and Fig 10 :24.

### F6. A consultation that decides the outcome has stopped being a consultation (D3)

Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
Fig 10 :48: *"a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority"*
Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

A public engagement exercise whose result is predetermined, or whose framing fixes which criticisms are admissible, is not a convention for sharing meaning. It has become a verdict authored by the institution and laundered through a public room. Fig 10 :45-46 permits the design seat to author conventions precisely because they carry no truth value; the moment a convention decides an outcome it has crossed into verdict territory and loses that permission. A published statement that the institution "values public input" declares a meaning and does not ground contact (Fig 10 :49).

**Verdict: `specified`, single-source on the figure side** (Fig 10 :47-49; the world sources on public engagement agree about the failure but use different vocabulary, so this is one figure line with low-overlap corroboration, not two independent figure witnesses).

**Refuter:** show a convention that fixes an outcome and still yields a checkable verdict about the world. That breaks Fig 10 :47.

### F7. "Trust in the institution" is the wrong residual (all domains)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

An aggregate trust score hides which specific need or criticism is live. An engagement index produced by the institution and then used to score the institution is a frame scoring itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27); it is a filter for where to spend attention (Fig 9 :60), never a witness for the verdict. Verdicts stay per slot: per specialty, per affected community, per criticism.

**Verdict: `general` (structural), triangulated** (Fig 1 :19/:22/:27 plus Fig 9 :60).

**Refuter:** show that an aggregate self-produced metric can locate a live unmet need at a specific slot. That breaks Fig 1 :22/:27.

### F8. The institution is the structure between two states; the two forms are isomers (D3)

Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
Fig 13 :75: *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*

At the institutional scale, the expert and the public are two states. They cannot be bonded directly; a structure must sit between them, and that structure is the institution's methods and channels. This is a mapping of the substrate onto institutional terms, not a claim that institutions are made of space and time; the strain is named and handled in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. Over a fixed atom set (same experts, same public, same needs, same methods), an insulating form and a porous form are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. What varies is **type, not degree**. Both share the functional group *"expert-and-habitat in contact with a ground"* (Fig 12 :123); the qualifier decides which member.

**Verdict: `specified` on the mediator requirement (triangulated: Fig 13 :75 plus Fig 4 :21), `single-source` on the isomer mapping** (Fig 12 :117 figure definition only; the fixed-atom condition is a live strain).

**Refuter:** show a direct expert-public bond with no mediating structure, or break the fixed-atom condition by showing the porous form requires an atom the insulating form lacks (then it is composition plus import, not isomerism).

### F9. The specific mechanism is a gap, reported as a verdict (all domains)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

No figure line returns a governance mechanism, a ratio of insulation to exposure, a named participation ritual, or a number of public seats. A genuine re-census finds no line governing those quantities. This is a `gap` of the **shape-not-a-name** kind, not unreachable in principle: the figure returns the shape (two non-updating grounds, independent low-overlap frames, enumerated habitat, reachable channels, refusable conventions, per-slot verdicts) and stops. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). This package reports the gap rather than inventing an org chart.

**Verdict: `gap`, declared.**

**Refuter:** derive a specific mechanism from the figures alone, with a source gate and shadow test. That would falsify the gap and is welcome.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds. These were written before the verdict section and before any theory was offered to a world source.

1. A competence improvement that changes no contact with either ground still increases capability at the slot (breaks Fig 11 :40).
2. Public need or human judgment is shown to be the proper anchor for technical correctness, or the technical anchor can update on frame opinion and still settle anything (breaks Fig 2 :33/:37).
3. A homogeneous expert room drawing only on its own evidence produces a genuine surplus against the ground (breaks Fig 8 :32 and Fig 7 :12-13).
4. High-overlap frames (institution and its own PR) produce a surplus, or a mechanism chosen before the residual was described remains checkable (breaks Fig 8 :49 and Fig 9 :27-28).
5. Channel decay is really ground decay: the anchor moves because frames push on it (breaks Fig 2 :33 and Fig 10 :24).
6. A convention that fixes an outcome still yields a checkable verdict (breaks Fig 10 :47).
7. A direct expert-public bond is admitted with no mediating structure, or the isomer claim is broken by a missing atom (breaks Fig 13 :75 or Fig 12 :117).
8. A specific governance mechanism is derived from the figures alone, which would falsify the declared gap (F9).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict is recalculated. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Stop treating the question as a trade-off. It is a union of two conditions with different anchors, and one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57). Keep the domain verdicts separate.
2. Preserve the **contact arrangement**, not the insulated expert (Fig 11 :39-40, :66-69). The unit of preservation is agent-and-habitat.
3. Keep two non-updating anchors. Do not let public approval set technical validity (Fig 2 :33-34), and do not let the discipline's convenience set what counts as a public need (Fig 2 :37).
4. Describe the residual per slot before choosing an engagement mechanism (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28). Import differing frames with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :51).
5. Maintain the channels and keep the conventions refusable (Fig 10 :24, :47). Drop any exercise whose outcome is predetermined.
6. Measure the round trip (Fig 4 :30). Any condensation of this shape into an institutional policy carries a gap R_T that must be recorded, not assumed zero.
7. Report the mechanism gap as `gap`, not silence, and ask outside readers for a refutation, not agreement (Fig 13 :134, Fig 7 :35).

- `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: *"They outrank everything else in the repo: the code, the rulings and the docs answer to them... the quotes are the authority, not this summary."* Also: *"a reading that makes one contradict another has misidentified its domain, not found a flaw."* And the canonical limit: the framework says *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
- `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`: mode rule, the three gates of step 3, step 5 (*"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*), and step 7's SEARCHED / INWARD / OUTWARD split.

### SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD (kept distinct)

- **SEARCHED:** is a governance mechanism already inside the institution's closure and merely unfound? Some are: existing ethics boards, ombuds offices, published complaint routes, peer review. Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change."* Distribute the search and keep searchers independent (Fig 6 :50). This is cheapest and was asked first.
- **INWARD:** sharpen our own instrument. What is the residual per slot, and what reading of it is currently saturated or pooled (Fig 10 :26)? Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Existing engagement records are the proto-instrument to improve.
- **OUTWARD:** import a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), characterised in effect terms before looking (Fig 9 :27-28), with source gate, shadow test and an independence debit (Fig 6 :24, Fig 8 :51). The extended peer community and lay-knowledge sources in `09` are this move.

These three are not alternatives to be averaged. INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and are never read against each other (formula step 7).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so insulation substitutes a habitat and silently drops the contact that made the expertise capable. The two conditions are not one dial: the technical ground and public need are separate non-updating anchors, and the frames must be independent and low-overlap for a surplus to exist. The institution is the mediating structure between expert and public, and the two institutional forms are isomers over a fixed atom set. What fails is the mechanism promise: no figure line names the specific engagement arrangement or a ratio, so that is a declared `gap`. Strains after re-census: one live (the Fig 13 substrate mapping), one live but narrowed (the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim), none hidden. Mode `specified` on F1 to F8, `general` on F7, `gap` on F9, nothing `grounded`.
