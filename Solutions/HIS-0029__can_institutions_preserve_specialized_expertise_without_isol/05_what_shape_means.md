## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: *capability belongs to expert-and-habitat; insulation substitutes a habitat and drops contact; two non-updating anchors; independent low-overlap frames; the institution mediates; the channel must stay reachable; verdicts per slot.*
- A name, withheld: *a particular governance structure, a participation quota, a named review ritual, a ratio of insulation to exposure.*

The shape is answerable now. The name is a `gap` (F9 of `01`).

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Applied here: describe what you would expect to see if an institution had lost contact with public need, before naming any engagement mechanism.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here, the search for the mechanism is where the shape is composed; the shape is the legitimate stop.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: a measured change in contact at a slot, with the technical validity still settling at the discipline's ground and public judgment still settling legitimacy, would license a name for that slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and shadow test. The extended peer community and lay knowledge are exactly this move: import a critic who holds facts the expert frame does not, record where the criticism came from, and check that it explains a gap.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Mapping the six-term substrate onto expert and public as states is a visible choice; `06` records it as a mapping with a named strain, not a discovery.

## 4. Why "trust in the institution" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

An aggregate trust score or an engagement index reads the artifact (compliance, approval, participation counts) and names its source as "legitimacy" or "expertise." That is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. People may comply because they agree, because they are tired, because the form was mandatory, or because a vocal minority dominated the room. The reading does not distinguish them. What is owed is a better instrument, per slot, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* An institution that publishes its own engagement score as evidence of its legitimacy is a frame validating itself. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an institutional arrangement? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own reading

**I initially overstated this rule and the correction is kept visible.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. "Expert and public" are not missing primitives; they are actors already enumerated in the habitat (Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*), and "institution as mediator" is a binding onto the held network and channel atoms. So naming that binding is **provenance**, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains forbidden, and is not retracted:

- inventing a seventh kind of actor to fill the slot;
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*);
- treating the mechanism as settled without a ground reading;
- presenting the specific governance arrangement the framework returns, which it does not return at all (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).

Both the correction and the surviving prohibition are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The same narrowing appears in `06` Q1 and in the retraction log of `01` section 7.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that an institution is trustworthy is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried at a specific slot, let the discipline's ground settle correctness, let human judgment settle need, and let a differing frame enter a refutation into the record. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any institution, it took no measurement, and it cannot settle the historical question it reports on. The same notice is carried in `README.md`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11`.
