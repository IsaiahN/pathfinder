## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* It cannot name the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel* (read): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*

For INS-0039 the shape is therefore **not** "open budgets work" or "FOI laws fail". It is: *a disclosure, an action path that can use the disclosure, and an anchor that does not move when the disclosed actors push on it*. That constrains any candidate answer without filling in which instrument (open data portal, audit publication, asset register, procurement portal, whistleblower channel) occupies the slot.

A shape `SPACE(???)TIME`-style marker for this question is: `DISCLOSURE(???)SANCTION`. The `???` is the action path and the anchor, and the framework does not name them.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was "information asymmetry", "sanction", "measurement", and the terms that recurred in the literature were "publicity condition", "accountability condition", "monitoring and enforcement", "subjective versus objective measures".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The residual was described as "information added, outcome unchanged", not as "principal-agent incentive failure", so that any field could recognise it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks the reader what they would expect to see, before showing the figure text.
- *Library of Babel* on the compound problem: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the direct warrant for splitting transparency, enforcement and outcome into three domains in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test. An import from the political-economy frame (Lindstedt and Naurin's "accountability condition") is licensed as a corroborating description only after provenance; it is not a figure-derived primitive.

*They're Scanning Books* (via exemplar extract) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six. Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming "enforcement" as *the* missing thing would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

**Important narrowing for this question.** The "shape, not a name" rule governs a primitive not held in the library. Here the relevant atoms (disclosure, action, anchor, outcome) are ordinary institutional terms, not new primitives. Naming a specific instrument is a binding from the world frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a seventh term. What is forbidden is claiming that the framework itself selects that instrument, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :22, *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."*).

## 4. Why measurement is part of the shape, not an afterthought

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A corruption perception index and an objective corruption record can both read "improvement" while having different sources: the transparency campaign changes what respondents notice, or actual malfeasance falls. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not an average across measures. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read): *"the output is only as good as the validator... You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

## 5. Why drift is the failure mode, and why the anchor must be separate

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read): *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* And: *"The reference point both sides check against has to stay put. If it moves, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* The safeguard named in that essay is concrete: *"Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move."* That is the enforcement/anchor role in this question, stated in a different field and arrived at independently. Low overlap, so it is weak but genuine corroboration.

The same essay: *"Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."* Applied here: a transparency campaign that produces many agreeing observers has produced a convention, not a verdict (Seat Map :163, *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*).

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim. The ground is never consulted. The error compounds... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous endorsees for "transparency works" is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried (a controlled intervention with objective measures) and lets the ground settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And the essay's own note: *"I am not claiming that the Tether is a self-validating axiom system. Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the transparency claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction recorded, not erased

An earlier reading of this question could have said: "the framework returns a shape, so it cannot say whether transparency works at all." That overstates the limit. The shape here *is* an answer: it excludes the stand-alone disclosure lever and requires an action path plus a non-updating anchor. What remains outside the framework is the empirical magnitude, which is owed to a measurement, not to a ruling by any seat (WATSON 60: *"The figures are the ground for design questions; Isaiah is the anchor for what the figures genuinely cannot settle."*). Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.
