# The Tether's answer on biological versus abiotic chemical signatures

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, `grounded` on none.** This is a shape and a route, not a detection. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No experiment was run and no instrument reading was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A chemical signature is a reading, not a source. The same reading can be produced by a biological process and by an abiotic chemical process, so the source slot carries no live signal from the reading alone. Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Aggregating into one abundance is how the source slot disappears. Biological versus abiotic is a union compound, not one hard question: Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Both conditions can hold at the same reading, so a single lever leaves the intersection standing.

What each option leads to: treating the reading as decisive gives fast classification but false positives and false negatives; treating it as one bearing among several, ranged against a ground that does not update, gives a checkable discrimination with a failure condition written in advance. The second is derived here.

## 2. The shape

1. **The discriminator must be an arrangement, not an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :96 "Two entries can share every ingredient and be inverses of one another." An enantiomeric excess, a functional group whose presence fixes the outcome, a catalyst that survives its own use, or a cycle that closes where a chain cannot, are all arrangement-level candidates. A list of detected gases is not.

2. **One bearing is not a decision.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." The decision needs two frames whose evidence pools do not overlap, both ranged separately against an anchor neither can move: Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Two labs reading the same spectrum are one witness wearing two names.

3. **The habitat must be enumerated, not subtracted.** Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." UV photolysis, volcanism, meteoritic delivery, mineral catalysis, radiation and contamination from the instrument itself are actors. They cannot be removed by assertion.

4. **Describe the residual before choosing a signature.** Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" The framework returns the form a discriminator must have, not the name of a molecule. Oxygen, methane, a particular isotope ratio or an enantiomeric excess enter as bindings from the chemistry and astrobiology frame, with provenance and a shadow test, and carry zero verdict weight until shown.

5. **A null result is unreached, not absent.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" The honest report names the instrument, the budget and the deadline, and says the source was not discriminated at that budget. It does not say no life is present. Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does": degradation is in the channel, not in what would settle the question.

6. **Filters are not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A detection limit or a confidence threshold is a budget filter. It cannot by itself deliver source attribution. Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A panel of agreeable readers does not settle the source.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that oxygen, methane, chirality or any isotope ratio is *the* biosignature. Those are names from another frame, not figure-derived results (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that a null detection proves absence. A null is unreached at a stated budget (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that agreement among experts settles attribution. A shared evidence pool makes agreement the pool talking (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- Not that one more spectrum on the same instrument closes the question. MINT composes inside the closure, so it cannot add a missing discriminator: Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
- Not that an abiotic pathway can be subtracted by decree (Fig 11 :61).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles any source attribution.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source, and the spectrometer resolved the case by adding an axis. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. An instrument that resolves an arrangement-level difference between the two candidate sources under a declared habitat, for example polarization to discriminate biotic from abiotic spectral edges or microspectroscopy to separate metameric matches.
2. A second frame whose closure differs and whose evidence pool is disjoint, both ranged against an anchor neither can move (Fig 2 :29-32, Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone").
3. The standing refuter from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6: an abiotic process composing the exact bond-level arrangement claimed diagnostic, under a declared habitat and budget, with the same behaviour under the discriminating instrument. If it fires, the shape is narrowed or withdrawn.
4. A complete, closed enumeration of abiotic pathways for a declared signature, which would break the exclusion asymmetry (Fig 11 :61).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 6 :40 "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution." An underivable-in-principle case and an unreachable-at-this-budget case look the same from inside the frame; this is an open strain, recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and not smoothed here. Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room." A reported failure is more useful than a reported success, and this derivation cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a biosignature. It says that a chemical reading is a projection that loses the source, so the discriminator has to be an arrangement, an isomer or enantiomer, a regenerative catalyst or a cycle that closes, not an ingredient list. One bearing is not a decision: the decision needs two frames with disjoint evidence pools ranged against a measurement neither can move, with the habitat enumerated rather than subtracted and a null reported as unreached at a stated budget. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, a refuter written before the run, and the instrument axes that would close it. Oxygen, methane and chirality are bindings from the chemistry frame, provenanced as such, and carry no verdict weight until a ground settles them. This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a detection.
