# Expert verification packet: the Tether on biological versus abiotic chemical signatures

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astrobiologist, geochemist or biosignature-instrument scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, `grounded` on none.** No detection is claimed. This is a shape and a route. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A chemical signature is a reading, not a source, and the same reading can be produced by biology and by abiotic chemistry. Fig 12 :5 puts the loss directly: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* So a discriminator has to be an arrangement, an isomer or enantiomer, a functional group whose presence fixes the outcome, a catalyst that survives its own use, or a cycle that closes where a chain cannot. One bearing is still not a decision: the biological and abiotic conditions form a union at the same reading, so the decision needs two frames with disjoint evidence pools ranged against an anchor neither can move, with the habitat enumerated rather than subtracted and a null reported as unreached at a stated budget. What the framework returns is the shape any answer must have, a refuter written before the run, and the instrument axes that would close it. It does not name a molecule. Specific signatures are bindings from the chemistry and astrobiology frame, provenanced as such, and carry no verdict weight until a ground settles them.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 9 and 12

- **Statement:** a signature is a projection that loses the source; the discriminator must be an arrangement, and the question is a union compound rather than one hard question.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :96 *"Two entries can share every ingredient and be inverses of one another."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** does the figure text say what the claim attributes to it, and does the union reading follow from the fact that both conditions can hold at one reading?
- **Refuter:** show a reading that uniquely determines its source with no habitat context, no second instrument and no provenance, across all processes known to produce it. Or show the conditions are a partition, not a union, so one lever clears them. Either breaks C1.

### C2. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 2, 8 and 11

- **Statement:** source attribution is triangulation, not a measurement or a vote. Two low-overlap frames ranged against an unmoving anchor, with the habitat enumerated, are required.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :31-32 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Fig 11 :61 *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*
- **What to check:** are the two frames genuinely low-overlap in the proposed test, and is the anchor something neither frame can move? Is the abiotic pathway list enumerated rather than composed from a remembered checklist?
- **Refuter:** show that two frames drawing on the same evidence pool settle attribution as reliably as two disjoint frames, or that one bearing plus context suffices with no second bearing, or produce a complete closed enumeration of abiotic pathways for a declared signature. Any one breaks C2.

### C3. Practice-facing, checkable against instrument capabilities

- **Statement:** a null result is unreached, not absent; filters do not deliver verdicts; the name of a specific signature is a binding, not a derivation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
- **What to check:** for a declared signature, can an instrument be named that would resolve an arrangement-level difference between biotic and abiotic production, and is the null report stated with instrument, budget and deadline? Is any gas name presented as derived rather than imported?
- **Refuter:** show a null reading under a complete instrument set that entails no biological source, or derive a particular molecule as the unique necessary biosignature from the figures alone with no binding from the chemistry frame. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that oxygen, methane, chirality or any isotope ratio is *the* biosignature. Those are bindings from the chemistry and astrobiology frame, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 9 :32, Fig 8 :51).
- Not that a null detection proves absence. A null is unreached at a stated instrument and budget (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that agreement among experts or a panel of readers settles attribution (Fig 2 :35, Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :35).
- Not that one more spectrum on the same closure adds a missing discriminator (Fig 6 :21).
- Not that an abiotic pathway can be subtracted by decree (Fig 11 :61).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment. The derivation is simulated and checked for internal consistency only.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green cannot comment on the source, and the spectrometer resolved that case by adding an axis. Here:

1. Name one instrument that would resolve an arrangement-level difference between biotic and abiotic production under a declared habitat. Polarization to discriminate biotic from abiotic spectral edges (Schwieterman 2018) and microspectroscopy to separate metameric matches (Wikipedia, Metamerism) are candidate instances, not answers.
2. State what the instrument would return if the source were biology and what if abiotic, under the same habitat, before the observation is made (Fig 9 :30-31).
3. Apply the standing refuter: an abiotic process composing the exact claimed arrangement under a declared habitat and budget, with the same behaviour under that instrument, narrows or withdraws the claim.
4. Report a null as unreached with instrument, budget and deadline, never as no life (Fig 8 :39, Fig 13 :134).
