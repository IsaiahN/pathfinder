## The residual, stated as an effect

A chemical signature is a reading, not a source. The same reading can be produced by a biological process and by an abiotic chemical process. The question asks which source produced the reading, and the reading by itself does not carry that answer. What each option leads to: treating the reading as decisive produces false positives and false negatives; treating it as one bearing among several, ranged against a ground that does not update, produces a checkable discrimination with a stated failure condition.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A discriminating **shape**: a bond-level arrangement that the abiotic closure cannot compose, plus two low-overlap frames ranged independently against a fixed ground, plus a recorded name-binding for any specific molecule claimed. The shape is checkable before any particular molecule is named.
