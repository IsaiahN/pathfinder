## Two findings in the wider literature that bear directly on this output

1. **The same gases are available to both sources.** Seager, Bains and Hu (2013) state that gases produced by metabolism extracting chemical potential energy *"will always have false positives because geochemistry has the same gases to work with as life does."* This is the composition-level case of Fig 12 :5: the ingredient list is the half that is recorded, and the half that could discriminate is not. It is why the derived shape asks for an arrangement and a second frame rather than a new molecule.
2. **The remedy is an instrument that discriminates sources, not another argument.** Ulses et al. (2025) show that an O3-land degeneracy is broken by UV spectroscopy down to 0.3 micron. Wikipedia's metamerism entry gives the same pattern at human scale: metameric fibres cannot be distinguished macroscopically or microscopically, but microspectroscopy resolves them. This is Fig 6 :32 in practice: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Stage 1 must be answered before stage 2 is shown.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were false before giving them the papers.
- **Seat Map :163:** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39:** *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* When a reader declines or does not reply, record it as no verdict, never as agreement or as disproof.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the answer before showing any theory; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and a third stage)

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded first, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 again: only methods cross up. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground and a pre-written refuter for their own problem.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 is what makes it survivable. If one of the six refuters fires, stop and narrow the claim before sending.
