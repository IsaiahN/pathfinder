## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; what it can report is a narrowed kind of thing and the conditions under which the answer would move.

*The Library of Babel* states the same limit operationally: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0087 a **shape** is the form a discriminator must have before we know which measurement supplies it: an arrangement-level difference between two sources, plus two independent bearings on a fixed ground. A **name** would be "oxygen plus methane", "D-amino acids", "carbon isotope fractionation". The first constrains; the second fills.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For a biosignature, the checkable description is: what would the instrument return if the source were biology, and what if abiotic, under the same habitat?
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means the residual, the refuter and the instrument list must have converged on the same description.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A new instrument axis extends what can be perceived at all; a new gas name does not.

For ORI-0087, O2, CH4, enantiomeric excess and isotopic ratios are names licensed only by (1) or (2). The derivation itself licenses none of them.

## 4. Provenance is the actual subject: source, projection and horizon

*The Hard Problem is About Source* is the essay that maps most directly onto this question. It defines the failure: *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"* and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The green reading *"cannot comment on the source."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* A chemical signature is exactly such a reading: composition is reported, source is not.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the two horizons that make the honest stop possible:

- *"There exists a 'distinction horizon' for every individual observer, the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."*
- *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."*
- *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

That is the precise status of a negative biosignature search. It also gives the case split the figures alone leave implicit: where a trace exists, instruments can eventually restore the distinction (the lab-grown diamond case, where isotope ratios did it); where no trace exists, only provenance from outside the projection can settle it. The same essay warns that *"The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* This corroborates Fig 8 :39, *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5. The direction limit: describe the residual first

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the trap in transmission terms: context, subtext and pretext decide what a reading means, and none of them decides whether it is true. The same failure appears for a biosignature. A terran pretext makes an oxygen-rich atmosphere look like life before any abiotic pathway has been enumerated. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Seat Map :192 adds the hard version: a frame's sought-for shape is a preference, not a held claim, so its own checks cannot see it. The remedy is to bind the shape as an ordinary term and write its refuter before the run.

The National Research Council (2007), an independent world source, reports the field-level version: life-detection experiments have been criticized as too geocentric, and the search has been governed by a model based on terran life. That is a pretext imported before the residual was described.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For ORI-0087 this rules out two shortcuts: a list of famous reviewers who endorse the shape, and a list of recipients selected because they are likely to agree. Both buy votes, not bearings.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of a biosignature and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. What this means for the question

The canonical reading of "shape" gives an honest answer to ORI-0087: the shape of a discriminator is available now (arrangement-level difference, two low-overlap frames, an unmoving anchor, an enumerated habitat, a null reported as unreached), but the name of any particular biosignature is not. Names enter as bindings from the chemistry and astrobiology frame with provenance and a shadow test, and they carry zero verdict weight until a ground settles them. Indiscernibility is not identity, and a projection below the union horizon is still a projection.
