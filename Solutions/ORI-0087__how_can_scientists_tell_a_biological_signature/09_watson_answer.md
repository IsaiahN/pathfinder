## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about distinguishing a biological signature from an abiotic chemical process. Option A: treat the reading as decisive and name a signature (oxygen, methane, chirality, an isotope ratio). Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and refuse the specific names until a ground settles them. What each leads to: A gives fast classification but false positives when abiotic chemistry mimics life and false negatives when biology does not leave the expected reading, and it spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives a checkable discrimination with a failure condition written in advance, an open slot for any particular molecule, and an instrument list that would close it.

- **D3, the habitat domain.** Actors: light, water, temperature, minerals, radiation, delivery, decay, contamination. Currency: contact and cascades. Anchor: the world, enumerated rather than composed. Union with D2; conditions co-bind.

Every domain names an anchor, so none is a gap after this census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific names do not. A signature is a projection that loses its source, so source attribution requires an arrangement-level discriminator (an isomer, enantiomer, functional group, regenerative catalyst or closed cycle), two frames with disjoint evidence pools ranged against an anchor neither can move, an enumerated habitat, a null reported as unreached at a stated budget, and filters kept distinct from witnesses. Oxygen, methane, chirality and isotopic ratios are bindings from the chemistry and astrobiology frame, provenanced and shadow-tested, carrying zero verdict weight until a ground settles them. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest outreach point is the pre-registered refuter: an abiotic process composing the exact claimed arrangement under a declared habitat and budget would narrow or withdraw the claim.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet records mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, or by sending the full framework before the verdict is recorded. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Every specific name is a removable binding; the shape does not depend on any of them. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A claim already sent can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no figure line has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that resolves an arrangement-level difference between biotic and abiotic production under a declared habitat would move the attribution; an abiotic process composing the exact claimed arrangement under a declared budget would narrow the shape; a complete closed enumeration of abiotic pathways would break the exclusion asymmetry; a null under a complete instrument set entailing no biological source would break the null rule. Prestige, endorsement and pooled agreement are none of these. Until one fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
