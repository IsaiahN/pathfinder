## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Symmetry: candidate readings are isomers, not spellings; the naming is symmetric under a symmetric bond but the arrangement is not (Fig 12 :92). Scale: per slot, per room, across differing closures.

- **D3 Frames and independence.** Actors: frames with differing closures. Currency: independence and surplus. Anchor: the ground. Admitted.
- **D4 Convention and meaning.** Actors: seats and conventions. Currency: shared meaning versus verdict. Anchor: the seam made statable. Admitted.
- **X1 "true meaning" free of ground.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "most likely reading" as sole cause.** Absorbed into D2 as a budget filter.

Union, not partition: within-room variation and between-frame difference overlap and have different instruments. Dependency order: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The vocabulary-to-objective link is the one with no instrument.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 12 :117/:5/:140 (isomer, bond, negation), Fig 2 :40/:41 (convention valence) and Fig 9 :40/:42 (reversible cuts, ground decides).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2 and `08` §1: keep readings per slot, hold them under a convention, narrow reversibly, refuse by adding, let the ground settle, no universal algorithm.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete system design that holds k readings, scores them, and deploys one.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing** becomes a single global confidence whenever an implementation averages over slots, which is exactly Fig 1 :22's failure;
- **the convention/verdict distinction** collapses whenever the shared meaning is used to decide an outcome, which is Fig 10 :47's failure;
- **the refusal operator** disappears whenever a dropped reading is overwritten instead of negated, so never-held and held-and-given-up become indistinguishable (Fig 12 :140, Fig 6 :42);
- **the gap itself** is erased whenever a deployment threshold is described as if the framework returned it (Fig 9 :32).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must be recorded for each reading, and the ground alone settles. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
