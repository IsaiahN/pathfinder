## Restating the question as an effect

The signal is fixed. The thing that varies is the library's response to it. In effect terms: `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per object slot. A system "represents ambiguity" when more than one candidate molecule φ_i carries live mass `R⁺_s` on the same slot and none has been settled by the ground. A system "prematurely collapses" when it sets all but one to zero, or averages the slot residuals into one global number, or promotes an ungrounded meaning to a verdict, before the ground has paid. The answer is bound as an ordinary term: *a ranked, reversible, per-slot set of held candidates with the convention/verdict distinction intact* (formula "Direction" step). That is what an answer would look like, so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the framework returns a universal algorithm, a holding-time constant, or a probability threshold for when to collapse. That is a declared `gap`, not silence.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence."* A list of agreeable readers cannot settle a shape claim.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the exact correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **A verifier who holds the interiors cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR or CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
