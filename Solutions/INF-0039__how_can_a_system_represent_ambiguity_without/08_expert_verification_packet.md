# Expert verification packet: holding ambiguity without premature collapse

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in semantics, cognition or inference can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal algorithm. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Ambiguity is not one interpretation with a confidence attached. It is a per-slot set of candidate readings, each a distinct bonded arrangement of the same held atoms, each carrying its own residual. A system can hold them without premature collapse by three moves: keep the residual indexed per slot instead of aggregating it; let agreement author a convention that makes the disagreement statable while denying it verdict standing; and narrow only with ranked, reversible cuts, refusing a reading by adding a negation to the record rather than deleting it. The ground, a source-discriminating contact, is the only selector. The framework returns this shape and no universal holding time, collapse threshold or encoding; that is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 12

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, and candidate readings are isomers over held atoms rather than spellings of one reading. Agreement is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
  - Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* and Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
  - Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*.
- **Refuter:** (a) two readings on one slot both carry live error and the correct ground-verified behaviour is to report exactly one with no ranked remainder; (b) two readings with identical atoms and different bonds are behaviourally identical under every ground test; (c) agreement among frames sharing one evidence pool legitimately settles a reading about the world.

### C2 — framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 10, 9, 6 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** a convention makes the disagreement statable without grounding it; narrowing uses ranked reversible cuts; a refusal is a negation that adds to the record and lowers the reach.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 10 :16 *"the convention does not fix the bug"* and Fig 10 :17 *"it makes the bug statable"*; Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
  - Fig 9 :40 *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible"* and Fig 9 :42 *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*.
  - Fig 12 :140 *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate"*; Fig 6 :42 *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."*; Operators :58 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.
- **What to check:** is the convention/verdict distinction actually load-bearing, or can the same multi-reading state be ranged against the ground with no shared meaning? Is the refusal operator required to keep never-held distinct from held-and-given-up?
- **Refuter:** (a) the system ranges multiple readings correctly with no convention making the disagreement statable; (b) a reading is dropped without any refusal operator and the record still distinguishes never-held from held-and-given-up; (c) a narrowing cut is irreversible and a later ground reading shows the kept reading was wrong.

### C3 — union and limit; checkable by reading Figures 7, 8, 9 and 13

- **Statement:** within-frame variation and between-frame difference are a union with independence as the parameter, not a count of readings; the framework returns a shape and a stop, not a universal algorithm.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
  - Fig 8 :49 *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*
  - Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.
- **What to check:** does adding a reading from a differing closure actually buy discriminating power that a same-pool reading does not? Is there a verbatim figure line that returns a holding time, threshold or encoding?
- **Refuter:** (a) a tenth reading from the same evidence pool buys as much as one from a genuinely differing closure, measured against the same ground; (b) a figure line is produced that selects a universal collapse rule. None was found after the census in `01` §1 and the re-census in `01` §5.

### Cross-cutting falsifier

If a discriminating instrument exists that resolves the candidate readings on a slot, or if the stem fails to distinguish a held-and-refused term from a never-held one, the whole shape is narrowed accordingly. Those are ground readings, not votes.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an algorithm, parameter, probability threshold or universal encoding for ambiguity. That is a declared `gap`.
- Not that readings should be held forever. The ground settles, then the settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level.
- Not that agreement among readers settles anything. Agreement is disqualifying for a verdict.
- Not that a probability ranking is a verdict. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that the lower-overlap analogies derive the answer. They corroborate the mechanism shape at other scales; the actors, currency and anchor change.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken for this packet.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating contact, not further agreement:

1. **Discriminator test:** an instrument that distinguishes the candidate arrangements on one slot rather than merely scoring them.
2. **Bond test:** identical atoms plus different bonds yielding different behaviour under that instrument.
3. **Refusal and independence tests:** a refusal that leaves the record intact while lowering the reach, and a differing-closure reading that buys more than a same-pool reading.
