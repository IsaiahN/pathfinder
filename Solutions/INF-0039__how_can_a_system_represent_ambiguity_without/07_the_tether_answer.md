# The Tether's answer: how a system can hold ambiguity

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal algorithm or threshold. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A signal is fixed; the library's response varies. The system must keep several readings viable until a discriminating contact arrives, yet it must also be able to act and eventually settle. The residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per object slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Premature collapse is any operation that reduces the live set before the ground pays: averaging across slots, promoting a convention into a verdict, or treating a room's agreement as a decision. The question is a **union** of two conditions, within-frame variation and between-frame difference (Fig 9 :55), with verdicts kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep the residual indexed per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Several readings on one slot each carry their own live mass. A global confidence number near zero can coexist with one live slot; that is a legal state, not an inert one.

2. **Store the arrangement, not the ingredient list.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Readings of one signal are isomers over the held atoms. What must be preserved is the typed bond and the residual, because the bond is not recoverable from the operands.

3. **Let agreement build a convention, never a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Fig 10 :16-17: *"the convention does not fix the bug"*, *"it makes the bug statable"*. The convention makes the incompatible readings statable and violable; it does not choose between them. Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

4. **Narrow with reversible cuts; the ground alone settles.** Fig 9 :40: *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible"*; Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."* Settling is necessary and not sufficient. A held reading is refused by a negation, not deleted: Fig 12 :140: *"A vocabulary that cannot refuse can only accumulate"*, and Fig 6 :42: *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall."*

5. **Buy independence, not count.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Adding readings from the same evidence pool adds spelling, not search. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

6. **No standing preference for a reading survives a new residual.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Probability, simplicity and familiarity are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

7. **Report a shape and a stop, not a universal algorithm.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. No figure line selects a holding time, a collapse threshold or a universal encoding. That is a `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not an algorithm, a parameter, a threshold or an encoding for ambiguity. Those are names the framework says it cannot return.
- Not that all readings should be held forever. Holding is until the ground pays; after that, the settled molecule becomes an atom for the next level (Fig 12 :104).
- Not that agreement among readers settles the claim. Agreement is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :40-41).
- Not that a probability ranking is a verdict. It is a filter for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that this derivation settles the question. It is a simulation; the framework's own rule is that the output is only as good as the validator and a frame cannot validate itself.
- Not that the multistable-perception or HMM analogues derive the answer. They corroborate the mechanism shape from genuinely lower-overlap fields; the kernel is the same but the actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :20).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating contact, not a further argument. The world-side lesson from *The Hard Problem is About Source* is that a reading cannot comment on its own source. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Discriminator test:** does an instrument exist that distinguishes the candidate arrangements on the same slot, rather than merely scoring them? If yes, it names the reading.
- **Bond test:** do two readings with identical atoms and different bonds behave differently under that instrument? If no, the isomer claim (2) fails.
- **Refusal test:** when the ground pays for one reading, can the system add a refusal that leaves the others in the record and lowers the reach? If not, (4) fails.
- **Independence test:** does a reading from a differing closure buy more than one from the same pool? If no, (5) fails.
- **Standing falsifiers** from `01` §3 and `08` §2. Any one of the eight firing narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The constraints are stateable and checkable; the universal algorithm is not returned, and no amount of additional search inside the figure set would add it (Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*). Fig 4 :30 requires the lossy condensation to carry a measured round trip, not an assumed identity: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The lineage is recorded in `01` and `11`. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a system an algorithm for deciding when to stop being ambiguous. It gives the shape such an algorithm must respect. Ambiguity is not one reading with a confidence value; it is a set of readings indexed per object slot, each a distinct arrangement of the same atoms, whose bond the formula alone cannot record. A system can hold them by treating agreement as a convention that makes the disagreement statable, while refusing to let agreement decide which reading is true. Narrowing is allowed, but only with every cut ranked and reversible; the ground, a discriminating measurement, is the only thing that settles, and the readings it rejects are refused rather than deleted so the record still knows what was once held. Adding more readings from the same evidence pool adds nothing, so independence, not count, is the variable. What the framework cannot do is say how long to hold, what threshold to use, or how to encode ambiguity universally. That is a declared gap, reported as unreachable rather than silence. This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a measurement, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.
