## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be stated before the name

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the form of a representation that can be stated before anyone can say which reading is correct. It constrains; it does not decide. Concretely here, the shape is: per-slot live residuals, isomer candidates with their bonds recorded, a convention that makes the disagreement statable, ranked and reversible cuts, the ground as the only selector, and negation as the refusal operator. A universal collapse algorithm is a name, not a shape, and is not returned.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "error is per slot" and "ingredients are not arrangements", then let the figures supply the bond, the convention and the refusal.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is described as an effect: a system reports incompatible readings with their errors intact until a discriminating contact arrives. One should not first assume a Bayesian, connectionist or symbolic mechanism and then map onto it.
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels."* The lower-overlap corroborators were found by describing that effect, not by searching for "ambiguity representation" as a phrase in one literature.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means the shape and the refuters are the same object seen from two ends.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A discriminating instrument that shows which reading pays on a slot would name the reading.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, mapping carried from the exemplar, sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So naming a bond or a convention is a visible choice, and must be recorded as such rather than presented as a discovery.

## 4. Why "just collapse to the most likely reading" is the named forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, mapping carried from the exemplar, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For this question: a confidence score is a reading of one instrument. It cannot comment on which interpretation produced it, and several arrangements can yield the same score. Collapsing to the highest score names a source from a projection. What is owed is a source-discriminating contact, not a decision rule. This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 9 :42, *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*.

## 5. What the shape is and is not for this question

**It is:** the set of constraints that any acceptable answer must satisfy. It is checkable from outside, because each constraint has a verbatim figure line and a refuter in `08`.

**It is not:** an algorithm, a parameter, a probability threshold, a universal encoding, or a claim that the framework settles the question. Section F9 of `01` records this as a `gap`. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the canonical README: the framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

**Does the world corroborate the shape?** Partly, and at the mechanism level only. Blake and Tong's multistable perception and the HMM forward/Viterbi distinction are genuinely different fields and both show the same shape: keep the alternatives live, then let a separate operation select. None of them returns a universal collapse rule either. They corroborate the constraint, not the derivation.

## 6. Correction to my own reading, kept visible

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming the convention or the bond in the answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Bonds, negation and the convention/verdict distinction are already in the framework's notation and seat map. Naming them is a binding onto held structure, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that naming them produces a universal algorithm; that would be naming a missing thing the framework says it cannot name. Both the overstatement and the correction are preserved here.

## 7. Prestige is not action, and this derivation is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of agreeable readers cannot substitute for a discriminating instrument.

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, this file and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
