## QUESTION

How can a system represent ambiguity without prematurely collapsing it into a single interpretation? Restated as an effect: `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is indexed per object slot; ambiguity is more than one candidate molecule φ_i with live mass `R⁺_s` on a slot; premature collapse is any operation that reduces that set to one before the ground pays. Option A: collapse early to one globally best reading. Option B: keep a per-slot ranked set of isomer candidates under a convention that makes the disagreement statable, narrow with reversible cuts, and let the ground settle. A spends the reader's independence and loses the signal that says where to look; B keeps the signal and returns a checkable shape with eight refuters.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: one-to-many, one slot with k candidate readings, carrying a disjunction bond (∥), a comparison bond (⋛) and the unary negation operator (¬). Symmetry: candidate readings are isomers, not relabelings; the naming is symmetric under a symmetric bond but the arrangement is not (Fig 12 :92). Scale: per slot, per room, and across differing closures. Fig 9 :55 names it a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition.
- **Domain 1, signal and slot.** Actors: observation o′, slots s, instruments. Currency: residual `R_s` and live mass `R⁺_s`. Anchor: o′, what actually happened. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, library and candidates.** Actors: atoms, seven bonds, negation, candidate molecules, closure(Γ). Currency: `|φ|`, `left(R,φ)`, reach. Anchor: settlement against the ground. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, frames and independence.** Actors: frames with differing closures and their evidence pools. Currency: independence and surplus. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, convention and meaning.** Actors: seats, conventions, shared meanings. Currency: conventions (no truth value) versus verdicts (truth value). Anchor: the seam made statable. Admitted.
- **X1 "the true meaning" free of any ground.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "the most likely reading" as sole cause.** Absorbed into D2 as a budget filter, not a separate domain.

D1/D2 form a partition boundary; D3/D4 are a union, because within-room variation and between-frame difference overlap and have different instruments. Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). The link most likely broken is vocabulary to objective: no term for holding candidates until settlement. Strain test: Fig 2 :40 versus :41 govern different quantities (verdict weight versus shared meaning), so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the universal algorithm does not exist to be returned. Ambiguity is a per-slot set of isomer candidate readings with their bonds and residuals recorded. A system holds them by treating agreement as a convention that makes the disagreement statable, by narrowing only with ranked reversible cuts, and by refusing a reading through addition to the record rather than deletion. The ground, a source-discriminating contact, is the only selector, and independence between frames, not the count of readings, is the parameter. No figure line selects a holding time, collapse threshold or universal encoding, so that is a `gap` reported as unreachable rather than silence. Mode `specified` on the domains, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the algorithm, ungrounded candidate. The lower-overlap world sources corroborate the mechanism shape, not this derivation.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states eight refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refusal is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted". The shape and the refuters are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator that names the reading on a slot, a demonstration that identical atoms and bonds behave identically under all tests, evidence that same-pool readings buy as much as differing-closure ones, or a verbatim figure line selecting a universal collapse rule would each narrow or reverse the verdict. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
