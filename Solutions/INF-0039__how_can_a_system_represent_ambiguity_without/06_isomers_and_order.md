# Isomers and order: why several readings are not one reading spelled twice

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).**

## RESIDUAL

The question asks how a system can represent ambiguity without premature collapse. Before choosing a figure line, two supporting questions are owed. (1) What *kind* of thing are several readings of one signal: variants of one object, or distinct objects? (2) What *order* does the search over them have, and where does it stop?

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: space, time, energy (states) and spectrum, gradient, network (structures), plus the seven bonds and the unary negation operator. Currency: description cost `|φ|`, leftover residual `left(R,φ)`, reach. No new primitive is available without import. The two conditions (within-frame variation and between-frame difference) are a union, so both get a separate loop and are never averaged.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are two readings of one signal isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
- Fig 12 :95: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing."**

An ambiguous signal supplies a fixed ingredient set, the atoms Γ can compose over. Each reading is a molecule: the same atoms joined by a different bond, or by the same bonds in an ordered arrangement. Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term, so a system that charges for the spellings overcharges by log₂(k!) and undercharges the bond (Fig 12 :92; Operators :59). Under an ordered bond, different orders are different terms and the charge is correct.

Therefore the readings are isomers when the bond or the order differs, and they are a single term only when the difference is a pure spelling under a symmetric bond. The system must record the bond, because *"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands"* (Fig 12 :46). Fig 12 :97 gives the external anchor: *"This is not an analogy. Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties"*.

**Result.** Same signal plus different arrangement yields different substance. Holding ambiguity means holding the arrangements, not the ingredient list. **Mode `general`. Triangulated** (Fig 12 bond pricing, Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance"*, Operators symmetry test; low overlap between the pricing rule and the route rule).

**Falsifier, written before the conclusion.** Exhibit two readings with identical atoms and identical bonds whose behaviour is identical under every ground test, so the bond the figure charges for carries nothing. If that happens, the isomer reading fails and the readings are spellings, in which case there is no ambiguity to represent.

### Q2 — What is the order of the search over readings?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**
- The dependency chain for this question: perception (slot and observation) → vocabulary (bonds, negation, convention) → objective (keep candidates live until settlement) → planning (SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD) → learn and carry (lineage and refusal).
- `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: *"SEACHED is it in here and unfound?"* (asked first, cheapest); *"INWARD extend the instrument"*; *"OUTWARD IMPORT"*. Fig 6 :31: *"Neither answer tells you much about the other. Run them as two tracks, and never read one against the other."*
- Fig 9 :45: **"Describing the residual is the expensive part. Leaving, looking up and recognising are one step each, and cheap only in that order."**

**Result.** The order is dependency order, not a preference order over readings. Ranking by residual is a filter for the budget; it is not a verdict. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The break, if there is one, is between vocabulary and objective: the system can name the candidate ingredients but has no term for "hold them until the ground settles one". Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

**Falsifier.** Show a system that correctly holds multiple readings with no term or convention for the objective, so the middle link is not load-bearing. If that is demonstrated, Fig 3's ordering does not govern this question and the claim is narrowed.

### Q3 — What ends the holding?

- Fig 5 :70: **"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."**
- Fig 9 :42: **"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"**
- Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."**

The stop is not a counter, a confidence threshold, or a fatigue rule. It is a ground reading that pays. Before that, the set is held and narrowed with reversible cuts (Fig 9 :40). After that, the settled reading becomes an atom for the next level, and the others are refused, not deleted (Fig 6 :42, Fig 5 :55). Note the boundary: Fig 12 :104 permits composition over what settled; it does not licence settling early. Fig 5 :69 is explicit that settling is necessary and not sufficient.

**Falsifier.** Exhibit a system that correctly ends the holding on an internal threshold with no ground contact and whose choice still ranges correctly against an independent ground on the next trial. That would break the claim that the ground alone settles.

### Q4 — Are within-frame variation and between-frame difference the same thing?

- Fig 7 :33: **"variation inside, selection between"**
- Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**
- Fig 8 :49: **"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."**

No. Within a room, several readings are cheap variation; their agreement is useful as a convention and worthless as a verdict. Between rooms with differing closures, a surplus exists, but only when a residual has been described first (Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second."*). An undirected union with no described residual is mute (Fig 8 :21). So the number of readings is not the variable; independence between the frames that produce them is (Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*).

**Falsifier.** Show that a tenth reading drawn from the same evidence pool buys as much discriminating power as a reading from a genuinely differing closure, measured against the same ground. If so, independence is not the parameter and the union reading fails.

### Q5 — What is the analogue in other fields, and how far does it carry?

Lower-overlap corroborators only; they corroborate shape, not the derivation.

- **Perception.** Blake and Tong (2008) describe *"physically invariant stimulation leads to fluctuations in perception"* and rivalry as a default when the brain fails to register matching features. The alternative reading stays live. Carries at the mechanism level, not at the level of a system's library.
- **Engineering.** The HMM entry distinguishes the forward and forward-backward computations, which sum over all state sequences, from Viterbi's *"most likely explanation"*, which takes a maximum. That is the keep-distribution-versus-collapse distinction in a deployed formalism. Carries at the operational level, not as a claim about when to deploy.
- **Logic.** Supervaluationism keeps a statement true under all admissible precisifications. Carries at the level of holding several admissible readings, not at the level of per-slot error.

Fig 4 :20 limits all of these: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* The kernel is the same; the ground is renamed per scale. Fig 9 :30: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So no analogue is promoted to a derivation.

## CONCLUSION

Two readings of one signal are **isomers** when their bonds or order differ, and a single term only when the difference is a symmetric spelling. The search over them runs in dependency order, not preference order, and ranking is a budget filter. The holding ends only when a ground reading pays; the settled reading then becomes an atom for the next level while the others are refused and retained. Within-frame variation and between-frame difference are a union, with independence, not count, as the parameter. Each claim has a falsifier above.

This rescues what is defensible in the ordinary phrase "represent ambiguity": the system keeps the arrangements and the per-slot errors, makes the disagreement statable, and refuses to call narrowing a settlement. It does not produce a universal algorithm, which remains a declared `gap` (F9 of `01`).
