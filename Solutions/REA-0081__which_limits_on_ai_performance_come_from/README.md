## What this folder answers, in effect terms

A performance shortfall has at least two candidate causes. An **architectural** cause means the system cannot compose the required term at all: the term lies outside `closure(Γ)` and only an import or a different frame moves that wall. A **resource** cause means the term is already inside `closure(Γ)` but was not found or not settled for lack of budget: data, compute, energy, or instrument resolution. The two are different facts with different instructions, but from inside the frame they look the same. The figures license the distinction in principle and refuse the claim that a single performance curve can assign a cause by inspection. A third category, contact with the habitat, is neither of the two.
