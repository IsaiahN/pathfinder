# The Tether's answer: which AI limits are architectural and which are resource limits

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the distinction, not `grounded`.** This is a decision procedure and a set of falsifiers, not a classification of any named model. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An AI system falls short of wanted performance. The shortfall can be produced by several conditions that a single curve does not separate. The question proposes two causes. The figures require at least four verdicts.

## 2. The shape: four verdicts, kept separate

1. **Outside the closure (architectural).** The capability cannot be composed from what the system holds. Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 13 :118: "infinite effort inside a finite reach." More data, compute, and energy are effort inside a wall. The remedy is import: a new primitive, or contact with a frame whose closure differs. Triangulated across three figures with low overlap.
2. **Inside the closure, unfunded or unfound (resource).** Fig 8 :41-42: "Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom." Fig 12 :127: "the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all." The remedy is distributed independent search, better allocation between bits and actions, or an instrument improved from a worse one that already returned something (Fig 6 :32). Triangulated.
3. **Same reach, unfound (neither).** Fig 6 :49: "more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited." No new atom is owed and no wall has moved. This is the condition the question's binary omits.
4. **Contact-limited (neither).** Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A benchmark solve can be real wiring and still say nothing about capability in a habitat. Single-source within the figures; compatible with the Apple collapse result but not settled by it.

**The two headline causes are genuinely different facts.** Fig 8 :40: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." But they cannot be read off one performance curve. Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

**Two currencies, never one score.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Data prices description; compute and energy price test coverage.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named current model's limit has been correctly assigned to a cause. That needs a ground reading.
- Not that scaling is unnecessary. Fig 8 :41-42 says the remedy for an in-closure shortfall is more search, not another atom.
- Not that an architecture change is automatically an import. If it rearranges existing atoms it is a search inside the same reach; it is an import only if it adds a kind.
- Not that a circuit-complexity impossibility theorem proves a production system's wall. Those theorems are conditional and asymptotic.
- Not that the four verdicts can be averaged into one number (Fig 1 :22).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at any scale settles a classification in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it, and the ground

A discriminating intervention, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Fixed-closure budget sweep.** Hold the architecture and instrument fixed and vary data, compute, and energy independently. A residual that persists and does not trend down under more search is the candidate for a wall (formula step 2's "persists and does not trend down" test, read with opposite sign).
- **Architecture swap at fixed or lower budget.** Change the closure while holding the resource account fixed. If the new frame reaches a capability the old one cannot at any tested budget, that is evidence for an architectural limit, and it is a `grounded` claim only when the experiment is actually run.
- **Metric swap on the same outputs.** If a claimed new capability becomes a smooth curve under a continuous metric, the limit was in the metric, not the model (Schaeffer et al., corroborating Fig 1 :27).
- **Real-habitat transfer.** If a benchmark solve does not transfer to a habitat with the same goal, the benchmark was a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70).

## 5. The honest stop

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The canonical README adds that indiscernibility is not identity, and that the framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is honest. The lineage here is: four domains, one round-trip gap `R_T` (recorded qualitatively in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6), two live strains (conditional complexity limits and the vocabulary collision over "architecture"). The stop is **unreached, not unreachable**: a discriminating instrument could move it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not say that current AI limits are all architectural or all resource limits. They say these are different facts with different instructions: if a capability cannot be composed from what the system holds, no amount of data, compute, or energy adds it and the remedy is an import from a frame whose closure differs; if it is already reachable and was not found, the remedy is more search, better allocation, or a sharper instrument, not a new kind of system. There is also a third case, more of the same space at the same reach, and a fourth, a limit of the habitat rather than the agent, which a benchmark solve does not reveal. The two headline causes look the same from inside a single performance curve, so the answer offered is a discriminating experiment and a refuter written in advance, not a classification of any named model. Data and compute are held in separate accounts and never summed. The result is a shape with a stop recorded, and it is `specified`, not `grounded`.
