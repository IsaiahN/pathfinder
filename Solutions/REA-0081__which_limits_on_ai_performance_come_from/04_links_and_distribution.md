## What the wider literature adds, without overruling the figures

1. **The resource side is measurable.** Chinchilla and the Epoch AI data-stock forecast show that allocation and exhaustion inside a fixed architecture are real, budget-shaped phenomena. They corroborate Fig 8 :41's "remedy is more search, not another atom" at the level of practice.
2. **The architecture side is conditional in the literature.** The parallelism tradeoff is a theorem under stated complexity assumptions and at large input size, whereas Fig 8 :32's closure idempotence is definitional. They have the same shape but different proof status. This is a named strain, not a conflict.
3. **The measurement side is decisive.** Schaeffer's metric result corroborates Fig 1 :27, that a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces. Shojaee's collapse result shows that a shortfall can persist despite adequate token budget, which is why the figures refuse to let one curve decide the cause.
4. **Energy is a physical budget.** The IEA and Brookings sources corroborate Fig 12 :127, that the budget decides how much of the space is reached, and add that the ceiling is partly physical rather than financial.
