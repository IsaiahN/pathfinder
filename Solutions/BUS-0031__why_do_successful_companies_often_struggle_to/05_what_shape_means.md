## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. In the Symbols table the residual itself is *"always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0031, a shape is: three distinguishable conditions whose conjunction keeps a firm's residual low across a habitat shift, plus the instruments that would tell their failures apart. A list of winning technologies, a named routine, or a forecast of which incumbent will win is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54 and :55 and is why this solution unbundles before choosing a lever.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* and :31 *"rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The three conditions in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are written that way.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that would be a prospective, per-firm market-ground measurement, not a case study and not an agreeable expert.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Calling the entrant's technology an import into the incumbent's library is legitimate only with that record.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Finally: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* This is why this solution never applies the six physical terms to a firm's library (retraction S2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`).

## 4. Why "which technology wins" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes can produce one reading, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The conclusion is *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

In BUS-0031 the reading is "firm was successful, then failed after the technology arrived". Several sources could produce that reading: channel failure, reach failure, middle-link failure, or habitat exposure selecting late-cycle firms. Naming "the disruptive technology" as the source from that reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is the discriminator, not the name. This is the ground of gaps F10 and F11.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous disruption experts to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the same rule in group form: *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."*

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; LLM output is *"simulacra"*. *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the practical warning that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle BUS-0031.

## 7. Canonical caution about self-validation

*Arrival of the Fittest* is explicit: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* Fig 8 :34 *"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe."* This solution therefore does not claim the figures prove the business answer. It claims only that the figures constrain the shape of any answer that would survive contact with the ground, and that the pre-registered refuters are the way to find out whether this one did.

## 8. Indiscernibility is not identity

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* If a future instrument cannot distinguish channel failure from reach failure in a named firm, the two are still different compounds. The lineage has to be recorded so a later reader can see which one the verdict was for. That is why `10_source_ledger.csv` records overlap and why `11_audit.md` records the round-trip gap rather than declaring success.
