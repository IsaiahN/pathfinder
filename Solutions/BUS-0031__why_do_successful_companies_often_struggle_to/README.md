## What an answer would look like, bound before the run

Following the formula's "Direction" discipline, the sought-for shape is written down as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused: a pair of conditions whose conjunction keeps a firm's residual low when the habitat changes, plus an instrument that tells a failure of one condition from a failure of the other, and an honest `gap` where no instrument reaches. The answer is a shape, not a list of winning routines.

## Honest standing of this document

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*; LLM output is *"simulacra"*. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

A practical constraint on outreach: the Academy of Management states at submission that *"Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This package is such a product and is offered only as a checkable object for a human author to write up, never as a submission.
