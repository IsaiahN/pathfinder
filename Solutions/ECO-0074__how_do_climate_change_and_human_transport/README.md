## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons from the framework's own words:

1. **Agreement from a shared pool is not a witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **An outside frame must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts a potential checker into a participant.
3. **A falsifier comes before the run.** Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* Every substantive claim in this folder carries its refuter beside it.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
