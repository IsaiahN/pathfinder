## Verified world sources actually fetched

| what | URL | verified content and bearing |
|---|---|---|
| Bellard et al. 2018, *Ecology and Evolution* | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6010883 | Systematic review of 423 modelling case studies. *"An important caveat to these findings is that researchers have rarely considered the effects of climate change on transport, introduction success, or the resulting impacts."* Also: *"the movements of alien species are facilitated by humans, and thus, alien species might move faster than native species."* Supports the union reading and locates the weak middle link. |
| IUCN issues brief, 2025 | https://iucn.org/resources/issues-brief/invasive-alien-species-and-climate-change | *"Extreme climatic events such as hurricanes, floods, and droughts can transport IAS to new areas and increase their spread by decreasing the resistance of habitats to biological invasions. Climate change is also opening up new pathways of introduction of IAS."* Supports the coupling of conditions and pathways, including the Arctic shipping example. |
| IPBES IAS learning tool, 2023 | https://www.ipbes.net/ias/learning | *"Climate change, interacting with land- and sea-use change, is predicted to profoundly shape and amplify the future threat from biological invasions."* Also lists trade and human migration as drivers mapped to the four invasion stages. Supports union and introduction readings. |
| Kaluza et al. 2010, *J R Soc Interface* | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2880080 | *"Transportation networks play a crucial role in human mobility, the exchange of goods and the spread of invasive species."* And: *"in general, the more clustered a network, the more vulnerable it becomes to the global spread of alien species, even for low invasion probabilities."* Independent network-physics corroboration of the vector as a structured actor. |
| Meijer et al. 2016, *PeerJ* | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5180588 | Enemy-release meta-analysis. *"non-native species may have been introduced without their natural enemies, and (2) potential enemies may not yet have colonized and/or adapted to the novel species."* Also reports *"no simple relationship between enemy release and the success of non-native species"*, which matches the figure's relativity-of-valence claim rather than a universal law. |
| Saebi et al. 2020, *PLOS ONE* | https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0220353 | *"the first-order Markovian assumption limits accurate modeling of NIS spread risks through the global shipping network"* and higher-order patterns *"provide more accurate NIS spread risk estimates."* Independent corroboration of the contact-cascade finding. |

**Overlap warning.** IPBES and IUCN draw on the same 2023 assessment lineage; Kaluza and Saebi use overlapping Lloyd's shipping data. These are fewer than six independent witnesses. Record in the ledger, never pool as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 "must not hold the interiors." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them anything.
- **Seat Map :163**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list ranked for receptiveness manufactures a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the local figure text and the six source links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

1. **Stage 1:** the residual, the union shape, and the four refuters in §5 of `01`. No theory, no source list. Record the verdict before anything else is shown.
2. **Stage 2 (opt-in only):** if they are curious, the local figure text, the canonical rule, and the six verified world links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user state their own mode and write their own refuter before running it. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. As drafted, the claim is a shape with stated refuters, not a settled result. If a refuter fires, withdraw and keep the refuter in the record (Fig 6 :42: *"A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted."*).
