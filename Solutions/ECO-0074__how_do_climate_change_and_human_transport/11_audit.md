- **Union or partition?** Union. Domains 2 and 3 overlap on Domain 1: warming changes conditions and opens routes, transport delivers into changed conditions.
- **Order.** Arrival then suitability then establishment then cascade. The middle link is where the instrument is missing.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: the habitat must be enumerated from contacts, and the two drivers form a compound whose intersection is categorically different.
