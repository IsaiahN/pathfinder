## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "climate change and transport form a union compound on a habitat" is a shape. "Species X will spread north at 4 km per year because of warming and shipping" is a name, and a measurement. The shape survives without the name; the name is owed by the ground.

**Correction kept visible.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. For this question we are not claiming the habitat, conditions or network are missing primitives. We are enumerating actors already in the world (Fig 11 :43) and describing the arrangement between them. So "shape, not a name" here means: state the union/compound form and the enumeration rule, do not pretend the framework hands you a particular species or a rate.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on the problem being the searcher, not the library: *"The Problem Is You"* and *"Your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."* This is why the audit begins with a residual stated in effect terms before any figure line is chosen.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* The stop is a shape that no further vocabulary improves, not an exhausted search.

Applied here: proximity vocabulary (propagule, pathway, suitability, regulator, cascade) narrows the space until the union/compound form is all that is left to state.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question the six world sources are imports, each with a URL, author, venue, year and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They corroborate the shape; they do not name the particular species or rate, and no world source was permitted to flip the verdict (Fig 2 :40 on shared pools).

## 4. The source/instrument distinction

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters when someone tries to name the driver from an observation. A color instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source"*, which might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the source: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: observing that a species spread after a warm year and a new shipping route tells you nothing about which driver, or which combination, produced the reading. Several causal paths give the same spread curve. What is owed is a discriminator instrument (an additive-versus-interaction test run on held-out regions), not a name assigned from the projection. An instrument that cannot distinguish these cases *"demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 5. Why the union reading, not a list of drivers, survives

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A list containing "climate change" and "human transport" records the ingredients and not the bond between them. The question asks *how they interact*, which is a question about the bond, so the answer must be a compound, not an inventory. Fig 11 :43 forbids composing the habitat from a checklist; it must be enumerated from the world.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous invasion biologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried (one of the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`) and lets the ground, an additive-versus-interaction test, settle it.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are **simulated derivations**, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. Five of the eight essays were not re-opened in this run; the three that were, plus the canonical README, are sufficient for the reading above and are the only essay material quoted.

## 8. Consequence for the outreach order

Because a shape is a constraint obtained before a name, and because an instrument is required to move from one to the other, the first contact carries the shape and the refuter, never the framework and never a claimed rate. This is Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* It is also the README's own limit: a shape, not a name, then an honest stop at what the instruments can distinguish.
