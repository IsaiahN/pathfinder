## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

> When and why did DNA replace RNA as the main hereditary material?

**What is being decided.** Whether a hereditary role moved from one polymer to another, what selected for the move, and whether a date can be attached to it.

**Options and what each leads to.**

- **A, displacement.** An RNA-only hereditary system was superseded by DNA in a later transition. Leads to: a selection story, a role sequence, and a date range to be sought in the rock record.
- **B, differentiation.** The two polymers partitioned roles early, DNA for long-term information and RNA for transient catalysis, with no single displacement event. Leads to: a functional-group story and no date to report.
- **C, mixed.** The role moved, but the molecules coexisted and the movement was gradual and complete only by LUCA. Leads to: a role sequence whose completion is bounded by a ground event, not by a single instant.

**What an answer would look like (Formula, Direction).** It states (i) the role that moved, (ii) the habitat pressure that selected the new occupant, (iii) an anchor that could settle the timing, and (iv) the shape a hereditary material must have to be kept in that habitat. Binding this as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual like anything else, so the sought-for shape is refusable.

**Preliminary finding before any figure line.** "When" and "why" do not share a ground. "When" is settled by radiometric dating and fossils; "why" is settled by chemistry and selection experiments. They are a union, not one hard question. No conclusion is drawn from this yet; the census comes first.

## 4. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - The question is a union and must be unbundled (decisive for method)

Cited above: Fig 9 :54, :55, and the compound test at Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. The "when" and the "why" have different anchors (D3 versus D1/D2/D4). A single answer sentence that gives both a cause and a date is a blend, not a result. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*

**Refuter:** show that dating and selection evidence share one anchor such that a single observation settles both. No such instrument is named in the sources opened.

**Verdict:** union compound, `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 and the domain census.

### F2 - "Replace" is an ordered bond, and the order is a claim

Fig 12 :72 and Operators :34, both cited above. The verb encodes a temporal sequence: RNA first, DNA later. The world record strains this. Wächtershäuser 2014 argues that *"the functional dichotomy between hereditary DNA with stability on geologic time scales and transient, catalytic RNA with stability on metabolic time scales existed since the dawn of the genetic machinery"*, and Wikipedia reports Forterre's view that viruses were instrumental in the transition with LUCA already RNA-based. So the sequence is not a given.

**Strain test and re-census.** Applied to molecule *origin*, the sequence strains against co-evolution. Re-census splits two objects: molecular origin (differentiation, no sequence) and **information-storage role occupancy** (a sequence is licensed, because the role did move in the lineages we can observe). After re-census the sequence claim is restricted to role occupancy. No strain remains.

**Refuter:** a reconstruction showing the hereditary role was jointly held by DNA and RNA in the ancestral lineage with no displacement of one by the other.

**Verdict:** `specified`, restricted to role occupancy, triangulated (Fig 12, Operators table, Wächtershäuser 2014, Higgs and Lehman 2015).

### F3 - No maintainer authored the switch

Fig 11 :36 *"Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Fig 11 :56 gives the unmaintained mode: *"nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."* *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says *"Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."* Lazcano et al. 1988: *"DNA was selected as an informational molecule in cells to stabilize earlier RNA-protein replicating systems."* So "DNA replaced RNA" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, not a designed substitution.

**Refuter:** evidence of a maintainer selecting the switch deliberately, which would move the claim into the maintained habitat of Fig 11 :58 and require a seat to be named.

**Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 11 and the RSI essay), with the specific pressures from Lazcano 1988 as `specified` corroboration.

### F4 - DNA is a mint inside RNA chemistry, not a new atom from outside

Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The three guards are a product (Fig 5 :33): SUPPORT is RNA fragility and mutation rate; REACHABILITY is supplied by the fact that deoxyribonucleotides are made by reducing ribonucleotides, so the atom set is already held; NOVELTY holds because DNA is not in the RNA-only library. Wikipedia: *"The deoxyribonucleotides used to make DNA are made from ribonucleotides, the building blocks of RNA, by removing the 2'-hydroxyl group."*

This does **not** rule out viruses as a carrier of the change (Wikipedia reports Villarreal and Forterre); it rules out calling the molecule itself an import that added an atom. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* A virus can move a gene; it cannot supply the atom.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that deoxyribonucleotides cannot be produced from ribonucleotides within prebiotic chemistry, so the reachability guard fails and an import is owed.

**Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 5, Fig 6, Wikipedia chemistry, Lazcano 1988).

### F5 - The "why" is an acceptance bargain in two currencies

Fig 5 :42 *"The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."* Fig 12 :125 *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* DNA does not improve the bit cost, the four-letter information is the same; it lowers the **action cost** of maintaining the information. Lazcano et al. 1988: *"the 2'-deoxy-containing phosphodiester backbone is more stable in aqueous conditions and in the presence of transition metal ions (such as Zn2+) than its ribo-equivalents"*, *"the absence of proofreading activity in RNA polymerases leads to a higher rate of mutation in RNA genomes relative to DNA"*, and UV pressure *"would have imposed an intense selection pressure favoring duplex DNA"*. Wächtershäuser 2014: *"DNA has a half-life on geologic time scales, while catalytic mRNA has a half-life on metabolic time scales."*

**Refuter:** a measurement showing the deoxy backbone costs more actions than the ribo backbone under the actual habitat conditions, so the bargain fails.

**Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 5, Fig 12, two independent world sources from different decades and venues). This is the strongest single "why" in the packet.

### F6 - DNA and RNA are a functional group, not isomers (retracted first reading)

Fig 12 :117 (quoted in the census) requires *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance."* DNA and RNA do **not** share the same atoms: deoxyribose versus ribose, thymine versus uracil. Fig 12 :97 *"Chemistry hit it first: silver fulminate and silver cyanate have the same composition and different properties"* shows what a true isomer requires. So my first reading, "DNA and RNA are isomers of a hereditary molecule", is retracted.

The correct figure term is Fig 12 :123 *"The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* They are two members of a hereditary-polymer functional group, with different chemical qualifiers. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3.

**Refuter:** a synthesis showing both polymers can be written over one identical atom set with only a bond change, which would restore the isomer claim.

**Verdict:** `specified`, single-source on the figure definition, triangulation pending; retraction kept visible.

### F7 - The "when" returns a shape and a ground, not a date

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No figure line yields a date. Fig 2 :29 anchors the question: the anchor is the rock record, a measurement, not a framework. Higgs and Lehman 2015 place the RNA world *"about 4 billion years ago"*; Wikipedia reports *"the RNA to DNA transition some 4 billion years ago"* and that this transfer happened before LUCA. These are world claims with a single evidence pool (reviews and encyclopaedia compiled from the same literature), so they are `single-source`, not corroboration.

**Refuter:** a dated, source-discriminating measurement that places the completion of the role move after LUCA, or that resolves the transition to a specific horizon.

**Verdict:** `gap` at figure level for the date, `single-source` at world level. Unreached, not unreachable (Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*).

### F8 - The habitat must be enumerated, not composed

Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* Actors in contact with the residual: the 2'-OH group, UV radiation, temperature and pH, Zn2+ and other transition ions, compartments, cytosine deamination, ribonucleotide reductase, reverse transcriptase, viruses. Valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49): UV is a malfactor to naked RNA and a selector for duplex DNA; the 2'-OH is a benefactor to catalysis and a malfactor to long-term storage. This is *"the tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside"* (Fig 10 :70), not a ladder.

**Refuter:** an actor omitted from the enumeration whose introduction demonstrably reverses the selection, for example a mineral that stabilises RNA enough to remove the storage disadvantage.

**Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 11, Lazcano 1988, Wächtershäuser 2014, Flores-García 2026 on copying fidelity and fitness tradeoffs).

### F9 - Filters are not witnesses

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The age of the RNA-world hypothesis, the number of papers on it, and the prestige of a reviewer are budget filters. They do not settle the chemistry. Fig 2 :40 (quoted above) applies to this very audit: the second seat shares the evidence pool.

**Refuter:** a protocol in which a source's prestige is entered as an evidence column. None is used here.

**Verdict:** `general`, triangulated (Fig 9, Fig 2, RSI essay *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."*).

### F10 - The round trip drops three constraints

Fig 4 :19 and :30 (quoted above). The phrase "RNA world then DNA world" is a lossy condensation. Going up gives a selection shape. Coming back down, three things fail to survive: the co-evolution alternative of F2, the role/molecule distinction of F2, and the absence of a maintainer of F3. Treating the phrase as identical to the evidence is dead reckoning. The gap is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`.

**Refuter:** a condensation that preserves all three constraints in one sentence without adding a term. None was found.

**Verdict:** `general`, single-source for the measurement rule (Fig 4), corroborated by the round-trip check in `11_audit.md`.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Chemistry fires.** A measurement shows the 2'-deoxy phosphodiester backbone does not lower maintenance cost under plausible prebiotic conditions, or that RNA copying fidelity already exceeds the error threshold without DNA. (Breaks F4 and F5.)
2. **Role fires.** A reconstruction shows DNA and RNA jointly held the hereditary role in the ancestral lineage with no displacement, or that a DNA-free lineage survived to LUCA. (Breaks F2 and option A.)
3. **Timing fires.** A source-discriminating instrument dates the completion of the role move and places it after LUCA, or resolves it to a horizon that contradicts the before-LUCA claim. (Breaks F7.)
4. **Habitat fires.** A seat or maintainer is shown to have deliberately authored the switch, moving the case from Fig 11's unmaintained mode to its maintained mode. (Breaks F3.)
5. **Figure fires.** A re-census admits the sequence bond over molecule origin rather than role occupancy, or a seventh operator is needed to write the transition. (Breaks F2 and the census.)

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the packet is retracted. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.
