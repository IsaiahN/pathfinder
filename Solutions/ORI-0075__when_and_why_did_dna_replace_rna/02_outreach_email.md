# Outreach draft: validate the shape before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the two retracted claims in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §8 are removed from the text you circulate. The shape must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the RNA to DNA role change, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one shape claim I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "when and why did DNA replace RNA" is not one question. It is a union of a chemistry question, a replication-fidelity question, and a dating question with different grounds (Fig 9). Strip those apart and the residue is this: a hereditary **role** moved from a hydrolysis-prone polymer to a more stable, higher-fidelity one, by unmaintained selection, with no maintainer authoring the switch. That is a shape, not a date and not a named mechanism.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that RNA and DNA are isomers (they are not, the sugars and bases differ); that DNA was imported into the RNA world as a new atom (ribonucleotides can be reduced to deoxyribonucleotides, so the atoms are already there); that the figures give a date (they do not); or that DNA was "fitter" in a property it carried. The word "selected" is retrospective, a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground.
>
> **What would refute it, written before this message:**
> 1. a measurement showing the 2'-deoxy backbone does not lower maintenance cost under plausible prebiotic conditions, or that RNA copying already clears its error threshold without DNA;
> 2. a reconstruction showing RNA and DNA jointly held the hereditary role in the ancestral lineage, with no displacement;
> 3. a dated instrument placing the completion of the role change after the last universal common ancestor;
> 4. evidence that a maintainer deliberately authored the switch.
>
> If any one holds, I retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop: `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Contact discipline

- One to one only. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are institutional or opt-in. Do not copy personal addresses that appear on those pages into a list.
- Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 **before** sending any framework material. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- Include an opt-out line in every message where the channel is an unsolicited institutional contact.
