## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "when and why did DNA replace RNA as the main hereditary material" is a union of a dating question, a chemistry and selection question, and a role-occupancy question, with different grounds (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Unbundled, the residue is a shape: a hereditary role moved from a hydrolysis-prone, lower-fidelity polymer to a more stable, higher-fidelity one, by unmaintained selection, with no maintainer authoring the switch. DNA was minted inside RNA chemistry (ribonucleotides can be reduced), not imported as a new atom. The reason is a two-currency acceptance bargain: the bit cost of the four-letter information is unchanged, the action cost of maintaining it falls. DNA and RNA are two members of a hereditary-polymer functional group, not isomers, and RNA is retained as catalyst. The figure level returns a shape for "why" and a gap for "when"; the date is left to geology.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading the figures

- **Statement:** the question is a union, and the verb "replace" licenses a sequence over role occupancy only, not over molecule origin. Co-evolution of the two polymers remains live.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Also Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." and Fig 12 :72 "A then B; B needs A to have happened". The Operators table :34 warns that "the only bond a flat chain can express, so every term today is a sequence whether or not it should be".
- **Refuter:** show that dating and selection evidence share one anchor so a single observation settles both; or show a reconstruction in which DNA and RNA jointly held the long-term hereditary role in the ancestral lineage with no displacement. Either breaks the union unbundle or the role-sequence restriction.

### C2: chemistry-facing, checkable by a chemist against the literature

- **Statement:** the cause is a maintenance-cost bargain in two currencies, and DNA is reachable inside RNA chemistry. The bit cost is unchanged; the action cost (hydrolysis resistance, copying fidelity, damage repair) drops. Deoxyribonucleotides are made by reducing ribonucleotides, so no new atom is owed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold." Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Supporting world claims: Lazcano, Guerrero, Margulis and Oró 1988 report that DNA was selected as an informational molecule to stabilize earlier RNA-protein replicating systems, that the deoxy backbone is more stable in aqueous and transition-ion conditions than its ribo equivalent, that RNA polymerases lack proofreading and so RNA genomes mutate faster, and that UV photochemistry imposed selection pressure favouring duplex DNA. Wächtershäuser 2014 reports that DNA has a half-life on geologic time scales while catalytic mRNA has a half-life on metabolic time scales. Wikipedia records that deoxyribonucleotides are made from ribonucleotides by removing the 2'-hydroxyl group.
- **What to check:** is the action-cost reading faithful to the cited chemistry? Do the two-currency distinction and the reduction route hold under the habitat conditions generally reconstructed for the transition?
- **Refuter:** a measurement showing the deoxy backbone costs more actions than the ribo backbone under plausible prebiotic conditions, or that RNA copying already clears its error threshold without DNA; or a demonstration that deoxyribonucleotides cannot be produced from ribonucleotides prebiotically, so the reachability guard fails and an atom import is owed.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. The corresponding questions are:

1. Does the 2'-deoxy backbone actually lower maintenance cost under the reconstructed habitat conditions? A direct hydrolysis or fidelity measurement answers this.
2. Did the long-term hereditary role move from RNA to DNA in the ancestral lineage, or was it jointly held? A ancestral reconstruction with source discrimination answers this.
3. When was the move completed relative to LUCA? A dated, source-discriminating instrument answers this.
4. Was there a maintainer? A seat or engineer shown to have authored the switch would move the case from unmaintained to maintained.

Agreement among reviewers is not such an instrument. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
