## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and the same section says the system cannot name the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0075 a **shape** is: *a hereditary role must be occupied by a polymer whose copying cost, per generation and under the actual habitat, is lower than the cost of maintaining the incumbent*. That is a constraint. It is not *"DNA, 4 billion years ago"*, which is a name and a date. The shape holds whether the occupant is called DNA, TNA, PNA, or something not yet synthesised.

**Qualification carried from `01` §8.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the two candidate polymers are already in the world's library. Saying "a stable hereditary polymer is required" is a shape; saying "DNA" is a binding from the chemistry frame onto that shape, provenanced as such. The shape is not a ban on naming a held molecule, it is a ban on presenting the name as a derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on vocabulary: use the terms you already have that are *"closest to the words that you wish to describe"*, then look for new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably"*. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: "hereditary role" is the near term; "fidelity", "hydrolysis", "error threshold", "functional group" are the gained terms.
- *Library of Babel*, on a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the union finding F1 in `01`. The "when and why" wording is exactly such a bundle.
- Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The effect description here is: *long-term sequence information survives more generations with fewer repairs when the backbone lacks the 2'-OH*. That is checkable by anyone, whatever they call the molecules.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This governs the outreach email in `02`: send the expected observation, not the Framework interior.

## 3. What licenses a name or a date

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question the ground is a measurement of hydrolysis and fidelity, and a dated datum. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The metamerism case is the model: a green reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can give the same reading. A dated fossil or a half-life measurement is the spectrometer, not an argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50). The binding of "DNA" onto the storage slot is an import from chemistry. It must be labelled as such.

*They're Scanning Books*: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the RNA-world vocabulary is a visible guess about early life, not nature's own list. Naming DNA as the unique answer turns that visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why "when" is the specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: a reading does not determine its source. Here the reading is *"all extant life has DNA"*, and the tempting source-naming is *"therefore DNA replaced RNA at time T"*. Several histories give the same extant reading: a late displacement, an early differentiation with RNA retained in other roles, or a lineage bottleneck that erased the alternatives. The extant reading cannot discriminate them. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

So the honest output for "when" is a shape plus a declared gap, not a date presented as a figure verdict. Fig 13 :134 gives the vocabulary: unreached, not silence. This matches `01` F7 and the retraction log.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming DNA?

**No, under the qualification in §1.** The shape constrains the role; the molecule is a binding from the chemistry frame. What is forbidden is presenting the binding as the framework's own derivation, or as an import that moved the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* The deoxyribonucleotide is composed from the ribonucleotide already held (see `01` F4), so no new atom was imported into the world. The name is a legitimate binding with provenance, not a discovery of a new primitive.

**Correction to §3 of my own reading.** I first wrote that naming DNA was outside what the framework can do. That overstated the rule, which governs *missing* primitives. Retracted as applied to a molecule already in the library; kept as applied to the date, which is not in the library at all. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous reviewers who agree about the RNA world is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to test the shape against chemistry and dating.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the RNA to DNA claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**A final limit from *They're Scanning Books*.** *"You cannot accumulate your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of thing."* More review articles about the RNA world do not produce a better instrument. What would produce one is a measurement that discriminates among the histories giving the same extant reading.
