## What the question decides, in effect terms

Not one question but a union of three (Fig 9 :54): a **when** (a geological record question), a **why** (a chemistry and selection question), and a **which molecule moved into which role** (a role-occupancy question). Option A, the standard reading, says an RNA hereditary system was displaced by DNA in a single later transition. Option B, the co-evolution reading, says the two polymers differentiated into stable heredity and transient catalysis from early on and no displacement event occurred. Option A leads to a selection story with a date. Option B leads to a role-partition story with no date. Both are recorded separately below and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes institutional addresses; they are deliberately not copied into `03`.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons from the figures:

1. **A room does not supply an anchor.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* More agreement is not a better instrument.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that empties their agreement.
3. **Sending the interior spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

## Honest standing

This folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on. The date question in particular is left to the ground (geology and dating), not decided here.
