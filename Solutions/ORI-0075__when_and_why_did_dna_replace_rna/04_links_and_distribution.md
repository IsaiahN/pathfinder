## Three things in the wider literature that bear on the question

1. **The cause is a maintenance-cost bargain, not an increase in information.** Lazcano 1988 lists four separate pressures: backbone stability in aqueous and transition-ion conditions, higher mutation rate without proofreading, cytosine deamination to uracil without a correcting enzyme, and UV photochemistry. Wächtershäuser 2014 reduces the dichotomy to timescales: geologic for DNA, metabolic for RNA. These are different frames reaching the same shape by different routes, so the agreement is weak corroboration at best, not two independent witnesses; both are chemistry reviews drawing on overlapping literature.
2. **There is a genuine conflict about timing.** Wächtershäuser's FeS-world account places the DNA/RNA functional dichotomy at the dawn of the genetic machinery, whereas the RNA-world account places DNA later. Lazcano 1988 and Wikipedia both describe a three-phase history with RNA before DNA. This conflict is recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` WORLD and sent back to the census; it does not flip the verdict because the figure verdict is about a role shape, not a chronology.
3. **The "when" is instrument-limited, not absent.** The NASA Ask an Astrobiologist page states that LUCA is inferred from comparing DNA of extant organisms and that *"we don't know what information might be missing about LUCA from all of the lineages that went extinct in the 3+ billion years since LUCA lived."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and the refuter first. Do not attach the framework before a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 2 :45** *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Giving the formula as a method is permitted; giving a recording of someone else's success is not.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before answering has joined the frame; their agreement is not evidence.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Venue prestige is a budget filter. Choose the venue by field fit, not by how impressive it sounds.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that answer; (4) only then offer the links, noting that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
