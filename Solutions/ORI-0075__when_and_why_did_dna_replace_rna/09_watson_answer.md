## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that DNA replaced RNA as the main hereditary material. The question is a union of a when (a geological record question), a why (a chemistry and selection question), and a which-molecule-moved-into-which-role question. Option A, displacement, says an RNA hereditary system was superseded by DNA in a later transition and leads to a selection story with a date. Option B, differentiation, says the two polymers partitioned roles early and no displacement event occurred, leading to a role-partition story with no date. Option C, mixed, says the role moved gradually and was complete only by LUCA. What each leads to: A spends the answer on a date the figures cannot give; B dissolves the sequence claim at the level of molecules but keeps it at the level of role occupancy; C makes completion a ground event. The Tether's decision is about the shape the answer must have, not about the chronology, and it keeps the three verdicts separate rather than averaging them.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the date does not. The figures return a union that must be unbundled, a hereditary role that moved from a hydrolysis-prone, lower-fidelity polymer to a more stable one by unmaintained selection, a maintenance-cost bargain in two currencies with DNA minted inside RNA chemistry, no maintainer authoring the switch, a functional-group relation rather than isomerism, and a role-occupancy sequence that is licensed while molecule origin remains open. The figures return a gap for "when" and decline to name a date. DNA and RNA are not isomers; DNA was not imported as a new atom; "selected" is retrospective; and the six-term substrate does not bear on these two polymers. The verdict is `specified` on the shape and mechanism, `general` on the structural claims, `gap` at figure level on the date, and candidate throughout. Nothing here is `grounded`. Verdicts are kept per domain and are not averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, pre-registered refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices in the line "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter, as `08_expert_verification_packet.md` does. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
- **undoable:** yes. The two retracted readings (isomerism and molecule-level import) are removed from the circulated text, and the narrowed claims (role-occupancy sequence, unreached rather than unreachable) are recorded rather than erased. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no figure line has to be rewritten.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measurement showing the deoxy backbone does not lower maintenance cost under plausible prebiotic conditions, or that RNA copying already clears its error threshold without DNA, breaks the chemistry finding. A reconstruction showing joint hereditary role occupancy with no displacement, or a DNA-free lineage reaching LUCA, breaks the role finding. A source-discriminating instrument that dates the completion of the role move after LUCA breaks the rough window. A shown maintainer breaks the no-author finding. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
