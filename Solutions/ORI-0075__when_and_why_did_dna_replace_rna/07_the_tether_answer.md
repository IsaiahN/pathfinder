# The Tether's answer: when and why did DNA replace RNA as the main hereditary material?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the mechanism, `general` on the structural claims. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken and no date is asserted as settled.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is not one question. It is a union of a when (a geological record question), a why (a chemistry and selection question), and a which-molecule-moved-into-which-role question (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). The when and the why have different grounds: radiometric dating and fossils for the first, measured chemistry and selection for the second. Fig 9 :57 states that "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either", so a single sentence that supplies both a cause and a date is a blend, not a result.

Two readings are kept separate and never averaged:

- **A, displacement.** An RNA-only hereditary system was superseded by DNA in a later transition. Leads to a selection story and a date to be sought in the rock record.
- **B, differentiation.** The two polymers partitioned roles early, DNA for long-term information and RNA for transient catalysis, with no single displacement event. Leads to a role-partition story and no date.

The Tether does not choose between these as a chronology. It returns the shape that any answer of either kind must have, and it marks the date as a gap.

2. **The verb "replace" is an ordered bond, and the order is a claim, not a given.** Mode `specified`, restricted to role occupancy. Fig 12 :72 "A then B; B needs A to have happened". 3. **No maintainer authored the switch.** Mode `general`. Fig 11 :36 "Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." and Fig 11 :56 "nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept". So "DNA replaced RNA" is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground, not a designed substitution. **Refuter:** evidence of a maintainer deliberately selecting the switch, which would move the case into Fig 11's maintained habitat and require a seat to be named.

4. **DNA is minted inside RNA chemistry, not a new atom from outside.** Mode `specified`. Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The three guards are a product (Fig 5 :33 "The three guards are a product, not a checklist."): SUPPORT is RNA fragility and mutation rate; REACHABILITY is supplied by the fact that deoxyribonucleotides are made by reducing ribonucleotides, so the atom set is already held; NOVELTY holds because DNA is not in the RNA-only library. This does not rule out viruses as a carrier of the change; it rules out calling the molecule itself an import that added an atom. Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." **Refuter:** a demonstration that deoxyribonucleotides cannot be produced from ribonucleotides within prebiotic chemistry, so the reachability guard fails and an import is owed.

5. **The why is an acceptance bargain in two currencies.** Mode `specified`. Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold." and Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." DNA does not improve the bit cost, the four-letter information is the same; it lowers the action cost of maintaining the information. Lazcano et al. 1988 give the concrete pressures: the deoxy backbone is more stable in aqueous and transition-ion conditions, the absence of proofreading in RNA polymerases raises the mutation rate of RNA genomes, uncorrected cytosine deamination makes uracil ambiguous, and UV photochemistry imposes selection pressure favouring duplex DNA. Wächtershäuser 2014 reduces the dichotomy to timescales. **Refuter:** a measurement showing the deoxy backbone costs more actions than the ribo backbone under the actual habitat conditions, so the bargain fails. This is the strongest single "why" in the packet.

Fig 12 :123 "The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." supplies the correct term: they are two members of a hereditary-polymer functional group with different chemical qualifiers. Fig 12 :119 "A part that survives its own use." explains why RNA is retained as catalyst after the role move. **Refuter:** a synthesis showing both polymers can be written over one identical atom set with only a bond change, which would restore the isomer claim.

8. **The habitat must be enumerated, not composed.** Mode `general`. ## 3. What this answer explicitly does not say

- Not a date. The figures return a shape for why and a gap for when. The date is left to geology and dating.
- Not that RNA and DNA are isomers. They are members of one functional group with different atoms (item 6).
- Not that DNA was imported into the RNA world as a new atom. Ribonucleotides can be reduced to deoxyribonucleotides, so the atoms are already held (item 4). Virus-mediated transfer of genes remains a `specified` carrier claim, not an atom import.
- Not that DNA was "fitter" in a property it carried. "Selected" is retrospective, a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground (item 3).
- Not that the sequence "RNA then DNA" applies to molecule origin. It applies to role occupancy only (item 2).
- Not that the six-term substrate of Fig 13 governs these two polymers. Forcing it onto them was a strain and was recorded as not bearing (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by a measurement taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the pre-registered falsifiers

Written before the conclusion in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds.

1. **Chemistry fires.** A measurement shows the 2'-deoxy phosphodiester backbone does not lower maintenance cost under plausible prebiotic conditions, or that RNA copying fidelity already exceeds the error threshold without DNA. (Breaks items 4 and 5.)
2. **Role fires.** A reconstruction shows DNA and RNA jointly held the hereditary role in the ancestral lineage with no displacement, or that a DNA-free lineage survived to LUCA. (Breaks item 2 and option A.)
3. **Timing fires.** A source-discriminating instrument dates the completion of the role move and places it after LUCA, or resolves it to a horizon that contradicts the before-LUCA claim. (Breaks item 7.)
4. **Habitat fires.** A seat or maintainer is shown to have deliberately authored the switch, moving the case from Fig 11's unmaintained mode to its maintained mode. (Breaks item 3.)
5. **Figure fires.** A re-census admits the sequence bond over molecule origin rather than role occupancy, or a seventh operator is needed to write the transition. (Breaks item 2 and the census.)

Until one of these is run, nothing here is `grounded`. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The age of the RNA-world hypothesis, the number of papers, and the prestige of a reviewer are budget filters, not witnesses.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a date for the RNA to DNA change, and it does not claim that DNA was designed or imported. It says the question is a union of a when, a why and a role question that must be unbundled. Strip those apart and the residue is this: a hereditary role moved from a hydrolysis-prone, lower-fidelity polymer to a more stable one by unmaintained selection in a habitat whose ground is chemistry, with no maintainer authoring the switch. The atoms were already in RNA chemistry, so DNA was minted inside the closure, not brought in from outside. The reason is an acceptance bargain in two currencies: the information cost is unchanged, but the action cost of keeping it falls. DNA and RNA are not isomers; they are two members of one hereditary-polymer functional group, with RNA retained as catalyst. The verb "replace" is a sequence that licenses role occupancy, not molecule origin, where co-evolution remains live. The figures return a shape for why and a gap for when; the date is left to the rock record. What is offered is the shape, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The centre stays open until the ground settles it.
