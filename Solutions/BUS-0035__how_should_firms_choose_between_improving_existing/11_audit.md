## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the firm's need for a choice rule between two slot actions. Arity: dyadic (frame plus action), indexed by slot, with an allocator over both. Symmetry: broken, because inward composition is repeatable inside a closure while radical novelty needs a different closure; the currency and the anchor change under swap. Scale: within-frame for improvement, across-frame for radical innovation. This is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not a single allocation problem.

- **D3 allocator.** Actors: senior team, budget, review cadence, decay rules. Currency: budget as contact cost and conventions. Anchor: the market(s), never internal agreement. Admitted.

- **X1 visionary leader as free-floating cause.** No nameable anchor apart from the habitat. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 universal optimal R&D ratio.** No actor, no anchor. Refused as a preference held as a term.

Union, not partition. Dependency order: perceive the slot residual, find vocabulary for what it affords, state a track-specific objective, plan inward or outward, learn and carry (Fig 3).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: two different operators, run separately, never one score, financial metrics as filters, senior team authors conventions.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete firm claim: a 70/20/10 split, a named winning technology, a single ROI score for both tracks, and a senior committee verdict on which bets are good.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- a **universal ratio** has no figure license; the shorthand says "convention", the concrete form smuggles in a number (Fig 5 :62-63, a preference held as a term);
- a **named winning technology** breaches "shape, not a name" (Fig 9 :32);
- a **single ROI score** breaches the two-currency rule (Fig 12 :125);
- a **senior committee verdict** breaches the convention rule (Fig 10 :47).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: improvement is inward mint and radical innovation is outward import, with separate grounds. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no firm ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
