## Restating the question as an effect

A firm allocates an action budget between two slots: the existing product (known ground, near returns, local search) and a radical opportunity (unknown ground, distant returns, new frame). The residual R is indexed per slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19). An answer would look like (a) a rule for what each option does to the firm's reach, (b) a condition under which each is licensed, and (c) a refuter that would break the rule. This package binds that sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else (THE_FORMULA.md, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A set of readers selected for receptiveness is a mirror, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Contact cost decides how we approach someone, never whether their answer counts.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in submission routes in `03` avoid both.
