## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the residual, shape claim and refuter first. The full framework is offered only after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Contact cost and receptiveness decide how we approach someone, never whether their answer counts.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) residual, shape claim and six refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1** carries the residual, the shape and the pre-registered refuter. The reader checks the output without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 offers a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user still needs a ground and their own pre-written refuter.

No packet is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are yours to do. Prefer an opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over a pushed attachment.
