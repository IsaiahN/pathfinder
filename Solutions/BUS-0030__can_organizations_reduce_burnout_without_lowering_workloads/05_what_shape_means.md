## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns what a missing thing would have to look like, *"a shape, not a name"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and it cannot name which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer that can be stated before a specific programme is named. The shape here is:

- burnout is a residual indexed per worker-slot, not an organizational average;
- it is generated in the contact between worker and job, so an improvement that does not change contact cannot close it;
- it is a union of conditions, so a single lever leaves a categorically different intersection standing;
- if workload and the formal design are held fixed, the only available change is in the bond around the work, and that is a contact change.

"A resilience app will fix burnout" is a name, not a shape. "A contact change is required, and the live slot must be enumerated first" is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on bootstrapping vocabulary: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Apply it here: start with the closest available terms (burnout, workload, job design, supervisor support, recovery) and let the recurring terms in the occupational-health literature narrow the residual.
- *Library of Babel* on bundled questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly Fig 9 :55-57's union compound. Do not search for one lever until the bundle is split.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "a sustained per-slot gap between predicted and observed sustainable effort", not as "low engagement" in the vocabulary of a survey the organization also administers.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the outreach order in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is reached when the per-slot residual no longer moves under any available lever and an outcome anchor confirms it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An outcome anchor, not a self-report the intervening frame also produces.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here that would be an occupational-health finding that predicts the shape of this residual and was derived without borrowing the framework's vocabulary.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* A burnout score is the reading of a projection. It cannot comment on the source. Asking whether the source is workload, design, relation, or recovery requires an instrument, not a name from the score. In this question the field's own high risk of bias from *"subjective participant-reported outcomes without blinding"* (Kiratipaisarl et al. 2024, read in `04_links_and_distribution.md`) is the instrument failure, exactly as the colour example describes.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit on naming: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality, and revision is still bounded by what can be conceived to revise toward."* Calling supervisor support "a bond" is a visible choice of vocabulary, not a discovery that the framework named it.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The ground here is an outcome that does not move when the intervening frame pushes on it: turnover, sickness absence, functional capacity, a clinically interpreted measure held by a party that did not deliver the intervention. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

The channel is the survey and the average. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*, with *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* at :26. A pooled burnout score that improves while one shift remains in crisis is the named failure. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* on the cost of high overlap between frames: *"Long, close contact pulls the frames together, so the process that makes communication easy slowly uses up that extra reach."* An HR team that designed the job, delivers the wellness programme and reads the survey is one frame wearing several names. Its agreement with the workers is the pool talking.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What would license a specific answer to "can organizations reduce burnout without lowering workloads or changing job design?"** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the shape: contact must change if a contact-generated residual is to close, a union must be split before a lever is chosen, and the slot must be enumerated. Which bond (supervisor support, feedback, predictability, autonomy in practice, meaning, recovery) closes which slot is licensed only by an outcome anchor applied per slot, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, listing those bonds is a candidate set from the world literature, recorded as `specified`, not a figure-derived name.

**Does the derivation commit the error the canonical rule forbids?** Not with respect to the shape. It does not name a missing primitive; it names relations already present in the habitat. But it would commit the error if it claimed that Fig 11 or Fig 12 tells an organization which programme to buy. The honest form is the shape plus the refuter, with the slot left open.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A well-known occupational-health researcher agreeing with the shape gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So this solution is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Prestige and fame in venue choice.** *Arrival of the Fittest* records three mappings offered and declined, including Bayesian updating, because there is *"no prior, no likelihood and no posterior anywhere in the mechanism"*. The publicity value of a famous name is excluded from verdict weight for the same reason. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen because they are institutional and opt-in, not because they are prestigious.
