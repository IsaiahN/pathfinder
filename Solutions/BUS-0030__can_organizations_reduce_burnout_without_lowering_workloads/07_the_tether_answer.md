# The Tether's answer: burnout without lowering workloads or redesigning jobs

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

Burnout is a per-slot residual: the gap between what the work system predicts a person can sustain and what actually arrives. WHO defines it in the occupational context, as chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed. The question holds two operands fixed, workload and the formal design of jobs, and asks whether a third lever exists. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." So the answer must be given per worker, role, shift or team, and never as an organization-wide average.

## 2. The shape, with verdicts kept separate

**D1, the worker alone: no.** Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." If the residual is generated by the contact between worker and job, a lever that changes only the person cannot close it. **Status: single-source for this application** (Fig 11 governs it directly; Fig 4 governs scale, a different quantity). **Mode: `specified`.**

**D1 x D2, the union: one lever is not enough.** Fig 9 :56: "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Demand and design are a union compound, not two separable questions. Cases where both bind are categorically different, not a harder case of either. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 9 union split and Fig 3 dependency order, low overlap). **Mode: `specified`.**

**D2, the relation: yes, but it is a contact change.** Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." Holding the formal job description fixed, one can still change supervisor support, feedback, peer relation, predictability, autonomy in practice, meaning and recovery opportunity. Those are relations in the habitat (Fig 11 :43-48). If R falls, contact changed, so this is not an agent-only fix. "Job design unchanged" survives only if design is read as the formal task description; under a broad reading the lever disappears. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 12 bond rule and Fig 11 habitat enumeration). **Mode: `specified`.** The definitional strain is recorded, not hidden.

**D3, measurement: no verdict without a ground.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A self-report administered by the same team that delivered the intervention is a channel reading, not the ground. Kiratipaisarl et al. (2024) found all included trials at high risk of bias from unblinded subjective participant-reported outcomes. The four burnout meta-analyses disagree with each other, but they draw on one healthcare evidence pool, so their agreement or disagreement is not a witness. **Status: triangulated for the anchor rule, `gap` for the specific lever.** **Mode: `specified` for the rule, `general` for the gap.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

These four verdicts are not averaged into a single score. The practical answer is: **yes, but only by changing the relations around the work, and that is a change of contact; no, if the proposed lever changes only the person.**

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that individual interventions have no measurable effect. The claim is structural: an agent-only change cannot close a contact-generated residual. The world literature's effect sizes are small and conflicting, and this derivation does not adjudicate them.
- Not that any named programme works. The specific bonds listed above are candidates from the world literature, held as `specified`, not figure-derived names.
- Not that workload reduction is useless. Bes et al. (2023) found workload-focused interventions effective on exhaustion. That is outside this question's constraint, recorded as a boundary of the evidence, not an answer to it.
- Not a ranking of organizations or people by likely agreement. Fig 2 :35 forbids negotiation as a method, and Fig 7 :35 says a room's agreement is variation, not a verdict.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken; no outcome anchor was applied to any live slot.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

An outcome anchor per slot, held by a frame that did not deliver the intervention. The question becomes answerable when an organization can report:

1. Which slots carry live positive residual, enumerated before any lever is chosen (Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :43).
2. What counts as less burnout, settled by an outcome the intervening frame cannot move (Fig 2 :29). Turnover, sickness absence and functional capacity are candidates; the self-report alone is not.
3. Whether a pure agent-only lever, with no contact change of any kind, moves that anchor. If it does, refuter 1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 fires and the shape is withdrawn.
4. Whether a relation change moves it while the formal job description is untouched. If it does, the narrow "yes" is confirmed as a contact change, not as an agent fix.
5. Whether a categorically different residue remains in the demand-and-design intersection. Fig 9 :57 says it will; a measurement can refuse that.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read in `05_what_shape_means.md` §3) gives the discriminator: a reading cannot comment on its source. A better instrument, not a further argument, is what closes the question. In this field that means an outcome anchor and a per-slot census, not another meta-analysis of the same trials.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 8 :39 forbids turning an unfound search into a proof of absence. The live thing that is missing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held: a per-slot outcome measure that can tell a contact change from a self-report change. Six inward sharpenings of the same questionnaire cannot manufacture it. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that wellness programmes fix burnout, and it does not say that workload and job design are irrelevant. It says that burnout is a residual indexed per worker, not per organization, and that it is generated in the contact between worker and job. An improvement that changes only the person cannot close a contact-generated residual. Because burnout is a union of conditions, a single lever clears the cases where only one condition binds and leaves a categorically different intersection standing. If workload and the formal job description are held fixed, the remaining lever is a change of bond, supervisor support, feedback, predictability, autonomy in practice, meaning, recovery. That is a real change of contact, so it is not an agent-only fix, and calling it "the same job" is a statement about the description, not about the work. The framework does not name which bond closes which slot; that is a recorded gap. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the outcome anchor that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
