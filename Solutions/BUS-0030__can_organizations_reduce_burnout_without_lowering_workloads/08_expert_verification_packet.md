# Expert verification packet: burnout without workload or design change

**What this is:** a claim in a form an occupational-health or job-design specialist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at any workplace is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. **Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Burnout is a residual indexed per worker, role, shift or team, never per organization. It is generated in the contact between the worker and the job, so a lever that changes only the person cannot close it while workload and the formal design of the job stay fixed. Because demand and design form a union of conditions, a single lever clears the cases where only one condition binds and leaves a categorically different intersection standing. If the two declared operands are held fixed, the remaining lever is a change of bond: supervisor support, feedback, peer relation, predictability, autonomy in practice, meaning, recovery. That is a real change of contact, so it is not an agent-only fix, and "job design unchanged" holds only as a claim about the job description. Which bond closes which slot is not named by the framework; it is a recorded `gap` that needs a per-slot outcome anchor.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11 and 9

- **Statement:** an agent-only improvement cannot close a contact-generated residual; a single lever leaves union intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 9 :56 *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*
- **What to check:** are these lines present verbatim in `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, and does the application to burnout follow without straining the quantities (capability, residual, conditions)?
- **Refuter:** a controlled trial with no contact change of any kind that produces a sustained clinically meaningful fall in per-slot burnout against an outcome anchor not administered by the intervention team; or a single lever that clears the demand-and-design intersection with no categorically different residue.

### C2 — framework-internal, checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** the lever available while the formal operands are fixed is a change of bond, and a bond change changes the substance, so it is a contact change. "Same job" is a notation claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*
- **What to check:** does the figure treat the bond as carrying information the operands do not, and does the habitat enumeration include relations such as support and feedback? Does calling a relation change "no change to job design" survive the definition of design in effect terms?
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that supervisor support, feedback, predictability, autonomy in practice and recovery are not in contact with the residual; or a reasoned case that they are part of "the underlying design of jobs" under the ordinary meaning, which collapses the narrow yes.

### C3 — measurement-facing, checkable against the field's own instruments

- **Statement:** the field's common outcome is a channel reading, not the ground; the four syntheses read here share an evidence pool, so their disagreement does not decide the question.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- **What to check:** in the four syntheses (Panagioti 2017, Dreison 2018, Bes 2023, Kiratipaisarl 2024), are the outcomes self-report inventories, and do the trial pools overlap? Kiratipaisarl et al. write that the high risk of bias *"primarily stemmed from the fourth domain, concerning subjective participant-reported outcomes without blinding"*.
- **Refuter:** an outcome anchor that moves with the intervention and is demonstrably not produced or interpreted by the intervening frame, together with a synthesis drawing on a genuinely disjoint trial pool that reverses the per-slot shape.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that individual interventions have no effect; only that an agent-only change cannot close a contact-generated residual.
- Not that any specific programme is the answer. The candidate bonds are from the world literature, held as `specified`, not named by the figures.
- Not that workload reduction is ineffective. Bes et al. found workload-focused interventions effective; that is outside this question's constraint and is recorded as an evidence boundary.
- Not a `grounded` result. No measurement was taken by this derivation.
- Not a ranking of people or venues by likelihood of agreement. Fig 2 :35 forbids negotiation as a method.
- Not that the framework settles the empirical question. The specific lever per slot is a `gap`: Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. A per-slot census: which roles, shifts or teams carry live positive residual before any lever is chosen.
2. An outcome anchor not administered or interpreted by the intervention team. Turnover, sickness absence and functional capacity are candidates; a self-report delivered by the same team is a channel, not the ground.
3. An agent-only arm with no contact change of any kind, and a relation-change arm with the formal job description unchanged. Compare both against the anchor.
4. A check for a categorically different residue in the demand-and-design intersection after whichever arm appears to work.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 9 :55-57, Fig 12 :5 and :94, and Fig 1 :19 (five short passages).
2. Say whether an agent-only intervention, as you practise it, ever leaves the worker-job relation entirely untouched. If it does not, C1's "no" is about a case that does not occur, and the useful claim becomes the contact-change one.
3. Name one outcome anchor you would trust more than the intervention team's own survey, and say why.
4. Look at the four syntheses you know and say whether their disagreement is about the level of intervention or about which slot and outcome was measured.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement; later agreement is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
