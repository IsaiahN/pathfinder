# WATSON answer: Can organizations reduce burnout without lowering workloads or redesigning jobs?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself") it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about reducing burnout while workload and the formal design of jobs are held fixed. Option A: an organization can close the residual by acting on the worker alone. Option B: an agent-only lever cannot close a contact-generated residual, but changing the bond around the same work can, and that is a contact change. What each leads to: A endorses programmes that leave the generating contact untouched and is vulnerable to the averaging and self-scoring failures the figures name; B gives a checkable shape, five pre-registered refuters, and an honest `gap` for which bond closes which slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per-slot gap between predicted and observed sustainable effort, observed as exhaustion, mental distance or cynicism, and reduced efficacy. Arity: at least three-place (worker, demand, resources and relations), indexed by worker, role, shift or team. Symmetry: none; effort is spent in one direction, so the operands cannot be swapped. Scale: single worker loop, team or organizational habitat, lossy membrane between. Union, not partition: a demand condition, a design or control condition, and social and recovery relations (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **D1 Worker (agent).** Actors: capacity, coping, self-regulation, recovery. Currency: finite effort. Anchor: an outcome not produced by the intervention, such as turnover, sickness absence or functional capacity. Admitted.
- **D2 Job habitat (contact).** Actors: tasks, schedule, supervisor, colleagues, tools, feedback, autonomy in practice, meaning. Currency: demands and resources. Anchor: workload records, job-design instruments, observed outcomes. Admitted.
- **D3 Measurement and verification.** Actors: HR, managers, survey instruments, meta-analysts. Currency: scores, averages, agreement. Anchor: the ground, which must not be a quantity this frame also produces. Admitted, currently often unanchored.
- **X1 Burnout as free-floating disposition.** No nameable anchor once the occupational context is removed. Not a domain; excluded by WHO's definition.
- **X2 Wellness app as fourth operand.** Already inside D1 and D3. Absorbed.

Union, not partition: D1 and D2 overlap in recovery and self-regulation; D2 and D3 overlap where managers who design the work also administer the survey. Order by dependency: perception of live slot, vocabulary for what the job affords, grounded objective, lever, learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test: an initial reading tried to make Fig 13's cross-kind composition rule govern the lever; it strains, so that line was re-censed as governing the substrate and excluded from the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is per domain and is never averaged. An agent-only lever cannot close a contact-generated residual (D1, `specified`, single-source). Burnout is a union compound, so one lever leaves a categorically different intersection (D1 x D2, `specified`, triangulated). If the formal job description is held fixed, the available lever is a change of bond around the same work, and that is a real change of contact, so the narrow yes is not an agent-only fix (D2, `specified`, triangulated). The specific bond that closes a specific slot is a `gap`, not a hidden answer, and the current measurement frame cannot decide it because it scores itself with the quantity it also produces (D3). The world sources corroborate the contact-and-union shape at the level of definitions and theory; the four syntheses disagree about intervention level but share an evidence pool, so they carry no verdict weight. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and waste. An organization failing a design or relation condition would be sold resilience training, while a live worker-internal slot would be met with job redesign. The avoidable cost is spending an outside checker's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a shape and refuter; Fig 2 :42 prices it as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." No term is deleted if the claim fails, so the cost is locatable and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no programme is banned. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing, so the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out. The shape and its five refuters are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. Concretely: a no-contact-change trial that moves an outcome anchor; a per-slot census placing the residual in a worker-internal slot untouched by the job; a reasoned case that the candidate relations are part of job design under the ordinary meaning; one lever clearing the demand-and-design intersection with no categorically different residue; or an aggregate moving on an outcome anchor with no live slot anywhere. A disjoint-pool synthesis using outcome anchors that reverses the shape would also reverse it. Prestige, agreement and more self-reports would not.
