## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the discriminator and the refuter. The framework goes later and only on request.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole derivation before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what consequence they would expect to move before offering a transparency tool.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, not an answer.
- **Seat Map :163** — *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send to believers produces a convention, not a check.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the claim and its four refuters, with the mode stated; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only if they ask, the figure text and the audit; (4) only if they ask again, the formula as a reusable method.

## The two-stage offer, with the third stage

1. **Stage 1:** validate the discriminator against the ground. No framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** read the derivation and the figures. The figures are the authority, not the summary. Any later agreement is never added to the evidence.
3. **Stage 3, opt-in:** reuse the formula on a problem the recipient knows and can check. Write the refuter first and state the mode.

The gap on the word "often" is part of stage 1, not a detail to defer. If a recipient supplies a measured frequency with a stated instrument and sampling frame, that closes F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and is worth more than any endorsement of the shape.
