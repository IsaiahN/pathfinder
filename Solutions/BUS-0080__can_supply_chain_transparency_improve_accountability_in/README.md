## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Seat Map :163).
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* The anchor here is worker outcomes, enforcement and remediation, not the number of signatories to a disclosure standard.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter first. Send the framework only if asked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public institutional venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
