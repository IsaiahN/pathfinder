Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55-59); a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: ordered. Disclosure to consequence runs one way through an import (Fig 6 :14); consequence to disclosure does not require the same artifact. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :27 (frame-internal scoring), Fig 2 :29-30 and :43 (anchor and convention), Fig 6 :14 and :23 (import), Fig 9 :56 and :60 (union, filters), Fig 11 :40 (contact), Fig 12 :111-112 (operators).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §2 and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §1: disclosure is frame-internal; consequence is an import; the two form a union; reporting lacks route, threshold, progress and refusal; contact decides.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim the question wants: that transparency does or does not improve accountability "in practice" and "often".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **the per-slot indexing.** The shorthand says "frame-internal"; the concrete claim wants a yes or no about a whole practice. Aggregating to a practice-level verdict is exactly the loss Fig 1 :22 names.
- **the conditionality.** The shorthand says disclosure crosses only when a violable anchor is importable. The question's binary options drop that conditional and ask for an unconditional verdict.
- **the frequency.** Nothing in the abstraction carries a base rate, so the concrete word "often" has no upstream counterpart. This is the largest single term in R_T and is recorded as `gap` in F9.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an audit score cannot be the anchor for the thing it audits. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
