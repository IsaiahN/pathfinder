## QUESTION

Option A: assert that visibility and disclosure produce consequences. Option B: assert that they only produce documents. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Disclosure and accountability are two conditions. Reports, audits and certificates are frame-internal quantities a reporting system produces about itself, so they serve as conventions and budget filters but cannot be the verdict (Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :43, Fig 9 :60). Accountability needs an anchor from a frame whose closure differs, a consequence that does not move when the firm disputes the report (Fig 2 :29, Fig 6 :23). Publishing more clears disclosure-only cases and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :56). A chain of certifiers compounds agreement without adding evidence (Fig 7 :13). The reporting vocabulary names states and lacks route, threshold, progress and refusal (Fig 12 :111-112). Contact is the deciding quantity (Fig 11 :40). So transparency can improve accountability when the disclosure crosses into a violable, enforced ground; where it does not, it stops at reporting and certification. The word "often" is a `gap`: the figures give the condition, and closing the frequency requires an outcome measurement. This verdict is `specified`, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The claim is stated per slot, with mode and four refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by treating a certificate count as evidence, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the discriminator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The conditional verdict can be reversed by outcome evidence without withdrawing any primitive or re-census of the six-term substrate. The `gap` on frequency is explicitly open, so no claim has to be retracted merely because a measurement later lands one way. A wrong import can be refused without deleting anything, because the record only grows (Fig 6 :42-45).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A disclosure measure whose value is set by the anchor and moves only with it; a study showing publication alone remediating across multiple suppliers; audit scores predicting worker outcomes out of sample from a source that does not share the auditor's evidence; a reporting vocabulary carrying route, threshold, progress and refusal and delivering consequences; or a measured frequency with a stated sampling frame. Any of these fires a pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. A figure re-census showing disclosure and consequence are one condition would also reverse C2. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
