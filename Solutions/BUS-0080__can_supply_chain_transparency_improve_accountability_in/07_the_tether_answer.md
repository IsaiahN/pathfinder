# The Tether's answer: can supply-chain transparency improve accountability?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the conditional verdict, `gap` on the frequency word "often". Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

For each slot (a supplier, factory, incident or worker cohort), the gap is between what the disclosure reports and what an anchor returns. Disclosure and consequence are two conditions forming a union compound (Fig 9 :55-59: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). A published supplier list can be perfect while one factory slot is live and unremediated. A single aggregate transparency score is the average that hides it (Fig 1 :19, :22).

## 2. The shape

1. **Reporting and certification are frame-internal.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A certificate, an audit score and a coverage percentage are outputs of the disclosure frame. They cannot be the measure of accountability, whatever their coordination value.
2. **The anchor is consequence, not document.** Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Penalties levied, back pay paid, injuries reduced, contracts terminated. Fig 2 :43: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* A certificate is fine as a convention (Fig 2 :41) and corrupts when it decides the outcome.
3. **Disclosure and consequence form a union compound.** Fig 9 :56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Publishing more clears disclosure-only cases and leaves the intersection untouched.
4. **More disclosure cannot compose accountability.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :14 names import as *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Disclosure is a filter for the budget, not a witness.
5. **Certification chains are mirror chains.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Four parties reading the same documents add agreement without adding an anchor.
6. **The vocabulary of reporting lacks the correction.** Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Accountability needs a route, a threshold, a per-slot progress subtraction and a standing refusal.
7. **Contact is the deciding quantity.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* An audit prepared for is a substituted habitat (Fig 11 :70) and proves the wiring of the disclosure system, not the capability of the chain.

The conditional answer: **disclosure can improve accountability when it crosses into a frame with an anchor that can be violated and consequences that can be spent. Where it does not cross, it stops at reporting and certification.** The figures give the condition. They do not give the frequency.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that transparency never works. Fig 6 :14 permits import from a different closure.
- Not that certification is always harmful. Fig 2 :41 makes agreement about shared meaning *"efficient for a convention"*.
- Not that the phenomenon is common. The word "often" is a frequency claim and no frequency was measured. `gap`, F9.
- Not which standard, platform, law or scheme succeeds. The framework returns a shape, not a name (`05_what_shape_means.md` §1 and §5).
- Not `grounded`. No outcome study was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

Per-slot outcome measurement with an anchor that does not move when the firm disputes it. The world sources read point at the following instrument questions:

- Does the measure move when the anchor moves and stay still when the report changes? (refutes or confirms F1, F3)
- Does publication alone, with no enforcement, remediate multiple suppliers across multiple slots? (F2, F5, F8)
- Do audit scores predict worker outcomes out of sample, from a source that does not share the auditor's evidence pool? (F2, F4)
- Does the reporting vocabulary carry a route, a threshold, a progress subtraction and a refusal, and does it deliver consequences? (F7)
- What is the actual frequency, with a stated sampling frame and instrument? (closes F9)

Fig 10 :19: *"install what can be violated"*. A declared disclosure meaning is not grounded until something can violate it in a way the standard cannot absorb.

## 5. The honest stop

**Condition reached, frequency unreached.** The framework says what the discriminator must look like and stops. The registry of modern slavery statements read for this solution holds 17,689 mandatory and 1,180 voluntary statements: reports, not remediation. That is an illustration of the condition, not a measurement of how often the condition is met. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A failure to measure a frequency is not evidence that the frequency is high or low.

**Mode honesty:** this is a `specified` shape with an internal `gap`, not a result. Prestige cannot close the gap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026-11-19.)

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Transparency and accountability are two conditions, not one. Reports, audits and certificates are quantities the reporting system produces about itself, so they are useful as a shared convention and as a way to aim an investigation, but they cannot be the measure of accountability. The anchor is a consequence that does not move when the firm disputes the report: a penalty paid, back pay delivered, an injury rate reduced. Publication only changes what happens when it crosses into a frame with that kind of anchor and a spendable difference; otherwise it changes only the report. This is a shape of the discriminator, not a list of tools and not a claim about how often it happens. The frequency is a gap that only an outcome measurement can close, and the refuters that would break the claim are written down in advance.
