## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Public institutional channels and submission portals only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
2. **The anchor must not update.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A reward signal that is adjusted to agree with the model stops being an anchor.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
