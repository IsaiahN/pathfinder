## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus a practical one:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention.").
2. **Never send the interiors first.** Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
3. **Declare the stake.** Fig 10 :59: "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." This question is about human authority, so the author's own position is a stake and is declared here, not hidden.
4. Unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `08_expert_verification_packet.md` and `02_outreach_email.md` to the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the answer before offering any framework material. A named failure is more useful than an endorsement.
