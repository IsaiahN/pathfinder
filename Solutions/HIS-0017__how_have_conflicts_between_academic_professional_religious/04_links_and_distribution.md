## 3. Wider literature that bears on the question

- Kuhn's incommensurability and Fleck's thought styles are the established philosophical vocabulary for frames that cannot be merged by translation. The SEP incommensurability entry is the accessible, refereed statement used here. It is **not** an independent witness to the figures: philosophy of science and the Tether share the problem of theory comparison, so the agreement on "keep separate" is weak corroboration, declared as such in the ledger.
- Collins's experimenter's regress and third-wave expertise work are the sociology-of-science account of why an anchor can be unavailable even inside a single discipline. This is the strongest low-overlap witness because its evidence is laboratory fieldwork, not philosophical argument (F4).
- Merton's norms are the classic statement of academic authority's own account of its anchor. The 2010 Anderson et al. paper also records counternorms (particularism, interestedness, organized dogmatism), which is the drift Fig 2 :34 predicts when an anchor starts updating on the frames.
- The TEK and AEK literature (Ludwig and Poliseli) is the strongest witness for traditional authority as a genuine frame with its own validation criteria, and for the difference between import and assimilation.
- The Galileo and Kitzmiller records are primary sources, not secondary commentary. They give provenance for concrete rulings. A ruling with clean provenance is still to be corrected by the ground (Fig 10 :65-66), so they are used as examples of mechanism, not as authorities.

## 4. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded rules for offering the material:

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Lead with the shape claim and its refuter. Sending the framework first sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the derivation before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** describe the effect before choosing a frame, or any frame will seem to fit. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure before sending any literature.
- **Seat Map :163:** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Do not select recipients by likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then, if invited, the sources above, with the note that the verbatim figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) if asked, offer the formula as a reusable method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.
