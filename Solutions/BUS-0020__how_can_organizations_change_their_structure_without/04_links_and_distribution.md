## Three things in the literature that bear directly on the residual

1. **The union is already inside the measurement definition.** Galan's 2023 review of 91 empirical studies (*The Learning Organization* 30(2)) operationalizes knowledge loss as *"the extent to which personnel's skills and partnerships (both internal and external) are lost because of organizational member departures."* Skills and partnerships are two dimensions of one construct in that literature, which is exactly the D1 and D2 split this derivation argues for. It also names downsizing and restructuring as a leading involuntary-turnover context. Do not present this as a second witness to the figures: it is one evidence pool speaking in the same direction.
2. **Disruption severs transmission chains, and basic function can survive while nuance does not.** Bates et al. (2025) find that elephant societies can function at a basic level after severe disruption but lose *"fine-scale social discrimination and interpretation of predatory threat"*, with aberrant behaviour in some cull-orphan populations. This is the shape of F6: reproduction numbers are not the residual; the lost nuance is. It is a genuinely low-overlap corroborator (animal behaviour), so it supports the mechanism, not the organizational fact.
3. **Knowledge of who knows what is a repository with a measurable payment.** Argote et al. (2025) show that transactive memory develops through working together, decays with membership churn, and predicts objective patient outcomes. This is Fig 12 :5 and Fig 12 :107-110 at a different stratum: the arrangement carries the information, and a boundary that drops the record makes the next cohort pay again.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the essays.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (leading clause: *"disqualifying for a verdict:"*). A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :61** - *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."* Anyone who has published on retention has standing in the outcome; declare it rather than treating agreement as neutral.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. This is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical README and the tacit-knowledge essay. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before this. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. This is permitted: generators may cross (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-one output survives. As drafted, the output is a shape, not a retention prescription; F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is an honest gap, not a gap to be filled with a number before sending.

Disclose that plainly, cite the figures as sources, and do not present it as an empirical finding.
