# Expert verification packet: the Tether on structural change and retention

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of organizational change or knowledge retention can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No organization was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Changing structure does not preserve or deplete one stock of "knowledge, relationships and trust". It acts on three conditions with different anchors, and each must be reported per slot: (a) knowledge survives when a method, not a recording of one occasion, crosses the structural boundary and when the arrangement is recorded or rebuilt through contact, because the bond carries information the atom list does not; (b) relationships survive when the contact the residual required is reproduced in the substituted habitat, since a restructuring never removes a habitat but replaces it and brings actors you did not choose; (c) trust survives when the channel to a ground that does not update is kept, together with a seat able to refuse a verdict. Over a fixed atom set, old and new structure are isomers: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The framework returns this shape and its refuters, not a retention percentage or a named ritual.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - The three losses are a union, indexed per slot, with no derivable percentage

- **Statement:** "knowledge", "relationships" and "trust" are not one quantity; they have different anchors; a global retention average destroys the live signal; no figure line returns a number.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* and Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.
- **Refuter:** one ground-anchored measurement in which knowledge availability, required contact and refusal capacity always move together in the same direction across every slot and every habitat, leaving no residue; or a figure line that derives a unique optimal retention arrangement. Either collapses the union or closes the gap.

### C2 - Method over recording; the bond carries what the atom list does not

- **Statement:** a handover that captures what an expert did on one occasion is a recording; what crosses usefully is a method that can be reapplied. Where the arrangement is not recorded, the result carries forward but the deriving does not, so the next cohort pays again. This is the figure-governed form of tacit knowledge.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :107-110 *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- **What to check:** is the distinction between codification and personalization in your field a difference in what crosses, or only in format? Argote et al. (2025, *Organization Science*) measure a "supra-individual knowledge repository" built from "who knows what" against patient outcomes; Meakins (2008) reports knowledge that "wasn't actually registered anywhere" lost in downsizing alongside knowledge passed "by observation or by working alongside the more experienced." Do these support the bond claim or merely the headcount claim?
- **Refuter:** a recorded artifact, with no new contact, that fully predicts post-change performance at every slot against a non-updating anchor, with no re-derivation cost rising anywhere. Then the bond claim is idle for this case.

### C3 - Habitat substitution and the refusal seat

- **Statement:** a restructuring substitutes a habitat rather than removing one; it fails silently by not reproducing required contact and by introducing actors you did not choose. Trust is not a stock of goodwill but an anchor that does not update plus a channel that still carries fresh readings and a seat that can refuse.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Fig 11 :68-69 *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"*. Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.
- **What to check:** Bates et al. (2025, *Philosophical Transactions B*) find that severely disrupted elephant groups "are less cohesive, may exhibit reduced fitness or calf survival and respond inappropriately to threats and predators" while basic function can persist. Is that the shape of a restructuring that keeps the org chart but loses the nuance? Does a transition committee that grades its own report retain a refusal seat?
- **Refuter:** a retention gain that survives per-slot indexing while the needed contact edge is not reproduced and no outside seat can refuse; or measured channel fidelity rising while refusal disappears and outcomes at the ground still improve. Either breaks the mapping.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a retention percentage, layer count, span of control, or named ritual such as pairing or documentation standard. F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a declared gap.
- Not that shared culture or a post-merger mindset proves retention. Agreement from a common evidence pool is disqualifying as a verdict (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*) and efficient only for a convention (Fig 2 :41).
- Not that all knowledge should be retained. The MDL bargain in Fig 5 :26 (*"does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?"*) applies; "valuable" is a threshold needing a ground.
- Not that people are interchangeable containers. The claim concerns where knowledge is carried: in bonds and records, not in names.
- Not grounded. No experiment, dataset or organization was measured by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

Instruments, not arguments:

1. **Availability test:** after the change, at each slot, is the needed term a lookup or a re-derivation? Rising re-derivation with no new contact falsifies C2.
2. **Correction-trend test:** does the size of newcomers' corrections shrink with contact? If not, contact is not rebuilding the arrangement.
3. **Contact-reproduction test:** does every contact edge the residual required before still reach it after, and which new actors were unintentionally introduced? (C3.)
4. **Refusal test:** is there a seat that can refuse a verdict it did not author, against a ground it cannot move? (C3.)
5. Any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :55, :57, Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 4 :22, :25, Fig 12 :5, :107-110, Fig 11 :66, :67 and Fig 10 :24, :42. Twelve short passages, all verbatim in the local figure text.
2. Check C2 against your own field: is the method/recording distinction real in your data, or is it a formatting distinction?
3. Check C3 against one change you know: was the failing thing a missing contact, a broken channel, or an absent refusal seat?
4. Name one instrument that would discriminate lookup from re-derivation at a slot, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
