## QUESTION

How can organizations change their structure without losing valuable knowledge, relationships, or trust?

Restated in effect terms: after a change of which actors carry the loop and which bonds join them, does `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` stay low at the slots that matter, or does the change create new residuals by making a needed term unavailable, breaking a required contact edge, or removing the seat that can refuse? Option A treats the three nouns as one stock to be preserved by a retention program. Option B treats them as three conditions with different anchors. What each leads to: A averages per-slot residuals, mistakes internal agreement for retention, and offers a single lever to a union; B reports three separate verdicts, keeps the anchors distinct, and declares the naming of a specific arrangement as a gap. The figures decide for B (Fig 9 :55).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: at least three places, `Γ`, `a`, `o'`, indexed by slot and ordered in time, with a fourth place for the bond between slots and a fifth for the anchor around which trust is measured. Symmetry: structure is an arrangement and the bond is not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46), so a re-bonding is a different substance, not a spelling (Fig 12 :94); "better" is an ordered comparison, not a symmetric join (Operators table :51). Scale: the person's loop, the team across the membrane (Fig 4), the organization across the time channel (Fig 10 :24). The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :27).
- **D1 The record (knowledge).** Actors: terms, atoms, bonds, documents, methods, carriers. Currency: cost of re-derivation, availability at a slot. Anchor: whether the needed arrangement is a lookup or must be re-earned (Fig 12 :107-110). Admitted.
- **D2 The habitat (relationships).** Actors: people, teams, customers, conditions, contact edges. Currency: contact, cascade, influence travel. Anchor: whether contact required by the residual is reproduced after the change (Fig 11 :67). Admitted.
- **D3 The channel and seat (trust).** Actors: the anchor, the verifier seat, members, the report. Currency: channel fidelity, refusal power. Anchor: the ground that does not update (Fig 2 :29) and a seat able to refuse (Fig 10 :42). Admitted.
- **D4 The change lever.** Actors: the redesign, its bonds, its instruments. Currency: description cost and action cost. Anchor: whether the lever reaches the broken link (Fig 3). Admitted.
- **X1 Knowledge as a free-floating stock.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain; a property of D1.
- **X2 Trust as a feeling; X3 Culture as sole cause.** Already inside D3 or D1. Absorbed.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 form the retention compound; D3 and D4 form the verification-and-intervention compound. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

**Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Diagnose which link stops the chain before redesigning above it: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test: my first census mapped the three nouns onto the six-term set, with trust as a seventh state. That strained Fig 13 :41 (*"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."*). ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: the question is a union of three conditions, not one stock. Knowledge is settled by availability at a slot and is carried by a method and a bond, not by an ingredient list; where the arrangement is not recorded, each lineage pays again. Relationships are settled by reproduced contact, because restructuring substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose. Trust is settled by a channel to a ground that does not update plus a seat able to refuse. Old and new structures over a fixed atom set are isomers; carriers divide into catalysts and spent parts. The specific retention arrangement is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The management evidence pool corroborates the split but carries no verdict weight; the low-overlap witness corroborates the mechanism shape only.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected intervention. A firm failing the contact condition would be sold better documentation, and a firm failing the refusal-seat condition would be told to increase alignment, so one lever would be applied to a union that needs three. The cost is bounded and locatable because the shape, the five refuters and the lineage are recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **undoable:** yes. Nothing is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Fig 6 amendments). The shape and its refuters are in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one without withdrawing a primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five pre-registered falsifiers: (1) one condition moving all three losses together across every slot in every habitat; (2) a retention gain with no reproduced contact and no method crossing; (3) a recording fully predicting post-change performance with no re-derivation cost; (4) durable refusal capacity with reconstructing verifiers and an outcome-fixing convention; (5) a figure line deriving a unique optimal retention arrangement. An instrument showing that per-slot availability is fully predicted by headcount alone, from a genuinely different evidence pool, would also reverse the D1 verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
