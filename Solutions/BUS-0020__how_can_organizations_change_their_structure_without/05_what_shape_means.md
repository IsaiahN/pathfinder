## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: a method must cross the boundary; the contact the residual requires must be reproduced; a ground that does not update and a seat that can refuse must remain reachable. A specific retention percentage, org chart, span, or named ritual is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the operative rule for a handover document: it can carry effects, not the history that built them.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A measured per-slot improvement after a specific redesign would name that arrangement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Bringing in a practice from another company is an import; it needs provenance and a shadow test, or it is a wrong atom at a cost above starting from primitives.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Tacit knowledge is the central case, and it is already figure-governed

This is where the essays and the figures meet most tightly for BUS-0020.

- Polanyi, quoted in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"we can know more than we can tell."* The essay adds: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."*
- Harry Collins's three kinds of tacit knowledge in the same essay: **relational** (*"things we could describe in principle if someone put effort into describing them"*), **somatic** (*"things our bodies can do but we cannot describe how, like balancing on a bike"*), and **collective** (*"knowledge we draw that is the property of society, such as the rules for language"*). All three need high contact to rebuild on the receiving end.
- Fig 12 :107-110 supplies the mechanism: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."* Where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving is a lookup; where it is not, *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
- Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A head start makes the next search cheaper; it is not the search result.
- Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* And Fig 8 :54: *"underivable, and searchable"* from contact. So tacit knowledge is out of reach in the text you hold and within reach through contact. That is a shape plus a refuter, not a silence.
- The essay's instrument: *"The size of a correction tells you how much of the knowledge is still tacit for that learner, and it should shrink as their experience builds. That makes tacit transfer measurable: watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* This is the missing instrument Fig 3 :27 demands: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 5. Why agreement after a merger is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow and blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A post-merger culture survey reads the organization's current state and cannot say which history produced it. Naming "shared culture" as the retained knowledge is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* The transition team's own report cannot validate the transition.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any concrete retention mechanism from being named. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in `Γ`. Network is already among the six (Fig 13 :24), the library and its bonds are already held, and the anchor and channel are already figure terms. Naming "preserve contact edges" or "keep a refusal seat" is a binding onto held atoms, which is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains forbidden is naming the specific arrangement that works in a given habitat without a ground reading, because that is a name where only a shape is available. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering well-known names to agree that a redesign retained knowledge is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any organization and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
