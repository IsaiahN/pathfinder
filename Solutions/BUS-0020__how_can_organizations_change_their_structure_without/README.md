## What is being decided, in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot (Fig 1 :19). An organization contemplates a change of structure, that is a change in which actors carry the loop and which bonds join them. The question names three things that might be lost: valuable knowledge, relationships, and trust. The effect question is whether those three are one stock that a redesign either preserves or depletes, or three different quantities with different anchors that must be reported separately. The figures say the latter: the three losses are a union of conditions, not one score (Fig 9 :55). What an answer would look like is bound before the run: for each loss it must name (a) the slot at which the loss registers, (b) the anchor that settles whether it occurred, (c) what is actually carried across the structural boundary, and (d) what would reverse the call (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Agreement among people who read the same internal report is the pool talking.
3. "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.

Legally: sending unsolicited bulk email to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
