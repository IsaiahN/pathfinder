**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 form the retention compound; D3 and D4 the verification compound. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 (union), Fig 4 :22 (methods up, head starts down), Fig 12 :5 and :107-110 (bond and record), Fig 11 :66-69 (substitution and silent failures), Fig 10 :24/:42 (channel and refusal), Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: three conditions with separate anchors; carry a method; reproduce contact; keep a non-updating ground and a refusal seat; old and new are isomers; no named ritual.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete retention program: a documentation standard, a mentoring scheme, a contact map, an outside review board, and a claim that the redesign "retained 90 percent of knowledge".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond type**. The shorthand says "reproduce contact"; the figure says the relation is typed and not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46). A contact map that records only who talks to whom loses which bond carried which knowledge;
- the **method versus recording distinction**. "Document the method" can silently become a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :25), which the shorthand forbids but the concrete program does not distinguish;
- the **refusal condition**. "Keep an outside reviewer" can become a verifier who reconstructs the claim (Fig 10 :42) or a convention that decides the outcome (Fig 10 :47). The shorthand's "seat that can refuse" is stricter than the concrete program;
- the **threshold on "valuable"**. The shorthand does not price a term; the concrete "90 percent retained" assumes all atoms are assets, against the MDL bargain in Fig 5 :26.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the three conditions must be anchored and reported per slot, and a method, not a recording, must cross the boundary. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
