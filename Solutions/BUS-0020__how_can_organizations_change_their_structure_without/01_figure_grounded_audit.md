## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

`R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|`, the gap between what an organization's model predicts a redesign will deliver and what the ground returns after the change. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The slots here are individual knowledge items, individual relationships, and individual decision or trust situations, each at a time index. The question asks: can a change of structure shrink the whole residual by preserving what is valuable, or does the change create new residuals in other slots while apparently fixing old ones?

The question's three nouns are not one stock. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Knowledge, relationships and trust have different anchors: knowledge is settled by whether a needed term is available and usable at a slot, relationships by whether a required contact edge still reaches the residual, trust by whether a ground still settles questions without moving when a frame reports success. A case in two conditions is categorically different from a case in one (Fig 9 :57), so a single overall retention score is not a verdict, it is an average that kills the signal (Fig 1 :22).

**Shape bound before the run.** An acceptable answer must, for each of the three losses: (a) name the slot where the loss registers, (b) name the anchor that settles whether it occurred, (c) state what actually crosses the structural boundary, and (d) state what would reverse the call. This is the derive-first discipline of Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

## 1. Census of R: arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity.** Relational, at least three places: the library `Γ`, the action `a` (the redesign), and the observation `o'` (what the ground returns). A fourth place carries the bond between two slots, which is where relationships and much of knowledge live. A fifth place is the anchor around which trust is measured.
- **Symmetry.** "Structure" is an arrangement, and the bond that holds it is not recoverable from the operands (Fig 12 :46). Changing one bond while holding the atoms fixed produces a different substance, not a spelling of the same one (Fig 12 :94). "Better" is a threshold comparison, an ordered bond, not a symmetric join (Operators table :51: *"a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one"*). None of the governing bonds is symmetric, so order and arrangement matter.
- **Scale.** The single person's loop (Fig 1), the team across the membrane between one agent and many (Fig 4), and the organization across time as channels keep or lose contact (Fig 10 :24). The transform between scales loses information (Fig 4 :27), so a claim about individual learning does not transfer to organizational retention without a measured round trip.

## 4. Findings F1 to F11

### F1 - The three losses are a union, not one stock (decisive for the framing)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*
Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Asking how to keep knowledge, relationships and trust treats three conditions as one list. The figures require unbundling: D1 has a record anchor, D2 has a contact anchor, D3 has a ground-and-refusal anchor. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. A program that keeps documentation but breaks contact has solved D1 and failed D2; calling that "80 percent retention" is the aggregation error.

**Mode:** `general` for the union rule, `specified` for the application here.
**Refuter:** one ground-anchored measurement in which knowledge availability, required contact and refusal capacity always move together in the same direction across every slot and every habitat, leaving no categorical residue. Then the union collapses to one loop and this finding fails.

### F2 - Each loss is per slot; an average hides the live one

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

An organization-wide "knowledge retained" score averages across teams and quarters, which is exactly the signal-killing move Fig 1 names. The relevant slot is the specific task where a needed term was unavailable, the specific relationship whose contact edge stopped reaching, the specific decision where nobody could refuse. The anchor must be something that does not move when the organization reports a successful transition: a task completion, a customer outcome, a failure that was caught.

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** a reported retention gain that survives per-slot indexing and is paid by an anchor that does not update on the organization's own transition report. Until such a measurement exists, any "we kept our knowledge" claim is a candidate, not a verdict.

### F3 - What must cross the boundary is the method, not the recording

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
Fig 4 :34-35: *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."*

A restructuring moves people, documents and reporting lines across a boundary. What survives is a generator someone can reapply, not an artifact of one occasion. A handover document that records what an expert did last March is a recording; a method for regenerating the answer under new conditions is a head start. Fig 11 :43 (*"The habitat is enumerated, never composed"*) adds the constraint: you cannot design retention by listing the things you think matter; you must read the list off the contact the residual actually has.

**Mode:** `general` for the membrane rule, `specified` for organizations.
**Refuter:** a retention intervention that works by replaying an exact recording of one occasion into a changed habitat, with per-slot `R` falling and staying down at the ground. That would falsify the claim that a recording cannot carry what a method carries.

### F4 - The bond carries knowledge the atoms do not

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

A list of who knew what, exported before a reorganization, names the atoms. It does not record the typed relation between them, so it has recorded half of itself. This is precisely why knowledge can be "present" in a firm's headcount and still unavailable: the arrangement that made it usable lived in the relation, not in the person taken alone. Argote, Guo, Haan, Rosengart, Teng and Kahn (2025, *Organization Science*) provide the world-side counterpart: a transactive memory system is a *"supra-individual knowledge repository"* built from *"who knows what"*, and stronger systems predict shorter patient stays. That is the bond, measured at a different stratum.

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** a case where the full atom list plus every explicit document fully predicts post-change performance at every slot, with no residual attributable to lost arrangement. Then the bond claim is idle here.

### F5 - Where the arrangement is not recorded, every lineage pays again

Fig 12 :107: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."*
Fig 12 :108-110: *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving is a lookup and the actions were spent once. Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so each new team pays the bill again. Tacit knowledge is this case: the compression hides the cost that built it. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026) states the mechanism in effect terms: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* The remedy is not a better document but contact, because "underivable is not unknowable" (Fig 8 :54). The world-side corroboration is unusually direct: Meakins (2008) reports knowledge that *"wasn't actually registered anywhere"* being lost during downsizing, alongside knowledge passed *"simply by observation or by working alongside the more experienced."*

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** a restructuring in which per-slot re-derivation cost does not rise after the carriers leave, measured against a non-updating task anchor, with no new contact introduced. That would show the arrangement was fully recorded and this finding is wrong for that case.

### F6 - Restructuring is habitat substitution, with two silent failure modes

Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."*
Fig 11 :68-69: *"what you failed to reproduce, and what you brought with you"*
Fig 11 :61: *"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*

You cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world that does not consult you. A new structure always substitutes a new habitat and always brings actors you did not choose. The two silent failure modes are what you failed to reproduce (the contact edge that carried a needed term) and what you unintentionally introduced (a new conduit, a new incentive, a new dependency). Both are invisible until the goal fails or until the introduced actor acts. Bates, Fishlock, Plotnik, de Silva and Shannon (2025, *Philosophical Transactions B*) review 95 papers on social disruption in wild elephants and show that disruption *"can sever information transmission chains"* and that severely disrupted groups *"are less cohesive, may exhibit reduced fitness or calf survival and respond inappropriately to threats and predators."* Their management recommendation is to preserve transmission chains, which is the same shape as reproducing required contact.

**Mode:** `general` for the substitution law, `specified` for organizations.
**Refuter:** a restructuring that removes a habitat without substituting one, so that the residual's required contact simply vanishes and the organization still meets its ground at every slot. That is an agent-only improvement the figure says cannot happen.

### F7 - Trust is channel reachability to an anchor that does not update

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
Fig 2 :33-34: *"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*
Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*

"Trust" has no anchor as a feeling, so it is not a domain on its own (X2). Re-grounded, it is a property of D3: the ground still settles, the channel still carries fresh readings, and no party can move the anchor by reporting success. A restructuring that replaces an outside checker with an internal one, or that pools all readings into one dashboard, does not "reduce trust" as a stock; it breaks the channel and lets the frames drift together, which is why nobody notices. Galan's review (2023, *The Learning Organization*) defines knowledge loss in a way that joins the two: the loss of *"personnel's skills and partnerships (both internal and external)"* on departure. Partnerships are D2; the willingness to share them is D3.

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** a measured rise in channel fidelity and independent anchor contact after a restructuring, with reported trust falling and performance at the ground deteriorating anyway. That would decouple the re-grounding from the phenomenon and falsify this mapping.

### F8 - The refusal seat must survive the change

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*
Fig 10 :61: *"And standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on."*

"Losing trust" often means losing the seat that could say no. If the people judging a transition are the people who designed it, and if the reporting convention has been promoted into the outcome, there is no refusal left, even when everyone is friendly. A structure that removes every outside checker produces exactly this. A structure that builds purpose-built substitute seats, each with one narrow slice of authority, has not removed accountability, it has redistributed it. Standing earned before the change is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on, so it must be declared rather than treated as neutral.

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** high measured refusal capacity in an organization where verifiers routinely reconstruct the claims they check and where the reporting convention fixes the outcome. That would show the seat condition is not necessary here.

### F9 - Diagnose which link stops before crediting the redesign

Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
Fig 3 :29: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*

A change of structure is an intervention on vocabulary and network, not on perception or the ground. If the broken link is the objective (nobody can say what would count as progress after the change), more autonomy pushes decisions down to people who cannot score them. Effort above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it. The instrument for tacit loss is a trend in correction size, not a count of training sessions: the tacit-knowledge essay says *"The size of a correction tells you how much of the knowledge is still tacit for that learner, and it should shrink as their experience builds."*

**Mode:** `specified`.
**Refuter:** a measured, durable improvement in per-slot `R` when the vocabulary and objective links are demonstrably absent and unmeasured. That would break the dependency order.

### F10 - A retention percentage, org chart or named ritual is a `gap`

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*
Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

No figure line selects a retention percentage, a layer count, a span, or a named ritual such as pairing or documentation standard. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Any working structure must keep a method crossing the boundary, reproduce the contact the residual requires, and keep a seat able to refuse. Which specific arrangement does that in a given habitat is a ground question requiring an instrument, not a figure question requiring more search. This is `unreachable`, not silence, and not an excuse to invent a number.

**Mode:** `general` for the gap.
**Refuter:** a figure line that derives a unique optimal retention arrangement, or a ground measurement that settles it across habitats with the same per-slot residual. Until one fires, the honest answer to "how much" is a declared gap.

### F11 - Strain recorded and resolved: trust is not a seventh term

My first pass tried to map the three nouns onto Fig 13's six terms, reading knowledge as library, relationships as network, and trust as a new state alongside space, time and energy. That strains Fig 13 :41: *"Each is indivisible: no arrangement of the other five yields it."* It only fits with a forced reading, so by the strain test it governs another quantity.

**Re-census:** Fig 13 governs the minimum set for any composed system. "Knowledge" is a property of the library and the bonds that join its atoms (D1); "relationships" is the network structure Fig 13 :24 already holds (D2); "trust" is a property of the anchor-and-channel relation (D3), not a member of the set. The earlier reading is kept here as evidence, not erased. See §7 and `11_audit.md`.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the derive-first discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is:

> The three losses are a union with separate anchors. Knowledge survives a structural change when a method, not a recording, crosses the boundary and when the arrangement is recorded or re-derived through contact. Relationships survive when the contact required by the residual is reproduced in the substituted habitat. Trust survives when the channel to a non-updating ground and a seat able to refuse are both kept. No figure line selects a retention percentage or a named ritual.

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. One ground-anchored measurement shows knowledge availability, required contact and refusal capacity moving together across all slots in every habitat under a single condition, leaving no categorical residue (against F1).
2. A retention gain survives per-slot indexing while the needed contact edge is not reproduced and no new method crosses the boundary (against F3, F5, F6).
3. A recorded artifact fully predicts post-change performance at every slot with no residual attributable to lost arrangement (against F4).
4. An organization shows durable refusal capacity with verifiers who can reconstruct the claims they check and a reporting convention that fixes outcomes (against F8).
5. A figure line is found that derives a unique optimal retention arrangement, closing the F10 gap.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Do not answer with a single retention score. Unbundle D1, D2 and D3 and report three verdicts, never an average (F1, F2).
2. Treat the handover as a generator transfer. Ask what can be reapplied under new conditions, not what was documented about last quarter (F3, F5).
3. Map the contacts the residual actually needs before drawing the new chart, and reproduce them deliberately; expect to inherit unintended actors (F6).
4. Keep an outside refusal seat. A transition committee that grades its own report is not accountability (F8).
5. Instrument tacit loss by the trend in correction size, not by counting sessions (F9).
6. Diagnose the broken link before intervening (F9).
7. Publish the F10 gap rather than filling it with a percentage. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
8. Offer the refuter before the theory. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

## 8. Verdict of this audit

The figures do not return an org chart, a retention percentage or a named ritual. They say the three losses are different quantities in a union (Fig 9 :55), each indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19); that what crosses a structural boundary must be a method and a head start, not a recording (Fig 4 :22, :25); that knowledge lives in the bond and in whether the arrangement is recorded (Fig 12 :5, :107-110); that a redesign substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose (Fig 11 :66, :67); and that trust is a channel to a non-updating anchor plus a seat that can refuse (Fig 2 :29, Fig 10 :24, :42). Those are separate verdicts, never one score. The retention arrangement is a declared gap. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Strains after re-census: none live; one retracted strain (F11) preserved in §4 and §7.
