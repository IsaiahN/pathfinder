## Three things in the literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The paradox is already stated as a union of three conditions.** The AMPS abstract lists the three mutually incompatible statements. That is the world-level counterpart of Fig 9 :4-5, "Split it rather than search." It corroborates the decomposition, not any resolution.
2. **The proposed resolutions share an evidence pool.** The Apologia has four authors in common with AMPS; ER=EPR is a response to the same construction. By Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool," so the literature's internal convergence cannot be counted as independent corroboration. Overlap is high and is declared as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
3. **The monogamy constraint is mathematics, not a Tether claim.** The Coffman-Kundu-Wootters trade-off is the low-overlap source for D3's anchor. It is a theorem about qubits, not a black hole result, which is exactly why it provides an independent check on the comparison reading in `06_isomers_and_order.md`. The Einstein Online article performs the same low-overlap service for the GR slot: it states the equivalence principle as a near-horizon, local, no-drama claim.
