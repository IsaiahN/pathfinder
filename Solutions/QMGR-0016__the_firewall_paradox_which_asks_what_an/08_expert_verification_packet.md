# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the firewall paradox

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structure of the question, `general` on the claim that a ground is owed, `gap` on the physical outcome. Not `grounded`.** No horizon measurement is claimed. This is a shape and an checking arrangement, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The firewall paradox is a union of three conditions (radiation purity, exterior effective field theory validity, and the infalling observer's local reading), not one hard question, so it must be split before it is searched (Fig 9). Because the question is constitutively about an observer's experience, the only legitimate anchor is a horizon detector reading that neither GR nor QM can move; current exterior radiation data are a projection that does not discriminate among source stories. The two frames must range against that anchor separately, and their present literature shares one evidence pool, so its internal agreement cannot count as corroboration. No instrument currently returns such a reading, so the question is unreachable at this budget (not underivable in principle), and no figure line selects smooth versus firewall. The framework returns the shape of the missing measurement, not the name of a microstate, so the physical verdict is a `gap` reported as a verdict, with refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 2

- **Statement:** a non-shrinking disagreement in which each rule works on a different subset is a union compound and must be split; the anchor for a question about experience is the experience-as-measurement, which must not move when a frame pushes on it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions do shrink with effort into one question with a falling residual, or exhibit a reading of the horizon state that moves when the interpreting frame changes. Either breaks the split or the anchor claim.

### C2: physics-facing; checkable by a physicist against the AMPS construction

- **Statement:** exterior radiation data do not discriminate among the proposed horizon sources; the monogamy constraint is a threshold comparison over an entanglement budget, and the smooth-horizon term is the held term with a persistent flat residual. The "firewall" term is assembled from held atoms with zero settlement, so it is a candidate, not an accepted term.
- **What to check:** is the AMPS three-statement form a faithful union of conditions? Does the Coffman-Kundu-Wootters trade-off (arXiv:quant-ph/9907047, "there is a trade-off between A's entanglement with B and its entanglement with C") apply to the relevant modes? Is any current observation capable of naming the source from the projection?
- **Source for the operator reading:** Operators table :47-51: comparison is "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes." Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a state violating the monogamy inequality for the relevant modes; or a ground reading that pays for one of the two terms; or an observation that discriminates among the source stories without a new instrument. Any of these breaks C2.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a resolution of the firewall paradox. No side is taken.
