## The question as an effect

Restated as a choice between two routes, with what each leads to:

- **Route A, take a side.** Assert either "the infalling observer crosses smoothly" or "the infalling observer meets a firewall." This yields a physical verdict, but by Fig 2 the verdict can only be legitimate if an anchor settles it and neither frame can move that anchor. No such horizon reading currently exists, so Route A delivers a `general`-mode verdict dressed as a grounded one.
- **Route B, decompose and range against a ground.** Treat the paradox as a union compound of three conditions that each work in their own domain (Fig 9), describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9), and report what a ground-discriminating measurement would have to look like. This yields a checkable shape, a written refuter, and an open slot until the ground settles it.

The Shape claim (what an answer would have to look like) is bound as an ordinary term before the run: *an answer names the local state a detector at the horizon would read, and says which of the two frames ranges correctly against that reading.* Binding it lets it accrue a residual like anything else instead of remaining an invisible preference (Fig 5 :61-66).

## Headline result

The figures decide the **checking arrangement**, not the **physical outcome**. They govern four things: split the union compound rather than search it as one question (Fig 9); the anchor for "what the infaller experiences" is that experience, which must not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2); GR and QM must range against that anchor separately, and agreement from one evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2); and no proto-instrument currently registers the near-horizon local state, so the question is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle (Fig 6, Fig 8). **No figure line governs smooth versus firewall, so the physical verdict is `gap` at this budget.** "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room is not an anchor. "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." (Fig 7 :35)
2. Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation. "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). The firewall literature is largely one pool, so its internal consensus is one witness.
3. Sending the interior before the residual spends the reader's independence. "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42)
