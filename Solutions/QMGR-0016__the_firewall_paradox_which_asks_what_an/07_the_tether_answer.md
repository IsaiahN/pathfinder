# The Tether's answer on the firewall paradox

**Mode: `specified` on the structure of the question, `general` on the claim that a ground is owed, `gap` on the physical outcome. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An infalling observer's local detector is predicted to read two incompatible things. Semi-classical GR plus the equivalence principle says there is nothing unusual at the horizon; the AMPS argument says unitarity plus exterior EFT plus entanglement monogamy forces a high-energy excitation. The disagreement does not shrink with effort while each condition keeps working on its own subset. Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

So the residual is a union compound, not one hard problem. Its live slot is the horizon reading, and that slot has no instrument.

## 2. The shape

1. **Split the compound; do not search it as one question.** Radiation purity, exterior EFT validity and the infalling reading each get their own loop. Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Clearing one condition leaves the intersection standing.
2. **The anchor is the horizon reading itself.** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." Because the question is constitutively about an observer's experience, that experience (as a detector reading) is the legitimate anchor, not a vote of experts.
3. **GR and QM must range against it separately.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Their current agreement and disagreement come from one evidence pool, so neither corroborates the other (Fig 2 :40).
4. **The question is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle.** Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it." Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Theory may precede its instrument, so this is not a ban on quantum gravity; it is a statement about what no instrument now returns.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." No figure line selects smooth or firewall, so the physical verdict is `gap`. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". The shape delivered is the split, the anchor requirement, the independence requirement, and the character of the missing measurement.
6. **What is owed first is a refusal, not a new physical term.** The held term "smooth horizon" has a persistent, flat residual, so `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 owes a refusal before a mint. ## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a resolution of the firewall paradox. No side is taken.
- Not a quantum gravity model, not a horizon microstate, not a claim that the horizon "is" a gradient, spectrum or network. Those are bindings from the physics frame, provenanced as such, and unproven here.
- Not a derivation of GR from QM or of QM from GR. The compound stays a compound.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an experiment.
- Not "consensus settles it." The firewall literature largely shares an evidence pool, so its internal agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating horizon instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Detector test:** what does a local detector carried across the horizon of a large black hole actually register? Any returned reading that neither frame can move becomes the anchor.
- **Analogue discriminator:** can an analogue platform register an interior-local observable rather than only exterior Hawking-like correlations? If not, the inward track has no edge to extend from (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Independence test:** can two frames with disjoint evidence be ranged against the same reading and remain distinct after it arrives? If they cannot, triangulation has not happened.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a verbatim figure line that directly selects the outcome after re-census; a shrinking of the three conditions into one question; an existing instrument already returning a horizon reading; a state violating the monogamy inequality for the relevant modes.
