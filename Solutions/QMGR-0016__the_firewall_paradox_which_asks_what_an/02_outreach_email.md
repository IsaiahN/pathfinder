# Outreach draft — check the residual and the refuter before the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is understood as a `gap` verdict. Do not present the Tether as a theory of quantum gravity or as a resolution of the firewall. If the message implies otherwise, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about the firewall paradox, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one structural claim about the firewall paradox that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`):** three propositions do not compose into one answer. Radiation purity, low-energy effective field theory outside the horizon, and an unremarkable local experience for the infalling observer each work well in their own domain, and the disagreement does not shrink with effort. The live residual sits in one slot only: what a detector carried across the horizon would actually read. The published options, smooth crossing or a high-energy firewall, are predictions from two frames that have not been ranged against a horizon reading, because no such reading exists.
>
> **The claim:** the figures I am using decide the *checking arrangement*, not the physical outcome. They say (1) split a union compound rather than search it as one question, (2) the anchor for a question about an observer's experience is that experience, which must not change when a frame pushes on it, (3) the two frames must range against that anchor separately, and agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two, and (4) the horizon reading is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle. The framework returns the shape of the missing measurement, not the name of a microstate. **The physical verdict is therefore `gap`: no figure line selects smooth or firewall.** That is a verdict, not a silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not that the Tether resolves the paradox, not that the metric or any other object *is* a figure primitive, not a new quantum gravity model, and not that a consensus of firewall papers constitutes corroboration. Those papers largely share an evidence pool. The claim is about the structure of the question and what would have to be measured.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these before writing any of the above:
> 1. A figure line that, after re-census, directly selects the horizon outcome.
> 2. Evidence that the three conditions shrink into one question whose residual trends down.
> 3. An instrument or observation that returns the local horizon state, with neither frame able to move it.
> 4. An existing proto-instrument already registering such a reading at some resolution.
> 5. A state violating the entanglement trade-off for the relevant modes.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
