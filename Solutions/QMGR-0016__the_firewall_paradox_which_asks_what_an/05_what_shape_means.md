## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `the horizon has a local state a crossing detector would read` is a shape (an ordinary term with a stated instrument).
- `the state is a firewall` or `the state is smooth` is a name.

The shape includes the split into three conditions, the anchor requirement, and the instrument that would close it. It does not include a choice between smooth and firewall.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with "trilemma," "monogamy," "equivalence principle," "horizon," and narrow toward "what a detector reads."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the stage-1 email: a description of what a horizon detector would return, checkable by someone who does not hold the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." Here the stop is earlier and honest: the question-as-tether is not satisfied, so we stop at the limitation of the instruments and record the lineage. "Nothing has been found yet" is not "nothing exists" (Fig 8 :39).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, a local horizon reading that neither GR nor QM can move.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A quantum gravity model could import a horizon microstate, but its provenance and its shadow (which residual it explains) would have to be recorded.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." So no amount of rearranging Space, Time, Energy, Spectrum, Gradient and Network produces a horizon measurement. A new axis, that is, an instrument, is owed.

## 4. Why naming the horizon outcome would be the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes create identical readings, so "behavior is a projection with lossy information." The ambiguity was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," the spectrometer.

Applied here: the exterior radiation record is a projection. It is compatible with several source stories (smooth interior with complementarity, firewall, ER=EPR, fuzzball-like microstructures). Reading "firewall" or "smooth" off that projection is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources give the same reading. What is owed is the discriminator, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A consensus of papers is not a validator; a horizon reading is.

## 5. Question answered: what licenses a particular horizon state?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular local state is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is why `01` records naming "gradient" at the horizon as retracted (§8) and why `07` leaves the slot open.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (matches exemplar's §5b move)

I initially wrote the rule as a blanket ban on names. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive *not held* in Γ. Smooth and firewall are not missing primitives; they are two bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms (Energy, Network, Gradient, Spectrum). Naming a held atom is a **binding**, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong in the popular framing is calling a binding a derivation that moves the wall: Fig 6 says "IMPORT adds an atom," and adding a binding to an already-held atom adds none.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the exterior reading still does not determine the source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are kept visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous firewall papers gathered to endorse the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, plus a refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "simulacra." So `01`, `06` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle the firewall question. No measurement was taken.
