## 0. The claim under audit

**Question (QM & GR Questions.txt line 23, Black Holes and Strong Gravity):** "The firewall paradox, which asks what an infalling observer actually experiences at the horizon."

**Effect restatement.** The AMPS argument states that three propositions cannot all be true: (i) Hawking radiation is in a pure state, (ii) low-energy effective field theory stays valid outside a microscopic distance from the horizon, (iii) the infalling observer encounters nothing unusual at the horizon. AMPS then notes that the conservative resolution is that "the infalling observer burns up at the horizon" (Almheiri, Marolf, Polchinski, Sully, arXiv:1207.3123, abstract, read 2026-11-23). So two well-confirmed frames issue incompatible predictions about one quantity: the local state read by a detector carried across the horizon.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run** (Formula "Direction"): *an answer names the local state a horizon-crossing detector would read, and says which frame ranges correctly against that reading.* Until a ground reading exists the term predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so no check in the loop can see it (Fig 5 :61-64). Binding it makes the preference refusable.

**Two routes, stated at the outset.** Route A asserts smooth or firewall and delivers a verdict with no anchor. Route B splits the compound, describes the residual, and reports the shape of a ground-discriminating measurement. This audit runs Route B and says plainly where it stops.

## 1. The residual in effect terms

R is measured per slot, never aggregated. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :28: "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."

| slot | object tracked | prediction from its frame | live residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | the external radiation record | radiation is pure, information preserved (unitarity) | near zero in its own domain |

Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state." The paradox is not that all of physics fails. It is that three conditions each work well on a different subset and only the intersection stays live. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

- **Arity:** three conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? - **Symmetry:** the monogamy constraint is a threshold relation over an entanglement budget, not a symmetric join. Operators table :47-51: "is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes." Smooth and firewall are ordered, competing predictions, not two names for one state.
- **Scale:** first order inside each frame (a horizon region for GR, a radiation Hilbert space for QM), second order at their intersection where the comparison is attempted.

**Strain test applied at the residual.** Reading the firewall as a single R would strain Fig 1 and Fig 9 together. Re-census gives three slots, and the strain disappears. This is the README's rule: "a reading that makes one contradict another has misidentified its domain, not found a flaw."

## 4. Findings

### F1 — The paradox is a union compound; split it rather than search it (primary, triangulated)

Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
Fig 9 :55-56: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

The AMPS abstract corroborates at the world level: three statements "cannot all be true," which is exactly a union of three conditions, each well formed on its own subset. The firewall question is not asking for a single lever. It is asking what happens where the three conditions overlap.

- **Mode:** `general` (structural), corroborated at the physics level.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 9 (split rule) and the AMPS three-statement formulation (low overlap: a figure about unions versus a 2012 physics paper).
- **Refuter:** show that the three conditions do shrink with effort into one well-formed question whose residual trends down, or that one condition is not actually satisfied in its own domain. If either holds, the split is wrong and the union reading is withdrawn.

### F2 — The anchor for "what the infaller experiences" is that experience, and it does not currently exist (primary, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."
Fig 2 :36-38: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it. Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

The question is constitutively about the local state read by an infalling observer. So the right anchor is a detector reading at the horizon, not a count of papers, not the reputation of a proposal, and not the elegance of a resolution. No infalling detector has ever returned such a reading. The exterior half is anchored (radiation correlations are measured), but the slot in dispute (S3) has no reading.

- **Mode:** `specified` on the claim that no anchor exists for S3; `general` on the anchor rule.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and the public instrument record (LIGO detects black hole mergers but reports no horizon crossing reading; low overlap, different domain).
- **Refuter:** produce an instrument or observation that returns the local horizon state and holds fixed under both frames' pressure. One that moves when a frame reinterprets it is not an anchor (Fig 2 :33-34).

### F3 — GR and QM must range against the anchor separately; one evidence pool is one witness (primary, triangulated)

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

D2 and D3 are not two witnesses to one fact. They draw on overlapping assumptions (semiclassical background, Hilbert space factorization, the same black hole solutions). The published debate is largely one pool: AMPS (2012), the Apologia (2013), and ER=EPR (2013) cite and respond to one another; the Apologia shares four of five authors with AMPS. Their internal agreement or disagreement is framework-internal motion, not independent corroboration. A source-discriminating anchor would be required to triangulate them.

- **Mode:** `specified` on the low independence of the firewall literature; `general` on the rule.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 and bibliographic provenance of the three papers (low overlap: figure rule versus publication record).
- **Refuter:** exhibit two frames with genuinely disjoint evidence that both range against the same horizon anchor and remain distinct after the reading arrives. Until an anchor exists, this refuter cannot fire, which is the point.

### F4 — No proto-instrument registers the near-horizon local state; unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle (primary, single-source on the figure rule)

Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."
Fig 6 :34-35: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."

Exterior analogues and merger gravitational waves have registered something, but they do not return what a crossing detector reads. Under Fig 6 the inward question (is our representation adequate to hold it?) and the outward question (does another frame already hold it?) are opposed and must be run as two tracks, never read against one another (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7: "INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed; never read one against the other.").

Fig 8 :40-41: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."
Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

The firewall question is not declared unanswerable. Theory may precede the instrument (Fig 6 :37-38). It is **unreachable at this budget**: the anchor exists in principle (a local reading) but nothing currently returns it. Importing a resolution against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed (Fig 6 :51-52).

- **Mode:** `specified` on the budget verdict; `general` on the instrument rule.
- **Status:** single-source for the figure rule (Fig 6, corroborated by Fig 8 with low conceptual overlap on the budget distinction).
- **Refuter:** name any existing instrument that returns a horizon-crossing local reading, at any resolution, or show that the inward and outward tracks are not opposed for this residual. Either would move the verdict.

### F6 — The substrate can host a horizon structure, but cannot name it (single-source, kept narrow)

Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."
Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."

One could be tempted to read the horizon as a structure (gradient, spectrum or network) mediating between an exterior and an interior state. That is a figure-licensed *shape*: if two states compose at all, a structure sits between them. It is not a derivation that the horizon *is* a gradient, a spectrum or a network. Fig 13 itself distinguishes the structures: "Space, time and energy are not three readings of one quantity." (Fig 13 :35)

- **Mode:** `specified` on the legal shape, `candidate` on any binding of a particular structure.
- **Status:** single-source (Fig 13) for the shape; the particular bindings are bindings from the physics frame, provenanced as such in §9 of `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
- **Refuter:** admit a direct state-state composition the framework cannot refuse, or derive one of the six from the other five (breaks Fig 13 indivisibility). Failing that, show that no structure can occupy the horizon slot, which would break the cross-kind rule.

### F7 — "Firewall" is a candidate term, not an accepted term (single-source on the guards)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."

Applied to the term "firewall":

- **REACHABILITY:** plausible but unproven. It composes from Energy and Network atoms already in Γ, but no composition has been settled.
- **NOVELTY:** relative to Γ, not to the world (Fig 6 :22). The high-energy excitation is assembled from held atoms, so it is not a new primitive.
- **SETTLEMENT:** **zero.** No horizon reading pays for it. By the product, the mint is inert.

Fig 5 :26: "does the new term cost less to state than the confusion it removes?" Without a ground reading that question cannot be answered, so "firewall" is `candidate`, not accepted. The same applies to "smooth horizon": it is the held term with a persistent residual and is the target of a refusal under Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." A refusal is owed before a mint (Formula step 2), which the popular framing reverses.

- **Mode:** `specified` on the guard status, not a verdict on either term's physics.
- **Status:** single-source (formula guards, corroborated by Fig 5 with low overlap on the fifth kind).
- **Refuter:** a ground reading that pays for one of the two terms, or a proof that the term is outside `closure(Γ)` so REACHABILITY fails outright.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the Seat Map, "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim of this solution is: *the firewall paradox is a union compound whose disputed slot has no ground reading, so the figures govern its checking arrangement and decomposition but return no physical outcome at this budget.* It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A verbatim figure line governs the horizon outcome directly after a genuine re-census (moves F5 from `gap` to figure-governed).

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn or narrowed, and the retraction is appended here rather than erased. Until one fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
