## What the answer is, in one line

Split the question. A precise definition of alignment is available as a **convention** (a shared meaning that makes disagreement statable and violable) and as a **local, ground-anchored shape** per deployment. It is not available as a single ground-settled verdict over contested values: aggregation is blocked from mathematics and from the figures, negotiation produces coherent drift or the shared pool talking, and the framework returns a shape rather than a name. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
