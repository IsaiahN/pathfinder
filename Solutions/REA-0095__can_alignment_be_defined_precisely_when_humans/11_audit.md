## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between system behaviour and what disagreeing people can say they want. Arity: N human frames, one system, one anchor, with N at least two. Symmetry: the frames are not interchangeable and their orderings are not interpersonally comparable; the anchor is asymmetric because it does not update on frames. Scale: individual judgment first order, a specification second order, the ground outside both. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Domain 1, human value plurality.** Actors: persons, cultures, moral frameworks. Currency: reasons, votes, orderings. Anchor: none internal, because the target moves when pushed. Fails the anchor test, so it is the residual, not a settling domain.
- **Domain 2, social choice.** Actors: orderings and an aggregation function. Currency: ordinal preference. Anchor: the proof of the impossibility, legitimate because the question is constitutively about the function. Decides the limit, not the values.
- **Domain 3, deployment and evaluation.** Actors: system, overseers, instruments. Currency: reward, loss, outcome. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not move.
- **Domain 4, convention.** Actors: rule authors and signatories. Currency: shared meaning. Anchor: the open procedure, not the content. Decides coordination, never the world.

**Union.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Values and acceptable risk have different anchors; their intersection is a specific compound. My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary began from Fig 1 and Fig 2, I began from the anchor test and Fig 9's union/partition split. Different starting figures, same boundaries: low overlap between routes in this simulated arrangement, still one evidence pool.

**Order by dependency.** State the residual per slot, acquire a refusal term, set the objective against a non-updating anchor, plan, carry methods. The break is link 3, the objective (Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*).
