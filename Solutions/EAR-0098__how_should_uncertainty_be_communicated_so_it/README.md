## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." A list of sympathetic recipients is a larger room, not an anchor.
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A receptiveness rank produces a convention, not corroboration.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. The institutional and opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
