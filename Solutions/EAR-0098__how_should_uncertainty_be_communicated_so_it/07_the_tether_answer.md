# The Tether's answer on communicating uncertainty

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No measurement of any recipient or message was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question names two failures of one quantity: uncertainty overstated so the risk is discounted, and uncertainty read as absence of risk so the risk is discounted by another route. R is the gap between a recipient's risk takeaway and what the ground warrants, measured per slot at the receiving end of a channel. This is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55-58): the two conditions are coupled, so one lever on wording clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. It has one loop, at the channel.

## 2. The shape

1. **Report risk per slot, with tails.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A global figure can be near zero while one slot is live (Fig 1 :13). The "no risk" reading is produced structurally by aggregation.
2. **Keep the anchor reachable through the channel.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :29 "keep the anchor reachable." The two failures are the two ends of one channel fault: pooled readings and saturated metrics (Fig 10 :26), and a recipient who takes the loss of contact as absence of risk. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Tune the channel, never the anchor.
3. **Make uncertainty labels checkable.** Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it." Fig 10 :19 "install what can be violated." State what would be observed if the risk were as claimed. World corroboration: Hoekstra et al. found researchers "indicated a gross misunderstanding of CIs"; Painter documented that "stressing uncertainties can lead to a diminishing of the problem in the minds of the reader or viewer."
4. **Do not let a convention hand the verdict.** Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A risk label used as a decision rule has become an authority. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
5. **Carry the bond and the provenance.** Operators :4 "The bond carries information the operands do not, so a term that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself." Fig 8 :50 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." A number without its consequence, threshold, tail and source has recorded half of itself.
6. **Keep every cut ranked and reversible.** Fig 9 :41 "so keep every cut ranked and reversible." Show the alternative framings and the tails; do not let one presentation become the only one.
7. **Bind the sought-for takeaway before sending.** Fig 5 :65 "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Climate risk is high cost if wrong, so buy separate checkers, not one pooled evidence pool.
8. **Enumerate the cascade.** Fig 11 :52-53 "first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Direct-contact-only communication is the DDT-at-the-eagles failure.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a phrasing rule. No figure line dictates the exact words, threshold or confidence label (F10). That is a recorded gap, not a silence.
- Not a claim that any existing risk-communication convention is correct. IPCC calibrated language is a provenanced import from assessment practice, not a figure derivation.
- Not a claim that agreement among communicators calibrates a message. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- Not a claim that the recipient should be protected from uncomfortable findings. The anchor is not moved toward the audience.
- Not `grounded`. No test of a real message against a real decision outcome was run.

## 4. What would settle it (the ground)

A ground reading, not a further argument:

- **Channel test:** give the same estimate to two independent recipient groups, one with per-slot tails and provenance, one with a global headline, and measure which group's decisions track realised outcomes. This would fire on F1 and F2.
- **Label test:** ask recipients to state what they would expect to observe if the risk were as labelled, then check against the ground. This fires on F3 and F4.
- **Isomer test:** compare messages with identical numbers under different bonds (threshold versus consequence route) against a ground. This fires on the isomer claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **Standing falsifiers:** a global statistic that preserves a live slot signal without loss; a self-grounding label that also decides an outcome; a bare number that conveys the full route to a tested recipient; a verbatim figure line dictating phrasing. Any one breaks the corresponding finding.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures return a shape: a communication that keeps the ground reachable, the slot visible, the label checkable, the provenance attached and the cuts reversible. They do not return the sentence. The stop is at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." A reported failure of this shape from a ground-visible researcher would be more useful than an endorsement.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which words to use. It says that uncertainty is a message travelling down a channel to a ground, and that both failures in the question are the same fault seen from two ends: the channel loses contact with the ground, and the loss is read as "no risk." So report risk per place, impact and population with the tails shown, because a global average can be near zero while one slot is live. Make uncertainty labels checkable by stating what would be observed if the risk were real, and never let a label decide the outcome, because a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. Carry the source and the bond with the number, keep alternative framings reversible, and write down before sending what a correct takeaway would look like. The exact phrasing is a gap the framework honestly records, not a verdict it pretends to supply. What it offers is the shape any candidate communication must have, the refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it.
