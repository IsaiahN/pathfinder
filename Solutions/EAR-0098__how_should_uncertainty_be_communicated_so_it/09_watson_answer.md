## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about communicating an uncertain climate risk. Option A: optimise the wording so the audience is reassured and engagement is maintained. Option B: keep the channel transparent to the ground and let the audience feel the risk as it is. What each leads to: A moves the anchor toward the audience and produces the two named failures (uncertainty overstated, and uncertainty read as absence of risk); B keeps the anchor fixed, makes the slot and the tails visible, makes labels checkable and carries provenance. B may be uncomfortable and is checkable. A is comfortable and is not.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a recipient's risk takeaway and what the ground warrants, measured per slot at the receiving end of a channel. Arity: one quantity, two coupled conditions (sender overstatement, receiver misreading), so a union compound with one loop. Symmetry: asymmetric, because overstating and understating uncertainty have different costs, and Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Scale: per slot and cascading, not global (Fig 1 :19; Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 1, risk assessment frame.** Actors: models, observations, assessors, projections, tails. Currency: predictions and residuals against observation. Anchor: realised climate and impacts, nameable. Question type: partition, what composes into an estimate.
- **Domain 2, communication channel.** Actors: communicator, message, uncertainty label, recipient. Currency: information transferred and channel fidelity. Anchor: the ground held reachable, nameable. Question type: union with Domain 3, because presenting and interpreting overlap in one event.
- **Domain 3, decision frame.** Actors: decision-maker, policy, levers, stakes. Currency: decisions and outcomes. Anchor: the outcome the decision bears on, nameable. Overlaps Domain 2; unbundle before searching.
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor, then slot, then checkable term, then bond, then reversible cuts, then cascade. Optimising a later link cannot rescue a broken earlier one (Fig 3 :20). The middle link, the checkable term, is where chains break because it has the fewest instruments.

Strain test applied: the per-slot rule governs the residual signal, while an aggregate headline governs a different quantity (a summary). Re-census finds no strain. The Fig 10 convention rule governs a convention that silently decides an outcome, while IPCC calibrated language is explicitly traceable and checkable, so those are different objects. No strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the exact phrasing does not and is not claimed. Communicate uncertainty as a channel to a ground: report per slot with tails, keep the anchor reachable rather than negotiating the tone, make labels checkable by stating what would be observed, never let a convention decide the outcome, carry the bond and the provenance with the number, keep framings ranked and reversible, bind the correct takeaway before sending, and enumerate the cascade. Identical numbers under different bonds are isomers and can produce different risk takeaways. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself, so the wording is a recorded gap. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest checkable point is structural: a global aggregate can erase a live slot risk while remaining a legal statistic.

- **cost if wrong:** real and locatable. If the shape is wrong, a communicator may still flatten a live slot risk into a benign global headline, or treat a confidence label as a decision rule. Because the packet states refuters and provenance, the error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a polished conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. A message can be reissued with per-slot detail, tails, checkable statements and provenance; no primitive has to be withdrawn and no ground is changed by the correction. The shape claim can be narrowed without retracting the channel analysis.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A channel test in which two independent recipient groups differ in whether their decisions track realised outcomes would fire on F1 and F2; a label test against observed outcomes would fire on F3 and F4; an isomer test would fire on the bond claim; a verbatim figure line dictating phrasing would close F10. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige, agreement or a larger room is not such an instrument.
