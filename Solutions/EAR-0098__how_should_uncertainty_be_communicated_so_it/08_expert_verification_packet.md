# Expert verification packet - the Tether on communicating uncertainty

**What this is:** a claim in a form a climate risk communication researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim. Not `grounded`.** No test of a real message against a real decision outcome is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Uncertainty communication is a channel between an assessment and a decision, and the two failures named in the question are one fault seen from two ends: the channel loses contact with the ground, and the loss is read as absence of risk. The figure-derived shape is: report risk per slot with the tails shown; keep the ground reachable and do not negotiate the anchor toward the audience; make uncertainty labels checkable by stating what would be observed; do not let a convention decide the outcome; carry the bond and the provenance with the number; keep every framing ranked and reversible; and bind the sought-for takeaway before sending. The framework does not supply the exact words. That is a recorded gap, not a hidden one.

### C1 - framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 10

- **Statement:** a global uncertainty aggregate can erase a live slot risk, and the two named failures are two ends of one channel fault. The fix is to keep the anchor reachable, not to negotiate the tone.

- **Refuter:** exhibit a single global statistic that preserves a live slot signal with no loss, or a message whose ground calibration is unaffected by pooling or saturation, or a case where negotiating the wording still settles the risk correctly.

### C2 - communication-facing, checkable against communication research

- **Statement:** an uncertainty label is a declared meaning until it is checkable against the ground, and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. A bare number kills the bond to consequence, threshold and source.
- **Source, verbatim:**

  - Fig 10 :19 "install what can be violated"
  - Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
  - Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
  - Operators :4 "The bond carries information the operands do not, so a term that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."
  - Fig 9 :30 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- **What to check:** does the recipient's use of a confidence label track the evidence and agreement behind it? Do the IPCC calibrated terms function as checkable conventions, or do they operate as decision rules? Would showing tails and per-slot ranges change the takeaway in a way a bare label does not?
- **Refuter:** produce a label whose meaning is settled by the ground with no further instrument and that decides an outcome without becoming an authority; or show a bare number conveying the full route, goal and comparison to a tested recipient.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** same-ingredient uncertainty statements under different bonds are isomers, and the communication chain is ordered by dependency with the checkable term as the fragile middle link.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."
  - Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
  - Fig 3 :26 "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress" (named as the link with "neither" an obvious failure nor an obvious test)
- **Refuter:** show two same-atom statements under different bonds that produce identical calibrated takeaways across tested recipients, or a communication chain that skips the checkable-term step and still produces grounded, per-slot, provenance-carrying decisions.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a phrasing rule, a threshold value or a confidence term. F10 records the gap: Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not a claim that IPCC calibrated language is figure-derived. It is a provenanced import from assessment practice.
- Not a claim that more agreement among communicators helps. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement.

1. **Channel test:** two independent recipient groups, one given per-slot tails and provenance, one a global headline; measure which decisions track realised outcomes.
2. **Label test:** recipients state what they would expect to observe if the risk were as labelled; check against the ground.
3. **Isomer test:** identical numbers under threshold versus consequence bonds, tested against a ground.
4. **Standing falsifiers:** a lossless global statistic; a self-grounding outcome-deciding label; a bare number conveying the full route; a verbatim figure line dictating phrasing.

4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
