**Mode: `specified` on the three domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the practice-to-slot binding. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census.

## QUESTION

How do caregiving practices shape children's attention, emotional regulation, and expectations of other people? Restated as an effect: for a child-slot s, R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|, what changes in the trajectory of R_s when a caregiver acts on the child's habitat? Option A: caregiving installs a trait in the child in isolation, predicting a standing practice list and one pooled effect. Option B: caregiving changes contact, predicting three per-slot effects with different currencies and anchors, a union of overlapping conditions, and a `gap` on the practice-to-slot binding until a discriminating instrument exists. The shape sought is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 0 before the run, so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency (Fig 3).

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Three-place plus index: caregiving act, child state, ground reading, indexed per child and time. Arity three, with three named outcome slots. Symmetry: "shapes" reads as ordered in ordinary language, but the observed object is two-way contact, so the one-way arrow is a live strain, not a settled bond. Scale: within one child loop, across the caregiver-child membrane, and across the developmental room chain. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **D1 Child learning loop.** Actors: child frame, Γ, belief b, weighting w, action a, observation o'. Currency: residual reduction and settlement. Anchor: what actually happens when the child acts. Status: admitted.
- **D2 Caregiving habitat.** Actors: caregiver, conditions, relations, introduced actors. Currency: contact and introduction, never subtraction. Anchor: child outcomes that settle whether a change of contact paid. Status: admitted.
- **D3 Social expectation frame.** Actors: caregiver, child, other people as separate frames. Currency: belief b, weighting w, prediction Γ(b,a) about others. Anchor: others' actual behaviour, which must not update on the child's belief. Status: admitted.
- **D4 Membrane and temporal channel.** Actors: methods, head starts, recordings, instruments, percepts. Currency: contact across scale and time. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Status: admitted.
- **X1 attention span as an agent-only trait.** No nameable anchor once contact is held fixed. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 caregiver personality as sole cause.** Already inside D2 as a habitat actor. Absorbed.
- **X3 one best parenting style for all three outcomes.** A standing list over three different anchors. Refused as a union.

**Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The three outcomes share atoms, so their overlap is a compound with its own loop. **Dependency order:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3); refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28); settle each first-order slot before crossing the membrane (Fig 12 :104).

**Strain test applied.** Mapping "caregiving" to the seat's introduction power and "child" to an isolated improved agent strains Fig 11 :40. Re-census: capability is agent-and-habitat, so the child alone is not the improvement target. No strain after re-census; the rejected reading is kept in `01` section 7.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Caregiving shapes by changing contact in a habitat, not by improving a child in isolation. Attention, emotional regulation, and expectations of other people are three indexed effects with different currencies and anchors, a union of overlapping conditions rather than one pooled effect. Regulation is channel maintenance, with methods and head starts crossing the membrane rather than recordings. Expectations are a weighting between private history and inherited prior, correctable only against a ground that does not move with the belief. Independence between caregiver and child frames is the source of surplus. The practice-to-slot binding and all numerical magnitudes are a declared `gap` with a source-discriminating instrument owed. Mode `specified` on the domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the binding, not `grounded`, candidate until a ground pays. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A wrong union reading would send a practitioner one lever for a compound of three conditions, leaving the intersections standing; a wrong contact reading would sell an agent-only intervention where the habitat is what must change. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its six refuters, and by recording an outside verdict before showing the source.
- **Undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term that refuses and removes nothing; the reach falls because something was added (Fig 6). The shape, the refuters and the retraction log are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10). No primitive has to be withdrawn and no practice list has to be unsent.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator separating which practice produced which outcome would close the `gap`; a child-slot gain under measured invariant contact would break D1; one lever moving all three slots identically with no categorically different residue would break D5; a regulation gain surviving habitat substitution with no reproduced contact would break D3; expectations unchanged under independent contradiction would break D4; a verbatim figure line naming a practice, or a practice derived as a seventh primitive, would break D6. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
