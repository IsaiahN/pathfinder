## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Write the child's loop as `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` for a tracked slot s (Symbols table). Γ is the child's library, b the belief formed from private history and inherited prior, a the child's action, o' what actually happens. The question asks what changes in the trajectory of R_s when a caregiver acts on the child's habitat.

- **Arity.** Three-place plus index: (caregiving act, child state, outcome), indexed per child and per time. Three outcome slots are named in the question: attention, emotional regulation, expectations of other people. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* So the residual is three indexed signals, not one pooled "child development" score.
- **Symmetry.** "Shapes" reads as an ordered bond (→ or ⇒, Fig 12), not a symmetric one (+). But the census below refuses a one-way arrow: caregiver acts on child and child acts on caregiver, so the real object is a two-way contact relation, a network bond (Fig 13 :24, *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*) with both as actors (Fig 11 :45, *"actors: what acts on it, and what it acts on"*). This correction is kept visible in section 7.
- **Scale.** Within one child's loop (one scale), across the caregiver-child membrane (Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*), and across development where the room must change with the question (Fig 10 :72: *"When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom."*).
- **Currency.** Caregiving deals in contact and introduction, not in traits (Fig 11 :61). Child learning deals in residual reduction and settlement. The two currencies do not add (Fig 12 :125-128).

**Bound shape (formula "Direction").** An answer would look like: for each of the three slots, name the actors in contact, the currency that moves, the anchor that settles it, and the figure line that governs it; keep the three verdicts separate; refuse to name which practice occupies a slot until a ground discriminates. This is written down before the run so it can accrue a residual and be refused like any other term.

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The three outcome conditions share atoms (the child's Γ, the habitat, the caregiver), so their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*; Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). One lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*). A caregiving change aimed at an objective cannot rescue a missing perceptual or vocabulary link (Fig 3 :19). Refuse before mint (formula step 2), describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53, Fig 9 :27-28), then settle each first-order slot before composing across the caregiver-child membrane (Fig 12 :104).

**Strain test applied up front.** A first pass wanted to map "caregiving" onto the seat's introduction power and "child" onto the agent being improved. That strains Fig 11 :40 only if one claims the child improves in isolation. Re-census: the child is not the isolated agent, the child-plus-habitat is the capability unit (Fig 11 :39). No strain after re-census; the rejected reading is kept in section 7.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - "Shape" is contact-changing seat action on the habitat, not trait installation (decisive, `general`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*

Reading: a caregiving practice shapes a child only insofar as it changes what the child is in contact with, which actors, conditions, and relations are present, and what the ground returns to the child's attempts. A practice that changes only the label a caregiver applies to the child, with measured invariant contact, is not shaping in the figure's sense. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22) states the human case directly: *"An apprentice isn't receiving messages; they're slowly building the history that makes the messages make sense."* and *"The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."*

Mode `general` for the structure, `specified` when applied to a named practice. Triangulated: Fig 11 and Fig 6 have low overlap (habitat/contact versus closure wall), and the tacit-knowledge essay is a separate human-cognition statement. Refuter: a documented gain in a child slot with a measured invariant habitat (no new actor, condition, relation, or instrument, and no change in ground feedback) breaks this finding. Refuted reading kept in section 7.

### F2 - Three slots, different currencies and anchors: a union, not one pooled effect (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*

The three slots are not three measurements of one thing:

| slot | currency that moves | anchor that settles | governing link |
|---|---|---|---|
| attention | what can register as a live residual; boundary diff aims the probe | whether the child's prediction fails against what was actually there | perception and vocabulary, Fig 3 :5-8 |
| emotional regulation | contact maintained with a non-updating ground; channel reach | whether the ground still pays under a changed stressor | closure and channel, Fig 6 and Fig 10 :24 |
| expectations of others | belief b and weighting w; prediction about other frames | others' actual behaviour, which must not move when the child's belief moves | Fig 2 :33-35, Symbols w |

World check, opened 2026-11-22: Prime et al. 2023, a meta-analysis of randomized parenting interventions, found *"significant improvements in mental abilities (g = 0.46, N = 5746; k = 33) and language (g = 0.25, N = 6428; k = 30)"* but *"Effect sizes were smaller and nonsignificant for executive functioning (g = 0.07, N = 3628; k = 14)"*. That is a measured instance of two currencies failing to move together. It **corroborates** the union reading; it does not refute caregiving shaping, because executive-function task scores and regulation-as-maintained-contact are different quantities (this narrowing is recorded in section 7). One intervention, one lever, one pooled "parenting works" effect is refused. Refuter: one lever moving all three slots by the same amount under the same anchor with no categorically different residue.

### F3 - Attention is shaped by habitat enumeration and the direction of the probe, not by installing attention span (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 3 :5: *"the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before"*

Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*

Reading: what a child attends to is set by which actors and conditions are in the enumerated habitat and by where the boundary diff points (caregiver and child jointly aiming the probe). Joint attention is a two-frame contact event, not a recording handed to the child. There is no figure line that returns a standing attention-span number (Fig 9 :32). Refuter: improved attention with measured invariant contact, for example a compound that changes alertness while the habitat and ground feedback are held fixed. That would be an agent-only improvement and would be a different quantity, not a refutation of the contact claim; both are recorded.

### F4 - Emotional regulation is a maintained channel to a ground that does not update; co-regulation crosses as a method or head start, never a recording (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*

Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."*

Reading: a caregiver co-regulates by keeping the channel to a non-updating ground reachable. The thing that can cross to the child is a reapplicable method or a head start, not a replay of one calm occasion. If the caregiver's own response becomes the reference the child checks against, the anchor updates on the child and the pair drifts together without noticing. Fig 11 :66 (*"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*) warns that removing a caregiver does not subtract the habitat; a new habitat with unchosen actors takes its place. World corroboration, low overlap with the developmental pool: Liu et al. 1997 (*Science*) found maternal licking and grooming *"program"* HPA responses in rat offspring, and Weaver et al. 2005 (*Journal of Neuroscience*) found the effect *"potentially reversible in the adult brain"* after methionine infusion. Reversibility is exactly what a maintained-method reading predicts and a permanent-trait reading does not. Refuter: a self-regulation gain that persists across a substituted habitat with no reproduced contact, or a regulation instruction replayed downward that produces a novel regulated response on first contact with a new stressor.

### F5 - Expectations of others are belief with a learnable weighting; they are correctable only against a ground that does not move with them (`specified`, triangulated)

Symbols table, `w`: *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."*

Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*

Fig 2 :46: *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."*

Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*

Fig 8 :48: *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*

Reading: a caregiver supplies the inherited prior and the history of past weightings, but the child's expectation of another person can only be corrected by that other person's actual behaviour, which must not change merely because the child predicts it. A caregiver who always confirms the child's reading turns the anchor into a mirror (Fig 2 :35). World corroboration from human developmental research: Colonnesi et al. 2019 (*Journal of Abnormal Child Psychology*) define *"nonattuned mind-mindedness (i.e., misinterpretations of the child's mental states)"* and found that frequent nonattuned comments predicted *"children's low social competence"* and externalizing problems. That is the figure's coherent-drift failure at the social slot: the caregiver reads the child from the caregiver's own frame rather than from an independent ground. Xu et al. 2024 found hostile attribution bias mediates the attachment-aggression link and that the association is stronger in Eastern than Western culture, an instance of Fig 11 :51 (valence and attribution change with the habitat). Refuter: expectations unchanged after an independent ground repeatedly contradicts them, or expectations that track caregiver influence only and not ground feedback.

### F6 - Which practice occupies which slot is a `gap`, not a hidden answer (`gap`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

No figure line selects licking and grooming, sensitivity, mind-mindedness, scaffolding, or any other named practice for a slot. The framework returns a shape. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument: a design that can tell which practice produced which outcome rather than reading a correlated outcome and naming its source (the metamerism point of *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*). Record `gap`, not silence, and not an invitation to invent a practice list. Refuter: a figure re-census that derives a practice as a seventh primitive, or a verbatim figure line that names one.

### F7 - The surplus depends on independence; caregiver and child must sometimes meet the world apart (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*

Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

Fig 10 :80: *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained; frames whose correlation is fixed at origin can only be rotated"*

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Reading: long close contact builds the shared understanding that makes communication easy and slowly spends the extra reach. The child needs separate experience, time alone at the bench, not only time beside the caregiver (tacit knowledge essay). Applied to outreach: a researcher or caregiverselected for likely agreement is one frame wearing two names. The author's own stake in the answer must be declared (Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*). Refuter: a child gain with caregiver and child drawing on the exact same evidence and no separate experience, with independence measured at zero.

### F8 - Introduction is available, exclusion is not; isolation substitutes a habitat (`specified`, single-source on the asymmetry, triangulated with the isolation law)

Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."*

Fig 11 :68: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*

Fig 11 :69: *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

Reading: a caregiver can add an actor, condition, or relation, and cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world. Two silent failure modes follow: a cabinet that removes everything the child needed to reproduce leaves the goal invisible until it fails, and an introduced actor acts later without warning. A "clean" environment is a substituted habitat with unchosen actors, not an absence of habitat. Refuter: a documented subtraction of an actor from the child's habitat with no substituted habitat and no unchosen actors inherited.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. The shape claim is refused if any of the following fires:

1. A child slot improves with measured invariant habitat contact: no new actor, condition, relation, or instrument, and no change in what the ground returns. This breaks F1.
2. One lever moves all three outcomes by the same amount under the same anchor with no categorically different residue. This breaks F2's union reading.
3. A self-regulation gain persists across a substituted habitat with no reproduced contact, or a replayed regulation instruction yields a novel regulated response at first contact with a new stressor. This breaks F4.
4. Expectations remain unchanged after repeated independent contradiction, or move only with caregiver influence and not with ground feedback. This breaks F5.
5. A genuine re-census admits a named practice as a derived primitive, or a verbatim figure line names one. This breaks F6's `gap`.
6. Independence between caregiver and child measures zero and the child still gains reach. This breaks F7.

Any one firing withdraws the affected finding, not the whole framework. Nothing may be labelled `grounded` until a ground reading pays for it.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is the residual per child or pooled?** Per child, per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22). Pooling is how the signal disappears.
2. **What does "shape" change, the agent or contact?** Contact (Fig 11 :39-40). Agent-only improvement changes nothing.
3. **Are the three outcomes one question?** No, a union of three overlapping conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :55-57). Unbundle before searching.
4. **Which figure governs attention?** The perception/vocabulary link plus habitat enumeration (Fig 3 :27, Fig 11 :44), not a standing trait.
5. **Which governs regulation?** Channel maintenance across a lossy membrane (Fig 10 :24, Fig 4 :22-25). A recording is not a method.
6. **Which governs expectations?** w and a non-updating anchor (Symbols w, Fig 2 :33-46). Coherent drift is the named failure.
7. **Can a practice be named?** Not by the framework (Fig 9 :32). A provenanced binding is a candidate; a discriminating instrument is owed. Route: `gap`, not `refuted`, because the sought-for shape was bound and its refuter recorded before the run.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: caregiving shapes by changing contact in a habitat, per slot, across a union of three conditions, with a method rather than a recording crossing the membrane, and a non-updating ground for social expectations. The specific practice-to-slot binding and all numerical magnitudes do not: they are a declared `gap` with a source-discriminating instrument owed. Strains after re-census: one live type question, the ordered "shapes" arrow versus the two-way network bond, kept open and named in `06`; one live but narrowed tension, Prime's EF null versus regulation-as-channel, recorded as a different quantity rather than hidden. Nothing here is `grounded`.
