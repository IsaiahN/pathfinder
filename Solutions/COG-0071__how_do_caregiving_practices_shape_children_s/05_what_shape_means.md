## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name which caregiving practice fills which slot. It constrains and does not fill. The shape is: contact-changing seat action on a habitat, per child-slot, a union of three conditions, a method rather than a recording crossing the membrane, and a non-updating ground for expectations. "Sensitivity shapes attention" is a name. The shape is what any such name must satisfy to be admitted.

## 2. How a shape is obtained: describe the effect, then choose the frame

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Also: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the residual is written as an effect on R_s, not as "parenting style causes outcome".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened directly: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the outreach rule in `02`.

Applied here: we did not start with attachment theory, mind-mindedness, or scaffolding and then map the figure text onto them. We described the residual per slot (section 0 of `01`) and only then read the caregiving literature as secondary corroboration.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A source-discriminating instrument that separates which practice produced which outcome would name the slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

They're Scanning Books (carried, not re-opened): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* and *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question: naming sensitivity, mind-mindedness, or licking and grooming as *the* practice that fills a slot is a binding from the caregiving frame onto an actor already in the enumerated habitat. It is a candidate with provenance, not a figure-derived name and not an import that adds a seventh primitive to the six-term set.

## 4. The human case is the apprentice case

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened directly, is the closest essay to this question. Its claims are worth quoting at length because they are about the transmission of care and skill, not about machines:

- *"Much of what they pick up never comes as instructions."* The apprentice learns *"not only through language but also by observation, imitation, and practice"* and gets *"correction through practice"*.
- *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part. The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."*
- *"A correction isn't a fourth thing to add to the list... The meaning is in the unstated gap between what you did and what they show you."*
- *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."* The reference point *"has to stay put. If it moves, 'everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices'."* (the embedded quote is Fig 2 :34).
- *"The apprentice needs time alone at the bench as well as time beside the master."* This is the independence condition of Fig 10 :80 applied to caregiving.
- *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down"* (Fig 4 :22, quoted inside the essay) and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40, quoted inside the essay).

This is why the answer refuses both a pure trait account and a pure instruction account. Caregiving transmits a method through two-way contact with correction, and the child must rebuild the understanding through contact. The essay's own warning applies: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world."*

## 5. The source problem: why the practice name is owed, not available

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here the reading is a child outcome (an attention score, a regulation measure, a social-expectation report) and the sources are many practices that could produce it. Naming one source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating design, not a better narrative. This is why F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a `gap` and not a hidden answer.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (the usual overstatement)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any caregiving practice. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. Sensitivity, mind-mindedness, and licking and grooming are actors already in the enumerated habitat (Fig 11 :43-48). Naming one is a **binding** from the caregiving frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding is figure-derived, or claiming it adds a seventh term (Fig 13 :116: *"Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh"*). Both my first reading and the corrected one are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

In this field that means a famous parenting author, a large meta-analysis, or a prestigious journal does not settle the shape claim. Each can give it a budget to be tried. The ground is the child outcome measured independently, and the answer is corrected by it, not by agreement with a respected name.
