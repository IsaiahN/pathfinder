# Expert verification packet: the Tether on caregiving and child outcomes

**What this is:** a claim in a form a developmental psychologist or caregiving researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the practice-to-slot binding. Not `grounded`.** No child measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

For a child-slot s, R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|. Caregiving shapes a child by changing what the child is in contact with, not by improving the child in isolation. Attention, emotional regulation, and expectations of other people are three indexed effects with different currencies and anchors, a union of overlapping conditions rather than one pooled parenting effect. What crosses the caregiver-child membrane is a reapplicable method or head start, never a recording of one occasion. Expectations of other people are belief with a learnable weighting between private history and inherited prior, and are correctable only against a ground that does not update on the child's belief. No figure line names which practice fills which slot; that is a declared `gap` owed a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus behavioural; checkable against the caregiving literature

- **Statement:** caregiving changes a child through contact, not through agent-only improvement. Per-slot effects must stay separate; the three outcomes are a union.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :57 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **What to check:** is there a documented child-slot gain with habitat contact measured invariant (no new actor, condition, relation, or instrument, no change in ground feedback)? Or, does one parenting lever move all three outcomes by the same amount under the same anchor with no categorically different residue? Prime et al. 2023 is the relevant published check: mental abilities g = 0.46 and language g = 0.25, but executive functioning g = 0.07, nonsignificant.
- **Refuter:** either of the two findings above fires, and the corresponding claim is withdrawn.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10 and 4, then against regulation research

- **Statement:** emotional regulation is a maintained channel to a ground that does not update; co-regulation crosses as a method or head start, not as a recording of one occasion.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- **What to check:** does a self-regulation gain persist across a substituted habitat with no reproduced contact? Does a replayed regulation instruction yield a novel regulated response at first contact with a new stressor, or only a copy of the trained occasion? Liu et al. 1997 and Weaver et al. 2005 are the low-overlap animal checks, including the reversibility result.
- **Refuter:** a gain that survives substitution with no reproduced contact, or a novel novel-context response from a pure replay, breaks this.

### C3 - framework-internal plus social-development research; checkable by a developmental psychologist

- **Statement:** expectations of other people are belief with a learnable weighting between private history and inherited prior; they require a ground that does not move with the child's belief; no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Symbols table, `w` - *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings."* Fig 2 :33 - *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- **What to check:** do expectations move when an independent ground repeatedly contradicts them, or do they track caregiver influence only? Colonnesi et al. 2019 is the published instance: *"nonattuned mind-mindedness (i.e., misinterpretations of the child's mental states)"* predicted low social competence and externalizing behaviour. Xu et al. 2024 show the attribution link changing with culture.
- **Refuter:** expectations unchanged after repeated independent contradiction, or expectations moving only with caregiver influence and not ground feedback, breaks this.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the child is a blank slate. Γ, b and w are held; only agent-only improvement is refused.
- Not that any named practice is figure-derived. Names are provenanced bindings and candidates.
- Not a universal effect size, a standing practice list, or a single lever for the three slots.
- Not that "shapes" is a symmetric correlation. It is two-way contact with typed direction per slot; the live strain is named in `06` Q1.
- Not that removing an actor subtracts a habitat. Isolation substitutes a habitat and inherits unchosen actors (Fig 11 :66-69).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by child measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument and independent readings, not further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here:

1. A design separating which practice produced which child outcome, rather than naming a source from a correlated reading.
2. The invariant-contact test for C1.
3. The substituted-habitat test for C2.
4. The independent-contradiction test for C3.
5. A figure re-census deriving a practice as a primitive, or a verbatim figure line naming one, which would close the `gap`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 1 :19, Fig 10 :24, Fig 4 :22, Fig 2 :33 and Fig 11 :51, six short passages.
2. Check C1 against Prime et al. 2023: do the reported effect sizes support three currencies or one?
3. Check C3 against Colonnesi et al. 2019: is nonattuned mind-mindedness a reading of the child from the caregiver's own frame, or an independent one?
4. Name one design that would discriminate which practice produced which outcome, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
