## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is what the ordered relation "caregiving practice shapes child outcome" can mean under the figures, and what each candidate meaning commits to. For a child-slot s, write R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|: the child's library Γ predicting an outcome from belief b and action a, against what actually happens. Option A: caregiving installs a trait in the child in isolation (an agent-only improvement). Option B: caregiving acts as a seat on the child's habitat, changing contact, so what can be settled changes (Fig 11 :39-40). What each leads to: A predicts a standing list of practices that improve all three outcomes by one lever; B predicts three per-slot effects with different currencies and anchors, a union of overlapping conditions, and a `gap` on the practice-to-slot binding until a source-discriminating instrument exists. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `06_isomers_and_order.md` so it can accrue a residual and be refused, per the formula's "Direction" step.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 ("Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."); Fig 2 :40 ("agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"); Fig 10 :42 ("A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."). Selecting caregivers or researchers for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless, and sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends their independence.
