# The Tether's answer: how caregiving practices shape child outcomes

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the practice-to-slot binding. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No child was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a child-slot s, R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|: the child's library predicting an outcome from belief and action, against what actually happened. The question asks how caregiving changes the trajectory of R_s for attention, emotional regulation, and expectations of other people. The residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*), and the three outcomes form a union with different currencies and anchors (Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*). One pooled "parenting effect" is the wrong object.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate and never averaged

**D1, capability and contact (`general` structural, `specified` in application, triangulated).** Caregiving shapes by changing what the child is in contact with, not by improving the child in isolation. Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* A practice that only changes the caregiver's label, with measured invariant contact, does not shape anything in this sense.

**D2, attention (`specified`, triangulated).** Attention is governed by the perception and vocabulary link and by which actors and conditions are enumerated in the habitat. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Joint attention is a two-frame contact event (Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*), not a standing trait installed in the child.

**D3, emotional regulation (`specified`, triangulated).** Regulation is a maintained channel to a ground that does not move when the child's reading moves. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Co-regulation crosses the membrane as a method or a head start, never as a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*). If the caregiver's own response becomes the reference, the anchor updates on the child and the pair drifts together without noticing (Fig 2 :34).

**D4, expectations of other people (`specified`, triangulated).** These are belief b with a learnable weighting w between private history and inherited prior. They are correctable only against other people's actual behaviour, which must not move because of what the child predicts (Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*; Fig 2 :46: *"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."*). No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51), so a caregiver rule of the form 'always trust others' is a candidate binding, not a universal term.

**D5, union and independence (`specified`, triangulated).** The three outcomes share atoms, so a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds. Independence between caregiver and child frames is the source of surplus: Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* The child needs separate experience as well as shared contact.

**D6, the practice name (`gap`, triangulated).** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. No figure line selects sensitivity, mind-mindedness, scaffolding, or any named practice for a slot. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better story.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the child is a blank slate. Γ, b and w are held; the claim is only about what an *improvement in isolation* can do.
- Not that any named practice is figure-derived. Names are bindings with provenance and candidates, not derivations or imports that add a seventh primitive.
- Not a single "parenting works" effect size. The three slots have different currencies and anchors; no figure line averages them.
- Not that contact is always more and better. Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61), and isolation is substitution of a habitat, not its removal.
- Not a symmetric correlation dressed as a cause. The bond is two-way contact with typed direction per slot; the live strain is named in `06` Q1.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles any of it.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and independent readings, not further argument. Concretely:

1. A design that identifies which practice produced which outcome rather than reading one outcome and naming a source. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
2. A habitat-contact measure held invariant while a child slot improves, which would break the contact claim.
3. A substituted-habitat test for regulation: does the gain persist with no reproduced contact?
4. An independent contradiction test for expectations: do they move with the ground or only with caregiver influence?
5. Standing falsifiers: a new primitive derived from the other held terms; a named practice derived from a figure line; one lever moving all three slots identically with no categorically different residue.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** No figure line returns a practice list or a developmental effect size. The missing thing is an instrument, characterised in effect terms but not held. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical figures README). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable, and the verdict is `candidate` until a ground pays for it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning parenting technique, and it does not average attention, regulation and trust into one "parenting effect". It says that caregiving shapes a child by changing what the child is in contact with, not by improving the child in isolation; that attention, emotional regulation and expectations of other people are three per-slot effects with different currencies and anchors, so they form a union that one lever cannot clear; that what crosses from caregiver to child is a reapplicable method or head start, never the recording of one calm occasion; and that expectations of others are correctable only against a ground that does not move when the child's belief moves. The framework refuses to name which practice fills which slot, because it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the discriminating instrument that would close the gap. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
