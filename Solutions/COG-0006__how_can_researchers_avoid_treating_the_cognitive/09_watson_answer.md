By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: LLM output can "simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula", but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself") it cannot settle the claim it reports on. No measurement of a human cognitive norm was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the practice of treating the cognitive norms of industrialized societies as a universal baseline. Option A: keep the practice and improve it by adding more and more varied samples from the same kind of societies. Option B: refuse the promotion until a separator is satisfied. What each leads to: A reduces sampling error inside one closure and spends the independence of the people asked to check the claim; B returns a shape a method must have, a refuter written in advance, and an instrument owed for the part that remains open. The question is a union compound of at least four overlapping conditions, not a two-way choice (Fig 9 :54-57).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a norm as it registers in an industrialized population and as it would register in another habitat under a task whose meaning may change. Arity: four overlapping conditions (sampling frame, instrument, ground, promotion rule), so a **union compound**, not a partition. Symmetry: the promotion is ordered (local reading → all populations), so a symmetric correlation cannot choose the arrow; the baseline is a comparison bond ⋛ that selects a reference. Scale: individual cognition at one scale, the habitat at another, with a lossy round trip between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, measurement and instrument frame.** Actors: researchers, tasks, items, translations, samples. Currency: readings per population-and-task slot. Anchor: the ground in that habitat, what the task actually affords there, which does not update on the theory. Question type: where the dependency chain breaks (Fig 3).
- **Domain 2, habitat and culture.** Actors: practices, language, institutions, environments. Currency: contact, conditions, relations. Anchor: the world read off the habitat, not a list composed in the lab (Fig 11 :48). Question type: habitat enumeration, never composition.
- **Domain 3, verdict and generalization.** Actors: populations as frames, the universality claim. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: a non-updating ground reached by triangulation, never agreement. Question type: union of conditions, per-frame verdicts, never averaged.
- **Domain 4, refusal and method.** Actors: the claim, its pre-registered refuter, the verifier. Currency: assertions and refusals. Anchor: the ground plus the recorded lineage. Question type: operator availability, can the frame say "this is false"?
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Perceive the slot, have terms for what the task affords here, say what would count as a promotion, choose the route (joint development or import with provenance), verify and carry, then refuse when the ground turns. Accept effort only at the link that is actually broken; effort above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19).

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between Fig 2 :40 and Fig 2 :41 is not a strain after re-census. One governs a verdict about the world, the other a convention about shared meaning. Same independence parameter, different objects. No conflict rule was triggered by a live contradiction.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide the rule, not the roster. An industrialized-sample baseline fails the anchor test. Search inside one closure cannot import the missing population, and same-pool agreement is one witness, not corroboration. The instrument must be improved from one already returning something in each habitat, with those populations represented at every stage; capability belongs to person and habitat, so no standing list of norms survives a change of question. Promotion from a local reading to a universal claim is permitted only with a non-updating ground in each habitat, an enumerated habitat, a measured round trip and a fireable refuter. Over a fixed atom set, the industrialized-first and jointly developed promotions are isomers. Which norms are universal is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence. Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the conditions, not `grounded`, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** the separable cost is spending the independence of the people asked to check the claim. If a packet leads with the full theory and asks for agreement, it manufactures the disqualifying correlation and the eventual "confirmation" is worthless (Fig 2 :40, Fig 10 :42). Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the six refuters. If the separator itself is wrong, the refuters are the cheapest way to find out.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no roster is retracted, because none was asserted. The conditions in `07` §2 can be amended one at a time as refuters fire, and the record keeps each firing with provenance (Fig 10 :62-66). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one with its reason attached.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. R1, a figure line that returns the roster; R2, a single-population sample shown to be a non-updating anchor; R3, same-pool agreement shown to carry verdict weight; R4, a cognitive performance invariant across differing habitats under a discriminating instrument; R5, task meaning invariant without instrument work; R6, more of the same population removing the residual; R7, an equal reading determining its own source. Also a source-discriminating instrument that names the source of an equal score, which would begin to close the declared gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
