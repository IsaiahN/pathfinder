## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a norm as registered in an industrialized population and as it would register in another habitat. Arity: at least four overlapping conditions (sampling frame, instrument, ground, promotion rule), so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not a partition. Symmetry: the promotion from local reading to "universal" is ordered; the agreement between studies is symmetric and cannot choose the arrow. The baseline is a comparison (⋛), which selects a reference and therefore carries a choice. Scale: individual cognition versus habitat, with a lossy round trip (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: researchers, tasks, items, translations. Currency: readings per slot. Anchor: what the task affords in that habitat, which does not update on the theory. Question type: which link in the dependency chain is actually broken (Fig 3).
- **Domain 2, habitat.** Actors: practices, language, institutions, conditions. Currency: contact and relations. Anchor: the world read off the habitat (Fig 11 :48). Question type: enumeration, never composition.
- **Domain 3, verdict.** Actors: populations as frames. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: ground via triangulation. Question type: union of conditions; per-frame verdicts never averaged.
- **Domain 4, refusal.** Actors: the claim and its refuter. Currency: assertions and refusals. Anchor: ground plus lineage. Question type: operator availability (can a negation be written?). Independence in this simulated run is zero: all domains draw on the same evidence pool.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); fix perception and vocabulary before the objective (Fig 3 :4-10); improve the instrument from one already returning something (Fig 6 :32); enumerate the habitat before composing a promotion (Fig 11 :43-48); end with a refusal (Operators :58).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 2 (anchor test) and Fig 6 (closure), I derived from Fig 9 (split before search) and Fig 3 (dependency order). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework. It is still one evidence pool, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the level of derivation order.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-33 (anchor test), Fig 4 :25-26 (recording versus method), Fig 6 :32 (instrument), Fig 8 :32 and :48 (closure and independence), Fig 11 :39 and :51 (capability and valence), Fig 9 :32 (shape not name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2 and `08` §1: a baseline that moves with the sample is a frame; more of the same population adds no atom; the instrument must be improved; capability is person-and-habitat; agreement inside a pool is one witness; the promotion needs a ground, a round trip and a refuter; the roster is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete study protocol: recruit participants in P and H, develop items jointly, establish a standard of comparison in each habitat, enumerate the habitat outward, test invariance as a convention, carry the local reading to a universal claim, measure R_T, write the refuter before the run.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19) is easy to state and easy to lose when a study reports one aggregate effect;
- the **source-discriminating instrument** requirement (the metamerism analogue and Fig 8 :39) is collapsed whenever an equal score is read as an equal capacity;
- the **independence debit** (Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 8 :48) disappears when the same instrument and the same literature are used in both sites;
- the **gap itself** (Fig 13 :134) is erased the moment a roster is offered.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a local norm that moves with the sample fails the anchor test, and the remedy is to change the bond to the ground, not to enlarge the sample. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
