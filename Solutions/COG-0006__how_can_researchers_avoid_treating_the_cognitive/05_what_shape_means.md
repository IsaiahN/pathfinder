## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `industrialized norm = human universal` is a **claim**;
- `the promotion must survive an anchor test, independence, habitat enumeration and a measured round trip` is a **shape**;
- `attention, memory and reasoning are universal in exactly these ways` is a **name**, and the figures do not return it.

The shape is what a method must have. It is checkable without accepting the framework.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for "new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms." Each new term "narrow[s] the space by definition and deepen[s] your vocabulary." Applied here: start with the practitioner's own terms ("bias", "equivalence", "standard of comparison") and let the residual narrow them.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Say "the answer moves when we change the population", not "WEIRD epistemology".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened 2026-11-22): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the single most useful sentence for outreach on this question: a cross-cultural methodologist can check an effect description without adopting the Tether.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For COG-0006, that means a specific norm becomes universal only after a discriminating instrument in each habitat pays for it, or after an import arrives with its provenance and a demonstrated shadow. Henrich et al. 2010 do not do this; they refuse the promotion and propose reorganization. Poortinga et al. 2025 and Boehnke 2026 supply procedures, not a roster. No source read in this session names a universal.

## 4. Why "standard of comparison" is not a neutral origin

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened 2026-11-22) gives the governing mechanism:

> "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."

> "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot. But visibility is not neutrality, and revision is still bounded by what can be conceived to revise toward."

A cognitive baseline built in an industrialized population is a declared primitive with a human sensorium and a particular habitat behind it. Declaring it as the baseline does not neutralize it; it makes the choice visible. That is the gain, and it is also the limit. The same essay adds, from the animal case: "Change the sensors, and not just the data changes, the shape of the compression changes: the categories, the primitives, what counts as similar to what." Wikipedia's *Umwelt* entry (opened 2026-11-22) is the low-overlap corroborator: organisms "can inhabit distinct perceptual realities" while sharing one physical environment.

This is why Fig 2 :36 matters here: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." A WEIRD sample is legitimate as an anchor for "how do people in this sample respond", because the question is constitutively about that sample. It is not legitimate for "what is human", because the question is not constitutively about that sample.

## 5. Q2, answered: what does "avoid" mean here?

**Avoid means change the promotion rule, not multiply the sample.** The framework returns a shape for the promotion, and the six refuters in `01` §4 are the operational form of it. Specifically:

1. do not promote a local reading until the anchor test is passed (Fig 2 :29-30);
2. do not count same-pool agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40);
3. do not specify a culture-free instrument from a description (Fig 6 :32);
4. do not enumerate a habitat by composing it from one population (Fig 11 :43-48);
5. do not treat absence of variation as a universal (Fig 8 :39);
6. do not assume the round trip is lossless (Fig 4 :30).

What remains open is a name: which norms, which populations, which item sets. That is a `gap`, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

Does the record commit the error the README forbids? **Yes, if it presents a roster.** No roster is presented in this folder. Naming an already-held atom as the structure in a slot is a binding from another frame and is permitted as provenance; calling the binding universal is not permitted without ground settlement. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22):

> "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous cross-cultural psychologists to endorse the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the separator a budget to be tried, then lets the ground in each habitat settle it. The contact points in `03` are public and opt-in for that reason. The IACCP role pages and ORPC submission portal are ways to obtain a trial, not ways to manufacture agreement.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay:

> "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

The essay also says "Metatheories cannot validate themselves", and that "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the cross-cultural claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. A real test needs a ground: an instrument in a habitat, a per-slot residual, and a refuter that can fire.

## 8. Two corrections kept visible

1. **First-pass overstatement in `01` F4.** I initially wrote that a local norm can never be called universal. Narrowed: promotion is available after ground settlement or a provenanced import; only the unexamined promotion is refused. The first wording is not erased.
2. **"Shape, not a name" applied too broadly.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive. Naming a norm already held in the library is a binding, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains wrong is treating the binding as a discovered universal. Both statements are preserved in `01` §7.
