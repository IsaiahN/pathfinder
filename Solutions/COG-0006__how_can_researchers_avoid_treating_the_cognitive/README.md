## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The IACCP contact page publishes role addresses, but the contact points below use public pages and portals, not those addresses.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that industrialized norms are parochial.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No standing list of which cognitive norms are universal. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32), and recording `gap` here is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

Three figure-grounded reasons for the refusals:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness to the claim is a mirror.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Cross-cultural researchers who learned the same canon and read the same WEIRD literature are one pool talking.
3. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
