# The Tether's answer: avoiding an industrialized universal baseline

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on each condition, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a roster. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The error to avoid is a promotion: a norm measured in an industrialized population is promoted to "human universal" without a check. The residual is the gap between that reading and what the same task would register in another habitat, indexed per population and per task slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a union compound, not a two-way choice: the sampling frame, the instrument, the ground and the promotion rule overlap and must be unbundled before being searched (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 2. The shape

1. **A single-population baseline is a frame, not an anchor.** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." A baseline that moves with the sample fails the test. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." The metrology analogue: a working standard decays and loses traceability; the primary standard does not.

2. **More of the same population cannot add the missing population.** Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Larger WEIRD samples reduce error inside one closure; they do not import a different one.

3. **The instrument is improved, not declared.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The known instances are joint development, content validity, partly overlapping item sets and emically generated items (Poortinga et al. 2025; Boehnke 2026). A "culture-free" test specified in advance is the forbidden move.

4. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." An item is not fair or biased in itself; its valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Enumerate the habitat outward (Fig 11 :43-48), including second- and third-order contact (Fig 11 :52-54).

5. **Agreement inside one evidence pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Measurement invariance is the latter: a convention for shared meaning, not a verdict about universality. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

6. **No variance found is not a universal.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The same score can come from several sources; without a discriminating instrument, the reading does not determine the source. The remedy is SEARCHED, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, kept distinct (Formula step 7).

7. **The round trip is owed.** Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." A promotion from local reading to universal claim must be brought back down and the gap R_T reported.

8. **The promotion must be refusable.** Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 5 :65: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." The pre-registered refuter is part of the method, not an ornament (seat map :287).

9. **The industrialization prior is a visible choice, not a neutral origin.** *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Naming the prior is the first step to revising it; pretending it is absent is what makes it invisible.

Over a fixed atom set, the industrialized-first promotion and the jointly developed promotion are **isomers** (Fig 12 :117: "same atoms, different bond, different substance"). The variable to change is the bond between item and ground, not the ingredient list. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that no cognitive capacity is shared across societies. The figures decide the promotion rule, not the roster.
- Not that any named norm is or is not universal. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
- Not that more countries, bigger samples or better translations fix the error by themselves. Each changes an ingredient or a budget filter, not necessarily the bond (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
- Not that measurement invariance is worthless. It is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41); it is disqualified as a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a ToE. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6); a final universal baseline would be one.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles any norm.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Applied here:

- **Anchor Test:** for a proposed baseline, what changes its answer? If the answer depends on which population is sampled, it is a frame (Fig 2 :29-30).
- **Independence Test:** do the agreeing studies draw on different closures and different evidence, or the same literature and the same instrument (Fig 2 :31-32, Fig 8 :48)?
- **Instrument Test:** was the measure improved from one already returning something in each habitat, with the populations represented at every stage (Fig 6 :32; Poortinga et al. 2025)?
- **Habitat Test:** has the contact cascade been enumerated outward until it stops mattering (Fig 11 :43-48, :52-54)?
- **Round Trip Test:** what is R_T when the local reading is carried up to a universal claim and brought back down (Fig 4 :30)?
- **Refutation Test:** what would make the promotion false, and was that written before the study (Fig 5 :65, seat map :287)?

Standing falsifiers from `01` §4: R1 a figure line returns the roster; R2 a single-population sample is a non-updating anchor; R3 same-pool agreement carries verdict weight; R4 performance is invariant across differing habitats under a discriminating instrument; R5 task meaning is invariant without instrument work; R6 more of the same population removes the residual; R7 an equal reading determines its own source.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not silent.** The six-term substrate and the figure set are a human's careful guess at compression axes; another sensorium's set need not be the same (*They're Scanning Books*). The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Here the missing thing is not a primitive but a roster of settled norms, and no source opened in this session supplies it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which cognitive norms are universal, and it does not add another culture to a sample and call the problem solved. It says that a baseline drawn from one population fails the anchor test, because its answer changes when you change the population; that more of the same population cannot add the missing one, because search inside a closure adds no atom; that the instrument must be improved from one that already returns something in each habitat, with those populations represented at every stage; that capability belongs to the person and the habitat together, so there is no standing list of norms whose valence survives every change of question; and that agreement among researchers who share one literature is one witness, not many. Promotion from a local reading to a universal claim is permitted only with a non-updating ground in each habitat, an enumerated habitat, a measured round trip, and a refuter written before the run. What is returned is the shape any answer must have. The roster stays open until the ground settles it.
