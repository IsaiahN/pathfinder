## Fetch failures and honest limits

- `https://www2.psych.ubc.ca/~henrich/pdfs/WeirdPeople.pdf` returned "Unsupported fetched file content type: application/pdf". The attempt through a text proxy returned 401. The paper was therefore read only through its Europe PMC abstract, and the ledger says so.
- `https://journals.sagepub.com/home/jcp` returned 403. JCCP submission information was taken from the IACCP journal page instead; the ledger and the contact CSV record the limitation.
- `https://scresearch.org/` was fetched and found to be a South Carolina research-studies directory, **not** the Society for Cross-Cultural Research. It is deliberately excluded from the contact points. A wrong venue is worse than a missing one.
- `https://psyarxiv.com/` returned no content to the fetcher. It is not listed as a verified venue.
- Wikipedia is an encyclopedic secondary source. It is used for definitions, bibliographic anchors and the record of published criticisms, not as ground for the human claim. The substantive claim rests on Henrich et al. 2010, Poortinga et al. 2025 and Boehnke 2026, with overlap declared.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A mass send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its six refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record that verdict; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" - and Fig 2 :45 - "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and their later agreement never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user writes a refuter before running it and states a mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape, not a roster, and the literal roster is declared `gap` in `01` §F10. Do not fill the gap with a list before sending; the absence is part of what is being checked.
