## 0. The residual under audit

The question is the discrepancy between early- and late-universe measurements of the expansion rate. Stated in effect terms:

- One route extrapolates a model conditioned on a high-redshift measurement. The Planck collaboration reports, under base ΛCDM, an *inferred* late-Universe Hubble constant of 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc (Aghanim et al., 2020, arXiv:1807.06209).
- The other route calibrates a local distance ladder. The SH0ES team reports a *local* 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc and a 5-sigma difference from Planck + ΛCDM, with *"no indication this arises from measurement errors or analysis variations considered to date"* (Riess et al., 2022, arXiv:2112.04510).
- The gap does not shrink with more data, with roughly 70 analysis variants, or with a second standard candle in the same hosts. The review by Di Valentino et al. (2021, arXiv:2103.01183) calls it a *"4-6σ disagreement"* and reports that *"no specific proposal makes a strong case for being highly likely or far better than all others."*

**The residual R, in effect terms:** two different instruments return non-matching values for a quantity both call H0. What is owed is a decision about whether these are two readings of one slot, two different slots with one name, a broken channel, a false held term, or a genuinely missing atom. This is a `specified` description of the residual, not a measurement.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"):** a statement of (a) which slots the two values occupy, (b) which term, if any, is false, (c) what atom would have to be imported to close the gap, and (d) the instrument that would settle it. Until (d) exists, any candidate is `candidate`, not `accepted`.

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The residual is indexed by slot, never averaged (`general` structural, `specified` application)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :27: **"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

A single consensus H0 formed by pooling 67.4 and 73.04 is exactly the aggregation Fig 1 disqualifies. It throws away the only signal, which is that the two slots disagree. The Di Valentino review records a related non-solution: *"Reduced tension might not simply come from a change in H0 but also from an increase in its uncertainty due to degeneracy with additional physics"* (arXiv:2103.01183). Widening the error bar reduces the sigma without closing the gap. That is a change in the frame's own score, not a change in contact with the ground.

**Triangulation:** Fig 1 and Fig 9 :4-5 are low-overlap routes (a per-slot accounting rule and a split-the-union rule) to the same instruction, so `triangulated`. World sources: Planck and SH0ES are low-overlap instruments, and both report their own value rather than a pooled one; the review is high-overlap with both. **Refuter:** if a principled, ground-backed procedure produces one H0 whose slot is identical for both routes and neither residual persists, the no-averaging finding fails. Until then, do not report a consensus number.

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Low overlap: a decision rule, a closure theorem and a literature assessment. Verdict `triangulated`. **Refuter:** a demonstration that all proposed causes reduce to one condition whose removal closes every subset at once would collapse the union to a partition and refute this finding.

### F6 - Any imported atom needs a source gate, a shadow test and a ground settlement (`specified`)

The Di Valentino review's warning that reduced tension can come from *"an increase in its uncertainty due to degeneracy with additional physics"* is exactly the case where a gate is mistaken for a ground: the model gains degrees of freedom and the error bar grows, while no new contact is made.

**Triangulation:** Fig 6, Fig 8, Formula step 5 and the two world sources. A more precise H0 from the same ladder that does not change contact with the ground changes nothing, however much it improves. The figures distinguish a channel failure from a ground change. ### F8 - Sigma is a budget filter, not a witness (`specified`)

Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

Fig 1 :27: **"A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

Gross and Vitells (2010, arXiv:1005.1891), in a genuinely low-overlap field (high-energy statistics), warn that *"the significance of observing a local excess of events must take into account the probability of observing such an excess anywhere in the range."* Here the range is the space of proposed solutions. The review's *"no specific proposal makes a strong case for being highly likely or far better than all others"* is the same point from cosmology.

**Triangulation:** Fig 9, Fig 1, Seat Map :281, and a statistics source with zero shared evidence with cosmology. ## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Two genuinely low-overlap measurements of H0 in the same operationally defined slot converge within systematics as data accumulate. This falsifies the "does not shrink with effort" trigger and the two-slot reading.
2. A specific held term is experimentally refuted by a source-discriminating instrument, and the gap closes when it is removed. This confirms the REFUTED branch and refutes any mint-first route; it does not refute the shape, so it is recorded as a route correction, not a withdrawal.
3. The figure census derives a numerical H0 or names the missing primitive from the six-term set. This breaks the shape-not-a-name limit and requires withdrawal of F9.
4. A proposed atom closes the gap and is confirmed by a probe with no shared data, prior or analysis chain with the fit. This closes the `gap` on the name and upgrades that atom from `candidate` to `accepted`.

None of these was run in this simulated derivation. Until one is run, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Unbundle before proposing anything** (F2). Separate the calibration question, the extrapolation question and the new-atom question. A single lever applied to all three will leave the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-58).
2. **Name the two slots before comparing them** (F3). State operationally what each route measures and at what redshift. Do not average.
3. **Run the independence audit first** (F4). Identify which headline analyses draw on the same data, and which independent candles or probes are genuinely low-overlap.
4. **Decide refusal before minting** (F5). Ask which held term has a residual that persists and does not trend down. Only then consider a new atom.
5. **Price imports honestly** (F6). Require provenance, a shadow test and a ground external to the fit. Do not count a better χ² as settlement.
6. **Build or use a source-discriminating instrument** (F7). More precision on the same ladder is not contact.
7. **Report sigma as a filter** (F8), never as a verdict.
8. **Offer the generator, not a recording** (Fig 4 :22-25: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*). Send the shape, the route and the refuter. Offer the links only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is there one H0?** No. Fig 1 :19, per slot, and Fig 13 :76, different states make different terms. Two slots with one name.
2. **Is this one hard question?** No. Fig 9 :4-5, a non-shrinking disagreement with each rule working on a different subset is a union. Split.
3. **Which link is broken first?** Vocabulary and perception (Fig 3 :25-27), before planning new physics. A reading below the break is a reading of nothing.
4. **Refusal or mint?** Formula step 2. Check for a term held that is false and persists; owe a refusal before a mint. A vocabulary without negation can only accumulate (Operators :58).
5. **Can a new atom be imported?** Fig 6 :12-14, only import moves the wall, with provenance and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52, without the record, convergent derivation and adopted import are indistinguishable.
6. **Is a fit a settlement?** No. Formula step 5, a gate is not the ground. A probe external to the fit is required.
7. **Can the framework name the answer?** No. Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134-135. The honest stop is a shape, a route and a refuter.
