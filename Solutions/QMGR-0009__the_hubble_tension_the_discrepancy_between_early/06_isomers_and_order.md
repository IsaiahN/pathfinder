## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Is a measurement chain a molecule?

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*

Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."*

A distance ladder and a CMB inference are not lists of ingredients. They are bonded arrangements. The same physical ingredients, a geometric distance, an ordering of luminosities, a redshift, can be bonded in different orders and with different typed relations, yielding different substances under the same label H0. This is the measurement analogue of the figure's structure claim, and it is `specified`: the figure licenses the form, not the particular physics mapping.

### Q2 - Are the two routes first-order molecules?

Fig 13 :74: **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."**

A first-order molecule is `state → structure → state`, one crossing.

- The late ladder reads approximately `Space(geometric distance) → Spectrum(luminosity ordering) → Time(redshift)`. First order, legal under Fig 13 :75.
- The early inference reads approximately `Space(angular scale) → Spectrum(ordering in the power spectrum) → Time(extrapolated epoch)`. First order, legal under the same rule.

Both are `specified` characterisations, not measurements. The physics check is external: the Planck value is model-conditioned and the SH0ES value is ladder-conditioned, which the two abstracts state in their own wording. Verdict: triangulated for the first-order form (Fig 13 plus the two world frames' own descriptions); single-source for the exact atom assignments.

### Q3 - Are they isomers, or only a functional group?

Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

- As the two routes are actually written, they do not share every atom. The late route uses parallax and masers as anchors; the early route uses the acoustic scale and a model. By Fig 12's definition they are **not isomers** as written. They are two members of the same **functional group** (`state → structure → state`) with different qualifiers.
- The stronger form: if both chains are written over the full shared atom set `{Space, Time, Energy, Gradient, Spectrum, Network}` and differ only in which structure mediates which state pair and in which bond joins them, then they **are** isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the checkable structural claim, and it is the analogue worth carrying, because it explains how one label can name two quantities.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either chain can be written without an atom the other requires (for example a ladder with no geometric Space anchor, or an inference with no Spectral ordering), the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands instead. Neither outcome decides which H0 is right.

### Q4 - What order is the union?

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**

Each first-order chain is settled against its own ground (parallax and masers for the ladder, the acoustic scale for the inference). Each can therefore serve as an atom at the next level. Their comparison is a **second-order composition**. This is Fig 12's recursion, not an import, and it needs no new primitive.

Type caveat, held openly: the Operators table defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* At second order the operands are molecules now serving as atoms. Fig 12 :104 permits the demotion to atom; whether an atom counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument. Marked strained-for-now, not smoothed.

### Q5 - Analogues that bear on the checking arrangement

- **Catalyst** (Fig 12 :119): *"A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A calibration standard that is regenerated at each rung behaves differently from one that is consumed by the analysis. The SH0ES paper's triple use of Gaia parallaxes, the NGC 4258 maser and LMC eclipsing binaries is a catalyst pattern; a single unregenerated zero point is the spent case. Whether a given standard is spent or regenerating is a `specified` question about the analysis, not a figure verdict.
- **Cycle** (Fig 12 :121): *"The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Analysis variants that reuse the same published data form a closing loop; independent candles and probes form a chain. This is the structural reason the review's agreement with both headline values is one pool (Fig 2 :40) rather than two witnesses.
- **Two currencies** (Fig 12 :125-127): *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* An early-dark-energy field is cheap to describe in bits and expensive to settle in actions. The review's observation that tension can fall by inflating uncertainty is a change in the bit price, not in the action price. A system holding only one accounting *"will not notice the shortfall"* and *"the budget is what decides how much of the space is reached at all."*

### Q6 - Order by dependency applied

Fig 3 :4-13 gives the five links: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Fig 3 :25-27 says the middle link, having terms for what a situation affords and what would count as progress, has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests, so it attracts the least attention. Here:

1. Perception: does each instrument see what it claims (crowding, zero points, acoustic-scale systematics)? Instrument available, partial.
2. Vocabulary: is the early-inferred H0 the same slot as the local H0? This is the middle link, and the one this folder addresses. Instrument: an operational definition of the quantity at a stated redshift and anchor. Largely unlooked-at.
3. Objective: what would count as progress, in the terms of each frame? A converged independent measurement, not a smaller sigma.
4. Planning: which atom or refusal to try. Cannot be diagnosed if link 2 has not been checked (Fig 3 :22-23).

A claim that jumps to planning (new physics) before link 2 is a reading below the break, which Fig 3 calls *"a reading of nothing."*

## CONCLUSION

The two measurement routes are first-order molecules of the same functional group; they are isomers only under the stronger form where both are written over the full shared atom set, and that form has a stated falsifier. Their union is second-order, licensed by Fig 12 :104 rather than by a new primitive, with one open type question about ⋛'s operands at that level. The dependency order puts the vocabulary link before any planning, which is why this folder describes slots before discussing new atoms. The catalyst, cycle and two-currency analogues explain why independent probes matter and why a better fit is not a settlement.

This **rescues** what is defensible in a naive "one number, two measurements" framing without pretending it is settled: the label is shared, the substances are not, and the difference is carried by the bond and the anchor. No atom is imported and no value of H0 is asserted. Mode `specified`, ungrounded.
