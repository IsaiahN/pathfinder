## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the discrepancy between early- and late-universe measurements of H0. Option A: treat it as one number with two disagreeing measurements and search for a single correct value or a single new-physics fix. Option B: treat the residual as per slot, split the union, check for a false held term before minting, price any import against an external ground, and stop at a shape with stated refuters. - **Domain 2, late-measurement frame.** Actors: Cepheids, SNe Ia, Gaia parallaxes, the NGC 4258 maser, LMC eclipsing binaries. Currency: calibrated distances and redshifts. Anchor: geometric parallax and maser distances, nameable. Type: ladder measurement.
- **Domain 3, independent-method frame.** Actors: TRGB, RR Lyrae, JAGB, SBF, lensing time delays, BAO, standard sirens. Currency: a second H0 from a probe not used in D1 or D2. Anchor: each method's own geometric calibration, nameable. Type: low-overlap measurement. This is where the union surplus lives.
- **Domain 4, model-building frame.** Actors: scalar fields, early dark energy, neutrino sectors, modified gravity. Currency: fit improvement and a shifted sound horizon. Anchor: must be a probe external to the fit, otherwise no nameable anchor exists. Type: proposed, ungrounded.

- **Partition or union.** A union of calibration, extrapolation and possible-new-atom conditions, overlapping. Fig 9 :54 requires unbundling before further search.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The vocabulary link, whether the early-inferred and locally measured values occupy the same slot, has the fewest instruments. A reading below the break is a reading of nothing.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the name is a `gap`. The residual is indexed by slot, so the two values must not be averaged. The disagreement is a union that must be unbundled into calibration, extrapolation and possible-new-atom conditions. The two routes occupy differently anchored slots under one label, which is why one number with two readings is the wrong description. A refusal may be owed before a mint: check for a held term whose residual persists and does not trend down. Any imported atom needs provenance, a shadow test and a ground external to its own fit. The owed instrument discriminates among the sources of a reading rather than adding precision to the same ladder. Sigma is a budget filter, never a verdict witness. The framework returns these constraints, four refuters written in advance and the kind of instrument that would close the question. It does not return H0 and does not name the resolution. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states a mode, three checkable sub-claims, refuters and provenance before its conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defective framing, one number with two measurements, is removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. If refuter 1 or 3 fires, however, the shape itself falls and must be retracted, not merely edited.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An operational H0 at a stated redshift and anchor that makes both routes the same slot, and two low-overlap frames that then converge within systematics, would refute the two-slot finding. A demonstrated failed calibration term whose removal closes the gap would confirm the refusal branch and correct the route. A new atom closed by the fit and confirmed by a probe with no shared data, prior or analysis chain would close the `gap` on the name. A re-census that admits the comparison bond over settled molecules at second order would clear the live type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
