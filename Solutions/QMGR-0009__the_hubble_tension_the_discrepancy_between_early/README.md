## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283). A campaign that collects agreement among cosmologists who already know the tension raises the vocabulary and adds no anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
