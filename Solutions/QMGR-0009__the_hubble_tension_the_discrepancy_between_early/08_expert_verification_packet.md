# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the Hubble tension

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or instrument builder can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the route, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the name. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the relevant redshift is claimed in this packet. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the tension it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The Hubble tension should not be treated as one number with two disagreeing measurements. The residual is indexed by slot, so pooling 67.4 and 73.04 into a consensus H0 discards the only signal. The two routes occupy differently anchored slots that share one label: a model-conditioned inference from the acoustic scale and a parallax-anchored local ladder. The disagreement is a union of calibration, extrapolation and possible-new-atom conditions and must be unbundled before any fix is proposed. Before minting new physics, check whether a held term is false and its residual persists; if so a refusal is owed, not an extra atom. Any imported atom needs provenance, a shadow test and a ground outside its own fit. Sigma is a budget filter, never a verdict. The framework returns this shape and this route, not a value of H0 and not a name.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1

- **Statement:** the residual is per slot, never aggregated. A consensus number formed by averaging the two routes is the named failure, and sigma cannot substitute for a ground verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** is there any published procedure in which the two values are combined into one reported H0 without a stated change of anchor or slot? If so, does that procedure preserve the per-slot residual? The review by Di Valentino et al. (2021) notes that tension can fall by *"an increase in its uncertainty due to degeneracy with additional physics"*, which is a fit-score change, not a ground settlement.
- **Refuter:** a principled, ground-backed procedure that yields one H0 whose slot is operationally identical for both routes, with no persisting per-slot residual, refutes this sub-claim.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against the two measurement chains

- **Statement:** the two routes are differently anchored molecules of one functional group, not two readings of one slot. Fig 13 :76 *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** Planck calls its number *"the inferred late-Universe parameters"* (arXiv:1807.06209); SH0ES calls its number *"the local value"* with a ladder anchored to Gaia parallaxes, the NGC 4258 maser and LMC eclipsing binaries (arXiv:2112.04510). Independent families return 68.52 (DESI BAO, arXiv:2404.03002), 69.8 (Population II candles, arXiv:2305.19437), 71.5 (independent TRGB, arXiv:2108.00007) and 73.3 (SBF, arXiv:2101.02221). Does an operational definition of H0 at a stated redshift and anchor make any two of these the same slot, or do they remain distinct molecules with one label?
- **Refuter:** a demonstrated common operational slot for two low-overlap routes whose values then converge within systematics as data accumulate. That kills the "does not shrink with effort" premise and the two-slot reading. The weaker isomer claim has its own falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3: if either chain can be written without an atom the other requires, the functional-group reading stands instead.

### C3 - framework-internal and model-facing; checkable against the route rules

- **Statement:** a refusal may be owed before a mint, and any import needs a ground external to its own fit. Fig 5 :53 *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"A gate is not the ground."* Fig 6 :12-14 *"source gate: provenance"*, *"shadow test: explains a gap"*, *"the only operator that moves the wall."*

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

1. **Slot test:** an operational H0 at a stated redshift and anchor that makes the two routes the same slot, or shows they are not.
2. **Refutation test:** a calibration or extrapolation term whose experimental failure closes the gap when removed.
3. **Independence test:** two frames with no shared calibration chain returning the same value within systematics. Enumerate the habitat (Fig 11 :44): TRGB, RR Lyrae, JAGB, SBF, lensing time delays, masers, standard sirens, BAO.
4. **Import test:** a new atom closing the gap and confirmed by a probe outside its fit.
5. **Round trip:** compare the shorthand used here (two slots, unbundle, refuse before mint, import with gates) against the concrete claim by measuring R_T, as `11_audit.md` does. Do not assume the condensation is identical to the law set.
