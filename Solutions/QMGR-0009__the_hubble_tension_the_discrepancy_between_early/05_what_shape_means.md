## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "the two values occupy differently anchored slots and the union must be unbundled before a new atom is proposed" is a shape. "Early dark energy is the answer" is a name. "H0 equals 68.7" is a number, and a stronger name still.

The shape is not vague. It rules out specific moves: pooling the two values, proposing a new atom before checking for a false held term, and counting a better χ² as settlement. A shape that rules things out is doing work.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* gives the search method: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The description of R in `01` §0 is written in effect terms (two instruments, one label, a disagreement that does not shrink), not in the vocabulary of any proposed fix. This is the expensive part. Fig 9 :45: *"Describing the residual is the expensive part. Leaving, looking up and recognising are one step each, and cheap only in that order."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when the finish line is also the starting place, because the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the finish line is not a value of H0; it is a source-discriminating instrument that makes the two frames range against the same ground.
- *They're Scanning Books* warns what a shape cannot do: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* An early-dark-energy field is a new term inside the existing model space; it is not automatically a new observable axis. Whether it adds an axis is a question for an instrument, not for the fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* In this question, that means an independent probe, not a refined fit to the same data.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The imported atom must explain the residual and must be debited against the independence of the source frame.

For the Hubble tension, neither condition is met by any source read this run. The Planck and SH0ES values are ground readings of their own instruments but not of the same slot; the independent-method values are ground readings of different slots; the early-dark-energy proposal is a candidate import with a conceded outstanding test. So no name is licensed.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the warning about the six-term substrate: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Applying the six-term set to measurement frames is a visible choice, recorded in `01` §0b and F3 as `specified`, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism this question most needs. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And crucially: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Map that to this question. A value of H0 is a reading. Several paths can produce a similar reading: a Cepheid ladder, a TRGB ladder, SBF, BAO plus a BBN prior, a CMB inference under ΛCDM. The reading does not determine the source. The Hubble tension is a case where the readings do not even agree, which is a stronger signal that the instruments and their anchors need discrimination. Naming early dark energy from the metric of a fit is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection.

Fig 10 :24 supplies the same distinction in the framework's own words: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Fig 10 :25 lists *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings."* A calibration drift is a channel failure until shown otherwise; it is not a change in the ground. The right question is which channel, if any, has drifted, and what instrument would show it.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**Question answered:** what does the framework say a resolution of the Hubble tension must look like, before it can name one?

**Answer (`specified` on the shape, `gap` on the name):** any closing answer must be a term or refusal that:

1. distinguishes the slot of the early-inferred value from the slot of the local ladder value, rather than averaging them (Fig 1 :19);
2. splits the union into calibration, extrapolation and possible-new-atom before searching further (Fig 9 :4-5);
3. checks whether a held term is false and persists, and if so refuses it before minting (Fig 5 :53, Formula step 2);
4. if it imports a new atom, supplies provenance, a shadow test and a ground that was not used in the fit (Fig 6 :12-14, Fig 8 :50-52);
5. is settled by an instrument that discriminates among sources, not by more precision on the same ladder (Fig 10 :24-25, Fig 11 :40);
6. reports its mode and states a refuter written in advance (Seat Map :281, :287).

The framework does **not** say which term is false, which atom to import, or what the correct H0 is. On those points the honest status is `gap`. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* *"Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."*

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The famous names attached to the two headline measurements give each claim a budget to be tried. They do not settle the disagreement. The useful correspondent is someone with a public, checkable record on a low-overlap instrument, not a famous name selected for likely agreement (Seat Map :163).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency. They are not tests of the Hubble tension and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**Practical consequence.** Approach one person at a time through the public venues in `03`. Send the shape, the six constraints above and the refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Do not ask them to trust the framework, do not show them the architecture until their stage-1 verdict is recorded, and never pool their later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Fig 10 :42).
