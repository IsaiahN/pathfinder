# The Tether's answer: the Hubble tension

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the route, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the name of the resolution. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No value of H0 is asserted as settled.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two instruments return non-matching values for a quantity both call H0. Planck + ΛCDM infers 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc from the acoustic scale; SH0ES measures 73.04 ± 1.04 km/s/Mpc from a parallax-anchored ladder and reports 5 sigma. The gap does not shrink with more data or with about 70 analysis variants (Aghanim et al. 2020; Riess et al. 2022). Independent methods return 68.52, 69.8, 71.5 and 73.3 km/s/Mpc (DESI, Madore and Freedman, Anand et al., Blakeslee et al.). The disagreement is a **union compound**, not one hard question: Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that does not shrink with effort, where each rule works well on a different subset, is several well-formed questions. It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The residual is per slot, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A consensus H0 formed by pooling the two values discards the only signal. Report the two slots separately.
2. **The two values occupy differently anchored slots that share a label.** Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* The early value is a model-conditioned inference from z about 1100; the late value is a local ladder measurement. They are members of one functional group (Fig 12 :124), not the same reading of one slot. Whether they are full isomers over the shared atom set is the checkable claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, with its falsifier stated there.
3. **Unbundle before solving.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Separate the calibration question, the extrapolation question and the possible-new-atom question. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections (Fig 9 :55).
4. **Refusal before minting.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 2 routes REFUTED before NOVEL; Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* Check for a held term whose residual persists and does not trend down (Fig 5 :59-60). If one is found, what is owed is a `¬` refusal, not an extra atom. The Operators table notes *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"* (Operators :58).
5. **Imports are licensed but priced.** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall."* A genuinely new atom may be imported, with a source gate, a shadow test and a debit against independence (Fig 6 :12-13, :24). Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* Poulin et al.'s early dark energy is a candidate import whose own abstract concedes that future surveys will further probe it. That is `candidate`, not `accepted`.
6. **A fit is not the ground.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"A gate is not the ground."* The Di Valentino review notes that reduced tension can come from *"an increase in its uncertainty due to degeneracy with additional physics."* Widening an error bar is a change in the frame's score, not new contact.
7. **Sigma is a filter.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Gross and Vitells (2010) supply the low-overlap warning that a local excess must be corrected for the chance of such an excess anywhere in the searched range.
8. **No name is returned.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The framework states the constraints any closing answer must satisfy. It does not select the atom, refuse the term, or produce the number.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a value of H0. No candidate number is endorsed, averaged or preference-ranked.
- Not that either headline measurement is wrong. Both are ground readings of their own instruments; the claim is about slots and routes, not about competence.
- Not that new physics is excluded. Early dark energy and other imports remain live candidates; they owe provenance, a shadow test and a ground outside their own fit.
- Not that a 5-sigma discrepancy settles anything. Sigma is a budget filter.
- Not a derivation of one measurement from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken in this run.
- Not that the six-term set is nature's own list. *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"* (*They're Scanning Books*, 2026-09-06).

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot test:** does an operational definition of H0 at a stated redshift and anchor make the early-inferred and locally measured quantities the same slot? If yes, the two-slot finding falls.
- **Refutation test:** is there a specific held term, a calibration zero point or an extrapolation assumption, whose experimental failure closes the gap when removed? That is the REFUTED branch and it is a correction to the route, not to the shape.
- **Independence test:** do two frames whose calibration chains share no data, prior or analysis return the same value within systematics? The TRGB, SBF, BAO, lensing and standard-siren families are the habitat to enumerate (Fig 11 :44).
- **Import test:** does a new atom close the gap and then get confirmed by a probe not used in its fit? That would move it from `candidate` to `accepted` and close the `gap` on the name.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** convergence of two low-overlap measurements in one operational slot; a derived numerical H0 from the six-term set; a figure re-census that admits ⋛ over settled molecules at second order clears the one live type strain.
