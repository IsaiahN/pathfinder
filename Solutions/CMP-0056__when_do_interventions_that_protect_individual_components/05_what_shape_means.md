## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the set of structural conditions under which protection flips sign, plus the measurement procedure that would settle a particular case. It is not the identity of the component to protect and not a numeric threshold. "Protect the hub" is a name. "Protection changes contact with a third-order slot while suppressing the small residual that carried the signal" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is Fig 9 :54-57 in a different vocabulary, and it is the reason the question is unbundled before any lever is chosen.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the outreach first sends conditions and a refuter, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The figures README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

A numeric fragility threshold for a named network is a name. It is licensed only by ground measurement with provenance, or by an import from another frame whose closure differs. Until one of those, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

## 4. Why the aggregate reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Library of Babel* warns that a searcher without the right vocabulary mistakes noise for signal. Here the analogous error is mistaking an aggregate for the ground. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* sharpens the source point: "the gap is worth more than the fix" and "An understanding that's never checked feels exactly as certain as one that has been." A system-level indicator that is never ranged per slot feels exactly as certain as one that has been. What is owed is a better instrument, not a more confident aggregate.

## 5. Q: does the "shape, not a name" rule forbid naming catalyst, cycle or habitat substitution? CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST READING

**First reading (retracted).** I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any mechanism in the answer, so even "the channel was cut" would be impermissible. That overstates the rule.

**Correction.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Catalyst, cycle and habitat substitution are not missing primitives; they are Fig 12 :119, :121 and Fig 11 :66-70, already in the figure set. Naming them is a binding onto held terms, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh term or a new mechanism outside the figure set;
- claiming a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall", and Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure", here it adds none);
- naming the system-specific numeric threshold without an import.

Both forms are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known researchers who agree that protection is harmful is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an instrument, an experiment or a stress test, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape in one sentence

Protection increases system fragility when it changes contact in a way that moves a live residual outward past first-order enumeration, cuts the small-signal channel that would have driven correction, substitutes a habitat without reproducing the goal, hides the residual in an aggregate, leaves the union intersection standing, acts as a regenerating part that flips a loop, or outlives the horizon over which safety was claimed. The framework returns that shape and a per-slot test, and withholds the name of the threshold until the ground pays for it.
