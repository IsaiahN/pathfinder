## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the answer a reader wants: a number for a named network and a named component to protect.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **system-specific threshold** (the figures return a shape and withhold the name);
- the **cascade horizon** (the figures say enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering, but do not say how far in a given network);
- the **independence specification** (the figures say buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong, but do not say how many frames);
- the **order of refusal before mint** (Formula step 2), which an armchair list of extra protections reverses.

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: valence is relative to the residual; a contact-free protection changes nothing; and the residual is per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
