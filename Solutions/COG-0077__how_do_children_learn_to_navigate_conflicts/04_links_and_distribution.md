## What the wider literature contributes (and its limit)

The developmental sources are a genuinely different evidence pool from the framework, which is why they are worth reading. Four points bear directly on the shape:

1. **The frames are distinct, not one undifferentiated rule set.** The SEP entry, summarising Turiel, reports that by about age four most children react systematically differently to moral and conventional transgressions, and that their normative cognition is not undifferentiated. This corroborates F1 at the world level. It does not establish the figure claim that frames differ in closure; it is compatible evidence, not a derivation.
2. **Incongruence is the common case, not the exception.** Wang et al. (2019) found congruent norms in only 16 percent of a large national sample. This corroborates F2 and F3: the object of study is navigation of a union, not compliance with one code.
3. **Children resist as well as conform.** Do et al. (2020) report low overall conformity and selective shifts by context. This corroborates F4 and the refusal requirement in F6. It is one small fMRI sample (n=39), so it is a weak witness, not a ground that settles the framework.
4. **The weighting itself is a developmental story.** Telzer et al. (2018) describe cross-pressure tasks, shifting reference groups, and the relative weight of definitions. This corroborates F5. Note the overlap: Telzer is a co-author on both the 2018 and 2020 sources, so those two are partly one pool, not two independent witnesses. The ledger records this.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. Note, however, that the world sources answer the empirical "how" that the figures leave as `gap` (F9). They are the better authority for mechanism and timing, but in WATSON they are secondary and cannot overrule a figure verdict; here they do not need to, because there is no figure line on mechanism to conflict with.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a source, not a characterisation. Lead with the four-part claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked. Record the stage-1 verdict first.
- **Fig 9 :27 to :28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the links.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Do not select recipients for likely agreement, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :41** - *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it."* In the convention domain that the question is about, agreement is what is being studied. This is a reason to be careful about the design of the study, not a licence to recruit believers.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the four-part shape and the three refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the sources, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer, staged

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape, and pre-registered refuter. No theory. Verdict recorded in writing before anything else is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the figure links and the world literature, in that order, after the stage-1 verdict is recorded. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse. This sends a generator, which is permitted (Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*), with the instruction that each user write their own refuter and state their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is a simulated derivation, and the empirical mechanism, timing and cultural variation are an admitted `gap`. Say so before sending; do not let the recipient discover it after agreeing.
