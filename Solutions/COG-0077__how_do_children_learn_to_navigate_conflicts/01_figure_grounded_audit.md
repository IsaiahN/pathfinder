## 0. The residual stated in effect terms

A child, in one concrete situation, receives conflicting prescriptions: the family says one thing, the peer group another, the institution a third. Let each situation be a slot s. The residual R is the gap, per slot, between what any one frame predicts or commands and what the child actually does and what the outcome returns. Two figure rules fix how R may be treated:

- Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

So "the child's overall conformity level" is the wrong quantity. The live signal is: in *this* kind of conflict, which frame governed, and was the child able to refuse the others.

**Arity.** Three normative frames plus the child's own bearing. This is a **union compound**, not three separable questions: Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"* and Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* The overlaps (family and institution both adult-authored; peer and institution both school-present) have their own loops.

**Symmetry.** The frames are not symmetric. They differ in currency, in the authority of their anchor, and in the cost of refusing them. A symmetric average of them is a category error, not a simplification.

**Scale.** First order inside one frame, second order at the contact between frames, and a third, developmental scale across time where methods are carried up and head starts sent down (Fig 4 :22). The loop recurses: the resolution of one conflict becomes an atom for the next.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** A mechanism that (i) tells which frame is speaking, (ii) weights inherited prior against private history, (iii) selects a frame against the outcome for that slot, and (iv) can refuse a rule where it does not apply. Binding this before the run makes the sought-for shape refusable by the same test as everything else (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*).

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain. Question type and dependency order follow Fig 3.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Family frame | caregivers, child | care, attachment, approval | yes: the caregiver's actual regulatory response and the child's welfare outcome; Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* | a frame, not the ground; must be ranged against |
| D2 Peer frame | peers, child | reciprocity, belonging, status | yes: actual peer acceptance or rejection in the situation | the low-overlap frame; candidate surplus site |
| D3 Institutional frame | teachers, rules, authorities | compliance, formal sanction | yes: the rule, exam, or counter; the most instrument-like anchor of the three | a frame that often shares a source with D1 |
| D4 Child's navigation mechanism | belief b, weighting w, habitat | residual reduction, weighted by w | yes: the outcome that pays, which does not decay; Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* | capability is agent-and-habitat, not agent alone |

**Partition or union.** D1, D2, D3 are a **union**, not a partition: a single slot can be in two conditions at once. D4 is the loop that runs over the union. Treating the union as a list of three separate questions is the "several well-formed ones" error in reverse; the compound is the object (Fig 9 :58 *"It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*).

**Dependency order (Fig 3, not importance).** (1) perception: can the child tell which frame is speaking; (2) vocabulary: terms for what each frame affords; (3) objective: what would count as a workable resolution; (4) planning: which frame to select for this slot; (5) learn and carry: which method generalizes. Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

**The likely break.** Fig 3 :26 *"the link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* The prediction is that the child's affordance vocabulary, not observable conformity, is the least-measured link. This is a hypothesis about where to look, not a finding about children.

## 2. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding carries its verbatim figure line first, then the derivation, then its own refuter and verdict. Triangulated means two figures with low overlap govern the same quantity. Single-source means one figure governs it. Strains are named, not smoothed.

### F1 - The three normative systems are frames, not one rule set (triangulated)

Fig 2 :13 *"no anchor; each updates on the others"*; Fig 2 :14 *"they converge, and what they converge on"*; Fig 2 :15 *"is whoever had the most influence"*.
Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

If the child updates each frame on the others' opinions, the three converge on whoever had the most influence, which is coherent drift, not learning. If the child averages across slots, the per-slot signal disappears (Fig 1 :22). So the first thing learned cannot be a single rule; it must be a way of keeping frames distinct and slots separate.

**Refuter:** a controlled study showing children resolving conflicts by a single dominant frame across all domains, with no frame-distinguishing behaviour and no per-slot difference.
**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 2 collapse-1 and Fig 1 per-slot rule; low overlap). World corroboration: the Stanford Encyclopedia entry reports Turiel's finding that *"young children react very differently when asked about prototypical moral transgressions"* and that *"It is not the case that young children think of all normative rules in the same way; their normative cognition is not 'undifferentiated'."*

### F2 - Family and institutional norms are often one evidence pool (single-source, with a named strain)

Fig 2 :18 *"one evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :19 *"they never talk, and still agree"*; Fig 2 :20 *"so the agreement is the pool talking,"*; Fig 2 :21 *"not two frames confirming each other"*.

Family and institutional rules are both authored by adults and both transmitted through the same enculturation channel. Where they agree, that agreement is a common cause, not two independent witnesses. The child cannot treat "parents and teachers both say so" as doubled evidence. This also means the peer frame is the only one of the three whose closure is reliably different.

**Strain, named:** whether a given family and a given school actually draw on the same evidence in a given domain is empirical. The figure licenses the diagnosis as a structural possibility, not as a measurement of any dyad. Re-census does not remove this strain; it locates it at the world interface.
**Refuter:** evidence that in the relevant domain family and institutional norms are independent frames with no shared adult source, so their agreement is genuine convergent derivation (Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*).
**Verdict:** single-source (Fig 2). World corroboration: Telzer et al. (2018) describe internalization of norms through social learning and social identity and note that *"when more than one social identity is activated, norm-conflict may occur, especially if there are inconsistencies across group norms."*

### F3 - The peer frame is the low-overlap frame where the surplus lives (triangulated)

Fig 8 :45 *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :48 *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Where F2 identifies the family/institution pool as one frame wearing two names, the peer frame is the different room. The surplus in a conflict is therefore expected at the peer/family or peer/institution boundary, not at the family/institution boundary. Navigation is selection between rooms against the residual each surfaces, not arbitration inside one room.

**Refuter:** a domain in which peer norms are shown to be a relay of adult norms with no independent closure, so no surplus exists at that boundary.
**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 8 union surplus and Fig 7 room selection; different figures, low overlap). World corroboration: Wang, Chen and Lee (2019) found that only 16 percent of 11,086 adolescents had congruent norms across family, peer, and school settings, and that *"The majority experienced incongruent social norms"*; Telzer et al. (2018) note peers become salient for identity and status topics while parents remain salient for ethical and legal ones.

### F4 - Navigation is frame selection against the outcome, not averaging or negotiating (triangulated)

Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The learnable move is: for this slot, pick the frame whose prediction best ranges against what actually happens, and keep that pick reversible when the next slot differs. Progress per handoff has two factors (Fig 7 :37), the bracket's fidelity and the ground's pressure; zero either and the chain runs in place. This is a selector, not a compromise.

**Refuter:** evidence that children resolve such conflicts by simple averaging or by fixed deference to one frame in every domain, with no sensitivity to outcome.
**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 7 selection, Fig 2 range-not-negotiate, Fig 1 per-slot; three low-overlap figures). World corroboration: Do, McCormick and Telzer (2020) found adolescents *"sticking with their pre-existing attitudes 65 % of the time"* and *"conform selectively based on the social context."*

### F5 - The developmental variable is a learnable weighting between inherited prior and private history (triangulated)

Symbols table :24 to :26: *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."*
Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

The child does not swap one frame for another wholesale; the weighting moves, and it moves from outcomes. What adults transmit is a head start (a method, a scaffolding), not a recording of a resolution. Fig 4 :34 *"A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* So the same transmitted rule lands differently in different habitats, which is exactly why family norms and peer norms diverge without either being "wrong" in the abstract.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the weighting is fixed by architecture and not updated by outcomes, or that what crosses from adult to child is a verbatim resolution rather than a reusable method.
**Verdict:** triangulated (Symbols w and Fig 4 membrane; low overlap). World corroboration: Telzer et al. (2018) state that *"the relative weight of such definitions will determine whether an adolescent endorses the social norm"* and report that cross-pressure conformity shifts with age; Wang et al. (2019) find distinct profile types rather than one trajectory.

### F6 - Refusal is required, and it is negation, not accumulation (triangulated)

Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*
Operators table :57 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"* and :73 *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."*

To navigate a conflict the child must be able to say, in effect, "this rule does not apply here." That is a refusal of a claim, which is ¬, not a subtraction of an operand and not merely holding three rules at once. A system that can only accumulate norms cannot carry the correction; it can only add another rule and drift.

**Refuter:** evidence that successful navigation is fully explained by rule accumulation and context tagging, with no behaviour equivalent to refusing a claim's standing to apply.
**Verdict:** triangulated (Fig 12 :111 and Operators :57/:73; the figure defines the operator set and the table defines the unary operator, different objects). World corroboration: the SEP entry reports that children judge moral transgressions to be authority independent and generalizable, and quotes Turiel that *"Moral prescriptions ... are universally applicable in that they apply to everyone in similar circumstances"*, which is a refusal of the institutional frame's authority to alter the rule; Do et al. (2020) measure "resistance" directly.

### F7 - Because the question is about people, agreement is the product in the convention domain (single-source, re-census resolved)

Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 2 :37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."* Fig 2 :41 *"agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"* and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

This is the finding that must not be imported from the physics exemplar. There, agreement among selected readers was disqualifying for a verdict. Here, part of what the child is learning *is* a shared social meaning, so agreement in the convention domain is the thing being produced, not a corruption of it. But the two clauses of Fig 2 :39 to :41 must be kept apart: *"Correlation has valence."* Shared meaning is the product; shared evidence used to certify a claim about the world is still disqualifying. The child must range the convention against an outcome the frames cannot move.

**Re-census (apparent strain resolved):** Fig 2 :40 and :41 look contradictory. They are not: :40 governs a verdict about the world, :41 governs a convention about shared meaning. Different quantities. Per the canonical rule, a reading that makes two figures contradict one another has misidentified its domain.
**Refuter:** a case where a purely conventional meaning has no outcome that can pay, so no ground exists for the frame to range against; then the convention collapses into a constant a seat authored (Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*).
**Verdict:** single-source (Fig 2), but decisive for how this solution must be read. World corroboration: the SEP entry describes convention as serving *"to facilitate social coordination"*, which is the product reading.

### F8 - The least instrumented link is the affordance vocabulary, not conformity (single-source)

Fig 3 :26 *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."* Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :29 *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*

The literature has sharp instruments for conformity choice (Do et al.'s Attitude Conformity task, Wang et al.'s norm profiles, Turiel's social-rules interview). It has less for the child's own terms for what each frame affords. The framework predicts the break is there, and says an unmeasured link is not a working link.

**Refuter:** a study that measures the affordance vocabulary directly and shows the chain does not stop there, or a demonstration that the break is downstream at planning.
**Verdict:** single-source (Fig 3). This is a prediction about where to look, not a finding.

### F9 - What the figures cannot supply: the mechanism, its timing, its cultural variation (gap)

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

After an honest census, no figure line governs the empirical developmental mechanism, the age at which each capacity appears, or its variation across cultures and family structures. Those are answered by world sources, which are secondary here, or by future measurement. The framework gives a **kind of thing**: a selector that keeps frames distinct, weights inherited prior against private history, ranges each against an outcome, and can refuse. It does not give the developmental answer, and a search that finds no figure line is not a proof that none exists.

**Refuter:** a figure line, found on re-census, that directly governs the developmental timing or mechanism; until one is produced, this stays `gap`.
**Verdict:** gap, stated plainly. *"Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."*

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Ground refuter 1:** a controlled study shows children resolving family, peer, and institutional conflicts by simple averaging or by one dominant frame across all domains, with no per-slot sensitivity (breaks F1 and F4).
2. **Ground refuter 2:** evidence that the family and institutional frames are fully independent in the relevant domain, with no shared adult source (breaks F2's common-cause diagnosis for those cases).
3. **Ground refuter 3:** evidence that successful navigation is fully explained by rule accumulation with no refusal of a claim's standing to apply (breaks F6).
4. **Structural refuter:** the figure set is shown to permit averaging across slots as a valid signal, or to permit the ground to be negotiated rather than ranged against (breaks Fig 1 :22 or Fig 2 :45).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and its refuter, not with the framework (Fig 10 :52). The first contact carries the residual and the falsifier.
2. Keep per-domain verdicts separate. Do not average D1 to D4 into a single "social development score" (Fig 1 :22).
3. Treat D1 and D3 as one evidence pool until shown otherwise (F2); look for the surplus at the peer boundary (F3).
4. Measure the affordance vocabulary before declaring the chain healthy (F8).
5. State the mode on every claim, and state that agreement in the convention domain is the product without letting that license selecting checkers for agreement (F7).
6. Send the public submission and contact routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv`, one-to-one, and ask for a refutation rather than belief.
