## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. Here the shape is: *a selector that keeps frames distinct, weights inherited prior against private history, ranges a frame against an outcome, and can refuse*. That is a shape. *"The child learns to count to five and then reverts to deference"* would be a name, and the framework cannot produce it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the question's own words (family norms, peer norms, institutional rules, conflict, learn) were kept in effect terms and mapped to frames, currency, anchor, weighting, selection and refusal. The figure lines were then grepped, not the other way round.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that would be a study in which the four-part selector is measured and either survives or fails the three pre-registered refuters.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. The developmental literature is such a frame, but its content is imported as secondary corroboration, never as a figure-derived primitive.

*Library of Babel* adds a caution: *"If the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed."* For this question the honest third possibility, supplied by the figures, is that the search is at the right shape but the answer is out of reach of the figure set (F9 in `01`). That is a verdict, not a silence.

## 4. Why this solution does not name the mechanism

The metamerism point from *The Hard Problem is About Source* is used in the exemplar and is canonical for this project: an instrument that reads a projection cannot comment on the source. Here, reading a child's conformity choice (the artifact) and naming the internal mechanism (the source) would be the same error. Several mechanisms could produce the same observed selection pattern. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

That essay was not fetched in full this session, so this paragraph records the project's canonical reading as inherited from the exemplar rather than presenting a new verbatim quote. The conclusion it supports is the same one Fig 9 :32 reaches on its own: the framework returns a kind of thing.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question: a list of well known developmental psychologists who agree with the shape is a prestige loop, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against an outcome, with a pre-registered refuter. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong... You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the developmental question they report on.

## 7. A correction carried forward from the exemplar

The exemplar's `05` §5b narrowed the phrase "shape, not a name" to govern a *missing* primitive, not the selection of an atom already held. That narrowing is a correction and is kept visible. It does not change this question's conclusion: the mechanism the question asks for is not among the figure set's atoms, so naming it remains outside what the framework can do. The narrowing matters only in that naming a *frame* or an *anchor* that is already nameable in the census (D1 to D4) is permitted; naming the developmental *mechanism* is not.
