# Union, order, and refusal: a supporting derivation

**Mode: `specified`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The census in `01` leaves three structural questions open before any verdict is stated: (1) is the family/peer/institution conflict a partition or a union; (2) what is the order among the three frames and among the learning steps; (3) is the needed move a recombination of held atoms or a new operator. This file answers those three, then states a falsifier written before the derivation.

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :54 *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"*
- Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
- Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

A child in one slot can be under a family rule and a peer rule at once; the school slot can put the institution and the peer group in the same room. So the three-norm conflict is a **union with overlapping conditions**, not three independent questions. The overlap is a compound with its own loop, and it must be unbundled before searching for a mechanism. Consequence: a study that measures only "parent versus peer" has already dropped the third condition, and its result is a reading of a different compound.

### Q2 - What is the order?

Two different orders must not be confused.

1. **Order among frames: none by rank, selection by residual.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 7 :36 *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* No frame is senior; the next one is picked against the residual the last one surfaced. The order is a route through a space, not a hierarchy.
2. **Order among learning steps: dependency, not importance.** Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Perception of which frame is speaking precedes vocabulary for what it affords; vocabulary precedes the objective; the objective precedes planning; planning precedes carrying the method forward. Effort spent above a break arrives at a step that cannot use it.

**Falsifier for Q1 and Q2:** if the three systems can be shown to be separable (each governing disjoint slots with no overlap) and the learning steps shown to be independent rather than dependency-ordered, both claims fail and the union/dependency reading is withdrawn.

### Q3 - Are the frames isomers?

- Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*

Family and institutional rules often share the same atoms (adults, prohibitions, sanctions, an expectation of compliance) but differ in the bond: care and attachment on one side, formal rule and sanction on the other. Under Fig 12 they are **isomers**: same atoms, different bond, different substance. Peer rules add an atom that the other two may lack (equal-status reciprocity), so the three are not one family of isomers over a single atom set; they are a functional group with different qualifiers, exactly as Fig 12 :123 describes.

This is why "they are all just rules" is not a harmless simplification. The formula that lists the ingredients is not the thing (Fig 12 :5). A child who treats a family prohibition and a school prohibition as the same substance has lost the bond, and the loss is invisible in the ingredient list.

**Falsifier for Q3:** if the three systems can be written over the same atom set with the same bond type, differing only in which authority issued them, the isomer claim fails and the weaker "one rule set, three issuers" reading stands.

### Q4 - Is the needed move a new atom or a new operator?

- Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- Operators table :57 *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*
- Operators table :73 *"A vocabulary with − and without ¬ can say what is missing from an arrangement and cannot say that an arrangement is wrong, so it can only ever accumulate."*

The child does not need a seventh normative system. The child needs to be able to refuse one: "this rule does not hold here." That is ¬, a unary operator, not a bond and not a new atom. The distinction matters because a vocabulary with only the accumulation bond `+` can hold any number of rules and still cannot carry the correction. It can add a rule saying "except at school," which is accumulation, but it cannot refuse the original claim's standing to apply. Operators :73 names the difference exactly.

**Falsifier for Q4:** if a vocabulary with only `+` (plus context tags) can reproduce the full refusal behaviour without any operator that negates a claim, the claim that ¬ is required is refuted.

### Q5 - What crosses between child and adults?

- Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- Fig 4 :23 *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*
- Fig 4 :24 *"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."*

Adults send down a head start: a method for deciding, a scaffold, a story, not a recording of the right answer for every situation. What the child carries up to later conflicts is a re-applicable method. This is why the same instruction lands differently in different habitats, and why the framework cannot derive the specific resolution from the general rule. It is a lossy transform, and Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip to be measured rather than assumed.

**Falsifier for Q5:** if what is transmitted is demonstrably a verbatim resolution replayed unchanged across situations, the membrane claim fails and the model becomes one of recording rather than method.

## CONCLUSION

The three-norm conflict is a **union**, not a partition; there is **no rank among frames**, only residual-driven selection, while the **learning steps are dependency-ordered**; family and institutional rules are plausibly **isomers** with peer rules as a functional-group variant; the decisive added capability is the **unary refusal operator** ¬, not a seventh atom; and what crosses between generations is a **method**, not a recording. None of this requires a new primitive, so it stays inside the figure set. The empirical mechanism, its timing and its cultural variation remain the `gap` recorded in `01` F9.
