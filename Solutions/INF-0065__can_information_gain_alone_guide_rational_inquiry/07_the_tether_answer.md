# The Tether's answer: can information gain alone guide rational inquiry?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` where it is applied to this question. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An inquirer has to decide the next move: probe, term, frame, or experiment. Option A says maximize information gain and let the scalar decide. Option B says gain is a filter, and the move requires relevance (which slot, which effect), cost (bits and actions), and consequences (what changes at the ground, and the cost if wrong). These are not points on one scale. They are a union compound (Fig 9 :55-57), and a single lever clears only one condition.

## 2. The shape

1. **A frame cannot score itself with the quantity it produces.** Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Information gain is produced by the scoring frame, so it cannot be the metric that settles the move. Fig 5 :48 "Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."

2. **Relevance is the slot index, not an added preference.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look." A scalar that sums gain over slots has the wrong symmetry: it is invariant where the residual is not.

3. **The acceptance rule is already a bargain, not a gain threshold.** Fig 5 :42 "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold." The leftover residual and the cost of stating the candidate are both in the inequality (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3). Gain is one term of the comparison, not the comparison.

4. **Two currencies, held apart.** Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :130 "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it. A price in bits and a price in actions are two readings, held apart." This is the sharpest reason the word "alone" fails.

5. **Consequences and independence are priced at the ground.** Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

6. **Relevance is contact with the residual.** Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." An actor or datum is relevant relative to the residual, not in itself.

7. **What survives of Option A.** Gain remains useful as a filter for where to look (Fig 9 :60) and as the curiosity drive when nothing is scoring: Fig 5 :47 "So a system that starts with nothing can still be shaped, by seeking the gap that is large and compressible, which is the same quantity the acceptance test scores." It is necessary and not sufficient.

**Verdict:** Option A is refused as a guide. Option B is the figure-grounded shape: gain must be combined with relevance, cost, and consequences. Verdicts are `triangulated` across Fig 1, Fig 5, Fig 12, Fig 2, Fig 9, and Fig 7, with low overlap between the figures' actors, and are never averaged.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a measurement. No experiment compares a gain-only policy with a four-leg policy here.
- Not an objective function. No weighting among the four legs is derived; naming one would be a binding from an applied field, not a derivation.
- Not a claim that information gain is useless. It is a filter and a gradient generator.
- Not a resolution of the live literature on Bayesian experimental design, rational inattention, or value of information. Those fields are corroboration, and some of them solve their own decision problems better than this shape can.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by an instrument.
- Not a claim that the four legs are a neutral or exhaustive list of everything rational inquiry needs. "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral" (*They're Scanning Books*). They are the constraints this derivation uses, visible so they can be revised.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A real comparison, not a further argument:

- An instrument that ranks two inquiry policies on the same residual, with gain held equal and relevance, cost, or consequence varied independently, would say whether each leg carries weight. That is the missing discriminator; the gain number alone is a projection that cannot comment on its source (*The Hard Problem is About Source*).
- A derivation showing bits and actions to be one currency with no loss would break leg 4.
- A case where an agent-only increase in gain moves the ground while contact stays fixed would break Fig 11 :40 and the whole capability reading.
- A re-census in which the four legs partition the move space, so gain alone clears everything, would break the union claim.
- The standing figure refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §2.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The answer stops where the figures stop: at a shape that constrains any inquiry guide, not at a named objective. "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity" (*How Intelligence Actually Works*). Another field's instrument may discriminate policies that this shape treats as indiscernible, and when it does, the lineage here records what was assumed so the correction is locatable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that information gain is useless. It says gain is a filter for where signal may be and a gradient generator when nothing is scoring, but it cannot be the guide on its own. A frame cannot score itself with the quantity it also produces; the residual is indexed by object slot, so a global gain average throws away the only signal that says where to look; the acceptance rule is a two-sided bargain that already charges cost and leftover; and bits price the description while actions price finding out whether it holds, with the two explicitly not adding. Consequences and independence are bought at the ground, not read off the scalar. So gain must be combined with relevance, cost, and consequences. This is a `general` shape, not a measurement, and the framework does not name the weighting among the four legs. What it offers is the constraint any candidate guide must satisfy, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close it.
