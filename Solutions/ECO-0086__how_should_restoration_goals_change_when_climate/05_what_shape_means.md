## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name

The canonical limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

*Arrival of the Fittest* records the rule in the form used by this project: an LLM given the figures produces a "simulated" output and the outputs "must be seen as simulacra"; it also says results "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

From the mapped *Library of Babel* entry, quoted in the exemplar: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question:

- A **shape** is: a goal whose target operand is rebound to the ground each loop, stated per slot as a falsifiable comparison over a trajectory, maintained by a seat that can add and cannot subtract.
- A **name** would be: "restore the mixed conifer assemblage of reference condition X," or "plant the novel community of species A, B and C." Neither is figure-derived. Both are bindings from the ecology frame that need local measurement and a source gate.

The shape constrains the form of any acceptable answer. It does not fill the species list.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For restoration, that means a long-term result under the actual climate, per slot, with a pre-registered success criterion. McNellie et al. 2020 supply the equivalent demand in the ecology frame: contemporary benchmarks are "measurable and falsifiable," whereas historical ones are "often unmeasurable" and "almost always unfalsifiable."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."

A species list is not made authoritative by being called native. It is authoritative when the ground pays for it under the conditions in which it is being asked to persist.

## 4. The source-discrimination point

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the measurement caution that matters most here: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information," and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied to restoration: a site that scores high on a single "fidelity to reference" index can be a persisting assemblage, a planted list kept alive by irrigation and replanting, or a transient state about to flip. The index reads green and cannot comment on the source. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among those sources (per-slot survival, recruitment, interaction persistence, function under drought), not another argument about which baseline to prefer. This is the world-side form of Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 5. Q: Does the framework name the replacement goal?

**No.** It returns the shape in §1 and the dependency order in audit §1. Which actor to introduce, which condition to treat as binding, and which threshold counts as success are bindings from the ecology and management frames, provenanced as such. The RAD framework (USGS CASC, 2026) supplies three real options, resist, accept and direct, and the figures can say what each leads to, but cannot select among them without a ground and a declared stake. Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral; it makes the choice visible.

## 6. Correction to my own §1 and §3

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any target at all. That is too strong, in the same way the exemplar corrected its own overstatement. The exact phrase governs "a **missing** thing": a primitive not held in the library. Species, conditions and relations are not missing from the manager's library; they are already there and are enumerated from contact (Fig 11 :43-48). Naming them is a binding from the ecology frame onto held terms, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in a naive answer is different: treating that binding as if the framework had named it, or as if naming it moved the wall (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world."). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From the mapped *Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* entry in the exemplar: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A well-known review endorsing a target gives the target a budget to be tried. It does not make the target correct.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The 2026-08-19 essay says outputs of this kind "must be seen as simulacra." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
