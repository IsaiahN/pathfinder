## The question as an effect

Restoration currently compares a site against a target state, historically a named reference community from a past climate. When climate moves, the target operand of that comparison goes stale: planted assemblages fail, recruitment shifts, interactions rearrange. Two options are being decided. Option A, keep the historical reference as the settlement criterion and treat change as a deviation to be corrected. Option B, treat the historical reference as a held term that is false as a settlement criterion, re-enumerate the habitat under the current climate, and restate the goal as a measurable, falsifiable, per-slot trajectory that the manager maintains. Each option leads somewhere different: A keeps a goal pinned to a thing that moves with climate and spends monitoring effort on a stale metric; B keeps the goal in contact with what actually persists and functions, and accepts that the work is permanent. The figures decide between them in `01` and `09`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). Gathering agreement about a target does not turn agreement into a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list chosen for likely assent is a list chosen from one pool.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interior.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public and opt-in, which avoids that failure as well as the structural one.
