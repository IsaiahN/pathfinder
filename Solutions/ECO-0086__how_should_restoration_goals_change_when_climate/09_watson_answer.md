## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a restoration goal should be when the site no longer resembles the historical conditions the goal was written for. Option A: keep the historical reference as the settlement criterion and treat divergence as an error to correct. Option B: treat the historical reference as a held term that climate has refuted, re-enumerate the habitat from present contact, and restate success as per-slot, falsifiable comparisons over a trajectory that is rebound as conditions move. What each leads to: A pins the goal to a thing that moves with climate and spends monitoring on a stale metric; B keeps the goal in contact with what persists and functions and accepts permanent work. The figures decide the form of the goal, not the name of the community.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Per-slot gap between the restoration plan's prediction and what is observed under the new climate: mortality, failed recruitment, shifted interactions, conditions leaving the target's range. Arity: many slots, each with its own sign and mass; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Symmetry: the goal is an ordered comparison `target ⋛ observed`, not a symmetric join; Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction." Scale: site, landscape, biome, with a lossy transform between and cascades two or three steps out.
- **Domain 1, the reference term.** Actors: historical reference state, target composition, benchmark. Currency: fidelity, distance to target. Anchor: none of its own; it borrows the ground's authority falsely and is a held term in `Γ`. Partition boundary: which compositions may serve as a settlement criterion.
- **Domain 2, the living habitat under climate.** Actors: species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact, survival, persistence, function. Anchor: the ground, what actually persists and functions under observed climate; Fig 11 :22 "the ground still decides". Partition: habitat is read off the world, never composed.
- **Domain 3, the management seat.** Actors: managers, plans, interventions. Currency: actions, with introduction available and exclusion not. Anchor: none; it reads the ground and authors conventions only. Partition: seat office, not a frame to be corrected by agreement.
- **Domain 4, the success criterion.** Actors: monitors, metrics, indicators, baselines. Currency: per-slot residual and ground readings. Anchor: the ground via a measurement that does not update on the frame. Partition: measurement link, downstream of domains 1 and 2.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Re-enumerate the habitat (perception), refuse the false reference term (vocabulary), restate the goal as a measurable comparison (objective), choose interventions (planning), measure the round trip and re-enumerate (carry). Naming a community before re-enumerating is a planning move made above the break.

Strain test applied: the first reading put climate in the anchor slot and so made "the ground does not update" conflict with "climate changes." Re-census resolved it: climate is a habitat condition (Fig 11 :46), not the ground; the ground is contact and persistence (Fig 11 :22). A second strain, "exclusion is not" read as "managers cannot remove invaders," was narrowed to the habitat, not the organism. Both re-censuses are recorded in `01` §5.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The form of the goal must change; the name of the target is not available. Historical fidelity fails the anchor test when climate moves, so the owed term is a refusal of fidelity as the settlement criterion, not a new named community. The habitat is re-enumerated from present contact, actor valence is relative to the residual, and there is no standing list of good actors. The work is permanent because introduction is available and habitat subtraction is not; moving material to track climate is a habitat substitution whose omissions are invisible until the goal fails. Success becomes per-slot, falsifiable comparisons over a trajectory, with the round trip between shorthand and site measured. Historical and adaptive goal forms are isomers over the same atoms with different bonds. This is `specified`, ungrounded, and conditional on the question's premise. Which band to steer toward remains a `gap` for the ground.

- **cost if wrong:** the main cost is a stale goal that keeps reporting success while the system it was written for is gone, plus monitoring effort spent on a metric that averages away the live per-slot signal (Fig 1 :22). A secondary cost is pushing an unproven planting list under the authority of the framework, which would spend an outside checker's independence. Both are bounded by leading with the shape and the refuter, which is Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The binding from the shape to a particular target is removable without touching the derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, because nothing was minted as a new primitive. What is not undoable is a habitat substitution already made in the field: "Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose" (Fig 11 :67).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Four pre-registered tests are in `01` §3: a target that keeps settling success under a climate push while remaining a non-deciding convention; a fixed species list with invariant valence across two residuals that still pays against the ground; one global fidelity score that predicts per-slot persistence as well as per-slot measurement across two climates; and a long-term multi-biome result in which historical-composition targets match or beat trajectory targets with no interaction with climate novelty. Any of these sends the derivation back to the census. A source-discriminating instrument that separates persistence from maintained appearance would name what a single current metric cannot. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
