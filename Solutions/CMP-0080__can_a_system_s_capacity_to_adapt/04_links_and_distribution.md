## 3. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35 and :40** - agreement selected for likely assent is a convention, not corroboration. No receptiveness rank, no bulk send, no personal emails.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A wrong call about a system's crisis resilience costs real losses, so invite a low-overlap frame rather than a friendly one.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the three-part separator, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing the framework, the figure text, or the model.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three framework sources above and the wider literature. State that the figures, not the summary, are the authority, and that their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Require each user to write their own refuter and state their own mode; a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The literal pre-crisis capacity prediction does not survive (F1, F4, F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`): it is refused as a `gap` and replaced by a shape plus refuter. Send the corrected packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, not the refused claim.
