# Expert verification packet - the Tether on pre-crisis adaptive capacity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complex-systems researcher or resilience scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three sub-claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the literal pre-crisis capacity number. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A system's capacity to adapt cannot be predicted from its structure alone before a crisis, because capability is a property of agent-and-habitat and the crisis ground settles the verdict. Structure constrains the shape of what can respond (per slot; same atoms, different bond, different substance; catalysts and loop signs that an inventory cannot see), but the contact field and the realized outcome are not in the pre-crisis structural description. The honest deliverable is a three-part separator: structural conditions that constrain the response, contact conditions that keep a non-updating ground reachable, and a threshold reading of the current trajectory against a limit, plus written failure conditions. A synthetic solve on a model of the structure proves wiring, never capability. The literal capacity number stays `gap` until a held-out crisis votes.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 1

- **Statement:** capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the structure alone, and the signal is indexed per slot, not averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Also Fig 11 :40 *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* And Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** a held-out demonstration that a structure-only description predicts realized adaptation across low-overlap systems and crises, above a habitat-aware baseline, against a non-updating outcome; or evidence that the crisis residual is genuinely global and not slot-indexed.

### C2 - structure-facing; checkable against a system's own components and bonds

- **Statement:** the relevant structural facts are the typed bonds, the renewal status of parts, and the sign of feedback loops, not the component count. Over a fixed atom set, two systems with the same components and different bonds are isomers with different behaviour.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
- **Refuter:** two systems with identical atoms, bond types and loop signs, whose parts are all catalysts rather than spent, with no habitat difference, yet adapting differently under the same measured residual. World cross-check: Kirschner and Gerhart 1998 (*PNAS*) describe structural properties (weak linkage, compartmentation, redundancy, exploratory behaviour) that reduce interdependence and confer evolvability, which corroborates the bond-level claim, not a component count.

### C3 - prediction-facing; checkable against the channel and the ground

- **Statement:** the operational pre-crisis reading is a threshold comparison of a trajectory against a limit, read through a channel that can decay while the ground does not. A harness result is wiring evidence, not capability evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :87 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (comparison `⋛`). Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 *"stale percepts, dropped transients,"*; Fig 10 :27 *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **Refuter:** a failed prediction while the channel demonstrably stayed reachable and the ground demonstrably moved; or a synthetic solve that transfers to a real non-updating crisis outcome without re-substituting the habitat. World cross-check: the early-warning-signal literature reads critical slowing down, autocorrelation and variance as trajectory signals, and also records that some regime shifts occur with no warning, which bounds the separator's false-negative mode.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a capacity index, resilience score or universal number read from structure.
- Not a standing list of adaptability features; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51).
- Not that early-warning signals are useless; they are channel readings with a false-negative mode.
- Not that an agent-based reproduction of adaptation is capability.
- Not that expert agreement settles the question; agreement from one evidence pool is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :35, :40).
- Not `grounded`. No crisis measurement was taken by this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. In priority order: (1) a held-out, non-updating crisis outcome that falsifies or confirms the structure-only baseline; (2) a harness-to-field transfer test; (3) an instrument that resolves the operand type of `⋛` at the threshold level; (4) a cross-residual census of a candidate feature's valence; (5) a channel-provenance check on any failed prediction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
