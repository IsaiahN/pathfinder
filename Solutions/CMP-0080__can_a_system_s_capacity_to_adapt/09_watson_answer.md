## VERDICT (from the figures)

**No, not from structure alone before the crisis.** Structure fixes a shape, not the capacity. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the structure alone (Fig 11 :39-40), and the capacity signal is per slot (Fig 1 :19). Structure constrains the response through bonds, catalysts, loop signs and threshold comparisons (Fig 12 :117, :119, :121, :87), so two systems with the same components can adapt differently, but the habitat and the ground are not in the pre-crisis structural description. A harness result proves wiring, never capability (Fig 11 :70), and a capacity index is a filter for the budget, not a witness for a verdict (Fig 9 :60). The literal pre-crisis capacity number is `gap`, unreachable at this time: unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134). What is delivered is a three-part separator plus six pre-registered failure conditions, mode `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the number, nothing `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, names the refuter before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording of one structural analysis instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). A wrong call about a system's crisis resilience has real losses, which is why the stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused claim is replaceable by the corrected packet without withdrawing any primitive or import. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already carry the corrected form. A sent claim can be superseded, and nothing has to be deleted from the record: a refusal adds a term and the reach falls by derivation (Fig 6 :42).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A held-out, non-updating crisis outcome in which a structure-only description beats a habitat-aware baseline would refute Fig 11 :39-40; a harness-to-field transfer without habitat substitution would refute Fig 11 :70; an instrument that resolves the operand type of `⋛` at the threshold level would clear strain S4; a structural feature with stable positive valence across a census of low-overlap residuals would refute Fig 11 :51; an exhibited figure line returning the number would clear the `gap`. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
