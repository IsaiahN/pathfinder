# Outreach draft: check the shape of an importance claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal addresses are used.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survive your own read of the verbatim figure lines. The offer collapses on contact if the shape claim does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One check on a "most important species" claim, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am writing about a specific claim about ecosystem functions, not asking you to read a framework. I have one output I would like you to check against what you already know. If it does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the refuters, not `grounded`):**
>
> "Most important species" is not a property a species carries. It is a relation between an actor or an interaction and one measured function at one place and time. So the question about pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation and carbon storage is not one ranking but five function-indexed ledgers over an enumerated habitat. Each ledger entry is a species or interaction whose removal leaves a measurable change in that one function, with the habitat enumerated outward from direct contact until the cascade stops mattering. This is a prediction of the **form** of the answer, not a list of names and not a measurement.
>
> Three things follow that you can check without seeing where the claim came from:
>
> 1. An actor that helps one function can harm another. A single cross-function ranking averages away the signal that tells you where to look.
> 2. Direct partners are not enough. An effect that matters can be two or three steps out, which is how a pesticide reached birds of prey whose food chain did not include it directly.
> 3. Species and interactions are the same kind of ledger entry under this claim. Both get a valence relative to the function, not a permanent label of "good" or "keystone".
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name the most important species for any of the five functions, that a centrality score or a citation count is an importance score, that the functions share one unit and can be added, or that anything here is settled by experiment. Those names are owed a removal or addition measurement, not an argument.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. One measurement ranks an actor across all five functions and does not change when the function changes.
> 2. An actor's importance is invariant to place, time and which function is measured.
> 3. A direct-partners-only census captures a documented third-order effect on one of the functions.
> 4. Agreement among researchers who share the same evidence pool changes a measured function.
> 5. A real intervention removes a species from a habitat, rather than substituting one habitat for another.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any holds, I withdraw the corresponding part. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Please answer this first:** for the function you know best, what would you count as a failure of the ledger form above? Record that before reading anything else.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the source material is below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, public repository: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Figures README, canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Keystone species and food webs, Jordán 2009: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685432/
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a function you can measure. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for your time,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Each reader checks the shape without holding the framework interior. |
| Do not ask for agreement or trust | Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Record the verdict before the theory is shown | Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Once a reader holds the interior, their agreement stops being evidence. |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| No named species in stage 1 | Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The framework returns a shape; the name is owed the ground. |
| Centrality withheld as a verdict | Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* |
| Habitat enumeration stated before any ranking | Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* |
| No request for prestige or endorsement | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* |

## Stage 2, opt-in only

Send the three links only after the stage-1 verdict is recorded. Stage 2 is not a persuasion step. If the reader is curious about what produced the shape, the figures are the authority. If the reader is not curious, stop.

## Stage 3, opt-in only

Offer the formula as a reusable generator, with the instruction that each user names their own ground and writes their own refuter before running it. A generator is permitted to cross (Fig 4 :22); a recording of one result is not.
