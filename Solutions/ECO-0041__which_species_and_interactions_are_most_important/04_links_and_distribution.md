1. **Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: the claim, three checkable sub-claims, verbatim figure lines, refuters and a request for the failure condition. Record the reader's verdict before anything else is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, send the three public links. The figures, not this summary, are the authority. A stage-2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a reusable generator. Each user names their own ground and writes their own refuter before running it; the figure's own "Direction" rule says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.
