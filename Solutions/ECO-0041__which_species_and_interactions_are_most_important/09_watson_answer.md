## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the request to name the species and interactions most important for pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation and carbon storage. Option A: produce one ranked list across the five functions. Option B: refuse the single list as a union compound, split it into five function-indexed ledgers over enumerated habitats, and let a per-function measurement fill the rows. A leads to an average that hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22) and a standing actor roster that does not survive a change of residual (Fig 11 :51). B leads to five checkable ledgers, explicit intersections and six refuters, with every name withheld until the ground pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicted and measured function under a species or interaction change, per slot `s = (F, P, T)`. Arity: five functions times sites and times, so a union of overlapping conditions, not one question (Fig 9 :5, :54-55). Symmetry: valence is not symmetric, an actor helps one function and harms another, so importance is a relation, not a kind. Scale: direct contact, then cascade to second and third order.
- **Domain 1, the five function residuals.** Actors: pollen deposition, soil formation rate, nutrient flux, water regulation, carbon stock. Currency: measured change under removal or addition. Anchor: the measurement, which does not move on expert opinion. Union, not partition. Order: first, can each function be read at the site.
- **Domain 2, the habitat and cascade.** Actors: every species in contact, conditions of light, water, temperature, season and terrain, relations of pollination, predation, competition and dependency. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the world, enumerated not composed. Neither partition nor union of theories; an enumeration. Order: after D1, because the residual selects which contacts matter.
- **Domain 3, management.** Actors: managers and restoration practitioners. Currency: introductions and interventions. Anchor: D1's measurement. Partition of maintained and unmaintained modes. Order: after D2, because you cannot plan through an unenumerated habitat.
- **Order by dependency:** enumerate, measure, compare, intervene, learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). A link with no instrument stops the chain before planning.
- **Strain test:** Fig 12 :123 (functional group) and Fig 11 :51 (no standing list) seemed to strain. Re-census: a functional group is a role class whose qualifier is supplied by the residual, while a standing list is a permanent roster of names. Different objects, no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The requested list is refused; the shape survives. **Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the refuters, nothing `grounded`.** Importance is a relation between an actor or interaction and one measured function at one place and time. The answer form is five function-indexed ledgers over enumerated habitats, with intersections as compounds, species and interactions treated identically, valence relative to the residual, no standing keystone roster, cross-function comparison at declared units and no sum. The ground is a removal or addition measurement with a control. A managed ranking is maintained work. No actor is named, because no measurement was read.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the union split is wrong, a single-function study will still work and only the cross-function claim fails; the cost is a wasted ledger, not a corrupted instrument. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a name before the shape and refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the packet's sub-claims.
- **undoable:** yes. The ledgers are empty by design; a measured row can be added without withdrawing the shape. If a cross-function common unit is found, the no-sum rule can be superseded by a provenanced import. If a direct-contact refuter fires, the enumeration rule is withdrawn and replaced. Nothing in the record has to be deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-function removal or addition experiments would fill the rows; a declared additive unit that predicts a removal residual would permit one ranking; a direct-partners-only census that captures a third-order effect would break Fig 11 :52-53; a real exclusion would break Fig 11 :61; an invariant actor score across all five functions would break Fig 11 :49. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
