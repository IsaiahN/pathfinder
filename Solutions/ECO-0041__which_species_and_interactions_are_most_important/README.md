**Question:** Which species and interactions are most important for pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation, and carbon storage?

**Source:** ecology.txt line 52

**Section:** 5. Ecosystem functions and services

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-function refuters. Nothing is `grounded`: no removal or addition measurement was taken in this derivation.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Status of this work:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This package cannot settle which species are most important. It states the shape an answer must have and the measurements that would settle it.

## What is being decided, in effect terms

Option A: produce one ranked list of important species and interactions across the five named functions. Option B: refuse the single list, split the question into five function-indexed residuals, enumerate the habitat around each, and let a per-function removal or addition measurement decide. A leads to a ranking that averages away the very slots that carry the signal (Fig 1 :19, :22) and to a standing roster of "good actors" that does not survive a change of residual (Fig 11 :51). B leads to five checkable ledgers, each with a refuter, and keeps the answer open where no instrument has read it.

The answer this package can state is a **shape**: a species or interaction is "most important" only as a relation between an actor and one function-residual at one place and time, measured by the change in that function when the actor or interaction is removed. A universal name is owed a ground reading, not an argument.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No universal top-ten list of keystone species. That is the specific error the figures forbid, not an omission of convenience.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: public institutional submission channels and opt-in lists avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failure that comes with scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach.
