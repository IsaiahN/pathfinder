# The Tether's answer on important species and interactions

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the six refuters, not `grounded`.** This is a form and a route, not a list of names and not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks which species and interactions are most important for pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation and carbon storage. The residual is the gap between what a manager predicts for each function and what is measured when an actor or interaction is present, removed or added, per site and time. The five functions are a **union** of overlapping conditions, not one question, and the ranking is demanded in units that cannot be added.

## 2. The shape

1. **Five ledgers, not one ranking.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A cross-function average is how the live signal disappears (Fig 1 :22). Each function gets its own ledger; actor overlaps between functions are kept as a compound.
2. **Importance is a relation, not a property.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* A species is important *to a measured function at a place and time*, never in the abstract.
3. **No standing keystone roster.** Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The framework can state the role class, a functional group in Fig 12 :123, but it cannot issue a permanent list of names.
4. **Enumerate the habitat; do not compose it.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* Direct contact is not enough: Fig 11 :52-53 records the DDT-to-eagles cascade three steps out.
5. **Species and interactions are the same kind of entry.** Fig 11 :47: *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"*; Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels."* Both are valence-bearing entries in the enumerated habitat, answered by the same removal test.
6. **The anchor is a measurement.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Agreement among researchers is not an instrument.
7. **No cross-function sum.** ⋛ is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table :48-51). A comparison at declared units is allowed; an addition across five units is not.
8. **Management is maintained work.** Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* A managed ranking is a maintained intervention, not a solved state.

## 3. Verdicts per domain, kept separate

| domain | verdict | mode |

**No averaging across these domains.** A strong result in D2 says nothing about D3. Fig 1 :22 warns that aggregation is how a live signal disappears.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of species. No actor is named, because no removal measurement was taken.
- Not a claim that biodiversity loss has no effect on these functions. The world literature says the opposite; this package only refuses to compress that effect into one ranking.
- Not that functions are independent. They overlap; the intersections are their own compound.
- Not that centrality, abundance or citation counts are importance. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the ground settles any row of any ledger here.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. For each of the five functions:

- **Removal or addition with a control**, read against the function's own units, as Paine's *Pisaster* removal was read against diversity.
- **Cascade extent**: enumerate out to where the cascade stops mattering, and check the third-order refuter. A direct-partners-only census that captures a third-order effect falsifies the method.
- **Incommensurability check**: a declared common unit that makes the five functions additive and predicts a removal residual would permit a single ranking. None is supplied here.
- **Management check**: a real exclusion rather than a substitution would break Fig 11 :61 and reopen the management verdict.

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5: invariance of an actor's importance across functions and sites; a direct-contact-only capture of a third-order effect; consensus changing a measured function; a derived universal keystone name with no ground reading.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figure lines govern the shape; the instruments for soil formation and water regulation at the relevant scale are often missing. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README's closing rule applies: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the most important species, and it does not add the five functions into one score. It says that pollination, soil formation, nutrient cycling, water regulation and carbon storage are a union of overlapping conditions, so the answer must be five function-indexed ledgers plus their intersections. In each ledger, a species or an interaction is important only as a relation to a measured function at a place and time: remove it and read the change. The habitat must be enumerated outward from contact until the cascade stops mattering, because the effect that counts can be three steps out. Valence is relative to the residual, so there is no standing keystone roster, and cross-function ranking is a comparison at declared units, never a sum. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, six falsifiers written in advance, and the measurements that would fill the rows. The ledgers stay open until the ground settles them.
