## The question, restated as an effect

Who should set a firm's objective (its purpose) and who should receive its residual (its profits)?
Each option leads somewhere: shareholder primacy makes capital the objective setter, manager
control makes the agent the objective setter, worker or stakeholder claims spread the setter across
the habitat. The answer we would look like is a rule for which seat may legitimately set the
objective, a separation of that objective from the metric, and an account of why no claimant can
occupy the anchor seat. That shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` before any figure line is
chosen, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. A room does not add an anchor. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. Sending the full theory before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style
rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
