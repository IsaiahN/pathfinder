## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public, opt-in venues in `03` avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one with a public, checkable record of contact with urban ecological ground, reached through an opt-in channel, positioned to refute the shape claim, not a list of people already inclined to agree. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
