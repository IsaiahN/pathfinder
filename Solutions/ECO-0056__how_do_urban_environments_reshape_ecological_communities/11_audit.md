## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: at least two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*), not one hard problem. Symmetry: the relations are directed and valence-bearing (Fig 11 :49-50), so a symmetric join would erase the difference between benefactor and malfactor. Scale: per place and per species slot, cascading outward, reaching a human institutional scale with a new anchor (Fig 1 :19, Fig 11 :52-54, Fig 10 :68).
- **Domain 1, the urban habitat and ecological community.** Actors: species, conditions and relations, read off the world (Fig 11 :45-48). Currency: contact, arrival and persistence (Fig 11 :56). Anchor: a non-updating ground, what survives under measurement (Fig 2 :29). Question type: enumeration and cascade, not composition.
- **Domain 2, the human seat.** Actors: residents, planners, managers, institutions. Currency: introductions, services and disservices, wellbeing readings. Anchor: human judgment is legitimate here because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36-37), but only an instrument for the ecology question (Fig 2 :38). Question type: a second track with an internal seam, not a fifth step.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers, sensors, plots, datasets. Currency: residuals and settled terms. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

Order by dependency: residual first, then direct contact, then outward cascade, then the seat's acts and readings, then verification (Fig 11 :44, Fig 3 :20). The link most likely to break is vocabulary/objective, where there is no instrument for what counts as progress in a relationship with people (Fig 3 :27).

My census matches the primary's three domains, its union reading and its dependency order. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-69 (habitat enumeration, cascade, substitution, introduction), Fig 2 :36-38 (two anchors), Fig 9 :54-57 (union) and Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the city substitutes a habitat; enumerate contacts outward; valence is relative to the residual; two anchors, two rows; urban and non-urban communities are isomers over a fixed atom set.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete urban ecology claim: a specific city, a specific canopy fraction, a named species list, and one wellbeing score.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source of the anchor** for the ecological row, which the shorthand leaves as "the ground" but which in a city must be an actual instrument or monitoring record;
- the **method-versus-recording distinction** (Fig 4 :22-25), which the shorthand preserves but which a concrete species list would discard;
- the **fixed-atom condition** on the isomer claim, which a real city with novel actors and conditions may not satisfy;
- the **gap itself**, which a concrete request for numbers would erase.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: substitution plus outward enumeration plus per-residual valence, with two separate anchors. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
