## Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I redid the census from the residual before comparing it with the primary's headings, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between a theory's exterior prediction and an instrument reading. Arity: plural theories, plural slots, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: the horizon is one-way, so no symmetric bond reaches the interior; the cutoff behaves like a threshold, not an identity. Scale: first order for local geometry, second order for dynamics, imported scale for the lab analogue.
- **Domain 1, exterior observation.** Actors: GW detectors, VLBI arrays, astrometric trackers, X-ray spectrometers. Currency: exterior observables and per-slot residuals. Anchor: the instrument reading. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 2, theory.** Actors: Kerr metric, boundary conditions, quasi-normal modes, Love numbers. Currency: predictions. Anchor: the same instrument ground plus consistency. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain 3, analogue laboratory.** Actors: BEC and superconducting qubit chain with a sonic horizon. Currency: Hawking-like correlations. Anchor: lab measurement. Nameable anchor: yes. Different closure, low overlap.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulation all seats share one pool, so independence is zero.
- **Candidate fifth domain, the interior.** Excluded: no reading varies with it without also varying with the theory. No anchor, therefore not a domain yet.

Order by dependency: perceive an exterior reading, then have terms for what it affords, then say what progress on each slot means, then aim a probe, then carry what worked. A demand for a direct interior reading is below the break. Partition or union: the probes partition only under an independence test; the theory contact is a union of overlapping conditions.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its exclusion of the interior, its union reading and its dependency order. ## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 6 :32-34 (instrument route and proto-instrument), Fig 9 :54-55 (union), Fig 13 :134 (open slot).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: test from outside by per-slot residuals against an unmoving anchor, unbundle, sharpen instruments, keep the interior open.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim such as "ringdown consistency rules out the alternative" or "the lab analogue confirms an astrophysical interior mechanism".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **independence condition**, which can collapse when two messengers share a calibration or prior;
- the **anchor condition**, which fails when a model-dependent reconstruction is treated as a measurement;
- the **scope of the analogue import**, which returns a mechanism term and not an interior term;
- the **null-result discipline**, which forbids promoting a failed search into an exclusion proof.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: residuals are per slot, and instruments are sharpened from ones already returning something. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. `09_watson_answer.md` states this honestly.
