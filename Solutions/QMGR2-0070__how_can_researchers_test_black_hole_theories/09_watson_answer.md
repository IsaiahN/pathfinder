## VERDICT (from the figures)

The method survives; any claim to have seen the interior does not. Exterior tests are legitimate because the anchor is a measurement that does not move with theory (Fig 2 :29), residuals are per slot (Fig 1 :19), and instruments are sharpened from ones already returning something (Fig 6 :32). The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (Fig 9 :55). Cross-messenger agreement counts only with real independence (Fig 2 :31). The interior stays an open slot: unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134), and a null result is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39). The analogue frame imports a mechanism, not an interior term. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. No theory is settled by agreement or prestige; only a source-discriminating ground reading would do that.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is treating model-dependent reconstructions as anchors or pooling two pipelines as two witnesses, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both are avoidable by naming the slot, the anchor and the evidence pool before ranging any theory.
- **undoable:** yes. A defective anchor or a collapsed independence claim is removable without touching the rest of the method; a theory can be reranged on a corrected slot, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating exterior observable that separates theories currently degenerate on the same shadow or spectrum would close a slot; a direct interior reading varying with interior theory would fill the open slot; a demonstration that two declared probes share a common determinant would collapse the independence claim; a demonstration that the union is separable would reverse the unbundling instruction. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
