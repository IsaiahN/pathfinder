Overlap warning: the roadmap, GWTC-1, EHT and GRAVITY papers are all part of the same broad gravitational physics evidence culture and several share collaboration authorship. Their agreement on Kerr is not four independent witnesses for a claim about an interior. The analogue papers are a genuinely different closure (lab systems), but Thebault's paper shows that their evidential reach is itself contested.

## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the shape claim and its refuter first. Do not attach the framework.
- Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The method is permitted to travel; a recording of one result is not.
- Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Ask what they would expect to see at a named slot before giving them the sources.
- Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask for agreement and do not select recipients for likely agreement.
- Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A non reply is data about reachability, not evidence against the claim.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send `02_outreach_email.md` as a one to one message or as a cover note to a public venue. It contains the residual, the shape claim and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask for the recipient's failure criterion and record it before anything else is sent.

**Stage 2, opt in only.** If they are curious, send the seven source links in section 1 with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority. The summary is a simulated derivation and must be labelled as such.

**Stage 3, opt in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground. A reported failure is more useful than a reported success.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `02` are the instrument for that check.

## 6. No zip, no push

There is no archive and no list to upload. Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting any packet and sending it is the user's act. Prefer an opt-in submission page or a public thread over an attachment pushed to a list, because an opt-in route lets a recipient decline contact without cost.
