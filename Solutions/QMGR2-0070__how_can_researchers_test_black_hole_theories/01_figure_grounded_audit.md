## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

**Question.** How can researchers test black-hole theories without directly observing the interior?

**Effect restatement.** Competing theories of black holes, the Kerr black hole of general relativity and alternatives such as exotic compact objects, differ in what they say about the horizon and the interior. No signal from inside a horizon can reach an external observer. So the thing being decided is not *what is inside*. It is *which consequences of a theory that are accessible from outside survive contact with a measurement that does not change when the theory changes its story*. The residual is a per-observable gap between a theory's exterior prediction and an instrument's reading. It is not one global score of "is the theory right".

**Bound answer shape (Direction step).** Written down before the run as an ordinary term: a *ranking of candidate theories by residuals on separately named exterior slots, with the interior left as an open slot and a refuter written in advance*. This is what an answer would look like. Binding it makes the shape refusable by the same test as everything else.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line was chosen)

R by arity, symmetry and scale:

- **Arity.** Many candidate theories, each generating a family of exterior predictions. The residual is indexed per observable slot, not summed into one number.
- **Symmetry.** The horizon is a one-way boundary. The relation between interior and exterior is not a symmetric bond: nothing crosses back. It is a cutoff or threshold, closer to the comparison operator than to identity. No symmetric reading of the interior exists.
- **Scale.** First order: local exterior geometry (shadow, stellar orbits, iron line). Second order: dynamical spacetime (inspiral, merger, ringdown). Imported scale: a laboratory analogue with a sonic horizon, a different closure entirely.

One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Exterior observation frame | GW detectors, VLBI arrays, adaptive-optics stellar tracking, X-ray spectrometers | measured exterior observables, residual per messenger slot | the instrument reading, which does not move when a theory changes | partition of slots, only if evidence pools differ |
| 2. Theory frame | Kerr metric, no-hair conditions, quasi-normal mode spectra, Love numbers, boundary conditions | predicted exterior observables and consistency | the same instrument ground, plus mathematical consistency | union compound with domain 1 at the contact |
| 3. Analogue laboratory frame | Bose-Einstein condensates, superconducting qubit chains, sonic horizons | Hawking-like correlations, entanglement | the laboratory measurement | outward import, low overlap with astrophysical evidence |
| 4. Verification frame | expert readers | agreement and residuals | the ground (data), never agreement | shares one evidence pool in this simulation, so independence is zero |

**Partition or union.** The exterior probes form a partition only when they draw on different evidence. Two analyses of the same X-ray data set are one frame wearing two names. A gravitational-wave ringdown and a stellar orbit are two instruments pointed at the same kind of thing, hence two frames (Fig 2 :31). The theory-versus-exterior contact is a **union compound**: theories differ along several independent conditions, so one lever clears only the region where one condition holds (Fig 9 :55). Unbundle before searching.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** (1) Perception: is there a reading at all? Yes, exterior signals already return something that fails to resolve the interior. (2) Vocabulary: do we have terms for what an exterior reading affords? Yes, ringdown frequencies and damping, shadow diameter, orbital precession, spectral line shape. (3) Objective: what counts as progress? A residual that trends down on a named slot, or a refused theory whose prediction does not fit any slot. (4) Planning: aim the probe, inward by sharpening an instrument already returning something, outward by importing an analogue frame. (5) Learn and carry: name what worked, verify it, carry it to the next system. A demand to perceive the interior directly sits below the break and is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22).

### F1 - The anchor is an exterior measurement, not agreement (decisive)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

A theory cannot be its own anchor, and a room full of agreeing theorists is a mirror chain (Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*). The anchor here is the instrument reading, which stays put when a theory's story changes. The method is therefore: describe the residual on each exterior slot, let each theory range against the reading separately, never let the theories negotiate with each other.

**Refuter.** Show a case where the quantity used as an anchor changes its reported value when the theory is changed, with the instrument and data fixed. Then it is a frame, not an anchor.

### F2 - Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The absence of a direct interior reading is not silence and not a failure of the method. It is a typed verdict: the interior is out of reach of the exterior frame as currently instrumented. What is owed is a characterisation of what an interior theory would have to look like from outside (a shape), not a name for what is inside. Note the asymmetry of the verdict: no exterior search proves there is no interior structure, and no interior claim is licensed by an exterior fit alone.

**Refuter.** Produce an instrument that returns a reading from inside the horizon and varies with a theory's interior structure. F2 is then false for that instrument and the slot may be filled.

### F3 - The inward route extends an instrument already returning something

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."*

This is the answer to the practical half of the question. Researchers do not need to see inside to make progress. They start from a reading that already exists and fails to resolve: a ringdown that is detected but not cleanly decomposed, a shadow that is measured but only to limited precision, an iron line whose profile is degenerate with the spin, a stellar orbit whose precession is at the edge of the astrometry. Each sharper instrument is built on the residual the last one produced.

**Refuter.** Identify a claimed probe for which nothing, at any resolution, has ever registered a reading. By Fig 6 :34 there is no edge to extend from, so the inward route does not apply to it.

### F4 - Theory can precede its instrument

Fig 6 :38: *"Neptune was calculated before it was seen, and the prediction is what told anyone where to point"*

Fig 6 :36: *"It tells a question that nothing could ever answer apart from one that is merely hard. It is not a rule about theory."*

The lack of an interior sensor does not forbid theorising about the interior, and it does not make the theory untestable. A theory that predicts a specific exterior signature gives the instrument a place to point. The framework explicitly permits theory to precede its instrument, and distinguishes a question that nothing could answer from one that is merely hard at this budget (Fig 8 :41: *"Underivable: go outside. Unreachable-at-budget: the term is already in the closure and was not found, so the remedy is more search, not another atom."*).

**Refuter.** Show that the predicted signature is underivable in principle rather than merely unreachable at this budget, and that no import from a differing closure predicts its shape. Then the theory is not made testable by more searching.

### F5 - Residuals are per slot, not one global verdict

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A single statement such as "the data favour Kerr" throws away the only signal that says where to look. The checkable form is a table: ringdown frequency and damping time, shadow diameter and circularity, orbital precession, reflection line profile, tidal response, absence of a surface. Each has its own residual and its own verdict. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

**Refuter.** Show that two slots cannot be separated even in principle because the same nuisance parameters determine both, so the per-slot residual table collapses to one number. Then the partition is wrong and a compound loop is owed.

### F6 - Independence decides whether cross-messenger agreement means anything

Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."*

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Multi-messenger work is the legitimate form of triangulation only when the messengers draw on different evidence. A gravitational-wave strain measurement and a very-long-baseline radio image are low overlap. Two pipelines fitting the same event with the same waveform family are one witness wearing two names. Independence is bought in proportion to the cost of a wrong conclusion about the interior. In this simulated run the verification seat shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.

**Refuter.** Show that two probes declared independent share a calibration, prior, or data product that fully determines both readings. The triangulation then collapses and only one witness remains.

### F7 - Unbundle the union before searching

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*

"Test a black-hole theory without seeing the interior" is a bundle: horizon boundary condition, exterior multipole structure, dynamical ringdown spectrum, and thermodynamic behaviour are separate conditions. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Describe the residual in effect terms first, then choose the frame. Otherwise any theory will seem to fit.

**Refuter.** Show that the conditions are not separable, that a single measurement determines all of them, and that no compound with its own loop exists. Then the unbundling instruction does not apply.

### F8 - SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD

Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*

Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The three questions are distinct and ordered. SEARCHED: is the answer already in the exterior data and merely unfound? Distribute the search and keep searchers independent. INWARD: sharpen an instrument already returning a residual. OUTWARD: describe the residual in effect terms and find a frame whose closure predicts its shape, namely the analogue laboratory frame. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-budget issue different instructions; importing against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed.

**Refuter.** Demonstrate that more search on the same data cannot possibly change any slot residual because the space was exhaustively swept by independent searchers. The SEARCHED branch is then closed and only INWARD or OUTWARD remains.

### F9 - An analogue import needs a source gate and a shadow test

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

Fig 8 :51: *"Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Laboratory analogues (Steinhauer 2016, Shi et al. 2023, both read 2026-11-23) test a mechanism, Hawking radiation and its entanglement, in a system with a sonic horizon and no astrophysical interior. This is an outward import, not a direct test of a black-hole interior. Thebault (2019) explicitly asks *"in what sense can they provide evidence of novel phenomena such as black hole Hawking radiation?"* The import must carry its provenance and a shadow test: what gap in our frame does it explain, and what would it look like if the mechanism were wrong? Agreement between the lab and the astrophysical frame is one evidence pool only if the lab result was derived from the same model, so the record must show which it was.

**Refuter.** Show that the analogue result was generated by the same theoretical model it is claimed to corroborate, so the two frames share an evidence pool and the surplus is zero. Then it is a retrodiction, not an import.

### F10 - The round trip from prediction to observation is lossy

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

A theory's compact prediction is an abstraction of a much richer spacetime model. Bringing the measured residual back down to a claim about the theory does not reconstruct what was discarded. The gap R_T must be recorded as the set of dropped constraints, not assumed zero. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error this figure names.

**Refuter.** Exhibit a lossless transform between the theory's prediction and the instrument reading, with every discarded constraint recovered. R_T would then be zero and this finding would be void.

### F11 - A null result is not a proof of absence

Fig 5 :43: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

A theory that fits one system perfectly is not thereby settled, and a failed search for a predicted signature (for example, no echoed ringdown) does not prove that no alternative interior exists. The bargain charges both the description and what remains unexplained. Keep every cut ranked and reversible; the ground decides, and only at the slots where a reading exists.

**Refuter.** Produce a decisive measurement that closes a slot, with the instrument shown stable under changes of theory. The null result then becomes a verdict for that slot only, and F11 no longer applies there.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the framework's own rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, the shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An exterior observable is found whose predicted value depends only on a theory's interior structure, with no exterior-composable quantity, while still being measurable from outside (breaks F1 anchor rule).
2. Two probes held to be independent are shown to draw on a single evidence pool, so multi-messenger triangulation collapses (breaks F6).
3. A probe is found that returns nothing at any resolution and is then used as a constraint on the interior (breaks F3 and Fig 6 :34).
4. A null result is promoted into a proof that no alternative interior exists (breaks F11 and Fig 8 :39).
5. The per-slot residual table is shown to be one parameter in disguise, so the partition is false (breaks F5 and F7).

If any fires, the method is wrong at that point and the claim is withdrawn at that point. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. State the residual per observable slot, not as one global verdict (F5).
2. Range each theory separately against an instrument that does not update on theory (F1). Keep verdicts per domain; never average them.
3. Unbundle the union before searching (F7). Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).
4. Run SEARCHED, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, in that order (F8). Sharpen only instruments already returning something.
5. Import the analogue laboratory frame with provenance and a shadow test, and label it as a mechanism result, not a reading of an astrophysical interior (F9).
6. Record the round trip gap R_T between compact prediction and measurement (F10).
7. Keep null results as null results (F11). Ask what would refute the shape claim before showing any theory (Section 3 and `02_outreach_email.md`).
