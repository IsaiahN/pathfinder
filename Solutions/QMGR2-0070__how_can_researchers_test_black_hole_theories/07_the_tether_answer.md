# The Tether's answer: testing black-hole theories from outside

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken here.

## 1. The residual it answers

Researchers must choose between competing black-hole theories while no signal from the interior can reach them. If they try to test by obtaining an interior reading, they are asking for an input that never arrives. If they test by ranging exterior predictions against instruments, they can make real progress without ever holding an interior term. The residual is a per-slot gap between prediction and reading, not one global verdict (Fig 1 :19).

## 2. The shape

1. **State the residual per observable slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Ringdown frequency and damping, shadow diameter, orbital precession, reflection line shape, tidal response and absence of a surface each get their own verdict. Verdicts are never averaged.
2. **Use an instrument as anchor, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Range each theory separately against a reading that stays put when the theory changes. Agreement among theorists is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35).
3. **Unbundle the union before searching.** Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Horizon boundary condition, exterior multipoles, dynamical spectrum and thermodynamics are overlapping conditions, not a list.
4. **Buy independence where the cost of being wrong is high.** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Gravitational-wave strain and a radio shadow image are low overlap. Two pipelines fitting the same event with the same waveform family are one witness.
5. **Run SEARCHED, then INWARD, then OUTWARD.** Is the answer already in the exterior data and merely unfound? If not, sharpen an instrument already returning something: Fig 6 :32, *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Then, if needed, import a frame with a different closure, casting the residual in effect terms.
6. **Keep the interior slot open and report its status.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Fig 6 :34: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Where nothing registers, there is no edge to extend from.
7. **Do not promote a null result.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
8. **Measure the round trip.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Record R_T between a theory's compact prediction and the instrument reading.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular alternative to Kerr is correct. Names from the physics frame are not returned by this derivation.
- Not that Hawking radiation has been observed from an astrophysical black hole. The analogue lab results are mechanism results in a different closure, with provenance and a shadow test.
- Not that a shadow image or a ringdown directly reads the interior. It reads exterior geometry and dynamics.
- Not that a failed search for echoes or other signatures closes the interior question.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles `left(R,φ)` in this simulated derivation. Prestige and agreement are not instruments (Fig 2 :35).

## 4. What would settle it, in stages

- **SEARCHED:** independent searchers sweep the existing exterior data with distinct pipelines. A residual that persists and does not trend down survives.
- **INWARD:** an instrument already returning a weak reading is sharpened, for example higher signal-to-noise ringdown decomposition, a larger VLBI baseline, or better astrometry of stellar orbits. Fig 6 :38: *"Neptune was calculated before it was seen, and the prediction is what told anyone where to point"*, so a theory's exterior prediction may name where to point next.
- **OUTWARD:** the analogue laboratory frame is imported with a source gate and a shadow test. It settles whether a mechanism works, not what is inside a specific astrophysical hole.
- **Ground:** a source-discriminating exterior observable that separates theories currently degenerate on the same reading, or a direct interior reading if one ever becomes possible. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.
- **Standing falsifiers:** see `01` §3. If any fires, the method is withdrawn at that point.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the method, unreached for the interior.** The method is complete for what it can do: rank theories by exterior residuals, keep independence explicit, sharpen instruments, import mechanisms. The interior stays an open slot. We cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it. The record of what was unreachable, what was imported and what would reverse the verdict is what keeps the stop checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> You do not need to see inside a black hole to test a theory about one. Treat the question as a bundle of separate exterior conditions, not one hard problem: horizon boundary, exterior geometry, ringdown dynamics and thermodynamics. For each observable slot, range each theory separately against an instrument reading that does not change when the theory changes, and keep verdicts separate rather than averaging them. Buy independence between messengers, because two analyses of one data set are one witness. Search the data you have, then sharpen instruments already returning a weak reading, then, if needed, borrow a laboratory analogue with its provenance shown. Report the interior as unreachable, which is a verdict and not a silence, and never turn a null result into a proof of absence. What the method offers is the shape any test must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close each slot. Nothing here claims to have measured the interior.
