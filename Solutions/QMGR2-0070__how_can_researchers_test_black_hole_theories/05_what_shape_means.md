## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 states that the system can report that it lacks something and *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the form that any test method must have, stated before the interior is named: exterior residuals per slot, an anchor that does not move with theory, explicit independence between messengers, an unbundled union, an instrument sharpened from something already registering, and an interior slot left open. The interior itself is not a shape we can fill. `Kerr`, `fuzzball`, `firewall`, `gravastar` are names from the physics frame, not shapes returned by this derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, watch for recurring terms that overlap with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a researcher would expect to see at a named slot before sending any theory.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :27: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*
- *Library of Babel* on a bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is exactly the union found in the census.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape: the ground settles a term, or a provenanced import arrives from a frame whose closure differs with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). An exterior fit names an exterior quantity when an instrument pays for it. It does not name an interior structure.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can yield the same reading, so *"behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The right response is *"a better instrument"*, not a name. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Applied here: a ringdown that fits both Kerr and an exotic object with the sameisospectral spectrum is a projection, not a discrimination. What is owed is a source-discriminating observable, not a choice of favourite interior.

## 4. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous referees who agree with a black-hole theory does not test it. Fame is a budget for a trial, never a verdict. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains public institutional venues and no personal addresses, and why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a failure criterion rather than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest status of this derivation

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The author adds: *"Metatheories cannot validate themselves."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures, not ground readings.** They cannot settle the question they report on. Every claim is labelled `general` or `specified`, never `grounded`. The verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` comes from the figures, and the world sources are secondary corroboration only. Fig 2 :40 warns that agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two.

## 6. Correction to my own reading

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding the naming of any specific observable. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the frame. Exterior observables such as ringdown frequencies, shadow diameter and orbital precession are already in the frame and can be named. What the rule forbids here is filling the interior slot with a name the ground has not paid for. Both readings are recorded so neither is silently lost.

**Falsifier for this section:** if a source-discriminating instrument returns a reading that varies with the interior theory while staying fixed under changes of exterior fitting convention, then the interior slot is no longer unreachable and the "shape, not a name" limit no longer applies to it. That would refute F2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, not merely refine it.
