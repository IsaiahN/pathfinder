# Expert verification packet: testing black-hole theories from outside

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement at a horizon is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Testing a black-hole theory without observing the interior" is not one hard question. It is a union of overlapping exterior conditions: horizon boundary, exterior multipoles, ringdown dynamics and thermodynamics. The method is: state the residual per observable slot and never aggregate it; range each theory separately against an instrument reading that does not move when the theory changes; keep cross-messenger independence explicit; unbundle the union before searching; run SEARCHED, then INWARD, then OUTWARD; import analogue laboratory mechanisms with provenance and a shadow test; record the round trip gap between prediction and reading; and keep the interior as an open slot, reported as unreachable rather than silence. A null result is never promoted into proof of absence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal and physics-facing: anchor per slot

- **Statement:** a theory is ranged against an instrument reading that does not change when the theory changes, and the residual is indexed per observable, not summed into one global score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** is the proposed quantity an instrument reading or a model-dependent reconstruction? Can two slots (say ringdown damping and shadow diameter) be varied independently, or are they one parameter in disguise?
- **Refuter:** the anchor changes its reported value when the theory is changed with instrument and data fixed, or the per-slot table collapses to one parameter. Then C1 fails.

### C2 - physics-facing: union and independence

- **Statement:** the conditions overlap, so unbundling is required before search; and cross-messenger agreement counts only when the messengers draw on different evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 2 :31 - *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
- **What to check:** do the gravitational-wave and electromagnetic analyses share a calibration, a prior, or a data product that fully determines both? If so they are one witness, not two. Are the conditions genuinely overlapping?
- **Refuter:** two probes declared independent share a common determinant so the triangulation collapses, or the conditions are shown separable so the union reading is false. Then C2 fails.

### C3 - framework-internal and instrument-facing: inward route and honest stop

- **Statement:** instruments are sharpened from ones already returning something; where nothing registers at any resolution, there is no edge to extend from, and the interior is reported as unreachable rather than filled. A null result proves nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 - *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34 - *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* Fig 8 :39 - *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** name one exterior probe that already returns a reading failing to resolve a theory. If none exists, there is no inward edge. Check whether a null result is being used as an exclusion proof.
- **Refuter:** a probe returns nothing at any resolution yet is used as a constraint, or a direct interior reading varies with interior theory. Then C3 fails at that point.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named alternative to Kerr is correct. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that Hawking radiation from an astrophysical black hole has been observed. The analogue results are lab mechanisms in a different closure. Shi et al. (2023) state it is *"quite difficult to directly observe the Hawking radiation of an astrophysical black hole"*; Thebault (2019) contests the evidential reach of analogues.
- Not that a shadow or a ringdown reads the interior. Both are exterior observables.
- Not that a null result closes a question.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the ranking in this simulated derivation.
- Not a request for agreement, trust, or endorsement. Prestige can fund a trial and cannot settle one.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **SEARCHED:** independent searchers sweep the existing exterior data with distinct pipelines; note which residuals persist and do not trend down.
2. **INWARD:** sharpen an instrument already returning a weak reading, and range the theory against the improved reading.
3. **OUTWARD:** import an analogue lab mechanism with a source gate and a shadow test, recording which model built the analogue.
4. **Ground:** a source-discriminating exterior observable that separates theories degenerate on a current reading, or a direct interior reading if one ever arrives. The source essay's spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the green-reading ambiguity.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :55, Fig 2 :31, Fig 6 :32 and :34, Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39. Eight short passages.
2. Check C1 against one real observable: is its anchor an instrument reading or a model reconstruction?
3. Check C2 against one real pair of messengers: do they share a calibration or prior?
4. Name one instrument that already returns a reading failing to resolve a theory, or say why none exists.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
