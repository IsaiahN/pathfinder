## Question as an effect

Two options are being decided. Option A: try to test a black-hole theory by obtaining a reading of the interior. Option B: test it by ranging its exterior predictions against measurements that do not require any reading from inside the horizon. A leads to a demand for a signal that, by the theory's own definition, never arrives, so it is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22). B leads to a shaped, checkable method: describe the residual per observable slot, range each theory's exterior predictions against an instrument that does not move when the theory changes, and keep the interior marked as unreachable rather than silently filled. What an answer would look like is bound in advance as an ordinary term: a ranking of theories by exterior residuals, with the interior left as an open slot and a written refuter.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with a theory.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, from the figures: Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 says *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 10 :42 says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and sending them the full framework before asking for a verdict spends the independence they contribute. Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid the structural failure and the legal one.
