## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade a label.** *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283). For corruption research, a pile of agreeable readers is one evidence pool, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. It is especially dangerous here, because a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 2 :43).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided: whether a deviation from official rules stays a one-off event in one office slot, or becomes the ordinary way that office allocates goods and settles disputes. Option A, isolated misconduct, leaves the written rule as the anchor and the deviation as a live residual that gets corrected. Option B, corruption as governance, replaces the rule with an informal convention that decides the outcome, moves the anchor so it updates on the frames, loses the monitoring channel, and copies a working method across offices. The residual disappears not because conduct improved but because the reference moved. That disappearance is the phenomenon to explain.
