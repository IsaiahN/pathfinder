# Expert verification packet: the Tether on corruption as governance

**What this is:** a claim in a form a corruption or governance researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the institutional application, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement of a real institution is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Corruption stops being isolated misconduct and becomes a system of governance when five conditions hold as a union: an informal convention crosses from accompanying the written rule into deciding outcomes; the monitor that should settle compliance updates on the office it monitors, so the drift is coherent and invisible; the channel to the ground loses discrimination while internal indicators stay green; a reapplicable method crosses from one office to others instead of remaining a single recorded act; and the office has no instrument for what would count as progress for a citizen. A rule-bound office and a convention-bound office are isomers over the same atom set, so counting rules, audits and codes of conduct cannot tell them apart. The framework returns this shape, not the name of the norm or the office. Seven falsifiers were written before the run.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention. This is the pivot of the question, not a side observation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :43, *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 10 :47, *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. Fig 10 :49, *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
- **What to check:** are these exact strings in the verbatim figure extract, and do they govern the institutional case without strain? The local cross-check is Seat Map :105, *"If a convention it authored decides an outcome, it has stopped being a convention and started being a verdict, and nothing flags the transition."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit an informal regularity that decides binding allocations while remaining a convention with no verdict-bearing force, with the written rule still settling disputes. If it exists, C1 fails.

### C2. Institutional, checkable by a governance researcher against known cases

- **Statement:** capture is an anchor-update failure (Fig 2 :34) and a monitoring failure is a channel failure (Fig 10 :24-28), not a change in the underlying world. The two are distinct conditions of the union.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :34, *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26, *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :28, *"you have lost touch"*.
- **What to check:** does a captured regulator in a known case fit panel 1 rather than panel 3? Does a green internal dashboard with a dead per-slot reading fit "lost touch" rather than "improved conduct"? The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are secondary and may be used to argue against this mapping, but the figures decide.
- **Refuter:** exhibit an oversight body whose reference moves under pressure yet whose corrections remain sound and discriminating against an independent ground; or a channel demonstrably saturated and pooled that still returns ground-verified per-slot readings. Either breaks C2.

### C3. Structural, checkable by reading Figures 9, 4 and 12

- **Statement:** the five conditions form a union, so a single lever cannot clear the intersections, and the two office types are isomers over the same atoms.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :56, *"leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 12 :117, *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
- **What to check:** does a single reform in a known case clear both the convention decision and the captured anchor with no outside witness? If yes, the union reading is wrong. Does an ingredient-counting accountability instrument distinguish the two office types? If yes, the isomer reading is wrong.
- **Refuter:** a single intervention applied inside the original frame that clears the union with the second condition still binding beforehand; or an office where the same atoms under the same bonds produce both types in the same period with no difference in monitor reference.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a diagnosis of any named country, ministry, official or practice.
- Not that a corruption perception index measures actual occurrence. It is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that "everyone is corrupt"; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not a single lever, not a big-bang necessity. Stephenson's result is cited as corroboration that a stable equilibrium is stable only against random perturbation, not against a change of parameters.
- Not a new primitive or a sixth condition. The transition is a re-bonding of held atoms (Fig 12 :94, *"The arrangement is the substance."*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing is asserted as settled by experiment or audit.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Can an instrument tell a compliant contract from a reciprocal one, so the convention-to-verdict crossing is observable?
2. Can a ruling from a frame that does not share the office's evidence show whether the monitor's reference moves?
3. Can a per-slot audit with a known-positive fixture distinguish a dead channel from improved conduct?
4. Can the lineage of a practice show whether a reusable method crossed, or whether each site re-derived the act?
5. Can an instrument at the vocabulary-to-objective link show that the comparison for citizen progress exists and is read?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47 (the convention guard), Fig 2 :34 and Fig 10 :24-28 (capture and channel), Fig 9 :55-56 and Fig 12 :117 (union and isomer). Six short passages.
