## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question, a shape is the set of conditions an institution must satisfy before we are entitled to call its corruption a system of governance rather than a run of isolated acts. It constrains: it says what must be true (a convention that decides outcomes, a moved anchor, a dead channel, a copied method, an instrumentless objective). It does not fill: it does not say which ministry, which official, which fee, or which date.

- Isolated misconduct: `this act deviated from the written rule and was corrected`.
- Shape of the transition: `the deviation stopped registering because the reference moved`.
- Name: `the permits office in ministry X takes a fixed fee since 2019`. The framework cannot produce that name. A ground reading must.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing and look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recursively. *"Your ignorance, of not knowing what you don't know, is the actual obstacle between you and the answers, not the vastness of the library."*
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* *"rather than the cause."* So the residual is described as an effect: the disappearance of the per-slot error signal, not as a moral category.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question: expect an informal rule to decide allocations while the written rule is still quoted, expect oversight findings to match the overseen office's own account, expect a practice to appear in offices that never exchanged a formal instruction.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."* Here, the shape stops when the five conditions are stated and each has a refuter. It does not stop at a named culprit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For corruption, that means a source-discriminating instrument: a procurement audit that can tell a compliant contract from a reciprocal one, a court that can refuse the office's own account, a whistleblower protection that lets a reading arrive.
2. **A provenanced import from a differing closure** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A foreign anti-corruption agency's method can cross in, but only with provenance and only if it explains a gap the office could not see.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens the limit: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming a specific norm as *the* cause of corruption in an office turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the score is not the verdict

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What resolved the case was the spectrometer, not an argument: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A national corruption perception index is exactly such a projection. Several institutional arrangements (a genuinely honest bureaucracy, a captured one with clean statistics, a set of offices with live per-slot residuals nobody reads) can produce the same number. The number cannot comment on the source. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates, not a better score. This corroborates F1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` but does not decide it.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive or atom not held in `Γ`. For this question, the missing thing is the outside witness that can settle whether a convention has crossed into deciding outcomes. The framework can characterize that witness in effect terms and cannot name the actual institution that would supply it.

What is **not** forbidden: describing the five conditions in ordinary institutional vocabulary. They are not new primitives; they are bindings of the figures' existing quantities (convention, verdict, anchor, channel, method, comparison) onto an institutional domain. What remains forbidden is inventing a sixth condition to make the story neater, or naming a specific office as the answer.

Both readings are kept: the narrowed one governs here, the overbroad one is retracted.

## 6. Prestige, agreement, and the limits of this document

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous anti-corruption names to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The Arrival of the Fittest post adds: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any institution and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What this means for the answer

The Tether's answer to INS-0031 is a shape plus a stop:

- The shape: five conditions in effect terms, stated as a union, each with a refuter.
- The stop: which condition holds in a given office is `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument and an outside frame are brought in.
- The lineage: the census, the quotes, the strains, the refuters, and the round-trip gap are recorded so a later reader can locate an error rather than only feel it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states the governing limit for a shape that stops at indiscernibility: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A stable set of conditions that fits every known case is still not a measurement of any case.
