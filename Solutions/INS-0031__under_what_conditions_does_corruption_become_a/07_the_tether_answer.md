# The Tether's answer: when does corruption become governance?

**Mode: `specified` on the institutional application, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading of any real institution.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the conditions under which a deviation from official rules stops being an isolated event and becomes the ordinary way an office allocates goods and settles disputes. Stated as an effect: when does the per-slot residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` stop registering, so that a system-wide near-zero becomes a legal-looking state while live deviations remain? Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. The disappearance of the error signal is the phenomenon, not the persistence of the act.

## 2. The shape

The conditions form a **union**, not a list. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever clears the region where only one holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56).

1. **A convention has crossed into deciding outcomes.** Fig 2 :43: *"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. The informal practice no longer accompanies the written rule; it replaces the rule as the reference for who gets what. The rule survives as a declared meaning (Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*).
2. **The anchor updates on the frames.** Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* The monitor that should settle whether the rule was followed takes its reference from the office it monitors. The drift is coherent, so insiders cannot see it.
3. **The channel to the ground has lost contact.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. The system's green indicators report *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :28), not that conduct improved.
4. **A method has crossed the membrane.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* One bribe is a recording of an occasion (Fig 4 :25); a governance system requires a reapplicable method, verified and copied across offices. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.
5. **The vocabulary-to-objective link has no instrument.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* "The permit was issued" counts as progress regardless of who got it, because the comparison that would say otherwise is not stateable in the office's vocabulary.

Isolated misconduct stays isolated when any one of these fails. The deviation then still registers as `R`, and a refusal is still available (Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"*).

The honest and the captured office are **isomers** over the same atoms (Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*). That is why counting rules, audits and codes of conduct cannot distinguish them.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named country, ministry, or office is in this state. No ground reading was taken.
- Not that a corruption perception index measures actual occurrence. It is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not that "everyone is corrupt". Actor valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*).
- Not that one reform clears the union. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
- Not which specific norm crosses, or at what threshold. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment or audit.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the informal practice actually decide allocations, or only accompany them? An instrument that can tell a compliant contract from a reciprocal one would name the crossing.
- Does the oversight body's reference move when the office pushes on it? A ruling from a frame that does not share the office's evidence would answer it.
- Is the monitoring channel still discriminating per slot? An audit with per-office readings and a known-positive fixture would tell contact from saturation.
- Did a reusable method cross, with head starts, or did each site re-derive the act independently? The lineage of the practice answers it.
- Does the office hold a comparison for what counts as progress for a citizen? An instrument at the vocabulary-to-objective link would answer it.

Standing falsifiers are the seven in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5. If one fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the shape is revised from the census.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question "which norm, in which office, at what threshold?" is not answered here. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every case we know is still not a measurement of any case.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a corrupt country or a bad official, and it does not offer a single reform. It says that corruption becomes a system of governance at a specific transition: an informal convention stops accompanying the written rule and starts deciding who gets what, the monitor that should settle the question starts taking its reference from the office it watches, the channel to the ground goes quiet while every indicator reads green, a working method is copied from office to office instead of staying a one-off act, and the office has no instrument for what would count as progress for a citizen. Rule-bound and convention-bound offices are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, which is why counting rules and audits cannot tell them apart. What the framework offers is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close it. Which norm crossed, where, and when stays open until a ground with a different evidence pool settles it.
