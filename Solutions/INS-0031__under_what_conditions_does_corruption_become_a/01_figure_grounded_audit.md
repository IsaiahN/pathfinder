## 0. The residual, in effect terms

Let `Γ` be the library an office actually uses: the written rules, the informal understandings, and the examples of what got done before. Let `b` be the official's belief about what the office requires, `a` the action taken, and `o′` the observed allocation or decision. The residual is `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, and it is indexed per office slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19).

Two shapes of `R` must be distinguished:

- **Isolated misconduct.** `R` spikes in one slot, is detected, and is corrected. The written rule remains the anchor. `o′` is read against the rule, the deviation registers, and a refusal or sanction follows.
- **Corruption as governance.** `R` falls toward zero system-wide even though the written rule has not changed. The deviation no longer registers. This is not improved compliance. It is a change in what counts as the expected outcome: an informal convention now decides who gets what, the anchor has been made to update on the frames, the monitoring channel no longer discriminates, and a working method has crossed to other offices.

So the thing to explain is the **disappearance of the error signal**, not the persistence of the deviation. Fig 1 :13 warns that *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*; the inverse is the trap: a global near-zero that is really many live slots whose signals were never read. Fig 10 :26 names the mechanism: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*, and :27-28 gives the correct output: *"the output is not a verdict, it is / you have lost touch."*

An answer would look like this, bound in advance as an ordinary term (Formula, "Direction"): a **union of conditions** such that (i) a convention decides an outcome, (ii) the anchor updates on the frames, (iii) the channel to the ground loses contact, (iv) a method crosses the membrane and is reapplied, and (v) the vocabulary-to-objective link has no instrument. We bind that shape now so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: at minimum triadic (a discretion holder, a beneficiary, and a rule contravened); in the stable form it is polyadic, a network of offices, brokers, and clients. It is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :11, :19). Symmetry: the exchange presents as symmetric (favour for payment) but the authority relation is asymmetric, one party holds the office. The operative bonds are `→` sequence, `⇒` production, `⋛` comparison, and the `cycle` structure (Fig 12 :121), not the symmetric `+` or `≡`. Scale: one decision, one office, the polity, with a lossy membrane between office and system (Fig 4).

One line per domain, with an anchor that can actually be named:

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 The official's decision loop | office-holder, belief b, library Γ, action a | per-slot residual R, description length, cost of a term | the written rule and the observed allocation | admitted |
| D2 Office habitat / patronage network | patrons, clients, brokers, gatekeepers, rotating officials | benefit flows, obligations, survival of practices, detection probability | actual rewards and sanctions, what the ground keeps | admitted |
| D3 Rule and convention layer | formal rules, informal norms, the seat that authors conventions | convention versus verdict, what can be violated | the ground, and whether the convention decides an outcome | admitted, pivot domain |

Not domains yet:

- **X1 "a corrupt culture" as a free-floating property.** No nameable anchor. It is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* (Fig 11 :36). Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "bad individuals" as sole cause.** Absorbed into D1. Actor valence is relative to the residual: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* (Fig 11 :49). No standing list of bad actors survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).

**Partition or union.** This is a **union**, not a partition. Corruption as governance requires overlapping conditions to hold at once. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* :56: *"leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever, more law, more audits, clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (can the office tell a deviation from compliance?) then vocabulary (terms for what the office affords: discretion, favour, obligation) then objective (what counts as progress: following procedure or completing the deal?) then planning then learn and carry (does the successful deviation get named, verified, copied?). The chain is ordered by dependency, not importance: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* (Fig 3 :20). The break is expected at vocabulary to objective, because that link has neither obvious failures nor obvious tests (Fig 3 :24-27).

## 3. Findings

### F1. The residual is per slot; an aggregate score hides it (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

*Department of the Figures*

Reading. A country-level corruption score can be stable while a particular permits office, procurement unit, or court remains live. `Mode: general` for the indexing law, `specified` for the institutional application. The national index is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict: Fig 9 :60 **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The UNODC teaching guide confirms the measurement distinction at ground level: indirect methods *"do not gauge the actual occurrence of corruption, but are rather based on perceived levels of corruption."* The CPI methodology describes an aggregate of *"perceptions among businesspeople and country experts."* Both are corroboration only.

**Refuter.** Show a case where every office slot has `R` genuinely near zero against a written rule, while the aggregate index is high and no pooling across slots or sources occurred. That breaks the indexing law.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 1 indexing, Fig 9 filter/witness split, distinct quantities).

### F2. The pivot: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (triangulated, decisive)

Fig 2 :43: **"Guard, from Figure 10: a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."**
Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**
Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
Fig 10 :49: **"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."**

Reading. This is the exact transition named by the question. An informal practice (a fixed fee, a fixed recipient list, a rotating favour) starts as a convention alongside the formal rule. The moment it decides who receives the public good, it is no longer a convention: it has become a verdict generator, and nothing flags the transition internally. The written rule survives as a declared meaning. `Mode: specified`.

The seat map states the same law from the checking arrangement: Seat Map :105, *"If a convention it authored decides an outcome, it has stopped being a convention and started being a verdict, and nothing flags the transition."* Seat Map :167: *"A convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority. Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Barbalet (2022/2023), reading Helmke and Levitsky (2004), defines informal institutions as *"socially shared rules, usually unwritten, that are created, communicated, and enforced outside of officially sanctioned channels"* and notes that violation *"generates some kind of external sanction."* That is the convention layer described in effect terms by a low-overlap field.

**Refuter.** Exhibit an informal regularity that decides binding allocations in an office while remaining a convention with no verdict-bearing force, and where the written rule still settles disputes. If that exists, F2 is false.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 alignment guard, Fig 10 maintainer failure, Seat Map office law; low overlap between their domains).

### F3. Capture is the anchor update, and the drift is coherent (triangulated)

Fig 2 :13: **"no anchor; each updates on the others"**
Fig 2 :15: **"is whoever had the most influence"**
Fig 2 :34: **"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**
Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."**
Fig 10 :93: **"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."**

Reading. A captured regulator, a prosecutorial office dependent on the executive it investigates, or an internal audit whose findings are cleared by the unit audited, has become panel 1. Agreement inside it converges on whoever had the most influence, and because the convergence is coherent, nobody notices. This is why "the anti-corruption agency is itself corrupt" is not a scandal in the system's own reading; it is the system reading itself. `Mode: specified`.

World corroboration, all secondary: Robert Michels (*Political Parties*, 1911/1966) observed that *"One who has for a certain time held the office of delegate ends by regarding that office as his own property"* and that *"The leader's principal source of power is found in his indispensability."* Douglass North (Nobel Lecture, 1993): *"if the institutional framework rewards piracy then piratical organizations will come into existence."* Acemoglu and Robinson (via MIT News, 2012): *"Most consequential 'policy mistakes' are by design,"* because leaders' *"objective is different: to hold onto power or simply enrich themselves."*

**Refuter.** Show an oversight body whose reference moves when the overseen frame pushes on it, yet whose corrections remain sound and discriminating against the ground. That contradicts Fig 2 :33-34.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 collapse 1, Fig 10 disqualification, Seat Map shelf life :233).

### F4. Monitoring failure is channel failure, not a changed world (triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :25: **"stale percepts, dropped transients,"**
Fig 10 :26: **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"**
Fig 10 :27: **"the output is not a verdict, it is"**
Fig 10 :28: **"you have lost touch"**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Reading. When every internal indicator is green and the corruption keepers all read the same source, the system has not learned that conduct improved. It has lost the reading. The remedy is to restore contact with an anchor that does not move on their say-so: an independent audit, a court, a free press, a citizen report, a cross-frame comparison. Seat Map :285: *"Every instrument points inward unless one is built to point out."* `Mode: general` for the channel law, `specified` for oversight.

**Refuter.** Show a channel that is demonstrably saturated and pooled and yet still returns discriminating, ground-verified readings of live slots.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 10 channel law, Fig 2 shared pool, Seat Map inward-instrument law).

### F5. An isolated act becomes a system when a method crosses the membrane (triangulated)

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
Fig 4 :23: **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**
Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**
Fig 7 :12: **"same closure, handed along;"**
Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Reading. One bribe is a recording of an occasion. Corruption becomes governance when the *method* (how to get a permit released, how to route a contract, how to reward a loyal clerk) is verified as working in one office and reapplied in others, with head starts sent down to newcomers. A chain of offices that hands along the same closure is one long room: motion without progress, but with perfect continuity of practice. `Mode: specified`.

World corroboration from a genuinely low-overlap field: Robert Axelrod's *The Evolution of Cooperation* asks, in the exact wording quoted by Barker's 2021 encyclopedia entry, *"Under what conditions will cooperation emerge in a world of egoists without central authority?"* Axelrod's answer, that reciprocity plus a shadow of the future can make a practice self-policing, corroborates the mechanism shape: the practice need not be endorsed by the formal system to be stable. Klitgaard's IMF article frames the same object as a system: *"Such systematic corruption makes establishing and maintaining internationally acceptable rules of the game impossible."*

**Refuter.** Show a corrupt practice spreading across offices with no reusable method, only recordings of one occasion, with each site re-deriving it independently and no head start passed down. If it still persists at the same rate, F5 is false.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 4 membrane, Fig 7 room chain, Axelrod on reciprocity from evolutionary game theory).

### F6. It is a union, and more search inside the frame cannot close it (triangulated)

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
Fig 9 :56: **"leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**
Fig 9 :57: **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."**
Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Reading. Adding laws, controls, and training inside the same office frame searches a closure that already lacks the needed atom: an independent witness. The atom must be imported from a frame whose closure differs. Fig 8 :53 **"Residual first, frame second."** and Fig 9 :27 **"describe it before you go looking, or"** :28 **"any frame you pick will seem to fit."** So the residual is characterized in effect terms first, then a frame is sought. Klitgaard's formula, *"Corruption equals monopoly plus discretion minus accountability"* (IMF, 1998), is a useful filter for where to look. It cannot be the verdict: Stephenson (2019/2020) shows that corruption's self-reinforcement *"does not logically entail the necessity of a big bang approach to reform"* and that a stable equilibrium is stable only against *"a random perturbation,"* not against a change of parameters. That is the frames-versus-ground distinction in the policy literature, corroboration only.

**Refuter.** Show one lever clearing the union: the convention stops deciding outcomes, the anchor is restored, and the method stops crossing, all from a single intervention applied inside the original frame with no import and no second condition binding.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 9 union, Fig 8 idempotence, Fig 6 import).

### F7. The break is at vocabulary to objective (triangulated)

Fig 3 :24: **"Why the middle link is where chains usually break."**
Fig 3 :26: **"being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."**
Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**
Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

Reading. An accountability system can perceive deviations and can plan sanctions. What it often lacks is a vocabulary and an instrument for what the office is for: a comparison that says what would count as progress for a citizen rather than for a broker. Without that, "the permit was issued" counts as progress regardless of who got it or why. Fig 12 :94 **"The arrangement is the substance."** A vocabulary that names only inputs (forms processed, files closed, bribes reported) can name a state and cannot name a route to one (Fig 12 :111). `Mode: specified`.

**Refuter.** Show an office where the objective link has a working, ground-checked instrument and still fails at the same rate as an office without one, with the instrument demonstrably read rather than written and uncalled.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 3 middle link, Fig 12 goal-as-comparison, seat map :184 on a vocabulary of ingredients).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Seat Map :287, *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. An informal regularity is exhibited that decides binding allocations while remaining a convention with no verdict-bearing force, and the written rule still settles disputes (breaks F2).
2. An oversight body is exhibited whose reference moves under pressure from the overseen frame, yet whose corrections remain sound and discriminating against the ground (breaks Fig 2 :33-34 and F3).
3. A monitoring channel is exhibited that is demonstrably saturated and pooled and still returns discriminating, ground-verified readings of live slots (breaks F4).
4. A corrupt practice is exhibited that spreads across offices with no reusable method crossing the membrane, each site re-deriving it, and persists at the same rate (breaks F5).
5. A single intervention applied inside the original frame is exhibited that clears the union with no import and with the second condition still binding before the intervention (breaks F6).
6. A ground reading is exhibited with per-slot residuals near zero in every office while the aggregate corruption index is high and no pooling occurred (breaks F1).
7. An office is exhibited with a working, ground-checked objective instrument that fails at the same rate as one without it (breaks F7).

If any fires, the affected finding is withdrawn and the shape is revised from the census. It is not rescued with a softer reading.

## 8. Updated verdict

The shape survives. Corruption becomes a stable system of governance at the intersection of five conditions: a convention that has crossed into deciding outcomes; an anchor that updates on the frames; a monitoring channel that has lost discrimination; a method that has crossed the membrane and is reapplied; and a vocabulary-to-objective link with no instrument. Isolated misconduct stays isolated when any one of these fails, because the deviation then still registers as `R` and a refusal is still available.

What is refused: a single-cause answer, a national-score answer, a named-norm answer, and any claim that this package has measured a real institution. The mode is `specified` on the institutional application and `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`.
