## Wider literature that bears on this question, grouped by pool

**Pool A, public economics and institutional analysis (one witness, not four).** Klitgaard's M + D - A formula, North's rules of the game, Shleifer and Vishny's weak-government result, and Acemoglu and Robinson's extractive institutions all describe corruption as a system property. They share a broad institutional-economics neighborhood, so their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40). Klitgaard's own formula is explicitly a heuristic: the World Bank *Corrupt Cities* text calls it *"a mathematical metaphor... heuristic only"* (seen in search extraction, not separately fetched). Stephenson's critique is the useful internal dissenter: a stable equilibrium is stable against random perturbation, not against a change of parameters. That is the figure distinction between a frame's internal agreement and the ground's verdict, corroborated but not decided by the literature.

**Pool B, political sociology and informal institutions (a second witness, lower overlap).** Michels on the technical indispensability of leadership and the office as property; Barbalet on Helmke and Levitsky's informal institutions and the external sanctions that enforce them; Axelrod on reciprocity without central authority. These fields do not draw on the public-economics pool, so their agreement with the shape carries more independent weight, but still none for the verdict.

**Pool C, measurement (a constraint on method, not a witness for the verdict).** UNODC and Transparency International both state that perception indices do not measure actual occurrence. This corroborates Fig 1 :19 and Fig 9 :60: the index is a budget filter, not a verdict.

The essays are all by the framework's author. They are used for the canonical reading of "shape" only, and carry no independent corroboration weight.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25.** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full architecture before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the claim and the refuter.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure; (3) record that answer; (4) only if they are still curious, offer the three links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

The proposition is: **(1)** check the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula on a problem the user can check themselves.

- **Stage 1 before Stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source,"* and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. The Stage 1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled.
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which Fig 4 :22 permits. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes a refuter first and states a mode.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if Stage 1 survives. The shape in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading, and the F9 gap is declared. Do not present it as a settled diagnosis of any real institution.
