## QUESTION

Under what conditions does corruption become a stable system of governance rather than isolated misconduct? Restated as an effect: when does the per-slot residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` stop registering, so that a system-wide near-zero becomes a legal-looking state while live deviations remain? Option A: treat it as one hard problem and search for a single cause and a single reform. Option B: census the domains, keep the union of conditions separate, and stop at the shape. What each leads to: A applies one lever to a compound and mistakes a budget filter for a verdict; B gives a checkable union, seven pre-registered refuters, and a declared gap where a ground instrument is owed.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at minimum triadic, a discretion holder, a beneficiary, a contravened rule; in the stable form polyadic, a network of offices, brokers and clients. Indexed per office slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the exchange presents as symmetric but the authority is asymmetric; the operative bonds are `→`, `⇒`, `⋛` and the cycle, not `+` or `≡`. Scale: one decision, one office, the polity, with a lossy membrane (Fig 4).
- **D1 The official's decision loop.** Actors: office-holder, belief b, library Γ, action a. Currency: per-slot residual, description length, cost of a term. Anchor: the written rule and the observed allocation. Admitted.
- **D2 Office habitat / patronage network.** Actors: patrons, clients, brokers, gatekeepers, rotating officials. Currency: benefit flows, obligations, survival of practices, detection probability. Anchor: actual rewards and sanctions, what the ground keeps. Admitted.
- **D3 Rule and convention layer (pivot).** Actors: formal rules, informal norms, the seat that authors conventions. Currency: convention versus verdict, what can be violated. Anchor: the ground, and whether the convention decides an outcome. Admitted.
- **D4 Monitoring and accountability.** Actors: auditors, courts, press, electorate, inspectors. Currency: agreement versus ground contact, independence. Anchor: the ground, which must not update on frames. Admitted.
- **D5 Cross-scale membrane.** Actors: many officials, a shared library, something that ranks what they produce. Currency: methods crossing up, head starts down, round-trip gap `R_T`. Anchor: the measured round trip. Admitted.
- **D6 The ground itself.** No actors, not a frame, no currency. Anchor: outcomes that settle, a service delivered, a contract awarded, a ruling made. Anchor, not a domain.
- **X1 "A corrupt culture" as free-floating property.** No nameable anchor; a label applied after the fact (Fig 11 :36). Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "Bad individuals" as sole cause.** Absorbed into D1; valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Not a separate domain.

**Union, not partition.** The conditions overlap; a case in two conditions is categorically different from a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). **Order by dependency:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3); the vocabulary-to-objective link is the likely break; seat failures act from above rather than as links.

Strain test applied: Fig 2 :41 (*"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*) was initially read as straining against Fig 10 :47 (*"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*). Re-census resolves it: :41 governs meaning-making, :47 governs outcome-deciding. Different quantities, no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Corruption becomes a stable system of governance at the intersection of five conditions: a convention that has crossed into deciding outcomes; an anchor that updates on the frames; a monitoring channel that has lost discrimination; a method that has crossed the membrane and is reapplied; and a vocabulary-to-objective link with no instrument. Isolated misconduct stays isolated when any one fails, because the deviation still registers and a refusal remains available. A rule-bound office and a convention-bound office are isomers over the same atoms, which is why ingredient-counting accountability cannot tell them apart. The specific norm, office and threshold are a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`/`general`, ungrounded, candidate. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A union treated as one lever sends more audits to an office whose real failure is a captured anchor, or more law to one whose channel is dead. The second-order cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by showing them a recording instead of a generator (Fig 4 :25, Fig 10 :42). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A refutation adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). No primitive is withdrawn and no institution is named, so a correction can be recorded in the provenance trail without retracting a diagnosis of a real place (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, in particular (1) an informal regularity that decides allocations while remaining a convention, (2) a captured-reference monitor whose corrections stay sound, (3) a saturated channel that still discriminates, (4) a practice that spreads with no method crossing, (5) one in-frame lever clearing the union, (6) a ground reading with all slots near zero and a high aggregate with no pooling, or (7) a working objective instrument that makes no difference. A world source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing these conditions do not co-vary would also send the derivation back to the census. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
