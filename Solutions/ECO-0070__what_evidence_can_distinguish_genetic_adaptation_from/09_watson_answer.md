**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

What evidence can distinguish genetic adaptation from behavioral flexibility or movement to a new habitat?

Restated as an effect: what would discriminate the source of a low residual when heritable change, within-lifetime re-composition, and habitat substitution can produce the same reading of persistence? R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per population-slot and per generation, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: a fitness or survival assay under controlled conditions, a class explicitly named in Fig 2 :29.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a relation among heritable library Γ, controller state b, action a, and environmental return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by generation g and time t; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "adapted" is a threshold relative to a local environment (Fig 12 :87), and the fitness assay does not update on the organism's opinion (Fig 2 :25). Scale: the within-lifetime controller, the population across generations, and the habitat field of contact; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

Union, not partition: D1/D3, D2/D3 and D1/D2 form three overlapping compounds; the whole is a union of C1 (heritable change), C2 (controller change) and C3 (habitat substitution) (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test applied: Fig 13's six physical terms do not govern biological response classes (re-census S1); the habitat updates while the ground settles (re-census S2); the source of heritable atoms is not the filter that settles them (re-census S4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** persistence is a reading with several possible sources, so evidence must change one state variable and hold the other two fixed. Heritable-state change paid by fitness distinguishes genetic adaptation; a reversible reaction norm or expression change under a held habitat distinguishes behavioral flexibility; a substituted field of contact with genomic and controller continuity distinguishes movement. The three are a union with overlapping compounds, so verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged; the anchor is a fitness assay, and which allele, behavior, or habitat acted in a given case is a declared `gap`. Mode `specified`. World sources corroborate the instrument shape from low-overlap fields and did not flip the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected conservation and management. A population failing C2 would be given genomic surveys, or a population failing C3 would be told to increase plasticity, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs three. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible; buying the wrong instrument is a budget error that Fig 9 :60 warns against, not a settled falsehood.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, its refuters and the strains are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a persistence reading that uniquely determines source, (2) genetic adaptation with no heritable state change in a common garden, (3) a plastic response with no controller difference under held genotype and environment, (4) a habitat substitution with no change in the enumerated actor list, (5) one lever that separates all three sources in every overlap, or (6) an averaged statistic that prospectively identifies source per slot and survives transplant. A figure re-census that admits a fourth state variable governing persistence would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
