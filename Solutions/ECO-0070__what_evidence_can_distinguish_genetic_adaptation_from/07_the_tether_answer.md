# The Tether's answer on distinguishing adaptation from flexibility or movement

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No common garden, transplant, or sequence time series in this folder pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would say what evidence could tell whether a population's persistence after environmental change came from a heritable change, a within-lifetime adjustment, or a move to a different habitat. R is indexed per population-slot and per generation, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about the **source of a reading**, not about the reading itself.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is three conditions, not one hard question with three rival answers:

- **C1:** did the heritable library change across generations, with the change paid by fitness?
- **C2:** did the within-lifetime controller change while genotype and habitat were held?
- **C3:** did the field of contact change while genotype and controller were held?

## 2. The shape

1. **Persistence is a reading with several possible sources.** Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."* Calling a persisting population "adapted" from the reading alone names a source that has not been measured.
2. **The anchor is a fitness assay, not agreement about mechanism.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Common gardens, reciprocal transplants, mark-recapture and evolve-and-resequence series are candidate anchors; a committee vote about what "really" happened is not.
3. **Genetic adaptation is the only one of the three that adds heritable atoms.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Mutation, recombination and introgression supply the heritable atoms; selection narrows what is already there. The discriminating evidence is heritable-state change across generations, paid by fitness (Schlötterer et al. 2015 on E&R; Bernatchez et al. 2024 on temporal genomics).
4. **Behavioral flexibility is the same atoms under a different bond, in the same habitat.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* The discriminating evidence is a reaction norm or reversible expression/behavior change under controlled common conditions (Sommer 2020; de Villemereuil et al. 2016; Rittschof and Robinson 2014).
5. **Movement substitutes a habitat; it does not remove the organism's library.** Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat’s actors, which you did not choose."* The discriminating evidence is a changed field of contact, enumerated rather than composed (Fig 11 :43), with genomic and controller continuity. Luqman et al. 2023 show why both must be measured: range shifts can also sieve alleles.
6. **The three conditions overlap, so the verdicts stay separate.** Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Donelson et al. 2019 say the mechanisms *"are not mutually exclusive"*; Botero 2026 shows behavioural buffering can slow short-term morphological evolution while increasing standing variation; Ho and Zhang 2018 show genetic change often reverses plastic change. A single lever cannot clear the union (Fig 9 :55).
7. **The break is at the middle link.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Persistence is easy to observe; source discrimination is not. Put the budget into the vocabulary and objective links, where the failure conditions live, not into more persistence measurements (Fig 3 :29).
8. **No species-level average can answer this.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A global range-shift rate or a pooled FST is a filter for where to look.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No local fitness measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a name. It does not say which allele, which behavior, or which habitat produced any particular persistence. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an either/or list. The mechanisms overlap, and compounds of them have their own loops.
- Not that plasticity always leads adaptation or always slows it. Ho and Zhang 2018 and Botero 2026 report opposite-looking effects at different strata; the shape does not choose between them, it asks what was measured.
- Not that the habitat is the ground. Fig 11 :73: *"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."*
- Not corroboration by agreement. The low-overlap ecology reviews corroborate the instrument shape, not the framework's authority, and the framework essays share one evidence pool with the figures.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument paid by fitness, not a further argument. The source essay says a reading *"cannot comment on the source"* and that *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are persistence, trait change, and range occupancy; the candidate sources are heritable change, controller re-composition, and habitat substitution. The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. persistence uniquely determines source with no controlled comparison;
2. genetic adaptation with no heritable state change across generations under a common garden;
3. plastic response with the same genotype, environment and history but no controller difference;
4. habitat substitution with no change in the enumerated actor and condition list;
5. one lever that separates all three sources in every overlap with no categorically different residue;
6. an averaged species-level statistic that prospectively identifies the source per slot and survives transplant;
7. failure of the framework's own closure rule inside a closed heritable set.

A prospective study that measures C1, C2 and C3 separately per population, records bonds as well as atoms, enumerates the habitat, and reports which condition failed before the response would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops where the available instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, and it is partly held: common gardens, E&R, and occupancy records are proto-instruments already returning readings that fail to resolve all three at once. Naming a source from a single reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The three conditions in section 2 are a shape; the fourth thing, which source actually acted here, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which allele, which behavior, or which habitat caused a population to persist. It says that persistence is a reading with several possible sources, and that a reading cannot tell you its source. To find the source you have to change one of three things and hold the other two fixed: the heritable library, the within-lifetime controller, or the field of contact. Heritable change needs allele-frequency evidence paid by fitness; flexibility needs a reaction norm or reversible expression change with genotype and habitat held; movement needs a substituted habitat with genomic and controller continuity. These are three failure modes with three instruments, not three degrees of one response, and they overlap, so the verdicts stay separate. The anchor is a common-garden or transplant fitness count, not agreement among researchers. What is offered is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which source actually acted stays open until the ground settles it, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
