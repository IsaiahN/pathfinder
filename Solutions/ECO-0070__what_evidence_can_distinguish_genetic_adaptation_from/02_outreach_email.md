# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no fitness-ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about what could tell three responses apart before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about adaptation evidence that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** when a population persists after its environment changes, the observation alone does not tell you the source. A heritable change, a within-lifetime behavioral or physiological adjustment, and a move to a different habitat can all produce the same reading of persistence. To tell them apart you have to change one of three things and hold the other two fixed:
>
> 1. **Heritable library:** did the heritable state change across generations? Look for allele-frequency or heritable-regulatory change, paid by a fitness count in a common garden, an evolve-and-resequence series, or a resurrection comparison.
> 2. **Within-lifetime controller:** with genotype and habitat held, did the phenotype change reversibly? Look for a reaction norm or an expression and behavior time course under common conditions.
> 3. **Habitat contact:** with genotype and controller held, did the field of contact change? Look for a substituted set of conditions, actors, and relations, with occupancy records and genomic continuity.
>
> These are three different failure modes with three different instruments, not three degrees of one response. They also overlap: movement can sieve alleles, and behavioral buffering can change the rate of genetic evolution. So the claim is a union to unbundle, not a list to choose from.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which allele, which behavior, or which habitat explains any particular case, that any named study is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of adaptation. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name. I am also not claiming that plasticity always leads adaptation or always slows it. The literature disagrees with itself there, and the disagreement is itself a reason to measure the discriminator rather than the slope.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a single reading of persistence that uniquely determines the source, with no controlled comparison;
> 2. a documented genetic adaptation with no heritable state change between ancestors and descendants while the trait change persists in a common garden;
> 3. a documented plastic response with the same genotype, the same environment, and the same history but no controller-state difference;
> 4. a documented habitat substitution with no change in the actor and condition list or occupancy coordinates;
> 5. a single intervention that separates all three sources in every overlap and leaves nothing categorically different behind;
> 6. an averaged species-level statistic that prospectively identifies the source per population-slot and survives a transplant or common-garden test;
> 7. a failure of the framework's own closure rule inside a closed heritable set.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape, and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Three failure modes kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map :287) |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the allele, behavior or habitat | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Habitat enumerated before attribution | *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* (Fig 11 :43) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23) |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
