## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**The three mechanisms are named together in the field.** Bernatchez et al. 2024 write that species cope with environmental change *"by genetically adapting to new environmental conditions, through range shifts or through phenotypic plasticity"*, and list different omics methods for each. This corroborates the shape from a field that does not share the framework's evidence pool. It is one low-overlap witness, not a verdict.

- **Genetic change needs heritable-state evidence.** Schlötterer et al. 2015 state that E&R *"can identify selected variants in controlled and replicable experimental settings"* by sequencing pooled individuals. Allele-frequency trajectories, not trait slopes, are the reading that pays for source A.
- **Plasticity needs controlled-environment evidence.** de Villemereuil et al. 2016 call the common garden *"specifically designed to deal with phenotypic plasticity"* and warn that *"phenotypic plasticity is hard to distinguish from local adaptation in wild populations"*. Sommer 2020 gives the definition and the mechanisms: reaction norms, developmental switch genes, genetic accommodation. Together they corroborate the discriminator for source B.
- **Movement needs contact evidence.** Donelson et al. 2019 say a species *"can move to new areas that become available within their thermal niche"*, and that the standard framing *"is an oversimplification and unlikely to reflect reality"*. Luqman et al. 2023 show that movement can also sieve alleles: adaptation and range shift are coupled, so the two must be measured separately. Together they corroborate the discriminator for source C and the union reading.
- **The literature itself conflicts on plasticity's role.** Ho and Zhang 2018 find that genetic change usually reverses plastic change; Botero 2026 finds behavioural buffering can increase standing variation while slowing short-term evolution. Both are real results at different strata. The conflict is a corroboration of the method rule in Fig 9 :30 (*"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"*) and :31 (*"rather than the cause"*), and a refusal of the naive "plasticity leads adaptation" answer.
- **The behavioral frame supplies a second instrument.** Rittschof and Robinson 2014 argue for moving *"beyond the phenotypic gambit"* and using expression profiles to evaluate plasticity. This corroborates F3 at a different scale, but behavior is part of the phenotype, so it is not a separate ground for the organism-level claim.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. Their author wrote the figures, so they are not independent world witnesses. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 11 :43:** *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Ask the recipient to list the conditions and actors they would measure before offering the framework's list.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. Many ecology and evolution publishers have policies governing AI-assisted work, and a submission must be authored and defended by a human who can own the derivation, with disclosure as the venue requires. Do not submit this packet as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human write whatever they conclude. No specific publisher policy is quoted here because none was opened in this session; this is a general constraint, not a claim about a named journal's rules.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
