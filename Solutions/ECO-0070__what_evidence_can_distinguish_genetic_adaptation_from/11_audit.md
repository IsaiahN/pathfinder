- **Arity:** a relation among heritable library Γ, controller state b, action a and environmental return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by generation g and time t. It has at least four operands and two orderings. A two-place reading (organism, persistence) loses both the controller and the source distinction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The fitness assay is an anchor and does not update on the organism's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Persisting" is a threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87). Reversibility is asymmetric: a reversed plastic change, a reverted allele frequency and a return migration are different events even if the phenotype ends in the same place.
- **Scale:** the slot can be an individual or a population, but "persistence across generations" exists only at the population scale (Fig 4 :5). The controller runs within a lifetime, the heritable library across generations, and the habitat at the field-of-contact scale. So the residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the membrane is a filter with a direction (Fig 4 :21).

## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *persistence has a source, and the source is found by changing one of three variables while holding the others fixed.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific field situation. Result: *measure the gene, watch the behavior, and note where the animal went.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into three independent switches. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a field biologist to pick one instrument, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Change one variable" does not specify that a fitness assay settles the result, so it permits a mechanism name to be validated by a trait slope (Fig 1 :27; Fig 2 :29).
3. It loses the habitat enumeration law. "Note where the animal went" is not Fig 11 :43, *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Coming back down, it records coordinates and misses the actor cascade three steps out (Fig 11 :52-54).
4. It loses the bond. "Measure the gene" reads atoms only, so it cannot see the reaction norm that is the same atoms under a different bond (Fig 12 :5, :117).
5. It loses the plasticity-direction conflict. The abstraction has no place for the finding that genetic change often reverses plastic change (Ho and Zhang 2018), so a user of the short form would read direction as source.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also the reason the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
