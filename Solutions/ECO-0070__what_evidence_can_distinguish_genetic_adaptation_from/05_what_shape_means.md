## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, opened directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ECO-0070 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about evidence that can be stated before any particular allele, behavior, or habitat is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *the source of persistence is identified by changing one state variable, heritable library, within-lifetime controller, or habitat contact, while holding the other two fixed, and by settling the result against a fitness ground.*
- Name, withheld: *this allele, this learned behavior, this microhabitat was the cause in this case.*

The distinction matters here because ecology and evolutionary biology are largely in the naming business. Bernatchez et al. name candidate SNPs, genomic offset, and eDNA signatures; Sommer names switch genes; Donelson et al. name thermal niches and dispersal kernels. Those are bindings from the biology frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop. The essay's *They're Scanning Books* point, known here only through the exemplar's mapping, applies: a new dimension of the space is not the same as a new term in the existing space.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "genetic adaptation", "phenotypic plasticity", "behavioral flexibility", "range shift", "common garden", "evolve and resequence", "resurrection ecology" and "genomic offset". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (source of a low-residual slot, indexed per population) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating ECO-0070 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with three rival answers. The field literature agrees: Donelson et al. 2019 call the standard *"move, acclimate/adapt or die"* framing *"an oversimplification and unlikely to reflect reality"*.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the three state variables are stated, the instrument for each is named, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the mechanisms is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means a fitness or survival count under controlled conditions, not a mechanism name read off a trait slope.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For ECO-0070, neither has happened locally. There is no common-garden or transplant result in this folder that pays for a particular allele, behavior, or habitat as the source. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The low-overlap ecology reviews corroborate the *shape of the evidence*, not the source of any particular population's persistence.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error an ecology explanation makes when it reads persistence and names its source. A persistent phenotype is a reading. It is consistent with a new favorable allele, a reversible reaction norm, a moved range, a maternal effect, a symbiont shift, or a mix. The reading does not determine the source. The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a mechanism.

The essay also says the resolution was not an argument but an instrument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* This is the strongest world-facing reason to lead with E&R, common gardens, and occupancy records rather than with a rearrangement of the three concepts.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to adaptation research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about populations.

For outreach this means: a list of famous evolutionary biologists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular allele or behavior is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. An allele already present in a population's library, or a reaction norm already expressible by its genome, is a binding from the biology frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding settles the source without a ground test. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about organisms. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For ECO-0070 the canonical shape is: **a three-variable discriminator with a union structure, three distinct failure modes, and an anchor that is a fitness assay rather than a mechanism name.** It is not a list of candidate mechanisms, not a name of an allele or behavior, and not a ranking of explanations. The names that appear in the ecology literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are partly held (common gardens, E&R, occupancy records) but no local settlement has been made, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
