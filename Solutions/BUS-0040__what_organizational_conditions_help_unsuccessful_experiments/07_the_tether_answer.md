# The Tether's answer on learning from a failed experiment

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is not whether an experiment failed but where its prediction gap goes. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed per experiment slot (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). Three routes are possible: the gap becomes a settled, reusable term (learning), the refusal is aimed at a person so the report stops tracking the world (blame), or the gap is pooled and dropped so nothing is carried forward (waste). This is a **union compound**, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the experiment loop (triangulated: Fig 1 + Fig 2).** The experiment outcome is the anchor, because the question is constitutively about it. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Keep the residual per slot; pooling it into an average is how the live signal disappears.

**D2, the reporting channel (triangulated: Fig 10 + Fig 2).** The channel must stay reachable. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :29: *"keep the anchor reachable."* Blame is structural: Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Punishment gives the reporter an interest in the reading, which moves the apparent anchor and decays the channel. The literature's "psychological safety" and "just culture" are bindings onto this condition, not the condition itself, and a self-report score is a channel reading, not the ground.

**D3, the sensemaking loop (triangulated: Fig 5 + Fig 4 + Operators table).** A failed experiment owes a refusal, not just an explanation. Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* Most "lessons learned" artifacts carry only subtraction, what is missing or what to add; a lesson that the governing belief was wrong requires negation, and Operators table :58 says *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* The refusal must also be aimed correctly: Fig 5 :62-64 says a frame *"can refuse the right kind of thing and still aim the refusal at the outcome it did not want rather than at the belief it holds."* That is the precise structural definition of blame. What is carried forward must be a method, not a recording: Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

**D4, the habitat (triangulated: Fig 6 + Fig 8 + Fig 11).** The review frame must differ from the one that authored the failed bet. Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* When the needed term is absent, a differing closure is the import operator, the only thing that moves the wall (Fig 6 :14). And capability is not in the heroic individual: Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*

**Verdict per domain kept separate, never averaged.** D1 is about an anchor, D2 about a channel, D3 about an operator and a target, D4 about a transform. Their agreement is not one composed score.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that a psychological safety score is the cause or the measure of learning. It is a frame-internal reading (Fig 1 :24-27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*) and one factor in a product (Fig 5 :33: *"The three guards are a product, not a checklist."*).
- Not that accountability should be abolished. Fig 10 :53: *"A prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong."* The form is keep the anchor reachable and declare the stake (Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."*).
- Not that one practice wins. Which specific practice produces learning is a `gap`: Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating instrument has settled which route a given organization actually took.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*, and *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Channel test:** compare reported incidents against an independent log of the same events. If the report tracks the log when leadership changes and stops tracking it when reporters are punished, C1 is real.
2. **Negation test:** inspect the postmortem artifact for a recorded false belief, not only a list of missing items. Then test whether a successor team, given only the carried-up method, avoids the same failure class. A report that survives its own use is a catalyst (Fig 12 :119).
3. **Closure test:** does the next review include a frame that did not author the failed bet, selected against the residual the last one surfaced (Fig 7 :36)? If not, it is a mirror chain.
4. **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** learning with a closed channel; declining residual with no recorded false belief; novel residuals closed by a homogeneous closure; one lever restoring all three conditions; a self-report score predicting learning at maximum independence.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The low-overlap imports (aviation listening, nuclear safety, publication bias) corroborate the mechanism at other strata; they do not name the practice here.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning learning-from-failure practice and does not treat a safety survey as proof. It says a failed experiment's gap belongs to that slot and must stay anchored to the experiment, not pooled into an average. From that it derives three conditions that must hold together for the gap to become learning: the reporting channel must stay reachable, so punishment cannot move the anchor; the vocabulary must be able to say a held belief was wrong and aim that refusal at the belief rather than at a person or an unwanted result; and the review must include a frame different from the one that authored the bet, so a mirror chain cannot pass itself off as corroboration. Blame is the compound where the first two fail, waste is the compound where the gap is dropped and nothing is minted. What it offers is the shape any candidate practice must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which practice actually wins stays open until a ground settles it.
