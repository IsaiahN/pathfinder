## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the three conditions that must hold for a failed experiment to reach learning, stated before we can name the practice that satisfies them in a given organization. It constrains; it does not fill. "The reporting channel must stay reachable" is a shape. "Install blameless postmortems" is a name.

This question is unusually clean for the shape discipline, because the business literature has already named many candidate practices. The framework's job is not to rank them. It is to say what any of them must do.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question that is why the residual is stated as a routed gap (learning / blame / waste) rather than as "low psychological safety," which is a borrowed cause-word from one field.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the checkable expectation is: if the channel condition holds, the reported incident count should track an independent log of the same events.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :55 says the same thing in figure language: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The three conditions in this folder are that bundle, unbundled before search.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: an instrument that tracks reports against independent events, or that tests whether a lesson changes behavior in a successor team.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The aviation listening study and the publication-bias literature are low-overlap imports. They predict parts of the shape; they are not the shape.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming "psychological safety" as *the* cause of learning turns a visible choice from one field into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the safety survey is not the ground (source discrimination)

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and I verified it directly this session. An instrument that reads green *"is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And the decisive line: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: a high safety score is a green reading. It does not say whether the channel to the ground is actually open, whether a belief was refused, or whether the review frame differed. Several very different organizational states can produce the same score. What is owed is a better instrument, not a heavier weight on the survey.

## 5. Q2 - what licenses a particular practice? ANSWERED

**Nothing in the framework selects a named practice.** The framework returns conditions. A particular practice is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the practice is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is a declared `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F10, not a hidden answer.

**Does the business record commit the error the README forbids?** Partly. It reads a self-report (the artifact) and names its source ("a safe culture") without an instrument that discriminates among sources. That is the metamerism error. The correct form remains the three effect conditions plus the demand for a discriminator.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3-§4

I first wrote that "the framework returns a shape, not a name" forbids naming any organizational condition. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. C1 (channel reachable), C2 (refusal with negation), and C3 (differing closure) are all already figure-derived conditions, so naming them binds onto held atoms; it does not invent a new one. What remains forbidden is naming a specific management practice as the answer, and claiming that a self-report score settles the ground. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 13).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** Verified directly this session from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The same essay says *"You may all agree and be wrong."* A list of well-known people who endorse a learning culture is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried (a refuter, a ground, an instrument), not an endorsement.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, verified: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"* however *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Honest stop

The essays are all by the framework's author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries no independent witness weight (Fig 2 :40). The `gap` is real: no figure line and no essay selects among blameless postmortems, just culture, after-action reviews, registered reports or safety training. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
