## QUESTION

What organizational conditions help unsuccessful experiments produce learning rather than blame or waste?

Restated as an effect: why does a slot's R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| sometimes become a settled, reusable term, sometimes get aimed at a person, and sometimes get dropped into an average? R is indexed per experiment, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The anchor is the experiment outcome, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A: learning is a property of a culture the organization owns, measured by a safety survey. Option B: learning is a maintained three-condition route from an experiment residual to a settled term. What each leads to: A installs a frame-internal score as the verdict and can declare success while the channel is closed; B predicts failure when any one of the three conditions fails and owes an instrument that would tell which one.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-factor story does not. A failed experiment reaches learning when three conditions hold together: C1 the channel to the ground stays reachable, C2 the vocabulary can refuse a held belief and aims the refusal at that belief rather than at a person or an unwanted outcome, C3 the review frame has contact with a closure that differs from the one that authored the bet. Blame is the compound where C1 and C2 fail; waste is the compound where the residual is pooled or dropped and no term is minted. The three conditions are a relation, an operator and a transform, not one list, which is why one lever cannot clear the bundle. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged; naming the specific practice that wins is a `gap`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The business pool corroborates C1 after re-mapping safety from "the ground" to "the channel reading"; the conflict with Eldor et al. was returned to census and did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism at other strata, not the firm-level fact.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A team failing C2 would be given more safety training, or a team failing C1 would be told to import more frames, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs three. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible. The avoidable part is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a recording instead of a generator (Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*).
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers: (1) learning with a demonstrably closed channel; (2) declining residual with no recorded false belief and no method; (3) novel residuals closed by a homogeneous closure over multiple cycles; (4) one lever restoring all three conditions with no categorically different residue; (5) a self-report safety score predicting learning at maximum independence from two frames with zero shared evidence. A source-discriminating instrument that tracks reports against an independent log, or that shows a successor team avoiding the failure class from the carried-up method alone, would move the verdict; agreement or prestige would not.
