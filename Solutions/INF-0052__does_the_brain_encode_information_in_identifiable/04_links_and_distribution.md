## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on the question

1. **Both sides are empirically live at once.** The "Neural coding" article presents population coding, temporal coding and correlation coding as well-formulated and widely used, while the "Grandmother cell" article presents invariant selective cells. The honest reading is that the literature contains evidence for both, so a verdict that picks one side by citation alone is a filter being used as a witness (Fig 9 :60 **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**).
2. **The single-neuron evidence is labelled sparseness by the researchers themselves.** The Quiroga excerpt and the Grandmother cell article both decline the strong grandmother reading. Quoting the neuron result as proof of localist coding would be an adopted import with no source gate (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).
3. **Level-relativity is visible inside the literature.** Population coding is described as noisy single neurons combined to gain precision, and population activity is described as constrained to low-dimensional manifolds. A low-dimensional manifold read at one level is a single identifiable operand at another. That is Fig 12 :104 in the world's own vocabulary. Triangulated for the shape; the specific mapping remains `specified`.
