## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :133: **"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, *"a representation is atoms plus typed bonds, and identifiability is level-relative"* is a shape. *"The brain uses sparse coding"* or *"the brain uses population vectors"* is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describing the residual in effect terms produced a vocabulary of atoms, bonds, levels and instruments, and that vocabulary narrowed the question to a union. The narrowing happened before any coding scheme was named.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means a source-discriminating instrument, not a further review article.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Claiming they are the brain's actual primitives would turn a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the question's `or` is the specific forbidden error

Fig 5 :62-63: **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."**

The question *"identifiable or distributed?"* holds one clean mechanism as a preference. That preference is not bound, predicts nothing, and accrues no residual. The loop's checks all run on the mechanism, none on the direction of the question. So a derivation can correctly process both sides of the debate and still be aimed at the wrong thing.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the companion warning: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* Selecting experts for likely agreement with either side spends the independence that makes their disagreement useful.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the stop condition: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A population reading cannot comment on whether the underlying code was localist or distributed. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 5. Q1 and Q2 answered

**Q1: Does the framework name which coding scheme the brain uses?** No. It returns a shape. A coding scheme is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Q2: Does the framework forbid calling a population pattern "identifiable"?** No. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive. Relata and relations are both already in the substrate. What the framework forbids is a seventh primitive and an import that adds no atom. Calling a settled population pattern an atom at the next level is Fig 12 :104, not a violation.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote §3 as if "shape, not a name" forbade selecting any concrete coding scheme. That overstates it. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in `Γ`. Atoms and bonds are held, so naming them is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. Retracted as applied to atoms and bonds; retained as applied to any seventh primitive.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a population reading cannot settle the code is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous neuroscientists who agree with either side is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
