## QUESTION

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question's `or` is a bundled union, so no verdict that picks one side survives. The shape that does survive: an adequate representation has identifiable relata and typed relations; a list of active units has recorded half of itself, and a network with nothing at the nodes is empty. Identifiability is level-relative, because a settled population pattern becomes a single operand at the next level. Two contents over the same unit set are isomers when the typed relation differs. The framework returns that shape, not the name of a coding scheme. Which mechanism the brain uses is unreached, not unreachable. This is a `general` structural shape and a `specified` union claim, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states a mode, four refuters and a lineage before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and refuters. A second cost is misreading Fig 1's per-slot rule as a brain claim; it is named as a strain in `01` and `06`.
- **undoable:** yes. No term was withdrawn and no primitive imported, so the union verdict can be superseded without touching the derivation. If a ground reading shows a genuine partition, the unbundling claim is dropped and the two domain verdicts stand on their own.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that holds the population projection fixed while varying the code would name the source; a necessity test that leaves behaviour unchanged when a claimed carrier unit is silenced refutes pure localist atoms; a content change when the active set is held fixed and the relation varies confirms the bond term; a demonstration that relata and relations are mutually exclusive in the brain would break the union claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
