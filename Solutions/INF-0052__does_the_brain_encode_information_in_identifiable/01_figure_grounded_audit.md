## 0. The question under audit, stated as a residual

Question INF-0052, Information Theory.txt line 65: *"Does the brain encode information in identifiable neural representations, or is information distributed across dynamics and connections?"*

Stated in effect terms:

- **Option A (identifiable).** A specific unit or small set stands for a specific content. Effect: reading which unit fires names the content; lesioning or silencing that unit should remove that content. The unit is the anchor of the readout.
- **Option B (distributed).** Content is carried by joint activity across many units and by the relations among them. Effect: no single unit is necessary; content is recovered by decoding the population pattern and its dynamics. The relation is the anchor of the readout.
- **`R`.** The gap between what a candidate predicts and what an observation shows, indexed per recorded unit or slot, not averaged (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a statement of which atoms and which bonds carry a given content, with a refuter that would fire if one of them were absent.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The `or` is a bundled union, not a partition (decisive)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :55-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**. Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

The question asks whether content lives in identifiable units **or** in distributed dynamics and connections. The figures answer at a different level: a representation is a molecule, atoms joined by typed bonds, and the bond carries information the operands do not. A readout that records only the firing units has recorded half of itself. A readout that records only the dynamics has no units at the nodes. The `or` is a false partition.

**Refuter:** show that the two conditions are mutually exclusive and exhaustive in the brain, so that a representation with identifiable atoms and no bonds, or with bonds and no atoms, is settled at the ground. If that holds, Fig 9's unbundling rule was misapplied.

### F2: A pure distributed account is empty

Fig 13 :29: **"A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes."** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :95-96: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."**

Connections and dynamics are the bond half. Without something identifiable occupying the nodes, an account of "distributed across dynamics and connections" has nothing to be distributed over. This is not a defence of grandmother cells; it is the structural requirement that a network have nodes.

**Refuter:** exhibit a settled representation with no identifiable relata at any level, where the relations alone carry content and no unit can be singled out even at the ground. That breaks Fig 13 :29.

### F3: A pure localist account records half of itself

Fig 12 :46-47: **"which one holds is not recoverable from the operands"** and **"A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."** Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**

Naming a single neuron that fires for a concept gives the atom and withholds the typed relation to every other atom. The world evidence supports sparse, selective responses, but selectivity is not sufficiency. Single-unit responses are invariant under transformation, which is a bond property: the same concept survives many different images.

**Refuter:** show that classification performance is invariant under every transformation with no change in any relation to other units, so the bond term is zero and the atom alone settles the content.

### F4: "Identifiable" is a fact about a level, not about the world

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**. Fig 13 :110: **"A settled composition enters the library as a term, and is then an operand like any other."**

A population pattern settled at one level becomes a single identifiable operand at the next. So "identifiable representation" and "distributed pattern" can describe the same physical state read at two levels. There is no privileged stratum at which the correct description must live.

**Refuter:** show a level at which a settled representation cannot be demoted to an atom for a higher composition, or a level where the demotion changes the ground reading.

### F5: Isomer, not identity: same units, different bond, different content

Fig 12 :92: **"Symmetry is what decides whether an arrangement is a new thing."** Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Fig 12 :123: **"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Two contents implemented over overlapping sets of neurons are isomers: same atoms, different typed relations, different substance. The stronger claim, that distinct contents over the same cellular set are different substances, is a checkable structural prediction. The weaker claim, that they share a functional group, is the safe fallback.

**Refuter:** if two distinct contents over the same unit set can be written with the same atom set and the same bond set, the isomer claim fails and only the shared-substrate claim remains.

### F6: The clean either/or is an invisible preference

Fig 5 :62-63: **"A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it."**

The shape the question wants, one clean mechanism, is held as a preference. It predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so none of the loop's checks can see it. The remedy is the `Direction` discipline in `THE_FORMULA.md`: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual. §0 does this.

**Refuter:** show a figure-level check that runs on the direction of the question rather than on the mechanism. No such check is present in the extracts read.

### F7: The observer indexes per slot; aggregation hides the live signal (strain)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

These two lines favour identifiable slots in the **observer's** bookkeeping. A population-average readout can hide a live single-unit residual. This is a methodological preference, not a neuroscientific finding, and applying it to the brain would be a category error. It is recorded as a strain, not smoothed into a verdict: it governs the measuring frame, not the measured one.

**Refuter:** a figure re-census that shows Fig 1 governs the system under study rather than the frame doing the measuring would flip this finding from a strain to evidence for option A.

### F8: Only a source-discriminating instrument picks a side

Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28, fetched 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. An instrument that reads a population pattern cannot comment on the source. The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the analogous green-light case. Agreement between localist and distributed researchers is one evidence pool talking.

**Refuter:** exhibit an instrument whose reading changes when the source changes while the projection is held fixed, and that returns different readings for the two candidate encodings. With such an instrument the question moves from `specified` to `grounded` or is refuted.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 10 :52 **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"** and the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A settled representation is exhibited that is a single atom with no typed bond to any other atom, and it carries content at the ground.
2. A settled representation is exhibited that is pure relation with no identifiable relatum at any level.
3. The localist and distributed conditions are shown to be a partition, mutually exclusive and exhaustive, in the brain rather than a union in the description.
4. A source-discriminating instrument returns the same reading for both encodings when the ground distinguishes them, or returns a different reading while the ground does not.

If any fires, the union verdict is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. SEARCHED, INWARD, OUTWARD (the three questions, kept distinct)

No single figure line closes the observational question of which scheme the brain uses, so `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 applies.

- **SEARCHED, asked first.** Is the answer already in the closure and unfound? Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."** The existing neuroscience literature already contains both atom evidence and bond evidence. More reading of the same pool adds spelling, not an atom. This is why the answer here is a shape, not a new experimental result.
- **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. Fig 6 :32-34: an instrument *"is improved from a worse instrument already returning something"*. Population decoding, single-unit recording, and optogenetic perturbation are already returning readings that fail to resolve the source. Sharpen those.
- **OUTWARD.** Import only if a frame whose closure differs predicts the residual's shape. No such import was needed here. The six-term substrate already holds states and structures, atoms and bonds. Importing a new primitive against a budget limit would add an atom that was not needed.

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and were never read against each other.

### Round trip note

The condensation used in `07` and `09` is: a representation is atoms plus typed bonds, and the localist/distributed distinction is level-relative. Sent up from Fig 12 :5, :104, :117 and Fig 13 :29, :74-75. Brought back down to the concrete question, two constraints drop out: the typed-bond requirement (lost when people say merely "connections") and the per-slot residual rule (lost when people average across units). The gap `R_T` is nonzero and is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set `R_T` to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.

### Verdict after re-census

The shape survives: any adequate answer must include identifiable relata and typed relations, and must treat the level at which either is called irreducible as a local fact. The clean `or` does not survive. Strains after re-census: one, F7, named and scoped to the measuring frame. Nothing hidden.
