# The Tether's answer on identifiable versus distributed coding

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the union claim, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observation is a pattern of neural activity. Single-unit responses are selective and survive stimulus transformation; population patterns carry content without any single unit being necessary. The question asks which mechanism the brain uses. The two candidate encodings are not mutually exclusive conditions. Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** This is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground, not one hard problem (Fig 9 :55-58).

## 2. The shape

1. **An adequate representation has atoms and typed bonds.** Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** A readout that lists active units has recorded half of itself. A readout that lists only connections has no units at the nodes: Fig 13 :29 **"A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes."**
2. **The either/or is unbundled, not decided.** Both sides of the literature are real evidence. Invariant single-unit responses are relata; population activity and dynamics are relations. Choosing one by citation is using a filter as a witness (Fig 9 :60).
3. **Identifiability is level-relative.** Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"**. A population pattern settled at one level becomes a single identifiable operand at the next. There is no privileged stratum at which the correct description must live.
4. **Two contents over the same units are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Where only the form is shared, the weaker reading is a functional group: Fig 12 :123 **"A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
5. **The framework does not name the brain's coding scheme.** It returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32). Selecting sparse coding, population vectors or temporal coding is a binding from the neuroscience frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation and not an import that moves the wall.
6. **The observer indexes per slot.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** This governs the measuring frame, not the brain. Kept as a named strain.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that grandmother cells are correct, and not that pure distributed dynamics carry no information.
- Not that the brain instantiates one of the six substrate terms literally, or that the six are nature's own list. They are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*).
- Not that a new experiment was run, and not that this is a result. No measurement at any scale settles `left(R,φ)` here.
- Not that using two frames together adds evidence. Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this evidence pool.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved the analogous case. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Necessity test.** Silence or lesion a unit that is claimed to carry a content while holding the population projection fixed. If behaviour is unchanged, the pure localist atom claim fails. If behaviour changes selectively while the projection is held fixed, the pure distributed claim fails.
- **Bond-discrimination test.** Hold the active unit set fixed and vary the typed relation among them. If the content changes, the bond carries information the operands do not (Fig 12 :5). If it does not, the isomer claim fails.
- **Level test.** Read the same physical state at two levels. If a population pattern at one level can serve as a single operand at the next without changing the ground reading, level-relativity is confirmed (Fig 12 :104).
- **Standing refuters** from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4: an atom with no bond carrying content; a relation with no relatum at any level; a demonstrated partition; an instrument that cannot distinguish the encodings where the ground can.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A population reading that no current instrument distinguishes from a localist code is still a population reading; the missing discriminator is owed, not a new term. No import was made, so no atom was added.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the localist versus distributed debate, because the either/or is a bundled union rather than a partition. Its structural claim is that an adequate representation has identifiable relata and typed relations between them: a list of active units has recorded half of the story, and a network with nothing at the nodes is empty. Which level you call identifiable is a fact about that level, not about the world, so a population pattern settled at one level becomes a single operand at the next. Two contents over the same cells stand as isomers, same atoms arranged differently. The framework returns that shape, not a name, so it does not say whether the brain uses sparse coding, population vectors or temporal codes. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The question stays open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
