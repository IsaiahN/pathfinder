## Three findings in the wider literature that bear on this question

1. **Gánti's chemoton is an explicit three-condition model.** It requires metabolism, self-replication and a membrane together, with the first two forming an autocatalytic subsystem enclosed by the third. This is the strongest published corroboration of the union verdict, while remaining a model rather than a chronology. Do not present it as a result the figures produced.
2. **Lincoln and Joyce dissolve the molecule/network split.** Their cross-replicating RNA enzymes are individual molecules whose replication depends on a two-member catalytic loop, and the loop then evolves under competition. This is a laboratory instance of the compound, not evidence that RNA came first in history.
3. **The protocell page notes the missing ground.** A functional protocell that evolves has not yet been achieved in the laboratory. That is exactly the honest stop the figure derivation reaches: the shape is available, the chronology is not.

A conflict check was run. No world source asserts that one of the three alone is sufficient for Darwinian evolution with the others absent. The conflict rule was therefore not triggered. If a source had done so, it would have been returned to the domain census, not used to flip the verdict (Fig 2 :40).
