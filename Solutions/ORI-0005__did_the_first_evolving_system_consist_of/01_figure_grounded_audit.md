## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

R is the forced choice among three candidate substrates for "the first evolving system": (A) an individual molecule, (B) a chemical network, (C) a compartment. Stated as an effect: which arrangement first made variation heritable and differential persistence possible, so that a lineage could accumulate novelty rather than merely repeat?

- Choosing A sends the search to a single self-copying replicator and to the RNA-first program.
- Choosing B sends the search to catalytic closure and to the metabolism-first or autocatalytic-set program.
- Choosing C sends the search to a boundary and to the protocell or compartment-first program.

The hidden term is "first." The question assumes the three options are alternatives arranged along a timeline, so that one can be earlier than the others. The audit tests that assumption rather than granting it. The source file's own preamble already says the programs "are not always mutually exclusive" and that the central challenge is linking "replication, energy use, and compartmentalization." We reach the same refusal from the figures, then ask what the figures can and cannot name.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step "Direction"): either (i) one of A, B, C is sufficient alone and prior to the others, or (ii) no one of them is sufficient and the residual is a compound whose chronology is owed to the ground. This binding is written down now so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## 1. Domain census

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three candidate substrates, but the relations between them are mutually enabling (a network is made of molecules; a compartment contains a network; closure needs a source of matter and energy). Symmetry: the three are not interchangeable roles. A molecule is a persistent operand, a network is a closing loop, a compartment is a spatial bound that makes "contained" and "isolated" meaningful. Scale: molecular, reaction-network, and mesoscopic boundary. The transform between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

**One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor.** A candidate with no nameable anchor is not yet a domain.

| id | domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | molecular inheritance | replicators, templates, catalysts that survive their own use | heritable variation, persistence across uses | whether a variant recurs after use | admitted |
| D2 | catalytic closure | reactions, catalysts, food molecules, reaction network | closure of the food set, self-production | whether the loop closes and persists without a maintainer | admitted |
| D3 | spatial boundedness | membrane, vesicle, mineral pore, contents, environment | inside/outside, containment, differential persistence of a unit | a region that makes "isolated" mean something | admitted |
| D4 | habitat and ground | environment, gradients, conditions, selection by contact | contact, differential persistence | the ground, which does not update | admitted |
| X1 | an isolated molecule as the whole evolving system | one molecule | self-copying | none nameable: without a pool of variants and a habitat, "evolving" has no referent | not a domain; absorbed into D1 + D4 |
| X2 | "first" as a linear stage | a sequence of stages | temporal priority | no figure supplies an ordered rank among the three conditions | refused, see F2 |

**Partition or union.** Union. The three candidates are overlapping conditions of one compound, not a list of alternatives. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** D4 (ground and habitat) must be in place before any of D1 to D3 can be called evolving, because selection is contact, not an intrinsic property of a molecule. Within the compound, each of D1, D2 and D3 presupposes the others. There is no first link. See F2.

**Strain test applied during the census.** My first pass tried to map "compartment" onto Fig 4's membrane. That was a strain: Fig 4's membrane is a boundary between *scales* (one agent versus a population), not a physical enclosure. Re-census sent the physical boundary to Fig 13's space state and Fig 11's isolation, where it fits without force. The retracted mapping is kept visible in section 6.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The forced choice is a union, not a partition (decisive, `general`)

Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."

Fig 9 :57: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

The three options overlap. A chemical network is made of molecules. A compartment contains a network. A network that is not bounded is not a unit that persists differentially; a bounded network with no heritable variation is a stable droplet, not an evolving lineage. A lever that installs only one condition leaves the intersections standing because the other conditions still bind. So the question "A, B or C?" is malformed in the specific way Fig 9 names. The honest answer is a compound, and the compound must be unbundled before it is searched again.

- **Mode:** `general` for the union rule, `specified` for its application here.
- **Status:** triangulated. Fig 9 (union/partition) and Fig 13 :3 ("No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others") are low overlap: the first governs question shape, the second governs the minimum set of a system.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where one of the three, with the other two provably absent, undergoes Darwinian evolution in the sense of heritable variation plus differential persistence across generations. If that case exists, the compound reading is false and the partition reading wins.

### F2: "First" is a chain assumption applied to a cycle (decisive, `general`)

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."

Fig 13 :4 to :5: "There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."

The enabling relations among molecules, closure and boundary form a loop: molecules catalyse the network that produces molecules; the boundary concentrates the network that maintains the boundary. A cycle has no first link by construction. Fig 3's dependency ordering governs *chains*; applying it here is the category error. Fig 13 adds that there is no rank among the kinds at all, so "which came first" has no answer in the framework even before the empirical record is consulted.

- **Mode:** `general` for the no-rank and cycle claims.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 12 (cycle) and Fig 13 (lockstep). Two distinct figures, different subject matter.
- **Refuter:** show an ordering of the three conditions that is both dependency-ordered and acyclic, with each condition independently sufficient to start evolution. That would make "first" well defined and break the cycle reading.

### F3: No candidate is sufficient in isolation (`general` for shape, `specified` for origins)

Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."

At origins there is no maintainer and no live frame in the seat. Fig 11 :36 states the consequence: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there." So the first evolving system is not a molecule considered alone, nor a network considered alone, nor a compartment considered alone. Capability belongs to the coupled system in contact with a habitat. This is also why X1 in the census has no anchor: "evolving" is not a property of an isolated molecule.

- **Mode:** `general` for the agent-and-habitat rule, `specified` for the prebiotic case.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 11 (habitat and the unmaintained mode) and Fig 2 :29 (anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change when a frame thinks otherwise).
- **Refuter:** demonstrate heritable differential persistence in a single molecular species held with no habitat, no pool of competitors and no differential contact. Under Fig 11 :40 that demonstration would move the ground and refute this finding.

### F4: The network supplies closure, not merely connectivity (`specified`)

Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input."

Fig 13 :24: "that entities relate, and influence travels."

These three lines separate a reaction network from a merely connected set of reactions. What matters for evolution is a catalyst that survives its own use (heritable persistence of a part) and a loop whose output re-enters as input (self-production). A linear chain cannot close. The world literature converges on the same object: the autocatalytic set is defined as a collection of entities each created catalytically by others in the set, such that the set as a whole catalyses its own production (Autocatalytic set, Wikipedia, fetched 2026-11-23). This corroborates the *shape* of D2, not name-level chronology.

- **Mode:** `specified`. The figure definition of cycle is `general`; the mapping to prebiotic chemistry is a binding from the chemistry frame.
- **Status:** single-source for the figure definition (Fig 12), corroborated by a low-overlap world source (complex systems), which is why the world source is secondary.
- **Refuter:** show a chemical system that accumulates heritable novelty across generations with no catalytic part surviving its own use and no closed loop. That would break D2.

### F5: The compartment supplies containment and a unit of selection (`specified`)

Fig 13 :10: "near, far, between, contained."

Fig 13 :62: "no spatial symmetry for Noether, and no region to make isolated mean anything."

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."

A boundary is what makes a region, a "contained" interior and a possible unit of selection. Without a region, "isolated" has no meaning (Fig 13 :62), so differential persistence of a bounded whole cannot be described. The protocell literature makes the same structural point: a protocell is "a self-organized, membrane-bound or membraneless compartment that concentrates biomolecules" and "a minimal unit that can undergo Darwinian evolution" (Protocell, Wikipedia, fetched 2026-11-23). Note the membraneless alternative: containment is the condition, a lipid bilayer is only one physical realisation of it. Naming the realisation is not licensed by the figure.

- **Mode:** `specified`. Containment as a state is `general`; vesicles and pores are bindings from the chemistry frame.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 13 (space state) and Fig 11 (isolation as substituted habitat), with the protocell literature as low-overlap corroboration.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a bounded unit of selection persisting differentially with no spatial or phase boundary of any kind. That would break D3.

### F6: The chronology is owed to the ground, not to agreement (`general`)

It is not settled by tallying research programs, and it is not settled by this derivation. - **Mode:** `general`.
- **Status:** triangulated across Fig 2 (anchor and independence) and Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."
- **Refuter:** a ground measurement that simultaneously shows which of the three conditions was present first and that the compound reading is unnecessary. Until then the chronology is not a verdict.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the design-instrument rule. The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. One of the three candidates, with the other two provably absent, undergoes Darwinian evolution with heritable variation and differential persistence across generations (kills F1 and F3).
2. A dependency ordering of the three conditions is shown to be acyclic and each condition independently sufficient, so "first" becomes well defined (kills F2).
3. Heritable differential persistence is demonstrated with no catalytic part surviving its own use and no closed loop (kills F4).
4. A bounded unit of selection persists differentially with no spatial or phase boundary of any kind (kills F5).
5. A temporal order of the three is derived from the six-term set alone, with no import (closes F7).

If any fires, the compound reading is withdrawn or narrowed, and the change is recorded here rather than silently edited.

## 7. Conclusion

The figures return a shape and a refusal. The first evolving system cannot be one of the three candidates taken alone, because no system functions with only one condition and capability is a property of system and habitat (Fig 13 :3, Fig 11 :39). The three candidates form a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :55, :57). Their enabling relations form a cycle, so "first" is not well defined (Fig 12 :121, Fig 13 :4 to :5). The chronology is a ground question and is declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39, Fig 2 :29).

Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the prebiotic application, nothing `grounded`. Verdict status: the compound reading is triangulated; the chronology is an honest `gap`. Strains after re-census: none hidden; the one live limitation is that a specific first substrate cannot be named without a ground and an import with a source gate.
