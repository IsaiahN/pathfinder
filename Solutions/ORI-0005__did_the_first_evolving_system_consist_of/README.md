## The residual as an effect

The question asks us to choose one of three material arrangements as "the first evolving system." In effect terms, it asks which arrangement first made variation heritable and differential persistence possible, so that a lineage could accumulate novelty. Choosing "molecules" sends the searcher to a single replicator. Choosing "networks" sends the searcher to catalytic closure. Choosing "compartments" sends the searcher to a boundary that makes a unit of selection possible. The answer we give changes the answers to the adjacent questions in this section: Q3 (simplest system that can evolve), Q4 (genes, metabolism, boundary or combination), Q6 (evolution before modern genes), and Q9 (when "life" began).

The source file's own framing already refuses a clean partition: the big programs "are not always mutually exclusive," and the central challenge is "how chemistry could link together replication, energy use, and compartmentalization, not just how one potentially useful molecule formed." The figure derivation below reaches the same place by a different route, and adds that the word "first" is itself the defect.
