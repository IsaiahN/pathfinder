## QUESTION

Did the first evolving system consist of individual molecules, chemical networks, or compartments? Restated in effect terms: which arrangement first made variation heritable and differential persistence possible, so that a lineage could accumulate novelty? Option A asserts one of the three alone. Option B asserts a compound with no well-defined first. What each leads to: A sends the search to a single research program and hides the other two conditions; B sends the search to the shape of any evolving system and leaves the chronology owed to the ground. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The forced choice is refused. The first evolving system was a compound of heritable persistence, catalytic closure and containment, coupled to a habitat that did the selecting. No one of the three is sufficient in isolation (Fig 11 :39, :40), no rank exists among the conditions (Fig 13 :4 to :5), and their enabling relations form a loop with no first link (Fig 12 :121). Which physical substrate actually appeared first is a declared `gap` owed to the ground (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39). Mode `general` on the shape, `specified` on the prebiotic application, nothing `grounded`. The compound reading is triangulated; the chronology is an honest `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If a single condition were shown sufficient with the others absent, the compound claim fails, and the refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` state exactly which observation would do it. The larger practical cost is sending a name where a shape is owed, which would spend an outside reader's independence on an answer the framework cannot give (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Leading with the refuter avoids that cost.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted (Fig 6 :42). The shape and its four falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62 to :64). A superseding packet can replace this one without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A Loop Test showing heritable novelty with no closed loop, a Boundary Test showing a bounded unit of selection with no boundary, or a Chronology Test distinguishing which condition crossed first with the others absent would each move the verdict. A derivation of a temporal order from the six-term set alone would close the `gap`. Agreement among researchers, prestige, and the number of labs working on a program are not such instruments (Fig 2 :35, Fig 9 :60).
