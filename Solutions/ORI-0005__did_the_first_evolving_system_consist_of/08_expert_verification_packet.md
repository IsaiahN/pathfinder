## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question asks whether the first evolving system consisted of individual molecules, chemical networks or compartments. The three are not alternatives. They are overlapping conditions of one compound: heritable persistence, catalytic closure and containment, coupled to a habitat that does the selecting. No one of them is sufficient in isolation. Their enabling relations form a loop, so "first" is not structurally well defined. Which physical substrate actually appeared first is a `gap` owed to the ground, not a name the framework can supply. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close the gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: The three candidates form a union, not a partition
- **Statement:** a case in two or three conditions is categorically different from a harder case of one; a lever that installs only one condition leaves the intersections standing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Also Fig 9 :57, "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 union rule and Fig 13 :3, "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others"). Low overlap: one governs question shape, the other the minimum set of a system.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a case where one of the three, with the other two provably absent, undergoes Darwinian evolution with heritable variation and differential persistence across generations.

### C2: "First" is a chain assumption applied to a cycle
- **Statement:** the enabling relations among molecules, catalytic closure and containment form a loop. A cycle has no first link. A dependency order governs chains, not loops.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121, "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Also Fig 13 :4 to :5, "There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 12 cycle, Fig 13 lockstep).
- **Refuter:** show an acyclic dependency ordering of the three with each independently sufficient, so that "first" becomes well defined.

### C3: No candidate is sufficient in isolation
- **Statement:** capability belongs to the system and its habitat. At origins there is no maintainer, so selection is pure contact rather than an artefact of a frame.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39, "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40, "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :56, "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept."
- **Status:** triangulated (Fig 11 habitat and Fig 2 :29 anchor rule).
- **Refuter:** demonstrate heritable differential persistence in one of the three held with the others absent and with no differential contact to a habitat.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Loop Test:** does a network close with a catalyst that survives its own use, or merely repeat?
2. **Boundary Test:** is there a region, phase or membrane making a bounded unit of selection possible?
3. **Heredity Test:** does variation persist across generations of the unit, not just in one occasion?
4. **Chronology Test:** can a ground reading distinguish which condition crossed its threshold first in a particular setting, with the others absent? This closes the `gap`.
