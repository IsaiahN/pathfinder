# Outreach draft: check one shape before deciding about the source

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive the recipient's own check. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if stage 1 does not hold.

---

**Subject:** One shape claim about the first evolving system, with the refuter written in advance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as posed:** did the first evolving system consist of individual molecules, chemical networks, or compartments? The question assumes a forced choice among three alternatives.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the prebiotic application, not `grounded`):** the three items are not alternatives. They are overlapping conditions of one compound. A chemical network is made of molecules; a compartment contains a network; an unbounded network is not a unit that persists differentially, and a bounded network with no heritable variation is a stable droplet rather than an evolving lineage. So the answer is not one of A, B or C. It is the compound.
>
> The second claim is about the word "first." The enabling relations form a loop: molecules catalyse the network that produces molecules, and a boundary concentrates the network that maintains the boundary. A cycle has no first link by construction. So "which came first" is not a question the structure can answer, even before the geochemical record is consulted. I am explicitly **not** claiming to know which physical substrate appeared first in time. That is owed to the ground, and I record it as `gap`, not as silence.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that RNA won; that metabolism came first; that lipid vesicles were the first compartments (containment can be membraneless); that the framework names a first substrate. Those are bindings from the chemistry frame, and convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Until provenance is shown they carry zero evidentiary weight for this claim.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before I sent this:**
> 1. one of the three, with the other two provably absent, undergoes Darwinian evolution with heritable variation and differential persistence across generations;
> 2. an acyclic dependency ordering of the three is shown, with each independently sufficient, so that "first" is well defined;
> 3. heritable differential persistence is demonstrated with no catalytic part surviving its own use and no closed loop;
> 4. a bounded unit of selection persists differentially with no spatial or phase boundary of any kind.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw or narrow the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` in the project folder
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Autocatalytic sets: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autocatalytic_set
> - Protocells: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protocell
>
> **If you want to reuse the machinery:** the formula is in the local `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figures and the literature. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

Nothing here asks anyone to trust the framework, and nothing is sent by this agent. You host and send.
