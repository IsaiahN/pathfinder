# The Tether's answer on state capacity

**Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the four-term application, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A reformer must allocate scarce effort and asks which of tax collection, public services, enforcement or information gathering matters most for effective governance. The residual is the gap between that request and what the observation can support. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: the four capacities overlap, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). The question asks for a single ordinal rank, which is an aggregation across slots, and Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The shape

1. **There is no rank inside a minimum viable set.** Fig 13 :4: *"There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* Fig 13 :3: *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* Fig 13 :48: *"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."* Applied at the institutional stratum under Fig 4 :20, the four capacities are jointly necessary and unordered. No one of them "matters most" in general. Mode: `general` for the law, `specified` for the four-term application.
2. **The live capacity is a slot reading, not a rank.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."* The binding capacity in any polity is the slot carrying positive residual there and then, which is owed to a measurement, not derivable from the theory.
3. **They are ordered by dependency, not importance.** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Information gathering is perception (link 1), public services state what counts as progress (link 3), enforcement is planning and action (links 4 and 5), and tax collection is the currency that feeds every link. This is a binding from the institutional frame, `specified`. Scott (2010) corroborates from a different field: *"To follow the process of state-making, then, is to follow the conquest of illegibility."*
4. **Capability belongs to state and polity together.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A reform that enlarges an agency without changing what the state can perceive, extract, compel or deliver has changed the agent, not the contact.
5. **The four form a union compound, and regimes over them can be isomers.** Fig 9 :55 and Fig 12 :117 (*"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*). Two polities with the same four capacities arranged differently are different substances, with a stated falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that information gathering is always the answer. It is usually upstream, but the live slot is a measurement.
- Not that tax collection is the foundation because it funds the rest. That is a `specified` historical claim (Tilly 1975, *"a surviving political organization inevitably formed from necessary tax collection and enforcement"*), not a figure-derived rank.
- Not that the four capacities are literally the six substrate terms space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network. They are a minimum viable set at a different stratum. No seventh primitive is claimed.
- Not that the framework can tell a reformer where to invest in a named country. That needs a ground reading.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument discriminates the source of a governance score in this derivation.
- Not a theory of everything. No final room: Fig 13 :118 *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."*

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminator, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- **Slot Test:** which capacity's residual is positive in this polity at this time, measured per slot, not inferred from a composite score? Existing administrative readings (registration coverage, assessment coverage, compliance rates, delivery coverage) already return partial signals and are the instrument to sharpen (Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*).
- **Dependency Test:** does the chain break at information, at objective, or at action in this case? Fig 3 :29 *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*
- **Isomer Test:** do two polities with the same four capacities in different orders diverge under the same stress? If they converge, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5:** remove one capacity and keep the outcome; show a context-free rank that predicts the live slot without measurement; show an agent-only improvement that changes contact; show the four are a symmetric conjunction. Any one firing withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The literal ranking question receives a verdict: its shape is not a term the framework holds. The framework says *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"* (Fig 13 :133). The minimum viable set at the institutional stratum is *"a human's careful guess"* at the compression axes, not nature's own list, and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank tax collection, public services, enforcement and information gathering, and it does not claim to know which one your country needs. It says that a minimum viable set has no rank and no dispensable member, so the question "which matters most" is asked in the wrong shape; that the binding capacity is always a per-slot reading in a particular polity, because aggregating slots is how a live signal disappears into an average; that the four are ordered by dependency, with information upstream, not by importance; and that capability belongs to the state and the polity together, so strengthening an agency without changing contact changes nothing. Regimes built from the same four capacities in different orders are isomers, a claim with a stated falsifier. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
