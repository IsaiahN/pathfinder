# Expert verification packet - the Tether on state capacity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scholar of state capacity can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the structure, `specified` on the four-term application, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed to settle which capacity is binding in any polity. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Which form of state capacity matters most: tax collection, public services, enforcement, or information gathering?" is asked in a shape the framework cannot return. A minimum viable set has no rank and no dispensable member, so the four capacities are jointly necessary and unordered. The binding one in any polity is the slot whose residual is positive there and then, which is a measurement, not a rank derivable from a theory. The four are ordered by dependency, with information gathering usually upstream, and capability belongs to the state and the polity together rather than to the state apparatus alone. Regimes built from the same four capacities in different arrangements are isomers, a claim with a stated falsifier. The framework returns the constraints any answer must satisfy, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the slot.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13

- **Statement:** no rank inside a minimum viable set. Each of the four is load-bearing and none is derived from the others.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :4 - *"There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* Also Fig 13 :3 - *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* And Fig 13 :48 - *"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."*
- **Scope note:** Fig 13 governs the six physical substrate terms. The claim here is that the structural law is domain-agnostic and recurs at the institutional stratum with different actors, currency and anchor (Fig 4 :20 - *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*). No seventh primitive is claimed. This scope note is part of the claim, not an afterthought.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a competent governance account in which one of the four can be removed and the remaining three still deliver the same outcome against the same ground; or show that the no-rank law does not recur at this stratum.

### C2 - policy-facing; checkable by a scholar of state capacity against data and literature

- **Statement:** the live capacity is a per-slot reading, never an aggregate; the four are ordered by dependency, not importance; capability is state-and-habitat.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 11 :39 - *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
- **What to check:** does a composite governance score hide which capacity is binding (the metamerism problem: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*)? Is information gathering empirically upstream of assessment and compliance, as Scott (2010) argues: *"To follow the process of state-making, then, is to follow the conquest of illegibility"?* Do Suryanarayan's 2024 findings that *"the various dimensions of state capacity-extractive, coercive, legal-do not necessarily move together"* support per-slot measurement rather than a single rank?
- **Refuter:** a stable, context-free rank that predicts the live slot out of sample without per-slot measurement; or an agent-only reform that leaves contact unchanged yet moves a governance outcome; or evidence that the four capacities commute, so their order makes no difference to outcomes.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a policy-facing test

- **Statement:** the four form a union compound; governance regimes over them can be isomers. "Most important" is a mistyped comparison, not a licensed bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 12 :117 - *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Fig 12 :87 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (the comparison bond ⋛).
- **What to check:** tax collection is a compound of information (who owns what), enforcement (compliance) and services (the return that sustains consent). Two polities with the same four capacities arranged differently should diverge under the same stress. Is that observationally plausible?
- **Refuter:** if two such polities converge to the same governance outcome under the same stress, the isomer claim fails and the weaker functional-group reading stands (Fig 12 :124 - *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*).
- **Open strain, named not hidden:** whether settled capacities count as *"quantities"* at the regime level for a ⋛ comparison is not decidable from the figure text. Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds. They are in different units, so a system holding one accounting cannot compare them."* This needs an instrument, not an argument.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that tax collection, or information gathering, is always the answer. The live slot is a measurement.
- Not that the four capacities are literally any of the six substrate terms.
- Not that the framework can name the binding capacity in a named country without a ground reading.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room (Fig 13 :118 - *"infinite effort inside a finite reach"*).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not that the world sources corroborate independently of one another. The state capacity overview, Scott, Tilly and Suryanarayan overlap heavily in evidence pool and tradition; they make the shape less implausible, not proven.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Slot Test:** which capacity's residual is positive in a named polity at a named time, measured per slot, not inferred from a composite score? Existing administrative readings already return partial signals and are the instrument to sharpen.
2. **Dependency Test:** where does the chain break in that case, information, objective or action? Fig 3 :29 - *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*
3. **Isomer Test:** do two polities with the same four capacities in different orders diverge under the same stress?
