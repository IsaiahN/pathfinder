## 3. Three findings in the wider literature that bear on the pitch

1. **The field itself refuses a single rank at the level of definition.** The *State capacity* overview says there are *"multiple dimensions of state capacity, as well as varied indicators of state capacity"* and lists Centeno's territorial, economic, infrastructural and symbolic types. That is a multidimensional object, which is the shape the figures return. Do not present it as if the field already agreed on one hierarchy.
2. **The dimensions do not move together.** Suryanarayan 2024 says extractive, coercive and legal capacity *"do not necessarily move together"* and calls for *"more nuanced conceptualization and measurement."* This looks like a conflict with Fig 13's lockstep. It is resolved by re-census, not by flipping the verdict: lockstep is structural joint necessity, not measured co-variation. The empirical non-co-movement is what Fig 1's per-slot residual predicts. Cite the apparent conflict openly, because an outside reader will notice it.
3. **Information gathering is a precondition, not just another item.** Scott 2010: *"To follow the process of state-making, then, is to follow the conquest of illegibility."* This is the lowest-overlap, highest-value corroboration for the dependency reading (Fig 3 :20). Tilly supplies the coercion-and-extraction side. Neither proves the figure verdict; both make it less implausible.

## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the no-rank claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A send selected for people already sympathetic produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Later agreement is never pooled.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and never re-scored afterwards.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* Each user writes a refuter before running it and labels the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 is live, not decorative. If it fires, the shape is withdrawn and no links are offered.
