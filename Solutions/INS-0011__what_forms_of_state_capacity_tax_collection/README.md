## Residual in effect terms

The decision is an allocation call. A reformer wants to know where to put scarce effort, and the question asks for a context-free ordinal ranking of four capacities. The effect of declaring one "most important" is that effort flows there and the others are treated as secondary. The figure-grounded derivation shows that this shape (a single rank) is not a term the framework holds, and that the live answer is always a per-case measurement of which slot carries positive residual. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 and `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the derivation.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to share a pool.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid both.
