## 0. The question under audit

> What forms of state capacity, tax collection, public services, enforcement, or information gathering, matter most for effective governance?

Restated as an effect: someone must allocate scarce reform effort. The question asks for a context-free ordinal ranking of four named capacities by their contribution to "effective governance." Option A: answer the ranking as posed, for example "tax collection matters most because revenue funds the rest." Option B: refuse the ranking as the wrong shape and return the constraints any answer must satisfy plus the measurement that would locate the live slot. What each leads to is recorded in section 7 and in `09_watson_answer.md`.

The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per Fig 5 :65: *"Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* Bound shape: **a single, stable, context-free ordinal ranking of the four capacities, such that knowing the rank tells a reformer where to invest without measuring the case.** That is the term under test. If it cannot be derived, the honest output is a refusal of the term plus a shape, not a substitute name.

## 1. Residual, stated in effect terms

**R (the residual).** A reformer predicts that investing in capacity X will improve governance. The observation is the governance outcome in a particular polity at a particular time. R is the gap. The residual is not one number. It has four named slots plus a contact slot (the state and the polity it governs).

- **Arity.** Four named capacity slots plus at least one habitat/contact slot. Under Symbols table, *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."*
- **Symmetry.** The capacities are not interchangeable. Swapping information gathering for enforcement changes the mechanism. Under Fig 12's tests, their relation is ordered (sequence, Fig 12 :71 *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*), not the symmetric conjunction where *"swap the operands. Does the meaning change? No"* (Fig 12 :59). A single additive score treats them as if the symmetric bond held. That is a type question, not a policy preference.
- **Scale.** The residual is read at the scale of the state-and-polity system, not the state apparatus in isolation (Fig 11 :39). The outcome is a system-level reading, but the live signal is per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- **What is missing or wrong.** Missing: an instrument that says which slot carries positive residual in this case. Wrong: the assumption that a context-free rank is a term the framework can return. Formula step 2 names the second as a refusal, not an explanation: *"REFUTED - a term held is false; what is owed is a refusal, not an explanation."*

**Why this is a union, not a partition.** Under Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"* and :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Tax collection needs information (you cannot assess what you cannot see, Scott 2010). Each capacity is a condition that overlaps the others; a case in two conditions is a specific compound, not a harder case of either. Fig 9 :57: *"it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* The four must be compounded and then measured per slot, not ranked as a list.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Information gathering is link 1 (perception: *"the system can tell what is in front of it, and tell it apart from what was there before"*, Fig 3 :5). Public services play the role of link 3, the objective, *"it can say what would count as progress here, in its own terms"* (Fig 3 :10). Enforcement is link 4/5, planning and action, *"a route to that objective, chosen under incomplete information"* (Fig 3 :13). Tax collection is the currency that feeds every link (Fig 4 :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*; Fig 13 :14 *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* for energy, :22 *"a difference that can be spent"* for gradient). This mapping is a binding from the institutional frame onto figure terms, declared as such, `specified`. It is not a derivation of new primitives.

Order by dependency: settle D2 (habitat enumerated) before D1; run D3's no-rank law before ranking anything; D4 reads last. Refuse the rank before minting a ranked answer (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27).

## 4. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The literal question has the wrong shape (decisive)

Fig 13 :4: *"There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* Fig 13 :3: *"No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."* Fig 13 :48: *"And the set is minimal: remove one and something stops."*

The minimum viable set has no rank and no dispensable member. Applied at the institutional stratum under Fig 4 :20 (*"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*), the four capacities form a minimum viable set for effective governance: remove information gathering and the rest go blind; remove tax collection and the gradient that can be spent disappears; remove enforcement and decisions do not change contact; remove public services and the objective has no visible referent. There is no senior term.

This is **not** a claim that the four capacities are literally the six substrate primitives space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network. They are not. The claim is that the structural law (no rank, joint necessity, lockstep) is domain-agnostic and recurs at the institutional stratum, with different actors, currency and anchor. Mode: `general` for the law, `specified` for the four-term application.

**Refuter:** exhibit a competent governance account in which one of the four can be removed and the remaining three still deliver the same governance outcome against the same ground. If that succeeds, the set is not minimal at this stratum and F1 falls.

### F2 - "Most important" is an aggregation that erases the live signal

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

A single ordinal rank is a reduction of four slots to one number. That is exactly the move Fig 1 forbids. A polity can have excellent revenue collection and a catastrophic information failure; the aggregate looks fine while one slot is on fire. The framework's answer to "which matters most" is therefore: the slot whose residual R⁺_s is positive here, and that is a measurement owed to the ground, not a rank derivable from the theory.

**Refuter:** demonstrate a stable rank that predicts which slot is live across polities and years without measuring R per slot. If such a rank exists and predicts out of sample, Fig 1's per-slot rule is not governing this domain and F2 falls.

### F3 - Dependency, not importance

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :29: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*

The four capacities are a dependency chain, not a leaderboard. Effort spent above the broken link does not arrive. The reformer's first question is where the chain currently stops, which is a reading, not a ranking. Scott (2010) supplies the low-overlap corroboration: *"To follow the process of state-making, then, is to follow the conquest of illegibility."* Information gathering is upstream of assessment and enforcement, which is why a state that cannot see cannot tax or compel reliably.

**Refuter:** show that the four capacities are related by the symmetric conjunction *"both hold at once"* (Fig 12 :57) rather than by sequence, so that any order change leaves the outcome unchanged. Then Fig 3 does not govern and the ranking question is at least well posed.

### F4 - Capability is state-and-habitat, not state-alone

Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and :40 *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*

"State capacity" is a relational property of a state and the polity it acts on. A reform that enlarges a ministry without changing what the state can perceive, extract, compel or deliver has changed the agent, not the contact, and has therefore changed nothing that governance can register. This is also why no capacity carries a standing value: information gathering is a benefactor to a tax reform and a malfactor to a repression programme, which is a relation to a residual (Fig 11 :50).

World corroboration from a genuinely different field: Gibson (1979), via the Wikipedia *Affordance* page, *"The affordances of the environment are what it offers the animal, what it provides or furnishes, either for good or ill. ... It implies the complementarity of the animal and the environment."* Low overlap with the state-capacity literature.

**Refuter:** produce a case where an agent-only improvement, demonstrably leaving contact unchanged, moves a governance outcome against the ground. That would break Fig 11 :40.

### F5 - The union cannot be ranked as a list

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"it stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

The four capacities overlap. Tax administration is a compound of information (who owns what), enforcement (compliance) and services (the return that sustains consent). Ranking them as if they were independent list items is the collapse Fig 9 names. A reform that clears the region where only one condition holds leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds.

**Refuter:** show that the four capacities can be operationalised as independent, non-overlapping variables with no interaction terms and that the rank still predicts outcomes. Then the union reading falls.

### F6 - The figure mapping must be declared as a binding, not an import that moves a wall

The four named capacities are not in Γ as framework primitives. Mapping information gathering to perception, tax collection to currency/energy/gradient, enforcement to action, and public services to objective is a binding from the institutional frame onto figure terms. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."* Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* No new primitive is added; the wall does not move. Calling the mapping a derivation of a seventh capacity would be the error the figures forbid.

**Refuter:** derive a new capacity from the six that is not already a composition of them. Then the wall does move and this binding is not merely a binding.

### F7 - Agreement among reformers cannot settle the rank

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

A panel chosen for receptiveness to a "tax first" thesis is a panel selected to share the conclusion. The verdict must come from a ground, meaning an instrument or a case outcome, not from the panel. This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks what would refute the shape claim before showing any theory, and why a later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Refuter:** exhibit two low-overlap instruments, one administrative and one survey based, that independently return the same live slot in the same polity, with the overlap measured and declared. That would be triangulation, and it would begin to settle a case rather than the general rank.

### F8 - A conflict with the literature sends us back to census, not to a new verdict

Suryanarayan (2024) writes in the abstract of *Endogenous State Capacity*: *"the various dimensions of state capacity-extractive, coercive, legal-do not necessarily move together. The findings point to the need for more nuanced conceptualization and measurement of state capacity."* Read naively, "dimensions do not move together" appears to conflict with Fig 13 :4's *"in lockstep."* The conflict rule is absolute: a conflict sends us back to the census, it never flips the verdict. Re-census resolves it. Fig 13's lockstep is a structural claim about a minimum viable set (joint necessity, no senior member). It is not a dynamical claim that every member's measured value co-varies. The figures already distinguish the set from its instantiations: Fig 13 :74 *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one"* and :118 *"infinite effort inside a finite reach."* Suryanarayan's non-co-movement is exactly what Fig 1 predicts: per-slot residuals that do not aggregate. The apparent conflict becomes corroboration after re-census.

**Refuter:** show that "lockstep" in the figures is intended as co-variation of measured values, not joint structural necessity. Then F8's resolution fails and the verdict must be reopened.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the standing instruction, a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A capacity can be removed from the four and the same governance outcome still be derived against the same ground (breaks Fig 13 :3 and :48).
2. A context-free, stable ordinal ranking is demonstrated that predicts which slot is live across cases without per-slot measurement (breaks Fig 1 :19 and :22).
3. An agent-only improvement is shown to change contact while leaving the state-habitat relation unchanged (breaks Fig 11 :40).
4. The four capacities are shown to be a symmetric conjunction rather than an ordered dependency chain (breaks Fig 3 :20 and the bond tests at Fig 12 :59).
5. An instrument returns the same live slot from two demonstrably low-overlap frames in the same polity. This does **not** refute the shape; it begins to settle a case, and is recorded here so the distinction between refuting the shape and filling the slot is visible before the run.

If 1 to 4 fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Strains and re-census

- **Fig 13 "no rank" versus Fig 3 "ordered by dependency."** Not strained after re-census. Fig 13 governs the six substrate primitives and the structural law of a minimum viable set. Fig 3 governs the five links of an agent's perception-to-action chain. Different objects. The same law recurs, but at different strata, which is the domain-agnostic reading in the figures README.
- **Fig 13 "in lockstep" versus Suryanarayan's non-co-moving dimensions.** Strained on a naive reading, dissolved by re-census in F8. Lockstep is structural joint necessity, not measured co-variation. Recorded, not hidden.
- **Mapping four institutional capacities onto abstract figure terms.** Strained if read as a derivation. Declared as a binding from the institutional frame, `specified`, adds no atom (F6). No re-census owed because no new primitive was claimed.
- **Fig 1 "per slot" versus the policy demand for one answer.** This is the residual itself, not a strain. The framework refuses the one-answer shape and returns the per-slot rule plus the instrument owed.
- **After re-census: two named strains, both resolved by distinguishing objects or strata; none hidden.**

## 7. Recommendation

1. Do not answer the ranking as posed. State the refusal first: no rank inside a minimum viable set, live slot per case.
2. Re-state the practical question as: which slot carries positive residual in this polity now, and what instrument would read it? Fig 3 :29: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*
3. Keep the four verdicts separate and never average them (Fig 1 :19). Report tax collection, public services, enforcement and information gathering on four rows with four anchors.
4. Put the refuter in front of the links. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
5. Treat any later agreement as a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*).

### Three-question stop (formula step 7)

1. **SEARCHED.** Is a general rank already in the institutional closure and merely unfound? No. Fig 13 :4 forbids rank inside a minimum set; Fig 1 :19 forbids aggregating slots. More search inside the frame will not produce one.
2. **INWARD.** Can the instrument hold the per-slot residual? Yes in principle: administrative data (registration coverage, assessment coverage, compliance rates, delivery coverage) already return partial readings. The instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*). This is the live route.
3. **OUTWARD.** Does another frame already hold a rank? The field offers many, but as case-specific findings (Tilly 1975 on war and extraction; Scott 2010 on legibility; Suryanarayan 2024 on non-co-movement). Their disagreement across cases is itself evidence that the rank is case-bound, not general. No import of a new primitive is owed.

## 9. Updated verdict

The literal question is refused as the wrong shape. **Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence** (Fig 13 :134). What is returned is a shape with four constraints: no rank inside a minimum viable set; per-slot residual, never an aggregate; dependency order, not importance; capability as state-and-habitat. The live capacity in any actual polity is the slot whose residual is positive, and that is owed to a ground reading, not to a theory. Mode `general` on the structure, `specified` on the four-term application, `ungrounded` throughout. After re-census, two strains named and resolved, none hidden. The pre-registered refuter in section 5 is unchanged and unclosed.
