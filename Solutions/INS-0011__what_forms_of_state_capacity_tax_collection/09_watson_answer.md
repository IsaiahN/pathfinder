## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a request for a single ordinal rank and what the observation can support. Arity: four named capacity slots plus a state-habitat contact slot, so not one number. Symmetry: the capacities are not interchangeable; their relation is ordered dependency (Fig 3), not the symmetric conjunction where swapping operands changes nothing (Fig 12 :59). Scale: read at the state-and-polity system, but the live signal is per slot (Fig 1 :19).
- **Domain 1, state-as-agent.** Actors: bureaucracy, officials, rules. Currency: decisions and actions. Anchor: actual outcomes in the polity (compliance, delivery, revenue, registration coverage, conflict). Question type: one link in a chain, downstream of D2 and D3.
- **Domain 2, habitat.** Actors: citizens, firms, local knowledge, terrain, other states. Currency: resources, compliance, information, legitimacy. Anchor: the ground, what actually happens. Question type: enumerated, never composed (Fig 11 :43). Upstream of D1.
- **Domain 3, composition and reach.** Actors: the four capacities as terms in Γ, the bonds among them. Currency: composition and settlement. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any reading. Question type: union compound with its own loop; unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Domain 4, measurement and verification.** Actors: reformer or evaluator, instruments. Currency: residuals and readings. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: separate seat; reads last.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat before assessing the agent; apply the no-rank law before ranking anything; refuse the rank before minting a ranked answer (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27).

Strain test applied: Fig 13 "no rank" governs the six substrate primitives and the structural law of a minimum viable set; Fig 3 "ordered by dependency" governs the perception-to-action links. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. Suryanarayan 2024 says the dimensions *"do not necessarily move together"*; read naively this conflicts with Fig 13's lockstep. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The literal ranking question is refused as the wrong shape. **Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence** (Fig 13 :134). What is returned is a shape: no rank inside a minimum viable set, so the four capacities are jointly necessary and unordered; the binding capacity in any polity is the slot with positive residual, a measurement, not a rank; the four are ordered by dependency with information usually upstream, not by importance; capability belongs to state and polity together; and regimes over the same four capacities in different orders are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier. "Most important" is a mistyped comparison applied across kinds, not a licensed bond. Mode `general` on the structure, `specified` on the four-term application, `ungrounded` throughout.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking as if the figures produced it, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters. A second cost is practical: a reformer who follows a false general rank invests above a broken link, and Fig 3 :19 says "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- **undoable:** yes. The rank is removed without touching the structural derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The open slot is designed to be filled case by case without changing the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates which capacity produced a governance score would name the live slot; a stable context-free rank that predicts the slot out of sample without per-slot measurement would break the no-rank verdict; a case in which one capacity can be removed with the outcome unchanged would break minimality; an agent-only improvement that changes contact would break Fig 11 :40; a demonstration that the four commute would break the dependency order. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
