## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework reports *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel* (re-fetched 2026-11-23): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can settle it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **The shape:** four jointly necessary, unordered capacities, ordered by dependency not importance, with the binding slot read per case, and capability located in state-and-habitat.
- **A name would be:** "tax collection matters most." That fills the slot and is exactly what the framework declines to do.

The distinction matters because the question is an invitation to name. The essays' whole discipline is to resist that invitation until a ground pays.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (re-fetched): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` section 1 is stated in effects (allocate effort, live slot, contact) before any figure line is chosen.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (exemplar record): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions (re-fetched): an exhaustive search that fails means *"the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed"*, possibly *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one."* This is precisely the Fig 9 union finding in `01` F5. The question "which form matters most" is malformed in the same way: it asks for one rank over a compound.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that would mean an instrument returning which slot is live in a named polity at a named time.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (exemplar record) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Equally, the claim that tax collection, public services, enforcement and information gathering form a minimum viable set for governance is a visible guess at the institutional compression axes. Declaring it makes the choice visible; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why a ranked answer is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (re-fetched 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a governance index (government effectiveness, or a composite state capacity score) reads a single number and cannot say which capacity produced it. Several configurations of the four can yield the same score. Naming "tax collection" as the source of a high score is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name, and still less a rank.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (exemplar record): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: what does "most important" mean under the essays? ANSWERED

**What licenses one capacity as most important in general? Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape: no rank in a minimum set, per-slot residual, dependency order, capability as state-and-habitat. A particular capacity is licensed as the binding one only when a ground reading returns positive residual on its slot in a named case, or when a provenanced import with a shadow test supplies the instrument. Until then, naming a winner is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does a ranked answer commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, in two ways. First, it names a winner where the framework returns an unordered minimum set. Second, it infers the winner from an aggregate governance reading, which is the metamerism error. The correct form is four rows with four slots and the instrument owed: `which slot is live here?`.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a missing primitive, one not held in Γ. Tax collection, public services, enforcement and information gathering are not missing framework primitives; they are terms from the institutional frame bound onto figure structure at a different stratum (Fig 4 :20 *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change"*). Naming them is not inventing a seventh member.

What remains wrong in a ranked answer is not the naming of the four, but the ranking and the source inference. My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a composite governance score still cannot comment on which capacity produced it. But the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held or frame-supplied terms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The same correction applies to my §5. The answer is not "the framework cannot name any capacity." It is "the framework cannot rank them, and cannot name the binding one without a ground reading that discriminates among sources."

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (exemplar record): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree that tax collection comes first is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground, meaning an instrument or a case outcome, settle which slot is live.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. The honest stop for this question

*They're Scanning Books* (exemplar record): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The missing thing here is a dimension of measurement, a per-slot discriminator for governance outcomes. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Existing administrative indicators already return partial readings (registration coverage, assessment coverage, compliance rates, delivery coverage), so this is INWARD work, not a hoped-for import. The answer stops at the limit of what those instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
