## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, the condition under which a rule changes a person's values (Effect A) and the condition under which a person follows a rule without adopting its underlying assumption (Effect B). Option A: treat compliance and endorsement as evidence that values changed. Option B: treat a declared rule as a convention until its content crosses into the person's library as a grounded atom, then require persistence when the contingency is removed as the discriminator. What each leads to: A mistakes a convention product for a verdict and authors a constant that decides an outcome; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a rule as declared meaning and a rule as grounded term in a person's library. Arity: two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question; a person can comply with one rule and internalize another. Symmetry: asymmetric, the institution can change the person's frame without the person changing the institution's (Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."). Scale: individual (one person's Γ) and population (a convention across rooms), with a lossy transform between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, the institution's rule-authoring seat.** Actors: rule authors, officials, the institution. Currency: declared meanings, conventions, authority. Anchor: none for the convention itself, which makes no claim about the world; the ground for its effects is measured behavior. Question type: the source side of the union.
- **Domain 2, the person's internal frame.** Actors: Γ, b, w, action a, observation o'. Currency: residuals, imports, settlement. Anchor: experience and outcome for what the person actually holds. Question type: the target side; import is the crossing.
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: experimenters, measures, outside frames. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: behavior when the audience, sanction and reward are removed. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Declare (1) before contact (2); settle (3) before discriminating (4) (Fig 3); refuse the term "agreement equals internalization" before minting a mechanism (Formula step 2); describe R before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no mechanism name or number is licensed. A declared rule is a convention and makes no claim about the world, so compliance and endorsement are its product, not evidence of value change. A rule changes a person's values only when its content crosses into the person's library as a grounded atom from a frame whose closure differs, the weighting w shifts so that action is generated from the held reason, and the content settles against experience. The signature is persistence when the audience, sanction and reward are removed, a cycle rather than an open chain. Enforcement alone turns the convention into an authored constant. Verdicts are per person-rule slot; population agreement is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict. This is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by treating their agreement as corroboration, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The compliance/internalization split is separable and the authored constant can be re-declared as a convention. No primitive must be withdrawn; a superseding packet can replace a sent one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator showing value change with no import or w shift; a grounded reason whose action fails to persist when the contingency is removed; a population agreement measure that genuinely discriminates the two effects; or a re-census admitting a figure line that collapses the convention/atom distinction. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
