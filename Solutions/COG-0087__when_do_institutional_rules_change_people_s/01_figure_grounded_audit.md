## 0. The residual, in effect terms

We observe two different effects of an institutional rule on a person.

- **Effect A, value change.** The rule's content becomes a reason that persists when the audience, sanction or reward is removed, and is integrated with what the person already holds. The person would act the same way unobserved, and would treat the content as a reason to give.
- **Effect B, compliance without adoption.** Behavior tracks the rule, but the underlying assumption is not taken as the person's own. Remove the contingency and the behavior can change or disappear.

The residual R is the gap between the rule as an external declared meaning and the rule as a grounded term in the person's library Γ. The question asks for the condition that closes R.

**Per-slot discipline.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The slot here is a person-rule pair. A population compliance rate is a frame-internal aggregate that can hide a live slot; Fig 1 :22 warns that aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average. Verdicts below are kept per domain and never averaged into a claim that the institution changed values.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, Formula step Direction).** A condition C such that: when C holds, the rule's content crosses into Γ as a grounded atom and the weighting w shifts (Effect A); when C fails, the rule changes only the action a and the observation o' while Γ, b and w stay unchanged (Effect B). Binding C lets it accrue a residual and be refused.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: two conditions (a rule is present; a person's valuation is at stake), so the split is a **union**, not a partition. The same person can comply with one rule and internalize another, and a single rule can be partly internalized. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single lever (sanctions) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. Symmetry: asymmetric. The institution can change the person's frame without the person changing the institution's frame (Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not"). Scale: individual (one person's Γ) and population (the convention held across rooms); cross-scale via the membrane, where Fig 4 :19-20 says only the actors, currency and anchor change.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - A declared rule is not a grounded value (governs Effect B)

Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"
Fig 10 :49: "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
Fig 10 :46: "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."
Fig 10 :45: "It does author conventions, because nothing else can see across a seam."

An institutional rule is authored by the seat as a convention, a shared meaning across a seam. That is its legitimate office. But a convention makes no claim about the world, so a rule that is obeyed because it was declared has not thereby become a value. This is the figure-grounded core of "follow rules without adopting their underlying assumptions": the rule coordinates action while the reason the institution gives for it is not held by the person.

**Strain test.** One might strain to read "convention" as only an informal norm, excluding formal institutional rules. Re-census: Fig 10 uses "convention" for any declared meaning the seat authors to bridge a seam, which includes a written institutional rule. No strain after re-census.

**Verdict: triangulated.** Fig 10 (convention authoring, low overlap with) Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Two figures, different quantities, same boundary.

### F2 - Compliance agreement is the product of a convention, not evidence of value change

Fig 2 :39: "Correlation has valence."
Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"
Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

This is the sharpest answer to the question as posed. "Everyone follows the rule" is exactly what a working convention looks like; it is the product, not a corruption. But if the question is a verdict about the world (did the person's values change?), agreement about the shared meaning cannot count. A survey in which people endorse the rule's stated justification is a reading of the convention, not an anchor for the verdict. Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

**Refuter for F2:** show a population agreement measure that discriminates a person who holds the reason from one who does not, when the contingency is removed. If such a measure exists, agreement can carry verdict weight here and F2 is narrowed.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 2 and Fig 7, different figures).

### F3 - Value change requires an import that adds an atom

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."
Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
Fig 6 :14: "the only operator that moves the wall"

Composing the rule's sentence with terms the person already holds changes what the person can say and do, but adds nothing new to Γ. To change values, the rule's content must cross in as an atom from a frame whose closure differs: another person, a cohort, an institution whose frame already holds the reason. This is why contact, not the rule text, is the condition. Fig 6 :30 (outward route): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." The description that travels is the effect the rule produces, not the institution's preferred wording.

**Strain test.** The person already knows the rule, so how can its content be a new atom? Re-census: the atom is the grounded reason or "ought," not the sentence. Fig 10 :18 ("a declared meaning is not a grounded one") resolves it: knowing the declared meaning is not holding the grounded atom. Fig 8 :51-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Provenance of the reason is therefore part of the observable.

**Refuter for F3:** show a rule changing a held value with no contact with a differing closure and no new atom entering Γ (pure composition suffices). Breaks the import condition.

**Verdict: single-source at the figure level (Fig 6), triangulated with Fig 10 and the Symbols w line below (low overlap: closure walls vs authored meanings vs belief weighting).**

### F4 - The mechanism is a shift in the weighting w

Symbols table :23-25: "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings."
Symbols table :20-21: "Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation."

The belief b is what the person takes to be the case before acting, formed from history and inherited prior, mixed by w. It is not the observation. So the internal transition named by Effect A is a shift in w: the rule's content moves from being an external contingency (the action channel) into the belief that generates action. Because w is learnable from outcomes of past weightings, this shift is a ground-driven process, not a declaration. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A rule that changes behavior without changing contact (w and Γ unchanged) has not made the person more capable of the value; it has only changed the action.

**Mode:** `specified`. We can state the condition; no measurement here fixes the value of w at which a convention becomes a value.

**Refuter for F4:** show a grounded atom whose action does NOT persist when the external contingency is removed, or show behavior persisting after contingency removal with no shift in w. Either breaks the weighting condition.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Symbols w and b, Fig 11 contact, Fig 12 cycle, different quantities).

### F5 - A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one

Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
Fig 10 :48: "a convention nothing can check is a constant the seat authored, carrying the seat's authority."

This is the guard on the compliance branch. When enforcement makes the rule decide outcomes, it is no longer purely a coordination convention; it has become a constant inside the person's frame, carrying the institution's authority rather than a reason the person holds. Sanctions therefore produce a characteristic failure that the framework names: the rule is present and effective, but it is an authored constant, not a grounded atom. Fig 10 :53: "A prohibition nothing can satisfy is a prohibition stated wrong." Applied here: a rule whose stated reason no real occupant could endorse is an idealisation, and inferences drawn from nominal compliance will be drawn by whoever finds them useful.

**Refuter for F5:** show a rule that decides outcomes through enforcement and still functions as a pure coordination convention with no authored constant in the person's frame. Breaks the guard.

**Verdict: single-source (Fig 10), consistent with the seat-map law at line 163 ("a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one").**

### F6 - Structural analogue: cycle versus chain, catalyst, functional group

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use."
Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

An internalized value is a cycle: the behavior it produces re-enters as input and reinforces the value, and the part survives its own use (catalyst). A compliance-only rule is a chain: behavior stops when the external input stops, and a chain cannot close by construction. This is an analogue, not a new claim; its own falsifier is stated in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**Verdict: single-source (Fig 12), corroborated by world source Bicchieri 2014 on unconditional vs conditional preference (world sources are secondary only).**

### F7 - Population rates are filters, not witnesses

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."
Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Compliance rates, survey endorsement and "culture change" indices are budget filters (where to look). They are not witnesses for the verdict about any person-rule slot. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." For this question the anchor must be something that cannot move when asked: behavior under removal of the audience or sanction, plus a measure that discriminates conditional from unconditional reasons.

**Verdict: triangulated** (Fig 1, Fig 2, Fig 9).

### F8 - Cross-scale: rooms, generations, and the lossy transform

Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."
Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

An institution outlives the people who founded it. Passing a rule along a chain of agreeing rooms (cohorts, generations) compounds agreement without adding evidence. A room in which the rule changed values must differ from the last in the relevant closure and match the residual the last room surfaced. Transferred values are not recordings: Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." What can cross between scales is a method for producing the value, never a replay of one person's compliance.

**Verdict: single-source for the population-scale reading (Fig 7), consistent with Fig 4's membrane rule.**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim: a rule changes a person's values iff its content crosses into Γ as a grounded atom from a frame whose closure differs, the weighting w shifts so action is generated from the held belief, and the content settles against the ground. A rule is followed without adoption when it remains a declared convention or authored constant that changes only the action channel.

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. A rule changes a held value with no import from a differing closure and no change in w (pure composition inside Γ suffices). Breaks F3 and F4.
2. A grounded atom exists whose action does not persist when the external contingency is removed, or behavior persists after removal with no shift in w. Breaks F4.
3. A population agreement measure is shown to be a verdict about values rather than a convention product. Breaks F2.
4. A rule that decides outcomes through enforcement still functions as a pure convention with no authored constant. Breaks F5.
5. A re-census finds a figure line governing a different quantity that dissolves the convention/atom distinction.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the two effects as separate slots (Fig 1 :19). Never report a population claim that values changed from a compliance rate.
2. Lead with the discriminator: behavior when the audience, sanction or reward is removed (Fig 2 :29, anchor test).
3. Treat declared reasons as conventions until provenance shows an import with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :51-52).
4. Price enforcement honestly: it produces compliance and authors constants; it is not a substitute for the contact that moves w (Fig 10 :47, Fig 11 :40).
5. Unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54). A lever that clears compliance-only cases leaves the intersection standing.
6. Send the generator, not the recording: the shape claim and its refuter first, links only after a verdict is recorded (Fig 4 :22, Fig 10 :52).

### Final audit verdict

The shape survives: the discriminator between value change and mere compliance is whether the rule's content has crossed into the person's library as a grounded atom from a differing closure, with w shifted and the content settled, so behavior persists without the contingency. Compliance alone is a declared convention or authored constant acting on the action channel. Verdicts are per person-rule slot; population agreement is a convention product, not a witness. Mode `specified`; no ground reading. Refuters are pre-registered above. Strains after re-census: none live; one gap (no figure line sets the numeric threshold of w).
