## Two things in the literature that bear directly on the shape claim

1. **Internalization is not a single step.** SDT separates introjection (taken in but not accepted as one's own) from integration (assimilated with the self), and Tyler separates normative from instrumental compliance. Both are the same two-effect split the figures make between a declared meaning and a grounded atom. They corroborate the shape but, being a different evidence pool with different methods, they are usable as secondary witnesses only.
2. **Conditional preference is the discriminator.** Bicchieri's "socially unconditional" norm and the SEP convention case (a fork placement is followed because others follow it) give an instrument-level reading of the figure distinction: remove the expectation of others' conformity and see whether the behavior remains. This is the ground test the Tether asks for, arrived at independently.

A declared overlap is recorded: the three Isaiah essays share the author and evidence pool of the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2:** Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the two-effect distinction without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction:** once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted:** Fig 4 :22, only methods cross up. Each user must state their own ground and write their own refuter before running the loop.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted it is a shape claim with three refuters, not a result. If a refuter fires, stop and retract before sending anything further.
