## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: "a shape, not a name." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures add that the framework "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands" but "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For COG-0087 the shape is:

> A rule changes values only when its content crosses into what the person holds as a grounded atom, from a frame whose closure differs, with the weighting w shifted so that the behavior is generated from the held reason; it is followed without adoption when it remains a declared convention acting on the action channel.

That is a shape. "Self-determination theory," "procedural justice legitimacy," "the norm internalization literature," and "w exceeds 0.6" are names or numbers. The framework does not supply them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." and Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect terms are "behavior persists when the contingency is removed" and "the reason is treated as the person's own." Those are checkable by anyone, whatever vocabulary they use for internalization.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "It stops when nothing you have registers a difference from it." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." And: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."

So the world literature on internalization is an import from a frame whose closure differs. It is welcome as corroboration, but the specific vocabulary it uses is its own contribution and must be recorded as such. The Tether does not derive "introjection," "integration," "legitimacy" or "conditional preference." It derives the shape those accounts fill.

## 4. The source-discrimination point for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: observed compliance is a projection. Several sources produce it: a held reason, a fear of sanctions, a desire for approval, a habit with no reason at all. An endorsement survey reads the projection and cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a discriminator (behavior under removal of the audience or sanction, plus a conditional/unconditional preference measure), not a name for the source. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." Applied here: a population that agrees the rule is legitimate is not a validator for whether any person holds the reason. "You may all agree and be wrong."

## 5. Q: Does the shape answer the question?

**Yes, at the shape level.** The question asks *when*. The answer gives the condition class: when the content crosses from declared meaning to grounded atom through contact with a differing closure, shifting w and settling against the ground, with persistence as the signature. When that crossing does not occur, the rule changes action but not value.

**No, at the number level.** No essay and no figure line gives the value of w at which the crossing counts as complete, or the number of unobserved trials required to distinguish a cycle from a chain. That is an instrument gap, recorded as such, not a silence filled with a guess.

**No, at the named-mechanism level.** Which psychological process carries the crossing (identification, procedural legitimacy, moralization, habit) is an import from a world frame. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 6. Correction to my own earlier reading

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any particular mental term in this answer. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." In COG-0087 the missing thing is the grounded reason in the person's Γ, which is genuinely not held before the crossing, so the rule applies with full force to *that* slot. But the framework's own atoms and bond vocabulary (Γ, b, w, import, convention, atom) are held terms and may be named; they are the grammar, not the missing content. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: the word "simulacra" appears in the Medium architecture article (2026-08-19: "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra"), not in the 2026-11-19 Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay. I cite the exact validation sentences from the latter and do not attribute the word to it.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." An institution's authority, or the eminence of a scholar who endorses the shape, gives the claim a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether people's values changed.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The Medium article adds that such outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such.
