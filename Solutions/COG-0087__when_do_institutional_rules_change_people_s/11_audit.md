## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as "The gap between what was sent up and what came back down: gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)). Measures what the coarser description cannot hold." Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the paired observation that rules sometimes coincide with changed values and sometimes only with changed behavior, across person-rule slots.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shape claim in `07`, a crossing from declared meaning to grounded atom with w shifted and persistence as the signature.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a specific person, rule, contingency and removal observation.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which frame supplied the reason atom, and whether its provenance was recorded;
- whether the atom actually settled against the ground, rather than merely being believed;
- the sign of the cycle (self-maintaining value versus self-defeating obligation), which the behavior reading alone does not show;
- the slot-level residual that tells a researcher where to look.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure (here, a grounding crossing) must sit between a declared rule and a held value, and compliance alone does not supply it. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
