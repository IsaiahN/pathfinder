# Expert verification packet: the Tether on institutional rules and values

**What this is:** a claim in a form an institutional analyst or behavioral researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the conditionals, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A declared rule is a convention: it coordinates behavior and makes no claim about the world, so obeying it does not show that anyone adopted its reason. A rule changes a person's values only when its content crosses into the person's library as a grounded atom from a frame whose closure differs, the weighting w shifts so that behavior is generated from the held reason, and the content settles against experience. The signature is persistence when the audience, sanction and reward are removed, a cycle rather than an external chain. When that crossing fails, the rule changes action but not values, and enforcement can turn the convention into an authored constant. Compliance rates and endorsement surveys are filters for where to look, not witnesses for what anyone holds.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Fig 10 and Fig 2

- **Statement:** a declared meaning is not a grounded value, and agreement about a shared meaning is the product of a convention, not a verdict about the world.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"; Fig 10 :46 "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."; Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"; Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **What to check:** are these two lines about different quantities (authoring a convention versus the valence of correlation), so that no re-census is needed? A reading that makes them contradict each other has misidentified the domain.
- **Refuter:** show a population agreement measure that discriminates a person who holds the reason from one who does not when the contingency is removed. If such a measure exists, agreement can carry verdict weight here and C1 is narrowed.

### C2 - Behavioral and institutional facing; checkable against the empirical literature

- **Statement:** the internal transition is a shift in w, and the observable signature is persistence of the behavior after the external contingency is removed. Verbatim support: Symbols table :23-25 "How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** does the world literature support the two-effect split? Deci, Eghrari, Patrick and Leone (1994, Journal of Personality) distinguish introjection, taking in a value "but not accepting it as one's own," from integration, assimilation "with one's core sense of self." Tyler (1990, Yale University Press) distinguishes normative commitment from instrumental compliance "dictated by the estimate of the likelihood that they will be punished." Bicchieri (2016, OUP) defines a norm as internalized when it becomes "an independent motivation to conform," and separates conditional from socially unconditional reasons. These are secondary corroboration from different evidence pools, not sources of the verdict.
- **Refuter:** show value change with no contact with a differing frame and no shift in w; or show a grounded reason whose action does not persist when the contingency is removed; or show behavior that persists after removal with no shift in w.

### C3 - Framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Fig 9 and Fig 12

- **Statement:** the two effects are a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled, structurally isomeric at the person-rule level, and ordered declare, contact, settle, discriminate. Verbatim support: Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."; Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."; Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
- **What to check:** is the split genuinely overlapping (a person can comply with one rule and internalize another)? Does the behavior reading alone leave the internal bond undetermined, as the metamerism case predicts?
- **Refuter:** show that every person-rule pair is determinately one effect or the other; or show that behavior plus stated reason determines the internal bond; or show that the discriminator can run before settlement.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28): a reading cannot comment on its source; the spectrometer resolved the color case. Here:

1. **Removal test:** behavior when the audience, sanction and reward are removed.
2. **Conditional/unconditional test:** whether the reason for acting depends on expectations about peers.
3. **Provenance test:** where the reason came from and when it entered the person's frame.
4. **Standing refuters in section 2 above.** Any one firing retracts the shape.
