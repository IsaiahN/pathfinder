# The Tether's answer: can a signal carry information if no system can detect or interpret it?

**Mode: `specified` on the four per-domain verdicts, `general` on the four-domain split, `gap` on naming content with no frame. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks whether removing every detector and interpreter removes information from a signal. Stated in effect terms: Option A says carriage is a property of signal and source, so the removal changes nothing about what the signal carries; Option B says information is a three-place relation and removing the third term removes the information, leaving only a physical pattern. The decision turns on whether "carrying", "being measurable", "being about something" and "being recoverable" are one quantity or four.

They are four. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The question is a union of four overlapping conditions, so the binary is refused as written.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **D1, the carrier. Yes, with no interpreter required.** A term carries its atoms and the bonds between them: Fig 13 :130 "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." And Fig 12 :5 "The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." A distinguishable arrangement is there whether or not anyone reads it. Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13.

2. **D2, the measure. Yes, with no decoder required.** Entropy, mutual information and channel capacity are fixed by the source and the channel statistics. Shannon's own framing is reproduction of "a message selected at another point" (Information theory, Wikipedia), and capacity depends on the channel, not on the receiver. Fig 12 :125-126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." The bit count does not change when the reading action is not taken. Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 12, with the mathematical symmetry noted in `06`.

3. **D3, aboutness. Carried yes; named no, recorded as `gap`.** The typed relation from signal to source is part of the term (F2), so carriage does not need an interpreter. But "which one holds is not recoverable from the operands" (Fig 12 :46), and "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it" (Fig 13 :128). With no frame whose closure predicts the content and no ground to settle truth (Fig 2 :45; Fig 10 :49), the specific content cannot be named. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." So the honest report is `gap`, not "no". Verdict `specified` for carriage, `gap` for the name.

4. **D4, detection and use. Detection is a separate contact question and is not a precondition of carriage.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Adding or removing a detector changes reachability and use. Symbols table :38-40: "what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read." The unread signal is residual, not nothing. A global zero with one live slot is legal (Fig 1 :20), and aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average (Fig 1 :22). Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 6.

**Cross-domain rule.** These four verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. An average would be exactly the Fig 1 :22 error, and it would hide which anchor settled which claim.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that an undetected signal has a **known meaning**. The content is a `gap` until a frame and a ground are supplied.
- Not that an undetected signal is **useful** to anyone. Use is D4 and requires contact.
- Not that no detector has ever been found. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The question stipulates absence; we do not verify it.
- Not that the absence is a **budget limit**. It may be genuine absence of any proto-instrument, which is "the whole of it" (Fig 6 :40). Three D4 states are distinguished in `01` F5.
- Not a definition change. "A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that "information" in Fig 12 is the same quantity as Shannon entropy. They are D1/D3 and D2 respectively (`06` Q5).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken and no instrument was built.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground, not agreement

A source-discriminating instrument is what is owed, the same kind of thing the spectrometer was for metamerism (*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"). Concretely:

1. **Bond-type test.** An instrument that reads which of the seven bonds holds between signal and source, without a reader's closure, and stays correct across changes of that closure. If it works, F4's `gap` closes. If no such instrument can even begin, Fig 6 :40 applies and the gap is genuine.
2. **Contact test.** Hold signal and source fixed and vary detector presence. If the bond changes, F2 collapses into F5 and the answer becomes B for carriage too. If only recoverability changes, the four-domain split is confirmed at the ground.
3. **Capacity test.** Hold channel statistics fixed and remove the decoder. If capacity changes, D2 is reader-relative after all.
4. **Miss test.** Signal detection theory already records the case "Stimulus Present, Respond Absent" as a Miss. A community that accepts that category has already conceded F5 in its own notation.
5. **Standing refuters.** The seven in `01` section 4, repeated in `08` and `09`.

None of these is an argument. All are checks. Agreement among readers, however numerous, is not one of them (Fig 2 :35, Fig 7 :35).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle for the four-domain split; `gap` for the content.** The four anchors are nameable, so the split is a `specified` result within the framework. The content of a particular undetected signal is unreachable with the instruments currently held, and the framework "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it" (Fig 13 :135). The split is also a visible choice, not nature's own list: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible" (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, inherited mapping). Another sensorium's set of quantities need not be these four.

What stops the answer is the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The shift from figure text to this four-domain shorthand carries a nonzero round trip gap R_T, enumerated in `11_audit.md`. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figure text would be dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The question bundles four questions. A signal carries distinguishable structure and a typed relation to its source; both are part of the term, so neither needs a reader. Its Shannon-style measures are fixed by the source and channel, not by a decoder. Detection is a contact property of an agent and its habitat, so adding or removing a detector changes what can be *read*, not what is *carried*; the unperceived is residual, not nothing. What the signal is *about* is a genuine gap without a frame and a ground: the relation is carried, but which relation and which referent cannot be read off the signal alone. So the answer is yes for carriage and measurability, gap for content, and detection belongs in its own domain. The shape comes with seven refuters written in advance and with the instrument that would close it named. No measurement here settles it; this is a `specified` shape, not a `grounded` result.
