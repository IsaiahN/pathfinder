# What "shape" means for INF-0004: canonical reading from the eight essays

**Sources.** Four essays were reopened directly on 2026-11-23: (1) https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-library-of-babel-locating-the , (2) https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-recursive-self-improvement-trap , (3) https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-hard-problem-is-about-source , (4) https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/decoding-tacit-knowledge-why-context . Four further essays are carried from the completed exemplar's canonical mapping and are marked inherited, not reopened this session: (5) How Intelligence Actually Works, (6) How a Society of Agents Would Work, (7) They're Scanning Books When They Should, (8) Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether. All eight share an author with the figures, so they are provenance for the vocabulary, not independent witnesses (Fig 2 :40).

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, reopened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> A signal carries distinguishable structure and a typed relation to a source; the relation is part of the term rather than a reader's addition; measurability is fixed by source and channel; detection is a contact property governing reachability; what the signal is about cannot be named without a frame and a ground.

That is a set of constraints on any acceptable answer. It is not a definition of information and it does not pick a winner among Shannon, Dretske, Floridi or Gibson. `Can a signal carry information if no system can detect or interpret it?` is the question; the shape is what an answer must respect.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary came from the question itself: signal, carry, detect, interpret. The recurring neighbours in the world literature were channel, bond, affordance, miss and residual.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Hence the effect-term census in `01` section 1: what each domain does, not what "information really is".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, reopened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a failure criterion rather than asking for agreement.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: if the final exhaustive search does not answer, one possibility is that "your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one". That is exactly the finding F1 in `01`: the question bundles four quantities.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question, a source-discriminating instrument (a way to read the bond type and referent off a signal without a reader's closure) would license a name. None was found.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Dretske's mind-independence and Gibson's hidden affordance are imports from philosophy of information and ecological psychology. They are recorded with author, venue, year, quote and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They explain a gap; they do not move this framework's wall by adding an atom.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (inherited mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The four-domain split is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. The specific error this question invites

The question invites a source-naming error. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, reopened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the corresponding move would be to read a signal's physical state and name its content or its interpreter from that reading alone. That is the same metamerism error. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* states the case almost literally for this question: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext"; "A manual outlives the craft when no one is left who can read it as it was meant"; and training on text reproduces "the traces the thought left, not the thought." A signal can carry traces after the last reader is gone. Naming the thought from the trace is the error.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, reopened this session: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So this derivation cannot certify its own shape.

## 5. The correction: "shape, not a name" governs missing primitives, not held atoms

The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a thing not held in the library. The bonds and the typed relation discussed in `01` F2 are already in the framework's vocabulary (Fig 12 :46, Fig 13 :130). So saying that a term carries its bonds is not naming a missing primitive; it is using a held atom.

What remains genuinely unnamed is **which** bond holds and **what** the signal is about for a particular case. Fig 12 :46: "which one holds is not recoverable from the operands." Fig 13 :128: "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available." That is a `gap` about a missing discriminator, not a ban on all naming.

I overstated the rule in an earlier reading of this section ("the framework cannot say what a signal is about, full stop"). That reading is corrected here and kept visible. The narrowed form is used in `01` F4 and in `07`, `08` and `09`.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Consequence 1, the binary is a preference held as a term.** The question wants a yes or no. The figures warn that a frame searching for an answer of a given shape holds that shape as a preference, not as a term: it predicts nothing and accrues no residual, so nothing in the loop can see it. The remedy is a discipline: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." That is why `01` section 0 binds the four-domain shape and section 4 writes seven refuters *before* the conclusion.

**Consequence 2, my own audit is a simulation.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." However, "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim, and they cannot settle it. The mode line on every file says so.

**Prestige is not action.** Same essay: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A list of famous information theorists who agree would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is for a refutation of the four-domain output from someone whose public work touches the residual. That is what `03` and `02` are for.

## 7. One-paragraph version

A shape is a constraint that can be stated before the answer can be named. For this question it says: keep carriage, measurability, aboutness and accessibility as four quantities with four anchors; the bond and the distinguishable state belong to the term, so no interpreter is needed for carriage; channel measures are fixed by source and channel; detection is contact and governs reachability, not existence; and the specific content is `gap` until a source-discriminating instrument or a provenanced frame supplies it. The shape refuses the yes/no binary as a bundle error, writes its refuters in advance, and stops at the limit of the available instruments with the lineage recorded.
