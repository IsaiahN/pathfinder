## 1. Independent domain census, written from the residual line alone

I wrote this before reading the primary's conclusions in `01` sections 2 and 3, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I then compared.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the inference from "no detector" to "no information". Arity: the verb "carry" can be read with two operands (signal, source) or three (signal, source, receiver). If the third is constitutive, Option B wins by definition; if not, the question changes domain mid-sentence. Symmetry: a correlation measure does not care which operand comes first, but "about" does; smoke is about fire, not the reverse. So I expect at least two quantities with the same name and different operand order. Scale: a single distinguishable state and a whole arrangement with a typed relation are different objects; the figures insist the residual is per slot.
- **Domain A, state and arrangement.** Actors: signal state, source, bond. Currency: distinguishability and what the arrangement records. Anchor: the state of the carrier, measurable. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain B, measure.** Actors: source distribution, channel. Currency: bits, entropy, capacity. Anchor: repeated trials over the channel. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Domain C, aboutness.** Actors: signal, state of affairs, typed relation, frame. Currency: meaning, truth. Anchor: the bond is in the term, but *which* bond and referent need a frame and a ground. Nameable anchor: partial, with an honest gap for the name.
- **Domain D, access.** Actors: instrument, perceiver, habitat. Currency: contact, recoverability. Anchor: an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve. Nameable anchor: yes.
- **Union, not partition.** A single case has a state, a measure, an aboutness and an accessibility at once; they overlap. Unbundle before search.
- **Order by dependency.** state → measure → aboutness → access. The question puts access first, which is the structural error.

My four domains match the primary's D1 to D4. I derived them from the arity and symmetry of "carry" and from the anchor test; the primary derived them from the same residual but emphasized the source-channel literature and the two-currencies line. Different starting points within one framework, same boundaries. That is low overlap **within** the framework, not independence from it. We still share the figure pool and the same world sources.
