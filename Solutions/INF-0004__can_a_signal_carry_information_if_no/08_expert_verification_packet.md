# Expert verification packet — the Tether on undetected signals

**What this is:** a claim in a form a signal, information or detection researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the split, `gap` on naming content without a frame. Not `grounded`.** No measurement by this author is claimed. This is a shape and a route, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can a signal carry information if no system can detect or interpret it?" bundles four quantities with four different anchors. Carriage of structure and of a typed relation is part of the term, so it survives the removal of every reader. Measurability is fixed by the source and channel statistics, so it survives removal of the decoder. Aboutness is carried as a bond but cannot be named without a frame and a ground, so the content is `gap`, not "no". Detection is a contact property of agent and habitat; it changes reachability and use, not carriage. The four verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. Accepting the bundle as one question is the Fig 1 :22 aggregation error: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13

- **Statement:** a term carries its typed relation as part of what it is. Removing readers removes readings, not the bond.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :130 — "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term." Also Fig 12 :5 — "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." And Operators table :4 — "The bond carries information the operands do not, so a term that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."
- **What to check:** does the figure actually make the bond part of the term, or only part of a notation? Read the two passages, not the summary.
- **Refuter:** produce a case where a signal's typed relation changes when all detectors are removed while signal and source are held fixed. Also: show that a bond is imposed at read time rather than carried, so that Fig 13 :130 is false as read here.

### C2 — framework-internal plus information theory; checkable by an information theorist

- **Statement:** D2 (entropy, mutual information, channel capacity) is fixed by source and channel, not by the decoder. Bits and actions are different currencies and do not add.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125 — "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126 — "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 1 :23 — "The ground enters as the only metric."
- **What to check:** is the claim faithful to Shannon? The channel literature says capacity depends "merely on the statistics of the channel", and that above capacity "it is impossible to transmit with arbitrarily small block error" regardless of decoder. Mutual information is symmetric, while semantic aboutness is directed; these are two quantities (see `06` Q2).
- **Refuter:** exhibit a channel whose capacity changes when the decoder is removed while channel statistics are held fixed, or show that I(X;Y) and "X carries information about Y" have the same symmetry and ground.

### C3 — cross-domain; checkable by a detection or perception researcher

- **Statement:** detection is contact, not carriage. A global zero with one live slot is a legal state, and what cannot be perceived is residual rather than nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 — "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 — "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Symbols table :38-40 — "what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read." Fig 1 :20 — "a global residual near zero with one live slot is a legal state".
- **What to check:** signal detection theory already scores "Stimulus Present, Respond Absent" as a Miss, which presupposes presence without report. Gibson's hidden affordance is "there are possibilities for action, but these are not perceived by the actor". Both are low-overlap corroborations from engineering psychology and ecological psychology.
- **Refuter:** hold signal and source fixed, vary detector presence, and show that the source-to-signal bond changes. Or show that a global zero entails zero mass on every slot (which would make Fig 1 :19-22 false).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that an undetected signal has a known meaning. Content is `gap` (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it").
- Not that no detector has ever been found. The stipulation is the question's, not a search result; Fig 8 :39 — "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- Not that the absence is a budget limit. Genuine absence of any proto-instrument is a different state: Fig 6 :40 — "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution. That is the whole of it."
- Not a definition change. Fig 10 :47 — "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- Not that "information" in Fig 12 is Shannon entropy. They are different domains with different symmetry (`06` Q5).
- Not `grounded`, and not a claim that this derivation settles the philosophy-of-information literature's dispute.
- Not a claim about one named signal. The units are domains, not cases.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer that settled the color case.

1. **Bond-type test:** read which bond holds between signal and source without a reader's closure, and stay correct as that closure changes.
2. **Contact test:** vary detector presence with signal and source fixed; observe whether only recoverability changes.
3. **Capacity test:** remove the decoder with channel statistics fixed; observe whether capacity is unchanged.
4. **Seven refuters in `01` section 4**, any one of which narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :130, Fig 12 :5, Fig 12 :125-126, Fig 11 :39-40, Symbols :38-40 and Fig 1 :20. Six short passages, all in `03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.
2. Decide whether C1's bond claim is a claim about the term or only about the notation. One sentence is enough.
3. Check C2 against your own knowledge of channel capacity and the symmetry of mutual information. Is there any sense in which the decoder enters the measure?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish bond types from a signal alone, or say why none is possible. This is the most valuable answer you can give.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and your verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any framework material is shown.
