## What the answer is, in one line

The binary is refused. Carrying, being measurable, being about something, and being accessible are four different quantities in four domains with four different anchors. A signal carries structure (the bond is part of the term) with no interpreter required; naming what the signal is about is a `gap` without a frame and a ground. Detection governs reachability and use, not carriage. Verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged.

## Relation to neighbouring questions in section 1

This solution isolates the interpreter-necessity question. It does not settle INF-0001 (physical property versus relation versus both), INF-0003 (information versus mere correlation) or INF-0007 (information without uncertainty). Each of those is a separate domain with its own anchor. Dependency order follows Fig 3: the carrier must be settled before aboutness, and aboutness before accessibility. Cross-links are noted so the coordinator can group them without merging their verdicts.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the philosophical question. It is a shape and a route, checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A checker must not hold the interiors.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **A convention is not a verdict about the world.** "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do." (Fig 10 :46). Redefining "information" to win the argument would be exactly this failure.
