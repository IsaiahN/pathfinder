## 0. The residual under audit

Question: what determines whether an ecosystem recovers after disturbance or shifts into a new state?

Stated as an effect: a system meets a perturbation. Recovery means the pre-disturbance arrangement re-forms. Shift means a different arrangement forms and persists. R is the difference in outcome under comparable disturbance. The question asks for the determining quantities, not for a species name or a number.

Shape claim bound before the run (Formula "Direction"): the determinants are a property of agent-and-habitat contact; the habitat is enumerated and its cascades reach beyond direct contact; the two outcomes are an ordered threshold comparison between settled arrangements; the question is a union of overlapping conditions; the answer is a shape, not a name. This bound is the ordinary term the refuter in §3 points at.

Option A: answer as a list of intrinsic traits of the species set (diversity, redundancy, connectivity) that confer recovery. Leads to a standing "good actor" catalog, which Fig 11 forbids.
Option B: answer as a shape over contact, enumeration, threshold and order. Leads to checkable sub-claims and an open slot until a ground settles a particular system. This audit derives B.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: two outcomes compared across one disturbance, so a comparison of at least two quantities (the disturbance magnitude and the system's distance to a boundary, Fig 12's ⋛: "a threshold relation between two quantities", Fig 12 :87). Symmetry: not symmetric. The forward shift and the return path differ (hysteresis), so the bond is ordered, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order at the species and interaction level, second order at the habitat and regime level; contact cascades several steps out (Fig 11 :52-53).

Actors, currency, anchor, one line per domain:

- **D1, the ecological habitat.** Actors: species, conditions (light, water, temperature, season, terrain), relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, that is, which configuration actually persists after the perturbation. A system with no nameable anchor here is not a domain yet; a trajectory that nobody measures is not a verdict.
- **D2, the threshold and state domain.** Actors: the six substrate terms and the bonds, especially comparison ⋛ and sequence →. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)` and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the ground settles which arrangement formed. Distinct from D1 because D1 enumerates actors while D2 compares arrangements.
- **D3, the observation domain.** Actors: the observer and its instruments (early-warning indicators, monitoring). Currency: the residual R and the anchor. Anchor: the actual trajectory, not agreement among models. Question 35 and 36 of this section are the adjacent instrument questions, so D3 is in scope.

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. The determinants overlap: disturbance magnitude, internal feedback sign, habitat connectivity, cascades, and the observer's window can each hold alone or in combination. Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. So the headline question is a specific compound with its own loop, not one hard question and not a list of separate ones.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (enumerate the habitat), then vocabulary for what the actors afford, then objective (what counts as recovery), then planning (what lever), then learn and carry. The break usually sits between perception and vocabulary, the link with no instrument: Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Effort spent above the break (more sophisticated models) cannot rescue a missing reading below it, Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The determinant is a property of agent-and-habitat, not the species set alone (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The recovery question is not "which species does it have" but "what is the structure of contact in the habitat." Two systems with the same species list can differ in outcome because the relations and conditions differ. This is corroborated at low figure overlap by Fig 2 :36's anchor rule: the question is constitutively about what persists on the ground, so a trait catalog that never meets the trajectory is not an answer. Mark `triangulated` across Fig 11 and Fig 2 (different quantities: contact structure versus ground legitimacy), but both figures are one framework evidence pool, so the triangulation is internal consistency, not two independent witnesses.

Refuter: a controlled comparison in which recovery is fully determined by an intrinsic species-set property while habitat and contact are held different but irrelevant, with the trajectory measured. If that holds, Fig 11's capability claim is broken here and F1 falls.

### F2 - Enumerate the habitat; the determinant is often three steps out (`general` on the method, `specified` on the cascade claim, single-source Fig 11)

Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
Fig 11 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."
Fig 11 :52-53: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

The list is read off the world, not invented. Actors, conditions and relations are enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering. The figure's own instance (DDT, Carson's Silent Spring 1962) is the precedent: the residual that mattered was two crossings away from the spray. Applied here: a disturbance may act on a predator, the predator on a grazer, the grazer on a foundation species, and recovery is decided at the third step. A first-order habitat scope produces a confident wrong answer.

Refuter: a case where the determinant is demonstrably first order and every second- or third-order cascade is shown to be inert for that residual. That would break the cascade claim, not the enumeration method.

### F3 - No standing list of good or bad actors (`specified`, single-source Fig 11)

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

So the headline "biodiversity confers recovery" cannot be a general law from the figures. It may hold for one residual and fail for another. A predator that restores a clear-water lake state can destabilize a different state. What is owed is the relation to the specific residual, not a valence carried by the actor.

Refuter: a standing actor class that is beneficial for every residual in a census of residuals, with no trade-off. That would break the relativity rule.

### F4 - Recovery versus shift is an ordered threshold comparison, not a symmetric pair (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities."
Fig 12 :112: "A goal is a comparison."
Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
Operators table :51: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one" (framework primary, not a figure-text line).

Recovery means the same arrangement re-settled; a shift means a different arrangement settled and became the atom for the next cycle. Because ⋛ is ordered, the forward and return paths are not the same comparison. Hysteresis is the expected shape, not an anomaly. Triangulated across Fig 12's bond definition and Fig 2 :29's anchor rule ("An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks"): what settles the outcome is which arrangement the ground keeps, not which one the observers prefer.

Refuter: a system where the disturbance needed to flip equals the disturbance needed to flip back for every measured state variable, with no path dependence at all. That would break the ordered-comparison reading and leave a single symmetric threshold.

### F5 - The question is a union compound, so unbundle before searching (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"
Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

This explains the shape of the real literature: many "determinants" each work on a subset, and the intersections remain standing after a single lever is applied. The compound is categorically different from either condition alone. Triangulated with Fig 3's dependency ordering: union describes the condition structure, dependency describes the link order; two different quantities, no strain.

Refuter: a demonstration that the conditions partition the cases cleanly, so a single determinant assigns every case to recovery or shift with no overlap. Then the union reading is wrong and the search should not be unbundled.

### F6 - The observer's instruments can fail on the axis that matters (`specified`, single-source Fig 2, corroborated externally)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
Fig 2 :38: "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure"
Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

An early-warning indicator is an instrument, not the ground. It can read stable when the system is close to a boundary, or warn when nothing will flip (the adjacent questions 35 and 36). Agreement among monitoring models is a filter for where to look, never a witness for the verdict.

Refuter: an indicator that is proven, on the relevant axis and across the relevant regime, to be identical to the trajectory's own decision (zero false alarms, zero misses, no unmeasured axis). Then the instrument is the ground for this purpose and the separation collapses.

### F7 - Restoration is permanent work; isolation substitutes a habitat (`specified`, single-source Fig 11)

Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."
Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."
Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."

A manager can add an actor and cannot subtract the habitat, because contact does not consult the manager. Reversing a regime shift is therefore not removing the disturbance: it requires reproducing what the old state needed and inheriting the actors of the new one. Fig 11 :65 names the move: "Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994." With the cascade unenumerated it is the same move with a different outcome.

Refuter: a restoration that achieves the old arrangement by a one-time subtraction of the new habitat's actors, with no ongoing work and no substituted conditions. That would break the asymmetry claim.

### F8 - The mechanism of the flip: a signed cycle and a compounding remainder (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."
Fig 13 :32: "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise"

A positive feedback loop is a cycle that closes; that is what holds a degraded state in place. A negative loop returns the ball. The residue of each turn re-enters the next, so a small disturbance can compound. Triangulated across Fig 12 (structure that closes) and Fig 13 (residue that amplifies): one names the bond, the other the iteration.

Refuter: a regime shift in which every feedback loop is demonstrably open and no residual compounds, so the flip is fully explained by an external step change with no internal closure. Then the cycle mechanism is not governing that case.

### F9 - The framework returns a shape, not the determinant's name (`general`, corroborated canonically)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Figures README (local canonical copy): the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."

So this audit does not name the species or the numeric threshold for any particular ecosystem. A particular determinant is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Nothing here is `grounded`.

### Step 7 triage, kept distinct

- **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest).** Is the determinant already inside the ecological closure and merely unfound? Yes: the literature holds many candidate determinants (resilience, latitude, resistance, precariousness, panarchy, feedback structure). More independent search is the remedy here, not a new atom. Fig 8 :32 "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."
- **INWARD (extend the instrument).** Are early-warning instruments adequate to hold the approach to the boundary? This is where adjacent questions 35 and 36 live. An instrument is improved from a worse one already returning something; the question is whether anything at any resolution already returns a failing signal.
- **OUTWARD (import).** Is there a frame whose closure predicts this shape? Low-overlap candidates are psychological resilience (human cognition) and supply-chain resilience (business/engineering). Both are secondary corroboration with a source gate and a shadow test, never a source of the verdict.

INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed; never read one against the other. Unreachable-at-this-budget and underivable-in-principle are different facts with different instructions (Formula step 7).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refused if any of the following holds:

1. Recovery is shown, in a controlled comparison with measured trajectories, to be fully determined by intrinsic species-set properties while contact and habitat differences are irrelevant (breaks F1).
2. A standing actor class is found that is beneficial for every residual without trade-off (breaks F3).
3. The forward shift and the return path are identical at every state variable with no path dependence (breaks F4's ordered comparison).
4. The conditions partition cases cleanly with no overlap, so no unbundling is owed (breaks F5).
5. The ecological determinants are derived entirely from instruments that are identical to the trajectory decision with no unmeasured axis (breaks F6).
6. One-time subtraction of the new habitat's actors restores the old arrangement with no ongoing management (breaks F7).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the shape is re-censused from the residual line. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a catalog of determinants (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
2. Enumerate the habitat for the specific system before naming any lever; go outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44).
3. Treat the two outcomes as an ordered comparison and record hysteresis explicitly (Fig 12 :87, :121).
4. Unbundle the union before searching; report per-condition and intersection verdicts separately, never averaged (Fig 9 :54).
5. Use Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global resilience score is the named way for a live signal to disappear into an average.
