## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands ... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: a threshold comparison over an enumerated agent-and-habitat contact, ordered in time, with no standing valence attached to actors. That is a shape. "Phosphorus loading above X in a shallow lake" is a name, licensed only by a ground reading of that lake.

The essay also gives the exit for a malformed question: if the final book does not answer, "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union reading in `01` F5.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and the paradox that by then you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the starting vocabulary is disturbance, recovery, state, threshold. The proximate new terms the world returns are resilience, resistance, latitude, precariousness, panarchy, alternative stable state, hysteresis, early-warning signal. Recursing on those narrows the answer to the five-point shape above. The bundle check then splits the headline into per-condition and intersection loops.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, a particular determinant is named only after a measured trajectory in a specific system pays for it. Citing the shallow-lake phosphorus case is a provenanced import from the ecology frame: the source gate is the Scheffer and Carpenter literature, and the shadow test is whether it explains the residual shape (a threshold with hysteresis). It does not become a general name for all ecosystems.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The ecological analogue is direct. A monitoring record shows "the system did not recover." That reading is a projection: the source could be a too-small disturbance, a high resistance basin, a lost mutualist three steps out, or an observer window that missed the crossing. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (a better trajectory measurement, a manipulative test), not a name.

Fig 2 :38: "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." This is why the adjacent questions about early-warning false alarms are part of this question's habitat, not a separate topic.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of the answer to "what determines recovery versus a new state"?** It is:

> An ordered comparison, at a threshold, between two settled arrangements of an enumerated agent-and-habitat field; the determining quantities live in the contact structure and in cascades that reach beyond direct effects; actor valence is relative to the residual; and the headline question is a union of overlapping conditions that must be unbundled before it is searched.

It is not: a list of species traits, a universal resilience score, a standing catalog of good actors, or a symmetric flip-flop with one threshold. The framework returns this shape and stops. Which quantity governs a particular ecosystem is a name, and a name requires the ground.

Mode: `general` for the structural skeleton, `specified` for the five-point shape. Nothing here is `grounded`.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known ecologists assembled to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a real trajectory.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**The first contact carries effects, not interiors.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why `02_outreach_email.md` opens with the residual and the refuter and withholds the framework until stage 2.
