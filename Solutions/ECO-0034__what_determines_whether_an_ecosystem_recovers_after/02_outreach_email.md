# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter are stated as below and the sender has recorded what they would count as a failure. A verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown.

---

**Subject:** One checkable shape about ecosystem recovery, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A system meets a disturbance. Sometimes the pre-disturbance arrangement re-forms; sometimes a different arrangement forms and persists. We want the determining quantities. The output is `specified`, not `grounded`: it is a shape, not a measurement.
>
> **The shape claim:**
> 1. The determinant is a property of the agent-and-habitat contact, not of the species set alone. Two systems with the same species can differ because the relations and conditions differ.
> 2. The habitat is enumerated, not composed. List what is in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. The decisive effect is often two or three steps out.
> 3. There is no standing list of good or bad actors. An actor's value is relative to the particular residual.
> 4. Recovery versus shift is an ordered threshold comparison between two settled arrangements, not a symmetric pair. The return path is expected to differ from the forward path.
> 5. The headline question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it must be unbundled before it is searched. A single lever clears the cases where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular species or numeric threshold is *the* answer; that biodiversity confers recovery as a general law; that an early-warning indicator is the ground rather than an instrument that can fail on the axis that matters. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim (written down before this send):**
> 1. recovery fully determined by intrinsic species-set properties while contact and habitat differences are irrelevant, with trajectories measured;
> 2. a standing actor class beneficial for every residual without trade-off;
> 3. forward and return paths identical at every state variable, with no path dependence;
> 4. the conditions partition cases cleanly, so no unbundling is owed;
> 5. a one-time subtraction of the new habitat's actors restoring the old arrangement with no ongoing management.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Ecological resilience, definitions and theory (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_resilience
> - Alternative stable state, hysteresis and evidence (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_stable_state
> - Catastrophic shifts in ecosystems (Scheffer et al., Nature, 2001): https://www.nature.com/articles/35098000
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link I can send on request. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
