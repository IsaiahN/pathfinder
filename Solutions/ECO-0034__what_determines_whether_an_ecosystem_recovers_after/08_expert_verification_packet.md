# Expert verification packet: ecosystem recovery versus a new state

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist or resilience scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the skeleton. Not `grounded`.** No ecological trajectory is claimed to have been measured here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The determinant of recovery versus a regime shift is a property of agent-and-habitat contact, not of the species set alone. The habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering, because the decisive effect is often several steps from the disturbance. No actor carries a standing valence: a species that helps one residual can hurt another. Recovery and a new state are an ordered threshold comparison between two settled arrangements, so the return path is expected to differ from the forward path, and the new state is held by a closed feedback loop. The headline question is a union of overlapping conditions, so per-condition and intersection verdicts must be reported separately, never averaged. The framework returns this shape, not the name of a species or a number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** capability is agent-and-habitat; the determinant lives in the contact structure; actor valence is relative to the residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
- **What to check:** does the claim survive a case where two systems share a species list but differ in relations and conditions? If so, the species-list explanation is insufficient.
- **Refuter:** recovery fully determined by intrinsic species-set properties while contact and habitat differences are irrelevant, with trajectories measured; or a standing actor class beneficial for every residual without trade-off.

### C2 - framework-internal plus habitat enumeration; checkable by reading Figure 11 and a case study

- **Statement:** the habitat is enumerated, never composed; the determinant is often a second- or third-order cascade. First-order-only scoping is the named failure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **What to check:** for a system you know, enumerate contacts outward. Does the accepted explanation stop at the direct effect when the measured determinant sits further out?
- **Refuter:** a case where the determinant is demonstrably first order and every second- or third-order cascade is inert for that residual.

### C3 - threshold and order; checkable by reading Figure 12 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** recovery versus shift is an ordered threshold comparison between two settled arrangements; return need not reverse forward; a positive loop can close the new state.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities." Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."
- **What to check:** in a documented regime shift, were the forward and return crossings at the same forcing value? If not, the comparison is ordered and the symmetric single-threshold model is incomplete.
- **Refuter:** forward and return paths identical at every state variable with no path dependence; or a shift fully explained by an external step change with no internal loop closure.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a colour "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Trajectory test:** measure whether the system returns or settles a different arrangement after a disturbance of known magnitude.
2. **Cascade test:** enumerate contacts outward and identify which cascade actually carries the determinant.
3. **Hysteresis test:** measure forward and return crossings with the same state variables.
4. **Union test:** report per-condition and intersection outcomes separately; a clean partition fires refuter 4 in `01`.
