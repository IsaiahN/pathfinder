# The Tether's answer: what determines recovery versus a new state

**Mode: `specified` on the five-point shape, `general` on the structural skeleton. Not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A system meets a disturbance. Recovery means the pre-disturbance arrangement re-forms; a shift means a different arrangement forms and persists. R is the difference in outcome under comparable disturbance. The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: disturbance magnitude, internal feedback sign, habitat connectivity, cascades, and the observer's window overlap (Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"). A single lever clears the single-condition cases and leaves the intersections standing, which is why the literature reports many "determinants" that each work on a subset.

## 2. The shape

1. **The determinant is a property of the agent-and-habitat contact, not the species set alone.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Two systems with the same species can differ because relations and conditions differ.
2. **The habitat is enumerated, never composed.** Fig 11 :43, :44. List everything in contact with the residual, then what contacts those, outward until the cascade stops mattering. Fig 11 :52-53: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." The decisive actor is often not the one the disturbance touched.
3. **No standing valence.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." So "biodiversity confers recovery" is not a general law from the figures; it may hold for one residual and fail for another.
4. **Recovery versus shift is an ordered threshold comparison between two settled arrangements.** Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities." The forward path and the return path are not the same, so hysteresis is the expected shape, not an anomaly. A new state is a new settled molecule (Fig 12 :104), held by a closed loop whose sign decides it (Fig 12 :121). Whether the two states are isomers depends on whether they rearrange a shared atom pool (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1, with its stated falsifier).
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Which quantity governs a particular lake, forest or reef is a name that requires that system's ground to settle it. There is no universal third species, no universal nutrient number.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a trait catalog. No actor is good or bad by kind.
- Not one threshold for all ecosystems. Each system's boundary is measured, not declared.
- Not a symmetric flip. Return is not the reverse of forward.
- Not that removing the disturbance restores the old state. Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not"; Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."
- Not that an early-warning indicator is the ground. Fig 2 :38 "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Indicators are filters for where to look, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not grounded. No trajectory in this derivation was measured.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating measurement on a specific system, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Trajectory test:** does the system return to the old arrangement or settle a different one after a disturbance of known magnitude? That is the ground for D1 and D2.
- **Cascade test:** enumerate outward and check which cascade actually carries the determinant; a first-order-only scope is the named failure (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Hysteresis test:** measure both the forward and return crossings with the same state variables. Equal crossings with no path dependence would fire refuter 3 in `01` §3.
- **Union test:** report per-condition and intersection outcomes separately. A clean partition would fire refuter 4.
- **Instrument test:** compare the early-warning reading against the trajectory itself on the axis that matters. A perfect discriminator with no unmeasured axis would fire refuter 5.

Standing falsifiers from `01` §3 apply as written.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The ecology closure already holds the candidate determinants (SEARCHED in step 7); what is missing is measurement on a specific system, not a new atom. The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 6. One-paragraph version

> The Tether does not give a universal list of traits that decide whether an ecosystem recovers. It gives the shape any answer must have. The determinant is a property of the agent-and-habitat contact, so the habitat must be enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; the decisive effect is often several steps from the disturbance. No actor carries a standing valence, so a species that helps one residual can hurt another. Recovery and a new state are an ordered threshold comparison between two arrangements, with the return path expected to differ from the forward one, and a new state held in place by a closed feedback loop. The headline question is a union of overlapping conditions and must be unbundled before it is searched. The framework returns a shape, not the name of a species or a number; which quantity governs a particular lake or forest is decided by that system's trajectory, not by agreement among models. Falsifiers were written in advance, and the derivation is a simulated check against the figures, not a ground reading.
