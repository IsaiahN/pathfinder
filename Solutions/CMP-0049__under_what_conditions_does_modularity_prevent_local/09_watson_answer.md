## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that modularity prevents local failures from becoming systemic cascades. Option A: treat it as one lever, raise modularity, and expect fewer cascades. Option B: treat it as a union of structure and dynamics, and report the conditions under which a boundary filters a named propagation class. What each leads to: A sends a single global metric that hides the live slot, predicts opposite outcomes for different failure classes, and cannot explain the opened world conflict. B sends a shape, four refuters written in advance, and a clear statement of what instrument would close the number.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of structure and dynamics, and it is answered with a shape, not a threshold. Containment obtains when all of the following hold together: the residual is measured per slot; the contact chain is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; the propagation class is named; the modules are genuinely independent on the channel that carries that class; the boundary's filter blocks the cascading class and passes what local repair needs; and no hidden shared source makes the modules fail together. The boundary is a filter with a direction, not a wall, so containment is always relative to a failure class. A numeric modularity threshold is a `gap`: the figures do not supply one and no source-discriminating measurement was taken. Domain 2 corroborates but is mixed by design; Domain 3 has zero evidence weight in this simulated run. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. This is `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, four refuters and the lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a single global metric instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-lever reading is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected shape, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. Isolation substitutes a habitat, so a coupling change can be reversed by another coupling change, though the unchosen consequences cannot be subtracted (Fig 11 :66-69).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual trace through a named cascade, a channel discriminator separating overload from exposure from common source, a boundary test recording which classes pass, or an overlap measure on the specific channel would move the numeric part from `gap` to `grounded`. A demonstration of a cascade with no contact chain and no shared source, or of a single global Q that predicts containment across interaction types and failure modes, would reverse the shape claim itself. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
