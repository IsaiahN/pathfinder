## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

*The Library of Babel* (opened this session): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form of the conditions under which containment holds, stated before any numeric threshold can be named. It constrains: the boundary must filter, the modules must be independent, there must be no hidden shared source, and the dynamics must use a channel the boundary passes or blocks. It does not fill: it does not give a Q value, a coupling strength or a named modularity algorithm as the answer.

- Shape: "a boundary that filters the failure's channel, with independent modules and no hidden common source."
- Name: "set Q above 0.6 and the cascade will stop." The framework does not license the second.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

*The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already hold that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

Applied here, the bootstrapping vocabulary is: local failure, module, boundary, coupling, overlap, common cause, bridge, load, threshold. The figure lines then do the narrowing (Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms.").

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach language is an effect description: a residual that stays on its original slot, a boundary that passes one failure class and blocks another, a co-failure explained by a shared source.

*The Library of Babel* also names the union condition directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is audit F6 in the essay's own vocabulary, and it is a low-overlap corroboration from the search essay rather than from the network literature.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means a measured cascade on a named network with the per-slot residual and the propagation channel recorded.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A modularity threshold imported from a published study is licensed only when its provenance, its failure mode and its substrate are stated, and when it explains a gap in this frame.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (exemplar pass): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set, including "network" as a structure (Fig 13 :23-24), is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why a single measured Q cannot name the answer

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's own resolution: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

A global modularity score is a reading of a projection. Several different structures (compartmented trophic, nested mutualistic, spatially clustered infrastructure with long bridges) can give the same Q while behaving oppositely under failure. The opened supply-chain paper reports the same thing in measurement language: "We find no particular topological measure that explains systemic risk mitigation" and the relevant structure is a mesoscale "systemic risk core". The essay's discipline is therefore to build the source-discriminating instrument, not to name the source from Q.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Q1 answered: does "shape, not a name" forbid using the word modularity here?

**No, with a narrowing.** The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. "Network" is already one of the six structures (Fig 13 :23-24: "network", "that entities relate, and influence travels"), and modularity is a property of the arrangement of its bonds (Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."). Using the word modularity is naming an arrangement over held atoms, not inventing a seventh.

What *is* forbidden is treating a specific modularity value, algorithm or partition as the answer the framework derived. That would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. Status: `general`, triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 12 and the canonical README.

## 6. Q2 answered: what licenses the union claim?

The union claim is licensed by two independent corridors:

1. *The Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the searcher's corridor, from a field (search and information theory) with low overlap to network science.
2. Fig 9 :54 and :57: "unbundle before you search again" and "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." This is the figure corridor, primary.

Because the corridors are low-overlap, the union reading is `triangulated` for the structural claim. The world conflict (Thébault and Fontaine 2010 versus Mou and Hayashi 2025) is secondary corroboration that the two conditions respond differently to the same lever.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of respected names agreeing that modularity works is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried on a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction carried forward

An earlier reading of mine treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on using the word *module* at all. That was too broad and is retracted here: the ban applies to naming a missing primitive or a specific numeric threshold, not to arranging atoms already in Γ. The narrower reading is kept in Q1 above so the correction stays visible.
