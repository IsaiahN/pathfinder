**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term).** An answer is a set of conditions on structure and on failure dynamics under which a residual that is live on one slot stays on that slot rather than appearing on many slots. This shape is written down first so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-66).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A pool of believers is one witness wearing many names.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the full architecture before asking for a verdict spends the independence of the only people who could refute it.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Use the public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
