## QUESTION

Can researchers identify how a specific institution caused a cognitive or behavioral change rather than merely accompanying it? Restated as an effect: for person-slot s, is the bond between institution I and change Δ the symmetric conjunction or hidden common cause of accompaniment, or the ordered productive bond of causation? Option A, "a precise association is an identification", leads to a symmetric bond being read as an ordered one and to the common-cause collapse going unchecked. Option B, "identification requires a pinned anchor, a traced lineage and a discriminating instrument", leads to a checkable four-condition shape and an honest gap where the specific cause is not named. The verdict is B in `specified` mode.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Co-occurrence of I and Δ at a person-slot. Arity: at least three place (I, Δ, hidden C), plus the instrument M, indexed by person and time. Symmetry: association is symmetric (conjunction), causation is ordered (production or sequence). Scale: individual loop, population estimate, institutional-historical time, with a lossy transform between. The residual is per slot: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19).
- **D1 Observational association.** Actors: researchers, datasets, measured I and Δ. Currency: association and estimated effect. Anchor: none inside the frame, because Δ moves with C. Status: admitted, anchor deficient.
- **D2 Identification by anchor.** Actors: randomized assignment, natural experiment, instrument, discontinuity. Currency: variation in I independent of C. Anchor: the assignment mechanism, "pinned: it does not update on A or B" (Fig 2 :25). Status: admitted, grounded only when the intervention exists.
- **D3 Mechanism / process.** Actors: intermediate steps, documents, traces, sequences. Currency: provenance and lineage. Anchor: the historical record, which does not update on the account built from it. Status: admitted.
- **D4 Measurement / construct.** Actors: test, score, observer, rating. Currency: contact with the construct. Anchor: a discriminating instrument, provisional: "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :38). Status: admitted.
- **X Free-floating cause property.** Actors: the institution alone. Currency: a property carried by the institution. Anchor: none nameable. Status: not a domain yet, absorbed into D1 to D4.

Union, not partition: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." (Fig 9 :55). Dependency order: perception of Δ, vocabulary for what the institution affords, objective as the estimand and counterfactual, planning as the anchor choice, then learn and carry (Fig 3 :20). Strain test applied: Fig 8 :39 governs what a search inside a closure cannot prove; Fig 2 :29 governs what an anchor can settle. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** yes in principle, but only by satisfying a four-condition union. Accompaniment and causation are different bonds over the same measurements (Fig 12 :56 versus :77), and the common-cause case is explicitly modelled (Fig 2 :19). Identification requires an anchor that does not update on the common cause (Fig 2 :29), which is an import of a new dimension of variation rather than more of the same data (Fig 6 :14). No frame certifies its own completeness (Fig 8 :35), so the "how" is read off the lineage (Fig 13 :129) and the reading is checked against the source (Fig 2 :38). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. Which specific institution caused a specific change is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Prestige or agreement is not an instrument.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted checking time. A case failing the anchor condition would be handed a better process trace; a case failing the measurement condition would be handed a cleverer identification design, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, because the shape, its mode and its falsifiers are written down before the claim is sent.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The four conditions and their refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) the same co-occurrence correctly described as both bonds at one level, (2) an ordered bond identified with variation fully determined by the common cause, (3) a procedure certifying no unmeasured confounders from inside the frame, (4) the lineage fully recoverable from the estimate, (5) a single lever clearing all four conditions, or (6) the population aggregate sufficient with no per-slot residual. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that a pinned anchor is neither necessary nor sufficient would also reverse it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
