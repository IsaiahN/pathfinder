# The Tether's answer on cause versus accompaniment

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a person-slot s, an institution I and a behavioral change Δ are observed together. The question is whether the bond is ordered (I produced Δ, so Δ would be absent without I) or symmetric/common-caused (both are outputs of a hidden source C, so they never talk and still agree). The two are isomers over the same atoms: same measurements, different arrangement (Fig 12 :117). Association is necessary but not sufficient; it has the wrong symmetry.

## 2. The shape

1. **Accompaniment and causation are different bonds, not different magnitudes.** Fig 12 :56 "both hold at once" versus Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before". Precision, sample size and effect size live inside the conjunction; they cannot convert it into production.
2. **The common-cause case is a named collapse.** Fig 2 :19 "they never talk, and still agree" and Fig 2 :21 "not two frames confirming each other". Selection into the institution and parallel background conditions both project the same reading as a genuine effect.
3. **Identification requires an anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Random assignment, a natural experiment, an instrument or a discontinuity supplies variation in I that is not produced by C. This is an import of a new dimension of variation (Fig 6 :14), not more of the same observations.
4. **No frame certifies its own limit.** Fig 8 :35 "A frame cannot certify its own limit." "All confounders controlled" is a convention; when it decides the outcome it has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47). A null result is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
5. **"How" is a separate question answered by lineage.** Fig 13 :129 "It is recoverable from the lineage." The intermediate steps and rival traces are the deliverable, not narrative added to an estimate.
6. **The reading is not the source.** Fig 2 :38 "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure". A score change is a projection on which several causes converge; what is owed is a discriminator.
7. **The four conditions are a union.** Slot-level association, an independent anchor, a traced lineage and a discriminating instrument all bind; a single lever clears only the intersections where one condition holds (Fig 9 :55). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
8. **The name is a gap.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns the shape an identification must have, not which institution caused a particular change.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that causal claims are impossible. An anchor can settle a bond; the claim is about what is required, not about impossibility.
- Not that a significant association is an identification, and not that a non-significant one is a refutation.
- Not that any particular method (IV, diff-in-diff, randomization, process tracing) is sufficient by itself. Each addresses one condition.
- Not that the instrument is valid. Its failure axes must be declared and checked.
- Not which institution caused a specific change, and not that this is the only method of identification.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a measurement.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating anchor, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Anchor Test:** exhibit variation in the institution that does not move with the suspected common cause, and show whether the change follows the variation. A negative result narrows the compound; it does not prove the institution had no effect (Fig 8 :39).
- **Trace Test:** find an intermediate step predicted by the ordered bond and absent under each rival. Record the lineage so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Discriminator Test:** show an instrument that distinguishes the construct change from measurement drift, or state honestly that none is available at this resolution (Fig 6 :34-35).
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** bond collapse; anchor-free identification; self-certification of no unmeasured confounders; lineage redundancy; a single lever clearing all four conditions; slot irrelevance. Any of these narrows or withdraws the shape.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are a shape, and the framework's own rule is that it returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself (Fig 9 :32). Declaring an instrument valid makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." Which specific institution caused a specific change stays open until an anchor and a discriminator are brought to that case.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not claim that causal institutional effects cannot be identified, and it does not name the cause in any particular case. It says that an institution which merely accompanies a change and one which produces it are different bonds over the same measurements, so no increase in precision turns one into the other: accompaniment is a symmetric conjunction or a hidden common cause, while causation is ordered and productive. Four conditions must hold together, a real slot-level co-occurrence, variation in the institution pinned against the common cause by an anchor that does not update on either, a traced mechanism lineage that discriminates rival explanations, and an instrument that tells the construct apart from its reading. No frame can certify on its own that the last condition is met, and a null result is never a proof of absence. The framework offers the shape any identification must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The specific cause stays open until the ground settles it.
