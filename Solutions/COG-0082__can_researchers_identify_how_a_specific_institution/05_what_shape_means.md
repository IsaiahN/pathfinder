## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For COG-0082 the shape is the four-condition union in `01` section 0b: slot-level association, a pinned anchor independent of the common cause, a traced mechanism lineage, and a discriminating instrument. The name, which specific institution caused a specific change, is not the shape. It is the thing the shape constrains.

**A shape constrains; it does not fill.** "An anchor that does not update" is a shape. "This particular schooling law caused the score rise in this district" is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The question is cast in effect terms throughout: not "is this institution causal" but "what would have to be true of the bond for the change to be attributable rather than merely accompanied." That description, not the vocabulary of any one method tradition, is what lets a low-overlap frame recognise it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework stops "at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, a randomization or a natural experiment is an import of a new dimension of variation, not merely another term inside the observational vocabulary. Declaring the instrument valid does not make it neutral; it makes the assumption visible, which is why provenance and declared stakes matter.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden failure here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

This is exactly the accompany-versus-cause problem. A score change is a reading; the institution, the common cause, and several rival mechanisms can all project the same reading. Reading the cause off the projection is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (the anchor, the trace, the better instrument), not a more confident interpretation of the same reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. What the framework does and does not return here

**Does return:** the conditions under which an ordered bond can be distinguished from a common-cause bond; the requirement that the anchor not update on either variable; the requirement that the lineage be recorded; the requirement that the reading discriminate the source. These are held terms, bound in section 0b before the run, so they can accrue a residual and be refused.

**Does not return:** the name of the institution, the identity of the hidden common cause in a specific case, the validity of a particular instrument, or a uniqueness claim that this is the only method of identification. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific institution at all. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in the frame's library. The four conditions are not missing from this derivation; they are the terms bound in `01` section 0b. What is genuinely missing, and therefore nameable only by a ground reading or a provenanced import, is the specific cause and source-discriminating instrument for a particular case. I keep both statements: the conditions are legitimate held terms, and the specific name is outside what the framework can return. This mirrors the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05` section 5b and is kept visible for the same reason.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prestigious methodologists who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a real case.

**The outside frame must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter. Agreement given after the framework is explained is not counted as evidence (Fig 2 :40).
