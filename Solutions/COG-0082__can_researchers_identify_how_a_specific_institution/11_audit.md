## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured. I ran the trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 bond types, Fig 2 anchor test and common-cause panel, Fig 13 lineage, and Fig 9 union rule.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: accompaniment is a symmetric bond, causation is ordered, four conditions must hold, the frame cannot certify its own completeness.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about an institution, for example "this compulsory schooling law caused the score rise in this district."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which anchor is legitimate in the specific case (the shorthand says "independent variation", the case requires a defence of the exclusion restriction);
- whether the measure discriminates the construct from drift at the resolution available (the shorthand says "discriminating instrument" as a condition, not as a verified property);
- which rival traces are actually observable in the historical record (the shorthand says "traced lineage", the case requires documents that may not exist).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: accompaniment and causation are different bonds, so an association cannot be promoted to an identification by precision alone. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
