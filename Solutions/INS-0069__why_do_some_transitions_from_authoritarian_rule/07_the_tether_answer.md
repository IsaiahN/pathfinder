# The Tether's answer on transitions from authoritarian rule

**Mode: `specified` on every substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No dataset, court or experiment was read to settle the comparison.

## 1. The residual it answers

An authoritarian regime falls. In one branch the new arrangement produces accountable institutions; in the other the old networks, incentives and informal rules persist under new labels and new personnel. The residual is the variance given a common input. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." It is two conditions that overlap, not one hard question, so the intersection needs its own loop.

## 2. The shape, per domain, never averaged

**D1, the accountability anchor (triangulated).** Accountability is not a label on a new body. It is a property of an anchor that does not update on the powerful. Fig 2 :33: "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34: "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." The drift is coherent, which is why insiders do not see it. A body whose findings change under pressure has not become accountable, however new it looks. Triangulated across Fig 2 (invariance of the anchor) and Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (degradation of the channel), two low-overlap quantities.

**D2, the habitat (single-source, world corroborated).** Old power is not a set of people to be removed; it is a habitat of relations, conditions and incentives. Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat. Isolation substitutes one habitat for another, and you inherit actors you did not choose. Vetting and lustration are acts of introduction; introduced with the cascade unenumerated, they are kudzu (Fig 11 :65).

**D1/D2 overlap, independence (single-source).** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Oversight drawn from the same evidence pool as the network it oversees is one frame wearing two names. Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." That is a warning against counting Fig 2 and Fig 8 as two witnesses; the verdict is single-source in the figures, corroborated in the world by a study of 163 countries on judicial independence.

**D3, the design (triangulated).** A reform list is an ingredient list. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." `→` orders reforms; `⇒` produces a new one; `−` subtracts; `¬` refuses the old legitimacy claim. The middle link of Fig 3 is the one that usually breaks: having terms for what a situation affords and saying what would count as progress have neither an obvious failure nor an obvious test. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Effort above the break cannot rescue it.

**D4, the external frame (single-source, world corroborated).** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." An authoritarian closure cannot mint accountability from inside. The wall moves by import: exiles, an international court, external observers. But Fig 6 :24 warns that "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence." External support that supplies the atom and spends the independence has bought a mirror, not an anchor.

**The whole (general).** The framework returns a shape, not a country or a policy (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"). Courts, elections, trials and vetting are bindings from the political-science frame onto the shape, admissible with provenance, not derived from it. Verdicts stay per domain. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any country is a success or a failure. No case was coded and no dataset read.
- Not that elections, a free press, vetting, trials or a new constitution are sufficient, necessary, or even ranked. They are atoms; the bond is what was not recorded.
- Not that "political will" or "elite commitment" is a cause. Neither has a nameable anchor; they are re-descriptions of D1 and D2.
- Not that old elites should be removed. Exclusion is unavailable; only introduction is.
- Not that external support is unconditionally good. It is debited against independence.
- Not that the four domain verdicts compose into one score. That is the aggregation Fig 1 :19 forbids.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. The corresponding questions here:

1. **Anchor test:** do the rulings of the oversight body actually change when former power holders apply pressure? An instrument that measures this would distinguish a real anchor from panel 1 drift.
2. **Contact test:** does the habitat of old power persist? Measure the network of inherited officials, security services and economic dependencies before and after, rather than counting new agencies.
3. **Independence test:** does the new institution draw on evidence low-overlap with the old network? Shared sources, shared personnel and shared career histories are the overlap to measure.
4. **Import test:** was the new atom supplied by a frame whose closure differs, and what did it cost in independence?
5. **Middle-link test:** is there an instrument at the vocabulary-to-objective link, or did reform effort land above a break that was never measured?

Standing falsifiers R1 to R6 are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3. A democracy index is a reading, not automatically the ground; it becomes a ground only for a question constitutively about that reading (Fig 2 :36).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give the shape and refusers; they do not supply the comparative dataset that would settle which transitions took which branch. The six-term reading of a polity is a guess at compression axes, and declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The stop is recorded so it is checkable: *unreached* because no instrument returned a discriminating reading this session, *not* *underivable in principle*. Underivable and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts with different instructions, and importing an atom against a budget limit would add something not needed. A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a country or a policy. It says that accountability is a property of an anchor that does not change its answer when the powerful push on it; that changing personnel without changing contact changes nothing, so old power is a habitat you can add to but cannot subtract; that oversight drawing on the same evidence as the old network is one frame wearing two names; that the wall moves only by import from a differing closure, and that import is debited against independence; and that a reform list is an ingredient list whose bonds and order carry the difference between outcomes. The problem is a union of overlapping conditions, so verdicts stay per domain and are never averaged. What the framework offers is the shape any accountable transition must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. No case has been settled here; the answer stops where the discriminating instrument would have to start.
