## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The variance in outcome given a common input: the regime falls, and in one branch accountability is produced while in the other old power is reproduced. Arity: two overlapping conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Symmetry: not symmetric; the transition is directed and the bonds are ordered. Scale: a single reform is first order; the regime arrangement is second order, and a settled reform becomes an atom at the next level.
- **Domain 1, the accountability anchor.** Actors: oversight bodies, courts, auditors, press, citizens as witnesses. Currency: whether a ruling changes when the powerful push. Anchor: what actually happened, which must not move when pushed. Question type: anchor test. It is a domain because the anchor is nameable.
- **Domain 2, the habitat of old power.** Actors: inherited officials, security services, party cadres, business networks, patronage ties. Currency: contact and influence. Anchor: none; the habitat updates and is explicitly not the ground. Question type: enumeration, never composition.
- **Domain 3, the transition design.** Actors: negotiators, drafters, reformers. Currency: bonds and order, including refusal. Anchor: the ground settles each reform; a gate is not the ground. Question type: dependency chain.
- **Domain 4, the external frame.** Actors: exiles, diaspora, international courts, donors. Currency: imports. Anchor: a frame whose closure differs. Question type: import with source gate and shadow test.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate D2, find a differing closure in D4, bond the reforms in D3, then settle whether the resulting body is an anchor in D1. Refuse the old legitimacy claim before minting (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). The middle link of Fig 3 is where chains usually break.

Strain test: the habitat updates while the anchor must not; these strain only if assigned to one domain. Fig 11 :73 separates them: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not." Re-census dissolves the strain. No hidden strains remain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a country name or policy list does not. Accountability requires an anchor whose ruling does not change when the powerful push, in a body whose evidence is low-overlap with the old network. Old power is a habitat you can add to but cannot subtract, so personnel replacement alone changes nothing and introduction must be done with the cascade enumerated. The wall moves only by import from a differing closure, and that import is debited against independence. Reforms are ordered bonds, not an ingredient list, and the middle link of the dependency chain needs an instrument before effort above it counts. The problem is a union compound, so the four domain verdicts stay separate and are never averaged. Everything here is `specified` and ungrounded; the framework returns a shape, not a name.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet leads with the shape and the refuters. The real cost is spending outside readers' independence by sending a recording (a list of policy recommendations) instead of a generator (the residual and its refuters). Fig 2 :42 prices it: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings to courts, elections, vetting and trials are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent packet can be superseded, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measure showing that oversight rulings change under pressure, or a before/after map showing the old habitat was genuinely dismantled, would break the corresponding verdict. A derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or a re-census showing independence is two quantities rather than one, would change the figure layer. A search that finds no discriminating instrument is `unreached`, not a proof of absence. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
