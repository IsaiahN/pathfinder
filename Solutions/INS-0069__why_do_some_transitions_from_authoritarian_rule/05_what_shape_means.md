## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A shape: an accountable transition is one whose oversight body does not update on the powerful, whose institutions have low evidence-overlap with the old network, whose habitat has been enumerated and worked, whose wall moved by a verified import, and whose reforms are bonded in an order with an instrument at the middle link.
- A name (not given by the figures): "Tunisia succeeded because of a truth commission," or "lustration is the answer," or "electoral competition produces accountability." These are bindings from the democratization frame onto the shape. They are hypotheses with provenance, not derivations.

The distinction matters here because the obvious move in this field is to answer with a country list or a policy checklist. The figures forbid that move at the record level: naming would mean already holding the term.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, then look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start from the effect terms (does the ruling change under pressure?) rather than from "democracy," "civil society," or "political will."
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly Fig 9 :55's union. The question must be unbundled before it is searched.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Saying "accountable institutions" borrows a field term; saying "a ruling that does not change when the powerful push on it" describes an effect anyone can check.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the whole reason `02_outreach_email.md` leads with failure conditions.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place; by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here that means the shape and the refuter are the same object viewed twice: R1 to R6 in `01` are the shape stated as failures.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question the ground would be an instrument that measures whether oversight rulings change under pressure and whether old-network contact persists. Democracy indices are candidates, but they are readings, and section 4 says why a reading is not automatically a source.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming "judicial independence" or "vetting" is such an import from political science or transitional justice. It is legitimate once recorded as such; it is illegitimate when presented as something the figure set derived.

## 4. Why the obvious reading is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists," and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Map that to this question. "A country held free elections" is a reading. Several sources could produce it: a genuinely non-updating anchor, a captured elite that can afford to lose an election, an external imposition, or a coalition that has not yet been tested. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a country story.

The same essay, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, give the second half: drift. "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together. The safeguard named in the essay is strikingly close to F1: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." That is Fig 2's anchor, arrived at from human cognition rather than from the figures. It is secondary corroboration, and I record it as such.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming courts, elections or vetting?

**No, with a condition.** The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Courts, elections, vetting and free press are not missing primitives of the figure set; they are terms from the political-science frame being bound onto the shape. Naming them is a **binding from a differing closure**, which Fig 6 permits as an import, provided it carries provenance and passes a shadow test.

What remains forbidden: presenting the binding as a derivation, claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world"), or treating "we held elections" as evidence that the anchor does not update. The shape governs; the binding is a candidate that must pay at the ground.

Both claims are now recorded: the shape-not-a-name limit governs missing primitives, and the import rule governs naming held-frame terms onto the shape. Neither is silently dropped.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A solution that gathers famous democratization scholars to agree with it is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried, with a refuter in front (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Also: "You may all agree and be wrong."

Same essay on standing: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And the rule that governs this whole folder: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and cannot settle it.

## 7. The one-paragraph version of "shape" for this question

A shape here is the form any accountable transition must have, stated before any country or policy is named: an oversight anchor that does not bend when the powerful push, institutions with low evidence-overlap with the old network, a habitat that has been enumerated and actively worked rather than relabelled, an import from a closure that differs, and a bonded sequence with an instrument at the middle link. The figures return that shape, not a name. Courts, elections, trials and vetting are bindings from the democratization frame onto the shape, admissible only with provenance and only after they pay at the ground. The refuters in `01` are the shape stated as failures, which is why they can be checked before any theory is shown.
