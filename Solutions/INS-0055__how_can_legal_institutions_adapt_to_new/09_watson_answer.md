## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a legal order must preserve while it changes. Option A: treat stability and predictability as a stock to be maximized, and adaptation as a loss to be minimized. Option B: re-ground stability as a non-updating reference with a monitored channel, and adaptation as a refuse-then-mint operation with provenance, per-slot verdicts and methods rather than one-occasion recordings. What each leads to: A gives a frozen library that can add rules but cannot record one as wrong, until the channel saturates and the drift is invisible; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters and a declared gap where no figure names an institution.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A rule that has stopped paying against the social condition it governs must change without ceasing to be a reliable reference for expectation. Arity: three conditions (predictability, responsiveness, legitimacy of the changing seat), so a **union compound**, not one dial. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Symmetry: "adapt" is ordered, a response to a residual; "stable" is a threshold relation, Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities". Scale: one case slot, the doctrine across cases, the social habitat, and the verification seat.
- **Domain 1, adjudication, per case slot.** Actors: judge, parties, held rule, case facts. Currency: a ruling that pays or fails. Anchor: the case record and evidence neither side can move. This is one slot of the union. Anchor is nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, doctrine and institution.** Actors: courts, legislature, precedent record, doctrine. Currency: methods and head starts across the scale membrane. Anchor: the practiced rule of recognition plus the legal ground. Overlaps D1 and D3. Live strain S1: the validity-anchor is itself a moving social practice.
- **Domain 3, social habitat.** Actors: conditions, customs, technologies, markets, affected people. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground that settles whether the rule still pays; the habitat itself updates and is not the ground, Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does."
- **Domain 4, verification and legitimacy.** Actors: reviewing seat, legal community, constitutional text, public. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. This is the seat over the other three. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero and its agreement is one pool talking.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); settle a first-order ruling before composing it into precedent (Fig 12 :104); keep the channel reachable throughout (Fig 10 :24). Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

**Strain test applied.** Fig 11 :73 versus the question's "new social conditions" is not a contradiction after re-census: conditions are habitat and update; the ground that settles the rule does not. Hart's practiced rule of recognition versus Fig 2 :33 is a live, narrowed strain S1, recorded rather than smoothed. The good regulator theorem versus Fig 10 :42 dissolved by seat: controller models, verifier does not.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The pair is a union of conditions, not a dial. Stability is a non-updating reference with a monitored channel, not frozen text; adaptation is refusal before mint, with the refused rule retained in the record; methods and bonds cross the case/doctrine membrane, not one-occasion outcomes; social conditions are enumerated habitat, not the ground; imports need a source gate and shadow test; agreement and prestige can fund a reform but cannot settle it; conventions must not be promoted into verdicts; the checking room follows the question. The concrete institution, threshold or stability metric is a `gap`. Verdicts by domain: D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` triangulated with live strain S1, D3 `specified` triangulated, D4 `general` structural triangulated with `gap` on occupancy. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A wrong refusal criterion is published with the shape and can be refuted per case slot; the cost of getting this wrong is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a blueprint or a recorded success instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A legal reform is costly and often hard to reverse, so independence is worth paying for here.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings are removable without touching the shape, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already withhold them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. Nothing has to be deleted from the record: a refused rule stays there and loses only its standing to be composed over.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent-seat measurement showing the reference moves when pushed, a demonstrated doctrine that transmits only outcomes and still decides novel cases correctly, a legal analogue reversing the robustness result at the maintaining level, or a figure re-census that returns a concrete institution would each fire one of the seven pre-registered refuters. Until one does, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
