# The Tether's answer: legal adaptation without losing stability

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the concrete blueprint. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §8), not a ground reading. No legal institution was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

A legal order must keep expectation stable while the rule changes when its social condition changes. The demand is a **union of conditions, not one dial**. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Predictability, responsiveness and the legitimacy of the changing seat have different currencies and different anchors, so no single "optimal balance" number is a verdict (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Stability is re-grounded, not frozen.** It is a non-updating reference plus a channel that is monitored for staleness. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 2 :34 "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." When the reference is pushed around by what the deciding seat believes the outcome should be, the pair becomes a mirror chain and predictability is lost without any instrument reporting a fault.
2. **The residual is indexed per case slot.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A society-wide stability index hides the cases where the rule has actually stopped paying.
3. **Change is refusal before mint.** The fifth bin sorts by what is *wrong*, not what is missing: Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." The rule that keeps being wrong and is not improving is the candidate (Fig 5 :59 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"). Nothing is deleted; the refused rule stays in the record and loses its standing to be composed over. Legal vocabulary for this already exists: "stare decisis is not an 'inexorable command'" where decisions are "unworkable or are badly reasoned" (Cornell LII).
4. **What crosses from a case into doctrine is the method, not the outcome.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." A ruling pays at first order, then becomes an operand. A bare replay of its result produces a doctrine that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one.
5. **Social conditions are habitat, enumerated, not the ground and not a standing list of good actors.** Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Introduction is available, exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."), so a managed legal order is permanent work, not a solved state.
6. **Borrowing needs provenance.** Fig 8 :52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." A transplant that resembles the problem will be adopted because it resembles it unless the failure was described first.
7. **Agreement and prestige cannot validate the change.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." And, from *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
8. **Conventions stay conventions.** Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Legal forms, default rules and terms of art are cheap coordination; the failure is promotion into a verdict about the world. Stakes must be declared (Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying.") and rulings carry provenance (Fig 10 :62 "A ruling carries provenance like an atom.").
9. **The room follows the question.** Fig 10 :72 "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." A permanent committee whose composition never changes will keep producing the same shape of answer, and the constancy will be mistaken for predictability.
10. **The institution itself is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". No figure returns a court, a threshold, a standard of review or a stability index. Naming one is a binding from the legal frame onto the shape, a candidate with provenance, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal amount of stability, an optimal amendment threshold, or a stability/adaptation ratio. That is a `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence (Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").
- Not that "stability" is a stock to be preserved by freezing text. Freezing is genotype-style robustness and reduces the variety of available responses (Wagner 2008, an analogue not evidence about courts).
- Not that any named court, doctrine or review standard is *the* answer. Those are bindings, not derivations.
- Not that agreement among scholars, judges or a homogeneous committee settles a reform. That is a convention or a mirror chain.
- Not that the social condition itself is the ground. It is the habitat; the ground is what settles whether the rule still pays.
- Not `grounded`. No court record, dataset or experiment was read as an anchor in this run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the reference actually stay unmoved when the deciding seat pushes on it, as measured by an independent seat with separate evidence? If it moves and nobody detects the drift, finding 2 fails.
- Is a refused rule recorded and thereby removed from the reach, with the old text still in the record? If a legal order can only accumulate and does not saturate, finding 3 fails.
- Does the transmitted object carry the reasoning bond, so a novel case can be decided, or only the one-occasion outcome? Test on cases the precedent did not cover (finding 4).
- Does the habitat enumeration include second-order contact, or only the direct actors? The residual that mattered is often three steps out (Fig 11 :52-54, DDT and the eagles).
- Does provenance actually separate convergent derivation from adopted import in the record, or can reviewers not tell them apart from the contents alone (finding 6)?

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five-part separator is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another legal culture's separator need not be these five. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :142 "The seed was always the seedline." Indiscernibility is not identity: a legal order that looks like the shape and one that merely recites it are different things, and only the record and the ground settle which is which.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter (repeated from `01` §6)

1. A figure line names a concrete institution or returns a universal ratio.
2. A legal order's reference moves when pushed and no independent seat reports drift.
3. A doctrine transmits only one-occasion outcomes and still generates novel correct rulings.
4. A legal system accumulates rules with no refusal operator and no fall of reach.
5. One lever clears both residuals with one anchor and no leftover compound.
6. A legal analogue reverses the robustness result at the maintaining level.
7. Provenance proves unnecessary because adopted imports and convergence separate by content alone.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a blueprint for legal reform, and it does not claim an optimal amount of stability. It says that stability and adaptability are different conditions with different anchors, so the question must be unbundled before it is searched. Stability is a reference the deciding seat cannot move, read through a channel that can go stale; it is not frozen text. Adaptation is a refusal before a mint: the rule that persists in being wrong is refused, kept in the record, and only then replaced, and what crosses from one case into the doctrine is the method and its reasoning, not a replay of the result. Social conditions are habitat to be enumerated, not the ground and not a standing list of good actors; borrowed doctrine needs a source record; agreement and prestige can fund a reform but cannot settle it; and the checking room must change when the question changes. What the framework returns is that shape, its seven written refuters, and the instruments that would close it. The concrete institution stays open until a ground settles it.
