## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> an unmoved reference, a monitored channel, a refusal available before a mint, a method rather than a recording crossing between a case and the doctrine, an enumerated habitat, a provenance record for imports, and verdicts kept per slot.

That is a shape. "A seven-member constitutional court with a two-thirds supermajority and strict scrutiny" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The residual was described as a reference that must not update, not as "the rule of law", before any legal frame was chosen.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the governing essay for outreach: send the failure description, not the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the loop stops at a shape and a refuter, not at a design.

## 3. What licenses a name, and the two errors that look alike

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." It also warns: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Declaring the primitives "does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Applied here: the five-part separator is a visible choice of what to measure in a legal order, not nature's own list of legal virtues.

Two errors must be kept apart, and *How Intelligence Actually Works* supplies the discriminator. The video-frame-versus-photograph thought experiment: the two artifacts are indiscernible and "carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which." That is provenance loss. And: "indiscernibility is not identity." A reform that looks like a convergent derivation from the figures and one that was adopted from a favourite legal theory look the same in their contents. Only the record separates them (Fig 8 :52).

## 4. Why the question's ordinary terms have to be re-grounded

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Several paths create the same reading: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What was owed was the instrument, not a name. "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: "stability", "predictability" and "adaptation" are readings, not sources. A stable output can come from an unmoved reference with a live channel, or from a room whose members all read the same evidence and drift together coherently. The reading cannot discriminate. Fig 2 :34 "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." What is owed is a discriminator, not a stability score. This is the deep reason F1 refuses the single dial in `01`.

The same essay supplies the legal anchor directly in *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move." That is Fig 2 :29 in legal vocabulary, and it is why D1 in the census has a nameable anchor.

## 5. Q2 answered: what licenses a particular legal institution?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular institution, test or threshold is licensed only by the ground settling it in a specific jurisdiction, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the folder commit the forbidden error?** No, but it came close. `01` F8 reports the concrete blueprint as a `gap` and binds no court or number. The earlier draft did name a specific review standard as if the figures returned it; that reading is retracted in `01` §8 and is preserved here as a warning.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially overstated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any legal mechanism. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. An unmoved reference, a channel, a refusal and a method are **all already available bindings** in the legal frame: evidence that neither side can move, published reasons, an unworkability exception, a doctrine of precedent. Naming them is a binding from the legal frame onto held structure, not the invention of a seventh term.

What remains wrong if one calls those bindings an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 "IMPORT adds an atom", and these add none. The binding changes what the legal frame can reach, not what the framework can express.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The readings ("stable", "predictable") still do not determine their sources; a discriminator is still owed. What changed is the reason: the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held mechanisms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result." And the exact rule: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers eminent names to endorse a reform is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the reform a budget to be tried against a ground that does not move, with a failure condition written in advance. *How a Society of Agents Would Work* gives the same rule in group form: "The ground sits under all three: no store's agreement replaces it", and "Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too." But "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The Medium article adds: outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

So `01`, this file and `06` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any legal claim, and they cannot settle the question they report on. That is stated on every substantive file in the folder.
