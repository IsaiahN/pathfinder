## 0. The residual, in effect terms

A legal order has to keep two things true at once. A person must be able to rely on what the rule says today (predictability), and the rule must stop saying something false when the social condition it governs changes (adaptation). The residual is not "how much reform is optimal." It is:

> What response to a rule that has stopped paying against its social condition preserves the rule's status as a non-updating reference for expectation, and what response quietly converts the reference into a frame that negotiates with its own opinion of what the condition should be?

The residual is **per case slot**, never a society-wide average. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A stability index pooled across all domains would throw away the only signal that says where the law has stopped fitting.

The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run (README): an anchor that does not update, a monitored channel, a refusal available before a mint, a method rather than a recording crossing between case and doctrine, and provenance for every import. That binding is written down here so it can be refused by the same test as everything else (Fig 5 :65-66; Formula "Direction").

Two options and what each leads to:

- **A, maximize stability:** freeze the text. Leads to a library that can add rules but cannot record that one is wrong, until the channel saturates and the drift is invisible.
- **B, re-ground the pair:** keep the reference unmoved, keep the channel reachable, refuse before mint, carry methods not recordings, record per-slot verdicts, and stop at the gap. Leads to a checkable shape and a refuter, not a blueprint.

The question is a **union of conditions, not one dial**. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". One lever, for example a higher amendment threshold, clears the region where only predictability binds and leaves the intersection where the rule is also false still standing.

## 1. Residual by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three conditions, predictability of expectation, responsiveness to changed social fact, and legitimacy of the seat that rules on the change. Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-55).
- **Symmetry:** "adapt" is an ordered relation, a response to a residual, while "stable" is a threshold relation between expectation and outcome. Different bonds, so an averaged "amount of change" is a category error. Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities". Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- **Scale:** (i) one case, (ii) the institution or doctrine across cases, (iii) the social habitat in contact with the law, (iv) the verification or legitimacy seat. Fig 4 :20 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The kernel is the same loop at each size.

## 4. Findings

### F1:The pair is a union of conditions, not a dial to be optimized (decisive for the framing)

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 9 :4-5 "Split it rather than search."

"Stability and predictability" has a different currency from "adaptation to new conditions." The first is a threshold on expectation (Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"); the second is an ordered response to a residual. Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." They do not add, so a single "optimal balance" number is not a verdict but a filter at best. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Refuter:** exhibit one lever, in one institution, that moves both quantities with a single anchor and leaves no residual in the intersection. Then the union reading is false and the question becomes one dial.

Triangulated: Fig 9 and Fig 12, low overlap (splitting rule versus pricing rule).

### F2:Stability is a non-updating reference plus a reachable channel, not frozen text

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34 "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings".

Predictability is not the property of a text that never changes. It is the property of a reference that the institution cannot push around, read through a channel that has not gone stale. A court that adjusts the meaning of the constitution to whatever it currently believes the good outcome is has converted the anchor into a frame, and the drift is coherent, so nobody notices. The legal-world articulation of this is exactly the justification of precedent: "promot[ing] the evenhanded, predictable, and consistent development of legal principles, foster[ing] reliance on judicial decisions" (Cornell LII, Wex, stare decisis). The framework's own essay is blunter: "Courts of law rule on evidence, which neither side can move" (*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*).

**Refuter:** a legal order whose ultimate reference demonstrably changes its answer when the deciding seat pushes on it, where an independent seat reports no drift and no stale channel. That would break the anchor test at the ground.

Triangulated: Fig 2 and Fig 10 plus the legal-world source, low overlap between the figure's control diagram and the doctrine of precedent.

### F3:Adaptation to a false rule is a refusal before a mint, not an added layer

Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Operators :58 "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate". Fig 5 :69 "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."

The four ordinary bins of a gap (held, new, mis-attached, mechanism broken) sort by what is missing. A rule that has become false owes a term that says so, and only then a replacement. Fig 5 :59 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at". In legal terms: the rule that keeps being wrong and is not improving is the candidate for overruling or repeal, and the test is persistent-and-flat residual, not the prestige of the rule's age. The U.S. Supreme Court's own formula, "stare decisis is not an 'inexorable command'", with departure where decisions are "unworkable or are badly reasoned" (Cornell LII), is the world's version of the fifth bin.

What a refusal does is subtle and must be stated precisely. Nothing is deleted from the record. What falls is the standing of the refused rule to be composed over. Fig 6 :42-43 (amendment): "What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall." So a legal system that can only add rules and cannot record one as refused accumulates a library that grows while its reach falls, and has no operator to say what is wrong.

**Refuter:** a legal order shown to accumulate rules without any refusal operator, where reach does not fall and the channel does not saturate. Or a demonstration that the persistent-and-flat residual test selects the wrong rule in a real line of cases.

Triangulated: Fig 5, Operators table, Fig 6, plus the legal-world statement of unworkability.

### F4:What crosses from a case into doctrine is the method, not the recording of one occasion

Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."

A ruling that pays against the ground settles at first order. It can then become an atom for the next case. What crosses is the reapplicable method, the reasoning and the bond that joined facts to rule, not the outcome as a replay. Replayed downward, a precedent produces "a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one" (Fig 4 :25 onward). This is the honest distinction between a precedent as a method and a precedent as a talisman. Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A headnote that records the result without the bond records ingredients, not the molecule (Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording.").

**Refuter:** a doctrine shown to transmit only one-occasion outcomes, with no method or bond, that still generates novel correct rulings under changed conditions across multiple independent case slots.

Triangulated: Fig 4 and Fig 12, different quantities (scale membrane versus composition structure).

### F5:Social conditions are habitat, enumerated, not the ground and not a standing list of good actors

Fig 11 :73 "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."

"New social conditions" are the habitat in contact with the rule. They are read off the world, including second-order contact. Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A reform that adjusts doctrine without changing contact with the condition is not adaptation. And the seat's power over the habitat is one-sided: Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A legislature can introduce a new actor or incentive; it cannot subtract the social fact that brought the case. That is why a managed legal order is permanent work, not a solved state.

**Refuter:** a legal system shown to remove a social condition from its habitat by fiat, with no substitution, and still settle the residual. Or a standing list of favoured actors shown to keep the same valence across a change of residual.

Triangulated: Fig 11 and the biology source (Wagner 2008) at low overlap; see `09_watson_answer.md` WORLD.

### F6:Borrowing a doctrine from another frame needs a source gate and a shadow test

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

Legal transplants, comparative borrowing and import of economic or technological concepts are imports. What they add is accessible, not a new atom in the world (Fig 6 :25-26). An import is legitimate only when the residual was described in effect terms first (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"), and when the record shows where it came from and what gap it explains. Fig 6 :30 "Cast it in effect terms." Without that, a borrowed test that resembles the problem will be adopted because it resembles it, which is the apophenia the figures name.

**Refuter:** an imported doctrine produced with no source record and no statement of the gap it explains, where independent reviewers can still separate convergent derivation from adoption by inspecting the contents alone.

Triangulated: Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 9.

### F7:Agreement and prestige cannot validate an adaptation; independence is bought by the cost of being wrong

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A reform endorsed by a like-minded committee, or by a respected name, is a convention or a prestige transfer, not a settlement. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." The legitimate ask is a budget to try the change against a non-updating ground and a written failure condition, not endorsement. Note that agreement about a shared meaning is good and is the product: legal language needs conventions. The failure is when a convention decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one").

**Refuter:** a reform validated solely by the authority of its authors or by unanimity of a homogeneous body, where the result still tracks an independent non-updating ground across changed conditions and no shared-evidence confound can be shown.

Triangulated: Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 10, and the essay (same author as figures, so the essay adds no independent witness weight).

### F8:The figures return a shape, not a court, a test, or a number

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Canonical figures README: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."

No figure line selects a constitutional amendment threshold, a standard of review, a term limit, or a stability index. The shape returned is the five-part separator in the README: unmoved reference, monitored channel, refusal before mint, method not recording, provenance for imports, plus per-slot verdicts. Specific institutions are bindings from the legal frame onto that shape. They are candidates with provenance, not derivations. This is not an invitation to invent a number. It is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence (Fig 13 :134).

**Refuter:** produce a figure line that names a concrete legal institution, or one that returns a universal stability/adaptation ratio. Either breaks this finding.

Triangulated: Fig 9, Fig 13, canonical README.

### F9:The checking room must change when the question changes

Fig 10 :72 "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 9 :60 separates budget filters from verdict witnesses.

A permanent committee whose composition is fixed while the social question moves will keep returning the same shape of answer, and the stability of its output will be mistaken for predictability. The fix is not to add more members of the same kind. It is to summon the next frame against the residual the last one surfaced, under the rule that the pool is unauthored first and examined second (Seat Map: a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed, or the habitat enumerated).

**Refuter:** a fixed-composition room shown to produce non-drifting, ground-tracked verdicts across a demonstrable change of the question, with independent confirmation that its members draw on separate evidence.

Triangulated: Fig 10, Fig 7, Seat Map.

### F10:A convention is efficient for coordination and fatal when it decides an outcome

Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it". Fig 10 :46 "The seat may author what has no truth value and nothing that does." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Fig 10 :59 "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."

Legal institutions legitimately author conventions: forms, default rules, terms of art, procedures. Those make coordination cheap and are the product, not a defect. The failure is promotion, when a declared meaning is treated as a settled fact about the world. A default rule that both coordinates behaviour and decides a contested fact without a ground reading has stopped being a convention. Where a stake cannot be removed it must be declared (Fig 10 :59), and a ruling must carry provenance: "A ruling carries provenance like an atom" (Fig 10 :62), so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it.

**Refuter:** a convention shown to decide a contested outcome while remaining checkable by an independent ground that does not update on the convention, with no hidden stake. Then the promotion finding is too broad.

Triangulated: Fig 2, Fig 10, Seat Map.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287 "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is produced that names a concrete legal institution, or returns a universal stability/adaptation ratio (breaks F8).
2. A legal order is demonstrated in which the reference for expectation changes its answer when the seat pushes on it, and an independent seat reports no drift and no stale channel (breaks F2).
3. A doctrine is shown that transmits only one-occasion recordings and still generates novel correct rulings across changed conditions (breaks F4).
4. A legal system is shown that can only accumulate rules, has no refusal operator, and suffers no fall of reach or channel saturation (breaks F3).
5. The union reading fails: one lever is found that clears both residuals with one anchor and no leftover compound (breaks F1).
6. A legal-analogue empirical study reverses the biology result, showing that freezing individual rules increases adaptive capacity at the maintaining level (breaks the world corroboration for D3 and sends the census back).
7. Provenance is shown to be unnecessary: adopted imports and convergent derivations can be separated reliably by content alone (breaks F6).

If any fires, withdraw the affected finding and re-census. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 7. Recommendation

1. Frame the question as a union of conditions and refuse the single dial (F1).
2. Re-ground stability as an unmoved reference with a monitored channel, not as frozen text (F2).
3. Put refusal before mint, and record the refusal without deleting anything (F3).
4. Carry methods and bonds across the case/doctrine membrane, not one-occasion recordings (F4).
5. Enumerate the habitat and never claim to subtract a social fact (F5).
6. Require a source gate and shadow test for every borrowed doctrine (F6).
7. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong; never pool agreement as corroboration (F7, F9).
8. Keep conventions as conventions; declare stakes; record rulings with provenance (F10).
9. Publish the refuter with the shape and stop at the gap (F8). Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator, not the conclusion.

## 8. Strains, re-census and retraction log

### Strains

- **S1, live and narrowed.** Hart's rule of recognition is a social rule that exists only because officials practice it (SEP, *Legal Positivism*). That looks like an anchor that updates with practice, against Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Re-census: two different questions. "Is this valid law here?" is constituted by an accepted social rule. "Does this rule still pay against the social condition?" is a ground question, and the ground for it is not moved by what officials think. The rule of recognition anchors validity, not the truth of the policy claim. The strain is recorded, not dissolved away: legal practice makes the anchor of validity a moving practice, so D2's anchor is partly a convention. This is why D2's verdict stays `specified` and the seat's binding stays a `gap`.
- **S2, dissolved by re-census.** Conant and Ashby's good regulator theorem says a regulator must acquire an internal model of its environment ("Every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system", via *Variety (cybernetics)*). That strains against Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Different seats: the institution acting as controller must model the condition; the outside frame checking the ruling must not hold the interior. Fig 10 :68 gives the level rule: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." No contradiction after re-census.
- **S3, dissolved by re-census.** "Stare decisis" looks like a standing rule that precedent is always good, which Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" refuses. Re-census: precedent is a held molecule and a method, not an actor with carried valence; its valence is relative to the residual. The maxim itself is a convention (F10), and the unworkability exception is the refusal operator (F3).
