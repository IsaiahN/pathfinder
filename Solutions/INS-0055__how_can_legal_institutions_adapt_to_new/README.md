## Effect restatement of the question

Decide what a legal order must preserve while its rules change, and what change counts as adaptation rather than as the rule losing its standing as a rule. Option A: treat "stability and predictability" as a stock to be maximized and adaptation as a loss to be minimized. Option B: re-ground stability as a non-updating reference plus a reachable channel to it, and re-ground adaptation as a refuse-then-mint operation with provenance, per-slot verdicts, and methods rather than one-occasion recordings. A leads to a frozen library that cannot record a rule as wrong. B leads to a shape with a pre-registered refuter and an honest gap where no figure names an institution.
