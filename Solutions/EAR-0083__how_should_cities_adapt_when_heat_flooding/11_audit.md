## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: four named conditions with overlapping drivers and shared infrastructure, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55-58), not four independent operands. Symmetry: not a conjunction; thresholds and order dominate, so this is an ordered and thresholded compound. Scale: first order in one hazard and sector, second order at intersections, third order at cascades.
- **Domain 1, hazard physics and exposure.** Actors: heat, flood, water stress, sea-level rise, subsidence, urban heat island. Currency: energy and water flows. Anchor: a gauge, thermometer, mortality record or loss ledger. Union.
- **Domain 2, the urban habitat.** Actors: grey, nature-based and social infrastructure, governance, finance, communities. Currency: contact and capability. Anchor: realised loss. Habitat to be enumerated, never composed.
- **Domain 3, decision and pathway.** Actors: city governments, regulators, insurers, communities. Currency: sequences and options kept open. Anchor: the same realised outcomes, never agreement. Dependency chain with a suspected break at the objective.

Order by dependency: perceive the compound and the cascade, find terms for second-order affordances, state an objective on the compound, choose a route under incomplete information, then monitor and carry (Fig 3 :20). Refuse the partition reading before minting measures (Formula step 2); describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 8 :53).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. I reached union from Fig 9's bundle clause and the figure's single-lever rule rather than from the IPCC compound-events vocabulary; the primary reached it from the same figure plus field corroboration. Different starting points inside one framework, so low overlap between routes but still one evidence pool. The agreement is weak internal consistency, not a second witness.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-58 (union compound), Fig 11 :43-64 (habitat, cascade, introduction, permanence), Fig 12 :111-112 (route), Fig 3 :27 and Fig 2 :29 (instrument and anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: union compound, enumerate outward, introduce not subtract, route with thresholds, instrument the objective, anchor on realised loss, no named technology.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** what a city actually receives if it acts on that shorthand: a named intersection list, a sequenced set of introductions, a threshold register, a compound residual series, and provenance for each local technology.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which hazards go into which intersection, and at which order (the figure gives union and order, not the city's specific pairs);
- which actors are in contact at the second and third step (the figure says enumerate, not what is found);
- what counts as a compound residual at all (the figure requires per-slot measurement, not an average);
- which introduction is available in a given governance and finance context (the figure says exclusion is unavailable, not what can legally or fiscally be introduced).

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: the union shape, the outward enumeration rule, and the requirement that the answer be a route with thresholds and a measured subtraction. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no city ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
