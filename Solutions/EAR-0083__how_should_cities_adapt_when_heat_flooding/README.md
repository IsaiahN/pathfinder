## Restatement in effect terms

A city must choose adaptation spending now. A lever that clears heat alone, or flood alone, or water stress alone, or sea-level rise alone leaves the coincident cases standing, and the losses concentrate where two or more conditions bind at once and cascade into other systems. The decision is between (A) four single-hazard programmes added together, which spend budget on regions where only one condition holds, and (B) a shape that first names the compound cases, enumerates the contact habitat outward to the third-order effect, and commits to a sequenced, measurable, revisable route. A is what most adaptation plans already do; B is what the figures license. What an answer would look like is bound in `01` section 0 as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are structural, not merely legal. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Seat Map :163 says *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Fig 10 :42 says *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless, and sending the whole framework before asking for a verdict spends the independence of the only people who could refute it.
