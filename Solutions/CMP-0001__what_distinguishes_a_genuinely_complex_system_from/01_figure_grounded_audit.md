## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

We hold two terms in common use in complexity science, *genuinely complex system* and *merely complicated system*, and no figure-derived criterion that separates them. The folk separator is numerosity: how many parts, how much detail, how hard to describe. The residual R is the missing criterion C* such that applying it to a system returns a different verdict for an ant colony than for a jet engine, and returns that verdict from contact with the system's behaviour rather than from our description of it.

**Direction (formula step, "Direction").** Write down what an answer would look like and bind it as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual:

> C* = a per-slot, ground-checkable test of how a system's parts are bonded and arranged: the number and kind of bonds, whether loops close, whether a compounding residual survives, and whether settled wholes become atoms at a higher level. It returns a shape and a check list, not a single scalar.

If C* collapses to a part count, it aggregates away the live signal and scores the system with a quantity the frame also produces, which Fig 1 disqualifies. If it cannot be ranged against an anchor, it is a convention, not a criterion.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity / symmetry / scale.** Arity: one place predicate on systems, but deciding it compares two classes, which makes the separator a threshold relation (comparison, ⋛, Fig 12 :85-88: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). Symmetry: ordered, not a symmetric join; complex and complicated are not interchangeable terms. Scale: any stratum, because composition is recursive and the level decides what is irreducible (Fig 12 :103). A cell and an economy must both be assessable.

**One line per domain (actors / currency / anchor).**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1, the composition of the system** | parts (atoms) and their relations (bonds) | description length of the arrangement; actions spent to test whether it holds (Fig 12 :125-126) | the behaviour of the whole, what the system actually does | partition of compositions into degenerate chain versus richly bonded arrangement |
| **D2, the diagnostic (the criterion and the observer)** | the frame, the object slots, the residual | residual indexed per slot | the ground: measured behaviour of the system, which does not move when our description changes | how we tell; whether the test aggregates slots or keeps them |

No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet. Both domains have one: D1's anchor is the behaviour of the whole, D2's anchor is the ground measurement. They are **a partition by question type** (what the system is, versus how we tell) with **a union at the shared anchor** (behaviour), because the same measured behaviour is both D1's output and D2's reference. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The two loops must be kept distinct even where they meet.

Inside D1, structure and dynamics are a union, not two domains. Fig 13 :28: *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."* A cycle is both a bond and a dynamic, so D1 owns the compound and runs one loop over it.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Establish that the anchor is reachable (perception, link 1), then acquire terms for what bonds and loops afford (vocabulary, link 2), then state what counts as the criterion (objective, link 3), and only then attempt to classify a particular system. Effort spent above a broken link cannot rescue one below it: Fig 3 :19 *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* and Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The most neglected link here is link 2, vocabulary for arrangement: Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 3. Findings, verbatim, with a refuter each

### F1. The parts are not the thing (D1, triangulated)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*
Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

So a system's part list underdetermines what the system is. Numerosity records the ingredients and drops the arrangement. Fig 12 :48: *"A molecule is a bonded arrangement. A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching. Nothing about a chain is a different kind of object."* A merely complicated system is that degenerate case: large, but one bond type, no branching, no closure, one level. A genuinely complex system is one whose bonds carry information the parts do not.

**Refuter:** two systems with identical part inventories but different bond structures show identical behaviour under every available ground instrument; or a system with strictly more parts but the same bond structure is ranked more complex by every such instrument. Either result kills the finding.

### F2. More parts do not add a kind (D1, triangulated across Fig 12 and Fig 13)

Fig 13 :113: *"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not. More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span."*
Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."*

This is the general form of F1. Adding atoms enlarges the inventory, not the kind. Complexity lives in the arrangements, which do not run out. Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* So scale is not the separator.

**Refuter:** a composition that adds a genuinely new kind using only parts already held, demonstrated under a ground test, breaks Fig 13's closure claim and this finding with it.

### F3. Loops and levels are the positive separator (D1, triangulated)

Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*
Fig 12 :103: *"Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."*
Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*
Fig 12 :106: *"Which is why a system that cannot settle has one level however large its library."*

A chain cannot close a loop by construction. A genuinely complex system can, and its settled wholes become atoms at the next level. A merely complicated system has one level however large its library. This gives the positive test: count the levels at which settled wholes become parts, and check whether at least one loop closes. Fig 12 :116: *"Four structures a compositional system exhibits, and each names something the flat form cannot write."* The four (Fig 12 :117 isomer, :119 catalyst, :121 cycle, :123 functional group) are what the flat inventory cannot express.

**Refuter:** a one-bond, acyclic, one-level chain that shows the same per-slot compounding residual and the same non-decomposable behaviour as a multi-bond cyclic recursive system under the same ground. Or: the apparent extra levels of a candidate system are built on unsettled terms, so the recursion is fake.

### F4. The remainder compounds (D1, single-source for the chaos reading; companion, not criterion)

Fig 13 :28: *"They alternate, and the alternation is where both the recursion and the randomness come from."*
Fig 13 :30: *"So each step acts on one kind and yields the other, and every link in the chain is changed by the link before it."*
Fig 13 :31-32: *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."*
Fig 13 :124: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*

The figures give a mechanism for emergence: each turn leaves a remainder that re-enters the next; when it compounds through an amplifying chain, it shows as chaos and noise. This is a companion of rich arrangement, **not the separator**. A world source forced the correction: Wiesner and Ladyman (2020) write *"Certainly, chaos and complexity are two distinct phenomena."* Re-census (section 5) keeps the criterion in bonds, loops and levels, and records chaos as a product a turn leaves. This is the one place where a world source conflicted, and it sent the derivation back to the census rather than flipping a verdict.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that every systems with a compounding remainder and closure is complex under the ground, independent of bond richness, would promote chaos to the criterion; the world source above predicts it will not hold.

### F5. The criterion must be per-slot and grounded (D2, triangulated)

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

A single number labelled "complexity" is exactly the aggregate that hides the live signal, and if the system or the proposing frame also produces that number, it cannot score the system. The criterion must be a set of readings against behaviour, slot by slot. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Refuter:** a single scalar that predicts every distinguishing behaviour across low-overlap fields, with an external ground that does not move when the frame changes. That would overturn the "shape, not a scalar" result. Note the world literature (Lloyd 2001, Mitchell 2009, Wiesner and Ladyman 2020) independently expects no such scalar.

### F6. The diagnostic is triangulation, not consensus (D2, triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*
Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*
Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

Two complexity scientists who learned the same textbooks are one witness wearing two names. Their agreement about the criterion is a convention, not corroboration. The right check is whether each frame, separately, ranges the shape claim against the system's behaviour.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that two low-overlap expert frames sharing no evidence pool still converge on the same classification for systems where the ground is silent; that would make consensus admissible here, which Fig 2 currently forbids.

### F7. Capability is an analogue, not the verdict (strain named)

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

This governs capability, not complexity. It is a different quantity in the same neighbourhood: it warns against locating the separator in the part inventory of the system alone, since capability and, by analogy, complex behaviour live in the system-in-contact. **Explicitly refused as a verdict.** The essays (*How a Society of Agents Would Work*, 2026) cite this same line and add that *"The arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'"*, which is why the analogy is tempting. It remains corroboration only.

**Refuter:** a figure line that governs complexity directly and contradicts the contact analogue; the re-census in section 5 found none.

### F8. The framework returns a shape (D2, triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
Fig 13 :135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*
Fig 13 :138: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*

So C* is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can measure it: bonds, closure, levels, per-slot residuals. It is not a numeric threshold and not a name for a new primitive. The honest output is a check list and an open slot. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A system that fails the checks is not proven non-complex in all possible frames; it is reported as not reaching the criterion with the instruments at hand.

**Refuter:** a figure line, found after a genuine re-census, that names a single sufficient quantity for complexity; none was found.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

C* is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A chain with one bond type, no cycle and one level shows the same non-decomposable, per-slot compounding behaviour as a multi-bond, cyclic, recursive system under the same external ground.
2. Two systems with identical part inventories but different bonds and levels behave identically under every available ground instrument.
3. The apparent higher levels of a candidate complex system are composed over unsettled terms (Fig 12 :106), so the recursion is fake.
4. C* is shown to be a frame-internal quantity the system or the proposing frame produces about itself (Fig 1 :27), with no anchor outside that frame.
5. A peer figure line, after a genuine re-census, governs a different quantity than the one cited here, so one of F1-F8 is a strain rather than a reading.
6. A single external scalar predicts every distinguishing behaviour across low-overlap fields, which would overturn F5 and F8.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

2. State mode on every claim (D1 and D2 are `specified`, the shape is `general`, nothing is `grounded`).
