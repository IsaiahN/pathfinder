## Summary verdict, per domain

- **Domain 1, the composition of the system.** The separator is the arrangement, not the inventory. A merely complicated system is the degenerate case: many parts, one bond type, no branching, no closing loop, one level, with behaviour recoverable from its ingredient list. A genuinely complex system carries information in the bonds, closes loops, compounds a residual, and has settled wholes that become atoms for a higher level. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 12 and Fig 13, low overlap).
- **Domain 2, the diagnostic.** The criterion must be read per slot against the system's behaviour, never aggregated into a part count, and never self-scored by the frame that proposes it. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low overlap).
- No domain ends in `gap`. One live strain is named rather than hidden: the figures return a shape and a check list, not a numeric cut-off. An earlier reading that made chaos the separator was **retracted after a world-source conflict and a re-census**; see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 5 and `09_watson_answer.md`.
