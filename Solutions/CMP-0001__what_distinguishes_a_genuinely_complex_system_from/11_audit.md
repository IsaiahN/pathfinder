## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :5, :48, :104, :121, :124 and Fig 13 :28, :113, :124, plus Fig 1 :19 and Fig 2 :29.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: parts are not the thing; chain is the degenerate case; loop closure and recursive levels are the positive separator; chaos is a companion; the test is per-slot and grounded; no scalar.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete classification rule that could be applied to a named system, for example a score assigning a system to "complex" or "complicated".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **cut between degenerate and rich arrangement**. The figures say a chain is the degenerate case and that genuine complexity needs closure and levels, but they give no instrument that returns the cut-off. Turning the shape into a binary label would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
- the **companion status of chaos**. Fig 13 :124 puts chaos among derived quantities. A concrete rule built on "chaos present, therefore complex" silently reintroduces a retracted reading. The shorthand carries the correction; the concretisation can lose it.
- the **per-slot constraint**. A concrete rule that reports one label per system has already aggregated the slots, which Fig 1 :22 says destroys the signal. The shorthand says "per slot"; the label does not.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a part list underdetermines a system, and a chain cannot close a loop by construction. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
