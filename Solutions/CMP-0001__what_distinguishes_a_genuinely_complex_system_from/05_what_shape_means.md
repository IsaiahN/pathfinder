## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can measure it. For CMP-0001 the shape is: *look at the bonds, the closure of loops, the compounding of the residual, and the number of levels at which settled wholes become parts; do not look at the part count.* That constrains; it does not fill. It gives no threshold number.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was the folk pair "parts/detail", and the nearby terms that narrowed it were bond, chain, cycle, level and residual.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence C* is stated as what you would observe (per-slot behaviour, closed loops, new levels), not as a mechanism name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email leads with a check list.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done because *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is a shape plus a refuter, not a numeric verdict.

## 3. The essays' own statement of the exact question

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* gives the closest public statement of the residual in this whole corpus:

> *"Brains did not become intelligent by aggregating more neurons, a whale has more neurons than a human and is not smarter. Brains became intelligent by structural reorganization under selection: the architecture changed, not merely the count."*

and the general form:

> *"Adding data and parameters to a fixed architecture is the whale strategy: more units, same structure, diminishing returns. The escape is not more of the same substrate. It is a change of structure, which is a bet on architecture, not on scale."*

This is the single strongest world-side corroboration for F1 and F2. It is high overlap (framework author), so it corroborates the shape but cannot independently settle it.

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* adds, from outside the framework's own essays: *"The arrangement mattered more than the members, which is Figure 12's 'The arrangement is the substance.'"* This is the same shape applied to groups rather than parts.

## 4. What licenses a name, and why this question does not need one

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that would be an instrument or experiment showing that the bond/loop/level check predicts a behaviour that the part count does not.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The bond vocabulary used in `01` is a visible choice from a particular frame, not nature's own list.

## 5. Correction to my own §4 and to the draft audit

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding the statement of *any* criterion. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing primitive**, one not held in Γ. C* does not name a new primitive. It names a shape made from terms the figures already hold: bond, chain, cycle, level, residual, per-slot. Those are all in the library. So naming C* is legal; claiming C* is a new primitive or a single number is not. The parallel correction in `01` section 5 (chaos demoted to companion) is recorded there and not silently erased here.

## 6. Prestige, and why this audit is a simulation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous complexity scientists who agree with C* would be the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Source-discrimination limit

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason a single scalar is unsafe here: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* A global complexity score would read the projection and name the source (parts, or bonds, or chaos) without an instrument that discriminates among them. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. This is the essay-side reason for F5 and F8 in `01`.
