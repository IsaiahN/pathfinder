# Expert verification packet: the Tether on complex versus complicated

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the criterion, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a check list, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A system's part list is a flat formula: it records the ingredients and drops the arrangement, and the bond carries the information the parts do not. A merely complicated system is the degenerate case of that machinery, many parts joined by one kind of bond with no closing loop and no higher level, its behaviour recoverable from its ingredient list. A genuinely complex system carries information in the bonds, closes at least one loop, compounds a per-slot residual instead of washing it out, and has settled wholes that become atoms at a higher level. Chaos, emergence and adaptation are products a composition leaves behind, not the separator. The test must be read per slot against the system's behaviour, never aggregated into a single scalar and never decided by agreement among frames that share an evidence pool.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 13
- **Statement:** the arrangement, not the inventory, carries the difference; the chain is the degenerate case, not a different kind of object; adding parts does not add a kind.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :48 *"A molecule is a bonded arrangement. A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching. Nothing about a chain is a different kind of object."* Fig 13 :113 *"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not. More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span."*
- **Refuter:** two systems with identical part inventories but different bonds and levels behave identically under a discriminating ground instrument; or a composition adds a new kind using only parts already held.

### C2 - systems-facing; checkable by a complexity researcher against real systems
- **Statement:** the positive separator is loop closure plus recursive levels, with a compounding per-slot residual as a companion. A one-bond acyclic one-level chain cannot close a loop by construction.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* Fig 12 :106 *"Which is why a system that cannot settle has one level however large its library."* Fig 13 :124 *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."*
- **What to check:** take one system you would call complex and one you would call merely complicated. Do the predicted differences actually appear: a closed loop, a higher level composed over settled wholes, a residual that compounds? Then try the falsifying case: an acyclic one-level chain with the same number of parts. Does it show the same non-decomposable behaviour?
- **Refuter:** the acyclic chain matches the cyclic recursive system under the same ground; or the apparent higher levels of the complex system are composed over unsettled terms; or chaos alone, independent of bond richness, predicts every case.

### C3 - diagnostic; checkable by anyone with a system and an instrument
- **Statement:** the criterion is per-slot, ground-ranged, and not a scalar; agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is not evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **Refuter:** a single external scalar predicts every distinguishing behaviour across low-overlap fields, or the criterion can only be scored by the same frame that proposes it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a complexity measure, index or ranking. No number is offered.
- Not a claim that chaos, emergence, adaptation or self-organization is the separator. An earlier draft made chaos the criterion and was **retracted** after Wiesner and Ladyman (2020) noted that *"Certainly, chaos and complexity are two distinct phenomena"*; the re-census is in `01` section 5.
- Not a claim that complex and complicated are different kinds of object. Fig 12 :48 says the chain is not a different kind of object.
- Not a claim that all complex systems share the same parts. Fig 13 :121 *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*
- Not a claim that capability (Fig 11) is the same quantity as complexity. It is an analogue only.
- Not `grounded`. This packet cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating ground, not more argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved that case. Here:

1. **Isomer test:** identical parts, different bonds and levels, measured with an instrument that can discriminate. Do they behave differently?
2. **Loop test:** does an acyclic one-level chain with the same part count match the cyclic recursive system under the same ground?
3. **Settlement test:** are the higher levels composed over terms the ground paid for (Fig 12 :106)?
4. **Scalar test:** does one external scalar predict every distinguishing behaviour across low-overlap fields?

Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. This derivation is produced by a language model inside the framework author's evidence pool. Its stake is in internal consistency, not in which value the ground returns. That is declared here rather than modelled silently (Fig 10 and Seat Map :171).
