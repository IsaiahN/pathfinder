## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when a political institution channels a contending conflict peacefully and when it intensifies it. The raw question invites a list of peaceful constitutions. That invitation is refused: which named institution works, in which conflict, is left as an open slot because no figure line governs that selection.

Two options are on the table. Option A: name an institution from a correlation, for instance a democracy, a court or a power-sharing deal, and call it peaceful. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, state the conditions in effect terms, write the refuters before the conclusion, and leave the name open until a ground settles it.

What each leads to. A sends a `general`-mode verdict with a correlation standing in for a cause and a binding presented as a derivation; it also spends the independence of the people asked to check it, because they receive a conclusion before they have described a residual. B sends a checkable shape, a dependency order, written refuters and an honest `gap`. The framework's own rule is that a shape can be narrowed and a name can be licensed only by a ground, never derived from a reading that cannot disclose its source.

The residual in effect terms. For each contending party slot `s`, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is the gap between what a party expected from the political order and what it actually got. The question asks which arrangements keep `R_s` arriving through a maintained channel to something that settles it, and which arrangements stop it arriving or turn the institution itself into another frame.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor. A candidate with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: more than two operands, at least two contending frames plus the institution, usually several parties, a media field and an external environment. Symmetry: ordered and comparative. Peace is a threshold relative to a baseline, not a symmetric join of the parties; a channel is a sequence, a goal is a comparison and progress is a subtraction, so a vocabulary without those bonds can name a state called peace and cannot name a route to one. Scale: three scales, the single dispute, the institution that receives it, and the chain of institutions across repeated disputes; the membrane between them is lossy, so a method that works at one scale is not a recording that transfers to another. Fig 9 names this a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

**Partition or union.** A union, not a partition. Peaceful channeling needs the conditions together; each can hold without the others, and the intersections are categorically different from any single condition. A single lever, for instance holding an election, clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, enumerate the habitat and read each slot's residual; vocabulary, terms for what the conflict affords; objective, say what counts as progress in the parties' own terms; planning, a route through institutions; learn and carry, name what worked, verify it, bring it to the next dispute. The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance, and the middle link is where political institutions usually break: having procedures is not the same as being able to say what counts as progress, and that link has the fewest instruments.

**Strain test applied before any verdict.** The common-cause line in Fig 2 governs source overlap, while the shared-atom line in Fig 8 governs convergent derivation from separate sources. These are different quantities, so there is no strain after re-census. The one live boundary is convention versus anchor, recorded in section 5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The conjunction survives, each condition kept separate and never averaged into an institutional quality score. An institution channels a conflict peacefully when its answer does not move when a party pushes on it and it is not counted as a party; when the conflict is read per contending group rather than as a national average; when the two sides draw on genuinely different evidence; when a compound conflict is unbundled before a single lever is applied; when the contact channel still resolves each live slot; when the keeper's stake is declared and a rule that decides a contested outcome has stopped being a convention; and, across repeated disputes, when the next arena differs from the last and matches the residual the previous one surfaced.

It intensifies the conflict when the institution becomes another frame, converges on whoever has most influence, pools the contending groups into one score, applies one lever to a compound whose intersections survive, lets its channel saturate while the ground is still reachable in principle, hides an interested keeper behind a clean procedure, or meets a changed conflict with an unchanged room.

Channeling and intensifying are isomers over the same institutional atom set: same ingredients, different bonds, opposite behaviour at the ground. The five conditions form a functional group whose presence fixes the kind; the named institution is the qualifier, which is why the framework can return the group and withhold the name. Conditions are ordered by dependency, not combined by a symmetric conjunction. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A ranking of institutions is a filter for where to look, never a witness for what channels conflict.

Which named institution satisfies the conjunction, in which conflict, is a declared `gap`, `unreached at this budget`. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument that separates the institution did not move under pressure from the metric saturated. Mode `specified` on all conditions, `general` on the bare shape, nothing `grounded`. Status `candidate` until a ground settles it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not zero. If the non-updating anchor condition is wrong, the whole packet misdescribes the mechanism and a researcher who tested a responsive, settled institution would dismiss the shape. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers: sending a recording before they have described a residual lets them agree with a conclusion rather than check a claim. That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual, the shape and the refuter, and by never pooling later agreement as corroboration. The open slot is stated plainly, so a wrong binding is not smuggled in as a derivation.

- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is committed and no institution is named as a result. Each of the conditions is a written term that can be withdrawn independently if its refuter fires, and the pre-registered refuter in section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` lists which observation withdraws which condition. A circulated packet can be superseded by a corrected one with the lineage intact. The one thing that is not undoable by revision is a spent reader's independence: once a reader has seen the conclusion before writing their own failure criterion, that check cannot be recovered.

- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot residual series showing that an institution which changed under pressure nonetheless settled every group and stayed settled would break the anchor condition. An aggregate-only institution that reduced each slot separately and sustainably would break the per-slot condition. Parties with near-total shared evidence whose settlement settled each slot at the ground would break independence. One lever that cleared every intersection of a compound conflict would break unbundling. A fixed institution whose fidelity and ground pressure stayed high across three changed disputes while residuals fell would break the room-chain condition. An interested, uncheckable keeper whose readings still settled every slot would break the keeper condition. A source-discriminating instrument that closes the open slot would improve the answer without changing the shape. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
