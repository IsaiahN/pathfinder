## Restatement as an effect

What is being decided is not which constitution is best. It is this: for each contending party slot `s`, the residual `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is the gap between what that party expected from the political order and what it actually received (Fig 1 :19). One option keeps `R_s` arriving through a maintained channel to a ground that settles it, so each party is corrected separately by something none of them can move. The other option turns the institution into one more frame that updates on the parties, so the readings drift together, the drift is coherent, and nobody notices (Fig 2 :33-34). The first is peaceful channeling. The second is intensification. An answer would look like a set of conditions stated in effect terms, with the name of the particular institution left open until a ground settles it.

**Bound term (formula step "Direction"):** "an institution channels a conflict peacefully" means a conjunction of conditions on the anchor, the channel, the independence of the frames, the unbundling of the conflict, and the selection between arenas. It is written down here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The institutional addresses printed on public venue pages (for example the Journal of Peace Research editorial office) are listed as institutional routes, not as personal contact details of a named person.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim. "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention" (Seat Map :163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
- No bulk-send plan. "A room does not upgrade the label" (Seat Map :283). Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
- No claim that any world source settles the question. Figures are primary and decide. World sources corroborate only, and most of them draw on the same broad evidence pool, so they are one witness, not several.
