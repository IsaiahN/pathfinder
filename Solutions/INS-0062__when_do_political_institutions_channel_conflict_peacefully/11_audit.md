## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the restated residual before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. A candidate with no nameable anchor was not admitted.

**R (arity / symmetry / scale).** For each contending party slot `s`, `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` is the gap between what a party expected from the political order and what it got. Arity: more than two operands, at least two contending frames plus the institution, usually several parties, a media field and external sponsors. Symmetry: ordered and comparative. A threshold is not a symmetric join; a channel is a sequence, a goal is a comparison, progress is a subtraction. A vocabulary without those bonds can name a state called peace and cannot name a route to one. Scale: three scales, the single dispute, the receiving institution, and the chain of institutions across repeated disputes, with a lossy membrane between them. Union, not partition: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either, so the verdicts must be kept per domain and never averaged.

**Order by dependency.** Describe the residual per slot before naming a frame; unbundle a compound before applying a lever; declare the keeper's stake before accepting a ruling; verify non-updating and independence before calling a result a verdict; select the next room against the residual after the current handoff. The dependency order is not cosmetic: reversing description and frame selection is the apophenia failure, in which a preferred constitution is chosen first and a conflict is then found to fit it.

My census matches the primary's five domains, its union reading, its two rejected candidates and its dependency order. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x).** The verbatim figure set: Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29, :33, :34, :35, :40, :41 and :46 (anchor and independence), Fig 8 :48 (source overlap), Fig 9 :32, :55-56 and :60 (shape, union, filters), Fig 10 :24, :47, :59, :60 and :72 (channel decay, convention boundary, stake, room), Fig 11 :39, :51 and :66 (habitat), Fig 7 :35-36 (handoff).
2. **Abstraction (T_A).** The shorthand carried in `01`, `07`, `08` and `09`: a non-updating anchor, a per-slot channel, independent frames, an unbundled compound, a room that differs from the last, and a declared stake. Channeling and intensifying are isomers over the same atom set, the conditions are a functional group, and the named institution is the qualifier.
3. **Concretisation (T_E).** The concrete claim a reader will try to apply: this court, this commission, this election or this power-sharing deal channels this conflict peacefully.

**R_T is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **The name of the anchor.** The shape says what an anchor must do; it cannot say which institution supplies it in a given conflict. F10 is a declared `gap`, so any concretisation that names a winner has added a binding rather than recovered one.
- **The convention-versus-anchor boundary.** A mutual veto is a convention between frames and admissible, but the same rule becomes a verdict the moment it decides the contested outcome. The shorthand compresses this to declare the stake; the two sides of the boundary are only separable at the ground.
- **The source-discriminating instrument.** The shape requires a non-updating anchor. The shorthand cannot distinguish an anchor that genuinely did not move from a metric that saturated, so the instrument needed to close F10 is lost in compression and must be rebuilt from per-group data on the way down.
- **Valence relative to the residual.** The shorthand invites the reading that an institution is peaceful in itself. Fig 11 :51 forbids a standing list across residual changes, so this caveat must be reinstated at the concretisation or the shape becomes a ranking.
- **The union intersections.** After one lever, the residue in the intersections is categorically different from the single-condition cases. A shorthand that reports conflict resolved has dropped the compound structure even when it retained the word union.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a ruling must not move when a party pushes, and readings must be kept per contending group. That is the residue worth sending.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the five-condition shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.

## 5. Strains and re-census

- **Convention versus anchor (live, not smoothed).** Fig 2 :41 calls agreement about shared meaning efficient for a convention, while Fig 2 :43 and Fig 10 :47 say a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one. Consociational power sharing gives each group a mutual veto, which looks like a convention deciding outcomes. Re-census: a veto is a convention between frames and is admissible as a convention; it becomes a verdict, and fails the anchor condition, the moment it decides the contested outcome rather than setting the rule under which the outcome is later settled. The strain is in the boundary, not in the figures. It is kept live because a real institution can sit on either side of it, and the check is empirical, not verbal.
- **Shared atoms versus shared evidence (resolved by re-census).** Fig 8's convergence line says two frames can hold the same atom by separate derivation, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Read as same conclusions, this strains against Fig 2 :40's disqualification of shared-pool agreement. Re-census: the quantity in Fig 2 is source overlap, the quantity in Fig 8 is convergent derivation from separate sources. Different objects, no contradiction.
- **Aggregate metrics (resolved by re-census).** Fig 1 :22 disqualifies averaging live slots while Fig 9 :60 permits filters for the budget. A national conflict index is admissible for deciding where to look and inadmissible as a witness for a per-slot verdict. Different offices.
