# The Tether's answer on COG-0072

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We predict how a child will attend, regulate emotion, expect others to behave, and understand agency. The prediction holds in some habitats and fails in others. The residual is per pattern, per slot, and per habitat. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :5: "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." So the question is a union of slots with its own loop, not one global question about "development."

## 2. The shape, per domain, verdicts kept separate

**Domain 1, the child's kernel.** Fig 4 :20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." What is robust is the kernel (the loop, the capacity to abstract from regularities, the recursion) and the invariant cross-kind compositions over it. What is local is whatever the conditions decide. `general`, triangulated (Fig 4 plus the kernel/seed language of the essays).

**Domain 2, the habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Family structure, schooling, and local work are habitat actors with no fixed valence; they bind a pattern when that pattern is composed over them. Their effect must be enumerated from the world, not composed from a theory (Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."). `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus Fig 4).

**Domain 3, the verification frame.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A pattern counts as robust only if it recurs under a shared instrument in habitats that do not draw on the same evidence. Many studies of one population, or one tradition using one task, are one room (Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."). `general` on the method, triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 7).

**The arrangement relation.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Same starting capacities under different cultural bonds give different developmental substances; where only the slot-filler changes, the cultures are members of one functional group (Fig 12 :124). `specified`, single-source for the figure definition, corroborated by the plasticity/canalization analogue in biology.

**The limit.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns the partition rule, not the inventory. Which specific pattern is universal, and what causes a given onset, are ground readings requiring a discriminating instrument in each habitat.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of universals. The figures return a shape and a rule, not names of patterns (canonical figures README: "a shape, not a name").
- Not that culture is a fixed cause. Actor valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that many studies agreeing makes a pattern robust (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that a cross-cultural difference is a deficit. An isomer is a different substance, not a broken one.
- Not that the six physical terms map literally onto child development. That reading strained and was retracted (`01` F9).
- Not `grounded`. No cross-cultural measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it, and what the world already says

The ground is a discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Presence and sequence**, separated from timing. Callaghan et al. (2005) used one procedure in five cultures and found the false-belief milestone at about five everywhere; they also note that "Schooling may help to refine understanding of false belief, but is not necessary for the onset of this understanding." That is a robust onset and a local refinement in one slot.
- **Trajectory versus rate.** Liu et al. (2008) found "parallel developmental trajectories of false-belief understanding" with timing differences of "2 or more years." Same capacity, different schedule.
- **What counts as the construct.** Rothbaum et al. (2000): "What constitutes sensitive, responsive caregiving is likely to reflect indigenous values and goals, which are apt to differ from one society to the next." The reading (a sensitivity score) does not determine the source (a culturally specific goal).
- **Sampling.** Henrich et al. (2010): "members of WEIRD societies, including young children, are among the least representative populations one could find for generalizing about humans." This is the world-side version of Fig 2 :40.
- **Habitat enumeration.** Super and Harkness (1986) enumerate the developmental niche as settings, customs, and caretaker psychology, a world-side match for Fig 11 :43-44.

Standing refuters (written before the run): a kernel-classed pattern absent in a low-overlap habitat under a standardised instrument; a local-classed pattern invariant across such habitats; evidence drawn from one pool; an agent-only change that moves the outcome without changing contact.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The figure set can say what robust and local would look like, and can rule out a global aggregated verdict, but cannot say which of the many slots fall where. That needs instruments in low-overlap habitats. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical figures README: "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a list of developmental universals, and it does not claim that everything is cultural. It says that development is a union of separate patterns, each with its own residual, so one global verdict is a category error. What is robust is the kernel: the loop, the capacity to learn from regularities, and the invariant compositions over them. What depends on family structure, schooling, or local work is the arrangement of that kernel in a habitat, and an actor's effect is relative to the pattern being tracked, not fixed. Same capacities under different bonds give different developmental substances, the way isomers share a formula and differ in behaviour. A pattern is only called robust if it recurs under one instrument in habitats that do not draw on the same evidence, because agreement from a single pool is the pool talking. The framework offers that partition rule, its four written refuters, and the instruments that would close each slot. The inventory stays open until the ground settles it.
