## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 13 :137-138: "And it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

*The Library of Babel* (**fetched**): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." It adds: "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a robust pattern is kernel-like and triangulated across low-overlap habitats" is a shape. "Pattern 71 is universal" is a name, and the framework does not return it.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel* (**fetched**): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. The anchor is the question itself: "If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (**fetched**): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For cross-cultural development, that means an instrument in each habitat, not a literature count.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Here the developmental-niche and plasticity literatures are such imports, debited against independence.

*They're Scanning Books* (**inherited quote**): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." The imported dimensions here are *habitat* and *reaction norm*, not new developmental stages.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**fetched**) states why a literature count is not a name: "You may all agree and be wrong." And: "An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."

## 4. Why a global list of universals is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (**fetched**) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce one reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." What resolves it was "the arrival of a better instrument," not an argument: "This answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: a cross-cultural study observes that children in many places pass a false-belief task at about five. That reading does not identify the source (biological maturation, universal experience, or a shared instrument tradition). Callaghan et al. say so themselves: "Whether the synchrony results more from biological maturation or from experiences that are universal across the cultures sampled, or both, remains at issue." Naming the source from the reading would be the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**fetched**): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A list of universals produced by this derivation cannot validate itself.

## 5. The answer to "what shape means" for this question

For COG-0072 the shape has four parts, all statable before any specific pattern is classified:

1. **A union of slots, not a single verdict** (Fig 1 :19, Fig 9 :5). The shape is a partition rule applied per pattern.
2. **A kernel and its conditions** (Fig 4 :20, :34-35). Robustness = invariance of the kernel or of an invariant cross-kind composition under habitat change; local dependence = composition over habitat actors.
3. **Same atoms, different arrangement** (Fig 12 :117). Cultural variation is a bond difference, not a different set of starting capacities.
4. **Triangulation across low-overlap habitats** (Fig 2 :31, :40). Robustness is a property of the evidence relation, not of the number of studies.

The shape does **not** include: which named pattern is universal, whether a given effect is biological maturation or universal experience, or how large any cultural difference is. Those are ground readings.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any developmental capacity. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like," a primitive not held in the library. Naming a capacity that is already in the child's held set is a binding from the world frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is naming the *source* of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources (my §4 metamerism point, not retracted). Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second correction: I first applied Fig 13's six physical terms literally to development. That was a strain and is retracted in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §F9. The invariant is Fig 4's kernel, not a literal six-term developmental vocabulary.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**fetched**): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cross-cultural psychologists who agree with the partition rule is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (**fetched**): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**A note straight from the essay about this very topic.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (**fetched**) begins from a human-development class: "The cognitive development I was thinking about was not actually a blank slate. Children had parents who taught them, nurtured them, corrected them. The blank slate was a half-truth. The child is a lineage." That is the world-facing reason the kernel/conditions split matters here: the kernel is real, and it is never alone.
