## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict" (Fig 7 :35). Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). Bring a characterised residual, never the source (Fig 10 :52).

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
