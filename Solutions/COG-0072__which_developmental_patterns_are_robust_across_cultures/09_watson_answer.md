## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how to separate developmental patterns that are robust across cultures from those that depend on family structure, schooling, or local work. Option A: produce a global list of universal and culture-specific patterns from the existing literature. Option B: keep the figure-derived partition rule and refuse the inventory. What each leads to: A aggregates slots, mistakes pooled agreement for triangulation, and delivers names the framework says it cannot return; B delivers a shape, a per-slot method, pre-registered refuters, and an open inventory until instruments settle each slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A prediction about a child's attention, emotional regulation, expectations of others, norms and agency holds in some habitats and fails in others. Arity: many slots, each its own residual. Symmetry: asymmetric across habitats, symmetric across instances of one pattern under one instrument. Scale: step (a learning trial and correction), cycle (a stage transition), level (the habitat), lineage (transmission). Fig 1 :19 says "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated," and Fig 9 :5 says such a bundle "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- **Domain 1, the developing child.** Actors: child, caretakers, peers, teachers. Currency: residuals and corrections. Anchor: the child's own contact with what works. This is the candidate kernel.
- **Domain 2, the habitat.** Actors: settings, customs of care, caretaker psychology, school, work. Currency: contact and introduction of actors. Anchor: the environment actually rewards some behaviour. The child-in-a-habitat is a union compound, not two partitions: Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
- **Domain 3, the verification frame.** Actors: researchers, samples, instruments. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground reached by an instrument in each habitat, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is low.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Enumerate the habitat first (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."); describe the per-slot residual second (Fig 9 :27-28); seek low-overlap habitats third (Fig 2 :31); classify the pattern last. A classification read before the enumeration is below the break.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources corroborate only; they never overrule the figures. Fields were chosen for low overlap.

- **Cross-cultural psychology / sampling.** Joseph Henrich, Steven Heine and Ara Norenzayan, *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*, 2010, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/abs/weirdest-people-in-the-world/BF84F7517D56AFF7B7EB58411A554C17 : "members of WEIRD societies, including young children, are among the least representative populations one could find for generalizing about humans." Predicts that a literature built on one population cannot establish robustness; corroborates Fig 2 :40. Overlap: high with other psychology sources, low with figures.
- **Developmental psychology / habitat enumeration.** Charles Super and Sara Harkness, *International Journal of Behavioral Development*, 1986, https://chhd.media.uconn.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3087/2024/06/Developmental-Niche-1986.pdf : "The developmental niche has three components: the physical and social settings in which the child lives; the customs of child care and child rearing; and the psychology of the caretakers." Predicts that the habitat must be enumerated before classification; corroborates Fig 11 :43-44.
- **Developmental psychology / robust onset.** Tara Callaghan et al., *Psychological Science*, 2005, https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.0956-7976.2005.01544.x/ : "With a standardized procedure, we found synchrony in the onset of mentalistic reasoning, with children crossing the false-belief milestone at approximately 5 years of age in every culture studied." Also: "Schooling may help to refine understanding of false belief, but is not necessary for the onset of this understanding." Predicts a robust onset and a local refinement in one slot; corroborates the kernel/local split.
- **Developmental psychology / timing.** Hua Liu et al. (with Olson, Zhai and Wellman), *Developmental Psychology*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1037/0012-1649.44.2.523 : "parallel developmental trajectories of false-belief understanding for children in China and North America coupled with significant differences in the timing of development across communities." Predicts same capacity, different schedule; corroborates the arrangement reading. Overlap: shares the false-belief evidence pool with Callaghan, so it is not an independent witness.
- **Cultural psychology / relative valence.** Fred Rothbaum, John Weisz, Martha Pott, Kazuo Miyake and Gilda Morelli, *American Psychologist*, 2000, https://weiszlab.fas.harvard.edu/files/jweisz/files/rothbaum_et_al_2000_j_ch_fam_studies.pdf : "What constitutes sensitive, responsive caregiving is likely to reflect indigenous values and goals, which are apt to differ from one society to the next." Predicts that the same construct name hides different goals; corroborates Fig 11 :49.
- **Evolutionary biology / plasticity and canalization.** Giuseppe Fusco and Alessandro Minelli, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, 2010, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2817147/ : "Phenotypic plasticity can be defined as 'the ability of individual genotypes to produce different phenotypes when exposed to different environmental conditions'." The same review defines canalization as reduced sensitivity to environmental variation. Predicts that one system can be buffered in one respect and conditional in another; corroborates the kernel/local split from a field with no contact with the framework. Overlap: low with all psychology sources.

Apparent conflict handled: Henrich et al. suggest little is safely universal; Callaghan et al. report a universal milestone. This is not a conflict at the figures' level. Per Fig 1 :19 they describe different slots and different quantities (variation of expression versus presence and sequence of onset). The conflict rule sent the reading back to the census, where it dissolved; it did not flip a verdict. No world source overrode a figure line. Agreement among the psychology sources is one pool talking, not multiple witnesses.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The partition rule survives; the inventory is refused. Robustness is kernel-like invariance plus recurrence under a shared instrument in low-overlap habitats. Local dependence is composition over habitat actors whose valence is relative to the pattern tracked. Same capacities under different bonds yield different developmental substances, as isomers do, with a functional-group fallback when only the slot-filler changes. The question is a union of slots, so no global answer is admissible. Which specific pattern is universal is unreached, not unreachable: it needs a discriminating instrument per habitat. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the packet is used as written, because it states its mode and four refuters before the conclusion. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of claimed universals instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A slot classification can be withdrawn without touching the partition rule. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove the inventory claim and the literal six-term map. No primitive has to be abandoned and no data are deleted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminating instrument showing that an apparent universal vanishes when the task tradition changes, or that a "local" pattern is invariant under an indigenous instrument in low-overlap habitats, would reclassify that slot. An agent-only intervention that moves an outcome without changing contact would refute Fig 11 :40 and the kernel/habitat split. A cross-cultural instrument returning a complete, correct inventory from the figure set alone would refute the shape-not-a-name limit. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
