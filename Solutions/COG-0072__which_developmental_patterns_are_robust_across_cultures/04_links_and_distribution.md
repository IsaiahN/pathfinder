## Wider literature that bears on the question

These are world sources, secondary corroboration only. The figures decide; none of these can replace a figure verdict. Each was opened and is quoted in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Cross-cultural psychology / sampling.** Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan (2010), *Behavioral and Brain Sciences*: "members of WEIRD societies, including young children, are among the least representative populations one could find for generalizing about humans." This is the world-side version of Fig 2 :40, that agreement within one evidence pool is not corroboration.
2. **Developmental psychology / habitat enumeration.** Super and Harkness (1986), *International Journal of Behavioral Development*: "The developmental niche has three components: the physical and social settings in which the child lives; the customs of child care and child rearing; and the psychology of the caretakers." This is a world-side enumeration of the habitat Fig 11 says must be enumerated, never composed.
3. **Cross-cultural developmental psychology / robust onset.** Callaghan et al. (2005), *Psychological Science*: "With a standardized procedure, we found synchrony in the onset of mentalistic reasoning, with children crossing the false-belief milestone at approximately 5 years of age in every culture studied." The same paper adds: "Schooling may help to refine understanding of false belief, but is not necessary for the onset of this understanding." Together these split a slot into a robust onset and a local refinement, exactly as the partition rule predicts.
4. **Meta-analysis / timing is local, trajectory is robust.** Liu et al. (2008), *Developmental Psychology*: "parallel developmental trajectories of false-belief understanding for children in China and North America coupled with significant differences in the timing of development across communities." Same capacity, different schedule.
5. **Cultural psychology / valence is relative.** Rothbaum et al. (2000), *American Psychologist*: "What constitutes sensitive, responsive caregiving is likely to reflect indigenous values and goals, which are apt to differ from one society to the next." This is the world-side match for Fig 11 :49.
6. **Evolutionary biology / plasticity and canalization.** Fusco and Minelli (2010), *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*: "Phenotypic plasticity can be defined as 'the ability of individual genotypes to produce different phenotypes when exposed to different environmental conditions'." The same review defines canalization as a reduction of sensitivity to environmental variation. That is the low-overlap analogue of the local/robust split: same genotype, different environment, different outcome, unless the trait is buffered.

Overlap note: sources 3 and 4 draw partly on the same false-belief evidence base, and sources 2 and 5 are both culture-and-development work. They are counted as a small number of evidence pools, not as six independent witnesses. Sources 1 through 5 are psychology; source 6 is evolutionary biology, a genuinely different field.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." **Fig 4 :25** - "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the partition rule were true, before giving them the framework.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Select for ability to refute, not for likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are authority; (4) if they are curious, offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write their own refuter and state their own mode.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source," and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the framework, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method**, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it.
