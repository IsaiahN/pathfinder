## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that the figure-grounded verdict is a ground reading.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map :283). Agreement by people selected for receptiveness is a convention, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40, Seat Map :163).
2. **A verifier who holds the interior cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Prestige is not action.** From the essay *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names asked to endorse the answer is the named collapse.

## Honest standing of this folder

This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. From the same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns a shape: a decision procedure for assigning harm-slots to levers, with falsifiers, and an honest stop where no instrument distinguishes the two conditions.
