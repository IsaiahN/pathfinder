## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`, indexed by affected group, application and time. Arity: four place (harm-slot, lever, arrangement prediction, ground outcome). Symmetry: asymmetric, because changing the model and changing the law are not interchangeable. Scale: one design loop, the deployment habitat, and the lossy channel between them.
- **D1, the model design frame.** Actors: parameters, training data, objectives, decoding, mitigations, developers. Currency: composition inside `closure(Γ)`, `|φ|` cost, per-slot residual reduction. Anchor: a held-out outcome the ground pays. Admitted.
- **D2, the institutional and market habitat.** Actors: regulators, courts, standards bodies, markets, deployers, affected communities. Currency: rules, prices, liability, audit duty, participation. Anchor: the market and the court, which are named candidates in Fig 2 :29. Admitted.
- **D3, the channel and instruments.** Actors: benchmarks, audits, impact assessments, incident reports, metrics. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, because the channel decays and the ground does not. Admitted.
- **D4, the affected people.** Actors: the persons who bear the harm. Currency: experienced harm, material loss, opportunity. Anchor: the outcome itself. Admitted.
- **Excluded before any figure line is applied.** A harm as a fixed property of the model alone has no lever-independent anchor. A harm as a fixed property of society alone has no lever either. Both are absorbed into the D1, D2 and D4 compounds.
- **Partition or union.** A union. D1 overlaps D3, and D2 overlaps D4, so there are two compounds with their own loops. The whole question is a union of two conditions, C1 (design changes contact) and C2 (habitat changes what the ground pays).
- **Order by dependency.** Name the anchor per slot, describe the residual before choosing a lever, check for refusal or SEARCHED before importing, then assign the lever and measure. The middle link, having terms for what design and habitat each afford and saying what counts as progress, has no instrument, so it is the link that breaks first.

Strain test: the six physical terms of Fig 13 were tried on harm-slots and only fit by renaming a training corpus as energy and a regulation as a gradient. That is a strain, so Fig 13 governs the honest stop here, not the lever assignment. After re-census no unresolved strain remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives: assign a harm-slot to model design when the change composes inside the model's closure and changes contact with the outcome that settles that harm; assign it to institutions, markets or law when the harm is constituted by habitat actors, conditions or relations; treat a harm in both conditions as a compound with its own loop, two levers and two instruments. Keep the verdicts per slot and never average them. Channel failures are design's cleanest case; agent-only improvements that leave the habitat unchanged are design's characteristic failure. Which named harm falls into which bucket at the ground is a declared gap, not a hidden answer. No world source flipped a verdict. This is `specified` and ungrounded, so it is a candidate, not an accepted result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, figure lines, non-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error can be found rather than absorbed. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a definitive harm table instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The named assignments were never made, so nothing has to be withdrawn. If a refuter fires, the decision procedure is corrected and the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is superseded. No primitive, law or habitat is removed by this solution.
- **reversed by:** a per-slot ground reading, never agreement. A prospective test comparing a model-only change with a habitat-only change while measuring the outcome that settles the harm would assign the lever for that slot. An anchor that moves when pushed, a channel independently measured as restored that leaves the harm unchanged, a model-only change that reduces a habitat-constituted harm, or a fixed assignment invariant across a change of residual would each break the shape. Until one fires, the verdict stays candidate.
