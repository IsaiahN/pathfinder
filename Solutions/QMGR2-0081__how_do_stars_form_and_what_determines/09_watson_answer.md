## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mechanism is a `gap`. The residual is per object and per mass regime, so the question is a union of overlapping conditions rather than one lever (Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 9 :54-57). Collapse is a legal first-order flow of energy and gradient (Fig 13 :74/:76). A core does not carry its final mass as a seed carries a small tree; conditions decide (Fig 4 :34-35). The mass distribution is a projection whose source needs a discriminator (Fig 9 :32, Fig 6 :33), and feedback has residual-relative valence (Fig 11 :49). The two channels are at minimum a functional group and possibly isomers (Fig 12 :117/:124). No figure line returns a Jeans mass, IMF slope or characteristic mass, so any such number is an import from astronomy, provenanced as such and not derived. This is `general` on structure, `specified` on the per-domain shape, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest figure-grounded points are the per-slot rule and the seed-not-the-tree rule.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a mechanism name instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No mechanism name is asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct the shape; a discriminating observation can be added and the functional-group-versus-isomer question resolved without touching the derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An observation that shows final mass fully set by initial core mass across environments would refute the seed-not-the-tree claim; a demonstration that per-regime residuals all trend down under one mechanism would refute the per-slot rule; a source-discriminating instrument would close the projection gap; a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the substrate. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
