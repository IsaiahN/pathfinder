## 0. The residual in effect terms

R is not "what is the true theory of star formation." R is the gap between what the instruments return and what any frame can compose from what it holds.

- **What failed to resolve:** a cloud of cold gas and dust, observed in many regimes, yields a distribution of stellar masses with a broad peak near a fraction of a solar mass and a power-law tail at high mass. We can read the distribution. We cannot read, from the distribution alone, which conditions set an individual star's endpoint.
- **Per slot, not per average.** The residual is indexed by object and by regime: low-mass core, intermediate protostar, massive cluster member. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single universal slope is an average over slots, and by the figure's own rule it is where the live signal goes to die.
- **Sought-for shape bound as an ordinary term.** Under the Direction step of the formula, the shape sought is written down and made refusable before the run. The bound claim is: the answer must be per-slot, union-compound, environment-decided rather than seed-carried, and projection-limited rather than source-naming. Section 3 writes the refuter before the verdict.

**Arity / symmetry / scale of R.** Arity: several overlapping conditions, not one. Symmetry: the observed mass distribution is an ordering (more and less), not a symmetric join of two operands, so the bond that describes it is comparison or spectrum, not conjunction. Scale: cloud (parsecs), core (tenths of a parsec), protostar, main-sequence star. Multiple scales with a membrane between them, so a method that works at one scale must be carried up and back down with a measured gap.

## 1. Domain census before choosing any figure line

One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not yet a domain.

### D1. The observational and theoretical physics of star formation
- **Actors:** molecular clouds, dense cores, filaments, disks, protostars, outflows, radiation, magnetic fields, dust, turbulence, neighbouring stars.
- **Currency:** mass, gravitational and thermal energy, momentum, angular momentum, accretion rate, metallicity-dependent cooling.
- **Anchor:** the instrument and the observation, checked by independent instruments. Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A survey or an interferometer is such an anchor; the fit of a model to its own output is not.
- **Question type:** a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition. Gravity, turbulence, thermal physics, feedback, accretion and environment all bind in intersections.

### D2. Framework-internal composition
- **Actors:** the six terms (space, time, energy states; spectrum, gradient, network structures), the seven bonds and negation.
- **Currency:** composition inside closure(Γ), then settlement against the ground.
- **Anchor:** the figures as metalaws, plus the ground for any measurement.
- **Question type:** a partition question about which compositions are legal across the two kinds.

### D3. Verification and outreach
- **Actors:** expert readers, journals, societies, proposal committees.
- **Currency:** agreement and residual.
- **Anchor:** the ground, never agreement. Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
- **Question type:** a checking arrangement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.

### Partition or union

D1 is a union, D2 is a partition boundary, D3 is a checking arrangement. The three do not merge into one question. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." The high-mass and low-mass regimes are specific compounds, not degrees of one difficulty.

2. Have terms for what a core affords: mass reservoir, gradient, flow, relation (Fig 3 :6-8 vocabulary link).
3. State what counts as progress in each regime before planning a universal mechanism (Fig 3 :9-11 objective link).

## 3. Findings F1 to F12

### F1. The residual must stay indexed per mass regime

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."

The IMF is itself a distribution, an aggregate over stars. Treating one slope as the answer collapses distinct formation channels into one number. The world literature already splits the problem: the broad peak and the high-mass tail are attributed to different physics (Hennebelle and Grudić 2024, Bonnell, Larson and Zinnecker 2006, both opened). Fig 1 does not tell us which physics; it tells us that a single averaged verdict is the wrong object.

**Refuter:** if a single mechanism were shown to drive the residual monotonically down in every mass regime with no regime split, and the per-regime residuals trended down together, the per-slot requirement would be an empty discipline and this finding fails.

### F2. The question is a union compound, not a partition

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
Fig 9 :4-5 "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Core mass, turbulence, thermal cooling, accretion history, feedback and environment are overlapping conditions. A single lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :56). This is why the question about final mass is not one variable to be isolated. The 2024 review reports several IMF regimes with universal shapes but non-universal transitions, exactly a union signature.

**Refuter:** if the conditions were shown to be independent, each sufficient on a disjoint set of objects, the union reading collapses to a partition and this finding is withdrawn.

### F3. The seed does not carry the tree

Fig 4 :34-35 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."
Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

This is the sharpest figure line bearing on the physical question. The final mass is not pre-loaded in the initial core like a small tree inside a seed. The core carries what can grow a star; the habitat it lands in, accretion, competition, feedback and ejection, decides the outcome. That is the structural content of the core-accretion versus competitive-accretion distinction in the literature: in one, the prestellar core mass largely fixes the stellar mass; in the other, gas accreted later from beyond the original core dominates (Myers, Dunham and Stephens 2023, opened). The figure does not choose between them. It says that "conditions decide" is the compositional shape, and it refuses the picture of a mass fully carried from the start.

**Refuter:** a clean demonstration that final stellar mass is fully determined by initial core mass across environments, with no detectable role for subsequent conditions, would refute this reading at the ground. The finding would be retracted, not softened.

### F4. Collapse is a flow; the distribution is a spectrum; the cloud is a network

Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow."
Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"
Fig 13 :20 "ordering, so more and less can be said"
Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"
Fig 13 :74 "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."

Under the six-term substrate, gravitational collapse is energy bonded to a gradient, giving a flow. The gravitational instability described by Larson (2002/2003, opened) is exactly a flow that starts when a perturbation grows: a density gradient plus gravity. The mass distribution is a spectrum (ordering, more and less), not a new primitive. The filamentary cloud is a network. These are bindings from the physics frame onto held atoms, not derivations of new primitives. Cross-kind composition is legal, so nothing here forces a forbidden state-state bond.

**Refuter:** if collapse required a seventh primitive not composable from the six, Fig 13 minimality breaks and this binding fails. Equivalently, derive one of the six from the other five.

### F5. The shape-not-a-name limit applies to the mechanism, not to held atoms

Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

The figures can return the shape of a mass-setting answer: per-slot, union, environment-decided, projection-limited. They cannot return the name of the mechanism or a numerical slope. Gradient, spectrum and network are already among the six, so binding "collapse" onto energy plus gradient is provenance, not the invention of a missing primitive. What is refused is any claim that the framework derives a Jeans mass or an IMF index. The canonical README's phrase is about a *missing* thing, and none of these atoms is missing.

**Refuter:** show a figure line that yields a specific characteristic mass or slope from the six-term set. None exists; finding one would refute the gap verdict.

### F6. The observed distribution cannot name its source

Fig 8 :50 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."
Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"

From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened): "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The IMF is a projection. Fragmentation, competitive accretion, ejection and feedback can produce overlapping mass distributions. Naming one as the source of a given reading is the metamerism error the essay describes. The essay's resolution is institutional, not argumentative: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists" and "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." This is a `gap` of the unreached kind, not a silence.

**Refuter:** an observation that uniquely discriminates among candidate mechanisms from the mass distribution alone would dissolve the metamerism and upgrade this finding.

### F7. The missing link is the objective instrument, not more ingredients

Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
Fig 6 :33 "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."

We have abundant ingredients: gas, gravity, turbulence, magnetic fields, feedback. What we lack is a discriminator that reads mechanism off outcome. Fig 6 :33 is the history of astronomy in one line: instruments are improved from worse instruments already returning something, never specified from an account of the target. The literature's call for observational tests that distinguish the models (Bonnell, Larson and Zinnecker 2006) is exactly this instrument-shaped request.

**Refuter:** exhibit a working instrument, at any resolution, that already returns a failing reading capable of being sharpened. If nothing anywhere registers, Fig 6 :40 applies and the search is for a proto-instrument, not a refinement.

### F8. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged

Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

D1 physics, D2 composition and D3 verification have different anchors. The near-universality of the IMF is a regularity within one evidence pool, so its agreement is efficient as a convention (Fig 2 :41) but disqualifying as corroboration of a mechanism. The review literature agrees on the shape and disagrees on variation (Bastian, Covey and Meyer 2010 versus Kroupa and Jerabkova 2019, both opened); that disagreement is a fact about the union, not a signal to average.

**Refuter:** if two genuinely independent instruments, drawing on different evidence, returned the same mechanism verdict, the agreement would be triangulation and this separation of verdicts could be relaxed for that claim.

### F9. Generators cross scales; recordings do not

Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

A fit to one star-forming region is a recording of one occasion. A method that predicts which conditions change the mass distribution in a new region is a generator. The condensation from the full figure set to the shorthand used here is lossy; `11_audit.md` records the round trip gap R_T as nonzero. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would be the dead-reckoning error.

**Refuter:** a round trip in which nothing is dropped would set R_T to zero and refute this. It is checked in `11_audit.md` and found false.

### F10. No last theory of star formation

Fig 7 :6 "the limit: no last room"
Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"
Fig 13 :90 "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."

A final, all-explaining theory of star formation would be a terminal room. The framework forbids that room. Each model closes some of the gap and no finite set of models arrives. This is a structural limit on the answer's form, not a claim that the physics is unknowable; the ground keeps voting.

**Refuter:** a closed theory that admits no further question and settles every regime would break this. None has been produced; the 2024 review still lists open problems.

### F11. Conditions have residual-relative valence

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."
Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Feedback is not intrinsically a mass-limiter or a mass-builder. Outflows can unbind gas and terminate accretion, or widen cavities that let radiation escape and thereby protect accretion; the valence depends on the residual and the regime. The habitat must be enumerated outward: cloud conditions, core, disk, outflow, neighbouring stars, metallicity, then the second-order effects three steps out. Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground" is the same selection logic at the unmaintained limit: what survives is what the ground kept, not what a frame predicted.

**Refuter:** a standing, unconditional list of mass-setting actors, valid across all residuals, would refute this. None exists in the sources read.

### F12. Two currencies, not one

Fig 12 :125 "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
Fig 12 :126 "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."

A compact IMF formula is cheap to state and may take a whole observing campaign to settle; a complex simulation is expensive to describe and may be refuted by one measurement. The budget decides how much of the mechanism space is reached, and it must not be smuggled into the verdict. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

**Refuter:** a single currency in which description cost and action cost genuinely add would dissolve this. The figures keep them apart by construction.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The following were committed before the synthesis in `07` and `09` was written. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A figure line is exhibited that returns a numerical characteristic mass or IMF slope from the six-term set. Then F5's `gap` is false.
2. Final stellar mass is demonstrated, across environments, to be fully carried by initial core mass with no detectable role for subsequent conditions. Then F3 fails at the ground.
3. Per-regime residuals trend down together under one mechanism with no regime split, so aggregation preserves rather than destroys the signal. Then F1 fails.
4. An instrument is produced that names the mass-setting source directly from the observed distribution. Then F6's metamerism limit is dissolved.
5. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a seventh is required to compose collapse. Then F4 fails.
6. Two genuinely independent instruments, with different evidence pools, return the same mechanism verdict. Then the separation in F8 is relaxed for that claim (it does not flip the physics verdict, which still comes from the ground).

Until one fires, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.
