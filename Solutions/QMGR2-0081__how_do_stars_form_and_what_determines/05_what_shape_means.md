## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its mechanism. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `per-slot, union-compound, conditions-decide, projection-limited` is a shape.
- "turbulent fragmentation sets the peak and competitive accretion sets the tail" is a name, and the figures do not return it. It is a world finding from Bonnell, Larson and Zinnecker (2006), provenanced as such.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from exemplar canonical mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." Applied here: describe what a mass-setting mechanism would predict observably, then let an astronomer check it against a survey.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (opened): "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." For this question the finish line is not reached; the shape stops at the instrument limit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** From the formula: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." An observation that discriminates among mechanisms would name the source.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing "Jeans mass" from physics into the framework is such a binding; it must be recorded as imported, not derived.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from exemplar canonical mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Binding collapse onto energy plus gradient makes that choice visible; it does not make it neutral.

## 4. Why the IMF reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several sources can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case.

The IMF is the reading. Fragmentation, competitive accretion, ejection, outflows and thermal physics are candidate sources. Reading a mechanism straight off the distribution repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation, not a cleverer fit. This is F6 and F7 in `01`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This shape cannot certify itself; it must be ranged against the astronomical ground.

## 5. The bundle case: why one mechanism is not enough

*The Library of Babel* (opened) states the union rule directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54-57 and it governs the mass question: the low-mass peak, the intermediate regime and the high-mass tail are searched separately, then combined. The 2024 review's list of several regimes with non-universal transitions is the world-side confirmation of the bundle.

## 6. Correction to my own §1 and §3 (kept visible)

I initially stated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any specific structure or process. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Energy, gradient, spectrum and network are all already held (Fig 13). Binding "collapse" onto energy plus gradient, or "mass distribution" onto spectrum, is a **binding from the astronomy frame onto held atoms**, not the invention of a missing primitive.

What remains wrong in a naive reading is claiming that binding moves the wall. Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." The binding adds no atom. It is provenance, and its value is that the choice is recorded. Both the rule and the correction are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous astronomers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to be tried and an observation that would refute it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is a simulacrum. So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures, not tests of the physics. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Shape for this question, stated once

> The figures constrain the form of any answer about stellar mass: it must be indexed per object and per regime, it must treat the conditions as a union of overlapping bindings rather than a single lever, it must refuse the idea that the endpoint is fully carried in the seed, and it must treat the observed distribution as a projection whose source requires a discriminator. It returns none of the masses, slopes or mechanisms. Those are imports from astronomy, provenanced as such, and open until the ground settles them.
