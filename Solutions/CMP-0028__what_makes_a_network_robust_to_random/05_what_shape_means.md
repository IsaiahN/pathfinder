# What "shape" means for this question

Sources opened 2026-11-22. Four of the eight framework essays were fetched and are quoted here. The remaining four essays are listed at the end for completeness but were **not** re-opened this session, so no quotation is taken from them. The canonical figures README local copy was also read.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the number is known. For this question the shape is:

- the quantity is indexed per slot, not aggregated, so the degree distribution matters and the mean degree does not (Fig 1 :19 and :22);
- the two removal regimes form a union compound and must be answered separately (Fig 9 :54-58);
- one regime has a ranking seat and the other does not (Fig 11 :37 and :58);
- the name "scale-free" and the number `f_c` are not part of the shape.

`(N, L)` plus a mean degree is a state description. `heterogeneity across slots plus a ranking seat` is a shape. A percolation threshold is a name with a number attached.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the bootstrapped terms are "slot", "arrangement", "regime", "seat", "threshold".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* That is why the residual is stated as "the same graph behaves two ways", not as "hubs fail".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The checkable description here is: under uniform removal the largest component should shrink slowly; under degree-ranked removal it should collapse at a small fraction.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the compound shape, once found, is exactly what the percolation experiment is designed to test.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A measured percolation threshold settles the number.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Scale-free", `gamma`, and `f_c` come from the physics frame with full citations in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They are imports, not derivations.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was *"proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* For this question: observing that a network stays connected under random removal does not by itself identify which source arrangement produced it. Heavy tails, modularity with redundant cross-links, and nestedness can all produce a late threshold. A degree-distribution measurement is the spectrometer here.

## 4. Why "robustness" is not a seventh primitive

Fig 13 :125: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."* Robustness and fragility are the same kind of leftover. Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*

This matters because the question is phrased as if robustness were a property a network *has*. The figure reading says it is a property of agent-and-habitat: Fig 11 :39 - *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Swap the habitat (uniform sampler versus degree-ranking attacker) and the same graph changes verdict. So the honest answer names the arrangement and the habitat, not a substance called robustness.

## 5. The metamerism warning applied here

A common shortcut is to read the removal curves and conclude "the network has hubs, therefore scale-free". That names a source from a projection. The world literature itself records the difficulty: Callaway et al. solve percolation for *"completely general degree distribution"*, and Nuwagaba et al. report that modularity, nestedness and connectance all correlate with robustness in ecological networks, with the sign of some correlations depending on the threshold chosen. Several sources produce a similar reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

This does **not** retract the shape. It narrows it: the figures license heterogeneity and a seat, not a specific distribution family.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous network scientists who agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, **not** a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
