## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies agreement (Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*).
2. **Sending the interior spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors.
3. **The falsifier comes first.** Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in channels avoid both.
