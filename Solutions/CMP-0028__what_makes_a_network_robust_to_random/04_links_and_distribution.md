## Wider literature that bears on this question

- **Barabasi and Albert 1999, Science 286, 509**, the growth and preferential-attachment model that produces the heavy tail. Cited inside Albert et al. 2000 and read there; not claimed as independently fetched here.
- **Dorogovtsev and Mendes 2001, Phys Rev Lett 87, 219801**, a comment reporting that the continuum approximation overestimates resilience to intentional damage. Seen in the Cohen et al. abstract page. It matters because it reinforces the gap on the numbers: the exact `f_c` is an instrument question, not a shape question.
- **Memmott, Waser and Price 2004**, pollination networks, cited in the ecological disassembly literature; the finding that generalists matter more than specialists is reported in the Nuwagaba et al. paper and in the PMC review seen while searching. Recorded as a secondary chain, not an independent witness.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them any theory.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is moderate: a wrong shape claim sent to a specialist costs a little credibility and can be corrected. One careful reader is worth more than a list.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, pre-registered refuter. No theory. Their verdict on this stage is recorded first.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure text and the one worked comparison. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each person writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. If a refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 fires, stop and retract before any send.
