## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to
look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books
returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the form any settling answer must take: a stated channel, a
measured rate, a background model, a sensitivity floor, and a provenance record. It is not the
identity of the dark matter particle and not a yes or no. The shape can be stated now. The answer
cannot.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and
  deepen your vocabulary." For this question the first terms are the ones the experiments already
  use: recoil, cross section, mediator, coupling, exclusion, neutrino fog.
- Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or
  measure yet is the residual." The channel list is enumerated from published instruments, not
  composed from the six-term set.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." An effect description here is "a channel that would produce a recoil, a
  missing-energy signature or a cavity photon above a stated background." That description travels
  between direct detection, collider and haloscope frames.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can
  send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the
  world." This is why the first contact carries the shape and its refuter, not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also
  be the starting place". Here the stop is honest only when the sensitivity floor and the lineage
  are recorded.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only
   by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A detection with a published significance, background
   model and data release would name a channel. A limit names nothing.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a
   shadow test. A new mediator born in a theory paper is not licensed until a detector pays for it.

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior,
then behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but
by the arrival of a better instrument." The neutrino fog is this exact structure: one reading,
several possible sources, and an instrument that cannot yet discriminate them. The correct response
is a better discriminator, not a name assigned to the reading.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of
what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."

Applied here: the anchor is the detector result. There is a subtlety worth recording. Dark matter
has already registered gravitationally, so the sensor exists and this is not a search for something
that has never registered anywhere (Fig 6 :40). What has not registered is the *channel*. A stack of
null results is a set of readings of that channel, and a reading does not determine the source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "the output is only as good as the
validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A summary
that cites ten papers agreeing with it has ten readings of one pool if the papers share models and
priors. The independent instruments identified in F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are the
exception, and their agreement is still only about the absence of a signal above their floors.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must an answer to this question have?** It must be:

1. indexed by channel and instrument slot, never a single global yes or no (Fig 1 :19, the residual
   indexed by object slot, applied here to instrument slots);
2. stated as a limit or a detection with a significance, a background model and a data release;
3. explicit about which of the two bundled questions it answers (Fig 9 :54-58);
4. provenanced, so a reader can tell a convergent derivation from an adopted import (Fig 8 :50-52);
5. honest about the point at which depth and absence become indiscernible (Fig 6 :27).

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it never writes "dark matter does not
interact with ordinary matter" as a settled sentence. That would be a `general`-mode verdict on an
empirical question with no ground reading in this folder. The correct form is: "no non-gravitational
coupling has been registered above the published floors of the enumerated channels; existence
remains `candidate`." That sentence carries its own refuter and can be checked by anyone with the
links in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session:
"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action.
It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can
settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous names who agree with
a reading of the residual is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the
claim a budget to be tried and lets a detector settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better
than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too" and "the truly testable output
would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a
simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the
question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
