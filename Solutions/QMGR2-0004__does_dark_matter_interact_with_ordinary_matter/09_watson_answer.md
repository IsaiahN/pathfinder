## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question of a non-gravitational coupling
between dark matter and ordinary matter. Option A: assert that such a coupling exists. Option B:
assert that gravity is the only channel. What each leads to: A asserts an unregistered channel with
no detector result; B converts a set of upper limits into an existence verdict. The figure-grounded
third reading is that the question is a union compound of existence and reachability, that the
figures return the shape of a settling answer and never the answer, and that neither A nor B is
`grounded` here. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run: a channel, a
rate, a background model, a sensitivity floor and a provenance record.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question does not have a figure-licensed yes or no. What survives is the shape. The anchor is a
detector result, never agreement. Gravity is already a relation under the substrate, so the live
residual is a second channel. Null results from independent instruments bound reach and do not
prove absence; depth and genuine absence look the same from inside the frame, and the neutrino fog
is the physical instance. The channel list is a habitat to be enumerated. A settled new coupling
would be an import with provenance, not a hidden bond in the six-term set. The existence claim is
`gap` from the figures and `candidate` from the world. This is `specified`, ungrounded, and it is not
a detector result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim states its mode, its refuters and its
  provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is
  spending the independence of an outside reader by sending a theory before a residual, or by
  reporting an upper limit as a negative existence verdict. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in
  proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the union split
  and the three refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The two overstatements are removable without touching the derivation: "dark
  matter couples only through gravity" is replaced by "no coupling registered above these floors",
  and "the figures predict no coupling" is replaced by "the figures return a shape". Nothing in the
  six-term set has to be withdrawn. A sent summary can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A provenanced non-gravitational detection in any
  enumerated channel would move existence to `grounded`. A discriminator that separates solar
  neutrino scattering from light dark matter scattering in the same channel would end the fog claim
  at that instrument. A closed-set no-coupling theorem would move the negative answer to `grounded`.
  A re-census forcing a seventh kind would break the closure claim. Until one of these fires, the
  verdict stands as `candidate`.
