## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **Exclusion, not discovery, is the universal form of the result.** XENONnT, LZ, ATLAS, CMS and
   the axion haloscopes all report upper limits or exclusions. In figure terms these are anchors
   that returned bounds. Fig 2 :29-30 defines such a reading as the thing that settles a question;
   here it settles "not above this floor", not "not at all". This is the single most important
   distinction to preserve in any summary (Fig 8 :39).
2. **The channels are genuinely independent instruments.** Xenon recoils measure nuclear recoil
   energy and position; the LHC infers missing transverse momentum recoiling against a visible
   object; haloscopes convert axions to photons in a magnetic cavity. By Fig 2 :31-32 these are two
   or more instruments pointed at the same residual, so their agreement is not one pool talking.
   It is still agreement about a state of knowledge, not a proof of the world.
3. **The neutrino fog is a named limit on the inward move.** Lowering threshold and adding exposure
   eventually returns diminishing sensitivity because solar neutrino scattering is indiscernible
   from light dark matter scattering. Fig 6 :27 says depth and genuine absence look the same from
   inside the frame. The physics literature gives the phenomenon a name and a curve; the figure
   gives the interpretation.
4. **Theory can precede the instrument but cannot replace it.** Fig 6 :38 has Neptune calculated
   before it was seen. The CMS review's mediator models tell experimentalists where to point, but
   the models are not the ground. A parameter space excluded in a review is a map of where the
   ground has been asked, not an answer.
5. **Complementarity is a union, not a single lever.** JCAP's "Celestial probes of dark matter
   electromagnetic interactions" (Ibarra et al, JCAP11(2026)048) reports that "celestial bodies
   provide leading constraints in several regions of parameter space" where direct searches lose
   sensitivity. That is Fig 9 :54-58 in physics language: different conditions bind in different
   regions, and a single lever clears only the region where one condition fails.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The output is the union split (existence versus
reachability), the claim that a null result is a bound, and the three refuters. No theory is shown.
Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious, offer the figure text, the figures README and
the two physics papers. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded. Later agreement is never
corroboration, because they now hold the interiors (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes a
refuter first and states a mode. Fig 4 :22 permits a method to cross up. *The Recursive Self-Improvement
Trap* warns that "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or
working the formula yourself", so no one should treat this folder's simulated run as a test.

**Hard precondition:** the two stages work only if the stage-1 output survives. The main live
defect in this question, recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, is conflating an upper limit
with a negative existence verdict. Repair that in any message before it leaves the machine.
