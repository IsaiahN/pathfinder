## 0. The residual under audit

> Does dark matter interact with ordinary matter through anything besides gravity?

Restated as an effect, with the two options bound as an ordinary term before the run:

- **R:** The gravitational readings (rotation curves, lensing, cosmic microwave background) register
  a massive non-baryonic component. The coupling of that component to the standard-model sector is
  not registered. The residual is the unaccounted-for channel, not the existence of the component.
- **Option A:** a non-gravitational coupling exists (nucleon scattering, mediator production,
  axion-photon conversion, electromagnetic moment, or another channel). Leads to: a new term must be
  imported from a detector result, with provenance and a shadow test.
- **Option B:** no such coupling exists, or it lies below every current floor. Leads to: an upper
  limit plus a stated sensitivity, which is a reach statement and not a proof of absence.

Arity, symmetry and scale of R:

- **Arity:** two sectors plus a relation. The relation is the operand of the question. "Dark matter
  interacts with ordinary matter" is already true in one channel, so the live residual is whether a
  *second* bond exists.
- **Symmetry:** a cross-section is not a symmetric join of two equal terms in the figure's sense; it
  is a threshold relation between a measured rate and a predicted rate (compare Fig 12 :90 on what
  counts as a bond and what counts as a claim not yet made).
- **Scale:** the substrate level (six terms, one of which is network), the instrument level (a recoil,
  a missing-energy signature, a cavity photon), and the habitat level (the enumerated experiments).

## 2. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

- **Domain 1, framework-internal composition.** Actors: the six terms (Space, Time, Energy as states;
  Spectrum, Gradient, Network as structures), the seven bonds and negation. Currency: composition
  inside `closure(Gamma)` and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the figures as metalaws, plus
  the ground for any measurement. Question type: partition (which compositions are legal).
- **Domain 2, the physics frame.** Actors: xenon nuclei, WIMPs, dark photons, axions, mediators,
  missing transverse momentum, electromagnetic moments. Currency: cross sections, couplings,
  exclusion limits. Anchor: the detector result. Question type: enumeration of contact channels,
  not derivation.
- **Domain 3, the verification and contact frame.** Actors: experimental collaborations and expert
  readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated
  run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so Domain 3 independence is zero.

**Partition or union:** a union. The question bundles at least two conditions: (i) does a coupling
exist at all, and (ii) is it within reach of a given instrument. Fig 9 :54-58 warns that a bunded
problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions and that a case in two conditions is not a
harder case of either. They must be split before a verdict is attempted. A single null result clears
the region where only condition (ii) fails and leaves condition (i) standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3):** first enumerate the habitat (Fig 11), because without a channel
list there is nothing to point an instrument at; then read the anchor (Fig 2 :29-30), because a
detector result is the only thing that can settle existence; then decide depth versus absence
(Fig 6 :27); only then state a verdict. Effort spent on the verdict before the habitat is effort
above the break in Fig 3, where the measurements do not yet exist.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

## 4. Findings

### F1: The figures cannot answer this question; they can only describe what would answer it

Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of
what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."

This is an empirical question. The anchor is the detector result. Every figure line invoked below
governs the *shape* of the answer, the order of the search, or the interpretation of a null result.
None of them returns "yes" or "no". Claiming otherwise would be a verdict in `general` mode, which
`THE_FORMULA.md` forbids. Verdict: `gap` on the existence claim from the figures alone. "Unreachable
is a verdict, not a silence" is the applicable rule (Fig 13 :134).

### F2: A null result is not a no

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."

Three low-overlap routes close on the same reading: a theorem-level limit (Fig 8), an argument about
sealed rooms (Fig 9), and a reachability distinction (Fig 6). Triangulated, not single-source. The
world literature agrees in the specific form of exclusion limits rather than discoveries. XENONnT
reports "we find no significant excess above background" and sets an upper limit; CMS reports
"There has been no sign of Dark Matter yet". An upper limit is not a negative existence verdict.
A detector that sees nothing is a frame ranged against the ground, and the ground returned a bound.

### F3: The neutrino fog is Fig 6 :27 made physical

The XENONnT light-dark-matter paper describes the "neutrino fog" as "the limitation where neutrinos
produce a signal that is indistinguishable from that of light DM-xenon nucleus scattering." That is
exactly the figure's "from inside the frame the two look the same": an irreducible background that
mimics the signal, so additional exposure gives diminishing returns. *The Hard Problem is About
Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the general form: "If several paths create the same behavior, then
behavior is a projection with lossy information" and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by
the arrival of a better instrument." What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a
further argument and not more of the same exposure. World prediction corroborates the figure
verdict.

### F4: The sensor does exist, because dark matter has already registered

Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution."

A strained first reading of this line would forbid the search altogether. Re-census: the *something*
that has never registered is the non-gravitational coupling. The gravitational component has
registered everywhere, and the text of the XENONnT paper opens with "Observational evidence from
galactic to cosmic scales indicates the existence of massive, non-baryonic dark matter (DM) in the
Universe." So a proto-instrument exists and the search has an edge to extend from. The strain is
dissolved by separating the two channels; it is not hidden. This makes the search well-posed and
distinguishes it from a search for something that has never registered at all.

### F5: A new coupling would be an import, not a rearrangement of the six

Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."
Fig 13 :116-118: "a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."
Fig 12 :90: "A relation that fails all six is not a bond you are missing. It is a claim that has not been made yet."

Under the substrate, gravity is already a relation: Fig 13 :24 names Network as the structure "that
entities relate, and influence travels." So "interacts with ordinary matter" is not the live
question; gravitationally, it does. The live question is whether an additional relation exists.
The figures do not smuggle a fifth force into the bond set. A candidate coupling is a claim with a
test; if a detector settles it, the new force carrier enters as an import with provenance and a
shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8). Before that it is a preference, not a term. Verdict: `specified`,
triangulated across Fig 13, Fig 12 and the Operators table.

### F6: Independent instruments are the only corroboration available here

Fig 2 :31-32: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"

Direct detection (liquid xenon recoils), collider searches (missing transverse momentum plus a
visible object), and axion haloscopes (microwave cavity conversion) are different instruments
pointed at the same residual. They are low-overlap frames by Fig 2 :31-32's own test, so their
agreement on "no signal yet" is real corroboration of a *state of knowledge*, not a shared pool
talking. It still does not convert a null into a no (F2). By contrast, this folder's simulated
second seat shares the primary's evidence pool and is not a witness; see `11_audit.md`.

### F7: Neither prestige nor headcount settles it

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with a reading of this question produces a convention,

## 6. Where the three exit questions land (Formula step 7)

- **SEARCHED (asked first, cheapest):** have we swept the channels we already hold? Partly. Direct
  detection, collider and haloscope channels have been swept to published floors, but parameter
  space at every mass is not closed. Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are
  different facts with different instructions (Fig 8 :40-43). The world shows the second, not the
  first.
- **INWARD:** extend an instrument already returning something. The XENONnT neutrino-fog paper is
  the exact case: lowering threshold and adding exposure returns diminishing information, so the
  next move is a different discriminator, not the same instrument sharpened forever.
- **OUTWARD:** characterise R in effect terms and find a frame whose closure predicts the shape.
  The effect description is "a channel that would produce a recoil, a missing-energy signature, or
  a cavity photon above a stated background." That description travels to the collider and haloscope
  frames. Import is gated by provenance and a shadow test, and debited against independence.

The three stay distinct. No answer from SEARCHED is read against INWARD or OUTWARD.
