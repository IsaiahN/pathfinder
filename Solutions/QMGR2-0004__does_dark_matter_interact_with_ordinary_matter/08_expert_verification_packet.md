# Expert verification packet: Does dark matter interact with ordinary matter through anything besides gravity?

**What this is:** a claim in a form a dark matter researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters
are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the limit. Not `grounded`.** No detector
result is claimed by this packet. It is a shape and a route, not an answer.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground
reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.**

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question bundles two conditions: whether a non-gravitational coupling between dark and ordinary
matter exists, and whether any such coupling lies within a given instrument's reach. Gravity is
already a relation, so the first condition is the live one. Published null results from liquid
xenon, collider and haloscope experiments return exclusion limits, which answer the reach condition
and not the existence condition. A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence; some
unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the
same. The framework returns the shape a settling answer must have (a channel, a rate, a background
model, a floor and a provenance record) and stops where the available instruments stop
discriminating. Refuters are stated in advance. This packet does not say which coupling, if any, is
real.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 2 and Figure 8

- **Statement:** the anchor for this question is a measurement, and a null result bounds reach
  without proving absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30, "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not
  change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a
  market, a patient outcome." Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of
  absence." Fig 9 :44, "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed
  room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
- **What to check:** does any quoted line fail to appear verbatim in
  `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`? Is the inference from an upper limit to "no existence
  verdict" the correct reading of those lines?
- **Refuter:** a closed-set theorem proving no non-gravitational coupling can exist at any mass, or
  a published result that demonstrably settles existence and reach at once. Either would move the
  negative answer to `grounded` and break the claim.

### C2: Physics-facing; checkable by a dark matter experimentalist

- **Statement:** the enumerated channels are independent instruments, not one evidence pool, and
  their null results are bounds. The neutrino fog is the physical case where depth and absence look
  the same.
- **What to check:** are liquid-xenon nuclear recoils (S1 and S2 discrimination), collider missing
  transverse momentum recoiling against a visible object, and axion haloscope cavity conversion
  genuinely different measurements with different systematics? Does the XENONnT light-dark-matter
  result really describe "the limitation where neutrinos produce a signal that is indistinguishable
  from that of light DM-xenon nucleus scattering"?
- **Provenance:** XENON Collaboration, "WIMP Dark Matter Search using a 3.1 Tonne-Year Exposure of
  the XENONnT Experiment", arXiv:2502.18005v2 / PRL 135, 221003 (2025), "we find no significant
  excess above background"; XENON Collaboration, PRL 134, 111802 (2025); CMS Collaboration,
  "Dark sector searches with the CMS experiment", 2024, "There has been no sign of Dark Matter
  yet"; ADMX Collaboration, PRL 134, 111002 (2025), "This work excludes (with a 90% confidence
  level) DFSZ axions with masses between 3.27 to 3.34 μeV".
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that these channels draw on one shared likelihood or calibration
  pool so their agreement is one witness, or a detector that discriminates the neutrino background
  from a light dark matter signal in the same channel (which would end the fog claim at that
  instrument).

### C3: Framework-internal derivation

- **Statement:** the channel list is a habitat to be enumerated, and a new coupling would be an
  import if a detector ever settles it, not a bond already hidden in the figure set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :48, "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what
  you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." Fig 13 :115, "The set is closed. The
  arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 12 :90, "A relation that fails all six
  is not a bond you are missing. It is a claim that has not been made yet."
- **What to check:** is the six-term set actually closed under its own composition rule, and does
  the figures' import operator require provenance and a shadow test before a new term is admitted?
- **Refuter:** a re-census showing that the six-term set must admit a seventh kind to hold a dark
  sector coupling, which would break the closure claim and require withdrawal of this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that dark matter couples only through gravity. That is an existence verdict with no ground
  reading here.
- Not that a non-gravitational coupling exists, and not that WIMPs, axions, dark photons or any
  other candidate are excluded.
- Not a theory of everything, not a final room, and not a unified field.
- Not a derivation of one sector from the other, no new primitive and no new bond in the figure set.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this author.
- Not a claim that the framework names the particle or the mediator. By Fig 9 :32 it returns "you
  get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

## 5. What would settle it

1. A provenanced non-gravitational detection in any channel: a recoil excess surviving background
   models, a mediator with a visible decay, a reproducible axion line, or a discriminated
   electromagnetic moment.
2. A source-discriminating instrument that separates solar-neutrino coherent scattering from light
   dark matter scattering in the same recoil channel.
3. A closed-set theorem over all masses and couplings (expected not to exist, since there is no
   sealed room, Fig 9 :44).
4. A re-census admitting a seventh kind, which would break the closure claim in C3.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Open `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and verify the three sets of verbatim lines in C1
   and C3, about eight short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: are those three channel types genuinely independent
   measurements, and is the neutrino-fog description accurate?
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate the neutrino background from a light dark matter
   signal in the same channel, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
