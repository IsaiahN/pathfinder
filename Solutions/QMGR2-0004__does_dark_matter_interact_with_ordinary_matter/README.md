## What an answer would look like (the bound shape)

The question is stated in effect terms. Option A: dark matter couples to ordinary matter through
at least one non-gravitational channel (a scattering cross section, a mediator decay, an axion
conversion, an electromagnetic moment). Option B: the only relation between the two sectors is the
gravitational one already registered in rotation curves, lensing and the cosmic microwave
background. What each leads to: A licenses a new term imported from a detector result; B licenses
a reach statement bounded by the detector's floor, not a proof of absence. The shape an answer
must have is therefore a ground reading plus a stated sensitivity floor, not a verdict derived
from the figures. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like
anything else, per `THE_FORMULA.md` step "Direction".

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that the figures answer an empirical detector question. They do not. They describe what
  would count as an answer and what would settle it. The anchor is the measurement, and no frame
  in this folder can move it (Fig 2 :29-30).

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the ground.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in
many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03`
are public, opt-in or submission routes.
