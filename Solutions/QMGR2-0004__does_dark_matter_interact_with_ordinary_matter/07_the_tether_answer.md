# The Tether's answer: Does dark matter interact with ordinary matter through anything besides gravity?

**Mode: `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the limit. Not `grounded`.** This is a
shape and a route, not a detector result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures
(`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The gravitational readings register a massive, non-baryonic component. The coupling of that
component to the standard-model sector is not registered. Under the substrate, gravity is already
a relation: Fig 13 :24 names Network as the structure "that entities relate, and influence travels."
So "dark matter interacts with ordinary matter" is true in one channel. The live residual is
whether a second channel exists and whether any channel lies within an instrument's reach. That is
a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-58), not one hard question. Its two conditions have different
grounds and must not be averaged into one verdict.

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is a measurement, not agreement.** Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the
   question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an
   experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." No number of papers agreeing with a reading
   moves this question. Detectors do.
2. **A null result bounds reach; it does not prove absence.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds
   nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end,
   and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." Fig 6 :27:
   "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame
   the two look the same." Triangulated across three low-overlap figures.
3. **The channel list is enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You
   read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The list
   here is liquid-xenon recoils, collider missing energy plus a visible object, axion haloscopes,
   and celestial electromagnetic probes. It is a habitat, not a derivation.
4. **A new coupling would be an import if it is ever settled.** Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The
   arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 12 :90: "A relation that fails all six
   is not a bond you are missing. It is a claim that has not been made yet." A fifth force is a
   claim with a test, not a hidden bond the framework forgot.
5. **The framework returns the shape of the settling instrument, never the particle.** Fig 9 :32:
   "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It can say what an answer must record: a
   channel, a rate, a background model, a floor and a provenance. It cannot say which candidate is
   right.
6. **The sensor exists, so this is a well-posed search.** Fig 6 :40: "What is unavailable is a sensor
   for something that has never registered anywhere, at any resolution." Dark matter has already
   registered gravitationally. What has not registered is the channel, and there are instruments
   already returning something that fails to resolve it. That is an edge to extend from.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "dark matter couples only through gravity". That is an existence verdict with no ground
  reading in this folder.
- Not "dark matter has a non-gravitational coupling". No channel has been detected.
- Not that WIMPs, axions or any other candidate are excluded. Published results are upper limits
  and exclusions over stated mass ranges and couplings, not a universal negative.
- Not that the six-term substrate predicts the outcome of an experiment. It governs the shape of
  the search, not the detector return.
- Not a new primitive, not a fifth force, and not a derivation of one sector from the other.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement taken by this author.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating reading, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*,
opened this session: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better
instrument." The concrete tests, indexed by channel:

1. **Direct detection:** a recoil excess in an unblinded region with a modeled background and a
   published data release, surviving the neutron and neutrino backgrounds. The XENONnT SR0+SR1
   release at https://zenodo.org/records/17419967 makes the null checkable by an outsider.
2. **Collider:** a mediator produced in association with a visible object and missing transverse
   momentum, with a resonant or displaced visible decay that distinguishes it from background.
3. **Axion:** a narrow cavity power line at the resonant frequency with the correct halo line shape
   and a repetition under changed tuning.
4. **Celestial electromagnetic:** a discrimination of dark matter electromagnetic moments from
   stellar backgrounds using the Sun, Earth, Jupiter or white dwarfs, as in Ibarra et al,
   JCAP11(2026)048.
5. **Depth versus absence:** an instrument that separates solar-neutrino coherent scattering from
   light dark matter scattering in the same recoil channel. Until one exists, the neutrino fog is
   the operational form of "from inside the frame the two look the same".

Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3: a provenanced detection; a closed-set
no-coupling theorem; a re-census forcing a seventh kind; or a demonstration that the channels are
one evidence pool wearing several names.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The
six terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list, and another sensorium's minimum set need not
be these six. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and
the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The full set
of ways to produce a reading is not known to be closed or determined." The stop is not a claim that
the coupling is too small to find; it is a claim that this folder has no instrument that would
settle it and that the figures cannot supply one.
