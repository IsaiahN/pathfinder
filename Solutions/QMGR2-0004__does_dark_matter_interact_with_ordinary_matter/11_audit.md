## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44

   coupling would be an import.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a reader might take away, namely "dark matter does
   not interact with ordinary matter except by gravity", or the opposite overstatement "the
   framework rules out or predicts a fifth force".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union split**: a null result answers reachability, not existence;
- the **independence condition**: agreement among null results is corroboration of a state of
  knowledge, and the essays in the ledger share an author and a pool;
- the **anchor requirement**: the figures cannot return a detector outcome, so the shorthand must
  not be concretised into an existence verdict.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the shape of a settling answer, a channel with a rate, a
background model, a floor and a provenance, together with three falsifiers. That is the residue
worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is
recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as
identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30
names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what
