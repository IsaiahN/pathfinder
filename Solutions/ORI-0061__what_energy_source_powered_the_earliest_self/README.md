## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not add ground.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Adding more readers raises the vocabulary, not the anchor.
3. **Showing the interior spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
