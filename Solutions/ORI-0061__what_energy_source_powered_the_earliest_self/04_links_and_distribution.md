## Three findings in the wider literature that bear directly on ORI-0061

1. **The literature does not name one source.** The NASA strategy lists six or more; Rimmer and Shorttle identify two contrasting classes; Michaelian argues for a third weighting. This is not a failure of the field. Under Fig 9 :32 it is exactly what is expected when the question asks for a name where the ground has not discriminated.
2. **The shared term across the competing schools is a sustained difference.** Barge and coauthors say *"appropriate and sustained disequilibria"*; Rimmer and Shorttle say *"chemical disequilibrium can provide an energy source for nascent life"*; Pascal, Pross and Sutherland require far-from-equilibrium conditions. That shared term is the figure-governed shape, stated in the literature's own vocabulary.
3. **UV changes valence with the residual.** Rimmer and Shorttle note UV is constructive for building blocks and destructive for complex strands. That is Fig 11 :49 (*"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"*) observed in prebiotic chemistry, and it is why no standing list of energy sources survives a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).

Overlap warning: the geochemistry and photochemistry papers cite overlapping reviews and share the astrobiology community. Their agreement on "disequilibrium" is one pool talking on that point (Fig 2 :40). The thermodynamic literature is a different pool but still secondary to the figures.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, pre-registered refuter. Ask for their verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the figure text, canonical README and essays. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (Formula "Direction"; Fig 4 :22).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. This package explicitly withholds the specific source name and marks it `gap`, so the stage-1 claim is the shape, not a contested binding. If a binding is later added to stage 1, the precondition fails and the packet must be rebuilt.
