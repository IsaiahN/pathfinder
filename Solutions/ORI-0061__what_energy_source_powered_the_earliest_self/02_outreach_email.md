# Outreach draft: check the output first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 is in the recipient's hands *before* any framework material. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the interior is shown first (Fig 10 :42).

**Subject:** One checkable output on early energy sources, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output about what powers the earliest self-maintaining chemical systems. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** energy by itself cannot drive a directed process, because it is conserved, finite and directionless on its own. What can be spent is a gradient, a difference with direction. The figures compose the two: energy plus a gradient makes a flow. So the answer term for an energy *source* is not a quantity but a sustained difference delivered as a flow through an open reaction cycle whose catalyst regenerates, at a site whose habitat keeps renewing the difference. This is a shape, not a measurement, and the slot where a particular gradient would go stays open.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the answer is UV light, or alkaline hydrothermal vents, or serpentinization, or any single named source. Those are bindings from the geochemistry and photochemistry frames onto a slot the framework leaves open. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. Until provenance is shown they carry no evidentiary weight. The framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a chemical network that maintains itself over many turnovers in a closed, equilibrated system with no through-flowing difference;
> 2. a self-maintaining system with no cycle and no catalyst that survives its own use;
> 3. a single source whose measured valence is invariant across two habitats with reversed conditions;
> 4. a habitat whose measured renewal of the gradient leaves the network's residual unchanged.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, I retract the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract in this repository): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. See Fig 13 :14, :22, :71, :76; Fig 12 :119, :121; Fig 11 :39, :49, :73.
> - Canonical usage rule (local copy): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`.
> - The eight essays, indexed in `05_what_shape_means.md` of the solution folder.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the figures link carries the formula. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage offer, stated plainly

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local figure and essay links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode (Fig 4 :22).

**Audience.** The people who can help are those with a public, checkable record of work on prebiotic energy flows, geochemical gradients, or far-from-equilibrium reaction networks, reachable through an opt-in or institutional channel. The venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are such channels. There is no receptiveness rank and no bulk list.
