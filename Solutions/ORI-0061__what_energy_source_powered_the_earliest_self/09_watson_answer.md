## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a prebiotic reaction network predicts at a site and what the environment returns there: which reactions keep running, whether the catalyst regenerates, and whether the difference is renewed. Arity: three conditions (supply, closure, habitat renewal), so a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). Symmetry: asymmetric and directed; a gradient has a direction and energy does not (Fig 13 :14, :71). Scale: molecular transfer, local site, planetary habitat; the transform between is lossy (Fig 4 :30).
- **Domain 1, reaction network and self-maintenance.** Actors: molecules, catalysts, compartments, cycles. Currency: reaction flux and persistence of a regenerating catalyst. Anchor: a laboratory flow-through result or measured turnover. Question type: closure, a cycle with a part that survives its use.
- **Domain 2, geochemical habitat and supply.** Actors: vents, UV flux, wet-dry cycles, minerals, redox couples, protons. Currency: sustained disequilibrium and spendable gradient. Anchor: a measured pH, redox or photon flux. Question type: a binding from the geochemistry frame onto held atoms, not a new member of the six; the habitat is not the ground.
- **Domain 3, thermodynamic substrate.** Actors: energy (state), gradient (structure), flow (composition). Currency: directedness and remainder per step. Anchor: the six-term set and the thermodynamic tests in Fig 13. Question type: cross-kind composition.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers and experiments. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool (the same figure text, essays and world pages), so their agreement is one pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency.** Supply before closure before habitat renewal before promotion to the next level (Fig 3 :20; Fig 12 :104); refuse the bare "energy" term before minting a source name (Formula step 2); describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: an early reading made Fig 13 answer the question directly by treating energy as one of six states and therefore as the source. That only fit by ignoring Fig 13 :14 (*"directionless alone"*). Re-census sent Fig 13 to the composition role: gradient plus state makes a flow. A second strain, calling the vent "the ground," was resolved by Fig 11 :73. No unresolved strain remains.

## WORLD (secondary sources read)

World sources are corroboration only. Most share the astrobiology community; overlap is declared rather than hidden.

- **Government strategy / field map.** NASA Astrobiology Strategy 2015 (NASA, 2015): *"How do different energy sources result in different organic production?"* and *"Energy sources for molecular transformations on Earth and other terrestrial bodies include high-energy discharge, UV radiation, heat, radiation bombardment, hydrothermal energy, and serpentinization."* Predicts no single name; corroborates the `gap`. Overlap: government strategy document, distinct from the papers below on the funding framing.
- **Geochemistry / prebiotic chemistry.** Rimmer and Shorttle, "Origin of Life's Building Blocks in Carbon- and Nitrogen-Rich Surface Hydrothermal Vents" (*Life*, 2019): *"vents possess natural pH, thermal, and chemical potential gradients that life can exploit for energy"*; UV is *"constructive for synthesizing and activating nucleotides and amino acids"* but *"destructive for more complex organic systems."* Predicts a spendable gradient and valence relative to the residual; corroborates Fig 13 and Fig 11. Overlap: moderate with the vent literature.
- **Thermodynamics / geochemistry focus group.** Barge, Branscomb, Russell et al., "Thermodynamics, Disequilibrium, Evolution" (*Origins of Life and Evolution of Biospheres*, 2016/2017): *"Life is a bounded self-organizing system driven by appropriate and sustained disequilibria"*; supplies *"well-ordered, predictable and sustained"* fuels and oxidants. Predicts the habitat-renewal condition; corroborates Fig 11 :73. Overlap: low with Rimmer and Shorttle (focus-group thermodynamics versus geochemical modelling).
- **Systems chemistry.** Ruiz-Mirazo et al., "Chemical roots of biological evolution" (*Open Biology*, 2017): *"self-maintenance is based on far-from-equilibrium reaction cycles in which there is a continuous turnover of the molecular species involved."* Predicts cycle plus open flow; corroborates Fig 12 :119, :121. Overlap: low with the geochemistry sources.
- **Kinetics and thermodynamics.** Pascal, Pross and Sutherland, "Towards an evolutionary theory of the origin of life based on kinetics and thermodynamics" (*Open Biology*, 2013): *"multiplying entities must be associated with the dissipation of free energy."* Predicts far-from-equilibrium persistence; corroborates F3. Overlap: moderate with Ruiz-Mirazo (systems-chemistry community).
- **Competing frame / conflict.** Michaelian, "Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamic Foundations of the Origin of Life" (*Foundations*, 2022): *"During the Archean, UVC photons would have provided orders of magnitude more free energy for dissipation"* than chemical potentials. This **conflicts** with the vent-school weighting, not with the figure verdict. The conflict was returned to the census and resolved as a union of different residuals (feedstock synthesis versus sustained maintenance); the verdict was not flipped. Overlap: low with the vent school on evidence pool, high with the dissipation literature.
- **Review framing.** "Hydrothermal vents and the origin of life" (*Nature Reviews Microbiology*, 2008): *"major unresolved issues concern the source of sustained chemical energy and the source of reduced carbon compounds."* Predicts that the specific source is still open; corroborates the `gap`. Overlap: high with the vent literature, so one witness.
- **Public education.** NASA Science, "Where could life have gotten started on Earth?" (NASA, 2026): *"There were many different kinds of environments on the early Earth that may have provided the conditions and energy needed for prebiotic chemistry to occur and to potentially evolve into biochemistry."* Predicts plural sites; corroborates Fig 11 :46. Overlap: high with the NASA strategy, so one witness.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict itself. The one internal world conflict (UV weighting versus vent weighting) was handled by re-census, not by averaging. Agreement among the astrobiology sources is one pool on several points and is recorded as such (Fig 2 :40).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name does not. Energy alone is directionless, so it cannot be the answer. The spendable term is a gradient; energy plus a gradient composes a flow. The earliest self-maintaining systems were therefore powered by a sustained difference delivered through an open reaction cycle whose catalyst regenerated, at a site whose habitat kept renewing the difference. Spatial and temporal gradients are different terms, so a vent membrane and a photon flux are not one source. The two dominant literature classes are a union of different residuals, not an isomer pair. Which particular gradient did it on Earth is a `gap` after re-census: the framework returns a shape, and the instrument that would discriminate among sources is not held. This is `specified` on the shape and `general` on the substrate; no part is `grounded`. The strongest outreach point is the shape itself, with four refuters written in advance.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet withholds the name and states its mode and refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost would be shipping a binding as a derivation, which imports an atom without a source gate; Fig 8 prices *"a wrong atom ... [above] starting from primitives."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A binding is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A flow-through experiment at a named site that measures the gradient, cycle closure, catalyst regeneration and habitat renewal, with the residual per site rather than averaged, would pay for the shape and a swap test could name the source. A closed equilibrated system that maintains itself over many turnovers, a maintained network with no cycle, a source whose valence is invariant across reversed habitats, or a derivation of one of the six from the other five would break the shape. A figure re-census that admits a bare conserved quantity as a power term would also reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
