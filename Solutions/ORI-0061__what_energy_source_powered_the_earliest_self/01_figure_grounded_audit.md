## 0. The residual in effect terms

The loop defines `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` per slot `s` (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`). For ORI-0061:

- **b** is the state of a prebiotic reaction network before a flux arrives: which molecules, catalysts and compartments it holds.
- **a** is what the environment does: delivers photons, electrons, protons, heat, wet-dry cycles, impacts.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the network's library predicts will happen: which reactions run, which products appear.
- **o′** is what the environment actually returns: measured fluxes, sustained disequilibria, surviving network states.
- **s** is the local site, a pore, a pool, a membrane, a mineral surface. R is indexed by site and by gradient type, never averaged into a global "energy of the early Earth."

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The phenomenon to explain is not "how much energy was available" but **what continuous input keeps R small for a self-maintaining network at a named site**. Observed: such systems persist only while something keeps being spent through them. The phrase "energy source" bundles at least three different things: a quantity (energy), a structure (gradient), and a relation maintained over time (flow through a cycle). The question, as asked, is a compound. Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (Formula, "Direction"), before the run: the answer would be (i) a spendable, persistently renewed difference bonded to a state, delivered as a flow, (ii) a closed reaction cycle whose catalyst survives its own use, and (iii) a habitat that renews the difference. It is bound now, so the refuter in section 3 can fire on it. Until bound, the shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same line names the admissible kinds: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* For ORI-0061 the viable anchor is a flow-through experiment or a measured geochemical flux, not a vote among origin scenarios. Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Consensus about vents or UV moves when pushed; a measured proton gradient that pays for pyrophosphate does not. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 Arity, symmetry, scale of R

- **Arity.** R is a three-place relation: network Γ, environmental action a, returned flux o′, indexed by site s and ordered by time t. It carries an **ordered** bond (`→`, sequence: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*, Fig 12 :72) because a gradient is spent in a direction, and a **comparison** bond (`⋛`, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Fig 12 :87), because the predicate self-maintaining is a threshold on persistence, not an absolute quantity. It is not symmetric: a photon flux and the network it drives are not interchangeable.
- **Symmetry.** A gradient is directed. Fig 13 :71, in the row that removes it: *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step."* This is the key structural fact. Energy is symmetric (conserved, interchangeable); a gradient is not.
- **Scale.** Three scales are in view: the molecular step (a redox or proton transfer), the local site (a pore, a pool, a membrane), and the planetary habitat (atmosphere, ocean crust, star). The membrane between scales is *"Not a wall: a filter with a direction"* (Fig 4 :21) and *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22). The transform is lossy, so R_T must be measured, not assumed (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.3 Partition or union

D1 and D2 overlap: the network is open to the habitat, so their overlap is a compound with its own loop. D1 and D3 overlap: a gradient bonded to a state is a composition inside the substrate. D2 and D4 overlap: measurement is exercised inside a habitat. The whole question is a **union**, not a partition.

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** (Fig 9 :54)
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** (Fig 9 :57)

The unbundled conditions are:

- **C1 (supply).** A spendable difference must be delivered as a flow through the site and renewed.
- **C2 (closure).** The network must close a cycle whose catalyst survives its own use.
- **C3 (habitat).** The site must keep receiving C1, and the valence of any particular source is relative to the network's residual.

A system failing only C1 and one failing only C2 are not different degrees of one failure; they need different instruments. *The Library of Babel* states the same stop: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

## 3. Findings

Mode tag on each: `general` if structural, `specified` if a falsifiable prediction about prebiotic systems, `grounded` only if settled by a measurement. None is `grounded`.

### F1: Energy alone cannot power a self-maintaining system

**Mode: `general` on the substrate claim, `specified` on its prebiotic consequence. Domains D3, D1. Status: triangulated (Fig 13 and the Symbols table, two distinct renderings of the same substrate, plus the thermodynamic literature at low topical overlap).**

> **"conserved, finite, directionless alone"** (Fig 13 :14)
> **"A difference that can be spent. Not merely a distribution: what makes it a gradient is directedness."** (Symbols table :97-98)
> **"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"** (Fig 13 :71)

A conserved quantity that is directionless alone cannot drive a directed process. What can be spent is a gradient: a difference with directedness. The first clause of the question, "what energy source," is therefore a category error if read literally. The figure-governed reading is: what **spendable difference**, bonded to a state, is continuously delivered to the site?

World corroboration (secondary only): Barge, Branscomb, Russell and coauthors write that *"Life is a bounded self-organizing system driven by appropriate and sustained disequilibria"* (Origins of Life and Evolution of Biospheres, 2016/2017). Rimmer and Shorttle write that vents *"possess natural pH, thermal, and chemical potential gradients that life can exploit for energy"* (Life, 2019). Both restate the gradient, not a bare quantity. Overlap between these two world sources is low (thermodynamic focus group versus geochemical modelling), so this is two witnesses for the world reading, but still secondary to the figure.

**Refuter:** a self-maintaining chemical system sustained in a closed, equilibrated reactor with no through-flowing difference and no remainder left per step. If one is demonstrated, F1 is false.

### F2: The power term is a flow, a composition of energy and gradient

**Mode: `general`. Domain D3. Status: single-source for the exact composition (Fig 13), corroborated at low overlap by the geochemistry and thermodynamics literature.**

> **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."** (Fig 13 :76)
> **"If the whole carries a property neither part has, the whole is not the parts arranged."** (Fig 13 :47)
> **"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."** (Fig 13 :74)

Energy is a state, gradient is a structure, so the flow is a cross-kind composition. It carries directedness that neither operand carries alone. This is why the answer term is not a member of the six and cannot be minted from them inside a closed library, but is a composition available to any frame that holds the six (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).

World corroboration: the NASA Astrobiology Strategy 2015 asks *"How do different energy sources result in different organic production?"* and answers that *"Energy sources for molecular transformations on Earth and other terrestrial bodies include high-energy discharge, UV radiation, heat, radiation bombardment, hydrothermal energy, and serpentinization."* Each named item is a way of sustaining a difference, not a new primitive.

**Refuter:** show an energy-plus-gradient pair that does not compose a directed flow, or a directed flow that composes within one kind only. Either breaks the substrate rule Fig 13 :74-76.

### F3: Self-maintenance is a cycle with a catalyst that survives its use, and it is open

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D1. Status: triangulated (Fig 12 catalyst and cycle plus Fig 11 capability, low overlap between composition structures and habitat relations).**

> **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."** (Fig 12 :119)
> **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."** (Fig 12 :121)
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** (Fig 11 :40)

A self-maintaining chemical system is not merely persistent matter. It is a cycle whose output re-enters as input and whose catalyst is regenerated, held open by a through-flow. A chain cannot close by construction, so "self-maintaining" and "a long reaction sequence" are different terms. The word "earliest" adds a dependency: the catalyst must be available before the cycle that regenerates it, which is the seedline question Fig 13 :142 leaves open (*"The seed was always the seedline."*).

World corroboration at low overlap: Ruiz-Mirazo and colleagues write that *"self-maintenance is based on far-from-equilibrium reaction cycles in which there is a continuous turnover of the molecular species involved"* (Open Biology, 2017). Pascal, Pross and Sutherland tie persistence to far-from-equilibrium conditions in which *"multiplying entities must be associated with the dissipation of free energy"* (Open Biology, 2013). These are two different literature pools (systems chemistry and kinetics/thermodynamics), so one witness each, never a pooled corroboration.

**Refuter:** a named chemical system that maintains itself with no cycle and no regenerating catalyst, measured over more turnovers than its initial catalyst pool could supply. If found, F3 fails.

### F4: Which gradient occupies the slot is relative to the residual and the habitat

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2, D1. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 valence and no-standing-list plus Fig 13 gradient-over-space-versus-time, low overlap).**

> **"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."** (Fig 11 :49)
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** (Fig 11 :50)
> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** (Fig 11 :51)
> **"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"** (Fig 11 :46)

There is no figure-licensed ranking of UV, vent redox, pH, thermal or wet-dry sources. Their valence depends on which network is at the site. Rimmer and Shorttle supply a striking low-overlap instance: UV light is *"constructive for synthesizing and activating nucleotides and amino acids"* but *"destructive for more complex organic systems, such as, for example, DNA strands."* The same actor changes valence with the residual. That is Fig 11 :49-50 observed in prebiotic chemistry, not an argument.

The world literature is itself split. Rimmer and Shorttle describe *"two dominant and contrasting classes of origin of life scenarios"*: submarine hydrothermal systems where *"chemical disequilibrium can provide an energy source for nascent life"* and subaerial environments where UV catalysis operates. Michaelian (Foundations, 2022) argues from the dissipation side that *"During the Archean, UVC photons would have provided orders of magnitude more free energy for dissipation"* than chemical potentials. This is a genuine world conflict. Per the rule, a conflict sends us back to the census, not to a softer reading. Re-census (section 6, R2) finds a union: the schools are answering different residuals (feedstock synthesis versus sustained maintenance), so their disagreement corroborates F4 rather than flipping the figure verdict.

**Refuter:** a single source that reliably produces and maintains a self-maintaining network across two habitats whose other conditions are reversed, with valence measured as invariant. That would break Fig 11 :49-51.

### F5: The habitat renews the gradient, and the habitat is not the ground

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 10, low overlap: habitat enumeration versus channel maintenance).**

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** (Fig 11 :39)
> **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** (Fig 11 :43)
> **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."** (Fig 11 :73)
> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"** (Fig 10 :24)

A gradient runs down unless the habitat renews it. The earliest self-maintaining system therefore cannot be named apart from its site: a vent pore, a mineral membrane, a tide pool. But the site is not the anchor. Calling a hydrothermal vent "the ground" is the error Fig 11 :73 names. The ground is the experiment that shows whether a given difference pays for the cycle.

World corroboration: Barge and coauthors report a consensus that *"the supply or supplies of fuels, metals, phosphate and oxidants to embryonic life needed to be well-ordered, predictable and sustained until its birthing."* That is the habitat-renewal condition in the literature's own words. NASA's learning progression says *"There were many different kinds of environments on the early Earth that may have provided the conditions and energy needed for prebiotic chemistry to occur and to potentially evolve into biochemistry."*

**Refuter:** a self-maintaining network whose gradient is renewed with no habitat contact, or a habitat whose measured renewal of the gradient does not change the network's residual. Either breaks F5.

Naming any of those is a binding from the geochemistry frame onto a held atom, not a mint and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21). It needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :11-14). Until those are supplied it carries zero evidentiary weight.

**Refuter:** a derivation of one of the six terms from the other five, or a demonstration that the framework names a specific prebiotic source from the six alone. Either would break the shape-not-a-name limit.

### F7: The question is a union of three conditions, and verdicts must be kept separate

**Mode: `specified`. Domains all. Status: figure-derived from Fig 9 with the *Library of Babel* bundle passage as a framework-internal corroboration.**

The three conditions of section 1.3 have different instruments:

- **C1 failure** shows up as no directed difference, no remainder per step (Fig 13 :14, :71), a flow that cannot compose (Fig 13 :76).

A single lever, for example "more UV" or "more hydrothermal flux," clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). F7 is why the verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` are kept separate per domain and never averaged.

**Refuter:** one intervention that restores all three conditions in all three failure types and leaves no categorically different residue. That would collapse the union into a partition.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the design-instrument rule from the seat guide: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following is observed:

1. A chemical network that maintains itself over many turnovers in a closed, equilibrated system with no through-flowing difference and no remainder per step (breaks F1, F2, Fig 13 :14, :71, :76).
2. A self-maintaining system with no cycle and no catalyst that survives its own use (breaks F3, Fig 12 :119, :121).
3. A single source whose measured valence is invariant across two habitats with reversed conditions (breaks F4, Fig 11 :49-51).
4. A gradient renewed with no habitat contact, or a habitat whose renewal leaves the network's residual unchanged (breaks F5, Fig 11 :73).
5. A derivation of one of the six terms from the other five, or a figure line that names a specific prebiotic source from the six alone (breaks F6 and the substrate).
6. One intervention that restores C1, C2 and C3 across all failure types and leaves no categorically different residue (breaks F7, Fig 9 :57).

No part of this solution may be upgraded to `grounded` until at least one of these has been run and has failed to fire, and until a flow-through ground measurement has paid for the shape at a named site.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not with a theory of abiogenesis (Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*).
2. Keep the three conditions separate in every report. A blended "energy availability index" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and destroys the diagnostic.
3. Ask, at each site, which link the chain stops at and whether that was measured (Fig 3 :29). Do not fund above the break.
4. Treat any list of candidate sources as Γ, that is, as a library whose reach must be tested against the ground, never as the answer (Fig 1 :27).
5. Use the origins literature as a set of budget filters and as at most two low-overlap witnesses, never as the verdict. Sources that quote the same review are one witness (Fig 2 :31).
6. Offer the formula as a method after a verdict is recorded (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
7. Stop at the shape. Do not name which gradient powered the earliest system; the framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32). Mark the name `gap` plainly.
