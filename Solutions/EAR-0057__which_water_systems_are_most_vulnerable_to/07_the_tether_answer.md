# The Tether's answer: which water systems are most vulnerable to four simultaneous pressures

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and the shape, `general` on the structural split, `gap` on the literal ranking of named basins, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The four pressures in the question are a union compound, not one hard question with four causes. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."* So the vulnerable system is not the top of an additive stress index; it is the system where the conditions coincide, with its own loop and its own ground. The residual is indexed per system (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*).

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, pressure and habitat. `specified`, triangulated (Figs 9 and 11).** The habitat must be enumerated outward, not composed: Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* A pressure's sign depends on the residual tracked, so there is no standing list of vulnerable infrastructure (Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*). The four conditions enter through four different bonds (subtraction for extraction, a changed gradient for warming, a quality-ordering change for pollution, a raised threshold for population growth), so a single lever clears only the single-condition region (Fig 9 :56). Where all four bind, and no pathway exports or flushes the pressure, the compound sits.

**D2, water-system substrate. `specified`, triangulated (Figs 13 and 12), one live type strain.** The six-term substrate instantiates without a seventh primitive: basin extent is space, recharge timing is time, head and pumping work are energy, the usable quality ordering is spectrum, the spendable head difference is gradient, and river and aquifer connectivity is network (Fig 13 :74: *"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*). The vulnerable configuration has a spent or effectively non-renewable gradient (Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"*), a tightly coupled network (Fig 13 :24: *"that entities relate, and influence travels"*), a return-flow cycle whose sign accumulates rather than clears (Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."*), and a quality state that the supply process does not restore. Quantity and quality are two currencies, not one score (Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*). **Type strain:** whether a basin-scale hydraulic head is the spendable gradient in the figure's sense needs an instrument (S5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 8).

**D3, measurement and verification. `general`, triangulated (Figs 2 and 7).** The ground settles, not agreement: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Independence is how little two frames draw on the same evidence (Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*). Four pressure communities reading one regional assessment are one room; a gravimetric storage reading and an in-situ quality reading pointed at the same basin are two. No ranking closes the question, because Fig 7 :13 says *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and there is no last room.

**The literal ranking of named basins. `gap`.** No figure line returns it. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A gap is a verdict here, not silence (Fig 13 :134).

**Concretely, as a `specified` prediction rather than a name:** the shape first selects closed or terminal basins and slow-recharge, groundwater-dependent systems in drylands, then coastal and small-island freshwater lenses, then heavily appropriated transboundary rivers with concurrent population growth. These are the classes in which all four conditions can bind one slot with no export pathway. The ordering among them is a hypothesis to be measured, not a result of the derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a leaderboard. No basin, aquifer or country is named as the figure-derived winner.
- Not a composite water-stress score. Aggregation destroys the per-system signal (Fig 1 :22).
- Not an additive model. The four pressures are four bond types, not four terms to sum (Fig 9 :55-56).
- Not that warming, extraction, pollution or population growth is always harmful. Valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49).
- Not that a declared water-quality or allocation standard settles anything. A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one (Fig 10 :47).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken; this is a candidate with a pre-registered refuter.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does a system carrying all four conditions show a compound residual that disappears when any one condition is removed? That is the direct union test.
2. Is there an export or flushing pathway that restores both quantity and quality on the management timescale? If yes, the no-export part of the shape fails for that system.
3. Do independent instruments (GRACE storage, in-situ quality, gauges) converge on the same per-system ranking, with low overlap between them? That is the anchor test (Fig 2 :29, :31).
4. Is the hydraulic head at basin scale actually spendable at the relevant timescale? That clears or kills the S5 type strain.
5. Standing falsifiers from section 4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`: a per-slot-preserving scalar ranking, an assignable partition of the four conditions, a contradiction from independent instruments, a seventh primitive for hydrology, or a measured quantity/quality exchange rate.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what this simulated derivation can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` corroborate the shape but cannot promote it to `grounded`, and they share a water-science evidence pool with each other, so they are one witness rather than four.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a leaderboard of doomed basins and does not claim a final answer. It says that warming, extraction, pollution and population growth are not four items on an additive score but four different kinds of pressure that can bind the same water system, so the vulnerable case is a compound with its own loop. That compound has a recognisable shape: no pathway that exports or flushes the pressure, a storage gradient spent or effectively non-renewable on the management timescale, a flow network coupled tightly enough that a local failure travels outward, a return-flow cycle that accumulates rather than clears, and a quality state the supply process does not restore. Quantity and quality are two currencies and do not trade in one number, and the residual must be read per system rather than averaged into a regional index. The framework refuses to name which basins occupy that shape, because it returns a shape and not a name; that list is owed from gauges, gravity and quality sensors, not from the closure. What it offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument tests that would close it. The ranking stays open until the ground settles it.
