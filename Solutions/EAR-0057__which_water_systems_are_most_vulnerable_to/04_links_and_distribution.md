## Wider literature that bears on the shape

The four sources above are secondary corroboration only. They are opened, quoted and provenanced in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`. Three points are worth carrying into a conversation:

1. **Terminal basins concentrate multiple pressures.** The endorheic editorial describes land-locked systems whose water loss is mainly evaporative and whose sensitivity to both climate and overexploitation is extreme. This is the class the shape criterion picks out first. It is corroboration, not the verdict: the verdict comes from Fig 9 and Fig 11.
2. **Quantity and quality are reported separately in the literature, and they interact.** The 2026 groundwater review says reduced recharge and intensive extraction jointly amplify contamination risk. That matches Fig 12 :125-130 (two currencies held apart) better than any composite index does.
3. **Population growth enters as a demand multiplier, not a background variable.** Rusli et al. report an almost twofold worsening when abstraction scales with population; Bierkens and Wada attribute demand growth directly to population and economic development. This is why population growth is a condition in the union, not a context label.

Prefer an instrument that reads a different quantity.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once the recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence (Fig 2 :40). The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 - only methods cross up. The loop is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** Formula *Direction*: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape it seeks is not a term it holds. Each user writes their own refuter first.
