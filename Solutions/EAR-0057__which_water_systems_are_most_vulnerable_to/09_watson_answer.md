## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-system gap between what a water system can supply and absorb and what four concurrent conditions demand of that same slot. Arity: four conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55, Fig 9 :57), not a partition with assignable shares. Symmetry: asymmetric; extraction is subtraction, warming changes a gradient, pollution changes a quality ordering, population growth raises a comparison threshold. Scale: first order per system, second order at the coincidence, third order through network cascades.
- **Domain 1, pressure and habitat.** Actors: the four conditions, irrigators, cities, upstream and downstream users, managers. Currency: quantity and quality, held apart. Anchor: gauges, GRACE storage, in-situ quality sensors, a basin budget that does not update on the report. Question type: union compound; habitat enumerated, never composed.
- **Domain 2, water-system substrate.** Actors: basin/aquifer extent, recharge timing, head and pumping work, quality ordering, spendable gradient, flow network. Currency: flow and storage. Anchor: conservation and measurement at the slot. Question type: cross-kind composition (Fig 13 :74).
- **Domain 3, measurement and verification.** Actors: independent instruments and expert frames. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero and agreement is one pool talking.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before choosing a frame (Fig 11 :43, Fig 9 :27-28); settle each first-order effect per slot before composing the compound (Fig 3 :20, Fig 12 :104); let the ground decide, never the agreement (Fig 2 :35, Fig 9 :60).

Strain test applied: Fig 9's union logic and Fig 11's habitat enumeration govern different objects and agree on the compound; Fig 13 governs the six primitives while the basin vocabulary is a binding onto held atoms, not a seventh term. One live type question remains, recorded in section VERDICT.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the leaderboard does not. The four pressures are a union compound of four different bond types, indexed per water system and enumerated outward through the habitat. The vulnerable configuration is: all four conditions binding one slot, no export or flushing pathway, a spent or non-renewable storage gradient, a tightly coupled flow network, an accumulating return-flow cycle, a collapsed quality ordering and two incommensurable currencies. Per-domain verdicts are D1 `specified` triangulated, D2 `specified` triangulated with one live type strain, D3 `general` triangulated, and the named ranking `gap`. The class-level prediction (terminal and slow-recharge dryland systems first, then coastal lenses and heavily appropriated transboundary rivers) is a `specified` hypothesis for measurement, not a figure-derived name. The whole result is ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** high and locatable. A wrong shape would misdirect adaptation investment toward systems that can flush or recharge and away from the compound cases, and a premature named ranking would harden into a convention that decides outcomes (Fig 10 :47). The avoidable part is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 prices this, so lead with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The class criterion and its refuters are revisable without withdrawing the substrate, and the named ranking is still owed rather than asserted. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet; no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A union test showing the compound residual vanishes when one condition is removed; an export test showing a flushing pathway restores quantity and quality; independent gravimetric, gauge and quality instruments converging on a contradicting per-system ranking; an instrument showing basin-scale head is not spendable at the relevant timescale (clears S5 either way); or a figure re-census producing a seventh hydrological primitive. Prestige or endorsement is not such an instrument.
