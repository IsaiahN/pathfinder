## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its instances. It constrains; it does not fill. For EAR-0057 the shape is the four-condition configuration with a spent gradient, a coupled network, no export pathway and two incommensurable currencies. "The Aral Sea" is a name. A ranked table of basins is a ground reading of the shape.

The same essay gives the clue that matters for a bundled question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :55 written in narrative form, and it is the reason the four pressures are not added.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on the new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question that is exactly the right move: describe what you would expect to see in a system where all four conditions bind, and let a hydrologist check it at a basin.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE, the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For EAR-0057 that means independent measurements at named slots, not another composite index built from the same regional report (Fig 2 :31, Fig 7 :13).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A hydrology result imported into the framework is an import, and its provenance is what separates it from a convergent derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The six-term substrate is a visible guess. Naming basin extent as the space atom is a binding, not a discovery of nature's own list.

## 4. Why a composite stress index is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. The same reading can come from several underlying configurations. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

For water vulnerability, a composite index is the green reading. Four different configurations (extraction dominated, warming dominated, pollution dominated, and the true compound) can produce the same score. Naming a basin as "most vulnerable" from that score is the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, not a finer index.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A framework that ranked basins and then cited its own ranking would be exactly that failure.

## 5. Does the question ask for a name?

It asks *which water systems*, which sounds like a name request. Read in effect terms, it asks which systems occupy the vulnerable configuration. The framework can answer that as a class criterion and refuse the leaderboard. The honest form is: *the systems that occupy the shape are those where all four conditions bind the same slot with no export pathway, a spent storage gradient, a coupled network and an unrestored quality state; which named basins those are is a ground reading, not a figure result.* That is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind (Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*), not a silence and not an invitation to invent a list.

## 5b. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Basin extent, recharge timing, head, quality ordering, hydraulic gradient and flow network are bindings onto atoms already in the six (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network). Naming them is not forbidden and does not move the wall. What remains wrong in the first pass is calling such bindings imports that add an atom: Fig 6 :21 says *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom,"* and these bindings add none.

The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted. A composite index still names a source from a projection where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a ban on describing held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering well-known hydrologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a gauge, a gravity measurement, a quality sensor, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
