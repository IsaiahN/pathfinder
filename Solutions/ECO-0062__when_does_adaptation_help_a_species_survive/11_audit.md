## Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the co-occurrence of a focal survival gain and an other-slot or function loss. Arity: at least three-place (focal trait, other actor or function, the two grounds). Symmetry: asymmetric, combining a subtraction on the focal slot and a comparison on a second slot; not a symmetric join. Scale: population over generations, community web, ecosystem function rate, and lineage persistence. I took the per-slot indexing from Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Domain 1, focal adaptive loop.** Actors: focal organism, heritable trait distribution, limiting condition. Currency: survival and reproduction. Anchor: measured focal survival. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, habitat and interaction web.** Actors: predators, prey, competitors, mutualists, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and valence. Anchor: independently measured other-species abundance. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, functions and cascade.** Actors: function carriers and their intermediates. Currency: function rate. Anchor: an instrument reading the rate. Admitted; this is where I expected the chain to break.
- **Domain 4, time, maintenance and seat.** Actors: ground, maintainer seat, room chain. Currency: introduction versus exclusion. Anchor: a ground that does not update. Admitted.
- **X1, adaptation as a fixed sign.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain.
- **X2, ecosystem health as one scalar.** No nameable anchor without a slot and a function choice. Not a domain.

Union, not partition: D1 and D2 form the valence-flip compound, D2 and D3 the cascade compound, D4 the verdict-permission question. Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20): trait shift, contact, valence flip, cascade, ground reading. I started from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 11's valence rule; the primary started from Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 11's capability rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :39-40, :48-54, :61-69, :73, Fig 1 :19/:22, Fig 2 :29/:35, Fig 9 :54-57 and Fig 12 :125.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `01` section 5 and `07` section 2: adaptation has no fixed sign; the harm is a union of four conditions; scope the habitat; the change is an addition that cannot be subtracted; keep the currencies separate.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim in `07` and `08`: a dispersal-enhancing trait in cane toads raises focal spread, kills naive predators, and reduces carrion removal by 52 percent through the loss of lace monitors.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19): the concrete story is told as one narrative about one species, and a reader can easily collapse it into a single "net impact" verdict;
- the **two-step enumeration** (Fig 11 :48/:52): the concrete story names the predator but only the measured study supplies the third step to the function;
- the **asymmetry and substitution clauses** (Fig 11 :61-69): the concrete story has no field test of whether the contact could be subtracted, so the permanent-work condition is asserted structurally, not measured;
- the **non-updating anchor** (Fig 2 :29): the carrion rate in the Jolly study is independent of this derivation's expectations, but the derivation did not itself verify that.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a trait's sign is relative to the residual against which it is ranged, and the same trait can carry opposite signs in two slots. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
