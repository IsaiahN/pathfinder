## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Cane toads as the worked instance.** The figure itself names cane toads and kudzu as *"the same move with the cascade unenumerated"* (Fig 11 :65). The Jolly 2015 and Hudson 2016 papers supply the measured cascade and the measured trait shift. Phillips 2006 supplies the fivefold acceleration of the invasion front. Read together they are one well-studied system, not three independent witnesses.
2. **Eco-evolutionary dynamics.** Pelletier 2009 supplies the mechanism line (adaptation of one species selects countermeasures in another) and the honest limit (no field study quantifying ongoing evolution's effect on ecosystem variables at that date). Aubree 2020 supplies the model result that adaptation can invert a biodiversity-function slope in either direction. This is the strongest low-overlap corroboration for the figure claim that valence is relative to the residual, because it shows the sign is not fixed even for a single metric.
3. **Enemy release and EICA.** Colautti 2014 is deliberately included as a partly conflicting source. It supports genetically based trait change after introduction but not the specific predicted form. It is a mechanism-level strain, returned to the domain census in `01` section 6 rather than used to flip the verdict.
4. **Parasite-mutualist continuum.** Drew 2021 corroborates Fig 11 :50 at the microbial scale: parasite and mutualist are ends of a continuum that a symbiont can traverse as context changes. This is a low-overlap field relative to invasion ecology.
5. **Networks and transport engineering.** Nagurney and Nagurney 2020 and Hayes 2005 corroborate the structural shape, not the ecological fact: adding an element that improves each user's local position can degrade the whole system, and this happens only under decentralized, user-optimizing behaviour. Mapped to the habitat, this is Fig 11 :61-62 (introduction available, exclusion not) plus Fig 11 :52-54 (cascades). It is one witness, not two.
6. **Framework essays.** The eight essays are framework-internal and carry maximal overlap with the figures, so they are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05` and are not counted as independent world witnesses.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the separator and its refuter first; the figures follow only if asked for.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not ask a recipient to endorse the framing. Ask what they would count as a failure.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or"* *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :28). Describe the per-slot effect before naming cane toads or any other case.
- **Fig 11 :48** *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* An expert's habitat list is an import with provenance, not a detail to be filled in by the sender.
- **Fig 7 :35** *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Several studies of the same toad system are one room; do not pool them as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong management decision, so independent frames are worth buying.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and which ground they would read; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## Two-stage offer (with the optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1.** Send the per-slot residual, the four conditions and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what would refute it and which function instrument they trust. Record their verdict **before** any framework is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three source links (figures extract, Jolly 2015, Pelletier 2009, plus the Braess analogue where relevant). Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because they have now seen the frame (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. The gap at F10 must stay declared; presenting a universal species list as if the figures supplied it would breach Fig 9 :32 and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
