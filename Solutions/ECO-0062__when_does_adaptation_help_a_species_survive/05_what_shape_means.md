## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ECO-0062 a **shape** is the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the species or the trait. It constrains and does not fill. "A per-slot separator of conditions, each checkable at a ground" is a shape. "Cane toads are bad," "downregulate defense whenever enemies are absent," or "the threshold is a 30% decline in a function" are names or numbers, and the figures do not supply them.

The question as posed violates this at the start. "Adaptation" and "harmful" both arrive as ordinary words with a standing sign. The derivation's first job is to bind them so they accrue a residual rather than being treated as self-explanatory.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* This is the governing rule for the habitat in this question.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* Transposed: if several habitat changes produce the same "harm" reading, the reading does not identify the source. What is owed is a discriminating instrument.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Applied here, the bootstrapping vocabulary is: residual, slot, contact, valence, cascade, introduction, substitution, ground. These are figure terms. The world terms (dispersal evolution, enemy release, parasite-mutualist continuum, user equilibrium, price of anarchy) are bindings discovered by proximity and must be provenanced, not treated as figure derivations.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar's mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For ECO-0062: naming cane toads, lace monitors, carrion removal or the Braess paradox is a binding from a world frame onto figure atoms (actor, valence, cascade, ground). It is licensed by the sources in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not by the figures. It does not move the wall (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).

## 4. Why the ordinary reading commits the source error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

The ecological analogue is direct. A measured decline in a function (say carrion removal) is a reading. Its source may be toxin-mediated predator mortality, or habitat change, or an introduced competitor, or a combination. Naming "the adaptation" as the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. The shape asks for the discriminator: a per-slot enumeration and a function instrument. Until that exists, the source is unreached, not absent. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

This is why `01` F9 refuses to treat the literature's *"we have no studies quantifying how ongoing evolution influences community or ecosystem variables in nature"* (Pelletier, Garant and Hendry 2009, opened this session) as evidence that the harm does not occur. It is a statement about the instrument.

## 5. Correction to my own earlier reading (kept visible)

I initially wrote the canonical limit as a blanket ban on naming anything the framework does not itself derive. That was an overstatement.

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- Actors, conditions, relations and functions in a habitat are **not missing primitives**. They are bindings read off the world onto figure atoms (Fig 11 :43-48). Naming them is provenance work, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
- What remains forbidden is the two moves that the exemplar also refuses: (a) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :23), and (b) naming the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources (section 4 above).
- The same correction was made in the exemplar `QMGR-0037/05` section 5b and in several BUS solutions. It is carried forward as a method, not as a recording.

Both the original overstatement and its narrowing are preserved here so neither is silently erased.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ecologists who agree that adaptation has hidden costs is the named collapse, not corroboration. Also from the essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* That is Fig 2 :34-35 in plain words.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The output is *"simulacra."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. Nothing in this folder settles whether a particular adaptation harms a particular ecosystem; that requires the ground reading the folder asks for.

## 7. The shape, stated once

> The answer is a per-slot separator, not a verdict on adaptation. It is obtained by enumerating the habitat, keeping focal survival and other-slot function in separate currencies, and checking whether the changed contact flips valence, cascades beyond first order, persists as an addition that cannot be subtracted, and is read against a ground that does not update. The particular trait, species, function and threshold are names owed by a ground reading. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
