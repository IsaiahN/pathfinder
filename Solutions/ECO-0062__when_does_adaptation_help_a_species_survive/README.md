## The question restated as an effect

A focal species' traits change in a direction that lowers its own residual against a limiting condition (it survives and reproduces better). The same change raises residuals in other slots: a predator declines, a competitor is displaced, or a function such as scavenging, nutrient cycling or primary productivity degrades. The question asks for the **separator**: the conditions under which the first effect and the second effect co-occur. What an answer would look like is a set of conditions, per slot, that can be checked at the ground; not a global "adaptation is good/bad" verdict and not a percentage. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 4 (formula step "Direction") so it can accrue a residual and be refuted.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that adaptation has hidden costs.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A set of recipients chosen because they already expect ecological harm from adaptation is a room, not an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
3. **Showing the framework before the verdict spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
