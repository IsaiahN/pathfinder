**Question:** What can comparisons with animal cognition reveal about the limits and possibilities of machine intelligence?

**Source:** reasoning.txt line 89

**Section:** 8. Human and machine intelligence

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`. Nothing here is settled by a measurement. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 8).

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## What this solves

The question is restated in effect terms before any figure is chosen. Option A reads the comparison as a template: a machine is limited where it lacks an animal faculty and possible where it can reproduce one. Option B reads the comparison as contact between two differently constituted frames: it reveals what makes a system capable, where its chain stops, and which axes its primitives cannot represent. What each leads to: A returns a faculty list and a rank, which the figures refuse as a shape-not-a-name error and an anchor error; B returns a checkable shape, per-domain verdicts, and a pre-registered refuter. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's Direction rule, so it accrues a residual and is refusable.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no scraped contact list.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. Receptiveness is forbidden as a selection criterion (Fig 2 :40; Seat Map :163, carried in the exemplar README). Selecting for agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
- No bulk-send plan. The contact file is public institutional submission and opt-in venues only.
- No claim that the framework settles whether a machine is intelligent. It returns a shape and a refuter, then stops.

## Ordering of the offer

Stage 1 sends the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Stage 2, opt-in only, offers the figure links. Stage 3 offers the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. A reply is a conversation, never corroboration.
