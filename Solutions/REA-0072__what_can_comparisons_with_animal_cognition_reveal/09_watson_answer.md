# WATSON answer: what animal cognition comparisons reveal about machine intelligence

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. It is checked for internal consistency against the figures. By its own rule (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself") it cannot settle the question it reports on. No animal was tested and no machine was measured.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a comparison between animal cognition and machine intelligence can reveal about the limits and possibilities of machine intelligence. Option A: the comparison is a template, so a machine is limited where it lacks an animal faculty and possible where it reproduces one. Option B: the comparison is contact between two differently constituted frames, so it reveals the shape of capability, the link where each system stops, and the axes its primitives cannot represent. What each leads to: A returns a faculty list, a rank and a borrowed anchor, and it spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B returns a per-domain shape, a declared gap and a pre-registered refuter. The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, so it is refusable.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between what a cross-frame comparison can license (a shape about contact) and what is usually claimed from it (a name, rank or standing faculty list). Arity: a union of at least three conditions, capability and contact, anchor legitimacy, frame independence and surplus. Fig 9 :55-57: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: the comparison is `⋛`, an ordered threshold, not `≡` identity or `+` conjunction. Scale: first order inside each frame, second order at the comparison. R is indexed per slot: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, the animal cognition frame.** Actors: animals, habitat, conditions, relations, selection. Currency: survival and contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground as selector; no maintainer in the seat (unmaintained mode, Fig 11 :56). Question type: one mode of a two-mode process.
- **Domain 2, the machine intelligence frame.** Actors: model, harness, benchmark, data, designers acting as a seat. Currency: loss, RLVR score, benchmark payment. Anchor: the benchmark only if it does not update on the system's own report (maintained mode, Fig 11 :57). Question type: the other mode.
- **Domain 3, the comparison.** Actors: two frames with differing closures. Currency: agreement and surplus. Anchor: a ground neither frame can move; independence is the parameter (Fig 8 :47). Question type: a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition.
- **Domain 4, the sought shape.** Actors: searcher and unbound shape. Currency: preference, not a term, until written down. Anchor: none until bound as an ordinary term (formula "Direction"; Fig 5 :65-66).
- **Order by dependency.** Refuse the "what is intelligence" framing before minting; describe the residual before choosing frames (Fig 8 :53); enumerate the habitat (Fig 11 :43-44); locate the broken link (Fig 3 :29-30); settle each first-order capability before the second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104); import last with gates (Fig 6 :12-13, :23).

Strain test before conclusion: Fig 11 :36 appears to deny intelligence to animals while the maintained mode grants work to a seat; re-census gives two modes, not a contradiction. Fig 8 :45 grants a union surplus while Fig 2 :40 disqualifies agreement; re-census gives one independence parameter read twice (Fig 8 :47). Both are logged, not smoothed.

- **Comparative psychology method.** Wikipedia contributors, "Comparative psychology" (Wikipedia, 2026): "intelligence" is generally defined as whatever is closest to human performance, and neglects capacities such as echolocation; early ranking attempts "floundered". Prediction: resemblance-based comparison is an instrument with limited axes and link-level failures. Corroborates C2 and the anchor verdict in Domain 3. Overlap: same broad comparative pool as the previous row, so these two are one witness.
- **Cognitive science.** Wikipedia contributors, "Embodied cognition" (Wikipedia, 2026), quoting Varela, Thompson and Rosch: cognition "depends upon the kinds of experience that come from having a body with various sensorimotor capacities". Prediction: capability is agent-and-habitat, not agent alone. Corroborates Domain 2. Overlap: low with the framework.
- **Evolutionary biology / ecology.** Wikipedia contributors, "Niche construction" (Wikipedia, 2026): organisms "actively construct important components of their niches"; Jones, Lawton and Shachak 1994 is listed. - **Philosophy of AI.** Wikipedia contributors, "Turing test" (Wikipedia, 2026): the test's results "would not depend on the machine's ability to answer questions correctly, only on how closely its answers resembled those of a human"; the first Loebner winner was "a mindless program with no identifiable intelligence". - **Machine learning.** Wikipedia contributors, "Overfitting" (Wikipedia, 2026): a model begins to "memorize" training data rather than "learning" to generalize, and an overfitted function "is likely to be less portable than a simple one". Prediction: a harness solve proves wiring, not capability. Corroborates Domain 2 and C3. Overlap: low with the framework.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The template reading is refused. The contact reading survives. Comparisons with animal cognition reveal that capability is agent-and-habitat, that limits are unreproduced habitat and severed contact before they are agent defects, that passes in a harness are wiring results rather than capability results, that the useful diagnostic is link location, and that the animal frame's usable contribution is an axis imported with provenance and a shadow test. Animal resemblance is an instrument, not the anchor, for a machine question. The comparison is an ordered threshold, not identity. The standing faculty list, rank and universal percentage are a declared `gap`, unreached rather than silence. Per-domain verdicts: Domain 1 `specified` triangulated; Domain 2 `specified` triangulated; Domain 3 `specified` triangulated with the isomer sub-claim `single-source` and strained on the fixed-atom condition; Domain 4 `general` gap. The whole is `specified`/`general`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the shape is sent with its refuter. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a faculty list and asking for agreement, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its four refuters. A second cost is importing an axis without a source gate, which Fig 6 :24 prices: "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Both are avoidable.
- **undoable:** yes. The faculty list and the rank are refused without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already state the refused form. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn; the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim is named and can be narrowed or dropped without affecting the contact verdict.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates reproduced contact from a recording; a substituted-habitat transfer with the required contact reproduced and inherited actors enumerated; a non-updating anchor constitutively about machine capability; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground (Fig 11 :71-72); a figure line that returns the faculty list. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
