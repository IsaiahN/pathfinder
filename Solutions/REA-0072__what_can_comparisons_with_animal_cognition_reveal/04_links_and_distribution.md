## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Send the shape claim and the refuter before any framework material.
- **Fig 2 :45** "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Ask what the reader would count as a failure, not whether they agree.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Describe the residual before naming the comparative frame.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Do not select recipients for receptiveness and do not pool their later agreement.
- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer; a recording of one success does.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Once a recipient has absorbed the framework interior, their agreement stops being evidence. Stage-1 verdicts are recorded before stage 2 is offered.

## 5. The two-stage offer, and the optional third stage

**Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuter conditions from `02_outreach_email.md`. Ask one question: what would you count as a failure here? Record the answer before showing anything else.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the figure links and the formula. State that the figures, not the summary, are the authority. Do not merge a stage-2 endorsement into the stage-1 verdict.

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes down what would refute their own result before running it and states the mode of the output. The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap warns that "the output is only as good as the validator" and that a theory "cannot validate itself"; the same discipline applies to anyone reusing this method.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim can survive on its own. If a reader needs the framework to understand the claim, the claim is not yet a stage-1 artifact and should be rewritten.

**No bulk send.** The CSV contains public institutional submission and opt-in venues. It contains no personal emails, no receptiveness estimate and no mailing plan. I cannot host or send material; the recipient of this packet does that.
