# Expert verification packet - the Tether on what parameters encode

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ML researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain separations, `gap` on the literal inventory. Not `grounded`.** No measurement on any model is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "what information do large learning systems actually encode in their parameters?" is a union of four overlapping conditions, the corpus trace, the representational geometry, what pays at deployment, and who can check, so a single inventory is the wrong object. The contents do not determine the source: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the weights, and only the provenance record separates them. What is encoded is a lossy, per-slot arrangement of feature directions and typed bonds, not a list of scalar values. Compression achieved and benchmark scores are frame-internal filters, not witnesses. A content claim is settled only by an intervention that changes ground-visible behaviour, of which causal tracing plus model editing is the current instance. The literal inventory is a `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 8

- **Statement:** the bundle is a union, not a partition, and the contents cannot settle the source.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 8 :51 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*.
- **What to check:** are the four conditions genuinely overlapping in practice? Can you exhibit two training histories with indistinguishable parameter geometry but different provenance? If so, the source is not readable off the contents.
- **Refuter:** a blind instrument that reads the source uniquely from the contents with no provenance and no ground contact; or a proof that the four conditions are a partition.

### C2 - interpretability-facing; checkable against current mechanistic work

- **Statement:** encoded content is per feature slot and is an arrangement, not an ingredient list.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 12 :5 - *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :111 - *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*
- **What to check:** do the current results support this? Elhage et al. 2022 report *"more features than they have neurons"* with features as directions. Meng et al. 2022 report factual associations that are *"localized, directly-editable computations"* in *"mid-layer feed-forward modules"*. Is the per-slot reading faithful, or is a whole-model scalar sufficient?
- **Refuter:** a single global scalar from the parameters that predicts ground behaviour across all slots and domains; or a complete ingredient inventory, with no arrangement information, that fully predicts intervention outcomes.

### C3 - framework-internal plus ground test

- **Statement:** settlement requires a contact-changing intervention; benchmarks and compression ratios are filters.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :40 - *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 1 :27 - *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** can you name a purely internal measure of parameter content that settles a contested ground question without an intervention? If not, what would such a measure have to look like, and is there a proto-instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :34)?
- **Refuter:** such a measure that works in a blind test; or a demonstration that the corpus-to-parameter transform is lossless on the content that matters (which would make the recording reading stand).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not an inventory of what any specific model encodes. That is a `gap`.
- Not that parameters are a lossless copy of the corpus or a reconstruction of the data-generating process.
- Not that a compression ratio, benchmark score, parameter count or author reputation settles content.
- Not a single global statement about "what the model knows". Per-slot indexing is structural.
- Not `grounded`. No model was run and no measurement was taken.
- Not that convergence of representations recovers the source. A shared statistical geometry is compatible with an unrecoverable lineage (Fig 13 :121).
- Not a validation of the Tether framework. Metatheories cannot validate themselves.
