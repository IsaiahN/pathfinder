# The Tether's answer: what information large learning systems encode in their parameters

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain separations, `gap` on the literal inventory. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No model was run.

## 1. The residual it answers

We hold a reading, a parameter vector, and ask what source produced it and what it contains. Four candidates overlap: a compressed copy of the corpus, a model of the world, surface statistical associations, and a geometric trace of human output. The question as posed asks for one inventory, which forces a partition onto a union. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

The binding used throughout: "encoded information" means the part of the parameters' arrangement that would have to change to change a specific ground-visible behaviour, together with a provenance record of where that arrangement came from. This makes the sought shape an ordinary, refusable term.

## 2. The shape

1. **The question is a union, so verdicts stay separate.** Source, content, settlement and checking are four conditions with different anchors. Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* No averaged statement such as "the model encodes a world model of reliability r" is licensed.
2. **The contents do not determine the source.** Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Two training histories can produce the same geometry. Provenance, not inspection, is the whole issue. The essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument analogy: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer settled it, not an argument.
3. **What is encoded is a per-slot arrangement, not an ingredient list.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global phrase like "the model encodes language" aggregates away the only signal that says where to look. Interpretability results match: features behave as directions (Elhage et al. 2022) and specific facts are localised and editable (Meng et al. 2022).
4. **Compression and benchmarks are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The information bottleneck's *"relevant compression"* is a normative ideal defined over a distribution the learner does not hold; Zhang et al. show networks fit random labels perfectly. Neither fact tells you what a given network encoded.
5. **Settlement requires a contact-changing intervention.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Causal tracing plus model editing is the current instance. A probe trained on the activations it is meant to explain is not an anchor: Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
6. **The exact lineage is not recoverable, but a statistical geometry can converge.** Huh et al. 2024 report convergence across models toward *"a shared statistical model of reality"*. The RSI essay says *"the best you could do is take traces of patterns"*. These are compatible: Fig 13 :121, *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Convergence of the geometry is not reconstruction of the source.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that parameters are a lossless recording of the corpus. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*
- Not that a model reconstructs the data-generating process. Text carries *"almost no pretext"*, so training reproduces *"the traces the thought left, not the thought"*.
- Not that a single global statement about "what the model knows" is licensed. Per-slot indexing is structural.
- Not a result. No figure line enumerates contents; that is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not a validation of the framework. A simulated derivation cannot validate itself.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the spectrometer resolved metamerism; here the corresponding questions are:

- **R1:** can an instrument read the source of a parameter set uniquely from its contents, blind, with no provenance and no ground contact? If yes, F2 falls.
- **R2:** can a purely internal content measure settle a contested ground question without intervention or benchmark? If yes, the anchor rule falls.
- **R3:** does one global scalar predict ground behaviour across all slots and domains? If yes, per-slot indexing falls.
- **R4:** is the corpus-to-parameter transform lossless on the content that matters? If yes, the recording reading stands.
- **R5:** does a figure line name a specific content, closing the gap without an instrument? If yes, the gap closes.
- **R6:** are the four candidates a genuine partition? If yes, the union falls.

These were written before the conclusion (section 6 of `01`). Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the inventory.** The six-term set and the bonds are *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*, so declaring them makes a choice visible, not neutral. The instrument that would name a source in general does not exist yet at any resolution: Fig 6 :34, *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* The answer stops there and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and the canonical README adds *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give an inventory of what a large model's weights contain, and it does not claim one is possible from the figures alone. It says the question is a union of four overlapping things, source, content, settlement and checking, so a single answer is the wrong object; that the weights' contents cannot tell you which source produced them, because convergent derivation and adopted import look identical and only the record separates them; that what is encoded is a lossy, per-feature arrangement of directions and bonds rather than a list of scalar values; that compression ratios and benchmark scores are filters for what gets tried, not witnesses for what is true; and that a claim about content is settled only by an intervention that changes ground-visible behaviour. What it offers is that shape, six written refuters, and the instrument still owed. The inventory stays open until the ground settles it.
