Symmetry: the map from source to parameters is many-to-one, so the reading is invariant under exchange of sources producing it. This is the decisive property: a content-only answer cannot discriminate sources. Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, probe, carry; settlement before naming. Fig 3 :20 (:dependency, not importance) and Fig 3 :27 (a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55-57 (union unbundling), Fig 8 :50-52 (provenance), Fig 1 :19/:27 (per slot, frame-internal), Fig 12 :5 (bond) and Fig 13 :134 (unreachable is a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: the question is a union; contents do not determine source; content is per-slot arrangement; filters are not witnesses; settlement needs intervention; the inventory is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete answer of the form "the weights encode feature F at layer L with reliability r", which is what the question as posed invites.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **provenance requirement** (Fig 8 :51), which a whole-model content claim silently drops;
- the **per-slot indexing** (Fig 1 :19), which an averaged content claim throws away;
- the **type of settlement** (Fig 2 :29, Fig 11 :40), which a benchmark score substitutes with a channel measure that can saturate.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the four conditions are separable, and a content claim needs an intervention plus a record. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no model was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
