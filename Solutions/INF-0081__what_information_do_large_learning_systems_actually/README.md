## Residual stated as an effect

The question asks which source produced a reading (the parameters) and what that reading contains. The candidate answers, a compressed copy of the corpus, a model of the world, surface associations, and a geometric trace of human output, are not mutually exclusive. An answer is useful only if it tells us which interventions change which behaviour and fails if wrong. The deliverable is therefore a shape plus a discriminator owed, not a name or an inventory.

## What an answer would look like

Bound as an ordinary term: "encoded information" means *the part of the parameters' arrangement that would have to be changed to change a specific ground-visible behaviour, together with a provenance record of where that arrangement came from*. That binding is refusable (Fig 5 :65, the direction of the question must be made an ordinary term before a run can check it).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade a label. Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*, and Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing an outside frame contributes. Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: public opt-in and institutional channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that attach to unsolicited bulk outreach. See `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
