## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a shape is: *a lossy, per-slot arrangement of feature directions and bonds, imported through a corpus that is itself a projection, whose source is undecidable from the contents and whose content is settled only by an intervention*. That constrains. It does not fill. "The model encodes X, Y and Z" is a name and an inventory, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary was per-slot residuals, bonds, import and ground, and the new terms that correlated were feature directions, superposition, causal intervention and representational convergence.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. We described the residual as an identifiability failure, not as a neural-network jargon term.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done because *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For this question the stop is not an inventory but a discriminator: we know the shape when we can say what instrument would have to exist (R1 to R6 in `01` section 6).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For parameters this means: saying "feature direction" or "MLP module" is a binding from the interpretability frame onto terms the framework already holds, not a discovery of a new primitive. Saying "this exact fact is at this exact layer, and editing it changes behaviour" is a name licensed by intervention (Meng et al. 2022). Saying "the weights contain a model of the world" is a name with no instrument behind it.

## 4. Why the "compressed copy of the corpus" reading fails

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

The parameter vector reads like the green. Many corpora, objectives and lineages could produce it. Naming one of them as the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator with provenance, not a name.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the specific loss: text carries context and some subtext but *"almost no pretext"*, so *"Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought'"*. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says the same of LLMs: *"the pattern encoded in the geometric weights"* comes from *"pages and books of humanity's work"*, and *"the best you could do is take traces of patterns"*. So the corpus-to-parameter transform is a projection of a projection. This is Fig 4 :27, *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point"*, and Fig 4 :25, *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

## 5. Correction to my own §1 to §4: the rule governs missing primitives

I nearly overstated the "shape, not a name" rule into a ban on naming anything in a model. That is wrong, and the correction is kept here rather than silently applied.

- The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. The subject is a primitive not held in Γ.
- Feature directions, the six structures of Fig 13, and the seven bonds of Fig 12 are **already held**. Naming one of them in a particular model is a **binding from the interpretability frame** onto an available term. It is provenance work, not the invention of a seventh primitive.
- What remains forbidden is presenting a binding as a figure-derived name, or claiming that the binding moves the wall. Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 6 :26: *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."*
- My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The error is still naming a source from a reading when several sources could give that reading. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a blanket ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

This mirrors the correction in the exemplar solution `QMGR-0037`, `05` section 5b, and is triangulated with Fig 13 :115, *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not."*

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous researchers who agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** an experiment on any model and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
