## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- A **shape**: a per-slot, across-scale, perturbation-linked record of bounded residual, ranged against an anchor that does not move, with the cascade and any substitution written into the lineage.
- A **name**: "resilience = recovery within 14 months", or "threshold at 30 per cent coral cover", or "presence of functional group X".

The shape is what `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 delivers. Every name is a binding from the ecology frame and needs its own provenance and ground settlement.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* uses proximity: start with the vocabulary you have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: start with "intact", "recovery", "disturbance", "function", and let the figure lines narrow toward "per-slot residual under change in the enumerated habitat".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." An effect description of resilience evidence is checkable by a field ecologist without their first adopting the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here: when the objective is bound and the refuters written, the search for evidence stops being a search for a definition and becomes a measurement.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works* on variety: "variance is built into the framework of everything. It's a robust property that hardens an entire population against something that would affect them en masse if they were homogeneous. That is why 'variation' ... Diversity is a natural hedge." This is the framework's own reason that a fixed roster cannot be the answer, and it aligns with the ecology literature on response diversity.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A threshold becomes a name when a perturbation dataset pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "Recovery within 14 months" imported from a lake study into a dryland is an import; it must say what gap it explains and carry its origin.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term set used in the audit is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Turning one of its structures into *the* mechanism of ecological resilience would hide the choice.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism relevant to ecological indicators. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument," and "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

Applied here: a function reading (primary production, nutrient retention, pollinator visits) can be produced by several underlying relation structures. The reading does not name the source. Redundancy, response diversity, and a lucky absence of disturbance can all produce "intact" at the same time. A resilience assessment that names a source from the reading commits the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states the validation limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A monitoring index produced by the same frame it certifies is not a validator.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the canonical shape of an answer to ECO-0100?** A kind of evidence, specified in effect terms:

> For a named function, at a named scale, over a named horizon: a per-slot record of the habitat's actors, conditions and relations, enumerated outward along the cascade, taken before and after a change, with recovery rate and distributional signals read on each slot by instruments that already return something, ranged against an outcome that does not move when a frame changes its mind, with the overlap between measuring frames declared and any substituted habitat's omitted and introduced elements written down.

This is a shape. It does not say which function, which threshold, which index, or which species. Those are bindings.

**Does the audit commit the forbidden error?** No, provided it keeps the word "resilient" bound to a stated function and change. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F11 records the limit and F6 records the binding discipline. The audit would commit the error if it delivered a universal resilience number; it does not.

### 5b. Correction to my own reading of "habitat"

I initially treated the Tether's *habitat* as the ecology textbook *niche*. That is the exact mapping the source declines. *Arrival of the Fittest* has a section titled "Three mappings offered and declined," and on the habitat it says: "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes." The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why Fig 11 :43 says *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed"* rather than borrowing the ecological term.

Retracted: the niche mapping. Retained: the enumeration rule, which is independent of the mapping. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

### 5c. Correction to my own reading of "variance"

I initially read "variation" in *How Intelligence Actually Works* as a generic plea for diversity. It is narrower: the hedge is against "something that would affect them en masse if they were homogeneous." That is a claim about correlated exposure, not about species counts. It supports per-slot, low-overlap measurement (Fig 2 :31) rather than a richness index. Narrowed accordingly.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well-known ecologists to endorse the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try it and a refutation if it fails.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds that such outputs "must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any ecosystem and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Independence is the scarce resource.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different" and "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." For this question, ten indices from one monitoring programme are one opinion. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*
