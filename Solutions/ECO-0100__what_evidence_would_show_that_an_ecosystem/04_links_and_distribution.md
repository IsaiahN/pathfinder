## What in the wider literature bears on this question

1. **The two definitions of resilience.** The *Ecological resilience* page distinguishes engineering resilience (speed of return to equilibrium) from ecological resilience (capacity to absorb disturbance and reorganize while retaining function and feedbacks). The question's wording, "not merely intact today, but able to adapt as species, relationships, and conditions change," is the second definition. Do not answer it with the first. Cite this when explaining why a snapshot cannot settle it.
2. **Cross-scale dependence.** Walker et al. 2004 make panarchy, the influence of scales above and below the focal scale, one of the four aspects of resilience and state that "No SES can be understood by examining it at only one scale." This is the world-side counterpart of Fig 4's membrane and Fig 11's cascade. It is corroboration, not derivation.
3. **Early-warning instruments already exist.** The *Critical transition* page documents critical slowing down, rising autocorrelation, variance, and flickering, tested in lakes, forests, food webs and drylands. These are Fig 6's instruments *"already returning something"* that can be sharpened inward. They are not proof of coverage (Fig 8 :39).
4. **Absence is not a verdict.** The *Regime shift* page states that failure to find multiple regimes does not establish a single regime. This is Fig 8 :39 and Fig 9 :44 in an independent field.
5. **The DDT cascade.** Fig 11 :52 names DDT reaching the eagles three steps out. The *Biomagnification* page corroborates the mechanism and the eggshell-thinning outcome. This is the clearest low-overlap check available for the enumerate-the-cascade rule.
6. **A definition is not an instrument.** The 167 definitions and 70 stability concepts on the *Ecological stability* page are the world-side evidence for Fig 3's broken objective link. Bind the objective before measuring.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the evidence shape and its refuter; offer the links after a verdict.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* An outside frame must not hold the interiors. Its verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient expects to see if the shape claim is true before giving them the reading list.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong resilience assessment, so buy independent frames: field, experiment, remote sensing, local knowledge, models, each declared with its overlap.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the evidence shape and the four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and for the system they know best; (3) record that answer; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, later agreement is not evidence. Stage 1's verdict is recorded first and never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user must write a refuter before running it, and state the mode of the result.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 shape is bound to a function, a change, a scale and a horizon. As currently drafted in the question alone, "resilient" is unbound and a recipient cannot check it. Bind it first; the four checks in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` section 6 do this.
