## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an intactness reading and a capacity claim. Arity: many slots (species, interaction, condition, function), indexed per slot, not aggregated. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Symmetry: asymmetric; influence travels along relations and cascades outward (Fig 11 :52), valence is relative to R (Fig 11 :49-50). Scale: multi-scale with a lossy transform; Fig 4 :30 measures the round trip and Fig 13 :31 names chaos as a remainder compounding through an amplifying chain.
- **Domain 1, ecological ground.** Actors: species, conditions, relations. Currency: contact, survival, function, recovery. Anchor: the post-change observation that does not move when a frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29). Present, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, indicator frame.** Actors: surveys, remote sensing, indices, models, local knowledge. Currency: readings. Anchor: only when pinned to Domain 1; a definition alone is a convention (Fig 10 :18). This is a frame at risk of anchor loss, not yet a domain in its own right.
- **Domain 3, perturbation / verification.** Actors: disturbances, natural experiments. Currency: residual under change, recovery rate, variance, autocorrelation, flickering. Anchor: the ground's response. Present.
- **Domain 4, management seat (conditional).** Present only where a maintainer acts; otherwise the habitat is pure contact (Fig 11 :56, :58). A partition boundary inside the question.

Question type: **union**, not partition. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, perturbation plan, learn and carry (Fig 3 :4-16); bind the objective before measuring (Fig 3 :27); refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the union from Fig 9 and the capability claim from Fig 11, I derived the union from Fig 2's independence rule and the empty-reading rule from Fig 3 :22-23. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :43-52 (enumerate the habitat and cascade), Fig 2 :29/:31 (unmoving anchor, independence), Fig 9 :32 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: "a per-slot, across-scale, perturbation-linked record of bounded residual in the enumerated habitat, ranged against an unmoving anchor, with cascade and substitution written down."
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a field claim about a specific ecosystem, e.g. "this lake is resilient to a phosphorus pulse."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- which slot carried the residual and which scale it was read on (Fig 1 :19);
- which second- or third-order relation produced the outcome (Fig 11 :52);
- which frame supplied which reading, and how much evidence the frames shared (Fig 2 :31);
- which elements a substituted habitat failed to reproduce or unintentionally introduced (Fig 11 :68-69).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a headline intactness score is not the discriminator, and the capability claim concerns contact under change. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this simulation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the shorthand is the full answer" would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
