# The Tether's answer: what evidence would show that an ecosystem remains resilient?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading and not a measurement of any ecosystem.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for evidence that separates "intact today" from "able to adapt as species, relationships, and conditions change." The first is a snapshot of composition and function at a time. The second is a capacity that registers only at the contact between system and habitat across a change. The residual is the gap between them, and it is indexed per slot: Fig 1 :19, **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22, **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

The three changes named in the question are a **union compound**, not one hard problem: Fig 9 :55, **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** Each needs its own loop, and their intersection needs its own.

## 2. The shape

1. **Intactness is not the answer.** A global reading near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state (Fig 1 :13). The evidence must be per slot: species, interactions, conditions, functions, and the scales on which each is read.

2. **Capability lives at the contact, not in the system alone.** Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**; :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."** So the evidence must be taken before and after a change, not while the habitat is held still. "Remains" is permanent work: Fig 11 :63, **"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."**

3. **The habitat is enumerated, and the cascade is included.** Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."** :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."** :52: **"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."** A first-order study is a different object, not a weaker version of the same one.

4. **Relations carry the difference; rosters do not.** Fig 11 :49: **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"**; :50: **"mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds"**; :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."** Two systems with the same species set but different relation structure are Fig 12 isomers: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

5. **The anchor must not move, and agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** :31: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** Field observation, experiments, remote sensing, Indigenous and local knowledge, and models are separate witnesses only to the degree that they draw on different evidence.

6. **The objective has to be bound first.** Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** "Resilient of what, to which change, at which scale, over which horizon" must be written down as an ordinary term with a refuter before measurement. Otherwise the program measures an assumed objective.

7. **Instruments that already return something are the ones to sharpen.** Critical slowing down, rising autocorrelation, rising variance, and flickering are readings from instruments in use; Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* But *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39). No warning signal is a budget report, not a certificate.

8. **Substitutions must declare their drops.** Fig 11 :66: **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."** The two silent failure modes are *"what you failed to reproduce"* and *"what you brought with you"* (Fig 11 :68-69). :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

9. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** A threshold or a species roster is a binding from the ecology frame onto the shape, with its own provenance and its own ground settlement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a resilience score, a threshold, or a single index. No verbatim figure line licenses one.
- Not that any species, trait, or functional group confers resilience. Valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that a mesocosm, a model, or a restored site proves field capability. A substituted habitat proves wiring.
- Not that absence of early-warning signals proves resilience. Search that finds nothing is not proof of absence.
- Not that the three change conditions are one problem. They are a union and are unbundled.
- Not `grounded`. No field measurement settled any of this. No ecosystem was read.
- Not that the Tether's "habitat" is the ecological niche. The source explicitly declines that mapping (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 5b).

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A ground reading, not a further argument. Concretely, the following would move the shape toward `grounded`:

- A **per-slot before/after record** across a real perturbation, with recovery rate, variance, autocorrelation and function persistence read on each slot and scale, not averaged.
- **Source-discriminating instruments** where a function reading has several possible sources. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here: an instrument that distinguishes redundancy from response diversity, or a rewired relation from a stable roster.
- **Low-overlap triangulation**, each frame ranged separately against the same outcome. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- **A named substitution audit** wherever a model, mesocosm, or restoration is used: what was not reproduced, what was unintentionally introduced.
- **Standing falsifiers, run and reported**, from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3: aggregation parity, roster parity, direct-contact parity, a moving anchor, or an agent-only improvement that moves the ground.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six"* (*They're Scanning Books*). Declaring primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of the instruments currently returning something and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**.

Round trip gap: the shorthand "this ecosystem is resilient" does not carry back down to every slot-level prediction. The gap is qualitative here (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q5), because no ground reading was taken. It is reported, not hidden.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a resilience score and does not claim any species or trait makes an ecosystem resilient. It says that "intact today" and "able to adapt" are different claims, and that a headline intactness score can be near zero while one live slot carries the signal that will decide the outcome. The evidence that would separate them is a kind of record, not a number: the actors, conditions, and relations in contact with a named function are enumerated outward along the cascade, read per slot and per scale before and after a change, with recovery rate and warning signals taken from instruments already in use, while the measuring frames are kept independent and ranged against an outcome none of them can move. It refuses to name a species roster or a threshold, because a system with the same species but a different relation structure is a different substance, and because the framework returns a shape, not a name. For a mesocosm or a model it writes down what was not reproduced and what was introduced. No warning signal found is a budget report, not proof of resilience. What it offers is the shape any admissible evidence must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The ground, not agreement, settles it.
