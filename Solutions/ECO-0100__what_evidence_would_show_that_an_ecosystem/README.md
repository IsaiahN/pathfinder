## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Ten indices reading the same monitoring dataset are one frame wearing ten names.
3. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict on the evidence shape spends the only thing an outside frame contributes.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public, opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
