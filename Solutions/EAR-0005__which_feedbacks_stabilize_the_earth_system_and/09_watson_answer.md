## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A perturbation is a gap between the Earth system state and a reference (radiative equilibrium, preindustrial baseline, model control). Arity: indexed by slot (region, subsystem, variable), not one global number (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: sign is directed (damping versus amplifying) and not recoverable from magnitude alone (Fig 13 :76). Scale: fast and slow, local and global, with the kernel invariant and actors, currency and anchor changing (Fig 4 :19 20). Fig 9 names the question a union compound: sign relative to residual is one condition, slot and scale another.
- **Domain 1, physical radiative frame.** Actors: Planck response, water vapour, lapse rate, ice albedo or surface reflectivity, clouds. Currency: radiative flux in W m^-2 K^-1. Anchor: measured outgoing longwave radiation and temperature. Union member.
- **Domain 2, biogeochemical and ecological frame.** Actors: ocean carbon uptake, chemical weathering, photosynthesis and CO2 fertilisation, permafrost, wetlands and methane, forests. Currency: carbon mass flux and concentration. Anchor: atmospheric CO2 and CH4 records. Union member with a different currency and ground from Domain 1.
- **Domain 3, figure and metatheory frame.** Actors: cycles, bonds, residuals, anchors, valence. Currency: composition inside closure(Gamma) and settlement against the ground. Anchor: the figures as metalaws. Governs the meaning of stabilize and amplify.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach frame.** Actors: researchers and reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Fix the anchor and reference, enumerate the habitat by contact outward, classify the loop sign relative to that residual, then ask which named feedback dominates (Fig 3 :20). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27 28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the standing list does not. A feedback is a closed loop whose sign is decided by the loop and is relative to a stated residual, slot and scale, so no list of stabilizers and amplifiers survives a change of reference. The classification must be per slot (a global average hides the live regional signal), scale relative, and alert to indirect cascades. Physical radiative and biogeochemical feedbacks have different currencies and form a functional group, not isomers, and must not be silently summed. The specific magnitude ranking and the identity of the dominant feedback are a `gap` after a genuine census, owed to instruments and model ensembles and imported with provenance. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest figure grounded point for outreach is the refusal of the standing list, Fig 11 :51, together with the per slot rule, Fig 1 :19.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape claim carries six written falsifiers and a declared gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list or a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual, the shape claim and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused list is removable without touching the derivation, and the ranking gap is declared rather than filled with a guessed name. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and refusing the list adds a term that refuses rather than deleting one (Fig 6 :42 43).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Flux observations that sign each named loop at a stated reference and slot, independent model ensembles and paleoclimate constraints that agree on the ranking without a shared evidence pool, or a source discriminating instrument that tells which loop produced a given radiative reading would move the ranking from `gap` toward `grounded`. A figure line that ranks named feedbacks would reverse the gap verdict itself. Any of the six falsifiers would break the shape claim. Prestige and endorsement are not such instruments.
