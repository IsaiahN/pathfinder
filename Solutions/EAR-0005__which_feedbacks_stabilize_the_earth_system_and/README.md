## What an answer would look like (binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term)

Writing down the sought-for shape before the run, per the formula's Direction step: an answer is a rule that, given a named residual (a perturbation from a stated reference), a slot (region, subsystem, variable) and a scale, assigns each closed feedback loop a sign relative to that residual, with the ranking of named physical feedbacks left to measurement. Refusing it is a prediction about the shape of any valid answer, not a claim about which feedback dominates.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk send plan.
- No claim that a figure line names which physical feedback stabilizes or amplifies. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 (a mirror cannot correct you), Fig 2 :40 (agreement from a shared evidence pool is not corroboration), and Fig 10 :42 (a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 9 :60). Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.
