## 1. A shape is a constraint that can be stated before a name is available

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for QMGR2-0055 a shape is: **a signed returning loop, between the black hole and an enumerated habitat, crossing a scale membrane, holding the pair on a per-object relation.** "AGN feedback is the regulator" is a name. The shape does not deliver it.

A correction carried from the exemplar applies here too. The phrase "a shape, not a name" governs a primitive *not held* in the library. "Regulator" is not a new primitive in this derivation; it is a binding onto held structural terms (cycle, catalyst, habitat, membrane), so naming a specific actor is not forbidden in principle. What is forbidden is presenting that binding as a framework derivation rather than as an import with provenance. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, and recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the starting vocabulary was "feedback", "scaling relation", "gas supply", "duty cycle"; the recurring new terms were "cycle", "valence", "cascade", "membrane".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So we describe "output re-enters as input", not "quasar mode feedback", when sending the claim to a reader outside the framework.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the essay's version of Fig 9 :54 and it applies directly: the regulator question is a union.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". In this run the stop is not a final name. It is the point where further search inside the figure set returns no new constraint and the remaining work is instrumental (R_T, time-domain valence).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question, an instrument that resolves which returning edge actually closes the loop in a statistical sample would name it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. The astronomy literature supplies bindings: outflow kinetic power (Ruffa et al. 2026), gas supply (Liu et al. 2026, Roberts et al. 2026), boundary conditions (Mountrichas et al. 2026). Each is recorded with author, venue, year and quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (mapped from the validated exemplar, not re-opened this session) is recorded there as saying: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." We do not use that quote as a primary claim here because it was not re-opened; it is noted for provenance only.

The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one of its structures as *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Source discrimination: why the scaling relation cannot name the regulator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied here: the M_BH to host correlation is a projection. Many combinations of feeding, feedback, morphology and merger history can produce the same small scatter. Reading "AGN feedback" off the correlation is the metamerism error: naming a source from a reading. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (resolved gas kinematics across a statistical sample, time-domain accretion states), not a sharper argument. That is exactly the gap Ruffa et al. (2026) identify.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* (opened) adds the general form: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A model that reproduces the correlation is not thereby the mechanism that produced it.

## 5. The union and the per-slot reading

Two essay results govern the census, not just the prose:

- *Library of Babel*: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" -> union, unbundle (Fig 9 :54).
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened): "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." -> the regulator is a property of the black hole and its habitat together; an agent-only account (a quasar with fixed efficiency) is the substrate question that dissolves, while the contact question does not. The same essay insists on per-slot rates: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." -> do not average galaxies into a cosmic star formation rate.

## 6. What licenses the claim that this derivation is honest about itself

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."

## 7. Consequence for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous astronomers who agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From the same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
