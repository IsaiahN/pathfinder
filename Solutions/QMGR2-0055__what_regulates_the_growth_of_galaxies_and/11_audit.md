## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :121 (cycle), Fig 11 :43, :49, :52 (habitat, valence, cascade), Fig 9 :54 (union), Fig 4 :21, :30 (membrane, gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: "regulation is a signed returning loop over an enumerated habitat, across a lossy scale membrane, in a union of three conditions".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete astronomy claims: quasar-driven winds quench star formation; stellar feedback indirectly regulates black hole growth through gas supply; halo binding energy carries the scatter.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **sign of the return in each condition**, which the shorthand labels "signed" but does not measure; the cycle figure supplies the sign requirement, not its value in a given object;
- the **identity of the returning edge**, which the shorthand leaves as a shape while the physics frame wants a specific agent (winds, jets, radiation, supernovae, mergers);
- the **magnitude of the parsec-to-kiloparsec gap**, asserted nonzero from Fig 4 :30 but not measured for the load-bearing quantities;
- the **valuation of each actor per residual**, which the shorthand records as relative but does not enumerate object by object.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the returning-loop structure and the union of conditions. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading was taken by this agent; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
