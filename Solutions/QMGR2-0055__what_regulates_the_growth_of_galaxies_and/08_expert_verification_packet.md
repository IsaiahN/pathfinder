# Expert verification packet: the Tether on galaxy and black hole growth

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astronomer or galaxy-formation theorist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No measurement by this agent is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Regulation of black hole and galaxy growth is not a one-way supply chain. It is a signed loop: the output of accretion re-enters as an input to the gas that feeds both the black hole and the stars, and the sign of the return decides whether growth self-limits or runs away. The black hole persists across episodes and can act repeatedly; its effect changes valence depending on which residual is considered, so the same outflow can quench star formation and self-limit black hole growth. The coupling cascades through molecular gas several steps out from the accretion event and crosses a lossy boundary between parsec and kiloparsec descriptions. The question is a union of at least three overlapping conditions, feeding and feedback, stellar coupling through gas, and halo and morphology supply. The framework does not name the unique regulator; it returns the shape any answer must have, with refuters written in advance.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Cycle check:** a statistical sample with resolved molecular gas response to a known accretion-state change, so the returning edge is measured, not inferred (Ruffa et al. 2026).
2. **Valence check:** matched hosts where the same outflow is measured both to suppress star formation and to reduce black hole accretion at the same epoch.
3. **Membrane check:** a measured parsec-to-kiloparsec transform and its round trip gap for the quantities that carry the scaling relation.

Standing falsifiers: a chain that produces the coupling without a return; invariant valence; zero R_T; one lever clearing all conditions.
