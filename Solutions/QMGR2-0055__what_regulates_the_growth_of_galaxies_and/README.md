## What the question asks in effect terms

The observed mass of a central supermassive black hole tracks properties of its host galaxy (velocity dispersion, bulge stellar mass, halo binding energy) across many decades in mass. Under a null model in which the two growth channels are independent, those correlations are not predicted. The residual is the gap between that null prediction and the observed coupling. "What regulates the growth" asks for the thing that couples the two channels and holds them on the observed relation. Option A: name one regulator (for example "AGN feedback") and assert it. Option B: derive the shape any regulator must have and leave the slot open until the ground settles it. This package takes B.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among selected readers is a weighted average of influence, not a ground reading.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors before it returns a verdict.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
