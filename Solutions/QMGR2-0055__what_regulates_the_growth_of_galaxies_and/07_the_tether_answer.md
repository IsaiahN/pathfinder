# The Tether's answer: what regulates galaxy and black hole growth

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Under a null model in which black hole accretion and star formation grow independently, the observed tight coupling between black hole mass and host properties is not predicted. R is the gap between that null prediction and the observation, indexed per object, not per cosmic average. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The question asks for the thing that couples the channels and holds them on the relation. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in three conditions is not a harder case of either. Each condition gets its own loop; the intersections get their own loop too.

## 2. The shape

1. **Regulation is a closing loop, not a one-way chain.** Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A supply chain can describe growth; only the returning edge can regulate it. The sign of that edge (heating and expulsion versus compression and funnelling) decides whether growth self-limits or runs away.
2. **The black hole is a catalyst.** Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Cold gas is the spent operand; the halo is a condition. The figure also warns that "nothing records which a term is", so whether an engine is spent or regenerating is a time-domain measurement we do not yet have.
3. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same outflow is a malfactor to star formation (it removes or heats the fuel) and a benefactor to the black hole's own self-regulation (it cuts off its own supply). "Negative feedback" is too flat. Fig 11 :50 adds: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
4. **The regulators are habitat actors, enumerated.** Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Cold molecular gas, stars and supernovae, the black hole's accretion states, halo binding energy, mergers, and the cosmic supply are read off the world. They are not derived from the six-term set.
5. **The coupling cascades.** Fig 11 :52: "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Accretion state -> outflow -> molecular gas -> star formation. The galaxy-scale measurement is several steps out from the engine.
6. **The question is a union.** Feeding and feedback, stellar coupling through gas, and halo and morphology supply are overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." No single lever clears the intersections.
7. **The coupling crosses a membrane with a nonzero round trip gap.** Fig 4 :21: "The membrane is the boundary between the sizes, and the rule about what may cross it. Not a wall: a filter with a direction." Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." The parsec and kiloparsec descriptions share a kernel but not a currency or an anchor.
8. **Growth is recursive.** Fig 13 :88-89: "The residual of the turn just closed is what the next turn composes against, so no two passes begin from the same place." The same loop produces quenching at one epoch and maintenance at another because the starting gas reservoir and halo are different.
9. **The framework does not name the regulator.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Naming AGN feedback, jets, winds, supernovae or mergers as *the* regulator is a binding from the astrophysics frame, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not a derivation.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any single agent is *the* regulator. The shape constraints are not a name.
- Not that the observed correlation proves causation. Fig 12 :94: "The arrangement is the substance." The correlation is the projection; the loop is the arrangement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that "cannot comment on the source" cannot settle it.
- Not that simulation agreement settles the mechanism. Several families share subgrid physics and calibration data (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the parsec to kiloparsec transform is lossless. R_T is asserted nonzero and unmeasured.
- Not `grounded`. No instrument of our own was run. This is a simulated derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminating instrument, not another argument. The opened sources point to three concrete checks:

1. **Cycle check.** Resolve the molecular gas response to a known accretion state change across a statistical sample, so the returning edge is measured rather than inferred. Ruffa et al. (2026) argue ALMA2040-scale sensitivity is what turns case studies into statistics.
2. **Valence check.** In matched hosts, measure whether the same outflow both suppresses star formation and reduces the black hole's own accretion rate at the same epoch. If the valences cannot be separated, the "relative to residual" claim needs refinement.
3. **Membrane check.** Measure R_T between parsec-scale outflow properties and kiloparsec-scale gas and SFR response for the quantities that carry the scaling relation. A zero gap would break the membrane claim.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3: a chain that produces the coupling without a return; invariant valence; zero R_T; a single lever clearing all conditions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The six-term set and the figure rules return a shape here. They do not select an actor, and no amount of searching inside them will. The stop is at the limit of what the current instruments distinguish; the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity." A model that reproduces the correlation is not thereby the mechanism.

One live strain is recorded, not hidden: control theory supplies a close analogue of the cycle but imports a designed set point, whereas this system has no external controller (Fig 11's unmaintained mode). The analogue corroborates the shape and does not decide it.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single regulator of galaxy and black hole growth, and it does not claim the last word. It says that regulation requires a returning loop and cannot be a one-way supply chain; that the black hole survives its own use and can act repeatedly, with its effect changing sign depending on which residual is being considered; that the coupling runs through molecular gas several steps out from the accretion event and crosses a lossy boundary between parsec and kiloparsec scales; and that the question is a union of feeding, stellar coupling and halo supply, not one question with one lever. The observed mass correlation is the ground reading that the loop must answer to, not evidence that names the loop by itself. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
