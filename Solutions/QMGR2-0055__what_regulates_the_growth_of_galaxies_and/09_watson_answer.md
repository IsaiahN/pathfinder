## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the null prediction of independent growth and the observed tight, per-object coupling between black hole mass and host properties. Arity: two growth slots plus a habitat (black hole, galaxy gas and stars, halo and cosmic supply). Symmetry: the coupling is ordered and signed, not a symmetric join; the return edge has a sign relative to the residual. Scale: parsecs for the engine, kiloparsecs for the gas and stars, hundreds of kiloparsecs for the halo. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, central engine.** Actors: black hole, accretion disk, radiation, winds, jets, ultra-fast outflows. Currency: accretion rate converted to energy and momentum. Anchor: resolved spectroscopy and X-ray timing. This is a cycle candidate, not a chain (Fig 12 :121).
- **Domain 2, galactic habitat.** Actors: cold molecular gas, stars, supernovae, morphology. Currency: gas mass and star formation rate. Anchor: CO kinematics, scaling relations, matched control samples. This is where the cascade's later steps live (Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 3, halo and cosmic supply.** Actors: dark matter halo, binding energy, mergers, cosmic web. Currency: gas accretion rate and duty cycle. Anchor: clustering, cross-correlations, merger trees. This supplies boundary conditions, not the returning edge (Mountrichas et al. 2026).
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: observers, simulators, readers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground (data), never agreement. Several simulation families share subgrid physics and calibration data, so their agreement is one evidence pool, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
- **Partition or union.** Union. Feeding and feedback, stellar coupling through gas, and halo and morphology supply are overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Enumerate habitat per slot, then characterise the engine's returning loop, then cross the scale membrane and measure R_T, then read the galaxy-scale correlation as the settling ground. Refuse a one-way chain before minting a regulator (Formula step 2, negation is not a bond).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape stands and the usual phrasing does not. Regulation is a signed returning loop between the black hole and an enumerated galactic habitat, not a one-way gas supply. The black hole is a catalyst with residual-relative valence. The coupling cascades through molecular gas and crosses a lossy parsec-to-kiloparsec membrane with a nonzero, unmeasured R_T. The question is a union of feeding and feedback, stellar coupling and halo supply, and the supply-chain and cycle readings are isomers of the same held atoms. Growth is recursive across epochs. The framework does not name the unique regulator; any such name is a binding from the astronomy frame and is recorded as provenance, not derived. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest outreach point is the four pre-registered refuters, not any claim to have found the mechanism.
