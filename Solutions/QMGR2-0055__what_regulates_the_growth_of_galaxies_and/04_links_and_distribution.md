## What the wider literature actually adds

1. **The cycle shape is the field's own framing, arrived at independently.** Ruffa et al. (2026) call the problem "the full AGN feeding-feedback cycle" and argue that the missing instrument is resolved molecular gas mapping over a statistical sample. That is the same shape Fig 12 :121 returns, reached from instrumentation rather than from the figures. One witness, low overlap.
2. **The union is measured, not asserted.** VariableTNG finds stellar feedback indirectly regulates black hole growth through gas supply; Roberts et al. find disk rotational support and merger history gate the fuel; Mountrichas et al. find large-scale environment sets boundary conditions only. These are three conditions, not three competitions. Record them as a union (Fig 9 :54).
3. **The parsec to kiloparsec gap is an open measurement.** Ruffa et al. say the current picture rests on "a limited number of exceptionally nearby systems." That is R_T asserted by an observational team, not a slogan from the framework.
4. **The simulation pool is partly shared.** VariableTNG, EAGLE-based work and ARTEMIS-based work draw on related subgrid families and are calibrated against some of the same local relations. Count their agreement once (Fig 2 :40). The X-ray survey result (Igo et al.) uses a different instrument and different inference, so it is the lowest-overlap astronomy witness in the set.
5. **The outside analogue is genuinely low overlap.** Control theory and cybernetics arrive at the same circuit structure and at the same warning that multiple loops cannot be shoehorned into one sign. It corroborates the shape; it does not decide it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" Do not send to a list selected for likely agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure and record that answer first; (3) only then the local figure text, the formula and the two arXiv links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
