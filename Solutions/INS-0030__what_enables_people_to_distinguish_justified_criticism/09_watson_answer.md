## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what enables an observer to sort a negative claim about an institution into one of two generations: a claim tracking the institution's actual failure against a standard it is answerable to (justified criticism), or a claim aimed at reducing its standing regardless of that failure (delegitimation). Option A: sort by surface marks such as tone, identity of the speaker, volume, or agreement. Option B: sort by contact with a non-updating ground, with independent frames, a declared stake in the reading, and valence relative to the specific residual. What each leads to: A makes agreement the anchor and fails the figure's correlation rule; B returns a refusable four-property shape with one honest operational gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The unobserved generation of the claim. Arity: one claim, two candidate generations, one observer, one institution, one or more grounds. Symmetry: the A/B comparison is an ordered threshold relation, not a symmetric join (Operators table :48, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). Scale: individual judgment and public judgment, related by a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :19). It is a union compound when a single document mixes a grounded claim and a standing attack (Fig 9 :55).
- **Domain 1, claim to ground.** Actors: critic, claim, institution, ground (measurement, outcome, declared standard, counter). Currency: contact with the ground, a residual on a slot. Anchor: a ground that does not move when frames push on it (Fig 2 :29-30). Question type: partition. Verdict: triangulated.
- **Domain 2, observer and frames.** Actors: observer frame, peer frames, evidence pools. Currency: independence and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :45). Question type: partition by overlap. Verdict: triangulated with declared moderate overlap.
- **Domain 3, institution as seat and channel.** Actors: the institution as maintainer of its own metrics and conventions, its standing. Currency: conventions, reach, stake. Anchor: the ground kept reachable (Fig 10 :29). Question type: seat test. Verdict: triangulated for the channel, `specified` for the conflict-of-interest case test.
- **Domain 4, valence and context.** Actors: critics, claims, conditions and relations in the habitat. Currency: relation to this residual. Anchor: the specific residual, re-described per case (Fig 11 :51). Question type: relation, not kind. Verdict: triangulated.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); check the anchor before the frames; keep the channel reachable and the stake declared; only then sort the claim. Unbundle unions before scoring (Fig 9 :54).

Strain test applied: the attempted mapping of delegitimation to negation was retracted (refusal is a legitimate term, Fig 5 :53; negation is unary, Operators :70-74). The blanket reading of Fig 10 :93 was narrowed to a case test. Both are recorded in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5b. No live strain remains after re-census.

## WORLD (secondary, each source opened or read via the page extract; corroboration only)

- **Cognitive science of communication.** Sperber, Clément, Heintz, Mascaro, Mercier, Origgi and Wilson, *Epistemic Vigilance*, Mind & Language 25(4), 2010. Quote: "vigilance can be directed at the source of communicated information: Is the communicator competent and honest? It can also be directed at the content of communication". Prediction: people sort claims by source interest and content consistency, not by headcount. **Corroborates** the two-part discriminator and the refusal of crowd agreement. Overlap: different author and field from the figures, low.
- **Cognitive psychology.** Gierth and Bromme, *Beware of vested interests: Epistemic vigilance improves reasoning about scientific evidence (for some people)*, PLoS ONE 15(4), 2020. Quote: "when people expect a non-trustworthy or non-expert source to be wrong, they scrutinize the claims made by that source more thoroughly and are thus able to identify logical inconsistencies." Prediction: a detected conflict of interest triggers falsification of the claim. **Corroborates** the stake test, with a caution that the effect was partial and source-specific. Opened in full.
- **Political science.** Easton, *A Re-assessment of the Concept of Political Support*, British Journal of Political Science 5(4), 1975. Quote: "These are the kinds of practical political problems to which the concept of political support, as found in systems analysis, has been directed." Prediction: performance dissatisfaction and institutional illegitimacy have a long formal separation. **Corroborates** the two-condition split. Opened in full.
- **Political science, same Easton pool.** Driscoll and Nelson, *There is No Legitimacy Crisis: Support for Judicial Institutions in Modern Latin America*, 2017. Quote: "specific support refers to performance satisfaction. Easton (1965) suggests that specific support is essentially 'a quid pro quo for the fulfillment of demands'". Prediction: justified criticism targets outputs; delegitimation targets the reservoir of goodwill. **Corroborates** the anchor test and the unbundling rule. Overlap: high with the previous source, so these two count as one witness.
- **Animal behaviour.** Searcy and Nowicki, *Animal Signals: Reliability and Deception in Signaling Systems*, Princeton University Press, 2005. Quote: "Reliability requires that there be a correlation between some characteristic of the signal and some attribute of the signaler or its environment that the receiver benefits from knowing about". Prediction: a reliable critical claim stays correlated with a ground attribute. **Corroborates** the relation-based reading of reliability at very low overlap.
- **Animal behaviour, competing result.** Penn and Számadó, *The Handicap Principle: how an erroneous hypothesis became a scientific principle*, Biological Reviews 95(1), 2020. Quote: "Honest or reliable signalling is particularly puzzling when there are conflicts of interest between senders versus receivers, and deception is feasible and potentially beneficial". Prediction: reliability comes from differential trade-offs and the cost of cheating, not from a signal's loudness. **Conflicts** with the naive "costly equals honest" mapping, which is not a figure claim; the conflict was sent back to the census and produced the S1/S4 retractions. - **Organizational research.** Edmondson and Kerrissey, *What People Get Wrong About Psychological Safety*, Harvard Business Review, May-June 2025; APA workplace guidance, 2023. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The discriminator is a four-property shape, not a verdict on a case: a non-updating anchor (domain 1), low-overlap independent frames (domain 2), a reachable channel with a declared stake in the reading (domain 3), and valence relative to the specific residual rather than to a standing roster (domain 4). Mixed documents are unbundled and scored in separate loops, never averaged. The speaker's honesty, the volume of agreement, and the identity of the institution are not discriminators. This is a `general` shape with `specified` domain predictions. It is `candidate`, not `grounded`. The operational instrument for measuring a person's ability to apply it is an honest `gap` (F7), with a candidate import from cognitive science recorded as an import, not as a figure-derived atom.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The four properties and five refuters are written down before any case, so a failure is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is treating pooled agreement or a prestigious roster as an anchor, which Fig 2 :40 disqualifies and Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refutable shape.
- **undoable:** yes. Each property can be retracted independently without disturbing the others, and nothing in this folder is sent to a live institution. A refutation is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminating instrument that returns a residual on real criticisms against an independently specified standard would move F7 from gap to grounded; an anchor that updates under a measured push and still sorts correctly, a high-overlap pool counted as corroboration, a roster that survives a change of residual, a saturated metric treated as ground, or a sort that works with no change in contact would each break a property. Prestige and endorsement are not such instruments.
