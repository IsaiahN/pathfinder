**Conflict rule applied.** Penn and Számadó refuse the simple costly-signal story. That conflict did not flip any verdict; it sent the reading back to the census and produced the S1/S4 retractions in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5b (speaker honesty is not a property carried by the speaker; agreement is not an anchor).

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**  -  *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the discriminator and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42**  -  *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**  -  *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the discriminator were true before giving them the source.
- **Fig 2 :40**  -  pooled agreement is disqualifying for a verdict. A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not a check.
- **Fig 7 :35**  -  *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four-property discriminator; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record it; (3) only if curious, the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method with a user-written refuter and a stated mode.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*). Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Stage 3 sends a method, which Fig 4 :22 permits, but each user must state their own ground and refuter because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, Direction).

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 are live, and F7 names an operational-instrument gap. Say so in stage 1 rather than discovering it in a reply.
