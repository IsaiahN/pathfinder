## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the final instance. It constrains; it does not fill. Applied here:

- Shape: *a discriminator with four properties, each refusable, each with a nameable anchor.*
- Filler would be: *the name of the correct critic, a verdict on a live institution, a score for a person's character.*

The first is what this folder offers. The second and third are refused.

**Important correction to the exemplar's overstatement.** The phrase is "a **missing** thing would have to look like." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The four properties here are not missing primitives; they are bound from the figure text itself (anchor, independence, channel, valence). Naming them is therefore allowed. What remains forbidden is naming the final instance (the correct critic or the live verdict). Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 2. How this shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session, on vocabulary bootstrapping: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The question itself is the tether: *"This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Hence §0 of `01` states the residual in effects (generations of a claim, not motives of a critic).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the outreach artifact leads with checkable predictions rather than with the architecture.
- On stopping: *Library of Babel* says *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the four-property discriminator with a live gap at F7, not a final verdict.

## 3. The essay that bears most directly on this question

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session, is effectively an essay about the failure this question asks about. Three passages are decisive.

> **"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."**

> **"The reference point both sides check against has to stay put. If it moves, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"**

> **"Deception runs on substitution: a false context, a false pretext, a false subtext."**

Mapping to the four properties:

- The first two passages are F1 (a ground that does not update) and F2 (independent frames). A public that only checks against itself, or an institution that only checks against its own press office, drifts coherently and cannot see it.
- The third passage is the mechanism of delegitimation as the framework would describe it: not the fabrication of a new fact, but the substitution of a standing frame for the ground. A false context stages the quote; a false pretext supplies a cover story; a false subtext insinuates what is not asserted. The readable text stays the same; what changes is what the reader is pointed at.

The essay also names the three drift-stoppers, which line up exactly with the figure-derived verdicts:

1. *"A ground that both are corrected by, separately."* (F1 and Fig 2 :45-46)
2. *"Separate experience."* (F2 and Fig 2 :31-32)
3. *"Claims in effect terms."* (F5 and Fig 9 :30-31)

This is corroboration from a low-overlap reading in the sense that the essay is a philosophical treatment of context rather than a study of institutions. It still shares an author with the figures, so its agreement is expected and carries little independent weight (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. What licenses an instance

Only two things:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question: an outcome or measurement shows whether the criticism tracked the standard, on a named slot, at a named scale.
2. **A provenanced import with a shadow test** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). For this question: the psycho-logical construct of epistemic vigilance (source vigilance plus content vigilance) is an import from cognitive science. It must be recorded as coming from that frame, with a source gate and a shadow test, not presented as a figure-derived atom. `09_watson_answer.md` does this; `10_source_ledger.csv` records provenance.

Until one of those lands, any claim to have identified a specific "correct critic" is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 5. The metamerism warning applied here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the discriminator's hardest case:

> **"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."**

And on the instrument: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The essay's resolution is historical: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied to this question: a critical claim and a standing attack can produce the same observable text, the same number of citations, the same tone, even the same dataset. A reader who sorts them by those observables is reading a projection and naming the source. That is precisely F7's gap: what is owed is a discriminating instrument, not another argument. The cognitive-science import (source vigilance and content vigilance) is a candidate proto-instrument, not a finished one.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this document

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with the discriminator is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried, never to endorse it. The same essay adds: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."*

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the discriminator, and it cannot settle the question it reports on. The honest label is on every substantive file.
