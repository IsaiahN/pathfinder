- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: readers, challenges. Currency: residuals and scores. Anchor: withheld data, never agreement.
- **Union, not partition** (Fig 9 :54-56): effect condition and objective condition overlap. **Order:** perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, carry (Fig 3 :20).
