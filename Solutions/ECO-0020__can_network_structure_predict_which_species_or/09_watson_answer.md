## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap, per object slot, between a structural priority prediction and what actually maintains the declared target when the system is perturbed. Arity: many slots, so per-slot indexing and never a global average (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: priority is a comparison and a goal, not a symmetric join; a node's valence changes with the target (Fig 11 :49-50). Scale: first order direct partners, second order neighbours of neighbours, third order cascades (Fig 11 :52-53). Fig 9 names it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, structural-pattern frame.** Actors: species as nodes, interactions as edges, degree, closeness, betweenness, modules, nestedness, motifs. Currency: topology and composition inside `closure(Gamma_structure)`. Anchor: the recorded interaction dataset, which can settle what pattern is present but not what to protect. No conservation-outcome anchor lives here, so this frame alone cannot settle priority.
- **Domain 2, dynamics-and-ground frame.** Actors: abundances, interaction strengths, removals, disturbances, traits, time series, withheld forecast targets. Currency: persistence and function. Anchor: a measured outcome that does not move when a frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). This is the ground for priority.
- **Domain 3, objective-and-decision frame.** Actors: the declared goal, managers, rightsholders. Currency: the stated objective. Anchor: the goal itself, declared in advance. It selects which residual Domain 2 must settle.
- **Domain 4, verification-and-outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers, forecasting challenges, reviewers. Currency: residuals and scored forecasts. Anchor: withheld ground data, never agreement.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (build the network) then vocabulary (terms for what nodes afford) then objective then planning then learn and carry. The objective is the link that can break unseen and is most often assumed rather than measured.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Priority is the overlap of a large-effect condition and a declared-objective condition. A single lever clears one region and leaves the intersection standing (Fig 9 :54-56). Unbundle before searching.

Strain test applied: the six-term structure rule governs what a network is; the objective link governs which comparison is being made. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. The ecology sources are one citation pool, so they are one witness, not three (Fig 2 :40).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Qualified affirm with a split.** Network structure predicts a shape, not a verdict. It gives the habitat of contact, a per-slot candidate list, a first-order map of who can affect whom, and a budget ordering. It does not predict which species or interaction to prioritize until a conservation objective is declared (Domain 3) and a ground reading settles which candidate survives (Domain 2). The decisive line is Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The exact goal bond type remains `single-source` from the Operators table. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. No species was named and no measurement was taken.

- **cost if wrong:** two costs. The ecological cost is a budget spent on structurally central nodes that do not maintain the declared function, with cascading loss that Fig 11 :52-53 shows can be several steps out. The figure cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a species list before the residual and refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both are avoidable by declaring the objective first and leading with the five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A priority ranking is revisable as ground data arrive, which is why Fig 11 :63 says "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." The defective form (a structural list presented as a verdict) is removable without touching the shape. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A removal or manipulation experiment showing the top centrality node has no effect on the target while a low-degree node does; one structural ranking settled for two different declared objectives; a structure with no states that carries priority magnitude; a proof that the two conditions are a partition; or a demonstration that all effects are first order. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate. A public forecasting challenge that submits a structural-priority forecast before target release is a practical instrument for firing it.
