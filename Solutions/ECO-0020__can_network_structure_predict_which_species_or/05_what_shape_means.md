## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, the **shape** is: a per-slot, objective-relative, multi-order candidate ranking whose top entries survive a ground settlement. The **name** would be a list of species, for example "protect the top-10 betweenness nodes in this food web." The framework can constrain the former; it cannot produce the latter without D3 and D2 from `01`.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the bootstrapped vocabulary is: slot, objective, cascade depth, layer, ground.
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the residual as "which node's loss changes the target function", not as "which node is structurally central."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (mapped from exemplar): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." For conservation this maps to adaptive management: the answer is never a final list, because the goal and the habitat both move (Fig 11 :63: "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state.").

## 3. What licenses a name (a species, an interaction)

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 2 :29 defines the anchor as "a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For conservation: a removal experiment, a monitored persistence outcome, a forecast scored after the target is revealed.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45), with a source gate and a shadow test. Demographic data, trait data, and interaction strengths are imports from the dynamics frame into the structural frame. Each must be recorded as an import: Fig 8 :50-52 says convergent derivation and adopted import "look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*They're Scanning Books* (mapped from exemplar): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Adding interaction strengths to a binary graph is a new dimension, not a new centrality formula. Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." Naming a subset of nodes as "the important ones" makes a choice visible that was previously hidden in the metric.

## 4. The specific forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (mapped from exemplar) gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The error in conservation prioritization is reading a centrality score (the artifact) and naming its source as "importance for persistence." Several different mechanisms give the same centrality: a hub maintained by abundance, a hub maintained by switching, a hub that is an artifact of aggregated links. Vasas 2009 (abstract read 2026-11-23) records that "the construction of a trophic network is far from trivial and the definition of nodes and links largely rely on the author's possibilities and opinion", and that "Topological keystone indices are increasingly used in ecological studies, but their verification is still missing." Fig 5 :69: "settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."

What is owed is a discriminator, not a better-sounding name. The forecasting challenges are an institutional version of the spectrometer: submit before the data exist, then let the withheld data decide.

## 5. Question answered: what does "shape" license here?

**Nothing about which species.** It licenses the constraints in §1 and the four refuter classes in `01` §5. A particular species is licensed only by (a) a declared objective and (b) a ground reading that pays for the binding, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the species name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the usual outreach answer commit the forbidden error?** Yes, when it presents a centrality list as a priority list. It names the source of a reading (centrality -> importance) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The correct form is `OBJECTIVE(???)PRIORITY`, with the slot open until D2 settles it.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3

I first wrote that a structural ranking could never be *called* a priority. That overstates the rule. Fig 11 :49-51 does not ban naming; it says the name is relative to a declared residual. A structural ranking **is** a legitimate name for the structural objective ("which node most affects connectivity"), because that objective is constitutively about the graph. What fails is exporting that name to a different objective ("which node most affects persistence of function F") without a new ground reading. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 2 :36-37 on legitimate anchors + the two essays opened this session).

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers respected ecologists to agree with a priority list is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, declare the objective, and let a removal experiment or a withheld-data forecast settle it. The public venues in `03` are chosen as opt-in routes to a ground, not as sources of endorsement.
