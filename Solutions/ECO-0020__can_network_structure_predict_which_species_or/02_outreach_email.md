# Outreach draft: check the output before deciding about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until you have stated your own objective and your own refuter alongside the shape claim. An output that cannot be refused collapses the two-stage offer on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about network structure and conservation priority

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an ecological network is a *structure*, one of the six kinds the framework assumes. The figures say "A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes" (Fig 13 :29-30). So a centrality ranking is a property of an arrangement, not a priority verdict. It can rank candidates for a budget, but the verdict of which species or interaction to protect depends on the objective you are trying to preserve. The framework puts it this way: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" (Fig 11 :51). The same node is a benefactor for one function and a hazard for another.
>
> **What it predicts.** Network structure gives you the habitat of contact, a per-species candidate list, and a first-order map of who can affect whom. It does not give you the final priority. That requires (1) a declared objective, (2) enumeration outward until the cascade stops mattering, because "the residual that mattered was three steps out" in the classic DDT case (Fig 11 :52-53), and (3) a ground reading that settles the candidate. The figures separate these: "the habitat is not the ground... the ground is what settles, and it does not" (Fig 11 :73). Centrality is "a filter for the budget", not "a witness for the verdict" (Fig 9 :60).
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular species is *the* priority; that centrality degree or betweenness is sufficient; that a structural ranking is `grounded`; or that a list of central species survives a change of the conservation goal. I am not asking you to endorse the framework, and I have not named a species.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. A purely structural metric, with no dynamics, demography, traits, or objective data, is settled by the ground as the correct priority for two different declared objectives.
> 2. A removal experiment shows the top node by centrality has no effect on the target function, while a low-degree node does.
> 3. The framework must admit a structure with no states at its nodes that still carries magnitude for a decision.
> 4. The two conditions (large effect, declared objective) are a partition, so one lever clears the whole question.
> 5. All priority-bearing effects are first order, so looking at neighbours of neighbours never changes the ranking.
>
> If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Figures README, canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The formula (eight-step loop and mode rule): local `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run the same discipline on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Stage 2 and stage 3

- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient returns a verdict on stage 1, and only then, offer the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
- **Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method. It is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must state their own ground and their own refuter before running it.
- Never pool a later endorsement as corroboration. Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
