# Expert verification packet: the Tether on network structure and conservation priority

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecological forecasting researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No ecological measurement is claimed here. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An ecological network is a structure, not a state, so a centrality ranking is a description of an arrangement and cannot carry a priority magnitude by itself. Which species or interaction is a priority is relative to a declared conservation objective: no standing list of good actors survives a change of residual. Network structure predicts the shape of the answer, a per-slot candidate list, the habitat of contact, and a map of effects that can extend three steps out; it does not predict the verdict until the objective is declared and a ground reading settles which candidate survives. Centrality is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict. The question is a union of an effect condition and an objective condition, so it must be unbundled before it is searched.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 11

- **Statement:** a network is a structure, not a state; a structure without states at its nodes is empty; priority is relative to the declared residual.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :29-30 - "A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes." Fig 11 :51 - "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 11 :50 - "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."
- **Refuter:** exhibit a structure with no states at its nodes that carries a priority magnitude; or show one purely structural ranking settled as correct for two different declared objectives.

### C2 - ecology-facing; checkable against the keystone and controllability literature

- **Statement:** centrality predicts which extinctions would cause large change better than it predicts how to attain or preserve a particular community state, and layer choice plus cascade depth can change the ranking.
- **What to check:** is that characterization faithful? Cagua, Wootton and Stouffer, *Journal of Ecology* 107 (2019), 1779-1790: "While centrality is useful when predicting which species' extinctions could cause the largest change in a community, it says little about how these species could be used to attain or preserve a particular community state." Dominguez-Garcia and Kefi, *PLoS Computational Biology* 20(1) (2024): in about 12 percent of their tripartite networks the whole-community plant importance ranking was "emergent", similar to neither single-layer ranking. Jordan, *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B* 364 (2009): "key species may be important interactors even if they only have a small number of direct partners in the web."
- **Refuter:** show that a first-order, single-layer centrality ranking is settled by the ground as the correct priority for a declared objective across two different networks with different layer composition, and that enumerating further never changes the order.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 3

- **Statement:** the question is a union, not a partition; the objective is a separate, often uninstrumented dependency link; centrality is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :56 - "A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Fig 9 :60 - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 3 :27 - "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Operators table :51 - "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one."
- **Refuter:** show the two conditions are a partition so one lever clears the whole question; or derive the conservation objective from topology alone, without a declaration from the decision frame.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that network structure is irrelevant. It supplies the habitat census and the budget filter.
- Not that any named species is a priority. No ground reading was run here.
- Not that degree, betweenness, modularity, nestedness or any index is sufficient.
- Not that a structural ranking is `grounded`. It is `candidate` until a measurement pays.
- Not that the question is one question. It is a union and is unbundled in `01` §2.
- Not that a list of central species survives a change of objective. Fig 11 :51 says it does not.
- Not a claim that the six-term set is nature's own list. It is a visible guess.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Five refuters were pre-registered in `01` §5:

1. One structural ranking settled as correct for two different objectives.
2. A removal experiment in which the top centrality node has no effect on the target while a low-degree node does.
3. A structure with no states at its nodes that carries priority magnitude.
4. A proof that the two conditions are a partition.
5. A demonstration that all priority-bearing effects are first order.

The practical instrument already exists in the field: submit a structural-priority forecast before the target is revealed and let withheld data score it. The NEON challenge states that "All forecasts and scores are publicly available through cloud storage and discoverable through our catalog", which is what makes its anchor hard to move.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :29-30, Fig 11 :50-51, Fig 9 :56 and :60, Fig 3 :27. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: does centrality really say little about attaining a particular community state, and can layer choice invert a priority ranking?
3. Name one declared objective for which a centrality ranking would reverse, or say why none exists.
4. Name one instrument that would score a structural-priority forecast against withheld data in your system, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and no agreement will be pooled as corroboration.
