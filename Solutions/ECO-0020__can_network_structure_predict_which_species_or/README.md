## Restating the question as an effect

The question asks whether a description of network structure alone is enough to choose which species or interaction to protect. Two options lead to different consequences.

- **Option A (affirm):** a centrality ranking, module role, or nestedness position is itself a priority verdict. This spends a conservation budget on nodes selected inside the structural frame, with no anchor outside that frame to correct it.
- **Option B (qualified):** network structure yields a habitat census and a ranked set of candidates, and a declared objective plus a ground reading settles which candidate is actually a priority. This keeps the candidate list revisable and the ground in charge.

## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Before deriving, the sought-for shape is written down so it can accrue a residual like anything else. **Priority verdict:** a per-slot ranking of species or interactions that (i) is indexed by object slot, not aggregated, (ii) is relative to a declared conservation objective, (iii) accounts for contact beyond first-order neighbours, and (iv) survives settlement by a measurement that does not move when the structural frame changes. A structural ranking that lacks (ii) or (iv) is a filter for the budget, not a verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Adding agreeable readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: public opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with scraping personal addresses for unsolicited outreach. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
