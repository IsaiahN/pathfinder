## What is deliberately not here

1. No personal emails of named individuals.
2. No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this derivation.
3. No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

- **A room does not upgrade the label.** "A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." (Seat Map :283). A list of people selected for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
- **Correlation decides the verdict's worth.** "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." (Seat Map :163). This is doubly pointed here, because the question under study is how agreement becomes a fact. A campaign that answers it by collecting agreement would enact the failure it claims to diagnose.
- **A checker must not hold the interiors.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

## What this solution can and cannot do

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** The framework's own validation rule applies: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." (Isaiah N, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026-11-19, fetched 2026-11-23). It therefore cannot settle the historical and philosophical question it reports on. It can state the shape a defensible answer must have, name the instruments that would close it, and write the refuter in advance. Historians and philosophers of observation settle the rest.
