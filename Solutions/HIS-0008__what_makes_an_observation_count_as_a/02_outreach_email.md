**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` survives the recipient's own check. The offer collapses on contact if the stage-1 claim does not hold.

**Subject:** What would you count as a failure of this claim about observation?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one checkable claim about what makes an observation a fact rather than an artifact of a method. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`general` shape, `specified` operational consequences, not `grounded`):** a recorded value does not say where it came from. Several different sources can produce the same reading, in color science the same perceived color can come from a single wavelength or from mixtures, and from inside the instrument the case where the instrument is too coarse and the case where there is nothing there look the same. So an observation counts as a fact only when four conditions hold: its reading can be ranged against a world anchor that does not change because of what any observer thinks; a second instrument whose evidence does not overlap the first corroborates it; the channel to that anchor is maintained and its calibration is traceable; and the artifact explanations are actively ruled out by a blind, a control, and a known-positive fixture. An observation counts as an artifact when the reading was generated by the method, when agreement comes from people who read the same report, when the instrument carries the very error it was meant to catch, or when a community convention decides the outcome.
>
> The historical case I would use to check this is Blondlot's N-rays. Hundreds of papers reported the effect. Robert Wood removed the prism in the dark and Blondlot reported the same spectrum. The agreement tracked the method, not the world.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that consensus never matters, that replication is useless, or that I can name the instrument that would settle any particular case. Consensus is a convention and replication under one method is a budget instrument, not a witness. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written down before this message:**
> 1. An observation counts as a fact with no anchor independent of the frames that produced it.
> 2. Two people reading the same report count as two corroborating instruments.
> 3. An instrument demonstrates its own source with no second instrument, control, or frame.
> 4. A convention that decides an outcome still functions as a convention.
> 5. A known-positive fixture establishes specificity as well as sensitivity.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figure text, not my summary, is the authority.

> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md

**Note on AI-assisted drafting.** This packet is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
