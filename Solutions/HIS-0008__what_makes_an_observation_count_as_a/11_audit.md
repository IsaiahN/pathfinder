I redid the census from the residual line alone, before using the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the source of the reading `o′` in `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|`, not the size of `R`. Arity: three-place, frame, method, world source, indexed by slot `s` and by repeat. Symmetry: asymmetric between frame and ground (Fig 2 :33); independence between frames is a second, separate quantity. Scale: per reading and per community habitat, with a lossy transform between them. Union: source discrimination and anchoring are two conditions (Fig 9 :54-57).
- **Domain 1, the single reading.** Actors: observer, belief, instrument, reading, slot. Currency: residual. Anchor: the world at `s`, reached only through a discriminator. Admitted as a candidate whose source is owed.
- **Domain 2, low-overlap triangulation.** Actors: two instruments with differing closures. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground each ranges against separately. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the calibration channel.** Actors: maintainer, instrument, surrogate signal, ground. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, if the surrogate is independently established. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, the community habitat.** Actors: community, conventions, prestige. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none internal; the habitat updates, the ground does not (Fig 11 :73). Admitted as a habitat only.
- **Excluded domains:** the self-authenticating reading, no anchor; expert reputation as fact-maker, no anchor independent of the world.

Order by dependency: perceive, name the artifact, bind the sought-for shape, blind and control, range a differing frame, let the ground decide and record provenance (Fig 3 :20; Fig 5 :61-64; Fig 10 :52).

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a fact needs a non-updating anchor, a low-overlap corroborator, a maintained channel, and blind and control; agreement that decides an outcome is a convention.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Which reference is the legitimate anchor for a given case.** Fig 2 :36-38 requires the anchor to be constitutively about the question and warns that instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. The shorthand says "anchor"; the case needs a named world quantity.
- **Whether the surrogate calibration is independent.** Fig 6 :32-35 and the Collins regress require something already returning a failing residual. The shorthand says "traceable calibration"; that can be satisfied by a surrogate that is not independent (SEP, Collins).
- **The order of refusal before mint.** Formula step 2 owes a refusal of the artifact hypothesis before a new term is admitted; the shorthand lists blind and control as a checkpoint without carrying the ordering.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a reading does not authenticate its source, and corroboration needs a differing closure. That is the residue worth sending.
