**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the operational consequences, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This run synthesizes material already in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `05_what_shape_means.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: the output is only as good as the validator, and a frame cannot validate itself) it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that an observation becomes a fact when it is recorded, repeated, and believed, versus the claim that it becomes a fact only when its source is discriminated and its reading ranges against a non-updating anchor. Option A: treat agreement and method reliability as sufficient. Option B: keep the four-condition shape (source discrimination, non-updating anchor, low-overlap corroboration, maintained channel with blind and control) and refuse the names that the framework cannot derive. What each leads to: A certifies N-rays whenever a community shares a method; B yields a checkable shape with written refuters and an open slot for the instrument that would close a given case.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The question is about the source of `o′` in `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` per slot `s`, not about the size of `R`. Arity: at least three-place, frame, method, world source, indexed by slot and repeat. Symmetry: asymmetric between frame and ground, since the ground does not update on frames; independence between frames is a separate quantity. Scale: per reading and per community habitat, with a lossy transform between. It is a union compound of source discrimination (C1) and anchoring (C2), not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, the single reading.** Actors: observer, belief `b`, instrument `a`, reading `o′`, slot `s`. Currency: residual. Anchor: the world at `s`, reachable only through a discriminator; the bare reading has none. Admitted as a candidate whose source is owed.
- **Domain 2, low-overlap triangulation.** Actors: two instruments with differing closures. Currency: independence. Anchor: the ground each ranges against separately. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, the calibration channel.** Actors: maintainer, instrument, surrogate signal, ground. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, if the surrogate is independently established. Admitted.
- **Domain 4, the community habitat.** Actors: community, conventions, prestige. Currency: agreement. Anchor: none internal; the habitat updates and the ground does not, so the anchor must be external. Admitted as a habitat only.
- **Excluded:** the self-authenticating reading (no independent anchor) and expert reputation as fact-maker (prestige can fund a check, not settle one).
- **Order by dependency:** perceive, name the artifact, bind the shape, blind and control, range a differing frame, let the ground decide and record the lineage.

Strain test applied: an early reading made Fig 11 decide the single-reading case by calling the instrument an agent and the reading a habitat. Re-census confined Fig 11 to Domain 4 (strain S1, `01` section 6). A second strain, treating Fig 13's six physical terms as a taxonomy of instruments, was withdrawn (S2). No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives: an observation counts as a fact when its reading can be ranged against a world anchor that does not move when frames change, when an instrument with a differing evidence base corroborates it, when the channel to the anchor is maintained with traceable calibration, and when artifact hypotheses are actively discriminated by a blind, a control, and a known-positive fixture. It counts as an artifact when the reading is generated frame-internally, when agreement comes from a shared evidence pool, when the instrument carries the defect it was built to catch, or when a convention decides the outcome. Which instrument closes a given case is a `gap`. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the consequences, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, refuters, and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is certifying an artifact as a fact, which is what N-rays were while hundreds of papers agreed. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by asking for a differing instrument and a blind rather than an endorsement.
- **undoable:** yes. A claim certified as a fact can be withdrawn when a source-discriminating instrument or a blind shows the reading was method-generated; N-rays were withdrawn in 1904. No new primitive is required to undo this derivation, and the four conditions can be reapplied to the corrected case. The lineage is kept in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` with the retracted readings visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that shows several world sources giving the same reading on the supposedly closing instrument would break C1. A reference that moves when frames change would break C2. Two readers of one report counting as corroboration, a convention deciding a world outcome, or a known-positive fixture establishing specificity would each fire a standing falsifier. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
