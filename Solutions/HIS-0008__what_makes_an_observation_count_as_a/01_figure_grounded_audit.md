## 0. The residual in effect terms

The loop defines `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` per slot `s` (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`). For this question:

- **b** is what the observer believes is there before observing: priors, background assumptions, training, the method's expected result.
- **a** is the act of observing: pointing the instrument, running the method, attending, recording.
- **Γ(b,a)** is what the observer's method, instrument, and library predict the reading will be.
- **o′** is the reading actually obtained and entered into the record.
- **s** is the object, quantity, or region being tracked. `R` is indexed by `s`, never averaged.

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** (Fig 1 :19)
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** (Fig 1 :22)

The question is not about the size of `R`. It is about the **source** of `o′`. An observation is an artifact when `o′` is generated by `Γ` and `a`, that is, by the method or instrument or observer, rather than by the world at `s`. It counts as a fact when `o′` ranges against something that is not a product of the frame that produced it.

**Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before the search** (Formula, "Direction"). The answer would be a conjunction of two conditions: (C1) a **source discriminator**, an instrument or procedure that separates readings produced by the world from readings produced by the method; and (C2) an **anchor**, a reference that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, against which the reading can be ranged. Until written down and bound, that shape is a preference, predicts nothing, and no check the loop runs can see it (Fig 5 :61-64). It is now bound, so the refuter in section 4 can fire on it.

**Anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The same passage names candidates: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* The world's behavior at `s` is the candidate anchor here. Consensus, reputation, and the instrument's own self-report move when pushed, so they are instruments at best, never anchors for this question.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 Arity, symmetry, scale of R

- **Arity.** The relation "reading `o′` counts as a fact" is at least three-place: (frame that produced it, instrument or method, world source at `s`), indexed further by `s` and by repeat. One reading is underdetermined, because several world sources and several method states can produce the same `o′`. This is the structure of metamerism (section 3, F1).
- **Symmetry.** The relation is asymmetric between frame and ground: the ground does not update on the frame (Fig 2 :33). Among frames, independence is a separate, near-symmetric quantity: *"two instruments pointed at the same thing are"* two frames (Fig 2 :31). Agreement between two observers who read the same report is not this relation at all.
- **Scale.** The reading is per slot (Fig 1 :19). Source discrimination operates at the scale of the individual observation and at the scale of the community that maintains the instrument and the channel to the ground (Fig 10 :24-29). The transform between them is lossy, so `R_T` must be measured rather than assumed (Fig 4 :30).

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

A domain is admitted only if an anchor can be named. Where none can be named, it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor, nameable? | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 The single reading** | observer, belief `b`, instrument `a`, reading `o′`, slot `s` | residual `R = |Γ(b,a) - o′|` | the world's behavior at `s`, if a discriminator can reach it; the bare reading has no anchor of its own | admitted as a candidate whose source is owed |
| **D2 Low-overlap triangulation** | two instruments or observers with differing closures | independence, how little they share evidence | yes: the ground each ranges against separately (Fig 2 :29-32, :45-46) | admitted |
| **D3 The calibration and maintenance channel** | maintainer, instrument, surrogate signal, ground | contact with the ground | yes, but only if the surrogate is independently established; *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24) | admitted |
| **D4 The community habitat** | research community, conventions, standards of proof, prestige | agreement and conventions | no internal anchor: community agreement updates on its members (*"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame"*, Fig 2 :47); the habitat updates, the ground does not (Fig 11 :73) | admitted as a habitat whose anchor must be external to it |
| **X1 The self-authenticating reading** | the reading alone | magnitude | **no** anchor independent of the arrangement that produced it | not a domain yet, excluded |
| **X2 Expert reputation as fact-maker** | the respected observer | prestige | no anchor; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action."* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-19) | excluded from the verdict, kept as contact cost only |

X1 is the exact error the question asks about: a reading treated as its own source. Its exclusion is structural, not a claim that observations are never facts. X2 is excluded because this question is constitutively about the world at `s`, not about a person (Fig 2 :36-38).

### 1.3 Partition or union

D1 and D3 overlap: the instrument's state bears on both the reading and its calibration, and that overlap is a compound with its own loop, named in the seat map as the observer's defect (Seat Map :275). D2 and D4 overlap: the community both maintains the instruments and supplies the conventions that decide what counts, so that overlap is a second compound. The whole question is a **union**, not a partition.

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** (Fig 9 :54)
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** (Fig 9 :55)
> **"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."** (Fig 9 :57)

The unbundled conditions are C1 (source discrimination: did this reading come from the world or from the method?) and C2 (anchoring: does the reading range against something that does not move when frames change?). An observation failing C1, such as Blondlot's N-rays, and an observation failing C2, such as a claim whose only support is community agreement, are not different degrees of one failure. They need different instruments.

### 1.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

> **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."** (Fig 3 :20)
> **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."** (Fig 3 :27)
> **"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."** (Fig 3 :29)

Order for this question: 1 perception (does anything register at all?) → 2 vocabulary (does the frame have terms for what the instrument affords, including terms for artifact?) → 3 objective (can it say what would count as the reading being a fact, that is, can it bind C1 and C2?) → 4 planning (design the blind, the control, the differing instrument) → 5 learn and carry (calibrate, record provenance, carry the method up). The middle link is where this question usually breaks: a vocabulary of readings can say what was seen, and cannot say whether it was a fact (Fig 12 :111; Seat Map :184). Most effort lands above the break because that is where the instruments already exist (Fig 3 :29-30).

## 3. Findings

Mode tag on each: `general` if structural, `specified` if a falsifiable operational consequence, `grounded` only if settled by a measurement here. None is `grounded`.

### F1 — A reading cannot comment on its own source

**Mode: `general`. Domains D1, D3. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 plus an independent color-science source, low overlap).**

> **"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."** (Fig 6 :26)
> **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** (Fig 6 :27)
> **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"** (Fig 9 :30) and **"rather than the cause"** (Fig 9 :31)

A recorded value does not encode which world source produced it. Isaiah N's source essay puts the mechanism directly: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The independent color-science corroboration is exact: in colorimetry, *"different combinations of light across all wavelengths can produce an equivalent receptor response and the same tristimulus values or color sensation"* (Wikipedia, *Metamerism (color)*, fetched 2026-11-23). Same reading, several sources. The essay's spectrometer is the point: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The figure consequence is that a reading alone leaves two possibilities, depth (the instrument is not fine enough) and absence (there is nothing there), and from inside the frame they are indistinguishable (Fig 6 :27). That is exactly the fact-or-artifact residual.

**Refuter:** an instrument shown to discriminate its own source without any change of instrument, control, or frame, in a case where several distinct sources demonstrably produce the same reading. That breaks metamerism and Fig 6 :27 together.

### F2 — Fact-hood requires an anchor that does not update on frames

**Mode: `general` structural, `specified` for the operational test. Domains D1, D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 plus the N-ray history, low overlap).**

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** (Fig 2 :29)
> **"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."** (Fig 2 :29-30)
> **"The anchor must not update."** (Fig 2 :33)
> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** (Fig 2 :35)
> **"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."** (Fig 2 :45)
> **"Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."** (Fig 2 :46)

An anchor is legitimate only when the question is constitutively about it: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* (Fig 2 :36). This question is about the world at `s`, so a world measurement is legitimate and human judgment is an instrument here, not the anchor. Fig 2 :37-38 warns that *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* N-rays are the historical instance: *"Not knowing the prism had been removed, Blondlot continued to insist he saw the very same pattern he had claimed to see when the prism was in place."* (APS News, 2007, on the 1904 event). The reading tracked the observer's expectation, not the world.

**Refuter:** a case in which an observation counts as a fact solely because every frame agrees, with no reference that any frame can fail to match. That breaks Fig 2 :29, :33, :35.

### F3 — Independence, not number of observers, is what makes agreement bear

**Mode: `general`. Domains D2, D4. Status: triangulated (Fig 2 plus a philosophy-of-science source, low overlap).**

> **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."** (Fig 2 :31)
> **"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."** (Fig 2 :31-32)
> **"Independence is what makes agreement mean something."** (Fig 2 :32)
> **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** (Fig 2 :40)
> **"the collapse is the same arithmetic read as a warning."** (Fig 2 :44)

The Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy gives the convergent case independently: Replicability is where *"experimental procedures differ to produce the same experimental result"*, and *"multiple experiments might obtain the result 'a fluid of type f has a boiling point b', despite having different kinds of thermometers"* (SEP, *Reproducibility of Scientific Results*, first published 2018, fetched 2026-11-23, reporting Radder). Different thermometers, same result, is Fig 2 :31-32 instantiated. Conversely, the N-ray episode produced *"Hundreds of papers"* and *"26 papers by Blondlot himself"* (APS News, 2007), all drawing on the same weak visual method. Agreement compounded without evidence.

**Refuter:** two observers drawing on the same report, the same instrument, and the same prior shown to corroborate a claim about the world in the Fig 2 sense. That breaks Fig 2 :31-32 and :40.

### F4 — The instrument is improved from a worse instrument already returning something

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D3. Status: triangulated (Fig 6 plus the Collins/SEP debate, low overlap).**

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** (Fig 6 :32)
> **"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."** (Fig 6 :33)
> **"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."** (Fig 6 :34-35)

So the discriminator for an artifact claim cannot be specified from a description of the artifact. Something must already be returning a residual that fails to resolve. In N-rays, that something was the unexplained inconsistency in Blondlot's own setup; MIT Libraries records that *"differences among the photographs were caused by inconsistencies in photographic emulsions, exposure time, and development of the film."* (MIT Libraries, 2011). In color, it was the eye's failure to separate sources, resolved by the spectrometer.

The SEP states the obverse as the experimenter's regress. Collins's question is *"To know an experiment has been well conducted, one needs to know whether it gives rise to the correct outcome. But to know what the correct outcome is, one needs to do a well-conducted experiment."* (SEP, quoting Collins 2016 :66). Its own summary is that *"calibrating an experimental apparatus by using a surrogate signal cannot provide an independent reason for considering the apparatus to be reliable."* (SEP, on Collins). Harry Collins states the circle in his own words: *"there are no direct measures of the proper execution of a skill except getting the right result. But in a scientific controversy the notion of 'right result' is itself the subject of dispute."* (Collins, *Paper 1*, Cardiff University site, originally *Sociology* 9(2), 1975). This is a genuine conflict with the figure requirement of a non-updating anchor, and section 6 sends it back to the census rather than softening the verdict.

**Refuter:** a source-discriminating instrument built directly from a description, with no prior instrument already returning a failing residual, that nonetheless separates the sources. That breaks Fig 6 :32-35.

### F5 — The observer's defect: the instrument can carry the artifact it was built to catch

**Mode: `specified`. Domains D1, D3. Status: triangulated (Seat Map plus two independent historical sources, low overlap).**

> **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"** (Fig 10 :24)
> **"saturated metrics, pooled readings"** (Fig 10 :26)
> **"you have lost touch"** (Fig 10 :28)
> **"keep the anchor reachable"** (Fig 10 :29)

The seat map names the failure precisely: *"The observer's defect. The instrument itself carrying the genus it was built to catch. A new instrument's first output is a claim about the instrument, not about the system, and a known-positive fixture proves sensitivity, never specificity. Both are required."* (Seat Map :275). Blondlot's eye, dark room, and phosphorescent screen formed an instrument whose output was a claim about the observer. The Institute of Physics records that *"Blondlot's observations were caused by psycho-physiological effects"* and that the episode is *"often cited as a demonstration of the dangers of experimenter bias."* (IOPSpark, *Blondlot's N-rays*). The named remedy in that history is blind measurement: the French account records that the affair *"a conduit à la pratique renforcée des mesures à l'aveugle"*, led to strengthened blind measurement (AfAS, Pierre Potier, 2025). In survey terms, the artifact case is exactly Schmidt's Function 2: *"an artifact of a faulty thermometer rather than an actual change in a substance's temperature."* (SEP, 2018).

**Refuter:** a known-positive fixture shown to establish specificity as well as sensitivity for a given instrument, with no change of observer, control, or frame. That breaks the observer's-defect rule and Seat Map :275.

### F6 — Agreement that decides an outcome has stopped being evidence

**Mode: `general` with a `specified` methodological consequence. Domains D4, D2. Status: triangulated (Fig 7, Fig 2, Fig 10, plus the N-ray history).**

> **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"** (Fig 7 :13)
> **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** (Fig 7 :35)
> **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"** (Fig 2 :41)
> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"** (Fig 10 :47)
> **"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"** (Fig 1 :26) and **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** (Fig 1 :27)

A community can legitimately agree on what a word means; that is a convention and the product of the arrangement. It cannot turn that agreement into a fact about the world without a ground vote. The N-ray community had the vocabulary, the journals, the prizes, and hundreds of agreeing reports, and still had an artifact. The historical consequence is recorded: *"Blondlot received the Academy's prestigious Le Conte Prize"* while the N-ray citation was reduced to *"hedged"* lines after the collapse (Ashmore 1993, reporting Nye 1980). Prestige tracked the convention, not the ground.

**Refuter:** a convention shown to settle a world question while remaining a convention, that is, while no frame is corrected by anything outside the agreeing set. That breaks Fig 10 :47 and Fig 2 :35.

### F7 — The break is usually at vocabulary and objective, not at perception

**Mode: `specified`. Domain D4, D1. Status: single-source (Fig 3, with Fig 12 :111 as a corroborating line from the same framework, so one witness).**

> **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."** (Fig 3 :19)
> **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."** (Fig 12 :111)

An instrument can register a reading perfectly and still have no term for "this reading was produced by my method." The N-ray observers had acute vision and no term for expectation-driven brightness. The operational consequence: the highest-value question about a disputed observation is not "was it seen?" but "at which link does its chain stop, and was that measured?" (Fig 3 :29).

**Refuter:** a documented disputed observation in which perception and vocabulary and objective all measure as working and the failure is located at planning or at carrying. That falsifies the "usually at the middle" claim, though not the dependency ordering.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the design-instrument rule: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* (Seat Map :287). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following is observed:

1. An observation counts as a fact with no anchor independent of the frames that produced it (breaks Fig 2 :29, :33, :35).
2. Two observers drawing on the same evidence produce a corroboration about the world in the Fig 2 sense (breaks Fig 2 :31-32, :40).
3. An instrument's output authenticates its own source without a second instrument, control, or frame, in a case where several sources give the same reading (breaks F1, Fig 6 :27).
4. A convention that decides an outcome still functions as a convention (breaks Fig 10 :47).
5. A known-positive fixture establishes specificity as well as sensitivity for the instrument that produced it (breaks Seat Map :275).
6. A source-discriminating instrument is built from a description alone, with no worse instrument already returning a failing residual (breaks Fig 6 :32-35).

No part of this solution may be upgraded to `grounded` until one of these has been run and has failed to fire, and until a world anchor has paid for the shape in a specific case.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not with a theory of evidence (Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*).
2. Keep C1 (source discrimination) and C2 (anchoring) separate in every report. A blended "confidence score" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and destroys the diagnostic.
3. Ask at each step which link the chain stops at, and whether that was measured (Fig 3 :29). Do not fund above the break.
4. Treat counts of agreeing observers as a budget filter, never a witness. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
5. Require a differing closure for corroboration: a different instrument, a blind, a control, a second method (Fig 2 :31-32). Two readings of the same report are one witness wearing two names.
6. Use the history of science as a low-overlap witness, not as the verdict. The N-ray case, color metamerism, and the Collins debate are three fields with different evidence pools, so their agreement is weak but not circular.
7. Stop at the shape. Do not name which instrument closes a given case; the framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).

## 7. Recursive derivation summary

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :54, :55, :57 plus *The Library of Babel*'s bundle passage: it is a union of source discrimination and anchoring, each with its own instrument.
2. **What is the anchor?** The world's behavior at `s`, legitimately, because the question is constitutively about it (Fig 2 :29, :36). Not consensus, not reputation, not the instrument's self-report (Fig 1 :27).
3. **Can the reading authenticate itself?** No. Several sources give the same reading (Fig 6 :26-27; metamerism). What is owed is a discriminator.
4. **Where does a discriminator come from?** From a worse instrument already returning a failing residual (Fig 6 :32-35), not from a description. Calibration by a surrogate alone is not independent (SEP, Collins).
5. **How does corroboration work?** Two frames with differing closures range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :31-32, :45-46). Different thermometers, same boiling point, is the convergent case (SEP, Radder).
6. **What does a crowd add?** Vocabulary and convention, not an anchor (Fig 7 :13, :35; Fig 10 :47). N-rays are the standing example.
7. **What can be said at the stop?** A shape: a non-updating anchor, low-overlap instruments, a maintained channel, blind and control, and traceable provenance. Not which instrument for a given case, and not a `grounded` verdict. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The shape survives: **an observation counts as a fact, rather than an artifact of a method or instrument, when its reading can be ranged against a world anchor that does not move when frames change, when the reading is corroborated by an instrument whose closure differs from the first one's, when the channel to that anchor is maintained and its calibration is traceable by provenance, and when the artifact hypotheses are actively discriminated by blind, control, and a known-positive fixture. It counts as an artifact when the reading is generated frame-internally, when agreement comes from a shared evidence pool, when the instrument carries the defect it was built to catch, or when a convention decides the outcome.**

The two conditions C1 and C2 are kept separate and never averaged. Which specific instrument closes a given historical case is a `gap`, not a silence: the framework returns a shape and the instrument that would name the source is not held here. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for its operational consequences. No world source flipped a verdict; the one genuine conflict (Collins) was returned to the census and re-mapped to the convention domain. No unresolved strain remains after re-census.
