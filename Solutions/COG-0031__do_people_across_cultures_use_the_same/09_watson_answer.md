## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, where the variation lives. Option A: the basic forms of reasoning differ between cultures (strong relativity). Option B: one set of forms is shared and cultures differ only in the problems and contents applied to it. Option C: a union of shared forms and locally instituted forms, with the overlap as its own compound. What each leads to: A leaves no shared kernel to search for; B predicts an invariant kernel plus a content map; C requires an unbundled, per-domain search with a separate ground for the overlap. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0 before the world search, so it could accrue a residual like anything else.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures give the **shape** and the **evidentiary rule**, and they do not settle the empirical yes or no. The shape: an invariant loop instantiated only in agent-and-habitat pairs, with variation in actors, currency and anchor; human judgment as the anchor and tests as fallible instruments; low-overlap convergent derivation as the only corroboration of a shared form; no averaging across cultures. The question is a union compound and must be unbundled. The framework returns a shape, not a name, so it cannot say which forms are universal. The empirical verdict is `gap` after a genuine re-census: **unreached, not unreachable**. Mode `specified`, candidate, not grounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, three checkable sub-claims and four refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before a residual, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter. A second cost is importing the six-term substrate into cognition (audit F7), which would put a name where the framework returns a shape.
- **undoable:** yes. The strained applications are removable without touching the core derivation: Fig 4's scale-to-culture move is narrowed by the canonical rule, and the Fig 13 substrate claim is quarantined. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A form/familiarity discriminator that moves with form and not familiarity would let the anchor be read; an independence audit that shows convergent derivation from low-overlap frames, or shows all agreement comes from a shared pool, would move the verdict; a habitat swap that shows a capability transferring with no change in contact would break Fig 11 :40; a demonstration that the kernel/cast distinction fails outside scale would break Fig 4 :19. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate` and the empirical question stands as `gap`.
