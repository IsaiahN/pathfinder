## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :23** - *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if a shared kernel were real before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict."* A bulk list is a room. One-to-one, opt-in channels only.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which frame they would use to test it; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the studies, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record the verdict *before* any theory is shown. Stage 1 is Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure extract, the canonical README, and the studies. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in.** Offer the formula as a reusable method. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"* (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. If a recipient shows that the form/application split is not separable, or that the cited agreement comes from a shared pool, the shape claim is rewritten before any theory is offered.
