## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 4 :19-20 (kernel/cast), Fig 11 :39-40 (agent-and-habitat), Fig 2 :36-38 (anchor), Fig 8 :50-52 (independence), Fig 9 :54-58 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: *"one invariant loop instantiated in agent-and-habitat pairs, with variation in actors, currency and anchor; shared forms corroborated only by low-overlap convergence; empirical yes or no is a gap."*
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question: *"people use the same basic forms of reasoning but apply them to different problems."*

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor condition** (Fig 2 :36-38): the shorthand says "human judgment is the anchor" but drops the condition that this holds only when the question is constitutively about the anchor. Since this question is about people, the condition is met here, but the abridged form would license the anchor everywhere;
- the **independence condition** (Fig 8 :50-52): the shorthand says "low-overlap convergence only" but drops the requirement that the *record* of where each term came from is what separates convergence from import;
- the **agent-and-habitat condition** (Fig 11 :39-40): the shorthand keeps the phrase but a reader can still hear "same form plus different inputs", which is the exact separation the figures refuse;
- the **union/unbundling requirement** (Fig 9 :54-58): the shorthand states the union but does not carry the instruction to split before searching, so it can be mistaken for a single hypothesis.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the kernel/cast distinction with variation in actors, currency and anchor, provided the three conditions above are restored. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the figures are the condensed version of the framework" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
