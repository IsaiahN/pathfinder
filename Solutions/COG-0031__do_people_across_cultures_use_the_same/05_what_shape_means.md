## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched and verified this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupants. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- a **shape** is: *"an invariant loop instantiated in agent-and-habitat pairs, with variation in actors, currency and anchor, and a union of form and application conditions"*;
- a **name** would be: *"analogy is the universal form and category formation is the cultural variable"*, or *"causal attribution is shared and dialecticism is learned."*

The derivation offers the first and explicitly refuses the second. The essay *They're Scanning Books* sharpens the distinction: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Naming a specific form would import a term from the culture-psychology frame and present it as a framework result.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, notice recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* (Verified in the fetched text.)
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Here the effect is "variation in reasoning across cultures"; the cause is not specified by the framework.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the two-stage offer in `02`: send what you would expect to see if a shared kernel were real.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question the ground is human judgment (Fig 2 :37), measured with an instrument that discriminates form from familiarity. Until such a measurement exists, no specific form is licensed as universal.
2. **A provenanced import from a low-overlap frame** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52) with a source gate and a shadow test. A core-knowledge result, for example, is a candidate import from developmental cognitive science. It adds an atom to *our* frame; it does not move the world's wall (Fig 6 :25, *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

*They're Scanning Books*: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* This is why F7 in `01` refuses to map the six onto cognitive primitives: that would turn a visible guess into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the cross-cultural agreement result is not a name

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: two cultures displaying the same reasoning behaviour does not tell us whether they share one form met by different problems, or different forms that produce the same reading. The figure-grounded requirement is a source-discriminating instrument, not more agreement. This is the same error structure as F3 (convergent derivation versus adopted import look identical in the contents).

## 5. Q: does the framework answer the yes/no question? ANSWERED, no

**What licenses the claim "people share basic forms"?** Nothing in the framework. The framework predicts the shape for a domain-agnostic composing system and supplies the rule that only low-overlap convergent derivation corroborates. It cannot say which forms, in which cultures, under which measures. That is for the ground.

**Does the question as asked commit a figure-forbidden error?** Yes, a small one, and it is recorded: the phrase "apply them to different problems" treats the problems as inputs external to the form. Fig 11 :39 says *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* So the correct shape keeps the habitat inside the capability (F5 of `01`). This is a correction to the question, not a refusal to answer it.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 1-3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any reasoning form anywhere. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Where a term is already in the library, naming it is a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive, and what is owed is provenance (Fig 8 :50-52), not silence. What remains wrong in the naive reading is treating such a binding as a framework derivation. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched and verified: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous cross-cultural psychologists assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the cross-cultural question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
