## The question restated in effect terms

What is being decided is *where the variation lives*. The observed differences in reasoning across cultures can be located in one of three places:

- **Option A, strong relativity.** The basic forms themselves differ between cultures. Leads to: no shared kernel to search for, each culture is its own closure, and cross-cultural comparison has no common measure.
- **Option B, universal kernel with variable application.** One set of basic forms is shared; cultures differ in the problems, contents, and anchors to which those forms are applied. Leads to: search for the kernel, and separately map the contents.
- **Option C, union compound.** Some forms are shared and some are instituted by local practice. Leads to: keep per-domain verdicts separate, never average them, and give the overlap its own loop.

An answer would look like a statement of *which slot varies* (form slot or application slot), a description of the shape of the invariant if there is one, and an anchor that would settle the claim against the ground. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term in `01` section 0, so it can accrue a residual like anything else rather than remaining an invisible preference (Formula, "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the question. It cannot.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of people chosen for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that disqualifies their agreement (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A shared evidence pool is one witness.** Fig 2 :40, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Two researchers reading the same review are one frame wearing two names.
3. **Show the residual, not the interiors.** Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame sees the residual and the refuter first, never the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
