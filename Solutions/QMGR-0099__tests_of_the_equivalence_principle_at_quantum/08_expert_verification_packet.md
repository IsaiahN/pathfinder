# Expert verification packet: the Tether on quantum-scale equivalence principle tests

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist or instrument scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape, `candidate` throughout. No measurement is claimed.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "tests of the equivalence principle at quantum scales" is not one hard question but a union of three conditions: A, there is no agreed quantum statement of the principle; B, whether it holds is an instrument question; C, a positive signal has several possible sources. Fig 9 :55-56: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. The Tether does not answer yes or no. It gives the shape any admissible answer must have: unbundle A → B → C before searching; compare two already-settled first-order descriptions at second order, because *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it"* (Fig 12 :104); express the relation at operator or reference-frame level rather than naming one formulation, because *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32); and leave the physical verdict to an instrument. Null η results bound the principle; they do not prove it exact. The status is `unreached, not unreachable`, because atom interferometers already return residuals that fail to resolve.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 3

- **Statement:** the three conditions must be unbundled and searched in the order A → B → C. A precision gain made while A is open optimises a link above the break.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 — *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56 — *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 3 :20 — *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*
- **What to check:** is A genuinely a prerequisite for B? Rosi et al. introduce a quantum formulation of EEP before interpreting their measurement; Cepollaro and Giacomini argue the classical EEP definition is not sufficient for a delocalized particle; Dobkowski et al. leave the QEP definition open. If a definition-free, source-discriminating violation detector exists, the order is wrong.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a signal that is discriminated from fifth forces and systematics with no prior statement of what is being tested. Then B can precede A and this sub-claim fails.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by an experimentalist against the published bounds

- **Statement:** the physical verdict is an anchor question, so no theoretical frame supplies it; all current null results are bounds at a stated sensitivity, never a proof that the EP is exact.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 — *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 8 :39 — *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44 — *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- **What to check:** Rosi et al. report upper limits, not a determination; Tino reviews sensitivities from 10^-7 to the 10^-12 to 10^-14 range across species and isotopes; Dobkowski et al. conclude only that *"in our low energy regime, the equivalence principle may be applied to the quantum domain"*. Altman and Bland, in medical statistics, make the identical error warning: *"all that has been shown is an absence of evidence of a difference"*.
- **Refuter:** a sealed-closure argument showing the instrument space is exhausted, or a ground reading that settles the physical verdict without any instrument. No frame is a sealed room, so the first is expected to fail; it is written down anyway.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 12, 9, 13 and the Operators table

- **Statement:** the answer is second-order over two settled molecules, and the framework returns a shape, not the name of a QEP formulation. Mass-operator equality, quantum reference frame equivalence and test-particle-dependent geometry are bindings, not derivations.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :104 — *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."* Fig 9 :32 — *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133 — *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*. Operators table :48 — *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*.
- **What to check:** GR and QM are each settled in their own domain, so the promotion rule applies. The comparison bond `⋛` is defined as a threshold relation between two quantities; at second order its operands are settled molecules now serving as atoms. Whether such an atom counts as a quantity at that level is **not decided here**. Das et al. show that if the QEP is violated, *"the observed geometry would effectively depend on the properties of the test particle"*, which would require a new anchor for quantum-scale tests. That is a live strain, not a contradiction between figures.
- **Refuter:** a derivation from the six-term set alone that uniquely names one formulation; or a proof that a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom; or a measurement showing the comparison is first-order. Any of these breaks this sub-claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a yes or no on the physical question. No instrument reading is reported here.
- Not a choice among the competing QEP formulations. Those are bindings from the physics frame and need provenance and a shadow test.
- Not a new primitive, not a seventh substrate member, and not an import that moves the wall.
- Not a claim that existing η bounds prove the EP exact, and not a claim that a violation has been seen.
- Not a theory of everything; no final room.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing in this packet is settled by experiment.
- Not a biological or statistical claim. The allometry and clinical-statistics sources are analogues and one witness each, never physics authorities.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument.

1. Does the metric behave as ordering (spectrum-like) or directedness (gradient-like) when the test body is a superposition? An instrument that discriminates these would name the slot, with provenance.
2. Does the `⋛` relation hold between the two settled molecules at the relevant scale, or demonstrably not, with operands that count as quantities at that level?
3. Does a differential-acceleration signal survive separation from a new force, from systematics and from a mis-stated principle, under two closures rather than one?
4. Do the four pre-registered refuters in `01` §4 and `07` §7 remain unfired?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

2. Check C2 against one paper you know well: are the reported η numbers upper limits at a stated sensitivity, or are they presented as determinations?
