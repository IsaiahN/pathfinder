**One-line answer.** The Tether does not say whether the equivalence principle holds at quantum scales. It says what shape any answer must have: a union compound of two settled first-order frames compared at second order, with an operator-level or reference-frame-level statement in the slot rather than a name, and with the physical verdict owed to an instrument. This is `unreached, not unreachable`, because atom interferometers already return phase residuals that fail to resolve.

**Boundary.** This package is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a ground reading and cannot settle the question it reports on. The figures are primary. World sources are secondary corroboration only, and every one was opened on 2026-11-23 and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Nothing outside this folder was modified.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this package determines the physical verdict.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.
