## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: a shape is what a missing thing would have to look like. Fig 9 :32 puts it in the figure text: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For QMGR-0099 this means the deliverable is not "the EP holds" or "the EP fails". It is a constraint set: the answer must be a union compound, must compare two settled first-order molecules at second order, must express equivalence at the level of operators or reference frames rather than classical worldlines, and must leave the physical verdict to an instrument. A shape constrains; it does not fill.

Concretely, `SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar has its analogue here: **EP-classical(??? )EP-quantum**. The slot is not filled by the framework. The three candidate fillings the world literature offers (mass-operator equality, quantum reference frame equivalence, or a violation with test-particle-dependent geometry) are bindings, not derivations.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."* Then: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is "universality of free fall", "local inertial frame", "mass", and the recurring new terms from the literature are "mass operators", "superposition of internal energy states", "quantum reference frame", "Eötvös parameter".
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with what a failure would look like rather than with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the shape itself, because the instrument has not returned a verdict.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question that means an interferometer result that discriminates violation from fifth forces and systematics.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming a QEP formulation from the physics literature is exactly such a binding, and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* Our three sub-residuals are themselves a choice of decomposition. They are declared and visible in `01` §0, not smuggled.

## 4. Why a clean verdict is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Applied here: a differential phase in an atom interferometer can be produced by an EP violation, a new composition-dependent force, or a systematic. The reading does not determine the source. Declaring "the EP is violated" from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a verdict.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the horizon idea: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* This is the essay-level version of Fig 8 :39 and governs how the η bounds in `04` must be reported.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q1 answered: what does "shape" license here?

**Nothing that names the principle, and nothing that returns a physical yes or no.** The framework licenses the four-part constraint in §1 and the rule that an instrument, not agreement, settles the remaining question. A particular QEP formulation is licensed only by the ground settling it, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. This is `01` F3, triangulated across Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :133, with the canonical README and the essays as corroboration.

**Does `01` commit the error the README forbids?** No. It names the three candidate formulations only as bindings from the world literature and explicitly withholds selection. Its one live strain (the violable anchor in Das et al.) is recorded rather than resolved by assertion.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that a particular QEP formulation is correct is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let an instrument settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains venues, not people, and why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a refutation.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The architecture post itself says: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* And its demo caveat warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. No claim in this folder is `grounded`.

## 8. Correction to my own §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that a positive interferometer signal should be called an EP violation only after a better instrument exists. That is too strong. The right rule is narrower: the signal must be **source-discriminating**, which can come from a second frame with different closure (two species, two instruments, or a modulation that separates a fifth-force signature), not necessarily from a better version of the same instrument. Fig 8 :47-49: independence is how much two frames avoid the same evidence. "Better" alone can simply make the same ambiguity more precise. Both the original claim and this correction are recorded, so neither is quietly lost.
