## 0. The question stated as a residual, in effect terms

The question asks what the Tether says about testing the equivalence principle (EP) at quantum scales. Stated as an effect, not a topic:

> Two theories each make settled predictions in their own domain. GR says that all freely falling bodies follow the same geodesics regardless of mass or composition, so a local freely falling frame eliminates gravity. QM says that a test body is a delocalized wave packet with internal energy operators, superpositions and no single classical worldline. The residual is the mismatch when the test body itself is quantum: does universality of free fall still hold, what would count as a failure, and what would a positive signal mean?

Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term before starting (Formula step "Direction"). An answer would look like: a precise statement of the principle at the quantum level (operator equality, or equivalence of quantum reference frames), a measurable quantity that discriminates violation from systematics and from new forces, and an instrument returning a residual that trends down or does not. This is the shape. Whether nature realises it is a ground question.

Three sub-residuals, kept separate:

- **A, the statement gap.** There is no agreed definition of the "quantum equivalence principle". Dobkowski et al. write: *"As there seems to be no agreement on how to exactly define the QEP, we leave this for future theoretical and experimental work"*. Herrmann et al. put the same point sharply: *"Since in the quantum domain all objects are extended, it is not clear whether the notion of a WEP in the quantum domain makes sense at all."* This is a vocabulary link, not yet a measurement question.
- **B, the verdict gap.** Does the EP hold? Only an instrument can answer. Fig 2 :29 names the anchor: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
- **C, the interpretation gap.** A positive differential-acceleration signal can mean EP violation, a new composition-dependent force, or an imprecisely stated principle. Unnikrishnan and Gillies: *"All these incorrect conclusions arise from imprecise statement of the equivalence principle itself"*. Das et al. show that if the quantum EP is broken, *"the observed geometry would effectively depend on the properties of the test particle"*.

A and C are framing gaps. B is a ground gap. The question bundles all three, so it is a union compound and must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).

## 1. Domain census

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- *Arity*: two conditions meet at the test body (gravity as geometry, quantum as superposition). Not one hard question.
- *Symmetry*: the classical EP is a local equivalence symmetry; at quantum scale the candidate symmetry is an equality of mass operators or an equivalence of quantum reference frames. The comparison bond ⋛ is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table :48), not a symmetric join of two same-level operands.
- *Scale*: first order inside each theory, second order at their contact. Both first-order molecules are already settled against the ground, so Fig 12 :104 licenses their promotion: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*

**One line per domain. Actors / currency / anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, classical gravity frame | EP, geodesics, inertial and gravitational mass, metric | geometric derivation, free-fall trajectory | torsion balance, lunar laser ranging, MICROSCOPE | yes |
| D2, quantum frame | wave packets, superpositions of energy and position, mass operators, clocks | quantum phase, operator prediction | COW neutron interferometry, atom interferometry | yes |
| D3, contact and instrument frame | atom interferometers, quantum Galileo interferometer, entangled clocks, cold-atom space proposals | measured phase residual, Eötvös parameter η, systematics | the instrument reading itself | yes |
| D4, verification frame | expert readers and experimental groups | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement | yes |

**Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are a partition at first order, each settled in its own closure. The contact of D1 and D2 in D3 is a **union compound**, not a harder instance of either: Fig 9 :55-56, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. D4 is not a physics domain but a checking arrangement, and its independence is zero in this simulated run because every seat reads the same evidence pool.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Settle each first-order molecule before the second-order comparison (Fig 12 :104). Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2). Ask SEARCHED before INWARD or OUTWARD (Formula step 7). Refine the instrument from a worse one already returning something (Fig 6 :32).

**Anchor test applied to each domain.** D1 and D2 have settled anchors and pass. D3's anchor is the instrument and passes, because a phase reading does not change when a theorist changes their mind (Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*). D4's anchor is the ground, but its simulated seats have no independence, so it fails as a witness and is recorded as internal consistency only.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The physical verdict is an anchor question, and no frame can supply it (decisive for mode)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*

Whether the EP holds for a superposition is settled by what an interferometer returns, not by which of GR, QM or the Tether has the more compelling story. The Tether cannot upgrade a `specified` prediction to a `grounded` result. **Triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 6 (low overlap: one figure defines the anchor, the other the instrument rule). World corroboration: Rosi et al. report *"genuine quantum aspects of EEP with no classical analogue"* and still state an experimental upper bound, not a verdict on the principle.

- **Refuter:** a derivation from the figures that returns a yes or no on the physical verdict without a ground reading. If one is produced, this finding is wrong and the mode claim collapses.

### F2 — The question is a union compound, not one hard question

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

The statement gap (A), the verdict gap (B) and the interpretation gap (C) are three conditions. A single lever that clears one leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). Searching for one quantum equivalence principle as a single phrase before describing A, B and C separately lets any proposed formulation seem to fit. **Single-source** for the union rule (Fig 9 only), corroborated in the world by Cepollaro and Giacomini, who show that the classical EEP definition is *"not sufficient for quantum test particles in a quantum superposition of positions and velocities"*, and by Dobkowski et al.'s open definition.

- **Refuter:** a single figure line that governs A, B and C as one quantity and closes the residual without unbundling. Then the union reading is a forced strain and must be re-censused.

### F3 — The framework returns a shape, not the name of the principle

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

The Tether can constrain the answer to have operator-level or reference-frame-level content (mass operators equal, or quantum frames equivalent), but it cannot select among the competing formulations. Naming one is a binding from the physics frame onto a held atom, not a derivation. This is the strongest single point in the package. **Triangulated** across Fig 9 and Fig 13 (different figures, low overlap). World corroboration: Dobkowski et al. explicitly leave the QEP definition open; Cepollaro and Giacomini say the status *"is still debated"*.

- **Refuter:** a derivation from the six-term set alone that uniquely names one QEP formulation. If it succeeds, the shape-not-a-name limit is false in this domain.

### F4 — This question is ground-available, unlike Planck-scale quantum gravity

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change. Same atoms, same wall, more of the space actually visited."*

Atom interferometers, the quantum Galileo interferometer and proposed clock interferometers are already returning phase residuals that fail to resolve. There is an edge to extend from. The remedy is therefore SEARCHED first, then INWARD (sharpen the existing instrument), not OUTWARD import of a new primitive. Formula step 7 keeps these three distinct, and the figures warn that *"INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed; never read one against the other."* **Triangulated** across Fig 2 :29 (anchor is an experiment) and Fig 6 :32 (instrument rule). World corroboration: Tino writes that *"qualitatively new tests of the weak equivalence principle can be devised with atom interferometry taking advantage of the quantum nature of atomic gravity sensors"*.

- **Refuter:** a demonstration that no existing or proposed instrument returns anything bearing on the quantum EP, so that there is no proto-instrument. Then the verdict changes from `unreached` toward `unreachable-at-this-budget`.

### F5 — Null results bound the principle; they do not prove it exact

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 8 :40-41: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."*

Published η constraints, from the low 10^-9 in Rosi et al. to the 10^-14 scale of classical tests cited by Tino, are bounds at a given sensitivity and mass range. They do not close the quantum-scale question. The low-overlap statistics source makes the same point in a different field: Altman and Bland write that calling a non-significant result negative *"wrongly implies that the study has shown that there is no difference, whereas usually all that has been shown is an absence of evidence of a difference"*. **Triangulated** across Fig 8 and Fig 9, with an independent low-overlap witness from medical statistics.

- **Refuter:** a sealed closure argument showing that the instrument space is exhausted. No frame is a sealed room, so this refuter is expected to fail, but it is written down before the conclusion as required.

### F6 — A positive signal is a mis-attached gap until it is discriminated

Fig 5 :13: *"mis-attached: refit what you have, do not invent"*. Fig 5 :41: *"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."*

An apparent EP violation from an interferometer may be a new force, an unmodelled systematic or an imprecise statement of the principle. Unnikrishnan and Gillies: *"All these incorrect conclusions arise from imprecise statement of the equivalence principle itself"*. Das et al. show that a genuine QEP violation would make the metric depend on the test particle's mass ratios. So minting a new "violation" term before a source-discriminating instrument exists is a mis-attached refit, not a mint. **Single-source** for the refit rule (Fig 5), with world corroboration from two independent papers.

- **Refuter:** a positive signal that survives separation from fifth forces, systematics and statement errors, measured by two instruments of different closure. Then a mint is owed and this finding is cancelled for that signal.

### F7 — The round-trip gap is nonzero: classical EP does not survive concretisation

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31-32: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*

Carry the classical sentence *"all bodies fall the same way"* up, then bring it down onto a quantum superposition. Three things are lost: point-like test body, single local inertial frame, and mass as a number rather than an operator. Treating the classical statement and the quantum statement as the same thing sets `R_T` to zero and is the dead-reckoning error the figure names. **Single-source** for the round-trip rule (Fig 4), corroborated by Cepollaro and Giacomini's argument that a straight application of the classical EEP to a superposition *"would necessarily lead to a violation of the EEP"* for reasons unrelated to gravity.

- **Refuter:** a formulation of the quantum EP in which all three losses are recovered without adding structure. Then `R_T` is zero for this passage and the finding is withdrawn.

### F8 — Filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Precision reach, publication count and proposal prestige decide which experiment gets funded first. They do not decide whether the EP holds. A list of advocates assembled by likely agreement is a convention, not a corroboration. **Triangulated** across Fig 9 and Fig 2.

- **Refuter:** a framework rule that lets a precision target or an endorsement count as a witness. No such line was found after a full census of the figure text.

If 1 to 4 fire, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this package may be labelled `grounded`.
