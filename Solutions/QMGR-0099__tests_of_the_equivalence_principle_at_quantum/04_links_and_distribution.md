## Wider literature actually read, grouped by what it bears on

**The statement gap (sub-residual A).**

1. Dobkowski et al., *"Observation of quantum free fall and the consistency with the equivalence principle"*, arXiv:2502.14535v4, 2025. https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14535. The clearest statement that the definition is open: *"As there seems to be no agreement on how to exactly define the QEP, we leave this for future theoretical and experimental work"*. Reports a measured free-fall phase with a bounded conclusion: *"in our low energy regime, the equivalence principle may be applied to the quantum domain"*. The words *"may"* and *"low energy regime"* matter. Cite it for boundedness, not for a verdict.
2. Herrmann, Dittus and Lämmerzahl, *"Testing the equivalence principle with atomic interferometry"*, Class. Quantum Grav. 29 184003, 2012. https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0264-9381/29/18/184003. States the residual directly: *"Since in the quantum domain all objects are extended, it is not clear whether the notion of a WEP in the quantum domain makes sense at all."*
3. Cepollaro and Giacomini, *"Quantum generalisation of Einstein's Equivalence Principle can be verified with entangled clocks as quantum reference frames"*, arXiv:2112.03303v5, 2024. https://arxiv.org/html/2112.03303v5. *"When the particles involved in the test exhibit quantum properties, it is unknown whether this principle still holds."* Argues that a straight application of the classical EEP to a superposition would fail for reasons unrelated to gravity.
4. Orlando, Mann, Modi and Pollock, *"A test of the equivalence principle(s) for quantum superpositions"*, Class. Quantum Grav. 33 19LT01, 2016. https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/0264-9381/33/19/19LT01. Proposes a concrete bound on phenomenological violating parameters. Useful as an example of an instrument-first move.

**The verdict and interpretation gaps (sub-residuals B and C).**

5. Rosi et al., *"Quantum test of the equivalence principle for atoms in coherent superposition of internal energy states"*, Nature Communications 8:15529, 2017. https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15529. *"The use of the superposition state allows testing genuine quantum aspects of EEP with no classical analogue, which have remained completely unexplored so far."* Reports an upper limit, not a determination.
6. Tino, *"Testing gravity with cold atom interferometry: results and prospects"*, Quantum Sci. Technol. 6 024014, 2021. https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/2058-9565/abd83e. Review of the instrument habitat. The line that bears most on F4: *"qualitatively new tests of the weak equivalence principle can be devised with atom interferometry taking advantage of the quantum nature of atomic gravity sensors"*.
7. Das, Fridman and Lambiase, *"General formalism of the quantum equivalence principle"*, Communications Physics 6:198, 2023. https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-023-01306-w. Formal operator statement and the violation consequence: *"the observed geometry would effectively depend on the properties of the test particle."* This is the source of the live strain in §7 of `01`.
8. Unnikrishnan and Gillies, *"Equivalence principles exotica"*, Frontiers of Physics 3(4):444-456, 2008. https://journal.hep.com.cn/fop/EN/10.1007/s11467-008-0031-1. *"All these incorrect conclusions arise from imprecise statement of the equivalence principle itself"*. This is the source-discrimination warning behind F6.

**Low-overlap corroboration.**

9. Altman and Bland, *"Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence"*, BMJ 311:485, 1995. https://www.bmj.com/content/311/7003/485.full. Medical statistics, not physics. *"This term wrongly implies that the study has shown that there is no difference, whereas usually all that has been shown is an absence of evidence of a difference."* One independent witness for F5. It does not share the physics evidence pool.
10. Kolokotrones, Savage, Deeds and Fontana, *"Curvature in metabolic scaling"*, Nature 464:753-756, 2010. https://www.nature.com/articles/nature08920. Biology, not physics. *"we show that the relationship between mass and metabolic rate has convex curvature on a logarithmic scale, and is therefore not a pure power law, even after accounting for body temperature."* An analogue, not an authority: a scaling principle that looks stable in one mass range turns out to need re-testing when the range changes. It corroborates the general habit of re-testing a principle at a new scale, and nothing about the equivalence principle specifically.

Note on independence: sources 1 to 8 all sit in the same broad physics pool and several cite each other. Sources 9 and 10 come from different fields. The agreement of 1 to 8 is one pool talking, not eight witnesses. Source 10 is an analogue by design and is never used as a physics authority.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the three-condition residual and the refuter first. The links come only after a verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Do not gather endorsements of a QEP formulation as if they selected one.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-condition residual and the shape claim; (2) the four refuters and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Residual, shape claim, refuters. No theory. Their verdict is recorded before anything else is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** The three framework links. They cannot be used as evidence for the stage-1 verdict.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

**Hard precondition:** all three stages depend on the stage-1 output surviving. The four refuters in `01` §4 and `08` §2 are written before the run. If one fires, stages 2 and 3 are not sent to that reader.
