## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and the physical verdict stays open. The question is a union compound of three conditions that must be unbundled in the order A → B → C. Any admissible answer compares two settled first-order molecules at second order, states the relation at operator or reference-frame level, and leaves the yes or no to an instrument. The framework returns a shape, not a name: mass-operator equality, quantum reference frame equivalence and test-particle-dependent geometry are bindings from the physics frame, not derivations. Atom interferometers already return residuals that fail to resolve, so this is `unreached, not unreachable`. Null η bounds do not prove the EP exact, and a positive signal is mis-attached until it is separated from new forces, systematics and statement errors. The classical EP does not carry into the quantum frame unchanged; point-like test body, single local inertial frame and c-number mass are lost in the passage, and that `R_T` is recorded qualitatively. One live strain remains: if a violation makes geometry test-particle dependent, a new anchor is owed. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 prices elsewhere in the same figure: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the three-condition residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings are withheld rather than asserted, so nothing has to be retracted inside the physics. A ground reading can name the slot without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that distinguishes ordering from directedness for a superposed mass would name the slot; a definition-free, discriminated violation signal from two independent closures would reorder A and B; a derivation from the six-term set that uniquely names a formulation would break the shape-not-a-name limit; proof that no instrument returns anything would change the status toward unreachable-at-this-budget; a discriminated violation signal would settle the world question while leaving the method intact. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
