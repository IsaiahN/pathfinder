**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about quantum tests of the equivalence principle

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question, in effect terms.** We have two theories that each work in their own domain. General relativity says all freely falling bodies follow the same geodesics regardless of mass or composition. Quantum mechanics says the test body is a delocalized wave packet with internal energy operators, superpositions and no single classical worldline. The question is whether universality of free fall still holds when the test body is quantum, what would count as a failure, and what a positive signal would mean.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** This bundle is not one hard question. It is three conditions: (A) there is no agreed statement of a quantum equivalence principle, (B) whether the principle holds is an instrument question, and (C) a positive differential-acceleration signal has at least three possible sources, including a new force and an imprecise statement. The framework's contribution is a shape, not a verdict: any admissible answer must compare two already-settled first-order descriptions at second order, must put an operator-level or reference-frame-level statement in the slot rather than a name, and must leave the physical verdict to an instrument. It does not say whether the principle holds. Current null results bound it; they do not prove it exact.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a particular formulation of the quantum equivalence principle is the right one, that existing interferometer bounds settle the question, or that a violation has been seen. The framework returns a kind of thing, never the answer itself. Naming a formulation would be a binding from the physics literature onto a term the framework already holds, and would need provenance, not a derivation.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. a figure line I have missed that governs the three conditions as one quantity and closes the residual without unbundling;
> 2. a derivation from the six-term set alone that uniquely names one quantum equivalence principle formulation;
> 3. a ground reading that settles the physical verdict with a direct first-order composition, with no second-order structure;
> 4. a demonstration that no existing or proposed instrument returns anything bearing on the question, so the honest status is unreachable at this budget rather than unreached.
>
> I wrote these before making the claim. If any holds, I withdraw the claim. If a violation is measured that survives separation from fifth forces, systematics and statement errors, that settles the world question and I will record it, though it does not change the method. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The full architecture: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Two-stage offer, restated

1. **Stage 1.** Send the three-condition residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, send the three links with the note that the figures are the authority, not the summary. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground.

**Hard precondition:** the three links do not make the stage-1 output true. If the output fails the check in §3 of `01`, do not send stages 2 and 3 to that reader.
