# The Tether's answer: do independent measurement methods converge on overlapping data?

**Mode: `general` for the structural law, `specified` for the application to H0 pipelines, nothing `grounded`.** This is a shape and a test, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two measurement frames claim independence while drawing on a common evidence pool, and their H0 estimates converge. The question is what that convergence licenses us to conclude. The residual is not "which H0 is right"; that is a ground question. It is: *what is the worth of this agreement, given the overlap?*

Fig 9 :55 applies: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The two frames plus the shared data are a union compound with its own loop, not two independent partitions. It must be unbundled before agreement is scored (Fig 9 :54).

## 2. The shape

1. **Shared evidence is not independence.** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Two analyses on the same supernova compilation, calibrator set or parallax solution can agree because the pool is talking, not because the ground is being confirmed (Fig 2 :19-21).

2. **Independence is one quantity read twice.** Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice." The same overlap that devalues agreement also shrinks the union surplus (Fig 8 :48). More teams on one data set buy spelling, not search: Fig 6 :53 "searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names."

3. **Convergence is real when the evidence is disjoint.** Fig 8 :50 "corroboration at maximum independence". A standard-siren distance and a CMB-inferred sound horizon that agree from different systematics are two witnesses. The discriminator is provenance, not labels: Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

4. **A chain of agreeing pipelines is one long room.** Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"; Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Ten fits to one compilation are one opinion stated ten times.

5. **Report residuals per rung.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A global agreement within one sigma is "a global R near zero with one live slot residual" (Fig 1 :13) and is a legal state while a single rung carries the live error. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

6. **The bond matters.** Two pipelines that share all evidence stand in an identity-like relation, two names for one referent (Fig 12 :67), not a conjunction of two witnesses. Partial overlap yields members of one functional group (Fig 12 :124), or isomers over the full shared atom set (Fig 12 :117). See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

7. **The ground decides the value.** Fig 9 :42 "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"; Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The figures cannot say whether H0 is 67 or 73, and they do not say which rung is wrong.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that either team is wrong, and not that the Hubble tension is a calibration problem or new physics. No measurement was taken.
- Not that agreement between overlapping methods is worthless. It is evidence about the shared pipeline, and that can be useful; it is just not independent evidence about H0.
- Not that more data cannot help. More data on the same axes is SEARCHED; a channel on a different axis is OUTWARD (`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7), and the two instructions are different.
- Not that team count, prestige, or instrument size are witnesses. They are filters for the budget: Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not a derivation of a new primitive. "Evidence overlap" is a reading of Fig 2 :31 in domain vocabulary, not a seventh term.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The world already supplies the discriminator in its own vocabulary. Brout et al. 2022 (ApJ 938 110) report that SNe Ia systematic uncertainties "comprise less than one-third of the total uncertainty in the measurement of H0 and cannot explain the present 'Hubble tension'". That closes one rung-level excuse; it does not decide the tension. Freedman et al. 2019 show that TRGB is "parallel to, but independent of the Cepheid distance scale" while sharing HST, the LMC modulus and SNe Ia, which is the overlap this answer predicts. The statistics literature gives the same arithmetic under another name: "The effective sample size, defined by Kish in 1965, is calculated by dividing the original sample size by the design effect" (Design effect, Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-23).

What would close the question at the ground:

1. An **independence audit**: for each pair of estimates, publish the shared covariance and the atoms each pipeline owns alone. State `rho`, or state that it is unknown. Then score agreement with the effective number of witnesses, not the raw count.
2. A **rung-residual table**: per calibrator, per parallax solution, per supernova compilation, per selection function. A global sigma hides the live slot (Fig 1 :22).
3. A **disjoint-channel test**: a measurement whose systematics share no rung with either ladder (standard sirens, masers, lensing with independent mass modelling, cosmic chronometers). Agreement or disagreement there is Fig 8 :50 corroboration or a real conflict, not a pool effect.
4. A **blind reanalysis**: analysis choices fixed before the combined fit, so the evidence is not used to shape the method that is then confirmed by it. The SEP entry on confirmation calls the failure "double-counting" and says the confirmational power becomes "dried out".
5. **Standing refuters from section 3 of the audit:** measured shared covariance removed and agreement still tracks the ground; a verbatim figure line reversing the law; a proof that unbundling is impossible in principle; a moving anchor that still settles.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the instruments stop distinguishing. The eight essays warn that declaring primitives makes a choice visible, not neutral: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible" (*They're Scanning Books*, 2026-09-06). Calling two estimates "independent" is such a choice; it must be recorded as a choice and audited, not trusted. The canonical figures README says the framework returns a shape, not a name, and "indiscernibility is not identity" applies to two pipelines that read alike. The lineage, in this case the provenance of each rung, is what keeps the result checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which Hubble constant is right. It says that the worth of an agreement between two measurement methods is set by how little they draw on the same evidence. Two analyses that share a supernova compilation, a calibrator or a parallax solution can never talk to each other and still agree, because the agreement is the shared pool talking, not two frames confirming each other. Adding more teams on that pool multiplies agreement, not evidence. Real corroboration is possible, but only when two frames reach the same shape from disjoint evidence, and only the provenance record can tell you which case you are in. What this offers is the shape the answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument-level tests, an independence audit, a rung-residual table and a disjoint channel, that would close it. The value stays open until the ground settles it.
