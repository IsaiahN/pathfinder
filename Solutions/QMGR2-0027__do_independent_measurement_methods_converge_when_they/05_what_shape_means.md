## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule in `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

From *The Library of Babel*, the searcher can stop when the returned set is "small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question". A shape narrows; it does not fill. For this question:

- A **shape** is: *agreement between two measurement frames is worth the independence between them, and data overlap subtracts from independence*. This can be stated without knowing which H0 value is correct.
- A **name** would be: *the tension is a Cepheid calibration error*, or *the tension is early dark energy*. The figures cannot produce either name. Naming one from this derivation would breach the limit.

The shape also carries a number-like discriminator, but not an H0 number: the amount of evidence overlap. That is a quantity to be measured, not a value to be asserted.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary. This is a recursive process."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." and Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The effect description here is "how much of the evidence do the two estimates share", not "are the teams different".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why the packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` asks what the recipient would expect to see if shared-data convergence were harmless.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the loop stops when the shape is stated and the refuter is written. It does not stop at a value of H0.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest". A measurement channel with disjoint calibration can name the source of a discrepancy; an argument about independence cannot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-51), with a source gate and a shadow test. An H0 value imported from a CMB likelihood into a distance-ladder argument is such an import; it must be marked as one.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." For the Hubble problem the needed import is not another fit to the same supernovae; it is a channel whose systematic axes are different, such as standard sirens, masers or lensing with independent mass modelling.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it maps exactly onto overlapping data. A color instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". Several sources produce the same reading, so the reading underdetermines its cause. The essay's verdict is checkable: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

Here, two analyses reading the same supernova compilation produce agreeing H0 values. The agreement no more comments on the source of the tension than the green reading comments on whether the source was RGB, CMYK or something not yet discovered. What is owed is a discriminator with different axes, not a name assigned to a projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* supplies the failure mode for a community that skips this step: "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time." This is Fig 2 collapse 1 expressed in ordinary language; the overlapping-data case is its quieter sibling in collapse 2.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What shape must an answer to "do independent methods converge on overlapping data" have?**

It must be a statement about the relation between three things, not two: frame A, frame B, and the evidence pool they share. It must name independence as the quantity that determines the worth of the agreement (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47). It must allow that convergence on disjoint evidence is real corroboration (Fig 8 :50), and it must refuse convergence on pooled evidence as a verdict (Fig 2 :40). It must leave the value of H0 and the cause of the tension to the ground (Fig 9 :42). Finally, it must be checkable without the framework: a reader should be able to ask for the shared covariance and refuse the claim if none is supplied.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** It does not name a cause. It does name a *quantity*, evidence overlap, which is not a new primitive imported into the framework but a reading of Fig 2 :31 in the vocabulary of the measurement domain. That is a binding, recorded as such in `06_isomers_and_order.md`, not a derivation of a new term.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A long list of famous teams who agree on a shared compilation is a prestige loop, not an instrument. The legitimate ask is for a discriminator, and the ask gives the claim a budget to be tried rather than asking for endorsement.

**Separate experience is the refresh.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources". A helpful line from the same essay: "Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained". The practical consequence for cosmology is that adding another analysis of the same data is rotation, not refresh (Fig 10 :80); adding a channel with a different noise budget is refresh.

**Ten copies are one opinion.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times." This is Fig 8 :49 "one frame wearing two names" applied to teams. It is also a direct statement of Fig 2 :44's effective-sample-size arithmetic without using its vocabulary.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". *Arrival of the Fittest* adds the sharper warning that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the Hubble tension and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
