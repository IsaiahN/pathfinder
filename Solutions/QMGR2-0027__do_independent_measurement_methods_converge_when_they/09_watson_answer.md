## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what convergence between two measurement frames is worth when those frames use overlapping data. Option A: two methods with different labels that agree are independent corroboration of the measured value, so agreement rules out method-specific error. Option B: evidence overlap subtracts from independence, so agreement on a shared pool is evidence about the pool, and only convergence from disjoint evidence corroborates. What each leads to: A spends the only resource that makes agreement meaningful and can declare a tension resolved or real on the strength of a shared calibration; B demands a provenance audit, an effective witness count, and a disjoint channel before the value is settled, and it keeps the empirical question open at the ground.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Option B. Data overlap subtracts from independence. Two determinations that share the evidence generating their agreement have corroborated the pipeline, not the value; their agreement is worth the effective number of independent witnesses, not the number of teams. Genuine convergence is real and valuable, but only when the frames reach the same shape from disjoint evidence, and only the provenance record can tell the two cases apart. A chain of pipelines on one pool multiplies agreement while evidence does not grow. The figures cannot say which H0 is right or whether the tension is physical; those are ground questions. This verdict is `general` on the law, `specified` on the application, and `candidate` because no ground reading was taken.

- **cost if wrong:** if we wrongly count pooled convergence as corroboration, we spend the independence that is the only thing agreement is worth, and we may declare the tension resolved or real on a shared calibration. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." If we wrongly refuse genuine convergence, we discard real corroboration and delay a true result. Both costs are bounded because the claim is a shape with a refuter, not a number.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. Relabel "independent convergence" as "pool agreement", publish the shared covariance, and the claim can be re-run on an independence audit. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independence audit showing that shared covariance is negligible and agreement still tracks the ground; a verbatim figure line reversing the overlap law; a proof that unbundling shared atoms is impossible in principle; a moving anchor that nevertheless settles; or a disjoint-channel measurement that closes the H0 question at the ground. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
