## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement under a shared pool is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that disqualifies the verdict.
2. **A mirror cannot correct you.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list chosen to agree is one frame wearing many names.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Sending the framework before asking for a verdict spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.

Legally, scraping personal addresses for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
