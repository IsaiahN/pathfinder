## Restating the question as an effect

The question assumes one dial, "how much connection," with a right setting that trades efficiency against exposure. In effect terms the residual is this: a structure is built to move flow cheaply, it is predicted to keep moving it, and then a local shock arrives and the prediction fails at particular slots while the system-wide average still looks healthy (Fig 1 :13, :19, :22). What is being decided is not a ratio but the arrangement of bonds, the reachability of a non-updating ground, and the independence of the frames that declare the system safe.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

A `specified` answer would (1) name the domains whose residuals differ, (2) give a figure-verbatim constraint per domain, (3) state what would refute each, and (4) name the kind of instrument that would turn the shape into a number. Anything that returns a single optimal coupling percentage without such an instrument is a verdict stated in the wrong mode.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. First, a room does not upgrade the label: *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor"* (Seat Map :283). Second, selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163). Third, asking a checker to trust before they check spends the only thing they contribute: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42).

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
