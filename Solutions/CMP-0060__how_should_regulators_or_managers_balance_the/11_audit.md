## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per slot), Fig 12 :5/:117/:121 (bond, isomer, cycle), Fig 13 :31-32/:115 (amplifying remainder, open arrangements), Fig 8 :39 and Fig 2 :40 (search and agreement).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `09`: per-slot residual; bond and cycle sign as lever; threshold compound; independent non-updating ground; seat able to refuse; fraction is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete regulator reply, for example "cut interconnection by 30 percent and run the same stress test twice."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source-discriminating instrument** is dropped, so the concrete reply reverts to a pooled reading that cannot name its source;
- the **bond-versus-node distinction** is dropped, so "cut interconnection by 30 percent" treats the amount as the lever the figures refuse;
- the **independence and non-updating-anchor conditions** are dropped, so running "the same stress test twice" is one frame wearing two names.

One thing survives cleanly: a cascade residual is legible only per slot, and the arrangement, not the ingredient list, carries the difference. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating any condensation here as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
