## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the set of constraints any acceptable answer must satisfy, statable before the number is known. The shape here is: residual indexed per slot; a non-updating ground kept reachable; contact enumerated outward; the bond and the cycle sign as the lever; independent frames for any safety verdict; a seat able to refuse; and a named instrument that would close the fraction gap. "Connect 30 percent of possible links" is a name. The shape is what that number must respect.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual was stated as a failed prediction at particular slots rather than as "too much connectivity."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A plain description of the cascade one would expect is checkable by a regulator without first accepting the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here the loop stops when the residual stops arriving, which includes the case where the channel to the ground has closed (Fig 4).

## 3. What licenses a number

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* A measured cascade record and an instrument that discriminates shock-absorption from shock-amplification would name the fraction.
2. **A provenanced import from a differing closure** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An ecology result about compartmentalization can travel here only as an effect description, not as borrowed jargon.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why the pooling of readings is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. An instrument that cannot distinguish sources demonstrates its own inadequacy.

A pooled systemic-risk score is exactly this. Several distinct causal structures, a genuinely diversified network and a set of correlated positions that happen to share one factor, can produce the same reading. Naming the source ("the system is safe") from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a stronger verdict.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A risk model inside the system cannot validate the safety claim it produces.

## 5. The question answered: no figure licenses a universal fraction

**What licenses a particular coupling fraction?** Nothing in the framework. The fraction is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, any number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question ask the wrong object?** Yes, but not in the sense that connection is irrelevant. The question bundles three conditions with different anchors (flow efficiency, failure propagation, and the monitoring arrangement). Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* *Library of Babel* gives the matching instruction: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"* before recombining. That is why `01` does not return one number.

## 5b. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially wrote this section as if naming any concrete lever were forbidden. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Naming an already available binding, such as a compartment, a buffer, an independent frame or a refusal seat, is a binding from another frame onto held atoms. It is provenance to be recorded, not an invented primitive and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). What remains wrong is naming the source of a reading from the reading itself, which is the metamerism point in section 4. Both claims are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for honesty

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well known names to agree about systemic safety is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground, with a refuter written in advance.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle anything they report on, and nothing in this folder is `grounded`.
