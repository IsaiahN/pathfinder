## Wider literature that bears on the residual

These are the substantive world sources, secondary only. They are recorded with author, venue and year in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Finance and systemic risk.** Reported in the opened Systemic risk article: Acemoglu, Ozdaglar and Tahbaz-Salehi 2015 (American Economic Review) show connection enhancing stability only up to a threshold density, then propagating risk. Glasserman and Young 2015 give general bounds without assuming a specific architecture. Danielsson et al. note that market-based measures sample events that happen several times a year while crises happen roughly once in 43 years, so the measured probability is not the risk. This predicts the threshold compound and the channel-decay reading; it corroborates F2 and F6.

2. **Engineering and cascading failure.** The opened Cascading failure article defines the phenomenon as progressive failure through positive feedback and notes that judicious disconnection can stop a cascade, while the control signal may be no faster than the overload. This predicts the feedback-cycle and seat-to-refuse reading; it corroborates F2 and F7. It is a separate evidence pool from the finance material.

3. **Ecology and network stability.** The opened Ecological network article reports that complexity was once thought to reduce stability by letting disturbance spread and amplify, that compartmentalization limits cascading extinctions, and that a trade-off between kinds of stability can produce a tipping point with simultaneous collapse (Lever et al. 2014). This predicts the bond, compartment and threshold reading; it corroborates F1, F2 and F3. It is the lowest-overlap witness.

4. **Framework essays (hidden overlap).** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, Hard Problem is About Source and Library of Babel essays are all by the framework author and share the figures' evidence pool completely. Their agreement with the figure verdict is expected and carries no independent witness weight. They are used for the canonical reading of "shape" and for the simulated-status and no-prestige rules, not as outside corroboration.

Overlap summary: the finance and ecology streams are genuinely low overlap with each other and with the framework essays. The three essays are one pool. Agreement among them is one witness talking, not three (Fig 2 :40).

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1: the residual stated in effect terms, the shape claim, and the pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks it without holding the framework's interior. Stage 2 is opt-in: only if curious, the source links. A Stage 1 verdict must be recorded before Stage 2 is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.

The hard precondition is that Stage 1 must survive. The packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the Stage 1 artifact. It carries its own mode and refuters, so a checker can refuse it in twenty minutes without reading the rest of this folder.

No archive is created or hosted by this agent, and nothing is sent. Hosting and sending are the owner's actions. Prefer an opt-in download page over an attachment pushed to a list, so a recipient can decline contact.
