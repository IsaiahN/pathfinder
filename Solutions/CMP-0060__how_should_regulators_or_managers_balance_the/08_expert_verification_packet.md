# Expert verification packet: the Tether on efficiency versus cascading failure

**What this is:** a claim in a form a risk researcher or regulator can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal fraction. Not `grounded`.** No cascade measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status: simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.**

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How tightly should we connect a system" is the wrong object to optimize. The residual is indexed per slot, so a system-wide average can hide a live cascade. The lever is the typed bond and the sign of the feedback cycle, not the link count: over a fixed set of components, a dense and a compartmentalized arrangement are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, with decisive behavioural difference. Connection behaves as a threshold compound, absorbing shocks over one range and amplifying past a tipping point. What should be governed is the arrangement, a ground that does not update on the regulated parties' own reports, genuinely independent readings, and a seat able to refuse. A passed stress test is a search that found nothing, not proof of absence. No universal coupling fraction is returned; that is a declared gap with an instrument owed.

### C2 World-facing; checkable against network and risk literature

- **Statement:** coupling is a threshold compound. Connection absorbs a shock over one range and amplifies it past a tipping point; the sign of the redistribution loop is the variable.
- **Figure source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. - **What to check:** is the threshold robust across finance, infrastructure and ecology, or does the shape come from one evidence pool? The ecology source is the low-overlap witness.
- **Refuter:** a measured monotone relation between coupling and cascading loss across the whole operating range, or a demonstration that the redistribution loop cannot close. Either sends the compound back to census.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal optimal coupling percentage, buffer size or diversification ratio. That is a `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that tight connection is always bad. It absorbs shocks over one range.
- Not that diversification always helps. It requires independent evidence (Fig 2 :31).
- Not that risk can be subtracted from a habitat. Buffers and circuit breakers substitute a habitat (Fig 11 :61, :66).
- Not that a passed stress test is an anchor. It is an instrument with a round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30).
- Not a derivation from one theory of another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement of a real cascade.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not an argument. Three concrete requests:

1. A measurement distinguishing a shock-absorbing arrangement from a shock-amplifying one under the same load, so the catalyst and spent part can be told apart.
2. A coupling-to-loss relation measured across the operating range, locating the tipping point or showing there is none.
3. Independent readings of the same residual from frames with measured, low overlap, with a non-updating anchor.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19 and :22, Fig 12 :117, Fig 13 :115, Fig 12 :121, Fig 8 :39, Fig 2 :40. Six short passages.
2. Say whether C1's isomer claim is faithful to network science as they know it. Name one case where the same components under a different bond behaved the same under shock, if such a case exists.
3. Say whether the threshold in C2 is robust across their field, or whether the ecology and finance results share a method and therefore one evidence pool.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish absorption from amplification under load, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
