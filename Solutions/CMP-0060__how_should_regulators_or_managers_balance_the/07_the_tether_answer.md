# The Tether's answer: efficiency versus cascading failure

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal fraction. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The system is built to move flow cheaply; the prediction is continued service; a local failure shifts load, the feedback loop closes, and the prediction fails at particular slots while the system-wide average still looks healthy. That residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22), and it is a union of at least three conditions with different anchors (Fig 9 :55), not one dial with an optimum. The question asks for a balance; the figures return a separator and the kind of instrument that would close the number.

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain

**D1, exposure and topology.** `specified`, triangulated. The lever is the typed bond and the sign of the feedback cycle, not the number of links. Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :121 *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Fig 13 :31-32 *"a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."* The same atoms under a different bond are a different substance (Fig 12 :117), so a dense and a compartmentalized network over the same institutions are isomers. Connection absorbs then amplifies: treat it as a threshold compound. Enumerate contact outward, because *"And contact cascades"* (Fig 11 :52) and the residual that matters can be three steps out.

**D2, measurement and ground contact.** `specified`, triangulated. Keep the residual per slot and the two currencies apart (Fig 12 :125, :130). A passing stress test is the "searched" branch, not proof of absence: Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Hidden exposures are channel decay, not ground failure: Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* A model fed the regulated firms' own reports is not an anchor, because Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Independence decides whether readings mean anything (Fig 2 :31, :40), so diversify across genuinely separate evidence, not across correlated positions that share one factor.

**D3, governance and the seat.** `specified`, triangulated. Agreement inside one evidence pool is not a verdict: Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Keep a seat able to refuse; a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42). A safety threshold that decides the outcome on crossing has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47), and the seat's stake must be declared because *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59). An actor's valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49, :51), so there is no standing list of safe arrangements.

**D4, the model as a frame.** `general` structural, triangulated. A frame inside the system cannot compose its way to the atom it lacks (Fig 6 :21), cannot escape its own closure (Fig 6 :23), and cannot tell depth from absence (Fig 6 :27). So a "safe" verdict that comes only from the internal model is a candidate, not settled. What is owed is an import with provenance and a shadow test from a closure that differs, described in effect terms first (Fig 9 :27-28).

**Gap, the exact fraction.** No figure line returns a coupling percentage, buffer size or diversification ratio. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Recorded as a `gap`, with the instrument owed named below.

Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. A single efficiency-exposure score is precisely the average Fig 1 :22 warns against.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal optimal amount of connection. No fraction, buffer size, or modularity index is returned.
- Not "tight coupling is always bad." Connection absorbs shocks over one range; the claim is a threshold compound.
- Not "diversify everything." Diversification buys nothing when readings share one evidence pool.
- Not "managers can remove risk." Buffers and circuit breakers substitute a habitat; contact is not subtracted (Fig 11 :61, :66).
- Not that a passed stress test is the ground. It is an instrument with a round-trip gap.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of a real cascade was taken.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The metamerism essay's spectrometer is the model: several causes can give the same reading, so an instrument that cannot separate them demonstrates its own inadequacy.

1. **Cascade instrumentation.** A measurement that distinguishes a shock-absorbing bond arrangement from a shock-amplifying one under the same load, rather than reading the pooled outcome after the fact. This would name which arrangements are catalysts and which are spent parts.
2. **Threshold test.** A relation between coupling and cascading loss measured across the operating range, showing where absorption turns to amplification, or showing there is no such turn. The second outcome fires refuter 3 and sends the union reading back to census.
3. **Channel test.** Independent readings of the same residual from frames with genuinely separate evidence, with the overlap measured, so agreement can mean something (Fig 2 :31-32).
4. **Standing falsifiers.** A scalar preserving all per-slot information; identical cascade behaviour for different bonds; agreement from one evidence pool paid for by the ground; a battery proven to contain every cascade mechanism. Any one breaks the corresponding finding in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached to a shape, not to a number.** The framework returns the constraints any regulator's answer must satisfy and the kind of instrument that would close the fraction. It refuses to invent the fraction. Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round-trip gap R_T is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`; treating a condensation of these findings as identical to the figures would set it to zero and be the dead-reckoning error. The line is held where the available instruments stop, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number for how tightly to connect a system, and it does not treat efficiency and exposure as two ends of one dial. The signal that matters is indexed per slot, so a system-wide score can hide a live cascade. The lever is the typed bond and the sign of the feedback loop: over the same set of institutions, a dense and a compartmentalized arrangement are different substances, and connection absorbs shocks up to a tipping point and then amplifies them. What a regulator should govern is the arrangement, the reachability of a ground that does not update on the regulator's own reports, the independence of the frames that declare the system safe, and the presence of a seat able to refuse. A passed stress test is a search that found nothing, not proof of absence, and agreement inside one evidence pool is not corroboration. The exact coupling fraction is a declared gap. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
