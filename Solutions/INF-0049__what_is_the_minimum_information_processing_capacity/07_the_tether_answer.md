# The Tether's answer on the minimum information-processing capacity

**Mode: `specified` for the structural verdict, `general` for the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measured threshold. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a minimum, but a minimum compares a system capacity with an environmental demand. It has two terms, so it cannot be read off the system alone. The effect we would have to observe is a loop that keeps closing against a ground as the environment varies: a residual registers, an action is taken, and the outcome is read back. Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric."

The question is also a **union compound**, not one hard problem. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." It must be unbundled into four overlapping conditions before any capacity is named, or any number will seem to fit.

## 2. The shape

1. **The minimum is relational, not intrinsic.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The substrate question (how much capacity is inside the agent) dissolves; the contact question does not. So "N bits for adaptive response" is not a figure-licensed answer.

2. **There is a constitutive minimum of kinds.** Fig 13 :3: "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds." Six terms in two kinds: states (space, time, energy) and structures (spectrum, gradient, network). Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." This is a minimum of substrate, not of throughput, and it is closed: "infinite effort inside a finite reach" (Fig 13 :118).

3. **The loop is per-slot, not aggregated.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A system with a near-zero global residual and one live slot is a legal state. A capacity average that collapses slots throws away the only signal that says where to look.

4. **The chain is ordered by dependency, and the first missing link sets the floor.** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Order: habitat and contact, perception, vocabulary, objective, planning and carry. Capacity added above a broken link cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

5. **A route needs bonds, not just ingredients.** Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A plan is a sequence, a goal is a comparison, progress is a subtraction. An "information store" measured only in items or bits misses the bond term.

6. **Bits, actions and joules are different currencies.** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Landauer's kT ln 2 per bit and Ashby's requisite variety are instruments for the budget and necessary conditions, not witnesses for the verdict.

7. **The framework returns a shape, not a number.** Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The number stays open until a ground, a habitat and an observer with powers of discrimination are named.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal bit count, state count or energy threshold.
- Not that Landauer's bound or requisite variety answers the question. Both are filters.
- Not that "intelligence" is the quantity being minimized. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."
- Not that six terms equal six bits. They are six kinds, indivisible and minimal.
- Not that a synthetic system with N bits demonstrates capability. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not grounded. No measurement in a named habitat settles the residual here.
- Not a partition of four independent conditions. They are a union compound with its own loop.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A measurement in an enumerated habitat, not a further argument. The questions to put to an instrument:

- In a named habitat with a named disturbance set, does a variant whose contact is unchanged but whose internal capacity increases show any gain in adaptive response? If yes, finding 1 breaks (Fig 11 :40).
- Can a global aggregated residual steer adaptation as well as a per-slot residual under the same disturbance? If yes, finding 3 breaks (Fig 1 :19).
- Derive one of the six from the other five, or exhibit a seventh. If yes, finding 2 breaks (Fig 13 :41, :48).
- Show a context-free number N that decides adaptive capacity across habitats. If yes, findings 1 and 7 break.
- Show an ingredient vocabulary taking a route. If yes, finding 5 breaks (Fig 12 :111).
- Show capacity added above a missing dependency link restoring response. If yes, finding 4 breaks.

Requisite variety offers a local necessary test: the regulator needs at least as much variety as the disturbance. It does not tell you which link is broken, and it does not make the regulator good. That still needs the ground.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The stop is at the limit of the instruments available here: no habitat was enumerated in this derivation and no disturbance set was measured. Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip to be taken and the gap measured: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." `11_audit.md` records that gap qualitatively. Fig 13 :134 says plainly: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a number of bits for adaptive response. It says the question is the wrong shape: a minimum compares a system with an environment, so it is a relation, not a property of the system alone. Capability belongs to agent-and-habitat, and an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing. What it offers is a structural minimum with four unbundled conditions: a per-slot residual rather than an average, a loop whose only metric is the ground, a dependency chain whose first missing link sets the floor, and an enumerated habitat. Underneath sits a closed set of six terms in two kinds, with composition only across kinds. Bits, actions and joules are separate currencies and do not add, so Landauer's bound and Ashby's requisite variety are budget instruments, not answers. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The shape comes with six refuters written in advance, and the number stays open until a habitat, a disturbance set and an instrument are named.
