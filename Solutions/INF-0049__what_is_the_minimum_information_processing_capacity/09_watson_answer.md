## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the record's question: what is the minimum information-processing capacity needed for a system to adaptively respond to its environment? Option A: give a universal number (bits, states, joules per bit) and treat it as the answer. Option B: reframe the question as a relation between system and environment, return the structured shape any adaptive system must have, and leave the number open until a ground settles it. What each leads to: A promotes a budget filter to a verdict, ignores the second term of the relation, and spends the independence of anyone asked to check it; B sends a checkable shape, six refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the limit of the instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** What would we have to observe to say a system has enough capacity to respond adaptively as the environment varies? Arity: two terms, a system capacity and an environmental demand, so "minimum" is a relation and not a one-term property. Symmetry: asymmetric, disturbance -> response -> outcome, so the comparison is a threshold relation rather than a symmetric join. Scale: a loop at one scale and a system in a habitat at the next, so a one-scale number does not transfer without a measured round trip. Fig 9 :22 names R by "arity · symmetry · scale."
- **Domain 1, the single-system loop.** Actors: library Γ, belief b, action a, observation o', residual R per slot s, ground. Currency: residual reduction per slot, settled by the ground. Anchor: the ground as the only metric (Fig 1 :7, :23). Legitimate domain, anchor nameable.
- **Domain 2, the minimum viable substrate.** Actors: space, time, energy (states); spectrum, gradient, network (structures); seven bonds and negation. Currency: composition across the two kinds. Anchor: "remove one and something stops" (Fig 13 :48), tested against established laws. Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 3, the capacity chain.** Actors: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. Currency: status per link. Anchor: an instrument at each link (Fig 3 :27). Legitimate domain, and the middle link is where instruments are scarcest.
- **Domain 4, agent and habitat.** Actors: habitat actors, conditions, relations, ground. Currency: contact, with introduction available and exclusion not. Anchor: the ground, which does not update (Fig 11). Legitimate domain.
- **Partition or union.** Union compound, not partition. Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." The four conditions overlap, so a single capacity lever leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Habitat and contact, perception, vocabulary, objective, planning and carry. Substrate underneath all links. Refuse before mint (formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test: Fig 11 (capability relational) and Fig 13 (constitutive minimum) are different quantities, so no strain after re-census. The relation between description bits, action costs and erasure joules is a world-source currency question, named in C3 and not resolved by summation.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed is the dissolved substrate question. The answerable form has a structural shape, and that shape is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so the minimum is a relation rather than an intrinsic bit count. There is a constitutive minimum of six terms in two kinds, composed only across kinds; a per-slot residual rather than an average; an ordered dependency chain whose first missing link sets the floor; and an enumerated habitat. Bonds are required to state a route. Bits, actions and joules are separate currencies and do not add, so Landauer and Ashby are budget filters, not witnesses. Six refuters were written before the conclusion. The numeric value is a genuine gap at the ground, reported as unreached, not as silence.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states six refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is promoting a budget number to a verdict, which sends readers away with a threshold that does not transfer between habitats. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. The shape claim can be refined, the four conditions re-ordered after an instrument test, and a local number added for a named habitat without touching the derivation. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An agent-only change that moves adaptive response with contact fixed would break the relational claim; deriving one of the six from the other five, or exhibiting a seventh, would break the substrate claim; a per-slot-versus-aggregated experiment would break the indexing claim; a context-free capacity number justified by the figures would break the shape claim; an instrument and enumerated habitat yielding the smallest system that keeps the loop closed would move a *local* number toward `grounded`. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
