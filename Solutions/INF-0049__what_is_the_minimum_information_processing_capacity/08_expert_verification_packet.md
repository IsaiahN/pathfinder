# Expert verification packet: the Tether on minimum adaptive capacity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in minimal information processing can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No universal number is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "what is the minimum information-processing capacity needed for adaptive response?" is the substrate question in a different costume: a minimum is a relation between a capacity and an environmental demand, so the answerable form is agent-and-habitat. The framework returns a shape, not a number. The shape has four unbundled conditions: a residual indexed per object slot rather than a global average; a loop with a ground that is the only metric; a dependency chain whose first missing link sets the floor; and a habitat that is enumerated, never composed. Underneath sits a closed minimum set of six terms in two kinds, composed only across kinds. Bits, actions and joules are separate currencies and do not add, so Landauer's bound and Ashby's requisite variety are budget filters, not verdicts. Six refuters were registered before the derivation concluded.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: capability is agent-and-habitat, so a capacity number is not the answer
- **Statement:** no agent-only improvement that leaves contact unchanged can produce adaptive response where none existed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39, "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40, "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** is there a case in your field where an internal-only change (more bits, more states, more parameters) changed adaptive response in a habitat held fixed? If so, this claim is false.
- **Refuter:** an agent-only change with contact held fixed produces adaptive response. Also: an instrument that reads intrinsic intelligence independently of contact and predicts response across habitats.

### C2: the minimum is a structured shape, not a scalar
- **Statement:** the residual must be per-slot; the chain is ordered by dependency; a vocabulary needs bonds to state a route. These three are independent and cannot be summed into one capacity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 3 :20, "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 12 :111, "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." Fig 13 :3, "No system functions without any of them, none is built from the others, and everything else is a composition across the two kinds."
- **What to check:** can a global averaged residual steer adaptation as well as a per-slot one under the same disturbance? Can an ingredient-only vocabulary take a route? Can one of the six terms be derived from the other five?
- **Refuter:** any one of those three demonstrated cases breaks C2 in whole or in part.

### C3: budget numbers are filters, and the number stays open
- **Statement:** Landauer's kT ln 2 per erased bit and Ashby's requisite variety are necessary or cost instruments. Promoting either to a verdict is the named error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 12 :125, "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 13 :133, "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."
- **What to check:** does requisite variety have a sufficient form in your field that settles adaptive success, not just bounds it? Does a physical cost per operation predict whether a system adapts?
- **Refuter:** a context-free number justified by the figures that decides adaptive capacity across all habitats. Until one exists, the verdict stays `candidate`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal bit count, state count or joule threshold.
- Not that the six terms are six bits. They are six kinds.
- Not that Landauer's bound or requisite variety answers the question. Both are filters.
- Not that a synthetic system with N bits demonstrates capability. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- Not that "intelligence" is the quantity minimized. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."
- Not grounded, and not a partition of four independent conditions. They are a union compound.
- Not a claim that this simulation settles the question. It is internally consistent with the figures; that is all.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Contact test:** hold the habitat fixed and change only internal capacity. Any response gain breaks C1.
2. **Aggregation test:** compare a global residual controller with a per-slot controller under the same disturbance. Equal performance breaks C2's first clause.
3. **Bond test:** show an ingredient-only system taking a route to an objective. Breaks C2's third clause.
4. **Substrate test:** derive one of the six from the other five, or exhibit a genuine seventh. Breaks C2's fourth clause.
5. **Number test:** produce a context-free capacity number that decides adaptive success across habitats. Breaks C3.
6. **Ground test:** enumerate a habitat, name the disturbance set and observer powers of discrimination, and measure the smallest system that keeps the loop closed. That would give a *local* number, not a universal one, and would move the verdict toward `grounded` for that habitat only.
