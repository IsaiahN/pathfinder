# What "shape" means for the minimum-capacity question

**Reading status, stated honestly.** Re-opened this session (2026-11-23): *The Library of Babel: Locating the Book of Books*, *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*. The other five of the eight essays are listed in the canonical mapping at `Solutions/QMGR-0037__spacetime_continuum_or_emergent/05_what_shape_means.md`, which I read as a local record; their quotes are not re-verified here and are not used as fresh citations in this folder. Figure lines are from the local verbatim extract and are primary.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can put a number on it. It constrains; it does not fill. "The minimum is a relation with two terms, a per-slot loop, an ordered chain, and an enumerated habitat" is a shape. "The minimum is 17 bits" is a name with no ground behind it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened this session: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." If you decide the answer is a bit count before describing the effect, every bit-count literature will seem to confirm it.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." The effect here is: the loop keeps closing as the environment varies.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A shape is exactly that sort of description.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."

## 3. What licenses a number (or a name)

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." A measurement in a named habitat, with a named disturbance set and a named observer with powers of discrimination, could settle a local minimum. It would not settle a universal one.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Ashby's requisite variety is such an import: it comes from cybernetics, not from the figures. It is recorded as a necessary condition, never as a figure-derived verdict.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the contact condition: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (quoted there as Fig 11 :40). A number that does not change contact changes nothing either.

## 4. Why a universal bit count is the specific forbidden error

Three independent reasons, each stated with its mode:

- **Frame-internal scoring (`general`).** Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." A bit count produced by the system's own description cannot also grade the system's success.
- **Arity (`general`).** The residual has two terms, capacity and demand. A one-term answer has thrown away the second term. This is a structural point, derived before any figure line was chosen (see `01` section 0).
- **Union, not partition (`specified`).** Fig 9 :54-58: "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single capacity lever clears the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

## 5. Q: does the framework forbid naming a number? Answered

**Nothing in the framework licenses a universal numeric minimum.** A local number is licensed only by a ground reading in a named habitat, or by a provenanced import recorded as such. The correct form for this question is the shape plus the four unbundled conditions, with the number left open. `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3 records this as F2.

**Does this folder commit the forbidden error?** No. It states a shape (F1 to F10), pre-registers six refuters, and explicitly refuses to convert the shape into N bits. It does cite Landauer and Ashby, but as budget filters under Fig 9 :60, not as the answer.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach, and the status of this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers prestigious names to agree with a capacity number is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And the limiting sentence: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
