## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the specific bindings do not. Space and time cannot bond directly, so a structure sits in the slot and the slot stays open. Each theory is a first-order derivative of the six-term set; their union is a second-order comparison, not a unified field, and over the shared atom set the cores are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier. The record's `metric tensor = Gradient` binding is unproven (the metric is ordering-like, the connection ∂g is gradient-like), ⋛ is mistyped as a comparison of two structures, the "spacetime" route is refused out of order, and framing a `Specified` record as settled violates its own mode. - **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet is mode-labeled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). - **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement.
