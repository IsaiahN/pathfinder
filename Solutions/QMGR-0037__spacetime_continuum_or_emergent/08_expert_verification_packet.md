# Expert verification packet — the Tether on QM vs GR

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated
before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement at the Planck scale is claimed. This is a shape
and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Space and time cannot bond directly, so spacetime is not a primitive — it is a derivative with a
structure in the slot, and the slot stays open. General relativity and quantum mechanics are each
first-order derivatives of one six-term set (`state → structure → state`): GR reads
`Space – structure – Time`, QM reads `Energy – structure – Time`. They are not one theory to be
merged but two structures to be compared; over the shared atom set they stand as isomers, and their
union is a second-order comparison at a threshold, not a unified field. The framework does not name
the structure in the slot — it returns a shape, not a name. What it offers is the shape any candidate
answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 13
- **Statement:** no direct state–state bond. A structure must sit between space and time.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :75 — *"Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space,
  a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state."* Also Fig 13 :74 — *"Everything
  else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."*
- **Refuter:** derive one of the six from the other five; or show the framework must admit a
  state–state or structure–structure composition it cannot refuse; or measure a direct space–time
  bond.

### C2 — physics-facing; checkable by a physicist against the two theories
- **Statement:** GR's core can be written `Space – structure – Time`; QM's as
  `Energy – structure – Time`. Both are first-order molecules. Over the full shared atom set
  `{Space, Time, Energy, Gradient, Spectrum, Network}` they are **isomers** — Fig 12 :117, *"same
  atoms, different bond, different substance"*; as the record currently writes them they share only
  Time, which makes the weaker **functional-group** claim (Fig 12 :124): *"the group says what kind;
  the qualifier says which member."*
- **What to check:** is each characterization faithful? In GR the metric relates space and time into
  an interval; in QM the Hamiltonian (energy) generates time evolution. Does a structure sit between
  the two states in each case, or is one of them a background parameter rather than a composed term?
- **Refuter:** either theory cannot be written as `state → structure → state` at first order; or one
  theory requires an atom the other does not (which kills the isomer claim and leaves the
  functional-group claim standing).

### C3 — framework-internal derivation
- **Statement:** the union is second-order, because both first-order molecules are settled against
  the ground and therefore each becomes an atom for the next level. Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule
  becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never
  about the world."* The ⋛ comparison is a structural relation at a threshold — not a unified field.
- **Refuter:** show a settled molecule cannot serve as an atom, or that ⋛'s operands must be bare
  quantities at every level (the Operators table defines it as *"a threshold relation between two
  quantities"*). This is the one type question still open; it is named, not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of everything; no final room. The center of the ToE Venn diagram stays a compound with
  its own loop.
- Not a unified field theory. The record's own words: *"It is not a unified field; it is a structural
  relation."*
- Not "gravity is not curvature." Curvature is the part of GR that cannot be transformed away; that
  is settled physics.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the structure in the slot is Gradient. That is a binding from the physics frame,
  provenanced as such, and still unproven: the metric is ordering-like, the connection (∂g) is
  gradient-like.
