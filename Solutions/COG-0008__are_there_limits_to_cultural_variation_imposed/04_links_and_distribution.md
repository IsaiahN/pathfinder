## Wider literature that bears on the question

Three clusters, kept separate because they are different evidence pools:

1. **Human universals and the non-nativist caveat.** Brown's list and the Wikipedia non-nativist section establish that cross-cultural recurrence does not by itself identify a common mechanism; similarity can arise from a common practical problem or from diffusion. This is exactly the source-discrimination problem in F5 and F7.
2. **Bounded species-typical form.** Fixed action patterns and Berlin and Kay's colour-term hierarchy are the two clearest public cases of a species-level regularity whose *form* still varies. Ethology and linguistic anthropology are low overlap with each other, so their agreement on the shape is worth more than two psychology pages saying the same thing.
3. **Discriminators.** Reaction norms, common-garden experiments and measurement invariance are three existing instruments that already return readings. They are the "worse instruments" from which a cultural discriminator is sharpened (Fig 6 :32). The Welzel versus Meuleman disagreement inside the invariance literature is recorded as a live instrument debate, not resolved by the figures.

Overlap declaration: eight of the twelve fetched pages are Wikipedia entries maintained by overlapping editor pools, so they are one broad secondary-reference witness, not eight independent witnesses. Their value here is that they let us quote the primary literature (Brown 1991, Berlin and Kay 1969, Lorenz and Tinbergen, Woltereck 1909, Vandenberg and Lance 2000) with checkable provenance. The figure lines remain primary and decide; these pages corroborate only.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52:** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full derivation before anyone has described what they would count as a failure sends the interior of the frame. Lead with the residual and the refuter; offer links after their verdict is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the architecture before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if a biological bound were present before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 9 :60:** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A list ranked by receptiveness is a budget filter misused as a witness.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Mislabelling a variable universal costs clinical and educational instruments their validity, so independent frames are worth real effort here.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which frames they would keep independent; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The reader checks the residual without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a reader has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The discriminator protocol is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per trait.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted it is a shape and a protocol, not a list; if it is reshaped into a list of universals, it fails F6 and F8 and must be repaired before sending.
