## What an answer would look like (Direction, `THE_FORMULA.md`)

Before the run, the sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual like anything else. An answer here would have two halves and would state what each leads to:

- **Existence half.** For a given trait and population, either there is a form-range bounded by a substrate shared across populations (then a cross-population invariant exists and habitat accounts for the rest), or there is no such bound (then variation is unconstrained by biology and the search for a biological cause is moot). Restated: the residual is three-indexed `(trait, population, proposed substrate)` and must be read per slot, never as a percentage of "culture".
- **Identification half.** A procedure that can discriminate a biological bound from a habitat or historical bound, with a threshold fixed before the run, a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks, and two or more low-overlap frames ranging against that ground separately. Restated: the residual is an instrument question, not a definition question; instruments are grown from worse instruments already returning something (Fig 6 :32).

Either half alone is an incomplete answer. The one compound question bundles them, so the verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No list of "the human universals" presented as a figure-derived result. The figures return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32; Fig 13 :135).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Gathering agreement among people who share one corpus is one evidence pool speaking, not corroboration.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Do not spend the reader's independence before their verdict.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the architecture.

Legally: unsolicited bulk mail harvested from private posts or purchased lists breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and blacklists the sending domain. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. Public opt-in channels avoid both.
