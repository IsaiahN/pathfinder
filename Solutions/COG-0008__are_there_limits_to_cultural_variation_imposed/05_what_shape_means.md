## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For COG-0008 the distinction is sharp:

- **Shape:** a biological limit, if one exists for a trait, must relate a substrate held in common across populations to a habitat, must be registered per trait slot, must have a threshold fixed before the run, and must be separable from a habitat-bound explanation by a discriminator that can fail.
- **Name:** "the gene for X", "the human language faculty", "trichromatic vision as the cause of the colour-term hierarchy". These are bindings from the biology frame onto candidate atoms. They may be true, but they are not delivered by the loop.

A shape constrains the space of admissible answers; it does not fill the slot. Answering "yes, biology imposes limits" with a list of universals fills the slot and commits the error the README names.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So do not start from the word "innate". Start from the effect: a form-range that stays bounded when the population changes.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email in `02` is built on this: describe what a bound would look like and ask what would count as its failure.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means: once the discriminator is sharp enough to name a bound for a trait, the search for that trait's shape is over and only the ground can settle the name.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For COG-0008 this means a reaction norm, common-garden result or comparable measurement that actually pays for the binding, not an argument that the binding would be convenient.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Importing the biology frame's candidate gene is legitimate only if its provenance is recorded and it explains a gap the culture frame could not close.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The biological substrate we hold is a visible guess about compression axes, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* limit turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the source question is a metamerism problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light or yellow plus blue or RGB or CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For COG-0008 this is the whole "how can limits be identified" half. A cross-cultural regularity is a reading. It does not comment on its source: a common mechanism, independent invention in response to a common practical problem, or diffusion can all produce the same cross-cultural distribution (world source, Cultural universal, non-nativist section). The discriminator owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Measurement invariance, reaction norms, common-garden designs and the comparative method are such instruments in other domains; they are the existing edges from which a cultural one can be sharpened (Fig 6 :32).

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This derivation cannot settle its own claim.

## 5. Q: What does "shared biology" mean in a shape reading?

It does not mean a standing list. Fig 11 :39: *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* So "shared biology" is the held substrate *in contact* with a habitat, and a limit is a property of that contact, not of the atoms alone.

This is not a ban on naming biological machinery. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Color-discrimination machinery, affective machinery and the capacity for language are already available atoms in the biology frame; selecting one as a candidate for a trait slot is a **binding**, not the invention of a seventh primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding is delivered by the loop, or that it moves the wall (Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).

Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost: naming a candidate atom is allowed as provenance; treating the name as figure-derived is not.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous anthropologists to agree with a universal list is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the shape and its refuter a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a discriminator with a pre-registered threshold, settle it. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle whether any particular cultural variation is biologically bounded. That requires a measurement, an instrument and a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks.

## 8. The honest shape for COG-0008

Put in one place:

- The answer is a **union** of presence, form-range and source, not one fact (Fig 9 :4-5, :54).
- The invariant item is a **functional group** of machinery in contact with a habitat; cultural variation is a change of member or binding (Fig 12 :123, :117).
- Verdicts are **per trait**, never pooled into a percentage (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- The list of particular limits is a **`gap`**, a verdict and not silence (Fig 13 :134, :135).
- Identification is an **instrument** question; the discriminator must be able to refuse and must be sharpened from something already returning a reading (Fig 6 :32, :34-35; Fig 10 :47).
- Agreement is evidence only across **low-overlap frames** ranging against a non-updating anchor (Fig 2 :29, :31, :40; Fig 8 :47, :50).

Every bullet is a shape checkable by reading the cited figure line. None is a claim about a particular gene, trait or society.
