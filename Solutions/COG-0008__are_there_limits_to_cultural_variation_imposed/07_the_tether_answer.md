# The Tether's answer: biological limits on cultural variation

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts and the identification protocol, `gap` on the list of particular limits. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three conditions that have different anchors and overlap: universal presence, bounded form-range, and source discrimination. Fig 9 :4-5: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

The effect to decide, per trait: does the form-range stay bounded when the population changes, and can a discriminator separate a substrate bound from a habitat bound? The answer is never a percentage of "culture". Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

## 2. The shape, domain by domain

Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

### D1, cross-cultural documentation (`specified`, triangulated)
Measure the trait per slot. A universal presence record and a bounded form-range are different quantities; the first does not imply the second. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The anchor is the published record treated as a measurement, not as agreement (Fig 2 :29). One corpus is one witness (Fig 2 :31).

### D2, human universals (`specified`, triangulated)
A universal is a pinned, open census. "No known exception" is not proof of absence, because no frame is a sealed room. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."* The cell for any named universal is therefore `candidate`, revisable by a new society or a finer trait description.

### D3, shared biology (`specified`, triangulated)
A limit is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of a gene in isolation. Fig 11 :39: *"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The form-bound is a reaction range over environments, not a fixed number. The world literature on reaction norms and fixed action patterns corroborates the shape: species-typical machinery with graded expression, not a rigid one-to-one form.

### D4, identification (`specified` protocol, triangulated)
A limit is identified by a discriminator that can fail:

1. Fix the trait slot and the bound threshold **before** the run. Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*.
2. Sharpen an instrument already returning a reading: cross-cultural measurement invariance, reaction norm, common-garden design, comparative method. Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
3. Range at least two low-overlap frames separately against a ground that does not update on what they think. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*
4. Record which conclusions were convergent and which were imported (Fig 8 :51). Independence is bought, not assumed (Fig 2 :42).
5. Write the refusal condition in advance (Operators :58, a refutation is a negation).

### D5, the framework (`general`, with a `gap`)
The framework returns the shape above. It does not return the identities of the limits. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Naming trichromatic vision or a language faculty as *the* bound for a trait is a binding from the biology frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation. Underivable from this closure is not the same as unreachable at this budget (Fig 8 :40-43): a biological frame may hold the names, but importing them buys no figure-derived verdict without the ground paying for the binding.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of human universals, and not a percentage of cultural variation attributable to biology.
- Not that cross-cultural recurrence proves a common mechanism. Independent invention and diffusion produce the same reading (world source, Cultural universal, non-nativist section); the reading does not comment on the source.
- Not that "culture and biology mutually constitute each other" is a verdict. That frame cannot be ranged against a non-updating ground; it is admitted as a convention about meaning only.
- Not that any named gene or faculty is the bound for any named trait.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried quote, not re-opened this session): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the proposed bound persist across habitats in a reaction norm, or does it move with the environment? An instrument that discriminates these would establish a bound for that slot.
- Does the cross-cultural form difference survive a common-garden or equivalent comparison, or does it collapse when transmission history is held fixed?
- Do two independent frames, one reading the cross-cultural record and one reading the biological substrate, converge without sharing an evidence pool? If they share a corpus or an author, they are one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Do the five refuters in `02` and `08` all fail to fire after a genuine run? Until then the verdict is `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Underivable from this closure, not silence and not a completed answer.** The figures stop where the instruments stop: they can say what a limit would have to look like, what a discriminator must be able to do, and what would reverse the reading. They cannot say which trait slot has one. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README, carried). The list stays a `gap` until a ground pays for a name.

## 6. What would settle it, in one list

1. A reaction norm or common-garden result that shows a form-bound invariant across habitats for a named trait.
2. A cross-cultural measurement-invariance result that shows the same construct is measured in both populations, so the comparison is admissible.
3. Two low-overlap frames converging with recorded independent provenance, not one corpus quoted twice.
4. A completed census with a closed trait-identity criterion for a presence claim, acknowledging that even it is not proof of absence.
5. A specific limit derived and then measured, converting that slot from `gap` to `candidate` and, with a ground payment, toward `grounded`.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer the question with a list of human universals or a percentage of culture explained by biology. It splits the question into presence, bounded form-range and source discrimination, and says their verdicts must be kept separate. Any biological limit, if one exists, is a relation between shared machinery and a habitat, registered per trait, not a standing list of genes; the invariant item is a functional group and cultural variation is a change of member or binding. The framework returns that shape, not the names, so the list of particular limits is a `gap` stated plainly. To identify a limit you sharpen an instrument that already returns a reading, fix the threshold before the run, range at least two independent frames against a ground that does not update on what they think, and write the refusal condition in advance. The center stays open until a discriminator pays for a name.
