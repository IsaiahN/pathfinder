## 0. The residual in effect terms

Question as posed: are there limits to cultural variation imposed by shared human biology, and how can those limits be identified?

Do not answer that as one question. State it as an effect and see what it decides:

> Given a trait form T, a population P, and a proposed shared substrate B, decide (a) whether the range of T across populations is bounded by B in a way that does not change when cultures change, and (b) which procedure could discriminate such a bound from a habitat-bound or historically-bound explanation.

What each option leads to:

- **A biological bound exists.** Then there is a cross-population invariant registered against a ground that does not update on what any frame thinks. Form differences beyond the bound are habitat and history. The claim is per trait. It is not a statement that "culture is mostly biology" or that a trait is "innate" in a standing sense.
- **No biological bound exists.** Then the form-range of T is set by habitat and transmission, no invariant is owed, and the search for the biological cause of T's particular form is moot for that slot.
- **Identification is possible only with a discriminator.** A procedure that cannot return "no bound" is not a discriminator. A threshold fixed after seeing the data is a convention deciding an outcome (Fig 10 :47), not a ground.

Describe R before choosing any frame, per Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* The words "biology", "culture" and "limit" are borrowed; the effect is a bounded form-range plus a discriminating instrument.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale:**

- **Arity.** At the measurement level a limit is a two-place threshold relation between a trait form and a bound. As a research question it is three-indexed `(trait, population, proposed substrate)`, because a bound for one trait in one population is not a bound for another trait (Fig 1 :19). A single global fraction of biological constraint is an aggregation that hides the live signal (Fig 1 :22).
- **Symmetry.** "Imposed by" is ordered, not symmetric: substrate to form, not form to substrate, at the timescales in question. The anchor must not update (Fig 2 :29). A symmetric reading *"culture and biology mutually constitute each other"* is a coherent frame that cannot be ranged against a ground; it is the mutual-update collapse of Fig 2, and is refused as a verdict though admitted as a convention about meaning.
- **Scale.** Three strata are in play: the within-population trait slot, the cross-population comparison (Fig 4 membrane, the same loop at a different size), and the species-typical or comparative frame (Fig 10 :67-69, different actors, currency and ground, lossy transform between). A result at one scale does not compose directly at another without the round trip of Fig 4 :30.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

Each finding states its mode, quotes verbatim figure lines before concluding, and gives a refuter. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and are never averaged.

### F1 : The question is a union compound, not one fact (general, whole question)

Fig 9 :4-5:

> **"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."**

Fig 9 :54:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**

Fig 9 :57-58:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

The nature/nurture phrasing of the question does not shrink with effort because "rule A" works on one subset of traits and "rule B" on another. There are three separable conditions: universal presence (D2), bounded form-range (D1 plus D3), and source discrimination (D4). They overlap: a trait can be universally present, biologically bounded, and still have its particular form set by habitat. One lever, for example one heritability estimate, clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Mode:** `general`, structural. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 split rule and Fig 1 per-slot rule, low overlap between the leave/arrive structure and the agent residual). **Refuter:** demonstrate a single instrument that settles presence, form-range and source for every trait in one measurement, with no residual left in any of the three conditions. **Domain:** whole question; D1 to D4 verdicts depend on it.

### F2 : Limits register per trait slot, never as a global fraction (general to specified per slot)

Fig 1 :19:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**

Fig 1 :22:

> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :28:

> **"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."**

The live question is not "what percentage of cultural variation is biological?" It is, for each trait slot, "does this form-range have a cross-population bound, and does the discriminator fire?" A pooled percentage discards the only signal that says which trait to investigate. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* An effect size is a filter for how much effort to spend on a slot; it is not a witness for a cause.

**Mode:** `general` on the rule, `specified` when applied to a named trait. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 and Fig 9, low overlap). **Refuter:** a law that returns the biological fraction for an arbitrary trait without per-slot measurement, and that reproduces every per-slot verdict. **Domain:** D1.

### F3 : A limit is a property of agent-and-habitat, not a standing list of genes (general)

Fig 11 :39:

> **"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**

Fig 11 :40:

> **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :49:

> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**

Fig 11 :51:

> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Applied to D3: a gene, a receptor or a developmental system imposes a limit only relative to a trait and a habitat. Trichromatic vision bounds colour *discrimination*; it does not by itself bound the number of basic colour *terms*, which varies (world source, Color term, Berlin and Kay 1969 as reported). The same substrate is a bound for one residual and irrelevant to another. A standing list of "the biological constraints on culture" has the same failure as a standing list of good actors: it survives no change of residual. This is not a denial that biology constrains; it is a refusal to name a constraint independent of the trait and habitat it constrains.

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* adds the diachronic point: a substrate that bound a form in one habitat can stop registering when the channel changes, without the ground having moved.

**Mode:** `general`. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 11 habitat rule and Fig 10 channel rule, low overlap). **Refuter:** a trait whose form-bound is invariant across every habitat with no reaction norm, demonstrated by a common-garden or equivalent instrument. That would defeat the agent-and-habitat claim for that slot. **Domain:** D3.

### F4 : "How identified" is an instrument question, and instruments grow from worse instruments (general to specified protocol)

Fig 6 :32:

> **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."**

Fig 6 :34-35:

> **"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."**

Fig 6 :27:

> **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

The identification half does not begin by defining "biological limit" and deriving a test from the definition. It begins with something already returning a reading: cross-cultural measurement-invariance tests already flag when the same construct is not measured across groups; reaction norms already return a form-range for one genotype across environments; common-garden experiments already separate population differences from environment; the comparative method already returns homology versus convergence. Each is sharpened from a worse version of itself. Where nothing at any resolution is returning a reading for a trait, there is no edge to extend and the honest report is `unreachable` at this budget, not silence and not an invented instrument.

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Any compression of a discriminator into a protocol carries a round-trip gap `R_T` that must be measured by taking the protocol back to the concrete trait, not assumed to be zero.

**Mode:** `general` on the instrument rule, `specified` on the protocol in `07` and `08`. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 6 instrument rule and Fig 4 round trip, low overlap). **Refuter:** a working discriminator produced from a definition alone, with no prior instrument returning anything that it sharpened. **Domain:** D4.

### F5 : "Universal" rests on a non-updating census, and a failed search is not proof of absence (general)

Fig 2 :29:

> **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."**

Fig 2 :31:

> **"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."**

Fig 8 :39:

> **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

Fig 9 :44:

> **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."**

Brown's definition, as reported in the world source, is *"those features of culture, society, language, behavior, and psyche for which there are no known exception"*. The phrase "no known exception" is an open census, not a sealed room. The anchor for a universal claim cannot be the agreement of anthropologists reading the same corpus, because that is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31); it must be a published, checkable record with a trait-identity criterion fixed before the search. Even then, finding no exception does not prove there is none. A universal claim is a pinned measurement with a declared openness condition, always revisable by a new society or a finer trait description.

**Mode:** `general`. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 anchor and Fig 8/Fig 9 sealed-room limit, low overlap). **Refuter:** a completed census of all human societies, past and present, with a closed trait-identity criterion that does not move when a frame pushes on it. **Domain:** D2. **Gap note:** the actual list of universals is not derivable from the figures; this finding governs the *shape* of a universal claim, not its content.

### F6 : Naming a particular limit is a binding or import, not a derivation (general; records the central gap)

Fig 9 :32:

> **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

Fig 13 :134:

> **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**

Fig 13 :135:

> **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

Fig 6 :23:

> **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."**

Fig 8 :51:

> **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

The figures can return that a bound, if one exists, must relate a held substrate to a habitat, be registered per trait, and be separated by a capable discriminator. They cannot return *which* gene, receptor or faculty is the bound for *which* trait. Naming trichromatic vision as the limit on colour lexicons is a binding from the biology frame onto a candidate atom; it is licensed by the ground or by a provenanced import with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13, Fig 8 :27), not by the loop. The figures' own canonical rule is a shape, not a name.

This is a `gap`, plainly: the identities of the limits are unreachable from the figures. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence. It is the *underivable from this closure* kind, not necessarily unreachable at this budget; a biological frame may hold the names, but importing them buys no figure-derived verdict without the ground paying for the binding.

**Mode:** `general`. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 9, Fig 13 and Fig 6, three low-overlap routes to the same limit). **Refuter:** derive a specific biological limit for a specific trait from the six-term set and the operators alone, with no import and no measurement. **Domain:** D5 and D3.

### F7 : Agreement across frames counts only when the evidence pools differ (general)

Fig 2 :40:

> **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 2 :42:

> **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

Fig 8 :47:

> **"Independence is one quantity read twice."**

Fig 8 :50:

> **"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."**

Ethnography, psychometrics, molecular genetics, reaction-norm biology and animal behaviour are different closures with different actors, currencies and anchors. Two papers quoting the same review are one witness wearing two names. Convergence, where two frames reach the same atom separately without importing it, is the only strong case; the record must show which happened, because Fig 8 :51 says the contents alone cannot tell. Cost if wrong is high here: a false universal exported into clinical or educational instruments misdiagnoses people (world source, Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan 2010, as reported), so independence should be bought in proportion to that cost.

**Mode:** `general`. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 and Fig 8, low overlap). **Refuter:** demonstrate that two frames declared independent actually draw on the same dataset, author or sample, and that the agreement was counted as two witnesses. **Domain:** D1, D2, D3, D4.

### F8 : The discriminator must be able to refuse; a threshold fixed after the data is a convention deciding an outcome (general to specified)

Fig 10 :47:

> **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**

Fig 10 :42:

> **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**

Fig 10 :52:

> **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."**

Fig 2 :35:

> **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

A pre-registered threshold for what counts as a bound is a convention: it makes the claim statable and has no truth value by itself (Fig 10 :18-19, *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*, *"→ install what can be violated"*). If that threshold is adjusted after seeing the form-range until a bound appears, the convention has decided the outcome and has stopped being a convention. The outside checker must see the residual and the failure condition, not the full derivation; if they can reconstruct the claim from the framework, they have joined the frame and their verification is sound only against the frame's own picture (Fig 10 :50-51). The author's unavoidable interest in the outcome is declared and handled by writing the refuter before the run, not by asserting neutrality (Fig 10 :58-60).

**Mode:** `general` on the rule, `specified` on the pre-registration protocol. **Mark:** `triangulated` (Fig 10 and Fig 2, low overlap). **Refuter:** show a threshold that was changed after data inspection without record or penalty and still treated as pre-registered. **Domain:** D4.

### F9 : Universal machinery and variable form stand as functional group and isomers (specified, structural)

Fig 12 :123:

> **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

Fig 12 :117:

> **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Fig 13 :115:

> **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

Over a fixed set of biological atoms shared by the species, different cultures compose different bonds: habitat, learning, convention, transmission history. The invariant item is the functional group (the kind of machinery: colour discrimination, affective response, language capacity), and variation is the member or binding. Different cultures' colour lexicons are isomers over a shared perceptual atom set: same atoms, different bond, different substance, and the difference is decisive in behaviour even when it is invisible in a formula that lists only the atoms. This is a structural reading of why a universal can coexist with deep variation without reducing that variation to individual differences.

**Mode:** `specified` (structural), ungrounded. **Mark:** `single-source` on the figure side for the isomer definition (Fig 12 only); the world corroboration from colour-term research is a separate witness but shares no figure pool, so the combined reading is `triangulated` at the level of shape. **Refuter:** a cultural form that requires a biological atom the universal machinery does not contain; that defeats the isomer reading and requires an import that moves the wall. **Domain:** D2 and D3.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Fig 10's discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. Committing a prediction before the run is the formula's remedy for the fact that a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

The shape claim is refuted, and the relevant section withdrawn, if any of the following holds:

1. **Union refuter.** A single instrument settles presence, form-range and source for every trait in one measurement, leaving no residual in any of the three conditions (breaks F1).
2. **Slot refuter.** A law returns the biological constraint for an arbitrary trait without per-slot measurement and reproduces every per-slot verdict (breaks F2).
3. **Habitat refuter.** A trait's form-bound is shown invariant across all habitats with no reaction norm, by a single common-garden or equivalent instrument (breaks F3).
4. **Instrument refuter.** A working discriminator is produced from a definition alone, with no prior instrument returning anything that it sharpened (breaks F4).
5. **Census refuter.** A completed census of all human societies with a closed, non-updating trait-identity criterion is produced (breaks F5's openness limit).
6. **Naming refuter.** A specific biological limit for a specific trait is derived from the six-term set and the operators alone, with no import and no measurement (breaks F6 and would convert the `gap` to a derivation).
7. **Independence refuter.** Two frames counted as independent are shown to draw on the same evidence pool (breaks F7 and removes their triangulation mark).
8. **Threshold refuter.** A pre-registered threshold is shown to have been changed after data inspection without record (breaks F8).
9. **Isomer refuter.** A cultural form is shown to require a biological atom the shared machinery does not contain (breaks F9 and requires an import that moves the wall).

If any fires, the figure line it governs is re-censused before the verdict is changed. A conflict sends the analysis back to the domain census; it never flips a verdict by itself. Until one is run, no part of this folder may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Answer the existence half with the union shape and keep the per-trait verdicts separate; answer the identification half with a discriminator protocol that can fail (F1, F2, F8).
2. Record the list of particular limits as a `gap`, plainly and with the reason: the figures return a shape, not a name, and underivable from this closure is different from unreachable at this budget (F6).
3. Treat "shared biology" as agent-and-habitat contact, never as a standing list (F3).
4. Grow the discriminator from instruments already returning readings; sharpen in our own terms inward, and import the biological frame outward only with provenance and a shadow test (F4, F6).
5. Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong: cross-cultural records, psychometric invariance, reaction norms and comparative ethology must be separate frames with separate anchors, and overlap must be recorded (F7).
6. Fig 4 :23: *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Send the protocol and the refuter, not a list of universals.

## 8. Updated verdict

The figures decide the shape and the identification discipline; they do not decide which traits are limited or which genes bound them.

**Existence half.** Yes in shape, unresolved in content. There are limits in the sense that a trait's form-range must, if bounded at all, be bounded by a substrate-in-habitat relation registered per trait; whether any particular trait has such a bound is a ground question, and the list is a `gap`. Presence-universality is a separate condition from a bounded form-range.

**Identification half.** Specified. Identify a limit by (i) fixing the trait slot and the bound threshold before the run, (ii) sharpening a discriminator that already returns a reading (invariance, reaction norm, common garden, comparative method), (iii) ranging at least two low-overlap frames separately against a non-updating anchor, (iv) recording which conclusions were convergent and which were imported, and (v) writing the refusal condition in advance.

**Marks.** `general` on the structural shape, `specified` per domain and on the protocol, `gap` on the list of particular limits, not `grounded`. Every substantive claim in this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
