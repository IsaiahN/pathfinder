## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One pool, one witness.** Kabia et al. 2021, Jalali et al. 2026, the Malawi 2026 survey, Giannouchos et al. 2020 and the WHO 2019 review are all health-systems informal-payments research. They agree on the broad shape: payments persist under weak regulation and low provider pay, they buy speed and access, and they fall hardest on people with least ability to pay. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one pool talking, not five confirmations.

**The economics pool is a second frame, with low overlap in data but a shared question.** The grease/sand literature (Leff, Huntington, Lui, Shleifer and Vishny, Kaufmann and Wei, Campos and Saleh, Aidt, Nur-tegin and Jakee) uses firm surveys, national accounts, and bank records rather than patient outcomes. It splits in a way that maps onto the figure derivation:

- The grease reading: an inefficient bureaucracy is *"a major impediment to economic activity that some 'speed' or 'grease' money may help circumvent"* (BOF summary of Leff, Leys and Huntington, read via search excerpt). This predicts the C1 condition (contact restored) at the level of a transaction.
- The sand reading: Shleifer and Vishny (1993) find *"the illegality of corruption and the need for secrecy make it much more distortionary and costly than its sister activity, taxation"*. This predicts the C2 failure (a standing gate with hidden costs).
- The disaggregated result: Nur-tegin and Jakee (2020) report *"Using aggregate indices to study corruption leads to misleading results"* and *"While certain types of corruption facilitate growth, most types impede it"*. This corroborates Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot, not averaged) and Fig 9 :54-58 (unbundle a union) from a different pool, as one witness.
- The meta-analysis: Campos and Saleh (2010) report 460 estimates from 41 studies, with about 32 percent significantly negative, 62 percent insignificant, and about 6 percent significantly positive (ledger). This is a filter for how contested the pooled question is, not a verdict for any payment (Fig 9 :60).
- The endogenous-harassment warning: Kaufmann and Wei (2000) find *"Firms that pay more in bribes are also likely to spend more, not less, management time with bureaucrats in negotiating regulations."* The AEA summary of Mironov and Zhuravskaya (2016) reaches the same shape with hard data: corrupt municipalities do not promote the most efficient firms, so *"residents there are getting less value for their municipal tax dollars."* Both are the catalyst/cycle claim of Fig 12 :119 and :121 in economic vocabulary.

**Framework-internal essays** are used only for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md`. Their author wrote the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

**No world source settles the question.** Every source reports associations, prevalence, or cross-country correlations. None provides the per-payment, per-residual, outcome-anchored comparison the shape requires. That is a gap at the ground, recorded in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The BMC Health Services Research submission guidelines state: *"a manuscript can only be submitted by an author of the manuscript and may not be submitted by a third party."* SSRN also requires an author account. This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy. U4's helpdesk is the cleanest opt-in route for a research question of this kind; use it for the question, not for the theory.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the figure text and links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
