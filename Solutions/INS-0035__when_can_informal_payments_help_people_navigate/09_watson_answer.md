## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a side payment made to navigate a dysfunctional institution. Option A: the payment is grease that helps the payer. Option B: the payment is a standing gate that deepens exclusion and inefficiency. What each leads to: A taken as a universal verdict ignores that the same payment harms another person with an equal entitlement (Fig 11 :50); B taken as a universal verdict ignores that a payment can restore a blocked entitlement with no downstream gate (Fig 11 :49). The answer needed is a shape: the conditions that separate A from B and an instrument that would discriminate them.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a person's entitlement and what the formal channel delivered, measured per person and per transaction. Arity: two conditions, payer access and system exclusion, so a **union** to be unbundled (Fig 9 :55), not one hard question. Symmetry: the payment is directed from payer to gatekeeper and its repetition changes the gatekeeper's incentives, so the loop has a sign (Fig 12 :121). Scale: first order at the transaction, second order at the side channel across uses, third order at the habitat.
- **Domain 1, the formal transaction.** Actors: seeker, gatekeeper, entitlement. Currency: the formal right, published tariff, queue position, free-service entitlement. Anchor: the delivered service or patient outcome. Nameable, so this is a domain; Fig 2 :29 says an anchor is *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."*
- **Domain 2, the side channel.** Actors: payer and gatekeeper in a repeated relation. Currency: informal money or gift. Anchor: whether contact with the outcome changes (Fig 11 :40). Question type: catalyst versus cycle, a bond question.
- **Domain 3, the habitat.** Actors: other seekers, the poor, regulators, rival providers. Currency: access, trust, aggregate cost. Anchor: counted system outcomes (access rates, delay counts, exclusion counts). Fig 11 :52 supplies the cascade: *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."*
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perceive the residual per slot, then find vocabulary for what is entitled versus discretionary, then state what counts as progress, then choose the side action, then carry what worked with verification. Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*
- **Union, not partition.** C1 (contact restored) and C2 (no standing gate) overlap. A single lever clears the region where only one holds and leaves the intersections standing.

Strain test applied: treating "grease" as helpful by kind strains Fig 11 :49. Re-census sends it back to Domain 1, where the payment is helpful relative to a specific blocked entitlement, not in itself. One live strain remains and is named: the catalyst/cycle test needs a solicitation and repetition instrument that most prevalence studies do not record.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a universal verdict in either direction does not. Valence is relative to the residual, so an informal payment can be a benefactor to one person's blocked entitlement and a malfactor to another's at the same time. The first discriminator is contact with a measured outcome, anchored by a patient outcome or a counted delay, not by opinion. The second is the bond: a spent one-off payment and a solicited, regenerating gate are isomers under the same currency, and an amount-ledger cannot tell them apart. Help versus harm is a union of C1 and C2, to be unbundled and read per slot. "Grease" and "sand" are imports with their own evidence pools, not figure results. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. No real institution's payments are settled here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode and refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recorded conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter. A second cost is acting on a pooled average and thereby missing who is excluded, which Fig 1 :22 names directly.
- **undoable:** yes. The universal readings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent packet can be superseded by the corrected one, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The refuters are written down, so a failure reverses the specific claim rather than the whole frame.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An outcome-anchored comparison showing the same payment sign for every residual would break Fig 11 :49-51; an unchanged measured outcome still claimed as help with a shifting anchor would break Fig 11 :40 and Fig 2 :29; evidence that solicitation does not regenerate access conditions, or that one-off payments reliably become standing gates, would break Fig 12 :119 and :121. A figure re-census that admits a standing list of helpful payments would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
