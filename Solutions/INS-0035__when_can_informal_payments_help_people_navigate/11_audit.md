## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md`: valence is relative, contact with a measured outcome discriminates, spent and regenerating payments are isomers, help and harm are two conditions, no named verdict.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example "the envelope payment in hospital X is harmful because it is corruption" or "the speed payment in office Y is efficient grease."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **instrument for the bond**, whether the payment is solicited, expected, and repeated. Fig 12 :119 says *"nothing records which a term is"*; prevalence studies record amounts, so the concrete claim cannot be settled with the data they collect;
- the **anchor discipline**, that the outcome must not move when payer, provider, and regulator are asked. A concrete claim built on self-reported satisfaction has dropped this;
- the **union split**, that C1 and C2 must be unbundled and read per slot. A concrete claim built on a pooled average has dropped this.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: valence is relative to a measured residual and a spent payment is not a regenerating gate. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
