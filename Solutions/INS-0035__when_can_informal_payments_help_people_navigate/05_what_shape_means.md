## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The local metalaw note quotes Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the case. It constrains, it does not fill. For this question the shape is: "valence is relative to the residual; the discriminator is contact with a measured outcome; the help/harm split is a union of two conditions." A verdict saying "informal payments in hospital X are helpful" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- The same essay on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is Fig 9 :54-57 in the essay's own vocabulary, and it is why F4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` splits the question into C1 and C2 before searching.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality"* and *"This anchor is provided by your own question."* Here the question supplies the anchor as a measured outcome (Fig 2 :29-30), so the search can be steered rather than drifted.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, "grease" and "sand" are names imported from the economics frame. They arrive with their own evidence pools and their own interests. Until a source gate (provenance) and a shadow test (do they explain a gap in this residual?) are run, they are candidates, not the framework's own output. Section 4 of `04_links_and_distribution.md` records the two pools and their overlap.

## 4. The source error and why it matters here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a reported "successful navigation" after a payment is a reading, not a source. Several paths give it: the payment restored an entitlement (C1 holds), the payment bought a private substitute, the provider would have delivered anyway, or the respondent reported what they thought the interviewer wanted. A prevalence survey that records only "paid" and "got service" has the reading and not the source. What is owed is a discriminating instrument (measured outcome, solicitation record, comparison group), not a moral label. The essay's own words: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 5. Q1: Does the framework name the helpful payment? No.

**Answer:** nothing in the framework licenses naming a specific payment as helpful or harmful without a ground reading. The framework returns the conditions (C1, C2) and the discriminator (contact with a measured outcome). A specific verdict requires a source gate or the ground.

**Does this record commit the source error?** Not if it stays at the shape. It would commit the error if it took a self-reported "I got faster care because I paid" and named the source as "grease" without an instrument that separates restoration of entitlement from private substitution or from a provider who would have complied anyway. Section 4 above names that as the live risk, and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` turns it into a checkable sub-claim.

## 6. Q2: Are the discriminating conditions themselves just another name? Correction to an overstatement

A tempting overstatement of the "shape, not a name" rule would say the framework cannot even state contact, solicitation, or repetition as conditions. That is wrong, and the correction is recorded because corrections must stay visible.

The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is something not held in Γ. Contact, cascade, catalyst, and cycle are already held verbatim in the figures (Fig 11 :40, :49-51, :52; Fig 12 :117, :119, :121). Stating them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive and not a claim to have named a missing thing. What remains forbidden is asserting a verdict for a specific institution without a ground. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A petition signed by famous anti-corruption economists would fund the question's visibility; it would not settle it. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried and let a measured outcome settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
