## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how culture changes the allocation of attention over a scene. Option A: culture changes the perceptual substrate, the slots or primitives themselves. Option B: culture changes the inherited weighting `w` and the transmitted vocabulary and objective over a shared substrate. What each leads to: A leads to instruction-resistant difference and a new primitive, with no figure line supporting it; B leads to instruction-sensitive difference, written refuters, and an open slot for the specific practice until a ground settles it. The residual is R_s = |Γ(b,a)_s − o′_s| per object slot s.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: many slots, one residual each. Symmetry: not aggregatable, a live slot vanishes into a global average. Scale: two, one loop inside a perceiver and one across perceivers.
- **Domain 1, individual perception and attention.** Actors: perceiver, object slots, action, observation. Currency: per-slot residual, prediction Γ(b,a), boundary diff. Anchor: the environment and task that actualise o′. Nameable, so this is a domain.
- **Domain 2, cultural inheritance.** Actors: population, shared library, inherited prior, weighting w. Currency: head starts that make the next search cheaper; w_A + w_B = 1. Anchor: the ground contacted by practice, which does not move when a frame changes its mind. Nameable.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: experimenters, tasks, instructions, measurements. Currency: residual under instruction shift. Anchor: measured attention, never agreement. Nameable. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so independence is zero.
- **Union, not partition or average.** All three conditions hold at once for a cultural difference in attention. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, then vocabulary (terms for affordances), then objective (what counts as progress), then planning and carry. Culture enters at vocabulary and objective.

Strain test: Fig 1 governs residual indexing while the Symbols table governs belief composition. Re-census shows different quantities that compose, `b` and `w` feed Γ which generates the per-slot residual, so no strain. - **Perception / ecological psychology.** Wikipedia contributors, "Affordance" (Wikipedia, accessed 2026-11-22), citing Gibson (1979): *"The affordances of the environment are what it offers the animal, what it provides or furnishes, either for good or ill."* Also *"Needs control the perception of affordances (selective attention) and also initiate acts."* Predicts that affordances are relational to the perceiver's intentions, so a different inherited objective changes what is offered; corroborates F3. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

Culture shapes attention through the inherited weighting `w` and the transmitted vocabulary and objective, over a shared per-slot substrate. It does not add slots, does not rewire perception, and does not make a culture's agreement into a verdict. Attention is indexed per slot, belief mixes private history and inherited prior, "important" is the vocabulary/objective link, the ground settles which weightings fit, and the framework returns a shape, not the name of the practice. This is `general` on structure and `specified`, ungrounded on the channel. The specific practice is a `gap` at this budget.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The answer names the channel and writes five refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished theory instead of a characterised residual. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The channel claim is removable without touching the figure census, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` already states it as `specified` with its non-claims. No primitive is withdrawn and no sent claim is required to stand. A superseding packet is enough.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A matched-`w` and matched-vocabulary experiment that still finds a robust slot-allocation difference would break F2. An importance weighting that survives vocabulary removal would break F3. A demonstration that cultural transmission installs a new perceptual primitive would break Fig 4 and Fig 13. An outside frame with a different evidence pool and a real anchor returns a different allocation pattern would count as a witness rather than a mirror. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
