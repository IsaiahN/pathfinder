## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the claim

1. **The instruction-shift result.** Park and Huang report that when older East Asian participants were instructed to attend to the object, a robust object-processing area emerged. This is the world-side version of the figure claim that attention is `w`-driven and objective-driven, not fixed hardware. Use it as corroboration only; it is one pool, not an independent witness.
2. **The replication conflict.** Park and Huang also cite Rayner and colleagues failing to find more context attention in East Asians and failing to find culture differences in processing unusual scene elements. Per the figure rule, a conflict sends the derivation back to the census. Here the re-census reinforces the instruction-sensitive reading and does not flip the verdict. Do not present the world literature as unanimous.

## The two-stage offer, and the optional third

**(1)** Validate the output against a ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the framework that produced it. **(3)** Optionally, reuse the formula on other problems, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode.

- Stage 1 before stage 2 is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
- Stage 2 is opt-in by construction. A recipient's agreement after seeing the theory is never pooled as corroboration, because they then share the evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted: Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem. The formula's "Direction" section says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; each user must write down what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As currently drafted in the question itself, it is only a question, not a testable output. The testable output is the shape claim and its five refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6. Lead with that.
