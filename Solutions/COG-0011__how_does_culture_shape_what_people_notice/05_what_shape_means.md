## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the cause is named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a cultural difference in attention is a difference in the inherited weighting `w` and vocabulary, allocated per slot, ranged against the same ground.
- **Not a shape:** "this practice, in this population, makes people notice that feature." That is a name, and it is owed a measurement or a provenanced import.

## 2. How the shape is reached

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. *"you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the ordinary terms notice, ignore, important, then narrow them into per-slot residual, `w`, affordance vocabulary and objective.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So the residual was described as a slot-allocation effect before any cultural frame was chosen.
- The corpus is enumerated, not composed. Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* For COG-0011 the cascade runs from the attended slot out to the child's caregivers, the daily practices, the language, the task, and the reward structure. Several of these are second- and third-order; a first-order list would miss them.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place."* Here that means the shape is stable once the same channel explains notice, ignore, and important without a new primitive.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things only, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question that means: eye-tracking or matched-task experiments are the ground-side instruments. The cultural-psychology literature is an import, and its provenance must be recorded, not silently treated as a derivation.

## 4. The specific error this question invites: naming the source from the reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered all produce the same reading. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and closes: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: observing that two groups allocate attention differently is a reading. Naming "individualism" or "collectivism" or "schooling" as the source of that reading is the metamerism error when several practices could produce the same allocation. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

This is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` leaves the specific practice open and states refuters instead.

## 5. The transmission channel is a head start, not a recording

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says a compressed message only unpacks for a listener with the right history, and that *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* It names the three parts of that history as context, subtext and pretext, where pretext is *"the history you bring to the words"*. It then maps this to culture directly: *"Culture is a shared backdrop, Collins's collective tacit knowledge. Its members can hold very different mindsets; what they share isn't a set of beliefs but the backdrop they read each other against."*

Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The essay's phrasing matches: *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself."*

So culture shapes attention by supplying the backdrop (the inherited prior and `w`) and the vocabulary that makes the next search cheaper. It does not transmit a fixed list of what to look at, and it does not change the perceiver's slots.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that the shape limit forbids naming *any* part of the mechanism. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs "a **missing** thing"; it limits naming a primitive not held in the library. Here the primitives involved (object slots, belief, `w`, vocabulary, ground) are already in the notation set. What the limit forbids is naming a *new* primitive or a specific cultural cause as if it were derived. Naming the channel is allowed as a `specified` binding; naming the practice is not. Both readings are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Why this audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator."* An LLM *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

Therefore `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** measurements of any cultural group. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 8. Prestige is not action, for this question

The same essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A list of famous cultural-psychology names assembled to agree with the shape claim would be the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against a ground, and let the ground settle it. That is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains only institutional, opt-in venues, and why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for a refutation rather than an endorsement.

## 9. Falsifier for this reading of "shape"

If a figure derivation is produced that names a specific cultural practice as the unique cause of a specific attentional difference, with no ground reading and no provenanced import, then this reading of "shape" is wrong and the canonical limit has been misapplied. Equally, if a source-discriminating experiment shows that the same `w` and vocabulary produce different allocations in different habitats with no other variable, then the channel claim in `01` fails. Both are recorded before the conclusion.
