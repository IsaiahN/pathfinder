## Independent domain census from the residual line alone

I redid the census before using the primary's conclusions, in WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a condition-sequence claim and what an experiment returns. Arity: one slot per condition plus one per interstep carry-over, so n + (n-1) slots. Symmetry: ordered. The swap test decides it, and if swapping changes nothing the object is a conjunction and one term under a symmetric bond. Scale: first order inside a step, second order across the chain, habitat-substitution scale across the bench/nature boundary.
- **Domain 1, chemical reaction frame.** Actors: feedstocks, intermediates, products, energy inputs. Currency: yield and selectivity at one condition. Anchor: analytical measurement. Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 2, geochemical frame.** Actors: environments, cycles, fluxes, conditions. Currency: compatibility of the order with geological constraints. Anchor: the geological record, which does not update on what is convenient. Legitimate domain.
- **Domain 3, experimental simulation frame.** Actors: protocol, apparatus, operator, interventions. Currency: reproduced conditions and the count of discontinuities. Anchor: an experiment aimed at the right step. Legitimate domain, and the one where the question's word "reproduce" actually bites.
- **Domain 4, verification frame.** Actors: reviewers. Currency: agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Legitimate domain with zero independence here.
- **Union.** Chemical tractability, geological coherence and substitution fidelity bind jointly. A single lever clears only the region where one condition is missing. I reached this from Fig 9 :54 to :58, whereas the primary reached it from the three-domain structure in its census. Different starting lines, same boundaries.
- **Order.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Refuse the state term before minting a route term (Formula step 2).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. We used different entry figures: I started at Fig 9 and Fig 3, the primary at Fig 12 and Fig 11. Within one evidence pool that is low overlap between *routes*, but it is still one pool, so the agreement is expected.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :111, Fig 11 :66 and :70, Fig 1 :19, Fig 4 :30 and Fig 9 :55.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a route is not a state; the lab is a substituted habitat; read the residual per step; measure the gap; the verdict belongs to an experiment.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim of the form "this published multi-step protocol reproduces the natural sequence."

**R_T is nonzero.** At least four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **swap test** that decides sequence versus conjunction (Fig 12 :70 to :74), which the shorthand compresses into "ordered" without an operational criterion;
- the **cycle structure** of Fig 12 :121, which "sequence" alone does not carry;
- the **Fig 5 acceptance bargain**, which "a state is not a route" does not carry;
- the **Fig 2 anchor and independence distinction**, which "the verdict belongs to an experiment" carries only if the experiment is aimed at the right step and the checking frames are independent.

One thing survives cleanly: a step result does not answer a route question, and a bench habitat is a substituted one. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
