## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about preserving a prebiotic chemical record in ancient rocks. Option A: name the rocks that preserve such a record and treat an observed signature as evidence. Option B: keep preservation and source discrimination as two conditions of a union, enumerate candidate media as slots, and return the shape an admissible record must have, with per-medium verdicts and refuters written in advance. What each leads to: A names a source from a reading and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives a checkable shape, an open slot until a source-discriminating measurement arrives, and an honest unreachable where no instrument can discriminate. The framework decides only the shape, never the rock. The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run so it could accrue a residual and be refused.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: two conditions, preservation and source discrimination, so a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Symmetry: an isotope profile is ordering without directedness, a mineral reaction front is directedness that can be spent; they are different frames, not two names for one reading. - **Domain 1, the rock record.** Actors: clay and silt, chert, carbonate laminae, banded iron formation, iron sulfides and phosphates, sedimentary organics, meteorite and returned-sample grains. Currency: what survives alteration. Anchor: the specimen and the instrument reading on it, a nameable immovable ground. Union member, preservation condition. This is where ORI-0086 stops.
- **Domain 2, the prebiotic chemistry frame.** Actors: feedstock molecules, mineral surfaces, catalysts, reaction networks. Currency: products and pathways reproducible in a lab. Anchor: laboratory reproduction under stated conditions. Union member, source condition; ORI-0087 owns it. It is a dependency, not the verdict of this run.
- **Domain 3, the instrument and source-discrimination frame.** Actors: mass spectrometer, Raman and laser spectrometer, X-ray lithochemistry, ion microprobe, microscopy. Currency: a reading difference that survives under two source hypotheses. Anchor: the measurement that refuses an attribution. Union overlay; both D1 and D2 must pass through it.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: specialist readers. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Enumerate the habitat before ranking it (Fig 11 :48); settle the instrument reading before composing a timeline (Fig 12 :104); refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). There is no fifth domain: a signature with no host medium and no instrument reading has no anchor and is not admitted yet.

## VERDICT

The verdict comes from the figures, not from the world sources. The shape survives; any name of a single rock does not. The question is a union of preservation and source discrimination, so a list of candidate media is a habitat enumeration and a budget filter, never a verdict. The admissible record is a medium that has held a source-discriminating trace through the alteration cascade and on which an instrument already returning a reading can be sharpened to separate prebiotic from abiotic source. Fine-grained clastic sediment, chert, laminated carbonates, banded iron formation, iron sulfides and phosphates, sedimentary organics and meteorite or returned-sample grains are `candidate` slots with separate verdicts. A reading does not determine its source: ordering is not directedness, sequence is not production, and a lamina is not a fingerprint. No trace found is `unreachable-at-this-budget` or below the union horizon, never `no`. Per domain: D1 `triangulated` for the union split and `single-source` for enumeration; D2 `single-source` as a dependency; D3 `triangulated` for instrument extension; D4 `gap` for corroboration. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, and the framework cannot settle which medium, if any, actually carried the record.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet names its mode, states five refuters before the conclusion and records the field provenance, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of rocks as if it were a verdict, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate bindings are removable without touching the derivation: `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already present them as slots, not conclusions. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A refused binding adds a term and lowers the reach without deleting the record (Fig 6 amendment).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A calibrated source-discriminating measurement on a named specimen would name the slot (R1 or R2); proof that alteration erases the trace in every medium would fire R3 and move the preservation question to `unreachable`; a single criterion that settles both union conditions would fire R5 and dissolve the split; an instrument returning a reading no current primitive can represent would require an import with a source gate and shadow test (R4). Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige, publication count and reader agreement are not such instruments.
