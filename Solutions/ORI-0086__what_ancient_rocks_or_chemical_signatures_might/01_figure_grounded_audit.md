## 0. The question restated in effect terms, before any figure line is chosen

**Question:** What ancient rocks or chemical signatures might preserve evidence of prebiotic chemistry?

**Residual R:** A reader wants a distinction that has survived deep time: between a chemical pattern produced by a prebiotic network and the same pattern produced by abiotic chemistry. What is missing is not a rock. It is a *source-discriminating trace* in a medium that has remained readable after alteration.

**What is being decided:** Which candidate media are admissible as carriers of that trace, and which claims about them are only budget filters. Not decided here: whether any particular specimen actually carries it (no ground reading was taken).

**Sought-for shape, bound as an ordinary term (Formula, Direction):** an admissible record is a medium M such that (a) M is in contact with the residual, (b) M has retained a trace that differs between at least two candidate sources at some resolution, and (c) an instrument already returning a reading on M can be sharpened to that resolution. Binding it makes the preference refusable by the same test as every other term instead of an invisible direction (Fig 5 :62-63: *"a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term"*; Formula, Direction: *"write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else"*).

**Option A:** name rocks and treat any observed signature as evidence. **Option B:** keep per-medium verdicts, require a source-discriminating trace, report `unreachable` where none is available. Option A's cost is a source named from a projection. Option B's cost is an open slot until an instrument settles it.

## 1. R by arity, symmetry, scale, and per-slot status

- **Arity:** two conditions. A medium must *preserve* a trace, and the trace must *discriminate* a source. These are not one hard question. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*; Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The neighbour ORI-0087 owns the discrimination condition. This file owns preservation, and borrows discrimination only as a dependency.
- **Symmetry:** an isotope ratio is an ordering (spectrum-like); a mineral reaction front is directedness (gradient-like); a stratigraphic contact is a relation (network-like). They are not interchangeable readings of one quantity. Agreement between an ordering reading and a directedness reading is meaningful only if the two instruments draw on different evidence (Fig 2 :31-32).
- **Scale:** billions of years of time, bed to grain to molecule in space, depositional through diagenetic to metamorphic in process. Each scale crosses into the next with loss, so the record's lineage is part of the evidence.
- **Never averaged:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Each candidate medium below is a slot with its own verdict. "Rocks preserve evidence" is not a single claim to be scored globally.
- **Support:** `|R⁺_s| > 0` for some slot before the search (Formula step 3). Support exists: the world already returns readings that fail to resolve source (isotopic excursions, laminae, mineral assemblages, organic molecules). This is not a search for a sensor that has never registered anywhere.

## 4. Findings F1 to F10

### F1 - The question is a union compound, not one question (decisive for scope)

Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* and :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*.

The two conditions are preservation (this question, ORI-0086) and source discrimination (ORI-0087). Their intersection is a compound: a signature that is preserved but not discriminating is not evidence, and a discriminating signature that was destroyed is not a record. A single lever that lists rocks cannot clear the intersection because the second condition still binds.

**Refuter:** if a reader can exhibit one criterion that simultaneously settles preservation and source discrimination without reference to a second instrument, the union claim fails and the two questions should be merged. Until then, ORI-0087 is a dependency, not a subcase.

**Verdict D1/D2/D3:** `triangulated` for the union split (Fig 9 plus the Library of Babel essay's own bundle passage, read 2026-11-23), low overlap between the figure and the essay route only if provenance is kept; both are framework-author sources, so treat as one pool with two views.

### F2 - The anchor is the specimen and the reading, not agreement

Fig 2 :29-30 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Seat Map :283 *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*

A claim that a rock preserves a prebiotic signature is settled by a measurement on that rock that does not move when a community discusses it. The NASA 2025 report on the Perseverance "Sapphire Canyon" sample is the world's own demonstration: a potential biosignature was announced with the explicit statement that abiotic explanations *"cannot rule them out"* (Taveau, NASA, 2025), so the reading stayed a candidate until a discriminator arrived. The number of readers did not settle it.

**Refuter:** a case where community agreement alone moved a signature claim from candidate to settled, with no new measurement, would break this finding.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 2 plus Seat Map plus the NASA case), but the figure and seat map are one framework pool; the NASA case is an independent field instance.

### F3 - The habitat must be enumerated from the world, not composed from a list

Fig 11 :48 *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."*

Read off the world, the habitat of this residual is:

- **Actors (media):** fine-grained clastic sediment (clay and silt), silica-chert permineralization, carbonate laminae (stromatolites and thrombolites), banded iron formation, iron sulfides and iron phosphates (greigite, vivianite), sedimentary organic matter, meteorite and returned-sample grains.
- **Conditions:** shallow water, hypersalinity, hydrothermal flux, oxygenation state, burial temperature, metamorphic grade, time.
- **Relations:** cementation, mineral replacement, isotopic exchange, redox reaction fronts, biomagnification-like cascades of alteration, contamination.

Fig 11 :52-53 *"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles"*: a first-order reading of a mineral is not the end of the question. The trace that matters can be three steps out, in the diagenetic history that changed the mineral after deposition. Bennu's parent asteroid is the world's example: *"minerals in the parent asteroid likely formed, dissolved, and reformed over time"* (Gaskill, NASA, 2025).

**Refuter:** a candidate medium with no nameable specimen, no host conditions and no described alteration path is not a domain member. If a reader can name such a medium with an anchor, I add it; until then it is vocabulary, not evidence.

**Verdict:** `single-source` for the figure's enumeration rule; the enumerated items themselves are world-sourced and per-slot, not figure-derived.

### F4 - Instruments are extended from readings already returning something

Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34-35 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

The relevant instruments already exist and already return unresolved readings: stable-isotope mass spectrometry (carbon, nitrogen, sulfur ratios), Raman and luminescence spectroscopy (organic and mineral signatures), X-ray lithochemistry (elemental maps), nanoscale ion microprobes (grain-scale isotope ratios), optical microscopy of laminae. NASA's own FAQ describes rovers feeding samples to instruments that analyse *"according to its mass (using a mass spectrometer), its volatiles (using gas chromatography) or its laser-induced signature (using a laser spectrometer)"* (Ask an Astrobiologist, NASA). The edge to extend from is a reading that currently fails to resolve source. A hypothetical portable "tricorder" is not an instrument in this framework's sense.

**Refuter:** show an instrument built from a pure description with no predecessor returning a reading on the same quantity, and Fig 6's rule fails for this case.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 6 plus NASA instrument practice), with the overlap declared: the NASA pages are secondary and do not draw on the framework.

### F5 - A reading does not determine a source

Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 8 :50-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

Isaiah N's *The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026, read 2026-11-23) states the mechanism in a different field: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The same structure appears in the world literature on stromatolites: *"Finding reliable ways to distinguish between biologically formed and abiotic stromatolites is an active area of research in geology"* (Wikipedia, Stromatolite, accessed 2026-11-23). An isotopic excursion, a lamina, or an iron-sulfide mineral is a projection. It can be produced by more than one pathway. Two instruments reading the same projection through the same literature are one witness, not two.

**Refuter:** an instrument whose reading is one-to-one with source across all known pathways, with published calibration, would break the metamerism constraint for that reading. Then a name is licensed by that instrument, and the slot can close. I would record the calibration as the ground that did it.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 plus the source essay plus the geology source), with the framework-author pool declared high overlap. Field-specific instance is independent.

### F6 - Preservation is a lossy, asymmetric channel

Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :68 *"Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."* Fig 11 :61 *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

The past is not available to be re-run. You can introduce an instrument; you cannot subtract two billion years of diagenesis from a specimen. The channel from a prebiotic network to a rock reading drops information at every crossing, and the round trip from the shape back down to a concrete rock does not reproduce what was sent up. `R_T` is nonzero and was not measured numerically here (see `11_audit.md` §4).

**Refuter:** a specimen whose alteration history is fully reversible by a procedure that reconstructs the original source distribution with calibrated error would set `R_T` to zero for that case. Record the procedure and the calibration; do not assert it from the framework.

**Verdict:** `single-source` for the asymmetry rule (Fig 11); the lossy-transform claim is `triangulated` across Fig 4, Fig 10 and NASA's Bennu alteration report, with cross-field overlap low.

### F7 - Absence of a trace is not absence of the process

Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :135 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

A rock suite that returns no discriminating trace reports: no trace found at this resolution, in these media. It does not report that prebiotic chemistry did not occur. NASA's evidence workshop report states the same in field terms: *"it is unlikely to be unambiguous"* and *"multiple measurements and approaches, spanning disciplines and missions, will be needed to make a convincing claim"* (Meadows et al., arXiv:2210.14293, 2022). Where no instrument registers anything, the correct output is `unreachable-at-this-budget` or `below the union horizon`, not `no`.

**Refuter:** a closed enumeration of all candidate media with a complete instrument set and a proof that no trace exists anywhere would justify `absence`. Fig 8's rule says no frame is a sealed room, so this refuter is written but not expected to fire. It is recorded as a design instrument, not a loop hole.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 8 plus Fig 13 plus the workshop report), low overlap between the figure and the field source.

### F8 - Catalysts and functional groups describe what a signature can fix

Fig 12 :119 *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."*

Mineral surfaces and redox couples in prebiotic chemistry are catalysts in the figure's sense: parts that survive their own use. In a record, the isotope-fractionation pattern fixes the *kind* of process (a functional group), while the host mineral and stratigraphic position fix the *member*. Once a signature is settled against the ground, it becomes an atom for the next level, the geological timeline, and is not re-earned at each reading. This is an analogue from Fig 12's bond theory, not a geochemical law; its mode is `specified`.

**Refuter:** show that the host mineral does not fix a member (same pattern, same kind, indistinguishable members across environments) and the functional-group reading collapses into a kind-only claim. That is a real and common outcome in stromatolite morphology, so the narrower form must be kept visible.

**Verdict:** `single-source` for the bond-theory mapping, with a field instance (stromatolite morphology ambiguity) that conflicts with the strong form and supports the narrow form. Recorded as a strain, not hidden.

### F9 - Per-medium verdicts, never a global average

Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Each medium gets its own line: chert and shale, carbonate laminae, iron formations and sulfides, sedimentary organics, meteorite and returned-sample grains. "On average, ancient rocks preserve signatures" is exactly the aggregation the figures forbid. Likewise, a panel of geochemists agreeing is variation inside a room; the ground votes between rooms.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a global preservation score predicts per-medium outcomes better than per-medium readings would break the per-slot rule. It would have to be measured, not asserted.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 1 plus Fig 7), low overlap between the two figure routes.

### F10 - Filters for budget, witnesses for verdict

Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Seat Map :163 *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*

Ranking candidate media by preservation potential is a filter that decides which specimens get instrument time first. It is not a witness that the winner preserves a prebiotic trace. Selecting expert readers for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their agreement from verdict weight. The public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are chosen for ground visibility and opt-in status, not likely agreement.

**Refuter:** a case where a budget filter was used as the sole evidence for a verdict, and the verdict was later settled by the ground unchanged, would break this finding as a practical rule. The framework rule is structural and would still stand; the practical claim is the one at risk.

**Verdict:** `triangulated` (Fig 9 plus Seat Map), same framework pool; declared.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287)

The shape claim in `README.md` is refused if any of the following fires:

1. **Source collapse (R1).** A candidate medium whose reading is identical under a prebiotic pathway and an abiotic pathway at every resolution any existing instrument can reach, with a calibration showing the identity. The medium is reported as non-discriminating, not as absent.
2. **Attribution reversal (R2).** An independent source-discriminating instrument reverses the attribution of a signature previously read as prebiotic. The binding is refused, the record keeps both terms, and the reach falls because a refusing term was added (Fig 6 amendment: nothing is deleted).
3. **Cascade erasure (R3).** A demonstration that alteration removes the discriminating trace in every candidate medium at every accessible resolution. The preservation question is `unreachable`, reported as a verdict, not as silence.
4. **New axis (R4).** A residual that no current primitive can represent, with an instrument returning a reading no current frame can hold. That requires an import with a source gate and shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8), not more search at the same reach.
5. **Union failure (R5).** A single criterion that settles preservation and discrimination together without a second instrument. The union split is withdrawn and the two questions merge.

These were written before the findings were consolidated. A failure is more useful to me than an endorsement.

## 10. Consolidated verdict per domain (never averaged)

- **D1 rock record:** `specified`, candidate per medium. Fine-grained clastic sediment, chert, carbonate laminae, iron formations and sulfides, and sedimentary organics are admissible slots because they are in contact with the residual and already return readings. No slot is settled. `single-source` for the figure enumeration rule; each world claim is separately sourced.
- **D2 prebiotic chemistry:** `specified`, dependency. It supplies the source hypotheses, not the verdict. `triangulated` with D3 only through laboratory reproduction.
- **D3 instruments:** `specified`, triangulated. Extension from existing readings, not from a description. Source discrimination is the open condition and belongs to ORI-0087.
- **D4 verification:** `gap` for corroboration. A simulated second seat shares this run's evidence pool; its agreement is internal consistency, not a witness (Fig 2 :40).

The honest stop: the shape is *a medium that has held a source-discriminating trace and that an existing instrument can sharpen to read*. Which medium, if any, actually did so is left to the ground.
