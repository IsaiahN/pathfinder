## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and stops at the limit of the available instruments, recording the lineage because *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is therefore not "chert" or "stromatolite" or "carbon isotopes". It is the constraint an answer must satisfy: *a medium that has held a source-discriminating trace and on which an existing instrument can sharpen a reading to separate prebiotic from abiotic source.*

- `chert and shale preserve prebiotic chemistry` is a name with a verdict attached, not a shape.
- `a medium must have retained a trace that differs between at least two source hypotheses at some resolution` is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* gives the bootstrap: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is close to the residual: lamina, isotopic ratio, mineral assemblage, alteration, source. Each world source adds a term, and the space narrows toward a rule rather than a specimen.
- *Library of Babel* gives the union warning: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the reason ORI-0086 splits preservation from ORI-0087's source discrimination before searching.
- Fig 6 :30 and Fig 9 :30-31 require effect terms: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* and *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The shape is stated as an effect (a discriminating reading survives), not as a cause (a particular pathway operated).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is the outreach form of the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** A calibrated, source-discriminating measurement on a named specimen. *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). A laboratory reproduction of the exact conditions implied by a host rock is such an import, provided its provenance is recorded.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. A list of candidate rocks is the same kind of visible guess: useful as a habitat enumeration, not a discovery of nature's own list.

The same essay names the harder import: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* If a residual requires an axis none of the current instruments can represent, adding another rock to the list does not help. R4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 is the refuter for that case.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* is the essay that bears most directly here. Its metamerism case: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* It adds: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the remedy is not an argument but *"the arrival of a better instrument"*.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the same structure in a more useful contrast. The colour case: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* The gemstone case: *"In the gemstone case, provenance is recoverable: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so if you have a tool that can perceive the trace, you regain distinction."*

This is the entire answer to the temptation to answer this question with either extreme:

- "Nothing can be known from ancient rocks" is wrong. Some traces discriminate, and instruments can be improved from ones already returning readings (Fig 6 :32).
- "This isotope ratio proves life" is wrong. The reading does not determine the source; several pathways can produce it, and the full set of pathways is not known to be closed.

So the correct unit of verdict is the *medium plus the discriminating instrument*, per slot, never "rocks" in general (Fig 1 :19).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does "shape" mean here?** It means the narrowed form of an admissible record, statable before any particular rock is named and checkable against the world by someone who does not hold the framework. Concretely: a host in contact with the residual, a trace retained through the alteration cascade, and an instrument already returning a reading that can be sharpened enough to separate two source hypotheses. **Which** host, **which** trace and **which** resolution are not given by the framework. They are bindings from geology and chemistry, provenanced as such.

**Does this derivation commit the forbidden error?** It would if it named "chert" or "stromatolites" as *the* answer. `07_the_tether_answer.md` names them only as enumerated habitat members with per-slot `candidate` status, and keeps the source-discriminating condition open. The correct canonical form is the shape, with the list as a budget filter under Fig 9 :60.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A panel of respected origin-of-life researchers agreeing that a rock "looks biological" is the named collapse, not corroboration.

**Separation is the resource.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* The useful outside frame is a geochemist whose closure differs from the framework's, reached through a public opt-in venue, not a list selected for likely agreement (Seat Map :163).

**A reply is a conversation, never corroboration.** If a specialist returns a refutation, it is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated as if it came from any other source.

## 7. Simulation caveat

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says LLM output is *"simulacra"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"*. *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

This file, and the whole solution folder, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a measurement, not a ground reading, and **cannot settle** which ancient rocks, if any, preserve evidence of prebiotic chemistry. Every substantive claim carries a mode, and the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 is the instrument by which a real reader could begin to settle it.
