## 2. What in the wider literature bears directly on this question

1. **The preservation medium is already named in the field.** NASA's Perseverance release says clay and silt are *"excellent preservers of past microbial life"* and that the measured minerals *"can be generated abiotically"*. That single page holds both halves of the union: a good host is not a discriminator. Cite it when explaining why a list of rocks is a filter, not a verdict.
2. **Deep time degrades the record.** NASA's Episode 63 says the samples are *"billions of years old"* and the evidence *"is often degraded and difficult to detect"*. This is the channel-loss claim of Fig 10 :24 without using the framework's vocabulary.
3. **Some traces do discriminate, and that is case-dependent.** "How Intelligence Actually Works" gives the gemstone contrast: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so a tool that perceives the trace regains distinction. That is the strongest reason not to answer this question with a blanket "nothing can be known": the honest claim is that discrimination must be shown per medium, not assumed or denied in general.
4. **The field itself demands multiple approaches.** The Biosignatures Standards workshop report says a convincing claim will need *"multiple measurements and approaches, spanning disciplines and missions"*. This is a field-facing analogue of triangulation, and it is a low-overlap witness relative to the framework essays.
5. **Morphology alone is contested.** Wikipedia's stromatolite entry states that finding reliable ways to separate biologically formed from abiotic stromatolites is *"an active area of research in geology"*. Keep the functional-group claim narrow accordingly.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. The eight essays share an author with the figures, so their agreement is expected and carries little independent weight; the NASA and arXiv sources are the low-overlap material.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 -** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before a specialist has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 -** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 -** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape claim were true before offering the article.
- **Fig 9 :60 -** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A candidate-rock ranking decides instrument time, not truth.
- **Seat Map :163 -** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 8 :39 -** *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Do not let a null result in one medium be read as a verdict about all media.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, pre-registered refuter. No source material, no request for agreement or trust.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** the four links. The figures are the authority. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3, opt-in:** the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it, because the framework says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted, it is a shape and a dependency map, not a name of a rock. If a reader supplies R1 or R2 from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, the claim is refused and a correction is recorded rather than quietly rewritten.
