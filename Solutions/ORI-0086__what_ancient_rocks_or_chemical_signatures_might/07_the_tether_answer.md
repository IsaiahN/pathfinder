# The Tether's answer: which ancient rocks or chemical signatures might preserve a prebiotic record

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and the per-medium candidate verdicts, `general` on the structural constraints. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No specimen was measured and no instrument was run. Every figure line quoted below is verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`.

## 1. The residual it answers

A reader wants a distinction that survived deep time: between a pattern made by prebiotic chemistry and the same pattern made by abiotic chemistry. What is missing is not a list of rocks but a *source-discriminating trace in a surviving medium*. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"): preservation (this question, ORI-0086) and source discrimination (ORI-0087) are two conditions whose intersection is a specific compound. Each candidate medium is a slot, never part of an average (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). The sought-for shape was bound as an ordinary term before the run so it could accrue a residual and be refused like any other claim (Formula, Direction; Fig 5 :62-63: "A frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term.").

## 2. The shape

1. **The admissible record has three properties.** It is a medium in contact with the residual, it has retained a trace that differs between at least two source hypotheses at some resolution, and an instrument already returning a reading can be sharpened to that resolution. This is a shape, not a name, because the framework returns "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32) and stops at the instrument limit, recording the lineage.
2. **The habitat is enumerated from the world, not composed from a preference.** Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." Read off the world, the candidate slots are fine-grained clastic sediment (clay and silt), silica chert with permineralization, carbonate laminae (stromatolites and thrombolites), banded iron formation, iron sulfides and phosphates (greigite, vivianite), sedimentary organic matter, and meteorite or returned-sample grains. NASA's own report says clay and silt are "excellent preservers of past microbial life" while the minerals "can be generated abiotically" (Taveau, NASA, 2025). These are `candidate` slots and budget filters, not verdicts (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
3. **A reading does not determine a source.** An isotope ratio is ordering without directedness (Fig 13 :20: "ordering, so more and less can be said"); a mineral reaction front is directedness that can be spent (Fig 13 :46-47: "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."). Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." The source essay states it plainly: "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The gemstone case in *How Intelligence Actually Works* is the counterweight: "In the gemstone case, provenance is recoverable: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so if you have a tool that can perceive the trace, you regain distinction." So discrimination is case by case, not a blanket yes or no.
4. **Alteration is an asymmetric, lossy channel.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." You can add an instrument; you cannot remove two billion years of diagenesis. The round trip from the shape down to a named specimen carries a gap `R_T` that must be measured, not set to zero (Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").
5. **Absence is not a verdict of absence.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A null result is reported per medium at a stated resolution, as `unreachable-at-this-budget` or below the union horizon, never as `no`.
6. **The arrangement is the substance.** Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Same inventory, different arrangement (lamina versus reaction front) is the isomer-shaped question worth testing. Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Mineral surfaces are persistent actors, which is why a mineral-hosted record accumulates anything at all. The analogy is a shape constraint, not a geochemical law (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named rock proves prebiotic chemistry. Every host is an enumerated `candidate` with a falsifier, not a conclusion.
- Not that an isotope excursion, a lamina, greigite or vivianite is biological. Each can be produced abiotically; the field's own wording is "potential biosignature", with "we cannot rule them out".
- Not that no trace means no prebiotic chemistry. Null results are `unreachable-at-this-budget` or below the union horizon, never `no`.
- Not a single unified question. Preservation and source discrimination are a union; ORI-0087 owns the second condition.
- Not a seventh primitive or a new bond. The field terms are bindings from geology and chemistry with provenance, not additions to the six.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R,φ)` for any candidate.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument on a named specimen, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads a colour "cannot comment on the source"; the case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here:

- **R1, source collapse:** a calibrated demonstration that a candidate's reading is identical under a prebiotic and an abiotic pathway at every accessible resolution. The medium is non-discriminating, reported per slot.
- **R2, attribution reversal:** an independent instrument reverses a prior prebiotic attribution. The binding is refused; nothing is deleted, the reach falls because a refusing term was added (Fig 6 amendment).
- **R3, cascade erasure:** alteration removes the trace in every candidate medium at every accessible resolution. The preservation question is `unreachable`, not `no`.
- **R4, new axis:** an instrument returns a reading no current primitive can represent. That needs an import with a source gate and shadow test, not more search at the same reach (Fig 6 :32; Fig 8 :39-43).
- **R5, union failure:** one criterion settles preservation and discrimination together. The split is withdrawn.
- **Standing field test:** the Biosignatures Standards workshop report says a convincing claim needs "multiple measurements and approaches, spanning disciplines and missions" (Meadows et al., 2022). That is the ground-facing form of per-slot triangulation.

The full pre-registered set is in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5. It was written before the findings were consolidated, so a failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The instruments already return unresolved readings, so there is an edge to extend from (Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."). What is not available here is a ground reading; this is a simulated derivation. The six-term substrate is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*), not nature's own list, and a public contact list is a contact-cost filter, never a witness. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Fig 9 :60 adds the budget filter: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a rock that proves prebiotic chemistry. It says the question is a union of two conditions, preservation and source discrimination, so a list of ancient rocks is a budget filter rather than a verdict. The shape an admissible record must have is a medium that has held a source-discriminating trace through the alteration cascade and on which an instrument already returning a reading can be sharpened to separate a prebiotic source from an abiotic one. Fine-grained sediment, chert, laminated carbonates, iron formations and sulfides, sedimentary organics and returned-sample grains are candidate slots, each with its own verdict and its own refuter. A reading does not determine a source: isotope ratios order, reaction fronts have direction, and a lamina is a sequence while a produced mineral is a new substance. No trace found is reported as unreachable at this resolution, never as absence. What would settle it is a calibrated, source-discriminating measurement on a named specimen. Until one fires, the answer stays a shape with falsifiers written in advance, not a result.
