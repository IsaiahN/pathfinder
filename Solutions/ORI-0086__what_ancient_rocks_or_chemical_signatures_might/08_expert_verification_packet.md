## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question of which ancient rocks or chemical signatures might preserve prebiotic chemistry is a union of two conditions, preservation and source discrimination, not one hard question, so a list of rocks is a budget filter rather than a verdict. The shape an admissible record must have is: a medium in contact with the residual, which has retained a trace that differs between at least two source hypotheses at some resolution, and on which an instrument already returning a reading can be sharpened to that resolution. The framework does not name the medium. Fine-grained clastic sediment, chert, laminated carbonates, banded iron formation, iron sulfides and phosphates, sedimentary organics and meteorite or returned-sample grains are enumerated candidate slots, each with its own refuter. A reading does not determine a source: an isotope profile orders without directedness, a mineral reaction front has directedness that can be spent, a lamina is a sequence and a newly formed mineral is a production. No trace found is reported as unreachable at a stated resolution, never as absence.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9, 11 and 1

- **Statement:** the residual is a union compound and must be split before search; the media are enumerated from the world; verdicts stay per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." and :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"; Fig 11 :48 "You do not invent the list. - **What to check:** whether preservation and source discrimination really are two conditions in the reader's field, or whether one test already clears the intersection in practice. If one test does, the union split is wrong here.
- **Refuter:** exhibit one criterion that settles preservation and source discrimination together without a second instrument (the union split fails), or a candidate medium with an anchor that was omitted from the enumeration (the habitat list is incomplete; amend it and keep the shape).

### C2 - field-facing; checkable by a specialist against known media and instruments

- **Statement:** the candidate slots are real preservers but non-unique sources; the instrument edge exists already and must be sharpened, not invented.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :34-35 "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."
- **What to check:** is it true that clay and silt are excellent preservers of past microbial life while the associated iron minerals can be generated abiotically? NASA's Perseverance release says exactly that: "the formation's sedimentary rocks are composed of clay and silt, which, on Earth, are excellent preservers of past microbial life" and "The minerals also can be generated abiotically, or without the presence of life" (Taveau, NASA, 2025). Is it true that existing instruments already return unresolved readings? NASA's Ask an Astrobiologist FAQ describes mass spectrometry, gas chromatography and laser spectrometry on rover samples. A hypothetical portable tricorder is not an instrument in this framework's sense.
- **Refuter:** a calibrated demonstration that one of the listed media gives an identical reading under prebiotic and abiotic pathways at every accessible resolution (R1), or that alteration erases the discriminating trace in every listed medium (R3). Both are answered per medium, not globally.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a field instance

- **Statement:** a reading does not determine a source; ordering and directedness are different frames; the arrangement is the substance; a null result is an honest stop, not an absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."; Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."; Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** the source essay's metamerism claim, "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source", against the counter-instance in *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "In the gemstone case, provenance is recoverable: isotope ratios genuinely differ, so if you have a tool that can perceive the trace, you regain distinction." Do these not contradict each other? They do not: the first says a reading alone is insufficient, the second says a *discriminating* trace plus a tool can settle the source. The distinction is the whole claim.
- **Refuter:** an isotope profile shown to carry directedness on its own, or two media with the same inventory and different arrangement returning indistinguishable discriminating information at every resolution. Then Q2 or Q1 of `06_isomers_and_order.md` is withdrawn. A closed enumeration with a complete instrument set and a proof of no trace anywhere would license `absence` instead of the honest stop; Fig 8 :39 says no frame is a sealed room, so this refuter is written but not expected to fire.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named rock proves prebiotic chemistry. Every host is a `candidate` slot.
- Not that an isotope ratio, a lamina, greigite or vivianite is biological. The field's own wording is "potential biosignature", with "we cannot rule them out".
- Not that no trace means no prebiotic chemistry. Null results are `unreachable-at-this-budget` or below the union horizon, never `no`.
- Not a single unified question. ORI-0087 owns source discrimination; this packet owns preservation.
- Not a seventh primitive, a new bond, or an import that moves the wall. The field terms are bindings with provenance.
- Not `grounded`. The strongest world statements are explicitly provisional, and this packet was compiled without a measurement.
- Not a consensus claim. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Seat Map :283: "Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."

## 5. What would settle it

A calibrated, source-discriminating measurement on a named specimen, plus an independent line from a different closure. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the colour case was settled "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here that means:

1. R1 or R2 as defined above and in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5, fired per medium.
2. R3, cascade erasure, reported as `unreachable` if it fires.
3. R4, a new axis requiring an import with a source gate and shadow test.
4. R5, a single criterion that clears both union conditions, which withdraws the split.
5. The field's own standard: "multiple measurements and approaches, spanning disciplines and missions" (Meadows et al., 2022).

Agreement from readers is not any of these. Prestige funds an action and cannot settle one (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, 2026).
