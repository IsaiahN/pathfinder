## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before consulting the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Arity: two conditions, preservation and source discrimination, so a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Symmetry: an isotope profile is ordering (more or less fractionated) while a mineral reaction front is directedness that can be spent; these are different frames, and treating them as two readings of one quantity would be a category error (Fig 13 :35-36: "adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."). Scale: billions of years of time, bed to grain to molecule in space, depositional through diagenetic to metamorphic in process.
- **Domain 1, the rock record.** Actors: fine-grained clastic sediment, chert, laminated carbonates, iron formations and sulfides, sedimentary organics, meteorite and returned-sample grains. Currency: what survives transport, burial and alteration. Anchor: a named specimen plus a reading on it, nameable and immovable by agreement. Question type: the preservation member of the union. Per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").
- **Domain 2, the prebiotic chemistry frame.** Actors: feedstock molecules, mineral surfaces, catalysts, reaction networks. Currency: laboratory reproduction under stated conditions. Anchor: the reproduction that supplies a source hypothesis. Question type: the source member of the union, an outside frame relative to Domain 1 (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs.").
- **Domain 3, the instrument frame.** Actors: isotope mass spectrometry, Raman and laser spectroscopy, X-ray lithochemistry, ion microprobe, microscopy. Currency: a reading difference that survives under two source hypotheses. Anchor: the measurement that refuses an attribution. Question type: an overlay through which both union members must pass; instruments are extended, not invented (Fig 6 :32 "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something.").
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: specialist readers and public venues. Currency: residuals and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: a checking arrangement, not a witness set. In this simulated run its independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before selecting a frame (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"); enumerate the habitat before ranking it (Fig 11 :48); sharpen a reading already returning something before importing an instrument; settle a reading before composing it into a timeline (Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it."). There is no fifth domain: a signature with no host medium and no instrument reading has no anchor.

My starting points differed from the primary's. The primary entered through the union split and the habitat figure (Fig 9, Fig 11); I entered through per-slot indexing and closure (Fig 1, Fig 6). Different entry routes, same four domain boundaries, which is weak agreement inside one evidence pool, not independent confirmation.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Formula step 6 defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :54 and :57 (union compound), Fig 11 :48 (enumerate the habitat), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 6 :32 (instrument extension) and Fig 8 :39 (no proof of absence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the README and `07_the_tether_answer.md`: a medium in contact with the residual, holding a source-discriminating trace, readable by an instrument that can be sharpened.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete candidate, for example laminated chert from a named formation with a reported carbon-isotope excursion.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The shape says every medium gets its own verdict; the concretisation tends to become a statement about rocks in general, which Fig 1 :22 warns against.
- **The union condition.** The shape requires a discriminating trace as well as preservation; the concretisation tends to treat the preserved excursion alone as evidence, which reinstates the condition ORI-0087 owns.
- **Provenance.** The shape requires a record of where the reading and the binding came from (Fig 8 :50-52); the concretisation tends to report the measurement without its route, which is exactly the convergent-import ambiguity.
- **The honest stop.** The shape says a null result is unreachable at a stated resolution (Fig 13 :134); the concretisation tends to read it as absence of prebiotic chemistry (Fig 8 :39).

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must be in contact with the residual, must have held a trace, and must be readable by an instrument that already returns something. That residue is worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for a specimen in this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the shape as identical to the named rock would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
