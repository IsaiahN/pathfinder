## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when a work arrangement (office, hybrid, fully remote) improves productivity and when it weakens learning and collaboration. Option A: treat the question as one variable and report which mode wins. Option B: treat it as a union of conditions indexed by slot and report the shape each slot requires. What each leads to: A sends a global verdict that averages away the live signal and cannot explain why one study finds a gain and another a loss; B sends two separate verdicts, each with its own instrument and its own refuter, and stops at a shape rather than a day count.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The divergence of outcomes under one arrangement across slots. Arity: four-place (arrangement, slot, organization Γ, realized outcome), ordered in time. Symmetry: not symmetric; "improves" is a threshold against a baseline, not a symmetric join. Scale: individual loop, team population, organization across quarters. Fig 1 indexes R per slot and forbids aggregation. The question is a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, individual focus loop.** Actors: worker, their Γ, their instruments. Currency: output per unit time and residual reduction on independent tasks. Anchor: a realized output counter on the task. This is a partition-boundary question about which tasks can be counted.
- **Domain 2, team habitat / contact field.** Actors: teammates, newcomers, mentors, cross-team peers. Currency: knowledge crossed, competence transferred, new bridges. Anchor: demonstrated new competence and observed new cross-team work. Question type: habitat substitution, not subtraction.
- **Domain 3, channel / perception.** Actors: metrics, dashboards, customer contact, percepts. Currency: fidelity of contact with what pays. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay while the channel does. Question type: instrument discrimination.
- **Domain 4, closure / reach.** Actors: the team's library and other frames (teams, disciplines, entrants). Currency: reach expansion through contact with a differing closure. Anchor: ground settlement of a new term. Question type: import and room selection.
- **X1 "Culture" as free-floating property.** No anchor independent of measured behaviour, so not a domain yet. **X2 "Productivity" as one scalar.** Measurable but conflates D1 and D3, so absorbed.
- **Order by dependency.** C1 (channel to ground maintained) before C2 (habitat reproduced and reach renewed), because C2's instrument is itself a reading; refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a single mode verdict does not. Effects are per slot. A work mode changes a habitat, so the question is which contacts changed and whether the task's goal required them. Productivity improves on independent, countable slots when the substituted habitat preserves what the goal needed and the channel to a paying ground stays reachable. Learning and collaboration weaken when the substituted habitat silently drops tacit transfer, observation or cross-group bridges, and a handbook carries context but not the pretext of practice. Hybrid can concentrate the cost when attendance is scattered and the shared channel is spent. The office-day count is a `gap`, not a name: the framework returns a shape, and the available trials explicitly decline to generalize below three office days. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a global verdict instead of a per-slot generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The global verdicts are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An independent per-slot output counter that survives replacing the management metric would name the productivity source; a ground competence bar reached by remote newcomers without observed mentoring would break the isolation consequence; two years of new cross-team bridges under hybrid with no differing closure would break the room verdict; and a single lever restoring both conditions with no residue would collapse the union. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
