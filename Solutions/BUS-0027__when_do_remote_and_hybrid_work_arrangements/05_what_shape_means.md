## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 says "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 says the system "can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For BUS-0027, "keep the channel to the paying ground reachable and reproduce the habitat the goal required" is a shape. "Three office days and two home days" is a name. The distinction is not academic: Bloom et al. 2024 explicitly decline to generalize the three-day result to once-a-week-or-less office attendance, which is exactly the difference between a shape and a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." For this question the starting vocabulary was "productivity", "learning", "collaboration"; the closer terms found by the census were "slot", "habitat", "channel", "closure", "valence".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the outreach rule and also the rule for stating a shape: say what you would observe if the condition held, not what mechanism you believe produced it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." In this question the finish line is the two conditions in `01` section 1.3.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* was not opened this session, so its "new dimension of the space" line is not used here. The point stands from the README alone: declaring primitives does not make them neutral, and turning a visible choice into an invisible one is the named failure.

## 4. Why a single productivity score is the forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". One reading can come from several sources. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And the resolution was instrumental: "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

A productivity dashboard reads output but cannot say whether the source was focus, presenteeism, a saturated metric, or an unsustainable intensification of work. A satisfaction survey reads mood but cannot say whether the source was flexibility or the absence of correction and challenge. What is owed is a discriminator per slot, not a better argument and not a blended index (Fig 1 :22).

## 5. The tacit-knowledge bridge, and a correction

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says tacit knowledge is "knowledge that is difficult to extract or articulate", learned "not only through language but also by observation, imitation, and practice". It says "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." And it says "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (Fig 11 :40).

**Correction to my own reading.** I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any concrete statement about arrangements. It does not. The README's phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like", that is, a primitive not held in Γ. Slot, habitat, channel and closure are already in the figure vocabulary. Naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a seventh. What remains forbidden is naming the office-day count that optimizes every team, because no held instrument discriminates it. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

In this field the prestige leak is unusual: it runs both ways. A famous executive declaring remote work a productivity disaster, or a famous platform declaring it the future, is prestige standing in for proof. The same essay says "You may all agree and be wrong." A campaign that gathers agreement from people who already prefer one arrangement is the named collapse, not corroboration.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The business literature is reported as what those authors found, not as something this simulation produced.
