# Expert verification packet - the Tether on remote and hybrid work

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher of remote work or organizations can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement in this folder settles the claim. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The effect of a work arrangement is indexed by slot, not by the organization as a whole. A mode change is a change of habitat, so its effect is the change in contact, not a property of the person alone. On a slot whose goal is independent, codifiable and countable, an arrangement can improve measured output when the substituted habitat preserves what that goal needed and the channel to the paying ground stays reachable. On a slot whose goal requires tacit transfer, observation of an expert, or cross-group bridges, the same arrangement can weaken learning and collaboration because isolation substitutes a habitat and what was not reproduced is invisible until the goal fails. A single firm-wide average destroys both signals. The framework returns the conditions and their falsifiers, not an office-day count.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 11

- **Statement:** effects are per slot, and capability belongs to the arrangement-and-habitat, not the arrangement alone. A global verdict of "remote work is productive" is inadmissible.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."
- **Refuter:** a controlled case in which a team's measured performance is unchanged across a habitat substitution that demonstrably removes the ground's payment for what it does, or a single averaged statistic that predicts prospectively and per slot which team will lose contact next.

### C2 - workplace-facing; checkable against the empirical literature

- **Statement:** the learning and collaboration cost is a habitat-substitution failure, not a decay of the ground. The silent failures are the relations that were required but not reproduced, and the new actors unintentionally introduced.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
- **What to check:** Bloom, Han and Liang (Nature 2024) excluded interns and probationary rookies because on-site learning and mentoring matter for them, so the trial's null on performance and promotions is silent on the learning slot. Yang et al. (Nature Human Behaviour 2022) found firm-wide remote work made the network "more static and siloed, with fewer bridges between disparate parts." Gibbs et al. (Scientific Reports 2024) found hybrid idea quantity fell "particularly in teams with high variation in office presence." Are those characterizations faithful? Are they the same failure class?
- **Refuter:** a remote-only team whose newcomers match a co-located control on a ground competence measure while mentoring and expert observation are documented as absent. If it holds, the learning consequence is false.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a workplace test

- **Statement:** repeated closure stops progress, and reach expands only through contact with a closure that differs. A shared channel is a catalyst that can be spent when attendance is scattered.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 12 :119 "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
- **Refuter:** a hybrid team that gains new cross-team bridges over two years with no import from a differing closure; or a fully remote team whose innovation quantity and quality match co-located controls with no substitute channel. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that remote work is good or bad in general, and not a day count.
- Not that employee satisfaction, self-assessed productivity or a dashboard is the ground. Fig 2 :29-30: an anchor must not move when pushed.
- Not that a written procedure transfers a skill, and not that a test harness proves capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not that "hybrid is the best of both worlds". Gibbs et al. 2024 is a warning against that reading.
- Not that the three workplace studies agree independently. They overlap in the workplace-research pool, so they count as one witness at most (Fig 2 :31, :40).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was measured in this folder.

## 5. What would settle it

A per-slot ground counter and an instrument that discriminates sources, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading a result "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the case.

1. Does the output gain survive replacing the management metric with an independent per-slot counter? If not, the gain was a saturated metric (Fig 10 :26).
2. Do newcomers reach a demonstrated competence bar on a fixed schedule, with observation and correction either present or explicitly substituted? The trend in correction size is the discriminator.
3. Does the collaboration network gain new cross-group bridges over two years, or does agreement compound inside the same room (Fig 7 :12-13)?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22, Fig 11 :39, :66, :68 and Fig 7 :36, six short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge: does the three-study record (retention null, siloed network, hybrid idea penalty) describe one failure class or three unrelated things?
3. Name one per-slot ground instrument that would discriminate "quiet focus" from "saturated metric" on your own team, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
