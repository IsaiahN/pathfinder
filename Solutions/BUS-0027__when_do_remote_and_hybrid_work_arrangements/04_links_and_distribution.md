## Three things in the world literature that bear directly on the framing

1. **A null on one slot is not a null on all slots.** Bloom et al. 2024 report no effect on performance grades or promotions for experienced graduates under two home days, while their Methods explicitly exclude probationary rookies because on-site learning and mentoring matter for them. Their Discussion declines to generalize to once-a-week-or-less office attendance. The study therefore cannot support a universal "remote work is fine" or "remote work is harmful" claim. Cite it for the per-slot shape, not as a blanket verdict.
2. **Hybrid can concentrate a cost rather than split a difference.** Gibbs et al. 2024 find idea quantity fell in hybrid but not fully remote, with the sharpest drops in teams whose office attendance varied most. Yang et al. 2022 find fewer bridges and more siloed networks. These are different instruments (idea portal, communication telemetry) in different firms, so they count as two reports of the same failure class and one evidence pool at most.
3. **The productivity gain has a source.** CTrip's 13 percent came from more minutes and a quieter home environment, while promotions fell nearly 50 percent. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading output "cannot comment on the source." A per-slot ground counter is the discriminator, not a better argument.

## The two-stage offer

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the output against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read the theory that produced it, **(3)** use the formula recursively on other problems.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The formula says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user writes down what would refute their result before running it, and labels the mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if stage 1 survives. The output survives as a shape with an honest stop, not as a day count. Do not strengthen it into "hybrid is best" on contact.
