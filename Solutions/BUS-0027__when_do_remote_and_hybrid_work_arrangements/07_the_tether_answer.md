# The Tether's answer on remote and hybrid work

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

One arrangement produces different outcomes on different slots: output can rise while learning and cross-group collaboration fall, and a firm-wide average hides both. The question is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). Each condition has its own loop and its own instrument.

## 2. The shape

1. **The effect is per slot, never a firm-wide average.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A single "productivity score" has already thrown away the signal that says where to look.

2. **A work mode changes a habitat, not an isolated worker.** Fig 11 :39 "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." So the question "which mode is better" is malformed; the answerable question is which contacts changed and whether the task's goal required them.

3. **Productivity improves when the goal is independent and countable, and the substituted habitat keeps what that goal needed.** On CTrip's call-centre slot, working from home raised measured performance by 13 percent, with more minutes and a quieter environment cited as sources (NBER Digest 2013). On Trip.com's experienced graduate roles, two home days left performance grades and promotions unchanged while cutting attrition by a third (Bloom et al. 2024). Both are `specified` world reports, not ground settlements of the framework. The framework's own contribution is the condition, not the percentage: an arrangement improves a slot when the residual on that slot shrinks against a ground counter, not against a management metric (Fig 10 :24-26).

4. **Learning and collaboration weaken when the substituted habitat silently drops the relations the goal required.** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." Mentoring, observation of an expert, and weak cross-group ties are relations, not properties of a building. Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A bigger handbook sends a recording, not a method.

5. **Hybrid can be worse than either pure mode when the channel is spent.** Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced." Gibbs et al. 2024 found idea quantity fell under hybrid but not fully remote, with the sharpest drop in teams whose office attendance varied most. Yang et al. 2022 found fewer bridges and more siloed networks. The framework's condition: reach expands only on contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23), and agreement inside a repeated closure compounds without evidence (Fig 7 :13).

6. **The valence of a mode is relative to the slot.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Quiet is a benefactor to focus and a malfactor to apprenticeship. Nothing in the phrase "work from home" records which.

7. **The answer stops at a shape, not a day count.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The framework returns the conditions an arrangement must satisfy on each slot; it cannot name the office-day number that optimizes every team. Bloom et al. explicitly decline to generalize their three-office-day result to once-a-week-or-less attendance. That is a `gap`, not a silence.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "remote work is productive" and not "remote work destroys collaboration". Both are global verdicts over slots.
- Not that satisfaction is a ground. Satisfaction moves when pushed (Fig 2 :29-30); it is an instrument.
- Not that a written procedure transfers a skill, and not that a test harness proves capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not a universal hybrid prescription. Gibbs et al. 2024 is a direct warning against "best of both worlds".
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this folder settles `left(R,φ)` for any organization.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A per-slot ground counter and an instrument that discriminates sources, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading a result "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not a better argument, resolved the case.

- **Output slot:** does the gain survive replacing the management metric with an independent counter measured per slot (revenue, accepted work, defect rate)? If not, the "productivity gain" was a saturated metric (Fig 10 :26).
- **Learning slot:** do newcomers reach a demonstrated competence bar on a fixed schedule, with the required observation and correction relations either present or explicitly substituted? The size of the correction trend, not the count of training sessions, is the discriminator.
- **Collaboration slot:** does the network gain new cross-group bridges over two years, or does agreement simply compound inside the same room (Fig 7 :12-13)?
- **Standing falsifiers:** the three in `01` section 4, plus the two figure-level ones (Fig 6 idempotence and Fig 1 per-slot indexing).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The habitat is enumerated from the world, not composed from the framework (Fig 11 :43-44), and the anchor is the thing that pays, not the thing that is easy to count. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." No world source was allowed to flip a verdict: the two apparent conflicts (CTrip's gain versus Fig 11 :39, and hybrid's penalty versus Fig 8 :45) were returned to the census and re-mapped.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winner between office, hybrid and home. It says the effect is indexed by slot, so a single productivity number hides both the gain and the loss. A work mode is a change of habitat, not an improvement to a worker in isolation: a home day can shrink the residual on independent, countable tasks by removing interruption, while the same day can grow the residual on learning and cross-group work by silently dropping the mentoring, observation and weak ties that those goals required. A written procedure carries context and not the history that makes a compressed instruction mean something, so onboarding needs two-way correction, not a bigger handbook. Hybrid can concentrate the cost when a team's office attendance is scattered and the shared channel is spent. What the framework offers is the shape an answer must have, the conditions that must hold on each slot, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The office-day count stays open until the ground settles it.
