# Expert verification packet, QMGR2-0068

**What this is:** a claim in a form a gravitational-wave or compact-object physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement at the source-discrimination level is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Any observable that distinguishes a black hole from another compact object must satisfy four conditions: it must be a ground-side measurement that does not move when a frame changes its mind (Fig 2 :29); it must be indexed per object and never averaged into a population score (Fig 1 :19); it must already be returned by an instrument that fails to resolve the classes (Fig 6 :32-35); and it must constrain the hidden source rather than only the projection (Fig 10 :52). Readings of that shape exist, so the question is not whether information is available but which reading discriminates. Distinguishing is an ordered threshold comparison at a stated precision, not a categorical identity proof. The figures name no specific observable; mass, spin, ringdown modes, shadow diameter and tidal deformability are bindings from the physics frame with provenance. The single reading that separates every compact-object class at every mass and spin is not in hand and is reported as a `gap`.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 6

- **Statement:** a distinguishing observable must be a ground-side, per-slot reading that an existing instrument already returns in a form that fails to resolve the classes.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34-35 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
- **Refuter:** a proposed discriminator whose value is set by convention or prior rather than by a reading; or a demonstration that every candidate reading is a pipeline artifact with no ground contact, so no instrument returns anything that fails to resolve.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a compact-object physicist

- **Statement:** reported discriminations are threshold comparisons at stated precision, and a Kerr-consistent reading is not a source identification.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :86 *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 12 :87 *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*; Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*; Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
- **What to check:** GW250114 reports the dominant quadrupolar mode and its first overtone matching the Kerr prediction to tens of percent and the quadrupolar frequency to within a few percent. EHT reports an image *"consistent with expectations for the shadow of a Kerr black hole as predicted by general relativity."* Are these threshold consistency statements, or do the papers claim categorical source identity? Is there any source-discriminating reading among them, i.e. one that an exotic compact object could not reproduce?
- **Refuter:** a class identity inferred with no threshold and no residual; or a demonstration that a single projection uniquely determines the hidden class with no other source able to produce it.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a physics-facing type strain

- **Statement:** the classes form a union, so a discriminator against one class leaves the others standing; and the specific discriminators form a functional group that becomes an isomer claim only over the full shared atom set.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*; Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*; Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*
- **Refuter:** one lever that separates black holes from all exotic compact objects at once with no categorically different residue; or a demonstration that the candidate observables are not expressible as a mass plus a class-sensitive structural reading.
- **Named strain:** ⋛ is defined over two quantities while this claim compares two classes through their readings. This is a level shift, recorded, not smoothed. A re-census that forbids the shift would remove C2's bond type.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the figures derive mass, spin, ringdown modes, shadow diameter or tidal deformability. Those are imports from the physics frame with provenance (Fig 8 :50).
- Not that "consistent with Kerr" proves the object is a black hole.
- Not that agreement among analyses of the same strain is corroboration. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- Not that SNR, false-alarm rate or sigma settles the class. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that the absence of a found discriminator proves none exists. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment in this run.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument:

1. An instrument returning per-slot readings whose distributions for the black-hole class and an exotic-compact-object class do not overlap at any resolution.
2. A reading that constrains the hidden constitution rather than only the projection, breaking the metamerism named in C2.
3. A demonstration that the existing candidate readings are ground-contacted and not pipeline artifacts.
4. The absence case: a proof that every candidate reading is many-to-one onto all classes at every resolution would refute the shape claim outright.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29, Fig 6 :32 and :34-35, Fig 10 :52, Fig 12 :86-87 and :94 (short passages).
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: are the GW250114 and EHT statements threshold consistency results, or source identifications? Do any of the readings discriminate an exotic compact object?
