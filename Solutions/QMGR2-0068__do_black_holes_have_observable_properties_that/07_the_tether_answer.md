# The Tether's answer on black hole observables

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An observer points an instrument at an object-slot and gets a reading. The question asks whether a reading exists whose value on black-hole slots and on other compact-object slots has a gap at some resolution, so the residual under the wrong hypothesis persists and does not trend down. R is per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The question is a union of two conditions: is any class-separating reading reachable, and does that reading discriminate the hidden class (Fig 9 :57).

## 2. The shape

1. **A distinguishing observable must be a ground-side anchor, indexed per slot.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Consensus, nomenclature and model priors are not anchors. The reading is.
2. **The proto-instrument already exists.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Black-hole candidates already register on gravitational-wave detectors and on event-horizon-scale VLBI, so condition C1 is satisfied. This is reachable, not a blank-field gap.
3. **Distinguishing is an ordered comparison at a threshold, not an identity proof.** Fig 12 :86: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* The result is a class separation at stated precision, as the GW250114 analysis reports constraints *"to tens of percent"* on the modes and *"to within a few percent"* on the quadrupolar frequency.
4. **A reading does not name its source.** *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the instrument *"cannot comment on the source"*. Kerr-consistent ringdown and Kerr-consistent shadow are projections, not source identifications. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
5. **The specific observables are provenanced bindings, not figure derivations.** Mass, spin, ringdown modes, shadow diameter and tidal deformability come from the physics frame. Under Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*, the figures name none of them.
6. **The single categorical discriminator is a `gap`.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* No figure line, and no source read, supplies an observable that separates every compact-object class at every mass and spin. The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterisable in effect terms but not held.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures prove a black hole "really is" a black hole. They constrain the shape of a distinguishing reading; the ground settles any name.
- Not that "consistent with Kerr" is "identified as Kerr". The first is a threshold reading at stated precision; the second would require a source-discriminating instrument.
- Not that agreement among analyses settles anything. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Several ringdown analyses of one strain are one witness.
- Not that SNR or significance is a verdict. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that absence of a found discriminator proves none exists. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not grounded. No measurement in this simulated run settles `left(R,phi)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. Concretely:

- **Class-separation test:** an instrument returning per-slot readings whose distributions for the black-hole class and at least one exotic-compact-object class do not overlap at any resolution, with the missing thing named only after it is held.
- **Source-discrimination test:** a reading that constrains the hidden constitution rather than only the projection, so the metamerism of section 2.4 is broken.
- **Reachability test:** a demonstration that the existing candidate readings are ground-contacted and not pipeline artifacts (links 1 and 3 of Fig 3).
- **Standing falsifiers from the record:** a discriminator fixed by convention (breaks Fig 2 :29); a proof that all readings are many-to-one at every resolution (breaks Fig 6 :27); two "independent" confirmations sharing one strain pool (breaks Fig 2 :40); a source name assigned from a single projection (breaks Fig 10 :52).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instrument list is a visible choice, and the six-term set of the framework is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (*They're Scanning Books*, 2026-09-06). Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish the classes and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 13 :139: *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a black-hole signature, and it does not claim the final classification of compact objects. It says that any distinguishing observable must be a ground-side, per object-by-object reading that an instrument already returns and that constrains the hidden class, and that readings of that shape exist: waveform phasing, ringdown modes, a VLBI shadow, and tidal deformability. It refuses to turn "consistent with Kerr" into "proven to be a black hole", because a reading does not name its source. The single observable that would separate black holes from every exotic compact object at every mass and spin is not in hand, and the framework reports that as a gap with the missing instrument described in effect terms. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the gap. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
