## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the set of conditions a distinguishing observable must satisfy before any particular observable is named. It is: ground-side, per object-slot, already returning something that fails to resolve on an instrument, and source-discriminating. That shape constrains; it does not fill. "A class-separating reading on a detector" is a shape. "The l=m=2 ringdown overtone ratio" is a name from the physics frame.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* In this question the starting vocabulary is "mass", "spin", "spectrum", "shadow", borrowed from the physics frame.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the residual as "a reading that does not overlap between classes", not as "the presence of an event horizon", which is a cause-level name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (2026-11-10): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is exactly the outreach rule for a shape claim about observables: send what a reader would see on an instrument, not the framework's interior.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place."* For this question that would mean the vocabulary of discriminating readings is complete only when no new reading would narrow the class set further. It has not happened: the spectroscopy review is explicitly forward looking (Berti et al. 2026).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An instrument that returns non-overlapping readings for two classes settles the name.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming tidal deformability or shadow diameter is an import from the physics frame.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (2026-09-06) sharpens the second point and is directly relevant to black hole observables: *"Every instrument is a crystallized hypothesis about what is worth measuring, and the hypothesis comes from human awareness."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* So the list of observables is a visible choice, and the choice is allowed only if its provenance is recorded.

## 4. Why "consistent with" is not "identified as"

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (2026-08-28) gives the mechanism, and it bears directly on this question. A colour instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The reading does not determine the source. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*, and the resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a Kerr-consistent ringdown and a Kerr-consistent shadow are readings, not source identifications. Several proposed compact objects are constructed to mimic them. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (echoes, overtones, tidal response, or something not yet built), not a firmer assertion of the name. This is the deepest reason the categorical discriminator is a `gap` in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8.

## 5. Q1 and Q2 answered

**Q1: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming mass, spin or shadow diameter?** No, and this corrects a possible overreading. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Gamma. Mass, spin, ringdown modes, shadow and tidal deformability are all already held in the physics frame. Naming them is a binding from that frame onto a reading we can already make. What is forbidden is (a) claiming the figures derived the specific list, (b) claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6: IMPORT adds an atom, and this one adds none), and (c) naming the hidden source from a single projection (section 4). Triangulated: README plus Fig 9 :32 plus Fig 6.

**Q2: do the figures say black holes are distinguishable?** In the `specified` shape sense, yes: the conditions for a distinguishing observable are figure-derived and are satisfied by existing instruments. In the categorical sense, no: which reading separates every class at every resolution is not figure-derived and is reported as `gap`. Both halves are recorded; neither is quietly dropped.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (2026-11-19): *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that black holes are distinguishable is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape claim against a ground.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the physics and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
