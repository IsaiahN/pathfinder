# WATSON answer, QMGR2-0068

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No measurement was taken in this run.

## QUESTION

Do black holes have observable properties that could distinguish them from other compact objects?

Restated as an effect: does an instrument reading O exist whose value on the black-hole slots and on the other compact-object slots has a gap at some resolution, so that the residual R under the wrong hypothesis persists and does not trend down? R is indexed per object-slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the measurement, named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A commits to a ground-side class-separating reading; Option B commits to the much stronger claim that all readings are many-to-one onto the classes at every resolution.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** Arity: a discrimination relation between an object class and an instrument reading, many-way because several compact-object classes compete. Symmetry: "distinguish" is an ordered comparison, a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :86), not a symmetric join; swapping the classes changes the statement. Scale: single object-slot, event, population catalog, and the model frame spanning them; the membrane from reading to catalog is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).
- **D1 Instrument / ground.** Actors: interferometers, VLBI arrays, X-ray and gamma instruments, the object-slot. Currency: readings o' and per-slot residuals R. Anchor: the measurement that does not update on what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 Object / source.** Actors: the compact object and its hidden constitution (horizon, material surface, exotic state). Currency: mass, spin, tidal response, mode spectrum, shadow diameter. Anchor: not nameable from one reading; becomes anchorable only when an instrument discriminates. Provisional until discriminated.
- **D3 Model / frame.** Actors: GR, modified gravity, equations of state, waveform templates Gamma. Currency: predictions and leftovers left(R,phi). Anchor: the ground through settlement, never internal agreement. Admitted.
- **D4 Verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers and venue editors. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Admitted; independence is zero in this simulated run because every seat reads the same evidence pool.

**Union, not partition.** C1 (is any class-separating reading reachable) and C2 (does that reading discriminate the hidden class) are two conditions, so a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). D2 itself is a union over several compact-object classes and must be unbundled (Fig 9 :55). **Order by dependency:** perceive whether an instrument returns anything that fails to resolve, then name the readings, then state what counts as a separation, then choose the probe, then carry the result (Fig 3 :20). Strain test: ⋛ is defined over quantities while the question compares classes; the comparison is performed through the quantities each class returns, recorded as a level strain, not smoothed.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The shape survives; the specific names are provenanced bindings; the categorical discriminator is a `gap`.** A distinguishing observable must be a ground-side anchor, indexed per object-slot, already returning something on an instrument that fails to resolve, and source-discriminating. Readings of that shape exist: waveform phasing gives mass and spin, ringdown gives quasinormal modes, VLBI gives a shadow diameter, and inspiral phase gives tidal deformability. The figures name none of these; the physics frame supplies them, with source gate. Discriminations are threshold statements at stated precision, never categorical identity proofs. The single observable that would separate a black hole from every proposed exotic compact object at all masses and spins is not held and is reported as `gap`, with a described missing instrument. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, kept per domain and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The corrected packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is presenting frame-internal agreement or a budget filter as a ground-side discriminator, which Fig 2 :40 prices as a disqualified verdict. That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43, read with the Symbols table). The shape and its four falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument returning non-overlapping readings for the black-hole class and an exotic-compact-object class would close the `gap`; a proof that every candidate reading is many-to-one onto all classes at every resolution (Option B) would refute the shape; a demonstration that the candidate readings are pipeline artifacts with no ground contact would break D1; or a figure re-census that permits a categorical class comparison without quantities at that level would clear the recorded strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
