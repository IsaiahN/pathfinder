## 0. The question in effect terms (residual)

The question asks whether an observable exists that separates black holes from other compact objects. Stated as a residual, not as a taxonomy:

> An instrument is pointed at object-slot s. The frame predicts Gamma(b,a) for that slot under the hypothesis "black hole" and under the hypothesis "other compact object". The ground returns o'. R = |Gamma(b,a) - o'| is carried per slot, never averaged: Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A distinguishing observable is a reading O whose distribution over the black-hole slots and over the other slots has a gap at some resolution, so that R under the wrong hypothesis persists and does not trend down.

Three consequences before any figure line is chosen:

1. The thing being tested is the **reading**, not the label. Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."* Whether someone calls the object a black hole is frame-internal; what the detector returns is the metric.
2. The question is plural. "Other compact objects" names neutron stars, white dwarfs, and proposed exotic compact objects. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The bundle must be unbundled before a search (see section 1).
3. The sought-for shape must be an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"). Bind it here: **a distinguishing observable is (i) a ground-side anchor that does not move when a frame changes its mind, (ii) indexed per object-slot, (iii) already returning something on an instrument that fails to resolve, and (iv) source-discriminating, meaning the reading constrains which hidden class produced it.** Until this is bound, it is a preference, not a term, and nothing in the loop can check it.

What each option leads to: Option A (a distinguishing observable exists) commits us to finding a ground-side quantity with a class gap and to naming instruments that read it. Option B (none exists) commits us to showing that every candidate reading is many-to-one onto the classes at all resolutions, which is a much stronger claim than "we have not found one yet" (Fig 6 :27, Fig 8 :39).

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: a discrimination relation between an object class and an instrument reading, effectively many-way because several compact-object classes compete. Symmetry: "distinguish" is a comparison, a threshold relation between two quantities (Fig 12 :86, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; test at Fig 12 :87, *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*). It is an **ordered** bond, not a symmetric join: which class gives the larger value matters, and swapping the classes changes the statement. Scale: a single object-slot at the detector; an event; a population catalog across events; and a model frame that spans all of them. The membrane between a single reading and a population inference is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).

**One line per domain. Actors, currency, anchor. No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D1 Instrument / ground | detectors (interferometers, VLBI arrays, X-ray and gamma instruments), the object-slot | readings o' and per-slot residuals R | the measurement that does not update on what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29) | admitted |
| D2 Object / source | the compact object and its hidden constitution (horizon, material surface, exotic state) | mass, spin, tidal response, mode spectrum, shadow | not nameable from one reading alone; becomes anchorable only when an instrument discriminates | provisional until discriminated |
| D3 Model / frame | GR, modified gravity, equations of state, waveform templates Gamma | predictions and leftovers left(R,phi) | the ground, through settlement, never internal agreement | admitted |
| D4 Verification / outreach | expert readers, venue editors | agreement and residuals | the ground, never agreement | admitted; independence zero in this simulated run |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. Two conditions must both hold: C1, a reading exists that is reachable at all (an instrument returning something that fails to resolve, D1); C2, that reading discriminates the hidden class (D2 through D3). Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* D2 itself is a union over several compact-object classes; unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :55, Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Order: 1 perception, does any instrument already return something that fails to resolve (Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*): 2 vocabulary, do we have terms for the readings (mass, spin, tidal deformability, quasinormal frequency, shadow diameter): 3 objective, what would count as a separation (non-overlapping readings at stated confidence): 4 planning, which instrument and which probe: 5 learn and carry, the catalog and the public data release. A reading taken below a broken link is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23), so we must not ask "which observable wins" before asking whether an instrument returns anything.

Strain test applied during census: the comparison glyph ⋛ is defined over two quantities. Applying it to two *classes* is a level shift, not a category error, because a class comparison is performed through the quantities each class returns on an instrument. Named as a strain in section 7 rather than smoothed.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union and must be unbundled (structural, decisive for method)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The Library of Babel essay (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02) names the same failure: *"your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

Split into C1 (is any class-separating reading reachable at all) and C2 (does that reading discriminate the hidden source). A single lever applied to the bundle clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections (Fig 9 :56). Refuter: show one observable that closes both conditions at once with no remaining compound.

### F2 - A distinguishing observable must be a ground-side anchor, indexed per slot

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :23: *"The ground enters as the only metric."*

World check (secondary): the candidate discriminators are all instrument readings, mass and spin from waveform phasing (LIGO/Virgo 2016), ringdown quasinormal frequencies (LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA 2026), shadow diameter from VLBI (EHT 2019), and tidal deformability from inspiral phase (De et al. 2018). None is a vote or a convention. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 1 and Fig 2, low overlap: slot indexing versus anchor rule). Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for the binding. Refuter: a proposed discriminator whose value is fixed by editorial convention or model prior rather than by a reading.

### F3 - Reachability: the proto-instrument already exists (decisive against "no information")

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :33: *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*

World check: black-hole candidates already return failing readings on several instruments. GW150914 matched the GR inspiral-merger-ringdown waveform (LIGO/Virgo, PRL 2016); M87 returned an asymmetric bright ring with a central depression, *"consistent with expectations for the shadow of a Kerr black hole"* (EHT, ApJ 2019); GW250114 resolved at least two ringdown modes (LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA, PRL 2026). So condition C1 is satisfied and the search is **reachable**, not a blank-field gap. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 6 instrument rule and Fig 3 link rule, plus three independent instrument families). Refuter: demonstrate that every one of these readings is generated by the analysis pipeline and not by the ground, so no instrument returns anything at all.

### F4 - Distinguishing is an ordered comparison at a threshold, not a categorical identity proof

Fig 12 :86: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 12 :87: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

World check: the no-hair test is explicitly comparative. GW250114 constrains the dominant mode and its first overtone to the Kerr prediction *"to tens of percent"* and the quadrupolar frequency *"to within a few percent of the GR prediction"*, and the paper calls this *"the most stringent single-event verification of GR and the Kerr nature of black holes to date."* That is a threshold result at a stated precision. It is not "this object is proven to be a black hole with zero uncertainty." Status: **triangulated** (Fig 12 comparison rule and the Operators table, both framework-internal, plus the physics statement of percent-level constraints). Mode `specified`. Refuter: a reading that yields class identity with no threshold and no residual, which would break the comparison form.

### F5 - A reading does not name its source; only an instrument can

Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28): *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

World check: a Kerr-consistent ringdown and a Kerr-consistent shadow are two readings that several source classes can, in principle, produce. The metamerism point is that "consistent with Kerr" is a projection statement. The instrument that would discriminate source is the goal, not the label. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 10 and the source essay). Refuter: show a reading whose mapping to hidden class is one-to-one and proven so, with no other source able to produce it.

### F6 - Independence: count instruments, not agreements

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*

World check: LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA strain analysis and EHT VLBI imaging draw on different instruments and different evidence pools, so their joint agreement on compact-object constitution is weak but genuine corroboration. By contrast, two ringdown analyses of the *same* GW250114 strain are one pool wearing two names. Status: **triangulated** (Fig 2 and Fig 7). Refuter: count two analyses of one data set as two independent witnesses.

### F7 - No last room: even a perfect discriminator is a room, not a final answer

Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room"*; Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The figures README has a section titled "This is not a Theory of Everything and that is the point."

A catalogue of distinguishing observables is a room. It closes some of the gap and cannot arrive at a final classification scheme. If a search for a further discriminator finds nothing, that is not proof no discriminator could exist (Fig 8 :39). Status: **triangulated** (Fig 7, Fig 8, canonical README). Refuter: a completed classification with no outside left to import from.

### F8 - Which single observable categorically separates all classes: declared `gap`

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; Fig 13 :139: *"narrowing gets you close while the ground decides."*

After a genuine re-census, no figure line names the observable that would separate a black hole from every proposed exotic compact object at all masses and spins. The world literature agrees that this is open: the Berti et al. spectroscopy review (CQG 2026) frames the field as *"the bright prospects of the field in the era of LISA and next-generation ground-based gravitational-wave detectors"*, and the Shen et al. review (2026) notes that *"the asymptotic behavior of high overtones is closely associated with the phenomenon of black hole echoes"*, which remain unconfirmed. The missing thing is a **source-discriminating instrument**, characterisable in effect terms (a reading on which the BH class and the ECO class do not overlap at any resolution) but not held. Status: `gap`. Refuter: produce such an instrument and the gap closes.

### F9 - Filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* The reported signal-to-noise ratios (24 for GW150914, 76 for GW250114) and false-alarm rates price the observation; they do not by themselves settle the class. Mode `general`. Refuter: a classification that follows from the SNR alone with no ground-side discriminating quantity.

### F10 - The bindings are imports with provenance, not figure derivations

Fig 8 :50: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents."* The mapping "mass plus spin plus a Kerr-consistent ringdown implies a black hole" comes from the physics frame, not from the six-term set. The figures return a shape: ground-side, per-slot, already-registering, source-discriminating. The names of the observables are world-sourced and must carry their source gate. *They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (Isaiah N, 2026-09-06) is blunt: *"Every instrument is a crystallized hypothesis about what is worth measuring, and the hypothesis comes from human awareness."* Status: **single-source** for the shape-not-a-name limit across Fig 9 and the canonical README; the binding itself is world-sourced. Refuter: derive a specific observable from the figures alone.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim (a distinguishing observable must be ground-side, per-slot, already registering, and source-discriminating, and such observables exist as readings) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A class-separating reading is found whose value is fixed by frame-internal convention or prior rather than by a measurement (breaks Fig 2 :29).
2. Every candidate reading is proven many-to-one onto all compact-object classes at every resolution, with no sharper instrument possible, so the classes are genuinely indiscernible and not merely unresolved at this budget (breaks Fig 6 :27 and Fig 8 :39, read together).
3. Two "independent" confirmations are shown to draw on the same strain data or the same evidence pool, so their agreement is the pool talking (breaks Fig 2 :40).
4. A source name is assigned from a single projection with no discriminating instrument, and the assignment survives challenge (breaks Fig 10 :52 and F5).

If any fires, the corresponding claim is withdrawn. None has fired in this simulated run. Until a ground reading settles it, no part of this is `grounded`.
