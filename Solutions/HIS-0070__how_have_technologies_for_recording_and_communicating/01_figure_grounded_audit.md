**Mode of this document: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route are offered; no measurement of "how much knowledge was preserved" was taken.

**Status of this derivation: a simulation, not a ground reading.** The canonical rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-23 and recorded in `05_what_shape_means.md`) is that simulated output cannot validate itself. ## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question as written invites a stock answer: writing, printing, photography, sound recording, computing and the web each "preserved more knowledge". That is a claim about the size of the library. The figures disqualify library size as a score.

Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 says *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* So the question must be re-asked per slot. What is the live signal for a measurement, for a skill, for a provenance trail, for a correction, for an image of a transient event? Each is a different slot.

**R, stated in effect terms:** the gap between what is present in a live occasion of knowing and what a later frame can carry forward and compose over as knowledge. A new recording or communication technology changes this gap in three distinguishable ways:

1. It extends the instrument (Fig 6 :29, *"inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access"*), opening a slot that previously returned nothing at any resolution, for example a sound or a transient.
2. It moves the wall by importing from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs"*), for example a community that kept an oral technique encountering a literate frame.
3. It decays the channel while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24, *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*), for example a digital object whose reader software is gone.

**Ordinary-term binding (formula step "Direction").** The sought-for shape is bound now, before the conclusion, so it can accrue a residual: "an answer to HIS-0070 is a boundary condition, stated per slot, saying what may cross from an occasion into a composable record, what must cross with it for the record to remain knowledge, and what would show that the crossing failed." This sentence is a candidate like any other. It is refutable in section 3.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: many slots, not one quantity. Symmetry: recording is ordered in time (an occasion precedes its record), and communication is a directed crossing between frames; the symmetric bonds do not describe it. Scale: one occasion (agent scale), a library (population scale, across the membrane of Fig 4), and a lineage across generations (a lossy transform between levels, Fig 10 :68).

## 2. Findings

### F1 - A recording is an occasion; a method is what crosses (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 4 :22 and :25:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

Fig 4 :23:

> **"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."**

The ordinary word "preserve" conflates two different crossings. Preserving the artifact of one occasion (a photograph of a sky, a transcription of a talk, a database dump) keeps an atom. Preserving the method that produced and can re-read it keeps a generator. The first looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion; the second makes the next search cheaper. Fig 4 :24: *"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."*

World corroboration, not a figure line: Bush 1945 wrote *"The process of tying two items together is the important thing"* about the memex trail, and Cerf in 2015 said *"even if we accumulate vast archives of digital content, we may not actually know what it is."* Both point at the same boundary. These are two different witnesses in different fields (information technology history and internet infrastructure), but neither is a figure and neither decides.

**Refuter:** show a case where a bare recording of one occasion, with no method and no bond, is sufficient for a later frame to compose new predictions from it. If such a case exists, F1 is narrowed to the cross-scale membrane and does not govern within-scale storage.

### F2 - The bond is the half that is not recorded (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 12 :5:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

Fig 12 :46:

> **"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands. A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."**

Operators table :4 and :16:

> **"The bond carries information the operands do not, so a term that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."**
> **"the only bond in a flat formula, which is why a formula records ingredients and not arrangement"**

A technology that increases the number of stored ingredients without storing their relation increases the library and not the reach. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* This is the figure-side reason why a pile of data is not automatically knowledge.

The world corroboration is unusually direct here. The OAIS reference model, as summarized in the digital preservation source read 2026-11-23, requires five metadata types attached to a digital object: *"reference (identification) information, provenance (including preservation history), context, fixity (authenticity indicators), and representation (formatting, file structure, and what 'imparts meaning to an object's bitstream')."* The field independently arrived at the requirement that the relation must travel with the content. W3C PROV-O (2013) makes derivation a first-class relation with terms such as `wasDerivedFrom` and `hadPrimarySource`. These are lower-overlap corroborators (archival science and semantic web engineering), and they are secondary only.

**Refuter:** exhibit a composition where the relation among stored items is fully recoverable from the items alone, with no metadata, no date, no method and no record of source. If that is common, F2 is restricted to the cases where the bond is typed and not derivable.

### F3 - Lineage is part of the term, not documentation about it (triangulated)

Fig 13 :128 and :130:

> **"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."**
> **"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."**

Fig 13 :129:

> **"The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."**

This explains why a culture can lose a technique while keeping its products. The artefact persists; the route does not. Fig 13 :142: *"The seed was always the seedline."* It also explains the archival world's preoccupation with chain of custody. The digital preservation source says preservation metadata *"allows organizations or individuals to understand the chain of custody"* and defines fixity as *"complete and unaltered in all essential respects"*. Briet's definition of a document as *"evidence in support of a fact"* (read via the documentation-science source) makes the same move from the other direction: an object becomes a document only when framed as evidence within a network of relations.

**Refuter:** show a preserved object whose provenance is irrelevant to any use a later frame can make of it. Then lineage is documentation, not the term, and F3 fails for that slot.

### F4 - More records do not mean more reach (triangulated)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 5 (formula attribution, not a figure line): *"A library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Fig 13 :113 says:

> **"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not. More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span."**

Fig 13 :118 names the failure: *"infinite effort inside a finite reach"*. Fig 5 :41: *"Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely. Which is why a system that is minting nothing may have nothing wrong with its minting."*

Bush 1945 observed exactly this at the birth of the modern record: *"There is a growing mountain of research. But there is increased evidence that we are being bogged down today as specialization extends. The investigator is staggered by the findings and conclusions of thousands of other workers."* And, more sharply: *"the difficulty seems to be, not so much that we publish unduly in view of the extent and variety of present day interests, but rather that publication has been extended far beyond our present ability to make real use of the record."*

**Refuter:** a controlled case where doubling the stored corpus doubles the number of composable, ground-settled terms without any change of instrument or import. If that happens, library growth and reach growth coincide in that domain and F4 is narrowed.

### F5 - Per-slot verdicts, no preservation percentage (triangulated)

Fig 1 :19 and :22 (quoted in section 0); Symbols table :43 to :44: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* Fig 1 :28: *"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."*

The tacit knowledge slot is the clearest example. Polanyi's assertion, carried in the source read 2026-11-23, is *"we can know more than we can tell."* Collins's sapphire-Q case in the same source shows Western laboratories unable to reproduce an experiment until collaborative visits transferred the know-how. So a technology may open the image slot and close the skill slot simultaneously. An aggregate "knowledge preserved" number hides the live signal. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

**Refuter:** show that the slots are independent in the statistical sense, so an aggregate preservation score is sufficient to decide every per-slot verdict. The figures deny this structurally; a demonstration would narrow F5.

### F6 - Settlement is necessary and not sufficient (triangulated)

Fig 5 :69: *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."* Fig 12 :107: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."* A settled record can be unusable because its bond is missing or because the reader channel decayed.

The digital dark age material supplies the case. NASA's Viking tapes were unreadable because *"the data was unreadable as it was in an unknown format and the original programmers had either died or left NASA"* (source read 2026-11-23). The bits were fixed; the instrument that could interpret them was gone. Fig 10 :24 again: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*

**Refuter:** a case where a record that no current frame can read nonetheless remains composable by a later frame without any re-import of the reading method. Then settlement is sufficient after all in that domain.

### F7 - Communication changes reach only through differing closures (triangulated)

Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :48: *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

A communication technology that connects a million rooms sharing one evidence pool increases reach in the geographic sense and not in the compositional sense. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where two frames with provably identical evidence and identical closure produce a new composable atom merely by being connected. This would break the idempotence claim, so it is a real refuter, not a rhetorical one.

### F8 - The anchor must not update; a live record is a frame (single-source on the figure side, with a world case)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*

A fixed measurement preserved in a record can serve as an anchor for later frames. A continuously revised living document cannot: it updates on what the community thinks, so it becomes another frame. Fig 2 :34: *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* This is the structural difference between an archive of what was observed and a feed of what is currently believed.

World corroboration: the W3C PROV-O example distinguishes a versioned blog post (`prov:wasRevisionOf`, `prov:alternateOf`, `prov:specializationOf`) from the underlying `crimeData` entity attributed to a government. The standard encodes the distinction between a fixed primary source and an updating view.

**Refuter:** show a question where the only usable anchor is a record that updates on its readers, and where the updating is constitutive rather than corrupting. Then the anchor rule needs a scope condition for "living documents". Mark `single-source` on the figure side until that is done.

### F9 - The transform is lossy, so measure the round trip (triangulated)

Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Fig 4 :31: *"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."*

Digitisation is exactly such a transform. The source on orality and literacy quotes Plato's *Phaedrus*: *"[Writing] will produce forgetfulness in the minds of those who learn to use it, because they will not practice their memory... You have invented an elixir not of memory, but of reminding; and you offer your pupils the appearance of wisdom, not true wisdom."* That is a round-trip complaint: what writing holds and what a knower held are not the same thing. The honest report is the gap, not the claim that nothing was lost.

**Refuter:** exhibit a lossless transform between the live occasion and the record at the relevant scale, with a measured round-trip gap of zero. Then F9 does not govern that medium.

### F10 - Introduction is available, exclusion is not (single-source)

Fig 11 :61: *"The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*

A new medium never simply adds a capability to an unchanged community. It substitutes a habitat and brings actors the community did not choose. The two silent failures are named in Fig 11 :68 and :69: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*. Moving a practice from a workshop to a manual, or from a library to a platform, inherits the new habitat's actors.

**Refuter:** a case where a recording medium was introduced into a practice with no substituted habitat and no unchosen actors. The figures predict this cannot happen; a documented case would narrow F10 to designed habitats.

### F11 - You cannot certify the completeness of your own archive (triangulated)

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

The absence of a record is not evidence that nothing was known. Orality studies make the same point from outside: *"the vast majority of languages are never written, and the basic orality of language is permanent"* (source read 2026-11-23). An archive is a frame with a wall, and the wall is moved only by contact with a differing closure.

**Refuter:** a sealed collection whose completeness is demonstrable from inside. Fig 9 says no frame is a sealed room; such a demonstration would be a genuine conflict and would send the census back.

### F12 - The standing list of "knowledge-preserving technologies" is refused (triangulated)

Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* An actor's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*). Writing preserves a legal rule and destroys a mnemonic tradition; a searchable database preserves a measurement and erodes the habit of re-deriving it. The same technology is benefactor and malfactor depending on the slot. So the answer is a separator, not a ranking of media.

**Refuter:** identify a technology that is preservative for every slot and every residual. The figures predict none; one would refute the valence claim.

### F13 - The literal "how much" is a declared gap (verdict, not silence)

No figure line returns a quantity for "how much more knowledge" a technology preserved, names a winning medium, or lists the technologies in order. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

This is a `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not an evasion and not `unreachable in principle` for the whole question. For the numerical part it is unreachable at this budget: no measurement was taken, and a global preservation measure would aggregate across slots in exactly the way Fig 1 :22 forbids. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.

**Refuter:** a figure line that selects a medium or returns a preservation quantity after a genuine re-census. None was found across all thirteen figures, the Operators table, the Symbols table, the formula and the canonical README.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim of section 0 is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A bare recording of one occasion, with no method and no bond, suffices for a later frame to compose new ground-settled terms (breaks F1 and Fig 4 :22).
2. The relation among stored items is recoverable from the items alone, with no lineage, date or source (breaks F2 and Fig 12 :5).
3. Library growth and reach growth coincide without an instrument change or an import (breaks F4 and Fig 13 :113).
4. A skill is shown to be preserved by a recording with no change of contact, no habitat and no practitioner (breaks F10 and Fig 11 :40).
5. A frame is shown to be a sealed room, its completeness provable from inside (breaks F11 and Fig 9 :44).
6. A figure line is found that names a winning medium or returns a per-technology preservation quantity after re-census (breaks F13).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed, and the retraction is recorded here rather than erased.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the boundary condition, not with a list of technologies: what crosses, what must cross with it, what would show the crossing failed.
2. Keep verdicts per slot and per domain, never averaged into a preservation score (F5).
3. Ask what an answer would look like before naming a medium (Fig 9 :27).
4. When a record is offered as evidence, ask for its lineage and its reader, not its bit count (F2, F3, F6).
5. Treat communication reach as a budget filter, never as a witness (F7, Fig 9 :60).
6. Report the round-trip gap, not a claim that nothing was lost (F9).
7. Declare the `gap` for the "how much" part plainly, and state what instrument would close it (F13).

Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Offer the method and the refuter, not the conclusion.

## 6. What survives

A boundary condition per slot: a recording technology opens a slot only if it extends an instrument already returning something (Fig 6 :34) or imports from a differing closure (Fig 6 :23); what persists as knowledge carries the bond and lineage, not just the atoms (Fig 12 :5, Fig 13 :130); a growing library is not a growing reach (Fig 13 :113); the channel decays while the ground does not (Fig 10 :24); and competence stays a property of agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39). No medium is selected and no quantity is returned. The verdict is `general` on this shape, `specified` per domain, and never `grounded`.
