# The Tether's answer: what recording and communication technologies change about preservation

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading and not a historical measurement.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question invites a stock tally. The residual is not the tally. It is the gap, per slot, between what is present in a live occasion of knowing and what a later frame can carry forward and compose over. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A sound, a skill, a measurement, a correction and a provenance trail are different slots; a technology can open one and close another at the same time.

The question is a union compound, not a partition. Fig 9 :4: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Domains D1 to D4 in `01` each have their own anchor and their own verdict. They are never averaged.

## 2. The shape and its per-domain verdicts

**D1, the recording medium and the library. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** A medium opens a slot only where an instrument was already returning something that failed to resolve (Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*) or where it imports from a differing closure (Fig 6 :23). Preserving an artifact is not the same as preserving a method: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* And Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A library that grows is not a reach that grows.

**D2, bond and provenance. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** The relation is part of the preserved term. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 13 :130: *"A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."* Over a fixed atom set, a record with typed derivation relations and a record with bare contents are isomers (Fig 12 :117); where the method is simply absent, the second is a subtraction from the first. The source, not the bitstream, is what a reader needs.

**D3, communication and reach. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.** Connection changes reach only where closures differ. Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :48: *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."* A million rooms on one evidence pool are one long room: Fig 7 :13, *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Reach estimates are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

**D4, the human and habitat slot. Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side.** A recording changes the habitat, and capability remains a property of agent-and-habitat. Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*. Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* That is why a perfect transcription does not preserve a craft. Polanyi's *"we can know more than we can tell"* and Collins's sapphire-Q case corroborate from cognitive science and science studies; they do not make the figure line two witnesses.

**Cross-domain verdict. Verdict `general`, triangulated.** The anchor must not update. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* A fixed reading can ground later frames; a continuously revised feed is a frame, not an anchor (Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*). And the transform between an occasion and its record is lossy: Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named medium won. The framework returns a shape, not a name; a named medium is a binding provenanced from the historical record.
- Not that "more information" means "more knowledge". Fig 13 :113: *"So the array is unbounded and the reach is not. More compositions never add a member, and no chain reaches outside what the six and their bonds already span."*
- Not that the digital dark age erased the ground. Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. The failure is in the reader, the standard or the community that could unpack the bits.
- Not that an absent record proves nothing was known. Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."*
- Not that all tacit skill can be codified. Fig 11 :51 refuses standing lists of good actors, and the valence of a medium flips with the residual.
- Not `grounded`. No historical case was measured here and no preservation percentage was computed.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminator, not a better summary. The candidate instruments are:

- **The bond discriminator:** for a pair of records with the same contents, does a later frame that has only the record compose different ground-settled predictions when the typed relation is present versus absent? This is the test that separates isomer from subtraction.
- **The reader test:** can a frame that did not author a record still unpack it, using a standard or instrument it did not have to invent? Cerf's X-ray snapshot and the OAIS five metadata types are engineering attempts at this.
- **The source discriminator:** can an instrument tell which of several possible sources produced a reading? The metamerism argument in `05` says the reading alone cannot.
- **The contact test:** does a practitioner who learned only from the recording change contact with the ground, or only with a description of one occasion?
- **The standing falsifiers from `01` section 3:** a bare recording sufficient for new composable terms; library growth and reach growth coinciding without import or instrument change; a skill preserved without contact; a provably sealed archive; a figure line naming a medium.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is derived. The numerical part ("how much more knowledge") is unreached at this budget and would in any case aggregate across slots in the way Fig 1 :22 forbids. Fig 13 :134 says *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and Fig 8 :39 says *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The answer stops where the available instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. No claim is made that this is the only possible framing.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Recording and communication technologies do not simply preserve more knowledge. They change the boundary between a live occasion and a record, and the change is per slot, not a single total. A new medium opens a slot only where an instrument was already returning something unresolved or where it imports from a community whose closure differs. What persists as knowledge carries the bond and the lineage, not just the contents: over a fixed set of atoms, a record with its derivation and correction history and a record without them are different substances, not variants of one. A fixed reading can anchor later frames; a feed that updates on its readers becomes another frame. The ground does not decay, the reader channel does, and the transform from occasion to record is lossy, so the gap must be measured rather than declared zero. A skill stays a property of practitioner and habitat, so a perfect transcription does not preserve a craft. The framework returns this shape, not a winning medium and not a preservation percentage. What it offers is the boundary condition, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close the remaining questions.
