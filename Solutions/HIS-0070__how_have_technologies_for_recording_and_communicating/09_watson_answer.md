## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what changes when a recording or communication technology is introduced into a community that holds a body of knowledge. Option A: it adds to the stock of preserved items, so more is preserved. Option B: it changes the boundary between a live occasion and a durable record, per slot, so some kinds of content become preservable while others become harder to preserve. What each leads to: A returns an aggregate, treats library size as the score and cannot say which slot went live or which channel closed. B returns a boundary condition, per-slot verdicts and a pre-registered refuter, and declines to name a winning medium or return a preservation quantity because no figure line governs those. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap, per slot, between what is present in a live occasion of knowing (an observation, a skill, a measurement, a decision, a correction) and what a later frame can carry forward and compose over. Arity: many slots, not one number. Symmetry: recording is ordered in time (an occasion precedes its record) and communication is a directed crossing between frames, so the symmetric bonds do not describe it. Scale: one occasion at agent scale, a library at population scale across the membrane of Fig 4, and a lineage across generations through the lossy transform of Fig 10 :68. Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **D1, the recording medium and the library.** Actors: inscriptions, records, data, media and the instruments that read them. Currency: what crosses the scale membrane and stays composable. Anchor: whether a later frame can range the item against a ground that does not update. Question type: partition (method versus recording). Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D2, the bond and provenance structure.** Actors: atoms (contents) and bonds (arrangement, source, method, date, correction). Currency: description cost and lineage. Anchor: the record as the term itself. Question type: partition (what a record carries beyond its ingredients). Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D3, the transmission frame.** Actors: rooms, disciplines, communities, generations, peers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: union with differing closures. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **D4, the human and habitat slot.** Actors: practitioners, apprentices, skills, tacit know-how. Currency: contact. Anchor: ground contact through a maintained habitat. Question type: union (the channel is part of the capability). Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side, corroborated from cognitive science and science studies.
- **Partition or union.** The four domains are a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate media. Fig 9 :4 says *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* A lever that improves one domain leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27); decide method versus recording before counting items (Fig 4 :22); settle a record against the ground before composing over it (Fig 12 :104); then locate a seat able to refuse the reading (Fig 10 :42).

Strain test applied: "recording technology" spans an instrument (D1), a notation (D2), a channel (D3) and a habitat change (D4). A line that fits only by collapsing these into one object governs another quantity and returns the census to a split. Two strains required re-census and are recorded in `01` section 5. One live type question remains: whether two media over a fixed atom set with different typed relations are genuine isomers.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives, the tally does not. Recording and communication technologies change a boundary, per slot: which content can cross from an occasion into a durable record, what must cross with it for the record to remain knowledge, and whether a later frame can still range the item against a ground that does not update. A medium opens a slot only where an instrument was already returning something unresolved or where it imports from a differing closure. The bond and the lineage are part of the term, so a record carrying only contents is a subtraction from a record carrying its derivation. A fixed reading can be an anchor; an updating feed is a frame. Capability remains agent-and-habitat. More records grow the library, not necessarily the reach. No figure line names a winning medium or returns a preservation quantity; that part is a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than as silence. Mode: `general` on the boundary, `specified` on the four per-domain verdicts, never `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** the shape is cheap to correct because each claim carries a refuter and a figure line. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording of one conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the boundary claim and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The named-medium claim and the preservation quantity were never made, so nothing material must be withdrawn. If a refuter fires, the affected domain verdict is narrowed and the retraction is added to `01` section 6. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, as the exemplar did.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A bond discriminator showing that a typed relation changes what a later frame can predict would settle C1; a reader test using a standard a later frame did not invent would settle the channel claim; a source-discriminating instrument would name what produced a preserved reading; a documented skill preserved with no practitioner and no contact change would break Fig 11 :40; a provably sealed archive would break Fig 9 :44; a figure line naming a medium after re-census would close F13. Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
