## Nearby literature that bears on the claim

1. **The trail is the bond.** Bush's memex is not a storage device but a machine for *"association"* and *"trails"*, where *"the process of tying two items together is the important thing"*. This is Fig 12's bond and Fig 13's lineage stated by an engineer in 1945, before the framework. It is the strongest low-overlap corroborator in the set: the field is information technology history, not the framework's own essays.
2. **Storage without interpretation is not knowledge.** Cerf's X-ray snapshot proposal says the content, application, operating system and machine description must travel together, and that the open problem is whether the standards for unpacking them will still be known. This is the bond plus the reader instrument. The Viking tape case is the negative instance.
3. **The read does not determine the source.** Briet's document-as-evidence definition and the documentation literature's warning that different readers interpret the same document differently both corroborate the per-slot verdict: the same bitstream is a record for one frame and noise for another.
4. **Some knowledge cannot be aggregated.** Polanyi and Collins show that a slot can be live in a community of practitioners and closed in every archive. This is why no preservation percentage is returned.
5. **Writing closes a slot as it opens one.** The *Phaedrus* passage and Ong's orality work corroborate the round-trip gap: a new medium makes some things rememberable and makes other practices decay.
6. **Provenance is engineerable.** PROV-O turns derivation, primary source, revision and attribution into first-class relations. This is an independent field arriving at Fig 13 :130's claim that the lineage is part of the term.

Overlap declaration: the BBC article and the Wikipedia "Digital dark age" page both carry Cerf's 2015 remark, so they are one witness for that quote, not two. The three Wikipedia pages on preservation and documentation are in one adjacent evidence pool (library and information science), so their agreement is one pool talking. Bush (1945, engineering), Ong (orality studies), Polanyi and Collins (cognitive science and science studies) and W3C PROV-O (semantic web standards) are genuinely lower-overlap witnesses. The framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool completely and carry no independent witness weight.

## Distribution rules, offer do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the boundary claim and the refuter first. A full framework summary sent before anyone has described a residual is a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42**, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A reader who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27**, *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*; ask what the reader would expect to see if the claim were true before offering the links.
- **Fig 2 :40**, *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Never select recipients for likely agreement and never pool later agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the boundary claim; (2) the four refuters; (3) ask what the reader would count as a failure; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures and the primary historical sources, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** reader checks the output against a ground they already know. Their verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in:** if curious, the reader receives the source links. Their later agreement is never counted as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the requirement that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim survives. The refuters in `02` and `08` are the test; send the claim as a candidate, not as a conclusion.
