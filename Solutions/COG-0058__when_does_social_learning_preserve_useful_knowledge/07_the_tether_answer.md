# The Tether's answer on social learning and useful knowledge

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a given practice, source and receiving habitat, why does the gap between what the transmitted practice predicts and what the habitat returns stay small across handoffs in some cases and grow in others? R is indexed per practice, per receiver and per habitat, never averaged into a "culture works" score (Fig 1 :19, :22). The phenomenon is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :55, :57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1/D2, item type (C1).** What crosses must be a reapplicable method, not the recording of one occasion.

- Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- Fig 4 :25-26: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*
- Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*

A verbatim replay can preserve the appearance of knowledge while losing the ability to produce a new success. The figure does not say fidelity is bad; it says fidelity to a recording is a different object from carrying a method. Status `triangulated` (Fig 4 and Fig 11, membrane versus agent-and-habitat, low overlap). Mode `specified`.

**D4/D2, habitat and channel (C2).** The receiving habitat must still pay, and the receiver's channel to that payment must still be reachable.

- Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*.
- Fig 7 :37-39: *"Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:"* *"the bracket’s fidelity across the handoff"* *"the ground’s pressure on the room"*.
- Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."*

Quick corruption is usually channel failure under a ground that has not moved. Slow corruption is usually a habitat change that flipped the practice's sign while the channel kept working. Status `triangulated` (Fig 10 and Fig 7). Mode `specified`.

**D3, the population chain.** A single-source chain cannot add evidence, however long it runs.

- Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.
- Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*
- Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

A thousand carriers who all learned from one demonstrator are one witness with a thousand spellings. Status `triangulated` (Fig 7 and Fig 2, low overlap). Mode `general` for the witness rule, `specified` for transmission chains.

**D3/D5, valence.** The same practice preserves knowledge in one habitat and spreads harm in another, without changing.

- Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
- Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*

Status `triangulated` (Fig 11 and Fig 12, habitat relations versus composition structures). Mode `specified`.

**The refusal requirement.** A chain with sequence but no negation can accumulate and cannot correct.

- Formula step 2: a term whose residual *"PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN"* owes *"a refusal, not an explanation"*.
- Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.

Status `single-source` at figure level (the Operators table and Formula are one framework); corroborated at the world level by Enquist et al. 2007's *"identify and discard maladaptive culture"*, high overlap with the same field pool. Mode `general`.

**Which practice wins in a named habitat: `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The framework returns the two conditions and the instrument shape, not a named winner. The discriminator is described in effect terms in `01` F8 and `05` §4 but is not held here.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that social learning is generally good or generally bad. It is a sign that depends on C1 and C2.
- Not that copying is always cheaper or always adaptive. Kameda and Nakanishi 2003 and Rendell et al. 2010 disagree about that, and the conflict was returned to the census, not decided by choosing a side (`01` F9).
- Not that fidelity is the problem. Recording fidelity without provenance is a failure of C1; method fidelity with provenance is the success case.
- Not that "culture" is a substance with a valence. It failed the anchor test and is not a domain yet (`01` X1).
- Not which practice wins. That is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.
- Not `grounded`. No habitat measurement pays for the two-condition shape here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding tests are those in `01` section 4. The most field-ready of them:

1. **Method versus recording:** does a practice keep paying across a documented habitat change with no reapplicable rule retained? If yes, F1 fails.
2. **Channel versus ground:** does a tradition become harmful while the habitat's payment is unchanged and receiver readings measure fresh, unsaturated and unpooled? If yes, F2 fails.
3. **Independence:** can a single-demonstrator chain produce new ground-verified discriminations absent in the demonstrator? If yes, F3/F4 fail.
4. **Valence:** is a practice's payoff contribution invariant across a documented habitat change? If yes, F5 fails.
5. **A closer instrument already described:** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* says *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* A shrinking correction trend is a ground-visible signal that a method is being rebuilt rather than a recording replayed. This is the discriminator closest to being held.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The two-condition shape is what survives a figure re-census; the specific bindings to named transmission biases are bindings from the world frame, provenanced as such, not derivations. Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The round-trip gap R_T is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md`. The answer stops where the available instruments can no longer distinguish which path produced a widely held reading, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that social learning is good or bad, and it does not name which tradition wins. It says that copying preserves knowledge when what crosses between people is a reapplicable method whose grounding travels with it and the receiving habitat still pays for it through a channel the receiver can read. It spreads ineffective or harmful practices when what crosses is a recording of one occasion, when all receivers draw on one evidence pool, when that channel decays, or when the habitat changes so that a former benefactor becomes a malfactor. Those are two conditions with different instruments, not one lever, and the framework refuses to blend them. It offers the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which practice wins stays open until a ground settles it.
