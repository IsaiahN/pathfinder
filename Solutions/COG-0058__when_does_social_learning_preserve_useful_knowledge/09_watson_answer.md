## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** For a practice p, source frame f and receiving habitat h, `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` with `b` mixed by `w` between private history and inherited prior (Symbols table :24). Arity: three-place relation ordered by transmission time; carries an ordered sequence bond and a comparison bond, not a symmetric join. Symmetry: asymmetric in copy direction and in time; "useful" is a threshold relative to a baseline in h. Scale: individual loop, transmission dyad, population tradition, intergenerational chain; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :21, :30).
- **D1 Individual learning loop.** Actors: learner, Γ, belief b, weighting w. Currency: residual reduction. Anchor: direct performance in the local habitat. Admitted.
- **D2 Transmission dyad / membrane.** Actors: source model, receiver, item crossing. Currency: what crosses and the round-trip gap R_T. Anchor: re-performance of the item in the receiver's habitat (Fig 4 :25-26, :30). Admitted.
- **D3 Population tradition / room chain.** Actors: demonstrators, copiers, majority, prestige models. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: ground selection between rooms (Fig 7 :35). Admitted. Prestige bias is absorbed here, not a separate domain.
- **D4 Habitat of the practice.** Actors: conditions, relations, competitors, tools. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not update (Fig 11 :73). Admitted.
- **D5 Verification frame.** Actors: researchers, experiments, reviewers. Currency: evidence and residuals. Anchor: the experimental result, never agreement. Admitted, but in this simulated run it shares one evidence pool with every other seat.
- **X1 Culture as a substance.** Actors: tradition. Currency: none independent of a habitat. Anchor: none nameable. Not a domain yet; excluded before any figure line is applied.

Union, not partition: D1/D2 and D3/D4 form two overlapping compounds, and the whole is a union of C1 (bracket fidelity of the item) and C2 (ground pressure in the receiving habitat). Order by dependency: perception -> vocabulary -> objective -> planning -> learn and carry (Fig 3 :20); the residual selects the next receiver rather than temporal order alone (Fig 7 :40).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: social learning preserves useful knowledge when a reapplicable method whose grounding travels with it crosses into a habitat that still pays for it through a readable channel; it spreads ineffective or harmful practices when a recording crosses instead, when all copiers draw on one evidence pool, when the channel decays, or when a habitat or residual change flips a formerly good practice's sign. The two conditions are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Which practice wins in a named habitat is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world pool corroborates the mechanism shape but cannot supply the ground reading, and its one genuine conflict (Rogers versus Rendell) was re-mapped without flipping the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A chain failing C1 would be given more demonstrators or higher teaching fidelity, and a chain failing C2 would be given better provenance tools, so a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and reversible if the two conditions are kept separate.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails. A refutation is added and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the five pre-registered falsifiers, in particular a verbatim replay that keeps paying across a habitat change; harm appearing while payment is unchanged and readings measure fresh; a single-demonstrator chain producing new ground-verified discriminations; a payoff contribution invariant across a habitat change; or one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-practice persistence is fully predicted by a culture-wide average would also reverse it. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
