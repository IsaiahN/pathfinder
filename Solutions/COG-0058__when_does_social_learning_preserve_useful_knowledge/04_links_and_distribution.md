## Wider literature that bears on the residual

The field evidence splits along the same two conditions the figures name.

1. **What crosses (C1).** Rendell et al. 2010: *"Social learning proved advantageous because other agents were rational in demonstrating the behavior in their repertoire with the highest payoff, inadvertently filtering information for copiers."* This is the method side. Lyons et al. 2011 is the recording side: children copy *"the actions that were causally necessary but also those that were clearly superfluous"*, and the effect is *"constrained by intentionality"*. Boyd and Richerson 2024 add that *"errors and misunderstandings may cause social learners to transform complex adaptive traits that they learn in ways that make them less functional, a kind of misguided variation that may play a crucial role in impeding cultural adaptation."*
2. **Does the habitat still pay (C2).** Laland and Williams 1998: guppies kept the costly long route after all trained founders were removed, and learned the short route more slowly when shoaling with long-route founders. Kameda and Nakanishi 2003 (abstract via search result): Rogers's result that at equilibrium a mixed cultural population has fitness *"exactly identical to the mean fitness of an 'acultural' population consisting only of individual learners"*, and that environmental fluctuation poses the challenge. Boyd and Richerson 1985 (text via Internet Archive search result): *"increasing the predictability of the environment decreases the optimal amount of individual learning."*
3. **Independence.** Kendal et al. 2018 (rendered text via search result): conformity is *"sensitive to whether the demonstrators have obtained their information from independent sources or from each other"*. This is Fig 2 :31-32 applied to culture.
4. **Selectivity, not indiscriminate copying.** Laland 2004; Enquist et al. 2007 (abstract via search result): *"culture can be adaptive, and refined social learning can evolve, if individuals can identify and discard maladaptive culture."* That last clause is the figure's refusal operator under another name (Formula step 2, `REFUTED`), and it is the strongest bridge between the framework and this field.

These sources are not independent witnesses for the verdict. They share a broad cultural-evolution and animal-behaviour literature, and several share authors. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. They corroborate the mechanism shape, not the field-level verdict, and the verdict comes from the figures.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has separated the two conditions sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the residual and the five refuters; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if each condition were the failing one before giving them the article.
- **Seat Map :163**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the two conditions; (2) ask which failure class they would expect in their own study system and what would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the two conditions and the five pre-registered refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record the verdict before showing any theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the figure extract, the public repository and the society page. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives contact. As drafted, it is a shape with declared refuters, not a field result. Do not upgrade it to a verdict.
