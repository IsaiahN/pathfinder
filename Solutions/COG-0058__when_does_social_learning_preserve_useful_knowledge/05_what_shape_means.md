## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its occupant. It constrains; it does not fill. For COG-0058 the shape is *two conditions whose conjunction keeps the residual small across a chain of transmissions, plus an instrument that separates a failure of one from a failure of the other*. Naming "conformist transmission", "prestige bias", or "cumulative culture" as *the* answer would be filling the slot, not constraining it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is the essay most directly on this question: it says that *"what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself"* and cites Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the essay-level source for the union reading in `01` section 1.3.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here: a measured consequence in the receiving habitat.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the first: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The term "culture" as a substance with a valence is not a new dimension; it is a name applied to readings that do not discriminate their source.

## 4. The specific error here: naming the source of a transmission from its output

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can all produce it. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The key sentence for this question: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: we observe that a practice is widely held and that carriers agree. From that reading we name its source as "useful knowledge" or "harmful tradition". But several paths produce the same reading: ground-filtered learning, a single-source mirror chain, prestige imitation, habitat change that flipped a formerly good practice, or over-imitated inert steps. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is why F8 is a `gap` and why the five refuters are stated as instruments rather than as labels.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the closest thing to a discriminator already described: *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* A practice whose transfer requires repeatedly large corrections in the receiving habitat is carrying a recording with missing pretext; a practice whose correction trend shrinks is being rebuilt as a method. That is an effect description anyone can check, which is exactly what the essay says travels.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" mean for COG-0058? ANSWERED

**The shape is a two-part constraint:** (C1) the item crossing must be reapplicable and carry its grounding, not be a recording of one occasion; (C2) the receiving habitat must still pay for it through a channel the receiver can read. Neither part licenses a name. "Useful" and "harmful" are threshold readings relative to a baseline in a habitat, not properties of a practice. The missing discriminator, which would name which practice wins in a named habitat, is not held; it is characterized in effect terms in F8 and left open.

## 6. Correction to my own §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any social-learning mechanism, including "payoff-biased transmission" or "copy when uncertain". That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive or discriminator not held in Γ. Payoff bias and copy-when-uncertain are already terms in the world literature, so naming them is a **binding from that frame** onto a described effect, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) or that the framework itself names the winner in a habitat. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.

The metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted. Naming a tradition "useful" from the single reading "widely copied and agreed with" is still a source named from a projection. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious names to endorse the two-condition claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, then let the habitat settle it. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* makes the same point for this exact field: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."*

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
