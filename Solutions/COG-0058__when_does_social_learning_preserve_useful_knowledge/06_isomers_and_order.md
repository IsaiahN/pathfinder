**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §7).

## RESIDUAL

Two questions are owed after `01`: (1) is the difference between preservation and corruption an **isomer** difference under Fig 12's definition, or a difference of item type (method versus recording); and (2) what is the order of a transmission chain, and which analogues are licensed? ### Q2 - Is "preserve" versus "spread harm" an isomer difference?

- Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**
- A preserved method and a frozen recording can hold the **same observable atoms**: the same visible actions, the same words, the same tool sequence. What differs is the bond arrangement, which is not recoverable from the atoms. A method carries a `→` to re-performance and a `⋛` against a habitat baseline; a recording carries only a replay of one occasion. Under Fig 12 :117 the two are **isomers**, and the difference is invisible in an inventory of "what the tradition contains".
- The stronger version: both are built from the same atom set `{visible action, stated rule, provenance, habitat reading, correction history}`, differing in which bonds connect them. The recording drops provenance and correction history (a `−` subtraction); the method keeps them. If both can be written over that set, the isomer claim is real.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if a practice can be shown to preserve its payoff across habitat changes with no provenance and no correction history retained, the isomer claim fails and the distinction collapses into "fidelity of replay". This is refuter 1 in `01` section 4.

### Q3 - What order is a transmission chain?

- Fig 12 :72: **"A then B; B needs A to have happened"** defines `→`. A chain is the degenerate case of a molecule with one bond type (Fig 12 :48). Naming only the ingredients of a chain records half of it.
- Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A chain of identical rooms is one long room; order without difference is motion, not progress.
- Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* So the order that matters is not merely temporal A then B; it is *residual then differently-fitting room*. The residual selects the next transmitter (Fig 7 :40).
- Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Within one receiver: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry. Transmission sits at link 5, so a chain can carry only what links 1 to 4 produced. A receiver with no term for what the practice affords here, and no account of what progress would be, cannot assess the copy.
- **Consequence:** "who copied whom" is not enough; what is owed is "which residual surfaced at handoff n and which receiver was selected against it". That is the lineage, and the lineage is the proof (Fig 13 :127-130).

### Q4 - Which analogues are licensed, and which are strained?

| analogue | status | reason |
|---|---|---|
| Bartlett-style transmission chain / telephone game as a mirror chain | licensed, `specified` | *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Nobody lied. Each person heard it through the understanding they had, and the errors compounded in one direction"*. Low-overlap content, same structure. |
| Guppy foraging transmission chain as a mirror chain | licensed, `specified` | Laland and Williams 1998: the costly long route persisted after founders were removed and inhibited learning of the short route. The chain is one long room. |
| Overimitation as a recording that outranks a method | licensed, `specified` | Lyons et al. 2011: causally superfluous actions were copied even at a competitive cost, but the effect was *"constrained by intentionality"*. Intentionality is the provenance cue. |
| Apprenticeship as a chain of differing rooms with a maintained channel | licensed, `specified` | *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: high two-way contact plus a ground both are corrected by separately; *"watch the trend in correction size, not the count of lessons given."* |
| A market or an ecosystem as the transmitting population | strained, sent back | Only fits by treating price or predation as "agreement". Re-census: a market is an anchor in one domain (Fig 2 :29) and a habitat in another (Fig 11); it is not a copy channel. |
| Fig 13's six physical terms as cultural content | refused, strain S1 | Only fit by renaming a norm a gradient. Fig 13 governs the physical substrate, not cultural traits. |

### Q5 - Does the refusal operator exist in this chain?

- Fig 12 `−` is subtraction, the absence of an operand. Fig 12 amendment and the Operators table add `¬` negation: *"this does not hold"*. A refutation is a negation, not a subtraction.
- Formula step 2 routes a held claim whose residual *"PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN"* to `REFUTED`, where what is owed *"is a refusal, not an explanation"*.
- Enquist et al. 2007 report the field-level counterpart: *"culture can be adaptive, and refined social learning can evolve, if individuals can identify and discard maladaptive culture"* (abstract via search result). Discarding is the refusal operator in the world vocabulary.
- A chain with `→` but no `¬` can only accumulate practices. It can report that a practice stopped paying and cannot report that it is wrong. This is the structural reason "more transmission" is not a remedy for harmful spread.
- **Falsifier:** if a community is observed to remove a practice's standing to be copied without adding a new term and without a source gate for the refusal, the claim that refusal requires `¬` is false.

## CONCLUSION

The preservation/corruption difference is best read as an **isomer** difference over a shared atom set: same visible content, different bond arrangement, decisive behavioural difference, invisible in an inventory. The chain order that matters is residual first, then a differently-fitting receiver, not mere temporal succession. Bartlett-style chains, guppy chains, overimitation and apprenticeship are licensed analogues; markets and ecosystems as copy channels are strained and were returned to the census. The chain needs a negation operator as well as a sequence bond, otherwise it can accumulate but cannot correct. No new primitive was imported and no figure line was strained after re-census.
