- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between what the learner predicts on a task it has seen before and what that task's ground returns. Arity: k slots plus two conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :4-5; Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*), not one hard question. Symmetry: the interference is directed old-to-new, so it is not a symmetric bond; the comparison of old and new reach is a threshold relation (Operators table :48-51). Scale: first order within a task sequence, second order when a settled learner becomes an atom for a later generation (Fig 12 :104).
- **Domain 1, library and reach.** Actors: Γ, atoms, closure, candidate terms, record. Currency: composition then settlement. Anchor: the ground's payment. Question type: partition at the record/reach boundary. I derived this from Fig 6 :42-43 and Fig 5 :72.
