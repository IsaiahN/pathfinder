## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: can AI systems learn continuously without forgetting earlier knowledge or becoming unstable? Option A says yes as a universal engineering target with one recipe. Option B says no, forgetting is inevitable so a frozen system is the only safe one. Option C, the figure-grounded reading, says the question bundles two failures with different anchors, so it is a union of conditions and the answer is a shape with declared gaps. What each leads to: A returns a universal ratio the figures do not give and hides a live old-slot residual in an average; B ignores real designs that slow reach decay; C returns a checkable separator with per-domain verdicts, pre-registered refuters and a stated instrument gap. The verdict recorded here is C.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency. Written before the figure lines were chosen.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: k task-slots plus at least two conditions (retain, adapt). Symmetry: forgetting is directed interference, old to new, not a symmetric join; the old-versus-new comparison is a threshold relation, not co-presence (Operators table :48-51). Scale: first order inside one task sequence, second order across model generations where a settled learner becomes an atom for the next (Fig 12 :104). Fig 9 :4-5 makes it a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Domain 1, library and reach.** Actors: Γ, atoms(Γ), closure(Γ), candidate terms, the record. Currency: composition then settlement. Anchor: the ground's payment. Partition boundary: what stays reachable after new terms are admitted. Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 :42-43 and Fig 5 :72 (reach monotonicity versus library growth, low overlap).
- **Domain 2, ground and channel.** Actors: the anchor, the channel carrying percepts and metrics, the maintainer. Currency: contact with a reference that does not update. Anchor: the ground, not the channel. Mirror-collapse question: does the reference update on the learner's own output? Verdict `specified`, triangulated across Fig 2 :33-34 and Fig 10 :24-29 (anchor update versus channel decay, different mechanisms).
- **Domain 3, habitat and task sequence.** Actors: tasks, distributions, replay buffer, prior knowledge, habitat conditions and relations. Currency: contact with each slot's ground. Anchor: each task's ground, reproduced at the switch. Union of overlapping conditions. Verdict `specified`, single-source on the figure side (Fig 11 :66-69), corroborated by low-overlap cognitive-science sources.
- **Domain 4, verification and metric frame.** Actors: evaluator, metrics, instruments. Currency: per-slot residuals and independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Partition boundary: per-slot residual versus aggregate. Verdict `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 1 :19/:22 and Fig 9 :60.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, then vocabulary for an old-slot residual, then objective, then planning, then learn and carry (Fig 3 :14-15). Refuse before mint. Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). The chain breaks at vocabulary/objective, where there is no instrument for the three failure sources (Fig 3 :27).

Strain test applied: Fig 6 :42 (reach can fall) versus the world EWC result (old expertise maintained) is not a strain, because EWC is a design that slows a fall and the figure is a law about two quantities. Fig 4 :25 (no recording) versus replay buffers dissolved by re-census: buffers are head starts by use, not retained knowledge by data structure. Two live strains remain, the proxy-anchor problem and the benchmark-as-habitat problem, both named in `01` §7 and `11`.

- Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." -> a learner graded on its own outputs has made itself its reference.
- Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" -> the failure is lost contact, not necessarily a changed world or changed weights.
- Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." -> stability cannot be a parameter-stasis property of the model considered alone.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound. Forgetting is a fall in reach while the record grows; instability is drift or lost touch when the reference updates or the channel decays. Reach preservation is read per slot against a ground frozen before the sequence, not as an average. Retention carries methods and head starts, not recordings of one occasion, and positive transfer is composition over a ground-paid term. A task switch substitutes a habitat and inherits actors you did not choose, and a new task that refutes an old term owes a refusal. The figures return that shape and no more: no universal no-forgetting guarantee, no stability-plasticity ratio, no named algorithm. Those are declared `gap`s. Mode `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the universal claim, not `grounded`. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** the shape is falsifiable and the cost of being wrong is locatable. If reach is in fact monotone under arbitrary updates, or a single aggregate metric is a sufficient witness, the per-slot requirement and the union split fall, and they fall at named figure lines (Fig 6 :42, Fig 1 :22). The larger practical cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording of this derivation instead of the refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdict is a separator plus conditions, not a primitive or a number. A counterexample that measures per-slot reach against an independent ground reverses the relevant domain verdict without withdrawing any term from the figure set. The two live strains (proxy anchor, benchmark as habitat) are recorded with refuters and can be closed without rewriting the rest.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-slot reach on all old tasks staying non-decreasing under unbounded updates with no replay, consolidation or capacity growth reverses D1. An independent frame showing a self-graded learner preserves old reach reverses the mirror-chain reading of D2. A standard instrument that separates reach decay, channel decay and habitat substitution on one run closes the verification gap. A figure line governing the exact ratio or naming the mechanism closes the universal gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
