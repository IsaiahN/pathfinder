# Expert verification packet - the Tether on continual learning

**What this is:** a claim in a form a continual-learning researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain verdicts, `general` on the union structure, `gap` on the universal guarantee. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at a ground was taken in this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "can AI systems learn continuously without forgetting earlier knowledge or becoming unstable?" is two questions with different anchors, not one. Forgetting is a fall in reach on an old task-slot while the stored record grows; instability is drift or lost touch when the reference updates on the learner's own output or the channel decays. A continual learner is checkable by four conditions: measure the residual per slot against a ground frozen before the sequence began; carry a reapplicable method or head start across tasks rather than a recording of one occasion; keep the reference from updating and the channel reachable; and reproduce at each switch the contact the old goal required. A refusal operator is owed when a new task refutes an old term. The figures return that shape, not a stability-plasticity ratio and not the name of an algorithm. Those are declared gaps.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 6
- **Statement:** forgetting is a fall in reach, not a deletion from the record; the witness is per-slot, not an aggregate score.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :42-43 - *"What is recorded only grows. What is reachable is derived, and it can fall. A refutation adds a term that refuses; it removes nothing, and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was deleted. So the closure and the reach are two quantities: one monotone, one not."* Also Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* and Fig 1 :22 - *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** do the two quantities behave as stated? In particular, is a global accuracy near zero with one dead old slot a legal state (Fig 1 :13)?
- **Refuter:** show reach monotone with the record under arbitrary new terms; or show a single aggregate metric guaranteed to expose a live residual on any old slot without per-slot access.

### C2 - ground-facing; checkable by a continual-learning researcher against their own benchmarks
- **Statement:** three distinct failure sources hide behind one score fall: reach decay, channel decay, and habitat substitution. A benchmark is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, not capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 11 :67-68 - *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose... what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails."*; Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- **What to check:** can current evaluation protocols separate these three on a single run? The opened surveys define AA, FM and BWT as metrics, and van de Ven et al. note that a 0/100 model and a 100/0 model share an average; neither supplies a three-way discriminator. Is that absence real?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a standard instrument that discriminates the three sources without an independent ground per old task.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a world-facing consequence
- **Statement:** retention carries methods and head starts, not recordings of one occasion; positive transfer is composition over a settled term; a vocabulary without refusal can only accumulate. No figure line names an algorithm or returns a ratio.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :23 - *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."*; Fig 5 :69 - *"settling is necessary for a term to be composed over, and it is not sufficient."*; Fig 12 :104 - *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*; Operators :58 - *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*; Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **What to check:** does the head-start/recording distinction track the difference between using replay samples as probes and using them as the answer? Is the gap claim honest given the opened literature?
- **Refuter:** show a one-occasion recording producing a genuinely new success without re-derivation; or produce a figure line governing the exact stability-plasticity ratio.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Reach test:** per-slot residual on all old tasks against grounds frozen before the sequence, never reused for updates. Non-decreasing reach under unbounded updates with no replay or capacity growth breaks C1.
2. **Drift test:** an independent frame that did not share the training data or metric. A self-graded learner preserving reach there breaks the mirror-chain reading of C2.
3. **Three-way discriminator:** an instrument that separates reach decay, channel decay and habitat substitution on one run. Its absence is the reason the question looks like one yes/no problem.
4. **Transfer/refusal tests:** transfer from an unsettled term, or an additive learner correcting a refuted old term, breaks C3.
