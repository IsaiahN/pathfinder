# The Tether's answer: how diseases move through ecological networks

**Mode: `specified` where applied to wildlife/livestock/people, `general` for the structural rules. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route with falsifiers, not a measured transmission path. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An infection appears in one host class and is not accounted for by a direct-contact list inside that class. The residual is indexed by slot: a particular pathogen, host and time (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: the donor, the recipient, the pathogen, the vector, the environment and non-host species all bind at once, so the overlap gets its own loop rather than an average (Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound.").

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**Domain E, ecological habitat and contact frame : verdict `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 + Fig 13).**
1. **The habitat is enumerated, never composed.** Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." :48 "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." What has not been observed is residual, not absent.
2. **The network is a structure in which influence travels.** Fig 13 :23-24 "network" / "that entities relate, and influence travels". It is one of the six substrate structures, not an aggregate of hosts. Craft 2015 corroborates from outside: "Contact networks are thus conduits for transmission pathways of infectious diseases in populations."
3. **Movement is a contact cascade, and order one is not enough.** Fig 11 :52-53 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." The first-order list is a hypothesis. The consequential step is typically a bridge host, a vector, environmental persistence or a trophic side effect at order two or three.

**Domain P, pathogen and host-shift frame : verdict `general` on the shape, single-source in figures, world-corroborated.**
4. **Roles are relative to the pathogen, not carried by the species.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds." :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Wildlife is not inherently reservoir, livestock inherently bridge, people inherently victim; the assignment changes with the pathogen. Hassell et al. 2017 independently distinguish maintenance, non-maintenance, bridge and dead-end hosts.
5. **A host jump is a lossy crossing, not a copy.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 10 :70 "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside." The branching phylogeny and the changed competence in the new host are the remainder.

**Domain M, management and intervention frame : verdict `general`, single-source in figures.**
6. **Intervention adds; it does not subtract.** Fig 11 :62 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you." Vaccination, barriers and alternative resources are additions to a habitat that persists. Roberts et al. 2021: "Removing a particular species identified as a reservoir from the ecosystem will not necessarily remove the pathogen."

Verdicts are not averaged. E is triangulated inside the framework; P and M are single-source there. Their world corroboration is real but does not upgrade them to `grounded`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a named route for a named disease. No species, place or date is asserted.
- Not a claim that wildlife is always the source or people always the victim. Roles are relative to the pathogen.
- Not a claim that every disease has a third-order effect. The rule is to enumerate until the cascade stops; where it stops is measured, not legislated.
- Not that a contact list is ever complete. Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."
- Not grounded. No measurement at the route's ground was taken in this run.
- Not that the framework's vocabulary replaces the field's. Network, reservoir, bridge and competence are bindings from the disease-ecology frame, provenanced as such.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another review. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the analogue is paired contact and genetic or serological data in one landscape, which can show which edges actually transmitted and where the cascade stopped. Craft 2015 gives the operational field rule: define the pathogen first, then build the contact network at the pathogen's timescale.

Standing falsifiers (pre-registered in `01` §5):
1. zero residual after order-1 enumeration for a named disease, with no bridge, vector, environmental stage or third-order effect;
2. a host class with fixed valence across pathogens;
3. a contact deleted from the habitat without maintenance;
4. a lossless, non-branching cross-species transfer;
5. derivation of Network from the other five substrate terms.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached:** the shape of a route, the order of the derivation, four refuters. **Unreached:** the actual path of any actual pathogen in this system. That is a `gap` for the ground, not a silence. "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence" (Fig 13 :134). The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; declaring it does not make it neutral. The answer stops where the instruments stop distinguishing and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a path for a particular disease and does not claim the contact list is complete. It says that a disease route through wildlife, livestock and people has the shape of a contact cascade over a habitat that must be enumerated outward from the pathogen, not composed from host classes; that the network is a real structure in which influence travels, not a sum of hosts; that reservoir, bridge, amplifier and dead end are roles borrowed from the pathogen rather than properties carried by a species; that the consequential transmission usually sits two or three steps out, so a first-order list is only a hypothesis; and that management adds actors to a habitat it cannot subtract. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, the enumeration rule, and four falsifiers written in advance. The named route stays open until paired contact and genetic data in one landscape pay for it.
