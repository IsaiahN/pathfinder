# Expert verification packet : the Tether on disease movement through ecological networks

**What this is:** a claim in a form a disease ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` for the host system, `general` for the structural rules, not `grounded`.** No named transmission path is asserted and no measurement was taken.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A disease route through a network connecting wildlife, livestock and people has the shape of a contact cascade over an enumerated habitat. The habitat is read off the world outward from the pathogen until the cascade stops mattering; unobserved contacts are residual, not absent. The network is a substrate structure in which entities relate and influence travels, not a sum of hosts. Reservoir, bridge, amplifying host and dead end are roles assigned by the pathogen, not properties carried by a species. The consequential transmission typically sits two or three steps out, so a first-order contact list is a hypothesis. Management adds actors to a habitat it cannot subtract. The framework returns this shape and its refuters; it does not name the route of any particular disease.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11 and 13
- **Statement:** the habitat is enumerated outward from the residual; the network is a structure in which influence travels; a first-order contact list is insufficient in general.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels". Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."
- **Refuter:** a named disease whose transmission is fully accounted for by first-order direct contacts, with no bridge, vector, environmental stage or trophic side effect, and with residual zero after enumeration.

### C2 : ecology-facing; checkable against the recipient's own system
- **Statement:** host roles are relative to the pathogen. The same actor can be reservoir for one pathogen and bridge or dead end for another; there is no standing list of source species.
- **What to check:** in a system they know well, can a species change epidemiological role when the pathogen changes? Hassell et al. 2017 (opened) distinguish maintenance, non-maintenance, bridge and dead-end hosts; Craft 2015 (opened) shows the transmission network is a subset of the contact network and that the relevant contact depends on the pathogen.
- **Refuter:** a host class with a fixed valence as source, bridge or sink across pathogens, such that the same actor cannot be re-assigned when the residual changes.

### C3 : framework-internal derivation, with a management consequence
- **Statement:** a host jump is a lossy crossing into a habitat (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."; Fig 10 :70 "If levels were copies there would be one lineage drifting and no branches. The tree of life is what a non-invertible transform looks like from outside."), and interventions add actors rather than subtracting the habitat (Fig 11 :62 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you.").
- **What to check:** is a host shift ever a lossless copy with unchanged competence and no branching? Can a manager delete a contact from the habitat and keep it deleted without maintenance?
- **Refuter:** a lossless, non-branching cross-species transfer; or a contact deleted from the habitat without continuing maintenance, confirmed by the ground.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a theory of the route of any named disease, and no named species, place or date.
- Not that wildlife is always the source, livestock always the bridge, or people always the victim. Roles are relative to the pathogen.
- Not that every disease has a third-order effect. Enumeration stops where the residual stops, and that is measured.
- Not a complete contact list. Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."
- Not grounded. No measurement pays for any claim here.
- Not a replacement for disease-ecology vocabulary. Network, bridge host, reservoir and competence are bindings from that frame, provenanced in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating measurement, not further argument: paired contact-network and pathogen genetic or serological data across wildlife, livestock and people in one landscape, showing which edges transmitted and at what order the cascade stopped. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the instrument reads green but "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved the analogue. Until such data exist, the verdict stays `candidate`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :43-44, :49-53 and :62, and Fig 13 :23-24. Five short passages.
2. Name one pathogen they know well and list the contacts they would enumerate at orders 1, 2 and 3.
3. Say whether a species in that system changes role when the pathogen changes. If yes, C2 is corroborated; if no, state the case.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish among candidate sources of the same infection pattern, or say why none exists.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
