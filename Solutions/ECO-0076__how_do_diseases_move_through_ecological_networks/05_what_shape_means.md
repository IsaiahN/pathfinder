## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the form a route must have before any named pathogen has been traced. It constrains: a route is a cascade over an enumerated habitat, with typed contact bonds and roles relative to the pathogen. It does not fill: it does not say that H5N1 went from wild birds to poultry to people in a particular market on a particular date. `route = network over hosts with typed bonds, cascaded to the order where the residual stops` is a shape. `wild bird → pig → person` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Here the nearest available terms were contact network, reservoir, bridge host, spillover, competence, cascade.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". A description in effect terms is "what you would see in the case data if this route were real".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` asks for an expected enumeration rather than an endorsement.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." The run stops when the shape and the refuter are the same object, not when a story feels complete.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For ECO-0076 that means paired contact and genetic or serological data in one landscape.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test. The four disease-ecology reviews are such imports; they are recorded with author, venue, year, quote and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

A specific named route is licensed only by one of these. Until then, naming it is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The difference between a route and a reading of a route

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same error is live here. An infection cluster in people is a reading. It does not determine whether the source was a wildlife reservoir, a livestock amplifying host, a vector, an environmental stage, or several of these in sequence. Craft 2015 makes the operational version of the point: "the transmission network is typically a subset of the contact network, as not all contacts lead to disease transmission." A contact map alone is the projection; the source needs a discriminating instrument (genetic or serological data), which is the spectrometer analogue.

So the honest output of this derivation is the set of source-discriminating questions, not a source.

## 5. Consequences for this question

**What the framework can say.** The shape any answer must have: enumerate the habitat outward; treat network as the structure in which influence travels; assign roles relative to the pathogen, not the species; expect the consequential step at second or third order; expect interventions to add rather than subtract.

**What the framework cannot say.** Which species, in which place, carried which infection. That is a slot the figures leave open. Naming it requires the ground.

**A correction I made to my own first reading.** I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming any actor in the route. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Wildlife, livestock and people are already named in the question; they are the given actors, not missing primitives. What is missing is the **assignment of roles and the order of contact**, and that is exactly what stays open. The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and this audit is a simulation

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous reviewers who agree with the route is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against case data.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the route and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-sentence answer to "what shape means" here

A shape is the constraint on a disease route that can be stated before any transmission event is measured: a cascade over an enumerated habitat, with the network as the structure in which influence travels and every actor's role borrowed from the pathogen rather than carried by the species; the named path is owed only when the ground pays for it.
