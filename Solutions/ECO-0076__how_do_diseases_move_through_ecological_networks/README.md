## What this folder is

A derive-first package that asks what the Tether figures, read as metalaws, license us to say about the route a pathogen takes through a wildlife/livestock/human network. The figures are primary and decide. The four opened disease-ecology reviews are secondary corroboration only. The whole run is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself") it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Boundary.** No file outside this folder was read for the purpose of editing and no file outside it was changed. Framework sources were the canonical local copies in `FRAMEWORK/reference/` and `FRAMEWORK/figures/`. World sources were opened on 2026-11-23 and are listed with quotes in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## The residual in effect terms

What has to be explained is an infection appearing in one host class (say people or cattle) that is not accounted for by a list of direct contacts inside that class. Option A treats movement as a traceable chain of direct host-to-host contacts. Option B treats movement as a **contact cascade through an enumerated habitat**, where each actor's role (reservoir, bridge, amplifying host, dead end) is a relation relative to the particular pathogen and where the consequential step sits two or three links out. A answers with a path; B answers with a shape and a route plus a stated refuter. What each leads to: A produces a tidy line that misses the bridge host and the environmental stage; B produces a checkable enumeration that says which links must be measured before a path can be named. An answer is bound as the ordinary term `route = network(structure) over hosts(actors) with typed contact bonds, cascaded to the order where the residual stops moving`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, recorded once here and developed in `01`/`04`: Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."; Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A review selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
