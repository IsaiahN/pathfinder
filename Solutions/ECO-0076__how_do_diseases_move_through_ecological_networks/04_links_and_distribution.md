## What the wider literature adds, and what it does not

1. **Contact is pathogen-specific.** Craft 2015 shows that the same physical contact is relevant for one pathogen and irrelevant for another, and that the transmission network is a subset of the contact network. This corroborates the figure rule that valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49-51) and the instruction to define the pathogen before enumerating.
2. **Interfaces are networks of interfaces.** Hassell et al. 2017 explicitly model the system as "a network of interfaces" and distinguish maintenance, non-maintenance, bridge and dead-end hosts. This is the strongest world corroboration for F3 and F4. It comes from a different evidence pool from the framework, so it is a real witness at low overlap.
3. **Barriers are hierarchical and role varies.** Meurens et al. 2021 and Roberts et al. 2021 both emphasize that host competence varies and that spillover requires passing a series of barriers. This corroborates the barrier shape in Fig 6 :23 and Fig 10 :70 without supplying a figure line.
4. **Agreement among the reviews is not four witnesses.** Three of the four draw heavily on the same outbreak and modelling literature (Jones et al., Plowright et al., Becker et al. recur across them). Per Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool," their convergence is one pool talking. It is recorded as corroboration of shape, not as proof of a route.

**No world source conflicts with the figure verdict.** The conflict rule was not triggered. If a source had reported a fully first-order, single-class transmission with residual zero and no bridge or environmental stage, it would have fired refuter 1 and sent the derivation back to the census; none did.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Lead with the shape claim and the refuter. The full framework goes only after a stage-1 verdict is recorded.
- Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Ask what the recipient would expect to see in their own data; do not ask whether they like the framework.
- Fig 9 :27-28: describe the residual before naming a frame, or any frame will seem to fit. The first contact therefore names no journal, no authority and no author.
- Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." No list selected for receptiveness, no bulk send, no later agreement pooled as corroboration.
- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the enumeration method and its refuter is a generator; sending this whole folder is a recording of one occasion. Send the former first.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask for the enumeration they would expect and the order at which they would look; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then offer the three local framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
