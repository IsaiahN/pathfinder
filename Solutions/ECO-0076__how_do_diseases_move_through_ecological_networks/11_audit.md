## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`; Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-44, :49-53 and :62, Fig 13 :23-24, Fig 6 :23, Fig 10 :70.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand the folder actually carries: diseases move by contact cascade over an enumerated habitat; roles are relative to the pathogen; interventions add, not subtract; the named path stays open.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example "avian influenza moved from wild birds through farmed poultry to people."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three constraints held by the shorthand do not survive the return:

- the requirement to **enumerate outward until the cascade stops**, which real accounts truncate at the directly observed host pair;
- **role relativity**, which is frozen into "wild birds are the source" as though valence were carried by the species;
- the **asymmetry of intervention**, which is replaced by plans to remove the reservoir rather than add barriers and surveillance.

One thing survives cleanly: a structure must sit between the observed case and the claimed source, and the contacts must be enumerated before a route is named. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figure set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
