## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the source. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **Shape:** a precisely measured ratio (`theta_*`), an imported absolute scale (`r_s`), an estimate that moves with the assumed pre-recombination model, a residual localized in one slot, and a bounded round-trip gap.
- **Name (not supplied):** "the missing component is early dark energy" or "it is extra relativistic species". Choosing one is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such. The framework cannot settle it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Recurse on proximity between known and recurring new terms.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with an effect description (the ratio-versus-import claim) and a refuter, not with the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is the bound on `r_s`, not a named component.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So assuming base Lambda-CDM is a visible choice, not a neutral default. The whole question is about what that choice costs.

## 4. Why this question is specifically a source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it maps unusually well onto CMB inference. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, each gives the same reading. The essay states: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

Here the reading is `theta_*`. Several pre-recombination histories (standard radiation content, extra relativistic species, early dark energy, altered recombination) can produce the same `theta_*` with different `r_s`, hence different H0. The reading does not determine the source. What resolved the color case was "the arrival of a better instrument", the spectrometer, not an argument. The analogue here is an independent sound-horizon measurement (BAO plus BBN, as DESI reports), not a better fit to the same spectra.

This is not an analogy imposed on the question. The figure rule Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause" and the source essay's instrument argument both point to the same limit: the observable is an effect, the source is underdetermined until an instrument discriminates.

## 5. Q answered: what licenses a particular early-universe component?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape: an imported scale and a bound on its movement. A particular component is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

Planck's own wording matches: "These results are only weakly dependent on the cosmological model and remain stable, with somewhat increased errors, in many commonly considered extensions." The 0.03% measurement is stable; the inferred H0 is conditional. Poulin, Smith and Karwal report that "current data remain inconclusive on the existence of an EDE phase". So the source is still open, and naming it would be premature.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially treated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming `r_s` at all. That was an overstatement, and it is corrected here rather than erased.

- The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library.
- `r_s` is not a missing primitive. It is a derived quantity that the early-universe frame already computes from held atoms (baryon density, radiation content, expansion history). What the inference frame lacks is access to it, which is why it is imported (Fig 6 :23).
- So naming `r_s` is permitted and necessary. What remains forbidden is naming **which** new component nature used to change it. That is still a source claim, and the metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted: the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming derived quantities.
- I also said the six-parameter Lambda-CDM fit "drops" early-universe physics as if the compression were lossless elsewhere. Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." The dropping is the point, and it is what the question is about. Retained and sharpened.

Both the original and the correction are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous cosmologists who agree with the framing is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an instrument, an independent sound-horizon measurement, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay also says "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
