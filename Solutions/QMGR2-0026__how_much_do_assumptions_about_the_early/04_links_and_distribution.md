## The two-stage offer, and why the order matters

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the framing, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict must be recorded first.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator; giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction": a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive. If a cosmologist points out that the ratio-versus-import distinction is already standard in the field, that is not a refutation of the shape; it is a correction to its novelty, and it should be recorded as such. If they show that H0 can be extracted without an early-universe model, the claim is refuted and nothing further is sent.
