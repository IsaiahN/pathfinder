# Outreach draft - check one output before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output below must survive the reader's check before any framework material is offered. If it does not, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about what the CMB can and cannot tell us about H0

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read any theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` shape, `specified` load-bearing claim, not `grounded`):** the cosmic microwave background tightly measures a ratio, the acoustic angular scale `theta_* = r_s / D_A`. Planck reports 100 theta_* = 1.0411 +/- 0.0003, about 0.03%. It does **not** measure the sound horizon `r_s` or the Hubble constant directly. To turn the ratio into an H0 you must supply `r_s`, and `r_s` is fixed by assumed pre-recombination physics: the effective number of relativistic species, the recombination history, and whether any extra energy density was present before decoupling. So the estimate is a function of (data, assumed early-universe model), not of data alone.
>
> The consequence is that the same observed sky yields different H0 values under different early-universe assumptions. Under base Lambda-CDM, Planck infers 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc. The local distance ladder reports 73.04 +/- 1.04. The difference is about 5 sigma. My claim is that the early-universe import is the dominant systematic in that inference, while the measured ratio itself is essentially assumption-free. That is a claim about which quantities a measurement holds and which it imports, not a claim that any particular new component exists.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that early dark energy is the answer; that extra relativistic species are the answer; that the tension is therefore solved; or that the CMB is wrong. The framework returns a shape, not a name. It says a sound horizon must be imported and that the size of the effect is bounded by how far that imported quantity can move under allowed assumptions. It does not say which primitive is missing.
>
> **What would refute the claim (written down before this message):**
> 1. an extraction of H0 from CMB data that supplies no pre-recombination model and still reaches the precision of the model-dependent inference;
> 2. varying N_eff, any early dark energy fraction and recombination history within allowed ranges leaves the inferred H0 unchanged within the quoted errors;
> 3. a demonstration that the sound horizon is already determined by the measurement alone, so no import occurs;
> 4. a shared calibration or evidence pool between the CMB inference and the local ladder large enough to explain the whole offset.
>
> If any holds, I withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Planck 2018 VI (cosmological parameters): https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209
> - Riess et al. 2022 (SH0ES local H0): https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04510
> - Poulin, Smith and Karwal 2023 (early dark energy review): https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09032
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula and figures are public at https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures . Run them on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links with the note that the figures, not our summary, are the authority. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.
