# The Tether's answer: how much early-universe assumptions affect CMB estimates

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the load-bearing claim. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a bound, not a new measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The same observed CMB sky yields different inferred H0 values depending on the assumed pre-recombination model. In effect terms the estimate is `H0_hat = F(data, model)`, not `F(data)`. The residual is not a global fit failure: Planck finds "good consistency with the standard spatially-flat 6-parameter Lambda-CDM cosmology" and "no compelling evidence for extensions". It is a per-slot residual in the H0 slot when the inference is ranged against an independent local measurement. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

This is a **union compound**, not one hard question: a CMB-inferred H0 and a locally measured H0 are two conditions, and a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9).

## 2. The shape

1. **The CMB measures a ratio, not a rate.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The observed acoustic scale `theta_* = r_s / D_A` is measured to 0.03% by Planck. H0 is not in that reading.
2. **The sound horizon is imported from the assumed early-universe frame.** Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." `r_s` is not in the measurement closure; it is supplied by the model. Fig 6 :22 "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
3. **Therefore the estimate moves when the assumptions move, and is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :29-30 "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." The anchor is the measured ratio; the inferred H0 is a frame output.
4. **The effect is large, but it costs an atom.** Reducing `r_s` by a few percent can raise the inferred H0 from 67.4 into the low 70s, close to the local value 73.04. Poulin et al. 2019 find regions where the Planck-inferred H0 "agrees with the SH0ES local measurement". But the mechanism adds new early-universe content, and Fig 6 :24 "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Poulin, Smith and Karwal 2023 report that "current data remain inconclusive on the existence of an EDE phase".
5. **The two frames have low overlap.** Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 8 :48-49 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Verde, Treu and Riess 2019: the tension holds for "any three independent approaches" and "does not appear to be dependent on the use of any one method, team, or source".
6. **The bound is the movement of the imported scale.** Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Compressing full pre-recombination physics into a six-parameter fit loses exactly the options that move `r_s`. The honest answer to "how much" is the size of that round-trip gap R_T, which is nonzero but bounded by data.
7. **No primitive is named.** Fig 13 :134 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Whether the missing piece is extra relativistic species, early dark energy, or something else is left to the ground.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the CMB is wrong, or that Planck's 0.03% acoustic-scale measurement is in doubt.
- Not that the tension is solved. Reachability is not settlement; Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- Not that early dark energy, extra neutrinos, or altered recombination is the answer. Those are bindings from the physics frame, not figure outputs.
- Not that the local ladder is assumption-free. It has its own calibration chain; the point is that its anchor is geometric distances, which do not move with pre-recombination physics.
- Not `grounded`. No new measurement was taken here; the numeric values are world facts from opened sources.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no new primitive.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further fit to the same spectra. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a color instrument "cannot comment on the source"; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here:

- An **independent sound-horizon measurement** at decoupling, so `r_s` stops being an import. DESI 2024 VI, with BAO plus a BBN baryon-density prior plus the CMB acoustic scale, finds "H0=(68.52+/-0.62) km s-1 Mpc-1", staying near the low value and bounding how far assumptions can move the inference.
- A **direct, assumption-light H0 from the CMB** that does not require a pre-recombination model at comparable precision. That would falsify the load-bearing claim.
- An **invariance test**: vary N_eff, early dark energy fraction and recombination history within allowed ranges and check whether the inferred H0 stays put. If it does, the claim fails.
- A **shared-pool finding**: show that the CMB inference and the local ladder share enough calibration or evidence to explain the whole offset. Then the "tension" is a convention, not two frames ranging against the ground.
- The pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 lists these before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is stable: a ratio is measured, a scale is imported, the estimate moves with the assumptions, the residual is per slot, the gap is cross-scale. The size is bounded by opened world sources, not by the figures. The framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest". "Indiscernibility is not identity": several early-universe histories give the same acoustic reading, and an instrument, not a preference, must separate them. Which component (if any) nature used remains open.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The CMB does not measure the Hubble constant. It measures an acoustic angle, `theta_* = r_s / D_A`, to about 0.03%. Turning that ratio into H0 requires a sound horizon `r_s`, and `r_s` is fixed by assumed pre-recombination physics, not by the data. So the inferred H0 is a function of the data and the assumed early universe together. Under base Lambda-CDM Planck gets 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc; the local distance ladder gets 73.04 +/- 1.04; the gap is about 5 sigma. Early-universe assumptions are therefore not a small correction: they are the dominant systematic in the CMB-to-H0 inference, and plausible changes can move the estimate by roughly the size of the gap, at the price of adding new physics that current data do not yet require. What the framework supplies is the shape of that answer and its bound, not the name of the missing component and not a claim that the tension is solved. An independent sound-horizon measurement, not another fit to the same sky, is what would settle it.
