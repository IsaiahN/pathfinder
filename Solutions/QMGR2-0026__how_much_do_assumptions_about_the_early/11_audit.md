## Round trip gap R_T

- **the level of the import.** The shorthand says "`r_s` is imported"; the concrete derivation requires the model-frame mint / inference-frame import split, or the word "import" is misapplied (Fig 12 :103).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a ratio is measured, an absolute scale is imported, and the estimate is a function of the assumed model. That is the residue worth sending.
