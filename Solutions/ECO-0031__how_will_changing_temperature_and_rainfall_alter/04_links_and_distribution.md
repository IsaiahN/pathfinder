## Wider literature that bears on the residual

These were encountered while opening the sources above and are listed as context, not as independently opened primary rows. They are not used as witnesses in `09_watson_answer.md`.

- **Phenological mismatch.** Visser & Gienapp 2019, Nature Ecology & Evolution, on demographic consequences of phenological mismatches; cited in the Carroll et al. reference list.
- **Match-mismatch evidence dispute.** Kharouba & Wolkovich 2023, Ecology Letters, *"Lack of evidence for the match-mismatch hypothesis across terrestrial trophic interactions"*, cited in the Carroll et al. reference list. This is a genuine conflict in the literature and is named in `09_watson_answer.md` as a source of direction uncertainty, not smoothed away.
- **Biotic interactions at range edges.** Paquette & Hargreaves 2021, Ecology Letters, on biotic interactions being more often important at warm range edges; cited in the Carroll et al. reference list.
- **Co-occurrence is not interaction.** Blanchet, Cazelles & Gravel 2020, Ecology Letters; cited in the Carroll et al. reference list. This is a direct warning against inferring interactions from range overlap, which is the core of the residual.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Send the three routes and the refuter first. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if each route were real before giving them the article.
- **Fig 1 :22** *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Ask them to name one interaction, not one network index.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Never rank recipients by likely agreement, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a recording of a conclusion is a replay; sending the generation method and the refuter is a generator.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the three-route shape; (2) the pre-registered refuter, and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict before any theory; (4) only if they are curious, the three links, with the note that the figures are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground. The recipient checks the decomposition without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the figures and the loop. Their stage-1 verdict was already recorded, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. If a checker shows that an interaction type is fixed by species identities alone, or that effects never propagate past direct contact, the three-route decomposition is withdrawn.
