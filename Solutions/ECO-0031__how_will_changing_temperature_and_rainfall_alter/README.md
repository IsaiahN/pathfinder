## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list of agreeable readers is a weighted average of influence, not an anchor.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The outside frame must not hold the interiors before giving its verdict.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## What this solution does not claim

The figures constrain the shape of any answer to this question. They do not name which interaction will strengthen or weaken in a given place. That directional question is a genuine `gap` at this budget, recorded as such in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`. A shape is not a silence, and unreachable is a verdict.
