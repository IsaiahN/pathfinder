## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name a single interaction:

- not "bee X will stop visiting flower Y" (a name and a direction);
- but "interaction change decomposes into three routes, tracked per interaction, with direction left to the ground" (a constraint).

The shape constrains; it does not fill. This is why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` binds the shape as an ordinary term before the run and lets it accrue a residual, instead of leaving it as an invisible preference (Fig 5 :65).

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct warrant for splitting the question into a union compound (F6) before searching.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So the shape is stated as changes in relations and contact, not as a mechanism name borrowed from a subfield.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email therefore asks what a checker would expect to see under each route before showing them the framework.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means field observation of a specific interaction under a measured temperature and rainfall.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The seven world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are exactly such imports: each was opened, dated, and recorded with the gap it explains.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this (quoted in the exemplar): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral.

**Correction carried from the exemplar.** The exemplar overstated "shape, not a name" and then narrowed it: the phrase governs a *missing* primitive, not the selection of atoms already held. For this question the correction matters differently. The directional answer is not a selection among held atoms; it is a prediction about an enumerated habitat, so it genuinely is not in the closure. The `gap` in F8 stands. I do not use "shape, not a name" to excuse refusing a direction that could be measured; I use it to explain why the framework alone cannot supply that direction.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. And: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The resolution was not an argument: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Applied here: a network-level metric (connectance, nestedness) is a projection. It can stay nearly constant while the underlying links change. Several different interaction histories give the same reading. The instrument that discriminates is a per-interaction observation, not a better aggregate formula. This is F4's warrant from a different (human cognition / instrumentation) field.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What is the shape of an answer to "how will changing temperature and rainfall alter species interactions?"**

A three-route decomposition, per-interaction tracking, and an explicitly open direction:

1. range-mediated contact change (who meets whom);
2. condition-mediated valence change (how the same pair relates under new conditions);
3. cascading indirect effects (what happens three steps out).

The routes are a union compound, not a list; each needs its own ground. The shape says what any acceptable answer must contain. It does not say which interaction changes, in which direction, in which place.

**Is the directional question answerable from the figures?** No. It needs habitat enumeration and field interaction data. That is a `gap`, recorded plainly. "Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence."

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-cited ecologists selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a field measurement, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
