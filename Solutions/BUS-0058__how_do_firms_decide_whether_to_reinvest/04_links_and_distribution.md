## 3. Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on the pitch

1. **The hierarchy in Brav et al.** is not a universal rule but a measured priority ordering: dividend level protected, repurchases made from residual cash after investment. This corroborates the F1 and F3 order (search and settle first, refuse and distribute second) without importing the framework into the finance result. It is a secondary witness, not the verdict.
2. **Miller and Modigliani's irrelevance theorem** is the strongest apparent conflict with Fig 12's "the bond is the rest of the information". The conflict is dissolved by mode, not by denial: MM is `general` inside a perfect-market frame, while the figures bind notation in any frame and the empirical record (taxes, smoothing, asymmetric substitution) is ground-side. This is S2 in `01` and must be disclosed rather than smoothed over. A finance audience will notice it immediately.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and the refuters; offer the links after stage 1 is recorded.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the theory before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".** Do not solicit agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** A wrong routing destroys real cash, so the cost of being wrong is high; that is why outside checkers are worth buying and why their independence must be preserved.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual in effect terms and the five refuters; (2) the stage-1 question, what would you expect to see and what would make you reject it; (3) record that answer before any theory is shown; (4) only then, if the recipient opts in, the figure text and formula links, with the note that the figures and not this summary are the authority; (5) only on request, the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## 5. Legal and honesty constraints

- Public institutional and opt-in channels only (`03_public_contact_points.csv`). No personal emails, no purchased lists, no bulk send, no misleading subject lines. An opt-out line belongs in every one-to-one message.
- This folder is a **simulated derivation**. Any submission through a journal or conference must declare that the work was model-assisted and has not been checked against a ground. Do not present it as an empirical finance result.
- Presenting the framework as having settled the payout question would be a mode violation under `THE_FORMULA.md`: *"Accepted means the ground settled it; otherwise candidate."* The correct label is `specified` plus `gap`, candidate.
