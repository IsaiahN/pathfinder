## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to accept the shape.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. Frames range against the ground, they do not negotiate with it: Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." A list selected for agreement is a list selected to be a mirror.
2. Correlation disqualifies a verdict: Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Asking a finance audience to endorse a shape manufactures the very correlation that would void its agreement.
3. An outside frame must not hold the interiors: Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
