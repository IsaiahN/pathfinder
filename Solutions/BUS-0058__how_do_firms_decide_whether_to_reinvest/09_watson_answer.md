## QUESTION

Option A: treat them as four alternatives on one menu and return a universal rule or percentage. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R, the residual.** Free cash flow after obligations, held by a firm and awaiting routing. Arity: one residual quantity, four visible routes, at least four overlapping conditions. Symmetry: dividends and buybacks are near-isomers over a fixed atom set; reinvest and payout are ordered, not symmetric; debt reduction ranges against a different claimant. Scale: level 1 is the per-slot routing decision, level 2 is the accumulated capital structure. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The structure is a union, not a partition: Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
- **D1, internal investment.** Actors: management as maker, projects, the firm's held capabilities. Currency: net present value and return on invested capital versus cost of capital. Anchor: the market that actually pays for the output, which does not update on management's forecast. Question type: partition inside the closure, is there a paying use in here?
- **D2, capital structure and creditor.** Actors: lenders, covenants, treasury, the market's rating. Currency: interest, tax shield, distress cost, reserve borrowing power. Anchor: the market pricing of the debt and the fixed contractual claim. Question type: a threshold comparison with a target that must remain violable.
- **D3, shareholder payout.** Actors: shareholders as residual claimants, board, management. Currency: cash per share, tax treatment, signal, flexibility. Anchor: the share price and total return the market pays, which do not update on stated intent. Question type: a union inside the payout domain, two bonds over the same atoms.
- **D4, governance and agency.** Actors: shareholders, board, management as agent, the market for corporate control. Currency: free cash flow under discretionary control. Anchor: the capital market the firm must face when internal cash runs out. Question type: refusal, which held term has a residual that persists and does not trend down?
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1, D2, D3 and D4 can bind on the same dollar; a single lever clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-57).
- **Order by dependency.** Measure per slot; name what cash affords; state the objective; route the cash; carry the method forward. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Within routing: search the closure, sharpen inward if needed, import outward with provenance, refuse the flat residual, return it to the claimant. Refuse before mint.

Strain test applied: the MM irrelevance result versus the bond-as-information claim is a mode difference, not a figure conflict (different frames, `general` theorem versus notation in any frame, `01` S2). The foraging analogue is restricted to the stay-or-leave shape because its fitness currency is single and the firm has several claimants (`01` S5). After re-census, no unhandled contradiction.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The four options are four routes for one per-slot residual, governed by a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, not four answers to one menu question. Search the existing reach first; only a new capability is a wall-moving import with provenance and a shadow test. Anchor every domain on a market that does not update on the firm's own report. Treat a leverage target as a violable comparison. Treat dividends and buybacks as isomers whose bond is priced. Treat free cash with no paying use as a term to refuse, not to explain again. Accumulate settled routes into capital structure at the next level; carry methods, not recordings. Return no ratio. The result is `specified` on the routing claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the number, ungrounded and candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** real cash is routed to a channel that does not pay and the channel to the market anchor decays while the anchor itself does not (Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"). There is also the cost of spending outside checkers' independence by sending a convention instead of a separator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The pre-registered refuter is what makes the cost locatable rather than invisible.
- **undoable:** partly. A single period's routing can be superseded next period, and a wrongly named binding can be corrected without withdrawing the shape. But the bonds are not symmetric: a dividend cut is punished and a buyback program is easier to stop, while retired debt is hard to reissue on the same terms. The asymmetry is itself a finding (Brav et al. 2005), so undoability is per domain and is never averaged.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at the firm's own slot that shows which route actually paid; an anchor test in which the value metric refuses to move with management's forecast; a jurisdiction in which the two payout bonds are identical; a demonstration that persistent free cash with no paying use increases value with contact held constant; or a verbatim figure line that returns a universal ratio. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and endorsement are not such instruments.
