# The Tether's answer on how firms route free cash flow

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the firm-specific number. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A firm holds free cash flow after obligations. That cash is a residual indexed per firm and per use-slot, not a global number. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The four visible routes, reinvest, reduce debt, pay dividends, buy back shares, are not four answers to one question. They are the tips of four overlapping conditions with different anchors, so the structure is a **union compound** and each domain gets its own loop and its own verdict (Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged into a "correct payout policy".

## 2. The shape

1. **Search the existing reach first.** Reinvestment is tested before distribution, but only if a paying use is reachable with capabilities the firm already holds. Ordinary reinvestment is composition inside the closure and adds no atom (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). A paying use that needs a new capability is an import and requires provenance and a shadow test. This order is SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD from Formula step 7.
2. **The anchor is a market that does not update on the firm's report.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A forecast, a signal, a target ratio chosen by the board are frame readings, not anchors. The anchor is the price actually paid, the customer who actually buys, the claim a lender can actually enforce. If a value metric moves when management changes its own assumption, it has become a frame (Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update.").
3. **Debt reduction is a violable comparison.** A leverage target is a goal, hence a threshold comparison between quantities. It must remain something the market can violate; a target no one will break has stopped being a convention and fixes the outcome (Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"). In the creditor's frame the retired claim is absent; in the firm's frame the debt term loses its standing to be composed over.
4. **Dividends and buybacks are isomers, not synonyms.** Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance." Over the fixed atom set {cash, shares, shareholders}, a dividend is a recurring obligation that is costly to cut, while a buyback is a discrete repurchase with no such obligation. The empirical record (Lintner 1956, Brav et al. 2005) shows the bond is priced: dividend cuts are punished and substitution between the two is asymmetric. Which bond pays in a given jurisdiction is not answered here.
5. **Free cash with no paying internal use is a refusal, not another explanation.** Its residual persists and does not trend down, so the owed term refuses the standing of retention: Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". A payout removes management's standing to keep composing over cash that has no paying use. This is `¬` in the firm's frame, not `−`.
6. **Capital structure is a second-order accumulation.** Each settled period's routing becomes an atom at the next level: retained earnings accumulate, retired debt stays retired, a dividend policy becomes an expectation (Fig 12 :104 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."). Only methods and head starts cross between periods, never a recording of one year's allocation (Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").
7. **The number is a declared gap.** No figure line returns a payout ratio, a dividend amount, a buyback schedule or a target debt ratio. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The framework returns a kind of thing, and the stop is recorded: Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The instrument owed is a market-side, source-discriminating reading of the marginal return to each route at the firm's own slot.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a universal payout percentage, target leverage, dividend growth rate or buyback schedule.
- Not that one of the four options is always best, or that the pecking order or the static tradeoff has been derived. Those are bindings from the finance frame with provenance owed.
- Not that Miller and Modigliani were wrong. Their irrelevance result is a `general` theorem within a perfect-market frame; the figures bind notation in any frame, and the empirical record supplies taxes, rigidity and asymmetric substitution at the ground. The tension is named, not smoothed (`01` S2).
- Not that firms are foragers. The animal-behaviour source corroborates only the shape of the stay-or-leave test (`01` S5).
- Not a claim about any particular firm. There is no measurement in this folder.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by market payment.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-22: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Route discriminator.** An instrument that shows which route actually paid at the firm's own slot, separating a project's market payment from a favourable habitat, a tax change, a buyback-driven accounting effect and an unrelated market move.
2. **Anchor test.** Does the candidate value metric move when management changes its own forecast? If yes, it is a frame, not an anchor.
3. **Bond test.** In the relevant jurisdiction and horizon, do dividend reductions and buyback cancellations have different market consequences, as the isomer claim predicts? If they are identical in bond, the claim fails.
4. **Refusal test.** Does persistent free cash with no paying internal use fail to increase value once contact is held constant? If it does increase value persistently, the refusal verdict fails.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §5:** derive a universal ratio from a figure line, move the wall with an atomless reinvestment, collapse the two payout bonds, partition the four options with an assignable shared fraction, or void the pre-registered refuter.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** The routing shape is stated and its falsifiers are written. The number is not, because the instrument that would discriminate the paying route is not held at this budget. Importing a conventional ratio to fill the gap would add an atom that is not needed and would cost more than starting from primitives (Fig 8 :39 "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"). The record stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical figures README).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell a firm what percentage to pay out, and it does not pick a winner among dividends, buybacks, debt retirement and reinvestment. It says the four options are not a menu but the tips of four overlapping conditions on one per-slot free-cash-flow residual, each settled by a different market anchor that does not update on the firm's own report. A firm searches its existing reach for a paying use first, and only a genuinely new capability is an import needing provenance; a leverage target is a comparison the market must be able to violate; dividends and buybacks are isomers over the same atoms with different bonds, one recurring and costly to cut, the other discrete and flexible; and free cash with no paying use is a term whose residual keeps being wrong, so the owed move is a refusal that removes management's standing to compose over it. Each settled period becomes an atom for the next, while only methods and head starts cross between periods. No figure line returns a ratio, so that part is reported as a declared gap with the instrument it would take to close it. What is offered is the shape, the separate verdicts, the falsifiers written in advance, and the stop recorded honestly.
