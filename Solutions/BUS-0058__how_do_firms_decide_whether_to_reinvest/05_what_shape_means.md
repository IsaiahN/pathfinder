## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish, and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, opened:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0058 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of a routing rule that can be stated before any particular ratio, target or payout instrument is named:

- Shape: *a union of four overlapping conditions on a per-slot free-cash-flow residual, each with an anchor that does not update on the firm's own report; a search-then-refuse order; two payout bonds that are isomers over a fixed atom set; and a second-order accumulation of settled routes. The number stays a declared gap.*
- Name, withheld: *a 40 percent payout ratio, a target debt-to-equity of 1.0, a dividend-smoothing coefficient, a buyback schedule, or "the pecking order is the correct policy".*

The distinction matters because corporate finance is almost entirely in the naming business. Myers names a pecking order and a static tradeoff. Jensen names free cash flow and debt as a bonding device. Lintner names a partial-adjustment coefficient. Brav et al. name a priority ordering. Those are bindings from the finance frame onto this shape, not outputs of the loop. Naming them is allowed (see §6), but calling them figure-derived would be false.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure, opened:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "payout policy", "free cash flow", "capital structure", "agency cost", "dividend smoothing" and "share repurchase". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second." The description in effect terms (a per-slot free-cash-flow residual routed across conditions with different anchors) was fixed before the finance frames were selected. Reversing that order would make any cited paper seem to fit, which is the Fig 9 :27-28 failure: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

The essay also names the bundle stop, opened:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the warrant for treating the four options as a union in `01` §1.3 and finding F2, rather than as one hard question with four answers.

The stop condition, opened:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

Here the stop is reached when the four conditions, their anchors, the refusal order, the isomer relation and the gap are all stated and each has a falsifier. Further searching that only renames a ratio is motion, not progress: Fig 7 :32 "one long room: motion, no progress".

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0058 neither has happened for the number. There is no source-discriminating instrument here that shows which route paid in a given slot, and the conventional ratios were adopted by the finance frame without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` in `01` F9. The animal-behaviour source supplies a shape analogue (leave versus stay), not a name for a ratio, and its scope is narrowed in `01` S5.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened:

> *"What if you have metrics that do not discriminate about its inputs?"*
> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A high return on equity or a rising share price is a reading. It is consistent with a protected internal use, a favourable habitat, a tax change, a buyback-driven earnings-per-share effect, an accounting choice, or a market that has moved for reasons unrelated to the payout. The reading does not determine the source. When a firm says "the buyback created value" from the price reading alone, it commits the metamerism error: a source named from a projection. No amount of agreement among CFOs or analysts fixes it; what is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources. Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the verification packet asks for a failure condition and a discriminator before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to finance outreach

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

A list of famous finance professors who endorse the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs. This is the author's own agent-design instance of Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict" and Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does", so it is framework-internal corroboration, not independent evidence about firms.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any particular payout ratio or target as even a candidate. That was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. A target payout ratio, a leverage target or a smoothing coefficient is already a term in the finance frame; naming it is a binding from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about firms and `general` for the reading of shape. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. Contact, subtext and the limits of a written packet

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened:

> *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
> *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."*
> *"Two people who only check against each other drift, and they drift together, so neither notices."*

A written verification packet is a plain effect description by design. It cannot carry the author's finance experience, and it should not try. It asks the recipient to supply their own ground reading and to state what they would expect to see. The drift warning is the reason stage 1 is recorded before stage 2: once the recipient shares the framework's pretext, their agreement is no longer a check. The same essay's remedy is Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."

## 9. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0058 the canonical shape is: **a union of four conditions on a per-slot free-cash-flow residual, with an anchor for each, a search-then-refuse order, an isomer relation between the two equity payout bonds, a second-order accumulation of settled routes, and a declared gap for the number.** It is not a ratio, not a ranking of payout methods, and not a claim that the framework settled corporate finance. The ratios and targets that appear in the literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` plus `gap`, and the stop is recorded.
