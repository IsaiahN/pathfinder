## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: a union of four conditions with different anchors; search then refuse; two payout bonds are isomers; the number is a gap; capital structure is second-order.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a finance reader will hear: internal use first, leverage target, protect the dividend, buy back with surplus, retire debt when high, and no universal percentage.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** collapses in ordinary finance talk into a firm-level payout ratio, which is exactly the aggregation Fig 1 :22 disallows;
- the **refusal operator type** is lost, because a payout is heard as a subtraction of cash rather than as the refusal of management's standing to compose over a flat residual;
- the **anchor condition** is silently weakened, because a "market-based" value metric in practice often updates on the firm's own forecast and earnings narrative.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: dividends and buybacks are different bonds over the same atoms, and the empirical asymmetry confirms that the distinction matters. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no market reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
