## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134-135: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: a comparison is a round trip with a measured gap, the readings stay indexed to their scale, and the anchor is a third thing. A single order parameter that says "the system is 0.6 ordered" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The shape is reached by rejecting the wrong frames first. *Library of Babel* on bundled questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is Fig 9 :54's union/partition rule in the essay's vocabulary, and it is why `01` splits the question before searching.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a particular scale-invariant quantity as *the* comparison measure is a name. It is licensed only if an instrument shows the quantity survives the round trip with a measured gap. The renormalization group and scale invariance sources identify candidates, but they do not settle which one works for a named system. That is the work of a ground reading.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes create identical readings. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The instrument that reads green does not determine whether the source was one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered.

The resolution in that essay is not an argument but an instrument: *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

Applied here: a measurement that returns "ordered" at one scale and "disordered" at another is a projection. Several arrangements of the same system could give those readings. Naming the source from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is the round-trip instrument that measures the gap, not a verdict about which reading is real. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* adds: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality."*

And the data-loss premise for scale is stated directly in the same essay: *"On every level of a system, data is lost."* That is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 4 :27 (*"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does a licensed comparison of scale-dependent readings look like, before we know the system?** It is a shape with four constraints:

1. The two readings are indexed to their scales and never pooled into one scalar (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. The comparison is a round trip on the measurement procedure, with the gap measured rather than assumed (Fig 4 :27-30; Symbols :77-78).
3. The anchor is external to both readings and does not update when either reading changes (Fig 2 :29-30, :47).
4. The person or instrument checking the claim is not given the framework's interior before recording a verdict (Fig 10 :42, :52).

**Which specific quantity, if any, has a zero gap is not given by the framework.** That is a name, and it is settled by a source-discriminating instrument. The shape is the slot; the quantity is what the ground pays into it.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of people selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the comparison a budget to be tried and lets an instrument decide.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the comparison and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

**Consequence for the two-stage offer.** Stage 1 sends only the residual, the shape and the refuter. The recipient's verdict is recorded before the theory is shown, because a verifier who can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it. This is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and it is why no link appears before the refuter in `02_outreach_email.md`.
