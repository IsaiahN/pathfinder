## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about comparing two readings of one system when one returns ORDER and the other DISORDER at a different scale. Option A: declare one scale correct. Option B: average the readings into one order parameter. Option C: refuse to compare. Option D: measure the round-trip gap between the scale descriptions, keep the readings indexed to their scale, and range each against an anchor external to both. What each leads to: A is a verdict from inside one frame (Fig 2 :34), B discards the live signal (Fig 1 :22), C is silence, D returns a checkable quantity and a refused slot.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The comparison is a round-trip gap, not a verdict and not an average. Coarse graining is lossy and non-invertible, so order at one scale and disorder at another is the expected signature of a transform that discards detail, not a contradiction. Keep both readings indexed to their scale; treat them as one system at two sizes, not two independent witnesses; range each against a third anchor that does not update. Order is a structure and disorder a composition residue, so they are not two values of one scalar. Where the readings share an atom set they are isomers at `specified` mode, with the difference in arrangement rather than formula. The framework refuses to name an invariant; which quantity has a zero gap is a ground result. It is `specified` and ungrounded. No scale is declared correct, no order parameter is offered, and no invariant is named.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three refuters and provenance before any contact, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is operational: picking one scale or pooling readings hides a live residual at the other scale and leads to dead reckoning (Fig 4 :30), which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by measuring the gap and keeping the readings separate.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn and no scale is discarded. A defect in the comparison is repaired by re-measuring `R_T` on the named system and re-enumerating the atoms; a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. An instrument measuring `R_T = 0` for a named system with an invertible transform; a demonstration that pooling preserves the live residual; a pair of scale readings with genuinely independent instruments and evidence; a single invariant predicting both readings with no residue; or a re-census admitting that recordings, not just methods, can cross the membrane. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
