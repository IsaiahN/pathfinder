## The answer in one line

Compare scale-dependent readings by the round-trip gap between their descriptions, keep both readings indexed to their scale instead of averaging them, treat them as one system at two sizes rather than two independent witnesses, and range each against an anchor that neither reading can move. Order is a structure and disorder a composition residue; same-atom readings are an isomer claim at `specified` mode. No invariant is named and no scale is declared correct.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a single order parameter or universal scale-invariant quantity settles the comparison.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.
2. **A verifier who holds the interior cannot verify.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Hence the residual and refuter are sent before any theory.
3. **A recording is not a generator.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the procedure and the refuter, not the conclusion.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional and opt-in channels avoid that practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

Every substantive derivation in this folder is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* No measurement of `R_T` was taken. The second seat in `11_audit.md` shares the primary's evidence pool, so its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration.
