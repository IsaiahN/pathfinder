# The Tether's answer on comparing scale-dependent measurements

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

One system gives two readings: ORDER at scale A, DISORDER at scale B. The question asks how to compare them. It is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-58), not one hard question, so it must be unbundled before search: can each scale resolve a residual, do we have terms for ordering and for the chaos residue, what counts as a comparison, and what instrument measures the gap. The chain usually breaks at the vocabulary-to-objective link, the one with the fewest instruments (Fig 3 :24-27).

## 2. The shape

1. **The two readings are not a contradiction to be settled by a verdict.** Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."* Going up throws detail away and the trip is not reversible. Order at one scale and disorder at another is the expected signature of a coarse graining, not a paradox.
2. **The comparison is the round-trip gap.** Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* Carry the measurement procedure up, bring it back down, and report `R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. The gap is *"not an error to eliminate"* but *"the only honest report of how much was given up"* (Fig 4 :31-32). Option A (pick a scale) is dead reckoning.
3. **Do not aggregate.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global order parameter near zero is compatible with one live residual at a single scale (Fig 1 :13, :22). Keep each reading indexed to its scale.
4. **The two readings are one frame at two sizes, not two witnesses.** Independence requires disjoint evidence (Fig 2 :31-32). Both readings come from the same system and the same ground, so their agreement or disagreement is not corroboration. The anchor must be a third thing.
5. **The anchor does not update.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* The finer reading cannot anchor the coarser one: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame"* (Fig 2 :47). Watch the channel as well, because *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* and pooled readings are among the failures (Fig 10 :24-26).
6. **Order and disorder are different kinds of thing.** Ordering is spectrum, a structure; chaos is a residue a composition leaves behind: *"Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member"* (Fig 13 :124-125). They are not two values of one scalar. Where both readings use the same atom set, they stand as isomers: Fig 12 :117, *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* That mapping is `specified`, with the enumerable-atom falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
7. **The object that crosses the membrane is the method, not the number.** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down"* (Fig 4 :22). `R_T` is a gap between descriptions, not a comparison of two recorded values.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that one scale is correct and the other an artifact.
- Not that there is a single order parameter, entropy measure, or invariant that settles the comparison.
- Not that chaos is really order at a finer resolution, or that order is really disorder averaged.
- Not that the two readings are independent witnesses. Agreement between them is one pool talking.
- Not that the framework can name which scale-invariant quantity survives the round trip. That is a ground result.
- Not a derivation of one scale from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No gap has been measured for a named system in this folder.

## 4. What would settle it, and the falsifiers

A source-discriminating instrument measures `R_T` for a named system at a named pair of scales, with an anchor neither reading can move, and enumerates the atoms each reading uses. Standing falsifiers, written in `01` section 3 before the world search:

1. an information-preserving, invertible scale transform with measured `R_T = 0` for a named system (breaks Fig 4 :28-30);
2. averaging across scales preserving the live residual each scale carries (breaks Fig 1 :22);
3. the two readings shown to use disjoint instruments and disjoint evidence, so triangulation applies;
4. a single invariant quantity predicting both readings with no residue (breaks Fig 13 :35, :38-39);
5. a pooled reading that improves contact with the ground relative to the separate readings (breaks Fig 10 :24).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (`05_what_shape_means.md` derives this from the essays); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"* (Fig 8 :39): not finding a universal invariant in these sources is not a proof that none exists.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a winning scale and does not collapse order and disorder into one order parameter. It says that coarse graining is lossy and not reversible, so the honest comparison is the round trip: carry the measurement procedure to the coarser scale, bring it back, and measure the gap. That gap is the comparison, not an error to hide and not a verdict about which scale is real. Both readings stay indexed to their scale, because a global average near zero is compatible with one live residual at a single scale. They are one system at two sizes, not two independent witnesses, so the anchor must be a third thing that neither reading can move. Order is a structure and disorder is a residue a composition leaves behind; where both readings use the same atoms they stand as isomers, with the invisible difference in the arrangement rather than the formula. The framework refuses to name a scale-invariant quantity, because which one has a zero gap is a measurement. What it offers is the shape any comparison must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the instrument that would close it. The gap stays unmeasured until the ground settles it.
