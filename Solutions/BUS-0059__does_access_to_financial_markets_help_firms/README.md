## The question restated as an effect

A firm chooses investments whose returns arrive at different times. Exposing those choices to a public financial market does two things at once: it brings capital and a distributed price signal into the firm's reach, and it couples the firm's decisions to a frequent public number that rewards short-horizon performance. Option A says the first effect dominates, so market access improves investment. Option B says the second dominates, so market access increases myopic pressure. What each leads to: A predicts better per-project selection when capital was the binding constraint; B predicts the abandonment of positive-net-present-value projects when the quarterly number is binding. The answer is bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: **"market access changes the firm's contact with the ground, and the sign of the change depends on which condition is binding."** That binding is a `specified` shape, not a measurement.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Gathering more finance academics to agree does not add a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, and gets the sending domain blacklisted. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid the practical failure as well as the structural one.

## Boundary and status of this derivation

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, NOT a ground reading.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-22) says LLM output is a simulation of an answer and that *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on.
