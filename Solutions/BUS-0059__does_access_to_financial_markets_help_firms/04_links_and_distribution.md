## What the wider literature does for this question

1. **The harm side is measured, not merely asserted.** Asker, Farre-Mensa and Ljungqvist compare similar public and private firms and find public firms invest less and respond less to opportunities. Their cross-sectional test ties the gap to how sensitive prices are to earnings news. Graham, Harvey and Rajgopal find 55 percent of surveyed CFOs would skip a very positive NPV project to meet the quarterly consensus. Stein gives the mechanism: a signal-jamming equilibrium in which the market is not fooled but managers still behave myopically.
2. **The help side is also measured.** Bond, Edmans and Goldstein show prices carry information useful to real decisions, and distinguish the feedback to real choices from mere cash-flow forecasting. This is why F7 in `01` is a genuine benefit and why a blanket "markets hurt" verdict would be wrong.
3. **The two literatures are not a conflict to resolve by averaging.** They are the two conditions of the union compound in Fig 9 :55. Read together, they corroborate the requirement that the verdicts stay separate. Fig 8 :48 says *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."* These two literatures have substantial overlap in the finance pool, so their agreement is one pool talking; their separation into two mechanisms is what carries information.
4. **The target-corruption finding is corroborated from outside finance.** Campbell and Goodhart, via Rodamar and the arXiv paper, state the general measurement law that a metric used for control loses its meaning. Fig 2 :43 says *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."* Different fields, low overlap, so this is genuine corroboration of F5. The original 1975 and 1997 texts were not read here, so the attribution caveat is recorded.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A list selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A venue's prestige is a filter for where to try, not a witness for whether the claim is right.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then, if they opt in, the links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
