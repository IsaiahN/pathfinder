## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: two conditions, two grounds, two units, and a refusal of the single averaged verdict. The magnitude of the net effect is not a shape the figures return.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* and Fig 9 :30 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe the residual as "capital becomes reachable and a quarterly number becomes binding," not as "markets are good" or "markets are bad." The effect description is checkable by any finance researcher against data.

## 3. The bundle is part of the shape, not noise

*The Library of Babel* names the case directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* Fig 9 :55 says *"a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

The A-or-B form of BUS-0059 is exactly such a bundle. Treating it as one hard question is the failure the figures and the essay independently name. The shape answer therefore has two verdicts, not one.

## 4. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means realised long-term project returns, measured per project.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Asker and Stein results are imports from the finance frame into the shape; they must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So naming "share price" as the ground for investment quality would turn a visible choice into an invisible one. It is a binding from the finance frame, and it must be recorded as a binding.

## 5. Why the aggregate cannot name the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here a high share price can be produced by genuinely good long-term investment or by earnings inflation that borrows from the future (Stein's signal jamming). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 of the audit

I initially wrote F3 as if Fig 2 :29's list of anchor examples (which includes *"a market"*) made the price the anchor for this question. That overstates the line. The README's phrase *"a shape, not a name"* governs a primitive not held in the library, and the anchor clause Fig 2 :36 governs a question constitutively about the candidate anchor. BUS-0059 is constitutively about real investment, not about the market, and a price moves when firms act on it (Fig 2 :30). ## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-known finance academics to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets realised outcomes settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The canonical article adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. What the eight essays jointly say about "shape" here

Taken together, the essays give one discipline: describe the effect before choosing a frame, keep the bundle unbundled, record the source of every import, refuse the name where only a shape is owed, and stop honestly at the limit of the instrument. The canonical article states the framework's own boundary: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* That boundary is why this solution ends in `specified` and a pre-registered refuter rather than a verdict.
