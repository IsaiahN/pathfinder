# Local and specialist knowledge as isomers, and the order of refusal

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground
reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## RESIDUAL

The audit leaves two structural questions open. (1) Are local knowledge and specialist knowledge
different *substances* built from different stuff, or the same stuff arranged differently? (2) In
what order should a failed credential claim be handled: mint a new credential, or refuse the old
one? Both matter because the answer decides whether the gap is an import (new atom) or an
arrangement (new bond).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms available to both frames: observation, classification, causal inference, transmission to
another person, correction by an outcome. Bonds available: the seven, including sequence `→`,
comparison `⋛`, conjunction `+`, identity `≡`, and the unary negation `¬`. No new primitive is
licensed; the figures' substrate is closed (Fig 13 :113 "The set is closed. The arrangements are
not.").

## DERIVATION

### Q1 Are they isomers?

Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."

Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance."

Write the two frames over the same atom set:

```
local:      observation + observation + ... --(place-indexed, oral, long-horizon)--> classification
specialist: observation + observation + ... --(standardised, credential-indexed)--> classification
```

The atoms are the same: both frames observe, classify, infer, transmit and get corrected. The
bond differs: local knowledge is bonded to a particular place and a particular lineage of
transmission; specialist knowledge is bonded to a standardised method and a certifying gate. The
world source for traditional ecological knowledge supports the bond reading: TEK is "a family of
culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews",
and its acquisition is by "long-term observation, cultural methods, and experiential learning,
rather than the standardized experimental and quantitative methods typical of Western ecological
science". Both use observation; the validation relation is what differs.

⇒ The two are **isomers** under Fig 12 :117. This is not a claim that one is better. It is a claim
that the difference lives in the bond, which is exactly where Fig 12 says the difference is
invisible to a flat ingredient list.

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if either frame requires an atom the other cannot express,
for example a standardised instrument reading that is a genuinely new primitive rather than a
re-arrangement of observation, the isomer claim fails and the two are different substances built
from different atoms. The answer then needs an import (Fig 6), not a re-bonding. Mark `specified`,
unmeasured.

### Q2 What is the bond, exactly?

The candidate bonds:

- `+` conjunction: both hold at once. Symmetric. Does not capture the difference.
- `≡` identity: two names, one referent. Would collapse the two frames, which the world source
  denies.
- `→` sequence: one after the other, ordered. This fits the transmission line but not the
  validation relation.
- `⋛` comparison: "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Operators table). This is the
  bond at the *decision*, when the community compares the two frames' predictions against the
  same outcome.
- validation itself: the relation between an observation and the standing granted to the
  observer. The figures do not name this as a distinct bond; it is a composition of sequence and
  comparison under a closure (Fig 6).

So the honest reading: the *internal* bond difference between the two knowledge systems is a
composition of available bonds, not a new bond. The *decision* bond between them is `⋛`, a
threshold comparison against the ground. Fig 12 :111 "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state
and cannot name a route to one." Naming both frames without saying how they are bonded is the
flat-form failure.

### Q3 What order does the work?

Formula step 2 routes a gap by kind. The fifth kind is REFUTED:

Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so"

A failed credential claim is a REFUTED case, not a NEW case. What is owed is a refusal, not a new
credential and not a new theory of expertise.

`THE_FORMULA.md`: "NOTHING IS EVER DELETED, and the reach falls because something was ADDED."
The old credential claim stays in the record with a refusal attached; what falls is its standing
to be composed over. That is why the community can keep respecting the specialist frame in other
slots while refusing it here. This is the per-slot result of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1,
arrived at by a different route (the formula's route rather than the habitat route).

**Order by dependency:** describe the residual per slot (Fig 9 :27), refuse the failed claim
before minting a replacement (Formula step 2), then compare the surviving frames against the
ground (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.").
Reversing the order, minting a new credential theory before refusing the failed one, is the
error the amendment in Fig 5 names: a frame can refuse the right kind of thing and aim the
refusal at the outcome it did not want rather than at the belief it holds.

### Q4 What is the second derivation for?

It protects the answer from a category mistake. If the two frames are isomers, then the honest
question is not "which one is really knowledge" but "which arrangement reaches the ground on this
slot, and what did the arrangement cost to establish?" Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the
description and not what it cost to establish." An elder's compressed judgement and a
specialist's compressed protocol both carry a cost that the artefact does not show. A comparison
that reads only the artefact is the metamerism error the canonical reading warns about.

## CONCLUSION

Local and specialist knowledge are **isomers** over a shared atom set: same observation,
classification, inference, transmission and correction, bonded differently to place and lineage
versus standard and gate. The decision between them is a `⋛` comparison against the ground, not
a ranking of kinds. A failed credential claim is a REFUTED case and owes a refusal before any new
term is minted. Both findings are `specified`, single-source at the figure level (Fig 12 and the
Formula), with the world sources on TEK and credentialism as low-overlap corroboration. Neither
is `grounded`.
