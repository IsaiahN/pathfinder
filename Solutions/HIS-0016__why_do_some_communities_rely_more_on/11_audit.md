## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires
it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder
actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, Fig 2 :29, Fig 9 :60, Fig 10 :24,
   Fig 11 :49 and Fig 12 :117.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: trust is routed per slot to
   the frame with live contact and intact independence; credentials are a budget filter; the two
   knowledge systems are isomers.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete field claim, for example a specific history of a community
   preferring elders over extension officers in a named place and decade.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union unbundling** (Fig 9 :54). The shorthand says "bundled conditions" but a concrete
  history must name which conditions held and which lever cleared which intersection;
- the **negotiated-outcome risk**. The shorthand says "the outcome anchors the decision", but if
  what counts as a good outcome is itself set by the frame under test, the anchor has moved and
  Fig 2's coherent drift applies;
- the **provenance requirement** (Fig 8 :51). A concrete case must show whether local and
  specialist agreement is convergent derivation or adopted import; the contents alone cannot say;
- the **order of refusal before mint** (Formula step 2). A concrete account that immediately
  proposes a new credential regime has reversed it.

What survives cleanly: trust is routed per slot, the outcome is the anchor in principle, and the
credential is a filter rather than a witness. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not**
measured numerically because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the
enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the
metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
