## QUESTION

Decide in effect terms what the Tether says about the observed effect: for a decision, a community
acts on an uncredentialed practitioner, witness or elder rather than on an available credentialed
specialist. Option A: explain it as a community-level attitude, a deficit of trust in experts, or
a cultural preference for tradition. Option B: keep the per-slot contact shape and refuse the
community-level name. What each leads to: A aggregates away the live signal, treats a credential
as if it were an outcome reading, and selects a frame before describing the residual; B states a
checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and an honest stop until a ground vote is run.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor.
Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the credential prediction (the better credentialed source
  should be preferred) and the observed routing to the local source. Arity: two frames compared
  per decision slot, with at least four bundled conditions, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54).
  Symmetry: non-symmetric, valence is relative to R. Scale: individual choice, community
  convention, institutional credentialing, recursive (Fig 12 :103).
- **Domain 1, local knowledge frame.** Actors: elders, practitioners, witnesses, local
  environment. Currency: orally transmitted, place-indexed observation over long horizons.
  Anchor: the outcome of the advice in that place. Live frame with a working bracket.
- **Domain 2, specialist / credential frame.** Actors: specialists, certifying bodies. Currency:
  qualifications and institutional standing. Anchor: the same ground in principle, but the
  credential is a proxy that can decouple. A frame whose channel can decay.
- **Domain 3, authority-assignment frame.** Actors: community decision makers, the two frames as
  candidates. Currency: trust and deference. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Union compound.
- **Domain 4, habitat.** Actors: conditions, seasons, terrain, instruments, incentives, history of
  contact. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground. Enumerated, never composed.
- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 have different closures; their union can have a surplus only if
  a residual is described first. The question itself is a union and must be unbundled.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the slot difference, acquire terms for what each source
  affords and what counts as progress, refuse the failed claim before minting, compare against the
  outcome, then carry the result. Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break."

Strain test: applying the ecological habitat figure to human institutions is not strained because
the figures are domain-agnostic (Fig 4 :19). The label stays `specified`. One live failure mode is
carried forward: if what counts as a good outcome is negotiated by the same frame under test, the
anchor has been moved and Fig 2's coherent drift applies. That is a named risk, not a smoothed
strain.

  Corroborates F6 and C3. Overlap: low; cites Berkes, Houde, Turner.
- **Cognitive science / expertise.** Wikipedia contributors, "Naturalistic decision-making"
  (Wikipedia, 2026). Quote: "limited time, uncertainty, high stakes, team and organizational

  built against a demanding local environment, so a credential is a weak proxy for it.
  Corroborates F5. Overlap: low, independent field.
- **Sociology / education.** Wikipedia contributors, "Credentialism and degree inflation"
  (Wikipedia, 2026). Quote: "Credentialism is a reliance on formal qualifications or

  is exactly Fig 9 :60's budget filter. Corroborates F3 and F9. Overlap: low.
- **History of science / science communication.** Wikipedia contributors, "Public awareness of
  science" (Wikipedia, 2026). Quote: the experts in the Cumbrian case "were unaware of the local

  Corroborates F2 and F5. Overlap: low; cites Wynne 1996.
- **Human cognition / tacit knowledge.** Isaiah N, "Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context,

- **Validation / prestige.** Isaiah N, "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap" (adventuresinml,

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the community-level names do not. Reliance follows live contact with the
ground per decision slot, under independence. Credentials are a budget filter that becomes a
structural error when the certifying frame is mistaken for the anchor. Local and specialist
knowledge are isomers over shared atoms, bonded differently. A failed credential claim is REFUTED
and owes a refusal before a new term is minted. The specific history (which communities, which
periods, what magnitudes) is unreachable with these instruments and is recorded as unreachable,
not as silence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result and remains a candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable but not trivial. The packet is mode-labelled, states
  five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than
  invisible. The real cost is a false licence to dismiss credentials, which would be answered
  immediately by refuter 3. The figures price the opposite error too: treating a filter as a
  witness spends the independence of the people asked to check (Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in
  proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Both costs are reducible by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The community-level names are removable without touching the per-slot
  derivation, and `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove
  them. No primitive has to be withdrawn; a refused claim stays in the record with a refusal
  attached (Formula route: nothing is ever deleted).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot outcome comparison in which the
  specialist source repeatedly matches the outcome better and reliance does not shift (refuter 1);
  near-total evidence-pool overlap with no loss of independence (refuter 2); a credential that
  functions as an anchor across slots (refuter 3); the chain shown to break at planning rather
  than vocabulary and objective (refuter 4); or one source type winning across different grounds
  with no change in contact (refuter 5). Any one moves the result; a survey of attitudes or a list
  of agreeing experts does not.
