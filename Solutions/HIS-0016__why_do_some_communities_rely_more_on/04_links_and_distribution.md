## Three things in the world sources that bear directly on this question

1. **The Wynne case is a channel-decay case, not an ignorance case.** The nuclear experts were not
   less credentialed; their experimental models failed because the local conditions and sheep
   behaviour were not in their closure. That is Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel
   does" arriving from an independent field. It is the strongest world corroboration and it is a
   retrodiction, not something the framework generated.
2. **Credentialism describes a permission filter.** The Wikipedia definition is about who is
   permitted to undertake a task or speak as an expert. That matches Fig 9 :60 rather than a claim
   about outcomes. Do not present credential criticism as a claim that credentials carry no
   information; presented that way it is false and easily refuted.
3. **Tacit knowledge explains why the person, not the certificate, carries the answer.** The
   apprenticeship account in the tacit-knowledge essay: writing carries context and some subtext,
   almost never the "you had to be there" pretext; the knowledge is rebuilt through contact. This
   is a high-overlap source (framework author), so it corroborates the figure reading only weakly.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full framework
  before anyone has described a decision slot sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the
  per-slot claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has
  absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".** Ask
  what they would expect to see if the contact claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."** Buy a small number
  of genuinely different frames, not a large number of agreeing ones.
- Fig 7 and Fig 2 :40: a room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Do not pool later
  agreement as corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the per-slot shape claim and the five refuters; (2) ask what
they would count as a failure, and record the answer before stage 2; (3) only then the links, with
the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method
only if they ask.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the output against a decision you can check. **(2)** Only if curious, read the
theory that produced it. **(3)** Optionally, use the formula on another problem, writing your own
refuter first. This is the right order and it follows from Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range
against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because a
reader who has absorbed the theory before recording a verdict has lost the independence that made
their check worth asking for.
