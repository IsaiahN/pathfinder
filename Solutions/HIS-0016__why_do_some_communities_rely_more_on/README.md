## The question restated as an effect

The observation to be explained: for a given decision, a community routes trust to a person
with no formal credential (a practitioner, a witness, an elder) rather than to a credentialed
specialist, even when a specialist is available. The residual is the gap between a prediction
(the better credentialed source should be preferred) and that observation. The answer to be
derived is not a name of a community, but the **shape** any answer must have: on which slots
reliance tracks the liveness of a frame's contact with the ground, and why a credential is a
budget filter and not a witness for the verdict.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that local practitioners are intrinsically more reliable than specialists, and no
  claim that credentials are worthless. Valence is relative to the residual, not carried by an
  actor (Fig 11 :49).

Three reasons, in the figures' own words. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not
negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the
cost of being wrong." Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."
A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make agreement
worthless, and showing the framework before asking for a verdict spends the reader's
independence.
