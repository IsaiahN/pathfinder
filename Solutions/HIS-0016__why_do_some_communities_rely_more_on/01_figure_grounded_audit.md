## 0. The residual in effect terms

Do not begin with the theory. Begin with what is observed and what was predicted.

- **Observation.** For a decision slot (a crop choice, a diagnosis, a weather call, a land
  dispute), a community acts on the testimony of a practitioner, witness or elder who holds no
  formal credential, and does not act on an available credentialed specialist.
- **Prediction being violated.** A global metric of "expert quality" (credential level, years of
  formal training, institutional affiliation) should select the specialist. The observation
  contradicts that metric on this slot.
- **R (the residual).** The gap between that prediction and that observation. R is a *slice*, not
  a global distrust score. It is indexed by decision slot.

The first figure line is therefore cited before any conclusion:

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

So "which communities trust experts less" is already the wrong shape of question. It averages
the very signal that says where to look. The answerable question is: on which slots, and under
what contact conditions, does the local frame win?

An answer would look like this, bound as an ordinary term before the run (formula step
"Direction", Fig 5 :65 to :66): **trust follows the frame whose channel to the ground for that
slot is live and whose independence from the claim is intact; a credential is a cheap filter
that narrows candidates for a budget, and becomes a verdict only when a frame forgets it is a
filter.** This is a shape. It names no community and no date.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

Describe R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and
anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

### R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** Two source frames are compared per decision slot. The comparison is not a symmetric
  join; which source wins depends on which frame's contact reaches the ground for that slot. The
  question also bundles several conditions (contact, relevance, accessibility, history of
  contact). That is a union compound, not one hard question.
- **Symmetry.** The valence of a source is not a property it carries. Fig 11 :49 "An actor's
  valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same elder is a benefactor
  for one residual and irrelevant for another. So "local" and "credentialed" are relations, not
  kinds, and the comparison cannot be read as a fixed ranking.
- **Scale.** Individual choice, community convention, institutional credentialing. Three strata,
  recursive: Fig 12 :103 "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."

### Domains

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Local knowledge frame | elders, practitioners, witnesses, local environment, the community | orally transmitted, place-indexed empirical observation over long horizons | **yes**: the outcome of the advice in that place (did the crop survive, did the patient recover, did the forecast hold) | live frame with a bracket to the ground |
| D2 Specialist / credential frame | specialists, certifying bodies, professional associations | formal qualifications, peer review, institutional standing | **yes in principle**, but the same ground; the credential is a proxy that can decouple from it | frame whose channel can decay |
| D3 Authority-assignment frame | community decision makers, the two frames as candidates | trust, deference, authority | **yes**: the ground, never agreement | union compound, unbundle before search |
| D4 Habitat (contact field) | local conditions, season, terrain, instruments, institutional incentives, history of contact between community and each frame | contact | **yes**: the ground, which does not move | enumerate, never compose |

Each domain has a nameable anchor, so each is a genuine domain. D1 and D2 are two frames with
different closures. Their union can have a surplus if a residual is described first (Fig 8 :53
"Residual first, frame second."), but the observed reliance is a *selection* of one frame for a
slot, not a union. D3 is where the question lives and is a union of overlapping conditions.

### Partition or union

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

"Why rely on local rather than specialist" bundles at least four conditions: (a) which frame's
contact reaches the ground for this slot, (b) whether the credential is relevant to that slot,
(c) accessibility and cost of being wrong, (d) the community's history of contact with both
frames. A single lever (for example, "issue more credentials") clears the region where only (c)
holds and leaves the intersections standing. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder
case of either."

### Dependency order

Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

1. perception: can the community detect the relevant difference between sources?
2. vocabulary: does it have terms for what each source affords for this slot?
3. objective: what counts as a good outcome here?
4. planning: which source to consult.
5. learn and carry: name what worked and carry it.

Fig 3 :24 "Why the middle link is where chains usually break."

Credentials strengthen links 4 and 5 of the specialist frame. If links 2 and 3 (what the sources
afford, what counts as progress here) are not instrumented, effort lands above the break:

Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

## 3. Findings (verbatim figure lines, mode, refuter)

### F1 The residual is per slot, not a global trust score (general)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

A community is not "trusting or distrusting experts". It routes trust per slot. The elder who is
authoritative for a land boundary is not authoritative for an antibiotic dose. Any explanation
that begins with a community-level attitude has already aggregated away the signal.

**Refuter:** show a community whose routing is demonstrably slot-independent across at least two
different domains with different grounds (for example identical source choice for a weather call
and a surgical decision), with no change in contact between the slots. That would falsify the
per-slot claim.

**Mode:** `general`, structural. Triangulated across Fig 1 and Fig 11 (low overlap: the agent's
residual index versus the habitat's valence rule).

### F2 The anchor for the decision is the outcome, not the credential (specified)

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Fig 1 :23 "The ground enters as the only metric."

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does."

For a decision slot the anchor is the consequence of the advice in that place. A credential is a
reading of a frame's own standing, not a reading of that consequence. When the channel from the
specialist frame to local outcomes decays (the specialist's model stops tracking local
conditions), the ground has not changed; the channel has. The community's turn to the local
practitioner is then a turn to the frame whose channel is still live.

**Refuter:** produce a slot where the credentialed source's advice repeatedly matches the local
outcome better than the local source's, measured against the same outcome, and the community
still routes to the local source with no change in contact. That breaks the contact explanation.

**Mode:** `specified`. Triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 1 and Fig 10.

### F3 Credentials are frame-internal and cannot score the frame that produces them (general)

Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."

The surrounding lines name the disqualified quantities: "coverage, and compression achieved",
"predicates minted, and credibility accrued". A credential count is credibility accrued. A
specialist who claims authority because of credentials is the frame scoring itself with a
quantity it also produces.

Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."

When the certifying institution is admitted as the party that settles the dispute, panel 3 of
Fig 2 has turned into panel 1: the anchor has become another frame and drift is coherent.

**Refuter:** show a credential that does not move when the certifying frame thinks about it and
that predicts local outcomes better than local testimony across several slots, so it functions as
an anchor in Fig 2's sense. That would break F3 and F9 together.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F9.

### F4 Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation (general)

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A community that holds both a local frame and a specialist frame, each ranged separately against
the same local outcome, is in a position to triangulate. A community that holds only credential
readings, all drawn from the same certifying evidence pool, holds one frame wearing many names:
their agreement is the pool talking. Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the
agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

**Refuter:** measure near-total overlap between the "local" and "specialist" evidence pools in a
case where reliance nevertheless shifts. If overlap is 1, the independence term is zero and the
triangulation reading is void for that case.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 8.

### F5 The middle link of the dependency chain is where the break is (specified)

Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Specialist training invests heavily in perception and planning (links 1 and 4). What often has no
instrument is link 2 or 3: what a local situation affords, and what would count as progress in
that particular place. Credential reform lands above the break. This matches the world source on
public awareness of science: in the Cumbrian sheep-farmer case, nuclear experts "were unaware of
the local environmental conditions and the behaviour of sheep and this has eventually led to the
failure of their experimental models" (world source, science communication, read 2026-11-23).

**Refuter:** show that for the relevant decisions the chain breaks at link 4 or 5 (poor planning
or failure to carry lessons), while links 2 and 3 are well instrumented. Then Fig 3's middle-link
diagnosis is wrong for this case.

**Mode:** `specified`. Triangulated (Fig 3 plus the world case; the figure does the deciding).

### F6 Valence is relative to the residual, not a property of the actor (general)

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :50 "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

This is the figure's own guard against romanticising local knowledge. The elder is not
intrinsically more trustworthy; the specialist is not intrinsically less. For a given slot,
whichever frame's contact reaches the ground is the one that should be routed to. Fig 13 :4 "There
is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather
than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."

**Refuter:** a single case where one source type is demonstrably better across slots with
different grounds and different residuals, with no change in contact, would break the relativity
claim. Expected result: such a cross-slot constant is exactly what the figures deny.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F1.

### F7 Capability is agent-and-habitat; contact is the lever (general)

Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

A community cannot subtract the specialist frame from its habitat. It can introduce a live local
frame, or an instrument that carries a local reading into the specialist's closure. Adding
credentials without changing contact does not change capability for that slot. World source,
ecology/anthropology: TEK "does not function as a universal set of principles, but as a family of
culturally situated rules and practices rooted in specific places and ecological worldviews"
(read 2026-11-23), which is a statement about contact, not about intelligence of persons.

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement (better credentials, better training in abstraction) that
demonstrably moves the ground outcome without changing contact would refute the capability claim
in Fig 11, as the figure itself says at :72.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F2.

### F8 The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (general)

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."

"Local versus specialist" is not one question. It bundles ground contact, relevance of the
credential, accessibility and cost of error, and history of contact. Describe the residual per
slot first, then pick the frame that predicts its shape. If the frame is picked first (for example
"we will study traditional knowledge" or "we will study credentialism"), any frame will seem to
fit: Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the four conditions collapse to one factor in the data, with the
same lever clearing every intersection, would dissolve the union reading. Then the compound is a
partition after all.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F1.

### F9 Filters for the budget, witnesses for the verdict (general)

Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"

A credential is efficient for a convention. It cheaply narrows who gets a hearing, which is a
budget decision. It is disqualifying as a verdict about the world on this slot, because the
credential is drawn from the same frame whose claim is being checked. World source,
sociology/education: "Credentialism is a reliance on formal qualifications or certifications to
determine whether someone is permitted to undertake a task, speak as an 'expert' or work in a
certain field" (read 2026-11-23). That is a description of a permission filter, not of a ground
reading.

**Refuter:** same as F3. A credential shown to be an anchor in Fig 2's sense breaks F9.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F3.

### F10 No frame escapes its own closure; the local frame can be an import (general)

Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."

Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

A community sealed inside the specialist frame cannot derive place-indexed local knowledge from
inside that closure. The local practitioner is a contact with a frame whose closure differs, an
import that moves the wall. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What
can cross is a method for reading the local ground, not a recording of one elder's answer.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the local frame's relevant content is already derivable inside
the specialist closure means no atom is imported and the "wall moves" reading fails for that case.

**Mode:** `general`. Triangulated with F7.

### F11 Convergence and adopted import look identical; provenance decides (general)

Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"

If the elder and the specialist independently arrive at the same prediction from separate
experience, that is corroboration at maximum independence. If the specialist simply adopted the
elder's report, it is one evidence pool wearing two names. The contents cannot tell them apart;
only the record can. This is why the field guide for outreach insists on provenance before
agreement.

**Refuter:** a case where the record of origin changes nothing about the reliability of the
prediction would break the provenance requirement. The figures predict this will not happen where
independence matters.

**Mode:** `general`. Single-source for the figure definition (Fig 8), with the canonical Figures
README as the low-overlap second frame ("Indiscernibility is not identity").

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by seat reasoning: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape
claim is: **for a decision slot, reliance tracks which frame has live contact with the ground for
that slot and intact independence from the claim; credentials are a budget filter, not a
witness.**

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Contact counterexample.** A slot where the specialist's advice repeatedly matches the
   ground better than the local source's, measured against the same outcome, and the community
   still routes to the local source with no change in contact (breaks F2, F7).
2. **Zero independence.** The local and specialist frames are shown to draw on the same evidence
   pool (overlap near 1) in a case where reliance nevertheless shifts (breaks F4).
3. **Credential as anchor.** A credential that does not move when the certifying frame thinks
   about it predicts local outcomes better than local testimony across several slots (breaks
   F3, F9).
4. **Wrong break.** For the relevant decisions the chain is demonstrably broken at planning or
   carrying, not at vocabulary or objective (breaks F5).
5. **Slot independence.** One source type wins across slots with different grounds and different
   residuals, with no change in contact (breaks F1, F6).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Strain test and re-census

- **Attempted strain: Fig 11's habitat applied to human institutions.** The habitat figure is
  written in ecological terms (pollination, predation, DDT). Re-census: the figures are explicitly
  domain-agnostic. Fig 4 :19 "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." The Symbols
  table defines the habitat as "Everything in contact with the residual: actors, conditions,
  relations. Enumerated, never composed." People and institutions qualify. **Not strained**, but
  the mapping is recorded as `specified` analogy, not as a `grounded` result.
- **Attempted strain: Fig 2's "anchor must not update" applied to community outcomes.** If what
  counts as a good outcome is itself negotiated by the same frame whose authority is being
  judged, then the anchor has been moved and Fig 2's panel 1 applies. Re-census: this is not a
  strain in the figure, it is one of the failure modes the figure names (Fig 2 :34 "everything
  drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"). Recorded as a live
  risk, not smoothed over.
- **Attempted strain: Fig 3's middle link versus credential reform.** Re-census: credentials act
  on links 4 and 5; the middle link is vocabulary and objective. Different objects. No strain.
- **After re-census: no strain left hidden.** The one live item is the negotiated-outcome risk,
  named above and carried into `09_watson_answer.md`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Ask the per-slot question, never the community-level attitude question (F1).
2. State the mode on every claim. "Communities trust elders because of their culture" is a
   `general` guess delivered as a verdict; the figure-derived form is a `specified` prediction
   about contact per slot (F2, F6).
3. Treat credentials as a filter for who gets a hearing and require a separate ground reading for
   the verdict (F9).
4. Unbundle the compound before searching the literature. Four conditions, four loops (F8).
5. Report provenance: convergent derivation and adopted import look the same in the contents (F11).
6. Lead outreach with the refuter, not with the framework. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised
   residual, never the source."

## 8. Verdict

The shape survives; the tempting names do not. Reliance follows live contact with the ground per
decision slot, under independence, and credentials are a budget filter that becomes a verdict
only when the certifying frame is mistaken for the anchor. No community is named, no historical
period is dated, and no magnitude is claimed. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a
silence". The specific history is unreachable with these instruments; that is a verdict, not a
silence, and it is recorded as such.

**Triangulation status.** F1, F2, F4, F7 and F8 are triangulated across two or more figures with
low overlap between their routes. F11 is single-source for the figure definition with canonical
README corroboration. Nothing here is `grounded` because no measurement was taken.

**Strains after re-census:** none hidden; one live failure mode (negotiated outcome) carried forward.
