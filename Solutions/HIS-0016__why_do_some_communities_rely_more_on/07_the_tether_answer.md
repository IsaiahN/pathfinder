# The Tether's answer: why some communities rely more on local practitioners

**Mode: `specified`  - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a historical result.
Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground
reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

For a given decision, a community acts on the testimony of an uncredentialed practitioner,
witness or elder instead of an available credentialed specialist. The violated prediction is that
the better credentialed source should be preferred. R is the gap between that prediction and that
observation, and it is indexed by decision slot, not by community: Fig 1 :19 "The residual is
indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

The question as asked is therefore a compound. It bundles ground contact, relevance of the
credential, accessibility and cost of being wrong, and history of contact. Fig 9 :54 "And when the
split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

## 2. The shape

1. **Trust is routed per slot, not per community.** The same person is authoritative for one
   residual and irrelevant for another. Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the
   residual, not a property it carries." There is no stable ranking of elders versus specialists
   across decisions with different grounds.

2. **The anchor for a decision is its outcome in that place.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever
   settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." The
   credential is a reading of a frame's standing, not a reading of that outcome. It cannot settle
   the verdict.

3. **A credential is a filter for the budget, not a witness for the verdict.** Fig 9 :60 "Use
   filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
   Credentials are "efficient for a convention" (Fig 2 :41): they cheaply decide who gets a
   hearing. They become a structural error only when the certifying frame is mistaken for the
   anchor (Fig 2 :47 "A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame.").

4. **When a specialist channel stops tracking local conditions, the ground has not moved, the
   channel has.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does." The community's turn
   to the local practitioner is a turn to the frame whose channel still reaches the outcome. This
   is `specified`, not a claim about any named community. The world source on the Cumbrian sheep
   farmers is a concrete instance reached independently: the experts' models failed because local
   conditions were outside their closure.

5. **Capability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact
   changes nothing, however much it improves." Adding credentials without changing contact with
   the local outcome does not change capability for that slot. Introduction is available;
   exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61).

6. **Two low-overlap frames can triangulate; a single evidence pool cannot.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment
   is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Where local and specialist
   knowledge reach the same prediction by separate experience, that is corroboration at maximum
   independence. Where one is merely an adopted report of the other, their agreement is one pool
   talking (Fig 8 :51).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that local practitioners are intrinsically wiser or more honest (F6).
- Not that credentials are worthless. They are a legitimate budget filter and a convention.
- Not that distrust of specialists is always rational, or that all institutional knowledge is
  corrupt. Valence is relative to the residual.
- Not that a community's consensus is evidence for the claim. A room's agreement is variation,
  not a verdict (Fig 7).
- Not a name, a date, a region, or a magnitude. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken.

## 4. What would settle it  - the ground

A per-slot outcome comparison, not a further argument. For a named decision in a named place:

1. Record the predictions of the local source and the specialist source separately, in effect
   terms that anyone can check (Fig 6 :30).
2. Measure each one's contact with the outcome, not with the other source's opinion.
3. Measure the overlap of their evidence pools. If overlap is near 1, the independence term is zero.
4. Compare both against the outcome. That is the ground vote.
5. Apply the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. If any fires, the
   shape is withdrawn.

A single historical case study, such as the Wynne sheep-farmer material, *corroborates* the shape
but does not settle it: one case, one field, one evidence pool. Settlement requires the
per-slot comparison run across independent cases.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is derived and internally consistent. The history is
not: the figures give no instrument for naming which community, which period, or how large the
effect is. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The stop is recorded with
its lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Fig 4 :30 "So take
the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident
about a position nobody checked."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that elders are right and experts are wrong. It says that trust is
> routed per decision, not per community: on each decision the source that wins is the one whose
> channel to the outcome in that place is live and whose independence from the claim is intact. A
> credential is a cheap way to decide who gets a hearing, and it is disqualifying as a verdict
> about the world, because it is drawn from the same frame whose claim is being checked. When a
> specialist's model stops tracking local conditions, the ground has not moved; the channel has,
> and reliance shifts to the frame that still reaches it. Local and specialist knowledge are
> isomers over the same atoms of observation and inference, bonded differently to place and to
> standard. What is offered here is that shape, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of
> per-slot measurement that would settle it. The answer stops there until the ground votes.
