## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to
look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
Fig 13 states that the system "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean
already holding it."

For HIS-0016 a **shape** is therefore not "the elders are right" and not "credentialism is
bad". It is the constraint any answer must satisfy:

> reliance is routed per decision slot to the frame whose channel to the outcome for that place
> is live and whose independence from the claim is intact; credentials narrow the candidate set
> for a budget and do not, by themselves, settle the verdict.

That constrains. It does not name a community, a decade, or a percentage. "Why communities trust
elders" is a name-shaped question; the figure returns the form of the answer, not the answer.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The residual was described in effect terms first (a gap between a credential prediction and an
observed routing), then figures were chosen by bearing. Fig 8 :53 "Residual first, frame second."
Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
in effect terms." Had the frames been chosen first (say, "study traditional knowledge" or "study
credentialism"), any of them would have seemed to fit: Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go
looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit".

The vocabulary was bootstrapped by proximity from the residual: contact, channel, anchor,
frame, filter, valence, habitat. Each term was checked against the verbatim figure text before
being used.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by
   what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question that means an outcome comparison per
   slot, not a survey of attitudes.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :51),
   with a source gate and a shadow test.

Neither is present here. So the answer stays a shape.

### Correction to my own reading

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any naming of local practitioners or
credentialing as the relevant frames. That was an overstatement. The canonical phrase governs a
**missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Here we are not inventing a seventh term; we are binding
already-available frames to an effect. The prohibition that actually bites is the one against
claiming the binding settles the question. Fig 8 :51 "Convergent derivation and adopted import
look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" is the
relevant rule: record the binding as a binding. Both readings are kept so neither is quietly
lost.

## 4. Why the person carries what the certificate does not

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read 2026-11-23): "Writing carries the context well and the
subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." The
essay's example is an apprentice learning a trade by observation, imitation, practice and
correction. Polanyi's "we can know more than we can tell" is quoted there. When contact is not
possible, "the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because
anyone can check that against the world."

That is a high-overlap source (framework author), so it corroborates weakly. But the mechanism it
names is independently visible in the world sources: traditional knowledge is "transmitted orally
across generations", and TEK is place-specific rather than universal. A certificate is a
compressed reading of a person's standing; it does not carry the history that makes the local
reading work. Fig 12 :107 "Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."

The essay also gives the failure mode: "Communities and institutions that only check against
themselves drift together and don't notice." That is Fig 2's coherent drift, and it is the reason
a credential frame that stops checking against the local outcome can remain perfectly
self-consistent while losing contact.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to
validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to
be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer
must be blind to prestige."

For this question the credential is a prestige proxy with a real budget function. It is efficient
at giving a claim a hearing (Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared
meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"). It becomes a structural error only when it is
used to skip the outcome (Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict.
Never let a filter hand you a verdict."). This is why the solution refuses both naive positions:
credentials are neither worthless nor verdicts.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output", but "the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against
the figures. It is not a test of the historical claim and cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Consequence for this solution

The deliverable is a shape plus a pre-registered refuter, not a historical narrative. Fig 4 :22
"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What can be offered to an expert is the
method for checking a decision slot, not a recording of one community's story. A failure is more
useful than an endorsement.
