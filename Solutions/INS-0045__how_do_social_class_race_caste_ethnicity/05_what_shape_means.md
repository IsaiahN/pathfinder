## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The essay adds that the searcher's problem is vocabulary, not the size of the library, and that the way to improve the vocabulary is proximity: use the words already held that are closest to the thing being described, then recurse.

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For INS-0045 it is: per-slot outcome residual, an unmoved outcome anchor, and a union of channel, procedure and habitat conditions. It is not: a ranked list of which category causes the most inequality.

## 2. How the shape was obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: start with the vocabulary closest to the residual, look for terms that overlap or recur, and narrow recursively. The essay says *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :30–31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- The essay's own warning applies directly: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is why the audit refuses to treat authority access and legal protection as one question.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a name would be licensed by an outcome record that discriminates among the candidate conditions.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50–52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The world sources in `09` are used this way. Kleinberg, Mullainathan and Raghavan (2017) is an import from mathematics/computer science: its closure differs, so its incompatibility result can explain a gap in the union reading. It does not name the social cause. Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan (2023) supplies the three-level decomposition as a habitat enumeration, not as the framework's answer.

## 4. Source, projection and the missing discriminator

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green but *"it cannot comment on the source."* The same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, red plus green plus blue, or a scheme not yet discovered. The essay's conclusion: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is exactly the situation at the end of `01`. A recorded group disparity is a projection. It can come from a procedure treating groups differently, a habitat supplying different resources, or a channel failing to record outcomes. An aggregate "fairness score" is the instrument that cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. The framework's own name for the stop: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For INS-0045 this forbids both kinds of prestige leak: citing famous authors on inequality instead of outcome records, and gathering prestigious endorsements of the shape. A well-known reviewer's agreement is a budget to be tried, not a verdict. The ask in `02` is therefore for a refutation from someone whose evidence pool differs, not for endorsement.

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* But *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is treated as simulacra here.

So `01` through `11` are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of any claim about class, race, caste, ethnicity or gender, and they cannot settle the question. They say what a checkable answer would have to look like, and where it honestly stops.

## 7. Correction carried forward from the exemplar

The exemplar's `05` section 5b records an overstatement of the "shape, not a name" rule: it governs a **missing** primitive, not the selection of atoms already held. That correction is carried here without re-arguing it. In INS-0045 the five named categories are already in the world; they are habitat members read from the residual (Fig 11 :43). What is missing is the discriminator among conditions, so the "shape, not a name" rule applies to the **lever**, not to the list of categories. This avoids the exemplar's error without erasing its record.
