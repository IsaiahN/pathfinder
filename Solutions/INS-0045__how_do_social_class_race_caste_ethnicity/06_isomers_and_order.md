## DERIVATION

### Q1: Partition or union?

- Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
- Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**

The question bundles at least two conditions: access to public authority and access to legal protection. A person who fails both is not at the extreme of one; they are in a specific compound whose loop must be read separately. The three conditions in `01` (channel, procedure, habitat) overlap the same way. Partition would allow a single lever per condition; union requires that the intersections be named before search.

**Falsifier:** a complete partition of the observed disparities into disjoint single-condition sets, with no case belonging to two conditions and no interaction term.

### Q2: What is the order?

- Fig 3 :20–21: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
- Fig 3 :27: **"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."**

Order: perception (can the institution distinguish the person and the event) → vocabulary (are the relevant harms nameable) → objective (what counts as equal protection) → planning (a remedy) → learn and carry (record and carry forward). The residual line itself forces only the first three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by a frame. A remedy offered before the vocabulary link exists lands above the break. This is a dependency claim, not a claim that one social category is more important than another.

**Falsifier:** a case where planning succeeds while vocabulary is absent and perception is broken, measured against the outcome anchor.

### Q3: Is the effect an isomer?

- Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Two groups with the same observable resources and the same formal rights can have different access if the arrangement differs: one reaches an official through a network, the other only through a form that is never processed. Same listed atoms, different bond, different substance. The isomer reading is licensed only at this structural level. It does **not** license saying that two racial or caste categories are chemically identical; they are habitat members with different histories, which is why Fig 11 :51 forbids a standing list. Mode `specified` for the structural application, `general` for the operator.

**Falsifier:** two groups with identical arrangements whose access outcomes differ systematically while the anchor and habitat are held fixed.

### Q4: Which analogies are licensed?

- Fig 9 :27–28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"**
- Fig 8 :50–52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."**

Licensed analogues are those that share the residual's shape and arrive from a closure that differs from the framework's:

1. **Simpson's reversal** (Sprenger and Weinberger, SEP 2026): an aggregate association disappears or reverses within subpopulations. Low overlap, corroborates Fig 1 :22. Used as a method warning, not as a social cause.
2. **Fairness incompatibility** (Kleinberg, Mullainathan and Raghavan, 2017): three plausible criteria cannot generally hold together. Low overlap, corroborates the union reading in Q1.
3. **Three-level discrimination** (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, 2023): structural, organizational, interpersonal. Different field, corroborates habitat enumeration. Its evidence pool overlaps with the institutional literature, so it is one witness, not three.

Refused analogues: treating the five categories as the six physical substrate terms of Fig 13 (that would rename social positions as states and structures, a strain); treating "public authority" as a conserved quantity (no instrument returns such a reading). A strain sends the derivation back to the census, it does not soften the reading.

### Q5: Negation, not just accumulation

Fig 12 :112: **"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."**

A solution folder that only accumulates explanations of why groups differ has not carried the correction. It needs the refusal term: which candidate cause is being refused, and on what reading. The five pre-registered refuters in `01` are that ¬ term. Without it the folder can be corrected and cannot carry the correction.

## CONCLUSION

The bundle is a union (Q1), its dependency order runs perception to carrying and the residual forces only the first three links (Q2), the group-level difference has a licensed isomer reading at the arrangement level only (Q3), and the admissible analogues are low-overlap method warnings rather than causes (Q4). The refusal term is written in advance (Q5). No new primitive is imported, no category is ranked, and the `gap` for the discriminating instrument is preserved.
