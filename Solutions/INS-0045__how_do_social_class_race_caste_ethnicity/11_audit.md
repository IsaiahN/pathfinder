## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among an institutional library Γ (rules, categories, routines, belief), an action (ruling, referral, delay, silence), an observation (recorded outcome) and a group slot, with a time index because encounters accumulate. A two-place reading (group, outcome) loses the procedure and the prediction, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the institution's opinion. "Access" and "protection" are thresholds comparing a quantity against another quantity, not symmetric conjunctions (Fig 12 :87, *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). The five named categories are also not interchangeable: their histories differ, so swapping them changes the meaning.
- **Scale:** the slot is a person, the ranking is population-level, and the time index crosses the membrane between an encounter and a pattern. Intrinsically cross-scale (Fig 4 :20).

## 4. Round-trip gap `R_T`

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's three-condition union to one sentence. Result: *group differences in access come from how well an institution stays in contact with the people it decides about.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that sentence into a specific housing dispute. Result: *listen harder to applicants and make the form easier to find.*

**`R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`.** The abstraction loses four things:

1. It collapses the union into one lever. The returned advice treats a channel failure and a habitat failure as the same fix (Fig 9 :57).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Stays in contact" does not say that the outcome record, not internal agreement, settles the question (Fig 2 :29, :35). Coming back down, it permits an institution to measure its own success (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses the valence flip. "Listen harder" has no place for the possibility that a previously helpful procedure changed sign when the residual changed (Fig 11 :49, :51).
4. It loses the convention rule. Nothing in the abstraction says that a formally impartial rule becomes a verdict once it distributes protection (Fig 10 :18, :47).

This gap is not an error to eliminate, it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is why the packet sends the generator, not the one-paragraph version.
