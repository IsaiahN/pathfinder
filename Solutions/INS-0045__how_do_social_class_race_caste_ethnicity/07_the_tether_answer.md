# The Tether's answer on INS-0045

**Mode: `specified` for the application, `general` for the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading. No outcome dataset was read.

## 1. The residual it answers

An institution's procedure predicts that a person meeting its criteria receives a defined outcome. The recorded outcome for a person in group slot `s` departs from that prediction by `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`. The question is why that gap is small for some class, race, caste, ethnic or gender slots and large for others. It is a per-slot question, not an aggregate one, and it is a union rather than a single question.

## 2. The shape

1. **The effect is indexed by group, never averaged.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A near-zero overall fairness headline with one failing group is a legal state under these figures, not a contradiction.
2. **The anchor is the recorded outcome distribution, not the institution's account of itself.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* This question is constitutively about people's access and protection, so outcomes for people are the right anchor. Fig 1 :27: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
3. **Three conditions hold together, as a union.** Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The conditions are: a channel still recording what happens to that group (Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*); a procedure that can be ranged against an unmoved anchor (Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*); and a habitat read from the world, whose actor valences are relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*). When two fail, the case is categorically different: Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
4. **Formal impartiality is a convention until it decides outcomes.** Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*; Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. A written equal-protection rule makes no claim about the world until applied; once its application distributes protection, it is graded as a verdict against the anchor.
5. **Effective access belongs to person-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* So a person's access is not a property of their merit alone.
6. **The framework does not name the lever.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Which category binds in which dispute is enumerated from the world (Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*), not fixed in advance. The specific reform that closes a disparity is a declared `gap`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking of which category matters most. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not a single cause, not a single lever. The conditions are a union; a single lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54–57).
- Not that "impartial law" is sufficient. A convention that decides an outcome is no longer merely a convention (Fig 10 :47).
- Not that aggregate fairness statistics are irrelevant. They are a filter for where to look, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not `grounded`. No outcome measurement was used in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the recorded outcome move when the procedure is held fixed and the habitat changes?
- Does it move when the habitat is held fixed and the channel is repaired?
- Does it move when the formal rule is held fixed and its application changes office?
- Are the five refuters in `01` section 4 still standing after these readings?

A group-specific outcome record that discriminates these cases would name the lever. An aggregate headline cannot.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: an instrument that separates procedure failure, habitat failure and channel failure. It is not named. The lineage is recorded so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62–64).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank class, race, caste, ethnicity or gender, and it does not claim a single cause or a single reform. It says that access and protection must be read per group, because an average hides the one slot that is failing; that the anchor is the recorded outcome for people, not an institution's own account of its fairness; and that three conditions hold together as a union, a channel that still records, a procedure rangeable against an unmoved outcome, and a habitat read from the dispute rather than from a standing list. A formally impartial rule is a convention until its application distributes protection, at which point it is graded like any other verdict. What it offers is the shape any checkable answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and an honest `gap` where a source-discriminating instrument is needed. The specific lever stays open until the ground pays for it.
