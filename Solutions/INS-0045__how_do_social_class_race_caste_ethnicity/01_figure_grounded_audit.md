## 0. Residual, stated in effect terms

An institution's procedure predicts that a person who meets its published criteria will receive a defined outcome: a hearing, a licence, a benefit, a reachable official, a protection under law. The observed outcome `o′` for a person in group slot `s` departs from that prediction by `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|`. The question asks why `R_s` is small for some slots and large for others, and why the gap tracks class, race, caste, ethnicity or gender.

The object of explanation is **the systematic variation of a per-slot residual**, not "inequality" in the abstract. Nothing in this statement names a mechanism. In particular it does not yet name prejudice, a legal rule, a resource, a network, or a culture. Each of those is a candidate frame picked before the residual was described, which Fig 9 :27–28 warns against.

**Anchor check.** A nameable anchor exists. It is the recorded distribution of outcomes themselves: who was heard, who was protected, who obtained the licence, who was refused. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* And Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* This question is constitutively about people's access and protection, so outcome records are the right anchor. The institution's own account of its fairness is not.

## 2. Domain census

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

**Arity.** At least four operands: an institutional library Γ (rules, categories, routines, belief `b`), an action `a` (a ruling, an allocation, a referral, a silence), an observation `o′` (the recorded outcome), and a slot `s` (a person or group). There is also a time index, because access is cumulative across encounters. A two-place reading (group, outcome) would lose the procedure and the prediction, so it is rejected.

**Symmetry.** Not symmetric. The anchor does not update on the frame's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Access" and "protection" are threshold comparisons (Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), so one operand is a quantity compared against another, not merely conjoined with it. The named social categories are not interchangeable symmetric operands either: their histories differ, so swapping them does change the meaning.

**Scale.** The slot is a person, but "authority" and "inequality" exist only at population scale. The time index crosses the membrane between an individual encounter and a population-level pattern (Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."*). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale.

|---|---|---|---|---|

No nameable anchor means not a domain yet, so X1 is held open rather than admitted. X2 and X3 are absorbed, not refused.

### 2.3 Partition or union

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D3 form one compound (contact maintained); D2 and D4 form another (reach and claim standing). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

"The list of five categories" is itself a union that must be unbundled before searching: a poor woman of a marginalized caste seeking a legal remedy is not a harder case of any one category. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The world source in `09` (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, 2023) independently organizes discrimination as structural, organizational and interpersonal levels, which is the same union read from a different field.

### 2.4 Dependency order (Fig 3)

Perception (can the institution distinguish who is who and what happened) → vocabulary (does it have terms for the relevant categories and harms) → objective (what counts as progress, equal protection) → planning (remedies) → learn and carry (does it record and carry forward). Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Effort spent on a remedy before the vocabulary link exists lands above the break.

## 3. Findings

### F1: Aggregate fairness statistics throw away the only signal (decisive for method)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

An overall "equal access" rate, or a single national rule-of-law score, cannot answer this question because it sums the slots whose residual is live and the slots whose residual is near zero. Fig 1 :13 permits a near-zero global reading with one live slot; that is exactly the legal case where a group's access fails while the headline looks clean. Per-slot indexing is therefore a methodological obligation, not a preference.

**Refuter:** a group-specific access failure that is fully visible in, and correctly predicted by, the aggregate statistic with no loss of information. If that holds, indexing by slot adds nothing.

**Triangulated:** Fig 1 and the mathematics of aggregation reversal (Sprenger and Weinberger, SEP 2026, read 2026-11-23; a world source, used only as corroboration). Low overlap with the framework. World status: corroborates.

### F2: The institution cannot score itself with its own fairness metric

Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

A court reporting that it is impartial, a registry reporting that it serves everyone, and an agency reporting its own compliance rate are all quantities the frame produces. They are real records of the frame's self-model, not readings of the ground. The anchor is the independently recorded distribution of outcomes. This is why the outreach packet asks a checker *what would count as a failure*, not whether the institution's account is convincing.

**Refuter:** an institutional self-assessment that predicts independently recorded group outcome distributions at low residual, across a change of institution and a change of time.

**Triangulated:** Fig 1 and Fig 2, two figures with different offices (residual production vs ground). World corroboration from the fairness-testing literature: a system passing a fairness test is not a certificate that it is fair (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, *Testing discrimination in practice*, 2023, read 2026-11-23).

### F3: A declared impartiality is a convention, and can stop being one

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**
Fig 10 :47: **"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"**

This is the figure-grounded core of the puzzle in section 46 of the same source file ("Can institutions be formally impartial while producing systematically unequal outcomes?"). A written equal-protection rule is a convention: it makes a claim about nothing in the world until it is applied. Once the rule's application systematically distributes protection and authority, it is grading outcomes, so it must itself be graded against the anchor. Declaring the category "impartial" does not ground it.

**Refuter:** a formally impartial rule whose application produces a recorded outcome distribution that is independent of group slot at every level of aggregation, and that remains so when the rule is applied by a different office.

**Triangulated:** Fig 10 and Fig 2 :36 (the anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it). World corroboration: "Formal equality under the law primarily addresses direct discrimination and has relatively little effect on indirect discrimination" (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, 2023).

### F4: The relevant groups are enumerated from the world, not composed from a list

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :44: **"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."**
Fig 11 :51: **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

The five named categories in the question are a starting habitat, not a closed set. Which of them is load-bearing depends on the residual: in a housing dispute, class and race may bind while caste does not; in a different institutional setting the ordering flips. A category's valence is relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49). Therefore any answer that fixes a permanent ranking of which category "matters most" is a standing list of good actors, forbidden by Fig 11 :51.

**Refuter:** a fixed ordering of category importance that survives a documented change of residual and a change of institution.

**Triangulated:** Fig 11 and Fig 4 (anchor/actors/currency change across scales). World corroboration: the three-level structural, organizational and interpersonal decomposition (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, 2023), which is an enumerated habitat rather than a composed essence.

### F5: Effective access is a property of person-and-habitat, not of the person

Fig 11 :39: **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Transposed to this question at the individual stratum: a person's effective access is not a property of the person's merit, preparation or "deservingness" alone. It is a property of the person in the institutional habitat. Training an applicant to navigate a procedure without changing the procedure changes contact only if the training changes what the anchor returns; otherwise it is an improvement that changes nothing. This is a structural claim, not a denial of individual agency.

**Refuter:** an individual-level intervention that reliably moves the group outcome distribution while the institutional procedure and habitat are held fixed, measured against the anchor.

**Triangulated:** Fig 11 and Fig 10 :24. World corroboration from human cognition: stereotype threat changes measured performance when social identity is made salient (Steele and Aronson, 1995, abstract read on PubMed 2026-11-23), so a person's reading can move with the habitat without any change in the person.

### F6: The channel decays, and pooled readings hide the group signal

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**
Fig 10 :26: **"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"**

A registry that stops recording refusals, a complaint form that is only available online, a metric that counts applications but not outcomes: these are channel failures. They are not the same as the institution changing its mind. Because pooled readings are named as a channel failure, an aggregate statistic that averages across groups loses contact with the ground in exactly the way F1 predicts. Distinguishing channel failure from procedure failure requires an instrument that reaches the unrecorded cases.

**Refuter:** group outcome disparity that persists while the channel returns complete, current, unpooled readings for every group slot.

**Triangulated:** Fig 10 and Fig 1. World corroboration: audit studies and outcome tests are built precisely to reach unobserved decisions, and their interpretation is contested when the channel is selective (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, *Testing discrimination in practice*, 2023).

### F7: A categorical line in the law is a comparison threshold, not a possessed property

Fig 12 :87: **"a threshold relation between two quantities"**

"Access to public authority" and "legal protection" are not states a person either has or lacks in the abstract. They are threshold readings: how many refusals before a claim is heard, what share of comparable cases receive protection, at what point a contact becomes a decision. Treating them as possessed properties produces the flat-formula error of Fig 12: the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not.

**Refuter:** a case where "access" is demonstrably a state with no threshold structure and no comparison against another quantity.

**Single-source** for the operator reading (Fig 12 plus the Operators table, one figure family). The threshold claim is corroborated in the world by the disparate-impact doctrine, which is triggered by a disproportionate effect rather than an intent (Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, *Understanding United States anti-discrimination law*, 2023). Mode `general` for the operator, `specified` for the legal application.

### F8: Several impartial criteria cannot all be satisfied at once

Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."**
Fig 8 :47: **"Independence is one quantity read twice."**

The question bundles access to authority and access to protection, and each has its own measurement criterion. Requiring all criteria to hold simultaneously is not a harder version of requiring one; it is a different compound. The world literature supplies a precise low-overlap instance: no single test is both necessary and sufficient for fairness, and three plausible fairness conditions cannot in general be satisfied at once (Kleinberg, Mullainathan and Raghavan, 2017; Barocas, Hardt and Narayanan, 2023). This corroborates the union reading and refuses a one-number answer.

**Refuter:** a single criterion that is sufficient for both access to authority and legal protection across institutions, with no trade-off against any other criterion.

**Triangulated:** Fig 9 and Fig 8. World corroboration from mathematics/computer science, low overlap.

### F9: Agreement among like frames is not corroboration

Fig 7 :13: **"agreement compounds, evidence does not"**
Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Ten studies that use the same administrative dataset are one witness wearing ten names. The source ledger therefore records overlap explicitly. A genuine second witness must draw on a different evidence pool: audit studies, bounded by their own critiques, are closer to that than a second summary of the same register.

**Refuter:** two frames drawing on demonstrably disjoint evidence whose agreement still adds no information about the group outcome distribution.

**Triangulated:** Fig 7 and Fig 2.

### F10: The specific lever is a gap, and the stop is honest

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**
Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**

No figure line governs which single reform closes a given disparity in a given institution. After a genuine re-census this remains a `gap`. The missing thing is characterized in effect terms: an instrument that can discriminate (a) procedure treating groups differently, (b) habitat supplying different resources, and (c) channel failing to record outcomes. It is not named, because naming it would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135).

**Refuter:** a reform that closes a disparity across institutions and across changes of residual with no further discrimination among the three conditions.

**Triangulated:** Fig 8, Fig 13 and Fig 9, three distinct offices.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. Aggregating outcome data across group slots loses no information about any group's access, contradicting Fig 1 :19 and :22.
2. An institution's own self-assessment reliably predicts independently recorded group outcomes across a change of office, contradicting Fig 1 :27 and Fig 2 :35.
3. A formally impartial rule is shown to produce a group-invariant outcome distribution at every aggregation, contradicting F3.
4. A fixed ranking of which social category matters survives a change of residual and institution, contradicting Fig 11 :51.
5. An individual-level change moves group outcomes while procedure and habitat are held fixed, contradicting Fig 11 :40.
6. A single fairness criterion is sufficient for both authority access and legal protection across institutions, contradicting F8.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Index every outcome claim by group slot and publish the per-slot residual, not a single fairness headline (F1).
2. Range every institutional claim against the recorded outcome distribution, never against the institution's self-model (F2, F3).
3. Enumerate the habitat for each specific dispute before naming categories or remedies (F4, F5).
4. Audit the channel first: ask what is missing from the record, not only what is in it (F6).
5. Treat the two conditions as a union and keep the intersection as its own case (F7, F8).
6. Send the generator, the shape and the refuter, not a conclusion. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **What is indexed?** A person/group slot, per Fig 1 :19. Aggregate statistics are refused.
2. **What settles it?** Recorded outcomes, per Fig 2 :29 and :36. Institution self-report refused.
3. **Is this one question?** No. Union of contact, reach and convention (Fig 9 :54-57).

6. **What is settled?** Nothing in this derivation. Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Status `candidate`.
7. **Where does it stop?** At a shape with a declared `gap` for the lever. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: access and protection vary **per group slot**, are **settled by recorded outcomes rather than institutional self-description**, and depend on a **union** of a maintained channel, a procedure ranged against an unmoved anchor, and a habitat whose relevant actors are enumerated from the world. No specific category ordering, no single cause, and no particular reform survives. Strains after re-census: **none unresolved**. The verdict is `specified`, never `grounded`, and the lever remains a declared `gap`.
