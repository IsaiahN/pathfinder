# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the thermodynamic cost of computation with gravity present

**What this is:** a claim in a form a statistical physicist, gravitational theorist or reversible-computing researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the sub-claims, `gap` on the numerical correction. Not `grounded`.** No measurement with the gravitational variables controlled is claimed. Simulated derivation, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The request for *the* thermodynamic cost of computation in a gravitational setting bundles at least three conditions with different currencies and anchors. A description price in bits and a physical settling price in actions do not add, and the gravitational factor rebinding local energy to energy at infinity is a third term, not a new local law. The local erasure price is the choice erased, logarithmic in the alternatives; whether the bill recurs depends on whether the computation history is retained. Gravity supplies a spendable gradient, and the price must be stated per memory, per reservoir and per background, never as a global average. The figures constrain this shape and stop before a number, which remains open until an instrument settles it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** two currencies are held apart and cannot be added into a single cost.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125, *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 12 :126, *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* Fig 12 :130, *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*
- **What to check:** whether the pricing rule in Fig 12 is about description and settlement cost rather than joules of heat. If so, the "single number" reading is a notation error, not a numerical estimate. The thermodynamic heat is a world claim (Bérut, Landauer, Bennett), not a figure line.
- **Refuter:** a measurement showing that one context-free number settles the cost for all backgrounds and reservoirs without separating the two currencies.

### C2 — framework-internal with a physics-facing consequence; checkable against Figure 12 and the reversible-computing literature

- **Statement:** the local price is logarithmic in the erased alternatives, and it recurs unless the arrangement is recorded.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :99, *"A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."* Fig 12 :107, *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."* Fig 12 :108/109, *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
- **What to check:** Bennett 1973 states that *"a computer must dissipate at least kT ln 2 of energy ... for each bit of information it erases or otherwise throws away"* and that machines *"may be made logically reversible at every step."* Does the recording condition in Fig 12 match the mechanism in Bennett? Note the overlap is low (bond pricing versus computation theory), so agreement here is triangulation, not one pool talking.
- **Refuter:** a controlled experiment showing that retaining versus discarding the history makes no difference to dissipated heat per logical operation.

### C3 — framework-internal plus world; checkable against Figures 13 and 4 and the gravitational literature

- **Statement:** gravity contributes a spendable gradient and a rebinding of energy between local and referred-to-infinity values, not a seventh primitive; the rebinding carries a nonzero round-trip gap.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14, *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (energy); Fig 13 :22, *"a difference that can be spent"* (gradient); Fig 4 :30, *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
- **What to check:** the opened Bardeen paper states that *"the lapse represents the gravitational redshift, rather than a modification of the local thermodynamic statement itself."* The hairy-black-hole paper states that *"gravitational hair tends to reduce the Hawking temperature and the minimum Landauer erasure cost."* Do these support a rebinding, as C3 says, rather than a new local law? The direction of the correction is not claimed.
- **Refuter:** a derivation of a gravitational temperature or correction from the six-term set alone, with no import and no ground reading; or a measurement showing the local and referred-to-infinity costs are identical with no slicing term.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal number or a gravitational correction to `kT ln 2`.
- Not that the two currencies are convertible at a fixed rate.
- Not that gravity changes the local erasure law; the lapse is a redshift.
- Not that the six-term substrate derives gravitational thermodynamics; the Tolman and Hawking quantities are imports with provenance.
- Not that the three conditions are one question. They are a union with a second-order composition.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement in this derivation.
- Not a claim that any of the opened papers is wrong. Their disagreement in direction is recorded, not adjudicated.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Currency separation:** an experiment pricing a logical operation in bits and its physical settling in actions, testing whether a fixed exchange exists.
2. **Recording test:** heat per logical operation with history retained versus discarded.
3. **Rebinding test:** the same device at two potentials, resolving local and referred-to-infinity energy and the round-trip gap.
4. **Per-slot test:** resolution by reservoir level and horizon area level.
5. **Standing refuters:** a context-free number; one predicate for all three conditions; a six-term derivation of a gravitational temperature; a global average that settles the question.
