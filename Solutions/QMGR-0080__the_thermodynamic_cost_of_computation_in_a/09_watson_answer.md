## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single number does not. Two currencies are held apart and never added. The local erasure price is logarithmic in the erased alternatives and recurs unless the history is retained. Gravity supplies a spendable gradient and a rebinding between local and referred-to-infinity energy, not a seventh primitive. The request is a union of three conditions that must be split, and the price is per slot, never a global average. The numerical gravitational correction is a `gap` at this budget, with named instruments that could close it. Mode `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the form and explicitly disagree on the value, which corroborates the open slot rather than filling it.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, names the refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-number recording instead of a split generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-number framing is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. No primitive has to be withdrawn, and no survey or bulk contact has occurred. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment resolving the two currencies for the same device, a controlled retained-versus-discarded history comparison, the same device at two gravitational potentials with local and referred energies both resolved, or a per-level measurement by reservoir and horizon area would move the relevant finding to `grounded`. A measurement showing one context-free number for all backgrounds, or a six-term derivation of a gravitational temperature with no import, would break the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
