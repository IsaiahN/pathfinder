## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its number. For this question the shape is:

> Any admissible answer must price a logical description and a physical settling in different currencies, must price them per memory, per reservoir and per background rather than as a global average, must say whether the bill recurs when the history is discarded, must treat the gravitational factor as a rebinding between local and referred-to-infinity energy rather than as a new primitive, and must name the instrument that would settle the remaining number.

That is a constraint set. It is not a value of `kT ln 2` corrected by a lapse factor.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Fig 9 :30/31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*

Applied here: the effect is "a memory is changed and heat appears," and the question is what in that chain the gravitational background changes. The world sources (Bérut, Bennett, the black-hole papers) are the borrowed vocabulary. They narrow the shape without naming a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that is a controlled erasure measurement with the gravitational variables resolved.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Tolman temperature and the lapse factor are imports from gravitational physics into the information-thermodynamic frame. They are legitimate imports with provenance, but they do not derive a new primitive from the six-term set.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* (opened 2026-11-23) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* It also says: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the primitives makes the choice visible, not neutral. So the six-term substrate cannot be used to declare a gravitational correction by fiat.

## 4. Why "one number" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several distinct sources give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a single number "the thermodynamic cost of computation in a gravitational setting" is a reading that conflates at least three sources: erasure of logical alternatives, the local-versus-infinity referral of energy, and horizon area growth. Without an instrument that discriminates those sources, naming one number from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This derived shape cannot validate its own number; it does not have one.

## 6. Correction to my own §3

I initially treated the phrase *"shape rather than a name"* as if it forbade using terms already in the six-term set. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs *"a **missing** thing"*, a primitive not held in the library. Energy, gradient and comparison are already held. The forbidding applies to naming a new numerical law for a missing gravitational term, not to binding a held atom to an imported physical quantity. Both statements are kept: the binding is legitimate with provenance; the number is not licensed. See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the parallel correction on the structure side.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* The ask gives the shape a budget to be tried; it does not gather names to endorse it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
