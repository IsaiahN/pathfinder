## What the wider literature does for this question

1. **The local bound is real and measured.** Bérut et al. and the Georgescu review give the ground reading that the figures cannot supply: erasure has a bound and it has been approached experimentally. This corroborates the shape at F2, it does not derive it from the framework.
2. **Reversibility changes the bill.** Bennett 1973 is the independent, different-field witness for F3: retaining the history is the mechanism by which the recurring price is avoided. Low overlap with the substrate census, so this is the strongest triangulation in the folder.
3. **Gravity changes the accounting, and the literature disagrees on direction.** The hairy-black-hole and cosmological papers report a decreasing minimum heat; the Bardeen paper reports that vanishing Hawking temperature does not force the heat to vanish, and that the lapse is a redshift, not a new local law. These three do not share a single bracket. They corroborate the *form* of the answer (per-slot, background-dependent, open number), not a value. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* applies with extra force because these papers also share methods.
4. **No source was found that settles a universal gravitational correction.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The gap is recorded, not presented as a proof of impossibility.
