## 0. The residual in effect terms

The question asks for the thermodynamic cost of computation in a gravitational setting. Read as an effect, it asks what a device must pay, in physical currency, to change a memory when the background spacetime, horizon or gravitational potential is part of the situation. Three readings are available:

- **A.** There is one universal price, a gravitational correction to the Landauer bound `kT ln 2` fixed by the local or Hawking or Tolman temperature and the lapse. One number, one law.
- **B.** The request bundles distinct conditions: the logical irreversibility cost of the memory operation, the referencing of energy between a local device and infinity, and the horizon information bound. These are priced separately and their union is a compound with its own loop.
- **C.** There is no figure-governed price at all, only a shape that any answer must have, and the numerical slot stays open until an instrument settles it.

What each leads to: A adds two currencies that the figures hold apart and delivers a `general` verdict with no ground reading; B splits the union and keeps the currencies separate; C is the honest stop when the instrument is missing. The derivation below finds B for the shape and C for the number.

**Bound term (Formula step "Direction").** The sought-for shape is made an ordinary term: an answer must specify (i) currency, (ii) slot, (iii) whether the price recurs, (iv) the settling instrument. It then accrues a residual like anything else and can be refused.

## 3. Findings

### F1 — "Cost" is two currencies, and they do not add (decisive for A)

Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*

Fig 12 :126: *"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."*

Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

Operators table :46: *"progress is a subtraction, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot say how far along it is"*

**Strain test and re-census.** My first reading tried to make Fig 12 price joules of heat directly. That strains: Fig 12 prices a *description* (bits) and *actions spent to settle it* (settlement cost), not thermodynamic heat. A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity (canonical usage rule). Re-census: the question's single "cost" is a **compound** of a logical description price and a physical settling price, plus a gravitational rebinding. So F1 does not say "there is no cost"; it says the request for *one* cost is a category error under the figures until the currencies and the rebinding are separated. A is refused as written.

**Refuter:** a measurement showing a single context-free number settles the cost for all backgrounds and all reservoirs without separating description bits from physical actions would refute F1.

**Mode:** `general` (structural), triangulated across Fig 12 and the Operators table (low overlap: pricing rule versus bond semantics).

### F2 — The local price is a bond price, not a constant of nature

Fig 12 :99: *"A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."*

Fig 12 :100: *"The price of an arrangement is the choice it records, so it is logarithmic in the number of bonds available."*

Fig 12 :101: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."*

Erasure collapses two states (0 and 1) into one. The choice erased is the choice the bond records; the price is logarithmic in the alternatives. Landauer's `kT ln 2` is the physical instance of this shape, not a figure line. World corroboration (opened): Bennett 1973, *"a computer must dissipate at least kT ln 2 of energy ... for each bit of information it erases or otherwise throws away"*; Bérut et al. 2015, *"the mean dissipated heat saturates at the Landauer bound"*.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that erasing a bit with a fixed set of accessible alternatives has a price independent of the number of alternatives would refute F2.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 12 pricing rule plus a different-field world source, Bennett, low overlap). Not `grounded` by the figures.

### F3 — Whether the price recurs depends on whether the arrangement is recorded

Fig 12 :107: *"Settling compresses the description and not what it cost to establish."*

Fig 12 :108/109: *"where the arrangement is recorded, re-deriving it is a lookup and the actions were spent once. Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*

This is the sharpest figure-grounded claim in the audit. A reversible computation retains the history, so the intermediate arrangement is recorded locally and the actions are spent once; an irreversible one discards it, so the bill comes due again on every lineage. This is a *structural* claim about recording across a boundary, not a claim about a particular engine.

Fig 4 :30 requires the boundary be measured: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

World corroboration (opened, low overlap in field): Bennett 1973, *"such machines may be made logically reversible at every step, while retaining their simplicity and their ability to do general computations"*; the Reversibility Institute principles page, *"Build a computation that never forgets and the bill, in principle, vanishes."*

**Refuter:** a controlled experiment showing that retaining versus discarding the history makes no difference to the dissipated heat per logical operation would refute F3.

**Mode:** `general` for the recording rule, `specified` for its application to a given device. Triangulated (Fig 12 plus Bennett, different fields).

### F4 — Gravity enters as a gradient and a rebinding, not a new primitive

Fig 13 :14: *"conserved, finite, directionless alone"* (energy).

Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"* (gradient).

Fig 13 :49: *"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard."*

Fig 13 :71: *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"* (gradient load-bearing in the second and third laws).

Fig 13 :32: *"the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise."*

**Re-census note.** My first reading said "energy is directionless alone, therefore gravity adds the direction." That strains against Fig 13 :22, which gives directedness to the gradient, not to energy. Re-census: a gravitational setting supplies a **gradient** (potential difference, horizon surface gravity) that can be spent, and the realized cost is the remainder the crossing discards. No seventh primitive is introduced. This keeps Fig 13 consistent with itself, as the canonical usage rule requires.

World corroboration (opened, gravitational physics): Andrade et al. 2026 (hairy black holes), *"gravitational hair tends to reduce the Hawking temperature and the minimum Landauer erasure cost, while simultaneously increasing the horizon area and the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy"*; Vakili 2026, *"the minimum heat required to erase one bit decreases while the entropy-equivalent information content of the horizon increases"*; Andrade et al. 2026 (Bardeen), *"the lapse represents the gravitational redshift, rather than a modification of the local thermodynamic statement itself."*

The last quote is the rebinding in one sentence: the gravitational factor changes where the energy is referred, not the local thermodynamic statement. It also explains why F1 survives: adding a lapse factor to a local bound is a change of accounting, not a new law.

**Refuter:** a derivation of a gravitational temperature from the six-term set alone, with no import and no ground reading, would refute the claim that the binding is an import rather than a derivation.

**Mode:** `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 substrate plus multiple gravitational-physics sources with low overlap with the substrate census). The exact numerical law is not claimed.

### F6 — The price is per slot, never a global average

A single "cost of computation in a gravitational setting" in joules per operation averages over the very thing that carries the signal: which memory, which reservoir level, which radius, which horizon area.
