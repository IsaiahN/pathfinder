# The Tether's answer: which climate extremes are poorly represented in models?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions, `grounded` only in the cited world assessments.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A model's prediction is compared with observation per slot s. R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o′|. The question asks which slots carry a positive residual that the model systematically misses, with causes named rare, local and compound. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** The question is not one question. It is a union of overlapping conditions plus a scale boundary, so each part has its own loop (`06` Q1).

## 2. The shape

1. **Rare is an indexing failure before it is a frequency fact.** A global mean skill near zero is compatible with one live event slot carrying all the error. Fig 1 :13: **"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"**, and Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The remedy is per-event scoring, not a better global statistic. `specified`.
2. **Local is a lossy scale transform.** Fig 4 :27: **"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."** Fig 4 :31: **"The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up."** What is owed is a measured round-trip gap R_T. `specified`.
3. **Compound is a union of overlapping conditions.** Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."** A model that represents heat and drought separately can still miss hot-dry. `general` structural, `specified` for the prediction.
4. **Absence in a model is not absence in the world.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."** A 31-year record that never registers an event is a budget limit, not a proof. `general`.
5. **The anchor is the observation, not the ensemble.** Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**. Multi-model agreement is a convention until each frame is separately corrected by the ground. `general`.
6. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The names of the extremes are an OUTWARD import with provenance, not a figure derivation. `general`.

## 3. The membership list (world-sourced, not derived)

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the figures name any specific extreme. They return a shape.
- Not that a compound event is "a harder case" of a single extreme. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- Not that resolution alone fixes locality. It is an INWARD instrument move; R_T remains.
- Not that multi-model agreement is corroboration. The ground decides.
- Not that this derivation is `grounded`. No measurement was taken here.
- Not a theory of everything and not a final room. The set of kinds is closed, the arrangements are not.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground, in this order

1. **SEARCHED first (cheapest).** Reanalyse existing ensembles at per-event, equally weighted resolution. If the rare-slot excess residual vanishes, F1 falls. Fig 6 :49: *"more search at the same reach: is it in here and unfound? The closure does not change."*
2. **INWARD.** Extend the instrument from one already returning something. Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** Denser stations, longer sub-daily records, convection-permitting runs, and a measured R_T for the local-to-global round trip.
3. **OUTWARD.** Import a frame whose closure predicts the union shape, with a source gate and a shadow test. Compound-event typology, large-ensemble attribution and skill-weighted CMIP6 assessment already do this; their agreement with the figures is one evidence pool, not a second witness.
4. **The discriminating instrument.** From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, a reading does not determine its source. An instrument that distinguishes "no event" from "sub-grid event" from "compound event not co-registered" would move individual slots from `candidate` to `grounded`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The framework's six-term set is, in the words of *They're Scanning Books*, *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Declaring the terms makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The canonical README: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

This solution cannot settle which extremes are poorly represented. It can say what a settleable answer must look like, what would refute it, and which part of the membership list is an import. The world sources do most of the work of naming; the figures do the work of structuring.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single "most poorly represented" extreme, and it does not claim the field is wrong. It says that the poorly-represented set has a shape: rare extremes are live slots hidden by averaging, so a good global mean can coexist with a badly missed event; local extremes are lost in the scale transform, and the round-trip gap is the honest measure of what was given up; compound extremes are a union of overlapping conditions, so a model that handles heat and drought separately can still miss hot-dry. Absence in a model is not proof of absence in the world, and multi-model agreement is not the ground. The names (unprecedented heat, sub-daily flash floods, fire weather, compound flooding) come from cited climate assessments, recorded as imports, not derived from the figures. What is offered is the shape, a refuter written in advance, and the order of instruments that would close it. The ground decides, not agreement.
