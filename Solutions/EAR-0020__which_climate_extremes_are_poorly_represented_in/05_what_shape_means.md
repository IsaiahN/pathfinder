## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the member

The canonical README (local copy) says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 says *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says it *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed characterization of the poorly-represented set that can be written before the membership list: a live per-slot residual that averaging hides (Fig 1 :19), a lossy scale transform with a measurable gap (Fig 4 :31), and a union of overlapping conditions whose intersection is a different object (Fig 9 :57). `RARE(???)LOCAL(???)COMPOUND` is a shape. `atmospheric river`, `fire weather` and `compound flooding` are names.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the target, then look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* In this derivation the starting vocabulary was "model residual", "resolution" and "joint probability", and the terms gained were "per-slot", "round-trip gap" and "union".
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is Fig 9 :54 in the essays' own vocabulary and is why the census in `01` did not accept a three-way partition.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email in `02` leads with "per-slot skill will show excess residual at rare slots" rather than with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."*

The climate-extreme names are licensed by path 2. IPCC AR6, Zscheischler et al., Bevacqua et al. and Ridder et al. are frames whose closures differ from the figure set; each was opened and recorded with a quote. Their names are imports, and the ledger is the provenance.

## 4. Why a reading does not determine the source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* Several paths create the same reading, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. The resolution came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*.

Applied here: a coarse model field that reads "no extreme at this grid cell" does not determine whether the source was (a) no event, (b) an event below the resolution, (c) a sub-grid event aliased into the mean, or (d) a compound event whose constituents were never co-registered. Naming source (a) from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument or a finer record, not an assertion.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* makes the same point for generated artefacts: *"the frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants."* And: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. Q for this question — does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any extreme?

**No, with a condition.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* thing, a primitive not held in Γ. The relevant atoms here (per-slot residual, transform, union, bond) are already in the figure set, so describing them is not naming a missing primitive. What is forbidden is presenting a world-frame name as if the figures derived it. The correct form is: the figures yield `RARE(???)LOCAL(???)COMPOUND` as a structural shape, and the membership of the three slots is filled by cited climate assessments, recorded as imports.

**Did I get this wrong earlier?** Yes. My first reading of F1 treated "rare" as a property of the atmosphere (a low-frequency event) rather than of a slot under an evaluation weighting. The re-census in `01` §6 retracted that: rarity is relative to the residual's indexing, per Fig 1 :19 and Fig 1 :28. Both readings are recorded so the correction is visible. The *Hard Problem* point in §4 is **not** retracted: the coarse reading still fails to discriminate among sources, and the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous climate institutes whose names appear on a claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against observations.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* From *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"you get a plausible simulated output to an issue in any given domain that the LLM has knowledge of"* and *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of which extremes are poorly represented and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The five rules as a reading aid

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*, the five rules distilled from the six-term set: cross-kind composition only; second-order derivatives may pair with either kind; every composition introduces residual; similar paths can yield identical artefacts distinguished only by lineage; outward traversal needs selection, inward traversal needs elimination. Rule 3 is the reason the round-trip gap in F2 is a permanent measurement rather than a fixable bug. Rule 5 is the reason `01` §4 runs SEARCHED (elimination inward) before OUTWARD (selection of an importing frame).

From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A method that is brilliant in one setting must not be drowned by an average across all of them."* The essay cites Fig 1 directly for this. It is the social-scale version of F1 and it is why the skill weighting in Ridder et al. (2022) matters.
