## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a model prediction and observation per slot s. Arity: at least two conditions bound in one slot for compound cases. Symmetry: not symmetric; a compound is an ordered or overlapping binding, not a commutative join. Scale: from sub-grid local slots to global multi-model ensembles, so a lossy transform is in play. IPCC AR6 (world, secondary) notes tornadoes at under 100 metres and minutes, versus multi-year droughts over vast regions.
- **Domain 1, the model frame.** Actors: grid cells, parameterisations, ensemble members, skill scores. Currency: prediction and residual. Anchor: observation. Question type: per-slot indexing rather than aggregation.
- **Domain 2, the scale boundary.** Actors: the resolved field and the sub-grid event. Currency: information lost in the coarse/fine transform, R_T. Anchor: the same observation at the finer scale. Question type: lossy transform, not identity.
- **Domain 3, the compound condition.** Actors: two or more drivers bound in one event. Currency: joint frequency and dependence. Anchor: observed joint occurrence. Question type: **union** of overlapping conditions, not a partition (Fig 9 :54-58).
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: modellers, reviewers, multi-model ensembles, attribution studies. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is low; the world sources form a second, partially overlapping pool.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, then vocabulary, then objective, then planning, then learn and carry. SEARCHED is asked before INWARD and OUTWARD (formula step 7).

Strain test applied: Fig 1 (how R is indexed) and Fig 9 (how conditions are bundled) govern different quantities, so their apparent tension dissolves on re-census (`01` §6). The Fig 11 DDT cascade is an ecology analogue, low overlap with the climate frame, so it corroborates the cascade shape without supplying a climate mechanism.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the membership is imported. Rare is a live slot hidden by averaging, local is a lossy scale transform with a measurable gap, and compound is a union of overlapping conditions whose intersection is a different object. Absence in a model is not proof of absence in the world, model agreement is not the ground, and the figures return a shape rather than a name. The membership list (unprecedented heat, sub-daily flash floods, hot-dry, fire weather, compound flooding, spatially concurrent breadbasket heat) is `grounded` in IPCC AR6 and the cited papers, not derived here. Three verdicts are **single-source** within the figures (dependency order, cascade rule, and the bond mapping in `06`), and all three are stated with live refuters. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result of this derivation.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states modes, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a conclusion) instead of a generator (the shape and its refuter). Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A second cost is applying a lever to the wrong link in the dependency chain, which Fig 3 :19 says cannot rescue a broken link above it.
- **undoable:** yes. Any of the three structural conditions can be withdrawn without touching the others; the union reading in `06` is modular. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The membership list is provenance-stamped, so an import can be replaced when a better source arrives.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-event equally weighted skill that removes the rare-slot excess (C1), a downscaling round trip with R_T indistinguishable from zero for a named local quantity (C2), a driver-independent model that reproduces a compound joint frequency (C3), or an instrument that discriminates no event from sub-grid event from unregistered compound event at one slot. A figure re-census that shows rare, local and compound form a partition would also reverse the union verdict. Prestige or receptiveness is not such an instrument.
