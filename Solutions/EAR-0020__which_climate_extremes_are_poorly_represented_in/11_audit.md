- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a model prediction and an observation per slot s. Arity: at least two conditions bound in one slot for a compound case, so the residual is not scalar. Symmetry: not symmetric; the binding is ordered or overlapping, and Fig 9 :57 says a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Scale: sub-grid to global ensemble, so a transform between scales is mandatory. This matches the primary's R.

Question type: **union** with overlapping classes, not a partition. I derived this from Fig 9 :54-58 and the Operators table, before reading F3. The world typology (Zscheischler et al. 2020) independently says classes have soft boundaries.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: modellers, ensembles, reviewers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is low; the climate papers form a second, partially overlapping pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :13, :19, :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 4 :27, :31 (lossy scale), Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 2 :35, :40 (anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` §2 and `09` TETHER: rare equals indexing, local equals lossy transform, compound equals union; membership is imported.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim in `07` §3: unprecedented heat, sub-daily flash floods, fire weather, compound flooding, spatially concurrent breadbasket heat.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond assignment** for each imported compound class. The figures supply seven typed bonds, but which one maps onto preconditioned, multivariate, temporal or spatial compounding is a `specified` mapping with a shadow test, not a derivation;
- the **dependency order** (Fig 3), which the world sources do not test directly. Bevacqua et al. (2023) show a perception/vocabulary failure in effect terms, but do not establish that planning effort cannot rescue it;
- the **no-proof-of-absence limit** (Fig 8 :39), which a practical modelling workflow routinely violates by reading "not in the ensemble" as "not there."

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the live slot must be indexed per event, the transform is lossy with a reportable gap, and the three causes form a union. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
