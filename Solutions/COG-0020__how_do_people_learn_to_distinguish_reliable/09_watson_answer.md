## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about learning to distinguish reliable signals from noise across environments with different risks and information sources. Option A: treat reliability as a property of cues and sources, and return a checklist plus a universal accuracy target. Option B: treat reliability as a residual-relative relation, return the per-slot shape, the risk-set threshold and the conditions under which a verdict can be trusted, and report the missing curriculum as a gap. What each leads to: A produces a frame-internal score that a frame produces about itself, selects sources by a correlation that disqualifies a verdict, and replays recordings in changed habitats; B produces checkable sub-claims, refuters written in advance, and an instrument owed at each slot.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A learner's gap between what the current library predicts about a cue and what pays at a slot. Arity: a cue-source-risk triple, indexed per slot. Symmetry: the two errors are not interchangeable; the split is an ordered comparison with asymmetric costs. Scale: one lifetime for individual learning, across scales for teaching and culture. Symbols table :38-40 says what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **D1, individual perception and attention.** Actors: a learner, a stream of observations, a per-slot prediction. Currency: hits, misses, false alarms, correct rejections. Anchor: outcome feedback that does not move with confidence. Question type: union of perception and objective links, ordered by dependency (Fig 3).
- **D2, decision under risk.** Actors: a decision maker, competing cues, a cost pair. Currency: expected cost of each error type, criterion placement. Anchor: a consequence that pays. Question type: threshold comparison, not a symmetric join (Fig 12 :87).
- **D3, social and information sources.** Actors: peers, institutions, media, testimony. Currency: transmitted reports and credibility labels. Anchor: independent ground contact at the slot. Question type: union of mutual update, common cause and triangulation (Fig 2).
- **D4, accumulation across learners.** Actors: a population over time. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground persists while the channel decays (Fig 10 :24). Question type: membrane crossing and room selection (Fig 4, Fig 7).
- **D5, curriculum and criterion value.** Actors: none nameable. Currency: none. Anchor: none. Not yet a domain; reported as `gap`.
- **Union, not partition.** D1 to D4 are overlapping conditions with different anchors, so one lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57). Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse before mint (formula step 2); refit before inventing (Fig 5 :13); settle before carrying (Fig 4 :22-25); change the room when the question changes (Fig 10 :72).

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :32 (noise as discarded cross-scale detail) and detection theory's noise (a within-level distractor distribution) govern different objects. Re-census dissolves the apparent conflict, and both are kept. AUC is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60) rather than a verdict witness, so Fig 12 :87 and ROC analysis do not strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Reliability is a residual-relative relation, not a property carried by a cue or source. The split is an ordered threshold with asymmetric costs, so different risks change the criterion and the amount of independence bought, not the method. Frame-internal readings cannot settle a verdict; an outcome that does not move with confidence can. The usual learning break is the objective link, and what crosses to another learner must be a checking method rather than a recording. The observed regularity's source remains unresolved until an instrument discriminates among candidates. The missing curriculum and criterion value are a declared `gap`. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a credibility checklist instead of a generator and a refuter. Fig 2 :42 prices that cost: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." It is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The checklist and accuracy-target readings are removable without touching the separator; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. Fig 6's amendment is relevant: a refusal adds a term and lowers the reach without deleting anything.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument at a slot that separates competing candidate sources would name what the framework leaves as a shape; an outcome that moves with confidence would break the anchor test; a cue whose reliability survives a residual change with no re-enumeration would break Fig 11 :49-51; practice without a stated objective that reliably produces above-chance separation in a regular environment with feedback would break the objective-link diagnosis; a recording that produces a new success in a changed habitat would break Fig 4 :22-25. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
