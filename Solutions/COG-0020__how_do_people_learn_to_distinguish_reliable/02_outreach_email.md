# Outreach draft: check one claim before deciding whether the framework is worth reading

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four findings in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` marked `specified` have been read in full by the sender. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about signal detection, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** In a stream of observations, reliability is not carried by a cue or a source. It is a relation between a source, a residual, and a habitat. Fig 11 :49 says "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries" and Fig 11 :51 says "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." The signal-noise split is an ordered threshold between two quantities (Fig 12 :87), with a different cost for each error, so different risks change the criterion and how much independent checking is worth, not the method. Fig 2 :42 puts it as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
>
> The corollary is that a pooled detection score is the wrong object. Fig 1 :19 says "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" and Fig 1 :22 says "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Confidence, fluency and agreement inside one evidence pool are frame-internal; Fig 1 :27 says a frame "cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." What settles a slot is an outcome that does not move when the learner's confidence moves (Fig 2 :29-30).
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not claiming the figures name a reliable cue, a criterion value, a training schedule, or a universal accuracy number. They return a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"). I am not claiming that established signal detection theory is wrong. Detection theory's separation of sensitivity from response bias and its asymmetric criterion under different payoff matrices are the things I am using as an outside check, not replacing.
>
> **What would refute the claim.** I wrote these down before sending this.
> 1. A cue or source's reliability survives a change of residual with no re-enumeration.
> 2. A frame-internal quantity, confidence, coherence, or agreement within a shared evidence pool, settles a reliability verdict against a non-updating outcome.
> 3. A recording of one past success, replayed in a changed habitat, produces a new success with no method passed.
> 4. In a regular environment with immediate, accurate feedback, practice does not improve separation of hits from false alarms.
>
> If any one holds, the claim is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula for direct download, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output first, theory second | Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Each frame ranges against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45). |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not an honesty device. Fig 5 :65-66: "Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else." |
| Mode stated on every claim | `THE_FORMULA.md`: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No universal accuracy number | Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." |
| Independence priced by risk | Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." |
| New sources are imports with provenance | Fig 8 :50-52: convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them. |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." |
| The framework returns a shape, not a name | Canonical rule: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The email never asserts which cue occupies a slot. |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |
| Ask users to state their own ground and refuter | `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. |

**Contact discipline.** Use the public submission or contact route of the venue, never a personal email. One recipient at a time. Record their verdict on stage 1 before showing any theory. Do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
