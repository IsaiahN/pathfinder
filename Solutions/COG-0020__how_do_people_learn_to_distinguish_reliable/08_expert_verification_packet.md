# Expert verification packet: the Tether on reliable signals and noise

**What this is:** a claim in a form a judgment, decision-making or signal-detection researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the separator, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the curriculum and criterion value. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at a slot is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Reliability is not carried by a cue or a source. It is a relation between a source, a residual and a habitat, so no standing list of reliable signals survives a change of environment. The signal-noise split is an ordered threshold between two quantities with different costs for the two errors, so different risks change the criterion and how much independent checking is bought, not the method itself. Confidence, fluency and agreement among sources that share an evidence pool are frame-internal and cannot settle a verdict; only an outcome that does not move with the learner's confidence can do that. The usual learning break is the objective link, not perception, and what crosses to another learner is a testing method and a head start, not a recording of the sources that worked last time. The framework returns this shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument owed at each slot. It does not name the cues or set the number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal, checkable by reading Figures 11, 1 and 2

- **Statement:** reliability is residual-relative; a frame-internal reading cannot settle a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check:** is the ordinary phrase "reliable signal" a property of the cue or a relation to a task and a habitat? Detection theory's separation of `d'` from response bias and Kahneman and Klein's warning that "a feeling of recognition does not by itself show that the recognized pattern is predictive" are outside checks in the same direction.
- **Refuter:** show a cue or source whose reliability survives a change of residual with no re-enumeration, or show a frame-internal quantity settling a verdict against a non-updating outcome.

### C2: decision-facing, checkable against signal detection theory and risk research

- **Statement:** the split is an asymmetric threshold, and risk changes the criterion and the amount of independence bought.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :31 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 8 :47 "Independence is one quantity read twice."
- **What to check:** does the ROC framework treat the criterion as a separate parameter from sensitivity, with the operating point chosen by the payoff ratio? Does the false positive paradox show that the same test has a different positive predictive value in a low-prevalence population? Are two informants drawing on one feed one witness or two?
- **Refuter:** show that the two errors are interchangeable under the cost structure for some slot, or that a pooled accuracy figure is sufficient to choose an operating point without stating the costs.

### C3: learning and transfer, checkable against expertise research and the dependency chain

- **Statement:** the usual break is the objective link; a recording of one success cannot replace a passed method.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :25-27 "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Fig 7 :36 "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."
- **What to check:** does a learner given an explicit prediction and refuter outperform one given only more exposure or more sources, in a regular environment with feedback? Does a rule of thumb transferred to a changed habitat fail where a checking procedure succeeds? Kahneman and Klein 2009 give the outside condition: "Two conditions must be satisfied for skilled intuition to develop: an environment of sufficiently high validity and adequate opportunity to practice the skill."
- **Refuter:** show that a recording of one past success, replayed in a changed habitat, produces a new success with no method passed, or that practice without a stated objective reliably produces above-chance separation in a regular environment.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a checklist of reliable cues or sources, and no standing credibility list.
- Not a universal accuracy target, criterion number, or training curriculum. Those are a declared `gap` (Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence").
- Not that detection theory, base-rate research or the expertise literature is wrong. They are secondary corroboration, not replaced.
- Not that confidence is useless. It is data, disqualified only as a verdict (Fig 1 :27).
- Not that agreement is always corrupt. Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." It becomes worthless as a verdict when the frames share an evidence pool.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at a slot was taken.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument at the slot, not a longer argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source" and that the case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Concretely: (1) can a reading separate competing candidate sources of the same regularity? (2) does the outcome used as anchor stay fixed when confidence changes? (3) do two sources draw on different evidence? (4) does a passed method produce a new success in a new habitat? The five standing refuters are in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 and `07_the_tether_answer.md` section 5.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :49-51, Fig 1 :19/:22/:27, Fig 2 :29-31/:42, Fig 12 :87, Fig 4 :22-25 and Fig 3 :25-27. Nine short passages.
2. Check C2 against your own knowledge of criterion placement and base rates: are sensitivity, bias and prevalence three separate quantities here?
3. Name one instrument at a slot you know that would distinguish two competing sources of a reading, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
