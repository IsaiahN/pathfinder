## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Law in books versus law in action is the field's own founding distinction, not an invention of this framework.** Pound's 1910 title and Holmes's prediction thesis both say that the operative rule is what officials do. That corroborates F1 and F8 at the level of the field's vocabulary, but realism and sociological jurisprudence share an evidence pool with each other (Pound is a forerunner of realism), so they count as one witness, not three (Fig 2 :31, :40).
2. **Ehrlich's living law is a low-overlap corroboration of the same shape from legal sociology.** The distinction between state-enforced positive law and rules people actually obey is Fig 10 :49 in a different vocabulary. Because it comes from a different discipline and a different century, it is weaker on provenance overlap than the American realist sources and therefore slightly stronger as an independent witness.
3. **Decoupling is the organizational-sociology analogue.** Meyer and Rowan and DiMaggio and Powell explain organizational practice by legitimacy rather than efficiency, with formal structures as myths and ceremonies. That corroborates F6 (same atoms, different bond, different substance) from a field with genuinely low overlap with legal theory. It is the strongest external witness here.
4. **Procedural justice supplies the person's side.** Tyler's finding that voice affects perceived fairness even when non-instrumental, and the distinction between mandatory and discretionary behaviour, corroborate the claim that compliance is not a single quantity: it splits into sanction-driven and internally valued behaviour, which is two different bonds at D3/D4. This is a social-psychology witness, low overlap with legal doctrine.
5. **Legal pluralism supplies the differing closure.** The observation that people choose the system offering the best advantage, and that formal and informal ideologies coexist inside a courthouse, corroborates F5 and F9. It shares the law-and-society pool with realism, so it is not fully independent.
6. **Unenforced law and selective enforcement supply the mechanism vocabulary, not independent evidence.** Both are reference pages describing the phenomenon the question already states. They are used to orient the census, not to carry the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
7. **No world source conflicts with the figure verdict**, so the conflict rule was not triggered. The nearest tension is between the realist prediction thesis and any purely textual account of law; that was returned to the census and resolved as F1 rather than treated as a flipped verdict.

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Seat Map :163** (local) - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure at their own slot; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1** sends the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user writes down what would refute their result and labels the mode.

**Submission-policy caveat.** Several of the venues above are peer-reviewed journals or conference programs. Many such venues restrict work generated by AI tools or require that the author take responsibility for every claim. This package is explicitly a simulated derivation, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), and it makes no claim to have run a measurement. Before submitting, check each venue's current author policy. If the policy forbids this kind of output, do not submit: offer the stage-1 claim and refuter through a public discussion or workshop route instead, with the simulation status stated on the first line.
