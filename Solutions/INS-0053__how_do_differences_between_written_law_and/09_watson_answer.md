# WATSON answer, INS-0053

**Mode: `specified` on substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal effect size. Not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. No enforcement event or expectation was measured.

## QUESTION

How do differences between written law and everyday enforcement shape people's expectations and behavior?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| stay live at some person-domain-case slots and collapse at others when the written rule and the enforcement event disagree, and which signal does the person's controller range against? R is indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the realized enforcement event plus the person's subsequent behaviour, not an official opinion about the rule (Fig 2 :29).

Option A: the written text alone determines expectations, so changing text alone moves behaviour even with the channel held constant. Option B: a declared meaning does not ground itself, so expectations follow whichever signal has a reachable channel to the ground, and the question splits into three conditions with different instruments. What each leads to is written down and bound before the run (Formula, "Direction"). The verdict recorded below is Option B in `specified` mode.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a three-place relation (person, written rule, enforcement event), indexed by slot and ordered by time; carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: text and event are not interchangeable, and a national average destroys the slot signal (Fig 1 :22). Scale: person, office, population, cohort; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

Union, not partition: D1/D2, D2/D3 and D3/D4 form three compounds, and the whole is a union of C1 (declared meaning grounded), C2 (channel reachable) and C3 (a seat able to refuse) (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3). Strain test: applying Fig 13's six physical terms to legal rules only fit by renaming a statute a spectrum and a sanction a gradient; re-census sent Fig 13 to the honest-stop role (strain S2 in `01` section 6).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: **a written rule is a declared meaning and does not ground itself. Where text and enforcement diverge, expectations and behaviour follow whichever signal keeps a reachable channel to the ground; divergence persists when expectation and enforcement mutually update without a non-updating anchor; agreement between the two frames is evidence only when they do not share an evidence pool; the same statutory atoms under a different enforcement bond are a different social substance. The question is a union of three conditions, C1 grounded meaning, C2 reachable channel and C3 a seat able to refuse, and the verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged.** Mode `specified` on substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. The law-and-society evidence pool corroborates the shape after re-census but shares provenance, so it is one witness set; organizational sociology is the genuinely low-overlap corroborator. Naming the universal effect size or the winning rule is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis and wasted reform. A jurisdiction failing C2 would be sold clearer drafting, or a jurisdiction failing C1 would be told to add enforcement, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs three. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible, because the packet states its falsifiers first.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its four falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the four pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a text-only change with a held channel moving expectations at the slot, (2) collapse at high bracket fidelity and high ground pressure, (3) an averaged statistic predicting prospectively per slot, or (4) identical statutory atoms with different bonds giving invariant expectations. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot expectations are fully predicted by the text alone would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
