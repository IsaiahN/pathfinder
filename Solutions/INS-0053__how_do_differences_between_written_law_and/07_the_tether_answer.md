# The Tether's answer on written law and everyday enforcement

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A written rule predicts one outcome; enforcement at the slot returns another. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| stays live at some slots and collapses at others, and is indexed per person, domain and case, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is not "does law matter" but *which signal a person's controller ranges against when the two disagree*. The anchor is the realized enforcement event plus the person's subsequent behaviour, measured at the slot (Fig 2 :29-30). The question is a union of three conditions with different instruments (Fig 9 :54-57), not one dial with a right setting.

## 2. The shape

1. **A written rule is a declared meaning, and a declared meaning does not ground itself.** Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*; :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* It becomes operative only when the habitat pays for it. The realist prediction thesis and Ehrlich's living law are the field's own names for this, recorded as bindings, not derivations.
2. **Where text and event diverge, expectations and behaviour follow the reachable signal.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :27: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*. The statute stays on the books; the channel to what pays decays.
3. **Divergence persists through mutual update when no anchor is pinned.** Fig 2 :13: *"no anchor; each updates on the others"*; :15: *"is whoever had the most influence"*; :33: *"The anchor must not update."*; :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A person's expectation and an office's enforcement priority can converge on each other while the drift remains invisible.
4. **Agreement between text and practice means one of two different things depending on independence.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*. Shared drafting means the agreement is efficient for a convention and worthless as evidence of justice. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents (Fig 8 :52), so provenance carries the decision.
5. **The same statutory atoms under a different enforcement bond are a different social substance.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* This is why identical text produces different expectations across offices and why a transplant does not transplant the practice.
6. **A legal order renews only by contact with a differing closure.** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Imports carry a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13).
7. **A convention that decides an outcome stops being a convention.** Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. A declared rule cannot certify its own legality (Fig 5 :50, :53).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a universal percentage for how much behaviour changes when text changes. That is a `gap`: no figure line returns a number, and the discriminating instrument is not held (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not "enforcement is everything and text is irrelevant." Text is a candidate library Γ whose reach must be tested against the ground (Fig 1 :27).
- Not a policy recommendation, not a rule to enact, not an identification of which jurisdiction is a success.
- Not a standing list of "good laws". Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not a derivation of one legal system from another, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an enforcement measurement.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

An instrument that discriminates sources at the slot, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does the person's expectation at the named slot move with a specified change in the enforcement event while text is held constant, or with a specified text change while the event is held constant?
- **Threshold Test:** is there a measurable threshold at which a declared rule starts paying at the slot, or demonstrably not (Fig 12 :87, comparison bond)?
- **Shared-pool Test:** do the statute and the enforcement record draw on the same evidence, or are they genuinely low-overlap frames, so that their agreement could count as triangulation (Fig 2 :31)?
- **Refusal Test:** can the slot's checker refuse a reading that both the text and the office endorse, or has the convention become a verdict (Fig 10 :47, Operators :58)?
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: text-only change with held channel moves behaviour; collapse at high fidelity and high ground pressure; averaged statistic predicts prospectively per slot; identical atoms with different bonds give invariant expectations; one lever restores all three conditions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term physical substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* and does not govern legal rules; mapping it onto statutes was a strain and was re-census-ed to the honest-stop role. Declaring the legal vocabulary makes a choice visible, not neutral. Another discipline's minimum set need not use these terms. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that written law is irrelevant, and it does not return a number for how much behaviour a text change will move. It says that a written rule is a declared meaning, and a declared meaning does not ground itself: where the statute book and everyday enforcement diverge, expectations and behaviour follow whichever channel to the ground stays reachable. Divergence persists when a person's expectation and an office's enforcement priority update on each other without a non-updating anchor, and agreement between the two frames is evidence only when they do not share an evidence pool. The same statutory text under a different enforcement bond is a different social substance, which is why identical laws behave differently and why transplants fail. The question splits into three conditions with different instruments, and the framework offers their shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. It stops there: which rule works, and how much, is not held.
