## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, fetched 2026-11-23: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0053 the shape is: a written rule is a declared meaning; an enforcement event is a candidate ground; expectations follow whichever channel to the ground is reachable; the question splits into three conditions with different instruments. The universal effect size, the particular statute, and the particular office are names, not shapes.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* rather than *"the cause"*.
- The nearest available vocabulary here was legal realism's "law in action", Ehrlich's "living law", and organizational institutionalism's "decoupling". Those were used to bootstrap the census, not to supply the verdict. The residual was stated first (section 0 of `01`), and only then were frames chosen (Fig 8 :53, *"Residual first, frame second"*).
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is the authority for splitting INS-0053 into C1, C2 and C3.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* - and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens this. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"* - one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: observing that a person complies does not tell you whether they complied because of the text, because of the sanction, because of a peer, or because of habit. Several sources create the same behaviour. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources at the slot, not a name chosen from a survey response. This is the direct reason the universal "how much" question is a `gap` in this solution.

## 4. Why "law in action" is a binding, not a derivation

The phrase *"law in action"* names the realist school's observation, not a figure line. It is useful as a proximity word in the Library-of-Babel sense: it is the vocabulary already closest to the thing. Fig 9 :30-31 warns that the words are borrowed and that the effect should be described rather than the cause. So the audit describes the effect (a declared meaning that does not ground itself; a channel that decays; a bond that changes the substance) and records "law in action" as the field's own name for the same observation. Calling it the framework's answer would be apophenia: picking a frame first and mapping the residual onto it afterwards (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a legal institution or rule?

**No, under the narrowed reading.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Naming an available institution (a court, a statute, an enforcement office) is a binding from the institution's frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a seventh kind.

What remains forbidden:

- inventing a new primitive to make the legal domain fit, which the strain test caught in `01` section 2 (strain S2);
- claiming that such a binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*);
- treating the binding as a derivation when only a provenanced import with a shadow test would make it one (Fig 8 :52).

**Correction to my own earlier practice.** In a first pass I treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any legal institution. That overstates the rule. The rule governs missing primitives. Naming a held institution is provenance work, and it carries zero evidentiary weight until the source gate and shadow test are shown. Both the narrowed rule and the original overstatement are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README + Fig 6 + Fig 8).

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, fetched 2026-11-23: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers prestigious legal names to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground, a named slot with a measured enforcement event and a measured behavioural response, settle it. Fig 10 :59 adds the honest constraint: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* The author's stake in this derivation is structural; it is declared, not asserted away.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. No enforcement record was opened, no person was observed, no expectation was measured.

## 8. Consequence for this question

The honest stop is a shape with three conditions and four refuters, not a legal-policy recommendation:

- the figure-grounded shape says what must be true for a written rule to shape behaviour at a slot;
- it says which instruments would distinguish the three failure conditions;
- it does not say which rule to enact, how large the effect is, or which jurisdiction is a success;
- the instrument that would name the source of a compliance reading is not held here, so the universal quantitative question is `unreached`, not `unreachable in principle` and not silence (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).
