## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the disagreement between a written rule's prediction and an enforcement event at a person's slot. Arity: three-place (person, text, event), indexed by slot and ordered by time. Symmetry: asymmetric; a text and an event are not interchangeable, and averaging across slots discards the only signal (Fig 1 :19, :22). Scale: person, office, population, cohort; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :30).

- **D3 Person's expectation loop.** Actors: person, belief b, weighting w. Currency: prediction and behaviour. Anchor: subsequent behaviour observed at slot. Admitted.
- **D4 Legitimacy and refusal seat.** Actors: community, peers, outside checker. Currency: trust and cooperation. Anchor: cooperation observable when sanction is absent. Admitted.
- **D5 Time channel.** Actors: cases, habits, precedents. Currency: contact across cohorts. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Admitted.
- **X1 Text as free-floating cause.** No anchor independent of D2. Excluded, as a structural exclusion not a claim that texts never matter.
- **X2 Culture.** Already inside D2 and D4 as habitat conditions and relations.

Union, not partition: D1/D2, D2/D3 and D3/D4 form three compounds, and the whole is a union of grounded meaning, reachable channel and a refusal seat (Fig 9 :54-57). Dependency order: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3 :20).
