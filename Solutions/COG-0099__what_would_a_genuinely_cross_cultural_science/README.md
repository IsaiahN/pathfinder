## Residual in effect terms

A prediction formed inside one cultural frame is exported to another population and the reading it returns fails to register against what is actually there. The failure is reported either as a difference that the ground does not pay for, or as the absence of a difference that the ground does pay for. Stated as an effect: the measuring frame is silently included in the measurement, so the instrument scores itself with a quantity it also produces. The question asks what methods, concepts, and standards of evidence would make that residual shrink honestly rather than being averaged into a grand mean, explained away as noise, or settled by agreement among frames that share the same assumptions.

Bound ordinary term (formula step "Direction"): an answer would look like a **separator plus a shape**. The separator says which conditions must hold for a reading to count as evidence about the mind rather than about the measuring frame. The shape says what form any candidate answer must take: per-frame residuals, independent frames ranging against a ground that does not update, effect descriptions that travel, imports with provenance, a refusal available, and no final room. No figure line supplies the names of the specific techniques or the content of a universal concept list, so that part is declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons, stated in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not add an anchor.** Fig 7 :12-13, "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 7 :35, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
2. **Correlation has valence.** Fig 2 :39-40, "Correlation has valence." and "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Cross-cultural psychology already has one dominant evidence pool (WEIRD samples); selecting for agreement with that pool is the named collapse.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
