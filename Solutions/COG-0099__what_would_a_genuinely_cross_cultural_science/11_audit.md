## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

Scale: individual mind, frame, and the membrane between frames (Fig 4 :2).

- **Order by dependency.** Vocabulary before instrument before evidence standard; habitat enumerated throughout. The likely break is between vocabulary and objective, where no instrument exists (Fig 3 :24-27). Fig 3 :19, "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- **Union, not partition.** Verdicts per domain, never averaged. Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :35/:40 (triangulation, pooled agreement), Fig 6 :12-13/:24/:32 (import gates, instruments), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :43 (habitat), Fig 12 :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `09`: unbundle the union; keep readings per frame; non-updating ground; independence bought; declared primitives with refusal; effect descriptions; no last room; specific techniques are a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete best-practice package: measurement invariance testing, multigroup confirmatory factor analysis, a fixed number of cultures, a universal trait list, and a threshold for acceptable agreement.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing** survives in the abstract but is routinely lost when a culture mean becomes the unit of analysis;
- the **source gate and shadow test** on a category imported between frames are absent from a package that merely adds sites;
- the **non-updating anchor** is replaced by agreement when a standard is chosen because panels agree;
- the **gap itself** is erased when a named technique is presented as figure-derived.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the union separator and the refuter, because both were bound as ordinary terms before the run (Fig 5 :65, "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."). That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
