## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what shape a genuinely cross-cultural science of mind would have in methods, concepts and standards of evidence. Option A: treat the three as facets of one universal method and report a single best-practice package. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, keep verdicts per domain, return the shape the figures license, and declare a gap for what the figures cannot name. What each leads to: A aggregates away the per-slot signal, mistakes a shared evidence pool for corroboration, and presents a world technique as a figure result; B gives a checkable separator, refuters written in advance, and an open slot until a ground pays.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A prediction formed in one frame fails to register against the ground in another population: either a difference appears that the ground does not pay for, or no difference appears where one does. Arity: at least three conditions (method, concept, evidence), so a union compound (Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."), not one hard question. Symmetry: the comparison between frames looks symmetric but the bond decides whether it is triangulation or a shared pool; Fig 12 :5, "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Scale: individual mind, frame, and the membrane between frames (Fig 4 :2, "The Membrane Between Scales").
- **Domain 1, instrument and method.** Actors: researchers, tasks, scales, participants, instruments. Currency: prediction and residual per slot. Anchor: the ground, which does not update on the frame (Fig 2 :29). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, concept and vocabulary.** Actors: categories of mind, declared primitives and their bindings, local terms. Currency: composition inside closure(Γ). Anchor: figures as metalaws for shape, ground for settlement. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated for structure, with a named binding left open.
- **Domain 3, verification and evidence.** Actors: frames, seats, replications, registered reports. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: `general`, triangulated.
- **Domain 4, habitat and contact.** Actors: language, schooling, migration, media, ecology, institutions, testing situation. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground (does not update) and the habitat (does). Verdict: `general`, triangulated.
- **Union, not partition.** One lever clears single-condition regions and leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-57). Verdicts per domain are never averaged. **Order by dependency:** vocabulary then instrument then evidence standard, with habitat enumerated throughout (Fig 3 :20, "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."). The middle link, terms for what a situation affords plus what would count as progress, has no instrument and is where chains break (Fig 3 :27, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at.").

Strain test applied: Fig 13 's physical six-term substrate applied to mind was narrowed by re-census to a shape-level claim using Fig 4 :19-20 (kernel invariant, actors/currency/anchor change); Fig 2 :40 versus Fig 8 :50 was dissolved as one independence parameter read twice; Fig 11 :51 was narrowed to actor valence relative to a residual. See `01` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-method package does not. A genuinely cross-cultural science is a union of method, concept and evidence conditions with different anchors. Readings stay per person and per frame; the ground must not update on the frame; instruments grow from what is already returning something; categories are declared with provenance and can be refused; cross-frame agreement counts only at maximum independence; habitats are enumerated; effects are described rather than causes exported; imports carry a source gate and shadow test; and the science has no last room. The isomer claim is real only over a fixed atom set with a changed bond to the ground. The specific technique, concept list and culture count are a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not silence and not an invitation to invent a package. Mode `specified` on per-domain claims, `general` on structure, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a best-practice package) instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter. A second cost is importing a wrong atom across frames: "A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives" (Fig 6 :24).
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings and imports are correctable by adding provenance and a shadow test; a refuted condition can be withdrawn from the shape without touching the others; the refuter can be re-registered before the next run. No primitive has to be discarded and nothing is deleted from the record.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates construct difference from response style, comprehension and testing situation would name the source; a demonstration that two supposedly independent frames shared one evidence pool would collapse the corroboration; a single intervention removing the residual in all conditions at once would break the union claim; an instrument built from a pure description that worked at first contact would break Fig 6 :32; a fixed-atom test in which different bonds to the ground behave identically would reduce isomers to a functional group; a closing frame that still improved its predictions would break no-last-room. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
