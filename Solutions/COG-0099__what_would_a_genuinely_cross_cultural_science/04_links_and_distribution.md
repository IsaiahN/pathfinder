## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **Pseudoetic measurement.** The Cross-cultural psychology article: "This pseudoetic approach is actually an emic based approach developed in a Western culture while being designed to work as an etic approach", and "Irvine and Carroll brought an intelligence test to another culture without checking whether the test was measuring what it was intended to measure." This is the method condition in the field's own vocabulary: the instrument is a frame-internal metric.
2. **One evidence pool.** The Cultural psychology article: "Findings from psychology research utilizing primarily W.E.I.R.D. populations are often labeled as universal theories and are inaccurately applied to other cultures." Fig 2 :40 calls this "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **Measurement invariance as an aid and a warning.** The Measurement invariance article supplies both the positive account and a live dispute. Welzel and colleagues "criticize the excessive reliance on invariance tests as criteria for the validity of cultural and psychological constructs" and hold that "the invariance criteria favor constructs with low between-group variance"; proponents reply that "the reliance on nomological linkage ignores that such external validation hinges on the assumption of comparability." Do not present invariance testing as a settled figure-derived standard. It is a world import with its own ground and its own disagreement.
4. **Source discrimination.** The Animal culture article's latent-solutions hypothesis is the low-overlap analogue of the metamerism point in *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "it cannot comment on the source." The same observed behaviour can arise by reinvention or by transmission, so the reading does not determine the source.
5. **Pre-commitment.** The Registered report article and the PSA submission page both show a standard that is fixed before results are known. This corroborates the pre-registered refuter but does not derive it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**, "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the union separator and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**, "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28**, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the separator were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat Map style rule, from the canonical leads policy:** contact cost is a separate axis from evidence; no receptiveness rank, no personal emails, no bulk send.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the verdict before any theory is shown; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the union shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what they would count as a failure. Record their verdict before showing any theory. This is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure links and the essays. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interiors their agreement is one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question" (THE FORMULA, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. An expert can reasonably reject the union claim or the independence requirement. That rejection is the useful outcome; it is not a failed send.
