## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is a separator plus a discipline: per-frame residuals, independent frames against a ground that does not update, effect descriptions, declared primitives, available refusal, and no final room. The names of the statistical tests and the content of a universal concept list are not part of the shape.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the reason the lineage matters even when a candidate looks exactly right: "the projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." Applied here, a measure that behaves identically in two cultures does not thereby measure the same thing. Indiscernibility is not identity.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This is the direct warrant for the union census in `01`, F1.
- Fig 6 :30, "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31, "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A cross-cultural claim has to be stateable this way, or it is a private shorthand.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." But for a genuinely cross-cultural science this is a local stop, not a final one, because Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room".

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the vocabulary is not neutral

*They're Scanning Books* states the concept condition directly: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."

The same essay names what genuine cross-cultural contact would require: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space. Importing a term extends what can be said; importing an axis extends what can be perceived at all." A science that adds more populations to the same instrument has imported a term, not an axis.

Fig 13 :16, "it names what is being measured"; Fig 13 :26, "it decides what the measurement would mean". The concept set decides both. So "what counts as intelligence or rationality" is not a definitional preliminary to the method: it is part of the measurement apparatus and must be declared and provenanced. Fig 12 :111, "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A list of traits names states; a culture's actual practices require bonds, sequence, production, comparison and negation.

Only two things license a particular name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test, and debited against independence.

## 4. Source discrimination: a reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism this question needs most. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". The source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or "a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered". And: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The resolution is not an argument: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." The spectrometer, not a debate, settled it.

Applied here: a scale score, a reaction time, or an EEG component is a reading. It does not say whether the reading arose from the construct the researcher names, from response style, from comprehension of the task wording, from the testing situation, or from a mix not yet enumerated. The low-overlap corroborator from animal behaviour makes the same point independently. The "zone of latent solutions" hypothesis holds that "many behaviours can be individually reinvented, while social learning influences their frequency or distribution without transmitting the behavioural form itself." The same observed tradition can have different sources. A source-discriminating instrument is what is owed, not a stronger assertion.

This is why F7 in `01` declares a gap rather than selecting a technique. Measurement invariance tests are a real attempt at a source-discriminating instrument, but the field itself disputes them (see `01`, S4). The framework cannot presently say which instrument wins.

## 5. The specific shape for this question

Putting sections 1 to 4 together with the figures:

1. **Union, not facets.** Methods, concepts and standards of evidence are three conditions with different anchors. Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.
2. **Per-slot residual.** Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A cultural mean is not a finding about a mind.
3. **Non-updating ground.** Fig 2 :33, "The anchor must not update." The ground is "whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks".
4. **Independence, not agreement.** From *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." and "The group's agreement is never the verdict." Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
5. **Declared primitives and refusal.** From *They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral." Operators :58, "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."
6. **Effect descriptions that travel.** Fig 9 :31, "describe the effect rather than the cause"; Fig 6 :30, "Cast it in effect terms."
7. **No final room.** Fig 7 :6, "the limit: no last room". A genuinely cross-cultural science stays open to new closures; its standard of evidence includes the obligation to keep importing from frames that differ.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any universal category in this answer. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in Γ, not the selection of an atom already held. Naming an already-available category as a candidate universal is a **binding** from a frame onto a held atom, and is legitimate if its provenance is recorded. What remains wrong is claiming that the framework *derives* which category occupies the slot, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). Both readings are kept visible: the binding is allowed, the derivation is refused. This is the same narrowing the exemplar records in its `05` section 5b.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a binding needs a source-discriminating instrument is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms.

## 6. Prestige is not action

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For this question the failure is concrete. A cross-cultural result backed by a famous laboratory, a large multi-country sample, or a prestigious reviewer gains a budget to be tried. It does not gain truth. If the frames share the same instrument, the same sample pipeline and the same categories, their agreement is "the pool talking", in the words of *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "the agreement is the pool talking". Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

*Arrival of the Fittest* adds the self-validation limit: "Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself." The architecture article also warns that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So this reading is offered as a candidate generator with a refuter, not as a verdict.

## 8. What would actually settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: it was "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument" that the green case was resolved. Here that means an instrument that can tell whether a cross-cultural score difference comes from the named construct, from response style, from comprehension, from the testing situation, or from a source not yet enumerated. Until such an instrument pays for the discrimination, the answer stops at the shape and records the lineage so the stop is checkable.
