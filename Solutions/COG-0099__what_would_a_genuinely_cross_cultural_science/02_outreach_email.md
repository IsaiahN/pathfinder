# Outreach draft: check the separator first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the union reading and the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are intact. The output must survive checking or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable separator for cross-cultural mind research, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on one specific output and would like you to check it against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` per domain, not `grounded`):** a genuinely cross-cultural science of mind is not one method with three facets. It is a union of at least three conditions with different anchors, so a single lever clears the places where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The conditions are: (1) an instrument condition, where the reading must register against something that does not change because the frame thinks otherwise; (2) a concept condition, where categories are declared choices with recorded provenance, not neutral discoveries; and (3) an evidence condition, where agreement counts only when the agreeing frames actually draw on different evidence. Residuals stay per frame and per person. Averaging cultures into one mean throws away the only signal that says where to look.
>
> **What would refute it.** I wrote these down before sending this:
> 1. one lever removes the residual in all three conditions at once, with no remaining compound;
> 2. a frame that can move the thing it measures still predicts the ground better than an independent frame;
> 3. an instrument built from a pure effect description, with no prior instrument returning anything, registers correctly in a new culture at first contact;
> 4. a universal concept list derived from held categories alone, with no provenanced import, discriminates a new population at first contact;
> 5. two frames known to share one evidence pool nevertheless produce corroboration that moves the ground estimate;
> 6. a closing frame with no outside left to import from improves its predictions.
>
> If any one holds, the affected part is withdrawn and the census is redone. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a named statistical package is the figure-derived standard, that a universal concept list can be read off the framework, that typical cross-cultural agreement is corroboration, or that this is a final theory. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Specific techniques are real and useful, but their authority is the ground of psychometrics, not the figures. I am also declaring my stake: I am interested in the outcome, and I have tried to keep that from becoming an interest in the reading by writing the refuter first.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula, verbatim with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - The eight essays and architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

> [name]
