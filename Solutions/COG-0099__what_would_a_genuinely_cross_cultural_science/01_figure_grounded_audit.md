## 0. The residual, stated as an effect

A claim about the mind is formed inside a frame: a laboratory tradition, a language, a scale, a task, a sample. It is then exported to another population. Two failure modes follow.

1. The imported instrument returns a difference that the ground does not pay for. The frame is reading its own category back out of the new population.
2. The imported instrument returns no difference where the ground does pay for one. The relevant variation is invisible because the frame has no term for it.

Both are the same structural fault: the measuring frame is a party to the measurement, so the frame updates on what it measures and the anchor moves. Fig 1 :23, "The ground enters as the only metric." Fig 1 :27, "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 2 :33, "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34, "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

**Bound ordinary term.** An answer would look like a separator plus a shape. The separator distinguishes a reading that is about the mind from a reading that is about the measuring frame. The shape constrains any candidate: residuals kept per frame and per slot, frames ranging against a non-updating ground, effect descriptions that travel, imports with provenance, a refusal available, and no final room. Binding it as an ordinary term lets it accrue a residual like anything else (Fig 5 :65), rather than remaining an invisible preference the loop cannot see (Fig 5 :62, "holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term").

## 1. Domain census, before any figure line is chosen

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** At least three conditions are bundled in the question: methods, concepts, standards of evidence. They are not three facets of one method with one anchor. Test: a larger and more representative sample fixes one condition (sampling) and leaves the category and the standard conditions binding. Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"
- **Symmetry.** The word "cross-cultural" suggests a symmetric comparison of two frames, A and B. But the comparison bond matters: treating the two frames as interchangeable operands on one scale is the flat-formula collapse, which records ingredients and not arrangement (Fig 12 :5, "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."). The valence of any method or category is relative to the residual, not carried by it (Fig 11 :49, "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries.").
- **Scale.** Three scales are in play: the individual mind (one agent, one slot), the frame (a discipline's categories and instruments), and the between-frame membrane (Fig 4 :2, "The Membrane Between Scales"). The kernel is invariant while actors, currency and anchor change (Fig 4 :19-20, "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change.").

### 1.2 One line per domain: actors, currency, anchor

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | nameable anchor? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Instrument and method | researchers, tasks, scales, participants, instruments | prediction and residual, R = |Γ(b,a) − o'| per slot | the ground: what the mind actually does; a measurement that does not update on the frame | yes |
| D2 Concept and vocabulary | categories of mind, the six-term set's primitives and their bindings, local terms | composition inside closure(Γ); bonds between atoms | the figures as metalaws for shape; the ground for settlement | yes |
| D3 Verification and evidence | frames, seats, expert readers, replications, registered reports | agreement and residuals | the ground, never agreement | yes |
| D4 Habitat and contact | culture, language, schooling, migration, ecology, institutions, media | contact; cascade of effects | the ground, which does not update; the habitat, which does | yes |

No domain lacks a nameable anchor, so all four are domains. Fig 2 :29 defines the test: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."

### 1.3 Partition or union

**Union**, with a dependency order. D1 to D4 are overlapping conditions, not a partition: an instrument can be well built and still carry a borrowed category; a category can be locally adequate and still fail at the evidence standard; a frame can be independent and still share the same instrument. One lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, quoted above). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged. Averaging across frames is precisely the aggregation error: Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

### 1.4 Order by dependency (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20, "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." The order here is: **concept/vocabulary (D2), then instrument/method (D1), then evidence standard (D3), with habitat (D4) enumerated throughout**. The reason is that the vocabulary link and the objective link are the middle of the chain, where chains usually break and where no instrument exists. Fig 3 :25-27: "The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 3 :27, "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 3 :19, "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." So adding more subjects to a flawed category (optimising the method link) cannot rescue the missing concept of what the task affords in that culture.

## 3. Findings

### F1: The question is a union compound, not one question with three facets (triangulated)

Fig 9 :4-5: disagreements "are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57, "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Library of Babel: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" Cross-cultural psychology makes the same point from inside the field: "Some critics have pointed to methodological flaws in cross-cultural psychological research, and claim that serious shortcomings in the theoretical and methodological bases used impede, rather than help, the scientific search for universal principles in psychology."

So there is no single "cross-cultural method" that could be reported as one result. There is a per-domain verdict. Refuter: if a single lever (for example, a bigger and more diverse sample) removed the residual in all three conditions at once, the union reading would fail and a single-method reading would be owed. This refuter is recorded before the recommendation.

### F2: Methods: an instrument is improved from a worse instrument, not built from a description (triangulated)

Fig 6 :32, "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced." Fig 6 :34, "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."

This governs the import of an instrument into a new culture. You cannot design a culture-fair test from a description of fairness. You extend a task that is already returning something in the new population and track the residual it produces. The field's own term for the failure is revealing: the pseudoetic approach is "actually an emic based approach developed in a Western culture while being designed to work as an etic approach", and "Irvine and Carroll brought an intelligence test to another culture without checking whether the test was measuring what it was intended to measure."

Two further method constraints follow from separate figures. First, the residual stays per slot (Fig 1 :19, quoted in 1.3), so a culture mean is not evidence about a person. Second, the reading is corrupted by pooled and saturated channels: Fig 10 :24, "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :26, "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings". A scale whose responses are pooled into a national mean is a saturated metric.

Refuter: show an instrument designed from a pure effect description, with no prior instrument returning anything, that nevertheless registers correctly at first contact. That would break Fig 6 :32 and would have to be recorded as a figure failure, not a methodological success.

### F3: Concepts: no universal list is neutral, and no standing list survives a change of residual (triangulated)

They're Scanning Books: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." This is the concept question in the framework's own terms: a culture's categories are a declared primitive set, not a discovery of nature's own list.

Fig 13 :16, "it names what is being measured"; Fig 13 :26, "it decides what the measurement would mean". The concept set decides both. Fig 12 :111, "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A list of traits names states; it cannot name what a situation affords or how a person gets somewhere from it. That is the middle-link failure of Fig 3 again, now at the concept level.

Fig 11 :51, "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Re-census required here (see S3 in section 6): this governs the valence of actors relative to a residual, not the composability of a concept set. What survives is the narrower claim that a concept's standing as helpful or harmful is relative to the residual being tracked, so a universal category cannot be licensed by its past usefulness. Emic and etic corroborates from anthropology: Pike discounted "the possibility of a truly objective description", and an etic account "attempts to be 'culturally neutral', limiting any ethnocentric, political or cultural bias or alienation by the observer" (the aim, not the achievement).

What a cross-cultural science needs at the concept level is a new axis, not a new label: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." (They're Scanning Books). And it needs refusal: Operators :58, "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." A vocabulary of traits with no negation can only add traits.

Refuter: derive a universal concept list from the six-term set alone, with no ground reading and no provenanced import, and show it discriminates a new culture's mind at first contact. That would break the shape-not-a-name limit and the non-neutrality claim together.

### F4: Standards of evidence: triangulation, not negotiation (triangulated)

Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :31-32, independence: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

Fig 7 :12-13, "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 7 :35, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36, "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

Fig 8 :45, "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :48-49, "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." Cross-cultural psychology supplies the actual one-pool fact: an analysis of top journals found "96% of subjects who participated in those studies came from Western Industrialized countries, with 68% of them coming from the United States", and "Findings from psychology research utilizing primarily W.E.I.R.D. populations are often labeled as universal theories and are inaccurately applied to other cultures."

So agreement among studies that share samples, measures, authors and categories is one witness spelled many ways. Genuine evidence requires frames whose closures differ, ranged separately against a ground they cannot move (Fig 2 :46, "Each frame is corrected by it separately, and what makes the correction sound is that neither frame can move it."). Convergent derivation at maximum independence is the strongest form: Fig 8 :50, "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

Refuter: show that two frames which reached the same conclusion by separate routes actually drew on the same evidence pool, or show that a shared-pool agreement nonetheless moved the ground estimate. The first dissolves the corroboration; the second breaks Fig 2 :40.

### F5: Effect descriptions travel; causes and borrowed words do not (triangulated)

Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Fig 9 :30-31, "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Fig 6 :30, "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

Decoding Tacit Knowledge: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the operational rule for a cross-cultural measure: state what would be observed if the claim were true, in terms a reader in another culture can check, rather than exporting the category name.

Importing a category between cultures is an import across frames and requires both gates. Fig 6 :12, "source gate: provenance"; Fig 6 :13, "shadow test: explains a gap"; Fig 6 :24, "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Fig 8 :51, "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." An emic term adopted into an etic instrument is an adopted import; it may be legitimate, but only the record separates it from independent convergence.

Refuter: exhibit a category transferred between cultures with neither provenance nor a gap it explains, and show it still improves prediction at the ground. That would remove the need for the source gate and shadow test.

### F6: No final room: a genuinely cross-cultural science stays open (triangulated)

Fig 7 :6, "the limit: no last room". Fig 7 :30, "every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives". Fig 13 :90, "One chain is one route through a space that has no last route." Fig 13 :118, "infinite effort inside a finite reach."

A universal science of mind that terminates in one culture's final account is a terminal room. The framework forbids it definitionally. What survives is a generator that keeps being corrected: a science whose standard of evidence includes the obligation to keep importing from closures that differ, and whose categories remain revisable. That is also why the framework itself cannot be the final answer. From the canonical figures README: "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then it stops at the limit of what instruments can distinguish.

Refuter: produce a closing frame with no outside left to import from that still improves its predictions. That would require both a bottom and a top, which the framework rules out.

### F7: The literal "what it would look like" is a declared gap, not a silence (gap)

No figure line names a statistical technique (measurement invariance, multilevel modelling, item response theory, multigroup confirmatory factor analysis), no figure line lists the universal concepts, and no figure line supplies a threshold for how many cultures or how much independence is enough. Fig 9 :32, "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134, "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". Fig 8 :39, "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

The framework returns the shape of a cross-cultural science, not its name. The concrete techniques are real and useful in the world, but their authority is the ground of psychometrics, not the figures. Measurement invariance is described in the world literature as "a statistical property of measurement that indicates that the same construct is being measured across some specified groups", with "Violations of measurement invariance may preclude meaningful interpretation of measurement data"; but the same source records a live dispute about it (see S4). A figure-grounded solution declares the gap and does not smuggle in a technique as if it were derived.

Status: `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind for the specific list, and shape-not-a-name for the universal concepts. Not `unreachable-in-principle`: an instrument or a provenanced import could close part of it.

### F8: World sources corroborate the shape; one live field conflict is kept as evidence (see section 6)

Low-overlap corroborators, each opened (full provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`):

- **Animal behaviour.** The "zone of latent solutions" hypothesis: "many behaviours can be individually reinvented, while social learning influences their frequency or distribution without transmitting the behavioural form itself." Predicts that an observed behaviour cannot name its source, which is the metamerism point of *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "it cannot comment on the source."
- **Metascience.** Registered reports are "peer-reviewed before data are collected or analyzed" and "manuscripts are not rejected on the basis of null or statistically non-significant results"; Scheel et al. found "approximately 96% of traditional psychology articles reported statistically significant results, whereas only 44% of hypotheses tested in Registered Reports yielded significant findings." Predicts that a pre-committed standard, not a result-selected one, is what keeps the channel to the ground.
- **Psychometrics.** Measurement invariance and its discontents (S4).
- **Anthropology.** Emic and etic, including Pike's rejection of a view from nowhere.
- **Cross-cultural and cultural psychology.** The pseudoetic failure, the WEIRD sampling fact, and the criticism that "serious shortcomings in the theoretical and methodological bases used impede, rather than help, the scientific search for universal principles in psychology."

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict at the level of shape. The measurement-invariance dispute is internal to the field and is recorded as evidence for the union reading, not used to flip a figure verdict.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the recommendation and before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: Fig 5 :65, "The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds.

1. **Union refuter.** A single lever removes the residual in method, concept and evidence conditions at once, with no remaining compound. (Breaks Fig 9 :54-57.)
2. **Ground refuter.** A frame is found that can move the thing it measures, and the resulting agreement still predicts the ground better than an independent frame. (Breaks Fig 2 :33-35, :40.)
3. **Instrument refuter.** An instrument built from a pure effect description, with no prior instrument returning anything, registers correctly in a new culture at first contact. (Breaks Fig 6 :32-35.)
4. **Neutrality refuter.** A universal concept list is derived from held atoms alone, with no provenanced import, and discriminates a new population at first contact. (Breaks Fig 13 :16/:26 and the non-neutrality claim.)
5. **Independence refuter.** Two frames shown to share an evidence pool still produce corroboration that moves the ground estimate. (Breaks Fig 2 :40 and Fig 8 :48.)
6. **Last-room refuter.** A closing frame with no outside left to import from improves its predictions. (Breaks Fig 7 :6, Fig 13 :90.)
7. **Instrument discriminator.** A source-discriminating instrument shows that two different local sources produce an identical reading, yet the reading still licenses the same universal claim. (Breaks the metamerism constraint from *The Hard Problem is About Source*.)

If any fires, the affected finding is retracted and the census is redone. Until one is run, none of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Unbundle before you search.** State the method, concept and evidence conditions separately, with a separate anchor and verdict for each (F1).
2. **Lead with the ground, not with agreement.** Show what would have to be true for a reading to be about the mind rather than the frame; ask what a failure would look like before showing any theory (F2, F4, Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
3. **Declare the primitives.** Record which categories are borrowed from which frame, with provenance and a shadow test, and keep a refusal available (F3, F5, Operators :58).
4. **Buy independence deliberately.** Prefer frames with different closures and separate experience over more of the same sample. Larger samples are a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60, "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.").
5. **Keep the gap open.** Do not present a statistical package as figure-derived. Name it as a world import with its own ground (F7).
6. **No final theory.** Offer a generator with a refuter and a lineage, not a closing account (F6). Fig 4 :25, "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the method and the refuter, not the conclusion.

### 6.2 Strains and re-census

- **S1, Fig 13 physical substrate applied to mind.** Fig 13 's six terms (space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient, network) are introduced as a physical substrate. Reading them as a psychology vocabulary strains. Re-census: the figures are domain-agnostic, and Fig 4 :19-20 says only actors, currency and anchor change with size. The strain is narrowed to a shape-level claim: any vocabulary must have states and structures in lockstep and must compose across kinds; which atoms a given culture holds is a binding, declared and provenanced. The strain is recorded, not hidden.
- **S2, Fig 2 :40 versus Fig 8 :50.** Agreement is disqualifying when frames share an evidence pool, yet convergence at maximum independence is corroboration. Not strained after re-census: the same independence parameter is read twice, once for verdict weight and once for union surplus. Agreement from a pool is one witness; convergence from separate derivation is two. Different objects, one quantity.
- **S3, Fig 11 :51 versus universal concepts.** Re-census in F3: Fig 11 governs the valence of actors relative to a residual, not the set of composable concept atoms. The narrower surviving claim is that a concept's standing is residual-relative, so past usefulness does not license universality.
- **S4, measurement invariance dispute in the field.** Welzel and colleagues "criticize the excessive reliance on invariance tests as criteria for the validity of cultural and psychological constructs", arguing that "the invariance criteria favor constructs with low between-group variance"; proponents reply that "the reliance on nomological linkage ignores that such external validation hinges on the assumption of comparability." This is a live conflict *inside* the world source, not a figure conflict. The conflict rule sends us back to the census: it corroborates F1 (the evidence standard is its own condition with its own ground) and F7 (no figure names a technique). It does not flip any figure verdict. Recorded as evidence, not resolved by assertion.

After re-census: one narrowed strain (S1) and one live world conflict (S4), both named; no hidden contradictions.

### 6.4 Updated verdict

The shape survives: unbundle the union, keep readings per frame, require a non-updating ground and real independence, grow instruments from existing ones, declare primitives with provenance and refusal, describe effects rather than causes, and keep the science open. What does not survive is any claim to a named technique, a universal concept list, a final theory, or corroboration from agreement among frames that share the WEIRD evidence pool. Strains after re-census: two, both named, none hidden. The whole folder remains `specified`/`general` with a declared `gap`, and is a simulated derivation, not a ground reading.
