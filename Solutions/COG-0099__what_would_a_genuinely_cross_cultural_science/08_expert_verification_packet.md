# Expert verification packet: the Tether on a cross-cultural science of mind

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cross-cultural methodologist, psychometrician or cultural-evolution researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `gap` on the specific techniques, not `grounded`.** No cross-cultural measurement is claimed. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A genuinely cross-cultural science of mind is a union of conditions, not one method with three facets. Its method grows instruments from tasks already returning something in each population and keeps residuals per person and per frame. Its concepts are declared choices with provenance and an available refusal, because no vocabulary is neutral. Its evidence is triangulation between frames with genuinely different closures against a ground none can move; agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not corroboration. It enumerates the habitat around each residual, describes effects rather than exporting borrowed causes, imports with a source gate and shadow test, and stays open because there is no last room. The figures return this shape and do not name the statistical package, the universal concept list or the number of cultures. That is a declared gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 9

- **Statement:** the residual must stay per frame and per slot, and the ground used to settle it must not update on what any frame thinks. Cross-cultural agreement from a shared pool cannot count as a verdict.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19, "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22, "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 2 :33, "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :35, "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40, "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 7 :35, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- **What to check:** are the five lines present as quoted? Does the per-slot rule actually forbid interpreting a culture mean as a statement about a person? Does the anchor rule actually forbid a measure whose reference moves when the researcher's frame changes?
- **Refuter:** show a frame that can move the thing it measures and still predict the ground better than an independent frame; or show that two frames known to share one evidence pool nevertheless produce corroboration that moves the ground estimate.

### C2: method-facing; checkable by a methodologist against measurement practice

- **Statement:** an instrument is extended from a worse instrument already returning something in the new population; it is not built from an effect description. Categories are declared with provenance, and imports carry a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :32, "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :23, "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :12, "source gate: provenance"; Fig 6 :13, "shadow test: explains a gap"; Fig 6 :24, "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." Fig 9 :31, "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- **What to check:** is there a real case where a measure was successfully designed from a pure description with no prior instrument returning anything in the target population? If yes, the first line is broken. Does the world literature distinguish an emic term adopted into an etic instrument (an adopted import) from the same term reached independently in two frames (convergent derivation)? Fig 8 :51 says "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".
- **Refuter:** exhibit a category transferred between cultures with neither provenance nor a gap it explains, and show it still improves prediction at the ground; or exhibit an instrument built from a pure description that registers correctly at first contact.

### C3: structural; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the question is a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition, and two cultural applications are isomers only over a fixed atom set with a different bond to the ground. As normally reported with different atom lists they are members of a functional group, not isomers.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54, "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55, "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 12 :117, "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :123, "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :94, "The arrangement is the substance."
- **What to check:** does one lever (a bigger, more diverse sample) leave the concept and evidence conditions binding? If yes, the union claim holds. Can the arrangement to the ground be recovered from an ingredient list of sample, task, scale and p value? If yes, the isomer claim falls to the functional-group reading.
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a single intervention that removes the residual in method, concept and evidence conditions at once; or show that two applications with identical atoms and different bonds to the ground behave identically.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that cross-cultural differences are mostly artifacts of measurement. That is the single-lever collapse the union refuter tests.
- Not that culture-fair instruments can be designed from first principles.
- Not that agreement between cultures is corroboration.
- Not that a universal concept list can be read off the framework. Naming an already-held atom is a provenanced binding, not a derivation.
- Not that measurement invariance, multilevel modelling or registered reports are figure-derived standards. They are world imports with their own grounds, and invariance testing has a live dispute inside its own field.
- Not that the answer is a final theory. Fig 7 :6 names "the limit: no last room".
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and it was "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument" that the case closed. Here:

1. Does a score difference come from the named construct, from response style, from comprehension of wording, from the testing situation, or from a source not yet enumerated?
2. Are the two frames drawing on different evidence, or are they one pool wearing two names?
3. Over a fixed atom set, do different bonds to the ground produce different behaviour?
4. Does any of the seven standing falsifiers in `01` section 4 fire?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, :22; Fig 2 :33, :35, :40; Fig 6 :12-13, :23, :32; Fig 7 :35; Fig 8 :51; Fig 9 :31, :54-55; Fig 12 :94, :117, :123. These are short passages.
2. Check C2 against one measurement you know well: what prior instrument was it extended from, and what is its provenance record when it moved between populations?
3. Name one source-discriminating instrument that would separate construct difference from response style and comprehension difference in that measure, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
