## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a system learning from feedback that is sparse, inconsistent, or biased. Option A: affirm a single yes with a reliability figure obtained by aggregating and noise-robust estimation. Option B: split the three conditions, re-ground "reliably" as five contact conditions, keep verdicts per domain, and report the numerical guarantee as a `gap`. What each leads to: A produces a frame-internal metric that the figures disqualify and spends the independence of the people asked to check it; B produces a checkable shape, five written refuters, and an open slot until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

`R` by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** `R_s = |Γ(b,a) − o'_s|` per slot. Arity: three conditions, sparse, inconsistent, biased, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57); a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: prediction to observation is ordered, and "reliably" is an undefined threshold the frame cannot check from inside (Fig 12 :87). Scale: steps 1-5 close within one agent; step 6 and Fig 7 add cross-scale and cross-room places for the channel to fail.
- **Domain 1, signal availability (sparse).** Actors: slots `s`, live mass `R⁺_s`, curiosity drive, any instrument already returning something. Currency: per-slot positive residual mass. Anchor: an instrument already returning a reading at some resolution (Fig 6 :34-35). Type: partition of slots into live and silent; union with D2 and D3.
- **Domain 2, conflicting readings (inconsistent).** Actors: frames A and B, hidden shared source, chain of rooms. Currency: agreement versus residual, which are different quantities. Anchor: a ground pinned outside both frames that does not update (Fig 2 :24-27). Type: union compound, split before search.
- **Domain 3, self-referential feedback (biased).** Actors: belief `b`, weighting `w`, private history, inherited prior, seat with a stake. Currency: valence of the observation relative to `R`, interest in the reading. Anchor: a ground that does not move when pushed (Fig 2 :29-30); **absent that, no nameable anchor yet**, so not a domain in which reliability can be assessed.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: outside frame, ground maintainer, expert reader. Currency: residuals and provenance, not agreement. Anchor: the ground, never votes (Fig 2 :45-46). In this simulated run all seats share one evidence pool, so agreement here is internal consistency, not corroboration.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); establish per-slot support before a mint (Fig 5 :29); refuse before mint (formula step 2); settle before composing (Fig 5 :69-70); promote methods across the membrane with a measured round trip (Fig 4 :30). The break is at the objective link (Fig 3 :25-27).

Strain test applied: feedback is a channel and the ground is what settles (Fig 2 :29-30; Fig 10 :24), so they are different objects and the apparent contradiction dissolves by re-census. The six-term physical substrate is not the loop's atom set; using Fig 12's isomer rule for the loop's symbols is an analogy, declared in `06`, not a claim that the loop's atoms are space, time, energy, spectrum, gradient and network.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question as posed cannot be answered with one word. The three conditions form a union with overlapping intersections and different anchors, so the verdict is per domain and never averaged. Sparse feedback is learnable when some slot carries live mass and the signal is not averaged away, and `unreachable` where no instrument registers at all. Inconsistent feedback is learnable only after splitting into well-formed conditions and ranging each separately against a ground that does not update; agreement from a shared evidence pool is the pool talking. Biased feedback is not learnable while the channel depends on the belief that produced the prediction, because the anchor has become a frame and the drift is coherent and invisible from inside. A numerical reliability guarantee is a declared `gap`, not a silence and not an invitation to invent a number. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, with `general` force in the structural separator.

- **cost if wrong:** the separable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. The corrected packet states its mode, five refuters and a declared gap, so an error is findable rather than invisible. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and never pooling later agreement as corroboration.
- **undoable:** yes. The three per-domain verdicts can be narrowed or withdrawn without touching the separator; `07` and `08` already state the non-claims. If a controlled run fires one of the five refuters, that sub-claim is withdrawn and the change is logged in `01` section 6. No primitive has to be withdrawn to do it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument showing live mass under a sparse channel and its loss under averaging would settle D1; a non-updating ground and two low-overlap frames would settle D2's split; a fixed-presentation control separating belief-dependent from belief-independent feedback would settle D3; a persisted non-trending residual with a refusal would settle S5; and a derived numerical bound would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, every finding remains `candidate`.
