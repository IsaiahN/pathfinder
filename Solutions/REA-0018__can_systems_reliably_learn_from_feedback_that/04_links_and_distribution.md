## 4. Distribution rules: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22-25:** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." and "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42:** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." This is a rule for how much checking effort to spend, not a reason to expect agreement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim S1-S5; (2) the five refuters; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) only if asked, the formula as a method.

## 5. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** A recipient who later agrees has now seen the same sources we did, so that agreement is one evidence pool talking. It is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** under Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator. Giving it away does not give away an answer.
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so each user writes a refuter before the run and labels the mode of the result.
