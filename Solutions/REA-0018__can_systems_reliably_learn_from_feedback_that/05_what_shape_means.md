## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." The same essay gives the bundle warning that governs this question directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For REA-0018 the shape is:

> degraded feedback is a channel property, not one noise dial; three conditions with different failure mechanisms; a non-updating anchor and separate frames are necessary for a verdict; a numerical "reliability" is not returned.

That is a shape. "System X is 94% reliable under label noise" is a name, and the figures do not license it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*, opened 2026-11-23: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also identifies the anchor of the search: "One false misstep without a tether can leave you stranded or descending into oblivion. This anchor is provided by your own question."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." Here the effect terms are "channel goes quiet", "two readings disagree without shrinking", and "the reading depends on what the frame already believes", not the jargon of any one noise model.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened 2026-11-23: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the first-stage outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Applied to this question: a specific noise-robust loss, annotator-reliability estimator, or reward shaping scheme is licensed only by a ground reading at a named slot, or by an import with provenance. Calling one "the reliable learner" from its own benchmark is the metamerism error in section 4.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried from the exemplar's mapping, not re-opened this session) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A declared noise model is a visible choice, not a discovery about the world.

## 4. Why a performance reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened 2026-11-23, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source", because one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The same structure appears in the world literature opened this session. Hosking et al. 2024 find that "the assertiveness of an output skews the perceived rate of factuality errors": a single preference score is a projection of several causes. Zhang et al. 2025 find that most annotator disagreement comes from task underspecification and response style, so the score conflates several valid readings. Ghafouri et al. 2026 state that "measurement validity is logically prior to preference aggregation": before deciding how to combine readings, ask what they are readings of.

So a claim like "the system learned reliably from biased feedback, because its reward went up" reads a projection (the reward) and names its source (reliable learning) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The metamerism point is **not** retracted and does not depend on the "shape, not a name" rule: it depends on the missing discriminator.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why this whole package is labelled a simulated derivation.

## 5. Q: What does "shape" license for sparse, inconsistent, biased feedback?

**Answer:** a four-part shape and one declared gap.

1. **The three adjectives are not one variable.** *Library of Babel*, opened this session: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." Fig 9 :54-57 gives the figure-level rule.
2. **The anchor cannot update on the frame.** Fig 2 :33-35, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "the reference point both sides check against has to stay put. If it moves, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."
3. **Agreement is not evidence when the frames share a pool.** Fig 2 :40; *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Shared pretext is good for one thing and ruins another. Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."
4. **Capability is contact, not the agent alone.** Fig 11 :39-40, and *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
5. **No numerical reliability is returned.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." This is a declared `gap`, not an invitation to invent a number.

## 6. Correction to my own first-pass reading

My first pass treated "sparse, inconsistent, or biased" as three settings of one noise parameter and went looking for the noise-robust method that wins across all three. That was wrong twice.

- Merging the three into one dial contradicts Fig 9 :54-57 (a union is not a harder instance of one condition) and the *Library of Babel* bundle warning quoted in section 1.
- Looking for the winning method asks the framework for a name where it returns a shape. What it can return is the separator S1-S5 and the refuters in `01`, not an algorithm.

Both readings are preserved here so the change is visible. The corrected form is: three conditions, per-domain verdicts, one `gap`, five refuters.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly", and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous practitioners selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
