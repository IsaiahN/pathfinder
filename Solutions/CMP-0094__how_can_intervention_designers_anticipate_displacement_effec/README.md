## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
