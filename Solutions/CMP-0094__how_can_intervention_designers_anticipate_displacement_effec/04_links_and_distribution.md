## Wider literature that bears on the question

Two things in the read sources matter directly.

1. **Segovia-Martin et al. 2023 models three sequential domains and shows the pressure moving downstream.** Their sentence "this merely displaces the unsustainable exploitation pattern to subsequent domains" is the same shape as Fig 11 :52, arrived at independently in a different field. They also state that "research on rebound effects tends to only examine within-domain or cross-domain effects, but not both", which is the same union-compound problem as Fig 9 :54-57: a single lever clears one condition and leaves the intersection standing. Use this as corroboration, not as a figure line.
2. **Carbon leakage gives a ratio, not a name.** "the increase in CO2 emissions outside the countries taking domestic mitigation action divided by the reduction in the emissions of these countries." That is a displacement measured as a relation between slots. It supports the per-slot claim (F1) without supplying a universal checklist, and it is a binding from environmental economics, not a derivation.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Check the output against the ground. **(2)** Only if curious, read the theory that produced it. **(3)** Use the formula recursively on other problems, writing a fresh refuter each time.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-one verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you.").
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted** (Fig 4 :22). But each user still needs a ground: a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds" (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction).
- **Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-one output survives. The pre-registered refuter in `01` and `02` is what makes that checkable.
