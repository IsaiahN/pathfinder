## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit is that the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "Your search space will narrow with every vocabulary word that you believe is in the answer or associated with the answer."

So for CMP-0094 a **shape** is the narrowed form of an anticipation method that can be stated before any specific displacement is measured. It constrains: keep R per slot, enumerate outward, re-derive valence, use a non-updating anchor, report unreached. It does not fill: it does not say "the displacement will be X at slot Y", and it does not return a universal checklist. "Keep R per slot" is a shape. "Risk compensation will move harm to pedestrians" is a name from another frame.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recursively. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also names the bundle case: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is Fig 9 :54-57's union, and it is exactly the shape of the displacement question: local reduction plus cascade plus measurement.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, opened this session: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach packet asks what the reader would expect to see at receiving slots before showing the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." And the paradox: "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with source gate and shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar mapping, not reopened here) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

Applied here: naming "risk compensation" or "carbon leakage" as the displacement mechanism in a given intervention is a binding from a world frame. It is allowed once provenanced and once it passes the shadow test (it explains a gap the design frame left). It is not allowed as a figure-derived name. My first pass did the opposite and that error is preserved in `01` section 8.

## 4. Why "no side effects found" is a metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several distinct sources can produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was "the arrival of a better instrument", not an argument, that resolved the case. And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied here: a net risk score that falls after intervention is a reading, not a source. It cannot comment on whether the risk was removed or relocated. Several arrangements of contact produce the same aggregate fall. So "we measured a reduction and found no side effects" is exactly the projection error: a single reading treated as if it discriminated among sources. What is owed is an instrument at receiving slots, not a stronger statement of success.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) adds: outputs are not generators, so a catalogue of past displacement cases is not a method for anticipating the next one.

## 5. The five-part shape, stated as an ordinary term

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", the sought-for shape must be written down, bound, and allowed to accrue a residual like any other term. Written here:

**S = keep R per slot; enumerate habitat outward until cascade stops; valence relative to residual; anchor at receiving slots that does not update on the target report; unreached reported as a verdict.**

- S is a shape: it constrains any anticipation method without naming a slot or a magnitude.
- S is refusable by the six pre-registered conditions in `01` section 4.
- S is not yet settled: no receiving-slot ground reading was taken in this derivation. Mode `specified` on the structure, `general` on the method, candidate.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly". "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Also: "I separated Prestige from Action Budget" with a "PrestigeFirewall" that "enforced the separation at runtime", because otherwise "the highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."

For intervention work, a list of well-cited displacement case studies can fund the attempt to check; it cannot settle whether this intervention displaced risk. And a panel chosen for its agreement "looks like agreement, and agreement feels like truth" while the ground is never consulted. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the method against receiving-slot readings.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay, opened this session: "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong."

Therefore `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the displacement question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Correction to my own §3/§4

I initially applied "shape, not a name" to every mechanism name, which overstates it. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Γ. "Risk compensation", "carbon leakage" and "rebound" are not missing primitives; they are bindings from world frames onto contacts already enumerable in the habitat. Naming them is therefore provenance work, not the invention of a seventh term and not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."). Both readings are kept: the correction narrows the ban, and §4's metamerism point stands because what is missing is the source-discriminating instrument, not a name.
