# The Tether's answer: can a system evolve before it has genes?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a dependency order, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No experiment was run.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three conditions: does differential persistence accumulate adaptations without a modern gene, what carries the continuity, and what do we choose to call "evolution" and "genes in the modern sense"? Fig 9 :55: "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." A single yes or no would average three different verdicts, so they are kept separate below.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**Domain G, the process. Yes, in the structural sense.** Selection is unmaintained ground contact. Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." The two factors are given in Fig 7 :33, "variation inside, selection between", and Fig 7 :35, "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A gene is not one of the factors. `general`, triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 7.

**Domain H, the carrier. The gene is later, not the floor.** Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not, and the level it lands on remakes it." Fig 4 :34-35: "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." Fig 13 :142: "The seed was always the seedline." A modern gene is the compressed record of a lineage that already met its ground, a generator that crosses to the next level. Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it." The bond to the earlier process is production (`⇒`), not identity (`≡`, see `06` Q2). `specified` on the mapping of molecular vocabulary onto these figure roles, triangulated.

**Domain H, second verdict. Adding genes adds access, not a world atom.** Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." Fig 6 :26: "The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access." The molecules, cores and compartments were already there; the gene changes what a frame can carry. `general`, single-source on the "no atom" phrasing.

**Domain D, the definition. Gap.** No figure line selects which definition of "evolution" or "gene" wins. The Major Evolutionary Transitions objection, read in Schoenmakers et al. 2024, says prebiotic chemistry would not be "evolution proper" without unlimited hereditary replicators. That is a convention dispute about the word, not a refutation of the process verdict. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,". This is a `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not underivable in principle by an experiment. Unanswered here, not silent.

**Operational discriminator.** A reaction that merely repeats has neither factor. Fig 7 :37: "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:". Catalysts (Fig 12 :119, "catalyst. A part that survives its own use.") and cycles (Fig 12 :121, "cycle. The output re-enters as input.") supply continuity and closure, not selection. `specified`, no assay claimed.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a date, a setting, or a named first carrier. No claim about RNA-first, metabolism-first, hydrothermal vents, or 3.8 billion years ago.
- Not that all chemistry is alive, or that repetition is evolution.
- Not that genes are irrelevant. They are a major downstream arrival in hereditary fidelity; the claim is only that they are not a precondition for selection.
- Not that the Major Evolutionary Transitions account is wrong. Its word-level objection is recorded in Domain D and left open.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an instrument, an experiment, or a historical dataset.
- Not a derivation of molecular biology from the six-term set. The binding of "core", "compartment" and "gene" onto figure vocabulary is provenance-marked, as required by Fig 8 :50-52.

## 4. What would settle it, and the pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion (full set in `01` section 4):

1. a demonstration that differential accumulation of adaptations requires template-dependent replication;
2. a repetition-only system that nonetheless accumulates adaptations;
3. a derivation making gene-level heredity a primitive on which selection depends;
4. a hereditary template adding a world primitive rather than frame access;
5. a ground reading showing every evolving chemical system required a modern gene.

The ground in this field is an experiment or a decisive historical dataset, not a definition and not agreement among researchers. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The origins-of-life sources read here share one evidence pool, so their agreement is one witness; the conflict inside that pool (the MET objection) is more informative than the agreement.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> Yes, in the structural sense. The figures treat selection as what happens when nothing maintains the ground: variation inside, selection between, and the ground votes. No gene appears in that account. A modern gene is a later arrival, the compressed record of a lineage that already met its ground, a generator that crosses to the next level and gives a frame access rather than adding a new thing to the world. So the dependency order runs selection first, gene downstream, and the carrier that did the work first stays open. We do not name RNA or metabolism, we do not date the transition, and we do not let the phrase "in the modern sense" settle a definition dispute by convention. Repetition is not evolution; the discriminator is variation plus differential persistence. The answer offered is a shape plus five refuters written in advance. An experiment, not agreement, would close the carrier slot.
