## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From the Library of Babel essay (opened):

> "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is:

> differential contact decides what persists; variation inside and selection between are the two factors; a carrier is required but the modern gene is a later record, not a precondition.

That is a shape. "It was RNA, 3.9 billion years ago, in a alkaline hydrothermal vent" is a name plus a date plus a setting, none of which the figures supply.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- The essay also names the failure this question is prone to: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is the union of process, substrate and definition in `01` section 0.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Fig 9 :32 again: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The kind here is "a hereditary record composed over an already-running selection process."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that would mean an experiment or a decisive historical dataset, not an argument about definitions.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming RNA or a metabolism-first network would be such an import. `01` F6 declines it because the residual was not described before the frame was chosen, and no source gate or shadow test was supplied.

The Library of Babel essay adds a stopping rule: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here we are not done: the carrier slot is still open.

## 4. Selection is an instance, not the whole loop

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay (opened) makes the point that matters most for this question:

> "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground."

> "Natural selection is not the whole answer. It is a special case. The full answer is the loop itself."

And `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md` says the figures are "metalaws that predate evolution". So in the framework's own account the loop is more general than biological evolution, and selection is the unmaintained case of it. A gene cannot be a precondition for a case of the loop that is defined by having no maintainer; the gene belongs to a later, record-carrying arrangement. This is Figure 11 :36 in prose: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."

## 5. Q: what licenses the *absence* of a modern gene in the shape?

**Answer: the figure's own account of selection, not an inference from chemistry.**

Fig 11 :36 does not mention genes, intelligence, or a maintainer. Fig 7 :33 gives two factors, "variation inside, selection between", and a gene is not one of them. Fig 7 :37: "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:". A shape that included a modern gene as a necessary factor would have to add a third factor that the figure does not have. So the absence is a constraint from the figure, not a gap in the biology.

**Does the "shape, not a name" rule forbid naming a core, a network or a gene?** No, and this is the correction to keep visible. The figures README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Cores, networks and genes are bindings onto held vocabulary or imports with provenance. What remains forbidden is claiming that naming one of them moves the wall: Fig 6 :25, "An import adds no atom to the world." I initially overstated this rule in the earlier folder style; the narrowed form is recorded here and in `06` rather than silently substituted.

## 6. The label is applied afterwards

The outcome-bias literature (Wikipedia page opened 2026-11-23) reports that "the outcome bias is an error made in evaluating the quality of a decision when the outcome of that decision is already known." This is a low-overlap corroboration of Fig 11 :36: the word "intelligence", and by extension the word "advanced" or "evolvable", is attached after the fact to whatever met its ground. Applied to this question, "it had genes, therefore it was evolving" reverses the order. The lineage met its ground first; the gene is what the lineage came to carry.

Capability is likewise not a property of the carrier alone. Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." A gene in a habitat with no differential pressure does nothing; a core in a habitat with variation and selection is already doing the work.

## 7. Why this derivation cannot settle the question

From the same essay:

> "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

> "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."

This document, and every other file in this folder, is a **simulated derivation** checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle whether a particular chemical system on early Earth actually evolved before genes. That is a job for an instrument, an experiment or a decisive dataset. Agreement among these files is one evidence pool, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
