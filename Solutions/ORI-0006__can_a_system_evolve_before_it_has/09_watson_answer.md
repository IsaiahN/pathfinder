## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether differential persistence can accumulate adaptations before a modern gene exists. Option A: the gene is a precondition; before templates there is chemistry, repetition or drift, and calling it evolution is a category mistake. Option B: selection is unmaintained ground contact with two factors, variation inside and selection between; the gene is a later hereditary record composed over that process. What each leads to: A draws a hard boundary at the gene and makes the definition do the work of an anchor; B gives a dependency order with the gene downstream, an open carrier slot, and a real refuter. The question bundles a definition dispute, so verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Yes for the process, with the gene downstream and the carrier open. Selection is ground contact that runs before any modern gene; its factors are variation inside and selection between, and a modern gene is a later hereditary record, a generator that crosses levels and adds access rather than a world atom. Repetition without variation and differential persistence is not evolution. The definition of "evolution" is a gap, not a verdict. This is `general` on structure, `specified` per domain, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single figure line is Fig 11 :36; the strongest corroboration from outside the framework is Vasas et al. 2012, and the most informative item in the world pool is the MET conflict inside Schoenmakers et al. 2024.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. Each verdict names its own refuter, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and R1 to R5.
- **undoable:** yes. The carrier claim is open rather than asserted, the definition dispute is recorded as a gap, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet if a refuter fires.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment showing that accumulation of adaptations requires template replication, a repetition-only system that nonetheless accumulates adaptations, or a historical dataset showing every evolving lineage already had a modern gene would fire the refuters and reverse the process verdict. A definition convention alone cannot do it, because a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.
