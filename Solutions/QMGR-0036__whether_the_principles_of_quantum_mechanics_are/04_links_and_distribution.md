## Wider literature that bears on this question

These are the world sources used in `09_watson_answer.md` and recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They are outside the framework evidence pool, so they are genuine secondary corroboration, not an echo.

**Uniqueness side (within-closure reconstruction results).**

- Lucien Hardy, "Quantum Theory From Five Reasonable Axioms" (2001), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0101012. Abstract: quantum theory "can be derived from five very reasonable axioms"; dropping Axiom 5, or just the word "continuous", yields classical probability theory. This is the clearest demonstration that "the principles" are a bundle and that uniqueness is conditional on the axioms held.
- Maria Pia Soler's theorem, reported by Samuel S. Holland, "Orthomodularity in infinite dimensions; a theorem of M. Soler" (1995), https://www.ams.org/bull/1995-32-02/S0273-0979-1995-00593-8. "an orthomodular form that has an infinite orthonormal sequence is real, complex, or quaternionic Hilbert space." A mathematical uniqueness result from a different field, corroborating set-level closure at its own level.
- Peter Janotta and Haye Hinrichsen, "Generalized Probability Theories: What determines the structure of quantum theory?" (2014), https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6562. "the aim is to understand what distinguishes quantum mechanics as the fundamental theory realized in nature." Corroborates F6: the ground question is open.

**Modification side (imports and refusals).**

- Steven Weinberg, "Testing Quantum Mechanics" (1989), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0003491689902765. "a general framework for introducing nonlinear corrections into ordinary quantum mechanics, that can serve as a guide to experiments." A refusal of linearity plus a proposed ground test.
- Joseph Polchinski, "Weinberg's nonlinear quantum mechanics and the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paradox" (1991), https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.397. Nonlinear QM "leads either to communication via Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen correlations, or to communications between branches of the wave function." Corroborates that a modification carries a price and is not free.
- Angelo Bassi and GianCarlo Ghirardi, "Dynamical Reduction Models" (2003), https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0302164. "nonlinear and stochastic modifications of the Schroedinger equation" with "experimental implications ... which should allow, in principle, to test it against quantum mechanics." A concrete modification with a ground still outstanding.
- Achim Kempf, Gianpiero Mangano and Robert B. Mann, "Hilbert space representation of the minimal length uncertainty relation" (1995), https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9412167. "The existence of a minimal observable length has long been suggested, in quantum gravity, as well as in string theory." This is the direct "extreme scales" case, and it is still a theoretical proposal.

**Ground side and humanities cross-check.**

- Nobel Prize in Physics 2022 press release, https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2022/press-release. "for experiments with entangled photons, establishing the violation of Bell inequalities and pioneering quantum information science." Shows what a ground test can settle, and that it is regime-limited.
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, "Philosophical Issues in Quantum Theory" (2016, revised 2022), https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/qt-issues/. "Hidden-variables theories and collapse theories, involve formulation of physical theories distinct from standard quantum mechanics." A low-overlap humanities source corroborating that modifications are distinct theories, not rearrangements.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the ground. Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three framework links. Their agreement after seeing the theory is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. The refuters in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` are written so a failure can be reported in one line.
