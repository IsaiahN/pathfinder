# The Tether's answer: unique principles or modifiable at extreme scales?

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The raw question bundles two conditions with different grounds: is the rule set that fixes QM rigid (C1), and could it change at extreme scales (C2)? Fig 9 :4-5 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." The bundle must be split before it is searched. The answer below is a shape with an open slot, not a physics verdict.

## 2. The shape

1. **Uniqueness is true of the set and false of the arrangements.** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :116-118 "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh" So neither "uniquely determined" nor "modifiable" is correct as a single verdict. The set-level structure is closed; the laws composed from it are not forced. This is the central answer.
2. **A genuine modification is a refusal or an import, never a recombination.** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Nonlinear QM, collapse models, GPTs and a minimal length are all imports or refusals from frames whose closure differs, not rearrangements of the standard axioms.
3. **Scale is a membrane, and the kernel does not change with it.** Fig 4 :19-20 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size" The question "could principles change at extreme scales" is only well formed once it says which part is kernel (scale-invariant) and which is cast (regime-dependent). The membrane is a filter with a direction, not a licence to rewrite the rules (Fig 4 :21-23).
4. **Irreducibility is level-relative.** Fig 13 :104-105 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world." A principle that is uniquely determined at accessible scales can be a derivative that breaks down at another level. "Unique at this level" and "modifiable across levels" are compatible, so the raw binary is a false dichotomy.
5. **Reconstruction theorems are filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Hardy, Soler and the GPT programme establish conditional, within-closure uniqueness. They do not establish physical necessity, and "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence" (Fig 8 :39).
6. **The physical question is unreached.** Fig 6 :34-35 "the question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." No instrument at an extreme scale currently returns a failing reading. The honest status is `candidate`, `specified`, unreached at this budget (Fig 8 :40), not `grounded` and not `unreachable in principle`.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the six-term substrate is the axioms of QM. That is a category error; D1 and D2 are different domains (see `01` F3).
- Not that QM is uniquely determined, and not that it will be modified at the Planck scale. Both are names where the framework gives a shape.
- Not that Hardy's or Soler's theorems prove nature must be quantum. They are conditional results inside a closure.
- Not that any particular modification (nonlinearity, collapse, minimal length) is realized. Those are provenanced imports with their own grounds outstanding.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at an extreme scale settles `left(R,φ)`.
- Not a theory of everything. The center stays a compound with its own loop, and there is no last room (Fig 7 :6, Fig 4 :33).

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading cannot comment on its own source, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here:

- **Extreme-scale discriminator:** an instrument at the Planck or strong-field scale that returns a deviation from a standard QM principle, or that resolves the principle holding where it was expected to fail. This settles C2 at the ground.
- **Set-level falsifier:** derive a seventh term from the six, or show the arrangements over them are finite.
- **Operator falsifier:** exhibit a within-closure recombination that adds a genuinely new principle with no new atom and no refusal.
- **Scale falsifier:** show the kernel itself changes with size with the ground paying for it.
- **Level falsifier:** show irreducibility is absolute at some level.

The five refuters are pre-registered in `01` section 5 and restated in `08`. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, and never settled by agreement.** Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books*); declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. Another sensorium's minimum set need not be these six. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "unique or modifiable" with a single verdict, because the question bundles two different questions. It says that the set of primitives is closed, no seventh member can be derived from the six, but the arrangements over them never run out; so uniqueness is true of the set and false of the laws composed from it. It says that a genuine modification of a principle cannot be a recombination of existing ones, it must be a refusal of a held term or an import from a frame whose closure differs. It says that scale is a membrane: the kernel, the loop and the naming rule, does not change with size, only the cast of actors, currency and anchor does; and that what is irreducible at one level can be a derivative at another, so "unique here" and "modifiable there" are compatible. Reconstruction theorems are conditional filters, not witnesses, and no instrument at an extreme scale currently returns a failing reading, so the physical question is unreached at this budget. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it. The open slot, which axiom if any nature changes, stays open until the ground settles it.
