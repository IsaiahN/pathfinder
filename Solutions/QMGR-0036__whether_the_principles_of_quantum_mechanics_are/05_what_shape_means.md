## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", and :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: split set-level closure from arrangement-level openness; treat a modification as a refusal or an import, not a recombination; separate kernel from cast across scale; and leave the ground question open. That is a shape. "Quantum mechanics is uniquely determined" and "quantum mechanics will be modified at the Planck scale" are both names where the framework can only give a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The stopping rule: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual in `01` is stated in effect terms (uniqueness of a closure, change of a rule with size), not in the vocabulary of any one interpretation of QM.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is the outreach rule in `02`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The operational stop is indiscernibility, not identity: "the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity" from *How Intelligence Actually Works*.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question the instrument would have to return a deviation at an extreme scale (Fig 6 :34-35).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming Soler's theorem or a minimal-length model as "the" answer to the uniqueness question is an import and must be recorded as such.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

The six are a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. That is exactly why the solution refuses to identify them with the axioms of QM.

## 4. Why this question is a source-discrimination problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source" one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a reconstruction theorem reads the same formal structure whether that structure is uniquely forced by nature or merely the only arrangement reachable from the chosen axioms. The reading cannot comment on the source. "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :50-52). What is owed is a discriminating ground or an honest provenance record, not a verdict.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A reconstruction programme cannot certify that its own axioms are the only possible ones.

## 5. What this question's shape is, stated positively

The shape delivered by `01` and `07` is four constraints plus one open slot:

1. **A split constraint.** Set-level closure and arrangement-level openness are different quantities. Any answer that gives one verdict for both is malformed (Fig 13 :115-118).
2. **An operator constraint.** A modification is a negation of a held term or an import from a different closure, not a recombination. The seven bonds cannot express it (Fig 5 :53, Operators :58).
3. **A scale constraint.** The kernel does not change with size; only cast does. Irreducibility is level-relative (Fig 4 :19-20, Fig 13 :104-105).
4. **A ground constraint.** The physical question is unreached at this budget. Absence of a detected modification is not proof of uniqueness (Fig 8 :39, Fig 6 :34-35).
5. **An open slot.** *Which* axiom, if any, nature changes at an extreme scale is not named by the framework. It is the `???` in `SPACE(???)TIME` written large over the axiom set.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any term in an answer. That is too strong. The canonical README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". It governs a primitive not held in Γ (Fig 13 :135). Held terms can be named; what matters is their provenance. So naming linearity, unitarity or the Born rule is not forbidden. What is forbidden is presenting a binding from the physics frame as a derivation from the framework, or claiming the framework names which axiom nature changes. The correction is recorded here rather than silently applied.

My §4 source-discrimination point is **not** retracted. The reconstruction still cannot comment on whether its closure is nature's, and the provenance record is what separates convergence from import. Both claims are now recorded.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly. ... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that QM is unique, or that it must be modified, is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be checked, and let the ground, an instrument or an experiment, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. ... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
