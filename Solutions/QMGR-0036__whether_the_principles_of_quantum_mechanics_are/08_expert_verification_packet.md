# Expert verification packet - the Tether on quantum axioms

**What this is:** a claim in a form a quantum foundations researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No measurement at an extreme scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "are the principles of quantum mechanics uniquely determined or modifiable at extreme scales" bundles two conditions with different grounds, and the figures separate them. The primitive set is closed, no seventh member is derivable, but the arrangements over it are unbounded: uniqueness is true of the set and false of the laws composed from it. A genuine modification of a principle is a negation of a held term or an import from a frame whose closure differs, never a recombination. Scale is a membrane whose kernel does not change with size, only the cast of actors, currency and anchor, and irreducibility is a fact about a level. Reconstruction theorems are conditional filters, not witnesses, and the physical question of whether nature departs from QM at an extreme scale is unreached at this budget. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** the primitive set is closed, no seventh member is derivable, but the arrangements over the primitives are not closed. A principle is irreducible only at a level.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :115 "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 13 :116-118 "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held. Meanwhile the arrangements over those six do not run out. So a frame can search without end and still not arrive: infinite effort inside a finite reach." Fig 13 :104-105 "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."
- **Refuter:** derive a seventh term from the six; show the arrangements are finite and exhaustible; or show irreducibility is absolute at some level.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against the reconstruction and modification literature

- **Statement:** a genuine modification of a QM principle is a negation of a held term or an import from a frame whose closure differs, not a recombination of the standard axioms. Reconstruction results are conditional, within-closure uniqueness, and do not settle physical necessity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 5 :53 "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so." Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **What to check:** is the characterization faithful? Hardy (2001) shows that dropping the continuity axiom gives classical probability theory, so the "principles" are a bundle and the uniqueness is conditional. Nonlinear QM (Weinberg 1989), collapse models (Bassi and Ghirardi 2003) and a minimal length (Kempf, Mangano and Mann 1995) each change an axiom or add a new assumption, which is an import or a refusal, not a recombination.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a within-closure recombination of the standard axioms that adds a genuinely new principle with no new atom and no refusal; or produce a reconstruction that derives QM with no conditional axioms and is settled at the ground.

### C3 - scale and ground; checkable against experimental reach

- **Statement:** the kernel does not change with size, only cast does, and the physical modification question is unreached at this budget. Absence of a detected deviation is not proof of uniqueness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :19-20 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 6 :34-35 "the question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** can a reader name an instrument at an extreme scale that is already returning something that fails to resolve? If not, the claim of unreached status stands. If yes, the physical question can be closed at the ground.
- **Refuter:** an instrument at an extreme scale that returns a deviation from, or a confirmation of, a standard QM principle; or a demonstration that the kernel changes with size with the ground paying for it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the six-term substrate is the axioms of quantum mechanics. That is a category error between two domains.
- Not that QM is uniquely determined, and not that it is modified at the Planck scale. Both are names where only a shape is given.
- Not a derivation of one theory from another, and no new primitive.
- Not that Hardy's, Soler's or any GPT reconstruction proves nature must be quantum. They are conditional, within-closure results.
- Not that any particular modification is realized in nature.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a theory of everything, and no last room.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading "cannot comment on the source" and the spectrometer resolved the case. Here:

1. An extreme-scale discriminator returning a deviation from, or a confirmation of, a standard QM principle (settles C2 at the ground).
2. A seventh term derived from the six, or finite arrangements over them (settles C1 set-level closure).
3. A within-closure recombination adding a new principle (settles C2 operator claim).
4. The kernel changing with size with the ground paying (settles C3 scale claim).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :115-118, Fig 13 :104-105, Fig 4 :19-20 and Fig 9 :60, four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the reconstruction and modification literature: is a proposed modification really an import or a refusal, and is the uniqueness really conditional?
3. Name one instrument at an extreme scale that is already returning a failing reading, or say why none exists.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
