**Question:** Whether the principles of quantum mechanics are uniquely determined or could be modified at extreme scales

**Source:** `Questions/QM & GR Questions.txt` line 46

**Section:** Quantum Foundations and Measurement

**Mode:** `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. No experiment at an extreme scale settles the binding, so nothing here is `grounded`.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Field for public contact points:** quantum axioms research (foundations of physics, axiomatic and reconstruction approaches, general probabilistic theories, tests of quantum linearity).

**Restatement in effect terms (Direction).** The question asks whether the rule set that fixes quantum mechanics is rigid, so that any change gives inconsistency or a non-quantum theory, or contingent, so that a different arrangement could hold, in particular at scales where present instruments stop resolving. What an answer would look like: a shape that says which part of a theory is fixed by closure and which part can vary with scale, plus a stated ground that would decide. That shape is bound here as an ordinary term and tested against the figures with a pre-registered refuter.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding expert readers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that makes their agreement worthless in Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The outside frame must not hold the interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All channels in `03` are public or opt-in.
