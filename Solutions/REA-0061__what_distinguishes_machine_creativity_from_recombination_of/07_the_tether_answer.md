# The Tether's answer: what distinguishes machine creativity from recombination

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a measurement. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a distinction that survives checking. Both obvious answers fail. Collapsing creativity into recombination leaves no evaluator, because a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27). Asserting a difference in kind requires naming an ingredient the framework says it cannot name (Fig 13 :135). The residual is the gap between the demand and those two failures. R is indexed per output slot (Fig 1 :19), not a global property of a model.

## 2. The shape, per domain, kept separate

**D1, the generative frame.** Recombination is a mint inside `closure(Γ)`: Fig 6 :21 **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Novelty there means *"novel relative to atoms, not to the world"* (Fig 6 :22). So on the machine's own terms there is no defect and no distinction to be drawn: generation from training patterns is the licensed, ordinary operation. Boden's combinatorial and exploratory creativity (SEP) corroborate this from outside the framework. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 6 plus Fig 5's novelty guard plus SEP), low overlap between the figure text and SEP.

**D2, the crossing.** The only operator that expands reach is import, which **"moves the wall"** (Fig 6 :14) and requires *"contact with a frame whose closure differs"* (Fig 6 :23). It is gated by provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :10-13). Crucially **"An import adds no atom to the world"** (Fig 6 :25): what changes is the frame's access, not the world's primitives. So "genuinely new" cannot mean a new world-object; it means a new reachable composition, recorded as a crossing. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated across Fig 6 and Fig 13 :115, with the SEP transformational-creativity analogue as secondary corroboration.

**D3, the evaluation frame.** The artifact cannot discriminate: Fig 8 :50-52 **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."** The label is downstream: Fig 11 :36 **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."** The anchor must not update (Fig 2 :33), and because creativity is constitutively a question about value, *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people"* (Fig 2 :36). Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the figure rule, with Csikszentmihalyi's field-based account (Wikipedia Computational creativity) as independent corroboration.

**Across the domains.** The question is a union, not a partition (Fig 9 :54): mechanism, provenance, and anchor overlap and cannot be averaged. The artifact is the same term under two lineages; over the agent-and-habitat the two descriptions are isomers with a stated falsifier (`06` Q2). The distinction being asked for is charged, not symmetric, and the load-bearing operator is import, which is not one of the seven bonds.

## 3. The answer in one sentence

**Nothing in the generated artifact distinguishes machine creativity from recombination of training patterns; what distinguishes them is a documented crossing into a differing closure plus a settlement by an anchor that does not update on the generator's own output, reported per output slot rather than as a global score.** `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that recombination is inferior. Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* A new bond over old atoms is a new substance (isomer, Fig 12 :117).
- Not that a benchmark, a model's own confidence, or a rater pool certifies creativity. Fig 1 :27 and Fig 2 :40 disqualify self-scoring and pooled agreement.
- Not that there is a special spark or non-computable ingredient. Fig 13 :135 forbids naming an unheld primitive.
- Not that "novelty relative to human knowledge" is a limitation to remove. Fig 2 :36 makes it the constitutive anchor.
- Not that this is `grounded`. No experiment, field study, or benchmark was run for this package.
- Not a definition of creativity. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

A discriminator and a ground, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Route discriminator.** An artifact-only test that separates convergent derivation from adopted import with a published error rate, at the ground. If it works, F3 narrows; the distinction moves partly into the artifact.
2. **Anchor rigidity test.** Push on the field's judgment with the generator's own output and show it does not move (Fig 2 :33). If it moves, the verdict is coherent drift, not settlement.
3. **Reach test.** Measure whether reach expands only on contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23), with provenance and a shadow test. If reach expands under mint alone, the shape breaks.
4. **Agent-only intervention.** Hold contact fixed and improve the agent; if the field's settlement changes, Fig 11 :40 is refuted.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** a composition that adds an atom inside a fixed closure; an anchor that updates and still settles; two same-atom different-bond outputs that are behaviourally indiscernible at every instrument.

**Honest stop: unreached, not unreachable.** The question asks for a kind of thing, and the figures return a shape. The stop is at the limit of available instruments, and the lineage is recorded so it is checkable. *Indiscernibility is not identity.* Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** A `gap` is not claimed here: after a genuine census, figure lines govern every condition. What is absent is a ground reading, which would move the verdict from `candidate` to `grounded`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that machine creativity is a special ingredient, and it does not say that it is nothing but pattern recombination. It says those two descriptions can be the same artifact, so the distinction cannot live in the text, image, or idea alone. Recombination is composition inside a fixed library, where novelty is relative to that library's own atoms. The only thing that expands the library is contact with a source whose library differs, and that crossing has to be documented by where the new item came from and by the gap it explains. The word "creative" is then applied afterwards by a field whose judgment does not move when the machine's own output changes, and it is applied per case, not as a global score. What the framework offers is the shape any adequate answer must have, five falsifiers written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The verdict stays `candidate` until a ground settles it.
