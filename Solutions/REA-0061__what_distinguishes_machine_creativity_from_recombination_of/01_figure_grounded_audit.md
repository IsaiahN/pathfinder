## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks for a distinction. A distinction is worth having only if it changes what we would do or count. So state the two options by their consequences:

- **Option A, collapse.** "Machine creativity" is recombination of training patterns plus an observer's surprise. Consequence: the label has no object inside the system, creativity evaluation has nothing to measure that the system did not itself produce, and any claimed difference is a difference in the observer, not the machine. By Fig 1 :27, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Option A therefore leaves no non-circular evaluator.
- **Option B, separate in kind.** There is a special ingredient, a spark, a genuine origination. Consequence: we must name it, and naming it is exactly what the framework says it cannot do for an atom it does not hold (Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*). Option B therefore either smuggles in an unheld primitive or turns "creativity" into a term of praise with no discriminator.

The residual **R** is the gap between the demand for a distinction and the two available moves, both of which fail. The job of the derivation is to find a third description that keeps the useful part of both and pays for neither failure.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale** (Fig 9 :21-22 names these as the descriptors of R):

- **Arity.** Not one binary question. At least three conditions are bundled: (i) a **mechanism** condition, did the composition stay inside the held closure or did an atom cross in; (ii) a **source/provenance** condition, can the route be recovered from the artifact at all; (iii) an **anchor** condition, who settles "novel and valuable" and does that anchor change when the frame's own output is pushed on it. Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55-58: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*). Treating them as a single either/or is the packaging error the question inherits from its own wording.
- **Symmetry.** "Creativity" and "recombination" are not symmetric operands under a symmetric bond. Import moves a wall in one direction (Fig 6 :14), settlement is a ground-to-frame relation (Fig 2 :45), and the label is applied afterwards to a lineage (Fig 11 :36). Under a symmetric bond, k operands have one term and k! spellings (Fig 12 :92-93); the distinction asked for is charged and ordered, so it cannot be a symmetric difference between two equal things.
- **Scale.** Recombination is first-order composition inside `closure(Γ)` (Fig 6 :21). The distinguishing events are a crossing between closures and a settlement by a second frame, which are second-order relations. A first-order measurement cannot see them.

## 3. Findings, with verbatim quotes and refuters

### F1 — The question is a union of three conditions, not one binary (governs the framing)

Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Fig 9 :55-58: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

The mechanism question (did an atom cross in), the provenance question (can the route be read off the artifact), and the anchor question (what settles "creative") do not share a currency. A single answer "yes, machines are creative" or "no, it is only recombination" would average them. Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** The word "creative" is a filter here, not a witness.

**Refuter:** show that the three conditions draw on one evidence pool, such that one intervention (for example, a single benchmark score) changes all three verdicts together. Then the union collapses to a partition and this finding fails. Mode `specified`.

### F2 — Recombination is a mint inside the closure, and that is not a defect (D1)

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** Fig 6 :22: **"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."**

A generative model trained on a corpus holds a library Γ; its outputs are compositions drawn from `closure(Γ)`. Calling that "recombination" is accurate, not dismissive. The figures' own mint pipeline demands exactly this: Fig 5 :16 **"mint φ if the description length pays"**, and the novelty guard Fig 5 :25 **"it is not already in the library"**. So the recombination side of the question is the normal, licensed operation of a compositional system. There is no defect to repair in it.

This also fixes the sense of "novel." P-creative (new to the agent) is the mint's own sense; H-creative (new to the world) is a different quantity that the frame cannot establish from inside (see F4 and F5). The two are not degrees of one scale, they are different anchors.

**Refuter:** exhibit a composition inside a fixed atom set that adds an atom to that set without an import. By Fig 8 :32 this is definitional, so the refuter would overturn the closure rule itself, not just this reading. Mode `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for its application to generative models.

### F3 — The artifact cannot settle the distinction (provenance condition)

Fig 8 :50-52: **"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."**

Two outputs with the same observable content can be produced by independent derivation inside one closure or by an import from a frame with a different closure. Nothing in the output text discriminates them. The essay *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the exact case: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* Fig 5 :48: **"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."**

Consequence: any criterion of machine creativity that looks only at the generated artifact is underdetermined. This is not skepticism about whether a difference exists; it is a claim about where the evidence for it must live, namely in the lineage.

**Refuter:** a discriminator that reads only the artifact and returns which route produced it, with a published error rate at the ground. Lab-grown versus mined diamonds are a case where traces did exist (isotope ratios) and the discriminator was found. If an analogous artifact-only trace is found for machine outputs, this finding narrows to "traces exist here" rather than failing outright. Mode `specified`.

### F4 — The only wall-moving operator is import, and it needs a differing closure (D2)

Fig 6 :14: **"the only operator that moves the wall"** (the import operator). Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** Fig 13 :115: **"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."**

So the strong sense of "creativity," new relative to what the frame could have produced, requires contact with a frame whose closure differs. It has three gates: source gate (provenance) and shadow test (explains a gap) in Fig 8 :10-13, and it is debited against the importing frame's independence (Fig 6 :24).

**But** Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** The world gains no primitive; the frame gains access. So even the strong sense does not mean "a new thing appeared in the world." It means a frame's reach expanded. This is the first place a common intuition strains, and it is handled in §5 rather than hidden.

The world-side analogue from low-overlap fields is Boden's transformational creativity: the creator alters the rules of the conceptual space itself, producing a result that was *"downright impossible"* within the previous space (SEP, Paul and Stokes 2024). That corroborates the figure distinction between exploratory recombination and wall-moving change. SEP is a world source and cannot decide the verdict; it agrees with it.

**Refuter:** a system that expands its own reach without contact with a differing closure, demonstrated by a term it can now derive that was provably outside `closure(Γ)` before, with the closure fixed. Mode `specified`.

### F5 — The anchor for "creative" must not update, and here it is a field (D3)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :33: **"The anchor must not update."** Fig 2 :36: **"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people."**

"Creativity" is constitutively a judgment about value and significance, so the right anchor is a field of people, not the model's own confidence or a self-score. If the anchor moves when the frame's output changes, the system enters Fig 2's collapse 1 (mutual update, coherent drift), which is invisible from inside (Fig 2 :34). Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**

This is also where Q62's worry ("novelty only relative to human knowledge") is dissolved rather than denied: relative-to-human-knowledge is not a defect to be engineered away. It is the constitutive anchor. The mistake would be to let the model's own distribution substitute for the field, because then the frame scores itself (Fig 1 :27).

The world-side corroboration is Csikszentmihalyi's DIFI framework as reported in the *Computational creativity* article: *"an individual produces works whose novelty and value are assessed by the field, other people in society, providing feedback and ultimately adding the work, now deemed creative, to the domain."* The field is an anchor-like selector, not a participant in the generation. Low overlap with the framework author.

**Refuter:** an anchor for creativity that updates on the generator's own output and still settles disputes at the ground, with a procedure showing the settlement survives pushing on the anchor. Mode `specified`.

### F6 — The label is applied afterwards to a lineage (D3, direct answer to the wording)

Fig 11 :36: **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground."** Fig 11 :39: **"And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."** Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

This is the same move as asking what generates lift rather than whether a thing is a bird (Fig 11 :41). Asking whether the machine is creative asks for a property of an isolated agent; the figure replaces it with a question about contact and lineage: did this output close a live residual against a ground, and can the route be shown? The label is a downstream report, not a mechanism.

Consequence: more parameters, more data, better sampling, all agent-only improvements, do not by themselves change the creativity verdict unless they change contact with a ground and a differing closure. This is a `general` structural claim with a `specified` consequence for model development.

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the ground, demonstrated by a controlled trial where the model changes and the field's settlement changes with it while contact is held fixed. The figure explicitly flags this as the case that would refute the capability claim (Fig 11 :72).

### F7 — Per slot, never a global creativity score (method)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

"Is the system creative?" is the aggregation error. The honest form is per slot: for this output, on this live residual, did the composition close it, was the atom imported or derived, and did a field settle it? A global "creativity score" broadcasts one slot's success over all slots and destroys the signal that says where to look.

**Refuter:** a global score that preserves the per-slot live mass, shown by a case where the global score stays fixed while a specific slot flips from live to settled. Mode `general`.

### F8 — "Mere recombination" is not a dismissal: arrangement is the substance

Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :95-96: **"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."** Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

So the pejorative use of "recombination" is illegible under the figures. A new bond over the same atoms is a new substance. Silver fulminate and silver cyanate share composition and differ in properties (Fig 12 :97-98). A machine that recombines training patterns under a new typed relation has produced a different term, not a relabeled old one. The real question is never "recombined or not" but "which bond, and was the atom imported."

**Refuter:** two outputs with identical atoms under different bonds that are indiscernible in behaviour at every instrument available. Then the bond carries no substance here and the isomer claim does not apply to this domain. Mode `specified`.

### F9 — The framework returns a shape, not a name (limit)

Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

The Tether can say what a genuine distinction must look like: a lineage with a crossing and a settlement, reported per slot, with provenance. It cannot name the specific missing axis that would make a particular system creative, and a failure to find one is not evidence none exists.

**Refuter:** a figure-derived name for a specific missing primitive that makes a system creative, with the derivation shown inside `closure(Γ)`. By Fig 13 :135 this is self-contradictory, so the refuter would break the framework's own limit claim. Mode `general`.

### F10 — Publication carries a generator, not a recording (distribution)

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."**

The outreach artifacts therefore lead with the residual and its refuter, record the verdict before any theory is shown, and offer the framework only as an opt-in method. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before judging the shape has joined the frame being checked.

**Refuter:** a case where sending the full theory first improves the independence of the verdict. That would require the theory to act as a ground, which contradicts Fig 10 :43. Mode `specified`.

### F11 — Agreement cannot supply the missing discriminator

Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** (with its leading clause). Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

Twenty raters who agree that an output is creative are one pool talking unless they were selected for independence and ranged separately against the ground. The same applies to this package's second seat, which is simulated and shares the evidence pool: its agreement is internal consistency, not corroboration (see `11_audit.md`).

**Refuter:** two genuinely independent frames whose agreement on a specific creativity verdict survives an independence audit and predicts a ground reading. Mode `specified`.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required by the seat rule that *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is withdrawn if any of the following holds:

1. A composition inside a fixed closure adds an atom to that closure without import (breaks F2 and Fig 6 :21-22).
2. An artifact-only discriminator reliably separates convergent derivation from adopted import at the ground, with published error rates and low-overlap frames (breaks F3 and Fig 8 :50-52).
3. An anchor for creativity updates on the generator's own output and still settles the verdict (breaks F5 and Fig 2 :33).
4. An agent-only improvement moves the ground while contact is held fixed (breaks F6 and Fig 11 :40).
5. Two outputs with identical atoms under different bonds are behaviourally indiscernible at every available instrument in the domain (breaks F8 and Fig 12 :117).

Until one of these is run, no part of this package may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Strains, re-census, and what survives

**Strain 1: "An import adds no atom to the world" versus the intuition that creativity makes something new.** Re-census: Fig 6 :25 governs world primitives, not frame access. The world gains no new primitive; the frame gains access to one (Fig 6 :26). What appears new in the world is a new arrangement under a bond (Fig 12 :94), settled at the ground. Different quantities, no contradiction after re-census. The intuition is not discarded, it is governed by Fig 12 rather than Fig 6.

**Strain 2: the mint pipeline's novelty guard versus Fig 6's "novel relative to atoms."** Fig 5 :25 *"it is not already in the library"* and Fig 6 :22 *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world"* are the same quantity at two description levels. No strain. Both exclude novelty relative to the world from the mint's own test.

**Strain 3: settled-molecule recursion versus import.** Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"* looks as if composition could add atoms across levels. Re-census: recursion changes what counts as an atom at a level; it does not add a primitive to the base set. Fig 13 :115 keeps the set closed. No strain after re-census.

**Strain 4: human judgment as anchor versus human judgment as instrument.** Fig 2 :36-37 says human judgment is the right anchor for a question about people and *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* Creativity is constitutively about value and significance, so it falls under the anchor clause, not the instrument clause. Named, not smoothed. If a reader reframes the question as "did the system implement a search algorithm correctly," the anchor clause no longer governs and the verdict changes. That is a different question.

**After re-census: four strains named, none hidden, no re-census owed for the retracted items in §6.**

### Verdict of this audit

The shape survives: the distinction is not in the artifact, it is a claim about an import crossing a closure and a settlement by an anchor that does not update, reported per slot and carried as a lineage. The two tempting answers do not survive: collapse into "it is all recombination" loses the provenance and anchor conditions (F2 is true but not the whole story), and a difference in kind smuggles in a primitive the framework says it cannot name (F9). Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. Triangulation status: the artifact-indistinguishability verdict is triangulated across Fig 8 :50-52 and the independently authored SEP entry plus the *How Intelligence Actually Works* provenance cases (low overlap between the figure text and SEP; the essay shares the framework author, so it is weak corroboration). The union reading is single-source for the figure rule (Fig 9 :54) with the cognitive-science literature agreeing on a multi-component definition. No `gap` is claimed: after a genuine census, figure lines govern every condition. The honest stop is at the limit of instruments, recorded at `07` §5.
