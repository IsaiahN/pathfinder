## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** Fig 13 :134-135: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

For this question a **shape** is therefore not a definition of creativity and not a list of properties of creative outputs. It is the form any adequate distinction must have: it must be about a route and a settlement, it must be per case, and it must survive being checked without access to the artifact's source. `SPACE(???)TIME` is a shape; naming Gradient is a name. Here, *"a difference in lineage plus a non-updating anchor"* is a shape; *"the system has a spark"* is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: effect terms first

Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."**

The effect terms used in `01` are: does the artifact close a live residual, did an atom cross a closure boundary, can the route be recovered from the artifact alone, and does the settlement anchor move when pushed. None of these requires the word "creativity" to be defined first.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the reason: an instrument reads green but *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer resolved the case, not an argument. Asking "which source produced this output?" is the wrong first question; asking "what would discriminate the sources?" is the shape.

*The Library of Babel* adds the stopping rule: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. Provenance is part of the shape, not documentation of it

Fig 10 :62: **"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."** The lineage is the term, not an appendix to it.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* is unusually explicit: *"The frame and the photograph are indistinguishable as artifacts, and they carry different warrants. One was taken; one was extracted. And nothing in the image records which."* And: *"often, very little about a composed term tells you, from looking at it alone, which route produced it."* It introduces the **distinction horizon** (an observer cannot tell two generation methods apart) and the **union horizon** (no observer with any available instrument can discriminate them), then warns that being below the union horizon *"is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."*

This is exactly Fig 8 :50-52 in the essays' vocabulary. So the shape includes a provenance requirement: a machine-creativity claim without a route record is not a weaker version of the claim, it is a different and uncheckable claim.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the source side: *"Outputs are not generators"* and *"You cannot accumulate your way from the category of outputs to the category of generators; they are different kinds of thing."* The genuine crossing is *"not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Corroboration for Fig 6 :14, high overlap with the framework author.

## 4. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here that means a field whose judgment does not update on the model's own output (Fig 2 :33, :36).
2. **A provenanced import from a closure that differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :10-13).

Until one of these, any specific name for "the creative ingredient" is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. *They're Scanning Books* makes the same point from inside the author's own framework: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible"*, and a minimum viable set is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes."*

## 5. Where this question's shape stops

The shape says:

- not "the artifact is different" (F3), but "the route is different and recorded";
- not "novel relative to the world" as an internal test (Fig 6 :22), but "an atom crossed from a differing closure" (Fig 6 :23);
- not "the model is creative" as a global property (Fig 1 :19), but "this slot closed this residual and a field settled it" (Fig 11 :36);
- not a definition of creativity, but the form an answer must take (Fig 9 :32).

It does **not** say which model, which benchmark, or which new primitive. It cannot: naming an unheld primitive would mean already holding it (Fig 13 :135). Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and for this package

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Getting well-known people to agree that a machine is creative is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried.

**This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction carried forward from `01`

An earlier reading of mine treated "novelty relative to human knowledge" as a limitation to be engineered away. That is retracted. Fig 2 :36 makes human judgment the right anchor when the question is constitutively about people, and creativity is such a question. The engineering task is to keep that anchor unmovable, not to remove it. Both the original and the correction are kept visible here so neither is quietly lost.
