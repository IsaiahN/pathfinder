## Question restated as an effect

Network size `N` changes two different quantities. It changes what a user gets from the platform (matches, content, complementors, but also congestion and noise), and it changes the height of the wall a challenger must cross (installed base, multi-homing, data, tipping). The question asks for the conditions that separate the cases. The two halves have different slots and different readings of the same market anchor, so they form a **union**, not one dial with two directions. An answer is a pair of condition sets with an instrument that discriminates a failure of one from a failure of the other. That shape is bound as an ordinary term before the run, so the pre-registered refuter can fire on it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Selecting recipients for likely agreement is handing the claim the same room.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list ranked by receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
