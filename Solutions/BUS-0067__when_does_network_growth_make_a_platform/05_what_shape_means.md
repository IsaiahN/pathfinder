## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13: *"It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For BUS-0067 a **shape** is the pair of condition sets and the discriminator we can state before we can name a critical mass or a winning design. It constrains; it does not fill. "Usefulness and challenge are a union with different conditions, and the feedback sign, closure, head start, and valence decide the cases" is a shape. "The tipping point is at 10,000 users" or "two-sided platforms always win" is a name, and is not returned by the framework.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the starting vocabulary was residual, slot, habitat, cycle, closure, wall, and head start; the effect terms came first, the business names later.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* *"rather than the cause"*. So the residual was described as "per-user value gap as `N` grows" and "per-challenger viability gap as the incumbent's `N` grows", not as "network externalities" or "moat".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar mapping): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email does exactly this.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct warrant for splitting the question's two "when" clauses into a union (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A market measurement of per-slot value and entry outcomes would settle the business version. The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The economics sources name "tipping" and "two-sided market"; those names enter as imports with provenance, not as framework derivations, and they carry zero evidentiary weight until a source gate and shadow test are supplied.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from the exemplar mapping) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why the critical-mass number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A platform size reading has the same form. The number of users, the engagement count, and the valuation are readings. If several sources produce the same reading, a growth curve cannot tell which source produced it: genuine catalyst contribution, a head start, preferential attachment, a subsidised side, or a saturated metric. Naming "critical mass" from the curve is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading, where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a number. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the conditions (cycle, closure, head start, union)?

**No, and this is a correction to an overstatement I considered.** The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is something not held in Γ. The conditions named here are not missing primitives: cycle, catalyst, closure, head start, and union are all already available in the figure vocabulary (Fig 12 :119, :121; Fig 6 :14, :23; Fig 4 :11, :22; Fig 9 :54-57). Naming them is a **binding** from the platform frame onto held vocabulary, not the invention of a new term. What remains forbidden is inventing a seventh substrate term and claiming that naming the critical mass derives it. This is the same narrowing the exemplar made in its `05` section 5b and `06` Q1, and the same narrowing the BUS-0009 and BUS-0015 solutions record.

**What is genuinely not available:** the numerical threshold. No figure line holds a critical mass, a tipping elasticity, or an optimal subsidy split. That is a `gap`, recorded as `unreachable` in `01` section 4 and `09` VERDICT, not as silence and not as an invitation to invent a number (Fig 13 :134, Fig 8 :39).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous platform founders or well-cited strategy professors to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the market, an experiment, or an entry outcome settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the platform question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction kept visible

An earlier draft of this file treated "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any of the mechanisms and therefore refused even the word "cycle". That was an overstatement. The ban governs a missing primitive. Cycle and closure are held vocabulary, so naming them is a provenanced binding and is allowed; the numerical critical mass is what cannot be named. The earlier reading is preserved here rather than silently erased, consistent with the project's rule that corrections stay visible.
