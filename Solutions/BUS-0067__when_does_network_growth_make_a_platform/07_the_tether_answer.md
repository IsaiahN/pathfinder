# The Tether's answer on network growth and platform challenge (BUS-0067)

**Mode: `specified` on the platform claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical threshold. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a market result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Network size `N` changes two different quantities in two different slots: what a user gets from the platform, and how hard the platform is to challenge. Each is a residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` indexed per slot, never a mean (Fig 1 :19, :22). The market is the anchor for both, read two ways: realized user value and realized entry or survival (Fig 2 :29). The two halves are a **union compound**, not one dial: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :57). An answer must give two condition sets and an instrument that discriminates them.

## 2. The shape

1. **Usefulness is platform-and-habitat, not platform alone.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* The users, content, and complementors are part of what makes the platform capable. An agent-only improvement that does not change contact changes nothing (Fig 11 :40).
2. **The marginal user changes valence with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* A participant is a benefactor for matching and a malfactor for attention (Fig 11 :50). Growth helps where contributions regenerate (catalysts, Fig 12 :119) and hurts where they are spent or where the channel saturates (Fig 10 :26), so a pooled network-effect score hides both signs.
3. **The feedback is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes.** Fig 12 :121: *"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."* Positive closes and tips; negative unwinds. The framework does not compute the sign; an instrument is owed.
4. **Hardness to challenge is a wall that only an import from a differing closure moves.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Minting inside the closure can never add an atom (Fig 6 :21). The challenger's import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).
5. **Size is a head start, not a generator.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* A large network makes the next search cheaper (Fig 4 :12) but does not prove the method transferred. The transform is lossy, so the round-trip gap `R_T` must be measured (Fig 4 :30).
6. **Entrenchment can be a chain of mirrors.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. A platform that keeps handing itself the same users compounds agreement without ground evidence; progress needs both bracket fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39).
7. **The two halves are a union with two currencies.** Fig 9 :55, :57. Usefulness and defensibility are not a single score; a lever that adds users clears only the region where one condition holds. Fig 12 :125-126: bits price the description and actions price finding out whether it holds, and they do not add.
8. **The numerical threshold is a declared `gap`.** No figure line returns a critical mass or tipping number. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that growth is always good. Valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49), so the same growth can help one slot and hurt another.
- Not that lock-in is inevitable. The world literature records both tipping and the exhaustion of scale benefits above a small critical mass; the conflict is kept as evidence for the union, not resolved by choosing a side.
- Not that data or an installed base is automatically a moat. A wall claim needs a differing closure and a provenanced import (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).
- Not that a size-value correlation establishes causation. Correlation is symmetric, the claim is ordered (Fig 2 :39-40; Fig 12 :87).
- Not a derivation of one platform type from the other, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a market measurement.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Catalyst / spent-part discriminator:** an instrument that reads, per user cohort and per residual, whether a marginal participant regenerates their contribution across uses or is spent after one (Fig 12 :119). This names the sign of the cycle.
- **Closure-crossing instrument:** a record with provenance showing whether a challenger's decisive atom came from contact with a differing closure or was already in its own library (Fig 8 :50-52). This separates a wall-crossing from convergence.
- **Head-start round trip:** measure `R_T` between the incumbent's growth method described at the population level and concretised in a new habitat (Fig 4 :30). A head start that carries no method shows up as a nonzero gap.
- **Exogenous size variation:** an instrument or natural experiment that varies `N` independently of prior value, with a declared source gate, so the arrow can be chosen (Fig 2 :39-40).
- **Standing falsifiers:** the eight conditions in `01` section 4 and `08` section 2, written before this conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The vocabulary used here is a visible choice, not nature's own list; *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Another frame's minimum set need not be these terms. The answer stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish a catalyst from a spent part, an import from a convergence, or a head start from a method, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a critical mass or a winning platform design. It says that usefulness and hardness to challenge are two different conditions with different readings of the market anchor, and that they must be kept separate. Growth helps users where added participants stay in contact with the user residual as regenerating contributors, because the effect is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes; the same participant can help matching and hurt attention, so a mean over users hides both signs. Growth entrenches an incumbent where its installed base forms a closure that a challenger can move only by importing from a differing closure, carried by a head start that lowers the next search cost without proving the method transferred. Entrenchment can grow while ground contact decays, because a chain of mirrors compounds agreement without adding evidence. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, eight falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The threshold stays open until the ground settles it.
