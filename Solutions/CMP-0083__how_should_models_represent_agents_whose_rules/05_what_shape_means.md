## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (OPENED): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a per-slot, typed, re-grounded representation with a rule-identity gap" is a shape. "Use agent type hierarchy number 4 with a Bayesian update every 20 steps" is a name.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (OPENED): use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns that if the final book does not answer the question, "perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one". That is the union census in `01` §1c.
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." This is why the residual in `01` §0 is stated in effect terms (signal lost, source undiscriminated) rather than as a proposed data structure.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (OPENED): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A shape is checkable by anyone who shares the effect vocabulary, without entering the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (OPENED): "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."; Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (CARRIED): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

For this question, the imports are the ODD protocol, POMDP policies, behavioural reaction norms and concept-drift detection. Each predicts part of the shape (state variables vary over time; policies map history to action; behaviour is a function of an environmental gradient; static rules decay). None authorizes naming a single correct representation, and each is recorded with provenance in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 4. Why "which rule is live" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (OPENED) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A model that reads an agent's behavior and names the internal rule is making the metamerism error: a source named from a reading, where several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a better instrument (an intervention, a perturbation, an internal-state probe), not a name. This is F7 in `01` and it is why `07` and `09` carry a `gap` rather than a rule taxonomy.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A model that certifies its own representation from its own output is the named collapse.

## 5. Q : Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming the operator kinds? ANSWERED with a correction

**Question:** may the answer say "a rule is a sequence, a goal is a comparison, an information limit is a subtraction, a rule replacement is a negation"?

**Answer:** yes, with provenance. The figures README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The operators are already held and named in the Operators table: "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one" (Operators :51), "progress is a subtraction" (Operators :46), "a refutation is a negation" (Operators :58). Naming them is a binding onto held atoms, not the invention of a new primitive.

**Correction to my own first pass.** In `01` §0 and the first draft of `06` I initially treated "shape, not a name" as forbidding the whole operator mapping. That overstated the rule. The narrowed position, matching the exemplar's own §5b correction (`QMGR-0037/05_what_shape_means.md`), is: naming held bindings is provenance; claiming the framework names the specific *live rule* of a particular agent is forbidden, because that source is not held. Both the overstatement and its narrowing are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (README plus Fig 13 :135 plus Operators table).

## 6. Consequence for what this package may claim

The framework returns the constraints any adequate representation must satisfy:

- the residual must stay indexed per agent-slot (Fig 1 :19);
- the representation must carry the bond, not just the ingredient list (Fig 12 :5, :94);
- a method rather than a recording must cross the context boundary (Fig 4 :22, :25);
- the room must change when the question changes (Fig 10 :72);
- the answer must stop at the instrument limit and record the lineage (canonical README).

It does **not** return: a specific agent architecture, a parameter count, a type taxonomy, a rule-identification procedure, or a claim that one modeling formalism is best. Those are names and must be purchased by a ground or imported with provenance.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (OPENED): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well known agent-based modelers to agree with this shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, write the refuter first, and let the ground, an intervention in a real modeled system, settle it.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (OPENED): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. No agent-based model was built, run or measured for this package.
