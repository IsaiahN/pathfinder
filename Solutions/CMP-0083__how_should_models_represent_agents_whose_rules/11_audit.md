## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the loss of the live per-agent signal when agents are pooled, plus the inability to identify which rule governs at a slot. Arity: three operands (agent, rule/goal/information, context or step), indexed twice, by slot and by step (Fig 1 :11, :19). Symmetry: agents are not interchangeable, so the bond is ordered; two readings of one event at one slot are one report (Fig 2 :18-21). Scale: step loop, population, lineage across contexts. I classify it as a **union** because within-agent change, between-agent difference and across-context change overlap; a single lever leaves intersections standing (Fig 9 :57).
- **Domain 1, the per-agent loop.** Actors: one agent, `b`, `w`, `a`, `o'`, `R` per slot. Currency: per-slot residual. Anchor: the ground as the only metric. Question type: indexing.
- **Domain 2, the population.** Actors: many agents, shared library, ranker. Currency: methods up, head starts down. Anchor: the ground ranking outputs. Question type: signature versus label.
- **Domain 3, time and context.** Actors: rooms and surfaced residuals. Currency: variation inside, selection between. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: method versus recording, and re-enumeration.
- **Domain 4, the encoding.** Actors: atoms, typed bonds, unary negation. Currency: bits and actions. Anchor: the ground settling. Question type: operator mapping and the gap on rule identity.
- **Order by dependency.** Slot index before bonds, bonds before objective, objective before ground (Fig 3 :20). The missing instrument sits at the bond/slot binding (Fig 3 :27).

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22/:28 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :22/:25 (method versus recording), Fig 10 :72 (room follows question), Fig 11 :51 (no standing list), Fig 12 :112/:117 (bonds and isomers) and Fig 13 :135 (shape not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and the verification packet: index per agent; store typed relations; carry methods; re-enumerate; two currencies; rule identity is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete modeling prescription, for example a per-agent state table with typed transition, threshold and absence fields, a rule provenance stamp, a context-change trigger and a source-discriminating intervention.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **choice of which typed bond holds for a given agent** is not determined by the figure set. Fig 12 :46 says the bond is not recoverable from the operands, and the figures do not supply a rule-selection operator. A concrete model must choose, and that choice is a binding with provenance, not a derivation.
- the **cost of unary negation** is open (Operators :77-78), so the price of representing "old rule is false" cannot be carried down from the priced bond formula.
- the **instrument for rule-to-slot binding** is missing (Fig 3 :27), so a concrete model cannot verify that the encoded bond is the live one without an intervention the framework does not specify.
- the **two-currency accounting** cannot be collapsed into one score (Fig 12 :130), so any implementation that reports a single quality number drops a constraint the shorthand preserved.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a pooled score hides a live slot signal, so per-slot indexing is mandatory. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no model or ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09` states.
