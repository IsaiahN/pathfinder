# Tether outreach: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Do behavioral economics findings require replacing standard models of rational choice, or mainly modifying them?

**Source:** economics questions.txt line 127

**Section:** 10. Economic methods, welfare, and alternative frameworks

**Mode:** `specified` on the per-domain claims and the per-slot routing rule, `general` on the structural shape (union not partition), `gap` on which named behavioral term wins a given slot. Not `grounded`. Every substantive file states that this is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading, so it cannot settle the question it reports on.

**Status:** done

**Boundary.** Work was confined to this folder. The only files read outside it were the canonical framework files under `FRAMEWORK/` (figure text, figures README, THE_FORMULA, exemplar) and public web pages opened for the WATSON run. No framework file was modified.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that behavioral economics modifies rather than replaces.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. A room does not upgrade the label. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Agreement among selected believers is a weighted average of influence, not a verdict.
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Fig 2 :39-41: "Correlation has valence" and "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. Asking people to trust before checking spends the only thing they contribute. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public, institutional, and opt-in.

## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

Per the formula's "Direction" discipline, the sought-for shape is written down so it can accrue a residual like any other term. Here it is: a per-slot routing of behavioral findings into the figure's five gap kinds, with a default to re-fit when a term composes inside the standard closure, a named boundary where a new atom is owed, a refusal where a held term is false, and a ground test (held-out predictive success) that decides which binding wins. The answer is not the word "modify" and not the word "replace". It is that routing plus the boundary plus the refuter. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §0 and §6.
