## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question, a **shape** is the form the answer must have before anyone can name the winning model: a per-slot routing of deviations, a default to re-fit, a boundary where a new atom or a refusal is owed, and a ground test that decides the binding. "Behavioral economics mainly modifies rational choice" is a name, and a premature one. "A deviation is routed by what is wrong, and the response follows from the kind of gap" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the deviation in effect terms (a choice difference under a manipulation, with a stated anchor) before picking "prospect theory" or "rational choice" as the frame. Fig 9 :28 warns "any frame you pick will seem to fit" otherwise.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question, the ground is held-out predictive success, not the number of anomalies a model can retrodict.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The animal-behaviour quasi-hyperbolic result in `04` is a candidate import with a genuinely different closure, but it still owes a shadow test before it can license a claim about economic choice.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The standard frame's primitives are a visible choice, not nature's own list; so is any behavioral replacement.

## 4. The source-discriminator point for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and "cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the analogue is direct. A choice pattern (say, rejecting a fair bet or failing to rebalance a portfolio) can be generated by at least three sources: a preference with reference dependence, a belief distortion, or a computational constraint that makes the optimum intractable and returns an approximation. The observed choice is a green reading. Gershman, Horvitz and Tenenbaum 2015 (Science) make the same point from the computational side: under bounded resources, "fallacies" may arise as a natural consequence of optimisation. Naming "loss aversion" as the source of a reading without an instrument that discriminates among these paths is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better discriminator, not a name. This is why the F5 axiom slot remains a declared `gap`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming prospect theory or hyperbolic discounting. That overstated the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the frame. Prospect theory's atoms (outcomes, probabilities, a value function, a reference point) are mostly bindings onto terms already discussed in economics; naming them is not inventing a seventh primitive. What remains genuinely open is the *reference point*: is it a re-arrangement of held atoms, or a new atom imported from psychology? Fig 6 :22 says "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure", so calling a re-arrangement a wall-moving import would be wrong. Both claims are recorded: the correction narrows the ban to missing primitives, and the metamerism point in §4 is **not** retracted because the source of a choice reading is still undiscriminated. Triangulated (README + Fig 13 :133 + Fig 6 :22).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous behavioral economists to agree with the routing is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the routing against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
