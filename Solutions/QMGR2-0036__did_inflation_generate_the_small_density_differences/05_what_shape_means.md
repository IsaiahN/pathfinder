## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, and that *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

From the Library of Babel essay (read): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the writer. The shape is: a source must reproduce a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, nearly Gaussian spectrum at the observed amplitude, with a tensor sector consistent with the current bound, and it must be separable from rival sources by some observable. `SPACE(???)TIME`-style open slots are the right form; here the open slot is `SOURCE(???)`. Naming "inflation" in the slot is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Library of Babel (read): use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* In practice: write down the statistical residual first (n_s, r, f_NL, isocurvature bound), then ask which early-universe frame predicts it.
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"* We describe the effect (the perturbation statistics), not the cause (the inflaton).
- Library of Babel on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the search converges on a shaped constraint set, not on a photograph of the early universe.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground discriminates.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A measurement that separates inflation from alternatives would name the slot.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). A name carried in without a record is an adopted import that looks like a convergent derivation.

The Hard Problem is About Source (read) gives the mechanism in the exact terms this question needs. An instrument reads green, but *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*, and it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. The CMB perturbation pattern is our green reading; inflation and the alternatives are different source mixtures that can produce it.

Low-overlap world corroboration: the Wikipedia metamerism entry (read 2026-11-23) defines *"a perceived matching of colors with different (nonmatching) spectral power distributions"*, and the Stanford Encyclopedia entry (read) defines contrastive underdetermination as evidence that may support more than one theory. These corroborate the shape limit but do not decide it; the figures do.

## 4. Q answered: what licenses the name "inflation" here?

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns the constraints any writer must satisfy. To name inflation as the writer requires a source-discriminating measurement plus a record of the route. A statistical fit to the pattern is not that: several routes compose the same substance (Fig 13 :121). The honest form remains `SOURCE(???)` with a list of discriminators, until a ground reading fires.

## 5. Correction to my own reading (kept visible)

I initially treated *"shape, not a name"* as a blanket ban on discussing inflation at all. That overstates it. The canonical phrase governs **what the framework can derive**, namely a missing primitive or, here, a missing source discriminator. It does not forbid discussing a candidate that is already in the world literature, provided the candidate is recorded as an import with its provenance and is not presented as a figure result. So the corrected position is: discuss inflation freely, but label it a candidate binding, not a name the figures produced. Both the initial reading and its narrowing are kept here.

A second correction concerns the word "shape" itself. In cosmology, "the shape of the perturbation spectrum" is an ordinary technical phrase for its scale dependence. That is **not** the framework's "shape, not a name". This folder uses "shape" in the framework sense unless it explicitly says "spectral shape".

## 6. Two consequences for outreach and status

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (read): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous cosmologists who agree that inflation did it is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the discriminator.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs can *"simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They cannot settle whether inflation generated the perturbations.
