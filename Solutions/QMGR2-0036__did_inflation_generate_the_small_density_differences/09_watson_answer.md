## VERDICT (from the figures)

The pattern is settled; the writer is a shape with an open slot. Inflation is the leading candidate binding in the world literature, recorded with provenance, but no figure line names it and the observed spectrum does not uniquely encode it. Different routes compose the same substance, so alternatives are not refuted by the pattern; over the full observational atom set they are a real isomer claim with a stated falsifier. What is owed is a source-discriminating measurement, built by improving the scalar-index, tensor, non-Gaussianity and isocurvature instruments we already have. The figure verdict is `specified` and ungrounded on the shape, `general` on the shape-not-a-name limit, and `gap` on the name itself. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The four refuters are written before the run and the provenance is recorded, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The candidate binding can be withdrawn without touching the shape derivation; `07` and `08` already withhold the name. A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive has to be surrendered.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A measured tensor signal, non-Gaussianity shape or isocurvature component that separates the candidates would name the slot; a demonstration that only one source composes the observed atom set, or a statistic outside the inflationary prediction space, would fire a refuter. Until one of these happens the verdict stands as `candidate`.
