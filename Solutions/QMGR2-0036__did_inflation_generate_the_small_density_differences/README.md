## The question restated as an effect

The CMB and large-scale structure carry a **reading**: a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, nearly Gaussian pattern of density contrast at roughly the 10^-5 level. The question asks which process **wrote** that reading.

- Option A: inflationary quantum fluctuations, stretched to cosmic size, generated the seeds that later grew into galaxies.
- Option B: a different early-universe process produced a functionally identical reading, so the pattern alone does not select the writer.

What each leads to: A names a source from a projection; B keeps the pattern settled by the ground and leaves the source open until a source-discriminating observable decides. A complete answer would look like a **shape**, the set of statistical constraints any writer must satisfy plus a named discriminator, rather than a name. Following the formula's "Direction", that sought-for shape is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused, instead of remaining an invisible preference (Fig 5 :65: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that inflation did it.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A contact list chosen for people already inclined to answer "yes" is one pool wearing many names.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
