## 0. The residual in effect terms

**Effect statement.** The CMB and large-scale structure deliver a **reading**: a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, nearly Gaussian spectrum of density contrast at roughly 10^-5, which gravity later amplified into galaxies. The question is not *what is the pattern* (the ground settled that) but *which process wrote it*. Inflation is one named candidate writer; alternatives are others. The residual is a **source-attribution residual**: the map from a settled reading back to its producing route is many-to-one unless a discriminating observable exists.

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**

- **Arity:** one observed effect, several candidate sources. This is a contrastive set of routes, not a list of independent conditions. Where it behaves as a compound is the second-order question "which source", which has its own ground and its own loop.
- **Symmetry:** the pattern carries statistical symmetries (translation invariance, statistical isotropy, near-Gaussianity). These constrain any writer but do not identify one. They are properties of the projection, not of the source.
- **Scale:** the claimed source operates at microphysical scales and is stretched to horizon and galactic scales; the reading is made at the latter. The transform between them is lossy, so the route is not recoverable from the artefact (Fig 13 :128).

**Direction (binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term).** A satisfying answer would look like: (a) the statistical constraints any writer must satisfy; (b) at least one observable whose value separates candidate writers; (c) a recorded lineage saying which route was actually taken. This shape is bound now so it can accrue a residual and be refused, as Fig 5 :65 requires: *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"* (`general`).

The chain usually breaks between vocabulary and objective: we can describe the pattern, yet have no instrument that uniquely names its source. A reading taken below that break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

## 3. Findings

### F1 — The residual is source attribution, and the pattern is already settled

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."*

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Mode: `general`, structural. The live question is not "were there perturbations" (settled) but "which slot in the source census wrote them". Collapsing the answer to a single yes/no discards the discriminators (n_s, r, f_NL, isocurvature) that carry the residual. **Status: triangulated**, Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and Fig 2 (anchor definition), low overlap between them. **Refuter:** show that the residual is a single scalar pattern question with no live source slot, or that no instrument settles the pattern.

### F2 — Different routes arrive at the same substance (governing finding)

Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."*

Fig 13 :128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 13 :129: *"It is recoverable from the lineage."*

Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*

Mode: `specified`, a structural prediction about any reading of this kind. The observed perturbation spectrum is the artefact; inflation is one route to it and alternatives are other routes. The artefact does not encode which route was taken. What separates the routes is the **record**, not the pattern. This is why the honest answer to "did inflation generate them" is a shape plus a refused slot, not a name read off the CMB. **Status: triangulated**, Fig 13 (lineage and convergence) with Fig 8 (provenance), genuinely low overlap. **Refuter:** exhibit a reading from which the producing route is deterministically recoverable, or show that only one candidate source can compose the observed statistic.

### F3 — Shape, not a name, and what would license a name

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*

### F4 — Describe the residual before choosing the frame

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* Fig 9 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

Mode: `general`. The discipline is to state the statistical residual (adiabatic, n_s near 1, Gaussian bound, tensor bound) before asking which frame predicts it. Otherwise any early-universe frame can be made to fit after the fact. **Status: single-source** within the figures (Fig 9 only); corroborated secondarily by the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on underdetermination. **Refuter:** show that the inflationary frame was derived from the residual before the statistics were known, in which case the caution is satisfied rather than violated.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per the discipline that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim, that the pattern does not by itself name its source and that a discriminator is owed, is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A source-discriminating observable is measured that uniquely selects inflation, or uniquely excludes it, at the ground. The slot may then be named and the mode raised to `grounded`.
2. It is demonstrated that only one candidate source can compose the observed statistic, so different routes cannot arrive at the same substance here (breaks Fig 13 :121).
3. A measured statistic falls outside the inflationary prediction space, so the inflation binding is a term whose residual persists and does not trend down (Formula step 2, a refusal rather than an explanation).
4. The lineage rule fails: a reading is shown to recover its producing route deterministically (breaks Fig 13 :128).

If any fires, the "shape only" verdict is withdrawn and the record is corrected here, not quietly rewritten.
