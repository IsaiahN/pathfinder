# The Tether's answer on inflation and the density differences

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the shape limit. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No new measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

The perturbation pattern is settled by instruments. The question "did inflation generate it" asks which process wrote that pattern. That is a source-attribution residual, not a pattern residual. Fig 13 :121: *"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."* Fig 13 :128: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The pattern is ground-settled; the writer is not.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Instruments settle the spectrum. They do not, by themselves, settle the source.
2. **A shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Any writer must reproduce a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, nearly Gaussian spectrum at the observed amplitude, with a tensor sector consistent with the current bound. The framework does not answer "inflation" and does not answer "not inflation".
3. **Inflation is the leading candidate binding, recorded as such.** In the world literature, quantum fluctuations of the inflaton are stretched to cosmic size and become the seeds of structure (Baumann 2009; Planck 2015 and 2018; Wikipedia Cosmic inflation, all read 2026-11-23). That is an import from the physics frame onto an open slot, with provenance. It is not a figure derivation and it does not move a wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*).
4. **Alternatives are not refuted by the pattern.** Because different routes compose the same substance, an ekpyrotic, curvaton or defect-seed route can reproduce the same spectral atoms. Under Fig 12 :117 they are *"same atoms, different bond, different substance"*; as a weaker claim they share a functional group (Fig 12 :124).
5. **What is owed is a discriminator, not more of the same reading.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The scalar index and the bounds on r, f_NL and isocurvature are the worse instruments we already have. A measurement that separates candidate writers would name the slot.
6. **No search of the pattern alone proves absence.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Failure to discriminate at this budget is not proof that no discriminator exists.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No measurement here raises the source claim to accepted.
- Not "inflation definitely did it", and not "inflation did not do it". Both would be verdicts without a discriminating ground.
- Not that the CMB pattern is in doubt. The pattern is settled; the question is about its source.
- Not that alternatives are equally likely. Likelihood ranking belongs to the physics frame, with its own ground. The figures only say that the reading underdetermines the route.
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 13 :90: *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*

## 4. What would settle it, and the falsifiers

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The Hard Problem is About Source (read) showed that the spectrometer, not a debate, resolved the analogous green-reading case: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Pre-registered falsifiers (from `01` §4):

1. A discriminator is measured that uniquely selects inflation, or uniquely excludes it. The slot may be named.
2. Only one candidate source can compose the observed statistic (breaks Fig 13 :121).
3. A measured statistic falls outside the inflationary prediction space, so the inflation binding is refused.
4. A reading is shown to recover its producing route deterministically (breaks Fig 13 :128).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The source slot is open because the discriminating instrument has not fired, not because it cannot exist. The answer stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing writers, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. The canonical README's rule, *"Indiscernibility is not identity"*, applies: two routes that give the same reading are not thereby the same route. And the agents' agreement about inflation is not a ground: Fig 7 :35, *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not answer "yes, inflation did it", and it does not answer "no". It says the primordial density pattern is already settled by instruments, but a pattern does not carry the name of the process that wrote it: different routes can arrive at the same substance, and only the record of the route separates them. Inflation is the leading candidate in the physics literature, where quantum fluctuations are stretched to cosmic size and become the seeds of galaxies, but that is a provenanced binding onto an open slot, not something the framework derived. What the framework returns is a shape: any source must reproduce a nearly scale-invariant, adiabatic, nearly Gaussian spectrum and must be separable from rivals by an observable. The scalar index and the bounds on tensors, non-Gaussianity and isocurvature are the instruments we already have; a sharper version of one of them would name the writer. Until that fires, the slot stays open, the four refuters are on record, and agreement among cosmologists is not allowed to stand in for the ground.
