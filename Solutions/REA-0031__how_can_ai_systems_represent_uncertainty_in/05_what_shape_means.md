## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the three-condition form C1 (per-slot residual with live-mass support), C2 (anchor not authored by the graded frame), C3 (method transfer across the membrane with source gate, shadow test, and negation), plus an instrument that discriminates their failures. "A well calibrated 0.7" is a name, not a shape. "One confidence score calibrated on a held-out set" is a name for a method, not the shape of what must hold.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- THE_FORMULA, "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds."* The remedy is to bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual and be refused.

Applied here: the effect description is "a stated uncertainty that tracks the ground per slot when the slot moves into a new closure." That description selects frames. If it had been stated as "find the best calibration algorithm," any algorithm would have seemed to fit (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The six-term set is a visible guess, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming one calibration algorithm as *the* answer to "how should uncertainty be represented" turns a visible choice into an invisible one. That is why F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a `gap` and not a hidden answer.

## 4. Why reading the metric as the source is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or an as-yet undiscovered scheme can produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Here an expected calibration error reads one number. It cannot say whether the cause was a hidden live slot (F1), a saturated or pooled channel (F3), or a missing atom in the closure (F4). Those several paths give the same reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a better name for the reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So a calibration metric produced by the model it grades cannot settle the model's calibration.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (visible retraction)

I first read *"a shape, not a name"* as forbidding the mention of any named calibration method. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in Γ. Temperature scaling and conformal prediction are not missing primitives of the figure set; they are methods from another frame. Naming them is a binding from that frame, licensed only by provenance and a shadow test, not by the figures. What remains wrong in the common account is treating the binding as figure-derived and claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :23; *"IMPORT adds an atom"* is the relevant test: a method binding adds no figure atom). My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: the reading still does not determine the source, and the instrument is still what is owed. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. The two consequences for verification

**A room does not upgrade the label.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* and *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* *Arrival of the Fittest*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* A survey of people who already agree that a method works is not a ground.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A famous method's name gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not settle the claim.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 8. Q answered

**What licenses a particular calibration method as the answer to this question?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular method is licensed only by a ground settling it on the task in question, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test from a frame whose closure differs. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The correct form of the answer stays `three conditions, method slot open`.
