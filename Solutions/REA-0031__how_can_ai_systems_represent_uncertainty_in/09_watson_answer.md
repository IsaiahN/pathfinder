## QUESTION

How can AI systems represent uncertainty in ways that remain calibrated across unfamiliar tasks? Restated as an effect: why does a stated uncertainty stop tracking R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| when the slot moves into a new closure, and what must such a representation contain for tracking to resume? Option A: one confidence scalar calibrated by an average held-out error. Option B: a per-slot residual held against an immovable anchor, with method transfer across the membrane, a refusal operator, and the method slot left open. What each leads to: A hides live slots, lets the frame score itself, and replays a recording into a substituted habitat; B sends a checkable shape with pre-registered refuters and an honest `gap` where the figures name no algorithm.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged. Uncertainty that stays calibrated across unfamiliar tasks must be a per-slot residual reported without hiding live slots, ranged against an anchor the graded frame cannot move, carried across the membrane as a method rather than a recording, and equipped with negation so a held confidence can be refused. A saturated metric is a channel alarm. An unfamiliar task is a new closure: run SEARCHED first, then INWARD, then OUTWARD, with source gate and shadow test on any import. Which named algorithm wins is a `gap`, not a hidden answer. World sources corroborate pieces of the mechanism but are secondary and do not overrule the figures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable but real. A team that treats a saturated confidence as a result will retune a scalar when it should restore contact; a team that treats a benchmark as a ground will ship a substituted habitat and call it capability. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording or a named method before they have graded the shape. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its five refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because something was added rather than deleted (Fig 6 :42-43). The three conditions, the refuters and the `gap` are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet without withdrawing any primitive.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A live positive residual that stays visible after per-slot measurement breaks F1; an anchor that moves under push breaks F2; an instrument that stays discriminating while the ground shifts breaks F3; reach renewed by pure internal composition with no new atom breaks F4; a recorded confidence transferred unchanged with no method and no new contact breaks F5; standing removed by addition alone breaks F6. A figure line governing method choice after re-census, or an instrument that settles the method on the new task, closes the `gap`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
