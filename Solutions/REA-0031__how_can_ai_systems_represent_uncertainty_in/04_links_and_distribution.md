## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

- **Calibration is not automatic and is architecture-dependent.** Guo et al. found modern networks *"poorly calibrated"* and tied it to depth, width, weight decay and normalisation. This corroborates the channel-decay finding (F3) but is silent on arbitrary unfamiliar tasks; it does not establish transfer.
- **Coverage guarantees are conditional.** Angelopoulos and Bates give non-asymptotic validity without distributional assumptions, but the conformal guarantee rests on exchangeability. In these terms exchangeability is a closure assumption: it corroborates the anchor/bracket finding (F2) and does not by itself supply a missing atom for a new closure (F4).
- **A single scalar cannot carry reliability, resolution and uncertainty at once.** The Brier decomposition and Tetlock's distinction between calibration and discrimination corroborate F1 and F8: averaging or collapsing slots loses the signal that says where to look.
- **Human calibration training transfers only to a limited extent.** Lichtenstein, Fischhoff and Phillips, as reported on the calibrated-probability-assessment page, found training improves calibration only up to a point. That is a low-overlap witness for the membrane finding (F5), not a measurement of AI systems.
- **Metacognitive efficiency is relative to task performance.** The metacognition reference page defines it that way and states that domain-generality is unresolved. That is a genuine world-level open question matching the figure-level `gap` on method choice (F10), but it is not a figure line and cannot close the gap.
- **Animal metacognition gives a second low-overlap frame.** Decline-the-test behaviour in rats and information seeking in chimpanzees are described on the same page. They are corroboration for the refusal requirement (F6) at a different stratum, not evidence for an AI design.
- **Multiple readings of one confidence value are a source-discrimination problem.** The Hard Problem essay's metamerism case corroborates the demand for a source-discriminating instrument rather than a name, which is the honest stop in `07_the_tether_answer.md`.

Agreement among these sources does not make two witnesses where there is one pool. Guo, conformal pages and the ML venue pages share the machine-learning frame; Tetlock, calibrated assessment and metacognition share the human-judgement frame; the essays share the framework author. Overlap is recorded per row in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** — *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends the interiors of a frame to a would-be verifier.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing any theory.
- **Fig 7 :35** — *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list selected for likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* — a later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22** — *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted; sending a recorded answer is not.

**Order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the verdict; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a method with the instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the three-condition shape, and the pre-registered refuters. No theory, no links, no request for agreement. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three links above. Their stage-1 verdict must already be recorded. Later agreement is a conversation and carries zero verdict weight.

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get, because THE_FORMULA's "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question and the architecture article warns that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the artefact to send; `11_audit.md` records the independent check. Nothing here has been measured against a ground.

## 5. Practical constraint on submission

The venue pages in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public and opt-in. They are not mailing lists of people selected for agreement. Any submission that presents a simulated derivation as a measured result would breach both the figures' mode rule and normal research ethics. State `specified`, state the refuter, and let the venue's own ground decide.
