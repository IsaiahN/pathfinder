## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :133: *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

For ECN-0030, the difference is concrete:

- A **name** would be: "issue a retail CBDC with a 3,000 euro holding limit and zero remuneration." That is a configuration, chosen from the design frame, and the figures cannot derive it.
- A **shape** is: "whatever is issued must remain an anchor, the funding cascade must be enumerated to three steps before introduction, the new claim must be declared an isomer of the deposit rather than a new primitive, and the unconditional 'should' stays a gap until a ground reading settles it."

The shape constrains any admissible answer. It does not fill in the answer.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- Describe the effect before choosing a frame. Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The residual here was stated as a change in contact (who holds what, who funds whom, what settles), not as "CBDC versus banks."
- Cast in effect terms. Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- Keep the sought-for outcome as an ordinary term. Formula, "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* The conditional rule in `01` §0 is that binding.
- Stop at the instrument limit. The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* The instrument limit here is the absence of a post-issuance ground reading in this run.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, that means measured post-issuance deposit flows, funding costs and a stability metric, range against the conditional rule.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The central banking sources are such a frame, but they are inside the same policy pool as the claim, so their agreement is weak corroboration, not a second witness.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument argument directly: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)"*. Applied here: a deposit outflow reading does not by itself name its source. It could be the introduction of the public claim, a rate change, a stress episode, or a regime shift. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name attached to a projection. The essay's own stop: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 4. Why the unconditional "should" is a gap, not an evasion

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* A genuine re-census produced no figure line governing "governments should issue" or "should not issue" unconditionally. That is reported as `gap` in `01` F6. The gap is not filled by counting how many agencies have published on CBDC; that is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

The missing thing can still be characterised in effect terms: an observation that would distinguish "the settlement asset stayed an anchor and the cascade was bounded" from "the issuer became a frame and the cascade amplified". Characterising it does not name it, and pretending otherwise would be exactly the error the README forbids.

## 5. The "shape, not a name" rule is narrower than it first appears

The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, one not held in Γ. The six terms of the substrate are not at issue here, and the monetary atoms (a claim, a unit of account, a debtor, finality) are already in the frame's library. So naming which bond holds between a public claim and a deposit is a **binding** onto held atoms, provenanced as such, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding adds an atom (Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*) or that the framework itself selected it. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this package

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* says: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of agencies or famous economists who endorse issuance is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and a refuter attached.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06` and `09` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of whether governments should issue digital money, and they cannot settle it.
