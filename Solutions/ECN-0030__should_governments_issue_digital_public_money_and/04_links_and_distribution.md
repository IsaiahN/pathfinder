## What the wider literature does and does not do

The central banking sources (BIS and ECB) share an evidence pool. They agree with the figure derivation on the anchor role and the deposit-flight cascade, but that agreement is one witness with several pages, not four witnesses (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

The two low-overlap sources are genuinely different fields. Acemoglu et al. work on interbank networks and phase transitions; MathisonSlee et al. work on social-ecological resilience. Neither draws on the figures. They corroborate the *mechanism shapes* (valence reversal, cross-scale cascade), not the monetary verdict. They cannot tell us whether a given jurisdiction should issue.

No source conflicts with the figure verdict, so the conflict rule (return to the domain census) was not triggered. Two tensions were noted and handled in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6 rather than treated as conflicts.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52**: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Lead with the conditional shape and its refuter. Send the architecture only after a reader has recorded a stage-1 verdict.
- **Fig 2 :45**: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* Ask what the reader would expect to see if the shape were true, before giving them the figures.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 7 :13**: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Passing the packet along a chain of agreeing central bankers adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
- **Seat Map :163**: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients by their likely agreement on public money manufactures the disqualifying correlation.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the conditional shape and the three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then offer the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The three-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground: does the anchor stay fixed under issuer discretion, and does the cascade enumerate before introduction?
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If curious, read the theory that produced it. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question (Formula, "Direction").

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 works only if the output survives. As written in the literature, most CBDC proposals have not fixed a refuter before deployment (audit F7), so the first task is to attach one.
