# Expert verification packet: the Tether on digital public money

**What this is:** a claim in a form a monetary economist, payment specialist or financial-stability analyst can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape and per-domain verdicts, `general` on the method, not `grounded`.** No post-issuance measurement is claimed. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "should governments issue digital public money, and how might that affect commercial banks and financial stability?" is a union compound, not one yes or no. The figure-derived shape is: a public settlement object is admissible only while it remains an anchor whose value and access do not change because any frame thinks otherwise; the effect on banks is a contact cascade of three orders (holdings, deposit funding, credit and systemic stability), which must be enumerated before introduction and which a pilot cannot prove; and the new claim is an isomer of the commercial bank deposit over the same atomic set, differing in the bond of debtor, finality and par convertibility. The unconditional "should" is a declared `gap`: no figure line settles it, and only a ground reading can. Three refuters were written before the derivation.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: Framework-internal and design-facing; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** an issuer that can change who holds the money, how much, or what it can be used for turns the settlement convention into an outcome-deciding verdict, and thereby loses the anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*
- **What to check:** is the anchor property being attributed to the right object? The claim is about the settlement property, not about the identity of the issuer. A public issuer can hold the seat if the property does not update.
- **Refuter:** a design and measurement showing that issuer discretion leaves settlement value, access and par convertibility unchanged, and that no outcome is decided by the convention.

### C2: Bank-facing; checkable against payment and funding data

- **Statement:** the effect is a three-order contact cascade, and a sandbox proves wiring rather than live behaviour.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52-54: *"Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :61: *"You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat"*
- **What to check:** are the three orders distinct observables (holdings, funding, credit/stability), or are they one measure relabelled? If they share a single evidence pool, their agreement is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- **Refuter:** post-issuance data showing no measurable movement in deposit stability, funding costs or credit volumes with holding limit and remuneration held constant, or second- and third-order effects with no first-order change in holdings (which would require a common-cause frame).

### C3: Framework-internal derivation with a monetary check

- **Statement:** a public digital claim and a commercial bank deposit are isomers over the same atomic set; the bond (debtor, finality, par convertibility) carries the behavioural difference that the ingredient list hides.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 13 :115: *"The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists."* Fig 6 :26: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*
- **What to check:** can both objects be written over the same set {a claim, a unit of account, a debtor, settlement finality, par convertibility} with only the bond changed? If the weaker functional-group reading is the accurate one, say so.
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that the new form needs a primitive absent from the existing monetary system, for example a unit not convertible at par with the established unit. This kills the isomer claim.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "issue" and not "do not issue". The unconditional normative is a `gap`.
- Not a number, a sign, or a jurisdiction-specific prediction.
- Not that banks are harmed by public money, or that any actor has a standing valence (Fig 11 :49, :51).
- Not that a public digital claim is a new primitive, and not that it is just cash digitised. It is an isomer with a different bond.
- Not that a pilot transfers to a live habitat.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settled any of this.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Anchor test:** does issuer discretion move settlement value, access or par convertibility, and is any outcome decided by the convention?
2. **Cascade test:** with limit and remuneration held constant, which of the three orders moves after introduction, and by how much?
3. **Isomer test:** is any new primitive required?
4. **Reversal test:** does a stress episode flip the sign of a design feature that looked stabilising in calm conditions? (Acemoglu et al. 2015 is secondary corroboration for this mechanism, not a figure line.)

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29 and :33, Fig 10 :47, Fig 11 :61 and :70, Fig 12 :117. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge of bank funding: are holdings, funding and stability three distinct observables?
3. Name one observation that would discriminate a source-driven deposit outflow from a common-cause outflow, or say why none is available.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
