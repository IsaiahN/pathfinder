## Restatement as an effect

The question asks whether a state actor should add a new kind of money to a monetary habitat, and what changes in contact when it does. The two limbs are not one question. Option A: issue, and accept whatever contact cascade follows. Option B: withhold, and accept whatever the current two-tier arrangement already does. What an answer would look like is bound here as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual (Formula, "Direction"): a conditional rule of the form "issuance is admissible if the settlement asset stays an anchor, the contact cascade is enumerated before introduction, and the new form is declared as a rearrangement rather than a new primitive, with a named ground test." That shape, not a yes or no, is what this package derives.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that governments should issue digital public money.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reason is structural, not cosmetic. Fig 2 :35 says *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40 says *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for receptiveness to a verdict on issuance manufactures exactly the correlation that makes the agreement worthless. We look for ground-visible, opt-in venues instead.

Legally, unsolicited bulk email to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public submission and consultation routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape, record the answer before showing the framework, and never pool later agreement as corroboration.
