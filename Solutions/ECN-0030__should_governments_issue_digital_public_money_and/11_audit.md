- **D2 Banking habitat.** Actors: banks, depositors, borrowers. Currency: deposits, funding, credit. Anchor: observed deposit and funding data. Admitted.
- **D3 Systemic network.** Actors: institutions, rails, overseers. Currency: contagion and trust. Anchor: stress events and spreads. Admitted.
- **D4 Configuration.** Actors: rulebook authors. Currency: the bond between holder and debtor. Anchor: post-issuance measurement. Admitted.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 and :33 (anchor), Fig 11 :52-54 and :61 (cascade and habitat), Fig 12 :117 (isomer), Fig 9 :32 (shape not verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: keep the settlement asset an anchor, enumerate the cascade, call the new form an isomer, leave the unconditional "should" as a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete policy reading: issue a retail public money with a holding cap, zero remuneration and an intermediated custody arrangement.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the anchor**, which the shorthand leaves as a property but a policy reading tends to reattach to the issuer;
- the **source discrimination** needed to attribute a deposit outflow to the introduction rather than to a common cause, which the shorthand calls for but no configured instrument supplies;
- the **operand type of the par comparison** at second order, which the Operators table restricts to two quantities (discussed in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3).

One thing survives cleanly: a new settlement object changes contact first in holdings, then in bank funding, then in systemic stability, and the unconditional normative cannot be settled by the figures. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was not measured numerically, because no ground reading was used; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
