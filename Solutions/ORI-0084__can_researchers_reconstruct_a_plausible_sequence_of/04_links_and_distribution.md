### Two findings that bear directly on the outreach claim

1. **The Miller–Urey page is the cleanest public example of the residual.** It treats monomer synthesis as accomplished and polymerisation as *"the next step of prebiotic chemistry schemes."* That is exactly the handoff language the claim asks a reader to check: one demonstrated class of reactions is not a connected route to the next class. Use this as the friendly entry example, not as proof of any particular pathway.
2. **Assembly theory is a useful independent measure, with a recorded dispute.** Its definition of assembly index as a shortest-path step count means the number of connected steps is a measurable property of a molecule, which supports treating a route as an object. But the same page records rebuttals to the claim that only life exceeds a given index. Cite the measurement idea, not the biosignature verdict.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a finished pathway list before anyone has described a handoff sends a recording. Lead with the route shape and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see at a broken handoff before naming a pathway.
- **Fig 2 :40** - **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A send list selected for people who already like the framing manufactures the disqualifying correlation.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the route-shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at a handoff; (3) record their verdict before showing any framework material; (4) only then offer the three primary links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (and the optional third)

1. **Stage 1:** the handoff claim plus the pre-registered refuter. No framework material. The recipient checks the output without holding the interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the primary links. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded; later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 claim survives. The refuters are in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`; if one fires, withdraw the claim before sending anything.

## What is deliberately not done here

No personal emails of named individuals, no receptiveness ranking, no bulk-send plan. The ISSOL public contact page was opened and does list named staff addresses; they are deliberately not reproduced in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. Use public society channels, submission portals and opt-in competitions instead. Legally, scraped personal emails for unsolicited outreach breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions; that is a practical failure on top of the structural one (Fig 2 :35).
