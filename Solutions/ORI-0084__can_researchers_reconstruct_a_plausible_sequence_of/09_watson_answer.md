## VERDICT (from the figures)

A connected prebiotic sequence is reconstructible as an object, and the figures specify what makes it one: ordered production and sequence bonds between settled reaction products, a measured residual at each handoff, an environment enumerated rather than composed, and a cycle when self-sustaining continuation is claimed. More isolated demonstrations are necessary atoms but not a sufficient route, because a growing library is not a growing reach and a set of ingredients cannot name a route. What is reconstructed is a **shape** any plausible route must have, not the unique route early Earth took: different routes can arrive at the same substance, so the product reading cannot name its source. The result is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the chemistry-facing predictions, with no `grounded` claim. Absence of a complete route in the literature is not proof none existed.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The route claim names each handoff and carries a pre-registered refuter, so a failure is found at a slot rather than diffused through a narrative. The real cost is presenting a recording (a list of successful occasions) as a generator (a checkable route), which Fig 4 :25 calls out, and spending the independence of outside readers by asking for agreement before measurement. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Both costs are avoidable by leading with the handoff claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A route claim can be withdrawn or reordered without touching the settled reactions beneath it; only the bonds and the slot assignments change. Nothing in the six-term substrate has to be abandoned. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected route diagram with a new falsifier.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Time-resolved per-intermediate measurement showing a handoff that never forms or persist, an order-swap experiment showing order is not load-bearing, a closure test failing for a self-sustaining claim, or a discriminating instrument that selects one isomeric route over another would move the specific claim. Deriving one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or showing that a list under the symmetric conjunction fully determines a route, would move the structural claim. Prestige, citation counts and reviewer consensus are not such instruments.
