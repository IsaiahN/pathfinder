## 0. The question under audit, in effect terms

> Source: `Questions/origins of life.txt` line 116, section 9.
> **Question:** Can researchers reconstruct a plausible sequence of connected steps rather than demonstrate isolated reactions?

Stated as a choice with consequences:

- **Option A:** Yes, by accumulating more isolated reaction demonstrations and arranging them as a pathway. Consequence: the handoffs between steps stay unmeasured, so a step whose product never reaches the next step is silently counted as a link.
- **Option B:** A connected sequence is a different object from a list of reactions. It requires ordered bonds, a per-slot ground reading at each handoff, an enumerated shared habitat, and a cycle if self-sustaining continuation is claimed. Consequence: the pathway is checkable, and its plausibility is a shape, not a settlement.

An answer would look like: a bindable ordinary term, `ROUTE(start -> ... -> target)`, with one slot per intermediate, a typed bond between consecutive slots, a measured yield and persistence per slot, and a stated falsifier. Binding that term is the framework's "Direction" discipline (Formula step "Direction"): *make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM* so it can accrue a residual like anything else.

## 1. Residual, arity, symmetry, scale

**R (the residual).** The gap between *a reaction demonstrated in isolation* and *a route that could run as one connected sequence under one plausible environment*. Expressed as a per-slot quantity: for each intermediate `i`, what failed to resolve is whether the product of step `i` is actually present, in sufficient amount, long enough, to be the input of step `i+1`, without a side reaction removing it.

- **Arity.** N, the number of steps. Each step consumes the output of the previous one. This is a chain (the degenerate bonded arrangement, Fig 12 :48), not a bag.
- **Symmetry.** Broken. The sequence bond `→` is ordered: *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"* (Fig 12 :72). The production bond `⇒` is ordered: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"* (Fig 12 :77). The flat list uses the symmetric conjunction `+`, under which permutations are one term (Fig 12 :92-93). The question's word *sequence* is precisely the demand for an ordered bond.
- **Scale.** Molecular/pathway scale here. The sibling question 85 (scaling from a tiny controlled system to a geologically realistic environment) is the membrane crossing (Fig 4). It is a separate loop and is not answered in this folder.

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A pathway reported as a single overall yield has thrown away the only signal that says which handoff failed.

## 4. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 A list of reactions cannot name a route (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 12 :111: **"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."**
Fig 12 :112-113: **"A system holding only ingredients can say what a situation contains and cannot say what would count as getting somewhere from it."**
Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**

A catalogue of isolated reactions is a vocabulary of states. It can say what each flask contained. It cannot say which output feeds which next input, because that is carried entirely by the bond. The two bonds a route needs are defined and priced:

- Fig 12 :72: **"A then B; B needs A to have happened"** (sequence, ordered).
- Fig 12 :77: **"A produces B; B did not exist before"** (production, ordered).
- Fig 12 :92-93: **"Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term, so a code that charges for the sequence overcharges by log₂(k!) while the bond term undercharges."** A "one-pot list of all reactions" is the symmetric `+` reading. It declares the permutations equivalent when in chemistry they are not.

Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**

**Refuter:** exhibit a notation of isolated reactions that fully determines a connected route, including order and every handoff, with no ordered bond anywhere. That would require order to be recoverable from the ingredient set, contradicting Fig 12 :92-93.

### F2 Settled reactions are necessary, not sufficient (triangulated)

Fig 12 :104: **"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom."**
Fig 5 :72: **"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."**

Each isolated demonstration that pays against the ground is admitted as an atom. That is what makes composition possible. But the library and the reach are two quantities. A database of demonstrated reactions is a growing library. The set of connected routes composable from it is the reach, and it is derived, not stored. Formula step 8: *"IT IS THE REACH THAT DRIVES THE LOOP, NOT THE LIBRARY."*

Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."** If a needed intermediate is not in the held set, no amount of rearranging demonstrations yields the route. What is owed is an import (a new reaction, a new condition), with a source gate and shadow test.

**Refuter:** show that reach is identical to library size, so every settled reaction is automatically composable into every route; or show a route composed entirely from reactions that never paid against the ground.

### F3 The handoff is a per-slot residual (single-source for the figure, ground-visible in principle)

Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

For step `i -> i+1`, the live mass is the concentration of intermediate `i` at the moment step `i+1` needs it. A pathway reported as total conversion can hide a step whose intermediate never accumulates. This is not a philosophical preference: it is a measurement instruction with an instrument (in situ or quench-and-measure at each handoff).

**Refuter:** demonstrate a route that runs reproducibly while every intermediate concentration at every handoff is unmeasured, and show that the total-yield figure is sufficient to diagnose a broken handoff. If it is, per-slot indexing buys nothing for this quantity.

### F4 The habitat is enumerated, never composed (single-source)

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**
Fig 11 :40: **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**
Fig 11 :52: **"And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles"**

The environment that makes a route run is read off the world: water activity, mineral surfaces, wet-dry cycling, UV, concentration. It is not derived from the reaction list. Fig 11 :70: **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."** A pathway that works only under a laboratory-specific condition has demonstrated wiring; the geological case is question 85 and stays open.

**Refuter:** derive a complete prebiotic environment from the reaction list alone, or demonstrate the same route in a substituted habitat with no domain loss and no inherited actors.

### F5 A self-sustaining route is a cycle, not a chain (single-source, world-corroborated)

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**
Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

If the claim is only "a product can be made by a connected route," a chain suffices. If the claim is "the route continues without external replenishment of its catalyst," the object required is a cycle with a sign that closes. Fig 12 :48: **"A chain is the degenerate case: one bond type, no branching."** The autocatalysis and formose sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are world corroboration for this structural requirement, not its source.

**Refuter:** exhibit a strictly linear sequence that regenerates its own starting catalyst without any loop, with the regeneration measured. Then a chain closes by construction and this finding falls.

### F6 The question is a union compound (single-source)

Fig 9 :54 and :55 (quoted in §2). A pathway is a conjunction of conditions with binding intersections, so optimizing each isolated reaction separately does not optimize the route. The residue after the easy steps is categorically different: it is a specific compound, not a harder case of either step.

**Refuter:** show that a route's behavior is the conjunction of its isolated steps' behaviors, with no interaction term at the intersections, for the system at issue.

### F7 Plausibility returns a shape, not a unique pathway (triangulated)

Fig 13 :121: **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**
Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**
Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

The word *a* in the question is important. The framework can return the shape any candidate route must have (ordered handoffs, per-slot settlement, enumerated habitat, closure if self-sustaining). It cannot select the unique route early Earth took, because multiple routes compose to the same product (isomerism) and the ground would have to discriminate them.

**Refuter:** derive a unique pathway from the feedstocks and the six-term substrate alone, showing that no alternative ordered composition reaches the same product.

### F8 The ground, not agreement, decides (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**
Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
Fig 10 :52: **"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."**
Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."**

A reviewer panel, a citation count, and a shared textbook are frames. The anchor is the measurement at the handoff. Agreement among people who read the same review is one evidence pool talking (Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**).

**Refuter:** show a case where agreement among pathway proposers, with no new measurement, settles which route actually ran.

### F9 Absence of a reconstructed route is not proof none existed (single-source)

Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**
Fig 9 :44: **"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."**
Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."**

A literature with no complete pathway is an observation about the literature and the current instruments, not about early Earth. The honest report is `unreachable at this budget` or `candidate`, with the missing handoff characterised in effect terms.

**Refuter:** produce a sealed frame in prebiotic chemistry whose closure can be searched to the end, and complete the search.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before outreach: a falsifier written in advance is a design instrument, not only an honesty device. The shape claim of this folder is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A notation of isolated reactions determines a connected route with order and all handoffs, with no ordered bond.
2. A successful route is demonstrated in which an intermediate at a handoff is never observed forming or persisting, and the total-yield measurement still diagnoses the broken handoff (breaks F3).
3. A strictly linear route is shown to be self-sustaining with no regenerated catalyst or closed loop (breaks F5).
4. A unique pathway is derived from the feedstocks and substrate alone, with no alternative ordered composition reaching the same product (breaks F7).
5. Agreement among frames, with no new ground reading, settles which route ran on early Earth (breaks F8).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn and the verdict re-derived from the census. Until one is run, nothing here is labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Report a pathway as a route: name each handoff, the bond type between slots, and the per-slot yield and persistence (F1, F3).
2. Unbundle the union before searching. Ask which condition is missing at the broken handoff, then look for the frame that predicts its shape (F6, Fig 9 :27-28).
3. Keep settlement and composition as separate quantities. Do not let a growing reaction database stand in for a growing reach (F2).
4. Enumerate the habitat; do not compose it, and do not present a flask-only result as a geological one (F4).
5. If continuation is claimed, show the cycle and its sign (F5).
6. State the mode, and say `candidate` until a ground reading at the handoffs pays. Ask for the refutation, not for agreement (F8).
7. Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."** Send the route structure and the refuter, not the list of successful occasions.

## 8. Verdict of the audit

The figures give a conditional yes and a structural specification. Connected routes are reconstructible as objects: they are compositions of settled reactions under ordered bonds, with a per-slot ground reading, an enumerated habitat, and a cycle when continuation is claimed. But a reconstructed *plausible* route is a shape and remains `candidate` until the handoffs are measured. The framework returns the form any candidate route must have, not the unique route early Earth took. Nothing here is `grounded`, and no measurement was taken.
