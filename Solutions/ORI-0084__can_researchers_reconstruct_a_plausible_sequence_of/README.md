## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Adding reviewers raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: **"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** The outside frame must not hold the interiors. The public ISSOL contact page was opened and does list named staff emails; they are deliberately not reproduced in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to scraped personal emails breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## The one-line verdict

A connected prebiotic route is a different object from a list of isolated reactions: ordered bonds, a measurement at every handoff, an enumerated habitat and a cycle if continuation is claimed. The figures license that shape and no unique pathway. `candidate`, not `grounded`.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute them at a handoff, not a purchased list of believers.
