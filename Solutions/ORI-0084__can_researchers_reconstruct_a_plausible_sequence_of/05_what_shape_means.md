## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*; the figure goes on to say it cannot name the missing primitive, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for ORI-0084 a **shape** is the narrowed form of a route that can be stated before the specific pathway is named. It constrains and does not fill. Concretely, the shape is:

> an ordered chain of N steps, each intermediate a slot with a measured yield and persistence, consecutive slots joined by production/sequence bonds, the conditions enumerated from the habitat rather than composed, and a closing loop if the claim is self-sustaining continuation.

That is a shape. "The formose reaction ran on the Hadean Earth and produced the ribose that entered RNA" is a name, and it is not licensed by the figures alone.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with the measured handoff, not with the name of a famous pathway.
- Fig 9 :30-31: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** Say "the intermediate is absent at the handoff," not "the network lacked the right catalyst."
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* For a pathway, this is the point at which the route you can state is the route you can measure.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* An instrument at each handoff that shows the intermediate forming and persisting would name that link.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A reaction demonstrated in a different lab under a different condition enters this route as an import and must be recorded as such.

The canonical limits matter here: the "shape, not a name" phrase governs a *missing* primitive. The bonds and the reaction vocabulary are already held, so saying "use a sequence bond" is not naming a missing thing; it is selecting an operator the library already contains. What cannot be done is naming the specific unique early-Earth route from the figures.

## 4. Why a product reading does not determine a route (the source error)

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

This transfers directly to prebiotic pathway reconstruction without metaphor:

- Finding a molecule in a flask (or in a meteorite) is a reading of a product.
- Several connected routes can produce the same product (Fig 13 :121: **"Different routes can arrive at the same substance."**).
- Therefore the product reading cannot name the route. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates among sources at the handoffs, not a preferred route asserted from the endpoint.

The essay's own resolution is methodological: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the colour case. The analogue here is time-resolved, per-intermediate measurement under one habitat, not a better story about the pathway.

## 5. Q: does "reconstruct a plausible sequence" demand a name? ANSWERED

No. The word *plausible* is the giveaway. A plausible sequence is a shape whose handoffs are individually checkable and whose habitat is enumerated; it is not an assertion that this is the route that ran. Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** Two routes with the same feedstocks and different bond order stand to each other as isomers: the endpoint formula cannot tell them apart, and the behaviour can.

The defensible claim is therefore: *a route of this shape is reconstructible and checkable; which of the competing routes ran is a ground question and remains open.* See `06_isomers_and_order.md` for the falsifier.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 (carried from the exemplar)

I initially wrote "the framework returns a shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any step. That is too strong, for the reason the exemplar records: the rule governs a primitive not held in Γ. Here the sequence and production bonds, and the atoms, are held. Selecting and naming a measured handoff is legitimate. What remains forbidden by Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :121 is the claim that the figures alone select a unique pathway. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A pathway claim endorsed by famous names is funded, not settled. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any prebiotic route and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
