## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". A shape narrows; it does not fill.

From *The Library of Babel*, read this session: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And the stopping rule: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Applied here: the shape is not "use a particular causal discovery algorithm". It is a set of constraints any answer must satisfy: a loop with acting in it, a per-slot residual ranged against a non-updating ground, an ordered production relation, a refusal operator, and an agent-and-habitat unit of capability. Any system meeting those constraints is a candidate; the figures do not pick one.

## 2. How the shape is obtained: proximity, then recursion

*The Library of Babel* gives the method. Use the words you already have that are nearest the thing, then "connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with." Each new term narrows the space. The stop is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place".

Fig 6 :30 gives the same rule in effect terms: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

In this question I described the residual as a slot-indexed gap that acting can close, before naming do-calculus or interventional equivalence classes. That order is the one Fig 8 :53 requires: "Residual first, frame second."

## 3. The figure's own correction to my earlier scope of "shape, not a name"

I initially treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on naming any component. That is too strong, and the canonical phrase is explicit that the subject is a **missing** thing. In this question the production bond, negation, and the loop are all already in the framework. Naming them is not invention; it is reading held atoms.

What remains forbidden is naming a seventh primitive, an architecture, or a ground-level success that the framework did not derive. Fig 9 :32 governs those. I keep both forms visible: the narrow canonical one (missing primitive) and the broader application here (no figure-derived architecture or settled capability). Neither is silently erased.

## 4. What licenses a name: an instrument, not an argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the sharpest case. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source". Yellow plus blue, red plus green plus blue, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The case was settled "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument", the spectrometer.

For causal learning the analogue is direct. An observational distribution is a projection. Several directed graphs produce the same independence statements. Naming one as the cause from that reading alone repeats the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, and intervention is the operation that supplies a second reading. This is why the answer in `07_the_tether_answer.md` refuses to name a specific system as the one that can do this, and states what instrument would name it.

## 5. Why contact is the measure, and why this document is not ground

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." It also repeats Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." The do-operation matters here precisely because it changes contact; it is not a cleverer way to fit the same data.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And the caveat that binds this whole solution: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, this file, and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency. Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." They cannot settle whether an AI system has this capacity. Only a ground reading can.

## 6. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not a ground

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".

This governs the outreach artifacts. The two-stage offer does not ask anyone to trust the framework, does not select people by likely agreement, and does not pool later agreement as corroboration. It asks for a refutation of a shape. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 7. The reading in one paragraph

For this question, "shape" means the checkable form of an answer you can state before you can name an architecture: a loop that acts as well as observes, a per-slot residual ranged against a ground that does not update, an ordered production bond that intervention can discriminate from association, a refusal operator that can withdraw a held relation, and capability located in the agent and its habitat together. It is obtained by describing the residual in effect terms before choosing a frame, by proximity and recursion rather than by analogy first, and it stops where the available instruments stop discriminating. A name is licensed only by the ground or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. This document is a simulation of that reading, not a measurement of any AI system.
