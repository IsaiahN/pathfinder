## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what makes a causal explanation more informative than a predictive correlation. Option A: "more informative" means better predictive fit, so the difference is one of degree. Option B: the difference is one of kind, a typed ordered bond, a generator's reach across contexts, and an anchor that does not move with a frame, with correlation retaining a legitimate convention role. What each leads to: A makes informativeness a frame-internal score and is refused by Fig 1 :27; B gives a checkable shape with a pre-registered refuter but stops short of naming any particular causal model.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a prediction built from co-occurrence and the observation returned when the action changes from passive observation to intervention, a new environment, or a source-discriminating instrument. Arity: two operands plus a hidden third term (confounder, mechanism or context) that a bare correlation drops. Symmetry: correlation is symmetric; causation is ordered. Scale: first order within one dataset; second order across interventions or frames.
- **Domain 1, predictive / statistical frame.** Actors: variables, observations, loss, fitted parameters. Currency: fit and compression on observed data, frame-internal. Anchor: held-out observations from the same pool, not a world-settling anchor. Question type: shares evidence with D2, so union with overlap.
- **Domain 2, causal / interventional frame.** Actors: variables, mechanisms, interventions, confounders, instruments. Currency: counterfactual reach priced in actions, plus bits for the typed bond. Anchor: the intervention outcome or discriminating instrument that does not update on the frame. Question type: adds a bond and a new anchor to D1; it is not a partition of disjoint questions.
- **Domain 3, verification / outreach frame.** Actors: expert readers, instruments, experiments. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all three domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing the frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse the fit-as-verdict reading before minting (formula step 2); settle each bond before composing over it (step 5); keep the two currencies apart (Fig 12 :125-126).

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between Fig 2 :41 (correlation efficient for convention) and Fig 12 :5 (the bond is the missing half) is resolved by re-census. Fig 2 governs the valence of agreement by use; Fig 12 governs what a composition carries. Different quantities, so no hidden strain after re-census. The live strain, the operand type of the comparison bond at second order, is named in the TETHER section.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The difference is one of kind, scoped by use. A causal explanation carries a typed, ordered bond where a correlation has symmetric co-occurrence; competing causal readings over the same observations are isomers, identical in the flat formula and decisive under intervention; and the causal claim is a generator that travels and ranges against an intervention anchor instead of against fit inside one evidence pool. Because fit is frame-internal and the two currencies do not add, "more informative" is not a scalar and is not a claim that causation beats correlation everywhere: correlation stays efficient for prediction and convention. An unpaid causal label, one with no discriminating instrument or recorded ground reading behind it, is not extra information and can be weaker than an honest correlation. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The framework returns a shape, never a named causal model.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and checkable. The packet in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` states three sub-claims with verbatim lines and refuters, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is persuading readers that a labelled causal story beats a correlation without a discriminating instrument; by Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong", that cost should be bought down with interventions, not with agreement.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape can be narrowed by removing the generator or anchor clauses without touching the operator distinction; no primitive must be withdrawn. A published claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and the refuter is written down so the reversal is visible rather than silent.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Show that observed joint distributions determine `P(Y | do(X))` for all interventions at issue (kills the operator distinction); that a correlation-only model transfers and acts as well as the causal one (kills the reach claim); that agreement within one evidence pool settles a causal verdict (kills the ground claim); or that bond cost and intervention cost add in one currency that reproduces the ranking (kills the domain split). Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
