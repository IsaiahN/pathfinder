facilitating disease, invasive species, or other harms?

## The residual in one sentence

A connectivity change reduces the residual of a focal species (movement, gene flow, rescue) and
reduces the residual of a harm actor (pathogen, invader, competitor) at the same time, because both
are users of the same contact. The figure-governed answer is not a named kind of corridor. It is a
shape: valence is relative to a residual, the help and harm conditions overlap as a union compound,
contact cascades outward, and the ground decides. The honest stop is `gap` for a standing safe list.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that more connectivity is good.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a
   verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Gathering more conservationists
   who like corridors adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless
   when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A recipient set chosen for receptiveness
   manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
3. **Sending the source before the residual spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that
   can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So stage 1 carries the residual and its refuter
   only.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email selected by receptiveness breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in
many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of
the structural one.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground
reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): LLMs *"can quickly
simulate an approximate answer/output"* but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived
from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the ecology question
it reports on. It can only state the shape any answer must have and the instruments that would
close it.
