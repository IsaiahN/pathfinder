## 2. What the wider literature contributes (secondary only)

The figures decide the verdict. These sources corroborate or sharpen it, and their overlap is
recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

1. **Species-specificity of the structure.** McRae et al. 2012 says barrier identity and impact
   *"will differ among species"*. Wikipedia's landscape-connectivity article says quantification
   is *"organism-, process- and landscape-specific"*. These are two different instruments (a
   modeling paper and an encyclopedic synthesis) but they share the landscape-ecology pool; treat
   them as one witness for Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 11's valence rule.
2. **The harm uses the same structure.** The ASF studies (Faustini 2025, Goicolea 2024) use
   connectivity models built for conservation to predict pathogen spread. They share an author
   and a modeling tradition, so they are one witness, not two. They corroborate the union-compound
   reading in F2: the same link serves D1 and D2.
3. **Dispersal mode flips the sign.** Sullivan et al. 2011 is the lowest-overlap witness: a
   replicated field experiment where corridors increased biotically dispersed parasites but not
   abiotically dispersed ones. That is exactly the claim that valence is a relation, not a kind.
4. **Removal is not free.** Wikipedia's invasive-species article says many invaders become
   essential and *"their removal could be harmful"*, and Dong et al. 2025 notes that management
   works through targeted intervention in less favourable landscapes. Both corroborate F6's
   substitution/asymmetry claim at world level, but neither derives it.
5. **A terminology caution.** The invasive-species article notes that *"invasive"* is *"poorly
   defined and often very subjective"*. This is a world-level warning that calling an actor a harm
   is itself a residual-relative act, matching Fig 11 :49-51. It does not change the figure verdict.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Stage 1 carries the
  four-part shape and the five refuters. Sending the framework first converts a potential checker
  into a reader of our interior.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who
  has absorbed the whole architecture before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :30-31** - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
  Ask what they would expect to observe if the shape claim were true, before giving them the
  figures.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where
  the ground votes."* No mass send selected for people likely to agree. One-to-one, opt-in venues
  only.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a
  failed conservation design, so independence is worth paying for.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the four-part shape; (2) ask what they would
count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three source pointers above, with
the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to
reuse.

## 4. The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** residual, shape claim, five refuters. The recipient checks the output without
   holding the framework's interior. Verdict recorded first (Fig 10 :52, Fig 2 :45).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the source pointers, if they are curious. Their later agreement is
   never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the
   agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a generator (Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head
   starts come down."*), with the instruction that each user states their own ground and writes
   their own refuter before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As written, the
shape claim is a `gap` for a named safe kind and a `specified` structural claim otherwise. It does
not assert any corridor design. Do not strengthen it into a recommendation during outreach.
