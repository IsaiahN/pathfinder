## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, local copy read this
session) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a
shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. The local
figure text at Fig 13 says the system *"can report that it lacks something rather than producing an
answer anyway"* and that *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel* (opened this session): *"you can cut your search short if the set of
books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that
satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For ECO-0053:

- *"a list of safe corridor types"* is a name.
- *"connectivity has no sign apart from a residual; name the actor, the residual, the arrangement
  and the cascade distance"* is a shape.

The shape is what survives the figures; the name is what the ground has to pay for.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for
  recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you
  are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast
  it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather
  than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened this session): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can
  send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against
  the world."*
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the
  paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
  *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*
  That last sentence is fig 9's union rule in essay form and governs this question directly.

Applied here: we described the residual in effect terms (focal residual down, harm residual down,
same contact) before choosing any figure line or any literature. The shape came out of that
description, not out of a preference for corridors.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by
   what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The usage rule says the framework *"stops at the limit of
   what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (exemplar-verified) sharpens this: *"The real import is
not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable
set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's
minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral.
It makes the choice visible."*

For ECO-0053 this means: a world study showing that a particular corridor design helped a focal
species without moving any enumerated harm residual is a **ground settlement** or a **provenanced
import**, depending on how it was obtained. Until such a reading exists, naming that design is the
searcher's contribution and must be recorded as such. Note the standing caution from the
invasive-species source (opened this session): even the label *"invasive"* is *"poorly defined and
often very subjective"*, so naming a harm actor is itself a residual-relative act.

## 4. Why a "safe corridor type" would be the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an
instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*. *"If several paths create the same
behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't
distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an
argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A connectivity metric (a graph centrality, a least-cost distance, a hectares-connected figure)
reads one number where several sources could give it: focal movement, pathogen movement, invasion,
or all three. Naming the source as "safe connectivity" from that reading is the metamerism error.
What is owed is a better instrument, not a name. This is the essay-level reason behind F1 and F9 in
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"the output is only as good as the
validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Q for this question - ANSWERED, no further essay needed

**What does "shape" mean for a question about kinds of connectivity?** It means the four-part
constraint that any answer must state before it can be checked: arrangement, actor, residual, and
cascade distance. The framework cannot supply the fourth part's value for a given landscape, and it
cannot name a standing safe kind. It can say that valence is a relation (Fig 11 :49-51), that the
question is a union compound (Fig 9 :54-58), that residuals are per slot (Fig 1 :19), and that
isolation substitutes rather than removes (Fig 11 :66-67). Those are constraints; the named
corridor design is the ground's to pay for.

**Does the question as asked commit the forbidden error?** Yes. It asks for a class of connectivity
that is safe by construction, which is a name where the framework returns a shape. The correct
form is not "kind K is safe" but *"for actor A and residual R, arrangement S changes contact out to
cascade distance D, and here is what would refute that."*

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 (kept visible)

I initially treated the phrase *"shape, not a name"* as if it forbade naming any component of an
answer. It does not. The canonical phrase governs **a missing thing**, a primitive not held in the
library. Network, gradient and spectrum are all already among the six substrate terms (Fig 13), and
corridors, stepping stones and resistance surfaces are ordinary ecology vocabulary. Naming them is
not the error.

The error is naming the **source of a reading** (this metric means "safe connectivity") without an
instrument that discriminates among sources, and claiming that a standing list survives a change of
residual. I correct §3 accordingly: the ban is on unlicensed naming from a projection and on
standing valence lists, not on using ordinary ecological terms. Both the earlier and the corrected
reading are recorded here so neither is quietly lost. This is the same correction the exemplar made
in its `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b about Gradient and Spectrum, reached independently for this
question's vocabulary.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session):
*"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an
action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground
can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers
conservation authorities to agree that connectivity is good is the named collapse. The legitimate
ask gives the shape a budget to be tried, and lets an instrument settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output"*; *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real
architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated
derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the
ecology question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
