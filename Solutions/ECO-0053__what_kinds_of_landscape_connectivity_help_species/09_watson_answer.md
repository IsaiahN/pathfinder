## QUESTION

 Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question: what kinds of landscape
 connectivity help species move without also facilitating disease, invasive species, or other harms?
 Option A: answer with a standing list of safe corridor types. Option B: answer with the shape any
 valid answer must have and record the named kind as a gap. What each leads to: A names a source
 from a reading that cannot discriminate among sources, spends the independence of the people
 checking it, and ignores that the same link bears both the benefit and the harm; B gives the
 four-part shape, five refuters, the union reading, the substitution ledger and the instruments
 that would close the gap. Figures decide; world sources corroborate but never overrule.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

 R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition
 or union, and order by dependency.

 - **R (the residual).** A connectivity change reduces the focal residual (isolation, lost gene
   flow, failed recolonisation) and, through the same contact, reduces a harm residual (pathogen,
   vector, invader, competitor) relative to the conservation goal. Arity: two conditions borne by
   one structure, therefore a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two conditions is not a
   harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*). Symmetry:
   the link is physically symmetric, its valence is not (Fig 11 :50). Scale: first order is focal
   movement, second and third order are transmission, establishment and trophic effects (Fig 11
   :52-53, DDT and the eagles).
 - **Domain 1, movement and gene flow.** Actors: focal species, patches, dispersers, alleles.
   Currency: movement, gene flow, colonisation, demographic rescue. Anchor: occupancy and genetic
   assignment data. Question type: one condition.
 - **Domain 2, harm and contagion.** Actors: pathogens, vectors, invasive plants, predators,
   competitors. Currency: transmission and establishment. Anchor: disease incidence and invasion
   records. Question type: one condition, negative valence relative to D1.
 - **Domain 3, the connector / landscape pattern.** Actors: corridors, stepping stones, matrix
   resistance, edges, barriers. Currency: contact created or restored. Anchor: the landscape read
   off the world (Fig 11 :43-48). Question type: a structure, a composition of state and network.
 - **Domain 4, management and the seat.** Actors: planners, agencies, landowners. Currency:
   introductions, restorations, substitutions. Anchor: monitored outcomes. Question type:
   asymmetric intervention, introduction available and exclusion not (Fig 11 :61).
 - **Domain 5, verification / outreach.** Actors: expert readers and venue reviewers. Currency:
   agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all five
   domains draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.

 Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat (perception), name the bonds (vocabulary), state which
 residual counts as progress (objective), choose an intervention (planning), monitor and carry
 (Fig 3 :19, :27). The objective link is the usual break because counting structures built is
 frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).

 Strain test applied: Fig 11's "enumerate, never composed" governs the habitat list; Fig 13's
 composition rule governs state-and-structure terms. Different objects, so no strain after
 re-census. Fig 9's "unbundle" is a description move; Fig 11's "exclusion is not available" is an
 intervention constraint. Different quantities, no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

 The shape survives; the requested named kind does not. Connectivity has no sign apart from a
 residual, so an answer must name the arrangement, the actor, the residual and the cascade distance.
 The benefit and the harm conditions form a union compound, so a single "add connectivity" lever
 clears the benefit-only cases and leaves the overlap standing. Residuals are reported per slot,
 never averaged; the habitat is enumerated outward until the cascade stops mattering; and reducing
 connectivity substitutes a habitat rather than subtracting contact, with two invisible costs. A
 "kind" is a bond arrangement, and continuous versus stepping-stone corridors are functional-group
 members rather than isomers. There is no figure line that governs a standing safe kind, so that
 part of the question is `gap`, reported plainly. This is a `general`/`specified`, ungrounded
 result. The strongest single point is Fig 11 :49-51: valence is a relation to a residual, so no
 standing list of safe kinds survives a change of residual.

 - **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and the union
   reading before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is
   spending the independence of outside readers by sending a corridor recommendation instead of a
   shape; Fig 2 :42 prices that (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*).
   A second cost is practical: a disconnection advised as subtraction would inherit unchosen actors
   and two invisible costs (Fig 11 :67-69). Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and the
   substitution ledger.
 - **undoable:** yes. The named-kind claim was never made, so nothing substantive has to be
   withdrawn. The shape can be refined by adding a measured case without touching the valence or
   union rules. No primitive has to be withdrawn and no site decision is bound by this folder.
 - **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A study that measures both residuals on the
   same connectivity map for a named actor and finds a design that closes the focal residual while
   leaving every enumerated harm residual unchanged would begin to name a kind. R1 (same-sign
   effects for all actors), R2 (help and harm form a partition), R3 (no harm beyond first order),
   R4 (contact removed without substitution) and R5 (an aggregate metric sufficient for every
   residual) would each break the shape claim. A re-census that finds a figure line governing a
   standing safe kind would also reverse the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as
   `candidate`.
