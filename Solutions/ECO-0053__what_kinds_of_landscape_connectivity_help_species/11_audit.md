## 1. Independent domain census (written from the residual line alone)

I redid the census before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity,
symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order
by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is a connectivity change that lowers the focal
  movement residual and lowers a non-target harm residual through the same contact. Arity: two
  conditions borne by one structure, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57-58: *"a case in two
  conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific
  compound."*). Symmetry: the link is symmetric, the valence is not; the same link is a benefactor
  to one residual and a malfactor to another (Fig 11 :50). Scale: first order is focal movement,
  second and third order are transmission, establishment and trophic effects, as with DDT and the
  eagles (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Domain 1, movement and gene flow.** Actors: focal species, patches, dispersers, alleles.
  Currency: movement, gene flow, colonisation. Anchor: occupancy and genetic assignment data.
  Question type: one condition.
- **Domain 2, harm and contagion.** Actors: pathogens, vectors, invaders, competitors. Currency:
  transmission and establishment. Anchor: incidence and invasion records. Question type: one
  condition, negative valence relative to D1.
- **Domain 3, connector / landscape pattern.** Actors: corridors, stepping stones, matrix, edges,
  barriers. Currency: contact. Anchor: the landscape read off the world (Fig 11 :43-48). Question
  type: a structure, a composition of state and network.
- **Domain 4, management seat.** Actors: planners, agencies, landowners. Currency: introductions,
  restorations, substitutions. Anchor: monitored outcomes. Question type: asymmetric intervention.
- **Domain 5, verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual.
  Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence in this run: zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat, name the bonds, state which residual counts as
progress, choose an intervention, monitor and carry (Fig 3 :19, :27; Fig 11 :43-44).

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The formula defines `R_T = gap(x, (T_E o T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came
back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the
condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43-69 (habitat enumeration, valence,
   cascade, asymmetry, substitution), Fig 9 :54-58 (union compound), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot
   residual), Fig 12 :94/:111/:117/:124 (arrangement, route, isomer, functional group), Fig 13
   :23-24/:29 (network structure).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: connectivity has no sign
   apart from a residual; answer as a four-tuple; union compound; per slot; enumerate outward;
   substitution not subtraction; ground decides; named kind is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question as originally posed, "which kinds of connectivity help
   without harming?", if someone tried to read a direct answer back out of the shorthand.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **four-part tuple** collapses if the recipient hears only "connectivity has no sign" and
  forgets that an actor, residual and cascade distance must still be named;
- the **union/partition distinction** collapses into "there is a trade-off", which is a different
  claim, since a trade-off admits a single optimum and a union compound does not;
- the **substitution law** collapses into "barriers are bad" or "barriers are good", losing both
  invisible costs (what you failed to reproduce, what you unintentionally introduced).

One thing survives cleanly: a connectivity change must be described per actor and per residual
before any sign is attached. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically,
because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped
constraints. Treating the folder's shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and
would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a
recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
