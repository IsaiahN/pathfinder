# Expert verification packet: the Tether on collective behavior without shared goals, control, or common information

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in collective behavior or complex systems can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` and ungrounded on any binding to a particular system, nothing `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three non-swappable conditions plus a verdict question. Under the figure set, shared goals are not necessary (selection does the work of purpose where nothing maintains anything), central control is not necessary (actors updating on each other converge without an anchor), and a communicated plan is not necessary (agents that never talk can agree from one common cause). What cannot be removed is contact with a habitat and a ground. The coordination so produced is real but is a convention or a variation, not a verdict, so a group can be coordinated and still be wrong together. The framework returns this shape and five refuters written in advance, and stops short of naming the mechanism for any particular system.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 11

- **Statement:** none of the three named prerequisites is individually necessary for collective order; contact is the residue that cannot be removed.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :13-15 "no anchor; each updates on the others / they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence." Fig 2 :18-21 "one evidence pool / they never talk, and still agree / so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **What to check:** are the three mechanisms genuinely non-swappable, so that removing each one leaves a different case? Does the contact clause do work that the three negative verdicts do not?
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a collective pattern maintained under complete isolation, with no agent-agent contact and no shared ground contact; or derive one prerequisite from the other two with the figure's bonds. Either fires the clause.

### C2 : World-facing; checkable against the reader's own field

- **Statement:** Boids alignment, stigmergy, Vicsek alignment with noise, and spontaneous order are a **functional group** rather than isomers as written: they share local interaction, a closing loop, and ground contact, but not a full atom set. Fig 12 :124 "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- **What to check:** is the characterization faithful? Does stigmergy really coordinate through a trace with no direct communication ("without need for any planning, control, or even direct communication between the agents", stigmergy source)? Does the Vicsek model produce collective motion from local alignment plus noise with no leader term? Does spontaneous order arise from individuals "none of whom intends or designs the overall results"?
- **Refuter:** show that one mechanism requires an atom none of the others can do without, for example that no non-trace mechanism works without a persistent medium. That kills the isomer reading and leaves the functional-group claim standing. A second refuter: a field case in which the coordination demonstrably required a controller, with the ground held fixed.

### C3 : Framework-internal derivation; checkable by reading Figures 7 and 8

- **Statement:** collective agreement is not a verdict, and a shared source is not proof of independent arrival. Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 2 :39-40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."
- **What to check:** does the figure really assign opposite valence to a convention and a verdict? Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." If it does, then a flock's coherence cannot serve as evidence that the flock's rule is true.
- **Refuter:** show that one-pool agreement constitutes a verdict about the world; or prove that the record cannot in principle separate convergent derivation from adopted import. Either breaks the clause.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that real flocks, nests, or markets never have leaders. The claim is about individual necessity under a figure set, not an empirical universal.
- Not that "emergence" is a mechanism. It is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground (Fig 11 :36).
- Not that no evidence pool is present. A pool is often present; it is not a communicated plan, and it does not make the agreement a verdict.
- Not that selection is the only mechanism. Mutual update and common cause are two others in Fig 2.
- Not that any mechanism has been identified for a particular observed system. That is `gap`.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not agreement. The five refuters are in `01` §3. Short form: complete isolation with maintained coordination; goal removal with unchanged outcome and a shared goal as the only explanation; one-pool agreement as a verdict; a seventh substrate member or a size-dependent kernel; and a proof that provenance cannot separate convergence from copying. Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes
