## 0. The residual, in effect terms

We observe coordinated order at the group level: a flock turns as one, an ant trail forms a shortest path, a market prices without a planner, a herd chooses a direction. The residual is the gap between that observed order and the three prerequisites the question assumes. Stated as a residual per slot (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."):

| slot | candidate prerequisite | effect if present | effect if absent |
|---|---|---|---|
| G | shared goals | a comparison can be stated (Operators table :51-52 "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one") | no common target; only local rules and selection |
| C | central control | a seat can author conventions and rank actors | no author; order must come from contact and local update |
| I | common information | a pool can be the source of agreement | no message, no shared pool; only contact through the environment |

**Question type.** The three slots are not the same condition under different names. A goal is a comparison, control is a seat, information is an evidence pool. Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a **union of overlapping conditions**, so it gets three loops plus one meta-loop, not one lever.

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** Before deriving, write down what an answer would look like: a shape with four clauses. (1) Each of G, C, I is marked `necessary` or `not necessary` for collective order. (2) A contact clause names what cannot be removed. (3) A verdict clause separates agreement from truth. (4) A mode clause says what would move the result from `general` to `grounded`. The shape is bound now so it can accrue a residual and be refused (Fig 5 :65-66: "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else.").

Removing C alone leaves mutual update (Fig 2 :13-15); removing G alone leaves selection (Fig 11 :36); removing I alone leaves common-cause agreement (Fig 2 :18-21). Their outcomes differ, so they are separate loops. Scale: the order is produced locally (neighbor to neighbor) and read globally (flock, trail, market), crossing the membrane of Fig 4.

## 2. Findings

### F1 : The question is a union, so one lever will not clear it (governs the question type)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :55-57: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds." Fig 9 :58: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."

The Library of Babel essay says the same in prose: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."

So the three slots are not one question asked three ways. Demonstrating that a flock needs no leader does not show that a market needs no shared price memory, and the overlap (both use a shared medium) is a compound with its own loop. **Refuter:** derive central control from shared goals and common information with the figure's bonds, so the three collapse to one condition. Until then the union reading stands. Mode `general`.

### F2 : Central control is not necessary for convergence (slot C)

Fig 2 :13-15: "no anchor; each updates on the others / they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence." This is Fig 2's "collapse 1: mutual update (coherent drift)". Actors with no controller and no anchor still converge. The convergence is not truth: Fig 2 :34: "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

World corroboration, low overlap with the figures: Boids, "the complexity of Boids arises from the interaction of individual agents (the boids, in this case) adhering to a set of simple rules"; Vicsek et al., "spontaneous symmetry breaking of the rotational symmetry"; self-organization, "the resulting organization is wholly decentralized, distributed over all the components of the system."

**Verdict for C: not necessary** for collective order, `general`, **triangulated** across Fig 2 and Fig 11 (low overlap: frames/agreement versus habitat/selection). The caveat is not an afterthought: order without a ground is drift, not correction. **Refuter:** produce a case in which convergence requires an anchor that itself updates on the frames and still counts as correction. That would break Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."

### F3 : Common information is not necessary as a communicated plan, but a shared source or shared contact is (slot I)

Fig 2 :18-21: "one evidence pool / they never talk, and still agree / so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other." Two frames with no communication at all still agree because one cause produced both readings. If "common information" means a message, a plan, or a shared intention, then it is **not necessary**.

But the figure does not say there is no shared anything. "One evidence pool" is common information in the broad sense. If the question means no shared source at all, then the remaining mechanism is contact with a ground: Fig 11 :56 "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." A stigmergic trace is shared *environment*, not shared *intent*, which is why it answers the narrow reading and not the broad one.

**Verdict for I: not necessary in the communicated-plan sense; a shared pool or shared ground contact is the residue that cannot be removed.** `general` for the distinction; `specified` for classifying any particular system. **single-source** for the Fig 2 panel-2 line, with world corroboration from stigmergy ("without need for any planning, control, or even direct communication between the agents") and Vicsek local alignment. **Refuter:** show a coordinated pattern maintained with no shared medium, no common source, and no contact with a shared ground.

### F4 : Shared goals are not necessary; selection does the work of purpose (slot G)

Fig 11 :36: "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran." Apparent directedness is a label applied after the fact. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." A goal is a comparison (Operators table :51-52), a specific operator, not a prerequisite for composition.

World corroboration, low overlap: spontaneous order, "the emergence of large-scale patterns of social coordination from the actions of many individuals, none of whom intends or designs the overall results"; collective animal behavior, "repeated interactions among individuals that produce large scale patterns"; Boids, "More complex rules can be added, such as obstacle avoidance and goal seeking", where goal seeking is an optional addition rather than a requirement.

**Verdict for G: not necessary**, `general`, **triangulated** across Fig 11 and Fig 7 (selection/seat versus room chain). **Refuter:** show a coordinated pattern that cannot be produced by selection-only or mutual-update processes once all shared goals are removed and the ground is held fixed.

### F5 : What cannot be removed is contact with a habitat and a ground

Fig 11 :39-40: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone. A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". If the channel goes, Fig 10 :27-28 says the output is not a verdict but "you have lost touch". Fig 11 :73: "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."

This is the positive residue behind the three negative verdicts. Collective order needs no goal, controller, or shared plan, but it does need actors in contact with each other and with something that does not move when they think. **Mode `general`**, triangulated across Fig 11 and Fig 10 (habitat/selection versus ground maintainer). **Refuter:** exhibit a collective pattern maintained under complete isolation, with no agent-agent contact and no shared ground contact. That breaks Fig 11 :40 and Fig 10 :24.

### F6 : The mechanism shape is already in the six plus the bonds; no new primitive is owed

Fig 13 :23-24: "network / that entities relate, and influence travels." Fig 12 :121: "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use." Fig 4 :19-20: "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

A trace left in an environment is a catalyst (it survives its use); alignment is a cycle whose sign decides closure; the flock is the same kernel at a larger size. Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." No seventh substrate member is needed to explain collective behavior. Fig 13 :31-32: "a remainder compounding through a chain that amplifies is chaos, and the detail a crossing discards reappears above it as noise" explains why local rules can yield unpredictable global order without any global plan.

**Verdict: shape only.** Which network, which loop sign, which trace is a `specified` claim for a particular system and is ungrounded until measured. `general` for the shape, **triangulated** across Fig 13, Fig 12, Fig 4. **Refuter:** derive a collective order that requires a seventh substrate member, or show the kernel changes with size.

### F7 : Agreement, however large, is not a verdict

Fig 7 :12-13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not." Fig 2 :39-41: "Correlation has valence. disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool / efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

A group can be coordinated and still be wrong together. The figure explicitly assigns one kind of valence to a convention and the opposite to a verdict. This matters here because the lay answer to "can collective behavior arise without shared goals?" is often supported by pointing at how many examples exist. That is a convention-style count, not ground contact. `general`, **triangulated** across Fig 7 and Fig 2. **Refuter:** show that agreement drawn from one pool constitutes a verdict about the world.

### F8 : "Shared information" splits into convergent derivation and adopted import

Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." Two agents who reach the same rule independently and two agents who copied it from one source look the same in their behavior. The distinction lives in the lineage, not the pattern.

So the answer to slot I depends on provenance that the observable behavior alone cannot supply. A flock whose members each estimate the local heading and a swarm whose members follow one leader's signal can produce similar order and carry very different evidence. `general` for the distinction, `specified` for any classification. **single-source** for the Fig 8 line (low-overlap corroboration from the Society of Agents essay's "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents"). **Refuter:** show that the record cannot distinguish them in principle, which would collapse the surplus claim.

### F9 : No import is needed to answer this question

Fig 6 :22-23: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure. No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 6 :26: "An import adds no atom to the world."

The mechanisms the question needs, local interaction, a loop, a surviving trace, selection against a ground, are already in the figure set. Importing "emergence" as a new primitive would add an atom that was not needed, which Fig 6 prices above starting from primitives. `general`, triangulated with F6. **Refuter:** demonstrate that the six plus the seven bonds cannot express one of the three negative verdicts.

### F10 : The honest stop: a shape, not a named mechanism for a given system

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." `THE_FORMULA.md`: "the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." The figures license the shape: three prerequisites not individually necessary, contact necessary, agreement not truth. They do not say which of alignment, stigmergy, selection, or quorum sensing produced a particular observed flock. That is ground work for an instrument, not a figure reading. `general` for what is licensed; `gap` for the system-level name. **Refuter:** produce a figure line that names a specific mechanism for a specific empirical case. There is none.

### F11 : A search that finds nothing is not proof of absence

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." If a reader looks for a leader in a flock and finds none, that does not prove the absence of centralized influence at every scale; it reports the limit of the instrument. Conversely the figure-derived shape does not prove that no real collective system ever has a controller. The verdict is about necessity under the figure set, not an empirical universal. `general`, single-source across the two figure lines and the canonical README. **Refuter:** exhibit a sealed frame in which absence can be proved, which Fig 9 :44 forbids.

### F12 : Filters are for the budget, witnesses for the verdict

Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A venue's prestige can get the packet read; it cannot make the shape true. The outreach files therefore ask for a failure and never ask for agreement. **Refuter:** show a case where a reader's reputation changes the ground's answer.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the exemplar's discipline and the formula's Direction step. The shape claim is **refuted** if any of the following fires:

1. **Contact refuter.** A collective pattern is demonstrated that persists with no agent-agent contact and no contact with a shared ground (complete isolation). Breaks F5, Fig 11 :40, Fig 10 :24.
2. **Goal refuter.** A controlled removal of all shared comparisons still yields the same coordinated pattern at the same rate, and the only remaining explanation is a shared goal. Breaks F4 and the Operators table definition of a goal.
3. **Verdict refuter.** Agreement among frames drawn from one evidence pool is shown to constitute a verdict about the world. Breaks F7, Fig 2 :39-41, Fig 7 :35.
4. **Primitive refuter.** A collective order is derived that requires a seventh substrate member, or the kernel is shown to change with size. Breaks F6, Fig 13 :115, Fig 4 :19-20.
5. **Provenance refuter.** The record is shown to be unable in principle to separate convergent derivation from adopted import. Breaks F8, Fig 8 :50-52.

If any fires, the corresponding slot is re-marked `necessary` or the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no claim here is `grounded`.

2. Keep the three slot verdicts separate; do not average them into a single yes or no (Fig 1 :19).

4. Ask what the reader would count as a failure before offering links (Fig 9 :27-28).

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is this one lever?** No. Fig 9 :54-58 union; three non-swappable prerequisites, so unbundle.
2. **Central control?** Not necessary. Fig 2 :13-15 mutual update converges with no anchor; order without a ground is drift (Fig 2 :34).
3. **Common information?** Not necessary as a communicated plan. Fig 2 :18-21 common cause; shared pool or shared contact is the residue.
4. **Shared goals?** Not necessary. Fig 11 :36 selection ran; Operators table makes a goal a comparison, not a precondition.
5. **What is necessary?** Contact. Fig 11 :40 and Fig 10 :24.
6. **Mechanism?** Shape only: network, cycle, catalyst, scale-invariant kernel (Fig 13 :23-24, Fig 12 :121, :119, Fig 4 :19-20). No import owed.
7. **What settles it?** The ground, not agreement. Fig 7 :35, Fig 2 :42. No instrument was run here, so mode stays `general`.

## 6. Honest stop

The shape survives: no shared goal, no controller, and no communicated plan is individually necessary for collective order; contact with a habitat and a ground is; and the order so produced is a convention or a variation, not a verdict. What does **not** survive is any claim that a particular observed flock, trail, or market has been shown by these figures to use one named mechanism. That is `gap` at the system level, to be closed by an instrument, not by a longer search inside the figure set. Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." After re-census, strains: none hidden, one narrow semantic strain recorded in the correction log (the two readings of "common information"), and one open gap (the empirical mechanism name).
