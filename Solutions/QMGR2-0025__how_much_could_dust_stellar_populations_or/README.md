## What the question decides, in effect terms

The local, late-universe determination of the Hubble constant disagrees with the early-universe (CMB + LCDM) inference at roughly 4 to 6 sigma. The question asks how much of the local measurement's offset could be produced by three named channel effects: dust extinction, stellar-population differences (metallicity, crowding, host-galaxy properties), and choices in the distance-ladder construction (which rung or anchor, which photometric system, which colour or Wesenheit correction). In effect terms: how much of the gap between a ladder frame's predicted distance moduli and the observed photometry is a defect in the measurement channel, rather than a change in the ground (the actual expansion rate)? What an answer would look like: a per-slot, independence-weighted error ledger, with the ground kept reachable and each slot's magnitude left open until a source-discriminating instrument settles it. That sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term here (Formula, "Direction"), so it can accrue a residual and be refuted.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"* and Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Gathering more agreement about a shared calibration produces a convention, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to trust before they check spends their independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR / CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute them, not a purchased list of believers. Ask what they would count as a failure. A refutation is more useful than an endorsement.
