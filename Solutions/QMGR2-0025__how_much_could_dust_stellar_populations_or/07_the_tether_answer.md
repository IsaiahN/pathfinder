# The Tether's answer on nearby distance-ladder systematics

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The local distance ladder (roughly 73 km/s/Mpc) and the CMB + LCDM inference (roughly 67.4) disagree at 4 to 6 sigma. The question asks how much dust, stellar populations and distance-ladder choices could move the local measurement. In effect terms: how much of the gap between a ladder frame's predicted brightnesses and the observed brightnesses is a channel defect, rather than a change in the ground? The residual is a **union compound** with three named slots and their intersections (Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"*), so it has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Index the residual per slot, never by a single total.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A global budget near zero with one live slot is a legal state, not a resolved one. The actionable content is the sub-ledger, not the total.
2. **The three effects are channel defects, not changes in the ground.** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."* Dust extinction, crowding, metallicity and host-mass effects and rung choices change the reading. The anchor (geometric distances, the true expansion rate) must be kept reachable.
3. **Order by dependency, not by size.** Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Settle the geometric anchor, then the host-galaxy standard candle, then the Hubble-flow standardization, then H0. Effort on rung 3 cannot rescue a broken rung 1.
4. **Independence decides whether agreement counts.** Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Two analyses sharing Gaia parallaxes, WFC3 zero points or the same SN compilation are one frame wearing two names. The TRGB-versus-Cepheid comparison with no measurements in common is the clean independent case.
5. **Describe the effect, not the cause; a shape, not a number.** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* The figures do not return "dust contributes 1.5 km/s/Mpc." They return the constraints any such magnitude must satisfy. The magnitudes stay open until an instrument settles a slot.
6. **A null test is a bound at the resolution reached.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* JWST crowding limits and near-IR checks bound slots; they do not close them. Conversely, Wojtak et al. 2025 and Gall et al. 2024 keep the dust slot live.
7. **Size rankings are budget filters, not witnesses.** Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Ranking slots says where to point the next observation, not whether the tension is physical.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a number for any slot. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not a verdict that the tension is systematics, and not a verdict that it is new physics. Both remain candidates; the ground decides.
- Not that the three effects are independent. They share calibration inputs; independence must be measured.
- Not that a total error budget is forbidden for reporting. It is forbidden as a substitute for the per-slot verdict (Fig 1 :13 permits a near-zero global R with a live slot).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement here settles `left(R, phi)` for any slot.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Crowding slot:** JWST/NIRCam source separation versus HST/WFC3 blending (Riess et al. 2023). Already bounds this slot tightly at the resolution reached.
- **Dust slot:** near-IR SN Ia standard candles (A&A 2018) and blue, minimally extinguished SNe (Gall et al. 2024). A cross-check that separates intrinsic colour from extinction would name the source.
- **Population / host slot:** matched-subsample comparisons of calibration and Hubble-flow SNe (Wojtak et al. 2025), and TRGB halo stars in low-reddening environments (Freedman et al. 2019).
- **Ladder-choice slot:** geometric anchors with no shared measurements (maser, DEBs, Gaia) compared across independent pipelines.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a figure line that returns a numerical systematic magnitude; a single generative mechanism for all three slots; two fully shared-calibration frames counted as independent; a dust change that moves the ground; a sealed frame in which a null search proves absence.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The question "how much" is answerable by instruments that already exist or are being built, so it is not underivable in principle. But the figures cannot produce the number; they are a metatheory of residuals and grounds. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* applies to the figure-level claim (the numerical slot magnitudes are out of reach *from the figures*), and the world sources supply the route to a ground reading. The answer stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not compute a number for dust or crowding in the Hubble tension. It says that the residual must be kept per slot, because aggregating systematics hides the live one; that these effects are defects in the measurement channel rather than changes in the expansion rate; that the ladder's rungs are ordered, so a flat error budget loses the information about where to fix; that agreement between analyses sharing calibration is not corroboration, while the TRGB-versus-Cepheid comparison with no measurements in common is; and that a null test bounds a slot at the resolution reached, it does not close it. What it offers is the shape any defensible magnitude must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would settle each slot. The number stays open until the ground decides.
