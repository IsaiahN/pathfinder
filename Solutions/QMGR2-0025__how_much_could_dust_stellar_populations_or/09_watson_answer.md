## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the size of channel effects (dust extinction, stellar-population and crowding differences, distance-ladder choices) on the local Hubble-constant measurement. Option A: report one total systematic budget in km/s/Mpc and treat it as the answer to "how much." Option B: keep the residual per slot, order it by dependency, measure independence between frames, leave each slot's magnitude open, and name it only when a source-discriminating instrument settles it. What each leads to: A produces a green reading that cannot comment on its source and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a checkable shape, written refuters, and a route to a ground reading. Figures primary, world secondary.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a ladder frame's predicted brightnesses and the observed brightnesses, heard as the local versus early-universe H0 disagreement (4 to 6 sigma). Arity: three named slots plus intersections, so a union compound with its own loop, not one hard number. Symmetry: ordered, not interchangeable; dust, populations and ladder choices act through different mechanisms and can converge on the same bias. Scale: per-object photometric residuals amplify across three rungs into H0. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
- **Domain 1, local ladder measurement.** Actors: Cepheids, TRGB, SNe Ia, NGC 4258 maser, detached eclipsing binaries, Gaia parallaxes, HST/WFC3, JWST/NIRCam, dust, crowding, metallicity, host mass, calibration choices. Currency: distance modulus and magnitude residuals. Anchor: geometric distances and the actual expansion rate. It does not move when teams argue.
- **Domain 2, early-universe inference.** Actors: CMB (Planck), BAO, LCDM, sound horizon. Currency: model-inferred H0. Anchor: observed CMB anisotropies is a ground reading, but the H0 value is model-mediated, so the inference is a frame. Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: independent teams, instruments and pipelines. Currency: residuals and independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so independence is declared, not assumed.
- **Domain 4, outreach and publication.** Actors: distance-ladder systematics researchers, reviewers, editors. Currency: checkable residuals. Anchor: published data, code and instruments.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle rung 1 before rung 2 before rung 3 (Fig 3); refuse a total-as-verdict before minting a slot (Formula step 2); describe the effect before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Union or partition: D1 and D2 are frames ranging against one ground, not two grounds. The three slots inside D1 are a union of overlapping conditions and must be unbundled before a search (Fig 9 :55). Strain test applied: the per-slot rule and a reported total budget govern different objects (where to look versus how to report), so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Keep the residual per slot, ordered by dependency, independence-weighted, channel-directed and instrument-settled. Dust, stellar populations and ladder choices are three live slots, not one total; a total near zero with one live slot is a legal state. Agreement among analyses sharing calibration is not corroboration, and a null test is a bound at the resolution reached. The figures return the shape any defensible magnitude must have, not the magnitude. World literature shows some slots bounded tightly (crowding, JWST) and others still live (dust model, ~1.5 km/s/Mpc; blue-SN subsample, ~3 km/s/Mpc), so the empirical answer to "how much" stays open. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a total-as-verdict instead of a per-slot generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The total-as-verdict framing is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no slot has to be deleted, only re-measured.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates intrinsic SN colour from extinction, or JWST source separation that drives the crowding slot below the required level, would name a magnitude; two independent anchors with no shared measurements converging on one H0 would settle the channel question. A figure re-census that returns a numerical systematic magnitude would refute the shape-not-a-number claim; a demonstration that dust moves the ground would refute the channel claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
