## Two findings in the world literature that bear directly on the pitch

1. **Independence is measurable, not assumed.** Freedman et al. 2019 explicitly construct a comparison with *"no measurements in common"*; this is the figure's independence condition (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :48) realized in an instrument. Cite it as the positive case, not as proof that systematics are excluded.
2. **Null results and live results coexist.** Riess et al. 2023 finds crowding is not a significant contributor at JWST resolution, while Wojtak et al. 2025 and Gall et al. 2024 still find a dust-model slot worth 30 per cent of the tension or about 3 km/s/Mpc. Fig 8 :39 (*"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*) is the rule that keeps both statements honest. Present them as bounds at different resolutions, not as a contradiction to be smoothed over.

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
