# Expert verification packet - the Tether on nearby distance-ladder systematics

**What this is:** a claim in a form a distance-ladder researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No numerical systematic magnitude is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The local-versus-early H0 residual should not be reported as one total systematic budget. It is indexed per object and per slot, so dust extinction, stellar-population and crowding differences, and distance-ladder choices must be kept as separate live slots; a total near zero with one live slot is a legal state. These are defects in the measurement channel, not changes in the ground, and the ladder's rungs are ordered, so a flat aggregate loses the information about which rung to fix. Independence, not agreement, decides whether two analyses corroborate each other: shared Gaia parallaxes, WFC3 zero points or SN compilations collapse two frames into one evidence pool, while the TRGB-versus-Cepheid comparison with no measurements in common is the clean case. Null tests bound a slot at the resolution reached and do not close it. The framework returns the shape any magnitude must have, not the magnitude itself.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 1 and Figure 10
- **Statement:** the residual is per-slot, and the three effects are channel defects. A single total cannot locate the fault.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does."*
- **What to check:** the Riess et al. 2019 LMC error budget is already a sub-ledger (LMC P-L 0.42%, DEB 1.20%, CRNL 0.11%, geometry 0.09%, photometry 0.14%). Does any current practice treat the total (1.28%) as if it named the source?
- **Refuter:** show that the three effects share one generative mechanism such that one aggregated residual is sufficient; or show a dust/population change that moves the ground rather than the reading.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by a distance-ladder researcher against published comparisons
- **Statement:** independence is the deciding quantity, and it can be measured. The TRGB/CSP comparison with no measurements in common is high-independence; two analyses sharing calibration are one pool.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
- **What to check:** Freedman et al. 2019 states that the TRGB and Cepheid determinations have *"minimal overlap in their systematics"* and *"There are no measurements in common between these two methods."* Is that claim accurate for the specific anchors and SN samples used? And do current multi-analysis H0 combinations double-count shared calibration?
- **Refuter:** show that two analyses sharing all calibration inputs are nonetheless independent in the figure's sense; or that their agreement is convergent derivation (Fig 8 :50) rather than a shared pool. If either holds, C2 fails.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with a physics-facing edge
- **Statement:** the rungs are an ordered sequence, so a flat conjunction error budget drops arrangement information; null tests bound slots rather than closing them.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **What to check:** JWST results (Riess et al. 2023) bound the crowding slot; near-IR and blue-SN results (A&A 2018; Gall et al. 2024) and two-population modelling (Wojtak et al. 2025) keep the dust slot live. Are these bounds at different resolutions, as the claim says, rather than a contradiction? Does rung order matter for where a fix applies?
- **Refuter:** show the rungs commute (final H0 invariant under permutation of anchor, host calibration and Hubble-flow standardization), which drops the sequence bond; or exhibit a sealed frame in which a null search would be a proof of absence.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a numerical contribution from dust, populations or ladder choices. The figures return a shape, not a name or a number.
- Not a verdict that the tension is systematics, nor that it is new physics. Both are candidates.
- Not that the three effects are mutually independent. They share calibration inputs.
- Not that a total systematic budget is forbidden for reporting. It is forbidden only as a substitute for the per-slot verdict (Fig 1 :13 makes a near-zero global R with a live slot legal).
- Not a theory of everything; no final room. Fig 13 :90 *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it.

1. Does a measurement separate intrinsic SN colour from extinction (near-IR, blue subsamples, matched host populations) well enough to name the dust slot's magnitude?
2. Does JWST source separation bound the crowding slot below the level needed to move H0 by the observed offset?
3. Do independent anchors with no shared measurements (TRGB/CSP vs maser+parallax Cepheids) converge on one H0? If they do not, the channel is still live.
