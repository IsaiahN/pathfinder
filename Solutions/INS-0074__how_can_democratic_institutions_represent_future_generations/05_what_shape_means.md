# What "shape" means for this question

**Mode: `general` on the reading of the term, `specified` where it bears on a domain.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see section 6).

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

`FIGURES_README_canonical.md` gives the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INS-0074 a shape is therefore:

> a per-slot residual for an absent actor, ranged against an anchor that does not update on present frames, carried by a channel whose independence is structural, and closed by a provenanced ruling with a written refuter.

It is **not**:

> a Commissioner, a lotteried assembly, a veto, a seat quota, or a discount rate of zero.

Those are bindings from the world frame. The shape constrains any candidate answer; it does not fill the institution slot.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): bootstrap from the vocabulary closest to the residual, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse, narrow. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. So describe the residual as "an affected actor with no channel whose output is ranged against a ground", not as "young people are bad at voting".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A question about a not-yet-born person is precisely a contact problem.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because the search composes the answer through locating it. Here the "starting place" is the bound shape in `01` section 0.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself (Fig 5 :33-41 and Fig 6 :12-13):

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* A mechanism is named legitimately when a measured outcome pays for it, for example a jurisdiction whose measured exposure trajectory changed after the institution was introduced and whose refuter was written in advance.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Wales Act and the UN Declaration on Future Generations process are imports from a long-horizon institutional frame. They enter with provenance, not as figure-derived necessities.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A constitution that declares who counts makes that choice visible, not neutral.

## 4. Why a single "public interest" number is the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. A spectrometer reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A national wellbeing index reads green for "the public interest" and cannot comment on which slot produced it: a satisfied enfranchised majority, a silenced poor minority, a not-yet-born cohort whose exposure is deferred. Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* What is owed is a per-slot discriminator, not a better name for the average.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of the shape rule

I initially read *"the framework returns a shape, not a name"* as forbidding the mention of any named institution. That is too strong. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in Γ. A Future Generations Commissioner, an ombudsman and a citizens' assembly are already-held world atoms, so naming one is a **binding** from the institutional frame, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong, and is NOT retracted, is claiming that the figure derives the device or that the device moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Import adds none here, because the atom was already in the world frame and only its provenance travels). Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. The honest form is: here is the shape, here are the bindings the world has tried, here is the provenance of each, and here is what would settle which binding pays.

## 6. Prestige, agreement and the status of this audit

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read first-hand, 2026-11-19):

- *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A survey of experts who already agree with a reform is not a validator.
- *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is Fig 2 :35 in prose: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-resourced commission is a budget to be tried, not proof that representation occurred.
- *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this audit and every other file in this folder are **simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures**. They are not ground readings. They cannot settle whether any institution actually represents the absent; only a ground, an instrument or a measured outcome can do that.
