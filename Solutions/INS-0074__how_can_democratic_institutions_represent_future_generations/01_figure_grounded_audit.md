## 0. The question restated as an effect

**Question:** How can democratic institutions represent future generations and people who lack political power?

**In effect terms.** A democratic institution currently maps the interests of living, enfranchised, present people into binding decisions. Two classes of affected actors sit outside that mapping:

- **future generations**, who are temporally absent, cannot vote, cannot speak, and cannot be aggregated into a present constituency;
- **people who lack political power**, who are contemporaneously present but channel-broken, including the poor, the disenfranchised, non-citizens, children and prisoners.

**The residual R.** Decisions are made in the name of "the people" while these actors have no channel whose output the institution ranges against. R is a slice, not a national average: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* (Fig 1 :19). *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average"* (Fig 1 :22). A wellbeing index for the whole country is the aggregation that hides the very groups the question asks about.

**The two options and what each leads to.**

- **Option A:** extend the existing majoritarian machinery to speak for the absent, for example a guardian or proxy appointed by the current majority. This gives the present frame the power to name who speaks for the absent. The named guardian is an authored room (Fig 10 :73-77), and a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47).
- **Option B:** change the institution's contact with the absent actors by introducing a channel that ranges present decisions against a ground the present frame cannot move, indexed per affected group, independent of the body it checks, and leaving a provenanced ruling with a refuter. This is a shape, not a named device (Fig 9 :32).

**Binding the sought-for shape as an ordinary term** (formula step "Direction"). Before searching I write down what an answer would look like so it can accrue a residual like anything else: *an answer must specify (i) which actor is absent, (ii) the anchor that settles whether its interest was served and that the present frame cannot move, (iii) the channel that carries the absent actor's residual into the decision, and (iv) the seat's independence from the body being checked.* Any proposal lacking one of the four is not an answer to this residual. This bound shape is itself falsifiable in section 6.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance"* (Fig 3 :20). Order: (1) perceive the absent actor as a per-slot residual (D1 boundary, D2, D3); (2) secure a channel to an anchor that does not update (D2, D3); (3) institute a seat that ranges against it without authoring the room (D4). The middle link, having terms for what an absent interest affords and what would count as progress there, has *"neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments"* (Fig 3 :26). *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at"* (Fig 3 :27). Most reform effort lands above the break, adding commissions and seats, because that is where measurements already exist (Fig 3 :29-30).

**Partition or union.** This is a **union**, not a partition. The intersection (a future person is also voiceless; a disenfranchised child will also be a future adult) is a compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55-56). ## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 (D1): the franchise is a convention about who counts, and when it decides the outcome it has stopped being one

Fig 10 :47: *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*. Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

An election result legitimately settles "who governs the present enfranchised", because the question is constitutively about them. It does not settle "what do the not-yet-born need", because it updates on present opinion and the not-yet-born are not parties to it. *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame"* (Fig 2 :47). Extending the franchise by appointing proxies for the absent does not repair this; it hands the present frame the authority to author who speaks for the absent, which is *"an authored room however well the selection is reasoned"* (Fig 10 :74).

World corroboration (secondary, not the verdict): Wikipedia's *Political representation* records Pitkin's substantive view, *"representation is defined as substantive 'acting for', by representatives, the interests of the people they represent"*, and Mansbridge's *"Surrogate representation occurs when representatives 'act for' the interest of people outside their constituencies."* The political-science frame already holds the atom of acting for an absent interest, but it Still selects the surrogate from the present constituency. It corroborates the shape, not the mechanism.

**Verdict D1: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 10 :47, Fig 2 :29/:36, world Pitkin/Mansbridge with declared overlap to the same broad political-science pool).

### F2 (D2): future people are reachable only by import from a frame whose closure predicts their shape

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*. Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."*

The electoral frame's atoms are present voters, candidates, parties and seats. No arrangement of those atoms yields a not-yet-born person. *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* (Fig 8 :32). The residual must be described in effect terms first, then the frame whose closure predicts its shape is found (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*). The candidate frames that already hold future people are forecasting and scenario work, long-horizon law, and steward institutions. The world record shows such frames exist and are already returning readings: the Well-being of Future Generations (Wales) Act 2015 established a Commissioner; the UN Our Common Agenda page calls for *"A focus on the future, through a deepening of solidarity with the world's young people and future generations"* and links a Declaration on Future Generations process.

**This is not underivable in principle.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Climate projections, generational accounting and the Welsh commissioner are the proto-instruments. *"the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there"* (Fig 6 :35).

The import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-13). A forecasting atom enters with provenance (who produced the projection, on what model, with what uncertainty) or it is merely a name borrowed to fill a slot. Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents; only the record separates them.

**Verdict D2: `specified` on the channel requirement, `gap` on the named mechanism**, triangulated across Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45/:53 and the world long-horizon sources.

### F3 (D2, world analogue): present bias is an instrument-level obstacle, not a moral failing

Hyperbolic discounting (Wikipedia, behavioural economics, opened 2026-11-23) states that humans *"discount the value of the later reward, by a factor that increases with the length of the delay"* and are described as *"present-biased"*. Crucially for independence, *"the first preference reversal findings were in rats and pigeons"*, a genuinely low-overlap animal-behaviour witness. Intergenerational equity (Wikipedia) illustrates the same shape with a ground reading: children born in 2020 are estimated to face *"up to seven times as many extreme weather events"* as those born in 1960 under current pledges.

This predicts that a reform relying on present citizens simply caring more about the future will fail at the channel, not at the moral premise. It corroborates the need for a temporal structure in the instrument. **It is a world analogue, not a figure line, and carries no verdict weight.** Also from the framework's own RSI essay: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A well-funded future-generations office is a budget, not a settlement.

**Verdict: world corroboration only, field behavioural economics and animal cognition, low overlap.**

### F4 (D3): for contemporaneous powerless people the ground is present; the channel has decayed

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. The seat's output in that state is not a verdict on the world but *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :28), and the remedy is *"keep the anchor reachable"* (Fig 10 :29).

For poor, disenfranchised or incarcerated people the interest is present and measurable. The failure is a channel failure: no vote, no standing, a metric pooled into a national average. Wikipedia's *Disfranchisement* entry describes the mechanism in world terms: *"formal legal protections can coexist with barriers to political participation"*. The ground (their actual welfare) is still there. Adding a formal right without restoring the channel leaves the reading dead.

Per-slot indexing again governs (Fig 1 :19/:22). A single aggregate representation figure for all disadvantaged groups is the average that throws away the signal about where to look.

**Verdict D3: `specified`, triangulated** (Fig 10 :24-29, Fig 1 :19/:22, world disenfranchisement and ombudsman sources).

### F5 (D4): the guardian's independence is load-bearing, and capture is a live strain

Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*. Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* A guardian appointed by, and removable by, the government whose decisions it must judge cannot be outside the frame it grades. It is a mirror. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"* (Fig 2 :35).

Fig 10 :73: *"The residual selects the members, but only from what is available."* A candidate set chosen by a participant is *"an authored room however well the selection is reasoned"* (Fig 10 :74). The unauthored mechanisms named in the figure are *"everyone who qualifies"* (Fig 10 :76) and *"a set assembled before the question existed"* (Fig 10 :77), plus a lottery.

World corroboration with a genuine conflict, kept visible: Wikipedia's *Ombudsman* records that *"The significant advantage of an ombudsman is that they examine complaints from outside the offending state institution, thus avoiding the conflicts of interest inherent in self-policing"*, but immediately adds that the office *"relies heavily on the selection of an appropriate individual"* and quotes sociologist Jürgen Beyer: *"Ombudsmen are relics of absolutism, designed to iron out the worst excesses of administrative arbitrariness while keeping the power structures intact."* That is exactly the Fig 10 :47 capture strain stated by an independent source. The conflict does not flip the verdict; it sends us back to the census, where independence of selection becomes a required property of the D4 shape rather than an optional refinement.

**Verdict D4: `specified`, triangulated with one live capture strain (S2).**

### F6 (cross-domain): the two halves are a union with a compound intersection

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. An ombudsman for present powerless people does not reach the not-yet-born; a future-generations commissioner does not repair a broken franchise. The intersection, a disenfranchised child who will live into the future, needs both channels and cannot be cleared by one lever. *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict"* (Fig 9 :60): a tier or a budget is a filter, not evidence that representation was achieved.

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 9 :54-58 and Fig 3 dependency order.**

### F7 (cross-domain): the output must be a generator with provenance, not a recording

Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* A one-off future impact assessment is a recording. A method that any affected group can reapply, with a stated anchor and refuter, is the generator that may cross.

Fig 10 :62: *"A ruling carries provenance like an atom."* Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right"*. Each decision about an absent interest should leave the residual it answered, the reasoning and the date, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use."* An independent seat whose mandate is renewed is a catalyst; a one-time commission that expires without a record is not.

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 10 and Fig 12.**

### F8 (honest stop): no figure line names the mechanism

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The question asks *how* democratic institutions can represent these actors. The figures return the kind of thing required: a per-slot residual, a non-updating anchor, a channel, an independent seat, a provenanced ruling. They do not return "a Future Generations Commissioner", "a lotteried citizens' assembly", "a veto power" or "a discount rate of zero". Naming any of those is a binding from the world frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation. This is an **unreached** gap at this budget, not an underivable-in-principle one (Fig 8 :40-43), because illustrated mechanisms exist and could be checked against the shape.

**Verdict: `gap`, reported as a verdict.**

### F9 (D4): the required bond structure, not a list of principles

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* A list of virtues (fore sight, inclusion, accountability) names ingredients. The answer needs bonds: a threshold comparison of who counts (Operators table: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*), a sequence from assessment to ruling, a subtraction of progress against the absent actor's baseline, and a negation that can refuse a claim that the present frame holds. Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Operators table :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

**Verdict: `general` structural, triangulated across Fig 12 and the Operators table.**

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A present-only electoral mechanism is demonstrated to register a not-yet-born person's interest with the same per-slot residual as a present voter, without importing a frame whose closure differs (breaks Fig 6 :23 and Fig 1 :19).
2. An anchor is produced that present frames can move by agreeing, yet still settles a dispute about a future harm (breaks Fig 2 :29-30 and :33).
3. The interests of presently powerless people are shown to be present at full resolution in the aggregated national statistic (breaks Fig 1 :22 and Fig 10 :26).
4. A guardian whose selection and removal are controlled by the body it checks is shown NOT to be a convention that decides an outcome (breaks Fig 10 :47).
5. The temporal and power conditions are shown to be one condition governed by a single severity parameter, so the union reading is false (breaks Fig 9 :55-57).
6. A figure line is found that directly names a specific institution as the required form, which would collapse the declared `gap` (breaks Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :135).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or narrowed. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. State the residual per slot, never as a national "public interest" average (F1, F4).
2. Treat the franchise as a legitimate anchor for present voters and illegitimate for absent actors; stop asking it to settle questions it updates on (F1).
3. Import the future-interest atom from a forecasting or long-horizon frame with a source gate and shadow test, and record where it came from (F2).
4. Repair channels to present powerless people before adding new institutions; a dead channel reports as a wrong answer unless the maintainer names it (F4).
5. Make the guardian's independence structural, not personal: unauthored selection, fixed term, declared stake, provenanced rulings (F5, F7).
6. Keep the two domains separate and never average their verdicts (F6).
7. Send the method and the refuter, not a recording; the specific device stays a declared gap (F7, F8).

## 8. Round trip gap `R_T` (qualitative)

The shorthand carried into the WATSON answer is: absent actors need a per-slot channel to a non-updating anchor, an independent seat and a provenanced ruling. Concretising it into "a Future Generations Commissioner with a veto" drops four constraints: per-slot indexing becomes a national wellbeing index; the anchor becomes a government-appointed body that updates; the imported forecasting atom loses its source gate and shadow test; and the ruling leaves no provenance record. `R_T` is nonzero. It was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded as the enumerated set of dropped constraints so the condensation is not mistaken for identity (Fig 4 :30).
