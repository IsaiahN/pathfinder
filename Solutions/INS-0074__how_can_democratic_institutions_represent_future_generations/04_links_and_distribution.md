## Wider literature that bears on the shape

1. **Pitkin and Mansbridge via *Political representation*.** The distinction between acting for present constituents and surrogate representation for people outside a constituency is the closest political-science atom to the absent-actor residual. It corroborates the D1 boundary, but it does not reach non-existent people, so it cannot settle D2.
2. **Tobin 1974 via *Intergenerational equity*.** *"The trustees of endowed institutions are the guardians of the future against the claims of the present"* is a convergent derivation of the trustee shape from institutional economics. Because it converges from a different field, it is corroboration at relatively high independence, but it is still a world source and cannot name the device.
3. **Ombudsman literature and the Beyer critique.** The capture strain is stated by the world source as explicitly as by Fig 10 :47. Use it when explaining why independence of selection is a requirement, not a refinement.
4. **Hyperbolic discounting and animal preference reversal.** A genuinely low-overlap mechanism analogue: temporal discounting is measured in rats and pigeons, so present bias is an instrument-level property, not a cultural choice. It supports the claim that a temporal channel structure is needed, and is never presented as a figure line.
5. **Wales Act and the UN process.** Existing proto-instruments (Fig 6 :32-35). They are bindings to test, not results the framework produced.
