# The Tether's answer on representing the absent

**Mode: `specified` on the four domain shapes, `general` on the union skeleton, `gap` on the named mechanism, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Decisions are made in the name of "the people" while two classes of affected actors have no channel whose output the institution ranges against: people not yet born, and people presently without power. The residual is a slice, not a national average (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is a **union of overlapping conditions**, not one severity dial (Fig 9 :55-57), so verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape (per domain, kept separate)

**D1, the electoral frame.** The franchise is a legitimate anchor for the question "who governs the present enfranchised", because the question is constitutively about them (Fig 2 :36). It is **not** an anchor for a question about absent actors, because it updates on present opinion (Fig 2 :29-30). *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame"* (Fig 2 :47). A convention that decides who counts for the absent has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.

**D2, future generations.** The electoral frame's closure does not contain not-yet-born people, and *"no search, however long, adds an atom"* (Fig 8 :32). The interest must be imported from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23), with a source gate and a shadow test. The proto-instrument already exists in forecasting, generational accounting and long-horizon law (Fig 6 :32-35), so this is unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle. The future state of the world, projected exposure and resource stocks, is the anchor that does not update on what present frames think. Verdict: `specified` on the channel requirement, `gap` on the named mechanism, triangulated.

**D3, contemporaneous people without power.** *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24). Their welfare is measurable now; what has failed is the channel, through stale readings, pooled metrics and no standing (Fig 10 :26). The seat's output is *"you have lost touch"* (Fig 10 :28), and the remedy is *"keep the anchor reachable"* (Fig 10 :29), not a new moral premise and not a national average. Verdict: `specified`, triangulated.

**D4, the verification frame.** The guardian or auditor must be unable to produce what it checks (Fig 10 :42). *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"* (Fig 10 :59). Selection must be unauthored: *"everyone who qualifies"* (Fig 10 :76) or *"a set assembled before the question existed"* (Fig 10 :77), not a candidate set chosen by the body being checked (Fig 10 :74). Each ruling carries provenance (Fig 10 :62), and provenance shows legitimacy, never correctness (Fig 10 :65). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated, with one live capture strain (S2).

**Union rule.** An ombudsman clears the present-powerless region; a long-horizon channel clears the future region; the intersection needs both in the defined order. *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57). Verdict: `general` structural.

**No name.** The figures return the kind of thing required, never the device (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :135). Commissioner, assembly, veto, quota and discount rate are bindings from the world frame; they are offered as candidates with provenance, not as derivations. Verdict: `gap`, reported as a verdict, not silence (Fig 8 :39).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution is *the* answer. The shape does not select among a commissioner, an ombudsman, a lotteried assembly or a court.
- Not that extending the franchise to proxies for the not-yet-born solves D2. It authors the room (Fig 10 :74).
- Not that a national wellbeing index measures representation. It is the average that hides the slot (Fig 1 :22).
- Not that present citizens simply need to care more. Present bias is a world analogue about the channel, not a moral verdict, and it is not a figure line.
- Not that an independent seat is automatically independent. Selection and rotation decide; capture is live.
- Not `grounded`. No measured outcome has paid for any binding here.

## 4. What would settle it (the ground)

1. **A per-slot discriminator** that registers a not-yet-born or powerless actor's interest at full resolution, where a national index currently reads the same value. The equivalent of the spectrometer in *The Hard Problem is About Source*, which could comment on the source and not only on the reading.
2. **A non-updating anchor test**: show a disputed future harm settled by a measurement that neither present side can move by agreeing.
3. **A channel-restoration test**: after the channel is repaired, does the previously invisible group's residual trend down while the aggregated index stays flat? If yes, the channel, not the verdict, was the fault.
4. **A capture test**: change the selection agent of the guardian. If the ruling about an absent interest changes with the selector, the seat was a convention deciding an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
5. **Standing figure falsifiers**: import a frame that yields a not-yet-born atom inside the electoral closure; derive one of the six from the other five; show a guardian selected by the body it checks is not captured; show the union is one dial; find a figure line naming a device. The six pre-registered conditions are in `01` section 4.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The instruments that would name the device are world instruments, not figure lines. Long-horizon law and forecasting already return something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35), so there is an edge to extend. The stop is at the limit of what the figures can distinguish, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README). Applying the six physical substrate terms to institutional power was tried and re-censused out (strain S1), so no claim here rests on that analogy.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a single institution that will represent the absent. It states the shape any such institution must have. The franchise is a proper anchor for present voters and an improper one for people not yet born, because it changes with present opinion; a not-yet-born interest has to enter from a frame whose closure already holds it, with its source recorded. For people presently without power the ground has not gone away, the channel has: pooled metrics and no standing make the institution lose touch, and the fix is to restore the reading rather than announce a new verdict. Any guardian or commissioner must be unable to produce what it checks, with selection the current body cannot author and rulings that carry provenance. The temporal and power conditions are a union with a compound intersection, so two channels, not one lever, are required. Which device pays is left open until a ground settles it. That is a verdict, not a silence.
