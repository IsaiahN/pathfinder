## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what a democratic institution must contain in order to register the interests of actors who cannot register them themselves. Option A: extend the present-majority machinery to appoint proxies for the absent. Option B: change the institution's contact with absent actors by installing a per-slot channel to an anchor the present frame cannot move, with an independent seat and a provenanced ruling. What each leads to: A authors the room and produces a mirror; B returns a shape with written refuters and leaves the device open until a ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** Decisions in the name of "the people" while affected actors have no ranged channel. Arity: two conditions, temporally absent and contemporaneously powerless, so a **union compound**; a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. Symmetry: asymmetric, present enfranchised have a channel and the others do not; introduction is available and exclusion is not. Scale: individual interest, constituency cohort, intergenerational chain. R is a slice, never a national average.
- **D1, electoral and representative frame.** Actors: voters, representatives, selection agents. Currency: votes, seats, authorisation. Anchor: the election result is legitimate for present voters and illegitimate for absent actors, because it updates on present opinion. Question type: partition boundary defining who is currently "present".
- **D2, future generations.** Actors: not-yet-born cohorts, present stewards, long-horizon institutions. Currency: future welfare, projected exposure, discount rates. Anchor: the future state of the world, which present frames cannot move. Question type: union with D3, import required from a frame whose closure differs.
- **D3, contemporaneous people without power.** Actors: the poor, the disenfranchised, non-citizens, children, prisoners. Currency: interests, harms, access. Anchor: their actual welfare, measured in disaggregated form, present but not ranged against. Question type: union with D2, channel repair rather than new premise.
- **D4, verification and institutional design.** Actors: commissioners, auditors, expert readers. Currency: conventions, rules, appointments, rulings. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: checking arrangement over D1 to D3; independence of selection is the load-bearing property.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive the absent actor as a slot, characterise the residual in effect terms, find the frame whose closure holds the atom, install the channel, then institute the independent seat and the provenanced ruling. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

Strain test: Fig 13's six physical terms were tried on institutional power and re-censused out; calling coercion energy and a constitution a gradient is a strain. Fig 13 is used only for the honest-stop role. No figure contradiction resulted.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no named mechanism does. The franchise is a legitimate anchor for present voters and an improper one for absent actors. A not-yet-born interest must be imported from a frame whose closure already holds it, with a source gate and shadow test; the ground is the future state of the world, which present frames cannot move. For presently powerless people the ground is present and the channel has decayed, so contact must be restored and the reading taken per slot rather than pooled. Any guardian must be structurally independent of what it checks, must declare its stake, and must leave a provenanced ruling with a written refuter. The temporal and power conditions form a union with a compound intersection, requiring two channels in dependency order. Which device pays is a `gap`, unreached rather than silence. Overall status: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so a bad binding can be found without re-deriving the shape. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording (a finished institution design) instead of a generator (the residual, the shape and the refuter), which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The bindings are separable from the shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, no primitive has to be withdrawn, and the fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim can be repaired by a stated shared-atom test.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot instrument that discriminates a not-yet-born or powerless actor's interest at full resolution would name the slot; a measurement showing that a present-updating anchor still settles a future harm would break D1 and D2; a channel-restoration test in which the invisible group's residual falls while the national index stays flat would confirm D3; a selection-change test in which the guardian's ruling moves with the selector would confirm the capture strain and require the seat to be rebuilt; a figure re-census that names a device, or that admits the six physical terms govern institutional power without strain, would collapse the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
