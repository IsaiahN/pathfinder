## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: *"A room does not upgrade the label"* (Seat Map :283), *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention"* (Seat Map :163), and *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation, and asking them to trust the framework before they check spends the independence they contribute.
