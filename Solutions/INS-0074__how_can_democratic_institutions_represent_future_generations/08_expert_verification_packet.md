**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in future generations representation, institutional design or political theory can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A democratic institution can register an absent interest only if it contains four things: a per-slot residual for the affected actor, an anchor that does not change its answer when present frames push on it, a channel that carries the residual into the decision, and a seat that cannot produce what it checks and leaves a provenanced ruling. The franchise is a proper anchor for present voters and an improper one for people not yet born. For people presently without power the ground is present and the channel has decayed. The temporal condition and the power condition form a union with a compound intersection, so one lever is insufficient. Which named device pays is a declared gap; the figures return a kind of thing, never the answer itself.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: the anchor boundary, checkable by reading Figures 1, 2 and 6

- **Statement:** an election result legitimately settles who governs present voters and cannot settle what not-yet-born people need. A not-yet-born interest must enter from a frame whose closure differs, with provenance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **Refuter:** demonstrate a present-only electoral mechanism that registers a not-yet-born person's interest with the same per-slot residual as a present voter, with no imported frame; or produce an anchor that present frames can move by agreeing yet still settles a future harm.

### C2: the channel boundary, checkable against disaggregated welfare data

- **Statement:** for presently powerless people the failure is channel decay (no standing, no vote, metrics pooled into a national average), not absence of a ground. Restoration of the reading is the remedy, indexed per group.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; Fig 10 :28 *"you have lost touch"*; Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** is there a disaggregated measure of the affected group's welfare that moves while the national aggregate stays flat? If not, the channel has not been restored and the claim is untested, not refuted.
- **Refuter:** show that the interests of presently powerless people are already present at full resolution in the national statistic, so no per-slot instrument is owed.

### C3: the independence requirement, checkable against an institution's selection rules

- **Statement:** a guardian or commissioner selected and removable by the body it checks is a convention that decides an outcome, not an outside frame. Independence of selection is load-bearing; the stake in the future is allowed, control of the reading is not.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 10 :47 *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"*; Fig 10 :59 *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*; Fig 10 :76 *"everyone who qualifies"* and :77 *"a set assembled before the question existed"*.
- **What to check:** who appoints, who removes, who funds, and who decides what counts as evidence? If all four are the body being checked, the seat is a mirror.
- **Refuter:** show a guardian selected by the body it checks whose rulings about absent interests are not captured by the selection. The world record contains a live challenge to this claim: the ombudsman source quoted in `09` includes the critique that such offices keep power structures intact. That is recorded as a strain, not hidden.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named institution (commissioner, ombudsman, citizens' assembly, court, quota) is *the* answer. Naming one is a world-frame binding with provenance, not a derivation.
- Not that extending the franchise to proxies for the not-yet-born solves D2. It authors the room (Fig 10 :74).
- Not that a national wellbeing index measures representation. It is the aggregation that hides the slot.
- Not that present bias is a figure line. It is a low-overlap world analogue from behavioural economics and animal behaviour.
- Not that an independent seat is automatically independent. Selection and rotation decide.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
