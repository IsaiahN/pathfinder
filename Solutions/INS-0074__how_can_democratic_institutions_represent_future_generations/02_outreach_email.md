# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the institution

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send anything that names a preferred device as if the figures derived it. The claim must survive as a shape with a refuter, or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about representing people who are not in the room

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I'd like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual:** democratic decisions are made in the name of "the people" while two affected groups have no channel whose output the institution ranges against, people not yet born and people presently without power. This is not one dial set too low. It is two conditions with an overlap, so a single reform lever clears one region and leaves the intersection standing.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** an institution can register such an interest only if it has four things. First, a per-slot reading of the affected actor, not a national average that pools the signal away. Second, an anchor that settles the question and does not change its answer when the current majority pushes on it. For present voters an election is such an anchor; for people not yet born it is not, because it updates on present opinion. Third, a channel that carries the absent actor's reading into the decision. Fourth, a seat that cannot produce what it checks, with its stake declared and its rulings recorded with reasons and a date.
>
> For people presently without power the ground has not gone away, the channel has: pooled metrics and no standing make the institution lose touch, and the fix is to restore the reading rather than announce a new verdict. For people not yet born, the interest has to enter from a frame that already holds it, such as long-horizon law or forecasting, with its source recorded rather than smuggled in.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named device is the answer. A commissioner, an ombudsman, a lotteried assembly, a court or a quota are bindings from the world, not derivations. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that a national wellbeing index measures representation, or that present citizens simply need to care more, or that a well-funded office has settled anything. A budget to try is not a ground.
>
> **What would refute the shape:**
> 1. a present-only electoral mechanism that registers a not-yet-born person's interest at the same per-slot resolution as a present voter, with no imported frame;
> 2. an anchor that present frames can move by agreeing, yet still settles a dispute about a future harm;
> 3. evidence that the interests of presently powerless people are already present at full resolution in the national aggregate;
> 4. a guardian selected and removable by the body it checks that is demonstrably not captured by that selection;
> 5. a demonstration that the temporal and power conditions are one condition with one severity parameter;
> 6. a figure line that directly names a specific institution as required.
>
> I wrote these down before this message. If any one holds, I will narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you'd rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figures with line numbers: `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` (local copy)
> - Canonical usage rule: `https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md`
> - Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (why a validator is needed): `https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/the-recursive-self-improvement-trap`
> - Future Generations Commissioner for Wales (a real institution to test the shape against): `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_Generations_Commissioner_for_Wales`
>
> **If you want to use the method:** take any absent interest, name the actor, characterise its residual in effect terms, identify the anchor that cannot move, and write down what would refute your result before you run it. Report failures; they are more useful than successes.

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter before theory | Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Do not ask for trust | Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map :287 *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on the claim | THE FORMULA: *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Shape, not a named device | Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; canonical README *"a shape, not a name"* |
| Per-slot, not a national index | Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* |
| Independence of the seat is load-bearing | Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*; Seat Map :163 *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* |
| Prestige is not a settlement | Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Ask for a refutation, not endorsement | Seat 4 produces *"corrections on shape, named failure classes, and one question: could this have been anything else?"* (Seat Map) |

**Record the recipient's verdict on stage 1 before showing any framework material.** Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
