## Wider literature actually read, and what it bears on

| field | source | finding relevant to this question |
|---|---|---|
| Offline RL | Levine, Kumar, Tucker, Fu, arXiv:2005.01643 (2020) | Offline RL is defined as learning from previously collected data "without additional online data collection"; the paper names distributional shift and the difficulty of learning from a fixed dataset. This is the clearest statement that the two regimes differ in habitat, not just in sample count. |
| Offline RL failure mode | Kumar, Zhou, Tucker, Levine, arXiv:2006.04779 (2020) | Standard off-policy methods "can fail due to overestimation of values induced by the distributional shift between the dataset and the learned policy." A fixed corpus cannot answer for states it did not cover. |
| Active learning | Ren et al., arXiv:2009.00236 (2020, v2 2021) | "Active learning (AL) attempts to maximize the performance gain of the model by marking the fewest samples." This is a currency claim: it buys label economy by choosing which data to observe. It does not license a universal winner. |
| Model-based RL | Janner, Fu, Zhang, Levine, arXiv:1906.08253 (NeurIPS 2019) | Short model rollouts from real data "surpasses the sample efficiency of prior model-based methods, matches the asymptotic performance of the best model-free algorithms." Efficiency is regime-relative and depends on where the rollouts are branched from. |
| Human cognition | Lake, Salakhutdinov, Tenenbaum, Science (2015) | People generalize from one example where machine methods need tens or hundreds. Bit-efficiency depends on the learner's prior habitat, not on data volume alone. |
| Neuroscience | Friston, Nature Reviews Neuroscience (2010) | A free-energy principle "accounts for action, perception and learning." Action and perception are one loop rather than two separate learners. |
| Animal behaviour | Kingsbury, May, Uchida, bioRxiv (2026) | In mouse foraging, dopamine signalled a temporal-difference reward prediction error used as a teaching signal to construct value functions. Live outcome supplies the residual that drives the next choice. |
| Human decision making | Yadid, Linkovski, Sharp, PsyArXiv (2026) | People "try to reduce uncontrollable time costs through switching to a new choice." Time cost competes with learned value, which is a second currency in the same task. |

Overlap declaration: the four RL sources share a community and three share an author (Levine), so for the claim about distributional shift they are one pool, not four witnesses. The cognitive science, neuroscience, animal behaviour and human decision sources are genuinely low-overlap fields and are used only as secondary corroboration.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." Send the generator, the currency split and the refuter. Do not open with a benchmark table.
- **Fig 10 :42** - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the two-currency claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 9 :60** - "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A sample count is an input to the budget, not a verdict.
- **Seat Map :163** - "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and which currency they would price; (3) only then the three framework links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1, before any theory:** send the per-slot claim, the two-currency type error, the habitat distinction and the five refuters. Ask for their verdict first. Record it before showing anything else.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the figure text, the canonical usage rule and the formula. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because once they hold the interior their independence is spent (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3, opt-in only:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. A generator may cross up (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state their own ground, their own currency and their own refuter before running it, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stage 1 only works if its output survives. The output is a shape and a `gap`, not a number. If a checker asks for the number, say plainly that none is licensed by the figures and that the response to a number request is the refuter list, not an estimate.
