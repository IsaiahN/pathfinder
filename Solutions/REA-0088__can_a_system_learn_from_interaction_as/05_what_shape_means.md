## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*, verified this session: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is:

> a per-slot residual comparison, priced in one declared currency, against a ground that does not update on the system's own report, with the habitat named and a refuter written in advance.

A single efficiency ratio would be a name where the framework can only give a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap it, recurse and narrow. Verified phrase: "general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, verified this session: the essay quotes Fig 9 :27-28, "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit", and Fig 11 :40, "an improvement that does not change contact changes nothing". When contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see.
- *How Intelligence Actually Works*, verified this session: "each time you apply it, you find more to map until you have so much of general shape that you no longer need to hunt for the traces of lineage."

Applied here: the effect to describe is not "learning speed" but "residual reduction at a named slot, in a named currency, against a ground that can refuse." That description travels to an expert in a different closure without borrowing their benchmark vocabulary.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*, carried from the exemplar ledger: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." A declared "sample" is such a choice: it silently packs bits and actions into one unit, which is the error Fig 12 names.

The six substrate terms are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one efficiency ratio as the answer turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why an instrument, not a further argument, is owed

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, verified this session in the phrase "It existed whether you had the instruments and tools to measure its existence or not." A reading does not determine its source: several different sources can create the same reading, so an instrument that cannot distinguish them demonstrates its own inadequacy. The discriminator, not the argument, is what settles the case.

Here, a benchmark score is the reading. It cannot comment on the source: the same score can come from a corpus-matched prior, from live action, or from a substituted habitat that happens to overlap the test. The world literature makes the same point in another field: offline RL's distributional shift is precisely a case where the reading from stored data does not carry over to the live habitat. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates the sources, not another aggregate score.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, verified this session: "On every level of a system, data is lost. Context changes based on the environment." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. The answer to Q2 for this question

**What licenses a particular efficiency number here?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A number is licensed only by a ground settling a minted term in one declared currency at one declared slot and one declared habitat, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, the number is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the headline question commit the forbidden error?** Yes. It asks for a name (one ratio) where the framework can give a shape (a per-slot, currency-declared comparison), and it names that ratio from a reading (benchmark samples) that cannot comment on its source (bits versus actions, live contact versus substituted habitat).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige can fund an action and give it a budget to be tried, but cannot settle it; only the ground can. A list of notable researchers who agree does not settle the efficiency question. The legitimate ask is to give the comparison a ground and a refuter, not to gather endorsements.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** From the same essay: LLMs "simulate an approximate answer/output", while "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests and cannot settle anything they report on. This is stated on every substantive file rather than once at the end.
