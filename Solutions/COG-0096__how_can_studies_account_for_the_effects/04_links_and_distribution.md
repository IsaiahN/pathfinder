## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** The cross-cultural methods sources (Sorokowski and Kowal 2024, and the invariance literature they cite: van de Vijver and Leung, Milfont and Fischer, Liu et al.) draw on the same journals, conferences and survey programmes. Their agreement is one witness, not several (Fig 2 :31). The framework essays share the framework author's evidence pool and carry no independent corroboration weight.

- **Schooling (Ritchie, Bates and Deary 2015).** *"the association of education with improved cognitive test scores is not mediated by g, but consists of direct effects on specific cognitive skills."* A within-population natural-experiment body of work showing that schooling changes specific bonds, not a general factor. It corroborates Fig 12 :95-96 and :117 (same total, different arrangement) at a different stratum; it is not a between-population measurement, so the cross-cultural application stays `specified`.
- **Media and cultural change (Jackson and Medvedev 2024).** *"A mass media perspective, like an ecological perspective, offers no clear predictions for whether national cultures are converging or diverging in their values."* Also: *"values have diverged globally but converged regionally."* Media has no single signed effect, which corroborates Fig 11 :49-51 and the refusal of a pooled lever (Fig 9 :55). Note that this source itself uses the WVS, so it shares a survey pool with the cross-cultural sources on the sampling question; it is low overlap only with the framework, not with all other world sources.
- **Urbanization (Lee and Thornton 2021).** *"Empirical studies are often based on generalisations about how urban and rural environments differ, and few studies attempt to quantify these differences directly."* A genuinely low-overlap witness from animal behaviour and urban ecology for the enumerate-don't-assume rule (Fig 11 :43-48). Their sign-flip observation about early versus established urban invasion corroborates Fig 11 :50.
- **Migration (Constant et al. 2016/2018).** *"immigrants arriving in the host country are, on average, healthier than comparable natives. However, their health status dissipates with additional years in the country."* Migrant samples are selected and time-varying, corroborating Fig 11 :67 and the Symbols `w` line. Low overlap with the cognitive and methods pools because it is migration economics and public health.
- **Causal inference (Munafò et al. 2018).** *"selection can induce collider bias... selection can lead to substantially biased estimates of associations."* From epidemiology and statistical genetics, a very low-overlap witness for the danger of conditioning on a selected sample, which is what a migrant or urban split does.
- **Population structure (Wikipedia 2026).** *"population structure is a common and powerfully confounding variable in medical genetics studies"*; the chopstick-gene example shows a reading named from a group difference. Very low overlap with the social-science pools; corroborates Fig 9 :27-28 (describe the effect before naming the cause) and Fig 12 :95-96.
- **Response style (Sorokowski and Kowal 2024).** *"the presence of response styles can introduce biases that stem not from genuine differences in the underlying construct but solely from differences in scale usage"*, and, against over-correction, *"amending the response style correction might sometimes not clarify but distort our understanding."* Two-sided corroboration of Fig 10 :24-29 plus the need for a discriminator.
- **WEIRD sampling (Henrich, Heine and Norenzayan 2010).** From the public abstract: *"members of WEIRD societies, including young children, are among the least representative populations one could find for generalizing about humans."* Direct fetch failed (PubMed required cookies; MIT OECS returned 403), so the abstract was read through the search result and is recorded with reduced weight. It corroborates the reference-group-as-instrument finding, F8.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The apparent tension between strict response-style correction and the 2024 critique was returned to the census and resolved as a dispute about a convention, not about the ground (section 6 of `01`).

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42:** *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Prefer frames with genuinely different evidence pools over more emails to the same pool.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** Several target venues are journal or association submission systems, not conversation channels. This solution is an LLM-assisted simulated derivation and is labelled as such everywhere. It must not be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude, with AI use disclosed under the venue's policy. Check each venue's AI policy before submitting anything.

**Order in any reply:** (1) the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter; (2) the question "what would you count as a failure here?"; (3) only after a verdict is recorded, the three source links with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the formula as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
