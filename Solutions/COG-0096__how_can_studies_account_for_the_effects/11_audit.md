## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the persistence of a per-slot gap after four exposure conditions have been recorded. Arity: four conditions indexed by person and site, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*), not one hard question with four covariates. Symmetry: "different" is a threshold relative to a reference group (Fig 12 :87), and exposure valence flips with the residual (Fig 11 :49-51). An ordered bond is required because exposure history changes the weighting `w` (Symbols :24-26). Scale: person belief, site habitat, and the between-site comparison across a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :21-22, :30).
- **Domain 1, sampled person's belief.** Actors: respondent, history, prior, learnable `w`. Currency: belief update and residual reduction. Anchor: a locally observable outcome the respondent cannot move by answering differently. Question type: how exposure sets the weighting.
- **Domain 2, habitat of the site.** Actors: migrants, urban residents, media sources, schools, conditions, relations. Currency: contact and local payoff. Anchor: the site's own ground, enumerated. Question type: enumeration, not composition (Fig 11 :43).
- **Domain 3, measurement channel.** Actors: translated items, response styles, recruitment, coding, retention. Currency: contact with the ground or loss of it. Anchor: a response-discriminating instrument. Question type: channel failure, including over-correction.
- **Domain 4, comparison frame.** Actors: reference group, investigator, reviewer, published norm. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground for the verdict; the reference group is demoted to instrument. Question type: anchor legitimacy.
- **Domain 5, convention layer.** Actors: exposure cuts and declarations. Currency: falsifiability. Anchor: none; conventions are admitted only while falsifiable (Fig 10 :47). Question type: a declaration that decides an outcome has stopped being one.
- **Excluded:** "culture" as a free-floating cause has no anchor independent of the readings it explains; "socioeconomic status" as sole cause is already inside Domain 2.

Union, not partition: D1/D3 and D2/D5 form two compounds, so the whole is a union of C1 history, C2 habitat, C3 channel and C4 convention. Order by dependency: perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3), with the exposure-history order supplied by `w`.

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Symbols :24-26 and Fig 11, I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 10's channel rule. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular at the level of figure selection. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Symbols :24-26 (`w` and history divergence), Fig 11 :43-52 (habitat enumeration and cascades), Fig 10 :24-29 (channel decay), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 12 :95-96 and :117 (isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` section 8 and `07` section 2: exposures change `w`; enumerate each habitat; keep four conditions separate; equal scores can hide isomers; the reference group is an instrument; naming the exposure is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete claim a researcher would act on: add these four checks to a multi-site design, record bonds as well as scores, declare the cut before the outcome, and look for a source-discriminating instrument.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **source-discrimination limit**, which the concrete claim tends to reintroduce by naming response style or selection as the source of a difference (Fig 10 :24 gives the channel rule, not an instrument that identifies which of several sources produced a reading);
- the **independence test**, which the shorthand compresses to "use low-overlap frames" but which requires an actual audit of evidence-pool overlap per site (Fig 2 :31);
- the **convention status** of the exposure cut, which a study will almost always treat as a settled definition rather than a falsifiable declaration (Fig 10 :47);
- the **two-sidedness of correction**, which the shorthand compresses to "measure the channel" but which requires watching both under- and over-correction (Sorokowski and Kowal 2024).

Conversely, three things survive cleanly: a between-population difference cannot be read as a construct difference without local ground contact; the four conditions are a union with distinct instruments; and equal scores do not establish equal composition. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no local ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
