**Mode: `specified` on the method consequences, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. ## QUESTION

How can studies account for the effects of migration, urbanization, media, and schooling on the populations they compare?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| persist per sampled person and site after the four exposures have been recorded, and why does its sign change across sites? R is indexed per slot, never pooled (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: a local, source-discriminating outcome the instrument authors cannot move (Fig 2 :29-30, :36-38).

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Arity: a four-place relation indexed by migration, urban residence, media diet and schooling, ordered by exposure history; it carries an ordered bond and a comparison bond, not symmetric ones. Symmetry: "different" is threshold-relative (Fig 12 :87), and exposure valence flips with the local residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Scale: the person's belief, the site's habitat, and the between-site comparison crossing a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :21-22, :30).

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated|single-source|gap)

**D1 Sampled person's belief, status triangulated (Symbols table and Fig 11, belief weighting vs habitat valence).**

> *"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* (Symbols :24-26)
> *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* (Fig 11 :49)

Reading: exposures act on the weighting between history and prior, not only on the score. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a sensitive task on which an exposure contrast leaves the measured weighting unchanged.

**D2 Habitat of the site, status triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 6, habitat census vs closure/import).**

> *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* (Fig 11 :43)
> *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* (Fig 11 :48)
> *"first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* (Fig 11 :52)
> *"Cast it in effect terms."* (Fig 6 :30)

Reading: enumerate each site including cascades. Mode `general` for the rule, `specified` for sites. Refuter: a site whose enumeration adds no residual beyond first-order covariates.

**D3 Measurement channel, status triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 9, ground maintainer vs describe-before-looking).**

> *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24)
> *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26)
> *"→ keep the anchor reachable"* (Fig 10 :29)
> *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :27-28)

Reading: apparent difference is often channel failure, so describe the effect before naming the frame. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a construct difference shown while all channel indicators measure stable.

**D4 Comparison frame, status triangulated (Fig 2 and Fig 10, anchor rule vs convention layer).**

> *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29)
> *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* (Fig 2 :30)
> *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* (Fig 2 :31)
> *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47)

Reading: the reference group is an instrument; same-pool agreement is not corroboration; a cut that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention. Mode `general`. Refuter: a cut and reference group invariant under all plausible redefinitions.

**D5 Structure and order, status triangulated (Fig 12, Fig 9 and Fig 3, distinct roles).**

> *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."* (Fig 12 :95-96)
> *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* (Fig 12 :117)
> *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* (Fig 9 :57)
> *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27)

Reading: equal scores can hide isomers; the union must be unbundled; the middle link is the usual break. Mode `specified` for sites, `general` for the structure. Refuter: one lever restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue.

**Naming the exposure that produced a particular gap, status `gap`.** No figure line governs this after a genuine re-census. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The missing thing is a source-discriminating instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** a between-population score difference may be read as a construct difference only when C1 (the history-versus-prior weighting is measured), C2 (each site's habitat is enumerated including cascades), C3 (the channel is reachable and not pooled) and C4 (the cut and reference group are declared before the outcome and kept falsifiable) all hold. The four are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Exposure valence is relative to the local residual, so no single pooled adjustment is legal; equal scores can hide isomeric bonds; samples are selected and time-varying; same-pool agreement is not corroboration; the reference group is an instrument. Which exposure produced a particular gap is a declared `gap` at the instrument boundary. Mode `specified` on the consequences, `general` on the shape. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism, not a local cognitive fact. The world sources did not flip any verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A study failing C3 would be sold more covariates, or a study failing C4 would be told to correct response styles, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs four. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is real but bounded and reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43, paraphrased from the amendment). The shape and its seven falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the seven pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) an exposure contrast with no change in the measured weighting, (3) one signed exposure coefficient with no sign flip, (4) a construct difference shown while channel indicators are stable, or (7) one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-site differences are fully predicted by averaged exposure effects would also reverse it. A source-discriminating instrument that names the source of a score difference would move the `gap` toward `grounded`.
