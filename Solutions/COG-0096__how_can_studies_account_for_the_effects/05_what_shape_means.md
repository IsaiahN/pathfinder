## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly:

> *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for COG-0096 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about comparison that can be stated before any particular exposure is named as the cause of a particular gap.** It constrains; it does not fill.

- Shape: *a difference may be read as a construct difference only when the history/prior weighting is measured, the habitat is enumerated, the channel is reachable, and the cut and reference group are declared before the outcome and kept falsifiable.*
- Name, withheld: *this gap was caused by migration, or by schooling, or by media exposure, or by urbanization; this group is more individualist; that test is culturally biased in a specified direction.*

The distinction matters here because the cross-cultural literature is almost entirely in the naming business. It names collectivism, acquiescence, tightness, WEIRD, modernization and response style. Those are bindings from the methods frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It’s this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "confounding", "covariate adjustment", "measurement invariance", "response style", "selection bias", "population stratification", "healthy migrant effect" and "urban adapter". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (a per-slot gap that persists after exposure is recorded, with a sign that changes across sites) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating COG-0096 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with four covariates.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the four conditions C1 to C4 are stated, an instrument is named for each, and six falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For COG-0096, neither has happened locally. There is no source-discriminating instrument in this folder that can tell a construct difference from an exposure-driven reading in a named study, and the methods-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."*

This is the exact error a comparison makes when it reads a score difference and names its source. A lower score is a reading. It is consistent with a construct difference, a migration selection effect, a response convention, a translated item that means something else locally, a motivation to perform, or simple sampling drift. The reading does not determine the source. Naming "media exposure" from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have.

The population-genetics example is a clean non-psychological instance: a group difference in chopstick use yields a spurious gene association (Wikipedia, *Population structure (genetics)*, read directly). The instrument reads a difference; the source is ancestry and practice, not a gene for chopsticks.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to cross-cultural methods

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is the author's own agent design, and an instance of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is framework-internal, it is an illustration of the rule, not evidence that cross-cultural comparisons behave this way.

For outreach this means: a list of famous cross-cultural methodologists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular exposure as the cause is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement is too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If an exposure is already an enumerated condition in the site's habitat, naming it is a binding from the methods frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall or settles the source (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*). The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

A second narrowing: Fig 2 :36-38 appeared to forbid human judgment as an anchor. Read carefully, it says human judgment is the right anchor for a question constitutively about people, and is only an instrument for a question about code. So local human outcomes may anchor a cross-cultural question, provided they are blind to the hypothesis. One culture's test norm is not such an anchor: it moves when pushed (Fig 2 :30). This narrowing is carried into `01` strain S2.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about research method. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

The tacit-knowledge essay adds the operational form of this: *"When contact isn’t possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you’d expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is what the pre-registered refuter is: an effect description anyone can check without first accepting the framework.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For COG-0096 the canonical shape is: **a four-condition conjunction with four distinct failure modes, a union that must be unbundled before any lever is chosen, and a source-discrimination gap at the instrument boundary.** It is not an exposure list, not a name of a cultural dimension, and not a ranking of populations. The names that appear in the methods literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instrument that would convert the shape into a grounded answer is not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
