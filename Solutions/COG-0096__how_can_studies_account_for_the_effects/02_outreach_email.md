# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no local ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about comparing populations before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about cross-cultural comparison that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** migration, urbanization, media and schooling are not nuisance variables that a rich covariate list makes harmless. They are the terms that change how a person weights their own history against what they inherited, and they are conditions in a local habitat whose valence depends on the question being asked. So a comparison is legitimate only if four conditions hold at once:
>
> 1. the change in that history-versus-prior weighting is measured across the exposure contrast, not inferred from a score difference;
> 2. each site's habitat is enumerated from the world, including second- and third-order contacts, rather than assumed from a universal exposure list;
> 3. the measurement channel is shown to be reachable: translation, response-scale use, recruitment and retention are not stale, saturated or pooled into an average;
> 4. the cut that defines who counts as exposed and the reference group are declared before the outcome is seen, and remain falsifiable.
>
> These are four different failure modes with different instruments, not four degrees of one problem. The same schooling can raise one subtest and leave an elementary processing task unchanged; the same media diet can push values in one direction in one region and the opposite direction in another. And two cohorts with identical scores can still be different in the way that matters, because the arrangement of identical answers carries information the answer list does not.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which exposure caused a given gap, that any particular study is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of comparison. I am offering a shape that any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a documented exposure contrast that produces no change in the measured history-versus-prior weighting on a task known to be sensitive to it;
> 2. a site whose enumerated habitat contributes no residual beyond the first-order covariates;
> 3. one signed exposure coefficient that predicts direction and magnitude on every task in every site, with no sign flip when the residual changes;
> 4. a between-site score difference independently shown to be a construct difference while every channel indicator measures stable and non-drifting;
> 5. two cohorts with identical scores, identical answer bonds and identical habitat histories whose outcomes diverge with no measured difference in contact;
> 6. one intervention that restores all four conditions in all failure types and leaves nothing categorically different behind.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers: the local canonical extract, from which every `Fig N :line` citation in the packet is taken.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a comparison you know well and can check against a real local ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Four conditions kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended exposure score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* (Seat Map :287) |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the exposure that caused a gap | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Effect described before the frame is named | *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (Fig 9 :27-28) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A reply from a direct-outreach recipient is a conversation, never corroboration (Fig 2 :40). If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59). Declare any stake in the shape rather than hiding it (Seat Map :171).
