# The Tether's answer on exposures in compared populations

**Mode: `specified` on the method consequences, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No local measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a cross-cultural instrument predicts for a sampled person and what that person actually returns persists, and changes sign across sites, after migration, urbanization, media and schooling have been recorded. R is indexed per person and per site, never pooled (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **the persistence and valence of contact**, not about the size of a group difference.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is four conditions, not one hard question with four covariates:

- **C1:** the change in the history-versus-prior weighting is measured, not inferred from a score.
- **C2:** each site's habitat is enumerated from the world, including cascades.
- **C3:** the measurement channel is shown to be reachable and not pooled.
- **C4:** the exposure cut and reference group are declared before the outcome and kept falsifiable.

## 2. The shape

1. **Exposure acts on the weighting between history and prior.** The Symbols table defines `b` as *"formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w"* and adds that *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge"* (Symbols :20-26). Migration, schooling, media and urban residence are the terms that set `w`, not noise to be partialled away. Mode `specified` for the method consequence.
2. **The habitat must be enumerated, not composed.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The four named exposures are a starting sentence, not a census; contact cascades three steps out (Fig 11 :52).
3. **Exposure valence is relative to the local residual.** Fig 11 :50: *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* No single signed adjustment is legal.
4. **Apparent difference is often channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. Translation, response-scale use, recruitment and retention are the channel. Both an uncorrected and an over-corrected channel lose the ground; what is owed is a discriminator.
5. **The effect must be described before the frame is named.** Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Otherwise every exposure seems to fit and the comparison confirms the investigator's prior vocabulary.
6. **Two equal scores can hide an isomeric difference.** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* A score inventory cannot tell two differently composed cohorts apart.
7. **Samples are selected and time-varying.** Fig 11 :67: *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat’s actors, which you did not choose."* A migrant cohort is not a random draw from its origin population, and the selection changes with time since arrival.
8. **The reference group is an instrument, not the anchor.** Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Because the question is about people, local human outcomes may anchor it, but only when blind to the hypothesis (Fig 2 :36-38). A published norm is an instrument.
9. **Same-pool agreement is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :31: *"Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full line begins *"disqualifying for a verdict:"*). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
10. **The break is usually at the middle link.** Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The vocabulary and objective links, and the carry link, have the fewest instruments, so analysis budget piles up above the break.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No local, source-discriminating measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which exposure caused a particular gap. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about what percentage of a score difference is "cultural", "compositional" or "methodological". Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that migration, urbanization, media or schooling are irrelevant. They are the terms that set `w` and the habitat conditions; the claim is that they do not settle the comparison on their own.
- Not a ban on covariate adjustment, invariance testing or response-style correction. They are instruments and imports with provenance owed; they are not verdicts (Fig 10 :47).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The cross-cultural methods sources share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from animal behaviour, epidemiology, population genetics and cultural evolution corroborate the mechanism shape, not a local cognitive fact.
- Not a theory of everything about comparison. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are score differences, and the candidate sources are a construct difference, an exposure-driven weighting, a translation artifact, a response convention, a selection effect and sampling luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. an exposure contrast with no change in the measured history-versus-prior weighting on a sensitive task;
2. a site whose enumerated habitat contributes no residual beyond first-order covariates;
3. one signed exposure coefficient with no sign flip across tasks or sites;
4. a construct difference shown while every channel indicator measures stable and non-drifting;
5. identical scores, bonds and habitat histories that still diverge in outcomes;
6. migration status independent of exposure and outcome, with conditioning on participation leaving all associations unchanged;
7. one intervention restoring all four conditions with no categorically different residue.

A prospective multi-site study that measures C1 to C4 separately, records bonds as well as item scores, enumerates each habitat, measures the translation round trip R_T and reports which condition failed before the difference appeared would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Naming an exposure from a score difference would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The ten conditions in section 2 are a shape; the eleventh thing, which exposure actually produced this gap in this study, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the exposure that caused a cross-cultural score difference. It says that migration, urbanization, media and schooling are not nuisance variables to be partialled out: they are the histories that set how a person weighs their own experience against what they inherited, and conditions in a local habitat whose valence depends on the question being asked. A comparison is legitimate only when four things hold: the change in that weighting is measured rather than inferred; each site's habitat is enumerated, including second- and third-order contacts; the measurement channel is shown to be reachable rather than stale, saturated or pooled; and the cut that defines who is exposed, together with the reference group, is declared before the outcome and kept falsifiable. Those are four conditions with four different failure modes, not four degrees of one problem. Two cohorts with identical scores can still be different, because the arrangement of identical answers carries information the answer list does not. The same-pool agreement of translated instruments is not corroboration. What is offered is that shape, seven falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which exposure produced a particular gap stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
