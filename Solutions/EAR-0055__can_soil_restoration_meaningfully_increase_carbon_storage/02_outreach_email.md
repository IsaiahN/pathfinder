# Outreach draft - check the residual first, then decide about the method

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three-slot separation in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F1 and F2 is intact in the text you send. If the three outcomes are averaged into one score, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Would you check one three-part claim about soil restoration before we discuss method?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific claim I would like you to check against your own ground. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** "Can soil restoration meaningfully increase carbon storage while also improving food production and water quality?" is not one question. It is three slots with three different instruments: soil organic carbon stock (paired coring, laboratory analysis, years), yield (harvest measurement, seasons) and water quality (nutrient and sediment loads at an outlet, storm events). They can move in different directions. No-till can raise topsoil carbon and raise nitrate leaching at the same time. Nonlegume cover crops can cut nitrate leaching by roughly 56 percent and, in a dry year, use water that the following cash crop needs.
>
> **The shape claim (`general` on shape, `specified` on signs).** Treat the three outcomes as a union compound, not an average. Each slot needs its own anchor. The joint case is a specific compound with its own loop, and it has to be measured at the outlet, not only in the plot. What I am claiming is the shape a checkable answer must have. I am not claiming that any single practice is good in every soil and climate.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that this email settles the empirical question. It does not. I did not run a field measurement. The signs reported in my packet come from sources I actually opened, and the verdict stays `specified` until a paired multi-site measurement settles it. I am also not claiming that reviews which cite the same trials are independent witnesses.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim, written before this message:**
> 1. one practice, with one instrument, shown to clear all three conditions across contrasting soils and climates in a pre-registered multi-site trial;
> 2. a measured case where any carbon gain is achieved only by a significant yield loss or a significant water-quality loss, with no bundle clearing the intersection;
> 3. a demonstration that all apparently independent supporting reviews draw on one evidence pool, leaving no independent room.
>
> If any of those holds, I will retract the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, where used, are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Jordon et al. 2022, Environmental Research Letters: https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ac8609
> - Thapa, Mirsky and Tully 2018, Journal of Environmental Quality: https://doi.org/10.2134/jeq2018.03.0107
> - Liang, Zhang and Zhao 2025, EGU abstract: https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu25-12605
> - FAO MRV protocol for soil organic carbon: https://doi.org/10.4060/cb0509en
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is public at https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]
