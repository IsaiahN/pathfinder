# What "shape" means for this question

**Honest scope of sources.** This session I opened the canonical figures README (local), the local metalaws note, the verbatim figure text, and one of the eight essays in full: Isaiah N, "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap" (adventuresinml, 2026-11-19). The exemplar solution maps the other seven essays, but I did not reopen them this session, so I do not quote them here or in the ledger. Nothing below is attributed to a source I did not read.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Local `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md` states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."**

So for EAR-0055 a shape is the form any acceptable answer must have before the signs are known:

1. three separate slots, not one averaged score;
2. an instrument and an anchor attached to each slot;
3. a union compound at the intersection, with its own loop;
4. a contact enumeration that reaches the receiving water body;
5. a stated condition set under which the three signs coincide.

A name would be "biochar is the answer" or "no-till is the answer." A shape is "a bundle must be chosen per habitat, and each of the three signs must be measured separately." The first is forbidden by the evidence read this session (results conflict by region and duration); the second is what the figures can deliver.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- Residual first, frame second. Fig 9 :27-28: **"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."** I described the residual as three measurement problems before choosing agronomy, hydrology and finance as frames.
- Unbundle before searching. Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
- Cast in effect terms. Fig 6 :30: **"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."**
- Refuse the sought-for shape as an unexamined preference. Local formula Direction section: make the sought-for shape an ordinary term, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else. That is why section 0 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` writes down the three-row sign table before the search.

## 3. What licenses a name (or a sign)

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** For this question that is paired soil coring, harvest measurement and outlet water sampling.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test. The hydrology result on nitrate leaching and the finance result on verification cost are imports, and their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

Until one of those fires, the signs stay `specified`. A review average is not a ground. Fig 7 :35: **"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."**

## 4. The answer the shape forbids

The forbidden answer is a single global yes with one number: "soil restoration increases carbon by X and improves food and water." Three separate reasons:

1. **Union, not partition.** Fig 9 :57-58: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."** The joint case is its own object.
2. **Per-slot residual.** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** A composite score can hide a nitrate loss behind a carbon gain.
3. **Valence is relative.** Fig 11 :50: **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."** Cover crops help nitrate and can hurt water availability in a dry year.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From the essay read this session, *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A list of famous soil scientists selected for likely agreement would be the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the three-slot claim a budget to be tried, let each recipient name what would refute it, and record that verdict before showing any theory.

## 6. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures and the sources opened this session. They are **not** a test of the soil claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What shape means for the WATSON answer

The WATSON verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` is therefore about the **form of the answer**, not a new measurement: the figure-derived constraint is that the question must be unbundled, anchored per slot and extended to third-order contact. The empirical signs are secondary world reports, each marked with its field, author, venue, year and overlap. Agreement among them is a pool, not a witness, unless their evidence is demonstrably different. The honest stop is: reachable in principle by a paired multi-site measurement, not settled in this folder.
