## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that soil restoration can meaningfully increase carbon storage while also improving food production and water quality. Option A: a single global yes with one headline number. Option B: a union compound of three slots, each with its own anchor, with a conditional empirical answer and a refuter written in advance. What each leads to: A hides a live water slot inside an average and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, three separate sign rows and the instruments that would settle them.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between a claimed joint benefit and three separate ground readings. Arity: three conditions. Symmetry: asymmetric, the slots can move in opposite directions under the same lever. Scale: plot, field and basin; years to decades for carbon, seasons for yield, storm events for water. Fig 9 :57-58 names the compound: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
- **Domain 1, soil carbon stock.** Actors: microbes, roots, mycorrhizal networks, clay minerals, organic amendments, tillage implements. Currency: SOC stock change per hectare at fixed depth or equivalent soil mass. Anchor: repeated paired coring plus laboratory analysis. Verdict domain: yes.
- **Domain 2, food production.** Actors: crops, farmers, pollinators, pests, inputs. Currency: yield and stability. Anchor: harvest measurement across seasons. Verdict domain: yes.
- **Domain 3, water quality.** Actors: rainfall, runoff, nitrate, phosphorus, sediment, receiving streams. Currency: nutrient and sediment load at an outlet. Anchor: paired water sampling. Verdict domain: yes.
- **Domain 4, habitat and conditions (carrier, not a verdict domain).** Actors: climate, texture, slope, season, land history, market. Currency: contact conditions. Anchor: weather station and soil survey. It governs the other three but gets no averaged verdict.
- **Partition or union:** union. Overlaps are carbon-yield coupling, erosion and dissolved carbon, nutrient management and runoff, and the triple whole-farm case. Each is a compound with its own loop.
- **Order by dependency:** instrument before score (Fig 3), describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9), unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54), settle each slot before the union.
- **Strain test:** the Fig 13 finiteness line was tried on carbon stock and retracted as a strain in `01`; saturation is world-sourced. No strain survives re-census for the verdict below.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figures decide the shape: this is a union compound, so it must be unbundled into three slots with separate anchors; a composite score is inadmissible; contact must be enumerated to the receiving water body; no practice has a standing good valence; and a bundle is an ordered molecule whose arrangement changes the outcome. The figures do **not** decide the empirical signs. From the world sources read, the sign table is conditional: carbon gains are real, finite and saturating; yields are usually not reduced but robust carbon-yield win-wins are uncommon; water-quality effects are lever-specific, with cover crops reducing nitrate leaching and no-till sometimes increasing it. The global "meaningful improvement in all three" claim is therefore **not established as general**. It is a `specified`, context-dependent candidate. This is a verdict about form plus a provisional empirical reading, not a ground settlement.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a success story instead of a three-slot measurement design, which Fig 2 :42 prices as *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The empirical reading is revisable by one paired multi-site trial without touching the figure-derived shape. No primitive has to be withdrawn; a null SOC result simply moves the carbon row to negative or zero and forces the union to be re-described.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A pre-registered multi-site factorial trial with paired carbon, yield and outlet-water measurement would settle the signs; a demonstration that all supporting reviews draw on one evidence pool would remove the weak corroboration; a factorial result showing practice order has no effect would reverse the molecule claim in `06`; a direct measurement of a third-order effect where none was enumerated would force a habitat re-census. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
