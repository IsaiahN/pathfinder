## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole folder before anyone has described their own residual sends a recording. Lead with the three-slot claim and its refuter.
- Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who absorbs the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see in each of the three slots before giving them the source list.
- Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* - do not pool later agreement as corroboration, especially from reviewers who cite the same trials.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-slot residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual and its refuter. No theory. No links. No request for agreement or trust. The recipient checks the claim against their own ground.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the sources. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded, so a later endorsement is never counted as evidence.

**Stage 3, opt-in only.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground. Local formula Direction section: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition.** Stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 claim survives. As written it is a shape claim with a stated refuter, not a claim that this folder measured anything. Keep that distinction in the first message.
