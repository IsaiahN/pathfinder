## The question restated as an effect

The question gives two options. Option A: a system whose only contact is text develops terms whose standing is settled by a ground. Option B: contact with the world or with other agents is necessary for that. What is being decided is not whether the system produces useful or coherent language. It is what would have to be present for a term to be *grounded* rather than merely declared or coordinated. In the figures' vocabulary, a grounded term is one whose standing is settled by an anchor that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks (Fig 2 :29), and a declared meaning is not such a term (Fig 10 :18, Fig 10 :49).

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula step "Direction", Fig 5 :65): a three-part shape, a commitment that can be violated, a non-updating anchor on the meaning question, and an import from a closure that differs on that question. The question's own nouns, "sensory" and "social", are candidate bindings onto that shape, not figure-licensed names for it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the conclusion.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure reasons, stated in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.
3. **Conventions and verdicts are different quantities.** Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. People are approached as checkers of a claim about the world, not as members of a convention.

Legally, public opt-in and institutional routes avoid the practical failure of unsolicited bulk email as well as the structural one.

## The verdict in one line, with its mode

Text supplies form and convention; it does not by itself supply a non-updating anchor on the meaning question. What grounding requires is stated in effect terms (a violable commitment, an unmoved anchor, an import from a differing closure), and the figures return that shape rather than a name for a particular sensorium. `specified` on that separation, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which discriminator would settle a concrete model. The whole sentence is a candidate, not a ground reading.
