## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `a term can be grounded by more fluent text` is a claim, not a shape.
- `grounding needs a commitment that can be violated` is a shape.
- `the required channel is camera input` is a name.
- `the required channel lets a non-updating ground refuse a term` is a shape.

The distinction is the difference between inventing an answer and narrowing what any answer must be.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect"* / *"rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and the searcher has *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here, the effect description is: a reading that can fail on the meaning question, a reference that does not move when the model speaks, and a partner or instrument whose closure differs. Nothing in that list picks a camera, a conversation partner, or a particular benchmark.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried quote): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* So the six are a visible choice, not nature's own list, and naming one as the required sensor turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

For this question, no ground reading is claimed and no source-gated import of a discriminator exists. So "sensory and social experience is necessary" is not licensed as a name. What is licensed is the effect shape. A particular binding can still be proposed, but only as a binding with provenance, recorded as the searcher's own contribution.

## 4. The source error on both sides of the question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Both options in the question make the same metamerism error in different ways:

- **"Grounded through text alone"** reads the fluent output (the projection) and names its source as understanding. The reading does not determine the source.
- **"Sensory and social experience is necessary"** reads the same requirement and names a source from a preferred frame before an instrument discriminates among sources.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* sharpens the first: writing carries context well, subtext partly, and almost never pretext, the *"you had to be there"* part. *"text is mostly context with some subtext and almost no pretext. A model trained on it learns a statistical stand-in for the history and sounds fluent."* And the practical test: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."*

The figures agree: Fig 2 :41 says agreement about a shared meaning is the product for a convention, while Fig 2 :40 says the same agreement is disqualifying as a verdict about the world. The two readings are a projection and a discriminator problem, not a volume problem.

## 5. The canonical shape for this question

Written down before the derivation, per Fig 5 :65: a **shape** for a grounded term has three parts:

1. **A commitment that can be violated.** Fig 10 :19: *"→ install what can be violated"*; Operators :58: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"*.
2. **An anchor that is constitutively about meaning and does not update on the frame.** Fig 2 :29 and Fig 2 :33; Fig 2 :36 on anchor legitimacy.
3. **Contact with a closure that differs on that question, so a correction can cross.** Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :45.

That is the answer the framework can return. It is a kind of thing, not a name. Sensory contact, social contact, a well-designed instrument, a court, a market or an experiment are all candidate frames that might satisfy the shape; Fig 9 :28 warns that *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the effect is not described first.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote §3 and §4 as if "shape, not a name" forbids mentioning sensory or social experience at all. That overstates the rule. The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. The effects it names (contact, a non-updating ground, a differing closure, a correction that can be refused) are already present in the figure text (Fig 2, Fig 6, Fig 10, Fig 11). Naming sensory or social contact as a **candidate binding onto those held effects** is a provenance matter, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong, and is **not** retracted, is naming a binding as if the framework derived it, and naming the source of a fluent reading without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The metamerism point in §4 stands; the reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held effects. Both readings are preserved here so neither is quietly lost. The same correction is recorded in `01` §7 R2 and checked independently in `11`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Gathering well-known names to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What "shape" is not, for this question

- Not a benchmark score. A score is a quantity the frame also produces (Fig 1 :27).
- Not a list of sensors. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- Not a claim about a specific model's internals. That is a ground question and no measurement was taken.
- Not a theory of everything. The question stays a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55).
- Not silence. Where no figure line governs the discriminator, the report is `gap` (Fig 13 :134).
