# Outreach draft: check the separator before deciding about the claim

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note for a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the distinction in F1 to F4 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is intact in the draft below. If the email collapses convention and grounding, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** Would you count this as a failure of a grounding claim?

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific separator I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the separator does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A term can be fluent, useful and widely agreed without being grounded. The question I am working on is what would have to be present for a term to be *grounded* rather than merely declared or coordinated. My first pass treated "text alone" and "sensory plus social experience" as two competing answers. I now think that is a bundled question with two different quantities and two different anchors.
>
> **The shape claim (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Grounding requires three things in effect terms: a commitment that can be violated, an anchor that is constitutively about meaning and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, and contact with a closure that differs on that question so a correction can cross. A text corpus is an import source, so text can add content. What a corpus anchors is which strings were written, not what a term refers to in a new situation. Shared agreement about a word is efficient for a convention and can still be worthless as evidence about the world. Sensory and social contact are candidate ways to supply the missing anchor; the framework I am using returns the shape, not the name.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that language models produce nothing, that scale can never help, that a benchmark score is a grounding test, or that sensorimotor contact is the one required primitive. I am also not claiming that this note is a measurement. It is a derivation checked for consistency against a fixed set of figure texts, which is a weaker thing.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these down before writing the claim:
> 1. A text-only term settled by an anchor that is about meaning rather than form, that does not update on the model's output, and that has actually refused a use of the term.
> 2. A text-immanent reading that is not a form distribution, not user agreement and not another text source, functioning as a meaning anchor.
> 3. An agent-only improvement, with contact held fixed, that changes a ground-verified reading on a grounding test.
> 4. Shared agreement shown to settle a world-facing question with no independent anchor, where the two cannot be separated by any instrument.
> 5. Two frames with identical evidence producing a correction neither could produce alone, with no third anchor.
>
> If any one holds, the separator is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** what is your instrument for the second condition? I am specifically looking for something that already returns a reading and fails to resolve, not a sensor specified in advance. I have no such instrument and I am recording that as a gap.
>
> **If you are curious, and only after your verdict on the above:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula, canonical branch (public repository): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - "Decoding Tacit Knowledge: Why Context, Subtext and Pretext Matter" (on context, subtext, pretext and why writing carries almost none of the last): https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/decoding-tacit-knowledge-why-context
> - "Climbing towards NLU: On Meaning, Form, and Understanding in the Age of Data", Bender and Koller, ACL 2020: https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.463/
>
> **If you want to use the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result before you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no automation, no misleading subject line, opt-out understood.
- Do not edit the refuter list after receiving a reply. If a checker fires one of them, record it as a refutation in `11_audit.md` with provenance, even if the wording is rough.
- Record their verdict on stage 1 before sending any theory link. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- A Track B reply is a conversation, never a witness. Prestige is a contact-cost estimate, not evidence.
