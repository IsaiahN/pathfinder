## QUESTION

Can language models develop grounded meanings through text alone, or is sensory and social experience necessary? (reasoning.txt line 81.)

Restated as an effect: can a term in a frame's library acquire a standing settled by an anchor that does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks, when the only external source available is written tokens? Option A: yes, text alone is sufficient. Option B: no, contact with the world or with other agents is necessary. What each leads to: A conflates a convention with a verdict, because agreement about a shared meaning is a product for a convention but disqualifying as evidence about the world (Fig 2 :40, :41). B names a frame before the effect is described, so any sensor or community will seem to fit (Fig 9 :27-28). The figure-derived route is a third thing: separate the two quantities, state the three-part shape, and report the discriminator as a gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, union or partition, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three-place (frame, term, ground), bundled by the question into a two-place choice. Symmetry: "grounds" is ordered and refusable, not a symmetric conjunction; "grounded" is a threshold standing, not a degree. Scale: one model loop, a population of rooms, and a habitat of contact with a lossy membrane (Fig 4 :20). Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The metric cannot be a single model-level fluency or agreement score (Fig 1 :27).
- **D1 Form prediction and convention.** Actors: model, corpus, tokens, users. Currency: prediction residual and shared agreement. Anchor: the held-out corpus for form, the user community for convention. Admitted for those questions only.
- **X1 Grounding from text.** Actors: same frame. Currency: claimed meaning with no reading that fails on the meaning axis. Anchor: **none nameable inside the frame.** Not a domain yet for grounding; it is a closure.
- **D2 Sensorimotor contact.** Actors: agent, sensors, effectors, world objects and affordances. Currency: categorisation and action that can fail. Anchor: the world's refusal, a measurement or outcome. Admitted.
- **D3 Social contact.** Actors: speaker, listener, community, shared history. Currency: repair and two-way correction. Anchor: an independent reading plus coordinated outcome, conditional on closures differing. Admitted with a condition.
- **D4 Instrument and verification.** Actors: experimenter and discriminator. Currency: readings that separate sources producing the same output. Anchor: the ground. Admitted; the gap lives here.

**Union, not partition.** D1, D2 and D3 overlap; a social partner can be a sensorimotor presence, and a world measurement can be reported in text. Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* One lever clears only the region where one condition binds. **Dependency order:** vocabulary then objective then refusal then loop then anchor (Fig 3 :20; Operators :58); describe the effect before choosing a frame (Fig 8 :53).

**Strain test applied.** The first pass read Fig 8 :32 as a claim that text adds no atom. That strains the figure and conflicts with Fig 6 :10. Re-census: Fig 8 :32 governs composition inside a fixed closure; Fig 6 :9-11 governs import from an external source, explicitly including corpus. Text imports atoms; the live question is about anchors. Recorded as retraction R1 in `01` §7.

## TETHER (primary, verbatim, per domain, triangulated | single-source | gap)

**D1 Form prediction and convention, status triangulated (Fig 6 and Fig 2, import versus anchor).**

> Fig 6 :10 *"nature · corpus · peer frame"*
> Fig 6 :14 *"the only operator that moves the wall"*
> Fig 2 :41 *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*
> Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*

Reading: text supplies real conventional meaning and imports atoms; that is the product, not a defect, but it does not settle a verdict about the world. Mode `specified`. Refuter: convention agreement shown to settle a world-facing question with no independent anchor.

**X1 Grounding from text alone, status triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 2, declaration versus anchor).**

> Fig 10 :49 *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*
> Fig 10 :18 *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*
> Fig 10 :19 *"→ install what can be violated"*
> Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when"*
> Fig 2 :33 *"The anchor must not update."*
> Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

Reading: there is no nameable anchor for meaning inside a text-only frame. A corpus anchors which strings were written, because that question is constitutively about the corpus; it is only an instrument for what a term means in a new situation. Mode `general` structural. Refuter: a text-only term settled by a constitutively meaningful non-updating anchor that has actually refused a use.

**D2 Sensorimotor contact, status single-source on the figure side (Fig 11 wiring versus habitat), with low-overlap world corroboration.**

> Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
> Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
> Fig 2 :45 *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

Reading: sensorimotor contact is a candidate binding because it supplies a refusal the frame cannot talk out of. It is not the name the framework returns. Mode `specified`. Refuter: a sensorimotor channel whose readings never refuse the frame's categories; that would be a harness, not a habitat. Fig 11 :71 acknowledges the capability claim has not been tested forward, and this is kept visible.

**D3 Social contact, status triangulated (Fig 6, Fig 8, Fig 10, differing closure).**

> Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
> Fig 8 :48 *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*
> Fig 8 :49 *"completely are one frame wearing two names."*
> Fig 10 :80 *"Frames whose independence is renewed by separate experience can be maintained"*
> Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

Reading: social contact grounds only when the partner's closure differs and both range against something neither can move. A partner sharing the corpus supplies a convention and is one frame wearing two names. Mode `specified`. Refuter: two frames with identical evidence producing a ground-correction neither could produce alone, with no third anchor.

**D4 Instrument and discriminator, status `gap`.**

> Fig 6 :32 *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."*
> Fig 6 :34 *"The question is not whether a sensor could exist. It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."*
> Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
> Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
> Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*

Reading: no figure line names a discriminator for a grounded term, and this run did not build one. The honest report is `gap`, with a search for a proto-instrument already returning a reading that fails to resolve. Unreached, not silence, and not an absence proof.

**Naming the required sensorium or community, status `gap`.** Fig 13 :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 9 :28 *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. The correction to my own earlier overstatement is kept visible in `05` §5b: the shape-not-a-name rule governs missing primitives, so naming a candidate binding onto held effects is provenance, while naming it as figure-derived is refused.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Text is an import channel and a convention generator, not a meaning anchor. By Fig 6 :10 a corpus supplies atoms; by Fig 2 :41 shared agreement is the product for a convention. A declared meaning is not a grounded one (Fig 10 :49), because grounding needs a violable commitment, an anchor that is constitutively about meaning and does not update on the frame, and a differing closure through which a correction can cross. The question's either/or is therefore a union of two quantities with different anchors, not one question with opposite answers. Sensory and social contact are candidate bindings for the shape, not names the figures return. Scaling the agent alone does not change contact (Fig 11 :40). No instrument here separates a grounded term from a fluent one, so that part is a declared `gap`, unreached rather than absent. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified` on the domains, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the discriminator, candidate throughout.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The cost is not a wrong sensor list, because no sensor is named. It is spending an outside checker's independence by asking for agreement with a convention while calling it a verdict, which Fig 2 :40 disqualifies and Fig 2 :42 prices (*"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*). A second cost is presenting an uncorrected first pass that says text adds no atom, which is why retraction R1 is visible in `01`. Both are avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter, and by keeping the retraction in the packet.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than anything struck out. The union split, the three-part shape, the six refuters and the re-census are recorded with provenance, so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it. A sent packet can be superseded without withdrawing the shape.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the six pre-registered refuters in `01` §5 would withdraw the shape: a text-only term settled by a constitutively meaningful non-updating anchor that has refused a use; a text-immanent meaning anchor that is not form, agreement or another text; an agent-only improvement that moves a ground reading; convention shown inseparable from verdict; identical-evidence frames producing a correction with no third anchor; or a verbatim figure line naming a required sensorium. A published discriminator that separates grounded from fluent terms on the same output would close the F11 gap and move the relevant domain toward `grounded`. Prestige and agreement are not such instruments.
