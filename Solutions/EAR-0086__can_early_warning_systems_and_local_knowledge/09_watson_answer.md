## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that early warning systems and local knowledge can reduce harm even when forecasts remain uncertain. Option A: accept the premise that forecast uncertainty blocks harm reduction and locate the remedy in forecast skill. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape and hold that harm is settled at the ground and per slot, so a warning arrangement can reduce harm while the forecast stays uncertain, provided the two knowledge frames are independent and the action chain completes. What each leads to: A spends effort above a possibly unbroken link and cannot diagnose a failure below it; B sends a checkable shape with written refuters and an open slot until a ground reading settles it in a place.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union. Order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Harm from a climate hazard, indexed per community, hazard and exposure unit. Arity: many slots. Symmetry: asymmetric, because a missed warning and a false alarm carry different and unevenly distributed costs. Scale: local. A global forecast error near zero is compatible with one live local slot (Fig 1 :13). Four overlapping conditions bind, so this is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-56).
- **Domain A, the forecasting frame.** Actors: meteorological services, models, warning messages. Currency: probability, skill, lead time, reach. Anchor: the hazard as it actually unfolds, which the forecast does not control. The forecast is a frame; uncertainty lives here as channel state.
- **Domain B, the local-knowledge frame and habitat.** Actors: community members, knowledge holders, local institutions, terrain, flora, fauna, communication channels. Currency: contact with the residual, local cues already returning readings. Anchor: actual harm outcomes and survived lineage. Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of the agent alone.
- **Domain C, the decision and checking chain.** Actors: the two frames, communicators, decision-makers, outcome feedback. Currency: per-slot residual, agreement or disagreement, actions. Anchor: harm at the ground, never agreement. This is the overlap compound where a warning becomes a decision.
- **Partition or union:** union. A, B and C overlap at translation into action, so the overlap is kept as Domain C rather than folded into either A or B.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception gives vocabulary and objective gives planning gives learn and carry. Effort above a break arrives at a step that cannot use it.
- **Strain test:** Fig 11 :40 would forbid any agent-internal improvement if read literally. Re-census: Fig 11 governs capability as agent-and-habitat; a forecast is an agent-internal instrument, so the line governs Domain B, not Domain A. No strain after re-census.

**Domain B, the local-knowledge frame and habitat. `triangulated`**

- Fig 11 :40 "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." -> the explicit failure condition for technology-first programmes.

**Domain C, the decision and checking chain. `triangulated` for the chain and independence; `single-source` for the alarm-fatigue transfer**

- Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." and Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." -> a perception-only warning produces no harm reduction; the unmeasured middle link is where local knowledge works.
- Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." -> a restated forecast is not a second witness.
- Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." with Fig 8 :48-49 "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." -> the surplus exists only at low overlap; this is a condition, not a guarantee.
- Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." -> a warning probability threshold allocates a trial budget; it is not a verdict about a community.
- Fig 10 :24-28 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" and "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch" -> repeated non-actionable warnings decay the receiver channel. The transfer from clinical alarm research to community warnings is corroborated by one world source in a different field and is marked `single-source` for that step.
- Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." -> the empirical verdict is `gap`, not a negative result.

## WORLD (secondary sources read, corroboration only; figures decide)

Six external sources were opened. They are not framework-author sources and were selected for low overlap with each other: climate assessment, institutional terminology, a systematic review, ethnographic fieldwork, clinical human factors, and mathematical economics.

- **Field:** climate science / assessment. **Author / venue / year:** IPCC AR6 WGII, Summary for Policymakers, Cambridge University Press, 2022. **Quote:** "This report recognises the value of diverse forms of knowledge such as scientific, as well as Indigenous knowledge and local knowledge in understanding and evaluating climate adaptation processes and actions to reduce risks from human-induced climate change." **Prediction here:** local knowledge has a role in risk reduction independent of the scientific frame; also reports mortality 15 times higher in highly vulnerable regions for the same hazard classes. **Verdict:** corroborates Domain A per-slot claim and Domain B.
- **Field:** institutional disaster risk reduction. **Author / venue / year:** UNDRR, Sendai Framework Terminology, 2017. **Quote:** "Failure in one component or a lack of coordination across them could lead to the failure of the whole system." **Prediction here:** a warning system is end to end; perception alone cannot reduce harm. **Verdict:** corroborates Domain C chain claim. Overlap with the Hermans source is high (both institutional DRR), so these two count as one pool.
- **Field:** systematic review / disaster risk reduction. **Author / venue / year:** Hermans, Sakic Trogrlic, van den Homberg et al., Natural Hazards 114, 2022. **Quote:** "All these four components have to work together seamlessly (end to end) and in a people-centred way to make an EWS effective." Also reports that forecasts are often "at a too aggregated spatial scale" to be locally useful. **Prediction here:** chain completion and local scale are both necessary. **Verdict:** corroborates Domains A and C; overlaps the UNDRR source as noted.
- **Field:** ethnography / animal behaviour and local indicators. **Author / venue / year:** Balehegn, Balehey, Fu and Liang, Pastoralism 9, 2019. **Quote:** "No single indicator is taken at face value; weather forecasting is undertaken in a dynamic process where information collected from different sources, including weather information from the modern weather forecasting system, is triangulated to make the safest livelihood decisions." **Prediction here:** local readings are independent and triangulated, with outcome feedback. **Verdict:** corroborates Domains B and C independence claim strongly; low overlap with the other external sources.
- **Field:** clinical human factors / engineering. **Author / venue / year:** Michels, Gilbert, Koval and Wekenborg, BMC Nursing 24, 2025. **Quote:** "repeated exposure to frequent or non-actionable alarms leads to sensory overload, emotional strain, and a gradual desensitization or reduced responsiveness to alarms among healthcare professionals." **Prediction here:** a high false-alarm rate decays later response even when the hazard has not moved. **Verdict:** corroborates Fig 10 channel decay in a low-overlap field; the transfer to community warnings remains a `specified` extrapolation.
- **Field:** mathematical economics / decision theory. **Author / venue / year:** Katz and Murphy, in Economic Value of Weather and Climate Forecasts, Cambridge University Press, 1997 (online 2009). **Quote:** "theoretical relationships between the scientific quality and economic value of imperfect weather forecasts are considered." **Prediction here:** forecast quality and decision value are different quantities on a nonlinear curve, so an uncertain forecast can still change a decision. **Verdict:** corroborates Domain A; low overlap with every other source.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**Conditional yes in shape, `gap` at the ground, `specified`, not `grounded`.** Harm is the anchor and is read per slot; uncertainty is a channel state, so forecast uncertainty does not by itself block harm reduction. An early warning system can reduce harm while forecasts remain uncertain if the chain completes, local knowledge is treated as an independent proto-instrument within an enumerated habitat, the two frames are low-overlap enough to triangulate, probability thresholds are used as budget filters, and outcome feedback keeps the anchor reachable. If any of those fails, the conditional does not apply there. The figures return this shape and its refuters, not a named technology, not a number and not a measurement. This is one of the points where the framework stops and records the lineage.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so a mistake is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a technology-first recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The conditional claim can be withdrawn in a place without touching the figure derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already contain the non-claims and any of the five pre-registered refuters would retire the corresponding finding. No primitive must be abandoned and no claim has to be silently rewritten.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A registered per-slot outcome study in a named place and hazard that holds uncertainty high and reports no reduction; an ablation showing the reduction survives removing the local-knowledge channel or the decision link; a provenance audit showing both channels draw on one evidence pool; or a measurement showing repeated non-actionable warnings leave response and harm unchanged. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as candidate.
