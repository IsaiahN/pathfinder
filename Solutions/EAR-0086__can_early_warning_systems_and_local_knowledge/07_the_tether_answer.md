# The Tether's answer on early warning and local knowledge under uncertainty

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not an evaluation. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Harm from a climate hazard is indexed per community, hazard and exposure unit. A forecast is a frame's prediction; uncertainty is a state of that frame's channel, not a change in the outcome. The question is whether an early warning system plus local knowledge can change actions so that harm falls while the forecast stays uncertain. It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: forecast skill, dissemination, local interpretability and capacity to act overlap, and a single lever clears only the region where one condition binds (Fig 9 :55-56).

## 2. The shape

1. **Uncertainty is in the channel, not in the ground.** Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 2 :29-30: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor." Harm to people is the right anchor for a question about harm to people (Fig 2 :37). So an uncertain forecast does not remove the thing being measured; it widens a prediction. The clause "even when forecasts remain uncertain" does not entail "cannot reduce harm".

2. **Harm must be read per slot.** Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A regional forecast error near zero can coexist with one exposed community carrying the whole live signal. Fig 1 :13: "a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state".

3. **The warning chain must complete.** Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Perception alone produces no harm reduction. The chain runs perception, vocabulary of affordances, planning, learn and carry. The unmeasured middle link is where local knowledge does its work.

4. **Local knowledge is a proto-instrument already returning readings.** Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 11 :39: "So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Dropping technology into a place without enumerating the habitat is an agent-only improvement. Fig 11 :43-44: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed. List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

5. **The two frames must be independent enough to triangulate.** Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are." Fig 8 :48-49: "How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus. Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names." A local message that restates the official forecast is not a second witness. Convergent independent readings are (Fig 8 :50).

6. **Probability thresholds are filters, not verdicts.** Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." A threshold allocates a trial budget; it does not decide that no action is owed to a community.

7. **The outcome channel must stay reachable.** Fig 10 :24-28 names the failure: stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings, after which "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch". Repeated non-actionable warnings decay the receiver's channel even though the hazard has not moved.

**Answer to the literal question:** yes in shape, conditionally, and unmeasured here. A warning arrangement can reduce harm while forecasts remain uncertain if all seven conditions hold. The figures return that shape, not a named technology and not a measurement.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that forecast skill is irrelevant. It is one of four binding conditions; effort above a break cannot rescue a lower one.
- Not that any particular local indicator works. Naming one is a provenanced import from the local frame, not a figure derivation (see `05` section 5).
- Not that more recipients, more agreement, or more prestige would corroborate anything. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that a technology dropped into a habitat improves capability. That is the agent-only error Fig 11 forbids.
- Not that this is `grounded`. No outcome study, harm count or forecast verification was run for this solution.
- Not that the figures return a name. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A registered outcome study in a named place and hazard, not a further argument. The corresponding questions are:

1. Does per-slot harm fall while forecast uncertainty is held high, measured against a pre-registered counterfactual?
2. Does the reduction survive removing the local-knowledge channel, or removing the decision link? If it does, the independence and chain claims are wrong.
3. Are the local readings shown to be independently produced, or do they draw on the same evidence pool as the official forecast? Provenance decides.
4. Does the warning channel remain reachable after a run of non-actionable alerts? Measure response time and harm, not message count.
5. Standing falsifiers from `01` section 3: a perception-only success, a skill-only explanation, a single evidence pool in every case, a moving outcome anchor, or unchanged response after repeated false alarms.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, at this budget.** The figures give the shape of an arrangement that can reduce harm and the conditions it must meet. They do not report harm anywhere. Fig 9 :42: "→ narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The verdict is `gap` at the ground, and a gap is a verdict, not a silence. What is owed is a counter in a place, not another derivation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that uncertain forecasts are useless, and it does not name a warning technology. It says that uncertainty is a state of the forecasting channel while harm is the thing the channel is trying to measure, so an uncertain forecast does not remove the outcome. Harm must be read per community, because a regional average can hide the one place still in danger. A warning only reduces harm if the chain runs past prediction into a local vocabulary of what the situation affords, a decision and a recorded outcome; local knowledge is not an ornament but a set of readings already returning a signal, and the two knowledge systems must be independent enough that their agreement means something. If the local message merely restates the official forecast, there is one frame wearing two names. The framework returns that shape and the falsifiers written in advance. It does not claim a number, a named indicator or a settled result: that requires an outcome study in a particular place, which is the ground this derivation cannot reach.

## 7. What an expert would be asked

One question, before any theory is shown: in the setting you know, which link stops the warning chain, and was that measured or assumed? A failure found there is more useful than an endorsement. See `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
