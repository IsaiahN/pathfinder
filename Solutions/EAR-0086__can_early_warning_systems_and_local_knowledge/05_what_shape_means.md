## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For EAR-0086, the shape is **not** a named technology (an app, a siren, a satellite feed, a particular local indicator). It is the narrowed set of constraints any harm-reducing warning arrangement must satisfy:

1. harm is read per slot, not as a regional average;
2. the outcome is the anchor and the forecast is only an instrument;
3. the two knowledge frames are independent enough that their agreement means something;
4. the chain runs from perception through a vocabulary of affordances to planning and learning;
5. the outcome channel stays reachable so the ground can correct the frame.

`SPACE(???)TIME` in the exemplar is a shape with a slot. The analogous form here is `WARNING → (???) → SAFER OUTCOME`: a warning must cross some local, situated link before it becomes an action. The `(???)` is not filled by the figures.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* bootstraps vocabulary by proximity: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the starting vocabulary is "harm", "warning", "uncertainty", "local observation". The new terms acquired from the figures are "slot", "anchor", "channel", "habitat", "proto-instrument", "dependency link". Each narrows what could count as an answer.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". So describe a warning in effect terms (what changes for a household before the flood arrives) rather than in instrument terms (which model produced the probability).
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the essay closest to this question: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Local knowledge is exactly the kind of history-bound knowledge that does not travel in a warning message. What travels is a described effect. The essay also says *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* (quoting Fig 11), and that *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* Both are the F3 and F4 claims in `01`.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here that means the shape of a good warning system is known before any specific local indicator is named.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For EAR-0086, an outcome study in a named place settles which indicator or which warning channel reduced harm there.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A local indicator imported into a warning protocol is legitimate if its provenance and its shadow (which gap it explains) are recorded.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a warning reading cannot name its own source. A color instrument reads green but *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The same green can come from several compositions. A hazard reading ("heavy rain likely") cannot say whether it came from a model, an ant-rout observation, or both. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* What is owed is a source-discriminating record, not a name guessed from the reading.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the status of the vocabulary: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A warning system built on one set of sensors and indicators is a visible guess. Treating it as nature's own list is the error.

## 4. Why the record cannot name the local indicator for you

The danger here is the inverse of the exemplar's error. In the physics case the record named Gradient where only a shape was licensed. Here the temptation is to name a specific local sign (ant behaviour, bird plumage, wind direction) as *the* answer to the `(???)` link. Balehegn et al. (2019) document many such signs, and explicitly report that *"No single indicator is taken at face value"* and that information is triangulated before a decision. The figures agree: no single frame settles a verdict, and an anchor is needed (Fig 2 :29-30).

So the record may list candidate indicators as **provenanced imports from the local frame**, each with a shadow test, but it may not present any one of them as the framework's answer. The framework returns the shape of the missing link, not its name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* puts the general limit: *"indiscernibility is not identity."* A warning that works and a warning that has been shown to work are different things; the second needs the ground.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming any specific local indicator in an early warning design. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Local indicators are not missing primitives; they are readings already held by a community frame, as Fig 6 :32-35 says of any proto-instrument: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."

**Naming a local indicator is therefore not forbidden.** It is a **binding** from the local frame onto a role already described by the shape (the situated link between warning and action). What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a seventh primitive or a new universal mechanism (there is no such slot);
- claiming the binding moves the wall without provenance (Fig 6 :23 says only an import from a differing closure does that, and it must be debited);
- claiming the framework selected the indicator (Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").

Both the original caution and this correction are kept so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this solution

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers well-known disaster-risk names to endorse the claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape in a place and let the outcome counts settle it. This is why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains institutions and public submission routes, not a ranked list of famous people.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."* And: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** an evaluation of any warning system and cannot settle the question it reports on. Mode `specified` is mandatory on every substantive claim.
