**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

## Question restated as an effect

The residual is harm from a climate hazard, indexed per community, hazard and exposure unit. A forecast is a frame's prediction of the hazard; forecast uncertainty is a property of that frame. The two options lead to different places. If an uncertain forecast cannot reduce harm, then effort belongs in forecast skill first and warning communication second. If harm is settled at the ground and read per slot, then an early warning system and local knowledge can reduce harm even while the forecast stays uncertain, provided two conditions hold: the two knowledge frames are independent enough to triangulate, and the chain runs past prediction into a decision and an action. What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: a per-slot harm reduction that survives a high forecast-uncertainty reading, with a stated refuter. See `01` section 3.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that early warning works.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that a large audience would corroborate the answer.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list of receptive readers is a mirror.
2. **Selecting for agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not this folder.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
