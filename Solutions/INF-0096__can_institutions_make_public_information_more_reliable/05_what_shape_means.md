# What "shape" means for INF-0096 - canonical reading from the essays

Sources read 2026-11-23, all by Isaiah N:

The canonical usage rule is local `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, also read this session. It says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"* and then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*

---

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"This means that you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed set of conditions that any working institutional intervention must satisfy, stated before anyone can name the correct label, threshold or source list. The shape is the three-condition conjunction in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.3: keep ground contact, keep reach to differing closures, keep conventions as conventions. A specific "trust and safety score" or a specific moderation rule is a name, not a shape.

This distinction is what stops the question from collapsing into a slogan. "Be reliable but fair" names no constraint. "Keep R_truth and R_silence in separate columns, range claims against a non-moving ground, preserve imports from differing closures, and do not let a convention decide an outcome" constrains.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* on vocabulary: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."* Here the first terms are "claim", "ground", "reach", "closure", "convention", "slot". They were taken from the figures, not from a field, so the residual was described before the frame was chosen (Fig 9 :27-28).
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* "Suppressing minority evidence" was restated as "cutting contact with a closure that differs, so no new atom can enter". That is an effect description, and it is why the field's own vocabulary (fact-checking, trust and safety, content moderation) was withheld until the census was done.
- *The Library of Babel* on bundled problems: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* That is Fig 9 :54-57 in essay form, and it is the reason the question was unbundled into three conditions rather than solved as one.
- Fig 2 :29 names the anchor as *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* In this domain the patient-outcome analogue is the per-claim return from the world, not an institution's sense of itself.

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A label, a threshold or a source list earns its name only when per-claim measurement pays for it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A community's evidence can enter the institution's library this way, but the record must show that it was imported rather than converged on by accident.

*"The Hard Problem is About Source"* gives the instrument discipline. Metamerism: a colour instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. It might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The essay states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a trust score reads "reliable" for a claim without being able to say whether the source was independent verification, familiar-source bias, or an evidence pool talking. Several paths create the same reading. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name, and this is why the source list row in F11 is marked `gap`.

## 4. Why "legitimate disagreement" is not a protected list

The question's phrase "legitimate disagreement or minority evidence" invites a list of protected speakers. The figures forbid a standing list. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49-50: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries. The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another."*

The canonical reading is therefore: what is preserved is **contact with claims that register against the ground for the current residual**, not an identity category granted permanent standing. This is not a permission to suppress. It is the reason preservation has to be implemented as an open habitat and an unauthored witness pool (Fig 10 :73-78) rather than as a curated roster. Seat Map :171 adds the honesty rule: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it, an undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

An institution's reputation, a famous fact-checker, a large follower count or a prestigious journal can give a checking method a budget to be tried. None of them can settle whether the method works. This is the precise reason `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains public institutional routes and no ranking of people by receptiveness or fame.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md`, `08_expert_verification_packet.md`, `09_watson_answer.md` and `11_audit.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the question and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Correction to my own section 3 and section 4

I first wrote that the framework's "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any particular institution or policy at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in Gamma. A trust score, a moderation rule, a source list and a community are already in the institutional library. Selecting one is a binding from the world frame onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in such a binding is the metamerism error of section 3: naming the source of a reading (a high trust score means reliable, therefore this source is good) without an instrument that discriminates among the paths that produce the reading. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. The practical consequence is unchanged: the shape can be stated now; the name awaits a discriminator.
