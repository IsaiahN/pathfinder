## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the pair (R_truth down, R_silence not up). Arity: two conditions with an intersection, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :54-57), not one hard question. Symmetry: both residuals are comparisons, but R_truth compares a claim with the ground while R_silence compares a closure's reach with the habitat; they are not interchangeable, and the comparison bond is ordered, not symmetric (Fig 12 :87). Scale: single claim check, population of claims under a ranking system, and the trust channel over time; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4 :21-30).
- **Domain 1, ground-contact verification.** Actors: claim, verifier, evidence, audience. Currency: R_truth reduction. Anchor: the ground, named in Fig 2 :29. Question type: partition over which compositions cross claim and evidence.
- **Domain 2, claim habitat.** Actors: claims, sources, communities, ranking rules. Currency: reach. Anchor: the ground for whether a suppressed claim would pay, and human judgment for the good-for-a-person part (Fig 2 :36-37). Question type: a binding from the world frame onto a held atom, not a new primitive.
- **Domain 3, institution library.** Actors: standards, routines, source lists, bonds. Currency: reach and ruling cost. Anchor: settlement, then promotion (Fig 5 :32). Question type: which rules may be composed over settled checks.
- **Domain 4, trust channel.** Actors: metrics, labels, percepts, reputation. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Question type: failure of contact, not failure of truth.
- **X1 Consensus as truth.** Actors: the crowd. Currency: agreement. **No anchor independent of the crowd.** Excluded.
- **X2 Procedural neutrality with no ground contact.** Actors: the moderator. Currency: procedure. **No nameable anchor.** Excluded.

Order by dependency: describe both residuals, refuse the one-dial framing before minting (Formula step 2), settle each first-order claim before composing a convention over it (Fig 12 :104), then promote the method (Fig 4 :22).

My census matches the primary's four domains, two exclusions and union reading. I got there by a different route: I started from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's level rule, whereas the primary started from Fig 1's per-slot residual and Fig 2's anchor test. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29/:31/:43 (anchor, independence, convention), Fig 6 :21/:23 (import and closure), Fig 9 :54-57 (union), Fig 11 :61-62 (asymmetric power).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: two residuals in separate columns, ground not consensus, imports from differing closures, introduction not subtraction, unauthored low-overlap witnesses.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an institutional policy demand, for example "publish a two-column integrity dashboard and require bridging review before a label is applied".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **competence condition** for a witness (Fig 2 :31 requires low evidence overlap, and Pennycook and Rand show familiarity matters). A two-column dashboard can be filled with correlated raters and look sound;
- the **unauthored selection rule** (Fig 10 :73-78). A real institution picking its own bridging reviewers authors the room however well it reasons;
- the **cascade check** after removal (Fig 11 :68-69). The shorthand says "introduction not subtraction" but does not carry the two-step invisibility with it;
- the **source-discriminating instrument** at the `gap` row. The shorthand can report a gap; it cannot name the instrument, and treating the word "gap" as if it closed the row would set R_T to zero by assertion.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure must sit between the two residuals, in the sense that ground contact and preserved reach cannot be collapsed into one score, and each is a comparison over a claim with a different anchor. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
