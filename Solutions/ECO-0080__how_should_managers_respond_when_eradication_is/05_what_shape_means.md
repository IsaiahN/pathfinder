## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule (local `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`): the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From The Library of Babel (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of a management answer you can state before you can name a species, a density, or a place. It constrains; it does not fill. "State the goal as a threshold comparison between invader density and unacceptable ecological change" is a shape. "Release agent X at site Y" is a name.

For this question the shape has four constraints, in the order derived in `01`: (i) the lever is not subtraction because exclusion is unavailable; (ii) the habitat must be enumerated outward to the cascade horizon, per slot; (iii) the objective is a comparison; (iv) containment is substitution, not removal. None of those selects an actor.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- The Library of Babel (opened 2026-11-23): "if you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"
- From Decoding Tacit Knowledge (as recorded in exemplar 05, read 2026-11-22): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- The Library of Babel on the union case: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level statement of Fig 9 :54-58 (F3 in the audit).

The derived shape was obtained in exactly that order: the residual was described in effect terms first (which slots, which cascade distance), and only then were the management literatures looked up. The Library of Babel warning applies directly: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit" (Fig 9 :27-28). Had "functional eradication" been chosen first, every result would have seemed to confirm it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome." For this question that means a local density-impact measurement, a cascade map, or a monitored outcome, not an argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Green and Grosholz's density-impact and recolonisation functions can cross as imports. Their agreement with the figure's ⋛ form is corroboration of shape, not a derivation of the threshold.

From They're Scanning Books When They Should (as recorded in exemplar 05, read 2026-11-22): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." For this question, declaring "the unacceptable level is X" is a choice made visible, not a fact delivered by the framework.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

The Hard Problem is About Source (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism that keeps a manager from naming wrongly. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Metamerism: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. The essay's rule: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a drop in target density is a reading, not a source. The same reading can come from genuine suppression, from redistribution into an unsampled patch, from a trophic release that removes a predator, or from a measurement channel that stopped registering the third-order effect. Naming "the invader is controlled" from the density reading alone is the metamerism error. F2's per-slot enumeration and Fig 10 :24 ("the ground does not decay, the channel does") are the two guards: keep the channel able to resolve sources, and keep the residual indexed by slot.

From The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." So a management plan that grades itself with "acres treated" or "predicates minted" is frame-internal scoring, the failure Fig 1 :24-27 names.

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the Tether mean by shape for this question?** The response form that any admissible management answer must have: subtraction of the actor is unavailable, so the manager works by introduction and contact change; the habitat is enumerated outward to the cascade horizon per slot; the goal is a threshold comparison, not a maximum-effort rule; containment is substitution with inherited actors and silent failure modes; the result is permanent monitored work, not a solved state.

**Does the framework name the control agent, the threshold, or the substitute habitat?** No. The specific import from ecology is a binding from that frame onto this residual; it requires provenance and a shadow test and is recorded as such. This was overstated in one earlier reading of this package: the phrase "shape, not a name" governs the missing thing, not the selection of an atom already held. Here nothing in the six-term substrate names a control agent or a density at all, so the limit applies without correction. No retraction is owed on that point.

**What settles the specific choice?** A ground reading. Until one exists, the specific choice is `candidate`, and the honest report is `gap` (F9).

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Also: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known invasion biologists who already agree that "acceptance is the right frame" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground (monitoring, an experiment, a cascade map) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** The same essay: LLMs "can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself". So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the management claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
