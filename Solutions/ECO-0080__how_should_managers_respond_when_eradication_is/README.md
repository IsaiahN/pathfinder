## Restating the question in effect terms

A manager is a seat inside a maintained habitat. The target is an established non-native actor. Two levers are normally available: exclusion or removal of the actor (eradication), and repeated action against it (suppression). The question states that the first lever is unavailable and that the second changes non-target actors and relations. The residual R is the gap between what the manager's frame predicts (more suppression moves the system toward a solved state) and what is observed (eradication cannot be achieved, and suppression itself degrades species and relations the manager did not intend to touch). R is indexed per slot: which actor, condition or relation is degraded, and how far the effect sits from the target. It is not one global "invasive species problem".

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction").** An answer is a response rule that (a) names which slots must be enumerated before action, (b) states the goal as a threshold comparison rather than a maximum-effort rule, (c) selects among introduction, contact-change and habitat-substitution using cascade rank rather than a standing good-actor list, and (d) treats the result as a maintained pattern with monitoring, not a solved endpoint. That description is written down here before the derivation so the preference is refusable by the same test as everything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that functional eradication or acceptance is the right answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own terms:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list ranked by likely agreement produces a convention, not corroboration.
3. **Showing the interiors before the verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally, harvested personal emails and unsolicited bulk mail breach GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional submission and contact routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
