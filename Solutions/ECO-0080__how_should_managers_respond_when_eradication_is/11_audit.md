## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions, in WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a prediction of monotone improvement under suppression and the two observations that the actor cannot be excluded and that the action degrades non-targets. Arity: two conditions that overlap, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55), not one hard problem; containment is a third, categorically different option. Symmetry: asymmetric contact, since introduction is available and exclusion is not. Scale: first order at the target, second and third order in the side effects. R is a slice per object slot, not a global number.
- **Domain 1, unmaintained habitat.** Actors: target, natives, predators, competitors, relations and conditions. Currency: survival, what the ground keeps. Anchor: the ground, which does not update. No seat. Question type: descriptive, not a design question.
- **Domain 2, maintained habitat.** Actors: the manager seat, instruments, introduced actors, the target. Currency: actions ranked by cascade. Anchor: the ground, which still decides even though the habitat updates. Question type: a union that must be unbundled; the seat acts but cannot subtract.
- **Domain 3, verification and contact.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence from domains 1 and 2 is zero because the evidence pool is shared.

Order by dependency: enumerate and acquire relational vocabulary before stating the objective; state the objective as a comparison before choosing a lever; refuse the eradication goal before minting a replacement; carry what worked only after it was verified. My starting figures were Fig 9 (union split) and Fig 3 (dependency), whereas the primary started from Fig 11 (habitat modes) and Fig 1 (per-slot residual). Different entry points, same three domain boundaries. That is weak structural agreement within one framework, not independent corroboration.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :44, :52, :61, :63, :66 to :69; Fig 1 :19 and :22; Fig 12 :112; Fig 9 :54 to :57.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` and `08`: exclude is unavailable, enumerate outward per slot, goal is a threshold comparison, containment is substitution, work is permanent.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a management recommendation for a specific invader at a specific site: which agent, what density, which containment geometry.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **per-slot indexing of R** (Fig 1 :19). The shorthand says "enumerate per slot"; a field recommendation collapses to a single impact score and the live slot disappears.
- the **residual-relative valence of actors** (Fig 11 :49 to :51). The shorthand says "no standing good-actor list"; a field recommendation names an agent and quietly inherits its valence from another case.
- the **two silent failure modes of substitution** (Fig 11 :68 and :69). The shorthand says "containment is substitution"; a field recommendation checks only whether the fence holds.
- the **permanence of the work** (Fig 11 :63). The shorthand says "not a solved state"; a field report declares success when density drops below target once.

One thing survives cleanly: because exclusion is unavailable, the response works by introduction and contact change and the habitat must be enumerated outward. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
