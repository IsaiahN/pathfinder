# Expert verification packet: invasive species management when eradication is impossible

**What this is:** a claim in a form an invasion biologist or land manager can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. Send this packet, not the whole framework.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** No local density-impact measurement, cascade map or trial result is claimed. This is a shape and a route.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

When the target cannot be excluded, the manager remains in a maintained habitat whose power is asymmetric: introduction is available, exclusion is not, and the work is permanent rather than a solved state. The side effects of suppression are cascade effects caused by scoping the habitat to direct contact, so the habitat must be enumerated outward through actors, conditions and relations until the cascade stops mattering, per slot and never as one averaged score. The goal must be a threshold comparison between invader density and unacceptable ecological change, with "eradication is the goal" refused as a negation rather than subtracted. Containment substitutes a habitat and inherits unchosen actors with two invisible failure modes, and no introduced actor inherits a fixed valence across a change of residual. The framework names none of the species, densities or sites; those are imports with provenance and a shadow test, or ground readings.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** in the maintained mode, exclusion is unavailable and the work has no terminal solved state. The consequence is that the response is introduction and contact change, not subtraction of the actor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :61 - "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 11 :32 - "→ introduce, never subtract." Fig 11 :63 - "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." Fig 11 :58 - "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there."
- **What to check:** does any real management system in the reader's experience restore exclusion once the established actor is embedded, without substituting a habitat? Does any programme reach a state where work and monitoring legitimately stop?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a manager in the maintained mode subtracting the habitat or excluding the actor with the authority the figure grants to introduction; or exhibit a solved state after which monitoring ceases.

### C2 - cascade and measurement; checkable by an invasion biologist against monitoring practice

- **Statement:** suppression side effects are second- and third-order, and the cause is first-order habitat scoping. The remedy is enumeration outward until the cascade stops mattering, with the residual indexed per slot. A single net-impact score is the named error.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52 - "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Fig 11 :44 - "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 - "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** in a case the reader knows, did the non-target effect sit at direct contact or further out? Did a single impact score hide a live slot? Pearson, Clark and Hahn 2022 (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9291768/) found unintended outcomes in 36% of reviewed cases and that screening more interaction types revealed more of them.
- **Refuter:** show the side effects are strictly first-order and fully captured by one global score with no live slot residual; or show that enumeration outward never changed a management decision in a documented case.

### C3 - the form of the goal and the form of containment; checkable by reading the Operators table and Figure 11

- **Statement:** the goal is a comparison between two quantities, not a maximum-effort rule; containment is substitution of a habitat, not removal; and actor valence is residual-relative.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :112 - "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond." Operators table: comparison is "a threshold relation between two quantities" and "a goal is a comparison, which is why a vocabulary without it cannot state one." Fig 11 :66 - "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68 - "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails." Fig 11 :69 - "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 11 :49 - "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."
- **What to check:** can the reader state a management goal that is not a threshold comparison and still give the plan a success criterion? Does a containment plan they know inherit unchosen actors or have an invisible failure mode? Green and Grosholz 2021 (https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fee.2277) define functional eradication as "suppressing invader populations below levels that cause unacceptable ecological effects" and say practitioners "must define 'unacceptable' change", which is the same bond form.
- **Refuter:** state such a non-comparative goal with a working success criterion; or show a containment action that substitutes no habitat, inherits no unchosen actors and has no failure mode invisible until the goal fails or the actor acts; or name an actor whose valence is fixed across a change of residual.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a species, a control agent, a dose, a release site, a density threshold or a substitute habitat. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that "do nothing" means abandoning the seat. Deliberate non-action with monitoring is a maintained-mode choice.
- Not that biological control or any particular introduction is safe. Cane toads and kudzu are "the same move with the cascade unenumerated" (Fig 11 :65).
- Not that suppression is useless; only that it is not sufficient in the intersection of the two conditions.
- Not a solved state, and not `grounded`.
- Not that agreement among managers or reviewers is evidence. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not that this simulated derivation can settle the claim. The author's RSI essay says the testable output must be derived by working the formula yourself.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not more agreement.

1. A per-slot cascade map that distinguishes real suppression from redistribution, trophic release, and a failed monitoring channel.
2. A local density-impact function and recolonisation rate that supply the two operands of the comparison, with "unacceptable" defined before the run.
3. A shadow test and provenance for any imported control actor, with its valence re-derived for this residual.
4. An explicit check of both invisible failure modes for any substituted habitat.
5. A documented case in which an agent-only improvement moved the ground (the framework's own standing refuter, Fig 11 :72).

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :61, :63, :52, :44, :49, :66, :68, :69 and Fig 12 :112 and the Operators entry for comparison. Nine short passages.
2. For one case you know, say whether the non-target effect was first-order or further out, and whether a single impact score hid a live slot.
3. Try to state the case's management goal without a threshold comparison. If you can and it still has a success criterion, C3 fails.
4. Name one instrument that would resolve the source of a falling density reading, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
