# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide whether the forest claim is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until section 3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` has been read and the refuter is reproduced exactly as written. The two-stage offer collapses on contact if the first-stage output cannot survive.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about forest tipping, with its refuter written in advance

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not
> asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the
> output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** "how close is this forest to a persistent shift"
> is not one number for all forests. It is, for each forest separately, a comparison between the
> forest's current state and a threshold, driven by a directed change, held inside a union of
> interacting conditions (warming, water, fire, pests, fragmentation, land use), and settled only
> by a ground that the measuring frames cannot move. The early-warning part of the claim is that a
> forest approaching such a shift should show a residual that persists and does not trend down:
> slower recovery, rising short-term memory, rising variability, occasional flicker. This is a
> shape and a route, not a measurement. I am not reporting how close the Amazon or the boreal
> forest actually is.
>
> **What I am explicitly not claiming:** that any particular indicator names a threshold; that
> critical slowing down is universal (Xu et al. 2023 shows regime shifts that do not exhibit it);
> that a satellite index is the ground (it is an instrument, and Magney and Pierrat 2025 lists its
> failure modes); that abruptness or irreversibility is required (Börner and Dijkstra 2026 defines
> a tipping event as a persistent nonlinear transition driven by self-amplifying feedback); or that
> a ranked list of forests is evidence. Agreement among groups using the same satellite product is
> one witness, not several.
>
> **What would refute it, written before I ran anything:**
> 1. A single aggregated index predicts each forest's persistent shift better than per-forest
>    indicators.
> 2. An adequately instrumented forest makes a persistent nonlinear transition with no prior
>    residual that persists and does not trend down.
> 3. Two frames I counted as independent turn out to share one evidence pool, and I used their
>    agreement as corroboration.
> 4. One control variable with no interaction terms predicts shifts in both Amazon and boreal
>    forests at comparable skill.
> 5. An observed shift is persistent but linear, with no self-amplifying feedback.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me
> than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the public sources. The figures, not my summary of
> them, are the authority.
> - Figures and formula, canonical SVG set: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Forest tipping definition (open access): https://esd.copernicus.org/articles/17/1671/2026/
> - Early-warning method (open access): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3398887/
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a forest you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.
>
> [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Output and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." Frames must range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." |
| Pre-registered refuter | A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not an honesty device. |
| Mode stated on every claim | THE_FORMULA.md: "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." |
| No headline score | Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." |
| Union of conditions, not a single cause | Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." |
| Indicators held as candidates | Fig 5 :59 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at" is a ranking test, not a certificate. |
| No request for trust or prestige | From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action." |
| Links only after the verdict is recorded | Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." |
| Offer the formula as a method | Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." |

## Contact discipline

- Public institutional submission and contact routes only, listed in `03_public_contact_points.csv`.
- One-to-one. No bulk send, no automation, no misleading subject line.
- The recipient's stage-1 verdict is recorded before any framework material is shown. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
- An opt-out line is included in every message. Public opt-in channels avoid both the structural failure and the legal one.
