**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.**

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION, not a ground reading.** This WATSON run synthesizes material from `01`, `05`, `06`, `07` and `08`. By the framework's own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22: "the output is only as good as the validator. ## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what an answer to "how close are major forests, including the Amazon and boreal forests, to persistent shifts in structure or function?" must look like. Option A: one global closeness score per forest, forests ranked and averaged, the score treated as a verdict. Option B: a per-slot threshold comparison, a union of interacting drivers, triangulated low-overlap frames, an ordinary bound definition of "persistent shift," and an open slot until a ground settles it. Option A leads to a headline that hides live signals and manufactures agreement inside one evidence pool. Option B leads to checkable sub-claims, per-forest refuters, and an instrument list. The figures decide B; world sources only corroborate.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between observed forest state and an answer about a persistent shift. Arity: many slots (Amazon, boreal, others), not one object. Symmetry: asymmetric, since ⋛ is an ordered threshold relation, not a symmetric join, and the recovery path differs from the collapse path. Scale: first order inside a forest, second order at the biome and Earth-system level. Fig 9 names a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, forest state and resilience signal.** Actors: tree cover, canopy indices, lag-1 autocorrelation, variance, return rate. Currency: resilience indicators as residuals. Anchor: measured recovery after perturbation and observed regime settlement. Question type: partition (which signals carry live mass).
- **Domain 2, forest habitat.** Actors: fire, drought, pests, permafrost, moisture recycling, fragmentation, land use. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the forest's actual response, not a model's. Question type: **union** (overlapping conditions), not a list.
- **Domain 3, monitoring and verification.** Actors: satellites, plots, flux towers, paleo records, models. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is low when all frames draw on one satellite product or one model ensemble.
- **Domain 4, decision and management.** Actors: managers and assessments. Currency: margin of safety, reversibility. Anchor: whether the forest actually persists. Introduction is available; exclusion is not, so this is permanent work.
- **Order by dependency.** Bind the definition of "persistent shift" before choosing an indicator (the formula's Direction rule); settle first-order molecules before second-order comparisons (Fig 12 :104); enumerate the habitat before fitting a control variable (Fig 11 :43); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: the catalogued ambiguity of "tipping" (Börner and Dijkstra 2026) is not a strain on Fig 12; it is a missing bound term, which the formula says must be made an ordinary term. The universality claim of critical slowing down is a genuine conflict with a strong reading of Fig 5 :59; the conflict sends us back to per-domain verdicts, not to a softer verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a proximity number does not. Closeness is per forest, not a global rank. A persistent shift is a self-amplifying nonlinear transition, so abruptness and irreversibility are not defining. The approach signal is a residual that persists and does not trend down, held as a candidate rather than a certificate. The drivers form a union whose habitat must be enumerated, and corroboration must come from low-overlap frames ranged against the forest itself. The framework returns this arrangement, not a number of years. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest world corroboration comes from a field outside ecology (Rizzi et al. 2025), which uses the same distance-to-threshold structure with no shared forest data.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, sub-claims and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a ranking instead of a shape. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No forest's status is asserted, so nothing factual must be withdrawn. The shape can be re-specified if a refuter fires, and the management implication (build a margin of safety rather than find the exact tipping point) survives independently, as Lovejoy and Nobre 2018 argues.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates recovery slowdown from trend and sensor artifacts would move the D1 signal toward `grounded`; persistence and feedback tests would settle whether a shift occurred; a one-control-variable result across both biomes would break the union claim; a demonstration that critical slowing down is absent in a well-instrumented shifting forest would break the strong D1 reading; a figure re-census admitting ⋛ over settled molecules at second order would clear the type strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
