## What this folder answers, in effect terms

The question asks for a decision about "closeness." Two readings lead to different actions.

- **Reading A (refused as written):** closeness is one global number per forest, and the major forests can be ranked and averaged into a single "how close are the world's forests" headline. This leads to a score that hides per-forest live signals and invites a verdict without a ground.
- **Reading B (the shape kept):** closeness is a comparison, per forest, between a current state and a threshold, driven by a directed gradient, held in a union of interacting conditions, and settled only by a ground that the frames cannot move. This leads to checkable sub-claims, per-forest refuters, and an instrument list.

The derivation keeps B and states why A is refused. It does not report how close any particular forest actually is.

## What is deliberately not here

- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.

- No numerical answer to "how close." That needs a ground reading, which this simulated derivation cannot perform.

## Honest standing of the whole folder

This is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-22): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." It cannot settle the question it reports on. It can only give the shape any answer must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.
