## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for EAR-0043 a **shape** is the form that any answer about forest closeness must take, statable before we can fill it with a number for a particular forest. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape here is:

> per forest slot, a threshold comparison, driven by a directed gradient, inside a union of interacting conditions, with a triangulated ground and a written refuter.

A headline "the world's forests are X years from tipping" is not a shape. It is a name put on a slot the instruments have not settled.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap by proximity. Use the vocabulary already known that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *The Hard Problem is About Source*: when contact is not possible, the best that can be sent is a plain description of what one would expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world. The essay's mechanism is metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." Several different sources can give the same reading.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place." The essay's paradox is that by then you have composed the answer through the process of locating it.

Applied here: "closeness" is a word borrowed from distance and time. Described in effect terms, it becomes "a comparison against a threshold that the ground can settle," and the vocabulary narrows toward resilience, return rate, autocorrelation, flicker, control parameter, and self-amplifying feedback. Each of those is a term gained, not a number claimed.

## 3. What licenses a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." For a forest the anchor is the forest's actual persistence or shift, not any index of it.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the first: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A rising NDVI autocorrelation may reflect recovery slowing, a trend artifact, seasonal structure, or sensor change. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The essay's own example is decisive: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the source.

Hirsch and Koren 2026 states this for forests directly: temporal autocorrelation "does not uniquely indicate proximity to a tipping point." Magney and Pierrat 2025 adds the instrument-level version: satellite indices are indirect proxies and misclassification is common in transitional boreal vegetation.

## 4. Why a "closeness score" is the specific forbidden error

A score reads the artifact (one green reading) and names the source (imminent tipping) without an instrument that discriminates among the sources. This is the metamerism error. It is also the aggregation error: Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The Amazon slot and the boreal slot do not share a single control, so averaging them produces a number that cannot decide either.

Keeping the shape open does not make the answer vague. It makes the answer checkable: instead of "close," we get five refuters and a list of instruments that would move the claim toward `grounded`.

## 5. Constraint on the verification setup

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." This is why `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sends the claim and its refuters before the framework, and why the recipient's stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown.

The same essay on prestige: "Prestige is not equal to actions." and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." A long list of respected signatories does not change a forest's recovery rate. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
