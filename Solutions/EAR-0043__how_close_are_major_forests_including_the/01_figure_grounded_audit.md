## 0. The residual in effect terms

**R** is the gap between what is observed in a forest (state, trajectory, indicators) and an answer to "is a persistent shift in structure or function imminent or underway, and how close is it?"

- **Arity:** many slots. The Amazon and the boreal forests are different object slots, not samples of one object.
- **Symmetry:** asymmetric. A threshold comparison ⋛ is ordered ("is it about which is larger, rather than both being present?"). Forest to savanna is not the reverse of savanna to forest; the recovery path differs from the collapse path.
- **Scale:** first order at the forest (a state, a structure between states, a state); second order at the biome and Earth-system level where forests compose with climate, fire, permafrost, and land use.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run, per the formula's "Direction"):** a per-slot comparison, against a named threshold, driven by a directed gradient, over a union of interacting conditions, corroborated by low-overlap frames, with the ground named and the refuters written in advance. A number without those properties is not an answer to this question; it is a score.

**Options and what each leads to:**

| option | what it leads to |
|---|---|
| A: one global closeness score, forests ranked and averaged | a headline that hides live per-slot signals and manufactures agreement inside one evidence pool |
| B: per-slot comparison, union habitat, triangulated ground, open threshold | checkable sub-claims, per-forest refuters, an instrument list, and an honest "not settled" |

This audit keeps B. A is refused in F1.

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

R by arity, symmetry and scale is stated in section 0. One line per domain gives actors / currency / anchor. A domain without a nameable anchor is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Forest state and resilience signal | tree cover, canopy indices, lag-1 autocorrelation, variance, return rate | resilience indicators as residuals | measured recovery after a perturbation; observed persistence of a new state | partition: which signals close to threshold |
| D2 Forest habitat (Earth systems) | fire, drought, pests, permafrost, moisture recycling, fragmentation, land use | contact and cascade | the forest's actual response, not a model's | **union**, not partition |
| D3 Monitoring and verification | satellites, field plots, flux towers, paleo records, models | residual versus agreement | ground, never agreement | triangulation across low-overlap frames |
| D4 Decision and management | managers, agencies, assessments | margin of safety, reversibility | whether the forest actually persists | permanent work, not a solved state |

**Why union, not partition.** The closeness of a forest to a shift is a compound of overlapping conditions (warming + drying + fire + fragmentation), not a list of separable causes. Removing one condition leaves the intersections standing. This is decided by the census and corroborated by Lovejoy and Nobre 2018 (negative synergies) and Lenton et al. 2008 ("complex interplay").

**Order by dependency (Fig 3):** perception (can we see the state?) → vocabulary (do we have terms for what a forest affords?) → objective (what counts as a persistent shift?) → planning (which instrument?) → learn and carry. The middle link is the one that is least instrumented in this field, so it is the one that must be made explicit.

## 3. Findings

### F1: Closeness is per slot; the global score is refused (decisive for the reading)

Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."

Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

Fig 2 :29 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

A single mean "closeness of major forests" deletes the only quantity that says where to look. The Amazon and the boreal forest have different controls, different structures and different transition timescales (Lenton et al. 2008 lists Amazon ≈50 yr gradual, boreal ≈50 yr gradual, with different controls). **Mode: `general` (structural).** Refuter: an aggregated index that, on a held-out set of forests, predicts each slot's persistent shift better than per-slot indicators. If that were shown, Fig 1's slot rule would be wrong for this domain.

### F2: "Persistent shift" must be an ordinary term before the run

The formula's Direction rule says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question, so the sought-for shape must be written down, bound, and allowed to accrue a residual. The shape claim bound here is from Börner and Dijkstra 2026, opened 2026-11-22: a tipping event is "a persistent nonlinear transition driven by a self-amplifying feedback process," which explicitly drops abruptness and irreversibility as necessary criteria.

Fig 12 :112 "A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."

Fig 2 :36 "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."

The question is constitutively about forest state, so the forest is the anchor; the indicator is an instrument, not the anchor. **Mode: `specified`.** Refuter: an observed forest change that is persistent but linear with no self-amplifying feedback. Under the bound definition it would not be a persistent shift of the kind being asked about, and the bound term would have to be re-specified.

### F3: The early-warning signal is a residual that persists and does not trend down

Fig 5 :59 "a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at"

Fig 13 :47 "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."

Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"

World corroboration is strong and independent of the framework: Dakos et al. 2012 documents rising lag-1 autocorrelation, variance, spectral reddening and flickering as generic leading indicators; Hirsch and Koren 2026 reports that rising temporal autocorrelation in kNDVI flags declining resilience across about one quarter of the world's ecoregions, including the Madeira-Tapajos moist forests; Rizzi et al. 2025, in a completely different field (epilepsy), finds that an EEG critical-slowing-down measure tracks "the distance of the system from ictal threshold" better than counting events, including when events are rare. **Mode: `specified`.** Refuter: an adequately instrumented forest that makes a persistent nonlinear transition with no prior persistent, flat residual; or a forest where the indicator rises and falls without any state change (false positive). Xu et al. 2023 explicitly warns "regime shifts do not all exhibit critical slowing down" and that CSD "may not be early enough to allow an intervention." That is a live limit on this finding, preserved here rather than hidden.

### F4: Corroboration must be triangulation, not pooled agreement

Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."

Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

Fig 7 :13 "agreement compounds, evidence does not"

Ten remote-sensing studies using the same MODIS product are one frame wearing ten names. Magney and Pierrat 2025 makes the point directly: satellite records struggle with patchy boreal vegetation, understory greenness, snow and cloud, and a 20 to 40 year window "often entails a space-for-time substitution which limits our ability to differentiate gradual change from abrupt transitions." Their remedy is explicit: combine long-term records, controlled experiments and satellite observations. Hirsch and Koren 2026 itself concedes that temporal autocorrelation "does not uniquely indicate proximity to a tipping point." **Mode: `general`.** Refuter: a demonstration that two apparently independent methods draw on one underlying dataset or model ensemble, and their agreement was counted as corroboration.

### F5: The habitat is enumerated, and the compound is unbundled

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."

Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."

Lovejoy and Nobre 2018 writes that "negative synergies between deforestation, climate change, and widespread use of fire indicate a tipping point" and puts the estimate at 20 to 25 percent deforestation, while Lenton et al. 2008 says "the fate of the Amazon may be determined by a complex interplay between direct land-use change and the response of regional precipitation and ENSO to global forcing." Magney and Pierrat 2025 lists the boreal feedback set: fire, insects, wind, albedo, water availability, permafrost. Fire is a benefactor or a malfactor depending on which residual is tracked; valence is relational. **Mode: `specified`.** Refuter: one control variable, with no interaction terms, that predicts persistent shifts in both the Amazon and the boreal forest at comparable skill. That would dissolve the union and send us back to the census.

### F6: The channel decays; the ground does not

Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"

Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

A loss of field plots, flux towers or long-term time series does not change the forest; it changes our reading of it. Lenton et al. 2008 says early warning depends on extracting a timescale from time-series data, and Dakos et al. 2012 notes that "in many systems, sampling schemes are designed explicitly to avoid temporal autocorrelation," which is exactly the property an early-warning indicator needs. Magney and Pierrat 2025 notes that detecting boreal transitions "is dependent on the spatiotemporal resolution of available data." **Mode: `specified`.** Refuter: a counterfactual in which degrading the monitoring channel leaves the estimate of closeness unchanged. If that held, this finding would be empty for this domain.

### F7: The verdict available here is a shape, not a proximity value

Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"

Fig 13 :136 "It can characterise what is missing in effect terms, which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it."

Fig 13 :138 "narrowing gets you close while the ground decides"

Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."

The framework returns the shape of an answer: a threshold relation, a directed gradient, a union of conditions, a triangulated ground. It does not return "the Amazon is 6 years away." From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-22): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." **Mode: `general`.** Refuter: an instrument that returns a discriminated threshold value for a forest, with the source of the reading distinguishable. That would move the claim toward `grounded`.

### F8: Filters are not witnesses

Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

A tier, a rank, a large ensemble or a famous reviewer decides what gets tried first; none decides whether a forest is close. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." **Mode: `general`.** Refuter: a verdict that changes when the same evidence is re-presented by a different institution. If that happened, prestige was doing the work of a witness.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A single aggregated index predicts each forest's persistent shift better than per-slot indicators (breaks F1, Fig 1 :19).
2. An adequately instrumented forest makes a persistent nonlinear transition with no prior residual that persists and does not trend down (breaks F3, Fig 5 :59).
3. Two frames counted as independent are shown to draw on one evidence pool, and their agreement was used as corroboration (breaks F4, Fig 2 :31 and :40).
4. One control variable with no interaction terms predicts shifts across Amazon and boreal at comparable skill (breaks F5, Fig 9 :54 and Fig 11 habitat enumeration).
5. An observed shift is persistent but linear with no self-amplifying feedback (breaks the bound term in F2).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or re-specified. Until one is run, no part of this folder is `grounded`.
