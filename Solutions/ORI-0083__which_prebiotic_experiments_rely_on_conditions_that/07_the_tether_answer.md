# The Tether's answer on prebiotic experiments and unrealistic conditions

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claim, `general` on the structural shape, `grounded` only on the narrow bench fact that a stated product formed under stated conditions as reported by an instrument. Nothing about early Earth is `grounded` here.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A compound is made in the lab under condition set C and is used as evidence about early Earth's condition set E. R is the per-slot mismatch between C and E, not a verdict on a theory. It is a union compound in two conditions: Fig 9 :57-58 - *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

## 2. The shape

1. **A bench experiment is a substituted habitat.** Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Success under C is evidence that the mechanism can run under C. It is not evidence that the pathway ran on Earth.
2. **The two failure modes are silent.** Fig 11 :68 - *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; Fig 11 :69 - *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."* These are exactly the two ways a condition can be unrealistic: an omitted Earth factor, or an introduced bench-only factor.
3. **Conditions are enumerated from the world, not composed from theory.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* The slots are atmosphere redox state and duration of reducing episodes, energy source and time profile, concentration mechanism, mineral surfaces and vessel walls, pH, temperature, starting-material purity and enantiomeric enrichment, and competing sinks. Order them by cascade (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).
4. **Conditions remake what a method becomes.** Fig 4 :34-35: *"The level a method lands on remakes it. A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes."* The same recipe in a substituted habitat is a different term; the round-trip loss is `R_T` and must be measured (Fig 4 :30).
5. **"Unrealistic" is a filter until witnessed.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A condition claim becomes a witness only when an observation could have returned the opposite result.
6. **The figures return a shape, not a list.** Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name"*; Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The names of particular experiments come from world sources and are revisable.

## 3. Which experiments, as far as the world sources take us

This section is **secondary**, mode `specified`, and carries no figure authority. It names candidates whose condition slots are currently disputed. It does not declare them impossible.

| experiment or route | condition that may differ from early Earth | why it is disputed, from sources actually read |
|---|---|---|
| Miller-Urey spark discharge (1953) | strongly reducing CH4/NH3/H2 atmosphere maintained continuously | Wikipedia (Miller-Urey) reports that recent models point to a weakly reducing CO2/N2 atmosphere, while post-impact models allow transient strongly reduced episodes. The slot is **duration and timing**, not "wrong atmosphere". |
| Oró adenine from concentrated HCN | high, sustained HCN concentration | Cleaves 2012 and the prebiotic-atmosphere page: HCN production depends on atmosphere redox state; in a global ocean cyanides would be too dilute without a local concentrating mechanism. |
| Sutherland pyrimidine route | continuous UV for photoanomerisation, phosphate control, enantioenriched glyceraldehyde, cyanamide and cyanoacetylene supply | Wikipedia (RNA world) records the route and its dependence on these conditions; Sutherland himself described the strict RNA world as a "restrictive, hypothetical arrangement". |
| RNA-oligomerisation and ribozyme experiments | availability of a single enantiomer, stable ribose and cytosine, strand-separation cycling | Wikipedia (RNA world): cytosine half-life 19 days at 100 C and 17,000 years in freezing water; wrong chirality terminates a chain. Recent work uses plausible cyclic phosphate ligation and oscillating Hadean environments to address this. |
| Hydrothermal-vent schemes | local concentration, mineral surfaces, high pressure and temperature | Prebiotic-atmosphere page: cyanides would be too dilute in an ocean; shallow vents with evaporative cycles or ferrocyanide lakes are proposed fixes. |
| Any spark-discharge experiment in glassware | catalytic vessel surface | Miller-Urey page: a 2021 comparison found borosilicate glass acted as a mineral catalyst relative to Teflon. The vessel is a condition, not a neutral container. |
| Experiments using purified starting materials | purity and absence of competing sinks | Cleaves 2012: organic compounds "would need to be concentrated and complexified by environmental mechanisms", and the primitive inventory was far more complex than a purified reagent set. |

Each row is a **hypothesis about a slot**, not a verdict. The Miller-Urey row is the clearest example: a condition once treated as simply wrong is now a question about the duration of post-impact atmospheres (Zahnle et al. 2020, arXiv:2001.00095).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any experiment is impossible, fraudulent, or worthless. A substituted habitat is a legitimate instrument for finding wiring.
- Not that the reducing-atmosphere critique is settled. It is a live, uncertain slot.
- Not that the RNA world, metabolism-first, or any other theory is refuted. The question is about conditions, not theory choice.
- Not that a meteorite match proves convergence. Provenance separates convergence from import (Fig 8 :51-52).
- Not `grounded` about early Earth. The Earth anchor returns ranges, not point values.
- Not a claim that more replications settle the transfer. Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating observation, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the question was settled *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

The corresponding questions here:

- **Atmosphere slot:** can the rock record or atmospheric models constrain the duration and timing of strongly reduced episodes? If yes, the Miller-Urey entry is settled either way.
- **Concentration slot:** do mineral or evaporite signatures show that local HCN/nucleotide concentrations reached the levels the bench used, at the right time?
- **Stability slot:** do temperature and pH reconstructions exclude or permit the stockpiles the route assumes?
- **Surface slot:** did the relevant mineral surfaces exist at the required abundance and exposure?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** habitat identity for a named experiment; direct transfer under reconstructed conditions; a figure-level reversal of Fig 11 :70; a specific measurement showing the disputed condition was present.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figures give the shape and the refuter and stop where the instruments stop. The Earth's condition values are reconstructed with uncertainty, so slot-by-slot comparisons inherit irreducible error. The list in §3 is ranked by how load-bearing and how uncertain each slot is, and kept reversible (Fig 9 :41: *"so keep every cut ranked and reversible"*). Indiscernibility is not identity: a bench result that looks like an early-Earth pathway is not thereby the same pathway. The lineage is the record that makes this checkable.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A prebiotic experiment is a substituted habitat, so a successful synthesis is evidence that a mechanism works under the conditions used, not that it worked on early Earth. The conditions that may have been unrealistic are exactly the ones the setup left out or introduced: atmosphere redox state and how long a reducing episode lasted, UV profile, concentration mechanism, mineral surfaces, pH, temperature, starting-material purity, and competing sinks. The Tether does not name a guilty experiment; it returns the shape a checkable answer must have, a per-slot comparison with the bench value, the reconstructed Earth range, a discriminating observation and a written failure. The lab facts are settled by instruments; the transfer is not. What would close it is a measurement of a disputed condition at the right time and place, not another argument or another replication in the same habitat.
