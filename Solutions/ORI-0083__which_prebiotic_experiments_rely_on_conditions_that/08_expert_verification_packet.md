# Expert verification packet - prebiotic experiment conditions and early Earth

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist, geochemist or planetary scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the transfer claim; `grounded` only on the narrow bench fact that a stated product formed under stated conditions as reported by an instrument; `general` on the structural shape. No claim about early Earth is `grounded`.**

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A laboratory prebiotic synthesis is a solve in a substituted habitat. Its success demonstrates wiring under the bench condition set, not capability under reconstructed early-Earth conditions. The conditions that "may have been unrealistic" are the slots omitted from or introduced into that habitat: atmosphere redox state and the duration of reducing episodes, energy source and time profile, concentration mechanism, mineral surfaces and vessel walls, pH, temperature, starting-material purity and enantiomeric enrichment, and competing sinks. A checkable answer is a per-slot comparison with a bench value, a reconstructed Earth value with uncertainty, a discriminating observation, and a written failure. The figures return that shape and its refuters; they do not return a list of guilty experiments.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** a bench setup is a substituted habitat, and a synthetic solve in it proves wiring, not capability. The two failure modes are silent: what was left out is invisible until the goal fails, and what was introduced is invisible until it acts.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :70 - *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."* Fig 11 :68 - *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; Fig 11 :69 - *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."*
- **Refuter:** show a bench solve in which the substituted conditions are identical, in every load-bearing slot, to the reconstructed Earth conditions; or show that Figure 11 admits a synthetic solve that counts as capability at the same level.

### C2 - Earth-facing; checkable by a geochemist or planetary scientist against the cited sources

- **Statement:** the condition slots are load-bearing and their Earth values are unresolved. The Miller-Urey reducing atmosphere is a question about the duration of transient post-impact episodes, not a settled error; concentration and starting-material stability are open; the vessel surface is a condition.
- **What to check:** Wikipedia (Miller-Urey) states that recent models point to a weakly reducing CO2/N2 atmosphere and that transient hydrogen-rich atmospheres follow large impacts; arXiv:2001.00095 (Zahnle, Lupu, Catling and Wogan, 2020) resolves the redox paradox with a reduced late veneer and ocean-vaporising impacts. Wikipedia (Prebiotic atmosphere) states the exact composition is unknown and records the cyanide-dilution objection. Cleaves 2012 (Evolution: Education and Outreach, open access) states that whether the early atmosphere was ever reducing remains contentious and that ocean temperature and pH remain poorly constrained. Wikipedia (RNA world) gives the cytosine half-life and homochirality constraints.
- **Figure warrant for the slot structure:** Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :46 - *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* (the figure's own example of a condition line).
- **Refuter:** for any named slot, a measurement that fixes the Earth value at the level and for the duration the experiment requires; or a demonstration that the slot is not load-bearing for that experiment's mechanism. The entry is then withdrawn and the change recorded.

### C3 - transfer and provenance; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 9

- **Statement:** the bench frame cannot derive Earth condition values from its own closure; they must be imported from a frame whose closure differs, with a source gate and a shadow test. "Unrealistic" is a budget filter until a witness is shown. A product matching a meteorite compound needs a provenance record, because convergence and import look identical in the contents.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 8 :51-52 - *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*
- **Refuter:** a derivation of the disputed Earth condition from inside the bench frame that predicts the rock record; or a case where a plausibility filter is shown to settle a condition without any measurement attached to it (that would expose a defect in practice, not refute the figure, so name which one you mean).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named experiment is impossible, worthless or fraudulent. A substituted habitat is a legitimate instrument for finding wiring.
- Not that the reducing-atmosphere critique is settled. The post-impact literature keeps it open.
- Not that the RNA world, metabolism-first, or any other origin-of-life theory is refuted.
- Not that a laboratory product matching a meteorite compound proves convergence. Provenance decides.
- Not `grounded` about early Earth. The anchor returns ranges, not point values.
- Not that more replications in the same habitat settle the transfer: Fig 7 :13 - *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*
- Not that the figures produce the list of experiments. Fig 13 :133 - *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Atmosphere:** a constraint on the duration and timing of strongly reduced post-impact episodes. Settles the Miller-Urey slot either way.
2. **Concentration:** mineral or evaporite evidence that local HCN or nucleotide concentrations reached bench levels at the relevant time.
3. **Stability and purity:** reconstructed temperature and pH excluding or permitting the assumed stockpiles; enantiomeric enrichment evidence for routes requiring a single handedness.
4. **Surface:** abundance and exposure of the relevant mineral surfaces.
5. **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** habitat identity for a named experiment; direct transfer under reconstructed conditions; reversal of Fig 11 :70; a specific measurement showing the disputed condition was present.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :43, :46, :68-70 and Fig 9 :60, :27-28. Five short passages.
2. Pick one experiment from the table in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §3. Say whether the condition listed for it is load-bearing and whether the Earth value is actually unresolved.
3. Name the observation that could settle that one slot, or say why none exists yet.
4. Try C1's refuter. If it works, the whole shape claim fails; say so plainly.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
