## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **The atmosphere condition is disputed, not settled.** Zahnle et al. 2020 and the prebiotic-atmosphere page together show why "Miller-Urey used the wrong atmosphere" is too quick: the baseline is weakly reduced, but large impacts can produce a strongly reduced atmosphere for up to millions of years. The correct verdict on that slot is "duration and timing unresolved", not "unrealistic".
- **Concentration is a condition, not a detail.** Cleaves 2012 notes that organic compounds "would need to be concentrated and complexified by environmental mechanisms". The prebiotic-atmosphere page records the objection that cyanides would be too dilute in an ocean, with evaporating lakes or shallow vents as the proposed local fix. This is a habitat substitution: the bench concentrates; the global ocean does not.
- **Starting-material stability is a condition slot.** The RNA-world page gives a cytosine half-life of 19 days at 100 C and 17,000 years in freezing water, and requires a single enantiomer because the wrong chirality terminates a chain. Any experiment that assumes a stock of free cytosine or racemic ribose has introduced a condition.
- **Energy sources are condition slots.** Spark discharge, continuous UV, and mineral catalysis are not interchangeable. The Miller-Urey page records that borosilicate glass acted as a catalyst in a 2021 comparison with Teflon, which makes the vessel itself a condition that early Earth must supply in some form.
- **Convergence needs provenance.** A product matching a meteorite compound may be a convergent derivation (strong) or an adopted import (not). Cleaves 2012 records both endogenous synthesis and extraterrestrial delivery, so the contents alone cannot decide.

1. **Stage 1, before anything else:** the condition slot, the bench value, the reconstructed Earth range, the instrument that could discriminate, and the pre-registered refuter. No theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious after recording a verdict, offer the figure text and the two science links. The figures are the authority.
