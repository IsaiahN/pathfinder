## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For ORI-0083 the shape is therefore **not** a list of experiment names. It is the constraint any such list must satisfy:

> Each entry is a condition slot, with the bench value, the reconstructed Earth value and uncertainty, a discriminating observation, and a written failure. An entry without those four fields is a label, not a shape-conforming answer.

`SPACE(???)TIME` from *How Intelligence Actually Works* is the model: the open slot is kept open. Here the analogous form is `EXPERIMENT(???CONDITION)EARTH`. Naming the condition is a world-source act, not a figure derivation.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* For this question the first vocabulary is the habitat list: atmosphere, UV, concentration, mineral surface, pH, temperature, cycling.
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The shape is obtained by enumeration from the world, not by composing theory families.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* A condition slot is a plain description of what one would expect to find in the rock record.
- *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: the arrangement uses *"a room per problem, and a maintainer who sets conventions but never verdicts."* Each condition slot is its own room. There is no single room for "prebiotic plausibility".

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* Here that means a measurement of the disputed condition at the relevant time and place.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Earth condition values are imported from geochemistry and planetary science, with dates and citations.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* A choice of which conditions go on the list is a visible choice and must be recorded as such.

So the world-source list in `09_watson_answer.md` names experiments and conditions, but with mode `specified` and with provenance. It does not acquire figure authority by being listed.

## 4. The specific forbidden error for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* The essay records that the question was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a bench yields glycine. The reading does not say which source produced it (atmospheric discharge, Strecker chemistry in the aqueous trap, delivery from a meteorite, vessel catalysis). Naming the early-Earth source from the bench reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating observation, not a verdict on the theory.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A synthesis that validates its own prebiotic relevance by pointing to its own yield is the named self-validation failure.

## 5. Answered question: what shape must the list have?

**Not a ranking of origin-of-life theories. A per-slot comparison with a witness.** The framework returns a shape; a particular condition value is licensed only by a measurement or a provenanced import. Until then the slot stays marked `???`, exactly as the derivation keeps `SPACE(???)TIME` open.

Does the record-style answer commit the forbidden error? Yes, if it says "Miller-Urey was unrealistic" without (a) which condition, (b) which reconstructed value and uncertainty, and (c) which observation would discriminate. The correct form is a slot: `Miller-Urey / atmosphere redox state / CH4-NH3-H2 bench vs weakly-reduced reconstructed baseline with transient impact episodes / witness: constraint on the duration and timing of the reducing episode`.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A campaign that gathers famous names to endorse a condition verdict is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is to give the condition check a budget and let the ground, an instrument or a rock record, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* warns that its triangulation claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 7. Correction carried forward

An earlier form of this reading treated "shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any specific thing. The canonical wording governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Here the conditions are all held as ordinary concepts in the geochemistry frame; the missing item is the Earth's value for each. Naming a slot is therefore allowed. Naming its value without a witness is not. Both statements are kept so neither is quietly lost.
