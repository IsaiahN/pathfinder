## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the loss between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :43, :66, :68-70 (habitat, substitution, silent failures, wiring versus capability), Fig 4 :34-35 (conditions remake the method), Fig 9 :60 (filters are not witnesses), Fig 13 :133-135 (shape, not name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: a bench result is wiring under its conditions, not capability on Earth; the condition list must be enumerated per slot and witnessed.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the world-source list in `07` §3: named experiments and named disputed conditions (Miller-Urey atmosphere, HCN concentration, Sutherland UV and enantiomeric purity, RNA stability, vent dilution, glassware catalysis).

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down cleanly:

- **Range-valued anchor.** The shorthand says "reconstructed Earth value with uncertainty"; the concretisation needs a specific value and time window for each experiment. The post-impact literature turns a binary redox slot into a duration question, so the comparison is not a simple inequality.
- **Provenance of convergence.** The shorthand says a meteorite match needs provenance; the concretisation can only supply dates and citations for some matches, not independence of the two frames. The surplus is claimed where closure differences are not fully demonstrated.
- **Cascade depth.** The shorthand enumerates slots; the concretisation shows that vessel surface (a second-order effect on HCN yield) is load-bearing. A first-order enumeration would have missed it.

Two things survive cleanly: a bench solve is a substituted habitat and proves wiring only; and a slot without a discriminating observation is a filter, not a witness. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not measured numerically**, because no ground reading exists for the disputed conditions; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and repeat the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
