# The Tether's answer on conflicts between claimant parties

**Mode: `specified` on the governance claims, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on which party prevails in a named dispute. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a verdict in a particular case. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The company action changes outcomes for five parties. Each party is a slot, and `R = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` is measured per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The conflict is not one hard question. It is a **union** of at least four overlapping conditions: a channel to a non-updating ground, a refusal seat outside the slice graded, a per-slot register, and an instrument for the party whose residual does not yet register (Fig 9 :54-57). A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either; it becomes a specific compound with its own loop.

## 2. The shape

1. **Keep residuals per party.** Shareholder primacy collapses five slots to one; stakeholder balancing collapses them to one score. Both aggregate and both hide the live signal (Fig 1 :19, :22). A score a firm computes from its own report is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27).
2. **Range each party against an anchor that does not update.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). *"The anchor must not update."* (Fig 2 :33). A payment record, a measurement, a contract performed or breached, a harm count, a court ruling. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). A process that gets the parties to agree against a moving reference is coherent drift, and nobody notices (Fig 2 :34).
3. **No standing priority list.** *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* (Fig 11 :51). A party's valence is relative to the harm or benefit in view (Fig 11 :49-50). Shareholder-first and all-stakeholders are opposed in content and identical in form: fixed lists with no priority rule derived from the residual.
4. **Unbundle the union.** A worker is also a community member; an owner also inherits the climate. One lever, such as a scorecard or a chief sustainability officer, clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55, :57). Verdicts stay separate per domain and are never averaged.
5. **Install a refusal seat outside the slice.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* (Fig 10 :85-86). The seat has an unavoidable interest in the outcome, which is declared, and a disqualifying interest in the reading, which is removed (Fig 10 :59). It can refuse: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate"* (Operators :58).
6. **A convention is not a verdict.** A purpose clause or code of ethics is a shared meaning, which is useful (Fig 2 :41). *"a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"* (Fig 10 :47). Install what can be violated (Fig 10 :19).
7. **Future generations are `unreached`, not ranked.** No party is present and no instrument returns their residual (Fig 10 :23). *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* (Fig 6 :34-35). What is owed is an imported instrument with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :12-14), not a name on a priority list. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134).
8. **Keep both costs.** *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* (Fig 12 :125). Report the cost of describing the arrangement and the cost of settling each claim separately.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not which party should win any named dispute. The framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that stakeholder capitalism is correct and shareholder primacy is wrong. Both are standing lists; the figures refuse the form, not a side.
- Not that a mission statement, an ESG score, or a stakeholder charter settles anything. A declared meaning is not a grounded one (Fig 10 :49).
- Not that hearing a party changes its outcome. A hearing is a sequence; a changed outcome is a production (Fig 12 :70-79).
- Not that a multi-stakeholder forum corroborates a claim. Agreement among frames sharing an evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not `grounded`. No company measurement pays for the shape here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A non-updating measurement per party, read independently by two frames with different evidence pools.

- Does the aggregate score predict, prospectively and per named party, which residual grows next? If yes, F1 falls.
- Does a negotiation against a moving reference produce an allocation stable across three independent outcome measurements? If yes, F2 falls.
- Does a standing priority order predict which claim prevails across three categorically different conflicts? If yes, F3 falls.
- Does one lever restore all four conditions and leave no categorically different residue? If yes, F4 falls.
- Is there a repeatable instrument that returns a per-decision residual for people not yet born, accepted by two low-overlap frames? If yes, F5's `gap` narrows.
- Does a seat inside the slice, with an undeclared interest in the reading and no refusal, produce stable outcomes? If yes, F6 falls.

The world supplies candidate instruments: an ombudsman for future generations, monitoring accountable to users (Ostrom), an independent criterion for a fair outcome (Rawls), bias suppression and correctability (Leventhal). Each is a binding onto the shape, recorded as such, not a derivation from it.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The five-party list is a visible guess about what counts, not nature's own partition. Another frame's habitat need not be these five slots. Where no instrument returns a reading, there is no edge to extend from; the correct report is the shape of the missing instrument and the lineage of why it is missing. *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (figures README). A search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39), and a claim that no future harm matters would be a verdict the figures do not license.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winner among workers, customers, owners, communities and future generations. It says that a company cannot settle their conflicts by adding them into one score, because that hides the only signal that shows where a party is being failed (Fig 1 :19, :22), and cannot settle them by negotiating them to agreement against a reference that moves when they push, because alignment is triangulation, not negotiation (Fig 2 :29, :35). Each party's residual must stay separate and be ranged against a measurement that does not change when anyone pushes on it, and the seat that grades the result must stand outside the parties and be able to refuse (Fig 10 :42, :85-86). No standing priority list survives a change in the underlying harm (Fig 11 :51), and because the parties overlap, the conditions form a union that one lever cannot clear (Fig 9 :55). For people not yet born, no residual currently registers, so the honest answer is `unreached`: an instrument is owed, not a rank. What the framework offers is that shape and a set of falsifiers written in advance, not a verdict in anyone's favour.
