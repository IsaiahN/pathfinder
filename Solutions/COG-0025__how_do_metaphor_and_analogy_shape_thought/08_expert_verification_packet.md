# Expert verification packet: the Tether on metaphor, analogy, and cross-language variation

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in metaphor or analogy research can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structure, `gap` on which specific mappings are universal. Not `grounded`.** No experiment was run in this derivation.

**Status:** simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A metaphor or analogy shapes thought by carrying the relations between two domains rather than their shared attributes, and by acting as an import that gives a frame access to structure in a differently closed frame without adding a primitive to the frame's generating set. The mechanism kernel is invariant across languages and traditions; each particular mapping lands lossily into a habitat whose conditions decide what it becomes, so a round-trip gap is expected. When two traditions use the same mapping, independent convergence, adoption through contact, and shared convention are indistinguishable in the mapping itself and are separated only by provenance, so agreement is not automatically two witnesses. The framework does not name which mappings are universal; that is recorded as a `gap` awaiting a source-discriminating instrument.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. The mapping carries relations, not just attributes (framework-internal plus psychology-facing)

- **Statement:** a shaping mapping transfers the relational structure between domains. A mapping that transfers only shared attributes is the flat case and does not do the shaping work.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :46 "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable." Independent corroboration: Gentner (1983, Cognitive Science) "Relations between objects, rather than attributes of objects, are mapped from base to target."
- **What to check:** does the human analogy literature in fact find relational dominance in analogical interpretation, and is it destroyed when only attributes are available? Gentner's own systematicity results are the place to start, but check for later contrary cases.
- **Refuter:** a controlled case in which an attribute-only mapping produces the same downstream inferential change as a relation-carrying mapping. If that holds, C1 is withdrawn.

### C2. Kernel invariant, landing lossy (framework-internal plus cross-cultural)

- **Statement:** the mapping operation is the same kind of thing across languages and traditions; the mapping content is conditioned by where it lands.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :19-20 "The kernel is the part that does not change with size: the same loop and the same rule for naming what worked, whether one agent is running it or a thousand. Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 4 :34-35 "A seed carries no small tree: it carries what will grow one, and the conditions it lands in decide what it becomes." World corroboration: Zibin and Solopova (2024) report both a shared bodily basis for conceptual metaphors and "different languages, cultures and discourses may emphasize various components, resulting in metaphor variation." Su (2020) reports a cross-tradition difference on Raven's Standard Progressive Matrices.
- **What to check:** are those two findings about the *same* quantity? A cross-tradition difference in task accuracy is a difference in the landing, not evidence of a different kernel. Look for a case where the mapping operation itself is absent.
- **Refuter:** a language or tradition in which no mapping operation is used at all, so the kernel cannot be said to be running. Mere differences in direction preference, source-domain choice or accuracy do not fire this.

### C3. Agreement needs provenance before it counts as corroboration (framework-internal plus comparative cognition)

- **Statement:** convergence, adoption and convention produce indistinguishable mappings; only the record separates them. A shared evidence pool makes many languages one witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :41 "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Low-overlap corroboration: Smirnova et al. (2015, via Phys.org) found relational matching in crows, showing that the relational operation does not require a human lexicon.
- **What to check:** in a case of a mapping shared by two traditions, is there evidence of contact, of independent derivation, or of convention? If none, the mapping must be recorded as one witness with an unknown source, not two.
- **Refuter:** two traditions sharing a mapping with no shared evidence pool, no convention and no contact, so the surplus cannot be attributed to convergence or adoption. Also: a demonstration that relational matching requires a natural-language lexicon would fire the related substrate-independence claim in C2.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any specific universal metaphor exists or does not exist. That is a `gap`, not an answer.
- Not that a shared bodily basis gives independent corroboration; it is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the crow result settles human language. It bears on the substrate-independence shape only.
- Not that two mappings with the same ingredients are the same term. Under Fig 12 :117 they may be isomers; under Fig 12 :124 they may be a functional group. The atom set must be shown.
- Not that the mapping is symmetric. Base to target is ordered; see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Transfer test:** attribute-only versus relation-carrying mappings, measured for downstream inference.
2. **Reach test:** does the mapping change what can be composed, with no new primitive added?
3. **Kernel test:** mapping operation absent, or merely different in direction and content?
4. **Provenance test:** contact, independent derivation, or convention for a shared mapping.
5. **Standing refuters:** `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4, all five.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :46, :117, :124 and Fig 4 :19-20, :34-35, Fig 8 :50-52, Fig 2 :40-41 (eight short passages).
2. Check C1 against your own knowledge of structure-mapping results: is relational dominance real, and what breaks it?
3. Name one discriminator that would separate convergence from borrowing in a pair of traditions, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
