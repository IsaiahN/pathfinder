## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the figures say about the change in a thinker's reachable compositions when a mapping from one domain to another becomes available, and how that change depends on language and tradition. Option A: treat the question as one empirical problem and answer with a list of universal and culture-specific mappings. Option B: keep the mechanism and variation conditions separate, derive the shape the figures govern, and record the specific-content part as a gap. What each leads to: A names contents the framework says it cannot name and averages two different quantities; B returns a checkable shape, a provenance requirement, refuters written in advance, and a clean stop at the limit of the instruments.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The change in a frame's reachable compositions after a metaphor or analogy is available, measured per slot, across a variation dimension. Arity: two mechanisms plus a variation dimension, so a union compound (Fig 9 :53 "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"), not one hard question. Symmetry: the mapping is directional, base to target, not an identity. Scale: first order inside one mind, second order across languages and history (Fig 7 :12-13 "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not").
- **Domain 1, the mapping mechanism.** Actors: thinker, source and target domains, relations and attributes, the library and its bonds. Currency: change in reach, that is what can now be composed. Anchor: the ground, does the mapping pay on a task or measurement. Question type: partition of legal compositions. This is where F1, F2, F3 and F6 land.
- **Domain 2, cross-language and cross-tradition variation.** Actors: languages, traditions, speakers, mappings as seeds, habitat conditions. Currency: a mapping that travels with loss; convergence versus adoption. Anchor: the ground for any claim about what a mapping means in a tradition, but the framework returns a shape, not that name. Question type: union compound of shared basis plus cultural elaboration. This is where F4, F5 and F7 land.
- **Domain 3, verification of the shape.** Actors: researchers, instruments, experiments, corpora. Currency: residual versus agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35). Question type: partition of admissible evidence. This is where the refuters and the two-stage offer land.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20).** Settle the mechanism before asking how it varies; describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); write the refuter before the run (Seat Map :287); let the ground settle last.

Strain test applied: no figure line needed forcing. One apparent strain, Fig 12's isomer term applied across traditions, was re-censused: isomerism requires a shared atom set; otherwise the correct term is functional group (Fig 12 :124). The re-census is recorded in `01` §6 and `06` Q2.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the list of universal mappings does not, and is not replaced by a guess. A metaphor or analogy shapes thought by carrying relations rather than attributes (Fig 12 :5), by acting as an import that changes reach without adding a primitive (Fig 6 :25, Fig 8 :45), by running the same kernel in every language and tradition while landing lossily into a local habitat (Fig 4 :19-20, :34-35), and by requiring provenance before apparent agreement counts as corroboration (Fig 8 :50-52, Fig 2 :40-41). Which particular mappings are universal is a `gap` at figure level: unreached, not unreachable in the world, and answerable only with corpora, elicitation, behavioural measures and historical records. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result with one named and repaired strain (isomer versus functional group, Fig 12 :117/:124). The mechanism and variation verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states four refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a list of conclusions instead of a shape and a refuter, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and the refuters. A second cost is naming a universal mapping without provenance and thereby counting one evidence pool as two witnesses.
- **undoable:** yes. The gap verdict is a stop, not a commitment; a source-discriminating study can close it without withdrawing the mechanism shape. The one strain (isomer versus functional group) is repaired in `06`. If F1 or F2 fails, the affected claim is retracted and the corrected packet supersedes the sent one. No primitive has to be withdrawn to make the repair.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An attribute-only mapping that changes downstream inference as much as a relation-carrying one reverses C1; a case where a mapping adds a primitive to the generating set reverses C2; a language or tradition with no mapping operation reverses C2's kernel claim; provenance showing no shared pool, no convention and no contact reverses C3; a figure line yielding a specific universal mapping without a ground reading reverses the gap verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige and acceptance are not such instruments.
