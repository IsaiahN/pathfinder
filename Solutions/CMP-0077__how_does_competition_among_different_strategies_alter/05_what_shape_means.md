## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13's commentary: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, a shape is a set of constraints on any acceptable answer about competition and shocks. It is not a resilience number, a variance formula imported from ecology, or a claim that diversity is always good.

## 2. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Followed by Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." This is how the question's cast is found: strategies, competitors, conditions, and the shock, read off the contact rather than assumed.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."

For this question, "variance in fitness" from evolutionary biology is a candidate import. It is not licensed as a name until a source gate and a shadow test are supplied. What is licensed now is the effect description: distinguishable alternatives must be present and must survive the handoff, or selection has nothing to vote on.

## 4. The mapping this question invites, and the author declines it

The canonical article has a section titled "Three mappings offered and declined". One of the declined mappings is **the habitat as an ecological niche**:

> "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a *residual*, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes. The resemblance is real and the definitions do not align, which is why the figure says *enumerated, never composed* rather than borrowing the ecological term outright."

This matters more here than in most questions. "Competition among strategies" and "response to shocks" sound like ecology. The framework refuses the shortcut. The strategy portfolio is not a niche; it is the set of actors in contact with a residual, and the shock changes the residual, so the same strategies form a different habitat afterward. That is why the answer is a mechanism plus a measurement rule, not an ecological resilience coefficient.

## 5. The shape for CMP-0077

Naming the shape before the run, so it can be refused:

1. **Competition is a relation, not a strategy property.** Its sign is relative to the post-shock residual (Fig 11 :49-51). Any answer assigning a fixed sign is out of shape.
2. **Variation inside, selection between.** Plurality supplies alternatives; the shock is when the ground votes between them (Fig 7 :33, :35).
3. **Independence is the parameter.** A count of strategies without an independence correction is a label count (Fig 6 :53).
4. **Per-slot reading.** The response is a vector of residuals, not a portfolio average (Fig 1 :19, :22).
5. **Two factors.** Fidelity of the handoff and ground pressure; either at zero and the chain runs in place (Fig 7 :37-39).

That is the shape. What would fill it is a ground measurement: a controlled shock with a monomorphic control, reported per slot, with ρ measured between strategies.

## 6. Why the framework cannot give a number

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here, a post-shock survival count is the reading. It cannot say whether selection between variants or a single pre-adapted strategy produced it. Several mechanisms create the same behavior. What is owed is a source-discriminating experiment, not a larger claim from the same reading.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 7. Prestige is not action, and my audit is a simulation

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A list of famous evolutionary game theorists who agree is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter.

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra". So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

**Correction carried forward from the exemplar.** In the QMGR-0037 exemplar, §3 and §4 of that solution's `05` overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. Here the analogue is simpler: strategies and residuals are already in the census, so the question is not missing a primitive but missing an instrument. The shape limit applies to the coefficient we cannot yet measure, not to naming the actors we can already enumerate. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.
