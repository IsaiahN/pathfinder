## 3. Relevant wider literature, read through the sources above

- Maynard Smith and Price, 1973, "The logic of animal conflict", *Nature* 246: 15-18; and Maynard Smith, 1982, *Evolution and the Theory of Games*. Read through the EGT and ESS entries. The origin of the strategy-frequency framing.
- Hofbauer and Sigmund, 2003, "Evolutionary game dynamics", *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society* 40(4): 479-519. Read through the replicator-equation references. The standard statement of the dynamics.
- Taylor and Jonker, 1978, "Evolutionary Stable Strategies and Game Dynamics", *Mathematical Biosciences* 40: 145-156. Read through the replicator-equation and SEP references.
- Weibull, 1997, *Evolutionary Game Theory*, MIT Press. Read through the ESS entry's further reading. A baseline textbook for a checker.
- Holling, 1973, "Resilience and stability of ecological systems"; Folke, Carpenter, Elmqvist, Gunderson, Holling and Walker, 2002, "Resilience and Sustainable Development", *Ambio* 31(5): 437-440; Walker, Holling, Carpenter and Kinzig, 2004, "Resilience, adaptability and transformability in social-ecological systems", *Ecology and Society* 9(2): 5. All read through the ecological-resilience entry. The low-overlap witness for the shock half of the question.
- Cohen, 1966, "Optimizing reproduction in a randomly varying environment", *Journal of Theoretical Biology* 12(1): 119-129; Olofsson, Ripa and Jonzen, 2009, "Bet-hedging as an evolutionary game", *Proceedings of the Royal Society B* 276: 2963-2969. Read through the bet-hedging entry. The bridge between bet hedging and evolutionary game theory.

Overlap declaration: the Wikipedia, SEP and ecology sources do not share an author with the figures. The two Substack essays do. The world verdict therefore rests on the independent sources; the framework-author essays corroborate but carry high overlap.

## 5. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the reader is curious, offer the three links in `02_outreach_email.md`, with the note that the figure text, not our summary, is the authority.
