## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room is not an anchor.** Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Adding readers raises the vocabulary and does not add a ground.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :39 "Correlation has valence." A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the only thing an outside reader contributes.** Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03` are public submission or contact routes, not people.
