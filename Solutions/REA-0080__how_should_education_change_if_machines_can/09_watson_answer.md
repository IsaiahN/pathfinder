## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says should change when a machine can produce the task education has been using as evidence of human understanding. Option A: keep the product as the verdict and add detection and policy. Option B: change the unit of evidence to a per-learner trajectory ranged against a non-updating anchor, preserve contact, import the tool with provenance, and declare the format choice a gap. What each leads to: A spends the channel further, because a detector is an instrument that cannot comment on source and a policy chosen for agreement is a convention that decides an outcome; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters and an open design slot until a local ground settles it.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The task product no longer discriminates the learner's capability from a machine's output. Arity: three conditions (assessment validity, learning contact, tool availability) with different anchors, so a union compound, not one hard question. Symmetry: the demonstration treats an ordered productive claim as a symmetric identity, so the reading carries the product and not the route that produced it. Scale: per learner slot, then the classroom and institutional membrane. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Domain 1, assessment and credentialing.** Actors: learners, tasks, products, grades, credentials, machines as a second producer. Currency: evidence and grades. Anchor: human judgment about the person, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people (Fig 2 :36, :37). Question type: union member; needs the objective link repaired before detection.
- **Domain 2, learning and capability.** Actors: learner, habitat (teachers, practice, correction), machine as an available actor. Currency: residuals worked through and contact with the ground. Anchor: what the learner can do when the tool is removed in a reproduced habitat (Fig 11 :39, :40). Question type: union member; upstream of Domain 1 because a credential about a capability presupposes the capability.
- **Domain 3, the tool frame.** Actors: models and their outputs. Currency: produced task output. Anchor: correctness of output against its own ground. Question type: differing closure, a peer frame; import into Domain 1 needs a source gate and a shadow test.
- **Domain 4, institutional and curricular membrane.** Actors: curriculum designers, institutions, accreditation, cohorts. Currency: methods and head starts crossing scales. Anchor: the learners' contact with the ground at population scale. Question type: scale of Domain 2, not a new anchor; downstream of Domains 1 to 3.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (machine output is visible), vocabulary (terms for trajectory and defense), objective (what counts as progress), planning, learn and carry. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Refuse the false product-as-anchor term before minting new formats (Formula step 2). Describe the residual before choosing a vendor or format (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: human judgment is both the legitimate anchor for a question about people and a frame that can drift; re-census assigns the first to the constitutive question and the second to the individual grader. Testing is both measurement and learning intervention; re-census assigns them to two currencies. One live type question remains about which evidence is admissible (Fig 9 :51), named and not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the design list is a gap. A produced task output is an instrument that estimates understanding and cannot say which source produced it, so adding a machine that writes the essay saturates the channel while the ground, the person's capability, stays put. Education should change the unit of evidence, not the format: a per-learner trajectory, a defense in the learner's own words and a situated judgment, with the product kept as one component; it should repair the objective link before adding detectors; it should preserve or increase the learner's contact with the ground, since capability is agent-and-habitat; it should import the tool with provenance and a shadow test, with the model's agreement counted as one evidence pool rather than two witnesses; and it should carry methods rather than recordings across scales. Old and new arrangements are isomers over a fixed atom set. No figure returns a format, rubric weight, percentage or tool policy; that is `gap`, unreached and not silence. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and never averaged. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet labels its mode, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a finished design instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No format is asserted, no primitive is withdrawn, and no institution has committed to a design. A local pilot can supersede the shape with a measured binding; the refuters in `01` section 5 can withdraw it cleanly.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A discriminator showing that a machine-produced product still separates capability at the same reading would break the metamerism finding. An agent-only improvement that moves the learner's ground without changing contact would break Fig 11 :40. A measured channel that did not decay would break the channel finding. A figure line governing a format would close the gap. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
