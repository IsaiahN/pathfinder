## The residual in effect terms

Education has been reading a learner's understanding off a produced task output: an essay, a problem set, an exam answer, a piece of code. When a machine can produce the same output, the reading no longer discriminates its source. The ground (what the person can actually do) has not moved; the channel (product quality as a proxy) has become pooled and saturated. What is owed is a reading that ranges against the person's capability separately, not a ban on the tool and not a new product format. See `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no automation.
- No asserted assessment format, rubric weighting, percentage or tool ban. The figures return a shape and a refuter, not a design spec.

Three reasons, in the framework's own words:

1. A room does not add an anchor. Seat Map, failure genera: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
2. Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation. Seat Map, the laws: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."
3. Asking a reader to trust the framework before checking the output spends their independence. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All contact routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are public institutional or opt-in channels.
