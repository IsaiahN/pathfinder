## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question asks what education should change when machines can perform many of the tasks traditionally used to demonstrate human understanding. Stated as an effect rather than as a topic:

> The evidence channel that education uses to infer a person's understanding is a produced task output. Once a machine can produce the same output, the reading no longer discriminates its source. Two different sources, a learner who understands and a machine that produces a passing product, create the same observable reading. The thing that settles the question, the person's actual capability, has not changed. The channel that carried it has become pooled and saturated. What is owed is a reading that ranges against the person's capability separately, not a prohibition on the tool and not a replacement product format.

Three properties of R:

- **Arity:** at least two conditions are bundled: assessment validity (what the product evidences), learning contact (what the learner actually does), and tool availability (the machine as a second producer). This is a union compound, not one hard question with two opposite answers. Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Symmetry:** the traditional demonstration treats an ordered, productive claim (a person comes to understand and thereby produces work) as if it were a symmetric identity (the work is the understanding). The machine exposes the difference. Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." An essay read off the page carries the product and not the route that produced it.
- **Scale:** the residual registers per learner, not per cohort. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." A rise in average grades when machine use rises is a pooled reading, not evidence that a particular learner's contact improved.

**What an answer would look like (the sought-for shape made an ordinary term).** Before searching, bind the shape: an answer must (a) name an anchor that does not update on the learner's or the institution's report of success, (b) keep the reading indexed per learner, (c) change contact rather than only the artifact, (d) keep the tool import provenanced, and (e) not claim a specific format the figures do not return. Writing this down first makes the preference refusable by the same test as everything else (Formula step "Direction"). The pre-registered refuter is in section 5.

**Union, not partition.** A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). Banning machine output (if that were available) would leave the assessment proxy and the learning-contact problem intact. Redesigning the assessment alone would leave the learner's contact unchanged. Each domain gets its own verdict; they are never averaged into one "education should" sentence.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Perception: institutions can already see that machine output is indistinguishable from student output. Vocabulary: there are terms for the product but weak terms for trajectory, ownership, defense and situated judgment. Objective: what would count as progress in understanding is the broken link. Planning and learn-and-carry come after. Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 3 :29-30: "Most improvement effort in practice lands above the break, because that is where the measurements already exist." AI detectors are effort above the break: they sit at perception, where instruments already exist, and cannot repair the objective link.

### F1: The product is an instrument, not the anchor (decisive for D1)

Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." Fig 2 :37-38: "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."

The question "does this person understand" is constitutively about a person, so human judgment is the legitimate anchor for it. A written product is a different object: it is an instrument that estimates the person, and it is silent on the axis the question now asks, namely which source produced the reading. The framework essay "The Hard Problem is About Source" names the exact failure: several underlying causes create identical readings, and the reading cannot comment on the source. An instrument that cannot distinguish those cases demonstrates its own inadequacy.

**Consequence:** the response is not to declare the product useless. It is to stop treating the product alone as the verdict and to add readings that can discriminate the source, each ranged separately against the anchor.

**Refuter:** a checkable demonstration that a machine-produced product still discriminates the learner's capability at the same reading, without any additional evidence. If that exists, F1 fails.

### F2: The channel decayed; the ground did not (D1, D4)

Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does." Fig 10 :25-27: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings" and "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Machine-produced work saturates the old metric. The metric was always a proxy; it stops discriminating when a second producer enters. The honest output of the assessment instrument is not "grades are lower" or "cheating is up." It is "you have lost touch with the reading you were using."

**Refuter:** evidence that the product channel still separates the learner's capability from a machine's output across the relevant population. If it does, the channel has not decayed and this finding is withdrawn.

### F3: The broken link is the objective, not perception (D1, D4)

Fig 3 :26-27: "having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Institutions can perceive that machine output is indistinguishable; detectors, submission logs and policy statements all sit at perception. What they lack is a term and an instrument for what would count as progress in understanding once the product is machine-producible. Effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it.

**Refuter:** a measured institutional program in which a stated objective for understanding was present and checkable, and the failure still occurred. That would move the break lower and require a re-census.

### F4: Capability is agent-and-habitat; only contact changes it (D2)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not."

If the machine performs the retrieval, the struggle and the correction, the learner's contact with the ground is reduced even while the produced artifact improves. That is a performance gain, not a capability gain. Conversely, a machine introduced as an actor that the learner must critique, correct and defend creates new contact. The seat may introduce; it cannot subtract the habitat. So "ban the machine" is not an available lever, and "allow the machine" without a change in contact is not a capability intervention.

**Refuter:** an agent-only improvement that moves the learner's ground without changing contact. The figures name this as the standing refuter of the capability claim (Fig 11 :71-72). If one is measured, F4 fails.

### F5: A substituted habitat proves wiring, not capability (D2)

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" and "what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

A timed, closed-book exam is a substituted habitat. It may still test something, but it removes the tools and conditions the learner will actually work in. Passing it proves that the arrangement is wired, not that the learner can judge when a machine output is wrong in a real setting. The design question is which parts of the real habitat must be reproduced for the goal to hold.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that a substituted habitat reproduces every condition the goal requires, including the actors and relations the learner will meet outside it. That would collapse the substitution distinction.

### F6: The tool is a differing closure; its agreement is one evidence pool (D3)

Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 2 :31: "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

A model that produces a passing essay and a learner who submits it draw on overlapping task structure and often the same corpus of examples. Their agreement on the product is not two witnesses. It is one pool talking. Treating "the model also passed" as corroboration of the learner's understanding is the named collapse.

**Refuter:** two frames with measurably separate evidence, each ranging against a ground neither can move, that still agree on the learner's capability. That would be corroboration at independence and would not contradict F6, but it would change the design.

### F7: The import needs a source gate and a shadow test (D3, D1)

Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence."

Letting a machine into the learning and assessment arrangement is an import from a frame whose closure differs. It can add an atom the classroom did not hold, for example a way to generate counterexamples on demand. It needs provenance (where the capability came from, what it was checked against) and a shadow test (which gap in the learner's evidence it explains). Without those, a wrong atom enters at a cost above starting from primitives.

**Refuter:** a measured case where the machine's contribution was identical to a term the classroom already derived, with a recorded lineage. Then nothing was imported and the debit is zero, which does not harm the verdict.

### F8: Product and trajectory are isomers, not variants (D1, structure)

Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

The old assessment and a trajectory-based assessment can be built from the same held atoms: learner, task, product, feedback, judgment, ground, tool. They differ in the bonds. The old arrangement bonds learner to product to judgment. A trajectory arrangement bonds learner to successive attempts to a defense to judgment, with the tool as a provenanced import. Same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. The product alone cannot show which arrangement produced it, which is why the record, not the artifact, is what can be checked. Full derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1.

**Refuter:** a new design that requires an atom not derivable from the held set. Then the relation is composition plus import, not isomerism, and the claim is re-censused rather than rescued.

### F9: The room must change when the question changes (D4)

Fig 10 :72: "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."

A curriculum that keeps the same tasks and adds an AI-detection policy has kept the room fixed while the question moved. What should cross between scales is a method for generating valid evidence of understanding, not a recording of one occasion (a fixed answer key, a banned-tool list). The round trip is lossy, so Fig 4 :30 requires the gap to be measured: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

**Refuter:** a room that stayed fixed while the question moved and still generated valid, checkable evidence at the ground. If measured, F9 fails.

### F10: No figure returns a specific format, weight or percentage (gap)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

After the census, no figure line selects oral defense over a portfolio, a percentage weight, a permitted-tool list, a grade curve or a rubric. Those are arrangements over held atoms, and the arrangements do not run out (Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not."). This is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, not a silence, not an invitation to invent a number, and not underivable-in-principle in the sense of Fig 8 :40-43.

**Refuter for the gap:** a figure line that directly governs a specific assessment format or numeric weight. If one is found and survives the strain test, the gap is withdrawn.

### F11: Refusal is a unary operator, not a new format (all domains)

Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate." Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."

An assessment vocabulary that can only add formats accumulates. It must be able to refuse an instrument: to say of a metric that its reading is being treated as the source when it is not, and to remove that metric's standing to be composed over without erasing the record that it was once used. Refusing a metric is not subtracting an item from a rubric; it is a negation of a claim about what the item measures. This is why the packet in `08` carries a failure condition rather than a list of recommended tools.

**Refuter:** an arrangement that can only add evidence channels and never refuse one, and still keeps its readings discriminating as conditions change. The figures predict that it cannot; a measured case would refute the claim.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

Four apparent strains were found. Each was re-censused rather than smoothed.

- **S1, human judgment as anchor versus human judgment as a frame that drifts.** Fig 2 :36 makes human judgment the right anchor for a question about people, while Fig 2 :34 says an anchor that updates becomes another frame and everything drifts together. Re-census: the legitimate anchor is human judgment about the person in the constitutive sense, not the individual grader's private model. Each individual grader still ranges against a ground they cannot move, which is why a second reader and a checkable trajectory are required. Strain dissolved by assigning the two lines to different objects. Kept visible.
- **S2, "authentic assessment" as a new atom.** The world literature recommends authentic tasks. Read loosely, that looks like importing a seventh term. Re-census: process documentation, oral defense and situated judgment are bindings onto atoms already held (process, network, comparison, ground). Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." No wall moves. Strain dissolved, correction preserved in `05` section 7.
- **S3, the testing effect versus the question's treatment of tests as measurement.** Roediger and Karpicke show that testing improves retention, while the question treats the test as a certificate. Re-census: the same event plays two roles, probe and contact. Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." A test as evidence is priced in bits; a test as contact is priced in actions. Different objects, no strain.
- **S4, "shape, not a name" applied to design choices.** The canonical README says the framework returns a shape, not a name, for a missing thing. Assessment formats are not missing primitives. Re-census: selecting among bindings is provenance-bearing work, not the invention of a new term. Recorded as a correction in `05` section 7 and `06` Q1.

**After re-census: no strain is hidden. One live type question remains:** Fig 9 :51 admits "evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it." Whether a particular institutional metric bears on the residual or only on the producer is a decision an outside frame must make, and this simulated derivation cannot make it from inside. Named, not resolved.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any verdict was recorded, per Seat Map: "And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A machine-produced demonstration of understanding discriminates the learner's capability at the same product reading, with no additional evidence (breaks F1 and the metamerism premise).
2. A checkable reading of a person's capability is available that needs no differing closure and no non-updating anchor (breaks Fig 2 :29 and the anchor requirement).
3. An agent-only improvement moves the learner's ground without changing contact (breaks Fig 11 :40, F4).
4. Evidence shows the product channel did not decay when machine task performance became available (breaks F2).
5. A figure line directly governs a specific assessment format, rubric weight, percentage or tool policy (breaks F10, the gap verdict).
6. A new assessment design requires an atom not derivable from the held set and cannot be refused (breaks F8 and F11).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn or re-censused. Until one is run, nothing here may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. Change the unit of evidence, not the format. Move from the product alone to a trajectory that can be checked per learner: successive attempts, a defense the learner must give in their own words, and a situated judgment under conditions the tool cannot pre-solve. Keep the product as one component, not the verdict (F1, F8).
2. Repair the objective link before adding detectors. Write down what would count as progress in understanding when the product is machine-producible, and instrument that (F3). Detectors may be one signal, never the anchor.
3. Protect contact. Ensure the learner still performs retrieval, struggle and correction. A machine that removes those reduces capability even while output improves; a machine the learner must critique creates contact (F4, F5).
4. Import the tool with provenance and a shadow test. Record which gap it explains and debit its independence. Do not mistake agreement between the model and the learner for two witnesses (F6, F7).
5. Change the room. Curriculum and credentialing should carry methods for generating valid evidence, not recordings of one occasion, and should change when the question changes (F9).
6. Declare the gap. No figure returns a format, weight, percentage or permitted-tool list. Run local pilots against a ground and record the lineage. Unreachable is a verdict, not a silence (F10).

### Seven-question recursive derivation, summary

1. **Is there a fact here?** Yes. Assessment validity, learning contact and tool availability are three conditions with different anchors, so union and split, not one search (Fig 9 :4-5, :54-57).
2. **Which term is wrong?** The held term "product demonstrates understanding" keeps a flat residual once a second producer appears. It is the refusal target, and the refusal is a negation, not a new format (Operators :58).
3. **Can a molecule be minted?** A trajectory arrangement composes from held atoms, passes support (the reading fails per learner), reachability (all atoms held) and novelty (the arrangement is new relative to atoms), and its cost must be less than the confusion it removes (Formula step 3).
4. **Where is the wall?** The machine frame's closure differs. Its output can move the wall only as a provenanced import with a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52); adding a format inside the old closure cannot.
5. **Who verifies?** An outside frame that reads the shape and the refuter but does not hold the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :42), ranging against the learner's capability rather than against agreement.
6. **What crosses scales?** A method for producing valid evidence, not a recording of one occasion (Fig 4 :22, :25), with the round-trip gap measured (Fig 4 :30).
7. **Where does it stop?** At the shape plus refuter. No format, weight or percentage is returned (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Ground pilots decide which binding pays.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives. Education should change the object of its evidence from a produced artifact to a checkable, per-learner trajectory ranged against a non-updating anchor; it should repair the objective link before adding detection; it should preserve or increase the learner's contact with the ground; it should import machine capability with provenance and a shadow test; and it should change the institutional room when the question changes. Every specific design choice is a `gap`, reported as unreached, not silence. Mode `specified` on the domain verdicts, `general` on the structural claim, not `grounded`. Strains after re-census: one live type question about admissible evidence (Fig 9 :51), named and not hidden.
