# The Tether's answer: how education should change

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on any specific format, weight, percentage or tool policy. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Education has been inferring a person's understanding from a produced task output. When a machine can produce the same output, the reading no longer discriminates its source. The ground, the person's capability, has not moved; the channel has become pooled and saturated. The question is a union of at least three conditions with different anchors, not one problem with two opposite answers (Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged into one "education should" sentence.

## 2. The shape

1. **Change the unit of evidence, not the format.** Fig 2 :37: "Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people." The written product is an instrument that estimates the person, and it is silent on which source produced the reading. Add readings that discriminate source: a per-learner trajectory, a defense in the learner's own words, and a situated judgment under conditions the tool cannot pre-solve. Keep the product as one component, not the verdict.
2. **Repair the objective link before adding detectors.** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Perception works; we can see machine output. The broken link is the objective, what would count as progress in understanding (Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."). Detectors sit above the break.
3. **Protect contact.** Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Capability is a property of agent-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39). A machine that performs retrieval and correction for the learner improves output without improving capability. A machine the learner must critique creates contact. Fig 11 :61: "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." So banning the tool is not an available lever.
4. **A substituted habitat proves wiring, not capability.** Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability." A closed-book exam is not the habitat the learner will work in; check which conditions must be reproduced.
5. **Import the tool with provenance and a shadow test.** Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." A model's output and a learner's output that agree on the product are one evidence pool, not two witnesses (Fig 2 :40).
6. **Product and trajectory are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Both arrangements use the same held atoms; the bond differs, so the product alone cannot tell which arrangement produced it.
7. **Change the room when the question changes.** Fig 10 :72: "When the question changes, the room should change. A room that stays fixed while the question moves is the failure; the correlation is only its symptom." What crosses scales is a method for generating valid evidence, not a list of banned tools (Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").
8. **No figure returns a design.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The specific format, weight, percentage and tool policy are a declared `gap`, reported as unreached rather than filled in (Fig 13 :134).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ban on machines. Exclusion is not available (Fig 11 :61).
- Not "essays are dead." The product stays as one component of a broader evidence base; what dies is its role as the sole verdict.
- Not a format, a rubric weight, a percentage, a grade curve or a permitted-tool list. Those are a `gap`.
- Not that oral defense is universally better. The Frontiers four-pillar paper records accessibility and equity concerns and unknown equivalence of alternative formats.
- Not that the model's judgment corroborates the learner's. Shared evidence pool, one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not a theory of education and not a final room. Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."
- Not grounded. No local assessment arrangement was measured here.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a local pilot, not a further argument. From the Hard Problem is About Source: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Discriminator test:** does a proposed evidence arrangement actually separate a learner who understands from a machine-produced passing product at the same product reading? A measured "yes" would settle F1 and move the channel finding.
- **Contact test:** does the arrangement increase the learner's retrieval, correction and situated judgment, measured after the tool is removed and in a reproduced habitat? This is where the testing-effect and desirable-difficulties literature predicts the effect.
- **Objective test:** can the institution state what counts as progress in understanding and instrument it, so Fig 3's middle link stops being a link nobody has looked at?
- **Import test:** what gap does the machine explain, what is its provenance, and is its independence debited (Fig 6 :24)?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5:** a machine product that discriminates source with no extra evidence; a capability reading with no differing closure; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a channel that did not decay; a figure line that governs a format; a design that needs a new atom and cannot be refused.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term set is "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (essay, carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping). Declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the shape plus refuter and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). What remains outside is a seat's office: an outside frame that reads the shape and the refuter without holding the framework's interiors (Fig 10 :42), and a ground that no frame can move.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say which assessment should replace the essay, and it does not claim any of this has been measured. It says that a produced task output is an instrument that estimates understanding and cannot say which source produced it, so adding a machine that writes the essay saturates the channel while the ground, the person's capability, stays put. The change is therefore in the unit of evidence: a per-learner trajectory, a defense in the learner's own words and a situated judgment, with the product kept as one component. The learner must still do the retrieval, struggle and correction, because capability lives in the agent-and-habitat arrangement; a machine the learner must critique creates contact, and one that removes the contact improves output without improving capability. The tool enters as a provenanced import whose agreement with the learner is one evidence pool, not two witnesses. Old and new assessments are isomers over the same atoms, different bonds and different substances. Which format, weight or tool policy to adopt is a declared gap. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
