## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says it "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From the Library of Babel essay: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be named. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, the shape is: the evidence channel must discriminate the learner's capability from a machine's output, the reading must be indexed per learner, contact must be preserved or increased, and the tool must be provenanced. That is a shape. "Use a 30 percent oral defense weight" is a name, and no figure line licenses it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- Library of Babel: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." And the anchor of the search is "your own question": the question tells you whether to stop or turn.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- Decoding Tacit Knowledge: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- Library of Babel on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place," and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: describe the failure in effect terms first (a passing product no longer identifies its source), then look for a frame whose closure predicts that shape (assessment validity research, cognitive psychology of retrieval, evaluation research on proxy corruption). Do not pick the frame first and then map it onto the vocabulary. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For education, that means a local pilot in which a proposed evidence arrangement actually predicts a learner's capability, checked against something the institution cannot move.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The four-pillar framework from the Frontiers paper enters this way: its authors used Messick's validity framework, declared their method and declared their AI use.

They're Scanning Books sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The six-term substrate is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why "product demonstrates understanding" is the specific forbidden error

The Hard Problem is About Source gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, a yellow and blue mix, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Education's task product is exactly such an instrument. A passing essay has at least two known sources, the learner's understanding and a machine's generation, and the reading does not determine which. Naming the source from the product is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument (a trajectory, a defense, a check under reproduced conditions), not a declaration about what the product "really" shows.

Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A credential system that grades the artifact it was given cannot validate the artifact.

## 5. Q2: what licenses a particular assessment design?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular design is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term in a local context, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, the design is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This is why `01` section 6 lists a direction of change and `07` section 6 gives one paragraph with no format, percentage or tool policy. The honest answer to "which assessment should replace the essay" is that this question is a `gap` here, reported as unreached rather than filled in.

## 6. Contact is the part that does not travel

Decoding Tacit Knowledge: "Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." Understanding is rebuilt by contact: trying, being corrected and trying again. "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see."

This bears directly on the question. A machine can hand a learner the product of a reasoning trajectory without the corrections that build the learner's trajectory. The written product carries the context and some subtext of expert understanding, but not the history that makes it the learner's own. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."

Arrival of the Fittest (carried from the exemplar's canonical mapping, not re-opened this session) states the capability claim in the human cognition register: asking what intelligence is replaces the answerable question of what makes a system capable, and capability is a property of agent-and-habitat.

## 7. Correction to my own earlier reading: what "shape, not a name" governs

I initially wrote the rule as if it forbade naming any specific design element. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library.

Process documentation, oral defense, situated judgment and the machine as an actor are not new primitives. They are bindings onto atoms the framework already holds (network, comparison, ground, import). Selecting them is provenance-bearing work, not the invention of a seventh term, and it does not move the wall: Fig 6 :21 says "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."

What remains wrong if a binding is presented as a derivation is the provenance error, not the choice itself. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." So the four pillars are usable only with their record attached: Messick validity, the authors' search method, their declaration of AI assistance, and their explicit statement that the tools are not psychometrically validated.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The product still cannot comment on its source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on choosing among held bindings. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 8. Two consequences for outreach and for this folder's status

**Prestige is not action.** Recursive Self-Improvement Trap: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers respected names to agree that essays are dead is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget for the claim to be tried against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." LLM output is "simulacra." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations, checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle what they report on.
