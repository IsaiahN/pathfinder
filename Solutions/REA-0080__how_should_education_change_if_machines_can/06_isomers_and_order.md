Three structural questions are owed before any format is named: (1) is the relation between the old assessment and a machine-aware assessment a union of conditions, a partition, or a compound with its own loop; (2) are the two arrangements isomers over a fixed atom set, or does one require an import; (3) in what order should the repair be attempted. ## DERIVATION

### Q1: Is the change a union, a partition, or a compound?

- Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
- Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- The conditions here do not share an anchor. Assessment validity ranges against human judgment about the person (Fig 2 :37). Learning capability ranges against what the learner can do when the tool is removed (Fig 11 :39-40). Tool availability ranges against the correctness of the model's output. Different anchors mean different domains, and verdicts must be kept separate.
- **Union, not partition.** Redesigning assessment and preserving contact overlap: a trajectory assessment can also be a contact intervention. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if one anchor could settle all three conditions, the question would be a partition with one verdict and the split claim would fail.

### Q2: Are the old and the new assessment isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
- Fig 12 :5: "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- Old arrangement: `learner → product → judgment`, with feedback late or absent. New arrangement: `learner → attempt → correction → attempt → defense → judgment`, with the tool as a provenanced actor the learner must critique.
- Both use the same held atoms. The bonds differ: the old uses product as the sole intermediary, the new uses a sequence of corrections and a comparison at the defense. Same atoms, different bond, different substance.
- **Isomer claim:** yes, over the fixed atom set `{learner, task, product, feedback, judgment, ground, tool, time}`. The two arrangements are not variants of one thing; they are different things whose product notation cannot tell them apart. This is why looking at a submitted essay cannot reveal which arrangement produced it.
- **Falsifier:** if the new arrangement requires an atom the old one did not hold, for example a form of evidence not derivable from the eight, then the relation is composition plus import (Fig 6 :23) and the isomer claim fails. Triangulated for the definition (Fig 12 :117 and Fig 12 :5), single-source for the specific atom list.

### Q3: What is the order of repair?

- Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."
- Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."
- The chain: perception (we can see machine output) → vocabulary (terms for trajectory, ownership, defense) → objective (what counts as progress in understanding) → planning → learn and carry.
- Perception works. Vocabulary is partial; there are terms for "product" and "detection," fewer for "trajectory that discriminates source." Objective is the break: Fig 3 :27 says "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Fig 3 :26-27 says the middle link "has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments."
- Therefore the order is: write down the objective in checkable effect terms, build the vocabulary to state it, then plan the evidence arrangement, then carry the method to the next cohort. Detectors and policy statements are effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
- **Falsifier:** if an institution has a stated, checkable objective for understanding and still fails when machine output enters, the break is lower than the objective and this order is wrong.

### Q4: Catalysts and spent parts

- Fig 12 :119: "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."
- A rubric that is reapplied and refined is a catalyst. A fixed answer key is a spent part: once the task is seen, its diagnostic value is gone. A detector is a spent part in a second sense: as evasion co-evolves, each use consumes its discriminating power (the Frontiers paper records detection-evasion cycles and the Campbell proxy-corruption finding records the same shape in evaluation research).
- **Consequence:** count which parts regenerate. Carried methods (Fig 4 :22) are catalysts; recordings of one occasion are spent parts.
- **Falsifier:** a claimed catalyst that loses its discriminating power after one use and is not regenerated is a spent part; the classification then fails.

### Q5: Two currencies, and they do not add

- Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."
- Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."
- An evidence arrangement is cheap in bits and expensive in actions (a written defense with a live examiner), or expensive in bits and cheap in actions (a detailed rubric that can be applied without checking). The two readings come apart in both directions, and summing them would hide the shortfall (Fig 12 :130).
- The Frontiers four-pillar paper gives a concrete action cost: roughly 17 hours of direct assessment for 100 students, 25 to 35 hours including preparation, and 50 hours for a cohort of 300 with sampled oral defense. It also records that random sampling reduces workload but weakens deterrence and applies the evidentiary logic to only a subset. That is a budget fact, not a verdict about validity.
- **Falsifier:** if one currency could be derived from the other for this arrangement, the two-currency claim would fail and a single score would be adequate.

### Q6: Refusal is not a new format

- Operators table :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."
- Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one."
- A metric that once estimated understanding but now estimates machine assistance can be refused: its standing as evidence is removed while the record that it was used remains. That is ¬, not subtraction. A vocabulary holding only "add another format" cannot do this, so it can only accumulate.
- **Falsifier:** an arrangement that adds channels and never refuses one, yet keeps discriminating as conditions change. The figures predict this is impossible; a measured case would refute the claim.

### Q7: Analogues, with their limits

- **Apprenticeship (Decoding Tacit Knowledge essay):** the learner works beside a practitioner, tries, is corrected, and rebuilds the history that makes a compressed message intelligible. "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see." Limit: an apprenticeship is a substituted habitat with actors the institution did not author (Fig 11 :66-67).
- **Retrieval practice (Roediger and Karpicke 2006; Bjork and Bjork via Looti 2026):** "Testing is a powerful means of improving learning, not just assessing it." A test is contact as well as measurement. Limit: the two roles are two currencies, not one (Q5).
- **Socratic dialogue (Crompton and Burke 2026):** an AI-powered dialogic assignment required students to identify and fix code errors and apply principles to real scenarios. Limit: if the same model supplies the dialogue and judges it, the two frames share an evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- **The garden (Fig 11 :58-60):** introduction is available, exclusion is not. A curriculum can add an actor that creates contact; it cannot remove the habitat the learner will actually work in.

## CONCLUSION

The change is a union compound, not one lever. The old and the new assessment are isomers over a fixed atom set, differing in bond rather than ingredients. The repair order is set by dependency: objective before planning, vocabulary before detectors. The parts that regenerate are methods; the parts that do not are recordings. Description cost and verification cost do not add, and the system needs a unary refusal operator or it can only accumulate formats. No figure line selects a format, weight or tool policy; those remain a `gap` reported as unreached. All claims are `specified`, ungrounded and simulated.
