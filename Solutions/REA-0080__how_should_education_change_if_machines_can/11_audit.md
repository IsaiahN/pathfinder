Type: union member; the anchor is legitimate but each individual grader is a frame that can drift.

- **Domain 4, institutional membrane.** Actors: curriculum, accreditation, cohorts. Currency: methods and head starts. Anchor: learners' contact at population scale. Type: scale of Domain 2, not a new anchor; downstream.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, what the coarser description cannot hold, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `01` and `07`: the product is an instrument, not the anchor; the unit of evidence should change; contact must be preserved; the tool is a provenanced import; the design is a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual institutional design: a rubric with stated weights, a defense protocol, a permitted-tool policy, a cohort schedule.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **discriminator** is not named. The shape says a reading must distinguish source; no figure or essay supplies an instrument that does it in a particular discipline. That is an instrument debt, not a design detail.
- the **contact condition** is not quantified. How much retrieval, correction and situated judgment is enough, and over what delay, is exactly what the testing-effect literature measures and the figures do not.
- the **admissibility test** is undecidable from inside (Fig 9 :51). Whether an institutional metric bears on the residual or only on its maker has to be decided by a frame outside the arrangement, which this simulated seat is not.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a product that cannot comment on its source cannot be the verdict for a question about the person, so an additional discriminating reading is owed. That is the residue worth sending.

R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists. It is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the full law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap.
