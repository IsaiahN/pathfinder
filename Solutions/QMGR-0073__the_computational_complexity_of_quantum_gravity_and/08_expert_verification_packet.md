# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the computational complexity of quantum gravity

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complexity theorist, mathematical physicist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it. It is one-to-one, not a bulk send.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the union split, not `grounded`.** No Planck-scale measurement and no complexity proof is claimed here. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three conditions with different anchors: whether a computational description is composable, what it costs in description bits and in settling actions, and which complexity class (if any) applies. Computation cannot be a seventh primitive, because the continuum was removed from the six-term set for exactly the reason that a generating set must not contain what its members produce; a computational description is a molecule built from the held states and structures and must compose across the two kinds. "Complexity" prices in two currencies that do not add. A named class is a provenanced import from the complexity frame with a shadow test, not a derivation, and the slot stays open until an instrument or a proof pays for it. Undecidability marks the limit of what a frame can certify about itself, not an answer about spacetime. The framework refuses the theory-of-everything reading and returns a shape plus six refuters.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 9

- **Statement:** the question is a union, not one number; computation is not a new primitive; any computational description composes across states and structures, never within one kind.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :43: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this". Fig 13 :44: "A generating set must not contain what its own members produce." Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." Fig 13 :75: "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fig 9 :55: "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."
- **Refuter:** derive computation as an indivisible seventh primitive that is load-bearing in an established law; or show the existence and class conditions share one anchor and one instrument so the union reading collapses.

### C2 - pricing; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 5, and by comparing with algorithmic information theory

- **Statement:** the description cost in bits and the settling cost in actions are two currencies that do not add; a single "complexity of quantum gravity" number is a category error under this framework. Description length is already inside the framework through MDL.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :125: "A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add." Fig 12 :126: "Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds." Fig 12 :130: "Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it." Fig 5 :45: "This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."
- **What to check against the literature:** Kolmogorov complexity is "the length of a shortest computer program (in a predetermined programming language) that produces the object as output" and is uncomputable (Wikipedia, algorithmic information theory, opened 2026-11-23); BQP is "the class of decision problems solvable by a quantum computer in polynomial time, with an error probability of at most 1/3 for all instances" (Wikipedia, opened 2026-11-23). These are different units by construction. World check is secondary and never decides the figure verdict.
- **Refuter:** exhibit one measure that prices description bits and settling actions in a single unit without loss, or prove they are the same quantity.

### C3 - limits and settlement; checkable by reading Figures 8, 6 and 2

- **Statement:** a frame cannot certify its own limit; a search that finds nothing is not proof of absence; no ground at this scale settles a class, so the status is `candidate`. Undecidability results corroborate the shape but do not name a class for quantum gravity.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :37: "A frame cannot certify its own limit. Chaitin: beyond a fixed constant, no system proves that any object exceeds its own complexity." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
- **What to check against the literature:** Cubitt, Perez-Garcia and Wolf, "Undecidability of the Spectral Gap", Nature 528, 207-211 (2015): "there exists no algorithm to determine whether an arbitrary model is gapped or gapless." Aaronson, "NP-complete Problems and Physical Reality", ACM SIGACT News (2005): "I do not believe that any of the proposals will let us solve NP-complete problems efficiently." Brown, Roberts, Susskind, Swingle and Zhao, "Complexity Equals Action", PRL 116, 191301 (2016): the holographic complexity duality is explicitly a conjecture. None of these is a ground reading at the Planck scale.
- **Refuter:** name a real instrument returning a discriminating reading at the relevant scale, or produce a proof of the exact class rather than a bound, with provenance and a shadow test. That moves the claim to `grounded`; failing it leaves it `candidate`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that spacetime is a computation, and not that the universe is a quantum computer. A test harness proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not that quantum gravity lies in any named complexity class. No figure line returns a class.
- Not that a holographic complexity correspondence settles a Planck-scale question; it is a conjecture from a differing frame.
- Not that complexity is one number. Description bits and settling actions are different currencies.
- Not a theory of everything; no last room (Fig 7 :6).
- Not `grounded`. The derivation is simulated and internally consistent, not a measurement.
