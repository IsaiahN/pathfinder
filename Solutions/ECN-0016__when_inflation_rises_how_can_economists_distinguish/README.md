## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the diagnosis.
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that agreement among economists decides the question.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the anchor.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :29-30: an anchor is *"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome"*. For inflation the anchor is the outturn data and a source-discriminating instrument, never a head count of forecasters.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.
3. **Showing the framework before taking the verdict spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
