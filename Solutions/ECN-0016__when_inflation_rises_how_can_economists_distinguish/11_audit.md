## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :30-32 (describe effect, shape not name), Fig 2 :35-42 (triangulation and anchor).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md` §2: read per component, add a low-overlap second observable, keep the union unbundled, settle at the ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete diagnosis of a named episode, for example "the 2022 energy-driven rise was supply, not demand".

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **component dispersion test** is a shape, not a number; deciding whether a rise is broad or concentrated requires a threshold and a reference period that the shorthand does not supply;
- the **direction test** assumes output and unemployment respond asymmetrically, which can fail when a supply shock coincides with tight demand (the mixed case the union cut is meant to catch);
- the **shape-not-a-name limit** is dropped by any concrete classification, because a classification names the source. That name is a binding from the economics frame and must be recorded as such.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: an aggregate reading underdetermines its source, and a discriminator needs an independent second reading ranged against outturn data. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no episode instrument was run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
