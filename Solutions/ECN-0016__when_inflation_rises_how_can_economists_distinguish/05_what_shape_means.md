## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form a diagnosis must have before any episode is named: the source of a rise in the price level is not carried by the price-level reading alone, so any discriminator must read the residual per component, bring a second observable whose response differs between the candidate sources, range both against outturn data, and keep a mixed episode unbundled. That is a constraint. "It was demand-pull" is a name, and is not licensed by the shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring terms that overlap it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* Here the plain description is: if it is demand, expect output up and unemployment down; if it is an adverse supply shock, expect prices up with output down and unemployment up.
- Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The BLS component table is the public instance of this: headline 3.7 percent against core 2.6 percent and energy 20.4 percent in the same month.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For an episode, that means the outturn data plus an instrument that separates the sources, not an argument from the price rise.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming output and unemployment as candidate discriminators is such an import from the economics frame: they are already public, checkable observables, and the import must be recorded as coming from that frame. It adds no primitive to the figures.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. Why source attribution is the forbidden error here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*, one green light or yellow plus blue or RGB or CMYK or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

An inflation rate is a green reading. Demand pressure and a cost shock can produce the same reading. The inflation rate cannot comment on the source. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (the second series plus component detail), not a name. The Wikipedia *Cost-push inflation* page makes the same point in economic language: each businessman sees costs rise, and the prior question is why costs rose. That is a source named from a projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. The question this essay answers

**What licenses naming the cause of a particular episode?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular cause is licensed only by the ground settling a candidate through an instrument, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the analyst's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the standard practice commit the forbidden error?** Often, yes. Calling a rise "cost-push" because energy prices rose is naming the source of a reading with an instrument that does not discriminate among sources, unless output, unemployment, core inflation and expectations are also examined. The correct form is the shape, with the name left open until the discriminator returns.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Output, unemployment and component indices are **already held observables in the economics frame**, not missing primitives. Naming them is a **binding from that frame**, not the invention of a new term.

What remains wrong is naming the **source** from the **reading**, where several sources can give the same reading. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held observables. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This correction mirrors the exemplar's own §5b, reached independently from the residual line.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A poll of prominent forecasters gathered to agree with a diagnosis is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the data settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any diagnosis and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
