## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about distinguishing a demand-driven inflation episode from a supply-driven one. Option A: treat the headline rate as a direct reading of the cause and classify from it. Option B: treat the headline rate as an underdetermined reading and require a source-discriminating shape. What each leads to: A names a cause from a lossy projection, aggregates away the per-slot signal, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B gives a checkable shape, written refuters and an open episode verdict until a ground settles it.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any episode-level name does not. A single aggregate inflation reading underdetermines its source. The discriminator must read per component, add a second observable with an opposite response under demand and supply, enumerate the published habitat, keep a mixed episode unbundled with reversible cuts, and settle the verdict at the ground rather than by agreement. The specific variables are world-sourced and `specified`, not figure-derived. The Fig 13 substrate reading and the ⋛ comparison reading were retracted as strains. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The strongest figure-grounded point is the aggregate to component move: Fig 1 :22 says an average throws away the only signal that says where to look, and the BLS component table shows it happening in public.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No classification is asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct the work; only the shape could be refined. If a classification were later sent, it could be superseded by a corrected one, and no primitive would have to be removed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A component dispersion test, a direction test on output and unemployment, or an expectations and wage test on a named episode could settle a source; the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 would withdraw the shape; a figure re-census that shows Fig 9 :30-31 does not govern source attribution would break F2. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
