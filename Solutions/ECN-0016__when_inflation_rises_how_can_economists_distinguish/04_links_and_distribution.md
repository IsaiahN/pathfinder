## Wider literature that bears on the question

1. **The disconfirming record inside the standard sources.** The *Cost-push inflation* page does not present the supply category as settled: it calls it "purported" and notes that *"Both accounts of inflation have at various times been put forward, with inconclusive evidence as to which explanation is superior."* That is a world-level warning against reading the cause off the price rise. It corroborates the figure shape but is not the source of the verdict.
2. **The mixed-episode record.** The *Stagflation* page says both supply shock and excess demand explanations are offered for the 1970s. That is why the derivation keeps a union and reversible cuts (Fig 9 :54-59) rather than forcing a single lever.
3. **The engineering analogue.** Blind source separation recovers source signals from mixed observations. One cannot specify a sensor from a description of the source; the underdetermination is structural and is narrowed by independence assumptions. This is a low-overlap corroboration of the requirement for a second, independent reading. It is *not* a figure line and cannot decide the verdict.
4. **The measurement anchor.** BLS publishes the component detail that makes the per-slot rule checkable in public. The headline and core numbers are separate published series, so the round trip gap is observable rather than assumed.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** : *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** : *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** : *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the sources.
- **Fig 2 :40** : *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A send selected for people already inclined to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 9 :60** : *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Contact scores and venue tiers are budget filters only.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what the recipient would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. The stage-1 verdict is recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :35, *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. Each user writes a fresh refuter and states a mode, because `THE_FORMULA.md` says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question"*.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must be a checkable shape with a named episode and a component split. As drafted, the shape is `specified` and ungrounded. That is honest, but it is not yet a ground verdict and must not be presented as one.
