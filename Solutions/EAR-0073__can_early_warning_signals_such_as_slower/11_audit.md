## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before relying on the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between an indicator prediction and an observation at a crossing. Arity: four cells, so not one scalar. Symmetry: ordered, because forewarning and hindsight are different readings of the same trend; the threshold relation `⋛` is a comparison between two quantities, not a symmetric join. Scale: first order inside one system's dynamics, second order at the observer arrangement. I reach the same union reading as the primary, but by a different route: I started from the four-cell structure and the orderedness of `⋛`, whereas the primary started from Fig 1's per-slot rule and Fig 9's split rule.
- **Domain 1, the indicator frame.** Actors: time series, variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, return rate, window, detrending. Currency: a pattern the frame produces. Anchor: none inside the frame. Not a domain with a ground.
- **Domain 2, the ground frame.** Actors: system, driver, crossing, replicated experiments. Currency: physical event. Anchor: the crossing, legitimate because the question is constitutively about it.
- **Domain 3, the observation channel.** Actors: sampling, series length, interpolation, window, case selection. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground, kept reachable. Both error directions live here.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: papers, reviews, multiple statistics from one series. Currency: agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run because all four domains are described from the same evidence pool.

Order by dependency: perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The objective link (what counts as reliable) is the break; effort spent adding indicators cannot rescue it. The union must be unbundled before search (Fig 9 :54).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading, and its dependency order. We used different starting figures: I began from the four-cell structure and the ordered comparison bond, the primary from Fig 1 and Fig 9. Low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29, :36 (anchor and legitimacy), Fig 9 :60 (filter versus verdict), Fig 10 :24, :25 (channel decay), Fig 8 :39 (no proof of absence).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2: the crossing is the ground; the signal is a channel; reliability is a per-class ordered pair of error rates; a warning threshold is a filter; no signal is not no threshold; the numeric rate is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete world claim an early-warning practitioner would act on: use rising autocorrelation or slower recovery in this basin as evidence that a crossing is approaching, with a stated lead time and false-alarm probability.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the crossing** at the ground. The figures say "range against the crossing"; the concrete case must choose a specific event in a specific record, and that choice is itself an authored selection (Dakos et al. 2008 determined transition points by eye, conservatively). Fig 10 :25 names pooled readings; case selection is the pooled reading before the analysis begins.
- the **error-rate objective**. The shorthand says "ordered pair"; the concrete case must pick a false-alarm tolerance before seeing the data. Boettiger and Hastings 2012b show that the answer changes radically with that choice (25 percent detection at 5 percent false alarms in simulation versus 5.4 percent in the glaciation record). Fig 3 :27 governs: an unmeasured objective is not a working link.
- the **resolution qualifier** on a null result. The shorthand says "no signal is not no threshold"; the concrete case must say at what sampling interval, window and detrending the signal failed to appear. Without those, the report is uninterpretable, and depth versus genuine absence stays open (Fig 6 :27).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a signal must range against a crossing, and the failure modes are channel failures. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists for this derivation; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which `09_watson_answer.md` states.
