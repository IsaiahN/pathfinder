## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that early warning works.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the framework's own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A larger audience of people who already like early-warning signals adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interior.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
