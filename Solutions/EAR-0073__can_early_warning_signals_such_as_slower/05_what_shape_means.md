## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can produce the number. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- shape: "an admissible reliability claim ranges against crossings, is per system class, reports an ordered pair of error rates, and discloses the channel" (mode: `general`);
- name or number: "slower recovery predicts a crossing with X percent sensitivity and Y percent false alarms in the Amazon basin" (mode: `grounded`, not available here).

Nothing in the figures, and nothing in this derivation, returns X and Y. Fig 9 :32 is the limit.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then follow *"new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms"*; *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The three parts of contact are a question, an encounter and an interpretation; a measurement with no question is noise, and a reading with no encounter is projection.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* The paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

The shape here was obtained by describing the residual in effect terms first (an indicator reading versus a crossing event), then letting the figures sort the result. The frame was not chosen first.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The world sources supply a number, but it is not the number for this question in general. Boettiger and Hastings 2012b report five different detection rates for five different dataset/indicator combinations. A reliability number imported from one of those datasets would be an import without a shadow test for any other system. It must be provenanced as "this dataset, this indicator, this error trade-off," not promoted to a universal.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the import rule: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* A likelihood-based indicator imported from statistics is a new dimension for the early-warning frame, not a new early-warning statistic. And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Declaring "saddle-node transition" as the assumed generative class makes that assumption visible, which is worth a great deal, but it is still an assumption.

## 4. Why a single "reliability" number is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A rising lag-1 autocorrelation is a green reading. It can be produced by a system genuinely approaching a fold bifurcation, or by a stable system on a chance excursion selected after the collapse (Boettiger and Hastings 2012a). A scalar "reliability" computed over these cases names a source from a projection. What is owed is a discriminator (replicates, a source-discriminating model, a longer series), not a number. This is the same structure as F4 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the limit vocabulary. There is a **distinction horizon** for each observer: *"the point at which the observer cannot discern any difference between one generation method and another."* And a **union horizon**: a projection sinks below it when *"no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* Crucially: *"Being 'below the union horizon' is never a settled verdict, only a report that nobody has reached it yet."* That is the precise canonical form of F7. A null early-warning result is a union-horizon report, not a proof of absence. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 5. The question is a bundle

*The Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* Fig 9 :4 says the same in the figure language: *"Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."*

"Can early-warning signals reliably indicate an approaching threshold?" bundles: (a) sensitivity, (b) specificity, (c) transfer across system classes, (d) the accepted error trade-off. The 2008 versus 2012 literature disagreement is exactly the symptom: one rule works well on the hindsight subset, another on the prospective subset. The bundle must be split before it is searched.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong."*

A highly cited review is a budget to try the claim, not evidence that the claim is true. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` never asks for agreement, and why `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails of famous researchers.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is simulacra. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of whether early-warning signals work, and it cannot settle the question it reports on.

*Arrival of the Fittest* records the same caveat about the formula itself: outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* This is why every stage-3 offer in this folder instructs the user to write their own refuter before running the machinery.

## 8. Society and habitat consequences

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different"* and *"the group's agreement is never the verdict."* Fig 1 :19, quoted there, is decisive: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Ten papers reusing one ice core are one observer with ten names. The same essay quotes Fig 10: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does,"* and reads a crash as *"a broken channel, not a broken world."* That is F4 verbatim in a different domain, a genuinely low-overlap corroboration from the essays.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* and *Arrival of the Fittest* both insist the ground is immovable and the frame cannot certify itself: *"Because a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So the verdict in `09_watson_answer.md` is from the figures, not from the number of world sources that happen to agree with them.
