# The Tether's answer on units, instruments and definitions in a time series

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain findings, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the numerical decomposition. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A recorded series shows a trend. Three changes can produce the same apparent trend without any change in the quantity: a change of unit, a change of instrument, a change of definition. The residual is the gap between the trend in the readings and the trend in the quantity. It is a union compound (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."). It has its own loop and its own ground in each domain. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

## 2. One loop per domain

### Domain 1, the quantity or ground

- **Actors:** the measured property, the world. **Currency:** the value the ground takes. **Anchor:** the ground itself.
- Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does."
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** the quantity does not move because the name, the scale or the sensor changed. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." A unit rescale is a re-expression, not a new member of the world.
- **Loop:** state R per slot (Fig 1 :19), range each proposed cause against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45: "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."), accept only what pays (THE_FORMULA.md step 5).

### Domain 2, the instrument or channel

- **Actors:** sensors, calibration, observing practice. **Currency:** readings `o′`. **Anchor:** the ground, reached through a discriminating instrument.
- Fig 10 :25-26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings." Fig 10 :27-28: "the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch."
- Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." Fig 6 :33: "Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars. A wandering point in the sky was already a reading, and every sharper instrument was built on the residual the last one produced."
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** an instrument change is a channel change. It can open a gap between readings and ground while the ground is still. The correction must be built from the old instrument's residual, which in practice means parallel measurements (old and new side by side), not from a verbal account of the quantity. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."
- **Boundary kept visible:** correcting the channel need not change the long-term slope; the ocean heat content record shows a case where decadal structure changed and the slope stayed similar. The claim is "can open a gap", not "always creates the trend".
- **Loop:** enumerate everything in contact with the residual (Fig 11 :43-44), find a second instrument pointed at the same thing so that independence is real (Fig 2 :31-32), measure the gap (Fig 4 :30).

### Domain 3, the unit or definition or convention

- **Actors:** standard bodies, category schemes, names, codes. **Currency:** shared meanings. **Anchor:** borrowed from D1; no independent truth anchor.
- Operators :25: "two names, one referent." Fig 10 :18: "a declared meaning is not a grounded one." Fig 10 :46: "A convention makes no claim about the world; a verdict and an atom both do."
- Fig 2 :41: "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it." Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** a unit or definition change is a convention, legitimate as shared meaning and forbidden from deciding the trend verdict. Fig 10 :47: "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one." A definition change re-slices the slots, so cases move between categories while the underlying thing does not; the fix is re-indexing, not averaging (Fig 1 :19).
- **Loop:** record which standard was in force on each date, re-map cases across the boundary, keep every cut ranked and reversible (Fig 9 :40-41: "a wrong cut removes the answer and speeds up so keep every cut ranked and reversible").

### Domain 4, the record or lineage

- **Actors:** station histories, revision dates, metadata, provenance. **Currency:** the trace of which change happened when. **Anchor:** the lineage itself, which is checkable.
- Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Fig 13 :130: "A term carries the atoms it was built from and the bonds that joined them. That record is not documentation of the term. It is the term."
- **Verdict (`specified`, triangulated):** provenance is the discriminator. A break in a series cannot be attributed from the break alone; two series can show the same step from a common standard or from independent convergence, and only the record separates them. Fig 6 :12: "source gate: provenance." Fig 6 :13: "shadow test: explains a gap."
- **Loop:** source-gate each cause and shadow-test it before admitting it; measure the round trip R_T rather than assuming the harmonised series equals the original (Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked.").

## 3. The shape in five moves

1. **Split** the series at every documented unit, instrument and definition change; treat intersections as their own cases (Fig 9 :4-5, :55-56).
2. **Bind** the convention as shared meaning, and never as evidence about the world (Fig 10 :47).
3. **Build** the channel correction from the worse instrument's residual, using parallel measurements (Fig 6 :32-33).
4. **Re-index** the slots across definition changes and carry the lineage (Fig 1 :19, Fig 13 :130).
5. **Keep the reference outside** the community doing the correcting (Fig 2 :33-35); refuse false terms before minting corrections (Operators :58: "a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate").

Over a fixed atom set, the pre-change and post-change series are isomers (Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance."). Where a definition change altered the counted world, they are members of one functional group instead (Fig 12 :124).

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The numerical share needs a ground reading and a source-discriminating instrument.
- Not that every apparent trend is an artefact. Real ground trends exist; the question is which part of the observed trend survives correction.
- Not that instrument correction always changes a slope. The ocean record is the boundary case.
- Not that a convention is false. It is true as shared meaning and silent about the world.
- Not a new primitive. Re-expression and reclassification add no atom to the world (Fig 6 :25).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument and a lineage, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: one green reading has many possible sources; "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." Here:

1. **Parallel measurement:** old and new instrument reading the same quantity over the same interval. If the offset is stable, the break is a channel change; if it is not, the residual is not yet resolved.
2. **Metadata check:** a station history or revision date that explains the gap. If no metadata exists, the cause is unreached at this budget, not absent (Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence.").
3. **Re-indexing test:** map every case across the definition boundary. If the apparent trend survives re-indexing, it is not definitional.
4. **Anchor test:** is the reference independent of the correcting community? If it updates on their own reports, coherent drift is the result (Fig 2 :34).
5. **Standing refuters from `01` §5:** a uniform correction that works everywhere; a unit change that moves the quantity; a channel correction that erases a real ground trend; a definition change with identical slot membership; a cause recoverable from values alone; an information-preserving harmonisation; a figure line that returns the numerical share.
6. **Round-trip measurement:** report what the harmonisation dropped instead of treating the new series as identical to the old.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :138-139: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself, and narrowing gets you close while the ground decides." The six-term substrate and the figure rules are a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; the lineages of real measurements decide which correction pays. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: a search that finds nothing is not a proof of absence. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A change of unit, instrument or definition does not move the quantity; it moves the name, the channel or the category through which the quantity is read. The three are a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors, so a single correction clears only the regions where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. What survives is a separator: segment at every documented change, treat a convention as shared meaning rather than evidence, build the channel correction from parallel measurements of the old and new instruments, re-index cases across definition changes, carry the provenance of each break, measure what harmonisation dropped, and keep the reference outside the correcting community. Over a fixed atom set the before and after series are isomers; where a definition changed the counted world they are members of one functional group. The framework does not return a percentage: that number needs a source-discriminating instrument and a lineage, which is a gap reported as a verdict, not a silence.
