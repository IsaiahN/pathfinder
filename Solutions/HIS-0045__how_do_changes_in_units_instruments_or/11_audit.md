## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x)), measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :29 (anchor), Fig 10 :18 and :24 and :47 (convention, channel, outcome), Fig 8 :51 (provenance), Fig 9 :54 (union).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07`, that a change of unit, instrument or definition moves the name, the channel or the category and not the quantity, so a separator is owed.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a named series, such as "the 1975 to 1985 ocean warm period was an XBT fall-rate artefact", or "the 2004 T2 jump in bladder cancer incidence was a staging-revision artefact".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **slot structure**: the shorthand says "per slot" but the concrete case must name which slots (Tmax versus Tmin, Tis versus T2), and the same instrument change has opposite valence in different slots;
- the **anchor provenance**: the shorthand says "a non-updating anchor", the concrete case needs a collocated CTD or a parallel thermometer, and the figures do not supply one;
- the **measurement of R_T**: the harmonisation itself drops information, and the concrete case must report what was dropped rather than presenting the new series as identical;
- the **boundary condition**: the shorthand can be read as "instrument correction always erases a trend", which the Levitus result contradicts. The correct reading is "can open a gap". This constraint is lost unless it is restated explicitly.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the quantity does not move when the name, code or channel changes, and the discriminator is provenance plus an instrument that separates sources. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading of a specific historical series was taken here; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
