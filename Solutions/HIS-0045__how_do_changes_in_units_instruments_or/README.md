## The verdict in one line

A change of unit, instrument or definition does not move the ground; it moves the channel or the convention through which the ground is read. The apparent trend is therefore a **union of at least three conditions with different anchors**, not one trend with noise. What is owed is a per-slot decomposition backed by provenance, an instrument that discriminates among sources, and a non-updating anchor. No figure line returns the numerical share of a trend attributable to each cause, so that share is a declared `gap`, which is a verdict and not a silence.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :13: "same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not". Gathering more readers of the same record does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :33).
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Fig 2 :41 permits that correlation only "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it", which is exactly why a unit is a convention and not a verdict.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in channels in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
