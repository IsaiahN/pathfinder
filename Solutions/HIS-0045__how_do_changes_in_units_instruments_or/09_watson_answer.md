## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, how far a trend in a recorded series is a trend in the quantity and how far it is produced by a change of unit, of instrument or of definition. Option A: treat the three as one measurement-error term and subtract a single correction. Option B: treat them as a union of overlapping conditions with different anchors and return a per-slot separator with provenance and a measured round-trip gap. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal, lets a convention decide an outcome, and manufactures confident dead reckoning; B keeps verdicts separate, names the instruments that would close each gap, and reports the share as `gap` until those instruments run.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the trend in readings `o′(t)` and the trend in the quantity. Arity: at least two time slices exposed to three conditions, so a union compound (Fig 9 :54). Symmetry: the causes are not interchangeable, since a unit rescale is an identity, an instrument change is an ordered channel replacement and a definition change alters the operand set. Scale: reading is first order, trend is a second-order composition over settled readings.
- **Domain 1, the quantity / ground.** Actors: the measured property and the world. Currency: the value the ground takes. Anchor: the ground (Fig 2 :29). Question type: what survives correction. Union member, last in dependency.
- **Domain 2, the instrument / channel.** Actors: sensors, calibration, observing practice. Currency: readings. Anchor: the ground reached through a discriminating instrument. Question type: channel change. Union member, first in dependency (Fig 10 :24).
- **Domain 3, the unit / definition / convention.** Actors: standard bodies, codes, category schemes. Currency: shared meanings. Anchor: borrowed from D1, since a convention alone has no truth anchor (Fig 10 :18). Question type: identity and slot re-slicing. Union member, second in dependency.
- **Domain 4, the record / lineage.** Actors: station histories, revision dates, metadata. Currency: the trace of which change happened when. Anchor: the checkable lineage. The seam binding the other three; required before the D1 verdict (Fig 13 :130).
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (is the channel returning anything) then vocabulary (is the code stable) then objective (what residual should shrink) then refuse before mint (formula step 2) then settle then promote. Fig 3 :22-23: "A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."

**Strain test applied.** Fig 10 :18 and Fig 2 :41 appear to conflict. Re-census: Fig 10 governs truth claims, Fig 2 :41 governs shared meaning. Different objects, so no strain. The D3 anchor is borrowed, which is recorded as a dependency rather than hidden as an independent anchor.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The union framing survives; the single-correction reading does not. A unit change is a convention and must not decide the trend verdict. An instrument change is channel decay while the ground does not decay, so the correction must be built from the old instrument's residual and checked against a second instrument pointed at the same thing. A definition change re-slices slots, so cases must be re-indexed and no standing correction table is valid. The discriminator is provenance plus a source-separating instrument, and harmonisation carries a measured round-trip gap. Over a fixed atom set the before and after series are isomers; where a definition changed the counted world they are members of one functional group. No figure line returns a numerical share, so that share is a declared `gap` of the unreached kind. Mode `specified` on domains D1 to D4, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`. The whole folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency, not a ground reading.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the separator is sent with its refuters, because each claim carries its own failure condition and provenance. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a finished percentage instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The union framing and the refuters can be revised without withdrawing the ground rule that the quantity does not move when the name or channel does. A published percentage could be superseded by a corrected decomposition once an instrument runs. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Parallel measurements with stable offsets that contradict the attributed cause; metadata that explains the gap differently; a re-indexing test that leaves the apparent trend intact against a definition change; a harmonisation with a demonstrated inverse; a figure line that returns the numerical share; or a universal correction that works on every interval and intersection. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
