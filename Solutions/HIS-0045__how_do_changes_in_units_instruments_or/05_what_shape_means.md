## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (carried): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can fill it. It constrains; it does not supply the number. For this question the shape is:

> a per-slot decomposition in which every change of unit, instrument or definition is attached to a provenanced record, checked against an anchor that did not itself change, and closed by an instrument that discriminates among candidate sources.

That is a shape. "The XBT fall-rate correction accounts for 60 percent of the 1975 to 1985 warm period" is a measurement, not a shape, and it comes from an instrument and a paper, not from the figures.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". The effect here is a break or slope change in a recorded series, described before naming a cause.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." A station history is exactly such a plain description: it says what would be seen if an instrument change had occurred.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it." In this question, the decomposition is obtained by enumerating the documented changes in the record; the "answer" is the enumerated lineage, not a number appended to it.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA.md step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means a parallel measurement or a physically independent instrument, such as XBT versus collocated CTD, or the old and new thermometer side by side.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, :12-13), with a source gate and a shadow test. An import is licensed when it explains a gap in the receiving frame and its origin is recorded.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

A choice of unit or category is such a visible choice. Naming it as *the* cause of a trend turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The instrument reads a projection, not the source

This is the governing essay for the present question. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened directly):

- "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."
- An instrument detects green but "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source could be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered.
- "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."
- The colour case "was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: the break in a temperature series is the green reading. Unit rescale, instrument replacement and category revision are the different sources that can produce it. A slope fitted to the series cannot name which source acted. What is owed is a discriminator, not a more confident interpretation. This is the deepest echo between the essay and Fig 8 :51: "Only the record of where each came from separates them."

## 5. Q for this question: what shape is the answer allowed to have?

**Nothing in the framework returns a numerical attribution of a trend to its causes.** The shape fixes the following, and no more:

1. The residual stays indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19), so maximum and minimum temperature, or Tis and T2 stage, keep separate verdicts.
2. The three causes form a union (Fig 9 :54-57), so a single lever leaves intersections standing.
3. A convention changes a name or a realisation, not the quantity (Operators :25, Fig 10 :18), and must not decide the verdict (Fig 10 :47).
4. An instrument change is a channel change, and the ground does not decay (Fig 10 :24).
5. The discriminator is provenance plus a source-separating instrument (Fig 8 :51, Fig 6 :12-13).
6. Harmonisation is a round trip with a measured gap R_T (Fig 4 :30).
7. The anchor must not update on the correcting community's own report (Fig 2 :33-35).

Any answer that supplies those seven items and then states the measured share from an actual instrument is a complete answer. Any answer that supplies the share without those seven items is a projection with the lossy information hidden.

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I overstated the "shape, not a name" rule in the first pass. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. A unit, a thermometer and a stage definition are **not missing primitives**; they are bindings onto things a historical record already holds. Naming them is a provenanced binding, not the invention of a new term. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the framework derives which one acted, and (b) claiming a mere re-expression moves the ground (Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world.").

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted: a break still cannot name its source from the reading alone, and the discriminator is still an instrument. The correction is only that naming a candidate source is allowed when its provenance is supplied. Both claims are kept visible so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous historians who agree with the decomposition is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the check against a real record.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the historical claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
