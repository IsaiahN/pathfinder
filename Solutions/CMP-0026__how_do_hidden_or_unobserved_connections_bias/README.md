## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, no scraped contact list.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A shared evidence pool is not corroboration.** Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures exactly the correlation that voids their agreement.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full framework before asking for a verdict converts a potential outside frame into a member of the frame being checked.
3. **Agreement is not a ground.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* The ask is for a refutation against a ground, not an endorsement.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That practical failure sits on top of the structural one. Nothing was hosted or sent by this agent.
