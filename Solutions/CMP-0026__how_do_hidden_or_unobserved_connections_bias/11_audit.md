## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-slot gap between conclusions from an incomplete graph and conclusions the full system would settle. Arity: at least two operands, usually with a third unobserved operand, so a bond relation. Symmetry: the error is not symmetric with its correction. Absence of a bond uses subtraction; calling a recorded bond wrong requires negation, which the seven bonds do not contain. Scale: first order in the graph, second or third order in the contact cascade, plus a round trip observed to inferred to observed.
- **Domain 1, bond grammar.** Actors: entities as atoms, edges as typed bonds, seven bonds plus negation. Currency: bits to state and actions to settle. Anchor: the figure definitions of bond and isomer, and the ground for measurement. Question type: partition at first order, union at the question level.
- **Domain 2, inference instrument.** Actors: observer, instrument, observed graph, model family, hidden nodes. Currency: residual and reliability. Anchor: the ground. Question type: three distinct questions, SEARCHED, INWARD and OUTWARD.
- **Domain 3, behavior.** Actors: processes, flows, gradients, cascades. Currency: influence over a network structure. Anchor: a measured process outcome. Question type: compound of structure with state and gradient.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: outside frames. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run its independence is zero.

Order by dependency: enumerate the habitat before modelling edges (Fig 11 :43); unbundle the union before searching (Fig 9 :54); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); separate the three step-7 questions; measure `R_T` (Fig 4 :30).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran it on the condensation the folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the operational claim a researcher would act on: infer specific missing edges, impute them, report a corrected network and its measures.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a hidden connection is a missing bond and its omission is invisible in an ingredient-only record, and the residual is per conclusion slot so verdicts must not be averaged.
