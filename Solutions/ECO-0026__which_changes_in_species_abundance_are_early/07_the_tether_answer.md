## 1. The residual it answers

At time t a monitor holds a time-indexed set of abundance readings for a community. At some later
time the community may undergo a regime shift, or it may not. The monitor must decide, before the
shift, whether a pattern in those readings is a precursor or noise. A false negative is an
unanticipated collapse; a false positive is an intervention with its own ecological and political
cost. The anchor is the realized future of that community: did it shift, and when. That anchor is a
measurement and does not move because an analyst prefers a different indicator (Fig 2 :25 "pinned:
it does not update on A or B", Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update.").

The question as phrased is not one question. It bundles at least five candidate change types: a
monotonic decline of one species; rising variability or memory in an existing species; change in the
covariance among species; change in the interaction structure; and loss of rare or functionally
redundant species. These are a **union compound**, not a list of separate hard problems. Fig 9 :54
"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."
Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and
becomes a specific compound." No single statistic can clear the intersections, so a census precedes
search.

The sought-for shape was written down as an ordinary term before the run (`01_figure_grounded_audit.md`
§0), so the preference for it can accrue a residual and be refused: a candidate answer (i) is indexed
by slot s, never only by a community total; (ii) is read against a fixed future anchor; (iii)
distinguishes the quantity that changes from the bond that changes; (iv) states its own failure
condition.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- It does not name rising variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, flickering, spectral reddening,
  a sentinel species, or any other abundance statistic as the answer. Those names are provenanced
  imports, not derivations.
- It does not say a community total is useless. It says the total is downstream of a per-slot call,
  and can be near zero while one slot is live.
- It does not say every declining species is a warning. A warning is owed only where live mass is
  present, and the bond type must be tested.
- It does not settle whether the warning precedes, produces, or thresholds the collapse in any
  particular ecosystem.
- It is not a new theory of critical transitions and introduces no new primitive.
- It does not treat agreement among indicators, or agreement among readers, as a verdict. Prestige
  and consensus are filters for where to spend attention, never witnesses for the claim.
- It is not `grounded`. Nothing here has been settled by a collapse dataset or a prospective test.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

The pre-registered refuters were written before the conclusion (full form in `01` §4). The shape
claim is withdrawn in part if any of the following fires:

1. A collapse is reliably forecast, on held-out data, by a community aggregate while every per-slot
   residual remains at zero (breaks the per-slot finding).
2. Two communities with identical ingredient sets and demonstrably different bond arrangements show
   the same collapse behaviour in a replicated experiment (breaks the bond finding).
3. Two statistics from the same time series are shown to be independent frames by a low-overlap test,
   or conditioning on observed collapse does not change the false-positive rate (breaks the fixed
   anchor and independence finding).
4. An indicator transfers from microcosm to whole ecosystem with a measured round-trip gap of zero
   on held-out data (breaks the cross-scale finding).
5. A specific abundance statistic is derived from the figure set alone and then settled by a ground
   reading (breaks the naming `gap`, the honest way to be wrong here).

Beyond refutation, settlement needs a discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The
Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): "the instrument is speaking only about what
it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The practical instruments here are a prospective
monitoring design with a fixed future anchor and independently calibrated channel; a replicated
arrangement experiment measuring bonds, not just ingredient lists; a measured `R_T` across scales;
and, for a name, a statistic that survives all five refuters and is then paid for by the ground.

## 5. The honest stop

The answer stops where the instruments stop. The figures decide the constraints a warning must
satisfy; they do not decide which abundance statistic satisfies them. Naming ends in `gap`, plainly,
and Fig 13 :135 explains why a gap is the correct output rather than a silence: the missing primitive
cannot be named without already holding it. Fig 9 :44 forbids converting a failed search into a
claim that no warning exists. The single live strain is the cross-scale round-trip gap, which is
named and owed a measurement rather than smoothed over; the bond type of a real collapse is an open
ground question, not a contradiction between figures.
