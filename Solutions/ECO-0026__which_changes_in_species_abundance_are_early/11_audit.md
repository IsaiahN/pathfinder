## 1. Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line in this folder's `README.md` before reading the primary's
conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors
/ currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between what the monitoring frame
  predicts and what the abundance channel returns, ranged against the realized future. Arity: many,
  one per species or functional slot plus residuals on the bonds between slots. A community total is
  one number and cannot be this residual. Symmetry: time is ordered, so `→`, `⇒` and `⋛` are
  different bonds; a precursor is not the same term as a producer. Scale: within-population,
  community-wide, and across ecosystem types, with a lossy transform between scales.
- **Domain A, the monitored community.** Actors: species, functional groups, their abundances and
  their relations (pollination, predation, competition, dependency). Currency: per-slot readings and
  live mass `R⁺_s`. Anchor: the realized future, the shift or its absence, which a monitor cannot
  move. Nameable anchor, so this is a domain.
- **Domain B, the monitoring frame.** Actors: channel, sampling schedule, detrending choices,
  summary statistics, analysts. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the same realized future,
  ranged against separately. Nameable anchor, so this is a domain. Two statistics from one series
  draw on one evidence pool and are one frame.
- **Domain C, the warning-to-collapse relation.** Actors: the abundance change and the regime shift,
  joined by `→`, `⇒` or `⋛`. Currency: order and threshold. Anchor: the collapse event, which fixes
  earliness. Nameable anchor, so this is a domain, but only the tests are available inside the
  figures, not the answer.
- **Domain D, the naming of a statistic.** Actors: candidate statistics. Currency: description cost
  and action cost. Anchor: none inside the figures. Not yet a domain; this is the open residue of A
  to C and must be reported as `gap`.
- **Partition or union.** A, B and C are a union compound, not a partition. A warning is a statement
  about a community made through a channel with a temporal relation to a threshold. The question as
  phrased bundles at least five change types, so unbundling precedes search. My route to this was
  Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search
  again." and Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a
  mixture and becomes a specific compound." The primary reached it from the five-condition census in
  `01` §0. Different starting points, same boundary.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception first: Fig 3 :5 "the system can tell what is in front of it,
  and tell it apart from what was there before". Then vocabulary for what a species or bond affords.
  Then the objective: an early correct call rather than a retrospective fit. Then planning under a
  finite budget. Then learn and carry: methods, not recordings. Fig 3 :19 "Optimising a link that
  already works cannot rescue one that does not."

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its decision to leave Domain D
as `gap`. The agreement is expected: we share one evidence pool, so it is internal consistency and
carries no independent weight.

## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what
came back down, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be
measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure lines, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22/:28 (per-slot residual),
   Fig 12 :95-96/:117 (bond and isomer), Fig 2 :31/:33/:40 (anchor and independence), Fig 10 :24-26
   (channel), Fig 9 :32 and Fig 13 :134/:135 (shape, not a name).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: warn per slot and per bond, fix the
   anchor, check the channel, measure the transfer gap, keep names as imports.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the operational reading a monitor would actually deploy, for example a
   named statistic such as rising variance or lag-1 autocorrelation applied to a total abundance in a
   lake, then transferred to a reef or forest.

**R_T is nonzero.** At least five constraints in the shorthand fail to survive the trip back down
when the concretisation is a named statistic on an aggregate:

- **Per-slot indexing** is dropped if the monitor reads a total; Fig 1 :28's pointer to where to look
  is gone.
- **Bond arrangement** is dropped when the species list is recorded without interactions; Fig 12 :117
  makes that a different substance, not a coarser version of the same one.
- **Anchor independence** is dropped if the indicator is tuned on cases selected because they
  collapsed; Fig 2 :40 disqualifies that agreement.
- **Channel state** is dropped if "no warning" is reported without a transient-preservation check;
  Fig 10 :24-26 makes that a report about the channel.
- **Bond type** is dropped if `⋛` is assumed rather than tested against `→` and `⇒`; Fig 12 :71/:77/:87
  keeps the three distinct.
- **Shape-not-a-name** is dropped if a provenanced import is presented as a figure output; Fig 8 :51
  requires the record to separate them.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a warning must be indexed by slot and bond against a fixed
anchor, with the channel intact. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured
numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of
dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the figures" as identity would
set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy
condensation with a recorded gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
