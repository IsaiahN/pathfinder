## Residual stated as an effect

At time t a monitor sees an abundance time series. At some later time the community may undergo a
regime shift, or it may not. Option A: read a change in a community-level aggregate (total abundance,
mean diversity, average cover) as the warning. Option B: read the residual on individual species or
functional slots, and on the bonds between them, with a fixed future anchor. A spends the only
signal that says where to look; B keeps it but cannot, from the figures alone, name a specific
indicator. What an answer would look like is written down and bound as an ordinary term in `01` and
`06`, so the preference for B is refusable rather than invisible.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that a returned agreement would corroborate anything.
- No assertion of a named indicator (variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, declining
  abundance of a particular species) as the figure-derived answer. The figures return a shape. The
  names in the world literature are recorded as provenanced imports, never as derivations.

## Key verdict, in one line

The figures decide that a warning must be read per slot and per bond against a fixed anchor, that
aggregate indices can hide a live signal, and that a warning is a shape, not a name; they leave the
choice of any particular abundance statistic to a ground reading, so the naming part of the question
ends in `gap`, plainly.
