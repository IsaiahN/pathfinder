## 2. What the wider literature bears on the residual

Read as secondary corroboration only. None of these names is presented as a figure-derived answer.

1. **Aggregate versus per slot.** O'Brien et al. 2023 reports that regime shifts do not occur
   uniformly across taxa and that work often focuses on "single representative measures of the
   system such as total abundance". This corroborates F1 from a different evidence pool: the
   aggregate can hide the slot-specific signal. Masuda et al. 2024 adds the network version: not all
   nodes emit equally useful warnings, and combining nodes helps only under certain noise and
   correlation structures.
2. **The selected-on-collapse trap.** Boettiger and Hastings 2012 shows that conditioning on an
   observed transition inflates apparent warning trends. This corroborates F3, the fixed-anchor
   rule, in the currency of statistics rather than the currency of frames.
3. **Indicators are probabilistic, not deterministic.** Dakos et al. 2015 and Alvarez-Martinez and
   Miramontes 2026 both state versions of the same limit: indicators say something is changing, not
   what or when. This corroborates the shape-not-a-name verdict in `01` F8.
4. **Mechanism specificity.** Dakos et al. 2015 lists six mechanisms of regime shift, only one of
   which is a slow approach to a bifurcation. Kuehn 2013 shows normal-form warnings can mislead.
   These are the world-side reason to keep the bond-type question in F9 open rather than assuming
   `⋛`.
5. **Pre-registration.** Autzen 2022 supplies the low-overlap, human-reasoning parallel for the
   pre-registered refuter: "the data may be used only once". This is an analogy about method, not a
   figure line and not a verdict in ecology.

Overlap is declared rather than hidden. Scheffer 2009, Dakos 2012, Dakos 2015 and Alvarez-Martinez
2026 share authors, institutions, and a common evidence pool; their agreement is one pool talking,
not four witnesses. Boettiger and Hastings, Kuehn, Autzen, Masuda et al. and O'Brien et al. draw on
different pools (statistics, mathematics, philosophy of science, network theory, empirical
limnology), so they are genuinely lower overlap. Status per source is in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the shape claim and
  its refuter. Do not open with the framework.
- **Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."** Do not
  ask for agreement. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure.
- **Fig 2 :31 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the
  same thing are."** Do not send the same packet to a group and count the replies as corroboration.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Stage 3 sends the formula as a
  method, not an answer.
- **Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you
  a verdict."** A venue's prestige, or a recipient's enthusiasm, is a filter for where to spend
  attention, never a witness for the claim.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) the question "what
would you count as a failure?" and the stage-1 verdict recorded; (3) only then the three local links,
with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) the method offer, with the
instruction to write a fresh refuter and state a mode.

## 4. The two-stage offer, and the honest third stage

The proposition is: **(1)** validate the shape against the ground, **(2)** only if curious, read
where it came from, **(3)** reuse the formula on a problem with a ground the user can check. This
order follows from Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate
with it." Stage 2 is opt-in by construction because Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a
recipient has absorbed the framework, their later agreement stops being evidence and is never
pooled. Stage 3 is permitted because a generator can be given away without giving away an answer,
but each user must supply their own ground and their own refuter, since `THE_FORMULA.md` says the
loop cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `01` F1, F2, F3 and
F5 are the figure-governed part and are the only part offered as a checkable output. The named
indicators are withheld pending a provenanced import and a ground reading.

## 5. What cannot be done here

A one-to-one packet can be prepared and hosted, but this folder sends nothing. Hosting a download
page that lets a recipient decline contact is closer to the figure's rule than an attachment pushed
to a list. No personal addresses appear in `03_public_contact_points.csv` and no bulk-send plan
exists anywhere in this folder. A Track B style receptiveness estimate is deliberately absent:
selecting recipients for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies their
agreement.
