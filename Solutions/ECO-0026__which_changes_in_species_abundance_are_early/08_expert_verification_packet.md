# Expert verification packet: Which changes in species abundance are early signs of a coming ecological collapse?

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist or dynamical-systems specialist can check. Mode,
derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what
would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape claims, `general` on the structural constraints, `gap` on the naming
of a particular statistic. Not `grounded`.** No abundance time series, collapse event or monitoring
instrument was read as a ground reading. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal
consistency against the verbatim figures, not a test of the ecological claim. Quotations marked
`Fig N :line` are verbatim from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A warning of ecological collapse has to be read per species or functional slot and per interaction
bond, against a fixed future anchor, before a community aggregate is formed. A total abundance or
mean diversity index can sit at a near-zero residual while one slot carries positive live mass, and
two communities with the same species list but different interaction arrangements are different
substances whose difference a total cannot record. The monitoring channel must be checked first,
because early warnings are transient, tail and high-frequency phenomena that routine sampling drops,
so a report of "no warning" may be a report about the channel. Agreement among indicators is not a
verdict unless the indicators draw on independent evidence, and a warning calibrated at one scale
carries a round-trip gap that must be measured when it is moved to another. The figures return this
shape only. They name no statistic, so the naming part of the question is reported as `gap`, with
variance, lag-1 autocorrelation and the rest kept as provenanced imports, never as figure outputs.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Per-slot and per-bond, not the aggregate (framework-internal and ecology-facing)

- **Statement:** the early sign cannot be a community total. It is a residual on a slot, or a change
  in the bond between slots, read before averaging. Two communities with identical ingredient sets
  and different arrangements are isomers under the bond figure.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."
  Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
  Fig 1 :28 "The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has
  thrown away the only signal that says where to look."
  Fig 12 :16 "and nothing signals that anything was lost"
  Fig 12 :95-96 "Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are
  different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by
  the bond."
  Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."
- **What to check:** is a community total genuinely a near-zero-residual state while a single species
  diverges in the reader's own data or experience? Is the interaction arrangement, not just the
  species list, what changes before the shift in the cases they know?
- **Refuter (written before the conclusion):** a documented case in which a collapse is reliably
  forecast on held-out data by a community aggregate while every per-slot residual stays at zero,
  with no slot carrying positive live mass at any sampling time; or a replicated experiment in which
  communities sharing every slot and differing only in measured bond arrangement show the same
  collapse threshold, timing and post-shift state. If either holds, C1 falls.

### C2. Fixed anchor, independent frames, intact channel (framework-internal and statistics-facing)

- **Statement:** the anchor is the realized future and must not update. Two statistics from one time
  series are one frame, so their agreement is not corroboration. A report of "no warning" is not a
  verdict about the ecosystem until the channel is shown to preserve transients and tail events.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update."
  Fig 2 :31 "Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the
  same thing are."
  Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
  Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
  Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the
  ground votes."
  Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does"
  Fig 10 :25-26 "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
  Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."
- **What to check:** do the reader's warning statistics come from one series or from truly separate
  measurements? Were the historical cases selected because they collapsed, which is the
  prosecutor's fallacy that Boettiger and Hastings 2012 documents? Was the sampling schedule designed
  to avoid exactly the autocorrelation a warning test needs, as Dakos et al. 2012 notes?
- **Refuter:** a low-overlap test showing that two statistics from one series are independent frames
  in the figure's sense, or that conditioning on the observed collapse does not change the estimated
  false-positive rate; plus an independent channel calibration preserving transients and tail events
  at the relevant scale in a system where no warning precedes a collapse. If both hold, C2 falls.

### C3. Shape, bond type and transfer gap (framework-internal, with an honest `gap`)

- **Statement:** the figures supply the constraints a warning must satisfy, the tests separating
  sequence from production from threshold, and the rule that a cross-scale transfer carries a
  measured gap. They do not supply the name of a statistic or the bond type of a real collapse.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"
  Fig 9 :44 "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So
  searching and finding nothing proves nothing."
  Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"
  Fig 13 :135 "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already
  holding it."
  Fig 12 :77 "A produces B; B did not exist before"
  Fig 12 :87 "a threshold relation between two quantities"
  Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead
  reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
- **What to check:** can the reader derive a specific abundance statistic from the figures alone?
  If not, the `gap` stands, and any named statistic must be declared as an import with a source. Does
  the claimed precursor merely precede the shift, produce it, or cross a bound?
- **Refuter:** a derivation, from the figure set alone, of a specific abundance statistic that pays
  its description cost and is then settled by a ground reading; or an indicator that transfers from
  microcosm to whole ecosystem with a measured round-trip gap of zero on held-out data. Either breaks
  C3 in the honest direction.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named statistic (variance, lag-1 autocorrelation, skewness, spectral reddening,
  flickering, dynamic network biomarkers, sentinel-node sets) is the figure-derived answer. Each is
  an import, recorded with author, venue, year, quote and overlap in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
- Not that a community total is useless. It is downstream of a per-slot call and can hide a live
  signal.
- Not that every declining species, or every rising variance, is a warning.
- Not that the bond type (sequence, production or threshold) is known for any given ecosystem.
- Not that a warning transfers unchanged between scales, and not that a round-trip gap is zero.
- Not that agreement among experts, or the prestige of a venue, corroborates the claim. Prestige can
  fund a test; only the ground can settle it.
- Not `grounded`. The derivation is simulated and checked for internal consistency only.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating test, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay,
opened 2026-11-23): "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the
source." For this question the loading tests are:

1. A prospective monitoring design with a fixed future anchor and an independently calibrated
   channel, run before the collapse is known.
2. A replicated arrangement experiment measuring bonds as well as ingredient lists, to test whether
   the bond carries a warning.
3. A measured round-trip gap `R_T` when a warning is moved from microcosm to ecosystem.
4. A ground reading that pays for a named statistic after it survives all five refuters in §2 and
   `01` §4.

A failure is more useful than an endorsement. If any sub-claim fails, the corresponding part of the
shape is withdrawn and the census is redone.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read the three sub-claims in §2 and the five refuters in `01` §4. They are stated before the
   conclusion, so you can stop here if one fires.
2. Open `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md` and check the ten `Fig N :line` quotes in §2 against
   the verbatim extract. They are short passages; no framework background is needed.
3. Name one dataset or one system you know where a community total stayed flat while a species or a
   bond changed. If you cannot, say so; that is a result, not a gap in the packet.
4. Say which bond you expect in your system: `→` sequence, `⇒` production, or `⋛` threshold, and what
   would discriminate them.
5. Name one way to check that a monitoring channel preserves transients and tail events at the
   relevant scale.
6. Write one refuter of your own, state a mode (`general`, `specified`, `grounded`), and record a
   stage-1 verdict before reading any framework material. Send the refutation. Agreement, if it
   comes, is never pooled as corroboration.
