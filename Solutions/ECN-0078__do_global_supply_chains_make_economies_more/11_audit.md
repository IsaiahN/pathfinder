**`R_T` is nonzero.** Four constraints held in the shorthand do not survive the return:

One thing survives cleanly: a cross-border chain is a changed habitat, and its two effects must be read at the same slots against different anchors. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was not measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
