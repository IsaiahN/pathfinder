## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that global supply chains make economies more efficient but more vulnerable to disruption. Option A: accept the claim as one trade-off dial and seek a net coefficient. Option B: treat it as a union of two conditions with different anchors in a changed habitat, keep the verdicts separate, and report the aggregate as `gap`. What each leads to: A hands a filter a verdict (Fig 9 filter rule) and hides a live per-slot signal inside an average; B returns a checkable separator, four failure conditions and the instrument that would be owed. The word "but" is a rhetorical contrast, not a figure-licensed operator.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** One intervention, global supply chain integration, appears to produce two effects in two currencies. Arity: two conditions plus a scope qualifier, so a union compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :55 says *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Symmetry: both effects are threshold comparisons, but of different quantities, so the coupling is not a symmetric bond. Scale: per firm, link and tier first, then economy; the membrane between is lossy.
- **Domain 1, efficiency / cost frame.** Actors: firms, suppliers, imported inputs, consumers, markets. Currency: cost, price, TFP (bits, the price of a description). Anchor: a paying market or measured cost, which does not move because a frame thinks well of it. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 2, disruption / resilience frame.** Actors: nodes, tiers, chokepoints, inventory, backup sources, shocks. Currency: realized loss and time to recover (actions, the cost of settling under disruption). Anchor: a realized disruption and recovery outcome. Verdict `specified`, triangulated.
- **Domain 3, network habitat.** Actors: nodes, edges, hubs, concentration, cascade paths. Currency: connectivity, path length, cascade reach. Anchor: observed topology and failure outcomes. Verdict `general` on the rules, `specified` on the application, triangulated.
- **Domain 4, aggregate "economies" frame.** Actors: researchers, models, national accounts. Currency: agreement and residuals. Anchor: none common to both effects. Two anchors, not one. Verdict `gap`.
- **Order by dependency.** Set the habitat first (Fig 11); read each effect per slot second (Fig 1); compose third; attempt the aggregate and refuse it fourth (Fig 9 :54). Refuse before mint (Formula step 2); describe the residual before choosing a frame.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the aggregate and the universal coefficient do not. Global supply chain integration changes the habitat by adding contact that cannot be subtracted, so the same arrangement lowers cost against a market anchor and changes exposure against a disruption anchor. The two readings are in two currencies that do not add, so they are kept per domain and never averaged. A link's valence flips with the residual, and over a fixed actor set lean and resilient configurations are isomers. The firm-level results can settle and become atoms at the economy scale, but the round trip is lossy and no figure composes them into a net verdict. This is `specified` on each domain, `general` on the structural rules, `gap` on the aggregate, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the separator is used, because its four refuters travel with it and a failure is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is taking the other branch: handing a filter a verdict (Fig 9 filter rule), hiding a live per-slot signal inside an economy-wide average (Fig 1 :19), and letting a saturated cost metric sever the channel to the disruption ground (Fig 10 :24). Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Here that means two low-overlap frames reading a shared shock dataset separately, not a larger agreement pool.
- **undoable:** yes. Each domain verdict is revisable without withdrawing the others. No primitive must be removed and no wall must be moved. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet with the same shape, and the `gap` can be closed by a new instrument rather than by a new assertion.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A linked dataset recording, per slot and per shock, cost saving, shock class, cascade depth, recovery time and the bond between actors would name the winner. A demonstration that one currency and one anchor measure both effects with a stable cross-slot exchange rate, or that first-tier enumeration fully predicts realized loss, would break the shape. A measured firm-to-economy round trip with small `R_T` would close the aggregate `gap`. Prestige and later agreement are not such instruments.
