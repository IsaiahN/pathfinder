## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the form of an answer you can state before you can name the specific filler. For ECN-0078 the shape is: two effects in two currencies, read per slot against two anchors, in a habitat whose contact has been changed. It constrains any acceptable answer. It does not fill in a net percentage, a particular supplier, or a particular buffer size. `LOW COST(???)LOW EXPOSURE` is a shape; `dual sourcing at 12 percent inventory` is a name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, note recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach email asks what a recipient would expect to see at each slot before showing the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* Here the stop is the separator, not a number.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A realized disruption and recovery outcome would name which bond actually paid.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For this question the held atoms are actors, links, inventory, capacity and contracts. Naming a particular source as *the* resilience answer is a binding from the operations frame, not a derivation. The figures return a shape; the data would return a name.

## 4. The source problem: a cost reading cannot name its cause

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

Applied here: a low unit cost is a reading. It is compatible with a well designed lean chain, with a hidden single-source dependency, and with an unpriced risk transfer down the tiers. The cost instrument cannot comment on which source produced it. What is owed is a discriminator that separates those cases, not a verdict read off the cost. This is the deepest reason F9 is a `gap`: no instrument in the ledger reads the source of a cost saving at the relevant slot.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

## 5. Correction to my own §3 (see `06` Q1)

I initially wrote "shape, not a name" as if it forbade naming any specific sourcing arrangement. It does not. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. An extra supplier, a buffer, a dual source and a capacity reservation are all already available bindings in the operations frame. Naming one is a **binding**, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong after the correction is: claiming that a binding moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Adding a real supplier to a real chain moves the wall in the world, but importing its description into a frame only adds an atom to the frame if the frame did not hold that kind of thing. The honest reason we cannot name a winner is not a ban on names; it is that no figure and no opened source discriminates among the bindings at the economy scale. Both the overstatement and the corrected form are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Why the "economies" aggregate is a shape failure

*Library of Babel*: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is the union verdict of `01` F1. The combination is not a weighted average. Fig 12 :130: *"Summing them into one figure is the error this names, not the fix for it."*

The shape survives as four conditions that any answer must satisfy: an anchor that does not update for each reading, per-slot indexing, a stated bond between the effects, and a measured round trip across the firm-to-economy membrane. The aggregate number does not.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well known researchers who agree that the trade-off exists is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the separator against a disruption dataset.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the economics and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. What would turn the shape into a name

A source-discriminating instrument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, the spectrometer is the model: an instrument that separates the paths that yield the same reading. Here the candidate is a linked dataset that records, per slot and per shock, the realized cost saving, the shock class, the cascade depth, the recovery time and the bond that connected the actors. Two frames with low overlap, one reading engineering cascade outcomes and one reading trade and macro outcomes, would then range against it separately (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :45). Until that exists, the name is withheld.
