## 3. What bears directly, and what does not

1. **The efficiency anchor is corroborated but not universal.** WTO 2021 and World Bank 2023 support a lower-cost and higher-productivity reading where a market pays. Review of World Economics 2025 narrows it: in that sample the gain is regional, not global. This is a scope correction, not a conflict with the figures. No figure returns the magnitude.
2. **The two signs come from different shock classes.** World Bank 2023 reports reduced domestic-shock vulnerability and increased external-shock sensitivity from the same integration. That is Fig 11 :49 in world terms: *"valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*
3. **The topology analogue is the strongest low-overlap witness.** Albert et al. 2000 gives one structure with two opposite verdicts depending on which residual is read. It corroborates the separator, not the economics.
4. **The complexity literature is a conflict returned to census.** May 1972 and Allesina and Tang 2012 disagree about whether complexity stabilizes. Fig 8 :48 governs evidence overlap, not connectance. The two are different quantities, so the conflict was re-censused and the verdict was not flipped. Both are recorded in the ledger and in `01` F10.
5. **The literature is one broad evidence pool.** The SCM and operations papers cite each other and share case studies. Their agreement is not two witnesses to the framework. Only the physics and ecology papers are genuinely low overlap.

## 4. Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the separator and its four refuters; offer the framework after.
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A reader who has absorbed the interiors before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28:** *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see at each slot before showing the article.
- **Fig 2 :40:** *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Do not rank recipients by likely agreement and do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- **Fig 6 :24:** *"A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."* An imported analogy that fits loosely is worse than no analogy. Say plainly which world sources corroborate structure and which only name a case.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure at each slot; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then, the three framework files, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## 5. The two-stage offer

**Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction:** once a recipient has read the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. **Stage 3 sends a method**, which Fig 4 :22 permits, because a generator given away is not an answer given away. Each user must still name a ground and write a refuter before running the formula, since a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. As drafted it does not yet engage a real disruption dataset, so the four refuters in `01` §4 stay attached and visible.
