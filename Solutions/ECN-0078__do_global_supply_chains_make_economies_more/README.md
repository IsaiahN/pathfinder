## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named researchers, even where a published CFP prints them.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are the figures' own: a room does not add an anchor (Fig 7 :13, Fig 2 :35); selecting for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40); and a reader who has absorbed the whole frame before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked (Fig 10 :42).
