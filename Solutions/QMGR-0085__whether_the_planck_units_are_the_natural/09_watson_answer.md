## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether the Planck units are the natural scale of quantum gravity. Option A: assert that they are, because `l_P = sqrt(ħG/c^3)` is the unique length from `c`, `ħ` and `G` and both theories are needed there. Option B: keep a shape (a threshold whose magnitude is owed to the ground), refuse the name, and split the bundled conditions. What each leads to: A sends a convention-dependent number as a ground verdict and points a programme at a scale that may not be nature's; B sends a checkable shape, written refuters and an instrument search, and stops honestly where instruments stop discriminating.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The claim "the Planck units are the natural scale of quantum gravity" is **refused as written**. D1 gives a convention-stable form but no anchor. D2 is the legitimate anchor and has not returned a Planck-scale reading. D3 keeps alternative scales live. D4 permits a shape only. What survives: there is a threshold at which the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, built from held constants; its magnitude is owed to the ground; Planck, Stoney, strong and reduced unit systems are isomers of one another. This is `specified`, ungrounded, `candidate`. The world sources independently reach "not established" through low-overlap fields, but they are secondary and share a pool for the magnitude claim.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a research programme pointed at a scale that is not nature's threshold, and, in outreach, spending the independence of outside readers by sending a convention-dependent number as if it were a ground verdict. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The magnitude claim is one sentence; no primitive has to be withdrawn, and the shape claim needs no repair. The isomer and order readings in `06_isomers_and_order.md` survive independently of the refused magnitude.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A theorem forcing the multiplier `k` with no naturalness assumption; an instrument returning a breakdown scale fitted to `l_P` within a convention-independent factor; a demonstration that convention, breakdown and theory parameter are one condition; or a verbatim figure line naming a magnitude. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and the stop is unreached rather than unreachable.
