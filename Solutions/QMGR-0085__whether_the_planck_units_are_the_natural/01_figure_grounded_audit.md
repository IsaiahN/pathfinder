## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question decides between two ways of stating the scale at which a theory of quantum gravity becomes necessary.

- **Option A (Planck units are the natural scale).** The unique combination of `c`, `ħ` and `G` gives `l_P = sqrt(ħG/c^3)` about `1.6e-35 m`, `t_P` about `5.4e-44 s`, `m_P` about `2.2e-8 kg` (`E_P` about `1.22e19 GeV`). The standard case: the Compton wavelength and Schwarzschild radius cross there, both theories are needed there, and perturbative quantum gravity becomes non-renormalisable there. This leads to a definite number and a research programme aimed at that scale.
- **Option B (the scale is a shape, not yet a name).** The dimensional argument fixes the form but not the value: a dimensionless factor multiplies `l_P`, alternative complete unit systems exist from the same or other constants, the true fundamental scale may be TeV with large extra dimensions, and the string length is a free parameter. This leads to a withheld number, a stated refuter, and an instrument search.

**Residual R in effect terms:** the mismatch between a *convention-stable dimensional form* (three constants force one combination up to a factor) and a *ground-settled magnitude* (no instrument has returned a reading at that scale that fixes which combination nature uses). Arity: one magnitude, but a union of at least three conditions (unit convention, theory breakdown, candidate-theory parameter). Symmetry: the dimensional form is symmetric under renaming of units and insensitive to direction; the ground question is not. Scale: orders of magnitude beyond any direct measurement, about 25 orders below electron microscopy and 15 orders above LHC energies.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a claim of the form "the QG scale is `k * sqrt(ħG/c^3)` with `k` fixed by [instrument/theorem], mode stated." Until `k` is fixed, the sought-for shape stays a preference, not a term, and cannot be tested. This is the discipline Formula step 2 demands: write the shape down so it can be refused.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 Dimensional uniqueness is frame-internal, so it cannot be the verdict

Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The fact that `l_P` is the unique length from `c`, `ħ`, `G` is a product of the D1 frame's own dimensional rules. Using it to declare what nature does is a frame scoring itself. The dimensional argument narrows the *shape* of any answer that uses exactly those three constants; it does not establish that nature uses exactly those three constants. Mode `general` for the shape, `specified` for the refusal to name.

**Refuter:** exhibit a convention-independent, dimensionless constant `k` of order 1 forced by a theorem from the low-energy constants alone, with no naturalness assumption. Then the dimensional argument would close and this finding fails.

### F2 D1 has no anchor; the Planck number is not stable under a push

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30: "you push on it, it is not an anchor."

Push the convention: `h` instead of `ħ` gives a `2π` factor; `8πG` instead of `G` gives the reduced Planck mass about `2.4e18 GeV` rather than `1.22e19 GeV`; a Stoney or strong unit system built from the same or other constants gives a length up to twenty orders of magnitude away. A quantity that moves this much under a naming choice fails the anchor test. The world literature is explicit here: Jacobs (2025) notes alternative natural unit systems from `G, c, e` and from `ħ, e, m_e` and reports Wilczek's "strong units" with a length about twenty orders of magnitude larger than `l_P`.

**Refuter:** show that the convention can be fixed by a ground reading rather than by decree. Until then the number is a convention, not an anchor.

### F3 The question is constitutively about the ground, so the anchor is D2

Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The question "at what scale does gravity require quantum mechanics?" is about a physical threshold. Only a reading that changes when nature differs is admissible. Candidate readings already in the world: GRB arrival-time dispersion, gravitational-wave interferometer noise spectra, inverse-square-law deviations down to about `0.01 cm`, LHC searches for ADD Kaluza-Klein gravitons with `M_U > 2.6 TeV` at 95 percent confidence (Aydemir 2018; PDG 2025). None has returned a Planck-scale reading.

Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." This is not silence. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

**Refuter:** any of the instruments above returning a scale-dependent signal whose fitted scale is `l_P` within a stated, convention-independent factor would settle the magnitude at the ground and move this finding to `grounded`.

### F4 This is a union compound; splitting is required before search

Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57 describes the residue as "a specific compound." The "natural scale" bundles a unit-convention question (D1), a theory-breakdown question (D2), and a candidate-parameter question (D3). Dimensional analysis clears D1 and leaves D2 and D3. A search that does not split will mistake a convention result for a ground result.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the three conditions are one, for example that fixing the unit convention simultaneously fixes where current theory breaks and the value of the string coupling. No source read does this.

### F5 No mint is licensed at D3; gates fail as a product

Fig 5 :41: "Any one of them at zero stops the mint entirely." `THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 gates a mint on SUPPORT (`|R⁺_s| > 0`), REACHABILITY (`φ ∈ closure(Γ)`) and NOVELTY (`φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`), with the ground settling it at step 5. For a claimed new scale term:

- SUPPORT: a residual is present (theories fail at their contact), so this gate passes.
- REACHABILITY: `sqrt(ħG/c^3)` composes from terms already held, so the form is reachable.
- NOVELTY: the combination is not new content; it is a bond over held constants. This gate is the one that fails for a *new* scale claim.
- SETTLEMENT: `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5, "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." No ground reading at `1e-35 m`.

So the term is a legitimate **composition with a shape**, not a settled new primitive. Mode `specified`.

**Refuter:** a theory positing a genuinely new primitive that predicts a scale and is then confirmed by D2 would pass NOVELTY and SETTLEMENT and would overturn this finding.

### F6 The framework returns a shape, not a name

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 on reporting lack. The figures license the statement: *there is a threshold at which the comparison of the two theories becomes operative; its magnitude is owed to the ground.* They do not license "the natural scale is exactly `sqrt(ħG/c^3)`." Naming is a binding from the physics frame onto held terms, provenanced as such, and it is not an import that moves a wall, because the constants are already in the library. This matches the correction recorded in the exemplar's `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b: "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, but a value is still not a primitive, and a number is not licensed by composition.

**Refuter:** a figure line that names a magnitude, or a ground reading that fixes one. Neither was found. If a reader supplies the latter, this finding is settled rather than refuted, and the mode moves to `grounded`.

### F7 Agreement among sources is one pool, not a witness

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The world sources read here overlap on the same physics literature (Baez is cited by Jacobs; PDG and Aydemir cite the same ADD literature; Wikipedia cites Baez, Wilczek and Jacobs' topic). It is recorded as corroboration of the figure verdict only where the field is genuinely low-overlap (philosophy of physics versus phenomenology versus reference work), and never as a second witness for the magnitude. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

**Refuter:** a source from a disjoint field (for example a laboratory inverse-square-law result with no theory priors) returning a different magnitude would be a true second frame. It would be weighed in `09_watson_answer.md`, not pooled.

### F8 No last scale

Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room." Treating `l_P` as the final, terminal scale at which all questions stop is a "last room" reading. The figures forbid it structurally: there is no terminal frame and no bottom. So even a future ground reading fixing `k` would not make `l_P` a final answer; it would be one settled term in an open reach. This is a `general` structural claim, not a physics prediction.

**Refuter:** a closed, self-certifying theory of everything whose closure contains the QG scale and cannot be extended. Fig 8 forbids a frame certifying its own limit, so this refuter is expected to remain unfired.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The following were written before the verdict in §5 and before `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md`.

1. **Ground refuter.** A source-discriminating instrument returns a scale where current theory breaks, and the fitted scale equals `l_P` within a convention-independent stated factor. Then "unreached" is settled and the magnitude moves to `grounded`.
2. **Theorem refuter.** A theorem forced from the low-energy constants alone fixes the dimensionless multiplier `k` of order 1 without assuming naturalness. Then F1 fails and the dimensional argument closes.
3. **Census refuter.** A demonstration that convention, theory breakdown and theory parameter are one condition, so the union in F4 is actually a partition. Then unbundling is unnecessary and the compound verdict is wrong.
4. **Figure refuter.** A verbatim figure line that names a magnitude or that admits a terminal scale. Then F6 or F8 fails. Until one of these fires, the verdict below stands as `candidate`.

## 5. Conclusion (after the refuter above)

The figures decide the **status** of the claim, not the numerical physics. The claim "Planck units are the natural scale of quantum gravity" is refused as written because its only decisive support is frame-internal (F1), the number fails the anchor test (F2), the question is constitutively about a ground magnitude (F3), and the problem is an unbundled union (F4). What survives is a shape: a threshold where the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, with the magnitude owed to the ground and currently unreached (F6, F3). This is `specified`, ungrounded. The world literature independently reaches the same "not established" conclusion through low-overlap fields (Jacobs in philosophy of physics, Baez in physics exposition, PDG in phenomenology), but those are secondary corroboration only and share a pool for the magnitude claim.

Cost if wrong: a research programme aimed at a scale that is not nature's threshold. Undoable: yes, the claim is one sentence and no primitive was withdrawn. Reversed by: a ground reading, a closing theorem, or the figure refuter above. Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." The honest stop is **unreached, not unreachable**.
