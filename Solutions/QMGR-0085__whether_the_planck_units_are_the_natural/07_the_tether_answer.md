# The Tether's answer: are the Planck units the natural scale of quantum gravity?

**Mode: `specified` on the structural and epistemic claims, `general` on the shape. Not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Dimensional analysis gives a convention-stable form for a length from `c`, `ħ` and `G`, but no instrument has returned a reading at `1e-35 m` that fixes which combination nature uses, or even that current theory breaks there. The question bundles three conditions: unit convention, theory breakdown, and candidate-theory parameter. Fig 9 :54: "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." It is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground, not one hard problem.

## 2. The shape

1. **Dimensional uniqueness narrows the form, not the magnitude.** Fig 1 :27: "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." The fact that `l_P` is the unique length from three constants is a property of that frame's rules.
2. **The Planck number fails the anchor test.** Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :30: "you push on it, it is not an anchor." Change `h` to `ħ`, or `G` to `8πG`, or switch to a Stoney or strong system: the number moves, sometimes by twenty orders of magnitude.
3. **The question is constitutively about the ground.** Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it." The admissible anchor is a measurement. The existing proto-instruments (GRB timing, interferometer noise, inverse-square-law tests, LHC ADD searches) are already returning something that fails to resolve this scale.
4. **There is a threshold where the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, and its magnitude is not named by the framework.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides." The shape is a threshold built from held constants; the name is a ground claim.
5. **Alternative scales are live, not refuted.** Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." TeV-scale ADD with six extra dimensions, string length tied to a coupling, and reduced Planck conventions are all in the literature without a verdict from nature. Refusing them would be a filter handing a verdict.
6. **No terminal scale.** Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room." Even a future measurement fixing the multiplier would settle one term, not close the reach.
7. **The unit systems are isomers.** Fig 12 :117: "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Planck, Stoney, strong and reduced units are the same constants bonded differently (`06_isomers_and_order.md`).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that quantum gravity begins at `l_P`, nor that `l_P` is a shortest length, nor that the Planck mass is a particle mass. Those are ground claims and none has paid.
- Not that dimensional analysis proves a threshold exists. It narrows the *form* a threshold would have if the three constants are the right ones. That "if" is the unbundled part.
- Not a derivation of one theory from the other, and no new primitive. Isomer and functional-group readings are compositions over held terms (Fig 12).
- Not that "natural" is a property of nature rather than a visible choice. *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at the Planck scale was read and no instrument was run.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a colour instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the analogues are:

- **Discriminator test:** a phenomenon whose scale depends differently on `ħ`, `G` and the compactification volume would tell which unit system nature uses. Candidate readings already returning something are in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.
- **Convention-independence test:** a theorem forcing the dimensionless multiplier of `l_P` at order 1 with no naturalness assumption. Jacobs 2025 identifies Action-Universality as the hidden assumption the current strongest argument needs.
- **Union test:** a demonstration that convention, breakdown and theory parameter are one condition, which would dissolve the split.
- **Standing refuters from `01` §4:** instrument result, theorem, union collapse, or a verbatim figure line naming a magnitude.

Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." There are proto-instruments; none resolves the scale. So the stop is **unreached, not unreachable**. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

## 5. The honest stop

The constants chosen as scale-setting are "a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes" (*They're Scanning Books When They Should*). Declaring them makes the guess visible, not neutral. The answer stops where the available instruments stop discriminating, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity" (figures README). Agreement among the sources read here is one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40), so it is not a second witness for the magnitude.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that the Planck units are the natural scale of quantum gravity, and it does not claim a final scale. It says that dimensional uniqueness narrows the *form* of a threshold without fixing its magnitude: the Planck number moves under changes of convention (`h` vs `ħ`, `G` vs `8πG`, or a Stoney or strong unit system), so it fails the test of an anchor, which is something that settles a question without moving when a frame changes its mind. The question bundles three things that must be split: a unit convention, a physics breakdown, and a candidate-theory parameter. Dimensional analysis settles the first and leaves the other two owed. What survives is a shape: a threshold at which the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, with Planck, Stoney, strong and reduced units standing as isomers of one another, same constants bonded differently. The framework returns a shape, not a name. The name is owed to an instrument, and none yet returns a reading at that scale. Unreached is a verdict, not a silence.
