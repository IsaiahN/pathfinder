## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README gives the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

*The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its magnitude. It constrains; it does not fill. "There is a threshold at which the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, built from `c`, `ħ` and `G` up to a factor" is a shape. "`l_P = 1.616255e-35 m` is the natural scale" is a name. The first survives the figures; the second needs a ground reading.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary: use the words you already have that are closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: begin with "scale", "constant", "threshold", let the residual (dimensional form versus ground magnitude) add "anchor", "convention", "union compound".
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the essay-level source for the union split in `01` §1 and Fig 9 :54.
- Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So describe the residual as "a convention-stable form with no ground-fixed magnitude", not as "a failure of quantum gravity" (which already assumes the breakdown).
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Here that would be an instrument returning a scale-dependent signal and a fitted magnitude.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6, Fig 8). A TeV-scale ADD compactification radius, or a string length tied to a coupling, is an import from a candidate-theory frame. It is licensed only by provenance plus a ground reading, and at present it has the first but not the second.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The choice of `c`, `ħ`, `G` as *the* scale-setting constants is such a visible guess. Calling the resulting length "natural" hides the choice.

## 4. Why "natural" is the specific forbidden move here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A colour instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source"; the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Here the "reading" is dimensional uniqueness: several routes (`G,c,ħ`; `G,c,e`; `ħ,e,m_e`) yield complete natural unit systems with wildly different magnitudes ( Jacobs 2025, §4.1; Wilczek as cited there). Dimensional analysis cannot comment on which source nature uses. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. Fig 6 :34: "Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there." Candidate proto-instruments already returning something are listed in `04_links_and_distribution.md`; none has resolved `1e-35 m`.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any constant or value? ANSWERED with a correction

**No.** I initially overstated the rule. The figures README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library, and by extension a magnitude not fixed by the ground. The constants `c`, `ħ`, `G` are already in the physics library, so writing `sqrt(ħG/c^3)` is a binding over held terms, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is calling that binding a ground-settled magnitude, or claiming it moves a wall (Fig 6: "IMPORT adds an atom" - this adds none).

The exemplar records the same correction in its `05_what_shape_means.md` §5b and `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1. I keep both readings visible: the strict form (no name for a missing primitive) and the narrowed form (a held constant may be named, a ground magnitude may not). The verdict in this folder uses the narrowed form. Triangulated (figures README + Fig 9 :32 + Fig 6 import rule).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous physicists who agree that the Planck scale is natural is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to have the shape claim tried against an instrument.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
