## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Dimensional analysis is a heuristic, not a proof.** Jacobs §4.1 and Baez both state this in terms: the dimensionless multiplier can be very large or small, and alternative complete unit systems exist. This is the world-facing twin of Fig 1 :27, "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." It corroborates the figure verdict; it is not a second, independent witness for it, because both are answering the same argument from the same literature pool.
2. **Lower scales are live, not refuted.** ADD with `M_D` near a TeV and the PDG bounds are discussed without a verdict from nature. Fig 8 :39: "a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." A world source that posits a different scale is not a conflict to be smoothed; it is exactly the union compound the census required us to split.
3. **The naturalness assumption is the hidden hinge.** Jacobs identifies Action-Universality as the unproved assumption behind the strongest (nonrenormalisability) argument. This matches Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Naturalness is a filter on which numbers are worth writing down, not a witness for a magnitude.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a failure, not agreement. Record the verdict before showing any theory.

**Stage 2, opt-in only:** if the reader is curious, offer the three links (Jacobs, Baez, the figure extract). The essays and the framework interior come only after stage 1 is recorded, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a generator. Formula "Direction" requires the user to bind what they are looking for before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The output in `02_outreach_email.md` is the one to send. Do not add a claim that the Planck scale is settled; that claim fails F1, F2 and F6 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## Legal and practical note

Use the public submission and contact routes in `03_public_contact_points.csv` only. No personal emails, no scraped lists, no bulk send. FQxI's own FAQ says it does not review unsolicited theories, so routing a claim there as a review request would waste the channel. An abstract submission or an essay competition is a legitimate opt-in venue. Unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions, which is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
