## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reason is structural, not politeness. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A set of recipients selected for receptiveness is a mirror with many faces. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." A scale claim that would steer a research programme is costly if wrong, so independence is bought by asking for a refutation, never an endorsement.

## One-line answer recorded here

The figures do not name a number. They license a shape: there is a threshold at which the comparison of the two theories becomes operative, and a name for that threshold is owed to the ground, not to dimensional analysis. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."
