## 5. The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2:** Fig 10 :52, "Bring a characterised residual, never the source", and Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors; once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user still needs a ground per problem and must write their own refuter (Formula "Direction").
