## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 says the system "can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ECN-0091, the shape is the set of conditions a reliable prediction must satisfy:

> per-slot residual, invariant term, non-updating anchor, independent checking frames, a seat able to refuse, and no universal reliability percentage claimed.

"Economic theories can predict adaptive systems" is a name with no shape. "Reliability holds if and only if the composition uses terms invariant to the intervention and the anchor does not update on the forecast" is a shape. The one-paragraph answer in `07` is the shape made explicit.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Same essay: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly the union finding in `01`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as an effect, "forecast error rises after the policy is announced", not as a cause, "rational expectations defeat the model".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from the exemplar mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when "the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it".

For this question the stop is reached when the four domains stop returning new constraints and the only remaining demand is a number that no figure line holds. That is the `gap` in `01` F9.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The six are a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one as *the* invariant turns a visible choice into an invisible one unless the binding is recorded.

## 4. The metamerism error relevant here

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the green could be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

Applied here: an aggregate regularity (a stable Phillips curve, a stable money-demand relation) is a reading. Naming its source as a single "deep parameter" from that reading alone is the metamerism error. Several different individual-level structures can produce the same aggregate reading, and the reading does not determine which. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, namely an invariance test under a changed regime, not a name.

The same essay warns that a frame can make an unfalsifiable boundary absolute by construction. Chalmers's zombies are "made functionally and behaviorally identical, which forces every observable outcome to be the same by definition." In economics the analogue is a model made robust by assumption: if every adaptation is absorbed into a parameter, no observation can tell you the prediction failed. That is why the refuter in `02` and `08` must be stated before the run.

## 5. Why the universal percentage is the forbidden error

The question asks "can economic theories make reliable predictions" as if one number answered it. But:
- the residual is per slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.");
- the domains form a union with different anchors (`01` section 1);
- effect sizes and accuracy figures are filters for the budget, never witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict.");
- the framework returns a shape, not a name or a number (Fig 9 :32).

So a universal reliability percentage is not a modest approximation of the answer. It is the specific error the method forbids: an aggregation across slots and anchors that discards the only signal saying where the prediction breaks.

## 6. Correction to my own sections 3 and 4 (kept visible)

I initially overstated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming preferences, technology or constraints as candidate invariants. The README phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ.

Preferences, technology and resource constraints are **already available terms in the economics frame**. Naming one as a candidate invariant is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is:
- claiming the framework *derives* which parameter is invariant (it does not);
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom.");
- naming the source of an aggregate reading without a discriminating instrument (the metamerism point in section 4, **not** retracted).

Both the correction and the surviving metamerism point are recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the correction in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers authoritative economists to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, an intervention, a natural experiment, a measured slot, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
