## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction", the sought-for shape is bound before the run so it can accrue a residual like anything else. Here "reliable prediction" is bound as:

> For a stated object slot s and a stated intervention, a theory's prediction Γ(b,a) stays inside a declared residual band of the observed outcome o' after the agents subject to the policy have adapted, and the band does not widen when the policy rule or the published theory changes.

An answer must therefore name the slots, the band, the intervention, and the condition under which the band holds. It cannot be a universal percentage over all theories and all adaptive systems. That universal is the `gap` recorded in `07` and `09`.

## What is being decided, in effect terms

Option A: reliability is achievable because invariant individual-level parameters can be found and composed across regimes (the microfoundations programme). Option B: reliability is defeated because prediction changes the predicted object (reflexivity, the Lucas critique, Goodhart's law). Each leads somewhere distinct. A leads to a programme of invariance testing against interventions. B leads to withdrawal from prediction. The figures return neither A nor B as a global verdict; they return the **shape** a reliable prediction must have, keep the verdicts per domain separate, and mark the global yes/no as a gap.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence." Adding forecasters raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." A list ranked by likely agreement is a list selected to agree (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Asking for trust before a check spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

Legally: harvesting personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public institutional submission routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
