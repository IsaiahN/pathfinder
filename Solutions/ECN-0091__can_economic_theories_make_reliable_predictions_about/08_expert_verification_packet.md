# Expert verification packet: the Tether on prediction under adaptation

**What this is:** a claim in a form an economic methodologist, econometrician or philosopher of economics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the universal yes/no. Not `grounded`.** No intervention was run. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Can economic theories make reliable predictions about systems where people adapt to policies and expectations?" is a union of four overlapping conditions, not one question with a yes/no answer. Reliability is a per-slot, per-intervention property. It requires a residual that stays indexed to the slot, a term invariant to the intervention, an anchor that does not update on the forecast, independent checking frames and a seat able to refuse. Where agents read the policy and change their decision rules, the naive anchor updates and the forecast and outcome drift together coherently; the expectation feedback is a cycle that a chain model cannot close; and a metric turned into a policy target stops being a measure. The framework returns the shape such a prediction must have, six refuters written in advance and the instruments that would close each domain. It does not return a universal reliability percentage. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 9

- **Statement:** the question is a union of conditions with different anchors, and the residual must stay indexed per slot; a global reliability percentage is a shape-not-a-name gap, not an approximation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself".
- **Refuter:** a controlled intervention in which the aggregate relationship survives unchanged, every slot-level residual stays flat, and agents demonstrably observed the policy; or a figure re-census yielding a line that returns a universal reliability coefficient.

### C2: the anchor and invariance; checkable by an econometrician against identification practice

- **Statement:** reliability is conditional on a term that does not move with the policy; where agents adapt to the published forecast, the outcome is produced by a frame that already saw the prediction, so predictor and object mutually update.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :34 "If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does". Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."
- **What to check:** is the Lucas-style invariant (preferences, technology, constraints) actually invariant across the regime change you care about, or does it move at the same rate as the reduced-form coefficient? Is there any outcome not produced inside the feedback loop?
- **Refuter:** a proposed deep parameter that moves systematically with the policy regime at the same rate as the reduced-form coefficient, so no invariant is available; or an intervention where agents demonstrably did not observe the forecast and the residual still fails to shrink.

### C3: cycles and targets; checkable against reflexivity and targeting practice

- **Statement:** the expectation feedback is a cycle that a chain vocabulary cannot represent, and a target metric stops being a measure once it decides an outcome.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :121 "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one". Goodhart's law (opened source): "Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes." Campbell's law (opened source): "The more any quantitative social indicator is used for social decision-making, the more subject it will be to corruption pressures and the more apt it will be to distort and corrupt the social processes it is intended to monitor."
- **Refuter:** an adaptive system with zero feedback sign whose dynamics remain an exact chain under intervention; or a target regime in which the measured variable remains an unbiased sufficient statistic for the underlying outcome with no gaming.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that economics cannot predict. The claim is conditional and per slot.
- Not that reflexivity makes prediction impossible. It makes a chain model of a cycle impossible and demands a different anchor.
- Not that any named "deep parameter" has been shown invariant. That is a binding from the economics frame, `specified` and unmeasured.
- Not that a forecaster consensus is corroboration. It is efficient for a convention and worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :41).
- Not a universal reliability percentage, and not a derivation of one theory from another.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by an intervention.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Slot test:** does the aggregate relationship survive an announced policy while every slot-level residual stays flat?
2. **Invariance test:** under a changed regime, does the proposed deep parameter move with the policy as much as the reduced-form coefficient?
3. **Target test:** does the metric remain an unbiased sufficient statistic after becoming a target, with no gaming?
4. **Independence test:** do two low-overlap frames against the same non-updating anchor agree more reliably than one shared pool?
5. The six pre-registered refuters in `01` section 4.
