## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about economic prediction when people adapt to policies and expectations. Option A: yes, reliability is achievable because invariant individual-level parameters can be found and composed across regimes. Option B: no, reliability is defeated because prediction changes the predicted object. What each leads to: A leads to a programme of invariance testing against interventions; B leads to withdrawal from prediction. The figure-derived answer is neither global verdict. The question is a union of four conditions with different anchors; the figures return the shape a reliable prediction must have, keep per-domain verdicts separate and mark the universal yes/no as a `gap`.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** `R = |Γ(b,a) − o'|` per slot s. Arity: at least three frames in contact, the forecaster, the adapting agents and the measurement frame, plus a ground that settles the prediction. Symmetry: the policy-to-outcome map is ordered, but the adaptation is a cycle in which output re-enters as input (Fig 12 :121), so a chain vocabulary misstates it. Scale: individual slot, population aggregate, policy regime, with a membrane between (Fig 4).
- **Domain 1, the model frame.** Actors: the model, reduced-form coefficients, deep parameters, the policy rule. Currency: residual R and description length. Anchor: a measured outcome that does not move when the model changes its story. Union condition: invariance to intervention.
- **Domain 2, the adapting agents.** Actors: people, firms, investors, their expectations and incentives. Currency: payoff and action. Anchor: their own constraints, though they update on policy and on published theory. Union condition: feedback closure.
- **Domain 3, the policy / seat frame.** Actors: the policy maker, the rule, the target metric. Currency: welfare and outcome. Anchor: the ground, unless the metric becomes a target. Union condition: convention collapse.
- **Dependency order.** Refuse a held but false regularity before minting (Formula step 2); read the residual per slot before aggregating (Fig 1 :19); settle invariants before planning the intervention (Fig 3 :19); describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union of four conditions, and the verdicts are kept separate. Reliability is conditional, per slot and per intervention: it holds where a non-updating invariant is found and composed, and fails where the forecast is fed back into the outcome, where the metric becomes a target, or where the frames checking the prediction share one evidence pool. The expectation feedback is a cycle that a chain model cannot represent; settled individual parameters become atoms for the policy model but settlement is not sufficient, an open measurement strain. The universal yes/no over all economic theories is a declared `gap`: the framework returns a shape, not a reliability number. Mode `specified` on domains, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`. Strongest single figure-grounded point: an anchor that updates on the forecast is not an anchor (Fig 2 :33-35), so the first question for any claimed reliable prediction is what does not move when the policy changes.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, shape and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a verdict instead of a generator, priced at Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.", and avoidable by leading with the shape and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. Each domain verdict can be revised on its own evidence; the six refuters can fire independently; no primitive has to be withdrawn and a sent claim can be superseded by the revised packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A per-slot invariance test, a demonstrable non-updating anchor, a target-regime measurement or a low-overlap independence test would move a domain to `grounded`; a demonstration that a settled parameter composes across regimes with no re-estimation would clear the live strain; a figure re-census returning a universal reliability coefficient would remove the `gap`. Prestige and consensus are not such instruments.
