## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*, and :135 *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- `HEAT DEATH(???)`, `BIG RIP(???)`, `BIG CRUNCH(???)` as a union of conditions is a shape.
- "`w = -1.3`, so the Big Rip at roughly 22 billion years" is a name, and it is a name the figures do not license.

The honest output of this folder is the shape: a union, a ground that decides, a spendable-difference account of heat death, and a refusal of the final-room framing.

## 4. Why naming the winning scenario is the forbidden move here

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, carried, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. Several sources can produce one reading, so *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it.

Applied here: an accelerating expansion is a reading. Phantom energy, a cosmological constant, and modified gravity can produce readings that look alike at some resolutions. Naming the terminal scenario from the reading without a discriminator is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument, not a preferred name. The DESI preference for evolving dark energy is precisely a discriminator-in-progress, not a settled source.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So this folder's own shape claim cannot validate itself against the cosmology it describes.

## 5. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially wrote the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any of the three scenarios or any of the six terms. That is too broad. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. It governs a primitive not held in `Γ`. Gradient, spectrum and the other five terms are already in the six, so naming one as the structural description of a scenario is a **binding** from the physics frame onto a held atom, provenanced as such. It is not the invention of a missing primitive and it does not move the wall (Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*).

What remains wrong in the naive reading is the metamerism point: naming the source of a reading (acceleration → phantom energy → Big Rip) without an instrument that discriminates among sources. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost.
