## The question restated as an effect

Which of several terminal states does the universe reach, and what would settle which one is actual? The three named options lead to different things:

| option | what it leads to | what would have to be true |
|---|---|---|
| heat death | thermodynamic equilibrium, no free energy left to spend, no further work | expansion continues, gradients run down, no remainder re-enters |
| Big Rip | scale factor diverges in finite time, all bound structures disrupted | phantom dark energy with `w < -1` persists |
| Big Crunch | recollapse to a hot dense state | sufficient matter plus geometry or dynamics that reverse expansion |

An answer to this question would be a claim about which region of parameter space the universe occupies, settled by measurement. The figures do not return that claim. They return the shape any such answer must have and the kind of instrument that would close it.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to accept the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

The figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*; Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting correspondents for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless, and asking them to trust the framework before they check the residual spends the only independence they contribute.
