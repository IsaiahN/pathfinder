# Expert verification packet: the Tether on the fate of the universe

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist or philosopher of physics can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the metatheoretical rules, `gap` on which scenario is actual. Not `grounded`.** No cosmological measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The three named fates form a union of overlapping conditions, not one question with three answers, and must be unbundled before search. The question is constitutively about a ground, and that ground is observation of geometry, matter and the dark-energy equation of state. Heat death has a structural signature: the exhaustion of differences that can be spent, so no residual re-enters the loop. The three scenarios are different arrangements over a shared small term set, with a genuine isomer claim when all are written over the same atom set. The framework returns none of `w`, `Ω`, or a timescale, and it refuses the framing of a single terminal answer that ends inquiry. What it offers is a shape, four refuters written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the question.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : union and ground (framework-internal, checkable by reading Fig 9 and Fig 2)

- **Statement:** the three scenarios are a union requiring unbundling, and the question is legitimately about an observational ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :5 *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 2 :36 *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*
- **What to check:** are the three scenarios actually different conditions, or is one parameter regime enough to generate all three? If the latter, the union claim fails.
- **Refuter:** a single mechanism with one parameter regime that generates all three and leaves no intersection standing; or a demonstration that no measurement could settle which regime is occupied.

### C2 : heat death as gradient exhaustion (physics-facing, checkable against thermodynamics)

- **Statement:** heat death is structurally the composition in which no spendable directed difference remains, so no residual re-enters the loop. The figures make this a substrate claim, not a field-theory claim.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :22 *"a difference that can be spent"*; Fig 13 :46-47 *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."*; Fig 13 :71 *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"*. Independent corroboration: the heat-death reference defines the state as one of *"no thermodynamic free energy"* in which the universe is *"unable to sustain any further thermodynamic processes"*.
- **What to check:** is the structural reading faithful to the thermodynamics, given the recorded objections that entropy may not be definable for the universe as a whole? Does any far-future state retain spendable directed differences?
- **Refuter:** a system at thermodynamic equilibrium with spendable directed differences that re-enter a loop and produce further work.

### C3 : different arrangements and the unreachable name (framework-internal derivation)

- **Statement:** over the full shared atom set the three scenarios are isomers; as the literature writes them they are a functional group with different qualifiers. Which regime is occupied is unreachable from the figure closure and must be imported from observation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117 *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."*; Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*; Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **What to check:** can all three be written over the same atom set with the difference carried by the bond? Is the measured equation of state an import with provenance, or is a figure line being made to do physics work it cannot do?
- **Refuter:** a scalar multiplier alone converts one scenario into another (kills the isomer claim); or a derivation from the six terms forces a unique scenario without importing a measurement (kills the `gap`).

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminator among the sources of a reading, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Regime Test:** read CMB, BAO and supernova frames separately, then compare the supported region of `w(z)` and curvature. DESI 2024 VI is the current instance: BAO alone gives `w = -0.99 (+0.15, -0.13)` while CMB and supernova combinations prefer evolution at a few sigma. That narrows; it does not decide.
2. **Source Test:** an instrument that distinguishes phantom energy from a cosmological constant from modified gravity where their expansion readings coincide.
3. **Thermodynamic Test:** does the far-future state retain spendable directed differences that re-enter a loop? A positive answer breaks C2.
