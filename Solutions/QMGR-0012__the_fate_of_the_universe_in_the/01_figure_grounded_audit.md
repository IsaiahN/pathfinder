## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The question names three terminal scenarios. It does not name the quantity that decides among them, and it does not say what would count as a settlement. Stated as an effect:

> There are several mutually exclusive descriptions of the universe's terminal state. We want to know which one is actual, and what would have to be observed or derived for that verdict to be more than a preference.

Each option leads somewhere different:

- **Heat death** leads to thermodynamic equilibrium with no free energy available to do work. The mechanism is the running down of differences that can be spent.
- **Big Rip** leads to a finite-time divergence of the scale factor under persistent phantom dark energy (`w < -1`), disrupting bound structures.
- **Big Crunch** leads to recollapse into a hot dense state if the expansion reverses.

### R by arity, symmetry and scale

- **Arity:** at least three conditions, not two. The wider literature also lists Big Bounce, Big Slurp and false-vacuum decay, so three is a working minimum, not a closed enumeration.
- **Symmetry:** the three are not interchangeable. They are not related by exchanging operands under a symmetric bond; each is a different mechanism in a different region of parameter space. Their names are labels for regimes, not variants of one state.
- **Scale:** cosmological, one universe, one terminal history. There is no second instance to compare and no experiment that reruns the universe.

**The residual is not one hard question with three answers.** Fig 9 :4: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort"* and Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Applying that test: the three scenarios do not disagree about one measurement; they disagree about which region of parameter space is occupied, and they keep working well on different subsets of the evidence. This is a union compound.

**Union.** The three scenarios are overlapping conditions, not a partition of one question. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces"*. The order here is:

## 3. Findings

### F1 : The three scenarios are a union, not one question (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :4: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort"* and Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

Each scenario keeps working on a different subset of the evidence: phantom-energy models on supernova distances, recollapse models on a hypothetical closed geometry, thermodynamic arguments on the long-time behaviour of gradients. One lever does not clear the intersections. The Library of Babel essay independently states the same shape for a malformed question: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

**Refuter:** a demonstration that one mechanism with one parameter regime generates all three scenarios as mere value settings of a single well-formed question, so that no unbundling is owed. Until then the union reading stands. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 plus the independent-shape claim in the essay, with the essay sharing the framework author's pool, so this is weak corroboration, not an independent witness).

### F2 : The question is legitimately about a ground, and the ground is observation (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

Cosmological fate is constitutively about measurements of geometry, matter content and the dark-energy equation of state. The anchor is legitimate because the question is about it, and it does not move when a scenario is preferred. NASA's WMAP science page puts the physical side plainly: *"These measurements reveal the size, matter content, age, geometry and fate of the universe."* This is not a frame negotiating with the universe; it is instruments ranging against a ground.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the terminal state is constitutively a matter of convention or preference, with no measurement that could settle it. **Status:** triangulated across two figures with different functions (Fig 2's anchor theory and Fig 9's ground-decides rule) and corroborated by an independent world source.

### F3 : Which scenario is actual is unreachable from the figure closure (`gap`, single-source on the figure side)

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

The six-term set has no term for `w`, for curvature, or for the observed acceleration. Arranging those six terms forever cannot produce the measured value of the dark-energy equation of state. That value must be imported from the observation frame, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52). Searching the figure closure for the answer is spending budget on a region that does not contain it.

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. So the honest report is `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind, not an omission and not an invitation to invent a scenario.

**Refuter:** a derivation from the six terms alone that forces a unique terminal state without importing a measurement. **Status:** single-source on the figure side (Fig 6 plus Fig 8, same framework). Independent sources confirm that the physics answer is measurement-driven, but they cannot corroborate a figure-internal reachability claim.

### F4 : Heat death is structurally the exhaustion of spendable gradients (`specified`, triangulated)

Fig 13 :22: *"a difference that can be spent"*. Fig 13 :46-47: *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."* Fig 13 :71: *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"*.

Heat death is the terminal composition in which no gradient remains that can be spent, so no residual re-enters the loop. This is not a metaphor imported from physics: the figures name the spendable difference as a primitive structure, and they make the thermodynamic laws a test of the set. Fig 13 :49: *"Read the established laws of thermodynamics as a test rather than a scorecard."*

The independent thermodynamics literature states the physical version: *"the universe will evolve to a state of no thermodynamic free energy and, having reached maximum entropy, will therefore be unable to sustain any further thermodynamic processes"* (Wikipedia, Heat death of the universe, 2026). That is the same shape arrived at in a different field, so this finding has genuine low-overlap corroboration.

**Refuter:** a case in which a system at thermodynamic equilibrium still has spendable directed differences that re-enter a loop and produce further work. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 13 gradient and thermodynamic-table lines plus an independent thermodynamics source).

### F5 : Big Rip and Big Crunch are different bond regimes, not variants of heat death (`specified`, single-source)

Fig 13 :76: *"A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."* Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

Under the substrate, the three scenarios are different arrangements of the same small set of terms, not different amounts of one quantity. Heat death spends the gradient until none remains. Big Rip is the regime in which the expansion-driving term diverges, so a comparison against a threshold (the `⋛` bond) is crossed and the composed structures come apart. Big Crunch is the regime in which the spatial and energetic terms re-compose toward a contracted state. The figures license the **shape** (different arrangement, different substance); they do not license the physics that decides which regime is occupied.

**Refuter:** a demonstration that the three scenarios compose identically over the substrate and differ only in a scalar multiplier, so the bond carries no information. **Status:** single-source on the figure side (Fig 12 and Fig 13, one framework), with the physics of the regimes supplied by world sources, not by the figures.

### F6 : The "one final fate" framing hits the no-last-room limit, but only in D3 (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*. Fig 7 :30-31: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"*. Fig 13 :90: *"One chain is one route through a space that has no last route."*

A demand for the single terminal answer that ends all further inquiry is a terminal room, and the framework rules terminal rooms out. This governs the **framing** in D3, not the physical question in D1. Saying that cosmology cannot have a settled fate would be a category error, and it is not what the figure says. The figure says there is no last room in the chain of explanation and prediction, which is why even a measured fate opens further questions rather than closing inquiry.

**Refuter:** a demonstrated last room, that is, a frame after which no further residual can arrive. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 7 and Fig 13, two figures with different functions).

### F7 : The ground decides, and sigma is a budget filter, not a witness (`general`, triangulated)

Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*. Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

A confidence level on a dark-energy parameter narrows the space; it does not decide which scenario is true. The decision belongs to measurements that do not update on the scenario we prefer. The cost of being wrong here is high enough to buy independence: read the CMB, BAO and supernova frames separately, not as one pooled consensus, because agreement that shares an evidence pool is one witness talking (Fig 2 :31-32 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."*).

**Refuter:** a case in which a statistical confidence level, with no ground reading, settles which scenario is actual. **Status:** triangulated (Fig 9 and Fig 2).

### F8 : Describe the residual before choosing a frame (`general`, single-source)

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*.

If the residual is described as "how does the universe end?", any of the three scenarios looks like an answer. If it is described as "which region of `w` and geometry is occupied, and what instrument discriminates among the regimes?", only the measurement frame fits. The residual description, not the preferred scenario, selects the frame.

**Refuter:** a case where frame selection is independent of how the residual is described. **Status:** single-source (Fig 9), corroborated by the Library of Babel essay's proximity-and-vocabulary rule, same author pool.

## 4. Strain test and re-census

Two apparent strains were examined rather than smoothed over.

**S1, Fig 7 versus D1.** Applied directly, the no-last-room rule would say cosmology has no answer about its fate. That strains the rule, because the physical question has a legitimate ground. Re-census: Fig 7 governs rooms in a chain of explanation and prediction (D3); D1 is a measurement question with a non-updating anchor. Different objects, so no contradiction. The strain is preserved here as a record of the re-census, not erased. This is the reason D3 was kept separate in section 1.

**S2, "gradient" as physics term versus gradient as substrate atom.** The physics literature uses gradient for spatial variation of a field; the figures use it for a spendable directed difference. Re-census: the substrate atom is the broader, mechanism-level object (Fig 13 :46-47), and a field gradient is one instance of it. The heat-death reading uses the atom, not the field instance, so no strain after re-census. The distinction is recorded because a reader could otherwise think the figure is making a field-theory claim.

**After re-census: two strains dissolved, none hidden, none smoothed into a false agreement.**
