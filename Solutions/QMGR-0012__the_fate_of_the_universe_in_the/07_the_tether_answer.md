# The Tether's answer on the fate of the universe

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claims, `general` on the metatheoretical rules, `gap` on which terminal scenario is actual. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles at least three terminal descriptions of the universe: heat death, Big Rip and Big Crunch. They do not disagree about one measurement. They occupy different regions of a parameter space described by curvature, matter content and the dark-energy equation of state, and each keeps working on a different subset of the evidence. Fig 9 :5: *"are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The residual is a **union compound** with its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Unbundle before searching.** One lever does not clear the bundle: Phantom-energy conditions, recollapse conditions and thermodynamic arguments clear different subsets and leave intersections standing (Fig 9 :54-55). Per-scenario verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
2. **The question is legitimately about a ground.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The anchor is observation of the CMB, baryon acoustic oscillations and supernova distances. It does not move when a scenario is preferred.
3. **Heat death has a distinctive structural signature.** A gradient is *"a difference that can be spent"* (Fig 13 :22). Heat death is the composition in which none remain, so no remainder re-enters the loop: *"no direction to drive the flow, and no remainder left after each step"* (Fig 13 :71). This is supported by an independent thermodynamics source, so it is triangulated rather than figure-only.
4. **The scenarios are different arrangements, not different amounts.** Fig 12 :117: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Over the full shared atom set they are a genuine isomer claim; as the literature actually writes them they are a functional group with different qualifiers (Fig 12 :124). The stronger claim is the one worth testing.
5. **Which regime is occupied is a `gap`.** The six-term set has no term for `w`, curvature or the observed acceleration. Arranging six terms forever cannot produce the measured equation of state; it must be imported from the observation frame with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*. This is unreachable-at-this-budget, not underivable in principle, and not an omission.
6. **A single terminal answer that ends inquiry is a terminal room.** Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*. This governs the framing of a final explanation, not the physical measurement of fate. Even a measured fate opens further questions.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a prediction of heat death, Big Rip, or Big Crunch, and not a date for any of them.
- Not a value of `w`, `Ω`, or `H(z)`. No figure line governs those.
- Not that the no-last-room rule denies cosmology a physical answer. It governs the explanatory chain, not the ground.
- Not a unified theory of the three scenarios, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No cosmological measurement was taken in this derivation.
- Not that agreement among people who prefer a scenario is evidence for it.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A discriminator among the sources of a reading, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Regime Test:** read CMB, BAO and supernova frames **separately**, then compare the supported region of `w(z)` and curvature. DESI 2024 VI already shows why this matters: BAO alone gives `w = -0.99 (+0.15, -0.13)`, while combinations with CMB and supernovae prefer an evolving equation of state at 2.5 to 3.9 sigma. That is a narrowing, not a verdict (Fig 9 :42 *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*).
- **Source Test:** an instrument that distinguishes phantom energy from a cosmological constant from modified gravity where their expansion readings coincide. Until such a discriminator, naming the source of the acceleration is the metamerism error.
- **Thermodynamic Test:** does the far-future state retain spendable directed differences that re-enter a loop? A positive answer breaks the heat-death structural reading; the standing objections about whether entropy is definable for the universe are recorded rather than dismissed.
- **Standing falsifiers from the audit:** derive a scenario from the six terms alone; show one mechanism generates all three; show equilibrium retains spendable differences; demonstrate a last room.

## 5. The honest stop

**Reached for the shape, unreached for the name.** The figures return the union, the ground, the spendable-difference account, the bond comparison and the refusal of a final room. They stop where the available instruments stop distinguishing among acceleration sources, and they record the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The consistent answer is that the question is a real one with a real ground, and that this framework does not own that ground.
