## Stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the residual, the shape claim and the four refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict **before** any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three local links. The figures, not the summary, are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

A stage 2 agreement is never pooled as corroboration. By Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*, a reader who has absorbed the framework before answering has joined the frame being checked.
