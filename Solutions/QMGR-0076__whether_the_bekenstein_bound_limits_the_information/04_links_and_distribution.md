## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **The bound is conditional, not a bare region fact.** Bekenstein's own abstract (2005) says *"complete weakly self-gravitating physical system"* and names both circumscribing radius and total gravitating energy. A question that asks about *any region of space* has silently dropped the energy condition. Cite this before citing any number.
2. **There are two grounds, and they are not the same.** Casini 2008 gives a theorem in flat space; Bousso 2002 gives strong evidence from a wide range of examples. Neither is a direct measurement of a finite region's capacity. The holographic-principle record also notes unresolved classical counterexamples (*"Wheeler's bags of gold"*). So the unconditional reading stays `candidate`.
3. **The framework has already declared this a retrodiction.** The architecture article's bibliography section *"The retrodicted cases behind triangulation"* lists this exact case as *"An instance"*, and Fig 8 :56-57 says the claim *"fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward"*. Do not present black hole entropy as something the Tether produced.
