## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of a limiting bound that can be stated before the particular inequality is chosen. It constrains: an ordered threshold, an ordering of states, a terminating count, an energy-and-region budget. It does not fill: it does not say `2*pi`, `1/4`, Bekenstein versus holographic versus covariant.

`S <= 2*pi*k*R*E/(hbar*c)` is a name. *A threshold between an entropy ordering and an energy-in-a-region budget whose count terminates* is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* on proximity: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, track recurring new terms that correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* So describe the effect (a finite count under a finite energy budget) before borrowing the word "Bekenstein".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* That is why the first contact in `02_outreach_email.md` contains the residual and the refuter, not the theory.
- *Library of Babel* on the compound: if the final search does not answer the question, one possibility is that the problem was *"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one"*. Fig 9 :54 gives the same instruction: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*

## 3. What licenses a name here

Two things, both outside the shape itself.

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For this question, a direct measurement selecting one inequality, or an accepted theorem with an unambiguous scope, would name the bound. Casini 2008 supplies a theorem in flat space; no direct measurement of a finite region's capacity exists. The unconditional reading stays `candidate`.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming Bekenstein's inequality is exactly this: a physics result imported into the framework's vocabulary, dated 1981, with the source recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."* The six-term set is a visible guess. It is not nature's own list of information-bearing quantities.

## 4. The source-discrimination point applies directly

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK. The essay's general statement is: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*. It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

The same structure appears here. Several distinct physical setups (a weakly gravitating box, a near-collapse star, a black hole horizon) can present the same kind of reading: a finite entropy in a finite region. The reading does not determine which bound is doing the work. Naming one source from the projection is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator of scopes, not a name chosen by argument.

## 5. The decisive provenance correction

An earlier draft of this solution treated the Tether as if it had produced the Bekenstein bound. That reading is **retracted**. The canonical article's bibliography has a section titled *"The retrodicted cases behind triangulation"*, and the Bekenstein, Hawking, 't Hooft and Susskind entry is labelled *"An instance."* Fig 8 :56-57 says: *"the triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward: Wiles and Frey, Montgomery and Dyson, Bekenstein and holography, the relativization barriers in complexity theory."*

So the framework describes an instance that was arrived at independently. This is not a weak corroboration to be hidden; it is the honest status of the claim. What survives is a retrodicted shape plus a provenance rule, not a derivation.

Narrowing note kept alongside the retraction: the README's *"shape, not a name"* governs a **missing** primitive or an undetermined coefficient. It does not forbid reporting a published physical result. The forbidden move is presenting that result as if the framework derived or selected it. Both claims are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for the answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of famous names agreeing that the bound is fundamental is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And from the architecture article: LLM outputs are *"simulacra"*. So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. What shape means, in one line for this question

A shape is the ordered-threshold form any finite information capacity must take, with a terminating count and an explicit energy-and-region condition, stated before choosing among the Bekenstein inequality, the covariant bound and the area law, and recorded with the provenance that the framework arrived at this as a known instance rather than a derivation.
