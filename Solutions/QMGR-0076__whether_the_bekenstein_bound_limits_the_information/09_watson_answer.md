## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the unconditional reading and the framework-derivation claim do not. A finite information capacity is possible because finiteness of energy in a finite region terminates the count (Fig 13 :68); any such bound is an ordered threshold between an entropy ordering and an energy-and-region budget (Fig 12 :87, Fig 13 :20); it recurs at every scale (Fig 12 :103); and the question is a union of a fixed-energy bound and a collapse-limited area bound (Fig 9 :54). The framework does not name the inequality, fix `2*pi` or `1/4`, or claim it derived the result: its own record calls Bekenstein and holography an already-known case not tested forward (Fig 8 :56-57). Status: `general` structural, `specified` physics reading, `candidate`, ungrounded.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states the union split, the shape and the refuters before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is presenting a conditional physics result as an unconditional framework derivation, which credits the framework with a retrodiction and spends the independence of outside readers. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The unconditional reading is removed without touching the shape; the coefficient and inequality are simply not asserted. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The retraction log in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` keeps the earlier reading visible.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An experiment measuring a finite region's capacity, or an accepted theorem fixing the coefficient and scope for all regions, would select the inequality and move the physics side to `grounded`; a finite-energy system with infinitely many distinguishable states not rescued by interactions would break Fig 13 :68; a symmetric or conjunctive comparator would break Fig 12 :87; a figure re-census showing the six-term set does not govern information capacity would yield an honest `gap`; and a dated forward derivation of a numerical bound from the framework would reverse the retrodiction finding. The live strain, theorem-ground versus experiment-ground, is carried into `11_audit.md`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
