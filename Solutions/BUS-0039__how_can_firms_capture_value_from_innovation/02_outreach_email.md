> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about appropriability and value capture. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** knowledge spreads, so the innovation document is not the defensible part of an innovation. The defensible part is the arrangement around it: manufacturing, distribution, service, brand, data, cumulative learning, and the record of who originated what. I am calling that the bond, and the claim is that two firms holding the same knowledge atoms but different bonds are different substances at the market, not variants of one thing. Value capture also looks like a chain of successive innovations rather than one protected moment, because each settled innovation becomes the starting point for the next. Whether this is true in your setting is exactly what I would like you to check.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that patents are worthless, that secrecy is pointless, or that a lead time is not real. They are tools. The claim is that a patent grant is an instrument that buys time and creates a record, not the thing that settles whether value was captured; and that secrecy exchanges one habitat for another rather than removing competitors. I am not claiming this framework is the only account of value capture or a final theory. No measurement here is `grounded`.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. Full disclosure with no complementary asset, no record, no legal right and no network position still yields durable private returns across repeated innovations, measured at the market.
> 2. An originator can permanently remove imitators from a habitat without substituting another habitat, with the market still paying for the same innovation.
> 3. One innovation keeps paying for decades with no successor and no new complement, while both the fidelity of the firm's reading of the market and the market's pressure are independently shown to be irrelevant.
> 4. A patent grant is immovable under push and predicts realized returns across innovations by itself.
>

## Constraint on submission

It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy.
