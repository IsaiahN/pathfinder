## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the per-innovation gap between what the firm's library predicts it will receive and what the market actually returns. Arity: three place relation (innovation, action, return) indexed by slot and time. Symmetry: asymmetric; originator and imitator are not interchangeable, and "capture" is a threshold against competitors, not an absolute quantity. Scale: first order inside one innovation, union across conditions, second order along a chain of innovations. Question type: **union compound**, not one hard problem.
- **Domain 1, knowledge and closure.** Actors: knowledge atoms, imitators. Currency: composition inside closures. Anchor: market payment. Question type: idempotence of closure; exclusion asymmetry.
- **Domain 2, arrangement and complementary assets.** Actors: firm, manufacturing, distribution, brand, service, data, learning, complementors. Currency: the bond, priced in actions. Anchor: market settlement. Question type: composition, bond versus ingredient.
- **Domain 3, legal and institutional.** Actors: law, courts, registries, standards. Currency: rights and time. Anchor: partial and moving, so instrument rather than ground. Question type: moving anchor, provenance.
- **Domain 4, market ground.** Actors: customers, entrants. Currency: payment. Anchor: realized return per innovation. Question type: settlement.
- **Domain 5, chain across innovations.** Actors: successive innovations and followers. Currency: progress per handoff. Anchor: fidelity and ground pressure. Question type: sequence.
- **Excluded:** knowledge as self-contained moat (no independent anchor), grant count as proof (frame-internal quantity, moves when pushed).
- **Order by dependency:** perceive returns and their recipients, build vocabulary for what the innovation affords, state what counts as capture, plan, then carry the method. Refuse the knowledge-as-moat term before minting.

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 12 (bond versus ingredient) and Fig 6 (closure idempotence), I derived from Fig 9's union and partition split and Fig 11's habitat asymmetry. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries. That is low overlap between **routes**, not between evidence pools, so the agreement is weak internal consistency. It is still one pool.
