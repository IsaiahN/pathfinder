# The Tether's answer on value capture from innovation

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the four-condition structure. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

An innovation creates value for customers, imitators, suppliers and complementors, while the originator's predicted private return Γ(b,a) exceeds the realized return o′. The gap is indexed per innovation and per time, never averaged across a portfolio (Fig 1 :19, :22). It is a **union compound**, not one hard problem: four conditions overlap and a single lever clears only the region where one holds (Fig 9 :54-57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The knowledge item is not the moat.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :47: *"recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."* The document transfers; the arrangement does not.
2. **The defensible part is a bond over held atoms.** Manufacturing, distribution, service, brand, data and cumulative learning are the bonds that make the same knowledge atoms a different substance at the market (Fig 12 :117). Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
3. **Imitation is closure idempotence, not a moral failure.** Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 11 :61: *"The seat’s power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not."* You can add a complement; you cannot subtract a competitor from the habitat.
4. **Legal IP buys time and a record; it does not settle the question.** Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."* The grant is instrument C3, not the verdict.
5. **The dated record is the provenance asset.** Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures"* *"differ."* Where an imitation and an independent invention look the same, only the record says which closure produced the artefact.
6. **Capture persists as a chain of differing rooms.** Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."* Fig 12 :104: *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* Each settled innovation is the atom for the next; no new primitive is required.
7. **The four conditions are a union, kept separate.** C1 a non-copyable bond, C2 maintained contact across handoffs, C3 a time-and-record instrument, C4 an enumerated habitat. They are not a menu and are never averaged into a score. A firm failing only C3 is not a weaker version of a firm failing only C1.
8. **Two currencies stay apart.** Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Bits price the disclosure; actions price the arrangement. Summing them is the error the figure names.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that patents are useless. They are C3: a real but moving instrument.
- Not that secrecy is pointless. It is a habitat substitution with costs invisible until they act (Fig 11 :66-70).
- Not a mechanism name for a specific industry. The framework returns *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a `grounded` result. No market measurement at the level of a named innovation is claimed here.
- Not a theory that selects the winner among patents, secrecy, lead time and complementary assets. James, Leiblein and Lu 2013 report that the field still lacks a robust theory of that selection; this solution does not pretend to supply one.
- Not a means of excluding imitators permanently. Exclusion is not available as a subtractive operation (Fig 11 :61).

## 4. What would settle it  -  the ground

A market measurement per innovation, not agreement. Fig 2 :29 names the anchor: *"A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* The corresponding questions are:

- Does the private return to the originator fall when the knowledge is disclosed while the complement is held, or only when the complement is also copied? That discriminates C1 from C2.
- Does a grant that is immovable under push predict realized returns better than a grant that can be invalidated? That discriminates C3 from the rest.
- Does an imitator's effect on the originator change with the surrounding habitat (alternatives, complementors, standards), as the low-overlap mimicry model predicts? That tests C4.
- Standing falsifiers from `01` section 4: full disclosure with no complement and no record still paying; permanent exclusion of imitators without habitat substitution; one room paying forever; a patent grant as an immovable anchor; provenance adding nothing; a single lever restoring all four conditions.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The conditions are drawn from a business frame that did not see the figures, and the figures are applied to it as a metalaw, not as a measurement. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The answer stops at the shape, with nine falsifiers written in advance, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Agreement from a strategy audience is not such an instrument (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say that knowledge can be locked up, and it does not name a winning appropriability mechanism. It says that the innovation document is only half the information, so the defensible part is the arrangement around it, that imitators compose over leaked knowledge by the ordinary idempotence of closure, and that a firm can add to a habitat but cannot subtract competitors from it. A patent buys time and creates a record; it is an instrument, not the market's verdict. Value capture persists as a chain of differing innovations, each settled one becoming the atom for the next, and it depends on four overlapping conditions held separate: a non-copyable bond, maintained contact with a paying ground, a time-and-record instrument, and an enumerated habitat. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, nine falsifiers written in advance, and the measurements that would close it. The winner stays open until the ground settles it.
