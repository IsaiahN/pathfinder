## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*.

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name its winner. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "Hold something the imitator cannot reproduce from the same knowledge" is a shape.
- "Build a patent thicket in semiconductor process technology" is a name.
- "Keep the chain of innovations differing from the last one" is a shape.
- "Adopt a 90-day release cycle" is a name.

The answer file (`07_the_tether_answer.md`) stays at the first level of that list.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` is stated as a market payment gap, not as a "knowledge leakage problem" or a "patent problem". Both of those names pick the mechanism before the evidence.
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the direct essay warrant for Fig 9 :54-57 and for keeping four conditions separate in F8.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For BUS-0039 this means: a claim about a particular industry's dominant capture mechanism is licensed only by realized returns measured per innovation, or by an import from a frame whose closure differs, with its provenance recorded. A survey result is an import from the strategy frame. It may be used as corroboration, but it does not name the mechanism for a particular firm.

## 4. Why "patent count proves capture" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session, gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* The reading does not determine the source. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The resolution came from a spectrometer, not an argument: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

A patent count, a grant, a publication count or an imitator's near-identical product is a reading. Several sources can produce it: genuine origin, independent reinvention, a licensing deal, a defensive filing. The reading does not identify the source. What is owed is a discriminator (dates, lab notebooks, provenance, realized returns), not a name inferred from the projection. This is the warrant for F4 and F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Correction to my own use of "shape, not a name"

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Patents, records, bonds, habitats and chains are **not** missing primitives in this derivation: they are already in the business frame's Γ or in the habitat. Naming them is a **binding** from that frame onto terms already held, not the invention of a new atom.

What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall. Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."* Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"* is IMPORT, and an import of an already-held term adds nothing. So the four conditions in F8 are legitimate as a shape, but the moment they are presented as a new discovery about the world, they become an unprovenanced import. Both statements are kept here so neither is quietly lost.

I initially wrote this section as if "shape, not a name" forbade naming patents or complementary assets at all. That was an overstatement. It is narrowed here to the correct target: the framework cannot name the **winner** for a given innovation, and it cannot name a missing primitive, but it can name conditions already held. Retracted in that narrower form only.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious strategy professors to agree with the claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the market, a dataset or a review settle it. The same essay notes that convergent structures from different fields can be checked by provenance: *"I could prove I had not copied her because my paper trail was different... But the structures matched."* That is exactly the Fig 8 :51-52 rule applied to scholarly analogy.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the business claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The stop for this question

The shape stops at four conditions and nine falsifiers. It does not stop at a named mechanism, an industry ranking, or a grounded verdict. *The Library of Babel* supplies the reason: the search ends when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then the searcher has composed the answer in the process of locating it. Here that means the four conditions are usable as a checklist for a reviewer, not as a doctrine. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
