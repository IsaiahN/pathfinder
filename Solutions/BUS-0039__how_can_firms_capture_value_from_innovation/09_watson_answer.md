## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about value capture when knowledge diffuses and imitators enter. Option A: value sits in the knowledge, so the answer is to protect the knowledge and treat the strength of protection as the verdict. Option B: value sits in the arrangement and the habitat while knowledge diffuses, so the answer is a four-condition union checked per innovation, with legal IP as a time-and-record instrument rather than a ground. What each leads to: A produces an exclusion-first prescription whose success measure is a frame-internal count and spends the independence of the people checking it; B produces a checkable shape, nine written refuters, and an open slot until the market settles which mechanism wins.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The per-innovation gap between predicted private return Γ(b,a) and realized market return o′, indexed by slot s and time. Arity: three place relation (innovation, action, return). Symmetry: asymmetric; originator and imitator are not interchangeable, and capture is a threshold relative to competitors. Scale: first order inside one innovation, union across four conditions, second order along a chain of innovations. Fig 9 names it a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, knowledge and closure.** Actors: knowledge atoms, imitators as frames with differing closures. Currency: composition over leaked atoms, closure and reach. Anchor: market payment, not the firm's claim (Fig 2 :29). Question type: closure idempotence and exclusion asymmetry.
- **Domain 2, arrangement and complementary assets.** Actors: the firm, manufacturing, distribution, brand, service, data, cumulative learning, ecosystem actors. Currency: the bond that turns knowledge into delivered value, priced in actions. Anchor: market settlement. Question type: composition, bond versus ingredient.
- **Domain 3, legal and institutional appropriability.** Actors: firm, patent and copyright and trade-secret law, courts, registries, standards bodies. Currency: exclusion rights, time bought, record created. Anchor: court and registry are partial and moving, so this is an instrument, not the ground. Question type: moving anchor and provenance record.
- **Domain 4, market and ground.** Actors: customers, entrants, complementors. Currency: payment, switching, network position. Anchor: willingness to pay and realized return, per innovation. Question type: settlement.
- **Domain 5, chain of innovations.** Actors: successive innovations, followers, dominant designs. Currency: progress per handoff. Anchor: bracket fidelity and ground pressure (Fig 7 :37-39). Question type: sequence, not a static asset.
- **Excluded.** Knowledge as a self-contained moat has no anchor independent of the market; patent count as proof of value is a frame-internal quantity that moves when pushed. Both are excluded as domains before any figure line is applied.
- **Order by dependency.** Perceive which returns arrive and to whom; build vocabulary for what the innovation affords; state what counts as capture; then plan a mechanism; then carry the method, not the recording, to the next innovation. Refuse the knowledge-as-moat term before minting the bond claim (Formula step 2).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six-term physical substrate only fit "knowledge assets" by renaming knowledge as space and spillover as gradient. That is a strain, so Fig 13 governs the honest stop only, not firm composition. No other strain survives re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the protection-first prescription does not. Knowledge diffusion is closure idempotence, so the knowledge item is not the moat. The defensible part is a bond the imitator cannot reproduce from the same atoms, held in an enumerated habitat, supported by a dated record and a legal right that buys time but does not settle value. Capture persists as a chain of differing rooms in which each settled innovation becomes the atom for the next. Four conditions (non-copyable bond, maintained contact with a paying ground, time-and-record instrument, enumerated habitat) form a union to be checked per innovation and never averaged. Which mechanism wins in a given industry is a `gap`, not a silence. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The protection-first reading is refusable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no atom has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a market measurement, not agreement. A per-innovation measurement showing that disclosure alone, with the complement held, does not change private return would break the bond claim. An immovable grant that predicts realized returns would promote the legal instrument to a ground. A permanent imitator removal with no habitat substitution would break the exclusion-asymmetry claim. A single lever restoring all four conditions would collapse the union. A source-discriminating instrument that names the origin of a return would close the `gap`. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
