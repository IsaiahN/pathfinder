## Three things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Shortcuts are the H2 case named precisely.** Geirhos et al. 2020 define shortcuts as rules that work on standard benchmarks and "fail to transfer to more challenging testing conditions." That is the residual in world vocabulary: benchmark success does not discriminate a method from a familiar cue.
2. **The literature contains a real, unresolved conflict.** Lake & Baroni 2023 claim human-like systematic generalization when a network is optimized for compositional skills; Goodale & Mascarenhas 2026 reply that the model "struggles to learn rules that are even slightly out of distribution" and "behaves unsystematically even on many within-distribution problems." Per the method, a conflict sends the derivation back to the census, never flips the verdict. Here it reinforces the figure demand for low-overlap instruments (Fig 2 :31) and for separating wiring from capability (Fig 11 :70). The conflict is shown to readers rather than hidden, because a reader who only sees one side has already been handed the answer.
3. **Metrics can manufacture the appearance of a new ability.** Schaeffer et al. 2023 show that "nonlinear or discontinuous metrics produce apparent emergent abilities, whereas linear or continuous metrics produce smooth, continuous predictable changes." This is Fig 1 :22 in world form: an aggregate or discontinuous score is how a live per-context signal disappears into an average.

A caution on overlap: Lake & Baroni 2018 and Lake & Baroni 2023 share authors, so their agreement is high-overlap, not two witnesses. The 2026 TACL paper is a different team and a conflicting verdict, which is genuinely independent evidence about the difficulty of the problem. Chollet 2019 and Arjovsky et al. 2019 come from different subfields (intelligence measurement and statistics) and corroborate the shape from low-overlap frames. No world source outranks a figure line.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :23-25** — *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Sending a package of conclusions before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** — *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"* and Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Ask what they would expect to see if flexible use were really present before showing the figures.
- **Fig 2 :40** — *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** — *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong verdict about what evidence counts, so buy at least two independent frames.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its pre-registered refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what their anchor would be; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (third stage optional)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the discriminating observation against the ground. The recipient checks the shape claim and refuter without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :23 permits sending a method. Each user still writes their own refuter, states their own mode and names their own ground, because a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. As drafted, it does in the sense that it is stated and refutable; if a reader fires any item in `01` §4, the shape is repaired or withdrawn before further contact.
