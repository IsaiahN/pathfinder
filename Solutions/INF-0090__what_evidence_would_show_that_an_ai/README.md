**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run).** Evidence is not a higher score on a familiar benchmark. Evidence is an observation that discriminates two hypotheses that the same in-distribution accuracy leaves standing: H1, the system reapplies a compressed method in a context whose arrangement differs; H2, the system reproduces associations familiar from training. The shape of the discriminating observation is derived in `01` and stated as a refuter before the conclusion.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people selected for receptiveness is a list selected to agree.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Seat Map :163 makes the same rule for conventions).
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and will get the sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
