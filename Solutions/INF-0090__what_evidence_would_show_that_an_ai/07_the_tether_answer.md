# The Tether's answer: what would show flexible use rather than familiar association

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the method-versus-recording distinction. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and an instrument list, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The same output, correct answers on a task, is consistent with two different sources. A method that can be reapplied in a new context, or an association familiar from training that recurs there. In-distribution accuracy does not discriminate them, because it is a quantity both sources produce (Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*). The residual is the missing discriminator. The question asks for evidence, so the answer is a specification of an instrument, not a verdict about any system.

## 2. The shape of the evidence

1. **A method that survives a round trip, not a replayed recording.** Fig 4 :23 *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 4 :25 *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."* Evidence is a compressed description carried into a new context and brought back with the gap measured: Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*
2. **Per-context residual, never an aggregate.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A near-perfect mean with one live slot is a legal state (Fig 1 :13).
3. **Same atoms, different bond.** Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Hold the ingredients fixed, change the arrangement or order, and see whether transfer survives. This is the cheapest one-pass discriminator (Fig 9 :29). Full derivation in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
4. **Low-overlap triangulation against an unupdating anchor.** Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Two checks from the same corpus or metric family are one witness, not two.
5. **A bargain that compression pays.** Fig 5 :44 *"a term that explains one occasion perfectly"* fails. A memorised association fits one occasion and compresses nothing; a method compresses many contexts under one description.
6. **A refuter written before the test.** Formula "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* Without a pre-registered failure condition, any result can be read as flexibility (Schaeffer et al. 2023 show how metric choice manufactures apparent new abilities).
7. **A stated stop.** Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* Depth and absence look the same from inside (Fig 6 :27), so report which instrument was missing.

No figure names a particular benchmark or number. That row of the census is a genuine `gap`, not a restrained claim: Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named system (GPT-4, MLC, ARC solvers) uses information flexibly. No measurement was taken.
- Not that a higher benchmark score is irrelevant to the budget. It is a filter for what gets tried, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not that compositionality benchmarks settle the question. Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not that the 2023 Nature result is true or the 2026 TACL rebuttal is true. They conflict; the conflict sends us back to the census.
- Not that the shape is a name. It returns what the evidence must look like, not which test to run.
- Not `grounded`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

The ground here is a substituted habitat in which a new bond appears, not a larger version of the training set. Concretely, the checks that would close the residual:

1. **Isomer transfer:** same atoms, changed bond, declared in effect terms before the run. The falsifier in `06` Q2 is written down first.
2. **Round trip gap `R_T`:** compress what the system does, carry it into a third context, bring it back, measure the gap rather than assume zero. Does the compressed method, not a lookup, predict the new contexts?
3. **Source discrimination:** the metamerism test from *The Hard Problem is About Source*. A reading of "correct" *"cannot comment on the source."* The corresponding instrument here must distinguish a reapplied method from a set of familiar associations that produce the same output.
4. **Independent frames:** at least two low-overlap checks, with the outside frame seeing the residual and the shape claim only (Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*).
5. **Refuter fired or survived:** the five conditions in `01` §4 are checked before any conclusion is drawn.

A source-discriminating instrument of this kind, not a further argument, moves the answer toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The residual can be specified and the instruments named in shape form, but nothing here has been measured against an AI system. This derivation stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *Indiscernibility is not identity.* The real literature already contains a conflict (Lake & Baroni 2023 versus Goodale & Mascarenhas 2026), which is a sign that the instrument question is live rather than settled. If a reader produces a low-overlap observation that discriminates H1 from H2, the shape is confirmed at the ground; if they fire a refuter, it is withdrawn or repaired. Until then the verdict stands as `candidate`.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> A better score on a familiar benchmark does not tell you whether an AI system is using information flexibly or just recognising familiar associations, because both produce the same reading and the reading cannot comment on its source. The Tether says what would discriminate them. Ask for a method, not a recording: compress what the system does, carry it into a genuinely new context and measure the round trip gap instead of assuming it is zero. Report the residual on each context, never as an average. Hold the ingredients fixed and change only their arrangement, the way silver fulminate and silver cyanate share atoms but not substance; a system tracking the method transfers, one keyed on familiar cues does not. Check this with two instruments that do not share an evidence pool, against an anchor that does not update, and write down what would count as failure before you run it. The framework returns that shape, not the name of a benchmark, and it does not claim any system passes it. A search that finds nothing is still a verdict, not a silence.
