## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :133 *"A system with a closure can answer that before it searches, and what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is: *the residual will be closed by an instrument whose currency and anchor differ from the electromagnetic one, and the population is a distribution inferred from per-slot detections*. A name would be: *the ten-solar-mass peak is the failed-supernova population*. The first is figure-derived. The second is imported from the gravitational-wave frame.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the starting vocabulary is "null reading" and "population"; the next term gained is "channel versus ground"; the one after that is "second closure".
- *Library of Babel* on steering: *"The books that you are returning in your searches need to be tested or ranged against the problem you want answered in order to steer the direction of the next search."* That is the Fig 2 ranging operation, applied to candidate frames.
- Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* The residual must be described as *a null on a non-luminous slot*, not as *a missing optical counterpart*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach in `02` sends an expectation, not a theory.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* For this question, a strain measurement with a selection function pays for a mass and spin estimate; a population claim needs many such payments plus a rate inference.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Caltech report of a ten-solar-mass peak attributed to failed supernovae is such an import. It is credited to its authors and dated, not presented as a figure derivation.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. The metamerism point survives for this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source."* One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered, all give the same reading. The text states: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"* and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

The electromagnetic null here is exactly that reading. Several sources produce it: an empty slot, a cold isolated black hole, a black hole whose accretion is obscured, a failed supernova that vanished. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. The gravitational-wave detector is the discriminator.

The same essay adds: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* That sentence is the substantive bridge from this essay to Fig 6 :27. It predicts the shape claim directly.

## 5. Q2 answered: what licenses a particular population here?

**Nothing in the framework.** The framework returns the second-closure shape. A particular population is licensed only by the ground settling a measurement, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

### Correction to my own reading of section 3

I initially read "shape, not a name" as forbidding any naming of a population. That is too strong, and it is corrected here rather than quietly kept. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in the library. Mass, spin and merger rate are already quantities held by the physics frame, so selecting one of them is a **binding** from that frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is presenting the binding as a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."*).

Both readings are preserved: the prohibition on inventing a new primitive, and the permission to bind a held quantity with provenance.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A campaign that gathers famous observatories or famous names to agree with the shape is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, let a detector settle it, and state the refuter first.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on. The astronomical fact is settled, if at all, by the catalogs and analyses cited in `09_watson_answer.md` and `10_source_ledger.csv`.
