## The question restated as an effect

A telescope returns no light for a set of object slots. The question asks whether that null is a fact about what exists, or a fact about the instrument's closure. Option A reads the null as absence: no population, stop looking. Option B reads it as depth: something is there that this instrument cannot represent, so range the residual against a second instrument whose closure differs. A leads to a closed search and a channel reported as ground. B leads to a second anchor and a countable population. What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a measurement by an instrument whose currency is not electromagnetic radiation, returning mass, spin and rate for slots that are null in the electromagnetic frame*. That bound shape is the pre-committed prediction in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation settles the astronomical question. The world sources in `09` and `10` do that work, and the figures are primary throughout.

Three reasons for the exclusions, in the framework's own words:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. **A room does not upgrade a label.** Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*
3. **An outside checker must stay outside.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the full architecture before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. The venues in `03` are public and opt-in.
