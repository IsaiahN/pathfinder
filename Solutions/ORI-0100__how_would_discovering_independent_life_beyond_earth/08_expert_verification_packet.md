# Expert verification packet: the Tether on a second independent origin

**What this is:** a claim in a form an astrobiologist or origin-of-life researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No emergence-rate measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The emergence rate currently rests on one observation slot, Earth, and one slot does not support a rate. Discovering independent life beyond Earth would not supply a second number to average; it would add a new atom to the estimation frame, the only operation that changes what that frame can reach, converting an unreachable rate into a bounded, per-site family of estimates. Three conditions gate the update: the new life must be genuinely independent, because shared ancestry makes two readings one evidence pool; each site must be kept distinct with its habitat enumerated, because aggregation discards the slot-level signal; and the step from a per-site reading to a galactic rate carries a nonzero round-trip gap that must be measured rather than zeroed. The framework returns that shape and its falsifiers, not a number.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 6, 8 and 13

- **Statement:** one observation does not license a rate; absence of a second origin is not evidence of rarity; a discovery changes the frame only as an import.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 8 :32 "no search, however long, adds an atom" Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and Fig 6 :22 "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."
- **What to check:** is the one-slot condition correctly described, and is the discovery correctly typed as an import rather than a further search inside the closure?
- **Refuter:** show that a single slot plus a declared prior already determines the rate with no new observation, that is, that composition inside a closure is not idempotent with respect to observations. Or exhibit an exhaustive sealed survey of eligible sites whose silence would then set a genuine bound.

### C2 : ancestry-facing; checkable by an astrobiologist against what a detection would actually deliver

- **Statement:** the update is conditional on independence. Shared ancestry is a common-cause collapse, not a second witness; same biochemistry does not establish either ancestry or independence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50-52 "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ." Fig 2 :18-21 "so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other" Fig 13 :121 "Different routes can arrive at the same substance." Fig 13 :128 "Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it"
- **What to check:** would genetic code, biochemistry and isotopic fractionation actually discriminate transfer from separate origin, or is the reading genuinely ambiguous? Does the claim correctly refuse to decide ancestry from resemblance?
- **Refuter:** show that a second life sharing ancestry still counts as a second independent witness for the *emergence* rate, or that ancestry cannot be discriminated even in principle. Either breaks the independence gate.

### C3 : estimation-facing; checkable by a statistician or modeller

- **Statement:** the update is per slot and the per-site to galactic step has a nonzero round-trip gap. The honest output is a bounded family of estimates with recorded dropped constraints, not a posterior point.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"
- **What to check:** with n = 1 or n = 2, is any point estimate of the rate defensible? Does the selection of Earth by the presence of observers bias the sample, and does the habitat list actually change the estimate?
- **Refuter:** exhibit a lossless collapse of the slot records into one posterior with zero residual on every slot, or a per-site to galactic transform whose round trip returns the site reading unchanged (R_T = 0), or a sampling procedure for Earth independent of observer presence.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a numeric emergence rate, not "life is common", not "the probability is near one".
- Not that the Fermi silence lowers the estimate. Absence was never proof of absence.
- Not that similar biochemistry proves common ancestry, or that different biochemistry proves independence.
- Not that one discovery settles the rate, and not that this packet is `grounded`.
- Not a theory of everything, and no final room.
- Not a claim about which specific body will supply the second origin. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. Specifically:

1. An ancestry-discriminating result for the second life (genetics, biochemistry, isotopic fractionation) that pays for "independent" or "transferred".
2. An enumerated habitat for each site, so the conditions attached to "suitable" are visible.
3. More independent origin slots, so the small-sample bound tightens.
4. A measured R_T for the per-site to galactic step, reported as the set of dropped constraints.
5. Any of the written refuters firing, which would require withdrawal or repair rather than defence.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 6 :21-23, Fig 8 :32, :39, :50-52, Fig 13 :121, :128, :134 and Fig 1 :19, :22, :42. Ten short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own knowledge: would a biosignature detection actually leave ancestry ambiguous, and what instrument would resolve it?
3. Check C3 against their own statistics: is any point rate defensible at n = 1 or n = 2, and is the Earth sample observer-selected?
4. Name the instrument that would discriminate transfer from separate origin, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
