## What this solution decides, in effect terms

The quantity "how often life emerges" is a rate over a population of eligible sites, but it is currently constrained by exactly one observation slot, Earth. The question asks what changes when a second, explicitly independent slot arrives. The three options and what each leads to:

- **Option A, "it would give us a second data point and let us average":** leads to an aggregation that discards the slot-level signal (Fig 1 :22) and to a scale crossing whose round-trip gap is assumed to be zero (Fig 4 :30).
- **Option B, "it would set the rate near one":** leads to a verdict stated without the ground, and ignores that the new observation must first be shown independent rather than a common cause (Fig 2 :18-21).
- **Option C, the figure-derived answer:** the discovery adds an atom to the estimation frame's closure, which is the only operation that moves the wall (Fig 6 :21-23). It converts an unreachable rate into a bounded, per-slot, habitat-conditioned family of estimates, conditional on independence and on a measured round-trip gap. It does not by itself produce a number or make the result `grounded`.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term before the run: *a valid estimate of the emergence rate requires at least two low-overlap independent origin slots, an enumerated habitat for each, and a measured round-trip gap between the per-site reading and the galactic rate.* That term is written down here so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 3, rather than hiding as an unbound preference.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Public institutional pages and opt-in channels only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree. Correlation disqualifies a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
- No bulk-send plan. A room does not add an anchor (Fig 7 :13).
- No numeric posterior for the emergence rate. The framework returns a shape, not a name or a number (Fig 9 :32).

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness would manufacture the very correlation that makes agreement worthless. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact therefore carries the residual and the refuter, never the framework's interiors.
