## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about this claim: discovering independent life beyond Earth would change scientific estimates of how often life emerges. Option A: treat the discovery as a second data point to average into a rate. Option B: treat it as a new atom added to the estimation frame, conditional on independence, per-slot recording and a measured round-trip gap. What each leads to: A discards the slot-level signal (Fig 1 :22), assumes a zero round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30) and counts a shared-ancestry reading as two witnesses (Fig 8 :50-52). B returns the shape of a valid estimate, five written refuters and an honest unreached verdict, and does not pretend to a number. B is the figure-derived option.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: one observation slot now, two after the discovery. Symmetry: an independent origin is a low-overlap second witness; a shared-ancestry origin is a common-cause collapse, not a second witness. Scale: per site versus galactic, so a lossy transform is implied. The residual is that one atom cannot support a rate and the new atom only counts if independent.
- **Domain 1, the estimation frame.** Actors: observation slots, rate parameter, priors. Currency: observations converted into a bound or posterior. Anchor: the actual count of independent origins on eligible sites. This is a domain; the anchor is a ground that does not move with what a frame thinks.
- **Domain 2, the detection frame.** Actors: telescopes, spectrometers, sample return, biosignature assays. Currency: signals and false positives. Anchor: the physical spectrum or sample. It is a domain; the instrument ranges against a physical reading.
- **Domain 3, the ancestry frame.** Actors: genetic code, biochemistry, metabolism, isotopic fractionation. Currency: evidence of common descent versus separate origin. Anchor: the actual genealogy, the record of where each lineage came from. It is a domain.
- **Domain 4, the verification frame.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Detection and ancestry are a union compound: both conditions must hold before the estimate is updated. Fig 9 :57 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54). Estimation depends on both.
- **Order by dependency.** Detection, then vocabulary (is it life, is it independent), then the rate objective, then planning, then learn and carry. A reading taken below the break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :22-23).

Strain test applied: "different routes can arrive at the same substance" (Fig 13 :121) versus "only the record of where each came from separates them" (Fig 8 :50-52). Re-census: one makes the case possible, the other says the contents cannot identify the route. Different questions, no strain.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the easy readings do not. One observation slot does not license a rate, and the absence of a second origin is not evidence of rarity. A confirmed independent discovery is the only legal update, because import adds an atom and search inside the closure cannot. That update turns an unreachable rate into a bounded, per-site, habitat-conditioned family of estimates, conditional on three things: independence established by the ancestry record, slot-level data kept distinct, and a measured round-trip gap between the site reading and the galactic rate. Same biochemistry does not decide ancestry, and the artefact does not reveal the route. The framework returns a shape and a set of written refuters, not a number. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but not trivial. If the independence gate is wrong, two readings could be counted as one witness and the rate understated; if the per-slot requirement is wrong, a real habitat signal is averaged away; if R_T is set to zero, a galactic number is presented as dead reckoning. The framework's own remedy is to keep the five refuters in front of the claim so an error is findable rather than invisible. The avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a conclusion instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The three gates are conditions on the update, not primitives. An ancestry result, a slot record and a measured R_T can be added without withdrawing the shape. A claim already sent can be superseded by a corrected packet with the gates filled in. Nothing in the six-term set has to be retracted.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates shared ancestry from separate origin would settle the gate; additional independent origins with enumerated habitats would tighten the bound; an exhaustive sealed survey would make silence informative; a transform with R_T = 0, a lossless slot aggregation, an observer-independent Earth sampling procedure, or a demonstration that one slot already determines the rate would each fire a written refuter and require withdrawal or repair. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`, and the honest report about the current rate is unreached rather than silence.
