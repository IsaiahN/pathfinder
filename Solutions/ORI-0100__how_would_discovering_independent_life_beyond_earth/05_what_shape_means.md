## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the form a valid estimate must have, statable before the estimate can be made. For this question it is: *the estimate must be built from at least two low-overlap independent origin slots, each with an enumerated habitat, and a measured round-trip gap between the per-site reading and the galactic rate.* This constrains; it does not fill. "Life is common" is a name for the answer, not a shape. "At least two independent slots are owed before a rate can be stated" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing you are seeking, notice recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, and recurse. The exact line: "you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Applied here: the vocabulary already held is "rate", "sample", "independence", "habitat", "bias". The new terms that recur in the sources are "zero degrees of freedom", "small sample size", "anthropic bias", "source discrimination". Narrowing on those yields the shape above.
- *Library of Babel* on splitting: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" This question is such a bundle: detection, ancestry and estimation. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." The shape is reached by unbundling, not by searching harder for a number.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox that by then you have "already composed the answer through the process of locating it". Here the starting place is the one-slot residual; the finish line is the recognition that a second independent slot is owed.
- Fig 6 :32: "An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something." The descent into the second site's habitat and the ancestry discriminator is an INWARD extension of an instrument already returning something (the first site's geochemistry and genetics), not a fresh invention.

## 3. What licenses a name or a number

Two things only, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." For this question that means additional independent origins, or an ancestry-discriminating instrument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The second biosphere is such an import; its atom count is licensed by contact, but the binding of that atom to "an independent origin" is licensed only by the ancestry record.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* sharpens the second point. Metamerism: a colour instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source"; the same reading can come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, "not by argument", that resolved it. A second life's biochemistry is a reading; "independent origin" and "transferred ancestor" are sources. Resemblance does not decide between them.

**Correction to an easy overstatement:** "shape, not a name" does not forbid naming ordinary things that are already in the library (see the exemplar's own correction in `05`, section 5b). It governs a *missing* primitive. Here the missing thing is the second independent origin, which is genuinely not held in the Earth-only closure. So the rule applies with full force: the framework can say a second atom is owed, but it cannot name which biosphere will supply it or what its biochemistry will be. Naming a specific moon or a specific pathway would be the forbidden move.

## 4. Prestige is not action, and agreement is not evidence

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." Also: "You may all agree and be wrong."

Ten astrobiologists agreeing that life is common is the named collapse, not a measurement. Fig 7 :13: "agreement compounds, evidence does not". Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried against a ground (another site, a better ancestry instrument), and records the failure if it fails.

## 5. Answer to the question this file exists to answer

**What licenses calling the new life "independent"?** Nothing in the framework's shape. Independence is licensed only by a ground-paid ancestry discrimination: genetic code, biochemical architecture, isotopic fractionation, and the record of where each lineage came from (Fig 8 :50-52). Until that pays, the honest report is "a second reading exists; its source is unreached", which is a verdict, not a silence.

**Does the framework license a number for the rate?** No. It licenses the shape of a valid estimate and the statement that the current one is unreachable. A number requires the ground to pay for additional slots and for the round-trip gap to be measured. Mark `candidate` until then.
