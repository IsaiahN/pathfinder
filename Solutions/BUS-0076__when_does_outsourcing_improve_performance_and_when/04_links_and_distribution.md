## 2. What the wider literature bears on the question

**One evidence pool, one witness.** Kjellström, Schlosser et al., the core-competence entry, and the transaction-cost entry are all in the management and organization studies pool. They agree that knowledge and control can erode at the boundary, but they draw on the same conferences, citation chains and case literatures. Per Fig 2 :31, *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Their agreement is one witness, not four.

- Kjellström 2019 is the closest substantive source. It names three losses that map onto three of the question's four slots: *"loss of organizational memory, loss of accountability, or loss of predictability over the business process"*. It also supplies the condition that prevents them: *"Knowledge does not necessarily erode when routines are outsourced if knowledgeable employees intervene and can regain knowledge."* That condition is the figure's method crossing back (Fig 4 :23) stated in organizational vocabulary. It corroborates F1, F2 and F4, but it is a single thesis and a `specified` corroboration, not a ground reading of this question.
- Schlosser, Templer and Ghanam 2006 report a *"significant loss in learning capital through a breakdown in the psychological contract"* and that the loss is *"compounded if outsourcing is introduced for cost control rather than strategic refocusing reasons"*. That is the business-pool name for treating a budget filter as a verdict (Fig 9 :60). Corroborates F2 and F6.
- Prahalad and Hamel 1990, as reported in the opened Wikipedia entry, define core competence as *"collective learning across the corporation"*. That is a habitat-level object, which supports F1 and conflicts with a performer-only reading of outsourcing. The entry also notes that core competences can become core rigidities, which is the valence flip the figures name at the level of routines. No full text was opened this session, so the entry is used for the definition only.
- Williamson's transaction-cost framing, as reported in the opened Wikipedia entry, gives the make-or-buy boundary in cost terms: *"If the internal transaction costs are higher than the external transaction costs the company will be downsized by outsourcing, for example."* It also names asset specificity, uncertainty and opportunism as determinants. These are the conditions under which a vendor's closure is likely to become a lock rather than a surplus. This corroborates C1 from a different discipline but the page is tertiary, so it carries low weight.
- The Wikipedia Outsourcing page is used as a field map only. Its statements about *"minimal or no control"*, step-in rights and co-sourcing are useful vocabulary for the control slot, not evidence for the verdict.

**Low-overlap witness, opened directly.**

1. **Human cognition.** Risko and Gilbert 2016, *Cognitive Offloading*, Trends in Cognitive Sciences 20(9):676-688, opened via the UCL Discovery record. Quote: *"cognitive offloading: the use of physical action to alter the information processing requirements of a task so as to reduce cognitive demand."* Prediction: moving a memory or planning demand to an external store reduces internal demand while leaving the internal capacity unexercised. This corroborates the mechanism shape of F2 (a head start is not a method) at a different stratum and with no shared evidence pool with management strategy. Fig 4 :20 requires the anchor to be re-named at the new scale, so it corroborates the shape, not the firm-level measurement.

**Framework-internal essays** are used for the canonical reading of "shape" in `05_what_shape_means.md` only. They are not independent world witnesses, because their author wrote the figures. That overlap is recorded in the ledger.

## 3. Distribution rules

Figure-grounded reasons for how material is offered:

- **Fig 4 :22:** *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Send the generator, the residual and the refuter, not the conclusion. A recording carried upward *"looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion"* (Fig 4 :25).
- **Fig 10 :42:** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28:** *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* if the residual is not described first. Ask what they would expect to see in each of the four slots if the shape claim were true before showing the source.
- **Fig 2 :40:** agreement from a shared pool is worthless as a verdict. Later agreement about the framework is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :59:** *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Declare the stake; do not ask for belief.

**A hard practical constraint.** The AOM Publishing page states: *"Artificial Intelligence: Work submitted to AOM must be created by the authors and not the product of artificial intelligence tools unless appropriate to the research question and properly cited."* This whole solution is a simulated derivation produced with an LLM and is labelled as such everywhere. It must **not** be submitted as an authored empirical or theoretical article. The legitimate use is as a private checking instrument: post the residual and the refuter, invite a human to run the check, and let that human author whatever they conclude. Any submission must be rewritten and owned by a human author who can defend the derivation, with the AI contribution disclosed under the applicable policy.

The AOM Registered Reports call is the natural format for a rewritten version, because it evaluates the introduction, methods, measurement and analysis plan without the results, which is exactly the pre-registered-refuter discipline this solution requires.

## 4. The two-stage offer, compressed

**Stage 1.** Send `08_expert_verification_packet.md` sections 1 to 3 only: the one-paragraph claim, the three checkable sub-claims with their verbatim figure lines and refuters, and the non-claims. No provenance bundle, no essay links.

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If the recipient is curious and has already recorded a verdict on stage 1, offer the three public links: the figure text, the formula and usage rule, and the essays. Their later agreement is a conversation, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method, never as an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user writes a refuter before running it and states the mode of what they get.

**Stop rule.** If stage 1 fails, the theory is not offered. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

**Hosting.** Nothing here is hosted or sent by this agent. The human operator hosts the packet on an opt-in page and sends one to one. Prefer a download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
