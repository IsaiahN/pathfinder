## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not whether outsourcing is good or bad, but which of four per-activity slots a make-or-buy action shrinks or grows. In effect terms: a firm's library Γ predicts what performing activity s inside the firm will return, the firm acts by making (a_make) or outsourcing (a_out), and o' is what actually happens. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is measured per activity slot, never averaged into one outsourcing ROI (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The slots named in the question are performance, control, learning, and accountability, and they are different quantities, not four readings of one quantity.

- Option A: "outsourcing improves performance" treated as a single verdict. Leads to the prediction that a cost saving closes the question.
- Option B: "outsourcing is a habitat substitution whose effect is per slot and conditional." Leads to the prediction that performance can improve while the other slots weaken, and that a single scalar cannot close the residual (Fig 9 :57).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a conjunction of conditions stated per slot, plus an instrument that discriminates "the vendor brought a method we could not build" from "we stopped maintaining the channel and the habitat." Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01` section 4 can fire on it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the business question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of outsourcing.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
