# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no per-slot measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about outsourcing and learning before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about make-or-buy decisions that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** outsourcing is not a change of performer, it is a change of habitat. Four things have to be judged separately, never blended into one ROI: delivered performance, control, learning, and accountability.
>
> 1. Performance improves when the outside party's way of working genuinely differs from the firm's and brings a method the firm could not build inside its own closure, and when the result is checked against an outcome the outside party cannot move.
> 2. Learning survives only when a method, not just a deliverable, crosses back and gets recorded inside the firm. If what comes back is a report or a metric, the firm gets a head start for this task and pays again on the next one.
> 3. Accountability fails when the outside party also grades its own work. A measure that the measured party authors is not an anchor; it becomes another frame, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices.
> 4. Control fails when the firm's own readings of the real outcome go stale, get dropped, saturate into vendor KPIs, or get pooled into an average. The market keeps paying someone; the firm stops hearing who.
>
> The claim is that these are different quantities with different instruments, not four degrees of one problem. A cost saving can be real while the other three slots weaken, and one score cannot display the shortfall.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can name which activities should be outsourced, that outsourcing is always bad, that any particular firm is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of outsourcing. I am offering a shape any candidate answer would have to have. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a performer swap with habitat and contact held constant improves both cost and the firm's measured ability to do the activity next time;
> 2. only deliverables cross back, with no method or recorded arrangement, and the firm re-derives the capability at zero further cost on the next activity;
> 3. the vendor authors its own verdict and an independently measured outcome still tracks that verdict through a documented perturbation, with no third frame;
> 4. the firm's direct contact with the ground is removed and its readings are pooled, yet its verdicts stay ground-verified prospectively;
> 5. a vendor whose way of working overlaps almost completely with the firm's nevertheless adds a durable, independently verified new capability;
> 6. one scalar metric predicts all four slots prospectively with nothing left over, or one lever restores both conditions with no categorically different residue.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Four slots kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Method versus recording stated before theory | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the right activities or vendor | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check what it is looking for; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A direct-outreach reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
- Record the stage-1 verdict before sending stage 2. A later "I agree" is not evidence (Fig 2 :40).
