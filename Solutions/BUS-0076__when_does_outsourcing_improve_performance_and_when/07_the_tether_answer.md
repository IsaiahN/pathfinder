# The Tether's answer on outsourcing, control, learning, and accountability

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading. No prospective per-slot measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why, after an activity is moved outside the firm, the performance slot can shrink while the control, learning and accountability slots stay flat or grow. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per activity and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about the **vector of four residuals after a boundary crossing**, not about the level of cost saving.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the external party brings a real method from a differing closure and the import pays at an independently measured ground.
- **C2:** the firm keeps a method, a channel, and a ground-maintaining seat on its own side of the boundary.

## 2. The shape

1. **Outsourcing is a habitat substitution, not an agent swap.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* A cheaper performer is an agent-only improvement unless the habitat and the contact change with it.
2. **Performance improves only when the closures differ and the import pays.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :45: *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* If the vendor does the same thing under a different name, the union adds nothing whatever the price.
3. **Learning depends on a method crossing back, not a deliverable.** Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Fig 12 :108-109: *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."* If re-derivation is not a lookup, no method crossed.
4. **Accountability fails when the vendor becomes its own anchor.** Fig 2 :47: *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."* Fig 10 :43: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A measure the measured party authors is a mirror, and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35).
5. **Control fails by channel decay, not ground decay.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :28: *"you have lost touch"*. The market keeps paying someone; the firm stops hearing who.
6. **The four slots cannot be averaged.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 12 :125: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* This is the structural reason both halves of the question can be true at once.
7. **Re-tendering to a similar vendor is motion, not progress.** Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*; :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
8. **Insourcing does not undo the substitution.** Fig 11 :66 and Fig 11 :67: the residual moves back into a habitat that changed while it was away, and what was not reproduced is invisible until the goal fails again.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective per-slot measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a make-or-buy rule. It does not say which activities to outsource or which vendor to choose. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. It makes no claim about the percentage of outsourcing engagements that succeed. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that cost savings are irrelevant. They are real in D1 and legitimate as a filter. The claim is that they do not close the other three slots.
- Not that the management literature independently corroborates this. Kjellström, Schlosser et al., core competence and transaction-cost work overlap in one evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The cognition source corroborates the mechanism at a different stratum, not the firm-level fact.
- Not a theory of everything about operations. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A per-slot discriminator, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: an instrument that reads a result *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are unit cost, service level and vendor KPIs; the candidate sources are a real imported method, a temporary price differential, a deferred quality problem, and a quietly destroyed capability.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. performer swap with habitat and contact held constant improves both performance and next-activity capability;
2. only deliverables cross and re-derivation is still a lookup;
3. vendor-authored verdict tracked by an independent outcome through a perturbation;
4. removed contact and pooled readings with verdicts still ground-verified;
5. near-complete closure overlap with a durable new atom acquired anyway;
6. one scalar metric predicting all four slots, or one lever restoring both conditions with no residue;
7. working vocabulary and carry with failure located at planning across documented cases.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per activity, records bonds as well as atoms, names who authors each verdict, and reports which slot failed first would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a per-slot source discriminator, characterized in effect terms but not held here. Naming the right activity or vendor from a cost reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The eight conditions and falsifiers in section 2 and section 4 are a shape; the ninth thing, which activity to outsource next, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say outsourcing is good or bad, and it does not name the activities to outsource. It says outsourcing is a change of habitat, not just a change of performer, and that four things must be judged separately: performance, control, learning, and accountability. Performance improves when the outside party's way of working genuinely differs and brings a method the firm could not build, checked against an outcome that party cannot move. Learning survives only when a method, not just a deliverable, crosses back and is recorded inside the firm, so the next task is a lookup rather than a fresh discovery. Accountability fails when the vendor also grades its own work, because a measure authored by the measured party is a mirror, not an anchor. Control fails when the firm's own readings go stale, saturate into vendor KPIs, or get pooled into an average. These are different quantities in different currencies, so a single ROI cannot show the shortfall. What is offered is that shape, eight falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which activities and which vendors occupy the slots stays open until a per-slot discriminator exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
