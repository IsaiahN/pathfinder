# Expert verification packet - the Tether on outsourcing, control, learning, and accountability

**What this is:** a claim in a form an operations or organization researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No prospective per-slot measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Outsourcing is a change of habitat, not just a change of performer, and four things have to be judged separately: delivered performance, control, learning, and accountability. Performance improves when the vendor's way of working genuinely differs from the firm's, brings a method the firm could not build inside its own closure, and is checked against an outcome the vendor cannot move. Learning survives only when a method, not just a deliverable, crosses back and is recorded inside the firm, so the next activity is a lookup rather than a fresh discovery. Accountability fails when the vendor also authors the verdict on its own work, because a measure the measured party writes is a mirror, not an anchor. Control fails when the firm's own readings of the real outcome go stale, saturate into vendor KPIs, or get pooled into an average. These are different quantities in non-additive currencies, so a single blended ROI cannot display the shortfall, and the two halves of the question can both be true at once. What is offered is a shape any candidate answer must have, not a make-or-buy rule.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal plus one business-facing check

- **Statement:** outsourcing is a habitat substitution, so a cheaper performer alone is an agent-only improvement; a performance gain requires a differing closure and a paid import verified at an independent ground.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."*
  - Fig 11 :40 - *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."*
  - Fig 11 :66 - *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*; Fig 8 :45 - *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."*
  - Fig 8 :49 - *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Kjellström 2019 reports that *"Knowledge does not necessarily erode when routines are outsourced if knowledgeable employees intervene and can regain knowledge."* Does that sentence not already name the boundary-spanning method that makes the closure differ, and is the vendor who brings nothing new not simply the second name in Fig 8 :49? A "no" to both is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a performer swap with habitat and contact demonstrably held constant that improves both first-order performance and the firm's measured next-activity capability; or a vendor whose closure is shown to overlap almost completely with the firm's that nevertheless adds a durable, ground-verified atom. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4, 12 and 3

- **Statement:** learning depends on a method crossing back rather than a recording; where the arrangement is not recorded on the firm's side, the firm pays the discovery cost again; the chain usually breaks at vocabulary and learn-and-carry, not at vendor selection.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*; Fig 4 :25 - *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*
  - Fig 12 :108-109 - *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
  - Fig 3 :19 - *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*; Fig 3 :27 - *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- **Refuter:** a firm that receives only deliverables, with no re-applicable arrangement and no recorded bond, yet re-derives the capability at zero further action cost on the next activity; or documented cases in which vocabulary and carry both measure as working and the failure is at planning. Either breaks C2.

### C3 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 10

- **Statement:** accountability fails when the measured party authors the verdict, and control fails by channel decay while the ground keeps paying someone; the four slots are in non-additive currencies and cannot be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :47 - *"A ground listed as one of the parties has already become a frame."*; Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
  - Fig 10 :43 - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*; Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
  - Fig 12 :125 - *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."*; Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **Refuter:** an arrangement in which the vendor authors its own verdict and an independently measured outcome still tracks that verdict through a documented perturbation with no third frame; or a single scalar metric that prospectively predicts all four slots with no residual left over. Either breaks C3.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not `grounded`. No measurement has paid for any part of this.
- Not a make-or-buy rule. No claim about which activities or which vendors to choose.
- Not that cost saving is irrelevant. It is real in the performance slot and is a legitimate filter for the budget, never a witness for the other three slots.
- Not an average. No claim about the percentage of engagements that succeed.
- Not that the management literature independently corroborates this. Kjellström, Schlosser et al., core competence and transaction-cost work share one evidence pool. The cognition source corroborates the mechanism at a different stratum, not the firm-level fact.
- Not that insourcing restores what was lost. The return trip is a habitat substitution with a lossy gap (Fig 11 :66, Fig 4 :30).
- Not that this packet, produced with an LLM, is submittable or publishable as authored work. See the AI-policy note in `04_links_and_distribution.md`.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-activity study that measures the two conditions separately, records bonds as well as atoms, names who authors each verdict, and reports which slot failed first. Concretely:

1. **Ground test:** is each slot's outcome measured by something the vendor cannot move? Indicators: a counter, an audit, a customer, not a vendor KPI (Fig 2 :29, :47).
2. **Closure test:** does the vendor hold a method the firm's closure lacks, and does the import pay at the ground? If the closures overlap almost completely, C1 is refuted (Fig 8 :49).
3. **Method test:** on the next activity, is re-derivation a lookup or a fresh discovery? If fresh, no method crossed (Fig 12 :107-109).
4. **Channel test:** are the firm's direct readings still current and unpooled, or stale, saturated and pooled (Fig 10 :25-26)?
5. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a real imported method from a temporary price differential, a deferred quality problem and a destroyed capability when all produce the same cost reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source"*.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40 and :66, Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :45 and :49, Fig 4 :22 and :25, Fig 10 :24 and :43, Fig 2 :47, Fig 12 :125, Fig 9 :60. Twelve short passages, about eight minutes.
2. Check C1 and C2 against your own knowledge of outsourcing: is there a documented case where a near-identical closure nevertheless added a durable new capability, or where a pure deliverable still made re-derivation free? About five minutes.
3. Name one instrument that would discriminate an imported method from a temporary price differential using observable per-activity data, or say why none exists. About five minutes.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
