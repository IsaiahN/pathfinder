## QUESTION

When does outsourcing improve performance, and when does it weaken control, learning, and accountability?

Restated as an effect: under which conditions does the action a_out shrink the performance-slot residual without growing the control, learning and accountability slot residuals, and under which conditions does it grow them while performance improves? R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per activity and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the realized outcome measured independently of the performer, for which Fig 2 :29 names *"a counter, a market, a patient outcome"* as instances. A vendor KPI moves when pushed, so it is an instrument, not an anchor.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** outsourcing improves performance when the vendor's closure genuinely differs and the imported method pays at an independently measured ground, and when the firm retains a method, a channel and a ground-maintaining seat. It weakens control, learning and accountability when what crosses is a recording rather than a method, when the habitat substitution removes routines that were not reproduced, when the channel to the ground degrades, or when the vendor is allowed to author its own verdict. The four slots are different quantities in non-additive currencies, so both halves of the question can hold at once and the verdicts are kept per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`. The management evidence pool corroborates the shape after re-mapping and counts as one witness; the cognition source corroborates the mechanism at a different scale. Which activities and vendors to choose is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A firm failing C2 would be sold a better vendor selection process, or a firm failing C1 would be told to document more, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its eight falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **Reversed by:** any of the eight pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) a performer swap with habitat and contact held constant that improves both performance and next-activity capability, (2) a pure deliverable that still makes re-derivation a lookup, (3) a vendor-authored verdict tracked by an independent outcome through a perturbation, (4) near-complete closure overlap with a durable new atom acquired anyway, or (5) one scalar metric predicting all four slots with no residue. A source with a genuinely different evidence pool showing that per-slot outcomes are fully predicted by the contract price alone would also reverse it.
