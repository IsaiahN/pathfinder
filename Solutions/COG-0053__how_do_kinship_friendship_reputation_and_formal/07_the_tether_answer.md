# The Tether's answer on kinship, friendship, reputation and formal rules

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain predictions. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §5), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Kinship, friendship, reputation and formal rules are usually listed as four things that "promote cooperation". A list does not answer *how they interact*. The residual R is the gap between the defection a one-shot, well-mixed self-interest frame predicts and the cooperation actually observed. It is a four-slot, cross-scale, asymmetric residual (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). Fig 9 names the object a **union compound**, not a list: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.

## 2. The shape

1. **The interaction is the bond, not the list.** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." Fig 12 :94 "The arrangement is the substance." So an answer has to say what relation holds between the four, not just that each helps. This is the decisive point of the whole derivation.
2. **The four close different slots under different conditions.** Nowak's five rules give the non-circular reason: r > c/b, w > c/b, q > c/b, b/c > k, b/c > 1 + n/m are five different inequalities in five different parameters. More kin warmth cannot buy anonymity; more reputation cannot help when a stranger's reputation is unknown. Fig 8 :45 "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
3. **The bonds are typed, not uniform.** Kinship and friendship are symmetric bonds; reputation is a comparison (an ordering, more or less trustworthy); formal rules are sequence and production (a rule laid down, a sanction that produces an outcome). Fig 12 :117 "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Treating all four as one conjunction is the flat-formula collapse.
4. **The four sit at different scales, and the membrane passes methods.** Kinship and friendship are a network structure (Fig 13 :24 "that entities relate, and influence travels"); reputation is a spectrum structure (Fig 13 :20 "ordering, so more and less can be said"); formal rules are a gradient structure (Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"). Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." What scales up is monitoring and graduated sanctioning, not a recording of who cooperated once. Marlowe et al. (2008) find the predicted direction: third-party punishment rises with society size as anonymity defeats reputation.
5. **Valence is relative to the residual.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." The same sanction sustains cooperation in one setting and crushes it in another. "Add formal rules" is not a monotone improvement. Cox et al. (2010) find externally imposed rules failing when they do not match local conditions.
6. **The union has an order and a cycle.** Reputation depends on a communication channel; formal enforcement depends on monitoring reputation cannot supply under anonymity. So the dependency runs face-to-face to reputational to formal, with the four composed into one compound (Fig 9 :57-58). Cooperation produces reputation, reputation produces cooperation: the loop is a cycle, not a chain (Fig 12 :121 "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction.").
7. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." It cannot say which mechanism dominates in a given society. That needs a ground reading or a provenanced import.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of four independent causes, and not an average of their effects.
- Not "more of any one is always better". Each bond closes under its own condition.
- Not a claim that formal rules replace kinship, friendship or reputation. They compose over them; the transferred object is a method.
- Not a name for the dominant mechanism in any particular society.
- Not `grounded`, and not a theory of everything. No behavioural measurement settles the arrangement here.
- Not a claim that reputation is ground. From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

An outcome measurement that discriminates bonds, not a further argument. The pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §5 are the test:

1. one mechanism, unbound, shown by measurement to sustain cooperation at every scale including large anonymous institutions;
2. the four shown to draw on one evidence pool, so they are one mechanism with four names;
3. formal rules shown to transfer no method across scale, only a recording;
4. a mechanism's valence shown to be invariant across residuals and societies;
5. a ground reading that names a unique dominant mechanism without a provenanced import.

Anything short of these is agreement, and Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The six-term substrate and the bond set give the shape of any adequate answer; the available instruments in this run cannot distinguish which arrangement obtains in a particular setting. The derivation stops there and records the lineage, per the canonical README: "Indiscernibility is not identity." A second seat that shares this evidence pool is a mirror with a different name, not a corroborator (`11_audit.md`).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank kinship, friendship, reputation and formal rules as four causes that add up. It says the *interaction* between them is the answer: they are four slots in one overlapping compound, joined by different kinds of relation. Kinship and friendship are mutual bonds, reputation is an ordering, and formal rules are a directed, spendable difference. Each slot closes under its own condition, so raising one does not substitute for another, and what scales from a face-to-face group to an institution is a method, monitoring and graduated sanctioning, not a record of past cooperation. The effect of any one mechanism depends on the problem it is used against, so the same sanction can help in one setting and harm in another. The framework refuses to name which mechanism dominates in a given society; it returns a shape, written refuters, and the instruments that would close the slot. The compound stays open until the ground settles it.
