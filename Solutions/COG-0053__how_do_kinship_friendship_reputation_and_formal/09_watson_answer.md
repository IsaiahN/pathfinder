## DOMAIN CENSUS

- **R (the residual).** The gap between predicted one-shot defection and observed cooperation. Arity: four slots. Symmetry: asymmetric, because kin and friendship are mutual, reputation is an ordering and formal rules are directed. Scale: face-to-face to community to institutional. Fig 9 :57-58 "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound." Union compound, not a list.
- **Domain 1, face-to-face relational.** Actors: kin, friends, dyads. Currency: direct benefit, grooming time, reciprocal help. Anchor: repeated interaction and observed helping. A partition boundary question at the network structure.
- **Domain 2, reputational / informational.** Actors: third parties, gossip network. Currency: reputation, an ordering over actors. Anchor: knowledge of past behaviour. This is the least-instrumented link of Fig 3's chain.
- **Domain 3, formal institutional.** Actors: monitors, judges, rule makers. Currency: sanctions, enforcement, compliance. Anchor: third-party punishment rates and compliance records.
- **Domain 4, composition (the question itself).** Actors: the four mechanisms as terms. Currency: the bonds between them. Anchor: measured cooperation across scales. This domain has its own loop.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception then vocabulary then objective then planning then carry (Fig 3). Reputation depends on a communication channel; formal enforcement depends on monitoring reputation cannot supply under anonymity. D1 to D2 to D3, with D4 composed over all three. ## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives. Cooperation is sustained by a priced, typed arrangement of the four mechanisms inside one union compound: kin and friendship are symmetric mutual bonds, reputation is an ordering, formal rules are a directed spendable difference; each slot closes under its own condition, methods cross the scale membrane, and valence is relative to the residual. Reputation-first and rules-first are real isomers with a stated falsifier. The framework does not name a dominant mechanism for any society; that slot stays open for a ground reading or a provenanced import. This is a `general` / `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. No single mechanism is sufficient, and no average of the four is an answer.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, writes its refuters before the conclusion and records its lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is misdirecting institutional design by treating mechanisms as substitutable, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter and by testing substitution directly.
- **undoable:** yes. The specific bindings (substrate typing, isomer order) are removable without touching the core shape; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already keep them separate. A claim sent to a public venue can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive must be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A behavioural measurement showing one mechanism sustaining cooperation at all scales without a bond, or cross-cultural data showing third-party enforcement substituting for reputation under anonymity, or evidence that a mechanism's valence is invariant across societies, would break the shape. A provenanced import with a shadow test that names a dominant mechanism for a given society would close the open slot. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
