## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" and "Indiscernibility is not identity." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question: "kinship, friendship, reputation and rules interact through typed bonds in one union compound, with methods crossing the scale membrane" is a shape. "Kinship dominates in small societies and reputation dominates in large ones" is a name, and this derivation does not have the ground to say it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of." And: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- The residual was therefore described in effect terms first (section 0 of `01`): mutual bond, ordering, directed difference, cross-scale compound. Only then were figure lines chosen.
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." That is the honest stop: the shape is where the instruments run out, not where certainty begins.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest." Here that would be a behavioural measurement that discriminates which bond is doing the work in a given setting.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51). Nowak's thresholds and Marlowe's cross-cultural data are imports of this kind; they *corroborate* the shape but they do not name a dominant mechanism for any particular society, and their provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

**Consequence for this question.** Naming reputation as "the" answer for large societies would be a name where only a shape is available. The world data make it plausible, but plausible is not settled. The slot stays open.

## 4. Reputation is not ground (prestige is not action)

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened), the rule that bears hardest on a question about reputation: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A high reputation can give a cooperation claim a budget to be tried. A prestigious reviewer cannot settle whether the cooperation happened. In this solution the five scientific world sources are weighted by what they measured, not by the standing of their authors, and the two framework essays are declared high-overlap in the ledger. This is the same rule that forbids ranking outreach recipients by how likely they are to agree.

## 5. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and `07_the_tether_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim, and they cannot settle anything they report on. This is stated on every substantive file.

## 6. The round trip must be measured, not assumed

Fig 4 :30 "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked." Compressing the figure set into the four-slot shape throws away detail, and the gap is recorded qualitatively in `11_audit.md` as R_T. Treating the shorthand as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error.
