## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about new instruments returning readings that holding theories cannot compose. Option A: technology simply adds facts that old theories digest with time, so no structural limit is owed. Option B: instruments extend what can be registered, old closures cannot add the term that would compose the reading, the break sits at the perception-to-vocabulary link, and the two historical directions select different remedies. What each leads to: A predicts no shape, no refuter and no honest stop, and it turns the question into a chronicle; B predicts a checkable compound, four refuters written in advance, and a stop at the ground. This answer takes B.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; a chronicle of instruments does not. New technology creates evidence by extending what can be represented at all (Fig 6 :26), built on readings already returning something (Fig 6 :32-33). The older theory cannot interpret that evidence because its closure is idempotent and cannot add the composing atom (Fig 8 :32, Fig 6 :21). The break sits at the perception-to-vocabulary link (Fig 3 :27), and a reading does not determine its source, which is why a name is premature. There are two directions, instrument-first and theory-first (Fig 6 :38), which are isomers over a shared atom set and select different first questions (`06`). The two remedies, inward and outward, are opposed and neither settles the case without the ground (Fig 6 :31, Fig 9 :42). The framework returns a shape, not a name, and "unreachable" is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :133-134). This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labeled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records provenance, so an error is findable. The real cost is sending a chronicle instead of a generator, which Fig 4 :25 prices: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and the four refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The two directions and the compound reading are removable without touching the figure lines; `07` and `08` already state them as `specified`. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a case record, not agreement. A reading with date and instrument showing the prior theory composed it with no residual; a demonstration that closure is not idempotent; a calibrated vocabulary link that still fails; one lever closing both conditions; a missing primitive named and settled with no mint or import; or a figure re-census clearing the ⋛ operand-type strain at second order. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
