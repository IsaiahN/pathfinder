## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly on this question

1. **Observation is instrument-inclusive, not pure.** SEP (Boyd and Bogen, 2026) says *"scientific observation is rarely unaided perception and instead usually employs sophisticated instrumentation"* and gives the fMRI case, where *"the production of fMRI images requires extensive statistical manipulation based on theories about the radio signals."* This is the philosophy-of-science version of Fig 6 :26: the instrument extends what can be registered, but the reading is not a bare given. Use it to explain why the mismatch is structural, not a failure of attention.
2. **The old library can be the thing that blocks interpretation.** In the electron case, the 1890 estimate was more than a thousand times too large, and *"little credence was given to his calculations at the time. This is because it was assumed that the charge carriers were much heavier hydrogen or nitrogen atoms."* That is Fig 8 :32 in a laboratory notebook: closure is idempotent, so no search inside the heavy-carrier library would produce a light carrier. Keep the dates and the order (reading first, interpretation later) so the provenance distinction is checkable.

## The two-stage offer

**(1)** Validate the shape against a historical record the reader knows well. **(2)** Only if curious, read the framework that produced it. **(3)** Optionally, use the formula recursively on other problems, writing a fresh refuter and stating a mode each time.

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown and never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
- **Stage 3 is permitted** because the formula is a generator: Fig 4 :22, *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*
- **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."* Each user must write down what would refute their result before running it and label the mode of what they get.
- **Prestige is not a witness.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is `specified`, not `grounded`, and its refuters are written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4. Do not soften them to make the offer easier.
