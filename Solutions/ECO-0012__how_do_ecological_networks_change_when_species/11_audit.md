## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency. I deliberately started from different figures (Fig 9 union/partition, Fig 13 substrate, Fig 2 ground) rather than the primary's Fig 11 and Fig 1, so the routes would differ.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** A network measured in conditions C1 fails to predict interactions in C2. Arity: three named axes with heavy overlap, season is largely a change in environmental conditions, and life stage is an internal operand change. I read this as a **union compound** from Fig 9 :54 and :57, before seeing the primary's F8. Symmetry: interaction change is not generally reversible. A predator switching prey is not the prey switching predator. Scale: edge, arrangement, cascade. Three scales, not one.
- **Domain 1, structure.** Actors: operands (species) and typed relations (interactions). Currency: influence travels. Anchor: the measured edge set under a condition. Question type: which compositions are legal. I reached this from Fig 13 :74 (*"Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one"*) and Fig 13 :23-24, not from Fig 1.
- **Domain 2, field of contact.** Actors: conditions, species, relations. Currency: contact. Anchor: the ground, which settles and does not update (Fig 11 :73). Question type: enumeration, not composition. I reached this from Fig 11 :43-44 after Fig 13 forced the question of where operands come from.
- **Domain 3, ordered difference.** Actors: the same organism at successive stages. Currency: ordered succession of differences (Fig 13 :12). Anchor: the stage-resolved record. Question type: ordered composition over a changed operand.
- **Domain 4, checking frame.** Actors: expert readers and instruments. Currency: residual and agreement. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Its independence is zero here because it shares the pool.

Order by dependency: enumerate conditions, classify relations, compose the network, read time, check ground. I derived this from Fig 3 :20 (*"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance"*) plus Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).

My census matches the primary's four domains, its union reading and its dependency order. We used different entry figures, which is low overlap *between routes inside the framework*, but we still share one evidence pool and one author. The agreement is expected and carries no independent weight.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22/:27, Fig 11 :43-53/:73, Fig 12 :46/:95/:117/:124, Fig 13 :23-24/:74.
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: per-slot, habitat-relative, bond-level, cascading, isomeric, shape not name.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ecologist's concrete question, which edge flips in which season and by how much.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not automatically survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the live slot**. The figure says the residual is per slot; it does not say which slot is live in a given dataset;
- the **order of unbundling**. Fig 9 :54 requires the union to be split before search, but the shorthand does not record which overlaps were actually separated in a given system;
- the **ground settlement**. Fig 2 :45 requires each frame to range against the ground separately; a reported "seasonal change" from one research group is a reading, not a settlement.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a structure of relations over states, changing by re-valencing bonds in a habitat that updates while the ground does not. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no interaction-level measurement was taken in this run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and commit the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
