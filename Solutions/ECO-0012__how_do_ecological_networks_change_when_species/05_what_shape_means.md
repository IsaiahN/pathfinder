## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says it *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- *Shape:* a change read per interaction, habitat-relative, bond-level, and cascading.
- *Name (not supplied by the framework):* "the pollinator species P switches from mutualism to parasitism in the dry season at site X."

The first is what this package returns. The second belongs to a measured system.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you know you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

For ECO-0012 the bootstrapping vocabulary came from the question itself: network, interaction, season, life stage, conditions. The figure text then gave closer terms: habitat, valence, bond, isomer, cascade, slot. Each narrowed the space. The stop is at a shape, not at a seasonal time series for a particular site.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* sharpens the second: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A shape that fits every network you have seen is still a shape. The empirical ecologists are not supplying a name from inside our frame; they are supplying ground readings from a frame whose closure differs, which is exactly what a provenanced import requires.

## 4. Why naming the edge from a network reading would be the forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. The essay's one-line verdict: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a network-level index (modularity, nestedness, H2') reads the network and cannot comment on which interaction produced the reading. Many seasonal rewiring histories can give the same modularity. Naming one history from the index is the metamerism error. What is owed is a per-interaction instrument, not a better summary.

## 5. Q: what licenses a particular prediction of edge change?

**Answer: nothing in the framework.** The framework returns a shape. A particular edge prediction is licensed only by a measurement under the specific condition, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the edge name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

This is where the temptation is strongest, because the ecological literature supplies an abundant vocabulary: rewiring, turnover, partner switching, phenological mismatch. Under §3 those are bindings from the ecology frame onto held relation terms. They are welcome as ground readings. They do not become framework discoveries, and they do not move the wall (Fig 6 :14).

## 5b. Correction to my own §3 and §4

I initially overstated the shape limit, as the QMGR exemplar did. The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* It governs a primitive not held in Γ. Network, time, and the relation terms are **already in the six-term set** (Fig 13 :23-24, :11-12), so selecting them is not inventing a missing primitive. What remains wrong in an uncorrected record would be calling the ecological vocabulary an import that moves the wall: Fig 6 says *"IMPORT adds an atom"*, and a binding onto held atoms adds none.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason it stands is the missing discriminator (a per-interaction, condition-resolved observation), not a ban on naming held atoms. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers respected ecologists to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let an interaction-level measurement settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* And *How Intelligence Actually Works*: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the ecological question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. What the essays add that the figures do not spell out

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives an ecological instance directly: *"when altering a habitat by unintentionally introducing a predatory threat into the ecosystem, or disturbing the balance by adding or removing vital resource points, you can inadvertently create major unpredictable consequences downstream."* It also says the slide-rule principle applies to *"ecological restoration"*. This is an instance, not a derivation: a habitat perturbation whose residual is several steps out. It corroborates Fig 11 :52-53 from an author's essay, which is the same evidence pool as the figures, so it is weak corroboration and is recorded as such in `10_source_ledger.csv`.
