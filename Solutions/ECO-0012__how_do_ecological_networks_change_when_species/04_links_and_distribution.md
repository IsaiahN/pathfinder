## Two things in the world literature that bear directly on the shape

1. **The per-slot / network-level split is live in the data.** Souza et al. (2018) report a network-level seasonal difference while finding no consistent species-level differences. Under Fig 1 :19 (*"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"*), that is a statement about a metric, not the discharge of the residual. This is a re-census, not a conflict: two different objects, one quantity used to summarise and one used to steer. Do not quote the paper as if it refuted the per-slot rule, and do not quote the figure as if it refuted the paper.

2. **"Rewiring" is an empirical result, not a framework primitive.** Lázaro and Gómez-Martínez (2022) and Yahaya et al. (2026) measure partner switching and interaction turnover. Under Fig 6, a binding onto the held bond vocabulary adds no atom and does not *"move the wall"*. Their measurements are the ground reading the figure cannot supply; their vocabulary is not a new substrate kind. Keep the two roles separate when citing them.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* and Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not corroboration.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record their verdict *before* any framework material is shown. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*, and Fig 2 :45, *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because a reader who has absorbed the source is no longer an outside frame.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits sending methods. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode before running it, because `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction" says a frame *"cannot check the direction of its own question."*

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. The stage-1 output here is the shape in `07_the_tether_answer.md` and the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`. If an ecologist fires one of the refuters, the packet is corrected before it is sent again.
