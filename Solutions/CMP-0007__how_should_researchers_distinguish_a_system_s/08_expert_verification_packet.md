# Expert verification packet - the Tether on principles versus measurement artifacts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a complex systems methodologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the protocol, `general` on framework-internal structural rules, not `grounded`.** No independent instrument is claimed to have paid for any particular pattern. This is a shape and a route, not a result, and it is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

There is no single property of a pattern that tells you whether it is a system's organizing principle or a product of the measurement and analysis choices. The distinction is made by a battery of tests whose failures are categorically different. A pattern that moves when you push on the instrument is not an anchor. Agreement between analyses that share data, metric and model class is one witness, not two. A frame cannot score itself with the patterns it produces. A pattern must be new relative to the analysis library and must pay for itself. Only the recorded lineage separates convergence from a choice made after seeing the result. The pipeline is a substituted habitat, so what it failed to reproduce and what it brought with it must both be written down. Declared thresholds must be violable, the round-trip gap between description and system must be measured, and smooth or scaling patterns may be remainders of a composition rather than primitives organizing it. The claim is that these are separate conditions with separate instruments, not degrees of one robustness score.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 1 and 10

- **Statement:** the anchor must not move when the analyst pushes on the instrument, and a quantity produced by the analysis cannot be a witness for the analysis. A convention that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :29 - "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."
  - Fig 2 :29-30 - "If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."
  - Fig 1 :27 - "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
  - Fig 10 :18 - "a declared meaning is not a grounded one"; Fig 10 :47 - "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"
  - Fig 10 :24 - "the ground does not decay, the channel does"; Fig 10 :25-26 - "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"
- **Refuter:** a pattern demonstrated to be an artifact of a shared assumption that is nevertheless invariant under every available instrument push and accepted as a principle; or a deterministic output of the fitting procedure admitted as ground evidence with no external probe that could fail; or a threshold that decides whether the pattern exists, with no independent test that could fail, still counted as grounded. Any one fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one low-overlap world check

- **Statement:** agreement means only what its independence is worth, and only the record separates a pattern found against the ground from one imposed by the analysis. Novelty is relative to the library.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 2 :31 - "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
  - Fig 2 :40 - "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"
  - Fig 8 :51 - "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"
  - Fig 6 :22 - "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world."
  - Fig 5 :42 - "The acceptance test is a bargain, not a threshold."
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Messias et al. 2026 write that "ground-truth community labels are often unavailable and most algorithms proposed in recent literature rely on the same benchmark datasets for model training and evaluation", and that generators "rely heavily on power-law degree distributions, despite recent evidence indicating that scale-free networks are rare". Does that sentence not already name both the shared-pool failure and the substituted-habitat failure, and is the remedy of a more independent benchmark anything other than the import described in Fig 6 :23? A "no" to both questions is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** two analyses drawing on one evidence pool counted as two independent confirmations; or a pattern whose lineage shows a post hoc choice of bin, metric or stopping rule still treated as settled by the same data; or a pattern derivable from the model's own atoms admitted as new with no improvement in `left(R,φ)`. Any one breaks the independence or provenance condition.

### C3 - Structure, order and the honest stop

- **Statement:** the failure conditions form a union that must be unbundled; two systems with the same atoms but different bonds are isomers, so an ingredient list cannot decide the question; patterns such as scaling and smoothness may be remainders of a composition rather than organizing primitives; the round-trip gap must be measured.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
  - Fig 12 :117 - "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."
  - Fig 13 :124-125 - "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member."
  - Fig 4 :30 - "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."
  - Fig 11 :70 - "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores all failing conditions in all failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or two systems with identical atoms and identical bonds whose patterns diverge with no difference in ground contact or imported atoms, which would move the explanation out of composition and into habitat alone; or a pattern treated as a remainder shown to be load-bearing in a law independent of the measuring transform.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument for a named system and a named pattern, not a further argument. Concretely:

1. **Instrument push:** vary the measurement while holding the system. Does the pattern persist, or move with the probe, the bin or the metric? (Fig 2 :29-30)
2. **Low-overlap witness:** find a second frame with different evidence whose closure predicts the pattern's shape before seeing it. (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :45)
3. **External probe:** name an observation that would be impossible if the pattern were purely a pipeline output, and run it. (Fig 5 :48, Fig 11 :70)
4. **Lineage audit:** were the bin, similarity metric and stopping rule fixed before the result? (Fig 8 :51, Fig 10 :47)
5. **Round trip:** carry the abstraction back down and measure what it dropped. (Fig 4 :30)
