# Outreach draft - check the output before deciding about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` protocol with no ground measurement behind any particular pattern. The legitimate ask is a failure of the protocol, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one test for measurement artifacts before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about complex systems methodology that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** there is no single property of a pattern that tells you whether it is a system's organizing principle or a product of the measurement and analysis choices. The distinction is made by a battery of separate tests, and each test fails differently. The ones I would put in front of you are these:
>
> 1. **Push on the instrument while holding the system fixed.** A principle survives the push; a pattern produced by the measurement moves with it. If it moves when you push on it, it is not the anchor.
> 2. **Use a second frame whose evidence pool does not overlap the first.** Two analyses that share the data, the metric and the model class are one witness wearing two names. Their agreement is a convention, not corroboration.
> 3. **Exclude anything the analysis produces from the witness list.** Clustering output, variance explained, compression achieved and fit statistics are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.
> 4. **Ask whether the pattern is new relative to the analysis library, and whether it pays for itself.** A pattern that is a recomposition of what the model already assumed is "the machinery worked; the answer was already known."
> 5. **Record the lineage.** Convergent derivation and a choice imposed after seeing the result look identical in the output. Only the record separates them, and a clean record still does not make the pattern right.
> 6. **Treat the pipeline as a substituted habitat.** State what it failed to reproduce from the system and what it brought with it. A synthetic solve proves wiring, not capability.
> 7. **Make every convention violable.** A bin width or threshold that decides whether the pattern exists has stopped being a convention and has become a hidden author of the result.
> 8. **Measure the round-trip gap.** The description is not the system; the gap between them is the thing to report.
>
> These are not eight degrees of one problem. They are eight conditions with different failure modes, so a blended robustness score destroys the diagnostic. What I am offering is that shape, not a name of which pattern is real in any particular system.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can tell you which pattern in your system is genuine, that any particular study is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only possible methodology. A shape is not a name.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a pattern that is later shown to be an artifact of a shared assumption, yet was invariant under every available instrument push and accepted as a principle;
> 2. two analyses drawing on one evidence pool whose agreement is counted as two independent confirmations;
> 3. a deterministic output of the fitting procedure admitted as ground evidence with no external probe that could fail;
> 4. a pattern derivable from the model's own assumptions admitted as new, with no improvement in what remains unexplained;
> 5. a pattern whose lineage shows a post hoc choice of bin, metric or stopping rule, still treated as settled by the same data.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. I will send URLs only after you have told me what you would count as a failure here. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers: the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the protocol without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35) |
| Failure modes kept separate | "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either" (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | The design-instrument rule: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device |
| Mode stated on the claim | "Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which." (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No name for the genuine pattern | "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself" (Fig 9 :32) |
| Links only after a verdict | "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it." (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A direct reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". "an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying." (Fig 10 :59).
- State the stake where it cannot be removed: this packet was produced with an LLM and is a simulated derivation. "Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it." (Fig 10 :60).
