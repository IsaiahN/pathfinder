- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The anchor is an intervention and does not update on the analyst's frame (Fig 2 :25). ## 4. Round-trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: "So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's ten-point protocol to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *a pattern is genuine when it survives the tests and the ground pays for it.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific methodology situation. Result: *run the standard robustness checks and publish the pipeline.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses the union into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, so "run the checks" becomes a checklist applied identically to every failure mode. Coming back down, it tells a researcher whose failure is a post hoc convention to collect more data, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the anchor rule. "Survives the tests" does not say that the ground, not the frame, settles the question. Coming back down, it permits a pipeline to validate itself with its own output (Fig 1 :27).
3. It loses independence. "Robustness checks" in most practice means repeated analyses in one evidence pool, which is Fig 7 :36's chain of mirrors.
4. It loses the lineage and the habitat substitution. "Publish the pipeline" records the artefact but not what was fixed before the result or what the pipeline brought with it (Fig 8 :51, Fig 11 :68).
5. It loses the remainder reading. The abstraction has no place for the possibility that the detected pattern is what a composition leaves behind (Fig 13 :124-125).

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator rather than the one-paragraph version. The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such.
