## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and "records the lineage so the stop is honest." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*, opened directly:

> "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for CMP-0007 a shape is: **the narrowed form of a method for deciding which reported regularities are the system's own and which are made by the measurement, statable before any particular criterion is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: a battery of tests whose failures are categorically different, with a named instrument and a recorded lineage for each.
- Name, withheld: "replication is the test", "pre-registration is the test", "triangulation is the test", "holdout is the test".

The distinction matters here because methodology literature is almost entirely in the naming business. Each named criterion captures one failure mode and is silent on the others. Naming one as *the* answer would be a category error of the same kind as naming a seventh substrate member (Fig 13 :43-44).

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> "If you don't have the answer to a question, it's because you are lacking certain things in the library (instruments, information) that would help, or perhaps you are asking the wrong question altogether."

Applied here: the closest available terms were "measurement artifact", "robustness", "sensitivity analysis", "reproducibility", "construct validity", "triangulation" and "pre-registration". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second." The description in effect terms (a regularity that changes when the choice changes while the system is held fixed) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"

That is the canonical warrant for treating the question as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one method question.

The anchor and the stop condition:

> "This anchor is provided by your own question: If the question is answered by your present collection of books from your search you know whether to stop or continue and which direction to turn next."

In this solution the stop is reached when each failure condition has an instrument, the union is unbundled, the round-trip gap is named and the refuters are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, "one long room: motion, no progress").

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: "But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."

For CMP-0007, neither has happened for any particular pattern. There is no instrument here that discriminates a genuine organizing principle from a carefully constructed analysis artifact in a named system. Hence the `gap` row in F11 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened directly:

> "The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."
> "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"
> "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."
> "What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

This is exactly the error a researcher makes on reading a pattern off an analysis and naming its source. A reported scaling law is a reading. It is consistent with a genuine constraint, with a generative mechanism already in the model, with a fitting choice, with a binning choice, or with a synthetic habitat built into the benchmark. The reading does not determine the source. Agreement among readers does not fix it. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

The clinical measurement review opened for `04_links_and_distribution.md` is a low-overlap instance of the same structure: a real state is present, but the reading moves with probe angle and fluid balance, and the review ends by calling for standardisation rather than by declaring the reading wrong. The figure-grounded remedy is the same in both places: an instrument that discriminates sources, not a further argument.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to methodology research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened directly:

> "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."
> "You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time."

The second quote is the author's own agent-level instance of Fig 7 :36 ("A chain of mirrors is one long room") and Fig 2 :35 ("Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness. It is recorded as an illustration of the rule.

For outreach this means: a list of famous complex systems researchers who agree with the protocol would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any pattern as a genuine principle. That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the analysis library. If a pattern is already expressible in the model's atoms, naming it is a binding from the analysis frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding settles the question without a source-discriminating instrument (Fig 6 :21, "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom").

The `gap` in F11 is therefore narrower than I first wrote: not "we may never say a pattern is real", but "we cannot say which pattern is real for this system with the instruments held here". Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, opened directly:

> "The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."
> "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post."
> "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about method. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For CMP-0007 the canonical shape is: **a union of independent failure conditions, each with a named instrument and a pre-registered refuter, run before any criterion is named, with the round-trip gap measured and the lineage recorded.** It is not a single test, not a robustness score, and not a name for the pattern that wins in any unexamined system. Which pattern occupies that position is an instrument-bound `gap`, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
