# Solution CMP-0007 - figure-grounded package

**Question:** How should researchers distinguish a system's organizing principles from patterns produced by measurement or analysis choices?

**Source:** `Questions/complexity science.txt` line 10.

**Section:** 1. Foundations, definitions, and explanation.

**Mode:** `specified` on the methodological protocol, `general` on framework-internal structural rules. Not `grounded`: no measurement here settles which pattern in a given system is genuine. Every substantive claim carries a mode in the files below.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending.

**Status of this whole package:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. Per *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-22): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This package cannot settle the question it reports on.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of researchers by likelihood of agreeing with the protocol.
- No bulk-send plan, no mail merge, no forwarded list.
- No claim that this package is a completed or publishable methodology paper.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." (Fig 2 :35). A list selected for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless.
2. "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." (Fig 1 :27). The central failure this question is about is exactly a frame using its own output as evidence. A campaign that gathers endorsements repeats that failure at the level of the field.
3. "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the source.

Legally: unsolicited bulk outreach to personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in venues avoid that failure and the structural one together.
