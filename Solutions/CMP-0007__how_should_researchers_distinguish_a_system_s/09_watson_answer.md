# WATSON answer: how to distinguish organizing principles from measurement artifacts

**Mode: `specified` on the protocol, `general` on framework-internal rules, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself.") it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what researchers should count as a system's organizing principle rather than a pattern produced by measurement or analysis choices. Option A: name one criterion, for example replication or pre-registration, and apply it as the test. Option B: keep the figure-derived shape, which is a union of independent failure conditions each with its own instrument, and refuse to name a single winning criterion. What each leads to: A produces a checklist that scores the frame with quantities the frame produces and mistakes same-pool agreement for corroboration; B produces a checkable protocol, pre-registered refuters and an open slot for the pattern that a source-discriminating instrument would fill.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A reported regularity P changes when the measurement and analysis choice M changes while the system S is held fixed. Arity: at least three places (S, M, observation), so it is a relation, not a property of data alone. Symmetry: asymmetric; changing M is not changing S, and that asymmetry is what the question exploits. Scale: the slot is a local reading, the pattern is a population or time structure, and the analysis transform crosses the membrane (Fig 4). "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." (Fig 1 :19).
- **D1 The system ground.** Actors: the system, its states and structures, its habitat. Currency: what the ground settles under intervention. Anchor: an instrument or intervention that does not move when the analyst's frame changes (Fig 2 :29). Admitted.
- **D2 The measurement and analysis frame.** Actors: instrument, metric, transform, pipeline, code, analyst. Currency: fitted residual, compression achieved, pattern counts. Anchor: none inside the frame; its only legitimate anchor is the external ground. Admitted as a frame, barred from verdict authority by Fig 1 :27.
- **D3 The pattern as composition.** Actors: atoms and the bonds between them. Currency: arrangement, `|φ|`, `left(R,φ)`. Anchor: settlement against the ground and an instrument that discriminates isomers (Fig 12 :117). Admitted.
- **D4 Independent witnesses.** Actors: a second frame with a differing closure, a second instrument. Currency: agreement under low evidence overlap. Anchor: the ground triangulated (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47). Admitted.
- **D5 The channel across choices and time.** Actors: percepts, metrics, conventions, thresholds. Currency: contact with the ground. Anchor: the ground that persists while the channel decays (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **X1 "The pattern is self-evident in the data".** No anchor independent of the analysis that made it salient. Not a domain yet.
- **X2 "A fit statistic is the verdict".** A convention inside D2, a budget filter not a witness (Fig 9 :60). Absorbed into D2.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry (Fig 3). Within step 7, SEARCHED first, then INWARD and OUTWARD as opposed tracks never read against one another (Fig 6 :31).

Strain test applied: an early attempt to apply Fig 13's six physical substrate terms to complex-systems patterns fit only by renaming, so re-census sends Fig 13 to the honest-stop and derived-remainder role (S1 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6). No unresolved strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; naming one criterion does not. A reported regularity is a candidate organizing principle only if it persists under a push on the instrument (Fig 2 :29-30), is confirmed by a witness with low evidence overlap (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47), is not a quantity the analysis produces (Fig 1 :27), is novel relative to the library and pays the MDL bargain (Fig 6 :22, Fig 5 :42), has a lineage that separates convergence from choice (Fig 8 :51), survives the habitat substitution the pipeline performs (Fig 11 :70), is not decided by a declared convention (Fig 10 :47), and is reported with a measured round-trip gap (Fig 4 :30). Smoothness, scaling and discreteness may be remainders of a composition rather than primitives (Fig 13 :124-125). The failure conditions form a union and are never averaged (Fig 9 :57). Which pattern occupies that position in a named system is a declared `gap`: the source-discriminating instrument is not held here. Mode `specified` on the protocol, `general` on framework-internal rules.

- **cost if wrong:** a field adopts a false pattern as a principle, or discards a real one as an artifact, and spends its budget on instruments aimed at the wrong condition. "agreement compounds, evidence does not" (Fig 7 :13) across a chain of analyses that keep the same frame; the cost is a literature that is internally consistent and ungrounded. The cost is bounded and locatable because the refuters and the lineage are written down, and it is avoided by leading with the shape claim and its refuter (Fig 2 :42, "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the shape fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6 :42-43). The protocol and its refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a pattern shown to be an artifact of a shared assumption yet invariant under every available push (breaks the anchor test); two same-pool analyses counted as independent, or a low-overlap witness dismissed without reason (breaks independence); a fitting output admitted as ground evidence with no external probe (breaks the frame rule); a post hoc choice of bin or metric treated as settled (breaks provenance and convention); a remainder shown to be load-bearing in a transform-independent law (breaks the remainder reading); or a figure re-census that admits a seventh substrate member. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
