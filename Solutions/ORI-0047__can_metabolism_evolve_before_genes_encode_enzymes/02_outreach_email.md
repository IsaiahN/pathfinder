**Subject:** One output on metabolism-before-genes to check against your own ground

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified` on the three domain verdicts, `general` on the shape, `gap` on two items, not `grounded`):** the question "can metabolism evolve before genes encode enzymes?" is not one question. It is a union of three conditions with three different grounds:
>
> 1. Can a chemical reaction network persist and be differentially retained without a gene? Under the figures, yes at the structural level: a catalyst that survives its own use, a loop that can close, and a gradient that can be spent are all it takes for selection to run where no one maintains anything.
> 2. Can that network *accumulate adaptations* across generations? The figures make progress per handoff a product of two factors: the fidelity of the bracket across the handoff and the ground's pressure on the room. Set either to zero and the chain runs in place. Chemistry supplies the pressure. The missing instrument is the fidelity of transmission in a pre-genetic compartment.
> 3. How does a hereditary encoding appear? The figures say a closed set of molecule kinds can be rearranged without end but cannot add a new kind of atom. Only an import moves that wall. So the shape of the missing thing is a hereditary carrier with sequence-like storage and templated copying, imported into the habitat that chemistry prepared.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the carrier *is* RNA, PNA, a coenzyme-lipid assembly, or any named molecule. The framework returns a shape, not a name. I am also not claiming that pre-genetic metabolism already underwent full Darwinian evolution, or that the historical order has been established. Both are gaps with named discriminators, not silences. I am not claiming this is grounded; no reactor result or phylogenetic measurement is asserted here.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before this message:**
> 1. a self-maintaining network with no differential retention among variants under a non-updating ground;
> 2. a compartmentalized chemical network that shows a heritable response to selection whose residual trends down across generations, under an assay that does not update on the network's own report;
> 3. a hereditary polymer derived purely by re-arranging the molecule kinds already in a pre-genetic network, with no new kind introduced;
> 4. a result showing template polymers preceded any self-sustaining network in the same lineage.
>
> If any holds, the relevant verdict is withdrawn. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
