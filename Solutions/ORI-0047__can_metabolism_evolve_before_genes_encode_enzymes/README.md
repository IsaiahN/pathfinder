## The question as an effect

The ordinary question hides a union. "Metabolism evolve" names two different effects: (1) a chemical reaction network persists, grows and is differentially retained, and (2) a lineage accumulates adaptations across generations with heritable variation. "Before genes encode enzymes" fixes an ordering that the question inherits rather than derives.

- **Option A.** A reaction network is itself a unit of selection with heritable variation, so adaptations accumulate in the network before any template gene. Leads to a metabolism-first origin where selection acts directly on networks or compartments.
- **Option B.** A reaction network can persist and change chemically but cannot accumulate adaptations without a hereditary encoding. Leads to a metabolism-first habitat that supplies chemistry and energy, then hands the actual evolutionary process to a genetic polymer.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: a three-condition separator with a different anchor per condition, a shape for the missing hereditary carrier, and a stated refuter for each condition. This is written down before the run and treated as an ordinary term (THE_FORMULA "Direction").

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes institutional officer addresses; those are deliberately not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`. The public society contact and journal submission routes are used instead.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan. Contact is one to one through opt-in or public venues.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words: a room does not add an anchor (Fig 2 :29); agreement that shares an evidence pool is disqualified as a verdict (Fig 2 :40); and a verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it (Fig 10 :42).
