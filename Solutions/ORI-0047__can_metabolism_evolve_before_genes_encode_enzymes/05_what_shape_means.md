## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 - "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134 - "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", followed by Fig 13 :135 - "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- Shape: *a hereditary carrier with sequence-like storage and templated copying must be imported into a prepared metabolic habitat before cumulative evolution can proceed.*
- Name (not returned by the framework): RNA, PNA, a coenzyme-lipid composome, a peptide-nucleic scaffold.

The distinction decides how the answer may be written. "Metabolism prepared the way for a genetic carrier" is a shape. "Metabolism prepared the way for RNA" is a binding from a world frame and must be provenanced as such.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For G1 that means an experiment that discriminates among carrier candidates, not another argument.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming RNA is an import from the genetics-first frame. It has a source gate (the world literature) but no shadow test that explains the specific residual of a pre-genetic network. Until it has one, it is a candidate, not an answer.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

The molecule set a metabolism-first scenario declares is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Naming one of its members as *the* first hereditary carrier turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why the discriminator matters more than the argument

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it is the most useful essay for this question. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; the reading is consistent with one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* The resolution came from the spectrometer, not from argument: *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Also: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: a reactor yield of pyruvate or an autocatalytic core count is a reading. Several chemical paths can produce it. It cannot, by itself, comment on whether the underlying process was heritable evolution, chemical relaxation, or a catalytic network with no heredity. What is owed for D2 is a discriminator: an assay whose response to selection can be separated from the background dynamics. That is the instrument named in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` §5 and in gap G2.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 and §4 (kept visible)

I initially wrote that the shape-not-a-name rule forbids naming *any* actor in the slot. That is an overstatement. The canonical phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. Cores, catalysts, gradients and compartments are already held or directly observed, so naming them is a binding, not the invention of a missing primitive.

What remains forbidden, and remains the live point, is naming the **hereditary carrier**. Relative to a metabolite-only pre-genetic library it is genuinely missing, so Fig 13 :135 governs it. The metamerism point in §4 is also not retracted; its reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held actors. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

A second overstatement, corrected: I initially read *"natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground"* as "selection is sufficient for evolution." It is not. Fig 7 :37-39 makes fidelity and ground pressure a product. The essay's own "not just evolution" sentence agrees. Both claims are kept with their proper objects.
