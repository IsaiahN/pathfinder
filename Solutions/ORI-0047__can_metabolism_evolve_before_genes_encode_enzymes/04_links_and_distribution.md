## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **Kauffman 1971 / 1986, autocatalytic sets**, and **Hordijk and Steel RAF theory**: the mathematical background for a self-sustaining, food-generated reaction network. The RAF definition used in Xavier et al. 2020 is the precise form. Relevant to D1, not to heredity on its own.
- **Eigen 1971, the hypercycle**, and **Eigen and Schuster 1977**: the classic template-free replication-and-selection models. Quoted inside Vasas et al. 2010 as motivation and as the reason Eigen discarded autocatalytic protein sets for lack of inheritance.
- **Maynard Smith 1986, The Problems of Biology**: the three properties of a unit of evolution (multiplication, variation, heredity), quoted inside Vasas et al. 2012. This is the closest external statement of the Fig 7 two-factor condition.
- **Gánti, Wächtershäuser, Lancet and Segré (GARD)**, **Fernando and Rowe**, and **Russell's alkaline vent model**: the four metabolism-first scenarios reviewed in Tessera 2018. Their shared weakness in that review is heredity, which is the finding of this audit's D2.
- **Sutherland 2009 and subsequent prebiotic nucleotide synthesis work**: cited in Tessera 2018 as the genetics-first route's answer to monomer availability, and as contested within that field. Relevant to G1 only as a candidate, not as a figure output.

These are background pointers for a checker, not sources this derivation relied on for its verdict. Overlap among the origins-of-life items is high and is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the three-condition separator and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42 - "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."** A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 - "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the articles.
- **Fig 2 :40 - "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."** A list selected for people who already favour metabolism-first produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Seat map: high correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention.** No receptiveness rank.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three-condition separator and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer before any theory is shown; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (with an optional third stage)

1. **Stage 1.** Validate the output against the ground: does the separator hold, and does any pre-registered refuter fire? This is Fig 10 :52 - "Bring a characterised residual, never the source" - and Fig 2 :45 - "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the two central papers. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3, a method to reuse.** The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must write down what would refute their own result before running it, and label the mode of what they get, because THE_FORMULA "Direction" says a frame cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if the stage-1 output survives. Strains S3 (the "before" ordering) and G2 (whether a chemical network can sustain fidelity) are live, and the email says so. Do not present an open gap as a result.
