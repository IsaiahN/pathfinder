# Expert verification packet - the Tether on metabolism before genes

**What this is:** a claim in a form a checker in origins-of-life research can test. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the three per-domain verdicts, `general` on the shape, `gap` on G1 to G3. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question is a union of three conditions, not one question with a yes or no answer. A self-maintaining reaction network with a regenerating catalyst, a closing loop and a spendable gradient can be differentially retained without any gene, so the narrow "yes" holds for persistence and chemical selection. Cumulative Darwinian evolution is a two-factor quantity: transmission fidelity across a generation multiplied by the ground's pressure on variants. Chemistry supplies the pressure, but whether a pre-genetic compartment has sufficient fidelity is an open measurement, with the world literature leaning toward limited heredity only. The step to gene-encoded enzymes requires an import of a new kind of atom, a hereditary carrier with sequence-like storage and templated copying, into the habitat that chemistry prepared. The figure set returns that shape but refuses to name the carrier. Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal plus chemistry-facing; checkable by reading Fig 11, 12 and 13

- **Statement:** a reaction network can persist and be differentially retained with no gene, because a catalyst survives its own use, a cycle can close, and energy plus a gradient compose a flow. No maintainer is required for selection to run.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :119 - "catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is." Fig 12 :121 - "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 13 :76 - "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." Fig 11 :36 - "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."
- **World check:** does autocatalytic, food-generated network organization exist in primordial-style metabolism, and can core reactions run without enzymes? Compare Xavier et al. 2020 and the Preiner/Martin/Moran reactor results reported by Callier 2025.
- **Refuter:** a self-maintaining network with no differential retention among variants under a non-updating ground; or a network whose self-maintenance requires no re-entrant loop; or a demonstration that encoded enzymes are required for the catalyst role at this level.

### C2 - the heredity condition; checkable by an experimental evolution design

- **Statement:** progress per handoff is a product of two factors, transmission fidelity and ground pressure. Either at zero and the chain runs in place. A network with low fidelity can change, or show limited selectable heredity, but not open-ended accumulation. The response must be read per compartment or core, never pooled.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 7 :37 - "Progress per handoff has two factors. Zero either and the chain runs in place:" Fig 7 :38 - "the bracket's fidelity across the handoff" Fig 7 :39 - "the ground's pressure on the room" Fig 1 :19 - "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 4 :22 - "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."
- **What to check:** does the field's own evidence show a heritable response to selection that persists above background dynamics across generations in a compartmentalized chemical system? Vasas et al. 2012 say yes under a specific structure (multiple cores, gain by rare reactions, loss at division); Vasas et al. 2010 say compositional genomes lack evolvability; Tessera 2018 says pre-Darwinian evolution is not plausible without heritable replicators. This is a live disagreement, and the packet records it rather than averaging it.
- **Refuter:** a heritable response to artificial selection whose residual trends down across generations under an assay that does not update on the network's own report. If that fires, D2 flips to yes and the packet withdraws its condition.
- **Named instrument:** an assay in which the selection response is separable from the background chemical dynamics. Without it, a reading of "evolution" is a metamerism error (see §5).

### C3 - framework-internal derivation of the import

- **Statement:** a hereditary polymer is a new kind of atom relative to a metabolite-only library. Re-arranging the held molecules cannot produce it; only an import moves that wall. Once settled, it becomes an atom for the next level, which is how gene-encoded enzymes become primitives for the cell.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :21 - "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :22 - "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure." Fig 8 :32 - "no search, however long, adds an atom" Fig 13 :115 - "The set is closed. The arrangements are not. That gap is why import exists." Fig 12 :104 - "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world"
- **Refuter:** a demonstration that a template-capable hereditary polymer is composable from the molecule kinds already held in a pre-genetic network, with no new kind introduced. Alternatively, a figure re-census showing that a different quantity governs the wall here (the closure strain S1 is already named in `01` §7).

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the carrier is RNA, PNA, a coenzyme-lipid composome or any named molecule. Fig 13 :135 - "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."
- Not that selection alone is evolution. Fig 7 :37-39 governs the two-factor product.
- Not that metabolism caused genes or was historically prior in a demonstrated lineage. The "before" is a question parameter (Fig 12 :30 defines sequence); historical order is gap G3.
- Not that catalyst equals encoded enzyme. Same functional role, different level and actors (Fig 10 :68).
- Not that the vent habitat is the ground. Fig 11 :73 - "The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."
- Not an isomer claim without its condition. Metabolism-first and genetics-first are isomers only over a fixed shared atom set; with a new polymer absent from the metabolite closure the relation is composition plus import (falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q3).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.

## 5. What would settle it

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; metamerism means several paths can give the same reading; *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

1. **D1:** a reactor comparison establishing differential retention among otherwise identical networks under a non-updating ground, or its absence.
2. **D2:** artificial selection on compartmentalized networks with an assay whose response is separable from background dynamics. A downward-trending residual fires R2; a flat result with intact background settles the limit.
3. **D3:** an experiment identifying a hereditary atom in a metabolite-only system and testing whether it is composable from held molecules. This would name the carrier and close G1.
4. **G3:** geochemical and phylogenetic reconstruction of the order of events. This is a world ground, not a figure output.

Standing refuters R1 to R5 are in `01` §5 and repeated in `09`.
