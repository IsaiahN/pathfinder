## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0051 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about corporate decision procedure that can be stated before any particular weighting, legal form or stakeholder priority is named.** It constrains; it does not fill.

- Shape: *keep each slot's residual against its own ground; enumerate the habitat from contact; keep each channel reachable; keep reach renewed from differing closures; keep verdicts separate.*
- Name, withheld: *shareholder primacy wins, or stakeholder balancing wins, or a 60/30/10 weighting, or the benefit-corporation form is the answer.*

The distinction matters here because governance and business ethics are largely in the naming business. Friedman names the owners' agent; Jensen names long-run firm value as the scorecard; the Business Roundtable names five stakeholder commitments. Those are bindings from the governance frame onto slots, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "shareholder primacy," "stakeholder theory," "enlightened value maximization," "balanced scorecard," "corporate purpose," "governance washing" and "rigidity trap." None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The effect description (which slots are counted, which ground settles each) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0051 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with one optimal weighting.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, the enumeration rule is given, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name, and why the weights are not licensed

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0051, no weighting has settled at a ground. The multiobjective-optimization literature is explicit that a weighted sum requires the decision maker to supply the weights, and that Pareto-optimal solutions are incomparable without additional preference information. That is an import of a *requirement*, not an import of the weights themselves. Jensen's "long-run firm value as the tradeoff criterion" is a candidate import from the finance frame, but it carries no source gate or shadow test for the corporate-governance claim, and it conflicts with the per-slot indexing requirement if read as the whole answer. Hence the `gap` rows in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error a governance explanation makes when it reads a high return or a signed stakeholder pledge and names its source. A profit reading is consistent with a protected asset, a paying habitat, a temporary information asymmetry, an accounting choice, regulatory favour, or luck. The reading does not determine the source. The Business Roundtable pledge is a second case: the same reading ("we committed to stakeholders") is consistent with actual governance change and with a public-relations move, and Bebchuk and Tallarita 2023 supply the document-level instrument that separates the two. Neither a profit number nor a pledge settles what the firm did. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have.

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to corporate governance

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about firms.

For outreach this means: a list of famous governance scholars or celebrated CEOs who endorse the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any weighting is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. A preference weight is not a missing primitive; it is an ordinary term that can be bound, stated and tested like any other (Formula, "Direction"). What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework *derives* the weight, or that a derived weight would be `grounded` without a ground reading. What is required is provenance: a declared preference imported from a frame whose closure differs, recorded as a convention rather than mistaken for a fact about the world (Fig 10 conventions). Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about corporate governance. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0051 the canonical shape is: **a per-slot procedure with two distinct failure modes, an enumerated habitat with relative valence, a declared and provenanced preference for any weighting, and an instrument boundary where the source-discriminator is missing.** It is not a doctrine of shareholder primacy, not a doctrine of stakeholder primacy, not a weighted index, and not a ranking of legal forms. The names that appear in governance literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
