# The Tether's answer on shareholder returns versus stakeholder balance

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No governance measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain how a corporation's decision procedure should count what is happening to the parties in contact with it. R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| is indexed per slot, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **which slots are counted and which ground settles each**, not about which doctrine has the better slogan.

The question is a union and must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is two conditions plus a gap:

- **C1:** each slot's channel to its own ground stays reachable.
- **C2:** the firm's reach keeps moving outward through contact with closures that differ from its own.
- **G:** the weights that would settle a genuine conflict between slots are not in the closure and are declared as a gap.

## 2. The shape

1. **A single-slot objective fails the anchor test for the whole question.** Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* The question names workers, customers, suppliers and communities, so a market price is the anchor of the capital slot and an instrument on one axis for the whole (Fig 2 :37-38). Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Shareholder return stays visible; it is not promoted.
2. **A blended stakeholder score is the same error with more terms.** Fig 1 :28: *"A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Balance is licensed only as separate verdicts per slot.
3. **The parties are a habitat to be enumerated, not a list to be ranked.** Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :49-51: valence is relative to the residual and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Second-order contacts count, because contact cascades (Fig 11 :52-53).
4. **Quick loss is channel failure.** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; :26: *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*; :29: *"→ keep the anchor reachable"*. A board that stops hearing what pays is not facing a changed ground; it has lost touch. Goodhart's law, from a low-overlap field, says the same thing about a metric made into a target.
5. **Long persistence is reach renewed from differing closures.** Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: closure is idempotent, so no amount of internal search adds an atom. Workers who leave, customers who switch, entrants, regulators and adjacent technologies are the operators that move the wall, not distractions from the core.
6. **The strength and the rigidity are the same object.** Fig 11 :50: the same actor is *"a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another."* Fig 12 :119: a catalyst is a part that survives its own use, and *"nothing records which a term is."* A buyback, a cost programme or a supply contract can change sign when the residual changes.
7. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Investor consensus, analyst consensus and ESG ratings are rooms, not anchors. The BRT pledge and the governance documents that failed to change are two readings of one claim, and a document-level instrument separated them (Bebchuk and Tallarita 2023).
8. **"Maximize" states one comparison; "balance" states a family.** Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* The figures contain no operator that composes a family of comparisons into one. The weights must be imported and declared; the optimization literature confirms from an independent field that a weighted sum needs a decision maker's preference. The framework does not supply it.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No prospective governance measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which weighting, which legal form, or which party should prevail in a specific conflict. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average. No claim about what percentage of value goes to whom. Such numbers are filters for budget, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that shareholders, directors, culture or resources are irrelevant. They are Γ and D2. The claim is that one slot's anchor does not settle a union (Fig 2 :36).
- Not that stakeholder primacy has been proved. The pledge-versus-documents result refuses that inference.
- Not a theory of everything about business. It stops where the instrument stops.
- Not corroboration by agreement. Friedman, Jensen and the Business Roundtable share one broad evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40). The low-overlap sources from measurement theory, optimization and ecology corroborate the mechanism and the gap, not the corporate verdict.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of green *"cannot comment on the source,"* and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the readings are return, pledge and rating; the candidate sources are a protected asset, a paying habitat, a temporary asymmetry, governance change, or luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. a single shareholder-return measure predicts, prospectively and per firm without pooling, which stakeholder relation fails next;
2. an aggregate stakeholder index preserves the per-slot diagnostic and pays at the ground;
3. a fixed priority list of stakeholder claims survives a documented change of the firm's residual;
4. a governance metric stays discriminating after a decade as the sole compensation target;
5. board and investor agreement is an independent anchor for what parties actually received;
6. a firm renews its reach purely by internal composition across a habitat change, with no new atom from outside;
7. a governance-form change reproduces every required contact and introduces no unchosen actor, both measured;
8. a derivation inside the figures yields the weights that settle a genuine inter-slot conflict.

A prospective study that measures C1 and C2 separately per slot, records the bonds as well as the atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing things are a discriminator of sources and a declared preference. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The two conditions in section 2 are a shape; the weight that decides a genuine conflict is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side between shareholder primacy and stakeholder balancing, and it does not claim the final answer on corporate purpose. It says that the gap between what a firm predicts and what actually happens has to be read per party, never averaged: one market return is the right ground for the capital slot, but the question is constitutively about workers, customers, suppliers and communities too, so one price cannot anchor the whole, and a blended score throws away the signal that says which relation is failing. The parties are a habitat to be enumerated from contact, not a fixed priority list, and the same arrangement can change sign when the situation changes, so a strength and a rigidity can be the same object. What is offered is that shape, eight falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which weight should settle a genuine conflict, and whether a corporation should maximize anything as a final verdict, stay open until a source-discriminating instrument and a declared preference exist, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
