# Outreach draft - validate the output first, then decide about the theory

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send. No personal emails are used; the recipient must have an opt-in or public institutional route.

**Hard precondition:** the stage-1 output must survive the recipient's check before any theory is shown. The output here is a `specified` shape with no ground measurement, so the legitimate ask is a refutation of the shape, never endorsement of it. If the recipient cannot be shown a failure condition they can actually test, do not send.

---

**Subject:** Check one claim about corporate purpose before you decide whether any of this is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one narrow claim about corporate governance that I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. I am asking for a failure. If the claim does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The claim (`specified`, not `grounded`):** neither "primarily maximize shareholder returns" nor "balance stakeholders by aggregating them" is the right decision procedure. A corporation has several slots in contact with its decisions: capital providers, workers, customers, suppliers, communities. The residual, the gap between what the firm's routines predict and what actually happens, has to be read per slot. A single shareholder-return measure is a legitimate ground for the capital slot, but the question is constitutively about the other parties too, so one market number cannot anchor the whole. A blended stakeholder score discards the very signal that says which relation is failing.
>
> The shape that survives is procedural: keep each slot's residual against its own ground, enumerate the parties in contact with the decision including second-order effects, keep the channel to each ground reachable (stale, saturated and pooled readings are the named failure modes), and keep the firm's reach moving outward through contact with ways of working that differ from its own. These are two conditions with different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. A governance arrangement that produced returns can change sign when the situation changes, so the strength and the rigidity can be the same object.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that I can tell you the right weights for a genuine conflict between a worker and a shareholder, that stakeholder primacy has been proved, that shareholder primacy is trivial, that any particular company is a case in point, that this is settled by evidence, or that it is the only account of corporate purpose. The framework returns a shape, not a name or a weight. The normative weights are a declared gap, and I am not filling it with my own preference.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. a single shareholder-return measure predicts, in advance and per firm without pooling, which stakeholder relation fails next;
> 2. an aggregate stakeholder index preserves the per-slot diagnostic and pays at the ground;
> 3. a fixed priority list of stakeholder claims survives a documented change in what the firm is doing;
> 4. a governance metric stays discriminating after a decade as the sole compensation target;
> 5. board and investor agreement is shown to be an independent anchor for what parties actually received, not a room agreeing with itself;
> 6. a firm renews its reach purely by internal composition across a habitat change, with no new atom from outside;
> 7. a governance-form change reproduces every required contact and introduces no unchosen actor, both measured.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the claim. A single line saying which one holds and why is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the three public sources below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
>
> - Figure text with line numbers (I can point to the exact lines): the local canonical figure text in this solution folder.
> - Formula and usage rule: the `figures` folder on the public `general-agent` branch of the `tether` repository.
> - The essays on source, search shape and the prestige/action separation: my Substack archive.
>
> I will send URLs only after you tell me what you would count as a failure.
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is public. Run it on a governance problem you know well and can check against a real ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory last | *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* (Fig 10 :52). The recipient checks the claim without holding the framework's interior |
| Do not ask for agreement | *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35) |
| Two conditions kept separate | *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9 :57); a blended score is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) |
| Pre-registered refuter | *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device"* |
| Mode stated on the claim | *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* (`THE_FORMULA.md`) |
| No weights and no named winner | *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32) |
| Market limited to its own slot | *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."* (Fig 2 :36) |
| Stake declared | *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."* (Fig 10 :60) |
| Links only after a verdict | Fig 10 :42, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Once the recipient has the framework, their later agreement is not corroboration (Fig 2 :40) |
| Prestige kept out of the ask | *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-22) |
| Offer the formula as a method, not an answer | *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22) |
| Ask the user for their own refuter | Formula, "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; make the sought-for shape an ordinary term |

## Sending rules

- One recipient at a time. No distribution lists, no mail-merge, no forwarding chain.
- Use only a public institutional contact, a call for submissions, or an address the person has published themselves. Record the source and date.
- A Track B style reply is a conversation, never corroboration. If a recipient returns a refutation, copy it into `11_audit.md` with provenance and have the auditor seat re-evaluate it as if it came from any other source.
- Do not follow up with the theory after a "no". An interest in the outcome is unavoidable; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59).
