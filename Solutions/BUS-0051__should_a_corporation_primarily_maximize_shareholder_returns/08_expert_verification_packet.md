# Expert verification packet - the Tether on shareholder returns versus stakeholder balance

**What this is:** a claim in a form a corporate-governance researcher, business ethicist or empirically minded director can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No prospective governance measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7).

**Boundary of the check.** The first three sections are the whole stage-1 ask. Provenance, links and the method offer come later and only if a verdict on stage 1 is recorded first.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Neither "primarily maximize shareholder returns" nor "balance stakeholders by aggregating them" is the right decision procedure. The residual, the gap between what a firm's routines predict and what actually happens, has to be read per party. A market return is a legitimate ground for the capital slot, but the question is constitutively about workers, customers, suppliers and communities too, so one price cannot anchor the whole; a blended stakeholder score discards the per-slot signal that says which relation is failing. The shape that survives is procedural: enumerate the parties in contact with the decision including second-order effects, keep each slot's channel to its own ground reachable, keep the firm's reach moving outward through contact with ways of working that differ from its own, and keep the verdicts separate. These are two conditions with different instruments, not two degrees of one problem. The arrangement that produced returns can change sign when the situation changes. The framework does not supply the weights that would settle a genuine conflict; that is a declared gap, not a hidden answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal plus one governance-facing check

- **Statement:** a single market return is the anchor of the capital slot only; promoting it to the whole question fails the anchor test, and aggregating the other slots destroys the diagnostic signal.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
  - Fig 1 :28 - *"The gap is measured per object, not per step. A system that averages across objects has thrown away the only signal that says where to look."*
  - Fig 2 :36 - *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*
  - Fig 2 :37-38 - *"It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Friedman 1970 says the executive is *"an employee of the owners of the business"* with responsibility *"to make as much money as possible while conforming to the basic rules of the society, both those embodied in law and those embodied in ethical custom."* Jensen 2001 says *"purposeful behavior requires a single‐valued objective function."* Is either sentence a claim about which slots exist, or a claim about whose agent the executive is and how to keep score? A "the former" answer is a real refutation of the mapping.
- **Refuter:** a prospective, per-firm study in which a single shareholder-return measure predicts, without pooling, which stakeholder relation fails next; or an aggregate stakeholder index that preserves the per-slot diagnostic and pays at the ground. Either fires against C1.

### C2 - Framework-internal derivation plus one measurement check

- **Statement:** quick loss is failure of the channel, not decay of the ground; renewal of reach requires contact with a closure that differs, because composition inside a closure cannot add an atom; internal agreement is not evidence.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*
  - Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."*
  - Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*
  - Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*
- **What a researcher can check against their own knowledge:** Bebchuk and Tallarita 2023 report that *"our findings support the view that the BRT Statement was mostly for show and that BRT Companies joining it did not intend or expect it to bring about any material changes in how they treat stakeholders."* Does that document-level result not separate the pledge from the treatment, as a source-discriminating instrument should? A "no" is a real refutation of the mapping. Goodhart's law, from a different field, says an observed regularity *"will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes"* when it is used for control.
- **Refuter:** a governance metric that remains discriminating after a decade as the sole compensation target; or a firm that renews its reach purely by internal composition across a habitat change with no new atom; or board agreement shown to be an independent anchor for what parties actually received. Any one breaks C2.

### C3 - Structure, order and the gap

- **Statement:** the union must be unbundled before a lever is chosen; the parties are an enumerated habitat with valence relative to the residual; "maximize" and "balance" are members of one functional group as usually stated and isomers only over a full shared atom set; the normative weights are a declared gap.
- **Source, verbatim:**
  - Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
  - Fig 11 :43 - *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."*; Fig 11 :51 - *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
  - Fig 12 :117 - *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*
  - Fig 12 :112 - *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."*
  - Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*
- **Refuter:** one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue; or a fixed priority list that survives a change of residual and pays at the ground; or a derivation inside the figures that yields the weights. The last would close the gap and change the verdict from `gap` to `specified`.

## 5. What would settle it

A prospective, per-slot study that measures C1 and C2 separately, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which condition failed before the decline. Concretely:

1. **Channel test:** does the firm's reading of a party degrade while the firm keeps paying someone elsewhere? Indicators: stale percepts, saturated metrics, pooled readings (Fig 10 :25-26).
2. **Pressure and fidelity test:** are bracket fidelity and ground pressure both nonzero at each handoff? Fig 7 :37-39 says zero either stops the chain.
3. **Import test:** did any new atom enter the firm's reach from a closure that differs, before the period of persistence? If not, C2 is refuted.
4. **Source-discriminating instrument:** can the study distinguish a protected asset from a paying habitat from luck when all three produce the same return reading? The source essay says a reading alone *"cannot comment on the source."*
5. **Preference provenance:** if a weighting is used, is it declared as a convention with a named source, or is it silently treated as a fact about the world? Fig 10 :47 says a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one.
