## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between "the institution produced the change" and the co-occurrence of institution and outcome. Arity: four named outcomes, so a **union compound** (Fig 9 :57, a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either), not one hard question. Symmetry: the asserted bond is ordered, so a symmetric conjunction cannot express it (Fig 12 :57, :72, :77). Scale: per unit and per period first, cross-setting transport second. What is unavailable is the bond, not more data.
- **Domain 1, observational association.** Actors: variables, samples, estimators. Currency: covariance. Anchor: none nameable for causation. Verdict: a filter, not a witness.
- **Domain 2, design with exogenous variation.** Actors: assignment, cutoff, shock, treatment and comparison. Currency: variation that does not respond to the outcome. Anchor: a source that does not move when pushed. Verdict: the decisive identification frame.
- **Domain 3, outcome measurement.** Actors: audits, surveys, administrative records, self-reports. Currency: contact with the real outcome. Anchor: the actual outcome, measured by something the institution cannot move. Verdict: load-bearing and separate from D2.
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: expert readers, replications, registers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: evidence only when closures differ.
- **Domain 5, transport.** Actors: settings and periods. Currency: a carried estimate. Anchor: the ground in the new setting. Verdict: nonzero round-trip gap.

Order by dependency: measure the outcome per slot; enumerate the rival bonds; state what would count as a refusal; choose a non-updating source; register the refuter before looking; select a different closure for replication; measure the round trip.

My census matches the primary's five domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived the gates from Fig 12 and Fig 2, I derived them from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 12's bond theory. Different starting figures, same boundaries; low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weakly non-circular inside the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 12 :46-47 and :95-96 (bond not in operands), Fig 2 :29-33 (non-updating anchor), Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 4 :30 (transport gap).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: name the bond, require a source that does not update, measure outside the institution's own reporting, read per unit, pre-register the refuter, count witnesses not volume.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete design claim, for example "a non-manipulable eligibility cutoff identifies the effect of this reform on trust in this city".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **manipulability of the running variable** (a cutoff is only an anchor if units cannot sort around it; this is a design fact, not a figure fact);
- the **independence of the outcome measure** (an audit and a perception survey are different grounds, and the shorthand does not say which one was used);
- the **definition of a slot** (region, school, household and individual give different effects, and the subgroup boundaries must be set before the outcome is seen);
- the **transport conditions**, since the habitat of a second setting is substituted, not merely moved (Fig 11 :66 and :70).

One thing survives cleanly: the source must not move with the outcome, and the bond must be named before the method. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
