## 0. The question under audit

> What evidence can establish whether an institution caused a change in trust, growth, corruption, or conflict?

The question is not "does this institution correlate with that outcome". It asks what would license the word **caused**. The audit therefore treats the word as a claim about a bond between an institution and an outcome, and asks which observations could distinguish that bond from the others that fit the same two operands.

---

## 1. The residual, stated in effect terms

The Symbols table defines the residual: *"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."* Here the residual R is the gap between the prediction **the institution produced the change** and the observation actually available, namely that institution I and outcome change Y were both present, in that order or not.

| property of R | reading for this question |
|---|---|
| **arity** | Four outcomes are named: trust, growth, corruption, conflict. This is a **union compound**, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Each outcome needs its own loop and its own ground. |
| **symmetry** | The claimed bond is ordered. Fig 12 :72 defines sequence as *"A then B; B needs A to have happened"*; Fig 12 :77 defines production as *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*. Both are asymmetric. A symmetric conjunction *"both hold at once"* (Fig 12 :57) cannot express which came first. |
| **scale** | Per unit and per period, then cross-setting transport. Symbols table: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."* Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* A national average can hide the only live slot. |
| **what cannot be read** | The bond itself. Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."* |

So the residual is a **bond-identification residual**, not a data-volume residual. More observations of I and Y do not shrink it, because the rival bonds predict the same operands.

One line per domain, with actors, currency and anchor. A domain with no nameable anchor is not a domain yet. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.

No independent anchor for *causation* here. | Partition by outcome. Union with D2 when a design is added. | Association only. Cannot settle a bond. |
| **D2, exogenous-variation design** | assignment, lottery, cutoff, policy shock, treatment and comparison groups | variation that does not respond to the outcome | **Yes:** a source that does not move when the outcome moves. ## 3. Dependency order (Fig 3)

Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

1. **Perceive.** Measure the outcome per slot, before and after, with an instrument the institution cannot move (D3).
2. **Vocabulary.** Name the candidate bonds: production (⇒), sequence (→), common cause (+ with a hidden third term), cycle (output re-enters as input), and refusal (¬, the claim that no bond holds).
3. **Objective.** Say what would count as progress: a change in Y that persists when the source of variation in I is held fixed against the rival bonds.
4. **Plan.** Choose a design with an anchor that cannot update (D2), and register the refuter before looking (D4).
5. **Learn and carry.** Replicate in a different closure, then measure the round-trip gap on transport (D5).

The middle link is where this chain usually breaks. Fig 3 :25-27: *"The link between them, having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments. A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* In institutional research, this is the step from "here is a reform and a trend" to "here is the exact rival bond this design would refuse".

Fig 3 :22-23: *"A step whose input never arrives cannot be diagnosed, only its predecessor can. Which is why a reading taken below the break is not a weak reading. It is a reading of nothing."* A sophisticated estimator applied to an ungrounded outcome measure is a reading below the break.

## 4. Findings, with verbatim figure lines

### F1 — Observing the operands does not identify the bond (decisive shape)

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."*

Fig 12 :95-96: *"Two entries with an identical ingredient set are not variants of one thing. They are different things whose notation cannot tell them apart, and the difference is carried entirely by the bond."*

Fig 12 :46-47: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands. A recipe that names only its ingredients has recorded half of itself."*

The institution and the outcome trend are the ingredients. The claim "I caused Y" adds a typed relation, most often production (⇒) or sequence (→). The rival claims add different relations to the same ingredients: common cause, where a third thing produces both; reverse sequence, where Y preceded I; or no bond at all. Since the bond is not recoverable from the operands, no amount of additional observation of those operands alone decides it. Fig 13 :128-129: *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available. The space has no last route and every turn begins from a remainder the last one left, so the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."*

**Refuter:** exhibit a case where the bond is determined by the operands alone, with no record of design, timing or external variation. If that is possible, F1 fails.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 12 bond theory and Fig 13 lineage theory, low overlap between composition and verification).

### F2 — The two canonical collapse modes are the two rival stories

Fig 2 names both failures. The first is mutual update: *"no anchor; each updates on the others"* and *"they converge, and what they converge on is whoever had the most influence"*. The second is *"collapse 2: common cause"* with *"one evidence pool"* and *"they never talk, and still agree"*.

In institutional terms, mutual update is the story that a reform and a rise in trust co-evolved through mutual adaptation, so each side's reading of the other moved. Common cause is the story that a third thing (a growth shock, a demographic shift, a change in reporting) produced both the reform and the measured outcome. Both generate the exact correlation that is being offered as evidence.

Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

**Refuter:** show that mutual update and common cause are not distinguishable in principle from a production bond, even with an external source of variation. If so, F2 fails and causal institutional evidence is unreachable.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 collapse taxonomy and Fig 7 mirror-chain rule).

### F3 — The anchor must not update: the shape of admissible evidence

Fig 2 :29-30: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome. If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*

Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."*

Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."*

This is the central positive result. The evidence that can establish institutional causation has the shape of a source of variation in the institution that does **not** respond to the outcome: an assignment, a lottery, a cutoff, a shock, an eligibility rule that units cannot manipulate. In the figures' language it is an import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6), and it is the witness that lets the rival bonds be refused separately. Fig 8 :27-28: *"1: describe R, then find the frame that already predicts its shape"*. Describe the bond residual first, then look for the design; otherwise any design will seem to fit.

The specific methods (randomized assignment, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, difference in differences) are **bindings** from the statistics frame onto an already-held shape. They are not new primitives; importing them adds no atom (Fig 6 :22). They are named in `06_isomers_and_order.md` with provenance, never presented as things the framework derived.

**Refuter:** exhibit a design whose source of variation moves with the outcome yet still separates the bonds. That breaks the anchor rule.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2 ground theory, Fig 6 closure theory, Fig 8 witness theory; three figure families with low overlap).

### F4 — Measure per slot, never only as a national average

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*

Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

Symbols table: *"R is measured per slot, never as a single global number."*

A reform that raises trust in one region and lowers it in another has a near-zero global residual with two live slots. Fig 1 :13 calls that *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state"*. So the evidence must be reported per unit and per subgroup, and the subgroup definitions must be registered before the outcome is seen. A single national before-and-after average is the canonical way to lose the signal.

**Refuter:** show that a global average preserves all live-slot information for this question. If it does, the per-slot requirement is unnecessary.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 1 and the Symbols table).

### F5 — The outcome measure is itself a ground candidate and can be captured

Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."*

Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."*

Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."*

If "corruption" is measured only by a perception index produced in the same institutional environment as the reform, the measure is inside the frame being graded. The same applies to trust surveyed by the institution whose trust is in question, or growth figures reported by the agency whose reform is being evaluated. World corroboration: the World Bank IEG is explicitly independent of Bank management (`ieg.worldbankgroup.org`), which is exactly this separation in practice.

Fig 9 :51: *"evidence bearing on the residual is admissible; evidence bearing only on its maker is a stake-inducing channel, and the verifier is denied it"*.

**Refuter:** show that an institution's own report of its outcome cannot move when the institution is pressured. If it cannot, the disqualification is too strong.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 10 seat rules and Fig 9 admissibility rule).

### F6 — Replication in the same evidence pool is not corroboration

Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are. Independence is what makes agreement mean something."*

Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*.

Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"*.

Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

Ten studies using the same survey, the same dataset, the same outcome index and the same author network are one instrument counted ten times. World corroboration: the AEA RCT Registry makes designs and outcomes public before the run, which is an institutional mechanism for keeping the record of where a result came from (`socialscienceregistry.org`).

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

**Refuter:** show two replications that draw on genuinely disjoint evidence while using the same outcome definition, and show that their agreement still adds nothing. If it adds nothing, the independence requirement is too strong.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 8).

### F7 — Transport has a nonzero round-trip gap

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

Fig 11 :66: *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."*

Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

A causal estimate from one city, one regime or one period, carried to another, is an abstraction and a concretisation with a gap. The gap is not an error to hide, it is *"the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold"* (Fig 4 :31-32). The honest evidence package reports the gap qualitatively by listing what did not survive the trip, because no ground reading exists in this simulation.

**Refuter:** a measured round trip with zero gap across two genuinely different closures. That would falsify the lossy-transform claim for this domain.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 4 transform theory and Fig 11 habitat theory).

### F8 — The framework returns the shape of evidence, not a named method

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.

Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

**Correction to my own first reading.** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming an identification strategy, on the analogy with "a shape, not a name". That was overstated. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Difference in differences, randomization and cutoffs are already held in the statistics frame; naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto a shape the figures already return, not the invention of a new atom. Fig 6 :22: *"NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* This one adds none. What stays forbidden is claiming the framework derives a specific method, or that a method moves the wall by itself. The correction is kept visible; the earlier reading is not erased.

**Refuter:** show that the figures uniquely select one named estimator as the answer to a causal question of arbitrary shape. They do not.

**Status: single-source for the figure text, corroborated by the canonical README (same evidence pool, weak weight).**

### F9 — Institutional self-report is a stake-inducing channel, so declare the stake

Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*.

Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it. An undeclared interest is modelled anyway and an unnamed model runs unchallenged."*

A research team funded by the institution it studies, or a reformer evaluating their own reform, has an interest in the reading. That does not disqualify the person from asking; it disqualifies their agreement from counting as a verdict. Fig 10 :65-66: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."*

**Refuter:** a case where an undeclared stake demonstrably changes nothing about the reading. Since undeclared means unmodelled, this is hard to show, which is the point.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 10 seat rules, Fig 9 admissibility rule).

### F10 — Pre-registering the refuter makes the sought-for shape refusable

Fig 5 :65-66: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM. Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."*

Seat Map, failure genera: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

Committing, before the data are seen, to the exact pattern that would refuse the production bond (for example, no discontinuity at a known eligibility cutoff, or a first-stage that moves with the outcome) turns the preference into an ordinary term. Without it, a frame will read whatever result it obtains as confirmation, because the shape it wanted was never bound and so never accrued a residual. This is the same limit `THE_FORMULA.md` names under "Direction": a loop cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Refuter:** a design in which the refuter is written after the result and still carries the same evidential weight. By the figure's rule it does not.

**Status: triangulated** (Fig 5 and the seat-map discipline, low overlap between mint pipeline and checking arrangement).

## 5. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A design is exhibited in which the source of variation in the institution responds to the outcome, yet the rival bonds are still separated (breaks Fig 2 :29-33).
2. The bond is shown to be recoverable from the operands alone, with no design, timing or external record (breaks Fig 12 :46-47, :95-96).
3. A global average is shown to preserve every live slot's causal information for this question (breaks Fig 1 :19, :22).
4. Two replications drawing on the same evidence pool are counted as independent corroboration and the grounds do not object (breaks Fig 2 :40, Fig 7 :13).
5. A round trip between two genuinely different settings is measured with zero gap (breaks Fig 4 :30).
6. A ground reading of a specific institution and outcome settles which bond held (this would move that specific claim from `candidate` to `grounded`, and would not refute the shape).

Until one of 1 to 5 fires, no part of this answer may be labelled `grounded`.

## 6. Recommendation

1. State the rival bonds before naming a method. Ask which one the design can refuse, not which method sounds rigorous.
2. Require an anchor that does not update (F3) and an outcome measure outside the graded slice (F5) as two separate gates, not one.
3. Report effects per slot (F4) and register subgroup definitions before looking.
4. Count witnesses, not publications. Two low-overlap designs beat ten same-pool replications (F6).
5. Report the round-trip gap when transporting an estimate (F7). Never present a local effect as a universal one.
6. Write the refuter before the run (F10), and put it in front of the links in any outreach.
7. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Send the generator (the residual, the bond census and the refuter), not a list of conclusions.

## 8. Strain test

Two candidate strains were tested.

1. **Fig 11 :40** *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* seems to deny that a better estimator helps. Re-census resolves it: that line governs an agent's capability relative to its habitat, not the evidentiary value of a design. A better design changes contact with the ground; it is exactly the kind of change the line permits. Different object, no strain.
2. **Fig 12 :77** production (*"A produces B; B did not exist before"*) seems to make most institutional causal claims impossible, because trust and corruption already exist before a reform. Re-census resolves it by unbundling: a change in an existing quantity is a comparison ⋛ (*"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, Fig 12 :87), not production. The claim must say which bond it means. No strain after re-census; the earlier reading was a misidentified bond.

After re-census: **no hidden strain. One correction kept visible (F8), and one bond-unbundling correction kept visible (F9 and section 7).**
