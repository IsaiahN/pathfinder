## The residual in one line

We observe an institution I and a change in an outcome Y (trust, growth, corruption, or conflict). The same observation is consistent with several different bonds: I produced Y, Y produced I, a hidden third thing produced both, or nothing connected them. The evidence needed is evidence about the bond, not more observations of the two operands.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* More readers of the same material do not add an anchor.
2. **Selecting for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation.** Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list selected for likely agreement is a list selected to share a pool.
3. **Trust before checking spends the reader's independence.** Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interiors.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions and will get a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.
