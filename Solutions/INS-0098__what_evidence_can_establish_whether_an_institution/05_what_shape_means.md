## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of admissible evidence that can be stated before any institution or dataset is named. It constrains: the evidence must be about a bond, must come from a source that does not update on the outcome, must be read per slot, and must carry a lineage that lets the bond be checked. It does not fill: it does not name a particular reform, country, estimator or dataset as *the* answer.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap with it. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on the question itself being a bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. This is the figure's union rule applied to the four outcomes: trust, growth, corruption and conflict are four loops, not one.

Applied here: describe the residual as a bond-identification problem in effect terms *before* reaching for a method. Otherwise whichever method is named first will seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A specific claim that institution X caused change Y in place Z is licensed only by a ground reading that pays for that specific bond.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Naming difference in differences as a method is such a binding from the statistics frame. It is recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism for why a name from a reading alone is unsafe: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* A before-and-after correlation is the instrument that cannot distinguish production, reverse causation, common cause and no bond. What is owed is a discriminating design, not a confident name.

## 4. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming an estimator? ANSWERED with a correction

**First reading (recorded, then corrected):** I wrote that the framework forbids naming a method, so the answer must stay at "some exogenous source of variation".

**Corrected reading:** The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. Difference in differences, randomization, regression discontinuity and instrumental variables are already held in the statistics frame. Naming one is a **binding** from that frame onto an already-held shape, not the invention of a new seventh primitive. Fig 6 :22: *"IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."* This adds none. What remains forbidden is (a) claiming the figures derive a specific method, and (b) claiming the method moves the wall by itself. Both readings are kept here so the correction is visible.

**Does the answer file commit the error the README forbids?** No. `07_the_tether_answer.md` states the shape and names methods only as provenanced bindings. It does not say which estimator settles any actual institution.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For institutional causal evidence this means: a well-cited study, a famous reform or a prestigious journal does not settle whether the bond held. It can fund the attempt to check. Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* The right ask to a checker is "what would refute this", not "do you agree".

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md`, `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of any institution and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
