## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, read this session) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"** and :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

For this question a shape is therefore the set of conditions any candidate explanation of institutional constraint must satisfy, stated before we know which norm is doing the work. It is not the name of a norm and not a score for "institutional quality".

The Library of Babel mapping (carried) gives the search-side version: cut the search short when the returned set is small and descriptive enough to give a general shape of the answer. In CMP-0079 the returned set is the habitat condition set (contact change, violable convention, relative valence, ground selection), not a list of institutions.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- **Describe the residual before choosing a frame.** Fig 9 :27-28: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Here the residual is the per-slot `R` of networked agents, not "institutions" in general. Picking institutional theory first would make any institution seem to fit.
- **Describe the effect, not the cause.** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. The effect is a change in which contacts determine what pays; the cause would be a named norm, which is withheld.
- **Encode the sought-for shape as an ordinary term.** `THE_FORMULA.md` Direction: a frame cannot check the direction of its own question because the sought-for shape is a preference, not a term. The pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 does this: it binds what an answer would look like and lets it accrue a residual.
- **A shape can be held while the slot stays open.** From *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened directly): *"The group's agreement is never the verdict."* The shape says how norms act; it does not say which norm acted here.

## 3. What licenses a name (and the correction I made)

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A measured change in per-slot `R` after a documented habitat substitution would name the norm.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). The polycentric-governance frame is recorded as such a candidate in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the shadow test is still owed.

**Correction to my own first pass.** I initially wrote that the framework forbids naming any norm at all, on the strength of *"a shape, not a name"*. That was an overstatement. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in `Γ`. The relevant atoms here (an actor, a convention, a threshold comparison) are already available; a binding onto them is a provenanced mapping, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21: **"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."**) and claiming the framework selected the norm. Both readings are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why a "good norm" verdict is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried mapping) gives the mechanism: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source."* Several sources can give the same reading; the reading does not determine the source. Applied here: observing that a group adapted after a norm was introduced does not identify which contact change did the work, because several habitat changes could produce the same `R` trajectory. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a name.

This is reinforced directly by *How a Society of Agents Would Work* (opened directly): the maintainer *"sets conventions, never verdicts"*, and *"It adds, it never removes. Figure 11: 'Introduction is available; exclusion is not.'"* A verdict that a norm "works" would let a convention decide an outcome, which Fig 10 :47 forbids.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened directly): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For CMP-0079 this is the reason an institution's standing cannot be used as evidence that its norm improved adaptation. The same essay: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is Fig 2's mirror collapse in the institution stratum, and it is Fig 10 :61's *"standing is a stake that survives the outcome it was earned on"*.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay (opened directly): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any institution or norm and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. The shape for this question, stated

> Institutions and informal norms are habitat, not agent internals. They change the trajectory of per-slot `R` by introducing contacts and violable conventions, never by subtracting contact. Their sign is relative to the residual, so no standing list of good or bad norms survives a change of residual. Constraint and redirection are a union of conditions, not one lever. Agreement inside a norm-governed group is variation; the ground selects. Which norm, in which case, and with what sign and magnitude is a declared gap, left open until a source-discriminating instrument closes it.

That paragraph is a shape and a route. It is `general` on the structure and `specified` on the application. It is not `grounded`, and it does not name the winner.
