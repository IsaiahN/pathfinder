# Expert verification packet : the Tether on gene-culture interaction

**What this is:** a claim in a form a researcher in cultural evolution, population genetics, cognitive development or animal behaviour can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the interaction, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** No slot-resolved measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Cultural transmission and biological evolution are two inheritance closures with different currency and anchor, so neither reduces to the other and their union reaches what neither reaches alone. Cultural transmission is itself a union of unmaintained contact (exposure, imitation, vertical and oblique copying) and maintained introduction (teaching, institutions), where introduction is available and exclusion is not. The two channels compose across a scale membrane that carries reapplicable methods up and head starts down, never recordings, and close into a cycle whose sign decides whether collective behavior stabilizes or runs away. Collective behavioral capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of genes or individual minds in isolation. The framework does not name which gene or norm fills a slot; it offers the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 : framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 10, 11 and 8

- **Statement:** two closures with different currency and anchor; culture splits into an unmaintained contact mode and a maintained introduction mode; the union has a surplus that neither closure reaches alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :68 : "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." Fig 11 :55 : "Two modes, and only one of them has a seat." Fig 11 :61 : "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." Fig 8 :45 : "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."
- **Refuter:** a single-channel model that predicts the observed rate, direction and slot-level distribution of cumulative collective behavior as well as the composed model, or a demonstration that one channel is derivable from the other's closure with no new currency or anchor. Then the surplus is zero.

### C2 : cycle and membrane; checkable by population-genetic and transmission data

- **Statement:** the relation is a production followed by feedback, so the composed object is a cycle with a sign, not a chain; across the scale membrane only methods go up and only head starts come down.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :77 : "A produces B; B did not exist before." Fig 12 :121 : "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 4 :22 : "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Fig 4 :23 : "upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."
- **What to check:** is there a measurable case where a transmitted practice changes the selective environment across generations, and the resulting genetic change alters a transmission bias? Does the practice that crosses scales remain a reapplicable method, or is it a recording of one occasion?
- **Refuter:** a practice that leaves the selective environment statistically unchanged while gene frequencies move in the predicted direction; or a genetic change that alters no transmission bias; or a practice that crosses up and returns down unchanged, so the membrane is not a filter.

### C3 : capability and isolation; checkable against experiments and field data

- **Statement:** collective capability belongs to agent-and-habitat, and a single-channel laboratory habitat proves wiring rather than capability.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 : "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40 : "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :70 : "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."
- **Refuter:** "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim" (Fig 11 :72), or a substituted habitat that reproduces the full cumulative phenotype including redirection of selection.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that culture is extended genetics, or that genes determine which norms spread.
- Not that the loop always stabilizes. The sign is unmeasured here.
- Not a named gene, norm or institution as a figure-derived result. Those are world bindings requiring provenance and a shadow test.
- Not that single-channel experiments are useless. They prove wiring; they do not prove capability.
- Not `grounded`, and not self-validating. The framework cannot validate itself; an external ground is required.
- Not a final theory. There is no last room.

## 5. What would settle it

A source- and slot-discriminating design, not more argument. The source-discrimination precedent is *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green reading "cannot comment on the source", and the case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

1. Measure behavior-slot spread rates and allele-frequency change in the same population.
2. Measure whether the transmitted practice changes the selective environment across generations, and whether the genetic response changes a transmission bias.
3. Measure the round-trip gap `R_T` for a practice carried up and brought back down.
4. Test an agent-only or gene-only improvement against the ground.
5. Test a substituted habitat for the full cumulative phenotype.
6. Seek two instruments with different evidence that agree on the same slot-level claim.
