## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And on method: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- "two inheritance channels with different currency and anchor" is a shape.
- "lactase persistence is the gene-culture case" is a name.
- "the loop is a cycle whose sign decides the outcome" is a shape.
- "conformist transmission always produces traditions" is a name, and the world literature itself disputes it (Federico et al., 2025, preprint).

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*, on bootstrapping: "This is a recursive process. With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place." The paradox is that by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

The derivation in `01` followed this order. It did not start from the phrase "gene-culture coevolution" and look for a figure to fit. It started from the residual stated in effect terms (behavior spreading faster than alleles and persisting more reliably than individual learning), did a domain census, and only then read figure lines.

## 3. What licenses a name here

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here that means slot-resolved behavioral and genetic measurement in the same population.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the warning. A color instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." Several underlying causes can create identical readings: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The resolution came "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Applied here: observing that a norm and an allele rose together does not identify which produced which. A design that discriminates source is owed, not a confident name.

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "It can be inherited or taught, so the recipient need not pay the bill themselves, but something, somewhere, already paid it." This is the closest essay line to the cultural transmission half of the question. It says the bill was paid somewhere in the lineage, which is exactly why the transmission channel cannot be treated as a free replay (Fig 4 :24: "downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once.").

## 4. Specificity to this question

The question asks *how* two channels interact. The figure-grounded shape has four parts:

1. **Different closures.** Different actors, currency and anchor (Fig 10 :68). No common unit makes one a fast version of the other.
2. **A membrane.** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." (Fig 4 :22) Behaviors carried upward must be reapplicable methods, not recordings of one success.
3. **A cycle with a sign.** "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes" (Fig 12 :121). Positive feedback can run away; negative feedback stabilizes. The composed outcome depends on the sign, which is a measurement question, not an argument.
4. **Agent-and-habitat capability.** "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" (Fig 11 :39).

This is a shape that any gene-culture case must have. It does not say which behavior, which allele, or which institution. It says what an answer would have to look like and what would refute it.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific mechanism. The canonical phrase governs "what a **missing** thing would have to look like". Lactase persistence, teaching biases and norm institutions are not missing primitives of a closed set; they are terms held in the world frames. Naming them is a binding with provenance, not the invention of a new primitive.

What remains wrong in a claim that names them without provenance is the metamerism point of §3: a joint reading of norm and allele does not identify the source. The reason is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held terms. Both claims are now recorded so neither is quietly lost. This mirrors the exemplar's own correction, reached independently.

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

A campaign that gathers well-known cultural-evolution researchers to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, an instrument, a dataset or an experiment, settle it. This is why `03` lists public opt-in venues and not a ranked list of famous people.

The same essay gives the relevant structural note for the domain census: "Natural selection is what the loop looks like when there is no one maintaining the ground. When there is no one in the Ground Maintainer seat, the environment is the only selector." That is domain D1, and it is why D2b (pedagogy, institutions) is a different mode rather than a faster D1.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the interaction claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on. The world sources are corroboration only, and most of them share an evidence pool.

## 8. What this means for the outreach

- Lead with the shape and the refuter; the receiver can check an effect description without holding the framework's interior (Fig 10 :52).
- Do not ask for trust or agreement (Fig 2 :35).
- Offer the links only after a verdict has been recorded (Fig 6 :30; Decoding Tacit Knowledge on plain effect descriptions).
- Keep the lineage: which figure line governed which claim, which binding came from which world source, and what would reverse it.
- Stop at the limit of the instruments. "Indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage is what keeps the stop honest.
