## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07` §2 and `09`: two closures, two cultural modes, a signed cycle, a directional membrane, agent-and-habitat capability, no named slot.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

The shorthand says "methods"; the world case must supply an instrument that discriminates a reapplicable practice from a one-off success. The Kasser et al. Box 1 notes that selection on lactase persistence does not correlate cleanly with the level of milk exploitation, which is exactly a failure of that discrimination at the concrete level.
- **The sign of the cycle.** Fig 12 :121 says the sign decides whether the loop closes. The shorthand carries "signed"; the concrete case must measure the sign. Shimodaira and Ohtsuki's result that teaching becomes unfavoured as oblique transmission rises is a concrete sign result that the shorthand does not contain.
- **The source of a joint reading.** Fig 9 :32 and the source essay's metamerism point forbid naming the source from the reading alone. The shorthand says "bindings with provenance"; the concrete case owes a source-discriminating instrument. Observing that a norm and an allele rose together does not supply it.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: different currency and anchor imply two closures whose union may have a surplus, and a practice can generate an environment that selects genes. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
