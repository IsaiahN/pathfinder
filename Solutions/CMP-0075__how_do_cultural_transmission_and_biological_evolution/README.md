## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor." A list of sympathetic readers adds vocabulary, not an anchor.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the very correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking for trust before checking spends independence.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework interior.

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the GDPR and CAN-SPAM style failures that come with scraping personal addresses for unsolicited outreach. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. The audience that can help is the one that has opted in to receiving claims and is positioned to refute the shape, not a purchased list of believers. Send the refuter before the theory, and do not pool later agreement as corroboration (Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong.").
