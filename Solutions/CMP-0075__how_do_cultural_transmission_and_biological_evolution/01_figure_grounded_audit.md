## 0. The claim under audit

> Cultural transmission and biological evolution are two inheritance channels whose composition, not either channel alone, produces cumulative collective behavior, and whose feedback can redirect genetic selection.

Stated as an effect: when a behavior spreads faster than allele-frequency change permits and persists more reliably than individual learning permits, and when the environment that selects genes is itself changed by transmitted practice, a single-channel explanation leaves a residual. The question asks what the composition of the two channels looks like and what it would take to settle it.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term before the run).** An answer must state (a) the actors, currency and anchor of each channel, (b) the typed relation between them, (c) which observable would change if the composition were absent, and (d) what would refute it. The shape sought is therefore: two closures with different currency and anchor, a cross-scale membrane, and a cycle with a sign. This shape is written down here before the figure search so it is refusable like any other term (Formula step "Direction"; Fig 5 :62-63: "a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term").

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two transmission channels, genetic and cultural, with the individual learner as their point of contact. Symmetry: the relation is ordered, not a symmetric join. Cultural practice can generate a selective environment that did not exist before (production, `⇒`), while genes send down learned biases as a head start; the two operands are not interchangeable. Scale: within-lifetime transmission and across-generation transmission are different scales joined by a membrane. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either." This is a **union compound**, not one hard question, so it has its own loop and its own ground.

**One line per domain: actors / currency / anchor.** No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | mode / question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, genetic evolution | alleles, organisms, external environment | differential reproduction (fitness) | population genetic data and the external environment; an anchor that does not move when a frame pushes it | **unmaintained habitat**, no seat. Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." |
| D2a, unmaintained cultural contact | models, learners, observers, copied variants | fidelity and bias of social learning | observed transmission rates and behavioral phenotypes | **unmaintained** contact: exposure, imitation, vertical and oblique transmission. |
| D2b, maintained cultural introduction | teachers, institutions, norm entrepreneurs | introduction of variants, pedagogy, conformity and prestige bias | the same behavioral anchor, plus the provenance of the introduction | **maintained habitat**, a maintainer-like seat that can introduce but not exclude. Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." |
| D3, collective behavior | the population's repertoire, norms, institutions | spread rate per behavior slot, cumulative retention | population-level behavioral and genetic measurements | **composition** of D1 and D2 across the scale membrane; the output is not a property of either channel in isolation. |

**Partition or union.** D1 and D2 are not a partition of one inheritance process. They are two closures with different currency and anchor, so their relation is a union (Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."). D2a and D2b are a union inside the cultural channel; the first is pure contact, the second has a seat. Treating all cultural transmission as maintained was my first reading and is retracted in §6.

**Dependency order (Fig 3).** Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not." Therefore: (1) resolve the residual per behavior slot before naming a channel (perception); (2) name the terms each channel can express (vocabulary); (3) state what would count as progress, that is, what the composed model predicts and a single-channel model does not (objective); (4) only then plan a test. The membrane rule (Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.") sets a second order: genes send down a head start (evolved learning biases), culture carries up a method (a reapplicable practice), and neither sends a recording of a past success.

**Strain test applied before concluding.** A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity, so re-census. My first attempt mapped "genes" and "culture" directly onto members of the six-term substrate (network, spectrum, gradient). That strains Fig 13, because the six are substrate states and structures, while genes and culture are frames or closures at a higher level. Re-census places them as domains with their own actors, currency and anchor, and keeps the six as the substrate the loop assumes throughout. The strain is recorded in §6, not smoothed over.

## 2. Findings

### F1 : Two closures, not one channel reduced to the other (triangulated)

Fig 4 :19-20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change." Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."

The genetic channel trades in allelic variation and differential reproduction; the cultural channel trades in transmitted information and fidelity or bias. Because the currency differs, there is no common unit in which one is a faster version of the other. Their union has a surplus that a single closure cannot reach (Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."). Fig 8 :50: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." This is why the world literature's separate modelling of cultural and genetic transmission is not two names for one process, nor is agreement between modellers who share a tradition a second witness.

- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 4, Fig 10 and Fig 8 with low overlap between the membrane rule, the level rule and the union rule.
- **Refuter:** a single-channel model (genes only or culture only) that predicts the observed rate, direction and slot-level distribution of cumulative collective behavior as well as the composed model. If it pays the same bargain, the surplus is zero and the union claim fails.

### F2 : Culture produces a selective environment, and the loop is a cycle (triangulated)

Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before." Fig 12 :121: "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." Fig 11 :65: "Introducing an actor for its cascade is ecosystem engineering, after Jones, Lawton and Shachak, 1994."

Cultural niche construction is a production bond: a practice brings about an environment (a resource, a norm, a disease regime) that was not there before, and that environment selects genes. Gene-based biases then alter what gets learned, closing the loop. The composed object is therefore a cycle with a sign, not a chain; a chain cannot close by construction. The world literature corroborates the shape: Kasser et al. (2025) write that "a key aspect of gene-culture coevolution theory is that biological and cultural evolutionary processes interact through mutual causal feedback"; Rendell et al. (2011) report that "cultural niche-constructing practices can generate selection for gene-based traits and hitchhike to fixation through the build up of statistical associations between practice and trait." These are corroborations only. They share a modelling tradition and count as one witness, not two.

- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 12 (production and cycle) and Fig 11 (ecosystem engineering), with world corroboration from a shared evidence pool.
- **Refuter:** a cultural practice that leaves the selective environment statistically unchanged across generations while gene frequencies move in the direction the practice would predict, or a genetic change that alters no cultural transmission bias. Either breaks the cycle.

### F3 : Capability is agent-and-habitat, not genes or minds in isolation (triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Fig 11 :72: "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."

Collective behavioral capacity is not stored in the genome and not stored in individual minds. It is a property of the composed agent-and-habitat. The world source closest to this is Katz (2011): "the structure and physiology of the nervous system helps direct the evolution of behaviour down certain paths that recur over evolutionary time." That is the biological side of the contact claim, arrived at independently in neuroscience and evo-devo.

- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11's capability claim and Katz's independent evo-devo finding (low overlap with cultural-evolution modelling).
- **Refuter:** an agent-only or gene-only improvement that moves the ground. Fig 11 :72 names this as the standing refutation, so it is not a rhetorical escape.

### F4 : Single-channel isolation is a substituted habitat (triangulated)

Fig 11 :66: "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Fig 11 :70: "A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."

A laboratory microculture in which only social learning operates, or a model in which only allele frequencies change, is a substituted habitat. It proves the wiring of that channel and not the capability of the composed system. The two silent failure modes are named at Fig 11 :68-69: "what you failed to reproduce" and "what you brought with you." A single-channel experiment omits the other channel (failed to reproduce) and imports the experimenter's own framing of what counts as behavior (brought with you).

- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11's isolation law and the two failure modes.
- **Refuter:** a substituted habitat that reproduces the full phenotype, including cumulative retention and redirection of selection, with the second channel absent. Then isolation did not substitute away the capability.

### F5 : The residual is per behavior slot, never a global average (single-source, framework internal)

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

"Culture" and "genes" as global rates average away the only signal that says where to look. The live question is which behavior slot is spreading faster than genes allow and which gene-frequency change tracks a transmitted environment. The world source from Creanza and Feldman (2014) is relevant here in a limited way: their model produces "oscillations, stable polymorphisms of all cultural phenotypes, and simultaneous stability of oscillation and fixation", so a global fixation narrative is not even the field's own default picture.

- **Verdict:** `general` structural rule with a `specified` application; single-source for the indexing rule (Fig 1), corroborated weakly by a modelling result that is not the same claim.
- **Refuter:** global aggregates that predict slot-level spread as well as slot-resolved residuals. Then the indexing rule buys nothing for this question.

### F6 : The ground settles it, not agreement between frames (triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."

The anchor here is population-level behavioral and genetic measurement, not consensus among cultural-evolution researchers. Kasser, Creanza, Rendell, Feldman and Laland draw on overlapping intellectual and data pools, so their agreement is one pool talking. Csibra and Gergely (2011) and Katz (2011) are lower-overlap frames; Csibra and Gergely's own falsifier is explicit: "These claims can be falsified by finding a species that teaches by communication or a human society that does not do so." That is the shape of a claim that can come back wrong.

- **Verdict:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 2 and Fig 9, with the seat map's correlation valence (Seat Map :163) as a third low-overlap route.
- **Refuter:** two genuinely independent instruments, drawing on different evidence, that agree on the same slot-level claim. That would be corroboration at maximum independence (Fig 8 :50), and it would move this verdict from shared-pool corroboration to a real second witness.

### F7 : The framework returns a shape, not a name (triangulated)

Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." The figures README states the limit as "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". For this question the shape is: two inheritance channels with different currency and anchor, joined across a scale membrane by a cycle whose sign decides whether behavior stabilizes or runs away, with collective behavior as the composition. The framework does not name which gene or which norm occupies a slot. Naming lactase persistence, a conformity bias or a particular institution is a binding from the world frame onto a held term, provenanced as such, not a derivation from the figures (Fig 8 :50-52).

- **Verdict:** `general`, triangulated across Fig 9 and the canonical README.
- **Refuter:** a specific gene-culture pair derived from the six-term set alone, with no import and no world binding. If that is possible, the shape-not-a-name limit does not govern this question.

### F8 : Selection between rooms, not a chain of agreeing models (single-source, framework internal)

Fig 7 :33: "variation inside, selection between." Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes." Fig 7 :36: "A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."

A pile of cultural-evolution models that all assume dual inheritance is one long room. Progress requires the next room to differ from the last and to match the residual surfaced, for example a low-overlap frame from neuroscience (Katz) or from animal teaching (Csibra and Gergely's nonhuman comparisons). The precedent in the figures is convergent derivation from separate fields (Fig 8 :50), not adoption from a shared pool.

- **Verdict:** `general`, single-source (Fig 7) with the world's field spread as corroboration of opportunity, not of outcome.
- **Refuter:** a chain of frames, each selected for agreement with the last, that nonetheless changes its verdict at the ground. Then agreement did no harm and the warning is idle here.

### F9 : Re-census of D2: cultural transmission is a union of two modes (strain resolved by splitting)

Fig 11 :55: "Two modes, and only one of them has a seat." Fig 11 :56: "Unmaintained. No maintainer and no live frame in the seat. The habitat is pure contact, nothing selects, and what survives is what the ground kept." Fig 11 :58: "Maintained. A seat exists and can act: choose the instruments, distil the impactful actors, introduce what was not there."

Most cultural transmission (exposure, imitation, oblique copying) is unmaintained contact. Pedagogy and institutions are maintained introduction. My first census called the whole cultural channel "maintained", which strained Fig 11 because it assigned a seat to pure contact. Re-census splits D2 into D2a and D2b, exactly as Fig 9 :54 requires for a union. The CES explainer corroborates the structural difference in world terms: "genes are inherited with very high fidelity, whereas cultural traits are often transformed and reconstructed as they are passed from person to person", and "cultural inheritance has many more pathways" than the parent-to-child route of genes.

- **Verdict:** `specified`, triangulated across Fig 11 and the CES field definition (low overlap between a metatheory figure and a society's public explainer).
- **Refuter:** a case of cultural transmission with no identifiable maintainer and no contact-driven transformation, in which variants nevertheless accumulate as they do under high-fidelity genetic inheritance. Then the two-mode split buys nothing.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before any outreach, per Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. A single inheritance channel predicts the observed rate, direction and slot-level distribution of cumulative collective behavior as well as the composed model. Then the union has no surplus (Fig 8 :45).
2. A cultural practice leaves the selective environment statistically unchanged across generations while gene frequencies move in the predicted direction, or a genetic change alters no transmission bias. Then the production cycle does not close (Fig 12 :77, :121).
3. An agent-only or gene-only improvement moves the ground. This is the standing refuter named at Fig 11 :72.
4. A substituted, single-channel habitat reproduces the full cumulative phenotype. Then isolation did not cost the capability (Fig 11 :70).
5. Global aggregates of genetic and cultural change predict slot-level behavioral spread as well as slot-resolved residuals (Fig 1 :19).
6. Cultural transmission is derived from the genetic closure with no new currency or anchor, so the two frames are one frame wearing two names (Fig 8 :48-49).

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this is labelled `grounded`.

### Seven-question recursive derivation (summary)

1. **Is this one question?** No. Fig 9 :54-57 makes it a union compound in two conditions; split D1, D2a, D2b and D3 before searching.
2. **Are the channels reducible to one another?** No. Different currency and anchor (Fig 4 :19-20, Fig 10 :68); their union has a surplus (Fig 8 :45).
3. **What is the typed relation?** Production (`⇒`, Fig 12 :77) from practice to selective environment, then a head start down the membrane (Fig 4 :24), closing as a cycle (Fig 12 :121).
4. **What is the anchor?** Population-level behavioral and genetic measurement (Fig 2 :29), not agreement among modellers (Fig 2 :35, :40).
5. **What moves the wall?** Contact with a closure that differs (Fig 6 :23). Inward: better transmission and selection instruments. Outward: import from evo-devo, animal behaviour or neuroscience. Never read one against the other (Formula step 7).
6. **What is settled?** Nothing in this run. World cases such as lactase persistence and runaway niche construction are retrodictions with provenance, not figures-derived results.
7. **What is owed?** Slot-resolved measurement of spread rates, gene-frequency change and the selective environment in the same population; two low-overlap instruments; and the pre-registered refuter run before any claim is sent.

### Updated verdict

The shape survives: two inheritance closures with different currency and anchor compose across a scale membrane into a cycle whose sign decides collective behavior, and the composed capability belongs to agent-and-habitat. The specific bindings do not follow from the figures; they are world imports requiring provenance. Strains after re-census: **none live**, one resolved by splitting D2, one resolved by re-censing genes and culture as frames rather than substrate members. The world literature corroborates the shape but mostly shares one evidence pool, so it is one witness rather than several.
