# The Tether's answer on gene-culture interaction

**Mode: `specified` on the interaction, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Genetic evolution alone predicts change on generation timescales. Individual learning alone predicts reversible, non-cumulative change. What is observed in collective behavior is often neither: practices accumulate, outrun allele-frequency change, and redirect the environment that selects genes. The residual is **per behavior slot**, not a global average. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The question asks what the composition of the two channels looks like and what would settle it.

## 2. The shape

1. **Two closures, not one channel at two speeds.** Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between." The genetic channel trades in alleles and differential reproduction; the cultural channel trades in copied information, fidelity and bias. Neither is a fast version of the other. Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." The interaction is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :54-57), so it has its own loop and its own ground.

2. **Cultural transmission is a union of two modes.** Most transmission is unmaintained contact: exposure, imitation, vertical and oblique copying. Pedagogy and institutions are maintained introduction. Fig 11 :55: "Two modes, and only one of them has a seat." Fig 11 :61: "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A cultural system can add a practice; it cannot subtract the habitat it lives in.

3. **The relation is a cycle with a sign, not a chain.** Fig 12 :121: "The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A transmitted practice produces a selective environment that did not exist before (Fig 12 :77: "A produces B; B did not exist before"), genes change, learned biases change, and the loop re-enters. Positive feedback can run away; negative feedback stabilizes. Which sign holds is a measurement, not an argument.

4. **The membrane carries methods up and head starts down.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A practice that crosses scales must be reapplicable, not the recording of one success (Fig 4 :23-24). Evolved biases descend as a cheaper search, never as a replay. The round trip has a nonzero gap `R_T`, which is the honest report of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :30-32).

5. **Capability belongs to agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39: "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone." Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Neither fitter genes nor better individual learners alone settle collective behavior.

6. **The specific gene, norm or institution is not named by the framework.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Lactase persistence, conformity bias, teaching and niche construction are bindings from world frames, provenanced as such. They corroborate the shape but are not derived from it, and the modelling sources largely share one evidence pool.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that culture is extended genetics, or that genes determine which norms spread. They have different currency and anchor.
- Not that the loop always stabilizes. The sign decides, and the sign is unmeasured here.
- Not that a single-channel experiment settles the interaction. A test harness is a substituted habitat and proves wiring, never capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not that any particular case (dairying, teaching, conformity) is a figure-derived result. Those are world imports with provenance.
- Not `grounded`. No slot-resolved measurement in this derivation decides which channel dominates.
- Not a theory of everything. There is no last room (Fig 7 :6); the center stays a compound with its own loop.

## 4. What would settle it : the ground

A source- and slot-discriminating design, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* records that an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source" and that the question was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here:

- **Slot test:** measure spread rate per behavior and allele-frequency change per locus in the **same** population. If global averages predict the slot-level result as well as slot-resolved measurement, the indexing rule in §1 buys nothing.
- **Cycle test:** does a transmitted practice measurably change the selective environment across generations, and does that change feed back into a transmission bias? If either leg is zero, the cycle does not close.
- **Capability test:** does an agent-only or gene-only improvement move the ground? Fig 11 :72 names this as the standing refuter: "an agent-only improvement that moved the ground would refute the capability claim."
- **Membrane test:** does a practice carried up and brought back down survive unchanged? A nonzero `R_T` is expected; a zero gap would mean the membrane is not a filter.
- **Single-channel test:** does a substituted habitat reproduce the full cumulative phenotype? If it does, isolation did not remove the capability.
- **Independence test:** do two instruments with different evidence agree on the same slot-level claim? Agreement inside one modelling pool is one witness.

## 5. The pre-registered refuter

Written before any outreach (full set in `01` §3): a single-channel model that pays the same bargain; a practice that leaves the selective environment unchanged; an agent-only improvement that moves the ground; a substituted habitat that reproduces the phenotype; global aggregates that predict slot spread; or a derivation of one channel from the other with no surplus. Any one fires a retraction of the corresponding claim.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The figure set can say what any gene-culture answer must look like: two closures, a directed membrane, a signed cycle, and capability in agent-and-habitat. It cannot say which behavior, which allele, or what the sign is in a given population. The essays warn that the recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and "indiscernibility is not identity." The lineage is kept so the stop is checkable. World sources corroborate the shape but mostly share an evidence pool, so they are one witness rather than several.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not reduce culture to genes or genes to culture. It says they are two inheritance closures with different currency and anchor, so their union reaches what neither reaches alone. Cultural transmission itself splits into unmaintained contact and maintained introduction: most copying is pure contact, while teaching and institutions can add a practice but cannot subtract the habitat. The two channels join across a scale membrane that carries methods up and head starts down, never recordings, and they close into a cycle whose sign decides whether collective behavior stabilizes or runs away. The capability that results belongs to the agent-and-habitat system, not to genes or to individual minds in isolation. The framework does not name which gene or which norm fills a slot; particular cases are bindings from the world literature, recorded as such. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, six refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The sign stays open until the ground settles it.
