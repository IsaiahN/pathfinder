## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the boundary of a transplant, statable before we can name which practice in a named firm is the generator. `COPY-SHAPE` (method crosses, recording does not; habitat reproduced; receiver remains a different room) is a shape. "Copy the competitor's loyalty program and you will win" is a name, and the figures do not issue names.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Here the closest held terms are membrane, bond, habitat, channel and anchor, not "best practice".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." That is exactly the outreach rule in `02_outreach_email.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on the union: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The question bundles method transfer, habitat fit, channel fidelity and the anchor's payment; F1 to F5 unbundle them.

## 3. Why copying is the tacit-knowledge case, and what that licenses

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the essay closest to this question. It says an expert's compressed message carries "context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." A competitor's visible success is the same kind of artifact: it carries the context (what they launched, at what price) and some subtext (the apparent positioning), but not the pretext, the history of trials and corrections that made it pay. The essay states the transfer rule in the framework's own words: "what passes down is ways of doing things and head starts, never the experience itself. 'Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down' (Fig 4 :22)."

That licences a name in one place only. When the competitor's method is genuinely transmitted through contact (people moving, joint work, correction), the receiver is not reading a recording and Fig 4 permits the crossing. When the transmission is a written case study or a product teardown, it is a recording and the pretext is absent. The figure line governs the boundary; the essay names the mechanism that makes it bite. This is `specified`, not `grounded`: no firm was measured here.

## 4. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE — the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." Here the ground is the market (Fig 2 :29). A firm's return after the transplant is what pays, not the coherence of the boundary claim.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. An acquisition of a competitor's team is not automatically an import that moves the wall; the record has to show which atom arrived and which gap it explains.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* supplies the discriminator. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." A market reading that a firm is profitable cannot say whether the method, the habitat or a transient advantage produced it. "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." This is why F6 stays a declared gap: what is owed is an instrument that separates method from recording in a named firm, not a better argument.

"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly" (Fig 5 :43-44). A copy that explains one competitor's success perfectly is exactly the failure the bargain names.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A famous competitor's strategy is not more copyable because it is famous; fame gives the copying attempt a budget and nothing more. In outreach, a list of prestigious firms that "did the same thing" is the named collapse, not corroboration. Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` through `11` are a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** a test of the boundary claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Two consequences for this question

- **Do not ask which practices are copyable.** That request asks for a name where the framework returns a shape. It also commits the source error from §4: it reads the source (method or artifact) off a projection (the observed success).
- **Ask what the market would have to show.** A source-discriminating observation, not agreement among managers, is what would close F6. Until then: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, with the boundary and its refuters sent ahead of the theory.
