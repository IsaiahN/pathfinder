## Wider literature that bears on this question

1. **Strategic imitation is not one behaviour.** Posen, Ross, Wu, Benigni and Cao (Academy of Management Annals, 2023), read via the University of Groningen portal and Imperial College Spiral: "imitation is core because it is assumed to undermine interfirm performance heterogeneity and erode leaders' competitive advantage", and "there is only one imitation strategy". The second quote is the paper's own named assumption that the boundary claim refuses. It also warns that "imitation may be a key source of dynamic capabilities and innovation", which is the figure-side point that a generator can cross. This source is the closest published statement of the question; it is business-field evidence, so it shares an evidence pool with the question itself and carries no independent stood for a figure verdict.
2. **Imitability and rareness as indicators.** Barney (Journal of Management, 1991), read via Open Access Button: the four indicators are "value, rareness, imitability, and substitutability". This corroborates the anchor condition (the market pays a difference) but says nothing about method versus recording.
3. **The visible artifact is a poor shadow of the competence.** Teece, Pisano and Shuen (Strategic Management Journal, 1997), read via a full-text reproduction: "the balance sheet is a poor shadow of a firm's distinctive competences", and "Whether and how a firm's competitive advantage is eroded depends on the stability of market demand, and the ease of replicability (expanding internally) and imitability (replication by competitors)." This is the closest business-source phrasing of Fig 12 :5 (the invisible bond).
4. **Copying succeeds while the copy is rare.** Cheney and Cote (Proc R Soc B, 2005), read via NCBI PMC: mimic "attack success ... was frequency-dependent, increasing as mimics became rarer relative to their model." An aggressive mimic that becomes common teaches victims to avoid the signal. This is the distinctiveness condition in animal behaviour, a genuinely low-overlap field.
5. **The advantage also fails when the model is absent or the mimic is abundant.** Ries and Mullen (Evolution, 2008), read via the NCBI eutils abstract: "Batesian mimics are predicted to lose their fitness advantage not only in the absence of an unpalatable model, but also when the mimic becomes relatively abundant." Independent low-overlap corroboration of F3, from a different taxon and method than source 4.
6. **Copiers import irrelevant actions as if causal.** Lyons, Young and Keil (PNAS, 2007), read via NCBI PMC: "children are highly prone to mis-encoding them as causally significant. The resulting distortions in children's causal beliefs are the true cause of overimitation". This is the human-cognition mechanism behind the method/recording distinction, from a field with no stake in strategy theory.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source." and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the boundary without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** The formula's "Direction" says a frame "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." Each user must write what would refute their result before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is the artifact to send; it states mode, three checkable sub-claims and their refuters before any theory.
