# Outreach draft — check one boundary before you decide about the theory

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until F1 to F5 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are stated with their modes and the pre-registered refuter is attached. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** A boundary on copying a competitor, with the failure conditions written down first

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about firms and competition. I am not asking you to read the theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** copying a competitor's successful strategy reduces the gap between what a firm expected the market to pay and what it paid when what crosses is a *generator*, a method that can be reapplied with its binding arrangement intact. It increases that gap, and destroys the paid-for distinctiveness, when what crosses is the *recording* of one success: the visible product, price or practice whose arrangement is invisible to a copy. The two further conditions are that the habitat the success depended on is reproduced, or the method is remade by the new level, and that the copying firm remains a different room from the competitor. If it converges to the same closure, the market has no difference left to pay for.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that copying never works, that a named practice is inherently copyable or uncopyable, that this is a result measured in a market, or that the framework can tell you which element of a particular strategy is the method. That last question is a declared gap. It needs an instrument that discriminates source from reading in a named firm; a figure, or a theory, does not supply it.
>
> **What would refute the boundary** (written before this message):
> 1. copying only the visible artifact, with no method transfer, reproduces the market return repeatedly in a new habitat;
> 2. a strategy transplanted into a genuinely different habitat pays the same return with no habitat reproduction and with the channel measured as degraded;
> 3. a market pays two near-identical firms above-average returns indefinitely with no residual difference at the anchor;
> 4. a copy from a same-closure peer moves the wall with no provenance and no gap it explains;
> 5. a recorded artifact copy pays for a fresh occasion without re-derivation, with the cost measured in actions.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw or narrow the boundary. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Figures and formula for direct download (SVG, branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Canonical usage rule: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Worked architecture and its own refused mappings: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a firm you know well and can check against a market. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
