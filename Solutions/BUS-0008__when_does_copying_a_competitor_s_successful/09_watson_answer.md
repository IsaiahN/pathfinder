## QUESTION

When does copying a competitor's successful strategy work, and when does it destroy the distinctiveness that made it successful?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o'| fall after a transplant in some firm-slots and rise in others? R is indexed per firm and per time, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Distinctiveness is a threshold relative to competitors, not a carried property (Fig 12 :87; Fig 11 :49). Option A, copy the visible artifact, is cheap and fast but carries a description of one occasion. Option B, acquire the generator and reproduce its habitat, costs more but lets the method be remade by the new level. What each leads to: A repeated success followed by a silent failure, or a mirror with no paid-for difference; B a preserved difference with the cost of establishment paid once and carried as a method.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Copying works when the generator crosses with its arrangement intact into a reproduced habitat through a maintained channel, and the receiver remains a differing room relative to the model. It destroys distinctiveness when a recording of one occasion crosses instead, the bonds are lost, the habitat is substituted, the channel is saturated, or the receiver converges to the model's closure. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged: method transfer (D3), habitat fit and channel (D2/D4), and the anchor's payment (D1/D2) each have their own loop. Mode `specified`, ungrounded. Which concrete elements are method and which recording is a declared `gap`, closed only by an instrument. The market, not agreement with the competitor, settles any instance.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A firm failing the habitat condition would be sold a better method; a firm failing the method condition would be told to copy harder; a firm that has converged would be told to copy more of the same. Single-lever advice on a union compound leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :57). In outreach, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real, bounded and largely reversible.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted". The boundary and its five refuters are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five refuters above would reverse it: repeated artifact-only returns in a new habitat; equal returns after habitat substitution with a degraded channel; a market paying near-identical firms indefinitely; a same-closure copy moving the wall with no provenance; a recorded copy paying for a fresh occasion with no re-derivation. A source-discriminating instrument that names method versus recording in a firm would close the gap. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
