## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a claim that an idea I changed a social change S, against the rival that I merely reflected S or a change already under way. Option A: read co-occurrence, citation and consensus as evidence that I produced S. Option B: treat the bond as unobserved, require a route-discriminating record and a low-overlap witness, and report `gap` where neither exists. What each leads to: A manufactures a convention out of agreement and writes a phantom lever into the record; B produces a checkable test sequence and an honest stop, at the cost of leaving some cases undecided.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The bond between I and S. Arity: at least three, idea, change, and a background condition that might produce both; a two-place reading cannot express the common cause. Symmetry: production is ordered and swapping operands changes the claim, while co-occurrence is symmetric and carries no direction. Scale: from a text or a reading to a law, an institution or a practice, with contact cascading several steps out (Fig 11). Fig 9 :57-58 names it a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, textual / idea frame.** Actors: authors, texts, concepts, readers, citation chains. Currency: readings, conceptual content, publication dates. Anchor: the dated corpus and archive. This frame can establish that I existed and what it said, and cannot establish route. `single-source`.
- **Domain 2, social-practice frame.** Actors: institutions, laws, behaviours, material conditions, adoption events. Currency: practices, outcomes, dated indicators. Anchor: records of practice, material evidence, administrative data. This frame can establish timing of S and cannot establish that I produced it. `single-source`.
- **Domain 3, contact / verification frame (the union).** Actors: the historian, natural experiments, differentially reached closures. Currency: provenance, lineage, discriminating tests. Anchor: a dated record that neither frame can move, arrival against change, matched sites where I arrived and did not. `triangulated` only when two low-overlap frames range against it separately; otherwise `gap`.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28). Establish absence before mechanism. Seek the route before the attribution. Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

Strain test applied: Fig 13 :4-5 (no rank, lockstep) governs the relation between the two operands; the before-test in Fig 12 :77-79 governs the bond in a single case. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. One live strain remains, the sufficiency of the before-test under preemption, recorded in `01` §6 and `11`.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives and no case is decided. The bond is not readable from content, because convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents and only the record separates them. A dated before-test is a necessary condition for production, not a verdict. A case moves from `gap` to `triangulated candidate` only with a route-discriminating record, a change in contact, and a witness frame whose evidence does not come from the same texts. Consensus, citation and retrospective labels are filters or symptoms, never witnesses. Where the record cannot discriminate, the honest entry is `gap`. This is `general` on the shape and `specified` on the tests, with no case `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. A wrong attribution writes a phantom lever into the record and later work builds on it; the opposite error, refusing a real effect, loses a lever but records the gap and the reason for it. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The avoidable part is spending a reader's independence by sending a finished attribution instead of a test sequence.
- **undoable:** yes. Verdicts are case-local and stamped with their lineage. A newly found dated record can reverse one case without changing the method or withdrawing any figure. No primitive is committed.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dated record showing the change or its necessary mechanism absent before the idea arrived in that closure, a matched pair of closures with the practice appearing only where the idea landed, or a trace that discriminates the two sources would move a case toward `grounded`. A validated route-invariant content marker would break the artefact-cannot-decide verdict. A case passing the before-test with a sufficient backup cause already present would confirm the preemption strain and keep the test necessary-only. An hypothesis that agreement alone settles a case would require the ground to be a frame, which Fig 2 forbids. Until any of these fires, every case verdict stands as `candidate` or `gap`.
