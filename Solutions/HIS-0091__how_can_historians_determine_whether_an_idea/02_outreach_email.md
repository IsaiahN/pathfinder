# Outreach draft: check the test first, then decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through a public venue in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 is stated in your own words and you can say what would change your mind. The offer collapses on contact if the first item is a theory rather than a checkable output.

---

**Subject:** One question about an idea and a social change, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I am working on a narrow methodological question in intellectual history and would like your judgement on one output before I ask you to look at anything else. I am not asking you to agree with a framework, and I am not asking you to trust me.
>
> **The question in effect terms:** for an idea I and a social change S in the same domain, what kind of bond holds? Two readings are on the table. Production: S was absent before I fired, so I changed the practice. Reflection: S or its causes were already present, and I expressed them. A third reading is a common cause behind both. Citation counts, influence, and agreement among historians do not separate these, because all three can produce the same text.
>
> **The output to check (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the tests, nothing `grounded`):** the bond is not recoverable from the artefact. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents, and only the record of where each came from separates them. What can be tested is a sequence: (1) was the change, or the mechanism that had to produce it, present before the idea arrived in this closure? (2) is there a dated record of the route, arrival against change, or matched sites where the idea arrived and did not? (3) does a second frame whose evidence does not draw on the same texts range against the same dated record separately? If (1) fails the production claim is removed. If (2) and (3) cannot be run, the honest entry is `gap`, not a guess.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that a widely cited idea was therefore effective; that an idea that reflected a change was therefore worthless; that any particular thinker did or did not cause anything. No case in this folder has been checked against a ground, so no case verdict is asserted.
>
> **What would refute the output, written down before this email:**
> 1. a marker in the text itself that can only be written before the change and never after, and that classifies cases correctly against a ground;
> 2. a case where the change demonstrably predates the idea's arrival and a correct account still calls the idea its producer, with no later redirection;
> 3. two independent frames agreeing on production against a ground that later shows no attributable change in contact;
> 4. a case decided with no route-discriminating record and later confirmed by one that was found.
>
> If any holds, I will withdraw the corresponding claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed. Please record your verdict on this item before reading any of the links below.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Full architecture, with the citation list and the section "Three mappings offered and declined": https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
> - Provenance and the distinction-horizon argument: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
>
> **If you want to reuse the method:** the formula is in the architecture link. Run it on a case you know well and can check against dated records. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. The architecture's own demo note says outputs produced this way are *"simulacra"* unless derived from the real architecture or the formula worked directly; a reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [name]
