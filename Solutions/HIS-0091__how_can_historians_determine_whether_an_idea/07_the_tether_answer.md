# The Tether's answer: cause or reflection?

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the per-case tests, not `grounded`.** A simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading and not a verdict on any actual case.

## 1. The residual it answers

An idea I and a social change S are observed together. Is the bond production (S absent before I fired, so I changed the practice) or reflection (S or its causes already present, so I expressed them)? A common cause behind both is a third live reading. The question is a **union compound**, not one hard question (Fig 9 :57-58: *"A case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **The bond is not readable from the artefact.** Fig 8 :51-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 13 :129: *"the order is not recoverable from the artefact. It is recoverable from the lineage."* An idea that caused a practice and an idea that summarized it can say the same words.
2. **Production has a signature, but it is a hoop test only.** Fig 12 :77: *"A produces B; B did not exist before"*; Fig 12 :79: *"is there a B before A fires? No."* Failing it removes the production claim. Passing it does not establish one, because causes need not be necessary when a second process was ready to produce the same effect. Fig 9 :42: *"narrowing gets you close, the ground decides"*.
3. **Use a low-overlap witness, not a room of agreeable readers.** Fig 8 :29: *"low overlap: B does not draw on A’s evidence"*; Fig 2 :31-32: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* A second frame whose evidence does not come from the same texts can range against the same dated record separately.
4. **"Changed society" must mean changed contact.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Citation counts and reputation are filters for which case to open, not witnesses (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
5. **The retrospective label is not a verdict.** Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."* A clean lineage shows a ruling was reached legitimately, not that it was right (Fig 10 :65-66).
6. **Idea and society are in lockstep, not senior and junior.** Fig 13 :4-5: *"There is no order of rank here. The two kinds are in lockstep, a crossing is a change of role rather than a step upward, and no member of the pair is senior to the other."* The direction is a finding of the case, not an assumption of the vocabulary.
7. **Where the record cannot discriminate, report `gap`.** Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.

The per-case verdicts are kept separate: a case with a dated arrival, an absent-before change, a credible mechanism and a low-overlap witness is `triangulated candidate`; a case with only sequence and content similarity is `single-source`, bond undetermined; a case with no discriminating record is `gap`. They are never averaged into "ideas usually matter".

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular idea caused or failed to cause anything. No case was checked against a ground.
- Not that an idea that reflected a change was worthless. An output can be a precise diagnostic of a process.
- Not that influence, citation or consensus evidence causation. They are filters for the budget.
- Not that absence of a record proves reflection. Absence is a report about the archive.
- Not that the before-test is sufficient. Preemption and overdetermination keep it a necessary condition.
- Not `grounded`. Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."*

## 4. What would settle it, case by case

A source-discriminating record, not a closer reading and not a vote. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: the instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Absence test:** was the change, or the mechanism that had to produce it, absent when the idea arrived in this closure? A dated record can fire this.
- **Route test:** is there a lineage record of arrival against change, matched sites where the idea arrived and did not, or traces that discriminate the two sources?
- **Contact test:** did a practice appear that had not appeared before, in the closure where the idea arrived and not in the matched closure where it did not?
- **Witness test:** does a second frame with non-overlapping evidence range against the same record separately and reach the same bond without being shown the first frame's conclusion?
- **Standing falsifiers from `01`:** a route-invariant content marker; a case where the change demonstrably predates the idea and production is still the correct description; independent agreement that the ground later reverses; a decision later confirmed by a record that was absent at the time.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached for most cases at this budget, not unreachable in principle.** The archive is not an anchor: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks... If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."* (Fig 2 :29-30). Traces exist for some cases and not others; *How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the contrast: *"Some processes leave traces and depth recovers them; some produce identical output and no depth helps."* The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not tell you which thinker mattered. It says that an idea and a social change can look identical whether the idea produced the change or merely reported it, because convergent derivation and adopted import look the same in the contents and only the record of where each came from separates them. What it offers is a test sequence: check whether the change was absent before the idea fired, but treat that as a necessary condition only; look for a dated route rather than a closer reading; bring in a second frame whose evidence does not come from the same texts; and count a change in contact, not citations or agreement. Where the record cannot discriminate, it writes `gap` rather than a guess. Consensus is a filter for which case to open, never a witness. Every claim carries its mode, and no historical case in this folder has been settled.
