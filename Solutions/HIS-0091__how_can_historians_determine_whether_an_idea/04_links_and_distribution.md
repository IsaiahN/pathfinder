## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending a finished attribution before anyone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the bond question and the refuter.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*. Ask what they would expect to see if the idea really had produced the change, before giving them the article.
- **Fig 7 :35** - *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Do not select recipients by likely agreement.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* A wrong causal attribution writes a phantom lever into the record, so spend effort on independence.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the bond question and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure, and record the answer; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** the residual, the shape and the pre-registered refuter. Ask for a verdict on the test sequence alone. This is Fig 10 :52, *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, offer the three framework links. Their stage-1 verdict is already recorded, so a later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method to reuse. Fig 4 :22 permits methods to cross. Each user must state their own ground, write their own refuter before the run, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The output here is a test sequence, not a case verdict. If a reader supplies F1's route-invariant content marker or one of the other four refuters, stop and re-census before sending anything else.
