## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone, before comparing with the primary's conclusion, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the unobserved bond between an idea and a social change. Arity: three, not two, because a hidden background condition can produce both; Figure 2 names that collapse as *"collapse 2: common cause (hidden shared source)"*. Symmetry: production is ordered, co-occurrence is symmetric and therefore directionless. Scale: the text or reading is first order, the practice or institution second or third order, with the residual cascading outward (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Domain 1, textual / idea frame.** Actors: authors, texts, concepts, readers. Currency: readings and content. Anchor: the dated corpus. This frame cannot see route, so its verdict on the bond must be `single-source` and non-deciding.
- **Domain 2, social-practice frame.** Actors: institutions, laws, behaviours, material conditions. Currency: dated practices and outcomes. Anchor: records of practice. Gives timing, not mechanism; `single-source`.
- **Domain 3, contact / verification frame.** Actors: the historian and a differentially reached closure. Currency: provenance and discriminating tests. Anchor: a dated record neither frame can move. `triangulated` only under low overlap and separate ranging; otherwise `gap`.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); establish absence before mechanism; seek route before attribution; use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

My census matches the primary's three domains and its union reading. ## Round trip gap `R_T`

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 8 :51-52 (route in the record), Fig 12 :77-79 (production test), Fig 11 :40 (contact), Fig 9 :60 (filters not verdicts).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand "the idea changed society" versus "the idea merely reflected the times", as used in the question and in ordinary reference work.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a case-level claim with a dated arrival, a dated change, a named closure, a matched comparison, and a witness frame.

**`R_T` is nonzero.** Five things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **bond type** itself, production versus common cause versus sequence;
- the **absence condition**, that the change or its necessary mechanism was absent before arrival;
- the **common-cause reading**, which the two-place phrasing cannot even express;
- the **contact requirement**, that a practice changed rather than a citation count;
- the **stop rule**, that no discriminating record means `gap`, not "probably reflection".

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the two readings are not the same claim, and the route is in the record. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
