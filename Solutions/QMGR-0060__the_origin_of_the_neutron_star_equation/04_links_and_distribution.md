## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- `Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."` and `Fig 4 :25: "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."` Sending a full EOS table or a completed framework before someone has described a residual sends a recording. Lead with the shape claim and its refuter.
- `Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."` A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- `Fig 9 :27-28: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."` Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- `Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"` A send selected for people already likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- `Fig 7 :35: "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."` Choose venues by ground visibility, not by receptiveness.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its five refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (4) only on request, the formula as a method with a fresh refuter required from each user.

1. **Stage 1:** residual and shape claim, mode labelled, five refuters written in advance. No theory, no links. This is `Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."`
