## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** `Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."` A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes its agreement worthless (`Fig 2 :40`).
2. **The outside checker must not hold the interiors.** `Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."` The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
3. **Prestige cannot settle the question.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read 2026-11-23: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*
