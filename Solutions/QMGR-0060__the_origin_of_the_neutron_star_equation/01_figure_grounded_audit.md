## 0. The residual under audit

`R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` per slot (`Symbols table :36-40`; Fig 1 :19).

- **What is being decided:** what pressure-energy relation `P(ε)` connects the microscopic theory to the measured star, and what produces it at `n ≈ 2 to 10 n_sat`.
- **Arity:** the EOS is a one-parameter relation, but its determination is a three-place contact between (i) a low-density frame, (ii) a high-density frame and (iii) an observation. The map from EOS to observables is many-to-one.
- **Symmetry:** the forward map (EOS to M, R, Λ through the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff equations) is ordered, not symmetric. Swapping an EOS for another that yields the same observables is not an equivalence that the ground can resolve.
- **Scale:** from the fermi scale of the strong interaction to the kilometre scale of the star. The membrane between them is lossy (Fig 4 :27-30).
- **What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (Formula "Direction"):** a shape with three parts: a low-density boundary band, a high-density boundary band, and an observational corridor, with an explicitly empty slot for the phase name. This is written down before the run so it can accrue a residual like anything else.
- **What each option leads to:** asserting a phase (A) yields an ungrounded name and spends the independence of the checker; keeping the shape (B) yields checkable constraints and a recorded stop.

Question-class first (`Fig 9 :4-5`): *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones."* The EOS question does not shrink with effort because the low-density rule, the high-density rule and the observational rule each work on a different density subset. Split it rather than search it.

**Partition or union?** Union, not partition (Fig 9 :54-57). D1, D2 and D4 overlap on the same `P(ε)`; the overlap is the pressure curve. A single lever, for example one favoured phase model, clears only the region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. Unbundle before searching again.

**Dependency order (Fig 3 :20).** Perception reached (D4 observations), vocabulary partly reached (D1 and D2 have terms for what the matter affords), objective not reached (what would count as "the origin" has no instrument), planning above the break. The chain breaks between links 3 and 4: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27). Effort spent above the break, for example more phenomenological EOS parameterizations, arrives at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :19).

**Census note kept visible.** The first pass treated D2 as a full domain with an anchor. That was a strain: asymptotic freedom does not settle `P(ε)` at `2 to 10 n_sat`, and the world source Zhou 2024 reports that pQCD uncertainties currently preclude meaningful bounds. Re-census demoted D2 to a boundary condition with a source gate, and promoted D4 to the only non-updating anchor. The correction is retained, not silently erased.

## 3. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument (Seat Map :287: *"And a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*). The shape claim is refuted if any of the following fires:

1. **Instrument fires.** A first-principles or quantum-simulation calculation returns `P(ε)` with controlled error at `2 to 10 n_sat`, passes a source gate and shadow test, and names a phase. (This would close the D3 gap.)
2. **Discriminator fires.** Two stars with indistinguishable `(M, R, Λ)` posteriors are shown to have different internal phases by an independent observable (post-merger spectral peak, cooling curve, or another mode). That breaks the many-to-one inversion of F7.
3. **Boundary fails.** A measurement shows χEFT failing below `1 to 2 n_sat`, or pQCD failing above `30 to 50 n_sat`, with an anchor that does not update. That breaks F2 or F3.
4. **Union fails.** A single lever clears all three unbundled conditions (low-density band, high-density band, observational corridor) with no categorically different residue. That refutes the union reading of F9/F10's structure.
5. **Framework level.** One of the six substrate terms is derived from the other five (`Fig 13 :41`), or a state-state/structure-structure composition is admitted that cannot be refused. That breaks the framework, not just this application.

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded` beyond the reported measurements themselves.
