# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide which phase it is

**Use:** one to one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the mode labels and the `gap` slot in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` are intact. If the slot gets filled with a favourite phase, the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

| the move | figure-grounded reason |

## Two-stage discipline

1. **Stage 1:** send the shape claim and the five refuters. Ask what the recipient would count as a failure. Record that verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt in only:** if they are curious, offer the links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (`Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"`).
3. **Stage 3, optional:** offer the formula as a method to reuse, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

The chain is valid only if the stage-1 output survives. The first refuter is the one most likely to fire first in a real check, so it is written plainly rather than technically.
