# The Tether's answer on the origin of the neutron star equation of state

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the name of the phase. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The pressure-energy relation `P(ε)` between roughly two and ten nuclear saturation densities is not returned by any single first-principles method with a non-updating anchor. Low-density chiral EFT returns a controlled band up to about one to two `n_sat`; high-density perturbative QCD returns a pressure at `30 to 50 n_sat` with uncertainties currently too large to constrain star densities; observations cut a corridor. The question asks for "the origin", which is a union compound of three conditions with an empty phase slot, not one hard problem (`Fig 9 :4-5`, `Fig 9 :55`).

## 2. The shape, one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate

**D1, low-density frame. `specified`, triangulated.** A structure must mediate between the boundary bands. `Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one."` The χEFT band is a settled molecule and becomes an atom for what composes over it (`Fig 12 :104`). Sharpening the same expansion does not add an atom (`Fig 6 :30`). Verdict: partial authority to `1 to 2 n_sat`, not extendable by refinement.

**D2, high-density frame. `specified`, triangulated.** pQCD is a differing closure, so it can supply an import, but the import is a thermodynamic boundary condition with a source gate and shadow test, not a ground reading at star densities (`Fig 6 :23`, `Fig 8 :51-52`). Verdict: boundary only; the world source Zhou 2024 reports its bounds are *"orthogonal to current or expected astrophysical bounds."*

**D3, intermediate regime. `gap`, triangulated.** `Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."` No such anchor exists at `2 to 10 n_sat`; the sign problem blocks the lattice instrument (`Fig 6 :34-35`). `Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"` and `Fig 13 :134: "So the system can report that it lacks something rather than producing an answer anyway. Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"` Verdict: the phase name is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle (`Fig 9 :44`). The habitat of candidate phases is enumerated, never composed (`Fig 11 :43`), and valence is relative to the live residual (`Fig 11 :49`).

**D4, observations. `grounded` for the measurements, `specified` for the EOS implication, triangulated.** `Fig 2 :36: "An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."` GW170817 and NICER do not update on the model. They cut the corridor and rule out models, but the inverse map is many-to-one (`Fig 9 :27-28`), so they cannot name the phase.

**D5, inference. `general` structural.** The posterior over EOS bands is a channel that can decay: `Fig 10 :24: "the ground does not decay, the channel does"` and `Fig 10 :26: "stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"`. Agreement among models sharing χEFT input is one evidence pool (`Fig 2 :40`).

**Combined, never averaged.** Over the full atom set, a first-order-transition EOS and a smooth-crossover EOS are isomers (`Fig 12 :117`); over the measurement corridor they are a functional group (`Fig 12 :124`). The three conditions are a union, so one lever leaves the intersections standing. There is no last room (`Fig 7 :6`), so no EOS closes further inquiry.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a theory of everything; no final EOS. `Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room"`
- Not a name for the phase. The slot stays `???`; deconfined quark matter, hyperons and meson condensates are candidates in the habitat, not the answer.
- Not that the sign problem is solved or that lattice QCD returns the core EOS.
- Not that perturbative QCD measures `5 n_sat`. It is a boundary condition with provenance.
- Not that a posterior is the ground, and not that agreement among models is corroboration.
- Not a derivation of one condition from the others, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement at `2 to 10 n_sat` settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved it. Here:

1. **Instrument test:** a sign-problem-free calculation or quantum simulation returning `P(ε)` at `2 to 10 n_sat` with controlled error, a provenance gate and a shadow test that names a phase.
2. **Discriminator test:** two stars with indistinguishable `(M, R, Λ)` posteriors but different internal phases shown by a post-merger spectral peak, cooling curve or another independent mode.
3. **Boundary test:** the low-density band failing below `1 to 2 n_sat`, or the high-density boundary failing above `30 to 50 n_sat`, against an anchor that does not update.
4. **Union test:** one lever clearing all three conditions with no categorically different residue.
5. **Framework test:** deriving one of the six substrate terms from the other five (`Fig 13 :41`).

Standing falsifiers are written before the run in `01` §3 and repeated in `08` and `09`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable in principle.** `Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."` The candidate-phase list is read off the world, not composed; what cannot yet be perceived or measured is the residual (`Fig 11 :48`). The six-term set is a visible guess, not nature's own list. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the origin of the neutron star equation of state, and it does not claim a final theory. It says the question is a union of three conditions with different anchors: a low-density band that returns something, a high-density pressure boundary that returns something, and mass, radius and tidal-deformability measurements that do not change because a model thinks otherwise. Between them, at two to ten nuclear saturation densities, the slot stays open because no instrument currently discriminates among the candidate sources of the same reading. Over a fixed set of degrees of freedom a first-order transition and a smooth crossover are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, decisive in behaviour; over the measurement corridor they are one functional group. What the framework offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the five falsifiers written in advance, and the kind of instrument that would close the slot. The centre stays open until the ground settles it.
