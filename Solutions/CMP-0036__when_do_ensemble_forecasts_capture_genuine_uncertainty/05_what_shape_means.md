## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical local figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel* (carried quote): *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question, the shape is therefore not a number. It is the four-condition separator: members differ in what they draw on; the variation changes reach; each member is ranged against a non-updating ground; the use rule is refusable. Those conditions constrain any acceptable answer and do not fill the slot with a dispersion value, a member count, or the name of a missing physics scheme.

A shape constrains; it does not decide. It says what an ensemble must be like before it can be called uncertain, and it refuses to say which specific parameterisation is missing in a given forecast problem.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (carried): use the vocabulary already available that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* This is why the residual in `01` §0 is written before the domains are named. If the domains were named first, any ensemble type would seem to fit.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping (carried): the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: describe what would have to be true of spread in effect terms (it must change when the ground changes, it must not be reproducible by reweighting one model, it must disappear when members are made dependent) before choosing an ensemble design or a verification metric. Otherwise climatology, hindcasts, or a favourite spread-skill score will seem to fit automatically.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* Here that means a verification that names which assumption change produced the change in outcome, on held-out observations.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. In forecasting, that is a member drawn from a genuinely different model family with a recorded ancestry and a demonstrated gap it closes.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens this: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

A model family is a visible guess, not nature's own list. Calling one family *the* answer to a slot turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a reading cannot settle its own source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow and blue, or RGB, or CMYK. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

For an ensemble, the reading is the spread. Several member structures can produce the same spread: many independent members, or few members with large perturbations, or many dependent members from one family. The spread does not comment on the source. Naming the source from the spread is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator (a dependence diagnostic, a genealogy check, a held-out verification), not a name read off the projection.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* This is Fig 1 :27 in the essays' vocabulary: a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid naming a model family or a parameterisation?

**No, with a condition.** The canonical phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in Γ. A model family, a parameterisation, or a data set is already an available binding in the forecasting frame. Naming one is a provenanced binding onto a held atom, not the invention of a new primitive. What is forbidden is two different moves:

1. claiming the binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :21: *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*);
2. calling the binding a derivation of the framework when it is an import, without source gate or shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52).

**Correction to my own first pass.** In `01` §3 F12 I initially wrote the gap as if the framework could not name any member or parameterisation at all. That overstates the rule. The correct form: no figure returns a universal *threshold*, and no figure names the specific *missing* atom in an instance; held bindings may be named if their provenance is recorded. Both the narrowed form and the original wording are preserved in `01` §6 rather than silently replaced.

## 6. Consequences for verification and outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (carried): *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known forecasters who agree with the separator is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the check against a ground.

**Correspondence across the crowd within.** The *Wisdom of the crowd* source opened this session reports Vul and Pashler's finding that averaging a person's two guesses works less well when the guesses are made in immediate succession, because *"guesses in the immediate condition were less independent of each other (an anchoring effect)"*. Rauhut and Lorenz found that *"asking oneself an infinite number of times does not surpass the accuracy of asking just one other individual."* This is the human-cognition version of F3: more draws from one closure do not buy what a different closure buys. It is a low-overlap corroborator, not a figure line.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (carried): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the separator and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. One-paragraph reading of "shape" for CMP-0036

A shape here is the four-condition separator that any genuine-uncertainty claim must satisfy: differing evidence among members, a change of reach rather than a re-spelling of one closure, a non-updating verifying ground against which each member ranges separately, and a use rule that stays refusable. It is not a dispersion value, a member count, or the name of a missing scheme. Those are licenses that the ground or a provenanced import must supply. The framework returns the kind of thing an answer must be, states what would refute it, and stops where the instruments can no longer tell sources apart (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Indiscernibility is not identity, and the record of the route is what keeps the stop checkable.
