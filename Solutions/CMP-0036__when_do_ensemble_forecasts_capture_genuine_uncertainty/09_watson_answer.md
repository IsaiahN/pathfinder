## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what makes the spread of an ensemble a reading of genuine uncertainty rather than a re-spelling of one generating closure. Option A: treat spread width as the measure of uncertainty. Option B: treat genuineness as a four-condition separator over independence, reach, ground contact and use. What each leads to: A makes a frame score itself with a quantity it produces and cannot distinguish a large but dependent ensemble from a genuinely diverse one; B yields per-domain verdicts, refuters written in advance, and an honest gap where no threshold is derivable. The residual is `R_s = |Γ(b,a) - o'|` per slot, where `Γ` is the generating frame, `a` a member run and `o'` the verifying observation.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: a union of four overlapping conditions (independence, reach, ground contact, use), not one dial. Symmetry: spread is symmetric under member swap, so it cannot encode which member carried the information or the direction of the error; the relation that matters is a typed bond. Scale: first order inside one model (parameter and initial-condition space), second order between model frames. Fig 9 :4-5 says a disagreement that does not shrink with effort across subsets is several questions, so split it rather than search.
- **Domain 1, ensemble generation.** Actors: member runs, initial conditions, parameter sets, physics schemes. Currency: disagreement, that is spread. Anchor: the generating model family and its data. Question type: partition at first order (what composes inside one closure) and union at second order (which closures are present at all).
- **Domain 2, structural reach.** Actors: model structures, parameterisations, discretization, imports. Currency: reach, that is what can be represented at all. Anchor: the closure boundary, which only an import from a differing frame moves. Question type: rearranged atom versus imported atom.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: observations, hindcasts, reforecasts. Currency: settlement, that is whether the ground pays. Anchor: the observation, which does not update on what the ensemble reported. Question type: triangulation, each frame ranged separately against the same anchor.
- **Domain 4, use.** Actors: forecasters, users, decision rules. Currency: cost of being wrong, value of independence. Anchor: a real outcome and loss, where one exists; otherwise this domain is not yet nameable. Question type: union of cost and convention, with the convention forbidden to decide the outcome.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual per slot before naming a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); refuse self-certification before minting a metric (Formula step 2); settle generation and reach before comparison (Fig 12 :104); range each member against the ground before use (Fig 2 :45-46); select the next member against the surfaced residual (Fig 7 :36); allow a use rule only after the ground is reached (Fig 10 :47).

Strain test applied: Fig 13's six physical terms were first mapped literally onto statistical ensembles, which strained. Re-census assigns Fig 13 to closure structure and finiteness of the atom set, not to meteorological variables, under the domain-agnostic rule Fig 4 :20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* No strain remains after re-census except the two live items named in the verdict.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

An ensemble captures genuine uncertainty when its members differ in evidence, the variation changes reach rather than reweighting one closure, each member is ranged against a non-updating observation, and the use rule stays refusable. It merely repeats structural assumptions when its members are perturbations of one family or nominally distinct models with shared ancestry and calibration data, when spread is used as its own evidence of quality, when calibration is fitted to the ensemble's own output, or when new members are added without being selected against the surfaced residual. The hidden substance is the dependence structure: same members, different bond, different verification behaviour. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result. The universal threshold and the name of a missing atom are declared `gap`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. If the separator is wrong, the error is in one of four named conditions with a named refuter each, so it can be found rather than hidden. The larger cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a number or a conclusion instead of a checkable separator. Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No closure has to be withdrawn to retract the claim. Each condition can be refused separately, the dependence diagnostic can be replaced, and a corrected packet can supersede a sent one. The record keeps the earlier wording visible in `01` §6.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A dependence diagnostic showing member count predicts skill after conditioning on ancestry would fire the first refuter; a perturbation that changes the representable outcome set and is paid for at the ground would fire the third; an instrument that distinguishes which assumption change produced a given spread would name the source and close the metamerism; held-out verification against a non-updating target would settle reliability; a threshold shown to be unrefusable would break Domain 4. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
