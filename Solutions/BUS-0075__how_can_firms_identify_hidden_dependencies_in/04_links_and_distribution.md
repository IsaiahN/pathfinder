## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **The residual is measured in the field.** The BCI and Zurich figure that 40 percent of disruptions originated upstream of prime contractors is the closest thing to a count of Fig 11 :52's "three steps out". It is a filter and one witness, not the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
2. **Propagation is a network property.** Yang et al. 2021 find amplification and discontinuous fragility in supply networks and that backup suppliers and surplus inventory improve robustness. That corroborates the bond reading (F6) and the habitat-substitution warning (F9) from a different field. It does not tell a firm which node to map; that stays a gap.
3. **The DDT case is already inside the figure.** Fig 11 :52-53 names Carson 1962 and biomagnification through the food chain to eggshell thinning. The ecology page confirms the mechanism and the raptor impact, but because the figure already cites the case, the page is not an independent witness. This is declared as high overlap in the ledger.
4. **Amplification upstream is a second operations witness.** The bullwhip literature corroborates the shape that a small disturbance at one level grows at the next. It shares the broad supply-chain pool with the BCI survey, so the two count as one witness for the verdict.
5. **A practical constraint on submission.** The AOM AI policy noted in the BUS-0001 pilot is a reminder that simulated packages like this one may not be submittable as empirical work. The CSCMP research-proposal route and SSRN preprint route are the appropriate opt-in venues here; check each venue's current policy before sending.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25**: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down. A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."* Sending the whole framework before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42**: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28**: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what the recipient would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40**: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: prestige gives a claim a budget to be tried; it does not settle it. The ask is for a check, not an endorsement.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record that verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1:** send the residual and the refuter, not the source. Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, offer the three links. Their verdict on stage 1 is recorded *before* any theory is shown, and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
- **Stage 3:** offer the formula as a method after the verdict. Fig 4 :22: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode, because *"the output is only as good as the validator"* (RSI essay).

**Hard precondition:** the three stages only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuter in `01` section 4 is written and visible, so the offer can be tested as it stands.
