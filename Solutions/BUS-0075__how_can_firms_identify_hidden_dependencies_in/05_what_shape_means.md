## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the thing that fills it. For BUS-0075, the shape is: *enumerate the habitat outward from the failed node until the cascade stops mattering, keep the channel to the realized event reachable, discriminate a shared source from independent failure with a grown instrument, and keep depth and discrimination separate.* The name would be "supplier X at tier 3 is the critical dependency". The framework does not supply that name.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurrent new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* In this question the known vocabulary is "direct supplier", "lead time", "quality escape"; the recurring new terms are "sub-tier", "shared source", "condition", "cascade".
- Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect / rather than the cause"*. Describe the stopped line and the depth at which it became visible, not "a resilience gap".
- Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* and *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."* This is the key difference from a bill of materials: the list is read off the world, not assembled from a parts taxonomy.
- Fig 11 :48: *"You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual."* The hidden dependency is precisely what cannot yet be perceived; it is the residual, not a missing row in a database.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* In supply-chain terms: when you can predict which nodes will co-fail under a perturbation, the map has become the answer.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Here the ground is the realized supply event. A source-discriminating instrument has to show that supplier A and supplier B failed because of one sub-tier source, rather than by coincidence.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A supplier's own supplier map, a logistics provider's shipment data or an industry registry can supply the atom. It must be recorded as imported, with provenance, or it is silently adopted and later mistaken for a derivation.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator argument directly. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* A supplier risk score that reads "high risk" for two suppliers cannot say whether they share one root. The resolution in the essay came *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument"*, the spectrometer. *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 4. Why "bigger map" is the specific forbidden error

A vendor risk register is a reading of a projection. Adding more suppliers to it adds more readings of the same projection; it does not add a discriminator. That is the metamerism error applied to procurement: several distinct underlying structures (one shared sub-tier source, two correlated geographies, genuine independence) produce the same "high risk" reading. What is owed is an instrument that distinguishes sources, not a larger table.

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A bill of materials records ingredients. The dependency bond between two tiers is the half that was not recorded, and nothing downstream signals that anything was lost. This is the structural form of the same error.

## 5. The question is a union, so two shapes are needed

The essay's bundle passage (*"a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*) matches Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* The two shapes are:

- **Depth shape (C1):** enumerate outward until the cascade stops mattering (Fig 11 :44, :52). Instrument: contact coverage by tier.
- **Discrimination shape (C2):** tell a shared source from independent failure (Fig 2 :6-7, :18-21). Instrument: co-failure under a perturbation with provenance for the shared atom.

A firm failing C1 and a firm failing C2 are not different degrees of one failure. Reporting a single "supply chain risk score" is a pooled reading (Fig 10 :26) and a frame-internal quantity (Fig 1 :27); it is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).

## 6. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A widely adopted visibility platform, a well-known consultancy framework or a famous analyst's ranking is prestige. It can fund a mapping exercise; it cannot settle whether a hidden dependency exists. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried against a realized event, and let the event decide.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* And: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the supply-chain claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 8. Correction to my own earlier reading

An early draft of this section treated "shape, not a name" as a blanket ban on identifying any specific supplier. That was overstated. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. In this question the atoms (suppliers, parts, sites, conditions) are all already in the firm's world; what is missing is the bond and the instrument that discriminates sources. Naming a sub-tier source is therefore not forbidden in principle. It is **unlicensed until an instrument discriminates it**, which is a different claim and the one now recorded in section 3 and in `01` F10. Both the earlier overstatement and its narrowing are kept here so neither is quietly lost.
