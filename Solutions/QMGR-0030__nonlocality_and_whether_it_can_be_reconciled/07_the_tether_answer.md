(2) Relativistic causality, which hides two conditions: no controllable superluminal signalling, and no preferred foliation in the fundamental dynamics. The first is settled at the operational level. Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either". So this is a union compound with its own loop and its own ground, not one hard problem to be solved by a single clever interpretation.
