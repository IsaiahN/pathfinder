## 3. What in the world sources bears directly on the claim

1. **The operational reconciliation is already there.** Chiao, Kwiat and Steinberg (1995) state that "quantum nonlocality is not inconsistent with Einstein causality." SEP §8.2 adds that quantum correlations cannot be exploited for superluminal signalling in the absence of nonlocal Hamiltonian terms. This is settled at the operational level. Do not present the question as if no reconciliation existed.
2. **No-signalling is not enough for the structural question.** SEP §8.2 says compatibility with a relativistic spacetime structure "is a distinct issue from Lorentz covariance of the equations expressing the theory's dynamical laws", and that de Broglie-Bohm requires a nonrelativistic spacetime structure while permitting no signalling. This is the reason for F1's split.
3. **Bell tests cannot identify which assumption failed.** Hossenfelder and Palmer (2020): "Bell-type tests cannot tell us which of the two options is correct." So the experiment refuses the local closure but does not name the import. This is the world-side counterpart of the figure's shape-not-a-name limit.
4. **A conflict, kept visible.** The Nobel press release says quantum mechanics "cannot be replaced by a theory that uses hidden variables"; SEP says a hidden-variable theory exists and Bell's theorem is not a no-go theorem for hidden variables tout court. The term was narrowed to *local* hidden variables in `01` F7. Both statements are kept.
