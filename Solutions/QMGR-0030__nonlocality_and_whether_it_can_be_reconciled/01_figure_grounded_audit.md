## 0. The residual in effect terms

**R:** Can Bell-violating correlation (nonlocality) and relativistic causality be composed into one deeper theory?

- **Arity:** two conditions, not one. The empirical record gives (i) correlations that violate a local-realist inequality and (ii) no controllable superluminal signal. The phrase "relativistic causality" hides a second split: no-signalling is one condition, absence of a preferred foliation in the fundamental dynamics is another.
- **Symmetry:** a Bell correlation is symmetric under exchange of the two wings. No-signalling is one-way (parameter independence: a local marginal does not depend on the remote setting). The framework's comparison bond ⋛ is "a threshold relation between two quantities" (Fig 12 :86), not a symmetric join. Reading the correlation as a one-way signal is a bond-type error, handled in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- **Scale:** first order at spacelike separation (the experiment); second order at the subquantum or ontological level (the deeper theory). The two scales are related by a lossy transform, so a result at one does not transfer to the other without a measured round trip.
- **Anchor:** experiment, not agreement. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

**Option A:** they cannot be reconciled; a real superluminal influence exists and Lorentz invariance fails at some level. Leads to a preferred foliation hidden at accessible energies.
**Option B:** they are already reconciled operationally (quantum theory is no-signalling), and the open task is ontological. Leads to a split residual and a search for an import beyond the local-realist closure.
**Bound answer term (Direction discipline):** "a deeper theory that reproduces the observed nonlocal correlations, preserves no-signalling, and has no observable preferred foliation." Binding this as an ordinary term makes it refusable, rather than leaving it as an invisible preference.

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1: The residual is a union of two conditions, not one hard question (governing finding)

Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"

Fig 9 :55-58: "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."

Bind the two conditions as ordinary terms:
1. **No-signalling / parameter independence:** a local outcome distribution is unaffected by the remote setting. This is settled operationally by experiment and by the no-signalling theorem (world sources, `09_watson_answer.md`).
2. **Relativistic spacetime structure:** the fundamental dynamics does not need a distinguished foliation. This is *not* settled by (1). The SEP entry on Bell's theorem states explicitly that no-signalling is insufficient, since de Broglie-Bohm theory does not permit signalling yet requires a nonrelativistic spacetime structure (Myrvold, Genovese and Shimony, 2024, §8.2).

The question as usually posed conflates them. Splitting is not a dodge: it is what the figure requires before a search. **Mode: `specified` for the split; the empirical status of each condition is reported separately.** Status: single-source for the figure line (Fig 9), triangulated with the SEP §8.2 distinction as low-overlap world corroboration.

**Refuter:** show that no-signalling and the absence of a preferred foliation are the same condition under every formulation, so one experiment settles both; or demonstrate a quantum correlation that permits controllable superluminal signalling. Either fires F1.

What falls is the reach: local-realist compositions can no longer be composed over as a route to the observed correlations. The framework's contribution is not the physics but the bookkeeping: a refusal is owed, not an explanatory story about how locality "really" works.

**Refuter:** exhibit a local hidden-variable model that reproduces all quantum predictions while keeping statistical independence (measurement independence). That would turn the refusal into a bad refusal and restore the reach.

Bell's theorem proves the quantum correlations are unreachable from that closure. This is *underivable in principle*, not *unreachable at this budget*, so the instruction is OUTWARD (import), not more search. The framework does not select among them. It says what the import must do: close R while paying the MDL bargain and surviving the source gate and shadow test (Fig 8 :9-13, Fig 6 :12-13).

### F5: No-signalling is not a verdict about relativistic spacetime structure

This finding comes from a re-census triggered by F1, not from a new figure line. The empirical facts are settled by the world; the figure contribution is the rule that an instrument can be wrong on an axis it does not measure (Fig 2 :37-38: "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure.").

SEP §8.2 states that the no-signalling theorem "is insufficient" for compatibility with relativity, because de Broglie-Bohm theory requires a nonrelativistic spacetime structure while permitting no signalling. Hossenfelder and Palmer (2020) add that Bell-type tests cannot tell which assumption was violated. So an experiment that confirms no-signalling does not settle the deeper-theory question. Two instruments, two axes.

**Mode: `grounded` for the empirical distinction (world), `specified` for the figure reading.** Status: single-source for the world claim (SEP); corroborated at low overlap by Hossenfelder and Palmer, which argues from a different closure (superdeterminism).

**Refuter:** a theory for which no-signalling provably entails a Lorentz-invariant fundamental dynamics; that would collapse conditions 1 and 2 of F1.

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

From Seat Map :287: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." These were written before the verdict in §4 and before the WATSON run in `09_watson_answer.md`.

The shape claim (a deeper reconciliation requires an import beyond the local-realist closure, preserves no-signalling and has no observable preferred foliation) is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A loophole-free experiment demonstrates controllable superluminal signalling in quantum correlations. (Breaks F1 condition 1 and the operational premise.)
2. A local hidden-variable model reproduces all quantum predictions while retaining statistical independence. (Breaks F2 and F3.)
3. No-signalling is proved to entail a Lorentz-invariant fundamental dynamics, so the two conditions of F1 are one. (Breaks the union split.)
4. The same reconciliation is derived inside the local-realist closure with no new atom. (Breaks the import requirement.)
5. A source-discriminating instrument shows an observable preferred foliation in a theory that still reproduces the Bell correlations. This does not refute reconciliation, but it changes the mode of the no-foliation clause from `specified` to `grounded` and names which branch of the import is real.

If any of 1 to 4 fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of the deeper-theory answer may be labelled `grounded`.
