## Restating the question as an effect

What is being decided is whether two things that are both observed can be composed into one deeper theory. The first is Bell-violating correlation between spacelike separated outcomes. The second is relativistic causality, which here splits into two distinct conditions: no superluminal signalling, and no preferred foliation in the fundamental spacetime structure. Option A says the two cannot coexist, so a real superluminal influence must be accepted and Lorentz invariance must fail at some level. Option B says they already coexist at the operational level, and the open work is an ontological reconciliation in a deeper theory. What each leads to: A spends the empirical record, which shows no signalling; B requires splitting the residual into two conditions and finding an import beyond the local-realist closure. An answer would look like a shape that any reconciling theory must have, with its refuter written in advance, not a name of one theory.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a specific deeper theory (Bohmian, superdeterministic, retrocausal, Everettian) is the answer.

The figures' own reasons, from the local canonical text and the seat map:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Asking for agreement is not checking.
2. Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Send the residual and its refuter before any framework interior.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The venues in `03` are public and opt-in.
