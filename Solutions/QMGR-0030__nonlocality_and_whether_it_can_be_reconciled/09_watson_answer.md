## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about reconciling Bell-violating correlation with relativistic causality. Option A: they cannot coexist in one deeper theory, so a real superluminal influence must be accepted and Lorentz invariance must fail at some level. Option B: they already coexist operationally, and the open work is an ontological reconciliation in a deeper theory. What each leads to: A spends the empirical record, which shows no controllable superluminal signal; B splits the residual into two conditions and searches outward for an import beyond the local-realist closure. An answer would look like the shape any reconciling theory must have, with a refuter written in advance, not the name of one theory.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the name stays open. Bell-violating correlation and relativistic causality are a union of two conditions, and the second condition must be kept on a separate instrument from no-signalling. The local-realist closure is refused, not deleted; the correlations are underivable from it; a reconciliation therefore requires an import from a frame whose closure differs. Any reconciling theory must reproduce the correlations, keep no-signalling, have no observable preferred foliation, and close the residual with at least one imported atom. Which import is real is a `gap` at these instruments, reported as unreachable rather than as silence. Mode: `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The two empirical inputs remain `grounded` in world sources. The strongest outreach point is the pre-registered refuter, not a claim about which interpretation wins.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, split, refuter and provenance before any conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a theory before the characterised residual, which Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." prices directly. Leading with the refuter avoids it.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive has to be withdrawn to correct this folder. A local hidden-variable counterexample or a demonstrated signal would retract the shape claim cleanly; a demonstrated preferred foliation would only change the mode of one clause from `specified` to `grounded`. All files carry the lineage so a later reader can locate the error rather than merely feel it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A loophole-free controllable superluminal signal, a local model retaining measurement independence, a proof that no-signalling entails a Lorentz-invariant fundamental dynamics, or a derivation inside the local closure would break the shape claim. A source-discriminating instrument showing which import is real would move the gap to a name. A figure re-census that treats the correlation as a sequence bond would remove the named strain. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
