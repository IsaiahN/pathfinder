## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From "The Library of Babel": "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for QMGR-0030 a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name the theory. It constrains; it does not fill:

- **Shape:** a deeper theory that reproduces the Bell correlations, preserves no-signalling, has no observable preferred foliation, and imports at least one atom beyond the local-realist closure.
- **Name:** Bohmian mechanics, superdeterminism, retrocausality or Everett. Those are bindings from physics frames, not outputs of the figure set.

The split in `01` F1 is itself part of the shape: it says what any answer must satisfy, not which answer is right.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- "The Library of Babel": "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Use the vocabulary already closest to the residual, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, and recurse.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- "The Hard Problem is About Source": "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The thing to send is a description of what would discriminate, since the reading alone does not name its source.
- "The Library of Babel" on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and the paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect terms are "nonlocal correlation", "no superluminal signal", "no preferred foliation" and "measurement independence". The frame is not chosen first.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

Hossenfelder and Palmer (2020), a world source outside the framework, put the same limit in physics terms: "Bell-type tests cannot tell us which of the two options is correct." The experiment tells us the local closure fails; it does not tell us which import is real.

"The Hard Problem is About Source" gives the instrument rule: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source", and "It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." Here the corresponding move is to stop asking which interpretation feels right and ask what instrument would discriminate among them. None was opened in this session that does so.

## 4. Why binding the shape matters here

`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction: "It cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not a term it holds." The remedy is a discipline: write down what an answer would look like, bind it, let it accrue a residual, and make it refusable.

The Architecture article's own caveat: results "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So the pre-registered refuter in `01` §5 is not decoration. It is what makes this output checkable by someone who does not hold the framework.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid naming any part of the physics here?

**No, narrowed.** The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. In QMGR-0030:

- The Bell correlations and the no-signalling theorem are **not missing**; they are observed and named in the physics frame.
- The import that would reconcile them with a preferred-foliation-free dynamics **is missing** from the local-realist closure. That is the thing whose shape, not name, the framework returns.

So naming the empirical facts is fine. Naming *the* deeper theory is a binding from a physics frame onto an import, provenanced as such. Claiming the framework derives the name would be the forbidden move. Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them".

**Correction to my own §1 wording:** I first wrote "the framework does not select among Bohmian mechanics, superdeterminism, retrocausality or Everett. Those are bindings." That is right about the framework but could be read as saying the terms themselves are unspeakable. They are speakable; they are just not licensed *by the figures*. Both statements are kept so the distinction stays visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** "The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap": "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous physicists who agree with this reading is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself", and "You may all agree and be wrong." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the reconciliation and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
