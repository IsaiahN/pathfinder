**Question answered here:** given the residual in `01` §0, what are the operands and bond types, in what dependency order must the work be done, and are the candidate deeper theories isomers under the framework's own definition? The question is asked in that order because a bond cannot be typed until the operands are described, and an isomer claim cannot be made until the atoms are fixed.

## RESIDUAL (restated from the effect line)

R: can Bell-violating correlation and relativistic causality be composed into one deeper theory? The phrase hides two conditions. (1) No controllable superluminal signalling, settled operationally. (2) No distinguished foliation in the fundamental dynamics, not settled by (1). Arity: two conditions, so a union not a single question. Symmetry: the correlation is symmetric under exchange of wings, while no-signalling is a one-way parameter-independence condition. Scale: first order at spacelike separation, second order at the ontological level. Anchor: the experiment, not agreement.

### Bond types, not just ingredients

Fig 12 :46: "A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."

The popular phrase "spooky action at a distance" writes a sequence bond between the two wings. The sequence bond is defined as Fig 12 :72: "A then B; B needs A to have happened". That reading fails the operational facts: the correlation is symmetric under wing exchange, and each local marginal is independent of the remote setting. There is no A-then-B and no message. The bond that fits the readings is a correlation under a common source, a symmetric conjunction at preparation, not a → connection across the wings. This is the named strain from `01` F8, kept visible rather than smoothed.

The comparison bond ⋛ is defined as Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities". It fits the *Bell test* (correlation strength against the bound 2), not the connection between the wings. Conflating the test relation with the physical relation would be a bond-type error. Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source for the figure typing; the physics check (whether any subquantum dynamics licenses a → bond at its own level) is unmeasured.

## ORDER: dependency, not importance

Fig 3 :20: "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."

The correct order for this residual is:

1. Describe R by arity, symmetry and scale before choosing a frame. Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."
2. Split the union. Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."
3. Refuse the local-realist closure before trying to mint a replacement. Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so".
4. Bind the sought shape as an ordinary, refusable term (`THE_FORMULA.md`, Direction). An unbound preference cannot accrue residual and cannot be checked.
5. Import from a frame whose closure differs. Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."
6. Let the ground decide among what survives. Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides".

The chain stops at the objective link. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." There is no opened instrument that discriminates among the candidate imports at the relevant scale, so step 6 is a stop, not a completed measurement. Underivable is not the same as unreachable at this budget; Fig 8 :40-43: "Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions." Here the obstruction is underivable from the local closure, so the instruction is outward, not more search.

## ARE THE CANDIDATES ISOMERS?

The figure's definition is strict. Fig 12 :117: "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." The weaker relation is Fig 12 :123: "functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."

Read the candidates off the world, not from a preferred list (Fig 11 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."):

| candidate | assumption refused | import added |
|---|---|---|
| de Broglie-Bohm | parameter independence / outcome independence | nonlocal ontology plus distribution postulate |
| superdeterminism | measurement independence | correlation of λ with settings, local continuity of action |
| retrocausal / future input | temporal asymmetry of the boundary | future boundary condition |
| Everettian | unique outcome | branching, no single-outcome fact |

**Narrow verdict: not isomers as written.** The atom sets are not the same across the four rows, so Fig 12 :117 does not apply directly. What they share is a *functional group*: each refuses one assumption of the local-realist closure (Fig 12 :123). The qualifier says which member.

**Stronger form, checkable but unproven:** if all four are rewritten over one full atom set, namely {locality, outcome independence, measurement independence, unique outcome} plus ¬, differing only in which term the refusal attaches to and which atom is imported, then they are isomers under Fig 12 :117. This is a structural claim with a stated test, not a figure verdict. Mode: `specified`. Status: single-source (Fig 12) with the physics-facing check unmeasured.

**Settlement is needed for composition at the next level.** Fig 12 :104: "A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world". The loophole-free Bell tests are settled against the ground (world sources in `10_source_ledger.csv`), so the refusal ¬ local closure is available as an atom for a second-order composition. That is why the candidate deeper theories are second-order structures over a settled refusal, not further refinements of the operational frame. Fig 13 :74: "Everything else is a composition across the two kinds, never within one." The operational fact and the ontological import are different kinds; a reconciling theory composes across them.

## FALSIFIER (written before the conclusion)

1. If two candidate theories can be written over an identical atom set differing only in bond type, the isomer claim is confirmed rather than refused. If one requires an atom the other cannot express, the functional-group reading stands and the isomer claim is withdrawn.
2. If any candidate is derived inside the local-realist closure with no refusal and no import, the dependency order above collapses and `01` F3 is refuted.
3. If a loophole-free experiment shows a controllable superluminal signal, the → bond reading is licensed and the bond census above is wrong.
4. If no-signalling is proved to entail a foliation-free fundamental dynamics, the union split in step 2 is wrong and the two conditions become one.

These are written before the conclusion, per the seat rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.
