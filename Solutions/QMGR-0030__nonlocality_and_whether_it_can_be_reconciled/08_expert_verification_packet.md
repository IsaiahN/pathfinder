**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check in twenty minutes. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** The two empirical inputs (Bell violation, operational no-signalling) are `grounded` in world sources. Everything figure-derived is `specified` until an instrument pays for it. ## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Bell-violating correlation and relativistic causality are not one question but two conditions, and the second condition splits again into no superluminal signalling (settled) and no preferred foliation in the fundamental dynamics (not settled by the first). Bell's theorem refuses the local-realist closure under statistical independence; that refusal removes standing to compose over the closure, it does not delete anything. The observed correlations are underivable from that closure, so any reconciling deeper theory needs an import from a frame whose closure differs. The framework returns the shape such a theory must have, not its name: reproduce the correlations, keep no-signalling, have no observable preferred foliation, and close the residual with at least one imported atom. Which import is real is a ground question and stays open.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1, framework-internal: the union split

- **Statement:** the residual is a union of two conditions, not one hard question. They need separate instruments and separate settlement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :4-5: "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search." And Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either".
- **Physics-facing check:** does the no-signalling theorem settle the spacetime-structure condition? The world sources say no: de Broglie-Bohm permits no signalling while requiring a nonrelativistic spacetime structure, and compatibility with a relativistic spacetime structure is a distinct issue from Lorentz covariance of the dynamical laws.
- **Refuter:** show that no-signalling and absence of a preferred foliation are the same condition under every formulation, so one experiment settles both; or exhibit a quantum correlation permitting controllable superluminal signalling. Either fires C1.

### C2, framework-internal plus physics: refusal then import

- **Statement:** Bell's theorem is a refusal, and the correlations are underivable from the local-realist closure, so an import is owed rather than more search inside the same assumptions.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 5 :53: "refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so". Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :23: "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs." Fig 8 :32: "no search, however long, adds an atom".
- **What to check:** is the refused term stated correctly (local hidden variables under statistical independence, not hidden variables tout court)? Is the obstruction underivable in principle rather than a budget effect? The Nobel 2022 press release says hidden variables are excluded, while the SEP entry describes a functioning de Broglie-Bohm theory. That conflict was narrowed rather than hidden in `01` F7: the press release means *local* hidden variables under the Bell assumptions.
- **Refuter:** a local hidden-variable model reproducing all quantum predictions while retaining statistical independence, or a derivation of the correlations inside the local closure with no new atom, or a demonstration that the gap is only a budget effect.

### C3, framework-internal: shape, not a name, and the ground decides

- **Statement:** the output is the bound shape of any reconciling theory, not a name; provenance separates a convergent derivation from an adopted import; agreement among readers is not the anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 8 :50-52: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them". Fig 9 :42: "narrowing gets you close, the ground decides". Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
- **What to check:** does the packet anywhere name a deeper theory as the figure's answer? It should not. Does it state the mode of every substantive claim? It should.
- **Refuter:** derive a unique deeper theory from the figure set alone, or show a case where agreement among frames with demonstrably disjoint evidence settled a foundational question without a ground reading. The latter would narrow the rule, not necessarily reverse it.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that nonlocality conflicts with relativity at the operational level. Chiao, Kwiat and Steinberg (1995) state that quantum nonlocality is not inconsistent with Einstein causality.
- Not that Bell's theorem rules out hidden variables without qualification. The theorem rules out local hidden variables under measurement independence.
- Not that no-signalling settles the reconciliation. It does not.
- Not a derivation of one deeper theory from the figures, no new atom claimed by the framework, and no `grounded` status for the deeper-theory clauses.
- Not a request for agreement. The ask is for a refutation, and a one-line failure is more useful than endorsement.

## 5. What would settle it
