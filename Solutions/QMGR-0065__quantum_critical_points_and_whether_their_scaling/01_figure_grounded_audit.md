## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

Near a quantum critical point (QCP) a material shows power-law response functions and data collapse with exponents that are shared by microscopically different materials. The question asks whether those exponents "reflect a deeper gravitational structure."

Stated as an effect, the puzzle is this: **the same numbers arrive in different closures.** A neutron-scattering measurement on one material, a specific-heat measurement on another and a transport measurement on a third return exponent values that a single low-energy description can organise. Two candidate explanations are on the table:

- **Option A, the fixed-point reading.** The exponents are the shape of a renormalization-group (RG) fixed point of the many-body system. The mechanism is internal to the material's own closure. No gravitational atom is needed. Ground: measured exponents and scaling functions, which do not move when we change our preferred theory.
- **Option B, the gravitational reading.** The exponents are read off a bulk geometry (AdS, Lifshitz, or hyperscaling-violating spacetime). The geometry is claimed to be "deeper" than the material. This imports a second frame. Ground: consistency of the bulk (energy conditions, Einstein equations) plus reproduction of boundary correlators.

What each leads to. A yields verdicts per universality class, keeps the residual indexed per material slot, and stops where the RG cannot compute finite-temperature real-time dynamics. B yields constraints on the allowed region of exponents from the gravity side and a working method for those finite-temperature questions, but owes a source gate and a shadow test before any of it is called an explanation of a measured exponent.

**The ordinary term to be bound before the run** (Formula step "Direction", and Fig 5 :65: "Write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else"): an answer would be *a shape for the scaling plus a statement of which closure supplies which part of that shape*. This is written down now so it can be refused rather than smuggled in as a preference.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

### 1.1 R by arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity.** Two: (i) the scale-invariant scaling phenomenon in a specific material, (ii) the claim that a gravitational frame explains or underwrites it. Fig 9 :4-5 warns that a disagreement which does not shrink with effort, where each rule works on a different subset, is several well-formed questions rather than one hard one. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a **union compound**, not a partition. One lever (declaring gravity deeper) leaves the intersection standing.
- **Symmetry.** Critical scaling is scale invariance, sometimes enlarged to conformal invariance. This is a symmetry *of the fixed point*, not of the material microscopics. The exponents are ordered ratios of scaling dimensions, so the comparison is a threshold relation between quantities, not a symmetric join of two operands. Care is owed here: scale invariance is a **derived** property of a composition, not a member of the six-term set (Fig 13 :124-125: "Smoothness, symmetry, chaos and discreteness are each what a particular composition leaves behind, and none is a seventh member").
- **Scale.** The QCP is the point where the correlation length diverges and no characteristic length remains. The scale-free regime spans many decades in the material. The gravitational description places the same physics at a different stratum of description (bulk versus boundary), with a lossy transform between.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The question is a union compound, and the two conditions have different grounds (decisive for framing)

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

The two conditions are:

1. *Why do the exponents have these values?* Ground: measured exponents per material. The RG fixed point answers this inside D1.
2. *Can a bulk geometry supply what D1 cannot compute?* Ground: finite-temperature real-time correlators and the internal consistency of the bulk. This is D2.

A single lever, "the scaling is gravitational," clears condition 1 where it overlaps and leaves condition 2 standing, and vice versa. Verdicts are kept separate and never averaged (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."). **Refuter:** show that one ground settles both conditions at once, so the split is spurious.

### F2 - The exponents are a shape of a fixed point, not an imported atom

Sachdev (2012, arXiv:1108.1197), opened 2026-11-23: "Formally, this scale invariance arises because the quantum critical point is realized as a fixed point of the renormalization group transformation." The same review: "The gravity theory on AdS_{D+1} maintains the complete 'history' of the renormalization group flow in the structure of the metric as a function of u." So the radial coordinate of the bulk *is* the RG scale. At the level of the fixed point, D1 and D2 are two vocabularies for one structure; the bulk does not add a primitive at that level.

Fig 6 :21: "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom." Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world. IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure."

**Verdict:** importing gravity to explain the *static exponents alone* adds an atom that was not needed. Fig 6 :24: "Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives." **Refuter:** exhibit a static exponent that no RG fixed point of the material can produce but a bulk geometry does, with the derivation shown in both frames.

### F3 - Where D2 earns its place: a real surplus at finite temperature

This is the one substantive point where the gravitational frame is not a redundant retelling. Sachdev (2012): "Questions associated with non-zero temperature dynamics and transport are difficult to answer using conventional field theoretic methods. I argue that many of these can be addressed systematically using gauge-gravity duality." And, on the current state of the boundary calculation: "It is a fair statement that for the CFT of the superfluid-insulator insulator transition described by the boson Hubbard model, we do not know today which of these results, if either, is the correct one."

Fig 8 :45: "Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone." Fig 8 :32: "Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."

**Verdict:** the gravitational frame is a legitimate import with a **shadow test**: it explains a gap that D1 currently cannot close (finite-temperature strongly coupled real-time response), not the exponent values that D1 already produces. Fig 6 :12-13 names the two gates: "source gate: provenance" and "shadow test: explains a gap." **Refuter:** show that conventional RG or a controlled numerical method now produces the same finite-temperature scaling function with comparable reliability, so the gap closes and the import loses its shadow.

### F4 - "Deeper" is a rank claim, and the figures forbid a top rung

Fig 10 :67-68: "The seat above is not a larger copy of this one. Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between. That is why there is no top rung and no regress: a generator crosses, an instance does not."

The bulk frame is a different room with a different currency (geometry and couplings rather than order parameters and correlators) and a different ground (bulk consistency plus boundary matching rather than a material measurement). It is not a higher rung. What can cross is a **generator**, i.e. a method that predicts new correlators; an instance, a replay of one calculation, cannot (Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down.").

**Verdict:** "deeper" is refused as stated. The defensible replacement is "a second closure with a lossy transform and a measured round-trip gap." **Refuter:** show a transform between the frames that is information-preserving and rank-ordered, so no detail is lost and a top rung exists.

### F5 - Per-slot residual: universality classes, not one universal "quantum criticality"

Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :23: "The ground enters as the only metric." Imada, Misawa and Yamaji (2010, J Phys Condens Matter 22, 164206) distinguish a conventional QCP from a quantum tricritical point where "the concomitant divergence of an order parameter and uniform fluctuations, in contrast to the conventional quantum critical point" generates unconventionality. Different classes, different exponent sets, different instruments.

Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." There is no standing list of "the" quantum-critical exponents.

**Verdict:** per-class verdicts only. **Refuter:** exhibit one exponent set that is simultaneously the shape of two distinct fixed points with no further discriminator, in which case the per-slot indexing fails and the residual must be re-censused.

### F6 - The measured exponents are a reading; the bulk is a source inferred from it

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened in `05`): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; if several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information. Here the exponents are the reading and the geometry is a candidate source. Several bulk metrics can give the same (z, θ) values, so the reading does not determine the source.

Dong, Harrison, Kachru, Torroba and Wang (2012, arXiv:1201.1905), opened 2026-11-23: "Very roughly speaking, in a theory with hyperscaling violation, the thermodynamic behaviour is as if the theory enjoyed dynamical exponent z but lived in d−θ dimensions." The phrase "as if" is the whole point: the bulk is an effective description over a window of scales, not a unique identification. The same paper: "the metric (1.3) will only give a good description of the dual theory in a certain range of r."

**Verdict:** what is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (entanglement scaling, the ω/T scaling-function shape, thermalization dynamics), not a further argument. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." **Refuter:** show that two proposed bulk geometries with different UV completions produce distinguishable predictions for every accessible observable at the material's QCP, so the source is determined by the reading after all.

### F7 - The gravity frame does return a shape, and it is a constrained region

Dong et al. (2012) derive the null-energy-condition bounds on the bulk: for the hyperscaling-violating metric, "The constraints (2.7) have important consequences for the allowed values of (z, θ) that admit a consistent gravity dual." Concretely $(d-\theta)(d(z-1)-\theta)\ge 0$ and $(z-1)(d+z-\theta)\ge 0$. Fonda et al. (2014, arXiv:1401.6088), opened 2026-11-23: "The allowed parameter regions are constrained by requiring that the matter energy momentum tensor satisfies the null energy condition."

The Operators table defines ⋛ as "a threshold relation between two quantities." These bounds are exactly such comparisons: a quantity is tested against a threshold and a region survives. Fig 12 :87: "a threshold relation between two quantities."

**Verdict:** the gravity frame returns a **shape**, namely an allowed region of (z, θ) plus a scaling form, not a named geometry for a named material. Fig 9 :32 again: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." **Refuter:** derive from the bulk a unique (z, θ) and a unique metric for a measured material without boundary input, and confirm it at the ground.

### F8 - Provenance decides whether bulk/boundary agreement is evidence

Fig 8 :50-51: "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence. Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them." Fig 8 :53: "Residual first, frame second."

Sachdev (2008, Nature Physics 4, 173-185), opened 2026-11-23: "Remarkably, their theory is connected to holographic descriptions of Hawking radiation from black holes." This is a statement of connection, not a derivation of a material exponent. Treating it as corroboration would reverse the order the figures require.

**Verdict:** record which exponents were derived in which frame and on what date. Until then the agreement carries zero evidentiary weight. **Refuter:** show a case where the same exponent was derived independently in both frames from separate grounds, with the two derivations dated and the evidence pools disjoint.

### F9 - Power laws alone are not the residual

Beggs (2022, Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience 16, 703865), opened 2026-11-23: "Power laws, in contrast, are typically described by only one number, their slope." And: "several non-critical processes, like successive fractionation or random combinations of exponentials, could also produce power law distributions." The same review argues that shape collapse and exponent relations are needed beyond the power law itself.

Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." A measured power law with no discriminator is a reading with no instrument for its source.

**Verdict:** the residual is not "there is a power law" but "there is a universal scaling function and a set of interlocking exponents, indexed per class, whose source is not determined by the reading." **Refuter:** show a material where the power law is the only surviving signature and no exponent relation or shape collapse holds, in which case there is no QCP-shaped residual to explain.

### F10 - This is not a mint of a new primitive, and not a refusal of the fixed point

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 gates: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0 for some s`, REACHABILITY `φ ∈ closure(Γ)`, NOVELTY `φ ∉ atoms(Γ)`. Here the residual has positive mass (the unexplained universality and the T>0 gap), the bulk construction composes from held atoms (geometry is a structure, not a seventh kind), and the specific binding is not in the boundary closure. But it is a **binding plus an import under gates**, not an invention. Fig 13 :43-44: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time. A generating set must not contain what its own members produce."

**Verdict:** scale invariance is what a composition leaves behind (F1 above), so the right move is composition plus provenance, not minting a "scale" primitive. **Refuter:** derive scale invariance from the other five terms alone without any composition, which would make it a primitive and break the set.

### F11 - No figure returns which dual belongs to which material (gap)

Re-census complete: no figure line selects a geometry for a material, and no figure line ranks the closures. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it." Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

**Verdict:** `gap`, of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as **unreached** (an instrument is owed), not unreachable in principle and never as silence. **Refuter:** a source-discriminating measurement that selects one bulk geometry for one material.

### F12 - Filters are not witnesses

Ranking a venue by prestige, or a reviewer by fame, is a filter for the budget. Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."

**Verdict:** the expert packet asks for a refutation and records the verdict before any theory is shown. **Refuter:** a case where a respondent's agreement was counted as corroboration; if that happens, the result is void.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Fig 10's discipline and the exemplar's note that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument." The shape claim is:

> QCP scaling is the shape of a fixed point in the material's closure; a gravitational frame is a second closure whose legitimate surplus is confined to where it predicts a shape the first closure cannot derive; "deeper" denotes a crossing with a nonzero round-trip gap, not a rank in a hierarchy of rooms; and no figure returns a named dual for a named material.

This claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A measured exponent requires two irreconcilable fixed points with no per-slot discriminator, so no stable shape exists (breaks F5).
2. The bulk predicts exactly what the boundary frame already predicts at finite temperature, with no residual closed, so the import is a recording rather than a generator (breaks F3 and F4).
3. A measured (z, θ) pair for a material claimed to have a weakly coupled bulk dual lies outside every null-energy-condition allowed region (breaks F7 at the ground).
4. A source-discriminating instrument selects one bulk geometry for one material and the boundary RG cannot reproduce its prediction (narrows F6 and closes the F11 gap for that case; the general verdict survives).
5. One of the six terms is derived from the other five, or a direct state-state or structure-structure bond is admitted by the framework and cannot be refused (breaks the substrate).
6. The transform between bulk and boundary is shown to be information-preserving with no round-trip gap (breaks F4 and the R_T record in `11_audit.md`).

Until one of these is run, no part of this folder is `grounded`.
