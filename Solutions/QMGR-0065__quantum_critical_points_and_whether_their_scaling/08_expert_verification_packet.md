## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Quantum critical scaling and its claimed gravitational explanation are a union of two conditions with different grounds, not one question with opposite answers. The exponent values and scaling functions are the shape of a renormalization-group fixed point inside the material's own closure, indexed per universality class; importing a bulk atom to explain the static exponents adds something the boundary closure does not need. The gravitational frame has a genuine and limited surplus at finite temperature and real time, where the boundary calculation is hard and the bulk geometry predicts a scaling form and a constrained region of (z, θ). "Deeper" is refused as a rank claim: the bulk is a second closure with a different currency, a different ground and a lossy transform, not a higher rung in a hierarchy of rooms, and the loss must be measured. No figure line returns a named dual for a named material, so that slot stays open and a source-discriminating instrument is owed.
