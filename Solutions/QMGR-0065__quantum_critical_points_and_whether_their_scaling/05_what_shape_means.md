## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

For this question a **shape** is the form that any answer about quantum-critical scaling must have, stated before any particular bulk geometry or exponent value is named. It constrains; it does not fill. Three constraints survive the census in `01`:

1. scaling is compositional, with a structure between two states, not a direct state-to-state bond (Fig 13 :75);
2. the exponent values are indexed per universality class, not pooled across materials (Fig 1 :19);
3. the gravitational frame is a second closure with a lossy transform, not a higher rung (Fig 10 :67-69).

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Here the returned set is a constrained region of (z, θ) and a scaling form, not a named dual. `z=1, θ=0` and `z=3/2, θ=1` are shapes; "the dual of material X is geometry Y" is a name, and no figure returns it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary. It's this recursive process that will help you converge on the answer." Applied here: start from "power law", add "scaling function", "exponent relation", "fixed point", "radial coordinate as RG scale", "null energy condition", and the search narrows from "does gravity explain it" to "which part of the scaling is fixed-point shape and which part needs a second closure".
- Fig 6 :30: "Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So "dynamical exponent z" is carried as "the relative rate at which the time axis stretches compared with the space axis", not as a symbol whose meaning is assumed.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here that happens when the question-as-tether (the measured scaling function) is the thing that tells us which frame to use next.

## 4. Why the "deeper" reading is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." And: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."

The measured exponents are a **reading**. "Bulk geometry Y" is a **source** inferred from that reading. Several bulk constructions can give the same (z, θ), just as several light mixtures can give the same green. The essay names the correct remedy: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." It was "proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument." So the move owed here is a source-discriminating measurement (entanglement scaling, the ω/T scaling-function shape, thermalization dynamics), not a further argument that one geometry feels more fundamental.

This is also why "deeper" fails twice: it treats a projection as a source, and it treats a different closure as a higher rank (Fig 10 :67-69).

## 6. Two consequences for this folder

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A claim supported by the reputation of holography, or by the number of people who find it elegant, is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a refutation at the ground: a measured exponent outside the allowed region, or a scaling function that distinguishes two proposed geometries.

**My audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, opened this session: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the physics and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle what it reports on.

**The framework cannot validate itself.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." An outside condensed-matter physicist or holographic theorist, given only the residual and the refuter, supplies the validator. Their verdict must be recorded before they see the framework (Fig 10 :42).
