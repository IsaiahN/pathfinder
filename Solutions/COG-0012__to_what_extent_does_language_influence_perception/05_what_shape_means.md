## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical rule is that the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So for this question a **shape** is a rule you can state before you can assign a number:

> Language changes the reach of a perceptual frame, not the ground it ranges against. The change concentrates at the link where a vocabulary supplies terms for what a situation affords, and where no instrument fixes the slot. It is carried by grammatical bonds rather than by label inventories. It is strongest under contact with a frame whose closure differs, and weakest where an independently calibrated anchor is live.

That is a shape. "Language determines perception" is a verdict stated without a ground. "Language has a 12 percent effect on perception" is a name for a quantity nobody has measured here. Neither is a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." Recurse until "the finish line will also be the starting place".
- Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: by the time you have the exact search terms, "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." That is why the shape is written down and bound before the run, not after.

Applied here: the effect was described first (which slot fails to resolve, under which task, with which instrument), then the figure lines were chosen. Going the other way, picking "language" and then finding a figure that fits, would be the echo Fig 9 :27-28 warns about.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical rule says the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51).

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible, which is worth a great deal, because a visible choice can be revised and an emergent one cannot."

Mapping "language" onto the library Γ is a visible choice, not a discovery of nature's own list. Naming a particular grammatical category as *the* cause of a perceptual shift would turn that visible choice into an invisible one. The figure returns a shape; the binding is the researcher's contribution and must be recorded as such.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism that matters most here. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

*How Intelligence Actually Works* states it at the level of perception itself: "Perception is the reception of a projection by something that is not its source." The green projection "can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it." And the closing discipline: "indiscernibility is not identity."

Applied to this question: a cross-linguistic difference in a task is a reading. It does not by itself name its source. The source could be grammar, vocabulary, ecology, instruction, task framing, or an instrument calibrated inside the same categories. What is owed is a source-discriminating design, not a name. *They're Scanning Books* states the general limit: "The recognizer can only register an anomaly it has the primitives to represent." A snake "cannot be surprised by a color." A speaker cannot be surprised along an axis their language gives them no primitive for. That is the strongest positive claim the framework permits, and it is `specified`, not `grounded`.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What does the framework say the answer must look like?** A conditional rule over slots and bonds, with an explicit source gate. Not a single extent. Strongest where the middle dependency link has no instrument; carried by grammatical bonds; extended by contact with a differing closure; bounded by an anchor that does not update. Weakest where a calibrated instrument pins the slot and where only labels differ. Magnitude is a declared gap.

**Does the framework license the claim that language determines perception?** No. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no atom to the world." A frame gains access, not a new world. Determinism would require the ground to move when a frame changes, which is panel 1 of Fig 2 (coherent drift), not panel 3 (triangulation).

**Does the framework license the claim that language has no effect?** No. Fig 12 :111: "A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one." A frame with no term for a route cannot compose one inside its closure. Zero effect would require the vocabulary link of Fig 3 to be idle, and the figures put it squarely in the chain.

## 5b. Correction to my own section 3 and 4 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md`)

I initially stated the "shape, not a name" rule as if it forbade naming any linguistic category as a cause. That overstates it. The canonical phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Grammatical number, spatial reference frames and color terms are not missing primitives in this derivation; they are bindings onto categories the psycholinguistics frame already holds, or imports from a frame whose closure differs. Naming them is permitted as a `specified` binding with provenance. What remains forbidden is pretending the framework derived the name, or claiming the binding adds an atom to the world.

My section 4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a task difference cannot name its source is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held atoms. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers well known names to agree with the conditional claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets an experiment, a task and an independently calibrated instrument settle it.

**Different closures are the only useful readers.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*: "A group is only worth having while its members stay different." Ten copies watching the same thing are "one opinion said ten times." This is why the contact points in `03_public_contact_points.csv` are venues, not a list of agreeable people, and why the outreach note asks for a refutation.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" but "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The article adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
