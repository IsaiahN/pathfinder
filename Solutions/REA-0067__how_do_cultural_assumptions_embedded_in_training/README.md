## Restatement in effect terms

The question asks for a mechanism: cultural assumptions in a corpus enter a system, and a changed reading of "social intelligence" comes out. The two options are (A) the benchmark score is a direct measurement of a property the system has, and (B) the score is a projection whose source is not determined by the reading, so the assumptions enter through the prior, the bonds, the evaluator and the habitat. Option A spends the independence of the people checking it and mistakes a shared convention for a ground. Option B returns a per-slot shape with a refuter, and stops where the instruments stop.

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term:** a four-domain map (corpus/model, evaluator, deployment habitat, source-discriminating instrument) whose per-domain verdicts are kept separate, plus a stated refuter for each. "Social intelligence" is refused as a single standing noun. The bound term is *apparent social reading per slot under a declared cultural ground*.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with this reading.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a named culture, dataset or benchmark is *the* source of a reading. The framework returns a shape, not a name.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. A list of evaluators drawn from the same cultural pool as the corpus is one witness wearing many names.
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the figure set.
3. **Interest in the reading is disqualifying.** Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying"*. The author's stake in a clean result is structural and is recorded, not asserted away.

Legally: public institutional and opt-in channels avoid the structural failure and the unsolicited-bulk-email failure at once. See `04_links_and_distribution.md`.

## Key verdict, in one line

Apparent social intelligence is a reading whose source is underdetermined by the reading; cultural assumptions enter as the inherited weighting and the bond arrangement of the corpus, are invisible to the frame that holds them, and are certified by evaluation pools that share their evidence, so the honest result is a per-slot shape, four separate verdicts, and a declared gap where a source-discriminating instrument is owed.
