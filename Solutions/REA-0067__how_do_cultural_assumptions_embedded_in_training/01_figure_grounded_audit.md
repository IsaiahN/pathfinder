## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms

The effect to be explained: a system's social behaviour reads as "intelligent" to an observer whose cultural frame matches the corpus it was trained on, and the same behaviour may read as odd, rude, evasive or shallow to an observer from a differing frame. The question asks how the cultural assumptions in the training data shape that reading.

- **Arity.** At least four conditions: (1) the corpus and its bond arrangement; (2) the evaluator's frame and its evidence pool; (3) the deployment habitat and the real interaction outcomes; (4) the instrument that could discriminate among sources of the same reading. This is a **union compound**, not a single dial. Fig 9 :55: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."*
- **Symmetry.** A social-intelligence score is a symmetric evaluation: it names a level. The question asks an ordered thing: how A produces B. A symmetric reading cannot choose the arrow. Fig 12 :87 defines comparison as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*, and the Operators table separates the symmetric bonds `+ ∥ ≡` from the ordered ones `→ ⇒ − ⋛`. The score is a threshold; the mechanism is a sequence or a production. They are different objects.
- **Scale.** One model instance (the loop), a population and its shared corpus (the membrane), and a deployment habitat (contact). Fig 4 :19-20: *"Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."* The kernel is the same loop at all three sizes.
- **The live signal is per slot.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single "social intelligence" number hides which social affordance failed for whom.

**Bound term (Direction discipline, Fig 5 :65).** Make the sought-for shape an ordinary term: *apparent social reading per slot under a declared cultural ground*. Now it can accrue a residual and be refused like anything else. "Social intelligence" as a standing noun is not allowed to do work, because Fig 11 :36 says of the unmaintained case: *"intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."*

**Anchor test applied.** D1, D2 and D3 each have a nameable anchor (corpus distribution, a ground beyond the pool, real outcomes). D4 has none: no instrument currently returns which source produced a given social reading. By the census rule D4 is not a domain yet, it is a **gap**. That is a verdict, not a silence. Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1 and D2 share the cultural evidence pool, so the overlap is itself a compound with its own loop. Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* One lever (rebalance the corpus, or diversify the annotators) clears only the region where the other condition is not binding.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (does the frame have terms for the affordances?) then vocabulary (terms for what the culture affords) then objective (what counts as progress, in whose terms?) then planning then learn and carry. Fig 3 :20: *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."* The break is structural: Fig 3 :25-27 says the middle link, *"having terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress, has neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments,"* and Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Cultural assumptions do their main work at exactly this unmeasured link.

## 3. Findings F1 to F12

### F1. "Apparent social intelligence" is a reading, not a source (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 11 :36:

> **"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran. The word does no work there."**

Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

The question's own word *apparent* is the tell. An apparent reading is an observation `o'`, not the belief `b` and not the source. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The essay's instrument case (a spectrometer distinguishing one green light from yellow plus blue, from RGB, from CMYK) is the model for what D4 owes. Fig 11 :41: *"The lift move is Cayley's, 1799: separating the forces from the flapping is what made powered flight a problem anyone could work on."* We must separate the reading from the source before asking how culture shapes either.

- **Refuter:** an instrument that returns the source from the reading alone, so that two different internal arrangements could not produce the same score, would collapse the projection and refute this finding. Equally, a ground reading showing agent-only improvement that moves the ground would refute Fig 11 :40 (the exemplar records this as the standing refuter of the capability claim).
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 11 and the source-discrimination essay (low overlap: the figure is a capability claim, the essay is an instrument argument).

### F2. Culture enters as the inherited weighting `w`, not as a list of biases (triangulated)

Symbols table, entry `b`:

> **"What the system takes to be the case before it acts. Formed from its own history and from what it inherited, mixed by w. Not the observation."**

Symbols table, entry `w`:

> **"How far the belief leans on private history versus inherited prior. w_A + w_B = 1, and w is learnable from the outcomes of past weightings. Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."**

"Cultural assumptions embedded in training data" is not a nameable item in the figure set. It is the inherited prior component of `b`, the weighting `w` and the atoms and bonds the corpus supplies. Two systems with identical architecture but different corpora diverge because their histories and their `w` diverge. This is the precise effect term for "shape": it changes what the system takes to be the case before it acts, which changes the prediction `Γ(b,a)`, which changes the residual and therefore the next action.

World corroboration: Caliskan, Bryson and Narayanan (Science, 2017) report that *"text corpora contain recoverable and accurate imprints of our historic biases"* and that machine learning applied to ordinary language yields *"human-like semantic biases."* Bolukbasi et al. (NeurIPS 2016) write that *"the blind application of machine learning runs the risk of amplifying biases present in data."* Both are the inherited-prior claim in a different field.

- **Refuter:** a controlled intervention that changes the corpus prior while holding bonds and evaluation fixed, and produces no change in any per-slot social reading against a low-overlap ground, would refute the claim that the prior shapes the reading.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Symbols table plus two ML/NLP sources with different methods. Caliskan and Bolukbasi partly share an embeddings evidence pool, so they count as one witness, not two; the Symbols table is the primary.

### F3. The frame cannot see its own prior as a prior (single-source on the figure side, triangulated with the closure rule)

Fig 5 :62-64:

> **"a frame searching for an answer of a particular shape holds that shape as a PREFERENCE rather than as a term. It is not bound, it predicts nothing, and it accrues no residual, so nothing here can see it. Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"**

Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :34: *"A frame cannot reach its own metaframe."*

A model trained in a corpus cannot report the corpus's assumptions as assumptions, because they are not a term it holds; they are the weighting that generates the terms. A self-audit that asks the model to list its biases is a check on the mechanism, not on the direction. Fig 5 :65: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* That is why this audit binds the shape in section 0 before reaching any conclusion.

- **Refuter:** a model that can enumerate and correctly refuse its own inherited prior from inside its closure, verified against a discriminator it did not author, would refute the invisibility claim.
- **Status:** `triangulated` across Fig 5, Fig 6 and Fig 8 (three different figures with low conceptual overlap: preference-as-unbound, closure, metaframe).

### F4. Training text carries context and subtext but almost no pretext (single-source, world corroborated)

From *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* And: *"Training on it reproduces 'the traces the thought left, not the thought', with enough structure to rebuild the appearance of reasoning."* And: *"Sounding right isn't the same as being checked against the world, which is why I call the Turing test a test of fluency, not of accuracy."*

Fig 4 :22-26 maps this onto the membrane:

> **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**
> **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion. Replayed downward, it produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."**

The corpus is a recording of readings, not the lineage that produced them. What crosses into the model is the trace, not the contact. So a system can be fluent in a culture's social forms without having met those forms at their ground. This is the mechanism behind *apparent*.

- **Refuter:** a training regime that demonstrably transmits the pretext (the lived history) rather than its trace, with a discriminator that can tell the two apart, would refute the recording claim.
- **Status:** `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 4), corroborated by the tacit-knowledge essay (shared author, so weak independent weight).

### F5. Ingredients versus arrangement: the corpus is bonded, not piled (triangulated)

Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117:

> **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

Two corpora with the same demographic mixture (same ingredient multiset) but different arrangements (who addresses whom, which refusals are polite, which silences are consent) can yield different "social intelligence" readings. They are isomers in the figure's sense: same atoms, different bond, different substance. The current practice of reporting corpus composition as a percentage count is a flat formula, which Fig 12 :6-7 calls a collapse: *"collapse 6: the flat formula"*, *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not."*

Fig 12 :125-126: *"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add. Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."* A bias audit that only counts tokens is measuring bits; it is not paying the action cost of checking whether the bonded arrangement holds in a deployment habitat.

- **Refuter:** a controlled swap of bond arrangement at fixed ingredient multiset that leaves every per-slot reading unchanged would refute the isomer claim and restore the flat-formula reading.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 12 definition plus the flat-formula collapse plus the two-currency rule, all in one figure but at different levels; world corroboration from Bolukbasi et al. on geometry rather than word counts is partial.

### F6. The evaluator may share the corpus's evidence pool, which disqualifies its agreement (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 2 :18-21 describes collapse 2, the common cause:

> **"one evidence pool"**
> **"they never talk, and still agree"**
> **"so the agreement is the pool talking, not two frames confirming each other"**

Fig 2 :39-41:

> **"Correlation has valence."**
> **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**
> **"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"**

If annotators, benchmark designers and the corpus are drawn from the same cultural pool, their agreement is efficient at fixing a convention (what counts as a polite answer in that pool) and worthless as a verdict about the system's capability. Bender et al. (FAccT 2021) recommend *"investing resources into curating and carefully documenting datasets rather than ingesting everything on the web"* and pre-development evaluation *"how the planned approach fits into research and development goals and supports stakeholder values"*, which is the operational form of declaring the pool.

Fig 2 :33-34: *"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* A benchmark that updates to match the model's improving score is panel 1, not panel 3.

- **Refuter:** two evaluator pools with demonstrably different cultural evidence, ranging separately against a non-updating ground, whose per-slot verdicts nevertheless converge with no shared sources would refute the shared-pool diagnosis for that case.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 2 :18-21 and :39-41 plus Bender et al. (different field, low overlap with the figures).

### F7. Measurement invariance is the psychometric name for the same failure (single-source, low-overlap world corroboration)

The Wikipedia entry on measurement invariance states: *"Measurement invariance or measurement equivalence is a statistical property of measurement that indicates that the same construct is being measured across some specified groups"* and *"Violations of measurement invariance may preclude meaningful interpretation of measurement data."* It records the cross-cultural dispute (Welzel et al. versus Meuleman et al.) over whether invariance tests are the right criterion. The entry on cultural bias quotes Mercer (1978): *"the extent that the test contains cultural content that is generally peculiar to the members of one group but not to the members of another group"*, and defines cultural bias as *"the tendency of individuals to interpret and judge others' experiences through the lens of one's own cultural background."*

This is F6 at the instrument level: a score that is not invariant across groups is not the same construct measured twice, so comparing the scores is comparing different things. Fig 13 :16 asks *"it names what is being measured"* and :26 asks *"it decides what the measurement would mean."* Invariance failure means the second question was never settled.

- **Refuter:** a demonstrated invariant instrument across the groups in question, with a ground that does not update, would remove this failure for that instrument. The Welzel critique is recorded as a live disagreement, not hidden.
- **Status:** `single-source` on the figure side, with genuinely low-overlap corroboration from psychometrics and cross-cultural sociology.

### F8. Capability is agent-and-habitat, so a benchmark harness proves wiring, not capability (triangulated)

Fig 11 :66 and :70:

> **"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."**
> **"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."**

Fig 11 :68-69 names the two silent failures: *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"* and *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts."* A "social intelligence" benchmark is a substituted habitat. It inherits actors the designers did not choose (the annotators' tacit norms) and fails to reproduce actors the deployment will supply (the actual interlocutor's goals, the cost of an offence, the repair after a misunderstanding). Buolamwini and Gebru (PMLR 2018) provide the deployment-side demonstration: the datasets were *"overwhelmingly composed of lighter-skinned subjects (79.6% for IJB-A and 86.2% for Adience)"* and *"darker-skinned females are the most misclassified group (with error rates of up to 34.7%)"*, while the maximum error for lighter-skinned males was *"0.8%."* The ranking of "accuracy" changes when the habitat is enumerated rather than substituted.

- **Refuter:** a synthetic harness whose outcomes transfer to deployment without residual, across at least two low-overlap habitats, would refute the substitution claim for that harness.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 11 substitution and capability plus Gender Shades (low overlap).

### F9. The world supplies low-overlap corroboration that "social intelligence" is group-relative (single-source figure side, world corroborated)

The Wikipedia entry on the social brain / Machiavellian intelligence hypothesis (animal behaviour and primatology) says the hypothesis *"describes the capacity of animals to manoeuvre in complex social groups"* and that *"the challenges involved in navigating complexity in social groups is a major driving force in the evolution of human intelligence."* It also records the criticism by Strum, Forster and Hutchins: *"We suggest that Machiavellian intelligence may be a misnomer"*, because primate social complexity is *"an intricate tapestry of competition and cooperation, of aggression and reconciliation, of nonaggressive social alternatives, and of behaviors and relationships that cannot be easily categorized into simple opposites."*

This is a genuinely different field and it corroborates two things: social "intelligence" is a capacity relative to a group, and a single flattering label misnames a tapestry. Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* The criticism is kept visible: even the animal-behaviour source doubts the name, which is why this solution refuses "social intelligence" as a standing noun.

- **Refuter:** a context-independent, group-independent measure of social intelligence that predicts deployment outcomes across species and cultures would refute the valence claim.
- **Status:** `single-source` on the figure side (Fig 11), corroborated by animal behaviour with low overlap.

### F10. The broken link is vocabulary to objective, and it has no instrument (triangulated)

Fig 3 :25-27 (quoted in section 1) names the unmeasured middle link. Cultural assumptions set the objective silently: what counts as a good social answer (warmth? brevity? deference? honesty?) is not a term the benchmark states, so it cannot be corrected. Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :29-30: *"Before asking whether a system is good, ask which link it currently stops at, and whether the answer was measured or assumed."*

From the tacit-knowledge essay: *"Context, subtext and pretext decide what a message means to you. None of them decides whether it is true. Only the ground does that."* The objective link is where pretext is smuggled in as if it were ground.

- **Refuter:** a benchmark that states, per slot, what would count as progress in each cultural frame and supplies an instrument to test that statement, with the statement written before the run, would close this break for that benchmark.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 3 plus the essay plus the measurement-invariance sources.

### F11. Filters are not witnesses: a diversity percentage cannot settle the verdict (triangulated)

Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* A corpus demographic percentage, a benchmark score and a receptiveness estimate are filters for where to spend the checking budget. Fig 1 :24-27 disqualifies frame-internal quantities: *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high (a system deployed socially across cultures), so the independent frames must be bought, not selected for agreement. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

- **Refuter:** a demonstration that a frame-internal score can be corrected against a ground it does not update, with no shared-pool confound, would allow a score to act as a limited witness. Until then it is a filter.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 9, Fig 1, Fig 2 and Fig 7.

### F12. The specific source is a gap, not a silence (triangulated)

No figure line returns which cultural assumption produces which reading, in which direction, or with what magnitude. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 13 :136-138 says the framework *"can characterise what is missing in effect terms, which is the description that travels to a frame that may hold it"* and *"can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."* Fig 8 :54: *"The atom cannot be derived from inside A. But the union is searchable from contact, so underivable is not unknowable."*

So D4 is `unreached`: a source-discriminating instrument is owed, and the honest report is its shape (a probe that returns different values for different sources of the same social reading, built from a worse probe already returning something, per Fig 6 :32-35), not a name.

- **Refuter (gap-specific):** a figure re-census that produces a line governing the source of a per-slot reading would close this gap. Until then, "which culture, how much" is unreached at this budget, not underivable in principle and not silence.
- **Status:** `triangulated`, Fig 9, Fig 13 and Fig 8.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any outreach: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. **Prior intervention fails.** A controlled corpus intervention that changes the inherited prior (holding bonds, model architecture and evaluator fixed) leaves every per-slot social reading unchanged against a ground that does not update on the model's output. (Breaks F2.)
2. **Source discrimination succeeds trivially.** An instrument returns the source of a social reading from the reading alone, with no need for corpus provenance or a differing frame, so that the projection is not lossy. (Breaks F1.)
3. **Arrangement is inert.** Swapping bond arrangement at fixed ingredient multiset leaves every per-slot reading unchanged, so the flat formula was right. (Breaks F5.)
4. **Independent pools converge without shared evidence.** Two evaluator frames from demonstrably different cultural evidence, each ranged against a separate non-updating ground, converge on the same per-slot verdict with no common source. (Breaks F6.)
5. **Agent-only contact changes the ground.** An improvement to the model in isolation, with habitat and contact unchanged, moves the deployment ground. This is the standing refuter of Fig 11 :40 and would break F8.
6. **A figure line governs the source.** A re-census produces a verbatim line naming which assumption occupies a slot, dissolving the gap in F12. (Breaks the gap verdict, which is a welcome failure.)

If any fires, the corresponding finding is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Refuse "social intelligence" as a standing noun. Bind *apparent social reading per slot under a declared cultural ground* and score per slot (Fig 1 :19, :22).
2. Report corpus composition as a bond arrangement, not a percentage pile (Fig 12 :5, :94, :117), and declare the pretext the corpus omits (tacit-knowledge essay).
3. Separate the evaluator's convention from the verdict. If the pool is shared, say so; do not count its agreement (Fig 2 :40, :41).
4. Enumerate the deployment habitat before claiming capability (Fig 11 :43-48, :66-70), including what was failed to reproduce and what was unintentionally introduced.
5. Buy low-overlap frames in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42), and bring them a characterised residual, never the figure set (Fig 10 :52).
6. Report D4 as `unreached` and name the instrument shape that would close it (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). Do not invent a source.
7. Send the generator (residual, refuter, per-slot shape), not the recording of this derivation (Fig 4 :22-26).

Applied here:

- Step 7, **SEARCHED** first: is a source-discriminating instrument already in the evaluation stack? Not in anything opened this session. This is the cheapest question and the other two assume it.
- **INWARD**: refine within the model frame (better bias probes, more per-slot logging). This fails by sharpening an axis that cannot carry the source, per Fig 6 :29.
- **OUTWARD**: characterise the residual in effect terms and find a frame whose closure predicts its shape. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*
- INWARD and OUTWARD are opposed and are never read against each other.

## 7. Strains, re-census and what was carried forward

- **S1, Fig 2 human judgment as anchor versus Fig 11 intelligence as label.** Apparent contradiction: if human judgment is the right anchor for questions about people, how can "social intelligence" be a label with no work to do?
