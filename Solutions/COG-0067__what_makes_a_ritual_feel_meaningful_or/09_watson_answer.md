- **Domain 1, participant experience.** Actors: the individual participant and their belief, expectation and arousal. Currency: felt meaning, efficacy judgment. Anchor: the participant's own verdict, legitimate because the question is constitutively about people. Question type: the anchor condition of the union.

Anchor: what survives contact across occasions, Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground." Question type: a condition of the union, consumes Domains 1 and 2.
- **Domain 4, observer and research frame.** Actors: ritual-studies scholars, cultural psychologists, instruments. Currency: theories, measurements, agreement. Anchor: must not be observer agreement; instruments are instruments, not the ground. Overlaps Domain 1 where the instrument is the participant's own report; the overlap is marked, not hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the single-cause slot does not. The participant's own verdict is the anchor, because the question is constitutively about people; outside measures are instruments that can be wrong on unmeasured axes. The verdict is per person, so a group mean hides it. Shared felt meaning is a convention whose agreement is the product, while a world-outcome claim needs a ground where agreement is disqualifying. Felt meaning is participant-and-habitat and lives in the arrangement of acts, so the same acts in a different order are a different substance. The question is a union that must be unbundled. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. "Collective effervescence", arousal, neural synchrony, belief and cost are provenanced imports, not names the framework licenses.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, five refuters and the derivation before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The single-cause bindings are removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The eight-step loop can be reused on another question with a new refuter.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that discriminates among arousal, co-presence, belief and arrangement as sources of a convergent rating, or a controlled order-permutation that moves the participant verdict with atoms fixed, would fill the cause slot. An outside measure that outperforms the first-person verdict on every axis would move the anchor. An agent-only change that moves the ground with contact fixed would fire Fig 11's own falsifier. Until one of these fires, the verdict stays `candidate`.
