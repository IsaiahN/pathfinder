## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the four-constraint set in `01` §3 and `07` §2. "Arousal is the cause" is a name. "The verdict is per participant and participant-and-habitat" is a shape.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** The residual was described as a *differential in felt verdict*, before any field was chosen.
- Fig 9 :27: **"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"** The four domains in `01` §1 were censused from the effect before ritual studies or cultural psychology was named.
- *Library of Babel* on the bundle: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* This is the essay form of Fig 9 :54-57.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox: *"you enter as a researcher looking for a book, and if successful, you will leave as the author."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, Durkheim's "collective effervescence" and Cho et al.'s "neural synchrony" are imports from the ritual-science frame. Each was given a source URL, an author, a venue, a year and a verbatim quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They predict parts of the shape; neither is promoted to the name of the cause. *Arrival of the Fittest* records three mappings offered and declined for exactly this reason.

## 4. Why "the same across people" is not "true"

*How Intelligence Actually Works* gives the mechanism with the green-light example: *"The projection of green can't tell you whether it was RGB, CMYK or something else that composed it."* Applied here: a group of participants converged on the same efficacy rating (Cho et al., 2018). That reading does not determine its source. It could be arousal, co-presence, shared belief, a common stimulus, or a combination. The same essay: *"indiscernibility is not identity."*

This is the reason F2 and F3 in `01` stay separate. Convergence is a fact about the agreement statistic (a convention), not a source discrimination. An instrument that cannot distinguish the sources demonstrates its own inadequacy; what is owed is a better instrument, not a name.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* also notes that the six-term set is a guess and the mapping is the intelligence: *"we are not the holders of that mapping, we are the mapping, the same way a seed is the seedline."* So mapping "ritual elements" onto the six terms does not make the six a discovery of ritual's causes. It makes the choice visible.

## 5. Q: does the framework name the mechanism of ritual meaning?

**Answered, no further question needed.** Nothing in the framework names a single mechanism for felt meaning. The framework returns a shape. A particular mechanism is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such. The single-cause slot is a `gap` at this budget with a stated refuter, not a silence.

## 6. Two consequences for this outreach

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous ritual scholars assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets the ground, a participant's own verdict under a controlled variation, settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *Arrival of the Fittest* adds: *"because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own first reading

In the first pass I treated F3 (Fig 2 :40 vs :41) as a genuine strain and nearly averaged the two agreement rules into one "agreement is neutral" statement. **Retracted.** Re-census shows they govern different quantities: a verdict about the world (where agreement is disqualifying) and a convention about shared meaning (where agreement is the product). Averaging them would destroy exactly the distinction the shape depends on. Both readings are now recorded separately in `01` §3 F3 and §6.

A second, smaller correction: I initially read *"a shape, not a name"* as forbidding any use of "collective effervescence" or "synchrony" in the answer. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** thing. These are not missing primitives; they are imports already held in the ritual-science frame. Naming them is allowed **as provenanced bindings**, not as framework derivations. The distinction is recorded, not erased.
