## Independent domain census

I redid the census before reading the primary's conclusions, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the differential in felt meaning across participants and rituals. Arity: three conditions (participant, form, habitat), so a **union compound**, Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Symmetry: the felt verdict is first-personal; agreement between participants is symmetric correlation, a different quantity. Scale: first order is one person in one performance; second order is a practice-lineage across occasions.
- **Domain 1, participant experience.** Actors: the individual and their belief, expectation, arousal. Currency: felt meaning, efficacy judgment. Anchor: the participant's own verdict, legitimate by Fig 2 :36 because the question is constitutively about people. Union condition, comes first.
- **Domain 2, ritual form.** Actors: acts, order, repetition, symbol, setting. Currency: arrangement and description length. Anchor: none of its own; a frame, not a ground. Union condition, ordered after the anchor.
- **Domain 3, habitat and tradition.** Actors: community, co-presence, transmitters, maintainers. Currency: contact and transmission fidelity. Anchor: what survives contact, Fig 11 :36 "Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground." Union condition, consumes Domains 1 and 2.
- **Domain 4, observer frame.** Actors: scholars and instruments. Currency: theories, agreement. Anchor: must not be agreement; instruments are not the ground. Overlaps Domain 1 where the instrument is a self-report; overlap declared.

Order by dependency: fix the anchor before the form (Fig 9 :27), refuse a single-cause name before minting (Formula step 2), settle each condition before the union comparison (Fig 12 :104).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 (per-slot residual) and Fig 2 (anchor), I derived from Fig 9's union/partition split and Fig 3's dependency order. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table and `THE_FORMULA.md` define R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E composed with T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 2 :36-43 (anchor and correlation valence), Fig 1 :19-22 (per-slot residual), Fig 11 :39-40 (habitat) and Fig 12 :94, :117 (bond and isomer).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the four-constraint shorthand in `07` §2: participant verdict is the anchor; per slot; convention split from verdict; participant-and-habitat with arrangement carrying the meaning.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete ritual claim, for example "high-arousal synchronized group rites are meaningful because collective effervescence causes the feeling."

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **anchor status** of the first-person verdict, which the concrete claim quietly replaces with an observer's arousal measure;
- the **per-slot indexing**, which the concrete claim collapses into a group mean;
- the **convention/verdict distinction**, which the concrete claim erases by treating agreement as evidence of efficacy;
- the **bond contribution**, which the concrete claim reduces to an ingredient list (arousal, synchrony, cost).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a felt verdict needs a legitimate anchor and must be read per person, and the arrangement of acts matters. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a measured gap, which is what `09_watson_answer.md` states.
