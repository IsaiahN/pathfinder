## The residual in effect terms

Across rituals, and across participants in the *same* ritual, an outwardly similar performance sometimes lands as meaningful and efficacious and sometimes as hollow and empty. The effect to be explained is that differential. Three options sit inside it: (A) properties of the ritual form (sequence, repetition, arousal, cost, symbol); (B) properties of the participant (belief, expectation, cultural competence); (C) properties of the habitat (co-presence, community, transmission, repetition across occasions). Option A leads to an ingredient checklist. Option B leads to a participant-only account. Option C leads to a community-only account. The framework requires the union to be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54), so the answer is kept as a shape, not a single cause.

**What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term, per Formula "Direction"):** "A ritual feels meaningful to participant *s* when condition *X* is present; it fails for *s* when *X* is absent; and here is the instrument that discriminates *X* for *s*." That sentence is treated as a refutable term, not a preference, so the pre-registered refuter can reach it.

**Verdict in one line:** the participant's own verdict is the anchor (Fig 2 :36-38); it is indexed per person (Fig 1 :19); shared meaning is a convention where agreement is the product (Fig 2 :41) while world-outcome claims need a ground where agreement is disqualifying (Fig 2 :40); felt meaning is participant-and-habitat (Fig 11 :39) and lives in the arrangement, not the ingredient list (Fig 12 :94). No single ingredient is nameable from here.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal email addresses of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk send.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation that would make the agreement worthless.
2. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). Sending the full architecture before asking for a verdict spends the reader's independence.
3. *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* (Recursive Self-Improvement Trap, 2026-11-19). The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried; it does not ask for belief.
