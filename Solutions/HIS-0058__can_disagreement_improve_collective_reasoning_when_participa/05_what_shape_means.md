## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)" (local copy). Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the missing thing. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "disagreement helps" is a name with no shape. The separator with five cases and their conditions is a shape, because a reader can test it before knowing which case a real group is in.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay gives the bundle diagnostic that governs F7 of the audit: a problem may be "a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one".
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause". This is why the audit re-described "standards of evidence" as a frame-internal weighting before choosing any figure line.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *The Library of Babel* on stopping: the finish line is also the starting place, and the searcher "has already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the search started from the ordinary words "disagreement", "collective reasoning" and "standards of evidence". The closest figure vocabulary was frame, residual, anchor, independence, closure, union, room and refusal. Recursing on those narrowed the answer to five cases, each with a refuter. That narrowing is the shape.

## 3. What licenses a name, and the necessary correction

Two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The canonical README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

**Correction to the naive reading of section 1.** The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." It governs a primitive not held in the library. The five cases named in `01` are not missing primitives. They are bindings onto figure objects the framework already holds: Fig 8's three panels, Fig 2's two collapses, and Fig 9's union split. Naming them is provenance, not invention. What remains forbidden is inventing a sixth case that no figure line supports, or claiming that naming a case moves the wall (Fig 6 :22: "IMPORT adds an atom, and the added atom is what expands the closure"; a binding adds none). This correction is the same one the exemplar records in its own section 5b, and it is stated here rather than silently applied.

*They're Scanning Books* (carried) sharpens the import rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So even the figure vocabulary is a visible guess, not nature's own list.

## 4. Why the question needs a discriminator, not a verdict

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". One green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, or CMYK can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

For this question the same error appears whenever a group reads "they disagree" from a surface signal and then names the source as "different standards of evidence". A measured disagreement could arise from one evidence pool with two vocabularies (blend), from differing closures with no aimed residual (mute union), or from differing closures plus an anchor (triangulation). The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a discriminator: per-slot residuals with provenance, an anchor push test, and an independence measure. `01` section 9 states those instruments.

## 5. The shape returned for HIS-0058

The shape is **not** "disagreement helps" or "disagreement hurts". It is the following separator, stated as an ordinary term so it can be refused:

- The unit is the per-slot residual, not a group average (Fig 1 :19).
- A standard of evidence is a frame-internal convention, not the ground (Fig 1 :27, Fig 10 :18).
- The first discriminator is whether an anchor exists that does not update when pushed (Fig 2 :29).
- The second is independence at the evidence level (Fig 2 :31, Fig 8 :47).
- With low overlap, a described residual and a non-updating anchor, disagreement can move the wall. Without those, it is mute, a blend, or coherent drift.
- A persistent disagreement that does not shrink and works on disjoint subsets is a signal to split the question (Fig 9 :4-5).
- The group needs a refusal operator to carry a correction (Operators :58).
- No figure line returns a universal amount, ratio or standing trait list (Fig 9 :32, Fig 11 :51).

That is a shape any answer must have, with its own refuters in `01` section 4. It does not fill in which real group is in which case.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A collection of famous people agreeing that disagreement is good is the named collapse, not evidence. The ask is a budget to try the separator against a measurable group.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any real group and cannot settle the question it reports on.
