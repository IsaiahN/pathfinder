## What is being decided, in effect terms

A collection of participants, each holding a library and a local standard of evidence, either does or does not reduce the gap between what it predicts and what an external ground returns when their standards differ. The word "disagreement" is not one thing under the figures. It bundles at least five structurally different effects: a blend of two names for one evidence pool, an undirected union with a surplus nobody aimed, an anchored triangulation, a mirror chain of mutual updating, and a diagnostic split of a compound question. The question therefore does not admit a single yes or no. It admits a separator, stated per domain and never averaged. That separator is the shape returned here.

An answer would look like a list of conditions under which introducing disagreement lowers the per-slot residual, together with the conditions under which it cannot. Those conditions are bound as ordinary terms with refuters, so the sought-for shape is refusable by the same test as everything else (`THE_FORMULA.md`, step 2 and "Direction"; Fig 5 :65).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the separator.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that a simulated derivation has measured a crowd.

Three figure reasons, stated in the figures' own words:

1. **Agreement is not the verdict.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the very correlation that makes agreement worthless as a verdict (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
3. **Independence is the parameter, not a caveat.** Fig 8 :47: "Independence is one quantity read twice." Fig 6 :54: "Independence decides whether more of them buy more search or only more spelling, which makes it the parameter rather than a caveat."
