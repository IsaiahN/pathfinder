## 1. A shape is the narrowed form of a route, not a mechanism's name

The canonical rule, from `FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* because *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* The same essay gives the failure mode that matters here: if the final search fails, *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"*

So for this question a **shape** is: a route in a union compound, indexed per object and per region, recursively composing settled bodies across scales, with an anchor that is a discriminating observation. It is not: "core accretion", "disk instability", "pebble accretion", or any other name the world frame supplies. `PLANET(???)ROUTE` is the analogue of the exemplar's `SPACE(???)TIME`; the slot stays open.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel*: start from the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that correlate with it, recurse; *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* For planet formation the bootstrapped vocabulary was grain, pebble, planetesimal, protoplanet, isolation mass, snowline. Each term narrowed the residual.
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30 to :31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Describing the residual as "a disk-to-planet route with a local-concentration requirement" travels; describing it as "run the streaming instability at Stokes number 0.1" does not.
- `60_FIGURES_AS_METALAWS.md` states that the figures are the ground for design questions and that the seat must not ask for rulings the figures already decide. Applied here: the union and route verdicts were derived, not escalated; the mechanism question was not escalated either, because it is a ground question, not a missing ruling.

## 3. What would license a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For planet formation this would be an instrument that discriminates planets from snowlines or from dust-opacity effects in a given disk, or a labelled meteorite chronology that forces one route.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to :52). Naming core accretion is such an import. It may be useful, but it adds no figure primitive and moves no wall. Its provenance must be recorded, and it carries zero verdict weight until the ground pays.

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The essay's spectrometer resolved the green reading; no argument did. The astronomical analogue is the AAS Nova (2018) result that planets or snowlines can cause the same gap. Naming the source of that reading from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a better instrument.

## 4. The distinction that matters for astronomy

The six terms are a visible guess at the substrate, not nature's own list. `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` says the framework stops *"at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest"* and that *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

In this question the indiscernibility is concrete: a dust gap, a gas gap, and a kinematic kink can be produced by more than one cause. The correct report is not "the cause is a planet" and not "the cause is unknown, so anything goes." It is: the cause is a kind of thing that reduces local material and reshapes the disk; the source slot stays open until an instrument that discriminates among sources returns. That is a shape, and it is checkable.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A review cited often enough becomes a prestige proxy. It can get a claim a hearing, a telescope slot, a submission slot. It cannot decide whether the route ran. The outreach artifacts therefore ask readers to check a refuter, not to endorse a consensus, and never select recipients for likely agreement (Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: the output is *"only as good as the validator"* and *"the agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"*; a truly testable output would have to be *"derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of planet formation theory and cannot settle which mechanism operated. I opened six astronomy sources and three framework essays; their agreement with the shape claim is one evidence pool plus the framework pool, not independent corroboration. The verdict stays `specified`, and the mechanism stays `gap`.
