## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the mechanism name does not. Planet formation is a union compound requiring a route of productive transitions, a per-slot residual that is a local concentration, recursive composition of settled bodies across scales, an enumerated habitat, and a discriminating observation as anchor. Core accretion, disk instability and pebble accretion are world-frame bindings with recorded provenance and zero verdict weight here. Whether planets or snowlines opened a given gap is not settled by the projection; the source slot stays open. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, and the mechanism identity is a `gap`, reported as a verdict rather than filled by guesswork.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, four refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending the framework interior before their stage-1 verdict; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." Leading with the shape claim and its refuters avoids that cost.
- **undoable:** yes. The mechanism bindings are withheld, not asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to correct this package. If a reader returns a refutation, it is copied into `11_audit.md` with provenance and re-evaluated by the auditor seat. The shape claim itself would be narrowed or withdrawn under any of the four pre-registered refuters; no primitive is lost in doing so.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An instrument that uniquely attributes a labelled gap to a planet rather than a snowline, or gas kinematics that show an embedded mass at the dust-gap radius, would name a source for that system. A uniform disk observed to produce a bound planet with no local concentration would break F1 and F5. A verbatim figure line selecting one mechanism would refute the `gap` verdict. A derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five would break the recursion claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
