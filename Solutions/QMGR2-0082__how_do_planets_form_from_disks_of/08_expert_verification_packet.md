# Expert verification packet: the Tether on how planets form from disks

**What this is:** a claim in a form a planetary scientist can check. Mode, derivation, and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim, not `grounded`.** No measurement here settles which formation mechanism ran. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"How do planets form from disks of gas and dust around young stars" is a union of overlapping conditions, not one mechanism, so it must be unbundled before a mechanism search. The figures decide the shape any answer must have: the route is a chain of productive transitions, not an inventory; the live residual is a local concentration, not a smooth disk average; each settled body becomes the operand at the next scale, so growth is recursive cross-scale composition; and the anchor is a discriminating observation, not agreement among models. The figures do not name a mechanism, so core accretion, disk instability, and pebble accretion are recorded as world-frame imports carrying zero verdict weight until the ground pays. The mechanism slot stays open. Four falsifiers were written before this packet.

### C2 : astronomy-facing; checkable against the disk record

- **Statement:** the live signal is per object and per region. Local concentration is a necessary condition; a globally averaged disk cannot be the site of planet formation. Growth transitions are productive, not merely sequential, and each settled product becomes the operand at the next scale.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 13 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*
- **What to check:** is there any accepted formation channel in which a spatially uniform disk goes directly to a bound planet without a concentration step? Does the four-stage route (grain, pebble, planetesimal, protoplanet) use productive bonds, meaning the product did not exist in that form before the transition? Is the meter-size drift problem in smooth disks the world-frame counterpart of Fig 1 :22?
- **Refuter:** a uniform disk observed to produce a bound planet with no local concentration at any scale; or a stage in the accepted route whose product was already present in the same form before the transition fired.

### C3 : the instrument claim; checkable against gap-interpretation work

- **Statement:** a disk gap is a projection that does not determine its source. Something reduced local material and reshaped the disk, but the slot (planet, snowline, dust-opacity effect) stays open until an independent observable discriminates. Therefore the figures' shape-not-a-name limit governs here.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 6 :34 *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*
- **What to check:** the AAS Nova (2018) summary of van der Marel, Williams and Bruderer reports that *"either planets or snowlines can cause the gaps and rings in the disk around HD 163296"*, and that gas distribution can discriminate because planets decrease both gas and dust density while a snowline does not. Is that still the state of the art, or is there now a reading that uniquely identifies the cause in a labelled system? ALMA (2016) reports gas gaps overlapping dust gaps in HL Tau, which is a stronger reading but not a unique one for every gap.
- **Refuter:** a disk-gap reading that uniquely identifies its cause without a second independent observable, shown to survive a blind reanalysis; or a result that disk gaps never trace forming planets in any system, which would remove the proto-instrument edge.
