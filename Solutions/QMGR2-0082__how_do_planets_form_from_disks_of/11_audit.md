- the **objective link**, Fig 3's middle link. The shorthand says "threshold vocabulary"; the ground has several competing operational definitions of pebble and planetesimal and several thresholds, so the link is not settled even though it looks named.

One thing survives cleanly: a route must be stated with typed bonds, the live signal is local, and settled products become operands at the next scale. That is the residue worth sending.
