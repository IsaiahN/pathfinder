## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. Public role addresses and institutional forms only.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan, and no claim that agreement from a selected audience would count as evidence.

Reason, in the figures' own words: Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list of people chosen for likely agreement manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*). Fig 10 :42 *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* So the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework's interior.

## Honest standing

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, which was opened this session: LLM output is a simulation and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* It cannot settle the question it reports on. That remains a ground question, and the figures return a shape, not a name.
