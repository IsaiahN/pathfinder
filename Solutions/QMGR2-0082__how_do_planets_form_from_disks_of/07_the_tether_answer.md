# The Tether's answer on how planets form from disks

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural claim, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We can read a young disk and we can read mature planets, but the route between them is not in either reading. The residual is a **route to a state** (Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."*), and it is a **union compound**, because terrestrial cores, giant cores, direct collapse, pebble accretion, and migration overlap (Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*). Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."*

## 2. The shape

1. **The answer is a sequence of typed transitions, not an inventory.** Fig 12 :112 *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction."* Growth steps are productive (`⇒`, a new body appears), the stages are ordered (`→`), and the gates are threshold comparisons (`⋛`: sticking velocity, Stokes number near one, Toomre Q near one, isolation mass, critical core mass).
2. **The live signal is local, not a disk average.** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A smooth disk has no planet in it; a pressure bump, snowline pileup, or streaming-instability filament does. The world frame corroborates with its own meter-size problem in smooth disks (Ormel 2024).
3. **Growth is recursive cross-scale composition.** Fig 13 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"*. A pebble clump that collapses becomes a planetesimal; the settled planetesimal becomes an operand for accretion; the settled core becomes the operand for gas accretion. No new primitive is minted per stage.
4. **The habitat is enumerated, never composed.** Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Gas, solids, ice lines, turbulence, pressure bumps, the star, and the birth cluster are read off the world, and the contact cascades outward (Fig 11 :44 *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*).
5. **The anchor is a discriminating observation, not model agreement.** Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* ALMA rings and gaps are a reading; a gas gap aligned with a dust gap, a kinematic deviation, or a directly embedded planet is a stronger reading. AAS Nova (2018) shows planets and snowlines can produce the same gap, so the source stays open.
6. **The mechanism name is a `gap`, not a silence.** No figure line selects core accretion, disk instability, or pebble accretion. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* so this is reported as a verdict. The world frame names candidates; the framework returns the shape they must satisfy.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named mechanism is the one that ran. That is a ground question, open here.
- Not that disk gaps prove planets. Planets and snowlines can produce the same gap (AAS Nova 2018).
- Not a single mechanism covering all planets. The residual is a union compound; a single lever leaves the intersections standing.
- Not a smooth-disk story. Smoothing the disk averages away the live signal (Fig 1 :22).
- Not a new primitive at each growth stage. Each stage composes over a settled lower one (Fig 13 :104).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this package settles `left(R,phi)` for a specific formation route.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session): *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* The spectrometer resolved green; no argument did. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a given disk gap reduce both gas and dust density, as a planet would, or leave gas unchanged, as a snowline would?
- Do gas kinematics show the local velocity kink expected from an embedded mass, at the same radius as the dust structure?
- Does a labelled planetary system's chronology force one productive route, such that the competing arrangement cannot reproduce the observations?
- Is there any system in which a spatially uniform disk produces a bound planet with no local concentration at any scale? If so, F5 fails.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The mechanism slot is open because the available instruments do not yet discriminate among all candidate causes everywhere, and because the figures return a shape rather than a name. Fig 11 capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, not of the agent alone: better models alone do not move the ground. The answer stops at the limit of what the observations can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the mechanism by which planets form, and it does not claim that one mechanism covers every planet. It says the question is a bundle of overlapping conditions, so it must be split before a mechanism search is run; that the answer is owed as a route of productive transitions rather than a list of ingredients; that the live signal is a local concentration, not a smooth disk average; that each settled body becomes the operand at the next scale, so growth is recursive composition; and that what settles which route ran is a discriminating observation, not agreement among models. Disk gaps are already returning a reading, but planets and snowlines can produce the same reading, so the source stays open. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, four falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close the slot. The mechanism stays open until the ground settles it.
