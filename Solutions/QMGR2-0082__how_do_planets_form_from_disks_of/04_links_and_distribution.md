- Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full framework before a reader has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape claim and the refuter; offer the links later.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim; (2) the four refuters; (3) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (4) only then the four links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (5) offer the method for reuse if they ask.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1, before any theory:** residual, shape claim, pre-registered refuter. The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior. This is Fig 10 :52 *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."*
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if they are curious, the four links above. Their stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown, because agreement after exposure is one pool talking, not a witness (Fig 2 :40 *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
3. **Stage 3, on request:** the formula as a method. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode. The framework cannot check the direction of its own question.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. If it fails, the failure is the useful result and nothing further is sent.
