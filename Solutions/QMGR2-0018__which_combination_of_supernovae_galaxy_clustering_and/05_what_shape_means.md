## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* because naming the missing primitive would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question, "a set of probes that is independent, spans geometry and growth, keeps per-slot residuals and holds calibration fixed" is a shape. "SNe + BAO + lensing beats SNe + BAO" is a name. Only the first is a figure-derived output.

The essay *How a Society of Agents Would Work* makes the same point about groups: *"Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times."* A list of ten probes can be one frame wearing ten names, which is a name multiplied, not a shape.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."* And the paradox: by the time you have the exact search terms, *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: the effect terms are "independent evidence pools", "two conditions of a union", "per-slot residual", and "an anchor that does not move". Those are checkable by an outsider without reading the framework. No ranking of named combinations is stated here, because a ranking is a name, not a shape.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second point: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

For probe combinations this means: adding a fourth probe to a list adds a term, not necessarily a new axis of measurement. A new axis would be a genuinely different way of perceiving expansion (for example standard sirens or cosmic chronometers), not another catalogue sharing the same calibration. Whether a given probe is a new axis or a new name is a question for the ground, not for the figures.

## 4. Why the specific ranking is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"it cannot comment on the source"*. Several underlying causes can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* The essay's conclusion: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A figure of merit that reports "combination X is best" reads the projection (a tight error ellipse) and names the source (a superior measurement of expansion). That is the metamerism error: several probe combinations with different systematics can produce the same constraint, so what is owed is a discriminator (independent anchor, per-probe cross-check), not a name. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity."* A combination whose forecast is indiscernible from the truth is still a forecast.

## 5. Why agreement cannot fill the shape

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."* This is Fig 2's coherent drift in plain language.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is explicit: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* In cosmology, teams that share calibrations, pipelines and priors have high contact; their agreement about "which combination is best" is a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :40-41).

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers prestigious cosmologists to agree that a combination is best is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground, an independent anchor and per-slot data, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds: *"Because it is a metatheory, it cannot be validated, nor can it validate itself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of which combination is best and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Answer to the question "what shape is owed here?"

Nothing in the framework licenses a ranking of named probe combinations. The shape owed is a **conjunction of checkable conditions**: independent evidence pools, a union spanning geometry and growth, residuals indexed per redshift slot, and a calibration anchor held fixed against probe preference. Each condition has its own instrument and its own refuter (F1 to F10 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`). Which combination satisfies them best is for the ground. This answer is `general` for the structural claim, `specified` for the prediction about probe combinations, and never `grounded`.
