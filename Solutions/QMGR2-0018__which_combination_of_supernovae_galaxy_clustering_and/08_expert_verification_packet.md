# Expert verification packet - the Tether on combining cosmological probes

**What this is:** a claim in a form a cosmologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the structural rules - not `grounded`.** No ranking of named probe combinations is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A combination of supernovae, galaxy clustering and gravitational lensing measures the expansion history well under four conditions: the probes draw on independent evidence and carry different systematics; their union spans both the geometry of the distance-redshift relation and the growth of structure; residuals are indexed per redshift bin and per probe rather than collapsed into one figure of merit; and calibration is held fixed against an anchor that does not update on the preference of the joint fit. The probes stand to one another as isomers over a shared atom set, candle, ruler and shear, and the union surplus comes from the difference in their physical bonds, not from their count. The framework returns this shape, not the name of a winning combination; the ranking is a declared gap for the ground.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2 and 8

- **Statement:** independence, not probe count, decides whether agreement is evidence and whether the union has surplus.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :31 - *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 8 :45 - *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone."* Fig 8 :48 - *"How much two frames draw on the same evidence decides both whether their agreement means anything and whether their union has any surplus."*
- **Refuter:** two probes that share calibration, pipeline and systematics have their agreement counted as independent corroboration, and the ground-tested constraint still improves. Under Fig 2 :40 and Fig 8 :48 that should be impossible; if observed, C1 is false.

### C2 - framework-internal with a physics-facing check

- **Statement:** the question is a union of geometry and growth, and residuals must be kept per slot.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Fig 1 :19 - *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :60 - *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- **What to check:** do supernovae and BAO principally constrain background distance, while lensing and redshift-space distortions principally constrain growth? If a single probe measures both at the same independence with no model degeneracy, the union collapses into a partition.
- **Refuter:** a single probe that measures both conditions at equal independence, or an averaged statistic that predicts prospectively and per bin which probe fails next.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation; anchor rule

- **Statement:** calibration must not update on the probes' preferences, or the frames drift coherently and the drift looks like agreement.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29 - *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :34 - *"If it does, panel 3 turns into panel 1: the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."* Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*
- **Refuter:** a joint fit that re-calibrates the anchor, calls the drift new physics, and for which no independent anchor later discriminates. If an independent anchor settles it, C3 is weakened rather than confirmed.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking. No claim that any named combination is best. The framework returns *"a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* (Fig 9 :32).
- Not that more probes is better. Overlapping probes add names, not evidence (Fig 8 :48).
- Not that the Hubble tension is new physics, nor that it is proved drift. Fig 2 :34 names a failure class; only an independent anchor settles a case.
- Not that a Fisher figure of merit or a mock catalogue is the ground (Fig 9 :60, Fig 11 :70).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that the six-term physical substrate maps onto probes. That mapping was retracted as a strain (S1 in `01` section 6).

## 5. What would settle it

An independent anchor and a source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. **Overlap test:** measure how much the probes share calibration, pipeline and priors. High overlap means one pool talking (Fig 2 :40).
2. **Anchor test:** hold the acoustic scale and the local distance ladder fixed and re-fit. If the result only survives when M_B or r_d floats, the anchor moved (Fig 2 :34).
3. **Union test:** report geometry and growth separately per redshift bin. If one condition carries the entire constraint, only half the question was answered (Fig 9 :57).
