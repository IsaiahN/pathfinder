**Subject:** Check one claim about combining supernovae, clustering and lensing

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** a combination of supernovae, galaxy clustering and gravitational lensing measures the expansion history well under four conditions. (1) The probes are independent: they draw on different evidence and carry different systematics, so their agreement is triangulation rather than one shared pool. Supernovae are a candle, BAO is a ruler, lensing is a shear. (2) Their union spans both conditions of the question, the geometry of the distance-redshift relation and the growth of structure; a combination that measures only one is answering only half of a bundled question. (3) Residuals are kept per redshift bin and per probe, not collapsed into a single figure of merit. (4) Calibration is held fixed against an anchor that does not update on what the joint fit would prefer.
>
> What does **not** follow: there is no ranking of named combinations here. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Which combination is best in practice is a question for the data and an independent anchor, not for the framework. A Fisher forecast can rank combinations for budget; it cannot tell you which is true.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. two probes that share an evidence pool have their agreement counted as independent corroboration, and the ground-tested constraint still improves;
> 2. a single probe measures both background geometry and growth at the same independence with no model degeneracy, so the two conditions collapse into one;
> 3. a reported constraint is an average over redshift bins that still predicts, prospectively and per bin, which probe fails next;
> 4. a joint fit re-calibrates the anchor, calls the drift new physics, and no independent anchor later discriminates.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.

> - Public figures folder, branch `general-agent`: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and label the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
