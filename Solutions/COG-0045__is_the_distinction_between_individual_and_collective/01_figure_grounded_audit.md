## 0. The claim under audit

> Is the distinction between individual and collective identity universal, or does its importance depend on social organization?

Option U: the distinction is a fixed feature of personhood, invariant in form and weight. Option C: the form recurs but its importance is a derived property of a person in a habitat. The question is posed as an exclusive or. The first derivation task is to test whether it is one question or a bundle of two (see F3).

---

## 0b. Residual in effect terms

| | statement |
|---|---|
| R | A disagreement that does not shrink when the same instrument is applied in different social organizations. Each rule keeps working well on a different subset of cases. |
| Arity | Two conditions bundled: a universal-form claim and a habitat-contingent-weight claim. Not one scalar. |
| Symmetry | Asymmetric. Universality is an invariance claim over a class; contingency is a dependence claim on a variable. Comparing them is a threshold/comparison question, not a symmetric join of two equal operands. |
| Scale | Three scales are implicated: the person (self-construal), the local social organization (kinship, subsistence, relational mobility), and the population aggregate (national or regional scores). A finding at one scale does not license a verdict at another. |
| What would settle it | An anchor that registers the distinction and its weight without moving when a frame pushes on it. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." |
| Failure mode if unexamined | Aggregating across slots. Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." A single universality score would average away the live slot that says where to look. |

## 1. Domain census (before any figure line is chosen)

**R by arity / symmetry / scale:** two bundled conditions, asymmetric, three scales. The bundle is a union, not a partition, because the two conditions overlap in the same people (Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again.").

One line per domain. A domain needs actors, a currency, and a nameable anchor; no nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.

| domain | actors | currency | anchor (nameable?) | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1, individual / social cognition frame | a person, self-construal, identity boundary | self-report and behavioural consequences, reaction time, self-circle size | yes: a measurement registering the boundary at the person level (sociogram, trait rating, priming task) | Is there a boundary form that recurs at every scale? |
| D2, social organization / habitat frame | subsistence practice, irrigation, kinship, relational mobility, institutions | coordination demand, division of labour, cost of exit | yes: a cross-context measurement of weight, e.g. rice/wheat region differences with relatedness controlled | Does the weight of the boundary depend on the habitat? |
| D3, instrument / measurement frame | scales (INDCOL, MXINDCOL), tasks, reviewers | reliability, configural/metric/scalar invariance | yes: an invariance test plus a source-discriminating instrument | Does one score read the same source in every context? |

**Union, not partition.** A person is simultaneously in D1 and D2; the overlap is a compound with its own loop. A single lever (one universal scale) clears the region where only D1 holds and leaves the intersections standing, because D2 still binds (Fig 9 :54-55).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (can we register a boundary at all?) to vocabulary (do "individual" and "collective" travel across frames?) to objective (what would count as the boundary being *important*?) to planning to learn and carry. The chain usually breaks between vocabulary and objective. Fig 3 :25: having "terms for what a situation offers and being able to say what would count as progress" has "neither, so it attracts the least attention and the fewest instruments." Here we have the terms "individual" and "collective" but no instrument that says what would count as importance independent of a habitat. A reading below that break is a reading of nothing (Fig 3 :23).

**Strain test applied up front.** A line that only fits with a strain governs another quantity. The universality word is strained if read as "same weight everywhere"; that reading governs D3 (invariance), not D1. Re-census separates form (D1) from weight (D2/D3). No strain remains after the split; see section 7.

## 3. Findings

### F1 - The form is a kernel invariant; the weight is not (decisive for the split)

Fig 4 :19-20: "Only the actors, the currency and the anchor change."

Fig 11 :39: "And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."

Fig 11 :40: "A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."

The first line says what does *not* change with size: the loop and the naming rule. The second and third say what does change: the property belongs to the agent-and-habitat, not the agent alone. Applied here, the *form* of a boundary between person and group recurs at every scale (a universal kernel), while its *importance* is a property of the person-in-social-organization. So neither "universal" nor "contingent" wins the whole question: they govern different quantities. Keeping them as one scalar is the error Fig 1 :19 names.

**Refuter:** find a social organization in which no individual/collective boundary of any form is registrable even with a refined instrument; or show that changing the habitat leaves the boundary's weight invariant across a properly controlled comparison. Either breaks the split. Status: `general` for the form, `specified` for the weight.

### F2 - Importance is a relation, not a kind

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."

Fig 11 :50: "The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."

Fig 12 :103: "Composition is recursive, and the level decides what is irreducible."

If importance were a property the distinction carries, it would be invariant. The figures treat valence as relative to the residual and level as deciding irreducibility. "Individual identity matters more than collective identity" is therefore not a property of a person or a culture; it is a relation between a boundary and a habitat at a stated level. This is why a national score cannot be the anchor: it changes the level of the measurement.

**Refuter:** a within-person measure of identity weight that is stable across radically different habitats and predicts behaviour equally in both, with invariance established rather than assumed. Status: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 12, low overlap between habitat valence and level-relativity).

### F3 - The question is a bundled union and must be split before search

Fig 9 :4-5: "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."

Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

The universal-form literature (Markus and Kitayama 1991, read 2026-11-22) and the social-organization literature (Talhelm et al. 2014, read via Bower 2014) each work well on a different subset. Forcing one answer manufactures a disagreement that effort cannot shrink. The honest move is to split D1 (form) from D2 (weight) and D3 (instrument) and keep the verdicts separate. The world literature agrees: Soler-Anguiano et al. 2023 state that "The polarized dichotomy of this construct has been deemed unproductive in the field of cultural comprehension."

**Refuter:** demonstrate that a single question with a single well-defined predicate ("the distinction is important") yields a stable yes/no across all three domains without splitting. Status: `general` structural, triangulated (Fig 9 split rule and the psychometric critique, low overlap).

### F4 - The anchor test fails for the naive universality claim

Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."

Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."

A national individualism score is not an anchor for this question: it moves when the instrument is reworded, when the sample changes, and when the frame changes (Soler-Anguiano et al. 2023 show INDCOL's fit and reliability degrade on translation). An anchor here would be a measurement that registers the boundary's weight and does not move with the frame. Until one is named, the universality claim has no ground and remains `candidate`.

**Refuter:** produce an instrument whose reading of identity weight is invariant under translation, sample change, and frame change, and that predicts behaviour at both D1 and D2. Status: `specified`.

### F5 - Habitat must be enumerated, not composed from a scale

Fig 13 :43: "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."

Fig 13 :44: "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."

A "collectivist culture" is not a habitat; it is a summary. The habitat is irrigation, labour exchange, relational mobility, kinship, and the cost of exit, read off the world (Bower 2014 describes rice irrigation requiring coordinated filling and draining and cooperative planting; Talhelm et al. 2014 measured relational mobility and the friend/stranger distinction). Enumerate those, then ask about identity weight. Composing a habitat from a score inverts the rule and produces a frame that agrees with itself.

**Refuter:** show that the habitat term adds no predictive variance over the aggregate score once the residual is described. Status: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 13 enumeration and Fig 11 agent-and-habitat, moderate overlap noted honestly since both are habitat figures).

### F6 - Describe the effect before choosing the frame

Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."

Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."

If we first pick "individualism/collectivism" as the frame, any culture will seem to fit. The residual must be described in effect terms first: *a boundary whose behavioural weight changes with coordination demands*. Only then do self-construal, subsistence practice, and measurement become candidate frames. Wong, Wang and Klann 2018 argue that "the practice of labeling societies and racial/ethnic groups as 'collectivistic' or 'individualistic' should be discontinued," which is the same refusal from the measurement side.

**Refuter:** show that picking the frame first yields the same cross-domain predictions as describing the residual first. Status: `general` method, triangulated (Fig 9 effect-before-cause and the I-C critique).

### F7 - No proof of absence from a null result

Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."

Fig 6 :29: "inward: is our representation adequate to hold it? The instrument extends access. Fails by refining forever on an axis that cannot carry it. Sharpen in our own terms."

Fig 6 :30: "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."

If no boundary is found in a culture, that is not yet evidence that the form is not universal. It may be an instrument failure (inward) or a frame that already holds the term under another name (outward). These two tracks are opposed and must not be read against one another. The distinction between underivable in principle and unreachable at this budget is preserved: a null at this budget is not a verdict of absence.

**Refuter:** an instrument already returning something that fails to resolve the boundary in that culture, so there is an edge to extend. Status: `general` method.

### F8 - A conflict sends us back to the census, never flips the verdict

Roberts 2015 (Frontiers in Psychology, opened 2026-11-22) conflicts with the rice/wheat causal story at the measurement level: "researchers should be wary of correlations that do not control for shared cultural history." The provinces are not independent samples; farming practice and cultural values can be inherited or borrowed.

Per the WATSON rule, a conflict sends us back to the census. Re-census: the conflict is in D3 (are the observations independent?) and does not touch the figure claim in D2 that importance is agent-and-habitat dependent. A correlated evidence pool weakens the *world witness* for the specific rice/wheat causal claim; it does not reverse the figure verdict. Fig 2 treats correlated frames as one pool talking, which is exactly what Roberts identifies. The verdict stands, and the world source is recorded as conflicting on a sub-claim rather than discarded.

**Refuter:** demonstrate that the independence failure is in the figure-derived claim itself, i.e. that agent-and-habitat dependence disappears when shared history is controlled. Status: `specified`, conflict logged, no flip.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat rule that "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." The split verdict is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A culture is found in which no individual/collective boundary of any form is registrable at any resolution after a genuine inward instrument search (breaks the universal-form claim; a bare null does not count, per F7).
2. A properly controlled cross-habitat comparison shows identity-weight invariance with scalar measurement invariance established (breaks the contingency claim).
3. A single scale achieves scalar invariance in every tested culture with no culture-specific items and predicts behaviour equally in all of them (breaks the instrument-contingency claim).
4. A figure re-census shows the kernel is not invariant with size, i.e. the rules themselves change when actors, currency and anchor change (breaks the Fig 4 reading).
5. A source-discriminating instrument shows the same reading arising from the same source in every habitat (breaks the metamerism reading in `05_what_shape_means.md`).

If any fires, the split is withdrawn and the question is re-censused. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Answer the question as a split, not an either/or: universal in form, contingent in weight. Do not average the domains (F1, F3).
2. State the scale and the level on every claim; a national score is not a person-level claim (F2, F4).
3. Enumerate the habitat before invoking a culture label (F5).
4. Describe the residual in effect terms before picking a theoretical frame (F6).
5. Record the Roberts 2015 conflict as a D3 measurement problem and keep the figure verdict separate from the world causal story (F8).
6. Send the residual and the refuter first; offer the figures only after a verdict is recorded (Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
