## What the wider literature does for the derivation

World sources are secondary. They corroborate or conflict; they do not decide.

1. **Form recurs, weight varies.** Markus and Kitayama 1991 supplies the independent/interdependent distinction as a universal form with culturally variable dominance. Bower 2014 and Talhelm et al. 2014 supply a ground-visible case where social organization moves the weight. Together they support the split, but they are not independent witnesses of the figure verdict: they are in the same cultural-psychology evidence pool.
2. **The instrument is not neutral.** Soler-Anguiano et al. 2023 and Wong et al. 2018 are lower-overlap witnesses (psychometrics and critique). They predict that a single universal scale will read the same score for different sources, which is the metamerism problem in `05_what_shape_means.md`.
3. **One real conflict.** Roberts 2015 conflicts with the rice/wheat causal claim at the independence level. It is logged as D3, not used to flip the verdict. A conflict sends us back to the census; here the re-census locates it in the instrument/sampling domain.

Fields covered: cultural psychology, social cognition, cultural evolution and phylogenetics, psychometrics, science journalism, sociology (secondary mapping). No field was chosen because it was expected to agree.
