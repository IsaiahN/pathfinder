## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that the distinction between individual and collective identity is universal, or that its importance depends on social organization. Option U: assert one universal predicate. Option C: assert one habitat-dependent predicate. What each leads to: U licenses a single travelling measure and a universal theory of identity, and it fails the anchor test because the score moves with the frame. C licenses a measure with an explicit habitat term and a refusal to read source off a score, and it must answer the independence objection. A third reading, derived below, keeps the verdicts per domain: universal in form, contingent in weight, instrument-dependent in reading. This is a union compound, not a single hard question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A disagreement that does not shrink when the same instrument is applied across social organizations. Arity: two bundled conditions, a universal-form claim and a habitat-contingent-weight claim. Symmetry: asymmetric; invariance over a class versus dependence on a variable, so comparison is a threshold question, not a symmetric join. Scale: person, local social organization, population aggregate. Fig 9 names a bundled union: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, individual / social cognition frame.** Actors: a person, self-construal, identity boundary. Currency: self-report and behavioural consequences. Anchor: a measurement registering the boundary at the person level (sociogram, rating, priming task). Question type: is there a boundary form that recurs at every scale?
- **Domain 2, social organization / habitat frame.** Actors: subsistence practice, irrigation, kinship, relational mobility, institutions. Currency: coordination demand, division of labour, cost of exit. Anchor: a cross-context measurement of weight with relatedness controlled. Question type: does the weight depend on the habitat?
- **Domain 3, instrument / measurement frame.** Actors: scales (INDCOL, MXINDCOL), tasks, reviewers. Currency: reliability and configural, metric, scalar invariance. Anchor: an invariance test plus a source-discriminating instrument. Question type: does one score read the same source everywhere?
- **Union, not partition.** A person is in D1 and D2 simultaneously; the overlap is a compound with its own loop. Order by dependency (Fig 3): perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. The chain stops at the objective "what would count as importance," which is unmeasured.
- **Strain test.** Reading "universal" as "same weight everywhere" strains the line and governs D3, not D1. Re-census separates form from weight. No figure-to-figure strain remains.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound and is answered per domain, never averaged. Universal in **form**: a boundary between person and group recurs at every scale while the actors, currency and anchor change. Contingent in **weight**: importance is a relation between a boundary and a habitat, not a property carried by the distinction. Instrument-dependent in **reading**: a single universal score fails the anchor test and exhibits metamerism because different habitats can produce the same reading. The specific rice/wheat causal story is contested at D3 by Roberts 2015; that conflict does not reverse the figure verdict. `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The split, its level claims, its analogy disclosure and its refuters are written before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single universal slogan instead of a checkable shape. Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. The binding of independent/interdependent onto the held poles is removable without touching the kernel, and the habitat term is additive rather than primitive. A superseding packet can correct the analogy strain without withdrawing the split.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that holds scalar invariance across habitats and predicts identity weight would reverse D3; a controlled comparison removing the weight dependence would reverse D2; a demonstration that the kernel changes with scale would reverse D1; a re-census showing the capability/valence analogy governs another quantity would send D2 back to census. Until one of these fires the verdict stands as `candidate`.
