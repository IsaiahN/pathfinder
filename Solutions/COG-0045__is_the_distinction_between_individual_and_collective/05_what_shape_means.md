## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 states "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 states it cannot say which primitive is missing, "because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened in full): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name which identity form occupies which slot. It constrains; it does not fill.

- Shape: *a boundary between person and group that recurs at every scale, whose behavioural weight is a derived quantity of the habitat.*
- Name (not given by the framework): *this culture is individualist / this person is interdependent.* Those are bindings from the cultural-psychology frame, provenanced as such.

The distinction between shape and name is exactly the distinction the question asks about, one level up. Treating the name as the answer is the metamerism error in section 4.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." The essay also names the bundle failure directly: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is F3 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (opened in full): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." The same essay records the drift warning that governs outreach: two people who only check against each other drift together, and "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." The paradox is that by then you have "composed the answer through the process of locating it."

Applied here: the effect description is "a boundary whose weight changes as coordination demands change." The vocabulary is borrowed from self-construal research and from subsistence studies. Neither is the answer; both are probes narrowed against the residual.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52).

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (opened in full) sharpens this: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."

So "independent" and "interdependent" are a declared choice from one sensorium, the WEIRD one that dominates the literature (the Wikipedia mapping notes most research comes from the US, Germany and the Netherlands). Declaring them makes the choice visible; it does not make it universal.

### Correction to my own section 3 (see `06_isomers_and_order.md` Q1)

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any identity form. That overstates the rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like." ## 4. Why the single universal scale is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened in full) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or red-green-blue, or cyan-magenta-yellow-black, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."

An individualism score is the green reading. Rice irrigation, wheat rainfall, relational mobility, kinship and market integration are candidate sources. Soler-Anguiano et al. 2023 show the reading degrades when the instrument is translated, and Wong et al. 2018 argue the labels themselves should be discontinued. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a more confident universal score.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened in full): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." A scale cannot certify its own universality.

## 5. Question answered: what licenses a universal-identity claim?

**Nothing in the framework, and nothing in the opened world sources, licenses a bare universal claim about weight.** The form is licensed as a kernel invariant (Fig 4 :19-20); the weight is licensed only by a ground reading in a named habitat with a source-discriminating instrument. Until one exists, the universal claim is a `candidate`, not an `accepted` result.

**Does the record commit the error the README forbids?** Yes if it names one identity form as *the* answer to the slot. The correct form keeps the slot marked: `individual/collective boundary (form invariant, weight = f(habitat))`. The specific binding is provenance from the cultural-psychology frame and must be recorded as such.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened in full): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A campaign that gathers famous names to agree with the split is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried, and let the ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Where "shape" lands for this question

The honest answer has the same shape as the question's own defect: the question is bundled, so the shape is a **split with a dependency**, not a single predicate. Universal in form, contingent in weight, and instrument-dependent in reading. Anything that collapses the three into one number has paid the aggregation cost Fig 1 :22 names: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
