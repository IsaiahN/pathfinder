## 2. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 says the system can report that it lacks something and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, opened this session: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* And on method: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, then let new terms that overlap or correlate narrow the space: *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For ORI-0055:

- *"Some compartments persist at the expense of others in a finite habitat"* is a shape.
- *"Oleate vesicles with 10 mol% dipeptide outgrow empty oleate vesicles"* is a name, a binding from the chemistry frame.
- *"Differential reproduction needs a positive heritability and a positive selection differential kept together by a compartment"* is a shape (Fig 7 :26).
- *"Φ29-based linear DNA replicator in liposomes"* is a name.

## 3. How the shape was obtained here

- Residual in effect terms first (compete versus reproduce across generations), then a domain census with actors, currency and anchor per domain (`01` section 1), then figure lines quoted before any conclusion (`01` section 2). This follows Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Without the census, any field, chemistry, evolutionary biology or astrobiology, would have seemed to fit.
- Effect terms rather than cause terms: Fig 9 :30-31, *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect is differential persistence and heritable difference, not "life" or "fitness" as a cause.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the three-condition separator plus the named `gap`; there is no further term to acquire that the available instruments could pay for.

## 4. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The figures README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The RNA-world frame supplies a heritable polymer by import, not by composing lipids (see `01` F6).

Carried essay *The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Applying this to ORI-0055: a growth curve is a reading, and several mechanisms (osmotic swelling, phospholipid fraction, encapsulated catalyst, light sensitivity) can produce it. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator instrument. That is why the historical question is a `gap` and not a name.

## 5. Correction to my own first reading of "shape, not a name"

First pass I applied the rule as a blanket ban on naming any protocell component. That was an overstatement, and the correction is kept visible.

- The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive **not held in Γ**.
- Amphiphiles, vesicles, catalysts and compartments are already available in the chemistry frame. Naming them is a **binding from that frame**, not the invention of a missing primitive.
- What remains forbidden is (a) inventing a seventh essential term, (b) claiming a binding moves the wall when it adds no atom (Fig 6 :22, *"IMPORT adds an atom"*; a binding adds none), and (c) naming the historical winner from a projection.
- Analogy with the exemplar: naming Gradient was a binding onto a held atom, not a forbidden invention; the metamerism objection still stood because no instrument discriminated sources. The same two claims are held here: binding is allowed, source-discrimination is still owed.

Earlier overstatement retracted as applied to held chemistry; metamerism point not retracted. Triangulated (README plus Fig 6 plus Fig 13 :134).

## 6. Simulation, not ground

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened this session: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is therefore a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the protocell claim and cannot settle it. It cannot settle which protocell won, whether a given vesicle population has measurable `h²`, or whether a Precambrian pond ran the loop. Only a ground reading can do that.

## 7. Prestige is not action

Same essay: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

For this question, the famous names in origin-of-life chemistry are useful as a way to get the claim a hearing and a budget to be tried. Their agreement is not evidence. The right ask is a refutation of the heritability condition, not an endorsement. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*

## 8. What this means for the verdict

The framework returns the shape of a checkable answer: variation inside, selection between, non-updating ground, a heritable carrier, per-lineage measurement, and a recorded `gap` where instruments stop. It does not return the name of a prebiotic protocell, a lipid recipe or a winning genotype. Any artifact in this folder that asserted one would be committing the error this file names.
