## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For ECN-0065 the shape is not a percentage. It is: the residual must remain indexed per worker-slot; the four named conditions are a union with an unassignable shared fraction; each has its own currency and instrument; and any universal number is `gap`. A shape constrains a candidate answer before it can be filled. A single national percentage is a filler masquerading as a shape.

The same essay names the exact structural error in this question: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is what the four conditions are. They are one aggregate question conflating several conditions, which is the shape reported in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F2.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not." Relevance is fixed by the residual, not by the popularity of a field.
- *Library of Babel* on method: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, then allow "new terms that overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably with your known terms." Each term narrows the space. Here the known terms are occupation, hours, discrimination, and caregiving; the new term the derivation binds is *union of overlapping conditions*, which overlaps with the empirical observation that one event (a birth) fires three channels at once.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach in `02_outreach_email.md` sends a checkable description of effects and refuters, not a theory.
- Fig 6 :30 (quoted in the same essay): "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as a variance between predicted and received pay per worker-slot, not as "the gender gap" treated as a substance.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stop is the honest `gap` on a universal number: the search ends where it began, with the four conditions still overlapping.

## 3. What licenses a name, a number, or an instrument

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For ECN-0065 the ground is the pay record plus a source-discriminating instrument, never the regression residual.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. The Simpson's-paradox mathematics (the SEP entry) is exactly such an import: it predicts that an association can reverse under partitioning, and its Berkeley case shadows the occupational partition of a pay gap.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the import rule: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." An instrument that measures differential treatment is a new dimension, not another control variable. The same essay states: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." The four condition names are visible guesses about compression axes, not nature's own list.

**The "how much" shape is stated by the author himself.** *How a Society of Agents Would Work*, discussing a comparable attribution question, says: "None of this gives one number for 'how much smarter.' It gives the dials: how different the members are, how much of the problem must be done in sequence, how costly it is to stay in sync, and how many generations the group keeps building. Each can be measured on a running group; none is a constant to pick." The same shape governs ECN-0065: the answer is the dials (occupation, hours, discrimination, caregiving, each with its own instrument), not one number, and none is a constant to pick. This is a within-framework corroboration, not an outside witness.

## 4. Why the unexplained remainder is the metamerism error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source." The reading may come from one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or "a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered." The essay's general claim: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". The consequence: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy", and the case was resolved "not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."

Applied here: a pay regression that returns an "unexplained" remainder cannot say which source produced it. Naming that remainder *discrimination* is the metamerism error, naming a source from a reading. What is owed is a better instrument, which is why F4 in `01` refuses D3 until an audit or blind-design instrument exists. *How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction-horizon limit: "A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin." A gap can be below the current union horizon without being unanswerable forever, which is why `gap` here is unreachable-at-this-budget rather than underivable.

## 5. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly" and "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."

For a labor-economics question this matters concretely. A widely cited decomposition, a Nobel prize, or a famous journal does not settle which condition changed a worker's pay. Those things give a claim a budget to be tried. The check is an instrument that refuses the claim. The 20-minute check in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` is written for this reason: it asks for a refutation, not an endorsement, and it records the answer before any framework material is shown.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3 (preserved)

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming occupation, hours, or caregiving, because a name would fill a slot the framework leaves open. That overstated the rule. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. Occupation, hours, and caregiving are already-available conditions in the world, so naming them is a binding onto held vocabulary, a provenance act, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is turning a binding into a derivation, or claiming it moves the wall (Fig 6 :23). The metamerism point in section 4 is **not** retracted: the reason the "unexplained" remainder cannot be named discrimination is the missing discriminator, not a general ban on naming. Both readings are recorded so neither is silently lost. This matches the correction preserved in the exemplar's `05` section 5b.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* is blunter: when an LLM is given the figures and formula, "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra", and the result "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."

Therefore every file in this folder is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the labor-economics claim, and it cannot settle the question it reports on. All empirical statements are attributed to sources actually opened and are secondary corroboration only. The verdicts in `09_watson_answer.md` come from the figures, with the mode stated on every substantive claim.
