# The Tether's answer on the gender pay gap decomposition

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No wage data were analysed for this folder.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observed gap is the difference between what a worker's situation predicts the pay will be and what the pay actually returns, indexed per worker-slot. The question asks to aggregate that residual into one number and divide it among four named conditions. Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The answer cannot be a global percentage split, because the operation asked for removes the signal that would steer a check. This is a union compound, not one hard question with four candidate answers (Fig 9 :57). It needs its own loop and its own instrument per domain.

The sought-for shape was bound before the run: the indexing the residual must keep, the union of the four conditions, their currencies, and an instrument per condition that can discriminate which one changed a pay outcome. It is now refusable by the same test as everything else (`01` section 4).

## 2. The shape, with verdicts kept separate

**D1 Occupation.** Occupation is a contact-changing sorting condition. Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A move into a different occupation changes the worker's contact with the pay ground, so it is a real lever, `specified`. It does not have a figure-assigned share. Blau and Kahn (2017) report that "gender differences in occupation and industry continued to be important" by 2010, while the 2023 Nobel press release reports that the bulk of the gap is now within occupation. The location of the explained part moves over time; that is F5, not a fixed fact. **Verdict: `specified` that occupation is a lever; `gap` on any universal share. Triangulated for the lever (Fig 11 and the world sources, with low overlap between a metalaw and an empirical survey).**

**D2 Hours.** Hours are a time quantity and a habitat condition (Fig 11 :46). Their valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49): a part-time schedule helps one worker and harms another. Goldin (2014) predicts that "The gender gap in pay would be considerably reduced and might vanish altogether if firms did not have an incentive to disproportionately reward individuals who labored long hours and worked particular hours." That is a contact claim about how jobs are structured, corroborated in a field that does not read the figures. **Verdict: `specified` that hours are a lever with residual-relative sign; `gap` on share. Triangulated (Fig 11 plus Goldin).**

**D3 Discrimination.** Discrimination is admitted only when an instrument makes differential treatment readable at equal observed productivity. Fig 3 :27: "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at." Without such an instrument, "discrimination" is the unexplained remainder of a model, which moves with the model and is a symptom of a dead channel (Fig 10 :28: "you have lost touch"). Bertrand and Mullainathan (2004) demonstrate the callback-audit class of instrument, with "White names receive 50 percent more callbacks for interviews." That is a race audit and gives only the method, not a gender effect size. **Verdict: conditional `specified` with an instrument, otherwise `gap`. Single-source for the figure requirement; the method analogue is one witness from labor economics.**

**D4 Caregiving burdens.** Caregiving is a habitat condition enacted as time out of work, reduced hours, and interrupted careers. Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." A birth is an event whose penalty is "driven in roughly equal proportions by labor force participation, hours of work, and wage rates" (Kleven et al. 2018). One event fires three channels, which is the empirical signature of a union. UN Women's public page corroborates in a policy frame: "because women perform the bulk of household work, they often have little time left to pursue economic opportunities." **Verdict: `specified` that caregiving is a contact-changing event with multiple channels; `gap` on share. Triangulated (Fig 11 plus low-overlap policy source and an evolutionary-biology mechanism analogue).**

**Across all four.** They form a union with an unassignable shared fraction (Fig 9 :54-57). A case in four conditions is a specific compound, not a mixture. Their shares cannot be added in a figure-supplied common currency (Fig 12 :125-130, `specified` by analogy, single-source). A variance share is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the cause (Fig 9 :60). **Verdict on the universal percentage: `gap`, unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence".

The four verdicts are not averaged. There is no "weighted overall conclusion" that trades one domain off against another.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a percentage, a range of percentages, or a new decomposition method.
- Not a claim that occupation and hours are "real" while discrimination and caregiving are not. Both halves of the question contain real conditions; the two halves form a union rather than a partition.
- Not a claim that discrimination does not exist. It is a conditional domain: admitted with an instrument, withheld without one.
- Not a claim that the figures measure a wage gap. They constrain the shape of an answer; the pay record is the ground for magnitude.
- Not a claim that caregiving is random, chosen, or a property of a person. It is a condition around a worker with residual-relative valence.
- Not a claim that a cross-country constant exists. The Danish child-penalty share moved "from about 40% in 1980 to about 80% in 2013" (Kleven et al. 2018).
- Not `grounded`. No measurement settles the shape claim, and no universal number is reported.

## 4. What would settle it, and what the ground is

The ground for magnitude is the pay record and the measured hours, occupation, care time, and treatment outcomes at the worker-slot. The ground for mechanism requires a discriminator, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case.

Checkable discriminators:

1. **Audit instrument:** does a randomized, name- or signal-blind treatment return a differential at equal observed productivity that is invariant to the controls in the earnings model? If yes, D3 is admitted as a domain with a reading.
2. **Event instrument:** does an event-dated care change move pay through participation, hours, and wage rates in the proportions the shape predicts, and does removing one channel leave the others standing? If yes, the union claim is confirmed at that slot.
3. **Partition test:** does a four-condition decomposition keep its shares when a fifth overlapping condition is added? If yes, F2 is false and a partition becomes available.
4. **Reversal test:** does the population-level gap reverse or change sign when partitioned by occupation, as Simpson's paradox predicts? If yes, the per-slot indexing is mandatory.
5. **Universal number:** is a single percentage replicated across datasets with non-overlapping instruments and invariant to new conditions? If yes, F9 is false.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The derivation stops where the available instruments stop discriminating sources. The four conditions are visible guesses about compression axes; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral (*They're Scanning Books*: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."). The record stops at the limit of what instruments distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "indiscernibility is not identity." A universal percentage is reported as `gap`, and a gap is a verdict (Fig 13 :134), not a silence and not a failure to try harder.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not produce a percentage split of the gender pay gap. It says that the residual must stay indexed per worker-slot, because an average across workers throws away the signal that says where to look. Occupation, hours, discrimination, and caregiving are a union of overlapping conditions with a shared fraction that cannot be assigned to any one of them: a worker in all four is a specific compound, not a mixture of four shares. Occupation, hours, and caregiving are real contact-changing conditions, and discrimination becomes an evidence-bearing claim only when an instrument makes differential treatment readable; otherwise it is the model's own unexplained remainder and a symptom of a lost channel. The shares have different currencies and do not add. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, an instrument per condition, falsifiers written in advance, and an honest `gap` where a universal number would be. The four verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
