## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that the gender pay gap can be divided into a part reflecting occupation and hours and a part reflecting discrimination and unequal caregiving. Option A: report a percentage split. Option B: keep the per-slot indexing, treat the four conditions as a union, demand an instrument per condition, and report the universal percentage as `gap`. What each leads to: A uses a filter as a witness and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a checkable shape, refuters written in advance, and an honest stop at the instrument limit.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and dependency order.

- **R (the residual).** The difference between what a worker's situation predicts the pay will be and the pay actually received, per worker-slot. Arity: four conditions plus a worker-slot and an institution/time. Symmetry: asymmetric; a career interruption is not recovered by reversing it. Scale: worker step, population of worker-slots, household and institutional habitat. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **Domain 1, occupation.** Actors: workers, employers, gatekeepers. Currency: sorting and occupational pay. Anchor: recorded occupation and pay, which do not move with our frame. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, hours.** Actors: workers, schedulers, employers. Currency: hours supplied and paid. Anchor: measured hours. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, discrimination.** Actors: employers, workers. Currency: differential treatment at equal observed productivity. Anchor: only an audit or blind instrument; without one the "anchor" is the model's own remainder and moves with it. Conditionally admitted, otherwise not a domain yet.
- **Domain 4, caregiving burdens.** Actors: caregivers, children or elders, households, employers. Currency: time out of work, reduced hours, unpaid care. Anchor: event-dated care time and labor-force attachment. Admitted.
- **Excluded or absorbed.** "The unexplained residual" has no anchor independent of the model and is absorbed into D3 as a symptom. "Gender" is the partition label, not a mechanism, and is dissolved into D1 to D4. "A universal percentage" has no anchor and is declared `gap` before any figure is applied.
- **Partition or union.** Union. D1 to D4 overlap on every slot. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :57 "What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."
- **Order by dependency.** Perception of which condition fired, then vocabulary for it, then the objective for that slot, then intervention, then carrying the method. Fig 3 :27 "A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."

Strain test: the first census tried to map the four conditions onto the six physical terms and only fit by calling each condition a kind of space or time. That is a strain, so Fig 13 does not govern the four domains; it is retained for the honest stop only. A second strain, the extension of Fig 12's two-currency rule to four causal currencies, is marked as an analogue, single-source. Both are visible in `01` section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The requested object, a universal percentage split, does not exist as a figure-licensed answer. The shape that survives is: the residual stays indexed per worker-slot; occupation, hours, discrimination, and caregiving form a union of overlapping conditions with an unassignable shared fraction; the four verdicts are kept separate and never averaged; occupation, hours, and caregiving are contact-changing conditions with residual-relative valence; discrimination is admitted only with a source-discriminating instrument, otherwise it is a symptom of a dead channel; the shares have different currencies and do not add; and a variance share is a budget filter, never a cause witness. The universal number is a declared `gap` of the unreachable-at-this-budget kind. Mode `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states mode and refuters before its conclusion. The real cost is using a filter as a witness: that misdirects research effort and spends the independence of outside readers who are shown a summary before they have recorded a verdict. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost is avoidable by leading with the shape claim and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No percentage is asserted, so nothing numerical has to be withdrawn. A local, named-population, named-year answer can be added for any one condition once an instrument exists, without touching the union shape or the per-slot indexing. A claimed universal percentage would be undoable by withdrawal; the figure verdict is reversible only by a figure re-census or a ground reading.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specific reversers: a decomposition invariant to added conditions and reference categories (kills the union verdict); one instrument discriminating all four conditions at the slot with no remainder (kills the union instrument claim); a stable universal percentage replicated with non-overlapping instruments (kills the `gap`); a demonstration that a global share predicts per-slot intervention outcomes better than the per-slot reading (kills F1); a figure re-census licensing a common additive currency (kills the non-additivity analogue). Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
