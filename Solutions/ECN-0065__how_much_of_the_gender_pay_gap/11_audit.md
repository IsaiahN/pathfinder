## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions, in the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry, and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the difference between a worker-slot's predicted pay and the pay received. Arity: a worker-slot, an arrangement, an outcome, an institution and time, with four candidate condition classes. Symmetry: asymmetric, because a birth precedes and is not undone by its penalty, and a move into an occupation is not recovered by reversing it. Scale: worker step, population of slots, household and institutional habitat. The request for a single split is an aggregation over the first scale that is then applied to the second, which is a filter problem before it is a measurement problem.
- **Domain 1, occupation.** Actors: workers, employers, gatekeepers. Currency: sorting and occupational pay. Anchor: recorded occupation and pay. Admitted.
- **Domain 2, hours.** Actors: workers, schedulers. Currency: hours supplied and paid. Anchor: measured hours. Admitted.
- **Domain 3, discrimination.** Actors: employers, workers. Currency: treatment differential at equal observed productivity. Anchor: only an audit or blind instrument; absent that, the anchor is the earnings model's own remainder and moves when the controls move. Conditionally admitted, otherwise not a domain yet.
- **Domain 4, caregiving.** Actors: caregivers, dependants, households, employers. Currency: time out of work and unpaid care. Anchor: event-dated care and attachment. Admitted.
- **Excluded.** The unexplained remainder, gender as a free-floating cause, and a universal percentage. None has an anchor independent of the model.
- **Union, not partition.** Each slot can be in all four conditions at once. A single lever clears a region where one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. This is the same structural shape as Fig 9 :54-57.
- **Dependency order.** Perception of which condition fired, vocabulary for it, objective for that slot, intervention, carried method. The aggregate decomposition is asked at link 3 with an observer-side statistic, which is the middle-link failure of Fig 3.

My census matches the primary's four domains, its conditional treatment of D3, and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 (per-slot indexing) and Fig 9 (union), I derived from the anchor test (Fig 2) and the dependency chain (Fig 3). Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap within the framework, so the agreement is weak but not circular. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E compose T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed zero. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot indexing), Fig 9 :55 and :57 (union), Fig 3 :27 (instrument), Fig 11 :40 and :49 (contact and valence), Fig 12 :125 (currencies), Fig 13 :134 (honest stop).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: the residual stays per slot; four conditions form a union; discrimination requires an instrument; shares have different currencies; the universal percentage is `gap`.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a requested national percentage split of the gap into occupation-and-hours versus discrimination-and-caregiving.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **per-slot indexing**, collapsed into a national average (Fig 1 :22);
- **the union shared fraction**, forced into an assignable part (Fig 9 :57);
- **the instrument requirement for D3**, replaced by an unexplained remainder (Fig 3 :27);
- **currency non-additivity**, replaced by a single percentage sum (Fig 12 :125).

One thing survives cleanly: conditions around a worker change contact with the pay ground, and their valence is relative to the residual. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
