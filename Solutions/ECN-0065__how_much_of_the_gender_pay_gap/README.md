## The question restated as an effect

The measured gender pay gap is a single aggregate number. The question asks for the opposite of the loop's own indexing rule: collect the per-worker residual into one global figure, then divide that figure among four named conditions. What is being decided is not a percentage. It is whether the four conditions form a partition whose shares can be assigned, or a union of overlapping conditions whose shared part cannot be assigned and whose currencies do not add. Option A (a fixed percentage split) leads to a filter being used as a witness. Option B (per-slot residuals, instruments, and separate verdicts) leads to a checkable shape with an honest stop. The answer bound in advance is: the indexing the residual must keep, the reason the four conditions are a union, and the instrument that would discriminate them at a named worker-slot.
