## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question"*

So for INS-0002 a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer about institutional emergence that can be stated before any particular country, constitution, leader, property regime or social norm is named. It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *inclusive institutions persist while a constraint channel is kept reachable and reach is renewed from a closure that differs; emergence has a threshold form; the institutions that once helped can change sign when the residual changes.*
- Name, withheld: *this society became inclusive because of its informal norms, its civil society, its inequality structure, its colonial legacy, or its founding constitution.*

The distinction matters here because institutional scholarship is almost entirely in the naming business. The 2016 "Paths" paper names state capacity, distribution of power, informal institutions and social norms; the 2005 book description names six dependencies. Those are bindings from the institutional-economics frame onto atoms, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "inclusive political institutions", "state capacity", "pluralism", "civil society", "credible commitment", "critical juncture", "levelling coalition" and "phase transition". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The description in effect terms (duration of a live constraint slot, indexed per institution) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating INS-0002 as a union (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the two conditions C1 and C2 are stated, the instrument for each is named, the threshold shape is stated, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For INS-0002 neither has happened at the institutional scale. There is no prospective measurement that pays for "this specific norm caused the opening of this slot", and the names from the institutional-economics literature were adopted without a source gate for the figure claim. Hence the `gap` row F9 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error the question invites when it asks how much institutions depend on prior arrangements. A stable inclusive institution is a reading. It is consistent with a favourable property regime, with a mobilized civil society, with an elite split, with an external shock, with a colonial legacy, or with a combination not yet named. The reading does not determine the source. Naming informal norms as *the* source from the reading is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among readers fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory, and why F9 stays a declared gap.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to institutional research

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is an instance, in the author's own agent system, of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence about institutions.

For outreach this means: a list of famous institutional economists who agree with the shape would be a monoculture, not a check. The ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular prior economic or social arrangement is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a property regime, a norm or a civil-society association is already in a polity's library, naming it is a binding from the institutional frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*) or that the framework itself selected the name. The binding still needs provenance and a source-discriminating instrument, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be. Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

A second narrowing: I initially wrote that the modernization result *conflicts* with the figure verdict and therefore weakens it. That was wrong in form. A world conflict sends the derivation back to the census (re-census R2 in `01`), where prior arrangements were mapped to habitat (Fig 11 :73) and import source (Fig 6 :23). The verdict was unchanged. Corroboration here is from the figures, not from whichever literature happened to be cited first.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about institutions. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For INS-0002 the canonical shape is: **a two-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, a threshold form, and a valve that changes sign.** It is not a list of institutional ingredients, not the name of a prior arrangement, not a cross-country coefficient, and not a ranking of constitutions. The names that appear in the institutional literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
