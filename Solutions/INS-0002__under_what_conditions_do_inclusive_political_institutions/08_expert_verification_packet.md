# Expert verification packet - the Tether on inclusive political institutions

**What this is:** a claim in a form a scholar of institutions can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** No institutional measurement is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Inclusive political institutions are not a stock a polity owns; they are a maintained relation with two conditions that must both hold. C1: the channel to the constraint record stays reachable, so the rule still binds the people who wrote it. C2: reach is renewed by contact with a closure that differs, here the economic and social arrangements named in the question. A failure of C1 shows up as saturated metrics and pooled readings; a failure of C2 shows up as a closure handing itself the same room. Emergence has a threshold shape rather than accumulating smoothly, and an institution that once constrained can change sign when the underlying problem changes. Dependence on prior arrangements is therefore structurally required but not monocausal, and which particular arrangement supplies the atom is a declared gap. What is offered is the shape any candidate answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 11, 10 and 9

- **Statement:** capability is polity-and-habitat, not polity alone; quick closure is a channel failure, not a decay of the ground; the question is a union of two conditions with different instruments.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 - *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 10 :24 - *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :26 - *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 9 :57 - *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **Refuter:** a polity whose inclusive constraint slots persist unchanged across a habitat substitution that removes the material and social base that sustained them; or a polity that loses effective constraint while bracket fidelity and ground pressure are independently measured as high and non-declining; or one intervention that restores both conditions in both failure types with no categorically different residue.

### C2 - institutions-facing; checkable by a scholar against the dependence literature

- **Statement:** renewal of reach requires an import from a closure whose composition differs; the particular economic or social arrangement that supplies it is not derivable from the political frame alone.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :23 - *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32 - *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- **What to check:** is the structural claim compatible with the case record? Acemoglu and Robinson (2016) report that inclusive institutions emerge from a balanced increase in state capacity and distribution of power and stress informal institutions and social norms; Ostrom's Nobel summary reports that active participation in rule making is essential and that insider monitoring works better. Do those read as two conditions plus an import requirement, or as one mechanism with a measurable coefficient? Note that these are bindings from other frames, not figure outputs.
- **Refuter:** a polity that renews inclusive reach purely by composing inside its own political closure, with no new atom from a differing economic or social closure, and still passes the ground test across a change of habitat. Also: a source-discriminating instrument that prospectively and per slot names the arrangement that reopened a channel, with independent replication.

### C3 - framework-internal derivation with one live strain

- **Statement:** the transition has a threshold shape; the comparison bond `⋛` is the relevant operator, but its operand type at the institutional level is strained, and the threshold analogue passes a shadow test rather than moving the wall.
- **Source, verbatim:** Operators table :48 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. Fig 12 :119 - *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* Fig 8 :50 - *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*
- **What to check:** the low-overlap analogue is Calmettes and Weiss, Heliyon 3(11):e00451, 2017: *"egalitarianism emerges suddenly as the optimal power-sharing arrangement in a population of selfish individuals without any inherently altruistic qualities"*, at a critical parameter where *"any individual alpha can be toppled by an anti-dominance coalition of resentful lower-ranking individuals"*. Does that explain the gap it is imported for, namely why emergence is abrupt rather than accumulated? The strain: the Operators table restricts ⋛ to quantities, while the political operands are distributions of power. Whether a distribution counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument.
- **Refuter:** smooth and monotone emergence in the same measured quantity where a threshold was pre-registered; or a demonstration that distributions of power cannot count as quantities at that level under the figure's own test.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a cross-country coefficient or a variance share. The "how much" answer is a structural dependency and a two-condition conjunction.
- Not that informal norms, civil society, inequality, property regimes or colonial legacy are *the* cause. Those are bindings from other frames, provenance owed and ungrounded here.
- Not that inclusive institutions cause prosperity, and not that they are only a consequence of it. Both readings name a source from a single observation.
- Not a general law about all polities; no constraint-event measurement pays for the polity-level version.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by an institutional measurement.
- Not a claim that the analogue in C3 supplies the institutional fact. It corroborates a shape from a different evidence pool.
- Not a final account. There is no last room; the union keeps its own loop.

## 5. What would settle it

A constraint-event instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does a named institution actually bind its authors, measured as sanction events against powerful actors rather than as constitutional text? That closes C1.
2. Can a source-discriminating instrument identify, prospectively and per slot, which prior arrangement supplied the atom that reopened a channel? That closes part of the F9 gap.
3. Does the transition show a pre-registered critical threshold in the same measured quantity? That tests S3.
4. Standing falsifiers: habitat substitution with no change in constraint; one lever restoring both conditions with no residue; constraint lost while fidelity and ground pressure stay high; reach renewed with no import; invariant actor valence across a change of residual.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :39-40, Fig 10 :24-26, Fig 9 :57 and Operators :48 - four short passages.
2. Check C2 against the case record you know best: does a two-condition plus import shape fit better than a single-coefficient story? Name one case that would refute it.
3. Name one instrument that would distinguish a dead constraint channel from an unrenewed reach, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
