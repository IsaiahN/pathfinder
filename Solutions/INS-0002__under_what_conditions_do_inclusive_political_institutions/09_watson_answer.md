**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## QUESTION

Under what conditions do inclusive political institutions emerge, and how much do they depend on prior economic or social arrangements?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| stay small for decades in some constraint slots and grow quickly in others, and which atoms from economic and social frames must cross into the political closure for that to happen? R is indexed per institution and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: the constraint event record, who was actually bound, not constitutional text or expert agreement (Fig 2 :29).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** inclusive political institutions emerge and persist when both C1 (a maintained channel to a constraint-paying ground) and C2 (renewed reach through contact with economic and social closures that differ from the political one) hold; they close quickly when either fails; emergence has a threshold shape; and the institution that once constrained can change sign when the residual changes. Dependence on prior economic or social arrangements is real and structurally required as an import condition, but it is not monocausal and the particular arrangement is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`. The one genuine world conflict (modernization) was returned to the census and re-mapped to habitat; it did not flip the verdict. Low-overlap fields corroborate the mechanism shape, not the institutional fact.

- **cost if wrong:** misdirected diagnosis. A polity failing C2 would be sold monitoring instruments, or a polity failing C1 would be told to import more actors, and a single lever would be applied to a union that needs two. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded and largely reversible if the refuter is in front of the claim.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape, the strain S3 and the gap F9 are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64).
- **reversed by:** any of the pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) constraint slots unchanged after the sustaining habitat is removed, (2) constraint lost while bracket fidelity and ground pressure both measure high, (3) two decades of the same closure with new ground-verified readings and no import, (4) invariant actor valence across a change of residual, or (5) one intervention restoring both conditions with no categorically different residue. A source-discriminating instrument naming which arrangement reopened a channel, or a figure re-census that resolves S3's operand type, would also change the verdict. Prestige or agreement is not such an instrument.
