# The Tether's answer on inclusive political institutions

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for the conditions under which a constraint slot stays live for decades, and for how much of that outcome is owed to prior economic or social arrangements. In effect terms, the event record of constraint is the ground, and the gap between what a polity's institutions predict and what the record returns is R, indexed per slot and per time, never averaged into a country score (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The situation is a **union compound**, not one hard question: C1, the constraint channel is maintained, and C2, reach is renewed by contact with a closure that differs (Fig 9 :54-57). It has its own loop and its own ground.

## 2. The shape

1. **Inclusive institutions are not a stock owned by a polity.** Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* So the answer cannot be a list of political attributes considered alone.
2. **Two conditions must both hold, and they have different instruments.** C1 keeps the channel to the constraint record reachable: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"* (Fig 10 :24), with the named decay modes *"saturated metrics, pooled readings"* (Fig 10 :26). C2 renews reach from a closure that differs: Fig 6 :23, *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* A polity failing only C1 and a polity failing only C2 are not degrees of one failure (Fig 9 :57).
3. **Persistence is a chain of handoffs, not a settled state.** Fig 7 :37-39: progress per handoff needs both *"the bracket's fidelity across the handoff"* and *"the ground's pressure on the room"*; zero either and the chain runs in place. Fig 7 :36: *"A chain of mirrors is one long room. The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*
4. **The transition has a threshold shape, not a stock shape.** The comparison bond `⋛` is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table :48). Applied here it makes inclusion a relation between concentrated power and the power that can constrain it, reached at a critical condition rather than accumulated as constitutional text. The operand type is an open strain, recorded as S3, not smoothed over.
5. **The institution that helped can change sign.** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"* and *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A court, a franchise or a mobilized group is a catalyst only while it regenerates across uses.
6. **Dependence on prior arrangements is structural but not monocausal.** Idempotence is definitional: Fig 8 :32, *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."* So an atom from a differing economic or social closure is required for renewal. But which particular arrangement supplies it is not derivable here. That is a declared gap, F9, with Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a cross-country coefficient. "How much" is answered as a structural dependency and a two-condition conjunction, not as a percentage of variance.
- Not that any named prior arrangement, informal norm, civil society, inequality or property regime, is *the* cause. Those are bindings from other frames, provenance owed (Fig 8 :51).
- Not that inclusive institutions cause prosperity, and not that they are merely a consequence of it. Both are the same metamerism error from opposite sides: a reading named as a source without a discriminator (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a general law about all polities. The claim is `specified` and waits for a constraint-event measurement.
- Not a final account. There is no last room; the union keeps its own loop and its own ground.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A constraint-event instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case. Here the corresponding questions are:

- Does a named institution actually bind the people who wrote it, measured as sanction events against powerful actors rather than as constitutional text? That is the C1 instrument.
- Can a source-discriminating instrument identify which prior arrangement supplied the atom that reopened a constraint channel, prospectively and per slot? That would close part of F9.
- Does the transition show a critical threshold in the same measured quantity, with the threshold pre-registered, or is it smooth and monotone? That tests the ⋛ binding (S3).
- Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4: habitat substitution that leaves constraint unchanged; one intervention restoring both conditions with no residue; constraint lost while fidelity and ground pressure stay high; reach renewed with no import from a differing closure; invariant valence across a change of residual.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** A cross-country index can rank institutions, but the framework's own rule is that a frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27), and filters must not become witnesses (Fig 9 :60). The stop is recorded with one live strain (S3) and one declared gap (F9). *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* (Fig 13 :134) and *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* (Fig 8 :39). What would settle it is an instrument with real ground contact in a closure that differs from this derivation.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name which prior arrangement makes a society inclusive, and it does not claim a final theory of institutional origins. It says that inclusive institutions are not a stock a polity owns but a maintained relation with two conditions: the channel to the constraint record must stay reachable, and reach must be renewed by contact with economic and social closures that differ from the political one. The first fails through stale percepts and pooled readings; the second fails when a frame keeps handing itself the same room. Emergence has a threshold shape rather than a stock shape, and a court or coalition that once constrained can change sign when the underlying problem changes. Dependence on prior arrangements is therefore real and structurally required, but the particular arrangement is a declared gap, not a hidden answer. What the package offers is the shape any candidate answer must have, the falsifiers written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would close it. One strain and one gap remain visible.
