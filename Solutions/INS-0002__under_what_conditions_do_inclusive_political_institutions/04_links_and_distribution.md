## What the wider literature bears on the question

1. **Two-condition shape, not one lever.** The 2016 "Paths" paper and the 2005 book description both put state capacity and broad distribution of power together, and the 2016 paper warns that the two *"may not necessarily be compatible with each other"*. That is the union claim in the institutions literature's own vocabulary. It is one evidence pool and therefore one witness (Fig 2 :40).
2. **The channel and the insider.** Ostrom's Nobel page supplies a genuinely different pool on the same mechanism: rules must be created and enforced by participants, outside-imposed rules have less legitimacy, and insider monitoring works better. That corroborates the Fig 10 :24 channel reading without sharing the institutional-economics pool.
3. **Voice and dissent as a measurable instrument.** Edmondson's team-scale result and the HBS practitioner summary make "can bad news travel" measurable. That is the vocabulary/objective link of Fig 3 :27 made checkable at a different stratum. It is a psychological-safety measure, not a national institution measure, so it is mechanism-shape corroboration only, with the anchor re-named per Fig 4 :20.
4. **Coalitions and thresholds from animal behaviour and computational social science.** The Tsimane coalition study and the egalitarian phase-transition model both show constraint emerging from coalitions at a critical condition, with no shared pool with institutional economics. They corroborate the shape of F5 and F6, not the institutional fact.
5. **A genuine conflict.** The modernization account receives a direct challenge in the 2025 survey. Per the figures, the conflict went back to the census (re-census R2 in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`), where prior economic arrangements were mapped to habitat and import source rather than to the settling ground. The verdict was not flipped to agree with either side.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the refuter and the shape claim; offer the links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* A recipient who has absorbed the entire framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :28** - *"any frame you pick will seem to fit"* (the line begins *"describe it before you go looking, or"*). Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (the full line begins *"disqualifying for a verdict:"*). Later agreement from a recipient is never pooled as corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* An institutional question with high consequences for policy deserves a real outside check, not a sympathetic audience.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what instrument would discriminate C1 from C2; (3) record their verdict on stage 1; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"* - and Fig 2 :45 - *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their verdict on stage 1 must be recorded before any theory is shown (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted.** Fig 4 :22 applies: the formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer.
4. **Stage 3 still needs a ground per problem.** `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question. Each user must write what would refute their result before running it, and label the mode of what they get.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. As currently derived it is a shape with one live strain (S3, the operand type of ⋛ at the institutional level) and one declared gap (F9, which prior arrangement supplies the atom). State both openly. Do not present the shape as settled.
