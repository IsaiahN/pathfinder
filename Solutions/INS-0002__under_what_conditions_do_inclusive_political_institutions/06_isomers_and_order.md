Two questions are owed: (1) are two such routes **isomers** under Fig 12's definition, or merely members of a functional group, and (2) what dependency order and what analogue licences the threshold reading without importing a name from another field?

## DERIVATION

### Q1 — Are two routes to inclusion isomers?

- Fig 12 :117: **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**
- Fig 12 :124: **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**
- Route A, written as *state capacity + distribution of power*, and route B, written as *civil society + informal norms*, do not share an identical atom set as named by their own literatures. As the literatures write them, they are **not isomers**; they are two members of a pattern that fixes the outcome, that is, C1 and C2 together, with different members in the slots.
- The stronger version: if both routes are written over the full shared habitat atom set `{property regime, inequality, civil society, informal norms, enforcement instrument, record}`, differing only in which atom mediates which state pair, then they **are** isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the claim worth testing.
- **Falsifier, written before the run:** if either route can be written without an atom the other requires, for example a durable constraint channel with no civil society and no informal norm, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands instead. Triangulated for the definition (Fig 12), single-source and unmeasured for the institutional application.

### Q2 — What is the order of the union?

- Fig 3 :20: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."**
- Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**
- The dependency order is perception of the distribution of power → vocabulary for what a political situation affords → objective (what counts as constraint on the powerful) → planning (coalition and reform) → learn and carry (settlement and transmission). Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*
- The middle link is the one without an instrument. Edmondson's team-scale safety measure and the Ostrom participation findings are candidate instruments at other strata; under Fig 4 :20 the kernel transfers while the anchor must be re-named at the new scale. So they are instruments for the mechanism shape, not for the national institution fact.
- **Falsifier:** a documented case where perception, vocabulary and objective all measure as working and the failure is located at planning. That would falsify the "usually at the middle" reading, though not the dependency ordering itself.

### Q3 — What licences the threshold and phase-transition analogue?

- Operators table :48 defines `⋛` as *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*. Fig 12 :87 gives the identifying test: *"is it about which is larger, rather than both being present? Yes."*
- The comparison bond is already in Γ. Applying it to "inclusive" is a binding onto political quantities, not a new primitive. The operand type remains strained (S3): the Operators table speaks of quantities, while the political operands are distributions of power. Whether a distribution counts as a quantity at that level needs an instrument, not an argument.
- The low-overlap analogue comes from Calmettes and Weiss (Heliyon, 2017): *"egalitarianism emerges suddenly as the optimal power-sharing arrangement in a population of selfish individuals without any inherently altruistic qualities"*, with a critical parameter at which *"any individual alpha can be toppled by an anti-dominance coalition of resentful lower-ranking individuals"*. Before using it, the shadow test is applied: does it explain a gap in the political derivation? Yes, the gap is *why a transition is abrupt rather than accumulated*, which the two-condition conjunction alone does not supply.
- Source gate: author, venue, year and a verbatim quote are recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`; the evidence pool is computational social science and statistical physics, with no overlap with institutional economics. Under Fig 8 :50, *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence,"* this is convergent derivation of a shape, not an adopted import that moves the political wall.
- **Falsifier:** an institutional emergence that is smooth and monotone in the same measured quantity where a threshold was pre-registered. That would break the ⋛ binding and send the analogue back to the census.

### Q4 — Are the two conditions a partition or a union, and what follows?

- Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."*
- C1 (channel maintained) and C2 (reach renewed by contact with a differing closure) overlap inside the polity, so this is a union. A case failing both is not a harder case of either; it is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :57).
- Fig 9 :55 predicts that a single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersection standing. Concretely: writing a better constitution can clear a C1-only failure and leave a C2-only failure untouched; importing a new social actor can clear a C2-only failure and leave a dead constraint channel untouched.
- **Falsifier:** one intervention that restores both conditions across both failure types and leaves no categorically different residue. That would collapse the union into a partition.

## CONCLUSION

The two routes are a **functional group** as their literatures write them and a real **isomer claim** only when both are expressed over the full shared habitat atom set. The dependency order runs through the vocabulary and objective link, which is the one without an instrument. The threshold shape is licensed by a bond already in Γ plus a convergent, low-overlap analogue that passes the shadow test; its operand type is the one live strain. The union must be unbundled before any lever is chosen. This rescues what is defensible in the institutional record without pretending the framework named the arrangements: no seventh primitive, no import that moves the wall, and no claim that the analogue supplies the institutional fact.
