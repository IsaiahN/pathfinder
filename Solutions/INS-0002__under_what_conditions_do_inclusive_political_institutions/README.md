## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *label* "inclusive" but the *duration for which a constraint slot stays live*. In effect terms (Formula step 1; Symbols table, `R`): a society's frame Γ predicts how a political contest Γ(b,a) will go. The ground o′ is what actually happens, that is, whether powerful actors are in fact constrained, whether violations are punished, and whether power turns over. R = |Γ(b,a) − o′| is measured per institutional slot s, never averaged into a national "inclusiveness score" (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22).

- Option A, "inclusive institutions are a stock a polity owns": leads to the prediction that emergence is a property of the political frame considered alone, and that prior economic or social arrangements are merely background.
- Option B, "inclusive institutions are a maintained relation": leads to the prediction that emergence is a property of polity-and-habitat, that the constraint channel must be kept reachable, and that reach must be renewed by contact with closures that differ, here the economic and social frames named in the question (Fig 11 :39, Fig 11 :40, Fig 6 :23).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a conjunction of conditions that keeps a constraint slot live, a statement of which imports from economic or social frames are structurally required, and an instrument that would discriminate a failure of one condition from a failure of the other. Until bound, that shape predicts nothing and no loop check can see it. It is now bound, so the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 can fire on it.

The second half of the question, "how much do they depend on prior economic or social arrangements", is in effect a closure question: can the political frame mint inclusiveness from its own atoms, or must it import something from a frame whose closure differs? Fig 6 :21 and Fig 8 :32 govern that, and the answer is a shape and a dependency claim, not a name of a particular arrangement.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the institutional question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of institutional emergence.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
