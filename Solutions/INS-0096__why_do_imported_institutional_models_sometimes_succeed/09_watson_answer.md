## QUESTION

Why do imported institutional models sometimes succeed and sometimes fail in different political and social settings? In effect terms: why does R_T = |Gamma_T(b_T, a) - o'_T| go near zero in some target slots and stay large in others when the written model is unchanged? Option A: success and failure are noise around a portable design quality, so one score predicts the outcome. Option B: the transfer is a union of conditions with different anchors, the effect belongs to model-and-habitat, and verdicts are per slot. What each leads to: A averages away the live slot signal (Fig 1 :19, :22) and hands a filter a verdict (Fig 9 :60); B produces a four-condition separator, five refuters and a declared gap.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor. Partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: at least four place (model, target slot, enactment action, observation), indexed per slot, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19). Symmetry: the written form is exchangeable, the outcome is not; the symmetry is broken by habitat, weighting, anchor and channel, which is the isomer condition (Fig 12 :117). Scale: step, cycle, level, population and lineage, with a lossy membrane between them (Fig 4 :27).
- **D1, the transferred text.** Actors: clauses, statutes, charts. Currency: legality, enforceability, description length, legibility. Anchor: the written text, installed so it can be violated (Fig 10 :19). Status: a convention, not a ground.
- **D2, the import seam.** Actors: translators, reformers, training, carriers, best-practice guides. Currency: what crosses the membrane, method or recording. Anchor: the source ground and the provenance record (Fig 8 :51-52).
- **D3, the target habitat.** Actors: citizens, officials, informal norms, networks, interests, conditions. Currency: contact, valence, cascade. Anchor: the realized outcome at the slot.
- **D4, inherited belief in the target.** Actors: priors, private history, framing, pretext. Currency: the weighting w. Anchor: the outcome, read separately per target.
- **D5, channel and seat.** Actors: inspectors, courts, metrics, refusal seats. Currency: contact fidelity. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 10 :24).
- **D6, comparison frame.** Actors: comparativists, rooms, journals, reviewers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement.
- **Absorbed.** Design quality alone has no nameable anchor independent of contact; culture and political will are inside D3, D4 and D5. They are not separate domains yet.

**Union, not partition.** D1 and D2 meet at enactment; D2 and D3 are the transplant compound; D4 runs through both; D5 crosses all. Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*. One lever leaves the intersections standing. Order by dependency (Fig 3 :20): describe the residual before choosing a frame; source gate and shadow test before import; refuse before mint; settle before compose.

**Strain test.** Fig 11 :40 governs an improvement to the agent in isolation; rewriting a rule so it changes who can be heard by the ground changes contact. Different objects, so no strain after re-census. Recorded in `01` section 1.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; any single-factor verdict does not. An imported model is a convention whose effect belongs to model-and-habitat. What crosses must be a method or head start carrying the arrangement, not a recording of one occasion; the target substitutes a habitat and inherits actors it did not choose; identical architecture diverges through history and weighting; and a success metric that updates on the institution's own report is drift, not success. Same atoms under different bonds are isomers, so a copied text can be a different substance. The import needs provenance and a shadow test and is debited against independence. The universal recipe and success percentage are a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind, reported as unreachable rather than silence. This is a `specified`, ungrounded, candidate result; the five pre-registered refuters are the route to reversing it. Verdicts per domain are kept separate and are never averaged.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a single-factor story instead of a generator, which Fig 2 prices in "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the separator and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than because anything was struck out (Fig 6). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument in a target, a matched pair that still diverges or that ignores a habitat substitution, an independently measured outcome that keeps tracking after the reporting channel is saturated, any of the five pre-registered refuters, or a figure re-census that supplies a transfer recipe, would move the verdict. Until one fires, it stands as candidate.
