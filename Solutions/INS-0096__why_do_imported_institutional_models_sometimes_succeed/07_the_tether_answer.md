# The Tether's answer: why imported institutional models succeed or fail

**Mode: `specified` on the four conditions and their verdicts, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on a universal recipe or success percentage, not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A written model M is the same in source and target; the residual R_T = |Gamma_T(b_T, a) - o'_T| differs persistently per target slot. The question is a **union** of overlapping conditions, not one problem with opposite answers (Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"*). It has its own loop and its own ground. Verdicts are per slot and are never averaged into a success rate.

## 2. The shape: a four-condition separator

1. **What crossed the border (D2).** The transfer carries a generator or a recording. Fig 4 :23 *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* The written clauses are ingredients; the bonds (who can refuse whom, what evidence reaches a decision) are *"the half nothing was recording"* (Fig 12 :5). Where the arrangement is not recorded, *"the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again"* (Fig 12 :107-109).
2. **The habitat it landed in (D3).** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"*. Moving a model substitutes a habitat: *"you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose"* (Fig 11 :67), with two silent failures, what you failed to reproduce and what you brought with you (Fig 11 :68-69). Valence is relative to the target residual (Fig 11 :49-51), so no standing list of good institutions survives the move.
3. **The history and weighting of the target (D4).** Symbols table :25-26 *"Two frames with identical architecture diverge because their histories and their w diverge."* Identical text plus different prior and private history gives different outcomes. This is the structural reason parallel adoption is not parallel outcome.
4. **The channel to a non-updating ground (D5).** Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. A success reported by the body it grades is a mirror, not an anchor (Fig 2 :35 *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."*). A transfer also needs a seat able to refuse the convention when the ground turns (Operators :58).

**Across all four:** the import needs provenance and a shadow test (Fig 8 :51-52), is debited against independence (Fig 6 :24), and must be sought residual-first (Fig 8 :53). Same atoms under different bonds are isomers (Fig 12 :117); the choice between the isomer and functional-group readings is empirical and has a stated falsifier in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

**The chain usually breaks at the objective link** (Fig 3 :25-27): reformers can measure perception and planning, but rarely have an instrument for what would count as progress in the target's own terms. Effort above the break cannot rescue it (Fig 3 :19).

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a list of institutions that transfer well. A standing list of good actors does not survive a change of residual (Fig 11 :51).
- Not a universal recipe, reform sequence, or success percentage. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134), reached after a re-census, not a silence and not an invitation to invent a number.
- Not "culture is the cause" or "political will is the cause." Those are absorbed into D4 and D5, not separate domains with their own anchors.
- Not a single-factor verdict. A success rate is a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
- Not grounded. No source-discriminating comparison of two targets was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a green reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding checks are:

- **Source discriminator:** an instrument that separates method-crossing, habitat reproduction, weighting and measurement in a target's success reading (F9 of `01`).
- **Matched pair:** two targets matched on arrangement, habitat contact, history and channel that still diverge, or one that produces identical outcomes across a habitat substitution.
- **Anchor test:** outcomes that keep tracking an independently measured ground after the institution's own reporting channel is saturated (Fig 10 :24-26).
- **Standing refuters:** the five conditions in `01` section 4 and `08` section 3. Any one fires and the corresponding finding is withdrawn.
- **Capacity test:** a transplant into a real habitat, not a pilot enclave, because *"A test harness is a substituted habitat"* (Fig 11 :70).

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The four conditions are a shape, not a recipe. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"* (Fig 13 :134), and the framework *"cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it"* (Fig 13 :135). The six-term substrate and these figure quantities are visible guesses, not nature's own list; declaring them makes the choice visible, not neutral (They're Scanning Books). The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say imported institutions fail because of culture, or succeed because of good design. It says the question is a union of four conditions with different anchors. First, what crossed the border: a reapplicable method or head start, or a recording of one occasion, where the written rules are only the ingredient list and the arrangement rarely travels. Second, the habitat: capability belongs to the model and its setting together, and moving a model substitutes a habitat with actors nobody chose and two silent failures. Third, the target's history and weighting: identical text read through different priors gives different outcomes. Fourth, the channel: a success reported by the body it grades is a mirror, not an anchor. The same atoms under different bonds are isomers, so a copied text can be a different substance. No figure returns a success percentage or a universal recipe; that is a declared gap. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The verdict stays `candidate` until the ground settles it.
