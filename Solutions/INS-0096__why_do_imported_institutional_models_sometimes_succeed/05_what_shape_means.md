## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For INS-0096 a shape is therefore the narrowed form of the answer we can state before we can name the institution or the recipe. The shape is the **four-condition separator** (what crossed, habitat, history and weighting, channel to ground). A specific verdict such as "judicial independence causes growth" or "the Nordic model transfers best" is a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on malformed questions: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually"*. Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* The transfer question is such a bundle.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For institutional transfer that means a source-discriminating comparison in a target, not a cross-country correlation.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. "This worked in Sweden and the mechanism was X, measured by an outcome that did not move when the program reported its own success" is a provenanced import; "Sweden is a best practice" is not.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Naming a transferred institution as *the* condition turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. Why a transfer success story is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*. One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered all give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*, and *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A report that "model M succeeded in S and failed in T" is exactly such a projection. The reading does not say whether what crossed was a method or a recording (Fig 4 :22-26), whether the habitat reproduced the needed contact (Fig 11 :66-69), whether the target's weighting differed (Symbols :25-26), or whether the success metric was a mirror (Fig 10 :24). Naming the cause from the reading as "the design" or "the culture" is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a narrative.

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* A framework that declares its own separator correct is a frame certifying itself (Fig 8 :37).

## 5. Q: can the framework name which institution will transfer? ANSWERED, no further question needed

**What licenses a particular institution as the transferable cause?** Nothing in the figures. The figure side returns a shape (the four conditions) and a refuter. A particular institution is licensed only by a source-discriminating ground reading or a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the comparator's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does naming an already-available condition violate "shape, not a name"?** No, and this corrects an earlier overstatement. The README's phrase governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Habitat, channel, weighting and arrangement are already-available bindings from the comparativist's frame onto general figure quantities (contact, ground, w, bonds). Naming them is provenance, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is pretending the figure returned the name of a specific institution, or treating a single success story as an atom that moves the wall without a source gate.

Both readings are kept visible: the limit on missing primitives and the permission to name held bindings with provenance.

## 6. Transfer is a tacit-knowledge problem as much as a rules problem

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* is the governing essay for this question: *"Writing carries the context well and the subtext partly, but it almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part."* An institutional model on paper is a compressed message. The history that made it work, the corrections that shaped it, and the local conditions it read against are the pretext, and they do not travel with the text. *"The understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."*

Fig 12 :107-109 says the same thing in the loop's vocabulary: *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."* A target handed only the written model must re-pay the derivation that the source already paid. Where the target is handed a head start and a contact channel instead, the search is cheaper. This is the mechanism behind Fig 4 :22-24, and it is why a transfer can succeed without a perfect blueprint and fail with one.

The essay's drift warning also applies: two offices that only check against each other drift together, so neither notices, which is Fig 2 :33-34 (*"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices"*). The essay states the trade-off plainly: *"High contact builds exactly the shared understanding that makes communication work, but that same factor makes the learner's agreement with the teacher worthless as a check."* A reform team embedded entirely in the source's literature is a mirror, not an outside frame.

## 7. Prestige is not action

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*

A model imported because a famous jurisdiction adopted it has a budget to be tried and no more. The name of the source cannot settle whether it worked here. *Dual inheritance theory* corroborates the mechanism from a different field: model-based biases include prestige bias, success bias and similarity bias, so what gets copied is biased toward who is copied, not toward what the ground paid for. One low-overlap witness, secondary to the figures.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra."* So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any transfer claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
