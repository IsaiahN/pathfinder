# Outreach draft: check the separator before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the figure quotes in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been checked against the figures yourself. The offer collapses on contact if a quote is wrong.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about why imported institutions work in some places and not others

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about institutional transfer. I am not asking you to read a theory first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the question "why do imported institutional models succeed in some settings and fail in others" is not one question with a positive and a negative answer. It is a union of at least four conditions with different anchors, and the effect is per target slot, not a country average. The four conditions, in effect terms:
>
> 1. **What crossed the border.** A reapplicable method or head start, or a recording of one occasion from the source. The written clauses are the ingredient list; who can refuse whom, and what evidence reaches a decision, are the arrangement that usually does not travel.
> 2. **The habitat it landed in.** The model and the target habitat are one object. An institution that works where its ground is reachable can fail where the same text is dropped into different actors, informal norms and conditions. Moving a model substitutes a habitat; you inherit actors nobody chose, and you may fail to reproduce what the model needed.
> 3. **The history and weighting of the target.** Two settings that receive the identical text still diverge, because each reads it through a different prior and a different private history.
> 4. **The channel to a non-updating ground.** A reported "success" is evidence only if the reference does not move when the institution's own report moves it. If the compliance metric is produced by the body it grades, the agreement is a mirror, not a check.
>
> **What would refute this, written before I wrote the output:**
> 1. two targets matched on arrangement, habitat contact, history and channel still diverge persistently;
> 2. the same model produces identical outcomes across a habitat substitution that changes actors, conditions and valence;
> 3. one independently measured score predicts all per-slot outcomes with no residual left after these four conditions;
> 4. an instrument shows the recorded arrangement, not contact or measurement, produced the success reading.
>
> If any one holds, I withdraw the separator. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any particular institution is good in general, that a specific reform sequence works everywhere, that culture or political will is the single cause, or that a success rate is a verdict. No figure line returns a universal transfer recipe or a success percentage. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and here the shape is the separator.
>
> **What I would value:** what you would count as a failure of this separator, and whether the four conditions are the right cut. Record that verdict before looking at any theory, because once you hold the framework's interior your agreement stops being a check.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the figures and the loop are below. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Canonical figures README (public repository): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Figures and formula for direct download (branch `general-agent`): https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - The worked architecture article: https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** run it on a transfer case you know well and can check against an outcome that does not update on your report. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name / role]
