# Expert verification packet: the Tether on imported institutional models

**What this is:** a claim in a form a comparativist or institutional scholar can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the conditions, `general` on the structural shape, `gap` on the recipe, not `grounded`.** No transfer case was measured. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status of this packet:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "why do imported institutional models succeed in some settings and fail in others" is a union of at least four conditions, not one question with opposite answers and not a single factor with a sign. The residual is per target slot, never a country average. The four conditions: (1) what crossed the border, a method or head start versus a recording of one occasion, with the written rules as an ingredient list and the arrangement as the part that usually does not travel; (2) the target habitat, because capability belongs to the model and habitat together and moving a model substitutes a habitat with unchosen actors and two silent failures; (3) the target's history and weighting, because identical architecture diverges through different priors and private histories; (4) the channel to a non-updating ground, because a success reported by the body it grades is a mirror, not an anchor. The import itself needs provenance and a shadow test and is debited against independence. No figure returns a universal recipe or a success percentage; that is a declared gap. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 4 and 12
- **Statement:** a written institutional model is an ingredient list. What must cross for success is a reapplicable method or head start carrying the arrangement, not a recording of one occasion. Where the arrangement is not recorded, the target re-pays the derivation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 4 :23 *"upward: a method that can be reapplied. Never the recording of a particular success."* Fig 4 :24 *"downward: something that makes the next search cheaper. Never a replay of what worked once."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :108-109 *"Where it is not, the result carries forward and the deriving does not, so every lineage pays again."*
- **What to check:** in a transfer you know, was the thing that crossed a method with its arrangement, or the text and the success story? Did the target have to re-derive what the source already knew?
- **Refuter:** a transfer where only the verbatim text and one-occasion story crossed, no bonds or head start were recorded, and the target still reproduced the source outcome on a non-updating ground in more than one slot.

### C2 - field-facing; checkable by a comparativist against transfer cases
- **Statement:** capability is a property of the model and its habitat together; moving the model substitutes a habitat, inherits unchosen actors, and has two silent failures. Valence is relative to the residual, so no standing list of good institutions survives the move.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :67 *"Move the residual and you must reproduce everything its goal required, and you inherit the new habitat's actors, which you did not choose."* Fig 11 :68 *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*; Fig 11 :69 *"what you unintentionally introduced is invisible until it acts"*. Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*
- **What to check:** in a transfer you know, was the unit of success the institution alone or the institution in its setting? Which conditions required by the source goal were not reproduced, and which unchosen actors acted?
- **Refuter:** identical outcomes across a habitat substitution that changes actors, conditions, relations and valence, with the same recorded arrangement and channel.

### C3 - framework-internal plus identification; checkable against a measurement design
- **Statement:** a success reading does not determine its source. The reading must be ranged against an anchor that does not update on the institution's own report, and the import needs provenance and a shadow test. A per-slot residual cannot be replaced by a success rate.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :128 *"Nothing about a composed term tells you, from looking at it, which route produced it or whether that route was available."* Fig 10 :24 *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 2 :29 *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"*. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
- **What to check:** does the "success" metric move when the institution reports its own success? Is there an independently measured outcome? Is the source's success story provenanced, with a gap it explains in the target?
- **Refuter:** an instrument that discriminates among the sources of a success reading and shows the recorded arrangement, not contact or measurement, produced it; or one independently measured score that predicts all per-slot outcomes with no residual left.

## 3. Full pre-registered refuter set

Written before the conclusion, repeated from `01` section 4. Any one firing withdraws the corresponding finding:

1. Two targets matched on arrangement, habitat contact, history and channel still diverge persistently per slot.
2. The same model produces identical outcomes across a habitat substitution that changes actors, conditions and valence.
3. A written rule constrains outcomes with no interpretation channel and no reachable ground.
4. One source-independent score predicts all per-slot outcomes with no residual left after the four conditions.
5. A source-discriminating instrument shows the recorded arrangement, not contact or measurement, produced the success reading.

## 4. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a list of institutions that transfer well; Fig 11 :51 refuses a standing list of good actors.
- Not a universal reform sequence, success percentage or single cause. That is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134).
- Not that culture, geography or political will is a separate domain with its own anchor; they are absorbed into the habitat and weighting domains.
- Not that a cross-country success rate is a verdict; it is a budget filter (Fig 9 :60).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by a transfer measurement.
- Not a claim that the six-term substrate is nature's own list. It is a visible guess; naming the four conditions is a binding from the comparator's frame, provenanced as such, not an invented primitive.
