## Short answer

The question is a union of three conditions, not one dial. A board keeps a channel to a ground that does not update on management's own report (Fig 2 :29, :33-34); keeps reach renewed by contact with closures that differ, with provenance and a shadow test (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :52); and keeps a seat able to refuse a held claim (Fig 10 :82, Operators :58). Checklists and risk scores are filters for the budget and witnesses only when they are paid by the ground; they are not the verdict (Fig 5 :33, Fig 9 :60). The residual is indexed per deployment slot, not averaged into one enterprise number (Fig 1 :19, :22). No figure line names the committee, the checklist items or the thresholds. That is a declared `gap`, and `unreachable` is a verdict, not a silence (Fig 13 :134).

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by likelihood of agreement.
- No bulk-send plan, no mail merge, no forwarding chain.
- No claim that this package is a validated governance framework, a legal opinion, or a substitute for a board's own judgment.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus a practical one:

1. **Agreement is not an anchor.** Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation (Seat Map :163: "High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention").
2. **The verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Seat 4 sees the residual and the shape claim only.
3. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Seat Map :283: "A room does not upgrade the label... Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."
4. **Legally**, unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what would refute the shape claim, record the answer before showing any theory, and never pool later agreement as corroboration. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
