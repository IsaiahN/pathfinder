## The two-stage offer

- **Stage 1 before stage 2** is Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 ("Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."). The recipient checks the output without holding the framework's interior.
- **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded before any theory is shown and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
