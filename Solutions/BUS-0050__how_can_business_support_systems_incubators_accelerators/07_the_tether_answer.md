# The Tether's answer on startup support and additionality

**Mode: `specified` - not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading. No per-startup market measurement pays for any claim here.

## 1. The residual it answers

An answer would explain why the gap between what a startup's routines predict customers will pay and what customers actually pay moves toward zero after some support inputs and not after others, and how much of that movement was already underway before the support arrived. R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is indexed by startup and by time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19, :22). The question is about **causal contact**, not about the level of performance and not about correlation.

The union must be unbundled before it is searched (Fig 9 :54). It is three conditions, not one hard question:

- **C1:** the support changes the startup's channel to a ground that still pays.
- **C2:** the support moves the startup's reach outward through contact with a closure that differs from its own.
- **C3:** the cohort and the comparison pool are unauthored, and the outcome is read per startup.

## 2. The shape

1. **"Would succeed anyway" is a no-support bin, not a smaller win.** Fig 5 :29: *"nothing new here"*; Fig 5 :30: *"the machinery worked; the answer was already known."* The three mint guards are a product; with no live positive mass on the slot, the support input cannot mint. Deadweight is the policy name for this. It is a budget fact, not a verdict about a startup.
2. **Support helps only by changing contact.** Fig 11 :39: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* A package of cash and office space is an agent-only improvement. The Start-Up Chile regression discontinuity found no effect of cash and coworking alone, but large effects of entrepreneurship schooling.
3. **Reach renewal needs an import from a differing closure.** Fig 6 :14: *"the only operator that moves the wall"*; Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* Fig 8 :32: *"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom."* Mentors, customers, adjacent technologies and peers are not decorations around the core; they are the only operators that move the wall.
4. **Selection is the failure mode.** Fig 10 :75: *"A conscientious selection is still a selection, so unauthored needs a mechanism:"* a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a set assembled before the question existed, or the enumerated habitat. Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* An accelerator that selects its own cohort on signs of likely success manufactures the correlation it later cites. Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*
5. **The market, not the programme, is the anchor.** Fig 2 :29 names *"a market"* as an anchor; Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 10 :93: *"A ground with a reputation to protect is a ground someone benefits from, and its keeper is thereby disqualified."* Pitch scores, demo-day attendance and alumni valuations are instruments the frame produces (Fig 1 :27).
6. **The break is usually at the middle link.** Fig 3 :24 and :27: the vocabulary/objective link has no instrument, and a link with no instrument is a link nobody has looked at. Founders *"systematically satisfice prematurely"*, and useful consultation *"reignit[es] search for problems that they already view as solved"*. Funding a link above the break cannot rescue one below it.
7. **The same input changes valence with the residual.** Fig 11 :49: *"An actor’s valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."* A mentor who regenerates is a catalyst; the same advice replayed after the market changes is a spent part.
8. **The per-startup counterfactual is unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*; Fig 6 :27: *"some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."* Imbens and Rubin: *"at most one of the potential outcomes can be realized and thus observed."* The population average is an estimable substitute, but Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not `grounded`. No per-startup market measurement has paid for this shape.
- Not a name. It does not say which accelerator, mentor, funding instrument or startup will work. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*
- Not an average as verdict. It makes no claim that a programme-level effect size settles any individual slot. Such numbers are budget filters (Fig 9 :60).
- Not a claim that money, founders, culture or networks are irrelevant. They are Γ and habitat; the claim is that Γ does not settle additionality on its own (Fig 11 :40).
- Not "selection is the whole answer". It is a union of three conditions with three instruments (Fig 9 :57).
- Not corroboration by agreement. The entrepreneurship-support studies share a topical evidence pool (Fig 2 :31, :40); the low-overlap sources from causal inference and ecology corroborate the method and the mechanism, not the startup-level fact.
- Not a theory of everything about business. It stops where the instrument stops.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The source essay's metamerism rule: a reading of a funding round *"cannot comment on the source"*, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists"*. Here the readings are revenue, retention and survival; the candidate sources are a protected capability, a paying habitat, an accelerator signal, a temporary asymmetry or luck.

The falsifiers, pre-registered in `01` and repeated in `08`:

1. post-support R unchanged while pre-support R was already flat near zero and no import was recorded;
2. cash and space increased with no contact change, yet the market ground moved per startup across a habitat change;
3. a participant-authored cohort keeps its outcome advantage under an unauthored near-cutoff or lottery comparison;
4. an instrument returns both potential outcomes for the same startup at the same moment;
5. a programme-produced metric predicts per-startup payment prospectively without being fitted to the same outcomes;
6. perception, vocabulary and objective all measure as working and the failure is only at planning.

A prospective, per-startup study that builds the unauthored pool before the cohort is chosen, records bonds as well as atoms, and reports which of C1, C2 and C3 failed before the decline would turn this from `specified` toward `grounded`.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* The shape stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. The missing thing is a discriminator of sources at the startup slot, and it is owed. Naming a winner from a reading would be the metamerism error the source essay forbids. The conditions in section 2 are a shape; the fourth thing, which startup will actually benefit and why, is not held and is not pretended.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank accelerators or name the resource that wins. It says that support helps a startup only when it changes the startup's contact with a market that still pays, when it moves the startup's reach outward through contact with ways of working that differ from its own, and when the cohort and the comparison group are assembled before the outcome is known and read per startup. "Favoring businesses that would succeed anyway" is not a smaller success: it is the case where the support input had nothing to work on, because the startup was already heading toward what customers pay. Selecting on signs of likely success is the failure, because the selection manufactures the correlation the programme then cites as evidence. Cash alone is an atom, not an import; the Start-Up Chile design found no effect for cash and coworking alone but large effects for schooling. The market, not the programme's own metrics, is the anchor, and the break is usually at the vocabulary link, not the planning link. What is offered is that shape, six falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. Which startup wins stays open until a source-discriminating instrument exists, and the stop is recorded rather than filled.
