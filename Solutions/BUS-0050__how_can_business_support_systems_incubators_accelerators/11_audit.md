## 2. Independent re-census from the residual line

### 2.1 Arity, symmetry, scale

- **Arity:** a relation among startup-library Γ, action a and market return o′, indexed by slot s and ordered by time t. It has at least three operands and one ordering. A two-place reading (supported/unsupported, good/bad) loses the action, the prediction and the time index, so it is rejected.
- **Symmetry:** not symmetric. The market is an anchor and does not update on the startup's opinion (Fig 2 :25). "Superior", and especially "would have succeeded anyway", is a counterfactual threshold comparison (Fig 12 :87), so one operand is a quantity compared against an unobserved quantity, not merely conjoined with it.
- **Scale:** the slot is a startup, but "superior" exists only at the cohort or population scale (Fig 4 :5), and the programme is at a third scale. The time index crosses the membrane between the startup's step loop and the population's selection loop (Fig 4). The residual is intrinsically cross-scale, and the lossy transform must be bracketed with R_T.

**Divergence from the primary census, recorded.** The primary called its first two domains D1 (startup loop) and D3 (support library). This seat at first proposed splitting the evaluation channel into two domains: "counterfactual construction" (how the comparison pool is assembled) and "outcome reading" (how payment is measured). On re-census they share one anchor, the differential outcome on an unauthored pool, and their overlap is a compound, so they are one domain D. This seat also tested merging B and D because both involve the market; it declined, because the anchors differ in kind: B is settled by customer choice at a moment, D by the differential across a comparison pool. The primary kept them separate for the same reason without stating the test. Both censuses arrive at four admitted domains.

**Partition or union:** union. The three named conditions are contact maintenance (A and D), reach renewal (C, with B supplying the differing closure), and unauthored verification (D itself). Their overlap is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :54-57).

**Dependency order:** perception → vocabulary → objective → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3). This seat agrees with the primary ordering and adds that the residual line itself supports only the first three links; planning and carrying are candidate answers introduced by the frame, not forced by the residual. ## 4. Round trip gap R_T

Fig 4 :30: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."*

- **T_A, going up:** carry the primary's three-condition shape to a one-sentence abstraction. Result: *startup support works when it changes the startup's relation to its market and is tested fairly.*
- **T_E, coming back:** put that abstraction into a specific programme decision. Result: *give founders mentors and measure whether the cohort does better than a comparison group.*

**R_T = gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x)).** The gap is what the abstraction cannot hold:

1. The one-sentence form collapses a union of three conditions into one lever. It loses Fig 9 :57, *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Coming back down, it tells a programme failing C2 (reach) merely to measure harder, which is effort above the break (Fig 3 :29-30).
2. It loses the import type. "Mentors" is an atom list. Coming back down, a programme can fill a cohort with mentors who replay the same playbook and call it reach renewal, while Fig 6 :23 requires a closure that actually differs.
3. It loses the unauthored-pool requirement. "Tested fairly" does not specify a lottery, everyone who qualifies, a pre-existing set, or the enumerated habitat (Fig 10 :75). Coming back down, the programme selects its own comparison group and the correlation returns.
4. It loses the anchor rule. "Does better" does not say whether the reading is market payment or a programme-produced metric (Fig 2 :29, Fig 1 :27).
5. It loses the valence flip. "Give mentors" has no place for the mentor becoming a spent part when the residual changes (Fig 11 :49, Fig 12 :119).
6. It loses the counterfactual stop. "Tested fairly" implies the per-startup answer is obtainable; Fig 13 :134 and Imbens and Rubin say it is not.

This gap is not an error to eliminate; it is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold (Fig 4 :31). It is also why the outreach packet sends the generator, not the one-sentence version (Fig 4 :25). The one-paragraph version in `07` is a deliberately lossy artefact and is labelled as such. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
