## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical statement in `FIGURES_README_canonical.md` is that the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops where instruments can no longer distinguish and records the lineage. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From the essay *The Library of Babel*, read directly:

> *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for BUS-0050 a shape is: **the narrowed form of an answer about support that can be stated before any particular accelerator, mentor, funding instrument or winning startup is named.** It constrains. It does not fill.

- Shape: *support helps only when it changes contact with a paying ground, moves reach through a differing closure, and is evaluated on an unauthored pool read per startup.*
- Name, withheld: *this accelerator works because of its brand, its partners, its demo day, its seed cheque, or its "network effects".*

The distinction matters here because the support field is almost entirely in the naming business. Lenihan names *"backing or picking winners"*; the meta-analysis names program duration, cohort size and sponsorship; Hallen et al. name *"broad, intensive, and paced consultation"*. Those are bindings from the entrepreneurship frame onto atoms and bonds, not outputs of the loop.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

The essay gives the bootstrapping procedure:

> *"If you don’t know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."*
> *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*

Applied here: the closest available terms were "additionality", "deadweight", "selection bias", "sorting", "signaling", "sorting versus learning", and "causal identification". None was used as the answer. Each was treated as a witness to be ranged against the residual, per Fig 8 :53: *"Residual first, frame second."* The effect description (causal change in R per startup, discriminated from a pre-existing trend) came first; the frames were selected only after that description was fixed.

The essay also names the bundle stop:

> *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."*

That is the canonical warrant for treating BUS-0050 as a union of three conditions (Fig 9 :54-57), not as one hard question with a single lever.

The stop condition:

> *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better."*

In this solution the stop is reached when the three conditions are stated, an instrument for each is named, an unauthored pool mechanism is specified, and the falsifiers are written. Any further search that only renames the conditions is motion, not progress (Fig 7 :32, *"one long room: motion, no progress"*).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :50: *"But shared atoms are not the signature. Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."*

For BUS-0050 neither has settled the name. There is no per-startup measurement that pays for "this mentor caused the decade of growth", and the programme-pool names were adopted without a shadow test. Hence the `gap` row in F10 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. What exists in the world is a set of population-level designs (regression discontinuity, randomized accountability) that estimate average effects. By Imbens and Rubin, that is the statistical solution to a per-unit impossibility, and Fig 9 :60 classifies it as a budget filter.

## 4. The source error is the specific forbidden move here

From *The Hard Problem is About Source*, read directly:

> *"The Problem: You can have several underlying causes that create identical readings but the definition of the output would be read as the same."*
> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"What the Spectrometer Example Reveals: The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

This is the exact error an additionality story makes when it reads a successful startup and names its source. A funding round is a reading. It is consistent with a protected capability, a paying habitat, a temporary information asymmetry, an accelerator signal, or simple luck. The reading does not determine the source. *"it cannot comment on the source"*, the essay says of the colour instrument. Naming the accelerator as the cause of a cohort's success is metamerism, and no amount of agreement among alumni fixes it. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources, which this solution does not have. Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."*

The essay closes: *"An instrument that can’t distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* That is why the expert packet asks for a failure condition before a theory.

## 5. Prestige is not action, applied to accelerator policy

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, read directly:

> *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."*
> *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."*
> *"If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."*

The last line is the author's own agent-system instance of Fig 7 :36 (*"A chain of mirrors is one long room"*) and Fig 10 :24 (*"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*). Because it is the author's own design, it is framework-internal corroboration, not an independent witness; it is recorded here as an illustration of the rule, not as evidence that accelerators behave this way.

For policy this means: a list of famous accelerators whose alumni agree that the programme worked is a monoculture, not a check. The meta-analysis's own finding of publication bias toward positive results (*"a tendency within the field to publish studies that confirm accelerator effectiveness"*) is the same mechanism in the literature. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried by someone outside; it does not solicit endorsement.

## 6. Correction to my own §3 (an overstatement, retained as evidence)

I initially wrote that naming any particular support input is forbidden by "shape, not a name". That statement was too broad and is withdrawn here. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. If a resource or a mentor is already in a startup's library, naming it is a binding from the entrepreneurship frame, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*), which is why Gonzalez-Uribe and Leatherbee's finding that cash alone had no effect is read as evidence that cash was an atom, not an import. The binding still needs provenance and a ground test, but it is not a category error in the way naming a seventh physical primitive would be.

What is **not** withdrawn is the gap: the per-startup counterfactual is not held, cannot be named from a single reading, and is unreachable with the instruments here (Fig 13 :134-135; Imbens and Rubin). Both the corrected and the withdrawn formulations are kept so the correction is visible rather than silent.

## 7. This derivation is a simulation, not a ground reading

From the same essay, read directly:

> *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift."*
> *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too. However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."*

So this `05` file, like `01`, is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is labelled `specified` wherever it makes a claim about support systems. It is not a test of the question and cannot settle it. The point of publishing it is to make the shape and its refuters easy to check by someone whose ground contact is real and whose closure differs.

## 8. The shape, stated once more

For BUS-0050 the canonical shape is: **a three-condition conjunction with two distinct failure modes, one verification failure, and a valve that changes sign with the residual.** It is not a list of accelerator features, not a name of a winning startup, and not a programme average. The names that appear in the entrepreneurship literature are bindings, provenance owed. The instruments that would convert the shape into a grounded answer are not held here, so the answer stops at `specified` and the stop is recorded.
