## QUESTION

How can business support systems (incubators, accelerators, and public funding) help startups without favoring businesses that would succeed anyway?

Restated as an effect: why does R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| move toward zero after some support inputs and not after others, and how much of that movement was already underway before the support? R is indexed per startup and per time, never averaged (Fig 1 :19). Anchor: realized customer payment and survival, the market named as an anchor in Fig 2 :29. Option A treats support as winner selection plus resource addition and reads a cohort average as the answer. Option B treats support as a change of contact and reach, verified on an unauthored pool per startup. Option A manufactures the correlation it cites; Option B sends a checkable shape and a pre-registered refuter.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Scale: the single startup loop, the cohort ranking and the policy level across programmes and years; the membrane between scales is lossy (Fig 4).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

**The figure-derived verdict:** support helps a startup when C1 (a maintained channel to a paying ground), C2 (renewed reach through contact with a differing closure) and C3 (an unauthored comparison pool read per startup) all hold. "Favoring businesses that would succeed anyway" is the zero-support deadweight bin, not a smaller success: the machinery worked and the answer was already known. Selecting on pre-support success is the failure, because an authored cohort manufactures the correlation it later cites. Cash alone is an atom, not an import; the population average is a budget filter, never a slot verdict; the per-startup counterfactual is unreachable and that is a verdict, not a silence. The verdict is the union kept separate per domain, never averaged. Mode `specified`. The support-research pool corroborates the selection and contact claims after re-mapping cash to Γ; the low-overlap fields corroborate the method and the mechanism, not the startup-level fact. Which startups will benefit is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected public money and misdirected diagnosis. A startup failing C2 would be given more cash, a startup failing C1 would be told to import more mentors, and a participant-authored cohort would be cited as a causal result when it is a correlation. In outreach terms, an expert's twenty minutes would be spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real, public and bounded, and largely reversible if the comparison pool is unauthored from the start.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because *"something was added rather than because anything was deleted"* (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its six falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A programme can change its selection mechanism without withdrawing any primitive.
- **Reversed by:** any of the six pre-registered falsifiers, in particular (1) unchanged performance with flat pre-support reading and no import, (2) cash-only contact-neutral support that still moves the ground per startup, (3) an authored cohort retaining its advantage under a lottery or cutoff comparison, or (4) an instrument returning both potential outcomes for the same startup at the same moment. A genuinely low-overlap source showing that per-startup persistence is fully predicted by a participant's selection score with an unauthored pool would also reverse it. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
