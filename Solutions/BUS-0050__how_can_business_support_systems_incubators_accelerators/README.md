## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the *level* of supported startups' performance but the *causal change in the residual per startup*. In effect terms: a startup's library Γ predicts a market return Γ(b,a); customers return o′; R = |Γ(b,a) - o′| is measured per startup-slot, not averaged across a cohort (Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :22). The *would succeed anyway* case is the zero-support bin in which Γ already tracks o′ and the support input mints nothing (Fig 5 :29-30).

- Option A, "support works by picking winners and adding resources": leads to the prediction that the programme's own selection score and resource disbursement explain the outcome, and that a cohort average is an adequate report.
- Option B, "support works only when it changes contact and reach, measured per slot against an unauthored pool": leads to the prediction that selection on pre-support success is the failure mode, that cash alone is inert, and that the average is a budget filter and never a verdict (Fig 11 :40, Fig 6 :23, Fig 9 :60).
- What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a conjunction of conditions under which a support input changes a startup's contact and reach, an instrument that discriminates a contact change from a pre-existing trend, and an unauthored selection mechanism that does not manufacture the correlation it then measures. That shape is the sought-for term; until bound it predicts nothing and no loop check can see it.

The verdict recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` is Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the policy question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of startup support.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room’s agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable accelerator founders manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163) and *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
