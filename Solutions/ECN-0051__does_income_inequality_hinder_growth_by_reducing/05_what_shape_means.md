## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical limit: a "shape, not a name." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." And: "you will have to use the existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of."

For ECN-0051, the shape is: **the answer is slot-indexed, the two mechanisms are a union to be unbundled, and the sign of any actor is relative to the residual.** The filler that the shape cannot supply is the net sign of dY/dX. `SHAPE = growth responds per slot; the mechanisms split; valence is residual-relative`. `NAME = inequality helps / inequality hinders`. The first is checkable; the second is the thing the shape refuses.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary... This is a bootstrapping convergence process." Here the nearest available terms are "marginal propensity to consume" and "marginal propensity to save," which already carry the slot structure.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Describing the residual before choosing economics as the frame is what kept the census from simply picking the familiar growth literature and finding it fit.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why the outreach email in `02` states what would count as a failure before any theory.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." This is the direct licence for F2: the question is a bundle of H and S, to be searched individually.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." *The Hard Problem is About Source*: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, no such ground reading is in hand, so naming a net sign would be the searcher's own contribution recorded as if it were a result. That is the error the method forbids.

## 4. The source-discrimination point

*The Hard Problem is About Source*: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." The green-light example: "the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."

Applied here, a single aggregate growth rate is the reading; "inequality helped" or "inequality hindered" would be a source named from that reading. Several distinct slot-level mechanisms can produce the same aggregate. The instrument does not discriminate among them. What is owed is a slot-indexed instrument, not a bigger panel.

*How Intelligence Actually Works*: "The recursion converges toward indiscernibility, and indiscernibility is not identity. The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect." So even a well-fitting cross-country regression is not the mechanism, and the lineage of which slots and which frame produced it must be recorded.

## 5. Q: does "shape, not a name" forbid using the words demand and saving?

No, and this is a correction to an over-reading I initially wrote.

- The canonical phrase governs "a **missing** thing," a primitive not held in Γ. Demand and saving are not missing primitives of the framework; they are bindings from the economics frame onto slot-level activities that the figures can describe in effect terms (actors, currency, anchor).
- The error would be to claim that the six-term set *derives* the marginal propensity to consume, or that the binding adds a new atom and moves the wall (Fig 6 : "IMPORT adds an atom" is about primitives, and this binding adds none).
- So naming the two mechanisms is permitted as `specified` bindings, provenanced as such. Naming the **net sign** is not permitted, because no ground settles it.

Both the correct narrow reading and the rejected over-reading are recorded here so neither is silently lost.

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to this field

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof. Someone let reputation substitute for result. Someone let the trusted name validate the untested claim. The ground is never consulted."

In inequality and growth research this is a live failure: a well-cited result is treated as settled because of the citation count, not because a ground paid. `03_public_contact_points.csv` therefore contains institutional venues, not a ranked list of famous economists. The ask in `02` is for a budget to try the shape claim, not for endorsement.

## 7. This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."
