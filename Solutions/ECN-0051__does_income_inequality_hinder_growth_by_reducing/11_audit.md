## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19/:22 (per-slot residual), Fig 9 :54-56 (union), Fig 11 :49-51 (valence), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 13 :134 (unreachable as a verdict).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07_the_tether_answer.md`: range growth per slot, unbundle the two mechanisms, mark the actor's sign as residual-relative, treat the index as a frame product, and report the net sign as a gap.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete economic claim: "inequality reduces demand" or "inequality rewards saving," with a single growth rate as the outcome.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- The **slot identity** is dropped when the outcome is written as one growth rate. Fig 1 :19 forbids this, and the concrete claim reintroduces the aggregate silently.
- The **union structure** is dropped when either option is asserted alone. Fig 9 :56's intersection has no place in a single-sign sentence.
- The **anchor distinction** is dropped when a published Gini or growth series is treated as the ground. Fig 2 :30 forbids that; the round trip turns an instrument into an anchor.

Two things survive cleanly: a structure must be ranged per slot, and the two mechanisms must be unbundled before either is searched. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the aggregate as a lossless summary of the slots would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
