# Expert verification packet: observer dependence of information content

**What this is:** a claim in a form an information theorist, physicist or philosopher of information can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so the reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the claims, `general` on the shape, not `grounded`.** No measurement in this derivation is claimed.

---

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "is the information content of a physical system observer-dependent or objectively specifiable?" is a union compound, not a partition. Once the physical ground is separated from the frame's pose, two things hold at different strata and neither reduces to the other. A frame's reading is indexed by its pose, coarse-graining, assigned distribution and measurement context, because the frame transform requires a pose. Objectivity is not the absence of a frame but invariance across frames with genuinely independent evidence, each ranged against a ground that does not update when a frame thinks. The genuinely observer-indexed remainder is the lossy abstraction between scales, reported as a round trip gap R_T rather than set to zero. The semantic part of the question is a `gap`, and the binding of a physical quantity to a particular one of the framework's six terms is unproven.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework- and physics-facing; checkable against the anchor test

- **Statement:** the observer's assignment cannot be the anchor for a question constitutively about a physical system. An anchor that changes when a frame changes is not an anchor.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." Fig 2 :37-38 "It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure." Symbols table :104-106 "It is not a frame and has no vantage to update from."
- **What to check:** is the quantity being proposed (Shannon entropy relative to an assigned distribution, von Neumann entropy relative to a subsystem algebra, semantic content relative to an interpreter) being used as an instrument ranging against a ground, or has it been promoted to the thing that settles the ground? In the Maxwell demon and Landauer settings, is the erasure cost or measurement outcome the anchor, or is the observer's state of knowledge the anchor?
- **Refuter:** a derivation or experiment showing that the ground for a physical system's information content must include the observer's current belief state, so that pushing on the frame moves the anchor. If that holds, C1 fails and the strong observer-dependence reading wins.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable against independent frames and the abstraction step

- **Statement:** the objective part is convergence of independent frames, and the frame-indexed part is the lossy cross-scale abstraction. The two are different quantities and must not be averaged.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." Symbols table :82 "Cannot be run without a pose."
- **What to check:** in a concrete case (a memory register at temperature T, a coarse-grained gas, a quantum measurement with context), identify (a) what the two independent instruments or frames draw on, (b) what each ranges against, and (c) what is thrown away by the abstraction. Does the claimed "objective value" survive if the two frames share a code or a prior? If it only survives inside one shared evidence pool, it is a convention, not a verdict.
- **Refuter:** for a fixed physical process and a declared coarse-graining, two observers known to draw on different evidence return systematically different values that do not converge against the ground. That breaks the convergence account. If the difference disappears when the coarse-graining is fixed, the residual was abstraction (C2's second half) rather than observer dependence of the ground.

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** information content cannot be a bare state or a bare structure; it is a first-order cross-kind molecule, and two structures cannot bond directly. The observer is not an atom in the six, so the two readings are not isomers as usually stated.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :29-30 "A state with no structure has no magnitude: three of what, ordered how, varying across what? A structure with no state is empty: an ordering over nothing, a network with nothing at the nodes." Fig 13 :75 "Two states never bond directly. It is not space-time but space, a structure, time; and two structures join only through a state." Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."
- **What to check:** does any proposed composite of entropy-like ordering and erasure-like directedness put a state between the two structures? Is the observer being used as a primitive, which would require an import under Fig 6 :23 "No frame escapes its own closure"? If both readings are forced onto the shared six-term set, do they differ by bond and therefore qualify as the figure's isomer claim?
- **Refuter:** a figure re-census that admits a direct structure-to-structure bond, or a derivation showing the observer-dependent reading closes over the six without import and is not a different closure. Either breaks the shape in `06`.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that "the answer is both" as a flat compromise. The bundle is split and the strata are kept separate.
- Not that any physical quantity *is* Spectrum, Gradient or any other of the six. That binding is a provenance-marked import from the physics frame and is unproven.
- Not that the semantic sense of information is settled. It is `gap` at this budget.
- Not that all observers must agree, nor that agreement from a shared evidence pool is corroboration.
- Not a derivation of one of the six from the others, and no new primitive.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a ground reading. This packet is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

1. **Convergence test:** independent instruments at a declared coarse-graining converge or demonstrably do not converge against the ground.
2. **Anchor test:** pushing on the frame does not move the ground, or it does.
3. **Composition test:** a re-census admits or forbids a direct structure-to-structure bond.
4. **Gap closure:** an instrument for semantic content would move D3 out of `gap`.

A disagreement that shrinks under effort is a ground question. One that does not shrink is several questions that need splitting (Fig 9 :4-5). Do not answer the bundle; split it.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :29-33 and Fig 8 :47-52 (five short passages). Decide whether C1 and C2 are fair readings.
2. Take one system you know (a memory register, a coarse-grained gas, a measurement context) and name its pose, its coarse-graining and its ground in three lines.
3. Say whether two independent instruments would converge on your "objective" value, or whether they share a code and a prior.
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish the candidate bindings, or say why none is possible.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
