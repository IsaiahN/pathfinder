# The Tether's answer on observer dependence of information content

**Mode: `specified` on the claims, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 7), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Given a physical system S, a quantity I(S), and a frame F that can change its pose, coarse-graining, assigned distribution, measurement context and interpreter, what happens to I when F changes? The question offers two exclusive answers. In the figures the offer itself is the defect: the bundle is a **union compound** (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones"), so a single verdict over "A or B" would be a blend, not a derivation. The answer refuses the "or" and splits the question.

## 2. The shape

1. **The observer cannot be the anchor for a question about a physical system.** Fig 2 :29-30 "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Fig 2 :33 "The anchor must not update." A frame's assigned distribution is an instrument, and "instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure" (Fig 2 :37-38). Promote the observer and the anchor becomes another frame, so everything drifts together and nobody notices (Fig 2 :34).
2. **Frame dependence in the harmless sense is structural.** Symbols table :82 "Cannot be run without a pose." The frame transform F maps allocentric to egocentric at one scale. A value read by a frame is pose-indexed because a pose is required to read it, not because the world changed. Symbols :111-112: frames "differ in what they can express and not in how they label it".
3. **Objectivity is convergence, not a view from nowhere.** Fig 8 :50 "Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence." Fig 2 :31-32 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Two observers with the same code and coarse-graining are one frame wearing two names; their agreement is the pool talking (Fig 2 :18-21). A view from nowhere would be a final room, which Fig 7 :6 forbids.
4. **The genuinely frame-indexed part is the abstraction gap.** Fig 4 :27 "The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point." Fig 4 :31-32 "The gap is not an error to eliminate. It is the measurement of what the coarser description cannot hold, and it is the only honest report of how much was given up." Report R_T rather than pretending the coarse and fine descriptions match. This is a coordinate cost, not a second ground.
5. **The residual is per slot, and a frame cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :27 "A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces." An information content assigned by a frame to its own library is frame-internal and disqualified.
6. **The semantic part stays a gap.** D3 has no nameable non-updating anchor in this figure set. Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Split it and ask it separately with an instrument that measures meaning, if such an instrument exists.
7. **No binding is certified.** The figure set returns constraints on the form of an answer, not a name for the structure. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Which of the six terms a physical information quantity corresponds to is a binding from the physics frame, provenanced as such, and remains unproven.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the answer is both". That is a blend of two conditions, not a union split with a dependency order.
- Not that information is a seventh primitive, or that the observer is one of the six. Neither is available without import.
- Not that all observers must agree. Agreement from a shared evidence pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that the semantic question is meaningless or answered. It is `gap` at this budget.
- Not that entropy *is* Spectrum or erasure cost *is* Gradient. Those are bindings, and the live strain in `01` section 4 is recorded rather than resolved.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles `left(R,φ)`.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

An instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23): an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Convergence test:** for a fixed process and declared coarse-graining, do observers or instruments with genuinely independent evidence converge on the same value? A systematic non-convergent difference against the ground refutes the objectivity claim.
- **Anchor test:** does pushing on the frame move the ground? If the ground for I includes the current belief state of the observer, Fig 2 :33 is violated and the shape fails.
- **Composition test:** can two structures bond directly in a re-census? If yes, the state-between-structures constraint is wrong and the molecule in `06` collapses.
- **Gap closure:** an instrument that settles semantic content would move D3 out of `gap`. That is a new ground, not a new argument.

Standing falsifiers from `01` section 5 are reproduced in the verification packet.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in "observer-dependent or objective". It refuses the choice as a bundled question. A frame's reading of a physical system is indexed by its pose, coarse-graining and assigned distribution, and the frame transform cannot run without a pose, so that dependence is structural and harmless. What "objective" means here is not a view from nowhere but invariance across observers whose evidence is genuinely independent, each ranged against a ground that does not move when a frame changes its mind. The part that really does depend on the observer is the lossy step between micro and macro descriptions, and the framework names that loss R_T and says to measure it rather than set it to zero. What it cannot do is name which physical quantity occupies which slot, or settle the semantic sense of information; those stay open as a provenanced binding and a `gap`. What it offers is the shape any answer must have, falsifiers written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The center stays open until the ground settles it.
