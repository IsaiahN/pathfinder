## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the slot

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134-135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (opened 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question." Also: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."

For INF-0016 the shape is therefore **not** "the answer is both" and **not** "information is a structure of kind X". It is the set of constraints any candidate answer must satisfy:

- state the pose and the coarse-graining that produced the value;
- show invariance across frames with genuinely independent evidence if you claim objectivity;
- separate the lossy abstraction gap R_T from ground contact;
- never promote the observer to the anchor of a question about a physical system;
- say which part remains a gap rather than filling it.

A shape constrains. It does not fill. `I(S) = relative to a declared pose and partition, invariant across independent frames at the ground` is a shape. `I(S) = entropy-as-spectrum` is a name, and the framework does not license it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained here

- **Library of Babel:** proximity bootstrapping. Use the vocabulary closest to the thing you cannot yet name, find recurring terms that overlap with it, recurse. "This is a recursive process. With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- **Fig 6 :30:** "outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."
- **Fig 9 :30-31:** "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- **Stopping:** the Babel essay says "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place". Here the stop is earlier: the figure set yields the constraints but not the binding, so the finish line is a declared `specified` shape with one live strain, not a filled slot.

The practical consequence for this question: describe the residual (what changes when the frame changes) *before* choosing a field or a formalisation. SEP's own census warns that Shannon, Kolmogorov and Floridi definitions "have next to nothing in common", so picking one first would make any field seem to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things only, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." For this question, an instrument that discriminates between candidate bindings at a declared coarse-graining.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. SEP's mathematical invariance result (two universal machines differ by a constant) is a candidate import of *form*, not of a physical binding: it corroborates the shape of objectivity, not the identity of the physical quantity.

Nothing else licenses a name. In particular, agreement among observers who share a code does not (Fig 2 :40).

## 4. Why the observer cannot be the ground: metamerism and the anchor

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument that detects green "is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source". Several underlying causes, one reading. The essay's rule: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

Applied here: an observer's assigned distribution is a reading. Naming the source of that reading (the "real" information content) from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is an instrument that discriminates sources at the declared scale. Until one is shown, the binding stays unproven. This is high-overlap corroboration (same author as the figures), so it illustrates the shape; it does not independently prove it.

## 5. Q for this question: is the semantic part answered?

**No.** D3 stays `gap`. The framework returns a shape for the physical and frame-transform parts, and no nameable, non-updating anchor for "meaning" appears in the figure set. Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The honest report is that the semantic condition was bundled into a physical question and must be split off (Fig 9 :54-58) before it is searched again.

This is not relativism about meaning. It is a refusal to settle a semantic question with a physical anchor that does not measure semantics.

## 6. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any of the six terms. That overstates the canonical rule. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)", governed by a primitive not held in Γ. For INF-0016 the candidate terms (entropy as ordering, gradient as directedness) are already in the six, so naming one is a **binding from the physics frame**, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains wrong is claiming the binding is derived, or that it moves the wall: Fig 6 :26 "An import adds no atom to the world." Both the original claim and this correction are kept visible. The correct limit is: the framework constrains the *form* of the binding and cannot certify *which* held atom the physical quantity corresponds to without an instrument.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened 2026-11-23): "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." Gathering well-known names to agree that the shape is right is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the shape against a ground, and a refutation if it fails.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post", and "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim, and it cannot settle anything it reports on.
