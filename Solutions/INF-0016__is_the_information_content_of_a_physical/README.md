## Restatement as an effect

The question forces a choice: either the information content of a system moves when the observer moves, or it does not. In effect terms the two options are these.

- **Observer-dependent.** The value is indexed by an observing frame (coarse-graining, probability assignment, measurement context, interpreter, pose). What follows: no frame-free value exists, and the thing that would settle the value is itself a frame that can change. The anchor would move when pushed.
- **Objectively specifiable.** The value is invariant across observers. What follows: the value must be fixed by something that does not change when a frame changes its mind, and the choice of coarse-graining must be accounted for without appealing to an observer's preference.

The figures refuse the forced choice rather than choosing a side. The question is a **union compound**, not a partition: a physical ground question, a frame-transform question, and a semantic question are bundled under one "or". Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54-58).

**What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"):** *the information content of a physical system is a value invariant across independent frames ranging against one unmoving ground; the part that varies with pose and abstraction is a coordinate choice, not a second ground; any candidate notion must state its pose, its coarse-graining, and its ground separately.* That sentence is written down here before the derivation, so it can accrue a residual and be refused by the same test as everything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reason, in the figures' own words: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map :163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly the correlation that makes their agreement worthless. And *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42): showing the whole framework before asking for a verdict converts a checker into a member of the frame being checked.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public submission and opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
