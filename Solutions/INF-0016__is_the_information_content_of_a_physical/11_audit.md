## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the gap between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the concrete question the package promises to answer: is the information content of a physical system observer-dependent or objectively specifiable?

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the quantity** being discussed. The figures decide the shape of a residual and the conditions on a ground; they do not identify which physical quantity is the information content, so a reader expecting a theory of entropy gets a constraint set;
- the **status of convergence**. Fig 8 :50 is a claim about convergent derivation of an atom in two frames, not a measured theorem about physical instruments. Applying it to measurement is a `specified` extension, and the shorthand can be read as `general` if the mode is not stated;
- the **semantic sub-question**. The shorthand "semantics is gap" can be heard as "semantic information is impossible", which is exactly the proof-of-absence move Fig 8 :39 forbids;
- the **structure binding**. Fig 13 :75 constrains the molecule but does not select which structure mediates which state pair; the shorthand "a state must sit between" does not recover a physical identification.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: the observer cannot be the anchor, and independence between frames is the quantity that makes agreement mean anything. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints.
