# The Tether's answer: what do intelligence tests measure?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` on the per-domain verdicts, not `grounded`, candidate.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No measurement was taken.

## 1. The residual it answers

A score is held; three candidate sources can produce a high score: general reasoning, learned conventions, familiarity with the test environment. The question asks which one the test measures. The effect is that a single reading is asked to identify its own source. Fig 13 :128-129: *the figure point that a composed term does not reveal its route of composition by inspection* The same line adds: *"It is recoverable from the lineage."*

The question is a **union**, not a partition (Fig 9 :4, :54, :57). Verdicts are therefore kept per domain and never averaged into a percentage.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D1, the reading (psychometric frame).** A score is a projection with the bond removed. It records ingredients and not the route. From it alone you cannot attribute a source. Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* A composite also aggregates away the per-slot signal (Fig 1 :19, :22) and is frame-internal (Fig 1 :27). Verdict: `general` that the attribution is unavailable; `specified` that the current single-score instrument cannot carry it. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 13 plus Fig 2 :37-38, plus independent psychometric corroboration).

**D2, capability and habitat.** The test environment is a substituted habitat; a good score proves wiring in the harness, not capability with the harness removed (Fig 11 :66, :70). Capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone (Fig 11 :39-40). "Intelligence" is an after-the-fact label where nothing maintains anything (Fig 11 :35-36). Verdict: the familiarity option is a real habitat effect, and it is the one the figures govern most directly; it is `specified` that a score gain without a transfer test changes no claim about capability. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 11 plus Fig 4 :25, and low-overlap comparative-cognition corroboration).

**D3, conventions.** Items that run on shared meanings measure a convention. Fig 2 :41: *"efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"*; Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* A convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention (Fig 10 :47). Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 10). Conventions are not errors; they are errors only when promoted to verdicts.

**D4, general reasoning.** It is a held psychometric term (g), not a missing primitive, so it may be named as a binding. But g is norm-referenced, not an absolute scale (Jensen 1992), and the framework does not attribute a source to a reading (Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*). Verdict: `general` that the attribution is refused pending a discriminator; `specified` that g is a useful summary of a positive manifold and not a source identification. **Status: triangulated** (Fig 9 plus the canonical README), with the overstatement of F9 retracted and kept visible.

**D5, the source of any particular score.** **Gap.** No figure line governs it. Fig 13 :137-138: *"it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands"*; Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* This is a shape-not-a-name gap, not unreachable in principle: a source-discriminating instrument could close it, more scoring with the same instrument cannot.

## 3. Structure: isomers, not a list

Over the full shared atom set `{item content, rule structure, exposure history, testing context, feedback}` the three candidates are Fig 12's isomers: *"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Under the narrower framings they are members of one functional group (Fig 12 :123, *"the group says what kind; the qualifier says which member"*). The conditional form is the one carried forward; the unconditional isomer claim is a named strain (S3 in `01` §7, and `06` Q1). The bond, not the atom list, is where the source information lives.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that intelligence tests measure nothing, and not that g does not exist. g is a well-replicated positive manifold; the refusal is of the attribution, not of the statistic.
- Not that the Flynn effect is an artifact or that all score gains are familiarity. The literature contains genuine environmental contributions (school years, nutrition, abstract thinking) and genuine measurement contributions (norm drift, item bias, order effects). They are a union, and this derivation does not assign a global percentage.
- Not that stereotype threat is large in operational settings. The 2016 operational meta-analysis reports d = -.14 with publication bias, conflicting in magnitude with the classic lab effect; the conflict is kept as union evidence.
- Not that one of the three words is the true answer.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by a measurement.
- Not a claim to know the source of any named person's score.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*; the green reading was resolved by a spectrometer, not by a debate.

Proto-instruments already returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34-35): retest gains, order-of-administration effects, framing manipulations, coaching designs, differential item functioning across cohorts, and transfer tests with the harness removed. The instrument is built from these, not designed from an account of intelligence.

The standing falsifiers, written before the run (full list in `01` §4):

1. A source-discriminating instrument assigns the source uniquely with the same score reproduced by the other two sources.
2. A score gain transfers to a naturally occurring task with habitat and environment unchanged.
3. The three candidates are a partition with an assignable shared fraction.
4. A composite retains a live per-slot residual and ranges against an anchor outside the test frame.
5. A convention-laden item predicts a non-conventional ground outcome after the convention is removed.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish sources, and the lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. The six-term substrate and the frames applied here are visible guesses, not nature's own list (*They're Scanning Books* `[carried]`: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*). Indiscernibility is not identity. One live strain (S1) and one conditional claim (S3) are carried, not hidden.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick one of the three options. It says the question is a union of overlapping conditions, so it needs separate verdicts rather than an average. A test score is a projection: it records the ingredients but not the route that produced them, so the source is in the lineage, not in the number. The test environment is a substituted habitat, so a good score proves that the wiring works in that harness and does not by itself prove capability with the harness removed. Items that run on shared meanings measure a convention, which is efficient as a convention and worthless as a verdict about the world. Over a shared item set the three candidates are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, which is exactly why the score cannot tell them apart. What the framework returns is the shape of the instrument that would: per slot, lineage-carrying, source-discriminating, ranged against a ground that does not update on the test frame. Which source produced any particular score stays open until such an instrument exists. That is a verdict, not silence, and nothing here is claimed as measured.
