## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :137-138: *"it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands"*; Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel* `[carried]`: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a **shape** is the form of the discriminator that could settle a source attribution: per slot, lineage-carrying, source-discriminating, ranged against a ground that does not update on the test frame. It is not one of the words "reasoning", "convention" or "familiarity" chosen and defended. A shape constrains; it does not fill. The answer "we do not yet know which source, and here is what an instrument would have to look like" is a shape answer, not a failure to answer.

## 2. The governing essay: the source is not in the reading

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened this session) is the direct match for REA-0007. Its central claim, verbatim: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)."* Its instrument example: *"You have an instrument that can detect color. the instrument detects green, and says that it's green. However, the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The possible sources are one green light, yellow plus blue, red plus green plus blue, CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."*

The essay's rule: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* And the honest stop: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

An IQ score is the green reading. General reasoning, learned conventions and test familiarity are the candidate light mixtures. Asking which one the score measures is asking the reading to comment on its source. Fig 13 :128-129 gives the same result in composition terms: the route is not recoverable from the artifact, it is recoverable from the lineage. The two are one claim, not two corroborations; the essay is the framework's own statement of the figure.

## 3. How a shape is obtained

- *The Library of Babel* `[carried]`: use the vocabulary already closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* `[carried]`: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* The discriminator for source will be built from instruments already returning something: retest designs, coaching experiments, differential item functioning, transfer tasks, framing manipulations. None of these was specified from an account of "intelligence itself" (Fig 6 :33, *"Nobody specified a telescope from an account of Mars."*).

## 4. What licenses a name, and why the three candidates may be named

Only two things license a name: the ground settles a minted term, or a provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs (Fig 6 :23; Fig 8 :50-52).

*They're Scanning Books* `[carried]`: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* And: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

**Correction to my first pass (kept visible; see also §6 and `01` F9).** In this question the three candidates are not missing primitives. General reasoning (g), learned conventions and test familiarity are already held terms in the psychometric and cognitive frames. Naming them is a **binding from those frames** onto held library terms, which is provenance work. It is not the invention of a seventh term and it does not move a wall (Fig 6 :21, *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom."*). What is not licensed is the **attribution**: calling a particular score a measure of one of them without the lineage and the source-discriminating instrument. The shape-not-a-name rule governs the attribution, not the vocabulary.

## 5. Capability, the habitat, and why the question's "general" is misdirected

*How Intelligence Actually Works* `[carried]` and *Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether* `[carried]` ground the capability claim that Fig 11 makes explicit: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* (Fig 11 :39). *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session) adds the mechanism: the ARC harness has *"a score. You either solve the level or you don't. That is the ground."* Yet the same essay warns that a hard harness is *"a proxy for the cruel and unknowable environment that their agents were ranging against"* - the harness is a substitute habitat, not the world (Fig 11 :70, *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*).

So "general reasoning" as a property of a person in isolation is the wrong object for a shape answer. The answerable question is what makes the system capable in a habitat, and which part of a score is contact with the substituted habitat rather than with the world.

## 6. Correction to my own §4: the "shape, not a name" overstatement

I initially wrote that naming g, conventions or familiarity was forbidden by the framework's shape-not-a-name rule. **That is retracted.** The canonical sentence is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Its subject is a primitive not held in Γ. All three candidates are already in the library. The correct claim is narrower and stronger: the framework cannot attribute a source to a reading without a discriminator and the lineage, because the route is not in the artifact (Fig 13 :128-129). Both the original overstatement and its retraction are preserved so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Simulation status and the prestige rule

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened this session): *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* This audit is such a simulation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

The same essay gives the outreach rule: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous endorsers of one of the three options would be a prestige loop, not a ground.

## 8. Consequences for this solution

1. The shape we return is a **separator plus a discriminator spec**, not a choice among three words.
2. The `gap` on which source produced a given score is a shape-not-a-name gap: another instrument could close it, more of the same scoring cannot (Fig 8 :39, *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence"*).
3. The three words are named as held bindings with provenance, and the attribution is refused pending a discriminator (§6).
4. Every substantive claim carries `general` or `specified`, none `grounded`, and the whole folder is labelled a simulated derivation.
