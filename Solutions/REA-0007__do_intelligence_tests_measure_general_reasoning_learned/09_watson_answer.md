## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether an intelligence test measures general reasoning, learned conventions, or familiarity with the test environment. Option A: pick one of the three as the true object. Option B: treat the three as a union of overlapping conditions, refuse the attribution the score cannot carry, and return the shape of the instrument that would settle it. What each leads to: A sends a named attribution that a projection cannot support, averages a union into a percentage, and promotes a convention into a verdict. B sends separate per-domain verdicts, five pre-registered refuters, and a declared `gap` on source attribution.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A test score and the claim that it measures one of three sources. Arity: three candidates, a union (Fig 9 :4). Symmetry: the candidates are not interchangeable (ordered bonds), but the score is symmetric with respect to all three, which is the difficulty. Scale: one test session for the reading, a lineage across a habitat for capability (Fig 11 :36, :39).
- **Domain 1, the scoring frame.** Actors: items, test-taker, composite, norming sample. Currency: score and rank. Anchor: none nameable from the score alone; the frame scores itself with a quantity it also produces (Fig 1 :27). This domain is the residual until an outside anchor is named.
- **Domain 2, capability and habitat.** Actors: agent, habitat, seat. Currency: contact and cascade. Anchor: the ground, what the capability does with the harness removed. Capability is agent-and-habitat, never agent alone (Fig 11 :39).
- **Domain 3, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert researchers and public venues. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 7 :35). In this simulated run all domains share one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero (Fig 2 :40).
- **Census result:** union, not partition (Fig 9 :54, :57). Three per-domain verdicts, never averaged.
- **Order by dependency:** settle the reading first, then the habitat substitution, then the source (Fig 3 :20, :27).

Strain test applied: the apparent conflict between Fig 2 :41 (conventions efficient) and Fig 10 :47 (a convention deciding an outcome is a failure) dissolves on re-census into two quantities, agreement about shared meaning versus promotion to a verdict. The isomer claim over the three candidates is conditional on a shared atom set (S3), kept visible.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The either/or is refused. The question is a union of overlapping conditions, so three per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. A score is a projection: the route is in the lineage, not the number. The test environment is a substituted habitat, so a within-harness success proves wiring, not capability. Learned conventions are efficient as conventions and disqualifying as verdicts when they decide outcomes. General reasoning is a held term that may be named as a provenanced binding but cannot be attributed to a reading without a discriminator. Over a shared atom set the three candidates are conditional isomers, which is exactly why the score cannot tell them apart. Which source produced any particular score is a declared `gap` of the shape-not-a-name kind: a verdict, not a silence. Mode `general` on the structure, `specified` on the per-domain claims, not `grounded`, candidate.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, lists five refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a concluded attribution instead of a separator; Fig 2 :42 says *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with C1 to C3 and the refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The refused attribution is removable without touching the derivation; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove it. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` stays open and a later instrument can close it.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that assigns the source uniquely with the score held fixed; a transfer test showing a score gain that changes contact outside the harness; a demonstration that the three candidates form a partition with an assignable shared fraction; a composite retaining a live per-slot residual ranged against an external anchor; or a figure re-census that finds a line governing attribution directly. Prestige or endorsement is not such an instrument. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
