## What the wider literature bears on the representation question

1. **The four variations are already treated as real dimensions in separate literatures.** Barrat et al. supply strength, Holme and Saramäki supply duration and time-respecting paths, Kivelä et al. supply multiple relation types, and Leskovec et al. supply sign with an explicit note that structural-balance theory clashes with the *"evolving, directed nature"* of the data. That is four pieces of corroboration for the claim that these are not one scalar. They are network-facing and partly share an evidence pool (Barthelemy appears in two), so they count as one nearby witness, not four independent ones.
2. **The low-overlap witness is ecology.** Berlow et al. show that interaction strength and sign depend on many measured attributes and that nontrophic effects cause a model to *"predictably fail"* when present. That corroborates the instrument point: a name read off a single projection is a metamerism error. It does not corroborate the seven-bond grammar itself, which is framework-internal.
3. **No source supplies the bond name for a specific link.** That is the `gap` in F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`. The literature offers representations, not a discriminator that selects one bond from the operands alone.
