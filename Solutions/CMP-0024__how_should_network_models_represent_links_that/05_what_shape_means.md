## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For CMP-0024 the shape is: *a link is two operands joined by one typed bond, with a symmetry, a price, and a record of origin, and the four observed variations are carried by four different figure terms.* A name would be: *this particular edge in this dataset is a production bond, not a sequence bond.* The first is licensed; the second is not, until a ground settles it.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *closest* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For this question that means describing what a link does (what changes in the observation when it is present) before choosing the word for what it is.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."*
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*
- The bundle check: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* For links that vary in four ways, this is the first move, not a last resort (Fig 9 :54-57).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a link type, that is an experiment or observation that discriminates one bond from another.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test. Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."*

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* was listed in the exemplar's own reading, but its full text was not opened this session, so no quote from it is used here. *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* supplies the same point directly: *"A declared meaning is not a grounded one"* is Fig 10 :18, and the essay adds that *"alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you"* (Fig 2 :35) when two sides only check against each other.

## 4. Why reading a bond off a projection is the named error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can all produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A network model's adjacency observation is exactly such a projection. Two different bonds can produce the same observed edge between the same two nodes. Naming the bond from the projection alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name. This is the reason F7 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a `gap` rather than a refusal: the missing thing is an instrument, characterized in effect terms but not held here.

## 5. The question this file answers

**What licenses a particular bond for a particular link?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape: a typed bond with a symmetry class, a price, and a provenance slot. A particular bond is licensed only by (i) the ground settling a minted term, or (ii) a provenanced import with a source gate and shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the modeller's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the default weighted adjacency commit the error the README forbids?** Yes, but not by naming a missing primitive. It commits the flat-formula error (Fig 12 :5, :6, :14) and the metamerism error at the naming step: one bond type with a scalar records one projection and treats it as the source. The correct form keeps the bond as an explicit, priced, provenanced choice.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-cited network scientists assembled to agree with a representation claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground (a trace, a removal experiment, a discriminating instrument) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the representation question and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
