# Expert verification packet: the Tether on variable network links

**What this is:** a claim in a form a network-modeling researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified`: not `grounded`.** No measurement on a specific network dataset is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A link is a typed bond between two operands, and which bond holds is not recoverable from the operands. The four ways a link varies belong to four different figure terms: strength is an ordering and a threshold comparison, direction is the symmetry class of the bond, duration is a time relation that may survive a use or close a loop, and meaning is the bond itself, licensed by provenance or a ground test rather than declared inside the model. A single weighted edge type is the framework's flat formula, in which the arrangement is unrecorded and the bond carries nothing. Two graphs with the same nodes and the same connected pairs but different bond types are isomers. Diagnostics must be indexed per link, not by graph-level fit. Which bond a particular empirical link has is a declared `gap`, not a hidden answer.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1: framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12

- **Statement:** a link is a typed bond, and the bond is not recoverable from its operands. A representation that records endpoints and a weight has recorded half of itself.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :46: *"A bond is the typed relation between two operands. There are seven, they are not interchangeable, and which one holds is not recoverable from the operands."* Also Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* And Fig 12 :101: *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."*
- **Refuter:** show, for a fixed pair of operands, that strength, direction, duration, and meaning are all recoverable from the operands alone with no bond record, and that an external measurement cannot distinguish two graphs differing only in bond type. Also: exhibit a one-bond-type representation with a provably nonzero bond term under Fig 12 :42.

### C2: the four-way mapping; checkable against the four figure terms

- **Statement:** strength maps to spectrum ordering plus ⋛ comparison; direction maps to the ordered versus symmetric bond classes; duration maps to time plus the catalyst and cycle structures; meaning maps to which bond holds and how it was licensed. Gradient is the wrong home for strength.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :20: *"ordering, so more and less can be said"*; Fig 12 :87: *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*; Fig 13 :36: *"adjacency is not duration, an ordering is not a pathway, a pathway is not a direction."*; Fig 13 :46-47: *"What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."*; Fig 12 :119: *"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."*; Fig 10 :18: *"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"*.
- **Refuter per row:** (strength) a strength difference that an ordering/comparison instrument cannot rank; (direction) a directed relation recovered from the unordered pair alone; (duration) a link whose persistence and regeneration are fully determined by adjacency and weight; (meaning) a bond selected by the figures from the atoms alone, with no import and no ground test.

### C3: network-facing; checkable against a dataset or a model

- **Statement:** two graphs with identical node sets and edge sets but different bond types can be decisive different substances, so a graph-level fit statistic cannot adjudicate them. Which bond holds in a named empirical link is `gap` until an instrument discriminates it.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*; Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*; Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*; Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- **Refuter:** two graphs with identical nodes and edges but different bond types that are indistinguishable under an otherwise sensitive instrument; or an averaged graph-level statistic that predicts prospectively and per link which named slot will fail under a bond substitution.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any particular empirical link is a production bond, a sequence bond, or any other named type. That needs a provenanced import or a discriminating instrument.
- Not that weighted, temporal, directed, multiplex, or signed representations are wrong. Each binds one or more figure terms and is legitimate once its provenance is recorded.
- Not that gradient is strength, or that adjacency is direction. Both were re-censused; see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.
- Not that a convention is meaningless. A convention makes a failure statable; it becomes a defect when it decides an outcome (Fig 10 :47).
- Not `grounded`. No system trace or removal experiment was run here.
- Not a claim that this framework is the only account of network representation. That would be an unsealed census.

## 5. What would settle it

1. A bond-discrimination observation separating sequence from production between the same two nodes at the same strength and duration.
2. A measured symmetry test: swap the operands of a link and see whether the system's response changes.
3. A catalyst test: does the link survive its own use, and is that difference recorded across uses?
4. A threshold test: does a ⋛ comparison hold between two measured link strengths, or are they merely ordered?
5. The pre-registered falsifiers in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4, run and failed to fire.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :46, :94, :101, :117 and Fig 13 :20, :36, :46-47. Seven short passages.
2. Check C2 against their own field: is strength really an ordering/comparison in their instruments, and is the gradient mapping a category error?
3. Name one observation that would distinguish a symmetric co-presence bond from an ordered production bond between the same two nodes, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
