## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"; the README adds "Indiscernibility is not identity."

*The Library of Babel* (read 2026-11-23): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. For this question the shape is the four-condition test in `01` section 4: independent grounding, bond and slot preservation, a real partition, and a description-length bargain. A shape is not "the correct taxonomy", which would be a name and would settle the question the framework says it leaves to the ground.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30 "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31 "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (read 2026-11-23): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."

The same essay supplies the diagnostic that drives F2: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." That is the natural-language form of Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

## 3. What licenses a name, and the correction to this reading

Two things license a particular class, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

**Correction carried from the exemplar and re-derived here.** The phrase "shape, not a name" governs a *missing* primitive, one not held in the library. Naming one of a set of categories already held is a binding from another frame onto an existing term, not the invention of a new primitive. Fig 13 :75 requires a structure between two states but does not select among the structures it holds. Applied to this question: saying "this class is bond-preserving" is a shape constraint; saying "this class is the natural kind" is a binding that needs a ground or a provenanced import. I overstate the rule if I forbid the latter outright. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 4. Why a source-named category is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (read 2026-11-23) gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

A classification that names the source of a reading (for example, reading a correlation and naming a natural kind) commits exactly this metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminating instrument, not a better name. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (read 2026-11-23): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."

## 5. Two more consequences from the essays

**Agreement has opposite valences.** *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "Agreement is the whole point when you're settling what words mean. It's worthless for settling what's true when both sides learned from the same sources." And: "A shared mindset lets two people drift together without either one noticing." This is Fig 2 :40 and :41 in plain language, and it is the reason F5 keeps the convention verdict separate from the truth verdict.

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of famous taxonomies gathered to agree with a claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is a budget to try the claim against a ground.
