# Expert verification packet: the Tether on classification

**What this is:** a claim in a form a philosopher or classifier can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `general` on the shape, `specified` on the tests, not `grounded`.** No instrument was run. This is a shape and a route, not a result about any named taxonomy.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A classification reveals a real pattern when four conditions hold across three domains: its cuts are ranged against an anchor that does not move when classifiers agree, by frames that draw on different evidence; the cut preserves the bond and the individual case rather than an ingredient list and an average; it is a real partition rather than overlapping conditions folded into one bin; and stating the classes costs less than the confusion they remove. It obscures complexity when any of those fail, above all when a convention useful for coordination is allowed to decide an outcome. Because the conditions live in different domains, verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. The framework returns the shape a good classification must have, not the name of the right one, and no cut is final.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 2, 9 and 10

- **Statement:** agreement is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict; a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being a convention.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 2 :41 "efficient for a convention: agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it"; Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :47 "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one"; Fig 10 :49 "Declaring a meaning does not ground it."
- **Refuter:** show a class whose boundaries are fixed by interest and yet settle a ground question, with an independent instrument redrawing the same boundary. If that cannot be shown, the distinction between convention and verdict stands as `candidate`.

### C2 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 12 and 1

- **Statement:** a classification by shared ingredients drops the arrangement, and an average across cases drops the live slot; neither is a finer version of the ground-paying cut.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."; Fig 12 :117 "isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 1 :22 "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."
- **What to check:** is the isomer claim faithful to the chemistry record, and is the slot claim faithful to how residual mass is tracked? The historical check is in `09` WORLD (Wentrup 2022; Science History Institute).
- **Refuter:** a feature-list classification that discriminates bonded isomers, or an aggregate that preserves a live slot, on a ground-paying axis without an added relation or instrument. Either would falsify the claim that what was lost is unrecoverable from the notation.

### C3 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 8

- **Statement:** a class that bundles two conditions is a disjunction with a live intersection; it must be unbundled before it is searched, and only independent frames give agreement meaning.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 9 :4-5 "Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."; Fig 8 :48-49 "Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."
- **Refuter:** a class defined as a disjunction of two independently measurable conditions whose boundary converges under effort and whose intersection behaves exactly like the single-condition regions; or two demonstrably independent classifications whose agreement persists on a held-out ground-paying slot.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that any named taxonomy is the correct one, or that the framework can name a missing kind.
- Not that there is a final classification. No last room; every cut stays ranked and reversible.
- Not that expert agreement makes a cut real. Agreement is a convention's product, not a verdict's evidence.
- Not that more categories means finer truth. Fig 13 :116-118 "Six members, and no amount of arranging produces a seventh: a new member would have to be a new kind, and composition only ever rearranges the kinds already held."
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not a claim that this derivation is a ground reading. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating instrument, not further agreement. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument reading green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

1. Does a class boundary track a real joint, shown by novel ground-paying predictions on held-out slots?
2. Can a flat ingredient list resolve bonded differences without an added relation?
3. Does a convention deciding an outcome improve contact with the ground, or only coordinate action?
4. Can a new kind be derived by rearrangement alone inside one closed vocabulary?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 2 :40-41, Fig 10 :47, Fig 12 :5 and :117, Fig 9 :54. Five short passages.
2. Check C2 against a classification you know: did the feature list drop a bond or an average drop a case that mattered?
3. Supply one instrument that would discriminate a class boundary, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
