## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a protected area. Option A: treat area designated as the win and stop when the boundary is drawn. Option B: treat changed contact with the pressure field as the win and require per-site measurement plus an enumerated cascade before calling the outcome conservation. ## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The gap between the prediction "area protected means nature conserved" and the observed per-site ecological trajectory. Arity: two conditions, so a union compound, not one hard question: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either (Fig 9 :56). Symmetry: not additive; a gain inside and a loss outside are different object slots. Scale: first order at the boundary, second and third order in the contact cascade (Fig 11 :52).
- **Domain 1, ecological frame.** Actors: populations, species, pressures (extraction, fishing, logging, poaching, fire, climate), conditions and relations. Currency: persistence, abundance, ecosystem function. Anchor: per-site counterfactual ecological outcome. This is the ground for the question.
- **Domain 2, management and policy frame.** Actors: managers, regulators, communities, funders. Currency: area designated, management effort, compliance, budget. Anchor: none from inside; it borrows D1's ground. Area is frame-internal, so this domain cannot carry the verdict alone.

- **Partition or union.** D1 and D2 form a union: persistence and governance both bind at the boundary. Displacement is the intersection. D3 is a frame over both, not a separate ground.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe the residual before choosing the comparator (Fig 9 :27); enumerate the habitat before acting (Fig 11 :43); settle first-order contact before reading the cascade (Fig 3 :20).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; no specific reserve, species or pressure is named by the framework. A protected area conserves when it changes the contact between the ecosystem and its pressures and the substituted habitat reproduces what the goal required. It displaces when isolation only substitutes habitat and the cascade is unenumerated: the excluded actors are inherited, what failed to reproduce stays invisible until the goal fails, and what was introduced stays invisible until it acts. Coverage is a frame-internal budget filter, not a verdict witness. The same boundary export is beneficial or harmful depending on the residual. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result. The strongest single figure-grounded point is Fig 11 :66: isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove the pressure field.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, names four refuters and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a generator. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong"; here the cost is a wrong conservation rule repeated across many reserves.
- **undoable:** yes. The shape claim can be narrowed without touching the ecological measurements: if a pressure is shown to have identical valence for all residuals, the valence finding is withdrawn; if a fully enumerated cascade shows no displacement, the cascade finding is withdrawn. A sent claim can be superseded, and no figure primitive has to be abandoned.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Per-site counterfactual trajectories with an instrument that distinguishes in-place recovery from relocated pressure would settle the mechanism; a controlled no-contact-change outcome would break Fig 11 :40; a genuine removal of the pressure field would break Fig 11 :66 and :67; a fully enumerated cascade with no second or third-order effect would fire refuter four. Until one of these lands, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
