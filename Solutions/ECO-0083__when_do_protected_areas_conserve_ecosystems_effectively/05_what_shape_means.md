# What "shape" means for this question

Canonical reading from the figures README and the eight essays by Isaiah N, all read 2026-11-23. Sources are linked at the end and listed in `10_source_ledger.csv`. Mode of this document: `general` on the reading of "shape", `specified` on the binding to protected areas.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence", and the following line notes that the missing primitive cannot be named, because naming it would mean already holding it.

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

A **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the specific thing that fills it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is: **the effect of a protected area is a contact condition, not an area count**. Any candidate answer must say what changed in the contact between the ecosystem and its pressures, must reproduce what the conservation goal required, and must enumerate the cascade to the point where it stops mattering. It does not say which reserve, which species, or which pressure. Those are bindings from the ecology frame.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary." The same essay warns that a bundle masquerading as one question must be split: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" That is exactly F1 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- Fig 9 :27: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit". Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is why `02_outreach_email.md` leads with what a refuter would look like rather than with the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", because by then "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." Here the stopping rule is operational: stop when no instrument returns a further displacement residual, not when the theory feels complete.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README adds that the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8), with a source gate and a shadow test.

The canonical README's phrase is about a **missing** primitive. "Displacement", "leakage", "spillover" and "per-site residual" are not new primitives; they are contact patterns already expressible in the habitat vocabulary. Naming them is a binding from the ecology frame, provenanced as such. What would need an import is an axis of measurement that no current instrument returns (see *They're Scanning Books When They Should*: "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space.").

## 4. Why a single aggregate reading cannot settle this question

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "it cannot comment on the source": one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. The reading does not determine the source. The essay's exact framing: "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information". It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a net positive population trend inside a reserve is a projection. It is compatible with genuine in-place recovery, with immigration from outside, and with displacement that has not yet arrived at the third-order effect. Fig 1 :19 and :22 forbid aggregating the slots; the essay forbids naming the source from the reading. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, per-slot and counterfactual, not a better argument. This is the strongest non-framework corroboration of F2 and F6.

## 5. Q: What is the shape of an effective protected area, then?

**Answered, with the binding kept separate from the shape.**

The shape, from the figures: a contact condition in which (a) the retained habitat reproduces what the goal required, (b) introduction is available and exclusion is not, so management is permanent work, (c) the cascade to second and third order has been enumerated, and (d) the residual is measured per slot against a ground that no frame can move.

The binding, from the ecology frame: "effective" is filled in by measured persistence, abundance, or function at the site, and "pressure" is filled in by whatever the enumerated habitat actually shows (extraction, fishing, fire, water, poaching, climate). The framework does not select among these for us; Fig 11 :49 says valence is relative to the residual. *They're Scanning Books* is explicit: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." And: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible." So the per-site measure is a visible choice, and it must be recorded as such.

## 6. Correction: the framework's "habitat" is not the ecologist's "habitat"

The canonical article *Arrival of the Fittest* has a section titled "Three mappings offered and declined". One of them is "The habitat as an ecological niche", declined in these words: "A niche is defined by an organism's relation to its environment. A habitat here is defined by contact with a residual, and the residual moves so the same actors constitute a different habitat when the question changes."

This bears directly on this solution. When this folder says "the habitat of a protected area", that is a **binding of an ecological area onto the framework's habitat term**, not an identity. The framework's habitat is enumerated from the residual outward; the ecological reserve is delimited by a legal boundary. They overlap but they are not the same object, and the overlap is exactly where displacement lives: a pressure outside the legal boundary is still inside the framework habitat if it contacts the residual.

**Earlier reading retracted.** The first pass wrote "the reserve's habitat" unqualified, which imported the ecological-niche sense the canonical article declines. All findings now say "the habitat in contact with the residual" and treat the legal boundary as a D2 convention. The correction strengthens F4 rather than weakening it: isolation substitutes a habitat *for the residual*, and that is not the same as fencing a piece of ground.

A second honesty note from the same article: a section "A note on what is not cited" lists the four claims this solution leans on most (valence relative to the residual, introduction available exclusion not, isolation as substitution, capability as agent-and-habitat) and says no prior source was found for them. It adds: "Absence of a citation is not a claim of originality." These are framework claims; they are not independently established ecology, and the world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` corroborate the consequences, not the axioms.

## 7. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A review that gathers respected names to agree that reserves work is the named collapse. The legitimate ask gives the shape a budget to be tried against per-site data.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." And: "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." *Arrival of the Fittest* adds that an LLM simulation of the framework produces "simulacra" and that "it could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency. It cannot settle whether any real protected area conserved anything.
