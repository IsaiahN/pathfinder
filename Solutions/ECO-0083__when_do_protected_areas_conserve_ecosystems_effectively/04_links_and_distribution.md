## What the wider literature does and does not do

1. It corroborates the shape at low overlap with the framework: Nowakowski is a global population analysis, Mora and Sale a technical review, the two overviews a synthesis of the management debate. None of them was produced from the figures.
2. It does **not** settle the question. All of these sources sit in one broad conservation evidence pool, and several cite each other. By Fig 2 :40, "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", so their agreement is one witness, not many.
3. The apparent conflict between beneficial spillover and harmful displacement is processed in F7, not by choosing a side. Fig 11 :49 makes valence relative to the residual.
