## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals, and no ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan and no claim that agreement from a shared evidence pool corroborates the verdict.
- No assertion that this derivation settles the ecological question. Figure-grounded agreement is internal consistency, not a ground reading (Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").

The audience that can help is the one that can refute the shape claim against a measured ground, reached through public opt-in venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
