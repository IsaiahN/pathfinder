# Expert verification packet: the Tether on protected areas and displacement

**What this is:** a claim in a form a conservation scientist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the mechanism, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** No ecological measurement was taken. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

Protected-area effectiveness is a contact condition, not an area count. A protected area conserves when the designation changes the contact between the ecosystem and its pressures and the substituted habitat reproduces what the goal required. It displaces when the boundary is merely redrawn: the excluded pressures reappear in the inherited habitat, the goal's requirements fail silently, and the effect arrives at second or third order through the cascade. Area designated is a quantity the managing frame produces and scores itself with, so it is a budget filter, never a verdict witness. The same boundary export is beneficial spillover for one residual and harmful leakage for another, because valence belongs to the residual, not to the actor. What would settle this is per-site counterfactual measurement with the habitat enumerated outward until the cascade stops.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 11

- **Statement:** isolation substitutes a habitat, it does not remove the pressure field; introduction is available to the seat, exclusion is not; management is therefore permanent work.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :66 "Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another." Also Fig 11 :61 "The seat's power is asymmetric. Introduction is available; exclusion is not. You can add an actor and you cannot subtract the habitat, because contact is a fact about the world and does not consult you." And Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."
- **Refuter:** show a real case in which the pressure field is removed rather than relocated, with the habitat enumerated to the point the cascade stops; or show a seat that can subtract the habitat. The first would break the mechanism; the second would break the asymmetry.

### C2 - ecology-facing; checkable by a conservation scientist against real assessments

- **Statement:** the unit of analysis must be the per-site residual. A global average or an area total can be positive while one site's live residual is still positive, and the same boundary export has opposite valence for different residuals.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Also Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." And Fig 1 :27 "They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."
- **What to check:** do the standard effectiveness assessments report per-site trajectories against counterfactual sites, or a pooled average? Are spillover and displacement ever treated as the same contact read against different goals? A published review that answers these from its own methods is enough; no agreement with this framework is needed.
- **Refuter:** a controlled per-site result showing that pooling does not hide any live residual; or an actor whose valence is the same for every residual in the system.

### C3 - measurement-facing; checkable against the study design

- **Statement:** displacement is a second or third-order contact effect. A study scoped to direct contact at the boundary cannot detect it, and its absence of detection is not evidence of absence.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :52 "And contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out." Also Fig 11 :44 "List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering." And Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."
- **What to check:** did the assessment enumerate displaced effort, prey, disease or market effects beyond the boundary, or only the first-order change inside it? A one-line answer is useful.
- **Refuter:** a fully enumerated cascade, in a system where displacement was expected, showing none at second or third order with an instrument already returning something. This refutes the claim. A null from an instrument that never resolved the effect does not.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that protected areas fail, and not that any named reserve, species or pressure is the answer.
- Not that the framework's "habitat" is the ecological niche. The canonical article declines that mapping; the legal boundary and the contact field overlap but are not the same object.
- Not that a planning model or reserve-selection algorithm demonstrates conservation. It proves wiring, not capability (Fig 11 :70).
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here is asserted as settled by experiment.
- Not corroborated by agreement. All world sources read for this packet sit in the same conservation evidence pool; their agreement is one witness, not many (Fig 2 :40).

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source". Here:

1. Per-site trajectories against comparable unprotected sites, with the residual indexed per slot.
2. An enumerated cascade out to the point where the effect stops mattering, including displaced effort.
3. A changed-contact test: does the pressure field's contact with the residual actually change, or only its location?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 11 :66, :61 and :52, plus Fig 1 :19. Four short passages, five minutes.
2. Check C2 against one assessment you know: are results per site with a counterfactual, or pooled?
3. Check C3 against the same assessment: was anything beyond the boundary enumerated?
4. Name one instrument that would distinguish in-place recovery from relocated pressure, or say why none exists.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
