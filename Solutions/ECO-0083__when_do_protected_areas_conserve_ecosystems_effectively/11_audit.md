## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation the folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 11 :66, :61, :52 and :49 (substitution, asymmetry, cascade, valence), Fig 1 :19 and :27 (per-slot, frame-internal), Fig 9 :54 and :60 (union, filters).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried across the folder: "a protected area conserves when contact changes and the retained habitat meets the ground per slot; it displaces when isolation only substitutes habitat and the cascade is unenumerated".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an ecological claim about a named reserve, a named species and a named pressure.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union condition**: the shorthand says "contact changes" but drops the requirement that conservation and displacement be measured as two conditions, not averaged;
- the **frame-internal ban**: "contact changes" can be read as a management metric, and Fig 1 :27 forbids the frame scoring itself;
- the **valence rule**: the shorthand does not carry that the same export is beneficial for one residual and harmful for another, so it can be misread as a standing list of good or bad boundary effects;
- the **instrument condition**: the shorthand says "meets the ground" without the requirement that a source-discriminating instrument already return something (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :39).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: isolation substitutes habitat rather than removing the pressure field, and the residual must be indexed per slot. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero, which is the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
