## The answer in one line

The figures do not return a number. They constrain the shape of the answer: the multiplier is a residual indexed by slot (state of the economy, country, and identifying frame), each slot settled by its own measured ground, and the variation across recession, expansion and country is a union of overlapping conditions to be unbundled rather than averaged. The spread of published estimates is the signal, not noise around one constant.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No single numeric multiplier asserted as the Tether's answer. The framework returns a shape, not a name or a number (Fig 9 :32, *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).

Three reasons, in the figures' own terms:

1. **The ground settles, agreement does not.** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* A list chosen for likely agreement is a mirror.
2. **Shared evidence is one witness.** Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Meta-analyses that mine the same post-war OECD datasets are one pool talking.
3. **Give a refuter, not a theory, to the outside frame.** Fig 10 :52: *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*

Legally: unsolicited bulk email with harvested personal addresses breaches GDPR/CAN-SPAM-style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in and institutional channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
