## Independent domain census

I redid the census from the residual line alone before reading the primary's conclusions: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the gap between a constant-multiplier prediction and the observed output response. Arity: a function of at least two indices (economy state, country) plus a third (identifying frame), so the target is a vector, not a scalar. Symmetry: swapping recession and expansion changes the value, so the spending-to-output bond is condition-dependent. Scale: decision at the fiscal authority, response at national accounts, cascade through households, firms, finance and trade.
- **Domain 1, fiscal/macro frame.** Actors: government, households, firms. Currency: spending, income, output, taxes. Anchor: an identified spending change and the measured output response. Question type: which object is being estimated.
- **Domain 2, monetary/financial frame.** Actors: central bank, bond market, banks. Currency: policy rate, money, inflation, debt service. Anchor: the policy-rate path and the lower bound. Question type: a separate closure that can accommodate or offset.
- **Domain 3, open-economy frame.** Actors: foreign sector, trade partners, exchange rate. Currency: imports, exports, exchange rate. Anchor: trade and balance-of-payments data. Question type: leakage and regime.
- **Domain 4, identification/verification frame.** Actors: researchers, datasets, estimators. Currency: estimates and intervals. Anchor: the same measured response reached through distinct routes. Question type: independence and overlap.
- **Partition or union.** Union. Conditions overlap; a case in two is a compound with its own loop (Fig 9 :55).
- **Order by dependency.** Identify, name conditions, state estimand, choose estimator, carry. A meta-average above a broken identification link is work at a step that cannot use it (Fig 3 :20-21).

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. Where the primary derived from Fig 1 and Fig 9, I derived from the arity/symmetry description and Fig 3's dependency chain. Different starting figures, same domain boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually makes.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19, :22, :28 (slot indexing), Fig 9 :54-58 (union), Fig 2 :29-30, :31-32 (anchor and independence), Fig 3 :20-21 (dependency).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the answer files: the multiplier is per slot, the conditions form a union, the anchor is a measurement, study agreement is weighted by independence.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a policy table with one row per regime and country, each row backed by an identified spending change, a measured response, the conditions of the slot and its identifying route.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down without an extra rule:

- which specific conditions occupy the slots (the figures name none; they must be enumerated and provenanced from the economics frame);
- how to weight overlapping conditions in a compound (Fig 9 says unbundle, not how to combine; that needs a model and a ground);
- how to decide that two estimates are independent (Fig 2 gives the definition, not a test for a given pair of datasets).

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a global average is the wrong object, and the conditions must be unbundled before estimates are compared. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists in this simulated run; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the figures would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
