## What the wider literature bears on the question

The economics sources are **secondary evidence about the slots**, not outputs of the figures. Their agreement is informative only after overlap is recorded:

- Ramey 2011, Auerbach and Gorodnichenko 2011/2012, IMF WP 12/86 and Gechert and Rannenberg 2018 draw on overlapping post-war OECD and US evidence. They are one pool with several voices, not four independent witnesses. Their convergence on "larger in downturns" is expected given shared data and shared methodology.
- House, Proebsting and Tesar 2017 uses a distinct episode (post-2010 austerity) and a distinct DSGE structure. It is a lower-overlap witness, though it still studies advanced economies.
- Silberzahn et al. 2018 (psychology) and Anoke et al. 2019 (biostatistics) are genuinely low-overlap fields. They corroborate the *shape* claim, not the economic content: analytic choices on one dataset produce wide spread, and a population mean can obscure systematic subgroup heterogeneity. Neither says anything about fiscal policy.

No world source conflicts with the figure verdict. The conflict rule was not triggered. A conflict would send the derivation back to the domain census, not flip the verdict.

## Distribution rules: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-23** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending a full framework before a recipient has described the residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the per-slot claim and its refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :52** - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* A reader who has absorbed the framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see in their country and episode before giving them the sources.
- **Seat Map :163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A list selected for people likely to agree manufactures the correlation that would make their agreement worthless.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is a wrong policy number, so independence matters; seek readers whose data and identification differ from the ones already cited.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and its three refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and what they would expect to see; (3) only then the links, with the note that the figures and the data, not this summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Residual, shape claim, refuter. Record the verdict before showing theory.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in.** If curious, offer the source links. Later agreement is not pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional.** Offer the method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Hard precondition: stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 shape survives. It is a shape claim, so it survives only if the refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 is actually run. Until then it is `candidate`.
