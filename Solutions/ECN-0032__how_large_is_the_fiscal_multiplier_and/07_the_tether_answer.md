# The Tether's answer on the fiscal multiplier

**Mode: `general` for the structural shape, `specified` for the per-slot and union claims. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading. No number is supplied.

## 1. The residual it answers

The observed pattern is that a unit of government purchases is reported as producing different output responses in recessions, expansions and different countries. Predicted "one constant `k`" versus observed response, per slot, is the residual. The question "how large is the multiplier?" treats that residual as noise around a constant; Fig 1 treats it as the object.

## 2. The shape

1. **The multiplier is indexed by slot, not averaged.** Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** Fig 1 :22: **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."** The slots are state of the economy, country, and identifying route. Each component has its own anchor: an identified spending change and the measured output response.
2. **The variation is a union compound.** Fig 9 :54: **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."** Recession, high debt, a binding lower bound and openness overlap. A case in two conditions is a specific compound (Fig 9 :57-58), not a harder case of one.
3. **The anchor is a measurement, not consensus.** Fig 2 :29-30: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."** Agreement among studies is not such a measurement.
4. **Independence decides what agreement means.** Fig 2 :31-32: **"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."** Estimates that reuse the same data pool are one witness.
5. **Identification is upstream of estimation.** Fig 3 :20-21: **"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."**
6. **The framework returns no coefficient.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The published estimates are secondary evidence about the slots, with provenance recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`. They are not figure outputs.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the multiplier is 1", or 1.5, or 2. The figures do not select a number, and the world estimates are not pooled into one.
- Not "recessions have multiplier X and expansions Y" as a figure-derived law. The figure supplies the union structure; the values are empirical per slot.
- Not "monetary accommodation causes a larger multiplier" as a derivation. That is a hypothesis from the economics frame, with its own ground to settle it.
- Not that study consensus settles the question. Fig 2 :35: **"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."**
- Not grounded. No measurement was taken here.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A slot is settled by an identified change in purchases plus the measured output response for that slot, with the conditions of the slot recorded. Specific instruments already visible in the literature:

- real-time forecast controls that purge predictable components of spending (Auerbach and Gorodnichenko);
- narrative or quasi-experimental shocks, where the timing is independent of the cycle;
- cross-country comparisons that differ in one condition while holding the others visible;
- subgroup/heterogeneity methods that refuse to substitute the population mean for the compound-specific effect.

A source-discriminating instrument is what is owed, not another average. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*; *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

## 5. Pre-registered refuter (carried from `01`, written before the conclusion)

1. A single constant multiplier predicts held-out output response as well as or better than a slot-indexed vector.
2. One condition, with no compound interactions, accounts for all cross-slot variation with no residual structure.
3. Two estimates from the same dataset and same identifying assumptions are counted as independent witnesses and that changes the verdict.
4. A figure line above is shown to govern a different quantity (strain that survives re-census).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, the result is `candidate`.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The figures are the wrong instrument for a number; the empirical literature is the right instrument but must settle each slot separately and must record its overlap. The framework stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Prestige, citation counts and agreement are not instruments (Seat Map :163: *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."*).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not supply a number for the fiscal multiplier, and it does not average the literature into one. It says the multiplier is a residual indexed by slot: state of the economy, country, and identifying route, with each component anchored to its own measured output response. Recession, expansion and country form a union of overlapping conditions, so a case in two conditions is a specific compound that must be unbundled, not a harder case of one. Agreement among studies counts only in proportion to their independence; estimates that reuse the same data are one witness. Identification comes before estimation in the dependency chain. What the framework offers is the shape any answer must have, three falsifiers written in advance, and the kinds of instrument that would settle each slot. The number stays open until the ground pays for it.
