## Independent domain census (written from the residual line before reading the primary's conclusions)

I redid the census from the question restated in effect terms, using the WATSON order: R by arity, symmetry and scale; one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (arity / symmetry / scale).** The residual is the bundle "are platforms natural monopolies, and if so should they be regulated as utilities". Arity: the question pretends to two, but the opened DMA source lists five forces behind gatekeeper position and the primary found four residual conditions. So a **union compound**, not a binary chain. Symmetry: a cost predicate is ordered (one below the sum over a partition); a coordination predicate is self-fulfilling and multiple-valued; a policy predicate is a form distinction. Scale: first order at a market slice for cost and coordination; second order for policy.
- **Domain 1, cost structure.** Actors: incumbent, entrants, cost function, demand. Currency: long-run average cost and subadditivity. Anchor: a cost measurement under a demand not moved by expectations. Threshold question. Anchor nameable, so a domain.
- **Domain 2, demand coordination.** Actors: users on each side, expectations, multihoming and switching decisions. Currency: utility as a function of compatible users. Anchor: adoption and tipping under stated multihoming conditions. Multiple equilibria with a signed cycle.
- **Domain 3, habitat and channel.** Actors: adjacent platforms, innovators, data and interoperability conditions, regulator. Currency: contact and reach. Anchor: the market outcome that pays. Union with the others.
- **Domain 4, policy form.** Actors: regulator, platform, business users, end users. Currency: rules and outcomes decided. Anchor: the ground the rule keeps reachable, never agreement. Partition into convention and outcome-fixing verdict, then union inside the convention class.

Order by dependency: range cost and coordination in parallel; let habitat consume both; let policy consume all three. The question's "if so" is a dependency marker, not an inference. Fig 3 :20 - *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance."*

My census matches the primary's four domains and its union reading. I derived the union from the DMA source's five-force list and from Fig 9 :55-56; the primary derived it from Fig 9 :4-5 and the opened economics definitions. Different starting points, same boundaries: low overlap between routes, so the agreement is weak but not circular within the framework. It is still one evidence pool.

## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured: *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :4-5 and :55-56 (union, split), Fig 1 :19 (per-slot residual), Fig 2 :29-30 (anchor), Fig 10 :45-48 (convention versus outcome-fixing), Fig 12 :125-130 (two currencies).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand in `07`: four conditions, per-slice verdicts, shape not predicate, two currencies, two regulatory forms, gap on "should".
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** the question as actually posed and as usually answered in policy debate: "yes, platforms are natural monopolies, regulate them as utilities" or the mirror "no, they are competitive, leave them alone".

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **union structure** is flattened into a binary chain with a conditional arrow;
- the **per-slot indexing** is aggregated into a category verdict, which Fig 1 :22 says destroys the live signal;
- the **two-currencies distinction** is collapsed, so tipping is read as a cost advantage;
- the **convention/outcome-fixing distinction** is erased, so "utility regulation" arrives as one undifferentiated lever.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a rule that decides the outcome has stopped being a convention, and a concentration reading cannot comment on its source. Those are the residues worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating the formula as identical to the law set would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
