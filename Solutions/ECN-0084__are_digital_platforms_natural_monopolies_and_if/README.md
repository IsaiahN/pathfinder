## Restatement in effect terms

The question is not one question with a conditional arrow. It is a bundle. Asked literally it commits the speaker to: one predicate ("natural monopoly") applied to one category ("digital platforms"), one inference from that predicate to a policy form ("public utility regulation"), and one answer for every member of the category. Each of those three commitments is a separate claim with a separate anchor. Fig 9 :4-5: *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The derivation splits before it answers.

The two halves are ordered by dependency, not by logic alone: the regulatory question presupposes a settled answer about what is to be regulated, but settling the cost question does not settle the conduct question, because demand-side coordination and habitat conditions intervene between them (Fig 3 :19-20). The "if so" is therefore a dependency marker, not a valid inference.

## What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term

Before the run, the sought-for shape is written down and bound so it can accrue a residual like anything else. An answer to this question would have to be:

1. **Per slot, not per category.** A verdict for a named market slice (search, app distribution, social networking, cloud, marketplace), with the condition under which it holds, because Fig 1 :19 says *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*
2. **Effect-characterised, not label-characterised.** It would say what would have to be measured for the cost-shape name to apply, not just repeat the name. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
3. **Split on the regulatory side.** It would distinguish a rule that keeps a non-updating ground reachable and remains a convention from a rule that decides the outcome and stops being one (Fig 10 :47).
4. **Honest about its own status.** It would state mode, write its refuter before the conclusion, and stop at the limit of the instruments.

Anything that returns a single "yes" or "no" for all digital platforms, or that derives a welfare verdict from a cost label, fails this binding and is not accepted as an answer.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.

The reasons are figure-grounded, not legalistic only. Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*. Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Selecting for agreement manufactures the correlation that voids the verdict, and showing the interior before asking for a check spends the only independence the checker had.
