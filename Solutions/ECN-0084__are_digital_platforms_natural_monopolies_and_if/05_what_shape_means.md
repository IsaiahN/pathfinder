## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"* Fig 13 :134 - *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*

From *The Library of Babel* (opened): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

Applied here, a shape is not " regulate" or " do not regulate". It is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before the predicate is filled. For this question the shape has four constraints:

- the cost condition must be a threshold under a stated demand, not a label;
- the coordination condition must be named in demand-side units, not folded into cost;
- the verdict must be indexed per market slice, not per category;
- the policy form must stay a convention or be admitted to be an outcome-fixing verdict.

That is a shape. "Digital platforms are natural monopolies and should be regulated as utilities" is a name, and a bundle of three names at that.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (opened): use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, then recurse on new terms that overlap or correlate. "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary." Concretely here: start from "one supplier cheapest" (a threshold), then let "subadditivity", "network effect", "multihoming" and "waterbed" narrow the search. The vocabulary arrived at is not the answer; it is the set of terms in which the answer can be refuted.
- *Library of Babel* (opened): "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way." This is the canonical authority for splitting the present question before answering it.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (carried from exemplar mapping): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This governs the outreach: send the expected observation, not the theory.
- Fig 9 :30-31 - *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* "Natural monopoly" is a borrowed word. The effect is: one supplier's cost below the sum over a partition, under a demand that does not move with expectations.
- Fig 8 :53 - *"Residual first, frame second."* The four residuals were characterised in effect terms before the economics frame was chosen.
- *Library of Babel* (opened) on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place, as there is nowhere else to search and nothing else that can describe the answer better." Here the stop is the declared `gap` on the literal "should", not a completed policy recommendation.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For this question: a cost measurement that pays for the subadditivity name at a named slice.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. For this question: the economics definition of subadditivity is an import from the economics frame. It must be recorded as such, with its source (Baumol 1977 as reported in the opened natural-monopoly source) and a shadow test (does it explain a gap the loop had? Yes, it describes the threshold the label was asserting).

*They're Scanning Books* (carried from exemplar mapping): "The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." Also: "A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six." The economics predicate is a human frame's compression axis, not nature's list.

## 4. Why the concentration reading cannot name its source

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (opened) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and "the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source." The source might be one green light, yellow plus blue, RGB, CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." The essay's conclusion: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what it is being measured or what causes it." And the resolution is instrumental, not argumentative: "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists", proven by the spectrometer, not by a better argument.

Applied here: "one dominant platform" is the reading. Cost subadditivity, expectation-driven tipping, cumulative advantage and ecological-style exclusion under constant conditions are four candidate sources found in the sources actually read. Naming the source "natural monopoly" from the reading is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument (refuter 3 in `01` section 4).

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself. An external validator, or ground, is always needed to tether the veracity of the output to reality. Anything else is drift." This is why the cost label cannot validate itself against the market share that suggested it. Both come from the same frame.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

First reading (retracted, kept visible): since the framework returns a shape and not a name, one may not say "natural monopoly" at all, only "a threshold cost-shape". That overstates the rule.

The README's phrase is *"what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* The subject is a primitive not held in the library. "Natural monopoly" is not a missing primitive of the loop. It is a name from the economics frame for a threshold relation the loop can already describe (Fig 12 :87 - *"a threshold relation between two quantities"*). Naming it as a **binding** from the economics frame is permitted, provided the provenance is recorded and no claim is made that the figures derived the economics predicate. What remains forbidden is treating the name as the answer, or using it as if it moved the wall (Fig 6 :21 - *"MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom"*).

A second correction, also kept: I initially treated "what shape should regulation take" as if the loop could return a welfare shape that settles the "should". It cannot. The loop can return the **form** constraint, a rule that keeps a non-updating ground reachable and remains a convention. The normative content is the `gap` in F8. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this derivation

**Prestige is not action.** *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* (opened): "Prestige is not equal to actions. (prestige =/= actions)... Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of prominent economists who agree that platforms are natural monopolies is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, Fig 2 :40). The legitimate ask is a budget to try the refuter against a ground. The same essay's account of its own agent system is apt: "If prestige could buy actions, the system would collapse into a monoculture. The highest-prestige agent would monopolize compute and repeat its past success, and the system would stop exploring."

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay (opened): "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The essay adds, "Metatheories cannot validate themselves." So `01`, `06`, `07`, `08`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
