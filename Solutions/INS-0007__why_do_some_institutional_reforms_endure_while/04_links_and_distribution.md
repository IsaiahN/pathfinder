## Two things in the wider literature that bear directly

1. **Form versus function.** Bridges and Woolcock (2018) report that nearly 70 percent of project indicators in their sample measured new laws, procedures and structures rather than whether government does anything differently. That is exactly the figure's "recording carried upward" failure (Fig 4 :25), arrived at from development practice rather than from the framework. It predicts that a reform can survive on paper while its channel to the ground is dead.
2. **Legacy after the originator leaves.** Laland, Matthews and Feldman (2016), following Jones, Lawton and Shachak (1994), describe modifications that outlast the organisms that made them. The figure's habitat line, "Introduction is available; exclusion is not" (Fig 11 :61), is a metalaw; the ecology literature is a low-overlap witness for the same shape, not a source for it.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture before anyone has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :35** "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." A send selected for people likely to agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost of being wrong here is a wrong theory of institutional durability applied to real reforms, so independent checkers are worth buying with a careful, two-stage approach.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the question "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record the verdict; (4) only then the three public links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.
