# The Tether's answer on institutional reform endurance

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the rules applied, not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6).

## 1. The residual it answers

Some reforms keep being obeyed and resourced after the government that made them leaves office; others are repealed, defunded, unenforced or hollowed out. The formal text does not sort them. The residual is the gap between a text-centred prediction and that observation. It is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."), so repeal, defunding, unenforcement and reinterpretation each get their own loop.

## 2. The shape, per domain

**D2, implementation and contact. Triangulated.** Fig 10 :24 "the ground does not decay, the channel does" and Fig 11 :40 "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." Outcomes do not stop existing when a minister is replaced; the reporting, inspection, budget and correction that keep the reform in contact with them can stop. Reversal at handover is therefore primarily a channel failure. It is invisible from inside because, as Fig 2 :34 says, "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."

**D3, constituency and stake. Triangulated.** Fig 11 :39 "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" and Fig 11 :61 "Introduction is available; exclusion is not." A reform can add an agency, a budget and a constituency. It cannot remove the informal habitat it lands in, so endurance is not a solved state: Fig 11 :63 "Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state." Fig 12 :121 adds "cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction." A reform whose operation reproduces its own defenders closes the loop; a reform that ends at enactment cannot.

**D4, norm and narrative. Triangulated.** Fig 10 :18 "a declared meaning is not a grounded one". If what the reform is for is defined by the incoming government's story, the reference point moves with the government. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." A narrative does not qualify.

**D1, formal text. Single-source, mostly negative.** Fig 4 :25 "A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion." A statute transplanted without a method carries a description of one occasion, not a way to re-establish contact. D1 has no anchor of its own; it is a recording carried by D2 and D3.

**The slot stays open.** Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". The figures do not name the arrangement that occupies the channel. Independent courts, entrenchment, veto points, broad coalitions and infrastructure lock-in are bindings from the world literature, not derivations. The derived claim is the **shape** those bindings would have to have.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named institution is the answer. No mechanism is certified by the figures.
- Not that the institutional-reform literature is refuted. North, Pierson, Jacobs and Weaver, Tsebelis and Acemoglu are secondary corroboration and share one evidence pool (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that durability is one property. Repeal, defunding, unenforcement and reinterpretation remain separate compounds.
- Not that a reform can be made permanent. "A managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state" (Fig 11 :63).
- Not `grounded`. No cross-country measurement has been taken here.

## 4. What would settle it: the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28): a color instrument "cannot comment on the source", and the spectrometer resolved it. Here:

- **Contact discriminator:** for each reform, does an independent measure of continued operation separate "repeal" from "still functioning but unfunded" from "funded but unenforced"? Without it, endurance is a metamerism: several causes read as one green.
- **Channel test:** hold the formal text constant and vary whether the outcome channel is maintained. If endurance does not track the channel, F1 breaks.
- **Closure test:** track whether the reform's operation creates or regenerates the constituency that defends it. If reform and reversal do not differ on this, F7 breaks.
- **Standing refuters from §3b of `01`:** a no-channel reform that consistently endures; a full-feature reform that consistently reverses; a single lever that erases all subtype differences; a bare text that reproduces itself in a different habitat.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The habitat must be enumerated from the world, not composed from the theory (Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed."). Fig 11 :48: "You do not invent the list. You read it off the world, and what you cannot perceive or measure yet is the residual." The derivation stops at the limit of what the available literature and instruments can distinguish, records the lineage, and does not fill the slot with a favourite. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name the institution that makes a reform last, and it does not claim the institutional-reform literature is wrong. It says that a reform is not a text but a rule plus a habitat: the outcome that does not move when the government does, the channel of measurement and enforcement that keeps the rule in contact with it, and the constituency and agency whose stake survives the original sponsors. When that channel is stopped at a handover, the rule drifts while everyone inside the new government experiences the reversal as common sense. When the reform's own operation regenerates the support that maintains it, the loop closes and the rule outlives its authors. The formal statute is a recording carried by those conditions, not a generator of them. What the derivation offers is that shape, four domain verdicts kept separate, and refuters written in advance. The concrete institutional arrangements are bindings from the research literature, provenanced as such, and the slot stays open until a ground settles it.
