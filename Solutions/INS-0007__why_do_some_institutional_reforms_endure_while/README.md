## What an answer would look like (bound as an ordinary term)

An answer is a checkable property P such that, across many reforms, P predicts whether a reform keeps operating after the office that enacted it is replaced, and such that P can be refused by a counter-case. The figures return a **shape**, not a name: P must be a maintained channel to a ground that does not move with political power, plus an introduced habitat of actors whose stake survives the original sponsors. Which concrete institution fills that slot is a binding from the world frame, provenanced as such, not a derivation.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the answer.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons: Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."; Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"; Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the disqualifying correlation, and sending the theory before the verdict spends the only independence a reader contributes. Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions.
