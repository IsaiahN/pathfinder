# What "shape" means for this question

Canonical reading from the eight essays and the figures README. All sources below were opened this session.

## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 13 :135: "It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel* (Isaiah N, 2026-10-02): "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

For INS-0007 a **shape** is therefore: a reform endures when it has a maintained channel to a ground that does not move with political power, an introduced habitat whose stake outlives the original sponsors, and a loop whose operation regenerates its support. A **name** would be: "an independent constitutional court", "a federal veto point", "a universal beneficiary coalition". Those are bindings from the institutional-reform literature onto the shape. The figures do not derive them.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, then look for "recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it" and recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (Isaiah N, 2026-11-10): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place" and "you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it".

Applied here: the residual was described in effect terms before any field was chosen. The field (institutional reform research) was selected because its literature touches that residual, not because it was expected to confirm a preferred answer (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground." The README: the framework "stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (Isaiah N, 2026-08-28) gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and "cannot comment on the source". Several underlying causes can give the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: observing that a reform "survived" is a reading. Repeal avoided, budget maintained, behaviour unchanged, and narrative continuity can all produce the same reading. Naming one cause from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a discriminator, not a name.

## 4. Why the literature cannot hand us the name

Fig 2 :40: "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". The institutional-reform literature draws on a shared canon (Arthur, David, North, Pierson, Mahoney, Thelen). Agreement within it is one witness. The essays give the same warning at scale: *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* notes that "Communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice", and *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* states "You may all agree and be wrong... You need ground truth to define where you stand."

Two low-overlap fields were therefore searched: evolutionary ecology (niche construction, ecosystem engineering legacies) and infrastructure engineering (lock-in). They do not tell us which institution to build. They do corroborate the abstract shape, that persistence is a property of an engineered habitat and its maintained legacies, from grounds that do not share the political-science canon.

## 5. Correction to my own §3

I initially wrote that the "shape, not a name" rule forbids naming any institution at all. That overstates it. The README's phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)". Its subject is a primitive not held in the library. Naming a concrete arrangement from the world frame is a binding, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming the figures named it, or treating the binding as an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world.").

Both readings are preserved here: the rule governs missing primitives; the institutional names are world-frame bindings with provenance. Neither is silently erased.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of eminent endorsees for the shape claim would be the named collapse, not evidence.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The architecture article adds that outputs "could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and `09_watson_answer.md` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency. They are not tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.
