One thing survives cleanly: the unit of analysis is rule plus habitat, not rule alone, and contact must be maintained. That is the residue worth sending. Treating "the formula is a condensed version of the figures" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
