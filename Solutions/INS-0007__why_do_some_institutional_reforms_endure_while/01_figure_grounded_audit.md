## 0. The claim under audit, stated in effect terms

> Why do some institutional reforms endure while others are reversed after political power changes hands?

**Residual R (effect form).** The observed quantity is whether a rule keeps being obeyed and resourced after the office that enacted it is occupied by different people. The current frame predicts that the formal text largely decides this. The residual is the gap between that prediction and the observation: some formally identical reforms persist, some are repealed or hollowed out, and the text alone does not sort them. We do not yet know which property P closes the gap.

**What an answer must do.** Name P in effect terms, state which domains P belongs to, keep the verdicts separate, and say what would refute P before the conclusion. Per `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": make the sought-for shape an ordinary term by writing down what an answer would look like, then let it accrue a residual like anything else. The sought-for shape is bound in the README as "a maintained channel to a ground that does not move with political power, plus an introduced habitat with a stake that survives the sponsors".

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.**
- **Arity:** several conditions, not one. A reform is simultaneously a text, an enforcement routine, a budget line, a constituency, and an intelligible norm (Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."). Union, not partition.
- **Symmetry:** the handover is ordered. `→` is sequence, "A then B; B needs A to have happened" (Fig 12 :70). Old office holders lose the seat, new ones gain it. Endurance and reversal are not symmetric outcomes of one symmetric lever.
- **Scale:** a change at the scale of the occupants (a government) is being compared with persistence at the scale of the institution (a rule that outlasts occupants) and the scale of the regime (the set of rules that decides whether reversal is even possible). Fig 10 :68: "Different actors, a different currency, a different ground, and a lossy transform between."

## 3. Findings, with verbatim figure lines and per-finding refuters

### F1 The termination event is a channel failure, not a ground failure (D2, triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: **"the ground does not decay, the channel does"**

Fig 10 :27-28: **"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"**

Fig 11 :40: **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Nothing in the figures says the outcome a reform was meant to affect stops existing when the government changes. What can stop is the channel by which the reform is kept in contact with that outcome: reporting, inspection, budget, correction. When the seat that maintained the channel is replaced and nobody maintains it, the reform drifts while looking normal. This is the figure's named failure, not a metaphor.

**Per-finding refuter:** show, in a documented case, a reform that lost all independent contact with its outcome (no measurement, no enforcement, no correction) yet continued to change that outcome in the intended direction after the handover. That breaks Fig 10 :24 and Fig 11 :40 for this domain. Mode: `specified`.

### F2 Endurance is a property of the reform plus its habitat, not of the reform text (D2 and D3, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"**

Fig 11 :73: **"The habitat is not the ground. The habitat is where actors come from and it updates; the ground is what settles, and it does not."**

Fig 11 :43: **"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."**

A reform cannot be made durable by improving the rule in isolation. The relevant unit is the rule together with the actors, conditions and relations in contact with it. This is why a census must list the habitat before naming a mechanism (Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit").

**Per-finding refuter:** a controlled comparison in which the rule text is held constant, the surrounding habitat is varied at random, and endurance is statistically independent of the habitat. That breaks the capability claim for this domain. Mode: `specified`.

### F3 Introduction is available, exclusion is not, so endurance is permanent work (D3, single-source for the figure line)

Fig 11 :61: **"Introduction is available; exclusion is not."**

Fig 11 :63: **"Which is why a managed ecosystem is permanent work rather than a solved state."**

A reform can add a constituency, an agency, a budget, a reporting duty. It cannot remove the habitat it finds itself in: informal norms, existing interests, and the contact that other actors already have with the residual. So an enduring reform is not a solved state but a maintained one. The new government does not have to defeat the old reform; it only has to stop maintaining the channel, and exclusion does the rest.

**Per-finding refuter:** exhibit a reform that permanently removed a hostile actor or an informal norm from its habitat by decree alone, with contact unchanged. That breaks Fig 11 :61 for this domain. Mode: `general` for the rule, `specified` for its application.

### F4 A reform anchored to the office holder is a frame, not an anchor (D4, triangulated)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."**

Fig 2 :34: **"the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices."**

Fig 10 :18: **"a declared meaning is not a grounded one"**

If what a reform is for is defined by the incoming government's narrative, the reference point moves with the government. The reversal then looks principled from inside, which is exactly why it is invisible there. A declared norm does not ground itself; it must be capable of being violated and checked (Fig 10 :19 "install what can be violated").

**Per-finding refuter:** a case where the same reform is interpreted identically by successive governments with opposed ideologies and the interpretation is shown to be fixed by a check neither can move. That breaks the drift finding for D4. Mode: `specified`.

### F5 A formal text by itself is a recording, not a generator (D1, single-source)

Fig 4 :22: **"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."**

Fig 4 :25: **"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."**

A statute copied from one jurisdiction into another is a description of one occasion. It carries no method for re-establishing contact in the new habitat. This is the figure's rule about what crosses a scale boundary, applied to an institutional transplant. The transplant is not a new term; it adds no atom (Fig 6 :25 "An import adds no atom to the world.").

**Per-finding refuter:** a case where a bare legal text, with no accompanying method, no trained staff and no created constituency, reliably produced the same behaviour in a different habitat. That breaks Fig 4 :25 for this domain. Mode: `specified`.

### F6 The handover is a union compound and must be unbundled (census finding, general)

Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."**

Fig 9 :57: **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either. It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

Fig 3 :19: **"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."**

"Reversal" is not one outcome. A reform can be repealed, defunded, unenforced, reinterpreted, or simply stop being used. These are different compounds with different levers. Effort spent on the statute (where measurements are easy) cannot rescue a broken enforcement or vocabulary link.

**Per-finding refuter:** a single lever demonstrably reverses every subtype at once across many cases, with no residual difference between them. That breaks the union reading. Mode: `general` for the figure rule, `specified` for the institutional application.

### F7 The self-reinforcing loop is a cycle whose sign decides whether it closes (D3, single-source for the figure line)

Fig 12 :121: **"cycle. The output re-enters as input. The sign of the loop decides whether it closes, and a chain cannot close by construction."**

Fig 12 :119: **"catalyst. A part that survives its own use. Spent and regenerating parts behave differently across uses, and nothing records which a term is."**

A reform whose operation creates the constituency that then defends the reform is a cycle: output re-enters as input. A reform that is enacted, implemented and then forgotten is a chain and cannot close. The catalyst line describes a standing agency or budget that is regenerated by its own use; this is an analogue, not a direct institutional law.

**Per-finding refuter:** a reform that persistently reproduces its own defenders yet is reversed at every handover, or a reform that never reproduces defenders yet never reverses. Either breaks the cycle reading. Mode: `specified`, single-source.

### F8 Agreement among researchers is not a witness for this answer (D5, triangulated)

Fig 7 :13: **"same closure, handed along; agreement compounds, evidence does not"**

Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**

Fig 2 :42: **"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."**

The institutional-reform literature is one evidence pool: the same canon of North, Pierson, Arthur, David and Mahoney is read by all of its members. Agreement inside it is the pool talking. The low-overlap corroboration comes from ecology (niche construction and ecosystem engineering legacies) and infrastructure engineering (lock-in), which reach similar shapes from different contact with their own grounds. Even there, agreement supports the shape only; it never sets the verdict.

**Per-finding refuter:** two genuinely independent instruments, drawing on disjoint evidence and with no shared canon, that converge on a different shape for the same residual. That would send the census back to step 1. Mode: `general`.

## 3b. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Required before any verdict, per the seat-map rule recorded in `THE_SEAT_MAP_Field_Guide_amended.md`: "a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device." It is stated here before §4 and §7.

The figure-derived shape is **refuted** if any of the following fires:

1. **Contact test.** A reform with no maintained channel to a non-moving ground and no introduced stake consistently endures across many handovers. This breaks Fig 10 :24 and Fig 11 :40.
2. **Closure test.** A reform with all four figure features (independent ground contact, maintained channel, introduced stake, closed loop) is consistently reversed. This breaks F1 to F3 and F7 together.
3. **Union test.** A comparison in which lever-level measures explain all of the variance in endurance with no residual difference between repeal, defunding, unenforcement and reinterpretation. This breaks Fig 9 :55-57.
4. **Text test.** A bare legal text, with no method and no constituency, reliably reproduces its behaviour in a different habitat. This breaks Fig 4 :25.
5. **Census test.** A genuine re-census finds a different domain with its own anchor that the figures govern directly and that makes the channel/habitat lines strain.

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn and the census is rerun. Until one is run, nothing here is `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Lead with the residual and the refuter, not with a theory of institutions (Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source.").
2. Keep the four domain verdicts separate. Do not average "legal design", "implementation", "constituency" and "norm" into one score (Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated.").
3. Unbundle "reversal" into repeal, defunding, unenforcement and reinterpretation before searching (Fig 9 :55-57).
4. For each reform, enumerate the habitat first: who acts on it, under what conditions, through what relations (Fig 11 :43-48).
5. Treat a proposed mechanism as a binding from the world frame with a provenance record, never as a name the figures produced (Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself").
6. Send the generator: the shape claim, the census rule and the falsifiers. Do not send a recording of one successful reform (Fig 4 :25).

## 7. Verdict of the audit

The figures close the residual at the level of **shape**, not at the level of a named institutional design. The sound verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate: endurance tracks maintained contact with a ground that does not move with political power, an introduced habitat with a stake that survives the original sponsors, and a loop whose operation regenerates its own support. Reversal is what happens when a reform stays a recording or a declared meaning whose only reference point is the office that can be lost. The four domain verdicts are kept apart and not averaged. There is one live limit: the figures cannot name which concrete arrangement occupies the channel slot. That is recorded as `gap`, not filled with a favourite from the literature. No figure line strained after re-census. See `09_watson_answer.md` for the canonical WATSON shape and `11_audit.md` for the independent second seat.
