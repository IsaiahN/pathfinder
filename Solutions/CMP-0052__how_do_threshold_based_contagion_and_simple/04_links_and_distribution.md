## What the wider literature adds, and where it conflicts

1. **The interaction already has a name in the field, but not a single settled law.** Min and San Miguel unify both rules in one family with heterogeneous adoptability and report a mixed phase and a double transition. Andres et al. classify three scenarios. This corroborates F2 (union compound) and corroborates it for a low-overlap reason: one is an annealed random-graph model, the other a temporal-network model.
2. **The order is upstream to downstream.** Both Min and Andres describe simple transmission governing early and triggering the threshold process. This corroborates F3.
3. **The aggregate cannot identify the bond.** The exact mapping in Hebert-Dufresne et al. is the strongest external check on F5. It is a different closure (well-mixed mean field) from the figures, so it is not circular with the framework.
4. **A genuine strain, declared.** The world literature writes simple contagion as the theta = 1 limit of a single model family (Min: theta = 1 recovers SIR). The figures write them as two different bonds. Both are recorded; the tension is resolved by scope (outcome-level collapse versus record-level typing), not by erasing either. See `01` section 6 and `11_audit.md`.
