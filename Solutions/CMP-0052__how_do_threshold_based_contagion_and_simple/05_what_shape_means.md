## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name it

The canonical figures README says the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (**a shape, not a name**)."* Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134 says it can report that the answer is out of reach, but *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."* And the operational rule: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For CMP-0052 a **shape** is therefore: two typed bonds over one substrate, simple transmission upstream of the threshold comparison, a union with a binding mixed region, and residual indexed per node. It is **not**: a value of θ, a value of λ, or a claim that a particular observed cascade was simple or threshold. Those are names, and the ground, not the shape, supplies them.

## 2. How the shape was obtained

- *Library of Babel* on vocabulary by proximity: *"You should then be able to connect and correlate combinations of the existence of your known words and their relative proximity to commonly recurring new terms that you are unfamiliar with."* And: *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Here the known terms were "exposure", "threshold", "cascade"; the recurring new terms were "complex contagion", "adoptability", "mixed phase", "double transition".
- *Library of Babel* on bundles: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* The residual was split on exactly this hint: one-exposure-suffices and reinforcement-required are two conditions, not one knob.
- Fig 9 :27 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." The effect was described first (what each rule does to a node's exposure count), then the competing-contagion frame was sought.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"the finish line will also be the starting place"* and *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is the point where the bond distinction becomes an ordinary term with a refuter, not a search for the true θ.

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the second: *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* And the standing caveat: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

So the six bond types and the two-rule distinction are visible choices, not readings off nature. A claim that "the threshold is really a comparison bond" is a binding from the framework onto the contagion frame, and must be provenanced as such.

## 4. Why this question is the source-discrimination case, not a naming case

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it.

Map that onto CMP-0052. The population-level incidence curve is the reading. The source is the bond (simple, threshold, or interacting contagions). Hébert-Dufresne, Scarpino and Young (2020) supply the published analogue: interacting simple contagions and social reinforcement give identical solutions in a well-mixed population. Reading the bond off the curve is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, per-node and co-infection event data, not a better name.

Fig 8 :51 says the same thing structurally: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* The essay and the figure are low-overlap routes to one verdict.

## 5. Q for this question: does "shape, not a name" forbid saying which bond?

No, and the reason matters. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in Γ. The two bonds here, disjunction ("either suffices; not both required", Fig 12 :62) and comparison ("a threshold relation between two quantities", Fig 12 :87), are **already in the operator set**. Naming which bond a rule uses is a binding onto a held operator, not the invention of a new one. What remains forbidden is:

- inventing a third contagion mechanism to make the fit look better;
- claiming the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 :21 "MINT composes inside the closure and can never add an atom");
- claiming the framework returned the value of θ or identified the source of a real cascade from its curve.

Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost: the bond binding is allowed and provenanced; the source naming is refused until an instrument discriminates.

## 6. Two consequences for the answer and for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And the precise rule: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous contagion modellers selected for likely agreement is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, per-node event data or a co-infection record, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"The catch is that the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* And: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong."* So `01`, `06`, `07`, `09` and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim, and they cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. Where the essays and the world sources meet

*They're Scanning Books* says the escape from a fixed representation is a new axis, not more terms inside the old one. That is exactly what the competing-contagion literature supplies here: Min and San Miguel add heterogeneous adoptability and a transmission probability, Andres et al. add time-varying interactions, and both thereby represent a mixed regime that a single-λ model cannot express. This is `general` structural agreement, not `grounded`: the new axis is in the model family, and whether it matches a real system is a question for the ground.
