## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency, and anchor. Partition or union, then order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A monetary authority must name a quantity and a threshold. Arity: three coupled conditions (price stability, real activity, debt and expectation dynamics), so a union compound, not one hard question. Fig 9 :54 says "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." Symmetry: a target is a one-sided threshold comparison of an observed quantity against an intended value, not a symmetric join; Operators table :48 defines comparison as "a threshold relation between two quantities." Scale: first order at the instrument and index, second order at the regime and expectation geometry.
- **Domain 1, measurement frame.** Actors: statistical agencies, the CPI or HICP basket, index weights. Currency: measured price change. Anchor: the collected prices, a counter that does not move because a frame thinks. Legitimate only for "what did the basket do", not "is this the right basket" (Fig 2 :36-38).
- **Domain 2, policy frame.** Actors: central bank, government, committee, reaction function. Currency: the target comparison and instrument setting. Anchor: realized outcomes, which do not move because a number is announced. Without a nameable anchor this is a frame, not a ground.
- **Domain 3, expectation and contract habitat.** Actors: households, firms, lenders, borrowers, wage bargainers. Currency: expectations, indexation, contract duration. Anchor: observed behavior, for example whether spending is postponed or wages indexed.
- **Domain 4, verification / outreach frame.** Actors: outside researchers, public submission venues. Currency: residuals and checkable records. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so their agreement is the pool talking, not corroboration.
- **Partition or union.** Domains 2 and 3 form a union around R; Domain 1 is a partition boundary fixing what the number is a number of. A single rate lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (measure a price change) already works. Vocabulary (make-up, bygones, headroom) is partial. The objective link (the target itself) is the under-instrumented middle link. Planning and learn-and-carry follow. Fig 3 :19: "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."

Strain test: an early reading pressed Fig 13 :75 into forbidding zero inflation directly. That strains a line governing composition of the six primitives. Re-census moved the claim to F6 as a `specified` structural prediction about a spendable gradient, corroborated by the zero-bound record.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

No universal number is settled. The figures yield a shape: a target is a comparison over a subtraction, so the slot comes before the number; a nominal target is a falsifiable convention, not the anchor, so realized outcomes settle it; valence is relative to the live residual, so no number wins everywhere; where the residual is debt deflation or a liquidity trap, low positive inflation with headroom is the figure-consistent branch and zero runs the spendable gradient down at the boundary; where the residual is inflation bias and credibility, a commitment-shaped rule is the figure-consistent branch provided the habitat changes with the number; rate and level rules are isomers whose bond is make-up; and every regime is a revisable handoff. The number stays open until a ground reading settles it. `general` on the shape, `specified` on the branches, not `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is presenting a convention as an anchor or a figure-derived number, which would spend the independence of outside readers. Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost of a wrong target regime is high, so independence is worth buying; a consensus-based verdict would be the expensive mistake.
- **undoable:** yes. The conditional branches are revisable without touching the shape. A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet, and no primitive must be withdrawn. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room." A regime that declared itself final would be the failure.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A slot-by-slot instrument that identifies the live residual would select the branch; a two-signed zero-inflation path would break the headroom prediction; observational equivalence of rate and level rules after a miss would break the isomer claim; a nominal target that is a genuine anchor would break the convention verdict. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
