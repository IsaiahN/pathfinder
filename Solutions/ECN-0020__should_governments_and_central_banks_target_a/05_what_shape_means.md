## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical usage rule (`FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`) states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :135: *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."* Fig 13 :137-138: *"And it can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands, so something has to be imported before it is asked again."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question:

- **A shape:** "a target must be a falsifiable convention anchored in outcomes, fitted to a named residual, and headroom-preserving where the residual is debt deflation or a liquidity trap."
- **A name (not delivered):** "the target should be 2 percent," or "0 percent," or "NGDP at 5 percent."

Naming a number is a binding from the policy frame onto the shape; it is not the framework's output.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* For monetary policy this means describing what would count as success (no debt spiral, no postponement of spending, stable expectations) before picking the index.
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: the searcher's own question is *"the one false misstep"* guard, *"This anchor is provided by your own question."* The equivalent here is the residual statement, which is why outreach leads with the residual and not the framework.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What would license a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For a monetary target that means realized outcomes, not the announcement.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A number borrowed from peer central banks is an import with a source, and Fig 6 :24 prices it: *"Import from a live peer is debited against that frame's independence. A wrong atom costs more than starting from primitives."*

The six-term set itself is, in the words carried into the exemplar, *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"*; declaring it makes the choice visible, not neutral. The same caution applies to a target basket: it is a visible choice of what to measure.

## 4. The source error applied to monetary targets

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. A color instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*: one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

Applied here: a headline CPI reading of 2 percent is a projection. Several sources can produce it: a balanced expansion, a commodity-price spike with weak demand, a base effect after a deep slump, or a re-indexation of the basket. The reading does not determine the source. What is owed is a better instrument (a slot-by-slot residual, a debt-service series, an expectation-distribution measure), not a declaration that 2 percent *is* price stability. This is the metamerism error named in F2 and F3 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.

## 5. Q: Does "shape, not a name" forbid discussing a target number? ANSWERED, with a correction to my own first reading

**First reading (retracted here):** "The framework returns a shape, not a name, therefore no target number may be discussed." This overstated the rule. The README's phrase governs a *missing* thing, a primitive not held in the frame. A target number is not a primitive of the six-term substrate; it is a binding inside the policy frame, a convention that compares an observable to an intended value. Discussing and testing such a binding is allowed. What is forbidden is presenting it as figure-derived, or treating the convention as the anchor.

**Correction:** the prohibition applies to inventing a seventh term or to claiming a declaration grounds itself (Fig 10 :18, :49). It does not apply to evaluating a policy convention against outcomes. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost. Triangulated (canonical README + Fig 10 + Fig 2 :29-33).

## 6. Prestige is not action, applied to central banking

From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* Also: *"You may all agree and be wrong. You may set your clock to someone else's, who was setting your clock to yours, and be off by many deviations over time. You need ground truth to define where you stand."*

The prestige of the institution announcing a target, or the number of institutions that adopted it, gives the policy a budget to be tried. It does not settle whether the policy is right. A committee of central bankers that all read the same research is the mutual-update collapse of Fig 2, panel 1: they converge on whoever had the most influence, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices. This is the figure-grounded reason `03_public_contact_points.csv` contains no personal emails and no receptiveness rank.

## 7. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of any monetary regime and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 8. What "shape" means, in one paragraph for this question

A shape here is the set of constraints any defensible target must satisfy, stated before a number is chosen: it is a comparison over a subtraction, so the slot matters; it is a convention, not an anchor, so outcomes must do the settling; its valence is relative to the live residual, so there is no universal number; where the residual is debt deflation or a liquidity trap, it must preserve headroom; a rate and a level are isomers, so make-up is a real structural difference; and it is a revisable handoff, not a last room. The framework returns that shape and stops. A number is owed only by a ground reading, a provenanced import with a shadow test, or an explicit binding recorded as our own contribution.
