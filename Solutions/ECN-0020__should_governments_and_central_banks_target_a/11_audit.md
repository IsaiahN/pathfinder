- **Domain 2, policy.** Actors: bank, government, committee. Currency: comparison and instrument. Anchor: realized outcomes. Without that anchor this is a frame, not a ground.
- **Domain 3, habitat.** Actors: households, firms, lenders, wage bargainers. Currency: expectations and contracts. Anchor: observed behavior (postponement, indexation).
- **Domain 4, verification.** Actors: outside researchers. Currency: residuals and records. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Independence is zero in this simulated run because all seats use one pool.

Order by dependency: describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle the measurement link before the objective link (Fig 3 :19-20); settle first-order comparisons before treating them as atoms at second order (Fig 12 :104).
