## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about AI assistance and human judgment. Option A: treat "AI improves judgment" as one claim and answer it with one lever (make the aid better). Option B: split it into Q1 (aided accuracy against the ground per task slot) and Q2 (unaided capability on held-out tasks after withdrawal), and give a shape with conditions under which each moves. What each leads to: A averages away the live signal and can spend the person's independence without noticing, B gives two checkable quantities, five pre-registered refuters and a held-out instrument that would close the question.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor; partition or union; order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The divergence between Q1 and Q2. Arity: two quantities over task slots. Symmetry: broken, because the aid is a different closure and not a mirror of the person. Scale: Q1 is per task and immediate; Q2 is across tasks and over time after withdrawal. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :4 splits it as a union: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **Domain 1, the judgment task.** Actors: human judge, AI system, the thing judged. Currency: error against the ground per slot. Anchor: the ground; for questions constitutively about people, human judgment itself (Fig 2 :36-37). Nameable: yes. Type: partition boundary, which compositions count as contact with this ground.
- **Domain 2, the person's own skill and understanding.** Actors: the person's held terms, bonds, memory, attention. Currency: can produce a new judgment versus replay a recorded one; held-out unaided residual. Anchor: unaided performance with feedback after withdrawal. Nameable: yes. Type: maintained habitat, introduction available and exclusion not.
- **Domain 3, design and verification.** Actors: designers, experimenters, users, public venues. Currency: public record, provenance, independence. Anchor: the ground, never agreement (Fig 2 :35, :40). Nameable: yes. Type: union of overlap conditions; unbundle before searching.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the bundled claim does not. AI assistance improves judgment when it is an instrument for the axis being judged and changes contact with the ground, leaving a re-applicable method; for questions about people, human judgment is the anchor and AI is an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. It weakens people's own skills and understanding when it substitutes the person's habitat with an answer to replay, so the loss stays invisible until the goal fails, and what crosses to the person is a recording rather than a route. The three domain verdicts are `triangulated`, kept separate and never averaged. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable if the refuters are run. The costly error is spending the person's independence without noticing: relying on an aid that is right on the measured axis and wrong on an unmeasured one, then being unable to detect the failure when it arrives. Fig 11 :68 says what was failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails. Fig 2 :42 *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost is avoidable with a held-out unaided test at multiple time points.
- **undoable:** yes. Introduction is available and exclusion is not (Fig 11 :61-62), so reintroduce unaided practice with feedback and make the method explicit at handoff. No term has to be deleted and no primitive withdrawn; a refusal only removes a term's standing to be composed over.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Specifically: a preregistered randomized trial showing unaided Q2 after answer-only assistance indistinguishable from a no-assistance control while Q1 improved; or an answer-only aid improving Q2 on genuinely new tasks; or an AI instrument wrong on an unmeasured axis settling a person-level question as well as human judgment; or Q1 and Q2 moving together on every slot. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
