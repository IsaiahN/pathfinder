## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Fig 13 :134-135: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence, It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

For this question a shape is the narrowed form of an answer we can state before we can name a single best tool. It constrains; it does not fill. The shape here has three parts:

1. contact with the ground is changed by the aid, not merely simulated by the aid's fluency;
2. what crosses to the person is a re-applicable method, not a recording of one occasion;
3. aided accuracy and unaided capability are read as two quantities on different slots.

A product name would be a name, not a shape. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."*

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30 *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31 *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the outreach packet asks what the recipient would expect to see if the shape were true, and why the first contact carries an effect description rather than the framework's vocabulary.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"You will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then you have *"already composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

Applied here: I described the residual in effect terms (two quantities that can diverge) before searching any field. Had I searched "AI and judgment" first, any literature would have seemed to fit.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"SETTLE the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* A preregistered trial with held-out unaided tests would settle Q2.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. A named result imported from the human-AI literature is a binding, and must be recorded as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator problem. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; several paths produce the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. The analogue here: a user's improved output does not discriminate between "the aid changed my contact" and "the aid substituted my habitat." Both produce the same reading. What is owed is a held-out unaided test, not a name.

## 4. Why "the AI made me better" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* again: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* Self-report of improvement is a reading, not a source discrimination. Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* explains why text and fluent output cannot hand over understanding: writing carries context and some subtext, almost never pretext, and *"the understanding has to be rebuilt by the learner, and the way it gets rebuilt is contact: trying, being corrected, and trying again, in both directions."* It adds that *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves"* (Fig 11 :40, quoted inside the essay). An AI answer that the person never has to defend rebuilds nothing.

The world literature corroborates from outside the framework: Lee et al. (CHI 2025) found that confidence in GenAI predicted less critical thinking; Glickman and Sharot (2025) found people often unaware of the AI's influence. Both are readings that cannot comment on the source of the person's improvement.

## 5. Q for this question - ANSWERED

**What is the shape of a correct answer to "when does AI assistance improve human judgment?"** Not a list of tools, and not a single threshold. The shape is a two-condition conjunction read per slot:

- condition A, contact: assistance changes the person's relation to the ground (extends the instrument, imports a provenanced atom, or lowers the cost of the next search) rather than substituting a habitat;
- condition B, reapplication: what crosses to the person is a method they can carry to a new case;
- with Q1 and Q2 measured separately.

**When does it weaken own skills or understanding?** When condition A fails by substitution and condition B fails by replay. Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 4 :25-26: a replayed recording *"produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one."*

**Does the framework license naming a winning product?** No. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*. Product-level verdicts are bindings from the world frame and carry zero framework weight until a ground settles them with a held-out test.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Someone let prestige stand in for proof... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A campaign that gathers famous names to agree that AI helps is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask gives the claim a budget to be tried and lets a held-out ground settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output"* and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. The four essays I cite are also by the framework author, so their agreement with the figures is expected and carries little independent weight. The world sources in `09_watson_answer.md` are the only low-overlap part of the evidence, and they corroborate; they do not decide.
