# Union, order and analogues for the AI-assistance question

**Mode: `specified`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures, not a ground reading (see `05_what_shape_means.md` section 6).

## RESIDUAL

The headline question bundles two quantities: aided accuracy Q1 and unaided capability Q2. Two supporting derivations are owed: (1) is this a partition or a union, and what order does the work have to follow; (2) are the aided and unaided states the same substance under a different bond, or different substances, and what do the analogues license.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

Atoms held: a person Γ, a task slot s, a ground o', an aid A, an import (the aid's answer), a method (a re-applicable bond), a recording (a one-occasion answer), a habitat (the set of actors in contact with the person). Bonds held: sequence `→`, comparison `⋛`, production `⇒`, conjunction `+`, subtraction `-`. No new primitive is available without an import with a source gate (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :51-52).

## DERIVATION

### Q1 - Partition or union?

Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55-56 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."*

The "AI improves judgment" claim is a union of two conditions: the aid is accurate on the axis, and the person's own contact survives. Giving a more accurate aid clears the first condition everywhere and leaves the second binding in the intersection. That is why Q1 gains and Q2 losses can coexist. Fig 9 :57 *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

**Falsifier, written before the run:** if a single lever (aid accuracy) is found to move Q1 and Q2 in the same direction on every slot, the union reading fails and the problem is a partition after all.

### Q2 - What order does the work follow?

Fig 3 :20 *"The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance. Each link consumes what the one above produces, so effort spent above the break arrives at a step that cannot use it."*

The order here is:

1. **Objective (link 3):** say what counts as progress for Q1 and for Q2 in each domain's own terms. This is the missing link. Fig 3 :27 *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* A held-out unaided test with feedback is the instrument for Q2.
2. **Refuse before mint (Formula step 2):** the term "AI improves judgment" is refused as written because it merges two quantities. A refusal adds a term and removes nothing; the reach falls because something was added.
3. **Describe the residual before naming a frame (Fig 9 :53 *"Nobody chooses. The gap does, and the gap is only as good as its description."*):** effect terms first, literature second.
4. **Settle Q1 and Q2 separately, then compose:** a head start crosses to the person (Fig 4 :24) only after both are settled. Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world, and there is no bottom"* permits composition over settled terms and not before.

Effort spent on model accuracy (above the break) cannot rescue a missing Q2 instrument. That is the dependency claim, not an importance ranking.

### Q3 - Are aided and unaided judgment isomers?

Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

Write the two states over the held atom set:

- aided state: `{person, task, ground, aid, import}`
- unaided state: `{person, task, ground, method, practice}`

As written they do not share the full atom set, so they are **not isomers** by Fig 12's definition. They are two members of a **functional group**: both are a person reaching a ground through a structure, Fig 12 :124 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* The group is `person - structure - ground`; the qualifier is which structure mediates.

The stronger version: if both states are written over the full shared set `{person, task, ground, aid, import, method, practice, habitat}`, differing only in which structure mediates and whether contact is preserved, then they **are** Fig 12 isomers: same atoms, different arrangement, different substance. That is the checkable structural claim, and it is the one worth taking to an expert.

**Falsifier:** if an aided state can be written without an atom the unaided state requires, or vice versa, the isomer claim fails and the functional-group reading stands. A case where the aid introduces a primitive the person could never hold (an import that moves the wall) is such a failure and should be recorded as a genuine difference, not smoothed into the shared set.

### Q4 - What do the analogues license, and where do they stop?

- **Apprenticeship** (*Decoding Tacit Knowledge*, read 2026-11-24): the learner rebuilds understanding by acting and being corrected. This licenses the claim that a method with feedback changes contact. It does not license any claim about AI specifically.
- **Extended mind** (Clark and Chalmers 1998, via the Wikipedia article read 2026-11-24): an external item counts only if constantly accessible, automatically endorsed and playing the same role as an internal process. This licenses "the aid can be part of the capability" only under coupling. Adams and Aizawa's "cognitive bloating" objection is the limit; mere availability is not enough.
- **Test harness / isolation** (Fig 11 :70 *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*): a benchmark success with the aid proves the wiring of the benchmark, not the person's capability. This licenses the demand for held-out unaided tests.
- **Automation (Bainbridge 1983; Endsley and Kiris 1995):** automating most work removes practice. This licenses the weakening half as a known failure genus, not as an inevitable law for every aid.

Fig 8 :53 *"Residual first, frame second."* These analogues were selected after the residual was described in effect terms; they were not used to decide what the residual was.

## CONCLUSION

The question is a union compound, not one hard question. The dependency order starts at the missing Q2 instrument, not at aid accuracy. Aided and unaided states are Fig 12 functional groups as commonly written, and a real isomer claim only over the full shared atom set. The analogues support the contact and method conditions under coupling and stop short of any product-level verdict. No new primitive is needed, and the shape costs no import that moves the wall.
