# The Tether's answer: when AI assistance helps and when it weakens

**Mode: `specified` on the substantive claims, `general` on the structural rule that capability belongs to agent-and-habitat. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles two quantities that can move in opposite directions: aided accuracy Q1 and the person's own unaided capability Q2. The residual is their divergence. Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 9 :4 *"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."* The compound is a union; a single lever cannot clear both conditions.

## 2. The shape, per domain, never averaged

**Domain 1, the aided judgment.** AI assistance improves judgment when the aid is an instrument for the axis the question is about and changes the person's contact with the ground. Fig 2 :36-37 *"Human judgment is the right anchor for is this a good answer for a person, because the question is about people. It is only an instrument for is this code correct, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure."* For person-level questions the human is the anchor, not because humans are unbiased but because the question is about people. An aid that is accurate on its measured axis improves judgment (Glickman and Sharot found exactly this); an aid biased on an unmeasured axis amplifies error. Fig 6 :23 *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* **Verdict: `triangulated`.**

**Domain 2, the person's own skill and understanding.** Assistance improves own capability when it leaves a re-applicable method and keeps a feedback channel to the ground; it weakens own capability when it substitutes the person's habitat with a recording. Fig 4 :22 *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Fig 11 :66 *"Isolation is not removal of the habitat. It is substitution of one habitat for another."* Fig 11 :68 *"what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails"*. A replayed recording *"produces a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one"* (Fig 4 :25-26). Fig 12 :111 *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* **Verdict: `triangulated`.**

**Domain 3, design and verification.** A claim that assistance helps is not settled by user agreement, literature consensus, or the aid's own confidence. Fig 7 :35 *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Fig 9 :60 *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Fig 8 :51-52 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* **Verdict: `triangulated`.**

The three verdicts are kept separate. No average is taken across them.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any named product, vendor, or interface pattern is good or bad. That is a binding from the world frame, unproven until a ground settles it.
- Not that humans are more accurate than AI. The anchor claim is constitutive for person-level questions, not an accuracy ranking. World evidence of human bias is recorded in `01` section 6.
- Not that all AI use deskills. Deskilling is the failure of the substitution path, not a law over every aid.
- Not that confidence or self-report measures either quantity. Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- Not a single universal threshold or tool. Fig 9 :32 *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*.
- Not `grounded`. No held-out unaided trial was run here.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A preregistered, randomized design with a held-out unaided test at multiple time points, and with the two quantities reported per task slot:

1. **Q1:** aided error against a ground that neither the person nor the aid can move.
2. **Q2:** unaided error after withdrawal, on tasks the aid never saw, with feedback.
3. **Condition A test:** aid that supplies a re-applicable method with feedback versus aid that supplies only a finished answer, versus no aid. If the method arm preserves Q2 and the answer-only arm does not, the contact/substitution claim is paid.
4. **Anchor test:** for person-level questions, compare AI-instrument advice against human judgment on axes the aid does not measure. If the instrument settles such questions as well as the anchor, Fig 2 :36-37 is broken.
5. **Standing refuters:** all five in `01` section 4. Write them before the run.

Fig 4 :30 *"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."* The aid-to-no-aid round trip is the measurement that is currently missing.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish. We can state the two conditions and the failure mode; we cannot name a winning tool or a universal threshold. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"*. The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable, and the world sources are marked as corroboration from partly overlapping pools, not as independent witnesses for the figures. ## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning AI tool. It says that "AI improves judgment" bundles two quantities that can move apart: accuracy with the aid, and the person's own ability after the aid is withdrawn. Assistance improves judgment when the aid is an instrument for the axis being judged and changes the person's contact with the ground; for questions about people, human judgment is the anchor and the AI is only an instrument, and instruments can be wrong on axes they do not measure. It weakens own skill when it substitutes the person's task habitat with an answer to replay, because what was failed to reproduce stays invisible until the goal fails, and a replayed recording can repeat a past success but cannot produce a new one. What leaves with the person must be a method, not a recording. The framework offers the shape, the two quantities measured separately, the pre-registered refuters, and the held-out test that would close it. The verdict is `specified`, not `grounded`, and no tool is named.
