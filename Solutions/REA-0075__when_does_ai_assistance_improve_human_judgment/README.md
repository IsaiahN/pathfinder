## The question restated as an effect

What is being decided is not the quality of an AI tool in the abstract. Two quantities move under assistance and they can move in opposite directions:

1. The aided judgment's residual against the ground, `R_aided = |Γ_h+AI(b,a) - o'|`, measured per task slot.
2. The person's own unaided reach after the aid is withdrawn, measured on held-out tasks against the same ground.

A single number such as "productivity with AI" merges them. That merge is the named error: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19) and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* (Fig 1 :22)

- Option A, "AI assistance improves judgment full stop": predicts that one lever (more capable assistance) moves both quantities the same way.
- Option B, "assistance changes the human-and-habitat system, and the two quantities must be read separately": predicts that the aided residual can shrink while the person's unaided reach falls, and that one lever cannot clear both conditions at once.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term before the run (Formula, "Direction"): a **shape** giving the two conditions that must hold for assistance to improve judgment without substituting the person's own contact with the ground, plus an instrument that would discriminate a gain in aided accuracy from a loss of unaided capability. Until that shape is bound it predicts nothing and no check in the loop can see it.

The verdicts recorded in `09_watson_answer.md` are Option B in `specified` mode, kept per domain and never averaged.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this derivation is `grounded`, no claim that it settles the question, and no claim that the framework is the only true account of human-AI judgment.

Three figure-grounded reasons, plus one practical one:

1. A room is not an anchor. *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* (Fig 7 :35) and *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35). Gathering agreeable readers manufactures the correlation that disqualifies a verdict.
2. Correlation has valence. *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). A list selected for receptiveness is one witness wearing many names.
3. A verifier must be outside the interior. *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The first contact therefore carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework.
4. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets the sending domain blacklisted. Public opt-in routes avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
