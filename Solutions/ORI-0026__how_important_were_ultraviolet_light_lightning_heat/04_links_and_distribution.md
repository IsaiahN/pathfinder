## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A shape plus a refuter is a method. A league table is a recording of one occasion. Lead with the method.
- Fig 10 :42 "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the whole framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before sending the links.
- Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." Do not ask for agreement.
- Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool." Do not pool later agreement as corroboration.
- Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." The cost here is a wrong ranking of an energy source that shaped a generation of experiments, so independent frames are worth the trouble.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual and the shape claim; (2) the pre-registered refuter and the request "what would you count as a failure?"; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then, if they are curious, the three evidence links, with the note that the figure text, not this summary, is the authority; (5) only then the method.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is the check. The recipient tests the shape claim without holding the framework's interior. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction and offers the links. Stage 3 is opt-in and offers the formula as a method, with the instruction to state a ground and a refuter for each new problem. Nothing is sent by this agent. Hosting and sending are the user's to do. Prefer an opt-in download page over an attachment pushed to a list.
