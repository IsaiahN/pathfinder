# The Tether's answer on prebiotic energy sources

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the substrate distinction. Not `grounded`.** Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks how important UV light, lightning, heat and chemical gradients were as energy sources. The residual is not a missing count of joules. It is the failure to say which input, present or absent, changes which synthetic outcome in which setting. The four arrive as a flat list. Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording." The Tether answers the shape of that failure, and refuses the league table the question appears to request.

## 2. The shape

1. **Energy and gradient are different kinds.** Fig 13 :14 energy is "conserved, finite, directionless alone". Fig 13 :22 a gradient is "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :46 "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness." Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow." UV, lightning and heat are energy inputs; a chemical gradient is the spendable, directed structure. One ranking of all four is the flat formula of Fig 12 :5.
2. **Importance is a relation, not a property.** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." So importance is indexed by transition: feedstock supply, concentration and activation, monomer synthesis, polymerisation, compartment formation, and persistence. The same source changes valence when the residual changes. Todd et al. (2021) show UV both synthesising and destroying, which is exactly this two-valence case.
3. **Flux is a filter, not a verdict.** Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Deamer and Weber (2010) report solar UV flux at 24,000 against electrical discharges at 2.9 kilojoules per square metre per year, and also report that discharge and geothermal sources "represent a tiny fraction of the total energy flux" while the Miller-Urey discharge experiment "revolutionized origins of life research". Huge flux and decisive slot effect are different quantities.
4. **The bundle must be split.** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." A surface setting receives UV, lightning products, heat and mineral gradients at once. Their effects interact: heat moves equilibrium only when concentration changes (Deamer and Weber, 2010); gradients maintain disequilibrium (Westall et al., 2018); realistic UV changes the outcome relative to narrow-band lamps (Rimmer et al., 2021). Unbundle by transition and setting first.
5. **Order is dependency, not chronology.** Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Fig 12 :75 "A then B; B needs A to have happened" is the sequence bond, and the sources do not license a single historical sequence of the four candidates. What they license is a dependency chain of transitions, with each input mattering at the link it supplies.
6. **The numeric ranking is unreachable here, not silenced.** Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Per `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7, SEARCHED has run across the opened sources; INWARD is a better instrument such as the StarLab solar simulator; OUTWARD is a frame with a different closure such as vent electrochemistry or field sedimentology. The field contains a genuine conflict: Jackson (2017) disputes the work a natural pH gradient can do, while Westall et al. (2018) and Spitzer, Pielak and Poolman (2015) treat gradients as central. That conflict returns us to the per-slot census; it does not select a winner.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that UV was the most important source, nor lightning, nor heat, nor gradients. No winner is named.
- Not that the four are four species of one thing. They are three energy inputs and one spendable structure.
- Not that raw flux determines importance. Flux is the budget.
- Not that the four acted in a single historical order. The order claimed is dependency.
- Not that the conflict in the literature is settled. Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
- Not `grounded`. Per `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A discriminating per-slot measurement, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy". The corresponding questions here are:

- **Slot discrimination:** for monomer formation, activation, polymerisation and compartment formation, measure the change in outcome when each input is removed while the others are held fixed. A total flux meter cannot do this.
- **Source discrimination:** several energy histories can produce the same molecular yield, so a yield alone is a projection with lossy information. An instrument that separates photochemical from discharge-derived from thermally driven product signatures would name the source for a slot.
- **Gradient work test:** does a natural pH or redox gradient do chemical work beyond the reaction approaching equilibrium? Jackson (2017) says the vent-reactor reduction was "a simple consequence of that reaction approaching equilibrium in the absence of an external energy source". A reactor that distinguishes the two would settle that slot.
- **Realistic-spectrum test:** Rimmer et al. (2021) built a young-Sun simulator and found that narrow-band lamp results do not transfer. Per-slot rankings must be reproduced under realistic spectra before they are `grounded`.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6:** a global ground-settled ranking independent of slot; directive energy with no gradient; same-kind peer status for all four; one frame settling the weights; monotonic benefit of one input across all slots.

## 5. The honest stop

**Shape reached, weights unreached at this budget.** The figures govern what composes and how valence attaches. They do not report which input dominated on the early Earth, and this derivation has no ground reading. The canonical rule returns "a shape, not a name". *The Library of Babel*, opened this session, states the alternative failure precisely: "Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?" The bundle has been split; the per-slot work is owed to experiments and field readings. Fig 9 :32 "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself". Indiscernibility is not identity, and a stop with a recorded lineage is checkable.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank ultraviolet light, lightning, heat and chemical gradients against one another, because the list mixes kinds. Energy on its own is directionless; a chemical gradient is a difference that can be spent; energy and a gradient compose a flow. Importance is therefore not a property a source carries. It is a relation to a particular synthetic slot in a particular setting, and the same source can help one slot and destroy another. UV is both a synthesizer and a destroyer; heat shifts equilibrium only when it changes concentration; gradients maintain disequilibrium; and raw energy flux is a budget filter, not a verdict. What the figures give is the shape any defensible answer must have: split the bundle by transition and setting, index importance per slot, compose energy with a spendable gradient, and range two low-overlap frames against the ground. The actual weights are unreached at this budget, not silenced, and they will be settled by discriminating instruments rather than by a larger league table.
