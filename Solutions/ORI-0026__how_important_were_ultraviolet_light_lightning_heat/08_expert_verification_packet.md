# Expert verification packet: the Tether on prebiotic energy sources

**What this is:** a claim in a form a prebiotic chemist, geochemist or origins researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the substrate distinction. Not `grounded`.** No per-slot measurement was taken. This is a shape and a route, not a ranking.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The four candidates are not peers. UV light, lightning and heat are energy inputs, which the substrate calls directionless alone; a chemical gradient is a spendable, directed structure, and energy plus a gradient compose a flow. Importance is therefore not a standing property of a source but a relation to a synthetic slot in a habitat, so the same input can help one transition and destroy another. Raw flux is a budget filter, not a verdict. The question is a union compound that must be split by transition and setting before it is searched. A numeric cross-slot ranking is unreachable at this budget and is owed to discriminating instruments, not to the figures.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 13 and 12

- **Statement:** energy is directionless alone; a gradient is a difference that can be spent; the two compose a flow, so the four candidates are not same-kind peers and a single ranking is a flat formula.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 13 :14 "conserved, finite, directionless alone"; Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent"; Fig 13 :46 "What makes a gradient a gradient is that it can be spent, and neither an ordering nor a span of space has directedness."; Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is."; Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."
- **Refuter:** show that energy is directive on its own with no spendable gradient, or that a gradient is merely a quantity of energy, or that all four reduce to the same atom under different bonds. Any of these breaks the claim.

### C2 - Field-facing; checkable against the prebiotic chemistry literature

- **Statement:** importance is per synthetic slot and per habitat, so no global league table is well defined. The same source changes valence when the residual changes. Flux does not settle it.
- **What to check:** Deamer and Weber (2010) report solar UV flux at 24,000 against electrical discharges at 2.9 kJ m-2 yr-1 and state that discharge and geothermal sources "represent a tiny fraction of the total energy flux", while the Miller-Urey discharge still "revolutionized origins of life research". Todd, Szostak and Sasselov (2021) state that early-Earth UV energy was "comparable or greater than that from lightning and atmospheric shocks" and document UV as both synthetic and destructive. Westall et al. (2018) call gradients "a means of maintaining systems out of equilibrium". Does any source support a single cross-slot ranking?
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :49 "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."; Fig 11 :51 "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."; Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."; Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."
- **Refuter:** produce one ground-settled importance number that remains well defined without specifying a transition or a habitat, or show that importance increases monotonically with flux across all slots.

### C3 - world-facing; checkable against the dispute over gradients and UV realism

- **Statement:** the four inputs form a union compound with interacting and sometimes opposite effects, and the field contains a genuine low-overlap conflict that returns us to the per-slot census rather than selecting a winner.
- **What to check:** Jackson (2017) argues that the vent-reactor CO2 reduction was "a simple consequence of that reaction approaching equilibrium in the absence of an external energy source" and criticises the natural pH gradient hypothesis. Westall et al. (2018) and Spitzer, Pielak and Poolman (2015) treat gradients as central to maintaining disequilibrium. Rimmer et al. (2021) built a young-Sun simulator and found that "experimental support does not involve sources of UV light that look like the young Sun", so narrow-band lamp rankings do not transfer.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."; Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."; Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."
- **Refuter:** show that the four inputs act on disjoint slots so an additive ranking is legitimate, or that one frame settles the weights without a low-overlap second frame.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- No winner is named. Not UV, not lightning, not heat, not gradients.
- Not that the four are four species of one kind. Three are energy inputs and one is a spendable structure.
- Not that flux determines importance.
- Not a single historical order of the four candidates. The order claimed is dependency (Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance.").
- Not that the vent-gradient dispute is settled.
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here has been paid by a measurement.
- Not a claim that the two prebiotic route families of `06_isomers_and_order.md` are proven isomers. That is a `specified` hypothesis with a stated falsifier.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating per-slot instrument, not more agreement. *The Hard Problem is About Source*, opened this session: "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy".

1. Remove each input in turn while holding the others fixed and measure the change on a named transition. A total flux meter cannot do this.
2. Use source-discriminating product signatures so that a yield is no longer a lossy projection of several possible energy histories.
3. Test whether a natural pH or redox gradient does work beyond a reaction approaching equilibrium (Jackson, 2017, versus the vent literature).
4. Reproduce per-slot rankings under a realistic young-Sun spectrum, as in Rimmer et al. (2021), before calling any of them `grounded`.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 13 :14, :22, :46 and :76, four short passages. Decide whether the state/structure distinction is a fair reading of the substrate.
2. Check C2 against the literature you know: can you name any result that defines importance independently of a slot and a habitat? If not, the shape stands.
3. Pick the conflict you know best, the vent-gradient dispute or the UV-versus-narrow-band-lamp problem, and say which side has a discriminating instrument.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
