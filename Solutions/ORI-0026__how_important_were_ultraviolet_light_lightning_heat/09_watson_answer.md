## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the question "How important were ultraviolet light, lightning, heat, or chemical gradients as energy sources?" Option A: return a single global league table of the four candidates. Option B: unbundle the question, separate energy states from the spendable gradient structure, index importance per transition and habitat, treat flux as a filter, and leave the weights to the ground. What each leads to: A commits a flat-formula error and spends the independence of the people checking it; B returns a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest unreached stop.

Symmetry: not symmetric, because energy and gradient compose in one directed cross-kind bond and which state the gradient bonds to changes what it is (Fig 13 :76). Scale: local and per-slot, not planetary flux (Fig 1 :19). The residual is the failure to say which input changes which synthetic outcome in which setting.
- **Domain 1, composition and substrate, figure internal.** Actors: the six terms (space, time, energy as states; spectrum, gradient, network as structures) and the seven bonds. Currency: composition across kinds, then settlement. Anchor: the verbatim figures as metalaws, plus the ground for a measurement. Question type: partition, what composes legally.
- **Domain 2, habitat and geochemical setting.** Actors: conditions including light and temperature, mineral surfaces, vent fluids, atmosphere and tidal settings. Currency: contact with the residual, valence relative to the residual. Anchor: the measurable local environment (Fig 11 :46). Question type: enumerate, never compose.
- **Domain 3, per-slot residual and measurement.** Actors: object slots, meaning monomer formation, activation, polymerisation, compartment formation and persistence. Currency: residual R per slot. Anchor: experiment and geochemical record (Fig 2 :29). Question type: where importance is actually measured.
- **Domain 4, verification and triangulation.** Actors: expert readers and low-overlap frames, lab photochemistry versus vent geochemistry versus field sedimentology. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run every seat draws on one evidence pool, so its independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** D1 is a partition. D2 and D3 form a union compound: the four inputs overlap in one habitat and a single lever leaves the intersections standing (Fig 9 :55). D4 is the checking frame.
- **Order by dependency.** Settle composition first, then enumerate habitat, then measure slots, then triangulate. Fig 3 :20 "The chain is ordered by dependency, not by importance." Describe the residual before choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27-28).

Strain test applied: a line about energy abundance only fits if importance equals flux, which is a strain. Re-census moved the governing quantity to the cross-kind composition and the per-slot residual. No figure conflict remained after re-census; the world conflict is reported in WORLD.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape is reached; the ranking is not. The four candidates are not peers: energy inputs are directionless alone, a chemical gradient is a difference that can be spent, and energy plus a gradient compose a flow. Importance is a relation to a synthetic slot in a habitat, so no standing global league table is well defined. Flux is a budget filter. The question is a union compound that must be unbundled by transition and setting, and the order implicit in it is dependency, not chronology. A numeric cross-slot ranking is `unreachable at this budget`, which is a verdict and not a silence. Nothing here is `grounded`; the ground is a discriminating per-slot instrument, and the three framework essays are not independent of the figures.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, separates the four candidate kinds, and writes refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by asking them to endorse a league table or to trust a framework before checking the shape. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the per-slot shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The ranking was never asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn to change course. A future ground reading can add per-slot weights without touching the shape, and the union can be split further as new transitions are measured. No primitive is at risk.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A single ground-settled importance number that is well defined without naming a transition or habitat would break the per-slot claim; a demonstration that energy is directive without a gradient, or that all four are same-kind peers, would break the state/structure claim; a demonstration that the inputs act on disjoint slots would make an additive rank legitimate; one frame settling the weights would break the independence requirement; monotonic benefit of one input across all slots would break the union reading. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
