## 0. The question as a residual

The source asks: *How important were ultraviolet light, lightning, heat, or chemical gradients as energy sources?*

Stated in effect terms: R is the gap between what prebiotic chemistry can predict and what it observes. The failed prediction is not "which source carried the most joules". It is "which input, present or absent, changes which synthetic outcome". The four candidates arrive as a list. A list of ingredients is precisely what Figure 12 warns is not a molecule:

> Fig 12 :5 "A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."

So the first thing to decide is whether the four candidates are peers. Figure 13 says they are not. Three are energy, which is a state. One, the chemical gradient, is a structure. The question has bundled a state and a structure into one ranking. That is the residual.

What an answer would look like, written down and bound so it can be refused: *a per-slot, per-habitat ranking of energy inputs by how much each reduces the residual on that slot, composed with a spendable gradient into a flow, triangulated across low-overlap frames, with weights settled by the ground.* This is the shape term. It is refusable by section 6.

## 1. Describe R by arity, symmetry and scale

Fig 9 :21 to :22 gives the three descriptors. They are applied before any figure line is chosen.

- **Arity: mixed, four candidates of two different kinds.** UV light, lightning and heat are inputs of energy. Chemical gradients are a spendable difference. Fig 13 :14 "conserved, finite, directionless alone" describes energy. Fig 13 :22 "a difference that can be spent" describes the gradient. Arity 4 is therefore 3 states plus 1 structure, which is a union, not four instances of one kind.
- **Symmetry: not symmetric.** The relation is not "swap the candidates and nothing changes". Energy and gradient compose in one direction. Fig 13 :76 "Energy and a gradient compose a flow. A gradient over space and one over time are different terms, because which state it is bonded to is part of what it is." The bond is cross-kind and the operand matters, so the ordering in the question is already a claim.
- **Scale: local and per-slot, not global.** Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." The relevant slots are individual transitions: monomer synthesis, activation, polymerisation, compartment formation, and the local settings in which they occur. A planetary energy flux is a different scale from a slot residual.

**Strain test applied at the start:** a line that says energy is abundant would only fit the question if importance equalled flux. That is a strain. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." So flux is a filter, and the line governing importance must be found elsewhere. Re-census proceeds.

## 4. Findings F1 to F8

### F1 - The four candidates are not peers (governing finding, triangulated)

Fig 13 :14 calls energy "conserved, finite, directionless alone". Fig 13 :22 calls the gradient "a difference that can be spent". Fig 13 :46 adds that an ordering or a span of space has no directedness. So UV light, lightning and heat are energy inputs; a chemical gradient is the spendable, directed structure. Fig 13 :76 says they compose into a flow, and that which state a gradient bonds to changes what it is.

A single ranking of all four under one heading is therefore the flat formula Fig 12 :5 warns about: it records ingredients and not the bond. The honest response to "which of the four was most important" is that the question compares a state with a structure. Verdict: **triangulated**, Fig 13 plus Fig 12. Mode `general` for the distinction, `specified` for its application to these four candidates.

Refuter: show that energy is directive on its own, with no spendable gradient required, or show that a chemical gradient is just another quantity of energy and not a difference that can be spent. Either breaks the finding.

### F2 - Importance is per slot and per habitat, not a standing property (triangulated)

Fig 11 :49: "An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries." Fig 11 :51: "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual." Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."

So "importance" is not a property of UV or lightning or heat or a gradient considered alone. It is a relation between an input and a slot: monomer formation, activation, polymerisation, compartment formation. A source can be decisive for one slot and irrelevant or destructive for another. The question's global "how important" is an aggregation that throws away the signal. Verdict: **triangulated**, Fig 11 plus Fig 1. Mode `specified`.

Refuter: produce a single ground-settled importance number that is well defined independently of which transition and which habitat is meant. That breaks the finding.

### F3 - Flux is a filter, never a witness (single figure plus world corroboration)

The literature supplies raw fluxes, for example Deamer and Weber's table with solar UV at 24,000 and electrical discharges at 2.9 kilojoules per square metre per year, and Todd, Szostak and Sasselov's statement that early-Earth UV energy was "comparable or greater than that from lightning and atmospheric shocks". These are real and useful. Fig 9 :60 "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict." Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."

So flux orders the budget, not the verdict. A source with tiny flux can be decisive on a slot, and Deamer and Weber's own review makes exactly that point: the Miller-Urey discharge revolutionised the field even though discharge was a tiny fraction of total flux. Verdict: **triangulated** across Fig 9 and Fig 2 for the rule, `single-source` for any particular flux number, which remains world-corroborated only. Mode `specified`.

Refuter: show that importance is monotonically determined by energy flux across all slots. No source read here does.

### F4 - The question is a union compound and must be unbundled (triangulated)

Fig 11 :43 "The habitat is enumerated, never composed." Fig 11 :46 lists "conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain" as things to enumerate. Fig 9 :55 "A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones." Fig 9 :54 "when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."

A surface environment receives UV, lightning products, heat and mineral gradients together. Their effects are not additive: UV both synthesises and destroys (Todd et al., 2021), heat speeds approach to equilibrium but does not move it (Deamer and Weber, 2010), and a gradient maintains disequilibrium (Westall et al., 2018; Spitzer, Pielak and Poolman, 2015). The bundle has to be split by transition and setting before it is searched. Verdict: **triangulated**, Fig 11 plus Fig 9. Mode `specified`.

Refuter: show that the four inputs act independently on disjoint slots, in which case a partition and a single additive ranking would be legitimate.

### F5 - The actual weights are unreachable at this budget, not silenced (single-source for the stop)

Nothing in the figures names which input dominated which transition on the early Earth. The anchor for that is an experiment or a geochemical reading (Fig 2 :29). Fig 13 :134 "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." Fig 8 :39 "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Per `THE_FORMULA.md` step 7, the three questions stay distinct: SEARCHED has been run across six opened world sources; INWARD would sharpen instruments such as realistic-solar-UV simulators (Rimmer et al., 2021 built one and found the answer changes); OUTWARD would import a frame with a different closure, meaning lab photochemistry versus vent geochemistry versus field sedimentology. INWARD and OUTWARD are not read against one another.

Verdict: **gap on the numeric ranking**, **shape reached** on what a valid ranking must look like. Mode `specified`, status `candidate`. This is not an evasive silence: it names what would settle it.

Refuter: a ground reading that assigns and defends per-slot weights across the four candidates would move the ranking from `unreachable` to `grounded`.

### F6 - The field itself contains a genuine conflict, which returns us to the census (world source, not a figure conflict)

Westall et al. (2018) treat gradients as "a means of maintaining systems out of equilibrium" and as sites where dissipated energy can do work. Jackson (2017) argues that a natural pH gradient at alkaline vents was unlikely to have driven prebiotic chemistry at all, and that the "origin of life reactor" CO2 reduction was "a simple consequence of that reaction approaching equilibrium in the absence of an external energy source". Todd et al. (2021) and Rimmer et al. (2021) likewise show that UV is both synthetic and destructive, and that narrow-band laboratory lamps misrepresent the young Sun.

By the WATSON rule, a conflict sends us back to the domain census; it never flips a figure verdict. Re-census result: the conflict is between two world frames about which input drives which slot, exactly as F2 and F4 predict. It corroborates the shape. It does not select a winner. Verdict: **single-source** for each side, `triangulated` for the meta-claim that the question is per-slot and contested.

### F7 - The verification frame must be independent, and this audit is not (single-source)

Fig 2 :31 "Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence." Fig 2 :35 "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you." The six scientific sources span bioenergetics, geochemistry, photochemistry, photobiology, physical chemistry and bioenergetic critique. They are genuinely low overlap with one another. The three framework essays, however, share an author and an evidence pool with the figures, and the second seat in `11_audit.md` shares this whole pool with the primary. By Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool", their agreement is one witness, not corroboration. Verdict: **single-source / internal consistency**, stated honestly.

## 6. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per the seat-map discipline: a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim in this package is refuted if any of the following holds.

1. A single global importance ranking of UV light, lightning, heat and chemical gradients is shown to be well defined and ground settled independently of which transition and which habitat is meant. This breaks F2 and Fig 11 :49 to :51 and Fig 1 :19 to :22.
2. Energy is shown to be directive on its own, with no spendable gradient required, or a chemical gradient is shown to be merely a quantity of energy rather than a difference that can be spent. This breaks F1 and Fig 13 :14, :22, :46, :76.
3. The four candidates are shown to be same-kind peers under one composition, so that a flat list was the correct notation. This breaks F1 and Fig 12 :5.
4. One frame, one laboratory protocol or one field site is shown to settle the weights without a low-overlap second frame. This breaks F7 and Fig 2 :31 to :35.
5. Adding more of one input is shown to be monotonically beneficial across all slots, with no destructive second effect. This breaks F4 and the UV synthesis and destruction result of Todd et al. (2021).

If any fires, the shape is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of the ranking is `grounded`.

## 7. Recommendation

1. Do not answer with a single league table. Answer per transition and per setting: which input reduces which slot residual under which conditions.
2. Separate energy inputs from the spendable gradient they feed. Treat "energy plus gradient compose a flow" as the governing composition, not as a tie to be broken.
3. Use flux as a budget filter only. Record the source and uncertainty of every flux number.
4. Unbundle the union. Split monomer formation, activation, polymerisation and compartment formation, and split surface ponds, tidal zones and hydrothermal settings.
5. Triangulate across low-overlap frames. Lab photochemistry, vent reactor electrochemistry and field sedimentology must each range against the ground separately (Fig 2 :45, "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it.").
6. Keep the conflict visible. Jackson (2017) versus Westall et al. (2018) and Rimmer et al. (2021) is a feature of a live field, not an obstacle to be smoothed over.
7. Send the generator, not the recording. Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." A shape plus a refuter is a generator; a league table is a recording of one occasion.
