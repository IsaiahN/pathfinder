## What this package decides, in effect terms

The question asks for an importance ranking of four candidate energy inputs to prebiotic chemistry. Stated as an effect: given a residual R, namely the failure of any origin account to say which input reduces which synthetic failure, we want to know which candidate, present or absent, most reduces R. Two options lead to different places. Option A returns one global league table of the four candidates. Option B unbundles the question, separates energy states from the spendable gradient structure, indexes importance per synthetic slot and per habitat, uses flux only as a budget filter, and leaves the numeric weights to experiment. This package derives B from the figures and records why A is a flat-formula error.

What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: *a per-slot ranking of energy inputs by how much each reduces the residual on that slot, composed into a flow through a spendable gradient, triangulated across at least two low-overlap frames, with the weights settled by the ground.* That term is the sought-for shape, made refusable by the pre-registered refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6.

## Boundary and standing of this derivation

All figure quotes come from `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, read this session. No quote comes from a summary. The canonical usage rule is `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`, also read this session.

**This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*, opened 2026-11-23: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post. It will often be better than the generic outputs that you will usually get otherwise too (intelligence is essentially arrangement). However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." This package cannot settle the empirical question it reports on. It can only say what form a settled answer must have and what would refute that form.
