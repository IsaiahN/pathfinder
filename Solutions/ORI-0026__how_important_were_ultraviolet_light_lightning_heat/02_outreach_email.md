# Outreach draft: check the shape before you decide about the ranking

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four candidates are unbundled in the recipient's own terms. The output must survive a refutation attempt on contact, or the two-stage offer collapses.

---

**Subject:** One checkable output on prebiotic energy sources, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with anything. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The question as I stated it to myself:** how important were ultraviolet light, lightning, heat and chemical gradients as energy sources for prebiotic chemistry? The effect I am trying to reduce is not a missing joule count. It is this: which input, present or absent, changes which synthetic outcome, in which setting?
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`):** the four items are not peers. UV, lightning and heat are energy inputs. A chemical gradient is a spendable difference. In the six-term substrate, energy is "conserved, finite, directionless alone"; a gradient is "a difference that can be spent"; and "Energy and a gradient compose a flow". That means a single league table of all four repeats an ingredient list where a bond was owed. It also means importance cannot be a standing property of a source. It is a relation to a particular slot: monomer formation, activation, polymerisation, or compartment formation, in a particular habitat. Raw flux is a budget filter, not a verdict. UV is both synthetic and destructive, so more of it is not monotonically better.
>
> **What this does not say:** it does not say UV was more important than lightning, or that hydrothermal vents win. It does not name a winner at all. It says what form a defensible answer would have, and that the weights are owed to experiments and field readings, not to a derivation.
>
> **What would refute it, written down before I sent this:**
> 1. A single, ground-settled importance ranking that is well defined without saying which transition or which habitat is meant.
> 2. A demonstration that energy is directive on its own, with no spendable gradient required, or that a gradient is only a quantity of energy.
> 3. A demonstration that the four inputs act independently on disjoint slots, so an additive ranking is legitimate.
> 4. A demonstration that one frame, one lab protocol or one field site settles the weights without a low-overlap second frame.
>
> If any one holds, I will retract the shape. A one-line "this fails because..." is far more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary of them, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract in my working folder): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - Bioenergetics and Life's Origins, Deamer and Weber, 2010: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2828274
> - A Hydrothermal-Sedimentary Context for the Origin of Life, Westall et al., 2018: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5867533
> - Timescales for Prebiotic Photochemistry Under Realistic Surface Ultraviolet Conditions, Rimmer et al., 2021: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8570677
>
> **If you want the method:** the figure set and the eight-step formula are public. Run them on a problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> Thanks,
> [name]

## Two-stage rule

Stage 1 records the verdict on the shape and its refuter. Stage 2, opt-in only, offers the links. Stage 3, opt-in only, offers the formula as a method. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration, because Fig 2 :40 "agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool".
