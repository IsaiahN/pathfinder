## Wider literature that bears on the question

- **SEP Information** establishes that the field itself does not have one notion of information: Shannon (probability), Kolmogorov (computation), Floridi (truth) "have next to nothing in common." This corroborates the union unbundling (F7) but, being a world source, does not create the verdict.
- **Wikipedia Information theory** gives the symmetry fact that makes F2 concrete: mutual information is symmetric, directed information is not.
- **Biosemiotics and ISBS** give the non-human domain (D4 by analogy), where meaning is relational and organism-relative. This corroborates the agent-and-habitat analogue (F6) but is secondary.
- **arXiv engineering papers** show two operational treatments of "semantic": as a geometric displacement in an embedding space (Fulbright) and as classification labels chosen for a task (Sagduyu et al.). Both are bindings onto held atoms, not new primitives (Fig 6 :22: "NOVEL means novel relative to atoms, not to the world").
- **The four essays** supply the canonical reading of "shape," in `05_what_shape_means.md`.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 4 :22** "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Send the derivation and the refuter, not a finished definition.
- **Fig 10 :42** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the framework.
- **Fig 2 :40** "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool". Agreement from a mailing list selected for interest is not corroboration.
- **Fig 10 :52** "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the unbundled shape claim and its refuter; (2) ask which of the three domains they are addressing and what they would count as failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then send the figure text and the SEP link, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1 before stage 2.** Fig 10 :52 and Fig 2 :45 "Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it." The recipient checks the shape without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2 is opt-in by construction.** Once a recipient has absorbed the theory, their agreement stops being evidence. Their stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3 sends a method.** Fig 4 :22. The formula is a generator, so giving it away does not give away an answer. Each user must write their own refuter and state their own mode, because "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives its pre-registered refuters. Until those are run, the packet is a candidate, not a result.
