## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For this question the shape is the seven-part constraint set in `01` section 0: per object, ground-ranged, source-discriminating, independent, union-aware, context-bearing, pre-registered. Naming a particular molecule (O2 plus CH4, or a fractionated isotope ratio) as *the* answer would be a name, not a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, find recurring terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: start with "a signal", then "a signal with a source", then "a source that competes with abiotic sources", then "independent measurements of the competition".
- Fig 6 :30: *"outward: does another frame already hold it? The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* An evidence standard is exactly such a description: what you would expect to see if a detection were real, stated so anyone can check it.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: you are done when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* The canonical README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50 to 52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Here, an astrobiology result imported into the derivation must be attributed, not absorbed.

*They're Scanning Books* sharpens the limit: *"A minimum viable set of six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set need not be those six."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* Our list of what counts as adequate evidence is a visible choice, not nature's own list. That is why the refuter is written down publicly.

## 4. Why "life detected" is the specific forbidden error

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* The source might be one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or *"a completely novel color scheme that humans have not yet discovered."* The essay's conclusion: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about what is being measured or what causes it."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved the case.

A life-detection claim reads a projection (a spectrum, an isotope ratio, a morphology) and names its source (biology). The shape demands a discriminator: either an instrument that separates biology from the abiotic routes, or separately derived lines that converge on the same source attribution without sharing a pipeline. Naming the source from the reading alone is the metamerism error.

*How Intelligence Actually Works* adds the distinction horizon: *"The projection that no instrument distinguishes from the thing is still a projection, which is why the lineage has to be kept even when the copy is perfect."* And the union horizon: *"A projection sinks below the union horizon when no observer, with any instrument any of them can build, can discriminate its origin."* For this question: a claim is not settled until no available instrument discriminates biology from abiotic, or until one does. Which of those is true is a fact about instruments, not about our confidence.

## 5. Independence is the load-bearing half of the shape

*How a Society of Agents Would Work*: *"A group is only worth having while its members stay different."* And: *"the group's agreement is never the verdict."* Ten copies watching the same thing are one opinion said ten times.

Fig 2 :31 gives the same rule *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* The *Society of Agents* essay translates it into a design rule: *"Independent discoveries count most. An agent that found something on its own is far stronger evidence than one that tried it because it ranked high."* For life detection this is the difference between two instruments and two reductions of one instrument.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* names the failure: two people who only check against each other *"drift together, and they drift together, so neither notices."* The three safeguards it lists are all present in the shape: a ground both are corrected by separately, separate experience, and claims in effect terms.

## 6. Why a threshold is not a verdict

*How a Society of Agents Would Work* states the rule plainly: *"Votes come from outcomes, not popularity"* and *"Deadlines are filters, never verdicts."* Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*

A signal-to-noise cut, a false-positive probability, a p value and a confidence tier are all filters. They decide how much budget to spend and what to try next. They never decide whether the world contained life. *Arrival of the Fittest* cites Goodhart and Campbell on proxies that become targets; a detection standard that becomes a target is the same failure.

## 7. Prestige is not a detection

*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly."* And: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

A famous team, a famous telescope and a large collaboration can fund the attempt. They cannot make the reading true. A detection claim that leans on who made it has already replaced the ground with a proxy. This is also why `03_public_contact_points.csv` has no receptiveness rank and no personal emails.

## 8. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself."* *Arrival of the Fittest*: attaching the formula to a prompt *"get[s] a plausible simulated output to an issue in any given domain"*, but because the library is a black box the outputs *"must be seen as simulacra"*, and the result *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."*

So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the verbatim figures. They are **not** a test of any detection claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle the question they report on.

## 9. Correction to my own §1 (kept visible)

I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific biosignature. That overstates it. The canonical README's phrase governs *"what a missing thing would have to look like"*, a primitive not held in the library. Here the question is not about a missing primitive; it is about what evidence would settle a claim. The correct narrower statement is: the framework can constrain the **structure** of adequate evidence, and will not select a particular feature for us. Naming O2 plus CH4 is a binding from the astrobiology frame onto that structure, provenanced as such. The broad ban is retracted; the narrower claim stands. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 10. What shape means here, in one sentence

A shape is the pre-registered, per-object, ground-ranged, source-discriminating, independently triangulated, union-aware and context-bearing form that any adequate life-detection claim must have, stated before a signal exists and refusable by the same test as any other term.
