## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three, a reading, its possible sources (biology, abiotic chemistry, contamination), and the ground that settles which obtained. Symmetry: not symmetric; a reading is compatible with several sources, but the attribution direction is not reversible. Scale: first order at the instrument and object, second order at the interpretation, third order at the community verification. Fig 9 :22 names R by *"arity · symmetry · scale"*.
- **Domain 1, measurement.** Actors: instrument, sample, blank, positive control, target object (slot s). Currency: residual per slot, signal above the limit of quantitation. Anchor: the observation itself, which does not change because a frame wants it to. Partition, per object.
- **Domain 2, interpretation.** Actors: astrobiologist, abiotic geochemistry models, contamination hypotheses, the environment. Currency: which hypothesis explains the reading, and which refusals are owed. Anchor: the same observation, plus the last-resort requirement. **Union compound**, not one problem.
- **Domain 3, verification.** Actors: independent instruments, independent teams, peer review, the scientific community. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never the agreement. Triangulation requiring low overlap.
- **Order by dependency.** Perception (real signal on this object, against controls), then vocabulary (terms for competing mechanisms), then objective (biology as last resort), then plan (disjoint independent lines), then context and lineage (Fig 3 :20, ordered by dependency not importance).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

Adequate evidence for a life detection has a shape, not a name. It is per object, ground-ranged, source-discriminating, independent across its lines, union-aware, context-bearing and pre-registered with written losing conditions and a lineage. Community scrutiny checks these; it is not itself the ground. The framework cannot say which feature will occupy the slot, and this derivation cannot say whether life exists anywhere. Status: `candidate`, `general` on the shape and `specified` in its predictions, ungrounded. The canonical README's limit is the strongest single point: the framework returns *"a shape, not a name"*, then stops where the instruments stop.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three checkable sub-claims, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is a false life-detection claim, which is expensive and irreversible in public understanding. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is reduced by leading with the shape and the refuter and by using low-overlap frames.
- **undoable:** yes for the claim, not for a published false detection. The shape can be tightened or withdrawn without touching the figures; `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already strip out the forbidden readings (a named biosignature, consensus as ground, thresholds as verdicts). No primitive has to be withdrawn. A claim already announced cannot be un-announced, which is why the refuter is written first.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A source-discriminating instrument that separates biology from the abiotic routes for a specific feature under a stated environment would move the claim toward settlement; a second line with demonstrably disjoint inputs would raise it; a demonstration that the competing conditions collapse to one would change the union to a partition. Conversely, a shared input between the lines, a source named without a discriminator, or a standard written after the signal would refute the shape claim. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
