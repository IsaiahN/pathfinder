## Wider literature that bears on the residual

1. **Instrument criteria versus context criteria.** Neveu et al. 2018 say the first three criteria (sensitivity, no interfering contamination, repeatability) are necessary but not sufficient. This is the cleanest world-side statement of "a gate is not the ground".
2. **The observer's defect.** Seat Map :275: *"A new instrument's first output is a claim about the instrument, not about the system, and a known-positive fixture proves sensitivity, never specificity. Both are required."* Positive controls and blanks in Neveu et al. are the field's version of this.
3. **Source discrimination.** The author's *Hard Problem is About Source* essay and *How Intelligence Actually Works* both use metamerism: several underlying causes create identical readings. A spectrometer, not an argument, resolved the case. For life detection the corresponding question is whether any instrument discriminates biology from abiotic chemistry at the relevant scale.
4. **Independence.** Kish's effective sample size correction and Fig 2's second collapse are the same arithmetic. Two instruments pointed at the same thing are two frames; two people reading the same report are one.
