## 0. The claim under audit

> The question asks for a **kind of evidence**, not a catalogue of biosignatures. Restated as an effect: what would have to be present in a body of evidence before the sentence "life has been detected beyond Earth" is licensed rather than merely hoped for?
>
> Option A: a single strong-looking reading is enough, provided the team and the instrument are credible. Option B: the sentence is licensed only by a shape of evidence that can survive a ground that does not move with the frame, with the source of the reading discriminated and the alternatives refused.
>
> **Direction term, written before the run.** The sought-for shape is bound as an ordinary term: *adequate evidence for a detection is per-object, ground-ranged, source-discriminating, independent across its lines, union-aware, pre-registered and provenance-carrying.* Once bound, it can be refused by the same test as anything else.

## 1. Domain census

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: the residual is a three-way relation between a reading, its possible sources (biology, abiotic chemistry, contamination), and the ground that would settle which source obtained. Symmetry: it is not symmetric. A reading can be compatible with several sources, but a source is not interchangeable with a reading; the direction of attribution matters. Scale: first order at the instrument and object, second order at the interpretation, third order at the community check. Fig 9 :21-22 names R by *"arity · symmetry · scale"* and Fig 9 :26 says *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*"*.

**One line per domain.**

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | question type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **D1 measurement** | instrument, sample, signal, blank, positive control, the target object (slot s) | residual R per slot; signal above the limit of quantitation | the observation itself, which does not change because a frame wants it to | partition, per object |
| **D2 interpretation** | astrobiologist, abiotic geochemistry models, contamination hypotheses, the environment | which hypothesis explains the reading; refusals | the same observation, plus the requirement that biology be the last resort | **union compound**, not one problem |
| **D3 verification** | independent instruments, independent teams, peer review, the scientific community | agreement and residual | the ground, never the agreement | triangulation, low overlap required |

**No nameable anchor means it is not a domain yet.** Each of the three has one. D1 and D2 share the same anchor (the observation); that is correct, because they are two different questions ranging against one ground rather than one question split in two. D3 has the same anchor too. Agreement is not an anchor in D3; it is a currency that must be cashed against the ground.

**Partition or union.** D1 is a partition across object slots: each object carries its own residual (F2). D2 is a union: a reading such as atmospheric O2 can sit in more than one condition at once, biology and photochemistry and meteoritic infall. Fig 9 :55 says *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* It must be unbundled before search (F5).

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (is there a real signal above the control, on this object) then vocabulary (do we have terms for the competing abiotic mechanisms and for contamination) then objective (what would count as progress: the last-resort hypothesis) then planning (which independent lines to acquire) then learn and carry (record the lineage and the refuter). Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."* A link with no instrument is not a link that works, Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."*

## 3. Findings

Mode is stated on each. `general` means structural and domain-agnostic. `specified` means it predicts what a detection claim here must contain. None is `grounded`.

### F1: The ground is the observation, not the community or the theory

**Mode: `general`, triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 13 plus the canonical README).**

Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks. A measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome."* Fig 2 :30: *"you push on it, it is not an anchor."* Fig 2 :36: *"An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it."*

The question is constitutively about what the world did at a particular object. So the anchor must be the observation or the sample. A journal, a panel, a majority or a detection standard that changes when people push on it is a frame and cannot settle anything.

**Refuter:** name a proposed standard whose verdict changes when the team, the venue or the majority changes; or show that the claim was accepted because of who asserted it rather than because the ground paid.

### F2: The residual is indexed by object, not aggregated

**Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 1 plus Fig 8 division of search).**

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

A detection claim attaches to an object: this meteorite, this plume sample, this atmosphere on this planet. A population average that crosses a threshold while no individual object carries a live residual is not a detection. It is a statistic about the sample set. The world source agrees in a different vocabulary: Neveu et al. 2018 distinguish N measurements on the same sample (repeatability) from N samples needed to capture heterogeneity, and note that an exoplanet atmosphere covering one pixel is one slot rather than permission to pool worlds.

**Refuter:** a claim accepted on a pooled average with no per-object residual surviving, or a claim where the live slot cannot be named.

### F3: A reading is not a source; refusals must be writable

**Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 plus Fig 5 plus the Operators table).**

Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* Fig 5 :59: *"a term whose residual PERSISTS AND DOES NOT TREND DOWN is the one to point the refusal at."*

A biosignature is a reading: green, or O2, or a fractionated isotope ratio. The claim "life" is a source attribution. Fig 5's fifth bin is REFUTED, not one of the four missing-things bins. What is owed when an abiotic mechanism keeps predicting the same reading is a refusal of that mechanism's standing to be ignored, not another confirmation of biology. The Operators table records *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."*

World corroboration is unusually direct. The author's *Hard Problem is About Source* essay: *"the instrument is speaking only about what it measured, it cannot comment on the source."* NASA's 2025 explanation of this exact question: *"Carl Sagan once said, 'Life is the hypothesis of last resort,' meaning that we ought to work hard for such a claim to rule out alternative possibilities."*

**Refuter:** a detection claimed because a biology-shaped pattern was found, while the abiotic and contamination hypotheses were never enumerated, each with a stated losing condition.

### F4: Independent lines are only independent if they do not share the evidence

**Mode: `general`, triangulated (Fig 2 plus Fig 8 plus the seat map).**

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence. Two people reading the same report are not two frames; two instruments pointed at the same thing are."* Fig 2 :32: *"is what makes agreement mean something."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."*

Fig 8 :50: *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."* So two lines that converge at the same conclusion are strong evidence **only if the record shows they were reached separately**. Two spectra reduced by the same pipeline are one line spelled twice.

Seat Map :163 says *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* NASA asks exactly the right question: *"do we have separate independent lines of evidence that corroborate what we've seen and increase our confidence of life?"*

**Refuter:** two supposedly independent confirmations that share an instrument, a calibration standard, a reduction pipeline, a prior, or the same sample.

### F5: A single biosignature is a union compound, not one hard question

**Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 9 plus Fig 8 plus Wikipedia/Neveu as secondary).**

Fig 9 :54: *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

O2 detection does not ask one question. It asks the intersection of three: could biology make this, could photolysis make this, could infalling meteoritic material make this. A single lever (detect O2) clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing. The compound needs its own loop and its own ground.

World corroboration: Wikipedia's Biosignature article states *"Finding oxygen alone in a planet's atmosphere is not enough to confirm a biosignature because of the false-positive mechanisms associated with it."* Neveu et al. 2018 define reliability as being *"measurably different from expected abiotic signals"* and insist the biological interpretation be the last-resort hypothesis.

**Refuter:** a claim that treats a single feature as decisive while acknowledging that abiotic routes to the same feature exist.

### F6: Non-detection is not proof of absence; budget and principle are different facts

**Mode: `general`, single-source for the theorem, corroborated by Cockell 2013 and by Fig 9.**

Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 8 :40: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

This cuts both ways and the two directions must not be read against one another (Fig 6 :31). A null result at one resolution is a fact about the instrument, not about life. But the remedy is not to demand an instrument for something that has never registered anywhere: Fig 6 :32 says *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34: *"It is whether anything, at any resolution, is already returning something that fails to resolve."* The proto-instrument must already be returning a residual.

World corroboration: Cockell 2013 proposes as a hypothesis to be tested that *"Most habitable worlds in the cosmos will have no remotely detectable signs of life"*, which is the same distinction stated from the other side.

**Refuter:** a claim that absence of a signal at one instrument resolution proves absence of life, or a claim that a signal which has never registered at any resolution can be searched for by building a sensor from a description alone.

### F7: The standard must be written before the observation

**Mode: `specified`, triangulated (THE_FORMULA Direction plus Fig 5 plus the seat map).**

`THE_FORMULA.md`, "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."* Fig 5 :65: *"The remedy is a discipline rather than a branch: make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM."* Seat Map :287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

NASA's *"We call this the podium test. What would it take for you personally to confidently stand up in front of an international audience and make that claim?"* is exactly this move. Neveu et al. 2018 likewise propose *"a small but expandable set of decision rules determining whether the abiotic hypothesis is disproved"* before evaluating past claims.

**Refuter:** a detection standard first written after the anomalous signal is in hand, or a refuter that cannot be applied because the losing condition was never named in advance.

### F8: Provenance shows legitimacy, not truth; mode must be stated

**Mode: `general`, triangulated (Fig 10 plus THE_FORMULA plus the seat map).**

Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* THE_FORMULA step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Seat Map :281: *"an unmeasured quantity is a specification of what to measure and is worth having, it is not a verdict."*

A fully documented, peer-reviewed reduction pipeline is a clean record of how a number was produced. It is not the number being right about the world. Every claim in this folder carries a mode; where quantities are unmeasured, the honest label is `specified` awaiting ground settlement.

**Refuter:** a claim labelled settled because the chain of custody is complete, or a threshold presented as a verdict rather than as a budget filter (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).

### F9: A community is not an anchor

**Mode: `general`, triangulated (Fig 7 plus Fig 2 plus the seat map).**

Fig 7 :13: *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Seat Map :283: *"A room does not upgrade the label. A room reaching a conclusion without the ground is agreement, and agreement among frames is a weighted average of influence. Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."*

NASA says *"Ultimately, all of this has to be looked at hard by the entire scientific community... the really operative word in our question is we."* This is **corroborated as a procedure, not as a ground**. Community scrutiny is the D3 mechanism for checking that each frame ranged against the observation separately. If the community's verdict itself becomes the standard, that is panel 1 of Fig 2: everyone updates on everyone and nobody notices the drift. This was the main re-census of this audit; see section 6.

**Refuter:** a standard whose final clause is "when enough experts agree", with no independent ground each expert had to range against.

### F10: Context is part of the measurement

**Mode: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 plus Neveu et al. 2018 plus Wikipedia).**

Fig 11 :43: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 11 :44: *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

A biosignature is only diagnostic relative to an environment. The stellar UV field, the water inventory, the mineral matrix, the sample's provenance and the possible contaminating routes are all in contact with the residual. Neveu et al. 2018: *"Establishing context encompasses the measurements seeking to determine the properties and processes (such as habitability or competing abiotic mechanisms) inherent both to the environment investigated and to the environments through which the sample provenance can be traced."* Wikipedia: *"Habitability indicators and biosignatures must be interpreted within a planetary and environmental context."*

**Refuter:** a detection claim that names a diagnostic feature without stating the environmental conditions under which that feature would not be diagnostic.

### F11: Relations between features, not a list of features

**Mode: `general` structural analogy, single-source from the figures. Explicitly an analogy, not a derivation.**

Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

A list of detected molecules is an ingredient list. What makes a set evidentially decisive is the relation among its members: co-localisation, a disequilibrium maintained against the background, a fractionation pattern across compounds, temporal covariance. Wikipedia's chemistry section: *"No single compound will prove life once existed. Rather, it will be distinctive patterns present in any organic compounds showing a process of selection."*

**Strain, named not hidden.** Fig 12's isomer is a claim about chemical molecules with identical composition and different bonds. Applying it to a set of biosignature observations is an analogy between a chemical molecule and an evidential compound. It is not a figure-derived chemical claim. It is retained as `general`, single-source, and would be withdrawn if the auditor seat rejects the analogy.

**Refuter:** a claim whose evidence is an unordered list of features with the relations among them left unspecified.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion, per Seat Map :287. The shape claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A detection is accepted on one instrument's reading with no independent frame and no discrimination of abiotic sources (breaks F1, F4).
2. A reading is treated as naming its source without a source-discriminating instrument or convergent derivation reached separately (breaks F3, F4).
3. The evidence is aggregated across objects so the live slot is averaged away (breaks F2).
4. A non-detection is reported as proof of absence, or a never-registered signal is demanded from a sensor built out of a description (breaks F6).
5. A panel selected for agreement is used as the anchor, or agreement alone closes the claim (breaks F1, F4, F9).
6. The claim is labelled settled by a vote, convention, declaration or clean provenance rather than by the ground (breaks F8).
7. The environment needed to make the feature diagnostic is omitted (breaks F10).

If any fires, the shape claim is wrong and is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Lead with the shape and the refuter, not with a list of candidate biosignatures. Bind the sought-for shape as an ordinary term (F7).
2. Report evidence per object and name the live slot (F2).
3. Describe the residual in effect terms before choosing the interpretive frame (Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit"*).
4. Write down, before the observation, which abiotic mechanisms would have to be refused and what each losing condition is (F3, F5).
5. Buy independence where being wrong is expensive, and log for each line whether it was derived convergently or adopted (F4).
6. Treat thresholds and signal-to-noise cuts as filters for the budget, never as witnesses for the verdict (Fig 9 :60).
7. Record the lineage and declare the stake. Provenance makes an error locatable, not impossible (F8).
8. Stop at the limit of what the instruments distinguish and record the stop. *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*
