# The Tether's answer: what evidence would justify saying life has been detected beyond Earth?

**Mode: `general` on the structural shape, `specified` in what it predicts a detection claim must contain. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a verdict about whether life exists. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 8), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

We are asked for a standard of evidence before any detection exists. Option A accepts one strong reading backed by credible people. Option B requires evidence with a particular shape. What each leads to: A gives a source attribution made from a projection, with no instrument that discriminates sources and an agreement pool that cannot check itself; B gives a checkable claim, written refuters, and an open slot until the ground settles it.

Fig 9 :26: *"R's own predicates name the habitat that holds φ*"*. Describing the residual first, in effect terms, is what keeps the search from fitting any frame we happen to pick.

## 2. The shape: seven constraints, three domains, kept separate

**D1, measurement.** The evidence attaches to a named object. Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* The anchor is the observation, which does not move when a frame pushes on it (Fig 2 :29 to 30). The instrument must already be returning something that fails to resolve (Fig 6 :34), not be imagined from a description.

**D2, interpretation.** A reading is a projection and the claim names its source. That attribution is licensed only when the abiotic and contamination routes have been enumerated and refused. Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* Fig 10 :49: *"Declaring a meaning does not ground it."* Fig 5 :53: *"refuted: a term we hold is wrong; a term is owed that says so."* The positive features compose only over the refusals. The case is a union compound, not one hard question (Fig 9 :55), so it must be unbundled before search.

**D3, verification.** Independent lines must not share evidence. Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42). Community scrutiny is a procedure, not an anchor: Fig 7 :13 *"agreement compounds, evidence does not."*

Across all three: the standard is written before the observation. `THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction": *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an answer would look like"*. And provenance does not substitute for settlement: Fig 10 :65 *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately."*

The seven constraints are: per object; ground-ranged; source-discriminating; independent across lines; union-aware; context-bearing; pre-registered with a refuter and a lineage. The verdicts for the three domains are kept separate and are never averaged into a single confidence score.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that any particular molecule or telescope would settle the question. The framework returns a shape, not a name.
- Not that a probability threshold, a signal-to-noise ratio or a confidence tier is a verdict. These are filters for the budget (Fig 9 :60).
- Not that agreement among experts makes a claim true. A room does not add an anchor.
- Not that a non-detection proves absence. Fig 8 :39 *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*
- Not a claim that life has been found anywhere. No ground reading was taken.
- Not a claim that this shape is nature's own list of what counts. *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."*

## 4. What would settle it / the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. The *Hard Problem is About Source* essay's spectrometer is the model: the green reading was resolved by an instrument, not by a debate about green. For life detection the corresponding questions are:

1. **Source test.** Does any available instrument distinguish a biological source from the abiotic routes for this feature under this environment? If yes, run it. If no, the claim stops at `candidate`.
2. **Union test.** Which of the competing conditions actually bind? A demonstration that one abiotic route is impossible under the stated conditions collapses the union and strengthens the claim; a demonstration that two routes produce identical readings keeps it open.
3. **Independence test.** Do the supposedly separate lines have disjoint input sets? If not, they are one line and the corroboration claim is withdrawn.
4. **Refuter test.** Did the losing conditions exist before the observation? If not, the claim has not met its own standard.
5. **Standing falsifiers:** a detection accepted on an averaged signal; a source named from a reading with no discriminator; a claim closed by consensus or provenance; an absence inferred from one instrument's null result.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape can say what adequate evidence would have to look like. It cannot say which feature will occupy the slot, because that is a name and the framework returns a kind of thing (Fig 9 :32). Fig 4 :30 requires the round trip gap to be measured: a compressed statement such as "independent lines are needed" loses the questions of what counts as independent and who decides. Those are not decoration; they are what the gap is made of.

World sources agree remarkably closely with the shape, but most of them are either the framework author's essays or a single astrobiology literature pool. Agreement from one pool is one witness (Fig 2 :40). No conflict with the figure verdict was found, so the conflict rule was not triggered. The answer stops where the instruments stop and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The framework does not say which molecule or telescope would prove life exists. It says what any such claim would have to look like. Evidence must attach to a named object rather than an average, because an average is where a live signal goes to disappear. It must range against an observation no frame can move, and it must discriminate the source of a reading rather than reading the source off the reading, because several abiotic processes can produce the same green. Its lines must be genuinely independent, which means they share no instrument, calibration, reduction pipeline or prior; two people reading one report are one witness. It must unbundle the case where several abiotic mechanisms produce the same feature before searching, name the environment that makes the feature diagnostic, and write its losing conditions down before the observation. Community scrutiny checks all of this; it is not itself the ground. What is offered is the shape any adequate claim must have, the refuters written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. The slot stays open until a ground settles it.
