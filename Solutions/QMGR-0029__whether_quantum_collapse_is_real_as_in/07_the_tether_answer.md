# The Tether's answer: real collapse or apparent collapse?

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** This is a shape, a route and an instrument list, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Standard quantum theory evolves states linearly and deterministically, then adds a Born-rule postulate for outcomes. The linear dynamics alone gives a superposition of apparatus records, not one record. The residual is the gap between that prediction and the observed single record, indexed per measurement context: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19).

The bare question is a **union compound**, not one binary: it mixes (i) is the dynamics exact or modified, (ii) what are the parameters if modified, (iii) can branching plus decoherence account for definite records without a modification. *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9). It must be unbundled before a frame is chosen (Fig 9 :27-28).

## 2. The shape

1. **The anchor is an instrument, not an interpretation.** *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* (Fig 2 :29). A majority of physicists, a citation count, or a well-known defender does not move the experiment. *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* (Fig 2 :35).

2. **One branch is a mint, the other is not.** Objective collapse adds a stochastic nonlinear localisation term whose standing must be settled by the ground; Fig 5 :43-44 gives the bargain: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."* Many-worlds mints no dynamical term; it keeps the Schrodinger equation and supplies a correspondence rule from branches to experience. Under the Operators table, the collapse term is a negation of strict unitarity: *"a refutation is a negation, which is why a vocabulary without it can only accumulate."* That asymmetry is what "real versus apparent" reduces to under the figures.

3. **The verdicts stay separate, never averaged.** Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

   | domain | verdict from the figures | status |
   |---|---|---|
   | D1 definite records with no new term | an account exists (branching plus decoherence) | `specified`, largely settled as an account |
   | D2 a real localisation term | a mint whose standing is owed by the ground | `specified`, candidate; parameter partially bounded |
   | D3 which nature implements | direct discriminator unreachable at this budget; indirect edge live | `specified`, open |

4. **The direct discriminator is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle.** Fig 8 :40-43: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Interference between macroscopic branches, or reversing a measurement, is described in the literature but *"remain strictly gedanken experiments, far beyond the reach of current or foreseeable technology"* (Vaidman, SEP 2026). The figures' answer to that is not silence: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* (Fig 13 :134). What is returned is a shape: a macroscopic-interference experiment.

5. **The indirect edge is live and already returning signal.** Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Spontaneous radiation (XENONnT, germanium searches), force noise (LISA Pathfinder, cantilevers, AURIGA, LIGO), heating (cold atoms, intergalactic medium) and matter-wave interferometry all already return readings. XENONnT (2026) excludes the originally proposed GRW parameter values at 9.1 sigma while stating that *"A wider parameter range remains to be probed for colored or dissipative model extensions."* A bound on a parameter point is not the end of a theory.

6. **The figures do not pick a side.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* They return the split, the operator asymmetry, the instrument and the honest stop. Choosing "real" or "apparent" as a conclusion about nature is the searcher's own contribution.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that collapse is real. No detection of a localisation term is claimed.
- Not that collapse is apparent. A null bound is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).
- Not that this is a mere convention or a difference of interpretation with no possible test. The direct test is out of reach, not impossible, and the indirect test is running (Vaidman, SEP; XENONnT 2026).
- Not that XENONnT has refuted collapse models. It excluded the Markovian white-noise GRW parameter point; coloured, dissipative and Diósi-Penrose variants retain viable regions.
- Not that GRW and many-worlds are isomers. That reading strained and was narrowed to a functional-group relation (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).
- Not that a consensus of experts decides this. Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- Not `grounded`. No measurement in this derivation settles the interpretation.

## 4. What would settle it, the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, not a debate. Here:

1. **Positive detection.** Spontaneous radiation, excess force noise or loss of interferometric contrast at a mass and separation where standard decoherence is accounted for, fitting a specific (lambda, r_C), replicated by at least two low-overlap instrument families. That would settle the standing of the mint toward "real" and move the relevant claim toward `grounded`.
2. **Full exclusion.** A null result closing the entire phenomenologically viable collapse parameter space, including coloured and dissipative extensions. That would push the dynamics branch toward strict unitarity. It must be a full-space exclusion, not one parameter point (Fig 8 :40-43).
3. **Direct discriminator.** A working experiment that performs interference between macroscopic branches or reverses a measurement, the test Vaidman describes as gedanken. That would decide the many-worlds versus collapse question in principle.
4. **Standing falsifiers of this shape:** a proof of strict empirical equivalence (then it is a convention); a re-census showing the binary is a single question; a result showing the isomer or functional-group claim is wrong in both directions. All four are written in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §3 before the conclusion.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached for the direct test, live for the indirect one, never silent.** The figures stop where the available instruments stop and record the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The current indiscernibility between decoherence-only readings and intrinsic collapse at the density-matrix level is a limit of the instrument, not a statement that no difference exists. The answer stops there with the instrument list attached, not with a verdict about nature. World sources corroborate the shape but come largely from overlapping evidence pools (the Trieste collapse-model group and the philosophy-of-physics literature); their agreement is recorded as one pool, not as several witnesses (Fig 2 :40).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say collapse is real, and it does not say it is apparent. It says the question is a bundle of three questions that need separate verdicts, and that the anchor is an instrument, not a majority opinion. Under its rules, objective collapse is a mint, a new stochastic term whose parameter must be settled by measurement, while many-worlds mints no dynamical term and supplies definite outcomes by branching plus decoherence. That operator asymmetry is the real content of the binary. The direct test, interference between macroscopic branches or reversing a measurement, is out of reach at this budget but not impossible in principle, so the verdict is "unreachable", not silence. Meanwhile the indirect instruments are live: spontaneous-radiation, force-noise and interferometry measurements are already bounding the collapse parameters, with a 2026 XENONnT result excluding the originally proposed GRW values while leaving extensions open. What the framework offers is that shape, four refuters written in advance, and the instrument list that would close it. Nature decides; agreement does not.
