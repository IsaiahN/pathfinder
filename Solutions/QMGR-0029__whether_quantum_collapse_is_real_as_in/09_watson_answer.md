## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** The linear Schrodinger dynamics plus a Born-rule postulate does not by itself yield one definite apparatus record. Arity: the presented binary is a **union compound** of three conditions (is the dynamics exact, what are the parameters if modified, can branching plus decoherence supply definite records with no modification). Symmetry: the alternatives are **not symmetric**, because objective collapse mints a stochastic term and many-worlds mints none; there is no operation exchanging them, so the verdicts stay separate. Scale: microscopic (unitary works), mesoscopic (where lambda and r_C live), macroscopic (definite outcomes and the amplification mechanism). Fig 9 names the union rule: unbundle before you search again.
- **Domain 1, formalism and interpretation.** Actors: wavefunction, Schrodinger evolution, decoherence, branching, observer records. Currency: empirical adequacy and internal coherence. Anchor: the formalism's predictions, which is a frame-internal anchor, not the ground for this question. Verdict: an account of definite records exists with no new term.
- **Domain 2, objective-collapse models.** Actors: localisation operator, collapse rate lambda, correlation length r_C, noise field, amplification rule. Currency: a modified dynamical equation with free phenomenological parameters. Anchor: experiment (interferometry, spontaneous radiation, heating). Verdict: a mint whose standing is owed by the ground; parameter partially bounded.
- **Domain 3, experimental ground.** Actors: XENONnT, molecular interferometers, LISA Pathfinder, cantilevers, cold atoms. Currency: spontaneous X-ray rate, interference contrast, force noise, heating. Anchor: the instrument, which does not update on what any frame thinks. Verdict: the original GRW parameter point is excluded; the wider space remains open.
- **Domain 4, verification and outreach.** Actors: expert readers. Currency: agreement and residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. In this simulated run all seats draw on one evidence pool, so this domain's independence is zero.
- **Partition or union.** Union across D1, D2 and D3; D4 is a checking frame over them. **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** establish the residual, acquire terms for a modification, state what would count as progress (a discriminating instrument), then plan. The historical break was at link 3; instruments are now arriving there.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; neither interpretation is chosen. The question is a union compound whose three branches carry separate `specified` verdicts: an account of definite records with no new term is available; a real localisation term is a mint whose standing is owed by the ground and whose original parameter point is now excluded; and which nature implements is undecided, with the direct discriminator unreachable at this budget and the indirect edge live. Objective collapse and many-worlds are a functional-group relation, not strict isomers. The anchor is the instrument, and the figures return a shape plus a refuter set rather than a name. The strong errors are: treating it as one binary, treating a parameter bound as a general refutation, treating it as a pure convention, and treating agreement as an anchor. Nothing here is `grounded`.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, because the packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion. The real cost is stopping the instrument programme early: if we call the matter a convention we never build the discriminator; if we call one parameter exclusion a general refutation we discard viable coloured, dissipative and gravitational variants. Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. That cost is avoidable by leading with the split and its refuters and by using several low-overlap instruments.
- **undoable:** yes. The verdict is `candidate`. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A new XENONnT data set, a LISA Pathfinder rotation analysis, a heavier molecular interferometer, or a revised coloured-noise bound can move the parameter verdict without touching the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A replicated positive localisation signal at a specific (lambda, r_C) would move D2 toward "real" and toward `grounded`; a full-space null exclusion including coloured and dissipative extensions would move it toward strict unitarity; a working macroscopic-interference or measurement-reversal experiment would decide the many-worlds-versus-collapse question in principle; a proof of strict empirical equivalence would reverse the verdict to "convention"; a re-census showing a single question would reverse the union split. Prestige or majority agreement is not such an instrument.
