## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :133: *"what it returns is a shape rather than a name."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before nature has settled it. It constrains; it does not pick a winner. The shape returned for QMGR-0029 is:

> a compound of three questions, each with a separate verdict, ranging against an instrument; one branch is a mint whose parameter is being bounded now, another mints no term, and the direct discriminator is out of reach at this budget.

That is a shape. "Collapse is real" and "collapse is apparent" are names of outcomes, and the framework does not provide them.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: bootstrap vocabulary by proximity, *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."* Applied here: begin with the residual (a missing definite outcome), then let the field's own terms arrive (localisation rate, correlation length, branching, decoherence, amplification) instead of starting from the two famous names.
- Fig 6 :30: *"Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The effect is a per-context gap between a predicted superposition of records and an observed single record.
- *Library of Babel* on bundling: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one?"* This is the exact warrant for splitting the binary in F5 of `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`.
- *Library of Babel* on the tether: *"you need something to tether and anchor you to reality."* Here the tether is the residual and the anchor is the instrument (Fig 2 :29).

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: the ground settles it and is verified only by what pays; a gate is not the ground. The README says the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

For this question, naming a specific collapse parameter value (for example the GRW point lambda = 10^-16 s^-1, r_C = 10^-7 m) is a binding from the physics frame onto a term already held in that frame. It is not a derivation from the figures. When XENONnT excludes that point, the ground is settling a binding, not a figure. Keep the two records separate.

## 4. The ground and the source problem

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism, and it bears directly on this question:

> *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information"*
> *"the instrument detects green... it cannot comment on the source"*
> *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy"*
> *"It was proven wrong not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."*

A decoherence measurement reads an interference contrast. It cannot by itself comment on the source, because environmental decoherence and an intrinsic collapse term produce very similar density-matrix dynamics. The 2023 review says exactly this: *"both collapse models and decoherence predict very similar effects to observe in the density matrix dynamics and great care has to be taken to distinguish the two effects."* What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. The indirect instruments (spontaneous radiation, force noise, heating) are attempts to build that discriminator; the direct macroscopic-interference reversal is the ideal discriminator and is out of reach.

Two consequences:

- Reporting a bound on lambda as "collapse is false" is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading where several sources could give the reading.
- Reporting the many-worlds account as "collapse is proven apparent" makes the same error in the opposite direction: an absence of detected source is not a proof of absence (Fig 8 :39).

## 5. Answer to the shape question

**What licenses a particular interpretation here?** Nothing in the figures. The figures return a shape and an instrument. A particular interpretation is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until then, choosing "real" or "apparent" is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does this folder commit the error the README forbids?** No. It does not name which nature implements. It does, however, name the components of the shape (a mint on one side, no mint on the other, an unreachable discriminator, a live indirect edge), and those names are figure-grounded structural descriptions, not outcomes. The one place where a name is borrowed is the collapse parameter point from the XENONnT report, and that is recorded as a physics-frame binding in `10_source_ledger.csv`, not as a figure result.

**Where the shape came from:** the residual was described in effect terms before the two frames were picked (Fig 9 :27-28). The frames then arrived as occupants of the already-censused domains D1 and D2. That order is the check against apophenia.

## 6. Consequences for contact

**Prestige is not action.** Prestige can fund an action, giving it a budget to be tried, but it cannot settle an action; only the ground can settle it. A list of famous interpreters who agree with one side is the named collapse, not corroboration (Fig 2 :35, :40).

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Like any LLM output, this derivation is a simulated answer checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of the interpretation and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle the question it reports on. What it can do is return the shape, the separate verdicts, the refuters and the instrument that would close the gap.
