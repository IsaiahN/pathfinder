## 0. The claim under audit and the residual

> Is wave-function collapse a real physical process, as in Ghirardi-Rimini-Weber type spontaneous localisation models, or only apparent, as in the Everett many-worlds interpretation where the universal state evolves unitarily and definite outcomes are branch-relative?

**Residual in effect terms.** The linear, deterministic Schrodinger evolution plus a Born-rule postulate does not by itself yield one definite outcome for a single measurement. Something is owed: either a term in the dynamics (objective collapse) or a reading of the same dynamics that accounts for definite records without a term (Everett plus decoherence). The residual is the gap between what the formalism predicts (a superposition of apparatus records) and what is observed (one record). It is indexed per measurement context, not averaged over interpretations: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* (Fig 1 :19).

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.**

- **Arity.** The question presents as a binary but is a **union compound**. It bundles at least three conditions: (i) is the dynamics exactly unitary or modified by stochastic and nonlinear terms, (ii) if modified, what are the values of lambda and r_C, (iii) can branching plus decoherence account for definite outcomes with no modification. These are not one hard question. *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"* (Fig 9, union/partition section). Unbundle before searching (Fig 9 :54).
- **Symmetry.** The alternatives are **not symmetric**. Objective collapse mints a new dynamical term (a stochastic localisation). Many-worlds mints none and rearranges what is already held. There is no symmetry exchanging them, so their verdicts must be kept separate and never averaged. Fig 2 :41 distinguishes a verdict from a convention: only where an instrument cannot discriminate does the difference become a convention.
- **Scale.** Microscopic systems are well described by unitary evolution; the modification is built to amplify with mass. The question turns on a mesoscopic-to-macroscopic threshold. That scale dependence is the bridge, and it is where the instrument must act.

**Domain census** (actors / currency / anchor, one line each; no nameable anchor means not yet a domain):

| domain | actors | currency | anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Formalism and interpretation | wavefunction, Schrodinger evolution, decoherence, branching, observer records | empirical adequacy, internal coherence | the formalism's own predictions; a frame-internal anchor, not the ground for this question |
| D2 Objective-collapse models | localisation operator, rate lambda, length r_C, noise field | a modified dynamical equation with free phenomenological parameters | experiment: interferometry, spontaneous radiation, heating |
| D3 Experimental ground | matter-wave interferometers, XENONnT, LISA Pathfinder, cantilevers, cold atoms | interference contrast, spontaneous X-ray rate, force noise, heating | the instrument itself, which does not update on what anyone thinks |
| D4 Verification and outreach | expert readers | agreement and residual | the ground, never agreement |

**Partition or union.** Union compound across D1, D2 and D3. D4 is a checking frame over all three. **Order by dependency (Fig 3):** establish that a residual exists (link 1), acquire terms for what a modification affords, namely the collapse parameters and decoherence (link 2), say what would count as progress, namely a discriminating measurement (link 3), then plan (link 4). Most public debate spends its effort above link 3 because that is where the arguments already exist, while the discrimination link has only recently acquired instruments. *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* (Fig 3 :27).

**Strain test applied before concluding.** An early reading tried to make "isomer" (Fig 12 :117) carry the real-versus-apparent distinction. It strains, because GRW adds a term and MWI does not, so they are not "same atoms, different bond" in the strict sense. Per the strain rule the line was returned to the census and now governs a different quantity (see F6 and the retraction log §6).

## 2. Findings

### F1 - The anchor for this question is an instrument, not an interpretation (decisive for the method)

Verbatim, Fig 2 :29: *"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

Both interpretations "range against the ground" (Fig 2 :45). A poll of physicists, a citation count, or a majority verdict is a frame-internal quantity and cannot settle whether a localisation term exists. The anchor is the set of measurements that would register such a term. Corroborated at low overlap by Carroll (2022), who writes that *"the experimental predictions are seemingly identical, or pretty close to it"* and that *"There might be new experiments that we haven't thought of, which could distinguish between them."* Triangulated (Fig 2 plus an independent general-physics source).

### F2 - This is a mint, not a rename (governs "real")

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 3 gates a new term by a product: SUPPORT `|R⁺_s| > 0` before the search, REACHABILITY `phi in closure(Gamma)` during, NOVELTY `phi not in atoms(Gamma)` after. Fig 5 :43-44 states the bargain: *"A term that explains everything by saying nothing fails this, and so does a term that explains one occasion perfectly."*

Objective collapse satisfies the shape of a mint: positive residual (definite outcomes missing), a candidate (stochastic localisation) built from held physics, novel relative to the unitary-only library, and it must be settled by the ground. Many-worlds mints no dynamical term; it keeps the Schrodinger equation and adds a correspondence rule between branch states and experience. This asymmetry is the whole content of "real versus apparent" under the figures: one side owes a settled new term, the other owes an account of definite records with no new term. Both debts are admissible; only one is a mint. Triangulated (Fig 5 plus Operators table, negation).

### F3 - The verdict is unreachable at this budget, not underivable in principle (decisive for the stop)

Fig 8 :40-43: *"Underivable-in-principle and unreachable-at-this-budget are different facts, and they issue different instructions."* Fig 8 :39: *"a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

The two discriminators have different statuses:

1. **Direct discriminator.** Reversing a measurement, or observing interference between macroscopic branches. Vaidman (SEP, 2026 revision) notes this is *"frequently claimed"* to be impossible in principle but *"This is not the case"*; the proposals *"remain strictly gedanken experiments, far beyond the reach of current or foreseeable technology."* That is `unreachable-at-this-budget`, and it is not silence: the figures return a shape, namely an interference experiment on a macroscopic body.
2. **Indirect discriminator.** Spontaneous radiation, force noise, heating, loss of interferometric contrast. This one is live. XENONnT (2026) reports that *"values in the originally proposed parameter ranges are experimentally excluded for the first time"* and that the GRW point deviates from best fit by 9.1 sigma. The parameter is being bounded now.

So the honest verdict is not "we can never know" and not "it is settled." It is: the direct test is out of reach at this budget, the indirect instrument edge is returning signal and is closing part of the collapse parameter space, and the ground has not yet settled the binary. Single-source for the figure distinction, corroborated at low overlap by the SEP and XENONnT reports.

### F4 - The indirect instrument edge is already returning something, so it can be extended

Fig 6 :32: *"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."* Fig 6 :34-35: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."*

The edge exists: matter-wave interferometry with molecules up to about 10^4 amu (Carlesso and Donadi 2019), spontaneous X-ray searches in germanium, LISA Pathfinder acceleration noise, nanomechanical cantilevers. Helou et al. (2016) state that *"LISA Pathfinder's measurement of a relative acceleration noise between two free-falling test masses ... appreciably constrains collapse models."* Carlesso and Donadi (2019): *"Since these models are phenomenological, the value of their parameters can be bounded and eventually identified only by experiments."* The inward instruction (Formula step 7) is therefore available: sharpen an instrument that already returns something, rather than importing a new frame. Triangulated (Fig 6 plus three independent experimental sources).

### F5 - The union must be unbundled before a side is picked (apophenia check)

Fig 9 :27-28: *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

The bare question names the frames first (GRW, many-worlds) and the residual second. If we pick a frame first, any frame will seem to fit. The residual was therefore described above in effect terms (a missing definite outcome, a per-context gap), and only then were D1, D2 and D3 enumerated. The split yields three sub-questions with separate verdicts: the dynamics question (D2, instrument live), the definite-records question (D1, largely settled as an account of experience), and the parameter question (D3, partially settled). Merging them back into one binary is the error Fig 9 :54 names. Corroborated by Bassi, Dorato and Ulbricht (2023): *"among the different formulations and interpretations of non-relativistic quantum mechanics, only collapse models ... have experimental consequences."*

### F6 - Are the two positions isomers? The strong claim is strained and narrowed

Fig 12 :117: *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."*

At first reading this looked like the exact structure of the question: same Hilbert-space formalism, different dynamical bond, different physical substance. It strains. GRW/CSL adds a stochastic nonlinear term that is absent from the unitary-only formalism; MWI adds no term. They do not share the same atom set in the sense Fig 12 requires, because the collapse term is a new atom on one side only. Re-census (strain rule): Fig 12 :117 governs compositions over a held atom set, so it applies to variants **within** collapse model space (GRW, CSL, Diósi-Penrose, dissipative and coloured extensions share the amplification-and-noise atoms and differ in the noise bond), not to collapse versus no-collapse. The surviving weaker claim is a **functional group** reading: both positions are ways of solving the same measurement problem from the same formal starting point, distinguished by whether a term is minted. Falsifier, written before the run: if a collapse variant can be written without the noise atom the others require, or if MWI is shown to require a new dynamical term, the functional-group reading fails. See `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

### F7 - Keep verdicts per domain, never average into "the answer"

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*

The three sub-verdicts must not be collapsed into one percentage or one consensus statement:

| domain | verdict from the figures | status |

Averaging these would erase the only signal that says where to look (Fig 1). Single-source for the figure rule; the domain split is corroborated by the review literature at low overlap.

### F8 - Majority agreement and prestige are not instruments here

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."*

The cost of being wrong is high (a foundational modification of the dynamics), so independence must be bought with several low-overlap instruments, not with one celebrated result. The XENONnT exclusion is from one collaboration; it should be ranged against interferometry, cantilevers, cold atoms and gravitational-wave detector data separately. Note also the declared interest: experimental collaborations reporting bounds have a stake in the outcome of their analysis, but not in the reading of the figures, which is the disqualifying one. Triangulated (Fig 2 :42 plus Fig 10 :59).

## 3. Pre-registered refuter

Written before the conclusion and before outreach, per the standing rule that a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument. The shape claim is: *the question is a union compound whose branches carry separate verdicts, whose anchor is an instrument, whose direct discriminator is unreachable at this budget while an indirect edge is live, and which the figures cannot settle in nature's favour.*

It is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A proof or replicated result shows that objective collapse and unitary-only Everettian quantum mechanics are strictly empirically indistinguishable for all possible measurements. Then the difference is a convention (Fig 2 :41), not a verdict, and F1, F2 and F3 fall.
2. A confirmed positive signal registers a collapse parameter at a specific (lambda, r_C) that survives independent instruments, **or** a null result closes the entire phenomenologically viable collapse parameter space including coloured and dissipative extensions. Then the ground has settled the parameter question and the mode on F9 row 4 moves toward `grounded`.
3. A working experiment performs interference between macroscopic branches or reverses a measurement, the discriminator Vaidman calls gedanken. Then F3's "unreachable at this budget" is false.
4. A re-census shows the binary is a single question rather than a union compound. Then F5 and F7 fall and the verdicts may legitimately be combined.

If any fires, the shape claim is withdrawn. Until one is run, no part of it may be labelled `grounded`.

## 4. Recommendation

1. Do not answer "real" or "apparent." Return the split, the separate verdicts and the instrument that would close each branch (F5, F7).
2. State mode on every sentence. The figures return a shape; they do not decide which interpretation nature implements (F9).
3. Lead with the residual and the pre-registered refuter, then ask what the recipient would count as a failure (Fig 10 :52).
4. Use several low-overlap instruments as the anchor, never a consensus or a single celebrated exclusion (F8).
5. Treat the XENONnT result as a bound on a parameter point, not as the end of collapse models. The paper itself says *"A wider parameter range remains to be probed for colored or dissipative model extensions."*
6. Send the method, not a recording: *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* (Fig 4 :22).
