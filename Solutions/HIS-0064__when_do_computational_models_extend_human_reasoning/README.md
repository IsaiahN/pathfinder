# Tether outreach — figure-grounded package

**Question:** When do computational models extend human reasoning, and when do they conceal assumptions behind complexity?

**Source:** history of science and knowledge.txt line 79 (question 64, section 7. Methods, technologies, and ways of knowing)

**Mode:** `specified` on the discriminator and on each per-domain prediction, `general` on the structural shape. Nothing here is `grounded`: the discriminator is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status:** done

**Registry status:** pending

**Boundary.** Everything in this folder was written for this question. No file outside the solution folder was modified, except `Logs/validation_report.csv` written by the required validator and the appended section in `Logs/completion_log.md`. Figure citations are checked against the local verbatim extract `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`. World sources were opened on 2026-11-23 and are recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.
- No claim that this package settles the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* A list selected for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that would make its agreement worthless (Fig 2 :40).
2. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Sending the framework before asking for a verdict on the residual spends the only thing the outside frame contributes.
3. **A filter is not a witness.** Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."* Parameter count, publication venue and prestige are budget filters here, never evidence that a model extends reasoning.

Legally: scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in institutional channels avoid that failure and the structural one.

## Suggested next step

Take `01` and `08` to the public venues in `03`. Ask what a checker would count as a failure of the discriminator. Record the verdict before showing any theory. A reported failure is more useful than an endorsement.
