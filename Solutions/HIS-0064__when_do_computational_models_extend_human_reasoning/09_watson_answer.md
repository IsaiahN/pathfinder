## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, when a computational model extends human reasoning and when it conceals assumptions behind complexity. Option A: treat complexity itself, measured by size, score or scale, as the discriminator. Option B: treat checkability as the discriminator. What each leads to: A substitutes a quantity the frame produces for the metric and spends the independence of the checker; B preserves a discriminator that an outside frame can test, records the lineage, and stops at the shape when no ground is available. Derived answer: B, with seven failure conditions written before the conclusion.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors, currency and anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Does a model output remain discriminable from the model's own assumptions by an outside frame ranging against the same ground? Arity: one relation with two routings (extension, concealment). Symmetry: asymmetric, the anchor must not update (Fig 2 :33). Scale: the model is one frame at one scale; human, model and ground form a membrane crossing. This is a union compound, not a partition: the domains overlap on the same output.
- **D1, the model as frame.** Actors: Γ (library, parameters), atoms, bonds, R per slot, candidate φ. Currency: bits for the description, actions for settling (Fig 12 :126). Anchor: the ground, meaning data, experiment, instrument. Verdict: extension requires a per-slot residual and a reach that grows; concealment begins when a global score replaces the signal.
- **D2, the human reasoner as frame.** Actors: belief b, weighting w, the sought-for shape as a preference. Currency: the ability to refuse a term and re-aim the question. Anchor: the ground, the problem actually being solved. Verdict: the loop checks its mechanism, not its direction, so the shape must be written down as an ordinary term.
- **D3, the checking arrangement.** Actors: checker, provenance record, channel, importer. Currency: evidence bearing on the residual versus evidence bearing on its maker (Fig 9 :51). Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Verdict: concealment is a channel and provenance failure, not a residual bin.
- **D4, the habitat of deployment.** Actors: habitat, instruments, conditions, introduced actors. Currency: contact (Fig 11 :40). Anchor: the ground. Verdict: extension requires changed contact; a synthetic solve proves wiring only.
- **Order by dependency.** Describe R first (Fig 9 :27-28); secure a non-updating anchor and a per-slot signal before evaluating reach; refuse before mint; keep the checker outside the interiors; test contact last (Fig 3 :20).

Strain test applied: "complexity" is not a figure term. It was re-censused into three figure quantities, library growth without reach growth, unrecorded arrangement, and frame-internal self-scoring. An initial attempt to map concealment to the fifth bin of Fig 5 was retracted and moved to D3. After re-census no strain remains hidden.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The discriminator survives; any ranking of named models does not. A model extends reasoning when its output carries a per-slot residual that an independent checker can range against an unmoved ground through a visible lineage, and when the improvement changes contact in the habitat. It conceals assumptions when a global score replaces the signal, when the anchor moves with the model, when an import is indistinguishable from a derivation, or when the checker holds the interiors. Reach is not library, and a gate is not the ground. Extension and concealment are isomers over one atom set with different bonds, so they cannot be averaged. The result is `specified`, ungrounded, and candidate. The strongest outreach point is canonical: no last room, so no final ranking of models.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and provenance before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside checkers by sending a recording instead of a generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* That cost is avoidable by leading with the residual and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The defensive parts (per-slot reporting, lineage records, outside checker before theory) are removable or revisable without touching the figures, and a sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet. No primitive has to be withdrawn. A narrow failure in D2 or D4 can be retracted without losing D1 or D3.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A protocol that discriminates a live per-slot residual from an aggregate score, an outside checker whose stage-1 verdict is stable after interiors are shown, an instrument that separates import from derivation in a contested case, a demonstration that an agent-only improvement changed contact, or a figure re-census that binds the sought-for shape as a term inside the loop would each reverse the relevant finding. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`. Prestige, venue and headcount are not such instruments (Fig 9 :60).
