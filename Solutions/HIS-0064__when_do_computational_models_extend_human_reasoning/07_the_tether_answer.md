# The Tether's answer: when models extend reasoning and when they conceal assumptions

**Mode: `specified` on the discriminator and on each per-domain prediction, `general` on the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a verdict about any named model. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question is a discriminating one about a checking arrangement. A model is a frame (Symbols table :111). The residual is: does a model output remain discriminable from the model's own assumptions by an outside frame ranging against the same ground? Arity: one relation with two routings. Symmetry: asymmetric, the anchor must not update. Scale: one frame at one scale, with human plus model plus ground forming a membrane crossing. This is a union compound of four domains (Fig 9 :57), so the domain verdicts stay separate and are never averaged.

## 2. The discriminator

1. **Extension begins with a per-slot residual.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A single score is not the signal.
2. **The residual must range against an anchor that does not update.** Fig 2 :33: *"The anchor must not update."* Fig 2 :45: *"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."* A benchmark the model can optimise is not an anchor in this sense.
3. **The lineage must be visible.** Fig 8 :51: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
4. **The checker must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."*
5. **Reach is not library.** Fig 5 :69: *"a library that grows is not a reach that grows."* Fig 6 :14 calls import *"the only operator that moves the wall."* Parameter count is a budget filter.
6. **Settlement is not a gate.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* Fig 5 :70: *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."*
7. **Direction is outside the loop.** Fig 5 :63: *"Every check above runs on the MECHANISM and none runs on the DIRECTION OF THE QUESTION"* The sought-for shape must be written down as an ordinary term and committed before the run (Fig 5 :65).
8. **Deployment is contact, not isolated capability.** Fig 11 :40: *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*

**The shape in one sentence:** a model extends reasoning when its output preserves a per-slot residual that an independent checker can range against an unmoved ground through a visible lineage; it conceals assumptions when a frame-internal proxy replaces any one of those three conditions.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a ranking of architectures, libraries, frameworks or research groups. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32).
- Not a claim that complexity itself is the defect. The defect is library growth without reach growth and unrecorded arrangement.
- Not a claim that a passing benchmark, a large parameter count, a prestigious venue or a consensual room is evidence. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not a claim that provenance is sufficient for truth. Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."*
- Not a claim that a model cannot extend reasoning. The claim is about the conditions under which the checking arrangement does or does not, and it is silent on any particular model until a ground settles that model.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of any named model was taken.

## 5. What would settle it, and the honest stop

A source-discriminating check, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* says an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"* and that the colour case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here the corresponding questions are:

- Can an instrument or protocol discriminate a genuine per-slot residual from an aggregated score that hides a live slot?
- Can a checker who has not seen the interiors range the output against the same ground and return a verdict before any theory is shown? If so, is that verdict stable when the interiors are later revealed?
- Can a lineage record distinguish an imported assumption from a convergent derivation in a case where both are plausible?
- Does an improvement in the model change contact in the habitat, or only performance in the harness?
- Does the model's own direction change when a refused term is added, or does it keep aiming at the original proxy?

Until such checks are run, the case-level answer is **unreached**, not unreachable and not silence. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The six-term substrate is *"a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes"* (They're Scanning Books), so declaring the discriminator makes the choice visible, not neutral. The answer stops at the limit of available instruments and records the lineage.

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not rank models as good or bad, and it does not claim that complexity itself hides assumptions. It says that a computational model extends human reasoning when its output keeps a per-slot error signal that a checker can test against a ground neither of them can move, and when the lineage shows which assumptions were imported from outside. It conceals assumptions when a score the model produces is substituted for that signal, when the anchor moves with the model, or when the checker is shown the interiors and can no longer stand outside the claim. These are a product, not a checklist: one condition at zero is enough. Reach is not library, and a gate is not the ground. What is offered is the shape any checkable answer must have, seven failure conditions written in advance, and the instruments that would close it. No particular model is named, because the framework returns a shape and the ground decides.
