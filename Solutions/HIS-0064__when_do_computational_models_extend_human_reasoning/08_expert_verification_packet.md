# Expert verification packet — the Tether on computational models and reasoning

**What this is:** a discriminator in a form a computational-modeling researcher can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` — not `grounded`.** No measurement of any named model is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

A computational model extends human reasoning when its output preserves a per-slot residual that an independent checker can range against a ground that does not move when either frame pushes on it, and when the lineage shows which assumptions were imported from outside. It conceals assumptions when a frame-internal proxy replaces any one of those conditions: a global score where a per-slot signal should be, a moving anchor where a fixed one should be, or a checker who already holds the interiors. The three conditions are a product, not a checklist: one at zero is enough. Reach is not library, and a gate is not the ground. No architecture is named. What is offered is the shape any checkable answer must have, with refuters written in advance.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — Framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 1 and 2

- **Statement:** a global score cannot substitute for a per-slot residual ranged against an unmoved anchor. The ground must not update on the model or the checker.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 1 :19 — *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :27 — *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* Fig 2 :33 — *"The anchor must not update."*
- **What to check:** does the proposed evaluation keep a live signal per object, region or condition, and is the reference point independent of the model's optimisation? A benchmark that the model can optimise is not an anchor in this sense.
- **Refuter:** exhibit a model whose global score improves while a named slot residual persists unfunded, with routing decisions still correct; or show that an anchor which updates on the frame still corrects the frame rather than drifting with it.

### C2 — Provenance-facing; checkable by inspecting any modeling pipeline

- **Statement:** without a recorded lineage, an assumption imported from a different frame is indistinguishable from something the model derived, and the missing bond information leaves no trace in the output.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :51 — *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them"* Fig 12 :5 — *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."*
- **What to check:** can a reader reconstruct which prior, objective, corpus or architecture choice came from outside, and which was derived inside the run? Are the bonds between them recorded, or only the ingredient list?
- **Refuter:** show a case where a fully provenanced output yields two incompatible source attributions and no instrument can discriminate them, while the lineage is still called white box; or show that provenance alone settles a claim against the ground.

### C3 — Framework-internal derivation with an engineering parallel

- **Statement:** a checker who can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it, and passing a gate is not the same as settlement against the ground.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 10 :42 — *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Fig 5 :70 — *"A term is settled when the ground pays for it. A term is usable when something can compose over it."*
- **What to check:** was the checker's verdict on stage 1 recorded before the interiors were shown? Is a held-out score being reported as settlement, when it is a gate?
- **Refuter:** show a checker with full access to interiors returning a verdict that is independent in the Fig 2 sense, measured by low overlap of evidence; or show that passing a declared gate guarantees ground settlement with no further contact.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not a ranking of architectures, libraries or research groups. The framework returns a shape, not a name (Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"*).
- Not that complexity itself is the defect. The defect is library growth without reach growth (Fig 5 :69) plus unrecorded arrangement (Fig 12 :5).
- Not that a benchmark, parameter count, prestigious venue or consensual room is evidence. Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*
- Not that provenance is truth. Fig 10 :65: *"Provenance shows a ruling was reached legitimately. It never shows it was right: the ground does that, and a wrong ruling stays wrong however clean its record."*
- Not that a synthetic solve shows capability. Fig 11 :70: *"A test harness is a substituted habitat. Which is why a synthetic solve proves wiring and never capability."*
- Not `grounded`. Nothing here was settled by measurement.

## 5. What would settle it

A source-discriminating check, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the colour case was resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* Here:

1. Does a protocol discriminate a genuine per-slot residual from an aggregate that hides a live slot?
2. Does an outside checker, denied the interiors, return a stable verdict on stage 1 before any theory is shown?
3. Does a lineage record distinguish an imported assumption from a convergent derivation in a contested case?
4. Does the improvement change contact in the habitat, or only performance in the harness?
5. Does a refused term change the direction of the model's question, or does it keep aiming at the old proxy?

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 1 :19, Fig 1 :27, Fig 2 :33, Fig 8 :51, Fig 12 :5, Fig 10 :42, Fig 5 :70. Seven short passages.
2. Check C1 against one evaluation you know: is there a per-slot signal and an anchor that does not move?
3. Check C2 against one pipeline you know: could a reader tell an import from a derivation?
4. Check C3 against one review you have seen: was the reviewer able to reconstruct the claim?
5. Name one instrument or protocol that would discriminate one of the five questions in §5, or say why none is possible.
6. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
