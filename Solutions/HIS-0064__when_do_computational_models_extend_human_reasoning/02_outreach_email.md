**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the three checkable sub-claims in `08_expert_verification_packet.md` have been re-read and the mode labels are intact. The stage-1 output must survive on its own.

**Subject:** One discriminator about computational models, checked before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about computational modeling. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. I am asking what you would count as a failure. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** A model is a bounded system with a library. The question is: when does a model plus a checker increase what can be checked against the ground, and when does the same arrangement make the model's assumptions uncheckable because they sit inside its complexity? This is not a question about whether models are good. It is a discriminating question about a checking arrangement.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A model extends reasoning when three conditions hold together:
> 1. its output carries a residual indexed per object slot, so a live signal is not averaged away;
> 2. an independent checker can range that residual against a ground that does not move when the model or the checker pushes on it;
> 3. the lineage is visible, so an assumption imported from outside is distinguishable from something the model derived.
>
> It conceals assumptions when any one of those three is replaced by a proxy the frame produces itself: a global score, a benchmark rank, a parameter count, or a provenance record treated as proof. The two regimes are not a matter of degree. They are different relations to the ground, and I keep them separate rather than averaging them.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not naming an architecture, a field or a technique as the extender and another as the concealer. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Complexity itself is not the enemy: the figure target is a library that grows without a reach that grows, plus an arrangement that is not recorded. Parameter count is a budget filter, never a witness. I am also not claiming that passing a validation gate settles a model against the ground. A gate is not the ground.
>
> **What would refute the discriminator.** I wrote these down before running this:
> 1. a frame scoring itself with a quantity it produces is admitted as a verdict;
> 2. growing the library alone increases the composable, settled reach without any import or new ground contact;
> 3. a checker who has been given the model's full interiors still returns a verdict that is independent in the sense that the two frames draw on different evidence;
> 4. an agent-only improvement changes the model's contact with the ground;
> 5. a model checks the direction of its own question from inside its own closure.
>
> Any one of these holds a real failure, not a stylistic objection. If one fires, I narrow or withdraw the claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **Before you answer, one question:** what would you expect to see if this discriminator were wrong? I want that recorded before I show you where it came from, because once you have the framework's interiors, your later agreement is not evidence.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** take the residual and the pre-registered refuter, not the conclusion. Run it on a modeling problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> — [your name]
