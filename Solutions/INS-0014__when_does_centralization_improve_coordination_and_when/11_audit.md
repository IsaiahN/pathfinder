## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down, `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually uses.

3. **Concretisation (T_E):** an actual institutional recommendation for a given decision class, for example moving procurement decisions to a central unit while leaving disability assessments local.

**R_T is nonzero.** Four things the figure set holds do not survive the trip back down:

- **Per-slot indexing.** The slogan says where to put decisions; it does not carry the slot index that tells you where to look (Fig 1 :22).
- **The import gate.** The slogan does not carry provenance or the shadow test needed before a local reading is imported upward (Fig 8).
- **The fixed-atom condition on the isomer claim.** "Same atoms, different bond" becomes false the moment the reform adds an audit office or removes a local board; the relation is then composition plus import (Fig 6 :23).
- **The source-discriminating instrument.** The slogan does not say what instrument would tell a method from a recording, or an anchor from a report (Hard Problem is About Source).

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: a re-applicable method may cross the membrane while a recording may not, and a reference that updates on the reports it grades is a frame. That is the residue worth sending. R_T was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
