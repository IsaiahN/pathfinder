## Headline verdict in one paragraph

The question is a **union of three conditions**, not one dial with a right midpoint. Coordination is a composition that pays when a re-applicable method crosses a scale boundary, when a shared meaning stays a convention, and when frames range against a ground that does not update on their own reports. Local knowledge is per-slot contact with a habitat; aggregation destroys its signal, and a center that sharpens it into center-only terms loses the very thing it was trying to use. Accountability is a checking arrangement, not a reporting line: it survives only while a seat can refuse and the anchor cannot be moved by the organization's own reports. Centralized and decentralized forms over a fixed set of actors and instruments are isomers, so type and bond are the variable, not degree. No figure returns an optimal span, layer count, or ratio. That is a declared `gap`, reported as unreachable rather than silence.
