## VERDICT (from the figures)

The question is a union compound, so there is no single answer and no optimal degree. Centralization improves coordination when what crosses the scale boundary is a re-applicable method or a head start, when a genuinely missing instrument is minted, and when a shared meaning stays a convention; it fails when a recording of one success is replayed as policy or when a convention decides an outcome. It undermines local knowledge where slots are pooled without the slot index, where a local reading is sharpened into center-only terms, or where a substituted habitat removes the contact that made the local reading correct. It undermines accountability when the reference updates on the reports it grades, when all checking happens in one room drawing on one evidence pool, or when a verifier holds the interiors it is meant to check. Over a fixed atom set the two forms are isomers, so the variable is bond type, not degree. No figure returns a ratio, span, layer count or named organ: that is a declared `gap`, unreached rather than unreachable. The whole result is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, and cannot settle the question it reports on.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states its mode, per-domain verdicts and refuters before the conclusion, so a wrong separator is findable. The real cost is spending the independence of an outside checker by sending a conclusion instead of a residual. Fig 2 :42 "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. A separator can be narrowed to the decision classes where a refuter fires without withdrawing the method. The live isomer strain can be scoped to fixed-atom reforms. A gap is not a debt: nothing invented has to be taken back.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An aggregate shown to preserve per-slot where-to-look signal; a reference shown to track the ground independently while updating on reports; a center shown to derive a correct local particular with no import; a local decision shown to remain correct after contact is removed; a centralized and decentralized pair with the same atoms and bonds behaving differently; a figure line returning a number or named organ. Any of these narrows or closes the relevant verdict. Until one fires, the result stands as `candidate`.
