# The Tether's answer: centralization, coordination, local knowledge, accountability

**Mode: `specified` on the per-domain separators, `general` on the structural shape, not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §7), not a ground reading. No state, agency, or policy was measured.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question asks for a condition under which centralization pays and a condition under which it fails. The residual is the per-slot gap between what the governing frame predicts and what the local ground returns, plus the gap between the reading a superior sees and the reading that could have settled the slot. It is indexed per slot (Fig 1 :19: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."). It is a **union compound**, not one hard question: coordination, local knowledge, and accountability have different currencies and different anchors (Fig 9 :57: "a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"). Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape

1. **Centralization improves coordination when what crosses is a method, not a recording.** Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." At the new scale the kernel is unchanged; only "the actors, the currency and the anchor" change (Fig 4 :20). A shared standard that stays a convention is a genuine gain, because "agreement about a shared meaning is the product, not a corruption of it" (Fig 2 :41). A recording of one success replayed downward produces "a system that repeats a past success and cannot produce a new one" (Fig 4 :25).

2. **It loses local knowledge by aggregation.** Fig 1 :22: "Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average." The center cannot derive what it has never received: "composition inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom" (Fig 8 :32). Local knowledge reaches a center only by contact with a differing closure (Fig 6 :23), with provenance and a shadow test; and "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms" (Fig 6 :30).

3. **Its advantage is contact, not virtue.** "capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone" (Fig 11 :39), and "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves" (Fig 11 :40). Moving a decision upward substitutes a habitat; "what you failed to reproduce is invisible until the goal fails" (Fig 11 :68). "No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual" (Fig 11 :51), so there is no permanent set of decisions that belong at one level.

4. **It undermines accountability when the reference becomes a report.** "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks" (Fig 2 :29). If the indicator is produced by the units it grades, "the anchor becomes another frame, everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices" (Fig 2 :34). "the ground does not decay, the channel does" (Fig 10 :24).

5. **Accountability needs a non-updating ground and a seat that can refuse.** "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it" (Fig 10 :42). A single central review room is "one long room" (Fig 7 :36); "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict" (Fig 7 :35). And "a convention that decides an outcome has stopped being one" (Fig 10 :47): a shared standard is fine until it fixes the verdict.

6. **The two arrangements are isomers over a fixed atom set.** "Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour" (Fig 12 :117). So type and bond are the variable, not degree. The fixed-atom condition is a live strain, named in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.

7. **There is no universal degree.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." No figure selects a span, layer count, ratio, or named organ. That is a declared `gap`: unreached at this budget, owed an instrument, not silence.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that centralization is always bad. It is the right carrier for methods, conventions, and genuinely absent instruments.
- Not that decentralization is always good. A local room with no external ground is still one room, and local agreement "is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" (Fig 2 :40).
- Not that a particular court, audit office, or chamber is the answer. The framework returns a shape; naming a held arrangement is provenance, not a derivation (`05` §6).
- Not a universal percentage, span of control, layer count, or org chart.
- Not grounded. No measurement at any institution was taken. Nothing here is marked `grounded`.
- Not a claim that the isomer reading is unconditional. It holds over a fixed atom set; otherwise the relation is composition plus import.

## 5. What would settle it (the ground)

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; the spectrometer settled it. Here:

- **Per-slot test.** Does the central reading preserve the slot index and the where-to-look signal, or does it pool them? (Fig 1 :19, :22).
- **Anchor test.** Does the reference move when the units it grades change their reports? If yes, it is a frame.
- **Import test.** Can the center point to the source and the gap that a local reading explained before importing it? (Fig 8).
- **Room test.** Is the checker's evidence drawn from a closure that differs from the checked one, with a ground neither can move? (Fig 2 :31, :35).
- **Standing refuters.** The seven in `01` §3. Any one firing narrows or withdraws the relevant finding.

## 6. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape stops where the available instruments stop distinguishing. Fig 9 :44: "Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room. So searching and finding nothing proves nothing." The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. "Indiscernibility is not identity." For any real state, the question of which boundary is right for which decision class needs an instrument and a ground, which this derivation does not supply.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not return a universal amount of centralization. It separates three conditions that the question bundles. Coordination improves when what crosses the scale boundary is a re-applicable method or a head start, and when a shared meaning stays a convention; it fails when a recording of one success is replayed as policy. Local knowledge is per-slot contact with a habitat, so an aggregate that drops the slot index destroys the only signal that says where to look, and a center can only receive local knowledge as an import with provenance. Accountability is a checking arrangement: it needs a ground that does not update on the organization's own reports and a seat able to refuse, not a bigger reporting room where everyone reads the same pool. Over a fixed set of actors and instruments the centralized and decentralized forms are isomers, same atoms arranged differently, so the variable is the bond and not the degree. No figure returns a ratio or an org chart; that is a declared gap, an instrument owed rather than silence. What is offered is the separator, refuters written in advance, and the test that would close it.
