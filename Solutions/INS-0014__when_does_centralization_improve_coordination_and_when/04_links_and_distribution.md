**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the separator and one refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1.** Send the residual, the union shape, and the seven refuters. Ask what they would count as failure. No theory is attached.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure link, the architecture essay, and the tacit-knowledge essay. Their agreement after this point is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. Each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode before running it.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if stage 1 survives. The universal percentage is already withdrawn as a `gap`; do not reintroduce it in the email to make the claim sound more practical.
