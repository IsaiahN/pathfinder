## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself." Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence," and the figure immediately adds that the system "cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

So a **shape** here is the narrowed form of an answer that can be stated before it can be settled. For this question the shape is:

> separate the currency and the anchor of each condition, keep the residual indexed per slot, carry methods and conventions across the scale boundary but never replays, keep the ground non-updating and at least one seat able to refuse, and decline the universal ratio.

That is a constraint set. It is not "centralize 60 percent of decisions". The latter is a name where the figure returns a shape.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary already held that is closest to the thing, look for new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term gained, you narrow the space by definition and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause." So the residual is described as "a per-slot gap between predicted and actual local outcome that disappears from view when slots are pooled", not as "a lack of coordination".
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world." This is the reason the packet in `08` sends effects and refuters rather than the architecture.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place", and "By the time you learn the EXACT search terms to find the CORRECT book, you won't need to read it anymore, because you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it." The parallel here is that once you can state the separator per decision class, the question "centralize or decentralize?" stops being meaningful.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays." For an institution, that means an outcome that does not move when the organization re-describes it: a service result, an audit finding, a court ruling, a measured local condition.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Bringing in a frontline measurement procedure from another jurisdiction is an import; it must say where it came from and which gap it explains.

*Hard Problem is About Source* gives the discriminator. An instrument that reads green "cannot comment on the source"; "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information (it cannot define consciousness in full)." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that settled it. For public administration the analogue is a source-discriminating reading: a satisfaction aggregate cannot tell you whether the cause was the policy, the local implementation, or the reporting incentive. "An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."

## 4. Why a central report cannot settle the local source

The central report is a reading off a projection. Hayek's point (1945, read this session) is that central statistics "cannot take direct account of these circumstances of time and place" because they lump together items differing in ways significant for the specific decision. The tacit-knowledge essay sharpens it: "we can know more than we can tell" (Polanyi 1966, quoted there); writing carries context, carries subtext partly, and "almost never carries the pretext, the 'you had to be there' part." What is lost is not a detail but the history that makes a compressed local signal mean anything.

Fig 11 :40: "An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves." A better central dashboard that never reaches the local ground is an improvement to the agent in isolation, which the figure refuses.

## 5. The question as a bundle

*Library of Babel* gives the instruction that governs F1 of the audit: "if the final book in your exhaustive search doesn't answer the question, then there are only two possibilities: the nature of your question was unanswerable, or malformed. Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually." Fig 9 :54 and :57 say the same in the figure language. So the shape returned by this run is a **separator**, not a verdict on centralization as such.

## 6. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I initially read the README's "a shape, not a name" as a ban on naming any specific institutional arrangement. That is wrong, and the correction is kept here rather than erased.

The README's full phrase is "what a **missing** thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." The subject is a primitive not held in Γ. A court, an audit office, a shared standard, or a local ombudsman are not missing primitives in this domain; they are bindings from the institutional frame onto arrangements the actors already hold. Naming one is a provenanced binding and a candidate, not the invention of a seventh term. What remains forbidden is claiming that the figure selected it, or that the binding moves the wall (Fig 6 : "IMPORT adds an atom" applies to a genuinely new atom, which this is not). The same correction appears in the exemplar's `05` §5b and is carried by the pilot solutions.

My §4 metamerism point is **not** retracted. The reason a central reading cannot name the local source is the missing discriminator, not a ban on naming held arrangements. Both claims are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 7. Two consequences for this answer

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly." And: "Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of distinguished commentators who agree with a centralization thesis is the named collapse. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the separator a budget to be tried, and let the local ground settle it.

**This audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." And the framework's own limit: "the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself." So `01` is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is not a test of any institution and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
