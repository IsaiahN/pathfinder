## 4. Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines `R_T` as `gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, the difference between what was sent up and what came back down, and Fig 4 :30 requires it to be measured rather than assumed.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 1 :19 and :22 (per-slot residual), Fig 12 :5 and :94 (bond carries information), Fig 2 :40-41 (convention versus verdict), Fig 8 :45 (two frames).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in the folder: same tokens, different residual; context supplies the bond; agreement is a convention; the ground decides.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim a specialist would test, for example "context is the library `Γ`" or "the bond context supplies is sequence `→`."

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the bond**. The shorthand says context supplies a bond; Fig 12 defines seven and selects none. The concrete claim must choose, and the choice is an import from another frame, not a derivation.
- the **mapping of "context"**. The shorthand treats context as one thing; the tacit essay splits receiver-side structure into context, subtext, and pretext, and Symbols splits it into `Γ`, `b`, and `w`. The concrete claim loses the selector unless it keeps both levels.
- the **valence of agreement**. The shorthand says agreement is not evidence; Fig 2 :41 preserves the other valence, that agreement about shared meaning is the product. Dropping it makes the shorthand misleading about what coordination is for.

Conversely, two things survive cleanly: information is a per-slot residual against a receiving frame, and the difference is carried by the arrangement rather than the token list. That is the residue worth sending. `R_T` was **not** measured numerically, because no ground reading exists; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the formula is the condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set `R_T` to zero and would be the dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names.
