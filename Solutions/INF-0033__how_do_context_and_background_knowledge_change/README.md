## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to believe the claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons, from the framework's own rules. First, agreement among frames that share an evidence pool is not a witness: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"* (Fig 2 :40). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures exactly that pool. Second, *"Two frames together reach what neither reaches alone"* (Fig 8 :45) depends on the frames differing, so selecting them to agree destroys the surplus. Third, *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it"* (Fig 10 :42), so the first contact carries the residual and its refuter, not the framework's interior. Legally, unsolicited bulk email to scraped addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. The public opt-in venues in `03` avoid that failure as well as the structural one.
