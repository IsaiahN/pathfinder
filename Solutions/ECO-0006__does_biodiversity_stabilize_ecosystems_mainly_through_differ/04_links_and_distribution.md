| Tilman, Reich & Knops 2006, Nature | https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04742 | Decade-long Cedar Creek grassland experiment. *"Temporal stability of the ecosystem increased with diversity, despite a lower temporal stability of individual species, because of both portfolio (statistical averaging) and overyielding effects. **Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, the shape and the refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure; (3) record their verdict; (4) only then the links, with the note that the figures, not this summary, are the authority; (5) offer the formula as a method to reuse.

## The two-stage offer

Stage 1 is a characterisation of R and its refuter, not a theory. Stage 2 is opt-in by construction: once a recipient has absorbed the frame, their agreement stops being evidence, so the stage-1 verdict must be recorded first and later agreement is never pooled as corroboration. Stage 3 sends a method, which is permitted, but each user must name a ground and write a refuter before running it. Hard precondition: stages 1 and 2 only mean anything if the stage-1 shape survives the checks in `08_expert_verification_packet.md`.
