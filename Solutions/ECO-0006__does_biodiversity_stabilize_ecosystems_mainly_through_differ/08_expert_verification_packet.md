# Expert verification packet — the Tether on diversity and stability

**What this is:** a claim in a form an ecologist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the union limit, NOT `grounded`.** No measurement is claimed by this derivation. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

The question "does biodiversity stabilize ecosystems mainly through differences among species, or through the presence of particular influential species?" is malformed as an either/or. The two conditions overlap, so the correct move is to unbundle them and examine the intersection. Three consequences follow from the figures: a species list records ingredients and not the arrangement that carries stability, so same-list communities with different covariance are different substances; influential actors are roles relative to a stated residual, and the figures license a functional kind more readily than an irreplaceable name; and stability is a property of community-and-habitat, with per-slot effects that aggregation hides. The framework does not return the fraction f carried by each route. That fraction is owed to identity swaps, targeted removals and component decomposition on a named stability property, habitat and perturbation.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 — framework-internal; checkable by reading Figures 9 and 12

- **Statement:** the `or` is a union, not a partition; unbundle before searching; the arrangement is the substance.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 9 :54 *"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* Fig 9 :55 *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."*
- **Refuter:** show one mechanism accounting for the whole diversity-stability effect across habitats with the other at zero; or show that a species list alone predicts stability as well as a list plus its temporal covariance structure, across the same habitats.

### C2 — ecology-facing; checkable against experiments and monitoring

- **Statement:** there is no fixed, context-independent list of influential species; the influential channel is a functional kind whose member varies with habitat and residual. The per-slot effect is real but is hidden by aggregation.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :51 *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* Fig 11 :49 *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."* Fig 12 :123 *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."*
- **What to check:** do targeted removal and substitution experiments support a role rather than a name? Is the keystone or dominant effect stable across habitats and perturbations, or does it reverse as the Menge and Paine case histories suggest? Does per-species analysis find a live slot that the diversity index hides?
- **Refuter:** a fixed species list predicting stability across residuals and habitats; or a substitution series in which functionally similar species never substitute; or evidence that averaging preserves the per-slot signal.

### C3 — framework-internal derivation with a world-facing consequence

- **Statement:** stability is not a property of a species set alone; habitat, trophic level and time window must be enumerated, and species-level results can become atoms for community-level composition.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 11 :39 *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :43 *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* Fig 12 :104 *"A settled molecule becomes an atom for whatever composes over it. So irreducible is a fact about a level and never about the world."*
- **What to check:** does the published effect survive when habitat, trophic level and time window are added as terms? Hsieh et al. 2026 find response diversity stabilizing within but not across trophic levels; Wagg et al. 2022 find the mechanisms change with community age. Meng et al. 2025 find species-specific dynamics, not interactions, dominant in grasslands. Do these locate to different habitats and scales, as the union reading predicts?
- **Refuter:** a species-only model predicting stability across habitats and trophic levels with no habitat term; or a demonstration that the Meng and Hsieh results are the same mechanism measured twice (shared evidence pool), not a context difference.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "the answer is niche complementarity", "the answer is dominance", or "the answer is keystone predation". These are names where the figures return a shape.
- Not that statistical averaging, compensatory dynamics, species-specific dynamics or biotic interactions is the winner inside the differences side. The world literature conflicts here.
- Not that richness is irrelevant, and not that a diversity index is useless. It is a lossy reading, not a source.
- Not a fixed list of influential species. Roles yes, names no.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment, dataset or monitoring analysis was run for this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it

A discriminating protocol, not a further argument:

1. Identity swap at fixed richness and habitat. Stability change implies identity work; no change implies the arrangement among equivalents.
2. Targeted removal of the dominant or keystone actor at fixed richness. A crash implies presence work.
3. Functional substitution. No loss of stability implies the kind, not the member.
4. Component decomposition of asynchrony (Zhao et al. 2022) or of interaction dependence (Meng et al. 2025), reporting f for a stated S, H and P.
5. Full habitat and trophic enumeration per Fig 11 :43-47, with time window reported per Hsieh et al. 2026.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 9 :54-55, Fig 12 :5, :94, :123, Fig 11 :39, :43, :49, :51 and Fig 1 :22. Ten short passages.
2. Check C2 against a system you know: does the influential actor keep its role across habitats and perturbations, or does it reverse?
3. Name one protocol that would distinguish differences from presence in your system, or say why none is possible.
4. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
