## 1. A shape is a constraint you can state before you can fill it

The canonical figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So for this question a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer that constrains without filling. The shape here is: a union of overlapping conditions, unbundle before searching; the arrangement carries information the list does not; influential actors are roles relative to a residual; the habitat and trophic level must be enumerated. A **name** would be "the answer is niche complementarity" or "the answer is keystone predation". The framework does not return either.

## 2. This question's specific bundle failure is named in the essays

*The Library of Babel* names the exact defect in this question's `or`: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually to find their answers and then combine them in a satisfactory way."* That is the essay-level counterpart of Fig 9 :54-55: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."* The same conclusion is reached from two different starting points, one about searching a library and one about compositions in closure(Γ). Low overlap, same shape.

The consequence for outreach is that the first question to an expert is not "which mechanism is right" but "what would you count as a failure of the union claim". That is a checkable effect description, not a request for belief.

## 3. How a shape is obtained here

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is *"closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp the shape of"*, then look for new terms that *"overlap, correlate, or are even used interchangeably"*. In this run, "variance reduction" was bootstrapped toward asynchrony, response diversity, statistical averaging, selection effects and keystone status.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* The email therefore describes what would be observed (a fraction f that changes under identity swaps and removals), not a mechanism name.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why the public contact note leads with a refuter.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: *"you know that you are done because the finish line will also be the starting place"*, and the paradox is that by then *"you have already composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the stop is when the residual stops arriving, not when a literature list is exhausted.

## 4. What licenses a name, and the source-error this question invites

Only two things license a name, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For ECO-0006 that means a measurement of f for a stated S, H and P.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Portfolio theory from economics is such an import: it predicts variance reduction from differences among assets. It corroborates the shape but does not decide the ecology.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the instrument warning that matters here: *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* Several mechanisms can produce the same reading of lower temporal variance. An instrument that reads the variance *"cannot comment on the source"*. The essay's example is metamerism in colour vision, resolved *"not by argument, but by the arrival of a better instrument."* The ecological analogue is already visible: Zhao et al. 2022 split asynchrony into statistical averaging and compensatory dynamics; Meng et al. 2025 split interaction-independent from interaction-dependent effects. Each is a better instrument, not a better argument. *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."*

## 5. The specific forbidden error in this question

The forbidden error is treating a diversity index as if it were the source rather than a reading. A single number (richness, Shannon, evenness) is a projection that cannot comment on which carrier produced the stabilization. Naming "niche complementarity" or "the keystone species" from that reading alone is the metamerism error: a source named from a reading when several sources could give the same reading. What is owed is a discriminating protocol (identity swaps, targeted removals, component decomposition), not a preferred vocabulary.

A second forbidden error is selecting the expert by likely agreement. *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"* and *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* A list of people chosen because they already believe in complementarity gives the claim a budget; it does not settle anything.

## 6. My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading

Same essay: LLMs *"can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*, but *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md`, `06_isomers_and_order.md` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the ecological claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report on.

## 7. One-paragraph canonical answer

> For this question, "shape" means the constraint that survives before any mechanism is named: the difference-versus-influential-species `or` is a union of overlapping conditions and must be unbundled; a species list records ingredients and not the arrangement that carries stability; influential species are roles relative to a stated residual, never a fixed list; and the habitat, trophic level and time window must be enumerated before any mechanism is credited. The framework returns that constraint and an open slot for the fraction f. It does not return "complementarity" or "keystone species" as the answer, and it does not return a number. The number is owed to a ground reading, and until one arrives the verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate.
