# Solution ECO-0006 — figure-grounded package

**Question:** Does biodiversity stabilize ecosystems mainly through differences among species, or through the presence of particular influential species?

**Source:** `Questions/ecology.txt` line 9. **Section:** 1. Biodiversity, stability, and resilience.

**Mode:** `specified` on the structural shape, `general` on the union limit, NOT `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this derivation. The empirical weighting between the two mechanisms is a `gap` from the figures and is owed to a ground reading.

**Status:** done. **Registry status:** pending.

## The question restated as an effect

Two candidate carriers are offered for one observed effect: higher temporal stability of an ecosystem property in a more diverse community. Carrier D says the effect is carried by the *differences* among species, their non-redundant traits, asynchronous fluctuations and distinct environmental responses. Carrier I says it is carried by the *presence* of particular influential species, through selection, dominance, foundation or keystone effects. The decision is not cosmetic. Under D, losing any species removes a distinct contribution and the identity of any one species is largely interchangeable. Under I, losing the influential species is catastrophic while losing the rest is nearly free. An answer is a statement of which intervention follows. It is bound as an ordinary term in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 1 and allowed to accrue a residual, per the formula's "Direction" rule.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three figure-grounded reasons:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."* Many reviewers selected for agreement produce a convention, not a check.
2. **Shared evidence disqualifies agreement as a verdict.** Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."* Several reviews citing the same grassland experiments are one witness wearing several names.
3. **A verifier must not hold the interiors.** Fig 10 :42: *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.

Legally, scraping personal emails for unsolicited bulk outreach breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions. Public opt-in channels avoid that failure as well as the structural one.

## Honest standing

The figures decide the *shape* of the answer, which is a union that must be unbundled; they do not decide which mechanism dominates in nature. That part is owed to experiments and monitoring, and the world sources are secondary corroboration only.
