## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about the claim that biodiversity stabilizes ecosystems mainly through differences among species (Option D) or mainly through the presence of particular influential species (Option I). What each leads to: choosing one side as written asks a union to behave like a partition, ignores the arrangement a species list omits, freezes influential actors into a fixed list, and sends an expert a name where the framework returns a shape. Keeping the shape instead unbundles the two conditions, preserves the per-slot signal, leaves the influential actor as a role relative to a stated residual, and hands the numerical fraction f to a ground reading with a pre-registered refuter.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The `or` is a union of overlapping conditions and must be unbundled before either carrier is searched. The arrangement among species carries information a species list does not, so same-list communities with different covariance are isomers rather than variants. Influential actors are roles relative to a stated residual, and the figures license a functional kind more readily than an irreplaceable name. Per-slot effects must be kept before aggregation, and stability belongs to community and habitat. The framework does not return the fraction carried by each route. That is a `gap` from the figures, owed to a ground reading. The verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The strongest single checkable point is the union claim: Wagg et al. 2022 independently report that the two mechanisms are not mutually exclusive.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, three sub-claims, verbatim lines and refuters before the conclusion, so an error is findable. The real cost is sending a name ("it is complementarity", "it is the keystone") where a shape is owed, which would convert an outside check into a convention (Fig 2 :40). That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. No primitive is withdrawn. A wrong union reading can be narrowed to a partition by the refuters in section 2 of the packet, and a corrected packet can supersede a sent one. The mechanism names are withheld precisely so nothing has to be retracted later.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Identity swaps at fixed richness, targeted removal of the dominant or keystone actor, and functional substitution would fix the fraction f; a fixed cross-residual influential list would break Fig 11 :51; a species list as informative as a list plus covariance would break Fig 12 :5; averaging that preserves the per-slot signal would break Fig 1 :22; a single mechanism at zero on the other across habitats would break Fig 9 :54. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
