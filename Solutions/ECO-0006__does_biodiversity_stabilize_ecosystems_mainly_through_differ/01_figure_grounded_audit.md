## 0. The claim under audit

> Option D: biodiversity stabilizes ecosystems mainly through **differences among species** (niche complementarity, asynchrony, response diversity, the portfolio or insurance effect).
>
> Option I: biodiversity stabilizes ecosystems mainly through the **presence of particular influential species** (selection and dominance effects, foundation species, keystone species, ecosystem engineers).
>
> The question is posed as an `or`. This audit treats the `or` itself as part of the residual, not as a neutral piece of grammar.

### 0.1 Residual, stated in effect terms

The observed effect is that temporal variability of an ecosystem property is lower in more diverse communities. Two carriers are proposed. They differ in the intervention each licenses:

| | carrier D, differences | carrier I, influential species |
|---|---|---|
| what does the work | the relations among species, their non-redundant responses | the identity of one or a few particular species |
| what is lost when a species disappears | a distinct contribution to variance reduction | nothing, unless it is the influential one |
| conservation implication | preserve variety and the arrangement | preserve the particular actor |
| figure analogue | the bond and the arrangement (Fig 12) | the slot with live residual (Fig 1), the functional group (Fig 12) |

**R by arity, symmetry and scale.** Arity: two conditions plus their intersection, so at least two, and the question is a union candidate rather than a partition. Symmetry: asymmetric. D is a between-species quantity (a relation, not an attribute of one entity). I is a slot-localized quantity (an attribute of one entity with cascading effects). They are not two readings of the same fact. Scale: D is a cross-scale composition from species-level fluctuations to community-level variance; I is a slot-level effect that can cascade across trophic levels. The answer therefore depends on which stability property, which perturbation and which habitat.

**Direction bound as an ordinary term.** "An answer would look like: for stability property S in habitat H under perturbation P, a measured fraction f of the diversity effect is carried by between-species differences and a fraction 1-f by the presence of particular species, with a stated protocol for holding richness constant while varying identity." This written shape is the bound term that accrues a residual and is refusable by the same test as everything else. It prevents the frame from smuggling in a preferred answer as an invisible preference (Fig 5 :65-66, *"make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM"*).

## 3. Findings F1 to F8

### F1 — The `or` is a union, not a partition (decisive, triangulated)

Fig 9 :54-56:

> **"And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again."**
> **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones. A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."**

Fig 9 :57-58:

> **"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either"**
> **"It stops being a mixture and becomes a specific compound."**

A plot can contain asynchronous species *and* a dominant species. Adding species is a single lever that can clear the region where only one condition holds, while the intersection stands because the second condition still binds. The Library of Babel essay says the same in its own vocabulary: *"Perhaps your problem was a bundle or conflation of several problems masquerading as one? You will need to search all of those problems individually."* The world literature corroborates from a different evidence pool: Wagg et al. 2022 report that the two stabilizing mechanisms *"are not mutually exclusive"* and that *"the underlying mechanisms by which species diversity stabilizes ecosystem functioning themselves can change as the communities develop over time."*

**Verdict:** the question cannot be answered as a clean A-or-B. It must be unbundled into conditions and run as one loop per condition. **Triangulated** (Fig 9 figure rule plus an ecological result on different data, low overlap). Refuter: a census across habitats and stability properties in which one mechanism accounts for the whole diversity effect with the other at zero.

### F2 — The arrangement is the substance (governs carrier D, triangulated)

Fig 12 :5 and :94:

> **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."**
> **"The arrangement is the substance."**

Fig 12 :117:

> **"Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."**

A species list is the flat formula of Fig 12 :14, *"the ingredients are recorded and the arrangement is not."* Two communities with the same species list but different temporal covariance are not variants of one thing; under Fig 12 they are different substances. The ecological literature calls the arrangement asynchrony, compensatory dynamics, statistical averaging, response diversity or complementarity. Zhao et al. 2022 find that *"statistical averaging, rather than compensatory dynamics, was the principal mediator of biodiversity effects on community stability"* in their grassland data; Hsieh et al. 2026 find response diversity stabilizes biomass with a *"context-dependent"*, time-varying effect. Both are readings of the arrangement, not of a list.

**Verdict:** carrier D is figure-licensed as a *relation among species*, not as a count of species. **Triangulated** (Fig 12 plus multiple ecological studies with different systems). Refuter: a species list alone predicts temporal stability as well as a list plus its covariance structure, across the same habitats.

### F3 — No standing list of influential actors survives a change of residual (governs carrier I, triangulated)

Fig 11 :49-51:

> **"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."**
> **"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another, so mutualism and parasitism are relations rather than kinds."**
> **"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."**

Fig 11 :36: *"Where nothing maintains anything, intelligence is a label applied afterwards to a lineage that met its ground. Nothing was intelligent; selection ran."*

This is the strongest figure line against a naive reading of "particular influential species". Whether a species is influential depends on which function, which perturbation and which habitat is the residual. The public reference on keystone species, fetched 2026-11-23, records exactly this: Menge showed the ochre seastar *"was a powerful keystone species in places exposed to strong wave action, but was far less important in sheltered places"*, and Paine noted that without the relevant mussel prey the predator was *"just another sea star"*. That is a different field and a different evidence pool from the framework.

**Verdict:** "particular influential species" is licensed only as a role relative to a stated residual, never as a fixed name. **Triangulated** (Fig 11 plus the ecological case history). Refuter: a fixed, context-independent species list predicts stability across residuals and habitats.

### F4 — Functional group, not irreplaceable individual (governs carrier I, single-source with corroboration)

Fig 12 :123:

> **"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."**

The figure supports the *presence* channel, but at the level of a kind: the group fixes the outcome and the qualifier only says which member. So the defensible form of carrier I is "presence of a functional kind", not "this exact species is irreplaceable". The ecological selection effect is defined in Wagg et al. 2022 as *"more diverse communities have a greater probability to contain species that dominate and have a strong effect on ecosystem functions"*, which is a presence/probability statement, also about a role more than a name.

**Verdict:** carrier I survives in a narrowed form. **Single-source** for the kind-versus-member distinction (Fig 12), corroborated by an ecological definition that does not itself decide the identity question. Refuter: a replacement experiment in which a functionally similar species substitutes perfectly for the putative influential species in every habitat (kills the member-level claim, leaves the kind-level claim standing).

### F5 — The slot matters; aggregation hides it (single-source, framework-internal)

Fig 1 :19 and :22:

> **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."**
> **"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."**

Fig 1 :13: *"a global R near zero with one live slot residual is a legal state."*

A diversity index averages over species slots. If one slot carries the stabilizing effect, the average is precisely the operation that hides it. The influential-species hypothesis is therefore not a rival to per-slot analysis; it is the per-slot residual that aggregation suppresses. The ecological literature's dominance effects (selection effects, dominant-species constraints on temporal variability) are the world-facing version of this slot.

**Verdict:** keep per-species resolution before computing any community-level index. **Single-source** from the figures for the general rule; the existence of slot effects is corroborated by the ecological literature but its magnitude is a ground question. Refuter: averaging across slots preserves the signal that per-slot analysis finds, in a census of habitats.

### F6 — Stability is agent-and-habitat, and contact cascades (context, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39-40:

> **"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."**
> **"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."**

Fig 11 :43-44: *"The habitat is enumerated, never composed."* / *"List everything in contact with the residual, then what is in contact with those, and outward until the cascade stops mattering."*

Fig 11 :46-47 names the kinds of things to enumerate: *"conditions: light, water, temperature, season, terrain"* and *"relations: pollination, predation, competition, dependency"*. Fig 11 :52-53 warns that *"contact cascades. Scoping the habitat to direct contact is how DDT reached the eagles: first-order enumeration, second-order effect, and the residual that mattered was three steps out."* A keystone predator's stabilizing effect is exactly such a cascade. The world literature agrees on context dependence: Wagg et al. 2022 find mechanisms change with community age; Hsieh et al. 2026 find response diversity stabilizes *"within but not across trophic levels"* and that the effect *"varies over time, being context-dependent on time-varying environmental conditions."*

**Verdict:** neither mechanism is a property of the species set alone. **Triangulated** (Fig 11 plus two independent ecological systems). Refuter: a species-only model predicts stability across habitats and trophic levels with no habitat term.

### F7 — The ground decides the weighting; agreement does not (anchor rule, single-source with world corroboration)

Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 2 :40: *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*

The world literature itself concedes the open ground. Zhao et al. 2022: *"whether other ecosystem types (e.g., forest, meadow, and plankton systems) show results similar to ours remains an important unanswered question."* Meng et al. 2025: species-specific dynamics, not interspecific interactions, explain much of the effect in grasslands. Hsieh et al. 2026: biotic interactions are the primary mechanism in Lake Geneva. These two conflict on the sub-mechanism, and the conflict is recorded rather than smoothed (see section 6).

**Verdict:** the numerical weighting is not decidable from the figures or from a consensus of reviews. It is a `gap` from the figures and a task for a ground reading. **Single-source** for the anchor rule; the world's own open questions corroborate the need for one. Refuter: a figure line that directly fixes the fraction f, or a measurement of f with a stated protocol and anchor.

### F8 — Step 7: searched, inward, outward (procedure)

`THE_FORMULA.md` step 7 asks three distinct questions when no term in closure(Γ) closes R.

- **SEARCHED** (asked first, cheapest): is the answer already in the ecological closure and unfound? No. This literature has been searched hard for decades and its own syntheses say the result is context-dependent. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 9 :44: *"Only a sealed room can be searched to the end, and no frame is a sealed room."*
- **INWARD** (extend the instrument from one already returning something): separate the components that a richness index conflates. Zhao et al. 2022 already built such an instrument by decomposing asynchrony into a compensatory effect and a statistical-averaging effect. Meng et al. 2025 partition interaction-independent from interaction-dependent effects. Hsieh et al. 2026 build a time-varying response-diversity index. These are sharpenings in the field's own terms.
- **OUTWARD** (import the frame whose closure predicts the shape): portfolio theory from economics. Markowitz's portfolio selection, per the Nobel Prize page fetched 2026-11-23, is *"a theory for optimal investment of wealth in assets which differ in regard to their expected return and risk."* It predicts variance reduction from differences among assets, not from a single asset. Fig 6 :23: *"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."* The import needs a source gate and a shadow test (Fig 8 :50-52) and is debited against independence.

**Verdict:** the shape is reached by SEARCHED plus INWARD; OUTWARD supplies a low-overlap corroborating frame, not the verdict. Never read INWARD against OUTWARD (Fig 6 :31).

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the WATSON run)

The shape claim is refuted, narrowed or withdrawn if any of the following holds:

1. A single mechanism (differences OR influential species) accounts for the whole diversity-stability effect across a census of habitats and stability properties, with the other condition at zero. This breaks F1's union reading.
2. Stability is shown to be a property of one species in isolation, invariant to habitat and to the presence of other species. This breaks Fig 11 :39.
3. A fixed, context-independent list of influential species predicts stability across different residuals. This breaks Fig 11 :51.
4. A species list alone predicts temporal stability as well as a list plus its temporal covariance or response structure, across the same habitats. This breaks Fig 12 :5 and :94.
5. Aggregating across species slots preserves the signal that per-slot analysis finds. This breaks Fig 1 :22.
6. The empirical fraction f is fixed by a figure line without a ground reading. This breaks the mode rule.

If any fires, record it visibly here and in `11_audit.md`. Do not silently soften the claim. A falsifier written before the run is a design instrument.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Ask a narrower, groundable question: **for stability property S in habitat H under perturbation P, what fraction f of the diversity effect is carried by between-species differences, and what fraction by the presence of particular species, and does the split change when the influential species is removed while richness is held constant?**
2. Run the discriminating protocols: hold richness constant and swap species identities; remove the dominant or keystone species while holding richness and compare; replace an influential species with a functionally similar one. The first tests identity, the second tests presence, the third separates kind from member (F4).
3. Keep per-species resolution until after the mechanism split is measured (F5).
4. Enumerate the habitat before naming influential actors (Fig 11 :43-44), and report trophic level, time window and perturbation, because Hsieh et al. 2026 show the effect is within-level and time-varying.
5. Treat a review consensus as a convention, not a verdict (Fig 2 :40). Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (Fig 2 :42).
6. Send the generator, not the conclusion: the residual, the shape and the refuter. Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*

## 6. Canonical usage check and conflict handling

Two governing rules were read after the first sections.

- `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`: *"They outrank everything else in the repo: the code, the rulings and the docs answer to them... the quotes are the authority, not this summary."* Also: *"a reading that makes one contradict another has misidentified its domain, not found a flaw."* And the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then stops at the limit of the instruments and records the lineage. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."*
- `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`: mode rule, step 7, and the Habitat rule *"Enumerate, never compose."*

**A genuine world conflict, handled by re-census.** Meng et al. 2025 report that *"species-specific dynamics observable in monocultures ... rather than interspecific interactions observable only in mixtures, explain much of the stabilizing effects of biodiversity"*; Hsieh et al. 2026 report that *"biotic interactions are the primary mechanism mediating this stabilizing effect"* in Lake Geneva. A naive read would call this a contradiction and force a winner. Per the canonical rule it sends us back to the census, where the two domains differ: European and North American grassland biodiversity experiments with monoculture-derived dynamics versus a 40-year monthly plankton time series in a single lake with time-varying Jacobian interactions. Different currency, different anchor, different scale. Both are instances of the union; the conflict is about the sub-mechanism inside carrier D, not about D versus I. It reinforces the context-dependence verdict and never flips it. Recorded as a strain, not hidden.

### Where the derivation stops

The shape survives: the `or` is a union of overlapping conditions, the arrangement carries information a species list does not, influential actors are roles relative to a residual rather than a fixed list, and the habitat and trophic level must be enumerated before any mechanism is named. What does not survive is any claim that the figures tell us which mechanism dominates in nature. That is `specified` at best until a ground reading pays for it.
