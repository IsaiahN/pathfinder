# The Tether's answer: differences or influential species?

**Mode: `specified` on the shape, `general` on the union limit, NOT `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a numerical result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The diversity-stability literature reports a robust effect (more diverse communities have less variable aggregate properties) and two candidate carriers. Carrier D locates the cause in the differences among species, their asynchrony, complementarity and response diversity. Carrier I locates it in the presence of particular influential species, through selection, dominance, foundation or keystone effects. The residual is not "which story is prettier" but: what would we have to observe to distinguish them, and does the question as posed permit a single answer at all?

## 2. The shape

1. **The `or` is a union of overlapping conditions, not a partition.** Fig 9 :54-55: *"when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again"* and *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A community can contain asynchronous species and a dominant species at once. Run one loop per condition, then examine the intersection, which is a specific compound (Fig 9 :58). Wagg et al. 2022 corroborate from a different evidence pool: the two mechanisms *"are not mutually exclusive."*
2. **The arrangement carries what the list does not.** Fig 12 :5: *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :94: *"The arrangement is the substance."* Two plots with the same species list and different covariance are isomers (Fig 12 :117), not variants of one thing. This is what licenses carrier D as a relation among species, not as a count.
3. **Influential species are roles relative to a residual, never a fixed list.** Fig 11 :49-51: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries"*, *"The same actor is a benefactor to one residual and a malfactor to another"*, *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."* The keystone case history confirms it: the same seastar is powerful in wave-exposed sites and *"just another sea star"* without its mussel prey.
4. **The influential channel is a functional kind, with the member left open.** Fig 12 :123: *"functional group. A substructure whose presence fixes the outcome. The group says what kind; the qualifier says which member."* The defensible claim is "presence of a role", not "this name is irreplaceable everywhere".
5. **The per-slot signal must be kept before averaging.** Fig 1 :19 and :22: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated"* and *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* A diversity index is the average that hides the dominant slot.
6. **Stability is agent-and-habitat.** Fig 11 :39-40: *"capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone"* and *"An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Enumerate conditions and relations (Fig 11 :46-47), and remember that contact cascades (Fig 11 :52). The world results agree on context: Hsieh et al. 2026 find the effect within but not across trophic levels and time-varying; Wagg et al. 2022 find the mechanisms change with community age.
7. **The numerical weighting is not in the figures.** It is a `gap` from the figures and a task for a ground. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "the answer is niche complementarity" and not "the answer is keystone species". Both are names where the figures return a shape.
- Not that statistical averaging, compensatory dynamics, species-specific dynamics or biotic interactions is the winner inside carrier D. Meng et al. 2025 and Hsieh et al. 2026 conflict on this, and the conflict is recorded, not resolved (`01` section 6).
- Not that richness is irrelevant. It is a projection that cannot comment on the source (Hard Problem is About Source), not a useless measurement.
- Not a fixed list of influential species. Roles yes, names no.
- Not `grounded`. No experiment or monitoring analysis was run in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A discriminating protocol, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; it was *"the arrival of a better instrument"* that resolved the analogue. Here the discriminators are:

1. **Identity-swap test:** hold richness and habitat constant and swap species identities. If stability changes, identity is doing work (carrier I). If not, the arrangement among equivalent species is the carrier (carrier D).
2. **Targeted removal:** remove the dominant or keystone actor while holding richness. If stability crashes, presence matters. If not, the name was decorative.
3. **Substitution test:** replace the influential actor with a functionally similar species. If stability is unchanged, the functional kind rather than the member is what matters (Fig 12 :123).
4. **Component decomposition:** apply Zhao et al. 2022's split of asynchrony, or Meng et al. 2025's split of interaction-dependent and independent effects, and report the fraction f for a stated stability property S, habitat H and perturbation P.
5. **Habitat and trophic enumeration:** report conditions, relations, time window and trophic level, per Fig 11 :43-47 and Hsieh et al. 2026.
6. **Standing refuters from the audit:** one mechanism with the other at zero; a species-invariant stability; a fixed cross-residual influential list; a species list as informative as a list plus covariance; aggregation preserving the per-slot signal.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The world literature itself leaves the ground open: Zhao et al. 2022 say other ecosystem types remain an unanswered question, and Meng 2025 versus Hsieh 2026 disagree on the sub-mechanism. We stop at the limit of what the available instruments distinguish and record the lineage so the stop is checkable. *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* Prestige or agreement is not a substitute instrument: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."*

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not pick a side in the diversity-stability debate, because the either/or is the defect. Its shape says that the two conditions overlap and must be unbundled before either is searched; that the arrangement among species carries information a species list does not, so same-list communities with different covariance are different systems; that influential species are roles relative to a stated residual rather than a fixed list, and the figure licenses a functional kind more readily than an irreplaceable name; that per-slot signals must be kept before averaging; and that stability belongs to the community and its habitat, not to a species alone. What it does not return is the fraction of the effect carried by each route. That number is owed to identity swaps, targeted removals and component decomposition on a named habitat and stability property. Until then the verdict is `specified`, ungrounded, candidate, with its refuters written in advance.
