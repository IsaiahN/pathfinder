## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what evidence should carry verdict weight when two or more economic theories
disagree. Option A: rank the five candidates (historical cases, natural experiments, randomized
trials, mathematical consistency, predictive accuracy) on one universal ladder. Option B: return the
shape decisive evidence must have, refuse the universal ladder as a gap, and resolve the ranking per
named disagreement. What each leads to: A hands a filter a verdict, treats frame-internal coherence
or pooled agreement as evidence, and spends the independence of the people checking it; B sends a
checkable shape, written refuters, and the ground reading that would close each case.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor, partition
or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Competing economic theories disagreeing about a claim, with five candidates
  for verdict weight. Arity: at least two frames plus a meta-question about ranking. Symmetry: the
  five kinds are not interchangeable; which bears is a function of the question, not an intrinsic
  rank. Scale: theory choice is second order relative to inference inside one theory. Fig 9 permits
  that this is several well-formed questions (a split) or one compound with two conditions (a union);
  the census resolves it by asking whether one ground settles all parts.
- **Domain 1, ground and anchor.** Actors: the world, measurement, experiment, market, outcome.
  Currency: residual against a ground that does not update. Anchor: the ground itself. Question type:
  partition, which candidate is ground for this question and which is frame-internal.
- **Domain 2, independence and frames.** Actors: the competing theories and their evidence pools.
  Currency: independence, how little the frames draw on the same evidence. Anchor: independence, never
  agreement. Question type: union collapse when frames share one pool.
- **Domain 3, prediction and settlement.** Actors: models, predictions, observations, held-out data.
  Currency: out-of-sample residual; in-sample fit is frame-internal. Anchor: the ground via out-of-sample
  contact. Question type: partition, ground contact versus internal coherence.
- **Domain 4, instrument design.** Actors: interventions, assignment mechanisms, records. Currency:
  causal identification and comparison conditions. Anchor: the world's response to the instrument.
  Question type: union, different instruments rather than one scale.
- **Order by dependency.** Anchor legitimacy (Fig 2 :36) before independence (Fig 2 :31); independence
  before ground contact (Fig 1 :23); instrument choice last (Fig 6 :32). Describe the residual before
  choosing a frame (Fig 9 :27).

Strain test applied: Fig 9 :4 (split a non-shrinking disagreement) and the union reading of a
two-condition compound govern different objects. Re-census resolves the apparent strain; no figure
contradicts another (see `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §6).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the universal ladder does not. Decisive evidence is an independent, unmoving,
question-constitutive anchor that registers ground contact. Mathematical consistency and pooled
agreement are disqualified as verdict; predictive accuracy counts only out of sample and from an
independent source; experiments and historical cases count when they measure the axis actually in
dispute. The universal ranking is a `gap`, plainly stated, because anchor legitimacy is
question-constitutive (Fig 2 :36). The per-disagreement ranking is determinate once the question is
described. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result; no actual economic disagreement was settled
here.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable, but real. Accepting a shared evidence pool or an internal
  consistency proof as a verdict produces coherent drift, which Fig 2 :34 describes as the case where
  "everything drifts together, and the drift is coherent, which is why nobody notices." The avoidable
  cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a methodology preference instead
  of a generator. Fig 2 :42 prices that: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. An evidence methodology is a convention and a filter, not a theory's content.
  It can be revised, narrowed and re-provenanced without withdrawing any economic theory. The
  corrected packet at `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already removes the universal-ladder claim
  and keeps the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A case in which one evidence kind is shown to be
  an anchor independent of the question; a demonstration that mathematical consistency changes
  contact with an independent ground; a demonstration that pooled agreement carries verdict weight;
  a measured disagreement in which out-of-sample prediction loses to internal consistency; or a
  figure re-census that licenses a fixed ordering of all five kinds for every question. Until one of
  these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
