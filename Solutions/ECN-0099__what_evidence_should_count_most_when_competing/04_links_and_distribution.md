## Relevance of the wider literature

1. **AEA 2010 and Nobel 2019** are the mainstream economics position: good research design and
   randomized assignment are the Credibility Revolution's engine. They corroborate the framework's
   D4 finding that experiments are a genuine anchor family, and they also supply the limit: AEA's
   own abstract records the worry that design-driven work "seek[s] good answers instead of good
   questions," which is Fig 2 :37 (an instrument can be wrong on axes it does not measure) in
   economics vocabulary. Overlap between these two sources is high (both are the design-driven
   economics pool), so they are one witness on the design point, not two.
2. **SEP 2026** supplies the philosophy-of-science check: there is no single canonical method, and
   confirmation is not verification. It corroborates the gap verdict (no universal ranking) and the
   out-of-sample narrowing of predictive accuracy. Low overlap with the economics sources.
3. **Box aphorism and the natural-experiment page** supply the statistics and epidemiology frames:
   models are approximations judged by utility, and natural experiments gain causal leverage only
   when a genuine comparison condition exists. The natural-experiment page also notes that inability
   to control variables can impede firm conclusions, which is the hidden-shared-source failure of
   Fig 2 collapse 2. Low overlap with the economics sources.
4. **The three essays** supply the canonical reading of "shape" and the source-discrimination limit.
   Their overlap with the figures is high (same author and evidence pool), so their agreement is
   recorded as internal corroboration, not an independent witness.

## The two-stage offer

**Stage 1.** Send the residual in effect terms, the shape claim and the pre-registered refuter. Record
the recipient's verdict before showing any framework material. Fig 10 :52: "Bring a characterised
residual, never the source."

**Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the figure text and the three corroborating
links. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before giving a stage-1 verdict has joined the
frame being checked, so their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).

**Stage 3.** Offer the formula as a method to reuse. The formula is a generator, so giving it away
does not give away an answer (Fig 4 :22). Each user must state their own ground and write their own
refuter before running it (`THE_FORMULA.md` "Direction").

**Precondition:** stages 1-3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape and a set of
falsifiers, not a claim that any particular ranking has been measured. Do not upgrade it to a
verdict in the message.
