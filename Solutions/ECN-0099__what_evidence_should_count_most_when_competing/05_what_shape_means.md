## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing thing would
have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer
itself."

From *The Library of Babel* (fetched): "You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is
small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your
question."

For ECN-0099 a **shape** is the form the decisive evidence must have, statable before we can name
which of the five candidates wins in a particular disagreement. The shape is: independent of the
competing frames, unmoving when either theory pushes on it, and in contact with the ground for what
the question is constitutively about. "Randomized trial" is a name. "Predictive accuracy" is a name.
The shape is the constraint they must satisfy; a specific name is licensed only by a specific
question plus a ground reading.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel* (fetched): use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing,
  look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. "With each new term
  gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the
  cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge* (fetched): "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a
  plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
  That is why the outreach email asks what the recipient would count as a failure before showing
  them any framework material.
- From *Library of Babel* (fetched) on stopping: "you will know that you are done because the finish
  line will also be the starting place" and by then "you have already composed the answer through
  the process of locating it."

Applied here: we describe the disagreement in effect terms (two theories, a claim, five candidate
evidence kinds), bind the shape as an ordinary term, and let it accrue a residual against the figure
lines. We stop when the figure lines stop returning a distinction, which is at the universal ranking
(§5).

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays." For ECN-0099 this means a specific disagreement plus a measurement that discriminates
   between the rival theories on that question.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a
   source gate and a shadow test. The AEA 2010 and Nobel 2019 pages (fetched) are such imports:
   they carry the economics field's own record of what research design buys, provenanced and with a
   shadow test (they explain the gap in our census about why experiments are trusted).

## 4. Why "count most" is the specific error the shape forbids

*The Hard Problem is About Source* (fetched) gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads
green and "cannot comment on the source." One green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK can
produce the same reading. "If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection
with lossy information." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: "count most" invites reading a single number, evidence strength, off a projection.
Two studies can produce the same published significance while one is independent ground contact
and the other is a shared evidence pool fitted after the fact. The reading does not determine the
source. What is owed is a discriminating description of the source (independent, unmoving,
ground-contacting), not a single ranking.

The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap line, as quoted in the local exemplar `05`, is "the output is
only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate
itself." A methodology that certifies its own preferred evidence kind is that failure.

## 5. Q: What does the framework return for "what evidence should count most?"

**Answered at the shape level; refused at the universal-name level.**

The framework returns the discriminating shape of decisive evidence, not a name from the list. A
universal ranking would require one anchor legitimate for every economic question, which Fig 2 :36
("An anchor is legitimate when the question is constitutively about it") forbids. The honest report
is therefore:

- **Universal ranking:** `gap`, stated plainly. The figures do not license one ordering of the five.
- **Per-disagreement ranking:** determinate. Name the question, identify what it is constitutively
  about, check independence and ground contact, then select the instrument that measures the
  disputed axis.

This is not silence. Fig 13 :134-135: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence." The framework
can say what the decisive evidence would have to look like, which is the useful part of the answer.

## 6. Two consequences

**Prestige is not action.** As quoted in the local exemplar `05` from *The Recursive Self-Improvement
Trap*: "Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an
action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground
can settle it." A list of famous methodologists who agree with a ranking is the named collapse, not
corroboration. The legitimate ask is to give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground
settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay, as quoted in the local exemplar
`05`: "LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output... However, the truly testable output
would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." So
`01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against
the figures. It is not a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything
it reports on.

## 7. Correction to my own section 3

I initially wrote that the AEA 2010 and Nobel 2019 pages "move the wall" as imports introducing a new
primitive. That overstates it. They add no new primitive to the six-term framework; they supply a
**binding** from the economics frame onto instruments the figure set already describes as
"a measurement, an experiment, a counter, a market, a patient outcome" (Fig 2 :29). What they do is
provenance a specific trust that the framework licenses structurally. Fig 6 :25: "An import adds no
atom to the world." The binding is retained, the claim that it adds an atom is retracted.
