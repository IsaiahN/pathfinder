**Overlap declaration.** Buldyrev and the "Cascading failure" article both rely on the 2003 Italy blackout, so those two are one witness on that particular case, not two. Vespignani comments on Buldyrev, so its agreement is expected and carries little independent weight. The two Wikipedia articles share the 2003 Blackout case. The IPCC assessment is a genuinely different evidence pool (assessed climate literature), so its agreement on cross-region cascades is the strongest of the five. None of the five contradicts the figure verdict; the conflict rule was not triggered.

## 3. Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded rules:

- **Fig 10 :52 "Bring a characterised residual, never the source."** Lead with the shape claim and its refuter. The framework itself comes later and only if asked.
- **Fig 4 :22 "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."** A recording of the whole architecture sent before anyone has described a residual is a description of one occasion, not a generator.
- **Fig 9 :27-28 "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."** Ask the recipient what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the literature.
- **Fig 2 :40 "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** A list selected for likely agreement manufactures the correlation that voids the verdict.
- **Fig 7 :35 "A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."** No bulk send, no receptiveness rank.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the shape claim and the four refuters; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record the answer; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority; (4) offer the formula as a reusable method, with each user writing their own refuter and mode.

The five source links and the local figure extract are public. **Nothing is sent by this agent; hosting and sending are the owner's action.** Prefer an opt-in download or submission page over an attachment pushed to a list, so a recipient can decline contact.
