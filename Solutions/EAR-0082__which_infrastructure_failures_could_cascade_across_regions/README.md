# Solution EAR-0082: figure-grounded package

**Question:** Which infrastructure failures could cascade across regions or sectors?

**Source:** `earth systems.txt` line 110 (question 82).

**Section:** 9. Human infrastructure, society, and adaptation.

**Mode:** `general` on the structural shape of a cascade answer, `specified` on the per-domain findings tied to figure lines. Nothing here is `grounded`: no network data, outage measurement, or experiment was read in this derivation.

**Status:** done.

**Registry status:** pending (registry is maintained outside this folder by the parent session).

**Boundary and honest standing.** This folder contains a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figure text. It is not a ground reading. By the framework's own validation rule (the Recursive Self-Improvement Trap essay: LLM output is "simulacra" and "the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself"), it cannot settle the question it reports on. It returns the shape any answer to "which failures" must have and the method to obtain the name; it does not return the name. The figures are primary; the five world sources below are secondary corroboration only, and each was opened and is recorded with author, venue, year, accessed date and a quote in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

## Restating the question as an effect

- **Option A:** hand back a fixed list of "critical" infrastructures that could cascade. This leads to a static name whose valence is actually relative to the residual (Fig 11 :49), so it silently changes meaning when the hazard changes, and it scopes the habitat to first-order contact (Fig 11 :52-53).
- **Option B:** hand back the shape of a cascade answer (enumerate the habitat by contact outward until the residual stops registering, unbundle unions, keep cuts reversible) plus the refusal to name the list until the ground settles it. This leads to a checkable generator and a recorded stop.
- **What an answer would look like, bound as an ordinary term (formula step "Direction"):** an ordered enumeration of contact links from a source node, extended to second and third order, with each node's valence relative to the residual, over a union of sector and region conditions that has been unbundled before search. Binding the shape makes it refusable by the same test as any other claim.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the shape claim.
- No bulk-send plan.

Reasons, in the figures' own words: a room of agreeing readers is variation, not a verdict (Fig 7 :35); agreement across frames that share an evidence pool is worthless for a verdict (Fig 2 :40); and a verifier who has already been handed the interiors cannot verify (Fig 10 :42). The first contact carries the residual and its refuter, never the framework.
