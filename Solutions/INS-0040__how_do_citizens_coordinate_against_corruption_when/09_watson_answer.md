## QUESTION

How do citizens coordinate against corruption when individuals face high personal costs for reporting it?

Stated as an effect: why does the per-citizen residual `R = |Γ(b,a) − o′|` stay positive when reporting is privately costly and the benefit is diffuse, and what arrangement would shrink it? Option A: add exhortation, rewards, and report targets on the assumption that the missing thing is more willingness or more reports. Option B: treat the gap as a union of conditions, change the habitat and the channel so contact survives, buy independence, install a ground-valid progress measure, and keep the verifier outside the graded slice. A sends frame-internal metrics and buys agreement from one evidence pool. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an honest `gap` where the mechanism name would be.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry, scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity three: private cost, channel contact, frame correlation. Symmetry: reporting is directed (report → institution → ground), carried by a sequence and a threshold comparison, not a symmetric join. Scale: individual, group, institution, societal norm, with a lossy transform between (Fig 4 :27). Fig 9 :57 makes it a union compound: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either.
- **D1 Individual reporting decision.** Actors: citizen, corrupt official, employer, family. Currency: private cost versus private benefit. Anchor: actual retaliation or protection outcome. Admitted, insufficient alone.
- **D2 Reporting channel / ground maintainer.** Actors: ombuds, audit, court, journalist, hotline, ALAC. Currency: contact with the ground, whether a report registers and is acted on. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24). Admitted.
- **D3 Coordination network / independent frames.** Actors: many citizens, peer groups, media, oversight bodies. Currency: independence of evidence (how little drawn from one pool). Anchor: the ground via triangulation (Fig 2 :31-32). Admitted.
- **D4 Habitat / norms and institutions.** Actors: laws, protection regimes, repeated interaction, social norms. Currency: capability of agent-and-habitat. Anchor: whether contact changes (Fig 11 :39-40). Admitted.
- **X1 Collective courage as a free-floating citizen property.** No nameable anchor that changes with it. Not a domain yet, absorbed into D4. **X2 More reporting as the goal.** Frame-internal (Fig 1 :24-27). Absorbed into D2 and D3 as a filter, never the ground.

**Union, not partition.** D1, D2, D4 form the contact compound; D2, D3 form the independence compound. **Order by dependency:** perception → vocabulary → objective (the middle link with no instrument) → planning → learn and carry (Fig 3).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: coordination under high personal cost is not a single lever and not a single institution. It holds when a union of conditions is split (private cost, channel contact, frame correlation); when the habitat changes so contact changes, rather than the citizen being improved in isolation; when the channel keeps the ground reachable and its silence is read as lost touch; when independence is bought in proportion to the high cost of being wrong instead of a larger agreeing crowd; when a ground-valid subtraction for progress replaces the frame-internal report count; and when the verifier sits outside the graded slice, selected by an unauthored mechanism. Captured and ground-reaching regimes are isomers over the same atom set: same institutions, different verification bond, different substance. Which mechanism wins in a given setting is a declared `gap`, not a hidden name. Verdicts are kept separate per domain and never averaged. Mode `specified`. The world sources corroborate the mechanism from low-overlap fields; the bystander counterexample narrowed the individual-level claim without flipping the union verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** misdirected reform. A programme failing at the channel is sold courage training; a programme failing at independence is sold a bigger hotline; a single lever is applied to a union of three conditions, so the intersections stand and the budget is spent on the easy region. In outreach terms, an outside frame's twenty minutes are spent refuting a shape that was never the right shape. The cost is real but bounded, and Fig 2 :42 prices the remedy: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added, and the reach falls because "something was added rather than because anything was deleted" (Fig 6 :42-43). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded with provenance so a later reader can locate the error rather than only feel it (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent packet can be superseded by a corrected one.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. Any of the five registered falsifiers: (1) a citizen-only intervention lowers corruption on a ground-valid measure; (2) a movable agency metric is treated as the anchor; (3) a single-source agreement chain moves the ground; (4) one lever clears all three conditions with no categorically different residue; (5) hand-picked correlated checkers produce ground-valid corrections with no unauthored selection. A source-discriminating progress instrument would fill the declared `gap` and name the mechanism. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
