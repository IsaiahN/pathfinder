# The Tether's answer: coordinating against corruption under personal cost

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the specific mechanism. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Each citizen faces a private cost to report and a diffuse shared benefit if corruption declines. `R` is per citizen, per episode, never aggregated (Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."*). The anchor is whether corruption actually declines and whether a report is acted on, not the report count. The question asks what arrangement lets `R` shrink when the private term is strongly negative.

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain, verdicts kept separate)

1. **It is a union, so split it before searching (method).** Private cost, channel contact, and frame correlation are three overlapping conditions. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* A single lever clears one region and leaves the intersections standing. *Verdict: `general`, triangulated (Fig 9 plus F1's re-census).*
2. **Change the habitat, not the citizen in isolation (D1).** Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Courage training and one-off rewards do not change contact. Protection, confidentiality, and follow-through do. *Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 11 and Fig 9, low overlap).*
3. **Keep the anchor reachable through the channel (D2).** Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*; Fig 10 :28-29: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"*, *"keep the anchor reachable"*. A silent channel is lost contact, not evidence of no corruption. The agency's own clearance statistic is not the anchor, because it moves when the agency pushes on it (Fig 2 :30). *Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 2).*
4. **Buy independence, not agreement (D3).** Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Many reporters drawing on one grievance and one source are a mirror chain (Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*). Distribute the search and keep the searchers independent (Fig 6 :50). *Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 2, Fig 7, Fig 8, distinct roles).*
5. **Install the missing middle link (D4).** Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* The unmeasured quantity is a ground-valid subtraction for progress. Report counts are frame-internal (Fig 1 :24-27). *Verdict: `specified`, single-source for the instrument gap (Fig 3 and Fig 12 triangulate the need, but no instrument is supplied).*
6. **Put the checker outside the slice, selected by an unauthored mechanism (D2 and D4).** Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* Fig 10 :76: *"a lottery"*, :78: *"a set assembled before the question existed."* The reporter's interest in the outcome is legitimate; an interest in the reading is disqualifying (Fig 10 :59). *Verdict: `specified`, triangulated (Fig 10 and Fig 2).*
7. **No final institutional form (D4).** Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*; :30: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"*. A reform that stays fixed while the residual moves is the failure, not the steady state. *Verdict: `general`, canonically confirmed.*
8. **Which mechanism wins is a `gap`.** Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* No figure line selects an agency design, sanction, or country package. *Verdict: `gap`, declared after re-census.*

Verdicts are kept separate, never averaged. The union is not one score.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not a policy recommendation. No agency, law, sanction, or national reform is named.
- Not a claim that reporting more is the goal. Report counts are a filter for the budget, never a witness for the verdict (Fig 9 :60: *"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."*).
- Not a claim that agreement among citizens is worthless for everything. Agreement about a shared meaning is efficient for a convention (Fig 2 :41); it is disqualifying only for a verdict about the world.
- Not a claim that citizens are lazy, corrupt, or apathetic. The private-cost structure is the object, not a moral judgement about the people in it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement of this derivation was taken.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. From *The Hard Problem is About Source*: an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it, and *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."* Here the corresponding tests are:

1. **Channel test:** does a report produce a ground-valid outcome the institution cannot move, or only a clearance statistic it can? (F3)
2. **Independence test:** do corrections survive when the reporting frames are made genuinely separate in evidence? (F4, F5)
3. **Middle-link test:** is there a progress measure that is a true subtraction against an external ground and not a count the institution also produces? (F6)
4. **Checker test:** do ground-valid corrections still appear when the checker is selected by an unauthored mechanism and sits outside the graded slice? (F7)
5. **Standing falsifiers** from `01` section 4: citizen-only improvement moves the ground; a movable "anchor" is treated as ground; a single-source agreement chain moves the ground; one lever clears the union; hand-picked correlated checkers succeed.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** The shape is stated; the instrument that would fill the `gap` is characterized in effect terms but not held. Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence."* The essays add: *"Indiscernibility is not identity."* The answer stops where the available instruments cannot distinguish one institutional bond from another on the ground, and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Prestige, endorsement, or a larger room does not move it (Fig 2 :35: *"A mirror cannot correct you."*).

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not name a winning anti-corruption programme and does not claim this derivation is settled. It says that the problem is a union of private cost, a channel that can lose touch with reality, and correlated frames, so one lever cannot clear it; that you cannot fix the brave citizen in isolation, because capability lives in the agent and its habitat; that a silent reporting channel means lost contact, not no corruption, so the job is to keep the ground reachable; that independence, not agreement, is what makes many reporters more than one reporter, and since the cost of being wrong is high you should buy a lot of it; that the missing middle link is a ground-valid measure of progress, not a count of reports; and that whoever verifies the result must sit outside the slice being graded, chosen by an unauthored mechanism. What it offers is a shape, refuters written in advance, and the tests that would close it. The specific mechanism stays an honest gap until a source-discriminating instrument settles it.
