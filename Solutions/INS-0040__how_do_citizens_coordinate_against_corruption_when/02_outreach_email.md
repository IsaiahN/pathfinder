# Outreach draft: check one output before you decide about the framework

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the shape claim and its refuter in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` survive the recipient's own check. The output must stand on its own or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

---

**Subject:** One checkable claim about coordinating against corruption under personal cost

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know from anti-corruption work. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual (`specified`, not `grounded`).** A citizen who reports corruption bears a private cost (retaliation, career damage, lost relationships) while the benefit is diffuse and shared with people who did not report. The gap is indexed per citizen and per episode, not aggregated into a national trust average. The anchor is whether corruption actually declines and whether a report is actually acted on, not how many reports were filed. A report count moves when the institution pushes on it, so by the framework's own rule it cannot be the ground.
>
> **The shape claim, before any theory.** Coordination fails when three conditions bind at once: the private cost, a channel that can lose contact with the ground, and frames that are correlated rather than independent. It is a union, not one hard problem, so a single lever clears the easy region and leaves the intersections standing. The figure-grounded reading is that capability lives in the agent-and-habitat, not in the brave individual; that the channel decays while the ground does not, so the job is to keep the anchor reachable; that independence, not agreement, is what makes many reporters more than one reporter; that the plan needs a goal and a subtraction, not just a name for the problem; and that whoever verifies the outcome must sit outside the slice being graded, selected by an unauthored mechanism.
>
> **What I am not claiming.** I am not naming a winning agency design, a sanction, or a country's reform package. The framework returns a shape, not a name. Which specific mechanism works where is a declared gap, and I am recording it as a gap rather than filling it. I am also not claiming this is grounded: no measurement of this derivation was taken.
>
> **What would refute it (written before this message):**
> 1. A citizen-only intervention, with no change to channel or habitat, lowers corruption on a ground-valid, independently verified measure.
> 2. A channel's own clearance statistic is treated as the ground and moves when the institution pushes on it, yet still counts as an anchor.
> 3. A chain of mutually agreeing reports drawing on one evidence pool moves the ground.
> 4. One lever clears all three conditions with no categorically different residue left over.
> 5. Hand-picked checkers who share the investigators' frame produce ground-valid corrections with no unauthored selection and no separation between reporter and verifier.
>
> If any one holds, the shape is wrong and I will retract it. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** here are the sources. The figures, not my summary, are the authority.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Canonical figures README: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/blob/general-agent/figures/README.md
> - Arrival of the Fittest: The Tether Architecture: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the figures and formula are public. Run them on an anti-corruption problem you know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
>
> - [your name]

## Why this version is ordered this way

| the move | figure-grounded reason |
|---|---|
| Residual and refuter first, theory second | Fig 10 :52 - *"Bring a characterised residual, never the source."* Fig 2 :45 - frames range against the ground separately. |
| Do not ask for agreement | Fig 2 :35 - *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* |
| Pre-registered refuter | Seat Map line 287 - *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."* |
| Mode stated on the claim | `THE_FORMULA.md` - *"Mode. general · specified · grounded. State which."* |
| Union stated before solutions | Fig 9 :55 - *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* |
| No programme name | Fig 9 :32 - *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* |
| Independence over agreement | Fig 2 :42 - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* |
| No request for trust or prestige | *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it."* |
| Offer the formula as a method to reuse | Fig 4 :22 - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* |
| Ask recipients to state their own ground and refuter | Formula "Direction": a frame cannot check the direction of its own question; *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap* - *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* |

**Two-stage order.** Stage 1 is the residual, the shape, and the refuter, with the recipient's verdict recorded before any theory is shown. Stage 2, opt-in only, is the three links. Stage 3 is the formula as a reusable method. Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*).
