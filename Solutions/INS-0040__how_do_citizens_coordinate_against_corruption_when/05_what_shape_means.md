## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and *"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INS-0040 the shape is the six-part constraint set in `01` F1 to F9 (union split, habitat change, reachable channel, independence bought, grammar at the middle link, checker outside the slice). The name of the winning agency or programme is not the shape and is withheld.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have *"closest"* to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause."* This is why the residual in `01` section 0 is written as a private-payoff gap and an outcome anchor, not as "low trust" or "weak institutions", terms that already smuggle in a frame.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* The outreach email therefore states what a failure would look like before anything else.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, with the paradox that you have *"composed the answer through the process of locating it."*

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."*
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered can give the same reading. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the **spectrometer**, not an argument, that resolved it: *"the answer is unreached until a better instrument exists."*

Applied here: a country's falling corruption score is a reading, not a source. Many institutional arrangements can produce the same reading. Naming one as *the* cause from the reading alone is the metamerism error. What is owed is a source-discriminating instrument, not a programme name. This is the concrete content of the declared `gap` in `01` F8.

## 4. Why the report count is the specific forbidden error

Fig 1 :24-27: *"coverage, and compression achieved"*, *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"*, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."* A reporting campaign that scores itself by reports filed is scoring itself with a quantity it also produces. The ground (corruption actually reduced, independently verified) is the only admissible settlement.

*Decoding Tacit Knowledge* adds the drift warning: *"communities and institutions that only check against themselves drift together and don't notice"*, and two frames that overlap almost completely are *"one frame wearing two names"*. A network of reporters who all read the same source is one frame wearing many names, exactly F4 and F5.

## 5. Answer to the question "what would license a specific mechanism?"

Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A specific mechanism is licensed only by a ground reading that discriminates among sources, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the name is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Correction to my own §3 of `01` (carried here for honesty).** I first wrote that "shape, not a name" forbids naming any specific atom. The canonical phrase governs a **missing** primitive, one not held in the library. The correct narrowing for this question: the missing things are (a) a source-discriminating progress instrument and (b) the mechanism name for a given residual. Both are withheld. The shape constraints themselves are ordinary terms and were bound in `01` section 0 as `COORD-SHAPE`, so they can accrue a residual and be refused. Both readings are recorded so neither is quietly lost.

## 6. Two consequences for this question

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly"*; *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of famous anti-corruption endorsers is a budget, not a verdict. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the shape a budget to be tried, and let the ground (an outcome, a court, a verified reduction) settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula"*; *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* LLM output is *"simulacra"*. So `01` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.
