## Wider literature, read and relevant

These are the world witnesses for the WATSON answer. None outranks a figure line.

- **Collective action problem stated.** Mancur Olson, *The Logic of Collective Action*, Harvard University Press, 1965, read via the public overview at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Logic_of_Collective_Action: *"Without selective incentives to motivate active participation, collective action is unlikely to occur even when large groups of people with common interests exist."* Predicts the private-cost residual. Corroborates F1 and F2 at the economics level. Same field as much institutional work, so treat as one witness.
- **Costly enforcement.** Ernst Fehr and Simon Gächter, *Altruistic punishment in humans*, Nature 415, 137 to 140, 2002, https://www.nature.com/articles/415137a: *"Altruistic punishment means that individuals punish, although the punishment is costly for them and yields no material gain. We show that cooperation flourishes if altruistic punishment is possible, and breaks down if it is ruled out."* Low overlap (human behaviour and evolution). Predicts that high personal cost does not by itself forbid collective enforcement; the habitat that makes enforcement possible governs. Corroborates F2 and F5.
- **Self-organisation.** Elinor Ostrom, Nobel Prize facts and prize motivation, 2009, https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/facts/: *"She showed that when natural resources are jointly used by their users, in time, rules are established for how these are to be cared for and used in a way that is both economically and ecologically sustainable."* Low overlap (commons governance). Predicts that affected users can build rules; corroborates the habitat claim in F2 and the unauthored-pool rule in F7. Different method from Olson, so a partly independent witness.
- **Diffusion of responsibility.** *Bystander effect*, public overview, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bystander_effect: *"individuals are less likely to offer help to a victim in the presence of other people."* The ombuds-practitioner section adds: *"the most important reasons cited for not acting were: the fear of loss of important relationships in and out of the workplace, and a fear of 'bad consequences'."* Low overlap (social psychology). Predicts the private-cost residual and F5's warning about correlated crowds. Note the page's own counterexample (Philpot et al. 2019) where intervention was the norm in over 90 percent of real conflicts; this is a partial conflict handled in `09_watson_answer.md` by returning to the census, not by flipping the verdict.
- **Safe reporting channel.** Transparency International, contact page, https://www.transparency.org/en/contact: *"Our 60 Advocacy and Legal Advice Centres (ALACs) provide free and confidential legal advice and allow people to safely report corruption, access public information and demand transparent, responsive, accountable government institutions."* Field: anti-corruption practice. Predicts that the channel can be designed to keep the anchor reachable; corroborates F3 at the practitioner level.
- **Research and helpdesk venue.** U4 Anti-Corruption Resource Centre, https://www.u4.no/: *"Inform your work with U4's quality research and practical guidance"*; *"Send in your corruption-related questions about development programmes, sectors, or countries. A free research service."* Field: anti-corruption practice. Predicts an opt-in institutional route for the shape claim; venue evidence only, no verdict bearing.
- **Peer-reviewed venue.** *Crime, Law and Social Change*, Springer, https://link.springer.com/journal/10611: *"a peer-reviewed journal that focuses on research addressing crime at all levels globally"* and *"Explores financial and environmental crime, political corruption, and the expropriation of resources from low- and middle-income nations."* Submission via https://www.editorialmanager.com/cris. ## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- **Fig 4 :22-25** - *"Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down."* Sending the whole framework before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the shape and the refuter; offer links after.
- **Fig 10 :42** - *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* Seat 4 must not hold the interiors. A recipient who has absorbed the framework before the verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- **Fig 9 :27-28** - *"describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit."* Ask what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- **Fig 2 :40** - *"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"*; **Seat Map line 163** - *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* A mass send selected for people who already agree produces a convention, not corroboration.
- **Fig 2 :42** - *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* The cost here is high, so prefer a few genuinely different frames over many similar ones.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the residual, shape, and refuter; (2) ask what they would count as a failure and record it; (3) only then the three links, with the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer

1. **Stage 1:** validate the output against the ground. Fig 10 :52 (*"Bring a characterised residual, never the source"*) and Fig 2 :45 (*"Frames range against the ground. They do not negotiate with it."*).
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. Seat 4 must not hold the interiors, so the stage-1 verdict is recorded before any theory is shown and later agreement is never pooled (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3, optional:** use the formula recursively on another problem, with each user writing their own refuter and stating their own mode. Fig 4 :22 permits sending a method; Formula "Direction" requires the user to bind the sought-for shape before running.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. The refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 4 are live. Repair or retract before sending.
