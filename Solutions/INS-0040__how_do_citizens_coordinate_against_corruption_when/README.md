## The question restated as an effect

Decide which of two things to do about a gap. Option A: keep adding exhortations, rewards, and reporting channels on the assumption that the missing thing is more individual willingness or more reports. Option B: treat the gap as a union of three conditions (individual private cost, a channel that can lose contact with the ground, and frames that are correlated rather than independent), split it, and build the habitat and the channel so that contact with the ground survives. A sends frame-internal metrics (report counts, pledges) and purchases agreement from people who share one evidence pool. B sends a checkable shape, written refuters, and an instrument for the middle link that is currently unmeasured. The residual is stated in effect terms in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` before any figure line is chosen.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree that corruption is bad or that this derivation is right.
- No bulk-send plan.

Three reasons in the figures' own words:

1. **A room does not upgrade the label.** *"Adding people raises the vocabulary and does not add an anchor."* (Seat Map line 283). A campaign of agreeable recipients is a larger room, not a ground.
2. **Correlation disqualifies a verdict.** *"High correlation is disqualifying for a verdict and efficient for a convention."* (Seat Map line 163). Selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. **Asking people to endorse before checking spends their independence.** *"A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it."* (Fig 10 :42). The outside frame must not hold the interiors before it gives a verdict.

Legally: unsolicited bulk email to harvested addresses breaches GDPR and CAN-SPAM style rules in many jurisdictions and gets a sending domain blacklisted. That is a practical failure on top of the structural one. All venues below are public, institutional, and opt-in.
