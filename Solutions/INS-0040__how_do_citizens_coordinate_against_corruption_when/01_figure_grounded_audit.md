## 0. The residual, stated in effect terms before any figure is chosen

A citizen sees corruption. Reporting it carries a high private cost (retaliation, career damage, loss of relationships) while the benefit (less corruption) is diffuse and shared by people who did not report. In loop terms:

> `R_slot = | Γ(b, a) − o′ |` for each citizen-slot s, where the bet `a` is "report" or "stay silent", the prediction `Γ(b,a)` is the expected private payoff, and `o′` is what actually happens to corruption and to the reporter.

Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* So R is per citizen, per institution, per episode. A national "trust in government" average is not this residual.

The anchor (Fig 2 :29) is whether corruption actually declines and whether a report is actually acted on, a counter and an outcome. It is not the number of reports filed, the number of pledges signed, or the institution's own self-reported clearance rate, because those move when the institution pushes on them (Fig 2 :30: *"If it moves when you push on it, it is not an anchor."*).

What an answer would look like (Formula "Direction": make the sought-for shape an ordinary term): a specification of which conditions must jointly hold for R to shrink, which of them can be measured, and which institutional lever changes which condition. Bound that as the term `COORD-SHAPE`. It is a shape, not a name: it must not select a single programme or a single country's agency.

## 1. Domain census (before choosing any figure line)

**R by arity, symmetry, scale.** Arity: three conditions, private cost, channel contact, frame correlation. Symmetry: reporting is a directed act from a citizen toward an institution and a ground, not a symmetric joining of equals; the bond is a sequence (`→`) and a comparison (`⋛`), not `+`. Scale: the individual decision, the small group, the institution, and the societal norm are four strata with a lossy transform between them (Fig 4 :27: *"The transform between scales loses information, and that is the point."*).

| domain | actors | currency | anchor | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 Individual reporting decision | citizen, corrupt official, employer, family | private cost versus private benefit | actual retaliation or protection outcome | admitted, insufficient alone |
| D2 Reporting channel / ground maintainer | ombuds, audit, court, journalist, hotline, ALAC | contact with the ground: does the report register and get acted on | the ground, which does not decay (Fig 10 :24) | admitted |
| D3 Coordination network / independent frames | many citizens, peer groups, media, oversight bodies | independence of evidence (how little drawn from one pool) | the ground via triangulation (Fig 2 :31-32) | admitted |
| D4 Habitat / norms and institutions | laws, protection regimes, repeated interaction, social norms | capability of agent-and-habitat | whether contact changes (Fig 11 :39-40) | admitted |
| X1 "Collective courage" as a free-floating citizen property | the heroic individual | virtue | none that changes with it and can be named | not a domain yet; absorbed into D4 |
| X2 "More reporting" as the goal | reports, hotlines, pledges | count of reports | none; frame-internal (Fig 1 :24-27) | absorbed into D2 and D3 as a filter, never the ground |

**Partition or union.** Union, not partition. D1, D2, and D4 form the contact compound; D2 and D3 form the independence compound. Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing.

**Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception (notice corruption) → vocabulary (name what it affords and what would count as progress) → objective → planning (a route to a channel) → learn and carry (a protected precedent). "Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not" (Fig 3 :19). The middle link, having terms for what a situation affords and saying what would count as progress, is where such chains usually break (Fig 3 :24-27), and it is the one with no instrument here.

**Strain test applied.** I first tried to make "collective courage" a missing primitive to import under Fig 6. That strained. Re-census shows it is a valence on the habitat (Fig 11 :49: *"An actor's valence is relative to the residual, not a property it carries."*), not an atom. No import is owed. Strain dissolved by re-census, not smoothed over. See section 7.

## 3. Findings F1 to F9

### F1 - The problem is a union, so a single lever cannot clear it (method, decisive)

Fig 9 :55: *"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."* Fig 9 :56: *"A single lever clears the region where only one condition holds and leaves the intersections standing, because the second condition still binds."* Fig 9 :57: *"What remains is smaller and it is also categorically different: a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*

The three conditions (private cost, channel contact, frame correlation) overlap. Raising rewards fixes D1 where D2 and D3 do not bind; building a hotline fixes D2 where D1 still binds; building trust fixes D3 where the channel still loses contact. Only the union is the question. Mode `general` for the union rule, `specified` for the three conditions. **Refuter:** one intervention clears all three conditions with no categorically different residue (Fig 9 :57 fails).

### F2 - You cannot fix the citizen in isolation (D1, triangulated)

Fig 11 :39: *"So the answerable question is not what intelligence is, but what makes a system capable. And capability is a property of agent-and-habitat, never of the agent alone."* Fig 11 :40: *"A system cannot be made more capable by improving the agent in isolation. An improvement that does not change contact changes nothing, however much it improves."* Triangulated with F1 (Fig 9 :55), low overlap (habitat capability versus union structure).

Training citizens to be braver, or paying a one-off reward, is an agent-only improvement if it leaves the channel and the norm unchanged. It does not change contact, so it does not shrink the ground residual. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a controlled citizen-only intervention that lowers corruption on the ground with no change to channel or habitat.

### F3 - The channel decays, not the ground; keep the anchor reachable (D2, triangulated)

Fig 10 :24: *"the ground does not decay, the channel does"*. Fig 10 :25-26: *"stale percepts, dropped transients, saturated metrics, pooled readings"*. Fig 10 :27-28: *"the output is not a verdict, it is you have lost touch"* and *"keep the anchor reachable"*. Triangulated with Fig 2 :29 (anchor definition) and Fig 2 :33 (*"The anchor must not update."*), low overlap (channel maintenance versus anchor definition).

A reporting channel that is not safe, not confidential, or not acted on is a decayed channel. Its silence means "lost touch," not "no corruption." The operational form is the one TI describes publicly: *"Our 60 Advocacy and Legal Advice Centres (ALACs) provide free and confidential legal advice and allow people to safely report corruption"* (world source, secondary). Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a channel that registers reports yet whose "ground" moves when the institution pushes on it (the metric is the institution's own clearance rate), which is Fig 2 panel 1, not an anchor.

### F4 - Independence, not agreement, is the currency of coordination (D3, triangulated)

Fig 2 :31: *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :35: *"Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."* Fig 8 :47: *"Independence is one quantity read twice."* Fig 8 :49: *"Two frames that overlap almost completely are one frame wearing two names."* Fig 7 :12-13: *"same closure, handed along;"* *"agreement compounds, evidence does not"*. Fig 7 :35: *"A room's agreement is variation, not a verdict. Selection between rooms is where the ground votes."*

A chain of citizens who all agree because they read the same grievance in the same media is a mirror chain, one long room. It has motion, no progress (Fig 7 :32). What makes progress is the next room differing from the last and matching the residual (Fig 7 :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*). Triangulated across Fig 2, Fig 7, and Fig 8, distinct roles and low overlap between them. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a coordinated campaign drawing entirely on one evidence pool that nevertheless moves the ground, showing that agreement, not triangulation, can settle it.

### F5 - Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong (D3, triangulated)

Fig 2 :42: *"Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."* Fig 6 :50: *"Distribute the search, and keep the searchers independent."* Fig 6 :53: *"searchers drawing on the same evidence are one searcher wearing many names."*

Here the cost of being wrong is high: retaliation is real and a false accusation harms the accused. The figure therefore prices a lot of independence, not a lot of agreement. "Distribute the search" licenses many reporters and many oversight bodies only under the independence condition. Triangulated with F4 (Fig 2 and Fig 6, distinct quantities: correction value versus search value). Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** a system where increasing the number of correlated reporters increases ground-valid corrections.

### F6 - The chain needs grammar at the middle link (D4 and method, triangulated)

Fig 12 :111: *"A vocabulary without bonds can name a state and cannot name a route to one."* Fig 12 :112: *"A plan is a sequence. A goal is a comparison. Progress is a subtraction. A refutation is a negation, and that one is not a bond."* Fig 3 :27: *"A link with no instrument is not a link that works. It is a link nobody has looked at."* Fig 3 :19: *"Optimising a link that already works cannot rescue one that does not."*

Citizens can name corruption (`state`) and can name a route (`plan`, `→`). What they and their institutions usually lack is the third link: an operational comparison stating what would count as progress (a goal, `⋛`) and a subtraction measuring it (progress, `−`). Without that instrument, report counts fill the slot and are frame-internal (Fig 1 :24-27: *"coverage, and compression achieved"*, *"predicates minted, and credibility accrued"*, *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*). Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** an institution that measures progress with a ground-valid subtraction (corruption incidents or losses actually reduced, independently verified) and shows the measure driving the correction, not just the count.

### F7 - Separate the reporter from the verifier; the pool must be unauthored (D2 and D4, triangulated)

Fig 10 :86: *"Outside the slice being graded. Whoever is measured by a reading cannot own it."* Fig 10 :91: *"Sees what agreement hides. The collapses are invisible from inside the frames that are collapsing."* Fig 10 :73: *"The residual selects the members, but only from what is available."* Fig 10 :76-79: *"a lottery"*, *"everyone who qualifies"*, *"a set assembled before the question existed"*, *"or the habitat itself, enumerated rather than composed."* Fig 10 :59: *"an interest in the outcome is unavoidable, an interest in the reading is disqualifying."* Fig 10 :60: *"Where a stake cannot be removed, declare it."*

A reporter has an unavoidable interest in the outcome (they want the corruption stopped), which is legitimate. If the same person also owns the reading of whether the corruption stopped, the interest becomes an interest in the reading and is disqualifying. So the accuser and the adjudicator must be different seats, and the pool of checkers must be assembled by a mechanism (lottery, all who qualify, a pre-existing set), not hand-picked for likely agreement. Fig 10 :74: *"Reverse the order and the stacking is the selection."* Triangulated with F4 (Fig 10 selection rule versus Fig 2 independence), low overlap. Mode `specified`. **Refuter:** ground-valid corrections produced by a hand-picked pool sharing the investigators' frame, with no unauthored mechanism and no separation of reporter and verifier.

### F8 - The framework returns a shape, not a programme name (method, gap boundary)

Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."* Fig 13 :134: *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"* and Fig 8 :39: *"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."*

After a genuine re-census, no figure line selects a specific anti-corruption agency design, a specific sanction, or a specific country's reform package. The figures constrain the shape (contact maintained, independence bought, union split, grammar installed, checker outside the slice) and refuse the name. This is a `gap`, declared, not a hidden answer. Mode `general` on the limit.

### F9 - No final institutional form (D4, general)

Fig 7 :6: *"the limit: no last room"*. Fig 7 :30: *"every room closes some of the gap; no finite number of them ever arrives"*. Fig 7 :36: *"The next room must differ from the last, and match the residual it surfaced."*

Any claim that one reform is the last word, or that a fixed anti-corruption body can be applied unchanged as the residual moves, is the terminal room the figures forbid. Capability changes valence when the residual changes (Fig 11 :51: *"No standing list of good actors survives a change of residual."*). Mode `general`. Refuter: a single institutional form that remains ground-valid across a documented change of the dominant corruption residual.

## 4. Pre-registered refuter (written before the conclusion)

Per Seat Map line 287: *"a falsifier written before the run is a design instrument, not only an honesty device."*

The `COORD-SHAPE` claim is refuted if any of the following holds:

1. A citizen-only intervention, with no change to channel or habitat, lowers corruption on a ground-valid, independently verified measure (breaks F2 and Fig 11 :40).
2. A reporting channel's own metric is the supposed ground and moves when the institution pushes on it, yet is still treated as the anchor (breaks F3 and Fig 2 :33).
3. A chain of mutually agreeing reports drawing on one evidence pool moves the ground (breaks F4 and Fig 7 :35).
4. One lever clears all three conditions with no categorically different residue (breaks F1 and Fig 9 :57).
5. Hand-picked checkers who share the investigators' frame, with no unauthored selection and no reporter/verifier separation, produce ground-valid corrections (breaks F7 and Fig 10 :73-79, :86).

Until one is run, no part of this may be labelled `grounded`. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. Recommendation

1. Split the union before searching (F1). Do not run a "more reporting" campaign over an unexamined compound.
2. Budget to change contact, not to improve the citizen in isolation (F2, Fig 11 :40). Protection, confidentiality, and follow-through are habitat changes; exhortation is not.
3. Treat the channel as a maintainer of the anchor (F3). Measure whether reports are acted on by an outcome the institution cannot move.
4. Buy independence in proportion to the high cost of being wrong (F5). Many correlated voices are one voice; require separate evidence and a differing next room (F4).
5. Install the missing middle link: a ground-valid subtraction for progress, not a count of reports (F6, Fig 3 :27).
6. Put the checker outside the graded slice and select by an unauthored mechanism (F7). Declare unavoidable stakes.
7. Send the generator, not a recording (Fig 4 :25: *"A recording carried upward looks like knowledge and is a description of one occasion."*). The verification packet carries the shape and its refuters, not a success story.
