## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*, then *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest. Indiscernibility is not identity."* Fig 9 :32: *"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."*

From *The Library of Babel*: *"you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."*

So a shape is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains; it does not fill. For INF-0013 the shape is:

> Any information change settled against the ground has a physical companion change in the carrier or its environment; **which** physical quantity pays is left open.

The first clause is a constraint. The second is an honest slot. Filling the slot with "kT ln 2" is a name, and the figures do not supply it.

## 2. How the shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."*
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* This is why `02_outreach_email.md` sends a refuter rather than a theory.
- *They're Scanning Books*: *"outputs do not contain their generator."* A reading of the carrier is an output; the physical process that produced it is the generator. Naming the source from the reading is the error the shape avoids.
- Fig 6 :30: *"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."* Effect terms here: "would an instrument see a state change?", not "is information metaphysically physical?"

## 3. What licenses a name

Two things, both outside the shape:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays. A gate is not the ground."* For INF-0013 this is a calorimeter, a detector, a counter (Bérut et al. measured the erasure bound; see `10_source_ledger.csv`).
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Naming entropy as the paying quantity is an import from thermodynamics into Γ, and it must be marked as such.

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the failure mode. An instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it. Here: several physical implementations can give the same information reading, so the reading cannot name its physical source.

## 4. The answer shape for this question

The shape has three bound terms and one open slot:

| bound term | source of the constraint | mode |
|---|---|---|
| an information delta is not free | Fig 12 :5, :99, :101 (bond and price) | `specified` |
| the arrangement is part of the carrier | Fig 12 :94, :117 (isomer, decisive in behaviour) | `specified` |
| a transfer is energy bonded to a spendable difference | Fig 13 :76 (energy plus gradient compose a flow) | `specified` |
| **which physical quantity pays** | no figure line | `gap` |

The last row is the slot. The figures return a shape, not a name, precisely where a primitive would have to be added to Γ. This is why `07_the_tether_answer.md` refuses to say "information is physical" as a figure-derived verdict and why `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` F8 is marked `gap` rather than answered.

## 5. Correction to my own reading of "shape, not a name"

I first read the rule as forbidding any physical naming at all. That overreads it. The README's phrase is about *"a **missing** thing"*, that is, a primitive not held in Γ. The bond vocabulary and the six terms are already held, so using them is not an import that moves the wall (Fig 6 :25: *"An import adds no atom to the world."*). What is missing here is the thermodynamic-cost mechanism, so the rule applies to that slot, not to the structural claims F1 to F7.

The correction is recorded, not erased. Earlier draft sentence: *"the framework cannot say anything physical about information."* Corrected sentence: *"the framework can constrain the physical companion change; it cannot name the thermodynamic quantity that pays."* Both are kept so the narrowing is visible.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well known people who agree with "information is physical" is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is: give the claim a budget to be tried, let a measurement settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post"*, and *"the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* The framework's own demo note says outputs *"could never be verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula."* So `01` and this file are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They are **not** tests of the claim and cannot settle it.

## 7. One-paragraph version

A shape is a constraint you can state before you can name the thing that satisfies it. For this question it says: an information change that registers against the ground always has a physical companion change, because the bond is where the information lives and the arrangement is the substance; but the figures price descriptions in bits and do not price joules, so which physical quantity pays is an open slot. The slot is closed by an instrument, not by an argument, and until then the honest report is a shape with a pre-registered refuter, not a verdict.
