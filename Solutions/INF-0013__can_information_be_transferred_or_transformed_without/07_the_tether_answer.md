# The Tether's answer: information and physical change

**Mode: `specified` on the structural verdict, `general` on the shape, `gap` on the thermodynamic-cost sub-question. Not `grounded`.** This is a simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` section 6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

The question bundles three conditions: an information delta, a physical state delta, and a dissipation delta. Fig 9 :55: **"A bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate ones."** A single lever cannot clear all three. The answer is therefore per domain, never an average.

## 2. The shape, domain by domain

**D1, informational or compositional. No settled information delta with zero change of arrangement.** Fig 12 :5: **"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."** Fig 12 :101: **"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."** An information change with no arrangement change is the flat case. It carries nothing, so it is not a transfer. Triangulated with Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."**

**D2, physical. The carrier includes the arrangement, and transfer is a flow.** Fig 12 :94: **"The arrangement is the substance."** Fig 12 :117: **"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."** So "same ingredients, different information" is not "same physical carrier": the behaviour differs. Fig 13 :76: **"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."** Transfer has a physical substrate, energy bonded to a spendable difference. Single-source for the flow claim; the contact rule in Fig 11 :40 is a different object and is not counted as a second witness.

**D3, frame transfer. What changes is the frame's library, not the world's inventory.** Fig 6 :25: **"An import adds no atom to the world."** Fig 6 :26: **"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."** Naming the carrier decides the verdict. If the carrier is Γ, it changed. If it is the world's atoms, it did not.

**D4, thermodynamic cost. Gap.** No figure line prices dissipation. Fig 5 :45 gives a price in bits: **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."** Bits are not joules. The figures can say a physical companion change is required; they cannot name the quantity that pays. The world literature (Landauer, Bennett, Sagawa, Maroney, Bérut et al.) answers that sub-question, but as secondary corroboration only. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"**.

**Scale caveat.** Fig 4 :30: **"So take the round trip and measure the gap. Without it you are navigating by dead reckoning: confident about a position nobody checked."** A verdict true at the atom level can be false at the arrangement level. Keep them separate.

## 3. Verdict

**No, for any information change that is settled against the ground.** Such a change always has a physical companion change in the carrier or its environment. The only changes that occur with no physical counterpart are frame-internal re-descriptions (identity relabelings, conventions, imports described purely as access), and those are not transfers of information in the physical sense. **Open, for the cost:** which physical quantity pays, and whether the transformation is thermodynamically reversible, is a `gap` inside the figures and a settled experimental question outside them.

## 4. What it explicitly does not say

- Not "information is physical" as a figure-derived verdict. That is a world claim, not a quoted line.
- Not a derivation of Landauer's bound, and not a claim that erasure always costs kT ln 2.
- Not that any logically reversible transformation dissipates zero heat. The figures do not decide this; Bennett 2003 and Sagawa 2014 say it can be arbitrarily small in principle, and the hardware still changes state.
- Not that semantic content is a physical substance. Semantic content fails the anchor test and is not promoted to a domain.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken in this derivation.

## 5. What would settle it: the ground

1. **R1:** a settled information transfer with unchanged physical state of carrier and environment at every scale. This would break the structural verdict.
2. **R2:** a bond carrying information at zero description cost, settled by an instrument. This would break the bond-price claim.
3. **R3:** a transfer with no energy and no spendable gradient anywhere, registered at the ground. This would break the flow claim.
4. **Cost:** a measurement of the paying quantity in a specific implementation (Bérut et al. measured heat at the Landauer bound for erasure; that is an anchor, not a figure line).
5. A figure re-census that produces a line pricing thermodynamic dissipation would close the gap and move the mode on that sub-question.

## 6. The honest stop

Unreached on the cost sub-question, not unreachable. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."** The framework returns a shape, not a name, and *"Indiscernibility is not identity"* (canonical README). The lineage is recorded so the stop is checkable. Agreement with the eight essays is one evidence pool talking, not corroboration (Fig 2 :40: **"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"**).

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The figures do not say "information is physical" and do not derive Landauer's bound. They say something narrower and checkable: information lives in the bond, not just in the ingredient list, and a bond that costs nothing carries nothing, so an information change that is settled against the ground always has a physical companion change. Same atoms under a different arrangement give a different substance with different behaviour. What moves in a transfer into a frame is the frame's library, not the world's inventory. The figures price descriptions in bits, not joules, so which physical quantity pays, and whether a reversible transformation dissipates anything, stays open and is answered by experiment. What is offered is the shape, the refuters written in advance, and the instrument that would close the slot.
