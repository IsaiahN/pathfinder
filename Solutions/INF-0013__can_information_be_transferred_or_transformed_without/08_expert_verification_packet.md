# Expert verification packet: the Tether on information and physical change

**What this is:** a claim in a form an experimentalist or theorist in information and physical change research can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion.

**Mode: `specified` on the structural claim, `gap` on the cost sub-question. Not `grounded`.** No new measurement is claimed here.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

An information change that is settled against the ground always has a physical companion change in the carrier or its environment. The bond carries the information the operands do not, and a bond that costs nothing is the flat case that carries nothing; so an information delta with no change of arrangement is not a transfer. Where two arrangements share all ingredients but differ in the bond, the behaviour differs, so the physical carrier did change. What moves in a transfer into a frame is the frame's library, not the world's inventory. Which physical quantity pays, and whether a logically reversible transformation dissipates heat, is **not** decided by the figures and is marked as a gap.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1 - framework-internal; checkable by reading Figure 12 and Figure 1
- **Statement:** no settled information delta with zero change of arrangement; a free bond carries nothing.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :5 *"A set of ingredients is not one. The bond is the rest of the information, and it is the half nothing was recording."* Fig 12 :99 *"A bond costs what it could have been, and nothing more."* Fig 12 :101 *"A bond that costs nothing is a bond that carries nothing, which is the flat case."* Fig 1 :27 *"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a bond carrying information at zero description cost that is settled by an instrument.

### C2 - physics-facing; checkable by anyone who works with carriers
- **Statement:** the carrier includes the arrangement, not just the ingredient set; and a transfer is energy bonded to a spendable gradient.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 12 :94 *"The arrangement is the substance."* Fig 12 :117 *"isomer. Same atoms, different bond, different substance. The difference is invisible in the formula and decisive in the behaviour."* Fig 13 :76 *"Energy and a gradient compose a flow."*
- **What to check:** does a real carrier (spin, charge, colloidal position, field mode) ever change its information content while every physical state, including arrangement and environment, stays identical? If you say yes, name the instrument that returned the different reading.
- **Refuter:** a settled transfer with no energy and no spendable difference anywhere, or two settled arrangements with identical atoms and bonds that differ in measured behaviour.

### C3 - framework-internal, with a stated gap
- **Statement:** a transfer into a frame changes Γ and adds no atom to the world; the thermodynamic cost of the transformation is not decided by the figures.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 6 :25 *"An import adds no atom to the world."* Fig 6 :26 *"The world does not gain primitives; a frame gains access."* Fig 5 :45 *"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."* Fig 13 :134 *"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence"*.
- **Refuter:** an import that adds a primitive to the world, or a figure line that prices thermodynamic dissipation directly.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not "information is physical" as a figure-derived verdict.
- Not a derivation of Landauer's bound or of kT ln 2.
- Not that logically reversible transformations dissipate zero heat. The figures are silent on this; Bennett 2003 and Sagawa 2014 are world sources, not figure lines.
- Not that semantic content is a substance, and not that an observer's interpretation is a physical field.
- Not `grounded`.

## 5. What would settle it

A ground reading, not agreement. The relevant instruments already exist in the field: calorimetric or feedback-trap measurements of erasure (Berut et al. 2015), reversible-circuit energy-recovery tests, and quantum no-deleting experiments. Three questions:

1. Does the physical state of the carrier change when the information reading changes? At what scale?
2. Is the paying quantity heat, another conserved quantity, or nothing at all in the quasistatic limit?
3. Can a purely conventional relabeling under identity change an instrument's reading? If yes, C1 fails.

## 6. What an expert can check in twenty minutes

1. Read Fig 12 :5, :94, :99, :101, :117 and Fig 1 :27. Six short passages.
2. Check C2 against one carrier you know well: does arrangement change with information content?
3. Say whether your favourite result in the field is a figure line or a world import. If it is an import, mark its source.
4. Name one instrument that would close the cost gap, or say why none is needed.
5. Send the refutation. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.
