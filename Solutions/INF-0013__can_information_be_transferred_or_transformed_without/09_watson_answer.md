## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, whether an information delta can occur with no corresponding physical change in the carrier. Option A: yes, information is abstract, so semantic content can change with no physical difference. Option B: no for anything settled against the ground; only frame-internal re-descriptions change with no physical counterpart, and they are not transfers. What each leads to: A gives an information measure with no nameable anchor, so no instrument can test it; B gives a checkable shape, a pre-registered refuter and an open slot for the cost sub-question.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

No, for any information change settled against the ground. A physical companion change in the carrier or its environment is required, because information is carried in bonds and the arrangement is the substance; transfer into a frame changes the library, not the world's inventory. The only changes with no physical counterpart are identity relabelings and conventions, which are not transfers. The thermodynamic-cost sub-question is a **gap** in the figure set and is answered, if at all, by world measurement. This is a `specified`, ungrounded result, with the cost sub-question explicitly open.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, carries its refuter before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending an outside reader's independence by sending a conclusion instead of a generator; Fig 2 :42 says "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong." That cost is avoidable by leading with the refuter.
- **undoable:** yes. The cost claim is not asserted, so nothing has to be withdrawn. The strong reading "information is physical, therefore kT ln 2" is never made. A world result can be imported later with its source gate and shadow test.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. R1, a settled transfer with unchanged physical state at every scale, breaks the structural verdict. R2, a bond carrying information at zero cost, breaks the price claim. R3, a transfer with no energy and no gradient, breaks the flow claim. A figure line pricing thermodynamic dissipation would close the gap and move the mode on that sub-question. An import with a source gate and a shadow test can add the paying quantity after the fact.
