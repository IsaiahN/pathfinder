**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the residual is unbundled and the mode labels in `07_the_tether_answer.md` are intact. The output must survive validation or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** Check one shape claim before you decide whether the framework is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The residual, in effect terms.** Two local facts are measured: space is very close to flat, and matched-circle searches have found no compact topology at scales below the last-scattering surface. Two global facts are not measured: whether the whole space is compact, and whether it is simply or multiply connected. The question bundles these into one "finite or infinite" verdict. The figures require an overlap like this to be unbundled before it is searched.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Local flatness does not decide boundedness. A flat compact 3-manifold and flat Euclidean 3-space show the same local geometry and differ only in global arrangement, which is decisive for whether space is finite. So the three conditions get three separate verdicts: curvature is measured; boundedness is unknown; connectivity is unknown. A null search is not a proof of absence. From inside the CMB frame, "compact but larger than our sky" and "not compact" look the same. The honest report is **unreached at this budget**, not "infinite".
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that the universe is infinite; that it is simply connected; that any named topology is the answer; and that this derivation is a measurement. It is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures. The framework returns a shape ("locally flat; if compact, the fundamental domain exceeds the observed last-scattering radius; if multiply connected, the identifications are undetected at current resolution"), not a name.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim.** I wrote these before sending this:
> 1. a single local measurement that determines both boundedness and connectivity;
> 2. a local reading that distinguishes a flat compact 3-manifold from Euclidean 3-space, or a derivation of "boundedness" as a seventh primitive;
> 3. a demonstration that the CMB frame is a sealed room, so a null search proves absence;
> 4. a derivation of a unique topology from the six-term set, or a ground reading that settles one.
>
> If any holds, I retract the relevant claim. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Canonical figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`
> - Planck Collaboration, "Planck 2015 results. XVIII. Background geometry & topology": https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01593
> - Cornish, Spergel, Starkman and Komatsu, "Constraining the Topology of the Universe": https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0310233
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.
