## The residual in effect terms

Two local facts are settled (space is very close to flat, and no matched circles have been found), while two global questions are not: is the whole space compact, and is it simply or multiply connected. The question bundles a measurable local geometry together with two unmeasurable-at-this-budget global conditions. What an answer would look like, written down and bound as an ordinary term so it can accrue a residual: a four-tuple `(local curvature, boundedness, connectivity, the instrument that would decide the latter two)`. An answer that drops the fourth element is not an answer to this question, it is a preference.

## What each option leads to

- **Option A (assert finite or infinite, name a topology now).** Converts a null search into an absence proof, names what the framework returns only as a shape, and spends the independence of the people asked to check it.
- **Option B (unbundle, narrow, record the stop).** Returns a checkable shape, a set of candidate conditions with ranked refuters, and an explicit account of what instrument would close each slot.

This solution takes Option B. It is a **simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures**, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the question it reports on.
