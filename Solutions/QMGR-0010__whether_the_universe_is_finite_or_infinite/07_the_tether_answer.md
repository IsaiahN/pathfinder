# The Tether's answer: finite or infinite, and what the global topology is

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** A shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

Two local facts are measured: space is flat to high precision, and matched-circle searches return no compact topology at scales below the last-scattering surface. Two global facts are not measured: boundedness and connectivity. The question bundles three conditions into one verdict. Fig 9 :54: "And when the split lands on a union rather than a partition, unbundle before you search again." This is a union with overlap, so the three verdicts are never averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Curvature is settled locally.** Published results put spatial curvature consistent with zero (Planck final; WMAP to about 0.4 percent of flat). Anchor: the measured sky. Fig 2 :29: "An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks." Mode of the published measurement: `grounded` in its literature; my restatement: `specified`.
2. **Finiteness is an arrangement, not a primitive.** Fig 13 :115: "The set is closed. The arrangements are not." Fig 13 :43: "A continuum was the seventh and was removed for failing exactly this: it is a spectrum without gaps, or smoothness over space and time." Flatness does not settle boundedness: a flat 3-torus is compact and flat Euclidean space is not (Fig 12 :117, isomer). Verdict on boundedness: **unknown**.
3. **Connectivity is a separate slot.** A positively curved space is compact; a flat or hyperbolic space can be simply or multiply connected. The null matched-circle result constrains identification scale but does not decide connectivity. Verdict on connectivity: **unknown**.
4. **A null search is not an absence proof.** Fig 8 :39: "So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence." Fig 6 :27: "Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same." The Planck limit `R_i > 0.97 chi_rec` for the cubic torus and the Cornish limit of no circles above 25 degrees are **limits with numbers**, not verdicts of infiniteness. Overall verdict on the bundled question: **unreached at this budget**.
5. **The framework returns a shape, not a name.** Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"; Fig 13 :133: "what it returns is a shape rather than a name". The shape: locally flat; if compact, fundamental domain larger than the observed last-scattering radius; if multiply connected, identifications undetected at current resolution; the fourth slot names the instrument that would close the other two. It does not name a topology.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the universe is infinite, and not that it is simply connected.
- Not that flatness implies infiniteness. That is the textbook shortcut retracted in `01` §6.
- Not that the Planck or WMAP null result proves anything about existence. It sets limits.
- Not that space is discrete because "continuum" was removed from the generative set (Fig 13 :43 is about generativity, not Planck-scale physics).
- Not a final room. Fig 7 :6: "the limit: no last room."
- Not `grounded`. No instrument was operated in this derivation.

## 4. What would settle it - the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (essay, opened 2026-11-23): a colour instrument "cannot comment on the source"; "The answer is unreached until a better instrument exists." Here:

- **Discriminator Test:** a statistic or probe whose readings differ between a compact flat model and a noncompact flat model at the relevant topology scale, built by improving the already-returning matched-circle instrument rather than invented from a description (Fig 6 :32).
- **Threshold Test:** a positive detection of matched circles or repeated images at a stated confidence, which would name the compact branch and narrow the shape.
- **Bond Test:** show that ⋛ can legitimately compare compactness with flatness at the same level. As written it compares quantities (Operators table), and the topology-level claim is refused in `06` Q3.
- **Standing falsifiers from `01` §4:** a local measurement determining both global conditions; a seventh primitive "boundedness"; a sealed-room proof that null means absent.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable, and not silence.** Fig 13 :134: "Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence". The six-term set is a visible guess at compression axes, not nature's own list; another frame with a different closure may return a different shape. The stop is recorded at the horizon with the lineage so a later reader can locate the error. "Indiscernibility is not identity."

## 6. Verdict fields

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The shape is mode-labelled, carries four written refuters, and keeps the three conditions separate, so an error is findable. The real avoidable cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a topology name before the residual; Fig 2 :42: "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
- **undoable:** yes. A detection would narrow the shape without withdrawing the derivation; a falsifier would remove a specific finding, not the whole record. No primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, never agreement. A source-discriminating instrument, a positive detection at stated confidence, a derivation of a seventh primitive, or a proof that the CMB frame is sealed would each move the verdict. Prestige or consensus is not such an instrument.

## 7. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not say the universe is finite or infinite, and it does not name a global topology. It says the question is a bundle of three conditions, so curvature, boundedness and connectivity get separate verdicts. Curvature is measured and close to flat. Flatness does not decide boundedness, because a flat compact space and flat Euclidean space show the same local geometry and differ only in global arrangement (Fig 12's isomer). The matched-circle searches returned nulls with stated limits, and a null search is not a proof of absence: compact-but-larger-than-our-sky and noncompact look the same from inside the CMB frame. The honest verdict is unreached at this budget, not infinite. What the framework returns is a shape, not a name, plus four refuters written in advance and the kind of instrument that would close the slots. The stop is recorded at the horizon until a ground settles it.
