# Outreach draft: check the shape before deciding whether the framing is worth reading

**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover letter attached to a submission through the public venues in `03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** do not send until the four strains in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` section 6 are understood and the numerical gap is stated as a gap. The output must survive the refuter offered below or the two-stage offer collapses on contact.

**Subject:** One checkable claim about compound disturbances, before any theory

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know. I am not asking you to read a framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The output (`general` on the shape, `specified` on the domains, `gap` on numbers):**
>
> Heat waves, droughts, fires, storms and floods are not five separable effects that add. They form an overlapping compound. Drought changes the fuel and the water status before the fire; fire changes the canopy and the soil before the rain or the storm; heat and low water act at once. The order and the kind of link are part of the object, not bookkeeping. "Fire then heavy rain" and "heavy rain then fire" are different compounds even when the event list is identical.
>
> The ecological effect does not stop at the disturbed patch. It runs through the material and information legacies that remain, through who survives and who colonises and who interacts, and it can be three steps out before the residual that matters appears. Because a legacy's sign is relative to the outcome you are tracking, the same remnant patch can buffer recovery from one event and amplify the next one. There is therefore no universal "synergistic" sign to assert.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** that any named compound produces a particular direction or magnitude of ecological change, that there is a threshold number I can supply, that the five disturbances map onto a six-term substrate, or that this replaces event-by-event field measurement. The framework returns a shape, not a name, and the directional question is a declared gap with named instruments that would close it.
>
> **What would refute the shape claim:**
> 1. the five effects are additive and separable in all systems and all orders;
> 2. a compound ecological outcome is derivable from forcing magnitudes with no habitat observation anywhere in the chain;
> 3. every ordering of a disturbance pair gives the same recovery trajectory and the same state outcome in every measured system;
> 4. a compound's measurable effect is strictly zero beyond directly affected individuals in every system studied;
> 5. one aggregate metric fully determines the per-slot response of every compound, with no slot-level information.
>
> I wrote these down before sending this. I have not run them; they are not rhetorical. If any one holds, I will withdraw or revise the decomposition. A one-line "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no reply is needed.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** the sources are below. The figures are the authority, not my summary of them.
> - Verbatim figure text with line numbers (local framework extract): `FRAMEWORK/reference/03-FIGURES_TEXT.md`, cited as `Fig N :line`
> - Canonical usage rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/reference/FIGURES_README_canonical.md`
> - The eight-step loop and mode rule (local): `FRAMEWORK/figures/THE_FORMULA.md`
>

Write down what would refute your result *before* you run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a reported success.

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1.** Send the shape claim and the five refuters. Record the reader's verdict before showing any framework material.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only.** If they are curious, offer the three local links. Their later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40).
3. **Stage 3.** Offer the loop as a reusable method, with the instruction that each user writes their own refuter and states their own mode.

Hard precondition: stages 1 to 3 only work if the stage-1 output survives. It is a shape claim with an open gap, not an ecological forecast. Do not strengthen it into one in order to make it sound more useful.
