## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The figures README states the limit: the framework returns *"what a missing thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)"*. Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself"**. Fig 13 :135: **"It cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."**

From *The Library of Babel*: *"You can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."* Also: *"The problem you have determines what makes a book relevant to you or not."*

For ECO-0033 a **shape** is therefore the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name the outcome. It constrains; it does not fill. "An ordered compound acting through an enumerated habitat, with a residual-relative valence and an open direction" is a shape. "Drought then fire in this forest will reduce carbon by 11 percent" is a name, and the figures do not supply it.

Concretely, the shape here has four constraints:

1. the five disturbances form a union compound with typed, ordered links, not a list;
2. the route from compound to ecological change runs through enumerated habitat actors, conditions and relations, not from forcing values;
3. the residual is indexed per compound and per ecological slot, never averaged;
4. the direction and magnitude of a named compound are unbound until the ground pays.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: use the vocabulary you already have that is closest to the thing, look for recurring new terms that overlap or correlate with it, recurse. *"With each new term gained, you narrow the space and deepen your vocabulary."* In this run the starting vocabulary was "interaction", "legacy", "recovery" and "compound"; the new terms that narrowed the space were "linked versus compound" (Buma 2015), "preconditioned / multivariate / temporally / spatially compounding" (Zscheischler et al. 2020) and "resilience debt" (Johnstone et al. 2016).
- Fig 6 :30: **"The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it in effect terms."** Fig 9 :30-31: *"your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than the cause"*. This is why the outreach email says "drought changes the fuel before the fire", an effect description anyone can check, rather than "drought is a preconditioned multivariate compound driver", a term only one literature reads.
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: *"When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."* For this question the checkable expectation is: order a pair of events differently in a real system and the recovery trajectory should differ; map the legacies and the effect should be steps out, not local.
- *Library of Babel* on stopping: the answer is reached when *"the finish line will also be the starting place"*, because by then *"you have composed the answer through the process of locating it."* Here the run stopped when the figure census (Fig 11, Fig 9, Fig 12, Fig 1) and the world sources converged on the same four constraints and no new figure line would add a sixth disturbance-specific clause.

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** Formula step 5: *"the ground settles it. Verified only by what pays."* The README: the framework *"stops at the limit of what the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."* For ECO-0033 the naming instrument would be a paired event-response dataset that discriminates the trajectory of drought-then-fire from fire-then-drought at the same site, with legacies mapped.
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs** (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52), with a source gate and a shadow test. Buma's linked/compound distinction is such an import into the derivation: it explains the gap between event lists and recovery trajectories, and its provenance is recorded in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

*They're Scanning Books When They Should* (mapped from the exemplar, not re-fetched this run): *"The real import is not a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space."* Also: *"Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the choice visible."* The attempt to declare heat, drought, fire, storm and flood as members of the six-term substrate was retracted for exactly this reason in audit section 6: it turns a visible choice into an invisible one.

## 4. The source problem: why a recovery curve does not name the compound

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. Metamerism: an instrument reads green and *"cannot comment on the source"*; one green light, or yellow plus blue, or RGB, or CMYK, or a scheme not yet discovered. *"If several paths create the same behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information."* And: *"An instrument that can't distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy."* It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved it.

Applied here: a landscape-level recovery index reads "ecosystem recovered to 90 percent of reference carbon by year 50" and cannot comment on the source. Drought-then-fire with retained legacies, fire-then-drought with no legacies, and heat-plus-drought with no fire can all produce that reading. Naming the compound from the recovery curve is the metamerism error. What is owed is a path-discriminating instrument, not a name assigned from a projection. This is the substantive reason F9 is a `gap` rather than a lazy omission.

## 5. Q2 from the exemplar, answered for this question

**What licenses a particular compound outcome?** Nothing in the framework. The framework returns a shape. A particular direction or magnitude is licensed only by the ground settling a minted term, or by a provenanced import with a shadow test. Until one of those, the outcome is the searcher's own contribution and must be recorded as such.

**Does the question's common framing commit the error the README forbids?** Often, yes. "Compound disturbances have synergistic effects" names an outcome direction from a reading (total impact larger than the sum) without an instrument that discriminates among the paths that can produce it. Lucash et al. (2018) found a less-than-sum result; Kleinman et al. (2019) found buffering interactions underrepresented in the literature; Burton et al. (2020) say promoting interactions *apparently* prevail. The honest form is the shape: sign is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49) and is a ground question.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"Prestige must never be allowed to validate an action directly... Prestige can fund an action. It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige."* A list of well-known compound-disturbance researchers assembled to agree with the shape is the named collapse, not corroboration. The legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a budget to be tried and let the ground, a paired event record or a legacy map, settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: *"LLMs can quickly simulate an approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post... However, the truly testable output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself."* So `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` is a simulated derivation, checked for internal consistency against the figures. It is **not** a test of the claim and must be labelled as such. It cannot settle anything it reports on.

## 7. Correction kept visible

I initially read the shape-not-a-name limit as forbidding any named disturbance or any named ecological outcome. That overstates it. The README's phrase governs a **missing** primitive or an unbound outcome. Naming an event that is already enumerated in the habitat is a provenanced binding from the world, not the invention of a new primitive. What remains forbidden is claiming that the framework derives the direction or magnitude of a compound's effect from the six terms plus the event list alone. Both the narrowed and the overstated reading are recorded here so neither is quietly lost.
