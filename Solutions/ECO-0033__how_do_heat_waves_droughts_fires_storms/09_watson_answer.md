## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about a question that names five disturbances and asks how they interact to produce ecological change. Option A: treat them as a list of separable events whose impacts add, and report one aggregate ecological impact. Option B: treat them as a union compound in which order and typed linkage are part of the object, route the effect through an enumerated habitat, track the response per compound and per slot, and leave numerical direction and magnitude to a ground measurement. What each leads to: A produces a single score and a single lever per event, and it averages away the intersections where the consequential change occurs; B produces a checkable shape, five refuters written in advance, and a named instrument debt, and it refuses to forecast a number it cannot derive.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Five named disturbances whose co-occurrence and sequence produce ecological change that single-event response functions fail to resolve. Arity: at least binary in events and third or higher order through the habitat, because contact cascades past direct effect (Fig 11 :52). Symmetry: the link is ordered and typed, so reversing the pair changes the compound; valence is residual-relative (Fig 11 :49). Scale: first order event and directly disturbed patch, second order legacy and contact path, third order community or landscape state. Fig 9 names a union compound, not one hard question and not a list.
- **Domain 1, disturbance regime.** Actors: heat, drought, fire, storm, flood as conditions and events in the habitat (Fig 11 :46 enumerates condition entries). Currency: forcing, that is intensity, duration, timing, sequence and bond type. Anchor: the meteorological and remote-sensing instrument record of the event, which does not move when a frame changes its mind. Question type: union with D2 and D3. Order: upstream.
- **Domain 2, habitat and contact.** Actors: survivors, biological legacies, species, relations (Fig 11 :45, :47). Currency: material and information legacies, contact. Anchor: field observation of who survives, colonises and interacts. Question type: a compound with its own loop, not a sum of D1 slots. Order: middle, and the unmeasured link.
- **Domain 3, ecological response.** Actors: populations, interactions, functions, state variables. Currency: resistance, resilience, recovery trajectory, state shift. Anchor: the measured recovery trajectory or state variable. Question type: compound; verdict per slot. Order: downstream.
- **Domain 4, verification and management.** Actors: ecologists, reviewers, land managers. Currency: agreement versus residual. Anchor: the ground, never agreement. Question type: outside frame. Order: last. In this simulated run all four domains draw on one evidence pool, so the second seat's independence is zero.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Event and sequence → habitat legacies and contact → response trajectory and state → management and verification. Refuse a universal sign before minting one (Formula step 2); describe the compound before choosing a literature frame (Fig 9 :27-28); settle before composing over a term (Formula step 5).

Strain test applied: Fig 9 :57's "two conditions" is a structural example, not a cardinality limit, so five overlapping conditions do not strain it. Fig 13 :31's amplifying remainder does not assert that every ecological chain amplifies, so the empirical dampening in Lucash et al. 2018 is a ground result, not a figure conflict. Four strains are recorded and kept visible in audit section 6.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the numbers do not exist in the figures and are not asserted. The five disturbances form a union compound with constitutive order and typed linkage (Fig 9 :55/:57, Fig 12 :94); the effect runs through an enumerated habitat and cascades past direct contact (Fig 11 :43/:48/:52); valence is residual-relative so no universal synergy follows (Fig 11 :49); response is tracked per compound and per ecological slot (Fig 1 :19); management adds legacies but cannot subtract contact (Fig 11 :61); and the numerical direction and magnitude of a named compound are a declared `gap` owed to a path-discriminating field instrument (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). The four domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged. This is `general`/`specified`, ungrounded, and simulated.

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet states mode, refuters and lineage before the conclusion, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a forecast instead of a shape, which Fig 2 :42 prices ("Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."). Also material: a wrong universal-synergy claim would have misdirected monitoring toward magnitude and away from order and contact paths. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. The retracted items (universal synergy, literal substrate mapping, statistical-interaction-only reading) are removable without touching the derivation. `07_the_tether_answer.md` and `08_expert_verification_packet.md` already remove them. A sent claim can be superseded by the corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. An order test showing reversed event order gives the same trajectory everywhere would break F3 and the isomer claim; a reach test showing no effect beyond direct contact would break F4; a sign test showing a legacy has a fixed sign would break F5; a discrimination test in which a recovery index cannot separate arrangements would confirm the metamerism gap rather than close it; a paired event-by-response dataset with legacies mapped across sites would name a compound outcome and move that specific claim toward `grounded`. A derivation of numerical direction from the six terms plus the event list alone would strengthen the framework but refute the claim that direction is owed to the ground. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
