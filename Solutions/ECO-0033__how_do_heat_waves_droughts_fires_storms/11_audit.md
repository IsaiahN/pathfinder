- **The numerical threshold and trajectory.** The shorthand says per-slot tracking; the figures supply no slot values, no trajectory shape and no threshold. That is the declared `gap` in F9.
- **Order of refusal before mint.** The shorthand records that a universal-synergy claim was refused first; a concretisation that simply forecasts a magnitude skips that refusal and reinstates the retracted reading.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a compound must be tracked by order and bond, through enumerated habitat contact, per ecological slot, with the direction left to the ground.
