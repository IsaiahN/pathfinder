# The Tether's answer: defining information when the distribution is unknown

**Mode: `specified` for the substantive claims, `general` for the structural shape. Not `grounded`.** This is a shape and a route, not a result. Simulated derivation checked against the figures (`05_what_shape_means.md` §6), not a ground reading.

## 1. The residual it answers

A frame owes a definition of "information" but does not hold the distribution p that Shannon's measure needs. Fig 1 :19: **"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."** The missing p is not a global ignorance; it is missing at individual receiver slots. The question bundles four different questions (carrier, receiver, missing distribution, instrument), so Fig 9 :4-5 applies: **"First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones. Split it rather than search."** Each domain keeps its own verdict; none is averaged.

## 2. The shape, per domain

1. **Bind information to per-slot residual reduction, not to an assigned prior.** Symbols table :36-40: **"It is the aim, not only the error signal, and it is always a slice: what cannot be perceived yet is residual and simply cannot be read."** A definition that works without p needs a prediction, an observation, a slot, and an anchor. This is a binding from the loop onto the word "information", not a derivation of the word from the six-term set. Mode `specified`, ungrounded.

2. **The anchor must not move when the assigning frame is pushed.** Fig 2 :29: **"An anchor is whatever settles the question and does not change its answer because of what any frame thinks."** Fig 2 :40: **"disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool"** A definition adopted by consensus is efficient for a convention and disqualifying for a verdict. The anchor is a receiver-level discriminating reading.

3. **A self-assigned distribution cannot score itself.** Fig 1 :27: **"They are frame-internal. A frame cannot score itself with a quantity it also produces."** Max entropy fills the gap with a number; the entropy of that number measures the frame's own choice. Using a prior as a working assumption is allowed; scoring that prior with its own entropy is not.

4. **The missing distribution is a reachability verdict, not a silence.** Fig 8 :32: **"Closure is idempotent. Composing inside a closed set never leaves it, so no search, however long, adds an atom. This one is definitional: it is what closure means."** Fig 6 :23: **"No frame escapes its own closure. The bound moves only on contact with a frame whose closure differs."** So the frame reports what it lacks in effect terms. Fig 13 :134: **"Unreachable is a verdict here, not a silence,"**

5. **The instrument comes from something already returning something.** Fig 6 :32: **"An instrument is not built from a description. It is improved from a worse instrument already returning something."** The proto-instrument here is the receiver's own error signal at a slot. Sharpen that before importing a new definition. SEARCHED first, then INWARD and OUTWARD as opposed tracks (`06_isomers_and_order.md` Q2).

6. **Shortest-description definitions are candidates and budget filters, not the verdict.** Fig 5 :45: **"This is minimum description length, Rissanen 1978, which is itself a composition of Kolmogorov complexity and Shannon coding put to work on model selection."** Fig 5 :48: **"Its own output is never a measure of success. A system can be enthusiastically curious and produce nothing."** Fig 9 :60: **"Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you a verdict."** Algorithmic complexity and MDL price a description; they do not tell us what information *is*.

7. **Keep bits and actions apart.** Fig 12 :125-126: **"A composition costs in two currencies, and they do not add."** **"Bits price the description. Actions price finding out whether it holds."** Any answer that adds the two is disqualified by construction.

8. **No single formula is named.** Fig 9 :32: **"you get a kind of thing, never the answer itself."** The six-term set cannot derive which substitute wins; naming one is a binding with provenance, not a result. The candidates are members of one functional group with different bonds, not isomers (`06` Q1). This is a declared `gap` on the unique definition.

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that information is Kolmogorov complexity, credal-set width, free energy, or Hartley log-cardinality. Each is a candidate binding with provenance, and none is settled.
- Not that the question is one problem. It is a union of four conditions with separate loops (`01` F6).
- Not that "unknown probabilities" means "no measure exists". Hartley's uniform-support measure exists for the finite equiprobable case; it is a filter for the support, not the general verdict.
- Not that more agreement among experts would settle it. Agreement about a convention is the product; agreement about the world from a shared pool is worthless as evidence.
- Not that a refusal is a deletion. Nothing is struck out; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added (Formula step 3).
- Not grounded. No instrument was run and no receiver reading was taken.

## 4. What would settle it — the ground

A source-discriminating instrument, not a further argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source*: a reading *"cannot comment on the source"*; several sources can give the same green. The corresponding questions here:

- **Slot test:** does a receiver's discriminable state change while the per-slot prediction error stays flat, or the error fall while no receiver state changes? (kills or keeps the binding at F4).
- **Closure test:** can a frame derive the missing p from inside its own closure, with no new atom and no peer frame, and have the derivation pay at the ground? (kills or keeps F3).
- **Anchor test:** does a candidate definition survive when every agreeing frame's evidence is replaced by a single pool, with no anchor that fails to move when pushed? (kills or keeps F1).
- **Union test:** is one definition necessary across physics, biology, cognition and society with one shared ground? (kills or keeps F6).
- **Currency test:** are description length and test cost the same quantity in the same units at the same level? (kills or keeps F7).

The five refuters were written before the run in `01` §4. A failure is more useful than an endorsement.

## 5. The honest stop

**Unreached, not unreachable.** Fig 6 :27: **"Which is why some unreachability is depth and some is genuine absence, and from inside the frame the two look the same."** Many cases of unknown p are unreachable-at-budget: the distribution is in the closure and more independent search would find it. Importing a new definition against a budget limit adds an atom that was not needed. Only a genuine underivability, shown by a source gate and a shadow test, licenses an import. The answer stops at the limit of the available instruments and records the lineage so the stop is checkable. Fig 8 :39: **"So a search that finds nothing is never a proof of absence."**

## 6. One-paragraph version for outreach

> The Tether does not give a new formula for information when the probabilities are unknown, and it does not claim to settle which existing definition is correct. It says that such a definition must work per receiver slot, must be anchored in something that does not move when the assigning frame is pushed, must not score a prior with a quantity the prior itself produced, must price the description and the test in separate currencies, and must stay a candidate until a ground pays. Missing probabilities are a reachability verdict, not a silence: search the current closure first, sharpen the receiver's error signal second, and only then import a frame with a different closure, with provenance shown. Shortest-description and credal-set definitions are useful filters and candidate bindings; they are not handed the verdict by the figures. What is offered is the shape any answer must have, five refuters written in advance, and the instrument questions that would close it. The slot stays open until the ground settles it.
