# WATSON answer, INF-0006

**Mode: `specified`, not `grounded`.** Figures are primary and decide. World sources are secondary, corroborate but never overrule, and a conflict returns the derivation to the domain census. This is a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading.

**Status: SIMULATED DERIVATION.** By its own rule (*The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: *"the output is only as good as the validator. The agent, or the tool, or the frame, or the theory, cannot validate itself"*), it cannot settle the question it reports on. No measurement was taken.

## QUESTION

How should information be defined when the relevant possibilities or probability distributions are unknown? In effect terms: what quantity does a frame track when its Shannon measure has no distribution to consume, and what would license a proposed replacement? Option A: keep the Shannon requirement and declare information undefined when p is missing. Leads to an honest silence at the cases of interest. Option B: name a distribution-free substitute as the definition. Leads to a name where the figures return a shape, a frame-internal self-score, and an import with no source gate. Option C: split the union, bind information to per-slot ground-anchored residual reduction, gate every candidate with guards and provenance, keep bits and actions apart, and declare a `gap` on the unique formula. Option C is what the figures decide.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain with actors / currency / anchor, partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** A three-place relation: frame-with-library, action/encoding, observation, indexed per slot. Arity three, ordered from source to channel to receiver. Symmetry: "information" is a comparison (more/less), so it carries an ordered/comparison bond, not a symmetric join. Scale: single receiver slot, a message or block, and a habitat of encoders and decoders across a lossy membrane. Fig 1 :19 "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated." Fig 9 :4-5 "First: is there a fact here? Disagreements that do not shrink with effort, where each rule keeps working well on a different subset, are not one hard question but several well-formed ones." "Split it rather than search."
- **D1 Carrier / source side.** Actors: signal states, channel, noise. Currency: distinguishable physical differences. Anchor: a reading at the receiver. Status: admitted.
- **D2 Receiver / frame side.** Actors: Γ, belief b, weighting w, per-slot R. Currency: residual reduction and description length. Anchor: the ground that settles whether the reading was right. Status: admitted.
- **D3 The missing distribution.** Actors: candidate distributions, priors, histories. Currency: weight between private history and inherited prior. Anchor: none nameable while p is genuinely unknown. Status: **not a domain yet**. It is the residual, the missing thing, not a domain with an anchor.
- **D4 Instrument / verification habitat.** Actors: instruments, encoders, decoders, peer frames. Currency: contact and independence. Anchor: the ground, which does not decay. Status: admitted.
- **X1 Information as a free-floating property of the signal alone.** No nameable anchor. Not a domain.
- **X2 Information as pure agreement among analysts.** Anchor is the agreement itself, which moves when pushed. Absorbed into D4 as a convention, never a verdict anchor.
- **Union, not partition.** Four domains with different anchors and different subsets. Fig 9 union rule and Fig 8 union-surplus rule govern. A case in several conditions is not a harder case of one.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** Perception, vocabulary, objective, planning, learn and carry. D1 before D2; D2's residual selects D4; D3 would have to be imported before it is asked again. SEARCHED first, then INWARD and OUTWARD as opposed tracks (Fig 6 :49-51).

Strain test applied: the no-self-score rule (Fig 1 :27) governs metrics a frame uses to grade itself; the MDL acceptance test (Fig 5 :45) governs the price of admitting a term into Γ. Different objects, so no strain after re-census.

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The figure-derived verdict: when the distribution is unknown, "information" should be bound to a per-slot, ground-anchored reduction of residual, with each candidate term passing support, reachability and novelty, paying an MDL bargain, priced in separate bit and action currencies, provenance-stamped for any import, and held as a candidate until the ground pays. It is a union of four domains with separate verdicts, never one averaged definition. The missing distribution is a `triangulated` reachability verdict across Fig 6 and Fig 8, not a silence. The unique formula is a declared `gap` (Fig 9 :32, Fig 13 :134). The binding of "information" to residual is `single-source` and framework-internal; the per-slot requirement is `triangulated`. Mode `specified`, ungrounded, candidate. The world sources corroborate the shape at low overlap where they are independent; they never hand down the verdict.

- **Cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states its refuters before the conclusion and records the lineage, so an error is findable. The real cost is spending a checker's independence by sending a theory before a residual, or importing a definition when the distribution was merely unreachable at this budget. Fig 2 :42 prices that: buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong. Both costs are avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuter and by running SEARCHED first.
- **Undoable:** yes. No term is deleted if the claim fails; a refutation is added and the reach falls because something was added rather than struck out (Formula step 3). The shape and its five falsifiers are recorded in a provenance trail so a later reader can locate the error (Fig 10 :62-64). A sent claim can be superseded by a corrected packet.
- **Reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A receiver-level instrument that discriminates two candidate definitions at a slot would name the slot; a frame deriving p inside its own closure with no new atom and paying at the ground would break the reachability verdict; one definition shown necessary across all four domains with one shared ground would collapse the union; bits and actions shown to be the same quantity would break the two-currency rule. Any of the five pre-registered refuters in `01_figure_grounded_audit.md` §4 also reverses it. Until one fires, the verdict stands as `candidate`.
