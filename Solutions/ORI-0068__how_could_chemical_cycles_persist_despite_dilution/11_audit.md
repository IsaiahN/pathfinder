## Round trip gap R_T

The Symbols table defines R_T as the gap between what was sent up and what came back down,
`gap(x, (T_E ∘ T_A)(x))`, measuring what the coarser description cannot hold. Fig 4 :30 requires it
to be measured rather than assumed. I ran the round trip on the condensation this folder actually
uses.

1. **Sent up (x):** the verbatim figure set, chiefly Fig 9 :55 (union compound), Fig 12 :119 and
   :121 (catalyst and cycle), Fig 13 :22 and :46-47 (spendable difference), Fig 10 :24 (channel
   versus ground), Fig 11 :39 and :66 (habitat and substitution).
2. **Abstraction (T_A):** the shorthand carried in `07` and `08`: a cycle must be a closed reaction
   set with a regenerated part, driven by a measured flux, embedded in a habitat that keeps
   supplying actors; changing conditions move the habitat, not the ground.
3. **Concretisation (T_E):** a concrete claim about a particular setting, for example that a given
   mineral pore or wet-dry pool was the persisting cycle.

**R_T is nonzero.** Three things the shorthand holds do not survive the trip back down:

- the **identity of the closure**: "closed under its own products" does not select which species
  close, and a particular pore is an import with a source gate, not a derivation (Fig 12 :104,
  Fig 8 :50-52);
- the **quantitative loss rate**: the shorthand carries no hydrolysis constant, dilution rate, or
  turnover count, and Fig 6 :32 requires an instrument improved from a worse one rather than an
  argument (F9);
- the **uniform reading of England's bound**: the shorthand says only "measured flux"; the world
  conflict with Kolchinsky 2024 shows that a universal inequality does not survive concretisation.

Conversely, one thing survives cleanly: a cycle must be a closure rather than a chain, and a
spendable difference plus a supplying habitat are required. That is the residue worth sending. R_T
was **not** measured numerically, because no coupled-versus-isolated reactor reading exists in this
folder; it is recorded qualitatively as the enumerated set of dropped constraints. Treating "the
formula is a condensed version of the metalaws" as identity would set R_T to zero and would be the
dead-reckoning error Fig 4 :30 names. The honest form is a lossy condensation with a recorded gap,
which is what `09_watson_answer.md` now states.
