## The question restated as an effect

A chemical cycle is a composition whose output re-enters as input. The question asks what must be
true of such a composition for it to keep closing when three erosions act at once: matter is
dispersed (dilution), products and bonds are broken (degradation), and the surroundings move
(changing conditions). Option A treats persistence as a property of the cycle alone, an intrinsic
robustness we could name. Option B treats it as a relation among a closing composition, a spendable
gradient, and a habitat that keeps supplying actors and lets selection run. Option A leads to a
name with no instrument behind it. Option B leads to a shape with three checkable sub-claims and a
refuter written in advance.

An answer would look like: a set of structural constraints that any persisting chemical cycle must
satisfy, stated before any particular molecule or setting is named, plus the measurement that would
settle each constraint. That sought-for shape is made an ordinary term in `01` and `09`, so it can
accrue a residual and be refused like anything else.

## What is deliberately not here

- No personal emails of named individuals. The ISSOL contact page publishes officer emails; they are
  deliberately not copied into `03_public_contact_points.csv`, and the CSV note says so.
- No ranking of people by how likely they are to agree with the claim.
- No bulk-send plan. No prestige-weighted list of famous origins-of-life researchers.
- No claim that this simulated derivation settles the question it reports on.

Three reasons, in the figures' own words:

1. Fig 2 :35: "Alignment is triangulation, not negotiation. A mirror cannot correct you."
2. Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool" - selecting recipients for receptiveness manufactures the correlation that makes their agreement worthless.
3. Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has absorbed the full framework before being asked for a verdict has joined the frame being checked.

Legally: public opt-in channels and manuscript portals avoid the failure that unsolicited bulk email
to harvested addresses creates in many jurisdictions. That is a practical failure on top of the
structural one.
