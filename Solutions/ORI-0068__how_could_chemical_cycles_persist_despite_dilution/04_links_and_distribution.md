## Wider literature named but not used as witnesses

The following appeared in the references of sources actually opened. They are listed as a map for a
reader, not as evidence: Kauffman 1971 and 1986 on autocatalytic sets; Eigen 1971 on
self-organization; Wächtershäuser 1990 on evolution of the first metabolic cycles; Baaske et al.
2007 on thermophoretic concentration in hydrothermal pores; Damer and Deamer 2020, "The Hot Spring
Hypothesis for an Origin of Life"; Russell et al. 2014 on wet and icy worlds. Each would need its own
fetch and ledger row before it could carry weight. They are not in `10_source_ledger.csv`.

A genuine conflict in the opened literature: England 2013 versus Kolchinsky 2024 on whether
dissipation bounds replicator growth and decay. By the figures' rule, a world conflict sends the
derivation back to the census. The re-census narrowed D2's anchor to a measured flux in a specific
habitat; it did not flip the qualitative verdict that a spendable difference is required. Agreement
from a shared evidence pool is one witness, not two; Xavier et al. and Schwander et al. both include
Martin and draw on the same ancient-autotroph literature, so their agreement is recorded as overlap.

## Distribution: offer, do not push

Figure-grounded reasons:

- Fig 4 :22: "Only methods cross up. Only head starts come down." Sending the full architecture
  before a recipient has described a residual sends a recording, not a generator. Lead with the
  three constraints and the refuter; offer links after.
- Fig 10 :42: "A verifier that can reconstruct the claim cannot verify it." A recipient who has
  absorbed the whole framework before the verdict has joined the frame being checked.
- Fig 9 :27-28: "describe it before you go looking, or any frame you pick will seem to fit." Ask
  what they would expect to see if the shape claim were true before giving them the article.
- Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict: agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing
  frames share an evidence pool." A list selected for receptiveness produces a convention, not a
  check.
- Fig 9 :60: "Use filters for the budget and witnesses for the verdict. Never let a filter hand you
  a verdict." Journal impact and author prominence are budget filters, never verdicts.

**Recommended order in a reply:** (1) the three constraints and their refuters; (2) ask what they
would count as a failure in their own system; (3) record that answer; (4) only then the links, with
the note that the figures, not the summary, are the authority.

## The two-stage offer (with a third, opt-in stage)

1. **Stage 1:** validate the shape claim against the ground. The recipient checks the three
   constraints without holding the framework's interior.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** if curious, read the theory that produced it. By Fig 2 :40, later
   agreement is never pooled as corroboration.
3. **Stage 3:** use the formula recursively on another problem, with a self-written refuter and a
   stated mode. This sends a method, which Fig 4 :22 permits.

**Hard precondition:** stages 1 to 3 work only if stage 1 survives. The refuters in `01` §5 and
`08` §2 are the test. If one fires, stop and rewrite rather than distribute.

## Hosting

Nothing is sent by this agent, and no archive is created here. If a packet is hosted, prefer an
opt-in download page that lets a recipient decline contact over an attachment pushed to a list.
That is closer to the figure's rule than a mass send, and it avoids the legal failure that
unsolicited bulk email creates in many jurisdictions.
