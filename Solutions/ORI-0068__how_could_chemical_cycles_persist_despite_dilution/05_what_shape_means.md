## 1. A shape is a constraint, not a filler

The canonical figures README (local copy) states the limit: the framework returns "what a missing
thing would have to look like (a shape, not a name)." Fig 9 :32: "you get a kind of thing, never the
answer itself." Fig 13: "It can say the answer is out of reach with the library as it stands... It
cannot say which primitive is missing, because naming it would mean already holding it."

From *The Library of Babel*: "you can cut your search short if the set of books returned is small
and descriptive enough that it provides a general shape of the answer that satisfies your question."

From *How Intelligence Actually Works*: "At some point the recursion of the 'shape' will have carved
away at so many aspects, that it will fall out of perceptible range and be indistinguishable, in
your frame, from the answer." And the boundary: "the recursion converges toward indiscernibility,
and indiscernibility is not identity."

So a **shape** is the narrowed form of an answer you can state before you can name it. It constrains;
it does not fill. For this question the shape is a three-slot relation, not "the molecule that did
it." Saying "a persisting cycle is closed under its own products" is a shape. Saying "thiol esters
in alkaline vents were the persisting cycle" is a name, and it needs a provenance record.

## 2. How a shape is obtained

- *Library of Babel*: "If you don't know the right terms to search for, you will have to use the
  existing vocabulary you do know that is closest to the words that you wish to describe and grasp
  the shape of." Then: "With each new term gained, you are able to narrow the space by definition and
  deepen your vocabulary."
- *Library of Babel* on what ends a search: one possibility is that "your problem was a bundle or
  conflation of several problems masquerading as one." That is exactly the finding in `01` F5: this
  question is a union of three conditions, so the shape has three slots.
- Fig 6 :30: "The description travels. Fails by being sharpened into terms only we can read. Cast it
  in effect terms." Fig 9 :30-31: "your words are borrowed too, so describe the effect rather than
  the cause."
- *Decoding Tacit Knowledge*: "When contact isn't possible, the best you can send is a plain
  description of what you'd expect to see, because anyone can check that against the world."
- *Arrival of the Fittest* carries the same instruction into the loop's "Direction" section: a frame
  "cannot check the direction of its own question, because the shape a frame is searching for is not
  a term it holds." The remedy is to "make the sought-for shape an ORDINARY TERM - write down what an
  answer would look like, bind it, and let it accrue a residual like anything else."

## 3. What licenses a name

Only two things, both outside the shape itself:

1. **The ground settles it.** `THE_FORMULA.md` step 5: "the ground settles it. Verified only by what
   pays. A gate is not the ground." The figures README: the framework "stops at the limit of what
   the available instruments can distinguish, and records the lineage so the stop is honest."
2. **A provenanced import from a frame whose closure differs**, with a source gate and a shadow test
   (Fig 6 :23, Fig 8 :50-52). Fig 8 :51: "Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in
   the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them."

*They're Scanning Books When They Should Be Studying Nature* sharpens this: "The real import is not
a new term in the existing space. It is a new dimension of the space." And: "A minimum viable set of
six terms is a human's careful guess at reality's compression axes; another sensorium's minimum set
need not be those six." Also: "Declaring the primitives does not make them neutral. It makes the
choice visible."

For origins of life this is the distinction between deriving the shape of persistence and importing
a particular geochemical venue. The venue is a world-frame import. It is welcome, but it carries its
source and its shadow test or it carries no weight.

## 4. Why the instrument, not a further argument, settles the thresholds

*The Hard Problem is About Source* gives the mechanism. An instrument reads green and "cannot comment
on the source." The reading does not determine the source. "If several paths create the same
behavior, then behavior is a projection with lossy information." And: "An instrument that can't
distinguish these cases demonstrates its own inadequacy, it cannot claim anything substantial about
what is being measured or what causes it." It was the spectrometer, not an argument, that resolved
it.

Applied here: a claim that "dilution alone explains the failure" reads one projection. Several
sources can give the same reading: loss of contact with a mineral surface, hydrolysis of the product,
a gradient that ran down, or a habitat substitution that failed to reproduce an actor. What is owed
is an instrument that discriminates sources: a coupled-versus-isolated reactor with a controlled
loss rate, of the kind Kato 2026 proposes as a test rather than as a result. `01` F9 records this as a
`gap`, not a failure to think hard enough.

## 5. What "shape" means specifically for this question

Putting §1 to §4 together, the shape an answer to ORI-0068 must have is:

- **closed under its own products**, with a part that is regenerated rather than spent;
- **driven by a difference that can be spent**, with a measured flux as the anchor, not a universal
  inequality;
- **embedded in a habitat** that keeps supplying actors and lets selection run, with encapsulation
  treated as a substitution of habitat rather than an escape from dilution;
- **stated with an instrument per slot**, so that a failure in one slot is visible and does not
  disappear into an average "robustness" score.

This is the ordinary term bound in `01` §0. It has accrued a residual against the three refuters in
`01` §5 and has not been refused by them within this simulated derivation. That is a result about
internal consistency, not about the early Earth.

### Correction to my own §3 (carried forward from the exemplar's discipline)

I initially wrote "the shape-not-a-name limit forbids naming any chemistry." That overstated it. The
README's phrase governs a **missing** thing, a primitive not held in the frame. Naming an atom that
the frame already holds, or importing a venue from a world frame, is not forbidden; it is a binding
that must be provenanced. What remains forbidden is presenting that binding as a derivation or as a
grounded answer. Both the narrower and the earlier reading are recorded here so the correction is
visible rather than silent.

## 6. Two consequences for outreach

**Prestige is not action.** From *The Recursive Self-Improvement Trap*: "Prestige can fund an action.
It can give an action a budget to be tried. But it cannot settle an action. Only the ground can
settle it. The Ground Maintainer must be blind to prestige." A list of well-known origins-of-life
researchers assembled to agree with the shape claim is the named collapse, not corroboration. The
legitimate ask is the opposite: give the claim a test, and let a reactor, a measurement, or a board
settle it.

**My own audit is a simulation, not a ground reading.** Same essay: "LLMs can quickly simulate an
approximate answer/output with the formula found in the architecture post" and "the truly testable
output would need to be derived from the real architecture or working the formula yourself." The
architecture essay adds: "the outputs generated must be seen as simulacra" and "could never be
verified if the solutions presented were not contrived to fit the formula." So `01`, `06`, `07`,
`09`, and `11` are simulated derivations checked for internal consistency against the figures. They
are **not** tests of the claim and must be labelled as such. They cannot settle anything they report
on.
