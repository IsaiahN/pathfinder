**Use:** one-to-one, or as a cover note attached to a submission through the public venues in
`03_public_contact_points.csv`. **Not** for bulk send.

**Hard precondition:** send the shape claim and its refuter only. Do not attach the framework, the

**Subject:** Check one structural claim about chemical-cycle persistence before deciding if the theory is worth reading

> Hi [name],
>
> I have one specific output I would like you to check against what you already know about prebiotic
> chemistry. I am not asking you to read the framework first, and I am not asking you to agree with
> it. If the output does not survive, there is nothing else to look at.
>
> **The context.** A chemical cycle is a composition whose output re-enters as input. The question
> is what lets it keep closing while matter is dispersed (dilution), products and bonds are broken
> (degradation), and conditions move. My derivation says these are three overlapping conditions,
> not one hard problem, so they need three separate answers and three separate refuters.
>
> **The output (`specified`, not `grounded`).** Three structural constraints:
>
> 1. The reaction set must be closed under its own products, with a part that is regenerated rather
>    than spent. A one-through chain cannot close by construction.
> 2. It must be driven by a difference that can be spent. Energy supplied without a gradient is
>    directionless on its own. I am not claiming a universal thermodynamic bound; the anchor here
>    is a measured flux in the specific setting.
> 3. Persistence is a property of the cycle and its habitat, not of the cycle alone. Dilution is a
>    loss of contact with actors, and in an unmaintained setting selection, not design, decides
>    what remains. Encapsulation substitutes a habitat; it does not remove one.
>
> This is a **shape**, not a name. It says what any persisting cycle must look like; it does not say
> which molecule, vent, or pool did it. If I named one without a measured, provenanced reason, that
> would be projection, not derivation.
>
> **What would refute it (written before this message):**
>
> 1. A linear reaction set, with no re-entry of output as input and no regenerated catalyst, holds a
>    steady product population indefinitely under the same loss rates as a matched closed cycle.
> 2. A cycle holds closure across repeated turnovers with zero net spendable gradient, measured with
>    an instrument that would register a flux if one were present.
> 3. Persistence in an isolated, unchanging habitat equals persistence in a replenished habitat with
>    selection acting, under the same perturbation schedule and starting chemistry.
> 4. The thing that decides persistence moves when I re-describe it, so that agreement rather than a
>    measurement settles the question.
>
> If any of these holds, I withdraw the corresponding claim and rewrite the synthesis. A one-line
> "this fails because..." is more useful to me than agreement. If you would rather not engage, no
> reply is needed.
>
> **What I am not claiming:** not a theory of everything; not that a particular molecule or site was
> the answer; not that membranes abolished dilution; not a derivation of a quantitative loss rate;
> not `grounded`. The quantitative thresholds are an open gap, and I am recording them as such.
>
> **If it survives, and only then:** I can send the sources. The figures, not my summary of them,
> are the authority.
> - Figures and formula for direct download: https://github.com/IsaiahN/tether/tree/general-agent/figures
> - Worked narrative and the six-term derivation: https://adventuresinml.substack.com/p/how-intelligence-actually-works
> - Full architecture (19 min): https://medium.com/@IsaiahNwukor/arrival-of-the-fittest-the-tether-architecture-1de7edf641ec
>
> **If you want to use the machinery:** the formula is in the figures link. Run it on a problem you
> know well and can check against a ground. Write down what would refute your result *before* you
> run it, and state the mode of what you get. A reported failure is more useful to me than a
> reported success.
>
> Thanks for reading,
> [name]

## Stage order

1. **Stage 1:** the three constraints, their refuters, and the request for a failure. Record the
   verdict before any theory is shown.
2. **Stage 2, opt-in only:** the three links, with the note that the figures are the authority.
3. **Stage 3:** the formula as a method to reuse, with each user writing their own refuter and
   stating their own mode.

Later agreement is never pooled as corroboration (Fig 2 :40: "disqualifying for a verdict:
agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool").
A reply from a Track B style direct contact is a conversation, never a witness.
