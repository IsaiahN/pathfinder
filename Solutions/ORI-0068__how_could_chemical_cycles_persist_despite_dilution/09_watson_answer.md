## QUESTION

Decide, in effect terms, what the Tether says about persistence of a chemical cycle under three
erosions: dispersal of matter (dilution), transformation of products and bonds (degradation), and
movement of the surroundings (changing conditions). Option A: treat persistence as an intrinsic
property of the cycle and name the robust molecule or site. Option B: treat it as a relation among a
reaction closure, a spendable difference, and a habitat that keeps supplying actors and lets
selection run. Option A leads to a name with no discriminating instrument behind it. Option B leads
to a three-slot shape, four pre-registered refuters, and a stated `gap` on thresholds.

## DOMAIN CENSUS

R by arity, symmetry and scale, then one line per domain giving actors / currency / anchor,
partition or union, and order by dependency.

- **R (the residual).** Arity: three overlapping conditions, so a **union compound**, not one hard
  question: "a bundled problem is usually a union of overlapping conditions, not a list of separate
  ones" (Fig 9 :55). Symmetry: not exchangeable, because the sign of the loop decides whether it
  closes (Fig 12 :121), and dilution removes actors while degradation transforms products. Scale:
  first order for one turnover of the cycle, second order for persistence across environments.
  Per-slot: "The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated" (Fig 1 :19).
- **D1, compositional closure.** Actors: molecule species as atoms, catalyses as bonds, cycle and
  catalyst as structures. Currency: whether the loop closes and whether the catalyst survives its
  own use. Anchor: the figure text defining cycle and catalyst, plus a stoichiometric closure test.
  Partition: which compositions close.
- **D2, thermodynamic drive.** Actors: energy, gradient, spectrum, network. Currency: what can be
  spent. Anchor: a measured flux in the specific habitat. Partition: which differences can be spent.
- **D3, habitat and selection.** Actors: the enumerated habitat (actors, conditions, relations),
  mineral compartments, protocell populations. Currency: contact and capability. Anchor: differential
  survival and product retention in a coupled-versus-isolated reactor. Union: overlapping conditions
  unbundled.
- **D4, degradation as a separate domain.** Considered and declined: no anchor distinct from the D2
  flux measurement and the D3 retention measurement. A reaction class without its own instrument is
  not yet a domain. Folded into D2 and D3.
- **Order by dependency (Fig 3).** D3 habitat supplies actors and food, D2 gradient supplies the
  spendable difference, D1 cycle composes the actors into a closure. Strengthening D1 cannot rescue
  a broken link in D3.

Strain test applied: reading "changing environmental conditions" as a moving ground strains Fig 10
:24. Re-census: the habitat changes, the ground does not. Reading "cycle" as one molecule's
trajectory strains Fig 12 :121. Re-census: the object is a structural closure of a reaction set.
Both strains were dissolved by re-census, not smoothed over (see `01` §7).

## VERDICT (from the figures)

The shape survives; the nominal answer does not. A persisting chemical cycle must be a closure under
its own products with a regenerated part, driven by a spendable difference measured as a flux, and
embedded in a habitat that keeps supplying actors and lets selection run. Changing conditions move
the habitat, not the ground; encapsulation substitutes a habitat rather than removing dilution. A
settled cycle becomes an atom for habitat-level selection, so the second order needs no new
primitive. The framework does not name a molecule or a site, and no figure line gives a quantitative
loss threshold. This is `specified`, ungrounded, and candidate. The strongest single figure-grounded
move is the habitat/ground reframe (F2), followed by the closure/catalyst distinction (F1).

- **cost if wrong:** bounded and locatable. The packet is mode-labelled, states four refuters before
  the conclusion, and records the lineage, so an error is findable rather than invisible. The real
  cost is spending the independence of outside readers by sending a recording instead of a
  generator, which Fig 2 :42 prices as "Buy independence in proportion to the cost of being wrong."
  That cost is avoidable by leading with the shape and its refuters.
- **undoable:** yes. A failed sub-claim can be withdrawn without touching the other two; the
  domains have separate verdicts and are never averaged. A claim already sent can be superseded by a
  corrected packet, and no primitive has to be withdrawn. The `gap` on thresholds can be closed by
  adding a measurement without revising the shape.
- **reversed by:** a ground reading, not agreement. A coupled-versus-isolated reactor showing that a
  linear chain persists as well as a closed cycle would break C1; an instrument registering zero
  spendable gradient while closure persists would break C2; equivalence of isolated and replenished
  habitats under the same perturbations would break C3; a settling quantity that moves when
  re-described would break the anchor. A figure re-census that admits a different object for
  "cycle" or "ground" would also require a rewrite. Until one of these fires, the verdict stands as
  candidate.
