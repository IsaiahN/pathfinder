# Expert verification packet: the Tether on black hole microstate counts

**What this is:** a claim in a form a physicist can check. Mode, derivation and refuters are stated before the conclusion, so a reader knows what to look for and what would settle it.

**Mode: `specified` on the physics-facing claims, `general` on the bond analysis, `gap` on the general identification. Not `grounded`.** No measurement at the horizon scale is claimed. This is a shape and a route, not a result.

**Status:** a simulated derivation checked for internal consistency against the figures, not a ground reading. It cannot settle the claim it reports on.

## 1. The claim, in one paragraph

"Does counting black hole microstates reproduce the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy" is two conditions in one sentence. Condition one: a count returns `A/4`. Condition two: the counted states are the degrees of freedom of a general black hole. Condition one is met, with a published derivation record, for extremal and near-extremal supersymmetric black holes. Condition two is not characterized for a non-supersymmetric hole. The equality between the two entropies is a comparison (⋛), not an identity (≡), and it is corroboration only when the count was derived independently in a closure that differs from the semiclassical one, because convergent derivation and an adopted import look identical in their contents. A successful count must quotient symmetric relabelings before taking `log Ω`, since a mispricing enters in the same logarithmic currency. The framework returns this shape and these refuters; it does not name the microstates by derivation.

## 2. Three checkable sub-claims

### C1. Physics-facing: the two conditions have different statuses

- **Statement:** the reproduction condition is established for a protected class; the identification condition is open for the unprotected class, so a single global "yes" is an invalid aggregation.
- **Source, verbatim figure lines:** Fig 1 :19 *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22 *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* Fig 9 :57 *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."*
- **World check:** the opened Wikipedia *Black hole thermodynamics* page states that after Strominger-Vafa, *"many similar computations of entropy of large classes of other extremal and near-extremal black holes ... always agreed with the Bekenstein-Hawking formula"* but *"for the Schwarzschild black hole ... the relationship between micro- and macrostates has not been characterized."*
- **Refuter:** exhibit a count that reproduces `A/4` for a non-extremal, non-supersymmetric black hole with an independent derivation record. If that exists, the `gap` on condition two is withdrawn and the global "yes" becomes defensible for that class.

### C2. Framework-internal plus physics-facing: convergence requires provenance

- **Statement:** two low-overlap closures reaching area scaling is corroboration at maximum independence only if their derivation records are independent. Otherwise they are one witness.
- **Source, verbatim:** Fig 8 :50 *"Two frames can hold the same atom by convergence, reaching it separately, which is corroboration at maximum independence."* Fig 8 :51 *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."* Fig 2 :31 *"Independence, between two frames, is how little they draw on the same evidence."* Fig 2 :40 *"agreement about the world is worthless when the agreeing frames share an evidence pool."*
- **What to check:** did each count fix its normalization from its own frame, or from the semiclassical area law? The opened Sen 1995 abstract says *"stringy effects could correct the Bekenstein-Hawking formula for the black hole entropy in such a way that it correctly reproduces the logarithm of the density of elementary string states."* That is a record to read, not an assumption to make.
- **Refuter:** show that both counts draw on one evidence pool, for example both normalized to the same semiclassical result. If so, they are one witness and the triangulation status is withdrawn.

### C3. Framework-internal: the equality is a comparison, and counting is bond-symmetric

- **Statement:** ⋛ is *"a threshold relation between two quantities"* (Operators table, Fig 12 :87), whereas identity ≡ is *"two names, one referent."* Substituting one entropy reading for the other loses the anchor on one side and the enumeration on the other, so the equality is not an identity. Separately, a count that charges for symmetric relabelings overcharges by `log₂(k!)`: Fig 12 :92 *"Under a symmetric bond, k operands have k! spellings and one term, so a code that charges for the sequence overcharges by log₂(k!)"*
- **Cross-check:** the opened Wikipedia *Entropy (statistical thermodynamics)* page says neglecting statistical dependencies *"will lead to an incorrect probability distribution on the microstates and hence to an overestimate of the entropy,"* and coarse graining leaves entropy *"defined only up to an additive constant."* Low overlap with the figures, so this is genuine corroboration of the shape, not a restatement of it.
- **Refuter:** if substituting the statistical reading for the geometric one loses nothing, the identity reading wins; if two counts of the same system that differ only in gauge quotienting give different logarithms, the symmetry-pricing claim is falsified.

## 3. What is explicitly not claimed

- Not that the microscopic degrees of freedom of a general black hole have been identified.
- Not that a numerical match settles the identification question. The match is a retrodiction: Fig 8 :56-57 *"The triangulation claim fits four cases already known and has not been tested forward: Wiles and Frey, Montgomery and Dyson, Bekenstein and holography, the relativization barriers in complexity theory."*
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."*
- Not that the string and loop counts are the same states. They are convergent derivations to one atom over different atom sets; the isomer version is a separate, falsifiable claim in `06_isomers_and_order.md`.
- Not that the exact coefficient `1/4` is derived inside each counting frame from the material read. Left open where the sources do not settle it.
- Not `grounded`. No measurement was taken by this agent, and the derivation is simulated.

## 4. Five pre-registered refuters (written before the conclusion)

1. A count that returns `A/4` only after normalizing to demand `A/4`, with no independent derivation record (adopted import, not convergence).
2. A count that cannot extend beyond its protecting symmetry, offered as a general answer (one-occasion failure, Fig 5 :43-44).
3. A demonstration that the two counting frames share one evidence pool (one witness, not two).
4. A forward prediction from the counted states that fails at a discriminator.
5. A derivation of one of the six substrate terms from the other five, or a direct state-state composition that cannot be refused.
