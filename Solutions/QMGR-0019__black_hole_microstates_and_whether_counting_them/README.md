## The residual in effect terms

There are two readings of one number. The geometric reading gets `S_BH = A/4` from horizon area plus thermodynamics. The microscopic reading gets `S_stat = log Ω` from counting quantum states in a candidate theory. The question asks whether these are the same quantity. Option A (the count reproduces the entropy) licenses treating black hole entropy as a statistical count and makes the counted states candidates for the degrees of freedom of the hole. Option B (the count matches only in a protected sector, or was tuned to match) leaves the equality as a retrodiction and the general microscopic origin open. The two options are not contradictories: the question is a **union** of a reproduction condition and an identification condition, with different anchors, so per-domain verdicts are kept separate and never averaged.
