## 1. The residual it answers

Two readings of one number. The geometric reading gets `S_BH = A/4` from horizon area plus thermodynamics. The microscopic reading gets `S_stat = k_B log Ω` from counting quantum states. The question asks whether these are the same quantity. The residual is not the arithmetic; it is the provenance of the match. Fig 8 :50-52: *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents. Only the record of where each came from separates them, and the surplus lives only where the closures differ."*

The question is a **union** of two conditions, not one hard question with a yes/no answer: reproduction of the number, and identification of the states. Fig 9 :57: *"a case in two conditions is not a harder case of either."* Verdicts are kept per domain and never averaged.

## 2. The shape (one loop per domain)

1. **The equality is a comparison, not an identity.** Fig 12 :87 defines ⋛ as *"a threshold relation between two quantities."* Identity is *"two names, one referent."* Substituting the statistical reading for the geometric one loses something: the anchor on one side, the enumeration on the other. So the two readings compare equal in a domain; they do not become one term. The word "equals" in the question is doing the work of ⋛, not of ≡.

2. **Ask per black-hole class, not globally.** Fig 1 :19: *"The residual is indexed by object slot, not aggregated."* Fig 1 :22: *"Aggregating across slots is how a live signal disappears into an average."* The live slot is the unprotected case. However, for the Schwarzschild black hole, viewed as the furthest-from-extremal black hole, the relationship between micro- and macrostates has not been characterized."*

## 3. What it explicitly does not say

- Not that the identification question is settled. It is a `gap` for the unprotected case, and `gap` is a verdict, not silence (Fig 13 :134).
- Not that a match of numbers is sufficient. *"Convergent derivation and adopted import look identical in the contents"* (Fig 8 :51).
- Not a theory of everything. Fig 7 :6 names *"the limit: no last room."* A final room for black hole microstructure would be exactly what the figures forbid.
- Not that any of this is `grounded`. No measurement at the horizon scale was taken, and this is a simulation.
- Not that the exact coefficient `1/4` is derived inside each counting frame from the abstracts read. That question is left open where the sources do not settle it.

## 4. What would settle it

A source-discriminating instrument, not another argument. *The Hard Problem is About Source* (carried) says an instrument that reads green *"cannot comment on the source"*; the spectrometer resolved it. Here the corresponding tests are:

- **Fitted-count test.** Is there an independent derivation record, or was a parameter chosen to force `A/4`? This is checkable from the literature today, without new apparatus.
- **Extension test.** Does the count survive removal of the protecting symmetry and return the entropy of a non-extremal black hole? The opened sources say this is not yet characterized.
- **Shared-pool test.** Do the string and loop counts draw on one evidence pool? If so, the convergence collapses to one witness (Fig 2 :40).
- **Forward test.** A definite prediction from the counted states at a discriminator that could fail. None is established in the sources read. Fig 6 :34: *"Where nothing is, there is no edge to extend from, and the search for a proto-instrument is a search for one that is already there."* Here stationarity and horizon area are already returning readings, so an edge exists; the search is unreached, not ruled out.
- Standing substrate falsifiers: derive one of the six terms from the other five, or admit a direct state-state composition the framework cannot refuse (Fig 13 :75).
